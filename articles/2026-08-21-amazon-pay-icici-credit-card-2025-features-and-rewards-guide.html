Amazon Pay ICICI Credit Card: 2025 Features and Rewards Guide

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Credit Cards & Cashback Original source ↗
Key points
  • 5% unlimited cashback for Prime members on Amazon India
  • 3% cashback for non-Prime customers on Amazon
  • Zero joining and annual fees for life
  • 1% cashback on all other eligible merchant spends

Understanding the Reward Structure

The Amazon Pay ICICI Credit Card remains a top choice for Indian consumers due to its straightforward cashback model. Prime members earn 5% cashback on all purchases made on Amazon.in. Non-Prime members receive 3% cashback on the same platform.

Beyond the Amazon ecosystem, users earn 2% cashback on payments made via Amazon Pay to partner merchants, including utility bills, recharges, and bookings. All other retail spends, excluding fuel, earn a flat 1% cashback. Rewards are credited directly as Amazon Pay balance after each billing cycle, eliminating the need for complex point redemption portals.

Fees and Financial Benefits

This card operates on a lifetime-free basis. You pay zero joining fees and zero annual maintenance fees. This structure makes it a low-risk addition to any wallet, as there is no recurring cost to maintain the account.

The card also offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver at all petrol pumps across India, provided the transaction is made using the ICICI Bank card machine. Users should note that cashback is not applicable on fuel transactions, gold purchases, or EMI transactions. Interest rates on outstanding balances apply as per bank policy, so clearing the full statement balance each month is essential to maintain the product's value.

Eligibility and Application Process

To qualify for this card, you must be an Indian resident between 18 and 60 years of age. While specific income criteria are determined by ICICI Bank’s internal underwriting, a stable credit history and a good CIBIL score significantly increase approval chances. You will need your PAN card, Aadhaar number, and proof of address for the digital verification process.

Applying online is the most efficient method to receive potential welcome rewards. Log in to your Amazon India mobile app and navigate to the 'Amazon Pay' section. Click on the credit card banner to initiate the application. The system will pre-fill your details if you are an existing Amazon customer. Complete the Video KYC process through the provided link to finalize your application without visiting a bank branch.

Who Should Use This Card?

This card is the ideal instrument for frequent Amazon shoppers who want to automate their savings. Because the cashback is credited as Amazon Pay balance, it functions like cash for future utility bills or online orders. If you are a Prime member who regularly buys groceries, electronics, or household goods online, the 5% return provides immediate value that few other entry-level cards can match. It is not, however, designed for users seeking luxury travel perks like international airport lounge access or comprehensive golf benefits.
