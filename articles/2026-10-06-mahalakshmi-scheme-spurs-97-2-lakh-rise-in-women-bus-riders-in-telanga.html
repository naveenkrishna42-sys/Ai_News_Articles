Mahalakshmi Scheme Spurs 97.2 Lakh Rise in Women Bus Riders in Telangana

  • The scheme has added nearly 10 million women passengers to TSRTC ridership since its launch.
  • Free travel access has improved women's workforce participation and educational attendance.

Overview of the Mahalakshmi Scheme

The Mahalakshmi scheme, introduced by the Telangana government in 2022, provides free bus travel for all women on state‑run TSRTC services. Its primary objective is to enhance women’s mobility, thereby supporting greater participation in education, employment, and healthcare. Eligibility extends to any female resident of Telangana, irrespective of income or age, and the benefit covers ordinary and express buses across the entire network.

Impact on Women's Mobility and Ridership

Official TSRTC data indicate that the scheme has contributed to an increase of 97.20 lakh women passengers since inception. This surge represents a significant shift in travel patterns, with many women reporting reduced reliance on private or informal transport. Studies conducted by local universities have linked the scheme to a measurable rise in female college enrollment and workforce attendance in urban centers such as Hyderabad and Warangal.

Changes in Travel Behaviour

Beneficiaries frequently cite cost savings as the main factor enabling more frequent trips, including non‑work‑related journeys like market visits and medical appointments. The scheme has also altered peak‑hour demand, leading to a flatter ridership curve throughout the day. Transport planners note that this has helped optimize bus deployment and reduce congestion during traditional rush hours.

Financial and Operational Implications for TSRTC

Providing free travel imposes a substantial subsidy burden on the state exchequer. While exact annual allocations are detailed in Telangana’s budget documents, the scheme’s cost is primarily absorbed through the transport department’s allocation, which must balance fare‑free service with maintenance, fuel, and staff expenses. TSRTC has reported a need for additional buses and increased frequency on high‑demand corridors to accommodate the extra load without compromising service quality.

Budgetary Considerations

State officials have emphasized that the Mahalakshmi scheme is funded through a dedicated line item in the annual budget, with periodic reviews to assess fiscal sustainability. Any adjustments to the scheme’s scope would require legislative approval and a clear impact analysis on both ridership figures and the state’s deficit targets.

Challenges and Future Prospects

Despite its successes, the scheme faces challenges such as overcrowding on popular routes, occasional delays in bus arrivals, and the need for robust monitoring to prevent misuse. Addressing these issues involves investing in fleet expansion, improving depot infrastructure, and implementing real‑time tracking systems for better service management.

Policy Outlook

Looking ahead, policymakers are evaluating complementary measures, including last‑mile connectivity solutions and targeted awareness campaigns, to maximize the scheme’s developmental benefits. Continued data‑driven assessment will be essential to ensure that the Mahalakshmi initiative remains both socially inclusive and financially viable for Telangana’s growing population.
