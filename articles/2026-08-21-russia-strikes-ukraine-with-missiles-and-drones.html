Russia strikes Ukraine with missiles and drones

📅 2026-08-21 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Russian forces launched a barrage of missiles and drones on Ukraine.
  • Kyiv was the main target, with attacks also reaching Romania and Moldova.
  • At least 16 people were killed in Ukraine's Kyiv region.
  • Ukrainian President Zelensky called for a 'global response' to the strikes.

Russian forces launch missile and drone barrage on Ukraine

Russian forces launched a barrage of ballistic, cruise, and hypersonic missiles, along with drones, on Ukraine overnight. Kyiv was the main target, with attacks also reaching Romania and Moldova. The strikes began shortly after midnight local time, with Russian forces targeting critical infrastructure and populated areas.

Casualties reported in Kyiv region

At least 16 people were killed in Ukraine's Kyiv region as a result of the Russian strikes. The attacks caused widespread damage to buildings and infrastructure, with many people left homeless. The Ukrainian government has not yet provided a full breakdown of the casualties, but officials are warning that the death toll could rise as rescue efforts continue.

Zelensky calls for global response

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has urged for a 'global response' to Russia's overnight strike. Speaking to the media, Zelensky said that the lack of Patriot air defense systems was costing lives. He called on the international community to provide the necessary support to Ukraine to defend itself against the Russian aggression.

Strikes spill over to neighboring countries

The Russian strikes did not stop at Ukraine's borders. Attacks reached Romania and Moldova, causing damage and disruption in these neighboring countries. Romanian and Moldovan officials have expressed concern over the escalation of the conflict and have called for a diplomatic solution to the ongoing crisis.

International reaction

The Russian strikes have sparked a strong reaction from the international community. Many countries have condemned the attacks and called for a ceasefire. The United Nations has also expressed concern over the escalation of the conflict and has called for a peaceful resolution.
