Markets End Flat as Volatility Persists

The BSE Sensex and NSE Nifty ended the trading session flat, with both indices hovering around their previous close levels. The session was marked by significant volatility, with the Sensex ranging between 82,500 and 83,000 points, and the Nifty between 22,500 and 22,600 points. This volatility was driven by a mix of domestic and global factors, including geopolitical tensions and economic data releases.

Sector Performance: IT, FMCG, Auto Struggle

The IT sector was a major drag on the markets, with the Nifty IT index falling by 1.2%. This decline was attributed to concerns over slowing demand in the US and Europe, which are key markets for Indian IT services. The FMCG sector also underperformed, with the Nifty FMCG index down by 0.8%. This was largely due to a slowdown in consumer spending, which is a key driver of growth in this sector.

The auto sector also faced challenges, with the Nifty Auto index falling by 1.5%. This decline was driven by a slowdown in demand for vehicles, which is a key driver of growth in this sector. However, the metals sector showed resilience, with the Nifty Metals index up by 0.5%. This was driven by a rally in global commodity prices, which is a key driver of growth in this sector.
