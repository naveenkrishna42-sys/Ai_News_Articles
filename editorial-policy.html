Editorial Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

1. How our content is produced

AI News Factory publishes AI-assisted news summaries. Stories are drafted with the help of AI language models based on publicly available source reporting, then formatted and scheduled for publication. We are transparent about this process — see our Disclaimer for the full AI-content disclosure.

2. Sourcing standards

  • Every article is based on identifiable, publicly available reporting and links back to the original source wherever possible.
  • We do not knowingly publish fabricated events, quotes, or statistics.
  • Breaking or developing stories may be updated as more information becomes available.

3. Corrections

If you believe an article contains a factual error, please contact us via the Contact page with the article URL and a description of the issue. We review correction requests and aim to respond within 5 business days. Material corrections are noted on the affected article.

4. Human oversight

Publishing decisions — what runs, when it runs, and whether a correction is warranted — are made by the people operating this site, not by an automated system acting alone.

5. Sponsored content & paid links

AI News Factory may accept paid placements, sponsored posts, or paid link insertions as part of its monetization. To stay compliant with search engine guidelines on paid links and to keep this clearly disclosed to readers:

  • Any link placed as part of a paid arrangement carries rel="sponsored" (and typically rel="nofollow") so it does not pass unearned SEO value — this is a requirement under Google's spam policies on link schemes, not an optional nicety.
  • Sponsored articles or paid placements are labeled as such (e.g. "Sponsored" or "Advertisement") near the top of the piece, so readers can tell the difference between editorial coverage and paid content.
  • We do not sell links that are disguised as ordinary editorial citations.
  • Advertising and sponsorship inquiries go through the Contact page.

See our Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy for how advertising more broadly is handled on this site.

6. Contact

Questions about how a specific article was produced or sourced? Reach out via the Contact page.
