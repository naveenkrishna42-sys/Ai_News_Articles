iPhone 18 Pro Release Date Leak, EU AirPods Rule Change Surface

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Diagnostic logs confirm iPhone 18 Pro Max camera specs with variable aperture.
  • iPhone 18 Pro price in India expected to be higher than iPhone 17 Pro.
  • New EU rule requires USB-C charging for all wireless earbuds, including AirPods.
  • Lego iMac G3 build surfaces in Apple Loop roundup.

A flurry of leaks and regulatory changes are shaping the narrative around Apple's upcoming hardware. The iPhone 18 Pro lineup is at the center of attention, with fresh details emerging about its camera system and pricing. Separately, the European Union has tightened rules for wireless earbuds, directly impacting the next generation of AirPods.

iPhone 18 Pro Max Camera Specs Leak

A diagnostic log, obtained and verified by multiple outlets, confirms key camera specifications for the iPhone 18 Pro Max. The log points to a variable aperture lens as the marquee upgrade. This would allow the camera to adjust the amount of light entering the sensor, offering greater control in different lighting conditions.

Sources suggest the variable aperture will work in tandem with a larger image sensor. The combination could significantly improve low-light photography and depth-of-field effects. Analysts believe this is Apple's response to growing competition from Android flagship devices that have offered similar hardware for years.

Price Hike Expected for Indian Buyers

Leaks from industry trackers indicate the iPhone 18 Pro will carry a higher price tag in India compared to the iPhone 17 Pro. The exact figures remain unconfirmed, but estimates point to an increase of several thousand rupees. Import duties and currency fluctuations are likely factors.

Apple has been steadily raising prices in India, positioning the Pro models as ultra-premium devices. The iPhone 17 Pro currently starts at over ₹1.2 lakh. The iPhone 18 Pro could cross the ₹1.3 lakh mark, making it one of the most expensive smartphones in the country at launch.

EU Mandates USB-C for AirPods

The European Union has expanded its common charger directive to cover wireless earbuds. The new rule mandates that all such devices sold in the EU must feature a USB-C charging port by 2027. This directly affects Apple's AirPods lineup, which currently uses Lightning for the charging case.

Apple will need to redesign the AirPods charging case to comply. The change is expected to roll out with the next major AirPods refresh. The EU directive aims to reduce electronic waste and simplify charging for consumers. Industry observers note that Apple has already transitioned iPhones to USB-C in the EU, so this move was anticipated.

Apple Loop and Other Highlights

In related news, a Forbes Apple Loop roundup highlighted a Lego build of the iconic iMac G3. The build, created by a fan, recreates the colorful all-in-one computer from the late 1990s. It serves as a nostalgic nod to Apple's design history.

The roundup also aggregated the iPhone 18 Pro release date leaks and the EU AirPods rule change. The convergence of hardware leaks and regulatory news underscores the challenges Apple faces in balancing innovation with compliance.

The iPhone 18 Pro launch is expected in September 2026, with pre-orders likely starting a week after the announcement.
