CJP Team Attacked During Rajasthan School Visit, BJP Blamed

📅 2026-08-21 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • CJP team conducting school inspection was chased and attacked by villagers in Rajasthan
  • Kejriwal, Dipke and Ashutosh Ranka condemned the incident, blaming BJP supporters
  • Police faced criticism for inaction during the clash near a school in Jaipur district

Incident During School Inspection

A team from Citizens for Justice and Peace (CJP) was attacked while conducting a school inspection in Rajasthan, according to multiple reports. The incident occurred near Jaipur, where the group had visited to verify educational facilities. Eyewitnesses described a confrontation that escalated quickly as the team approached what critics have referred to as a "school in buffalo shed."

The CJP volunteers claimed they were chased and physically attacked during the visit. The organisation has alleged that the assault was coordinated by supporters of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP). Officials have not yet confirmed the involvement of any political group in the violence.

Political Reactions

Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal condemned the attack, questioning the safety of civic volunteers across the country. "What's going on in the country?" he posted on social media, targeting the BJP over the incident. His remarks added to the growing political friction surrounding the inspection.

BJP leader Dipke criticised the police response, calling them "mute spectators" while the confrontation unfolded. Ashutosh Ranka, a CJP representative, blamed what he described as "BJP goons" for the violence. The party has denied any involvement and called the allegations politically motivated.

Context of School Inspections

CJP has been conducting inspections of schools in rural areas, reportedly checking whether children are attending classes or being pushed into labour. The organisation claims these visits expose infrastructure gaps and potential violations of child rights. Critics of the inspections argue they lack proper authorisation.

The reference to a "school in buffalo shed" points to concerns about the quality of rural education infrastructure. Such facilities have faced scrutiny in past reports highlighting inadequate learning environments in parts of Rajasthan.

Police Inquiry Underway

Authorities in Jaipur have registered a case and begun investigating the circumstances of the clash. Officers stated they are examining footage from the site and collecting statements from both parties. No arrests have been made so far.

Local officials urged calm while the inquiry continues. They acknowledged that tensions had been building over recent weeks due to competing claims about school operations in the area. The police have not disclosed whether any weapons were used during the confrontation.

What Happens Next

The Rajasthan government faces pressure to provide security for inspection teams operating in sensitive areas. CJP has indicated it will continue its outreach programme despite the setback. Watch for further political statements as the investigation progresses and details emerge about those involved in the attack.
