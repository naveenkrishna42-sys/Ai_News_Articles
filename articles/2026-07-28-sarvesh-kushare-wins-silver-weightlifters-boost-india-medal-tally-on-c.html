Sarvesh Kushare opens athletics account with historic silver

India's medal tally at the Commonwealth Games 2026 in Glasgow received a significant boost on Day 4 as high jumper Sarvesh Kushare clinched a silver medal. This marked India's first athletics medal of the ongoing Games.

Kushare, who has been consistent on the international circuit, delivered a season-best jump to secure the podium finish. The event saw stiff competition from athletes across the Commonwealth, but Kushare's composure under pressure earned him a historic spot.

Weightlifters add to India's medal haul

India's weightlifting contingent continued its strong showing, adding multiple medals to the country's overall tally on Day 4. The lifters, who have been a reliable source of medals for India at the Commonwealth Games, delivered impressive performances across weight categories.

While officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of medals won by weightlifters, the total count for India on Day 4 stood at six medals. The team's consistent form has kept India in contention among the top nations on the medal table.

Sharmila Dhankar wins gold in para athletics

In para athletics, Sharmila Dhankar brought home a gold medal in the women's shot put event. Her performance was a highlight of the day, demonstrating India's growing strength in para sports at the Commonwealth level.

Shilpa Shyla also contributed to the medal count by winning a bronze in the same event. The double podium finish in para shot put underscored the depth of talent in India's para athletics squad.

India's position on the CWG 2026 medal table

With six medals secured after four days of competition, India has climbed steadily on the Commonwealth Games 2026 medal table. The mix of medals across athletics, weightlifting, and para events reflects a balanced campaign so far.
