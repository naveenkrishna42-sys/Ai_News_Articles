New Malayalam and Tamil Titles Hit Streaming Platforms

This week's South Indian OTT releases offer a mix of comedy, thriller, and drama. The lineup is led by Tovino Thomas's much-anticipated film 'Pallichattambi', which drops on Netflix.

Alongside it, the crime comedy 'Con City' and the investigative thriller 'Case of Kondana' are also making their digital debuts. The week's slate includes Dhyan Sreenivasan's 'Secret of Kalinga' and several other titles spread across platforms like Disney+ Hotstar, Zee5, and Amazon Prime Video.

'Pallichattambi' Leads the Pack on Netflix

'Pallichattambi' is the biggest draw this week. Directed by Jagan, the film stars Tovino Thomas in a role that blends action with rural drama. The movie had a moderate theatrical run and now seeks a wider audience on the streaming giant.

Netflix has been aggressively adding Malayalam content to its catalogue. The platform is banking on Thomas's star power to drive viewership in Kerala and among the global Malayali diaspora.

'Con City' and 'Case of Kondana' Add Variety

'Con City', a crime comedy directed by Aneesh Upasana, explores the world of small-time cons in urban Kerala. The film has generated buzz for its sharp writing and ensemble cast.

'Case of Kondana', meanwhile, is a thriller that revolves around a missing person investigation. The film's plot promises twists and a gritty narrative, appealing to fans of the genre.

Another notable release is 'Balan: The Boy', a heartfelt drama that follows a young boy's journey. The film has been praised for its performances and emotional depth.

'Chinna Chinna Aasai' and Other Tamil Releases

On the Tamil front, 'Chinna Chinna Aasai' is a romantic drama that explores themes of love and ambition. The film is releasing on a leading OTT platform and is expected to resonate with younger audiences.
