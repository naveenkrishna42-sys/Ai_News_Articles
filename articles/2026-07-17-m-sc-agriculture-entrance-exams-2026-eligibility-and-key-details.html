M.Sc. Agriculture Entrance Exams 2026: Eligibility and Key Details

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • ICAR AIEEA is the primary national-level exam for M.Sc. Agriculture admission.
  • State-level exams like MAH ACET and OUAT ET offer regional options.
  • University-specific tests like LPU NEST and BHU UET provide direct entry.
  • Eligibility typically requires a B.Sc. Agriculture degree with 50-60% marks.
  • Application deadlines vary; candidates must check official websites for 2026 dates.

National-Level Options for M.Sc. Agriculture

Aspiring students looking to pursue a Master of Science in Agriculture in 2026 have multiple entrance exam pathways. The Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) conducts the All India Entrance Examination for Admission (AIEEA), the most prominent national-level test. This exam opens doors to ICAR-supported agricultural universities across India.

Eligibility for ICAR AIEEA requires a Bachelor's degree in Agriculture or a related field with a minimum of 60% marks. Reservations follow government norms. The exam typically tests knowledge in subjects like agronomy, plant breeding, and agricultural economics.

State-Level and University-Specific Exams

Several states run their own entrance tests. For example, the Maharashtra Agricultural Universities Common Entrance Test (MAH ACET) and the Odisha University of Agriculture and Technology Entrance Test (OUAT ET) are popular choices. These exams often have similar eligibility criteria but may include state domicile requirements.

Private universities also offer direct admission through their own exams. Lovely Professional University (LPU) conducts the LPU National Eligibility and Scholarship Test (LPU NEST). BHU UET is another key test for Banaras Hindu University's agriculture programs. Each university sets its own cut-off and application timeline.

Key Dates and Application Process

Application windows for most exams open between March and May 2026. ICAR AIEEA usually accepts applications from April onwards. LPU NEST has multiple cycles, with the first phase starting in January 2026.

Candidates should register online on official portals. Required documents include mark sheets, ID proof, and category certificates. Application fees range from ₹500 to ₹2,000 depending on the exam.

What to Watch in 2026

With competition intensifying, early preparation is crucial. Students must track individual exam notifications closely as dates can shift. Some universities may introduce changes in syllabus or reservation policies.

For the latest updates, candidates should bookmark official websites of ICAR, respective state agriculture boards, and university admission cells. A clear strategy based on target colleges and exam patterns will help secure a seat in the 2026 academic year.
