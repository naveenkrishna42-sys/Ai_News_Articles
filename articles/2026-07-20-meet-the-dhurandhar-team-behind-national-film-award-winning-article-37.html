Meet the 'Dhurandhar' Team Behind National Film Award-Winning 'Article 370'

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The film 'Article 370' won the National Film Award for Best Film on National Integration.
  • The 'Dhurandhar' team, including director Aditya Suhas Jambhale and producer Jyoti Deshpande, celebrated the win.
  • Yami Gautam stars in the film, which focuses on the abrogation of Article 370 in Jammu and Kashmir.

Award-Winning Film's Creators Speak

The team behind 'Article 370' has a reason to celebrate. The film, which delves into the abrogation of Article 370 in Jammu and Kashmir, won the National Film Award for Best Film on National Integration at the 72nd National Film Awards.

The 'Dhurandhar' team, as they call themselves, includes director Aditya Suhas Jambhale and producer Jyoti Deshpande. They spoke about the recognition, calling it a validation of their hard work.

The 'Dhurandhar' Vision

Director Aditya Suhas Jambhale said the award is a tribute to the crew's dedication. The film, starring Yami Gautam, was made with a focus on factual storytelling.

Producer Jyoti Deshpande added that the win reflects the audience's connection with the subject. The team aimed to present a balanced narrative about a historic decision.

Yami Gautam's Role

Yami Gautam plays a pivotal character in the film. Her performance has been widely praised, with critics noting her nuanced portrayal of a woman caught in the political turmoil.

The actress, who has been busy with promotions, expressed her gratitude for the award. She said the film's success is a team effort.

Other Notable Wins at the 72nd National Film Awards

The 72nd National Film Awards saw several big winners. Telugu cinema had a strong showing, with 'Balagam' winning Best Feature Film and 'RRR' taking home Best Music Direction.

However, some fans expressed disappointment as Prithviraj's 'The Goat Life' and Vikram's 'Thangalaan' missed out. Mammootty's 'Feminichi Fathima' led Kerala's wins.

Kartik Aaryan also celebrated his first National Award, winning Best Actor for 'Satyaprem Ki Katha'. He shared the moment with family.

What's Next for the Team

The 'Dhurandhar' team is now looking at future projects. Jambhale hinted at a new film with a similar socio-political theme.

Deshpande said the award will help them tell more stories about India's transformation. The team is expected to announce their next venture soon.
