Mahindra BE.07 Spotted Testing: SUV To Take On Tata Harrier

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mahindra BE.07 spotted testing again, likely to launch in 2026.
  • SUV positioned as rival to Tata Harrier, part of Mahindra's electric vehicle push.
  • Spy shots reveal coupe-like silhouette and new design language.

Mahindra's Electric SUV Ambitions Take Shape

Mahindra & Mahindra is gearing up for a significant launch in the Indian automotive market. The company's upcoming electric SUV, codenamed BE.07, has been spotted testing once again. The vehicle is widely seen as Mahindra's direct answer to the popular Tata Harrier.

The latest spy shots, shared by automotive website MotorOctane, show the BE.07 undergoing road trials. The test mule was spotted near Mahindra's testing facility, wearing heavy camouflage but revealing clear design cues.

Design and Platform Details

The BE.07 features a sharp, coupe-like roofline and a muscular stance. It appears to borrow design elements from the BE.05 concept, with a distinct front fascia and sculpted body panels. The SUV is built on Mahindra's new INGLO platform, which is dedicated to electric vehicles.

This platform is expected to underpin multiple Mahindra electric SUVs in the coming years. The BE.07 will likely be positioned as a premium offering, slotting above the XUV400 and directly competing with the Harrier in terms of size and price.

Battery and Range Expectations

While Mahindra has not officially confirmed specifications, industry sources suggest the BE.07 could come with multiple battery pack options. A 60-80 kWh battery pack is being speculated, offering a range of around 400-500 km on a single charge.

The SUV is expected to feature dual-motor all-wheel drive in top variants. This would give it performance credentials to match its sporty design. Mahindra is also working on fast-charging capabilities to reduce charging time significantly.

Market Positioning and Timeline

The BE.07 is expected to launch by late 2026. Its pricing is likely to start around Rs 20 lakh, going up to Rs 30 lakh for top-end variants. This would put it in direct competition with the Tata Harrier, as well as upcoming electric SUVs from Hyundai and Kia.

Mahindra has been aggressive in its electric vehicle push. The company has already unveiled the XUV400 and has plans for a full lineup of electric SUVs under the BE brand. The BE.07 will be a key part of this strategy.

What This Means For The Indian SUV Market

The Indian SUV segment is heating up. Tata has dominated the mid-size SUV space with the Harrier, while Mahindra has been strong in the larger SUV segment with the XUV700. The BE.07 aims to bridge that gap with an electric offering.

If Mahindra delivers on range and pricing, the BE.07 could become a strong alternative to the Harrier. It would also boost India's shift toward electric mobility, especially in the popular SUV category.

The next few months should bring more details. Mahindra is expected to reveal the production version of the BE.07 at an auto show before its official launch. Until then, the test mule sightings will keep enthusiasts guessing.
