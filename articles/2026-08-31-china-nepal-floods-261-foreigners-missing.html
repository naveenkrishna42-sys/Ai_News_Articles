China-Nepal floods: 261 foreigners missing

Recent floods in the China-Nepal border region have left 261 foreigners missing, with rescue efforts ongoing. The situation is further complicated by targeted online rumours, which have disrupted the rescue operations.

A Chinese rescue team has reported finding 'nothing but ruins' at the flood-hit Himalayan border crossing. This finding raises concerns about the severity of the damage and the potential for further casualties.

Exclusive aerial footage has shown national road repairs near Gyirong Port. These repairs are crucial for restoring communication and transport links in the affected area.
