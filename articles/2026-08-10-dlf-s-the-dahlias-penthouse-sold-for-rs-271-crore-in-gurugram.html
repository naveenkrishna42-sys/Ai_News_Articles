DLF's The Dahlias penthouse sold for Rs 271 crore in Gurugram

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Entrepreneur Manav Sardana purchased a penthouse at DLF's The Dahlias for Rs 271 crore.
  • The deal works out to roughly Rs 2.6 lakh per square foot, a record for the area.
  • The sale highlights strong demand for ultra-luxury homes in Delhi NCR.
  • The transaction is among the most expensive single-unit residential deals in India.

Record deal in Gurugram's luxury real estate

Entrepreneur Manav Sardana has bought a penthouse at DLF's The Dahlias in Gurugram for Rs 271 crore. The transaction, which works out to about Rs 2.6 lakh per square foot, is being described as one of the most expensive single-unit residential deals in the country.

The Dahlias, developed by DLF, is among the most prestigious addresses in the National Capital Region. The project has been in the spotlight for its ultra-luxury offerings, and this latest sale underscores the appetite among India's wealthiest for high-end homes.

A new benchmark for ultra-luxury homes

The deal sets a new benchmark for Gurugram's real estate market. Property experts say the price per square foot reflects the premium attached to exclusivity, location, and the brand value of DLF's flagship project.

Over the past year, several high-value transactions in Delhi NCR and Mumbai have pushed the envelope for luxury housing. But the Rs 271 crore penthouse purchase stands out, both for its scale and the profile of the buyer.

Who is Manav Sardana?

Manav Sardana is an entrepreneur, though details of his business interests have not been publicly disclosed. The acquisition places him among a select group of buyers who have spent hundreds of crores on a single residence.

Industry observers say such purchases are driven not just by lifestyle needs but also by the view of real estate as a stable asset class. For many ultra-rich individuals, a penthouse in a marquee project offers both prestige and long-term value appreciation.

Luxury housing market on a roll

The sale comes at a time when India's luxury housing segment is witnessing robust demand. From Mumbai's Malabar Hill to Gurugram's Golf Course Extension, developers are launching projects that cater to buyers willing to pay a premium for privacy, amenities, and panoramic views.

According to recent reports, homes priced above Rs 10 crore have seen a surge in sales across major cities. The Dahlias transaction is likely to fuel further interest in ultra-luxury inventory.

What this means for the sector

Real estate analysts believe this deal could prompt other developers to raise prices for their premium offerings. It also signals confidence in Gurugram's long-term growth as a hub for high-end living.

While the previous record for a single-unit sale in the region stood lower, the Rs 271 crore deal has reset expectations. DLF has not officially commented on the transaction, but the market is abuzz with the news.

What to watch next

The focus now shifts to whether similar deals will follow in other luxury projects across the country. With India's wealthy showing a growing preference for opulent homes, the ultra-luxury segment is expected to remain a bright spot in the real estate market.
