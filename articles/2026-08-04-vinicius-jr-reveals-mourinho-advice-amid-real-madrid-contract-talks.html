Vinicius Jr reveals Mourinho advice amid Real Madrid contract talks

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Vinicius Jr reveals Mourinho's advice to be himself on the pitch
  • Real Madrid set for crunch contract talks without increasing offer
  • Arsenal maintain interest in the Brazilian forward

Mourinho's simple message

Vinicius Junior has opened up about the guidance he receives from Real Madrid head coach Jose Mourinho. The Brazilian winger said the Portuguese tactician has a straightforward demand: be yourself on the pitch.

"Mourinho wants me to be myself on the pitch," Vinicius told reporters. "He wants me to be happy at Real Madrid." The comments come at a delicate time for the club, with contract negotiations looming large.

Contract standoff

Real Madrid are preparing for decisive talks with the 26-year-old forward's representatives. However, the club has made it clear they will not sweeten their current proposal, according to sources familiar with the matter.

The sticking point appears to be financial. Madrid's offer stands as it is, and the club expects Vinicius to accept or walk away. This hardline stance has raised eyebrows given the Brazilian's importance to the squad.

His current deal runs until 2027, but the club wants to secure his long-term future now. The ball, as they say, is in Vinicius's court.

Arsenal circling

Arsenal have maintained a persistent interest in the winger throughout the summer. The Premier League club see Vinicius as a marquee signing capable of transforming their attack.

Reports from England suggest the Gunners are monitoring the situation closely. If talks stall, they could formalise their interest with a concrete bid before the transfer window closes.

Vinicius has not publicly addressed the speculation. His focus, he insists, remains on the pitch and on Mourinho's instructions.

Precedent for concern

Behind the scenes, Real Madrid are wary of a familiar pattern. Club officials reportedly fear a repeat of previous high-profile exits where players forced moves after contract disputes turned sour.

"It won't end well" — that is the worry whispered in the corridors of the Bernabeu, according to Goal.com. The club has seen this movie before, and they do not like the ending.

Madrid's reluctance to raise their offer could be a calculated gamble. But with Arsenal lurking, the stakes are higher than ever.

What happens next

The coming weeks will be decisive. Vinicius will return to training under Mourinho, while his agents meet with club executives.

If a breakthrough comes, the noise around Arsenal will fade. If not, the transfer saga could dominate headlines until the window slams shut.

For now, the Brazilian seems calm. Mourinho has told him to be himself — and that is exactly what he intends to do.
