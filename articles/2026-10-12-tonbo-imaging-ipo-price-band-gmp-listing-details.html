Tonbo Imaging IPO Details

Defence technology company Tonbo Imaging has fixed its initial public offer (IPO) price band at ₹665-700 per equity share. The issue, valued at approximately ₹852 crore, is structured as a pure Offer For Sale (OFS), meaning existing shareholders will dilute stakes without fresh capital infusion into the company.

Issue Timeline and Structure

The IPO is scheduled to open for subscription on October 15, 2026. As a book-building process, price discovery within the ₹665-700 band will determine final allotment prices. Given the OFS structure, proceeds will primarily benefit selling shareholders rather than funding corporate expansion directly.

Grey Market Premium Outlook

Market buzz surrounding Tonbo Imaging has already triggered activity in the grey market, where shares trade ahead of official listing. Early indications suggest a positive grey market premium (GMP), reflecting investor confidence in the defence electronics sector amid India's push for self-reliance in defence manufacturing.

Investor Implications

A strong GMP typically signals oversubscription potential during the public issue phase. However, investors should evaluate valuations carefully, especially given the OFS structure where company fundamentals remain secondary to shareholder exit motivations. Defence contracts often carry long execution cycles, which can impact short-term earnings visibility.

  • Pros: Exposure to India's growing defence tech ecosystem; potential listing gains driven by GMP momentum
  • Cons: No fresh capital for company expansion; defence sector subject to regulatory and geopolitical risks
  • Key Highlights: ₹852 crore issue size; ₹665-700 price band; opens Oct 15

Listing and Post-Issue Considerations

Following the subscription close, shares are expected to list on Indian stock exchanges within the standard settlement window. Analysts will likely track post-listing performance closely, particularly if the grey market premium sustains into regular trading. Investors should monitor anchor investor portions and overall subscription metrics for directional cues.
