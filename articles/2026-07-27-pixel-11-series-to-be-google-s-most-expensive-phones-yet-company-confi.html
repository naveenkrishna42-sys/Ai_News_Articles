Pixel 11 series to be Google’s most expensive phones yet, company confirms

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Google confirms Pixel 11 series will be its most expensive phones yet, citing higher component costs.
  • Company reveals efforts to make Android more RAM-efficient, potentially allowing lower RAM in future models.
  • Price hikes will apply across all Pixel phones, including the upcoming flagship lineup.

Google Confirms Price Hike for Pixel 11

Google has officially confirmed that the upcoming Pixel 11 series will be its most expensive smartphones to date. The company acknowledged the price increase in a statement, attributing it to rising costs of premium components and advanced manufacturing processes.

The announcement comes as a surprise to many Pixel fans, who have historically seen the devices as more affordable alternatives to flagship phones from Apple and Samsung. Google did not disclose exact price points, but industry analysts expect the Pixel 11 Pro to start above the Rs 1 lakh mark in India.

RAM Efficiency Push

In a related development, Google revealed it is working on making Android more RAM-efficient. The company said it has dedicated engineering resources to optimize memory usage, potentially allowing future Pixel phones to ship with less RAM without compromising performance.

This move could help offset some of the cost increases for consumers. Currently, high-end Android phones often pack 12GB or 16GB of RAM, but Google believes that smarter software management can reduce the need for such large amounts of memory.

Component Costs Driving Prices Up

The price hike is not limited to the Pixel 11 series. Google confirmed that all Pixel phones will see a price increase, citing higher costs for processors, camera sensors, and displays. The company is reportedly using a new custom Tensor chip for the Pixel 11, which is more expensive to produce than previous generations.

Supply chain disruptions and inflation have also contributed to the rising costs. Google said it has absorbed some of these expenses in previous models but can no longer do so without affecting profitability.

What This Means for Buyers

For Indian consumers, the Pixel 11 series could become a premium offering starting around Rs 85,000 for the base model and crossing Rs 1.2 lakh for the Pro variant. This would put it in direct competition with the iPhone 17 Pro and Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra.

Google is expected to announce the Pixel 11 lineup in October this year. Early leaks suggest significant camera upgrades and a new design language, but the higher price tag may test brand loyalty.

The company hopes its RAM efficiency improvements will be a selling point, arguing that users won't miss the extra memory if the software is optimised well.

As Google prepares for the launch, all eyes will be on how consumers react to the price increase. The company may also introduce more aggressive trade-in offers or financing options to soften the blow.
