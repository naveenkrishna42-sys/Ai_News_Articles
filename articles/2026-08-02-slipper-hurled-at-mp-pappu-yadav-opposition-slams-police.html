Slipper hurled at MP Pappu Yadav; Opposition slams police

📅 2026-08-02 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Slipper thrown at MP Pappu Yadav during a press conference in Delhi.
  • The attacker was detained; police sources say he has no criminal record.
  • Opposition leaders slammed the police for the security breach.
  • Yadav alleged the attacker also pulled out a knife, claiming a bid on his life.
  • The incident is linked to a controversial Ram temple skit.

Attack during press meet

A man hurled a slipper at Member of Parliament Pappu Yadav during a press conference in Delhi on Sunday. The incident took place as Yadav was addressing the media, and the object was thrown from the crowd. The attacker was immediately detained by security personnel present at the venue.

Yadav, who represents the Purnea constituency in Bihar, claimed that the man also pulled out a knife during the scuffle. He alleged that this was a deliberate bid on his life. Police sources, however, have not yet confirmed the knife claim.

Attacker identified, no criminal past

The man who threw the slipper has been identified, according to police sources. Preliminary checks have revealed that he has no criminal record. The motive behind the attack is still under investigation, though reports suggest it may be linked to a controversial Ram temple skit that has been doing the rounds.

The police have not released the name of the detained individual. They have also not confirmed whether any formal charges have been filed. The investigation is ongoing, and further details are expected to emerge.

Opposition slams security lapse

The incident has drawn sharp reactions from Opposition parties, who have slammed the Delhi Police for the security breach. Leaders questioned how a person could get so close to a sitting MP with a weapon-like object. They demanded a thorough probe into the matter.

Several Opposition leaders took to social media to express their concern. They pointed out that such incidents raise serious questions about the safety of elected representatives. The ruling side has not yet issued a formal response.

Yadav's allegation of a knife

Speaking after the incident, Yadav said the attacker attempted to stab him. He described the moment as a close call and thanked his security team for their quick action. The MP has been in the news recently for his outspoken criticism of the government on various issues.

The police have not confirmed the presence of a knife, but they are examining the CCTV footage from the venue. The detained man is being questioned to ascertain whether he acted alone or as part of a larger plan.

Meanwhile, the controversy over the Ram temple skit continues to simmer. It remains unclear if the attack was directly connected to the skit, but police are looking into all angles.

What to watch

The police are expected to provide more details once the interrogation is complete. The Opposition is likely to raise the issue in Parliament when it convenes next. For now, the focus remains on ensuring the safety of public representatives and getting to the bottom of this brazen act.
