Odisha Prepares for Puri Rath Yatra: Why This Is the Best Time to Visit

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Religion Original source ↗
Key points
  • Puri Rath Yatra, Odisha's biggest festival, is set to attract millions of devotees.
  • The state government has enhanced security, accommodation, and transport for tourists.
  • Visitors can explore Puri's Jagannath Temple, Konark Sun Temple, and Chilika Lake.
  • Special train and flight services have been added to manage the rush.

Odisha Gears Up for the Grand Rath Yatra

Odisha is buzzing with preparations for the annual Puri Rath Yatra, one of India's biggest religious festivals. The state government is leaving no stone unturned to ensure a smooth experience for the millions expected to converge on the coastal town.

For travellers, this might be the best time to visit. The festival, which sees the three deities — Lord Jagannath, Balabhadra, and Subhadra — taken out in massive chariots, transforms Puri into a vibrant tapestry of faith and culture.

Enhanced Facilities for Visitors

The Odisha government has announced a series of measures to handle the massive influx. Additional temporary accommodation has been set up, and the Puri railway station is being spruced up.

Special trains are being run from major cities like Bhubaneswar, Kolkata, and Visakhapatnam. The Bhubaneswar airport is also seeing extra flights to manage the rush. Officials have confirmed that security has been tightened across the city.

Beyond the Chariots: What to See

While the Rath Yatra is the main draw, Odisha offers plenty more. The 12th-century Jagannath Temple is a must-visit, though non-Hindus cannot enter the main sanctum. The nearby Konark Sun Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage site, is a marvel of ancient architecture.

For nature lovers, Chilika Lake, Asia's largest brackish water lagoon, is a short drive away. Boat rides and bird-watching are popular activities. Puri's beach, though crowded, offers a classic seaside experience.

Travel Tips for the Festival Season

Book your travel and accommodation well in advance. Prices spike during the festival, and last-minute options are scarce. Light cotton clothes are advisable, as Odisha in June is hot and humid.

Stay hydrated and be prepared for large crowds. The chariot pulling ceremony itself is a chaotic but unforgettable spectacle. Local authorities have set up help desks for tourists at key points in Puri.

What to Watch Next

The Rath Yatra is scheduled to begin in the third week of June. Travelers planning to visit should monitor official announcements for exact dates and any last-minute changes. The festival offers a rare glimpse into Odisha's spiritual heart.
