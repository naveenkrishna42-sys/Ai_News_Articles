The India-US trade deal has brought significant benefits for certain products, including duty-free imports of Harleys and cheaper cars. This deal aims to boost trade between India and the US, creating opportunities for businesses and consumers alike.

For motorcycle enthusiasts, the duty-free import of Harleys is a major advantage, as it reduces the cost of purchasing these high-quality bikes. Similarly, car buyers can expect cheaper prices on imported vehicles. However, the impact on Tesla, a leading electric vehicle manufacturer, is less clear. While the deal may benefit other car imports, it remains to be seen how it will affect Tesla's business in India.
