Overview of October 2026 Financial Reforms

October 2026 brought a cluster of regulatory updates affecting banking, payments, subsidies and tax compliance. The changes were announced by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), State Bank of India (SBI), National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, and the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT). Together they shape the cost of cash access, digital payment economics, subsidy delivery and individual tax filing timelines.

RBI Monetary Policy Outlook

The RBI’s Monetary Policy Statement released on October 4, 2026 retained the repo rate at 6.50%, the same level set after the March 2026 meeting. Inflation, as measured by CPI, hovered at 4.2% for the quarter ending September 2026, within the tolerance band of 4% ±2%. The policy stance was shifted from ‘calibrated tightening’ to ‘neutral’, signalling that further rate moves would depend on incoming inflation and growth data. The standing deposit facility (SDF) and marginal standing facility (MSF) rates remained at 6.25% and 6.75% respectively.

SBI ATM Charge Revision

Effective October 1, 2026, SBI increased the charge for cash withdrawals at non‑SBI ATMs beyond the free‑transaction limit. Customers in metropolitan areas now receive three free transactions per month (unchanged), while those in non‑metro centres retain five free transactions. Each additional withdrawal incurs a fee of ₹25, up from the previous ₹20. The bank cited rising operational costs and the need to maintain ATM network viability as the rationale for the adjustment.

UPI Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) Framework

NPCI issued a circular on October 10, 2026 clarifying the MDR structure for UPI payments. For merchants with an annual turnover below ₹50 lakh, UPI transactions up to ₹2,000 attract zero MDR, reinforcing the government’s push for low‑value digital payments. Transactions exceeding ₹2,000 for these merchants, and all UPI payments for merchants with turnover above ₹50 lakh, carry an MDR of 0.30% of the transaction value. The framework aims to balance merchant cost concerns with the sustainability of the UPI ecosystem.

LPG Subsidy KYC Mandate

The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas made Aadhaar‑based Know Your Customer (KYC) compulsory for all LPG subsidy beneficiaries starting October 1, 2026. Users must complete KYC via the UIDAI portal or at authorised distributors by October 31, 2026 to continue receiving the subsidy directly into their bank accounts. Non‑compliant consumers will receive the subsidy only after successful verification, with a temporary shift to the direct benefit transfer (DBT) mode. The move targets duplicate connections and leakages in the subsidy chain.

Income Tax Return (ITR) Filing Deadline Extension

CBDT, via a press release dated October 15, 2026, extended the ITR filing deadline for the financial year 2025‑26 (assessment year 2026‑27) to November 30, 2026 for individuals, Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs) and other assessees not liable to undergo a tax audit. The earlier deadline of July 31, 2026 remains applicable for audit‑case assessees. The extension accommodates taxpayers who faced delays in obtaining Form 16 and other income documents due to prolonged payroll processing cycles in certain sectors.
