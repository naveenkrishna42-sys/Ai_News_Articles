Gift Nifty Hints at Flat Start for Indian Markets Despite Asian Rally

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Gift Nifty was trading near the 18,550 level, indicating a flat start for Indian indices.
  • Asian markets were mostly in positive territory, tracking gains on Wall Street.
  • The flat signal comes amid a lack of major domestic triggers and caution ahead of global cues.

Indian equity benchmarks are likely to open on a flat note on Tuesday, if early signals from the Gift Nifty are any indication. The index was hovering around the 18,550 mark, suggesting little change from Monday's close for both the Sensex and the Nifty.

This comes as a contrast to the broader trend in Asia, where most markets were trading in the green. Strong cues from Wall Street overnight had lifted sentiment across the region.

Gift Nifty Points to Tepid Start

The Gift Nifty, which trades on the NSE International Exchange, is often seen as a reliable indicator of the market's opening direction. Its current level points to a gap-down or a flat start for the domestic indices.

At the last count, the Nifty futures on the Gift platform were trading near 18,550, a level that implies little deviation from the previous session's close. This suggests traders are in a wait-and-watch mode.

Asian Markets Rally, But India Stays Muted

Across the region, markets in Japan, South Korea, and Australia were trading higher, buoyed by positive overnight cues from the United States. The S&P 500 and the Nasdaq had both ended in the green, supported by gains in technology stocks.

Despite this tailwind, Indian markets are showing signs of caution. Analysts pointed to the absence of any major domestic catalyst. The earnings season is largely behind, and no significant policy announcements are expected in the near term.

What to Watch for the Day

Traders will be keeping an eye on foreign portfolio investor (FPI) flows and the movement of crude oil prices. Any sharp move in global crude could impact domestic sentiment, given India's dependence on imports.

The rupee's movement against the US dollar will also be tracked. A weaker rupee could add to inflationary pressures and weigh on market sentiment.

For now, the market appears to be in a consolidation phase. With no strong triggers on the horizon, the Nifty is likely to trade within a narrow band. Investors will look to global cues and sector-specific news for direction in the coming sessions.
