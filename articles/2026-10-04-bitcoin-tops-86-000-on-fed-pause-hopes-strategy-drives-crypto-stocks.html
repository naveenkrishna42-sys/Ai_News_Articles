Bitcoin Breaks $86,000 Amid Fed Pause Expectations

On October 2, 2026, Bitcoin traded above $86,000 for the first time in several months, according to Barron's reporting. The upward move coincided with growing market speculation that the Federal Reserve might pause its tightening cycle after recent inflation data showed signs of easing. Traders interpreted the pause prospect as a reduction in opportunity‑cost pressure on non‑yielding assets, prompting renewed buying interest in the leading cryptocurrency.

Macro‑Economic Backdrop

The Federal Reserve’s policy stance remains a primary driver of risk‑asset sentiment. When investors anticipate a halt in rate hikes, the dollar often weakens and alternative stores of value, such as Bitcoin, attract capital. In this episode, the CME FedWatch tool indicated a 55% probability of a pause at the next meeting, up from 35% a week earlier. This shift in expectations helped lift Bitcoin’s price by roughly 4% intraday, pushing it through the $86,000 psychological barrier.

MicroStrategy’s Role in Crypto Equity Rally

MicroStrategy, the business‑intelligence firm known for its large Bitcoin holdings, saw its stock rise over 6% on the same day, leading a basket of crypto‑linked equities higher. The company’s balance sheet, which holds approximately 150,000 BTC, amplifies its exposure to Bitcoin price movements. As Bitcoin’s market value increased, MicroStrategy’s net asset value rose accordingly, reinforcing investor confidence in the stock as a leveraged play on cryptocurrency performance.
