Job Promise Turns into Trafficking Nightmare

A woman from Nagpur has alleged that she was trafficked to Portugal by a job firm based in Pune. The woman had been promised a job abroad, but instead, she was sold to a beer shop owner in Portugal.

According to reports, the woman had paid Rs 11 lakh to the job firm for the overseas job opportunity. However, upon reaching Portugal, she was forced into prostitution.

Complaint Filed with Police

A First Information Report (FIR) has been lodged with the police in Nagpur, and an investigation is underway. The woman has accused the Pune job firm of cheating and human trafficking.

Officials have not yet confirmed the details of the case, but it is believed that the woman was lured to Portugal with the promise of a well-paying job. Instead, she was exploited and forced into prostitution.

Investigation Underway

The police are currently investigating the case and are trying to gather more information about the job firm and its involvement in human trafficking. The woman's allegations have raised concerns about the safety of Indians traveling abroad for work.

Prevalence of Human Trafficking
