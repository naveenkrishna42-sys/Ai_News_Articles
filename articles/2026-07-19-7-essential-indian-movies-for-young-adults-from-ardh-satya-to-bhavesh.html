MensXP has published a list of seven Indian films it believes every young person in the country should watch. The selection, which includes titles like Ardh Satya, Bhavesh Joshi Superhero, and Arjun, aims to present stories that resonate with contemporary youth.

The list is not a ranking. It is a collection of movies that tackle themes of morality, rebellion, and the struggle against a flawed system.

From the 1980s to the 2010s: A Span of Four Decades

Om Puri's landmark performance in Govind Nihalani's Ardh Satya (1983) opens the list. The film follows an honest police officer caught between duty and a corrupt establishment.

Also featured is Sunny Deol's Arjun (1985), a story of a young man who takes on a corrupt political system after personal tragedy. These older films share space with more recent entries like Bhavesh Joshi Superhero (2018), a vigilante drama about a young man fighting water mafia and political cronyism.

The Common Thread: Systems vs. Individuals

Each film in the selection focuses on a protagonist who refuses to accept injustice passively. The characters are not superheroes in the traditional sense. They are ordinary people pushed to act.

Whether it is the angst of Ardh Satya's Sub Inspector Anant Velankar or the quiet determination of Bhavesh Joshi's Sattu, the core conflict remains the same: an individual against a broken system.
