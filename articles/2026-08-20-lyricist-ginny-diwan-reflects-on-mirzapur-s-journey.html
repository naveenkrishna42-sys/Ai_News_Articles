Lyricist Ginny Diwan discusses her Mirzapur journey

Lyricist Ginny Diwan has been an integral part of the Mirzapur franchise, having worked on three seasons of the acclaimed show. She recently spoke about her experience and the upcoming Mirzapur: The Movie.

Coming back to a familiar world

Diwan described her return to the Mirzapur world as feeling like coming back to a familiar place. "Returning for the film felt like coming back to a world I know well," she said. She praised the show's creators for maintaining the show's unique tone and style across the seasons.

Challenges and rewards

Working on Mirzapur has been both challenging and rewarding for Diwan. She had to adapt to the show's dark and gritty tone, but she found the experience to be incredibly fulfilling. "The show's unique world and characters have given me a lot to work with," she said.

Mirzapur: The Movie

The Mirzapur film is set to release in early 2027. Diwan has been working on the project for several months and is excited to see the final product. She believes the film will capture the essence of the show and provide a satisfying conclusion to the story.

Looking ahead

Diwan is looking forward to seeing how the Mirzapur film will be received by audiences. She hopes the film will be well-received and appreciated by fans of the show. She is also excited about the future of the Mirzapur franchise and the possibilities it holds.
