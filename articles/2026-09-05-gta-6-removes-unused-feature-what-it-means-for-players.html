Since GTA 6 is still in development, real-world performance cannot be assessed. However, Rockstar's history of polished releases suggests that removing underused features could lead to better optimization and fewer bugs at launch. The studio is known for extensive testing, and trimming unnecessary systems often results in smoother gameplay and faster load times. Early reports indicate the team is focusing on core mechanics, which should enhance immersion and reduce complexity for casual players.

GTA 6 will compete with upcoming open-world titles such as Saints Row reboot sequels and Elder Scrolls VI.
