Indian equities down as oil prices and Fed hikes weigh

Indian equities fall as oil prices and Fed hikes weigh

Indian equities fell sharply on Monday as investors reacted to rising oil prices and expectations of further interest rate hikes by the Federal Reserve. The benchmark Sensex lost over 200 points, while the Nifty 50 index dipped below 22,000. The market's sensitivity to global oil prices and Fed policy underscores the interconnectedness of the Indian and global economies.

HDFC Bank caps losses as oil-linked sectors under pressure

HDFC Bank, the country's largest private sector bank, capped losses as the broader market fell. The bank's shares were down by around 1.5% on Monday, reflecting the broader sell-off in oil-linked sectors. The bank's exposure to fuel expenses and higher borrowing costs has weighed on its stock price, despite its strong financial position.

Oil prices rise, boosting fuel expenses

Oil prices rose on Monday, with Brent crude reaching a three-month high. This increase is expected to boost fuel expenses for Indian refiners and consumers, adding to the pressure on the market. The rise in oil prices is a direct result of geopolitical tensions and supply disruptions, which have led to a global shortage of crude oil.

Fed hike bets drive market volatility

Investors are betting on further interest rate hikes by the Federal Reserve, which could lead to higher borrowing costs for Indian companies. This uncertainty has added to the market's volatility, with investors looking for safe-haven assets. The Reserve Bank of India's monetary policy is also under scrutiny, as the central bank may need to hike interest rates to combat inflation.
