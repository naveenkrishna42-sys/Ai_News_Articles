Archer's Blistering Spell Raises Questions

Jofra Archer's recent Test performance has sent shockwaves through the cricketing world. The English fast bowler recorded his fastest spell of the Test career, averaging 90.7 miles per hour (mph) against Pakistan at Lord's. This blistering pace has not only raised the bar for other fast bowlers but has also cast a shadow of doubt on the accuracy of speed guns.

Speed Guns Under the Microscope

Archer's comments about the 'dodgy' speed guns have sparked a debate among cricket analysts and fans alike. The accuracy of speed guns is a contentious issue, with some arguing that they can be influenced by various factors, including wind conditions and the angle at which the ball is bowled. Archer's criticism adds fuel to the fire, suggesting that the current system may not be reliable enough to measure a bowler's true speed.

England's Pace Attack Dominates Pakistan

The England pace pack, which includes Archer, Reece Topley, and Mark Wood, has been a nightmare for the Pakistani batting lineup. The trio has consistently bowled at high speeds, putting immense pressure on the Pakistani batsmen. The English bowlers have been able to exploit the conditions, making it difficult for the Pakistani team to adapt to their pace and bounce.
