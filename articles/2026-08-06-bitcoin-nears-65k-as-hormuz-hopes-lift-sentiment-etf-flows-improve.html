Bitcoin nears $65k as Hormuz hopes lift sentiment, ETF flows improve

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin price nears $65,000, driven by hopes of reduced geopolitical tensions in the Strait of Hormuz.
  • Improving inflows into spot Bitcoin ETFs signal renewed institutional interest.
  • Market analysts say a break above $65k could open the door to further gains, but resistance remains.

Bitcoin is trading near the $65,000 mark, buoyed by easing fears around the Strait of Hormuz and a noticeable pick-up in spot ETF flows. The world's largest cryptocurrency has climbed steadily over the past 24 hours, with market participants pointing to a combination of geopolitical relief and renewed institutional appetite as the key drivers.

Hormuz hopes ease risk-off mood

Reports suggesting a possible de-escalation in the tense standoff around the Strait of Hormuz have helped calm broader risk markets. For crypto, which has often traded as a risk asset in recent months, the shift in sentiment has provided a clear tailwind.

Oil prices, which had spiked on fears of supply disruptions, have also cooled, further supporting a move into riskier assets. Bitcoin's correlation with traditional risk gauges has been inconsistent, but the current rally appears to be riding the same wave of optimism that lifted equities in early trading.

ETF flows turn positive again

Spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds have recorded stronger inflows after a patchy few weeks. Data tracked by multiple sources shows investors adding exposure, a sign that institutional money is returning to the asset class despite lingering regulatory uncertainty.

The improvement in ETF flows is being watched closely by traders, as it often acts as a proxy for institutional demand. A sustained stretch of positive inflows, analysts say, could provide the foundation for a more durable move higher.

Technical levels in focus

With Bitcoin hovering just below the $65,000 level, the market is now eyeing a decisive break. A move above this psychological barrier could trigger a fresh wave of buying, with the next major resistance zone seen around $66,500.

On the downside, support is cited near $62,000, a level that has held firm during recent pullbacks. Traders are also keeping an eye on trading volumes, which have been moderate but could expand if the breakout gains momentum.

Broader market context

The broader crypto market has mirrored Bitcoin's gains, with most major altcoins trading in the green. Ethereum, BNB, and Solana have all posted modest advances, while total market capitalisation has edged closer to the $2.4 trillion mark.

Regulatory headlines remain a background risk, but for now, the macro narrative is taking precedence. The upcoming US inflation data and Federal Reserve commentary could still alter the course, but traders are cautiously optimistic.

What happens next hinges on whether Bitcoin can hold above $65,000 in the coming sessions. A sustained close above that level could invite further upside, while a failure might see profit-taking resume. Market watchers are also awaiting the next round of ETF flow data, which will offer fresh clues on institutional sentiment.
