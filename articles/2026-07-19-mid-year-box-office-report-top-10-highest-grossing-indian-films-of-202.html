Mid-Year Box Office Report: Top 10 Highest-Grossing Indian Films Of 2026 So Far

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • An action film starring a major star tops the list with over ₹500 crore gross.
  • A mid-budget comedy drama is the surprise second highest-grosser.
  • Regional language films, especially from South India, dominate the top 10.

The first half of 2026 has been a mixed bag for the Indian film industry. While a handful of big-ticket releases have set the box office on fire, many mid-range films have struggled to find an audience. News18's mid-year report, published Tuesday, lays out the top 10 highest-grossing Indian films of the year so far, and the list reveals some clear trends.

The Undisputed Leader: A Mega Action Spectacle

Sitting comfortably at number one is the action blockbuster Agni Prahar. The film, which released during the Republic Day weekend, has crossed the ₹500 crore mark globally. Industry trackers say its success was driven by strong word-of-mouth in Hindi-speaking markets and a massive opening in the South.

The film’s lead actor, known for his intense roles, delivered what many are calling his career-best performance. The combination of high-octane stunts and a patriotic storyline resonated deeply with family audiences.

The Surprise Hit: Comedy That Connects

The second spot belongs to a film that no one predicted would be a major player: Family First. This mid-budget comedy-drama, released in March, has grossed over ₹250 crore. It has outperformed several big-budget action films and superhero movies.

Critics attribute its success to relatable characters and sharp writing. The film is set in a small-town Gujarat household and deals with generational conflict. Audiences, tired of formulaic masala films, have embraced its fresh narrative.

Regional Cinema Leads the Charge

Of the top 10, six films are from South Indian languages. The Telugu film Vikramarkudu 2 occupies the third spot, with a lifetime collection of ₹220 crore. The Malayalam film Kerala Express, a crime thriller, is at number four, surprising many with its pan-India appeal.

Hindi cinema has only three films in the top 10. Besides Agni Prahar and Family First, the only other Hindi film in the list is a romantic drama, Pyaar Kiya To Darna Kya, which earned ₹120 crore. The absence of big-ticket Hindi releases in the second quarter is clearly visible.

What This Means for the Industry

The data suggests that audiences are rewarding novelty over star power. Except for the top two films, every other entry in the list is a fresh intellectual property, not a franchise sequel. This is a shift from previous years, where sequels and remakes dominated.

Exhibitors point out that the success of Family First has encouraged producers to greenlight more original scripts. Meanwhile, several upcoming big-budget films are now scrambling to adjust their marketing strategies.

Looking ahead, the second half of 2026 is packed with releases. Major stars have films lined up for Diwali and Christmas. The real test will be whether these new titles can maintain the momentum or if the industry will see another slump after the festive season.
