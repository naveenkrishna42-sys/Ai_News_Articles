Preeti Simoes on Being Known as Kapil Sharma’s Ex: 'It Is Very Frustrating'

Preeti Simoes, a well-known television producer, has spoken candidly about the persistent tag that follows her around: Kapil Sharma's ex-girlfriend. In a recent interview, she admitted that the label has become a source of deep frustration, overshadowing her professional achievements and personal identity.

Simoes, who worked closely with Kapil Sharma during the early years of his comedy shows, said the constant reference to her past relationship has been difficult to digest. She described the experience as exhausting, noting that years after the relationship ended, her name is still prefixed with the comedian's.

The 'Mad' Label After Quitting The Kapil Sharma Show

Simoes also revisited a particularly painful chapter of her career—the time she decided to walk away from The Kapil Sharma Show. She recalled how that decision was met with disbelief, with many in the industry and even acquaintances calling her 'mad' for leaving a successful show.

In her recollection, she said that the reaction to her departure was not about her professional judgment but about her personal association. She pointed out that the narrative was shaped around her being someone's girlfriend rather than a producer making a career decision.

Reduced to 'Someone's Girlfriend'

Simoes expressed her dismay at how her identity was compressed into a single relationship tag. She stated, "I was someone's girlfriend," highlighting how that overshadowed the work she had put in behind the scenes. The frustration, she said, comes from the fact that her own journey, struggles, and contributions are often ignored in favour of a simplistic, sensational label.

She noted that this is a common problem for women in the entertainment industry, where professional merit is frequently weighed against personal associations. Simoes said that while she respects her past and the time she spent on the show, she wishes people would see her as an individual first.

A Career Beyond a Relationship

Preeti Simoes is not just a name attached to a popular comedy show. She has carved a niche for herself as a producer, with a body of work that spans television and digital content. Her recent projects have been well-received, yet the shadow of her past relationship continues to loom large in media narratives.

In her interview, she made it clear that she is proud of her work and the path she has taken. She also acknowledged that while the frustration is real, she has learned to navigate it. She said she hopes that over time, her professional identity will take precedence over her personal history.
