Yash's 'Toxic' gets 'A' certificate with minor changes

Yash's upcoming film 'Toxic: A Fairytale For Grown-ups' has cleared the Central Board of Film Certification (CBFC) with an 'A' certificate. The board has asked for minor word changes and the inclusion of warnings in the film. The movie, starring Yash and Kiara Advani, is set to release soon.

Advance bookings begin, 85,000 tickets sold

Advance bookings for 'Toxic' have begun, with 85,000 tickets being sold in the first hours. The film has earned Rs 4.10 crore within hours of the bookings starting. The high demand for tickets indicates that the film is expected to be a blockbuster.

UK ratings for 'Toxic' harsher than Ranveer Singh's 'Dhurandhar'

The film 'Toxic' has received harsher ratings in the UK compared to Ranveer Singh's 'Dhurandhar'. This could impact the film's international release and distribution. The exact ratings have not been disclosed, but the difference in ratings suggests that 'Toxic' may face more restrictions in the UK.

Film's release date and marketing strategy

The release date for 'Toxic' has not been officially announced, but the film is expected to hit the screens soon. The marketing strategy for the film includes a mix of digital and traditional advertising, with a focus on the star power of Yash and Kiara Advani. The film's trailer and posters are expected to generate significant buzz.

What to watch next

As 'Toxic' prepares for its release, fans are eagerly awaiting more details about the film's plot, cast, and crew. The film's performance at the box office will be closely watched, as it will provide insights into the current trends in the Indian film industry. Additionally, the film's international ratings and distribution will be closely monitored, as they could impact its overall success.
