JioTag 2 Launched in India at Rs 1,249 with 2-Year Battery Life

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • JioTag 2 is priced at Rs 1,249 in India
  • Supports both Android and iOS devices
  • Offers up to 2 years of battery life

Reliance Jio has expanded its smart accessory lineup with the launch of the JioTag 2 in India. The Bluetooth tracker, which succeeds the original JioTag, is now available for purchase at a price of Rs 1,249. The device is designed to help users locate misplaced items like keys, wallets, and bags.

The JioTag 2 stands out for its cross-platform compatibility. Unlike many trackers that are locked to a single ecosystem, this one works with both Android and iOS devices. That means iPhone users and Android fans alike can use it without switching platforms.

Key Features and Specifications

The most notable feature of the JioTag 2 is its battery life. The company claims the tracker can run for up to two years on a single charge. This is a significant improvement over the previous model, which required more frequent charging.

The device uses Bluetooth technology to connect to a smartphone. When an item goes missing, users can open the JioTag app on their phone to see the tracker's last known location and make it ring. The app is available for both Android and iOS, making setup simple.

While the tracker does not include GPS, it relies on a crowd-sourced finding network. Other JioTag users' phones can anonymously pick up the signal from your tracker and update its location, similar to how other major trackers work.

Pricing and Availability

At Rs 1,249, the JioTag 2 is positioned as a budget-friendly option in the tracking device market. This price point is roughly $13, making it one of the more affordable trackers available in India.

The tracker is available for purchase through Jio's official channels and select online retailers. It comes in a compact design that can easily attach to keys, backpacks, or pet collars.

Compatibility and Ease of Use

Setting up the JioTag 2 is straightforward. Users need to download the JioTag app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, pair the device via Bluetooth, and it's ready to go. The app also allows users to check the last seen location on a map.

The device is water-resistant, which adds durability for everyday use. Whether you're in the rain or accidentally drop it in a puddle, the tracker is built to withstand minor exposure to water.

For those who frequently misplace items, the JioTag 2 offers a practical solution without breaking the bank. Its dual compatibility with Android and iOS removes the usual ecosystem restrictions, giving users more flexibility.

As Jio continues to build its smart device ecosystem, the JioTag 2 is a clear attempt to capture the growing demand for affordable tracking devices in India. With competitors like Apple's AirTag and various Android-compatible trackers, the market is heating up.

Moving forward, the real test will be how well JioTag 2 performs in real-world conditions, especially the crowd-finding network's effectiveness in India's densely populated areas. Users who have pre-ordered or bought the device will soon share their experiences, which will likely shape the tracker's reputation in the coming weeks.
