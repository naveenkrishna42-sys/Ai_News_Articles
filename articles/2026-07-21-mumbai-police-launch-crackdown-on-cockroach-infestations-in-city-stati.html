Mumbai Police Launch Crackdown on Cockroach Infestations in City Stations

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mumbai Police has begun a special operation to address cockroach infestations in police stations.
  • The drive focuses on deep cleaning and pest control measures to improve hygiene.
  • Officials aim to boost morale and health of personnel by ensuring cleaner facilities.

Cleanliness Drive Targets Pests at Police Stations

The Mumbai Police have launched a new operation, but it is not against criminals. This time, the target is cockroaches. The force has begun a city-wide cleanliness drive to tackle infestations at police stations.

Several stations across Mumbai have reported persistent issues with cockroaches. The problem has been affecting the work environment for officers and staff. The new initiative aims to address these hygiene concerns head-on.

Deep Cleaning and Pest Control Underway

The operation involves thorough deep cleaning of all areas within police stations. This includes offices, locker rooms, and common areas. Pest control measures are being implemented simultaneously.

Officials have instructed station house officers to ensure that all corners are cleared. Food waste and clutter, which attract cockroaches, are being removed. The drive is being conducted in phases to cover all 93 police stations in the city.

Health and Morale of Personnel a Priority

The initiative is part of a broader effort to improve working conditions for police personnel. A clean environment is expected to boost morale and reduce health risks. Cockroaches can carry diseases and trigger allergies.

Senior officers have stressed that hygiene is a non-negotiable aspect of a professional workplace. The drive is also expected to improve the image of police stations in the eyes of the public. Citizens often visit stations for various services, and cleanliness makes a positive impression.

Ongoing Monitoring and Future Plans

The police department has set up a monitoring mechanism to ensure the drive's effectiveness. Regular inspections will be conducted. Stations that fail to maintain cleanliness may face action.

This is not a one-time exercise. The plan is to make cleanliness a sustained effort. Officials are considering involving professional cleaning agencies for long-term pest control contracts. The success of this drive could lead to similar initiatives in other government buildings.

The Mumbai Police are now balancing crime-fighting with cockroach-fighting. The results of this unusual operation will be watched closely in the coming weeks.
