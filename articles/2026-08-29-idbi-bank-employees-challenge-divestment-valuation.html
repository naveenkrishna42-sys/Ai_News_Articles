IDBI Bank employees challenge divestment valuation

Employees and officers of IDBI Bank have raised concerns over the bank's divestment process, demanding a review of the valuation and property assets. The Association of IDBI Bank Officers and Employees (AIBOA) has urged the government to halt the divestment, alleging a breach of assurance to officers.

Key concerns and demands

The AIBOA has raised several concerns, including the valuation of the bank's assets and the proposed divestment process. The association has demanded a fresh parliamentary review of the stake sale, citing concerns over the transparency and fairness of the process.

The AIBOA has also sought intervention from the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) amid Life Insurance Corporation of India's (LIC) proposed stake sale in IDBI Bank. The association has alleged that the proposed stake sale could have adverse implications for the bank's operations and the interests of its employees.

Broader impact on PSUs

The divestment of IDBI Bank is part of the government's broader strategy to improve the financial health of public sector banks. However, the concerns raised by the AIBOA highlight the challenges and risks associated with such divestments. The proposed stake sale by LIC could have significant implications for the bank's operations and the interests of its employees.

The concerns raised by the AIBOA also reflect the broader concerns of employees and officers in the public sector.
