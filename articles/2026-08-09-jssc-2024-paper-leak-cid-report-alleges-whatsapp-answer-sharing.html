JSSC 2024 Paper Leak: CID Report Alleges WhatsApp Answer Sharing

📅 2026-08-09 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • CID report details 120 leaked answers shared via WhatsApp
  • Jharkhand students continue protests for 16 days over exam irregularities
  • Government announces fresh talks as fasting student leader's health worsens

Ranchi: A confidential CID report into the Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) 2024 examination paper leak has reportedly uncovered a sophisticated network. The document, accessed by NDTV, points to 120 answers being circulated through WhatsApp groups, raising serious questions about the integrity of the recruitment process.

The revelation has added fuel to an already volatile situation in the state. Student protests, now in their 16th day, have intensified, with demonstrators demanding a thorough investigation and cancellation of the tainted exams.

The WhatsApp Trail and 120 Answers

According to the CID report, the leak was not a simple case of a single paper being photographed. Instead, it describes a coordinated effort where 120 answers were distributed via the messaging platform. The findings suggest a premeditated operation, potentially involving multiple individuals.

Investigators are now scrutinising the digital trail, including phone records and server logs, to trace the origin of the messages. The report is considered a critical piece of evidence, potentially linking suspects to the distribution network. Officials have not yet confirmed how many candidates may have benefited from the leaked material.

Protests Intensify as Student Leader's Health Declines

The public mood in Jharkhand remains tense. The protest, which began over alleged irregularities in the JSSC 2024 exams, has seen students employ poetry, satire, and humour to press their demands. However, the situation has taken a sombre turn with the health of a fasting student leader deteriorating.

Medical teams are monitoring the leader's condition, but the protest has continued unabated. The student body has rejected earlier government overtures, alleging 'political manoeuvring' behind the scenes. They maintain that only a complete cancellation of the exams and a fresh, transparent recruitment drive will satisfy their demands.

Government Announces Fresh Talks

In response to the escalating crisis, the Jharkhand government has announced a fresh round of talks with student representatives. The decision comes after mounting pressure from various quarters, including public figures who have voiced support for the protesters.

The upcoming dialogue is seen as a crucial opportunity to de-escalate the standoff. The government's approach will be closely watched, particularly in light of the CID report's findings. The student body has agreed to attend but has cautioned that they will not accept anything short of concrete action.

Notably, the protests have drawn support from cultural figures, with actor Piyush Mishra backing the students' cause. This has amplified the movement's visibility, turning a local administrative issue into a broader debate about fairness in public recruitment.

What Lies Ahead

The state government faces a difficult path forward. The CID report provides a factual basis for action, but the political implications are significant. The upcoming talks will test the government's willingness to address the core grievances.

All eyes are now on the negotiating table. The outcome of the fresh dialogue will determine whether the protests can be peacefully resolved or if the movement will gain further momentum. The health of the fasting student leader adds an element of urgency to the proceedings.
