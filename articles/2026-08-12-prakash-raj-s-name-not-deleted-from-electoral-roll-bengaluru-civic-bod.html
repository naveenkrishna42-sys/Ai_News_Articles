Prakash Raj's name not deleted from electoral roll, Bengaluru civic body clarifies

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bengaluru civic body denies deletion of actor Prakash Raj's name from electoral roll.
  • Clarification comes amid rumours and confusion over voter list updates.
  • Officials urge citizens to verify their own enrolment status.

The Bengaluru civic body has clarified that actor Prakash Raj's name has not been removed from the electoral roll, putting an end to speculation that had spread on social media. The clarification was issued on Tuesday after reports suggested the actor's voter registration had been cancelled.

Official clarification

Officials from the Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) said that Prakash Raj's name remains on the electoral roll and that no deletion has been carried out. The clarification came after queries from citizens and media about the actor's voter status.

The civic body noted that routine updates to the electoral roll are carried out periodically as per guidelines from the Election Commission. Any changes, they said, are made only after due process and verification.

Rumour control

The confusion appears to have originated from a broader exercise of updating voter lists in the city, during which some names are added, corrected, or removed. This process, officials say, often triggers unnecessary alarm when individuals find their names missing from a particular draft list.

Prakash Raj, a well-known actor and political commentator, is not the first public figure to face such rumours. In the past, similar claims have been made about other celebrities, only to be later debunked by election authorities.

What citizens should know

BBMP officials have urged voters to check their names on the official Election Commission portal rather than rely on social media posts. They also reminded citizens that the final electoral roll is published after a specified period of claims and objections, during which any errors can be rectified.

For those who find their names missing, the civic body has set up helplines and designated officers at the ward level to assist with voter ID corrections and enrolments. The process, they say, is straightforward and does not require any payment.

No official statement from actor

Prakash Raj himself has not yet commented on the matter. His office did not respond to requests for clarification. The actor, who has been vocal on political issues in the past, may choose to address the situation in his own time.

The controversy comes at a time when electoral roll accuracy is under the scanner across the country, with several states undergoing intensive drives to update voter data. The Election Commission has repeatedly stressed the importance of maintaining error-free lists.

For now, the Bengaluru civic body's statement should put an end to the rumour. However, the episode highlights how quickly unverified information can spread and how civic authorities are often forced to respond to misinformation.

Looking ahead, the BBMP is expected to release a detailed circular on the electoral roll update schedule for the coming months. Citizens are advised to stay informed through official channels and participate in the claims and objections window to ensure their names are correctly listed.
