DR Congo Ebola outbreak: Funding crisis looms as death toll rises

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Health Original source ↗
Key points
  • DR Congo's Ebola outbreak has surpassed 2,500 deaths.
  • Funding for the response is running low, raising concerns about the future.
  • The WHO and Africa CDC have welcomed the allocation of Ebola vaccines to DR Congo.

DR Congo's Ebola outbreak surpasses 2,500 deaths

The Democratic Republic of the Congo (DR Congo) is facing a severe Ebola outbreak, with the death toll now surpassing 2,500. The outbreak, which began in August 2023, has spread across multiple provinces, including Équateur, North Kivu, Ituri, and South Kivu.

Funding crisis looms as response efforts falter

As the death toll rises, concerns are growing about the sustainability of the response efforts. The funding for the Ebola response is running low, with officials warning of a potential funding cliff. The Pope has called for more efforts to stop the Ebola outbreak in DR Congo, but the lack of adequate funding is a major concern.

WHO and Africa CDC welcome vaccine allocation

The World Health Organization (WHO) and the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention (Africa CDC) have welcomed the allocation of Ebola vaccines to DR Congo. More than 16,000 doses of the Ervebo vaccine have arrived in the country, which is expected to help in the fight against the outbreak.

Challenges in vaccine distribution and treatment

The distribution of the Ervebo vaccine and other treatment resources is facing several challenges. The remote and inaccessible nature of some affected areas is making it difficult to reach everyone in need. Additionally, there are concerns about the availability and quality of healthcare services in these areas.

What's next?

As the Ebola outbreak in DR Congo continues to spread, the focus will be on securing adequate funding for the response efforts. The WHO and Africa CDC will play a crucial role in ensuring the successful distribution of vaccines and other treatment resources. The international community will need to come together to provide the necessary support to DR Congo in its fight against the outbreak.
