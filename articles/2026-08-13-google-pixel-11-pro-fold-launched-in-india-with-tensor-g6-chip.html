Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold launched in India with Tensor G6 chip

📅 2026-08-13 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pixel 11 Pro Fold launched in India with Tensor G6 processor
  • Part of Pixel 11 series with upgraded cameras and AI features
  • Pixel Watch 5 also unveiled at Made by Google 2026 event

Pixel 11 Pro Fold arrives in India

Google has launched the Pixel 11 Pro Fold in India, bringing its latest foldable flagship to the market. The device is powered by the new Tensor G6 processor, which the company says delivers faster performance and improved AI capabilities.

The launch is part of the broader Pixel 11 series, which Google describes as "your most personal Pixel yet." The lineup includes multiple models, all featuring upgraded cameras and deeper AI integration.

Tensor G6: What's new

The Tensor G6 chip is designed to handle on-device AI tasks more efficiently. Google has focused on improving battery life and charging speeds with this generation, addressing a common complaint among Pixel users.

The new processor also enables advanced computational photography features, allowing the Pixel 11 series to capture better low-light shots and offer more editing tools.

Upgraded cameras and AI features

Google has upgraded the camera hardware across the Pixel 11 lineup. While the company hasn't disclosed full specifications yet, early reports suggest significant improvements in zoom and video stabilization.

AI features are central to the Pixel 11 experience. The phones come with new tools for photo editing, real-time translation, and voice assistance, all powered by the Tensor G6's neural processing unit.

The Pixel 11 Pro Fold, specifically, combines these features with a foldable design that offers a tablet-like display when opened.

Pixel Watch 5 and other announcements

Alongside the Pixel 11 phones, Google unveiled the Pixel Watch 5 at its 'Made by Google 2026' event. The smartwatch is expected to offer improved health tracking and deeper integration with the Pixel ecosystem.

The event also showcased several other AI-driven features across Google's hardware lineup, reinforcing the company's push to make AI a core part of its devices.

Pricing and availability in India

Google has not yet confirmed the exact pricing for the Indian market. However, based on previous launches, the Pixel 11 Pro Fold is expected to be positioned as a premium device, competing with other high-end foldables.

The company typically offers introductory offers and exchange deals during the initial sale period. Interested buyers can check Google's official store and major e-commerce platforms for availability.

Analysts suggest that the Pixel 11 series could help Google gain a stronger foothold in India's premium smartphone segment, where it has been steadily growing its presence.

More details about specifications and India-specific pricing are expected in the coming weeks as Google rolls out the devices to retail channels.
