Delhi T20Is: ACB confirms dates after BCCI approval

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • ACB announces schedule for Delhi T20Is against India.
  • BCCI grants clearance for the bilateral series.
  • Matches mark a significant cricketing engagement between the two nations.

The Afghanistan Cricket Board (ACB) has confirmed the dates for the upcoming T20I series against India, scheduled to be held in Delhi. The announcement comes after the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) gave its official greenlight for the fixtures, ending weeks of speculation over the venue and schedule.

While the exact match dates have been locked in, officials from both boards have refrained from publicly detailing the full itinerary. The series is expected to feature three T20Is, though the ACB has not yet confirmed the final number of matches in its latest release.

A rare bilateral outing

This will be a notable bilateral series for Afghanistan, who have often had to rely on neutral venues for home games due to security concerns in their own country. Playing in Delhi offers a familiar subcontinental setting, with conditions that closely mirror what the team would encounter at home.

For Indian fans, the series presents an opportunity to see the national side take on a rapidly improving Afghan squad. The two teams have shared memorable encounters in recent years, particularly in multi-nation tournaments, but bilateral meetings have been infrequent.

BCCI's nod after deliberation

The BCCI's approval was not immediate, with logistical and security assessments reportedly taking time. Sources indicate that the board conducted thorough reviews before clearing the fixtures, though no official statement has been issued detailing the nature of those checks.

Delhi's infrastructure and experience in hosting international matches likely played a role in the final decision. The city's cricket stadium has been a regular venue for India's white-ball games, and the ACB has previously expressed comfort with playing in the capital.

What this means for Afghan cricket

For the ACB, securing a confirmed series against a top-tier side like India is a significant achievement. It provides their players with high-pressure exposure and helps in the board's broader push to expand its international calendar beyond ICC events.

The financial implications are also non-trivial. A bilateral series with India typically brings in substantial broadcast and sponsorship revenue, which can be reinvested in grassroots development back in Afghanistan. The ACB has been vocal about using such opportunities to strengthen its domestic structure.

Neither board has yet revealed the squad lists or the exact venue within Delhi. The ACB is expected to announce its playing group closer to the series, with several senior players likely to be rested given the packed global schedule.

Looking ahead

The confirmation of dates now shifts the focus to preparation. Both teams will look to fine-tune their combinations, especially with the next T20 World Cup on the horizon. For Afghanistan, this series is a chance to test their bench strength against a formidable opponent.

Fans can expect ticket sales to open soon, with the ACB and BCCI expected to release further details in the coming weeks. All eyes will now be on the player announcements and whether the series produces the kind of competitive cricket that has come to define recent India-Afghanistan clashes.
