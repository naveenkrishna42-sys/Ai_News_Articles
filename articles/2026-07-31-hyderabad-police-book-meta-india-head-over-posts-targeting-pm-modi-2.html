Hyderabad Police Book Meta India Head Over Posts Targeting PM Modi

📅 2026-07-31 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hyderabad cybercrime police have booked Meta India's head over posts targeting PM Modi.
  • The case relates to alleged derogatory content during a protest involving the Chief Justice of India.
  • The Indian government is set to hold policy and tech discussions with Meta within a week.
  • The development follows a brief disappearance of PM Modi's reel from Instagram.

The Hyderabad cybercrime police have registered a case against the head of Meta India in connection with social media posts allegedly targeting Prime Minister Narendra Modi. The action comes amid heightened scrutiny of how the tech giant moderates content involving high-profile accounts.

Case Details and Complaint

Officials said the case was filed following a complaint regarding 'derogatory' posts that surfaced during a protest related to the Chief Justice of India. The complaint accused the Meta India head of failing to prevent the circulation of such content on platforms like Facebook and Instagram.

The police have not yet disclosed the specific nature of the posts or the timeline of their appearance. However, sources indicate that the investigation will focus on whether Meta complied with Indian laws regarding content moderation.

Government's Next Steps

In a parallel development, the central government has announced plans to hold policy and technical discussions with Meta within a week. The talks are expected to address the removal of a post by PM Modi that briefly vanished from the platform, raising questions about the company's moderation practices for VVIP accounts.

According to reports, the government is seeking clarity on the algorithms and oversight mechanisms that govern such accounts. This comes as part of a broader push to ensure accountability of social media intermediaries under India's IT rules.

What Is Known About Meta's Moderation

The incident has brought attention to how Meta handles content from top political leaders. While the company has standard protocols for public figures, the specifics of these rules remain opaque to most users.

Meta has maintained that it applies its policies consistently, but critics argue that automated systems can lead to errors. The government's upcoming meeting is expected to seek a detailed explanation of the incident involving the PM's reel.

Legal Implications and Reactions

The booking of the Meta India head marks a significant escalation in the standoff between the government and the social media giant. Legal experts note that the case could set a precedent for how foreign tech companies are held liable for content on their platforms.

Neither Meta India nor its head has issued a public statement yet. The company's legal team is likely to review the FIR and respond in due course, possibly seeking to quash the case.

Meanwhile, opposition parties have seized on the issue to question the government's handling of free speech, while supporters of the ruling party have demanded stricter action against those spreading derogatory content.

What to Watch For

The next few days will be crucial as the government-Meta discussions approach. The outcome could determine whether the case is pursued in court or resolved through regulatory adjustments. Observers are also watching for any response from Meta's global headquarters, which may weigh in on the matter.
