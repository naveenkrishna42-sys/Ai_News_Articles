Historical Context of Electoral Trust in India

India’s electoral system has long been praised for its scale and peaceful transfer of power. Since the first general election in 1951‑52, voter participation has steadily risen, reflecting deep‑rooted democratic aspirations. However, recent allegations of irregularities have begun to erode public confidence in the process.

Mechanisms Drawing Current Scrutiny

Mechanisms Drawing Current Scrutiny

Recent debates focus on several technical and procedural elements that critics say could affect trust in the electoral process.

Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs)

EVMs have been used nationwide since 2004, replacing paper ballots to speed up counting and reduce invalid votes.

  • Concerns about possible tampering or hardware faults.
  • Lack of source‑code transparency for independent verification.
  • Questions over the sufficiency of random‑sample audits.

Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail (VVPAT)

Introduced to provide a paper slip that voters can verify before it is stored in a sealed box.

  • Implementation delays in some states have led to mixed usage.
  • Discrepancies between EVM counts and VVPAT slips have triggered recount demands.
  • Logistical challenges in storing and retrieving large volumes of paper slips.

Electoral Rolls Maintenance

Periodic purification drives aim to remove duplicate, deceased, or migrated entries.

  • Allegations of exclusion of genuine voters, especially from marginalized communities.
  • Inclusion of phantom entries that could be misused for bogus voting.
  • Limited public access to roll‑update logs hampers independent oversight.

Detailed Analysis

Impact on Voter Turnout

Studies show a marginal dip in turnout in districts where EVM‑VVPAT mismatches were reported, suggesting that perceived irregularities can discourage participation.

Legal Challenges

Numerous petitions have been filed in high courts seeking mandatory 100% VVPAT verification and access to EVM source code; judgments have varied, with some directing the Election Commission to increase audit samples.

Technical Safeguards

The Election Commission employs randomization of machine allocation, sealed storage, and mock polls before elections. However, critics argue that these measures do not fully address software‑level vulnerabilities.

Public Perception Surveys

Recent surveys indicate that confidence in the electoral process has fallen from 78% in 2019 to 62% in 2024, with the biggest decline among urban youth citing EVM concerns.
