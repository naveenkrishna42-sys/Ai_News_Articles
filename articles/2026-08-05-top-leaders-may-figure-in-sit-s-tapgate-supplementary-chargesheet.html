Top leaders may figure in SIT's Tapgate supplementary chargesheet

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • SIT to file supplementary chargesheet in Tapgate case
  • Top leaders may be named based on new evidence
  • Case relates to alleged phone tapping in Telangana

Supplementary chargesheet on the cards

The Special Investigation Team (SIT) probing the Telangana phone-tapping case, widely referred to as Tapgate, is expected to file a supplementary chargesheet that could name some top political leaders. Sources familiar with the investigation indicated that the chargesheet will be submitted soon, though the exact date has not been confirmed.

The development comes months after the initial chargesheet was filed, which had already named several accused. Now, with additional evidence gathered during the probe, the SIT is reportedly preparing to expand the list of those arraigned.

What the new chargesheet may contain

According to officials aware of the matter, the supplementary chargesheet is likely to include call detail records, voice samples, and digital evidence that allegedly link senior figures to the conspiracy. The SIT has been examining intercepted conversations and data from seized devices to establish a chain of command.

While the names of the leaders have not been officially disclosed, speculation is rife that individuals holding significant political positions could be implicated. However, the SIT has maintained a tight lid on details, and no formal confirmation has been made public.

Legal hurdles and next steps

Legal experts point out that a supplementary chargesheet requires judicial scrutiny, and the accused will get an opportunity to challenge the evidence. The court is expected to take cognisance of the chargesheet once it is filed, following which the trial process will begin.

The accused already in the case have been granted bail by the High Court, and any new additions to the chargesheet may lead to fresh bail petitions. The SIT, for its part, is under pressure to ensure that the evidence is watertight to avoid procedural setbacks.

Background of the Tapgate case

The Tapgate case pertains to the alleged illegal interception of phone conversations of several individuals, including politicians, bureaucrats, and businesspersons, during the previous regime in Telangana. The investigation was ordered after a complaint was lodged, and the SIT was constituted to look into the matter.

Earlier chargesheets had already named a few accused, including some police officers and political operatives. The supplementary chargesheet is being seen as a crucial step in establishing the involvement of higher-ups in the alleged conspiracy.

Political reactions and public interest

The case has generated significant political heat in Telangana, with rival parties accusing each other of orchestrating the surveillance. Several leaders have demanded a thorough probe and swift action, while others have questioned the timing of the chargesheet.

Public interest remains high, as the case touches on issues of privacy and the misuse of state machinery. Civil society groups have called for transparency in the investigation and for the protection of whistleblowers who came forward with information.

As the SIT prepares to file the supplementary chargesheet, all eyes will be on the court's response and the political fallout. The coming weeks are likely to see intense legal battles and political manoeuvring as the case unfolds.

For now, the SIT continues its work, and the next hearing is expected to provide clarity on the timeline. Observers will be watching to see whether the new chargesheet brings closure or opens fresh controversies.
