Journalist Accuses UP of Harassment After Reporting Ram Temple Donation Theft

Journalist Abhishek Upadhyay has filed a petition in the Supreme Court, alleging harassment and a 'planted' FIR after he reported on alleged theft of donations meant for the Ram Temple in Ayodhya. The case has drawn attention to the delicate balance between investigative journalism and government accountability.

Background of the Case

Abhishek Upadhyay, a content creator and journalist, reported extensively on the alleged theft of donations for the Ram Temple. His reports sparked a controversy, leading to a First Information Report (FIR) against him for road rage in Ghaziabad. Upadhyay claims the FIR was planted to silence him.

Supreme Court Hearing Scheduled

The Supreme Court has scheduled a hearing for 25 August 2026 to examine Upadhyay's petition. The UP government is expected to present its defense, arguing that the FIR was filed based on a genuine complaint.

Broader Implications

This case raises significant questions about press freedom and the accountability of government officials. It highlights the challenges faced by journalists who investigate corruption and mismanagement, especially in politically sensitive areas.
