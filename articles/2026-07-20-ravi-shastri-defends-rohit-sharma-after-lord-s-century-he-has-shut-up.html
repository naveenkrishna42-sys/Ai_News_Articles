Ravi Shastri Defends Rohit Sharma After Lord's Century: 'He Has Shut Up Everybody'

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ravi Shastri said Rohit Sharma has 'shut up everybody' with his Lord's century.
  • Rohit Sharma stated he is unfazed by criticism, calling it part of the game.
  • The Indian captain dismissed retirement speculation, keeping World Cup 2027 in sight.

Shastri's Strong Defence of Rohit Sharma

Former India head coach Ravi Shastri has come out in strong defence of Rohit Sharma after the Indian captain's century at Lord's. Shastri said the knock has silenced all critics who questioned the batter's form and future in international cricket.

“He has shut up everybody,” Shastri said in a television interview. The former all-rounder did not mince words, pointing out that Rohit had once again delivered on the biggest stage when it mattered most. His comments come amid intense scrutiny of Rohit's recent performances.

Noise Is Part of the Game, Says Rohit

Rohit Sharma himself addressed the criticism after his innings. “If there's no noise, there's no fun,” he said with a shrug. The Indian captain made it clear he does not let external chatter affect his game.

Speaking to reporters at the post-match press conference, Rohit added, “Till I am playing, there will always be noise. That's how it has been, and that's how it will be.” His words reflect a man long accustomed to scrutiny, having led India through multiple high-pressure campaigns.

Retirement Talk Dismissed, World Cup 2027 in Sight

Speculation about Rohit Sharma's retirement had been growing in recent weeks, especially after a lean patch with the bat. But the Lord's century has put an end to those rumours, at least for now.

According to sources close to the team, the 39-year-old remains focused on the 2027 World Cup. The Indian team management has not indicated any imminent changes in leadership. Rohit himself has not given any hint of stepping away from the game.

Lord's Knock Silences Critics

The century at Lord's was vintage Rohit Sharma — composed, authoritative, and built on a foundation of patience. He paced his innings expertly, rotating strike early before opening up against the bowling attack.

Fans and former players alike have hailed the knock as one of his finest in Test cricket. Social media was flooded with praise, but Rohit remained grounded. “I just went out there to do my job,” he said later.

Shastri's defence echoes a broader sentiment among those who have worked closely with Rohit. The former coach knows the mental resilience required to lead India, and he believes Rohit has it in abundance.

What Lies Ahead for Rohit Sharma

With the World Cup still a year away, Rohit's form will be crucial for India's plans. The team's batting order revolves around his experience at the top, and his leadership remains unchallenged.

The BCCI has not made any official statement on Rohit's future beyond the ongoing series. But if this Lord's innings is any indication, the Indian captain is far from done. The next few months will reveal whether he can sustain this momentum.
