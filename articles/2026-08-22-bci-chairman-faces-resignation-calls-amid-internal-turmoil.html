Internal Crisis Hits Bar Council of India

The Bar Council of India (BCI) is currently navigating a significant leadership crisis. Chairman Manan Kumar Mishra is facing intense internal pressure after his co-chairman formally demanded his resignation. This development marks a sharp escalation in the ongoing administrative instability within the apex regulatory body for legal practitioners.

The demand for resignation was issued during a recent General Council meeting. The co-chairman has cited eight specific allegations against Mr. Mishra, casting a shadow over his continued tenure. These accusations have triggered a wave of discord among council members, who are now divided over the leadership’s future.

The NALSAR Controversy and Growing Dissent

The current unrest is linked to a controversy involving the National Academy of Legal Studies and Research (NALSAR). While specific details of the dispute remain under internal review, the fallout has directly impacted the council’s functioning. Mr. Mishra has reportedly refused to step down despite the formal ultimatum issued to him.

The ultimatum stipulates a 15-day window for the chairman to vacate his office. This public demand reflects the depth of the rift within the BCI, as members grapple with the fallout of the ongoing institutional friction. The refusal to resign has further hardened the stance of opposing factions within the council.

Legal Challenges and Supreme Court Involvement

The conflict has now transcended internal council meetings. A plea has been filed in the Supreme Court seeking the removal of Mr. Mishra. The petition argues that the current administrative gridlock necessitates judicial intervention to restore order within the BCI.

  • Demand for resignation issued with a 15-day deadline.
  • Eight distinct allegations leveled against the chairman.
  • Supreme Court petitioned for formal removal.

Legal observers point out that the BCI occupies a central role in regulating legal education and professional conduct across the country. Any instability at the top level carries potential implications for the broader legal fraternity. Consequently, the council is under heavy scrutiny as it attempts to manage this leadership deadlock.

Uncertainty Over Next Steps

As of now, the BCI chairman has maintained his position despite the growing chorus of dissent. Whether the council can resolve these grievances internally or if the Supreme Court will take up the removal request remains the primary focus. All eyes are now on the 15-day timeline as the legal community awaits a resolution to the crisis.
