New Appointment

Pralhad Joshi has taken charge as India's new education minister, replacing Dharmendra Pradhan. Joshi, who has been assigned this new role, has stated that education is a new subject for him. This change in leadership has sparked interest in the future direction of India's education sector.

Background

Pralhad Joshi's appointment comes after the resignation of Dharmendra Pradhan, who held the position prior. The reasons behind Pradhan's exit have not been officially confirmed. Joshi will now be responsible for overseeing the country's education policies and initiatives.
