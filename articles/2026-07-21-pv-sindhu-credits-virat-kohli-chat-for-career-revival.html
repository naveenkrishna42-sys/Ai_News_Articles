PV Sindhu Credits Virat Kohli Chat for Career Revival

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • PV Sindhu ended a long title drought by winning the Japan Open.
  • She credited a conversation with Virat Kohli for helping her mentally reset.
  • Sindhu adopted a data-driven training approach for her comeback.

The Conversation That Changed Everything

PV Sindhu has opened up about the turning point in her career. The two-time Olympic medallist revealed that a heart-to-heart chat with cricketer Virat Kohli played a pivotal role in her recent resurgence.

Speaking after her historic Japan Open victory, Sindhu said she was struggling with form and confidence. That was when Kohli stepped in. “When I needed someone, he was there,” she recalled. The conversation, she explained, helped her shift her mindset from self-doubt to self-belief.

Ending the Drought

The Japan Open win marked Sindhu’s first title in nearly three years. For a player of her calibre, the gap was unusual. Since winning the World Championships in 2019, she had reached several finals but failed to cross the line.

This time was different. Sindhu played with the aggression and precision that once made her the world No. 1. She dropped just one game in the entire tournament and beat top seeds along the way. The victory silenced critics who had begun to question her longevity in the sport.

The Data-Driven Makeover

Sindhu’s comeback was not just mental. Behind the scenes, she had overhauled her training regimen. Reports suggest she adopted a radical, data-driven approach, using analytics to fine-tune her game.

Her team tracked everything — shot placement, rally length, recovery time. The numbers helped identify weaknesses that the naked eye had missed. Sindhu then worked specifically on those areas, turning them into strengths. The result was a leaner, sharper player who made fewer errors and dictated rallies more effectively.

Support from a Former Rival

Among those celebrating Sindhu’s win was her longtime rival and compatriot Saina Nehwal. “You can never write a champion off,” Saina said in a statement. The two have had a storied rivalry, but Saina’s words reflected a sense of national pride.

India’s badminton ecosystem has long relied on these two players carrying the flag. With Sindhu back in winning form, the country now has two world-class shuttlers to bank on for the upcoming major championships.

What Lies Ahead

Sindhu is now expected to climb back into the top five of the world rankings. The Japan Open win also gives her crucial points for qualification to the next World Championships and Asian Games.

For now, she is taking it one tournament at a time. But if her recent performance is any indication, the Sindhu who once dominated the circuit is back. And she has Virat Kohli to thank, at least in part, for the revival.
