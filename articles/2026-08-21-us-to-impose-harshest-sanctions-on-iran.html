US to impose harshest sanctions on Iran

US President Donald Trump has announced that the United States will impose the 'toughest ever' sanctions on Iran, warning that countries supporting Iran face 'tremendous' economic consequences. The sanctions are part of what Trump has called an 'economic D-Day' for Iran.

US urges China to cooperate

President Trump has urged China to cooperate with the US in the sanctions. He has stated that China's support is crucial in the face of Iran's aggressive actions. The US has been pushing for allies and China to back Trump's economic war on Iran.

What are the implications?

The sanctions are expected to have significant economic impacts on Iran. The US has warned that Iran's allies will face severe economic consequences. However, officials have not yet confirmed the exact measures or the timeline for the sanctions.

Reaction from Iran

Iran has not yet commented on the US sanctions. However, the country has been on high alert, with officials warning of potential escalation in tensions. The US move comes amid ongoing negotiations between Iran and world powers.

What happens next?

The US and Iran are expected to continue their diplomatic efforts. However, the sanctions could potentially escalate tensions between the two countries. The US will be closely monitoring Iran's response and the level of cooperation from its allies, including China.
