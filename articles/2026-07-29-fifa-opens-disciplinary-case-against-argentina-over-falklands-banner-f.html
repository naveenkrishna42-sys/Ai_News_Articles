FIFA opens disciplinary case against Argentina over Falklands banner, final chaos

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • FIFA opens disciplinary case against Argentina for multiple potential breaches at the World Cup 2026 final.
  • Incident includes display of a banner referencing the Falklands Islands, referred to as 'Las Malvinas' in Argentina.
  • Leandro Paredes separately faces three assault charges related to post-match confrontations.

FIFA takes action after World Cup final incidents

FIFA has opened disciplinary proceedings against the Argentina national football team following several incidents during and after the 2026 World Cup final. The world governing body confirmed the move on Wednesday, citing potential breaches of its disciplinary code.

The case stems from two separate issues: the display of a controversial banner by Argentine fans and players, and post-match chaos involving the team. FIFA has not yet disclosed the full list of charges but confirmed they are investigating multiple violations.

Banner reignites political tensions

A banner referencing the Falkland Islands, which Argentina calls 'Las Malvinas', was prominently displayed during the final. The islands have been a source of tension between Argentina and the United Kingdom since the 1982 war.

FIFA regulations prohibit political statements at matches. The governing body is now examining whether the banner violated those rules. Argentina has not officially commented on the banner's display, but the incident has drawn sharp reactions from British officials.

Leandro Paredes faces assault charges

Midfielder Leandro Paredes has been separately charged with three counts of assault following the final. The charges relate to confrontations that broke out after the match, though FIFA has not provided specific details about the incidents.

Paredes was a key player for Argentina during the tournament. The charges could lead to a lengthy suspension if he is found guilty. FIFA's disciplinary panel will review the case in the coming weeks.

Broader implications for Argentina

The disciplinary proceedings come at a delicate time for Argentine football. The team has enjoyed a period of dominance, winning the 2022 World Cup and reaching the final again in 2026. But the off-field controversies risk overshadowing their achievements.

FIFA's disciplinary committee has the power to impose fines, points deductions, or even bans on players and officials. The severity of the punishment will depend on the evidence presented. Argentina's football association has not yet released a formal statement on the proceedings.

Observers note that FIFA has become increasingly strict about enforcing its code of conduct, especially regarding political statements. The outcome of this case could set a precedent for future tournaments.

What happens next

FIFA's disciplinary committee will now gather evidence and hear arguments from both sides. A decision is expected within the next few months. Argentina could appeal any sanctions through FIFA's appeals process or the Court of Arbitration for Sport. The football world will be watching closely to see how the governing body handles these high-stakes charges.
