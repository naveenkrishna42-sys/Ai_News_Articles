Sensex surges 550 points, Nifty reclaims 24,200 led by IT rally

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex opened over 550 points higher in early trade.
  • Nifty 50 index surpassed the 24,200 threshold.
  • IT sector led the market momentum during the session.

Market Opens in the Green

Indian equity benchmarks started Thursday's session on a positive note, with both the Sensex and Nifty recording significant gains. The 30-share BSE Sensex surged more than 550 points during the opening bell, reflecting renewed investor optimism.

The Nifty 50 index followed a similar upward trajectory. It comfortably moved past the 24,200 mark within minutes of the market opening. This broad-based buying interest signals a strong start for the trading day after recent fluctuations.

IT Stocks Spearhead the Rally

The information technology sector emerged as the primary driver behind the morning's gains. IT indices showed strength as investors reacted to sector-specific developments and broader market cues.

Most major technology players saw their shares climb, providing the necessary momentum to lift the headline indices. Analysts are closely monitoring whether this buying pressure will hold throughout the day or if profit-booking will emerge as the session progresses.

Broader Market Sentiment

While the IT sector led the charge, the rest of the market also displayed signs of stability. Investors appear to be positioning themselves in anticipation of steady economic indicators and corporate earnings performance.

Market participants are keeping a close watch on volume levels to gauge the sustainability of this rally. The shift in sentiment suggests that the initial jitters seen earlier this week have subsided, allowing for a more focused approach toward growth-oriented sectors.

What Investors Should Watch

Despite the strong start, traders remain cautious about potential intraday volatility. The ability of the Nifty to consolidate above the 24,200 level will be a crucial factor for the market's direction in the coming hours.

Attention will now turn toward mid-day trade data to see if the momentum can sustain or if the market faces resistance at higher levels.
