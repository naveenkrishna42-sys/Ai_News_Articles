Vivo S2 full specifications and pricing leak ahead of India launch

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Vivo S2 full specifications and pricing have leaked online ahead of the official launch.
  • The phone is expected to be powered by a MediaTek Dimensity processor and run Origin OS 6.
  • Vivo is reviving the S series in India after a seven-year gap.

Vivo is gearing up to bring back its S series to India after a seven-year hiatus, and the upcoming Vivo S2 has now been fully detailed in a major leak. The device, which is expected to launch in the coming weeks, has had its complete specifications and pricing revealed by multiple online sources.

Leaked specifications point to mid-range hardware

The Vivo S2 is tipped to feature a 6.7-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate. Under the hood, the phone is expected to pack a MediaTek Dimensity 7200 processor, paired with up to 12GB of RAM and 256GB of internal storage.

For photography, the device is said to sport a 64-megapixel primary sensor on the rear, accompanied by an 8-megapixel ultra-wide lens and a 2-megapixel macro shooter. The front camera is rumoured to be a 32-megapixel unit, housed in a centre-aligned punch-hole cutout.

Origin OS 6 and battery details

The Vivo S2 will run on Origin OS 6, based on Android 14, out of the box. This marks a shift from the company's older Funtouch OS skin. The software is expected to bring customisation features and smoother animations.

A 4,500mAh battery is expected to power the device, with support for 66W fast charging. Vivo is likely to bundle the charger in the retail box, a practice that has become less common among competitors in this segment.

Pricing leak suggests aggressive positioning

According to the leaked information, the Vivo S2 will be priced around Rs 24,999 for the base variant with 8GB RAM and 128GB storage. The top-end 12GB RAM and 256GB storage model could retail for approximately Rs 28,999.

If these prices hold, the Vivo S2 will directly compete with devices like the OnePlus Nord CE 4 and the Realme 12 Pro Plus. The company appears to be targeting users who want premium design elements and a capable camera setup without crossing the Rs 30,000 mark.

Revival of the S series

The S series was originally launched in India in 2019 but was discontinued within a couple of years. Vivo's decision to revive the lineup comes as the company looks to strengthen its presence in the competitive mid-range segment. The brand currently offers the V series for premium mid-range buyers and the T series for budget-conscious consumers.

Vivo has already started teasing the launch on social media, confirming that the S2 will arrive in India. The official teaser shows a slim profile and a large camera module, hinting at a design language similar to the company's higher-end X series.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact launch date or the final pricing. However, industry insiders expect the phone to go on sale by mid-August, likely through both online and offline channels.

What remains to be seen is how the market responds to the S series comeback. With aggressive pricing and a feature set that includes a high-refresh-rate display and fast charging, Vivo is clearly aiming to reclaim lost ground. The success of this revival will depend on whether the S2 can offer a distinct value proposition in a segment crowded with capable alternatives.
