Manav Suthar faces uphill task to prove himself in Tests

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mahela Jayawardene warns Manav Suthar about being declared a Test specialist too soon
  • Suthar needs to prove himself in different conditions to establish himself in Test cricket
  • Team India must address flaws to maintain momentum after Galle Test victory

Mahela Jayawardene warns Manav Suthar about early declaration

Sri Lanka's legendary batsman Mahela Jayawardene has reminded Manav Suthar that it's only been two Tests, and the young Indian batsman still has a long way to go before being declared a specialist in the format.

Jayawardene, who has extensive experience in Test cricket, has been impressed by Suthar's steady progress but believes he needs to prove himself in different conditions to establish himself as a regular in the Test team.

Suthar's performance in Galle Test

Suthar made his Test debut in the recent match against Sri Lanka in Galle, where he scored 42 runs in the first innings and 38 in the second. His performance was praised by many, but Jayawardene believes that Suthar needs to play more matches to understand the nuances of Test cricket.

In the Galle Test, Suthar's batting was steady, but his bowling was not as effective. He took one wicket in the match, conceding 60 runs in four overs. Jayawardene believes that Suthar needs to work on his bowling to become a complete all-rounder.

Team India must address flaws

After the victory in the Galle Test, Team India must address the flaws that led to their loss in the first Test. The team's batting lineup was inconsistent, and their bowling was not as effective as in the Galle Test.

Jayawardene believes that Team India must work on their technique and temperament to become a consistent Test team. The team's captain, Rohit Sharma, has been praised for his leadership, but Jayawardene believes that the team needs to work on their individual skills to become a formidable Test team.

Devdutt Padikkal aces the test of Test cricket

Devdutt Padikkal, another young Indian batsman, has been praised for his performance in the Galle Test. He scored 112 runs in the first innings and 68 in the second, helping Team India to a victory.

Padikkal's performance was a testament to his technique and temperament. He played with a calm and composed demeanor, and his batting was a model of consistency. Jayawardene believes that Padikkal's performance can serve as an inspiration for other young Indian batsmen.
