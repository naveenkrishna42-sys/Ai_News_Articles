Mobile Internet Suspended in Central Delhi Amid Protests at Jantar Mantar

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mobile internet services suspended in central Delhi around Jantar Mantar protest site.
  • Delhi University issues advisory cautioning students against unlawful gatherings.
  • Commuters and businesses affected by metro and internet curbs during ongoing protests.

Internet Blackout in Central Delhi

Mobile internet services were blocked in parts of central Delhi on Thursday, with the government directing telecom companies to suspend data services around the Jantar Mantar protest site. Sources confirmed the move, though authorities have not yet specified the exact duration of the restrictions.

Commuters and local businesses reported disruptions as the curbs extended to metro services in the vicinity. Police officials maintained there was no planned crackdown for tonight, but the measures were aimed at maintaining public order.

University Advisory Issued

Delhi University issued a fresh advisory cautioning students against participating in unlawful gatherings at Jantar Mantar. The university urged students to prioritise their safety and avoid any actions that could lead to disciplinary action.

The advisory comes as protests, led by the Confederation of Indian Professionals (CJP), continue to draw crowds demanding judicial reforms. Students and young professionals have been among the participants, raising concerns about campus unrest.

Authorities have not confirmed the number of protestors or any arrests made so far. The situation remains fluid, with security personnel deployed in large numbers around key government buildings.

Impact on Daily Life

The internet suspension has hit daily life hard in central Delhi. Commuters faced delays as metro stations near the protest site saw restricted entry and exit points. Local shopkeepers reported a drop in footfall, with many choosing to stay indoors.

“This is a major inconvenience. We rely on mobile data for everything from payments to navigation. The sudden block has disrupted our work,” said a vendor near Connaught Place, requesting anonymity.

Businesses in the area have urged authorities to restore services quickly, warning of potential economic losses. The government has not provided a timeline for when normalcy will return.

What to Watch

With no immediate end to the protests in sight, authorities are expected to keep the internet curbs in place until the situation de-escalates. Observers will closely watch for any official statement from the government or the CJP leadership on next steps.
