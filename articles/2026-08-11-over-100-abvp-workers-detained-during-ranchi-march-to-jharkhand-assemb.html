Over 100 ABVP workers detained during Ranchi march to Jharkhand Assembly

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • More than 100 ABVP workers detained during a march to the Jharkhand Assembly
  • Protest follows police lathi charge on students in Ranchi
  • MP Parimal Nathwani urges CM Hemant Soren to resolve demands through dialogue
  • Congress faces tightrope as it backs students while staying in government
  • Six layers of barricades set up for a 5-km march in Ranchi

Detentions during march

Over 100 Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP) workers were detained on Tuesday as they attempted to march towards the Jharkhand Assembly in Ranchi. The protest was against the police action on students that has sparked widespread outrage in the state.

The detentions came as authorities enforced strict security measures, with reports indicating that six layers of barricades were erected to prevent the marchers from reaching the Assembly. The 5-km route was heavily fortified, reflecting the tense atmosphere in the state capital.

Political reactions

Rajya Sabha MP Parimal Nathwani has urged Chief Minister Hemant Soren to resolve the students' demands through dialogue. His appeal follows the lathi charge on students, which has become a flashpoint in Jharkhand's political landscape.

The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) has seized on the issue, questioning the Congress's position in the state. The Congress, which is part of the ruling coalition, finds itself walking a tightrope—trying to back the students while remaining in government.

Congress's dilemma

The Congress's dual stance has drawn criticism from various quarters. While the party has expressed solidarity with the protesting students, its continued participation in the Hemant Soren-led government has raised questions about its commitment to the cause.

Political analysts note that the situation puts the Congress in a difficult spot, as it balances its alliance obligations with the need to address public sentiment. The party has yet to issue a formal statement clarifying its position on the police action.

Protest leadership

The Ranchi protest has been spearheaded by Devendra Nath Mahto, whose leadership has been praised by the Congress. However, the BJP has used this to question the Congress's stance, asking what the party has done for Jharkhand.

The protests have drawn attention to the broader issue of student rights and police conduct in the state. Demonstrators have demanded accountability for the lathi charge and assurances that such actions will not be repeated.

Security arrangements

The heavy security deployment, including the multiple layers of barricades, has been criticised by protesters as an overreach. Authorities, however, have defended the measures as necessary to maintain order and prevent any untoward incidents.

As the standoff continues, the state administration is under pressure to find a peaceful resolution. The detentions have only added to the tension, with ABVP workers vowing to continue their agitation until their demands are met.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of detained workers or the duration of their detention, but reports suggest that most were released later in the evening. The situation remains fluid, with further protests anticipated in the coming days.

All eyes now turn to Chief Minister Hemant Soren, who has yet to respond publicly to the calls for dialogue. Whether he chooses to engage with the students or maintain the current hardline approach will likely determine the trajectory of this unrest.
