PM Modi Chairs High-Level Meet on Middle East Crisis Preparedness

📅 2026-07-30 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • PM Modi chaired a high-level meeting with Amit Shah, other ministers, and NSA Ajit Doval.
  • Agenda reviewed preparedness for Middle East crisis, including energy security and fertiliser supplies.
  • A dedicated mechanism for the safety of Indian seafarers was ordered.
  • Meeting comes amid escalating West Asia conflict and concerns over global supply chains.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Thursday chaired a high-level meeting with senior ministers and National Security Advisor Ajit Doval. The unscheduled meeting at Parliament saw the attendance of Home Minister Amit Shah, Defence Minister Rajnath Singh, and other top officials.

The agenda was not officially disclosed, but sources indicate the meeting focused on India's preparedness for the ongoing Middle East crisis. The PM reviewed multiple aspects of the situation, including energy security and the safety of Indian seafarers.

Energy and Fertiliser Supplies Under Review

According to reports, the meeting covered India's strategy regarding LPG and Rabi fertiliser supplies. The Middle East conflict has raised concerns about global supply chains, and the government is assessing potential disruptions.

The PM is said to have ordered a dedicated mechanism for the safety of Indian seafarers working in the region. The move comes amid rising tensions that have put merchant vessels at risk.

CCS Meeting Amid Escalating Conflict

The meeting effectively functioned as a meeting of the Cabinet Committee on Security (CCS). The West Asia conflict has escalated sharply in recent days, prompting India to review its strategic and economic vulnerabilities.

Officials are believed to have presented detailed assessments of the situation. The government's primary focus remains on ensuring the safe passage of Indian nationals and securing critical energy imports.

Seafarer Safety Takes Centre Stage

The safety of Indian seafarers has emerged as a key concern. The PM's directive for a dedicated mechanism signals a proactive approach to protect Indian citizens working in the maritime sector.

Details of the mechanism have not yet been revealed. The government is expected to coordinate with shipping companies and international agencies to implement the plan.

The meeting underscores the government's swift response to the unfolding crisis. India imports a significant portion of its crude oil and fertilisers from the Middle East, making stability in the region crucial for its economy.

The government has not issued a formal statement on the outcomes of the meeting. However, the urgency of the gathering indicates the seriousness with which New Delhi views the current situation.

The coming days are likely to see further government action as the situation in West Asia evolves.
