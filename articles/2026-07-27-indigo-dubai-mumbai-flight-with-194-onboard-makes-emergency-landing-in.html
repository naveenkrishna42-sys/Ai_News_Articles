IndiGo Dubai-Mumbai Flight With 194 Onboard Makes Emergency Landing in Rajkot After Smoke Detected

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • IndiGo flight from Dubai to Mumbai made emergency landing in Rajkot.
  • Smoke detected in the cargo area prompted the diversion.
  • All 194 passengers and crew are safe; investigation underway.

A Dubai-Mumbai IndiGo flight with 194 people on board made an emergency landing at Rajkot airport on Monday morning after smoke was detected in the cargo hold. The aircraft, which was en route from Dubai International Airport to Mumbai's Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport, was diverted to Rajkot as a precautionary measure.

All passengers and crew are safe. Officials at Rajkot airport confirmed that emergency protocols were activated immediately upon the pilot's request for priority landing.

Smoke Scare Prompts Swift Action

The incident occurred around 10:30 AM IST when the flight crew detected smoke in the cargo area. The pilot declared an emergency and sought permission to land at the nearest suitable airport. Rajkot airport authorities cleared the runway and deployed fire tenders and ambulances as a precaution.

An airline spokesperson stated that the aircraft landed safely without incident. Passengers were evacuated using aerobridges, and no injuries have been reported. The cause of the smoke is not yet known, and an official investigation has been ordered.

Passengers Safe, Investigation Underway

IndiGo officials said that all 194 individuals onboard, including crew members, have been safely disembarked. The airline is arranging alternative transport for passengers to reach their final destination in Mumbai.

Rajkot airport has resumed normal operations. The Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) has been informed, and a team will inspect the aircraft to determine the source of the smoke.

What Happens Next

The aircraft will undergo a thorough safety check before it is cleared to fly again. Passengers are being accommodated in the terminal, and IndiGo is working to minimise delays. Officials have not yet confirmed whether the smoke was caused by an electrical fault or other technical issue.

This incident highlights the importance of strict safety protocols in aviation. The focus now remains on ensuring all passengers reach Mumbai safely and that a detailed probe prevents future occurrences.
