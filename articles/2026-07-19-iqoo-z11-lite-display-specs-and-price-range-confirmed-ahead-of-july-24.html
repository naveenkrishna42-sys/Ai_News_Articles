iQOO Z11 Lite Display Specs and Price Range Confirmed Ahead of July 24 Launch

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iQOO Z11 Lite launch set for July 24 in India.
  • Price confirmed to be under Rs 20,000.
  • Device will feature a 120Hz AMOLED display.
  • 5G connectivity expected across all variants.

Chinese smartphone maker iQOO has confirmed key details about its upcoming iQOO Z11 Lite ahead of its official launch on July 24. The company has revealed the device's display specifications and price segment, giving potential buyers a clearer picture of what to expect from the budget 5G offering.

Display and pricing confirmed

The iQOO Z11 Lite will feature a 120Hz AMOLED display, according to the company. This places it in the increasingly competitive segment of affordable smartphones with high-refresh-rate screens. The AMOLED panel is expected to offer deep blacks and vibrant colours, a feature usually reserved for devices in higher price brackets.

iQOO has also confirmed that the Z11 Lite will be priced under Rs 20,000 in India. This makes it a direct competitor to phones like the Realme Narzo 70 Pro and the Redmi Note 13 series, both of which offer similar display specifications at comparable price points.

5G connectivity and performance

Given the 'Lite' branding and the confirmed price range, the Z11 Lite is expected to be powered by a MediaTek Dimensity or a Qualcomm Snapdragon 4-series chipset with an integrated 5G modem. The company has not yet shared details about the processor, RAM, or storage configurations. Officials have not confirmed whether the device will ship with Android 14 out of the box or the newer Android 15.

Industry watchers expect the phone to support dual 5G SIMs and offer at least 6GB of RAM. Battery capacity and charging speeds remain unannounced, though a 5,000mAh cell with 33W or 44W fast charging is a likely specification for this segment.

Camera and other features

iQOO has not revealed the camera specifications of the Z11 Lite. Given the company's recent trend, a 50MP primary sensor paired with a 2MP depth or macro lens is probable. The front camera is expected to be a 16MP shooter housed in a punch-hole cutout.

The device will likely retain the 3.5mm headphone jack and include a side-mounted fingerprint sensor. IP rating for dust and water resistance has not been confirmed.

What to watch

The iQOO Z11 Lite will launch in India on July 24. The company is expected to reveal full specifications, including chipset, camera details, and battery capacity, at the event. Pricing and availability for the first sale will also be announced. The phone will compete in a crowded price segment where buyers are increasingly demanding high-refresh-rate AMOLED displays and 5G connectivity as standard features.
