FIFA World Cup 2026 stats: Mbappé tops Messi; Olise, Bellingham set new records

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kylian Mbappé scored his 22nd World Cup goal, surpassing Lionel Messi's record.
  • Michael Olise broke Pelé's longstanding record for most goals by a player under 21.
  • Jude Bellingham's 7 goals are the most ever by an England player in a single World Cup.

The FIFA World Cup 2026 has rewritten the record books. Kylian Mbappé, Michael Olise, and Jude Bellingham have etched their names into football history with landmark performances during the tournament.

Mbappé surpasses Messi's all-time goal record

Kylian Mbappé has now scored 22 World Cup goals, moving past Lionel Messi's previous record of 21. The French forward achieved the feat during the ongoing World Cup 2026, cementing his status as the competition's all-time leading scorer.

Messi had held the record after his 2022 campaign in Qatar. Mbappé's relentless pace and finishing power have pushed him to the top of the charts. He scored twice in a group-stage match to tie Messi, then added more in the knockout rounds to claim the record outright.

Mbappé is now the only player in men's World Cup history with more than 21 goals. The milestone marks a shift in global football's hierarchy, with a new generation taking center stage.

Olise breaks Pelé's long-standing record

Michael Olise has surpassed a record set by Pelé in 1958. The French winger scored his 7th goal of the 2026 World Cup, the most ever by a player aged 21 or younger in a single tournament.

Pelé had held the record since the 1958 World Cup in Sweden, where he scored 6 goals as a 17-year-old. Olise, 20, matched that tally in the group stage and then broke it in the round of 16.

Olise's performances have been a revelation. His dribbling and creativity have made him a key figure in France's attack, alongside Mbappé. The record adds to France's deep pool of young talent.

Bellingham's 7 goals set new England mark

Jude Bellingham has scored 7 goals in the 2026 World Cup, the most ever by an England player in a single edition of the tournament. The midfielder surpassed Gary Lineker's 6 goals from 1986 and Harry Kane's 6 from 2018.

Bellingham's goals have come from midfield, a testament to his box-to-box ability. He scored in all three group matches and added crucial strikes in the knockout stages. His all-round play has drawn comparisons to some of England's greatest players.

England have reached the semi-finals, and Bellingham's form has been central to their run. His 7 goals place him among the tournament's top scorers, alongside Mbappé and other elite forwards.

What happens next

The World Cup 2026 final is set for next week. Mbappé and Olise will aim to add to their tallies. Bellingham and England will face a tough semi-final opponent. The final outcome will determine if these records stand as part of a championship-winning campaign.
