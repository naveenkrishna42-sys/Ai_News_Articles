Hands-on use across a school week shows the earbuds holding a stable connection in crowded hallways, with ANC that dulls bus noise enough for focused study. Controllers with hall-effect sticks resist the drift that plagues cheaper units after three to four months of daily play. The 75 percent keyboard keeps a full arrow cluster and function row while still fitting in a backpack sleeve, and keycaps survive typical teen wear.
