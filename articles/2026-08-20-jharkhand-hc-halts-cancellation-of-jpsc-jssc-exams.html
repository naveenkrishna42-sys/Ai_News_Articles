Jharkhand HC halts cancellation of JPSC, JSSC exams

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jharkhand High Court stays state government's decision to cancel JPSC, JSSC exams
  • Protesters call off 26-day agitation after Supreme Court plea for CBI probe
  • Setback for Jharkhand students as exam cancellations and appointments are stayed

Jharkhand High Court Stays Government Order

The Jharkhand High Court has stayed the state government's decision to cancel the Joint Public Service Commission (JPSC) and Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) exams. The order, which was issued on August 19, was stayed by the high court on August 20, 2026.

Protesters Call Off Agitation

Protesters, who had been staging a 26-day agitation over alleged exam irregularities, have called off their protest. The agitation was called off after the Supreme Court of India issued a notice on a plea seeking a CBI probe into the exam irregularities.

Supreme Court Plea for CBI Probe

A plea has been filed in the Supreme Court seeking a CBI probe into the alleged irregularities in the JPSC and JSSC exams. The plea was filed by a group of students and lawyers, who have alleged that the exams were conducted in an irregular manner.

Setback for Jharkhand Students

The stay order by the high court has come as a setback for Jharkhand students, who were awaiting the results of the JPSC and JSSC exams. The exams were scheduled to be held in June 2026, but the government had decided to cancel them due to alleged irregularities.

What Happens Next?

The next hearing in the Supreme Court plea is scheduled for September 3, 2026. The outcome of the hearing will determine the future of the JPSC and JSSC exams, and the appointments of the candidates who had appeared for the exams.
