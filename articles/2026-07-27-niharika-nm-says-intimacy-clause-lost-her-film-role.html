Actress Niharika NM has revealed that an 'intimacy clause' in her contract once cost her a film role. In an exclusive interview with News18, she said the co-actor refused to work with a body double for intimate scenes.

The clause, which she insisted on, became a deal-breaker. Niharika did not name the film or the actor involved.

Why The Clause Was A Deal-Breaker

Niharika explained that she wanted a body double for certain intimate sequences. This was to ensure her comfort and safety on set.

But the lead actor opposed the idea. He reportedly wanted to shoot the scenes directly with her. This led to a deadlock.

“The actor didn’t want a body double,” she said. The production house eventually dropped her from the project.

Intimacy Clauses In Indian Cinema

Intimacy clauses are becoming more common in Indian film contracts. They outline what an actor is comfortable with during intimate scenes. They also specify if a body double will be used.

Many actors, especially women, now demand such clauses. They see them as a tool to prevent exploitation and harassment on set.

Niharika’s case shows that these clauses can still lead to professional setbacks. Not all co-actors or producers are willing to accommodate them.
