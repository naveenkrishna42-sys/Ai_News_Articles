Stocks, Gold Rise as Hyundai Expands; Markets Rebound

📅 2026-08-29 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nifty, Sensex rebound after initial drop
  • Hyundai announces major expansion plans
  • Gold, silver prices rise amid economic optimism
  • Market sentiment improved due to positive corporate news

Stock Market Recovery Amid Economic Optimism

Indian stock markets saw a rebound on Thursday, with the Nifty and Sensex recovering after an initial slide. The Nifty 50 index gained 0.5% to close at 22,450, while the Sensex added 0.3% to reach 74,200. The recovery was driven by positive corporate news and a rise in gold and silver prices.

Hyundai's Expansion Plans Boost Market Sentiment

Hyundai Motor India announced a significant expansion of its manufacturing facilities in India. The company plans to invest Rs 50,000 crore over the next five years to increase production capacity. This expansion is expected to create thousands of jobs and boost the Indian economy.

Gold and Silver Prices Rise

Gold prices rose by 0.8% to Rs 62,000 per 10 grams, while silver prices increased by 1.2% to Rs 75,000 per kilogram. The rise in precious metal prices is attributed to economic optimism and a stronger rupee. Investors are buying gold and silver as a hedge against economic uncertainty.

Market Analysis

Analysts attribute the market recovery to positive corporate news and a rise in gold and silver prices. The expansion plans by Hyundai are expected to have a positive impact on the Indian economy and boost investor confidence. The rise in precious metal prices is a positive sign for the economy and indicates that investors are optimistic about the future.
