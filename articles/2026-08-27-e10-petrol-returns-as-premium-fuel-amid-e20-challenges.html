E10 petrol returns as premium fuel amid E20 challenges

📅 2026-08-27 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • E10 petrol may return as a premium fuel option
  • India's ethanol production and distribution challenges
  • E20 petrol faced controversies and car damage concerns
  • Auto industry and government criticized for lack of public communication

Ethanol's Indian Math Isn't Adding Up

India's ethanol market is in turmoil. Despite government policies promoting ethanol blends, the country's ethanol production and distribution challenges have left many questioning the viability of higher ethanol blends like E20. The government's recent decision to allow E10 petrol to make a comeback, but as a premium fuel, suggests that the higher blends may not be feasible in the near future.

E20's Controversial Journey

The E20 petrol blend was introduced with much fanfare, promising to reduce India's dependence on crude oil imports. However, the blend faced significant backlash from the auto industry and consumers. Reports of cars stalling, reduced fuel efficiency, and increased maintenance costs led to widespread dissatisfaction. The government's decision to discontinue E20 and revert to E10, albeit as a premium fuel, reflects the challenges of scaling up ethanol production and distribution.

Premium E10: A Compromise Solution

The return of E10 as a premium fuel is a compromise solution aimed at addressing the ethanol challenges. Premium pricing can help offset the higher production costs associated with ethanol blends. This move also aligns with the government's broader strategy to promote alternative fuels while managing the associated risks. However, critics argue that this approach may not fully address the root causes of the ethanol distribution issues.

Industry and Government Criticisms

The auto industry and government have faced significant criticism for the lack of public communication regarding the E20 petrol blend. Experts like Anjan Ray, who was part of the original consultation process, have criticized the industry and government for failing to inform the public in time. This lack of transparency has contributed to the widespread dissatisfaction with the E20 blend and highlighted the need for better communication strategies.

Looking Ahead: The Future of Ethanol in India

The future of ethanol in India remains uncertain. While the government has taken steps to address the ethanol challenges, significant hurdles remain. The success of ethanol blends will depend on the ability to scale up production, improve distribution networks, and ensure consumer satisfaction. As India continues to explore alternative fuels, the lessons learned from the E20 experience will be crucial in shaping future policies.
