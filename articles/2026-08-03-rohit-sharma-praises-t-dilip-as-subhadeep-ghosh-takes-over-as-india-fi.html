Rohit Sharma Praises T Dilip as Subhadeep Ghosh Takes Over as India Fielding Coach

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Subhadeep Ghosh replaces T Dilip as India men's fielding coach
  • Rohit Sharma hails T Dilip's 'magic' and 'workhorse' efforts
  • T Dilip ends five-year stint with Indian cricket team

A Changing of the Guard

India's fielding drills will have a new voice at the helm. Subhadeep Ghosh has been appointed as the men's team's fielding coach, replacing T Dilip, whose five-year tenure drew to a close. The announcement came after a period of speculation, with the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) confirming the move without further elaboration on the reasons behind the transition.

Ghosh, a former Bengal cricketer, steps into a role that has grown in prominence within the modern game. His immediate task will be to maintain the high standards set by his predecessor, a man who reshaped how India approaches its work in the inner ring and the deep.

Rohit Sharma's Tribute

Skipper Rohit Sharma did not hold back when reflecting on Dilip's contribution. "You created magic," he said, in a remark that captured the sentiment of a dressing room that has often credited Dilip with transforming ordinary sessions into moments of genuine improvement. Rohit also described Dilip as a "workhorse," a word that speaks to the relentless energy the coach brought to every practice.

Under Dilip's watch, India's catching and ground fielding became a talking point for the right reasons. The team's athleticism in the field often turned tight matches, and players frequently pointed to Dilip's innovative drills as a key factor. Rohit's public acknowledgement underscores how deeply the coach's influence was felt, even as the squad prepares for a new chapter under Ghosh.

The Dilip Era

T.K. Dilip, a Hyderabad-based coach, took charge in 2021 and leaves behind a legacy of meticulous preparation. His methods were not always flashy, but they were effective. From sharpening reflex catches to improving throwing accuracy, his sessions were designed to simulate match pressure.

The BCCI has not yet detailed what Dilip's next move will be, though his reputation ensures he will not be short of offers. For now, his farewell is marked by the respect of the players he served, a rare tribute in a sport where results often overshadow the backroom staff.

Ghosh's New Challenge

Subhadeep Ghosh inherits a team that has set a high bar for itself. The former wicketkeeper-batter has previously worked with domestic sides and is known for a hands-on approach. His first assignment will likely be the upcoming home season, where India will face a packed schedule of Tests and limited-overs matches.

Ghosh will need to blend continuity with his own ideas. The core group of players remains intact, and their familiarity with Dilip's philosophy could ease the transition. Yet, the new coach will also want to leave his own imprint, perhaps by introducing fresh drills or a different tactical emphasis.

Observers will watch closely how the fielding unit responds to the change. India's standards in the field have been a source of pride, and any dip would be magnified. Ghosh's experience, however, suggests he is ready for the scrutiny that comes with the role.

What to Watch

All eyes will be on India's first training camp under Ghosh, likely in the coming weeks. The team management's choice of words—calling the move a "replacement" rather than an overhaul—hints at a seamless shift. For now, the focus is on ensuring that the 'magic' Rohit spoke of does not fade with Dilip's departure.
