Mayank Yadav scripts rare first-ball wicket record in India vs Zimbabwe T20I

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mayank Yadav claimed a wicket on the first ball of his spell on his international return.
  • India bowled out Zimbabwe for 125/7, a low total in the Harare T20I.
  • Yadav was named Player of the Match for his impactful performance.

Mayank Yadav has marked his return to international cricket with a rare achievement. The young pacer took a wicket on the very first ball of his spell during the T20I between India and Zimbabwe in Harare on Thursday.

Yadav's opening delivery removed Zimbabwe's opener for a duck, setting the tone for a dominant Indian bowling display. The hosts were eventually restricted to 125 for 7 in their allotted 20 overs.

A comeback to remember

This was Yadav's first appearance for India after a long injury layoff. The speedster, once touted as the next big thing in Indian pace bowling, had been sidelined for months due to fitness issues.

His return was not just about being fit again. The team management has worked on making him "fitter, faster, smarter" according to sources, and the results were visible on the field. Yadav consistently bowled over 145 kph and troubled the Zimbabwe batters with bounce and movement.

Rare record in T20Is

Taking a wicket on the first ball of a spell is a rare feat in T20I cricket. It requires precision and the ability to handle pressure from the outset. Yadav delivered exactly that, setting up the batter with a short ball followed by a fuller delivery that found the edge.

The dismissal sent the Zimbabwe camp into early trouble, and they never fully recovered. India's bowlers maintained tight lines, not allowing the hosts to build any significant partnerships.

India's next pace generation

Yadav's performance is being seen as part of a larger plan. The Indian team is currently giving opportunities to young fast bowlers in this series against Zimbabwe, aiming to build a strong pace battery for future tournaments.

With senior pacers like Jasprit Bumrah and Mohammed Shami being managed carefully, the team management is keen to test the depth of India's pace reserves. Yadav's impressive showing has strengthened his case for a longer run in the side.

Player of the Match performance

After the match, Yadav said, "It is a very special moment." He credited the support staff and his own hard work during the rehabilitation period for his successful return.

His figures of 1 for 18 in 4 overs were instrumental in keeping Zimbabwe to a below-par total. The Indian batsmen are now expected to chase down the target with ease.

Yadav's comeback has given Indian cricket fans a new reason to be excited about the team's pace future. All eyes will now be on his fitness and consistency in the upcoming matches.

The series continues with the next T20I in Harare, where Yadav will look to build on this strong start.
