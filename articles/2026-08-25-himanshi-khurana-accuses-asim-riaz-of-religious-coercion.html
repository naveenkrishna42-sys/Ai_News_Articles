Himanshi Khurana's Allegations

Himanshi Khurana, a well-known actress, has accused Asim Riaz, her former co-star and Bigg Boss 13 contestant, of pressuring her to convert to Islam. She claims that during their relationship, Asim Riaz subjected her to physical and verbal abuse, and threatened to expose her if she did not comply with his religious demands.

Khurana has stated that she has proof of these allegations and will not shy away from presenting it. She has warned Asim Riaz that she will not let him get away with his actions.

Asim Riaz's Denial

Asim Riaz has denied all the allegations made by Khurana. He has stated that he never asked anyone to change their religion and that the allegations are baseless. He has called Khurana's claims a personal vendetta and has asked her to stop spreading false information.

Riaz has also accused Khurana of making these allegations to tarnish his image and to gain media attention. He has stated that he has a clean record and that he has never been involved in any such incidents.

Broader Impact
