Google Pixel 11 Pro XL appears on Geekbench as hands-on images of Pro leak

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pixel 11 Pro XL spotted on Geekbench with Tensor G5 processor
  • Hands-on images of Pixel 11 Pro leak online
  • Google Pixel 11 series launch expected soon with four models

Pixel 11 Pro XL spotted on Geekbench

Google's upcoming Pixel 11 Pro XL has made an appearance on Geekbench, giving us our first look at its performance credentials. The listing reveals a motherboard codenamed 'komodo' with a Tensor G5 chipset, which is expected to power the entire Pixel 11 lineup.

The benchmark scores show a single-core result of 2,146 and a multi-core score of 6,432. These numbers suggest a significant leap over the previous generation, though they remain competitive rather than class-leading when compared to Qualcomm's latest offerings.

Hands-on images of Pixel 11 Pro surface

Alongside the Geekbench listing, hands-on images of the Pixel 11 Pro have leaked online, offering a closer look at Google's design language for this cycle. The images show a refined camera bar with a polished metal finish, and the device appears slightly thinner than its predecessor.

The display, visible in the leaked shots, shows minimal bezels with a centred punch-hole cutout for the selfie camera. The Pro model is tipped to feature a 6.3-inch OLED panel with a 120Hz refresh rate and peak brightness of 3,000 nits.

What the benchmark reveals

The Geekbench listing also confirms 16GB of RAM on the Pro XL variant, up from 12GB on the Pixel 10 Pro XL. This aligns with Google's push towards on-device AI features, which require more memory for large language models.

The Tensor G5 chip is built on a 3nm process, a first for Google's custom silicon. It brings improved efficiency and faster neural processing, which should enhance features like Magic Editor and real-time translation.

Launch expectations and lineup

Google is expected to announce four devices in the Pixel 11 series: the standard Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. The company has scheduled a launch event for later this month, though the exact date has not been officially confirmed.

The Pixel 11 Pro is rumoured to sport a 50MP primary sensor with a variable aperture, while the Pro XL could add a periscope telephoto lens with 5x optical zoom. Battery capacities are also expected to see a bump, with the Pro XL possibly packing a 5,100mAh cell.

What to watch

With the Geekbench listing and leaked images now in the wild, the remaining mystery is pricing and availability. Google has historically positioned the Pro XL as a premium flagship, and this year's model is likely to carry a starting price of around Rs 1,09,999 in India.

The Pixel 11 series will face stiff competition from Samsung's Galaxy S26 and Apple's iPhone 17 lineup. Google's bet on AI-driven features and cleaner software could help it carve out a niche, but the company will need to deliver on battery life and thermals to win over sceptics.

As the launch date draws closer, more details are expected to emerge. Keep an eye on official channels for the live stream announcement.
