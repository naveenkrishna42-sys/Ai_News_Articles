Kerala: RSS ideologue TG Mohandas sparks row over 'shoot protesters' remark

📅 2026-07-27 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • TG Mohandas made remarks about shooting protesters and made comments about women and rape.
  • The comments were made in the context of the 'Chakka Jam' protests in Delhi.
  • Complaints have been filed by the PDP, SFI, and other groups seeking legal action.

Controversial remarks by RSS ideologue

Thiruvananthapuram: TG Mohandas, a prominent RSS ideologue in Kerala, has landed in hot water after making inflammatory statements about student protesters. He reportedly said he would have shot the participants of the ‘Chakka Jam’ protest in Delhi.

The remarks were made during a public meeting, and have drawn sharp condemnation from political parties and student organisations across the state. The comments come at a time of heightened sensitivity around student activism in the country.

Context of the statement

The ‘Chakka Jam’ protest refers to a recent demonstration by students in the national capital. Mohandas’ comments on the protest have been widely circulated on social media, prompting outrage.

In addition to his comments about shooting protesters, Mohandas also made controversial remarks about women and rape, which have further inflamed the situation. The exact wording of these comments is being examined by authorities.

Political backlash

The People’s Democratic Party (PDP) has filed a formal complaint against Mohandas. The party’s leader stated that such hate speech cannot be tolerated in a democracy.

The Students' Federation of India (SFI) has also sought legal action, calling the statements a clear incitement to violence. The SFI has demanded that the Kerala Police register a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code.

Other groups, including the Congress party’s student wing, have joined in condemning the remarks. They have called for a thorough investigation into the matter.

Legal proceedings

Complaints have been filed with the Kerala Police, who are yet to confirm if a formal case has been registered. Officials have not disclosed the specific sections under which action might be taken.

The Police are reportedly reviewing the video evidence of Mohandas’ speech. A decision on whether to proceed with legal action is expected in the coming days.

Authorities are also examining if the remarks violate the model code of conduct for public speakers. The state government has not issued an official statement so far.

Wider implications

The incident has reignited the debate on freedom of speech and hate speech in India. Critics argue that such statements by public figures embolden extremist elements.

Supporters of Mohandas have defended his right to express his views, but this has been met with strong opposition from civil society groups. The controversy is likely to continue as more details emerge.

All eyes are now on the Kerala Police and the state government to see how they handle the complaint. The outcome could set a precedent for how hate speech cases are dealt with in the state.
