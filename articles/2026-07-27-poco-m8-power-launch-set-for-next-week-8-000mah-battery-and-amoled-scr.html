Poco M8 Power Launch Set for Next Week: 8,000mAh Battery and AMOLED Screen Confirmed

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Poco M8 Power 5G launching in India on August 4
  • Device packs an 8,000mAh battery, largest in Poco lineup
  • AMOLED screen confirmed, positioning as mid-range gaming phone

Poco is set to launch its next smartphone, the Poco M8 Power 5G, in India on August 4. The company has confirmed the date through official channels. The phone has been the subject of leaks and rumours for weeks.

The biggest talking point is the battery. The M8 Power will pack an 8,000mAh cell. That is a significant jump from the 5,000mAh or 6,000mAh batteries seen in most current mid-range phones. It also dwarfs the battery in Poco's own X8 Pro.

Battery and Display Details

An 8,000mAh battery is rare in smartphones. It suggests the M8 Power is built for heavy users — gamers, streamers, or people who want multiple days of use on a single charge. The trade-off could be added weight and thickness. Poco has not yet confirmed the charging speed. Fast charging support will be key to making such a large battery practical.

The phone will also feature an AMOLED screen. This is a step up from the LCD panels commonly used in budget and mid-range Poco devices. An AMOLED panel means deeper blacks, better contrast, and more vibrant colours. It is a strong fit for gaming and video consumption.

Positioning in the Market

The Poco M8 Power 5G is expected to sit in the mid-range segment, competing with devices from Realme, Xiaomi, and Samsung. Reports from GSMArena and other outlets suggest it will be a gaming-focused device. The large battery and AMOLED screen support that positioning.

Poco has not yet revealed the processor or RAM configuration. But given the 'Power' branding, a chipset that balances performance and efficiency is likely. The phone will support 5G connectivity, as the name indicates.

What to Watch For

The official launch on August 4 will reveal the full specifications, pricing, and availability. Key details to watch include the charging speed, the exact processor, and the starting price in India. The 8,000mAh battery is a bold move. If Poco gets the pricing right, the M8 Power could carve out a niche for itself in the crowded mid-range market.
