Sensex, Nifty Set to Open Higher as Crude Oil Prices Slide

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Markets likely to open higher after five consecutive sessions of decline.
  • Crude oil prices fell sharply over the weekend, providing relief to investors.
  • Auto and metal stocks had dragged the benchmarks lower in the previous week.

Indian equity benchmarks are expected to open higher on Monday, snapping a five-day losing streak, as a sharp decline in global crude oil prices boosted investor sentiment. The BSE Sensex and NSE Nifty 50 are likely to see gains at the opening bell, with analysts pointing to lower oil prices as a key catalyst that could ease inflation concerns and support corporate margins.

On Friday, the Sensex had fallen 332 points to close below the 23,770 mark, while the Nifty ended under 23,800. The markets had been under pressure for five consecutive sessions, dragged down by auto and metal stocks amid worries over rising input costs and global demand slowdown.

Crude Oil Slide Lifts Sentiment

Brent crude futures fell more than 2% over the weekend, trading near $78 per barrel, as concerns over weakening demand in major economies and a potential increase in supply from OPEC+ weighed on prices. The drop is seen as a positive development for India, which imports over 80% of its oil needs.

Lower crude prices reduce India's import bill and help contain inflation, which in turn supports the case for the Reserve Bank of India to maintain a dovish stance on interest rates. This is particularly important for sectors such as aviation, paints, and FMCG, which are sensitive to raw material costs.

What Dragged Markets Last Week

The previous week saw sustained selling pressure, with the Sensex dropping over 500 points in a single session on Thursday. Analysts attributed the decline to a combination of factors, including weak global cues, a stronger US dollar, and profit-booking after recent gains. Auto and metal stocks were among the worst hit, with companies reporting mixed quarterly earnings.

Investors also remained cautious ahead of the US Federal Reserve's policy meeting, with speculation that interest rates could remain higher for longer. This had triggered outflows from emerging markets, including India.

What to Watch Today

Market participants will closely track the movement of crude oil prices and the rupee against the dollar. The rupee had weakened to near 83.5 against the US dollar last week, adding to concerns about imported inflation. Any further fall in oil prices could help stabilise the currency.

Key stocks to watch include those in the oil marketing and aviation sectors, which stand to benefit from lower fuel costs. Shares of companies like Reliance Industries, Indian Oil, and InterGlobe Aviation may see positive movement.

Traders also await fresh foreign portfolio investment (FPI) data, as sustained selling by foreign investors has been a drag on the market. On the earnings front, a handful of companies are set to report quarterly results later in the day.

Global cues remain mixed, with Asian markets trading cautiously as investors weigh the impact of lower oil prices against persistent inflation concerns. The US dollar index held steady near 104, while gold prices edged higher.

All eyes will be on the opening bell to see if the positive trigger from oil prices is enough to reverse the recent downtrend and bring buying interest back to Dalal Street.
