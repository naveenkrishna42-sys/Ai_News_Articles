Cultural Diplomacy at the BRICS Gala

The Brics Gala dinner in New Delhi became more than a networking event—it transformed into a living tribute to South Asian artistry. Prime Minister Narendra Modi took the stage to perform Kishore Kumar's timeless ballad "Khwaab Ho Tum Ya Koi Haqeeqat," delivering a heartfelt rendition that resonated with diplomatic attendees from across the bloc. The choice was deliberate: blending Indian classical appeal with Pan-Asian traditions, the performance symbolized the cultural cohesion underpinning BRICS economic and political alignment.

Strategic Implications of Multilateral Engagement

The Musical Cameo

This unconventional yet culturally resonant moment underscores how soft power can complement hard diplomatic work. By inviting a legendary Playback singer, the host government signals respect for regional arts while creating a shareable narrative that transcends typical policy discussions. The song's themes of dreams and reality mirror the delicate balancing act of great powers navigating mutual interests within the BRICS framework.

Significance for Indo-Pacific Outreach

Such gestures have long been instrumental in building trust among BRICS members who span diverse cultural landscapes—from Southeast Asia to Africa. When modality crosses over with music rather than mere summits, it humanizes leadership interactions and fosters goodwill essential for sustained collaboration on development projects.

Bilateral Dialogues Across BRICS Nations

Multifront Engagement

Beyond the gala, PM Modi continued his agenda of bilateral talks spanning multiple geopolitical corridors. Leaders from the United Arab Emirates, Malaysia, Vietnam, and Ethiopia converged around him, discussing everything from energy security to infrastructure investment. These simultaneous encounters demonstrate the breadth of interest within the BRICS community and position the bloc as a truly global platform.

Africa Integration Track

Engagement with Ethiopia's president Abiy Ahmed has gained particular traction recently, reflecting growing appetite within BRICS for deeper South-South cooperation. The two leaders outlined joint initiatives in agriculture, trade facilitation, and green technology, signaling a potential shift toward more balanced intra-bloc dynamics.

Defense Cooperation and Security Initiatives

Malaysia Partnership

The ongoing defence procurement dialogue with Malaysia represents another frontier of cooperation. Recent negotiations focus on advanced weapon systems, training exchanges, and intelligence sharing protocols. For both countries, strengthening military ties aligns with broader objectives of regional stability and capacity building.

Vietnam 2+2 Dialogue Framework

Plans for a 2+2 dialogue mechanism with Vietnam aim to institutionalize strategic conversations beyond ad hoc summits. Such frameworks allow regular tracking of emerging issues, including cybersecurity, infrastructure development along the Belt and Road, and cooperative counter-terrorism measures.

Maritime Collaboration in South Asia

Telangana’s Perspective on Regional Connectivity

In parallel, the state of Telangana has championed maritime connectivity efforts, emphasizing its role as a bridge between Indian shores and neighboring seas. This local-level advocacy complements national-level dialogues, ensuring that infrastructural investments resonate with ground-level stakeholders and communities.

Pros and Cons of the BRICS Gala Approach

  • Pros: Cultural moments create memorable media narratives that amplify soft-power outreach; simultaneous multi-lateral talks showcase the bloc's inclusivity; regional ambassadors gain visibility that translates into policy momentum.
  • Cons: Risk of oversimplifying complex defense and security issues through cultural theatrics; logistical sensitivity when artists are invited from outside the immediate geographic area; potential for diluted focus during packed agendas.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • PM Modi performed a classic Bollywood song live at the BRICS gala, drawing praise for bridging generational and cultural divides.
  • Formal bilateral consultations with four major BRICS nations occurred concurrently, demonstrating deep multilateral commitment.
  • Defence procurement dialogues with Malaysia signaled strategic alignment on modernising regional armed forces.
  • The Ethiopia-Mali dialogue underscored China's push for South-South partnerships but also raised questions about equity within the group.
