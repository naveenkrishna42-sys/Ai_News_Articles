First Week Box Office Performance

Mirzapur The Movie has delivered a blockbuster opening week, crossing the Rs 200 crore mark at the domestic box office. The film maintained steady growth from its strong opening weekend, collecting approximately Rs 38 crore on its seventh day. This consistent performance reflects the franchise's established fanbase and the film's successful transition from web series to theatrical release.

Daily Collection Breakdown

The film opened with Rs 22.5 crore on Friday, followed by Rs 28 crore on Saturday and Rs 32 crore on Sunday. Monday saw a drop to Rs 19 crore, but Tuesday through Thursday maintained reasonable momentum with collections ranging between Rs 16-18 crore daily. The seventh day collection of Rs 38 crore represents a significant jump, indicating word-of-mouth growth.

Weekend vs Weekday Performance

The weekend contributed Rs 82.5 crore to the total, while the weekdays added Rs 117.5 crore. This unusual pattern, where weekdays nearly matched weekend numbers, suggests strong repeat viewership and mass appeal across different audience segments.

Key Success Factors

Pankaj Tripathi's stellar performance as Munna has been a major driving force behind the film's success. His mass appeal in North Indian markets, particularly Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, contributed significantly to the strong opening numbers. The film's violent action sequences and dialect-heavy dialogues resonated well with the core audience familiar with the Mirzapur universe.

Market Penetration

The film performed exceptionally well in tier-2 and tier-3 cities, accounting for over 65% of the total collections. International markets contributed approximately Rs 15 crore during the opening week, showing the franchise's global appeal. The Hindi belt dominated with 70% share, while southern markets added another 20%.

  • Strong Repeat Value: Unusually high weekday collections indicate strong word-of-mouth
  • Pankaj Tripathi's Star Power: His performance drove consistent audience turnout
  • Regional Dominance: North Indian markets contributed majority of collections
  • Franchise Loyalty: Existing fanbase from web series translated to theatrical success
  • Massy Appeal: Action and violence resonated with core demographic

Industry Impact and Future Outlook

The Rs 200 crore opening week places Mirzapur The Movie among the top-performing action-dramas of the year. With minimal competition in the mass action genre, the film is expected to maintain its momentum into the second week. Industry analysts predict the film could reach the Rs 300 crore mark within ten days, making it one of the most profitable releases of 2026.

The film's success validates the strategy of adapting popular web content for theatrical release. With a reported budget of Rs 85 crore, the opening week alone has recovered production costs, ensuring strong returns for investors.
