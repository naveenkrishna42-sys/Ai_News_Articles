ECR's Dominant Display Sets the Tone for ETPL 2026

Edinburgh Castle Rockers (ECR) made a statement in the European T20 Premier League (ETPL) 2026 season with a comprehensive 7-wicket victory over Glasgow Lions (GLC) in the tournament opener at The Hague on August 27, 2026. The result not only secured ECR's first win in the competition but also set the benchmark for the season's competitive balance.

GLC's Batting Struggles Raise Concerns

GLC's innings was a masterclass in how not to start a T20 campaign. They were bowled out for a paltry 102 runs in 15.2 overs, with ECR's bowlers dismantling their middle order. The innings featured a stunning 5-wicket haul by a Mumbai Indians pacer, who became the first player from the IPL to achieve this feat in the ETPL.
