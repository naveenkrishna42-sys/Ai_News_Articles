Release date confirmed for India

Bollywood heavyweights Salman Khan and Sanjay Dutt are set to share the screen once again, this time in the Saudi action thriller '7 Dogs'. The film, which has already generated buzz internationally, will now release in Indian theatres on August 21. The announcement was confirmed by multiple entertainment outlets on Monday, putting an end to speculation about when Indian audiences can catch the movie.

The film also features Italian actress Monica Bellucci, adding an international flavour to the project. While the exact nature of Khan and Dutt's roles has been a topic of discussion, reports suggest they appear in cameo capacities, lending their star power to the Saudi production.

An international collaboration

'7 Dogs' marks a notable crossover for both actors, who are known for their dominance in the Hindi film industry. The movie is being positioned as an action-packed thriller, with production values tailored for a global audience. The inclusion of Bellucci, a veteran of European cinema, further underscores the film's international appeal.

For Salman Khan, who has a massive fan following in the Gulf region, this film could strengthen his connect with that demographic.

While the plot details remain under wraps, the title '7 Dogs' suggests a narrative centred around a pack of canines, possibly with a rescue or heist angle.
