Apple's next-generation iPhone lineup, including the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Ultra, could be unveiled as early as September 2026, according to leaked reports. Reliable sources suggest that Apple's highly anticipated event will take place in September, with the new iPhones set to hit shelves shortly thereafter.
