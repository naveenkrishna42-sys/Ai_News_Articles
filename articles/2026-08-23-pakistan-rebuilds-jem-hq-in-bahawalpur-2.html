JeM's Bahawalpur HQ Rebuilt After 15 Months

Pakistan has reportedly completed the rebuilding of Jaish-e-Mohammed's headquarters in Bahawalpur, a facility destroyed by India in 2025 during Operation Sindoor. The reconstruction reportedly took 15 months to complete, according to reports from multiple news outlets.

Operation Sindoor Aftermath

Operation Sindoor, launched by India in December 2025, targeted several militant facilities across Pakistan. The Bahawalpur headquarters, a key operational base for JeM, was among the sites hit. The strike reportedly killed several militants and disrupted JeM's activities in the region.

India's Response

India has not commented on the rebuilding of the JeM headquarters. The Indian government has previously stated that it will continue to target militant facilities in Pakistan. The rebuilding of the JeM HQ could be seen as a setback for India's counter-terrorism efforts.

JeM's Activities

JeM is a Pakistan-based militant organization that has been involved in a number of attacks in India. The organization has been designated as a terrorist group by both India and the United States. The rebuilding of the JeM HQ could signal a resurgence in the group's activities.

Pakistan has not commented on the reports of the JeM HQ being rebuilt. The Pakistani government has previously denied providing sanctuary to militant groups. However, the rebuilding of the JeM HQ could be seen as a sign of the challenges faced by India in its counter-terrorism efforts.
