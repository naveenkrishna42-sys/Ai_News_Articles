Raja Chaudhary Alleges Cezanne Khan Called Him Shweta Tiwari's 'Unpaid Boy'

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Raja Chaudhary alleges Cezanne Khan called him Shweta Tiwari's 'unpaid boy' when confronted.
  • Chaudhary claims he was treated as 'unpaid househelp' during his marriage to the actress.
  • He revealed he has blocked his daughter, Palak Tiwari, on social media, saying she doesn't consider him a father.

Raja Chaudhary has made fresh allegations against his former wife, actor Shweta Tiwari, and her alleged relationship with her Kasautii Zindagii Kay co-star Cezanne Khan. In a new interview, Chaudhary claimed that when he confronted Khan about the affair, the actor insulted him, calling him Shweta Tiwari's 'unpaid boy'.

The remarks have added another layer to the long-standing public feud between the former couple. Chaudhary and Tiwari have been locked in a bitter legal and personal battle for years, with custody of their daughter Palak Tiwari also a point of contention.

The Confrontation with Cezanne Khan

Chaudhary stated that he directly approached Cezanne Khan to seek clarification about his relationship with Shweta Tiwari. Instead of an explanation, he claims Khan responded with a taunt. "He said I was Shweta's unpaid boy," Chaudhary alleged, suggesting that Khan dismissed him as someone who was not valued in the marriage.

The actor did not stop there. Chaudhary went on to describe how he felt he was treated like an 'unpaid househelp' during his time with Tiwari. These statements are the latest in a series of accusations he has made against the television star, painting a picture of a marriage where he felt demeaned and exploited.

Palak Tiwari and Ibrahim Ali Khan Rumours

The conversation also turned to his daughter, Palak Tiwari, who is now an actor in her own right. Chaudhary was asked about the persistent dating rumours linking Palak to Ibrahim Ali Khan, son of actors Saif Ali Khan and Amrita Singh.

He said he was not bothered by the speculation. "She doesn't consider me a father," he stated bluntly, revealing the deep rift between them. Chaudhary went on to disclose that he has taken the drastic step of blocking his daughter on social media, a move that underscores the estrangement. He offered no details on whether he had spoken to Palak about the rumours directly.

A Pattern of Public Allegations

Raja Chaudhary has not shied away from making his grievances public. He has previously alleged that his marriage to Shweta Tiwari was fraught with emotional and financial hardship. The latest claims about being called an 'unpaid boy' and treated as 'unpaid househelp' fit into that broader narrative.

Shweta Tiwari has not yet publicly responded to these specific allegations from her ex-husband. The actress has largely maintained her silence on the matter in recent years, focusing on her career and her daughter's burgeoning acting career.

The back-and-forth between the former spouses continues to play out in the media, with each new statement stirring public interest. The personal dynamics, especially involving their daughter, remain a painful and complicated chapter for the family.

What comes next is unclear. Neither Shweta Tiwari nor Cezanne Khan have issued a formal statement. The ball, for now, remains in their court as observers wait to see if they will respond to Chaudhary's latest round of accusations.
