Google Confirms Pixel 11 Price Hike as RAM Costs Surge Sixfold

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Google has confirmed that Pixel 11 prices will increase across the board.
  • The price hike is attributed to RAM costs surging sixfold compared to previous generations.
  • Google is undertaking a dedicated effort to make Android more RAM-efficient to offset hardware costs.

Google has confirmed that its upcoming Pixel 11 lineup will carry higher price tags. The company attributes the increase directly to a sixfold surge in the cost of RAM components.

The revelation came during a recent briefing where executives detailed the economic pressures shaping the next generation of Pixel phones. The confirmation follows weeks of speculation in tech circles about rising component prices.

RAM Costs Drive Up Prices

According to Google, the cost of RAM modules has skyrocketed six times compared to what it paid for memory in earlier Pixel models. This single component is now one of the most expensive parts in the entire device.

Industry analysts have noted that global demand for memory chips, coupled with supply chain constraints, has pushed prices to historic highs. Google appears to be the first major smartphone maker to publicly break down the impact on its retail pricing.

"The reality is that memory costs have gone up dramatically, and that has to be reflected in the price," a Google representative stated. The company did not provide exact figures for the expected price increase but confirmed it would apply to all models in the Pixel 11 range.

Android Gets a Memory Diet

In a parallel effort to manage costs, Google says it is reworking Android to use less memory. The initiative is described as a "dedicated effort" to make the operating system more RAM-efficient.

This move aims to allow future Pixel phones to deliver a smooth user experience even with lower amounts of RAM. It could also help keep base model prices from rising too steeply.

Google has not shared technical details about the changes. However, the company indicated that the optimisations would involve both the Android kernel and the application layer. The goal is to reduce the baseline memory footprint of the OS without sacrificing performance or features.

Consumer Impact and Market Reaction

For consumers, the announcement means that the Pixel 11 will likely be the most expensive Pixel series yet. Enthusiasts who have come to expect competitive pricing from Google may face a tough choice.

Tech commentators have pointed out that Apple and Samsung are also dealing with rising component costs, but neither has confirmed similar price hikes for their next flagships. Google's transparency on the issue is unusual but may help manage customer expectations.

The company has not yet announced a launch date for the Pixel 11. Based on previous release cycles, the phones are expected to debut in the latter half of 2026.

As Google works to trim Android's memory appetite, the industry will be watching closely. If successful, the software optimisations could eventually benefit all Android phones, not just Pixels. For now, buyers should prepare for a steeper bill.
