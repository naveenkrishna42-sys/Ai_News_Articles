Lawrence Bishnoi Gang Threatens Aamir Khan Over 'Love Jihad' Allegations

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Aamir Khan received a death threat allegedly from the Lawrence Bishnoi gang.
  • The threat accused the actor of promoting 'love jihad'.
  • Police have launched an investigation and are verifying the claim.

Bollywood actor Aamir Khan has received a death threat allegedly from the Lawrence Bishnoi gang, prompting a police investigation. The gang, known for its influence in the criminal underworld, accused the actor of promoting 'love jihad'. The threat message, which warned that the gang 'won't tolerate this man', has sent shockwaves through the entertainment industry.

Threat Details and Allegations

According to reports, the threat was communicated through a message that explicitly targeted Khan. The gang alleged that the actor was involved in activities that promote 'love jihad', a term used by right-wing groups to describe what they claim is a conspiracy by Muslim men to convert Hindu women through marriage. The message reportedly included a warning to 'choke the breath' of the actor.

The threat comes at a time when Khan has been in the public eye for his recent projects and personal life. The actor, who has often been a target of online trolling and protests by right-wing groups, has not yet commented on the threat. His security has been heightened following the incident.

Police Investigation Underway

Police have launched a probe into the threat, with officials confirming that they are verifying the authenticity of the claim. A senior police officer, speaking on condition of anonymity, said that the message is being examined for its origin and credibility. "We are taking this matter seriously. A team has been formed to investigate the threat and ensure the actor's safety," the officer said.

Authorities have not yet confirmed whether the threat is directly linked to Lawrence Bishnoi, who is currently in jail. The gang, which has been involved in several high-profile cases, including the murder of Punjabi singer Sidhu Moose Wala, has a history of issuing threats against celebrities and public figures.

Previous Threats and Security Concerns

This is not the first time a Bollywood celebrity has faced a threat from criminal elements. In recent years, several actors and filmmakers have received threats over their work or personal beliefs. Security has become a major concern for the film industry, with many stars opting for private security and police protection.

Khan, who has been a prominent figure in Indian cinema for decades, has often been at the center of controversy. His 2015 film 'PK' sparked protests from religious groups, and his comments on intolerance in India led to a backlash. The latest threat adds to the growing list of challenges faced by the actor.

What Happens Next

The police are expected to provide more details as the investigation progresses. The actor's security detail has been increased, and authorities are working to track down the source of the threat. For now, the industry and fans await further updates, hoping for a swift resolution to the matter.
