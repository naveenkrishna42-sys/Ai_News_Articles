Gift Nifty signals weak start; eight stocks to watch on July 23

Indian equity markets are expected to open on a muted note on Thursday, July 23, as Gift Nifty pointed to a negative start. The index, which trades on the NSE International Exchange, was seen hovering around levels that suggest selling pressure at the opening bell.

Gift Nifty hints at cautious opening

Gift Nifty, formerly known as SGX Nifty, is often used as a proxy for Indian market sentiment before domestic trading begins. On Wednesday evening, the contract traded lower, indicating that the Nifty 50 could open with a gap down.

Global cues remained mixed overnight. While US markets closed with marginal gains, Asian peers showed a subdued trend in early hours on Thursday. Investors are also watching the US Federal Reserve's stance on interest rates and the movement of crude oil prices.

Eight stocks to watch for day trading

Market analysts have shortlisted eight stocks that could see significant movement during Thursday's session. These picks are based on technical patterns, volume analysis, and recent price action.

  • Reliance Industries: The stock has formed a bullish flag pattern on the daily chart. A breakout above its resistance level may trigger fresh buying.
  • Infosys: IT stocks have been under pressure, but Infosys showed a bounce from its support zone. Traders are watching for a sustained move above its 50-day moving average.
  • HDFC Bank: The banking heavyweight has been consolidating in a narrow range. A close above its immediate resistance could lead to short-term gains.
  • Tata Motors: Auto stocks are in focus after positive sales data. Tata Motors has broken out of a descending trendline on the hourly chart.
  • ICICI Bank: The stock is showing a rounding bottom formation. Volume confirmation is awaited for a buy signal.
  • SBI: State Bank of India has been a laggard in the banking pack. However, it is approaching a key support level that has held multiple times in the past.
  • Bajaj Finance: NBFC stocks have seen some traction. Bajaj Finance is trading above its short-term moving averages, indicating strength.
  • ITC: The FMCG major has been in a steady uptrend. Traders are looking for a pullback to its support level for an entry point.

What traders should watch

The Nifty's immediate support is placed at the 19,800 level, while resistance is seen around 20,100. A break on either side could set the tone for the rest of the session. The Bank Nifty is also likely to remain volatile, with support at 45,500 and resistance at 46,200.

Volumes in the derivatives segment have been moderate, suggesting that institutional activity may dictate the market's direction. Foreign portfolio investors have been net sellers in recent sessions, while domestic institutional investors have provided support.

On the macro front, the rupee's movement against the US dollar and the 10-year bond yield will be closely tracked. Any sharp depreciation in the rupee could weigh on market sentiment.

Traders are advised to keep stop-losses tight and avoid leveraged positions until clearer trends emerge. The day's action will likely hinge on how global indices perform during Indian hours.

Thursday's session will set the stage for the weekly expiry of derivatives contracts. The market's ability to hold above key support levels will determine if the current corrective phase deepens or reverses.
