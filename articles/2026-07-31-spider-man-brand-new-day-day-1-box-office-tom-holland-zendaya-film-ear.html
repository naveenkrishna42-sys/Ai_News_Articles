Spider-Man: Brand New Day Day 1 Box Office: Tom Holland, Zendaya Film Earns Rs 70 Crore

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Day 1 India box office collection estimated at Rs 70 crore
  • Tom Holland and Zendaya return in lead roles
  • Opening figures among the highest for a Hollywood release in India

A Blockbuster Start for the Web-Slinger

Marvel's latest Spider-Man outing, Spider-Man: Brand New Day, has swung to a thunderous start at the Indian box office. The film, starring Tom Holland and Zendaya, collected an estimated Rs 70 crore on its opening day, according to early trade figures.

The number puts the movie firmly in record-breaking territory for a Hollywood release in India. Exhibitors and distributors are calling it one of the strongest Day 1 performances ever for an English-language film in the country.

Advance Bookings and Fan Frenzy

Trade analysts attribute the massive opening to unprecedented advance bookings. Multiplex chains reported sold-out shows in major metros like Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad well before the first screening began.

Fan events and midnight previews added to the momentum. Many theatres ran special marathon screenings of the previous Spider-Man films to build anticipation, and the strategy appears to have paid off handsomely.

The film's release comes after a lengthy marketing campaign that leaned heavily on the return of Holland and Zendaya as Peter Parker and MJ. Their on-screen chemistry has been a major draw for younger audiences.

Bigger Than the Multiverse

Day 1 collections of Rs 70 crore place Brand New Day ahead of several recent Marvel releases in India. The film has outperformed opening-day numbers of some Avengers titles in certain circuits, though exact comparisons will depend on final figures.

Single-screen theatres in smaller cities, historically a weak spot for Hollywood films, also reported healthy occupancy. This suggests the Spider-Man brand has penetrated beyond traditional urban centres.

Industry insiders note that the film's Hindi-dubbed version is performing particularly well, accounting for nearly 40 per cent of the total box office. This mirrors a growing trend where dubbed Hollywood films find significant traction in the domestic market.

What the Numbers Mean

A Rs 70 crore opening day is a strong indicator of the film's overall commercial viability in India. If the trend holds over the weekend, Brand New Day could challenge the lifetime collections of some of the biggest superhero films ever released here.

However, trade experts caution that weekday drops will be crucial. The film faces competition from a couple of mid-sized Bollywood releases in the coming weeks, which could impact its second-weekend performance.

For now, Marvel Studios and Sony Pictures have reason to celebrate. The film's performance in India will also boost its global box office narrative, as international markets have become increasingly significant for Hollywood blockbusters.

The opening weekend will be the real test. All eyes are now on Saturday and Sunday numbers to see if Spider-Man: Brand New Day can maintain its spectacular pace and set a new benchmark for Hollywood in India.
