Bitcoin's Uptrend Revives MicroStrategy's Appetite

Bitcoin, the world's largest cryptocurrency, has been on a tear recently, crossing the $25,000 mark for the first time since June 2022. This resurgence has caught the attention of institutional investors, with Michael Saylor's MicroStrategy leading the charge by purchasing Bitcoin for the first time in two months.

Saylor's Bold Bitcoin Strategy

MicroStrategy, a NASDAQ-listed business intelligence firm, has been one of the most prominent champions of Bitcoin. The company began investing in Bitcoin in August 2020, becoming the first publicly traded company to hold Bitcoin as a reserve asset on its balance sheet. Since then, MicroStrategy has purchased over 130,000 bitcoins, worth over $3.5 crore at current prices.
