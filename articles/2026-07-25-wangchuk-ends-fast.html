Wangchuk Ends Fast

📅 2026-07-25 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sonam Wangchuk ends fast after breakthrough
  • Denies cutting deal with government
  • PM Modi thanks people for suggestions on video

Protest Updates

Sonam Wangchuk, a key figure in the Jantar Mantar protest, has ended his fast after a breakthrough was reportedly reached. Wangchuk has denied cutting any deal with the government, addressing concerns that his decision to end the protest was a result of a negotiated settlement.

Wangchuk's decision to end the fast has been seen as a significant development in the ongoing protest, which has been gaining attention nationwide. The protest has been focused on various issues, with students and other groups participating in large numbers.

Government Response

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has thanked people for their 'insightful' suggestions on a video posted on Instagram. The move is seen as an attempt to engage with the public and address their concerns. However, officials have not yet confirmed whether the suggestions will lead to any concrete policy changes.

Protest Aftermath

The Jantar Mantar protest has been marked by widespread participation, with 18 metro stations closed at one point due to the large crowds. The protest has also drawn attention to various social issues, with many participants expressing their frustration with the current state of affairs.

Reactions

Wangchuk has slammed those alleging that he is not sincere in his demands, questioning the need to constantly prove his commitment to the cause. The Ladakh leader has also spoken about being woken up to witness Wangchuk end his fast, highlighting the significance of the moment.

The protest has been marked by a 'character certificate' jibe at critics by Wangchuk, who has been at the forefront of the movement. As the situation continues to unfold, it remains to be seen how the government will respond to the demands of the protesters.
