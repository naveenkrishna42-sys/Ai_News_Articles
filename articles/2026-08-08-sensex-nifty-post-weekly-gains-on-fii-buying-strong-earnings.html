Sensex, Nifty Post Weekly Gains on FII Buying, Strong Earnings

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty extended weekly gains, supported by FII inflows.
  • Strong corporate earnings season lifted market sentiment.
  • Investors eye global cues and domestic data for further direction.

Indian equity benchmarks rounded off the week on a firm footing, with the Sensex and Nifty extending their gains as sustained buying by foreign institutional investors (FIIs) and a robust earnings season underpinned sentiment. The market’s upward march reflected growing confidence in the domestic growth story, even as global cues remained mixed.

FII inflows drive momentum

Foreign institutional investors have been net buyers in recent sessions, providing a steady tailwind to the market. Their renewed interest in Indian equities comes amid expectations of stable interest rates and improving macroeconomic fundamentals. The sustained inflows have helped offset some of the volatility seen in global markets, keeping domestic indices on a positive trajectory.

Market participants noted that the buying spree was broad-based, with financials, IT, and consumer goods witnessing notable demand. The resilience of the Indian market, despite geopolitical tensions and concerns over global growth, has made it an attractive destination for overseas funds.

Earnings season boosts confidence

The ongoing corporate earnings season has been a key driver of sentiment, with several companies reporting better-than-expected results. Strong revenue growth and margin expansion across sectors have reassured investors about the health of corporate India. Analysts believe that the earnings upgrade cycle is likely to continue, supporting valuations at current levels.

While some sectors, such as autos and fast-moving consumer goods, have faced margin pressures due to input costs, the overall earnings trajectory remains encouraging. The market’s ability to absorb these challenges reflects the underlying strength of the economy.

Global cues and domestic data in focus

Investors are now turning their attention to global cues, including the US Federal Reserve’s policy stance and movements in crude oil prices. Any change in the interest rate outlook could influence FII flows, making the market sensitive to overseas developments. Domestically, inflation data and industrial production numbers due in the coming weeks will provide further clarity on the economic trajectory.

Traders also remain watchful of the monsoon progress, as a good rainfall season is critical for rural demand and agricultural output. A favourable monsoon could provide an additional boost to consumption-driven sectors, further supporting the market’s upward bias.

Technical outlook

From a technical perspective, the Nifty has been holding above key support levels, with analysts eyeing higher targets in the near term. The index’s ability to sustain above recent highs will be crucial for extending the rally. On the downside, any sharp correction could be viewed as a buying opportunity, given the strong underlying fundamentals.

The broader market has also participated in the rally, with midcap and smallcap stocks outperforming their larger peers. This broad-based participation is often seen as a sign of a healthy bull market, though it also warrants caution against excessive speculation.

What to watch

Looking ahead, the market’s direction will hinge on the pace of FII flows, the progress of the earnings season, and global macro developments. Any unexpected policy shift by major central banks or a spike in oil prices could trigger volatility. For now, the prevailing sentiment remains optimistic, but investors would do well to keep a close watch on the evolving data points.

The coming week will see a fresh set of earnings reports and key economic data releases, which could set the tone for the market in the near term. With valuations not cheap, the focus will be on earnings delivery to justify current prices.
