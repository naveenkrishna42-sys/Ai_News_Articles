Real Madrid eye Martin Zubimendi as Arsenal face summer exit battle

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Real Madrid are reportedly interested in signing Arsenal's Martin Zubimendi in a surprise move.
  • Chelsea are also being pushed to compete for Zubimendi, with Xabi Alonso reportedly involved.
  • Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta is said to have a plan to keep the midfielder amid transfer speculation.

Arsenal midfielder Martin Zubimendi has emerged as a surprise transfer target for Real Madrid, according to reports circulating on Tuesday. The Spanish giants are said to be weighing a move for the 27-year-old, who only joined the Gunners last summer from Real Sociedad.

The speculation has intensified following a series of reports from outlets including EPL Index and Football London, which suggest Madrid are monitoring Zubimendi's situation closely. The player's performances in north London have not gone unnoticed, and his profile fits the kind of midfield anchor that Real Madrid have long sought.

Real Madrid's interest and Arsenal's stance

Real Madrid's interest is described as genuine, though no formal bid has been made public. The Spanish club are known to be planning for the future, and Zubimendi's ability to dictate play from deep has made him an attractive option for their midfield rebuild.

Arsenal, however, are not believed to be willing sellers. The club invested significantly in Zubimendi, and manager Mikel Arteta is said to have a clear plan for the Spaniard in his squad. According to reports, Arteta views Zubimendi as a key component of his midfield setup, and the club would resist any approach unless the player himself pushes for a move.

Chelsea enter the race with Alonso's push

The plot has thickened with claims that Chelsea are also in the frame. Goal.com reports that Xabi Alonso, who has been linked with managerial roles across Europe, is pushing Chelsea to snatch Zubimendi from Arsenal. Alonso, a former midfielder himself, is said to be a big admirer of Zubimendi's style and believes he could be the anchor Chelsea need.

Chelsea's interest adds another layer of complexity to the situation. The Blues have been active in the transfer market in recent windows, and their ability to offer a lucrative package could test Arsenal's resolve. However, no official approach has been made by either club, and sources suggest that Zubimendi remains focused on his duties at the Emirates.

Arteta's plan and the player's role

Arteta's plan, as outlined in various reports, involves integrating Zubimendi into a more advanced role at times, while also using him as a shield in front of the defence. The manager is said to be confident that the midfielder can adapt to the physical demands of English football, and his early displays have been encouraging.

Zubimendi's versatility is one of the reasons he is so highly rated. He can play as a lone pivot or in a double pivot, and his passing range allows Arsenal to build from the back with ease. These attributes have made him a valuable asset, and losing him would be a significant blow to Arteta's plans.

What happens next

With the summer transfer window still open, the coming weeks will be crucial. Real Madrid and Chelsea are both monitoring the situation, but neither has made a decisive move yet. Arsenal, for their part, are preparing for the new season and will hope to keep their star midfielder out of the headlines.

Whether Madrid's interest translates into a formal bid remains to be seen. For now, Arsenal fans can breathe a cautious sigh of relief, but the transfer saga is far from over.
