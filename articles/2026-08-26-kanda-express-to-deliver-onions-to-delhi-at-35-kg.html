Kanda Express to deliver onions to Delhi at ₹35/kg

📅 2026-08-26 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kanda Express to transport 800 tonnes of onions to Delhi
  • Government sets retail price at ₹35/kg, much lower than market rate
  • Critics question affordability and potential supply chain disruptions

Government Launches Special Train to Stabilize Onion Prices

The Indian government has launched the 'Kanda Express', a special train carrying 800 tonnes of onions to Delhi, in a bid to stabilize prices that have surged by around 60% in recent months. The move comes as the government aims to sell the onions at a retail price of ₹35 per kilogram, significantly lower than the current market rate.

Why This Matters

The decision to bring onions to Delhi via a special train is a significant step in the government's efforts to curb the rising prices of this essential vegetable. Onion prices have been a major concern for consumers, with the surge affecting the cost of living and the overall economic stability. The government's intervention is seen as a proactive measure to ensure that the basic needs of the people are met.

Government's Strategy and Potential Challenges

The government's strategy involves tapping into the National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation of India Limited (NAFED) stocks and releasing supplies from the buffer stock to stabilize prices. However, critics argue that the affordability of the onions at ₹35 per kilogram may not be sustainable in the long run, especially if the supply chain is disrupted.

Expert Opinions and Critical Analysis

Economists and agricultural experts have mixed reactions to the government's intervention. While some praise the government for taking proactive measures to stabilize prices, others warn that the affordability of the onions at ₹35 per kilogram may not be sustainable in the long run. There are also concerns about the potential for black marketing and other supply chain disruptions that could undermine the government's efforts.

Moreover, the decision to bring onions to Delhi via a special train is seen as a temporary solution rather than a long-term fix. The underlying issue of supply and demand imbalances in the onion market needs to be addressed to ensure that prices remain stable in the future.
