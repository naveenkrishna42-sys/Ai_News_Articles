Sensex, Nifty End Higher on July 30; Auto Stocks Lead Rally

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex gained 274 points, Nifty closed above 24,300 on July 30.
  • Auto stocks led the rally, with major carmakers posting gains.
  • Markets remained resilient despite fresh US-Iran tensions.
  • Coal India was among the top gainers in the Nifty 50 pack.

Markets End Higher Despite Global Headwinds

Indian stock markets closed in positive territory on July 30, with the BSE Sensex gaining 274 points and the Nifty 50 settling above the 24,300 mark. The rally was led by auto stocks, which emerged as the top sectoral gainer of the day.

The broader market also showed strength, with midcap and smallcap indices outperforming the benchmarks. Analysts attributed the positive sentiment to domestic institutional buying and recovery in select heavyweights.

Auto Stocks Drive the Rally

Shares of major automobile companies surged, pushing the Nifty Auto index higher. Gains in Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors, and Mahindra & Mahindra contributed significantly to the benchmark indices' rise.

The auto sector has been in focus recently amid expectations of a strong festive season demand and easing supply chain constraints. Market participants are also tracking government policies on electric vehicles.

Fresh US-Iran Tensions Fail to Dampen Sentiment

Despite fresh geopolitical tensions between the United States and Iran, Indian markets remained resilient. The Sensex jumped as much as 888 points during intraday trade before paring some gains, as per reports.

Investors appeared to shrug off the risks, focusing instead on domestic economic data and corporate earnings. Oil prices, which often spike on such tensions, remained relatively stable, providing some relief to import-dependent India.

Coal India Among Top Gainers

Coal India was among the prominent gainers in the Nifty 50 pack, rising sharply during the session. The stock's rally added to the overall positive breadth on the exchanges.

Other index heavyweights like Reliance Industries and HDFC Bank also traded in the green, providing support to the benchmarks. However, IT stocks faced mild selling pressure, capping the upside.

What to Watch Next

Investors will now look forward to the monthly auto sales data due next week, which will offer clues on demand recovery. Global cues, including the US Federal Reserve's policy stance and oil price movement, will also influence near-term market direction.
