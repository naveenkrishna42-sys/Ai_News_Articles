Vaibhav Sooryavanshi’s form sparks debate on Sanju Samson’s India return

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Vaibhav Sooryavanshi won Player of the Match in the third T20I against Zimbabwe.
  • Irfan Pathan said Sooryavanshi 'won't let them come back' once he replaces someone.
  • Sanju Samson's place in the Indian team is under scrutiny amid Sooryavanshi's rise.

Sooryavanshi's rise sparks selection debate

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi's blistering form in the ongoing T20I series against Zimbabwe has reignited the debate over Sanju Samson's future in the Indian cricket team. The young batter has been unstoppable, scoring consistently and earning the Player of the Match award in the third T20I.

His performances have prompted strong reactions from former cricketers. Irfan Pathan, the former India all-rounder, made a pointed remark: "Whoever this kid replaces, he won't let them come back." The statement, widely shared on social media, has added fuel to the discussion around India's batting lineup.

Samson's uncertain road back

Sanju Samson, who has been in and out of the Indian setup for years, now faces a familiar but more intense scrutiny. The Kerala wicketkeeper-batter has shown flashes of brilliance but has struggled to cement his place. With Sooryavanshi delivering in high-pressure games, selectors may find it hard to ignore the youngster's case.

One Rediff comment captured the sentiment: "This is probably the right time to draft Sooryavanshi into India's ODI side." The 20-year-old has been in sublime touch, and his ability to anchor innings while accelerating makes him a valuable asset in limited-overs cricket.

Irfan Pathan's warning to incumbents

Irfan Pathan did not stop at the "wapis nahi aane dega" comment. In another interaction, he sent a direct warning to Abhishek Sharma and Sanju Samson, saying, "How special is Vaibhav Sooryavanshi?" The question was rhetorical, but the message was clear: the youngster is here to stay.

Pathan's remarks reflect a broader sentiment among former players and fans. Sooryavanshi's hunger for runs and his composure under pressure have drawn comparisons with some of India's finest young talents. The team management now faces a tough choice between backing experience and investing in form.

What this means for India's ODI plans

With the ODI World Cup still a few years away, India is in a transition phase. The middle-order slots are up for grabs, and Sooryavanshi's performances have made the selection committee's job more complicated. If he continues this run, a call-up to the ODI squad might not be far off.

Sanju Samson, meanwhile, will need to produce big scores in domestic cricket or the IPL to remind selectors of his talent. The competition for spots is healthy for Indian cricket, but for Samson, the window is narrowing.

For now, all eyes are on the remaining matches of the Zimbabwe series. Another strong showing from Sooryavanshi could force the selectors' hand sooner rather than later.
