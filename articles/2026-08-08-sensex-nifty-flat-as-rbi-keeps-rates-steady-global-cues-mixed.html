Sensex, Nifty flat as RBI keeps rates steady; global cues mixed

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • RBI kept key interest rates unchanged, aligning with market expectations.
  • Sensex and Nifty opened flat amid mixed global signals.
  • IT and banking stocks showed divergent trends in early trade.

Indian equity benchmarks opened on a subdued note on Saturday, with the Sensex and Nifty trading flat in early deals. The lack of directional cues followed the Reserve Bank of India's decision to keep key interest rates unchanged, a move that had been largely anticipated by market participants. Global signals remained mixed, adding to the cautious sentiment on Dalal Street.

RBI's status quo keeps rates steady

The central bank's monetary policy committee chose to hold the repo rate, prioritising inflation control over growth support. This decision came as no surprise to analysts, who had factored in a status quo given the prevailing inflationary pressures. The commentary accompanying the policy was parsed closely by traders for any hints about future rate trajectories.

With the rate decision out of the way, the market's focus now shifts to global cues and domestic earnings. The flat opening suggests that investors are adopting a wait-and-watch approach, unwilling to take large positions until clearer signals emerge.

Sectoral divergences in early trade

Early trading saw divergent moves across sectors. Information technology stocks showed some strength, while banking and financial shares remained under pressure. This mixed sectoral performance contributed to the benchmark indices hovering near their previous closing levels.

Midcap and smallcap stocks too displayed a lack of clear direction, mirroring the broader market mood. The volatility index, or India VIX, was largely range-bound, indicating that traders are not expecting sharp swings in the near term.

Global cues offer no clear direction

Overseas markets provided little impetus, with Asian bourses trading mixed. US futures pointed to a subdued start on Wall Street, while European markets were yet to open. Investors are keeping an eye on geopolitical developments and commodity prices, particularly crude oil, which has a direct bearing on India's inflation and trade deficit.

The strength of the US dollar and movements in the bond market also remain key monitors. Any sharp move in these could alter the risk appetite for emerging markets, including India.

What lies ahead for investors

Market participants are now looking ahead to the next set of economic data and corporate earnings for cues. The trajectory of monsoon rains and its impact on agricultural output will also be watched, given its influence on rural demand and food inflation.

In the coming sessions, the market's direction will likely depend on global developments and any fresh policy commentary from the RBI. Until then, the mood on Dalal Street is expected to remain cautious, with investors awaiting a clear catalyst.
