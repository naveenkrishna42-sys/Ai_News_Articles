Reliance Q1 Results: Revenue Crosses ₹3 Lakh Crore, Profit Drops 22%

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Revenue rose 25% year-on-year to exceed ₹3 lakh crore for the first time.
  • Net profit fell 22% YoY to ₹20,946 crore, hit by a one-time exceptional item.
  • The profit decline is linked to a prior-year gain from the sale of an Asian Paints stake.
  • Stock ended the session 2.5% higher ahead of the earnings announcement.

Revenue Milestone Amid Profit Dip

Reliance Industries Ltd (RIL) reported its first-quarter results for the financial year 2026-27 on Friday, posting a 25% jump in revenue to cross the ₹3 lakh crore mark for the first time. The consolidated revenue from operations stood at over ₹3 lakh crore, driven by strong performance across its retail and telecom segments.

However, net profit dropped 22% year-on-year to ₹20,946 crore. The decline was largely attributed to a one-time exceptional item — a base effect from the previous year when the company booked a significant gain from the sale of its stake in Asian Paints.

What Hit the Bottom Line

The profit dip is not reflective of underlying operational weakness, analysts noted. The exceptional gain in the year-ago quarter had inflated the profit base, making the current quarter's comparison unfavourable. Excluding that one-time item, the company's operational earnings remained robust.

Reliance's oil-to-chemicals (O2C) business continued to face margin pressures, but the digital services and retail verticals provided a strong cushion. Jio Platforms and Reliance Retail both reported steady subscriber and revenue growth during the quarter.

Market Reaction

RIL shares ended Friday's trading session 2.5% higher, hitting the day's high ahead of the earnings release. The positive close suggests that investors had already priced in the profit decline and focused on the revenue growth and long-term outlook.

The stock has been volatile in recent weeks amid global crude price fluctuations and concerns over telecom tariff hikes. However, the Q1 numbers appear to have reassured markets about the company's ability to scale revenue despite external headwinds.

Segmental Performance

While the company has not yet released a full segmental breakdown, preliminary data indicates strong momentum in its consumer-facing businesses. Jio added millions of new subscribers in the quarter, while Reliance Retail expanded its store network and digital commerce footprint.

The O2C segment, which refines crude oil and produces petrochemicals, faced lower margins due to volatility in global energy markets. But higher volumes partially offset the impact, keeping the segment's contribution to overall revenue stable.

What to Watch

Investors will now focus on the management's commentary during the earnings call, particularly on the outlook for retail margins, Jio's average revenue per user (ARPU), and any plans for further deleveraging. The company's ability to sustain revenue growth above ₹3 lakh crore in subsequent quarters will be a key monitorable.
