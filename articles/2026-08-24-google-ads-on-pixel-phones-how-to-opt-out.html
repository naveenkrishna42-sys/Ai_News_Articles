Google Ads on Pixel Phones: How to Opt Out

📅 2026-08-24 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Google is testing ads on Pixel phones
  • Users can disable these ads
  • This could set a new standard for ad targeting on smartphones

Google's New Ad Testing on Pixel Phones

Google has started showing ads on Pixel phones, marking a significant shift in its advertising strategy. This move, currently in testing phase, could redefine how users experience ads on their smartphones. The company is exploring new ways to monetize its devices, potentially through targeted advertising.

Why This Matters

The introduction of ads on Pixel phones raises several concerns. Privacy advocates are wary of increased data collection, while users may find the ads intrusive. Google's move could also influence other tech giants to follow suit, leading to a more ad-heavy smartphone ecosystem.

How to Turn Off the Ads

Users can disable these ads through their Google settings. Navigate to 'Ads' in the settings menu and toggle off the option to show ads on your Pixel phone. This step ensures users maintain control over their browsing experience.

Step-by-Step Guide

  • Open the Google app on your Pixel phone.
  • Go to 'Settings' and select 'Ads'.
  • Toggle off the 'Show ads on Pixel phones' option.
  • Save your settings.

Broader Implications

Google's ad testing on Pixel phones could set a precedent for other manufacturers. If successful, we might see more devices displaying ads, potentially leading to a more fragmented ad market. Users should stay informed and proactive in managing their ad preferences.

Expert Opinion

Tech analyst Ravi Kumar from TIVRA News suggests that Google's move is a strategic one. 'This is about monetization, but it also highlights the need for better user control over ads,' he says. Kumar advises users to regularly review their privacy settings to stay ahead of such changes.
