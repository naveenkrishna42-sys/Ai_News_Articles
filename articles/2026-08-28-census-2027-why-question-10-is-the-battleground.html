Census 2027: A Comprehensive Look at the Controversial Question 10

The upcoming Census 2027 in India is set to ask 40 questions, with Question 10 on caste being the most contentious. This question, which asks about an individual's caste, has sparked a debate about the census's integrity and its potential to manipulate data. The census, which aims to count India's 1.5 billion people, will be conducted by 3 million enumerators. However, the inclusion of a caste question has raised concerns among critics.

The Significance of Question 10

Question 10 is not just a simple demographic question. It is a sensitive issue that touches on the country's social fabric. The caste system, which has been a part of Indian society for centuries, is deeply ingrained in the lives of millions. The census's inclusion of this question has been seen as an attempt to document and understand the caste dynamics in the country.

However, the inclusion of this question has also been criticized. Critics argue that the census could be used to manipulate data, leading to a distorted picture of the country's social landscape. They also argue that the question could be used to target marginalized communities, leading to potential discrimination.
