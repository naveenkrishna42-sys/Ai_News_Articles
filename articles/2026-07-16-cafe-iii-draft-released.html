CAFE-III Draft Released

Key points
  • Centre floats draft CAFE-III norms
  • Special recognition for ethanol and biofuel cars
  • Tougher fuel efficiency norms proposed from April 2027

Centre Issues Draft CAFE III Norms

The Centre has released a draft of the Corporate Average Fuel Economy (CAFE)-III norms, which propose tougher fuel efficiency standards for passenger vehicles from April 2027. The new norms invite stakeholder suggestions, with the government aiming to reduce carbon emissions from the automotive sector. The draft also gives special recognition to ethanol and biofuel cars, promoting the use of alternative fuels.

Key Proposals

The proposed CAFE-III norms aim to improve fuel efficiency and reduce emissions. The government has proposed a CO2 credit system, which allows manufacturers to buy and sell credits to meet the norms. However, Tata Motors has opposed the mechanism, seeking changes to the proposed credit buying system.

Stakeholder Response

Tata Motors has expressed its opposition to the Bharat Energy Efficiency (BEE) mechanism on CO2 credit system under CAFE-2. The company is seeking changes to the proposed mechanism, which it believes is unfair. Other stakeholders are also expected to provide their feedback on the draft norms, which will be taken into consideration before the final norms are notified.

Implementation Timeline

The proposed CAFE-III norms are expected to come into effect from April 2027. The government has invited stakeholder suggestions, which will be accepted until a specified deadline. The final norms will be notified after considering the feedback from stakeholders.

The government's move to promote alternative fuels and improve fuel efficiency is expected to have a significant impact on the automotive sector. As the industry prepares to meet the new norms, manufacturers will have to invest in research and development to improve the fuel efficiency of their vehicles.

What happens next will depend on the feedback from stakeholders and the government's response to their concerns. The final notification of the CAFE-III norms will be closely watched by the industry, as it will have a significant impact on the future of the automotive sector in India.
