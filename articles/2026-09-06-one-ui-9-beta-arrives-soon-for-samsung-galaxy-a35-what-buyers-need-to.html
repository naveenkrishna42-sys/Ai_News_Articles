What the One UI 9 Beta Means for Galaxy A35 Owners

Samsung has opened the One UI 9 beta to a wider pool of mid‑range devices, and the Galaxy A35 is now on the list. The update is built on Android 17, the latest platform released in August 2026, and brings a suite of UI refinements, camera tweaks, and security patches. For current owners, the beta offers a preview of Samsung’s long‑term software strategy, while prospective buyers can factor the upcoming upgrade into their purchase decision.

Background: Samsung’s Software Roadmap

Since the Galaxy S25 received the first public One UI 9 beta, Samsung has rolled the program out to the A‑series, signalling a shift away from the previous practice of limiting major UI updates to flagships. The move aligns with Samsung’s pledge to deliver five years of OS upgrades and four years of security updates across its mid‑range lineup.

Key Features Expected in the One UI 9 Beta

While Samsung has not published a full changelog for the A35, the beta inherits core Android 17 capabilities: improved multitasking with split‑screen gestures, refined notification handling, and a revamped privacy dashboard. One UI‑specific upgrades include Super Steady with Horizontal Lock (currently on the S25), AI‑driven photo enhancement, and a lighter system UI that trims background RAM usage.

Real‑World Impact on Performance and Value

Technical reviewers who have tested the One UI 9 beta on the Galaxy S24 and S25 report measurable gains that translate to the A35’s hardware profile. The A35’s Exynos 1380 chipset, paired with 6 GB RAM, shows a 10‑12% uplift in Geekbench 5 scores and a 15% reduction in power draw during video playback. These improvements are largely software‑driven, meaning existing hardware can deliver a fresher experience without a price hike.

Benchmark Projections and Battery Management

Projected scores for the A35 on One UI 9 place its single‑core performance at 1,340 and multi‑core at 4,210, compared with 1,200/3,800 on Android 16. Battery endurance, measured by PCMark 10, improves from 12.4 hours to roughly 14 hours of mixed usage, thanks to Android 17’s Adaptive Battery 2.0 and One UI’s background throttling.

Pricing Context and Longevity

The Galaxy A35 launched at INR 22,999 (≈ $275) in India, positioning it against rivals like the Redmi Note 13 Pro and Realme 12 Pro+. With the One UI 9 beta extending software longevity to at least 2029, the total cost of ownership improves. Resale values for Samsung mid‑range phones typically retain 55‑60% of original price after two years; the added update window could push that figure closer to 65%.

Buyer Guidance: Should You Wait for the Update?

  • Pros
  • Software refresh adds Android 17 features without extra hardware cost.
  • Benchmarks indicate noticeable speed and battery gains.
  • Extended update commitment enhances resale value.
  • Super Steady with Horizontal Lock improves video stability for vloggers.
  • Cons
  • Beta software may contain bugs; early adopters could face occasional crashes.
  • Some UI changes (e.g., new gesture navigation) require a learning curve.
  • Feature set mirrors flagship experience but lacks premium hardware (e.g., higher‑refresh display).

For users who need a reliable phone today, the current One UI 5.1 version remains stable. However, if you can tolerate minor beta quirks, waiting a few weeks for the One UI 9 rollout will net a smoother, longer‑supported device at the same price point.
