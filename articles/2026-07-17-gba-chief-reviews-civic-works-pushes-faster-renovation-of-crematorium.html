GBA chief reviews civic works, pushes faster renovation of crematorium

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • GBA chief conducted a review of ongoing civic works in Bengaluru.
  • Renovation of a crematorium was highlighted as a priority project.
  • Officials have been directed to speed up work and maintain quality standards.

The Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) chief on Wednesday reviewed the progress of several civic works across the city, with a particular focus on accelerating the renovation of a key crematorium. The inspection comes amid growing public concern over delays in basic infrastructure projects.

Focus on crematorium renovation

During the review, the GBA chief stressed the need to complete the crematorium renovation without further delays. Officials were told to ensure the facility meets modern standards and serves the community efficiently.

The project, which has been pending for several months, is expected to address long-standing complaints about inadequate facilities. The chief directed the civic body to deploy additional manpower and resources to finish the work on time.

Other civic works under scanner

The review also covered multiple other civic projects, including road repairs, drainage improvements, and waste management initiatives. The GBA chief emphasised that quality should not be compromised for speed.

Residents in several wards have been raising issues about poor road conditions and waterlogging. Officials have been asked to submit a detailed timeline for completion of all pending works within a week.

“We are committed to delivering better infrastructure to Bengaluru’s citizens. Any slackness will not be tolerated,” the GBA chief told the gathering.

Accountability and monitoring

The GBA chief has instructed senior officials to conduct regular site visits and submit progress reports. A central monitoring cell will be set up to track the status of all ongoing projects.

Contractors found violating deadlines or quality norms may face penalties. The civic body is also exploring ways to use technology for better project management and transparency.

The review meeting was attended by senior engineers, zonal commissioners, and representatives from the public works department.

The GBA chief is expected to hold another review in two weeks to assess progress. Officials have been warned against any laxity in execution.
