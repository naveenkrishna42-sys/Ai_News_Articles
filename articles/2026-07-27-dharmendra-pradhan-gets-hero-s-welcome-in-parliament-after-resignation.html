Dharmendra Pradhan Gets Hero's Welcome in Parliament After Resignation

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • BJP MPs welcomed Dharmendra Pradhan with chants of 'Dharmendra Pradhan Zindabad' in Parliament complex
  • Congress called it 'celebration of children's ruined futures'
  • Pradhan resigned as education minister following the Jantar Mantar stampede tragedy

Parliament Welcome Sparks Political Firestorm

Former education minister Dharmendra Pradhan received a rousing welcome from Bharatiya Janata Party MPs as he entered the Parliament complex on Monday. Chants of 'Dharmendra Pradhan Zindabad' echoed through the corridors, with several BJP lawmakers queuing up to shake his hand.

The display of solidarity came less than a week after Pradhan resigned from the Union cabinet, accepting moral responsibility for the Jantar Mantar stampede that claimed the lives of several school children.

Opposition Fumes at 'Celebration of Tragedy'

The Congress party was quick to condemn the scenes. One senior Congress leader described the welcome as a 'celebration of children's ruined futures', accusing the ruling party of insensitivity.

Opposition MPs pointed out that the tragedy occurred during a school education event that fell under Pradhan's ministry. For them, the BJP's public show of support was a direct affront to the grieving families.

The Congress has demanded a thorough investigation into the stampede and has questioned whether resignations alone are sufficient accountability.

Pradhan's 'Street Fighter' Retort

When a Congress MP reportedly remarked to Pradhan that 'these things happen', the former minister shot back, according to sources. 'I'm a street fighter,' Pradhan was quoted as saying, in a moment that encapsulated his combative political style.

The exchange highlighted the deep partisan divide over the tragedy. While the BJP sees Pradhan's resignation as an act of honour, the opposition views it as an insufficient gesture that does not address systemic failures.

Analysts have noted that Pradhan remains a powerful figure within the BJP's organisational structure, particularly in his home state of Odisha. His swift resignation is seen by party insiders as a move that preserves his long-term political future.

Criticism Over 'Atonement Without Amendment'

Critics, including some commentators, have argued that Pradhan's resignation amounts to 'atonement without amendment'. The underlying safety protocols and crowd management measures that failed at Jantar Mantar remain unchanged, they point out.

Questions have been raised about whether the departure of one minister can fix a system that allowed a tragic crush of children to occur in the first place. Opposition parties have called for a comprehensive review of safety standards at all public events involving students.

The government has not yet announced any specific policy changes or new safety guidelines in response to the tragedy.

What happens next remains uncertain. While Pradhan has stepped down from his ministerial role, he continues to be a prominent face in Parliament and within the party. The opposition has indicated it will keep the pressure on, demanding not just accountability but concrete reforms to prevent any repeat of the Jantar Mantar disaster.
