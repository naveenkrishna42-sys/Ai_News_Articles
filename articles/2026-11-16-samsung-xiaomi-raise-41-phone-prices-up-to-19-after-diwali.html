Samsung, Xiaomi Raise 41 Phone Prices Up to 19% After Diwali

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Desk 📅 2026-11-16 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung and Xiaomi have raised prices on 41 smartphone models in India, effective November 16, with increases reaching 19%.
  • Samsung Galaxy M17, M17e and F70e rose by ₹2,000; Galaxy A57, A37, A56, A36, A17, A07 and F17 rose by up to ₹4,000.
  • Buyers should compare old-stock listings, exchange value and EMI outgo before assuming the new MRP is unavoidable.

Samsung and Xiaomi have reset smartphone pricing in India immediately after the Diwali sales cycle, with 41 models now carrying higher MRPs. Moneycontrol.com reported the broad increase, while Beebom, PaidFreeDroid and NewsBytes tracked model-level revisions across Samsung’s Galaxy M, A and F families and Xiaomi’s Redmi portfolio. The hikes take effect from November 16, 2026, and reach up to 19%.

What Changed on November 16

The revisions are not uniform. Samsung’s Galaxy M17, M17e and F70e moved up by ₹2,000. The Galaxy A57, A37, A56, A36, A17, A07 and F17 were revised by up to ₹4,000, according to the published rate checks. Xiaomi’s Redmi models were tipped for increases of up to ₹4,000 from the same date.

For buyers, the practical detail is the street price, not the MRP alone. Retailers often hold pre-hike inventory for a few days, and online listings may lag. A phone showing the old price is not automatically a stale unit; check the invoice date and warranty start.

Why the Post-Diwali Hike Matters

Festive demand usually pulls forward purchases with cashback, exchange bonuses and bank offers. Once that cycle ends, brands correct channel pricing to protect margins and absorb input-cost pressure.
