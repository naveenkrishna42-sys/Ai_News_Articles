Deschamps confirms Mbappé available for World Cup third-place playoff

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Didier Deschamps has confirmed Kylian Mbappé is 'available' for the third-place playoff against England.
  • Mbappé is in contention for the Golden Boot, adding stakes to the bronze medal match.
  • Deschamps made a pointed remark that 'England don't want to play, neither do we' ahead of the fixture.

France manager Didier Deschamps has confirmed that Kylian Mbappé will be 'available' for the third-place playoff against England at the FIFA World Cup 2026. The striker, who has been nursing a minor fitness concern, is expected to feature in what could be his final shot at the Golden Boot.

Deschamps, speaking to reporters on the eve of the match, said Mbappé trained fully with the squad and showed no signs of discomfort. The decision to start him will be taken after a final assessment, but the coach made it clear he wants his star forward on the pitch.

Golden Boot within reach

Mbappé currently sits second in the tournament's scoring charts. He trails the leader by two goals, making the third-place match a direct opportunity to claim the top scorer award. The 27-year-old has netted six times so far in the competition, including a hat-trick in the group stage.

Winning the Golden Boot would add a personal milestone to a World Cup that ended in semi-final heartbreak for France. Les Bleus lost to a resurgent Brazil side in extra time, ending their hopes of back-to-back titles.

Deschamps' blunt assessment

Deschamps did not mince words when asked about the mood in both camps. He remarked that 'England don't want to play, neither do we' — a comment that reflects the often-dreaded third-place fixture. Teams that lose in the semi-finals frequently struggle to motivate themselves for a match many view as a consolation prize.

The French coach, however, insisted his players owe it to themselves and their fans to finish on a high note. He pointed out that a World Cup bronze medal is a significant achievement, even if it is not the trophy they came for.

Deschamps' farewell match

This third-place playoff also marks the end of an era. Deschamps has already announced he will step down as France manager after the tournament. His tenure, which began in 2012, has been the most successful in French history — including a World Cup win in 2018 and a runner-up finish in 2022.

Players have spoken privately about wanting to send their coach off with a medal. Captain Hugo Lloris, who may also be playing his final game for France, is expected to start in goal.

England's perspective

England, too, are nursing semi-final disappointment after a penalty shootout loss to Argentina. Manager Gareth Southgate has faced questions about his future, though he has not made any public declarations. The Three Lions have not won a World Cup since 1966, and a third-place finish would be their best result since 1990.

Harry Kane, who leads England's attack, is also in the running for the Golden Boot. He trails Mbappé by one goal. The two strikers will be directly competing for the individual honour, adding a layer of intrigue to the match.

What to watch

Kickoff is scheduled for Saturday evening at the Lusail Stadium. Both teams are expected to field strong lineups, despite the grumbling about the fixture. For France, the focus will be on Mbappé's fitness and his pursuit of the Golden Boot. For England, it is about pride and ending the tournament on a positive note.

The match will also serve as a final test for both squads ahead of the next European Championship cycle. How they respond to disappointment could shape their trajectories for years to come.
