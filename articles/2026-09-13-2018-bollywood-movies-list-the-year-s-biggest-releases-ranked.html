Why the 2018 Bollywood Slate Still Matters to Viewers and Collectors

2018 was the year Hindi cinema stopped apologising for scale. Big-budget period dramas, biopics and horror-comedies shared screens with tightly budgeted thrillers, and several of them out-earned films costing five times as much. For anyone building a home collection, evaluating a streaming catalogue, or simply deciding what to watch on a weekend, the 2018 release list is a genuinely useful reference point.

This breakdown covers the major theatrical releases of 2018 with verified star cast, release windows and reported box office performance, plus practical notes on which titles hold up on repeat viewing and which were pure opening-weekend plays.

The Top 10 Bollywood Releases of 2018

1. Padmaavat

Sanjay Leela Bhansali's period epic, starring Deepika Padukone, Ranveer Singh and Shahid Kapoor, released on 25 January 2018 after prolonged protests and certification delays. Its reported domestic net collection of roughly Rs 300 crore made it one of the highest-grossing Hindi films of the year. The IMAX presentation and costume design justify a large-screen rewatch; the runtime of nearly three hours does not.

2. Sanju

Rajkumar Hirani's Sanjay Dutt biopic, led by Ranbir Kapoor, arrived on 29 June 2018 and became the biggest earner of the year in Hindi cinema, with reported domestic net figures around Rs 340 crore. Ranbir's transformation is the film's real product. Buyers should note the extended cut adds little beyond what the theatrical version already delivers.

3. Race 3

Salman Khan's action thriller released on 15 June 2018 and opened huge before word of mouth flattened its run. It earned a reported Rs 170 crore-plus domestically but is widely regarded as the weakest entry in the franchise. Watch it for the stunt choreography, not the plot.

4. Thugs of Hindostan

Amitabh Bachchan and Aamir Khan headlined this Diwali 2018 release, which posted a massive opening day before collapsing at the box office. Reported domestic net collections stalled near Rs 145 crore against a reported budget well above Rs 200 crore, making it the year's most expensive cautionary tale.

5. Andhadhun

Sriram Raghavan's black comedy thriller with Ayushmann Khurrana and Tabu released on 5 October 2018 and became the year's best value proposition, earning a reported Rs 70 crore-plus on a modest budget. Tightly written, endlessly rewatchable, and easily the strongest repeat-viewing title on this list.

6. Badhaai Ho

Ayushmann Khurrana and Sanya Malhotra starred in this October 2018 family comedy that turned a small budget into a reported Rs 130 crore-plus domestic haul. Its humour travels well on streaming and satellite.

7. Raazi

Meghna Gulzar's spy drama, with Alia Bhatt and Vicky Kaushal, released on 11 May 2018 and earned a reported Rs 120 crore-plus domestically. Understated, well-researched and one of the year's most critically respected films.

8. Zero

Shah Rukh Khan, Anushka Sharma and Katrina Kaif starred in this December 2018 release, which underperformed against its reported budget despite a strong opening. The visual effects work is ambitious; the screenplay is not.

9. Stree

Rajkummar Rao and Shraddha Kapoor's horror-comedy released on 31 August 2018 and became a sleeper hit with reported domestic earnings near Rs 130 crore. It revived the horror-comedy genre for Hindi audiences.

10. 2.0

Though a Tamil production dubbed into Hindi, Rajinikanth and Akshay Kumar's sci-fi spectacle released on 29 November 2018 and posted reported Hindi-version collections above Rs 180 crore. Its visual effects set a benchmark for Indian genre cinema.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • Content-driven mid-budget films such as Andhadhun, Badhaai Ho and Stree delivered the best return on investment of the year.
  • Star-driven mega-budget titles including Thugs of Hindostan and Zero underperformed relative to reported production costs.
  • Streaming and satellite rights became decisive profit drivers, often covering a film's budget before theatrical release.
  • Horror-comedy emerged as the year's most commercially reliable Hindi genre.
  • Period and biopic formats dominated critical conversation and awards season.

Buyer and Viewer Verdict

If you are buying physical media, prioritise Andhadhun, Raazi, Badhaai Ho and Stree — they reward repeat viewing and hold up on smaller screens. Padmaavat and Sanju merit large-screen or high-bitrate streaming playback for their production scale. Race 3, Thugs of Hindostan and Zero are one-time watches at best, and a rental is a smarter spend than a purchase.

For streaming subscribers, the 2018 catalogue is now widely distributed across major platforms, so comparing subscription tiers against the specific titles you actually want matters more than chasing a single service's library size.
