UPSC Mains Ancient History PYQs: 2025-2013 Questions Compiled

Key points
  • Compilation covers UPSC Mains Ancient History and Art & Culture questions from 2025 back to 2013.
  • Resource is curated by coaching institute Vajiram & Ravi, known for UPSC preparation.
  • Questions span various topics including architecture, sculpture, and cultural history.

Question Bank for Aspirants

Vajiram & Ravi, a well-known name in UPSC coaching, has released a compilation of previous year questions (PYQs) for Ancient History and Art & Culture from the UPSC Mains examination. The set spans 13 years, from 2025 all the way back to 2013.

This resource is specifically designed for candidates preparing for the UPSC Civil Services Mains. It covers the History paper (Paper I) and the General Studies paper I, both of which test knowledge of India's ancient heritage.

Scope of the Compilation

The PYQ bank includes questions on a wide range of topics. Aspirants can expect queries on temple architecture, sculpture, painting traditions, and cultural developments across different periods.

Questions from recent years reflect the exam's evolving focus. For instance, the 2024 paper asked about the contribution of the Chola period to temple building and bronze sculpture. The 2023 paper included a question on the significance of the Gupta age in Indian art.

Key Themes Covered

  • Architecture: From Harappan cities to medieval temples.
  • Sculpture: Mauryan pillars, Kushan art, and Chola bronzes.
  • Cultural history: Buddhism, Jainism, and Bhakti movement influences.

Older questions, such as those from 2015, often focused on the Indus Valley Civilization's urban planning. The 2013 paper tested knowledge of the Ajanta and Ellora caves.

Strategic Value for Preparation

Practicing these PYQs helps candidates understand the exam's pattern and difficulty. It also highlights recurring themes—like temple architecture—that examiners tend to revisit.

Vajiram & Ravi has arranged the questions year-wise. This allows aspirants to trace how the UPSC's emphasis has shifted over time. For example, questions on art forms like miniature painting have become more common in recent years.

Officials at the institute have not confirmed whether this compilation will be updated for future years. However, similar resources from the same publisher have been refreshed annually in the past.

What to Watch

Candidates should look out for the next UPSC notification, expected later this year. The PYQ bank remains a vital tool for anyone aiming to crack the Mains exam.
