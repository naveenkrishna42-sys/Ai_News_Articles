Mirzapur: The Movie Sells 11,000 Tickets in 3 Days

Mirzapur: The Movie, starring Ali Fazal, Pankaj Tripathi, and Divyenndu, has sold 11,000 tickets in national chains, with just three days left until its release. This is a strong indication of the film's potential at the box office. The film is expected to have an opening of Rs 15-20 crore on day 1, according to industry estimates.

Ticket Prices Soar to Rs 1800

As the advance bookings for Mirzapur: The Movie began, the ticket prices soared to Rs 1800. This is a significant increase from the initial ticket prices, which were around Rs 1500. The increase in ticket prices is a clear indication of the film's popularity and the high demand for its tickets.

CBFC Ordered 9 Changes to A-rated Mirzapur

The Central Board of Film Certification (CBFC) ordered 9 changes to the A-rated Mirzapur. These changes have sparked a debate among fans, who fear that the cuts will 'dilute' the film. The CBFC's decision to order changes to the film is a reminder of the strict regulations that films have to adhere to in India.

Expected Opening of Rs 15-20 Crore

Mirzapur: The Movie is expected to have an opening of Rs 15-20 crore on day 1 at the box office. This is a significant amount, especially considering the film's budget and the current state of the Indian film industry. The film's strong advance bookings and the high demand for its tickets are likely to contribute to its successful opening.

Fans Fear Cuts Will 'Dilute' Film

Fans of Mirzapur: The Movie are concerned that the CBFC's ordered changes will 'dilute' the film. They believe that the cuts will remove some of the film's most memorable and impactful scenes. The fans' concerns highlight the importance of the CBFC's role in ensuring that films are suitable for all audiences.
