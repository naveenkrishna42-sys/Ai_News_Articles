The Bank Bazaar Credit Card offers a rare combination of instant digital approval, no joining fee, and accelerated cashback on online spends, making it a top value proposition for first-time applicants and frequent shoppers.

Deal & Pricing Analysis

Bank Bazaar has rolled out a limited-period offer waiving the joining fee on its co-branded credit card, effectively reducing the first-year cost to zero. The card typically carries a ₹500 annual fee, but with this promotion, new applicants pay nothing upfront. Additionally, the card provides a welcome bonus of ₹500 cashback on the first transaction within 30 days of issuance. Compared to standard entry-level cards that charge ₹500–₹1,000 annually, this deal delivers immediate savings. The ongoing rewards rate—up to 5% cashback on online shopping, 2% on offline spends, and 1% on other categories—remains competitive. Given that the fee waiver is available for a limited time, this is an opportune moment to apply if you are in the market for a no-cost cashback card.

Real-World Performance & Daily Use

Applying for the Bank Bazaar Credit Card is a fully digital process. The online application takes under 10 minutes, and eligible users receive instant approval with a virtual card for immediate use. The physical card arrives within 7–10 business days. In daily use, the card shines for online shoppers—Amazon, Flipkart, Swiggy, and Zomato transactions earn 5% cashback, which is credited to the statement within 2–3 days. Offline spends at supermarkets and fuel stations earn 2% and 1% fuel surcharge waiver, respectively. The cashback is auto-credited, eliminating the need for manual redemption. However, the card lacks travel-specific benefits like airport lounge access or low foreign transaction fees, making it less ideal for international travelers. Customer service via the Bank Bazaar app is responsive, but some users report delays in dispute resolution. Overall, for a no-fee card, the value proposition is strong.

Competition & Market Alternatives

Compared to the Amazon Pay ICICI Credit Card, which offers 5% cashback for Prime members on Amazon only, the Bank Bazaar card provides broader online coverage but at a slightly lower effective rate for non-Prime users. The Flipkart Axis Bank Credit Card gives 5% cashback on Flipkart and Myntra, but charges a ₹500 annual fee (waived on ₹2 lakh annual spends). The Bank Bazaar card’s no joining fee and instant approval give it an edge for those with limited credit history. However, if you are a heavy Amazon shopper, the Amazon Pay card remains superior due to its no-cost structure and higher effective rewards. For offline spends, the Bank Bazaar card’s 2% rate is above average, but cards like the SBI SimplyCLICK offer 10x rewards on online partners. Ultimately, the Bank Bazaar card is a solid all-rounder for digital-first users.

Who Should Buy This Deal

This card is ideal for first-time credit card applicants, students, and young professionals who want a no-fee card with instant approval and straightforward cashback. If you do a lot of online shopping across multiple platforms and prefer automatic cashback without complex reward points, this card fits well. It is also a good choice for those with a limited credit history, as Bank Bazaar’s approval criteria are relatively lenient. However, if you are a frequent international traveler or someone who spends heavily on Amazon, you should consider the Amazon Pay ICICI card or a travel-focused card instead. Additionally, if you already hold multiple credit cards, the marginal benefit may be low. Given the limited-period fee waiver, applying now maximizes savings. Wait if you need premium perks like lounge access or lower forex fees.

The Bank Bazaar Credit Card is a compelling choice for online shoppers and first-time applicants seeking a no-fee card with instant approval and automatic cashback. The limited-period joining fee waiver makes it an even sweeter deal. Apply now if you fit the profile; otherwise, consider alternatives if you need travel perks or higher Amazon-specific rewards.
