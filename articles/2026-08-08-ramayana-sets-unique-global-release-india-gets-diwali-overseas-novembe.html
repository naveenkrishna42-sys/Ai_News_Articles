Ramayana Sets Unique Global Release: India Gets Diwali, Overseas November 6

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ramayana will release internationally on November 6 and in India on Diwali, November 8.
  • Producer Namit Malhotra explained the strategic reason behind the staggered rollout.
  • The film is eyeing over 59,000 screens worldwide, aiming to surpass records set by Avatar 2 and RRR.
  • The trailer starring Ranbir Kapoor and Sai Pallavi has already crossed 1 billion views.

A Release Plan Like No Other

The much-anticipated Ramayana is breaking convention with a release strategy that has left the industry buzzing. Producer Namit Malhotra has confirmed that the epic will hit international screens on November 6, while Indian audiences will have to wait two more days for the Diwali premiere on November 8.

The staggered rollout is deliberate. Malhotra explained that the decision was made to maximise the film's global impact while respecting the cultural significance of Diwali in India. The two-day gap allows overseas markets to build word-of-mouth momentum before the festival release at home.

This is not a logistical afterthought but a carefully calibrated plan. By releasing internationally first, the team hopes to create a wave of anticipation that peaks exactly when Indian families gather for the festival of lights.

Why Two Different Dates?

Speaking about the unusual move, Malhotra stressed that the strategy was rooted in audience behaviour. International viewers, he noted, often prefer weekend openings, and November 6 falls on a Friday—a prime slot in global markets. In contrast, Diwali, which falls on November 8 this year, is traditionally reserved for big-ticket Indian releases.

The producer also hinted at the logistical scale involved. With over 59,000 screens targeted worldwide, the film is aiming to surpass the theatrical footprints of Avatar: The Way of Water and RRR. That kind of scale requires staggered rollouts to manage print and digital distribution efficiently.

For Indian audiences, the wait will be short but symbolic. Releasing on Diwali is a nod to the film's subject—the return of Lord Ram to Ayodhya—making the date thematically resonant.

Trailer Mania and Record-Breaking Buzz

The hype is already unprecedented. The trailer, starring Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram and Sai Pallavi as Sita, has crossed 1 billion views across all platforms and languages. That figure, confirmed by multiple reports, places it among the most-watched trailers in Indian cinema history.

The numbers are staggering even by global standards. A billion views in just weeks speaks to the film's cross-cultural appeal, aided by multilingual releases and aggressive digital marketing. The makers have leveraged every platform, from YouTube to regional streaming services, to ensure maximum reach.

Industry observers note that the trailer's success has set the stage for what could be a historic box-office run. The question now is whether the film can convert that digital enthusiasm into physical footfall.

The Road to 59,000 Screens

The screen count itself is a statement. With 59,000+ screens, Ramayana is aiming for a global footprint that dwarfs most Indian films. For context, RRR released on around 10,000 screens worldwide, while Avatar 2 peaked at roughly 52,000 screens globally.

Securing such a wide release involves unprecedented coordination with exhibitors across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia. Malhotra's team has reportedly been in talks for months to lock in premium screens, including IMAX and other large-format venues.

The logistics are daunting. Dubbing in multiple languages, coordinating time zones, and managing piracy risks are just some of the challenges. Yet the producers remain confident, citing the trailer's global resonance as proof of demand.

What This Means for Indian Cinema

This release strategy could redefine how Indian epics are launched. Traditionally, big-budget films open simultaneously worldwide. By staggering the release, Ramayana is testing a new playbook—one that may become a template for future spectacles.

For now, all eyes are on November 6. If the international opening is strong, the Diwali release in India could become one of the biggest events in box-office history.

As the countdown begins, the industry will be watching closely. Will the strategy pay off? The first signals will come from overseas numbers within days of the international premiere.
