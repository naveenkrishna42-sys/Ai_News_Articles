Why the ₹100‑Per‑Day Claim Matters

India’s credit bureaus are mandated to correct errors within a stipulated period. When they fail, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) empowers borrowers to claim a compensation of ₹100 for every day the mistake persists. This rule not only deters negligence but also cushions the financial impact of a tarnished credit score.

Eligibility Checklist

Before initiating a claim, verify that you meet the following criteria:

  • The error must be documented – e.g., wrong loan amount, missed payment, or phantom account.
  • You must have raised a grievance with the credit bureau and received a written response within 30 days.
  • The dispute remains unresolved for more than 15 days after the bureau’s response.

Step‑by‑Step Claim Process

The procedure is straightforward but demands meticulous record‑keeping.

1. Gather Evidence

Collect the credit report, dispute acknowledgment, and any correspondence with the bureau. Screenshots of the erroneous entry add credibility.

2. Submit a Formal Complaint

Use the RBI’s online grievance portal or send a registered letter to the bureau’s grievance cell. State the error, the days elapsed, and the compensation sought (₹100 × days).

3. Await the Bureau’s Response

The bureau has 15 days to rectify the mistake. If it fails, the RBI’s ombudsman steps in.

4. Claim Compensation

💳 Compare Features & Eligibility

File a compensation claim with the RBI’s Consumer Protection Department, attaching all evidence. The department typically processes claims within 30‑45 days.

Real‑World Impact: Benchmarks and Pricing Context

Consider a scenario where an erroneous late‑payment tag stays on your report for 60 days. At ₹100 per day, the compensation equals ₹6,000 – a tangible offset against potential interest hikes on future loans.

Financial analysts estimate that a single wrong entry can raise loan interest rates by 0.5‑1% for a ₹5 lakh personal loan, costing borrowers up to ₹2,500 annually. The ₹100‑per‑day rule therefore serves as both a punitive and remedial tool.

Pros & Cons – What Consumers Should Weigh

  • Pros: Direct monetary redress; incentivises bureaus to act swiftly; protects credit score integrity.
  • Cons: Requires diligent documentation; claim amount capped by RBI discretion; potential delays in bureaucratic processing.

Practical Tips for Maximising Your Claim

Maintain a digital folder of all credit‑report screenshots and correspondence. Use certified mail for any physical letters to create a verifiable trail. Finally, monitor your credit report weekly after filing to confirm the correction and trigger compensation automatically.
