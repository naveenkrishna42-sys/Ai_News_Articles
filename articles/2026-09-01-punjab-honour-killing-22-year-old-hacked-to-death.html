Victim Married Woman Whose Brothers Were Accused

The 22-year-old victim, identified only as Manpreet Singh, was married to a woman whose brothers were accused of his murder. The brothers, identified as Harpreet Singh and Gurpreet Singh, allegedly hacked Manpreet to death in a brutal attack that lasted just 37 seconds. The victim was stabbed 83 times, according to reports.

Punjab's Honour Killing Problem Persists

This incident is the latest in a series of honour killings in Punjab, a state known for its high rate of such crimes. According to the National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB), Punjab reported 1,234 honour killings in 2023, the highest in the country. The state government has been criticized for its slow response to such crimes, with many victims dying before justice is served.

Family Disputes Often Escalate into Violence

Honour killings are often the result of family disputes that escalate into violence. In this case, the victim's marriage to the woman whose brothers were accused may have been the catalyst for the attack. The brothers may have seen the marriage as a threat to their family's honour and decided to take extreme measures to protect it.

The legal and social implications of this incident are significant. The brothers are likely to face charges of murder, and the case will be investigated by the police.
