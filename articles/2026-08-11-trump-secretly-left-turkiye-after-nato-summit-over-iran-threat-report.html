Covert exit from Ankara

Former US President Donald Trump secretly flew out of Turkiye after the NATO summit, according to multiple reports, citing an alleged threat from Iran. The departure, which contradicts public statements about his travel plans, involved a hidden route and a military jet.

Reports from The Hindu, BBC, and The New York Times suggest Trump was concealed in a catering truck as part of a covert plan to swap vehicles and board a military aircraft. The move was reportedly triggered by intelligence about a potential Iranian threat targeting the former president.

Details of the secret plan

Sources familiar with the operation told The Washington Post that Trump's team organised the secret flight without prior announcement. The catering truck was used to move Trump from the summit venue to a secure location, where he boarded a US military jet.

Officials have not yet confirmed the specifics of the threat, and no further details have been released. The reports indicate that the decision was made abruptly, with only a handful of aides aware of the plan.

Publicly, Trump's schedule had suggested he would remain in Turkiye for additional engagements. The secret departure has raised questions about the credibility of official statements regarding his movements.

Iran threat assessment

The alleged threat from Iran has not been officially corroborated by US or Turkish authorities. Intelligence assessments reportedly pointed to a credible risk, prompting the urgent security measures. The nature of the threat remains unclear, and officials have declined to comment on operational specifics.

This incident echoes past security concerns involving former US leaders. However, the use of a catering truck for a covert exit is unusual, as it underscores the perceived seriousness of the risk.

Turkish authorities have not issued a statement on the matter. The summit itself concluded without public incident, and other leaders departed as scheduled.
