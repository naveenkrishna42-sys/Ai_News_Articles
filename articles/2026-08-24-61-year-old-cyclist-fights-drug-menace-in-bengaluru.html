61-year-old cyclist's pedal-powered drug campaign

A 61-year-old Bengaluru resident is turning his bicycle into a weapon against the city's drug menace. This initiative, which began last year, has already mobilized over 10,000 volunteers across the city. The campaign, named 'Cycle Against Addiction,' aims to reach every nook and corner of Bengaluru, educating the youth about the dangers of drug abuse.

How the campaign works

The cyclist, who wishes to remain anonymous, covers an average of 20 kilometers daily. His route is meticulously planned to ensure maximum coverage. He carries with him a small but powerful message: 'Drugs destroy lives. Choose life, choose health.'

Impact and achievements

Since its inception, the campaign has seen a significant reduction in drug-related incidents in the areas he covers. The volunteers, who are mostly young people, have been instrumental in spreading the message. The campaign has also led to the establishment of several community centers where addicts can seek help.

Challenges and criticisms

Despite its success, the campaign has faced several challenges. One of the main criticisms is the lack of a centralized system to track the progress and impact of the campaign. Another challenge is the reluctance of some communities to participate. However, the cyclist remains undeterred, believing that every small step counts in the fight against drug abuse.

Future plans

The cyclist has ambitious plans for the future. He aims to expand the campaign to other cities in Karnataka and eventually to other states. He also plans to introduce a mobile app that will allow volunteers to report drug-related incidents in real-time. This, he believes, will help in creating a more responsive and effective system to combat drug abuse.
