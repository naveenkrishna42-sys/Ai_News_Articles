‘He is pure history’: Scaloni hails Messi before World Cup final

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Scaloni described Messi as 'pure history' ahead of the final.
  • The coach criticised FIFA for 'strange' training arrangements.
  • Scaloni said fans' passion drives Argentina's desire for another title.

Argentina head coach Lionel Scaloni has paid a glowing tribute to captain Lionel Messi on the eve of the 2026 FIFA World Cup final, calling him “pure history”.

Speaking at a press conference on Saturday, Scaloni deflected questions about tactics and instead focused on the man who has defined an era of Argentine football.

“He is pure history. There is nothing more to say about him that hasn't been said,” Scaloni told reporters. “We are lucky to have him, and we enjoy every moment he is on the pitch.”

Fans drive Argentina’s hunger for another title

Scaloni also acknowledged the role of the Argentine supporters in pushing the squad forward. “The fans drive us to want another World Cup triumph,” he said.

Argentina are aiming to win back-to-back World Cups for the first time since 1990. The team has been buoyed by massive support both at home and in the host nation, with thousands of travelling fans expected at the stadium.

Scaloni said the players feel the weight of that expectation but are not burdened by it. “We know what it means to our people. That pushes us, it doesn't scare us.”

Scaloni slams FIFA over ‘strange’ training schedule

On a more contentious note, Scaloni criticised FIFA for forcing Argentina into what he called “strange” training arrangements ahead of the final.

“We have been given a training slot that doesn’t suit our preparation. It’s unusual for a team playing the final,” he said without elaborating on specifics. “We adapt, but it’s not ideal.”

His comments came after reports emerged that Argentina and Spain had been assigned late training sessions on the eve of the match, while other finalists were given prime slots. The Argentine camp believes the scheduling disrupts their recovery and tactical drills.

FIFA has not responded publicly to Scaloni’s remarks.

Spain also hold press conference ahead of final

Spain, Argentina’s opponents in the final, also held their pre-match press conference on Saturday. Their coach, Luis de la Fuente, focused on his team’s youth and tactical flexibility.

De la Fuente said his side would not be intimidated by Argentina’s experience or Messi’s brilliance. “We respect them deeply, but we have our own strengths,” he said.

The final, scheduled for Sunday at the Lusail Stadium, is expected to be a close contest. Argentina are slight favourites, but Spain’s recent form has impressed many.

Kick-off is set for 6 PM local time. Millions across the globe will watch as Messi attempts to add another World Cup winner’s medal to his collection.

For Scaloni, the message is simple: enjoy the moment. “We have Messi. We have a great team. And we have a dream. That is enough.”
