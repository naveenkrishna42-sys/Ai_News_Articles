Court upholds Jain's arrest in Delhi Jal Board corruption case

📅 2026-08-19 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Delhi court rejects Jain's plea challenging arrest in corruption case
  • Jain and four others sent to judicial custody in Delhi Jal Board case
  • Punjab election timeline may impact investigation progress

Delhi court upholds Jain's arrest

Delhi's Tis Hazari court on Wednesday rejected former Punjab minister Satyendar Jain's plea challenging his arrest in a corruption case related to the Delhi Jal Board tender. The court ordered Jain and four other accused to be sent to judicial custody.

Background of the case

Jain, a former Punjab minister, was arrested by the Delhi Anti-Corruption Branch (ACB) on August 15 in connection with a corruption case involving the Delhi Jal Board tender. The case pertains to alleged irregularities in the tendering process for the supply of water to Delhi.

Punjab election timeline

Jain's legal team argued that the arrest was politically motivated, citing the upcoming Punjab election. The court, however, dismissed this argument, stating that the investigation into the case was ongoing and that the Punjab election timeline would not affect the judicial process.

Reactions from political leaders

Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) leader Satyendar Jain expressed his displeasure with the arrest, stating that it was done because the Punjab election was coming up. He claimed that he was in jail when the tender was floated and that the arrest was an attempt to silence him.

Next steps in the case

The Delhi Jal Board tender case is expected to come up for hearing in the Delhi court on September 2. The court will decide whether to grant bail to Jain and the other accused. The outcome of the hearing will have significant implications for the ongoing investigation into the corruption case.
