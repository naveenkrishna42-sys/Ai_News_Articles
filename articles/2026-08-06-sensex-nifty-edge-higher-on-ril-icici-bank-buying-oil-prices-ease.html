Sensex, Nifty Edge Higher on RIL, ICICI Bank Buying; Oil Prices Ease

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty50 opened higher in early trade on Thursday.
  • Gains led by Reliance Industries and ICICI Bank, with RIL and SBI jumping nearly 3% each.
  • Lower crude oil prices and hopes of a US-Iran peace deal boosted market sentiment.
  • Sensex ended 374 points higher, while Nifty held above 24,600.

Indian equity benchmarks opened on a firm footing on Thursday, with the Sensex and Nifty50 edging higher in early trade. Buying in index heavyweights Reliance Industries and ICICI Bank provided the primary thrust, while a dip in global crude oil prices added to the positive mood on Dalal Street.

The market's upward move was also supported by growing expectations of a potential US-Iran peace deal, which traders believe could ease geopolitical tensions and cool energy prices further. The optimism helped offset concerns over domestic valuations and global monetary policy uncertainty.

Reliance, Banks Lead the Charge

Reliance Industries emerged as the biggest contributor to the Sensex's gains, with the stock surging nearly 3% in intraday trade. ICICI Bank also witnessed strong buying interest, while State Bank of India jumped close to 3%, lifting the banking and financial services pack.

The rally in these heavyweights helped the Sensex climb 374 points to close higher, while the Nifty50 managed to hold above the 24,600 mark. Market breadth remained positive, with advances outpacing declines on the BSE and NSE.

Oil Prices Ease, Sentiment Improves

A decline in crude oil prices was a key catalyst for the day's uptick. Lower energy costs are seen as a positive for India, a major importer of crude, as they help contain inflation and reduce pressure on the fiscal deficit. The softening in prices came amid reports of possible diplomatic progress between the US and Iran, which could potentially increase global oil supply.

Analysts noted that the combination of cheaper oil and hope for a de-escalation in West Asia provided a much-needed breather for equities, which had been range-bound over the past few sessions.

Broader Market and Sectoral Trends

Apart from Reliance and banking stocks, other sectors such as oil & gas and financial services also witnessed healthy buying. However, gains were not uniform across the board, with some IT and FMCG stocks facing mild profit booking.

The Nifty's ability to hold above the 24,600 level is being closely watched by traders. A sustained close above this mark could signal further upside in the near term, though volatility remains a concern given global cues.

What Lies Ahead

Market participants will now keep a close watch on global developments, particularly any concrete progress on the US-Iran talks and its impact on crude prices. Domestic institutional flows and quarterly earnings updates from corporates will also dictate the market's trajectory in the coming sessions.

For now, the mood is cautiously optimistic, with investors hoping that a stable global environment and easing input costs can keep the bull run intact.
