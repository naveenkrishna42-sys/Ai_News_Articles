Tulsi Gabbard's Pre-War Predictions

Tulsi Gabbard, a former Republican congresswoman and 2020 presidential candidate, has been vocal about her predictions regarding Iran's war course. She has been warning about the potential for conflict between Iran and the US for several years. Her predictions have been largely ignored by the Trump administration.

Trump's Response to Iran's Escalating Conflict

Trump's response to Iran's escalating conflict has been characterized by a series of miscalculations and a disregard for intelligence warnings. The Trump administration has been accused of ignoring warnings from intelligence agencies and advisors, leading to a situation where the US is now six months into a conflict with Iran.

Six Months of US Miscalculations in Iran

The six months of US miscalculations in Iran have had a significant impact on the region. The conflict has raised concerns about regional stability and the potential for a wider war. The US has been accused of being caught off guard by Iran's actions, leading to a situation where the US is now in a weaker position than it was before the conflict began.

Iran's Escalating Conflict

Iran's escalating conflict with the US has been characterized by a series of attacks on US interests and a disregard for international law. The conflict has raised concerns about the potential for a wider war and the impact on regional stability. The US has been accused of being caught off guard by Iran's actions, leading to a situation where the US is now in a weaker position than it was before the conflict began.
