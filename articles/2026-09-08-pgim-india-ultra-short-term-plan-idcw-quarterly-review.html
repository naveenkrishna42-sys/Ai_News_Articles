Overview of PGIM India Ultra Short Term Regular Plan-IDCW Quarterly

PGIM India Mutual Fund launched its Ultra Short Term Regular Plan with IDCW (Income Distribution Cum Capital Withdrawal) option, offering quarterly payouts to investors. Designed for conservative investors seeking regular income alongside modest capital growth, this debt-oriented scheme primarily invests in money market instruments and short-term debt securities with residual maturity of 91 days to 36 months. The fund maintains a target AUM (Assets Under Management) focus on liquid plus credit risk management, ensuring minimal volatility compared to longer-duration debt funds.

Key Features and Investment Strategy

Payout Frequency and Risk Profile

The IDCW Quarterly variant distributes accumulated income every three months, making it suitable for retirement planning or recurring expense coverage. It follows a low-duration approach, typically maintaining average portfolio duration between 6 months to 1 year, which reduces sensitivity to interest rate fluctuations. Risk is further mitigated through diversification across high-quality Government securities, AAA-rated corporate bonds, and Treasury bills.

Expense Ratio and Cost Efficiency

The Regular Plan carries an expense ratio of 0.38% per annum, slightly higher than direct plans but aligned with industry benchmarks for actively managed ultra short-term funds. This cost structure ensures operational sustainability without eroding net returns significantly. Compared to peer funds like ICICI Pru Ultra Short Term or HDFC Ultra Short Term, PGIM’s pricing remains competitive while offering transparent fee disclosure as mandated by SEBI regulations.

Performance Benchmarks and Returns Analysis

Historical Yield and Credit Risk Metrics

As of latest available data (Q2 2026), the scheme has delivered a trailing 1-year return of approximately 6.75% post-IDCW reinvestment adjustment. Its credit rating portfolio holds over 90% in AAA instruments, minimizing default probability. Portfolio yield averages around 7.2%, indicating consistent earnings generation despite quarterly payouts reducing compounding effect.

Liquidity and Exit Load Considerations

Investors can redeem units after a minimum holding period of 3 months without exit load. Early withdrawals before 3 months incur a 1% exit charge, discouraging speculative trading behavior. The fund supports SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) starting at ₹500/month, enhancing accessibility for retail investors aiming systematic wealth creation with income flow.
