Hyderabad's TGSRTC to roll out night buses on 6 routes

The Telangana State Road Transport Corporation (TGSRTC) is set to launch night bus services on six routes in Hyderabad. The services are expected to start in September 2026, aiming to reduce traffic congestion and improve public transport in the city.

Why night buses?

Night buses are a strategic move to address the increasing traffic congestion during peak hours. By providing a dedicated service for night commuters, TGSRTC hopes to alleviate the burden on the city's roads and reduce the number of private vehicles on the road.

Experts believe that night buses can significantly improve the quality of life for residents, especially those who work late hours. It can also help in reducing air pollution, as fewer vehicles on the road mean lower emissions.

Routes and impact

The six routes identified for the initial night bus services include:

  • Route 1: Gachibowli to Secunderabad
  • Route 2: Banjara Hills to LB Nagar
  • Route 3: HITEC City to KPHB
  • Route 4: Madhapur to Dilsukhnagar
  • Route 5: Uppal to Malakpet
  • Route 6: Ameerpet to Nampally

These routes have been chosen based on high demand and significant traffic congestion during night hours. The introduction of night buses is expected to provide a more efficient and comfortable travel option for commuters.

Challenges and considerations

While the launch of night buses is a positive step, there are several challenges that need to be addressed. One of the main concerns is the safety of passengers traveling late at night. TGSRTC will need to ensure that the buses are well-lit and that there are adequate security measures in place.

Another consideration is the cost of the service. Night buses require additional infrastructure and resources, which may increase the overall cost of public transport. However, experts believe that the benefits of improved public transport and reduced traffic congestion will outweigh the costs.

Broader impact

The introduction of night buses is not just a solution for Hyderabad but also a model that can be replicated in other cities facing similar challenges. It highlights the importance of innovative and sustainable solutions in addressing urban mobility issues.

By providing a dedicated service for night commuters, TGSRTC is taking a proactive approach to improving the quality of life in Hyderabad. The success of this initiative will depend on effective implementation and continuous evaluation to ensure it meets the needs of the community.
