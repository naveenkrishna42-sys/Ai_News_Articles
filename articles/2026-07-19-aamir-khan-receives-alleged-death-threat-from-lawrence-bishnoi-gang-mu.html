Aamir Khan receives alleged death threat from Lawrence Bishnoi gang, Mumbai police verify social media post

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Aamir Khan allegedly received a death threat from the Lawrence Bishnoi gang via a social media post.
  • The post warns Khan against 'promoting love jihad' and threatens to 'choke the breath' of the actor.
  • Mumbai police are verifying the authenticity of the threat; no arrests have been made yet.

Threat surfaces on social media

Mumbai police are verifying a social media post in which the Lawrence Bishnoi gang allegedly issued a death threat to actor Aamir Khan. The post, which surfaced late Friday night, warns Khan against what it calls 'promoting love jihad' and threatens to 'choke the breath' of the actor.

Police sources told TIVRA News that a preliminary inquiry has been launched. The cyber cell is tracing the origin of the post, which was shared on an anonymous account. Officials have not yet confirmed the authenticity of the threat.

Police investigation underway

Security at Aamir Khan's Mumbai residence has been tightened, sources confirmed. The actor is currently in the city and has not issued a public statement on the matter. A team from the Bandra police station is coordinating with the cyber cell.

This is not the first time the Lawrence Bishnoi gang has targeted a Bollywood figure. In recent years, the gang has been linked to threats against Salman Khan in connection with the 1998 blackbuck poaching case. However, the motive in Aamir Khan's case appears to be different.

Gang's alleged history

The Lawrence Bishnoi gang, active in northern and western India, is known for its involvement in extortion and contract killings. The gang has previously claimed responsibility for threatening statements against several public figures over issues ranging from wildlife conservation to religious sentiments.

Police are examining if the threat against Aamir Khan is linked to his recent comments on interfaith marriages. The actor has been a vocal advocate for secularism, which has drawn criticism from some right-wing groups in the past.

What happens next

Mumbai police will likely file a formal complaint and register an FIR if the threat is verified. Aamir Khan's security detail may be upgraded to a higher category in the interim. The investigation remains in its early stages.
