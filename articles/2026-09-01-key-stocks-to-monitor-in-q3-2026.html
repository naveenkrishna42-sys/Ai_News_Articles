Key stocks to monitor in Q3 2026

📅 2026-09-01 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • ITC's FMCG segment shows resilience despite economic challenges
  • Happiest Minds' strong Q2 earnings signal growth in IT services
  • ICICI Bank's digital banking initiatives could drive future growth
  • PVR Inox's expansion plans and strong multiplex performance are promising
  • Cipla's focus on generics could boost market share

ITC: A Beacon of Stability in FMCG

ITC has consistently outperformed in the FMCG sector, with its Q2 earnings reflecting a 12% growth over the previous quarter. The company's diversified portfolio, which includes brands like Aashirvaad, Sunfeast, and Vedica, has helped it weather economic storms. Analysts attribute this resilience to ITC's strong brand equity and strategic acquisitions. However, the company's exposure to inflationary pressures in the food and beverages segment could pose challenges in the coming quarters.

Happiest Minds: IT Services Sector's Bright Spot

Happiest Minds Technologies has been a standout performer in the IT services sector. The company's Q2 earnings, up 18% year-on-year, are a testament to its strong client base and robust project pipeline. The IT services sector, which has been a growth engine for the Indian economy, is expected to continue its upward trajectory, barring any unforeseen disruptions.

ICICI Bank: Digital Banking as the Future

ICICI Bank has been at the forefront of digital banking in India. The bank's Q2 earnings, up 15% year-on-year, reflect the growing adoption of digital banking services. ICICI Bank's focus on fintech and digital banking could drive its growth in the coming quarters. However, the bank's exposure to interest rate risks could pose challenges in the long run.

PVR Inox: The Future of Entertainment

PVR Inox has been a consistent performer in the entertainment sector. The company's Q2 earnings, up 10% year-on-year, reflect its strong multiplex performance and robust ticket sales. PVR Inox's expansion plans, including the opening of new multiplexes and the introduction of new formats, could drive its growth in the coming quarters. However, the company's exposure to inflationary pressures in the entertainment sector could pose challenges.

Cipla: The Future of Generics

Cipla has been a consistent performer in the pharmaceutical sector. The company's Q2 earnings, up 8% year-on-year, reflect its strong performance in the generics segment. Cipla's focus on generics, which account for a significant portion of its revenue, could drive its growth in the coming quarters. However, the company's exposure to regulatory risks and patent expiry could pose challenges.
