OnePlus 16 design leak: ultra-thin bezels, refined rear ahead of October launch

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • OnePlus 16 design leak reveals ultra-thin bezels and a refined rear camera layout.
  • Device is tipped for an October launch, marking the first flagship after OnePlus's US exit.
  • Leaks suggest a 9,000mAh battery and Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 Pro SoC.

First look at the OnePlus 16

Leaked renders of the OnePlus 16 have surfaced, giving the clearest picture yet of the company's next flagship. The images, published by Android Authority and corroborated by multiple outlets, show a device with noticeably thinner bezels than its predecessor. The rear panel has also been refined, though the exact changes remain subtle in the leaked material.

The OnePlus 16 is expected to launch in October, according to reports from Gizmochina and GSMArena. This would be the company's first major flagship release after its exit from the US market earlier this year. OnePlus had announced it would focus on markets in Asia and Europe, where its brand presence remains strong.

Ultra-thin bezels and a refined rear design

The most striking change in the leaked design is the reduction in bezel size. The OnePlus 16 appears to have nearly uniform bezels around the display, a look that rivals current flagships from Samsung and Xiaomi. The front camera punch-hole has also been slightly moved, though its position is not yet confirmed.

On the back, the camera module has been tweaked. The housing appears more integrated into the phone's body, with a cleaner layout. Some leaks suggest the design borrows cues from the Oppo Find X10 series, which itself has been compared to the iPhone 17 Pro's rear panel. OnePlus and Oppo share development resources, so design overlaps are not surprising.

Battery and processor: big numbers

Reports from Gadgets 360 and other sources indicate the OnePlus 16 will pack a 9,000mAh battery. That would be a significant jump from the OnePlus 13's 6,000mAh cell. The device is also tipped to run on the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 Pro SoC, Qualcomm's next top-tier chip.

These specs, if accurate, would position the OnePlus 16 as a performance-focused device. The larger battery suggests the company is prioritising endurance, a key selling point in markets where users often rely on a single phone for long hours.

What the US exit means for the OnePlus 16

OnePlus's decision to exit the US market earlier this year was a strategic shift. The company had struggled to gain significant market share against Apple and Samsung in the premium segment. By focusing on India, China, and parts of Europe, OnePlus can tailor its devices more closely to local preferences.

The OnePlus 16 will be the first flagship to launch entirely without a US presence. This could allow the company to experiment with design and features that might not have worked in the American market. For instance, the massive 9,000mAh battery might have been considered too bulky for US buyers, but it could be a major draw in Asia.

What to watch next

OnePlus has not confirmed any of these leaks. The company typically releases its flagship models in the fourth quarter, so an October launch is plausible. More details are expected to trickle out over the coming weeks, including camera specs and pricing.
