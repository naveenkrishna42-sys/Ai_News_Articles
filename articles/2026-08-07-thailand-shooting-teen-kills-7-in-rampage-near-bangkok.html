Thailand shooting: Teen kills 7 in rampage near Bangkok

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Seven people were killed in a shooting rampage by a teen at a home and a school near Bangkok.
  • The suspect, described as 'under stress', reportedly planned the attack, according to Thai PM.
  • The gunman shot himself after the rampage; authorities have not confirmed further details.
  • A pupil described the harrowing moment a teacher was shot in front of her.

Attack Unfolds Across Two Locations

A teenage gunman killed seven people in a shooting rampage that spanned a private home and a school near Bangkok, Thai authorities said Friday. The suspect, whose identity has not been officially released, opened fire at both locations before turning the weapon on himself.

Police have not yet confirmed the exact sequence of events, but local reports suggest the attack began at a residence before moving to the school premises. The motive remains unclear, though officials have indicated the teen was under significant stress.

PM Says Attack Was Planned

Thai Prime Minister, speaking to reporters, described the suspect as 'under stress' and said the attack appeared to have been premeditated. The premier's remarks came as investigators combed through the suspect's background and possible digital footprint.

Authorities have not disclosed the ages of the victims or the total number of injured, if any. Emergency crews were dispatched to both scenes, and a local school has been temporarily closed as a precaution.

Witness Recounts Horror

One pupil, who spoke to BBC News, described the harrowing moment a teacher was shot in front of her. 'It happened so fast. We were in class, and then everything changed,' the student said, her voice trembling.

Such accounts have shaken the community, which is unaccustomed to violence of this scale. Thailand has seen occasional mass shootings, but this incident, occurring near the capital, has drawn national attention.

Official Response and Investigation

Local police have cordoned off the affected areas and are conducting a thorough investigation. Forensic teams are on site, and officials are working to establish a timeline of the suspect's movements.

Prime Minister has ordered a review of school safety protocols and urged calm. 'We must understand what led to this tragedy, and we must prevent it from happening again,' he said in a statement.

The suspect, after shooting himself, was taken to a hospital. His condition has not been officially confirmed, but initial reports suggest he sustained critical injuries.

As the investigation unfolds, questions are emerging about access to firearms and mental health support for young people. Thailand has relatively strict gun laws, but illegal weapons remain a concern.

Community in Mourning

Flags have been lowered at government buildings in the province, and a makeshift memorial has appeared near the school. Residents have gathered to pay respects, leaving flowers and notes.

Counselors have been deployed to the school to support grieving students and staff. The education ministry has announced it will provide additional resources to help the community cope with the trauma.

Officials have yet to confirm the full list of victims, pending family notifications. The coming days will likely bring more details as the investigation progresses and the suspect's background is examined.

This attack serves as a grim reminder of the fragility of safety in everyday spaces. The nation now watches closely as authorities work to piece together what drove a teenager to such violence.
