Sonu Nigam sings Mohammed Rafi classic during surgery, shares video

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sonu Nigam sang Mohammed Rafi's classic 'Suhani Raat Dhal Chuki' during a surgical procedure.
  • He shared a video of the impromptu performance with a heartfelt note about music's healing power.
  • The singer's act has gone viral, drawing praise from fans and fellow musicians.

The impromptu performance

Playback singer Sonu Nigam turned a routine medical procedure into an unexpected stage, crooning Mohammed Rafi's timeless classic 'Suhani Raat Dhal Chuki' while undergoing surgery. A video of the moment, shared by the singer himself, shows him humming the romantic number even as medical staff work around him.

The clip, now circulating widely on social media, captures Nigam's unmistakable voice filling the operation theatre. Despite the clinical setting, the singer appeared composed, delivering the melody with the same emotive depth that has defined his decades-long career.

A note on the joy of music

Along with the video, Nigam posted a heartfelt message reflecting on what he called 'the joy of music'. He described how the song emerged naturally, almost as a reflex, even in a moment of physical vulnerability.

Fans were quick to respond, with many praising his dedication to the craft. Some noted that singing through a procedure was a testament to how deeply music is woven into his identity. The post has drawn thousands of reactions within hours.

Rafi's enduring legacy

'Suhani Raat Dhal Chuki', originally sung by Mohammed Rafi for the 1949 film 'Dulari', remains one of Hindi cinema's most beloved melodies. Its choice by Nigam is significant, given his long-standing admiration for Rafi, whom he has often cited as a major influence.

Nigam has frequently paid tribute to Rafi in concerts and recordings, and this latest homage adds a uniquely personal chapter to that history. The song's gentle, romantic cadence seemed an unlikely yet fitting companion to the sterile ambience of an operation theatre.

Social media reaction

The video has sparked a range of reactions, from amusement to admiration. Some users joked about requesting songs during their own procedures, while others marvelled at Nigam's ability to stay in tune while under medical care.

Medical professionals who commented noted that patients often use music or conversation to manage anxiety during procedures. Nigam's spontaneous singing, they said, appeared to be a natural coping mechanism that also entertained those present.

What to watch

As the video continues to trend, fans are eagerly awaiting Nigam's next public appearance, where he may address the incident directly. The singer has not yet confirmed whether the surgery was planned or what it involved, but his light-hearted approach suggests he is in good spirits.

For now, the moment stands as a reminder of how art can surface in the most unexpected places. Whether this becomes a recurring feature of his medical visits remains to be seen, but the internet is already hoping for an encore.
