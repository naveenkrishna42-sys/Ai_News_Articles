Mamata Banerjee's convoy attacked with stones and mud in North 24 Parganas

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mamata Banerjee's convoy attacked with stones, mud, and shoes in North 24 Parganas.
  • Incident occurred during her visit to the residence of a Trinamool Congress worker who died in police custody.
  • Opposition leader Suvendu Adhikari claims the protest reflects public anger; TMC has not yet issued an official statement.
  • No injuries reported, but Banerjee said she 'could have been killed' in the attack.

Attack on the Convoy

West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee's convoy came under attack on Sunday in North 24 Parganas district, with stones, mud, and even shoes thrown at her vehicle. The incident took place as she was heading to the residence of a Trinamool Congress (TMC) worker who had died in police custody.

Banerjee, who was in the car at the time, later said she "could have been killed" in the attack. No injuries have been reported so far, but the vehicles in the convoy were smeared with mud and pelted with stones, leaving the security apparatus on alert.

Visit to the Deceased Worker's Family

The chief minister was on a visit to express condolences to the family of the TMC worker, whose death in police custody has triggered local outrage. The protest, however, turned violent as a group of people surrounded the convoy and began throwing objects.

Banerjee's visit was intended to reassure the family and the local community, but the hostile reception underscored the tense atmosphere in the area. The exact sequence of events leading to the attack is still being pieced together, and officials have not yet confirmed how many people were involved or whether any arrests have been made.

Political Reactions

The attack has drawn sharp reactions from political quarters. Suvendu Adhikari, the Leader of the Opposition in the West Bengal Assembly, claimed the protest reflected "public anger" against the state government. His remarks have added fuel to an already charged political climate in the state.

The Trinamool Congress has not yet issued an official statement on the incident, but party workers are expected to hold protests in response. The state police have been put on high alert in the region to prevent any further escalation.

Security Concerns and Aftermath

The incident raises serious questions about the security arrangements for the chief minister, especially during visits to sensitive areas. Banerjee's convoy is always accompanied by a heavy security detail, but the attack suggests that crowd control measures may have been inadequate.

Local residents in the area have expressed mixed feelings—some condemn the attack, while others say it reflects deep-seated grievances. The administration has promised a thorough inquiry into the incident, though details of the investigation have not been made public yet.

What Next

With tensions running high, the focus now shifts to how the state government and the opposition will navigate the fallout. A full statement from the TMC leadership is expected soon, and security around the chief minister is likely to be beefed up for future engagements. The coming days will reveal whether this incident leads to a political flashpoint or a cooling-off period in the region.
