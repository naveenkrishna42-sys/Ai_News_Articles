Spider-Man: Brand New Day breaks records in India, crosses Rs 42 crore on opening day

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day crosses Rs 42 crore on day one in India
  • Film becomes biggest Spider-Man opener in the country
  • US box office sees record $167 million opening day, $72 million in previews
  • Tom Holland starrer outperforms major Bollywood releases like Pathaan

Record-breaking start in India

Spider-Man: Brand New Day has swung into Indian theatres with unprecedented force. The Tom Holland-starrer collected over Rs 42 crore on its opening day, Friday, making it the biggest Spider-Man opener in the country. The film has surpassed all previous entries in the franchise, including the massive hits from the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Trade analysts point to the strong advance bookings and the star power of Holland as key drivers. The film's release was accompanied by a massive marketing push, and the response from fans in metro cities has been particularly enthusiastic.

Beating Bollywood blockbusters

The opening day collection has also outpaced several major Bollywood releases. According to early estimates, Spider-Man: Brand New Day has beaten the first-day numbers of films like Pathaan and Dhurandhar, which had set their own benchmarks upon release.

This is notable because Hindi film industry releases typically dominate the domestic box office. A Hollywood superhero film leading the charts on a non-holiday Friday signals a shift in audience preference, especially among younger viewers.

US box office sets new benchmarks

The film's performance is not limited to India. In the United States, Spider-Man: Brand New Day opened to a staggering $167 million on its first day, setting a record for the highest single-day gross in the franchise. The preview shows alone raked in $72 million, beating the previous record held by Avengers: Endgame.

These numbers suggest that the film is on track to have one of the biggest global openings in recent years. The combination of nostalgia, multiverse storytelling, and Holland's appeal appears to have struck a chord with audiences worldwide.

What this means for the box office

The success of Spider-Man: Brand New Day comes at a time when theatres are still recovering from the pandemic-era slump. The film's performance is being closely watched by exhibitors and distributors as a sign of the health of the theatrical business.

In India, the film is expected to continue its strong run through the weekend, with Sunday likely to see even higher numbers. The lack of major competing releases in the coming week could help the film sustain its momentum.

As the weekend progresses, all eyes will be on whether Spider-Man: Brand New Day can maintain its record-breaking pace and emerge as one of the highest-grossing Hollywood films in India. Early trends suggest that the wall-crawler is here to stay.
