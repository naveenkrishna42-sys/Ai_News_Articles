What reviewers generally say: Generally well-received by tech reviewers for its elite gaming performance and significant camera upgrades compared to its predecessor.

Pros
  • Industry-leading performance with the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3
  • Versatile camera setup including a high-resolution telephoto lens
  • Extremely fast 120W wired charging support
Cons
  • Bloatware remains a persistent issue in the software experience
  • Lack of wireless charging compared to other premium flagships

The iQOO 12 is a compelling proposition for mobile gamers and power users who prioritize raw performance and sustained gaming stability above everything else. The inclusion of a periscope lens finally makes it a competitive all-rounder for photography.

However, users who prefer a clean, bloatware-free software experience or require convenience features like wireless charging should look elsewhere. Skip this if you are a casual user who doesn't need flagship-grade processing power.
