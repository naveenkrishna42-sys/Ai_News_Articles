Context: A Strategic Diplomatic Move

Prime Minister Narendra Modi is embarking on a four-day visit to Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan, marking his first trip to Uzbekistan since 2018. This visit is part of a broader regional diplomacy strategy aimed at strengthening India's ties with key partners, particularly Russia, and enhancing India's influence in the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO).

Background: SCO Summit and Beyond

The SCO summit in Uzbekistan is a significant event, as it will see the expansion of the organisation to include six new members, including India. This expansion is seen as a major step towards India's integration into the global multilateral system. The summit will also see the election of new SCO leaders, with India likely to play a key role in shaping the organisation's future direction.

Beyond the SCO, PM Modi's visit is also expected to focus on the upcoming BRICS Summit and the election of a new member to the United Nations Security Council (UNSC). These events are critical for India's global standing and will provide PM Modi with an opportunity to showcase India's growing influence on the world stage.

Why It Matters: Strengthening Ties and Boosting Influence

The visit is crucial for strengthening India's ties with Russia and other SCO members. It will provide an opportunity for PM Modi to meet with Russian President Vladimir Putin, who is expected to attend the SCO summit. This meeting is expected to strengthen the strategic partnership between India and Russia, which has been a cornerstone of India's foreign policy.

Moreover, the visit is significant for India's efforts to enhance its influence in the SCO and beyond. The expansion of the SCO and the upcoming BRICS Summit and UNSC election are key focuses of the visit, and PM Modi's active participation will be crucial in shaping the outcomes of these events.

What to Watch: Key Focus Areas

There are several key focus areas to watch during PM Modi's visit. Firstly, the SCO summit will be a critical event, with the expansion of the organisation and the election of new leaders expected to shape the future of the SCO. Secondly, the meeting with Russian President Putin will be crucial for strengthening the strategic partnership between India and Russia. Finally, the BRICS Summit and the UNSC election will provide PM Modi with an opportunity to showcase India's growing influence on the world stage.

In addition to these key focus areas, the visit will also provide an opportunity for PM Modi to engage with young leaders from Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan. The Prime Minister has expressed happiness at the enthusiasm of young friends from Uzbekistan, indicating that the visit is expected to be a successful one.
