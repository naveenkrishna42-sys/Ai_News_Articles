Raveena keeps cool as dog tugs her outfit on Mumbai red carpet

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Raveena Tandon attended a Mumbai event on Friday evening.
  • A dog playfully grabbed her outfit during the red carpet moment.
  • The actress stayed calm and smiled through the incident.

Mumbai witnessed a light-hearted moment on Friday evening when Bollywood actress Raveena Tandon encountered an unexpected fashion mishap on the red carpet. A dog, apparently drawn to her attire, playfully grabbed hold of her outfit, creating a brief moment of surprise for onlookers and photographers alike.

The incident took place at a city event where Tandon was posing for photographs. As she struck a pose, a dog approached her and latched onto part of her clothing. Instead of showing any sign of irritation, the actress remained composed, letting the moment pass with a smile.

A calm response wins hearts

Eyewitnesses said Tandon did not flinch or attempt to shoo the dog away. She gently adjusted her outfit once the animal let go, and went on with her red carpet duties as if nothing had happened. Her composed reaction quickly became the talking point among those present at the venue.

Social media was abuzz within minutes, with fans praising the actress for her poise. Many noted that her handling of the situation reflected her known love for animals, a trait she has displayed on multiple occasions in the past.

Not the first animal encounter

Tandon has often spoken about her affection for pets and stray animals. Over the years, she has been spotted with dogs at various events and has used her public platform to advocate for animal welfare. Friday's incident, however, was unusual in that the dog was not her own but a stray that wandered onto the red carpet.

Organisers of the event did not immediately comment on the incident. It remains unclear whether the dog was a regular visitor to the venue or had strayed in from the surrounding area. Security personnel at the venue were seen gently guiding the animal away after the moment passed.

Fans react with warmth

Clips and photos from the red carpet circulated widely on social media platforms, drawing reactions ranging from amusement to admiration. Some users joked about the dog having good fashion sense, while others commended Tandon for her graceful handling of an unpredictable situation.

This is not the first time a celebrity has had an unscripted moment with an animal at a public event, but Tandon's calm demeanour stood out. In an industry often marked by tightly controlled appearances, such spontaneous moments tend to resonate with audiences.

What to watch next

As the event concluded without further drama, attention now turns to whether Tandon will address the incident in her upcoming public appearances. Given her history of engaging warmly with fans and the media, a light-hearted comment or post on social media would not be surprising.

For now, the red carpet moment serves as a reminder that even the most polished events can have unplanned, endearing interruptions.
