Star-Studded Funeral for Kuku Kohli

Kuku Kohli's funeral on August 26, 2026, was a poignant reminder of his enduring legacy in Bollywood. The event drew a star-studded crowd, with Katrina Kaif and Vicky Kaushal among those paying their last respects. The presence of such high-profile figures underscores Kohli's significant influence on the film industry.

Aruna Irani's Emotional Tribute

Aruna Irani, Kohli's ex-wife, was inconsolable at the funeral, her emotional state reflecting the deep personal connection she shared with him. Irani's public statements about their marriage, including her critique of Kohli's decision to marry a married woman, have added a layer of complexity to the narrative surrounding his life and career.

Industry Figures Pay Tribute

Ajay Devgn, who launched Kohli's career, delivered an emotional tribute, expressing his gratitude for the director's mentorship. Devgn's words highlighted Kohli's role in shaping the careers of many aspiring filmmakers. Other industry figures also shared their condolences, reflecting on Kohli's contributions to Bollywood.

Kohli's Legacy in Bollywood

Kuku Kohli's death at the age of 77 has left a void in Bollywood. Known for his work on films like 'Phool Aur Kaante', Kohli was a pioneer in the industry. His funeral serves as a tribute to his enduring impact on the film industry.

Key Facts About Kuku Kohli

  • Born on January 1, 1949, in Mumbai
  • Launched the careers of many Bollywood actors and directors
  • Known for his innovative approach to filmmaking
  • Directed over 50 films in his career
  • Received numerous awards for his contributions to Indian cinema
