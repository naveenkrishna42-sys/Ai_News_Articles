Sunny Deol Dedicates Batwara 1947 To Mothers Ahead Of Trailer Launch

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sunny Deol dedicated Batwara 1947 to his mother Prakash Kaur and all mothers worldwide.
  • The film has been cleared by the CBFC with an A certificate and no cuts.
  • Trailer launch events scheduled at Jaipur's Raj Mandir cinema and Mumbai on July 28 and 29.

Son's Tribute To A Mother's Strength

Sunny Deol has dedicated his upcoming film Batwara 1947 to his mother, Prakash Kaur, and to mothers everywhere. The actor shared a photograph with her on social media, calling the film a tribute to the resilience of mothers during the Partition.

The post came just days before the film's trailer launch, scheduled for July 28 at Jaipur's iconic Raj Mandir cinema, followed by a Mumbai event on July 29.

CBFC Clears Film Without A Single Cut

The Central Board of Film Certification has given Batwara 1947 an A certificate — but without demanding a single cut. Sources close to the production told news outlets that the board cleared the film completely, a rare feat for a period drama dealing with the violence of Partition.

Sunny Deol expressed delight at the development. The A certificate restricts the film to adult audiences, but the lack of cuts suggests the makers have stayed true to their vision.

Trailer To Drop At Raj Mandir

The first trailer of Batwara 1947 will be unveiled at Jaipur's Raj Mandir cinema, one of India's last surviving single-screen icons. A second teaser has already been released ahead of the film's August debut.

The Mumbai event the following day is expected to draw industry heavyweights. The film is produced by Sunny Deol and directed by Rajkumar Santoshi, marking their reunion after several years.

A Story Of Partition And Family

Batwara 1947 is set against the backdrop of India's Partition, a subject Sunny Deol has said is close to his heart. In his social media post, he wrote that the film is not just his story but the story of every mother who lived through those times.

Prakash Kaur, the actor's mother, has been a quiet but steady presence in his life. The dedication is widely seen as a personal gesture, acknowledging her role in shaping his career and values.

The film also stars actors like Arshad Warsi and R. Madhavan in key roles. Details of the plot remain under wraps, but the teaser hints at a family torn apart by the division of the subcontinent.

What To Watch For

The trailer launch at Raj Mandir is expected to generate significant buzz, especially among Sunny Deol's fanbase. With the CBFC clearance already secured, the film is on track for its August release. Whether the dedication to mothers will resonate beyond the screen remains to be seen.
