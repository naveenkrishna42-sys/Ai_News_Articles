Wall Street tumbles as yields rise, Walmart results disappoint

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Stocks fall sharply as Treasury yields rise
  • Walmart's earnings miss expectations, sending its stock tumbling
  • Investors react to potential Treasury buybacks exceeding $4 billion

Stocks plunge as Treasury yields surge

Wall Street experienced a steep sell-off on Thursday as Treasury yields rose, sending stocks tumbling. Stock futures were little changed after the market opened, but the sharp decline in yields had already sparked a significant drop in stock prices. Investors are reacting to the potential for Treasury buybacks to exceed $4 billion, which could further impact market sentiment.

Walmart's earnings miss expectations

Walmart's quarterly earnings report disappointed investors, with the company's revenue and profit falling short of expectations. The retail giant's stock price dropped significantly after the announcement, adding to the market's downward momentum. Analysts are now scrutinizing Walmart's financial performance and its plans for future growth.

Investors react to potential Treasury buybacks

Investors are closely watching the possibility of Treasury buybacks exceeding $4 billion. The potential for the government to repurchase its own bonds could have significant implications for the market, as it would effectively increase the supply of Treasury securities. This could lead to further declines in bond yields and put additional pressure on the stock market.

Market wrap: Oil prices, Bitcoin rise

Oil prices rose on Thursday, adding to the market's volatility. Meanwhile, Bitcoin prices also surged, reflecting the overall uncertainty in the market. Investors are closely monitoring these developments, as they could have a significant impact on the broader economy and financial markets.
