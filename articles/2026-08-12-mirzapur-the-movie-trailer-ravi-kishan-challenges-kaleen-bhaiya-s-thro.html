Mirzapur The Movie trailer: Ravi Kishan challenges Kaleen Bhaiya's throne

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Trailer of Mirzapur The Movie released, featuring Pankaj Tripathi, Ali Fazal and Ravi Kishan.
  • Ravi Kishan's character emerges as a new challenger to Kaleen Bhaiya's rule.
  • Farhan Akhtar says theatrical release is driven by fan demand.

The much-awaited trailer of Mirzapur: The Movie is out, and it promises exactly what fans of the series have come to expect: power, bloodshed and revenge. Pankaj Tripathi returns as the formidable Kaleen Bhaiya, but this time, he has a new rival eyeing his throne — Ravi Kishan.

The film, which continues the story of the hugely popular Amazon Prime Video series, brings back familiar faces. Ali Fazal's Guddu and the ever-volatile Munna are back, and the trailer suggests they are ready to bring the 'bhaukaal' once again.

A new player in the game

Ravi Kishan's entry into the Mirzapur universe is the biggest talking point of the trailer. His character appears to be a politician or a strongman with ambitions of his own, and he is not shy about challenging Kaleen Bhaiya's supremacy. The trailer shows him in several confrontational scenes, hinting at a power struggle that could tear the city apart.

For those who have followed the series, the film's plot seems to pick up from where season two ended. The throne of Mirzapur has always been contested, and now with more players in the fray, the battle for control is only getting fiercer.

Fan-driven theatrical release

Producer Farhan Akhtar has revealed that the decision to release the film in theatres was driven by the fans. "It is really the fans," he said, explaining that the overwhelming response to the series made a big-screen release inevitable. The film aims to give the audience a larger-than-life experience that the small screen could not fully capture.

The theatrical release also marks a shift for the franchise, which began as a streaming series. With the movie, the makers are betting on the franchise's ability to pull crowds to cinemas, something that is rarely attempted for web series spin-offs in India.

What the trailer shows

The trailer is packed with action sequences, dramatic dialogues and the gritty, violent aesthetic that defines Mirzapur. Kaleen Bhaiya is seen in his trademark style — calm, calculated and ruthless. Guddu, on the other hand, appears more aggressive, driven by revenge and a thirst for power.

Munna's character, played by Divyenndu, also gets significant screen time in the trailer. His rivalry with Guddu is expected to be a central plot point. The trailer also introduces new characters, but their roles remain under wraps.

Ravi Kishan's character, though new, seems to be a game-changer. His presence in the trailer is menacing, and his dialogue delivery suggests he is here to stay. Whether he succeeds in dethroning Kaleen Bhaiya is a question the film will answer.

What to expect

The film is directed by Gurmmeet Singh and written by Puneet Krishna, who have been part of the series from the start. This continuity should reassure fans that the movie will stay true to the show's tone and storytelling style.

With the trailer setting high expectations, the film is likely to be one of the biggest releases of the year. The makers have not yet announced an exact release date, but reports suggest it will hit theatres later this year.

As the release date approaches, more details about the plot and the new characters are expected to emerge. For now, fans have the trailer to dissect, and it has already sparked plenty of speculation online.
