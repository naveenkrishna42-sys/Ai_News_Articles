Galaxy Z Flip8 gets launch discount; Z Fold8 and Ultra also on sale

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Galaxy Z Flip8 receives a discount immediately after launch.
  • Z Fold8 and Z Fold8 Ultra are also on sale in India.
  • Samsung officially launched the new foldables alongside Watch Ultra2 and Watch9.
  • Reports suggest the Z Fold8 series may have crossed 5 million preorders globally.

Samsung's latest foldable lineup has barely hit store shelves, and already the deals are rolling in. The Galaxy Z Flip8, Z Fold8, and the new Z Fold8 Ultra are all seeing immediate price cuts in India, a move that's become a familiar pattern for the company's premium devices.

The launch, which also included the Galaxy Watch Ultra2 and Watch9, was confirmed earlier this week. Now, early buyers are being offered discounts that shave a chunk off the retail price, making the new hardware a bit more accessible for those who don't want to wait for the usual festive-season sales.

Discounts out of the gate

According to deal trackers, the Galaxy Z Flip8 is the standout, with a discount that applies right at the point of purchase. The Z Fold8 and Z Fold8 Ultra are also part of the promotion, though the exact amounts vary depending on the retailer and the storage variant chosen.

This isn't an isolated flash sale or a limited-period offer tied to a bank card. The markdowns appear to be straight price reductions, which suggests Samsung is keen to keep momentum going after a strong preorder phase.

Record preorders hint at strong demand

Industry watchers have pointed to reports that the Z Fold8 series may have crossed 5 million preorders globally, a figure that would set a new record for the company's foldable line. Samsung hasn't officially confirmed the number, but the buzz is hard to ignore.

In India, the phones went on sale through both online and offline channels, and early reviews have been positive. The Indian Express, in its review of the Z Fold8, called it a 'pocket phone' that actually fits in jeans, a nod to the slimmer hinge design and reduced thickness compared to earlier models.

What the new lineup offers

The Z Fold8 Ultra is the newest addition, slotting in above the standard Z Fold8 with a larger cover display and beefed-up camera hardware. The Z Flip8 continues the clamshell tradition with a focus on portability and a more refined outer screen.

Alongside the phones, Samsung also launched the Watch Ultra2 and Watch9, but the foldables are clearly the headline act. The combination of early discounts and strong reviews could keep the sales curve climbing well past the initial launch window.

For buyers in India, the discounts bring the starting price of the Z Flip8 down noticeably, while the Z Fold8 and Z Fold8 Ultra remain premium-priced but now slightly less daunting. Retailers are also bundling exchange offers and no-cost EMI options, which could further sweeten the deal.

Looking ahead

With the launch discounts now live, the next few weeks will show whether Samsung can sustain the preorder momentum. The company is likely to watch early sales data closely before deciding on any further price adjustments during the upcoming festive season.

For now, anyone eyeing a foldable has a rare chance to grab one at a discount straight out of the gate. Whether the deals last or are pulled once stock runs low remains to be seen.
