Ravi Kishan's Spain remark sparks political storm

Ravi Kishan, a prominent figure in Indian cricket and politics, recently made a remark comparing Gorakhpur, a city in Uttar Pradesh, to Spain. This remark has sparked a political storm, with Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath reacting with amusement to the comparison.

Background of the remark

The remark was made during a public event where Ravi Kishan was discussing the development of Gorakhpur. He said that Gorakhpur is a city that has a lot to offer and is similar to Spain in terms of its culture and development. This remark was met with mixed reactions from the public and political leaders.

Reaction from Yogi Adityanath

Yogi Adityanath, the Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh, reacted to the remark by saying that he found it amusing. He said that the remark was a light-hearted one and that he did not take it seriously. However, he also said that the development of Gorakhpur is a matter of concern for the state government.

Political sensitivity of the issue

The remark highlights the political sensitivity of issues related to Gorakhpur. Gorakhpur has been a hotspot for political tensions in Uttar Pradesh, with issues related to law and order, development, and politics often being discussed in the media. The remark by Ravi Kishan has added to the political tension in the state.

Public reaction to the remark

The public reaction to the remark has been mixed. While some people have found the remark amusing and light-hearted, others have criticized it for being insensitive to the issues related to Gorakhpur. The remark has also sparked a meme fest on social media, with netizens brutally trolling Ravi Kishan for his remark.
