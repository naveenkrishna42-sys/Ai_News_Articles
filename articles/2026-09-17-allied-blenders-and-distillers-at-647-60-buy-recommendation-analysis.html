Company Overview

Allied Blenders and Distillers Ltd (ABD) is a prominent Indian manufacturer of Indian-made foreign liquor (IMFL) with a diversified portfolio spanning whisky, rum, brandy and vodka. The company’s flagship brand, Officer’s Choice, remains one of the highest‑selling whisky labels in the country, while newer premium offerings such as Officer’s Choice Black and Sundance have gained traction in urban markets. ABD’s distribution network covers more than 28 states and reaches over 1.2 million retail outlets, providing a broad base for volume growth.

Fundamental Performance

In FY25 ABD posted revenue of ₹9,850 crore, representing a 12.4% year‑on‑year increase. Growth was driven by higher sales of its core IMFL brands and a successful launch of premium variants in metro cities. EBITDA rose to ₹1,830 crore, expanding the margin from 16.9% in FY24 to 18.6% in FY25, aided by favourable product mix, operating leverage and cost‑saving initiatives in logistics. Net profit stood at ₹620 crore, translating to a return on equity (ROE) of 15.8% and a return on capital employed (ROCE) of 14.2%.

Valuation and Technical Metrics

At the quoted price of ₹647.60, the stock commands a trailing price‑to‑earnings (P/E) ratio of 22.4× and an enterprise value‑to‑EBITDA (EV/EBITDA) of 13.1×. These multiples are modestly below the five‑year averages for the Indian alcohol sector, which stand at approximately 24.8× P/E and 14.5× EV/EBITDA. The company offers a dividend yield of 1.2% with a payout ratio of roughly 28% of net profit, indicating a balanced approach between shareholder returns and reinvestment for growth.

Risk Factors

ABD’s earnings are sensitive to fluctuations in raw material costs, particularly molasses and grain prices, which can affect gross margins if not passed on to consumers. The alcohol industry also faces periodic regulatory changes, including state‑level taxation adjustments and advertising restrictions, that could impact sales volumes. Additionally, increasing competition from international premium brands and craft distillers may pressure market share in the higher‑end segment.

Investment Outlook

Considering the steady revenue expansion, improving profitability and a valuation that sits below sector peers, ABD presents a reasonable risk‑adjusted opportunity for investors seeking exposure to the consumer‑staples segment with a growth tilt. The company’s focus on premiumisation, expanding rural penetration and ongoing cost‑efficiency programmes are likely to sustain mid‑single‑digit earnings growth over the next two‑to‑three years. Investors should monitor quarterly volume trends, raw material cost movements and any shifts in state excise policies as key catalysts.

Key Takeaways

  • Revenue grew 12.4% YoY in FY25, supported by strong IMFL sales.
  • EBITDA margin improved to 18.6% reflecting better mix and cost control.
  • Current P/E of 22.4× and EV/EBITDA of 13.1× are modestly below sector averages.
  • Dividend yield of 1.2% provides a modest income component.
  • Main risks include raw material price volatility and regulatory exposure.
  • Outlook remains positive if premiumisation and cost‑saving initiatives continue.
