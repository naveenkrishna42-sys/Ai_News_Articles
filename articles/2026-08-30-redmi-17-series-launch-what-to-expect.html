Redmi 17 Series Launch Date Confirmed

Xiaomi has officially confirmed the launch of the Redmi 17 series in India on September 3rd. The series includes three models: the Redmi 17 5G, Redmi Note 17 Pro Max 5G, and Redmi 17C 5G.
