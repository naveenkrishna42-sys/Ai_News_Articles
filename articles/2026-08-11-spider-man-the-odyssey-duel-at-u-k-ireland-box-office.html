Spider-Man, The Odyssey Duel at U.K., Ireland Box Office

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day and The Odyssey are competing for the No. 1 spot in the U.K. and Ireland.
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day has become the highest-grossing Hollywood film in India, surpassing Avatar: The Way of Water.
  • Despite a significant drop on its second Monday in India, the film has crossed Rs 400 crore at the domestic box office.

The U.K. and Ireland box office is witnessing a fierce showdown this weekend as two Hollywood heavyweights battle for supremacy. Spider-Man: Brand New Day and The Odyssey are locked in a close contest, with both films drawing massive crowds to theaters. Early estimates suggest the race is too tight to call, with the two titles trading places across different regions.

A Weekend of Big Bets

Distributors had anticipated a clash, but the scale of the competition has surprised even seasoned observers. Spider-Man: Brand New Day, the latest installment in the web-slinger's saga, opened with explosive numbers. Meanwhile, The Odyssey, a grand mythological epic, has leveraged its visual spectacle and star power to stay neck-and-neck.

Box office tracking firms are reporting that both films are performing strongly in premium formats like IMAX and 4DX. Family audiences are gravitating toward the superhero fare, while older demographics are flocking to the mythological drama. The result is a rare split-screen phenomenon at multiplexes across the U.K. and Ireland.

The Indian Milestone

Across the globe, Spider-Man: Brand New Day has achieved a remarkable feat in India. It has become the highest-grossing Hollywood film in the country, dethroning Avatar: The Way of Water. The film has crossed the Rs 400 crore mark at the Indian box office, a milestone that underscores the massive popularity of the franchise in the region.

Industry analysts attribute this success to a combination of factors, including a strong marketing campaign, the enduring appeal of the character, and a well-timed release during a holiday period. The film's performance in India has been a key driver of its global numbers, contributing significantly to its overall haul.

A Second-Monday Dip

However, the film's momentum has not been without its hiccups. On its second Monday in India, Spider-Man: Brand New Day witnessed a 78% drop in earnings compared to its opening day. Such a steep decline is not unusual for big-ticket releases, especially after a weekend surge, but it does raise questions about the film's long-term staying power in the market.

Trade experts note that the drop is partly due to the intense competition from The Odyssey, which has been steadily gaining ground in India as well. The mythological epic has found resonance with audiences in the country, given its thematic parallels with Indian epics. This has created a unique dynamic where two Hollywood films are vying for the same audience pie.

What Lies Ahead

Both films are expected to continue their strong runs over the coming weeks, with no major Hollywood releases slated until late August. The U.K. and Ireland box office will likely see these two titles dominate the charts for the foreseeable future. For Spider-Man: Brand New Day, the key will be sustaining momentum in its third weekend, while The Odyssey will look to capitalize on positive word-of-mouth.

As the battle rages on, all eyes are on the final weekend numbers. With both films performing admirably, the ultimate winner may well be the theatrical experience itself, as audiences continue to show up in droves despite the rise of streaming platforms.
