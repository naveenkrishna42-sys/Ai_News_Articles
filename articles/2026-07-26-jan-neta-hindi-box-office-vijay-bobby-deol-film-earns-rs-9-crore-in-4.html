Jan Neta Hindi Box Office: Vijay-Bobby Deol Film Earns Rs 9 Crore in 4 Days

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jan Neta earned Rs 9 crore nett in Hindi markets in four days since release.
  • The film features Thalapathy Vijay in a political drama, his second-last movie before a planned break.
  • Bobby Deol plays a key role in the film directed by Venkat Prabhu.
  • Worldwide, the film is nearing Rs 200 crore gross in three days.
  • Critical reception in Hindi circuits is mixed, with some reviews calling it a step back from Vijay's recent hits.

Thalapathy Vijay's political drama 'Jan Neta' has surprised trade analysts with its Hindi box office performance. The film, which also stars Bobby Deol, collected Rs 9 crore nett in Hindi-speaking markets over its first four days.

Released last Thursday, the film opened to mixed reviews in the Hindi belt. Critics pointed out narrative weaknesses, with some drawing comparisons to Vijay's earlier, less successful films like 'Sura' and 'Villu'. Despite this, audiences in single screens and multiplexes have shown up, particularly in North Indian cities with large Tamil diaspora populations.

A Political Drama with a Star Cast

Directed by Venkat Prabhu, 'Jan Neta' is a political thriller that sees Vijay playing a reluctant leader thrust into the spotlight. The film marks one of Vijay's final projects before his planned break from acting. Bobby Deol plays a pivotal supporting role, adding star power to the ensemble.

The movie's Hindi-dubbed version has performed better than expected. Trade sources say the film's reach in smaller towns has been strong, driven by Vijay's loyal fanbase and curiosity around Deol's performance.

Mixed Critical Reception

While some reviews have praised the film's emotional core and Vijay's screen presence, others have been less kind. The Indian Express called it a "propaganda film" that makes even Sura and Villu look like masterpieces. In contrast, The Shillong Times described it as "a powerful celebration of heroism and hope."

This split in opinion has not hurt the film's commercial prospects. On the contrary, the controversy may have drawn curious audiences to theatres. The film's worldwide gross is nearing Rs 200 crore in just three days, according to industry tracking reports.

Vijay's Farewell Tour

Thalapathy Vijay has announced that he will step away from acting after his next film, making 'Jan Neta' his second-last release. This has added a layer of urgency for fans to catch the star on the big screen. Trade analysts expect the film to hold steady through the week, especially in regions where Vijay has strong fan associations.

Bobby Deol's role has also generated discussion. The actor, who has reinvented himself in recent years with roles in 'Animal' and 'Jailer', brings a gritty edge to the political drama. His pairing with Vijay has been one of the film's talking points.

What's Next

With the Hindi market contributing a modest but respectable share, the film's overall performance will depend on word-of-mouth in the coming weeks. As the film completes its first week, all eyes will be on whether it can sustain momentum or fizzle out in the face of new releases.
