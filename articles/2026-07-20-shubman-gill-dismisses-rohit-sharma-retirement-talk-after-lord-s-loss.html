Shubman Gill dismisses Rohit Sharma retirement talk after Lord's loss

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Shubman Gill addressed rumours about Rohit Sharma's retirement after India's Lord's loss.
  • Rohit scored a brilliant century in the second innings, his first at the ground.
  • Multiple media reports had speculated about Rohit's future in Test cricket.

India vice-captain Shubman Gill has shut down talk of Rohit Sharma's retirement, insisting the captain still has plenty to offer after his magnificent century at Lord's went in vain.

India lost the second Test by 75 runs on Monday, giving England a 1-0 lead in the series. But the defeat was overshadowed by a brilliant 157 from Rohit — his first Test hundred at the home of cricket.

Gill backs captain to continue

Speaking after the match, Gill made it clear the team sees no reason for Rohit to step away. "He's batting as well as I've ever seen him. That innings at Lord's was special. There's no talk in the dressing room about him going anywhere," Gill said.

Gill added that outside noise should not dictate the captain's future. "These rumours come up every few months. He's shown again he's our best batter. Let the noise be there — we know what he brings."

Rohit breaks his silence

Rohit himself addressed the speculation after the match. "That's what I've been told," he said when asked about reports suggesting he might retire. He did not elaborate further but sources close to the team indicated the 39-year-old has no immediate plans to quit.

The Times of India quoted Rohit as saying: "I am good enough to play for a couple of years. My body feels fine, my mind is clear." The veteran opener has been under scrutiny after a lean run earlier this year, but his Lord's knock has silenced critics for now.

Brilliant hundred in a losing cause

Rohit's 157 came off 244 balls and included 18 fours and three sixes. He single-handedly kept India in the chase before falling to a sharp catch at gully. England set India a target of 359, and despite Rohit's heroics, the lower order collapsed under pressure.

"He carried the innings on his shoulders," Gill said. "To play like that on a pitch that had variable bounce shows his class. We just couldn't finish it off for him."

What happens next

The third Test begins at Headingley on August 6. India must win to level the series. All eyes will be on Rohit to see if he can back up his Lord's effort with another big score.

For now, the retirement talk appears to have been put to rest — at least until the next defeat.
