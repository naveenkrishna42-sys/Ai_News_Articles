Iran to release Khamenei footage amid health speculation

📅 2026-08-09 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Iranian officials say footage of Supreme Leader Khamenei will be released 'in future'.
  • Reports claim Khamenei is close to death, prompting speculations about his son Mojtaba.
  • President Pezeshkian's meeting with Khamenei has been confirmed by Iranian media.
  • Succession questions loom as Iran navigates regional tensions and US pressures.

Tehran has moved to quell swirling speculation about the health of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, stating that footage of the 87-year-old cleric will be released 'in future'. The announcement comes as multiple reports, including from regional outlets, suggest the leader is close to death, triggering intense focus on succession within the Islamic Republic.

Official reassurances and lingering doubts

Iranian state media, citing unnamed officials, said that video of Khamenei would be made available to the public 'in the coming days'. The statement is intended to counter what Tehran calls 'malicious rumours' about the Supreme Leader's condition. Yet the lack of a specific timeline has done little to dampen speculation.

Reports from sources within Iran's clerical establishment have claimed that Khamenei's health has deteriorated sharply, with some even describing his condition as 'critical'. Neither the office of the Supreme Leader nor the Iranian government has confirmed these reports, but the unusual silence has fuelled anxiety both domestically and abroad.

The succession question

At the heart of the speculation is Mojtaba Khamenei, the Supreme Leader's second son. Unconfirmed reports suggest that Mojtaba has been 'meeting' senior officials, including President Masoud Pezeshkian, amid the health crisis. These meetings, if they occurred, would mark a significant step in what many analysts see as a carefully managed succession process.

Mojtaba, a mid-ranking cleric, has long been considered a potential successor, though his elevation would require approval from the Assembly of Experts, the body tasked with selecting the next Supreme Leader. His reported interactions with the president and other key figures have raised eyebrows, though nothing has been officially confirmed.

Pezeshkian's meeting and regional tensions

Iranian media have confirmed that President Pezeshkian did meet with Khamenei recently, though details of the discussion remain scarce. The meeting comes at a time of heightened regional friction, with the US-Iran standoff showing no signs of easing. Reports indicate Iran has set conditions for reopening the Strait of Hormuz, demanding compensation from the US and relief from sanctions.

The convergence of a potential leadership transition with an external crisis places Iran in a precarious position. Any prolonged uncertainty over Khamenei's health could embolden adversaries and complicate Tehran's negotiating stance.

What to watch

The release of the promised footage will be the first real test of Iran's claims. If it shows a healthy, active Khamenei, the speculation may subside. If it is delayed or shows the leader in a weakened state, expect the succession jockeying to intensify.

For now, the world watches Tehran's next move, knowing that the fate of the Islamic Republic — and possibly the region — hinges on the health of one man.
