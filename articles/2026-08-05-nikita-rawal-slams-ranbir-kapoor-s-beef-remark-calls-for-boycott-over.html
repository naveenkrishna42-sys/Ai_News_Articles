The controversy centers on a years-old interview where Ranbir Kapoor discussed his dietary preferences. While the exact context of the remark has been debated, Rawal has seized upon it as evidence of insensitivity. She claims that someone who has publicly spoken about eating beef cannot embody the purity associated with Lord Rama.

This is not the first time Kapoor has faced backlash over the remark, but it is the first time it has been directly linked to his Ramayana casting. The film, directed by Nitesh Tiwari and produced by Namit Malhotra, is one of the most anticipated projects in Indian cinema. The casting of Kapoor was initially met with excitement, but Rawal's campaign has added a layer of controversy.
