Heavy Rain in Hyderabad Disrupts Evening Traffic

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Heavy rain lashed Hyderabad on Friday evening, disrupting traffic across the city.
  • Several key roads and junctions reported waterlogging, leading to long delays for commuters.
  • Officials have not yet confirmed the extent of the impact or issued any fresh weather alerts.

Hyderabad's evening rush hour turned into a crawl on Friday as a heavy downpour lashed the city, throwing traffic out of gear across several key corridors. Commuters heading home found themselves stuck in long queues, with many reporting waterlogging at major junctions and underpasses.

The sudden intensity of the rain, which began shortly after 3 pm, caught many off guard. Auto-rickshaws and cab services were in high demand, while those on two-wheelers struggled through slick roads and reduced visibility.

Waterlogging Hits Key Routes

Reports from across the city indicated that stretches like Begumpet, Ameerpet, and Khairatabad saw significant slowdowns. Low-lying areas, including parts of Secunderabad and the Old City, experienced water accumulation that slowed movement to a near standstill.

Traffic police were deployed at several intersections to manually manage the flow, but the sheer volume of vehicles made progress slow. Many office-goers took to social media to share photos of stalled traffic and waterlogged roads, with some reporting that what usually takes 30 minutes stretched to over two hours.

Commuters Bear the Brunt

For daily commuters, the disruption was a familiar but frustrating ordeal. "The rain started suddenly, and within minutes, the roads were jammed. I've been stuck near Punjagutta for almost an hour," said one commuter, echoing the sentiment of many others stranded on the roads.

Public transport, including city buses, also faced delays, with several routes running behind schedule. The Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) has not yet issued an official statement on the extent of waterlogging or the measures being taken to clear the roads.

Weather Outlook

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) had earlier forecast thundershowers for the city, but the intensity of the rain on Friday evening was more severe than anticipated. No fresh alerts have been issued, but the weather office has indicated that isolated showers could continue into the night.

Residents are advised to check traffic updates before heading out and to avoid low-lying stretches where waterlogging is common. Authorities are expected to assess the damage and clear the waterlogging overnight to ensure smoother movement on Saturday morning.

As the city recovers from the evening's chaos, all eyes will be on the weather over the next 24 hours, with the possibility of more rain threatening to disrupt the weekend.
