Mumbai Coastal Road Promenade Plunged Into Darkness Again

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Promenade lights on Mumbai's Coastal Road failed again, weeks after repair.
  • Stretches near marine drive remain dark, worrying regular visitors.
  • Civic body yet to issue statement on the recurring electrical issue.

Recurring Darkness on the New Promenade

For the second time in a month, the much-touted promenade along Mumbai's Coastal Road plunged into darkness on Tuesday night, leaving joggers and evening strollers navigating an unlit stretch. The failure, which began around 8 pm, affected the walkway near the Worli end, throwing the scenic route into an eerie gloom.

Regular visitors, who had welcomed the promenade's opening earlier this year, expressed frustration at the repeated breakdown. "We thought the initial glitches were sorted, but this is becoming a pattern," said a local resident who jogs there daily. The lights had been repaired just two weeks ago after a similar outage.

Safety Concerns Mount for Evening Visitors

The dark stretch has raised fresh worries about safety, especially for women and elderly walkers who frequent the promenade in the evening. With no illumination, uneven pavement and unmarked edges become potential hazards. "It's risky. You can't see where you're stepping, and there's no guard patrolling the area after sunset," noted another visitor.

Although the Coastal Road has lighting along its main carriageway, the promenade's decorative and pathway lights are a separate circuit, which appears to be the source of the recurring trouble. Commuters driving past reported seeing only the headlights of vehicles and the distant glow of the Bandra-Worli Sea Link, while the walkway remained in shadows.

No Official Word Yet

The Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC), which maintains the Coastal Road, has not yet issued a statement on Tuesday's failure. Officials familiar with the matter, speaking on condition of anonymity, indicated that a faulty cable connection might be responsible, but a detailed inspection is pending.

This is not the first time the infrastructure has come under scrutiny. Since its inauguration, the Coastal Road has faced multiple teething problems, from drainage issues to malfunctioning signages. However, the lighting problem stands out because it directly affects public use of the promenade, which was designed as a leisure space for Mumbaikars.

Residents Demand Permanent Fix

Local activists and residents' groups have urged the BMC to replace the existing lighting system with a more robust, weatherproof alternative. "Temporary patchwork won't work. We need a permanent solution before the monsoon worsens the situation," said a representative of a Worli-based residents' association. The onset of heavy rains could further damage exposed wiring, leading to longer outages.

Meanwhile, some visitors have begun carrying torches or using their mobile phone flashlights to navigate the promenade after dark. Others have chosen to stay away altogether, a loss for the city's public spaces. "It's a beautiful walk when the lights work. But now I think twice before coming here in the evening," added a college student who visits with friends.

What to Watch Next

The BMC is expected to release a repair schedule in the coming days. Civic officials have hinted at a comprehensive electrical audit of the entire Coastal Road stretch, though no timeline has been announced. Until then, Mumbaikars may have to keep their torches handy on their evening strolls.
