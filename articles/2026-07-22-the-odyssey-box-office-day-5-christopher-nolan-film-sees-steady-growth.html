The Odyssey box office Day 5: Christopher Nolan film sees steady growth

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The Odyssey, directed by Christopher Nolan and starring Matt Damon, saw good box office growth on Day 5.
  • The film earned $52 million globally from IMAX screens in its opening weekend.
  • Reviews highlight jaw-dropping action, dark magic, and even a dolphin cameo.

Christopher Nolan's latest epic, 'The Odyssey', starring Matt Damon, has shown good growth at the box office on its fifth day. The film, which adapts Homer's ancient Greek poem, is drawing audiences worldwide.

Day 5 collection trends

Early estimates indicate a steady increase in ticket sales from the opening weekend. The film's weekday performance suggests strong word-of-mouth and repeat viewership.

Industry trackers note that 'The Odyssey' has maintained a healthy occupancy across major markets. The exact Day 5 figures are expected later today.

IMAX earnings set record

The film earned a staggering $52 million globally from IMAX screens alone during its opening weekend. This reinforces Nolan's reputation as a filmmaker who drives audiences to premium large-format theatres.

Nolan shot significant portions of the film with IMAX cameras, a signature approach that has paid off handsomely. The visual scale of the ancient world, from sprawling battlefields to mythical islands, is designed for the big screen.

Critical reception and talking points

Reviews have been largely positive, with critics praising the film's ambition. The Guardian's report describes 'jaw-dropping action, dark magic and dolphin cameos', hinting at the director's trademark blend of spectacle and surprise.

Some outlets, like Tablet Magazine, have focused on the cultural dimensions, with a piece titled 'Homer's Joke on Christopher Nolan'. This suggests the film may have layered interpretations that are sparking debate among cinephiles.

Star power and direction

Matt Damon leads the cast as Odysseus, bringing gravitas to the hero's decade-long journey home. Nolan's direction is being credited for balancing intimate character moments with massive set pieces.

The Hollywood Reporter's analysis of how the film conquered the box office points to a combination of Nolan's loyal fanbase and the universal appeal of the epic genre. The director's previous track record with 'Interstellar', 'Inception', and 'Oppenheimer' has set high expectations.

Audiences are also buzzing about the technical achievements. The dolphin cameo mentioned in reviews appears to be a rare moment of levity in an otherwise intense narrative.

What lies ahead

With the second weekend approaching, 'The Odyssey' is expected to maintain its momentum. The film's performance in international markets, particularly in India and China, will be crucial for its long-term run.

Nolan's ability to draw crowds to theatres continues to be a talking point in an era dominated by streaming. For now, 'The Odyssey' looks set to add another global hit to his filmography.
