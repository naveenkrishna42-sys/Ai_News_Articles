Detailed Analysis

Following the initial overview, the device demonstrates a balanced blend of power efficiency and raw performance, positioning it competitively within its segment. Benchmarks reveal notable improvements in multi‑core tasks while maintaining thermal stability under prolonged loads.

Performance

The chipset, built on a 4 nm process, delivers up to 30 % higher CPU throughput compared to its predecessor. GPU performance sees a 22 % uplift in graphics‑intensive scenarios, making gaming and AR applications smoother.

  • CPU: Octa‑core (1×3.2 GHz Cortex‑X2 + 3×2.8 GHz Cortex‑A710 + 4×2.0 GHz Cortex‑A510)
  • GPU: Latest generation Adreno with hardware‑accelerated ray tracing
  • RAM options: 8 GB LPDDR5 or 12 GB LPDDR5
  • Storage: UFS 3.1, 128 GB/256 GB/512 GB variants

Design and Build

The chassis adopts a recycled aluminum frame with a matte‑finish glass back, reducing fingerprint smudges. IP68 rating ensures resistance to dust and water immersion up to 1.5 m for 30 minutes.

  • Dimensions: 158.2 mm × 73.6 mm × 8.4 mm
  • Weight: 189 g
  • Display: 6.7‑inch LTPO AMOLED, QHD+ (1440 × 3120), 120 Hz adaptive refresh
  • Battery: 5 000 mAh with 80 W wired and 50 W wireless charging

Camera System

A triple‑lens setup headlined by a 50 MP primary sensor with OIS, complemented by a 12 MP ultrawide and a 5 MP macro module. Software enhancements bring improved night‑mode dynamics and 8K video recording at 30 fps.

  • Main: 50 MP, f/1.8, 1.0 µm pixel size, OIS
  • Ultrawide: 12 MP, f/2.2, 123° FOV
  • Macro: 5 MP, f/2.4
  • Front: 32 MP, f/2.0

Key Takeaways

  • The device offers flagship‑level performance at a mid‑range price point, especially in the 8 GB/128 GB configuration.
  • Battery life comfortably exceeds a full day of mixed usage, bolstered by fast charging capabilities.
  • Camera versatility is strong, though the ultrawide lens lags slightly behind competitors in low‑light scenarios.
  • Build quality emphasizes sustainability with recycled materials without compromising durability.
  • Overall, it presents a compelling option for users seeking high performance, solid camera capabilities, and premium design without stepping into the ultra‑premium tier.
