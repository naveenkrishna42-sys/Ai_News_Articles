Tech Mahindra Q1: Profit up 28%, deal wins cross $1 billion for third quarter

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Net profit rose 28% year-on-year to ₹1,465 crore in the June quarter.
  • Total deal wins exceeded $1 billion for the third consecutive quarter.
  • The company may restart campus hiring after a period of caution.
  • Manufacturing segment growth and AI investments boosted margins.

Tech Mahindra posted a strong set of numbers for the first quarter of the current financial year. Net profit jumped 28% year-on-year to ₹1,465 crore, beating analyst expectations. Revenue growth was led by the manufacturing vertical, where clients ramped up spending on digital transformation.

Deal momentum continues

The IT services firm has now crossed the $1 billion mark in total contract value for three quarters in a row. This consistency in deal wins signals that clients are signing larger and longer-term agreements. The company did not break down the exact deal value for the quarter but confirmed that the pipeline remains robust across geographies.

Tech Mahindra has been focusing on large deals in telecom, manufacturing, and banking. The company's strategy of bundling traditional IT services with AI-led automation appears to be paying off. Margins improved partly because of these AI tools, which reduced costs in delivery and support functions.

AI bets lift margins

Artificial intelligence has become a central theme in Tech Mahindra's operations. The company has embedded AI into its service delivery platforms, which helped expand operating margins by over 100 basis points sequentially. Management indicated that more client engagements now involve AI-powered solutions, from predictive maintenance to customer analytics.

This focus on AI is also helping the company win deals where efficiency gains are a priority for clients. In the current economic environment, many enterprises are looking for ways to cut costs while modernising their IT stacks. Tech Mahindra's AI offerings address both needs.

Manufacturing growth offsets telecom drag

The manufacturing segment emerged as the star performer in Q1. Revenue from this vertical grew in double digits sequentially, driven by demand from automotive and industrial clients in Europe and North America. The telecom vertical, traditionally Tech Mahindra's largest, continued to show sluggish growth due to delayed spending by some large clients.

Banking and financial services also showed moderate growth. The company is trying to reduce its dependency on telecom by expanding in other sectors. Manufacturing and BFSI now account for a larger share of total revenue than a year ago.

Hiring outlook improves

Tech Mahindra had held back campus hiring in recent quarters due to lack of revenue visibility. That caution may be easing. The company hinted that it could restart fresher hiring in the coming months if the demand environment stays supportive. Attrition remained stable, and the firm continued to add employees in niche areas such as AI and cloud.

The broader IT sector has seen a pickup in hiring after a prolonged slowdown. Tech Mahindra's decision to resume campus recruitment would signal confidence in its growth trajectory for the rest of the year.

Market watchers will now look for signs that the company can sustain its deal win momentum and margin expansion. The next trigger will be the management's commentary on demand for the second half of the fiscal year.
