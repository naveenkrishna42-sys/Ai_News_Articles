Samsung launches Music Studio Wi-Fi speaker series in India

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung unveils Music Studio 5 and Music Studio 7 Wi-Fi speakers in India
  • Speakers feature Q-Symphony for synchronised audio with Samsung TVs
  • Sculptural design allows speakers to double as home decor
  • Wi-Fi connectivity enables multi-room audio setup

Samsung India has introduced the Music Studio series of Wi-Fi speakers in the country. The lineup includes two models — the Music Studio 5 and the Music Studio 7. The new speakers are designed for users who want high-quality audio alongside a sleek, sculptural aesthetic that blends into home decor.

Design and form factor

The Music Studio 5 stands out with its minimalist, sculptural silhouette. Samsung describes it as a speaker that doubles as art, aiming to make it a visual centrepiece as much as an audio device. The speaker's design departs from the typical boxy form, opting for clean lines and a refined finish.

The Music Studio 7 is the larger sibling in the series, offering higher output and presumably a bigger footprint. Both models are built around a Wi-Fi first approach, but also support Bluetooth streaming for convenience. The focus is on seamless integration into a connected home ecosystem.

Audio technology and Q-Symphony

The headline feature on both speakers is support for Samsung's Q-Symphony technology. This allows the Music Studio speakers to synchronise wirelessly with compatible Samsung TVs. Instead of replacing the TV's built-in speakers, Q-Symphony combines them with the external speaker for a wider, fuller soundstage.

Users can place the Music Studio speakers in the same room as the television to create an immersive experience without the clutter of wires or a full soundbar setup. The feature is particularly aimed at those who want to upgrade TV audio without a major overhaul of their living room arrangement.

Both speakers support multi-room audio over Wi-Fi. This means multiple Music Studio units can be placed in different rooms and controlled together or independently through the Samsung SmartThings app. The app also handles music streaming services and EQ adjustments.

Connectivity and ecosystem

The speakers are designed to work within Samsung's broader ecosystem. Besides Q-Symphony, they integrate with SmartThings for voice control and automation routines. Users can group speakers with other Samsung audio products such as soundbars or Galaxy home speakers.

Wi-Fi connectivity is the primary mode for high-resolution audio streaming. Bluetooth 5.2 is available for quick pairing with phones or tablets. The speakers do not have a built-in assistant, but they can be triggered via a connected Galaxy smartphone or Samsung TV remote.

Pricing and availability details have not yet been announced. Samsung is expected to reveal retail prices and launch dates in the coming weeks. The series will compete with offerings from Bose, Sonos, and JBL in the premium Wi-Fi speaker segment in India.

What to watch

The Music Studio series enters a market where Indian consumers are increasingly investing in smart home audio. Samsung will need to price the speakers competitively against established players to gain traction. The Q-Symphony integration could be a decisive factor for existing Samsung TV users looking to upgrade their audio setup.
