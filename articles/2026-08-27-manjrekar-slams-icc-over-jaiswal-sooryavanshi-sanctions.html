ICC Criticized for Lenient Action Against Indian Players

Cricket pundit Sanjay Manjrekar has taken a strong stance against the International Cricket Council (ICC) for what he perceives as lenient action against Indian players Yashasvi Jaiswal and Vaibhav Sooryavanshi. Manjrekar, known for his outspoken opinions, has criticized the ICC's handling of on-field clashes, arguing that the sanctions imposed are not enough to deter such behavior.

Manjrekar's Criticism of the ICC and BCCI

Manjrekar has accused the ICC and the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) of failing to address the root cause of on-field clashes. He argues that the ICC has not provided sufficient training to players on how to handle such situations, leading to repeated incidents. Manjrekar believes that the ICC needs to take a stricter approach to ensure that players understand the consequences of their actions.

Sanctions Confirmed by the ICC

The ICC has confirmed that sanctions have been imposed on Jaiswal and Fernando, but Manjrekar believes these are not enough. He argues that the sanctions should be more severe to serve as a deterrent for other players.

Manjrekar's Call for Better Training

Manjrekar has called for better training for players on how to handle on-field clashes. He believes that players should be trained to understand the importance of sportsmanship and the consequences of their actions. Manjrekar argues that the ICC and BCCI need to take a more proactive approach to address this issue.
