Optimum Nutrition's Gold Standard Whey has been the benchmark reference whey for over two decades, and the Double Rich Chocolate variant remains its strongest-selling flavor in India. Mixability is excellent with a standard shaker and cold water, producing a smooth, low-foam consistency within 15 to 20 seconds. The flavor profile is rich, mildly sweet, and cocoa-forward without the chalky aftertaste common to budget whey brands. Recovery support is reliable for athletes training four to six days per week, thanks to the fast-absorbing whey isolate and concentrate blend delivering 24g protein per scoop. Digestive tolerance is generally good for users who are mildly lactose sensitive, owing to the enzyme-treated blend. For daily users, the tub lasts approximately four weeks at one-scoop-per-day consumption, making the effective per-serving cost post-discount competitive against emerging Indian D2C whey brands.
