Current Chase Southwest Welcome Bonuses – An In-Depth Review

The Chase Southwest card lineup continues to attract applicants with tiered welcome offers designed to reward frequent travelers. New entrants see several competing packages, each with distinct eligibility requirements, signup costs, and ongoing benefit structures. Understanding the nuances between annual fees, signup bonus value, and long-term cost help buyers make informed decisions.

Top Welcome Bonus Opportunities

  • Offer #1: $300 Signup Bonus + 35,000 Miles

    This entry-level package requires $10,000 in opening balance within 90 days and monthly spend of $500 before closing. It delivers $300 in cash back or equivalent points plus 35,000 weighted airline miles usable for flights or hotel stays. The conditionality makes it suitable for new cardholders who want immediate value.

  • Offer #2: $500 Bonus + 50,000 Miles (No Spend Threshold)

    Designed for existing Chase cardholders, this offer waives certain requirements after achieving $15,000 in annual spending. Applicants receive $500 cash back or points redeemable for travel credits within three months of activation, plus 50,000 transferable partner miles.

  • Offer #3: $200 Minimum Balance Bonus + 25,000 Miles (High Spending Tier)

    Targeted at top-tier travelers, this tier demands $25,000 in qualifying spend within 60 days. Beneficiaries earn $200 flat plus 25,000 miles plus premium perk add-ons such as complimentary upgrades when available.

  • Offer #4: $0 Annual Fee + Points-Plus

    A no-fee variant aims to convert cardholders away from legacy travel cards. While it lacks a large signup bonus, it provides accelerated points earning on hotel bookings through airline partners and daily dining category multipliers that can offset ongoing costs for heavy flyers.

  • Offer #5: Travel Insurance Combo

    This limited-bonus offering includes $100 travel insurance alongside enhanced baggage protection. Though lower in overall value than the million-mile competitors, it appeals to budget-conscious travelers seeking basic coverage rather than extensive points accumulation.

Benefits Beyond the Introductory Period

Once the welcome period ends, the card transitions to its standard benefit architecture. Most variants feature unlimited flex points that can be converted to airline miles at favorable rates, typically 1.25x during peak redemption windows. Complimentary domestic flights may also be secured annually through promotional fares tied to the signup bonus.

Fee Structure and Cost Analysis

Understanding the true cost involves examining both upfront and recurring expenses. The base annual fee varies from $95 to $495 depending on the tier selected. Some plans waive the first year’s charge while others apply it retroactively. Additionally, foreign transaction fees are either $0 or 2% depending on the product version, affecting international travelers more than domestic ones.

Who Should Apply Now? – Pros and Cons

  • First-time travelers building credit: Offer #1 and #2 provide immediate spending incentives and welcome bonuses that accelerate credit-building milestones.
  • Established Chasse members: Offer #2 rewards sustained spending with higher mileage payouts, appealing to those already engaged with the Chase ecosystem.
  • Budget-conscious flyers: Offer #4 eliminates annual fees but returns fewer flight credits over time; best suited for light travelers who can offset minimal fees through point usage.
  • Travelers prioritizing simplicity: The no-annual-fee variant reduces administrative friction while still delivering meaningful points earning on select categories.
  • Those needing broad insurance: The limited combo offer stands out for visitors requiring robust trip cancellation and baggage coverage, though points returns remain secondary.
