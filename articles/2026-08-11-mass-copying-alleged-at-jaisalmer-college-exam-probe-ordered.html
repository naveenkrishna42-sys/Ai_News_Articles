Mass copying alleged at Jaisalmer college exam, probe ordered

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mass copying alleged during an exam at a Jaisalmer college
  • Authorities have ordered a formal probe into the incident
  • Investigation underway; officials yet to confirm details

Allegations surface at Jaisalmer college

Authorities in Rajasthan have ordered a probe after allegations of mass copying emerged during an examination at a college in Jaisalmer. The incident came to light following complaints from invigilators and students, prompting the college administration to take cognisance of the matter.

While the exact date and the specific examination involved have not been officially confirmed, sources suggest that the alleged malpractice occurred during a routine test. The college has not yet issued a public statement, and officials are refraining from commenting until the inquiry progresses.

Probe initiated

A formal investigation has been launched to look into the allegations. The probe will examine how the copying reportedly took place, who was involved, and whether any staff members were complicit. Local education department officials are expected to lead the inquiry.

This is not the first time such allegations have surfaced in the region. In recent years, several colleges in Rajasthan have faced scrutiny over examination irregularities, prompting stricter oversight measures. However, officials have not confirmed if this case is linked to any broader pattern.

What the probe will cover

  • Verification of CCTV footage and invigilator reports
  • Statements from students and staff present during the exam
  • Review of answer sheets for signs of copying

The findings are expected to determine whether any disciplinary action is warranted. If the allegations are proven, the college may face penalties, and students involved could see their results cancelled.

Response from authorities

District education officials have acknowledged the complaint and assured that the matter will be dealt with transparently. "We are taking this seriously and will act based on the inquiry report," a senior official said on condition of anonymity. No formal FIR has been registered yet, and the police have not been approached so far.

The college administration has reportedly pledged full cooperation with the investigating team. Meanwhile, students have expressed mixed reactions—some demanding a thorough probe to protect the institution's credibility, while others fear an unfair crackdown.

Looking ahead

The probe is in its early stages, and a detailed report is expected in the coming weeks. Depending on the findings, the college may revise its examination protocols to prevent future incidents. For now, all eyes are on the investigators to ensure that the truth comes out and that accountability is fixed.
