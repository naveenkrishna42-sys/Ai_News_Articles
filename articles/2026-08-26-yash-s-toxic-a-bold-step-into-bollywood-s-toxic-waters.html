Yash's 'Toxic': A bold step into Bollywood's toxic waters

Yash's 'Toxic' has hit the screens, marking a significant step into the darker, more controversial side of Bollywood. The film, which features a star-studded cast including Yash, Sonam Kapoor, and Anil Kapoor, delves into the dark side of the industry, exploring themes of power, corruption, and the price of fame.

Cast and Plot: A star-studded cast with a controversial plot

The cast of 'Toxic' is nothing short of impressive, with Yash playing the lead role of a Bollywood actor who is caught up in a web of corruption and deceit. Sonam Kapoor plays his love interest, while Anil Kapoor plays a powerful producer who is willing to do anything to get what he wants. The film's plot is a bold step into the darker side of Bollywood, exploring themes that have been avoided in the past.

CBFC Rating: A 'U' rating, but with criticism

The Central Board of Film Certification (CBFC) has given 'Toxic' a 'U' rating, which means that it is suitable for all ages. However, the rating has faced criticism from some quarters, with some arguing that the film is too dark and violent for children. The CBFC has defended its decision, arguing that the film is a work of fiction and that it is up to the parents to decide whether their children should watch it.

Runtime and Fees: A 2-hour and 30-minute runtime and a high production cost

'Toxic' has a runtime of 2 hours and 30 minutes, which is on the longer side for a Bollywood film. The film's production cost is estimated to be around ₹100 crore, which is a significant investment for a film that is exploring such dark themes. The film's marketing and promotion cost is also estimated to be around ₹50 crore, which is a significant amount of money.

Box Office Prediction: A mixed bag of predictions

The box office prediction for 'Toxic' is mixed, with some experts predicting a strong debut and others expecting a flop. The film's star power and the controversy surrounding it could both work in its favor and against it. The film's success will ultimately depend on how well it performs in the first few days, as word of mouth and reviews can have a significant impact on its box office performance.
