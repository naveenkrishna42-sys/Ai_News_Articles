Abhishek Sharma's Blitz Powers India to Comfortable Win

India kicked off their T20I series against Afghanistan with a dominant seven-wicket victory at the Arun Jaitley Stadium in Delhi on Sunday. The spotlight belonged to young opener Abhishek Sharma, who blazed to a 22-ball fifty – the fastest by an Indian batter in T20Is against Afghanistan – as the hosts chased down 150 with 26 balls to spare.

After Arshdeep Singh's three-wicket haul restricted Afghanistan to 149/8, India's reply was never in doubt once Abhishek shifted gears. The left-hander's innings of 58 not out off just 25 deliveries included five fours and four sixes, showcasing his fearless approach at the top of the order.

Arshdeep Sets the Tone with Early Strikes

Opting to bowl first, India's pacers made immediate inroads. Arshdeep Singh, returning to the side after a brief rest, struck in his very first over, removing Rahmanullah Gurbaz for a duck. He followed it up with the key wickets of Ibrahim Zadran and Azmatullah Omarzai, finishing with figures of 3/18 from his four overs.

Afghanistan's middle order offered resistance through captain Rashid Khan (32 off 24) and Najibullah Zadran (28 off 22), but the run rate never truly accelerated. Kuldeep Yadav and Axar Patel chipped in with a wicket each, ensuring the visitors were kept under 150.

Abhishek's Aggressive Approach Pays Off

Abhishek's knock was a statement of intent. He reached his fifty in just 22 balls, surpassing the previous record held by KL Rahul (24 balls) for the fastest T20I fifty by an Indian against Afghanistan. Speaking after the match, he said, "I just wanted to attack from the start. The wicket was good for batting, and I backed my instincts."

His partnership with Shubman Gill (34 off 28) yielded 72 runs in just 7.2 overs, effectively sealing the game. Captain Rohit Sharma, who promoted himself to No. 3, remained unbeaten on 23 off 18 as India crossed the finish line with ease.

Series Context and What Lies Ahead

This victory gives India a 1-0 lead in the three-match series. The teams will meet again in Indore on Tuesday, where Afghanistan will look to bounce back. For India, the performance reinforces their bench strength ahead of the T20 World Cup.

With the Asia Cup and World Cup on the horizon, India's selectors will be pleased with Abhishek's consistency and Arshdeep's wicket-taking ability in the powerplay. Afghanistan, meanwhile, need to find answers to their top-order woes.

Key Takeaways

  • Abhishek Sharma's 22-ball fifty is the fastest by an Indian in T20Is against Afghanistan.
  • Arshdeep Singh's 3/18 is his best bowling figures against Afghanistan.
  • India's win margin of 7 wickets with 26 balls remaining highlights their dominance.
  • Afghanistan's top order failed to provide a solid start, with both openers dismissed cheaply.

India will aim to seal the series in Indore, while Afghanistan will seek to level the score. The match will be played on September 15 at the Holkar Cricket Stadium.
