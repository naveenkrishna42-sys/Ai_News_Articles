Pakistan's envoy Asim Munir meets Iran military chiefs to revive US-Iran talks

Pakistan's envoy to Iran, Asim Munir, has met with Iran's military chiefs to discuss reviving US-Iran talks. The meeting comes amid rising tensions in the region, with the US and Iran on the brink of a potential conflict. Munir's efforts are crucial as Pakistan seeks to maintain its neutral stance and avoid being dragged into the conflict.

Iran's President Pezeshkian warns against US 'coercion and bullying'

Iran's President Masoud Pezeshkian has warned against US 'coercion and bullying' complicating the talks. He emphasized the importance of dialogue and diplomacy in resolving the issue. This warning comes as the US continues to impose sanctions on Iran, which has led to a series of escalations in the region.

US-Iran talks remain possible, says Pakistan envoy Asim Munir

Despite the tensions, Pakistan's envoy Asim Munir has stated that US-Iran talks remain possible. He has expressed hope that the talks can be revived and that a peaceful resolution can be found. Munir's optimism is based on the fact that both the US and Iran are aware of the potential consequences of a conflict.
