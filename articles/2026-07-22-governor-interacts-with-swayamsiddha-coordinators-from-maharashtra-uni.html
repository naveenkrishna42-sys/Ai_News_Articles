Governor Interacts with Swayamsiddha Coordinators from Maharashtra Universities

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Governor interacted with Swayamsiddha coordinators from various universities in Maharashtra.
  • Discussions focused on women's empowerment and skill development through the Swayamsiddha programme.
  • Coordinator's role in implementing initiatives at the university level was highlighted.

Mumbai: The Governor of Maharashtra on Tuesday held a meeting with coordinators of the Swayamsiddha programme from various universities across the state. The interaction took place at Raj Bhavan, Mumbai, and focused on the progress and implementation of the women's empowerment initiative.

Focus on Women's Empowerment

The Swayamsiddha programme aims to equip women with skills and knowledge to become self-reliant. The Governor discussed strategies to strengthen the initiative at the university level. Coordinators shared feedback on the ground-level impact and challenges faced by participants.

Officials from Raj Bhavan stated that the meeting underscored the importance of sustained efforts in promoting gender equality and economic independence among women. The programme has been active in several universities, offering training in diverse fields.

Role of University Coordinators

The coordinators present at the meeting are responsible for overseeing the implementation of Swayamsiddha activities within their respective institutions. They play a key role in mobilising students and ensuring the programme reaches its intended beneficiaries.

Discussions also touched upon the need for better coordination between universities and government departments. The Governor emphasised the need to track outcomes and share best practices to scale up the initiative effectively.

Skill Development and Economic Independence

A significant portion of the interaction revolved around skill development modules offered under the programme. The coordinators elaborated on the training sessions conducted in areas such as digital literacy, financial management, and vocational trades.

The Governor appreciated the efforts made by universities in creating an enabling environment for women. The meeting concluded with a call to expand the programme's reach to more districts and rural areas.

Last year, the state government had allocated additional funds to support the Swayamsiddha scheme. The programme has reportedly benefited thousands of women across Maharashtra since its inception.

The government is expected to release a detailed report on the progress of the initiative in the coming weeks. Further meetings with coordinators are likely to be scheduled to review the implementation roadmap.
