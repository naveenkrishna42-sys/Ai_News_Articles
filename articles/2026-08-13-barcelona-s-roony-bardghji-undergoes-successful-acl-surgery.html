Barcelona's Roony Bardghji undergoes successful ACL surgery

📅 2026-08-13 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Roony Bardghji tore his ACL in training and underwent successful surgery.
  • The injury rules out any potential transfer move this window.
  • Barcelona's medical staff will monitor his recovery; no return timeline confirmed.

Barcelona winger Roony Bardghji has undergone successful surgery after suffering a ruptured anterior cruciate ligament in his right knee during a training session. The club confirmed the procedure on Wednesday, bringing clarity to a worrying few days for the Catalan side and their young Swedish talent.

The 20-year-old, who joined Barcelona from Copenhagen in a deal that had raised expectations, sustained the injury in what appeared to be a routine drill. Medical staff immediately suspected ligament damage, and subsequent scans confirmed the worst-case scenario.

Surgery successful, recovery begins

The operation, performed at a Barcelona medical facility, was declared a success by the club's medical team. Bardghji will now begin a lengthy rehabilitation process, though the club has not set a definitive timeline for his return. Typical recovery periods for ACL injuries range from six to nine months, but each case varies.

Barcelona's medical department will oversee his recovery, with regular assessments to track progress. The club has not commented on whether the injury affects his long-term development plans, but the immediate focus is on a full recovery.

Transfer plans derailed

The injury has also scuppered any potential transfer movement involving Bardghji this summer. Reports earlier in the window suggested the winger could be loaned out or sold to generate funds and playing time. Those discussions are now moot, as the club cannot offload a player who will be sidelined for the foreseeable future.

For Barcelona, the timing is particularly awkward. The club has been working within financial constraints, and any planned departures were meant to ease wage bill pressures. Bardghji's situation adds another layer of complexity to an already busy transfer window.

Flick's dilemma and squad depth

Head coach Hansi Flick now faces a reshaped attacking unit. Bardghji was seen as a rotational option on the wings, capable of providing depth in a congested season. His absence means younger academy players or existing squad members will need to step up.

There is some relief elsewhere in the squad. Jules Kounde has returned to training, offering Flick a boost in defence. But the loss of Bardghji narrows options in wide areas, and the coaching staff may now revisit the market for a replacement, depending on financial headroom.

What's next for Bardghji and Barcelona

Bardghji's rehabilitation will be closely monitored, with the club prioritising a cautious return to full fitness. The coming months will be critical for his recovery, and Barcelona will hope the young winger comes back stronger.

For the club, the focus shifts to managing the squad through the early part of the season without him. Whether that means a late-window signing or relying on existing resources remains to be seen. All eyes will be on the club's next move as the transfer deadline approaches.
