Ravi Kishan's Wife Jokes About Locking Him Up Amid Meme Fame

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ravi Kishan says wife Preeti jokingly threatens to lock him indoors to curb his viral fame.
  • The actor-politician has become a meme icon, with his dance moves and expressions being widely shared.
  • He cites SRK and Michael Jackson as inspirations behind his energetic stage presence.

From Bhojpuri Star to Meme Icon

Ravi Kishan, the Bhojpuri superstar turned politician, has found himself at the centre of a new kind of attention. The internet has turned him into a meme, and his wife, Preeti Kishan, has a rather drastic solution to deal with the frenzy.

In a recent interaction, the actor revealed that his wife jokingly threatens to lock him in a room. The idea, she says, is simple: if he doesn't step out, he won't trend. 'Na yeh niklenge na yeh viral honge'—if he doesn't come out, he won't go viral—she reportedly tells him.

A Wife's 'Solution' to Viral Fame

Preeti's remark comes as Ravi Kishan's dance moves and expressions continue to dominate social media timelines. What began as a few shared clips has snowballed into a full-blown trend, with users remixing his stage performances and dialogue delivery.

The actor, who has been in the public eye for decades, says he takes the meme culture in his stride. He laughs off the idea of being confined, but admits that the online response has been overwhelming. His wife, however, seems to have had enough of the constant attention.

What's Driving the Meme Wave?

The latest spike in Ravi Kishan's popularity appears to be linked to his energetic stage presence. He has often been seen dancing with full enthusiasm at public events, and those moments are now being repackaged as viral clips. The actor himself has acknowledged the trend, saying he is not bothered by it.

In a separate conversation, he explained his recent maun vrat (silence vow), which left many fans puzzled. He said the decision was his own, aimed at introspection. But the internet had other theories, and the silence only added to his mystique.

SRK and Michael Jackson Inside Him

When asked about his dance style, Ravi Kishan was candid. He said he feels the energy of Shah Rukh Khan and Michael Jackson in his body when he performs. It is a statement that has itself become a talking point, adding another layer to his online persona.

The actor-politician seems to be enjoying the moment, even as his wife threatens to ground him. He has also addressed Gen Z directly, saying they have left him speechless with their creativity. For a man used to commanding attention, the tables have turned in an unexpected way.

Life in the Public Eye

Ravi Kishan's career has spanned films, television, and politics. He is known for his outspoken nature and his connect with the masses. The current meme wave, however, is a different beast altogether. It is not about his films or his speeches, but about how the internet chooses to see him.

His wife's comment, though made in jest, reflects a common sentiment among families of public figures. Viral fame comes with no manual, and sometimes the only reaction is to laugh—or lock the person in a room.

As the trend continues, Ravi Kishan shows no signs of slowing down. He is likely to keep appearing at events, dancing with the same vigour, and giving the internet more material to work with.

What remains to be seen is whether the meme wave will fizzle out or take on a life of its own. For now, the actor seems happy to ride it, even if his wife is ready to pull the plug.
