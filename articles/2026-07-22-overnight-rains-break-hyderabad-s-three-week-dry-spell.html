Overnight rains break Hyderabad's three-week dry spell

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Overnight rains broke a three-week dry spell in Hyderabad.
  • The showers brought relief from soaring temperatures and humidity.
  • Water supply concerns had been growing due to the prolonged dry period.

Respite after weeks of dry heat

After three weeks of relentless dry weather, Hyderabad finally saw its first significant rainfall overnight. The showers, which began late Monday and continued into Tuesday morning, brought much-needed relief to residents and the city's water infrastructure.

The dry spell had pushed temperatures consistently above 35 degrees Celsius, with humidity making conditions uncomfortable for daily life. Streets that had been dusty and parched for weeks were suddenly slick with water, and the air turned cooler and fresher by dawn.

Impact on daily life and water supply

For many Hyderabadis, the rain meant a break from fanning themselves and a chance to sleep without air conditioning. Early morning commuters found wet roads and occasional puddles, but traffic moved smoothly as the downpour was not heavy enough to cause waterlogging.

The city's water reservoirs, which had been depleting steadily due to the dry spell, received a modest boost. Officials from the Hyderabad Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board noted that while the rain would not solve the deficit entirely, it would reduce the immediate pressure on supply schedules.

Weather department's forecast

The India Meteorological Department's Hyderabad centre attributed the rain to a trough of low pressure over the Bay of Bengal, which pulled moist winds inland. They said similar conditions could persist for the next 24 to 48 hours, with a chance of more light to moderate showers.

Residents have been advised to stay cautious during thunderstorms, though no major weather warnings have been issued for the city. The Met department expects the rain activity to taper off by mid-week.

Relief for farmers and gardeners

Beyond the city limits, the overnight showers came as a boon for farmers in the surrounding districts who had been watching their crops wither. The dry spell had particularly affected vegetable and pulse crops, and the rain will help revive them if more follows in the coming days.

Gardeners and park authorities across Hyderabad also welcomed the downpour. Many public parks, which had been watering plants manually to keep them alive, will now see a natural recovery.

What to watch: The weather department will issue an update on Wednesday morning. If the trough strengthens, more widespread rain could be on the cards for the region later this week.
