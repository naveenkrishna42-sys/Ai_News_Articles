Tambaram police launch AI-powered WhatsApp bot for public

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tambaram police have launched an AI-powered WhatsApp bot.
  • The bot aims to simplify complaint registration and information queries.
  • Residents can access services through a dedicated WhatsApp number.

Tambaram police have taken a digital leap forward with the launch of an AI-powered WhatsApp bot, designed to make public interactions smoother and faster. The initiative, rolled out this week, allows residents to file complaints, check case status, and access frequently requested information without visiting a police station.

How the bot works

The bot operates through a dedicated WhatsApp number, which police have publicised across local stations and social media. Users can send a message in simple language—English or Tamil—and the system responds with relevant options, guiding them through the process.

For complaint filing, the bot collects preliminary details such as the nature of the issue, location, and contact information. It then generates a reference number and forwards the complaint to the appropriate officer for follow-up. Officials say this reduces paperwork and cuts down on initial response time.

Beyond complaints: information at your fingertips

The bot also handles routine queries—like how to obtain a police clearance certificate, the location of the nearest station, or the contact details of beat officers. It can even provide updates on lost-and-found items, a common request at many stations.

"We wanted to meet people where they already are—on WhatsApp," said a senior officer involved in the project. "The bot is trained on our standard procedures, so answers are consistent and reliable." The officer added that the system is being monitored closely to ensure accuracy.

A step toward smarter policing

This initiative is part of a broader push by Tamil Nadu police to adopt technology for citizen services. While the state has experimented with apps and online portals, this is among the first instances of an AI bot integrated with a messaging platform in the Chennai region.

For residents, the benefits are clear: no more long queues for simple queries, and a transparent trail of their requests. For officers, it frees up time to focus on field duties.

Challenges and next steps

However, not everything is seamless. The bot is currently in its initial phase, and police acknowledge that some complex complaints—like those involving multiple parties or legal nuances—will still require human intervention. "The bot handles the routine; our staff handle the rest," the officer clarified.

Tambaram police plan to expand the bot's capabilities in the coming months, including multilingual support and integration with the state's crime records database. They are also collecting user feedback to refine the system.

As the pilot progresses, all eyes will be on how effectively the bot reduces footfall at stations and improves response efficiency. If successful, this model could be replicated across other police districts in Tamil Nadu.
