Saira Banu's lifelong admiration for Dilip Kumar

Legendary actress Saira Banu, 82, has opened up about her lifelong admiration for Dilip Kumar, whom she dreamed of marrying as a 12-year-old. The revelation came during her 82nd birthday celebrations, where she reflected on the profound impact Dilip Kumar had on her life.

Childhood dreams and mother's gift

Saira Banu recalled that she had been 'hopelessly smitten' with Dilip Kumar since she was just 12 years old. Her mother, who was deeply influenced by Dilip Kumar's charm and charisma, gifted her an unforgettable present: a house built next to Dilip Kumar's residence. This gesture, Saira Banu believes, was a sign from her mother that she should pursue her dreams of marrying Dilip Kumar.

Unfulfilled dreams and lasting admiration

Despite her mother's encouragement, Saira Banu has not married Dilip Kumar, and the dream remains unfulfilled. However, her admiration for Dilip Kumar has only grown stronger over the years. She continues to be inspired by his legacy and the impact he has had on Indian cinema.

Reflections on a legendary actor
