Tecno Camon 50 Ultra launched in India with 144Hz display, 6500mAh battery

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tecno Camon 50 Ultra 5G launched in India
  • 6.78-inch 1.5K 144Hz AMOLED display
  • IP69K dust and water resistance rating
  • 6500mAh battery

Tecno has launched the Camon 50 Ultra 5G in India, marking a significant addition to its popular Camon series. The device brings a 6.78-inch 1.5K AMOLED display with a 144Hz refresh rate, alongside a robust IP69K rating for dust and water resistance.

The phone is powered by a 6500mAh battery, which is among the largest in its segment. The launch event took place on July 17, as previously announced by the company.

Display and Durability

The 6.78-inch panel offers a sharp 1.5K resolution, which Tecno claims delivers vivid colors and deep blacks. The 144Hz refresh rate ensures smooth scrolling and gaming, while the AMOLED technology supports always-on display features.

An IP69K rating means the device can withstand high-pressure water jets and dust ingress. This is a notable upgrade over the standard IP68 rating found on many flagships, as IP69K certification is typically reserved for rugged devices.

Tecno has not disclosed the exact display brightness or protection glass details at the time of launch. The company is expected to share those specifications in the coming days.

Battery and Charging

The 6500mAh battery is the headline feature for power users. It supports fast charging, though Tecno has not yet revealed the exact wattage. The company has confirmed that the battery can deliver over a day of heavy usage.

Wireless charging support has not been mentioned in the initial launch materials. The phone likely charges via USB-C, which is standard for this price bracket.

Camera and Software

Tecno is positioning the Camon 50 Ultra as a photography-focused device. The camera system includes multiple sensors, though the company has not yet detailed the full specifications. The Camon series is known for its selfie camera capabilities, and the Ultra variant is expected to continue that tradition.

The device runs on Android 14 with Tecno's HiOS skin on top. The company typically promises two major Android updates for its premium models, but that policy has not been confirmed for this model yet.

Pricing and availability details for the Indian market have not been announced. Tecno is expected to reveal them through retail partners and its official website shortly.

The Camon 50 Ultra 5G enters a competitive segment dominated by brands like Xiaomi, Realme, and Motorola. It will need to compete on camera performance and battery life to stand out.

Industry watchers will be keen to see how the IP69K rating holds up in real-world use, as most phones in this price range offer only basic water resistance. The display and battery specs are aggressive for the segment, but final impressions will depend on software optimization and camera tuning.

Tecno is expected to start sales in India within the next few weeks. The company has not yet confirmed whether the Camon 50 Ultra will be available through online-only channels or also in offline retail stores.
