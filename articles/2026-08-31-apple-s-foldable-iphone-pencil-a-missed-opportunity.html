Apple's foldable iPhone Pencil: A missed opportunity?

📅 2026-08-31 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple reportedly developed a magnetic Apple Pencil for its foldable iPhone.
  • The Pencil was scrapped due to design issues, potentially delaying its release.
  • This move could impact Apple's competitive edge in the foldable smartphone market.

Apple's Experiment with a Magnetic Apple Pencil

Apple has reportedly been working on a magnetic Apple Pencil for its foldable iPhone, codenamed iPhone Ultra. This development has sparked interest in the tech community, as it could offer a seamless writing and drawing experience. However, the project has reportedly been scrapped due to design issues.

Why the Apple Pencil for Foldable iPhone Was Scrapped

The magnetic Apple Pencil was reportedly designed to attach to the foldable iPhone in a way that it would not interfere with the device's folding mechanism. However, Apple faced challenges in ensuring the Pencil's design was both functional and aesthetically pleasing. The company reportedly decided to scrap the project to focus on other features.

The Broader Impact on Apple's Foldable Strategy

The scrapping of the Apple Pencil for the foldable iPhone could have significant implications for Apple's strategy in the foldable smartphone market. The Pencil could have been a unique selling point, setting Apple apart from competitors like Samsung and Huawei. Without it, Apple may need to rely on other features to differentiate its foldable iPhone.

Apple's Foldable iPhone: What's Next?

Apple is expected to unveil its foldable iPhone in September. While the magnetic Apple Pencil is not part of the initial plans, Apple may reconsider its design in the future. The company has a history of iterating on its products, and it's possible that the Pencil could make a comeback in a revised form.
