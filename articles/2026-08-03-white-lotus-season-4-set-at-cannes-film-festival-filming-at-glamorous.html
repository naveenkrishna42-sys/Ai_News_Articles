White Lotus Season 4 Set at Cannes Film Festival, Filming at Glamorous Hotels

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Lifestyle Original source ↗
Key points
  • The White Lotus season 4 is set at the Cannes Film Festival.
  • Filming will take place at several glamorous hotels on the French Riviera.
  • The show continues its tradition of exotic, high-end locations.

A New Riviera Setting for the Hit Series

The next chapter of HBO's acclaimed anthology series 'The White Lotus' is heading to the French Riviera, with the fourth season set against the glitz of the Cannes Film Festival. According to Vogue, the show will be filmed at several of the region's most glamorous hotels, continuing its tradition of using stunning, real-world backdrops.

While the network has not officially confirmed the full cast or plot details, the choice of setting promises a rich canvas for creator Mike White's signature satire of wealth, privilege, and social friction. The festival's blend of celebrity, power, and excess offers a natural playground for the show's themes.

Luxury Hotels as the New 'Lotus'

Specific hotel names have not been disclosed, but reports suggest the production will utilise multiple iconic properties along the Côte d'Azur. This approach mirrors past seasons, where the show has turned resorts like the Four Seasons Maui, the San Domenico Palace in Taormina, and the Four Seasons Koh Samui into characters in their own right.

The choice of Cannes is particularly apt, given the show's own ties to the film industry. Previous seasons have featured actors and industry figures as guests, and setting the story during the world's most famous film festival allows for a meta-commentary on the very world the show inhabits.

A Shift from the Past

This marks a departure from the show's earlier pattern of using single, isolated resorts. The Cannes setting implies a more sprawling, event-driven narrative, perhaps following multiple groups across different hotels and festival events. This could allow for a more dynamic and fragmented story, reflecting the chaotic energy of the festival itself.

Production is expected to begin later this year, with a release likely scheduled for 2026 or early 2027. Fans of the show are already speculating about which actors might appear, and how the festival's red carpets and velvet ropes will be skewered by the show's sharp writing.

Anticipation Builds for Season 4

The announcement has generated significant buzz, particularly among fashion and travel enthusiasts who follow the show for its aesthetic appeal. The Cannes setting ensures a visual feast of couture, yachts, and glamorous soirées, even as the narrative promises to peel back the veneer.

As with previous seasons, the new location is expected to boost tourism to the region, with fans eager to visit the filming sites. Local authorities in Cannes have reportedly welcomed the production, anticipating a significant economic boost during the filming period.

What remains to be seen is how the show will handle the logistics of filming during an actual festival, or whether it will recreate the event out of season. Either way, the fourth season is shaping up to be one of the most anticipated yet, with a setting that promises both spectacle and satire in equal measure.
