Spider-Man: Brand New Day Advance Booking Crosses Rs 25 Crore in India

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Advance booking for Spider-Man: Brand New Day has crossed Rs 25 crore in India.
  • The Tom Holland starrer is set for a strong opening weekend at the Indian box office.
  • The film faces competition from other summer releases, including The Odyssey in China.

Tom Holland's latest outing as the web-slinger is already breaking records before its official release. Spider-Man: Brand New Day has clocked pre-sales of Rs 25 crore in India, according to industry tracking reports.

The advance booking numbers, reported by Bollywood Hungama and other outlets, point to a massive opening weekend for the Marvel film. Fans have been lining up to book tickets since the预售 window opened, with multiplexes in major cities reporting sold-out shows for the first few days.

A Strong Start for the Summer Blockbuster

The Rs 25 crore figure marks one of the highest advance booking totals for a Hollywood release in India this year. Industry analysts expect the film to easily cross the Rs 100 crore mark in its opening weekend domestically.

BookMyShow and PVR INOX have both reported heavy traffic on their platforms. Premium formats like IMAX and 4DX are seeing particularly strong demand, with many shows already at full capacity.

Competition in the Chinese Market

While India is buzzing, the film faces a tougher battle in China. Global Times reports that Spider-Man: Brand New Day and The Odyssey are both vying for audience attention amid a crowded summer box-office season in the world's second-largest film market.

Chinese audiences have historically been receptive to superhero films, but the competition from local releases could dent the film's overall collection there. Early tracking suggests the film may open lower than its predecessor in China.

Tom Holland on Playing Peter Parker

In a recent interview with The New Indian Express, Tom Holland reflected on his connection to the character. He said his life has some kind of parallel with Peter Parker, drawing comparisons between the actor's own journey in the spotlight and the superhero's struggles with identity and responsibility.

Holland has played the character in six films so far, and Brand New Day marks a new chapter for the franchise. The film introduces fresh villains and a different emotional arc for the young hero.

What to Expect from Brand New Day

Directed by a new creative team, the film promises a lighter tone compared to the multiverse-spanning No Way Home. Early reviews have praised Holland's performance and the film's visual effects.

The advance booking numbers suggest that Indian audiences are eager to see the new direction. With a strong marketing campaign and positive word-of-mouth from early screenings, the film is positioned to be one of the biggest Hollywood releases of the year in India.

All eyes will be on the opening day collections when the film hits theatres this Friday. If the advance booking trend holds, Spider-Man: Brand New Day could swing past the Rs 50 crore mark on day one alone.
