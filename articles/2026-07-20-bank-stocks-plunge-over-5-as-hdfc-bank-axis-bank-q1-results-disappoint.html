Bank Stocks Plunge Over 5% as HDFC Bank, Axis Bank Q1 Results Disappoint

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • NIFTY Bank index slid more than 1.5% during Monday's trade.
  • HDFC Bank and Axis Bank shares tumbled about 5% each post Q1 results.
  • Weak net interest margins and higher provisions weighed on sentiment.
  • ICICI Bank bucked the trend, gaining marginally.
  • Analysts remain divided on near-term outlook for banking stocks.

Mumbai: The NIFTY Bank index took a sharp knock on Monday, sliding over 1.5% as investors punished lenders that reported disappointing April-June quarter earnings. The selloff was led by HDFC Bank and Axis Bank, whose shares tanked nearly 5% each, dragging the broader Nifty down with them.

HDFC Bank reported a net profit that met street estimates, but weak net interest margins and a sequential rise in provisions spooked the market. Axis Bank also faced scrutiny over asset quality concerns, even as its net profit showed growth. Kotak Mahindra Bank and Yes Bank joined the slide, falling up to 5.5% in intraday trade.

HDFC Bank: Margin Pressure Takes Centre Stage

HDFC Bank's Q1 numbers revealed a compression in net interest margin, a key profitability metric. Analysts at several brokerages cut their target prices, citing a slower-than-expected recovery in deposit growth and elevated cost of funds. The stock, a heavyweight in both the Nifty and the NIFTY Bank, has now erased most of its gains made earlier this year.

Despite the selloff, some analysts pointed to positives. The bank's core pre-provision operating profit remained steady, and management indicated that margin pressure could ease in the second half of the financial year. However, the near-term outlook remains cautious.

Axis Bank Trades Lower on Asset Quality Jitters

Axis Bank's Q1 earnings were overshadowed by an uptick in slippages from its corporate loan book. The bank reported higher provisions, which ate into its operating profit. Shares fell 5% in early trade, recovering slightly as the session progressed.

Brokerages remain split. Some believe the stress is transient and that the bank's retail franchise will support growth. Others have flagged that the macro environment is not yet favourable for a sustained re-rating of the stock.

ICICI Bank Stands Out, Others Bleed

ICICI Bank was the lone bright spot in the banking pack. Its shares gained modestly after the lender reported a strong set of Q1 numbers, with healthy loan growth and stable margins. The divergence highlights how stock-specific factors are driving the sector rather than a uniform macro narrative.

Kotak Mahindra Bank and Yes Bank saw declines of 3-4% each. Kotak's results were in line with estimates, but the market chose to book profits after a recent run-up. Yes Bank continues to struggle with legacy asset quality issues.

What Lies Ahead for the Banking Index

The NIFTY Bank index has underperformed the broader Nifty in recent weeks. With most Q1 results now out, the focus shifts to commentary from the Reserve Bank of India on interest rates and liquidity. A cut in the repo rate could provide some relief to lenders grappling with high deposit costs.

For now, investors are treading cautiously. The earnings season has shown that banks with strong retail franchises and stable margins are being rewarded, while those with corporate exposure or margin compression are being sold off. The coming weeks will reveal whether the current weakness is a buying opportunity or a sign of deeper trouble.
