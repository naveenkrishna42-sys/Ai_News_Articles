Oppo Find X10 Leak: Dimensity 9600M, Dual 200MP Cameras Explained

Key points
  • Oppo Find X10 series is tipped to run a MediaTek Dimensity 9600M chipset, with Geekbench scores suggesting a modest single-core gain over the Dimensity 9500.
  • Camera leaks point to dual 200MP sensors on the standard model, while the Pro Max variant reportedly adds an Open Gate video mode for creators.
  • Battery capacity may touch 8,000mAh, and leaked India pricing suggests the top variant could cross Rs. 1.5 lakh.

Oppo's Find X10 series has surfaced on Geekbench, and the benchmark entry points to a MediaTek Dimensity 9600M chipset rather than the standard 9600. Separate leaks from GSMArena, 91mobiles and Gadgets 360 indicate dual 200MP cameras on the base model, a Pro Max variant with Open Gate video, and batteries that could reach 8,000mAh. For Indian buyers weighing a Rs. 1.5 lakh flagship, the details matter more than the hype.

What the Geekbench Listing Actually Tells Us

The Geekbench entry lists a chipset with a prime core, a cluster of performance cores and efficiency cores, paired with a large RAM configuration. The Dimensity 9600M suffix suggests a MediaTek part tuned for sustained performance rather than peak single-core numbers. Expect multi-core gains driven by thermal headroom, not a dramatic architectural jump.

Benchmark listings are pre-release software, so final retail scores will vary with cooling and firmware. Treat leaked numbers as a directional signal, not a purchase benchmark.

Camera Strategy: Why Dual 200MP Matters

Two 200MP sensors on a flagship are unusual. The primary benefit is cropping flexibility: a 200MP main sensor can output a sharp 50MP or 12.5MP image while retaining detail for zoom. A second 200MP sensor, likely telephoto or ultrawide, extends that pixel-binning advantage across focal lengths.

Open Gate on the Pro Max is the more interesting addition. It captures the full sensor area in a taller aspect ratio, giving videographers room to reframe in post without losing resolution. That is a workflow feature aimed at creators, not casual users.

Battery, Charging and Daily Use

An 8,000mAh cell in a flagship would be a significant jump from the 5,000-6,000mAh norm. If accurate, expect two-day endurance for moderate users and lower charging anxiety for travellers. The trade-off is weight and thickness, which Oppo will have to manage carefully to keep the phone pocketable.

India Pricing and Value Assessment

Leaked pricing suggests the top Find X10 variant may cost up to Rs. 1.5 lakh, placing it against the iPhone Pro Max and Samsung Ultra lines. At that price, buyers should expect class-leading cameras, a bright LTPO display, fast wired and wireless charging, and long software support.

  • Pros: Dual 200MP cameras offer rare cropping flexibility; 8,000mAh battery could reset flagship endurance expectations; Dimensity 9600M promises strong sustained multi-core performance.
  • Cons: Leaked specs remain unverified; Rs. 1.5 lakh pricing puts it in ultra-premium territory; larger battery may add bulk; Open Gate video is limited to the Pro Max.

Buyer Verdict

The Find X10 series looks like a camera-first flagship with a battery advantage. Buyers who prioritise zoom versatility and endurance should wait for official pricing and camera samples before committing. Those wanting a proven ecosystem may find better value in current-generation flagships that will likely see price cuts at launch.
