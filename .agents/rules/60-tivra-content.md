# 60 — TivraNews Content Integrity Rules

TivraNews contains news, finance, business, technology, product deals and affiliate content.

## 1. Source-First Policy
- Always ground reporting in authoritative primary sources (e.g. RBI, PIB, Official Gazettes, Bank Master Circulars, Tech OEMs).
- Never fabricate prices, bank FD interest rates, discounts, product specifications, or executive announcements.

## 2. Zero-Placeholder Standard
- Never publish dummy names ("Bank A", "Bank B", "Company X"), placeholder discount percentages, fake URLs, or broken tables.
- Before publishing, enforce automated schema validation to verify that every entity name and interest rate is authentic.

## 3. Financial & Legal Content Strictness
- Time-sensitive financial information (FD rates, loan MCLR, credit card reward rules) must have a visible timestamp and explicit attribution to the issuing institution.
- Clearly distinguish between Official Fact, Historical Rates, and Editorial Interpretation.
