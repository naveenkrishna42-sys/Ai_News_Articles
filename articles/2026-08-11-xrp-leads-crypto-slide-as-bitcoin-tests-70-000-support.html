XRP leads crypto slide as bitcoin tests $70,000 support

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • XRP dropped more than other major cryptocurrencies on Tuesday, leading a broad altcoin decline.
  • Bitcoin traded near $70,000, a level traders see as critical for the next move.
  • Ethereum and other altcoins also slipped, with market sentiment cautious amid macroeconomic uncertainty.

Cryptocurrency markets turned red on Tuesday, with XRP leading the fall as traders fixed their gaze on bitcoin's struggle to hold above $70,000. The Ripple-linked token slipped sharply, dragging Ethereum and other major altcoins down with it, according to data tracked by CoinDesk.

Bitcoin, the world's largest cryptocurrency, hovered near the $70,000 mark, a level that has become a psychological battleground for bulls and bears alike. A sustained break below could trigger further selling, while a rebound might reignite bullish momentum.

XRP under pressure

XRP, the token associated with Ripple Labs, fell more than 4% in the past 24 hours, outpacing losses in most other top cryptocurrencies. The decline comes amid ongoing legal and regulatory overhangs, though no fresh catalyst was immediately apparent.

Traders pointed to profit-taking after a recent rally, as well as broader risk-off sentiment in global markets. Ethereum, the second-largest cryptocurrency, also dropped, though its losses were comparatively milder.

Bitcoin at a crossroads

Bitcoin's price action has been the focal point for traders, with $70,000 acting as a key support level. Analysts note that a decisive close below this mark could open the door to further downside, while a bounce would likely attract fresh buying.

Options markets suggest traders are bracing for volatility, with implied volatility ticking higher. Some investors are hedging against a potential drop toward $65,000, while others see $75,000 as the next upside target if support holds.

Altcoins feel the heat

The broader altcoin market followed XRP's lead, with most top tokens trading in the red. Solana, Binance Coin, and Cardano all posted losses, though none matched XRP's decline. The total cryptocurrency market capitalization slipped by around 2% over the day.

Liquidity in altcoins remains thin compared to bitcoin, which often amplifies price swings. Market participants say the current pullback is not unusual after a strong run, but caution that the next few sessions will be crucial.

What to watch

Traders are now eyeing upcoming US inflation data, which could influence the Federal Reserve's policy path and, by extension, risk assets like cryptocurrencies. A hotter-than-expected print could pressure bitcoin further, while a cooler number might provide a tailwind.

For now, the $70,000 level remains the line in the sand. How bitcoin closes the week will likely set the tone for XRP and other altcoins in the near term.
