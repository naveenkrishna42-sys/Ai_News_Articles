Market Context and Recent Trend

On Monday, 28 September 2026, the Sensex closed near 68,450 while the Nifty settled around 20,380. Both indices showed a modest gain of about 0.4% amid mixed global cues and steady domestic liquidity. The India VIX hovered near 13.5, indicating relatively low expected volatility for the coming session.

Global Cues: Kospi and Taiwan Markets

Kospi Influence

The Kospi ended the prior session up 0.6% after positive data on South Korean exports and a dovish tilt from the Bank of Korea. Historically, a positive Kospi open tends to lift Asian‑linked stocks in India, especially in technology and auto ancillaries.

Taiwan Market Signals

Taiwan's weighted index slipped 0.2% amid cautious sentiment ahead of TSMC's quarterly guidance. Semiconductor stocks listed on Indian exchanges often mirror Taiwan's moves; any weakness could exert pressure on IT and hardware counters.

Domestic Drivers

RBI Policy Outlook

Market participants await the RBI's monetary policy review scheduled for early October. Expectations of a status quo on repo rates have kept bond yields stable, supporting equity valuations. Any surprise hike could trigger profit‑booking in rate‑sensitive sectors like banking and real estate.

Q2 Earnings Momentum

Second‑quarter earnings disclosures have begun, with several large‑cap companies reporting revenue growth in the 8‑12% range year‑on‑year. Strong results in consumer goods and pharmaceuticals have provided a floor for the Nifty, while mixed performance in metals keeps sectoral rotation active.

Investment Considerations

  • Pros: Steady domestic liquidity, supportive global cues from Kospi, and improving Q2 earnings provide a constructive backdrop for selective buying.
  • Cons: External volatility from Taiwan's semiconductor outlook and uncertainty around RBI policy could trigger short‑term swings.
  • Actionable: Traders may consider long positions in large‑cap IT and FMCG on dips near support, while keeping stop‑losses below recent lows. Sectoral rotation toward defensives is advisable if the VIX spikes above 18.
