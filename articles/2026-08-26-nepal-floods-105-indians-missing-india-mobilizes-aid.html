Nepal floods: 105 Indians missing, India mobilizes aid

📅 2026-08-26 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • 105 Indians missing after Nepal flash floods
  • India to send relief materials; China coordinates search efforts
  • Monsoon 'low' over India blamed for extreme weather

Nepal Flash Floods Leave 105 Indians Missing

Nepal is grappling with severe flash floods that have left at least 105 Indians missing, with 384 tourists in total reported missing, according to reports. The floods, which struck over the weekend, have caused widespread destruction, with entire villages submerged and infrastructure damaged. The Indian government has confirmed that it will send relief materials to Nepal to assist in the rescue and recovery efforts.

India and China Coordinate Response

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has spoken with his Nepalese counterpart, Pushpa Kamal Dahal, to coordinate the relief efforts. China has also pledged its support, with President Xi Jinping ordering an 'all-out search' for the missing tourists. The Indian government has mobilized its disaster response teams and is preparing to deploy additional resources to Nepal.

Monsoon 'Low' Over India Blamed for Extreme Weather

Experts attribute the severe weather conditions to a monsoon 'low' that has converged over India, bringing heavy rainfall and flooding. The Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) has warned of further rain and flooding in the coming days, urging residents to stay vigilant and prepared.

Broader Impact and Analysis

The flash floods in Nepal have highlighted the vulnerability of the region to extreme weather events. The missing persons include both Indians and Nepalese, with the Indian government's swift response indicating the importance of regional cooperation in times of crisis. The monsoon 'low' over India has been a significant factor in the severe weather, with experts warning of further rain and flooding in the coming days.
