IHG One Rewards and Air India Launch Triple Miles Promotion for Late 2026 Stays

IHG One Rewards has partnered with Air India to offer triple miles on qualifying stays between September 15 and December 31, 2026. This promotion targets travellers who are members of both IHG One Rewards and Air India's Maharaja Club, allowing them to earn 3x the usual Air India miles on IHG stays. While the headline sounds lucrative, the actual value depends on your base earning rate and how you redeem Maharaja Club miles. Here's what you need to know before booking.

How the Triple Miles Promotion Works

Normally, IHG One Rewards members can choose to earn Air India miles instead of IHG points on their stays. The standard earning rate is 2 Air India miles per US dollar spent on qualifying room rates. With this promotion, that rate jumps to 6 miles per US dollar. For a 3-night stay costing USD 300 (approximately ₹25,000), you'd earn 1,800 Air India miles instead of 600. That's a 3x increase, but the absolute number remains modest compared to credit card sign-up bonuses or other airline promos.

The promotion applies to stays at most IHG brands, including InterContinental, Crowne Plaza, Holiday Inn, and Indigo. However, some franchise properties may not participate, so check the terms for your specific hotel. Registration is required before checkout, and you must be an IHG One Rewards member who has selected Air India as your earning preference in your profile.

Real-World Value and Redemption Strategy

Air India's Maharaja Club miles are most valuable for domestic India flights, where award tickets start at around 5,000 miles for short-haul routes. For example, a one-way Delhi-Mumbai flight might require 7,500 miles plus taxes, while revenue fares often exceed ₹5,000. That gives a redemption value of roughly 0.6 to 0.8 rupees per mile. Under this promo, earning 6 miles per USD spent translates to about 3.6 to 4.8 rupees per USD spent—a 4-6% return on hotel spend, which is respectable but not exceptional.

For international flights, Air India miles can be used on Star Alliance partners, but redemption rates are often less favourable. If you're a frequent IHG stays and Air India flyer, this promo can supplement your mileage balance, especially for short-notice domestic travel. However, if you value IHG points more (which can be worth 0.5 to 0.7 cents each), you might be better off earning points instead of miles.

Pros and Cons of the Triple Miles Offer

  • Pros: Triple miles on all qualifying stays; no minimum stay requirement; stacks with other IHG promotions like bonus points packages; easy registration online.
  • Cons: Base earning rate is low, so 3x is still modest; Air India miles have limited redemption options compared to IHG points; some hotels may not participate; you must forgo IHG points to earn miles.

Maximising Your Returns

To get the most out of this promotion, register early and set your earning preference to Air India miles before your first stay. Combine it with IHG's frequent 'Accelerate' or '2x points' promos if they allow dual earning—though typically you can't earn both points and miles on the same stay. Use a credit card that earns Air India miles or transferable points, such as the Air India SBI Card, to boost your balance further. For stays in India, consider booking Holiday Inn or InterContinental properties that are known to participate. Finally, redeem miles for domestic flights during peak season when cash fares are high.

Final Verdict

The IHG One Rewards Air India triple miles promotion is a solid but niche offer. It rewards IHG loyalists who fly Air India regularly, especially those who book paid stays in India. The 3x multiplier is genuine, but the underlying rate is low, so don't expect a windfall. If you have upcoming stays during the promo period, register and select Air India miles—but run the numbers against IHG points to ensure you're getting the best value.
