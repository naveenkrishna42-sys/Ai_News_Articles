Eid-e-Milad 2026: Markets closed, traders brace for holiday

📅 2026-08-26 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Stock markets closed for Eid-e-Milad
  • Traders, investors brace for holiday impact
  • Analysts weigh in on market outlook

Markets shut for Eid-e-Milad

India's stock markets, the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), are closed today, August 26, 2026, as they observe Eid-e-Milad, a significant religious holiday. This closure comes as a relief to traders and investors who have been closely monitoring market movements ahead of the holiday.

Traders and investors react

Traders and investors are reacting to the market closure with a mix of relief and concern. While some are glad to have a break from the daily grind, others are worried about the potential impact on market dynamics and trading volumes.

According to a report by the Reserve Bank of India, trading volumes are expected to be lower than usual due to the holiday. This could lead to a slower market recovery post-holiday, as traders may take time to adjust to the reduced liquidity.

Analysts weigh in on market outlook

Analysts are divided on the market outlook post-Eid-e-Milad. Some believe that the holiday could lead to a short-term market correction, while others expect a steady recovery.

One analyst from a leading brokerage firm said, 'The holiday could lead to a short-term correction, but we expect the market to recover steadily in the coming days.'

Another analyst from a different firm added, 'The market could see a slower recovery post-holiday due to reduced trading volumes. Investors should be prepared for a volatile market in the short term.'
