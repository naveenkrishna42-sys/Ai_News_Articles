Sensex surges 965 points, Nifty closes above 24,300; IT, auto and bank stocks lead rally

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex surged 965 points, Nifty ended above 24,300.
  • IT, auto and banking stocks led the rally.
  • Financials rallied ahead of key earnings announcements.

Indian stock markets closed sharply higher on Friday, July 17, with the Sensex surging 965 points and the Nifty ending above the 24,300 mark. Broad-based buying, led by IT, auto and banking stocks, powered the rally.

Bulls take charge

The BSE Sensex jumped 964.89 points to close at 79,845.12. The NSE Nifty 50 rose 285.75 points to settle at 24,334.35. This was one of the strongest single-day gains in recent weeks.

All sectoral indices ended in the green. The Nifty IT index climbed over 2%, while auto and bank indices gained more than 1.5% each. Metal and pharma stocks also contributed to the upmove.

Financials rally ahead of earnings

Banking and financial stocks were in focus as the market awaited quarterly earnings from several major lenders. HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and Kotak Mahindra Bank were among the top contributors to the index gains.

Private sector banks led the charge on the Nifty Bank index, which rose over 1.8%. Analysts said expectations of healthy earnings and strong loan growth supported sentiment in the sector.

IT stocks shine

IT stocks extended their recent rally. Infosys and TCS were among the top gainers on the Nifty 50. The sector has been buoyed by strong deal wins and optimism around demand from US and European clients.

Auto stocks also saw strong buying. Maruti Suzuki, Mahindra & Mahindra and Tata Motors all closed higher. The auto sector has been recovering on the back of improving rural demand and new model launches.

Broader markets also performed well, with the BSE Midcap and Smallcap indices rising over 1% each. Market breadth was positive, with about 2,200 stocks advancing against 1,200 declining on the BSE.

Global cues supportive

Positive global cues also aided the rally. Asian markets closed higher, with Japan's Nikkei and Hong Kong's Hang Seng gaining over 1% each. European indices also opened in the green.

Investors are now focusing on the upcoming US Federal Reserve meeting and domestic corporate earnings for further direction. The Indian rupee strengthened slightly against the US dollar, closing at 83.45.

Foreign institutional investors were net buyers in the cash market on Thursday, according to provisional data. Their buying activity is seen as a key support for the market.

Going forward, traders will watch for earnings from Reliance Industries and several IT majors next week. The market's ability to sustain above the 24,300 level on the Nifty will be crucial for further upside.
