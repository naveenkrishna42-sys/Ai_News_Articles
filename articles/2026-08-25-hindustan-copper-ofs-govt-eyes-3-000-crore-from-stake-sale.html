Hindustan Copper OFS: Govt eyes ₹3,000 crore from stake sale

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Business Original source ↗

  • Floor price set at ₹514 per share, 10% discount to market price
  • Potential proceeds of ₹3,000 crore from stake sale
  • Hindustan Copper shares in focus as OFS opens

Government to Sell Up to 6% Stake in Hindustan Copper

The Indian government is set to sell up to 6% stake in Hindustan Copper Limited (HCL) through an Offer for Sale (OFS). This move is part of the government's ongoing efforts to monetize its stake in public sector undertakings (PSUs).

Floor Price Set at ₹514 per Share

The floor price for the OFS has been set at ₹514 per share, which represents a 10% discount to the current market price. This discount is expected to attract investors and ensure a healthy participation in the OFS.

Potential Proceeds of ₹3,000 Crore

With the government holding a 64.1% stake in HCL, the sale of up to 6% stake could generate proceeds of up to ₹3,000 crore. This amount is significant and will contribute to the government's revenue and help in reducing its debt.

Hindustan Copper Shares in Focus

As the OFS opens, shares of Hindustan Copper are likely to be in focus. Investors will be closely watching the response to the OFS and the subsequent movement in the share price. The performance of HCL shares will also provide insights into the market's perception of the company's prospects.

Government Aims to Monetize Stake in PSU

The government's decision to sell its stake in HCL is part of a broader strategy to monetize its holdings in PSUs. This move is expected to generate significant revenue and help in achieving the government's fiscal targets. It also reflects the government's commitment to improving the performance and efficiency of PSUs.
