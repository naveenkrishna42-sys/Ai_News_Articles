Delhi Metro Shuts 16 Stations Over Security Concerns, Commuters Hit Hard

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • DMRC shut 16 metro stations citing security reasons amid a protest.
  • Affected routes include the Red, Yellow, Blue, and Violet lines.
  • Commuters reported long queues and confusion at closed stations.

The Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC) shut down 16 stations across its network on Wednesday morning, citing 'security reasons.' The sudden closures, announced just before peak hours, left thousands of commuters stranded and scrambling for alternative transport.

Why Were the Stations Closed?

The DMRC said in a brief statement that the closures were a precautionary measure. However, sources indicate the move was linked to a protest by the Central Jail Parivar (CJP) in the capital. The CJP has been demanding better conditions for inmates and faster trials.

Officials have not confirmed how long the stations will remain closed. Commuters were advised to use other modes of transport or plan alternate routes.

Full List of Affected Stations

The 16 stations that were shut down span multiple lines. Here is the complete list provided by the DMRC:

  • Red Line: Kashmere Gate, Tis Hazari, Pul Bangash, Pratap Nagar, Shastri Nagar, Inderlok
  • Yellow Line: Rajiv Chowk, Patel Chowk, Central Secretariat, Udyog Bhawan, Lok Kalyan Marg
  • Blue Line: Barakhamba Road, Mandi House, Supreme Court, Indraprastha
  • Violet Line: Delhi Gate, Jama Masjid, Lal Qila

Passengers on these routes were asked to exit at the previous station and find alternative transport.

Commuters Face Chaos and Confusion

Videos and photos from various stations showed long queues of passengers waiting outside locked gates. Many were unaware of the closure until they reached the station. 'I have to reach my office in Noida. No one told me about this. I will be late and might lose my daily wage,' said a commuter at Kashmere Gate.

At Rajiv Chowk, one of the busiest interchanges, security personnel were seen managing crowds. Some commuters complained of plainclothes men with nail-studded sticks at certain locations. The opposition has slammed the government over what they called a heavy-handed crackdown on protesters.

What About Jobs and Livelihoods?

Several commuters expressed anger over the lack of prior notice. 'Who will be responsible if we lose our jobs because of this? I have an important meeting today,' said a passenger at Mandi House. The DMRC has not announced any compensation or special shuttle services for affected passengers.

Auto-rickshaws and buses near closed stations saw a sudden surge in demand. Many passengers ended up paying higher fares to reach their destinations.

Security Measures and Government Response

The Delhi Police has deployed additional personnel near the affected stations. Officials said the situation is under control but advised people to avoid the area if possible. The government has not issued a detailed statement on the protest or the metro closures.

The opposition parties have accused the ruling dispensation of using excessive force. They demanded that the stations be reopened immediately and that commuters' inconvenience be minimised.

The DMRC has said it will issue further updates as the situation develops. No timeline for reopening the stations has been given.

What to watch: Commuters should check the DMRC website or app for real-time updates. The stations are likely to remain closed until the protest is resolved or security concerns are addressed.
