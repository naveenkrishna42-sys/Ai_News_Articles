History-sheeter found dead under suspicious circumstances in Chennai

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Body of a history-sheeter discovered in New Washermenpet
  • Police suspect foul play; investigation underway
  • Area cordoned off for forensic examination

Body discovered in New Washermenpet

Police recovered the body of a man with a criminal record from New Washermenpet on Saturday morning under circumstances that officials say are suspicious. The deceased, identified as a history-sheeter, was found in a narrow lane off the busy residential stretch around 7 am after local residents alerted the control room.

Initial reports suggest the man may have been assaulted before being dumped at the spot, though the exact cause of death is yet to be established. Forensic teams and fingerprint experts were summoned to the location, and the area was cordoned off for several hours as investigators collected evidence.

Victim had a long run-in with the law

The deceased was known to the Washermenpet police station, with multiple cases registered against him over the years, including charges of robbery and grievous hurt. Police sources said he was recently released on bail in a separate case and had been living in the neighbourhood for a few months.

While officers have not ruled out a personal enmity angle, they are also probing whether the death is linked to a turf dispute among local gangs. A senior police official told reporters that "all angles are being examined" and that the post-mortem report will be crucial to determining the sequence of events.

Investigation underway; no arrests yet

Police have registered a case of suspicious death under relevant sections of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita and are questioning residents and shopkeepers in the vicinity. CCTV cameras installed along the main road are being scanned for any suspicious movement in the early hours of Saturday.

No arrests have been made so far, and officials have not yet confirmed whether any weapon was recovered from the scene. The body has been shifted to a government hospital for autopsy, with the report expected within 48 hours.

Area residents on edge

The incident has left the neighbourhood rattled, with many residents expressing concern over safety in the locality. "We often see such elements roaming around, but this is shocking," said a local shopkeeper who did not wish to be named.

Police have increased patrolling in the area as a precautionary measure. Additional forces have been deployed in nearby markets and bus stops to reassure the public and prevent any flare-up.

Investigators are also verifying the victim's recent movements and his associates. They are likely to summon a few known contacts for questioning in the coming days.

What to watch

The post-mortem report and forensic analysis will be key to determining whether the death was a murder or an accident. Police are expected to make a statement once the findings are in, and further developments are likely as investigators piece together the events leading to the discovery.
