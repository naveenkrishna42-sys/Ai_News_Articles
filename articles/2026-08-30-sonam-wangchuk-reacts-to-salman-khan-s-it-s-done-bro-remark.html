Sonam Wangchuk's Reaction Sparks Debate

Sonam Wangchuk, the renowned Indian mountaineer and environmental activist, has reacted to Salman Khan's viral remark during a hunger strike. The remark, 'Sonam, it's done bro,' has sparked a heated debate on accountability and public figures' behavior.

Background of the Incident

Salman Khan, the Bollywood actor, was seen on camera making the remark to Sonam Wangchuk during a hunger strike in Mumbai. The remark was widely shared on social media, leading to a public outcry.

Sonam Wangchuk's Response

Sonam Wangchuk has finally reacted to the remark in a podcast interview with Prakhar Gupta. She stated that the remark was disrespectful and inappropriate, given the serious nature of the hunger strike. She also expressed her disappointment in the behavior of public figures.

The incident has raised questions about the behavior of public figures and their accountability. It has also sparked a debate on the role of celebrities in social activism and their responsibility towards the cause.

Conclusion

The incident has highlighted the need for a more responsible and accountable behavior from public figures. It has also brought to the forefront the debate on the role of celebrities in social activism.
