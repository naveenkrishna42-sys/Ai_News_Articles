Maharashtra FDA Cracks Down on Domino's Pizza

The Maharashtra Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has suspended the licences of four Domino's Pizza outlets in the state. The move comes after FDA raids found the outlets to be violating food safety norms and hygiene standards.

Hygiene Issues Cited as Reason

According to reports, three of the suspended outlets are located in Mumbai, while the fourth is located in another part of the state. The FDA cited hygiene issues as the reason for the suspension, stating that the outlets failed to maintain proper cleanliness and sanitation.

The suspension of the licences is a significant blow to the popular pizza chain, which has a large presence in Maharashtra. The FDA has not yet confirmed the exact duration of the suspension, but it is likely to be in effect until the outlets are able to demonstrate compliance with food safety norms.

Previous Violations

This is not the first time that Domino's Pizza has faced action from the FDA in Maharashtra. In the past, the chain has been fined and warned for violating food safety norms. However, the suspension of licences is a more severe penalty, and highlights the FDA's commitment to ensuring that food establishments in the state maintain high standards of hygiene and safety.

Impact on Consumers

The suspension of the licences is likely to affect consumers who frequent the affected outlets. However, it is also a reminder of the importance of food safety and the need for establishments to maintain high standards of hygiene and sanitation. Consumers can expect the FDA to continue to monitor food establishments in the state and take action against those that fail to comply with norms.

Officials have not yet confirmed the next steps that will be taken, but it is likely that the FDA will conduct further inspections of Domino's Pizza outlets in the state to ensure compliance with food safety norms.

Next Steps

The FDA's action against Domino's Pizza is part of a larger effort to improve food safety in Maharashtra. The state government has been taking steps to strengthen the FDA and increase inspections of food establishments. Consumers can expect to see more action from the FDA in the coming weeks and months as the agency works to ensure that food establishments in the state are safe and hygienic.
