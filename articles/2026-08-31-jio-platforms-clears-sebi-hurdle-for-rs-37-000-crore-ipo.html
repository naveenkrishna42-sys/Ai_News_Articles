Jio Platforms secures SEBI nod for Rs 37,000-crore IPO

Indian telecom operator Jio Platforms has secured approval from the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) for its proposed initial public offering (IPO) worth Rs 37,000 crore. The firm, backed by global tech giants Meta and Google, aims to raise funds to fuel its expansion plans in the Indian market.

Why this matters

The approval marks a significant milestone for Jio Platforms, which has been rapidly expanding its telecom services in India. The IPO is expected to attract significant investor interest, given the company's strong brand and market presence. However, the move also raises questions about the company's financial health and the potential risks associated with the IPO.
