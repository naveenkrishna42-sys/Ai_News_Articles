Karim Adeyemi credits Hansi Flick for Barcelona move

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Karim Adeyemi has completed his transfer from Borussia Dortmund to Barcelona.
  • Adeyemi says Hansi Flick was the decisive factor in his decision.
  • The winger revealed what Flick told him to convince him to join.
  • Adeyemi is looking forward to playing alongside Lamine Yamal.

Karim Adeyemi has revealed that Barcelona head coach Hansi Flick was the primary reason he chose to leave Borussia Dortmund for the Camp Nou. The German winger made the comments during his official presentation as a Barcelona player in Spain.

Adeyemi's transfer from Dortmund had been rumoured for weeks before being finalised. The 24-year-old is the latest addition to Flick's rebuilding project at the Catalan club.

Flick's decisive pitch

Speaking to reporters, Adeyemi did not hold back about the role Flick played. "Hansi Flick is the main reason I'm here," he said. The winger detailed the conversation that convinced him to make the switch.

"He told me he believed in my ability and that I could be a key player for him at Barcelona. That meant a lot. When a coach of his stature shows that kind of faith, you want to repay it."

Adeyemi had worked under Flick during their time together at the German national team setup. The existing relationship clearly helped smooth the transfer negotiations.

Looking ahead to life at Barcelona

Adeyemi also spoke about the prospect of forming an attacking partnership with teenage sensation Lamine Yamal. "I can connect with him," Adeyemi said. "He's young but so mature on the pitch. I think we will understand each other's game well."

The Barcelona squad is undergoing significant changes under Flick's tenure. Adeyemi's arrival adds pace and direct running to the forward line, qualities the German coach has prioritised.

Neither the club nor Adeyemi disclosed the full financial details of the transfer. Reports from Germany had suggested a fee in the region of €40 million, though officials have not yet confirmed the exact figure.

Pressure to perform

Barcelona fans will expect immediate returns from a signing of this magnitude. The club has struggled to return to its peak dominance in recent seasons, and Flick is under pressure to deliver trophies.

Adeyemi's record at Dortmund was mixed. He showed flashes of brilliance but also struggled with consistency and injuries. At Barcelona, he will need to produce at a high level week after week.

The winger is aware of the expectations. "I know what it means to play for this club. I am ready for the challenge," he said.

Barcelona's next competitive fixture will test how quickly Adeyemi can integrate into Flick's system. The club is banking on the coach's personal touch to bring the best out of their new signing.
