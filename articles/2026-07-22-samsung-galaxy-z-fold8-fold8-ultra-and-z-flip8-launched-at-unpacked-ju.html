Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8, Fold8 Ultra and Z Flip8 Launched at Unpacked July 2026

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Three new models: Galaxy Z Fold8, Z Fold8 Ultra, and Z Flip8 were announced.
  • The Z Fold8 features a wider aspect ratio for a more tablet-like experience.
  • Z Fold8 Ultra targets the premium foldable market with advanced specs.
  • Galaxy Z Flip8 gets a 50MP dual camera and a redesigned FlexWindow.

Samsung has pulled the wraps off its next generation of foldable phones at the Galaxy Unpacked event in July 2026. The South Korean giant introduced three devices: the Galaxy Z Fold8, the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, and the Galaxy Z Flip8. The company is positioning the lineup as foldables "perfected for every way of living."

The announcement comes as the foldable market gets more competitive. Chinese rivals have been pushing thinner designs and lower prices. Samsung is fighting back with refinements across the board.

Galaxy Z Fold8 Goes Wide

The standard Galaxy Z Fold8 is getting a significant change. Samsung has widened the aspect ratio of the inner display. Users who found the previous narrow cover screen cramped will find this iteration more natural to use.

The outer display now feels more like a conventional smartphone when folded. The inner screen, when opened, offers a squarer, more tablet-like canvas. This makes multitasking with three apps side-by-side less of a squeeze. Samsung has not yet confirmed exact display sizes or resolution figures for the Indian market.

Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra Aims for the Throne

The Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra is the new flagship of the lineup. Samsung is aiming this at power users who want the absolute best hardware. The Ultra is expected to pack a larger battery and a more advanced camera system than the standard Fold8.

GSMArena reports that the device is aiming for the "foldable crown." It likely includes the latest Snapdragon processor and support for the S Pen. The build quality is expected to be premium, with a titanium or enhanced aluminium frame. Pricing details for India have not been announced yet, but analysts expect it to cross the Rs. 1.5 lakh mark.

Galaxy Z Flip8: 50MP Camera and New FlexWindow

The Galaxy Z Flip8 is getting a major camera upgrade. The clamshell foldable now features a 50MP dual camera setup. This is a big jump from the 12MP sensors seen on previous models. The Time confirms the 50MP primary sensor is paired with an ultrawide lens.

Samsung has also redesigned the FlexWindow, the cover screen on the front. The new design is larger and more functional. Users can now reply to messages, control music, and take selfies with the main camera without opening the phone. The hinge mechanism has also been improved for a tighter fold and reduced crease visibility.

What This Means for India

Samsung is expected to launch these devices in India within the next few weeks. The company has been aggressively pushing its premium segment in the country. The Fold8 series competes directly with offerings from OnePlus and Vivo in the high-end foldable space.

Trade-in offers and bank discounts are likely to be aggressive at launch. Samsung is banking on the wider screen of the Fold8 and the camera upgrade on the Flip8 to attract upgraders. The Ultra model will cater to a niche but lucrative audience of early adopters and tech enthusiasts.

The next few weeks will reveal Indian pricing and availability. Samsung will also need to address service and repair concerns, which remain a key factor for foldable phone buyers in the country.
