Hyderabad IPS trainee accuses batchmate of throwing condoms at her, assault

📅 2026-07-20 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • A woman IPS trainee at Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel National Police Academy in Hyderabad filed a complaint against a male batchmate.
  • The complaint alleges groping, stalking, and the man throwing condoms at her.
  • The accused trainee officer has been booked under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code.
  • Reports indicate the accused officer collapsed after consuming alcohol and was hospitalised.
  • The accused officer is also reported to have attempted suicide.

Complaint Filed at National Police Academy

A woman trainee IPS officer at the Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel National Police Academy (NPA) in Hyderabad has lodged a formal complaint against a male batchmate. The allegations include sexual assault, stalking, and the man throwing condoms at her.

The complaint was filed with the academy authorities, who subsequently registered a case. The accused officer has been booked under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including those dealing with sexual harassment and assault.

Details of the Allegations

The woman officer, in her complaint, stated that the harassment had been ongoing for some time. She accused the male trainee of groping her and stalking her movements within the academy campus. The incident involving condoms was cited as a particularly degrading act.

Sources at the academy confirmed that the accused officer has been placed under watch. The NPA has not yet issued a detailed public statement on the matter, stating that an internal inquiry is underway.

Accused Officer's Condition

In a separate but related development, the accused trainee officer reportedly collapsed after consuming alcohol. He was immediately rushed to a nearby hospital. Medical officials have not yet confirmed his current condition.

Further reports suggest that the accused officer made an attempt to end his life following the registration of the case. The exact sequence of events leading to this is still unclear, and authorities are investigating the circumstances.

The woman officer has been provided security and is reportedly cooperating with the investigation. She has not spoken publicly since filing the complaint.

Wider Context

The National Police Academy is a premier institution for training India's top police officers. Incidents of this nature within its hallowed grounds are rare and draw significant scrutiny. The case has sparked discussions about the safety and conduct of trainees in such institutions.

Senior officials from the Ministry of Home Affairs are said to be monitoring the situation. The NPA has been instructed to conduct a thorough and time-bound inquiry.

What happens next: The internal inquiry will determine disciplinary action, while a police investigation will proceed on the criminal complaint. The academy may also review its protocols for handling harassment complaints.
