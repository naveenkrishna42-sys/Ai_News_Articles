Student Politics Take Center Stage

In a surprising turn of events, the student wings of the Akali Dal and the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) have joined forces to contest university elections. This development comes amid speculation about the future of the Akali-BJP alliance, which has been a key political force in Punjab.

Why This Matters

The collaboration between the Akali Dal and BJP student wings is significant because it reflects a strategic move to tap into the growing influence of student politics. As universities become hotbeds of political activity, parties are increasingly focusing on student wings to mobilize young voters.
