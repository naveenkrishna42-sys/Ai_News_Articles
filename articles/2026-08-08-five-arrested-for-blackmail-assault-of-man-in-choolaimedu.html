Five arrested for blackmail, assault of man in Choolaimedu

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Five people arrested in Choolaimedu for blackmail and assault
  • Victim was threatened with morphed photographs
  • Police are investigating if more people were targeted

The Chennai police have arrested five people for allegedly blackmailing and assaulting a man in Choolaimedu. The arrests came after the victim lodged a complaint at the Choolaimedu police station earlier this week.

The accused, whose names have not been officially released, are believed to be residents of the same locality. Police said the gang had been harassing the victim for some time before the situation escalated into physical violence.

How the blackmail unfolded

According to preliminary investigations, the accused allegedly obtained morphed photographs of the victim and threatened to circulate them. They demanded money in exchange for keeping the images private.

When the victim refused to pay, the gang turned to intimidation. The assault took place in a public area, drawing the attention of passersby who alerted the police.

Victim's complaint leads to swift action

The victim, a man in his 30s, approached the police with a detailed complaint. Based on his statement, a case was registered under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including extortion and assault.

Police teams scanned CCTV footage from the area and tracked down the suspects within 48 hours. All five were taken into custody and are currently being questioned.

Police probe extends beyond the single case

Investigators are now looking into whether the gang was involved in similar crimes in other parts of the city. Sources said the accused may have targeted multiple victims using the same modus operandi.

The police have also seized mobile phones and other digital evidence from the accused. Forensic examination is underway to identify any other potential victims whose photos may have been doctored.

Public urged to report such incidents

Cybercrime and extortion through morphed images have become a growing concern in urban areas. Police have urged citizens to immediately report any such attempts rather than giving in to demands.

Officials have not yet confirmed whether the accused have any prior criminal record. Their remand proceedings are expected to be completed shortly.

What happens next

The five accused remain in judicial custody as the investigation continues. The police are expected to file a chargesheet once the forensic analysis of the seized devices is complete. Further arrests cannot be ruled out if the probe uncovers additional links.
