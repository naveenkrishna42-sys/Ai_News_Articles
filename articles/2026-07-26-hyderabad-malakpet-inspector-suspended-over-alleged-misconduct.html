Hyderabad: Malakpet Inspector Suspended Over Alleged Misconduct

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Malakpet police inspector suspended by Hyderabad police commissioner.
  • Suspension follows a departmental inquiry into alleged misconduct.
  • Action taken to enforce discipline within the police force.

The Hyderabad police have placed the Inspector of the Malakpet police station under suspension, officials confirmed on Sunday. The action stems from a departmental inquiry that found the officer guilty of misconduct.

Departmental Inquiry Findings

The inquiry was initiated after complaints surfaced regarding the inspector's conduct during official duties. Sources within the police department said the probe revealed lapses in adherence to standard operating procedures.

Senior officers reviewed the inquiry report and recommended disciplinary action. The suspension order was issued by the office of the Hyderabad Police Commissioner.

Reasons for Suspension

While the exact nature of the misconduct has not been officially disclosed, police sources indicate it relates to dereliction of duty and alleged irregularities in handling certain cases. The inspector has been asked to report to the police headquarters pending further orders.

“Strict action has been taken to send a clear message that any deviation from duty will not be tolerated,” a senior police officer said, speaking on condition of anonymity.

Impact on Local Policing

The suspension has temporarily left the Malakpet station without a permanent inspector. A sub-inspector has been given additional charge of the station until a replacement is appointed.

Local residents and traders in the Malakpet area, a busy commercial and residential locality, have expressed mixed reactions. Some welcomed the action, saying it would improve accountability, while others hoped it would not affect routine policing.

Broader Context

The Hyderabad police have recently stepped up internal vigilance to ensure discipline among personnel. Several similar actions have been taken in the past year against officers found violating conduct rules.

Police officials have not ruled out a deeper investigation into the inspector's tenure. The officer has the option to appeal the suspension through departmental channels.

What happens next: The inspector will face a formal departmental inquiry, and based on its findings, further action—including possible criminal charges—could be initiated.
