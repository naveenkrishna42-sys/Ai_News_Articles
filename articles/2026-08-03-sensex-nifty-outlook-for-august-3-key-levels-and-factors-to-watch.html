Sensex, Nifty outlook for August 3: Key levels and factors to watch

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nifty 50 and Sensex expected to open flat-to-positive on August 3, tracking global markets and crude oil prices.
  • Key support for Nifty seen near 24,800, with resistance at 25,100, say technical analysts.
  • FII and DII flows, along with US Fed rate cues, will dictate intraday movement.
  • Investors to watch banking and IT stocks, while midcaps may see stock-specific action.

Market opening cues

Indian benchmark indices, the Sensex and Nifty 50, are likely to start the trading session on a steady note on Monday, August 3, as global cues remain mixed. While US markets closed with modest gains in the previous session, Asian peers traded cautiously amid fresh geopolitical tensions and uncertainty over global interest rates.

Gift Nifty, the early indicator for Indian markets, was hovering near the 25,000 mark, suggesting a flat-to-positive opening for the domestic indices. Analysts say the market is in a consolidation phase after recent gains, with investors awaiting fresh triggers.

Key levels to watch

Technically, the Nifty 50 has been holding above its short-term moving averages, which is a positive sign. According to market experts, immediate support for the index is placed at 24,800, while resistance is seen at 25,100. A decisive breakout above this level could push the index towards its all-time high.

On the Sensex, the 81,500-81,700 band is likely to act as a support zone, while 82,300 remains a key hurdle. Options data suggests that call writers are active at higher strikes, which may cap upside moves in the near term.

Sectoral and stock-specific action

Banking and financial stocks are expected to remain in focus after the Reserve Bank of India's recent policy stance and quarterly earnings. IT stocks may see a muted opening, tracking overnight moves in US technology shares. Midcap and smallcap indices have been outperforming benchmarks, but volatility could increase in the absence of strong triggers.

Auto and FMCG counters may see stock-specific movement based on monthly sales data released over the weekend. Investors will also keep an eye on the rupee's movement against the dollar, as a weaker currency could weigh on foreign fund flows.

Global factors at play

Crude oil prices, which have been oscillating on supply concerns, remain a key monitorable. Any sharp spike could impact India's import bill and inflation outlook. Meanwhile, the US Federal Reserve's stance on rates continues to influence global risk sentiment, with traders pricing in possible cuts later this year.

Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) have been net buyers in recent sessions, but their activity on Monday will be crucial. Domestic institutional investors (DIIs) have shown resilience, providing support at lower levels.

Strategy for traders

Analysts advise traders to maintain a stock-specific approach and avoid building aggressive positions ahead of key data releases later this week. The market may remain range-bound, with intraday volatility expected around the opening.

For positional traders, a close below the 24,800 support could signal further correction, while a sustained move above 25,100 would open the door for fresh highs. Investors with a longer horizon can use dips to accumulate quality stocks, given the overall uptrend remains intact.

What to watch next

Market participants will now await the release of domestic economic data, including services PMI, and any global cues from US jobs numbers due later this week. The trajectory of the monsoon and its impact on rural demand will also be closely tracked. For now, the market remains in a wait-and-watch mode, with direction likely to be set by global events and FII flows in the coming days.
