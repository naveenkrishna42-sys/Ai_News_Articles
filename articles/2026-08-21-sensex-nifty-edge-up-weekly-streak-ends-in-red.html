Sensex, Nifty Edge Up; Weekly Streak Ends in Red

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty closed with minor gains on Friday.
  • Benchmark indices recorded a cumulative decline for the week.
  • Market sentiment remained cautious despite the late recovery.

Indices Show Modest Recovery

Indian benchmark indices ended the final trading session of the week with marginal gains. The Sensex and Nifty attempted a recovery after a period of downward pressure, yet the modest uptick on Friday was insufficient to offset the losses accumulated over the previous four sessions.

Investors displayed a cautious approach throughout the day. While buying activity picked up in specific sectors, the overall momentum remained constrained by global cues and domestic profit-booking.

Weekly Performance Review

Despite the positive closing on Friday, the markets registered a decline on a weekly basis. The cumulative performance reflects the volatility that characterized the trading sessions since Monday.

Market analysts are observing how the indices handle resistance levels after this week's dip. The inability of the bulls to sustain a strong rally suggests that participants are waiting for clearer triggers before committing fresh capital to large-cap stocks.

Sectoral Trends and Market Outlook

  • Select banking stocks provided minor support to the indices.
  • IT and energy sectors witnessed mixed activity during the session.
  • Mid-cap and small-cap stocks showed varied reactions to the broader market trend.

The divergence between the daily gain and the weekly loss highlights the fragility of the current market sentiment. Traders are keeping a close watch on volume data to determine if the selling pressure is exhausting or if further corrections are likely in the coming week.

Looking Ahead

Market participants will now monitor macroeconomic data releases and institutional investor activity to gauge the direction for the next trading week. Whether the indices can reclaim lost ground will depend on broader sentiment shifts in the coming sessions.
