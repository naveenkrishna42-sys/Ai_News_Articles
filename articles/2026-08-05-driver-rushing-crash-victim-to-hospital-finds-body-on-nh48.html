Driver Rushing Crash Victim to Hospital Finds Body on NH48

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Driver was rushing a crash victim to hospital when he spotted a body on NH48
  • Incident occurred late Tuesday night; police have launched an investigation
  • The body's identity and cause of death are yet to be confirmed

Unexpected discovery on a rescue mission

In a grim turn of events on Tuesday night, a driver rushing an injured person to a hospital stumbled upon a body lying on National Highway 48, near Mumbai. The driver, who was already dealing with an emergency, had to stop and alert authorities about the second incident.

The discovery was made around 10:30 pm, according to initial reports. The driver had been transporting a crash victim when he noticed the lifeless figure on the highway shoulder. He immediately informed the police control room, who dispatched a team to the spot.

Police investigate parallel incidents

Officers from the local police station arrived at the scene and cordoned off the area. The body has been sent for post-mortem, and the identity of the deceased has not been established yet. Senior police officials have said that they are examining whether the death was a result of a hit-and-run or if there were other circumstances involved.

Meanwhile, the original crash victim was taken to a nearby hospital, where their condition is reported to be stable. The driver, who has not been identified publicly, has been praised by locals for his quick thinking in handling both situations.

Highway safety concerns resurface

This incident has once again raised questions about safety on NH48, a busy corridor that connects Mumbai with the rest of the country. Local residents and transport unions have repeatedly demanded better lighting and stricter enforcement of speed limits on this stretch, especially at night.

Officials have not yet confirmed whether the body was that of a pedestrian or a motorist. They are also checking if any CCTV cameras in the vicinity captured the sequence of events leading up to the discovery.

Investigation underway

The police have registered a case of accidental death and are awaiting the post-mortem report to determine the exact cause. They have also appealed to the public for any information that could help identify the deceased or the circumstances surrounding the death.

As the investigation progresses, authorities are expected to review traffic patterns and safety measures on that particular stretch of the highway. For now, the driver's prompt action has ensured that both the injured and the deceased received due attention from the authorities.

What remains to be seen is whether the police will treat this as a standalone case or link it to a larger pattern of accidents on NH48.
