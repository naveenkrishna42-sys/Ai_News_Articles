Man arrested after stabbing three women near Paris Porte de Clichy

📅 2026-07-27 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Three women were stabbed near the Porte de Clichy area in Paris.
  • An off-duty police officer detained the suspect at the scene.
  • The attacker was arrested and taken into custody; his motive is unclear.

Paris, July 27 – A man was arrested on Monday after stabbing three women near the Porte de Clichy area in northwestern Paris, authorities confirmed. The attack occurred in broad daylight, prompting a swift response from law enforcement.

An off-duty police officer who was in the vicinity overpowered the suspect and detained him until reinforcements arrived. The three victims, all women, were rushed to nearby hospitals with knife wounds. Their conditions were not immediately disclosed by officials.

Swift intervention by off-duty officer

Witnesses reported hearing screams near the busy transport hub around midday. The off-duty officer, who has not been named, intervened without waiting for backup. French media described the officer's action as critical in preventing further harm.

The suspect was taken into custody by the Paris police. His identity and possible motive remain under investigation. Anti-terrorism prosecutors have not yet taken over the case, suggesting the attack is not being treated as terror-related at this stage.

Victims hospitalised, investigation ongoing

Emergency services treated the three women at the scene before moving them to hospitals. Officials have not released their ages or nationalities. A security cordon was set up around the area, and police are reviewing CCTV footage to piece together the sequence of events.

This is not the first knife attack in Paris in recent years. The city has seen multiple such incidents, some linked to extremism and others to mental health issues. The current investigation is being led by the Paris judicial police.

Police presence increased in the area

Following the incident, additional police patrols were deployed around Porte de Clichy, a key transit point with metro and train connections. Commuters reported delays as authorities secured the scene. Local residents expressed shock but noted that such attacks have become a recurring concern in the capital.

French Interior Minister Gérald Darmanin has not yet issued a statement. The government has faced pressure to improve public safety in urban areas, particularly transport hubs.

Authorities are expected to provide more details in the coming hours. The suspect remains in custody, and judicial proceedings will follow as investigators determine whether the attack was premeditated or spontaneous.
