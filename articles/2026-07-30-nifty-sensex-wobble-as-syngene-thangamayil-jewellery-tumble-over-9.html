Nifty, Sensex Wobble as Syngene, Thangamayil Jewellery Tumble Over 9%

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nifty and Sensex traded choppy on Thursday amid mixed global cues.
  • Syngene International shares crashed over 9% after a weak earnings report.
  • Thangamayil Jewellery also slumped more than 9%, continuing its recent downtrend.

Indian equity benchmarks opened on a volatile note on Thursday, with the Nifty and Sensex swinging between gains and losses through the morning session. Selling pressure in select heavyweights and a sharp slide in Syngene International and Thangamayil Jewellery weighed on sentiment.

The Nifty 50 was trading near 24,850 levels, while the Sensex hovered around 81,200, as investors weighed mixed global cues and domestic earnings reports.

Syngene International Crashes Over 9%

Shares of Syngene International tumbled more than 9% in early trade, making it one of the biggest losers on the Nifty 500 index. The sharp fall came after the contract research organisation reported a disappointing quarterly performance, missing street estimates on revenue and margins.

Analysts cited weak order inflows and higher operational costs as key drags. The stock had already been under pressure in recent sessions, and Thursday's slump pushed it further into the red zone. Trading volumes were significantly higher than the 30-day average, indicating strong bearish interest.

Brokerages have started trimming their target prices for Syngene, with some downgrading the stock to 'sell' from 'hold'. Investors are now watching for any management commentary on the outlook for the second half of the fiscal year.

Thangamayil Jewellery Slumps Over 9%

Thangamayil Jewellery also sank over 9%, continuing a downward spiral that began after its quarterly numbers were released earlier this week. The jewellery retailer's profit margins took a hit due to higher gold prices and subdued consumer demand during the wedding season.

The company's management had flagged rising competition and inventory challenges, but the market reaction suggests deeper concerns about near-term growth. The stock has lost nearly 25% in the last five trading sessions.

Analysts pointed out that Thangamayil's expansion plans may face headwinds if gold prices remain elevated. The broader jewellery sector has been under pressure, but Thangamayil's decline has been steeper than its peers.

Broader Market Sentiment Mixed

While these two stocks dragged down sentiment, broader market breadth remained mixed. Banking and IT stocks provided some support, though gains were capped. The volatility index, India VIX, inched up slightly, reflecting cautiousness among traders.

Foreign institutional investors were net sellers in the previous session, while domestic institutions bought on dips. The lack of strong triggers on the global front kept most participants on the sidelines.

Oil prices edged higher, adding to inflation concerns, while the rupee remained under pressure against the dollar. Traders are now awaiting the release of monthly GST collections for further directional cues.

What to watch: All eyes will be on how Syngene and Thangamayil close the session. Any recovery could signal a bottom, but further selling may trigger stop-losses and deepen the rout. The next major trigger for the broader market will be the US Fed's policy decision next week.
