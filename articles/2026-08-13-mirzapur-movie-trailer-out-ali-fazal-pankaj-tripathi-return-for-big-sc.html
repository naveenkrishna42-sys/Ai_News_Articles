Mirzapur Movie Trailer Out: Ali Fazal, Pankaj Tripathi Return for Big-Screen Debut

📅 2026-08-13 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Official Hindi trailer of Mirzapur The Movie released on 4th September.
  • Ali Fazal, Pankaj Tripathi, and Divyenndu reprise their roles from the hit web series.
  • The film marks the franchise's transition from streaming to theatrical release.

The wait is over for fans of the blood-soaked Purvanchal saga. The official Hindi trailer of Mirzapur The Movie is out, and it promises the same gritty violence, dark humour, and power play that made the web series a cult phenomenon. The film, starring Ali Fazal, Pankaj Tripathi, and Divyenndu, is set to hit theatres on 4th September.

The trailer, released by Bollywood Hungama, offers a glimpse of a world where loyalty is fleeting and revenge is a family business. While the exact plot remains under wraps, the visuals suggest a high-stakes confrontation that could redraw the map of Mirzapur's underworld.

Return of the Favourites

Ali Fazal reprises his role as Guddu Pandit, the man who has clawed his way through grief and betrayal. Pankaj Tripathi is back as the menacing yet philosophical Kaleen Bhaiya, whose voice can be as deadly as his actions. Divyenndu returns as the volatile Phoolchand, adding to the ensemble that fans have grown to love and fear.

The transition from a streaming series to a full-fledged theatrical film is a significant leap. It signals the makers' confidence in the franchise's pull, which has consistently delivered high-octane drama since its debut.

A Theatrical Homecoming

For years, Mirzapur was a binge-watch favourite on digital platforms. Now, the big-screen version aims to amplify the experience—louder gunshots, wider landscapes of the heartland, and a story that demands collective gasps from a theatre audience.

Trade analysts are already betting on the film's opening weekend. The franchise's massive fan base, combined with the star power of Tripathi and Fazal, could translate into impressive box office numbers.

What to Expect

The trailer is a montage of intense dialogues, fleeting betrayals, and the unmistakable backdrop of Mirzapur's dusty lanes. One thing is clear: the battle for the throne is far from over.

While the film's runtime and full cast list have not been officially confirmed, the core trio's presence is enough to keep anticipation high.

As the release date approaches, expect promotional events, interviews, and perhaps a few more surprises from the makers.

The countdown has begun. On 4th September, the guns of Mirzapur will roar—this time, on the silver screen.
