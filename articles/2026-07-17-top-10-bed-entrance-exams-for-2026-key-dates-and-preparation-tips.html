Top 10 BEd Entrance Exams for 2026: Key Dates and Preparation Tips

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • Central and state-level BEd entrance exams for 2026 include CUET, UP BEd JEE, and Rajasthan PTET.
  • Exams assess general knowledge, teaching aptitude, and subject-specific knowledge.
  • Early preparation and understanding exam patterns are crucial for success.

Why BEd Entrance Exams Matter in 2026

Aspiring teachers across India are already looking ahead to 2026. The Bachelor of Education (BEd) degree remains the standard qualification for teaching in schools, and securing a seat in a good college requires clearing one of the many entrance exams.

Competition is stiff. With lakhs of candidates appearing each year, a structured preparation plan is non-negotiable. Here are the top 10 BEd entrance exams to target for the 2026 academic session.

National-Level Exams

CUET (Common University Entrance Test)

The CUET, conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA), is the gateway to central universities like Delhi University, JNU, and BHU. It tests language comprehension, general awareness, and teaching aptitude.

For BEd, candidates must choose subjects based on their intended teaching stream. The exam is computer-based and typically held in May-June 2026. Early registration is advised as slots fill quickly.

UP BEd JEE (Uttar Pradesh Joint Entrance Examination)

This state-level exam is one of the most popular, with over 3 lakh applicants annually. It covers general knowledge, mental ability, and subject-specific questions.

The exam is held in April-May. The state government uses the scores for admission to government and private BEd colleges across UP. Previous year papers are a good starting point.

State-Level Exams

Rajasthan PTET (Pre-Teacher Education Test)

Rajasthan's PTET is conducted by the state education department. It has two papers: one for primary level (classes 1-5) and one for upper primary (classes 6-8).

The exam is held in June. It tests reasoning, teaching aptitude, and general studies. The syllabus is broad, so starting early helps.

Maharashtra BEd CET

Maharashtra's common entrance test is mandatory for BEd admissions in the state. It covers logical reasoning, data interpretation, and teaching methodology.

The exam is held in May. The Directorate of Higher Education, Maharashtra, releases the schedule. A score in this exam is valid for both government and private colleges.

Madhya Pradesh BEd Samanveet Pariksha

MP's combined entrance test is for both BEd and BElEd (Bachelor of Elementary Education) programs. It tests general knowledge, teaching aptitude, and language proficiency.

The exam is held in June. The state's Professional Examination Board conducts it. Candidates can apply online from March 2026.

Other Key Exams

Telangana State BEd Entrance Test

Telangana's exam is for admission to BEd colleges in the state. It has two papers: one for arts graduates and one for science graduates.

The exam is held in May. The state's education board releases the notification in February. A good score ensures admission to top colleges in Hyderabad and other districts.

Haryana BEd Common Entrance Test

Haryana's exam is conducted by the state's higher education department. It tests general knowledge, reasoning, and teaching aptitude.

The exam is held in June. The state has a reservation policy for Haryana domicile candidates. The application process starts in March.

Punjab BEd Entrance Test

This exam is for admission to BEd colleges in Punjab. It covers English, Punjabi, general knowledge, and teaching aptitude.

The exam is held in May. The state's education department releases the schedule. A merit list is prepared based on the score.

Kerala BEd Common Entrance Test

Kerala's exam is conducted by the Commissioner for Entrance Examinations. It tests general knowledge, teaching aptitude, and subject-specific knowledge.

The exam is held in May. Candidates can apply online from January 2026. The state has a high pass percentage, so competition is intense.

West Bengal BEd Entrance Test (WBBPE)

West Bengal's exam is for admission to government and self-financed BEd colleges. It tests teaching aptitude, general studies, and language proficiency.

The exam is held in June. The West Bengal University of Teachers' Training, Education Planning and Administration (WBUTTEPA) conducts it. The application process starts in February.

Preparation Tips for 2026

Start early. Most exams follow a similar pattern – general knowledge, reasoning, teaching aptitude, and language. Use a common study plan for multiple exams.

Practice with previous year papers. Time management is key. Many exams have negative marking, so accuracy matters. Join a test series for better preparation.

Stay updated on current affairs, especially in education. Read newspapers daily. Focus on Indian education policies and recent reforms.

The 2026 academic session will begin in July-August. Exam notifications will be out from December 2025. Keep an eye on official websites for updates.
