Spider-Man: Brand New Day dominates global box office

📅 2026-08-24 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day remains No. 1 globally, grossing $109M in its fourth weekend.
  • The film has now surpassed $800M worldwide, becoming the sixth highest-grossing movie ever.
  • The Insidious franchise has also reached $800M globally, marking a significant milestone for the horror series.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day's Unstoppable Run

Marvel's Spider-Man: Brand New Day continues to dominate the global box office, maintaining its No. 1 position for the fourth consecutive weekend. The film has grossed an impressive $109 million worldwide, solidifying its place as a cinematic powerhouse.

Breaking Records and Setting Benchmarks

With its latest weekend haul, Spider-Man: Brand New Day has now surpassed the $800 million mark globally. This milestone places the film in the exclusive company of other blockbuster hits, making it the sixth highest-grossing movie of all time. The film's success can be attributed to its engaging storyline, stellar performances, and the enduring appeal of the Spider-Man character.

The Rise of the Insidious Franchise

While Spider-Man: Brand New Day continues to shine, another franchise has also reached significant milestones. The Insidious series has crossed the $800 million mark worldwide, marking a notable achievement for the horror genre. This success highlights the growing popularity of horror films and the ability of franchises to captivate audiences over time.

Pros and Cons of the Box Office Success

  • Pros:
    • Spider-Man: Brand New Day's continued dominance demonstrates the power of well-established franchises.
    • The Insidious franchise's success shows the growing appeal of horror films.
    • Both films have set new benchmarks, inspiring other filmmakers to aim for similar heights.
  • Cons:
    • The success of these films comes at a time when many theaters are struggling due to the rise of streaming services.

In conclusion, Spider-Man: Brand New Day's continued success and the Insidious franchise's milestone are significant indicators of the current state of the global box office. These achievements highlight the power of franchises and the enduring appeal of certain genres, while also pointing to the challenges faced by the industry.
