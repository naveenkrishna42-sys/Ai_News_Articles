Tempsens IPO Allotment Finalised: What You Need to Know

Tempsens Instruments India IPO allotment has been finalised, marking a significant milestone in the company's journey to go public. The IPO, which opened on 18 August 2026, saw strong investor demand with a subscription of 184.22 times. The final price band for the IPO was set at ₹300-₃13, with the grey market premium (GMP) indicating a listing price of around ₹313.

Listing Details

The company is set to list on the stock exchanges on 29 August 2026. The listing price is expected to be ₹313, reflecting the high investor interest and strong market sentiment.

How to Check IPO Status

Investors can check the status of their Tempsens IPO allotment through the following platforms:

  • KFin Technologies: The registrar for the IPO, KFin Technologies, provides detailed information on the allotment status. Investors can log in to their KFin account to check their allotment.
  • NSE (National Stock Exchange): The NSE website offers real-time updates on the IPO allotment. Investors can visit the NSE website and enter their PAN number to check their allotment status.
  • BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange): Similar to the NSE, the BSE website provides updates on the IPO allotment. Investors can check their allotment status by entering their PAN number on the BSE website.
