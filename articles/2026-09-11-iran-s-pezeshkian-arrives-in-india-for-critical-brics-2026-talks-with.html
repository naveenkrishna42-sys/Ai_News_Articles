Pezeshkian's Strategic Delhi Arrival

Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian touched down in New Delhi on Friday morning, marking a pivotal moment in India's diplomatic outreach ahead of the BRICS Summit 2026. The visit comes amid escalating tensions in West Asia, as Israel-Iran conflict continues to reshape regional geopolitics. Pezeshkian is scheduled to meet Prime Minister Narendra Modi later today at the Bharat Mandapam convention centre, where critical discussions on energy partnerships and regional security are expected to dominate the agenda.

BRICS Summit 2026: India's Mediation Mission

India has positioned itself as a key mediator between Iran and Gulf neighbours, particularly UAE, as the BRICS bloc prepares to expand its influence in the Middle East. The summit, hosted by India for the first time, will see participation from all 11 BRICS members including Russia, China, and South Africa. Officials indicate Pezeshkian's itinerary includes bilateral meetings with Modi, Russian President Vladimir Putin, and Chinese Premier Li Qiang to lay groundwork for coordinated positions on regional stability.

Expected Bilateral Outcomes

Diplomatic sources suggest the Modi-Pezeshkian meeting will likely conclude agreements on energy trade corridors, with both nations exploring alternatives to Strait of Hormuz oil shipments through Chabahar port connectivity. India currently imports over 10 lakh barrels daily of crude oil from Iran, representing roughly 15% of its total imports. The leaders may also discuss broader BRICS initiatives on global financial architecture, including potential de-dollarisation efforts and establishment of common payment systems.

Regional Implications Beyond BRICS

The summit arrives at a critical juncture when India faces balancing act between US-aligned counter-terrorism commitments and growing economic dependence on Iranian energy. With Pakistan expected to send its own delegation, the event also serves as a platform for India to showcase its strategic autonomy amid China-Pakistan economic corridor tensions. Security analysts observe that India's hosting of the summit demonstrates confidence in managing internal security challenges while projecting regional leadership credentials.

  • Modi's meeting agenda reportedly includes counter-terrorism cooperation despite differences on Gaza crisis handling
