Sensex, Nifty seen extending rally; key triggers to track today

Indian equity benchmarks are set to extend their winning streak on Tuesday, 7 July 2026, with the Sensex and Nifty likely to open on a firm note. Market participants are banking on positive global cues, sustained domestic buying, and hopes of a healthy earnings season to keep the momentum going.

Analysts, however, caution that the market is trading at elevated valuations, and any negative surprise on the earnings or macro front could trigger profit-taking. At the same time, the undertone remains bullish, with the broader market showing resilience despite intermittent volatility.

Global cues supportive

Overnight gains in global equities, particularly in the US and Asian markets, are expected to provide a tailwind for domestic indices. A stable dollar and easing crude oil prices have also improved risk appetite among investors.

Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) have been net buyers in recent sessions, adding to the positive sentiment. Domestic institutional investors too have maintained their buying interest, providing a cushion against any sharp declines.

Key triggers to watch

Investors will closely track the ongoing quarterly earnings season, with several blue-chip companies slated to announce their numbers this week. Any strong set of results could further boost market sentiment, while disappointments may lead to selective selling.

On the macroeconomic front, the focus will be on inflation data and industrial production numbers scheduled for release later this month. The Reserve Bank of India's monetary policy stance and global central bank actions will also remain on the radar.

Additionally, movement in the rupee, foreign fund flows, and crude oil prices will be monitored for their impact on the market. A sharp rise in oil prices could weigh on sentiment, given India's dependence on imports.

Technical view

From a technical perspective, the Nifty has formed a bullish pattern on the daily charts, indicating strength at higher levels. The immediate resistance is seen around the recent highs, while support is placed at lower levels.

Market experts suggest that as long as the index holds above key support zones, the bias will remain positive. However, they also advise traders to maintain strict stop-losses and avoid aggressive positions at current levels.

Broader market action

The broader market, including midcap and smallcap stocks, has been outperforming the benchmark indices in recent sessions. This trend is likely to continue, with stock-specific action dominating trade.

Sectors such as banking, IT, and auto are expected to remain in focus, while defensive plays like FMCG and pharma may see selective buying. Traders are advised to follow a stock-specific approach rather than taking broad market positions.

Overall, the market is expected to remain open on a positive note, but volatility cannot be ruled out. Participants will keep an eye on global events, including any developments on the geopolitical front, which could influence investor sentiment.

As the session progresses, the trend will depend on how the indices behave at higher levels. A sustained close above key resistance could pave the way for further upside, while a failure to hold gains may lead to consolidation.

Going ahead, the focus will be on the upcoming earnings announcements and macroeconomic data. Market participants will also watch the movement of the rupee and crude oil prices for further direction.
