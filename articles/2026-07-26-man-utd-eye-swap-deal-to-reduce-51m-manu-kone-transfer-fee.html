Man Utd Eye Swap Deal to Reduce £51m Manu Kone Transfer Fee

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Manchester United are looking to use a player as a makeweight to reduce the £51m fee for Manu Kone.
  • The club is in a now-or-never situation to finalise the transfer before the window closes.
  • A potential swap deal involving Marcus Rashford has been rumoured, though not confirmed.

New Strategy to Lower the Price Tag

Manchester United have identified a fresh approach to bring Borussia Mönchengladbach midfielder Manu Kone to Old Trafford. Instead of paying the full £51m release clause, the club is exploring a player-plus-cash swap deal. This 'secret weapon' could significantly reduce the upfront fee and make the transfer more palatable for the club's hierarchy.

United have held 'concrete conversations' with Kone's representatives in recent days. The 24-year-old Frenchman has been a long-term target, and the club now believes it is approaching a now-or-never moment to get the deal over the line.

Rashford Saga Looms Over Talks

Reports from Italy suggest that Roma and Manchester United are manoeuvring around a potential swap involving Marcus Rashford. While no formal offer has been made, the possibility of using the English forward as a bargaining chip has been discussed in backchannel talks. United are keen to offload Rashford's wages, and a move to Serie A could suit all parties.

However, Kone is not the only midfielder on United's radar. A second midfielder has also been offered to the club, indicating that recruitment chief Dan Ashworth is casting a wide net. Yet Kone remains the priority target for new manager Erik ten Hag.

Financial Reality Bites

The £51m fee has been a sticking point. United's summer budget is constrained by Financial Fair Play (FFP) rules and the club's recent spending spree. A swap deal would allow them to book a profit on a player like Rashford, easing the FFP burden while still landing a top target.

Investment analysts at simplywall.st have noted that the Kone deal could reshape the investment case for Manchester United (MANU). A successful transfer would signal the club's intent to rebuild, potentially boosting share prices. But if the deal falls through, it could be seen as a sign of continued dysfunction.

What Happens Next

United are expected to formalise a swap proposal within the next week. The clock is ticking, with the Premier League season just weeks away. If Gladbach reject the player-plus-cash offer, United may have to walk away or trigger the release clause — a move they are increasingly reluctant to make.
