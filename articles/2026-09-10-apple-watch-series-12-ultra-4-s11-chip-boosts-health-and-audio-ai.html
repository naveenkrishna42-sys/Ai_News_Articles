Apple Watch Series 12 & Ultra 4: S11 Chip Boosts Health and Audio AI

✍️ By TIVRA News Tech & Gadgets Desk 📅 2026-09-10 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • S11 chip delivers up to 30% faster sensor processing than S9.
  • Dual‑mic array enables on‑device audio intelligence for health alerts.
  • Ultra 4 starts at ₹1,09,900 in India; Series 12 pricing remains unconfirmed.

What’s new under the hood

Apple’s latest wearables arrive with the second‑generation S11 system‑on‑chip. Built on a 3 nm process, the S11 combines a six‑core CPU, four‑core GPU, and a dedicated Neural Engine that Apple claims is 30 % faster than the S9.

Enhanced sensor pipeline

The upgraded chip accelerates data from the optical heart sensor, blood‑oxygen monitor, and the new temperature‑tracking module. Faster processing reduces latency in health‑related notifications, a crucial factor for users relying on real‑time alerts.

Microphone array for audio intelligence

Both models now host a dual‑mic array tuned for on‑device speech recognition and ambient sound analysis. Apple’s “Audio Intelligence” can detect irregular breathing patterns, loud environments, and even early signs of hypertension, all while keeping data local to the watch.

Real‑world impact for buyers

Beyond raw specs, the S11 and microphones translate into tangible benefits for everyday users and professionals.

Health and fitness upgrades

Instantaneous heart‑rate variability (HRV) readings improve workout feedback. The new “Health Age” metric, displayed in the revamped Health app, aggregates fitness, sleep, and stress data to give a single, easy‑to‑understand score.

Audio‑driven safety features

With on‑device audio analysis, the watch can issue a vibration alert if it detects a sudden spike in ambient noise that may indicate a hazardous environment. For cyclists and hikers, the Ultra 4 can now warn of irregular breathing, prompting a pause for recovery.

Pricing and value proposition

Apple positions the Ultra 4 as a premium adventure companion, launching in India at ₹1,09,900. The Series 12, aimed at mainstream health enthusiasts, has not yet received an official Indian price; analysts predict a range of ₹55,000‑₹65,000 based on previous generations.
