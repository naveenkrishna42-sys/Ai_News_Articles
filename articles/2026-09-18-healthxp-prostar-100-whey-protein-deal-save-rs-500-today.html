Real-World Performance & Daily Use

In daily use, Prostar 100% Whey mixes cleanly with 200–250 ml of water or milk, with only minor clumping if you skip a shaker ball. The texture is moderately thick, and the flavour range — particularly Chocolate and Kesar Pista — avoids the artificial aftertaste that plagues several budget whey concentrates. The inclusion of a digestive enzyme blend is a thoughtful touch; users who typically experience bloating with lower-grade powders report noticeably fewer issues here. The 4 kg tub is bulky and not travel-friendly, so consider decanting into a smaller container for gym bags. Shelf life is generous, and the resealable lid holds up well over months of use. If you prioritise brand legacy, ON remains the safer pick; if you want better digestive comfort and a lower effective price, Prostar wins.
