Sensex, Nifty Set to Extend Gains After RBI Stability Boost

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty expected to open higher, continuing the momentum from the RBI's policy announcement.
  • Experts believe the central bank's measures have improved market sentiment, supporting a positive bias.
  • Key resistance and support levels for Nifty and Sensex will be crucial for intraday traders.

Market Poised for Positive Start

Indian equity benchmarks are expected to extend their recovery on Thursday, 6 August, as investor sentiment remains buoyed by the Reserve Bank of India's recent policy measures. Gift Nifty trends suggest a firm start for the domestic indices, with analysts pointing to sustained buying interest.

The RBI's decision to maintain a status quo on key rates while assuring adequate liquidity has injected a sense of stability into the market. This has prompted traders to build fresh long positions, especially in rate-sensitive sectors.

Expert Views on the Upside

Market experts believe the Nifty could test higher levels if it sustains above the immediate resistance zone. They see the index having a support base near the 24,000 mark, with an upside target of 24,300-24,400 in the near term.

Similarly, the Sensex is likely to find support around 79,000 levels, with a potential rally towards 79,800. Analysts suggest that any dips should be seen as buying opportunities, given the strong undercurrent.

Key Levels to Watch

For the Nifty, the 24,250-24,300 band remains a crucial resistance, and a decisive break above this could trigger short covering. On the downside, 24,050-24,000 is seen as a strong support zone.

The Bank Nifty, which has been volatile, is expected to move in tandem with the broader market. A close above its recent high could pave the way for further upside.

Sectoral Expectations

Rate-sensitive sectors like banking, auto, and realty are likely to remain in focus, given the RBI's accommodative stance. IT and pharma stocks may witness selective buying.

Meanwhile, midcap and smallcap stocks are expected to outperform, as retail participation continues to remain robust.

Global cues, including trends in the US and Asian markets, will also play a role in determining the direction. A stable rupee would add to the positive sentiment.

Trading Strategy

Traders are advised to keep a close watch on the options data for the weekly expiry, which could provide directional cues. A strategy of buying on dips with a strict stop-loss is being recommended by most analysts.

Long-term investors can use any volatility to accumulate quality stocks, especially in the banking and consumption spaces.

As the market builds on the RBI-led stability, the near-term trajectory appears constructive, though profit booking at higher levels cannot be ruled out.

The coming sessions will be key to confirming whether the current momentum can be sustained, with attention also on global oil prices and foreign fund flows.
