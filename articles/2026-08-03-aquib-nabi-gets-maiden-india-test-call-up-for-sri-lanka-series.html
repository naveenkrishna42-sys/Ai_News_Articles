Aquib Nabi Gets Maiden India Test Call-Up for Sri Lanka Series

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Aquib Nabi earns maiden India Test call-up for Sri Lanka series
  • He replaces injured Jasprit Bumrah in the squad
  • India's bowling attack gets a fresh face for the tour

New Face in India's Pace Attack

Uncapped pacer Aquib Nabi has been named in India's Test squad for the upcoming tour of Sri Lanka, replacing the injured Jasprit Bumrah. The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) announced the squad on Monday, confirming the young bowler's maiden call-up to the senior Test team.

Nabi's selection comes as a significant boost for the 23-year-old, who has impressed in domestic cricket with his consistent pace and ability to move the ball both ways. His rise through the ranks has been steady, and this opportunity marks a major milestone in his career.

Bumrah's Injury Blow

Bumrah's absence is a setback for India, given his record as one of the world's premier fast bowlers in all formats. The injury details have not been officially disclosed by the BCCI, but the team management has opted to rest him rather than risk further damage.

His replacement, Nabi, will be expected to step into a leadership role within the pace unit, alongside more experienced bowlers. The selectors have shown faith in his ability to adapt to international conditions, particularly on Sri Lankan pitches that often offer little assistance to fast bowlers.

Squad Composition and Balance

The squad for the Test series retains the core group of players who have been part of India's recent red-ball campaigns. The batting order remains largely unchanged, with the focus on maintaining stability at the top and middle order.

In the bowling department, the absence of Bumrah leaves a void that Nabi will look to fill. The team is expected to rely on a combination of pace and spin, with the spinners likely to play a crucial role in the series. Nabi's inclusion adds variety, giving the captain more options to rotate the attack.

Domestic Credentials

Nabi has been a consistent performer in the Ranji Trophy and India A tours, where he has taken wickets regularly. His ability to generate bounce and extract movement even on flat tracks has caught the eye of selectors.

He has also worked on his fitness and pace, clocking speeds that are competitive at the international level. Those who have followed his career believe he has the temperament to handle the pressure of Test cricket.

What Lies Ahead

The series against Sri Lanka will be a test of India's bench strength, particularly in the bowling department. Nabi will have the chance to learn from the senior players in the squad and prove his mettle on the international stage.

All eyes will be on the team management's strategy for the first Test, including how they deploy Nabi in the attack. His performance in the series could determine his future in the longest format.

The BCCI has not yet confirmed the exact nature of Bumrah's injury or his expected recovery timeline. Updates on his rehabilitation are likely to emerge in the coming weeks.

For now, Nabi's selection signals a new chapter for Indian cricket, as the team looks to build depth ahead of a busy international calendar.
