Chirag Paswan: Govt to engage if reservation demands presented systematically

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Chirag Paswan says government will engage if reservation demands are presented systematically
  • Delhi Police has denied permission for reservation reform protest at Jantar Mantar
  • Mayawati opposes demand for reservation on economic basis, saying it undermines constitutional safeguards
  • Anti-reservation stir: UP Deputy CM promises students meeting with Union minister

Government to engage if demands presented systematically

Chirag Paswan, the Union Minister for Social Justice and Empowerment, has said the government is willing to engage with the protesting community if their demands for reservation reform are presented systematically. The minister made this statement amid ongoing anti-reservation protests across the country. Paswan emphasized that the government is committed to addressing the concerns of the community but only if they are presented in a structured manner.

Police deny permission for protest at Jantar Mantar

The Delhi Police has denied permission for a reservation reform protest to be held at Jantar Mantar. The protest, which was to be organized by various student and political groups, was scheduled to take place on the same day as the ongoing anti-reservation stir. The police cited safety concerns as the reason for denying the permission.

Mayawati opposes demand for reservation on economic basis

Mayawati, the former Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh, has opposed the demand for reservation on an economic basis. She argued that such a demand undermines the constitutional safeguards that have been put in place to protect the interests of the marginalized sections of society. Mayawati's statement comes amid the ongoing anti-reservation protests, which have been led by various student and political groups.

UP Deputy CM promises students meeting with Union minister

The Deputy Chief Minister of Uttar Pradesh has promised to organize a meeting between the protesting students and the Union Minister for Social Justice and Empowerment. The meeting is expected to take place in the coming days and is aimed at addressing the concerns of the students regarding the reservation system. The Deputy CM's statement comes amid the ongoing anti-reservation stir, which has seen protests in various parts of the country.
