Congress leader Mallikarjun Kharge questions PM Modi, BJP over 'Vande Mataram' controversy

Congress leader Mallikarjun Kharge has questioned Prime Minister Narendra Modi and the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) over the 'Vande Mataram' controversy. Kharge, who is also the Union Minister for Information and Broadcasting, has cited the historical significance of the song and questioned why it was not sung in full all these years.

Historical significance of 'Vande Mataram'

'Vande Mataram' is a song that holds immense historical significance in India. It was first published in 1876 in the Bengali newspaper 'Yugantar' and was later included in the 'Anandamath' novel by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee. The song has been a symbol of national pride and unity for Indians for over a century.

BJP to start nationwide 'Vande' campaign

The BJP has announced that it will start a nationwide 'Vande' campaign, invoking Mahatma Gandhi. The campaign aims to promote the values of nationalism and unity in the country. The BJP has also stated that it will encourage people to sing 'Vande Mataram' in full and in its original form.

Attempts to dilute the glory of 'Vande Mataram'

Several attempts have been made to dilute the glory of 'Vande Mataram' over the years. Some people have tried to change the lyrics of the song, while others have tried to remove the song from the national anthem. However, these attempts have never succeeded, and 'Vande Mataram' continues to be a symbol of national pride and unity for Indians.

What happens next?

The 'Vande Mataram' controversy is likely to continue to be a topic of debate in the coming days. The BJP's nationwide 'Vande' campaign is expected to further intensify the debate, and people will continue to express their views on the song and its significance.
