Mumbai’s Taj Hotel bomb threat a hoax after security sweep

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mumbai's Taj Mahal Palace Hotel received a bomb threat call on Sunday morning.
  • Police and hotel security conducted a thorough search of the premises.
  • No suspicious items were found, and the threat was declared a hoax.
  • Investigations are underway to trace the caller.

Threat call triggers security response at iconic hotel

Mumbai's iconic Taj Mahal Palace Hotel was placed on high alert on Sunday morning after a bomb threat call was received. The caller warned of an explosive device placed somewhere on the property.

Hotel security immediately alerted the Mumbai Police, who rushed teams to the scene. Guests and staff were evacuated from certain areas as a precautionary measure.

Thorough search reveals no danger

Police personnel, along with the hotel's security team, conducted a comprehensive search of the building. Every room, corridor, and common area was checked using sniffer dogs and metal detectors.

After a sweep that lasted several hours, officials confirmed that no suspicious items or explosives were found. The threat was officially declared a hoax.

Police investigate the caller

Senior police officers stated that an investigation has been launched to trace the origin of the call. Authorities are examining call records and are working with telecom providers to identify the suspect.

Such hoax calls have become a recurring nuisance in Mumbai, often disrupting operations at high-profile locations. The police have warned that strict legal action will be taken against the perpetrator once identified.

This is the second such incident at a major Mumbai hotel this year. In April, a similar hoax call was made to another five-star property in the city.

Normalcy restored at the hotel

After the all-clear was given, the hotel resumed normal operations. Guests who had been evacuated were allowed back to their rooms. The management thanked the police and security teams for their swift response.

No injuries or panic-related incidents were reported during the evacuation. The hotel continues to operate with enhanced security measures.

What to watch: Police are expected to release more details on the caller's identity in the coming days. The incident may prompt stricter protocols for handling such threats at heritage properties in Mumbai.
