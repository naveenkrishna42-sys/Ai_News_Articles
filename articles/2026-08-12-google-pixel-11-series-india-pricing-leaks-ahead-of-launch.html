Google Pixel 11 series India pricing leaks ahead of launch

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pricing for all four Pixel 11 models reportedly leaked via 91mobiles
  • Pixel 11 Pro Fold may see significant price cut in India
  • Launch event expected to showcase new camera and AI features

Hours before Google's 2026 Pixel hardware launch event, a fresh leak has reportedly revealed the India pricing for the entire Pixel 11 series. The information, published by 91mobiles.com, covers the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and the Pixel 11 Pro Fold, giving prospective buyers an early look at what they might pay.

The leak comes as anticipation builds around Google's annual flagship refresh. Reports from The Hindu and The Verge have outlined expectations for the event, including upgraded cameras, new AI capabilities, and a potential design overhaul. The pricing leak adds a concrete layer to the speculation, though Google has not officially confirmed any figures.

What the leaked prices suggest

According to the 91mobiles report, the base Pixel 11 is expected to start at a price that undercuts its predecessor's launch price in India. The Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL are likely to slot in at higher price points, maintaining the premium tier. The Pixel 11 Pro Fold, however, is said to see a notable price reduction compared to last year's model, which could make foldables more accessible to Indian consumers.

These figures, if accurate, point to a deliberate strategy by Google to compete more aggressively in the Indian market, where rivals like Samsung and OnePlus dominate the premium segment. A lower entry price for the base model could help Google capture a larger share of the mid-to-high-end smartphone buyer base.

Cameras and features under the scanner

Earlier leaks, including one from GSMArena, suggested the Pixel 11 will feature a 10.8MP telephoto camera, a step down in resolution from some competitors but likely paired with Google's computational photography software. The same report indicated the device will not include an RGB notification LED, a feature some users had hoped would return.

Another leak from 9to5Google revealed that Google Contacts has been updated to list colors supported by the Pixel 11 HiLight feature, hinting at enhanced customization options for notifications and caller ID. These details, while minor, paint a picture of a phone focused on software experience rather than hardware spectacle.

India launch and availability

The Pixel 11 series is expected to be available in India shortly after the global launch, with online and offline retail channels likely to be stocked. In recent years, Google has expanded its India presence through partnerships with local retailers and carriers, and the new models are expected to follow a similar rollout.

Analysts will be watching the pricing closely, as India's smartphone market remains highly price-sensitive. A well-priced Pixel 11 could challenge the dominance of Apple's iPhone and Samsung's Galaxy S series in the premium segment, though Google's brand perception in India is still evolving.

What to watch next

All eyes will be on Google's official announcement, expected later today. The company is likely to confirm the leaked prices and reveal final specifications, including battery size, display details, and AI-driven features. Whether the leak proves accurate or not, the event will set the tone for Google's 2026 flagship lineup in India.
