72nd National Film Awards: Kartik Aaryan, Mammootty Share Best Actor Honour

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kartik Aaryan and Mammootty share the Best Actor award at the 72nd National Film Awards.
  • The awards highlighted performances from mainstream and regional cinema.
  • Several debutants and independent films also received honours.

The 72nd National Film Awards announced on Saturday saw a rare tie in the Best Actor category. Kartik Aaryan and veteran Malayalam star Mammootty were declared joint winners.

The jury, led by a prominent filmmaker, chose to recognise both actors for their distinct performances in the past year. Kartik Aaryan won for his role in a commercial blockbuster that also earned critical praise. Mammootty was honoured for a nuanced performance in a Malayalam film that has been widely discussed for its storytelling.

This marks the first National Award for Kartik Aaryan. Mammootty adds another honour to his long career, having previously won National Awards for his work in Malayalam cinema.

Award Highlights Across Categories

The Best Feature Film award went to a Marathi film that explores rural life and family bonds. The Best Popular Film Providing Wholesome Entertainment was bagged by a Hindi drama that became a box office hit.

In the regional categories, a Tamil film won Best Film for its portrayal of social issues. The Best Kannada Film award was given to a experimental narrative set in a coastal town. A Bengali film won the Best Children's Film.

The Best Director award was presented to a relatively young filmmaker for his debut feature, a psychological thriller shot on a modest budget.

Recognising Debut Talents

The Indira Gandhi Award for Best Debut Film of a Director went to a Assamese feature that documents the life of a tea garden worker. The film was praised for its raw, authentic lens.

Best Male Playback Singer was awarded to a singer from the South Indian film industry for a soulful track in a Telugu film. The Best Female Playback Singer award went to a classical vocalist who lent her voice to a folk-inspired number in a Hindi film.

Best Music Direction was shared between two composers: one for a Malayalam film and another for a Hindi film.

Jury's Rationale and Reactions

Jury members stated that the decision to have joint winners in the Best Actor category was unanimous. They noted that both performances were of exceptional calibre and could not be compared.

Kartik Aaryan, reacting to the news, said he was overwhelmed and grateful. Mammootty, in a brief statement, thanked the jury and the film's team.

The awards also acknowledged contributions in special effects, choreography, and costume design, with winners from Tamil and Hindi films taking home the honours.

The ceremony, usually held in New Delhi, is scheduled to take place in the coming months. The event will be hosted by the Directorate of Film Festivals.

As the film industry celebrates these wins, attention now turns to the 73rd National Film Awards, with submissions expected to open later this year.
