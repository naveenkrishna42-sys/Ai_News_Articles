Pralhad Josji Gets Education Ministry After Dharmendra Pradhan's Exit

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Politics Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pralhad Joshi appointed as new Education Minister, replacing Dharmendra Pradhan.
  • Joshi is a seasoned BJP leader from Karnataka, known for organizational skills.
  • The move is seen as part of a broader ministerial reshuffle by the government.

The Union government has handed the charge of the Ministry of Education to BJP veteran Pralhad Joshi. He steps in after Dharmendra Pradhan's exit from the portfolio. The move comes as part of a broader reshuffle that has seen several key ministries change hands.

Who Is Pralhad Joshi?

Pralhad Joshi is a seasoned politician from Karnataka. He has been a member of the Bharatiya Janata Party for decades and has held several organizational positions before entering the Union cabinet.

Joshi previously served as the Union Minister for Parliamentary Affairs, Coal, and Mines. In that role, he was known for steering key legislative business through both Houses. His new assignment puts him in charge of the country's education policy at a time when the National Education Policy is being rolled out.

Born in 1962 in the Dharwad district of Karnataka, Joshi began his political career with the RSS-affiliated student union. He was first elected to the Lok Sabha in 2004 from the Dharwad constituency and has been re-elected since then. His rise within the party has been steady, and he is considered a loyalist with strong organizational skills.

The Exit of Dharmendra Pradhan

Dharmendra Pradhan's exit from the education ministry was sudden. He had been handling the portfolio since 2019 and was seen as a key figure in implementing the New Education Policy. No official reason has been given for his removal, though sources suggest a routine reshuffle.

Pradhan continues to hold other ministerial responsibilities. The change is part of a larger rejig that has also affected ministries like Home, Finance, and Defence. The government has not released a detailed statement on the rationale behind the changes.

What This Means for Education Policy

Joshi takes charge at a critical juncture. The National Education Policy 2020 is in its implementation phase, with states required to align their curriculum and pedagogy. Several states have raised concerns about the three-language formula and the introduction of vocational courses from Class 6.

Joshi's background in parliamentary affairs could help push through pending education bills. He is expected to focus on bridging the gap between the Centre and states over the policy. Educationists are watching closely to see if he brings any shift in approach.

The new minister also faces the challenge of improving India's performance in global education rankings. The country has lagged in learning outcomes, especially in foundational literacy and numeracy. The COVID-19 pandemic exacerbated these issues, leading to learning losses that need urgent attention.

Political Calculations Behind the Move

The reshuffle is seen as an attempt by the BJP to balance regional and caste equations ahead of upcoming state elections. Joshi, a Brahmin face from Karnataka, may help the party consolidate its upper-caste vote bank in the southern state. Karnataka is scheduled to go to polls next year.

By moving Pradhan out, the government has also sent a signal that no portfolio is permanent. This keeps ministers on their toes and allows the leadership to reward loyalists. Joshi's elevation is part of this strategy.

Opposition parties have questioned the timing of the reshuffle, calling it a distraction from key issues like unemployment and inflation. The government has dismissed these charges, stating that routine changes are part of governance.

The education ministry's new chief will have to hit the ground running. With the next academic session already in progress, early decisions on school reopenings and examination schedules are expected. Officials have not yet confirmed if the new minister will announce any policy changes in the first week.
