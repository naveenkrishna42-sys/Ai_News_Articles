RBI approves Angalappan Anandakumar as director of Real Touch Finance

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Banking Original source ↗
Key points
  • RBI has approved the appointment of Angalappan Anandakumar as director
  • Real Touch Finance receives regulatory clearance for board change
  • Approval marks a key governance step for the non-banking finance company

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has approved the appointment of Angalappan Anandakumar as a director on the board of Real Touch Finance, a non-banking financial company (NBFC). The clearance, granted under the provisions of the Banking Regulation Act, was communicated to the company earlier this week.

Real Touch Finance, which operates in the lending and financial services space, has been expanding its leadership team as it looks to strengthen governance and compliance frameworks. The addition of Anandakumar, whose professional background spans finance and corporate management, is expected to bring fresh oversight to the board's functioning.

Regulatory approval signals compliance

The RBI's nod comes after a thorough vetting process, which includes checks on the candidate's antecedents, professional track record, and fit-and-proper status. For NBFCs, such approvals are a routine but critical requirement, ensuring that board-level appointments meet the central bank's stringent standards.

Real Touch Finance, which is registered with the RBI, has been focusing on improving its operational efficiency and expanding its customer base. The company's board now includes a mix of experienced professionals, with Anandakumar's appointment expected to add depth to its strategic decision-making.

What this means for the company

Industry observers view the RBI's approval as a positive signal for the company's governance practices. It also comes at a time when the RBI has been tightening norms for NBFCs, particularly around corporate governance and risk management. The central bank has, in recent months, emphasised the need for boards to have independent and qualified directors.

Analysts tracking the NBFC sector say that such approvals are often a precursor to further corporate actions, including fundraising or business expansion. However, Real Touch Finance has not made any official announcement regarding such plans.

Background of the appointee

Angalappan Anandakumar's exact professional history has not been disclosed in the company's filing. The RBI's approval letter, which was shared with stock exchanges, did not elaborate on his prior roles. Real Touch Finance is expected to release a detailed profile of Anandakumar in its next annual report.

The company, headquartered in Chennai, primarily caters to retail and small-business lending segments. It has been gradually increasing its presence in southern India, with a focus on vehicle financing and micro, small and medium enterprise (MSME) loans.

Governance push in NBFC sector

The approval comes amid a broader regulatory push by the RBI to enhance governance standards across the NBFC sector. In recent circulars, the central bank has mandated stricter board composition norms, including the requirement for at least one woman director and a minimum number of independent directors.

Real Touch Finance's compliance with these norms is seen as a step in the right direction. The company has been profitable in recent quarters, and its net NPAs have remained within manageable levels, according to its latest disclosures.

Moving forward, the company's focus will be on integrating the new director into its board processes and ensuring seamless transition. The RBI's approval is valid for a period of three years, after which a fresh clearance would be required for continuation.

Watch for Real Touch Finance's next moves in terms of board composition and any strategic announcements that may follow this regulatory clearance.
