iPhone 17 price hike hits India ahead of iPhone 18 Pro launch

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iPhone 17 prices rise across India, weeks before iPhone 18 Pro launch
  • Rupee depreciation and import duties are seen as key triggers
  • Buyers weigh waiting for iPhone 18 Pro against current price hike

Apple has quietly raised prices for the iPhone 17 lineup in India, just a month before the expected launch of the iPhone 18 Pro. The move has caught many buyers off guard, especially those who were planning to purchase the current model during the festive season.

The price increase applies across various storage variants, with the base model now costing more than it did at launch. While Apple has not issued an official statement, retail partners have confirmed the revised pricing to TIVRA News. The hike is believed to be in the range of a few thousand rupees, depending on the variant.

Why the sudden hike?

Industry insiders point to the depreciating rupee against the US dollar as a primary factor. Apple prices its devices globally in dollars, and a weaker rupee directly impacts local pricing. Import duties on smartphones have also remained high, adding further pressure on the company's margins.

Apple has historically adjusted prices in India when currency fluctuations cross a certain threshold. The company last revised iPhone prices in India earlier this year, and this latest adjustment suggests continued volatility in the forex market. Some analysts also speculate that Apple is testing demand elasticity ahead of the iPhone 18 Pro launch.

Impact on buyers

For Indian consumers, the timing is particularly tricky. The iPhone 17 series launched just under a year ago, and many buyers had been holding out for Diwali discounts. Now, with prices going up instead of down, those plans are in jeopardy. "This is unusual," said a Delhi-based electronics retailer. "Usually, prices drop as a new model approaches. This time, it's the opposite."

The price hike may also push fence-sitters toward the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro, which is expected to launch in India next month. Reports suggest the new device will bring significant upgrades, including a faster chip and improved camera system. However, the iPhone 18 Pro is likely to command a premium price, especially with the current economic conditions.

What about the iPhone Ultra?

Adding to the buzz, leaked Apple memos have hinted at a new iPhone Ultra model alongside the iPhone 18 Pro. This has created further confusion among buyers, who are now weighing whether to wait for the Ultra or settle for the Pro. The Ultra is expected to be positioned as a top-tier device, possibly priced higher than the Pro Max.

For now, Apple has not confirmed any details about the iPhone Ultra or the iPhone 18 Pro's India pricing. The company is also conducting a retail employee lottery for the September launch event, a standard practice to manage high demand. This suggests that Apple expects a strong response to the new lineup despite the economic headwinds.

Should you buy now or wait?

If you are in the market for an iPhone 17, the price hike might make you think twice. However, waiting for the iPhone 18 Pro could mean paying a significantly higher price at launch. Analysts recommend that buyers assess their needs carefully. If you need a new phone immediately, the iPhone 17 is still a solid choice, but you will have to pay more than you would have a week ago.

On the other hand, if you can wait a few more weeks, the iPhone 18 Pro launch could bring promotional offers from Apple and its retail partners. Trade-in deals and bank discounts are common during such launches, potentially offsetting the higher sticker price. Some experts also expect the iPhone 17 prices to drop after the new models arrive, as Apple typically clears older inventory.

The coming weeks will be crucial for Apple's India strategy. With the rupee under pressure and the festive season approaching, the company faces a delicate balancing act between maintaining margins and sustaining demand. The iPhone 18 Pro launch, expected next month, will provide clearer signals on how Apple plans to navigate these challenges.
