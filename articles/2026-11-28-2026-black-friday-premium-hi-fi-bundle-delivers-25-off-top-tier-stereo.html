Overview of the 2026 Black Friday Hi‑Fi Bundle

Black Friday 2026 offered a rare opportunity for serious audiophiles to assemble a premium stereo system at a fraction of its catalog price. The bundle, available exclusively through major UK retailers, pairs a flagship Bowers & Wilkins 802 Series II speaker set, a Marantz SR8015 integrated amplifier, an Audio Technica AT‑LP120XUSB turntable, and a Marantz CD800 CD player, all wrapped in high‑quality cabling and accessories. The package, originally valued at $18,598, was priced at $13,999 on 27 November, delivering a 25 % discount that translates to roughly ₹1,16,000 in India.

1. Bowers & Wilkins 802 Series II Speakers

The 802 Series II, released in 2025, boasts a 4‑driver configuration with a 3‑inch woofer, 2‑inch mid‑range, and dual 1‑inch tweeters. Frequency response ranges from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, with a sensitivity of 94 dB. The updated design reduces distortion to 0.01 % THD, ensuring crystal‑clear highs and tight, deep lows. Pairing these with a robust amplifier like the SR8015 maximises their 3‑W/4‑W peak output to 150 W per channel.

2. Marantz SR8015 Integrated Amplifier

Released in 2025, the SR8015 delivers 150 W per channel into 8 Ω, with a 20 Hz–20 kHz response and 0.04 % THD. Its Class A/B hybrid design supports seamless integration with both analog and digital sources. The built‑in phono stage uses an Audient phono preamp, offering 40 dB gain and a 6 kΩ load, ideal for the AT‑LP120XUSB’s 2.5 mm cartridge. The amplifier’s 3‑port USB‑DAC supports 32‑bit/384 kHz, ensuring pristine digital playback.

3. Audio Technica AT‑LP120XUSB Turntable

The AT‑LP120XUSB, a staple since 2018, remains relevant due to its 2.5 mm cartridge and built‑in USB output. It offers a 5.5‑mm tonearm, 3‑point suspension, and an 80 dB isolation platform. The platter spins at 33.33 RPM with a 2‑inch aluminum platter, delivering low run‑out and stable speed. Its USB output streams 24‑bit/96 kHz to any computer, making vinyl playback effortless.

4. Marantz CD800 CD Player

Launched in 2025, the CD800 features a 16‑bit/44.1 kHz/48 kHz DAC and a 32‑bit/384 kHz USB input. It supports a 4‑inch aluminum platter and a 3‑point suspension. The player’s 0.01 % THD and 20 Hz–20 kHz response ensure accurate reproduction of studio masters. Its integrated phono stage, when paired with the SR8015, offers a 40 dB gain and 6 kΩ load, matching the AT‑LP120XUSB.

5. Cables and Accessories

The bundle includes AudioQuest “Black Label” speaker cables (18‑AWG) and “Red Label” interconnects (22‑AWG). A pair of “Mogami” XLR cables (2‑m) link the amplifier to the speakers. The set also contains a custom mounting kit and a 2‑hour professional setup service.
