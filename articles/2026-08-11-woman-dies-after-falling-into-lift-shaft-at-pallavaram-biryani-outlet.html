Woman dies after falling into lift shaft at Pallavaram biryani outlet

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Woman employee died after falling into lift shaft at biryani outlet in Pallavaram
  • Incident reported on Tuesday; police have launched investigation

Fatal accident at Pallavaram eatery

A woman employee died on Tuesday after falling into a lift shaft at a biryani outlet in Pallavaram, a southern suburb of Chennai. The incident took place at the outlet, where the victim was working. Police have registered a case and are investigating the circumstances that led to the fall.

The woman's identity has not been officially released, and authorities are yet to confirm her age or role at the establishment. The exact sequence of events remains unclear, with investigators piecing together statements from colleagues and onlookers.

Investigation underway

Local police reached the spot after receiving information about the accident. The body was recovered and sent for post-mortem, with a report expected to determine the precise cause of death. Initial inquiries suggest the lift shaft may have been open or unguarded at the time, but this has not been officially corroborated.

Officials from the Tamil Nadu Labour Department are also likely to examine whether safety norms were followed at the outlet. The establishment's management has not issued a public statement so far.

Safety concerns in small eateries

The incident has reignited concerns over workplace safety in smaller food outlets and commercial establishments across the city. In many such premises, lifts and service elevators are often installed without adequate safeguards, leaving employees vulnerable to accidents.

Labour rights activists have repeatedly called for stricter enforcement of safety regulations, especially for women workers in the hospitality sector. This case may prompt a review of compliance standards in similar businesses in the Chennai region.

What happens next

The police investigation is expected to conclude in the coming days, with a detailed report on the cause of the fall. The post-mortem findings and any CCTV footage from the outlet could provide crucial evidence. Authorities may also question the outlet's owners regarding adherence to safety protocols.

As the family of the victim awaits answers, the incident serves as a grim reminder of the risks faced by workers in unregulated environments. Further action, including possible legal charges, will depend on the findings of the inquiry.
