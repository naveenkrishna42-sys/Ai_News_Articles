TN seeks Centre nod for three Chennai Metro extension projects

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tamil Nadu Finance Minister announced efforts to secure Centre's approval for three Chennai Metro Rail extension projects.
  • The projects aim to expand Chennai Metro Rail network to suburbs and key areas.
  • State government is coordinating with central ministries for faster clearances.

State pushes for Metro expansion

Tamil Nadu Finance Minister on Wednesday said the state government is actively pursuing the Centre's approval for three Chennai Metro Rail extension projects. The Minister, speaking to reporters in Chennai, said files have been moved and discussions are underway with the Union ministries concerned.

The proposed extensions are expected to add significant length to the existing Metro corridor, improving connectivity to the city's growing suburbs. The Minister did not specify the exact routes or the projected cost, but said detailed project reports have been submitted.

Awaiting central clearances

The Minister said the state has been in constant touch with the Union Housing and Urban Affairs Ministry as well as the Railway Board. He expressed confidence that the proposals would be cleared soon, given the strategic importance of the projects for Chennai's urban mobility.

Officials familiar with the matter said the three extensions are part of the phase-II expansion plan, which has faced delays due to funding and land acquisition issues. The state government has been pushing for a faster approval process to avoid cost overruns.

Why this matters for commuters

Chennai Metro currently operates on two corridors, connecting the city's major hubs. The proposed extensions would link areas like the northern and southern fringes, which are seeing rapid residential and commercial growth. Commuters have long demanded better last-mile connectivity, and these projects are seen as a step in that direction.

However, the Minister's statement comes amid ongoing debates over the financial viability of Metro projects in Indian cities. While the state is keen to expand, the Centre's approval is crucial for funding under the viability gap funding scheme.

What to watch next

The state government is expected to make a formal presentation before the Union ministry in the coming weeks. A decision on the extensions could be announced in the next few months, depending on the pace of inter-ministerial consultations. For now, Chennai residents will have to wait for the green signal.
