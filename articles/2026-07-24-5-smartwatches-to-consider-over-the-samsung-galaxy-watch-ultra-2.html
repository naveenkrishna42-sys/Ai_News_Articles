5 Smartwatches To Consider Over The Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung launched the Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 at Galaxy Unpacked 2026 alongside the Watch 9.
  • The Watch Ultra 2 comes with improved durability and longer software support, but may not suit everyone.
  • Alternatives include watches from Apple, Garmin, Google, and OnePlus, each with distinct strengths.

Samsung’s latest Galaxy Unpacked event brought the Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 and the Galaxy Watch 9 into the spotlight. The new Ultra 2 promises extended battery life, a tougher titanium build, and longer software support—up to seven years.

But not everyone needs that level of ruggedness or wants to spend a premium on features they might never use. If you’re weighing your options, here are five smartwatches that could serve you better, depending on what you prioritise.

Apple Watch Series 9

If you’re an iPhone user, the Apple Watch Series 9 remains the most seamless companion. It offers deep integration with iOS, a vibrant always-on display, and a robust health-tracking suite that includes ECG, blood oxygen monitoring, and temperature sensing.

The Series 9 is also faster than its predecessor thanks to the S9 chip, and it supports double-tap gestures for one-handed use. While it lacks the Ultra 2’s extreme durability, it is more comfortable for daily wear and comes in a wider range of styles.

Garmin Forerunner 265

For serious runners and triathletes, the Garmin Forerunner 265 is a dedicated training tool rather than a general-purpose smartwatch. It features a bright AMOLED display, multi-band GPS, and advanced metrics like training readiness and race predictor.

Battery life is a strong suit here—up to 13 days in smartwatch mode. Unlike the Galaxy Watch Ultra 2, the Forerunner 265 is lighter and designed specifically for performance tracking, making it a better fit for athletes who want data without the bulk.

Google Pixel Watch 2

The Pixel Watch 2 runs Wear OS and offers a clean, stock Android experience without Samsung’s custom skin. It includes Fitbit integration for health tracking, with features like stress management, skin temperature sensing, and automatic workout detection.

Its round, minimalist design is more subtle than the Ultra 2’s angular case. The Pixel Watch 2 also gets regular feature drops directly from Google, ensuring it stays updated with new capabilities over time. However, its battery life is shorter—around 24 hours with always-on display enabled.

OnePlus Watch 2

The OnePlus Watch 2 runs a dual-engine system that switches between Wear OS and a low-power RTOS to stretch battery life. In typical usage, it can last up to 100 hours, which easily beats the Galaxy Watch Ultra 2’s endurance.

It offers solid fitness tracking, including over 100 workout modes and built-in GPS. The watch also supports fast charging—a 10-minute charge can give you a full day’s use. It lacks some premium sensors like ECG, but it undercuts the Ultra 2 significantly on price.

Amazfit T-Rex Ultra

If you want ruggedness without the Samsung price tag, the Amazfit T-Rex Ultra is a direct alternative. It meets military-grade durability standards (MIL-STD-810G) and can handle extreme temperatures, high altitudes, and water immersion up to 100 metres.

It includes dual-band GPS, a 1.39-inch AMOLED display, and a battery that lasts up to 20 days in typical use. The T-Rex Ultra focuses on outdoor activities like hiking and mountaineering, and it costs roughly half of what the Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 retails for.

Each of these watches brings something distinct to the table. The Apple Watch Series 9 is best for iPhone users, the Garmin Forerunner 265 for athletes, the Pixel Watch 2 for stock Android lovers, the OnePlus Watch 2 for battery chasers, and the Amazfit T-Rex Ultra for budget-conscious adventurers.

Your choice will ultimately depend on which device ecosystem you live in and what features you use daily. The Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 is a capable device, but it isn’t the only—or necessarily the best—option for everyone.
