Sri Lanka coach urges fast bowlers to improve

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Head coach Chandika Hathurusingha urges Sri Lanka's fast bowlers to improve
  • Sri Lanka to face India in a three-match Test series
  • Hathurusingha emphasizes the need for consistency and better execution

Sri Lanka's Head Coach Calls for Improvement from Fast Bowlers

Chandika Hathurusingha, the head coach of Sri Lanka's cricket team, has called on the country's fast bowlers to 'raise their game a little bit' ahead of the upcoming three-match Test series against India. The series is set to begin in a few weeks, and Hathurusingha is looking for his pace attack to deliver better performances.

Focus on Consistency and Execution

Hathurusingha has emphasized the need for consistency and better execution from the fast bowlers. He has been working closely with the bowlers to identify areas where they can improve, such as their line and length, and to help them develop better strategies for taking wickets.

Upcoming Series Against India

The Test series against India is a crucial one for Sri Lanka, as it will be a good opportunity for the team to assess their strengths and weaknesses ahead of the upcoming ICC World Test Championship. Hathurusingha is confident that his team can compete with India, but he knows that they need to perform at their best to achieve a positive result.

Team's Preparation and Strategy

The Sri Lankan team has been preparing intensively for the series, with Hathurusingha focusing on both the technical and tactical aspects of the game. He has been working with the bowlers to improve their fitness and to help them develop better mental resilience. Hathurusingha is also looking to make some changes to the team's strategy, with a focus on exploiting the conditions and playing to Sri Lanka's strengths.

What Happens Next

As the series approaches, cricket fans will be eagerly awaiting the performances of Sri Lanka's fast bowlers. Hathurusingha's calls for improvement will be closely watched, and the team's ability to execute their plans will be crucial in determining the outcome of the series.
