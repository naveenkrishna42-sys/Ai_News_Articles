China-Nepal mudslide: Rescue race amid glacier risk

📅 2026-09-01 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mudslide on China-Nepal border kills at least 100, with toll expected to rise
  • China's Ministry of Water Resources warns of glacier collapse risk near Cuojian River
  • China provides USD 2.2 million in aid for Nepal's flood victims
  • Authorities in China punish 10 individuals for spreading 'fake' flood toll reports

Rescue efforts underway as death toll climbs

Emergency services are racing against time to locate and rescue survivors trapped under the debris of a massive mudslide that struck the China-Nepal border region. Initial reports suggest at least 100 people have perished, with the death toll expected to rise as search and rescue operations continue.

Glacier collapse risk looms

China's Ministry of Water Resources has issued a warning that the glacier collapse risk remains significant around the Cuojian River impact crater. Authorities are closely monitoring the situation, fearing that further collapses could exacerbate the disaster.

China's aid response

In a show of solidarity, China has provided USD 2.2 million in aid to help Nepal cope with the aftermath of the mudslide. The financial assistance aims to support relief efforts and provide immediate aid to the affected population.

Controversy over death toll reports

Amidst the chaos, authorities in China have punished 10 individuals for spreading 'fake' flood toll reports. The move underscores the importance of accurate information dissemination during crisis situations.
