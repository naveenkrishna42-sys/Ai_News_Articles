Spider-Man: Brand New Day presales hit Rs 80 crore in India, record in sight

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Advance bookings for Spider-Man: Brand New Day have crossed Rs 80 crore in India.
  • The film is on track to break the all-time advance sales record for a Hollywood release in the country.
  • Globally, the movie is projected to open at a staggering $800 million.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day is swinging into Indian theatres with unprecedented momentum. Advance ticket sales for the Tom Holland starrer have already crossed the Rs 80 crore mark, according to early estimates from trade sources. The number puts the film on track to shatter the all-time record for advance bookings for a Hollywood release in India.

Record-breaking presales signal massive opening

Industry trackers say the frenzy for Brand New Day has surpassed expectations. The previous record for Hollywood advance sales in India stood at around Rs 60 crore, set by Avengers: Endgame. Crossing Rs 80 crore in presales alone means the film is headed for a historic opening weekend.

Multiplex chains across metros and tier-2 cities have reported near-sellout shows for the first three days. Exhibitors have added extra morning and late-night screenings to meet demand. The response has been particularly strong in Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad.

Global box office juggernaut

The film's Indian success is part of a much larger global phenomenon. Early projections suggest Brand New Day could open at a staggering $800 million worldwide. In China, the movie has already recorded the biggest superhero opening in seven years, a significant recovery for Hollywood in that market.

Critical reception has been mixed but largely positive. The Guardian called it a 'semi-Cronenberg-style superhero' film, praising Zendaya's performance as a key factor that 'saves' the movie. The BBC, meanwhile, has questioned whether Holland's Spider-Man needs to evolve or hang up his suit, hinting at a darker, more mature tone for the latest installment.

India's love affair with Spider-Man

Spider-Man has consistently been one of the most beloved superhero franchises in India. Holland's iteration, in particular, has resonated with younger audiences. The combination of action, humour, and emotional stakes has proven to be a winning formula at the Indian box office.

Trade analysts note that the film's advance booking numbers are also being driven by repeat viewers. Fans are eager to catch the film on the biggest screen possible, with IMAX and 3D shows commanding a premium. The Rs 80 crore figure includes tickets sold across all formats.

What happens next

If the current trend holds, Spider-Man: Brand New Day will likely cross the Rs 200 crore mark in its opening weekend in India. The final tally will depend on walk-in audiences over the weekend. All eyes are now on Saturday and Sunday to see if the film can sustain the momentum and set a new benchmark for Hollywood in India.
