Monsoon Trough Revives, Heavy Rain Alerts for North and East India This Week

📅 2026-07-20 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Monsoon trough has become active again, promising widespread rain.
  • IMD has issued heavy rain alerts for North, East, and Northeast India.
  • Bengal rain forecast: heavy rain to shift from north Bengal to Gangetic districts from July 22.
  • Rains to ease in north Bengal, intensify in Gangetic plains from Wednesday.
  • City-wise forecasts have been released by the IMD.

Monsoon Trough Revives

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has announced a significant revival of the monsoon trough. After a period of relative lull, the system is set to bring a strong comeback of rains across several parts of the country this week.

Forecasters expect widespread precipitation to lash North, East, and Northeast India. The revival is attributed to a fresh cyclonic circulation and the strengthening of the monsoon axis.

Heavy Rain Alerts Issued

The IMD has issued heavy rain alerts for multiple states. Regions expected to be most affected include Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand, West Bengal, and the northeastern states.

Officials have warned of potential flooding in low-lying areas. Residents in these zones have been advised to remain cautious and stay updated with local warnings.

Bengal Rain Forecast: Shift to Gangetic Districts

In West Bengal, the pattern of rainfall is set to change. Heavy rain, which has been concentrated in north Bengal, will gradually shift to the Gangetic districts starting July 22.

According to the Telegraph India, rains are expected to ease in the northern parts of the state. The Gangetic plains, including Kolkata and surrounding areas, will see an intensification of showers from Wednesday.

ThePrint has also confirmed this shift, adding that the change is due to a trough moving southwards.

City-Wise Forecast

The IMD has released a detailed city-wise forecast for the week. Delhi is likely to experience cloudy skies with moderate rain, while Mumbai may see intermittent showers.

Kolkata is bracing for heavy downpours from mid-week. Chennai, meanwhile, is expected to remain mostly dry with only isolated light rain.

The forecast for other major cities like Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Lucknow indicates varied activity, with some areas receiving heavy spells.

What to Watch

Authorities are monitoring the situation closely. The IMD will continue to issue updates as the monsoon trough evolves. Residents in affected areas should check local advisories before traveling.
