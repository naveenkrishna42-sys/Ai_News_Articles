Sensex rises for 3rd day, ends 166 pts up; Nifty at 24,384

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex closed 166 points higher, marking a third consecutive day of gains
  • Nifty settled at 24,384, with auto stocks leading the advance
  • Broader market sentiment remained positive amid sectoral buying

Indian equity benchmarks extended their winning streak for a third consecutive session on Friday, with the Sensex closing 166 points higher. The Nifty settled at 24,384, as auto stocks led the charge, lifting market sentiment across the board.

The 30-share Sensex ended the day with a gain of 166 points, while the broader Nifty held above the 24,300 level throughout the session. Traders said buying was broad-based, though the auto sector emerged as the clear leader, with several index heavyweights contributing to the uptick.

Auto stocks drive momentum

Auto shares were the standout performers, with investors betting on sustained demand and improving earnings visibility. The sector's strength helped offset profit-taking in some pockets, keeping the indices in positive territory for most of the day.

Market participants noted that the advance was supported by steady foreign and domestic institutional flows, though volumes remained moderate. The third straight day of gains has brought some relief to investors after a choppy patch earlier in the week.

Broader market holds firm

The broader market also traded with a positive bias, with midcap and smallcap stocks showing resilience. However, the gains were not uniform across all sectors, as some defensive and export-oriented spaces saw mild selling pressure.

Analysts said the market's ability to hold gains despite global uncertainties suggests underlying strength, but they cautioned that the rally needs fresh triggers to sustain. The absence of any major negative news on the domestic front has helped keep sentiment intact.

What traders are watching

With the earnings season largely behind, investors are now turning their attention to global cues, including US Federal Reserve policy signals and crude oil prices. Domestic macroeconomic data and monsoon progress are also on the radar.

Volatility is expected to remain a feature in the near term, especially with derivatives expiry-related moves and positioning by institutional players. Market experts advise staying selective, given that valuations in some pockets remain elevated.

The day's close marks a positive end to the week, but the sustainability of the rally will depend on how global markets behave in the coming sessions. Any sharp move in oil prices or a surprise in US economic data could alter the trajectory.

For now, the undertone remains cautious optimism, with investors hoping that the momentum carries into the next week. A clear break above key resistance levels on the Nifty could invite fresh buying, while any dip below support may trigger profit-booking.

As always, the market's direction will hinge on a mix of domestic policy cues and global developments. Traders are likely to keep a close watch on the auto sector's continued performance, given its leading role in this rally.
