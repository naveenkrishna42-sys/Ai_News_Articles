Bengaluru Authority Orders Demolition Plan for Tilted Building Wing

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Greater Bengaluru Authority seeks demolition action plan for tilted wing of 18-storey building
  • Officials directed to assess structural stability and prepare safe demolition strategy
  • Residents of affected wing evacuated; authority cites immediate safety concerns

Demolition Directive Issued for Unsafe Tower Wing

The Greater Bengaluru Authority has directed its officials to prepare a demolition action plan for a tilted wing of an 18-storey residential building. The order comes after structural assessments revealed that the wing is leaning at a precarious angle, posing a significant safety hazard.

Authorities have not disclosed the exact location of the building or the developer involved. However, they confirmed that the tilt was first noticed during routine inspections earlier this month. Engineers who examined the site reported that the wing had shifted noticeably from its original alignment.

Evacuation and Safety Measures Underway

Residents of the affected wing have already been relocated to temporary accommodation. The authority has cordoned off the area around the building to prevent any untoward incident. Officials stated that no injuries have been reported so far.

The Greater Bengaluru Authority has formed a technical committee to oversee the demolition process. The committee will submit a detailed plan within a week, outlining the method and timeline for bringing down the tilted structure safely. Experts suggest controlled implosion or piece-by-piece dismantling as possible options.

Structural Integrity Under Scrutiny

This incident has raised questions about the quality of construction in the city's high-rise buildings. The authority is likely to conduct a broader audit of similar structures in the vicinity. Builders and developers may face increased scrutiny regarding compliance with safety norms.

Local residents have expressed relief that the tilt was detected before any disaster occurred. Many are now demanding stricter oversight of ongoing construction projects in Bengaluru. The authority has assured that all necessary steps will be taken to prevent such occurrences in the future.

The demolition plan is expected to be finalised in the coming days. Officials will then seek approval from higher-ups before executing the operation. The entire process could take several weeks, depending on the complexity of the structure and the chosen demolition method.
