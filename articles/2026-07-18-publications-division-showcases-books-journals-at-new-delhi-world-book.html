Publications Division Showcases Books, Journals at New Delhi World Book Fair 2026

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Culture & Arts Original source ↗
Key points
  • Publications Division sets up a stall at the New Delhi World Book Fair 2026.
  • The collection includes books on Indian history, culture, and governance.
  • Journals and magazines published by the division are also on display.
  • The fair aims to promote reading and literary awareness among visitors.

New Delhi: The Publications Division, a premier publishing house under the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, has brought a rich collection of books and journals to the New Delhi World Book Fair 2026. The stall, located at Hall No. 5, offers visitors a chance to explore works on Indian history, culture, art, and governance.

Diverse Collection on Display

The division's stall features over 200 titles, including classic works on India's freedom struggle, biographies of national leaders, and books on traditional arts and crafts. Visitors can browse through volumes on Indian philosophy, science, and literature. The display also includes recent publications on contemporary issues such as climate change and digital governance.

Regular journals like 'Yojana' and 'Kurukshetra', known for their in-depth analysis of rural development and economic policies, are available. The division's children's books, with colourful illustrations and stories rooted in Indian folklore, have drawn young readers.

Special Discounts and Activities

The Publications Division is offering a flat 20% discount on all its titles. Additionally, a 30% discount applies to students who show valid ID cards. The stall has a reading corner where visitors can sit and browse books before buying.

To engage young visitors, the division has organised storytelling sessions and quiz competitions. Winners receive book sets and certificates. These activities aim to foster a reading habit among children and teenagers.

Significance of the Fair

The New Delhi World Book Fair, organised by the National Book Trust, is one of Asia's largest literary events. It brings together publishers, authors, and readers from across the country and abroad. The fair provides a platform for government publishers like the Publications Division to reach a wider audience.

The division's presence at the fair underscores its role in disseminating knowledge about India's heritage and development. Officials say the response from visitors has been encouraging, with many showing interest in the division's affordable and authoritative publications.

The fair continues until 18 January 2026. The Publications Division stall remains open from 10 AM to 8 PM daily. Visitors can also place orders for home delivery through the division's website.

As the fair draws thousands of book lovers each day, the division expects robust sales. The focus now is on attracting first-time visitors and expanding its readership base among young Indians.
