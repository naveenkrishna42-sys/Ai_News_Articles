Yash Raj Films launches Raah Records; Aman to debut with Jaadugari

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Yash Raj Films has launched a new music label, Raah Records.
  • Aman is the first artist signed to the label.
  • His debut single is titled Jaadugari.

Mumbai: Yash Raj Films, one of India's most prominent film production houses, has officially forayed into the music industry with the launch of its new record label, Raah Records. The announcement, made on Tuesday, signals the company's ambition to diversify beyond film production and distribution.

The label's first signing is Aman, an artist whose debut single, titled Jaadugari, is set to release under the Raah Records banner. While details about the track and its music video have not been fully disclosed, the move marks the beginning of a new musical journey for both the artist and the company.

Raah Records: A New Chapter for YRF

Raah Records is positioned as a dedicated platform for discovering and nurturing fresh musical talent. The label aims to produce and promote music across various genres, with a focus on creating a distinct identity in the competitive Indian music market.

Yash Raj Films has a long history of integrating memorable soundtracks into its movies, from the soulful melodies of Chandni to the upbeat numbers of recent blockbusters. With Raah Records, the company appears to be leveraging this legacy to build a standalone music business.

Aman's Debut: Jaadugari

Aman, the first artist to be signed, is described as a promising newcomer. His debut track, Jaadugari, is expected to showcase his vocal range and style. The title, which translates to 'magic' or 'enchantment,' hints at a song that could blend contemporary sounds with a touch of whimsy.

Industry observers will be watching closely to see how YRF promotes Aman, given the company's extensive network in film and media. The release strategy for Jaadugari has not been announced, but it is likely to include a robust digital campaign, given the current trends in music consumption.

YRF's Music Legacy

Yash Raj Films has been a dominant force in Bollywood for decades, producing classics like Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge and Ek Tha Tiger. Its music division, YRF Music, has released numerous hit soundtracks, and the launch of Raah Records suggests a more aggressive push into the non-film music space.

This move comes at a time when several Indian film studios are exploring standalone music labels, capitalizing on the growing streaming audience. Raah Records will compete with established players and new entrants, aiming to carve out a niche with a mix of fresh voices and high-quality production.

What to Watch

The immediate focus will be on the release of Jaadugari and the response it garners. If the single performs well, it could pave the way for more signings and a steady pipeline of releases from Raah Records.

For now, fans of YRF and music enthusiasts alike will be eager to hear what Aman brings to the table. The label's first act is a statement of intent, and the coming months will reveal whether Raah Records can turn its debut into a sustained success.
