PM Modi inaugurates redeveloped Park railway station in Chennai

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • PM Narendra Modi inaugurated the redeveloped Park railway station in Chennai.
  • The station has been modernised with improved facilities and better connectivity.
  • The redevelopment is part of a broader push to upgrade railway infrastructure across the country.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Friday inaugurated the redeveloped Park railway station in Chennai, a key suburban rail hub that has seen a major facelift. The event marks another milestone in the Centre's ongoing push to modernise railway infrastructure across the country.

The station, located in the heart of Chennai, serves thousands of commuters daily. Its redevelopment includes upgraded passenger amenities, improved signage, and better access for differently-abled passengers.

Modern facilities and better connectivity

The revamped station now features wider platforms, new waiting halls, and enhanced lighting. Officials said the design focuses on seamless passenger movement and integration with other modes of transport.

Park station connects to Chennai Central and other key points in the city's suburban network. The upgrade is expected to ease congestion and improve the daily commute for lakhs of passengers who rely on the suburban rail system.

As part of the redevelopment, the station has also been equipped with digital displays, public address systems, and CCTV cameras for security. Cleanliness and maintenance have been given priority, with dedicated teams assigned for upkeep.

Part of a larger railway modernisation drive

The inauguration is part of a broader plan to redevelop over 200 railway stations across India. The government has been pushing for private investment and modern design to transform these hubs into world-class facilities.

Chennai's Park station is one of the oldest in the city, and its redevelopment was long pending. Local commuters have welcomed the move, though some have pointed out that more such upgrades are needed across the suburban network.

Officials said the project was completed on schedule and within the allocated budget. They have not yet disclosed the exact cost of the redevelopment.

Political and public reactions

The Prime Minister's visit to Chennai for the inauguration drew political attention. Leaders from the ruling party highlighted the government's focus on infrastructure, while opposition parties questioned the timing and pace of such projects in other parts of the state.

Commuters expressed cautious optimism. Many said the upgraded station is a welcome change, but they stressed the need for similar improvements at other stations and better last-mile connectivity.

Railway officials said the redeveloped station would be fully operational from Saturday, with all facilities open to the public.

What happens next: The railway ministry is expected to announce timelines for redevelopment of other stations in Chennai, including Chennai Egmore and Tambaram, in the coming months.
