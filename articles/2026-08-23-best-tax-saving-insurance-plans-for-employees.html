Tax-Saving Insurance Plans Under Section 80C and Section 80D

Tax-saving insurance plans are a great way to reduce your taxable income and save on taxes. In India, there are two main sections under the Income Tax Act that allow for tax savings: Section 80C and Section 80D.

Section 80C

Section 80C allows for deductions on investments made in certain financial instruments. The maximum deduction allowed under this section is Rs. 1.5 lakh per year. This can include investments in:

  • Public Provident Fund (PPF)
  • National Savings Certificate (NSC)
  • Life Insurance Corporation (LIC) policies
  • Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS)
  • Unit Linked Insurance Plans (ULIPs)
  • Sukanya Samriddhi Account (SSA)

Section 80D

Section 80D allows for deductions on premiums paid for health insurance policies. The maximum deduction allowed under this section is Rs. 25,000 per year. This can include premiums paid for:

  • Health insurance policies
  • Preventive health check-up policies
  • Ayushman Bharat Health Account

Salary-Wise Checklist for Insurance Plans

Choosing the right insurance plan depends on your salary and financial goals. Here is a salary-wise checklist for insurance plans:

Salary Below Rs. 5 Lakh

For those earning below Rs. 5 lakh, it is recommended to invest in a combination of PPF, NSC, and LIC policies. These investments offer a good return on investment and can help you save on taxes.

Salary Between Rs. 5 Lakh and Rs. 10 Lakh

For those earning between Rs. 5 lakh and Rs. 10 lakh, it is recommended to invest in a combination of PPF, NSC, LIC policies, and ELSS. ELSS can provide tax savings and a good return on investment.

Salary Above Rs. 10 Lakh

For those earning above Rs. 10 lakh, it is recommended to invest in a combination of PPF, NSC, LIC policies, ELSS, and ULIPs. ULIPs can provide tax savings and a good return on investment, along with insurance coverage.

How to Apply for Insurance Plans Online

Applying for insurance plans online is a simple and convenient process. Here are the steps to apply for insurance plans online:

  1. Visit the official website of the insurance company or the IRDAI portal.
  2. Fill in the required details, including your personal information, financial details, and insurance preferences.
  3. Choose the insurance plan that best suits your needs and budget.
  4. Pay the premium using your preferred payment method.
  5. Download and save your policy document for future reference.

It is important to compare different insurance plans and their features before making a decision. This will help you choose the plan that best suits your needs and budget.
