Dhamaal 4 Nears Welcome To The Jungle Lifetime In India, Earns Rs 128.50 Crore In 12 Days

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Dhamaal 4 has collected Rs 128.50 crore in India after 12 days.
  • The film is close to surpassing the lifetime collection of Welcome To The Jungle.
  • Worldwide gross stands at over Rs 182 crore, making it the top-grossing film of the franchise.

Ajay Devgn's comedy caper Dhamaal 4 continues to hold strong at the Indian box office, even as new releases compete for screens. The film has collected an estimated Rs 128.50 crore in its first 12 days, according to early trade reports.

This puts it within striking distance of Welcome To The Jungle, the 2023 comedy starring Akshay Kumar that ended its run at Rs 134.5 crore in India. Trade analysts expect Dhamaal 4 to cross that mark by its second Wednesday.

Second Tuesday holds steady

On its 13th day — the second Tuesday after release — the film earned approximately Rs 2.75 crore in India. While this is a slight dip from the previous day, it remains a respectable number for a comedy film in its second week.

Industry tracker Sacnilk reported a 12-day total of Rs 128.50 crore net in India. The film has managed to hold its ground despite the release of a major Hollywood epic, which some expected to eat into its audience.

Crosses Rs 182 crore worldwide

Globally, Dhamaal 4 has crossed the Rs 182.62 crore mark, according to data from The Times of India. This includes strong contributions from overseas markets, particularly the UK where the film remains inside the top 15 of the weekly box office chart.

The film has now become the highest-grossing entry in the Dhamaal franchise, surpassing the lifetimes of all three previous instalments. The franchise, which began in 2007, has seen growing returns with each new film.

Weekend momentum key

With the third weekend approaching, the film's ability to add more numbers will depend on how well it holds screens. Multiplexes in metropolitan centres continue to show good occupancy for evening and night shows.

Single-screen theatres in smaller towns, however, have seen a sharper drop-off. The film's comedy-driven plot and ensemble cast — featuring not just Devgn but also Madhuri Dixit and a supporting cast of comic actors — have been the main draw for family audiences.

Trade pundits note that Dhamaal 4 has benefited from positive word-of-mouth, particularly among younger viewers who are returning for repeat watches. The film's music and slapstick sequences have been widely shared on social media, boosting its visibility.

The next milestone to watch will be the Rs 150 crore domestic mark. Given current trends, the film should cross that figure by the end of its third week, provided it does not face too much competition from upcoming releases.
