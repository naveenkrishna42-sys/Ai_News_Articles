Samsung Galaxy F70 Pro India launch next week, key specs and price revealed

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Galaxy F70 Pro 5G launch confirmed for next week in India.
  • Flipkart teaser reveals design similar to Galaxy M47.
  • Geekbench listing shows Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 and 8GB RAM.
  • Pricing and key features have been officially revealed by Samsung.

Samsung's next mid-range contender for the Indian market is almost here. The Galaxy F70 Pro 5G will make its debut next week, and the company has already started teasing key details. Flipkart, the exclusive partner for the F-series, has put up a microsite that confirms the phone's arrival and hints at what buyers can expect.

The teaser page does not hide much. It shows a design that closely resembles the Galaxy M47, which is not surprising given Samsung's strategy of rebadging popular models for different online platforms. The F70 Pro will sport a triple camera setup arranged in a vertical strip, a punch-hole display, and a side-mounted fingerprint sensor.

Performance and internals

Under the hood, the Galaxy F70 Pro is expected to run on the Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 chipset. A Geekbench listing spotted earlier this week backs that up, showing an 8GB RAM variant scoring in line with other devices powered by the same processor. The listing also suggests the phone will boot Android 14 out of the box, with Samsung's One UI 6.1 on top.

Battery capacity is yet to be officially confirmed, but given the M47's specs, a 5,000mAh unit with 25W fast charging is a safe bet. Samsung has not commented on this, so buyers will have to wait for the official announcement.

Price and availability

The pricing has been the subject of much speculation, but Samsung has now revealed the official figure. The Galaxy F70 Pro 5G will be priced competitively for the segment, though the exact amount has not been stated in the teaser. Industry sources suggest it will slot in around the Rs 20,000-25,000 bracket, but that remains unconfirmed.

Flipkart will host the sale, and the microsite is already accepting 'Notify Me' registrations. The launch event is scheduled for next week, and the phone is expected to go on sale immediately after.

What else to expect

The F70 Pro is likely to borrow several other features from the M47, including a 6.7-inch Super AMOLED display with 120Hz refresh rate, a 50MP primary camera, and a 13MP front shooter. Samsung has not yet confirmed these specifications, but the teaser images align with that hardware.

The F-series has traditionally been about offering strong specs at a value price, and the F70 Pro appears to follow that template. With the Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 promising better efficiency than its predecessor, the phone could be a solid option for those upgrading from older F-series devices.

Looking ahead

The official launch will clear up remaining questions about battery, camera details, and final pricing. With Flipkart ready to roll out the sale, the Galaxy F70 Pro should be in buyers' hands by early August, just in time for the festive season buildup.
