Motorola Edge 70 Max Launches as First Android Phone with Full Qi2 Magnetic Charging

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Motorola Edge 70 Max is the first Android phone with full Qi2 magnetic charging certification.
  • It packs a 5,500 mAh battery and MediaTek Dimensity 8300 processor.
  • The phone launches in India with AI features, including real-time translation and photo editing.
  • Industry experts raise concerns over Motorola's software update policy for the Edge series.

Motorola Edge 70 Max Launches with Qi2 Magnetic Charging

Motorola has officially launched the Edge 70 Max in India. The company claims it is the first Android phone to carry full Qi2 certification for magnetic wireless charging. That means it supports the same magnetic alignment standard Apple brought with MagSafe — now native on Android.

Qi2 uses magnets to snap accessories like chargers, power banks, and car mounts into perfect alignment. Until now, only iPhones had this feature baked into the hardware. Motorola wants to change that with the Edge 70 Max.

Specs and Performance

The Edge 70 Max runs on a MediaTek Dimensity 8300 processor. It is paired with up to 12 GB of RAM and 256 GB of storage. The phone has a 5,500 mAh battery that supports 68W wired charging and 15W wireless charging.

On the front, it has a 6.7-inch pOLED display with a 144 Hz refresh rate. The display is FHD+ resolution and gets up to 1,200 nits of peak brightness. Motorola has also included an IP68 rating for dust and water resistance.

Camera and AI Features

The rear camera setup includes a 50 MP primary sensor with OIS, a 13 MP ultra-wide lens, and a 2 MP depth sensor. The front camera is 32 MP.

Motorola is pushing several AI-powered features. The phone comes with real-time voice translation for calls and messages, an AI photo editor that can remove objects and reframe shots, and an adaptive battery management system that learns user habits to optimise charging cycles.

Pricing and Availability in India

The Motorola Edge 70 Max is priced at Rs 34,999 for the 8 GB RAM variant and Rs 37,999 for the 12 GB version. It goes on sale starting July 20 on Flipkart, Motorola’s official website, and select retail stores.

Colour options include Black Pearl and Forest Green. Motorola is offering a bundle deal that includes a free Qi2 wireless charger with every purchase for the first week.

Software Update Concerns

Industry analysts and tech reviewers have pointed out a recurring issue. The Edge 70 Max ships with Android 15 and Motorola’s Hello UI. But Motorola has historically offered only two major OS updates for its Edge phones, while competitors like Samsung and OnePlus now offer four to five years.

GSMArena noted that the Edge 70 Max exposes a bigger problem with Motorola’s software update policy. Users who spend over Rs 35,000 on a phone may expect longer support. Motorola has not yet confirmed the update commitment for this model.

What Comes Next

Motorola is betting that Qi2 support and a large battery will attract buyers in the mid-premium segment. The phone will compete directly with the OnePlus Nord 4 and the Samsung Galaxy A55. Whether the software update policy becomes a dealbreaker will depend on how Motorola responds in the coming weeks.
