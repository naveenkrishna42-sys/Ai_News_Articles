Celebrating Friendship Day with Bollywood's Best

Friendship Day falls on the first Sunday of August every year, and this year it is on August 2. For those planning a movie marathon with their squad, a fresh list of 12 Bollywood films has been curated to mark the occasion. These are not just any films — they are stories that have defined friendship on the Indian screen for decades.

The list, compiled by Mid-Day, offers a mix of classics and modern favourites. From the road trip adventures of Zindagi Na Milegi Dobara to the hostel camaraderie of 3 Idiots, the selection covers different emotions and settings. Each film brings its own flavour of bonding, humour, and sometimes, tears.

From Dil Chahta Hai to Yeh Jawaani Hai Deewani

No friendship-themed list is complete without Dil Chahta Hai, the 2001 film that redefined urban friendship for a generation. The film, starring Aamir Khan, Saif Ali Khan, and Akshaye Khanna, remains a benchmark for its portrayal of three friends navigating love and life in Mumbai.

Equally significant is Yeh Jawaani Hai Deewani, which pairs the energy of youth with the bittersweet reality of growing apart. The film's central message — that friendships survive distance — resonates with many who watch it with their own childhood friends.

Quirky Picks and Underrated Gems

The list also includes less obvious choices like Sholay, the 1975 classic that celebrates the legendary bond between Veeru and Jai. For those who prefer a lighter watch, Chhichhore offers a mix of college nostalgia and life lessons, while Dil Dhadakne Do brings a family cruise trip where friendships are tested and strengthened.

Also included are Rang De Basanti and 3 Idiots, both of which use friendship as a lens to address larger societal issues. The list may not be exhaustive, but it aims to spark conversations and memories for viewers of all ages.

Why These Films Work for Group Viewing

What makes these films ideal for a group watch is their universal appeal. They mix humour, emotion, and relatable situations that allow friends to laugh, argue, and perhaps even tear up together. Films like Queen and Angrezi Medium also find a place, showing that friendship is not limited to male bonding.

For those planning a virtual watch party, several of these films are available on streaming platforms, making it easier to sync viewing across cities. The idea is to celebrate the day with stories that mirror the viewers' own friendships.

As Friendship Day 2026 approaches, these 12 films offer a ready-made playlist. Whether you are revisiting an old favourite or discovering a new one, the day is best spent with the people who matter most.

Viewers can expect more curated lists from entertainment portals as the day approaches, but this one is a solid starting point for any Bollywood-loving group.
