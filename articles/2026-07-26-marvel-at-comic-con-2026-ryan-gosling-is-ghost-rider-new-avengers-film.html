Marvel at Comic-Con 2026: Ryan Gosling is Ghost Rider, New Avengers Film Revealed

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ryan Gosling cast as Ghost Rider in a film directed by Shawn Levy, set for a 2028 release.
  • A new Avengers film was announced, though details on the cast and plot remain scarce.
  • Multiple new Disney+ series were confirmed, expanding the MCU's television footprint.

Ghost Rider Rides into the MCU with Ryan Gosling

Marvel Studios made its biggest splash at Comic-Con 2026 by finally confirming that Ryan Gosling will play Ghost Rider. The announcement came during the studio's highly anticipated panel in Hall H, drawing a thunderous response from the crowd.

The film will be directed by Shawn Levy, known for his work on Deadpool & Wolverine and Stranger Things. It is scheduled for a 2028 release, giving the studio ample time to develop the character's supernatural corner of the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Gosling's casting had been speculated for years, with fans often campaigning for him to take on the role. The actor's previous near-misses with the franchise — he was reportedly in talks for other roles — made this announcement a cathartic moment for many in attendance.

A New Avengers Film on the Horizon

Beyond the Ghost Rider reveal, Marvel Studios confirmed that a new Avengers film is in development. While no title, director, or specific cast members were announced, the news signals the next major crossover event for the MCU.

This marks the first Avengers project since the events of Avengers: Endgame and the subsequent Multiverse Saga. The studio is likely planning to assemble a new roster of heroes, though official details remain under wraps.

Fans have been eager for a cohesive team-up film, especially after the sprawling narratives of recent phases. The announcement suggests Marvel is ready to bring its current threads together.

Disney+ Series Expansion Confirmed

Marvel also used the panel to announce several new series for Disney+. While specific titles were not all disclosed, the streamer will continue to be a key platform for character-driven stories that feed into the larger cinematic narrative.

The studio emphasized that these shows will feature both established heroes and newer characters introduced in recent films. This approach mirrors previous strategies, where series like WandaVision and Loki set up major plot points for movies.

One confirmed project is a series centered on a previously hinted-at supernatural team, tying directly into the Ghost Rider film. This interconnected storytelling remains a hallmark of the MCU's planning.

Fan Reaction and Social Media Frenzy

The announcements triggered an immediate wave of excitement online. Social media platforms saw a surge of posts, with the phrase 'Ryan Gosling Ghost Rider' trending globally within minutes of the reveal.

Videos from Hall H show fans screaming and applauding as Gosling was brought on stage. The actor reportedly told the crowd, 'I've been waiting for this call my whole life,' which only amplified the emotional response.

Industry analysts noted that the 2028 release date gives Marvel time to properly integrate the character into the broader MCU timeline, especially with the new Avengers film also in the works.

What remains unclear is how Ghost Rider will fit into the existing cosmic and mystical hierarchy of the MCU. Characters like Doctor Strange and Blade are expected to play roles in introducing this new element.

Marvel Studios has not yet confirmed a release date for the new Avengers film, nor has it revealed which heroes will be part of the lineup. Observers expect more details to emerge at future events, including D23 and next year's Comic-Con.
