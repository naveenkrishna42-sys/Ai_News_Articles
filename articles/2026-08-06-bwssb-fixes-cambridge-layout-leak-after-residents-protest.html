BWSSB fixes Cambridge Layout leak after residents' protest

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • BWSSB repaired a water leak in Cambridge Layout after residents complained.
  • Residents had demanded urgent action to stop water wastage.
  • The board's quick response has been welcomed by the local community.

Residents of Cambridge Layout in Bengaluru can breathe easy after the Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board (BWSSB) finally plugged a persistent water leak that had been wasting precious water for days. The repair comes after mounting pressure from the local community, who had grown increasingly frustrated with the delay.

Residents' patience wears thin

For several days, a steady stream of water had been flowing out from a damaged pipeline, causing not only wastage but also inconvenience to pedestrians and motorists. Locals said the leak had turned the area muddy and slippery, posing a safety hazard.

Residents of Cambridge Layout took to social media and lodged complaints with the BWSSB, demanding immediate action. They pointed out that in a city already grappling with water scarcity, such negligence was unacceptable.

BWSSB swings into action

Following the complaints, a BWSSB team arrived at the spot and carried out the necessary repairs. The board has not yet confirmed the exact cause of the leak, but officials said the pipeline has been restored and water supply in the area has returned to normal.

The swift action by the BWSSB has been appreciated by the residents, who had earlier threatened to escalate the matter if the leak was not fixed. A local resident, speaking on condition of anonymity, said, "We are happy that the issue was resolved, but it should not have taken this long."

Broader concerns remain

While this particular leak has been attended to, the incident has reignited concerns about the state of Bengaluru's aging water infrastructure. Residents point out that many other parts of the city face similar problems, and they want the BWSSB to adopt a more proactive approach.

The BWSSB has urged citizens to report leaks and other water-related issues promptly, assuring that teams will be deployed to address them. The board has also said it is working on a comprehensive plan to upgrade old pipelines across the city.

For now, the people of Cambridge Layout are relieved that the water flow has been stopped. But they remain watchful, hoping that the BWSSB will act before the next leak surfaces rather than after.
