CJP Team Attacked During Rajasthan School Inspection

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • A CJP delegation faced physical confrontation while en route to a Rajasthan school.
  • Team leader Ashutosh Ranka alleged that the attackers were BJP workers, not local villagers.
  • The BJP has rejected the allegations and stated the group must face consequences.

Confrontation During School Visit

A fact-finding team from the Citizens for Justice and Peace (CJP) reported a violent confrontation on Friday while attempting to reach a school in Rajasthan. Members of the team stated that their vehicles were targeted as they traveled, leading to a tense standoff in the area.

Abhijeet Dipke, a member of the delegation, alleged that the group was chased and subjected to stone-pelting. The incident caused significant damage to their vehicles, though the full extent of the property loss is still being assessed by local authorities.

Allegations Against BJP Workers

Ashutosh Ranka, who led the CJP team, was quick to identify the assailants. He explicitly denied that the group had been confronted by concerned local residents or villagers. Instead, Ranka stated that the individuals involved in the attack were "BJP goons" acting with political motives.

The CJP team asserts that their primary objective was to conduct an inspection of the school premises. They claim this effort was obstructed by organized groups intent on preventing them from documenting conditions at the site.

Political Rebuttal and Escalation

The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) has issued a firm denial of these claims. Party representatives in Jaipur dismissed the accusation that their workers were involved in any act of violence or intimidation against the delegation.

Instead, the party took a confrontational stance regarding the CJP’s presence in the region. A spokesperson for the BJP suggested that the group's actions had crossed a line, stating that they "must now learn a lesson" for their unauthorized activities in the area.

  • CJP team reports vehicle vandalism and stone-pelting.
  • Ashutosh Ranka claims attackers were politically motivated.
  • BJP rejects the allegations, citing the group's conduct.

Local law enforcement officials are currently reviewing evidence from the scene, including video footage captured during the altercation. No arrests have been officially confirmed as of Friday morning, and the investigation remains in its early stages.

Observers will be monitoring whether local police file formal charges or if the political rhetoric between the CJP and regional BJP leadership leads to further unrest in the district.
