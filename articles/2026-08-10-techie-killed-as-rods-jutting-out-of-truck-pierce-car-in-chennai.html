Techie killed as rods jutting out of truck pierce car in Chennai

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 28-year-old software engineer was killed when steel rods from a truck pierced his car's windshield in Chennai.
  • The incident occurred on a main road, causing traffic disruption as authorities cleared the scene.
  • Police have registered a case and are investigating the truck driver's role in the accident.

Chennai: A 28-year-old software engineer died on Monday morning after steel rods jutting out of a truck pierced the windshield of his car, in a gruesome accident on a city road. The victim, identified as R. Karthik, was driving to work when the incident occurred around 8:30 am near the Madhya Kailash junction on the Rajiv Gandhi Salai (OMR), a busy IT corridor.

According to police, a truck carrying steel rods was moving ahead of Karthik's car when the rods, which extended beyond the vehicle's length, suddenly pierced the rear windshield and struck him. The impact was fatal, and Karthik died on the spot. Passers-by alerted the traffic police, who arrived at the scene and diverted traffic for nearly an hour.

Accident details emerge

Initial reports suggest that the truck driver may not have been aware of the rods protruding from his vehicle. The rods, which were longer than the truck's bed, appeared to have swung or shifted during transit, causing them to jut out dangerously. Police have seized the truck and are questioning the driver, who has been identified but not yet named officially.

The victim's car, a white hatchback, sustained severe damage to its front windshield, with several rods penetrating the glass and cabin. Emergency responders, including fire and rescue personnel, had to cut through parts of the car to extricate the body. The area witnessed heavy traffic pile-up as curious onlookers gathered, and police had to use barricades to manage the crowd.

Police investigation underway

The Chennai traffic police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including causing death by negligence. The truck driver has been taken into custody for questioning, and investigators are examining whether the rods were properly secured. Preliminary findings suggest that the rods were not tied down adequately, a common violation seen on city roads.

Speaking to reporters, a senior police official said, "We are verifying the exact sequence of events. The driver's statement will be crucial in determining negligence." Officials have not yet confirmed whether the truck was overloaded or if the driver had prior violations.

Road safety concerns resurface

The incident has once again highlighted the dangers posed by vehicles carrying protruding loads on Chennai's busy roads. In recent years, several similar accidents have been reported, prompting calls for stricter enforcement of load-carrying norms. However, authorities have struggled to curb such violations, especially on arterial roads like OMR, which see heavy movement of goods vehicles.

Local residents and commuters expressed shock at the incident, with many demanding better regulation of heavy vehicles during peak hours. "This is a daily risk we take," said a commuter who witnessed the aftermath. "The authorities must act before more lives are lost."

The victim, Karthik, was a native of Madurai and had been working in a private software firm in the city for the past three years. He is survived by his parents, who have been informed and are expected to arrive in Chennai shortly. His body has been sent to a government hospital for post-mortem, after which it will be handed over to the family.

As the investigation continues, police are also checking CCTV footage from nearby cameras to piece together the exact moment of the crash. The truck driver may face further charges if evidence of reckless driving emerges.

Moving forward, traffic police have announced a special drive to check vehicles carrying protruding loads across the city, starting this week. Officials say they will impound vehicles found violating safety norms and prosecute drivers under relevant laws. Whether this will prevent future tragedies remains to be seen, but for now, the focus is on ensuring accountability in this case.
