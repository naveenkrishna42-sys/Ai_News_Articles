Introduction to the Incident

Siwet Tomar, a participant in the web series Rise and Fall 2, has denied allegations of physically assaulting his co-participant Shehzad Poonawalla. According to Tomar, he was provoked for 15-20 minutes before the incident occurred.

Claims of Provocation

Tomar claims that he only held Poonawalla's collar and did not hit him. This statement has been made in response to reports of a physical altercation between the two participants on the sets of Rise and Fall 2.

Statement from Siwet Tomar

Tomar stated that he was provoked for an extended period, which led to him holding Poonawalla's collar. However, he denies any further physical contact or assault.

Reactions from Other Participants

Other participants in the web series, including Karan Patel and Swara Bhasker, have reacted to the incident. While some have come out in support of Tomar, others have condemned his actions.

Eviction from the Show

As a result of the incident, Siwet Tomar has been evicted from the show. This decision has been made by the producers of Rise and Fall 2, citing the need to maintain a safe and respectful environment for all participants.
