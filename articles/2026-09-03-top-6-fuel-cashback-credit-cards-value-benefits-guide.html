Introduction to Fuel Cashback Credit Cards

Fuel cashback credit cards offer significant rewards for petrol and diesel purchases, making them a popular choice among drivers. These cards provide cashback on fuel expenses, which can be redeemed as statement credits or directly into bank accounts. The rewards are typically calculated as a percentage of the fuel amount spent, and the cashback amount varies depending on the card and the fuel station.
