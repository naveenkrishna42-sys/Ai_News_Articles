Telangana Extends Indiramma Houses Towers Application Deadline to Aug 15

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Application deadline extended to August 15
  • Scheme targets urban poor under Indiramma housing initiative
  • Extension aims to ensure wider coverage and convenience

More Time for Applicants

The Telangana government has extended the deadline for submitting applications under the ‘Indiramma Houses Towers’ scheme to August 15. The move comes as authorities look to accommodate more applicants for the urban housing initiative.

Originally set to close earlier, the new date provides additional breathing room for eligible residents across the state, particularly in Hyderabad and surrounding municipal areas. Officials have urged prospective beneficiaries to complete their submissions before the revised cutoff.

What is Indiramma Houses Towers?

The scheme is part of the broader Indiramma housing programme, which aims to provide affordable housing to economically weaker sections and lower-income groups in urban pockets. The ‘Towers’ component specifically focuses on multi-storey residential buildings, designed to optimise land use in crowded city limits.

Under this initiative, the government plans to construct high-rise complexes with basic amenities, targeting families that lack permanent shelter. The application process is being managed through designated online portals and local municipal offices.

How to Apply

Interested applicants can submit their forms through the official web portal or visit their respective municipal corporation offices for assistance. Required documents typically include proof of identity, residence, and income certificate, though authorities have not yet specified any changes to the existing checklist.

Officials have advised applicants to double-check their details before submission, as incomplete or incorrect forms may lead to rejection. They have also cautioned against approaching intermediaries, stating that the process is entirely free and transparent.

Why the Extension?

Sources indicate that the extension was prompted by a lower-than-expected number of applications in certain zones, along with technical glitches reported on the submission portal. The state government is keen to ensure that no eligible family misses out due to procedural hurdles.

By pushing the deadline to August 15, the administration hopes to achieve fuller coverage and streamline the verification process. This also aligns with the state’s broader push to expedite urban housing delivery ahead of upcoming civic assessments.

What’s Next

Once applications close, officials will begin the scrutiny and verification phase, followed by a draw of lots or merit-based allocation, depending on the scheme’s guidelines. Beneficiaries are expected to be announced in the following months, with construction slated to begin in select locations soon after.

For now, all eyes are on the August 15 cutoff, with civic bodies ramping up outreach to push last-minute enrollments. Applicants are advised to stay updated through official channels for any further announcements.
