Ruckus at Secunderabad Bonalu cheque distribution event

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Scuffle broke out at cheque distribution event in Secunderabad
  • Crowd rushed towards stage, leading to chaos and police action
  • Event part of annual Bonalu festival aid distribution

Chaos at Secunderabad event

A cheque distribution programme held as part of the Bonalu festival in Secunderabad descended into chaos on Friday, with video footage showing crowds jostling and pushing near the stage. The event, organised to provide financial assistance to eligible beneficiaries, saw a sudden surge as people rushed forward, prompting security personnel to intervene.

Visuals from the venue show a thick crowd pressing against barricades, with some people climbing over them. Police were seen trying to restore order as shouts and pushing continued for several minutes. The exact reason for the sudden rush remains unclear, though initial reports suggest a large number of attendees attempted to reach the dais simultaneously.

Police intervene to control situation

Personnel from the local police station were deployed in strength to manage the crowd. They were seen forming a cordon and physically pushing back those trying to advance. No injuries have been reported so far, and officials have not yet commented on whether any arrests were made.

The programme was part of the annual Bonalu festivities, a prominent festival in Telangana dedicated to Goddess Mahakali. Such cheque distribution events are commonly held across the state to disburse welfare funds, but this year's gathering in Secunderabad appears to have been poorly managed in terms of crowd control.

Eyewitness accounts

Some attendees claimed that the organisers had underestimated the turnout, leading to inadequate arrangements. Others said confusion over token distribution triggered the rush. Security personnel were seen escorting a few people away from the stage area, though their identities were not immediately known.

The event was eventually resumed after the situation was brought under control, according to local reports. However, the incident has raised questions about the planning of such public programmes, especially during festival seasons when crowds tend to be larger.

Background of Bonalu aid distribution

Bonalu is celebrated with great fervour in Hyderabad and Secunderabad, with processions, offerings, and cultural events. The state government often uses the occasion to distribute cheques to beneficiaries under various welfare schemes, making these events politically significant.

In the past, similar programmes have seen large gatherings, but Friday's ruckus stands out for the level of disorder captured on camera. Authorities are expected to review the arrangements for upcoming events in the city to avoid a repeat.

Officials have yet to release an official statement regarding the incident. The district administration may conduct an inquiry into how the crowd was managed, though no such announcement has been made so far.

What to watch

With the Bonalu festival continuing over the coming days, attention will be on how authorities handle subsequent distribution programmes. Any official response or action against those responsible for the chaos will also be closely followed.
