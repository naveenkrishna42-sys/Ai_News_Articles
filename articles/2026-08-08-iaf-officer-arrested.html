Arrest and Investigation

An Indian Air Force (IAF) officer has been arrested for allegedly leaking sensitive information to a Pakistan-based operative. The officer was reportedly lured by a woman on social media chats to share defence secrets with a Pakistani handler.

According to the police, the officer was trapped in a honey-trap operation and shared military information with the handler. The officer has been booked under the Secrets Act for sharing defence information.

Details of the Case

Officials have not yet confirmed the specifics of the case, including the rank and name of the IAF officer. However, it is reported that the officer was in contact with a woman on social media, who was allegedly working for a Pakistani intelligence agency.

The woman reportedly built a relationship with the officer on social media and convinced him to share sensitive information about the Indian military. The officer allegedly shared the information with the woman, who then passed it on to her Pakistani handler.
