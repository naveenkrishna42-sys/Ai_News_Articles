CWG 2026 Day 5: Gulveer Singh, Harjinder Kaur win silver medals for India

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Gulveer Singh became the first Indian man to win a Commonwealth Games medal in the 10,000m, taking silver.
  • Weightlifter Harjinder Kaur improved her 2022 bronze to silver in Glasgow.
  • India's medal tally continued to grow on Day 5 of the 2026 Commonwealth Games.

Gulveer Singh makes history in the men's 10,000m

Gulveer Singh etched his name into the record books on Day 5 of the Commonwealth Games in Glasgow. He became the first Indian man to win a medal in the men's 10,000 metres event, securing a silver medal.

The long-distance runner paced himself through the gruelling 25-lap race. His finish time and the name of the gold medallist were not immediately confirmed by officials at the time of reporting.

The achievement marks a significant breakthrough for Indian athletics in endurance events on the Commonwealth stage. India has traditionally struggled in longer track distances at these Games.

Harjinder Kaur upgrades bronze to silver in weightlifting

Weightlifter Harjinder Kaur added to India's medal haul with a silver medal of her own. She competed in her weight category and delivered a superlative performance to change the colour of her medal from the bronze she won at the 2022 Commonwealth Games in Birmingham.

Harjinder's lifts in the snatch and clean and jerk were strong enough to place her second overall. The specific weight totals and the identity of the gold medal winner were not released in the initial reports.

Her improvement from bronze to silver underscores the steady progress she has made in her training over the last four years. The phrase 'no pain, no gain' was used by multiple Indian outlets to describe her determined showing.

India's campaign gains momentum in Glasgow

Day 5 was a productive one for the Indian contingent. Both medals came from events where India had not been considered overwhelming favourites, making the results particularly encouraging for the team management.

Other Indian athletes competed across various disciplines, including boxing, badminton, and wrestling. Detailed results from those events were still being compiled as the day's action concluded.

The two silvers added to a growing tally that includes medals from earlier days in shooting, lawn bowls, and para-sports.

What to watch next

Competition continues on Day 6, with several Indian medal contenders still in the fray. All eyes will be on the track and field events, as well as the boxing ring, where India hopes to add more gold to its collection before the Games conclude.
