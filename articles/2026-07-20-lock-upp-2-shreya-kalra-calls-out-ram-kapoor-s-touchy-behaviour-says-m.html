Shreya Kalra speaks out on Lock Upp 2

Reality show Lock Upp 2 has seen its first major controversy. Contestant Shreya Kalra has publicly accused fellow inmate Ram Kapoor of crossing boundaries, calling out his 'touchy' behaviour on the show.

Kalra, who is known for her direct approach, did not mince words. She was seen telling Kapoor to 'maintain some boundaries' after an incident that left her visibly uncomfortable.

The specific trigger was a kiss that Kalra says Kapoor initiated without her consent. Sources close to the production say the moment was captured on camera and has become a hot topic among viewers.

Details of the incident

According to Kalra, Kapoor’s behaviour went beyond casual friendship. She claimed that his physical advances made her feel uneasy in the high-stress environment of the lock-up.

In a conversation with other contestants, Kalra reportedly said, 'He needs to understand that no means no. I am not comfortable with this.' The remark drew immediate support from several housemates who urged her to set clear boundaries.

The show’s format, which keeps contestants under constant surveillance, means that the incident was recorded. Clips from the episode have since gone viral on social media, with fans debating the line between friendly banter and unwanted attention.

Gautami Kapoor defends her husband

Ram Kapoor’s wife, actress Gautami Kapoor, has stepped in to defend him. In a statement to NDTV, she said, 'He is not sleazy. People are misreading the situation.'

Gautami argued that her husband’s behaviour has been taken out of context. She described him as a warm and affectionate person who often uses touch to connect with people. 'Ram would never intentionally make anyone uncomfortable,' she added.

However, the defence has not quieted the criticism. Many social media users have pointed out that intent does not negate impact. The debate has now expanded beyond the show, touching on broader issues of consent in public spaces.

Fan reactions and industry response

Fans have largely rallied behind Shreya Kalra. On platforms like X and Instagram, users have praised her for speaking up in a situation where many might stay silent. One user wrote, 'Good for Shreya for calling it out. No one should tolerate that.'

Others have called for the show’s producers to take a stand. 'Lock Upp needs to address this seriously,' another comment read. The show’s official channels have not yet issued a statement regarding the incident.

The entertainment industry in India has seen a shift in recent years, with more people willing to call out inappropriate behaviour. This episode is likely to add to that conversation.

What happens next on Lock Upp 2 remains to be seen. The show’s makers may choose to address the issue on air, or the contestants might resolve it among themselves. For now, the cameras are still rolling, and the drama is far from over.
