Overview of the Contenders

Three smartphones target the sub‑₹20,000 segment in India: Lava’s newly launched Virat Curve 5G, Poco’s M8 Power and Vivo’s T5 Lite. Each brand positions its device as a balanced option for users seeking decent performance, long battery life and modern design without stretching their budget. This analysis breaks down the key attributes that affect everyday usage and long‑term ownership.

Design and Display

Lava Virat Curve

The Virat Curve features a 6.6‑inch FHD+ LCD with a 90 Hz refresh rate, delivering smooth scrolling for social media and gaming. Its chassis adopts a curved rear panel that improves grip, and the device measures 164.5 × 76.2 × 8.9 mm, weighing 202 g.

Poco M8 Power

Poco’s offering sports a 6.5‑inch HD+ LCD with a standard 60 Hz panel. The build uses a polycarbonate back with a glossy finish, dimensions of 164 × 76 × 9 mm and a weight around 195 g.

Vivo T5 Lite

Vivo equips the T5 Lite with a 6.58‑inch FHD+ LCD, also limited to 60 Hz. The phone’s design emphasizes a slim profile at 162 × 75.5 × 8.4 mm and a weight of approximately 188 g.

Performance and Battery

Lava Virat Curve

Powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 7100 SoC, the Virat Curve pairs an octa‑core CPU with a Mali‑G57 GPU, providing capable performance for mid‑range gaming and multitasking. The device ships with 6 GB RAM and 128 GB internal storage, expandable via microSD. Its standout feature is the 6,000 mAh battery, supporting 18 W fast charging, which typically yields over a day of heavy usage.

Poco M8 Power

The M8 Power relies on the Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 1 chipset, coupled with 4 GB RAM and 64 GB storage (expandable). While adequate for everyday tasks, the GPU is less suited for sustained gaming. A 5,000 mAh cell with 10 W charging delivers moderate endurance, often requiring a top‑up before the end of a demanding day.

Vivo T5 Lite

Vivo’s model uses the MediaTek Helio G85 processor, 4 GB RAM and 64 GB storage, targeting basic productivity and light gaming. The 5,000 mAh battery supports 10 W charging, offering similar battery life to the Poco M8 Power under comparable usage patterns.

Price and Value Proposition

Lava prices the Virat Curve at ₹19,999, positioning it as a premium‑budget option with 5G connectivity and a large battery. Poco lists the M8 Power at approximately ₹14,999, while Vivo’s T5 Lite is marketed around ₹13,999. The price gap reflects differences in chipset generation, battery capacity and build materials.

For users prioritizing longevity and future‑proof 5G support, the Virat Curve justifies its higher cost. Buyers focused on immediate savings and acceptable performance may find the Poco or Vivo models adequate, especially if they do not require extended battery life or the latest processor.
