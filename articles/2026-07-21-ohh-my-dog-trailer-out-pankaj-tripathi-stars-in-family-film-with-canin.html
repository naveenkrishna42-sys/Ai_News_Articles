Ohh My Dog Trailer Out: Pankaj Tripathi Stars in Family Film with Canine Co-Stars

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pankaj Tripathi stars in 'Ohh My Dog', a family entertainer directed by Amit Rai.
  • The trailer showcases the bond between a man and his dogs, played by canine actors Oscar and Brun.
  • Tripathi reportedly forwent his fee to support the film's production.
  • Director Amit Rai emphasizes the film's message that 'your dog raises you, not the other way around'.

Mumbai: The trailer for 'Ohh My Dog' dropped on Tuesday, offering a glimpse of what promises to be a wholesome family entertainer. The film stars Pankaj Tripathi alongside two canine co-stars, Oscar and Brun.

Directed by Amit Rai, the film explores the unique bond between humans and their four-legged companions. The trailer leans heavily on emotional beats and light-hearted moments, positioning it as a film for all ages.

A Story of Companionship

The narrative centers on a man whose life is transformed by his dogs. Early footage shows Tripathi's character navigating everyday struggles, with the dogs providing both comic relief and emotional support. The tagline, "Your dog raises you, not the other way around," sets the tone.

Director Amit Rai spoke about the film's core theme. He called it a celebration of the human-canine relationship, stressing that it is not just a pet movie but a story about mutual growth. Rai has previously directed smaller independent projects.

Pankaj Tripathi's Commitment

Tripathi, known for his versatile roles in films like 'Mimi' and 'Newton', reportedly took a pay cut for this project. Several reports suggest he forwent his fee entirely to ensure the film could be made without financial hurdles. This move has drawn appreciation from industry peers.

In the trailer, Tripathi shares screen space with the dogs in almost every scene. His trademark deadpan delivery appears intact, even when interacting with his canine co-stars. The actor has not publicly commented on the fee waiver but has shared promotional material on social media.

Canine Stars Oscar and Brun

The dogs, Oscar and Brun, are trained animals brought in specifically for the film. Their scenes required multiple takes and specialized handling. The production team worked with animal trainers to ensure the dogs' comfort and safety on set.

Animal welfare guidelines were followed during the shoot, according to the production house. The dogs are expected to be a major draw for family audiences, especially children.

The film is produced under a relatively small banner, and the team has relied on word-of-mouth from the trailer release. Early reactions online have been positive, with many praising the concept and Tripathi's involvement.

'Ohh My Dog' is slated for a theatrical release later this year. The exact date has not been announced. The film will compete with other family releases during the festive season.
