Hina Khan on Aamir Khan's third marriage: Not considered good in our society

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hina Khan said multiple marriages are not considered good in Indian society.
  • She reacted to criticism faced by Aamir Khan after his third marriage to Gauri Spratt.
  • Rubina Dilaik also discussed the social perception of Aamir Khan's marriage.
  • The comments come amid ongoing public debate around Aamir Khan's personal life.

Hina Khan's take on societal norms

Actress Hina Khan has weighed in on the controversy surrounding Aamir Khan's third marriage to Gauri Spratt. Speaking to the media, she said that multiple marriages are not viewed positively in Indian society.

“It isn’t considered very good in our society,” Hina Khan stated. Her remarks come as the actor faces widespread criticism for marrying for the third time.

Rubina Dilaik joins the discussion

Television actress Rubina Dilaik also shared her thoughts on the matter. She echoed Hina Khan's sentiments, saying that in Indian culture, remarrying multiple times is often frowned upon.

“Humare samaj mein bahut acha nahi maana jata,” Rubina Dilaik said, referring to the social perception of multiple marriages. The discussion has sparked debate across social media platforms.

Aamir Khan's marriage to Gauri Spratt

Aamir Khan married Gauri Spratt earlier this year. The wedding was a private affair attended by close family and friends. This is the actor's third marriage, following his divorce from Kiran Rao in 2021.

The announcement drew mixed reactions from fans and industry insiders. While some congratulated the couple, others questioned the actor's decision to marry again at this stage in his life.

Public reaction and criticism

Social media has been divided over Aamir Khan's personal choices. Many users have criticised the actor, citing his previous marriages as a point of contention. Others have defended his right to privacy and personal happiness.

Hina Khan's remarks add to the ongoing conversation. She did not directly criticise Aamir Khan but highlighted the societal lens through which such decisions are judged.

“Seriously? Getting married again?” was a common reaction from netizens, as reported by multiple news outlets. The backlash has been particularly sharp given Aamir Khan's public persona as a thoughtful and progressive figure.

What lies ahead

Aamir Khan has not publicly responded to the criticism. His upcoming projects include a film scheduled for release next year. The debate over his marriage is likely to continue as more celebrities speak out.
