Vijay Sethupathi Calls CM Vijay 'Cute Fellow' Amid Bigg Boss Tamil Row

Key points
  • Vijay Sethupathi publicly dismissed claims that he shaded Tamil Nadu CM Vijay on Bigg Boss Tamil, calling the Chief Minister a 'cute fellow'.
  • Director Atlee urged fans not to fall into online traps designed to manufacture a Vijay vs Vijay Sethupathi rivalry.
  • Vijay Sethupathi recalled an unexpected phone call from Rajinikanth during Jailer 2, calling it a career highlight.

The Controversy, Explained

Vijay Sethupathi has broken his silence on the viral claims that he took a veiled dig at Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Vijay during his appearance on Bigg Boss Tamil. Speaking to reporters at a promotional event, the actor dismissed the narrative outright, describing the Chief Minister as a "cute fellow" and insisting there was no subtext behind his remarks on the show.

The row began after a clip from the reality show circulated widely, with sections of social media interpreting a light-hearted comment as a political swipe. Within hours, hashtags trended and fan armies on both sides traded barbs — a familiar pattern in Tamil cinema, where on-screen rivalries are routinely amplified into offline conflict.

Atlee Steps In: 'Don't Fall Into Traps'

Director Atlee, who has worked closely with CM Vijay across multiple blockbusters, weighed in publicly, urging fans not to "fall into traps" engineered to divide the industry. His intervention matters commercially: Atlee remains one of the most bankable directors in Indian cinema, and his statement carries weight with distributors and exhibitors who track star-fan friction closely before locking release windows.

For the Tamil film trade, such controversies are not trivial. A sustained fan war can affect opening-weekend occupancy, social media sentiment, and even OTT acquisition pricing for upcoming projects. Both Vijay and Vijay Sethupathi have films in various stages of production, making de-escalation commercially sensible.

Jason Sanjay and the Next Generation

Vijay Sethupathi also addressed Jason Sanjay, son of CM Vijay, who is carving out his own space in the industry. The senior actor called him "cute" — a warm, offhand endorsement that carries real value for a debutant trying to build credibility beyond his family name.

In an industry where lineage opens doors but does not guarantee longevity, praise from a performer of Vijay Sethupathi's calibre is meaningful. It signals acceptance from the craft-driven wing of Tamil cinema, not just the star-system ecosystem.

Rajinikanth, Jailer 2, and the Humility Factor

Vijay Sethupathi revealed that Rajinikanth personally called him during the making of Jailer 2 — a moment he described as unexpected precisely because of the superstar's stature. "I didn't expect that because he's a big superstar," he said, recounting the exchange.

That anecdote does more than humanise a veteran; it reinforces Vijay Sethupathi's positioning as an actor rooted in humility and craft rather than hype. For audiences deciding where to spend on theatrical tickets, that authenticity is part of the draw.

Real-World Takeaways for Fans

  • No genuine feud: Vijay Sethupathi explicitly denied shading CM Vijay, calling him a "cute fellow".
  • Industry de-escalation: Atlee's appeal to avoid traps shows senior talent actively cooling fan wars.
  • Young talent boost: Praise for Jason Sanjay adds early credibility to a rising actor.
  • Rajinikanth's gesture: The Jailer 2 phone call underscores cross-generational respect in Kollywood.
  • Commercial stakes: Fan friction can influence box office and OTT deals, making peace the profitable option.

Why This Matters Beyond the Headlines

Tamil cinema's fan culture is among the most passionate in India, and manufactured controversies can spiral quickly. Statements from figures like Atlee and Vijay Sethupathi act as circuit breakers, redirecting attention toward the work itself — upcoming releases, performances, and collaborations rather than invented rivalries.

For Vijay Sethupathi, the episode reinforces a public image built on versatility and restraint. For CM Vijay, the absence of escalation keeps the focus on governance and cinema alike. And for fans, the message is plain: the trap is the trend, not the talent.
