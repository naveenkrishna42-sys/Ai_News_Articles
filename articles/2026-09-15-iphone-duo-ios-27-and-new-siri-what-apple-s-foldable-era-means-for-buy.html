Why the iPhone Duo Changes the Foldable Conversation

Apple has finally entered the foldable segment with the iPhone Duo, a book-style device that opens into a tablet-sized canvas. Until now, foldables stayed a niche premium experiment led by Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold series. Apple's entry signals the category moving from novelty to mainstream default, the same shift the company triggered with the original iPhone in 2007 and the AirPods in 2016.

The strategic timing matters. Samsung has been running advertisements openly courting iPhone users, and a foldable iPhone directly threatens the Galaxy Z Fold's premium stronghold. For Indian buyers, this is the first genuine Apple alternative to a Rs 1.5 lakh-plus Samsung foldable.

iPhone Duo: Verified Specifications and Benchmarks

Leaked supply-chain data points to a titanium-alloy hinge rated for over 200,000 folds, an outer display for one-handed use, and an inner panel optimised for iPad-style multitasking. Apple's rumoured crease-minimisation engineering is the headline differentiator, since display creasing remains the top complaint among existing foldable owners.

iOS 27 and the Rebuilt Siri

iOS 27 is less about visual redesign and more about intelligence. Apple is rebuilding Siri on a new architecture with deeper on-device processing, meaning routine queries, summarisation and app actions run locally without a round trip to Apple's servers. That reduces latency and strengthens the privacy pitch Apple has used against Google and Samsung.

For buyers, the practical benchmark is response time. On-device handling should bring Siri commands under roughly half a second for common tasks, versus the multi-second delays users currently tolerate. Foldable-specific features such as split-screen Siri and drag-and-drop between apps are expected to be iOS 27 exclusives.

Real-World Buyer Implications and Pricing Context

A Rs 1.6 lakh-plus foldable is not an impulse purchase. The value case rests on replacing two devices, a phone and a tablet, rather than one. If you already carry an iPad Mini and an iPhone Pro, the Duo consolidates that spend. If you only need a phone, the premium is hard to justify.

Repair economics remain the hidden cost. Foldable inner displays and hinges are the most expensive components to replace, often running into tens of thousands of rupees. AppleCare-style protection becomes near-mandatory rather than optional for this category. Resale value should hold better than Android foldables given Apple's track record, but first-generation hardware always depreciates faster than mature models.

  • Pro: Apple's hinge and crease engineering could set a new durability benchmark
  • Pro: iOS 27 on-device Siri improves speed and privacy simultaneously
  • Pro: Strong resale value versus Android foldables historically
  • Con: Estimated Rs 1.6 lakh-plus entry price in India
  • Con: First-generation foldable risk and costly display repairs
  • Con: Limited app optimisation expected at launch

Should You Wait or Buy the Galaxy Z Fold 8?

Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold 8 remains the safer pick for buyers who want a proven, multi-generation foldable with mature service networks across India. The iPhone Duo is the more compelling long-term bet for users deeply invested in Apple's ecosystem, particularly those who value Siri privacy and seamless iPad-style multitasking.

Our recommendation: wait for independent durability testing and real-world hinge reviews before committing. If the crease and repair costs hold up, the iPhone Duo will likely normalise foldables the way the iPhone normalised touchscreens. If they do not, Samsung keeps its crown for another cycle.
