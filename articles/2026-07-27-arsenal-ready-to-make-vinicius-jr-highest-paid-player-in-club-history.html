Arsenal ready to make Vinicius Jr highest-paid player in club history

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Arsenal willing to make Vinicius Junior their highest-paid player ever
  • Real Madrid are pushing to extend Vinicius's contract amid interest
  • Transfer could reshape Premier League wage structure

Record-breaking offer on the table

Arsenal are ready to smash their wage structure to bring Real Madrid winger Vinicius Junior to north London. Sources indicate the Gunners are prepared to make the Brazilian their highest-paid player in club history if he agrees to a transfer.

The move would see Vinicius earn more than any Arsenal player before him. Current top earners like Bukayo Saka and Martin Ødegaard would be eclipsed by the new deal.

Real Madrid's counter-moves

Real Madrid are not sitting idle. The La Liga giants have stepped up efforts to renew Vinicius's contract, with reports suggesting 16 different factors are pushing the club to secure his long-term future.

Madrid are also exploring a deal for RB Leipzig midfielder Yan Diomande, a move that could signal their intent to reshape the squad. Whether that includes letting Vinicius go remains unclear.

Interest from multiple clubs

Arsenal are not the only club tracking the 26-year-old. The Athletic and Sky Sports have both reported that the Gunners are actively exploring a deal, but other European heavyweights are also monitoring the situation.

Vinicius has been a key figure for Madrid since joining from Flamengo in 2018. His pace and dribbling have made him one of the most dangerous wingers in world football.

What this means for Arsenal

Signing Vinicius would be a statement of intent from Arsenal. The club has steadily climbed back into Champions League contention under Mikel Arteta, and adding a player of Vinicius's calibre would signal title ambitions.

However, the financial commitment would be enormous. Arsenal would need to balance their books while complying with Premier League spending rules. The wage bill would see a significant spike.

Contract talks in limbo

Vinicius's current deal with Real Madrid runs until 2027, but negotiations over an extension have been ongoing. BBC Sport reports that the player's future is still uncertain, with no agreement yet reached.

Madrid are keen to avoid losing another star player on favourable terms. The club has learned from past departures of players like Cristiano Ronaldo and Casemiro.

What happens next

The transfer window remains open, and talks are expected to intensify in the coming weeks. Arsenal will have to convince both Real Madrid and Vinicius that a move to the Emirates is the right step.

For now, all eyes are on whether Madrid can finalise a new contract or whether Arsenal's record offer will prove too tempting to refuse.
