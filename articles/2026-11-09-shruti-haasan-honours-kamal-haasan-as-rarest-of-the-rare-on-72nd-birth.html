Tribute from Shruti Haasan

On his 72nd birthday, Shruti Haasan honoured Kamal Haasan as one of cinema's most rare and enduring figures. The tribute, described as 'rarest of the rare,' captures the depth of admiration fans and colleagues have long held for the superstar. Shruti Haasan's remarks reflect not just personal respect but a recognition of how Kamal Haasan has shaped Tamil and pan-Indian cinema over decades.

CM Vijay's Birthday Wish

Tamil Nadu Chief Minister M K Stalin's representative Vijay echoed the sentiment, wished Kamal Haasan 'May you achieve more milestones.' This public endorsement from political leadership underscores the stature Kamal Haasan holds across state and industry corridors. It signals that his influence transcends entertainment, touching governance and cultural policy discussions.

Kalki 2898 AD Team Tribute

The production team behind Kalki 2898 AD expressed their own tribute, calling Kamal Haasan a 'supreme force of cinema.' They shared an unseen Behind-the-Scenes moment with actress Nag Ashwin. This collaboration between the film's creative team and a leading actress highlights how Kamal's work continues to inspire current projects and young talent.

Wider Industry Response

Multiple outlets—Telegraph India, The Times of India, Cinema Express—reported simultaneous wishes from Simran, Mammootty, and other industry stalwarts. The breadth of recognition confirms Kamal Haasan remains the connective tissue around which Tamil film circles operate. His 72 years of screen presence make him a benchmark that newer actors reference when defining their craft.
