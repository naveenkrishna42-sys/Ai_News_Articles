Zaheer Khan backs Gurnoor Brar as India's next fast-bowling star

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Zaheer Khan calls Gurnoor Brar an exciting fast-bowling prospect
  • He approves of the fast-tracking of Brar into India's Test squad
  • Brar is being considered for the upcoming Sri Lanka Test series

A veteran's endorsement

Zaheer Khan, one of India's most decorated fast bowlers, has thrown his weight behind young pacer Gurnoor Brar. In an exclusive interaction, the former left-arm spearhead made his admiration clear, saying, "Gurnoor Brar is an exciting player... expect a lot from him."

The praise comes at a pivotal moment. Brar, who has been turning heads in domestic cricket, is now being seriously considered for India's Test squad for the upcoming series against Sri Lanka.

Why Brar stands out

Zaheer, known for his shrewd reading of the game, did not hold back on specifics. He highlighted Brar's raw pace and his ability to generate bounce as key differentiators. "It's good that he has been fast-tracked," Zaheer said, referring to the selectors' decision to accelerate Brar's international progression.

The veteran's words carry weight. Having taken 311 wickets in 92 Tests, Zaheer understands the demands of the longest format. His endorsement suggests that Brar is not just a white-ball novelty but a bowler with the toolkit to thrive in red-ball cricket.

A selectorial gamble?

India's pace attack has long been the envy of world cricket, with a conveyor belt of talent emerging from the domestic circuit. Brar's potential inclusion in the squad for the Sri Lanka Tests signals a shift in philosophy — a willingness to invest in youth ahead of a busy international calendar.

However, fast-tracking a young bowler is not without risks. The step-up from domestic to international cricket can be brutal, and managing workload becomes crucial. Zaheer, who himself dealt with injuries during his career, seems confident that Brar has the temperament to handle the pressure.

"He's got the pace, he's got the aggression," Zaheer added, indicating that the mental aspect of the game is just as important as the physical.

The road ahead

The Sri Lanka series, expected to be played on spin-friendly pitches, will test Brar's patience and control. But if Zaheer's assessment is anything to go by, the young pacer is ready to seize the opportunity.

Indian cricket fans, who have seen the rise of Jasprit Bumrah and Mohammed Siraj in recent years, will be eager to see if Brar can follow in their footsteps. The selectors' decision to fast-track him suggests they believe he can.

With the Test series against Sri Lanka on the horizon, all eyes will be on the squad announcement. If Brar makes the cut, he will have the chance to justify Zaheer Khan's faith — and begin a journey that could define Indian fast bowling for years to come.

For now, the young pacer must focus on what he does best: hitting the deck hard and making the ball talk. Zaheer, for one, is convinced that big things are on the horizon. "Expect a lot from him," he said, and for a player who has seen it all, that is no small compliment.
