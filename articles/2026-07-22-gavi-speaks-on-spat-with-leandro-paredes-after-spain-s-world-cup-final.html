Gavi speaks on spat with Leandro Paredes after Spain’s World Cup final win

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Gavi has spoken publicly about the altercation with Leandro Paredes following Spain's World Cup final victory over Argentina.
  • FIFA is investigating the violent clash that broke out after the match, which involved multiple players from both sides.
  • Argentina's Paredes has also broken his silence, stating he owes no apology for his role in the incident.
  • Spain's Dani Olmo criticised Argentina for turning their backs on Spain's trophy celebration, citing a lack of values.

Gavi breaks silence after final brawl

Spain midfielder Gavi has addressed the heated confrontation with Argentina's Leandro Paredes that erupted after the World Cup final. The incident marred what should have been a moment of triumph for La Roja, who defeated Argentina to lift the trophy.

Speaking to reporters, Gavi described the altercation as an unfortunate end to an otherwise historic match. He did not elaborate on specific exchanges but acknowledged the tension that boiled over on the pitch.

FIFA has launched an investigation into the violent clash. Officials are reviewing footage and match reports to determine whether disciplinary action is warranted against any players.

Paredes refuses to apologise

Argentina's Leandro Paredes has also broken his silence. In a statement to media, he said he owes no apology for his role in the brawl. The midfielder insisted his actions were provoked by Spain's behaviour during the match and the post-game celebration.

Paredes was involved in a physical confrontation with Gavi and other Spanish players as the final whistle blew. Television replays showed the Argentine appearing to swing an arm in the direction of Gavi, though no red card was issued on the field. FIFA later reviewed the incident and sources suggest Paredes could face retrospective punishment.

The Times of India reported that Paredes made clear he does not regret his actions. He has not issued any official apology to Gavi or the Spanish team.

Olmo fires back at Argentina

Spain forward Dani Olmo did not hold back in his assessment of Argentina's conduct. He accused the Argentine team of lacking values after they turned their backs on Spain's trophy ceremony.

"We're an example of how to win with dignity," Olmo told Goal.com. He said Argentina's refusal to stay for the full celebration showed disrespect. Olmo's comments came after footage emerged of several Argentine players walking away as Spain lifted the trophy.

The incident has deepened the rivalry between the two footballing nations. Both teams had clashed earlier in the tournament, but the final brought tensions to a head.

What happens next?

FIFA's disciplinary committee is expected to release its findings in the coming days. Gavi and Paredes could face fines or suspensions depending on the severity of their involvement. The football world is watching closely to see if this on-field rivalry spills over into future international fixtures.
