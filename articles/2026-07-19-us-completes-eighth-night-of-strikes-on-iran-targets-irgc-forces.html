US Completes Eighth Night of Strikes on Iran, Targets IRGC Forces

📅 2026-07-19 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • US airstrikes on Iran continue for an eighth consecutive night.
  • Targets include Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) positions.
  • Strikes are a response to an attack on American troops in Jordan.
  • Tensions between the US and Iran escalate across West Asia.

Eighth Night of Strikes

The United States military has completed its eighth consecutive night of airstrikes on Iranian territory, targeting positions held by the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC). The strikes are a direct response to a recent attack on American troops stationed in Jordan, which resulted in casualties.

Pentagon officials confirmed the latest wave of strikes late Saturday, stating that the operations were focused on IRGC infrastructure and command facilities. The US has described the action as a measured but firm response to protect its forces.

Retaliation for Jordan Attack

The escalation began after a drone strike hit a US base in Jordan, killing two American soldiers and wounding several others. Washington blamed Iran-backed militias for the attack and vowed to hold Tehran accountable.

President Joe Biden authorized the initial round of airstrikes, which have since expanded into a sustained campaign. The US military says it is targeting facilities used by the IRGC to plan and execute attacks against American interests.

Scope of Operations

US Central Command (CENTCOM) has not released a full list of targets, but reports indicate that the strikes have hit ammunition depots, drone launch sites, and command centers in several Iranian provinces. The operations have involved both manned aircraft and drones.

The US military has stated that it is taking precautions to avoid civilian casualties, but independent verification of the impact remains difficult. Iranian state media has reported explosions in multiple locations but has not provided a comprehensive casualty count.

Regional Tensions Rise

The sustained bombing campaign has significantly heightened tensions across West Asia. Iran has condemned the strikes as a violation of its sovereignty and has vowed to retaliate. Hezbollah and other Iran-backed groups have also issued statements threatening to escalate attacks on US targets.

Diplomatic efforts to de-escalate the situation have so far failed to gain traction. The United Nations has called for an immediate ceasefire, but both Washington and Tehran appear committed to their current courses of action.

What to Watch

With no signs of a diplomatic breakthrough, the immediate focus will be on Iran's next move. Whether Tehran chooses a direct military response or continues to rely on proxy forces will determine the trajectory of this rapidly escalating conflict.
