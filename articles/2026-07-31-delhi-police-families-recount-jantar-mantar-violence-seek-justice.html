Delhi police families recount Jantar Mantar violence, seek justice

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kin of injured Delhi Police personnel narrate harrowing accounts of the July 20 protest violence.
  • Families demand a thorough inquiry and action against those who attacked police personnel.
  • Retired ACP questions the attackers' identity, stating police were not against students.
  • Relatives appeal to Parliament to acknowledge the trauma faced by police families.

Families speak out after Jantar Mantar clash

NEW DELHI: The families of Delhi Police personnel injured during a protest near Jantar Mantar on July 20 have come forward with harrowing accounts of the violence. One relative said, "My father was almost lynched near the Jantar Mantar stage," describing the fear and chaos that unfolded during the demonstration.

The protest, linked to the Centre for Justice and Peace (CJP), turned violent, leaving several police officers with bloodied uniforms and broken helmets. Now, their families are seeking Parliament's attention, urging authorities to recognise the ordeal their loved ones endured while on duty.

'Who attacked us?' asks retired ACP

A retired Assistant Commissioner of Police (ACP), whose relative was injured, voiced a pointed question: "We are not against students, but who attacked us?" The remark underscores a growing sentiment among police families that the narrative of the protest has overlooked the injuries suffered by the personnel.

Families insist the officers were performing their lawful duties, not acting against any particular group. They argue the attackers were a separate element that exploited the protest, a claim that has not been independently verified so far.

Bloodied uniforms, broken helmets

Visuals from the scene showed officers in torn uniforms, some with visible wounds. Relatives described waiting anxiously at hospitals, unsure if their family members would recover fully. One family member said the officer returned home with a fractured hand, another with deep cuts on the head.

The injured personnel included constables and head constables, many of whom have served for over a decade. Their families say the physical injuries are matched by emotional trauma, with some officers now reluctant to discuss the incident.

Demand for accountability

The families are now appealing to Members of Parliament to take up the matter, hoping for a formal inquiry into the sequence of events. They want a clear identification of those who attacked the police, and a guarantee that such incidents will not recur.

"Behind every uniform is a family," said one relative, echoing a sentiment that has gained traction on social media. The plea is simple: acknowledge the sacrifice, investigate the violence, and ensure justice for the officers.

Delhi Police officials have not yet issued a detailed statement on the families' demands, though an internal review of the protest handling is believed to be underway.

What to watch next

All eyes are now on Parliament, where the matter may be raised in the coming session. The families have promised to keep pressing for a transparent inquiry, and further protests by police associations cannot be ruled out if their demands are not met.
