5 Semiconductor Stocks to Watch as India Launches Semicon Mission 2.0

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Cabinet approves ₹1.27 lakh crore for Semicon India Mission 2.0
  • Mission targets mobile manufacturing, new highways, and equity stakes in startups
  • PM Modi praises the approvals as a boost for electronics manufacturing
  • 5 semiconductor stocks identified as key beneficiaries

Cabinet Clears ₹1.27 Lakh Crore for Semicon Mission 2.0

India's semiconductor push got a major financial boost on Thursday. The Union Cabinet approved ₹1.27 lakh crore for the second phase of the Semicon India Mission. The fresh outlay is aimed at accelerating domestic chip production and reducing reliance on imports.

The decision was part of a broader set of approvals that also covered mobile manufacturing and new highway projects. The government has indicated it will take equity stakes in semiconductor startups under the mission. This marks a shift from earlier incentive-only models.

Mobile Manufacturing and Highways Get Simultaneous Push

The Cabinet's approvals were not limited to chips. Mobile manufacturing units will receive production-linked incentives under the same package. New highway projects have also been cleared to improve logistics for electronics clusters.

Officials say the integrated approach is deliberate. Semiconductor fabs need reliable power, water, and transport. The highway component is meant to connect proposed manufacturing zones to ports and airports.

Government to Take Equity in Chip Startups

In a notable departure from past policy, the government will now take direct equity stakes in semiconductor startups. This is designed to give the state a say in strategic decisions and ensure long-term viability of funded ventures.

The Times of India reported that the equity model will apply to both fab and fabless startups. Details on the exact stake percentage and exit timeline have not been disclosed yet. Industry bodies have broadly welcomed the move, though some caution about bureaucratic oversight.

PM Modi Hails Cabinet Approvals

Prime Minister Narendra Modi praised the Cabinet's decisions on social media. He called the semiconductor and mobile manufacturing approvals a 'quantum leap' for India's electronics ecosystem. The PM also highlighted the urea subsidy component, which was cleared alongside the tech-focused measures.

The Telegraph India quoted PM Modi as saying the approvals would 'create lakhs of jobs and reduce import dependence'. The government is betting big on semiconductors as a cornerstone of its 'Make in India' reboot.

5 Semiconductor Stocks to Watch

Equitymaster has identified five stocks that could benefit from the Semicon India Mission 2.0 rollout. These include companies involved in chip design, packaging, and assembly. The list also features firms with existing government contracts or joint ventures with global semiconductor players.

Analysts say the ₹1.27 lakh crore outlay will flow into both greenfield fabs and upscaling existing facilities. Stocks in the electronics manufacturing services (EMS) space are also expected to see momentum. Investors should watch for quarterly order books and partnership announcements.

The mission's second phase is expected to be more targeted than the first. The government has learned from delays in earlier projects and is now tying funding to milestones. This could mean faster execution for approved units.

What to watch: The exact timeline for disbursal of funds under Semicon Mission 2.0 and the first set of startups selected for equity investment. The government is expected to release detailed guidelines within 30 days.
