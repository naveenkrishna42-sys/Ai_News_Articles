Rahul Gandhi slams Sonam Wangchuk's removal from Jantar Mantar, calls it 'wrong'

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Rahul Gandhi called the removal of Sonam Wangchuk from Jantar Mantar 'wrong', accusing the Modi government of 'Asatya' (untruth) and 'Hinsa' (violence).
  • Left parties, including the CPI(M), condemned the police action against Wangchuk and backed the ongoing protest.
  • Opposition leaders described the incident as a 'blot on democracy and the Constitution'.

Congress leader attacks government over activist's eviction

Rahul Gandhi on Saturday launched a sharp attack on the Modi government, describing the removal of climate activist Sonam Wangchuk from Jantar Mantar as fundamentally unjust. The Congress leader accused the administration of operating on the core tenets of 'Asatya' and 'Hinsa' — untruth and violence.

Gandhi's remarks came hours after police evicted Wangchuk and his supporters from the historic protest site in central Delhi. The activist, known for his work in the Himalayan region, had been staging a sit-in demanding action on climate change and the rights of Ladakh's indigenous communities.

Opposition unites in condemnation

The Congress chief's criticism was echoed by several opposition parties. Left parties, including the CPI(M), condemned the use of force against the activist. CPI(M) leader M A Baby specifically denounced the police action against Wangchuk and his colleague, Dipke.

Opposition leaders described the eviction as a 'blot on democracy and the Constitution'. Multiple parties have now backed the ongoing protest, vowing to continue the agitation despite the police crackdown.

Background of the protest

Sonam Wangchuk, who gained national recognition for his work on education reform in Ladakh, had been demonstrating at Jantar Mantar for over a week. The activist was demanding that the government address long-pending issues related to the region's ecological fragility and the rights of its people under the Sixth Schedule of the Constitution.

Police officials said the removal was carried out after the protestors failed to comply with orders to vacate the site. However, opposition leaders have questioned the timing and manner of the eviction, calling it an overreach by the authorities.

Political fallout likely

The incident is expected to further escalate political tensions. With the opposition already on the offensive over a range of issues, the Wangchuk eviction has given them a fresh rallying point. Rahul Gandhi's strong language signals that the Congress will continue to press the matter.

What happens next depends on whether the government engages with the protestors' demands or continues to hold its ground. The opposition has indicated it will take the issue to Parliament.
