Bandhan Bank Enters Competitive Credit Card Market

Bandhan Bank has expanded its retail product portfolio with the launch of four new credit cards, aiming to capture a larger share of India's growing credit card market. The new lineup includes the Bandhan Visa Platinum, Bandhan Visa Signature, Bandhan American Express Gold and Bandhan American Express Platinum Reserve. Each card is tailored to distinct customer segments, from entry-level spenders to high-value premium customers.

Fee Structure and Target Segments

Entry-Level Offering

The Bandhan Visa Platinum serves as the entry point with an annual fee of Rs 499 plus applicable taxes. It targets salaried individuals making consistent but moderate monthly expenditures. The card offers standard cashback rewards and basic insurance covers, making it suitable for users seeking reliable features without premium costs.

Mid-Tier Option

Positioned at an annual fee of Rs 1,999, the Bandhan Visa Signature caters to frequent shoppers and travelers. It provides enhanced reward rates, complimentary lounge access and higher spending limits compared to the base variant.

Premium Segment

The Bandhan American Express Gold card commands an annual fee of Rs 4,999. Designed for affluent consumers, it delivers accelerated rewards on dining, entertainment and international transactions, alongside concierge services and exclusive member events.

Top-End Variant

At the highest tier, the Bandhan American Express Platinum Reserve charges Rs 9,999 annually. It targets ultra-high-net-worth individuals with benefits including unlimited global lounge access, priority boarding, hotel elite status matches and dedicated relationship managers.

Reward Rates and Cashback Benefits

Reward structures differ significantly across the four cards. The Visa Platinum offers a flat 1% cashback on all purchases, while the Visa Signature increases this to 1.5% with additional bonuses on groceries and fuel. The Amex Gold card provides up to 3% cashback on dining and entertainment, plus 5% on international purchases. The Platinum Reserve card leads with up to 5% rewards on select luxury categories, including airlines and high-end retail.

Airport Lounge Access and Travel Perks

Lounge access remains a key differentiator. The Visa Platinum does not include lounge access, whereas the Visa Signature offers six complimentary domestic lounge visits per year. Both Amex variants provide unlimited domestic and international lounge entries through Priority Pass and VISA Infinite networks. Additional travel perks include flight accident insurance, lost card liability coverage and baggage delay assistance on premium cards.
