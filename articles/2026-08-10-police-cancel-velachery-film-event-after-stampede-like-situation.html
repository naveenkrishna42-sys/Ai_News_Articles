Police cancel Velachery film event after stampede-like situation

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Event at a Velachery mall cancelled due to stampede-like crowd surge
  • Police intervened to control the situation and ensure safety
  • No injuries reported; organisers and mall management yet to comment

Crowd surge forces cancellation

Police on Sunday cancelled a film promotional event at a shopping mall in Velachery after a stampede-like situation developed. The event, which was expected to draw a large crowd, turned chaotic when visitors rushed towards the venue, prompting authorities to step in.

Officials said the situation was brought under control quickly. No injuries have been reported so far, but the decision to call off the event was taken as a precautionary measure to avoid any untoward incident.

What happened at the venue

The incident occurred in the evening when the crowd gathered for the event swelled beyond expectations. Eyewitnesses said the surge was sudden, with people pushing forward to get a better view. Security personnel and police at the scene acted swiftly to disperse the crowd.

A police official, speaking on condition of anonymity, said the organisers had not anticipated such a large turnout. "The situation could have turned serious if we had not intervened," the official said. The event was cancelled within minutes of the surge being noticed.

Police response and safety measures

Police have cordoned off the area around the mall and are monitoring the situation. Additional personnel have been deployed to manage the crowds that remained in the vicinity. The mall management has been asked to review its crowd management protocols for future events.

This is not the first time a film event in Chennai has faced crowd-related issues. In recent years, several such events have been cancelled or scaled down due to safety concerns. Experts point out that organisers often underestimate the draw of popular film stars, leading to inadequate arrangements.

Organisers yet to respond

The event organisers and the mall management have not yet issued an official statement. Police have said they will file a detailed report on the incident. Whether any action will be taken against the organisers for failing to ensure adequate crowd control remains unclear.

Meanwhile, fans who had gathered for the event expressed disappointment. Many said they had travelled long distances to attend. The police have urged the public to cooperate with authorities during such events and to follow safety guidelines.

What to watch

Police are likely to issue fresh guidelines for public events in shopping malls across the city. The incident may also prompt stricter scrutiny of permissions granted for such events, especially those involving film personalities. Further details are awaited as authorities compile their report.
