2026 Cashback Wars: Who Delivers the Most Value?

The Indian fintech landscape has evolved, with payment apps now offering cashback and rewards as a key selling point. In 2026, five apps stand out for their ability to return value to users. But which one truly delivers the best returns?

Paytm First: The Cashback Champion

Paytm First leads the pack with a 10% cashback on dining and groceries, making it the clear winner for these categories. The app's partnership with over 2 lakh merchants ensures wide applicability. However, its 1.5% annual fee may offset some rewards.

PhonePe vs. Google Pay: The Cashback Dilemma

PhonePe offers 5% cashback on UPI transactions, but its lack of merchant partnerships limits its appeal. Google Pay's 3% universal cashback is more accessible but pales in comparison. Both apps have strong UPI adoption but fail to match Paytm First's category-specific rewards.

Airtel Thanks: High Rewards, High Barriers

Airtel Thanks provides 8% cashback on travel and fuel but requires a minimum spend of ₹5,000. This exclusivity may appeal to frequent travelers but excludes casual users. The app's 2% annual fee adds another layer of complexity.

MobiKwik's Niche Appeal

MobiKwik's 7% cashback on entertainment makes it the best choice for users focused on movies, music, and gaming. However, its 1.2% annual fee and limited merchant base restrict its overall appeal.

Beyond the Numbers: What This Means for Consumers

The cashback wars of 2026 reveal several trends. First, category-specific rewards are winning over universal cashback. Second, annual fees are becoming a significant consideration. Third, merchant partnerships remain crucial for maximizing returns. Consumers who understand these dynamics can make more informed choices about their payment apps.

While Paytm First leads in overall value, other apps may be better suited for specific needs. The key takeaway is that cashback isn't just about the percentage - it's about how well the rewards align with your spending habits and the overall cost of using the app.
