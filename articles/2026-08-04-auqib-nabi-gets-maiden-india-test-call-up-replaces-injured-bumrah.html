Auqib Nabi Gets Maiden India Test Call-Up, Replaces Injured Bumrah

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Auqib Nabi replaces injured Jasprit Bumrah in India's Test squad for Sri Lanka series.
  • The pacer's journey from borrowing spikes to national call-up has caught attention.
  • Bumrah's injury has put BCCI's Centre of Excellence under scrutiny.

A Fairytale Selection

Auqib Nabi's phone rang on a quiet Tuesday morning. The voice on the other end told him he was in India's Test squad for the Sri Lanka series. He had replaced Jasprit Bumrah, who is out with an injury. For a pacer from Jammu and Kashmir, this is the stuff of dreams.

Nabi's story is not your usual cricketing rise. He has had to borrow spikes to bowl in local tournaments. Now he will share a dressing room with the likes of Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli. The Board of Control for Cricket in India confirmed the development on August 4.

From Borrowed Spikes to International Stage

Nabi's journey has been anything but smooth. In his early days, he did not have his own bowling spikes. He would borrow them from teammates, often wearing ill-fitting shoes that hurt his feet. But his pace and swing made people sit up and take notice.

He has been a consistent performer in domestic cricket. His ability to move the ball both ways and hit the deck hard earned him a spot in the selectors' radar. Now, he gets a chance to prove himself on the biggest stage of all.

Bumrah's Injury Raises Questions

Jasprit Bumrah's recurring injury issues have become a cause for concern. The pacer has been the backbone of India's bowling attack across formats. His absence from the Sri Lanka Tests has put the National Cricket Academy's Centre of Excellence under the scanner.

Questions are being raised about workload management and injury prevention. The BCCI has not yet specified the exact nature of Bumrah's injury or the expected recovery time. But the timing, just ahead of a crucial series, is not ideal.

What This Means for India's Bowling Attack

Nabi's inclusion adds a fresh dimension to India's pace attack. He is known for his accuracy and ability to bowl long spells. With Mohammed Shami and Mohammed Siraj expected to lead the attack, Nabi could provide the support act.

The management will be keen to see how he adapts to international conditions. Sri Lankan pitches may not offer much pace, but they do reward disciplined bowling. Nabi's skill set could come in handy, especially in the second and third innings.

A Dream Come True for Jammu and Kashmir Cricket

For Jammu and Kashmir cricket, Nabi's selection is a moment of pride. The region has produced talented players in the past, but few have made it to the Test squad. Nabi's story is an inspiration for young cricketers who struggle with limited resources.

Local coaches and former players have hailed the selection. They believe Nabi's work ethic and determination will help him succeed at the highest level. The team management will hope he grabs this opportunity with both hands.

As the series approaches, all eyes will be on Nabi. Can he fill the void left by Bumrah? Only time will tell. For now, the young pacer is living his dream, one borrowed spike at a time.
