Farhan Akhtar on Ranveer Singh's Don 3 exit: 'A pattern nowadays'

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Farhan Akhtar says actors leaving films is a 'pattern nowadays'
  • Comment seen as a dig at Ranveer Singh's Don 3 exit
  • Akhtar recalled Saif Ali Khan almost quitting Dil Chahta Hai
  • Don 3 casting changes have sparked industry-wide debate

Akhtar's remark on film exits

Filmmaker-actor Farhan Akhtar has weighed in on the ongoing Don 3 casting controversy, saying that actors walking out of projects has become a recurring trend in Bollywood. His comment, made during a recent interaction, was widely interpreted as a subtle jibe at Ranveer Singh, who reportedly exited the much-anticipated action franchise.

Akhtar did not name Singh directly, but his phrasing left little room for ambiguity. "Seems to be a pattern nowadays," he said, when asked about actors leaving films mid-development. The remark has since sparked a flurry of reactions on social media, with fans and industry watchers debating whether the comment was aimed squarely at the Gully Boy star.

Context of the Don 3 controversy

Ranveer Singh's departure from Don 3, which was announced with much fanfare in 2023, has been a subject of intense speculation. The film, directed by Akhtar, was expected to feature Singh in the titular role, taking over from Shah Rukh Khan. However, reports of creative differences and scheduling conflicts began surfacing earlier this year.

Neither Akhtar nor Singh has publicly detailed the reasons for the split. However, Akhtar's latest comment suggests a degree of frustration, even as he framed it as an observation about the industry's shifting dynamics. The director has previously spoken about the challenges of mounting a project of Don 3's scale, especially after the success of the previous installments.

Saif Ali Khan's near-exit from Dil Chahta Hai

In the same conversation, Akhtar recalled how Saif Ali Khan had almost walked out of his directorial debut Dil Chahta Hai in 2001. The actor, who was then transitioning from romantic leads to more unconventional roles, had reservations about his character, Sameer. Akhtar revealed that Khan was initially hesitant but eventually stayed on, delivering one of the most memorable performances of his career.

The anecdote served as a contrast to the current situation, highlighting how earlier disagreements were resolved through dialogue and trust. Akhtar's tone, however, was not entirely nostalgic. He used the example to underscore his point about the industry's changing temperament, where exits seem to happen more frequently and with less deliberation.

Industry reaction and speculation

The Don 3 casting saga has drawn reactions from several quarters. Some have defended Ranveer Singh, citing the demanding nature of the role and the pressures of multi-film commitments. Others have sided with Akhtar, arguing that professionalism should prevail over personal considerations.

Trade analysts note that high-profile exits are not new, but the public nature of such disputes has increased with social media scrutiny. The Don 3 project, which also features Kiara Advani in a leading role, remains in development, though no new lead has been announced yet. Fans of the franchise are eager to know who will step into the iconic character, a decision that Akhtar has said will be made "at the right time."

What lies ahead for Don 3

As of now, there is no official confirmation on a replacement for Ranveer Singh, nor any statement from the actor addressing Akhtar's remarks. The film's production timeline remains uncertain, with reports suggesting that the script is being revised to accommodate a new lead. Akhtar, known for his meticulous approach, is expected to announce the casting once finalised.

For now, the spotlight remains on the simmering feud, which has become one of the most discussed topics in Bollywood. Whether this 'pattern' of exits will affect the industry's long-term project planning is a question that only time will answer. Observers will be watching closely for any further developments from either camp.
