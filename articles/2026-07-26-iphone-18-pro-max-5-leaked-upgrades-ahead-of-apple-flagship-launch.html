iPhone 18 Pro Max: 5 leaked upgrades ahead of Apple flagship launch

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Leaks indicate a variable aperture camera for improved low-light photography.
  • A new cooling system is expected to address overheating concerns seen in previous models.
  • The US price could rise by up to $300, with a similar increase likely in India.
  • Reports suggest a spring delay to the iPhone 18 release could boost autumn Pro sales.

Variable Aperture Camera: A First for Apple

One of the most talked-about upgrades in the iPhone 18 Pro Max is a variable aperture camera lens. This would allow the phone to physically adjust the amount of light entering the sensor, a feature common in professional cameras but rare in smartphones.

Leaks suggest this could dramatically improve low-light photography and depth-of-field effects. If true, it would mark a significant shift from Apple's current fixed-aperture approach.

Better Cooling to Tackle Overheating

Apple is reportedly working on a new cooling system for the Pro Max model. The iPhone 17 Pro series faced criticism for overheating during heavy use, and the company appears keen to avoid a repeat.

Details are scarce, but sources indicate a vapor chamber or graphite sheet design could be used. This would help the A19 chip run at peak performance without thermal throttling.

Price Hike on the Horizon

Several reports point to a price increase for the iPhone 18 Pro Max in the US, possibly by up to $300. That would push the starting price past the $1,399 mark. In India, the price could rise by a similar proportion, potentially crossing the Rs 1.5 lakh threshold.

Apple has not commented on pricing. The increase is believed to be driven by higher component costs and the introduction of new display and camera technologies.

Foldable iPhone in the Works

The iPhone 18 series may also include Apple's first foldable device. While details remain unconfirmed, leaks suggest a clamshell design similar to Samsung's Galaxy Z Flip lineup. This would be a major departure from Apple's traditional slab design.

Industry watchers expect the foldable to launch alongside the standard iPhone 18 models. Apple's approach to durability and software optimisation will be closely watched.

Release Date and Market Strategy

Reports indicate a potential spring delay for the iPhone 18 release, which could push the launch to later in the year. This delay might be strategic, allowing Apple to boost autumn sales of the Pro models by creating a longer gap between releases.

The iPhone 18 Pro Max is still months away from an official unveiling. Apple has not confirmed any of these features. As leaks continue to surface, the company's ability to manage expectations will be tested.

What to watch for next: Official component orders and supply chain reports in the coming months will reveal whether the variable aperture and foldable designs are moving into mass production.
