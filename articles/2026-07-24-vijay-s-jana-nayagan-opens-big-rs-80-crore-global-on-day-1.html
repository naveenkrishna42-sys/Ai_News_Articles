Vijay’s Jana Nayagan Opens Big: Rs 80 Crore Global on Day 1

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jana Nayagan collects Rs 80 crore globally on day one
  • Film crosses Rs 10 crore in India by Friday morning
  • Fans queued overnight across Tamil Nadu theatres
  • Actor Ravi Mohan watched the film with fans, blew whistle during Vijay’s entry
  • Strong weekend expected as advance bookings remain high

Opening Day Numbers

Vijay’s final film Jana Nayagan has stormed the box office on its first day. The film earned an estimated Rs 80 crore globally, according to early trade reports. In India alone, the movie crossed the Rs 10 crore mark by Friday morning.

Distributors say the numbers could climb further once final night shows are tallied. The film released across more than 2,500 screens in Tamil Nadu and saw near-capacity occupancy in many centres.

Fans Queue Overnight

Theatre owners reported unprecedented turnout. Fans queued overnight outside several multiplexes and single screens in Chennai, Coimbatore, and Madurai. Many carried posters and burst crackers as the first shows began.

At a theatre in Chennai’s Tambaram area, security had to be doubled to manage the crowd. Police were deployed at several locations to ensure orderly entry. No untoward incidents were reported.

Ravi Mohan Joins Fans

Actor Ravi Mohan surprised fans by watching the film in a theatre alongside them. During Vijay’s entry scene, Ravi Mohan blew a whistle, matching the excitement of the audience. The moment was captured on video and quickly went viral on social media.

Ravi Mohan later posted on X: “Unforgettable experience. The energy in the theatre was electric. Congratulations to the entire team of Jana Nayagan.”

What the Numbers Mean

The Rs 80 crore global opening puts Jana Nayagan among the top opening days for a Tamil film this year. Trade analysts say the film’s advance booking for the weekend remains strong, especially in Tamil Nadu and Kerala.

Overseas markets, led by the US, UK, and UAE, contributed roughly Rs 30 crore of the day-one total. The film is expected to hold well through Sunday, with family audiences likely to drive matinee and evening shows.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact day-two figures. Early estimates suggest the film could cross Rs 150 crore globally by the end of the weekend.

The movie is directed by a top filmmaker and marks Vijay’s last appearance before he transitions to full-time politics. The cast includes several well-known actors from the Tamil film industry.

With no major competition this week, Jana Nayagan is set to dominate screens for at least the next 10 days. The real test will come on Monday, when weekday footfalls typically drop.
