Russian Drone Targets Vegetable Seller in Kherson, Ukraine Alleges War Crime

📅 2026-08-04 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • A video shows a Russian drone chasing a vegetable seller at a Kherson market, which Ukraine has condemned as a war crime.
  • The incident has drawn international attention, with multiple outlets reporting on the footage.
  • Separately, Russian shelling in the Kherson region killed one person and injured seven, including three medical workers.

Drone Footage Sparks Outrage

A new video has emerged showing a Russian drone chasing a street vendor at a market in Kherson, Ukraine. The footage, which has been widely shared, depicts the vendor fleeing as the drone follows him. Ukrainian officials have labelled the act a war crime.

The video, reported by multiple international outlets including Reuters and the Kyiv Independent, shows the drone pursuing the vegetable seller in what one report described as a 'human safari' attack. The vendor reportedly said, 'It started flying right at me.'

Ukraine's Accusation

Ukraine has formally accused Russia of committing a war crime in connection with the drone attack. The incident has drawn sharp condemnation from Kyiv, which has repeatedly documented what it says are Russian attacks on civilians and civilian infrastructure.

The term 'war crime' carries significant legal weight under international law, and Ukraine has been pushing for greater accountability for such actions. However, independent verification of the video's authenticity and the full circumstances around the attack has not yet been completed.

Shelling in Kherson Region

In a separate development, Russian forces shelled the Kherson region, killing one person and injuring seven others. Among the injured were three medical workers, according to local reports. The shelling adds to the ongoing violence that has plagued the region since the start of the war.

The Kherson region has been a focal point of conflict, with frequent attacks on both military and civilian targets. The latest shelling underscores the continued threat to residents and first responders in the area.

International Reaction

The drone attack video has sparked international concern, with observers pointing to the apparent targeting of a civilian as a potential violation of the rules of war. The footage has been described as 'horrific' by some outlets, highlighting the dangers faced by ordinary Ukrainians.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact date or location of the drone incident, but the reports have amplified calls for investigations into such attacks. The international community has been urged to document and pursue accountability for potential war crimes in Ukraine.

What to Watch

As investigations into the drone attack and the shelling continue, attention will likely focus on how Ukraine and its allies respond. The incidents may further fuel diplomatic efforts to hold Russia accountable, though concrete legal actions remain uncertain.

Residents of Kherson and other frontline areas continue to face daily risks, and the coming weeks will be critical in assessing the toll of the conflict on civilians.
