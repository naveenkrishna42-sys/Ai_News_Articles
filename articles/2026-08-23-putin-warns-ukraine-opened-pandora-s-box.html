Putin warns Ukraine opened 'Pandora's box'

Russian President Vladimir Putin has warned that Ukraine has opened 'Pandora's box' with strikes on Russian economic infrastructure. The warning came as Russia increased its attacks on Ukrainian enterprises in response to Ukrainian strikes on civilian facilities in Russia.

Economic retaliation

Putin said the escalation of attacks on Ukrainian enterprises was a response to the strikes on civilian facilities in Russia. He acknowledged that the strikes on Russian refineries had had an economic impact, but he warned that the situation was now more complex.

Ukraine's response

Ukraine has been increasing its strikes on Russian economic infrastructure in recent months. The strikes have targeted refineries, pipelines, and other key economic facilities. The strikes have had a significant economic impact on Russia, but they have also raised concerns about the potential for further escalation.

International reaction

International reaction to the escalation of attacks has been mixed. Some countries have condemned the strikes on civilian facilities in Russia, while others have called for a de-escalation of the conflict. The United Nations has called for a ceasefire in the conflict, but so far, the call has not been heeded.

What happens next

The escalation of attacks on Ukrainian enterprises is likely to lead to further escalation in the conflict. The strikes on Russian economic infrastructure have already had a significant economic impact on Russia, and they are likely to have a similar impact on Ukraine. The situation is now more complex, and it is unclear what the final outcome will be.
