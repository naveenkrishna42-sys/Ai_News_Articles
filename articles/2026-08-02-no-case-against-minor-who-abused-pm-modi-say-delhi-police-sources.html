No Case Against Minor Who Abused PM Modi, Say Delhi Police Sources

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Delhi Police sources say no case has been filed against the minor accused of abusing PM Modi.
  • The teenager's mother has thanked PM Modi for 'forgiving' her daughter.
  • The minor had earlier disclosed receiving rape threats, but no police probe has been initiated.

New Delhi: In a significant development, Delhi Police sources have indicated that no formal case has been registered against the minor girl accused of using abusive language against Prime Minister Narendra Modi. The clarification comes amid a politically charged atmosphere, with opposition parties accusing the ruling dispensation of hypocrisy over the handling of the incident.

No FIR, No Probe

According to sources within the Delhi Police, the complaint received regarding the alleged abusive comments made by the minor has not been converted into a First Information Report (FIR). This effectively means that no legal proceedings have been initiated against the teenager in the national capital.

The development follows a series of conflicting reports and public statements that have swirled around the case since the alleged incident came to light. Police officials have not officially confirmed the reasons for not filing a case, but sources suggest that the matter may have been resolved at a preliminary stage.

Meanwhile, the minor, a resident of Noida, had earlier disclosed to media that she had received rape threats following the controversy. However, no police investigation has been launched into those threats so far, raising concerns about the safety of the young girl.

Mother's Gratitude, Teen's Apology

The teenager's mother has publicly expressed her gratitude towards Prime Minister Modi, stating that he "gave jeevandaan" (life) by forgiving her daughter. The emotional statement comes as the family continues to face scrutiny and pressure in the wake of the incident.

The minor herself has apologised again, saying, "PM forgiving me is a very big thing." Her repeated apologies have been seen as an attempt to bring closure to the episode, which has sparked a nationwide debate on freedom of expression, the conduct of public figures, and the treatment of minors in politically sensitive cases.

Political Reactions and Accusations

The news that no case has been filed has drawn sharp reactions from opposition parties. They have pointed to what they describe as a double standard in the handling of such matters, reminding the BJP of past instances where abusive remarks against opposition leaders necessitated apologies.

Opposition leaders have questioned why the minor was initially targeted and why her alleged remarks drew such widespread attention, while similar abuses against other political figures have often been overlooked. The ruling party, however, has maintained that the Prime Minister's act of forgiveness reflects his magnanimous character.

The incident has also ignited a broader conversation about the role of social media in amplifying such controversies and the responsibilities of minors in public discourse. Legal experts have noted that under Indian law, minors are entitled to special protections, and any case involving them must be handled with sensitivity.

What Happens Next

As the dust settles, all eyes are now on whether the Delhi Police will make any formal statement regarding the non-registration of the case. The minor's family has appealed for privacy, while civil society groups have urged authorities to probe the rape threats made against her.

The coming days are likely to see continued political sparring, with opposition parties expected to keep the issue alive in Parliament and on the campaign trail. For now, the teenager's future remains uncertain, but her mother's words suggest a family eager to move past the ordeal.
