Malkajgiri railway junction may become secondary terminal for Hyderabad

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Malkajgiri railway junction is being evaluated as a secondary terminal for Hyderabad.
  • The move aims to decongest Secunderabad and Hyderabad (Nampally) stations.
  • Officials are conducting feasibility studies; no final decision has been announced.

Proposal to ease congestion at key stations

Malkajgiri railway junction, a busy stop on the city's suburban network, is being examined as a potential secondary terminal for Hyderabad. The proposal, if implemented, would help divert some train services away from the overburdened Secunderabad and Hyderabad (Nampally) stations.

Railway officials have confirmed that a feasibility study is underway, though no timeline has been given for a final decision. The move is part of a broader effort to streamline train operations in the twin cities, where passenger and goods traffic has grown steadily over the years.

Why Malkajgiri?

Malkajgiri lies on the Secunderabad–Guntakal section and already handles significant suburban traffic. Its location on the eastern side of the city makes it a natural entry point for trains arriving from Warangal, Vijayawada, and other parts of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

Officials believe that developing Malkajgiri as a secondary terminal could reduce platform pressure at Secunderabad, which currently handles a large number of long-distance and suburban trains. It could also offer passengers an alternative boarding point, potentially easing road congestion around the main stations.

Infrastructure and connectivity

The junction has adequate land for additional platforms and stabling lines, but significant upgrades would be required. These include extended platforms, improved signalling, passenger amenities, and better road access for commuters.

Connectivity to the rest of the city is another factor under review. Malkajgiri is linked to the MMTS (Multi-Modal Transport System) network, which could help passengers transfer to other parts of Hyderabad. However, last-mile connectivity from the station remains a concern that officials are yet to address.

Current status and next steps

Sources indicate that the zonal railway administration has held preliminary discussions with engineering and operating departments. A detailed project report may be prepared if the feasibility study throws up positive results.

No official statement has been issued yet, and the proposal is still at a nascent stage. The South Central Railway has not confirmed any timeline for commissioning or investment estimates.

Passenger groups have welcomed the idea, saying it could decongest not just the stations but also the surrounding road network. However, they have urged authorities to ensure that new facilities at Malkajgiri match the standards of existing terminals.

The coming months will be crucial, as railway officials are expected to complete the feasibility study and decide on the way forward. If approved, Malkajgiri could become a key hub for train services in the region, reshaping how the city manages its growing rail traffic.
