The Great Indian Festival Sale Price Dump

The Great Indian Festival Sale has transformed the Apple product landscape, delivering deep cuts across both MacBook and iPhone lines. Original retail prices were steep, but the multi-week event has driven notable markdowns that make premium computing and mobile devices far more attainable.

MacBook Pro and Air Max Discount Breakdown

MacBook Pros, once flagship powerhouses starting at ₹250,000+, now offer significant relief. The M3 generation models show particular strength in sales velocity, with base configurations dropping nearly 25% compared to pre-sale baselines. These reductions reflect Apple's strategy to clear holiday inventory and maintain market share against emerging competitors.

iPhone 15 Series Value Shock

Apple's latest iPhone lineup has experienced perhaps the most dramatic price compression of the year. Base models previously retailing near ₹80,000 are now available at a fraction of that cost, with some variants selling for less than ₹60,000 despite retaining full iOS functionality and advanced camera systems. The discount varies significantly by configuration, emphasizing Apple's tiered approach to device accessibility.
