FMCG CEOs face boardroom upheaval

India's fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) sector is witnessing a wave of leadership changes, with multiple CEOs stepping down from top companies. The Economic Times reports that HUL, GCPL, Britannia, Dabur, and Colgate are among the firms experiencing boardroom churn. This wave of resignations comes amid investor scrutiny and a broader trend of CEO exits at India Inc.

Why the leadership shake-up?

Several factors are driving the leadership changes in the FMCG sector. Investors are increasingly demanding transparency and accountability from corporate leaders. The performance of these companies has come under scrutiny, and the market is reacting to the lack of clear succession plans. Additionally, the broader trend of CEO exits at India Inc suggests that the corporate governance landscape is evolving.

Market reaction

The market's reaction to the leadership changes has been mixed. While some stocks have seen a positive response, others have experienced a decline. The Economic Times notes that the investor heat is affecting the stocks of these companies. This volatility reflects the market's sensitivity to leadership changes and the broader economic environment.
