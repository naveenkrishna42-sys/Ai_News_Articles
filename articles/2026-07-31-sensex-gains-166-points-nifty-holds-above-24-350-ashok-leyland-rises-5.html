Sensex Gains 166 Points, Nifty Holds Above 24,350; Ashok Leyland Rises 5%

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex ended 166 points higher, Nifty settled above 24,350.
  • Ashok Leyland shares jumped 5% during the session.
  • Market breadth remained positive, with advances outpacing declines.

Indian stock markets closed on a positive note on Friday, extending their recent winning streak. The BSE Sensex gained 166 points to settle at 79,724.11, while the NSE Nifty finished above the 24,350 mark, at 24,351.20. The session saw broad-based buying, though gains were led by select heavyweights and auto stocks.

Ashok Leyland Leads Auto Pack

Ashok Leyland emerged as the top gainer among large-caps, surging 5% during the day. The commercial vehicle maker's rally came on the back of strong quarterly numbers and expectations of improved demand in the truck and bus segment. The stock's upward move also lifted sentiment across the broader auto sector, with several other auto stocks closing in the green.

Market participants said the buying interest in Ashok Leyland was driven by its better-than-expected operational performance and a positive outlook for infrastructure-led demand. The company's recent order wins and focus on cost efficiency are seen as key triggers for investor confidence.

Broader Market Holds Firm

The broader market indices also ended higher, with the BSE Midcap and Smallcap indices adding nearly 0.5% each. Advancing shares outnumbered decliners on both the BSE and NSE, reflecting a healthy risk-on mood among investors. The Nifty Bank index also closed with gains, supported by steady buying in private sector lenders.

Sectorally, auto, IT, and FMCG indices were among the top performers. On the other hand, metal and realty stocks witnessed some profit-taking after recent gains. The market's resilience comes even as global cues remained mixed, with investors tracking US Federal Reserve commentary and domestic quarterly earnings.

What Drove the Gains?

Analysts pointed to sustained domestic institutional buying and easing concerns over near-term interest rates as key factors behind the rally. Foreign institutional investors have also turned net buyers in recent sessions, adding to the positive momentum. Additionally, strong GST collections and improving corporate earnings have bolstered confidence in the Indian growth story.

However, some traders remained cautious ahead of the upcoming US jobs data and its potential impact on global rate decisions. The rupee traded in a narrow range against the dollar, while crude oil prices stayed below the $80 per barrel mark, providing some comfort to importers.

Day's Movers and Laggards

Apart from Ashok Leyland, other gainers included Bajaj Auto, Mahindra & Mahindra, and Tata Motors, which rose between 1% and 3%. On the losing side, shares of HDFC Bank and Reliance Industries ended marginally lower, capping the Sensex's upside. Midcap names like Bharat Forge and Motherson Sumi also saw strong buying interest.

The Nifty volatility index eased during the session, indicating subdued fear among traders. Options data suggested a strong support base for Nifty around the 24,200 level, with immediate resistance seen at 24,500.

As the earnings season winds down, market watchers now shift focus to macroeconomic data and monsoon progress for cues. A pickup in rural demand and sustained monsoon rains could further support auto and FMCG stocks in the coming weeks.

Going ahead, investors will watch the US non-farm payroll data and the RBI's policy stance for directional cues. The market's near-term trend is likely to remain positive, but volatility may spike around key data releases.
