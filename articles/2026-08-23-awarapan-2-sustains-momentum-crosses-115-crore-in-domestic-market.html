Steady Performance at the Box Office

The Emraan Hashmi and Disha Patani starrer Awarapan 2 has maintained a firm grip on the domestic box office as it enters its second weekend. Trade analysts confirm the film has successfully crossed the 115 crore mark in India, signaling sustained audience interest despite new releases.

The film’s performance on its eighth day suggests a stable trend. While many big-budget projects see a sharp decline after their opening week, this production has managed to sustain its hold, drawing consistent numbers from both multiplexes and single-screen theatres across the country.

Global Reach and Worldwide Success

The financial success of the project is not limited to the domestic market. Reports indicate that the film has surpassed the 180 crore mark in worldwide collections. This global surge highlights the appeal of the lead pair and the narrative resonance of the project in international markets.

Market observers note that the film’s ability to draw crowds has remained unaffected by the ongoing clash with other releases like Batwara 1947. The 43.5% surge in earnings observed recently points toward positive word-of-mouth driving repeat viewings.

The box office space is currently crowded, with multiple films vying for screen space. However, Awarapan 2 appears to have carved out a loyal audience base. Industry experts often point to the balance between star power and content as the primary driver for such consistent figures.
