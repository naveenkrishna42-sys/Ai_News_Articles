Galaxy S27 could feature One UI 9.5 and Galaxy AI 2.0

Rumors suggest Samsung's upcoming Galaxy S27 series will run on One UI 9.5, the latest version of the company's custom skin for Android. This update is expected to bring new features and improvements to the user interface, enhancing the overall user experience.

Additionally, the Galaxy S27 could introduce Galaxy AI 2.0, an upgraded version of the AI assistant that debuted on the Galaxy S23 series. This new AI feature is rumored to offer more advanced capabilities, potentially integrating more seamlessly with other smartphone functions.

Samsung may redesign the Galaxy S27 camera system

Leaks indicate that Samsung is working on a major redesign of the Galaxy S27 camera system. This could include a new sensor layout, improved low-light performance, and enhanced computational photography features. The redesign is expected to address some of the criticisms from the previous models.

Samsung has filed patents hinting at a significant camera redesign, which has already sparked a debate among fans. The new design could potentially include a periscope lens for the first time in a Galaxy smartphone, offering a more compact form factor while maintaining high-quality imaging capabilities.

Leaks hint at a new Galaxy S27 Ultra design

Rumors suggest that the Galaxy S27 Ultra could feature a radically different design compared to previous models. Leaks from Sammy Fans have teased a new design that could include a more aggressive edge-to-edge display, a new camera bump configuration, and potentially a new color option.

PhoneArena has published an image that appears to show the new design, sparking speculation about the final product. The design change could be a significant departure from the Ultra's traditional look, which has been a staple of the series since its inception.

Rumors suggest Satellite and NFC features may return

Leaks also suggest that Samsung may bring back the Satellite and NFC features in the Galaxy S27 series. These features were absent in the Galaxy S23 series but were present in previous models. The return of these features could be a welcome addition for users who rely on them.

Satellite connectivity would allow users to make calls and send messages via satellite when they are in remote areas with no cellular or Wi-Fi coverage. NFC, or Near Field Communication, would enable users to make payments, share files, and connect to other devices with a simple tap.
