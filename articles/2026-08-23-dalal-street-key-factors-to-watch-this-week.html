Markets seek recovery after two-week slide

Dalal Street is set to open a new trading week on a cautious note, as investors grapple with a two-week slide in market sentiment. The SENSEX and NIFTY50 have shed nearly 1% amid high elevated oil prices and geopolitical tensions. Tata Motors PV has emerged as the top loser, adding to the concerns of the investors.

US-Iran tensions and crude prices remain key factors

The situation between the United States and Iran is expected to remain a key factor in the coming week. Any escalation or de-escalation in the tensions could have a significant impact on the stock market. Additionally, the crude oil prices are also expected to remain in focus, as they have a direct impact on the economy.

Warsh's debut at Jackson Hole could impact sentiment

Warsh's debut at the Jackson Hole Economic Symposium is likely to be a significant event for the stock market. The symposium is known for its impact on market sentiment, and Warsh's participation could provide some clarity on the economic outlook.

Q1 earnings of Upstox likely to influence stock market action

The Q1 earnings of Upstox, a popular stockbroking firm, are expected to be a key factor in the stock market action in the coming week. The earnings report could provide some insight into the performance of the stockbroking sector, which is a significant part of the stock market.

What to watch next

The coming week is likely to be a crucial one for Dalal Street, as investors await clarity on the US-Iran situation, the economic outlook, and the performance of the stockbroking sector. The market is expected to remain volatile, and investors are advised to stay cautious and well-informed.
