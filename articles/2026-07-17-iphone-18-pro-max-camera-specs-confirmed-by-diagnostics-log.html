iPhone 18 Pro Max camera specs confirmed by diagnostics log

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Diagnostics log leak confirms 48MP main camera with f/1.78 aperture for iPhone 18 Pro Max
  • 12MP periscope telephoto lens with 5x optical zoom and f/2.8 aperture included
  • Device expected to launch in September 2026 with potential price increase

Leaked log reveals camera details

An exclusive diagnostics log has confirmed the complete camera specifications for the iPhone 18 Pro Max, months before Apple's expected September launch. The log, obtained by Notebookcheck, details a triple-camera setup with significant upgrades over the current generation.

The main sensor is a 48MP unit with an f/1.78 aperture, capable of capturing more light than previous models. This sensor is paired with a 12MP ultra-wide-angle camera featuring an f/2.2 aperture and a 120-degree field of view.

Periscope telephoto and LiDAR

The telephoto lens is a 12MP periscope design with 5x optical zoom and an f/2.8 aperture. This marks a notable improvement for Apple, which has traditionally used smaller sensors in its telephoto modules. The log also confirms a LiDAR scanner for improved AR capabilities and low-light autofocus.

All three rear cameras support 4K video recording at up to 60 frames per second. The telephoto lens gains a dedicated autofocus system, a feature previously reserved for the main sensor.

Expected launch timeline

Multiple reports from India Today and MSN indicate a September 9 launch date for the iPhone 18 Pro series. The Economic Times has listed expected prices in India, suggesting a starting price of around ₹1,39,900 for the Pro Max model, a slight increase over the iPhone 17 Pro Max.

Colour options are rumoured to include Space Black, Silver, Gold, and a new Deep Purple variant. The Pro Max is also expected to feature a larger 6.9-inch OLED display with ProMotion 120Hz refresh rate.

What this means for consumers

The diagnostics log leak provides the most concrete evidence yet of Apple's camera strategy for 2026. While leaks often change before final production, the log's level of detail suggests these specifications are locked in for mass production.

Competitors like Samsung and Google have already introduced periscope zoom lenses in their flagship devices. Apple's move to a 5x optical zoom on both Pro models brings it in line with industry standards, though it still trails the 10x optical zoom found on the Galaxy S25 Ultra.

Industry analysts expect the iPhone 18 Pro Max to be a major driver of upgrades for users with iPhone 14 or older models. The camera system, combined with the new A18 Bionic chip and improved battery life, could make this one of the most significant iPhone updates in years.

Apple has not commented on the leak. The company typically refrains from discussing unannounced products.

With the launch just two months away, more leaks are likely to surface in the coming weeks. The final specifications will be confirmed only when Apple takes the stage in September.
