Mirzapur The Movie Box Office: Second Saturday Delivers Rs 13.50 Crore

Mirzapur: The Movie continues its remarkable theatrical run, netting Rs 13.50 crore on its second Saturday (day 9). The Ali Fazal, Pankaj Tripathi, and Divyenndu starrer has now crossed the Rs 200 crore mark domestically in just nine days, according to trade data. The second Saturday figure represents a solid hold for a film that opened to massive numbers, signalling strong word-of-mouth among the franchise's dedicated fanbase.

The film's performance is particularly impressive because it is a theatrical adaptation of a popular streaming series, a category that has historically struggled at the box office. Mirzapur: The Movie has defied that trend, drawing audiences back to theatres with its larger-than-life portrayal of the Kaleen Bhaiya and Guddu Pandit rivalry.

Here is a look at the film's key box office metrics as reported by trade sources:

The film's first weekend was explosive, and its second Saturday hold indicates that it is not merely a front-loaded affair. The drop from the first Saturday to the second Saturday is significant, but the absolute numbers remain healthy for a film of this scale.

Why Mirzapur: The Movie Is Working

Several factors contribute to the film's success. The franchise has a massive built-in audience from three seasons of the web series. The theatrical version offers a spectacle that streaming cannot match—larger-than-life action sequences, a booming background score, and the collective energy of a packed cinema hall. The performances of Pankaj Tripathi as Kaleen Bhaiya and Ali Fazal as Guddu Pandit have been widely praised, with Divyenndu's return as Munna Bhaiya adding nostalgic value.

Additionally, the film's release timing—with no major Bollywood competition—has allowed it to dominate screens. The second weekend saw a slight dip due to new releases, but the core audience remains loyal. Multiplex chains report strong occupancy in mass-market centres, particularly in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, and Maharashtra.

Box Office Comparison and Real-World Implications

To put the Rs 200 crore figure in context, only a handful of Hindi films have crossed this milestone in 2026. Mirzapur: The Movie is now among the top three highest-grossing Hindi releases of the year. Its success has significant implications for the industry: it validates the crossover potential of OTT-origin IPs, potentially paving the way for more series-to-screen adaptations.

For exhibitors, the film has been a saviour in an otherwise lean September. Single-screen theatres in tier-2 and tier-3 cities are reporting housefull shows, a rarity for a non-festival release. The film's performance also boosts the ancillary revenue from merchandise and music rights, with the title track becoming a chartbuster.

Key Insights: Pros and Cons

  • Pro: Strong franchise recall ensures a built-in audience, reducing marketing spend.
  • Pro: The film's Rs 200 crore domestic haul in nine days places it among the year's biggest hits.
  • Pro: Pankaj Tripathi and Ali Fazal's performances have generated positive word-of-mouth.
  • Con: The second Saturday drop suggests that the film may not sustain momentum beyond the second week.
  • Con: Competition from new releases in the coming weeks could limit its run to Rs 250 crore.

What Lies Ahead

With Rs 200 crore already in the bank, Mirzapur: The Movie is well-positioned to cross Rs 250 crore domestically. The second Sunday and the weekdays will be crucial. If the film maintains a steady hold, it could challenge the lifetime totals of some of 2026's biggest hits. The overseas performance, particularly in the UAE and US, has also been strong, adding to the overall tally. For now, the film stands as a testament to the power of well-executed franchise storytelling.
