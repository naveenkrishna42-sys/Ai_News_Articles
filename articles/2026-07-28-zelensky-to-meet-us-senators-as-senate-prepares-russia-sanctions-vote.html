Zelensky To Meet US Senators As Senate Prepares Russia Sanctions Vote

📅 2026-07-28 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky will meet US senators in Washington.
  • The US Senate is expected to vote on new sanctions against Russia.
  • The visit coincides with the funeral of Senator Lindsey Graham.
  • Zelensky is also scheduled to meet former President Donald Trump.
  • The meetings come amid ongoing conflicts in Ukraine and the Middle East.

High-Stakes Washington Visit

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky is set to meet with US senators in Washington today, as the Senate prepares for a crucial vote on new sanctions against Russia. The visit comes at a tense moment, with the war in Ukraine grinding on and US foreign policy facing fresh scrutiny.

Zelensky's schedule includes a stop at the White House, where he is expected to meet former President Donald Trump. The meeting follows talks between Trump, Zelensky, and Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu at the funeral of Senator Lindsey Graham.

A Vote on Russia Sanctions

The Senate vote on new Russia sanctions is expected to be a key focus of Zelensky's visit. The proposed measures aim to tighten the economic noose on Moscow, targeting key sectors of the Russian economy. Lawmakers have been debating the scope and timing of the sanctions for weeks.

Zelensky’s presence on Capitol Hill is a clear signal of Ukraine's urgency. He is expected to press senators to pass the strongest possible package, arguing that Russia's aggression shows no signs of abating.

Graham's Funeral and Political Crossroads

The diplomatic choreography is unfolding against the backdrop of Senator Lindsey Graham's funeral. The late senator was a key figure in US foreign policy, known for his hawkish stance on Russia and support for Ukraine. His passing has reshaped the political landscape in Washington.

Reports indicate that Trump joined Zelensky and Netanyahu at the funeral after holding separate talks. The Guardian described the scene as one of "blurred front lines," noting that the wars in Ukraine and Iran are colliding in the corridors of US power. The BBC characterized the dynamic as Zelensky and Netanyahu having to navigate the "whims of Trump's darkly confounding White House."

Uncertainty Over US Policy

The meetings highlight the uncertainty surrounding US policy toward both conflicts. Trump's potential return to the White House has raised questions about continued support for Ukraine, while the situation in the Middle East remains volatile. Zelensky's visit is an attempt to secure concrete commitments from Washington.

Details of the closed-door discussions have not been released. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact nature of the sanctions vote or whether a final deal has been reached. What is clear is that Zelensky is using every available channel to keep Ukraine's cause at the center of US political attention.

What to Watch

The Senate is expected to hold the sanctions vote later this week. All eyes will be on whether the bill passes with broad bipartisan support, and what message that sends to Moscow. Zelensky will continue his Washington meetings before returning to Kyiv.
