Hyderabad IT Corridor Flyovers Set for May 2027 Completion

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Flyovers at busy junctions in Hyderabad's IT corridor have a new completion target of May 2027.
  • The project aims to ease chronic traffic congestion in the area, which houses major tech firms.
  • Officials are working to expedite construction to meet the revised deadline.

Revised Deadline for IT Corridor Flyovers

The Hyderabad traffic police and the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) have set a fresh target of May 2027 for completing flyovers at several congested junctions along the city's IT corridor. The announcement comes after repeated delays in the project, which is critical for thousands of daily commuters.

The flyovers are planned at key intersections such as Hi-Tech City, Madhapur, and Kondapur. These areas witness severe gridlock during peak hours, impacting employees of major tech firms and residents alike.

Why the IT Corridor Needs These Flyovers

The IT corridor, stretching from Jubilee Hills to Gachibowli, is the nerve centre of Hyderabad's technology industry. With the rapid expansion of office spaces and residential complexes, traffic volumes have surged beyond the capacity of existing roads.

Officials believe the new flyovers will significantly cut travel time by eliminating the need for vehicles to stop at traffic signals at these busy junctions. The project was originally conceived years ago but faced hurdles related to land acquisition and utility shifting.

Progress and Challenges

Construction work is currently underway at multiple sites. Authorities have assured that the pace has been ramped up to meet the May 2027 deadline. However, commuters have expressed scepticism given the history of delays.

One of the main challenges has been the coordination with underground utility networks, including water pipelines and optical fibre cables. GHMC officials stated that most of these issues have now been resolved, allowing for smoother progress.

The total cost of the flyover project has not been officially confirmed in recent updates, but earlier estimates placed it in the range of several hundred crores.

Impact on Daily Commute

For tech employees working in the area, the flyovers cannot come soon enough. A typical commute that should take 20 minutes often stretches to over an hour during rush hour. The new infrastructure is expected to improve travel times by at least 30-40%.

Residents in nearby localities have also welcomed the move, though some have raised concerns about construction-related dust and noise. The authorities have promised to minimise disruptions and adhere to safety standards.

What Happens Next

With the May 2027 deadline now set, all eyes will be on the construction agencies to deliver on time. Regular reviews are planned to track progress and address any emerging bottlenecks.

Commuters can expect intermittent traffic diversions and slow movement in the affected zones until the flyovers are operational. The completion of this project will be a key milestone in Hyderabad's efforts to modernise its urban transport infrastructure.
