Zeeshan Ayyub denies role in Ajay Devgn's 'Chauhaan', says voice misused

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mohd Zeeshan Ayyub has denied any formal involvement in Ajay Devgn's film 'Chauhaan'.
  • The actor claims his voice was used in the teaser without his full consent or reference.
  • He says he only dubbed a single line without being given the full context of the project.

Actor Mohammed Zeeshan Ayyub has publicly distanced himself from the upcoming Ajay Devgn-starrer Chauhaan, alleging that his voice was used in the film's teaser without proper authorisation.

In a statement that has quickly gained traction online, Ayyub said he only recorded a single line for the teaser and was never formally attached to the project. The actor claimed he was not given any reference material or context about the film when he dubbed that line.

What the actor said

Ayyub took to social media to clarify his position, stating that he had "strong objections" to the use of his voice in the teaser. He emphasised that his contribution was limited to one line and that he had no knowledge of the larger project or its narrative.

"I dubbed a line without any reference," Ayyub wrote. He added that the teaser was released without his consent, and he has since demanded that the audio be removed. The actor has not yet specified whether he will take legal action.

The controversy around 'Chauhaan'

The teaser for Chauhaan, starring Ajay Devgn, was released recently and immediately sparked debate over its title and content. While the film has not yet been officially announced by the makers, the teaser created buzz for its aggressive tone and historical themes.

Ayyub's involvement was initially seen as an endorsement of the project. However, his latest statement suggests otherwise. The actor, known for his roles in films like Raees and Article 15, has often spoken about his political views and has been vocal on social issues.

Makers yet to respond

As of now, the production team behind Chauhaan has not issued a response to Ayyub's claims. Ajay Devgn has also remained silent on the matter. It is unclear if the teaser will be re-edited to remove Ayyub's voice, as he has demanded.

Several media outlets, including The Times of India and The Indian Express, have reported on the controversy. NDTV noted that Ayyub alleged his voice was "misused" in the teaser. Bollywood Bubble quoted the actor as saying he had "strong objection" to the way his voice was used.

This incident adds to a growing trend of actors distancing themselves from projects after their involvement is made public, often citing creative differences or lack of transparency.

What happens next depends on how the makers of Chauhaan respond. If they accede to Ayyub's demand, the teaser may be pulled or re-edited. If not, the dispute could escalate into a legal battle — something Bollywood has seen more of in recent years.
