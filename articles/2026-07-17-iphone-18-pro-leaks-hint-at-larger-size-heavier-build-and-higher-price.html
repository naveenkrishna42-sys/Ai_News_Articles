iPhone 18 Pro leaks hint at larger size, heavier build, and higher price

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iPhone 18 Pro expected to be larger and heavier than iPhone 17 Pro.
  • September 9 launch date tipped for the series.
  • India prices could see an increase of up to ₹20,000.
  • New addition boosts the iPhone 18 Pro's chances of success.

Leaks and rumors surrounding Apple's next flagship, the iPhone 18 Pro, are pointing toward a device that is physically bigger, heavier, and pricier than its predecessor. Reports from multiple sources, including Creative Bloq and Mashable, indicate that Apple is making significant design and hardware changes for the 2026 lineup.

Larger chassis, added weight

The iPhone 18 Pro is expected to sport a larger display and a thicker chassis, contributing to a noticeable increase in weight. While exact dimensions remain unconfirmed, early leaks suggest the phone will be heavier than the iPhone 17 Pro, which itself was already a substantial device. This could be attributed to a larger battery, upgraded camera hardware, or a more robust build using new materials.

Industry watchers point out that a heavier iPhone may not appeal to all users, but Apple appears to be prioritizing added functionality and durability over slimness. The design shift marks a departure from the trend toward ever-thinner smartphones.

September launch and India pricing

Multiple reports peg September 9 as the likely launch date for the iPhone 18 Pro series. This follows Apple's typical launch timeline for its premium models. For Indian buyers, however, the news comes with a sting. Sources, including MSN and Mumbai Mirror, indicate that the iPhone 18 Pro could cost up to ₹20,000 more than the current generation. This would push the starting price well past the ₹1.5 lakh mark for the base variant.

The price hike is linked to rising component costs and a weaker rupee. Apple has not officially commented on pricing, but analysts expect the increase to be across the board for the Pro and Pro Max models.

A new feature tipped to boost appeal

Despite the higher cost and heavier build, analysts believe a new addition to the iPhone 18 Pro could drive strong sales. PhoneArena reports that an unspecified feature—possibly related to camera capabilities or AI integration—is being added to increase the phone's chances of commercial success. Details remain scarce, but the feature is said to be exclusive to the Pro models.

Past iterations have seen Apple introduce major camera upgrades or display innovations in Pro models to justify a higher price. The iPhone 18 Pro is expected to continue that strategy.

What to watch

All eyes will be on Apple's official announcement expected in early September. If the leaks hold true, the iPhone 18 Pro will be a heavier, costlier device that banks on a single standout feature to win over buyers. Whether that trade-off pays off will depend on how much value Indian consumers place on the new addition.
