Sai Sudharsan out of Sri Lanka Tests; Sarfaraz Khan named replacement

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sai Sudharsan ruled out of Sri Lanka Tests due to injury
  • Sarfaraz Khan named as his replacement in the squad
  • BCCI and COE cite fitness concerns as reason for the change

Sudharsan's Setback

In a significant blow to India's Test plans, top-order batter Sai Sudharsan has been ruled out of the upcoming series against Sri Lanka. The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) confirmed the development on Monday, citing an injury that has forced the young opener out of the squad.

The selectors have moved quickly to name Sarfaraz Khan as Sudharsan's replacement. Khan, who has been a consistent run-scorer in domestic cricket, will now join the squad for the Test series. The BCCI's statement did not specify the exact nature of Sudharsan's injury but indicated that the decision was made following assessments by the Centre of Excellence (COE) medical team.

Fitness Concerns and the COE Call

The BCCI's decision to replace Sudharsan comes amid growing fitness concerns around the player. Reports suggest that the COE played a cautious role in the assessment, preferring to rest the batter rather than risk a more serious setback. Sudharsan had been training at the National Cricket Academy in Bengaluru ahead of the series.

The Telegraph India reported that the COE's approach was conservative, with the medical staff prioritising long-term fitness over short-term availability. This decision underscores the board's increasing emphasis on player workload management, especially for young cricketers with limited Test experience.

Sarfaraz Khan's Return

Sarfaraz Khan's selection marks a notable return to the national fold. The Mumbai batter, who has been in prolific form in domestic cricket, had been overlooked in recent selections. His first reaction to the India call-up was one of excitement and gratitude, according to The Times of India.

Khan's inclusion adds depth to India's batting line-up, though it remains to be seen whether he will slot directly into the playing XI. With Sudharsan's absence, the top order will likely see a reshuffle, with options like KL Rahul and Shubman Gill expected to take on more responsibility.

Squad Dynamics Ahead of the Series

The Sri Lanka Tests were already shaping up to be a challenging assignment, with India looking to fine-tune their red-ball game. Sudharsan's injury has now disrupted those plans, forcing the think tank to rework their combinations. The series holds significance as India continue to build towards future overseas tours, and the management will want to give the replacement ample time to acclimatise.

Notably, the ICC's original report on the matter highlighted Sudharsan's absence as a key talking point, while Cricbuzz and other outlets confirmed the replacement. The BCCI has not yet announced whether any additional players will be added to the squad as cover.

What to Watch

All eyes will now be on Sarfaraz Khan's integration into the Test squad and whether he gets a game in the series opener. The team management will also monitor Sudharsan's recovery timeline, as his availability for future assignments remains a concern. The first Test is expected to begin later this month, and India will hope to put this disruption behind them swiftly.
