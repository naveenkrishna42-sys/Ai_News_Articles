Welcome To The Jungle Box Office Day 23: Akshay Kumar Film Picks Up on Weekend

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • The film earned around ₹3.75 crore on its fourth Saturday.
  • Total domestic collection now stands at approximately ₹135 crore.
  • Growth attributed to family audience and holiday weekend effect.

Weekend Push for Akshay Kumar's Comedy

Akshay Kumar's 'Welcome To The Jungle' witnessed a noticeable uptick at the box office on its fourth Saturday. The film, which entered its 23rd day in theatres, collected an estimated ₹3.75 crore across India.

This marks a growth of nearly 40% compared to its Friday earnings of ₹2.70 crore. The jump is typical for a family entertainer on a weekend, especially one running in its extended phase.

Competition from New Releases

The film has been facing tough competition from recent big-ticket releases, including the much-anticipated 'T'. Despite this, 'Welcome To The Jungle' managed to hold its ground at multiplexes in metropolitan cities.

Trade analysts point to the holiday weekend as a key factor. Many families opted for a light-hearted comedy over darker or more serious new offerings.

Total Collection Crosses ₹135 Crore

With the day 23 earnings, the film's total domestic net collection now stands at approximately ₹135 crore. This puts it in a comfortable position, though it remains to be seen if it can sustain momentum through the week.

The film had a strong opening, earning over ₹30 crore in its first weekend, but saw a sharp drop on weekdays. The current trend suggests a lifetime collection in the ₹145-150 crore range, barring a major mid-week slump.

What's Working for the Film

Industry insiders say the film's ensemble cast—featuring Akshay Kumar alongside an array of comic actors—continues to draw audiences. Positive word-of-mouth in family circuits has helped it stay afloat.

Single-screen cinemas in smaller towns and cities have also contributed significantly. These venues traditionally support comedy franchises, and 'Welcome To The Jungle' is no exception.

The Road Ahead

With the fourth weekend nearly over, the film will now face another week of competition from holdover titles and possibly a new release next Friday. Its weekday performance will be critical in determining if it can cross the ₹150 crore mark.

Producers are banking on the upcoming festive period to give it a final boost. Whether the film can sustain or will fade in its fifth week remains to be seen.
