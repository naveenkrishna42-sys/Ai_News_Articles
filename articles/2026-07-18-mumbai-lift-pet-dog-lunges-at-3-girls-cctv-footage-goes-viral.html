Mumbai lift: pet dog lunges at 3 girls, CCTV footage goes viral

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Three girls were trapped in a lift with an aggressive pet dog in a Mumbai building.
  • CCTV footage shows the dog lunging at the girls, causing panic.
  • The video has gone viral, raising questions about pet safety in shared spaces.
  • Authorities have not yet confirmed if any action has been taken against the dog owner.

Panic in the lift

A routine elevator ride turned into a terrifying 60-second ordeal for three young girls in a Mumbai residential building. CCTV footage that has since gone viral shows a pet dog, reportedly muzzled, suddenly turning aggressive and lunging at the girls inside the confined space.

The video captures the girls huddling in a corner of the lift as the dog repeatedly jumps at them. The animal’s owner is seen struggling to restrain it, but the dog continues to lunge, causing visible panic among the children. The lift doors eventually open, and the girls are seen rushing out.

Viral video sparks debate

The incident, recorded on the building’s security camera, was widely shared on social media platforms. Netizens expressed outrage and concern over the safety of residents, especially children, in shared spaces like elevators. Many called for stricter enforcement of pet ownership rules in apartment complexes.

Animal behaviour experts have pointed out that even a muzzled dog can be a threat if it feels threatened or cornered. The video has reignited discussions about responsible pet ownership and the need for clear guidelines for using common areas.

Safety concerns in residential buildings

This is not an isolated incident. There have been several reports of pet-related confrontations in high-rise buildings across Indian cities. Residents have often complained about dogs being allowed off-leash in corridors, stairwells, and lifts, creating anxiety among those who are uncomfortable around animals.

Building associations in Mumbai have previously attempted to draft rules on pet movement, but enforcement remains inconsistent. The Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) has not yet issued a statement on this specific incident. Local police have also not confirmed whether a complaint has been filed.

What the footage shows

The CCTV clip, lasting about a minute, begins with the three girls entering the lift. Moments later, the dog owner steps in with the animal. Almost immediately, the dog begins to growl and lunge. The girls retreat to the back of the lift, screaming. The owner pulls on the leash, but the dog continues its aggressive behaviour. When the doors open, the girls flee.

The owner is seen leaving the lift with the dog shortly after. It is unclear from the video if any injuries occurred. No official report of injury has been released.

What happens next

The building’s management is expected to review the footage and decide on any action against the dog owner. Resident associations may use this incident to push for stricter rules on pet access in lifts and common areas. Authorities have not yet confirmed if a formal investigation has been launched.
