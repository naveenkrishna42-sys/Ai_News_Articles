Matthew VanDyke, a U.S. national, is facing a critical hearing on September 8 as he awaits trial on terror-related charges in India. Background of the case

VanDyke, a former U.S. Army Ranger, was arrested in 2023 on charges of providing material support to terrorists and illegal arms trafficking. He has been held in Tihar Jail, New Delhi, since his arrest. His family claims he has been subjected to harsh interrogation and poor medical care, leading to his health worsening.

Family's allegations and demands

The VanDyke family has accused Indian authorities of withholding medical treatment and providing inadequate care to their relative. They have demanded immediate medical intervention and a halt to any further interrogation until VanDyke can be properly treated. The family has also called for a diplomatic meeting with the U.S. government to address their concerns.
