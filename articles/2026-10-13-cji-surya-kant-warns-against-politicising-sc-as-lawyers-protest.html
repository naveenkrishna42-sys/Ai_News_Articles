CJI Surya Kant Addresses Protests by Legal Community

Chief Justice of India (CJI) Surya Kant on Monday acknowledged the right of lawyers to stage peaceful protests but firmly cautioned against involving the Supreme Court in political battles. His remarks surfaced during a period of heightened tension between the legal fraternity and the government over alleged interference in judicial appointments and the functioning of the Collegium system.

Background of the Protests

The protests were initiated by several advocates and bar associations in response to perceived attempts to influence the judiciary’s independence. The demonstrators have been voicing their concerns over delays in judicial reforms and the handling of pending cases related to constitutional appointments. Speaking to the media outside the Supreme Court premises, CJI Surya Kant stated, “Every lawyer has the right to be part of peaceful protests. However, the Supreme Court must not be dragged into political battlefields.”

Balancing Democracy and Institutional Integrity

While affirming the importance of democratic expression, CJI Surya Kant emphasized that the judiciary, particularly the apex court, should remain insulated from partisan politics. He reiterated that although protests are essential in a democracy, they must not undermine public confidence in institutions. “We welcome dissent, but it must not erode the dignity and impartiality of the courts,” he said.

Supreme Court’s Stance on Administrative Powers

In response to criticism from certain political quarters, including the Congress party, CJI Surya Kant clarified that the Supreme Court does not possess administrative powers beyond those granted under the Constitution. This statement came after opposition leaders accused the judiciary of overstepping its mandate. The CJI further noted that the court respects all constitutional frameworks and operates within clearly defined boundaries.

Reactions from the Bar and Political Circles

Legal experts and senior counsel have lauded CJI Surya Kant’s balanced approach, calling it a much-needed corrective in times of growing polarisation. Meanwhile, some political leaders have expressed dissatisfaction with his comments, alleging bias. However, the Bar Council of India issued a statement supporting the CJI’s appeal for restraint and responsible protest conduct.

  • Right to Protest: CJI confirmed that peaceful protests by lawyers are legally protected and constitutionally valid.
  • Institutional Neutrality: He reiterated that the Supreme Court cannot and will not become a platform for political agendas.
  • Constitutional Limits: The CJI clarified that the SC has no administrative powers beyond what the Constitution prescribes.
  • Call for Unity: Urged both the legal community and political actors to maintain decorum and uphold democratic values.
