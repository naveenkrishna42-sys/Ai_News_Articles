Acumatica opens GCC in Hyderabad, plans to ramp up hiring

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Acumatica has opened a global capability centre (GCC) in Hyderabad.
  • The company plans to ramp up hiring at the new centre.
  • The move underscores Hyderabad's growing appeal for tech investments.

New GCC marks company's expansion in India

Acumatica, the US-based cloud enterprise resource planning (ERP) software provider, has opened a global capability centre (GCC) in Hyderabad. The company said it will scale up recruitment at the facility as part of its broader growth strategy in India.

The new centre is expected to support Acumatica's product development, engineering, and client services operations. While the company did not disclose specific hiring numbers, officials indicated that the Hyderabad team will grow significantly over the coming months.

Hyderabad's tech ecosystem attracts another player

The city has emerged as a preferred destination for global capability centres, with several multinational firms setting up operations in recent years. Acumatica's decision adds to this momentum, leveraging the local talent pool in cloud computing and enterprise software.

Industry observers note that Hyderabad's robust infrastructure and skilled workforce continue to draw investments from technology companies across the world. The state government has also been proactive in offering incentives to such firms.

What the centre will focus on

Acumatica's GCC will primarily handle product innovation, cloud engineering, and customer support. The company aims to strengthen its delivery capabilities and accelerate its product roadmap from the Hyderabad base.

“This centre will play a key role in our global operations,” a company spokesperson said, without elaborating on the timeline for full-scale operations. The facility is already operational, and hiring is underway for various roles.

Broader context of GCC growth in India

The GCC landscape in India has expanded rapidly, with over 1,700 centres employing more than 1.9 million professionals as of recent estimates. Hyderabad accounts for a significant share of this growth, particularly in sectors like IT, BFSI, and healthcare technology.

Acumatica's entry is expected to create new job opportunities for engineers, developers, and support staff in the region. The company will compete with other cloud ERP providers for top talent, which could drive up salary offers in the segment.

Looking ahead

As Acumatica ramps up its Hyderabad operations, the coming quarters will reveal the pace of hiring and the scale of projects it takes on. The company's investment signals confidence in India's tech capabilities, and the GCC is likely to become a critical node in its global delivery network.

Market watchers will be keen to see how the centre evolves and whether it attracts additional investments from the parent firm.
