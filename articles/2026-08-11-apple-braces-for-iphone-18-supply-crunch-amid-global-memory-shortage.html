Apple braces for iPhone 18 supply crunch amid global memory shortage

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Global memory shortage threatens iPhone 18 production and availability.
  • Component costs for the iPhone 18 Pro could rise nearly 40%, pressuring margins.
  • Apple is reportedly gearing up for a September launch despite supply constraints.

Apple is preparing for a turbulent launch season. The company is bracing for potential iPhone 18 shortages, driven by a widening global memory shortage that has begun to squeeze component supplies across the tech industry. The constraints come at a critical time, with the next-generation device expected to debut in September.

Memory crunch tightens its grip

The global memory market has been under strain for months, with demand for DRAM and NAND chips outstripping supply. This has pushed prices upward and forced manufacturers to prioritise allocations. For Apple, which relies on high-performance memory chips for its flagship iPhones, the ripple effects are becoming impossible to ignore.

Industry analysts tracking the supply chain suggest that the shortage could limit how many iPhone 18 units Apple can produce in the initial months after launch. While the company has historically managed supply chain disruptions well, the scale of this memory deficit is unprecedented in recent years.

Costs climb, margins under pressure

Adding to the complexity, a recent report from TrendForce indicates that the bill of materials (BOM) cost for the iPhone 18 Pro is expected to surge by nearly 40% compared to its predecessor. This sharp increase is largely attributed to the inflated prices of memory components, alongside other premium parts.

For Apple, this creates a delicate balancing act. The company must decide whether to absorb the higher costs, sacrificing margins to sustain shipment volumes, or pass some of the burden to consumers. Historical patterns suggest Apple is likely to prioritise market share and ecosystem growth, but the scale of the cost hike may force tough decisions behind closed doors.

Demand remains robust

Despite the supply-side headaches, consumer interest in the iPhone 18 lineup appears strong. Reports from Forbes indicate that demand for the iPhone 18 Pro is driving an accelerated September launch timeline. Apple's retail teams are already preparing for the release, with a Bloomberg report noting that the company has initiated an internal lottery system for retail employees to secure launch-day units—a sign of high anticipation.

The combination of robust demand and constrained supply could lead to extended shipping times and limited availability at retail stores, a scenario that Apple has navigated before with mixed results.

What this means for buyers

For prospective buyers, the shortage could translate into longer wait times, especially for the Pro models, which are typically the most popular. Some analysts also speculate that Apple may adjust pricing tiers, though no official announcements have been made. The company has not yet confirmed any changes to its launch schedule or pricing strategy.

Meanwhile, those considering the iPhone 17 may face a different dilemma. A separate report from Gadgets 360 suggests a surprise price hike could be on the horizon for the current generation, potentially pushing some consumers to wait for the newer models—or vice versa.

The bigger picture

The memory shortage is not an Apple-specific problem. Across the industry, smartphone makers, PC manufacturers, and data centre operators are competing for the same limited supply. This has led to a broader recalibration of production targets and pricing strategies, with smaller players feeling the pinch most acutely.

Apple's scale and purchasing power give it an advantage, but not immunity. The company is likely to leverage long-term contracts and strategic stockpiles to mitigate the impact, yet the constraints are expected to persist well into the next year.

As September approaches, all eyes will be on Apple's official announcement. The company is expected to reveal the iPhone 18 lineup alongside its usual slate of software updates. Whether the devices will be available in sufficient quantities remains an open question.

The coming weeks will clarify how Apple plans to navigate this supply squeeze. Watch for pre-order dates, regional availability, and any commentary from executives during the launch event. For now, the message is clear: the iPhone 18 is coming, but getting one might require patience.
