Sebi tightens rules for online bond platforms

Sebi proposes stricter ad rules for online bond platforms

Sebi has proposed new advertising rules for online bond platforms, aiming to curb misleading claims and protect retail investors. The regulatory body has sought to ban 'fear of missing out' (FOMO) and 'fixed returns' claims in ads, and requires platforms to disclose risks more clearly.

Key provisions of the new rules

The new rules propose that online bond platforms must clearly disclose the risks associated with bond investments. This includes the possibility of capital loss, interest rate risks, and credit risks. The rules also ban the use of FOMO tactics, such as limited-time offers and urgency-driven language, in ads.

Additionally, the rules seek to curb the use of 'fixed returns' claims in ads. Platforms will be required to clearly state that bond returns are not guaranteed and can vary based on market conditions.

Regulatory timeline

Sebi has proposed these rules as part of its broader efforts to regulate the online bond market. The regulatory body has stated that it aims to finalise the rules by the end of Q3 2026. Once finalised, the rules will be applicable to all online bond platforms operating in India.

Impact on the market

The new rules are expected to have a significant impact on the online bond market. Platforms will need to revise their advertising strategies to comply with the new rules. This could lead to a more transparent and informed bond investment market.

However, the rules could also pose challenges for platforms, especially those that have been relying on aggressive marketing tactics to attract investors. The new rules could potentially limit the growth of some platforms, while benefiting others that focus on transparent and informed investing.

What happens next?

The next step is for Sebi to finalise the rules and issue a public consultation paper. This will allow stakeholders, including online bond platforms, retail investors, and market experts, to provide feedback on the proposed rules. Based on the feedback, Sebi will finalise the rules, which are expected to be notified by the end of Q3 2026.
