How to Apply for a Credit Card

To apply for a credit card, visit the bank's website and fill out the online application form. You will need to provide personal and financial information, as well as proof of identity and address. Once your application is approved, you will receive your credit card in the mail.

Many credit cards offer welcome rewards or joining cashback bonuses. To claim these rewards, make your first purchase within the specified time frame. For example, the HDFC Bank Millennia Credit Card offers a 10% cashback on your first online purchase.

Final Recommendation

Online shoppers in India can benefit from the cashback and rewards offered by these credit cards. The Amazon Pay ICICI Bank Credit Card and the SBI Card PRIME are good options for those who shop primarily on Amazon. The HDFC Bank Millennia Credit Card, Axis Bank Select Credit Card, ICICI Bank Credit Card, and Kotak Bank Kotak Credit Card are good options for those who shop on multiple platforms.
