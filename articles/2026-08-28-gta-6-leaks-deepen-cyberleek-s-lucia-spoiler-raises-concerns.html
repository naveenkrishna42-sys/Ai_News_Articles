GTA 6 Leaks Deepen with Cyberleek's Lucia Prologue

Ethical Concerns and Industry Reaction

The release of the Lucia prologue has raised significant ethical concerns within the game industry. The gaming industry has also been quick to respond to the leaks, with some companies taking legal action against the hackers.
