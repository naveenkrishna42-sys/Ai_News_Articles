Narcotics Worth Rs 28 Crore Seized, 7 Interstate Peddlers Held in Bengaluru

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Narcotics valued at Rs 28 crore were seized in Bengaluru.
  • Seven individuals involved in interstate drug trafficking were arrested.
  • The operation targeted cross-border networks supplying synthetic drugs.
  • Police are investigating the supply chain and possible links to other cities.

Bengaluru police have arrested seven people suspected of being part of an interstate drug trafficking network and seized narcotics valued at around Rs 28 crore. The operation, carried out by the city's crime branch, dealt a significant blow to cross-border drug supply chains operating in the region.

The arrests were made following weeks of surveillance and intelligence gathering. Officials said the accused were transporting a large consignment of synthetic drugs, which were intercepted before they could hit the local market.

Details of the Seizure

The drugs recovered include high-grade MDMA, methamphetamine, and other synthetic substances. Police said the street value of the haul is estimated at Rs 28 crore, making it one of the larger drug busts in Bengaluru this year.

The seven accused are believed to be part of a well-organised network that sourced narcotics from outside the state and distributed them in Bengaluru's party circuits and college hubs. Investigators are now trying to trace the suppliers and identify other members of the syndicate.

Interstate Links Under Scrutiny

Preliminary investigations suggest the drugs were brought in from other states, possibly from the Northeast and Maharashtra. The accused have been booked under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act.

“This is a major breakthrough in our fight against interstate drug trafficking. We are interrogating the accused to understand the full extent of the network,” a senior police officer said, requesting anonymity.

The arrests come amid heightened concern over the rising use of synthetic drugs among young people in Bengaluru. The city has seen several high-profile drug busts in recent months.

What’s Next

Police are expected to conduct further raids based on leads from the interrogation. They are also examining the financial transactions of the accused to identify possible money laundering angles.

The accused will be produced in court and are likely to be remanded in police custody for further questioning.
