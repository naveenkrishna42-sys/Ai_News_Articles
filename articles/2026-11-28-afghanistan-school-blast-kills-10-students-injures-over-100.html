Afghanistan School Blast Kills 10 Students, Injures Over 100

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Desk 📅 2026-11-28 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • A tragic explosion in Afghanistan's Kunar province claims the lives of at least 10 students.
  • Over 100 individuals, predominantly students, sustained injuries, straining local medical facilities.
  • The incident underscores persistent security vulnerabilities impacting civilian and educational sites in Afghanistan.

Devastating Blast Rocks Kunar Province

A powerful explosion near a school in Afghanistan's eastern Kunar province has resulted in the deaths of at least 10 students and left over 100 individuals injured. The tragic incident occurred on Saturday, 28 November 2026, sending shockwaves through the local community and drawing international condemnation. Authorities are investigating the cause and circumstances of the blast, which impacted a site frequented by young learners.

High Civilian Casualties, Strained Resources

Local officials confirmed the death toll, emphasizing that the majority of victims were children attending or near the school. Initial reports from agencies including Al Jazeera and BBC indicate more than 100 people required immediate medical attention, with some sources, including CNN, suggesting the injured count exceeded 110. The sudden influx of casualties is placing significant pressure on already limited medical resources in Kunar, a province that has historically faced infrastructural challenges. Emergency services, supported by local residents, worked tirelessly to transport the wounded to nearby hospitals and clinics.

A Pattern of Vulnerability for Educational Institutions

While the exact nature of the blast remains under investigation, its proximity to a school raises grave concerns about the targeting of educational facilities and civilian populations. Such incidents not only inflict immediate physical harm but also cast a long shadow over the future of education in the region. Schools in Afghanistan have, unfortunately, been vulnerable to conflict and violence for decades, disrupting learning and creating an environment of fear for students and educators alike. This latest attack serves as a stark reminder of the persistent threats faced by ordinary Afghans, particularly children, in their daily lives.

Persistent Instability and Humanitarian Crisis

The explosion in Kunar province comes amidst ongoing security challenges across Afghanistan. Despite efforts to establish stability, various factions and insurgent groups continue to operate, often leading to indiscriminate violence that disproportionately affects civilians. This incident highlights the urgent need for enhanced protective measures around educational and public spaces, alongside sustained humanitarian aid. The international community has repeatedly called for an end to violence against civilians, emphasizing the importance of safeguarding fundamental rights, including the right to education in a secure environment.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • At least 10 students tragically lost their lives in a blast near a school in Kunar, Afghanistan.
  • Over 100 individuals, predominantly children, sustained injuries, overwhelming local medical facilities.
  • The attack underscores the severe and ongoing security risks faced by civilians, especially children, and educational institutions in Afghanistan.
  • The incident demands renewed focus on protecting vulnerable populations and ensuring safe access to education amidst persistent regional instability.
