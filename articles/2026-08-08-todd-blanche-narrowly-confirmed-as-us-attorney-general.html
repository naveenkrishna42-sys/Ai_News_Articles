Todd Blanche narrowly confirmed as US attorney general

📅 2026-08-08 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Todd Blanche confirmed as attorney general by a narrow Senate margin
  • Blanche is Donald Trump's former personal lawyer
  • His confirmation reshapes the Justice Department under Trump's influence
  • A fund for Trump allies was rescinded after GOP senators threatened to block the bid

A Narrow Victory in the Senate

Todd Blanche, the former personal lawyer to Donald Trump, has been confirmed as the United States attorney general. The Senate vote was tight, reflecting deep divisions over his nomination and the direction he plans to take the Justice Department.

Blanche's confirmation clears the way for him to lead an agency that critics say is being reshaped to serve the president's interests. Supporters argue he will restore order and loyalty to an institution they believe has been politicised.

Trump's Tax Audit Immunity Stays

As Blanche's path to confirmation was cleared, a key protection remained intact: Trump's tax audit immunity. The provision, which shields the president's tax returns from scrutiny, stays in place, raising questions about the independence of the department under its new leadership.

This development has drawn sharp reactions from legal experts and opposition lawmakers. Some have called for transparency, while others have warned that the immunity could set a troubling precedent.

A Fund Rescinded Amid GOP Pressure

Before the final vote, Blanche made a significant concession. He rescinded a fund intended for Trump allies after Republican senators threatened to block his confirmation. The move was seen as a strategic retreat to secure the necessary votes.

The fund's cancellation has not fully appeased critics. Several senators remain cautious, and the episode has highlighted the political tightrope Blanche must walk as he assumes office.

A Justice Department Shaped for Trump

Reports from multiple outlets, including CNN and AP, describe a Justice Department being moulded to serve Trump's agenda. Blanche's appointment is viewed as a pivotal step in this transformation, with potential implications for ongoing investigations and policy priorities.

Observers note that the department's focus may shift towards issues aligned with the president's interests. This has sparked debate about the balance between political loyalty and the rule of law.

Blanche's legal background, which includes defending Trump in high-profile cases, positions him as a trusted figure within the president's inner circle. However, his lack of administrative experience has raised concerns about his ability to manage a sprawling federal agency.

What Happens Next

Blanche is expected to be sworn in soon, with his first actions likely to set the tone for his tenure. Lawmakers and legal watchdogs will be watching closely to see how he handles sensitive cases and whether he maintains any distance from the White House.

The coming weeks will reveal the practical impact of his leadership on the Justice Department's operations and its relationship with Congress.
