Ishan Kishan's Red-Ball Passion

Indian wicketkeeper-batsman Ishan Kishan has always been a fan of red-ball cricket. His love for Test cricket is evident in his performances and his words. He has been a part of India's Test squad for a while now, but he is keen to regain his place and make a mark in the longest format of the game.

Regaining His Test Place

Kishan's recent performances in the Duleep Trophy have been impressive. He has been consistent with the bat and has also shown his wicketkeeping skills. His coach, Uttam Mazumdar, believes that Kishan can be India's next Virender Sehwag. This is a huge compliment, given Sehwag's status as one of the greatest Test batsmen in Indian cricket history.

Why Red-Ball Cricket?

Kishan's love for red-ball cricket is not just about his performances. It's also about the challenge and the satisfaction he gets from playing the game. He has said that he really enjoys the red-ball cricket and that's what makes him love the game.

Future Ambitions

Kishan is ambitious and he has set his sights on the future. He wants to be a part of India's Test squad for a long time and make a significant impact in the game. His coach's belief in him is a huge boost for his future ambitions.
