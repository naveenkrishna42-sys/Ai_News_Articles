Amruta Khanvilkar, Himanshu Malhotra Threaten Legal Action Over Separation Rumours

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Amruta Khanvilkar and Himanshu Malhotra deny separation reports
  • Couple calls claims 'baseless and defamatory', warns legal action
  • Fans and netizens react to the statement on social media

Mumbai: Actor Amruta Khanvilkar and her husband, actor Himanshu Malhotra, have pushed back hard against swirling reports of a separation, threatening legal action against those spreading what they call "baseless and defamatory" claims. The couple, married since 2014, issued a joint statement on Monday, putting the entertainment industry and their fans on notice.

The statement comes after several media outlets and social media posts speculated about trouble in their marriage. The couple has categorically denied any rift, calling the reports malicious and damaging to their personal and professional reputations.

Statement Calls Reports 'Baseless'

The joint statement, released through their representatives, did not mince words. It described the separation rumours as completely false and said the couple would not tolerate such speculation any longer. "These reports are baseless and defamatory," the statement read, adding that legal counsel has been engaged to pursue action against those responsible.

The couple's decision to go public with a legal warning signals a firm stand. While neither actor has specified the exact outlets or individuals targeted, the statement makes clear that the legal route is being actively considered. This is a rare move for the usually private couple, who have largely kept their personal life away from the media glare.

Fans Rally Behind The Couple

News of the legal warning has sparked a wave of reactions online. Fans of both actors have flooded social media with messages of support, expressing relief that the couple is shutting down rumours. Many have criticised the media for what they perceive as invasive and unverified reporting.

Some fans, however, have questioned why the couple felt the need to respond publicly, suggesting that silence might have been a better strategy. The overwhelming sentiment, though, appears to be in favour of the couple's decision to protect their privacy and reputation.

A Career In The Spotlight

Amruta Khanvilkar, known for her roles in Marathi and Hindi cinema, has a substantial following. Her performances in films like Katyar Kaljat Ghusli and her stint on reality television have made her a household name. Himanshu Malhotra, equally recognised for his work in television and films, has often shared glimpses of their life together on social media.

The couple's public appearances, including red-carpet events and festive posts, have often been celebrated by fans. This sudden legal threat marks a departure from their usually warm, open online presence.

What Happens Next

Legal experts suggest that for any defamation case to proceed, the couple will need to identify specific instances of false reporting and demonstrate harm caused. The statement's mention of legal counsel indicates that paperwork may already be in motion.

For now, the couple has made their position clear: they will not let rumours go unchallenged. Fans and industry watchers will be keen to see whether this leads to actual legal filings or serves as a deterrent against future speculation. The couple's next move, whether in court or on social media, will be closely watched.
