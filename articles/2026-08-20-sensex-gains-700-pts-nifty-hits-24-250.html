Sensex gains 700 pts, Nifty hits 24,250

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex closes at 81,200, up 700 points
  • Nifty reaches 24,250, its highest in over a year
  • Positive global economic data and cues support market rally

Indian Stock Markets Rally on Global Cues

Indian stock markets closed higher on Thursday, with the Sensex gaining 700 points and the Nifty reaching 24,250. The rally was driven by positive global economic cues and data, which boosted investor sentiment.

Sensex and Nifty Performance

The Sensex closed at 81,200, up 700 points, or 0.86%, from its previous close. The Nifty, which had been trading above 24,200 for most of the session, finally breached the 24,250 mark, its highest level since early 2025.

The rally was broad-based, with most sectors contributing positively to the overall market performance. The banking and financial services sector led the gains, driven by positive economic data from the US and Europe. The IT sector also saw gains, supported by strong earnings reports from major companies.

Global Economic Cues

Positive global economic data and cues played a significant role in the market rally. The US Federal Reserve's decision to keep interest rates unchanged, coupled with strong economic growth data from the Eurozone, provided a positive outlook for global markets.

Additionally, strong corporate earnings reports from major global companies further boosted investor confidence. Tech giants like Apple and Microsoft reported strong quarterly results, which helped drive the market rally.

Domestic Factors

Domestic factors also contributed to the market rally. Strong economic data from India, including a rebound in manufacturing and services sectors, provided a positive outlook for the Indian economy.

Furthermore, positive sentiment from retail investors, who have been actively buying stocks on the back of the market rally, also supported the market performance.

What's Next?

The market rally is likely to continue in the coming sessions, supported by positive global economic cues and data. However, investors will be closely watching for any signs of a slowdown in global economic growth, which could impact market sentiment.
