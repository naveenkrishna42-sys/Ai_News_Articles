Astronauts halt antenna replacement during ISS spacewalk

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Science Original source ↗
Key points
  • Astronauts Anil Menon and Sophie Adenot spent over six hours performing maintenance outside the ISS.
  • The crew successfully removed a failed antenna but could not complete the installation of the new unit.
  • The mission marks a historic milestone as the first spacewalk performed by a French woman.

Maintenance mission cut short

Two astronauts aboard the International Space Station (ISS) returned to the airlock after a lengthy spacewalk that spanned six hours and 23 minutes. During the operation, NASA astronaut Anil Menon and his colleague Sophie Adenot successfully removed a failed antenna from the station's exterior.

Despite the successful extraction, the pair ran out of scheduled time before they could secure the replacement hardware. The astronauts focused their efforts on clearing the mounting site, ensuring the station remains in a safe condition for future maintenance activities.

Historic milestone in orbit

This mission holds particular significance for the European space community. Sophie Adenot became the first French woman to conduct a spacewalk, marking a notable achievement for the Expedition 75 crew. Her participation in the extravehicular activity highlights the collaborative nature of international space operations.

The astronauts spent the duration of the walk navigating the complex exterior of the ISS. They relied on station robotic systems and manual tools to manage the bulky communications equipment while tethered to the orbiting laboratory.

Focus on microgravity research

Following their return to the station, the crew shifted their focus back to internal operations. NASA reports that the Expedition 75 members are now engaged in cleaning up equipment and resuming their regular microgravity research experiments.

Maintenance tasks like these are routine but technically demanding. They require precise coordination between the astronauts and ground control teams to ensure that power levels and communication links remain stable during the process.

Next steps for the ISS

Ground controllers are currently reviewing the mission timeline to determine when the installation of the spare antenna can be completed. Officials have not yet confirmed a date for the follow-up spacewalk, which will be required to restore the station's full communications capability.
