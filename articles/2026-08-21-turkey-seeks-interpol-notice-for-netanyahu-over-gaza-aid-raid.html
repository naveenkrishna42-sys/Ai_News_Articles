Turkey seeks Interpol notice for Netanyahu over Gaza aid raid

Turkey issues arrest warrant for Israel PM Benjamin Netanyahu

Turkey has issued an arrest warrant for Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, seeking an Interpol Red Notice over his alleged involvement in the Gaza aid raid. The move comes amid escalating tensions between Turkey and Israel over the incident.

Turkey seeks Interpol Red Notice over Gaza aid raid

Turkey is seeking an Interpol Red Notice for Netanyahu, which would allow for his arrest if he enters Turkey. The request is based on allegations that Netanyahu was involved in the raid on a Turkish aid convoy in Gaza in May 2023. Turkey claims the raid resulted in the deaths of nine Turkish citizens.

Netanyahu calls Turkish president 'antisemitic dictator'

Netanyahu has responded to the arrest warrant by calling Turkish President Recep Tayyagöl 'an antisemitic dictator'. He accused Erdogan of using the incident to further his political agenda. Netanyahu's office has also described Erdogan's actions as 'antisemitic'.

Netanyahu's office slams 'antisemitic dictator' Erdogan

Netanyahu's office has slammed Erdogan's actions as 'antisemitic' and 'unprecedented'. The office has accused Erdogan of using the incident to further his political agenda and to target Israel. The office has also called for Erdogan to be held accountable for his actions.

Escalating tensions between Turkey and Israel

The arrest warrant and the Interpol Red Notice come amid escalating tensions between Turkey and Israel. The two countries have been at loggerheads over the incident in Gaza, with both sides accusing each other of using the incident to further their political agendas. The incident has also raised concerns about the safety of Turkish citizens in the region.
