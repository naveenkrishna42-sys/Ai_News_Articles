OnePlus N6X to Launch July 31 with Snapdragon 695 5G, 7000mAh Battery

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • OnePlus N6X will feature a Snapdragon 695 5G processor.
  • The phone packs a 7000mAh battery, its biggest selling point.
  • Launch is confirmed for July 31; includes a 3.5mm headphone jack and Burgundy Red color option.

Launch Date and Processor Details

OnePlus is gearing up to launch its latest mid-range smartphone, the OnePlus N6X, on July 31. The device is expected to be powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 695 5G chipset, a reliable processor for the segment that supports 5G connectivity.

This chipset is paired with an Adreno 619 GPU, promising smooth performance for everyday tasks and casual gaming. The N6X will likely come in multiple RAM and storage configurations, though OnePlus has not yet confirmed the exact variants.

Massive Battery is the Star Feature

The biggest talking point around the OnePlus N6X is its 7000mAh battery. This is a significant upgrade over typical mid-range phones, which usually pack 4500mAh to 5000mAh units. The larger capacity could easily deliver two days of moderate use on a single charge.

Fast charging support is expected, but the company has not disclosed the wattage. Given the battery size, a 65W or higher charger would be ideal to keep downtime short. The phone also retains the 3.5mm headphone jack, a feature increasingly rare in 2026.

Design and Color Options

OnePlus has revealed that the N6X will come in a Burgundy Red finish, alongside standard color options. The design is expected to follow the company's recent aesthetic, with a flat display and a rectangular camera module on the back.

The phone is likely to feature a 6.5-inch or larger IPS LCD screen with a 120Hz refresh rate, though OnePlus has not confirmed the display specifications yet.

Expected Price and Competition

Industry estimates place the OnePlus N6X in the sub-Rs 20,000 price bracket. This would pit it against rivals like the Redmi Note series and Realme number series. The combination of a 5G chipset, a massive battery, and a headphone jack gives it a distinct edge in the segment.

OnePlus has not yet announced any pre-order or early bird offers. Details on camera specifications and software update policy are also pending.

With the launch just days away, all eyes will be on how OnePlus prices the N6X. The phone's success will depend on whether the company can undercut competitors on price while delivering on the battery promise. Early reviews should surface shortly after the July 31 announcement.
