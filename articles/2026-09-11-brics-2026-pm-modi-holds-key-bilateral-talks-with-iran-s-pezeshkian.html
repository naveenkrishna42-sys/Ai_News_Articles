BRICS 2026: PM Modi Holds Key Bilateral Talks with Iran's Pezeshkian

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Team 📅 2026-09-11 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • PM Modi and Iran President Pezeshkian held high-level bilateral talks at BRICS 202.com, emphasizing strategic energy partnerships and regional security coordination.
  • Discussions centered on enhancing Indo-Persian connectivity through the Chabahar Port, a critical trade corridor linking India to Central Asia and beyond.
  • Both leaders reaffirmed commitment to BRICS-led multipolar reforms, aligning with India’s Act East Policy and Iran’s West Asia engagement strategy.

Strategic Dialogue at BRICS 2026

Prime Minister Narendra Modi engaged in comprehensive bilateral discussions with Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian during the BRICS 2026 summit. The talks underscored mutual interests in deepening energy cooperation, expanding trade ties, and strengthening regional security frameworks. Both sides emphasized the importance of a multipolar world order, reflecting shared concerns over global governance reforms.

Energy and Connectivity Ties

A key focus of the discussions was bolstering Indo-Iran energy collaboration, particularly in liquefied natural gas (LNG) sourcing and petrochemical investments. Iran's strategic position as a major gas supplier makes it pivotal for India's energy security, especially amid evolving global supply chain dynamics. Additionally, the role of Chabahar Port as a gateway for Indian trade with Afghanistan and Central Asia was reinforced, despite past geopolitical headwinds.

Trade Diversification and Economic Synergies

India and Iran are exploring enhanced cooperation in sectors such as pharmaceuticals, information technology, and agriculture. With bilateral trade currently valued at around ₹1.2 lakh crore annually, both nations aim to expand commercial linkages through Preferential Trade Agreement (PTA) mechanisms and easier visa regimes for business entities. India’s push for self-reliance under Atmanirbhar Bharat finds resonance in Iran's sanctions-resilient economy model.

Regional Stability and Multipolar Vision

The bilateral meeting also addressed security challenges in the Persian Gulf and broader West Asia region. PM Modi reiterated India’s stance on maritime safety and the protection of seafarers, aligning with Iran’s call for de-escalation in regional conflict zones. Both countries share convergent views on countering terrorism and promoting inclusive dialogue in conflict-affected areas.

BRICS Alignment and Global Reform Agenda

As BRICS continues to challenge Western-dominated financial institutions, India and Iran find common ground in advocating for reforms within the UN Security Council and the World Bank. Their joint push for local currency trade settlement and alternative payment systems reflects growing disillusionment with the dollar-based global economy.
