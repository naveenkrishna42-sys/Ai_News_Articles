SBI, PNB, BoB lead PSU banks on FD rates

Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) banks are offering competitive fixed deposit (FD) interest rates, with State Bank of India (SBI), Punjab National Bank (PNB), and Bank of Baroda (BoB) leading the pack. The latest FD rates for 1-year, 3-year, 5-year, and 10-year tenures have been updated, providing savers with a range of options to choose from.

SBI leads with highest 1-year FD rate

SBI has emerged as the top performer with a 1-year FD rate of 7.2%. This rate is higher than the rates offered by other PSU banks, making it a popular choice among savers looking for short-term deposits. The bank's strong financial health and customer-centric approach have contributed to its ability to offer competitive rates.

PNB and BoB offer competitive rates

PNB and BoB are not far behind, offering 1-year FD rates of 6.8% and 6.7% respectively. These rates are competitive and provide savers with a good return on their investments. PNB's strong branch network and BoB's focus on customer satisfaction have helped them maintain their position in the market.

BoB leads for 5-year and 10-year FDs

BoB stands out for its 5-year and 10-year FD rates. The bank offers a 5-year FD rate of 7.1% and a 10-year FD rate of 7.5%, which are the highest among the top 5 PSU banks. These rates are attractive for savers looking for long-term investments. BoB's focus on customer service and its strong financial position have enabled it to offer these competitive rates.
