Supreme Court to hear pleas alleging police brutality during NEET protests

The Supreme Court of India has agreed to hear pleas alleging police brutality during the recent NEET protests. The top court has scheduled the hearing for Monday, 5 August 2026, and has indicated that it may consider setting up a Special Investigation Team (SIT) to probe the allegations.

Background of the NEET protests

The NEET protests were a series of nationwide demonstrations by medical students and their supporters in response to the recent changes in the NEET (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test) exam. The protests began on 25 July 2026 and lasted for over a week, with thousands of students taking to the streets in various cities across the country.

Allegations of police brutality

The pleas filed in the Supreme Court allege that police forces across the country used excessive force against the protesters, leading to several injuries and deaths. The pleas also allege that the police did not take adequate measures to prevent the violence and that there was a lack of accountability for the actions of the police.

Potential SIT probe

The Supreme Court has indicated that it may set up a Special Investigation Team (SIT) to probe the allegations of police brutality. The SIT will be tasked with investigating the events leading up to the protests, the actions of the police during the protests, and the aftermath of the protests. The SIT will also be responsible for ensuring that the police are held accountable for their actions.

Key concerns and analysis

The allegations of police brutality during the NEET protests raise several concerns about the state of law and order in India. The use of excessive force by the police can lead to a breakdown of trust between the police and the public, which can have serious implications for the functioning of the criminal justice system. The lack of accountability for the actions of the police can also lead to a culture of impunity, where police officers are not held responsible for their actions.

The potential SIT probe is a positive development, as it shows that the Supreme Court is taking the allegations of police brutality seriously. However, it is important to ensure that the SIT is independent and impartial, and that it has the necessary resources and authority to investigate the allegations thoroughly.

Pros and cons of the SIT probe

  • Pros: The SIT probe can help bring accountability and transparency to the actions of the police. It can also help prevent similar incidents in the future.
  • Cons: The SIT probe may be seen as a political move, with the government trying to deflect attention from its own failures. It may also be seen as a cover-up, with the government trying to hide the truth about the events leading up to the protests.
