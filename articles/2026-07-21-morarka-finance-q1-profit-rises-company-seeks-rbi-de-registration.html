Morarka Finance Q1 profit rises, company seeks RBI de-registration

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Banking Original source ↗
Key points
  • Morarka Finance reported a rise in Q1 net profit, though exact figures were not disclosed in the initial release.
  • The company has formally applied to the Reserve Bank of India for voluntary de-registration as an NBFC.
  • The move appears to be part of a strategic shift away from regulated financial services.

Q1 performance shows improvement

Morarka Finance has reported a rise in its net profit for the first quarter of the current financial year. The company, a non-banking financial company (NBFC), did not immediately disclose the exact percentage or rupee value of the increase. Officials have not yet confirmed the specific figures driving the profit growth.

The improvement in quarterly earnings comes amid a broader strategic review at the firm. Details on the revenue and expense drivers behind the profit jump are expected in the detailed financial filings due later this month.

Application for RBI de-registration

In a parallel development, Morarka Finance has applied to the Reserve Bank of India for voluntary de-registration as an NBFC. The application, confirmed by company sources, marks a significant step in the firm's evolution. If approved, the company would cease to be regulated by the central bank's NBFC framework.

The reasons for seeking de-registration have not been officially stated. Market observers are speculating that the move could be driven by a desire to reduce compliance costs or to pivot away from core lending activities. The company has not yet issued a detailed rationale to stock exchanges.

What de-registration means

Voluntary de-registration as an NBFC would relieve Morarka Finance from adhering to RBI's stringent norms on capital adequacy, asset classification, and reporting. The company would still need to comply with other corporate laws under the Companies Act.

Investors will be watching to see if the firm plans to restructure its business entirely or exit the financial services sector. The RBI's decision on the application could take several weeks or months, depending on the regulator's review of the company's liabilities and depositor interests.

Background of Morarka Finance

Morarka Finance is a part of the Morarka Group, which has interests in commodities, finance, and other sectors. The company has been relatively low-profile in recent years, with its NBFC operations forming a small part of the group's overall portfolio.

The stock of Morarka Finance saw muted trading on Tuesday following the twin announcements. Analysts are likely to seek more clarity on the management's long-term vision during the next earnings call.

The coming weeks will reveal whether Morarka Finance's board plans to distribute accumulated reserves or pursue new lines of business outside the NBFC ambit. The company's future direction hinges on the RBI's approval and the board's subsequent strategic decisions.
