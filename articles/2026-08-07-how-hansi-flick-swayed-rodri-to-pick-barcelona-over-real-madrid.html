How Hansi Flick swayed Rodri to pick Barcelona over Real Madrid

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hansi Flick personally convinced Rodri to join Barcelona, swaying the midfielder from Real Madrid.
  • Rodri has reportedly given Barcelona the green light for a summer transfer from Manchester City.
  • The decision has sparked anger among Real Madrid fans, who had hoped to land the Spain international.

Flick's decisive pitch

Barcelona have won the race for Rodri. The Manchester City and Spain midfielder has reportedly given the Catalan club the green light for a summer move, and the man who tipped the scales is Hansi Flick. The Barcelona manager personally convinced the 30-year-old that his future lies at Camp Nou, not the Santiago Bernabéu.

Flick's intervention proved crucial. Real Madrid had been circling, and sources indicate the player was weighing his options carefully. But the German coach's vision for the team — and Rodri's role in it — swayed the decision.

The Athletic reported earlier this week that Barcelona were exploring a deal for Rodri, with the midfielder preferring a move to Camp Nou. Now, with Flick's personal touch, that preference has solidified into a commitment.

Why Rodri chose Barcelona

Rodri's choice is not merely about tactics. It is about trust. Flick has a track record of building teams around a dominant defensive midfielder, and he made that clear in his conversations with the player. The promise of being the on-field anchor, the metronome of a resurgent Barcelona, appealed to Rodri more than a supporting role in Madrid's star-studded engine room.

At Manchester City, Rodri has been the fulcrum of Pep Guardiola's system. He has won trophies, earned individual accolades, and established himself as one of the world's best in his position. But the lure of a new challenge in La Liga, and the chance to lead Barcelona's midfield revival, proved irresistible.

Real Madrid's interest was no secret. Their fans had even begun to celebrate the prospect of Rodri in white. That anticipation has now soured into anger.

Madrid fans react with fury

News of Rodri's decision has not gone down well in the Spanish capital. Social media is ablaze with frustrated Madridistas, some questioning the club's transfer strategy, others lashing out at the player. The sight of a top-tier Spanish midfielder picking their fiercest rivals is a bitter pill to swallow.

Barcelona, meanwhile, are quietly confident. The club have been working behind the scenes to structure a deal that satisfies Manchester City's demands. Financial constraints remain a reality at Camp Nou, but the prospect of landing Rodri has energised the board and the fanbase alike.

What this means for both clubs

For Barcelona, Rodri would represent a statement signing. He would shore up a midfield that has lacked a true defensive presence since Sergio Busquets' departure. His arrival would also send a message — that Barcelona can still compete for the world's elite, even amid financial turmoil.

For Real Madrid, missing out on Rodri is a setback. They will need to pivot to other targets, though their squad remains formidable. The saga also highlights a growing rivalry in the transfer market, with both Spanish giants now battling for the same profiles.

Manchester City, for their part, are reluctant to lose a player of Rodri's calibre. But with the midfielder's preference now clear, a sale could be negotiated before the window closes. City are known for driving hard bargains, and Barcelona may need to get creative with add-ons and payment structures.

What's next

All eyes now turn to the negotiation table. Barcelona must agree a fee with Manchester City, and Rodri's camp will push for personal terms. If the deal goes through, it could reshape the balance of power in Spanish football. If it collapses, Real Madrid might swoop back in — but for now, the momentum is firmly with Flick and Barcelona.
