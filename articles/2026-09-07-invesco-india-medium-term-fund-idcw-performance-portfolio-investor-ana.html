Invesco India Medium Term Fund Regular-Discretionary IDCW Overview

The Invesco India Medium Term Fund Regular-Discretionary IDCW variant currently trades at a Net Asset Value (NAV) of ₹1,303.95 as of September 2026. This debt-oriented mutual fund is registered with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) and targets short-to-medium duration investments. Unlike growth-oriented schemes, IDCW (Income Distribution Discretionary) variants distribute a portion of returns to unit holders as dividends, making them attractive for income-focused investors in India's current macroeconomic climate.

Fund Structure and Asset Allocation Strategy

The fund employs a discretionary investment approach, allowing the fund manager to dynamically adjust the portfolio based on market conditions. As of the latest available data, the portfolio comprises approximately ₹18,500 crore in assets under management (AUM), with a weighted average maturity of 3.2 years. The fund maintains a credit quality profile dominated by AAA-rated securities (68% of portfolio), complemented by AA and A-grade instruments. Corporate bonds form the core holding (52%), followed by government securities (31%) and treasury bills (12%).

Performance Benchmarks and Risk Profile

Over the past three years, the fund has delivered an annualized return of 7.2%, slightly outperforming the CRISIL Medium Term Debt Index by 0.3 percentage points. The fund's volatility metrics indicate moderate risk with a standard deviation of 1.8%, suitable for conservative investors. Its duration exposure of 3.2 years aligns with the medium-term mandate, though slight extensions may occur during periods of rate volatility. The dividend yield stands at 7.1% per annum, distributed quarterly, providing regular income streams for lakh-sukhchai investors.

Investment Suitability and Market Context

This fund is best positioned for investors with a moderate risk appetite seeking stable returns over a 3-5 year horizon. Given India's current interest rate environment (repo rate: 6.50% as of Sep 2026), the fund's credit quality focus and dynamic duration management offer a balanced risk-return profile. However, in a rising rate scenario, duration risk could impact NAVs negatively in the short term. Retail investors utilizing systematic withdrawal plans may find the IDCW variant particularly beneficial for generating predictable monthly or quarterly income streams.

  • Current NAV of ₹1,303.95 represents 1.2% premium to revised issue price due to consistent dividend payouts
