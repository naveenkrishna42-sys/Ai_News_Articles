Speeding Porsche crashes into divider, hits car on Mumbai Coastal Road, girl hurt

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • A Porsche lost control and hit a divider on Mumbai's Coastal Road.
  • The car then collided with another vehicle, injuring a minor girl.
  • Police have registered a case and are investigating the cause of the accident.

Crash on Coastal Road

A speeding Porsche crashed into a divider on Mumbai's Coastal Road early Sunday morning, before hitting another car. A minor girl travelling in the second vehicle was injured in the incident.

The accident occurred around 8 am near the Worli end of the sea-link road. The Porsche, driven by a man in his 30s, was heading south when the driver lost control.

Sequence of Events

Witnesses told the police the Porsche first hit the concrete median divider on the left side of the road. The impact sent the luxury car spinning, and it then collided with a Maruti Suzuki Swift Dzire that was travelling in the same direction.

The Dzire had three occupants, including a 10-year-old girl who sustained a minor injury to her arm. She was taken to a nearby hospital for treatment and later discharged.

Driver Questioned

The Porsche driver was not injured. Police officers at the scene conducted a breathalyser test, but the results have not been made public. The driver has been taken to the Worli police station for questioning.

Officials have not yet confirmed if a case of rash driving has been filed. The car has been seized for mechanical inspection to determine if a brake failure or tyre burst played a role.

Investigation Underway

Mumbai traffic police are reviewing footage from CCTV cameras installed along the Coastal Road. The stretch is known for high-speed traffic, especially during early morning hours.

This is not the first accident on this route. In January, a BMW had crashed into a barrier near Marine Drive, killing two people. Authorities have since installed additional speed breakers and signage, but enforcement remains a concern.

Police are also trying to ascertain if the Porsche driver had consumed alcohol. Sources said his blood samples have been sent for analysis. Results are awaited.

What Next?

The injured girl is recovering at home. The Porsche driver may be charged under the Motor Vehicles Act for negligent driving. A detailed report from the forensic team is expected within a week.
