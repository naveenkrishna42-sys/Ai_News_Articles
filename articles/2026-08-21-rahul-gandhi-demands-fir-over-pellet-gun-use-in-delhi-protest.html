Rahul Gandhi Demands FIR Over Pellet Gun Use in Delhi Protest

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Rahul Gandhi sat with a pellet gun victim outside a senior cop's office, demanding criminal action against police
  • The protest was linked to a CJP (Citizens for Justice and Peace) demonstration in Delhi
  • Congress party workers joined the dharna as police held consultations on whether to register a case

Rahul Gandhi Demands Criminal Case Against Police

Congress leader Rahul Gandhi staged a sit-in protest outside a senior police officer's office in Delhi on Friday, demanding that an FIR be registered against officers who used pellet guns during a CJP (Citizens for Justice and Peace) protest.

Gandhi sat with a pellet gun victim at the demonstration, highlighting the injuries sustained during the earlier protest. The Congress leader called for criminal action against the police personnel responsible for using the weapon.

Standoff Outside DCP Office

The protest unfolded outside the Deputy Commissioner of Police's office as Gandhi pressed his demand for a First Information Report. The dharna drew Congress party workers who joined the leader in seeking accountability for the use of pellet guns against demonstrators.

Police were seen holding consultations on whether to register a case following Gandhi's protest. Officials have not yet confirmed the outcome of those discussions.

Congress Latches Onto Protest

The sit-in drew immediate support from the Congress party, which backed Gandhi's demand for action against the police officers. Party workers gathered at the site as the protest continued through the day.

The use of pellet guns against protesters has been a recurring point of contention. The weapon, known for causing severe injuries at close range, has been deployed during several demonstrations in recent years.

Police Response Under Scrutiny

The Delhi police have not issued a formal statement on whether an FIR will be registered. The force has faced criticism over its crowd control methods during various protests in the capital.

Gandhi's protest puts renewed focus on police protocols for dispersing demonstrations. The leader's intervention with a victim in tow underscored the human cost of the confrontation.

Next Steps Awaited

Authorities are expected to hold further discussions on the demand for a criminal case. The outcome of the sit-in could set a precedent for how complaints against police conduct during protests are handled in the capital.

Watch for any official response from the Delhi police or the home ministry in the coming days.
