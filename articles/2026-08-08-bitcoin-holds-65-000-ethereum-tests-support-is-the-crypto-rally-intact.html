Bitcoin Holds $65,000, Ethereum Tests Support: Is the Crypto Rally Intact?

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin maintains $65,000 support level amid market consolidation.
  • Ethereum tests critical support, raising questions about altcoin strength.
  • Analysts split on whether the broader crypto uptrend remains valid.

Bitcoin Steadies Above Key Level

Bitcoin is holding its ground above the $65,000 mark, a level that traders are watching closely as the market searches for direction. The leading cryptocurrency has spent the past 48 hours in a narrow band, suggesting a pause after recent volatility.

Market participants say the $65,000 zone has become a psychological and technical floor. A decisive break below this could trigger fresh selling, while a bounce might reignite bullish momentum.

Ethereum Faces Critical Test

Ether, the second-largest digital asset, is now testing a support level that analysts describe as crucial for the near-term outlook. If this support fails, the altcoin could see deeper correction; if it holds, the stage may be set for another leg up.

The divergence between Bitcoin's resilience and Ethereum's fragility has caught attention. Some traders interpret this as a sign of profit-taking in altcoins after a strong run, rather than a broad market reversal.

What the Charts Say

Technical analysts point to lower timeframes showing a consolidation pattern, with Bitcoin forming a potential base above $65,000. Volume has thinned during this phase, which often precedes a sharp move in either direction.

For Ethereum, the support zone aligns with a Fibonacci retracement level, adding to its significance. A close below this area on daily charts could invite bearish bets.

Mixed Signals From Derivatives

Funding rates across major exchanges have cooled from recent highs, indicating that leveraged longs are not overcrowded. This is seen as a healthy reset, reducing the risk of a short squeeze or a long liquidation cascade.

Open interest remains elevated, however, suggesting that market participants are positioning for a breakout. The coming sessions could offer clarity on whether the uptrend resumes or stalls.

Is the Breakout Still Alive?

Opinion is divided. Bulls argue that Bitcoin's ability to hold $65,000 after a pullback shows underlying demand, while bears caution that repeated tests of support weaken the structure.

For the broader crypto market, Ethereum's performance will likely set the tone. If it rebounds, altcoins may follow; if it breaks down, Bitcoin could eventually come under pressure despite its relative strength.

Regulatory news and macroeconomic data remain wildcards. Any surprise from central banks or policy shifts could easily override technical patterns.

What to Watch Next

Traders are now focused on the daily close for both assets. A strong close above resistance for Bitcoin or a reclaim of support for Ethereum could signal the next move. Over the weekend, thin liquidity may exaggerate price swings.

The crypto market often makes its biggest moves when least expected. Until a clear breakout or breakdown occurs, caution is likely to prevail.
