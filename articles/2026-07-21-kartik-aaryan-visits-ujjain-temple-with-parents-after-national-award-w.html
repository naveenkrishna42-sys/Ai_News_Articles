Kartik Aaryan Visits Ujjain Temple With Parents After National Award Win

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kartik Aaryan visited Mahakaleshwar Temple in Ujjain with his parents after winning his first National Film Award.
  • The actor's visit follows the 72nd National Film Awards ceremony where Telugu cinema won nine awards.
  • Aaryan has not yet publicly commented on his win or the temple visit.
  • Other actors including Yami Gautam and Suriya have also reacted to the awards.

Actor Kartik Aaryan visited the Mahakaleshwar Temple in Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh, with his parents on Tuesday. The visit comes days after he won his first National Film Award.

Photographs from the temple show Aaryan in a simple white kurta-pajama, holding his mother's hand as they walked through the premises. Security personnel surrounded the actor as devotees and fans gathered to catch a glimpse of him.

First National Award for the Actor

The National Film Awards ceremony for the 72nd edition was held recently in New Delhi. While the full list of winners was announced, Aaryan's win has been widely discussed among his fan base.

Sources close to the actor said the temple visit was a personal affair, planned by his family to offer thanks. Aaryan has not yet made any public statement about the award or the visit.

Telugu cinema dominated this year's National Film Awards, winning in nine categories across acting, direction, and technical fields. This has sparked conversations in the industry about regional cinema's growing footprint at national honours.

Other Reactions to the Awards

Actress Yami Gautam, who also won a National Award, described it as a long-held dream. In a statement, she said the recognition is something she has carried in her heart for years.

Tamil star Suriya congratulated all winners, praising their pursuit of excellence. Political leaders from Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, including Naidu and Jagan, have also lauded Telugu cinema's performance at the awards.

Kartik Aaryan's win has been seen as a milestone in his career, which began with small-budget films before he moved to larger commercial projects. He has not confirmed what his next project will be.

What to Watch

Details about the specific category in which Aaryan won the National Award are yet to be confirmed by the actor or his team. The actor is expected to announce his upcoming film projects in the coming weeks.
