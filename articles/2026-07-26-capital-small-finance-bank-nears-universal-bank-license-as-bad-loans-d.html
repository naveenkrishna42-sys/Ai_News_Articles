Capital Small Finance Bank nears universal bank license as bad loans drop

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Banking Original source ↗
Key points
  • Capital Small Finance Bank is close to meeting the final RBI metric for a universal bank license.
  • The bank's bad loans have fallen significantly, improving its financial health.
  • A universal bank license would allow it to offer a wider range of banking services.

Bad loans fall sharply

Capital Small Finance Bank is closing in on the final regulatory hurdle needed to convert into a universal bank. The lender's non-performing assets have fallen substantially in recent quarters, bringing it closer to the Reserve Bank of India's threshold.

Sources familiar with the matter said the bank's gross NPA ratio now stands at around 2.5%, down from over 4% a year ago. This improvement has put the bank in a strong position to meet the RBI's final metric for a universal banking license.

What a universal bank license means

A universal bank license would allow Capital Small Finance Bank to offer a full suite of banking services, including corporate loans, foreign exchange, and investment banking. Currently, as a small finance bank, its operations are more restricted.

The bank has been working towards this upgrade for several years. It had earlier met most other RBI criteria, including capital adequacy and net worth requirements. The bad loan metric was the last major hurdle.

RBI's strict criteria

The RBI has set stringent conditions for small finance banks seeking to become universal banks. These include a net NPA ratio of less than 3% and a capital adequacy ratio of at least 15% for the past two years.

Capital Small Finance Bank's capital adequacy ratio is well above the minimum requirement. The bank's strong capital position and improving asset quality have strengthened its case.

Bank's performance improving

The bank reported a net profit of Rs 45 crore in the June quarter, up 30% year-on-year. Its net interest margin improved to 4.2% from 3.8% a year ago.

Loan growth has also been robust, with advances rising 18% to Rs 8,500 crore. The bank's deposit base grew 15% to Rs 10,200 crore during the same period.

Officials have not yet confirmed a timeline for the license approval. However, sources said the bank expects to hear from the RBI in the coming months.

What happens next

The RBI will now review the bank's application based on the latest financial data. If approved, Capital Small Finance Bank would become the first small finance bank to convert into a universal bank in the current cycle.

Industry watchers say this could set a precedent for other small finance banks looking to expand their operations. For now, the bank's management remains focused on maintaining asset quality and meeting all regulatory requirements.
