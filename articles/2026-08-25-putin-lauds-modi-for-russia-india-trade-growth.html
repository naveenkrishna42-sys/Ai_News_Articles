Putin lauds Modi for Russia-India trade growth

Putin credits PM Modi for growing Russia-India trade

Russian President Vladimir Putin has credited Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi for the significant growth in trade between the two nations. This was revealed during their recent talks in Moscow, where External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar was also present.

The two leaders discussed various areas of cooperation, including trade, defence, and energy. Putin's comments come at a time when both countries are looking to strengthen their bilateral relations, which have been on a steady upward trajectory.

Russia pledges to increase fertiliser supplies to India

One of the key areas of focus during the talks was agriculture. Putin announced that Russia will further increase its supplies of fertilisers to India, a move that is expected to benefit Indian farmers. This comes at a time when India is facing a shortage of fertilisers, which has been a major concern for the government.

The fertiliser exports from Russia to India have been growing steadily, with the two countries signing a memorandum of understanding to increase the volume of trade. This move is seen as a significant boost for India's agricultural sector, which is crucial for the country's economic growth.

Nuclear energy cooperation is a priority for India

Another area of cooperation that was discussed was nuclear energy. Putin expressed Russia's willingness to work with India on this front, with nuclear energy being a priority for India. This move is seen as a significant step towards strengthening the strategic partnership between the two nations.

The two leaders also discussed the possibility of setting up a joint venture to build nuclear power plants in India. This move is seen as a significant boost for India's nuclear energy sector, which is crucial for the country's energy security.

Putin expects to meet PM Modi at SCO summit next week

Putin has expressed his hope to meet PM Modi at the upcoming Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) summit next week. This move is seen as a significant step towards strengthening the strategic partnership between the two nations.

The SCO summit is expected to be a significant event, with leaders from various member countries expected to attend. This move is seen as a significant boost for the SCO, which is crucial for regional security and economic cooperation.
