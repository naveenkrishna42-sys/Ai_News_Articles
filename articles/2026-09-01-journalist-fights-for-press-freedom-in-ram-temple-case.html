Journalist Challenges UP Police's Request for X Account Data

Journalist Abhishek Upadhyay has moved the Supreme Court against the Uttar Pradesh Police's request for digital information of his X (formerly Twitter) account. The case revolves around allegations of theft of donations meant for the construction of the Ram Temple in Ayodhya. Upadhyay's move is seen as a significant step in protecting press freedom and digital privacy in India.

Background of the Case

The case dates back to 2024 when Upadhyay reported on the theft of donations meant for the Ram Temple. The Uttar Pradesh Police has been investigating the case and has now sought digital information of Upadhyay's X account. Upadhyay has argued that this request is a violation of his right to privacy and press freedom.

Supreme Court's Role

The Supreme Court has been approached by Upadhyay to intervene in the case. The court will now consider whether the UP Police's request for digital information is justified. This case is significant as it raises important questions about press freedom and digital privacy in the digital age.

Concerns and Criticisms

There are concerns that the UP Police's request for digital information is a step towards a more intrusive form of journalism. This could potentially lead to a situation where journalists are more cautious about what they report, fearing that their digital footprints could be used against them. This could have a chilling effect on press freedom in India.
