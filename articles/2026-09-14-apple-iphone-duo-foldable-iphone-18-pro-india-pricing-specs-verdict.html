Apple Finally Enters the Foldable Arena

Apple has officially unveiled the iPhone Duo, its first foldable smartphone, ending years of speculation. Announced alongside the iPhone 18 Pro, the move places Apple directly against Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold series and Google's Pixel Fold. For Indian buyers, this is the first time a mainstream Apple foldable will be available through official channels, with pricing expected to reflect the premium category.

The launch marks a strategic shift. Apple rarely enters a product category first; it waits, refines, and then scales. The iPhone Duo follows that playbook, arriving years after Samsung proved the form factor. The question is whether Apple's execution justifies the inevitable price gap.

These figures place the Duo squarely in ultra-premium territory. The ₹20,000 gap between the Duo and the 18 Pro Max is significant, but not unexpected for a first-generation foldable.

What the Foldable Form Factor Actually Delivers

The iPhone Duo's 7.8-inch inner display transforms multitasking. Split-screen apps, a desktop-class Safari, and a larger keyboard make it a genuine mini-tablet replacement. Apple has also optimised iOS 20 for the foldable, with new continuity features that let apps transition seamlessly between cover and inner screens.

Durability remains the critical unknown. Apple claims a new hinge mechanism rated for 300,000 folds, but real-world Indian conditions—dust, humidity, and pocket grit—will test that. Buyers should consider AppleCare+, which is likely to be essential rather than optional for this device.

Pros and Cons: Real-World Buyer Guide

  • Pros: Largest iPhone display ever; true multitasking; premium build; strong resale value expected; ecosystem integration is unmatched.
  • Cons: Very high entry price; first-generation hinge and crease risks; heavier than an 18 Pro Max; limited app optimisation at launch; repair costs likely to be steep.

The iPhone 18 Pro, by contrast, offers the same A20 chipset in a conventional slab design. It is lighter, cheaper, and proven. For most buyers, it remains the safer choice.

India Pricing and Value Justification

At an indicative ₹1,79,900, the iPhone Duo costs more than a fully loaded MacBook Air. That price buys innovation, but not necessarily reliability. Indian buyers should weigh the Duo against the Galaxy Z Fold 7, which offers a more mature foldable experience at a similar price.

Samsung's response was swift. The Verge reported Samsung saying 'Tim Cook bought a Galaxy Fold'—a cheeky acknowledgment that Apple is now chasing a category Samsung defined. That competitive pressure may benefit buyers through faster innovation and eventual price corrections.

Final Verdict for Indian Buyers

If you want the largest iPhone screen, the iPhone Duo delivers. If you want the best iPhone value, the iPhone 18 Pro wins. The Duo is a statement device—impressive, expensive, and unproven. The 18 Pro is the pragmatic pick. For most Indian buyers, the smart money stays with the slab.
