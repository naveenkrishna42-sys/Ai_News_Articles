Spider-Man: Brand New Day Becomes Top Hollywood Grosser in India

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day becomes highest grossing Hollywood film in India
  • Film set to cross Rs. 400 crore mark today
  • Bollywood Hungama reported the box office milestone

Spider-Man: Brand New Day has swung past every Hollywood release in India to claim the top spot at the domestic box office. According to Bollywood Hungama, the film is now the highest-grossing Hollywood title ever in the country, and it is poised to cross the Rs. 400 crore mark today.

The milestone comes barely weeks after the film's release, underscoring the massive draw of the Spider-Man franchise among Indian audiences. Trade analysts had predicted strong numbers, but the actual run has exceeded most expectations.

A Record-Breaking Run

The film's collections have been steady across all regions, with metropolitan centres leading the charge. Multiplex chains have reported packed shows, especially during the opening weekend and subsequent holidays.

Bollywood Hungama's report confirms that the film has outpaced previous Hollywood record-holders in India. The exact figures for the current total have not been officially released, but the trade body's tracking suggests the Rs. 400 crore milestone is imminent.

What This Means for Hollywood in India

Hollywood studios have long eyed the Indian market as a growth area, and this performance reinforces that belief. A superhero film crossing Rs. 400 crore is no longer a rarity, but it is a significant marker for non-Indian language releases.

Industry watchers note that the success is not just about the franchise's popularity. It also reflects wider trends—higher ticket prices in premium formats, dubbed versions in multiple languages, and a younger audience that consumes Hollywood content regularly.

The Road Ahead

With the Rs. 400 crore mark expected to be crossed today, the film's momentum is unlikely to slow immediately. The coming weeks will determine whether it can stretch further and set an even higher benchmark.

For now, Spider-Man: Brand New Day stands as the new standard for Hollywood releases in India. The previous record holders will have to watch from the sidelines as this web-slinger continues to spin box office gold.

As the film enters its next phase, the focus will shift to its performance in smaller cities and whether it can sustain collections through the week. Trade experts will be watching closely to see if the film can push beyond Rs. 450 crore or even Rs. 500 crore in the long run.
