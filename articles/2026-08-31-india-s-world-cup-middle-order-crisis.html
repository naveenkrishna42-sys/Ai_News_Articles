India's Middle-Order Struggles

India's ODI World Cup campaign in 2027 is hanging by a thread, with the middle-order batting proving to be a significant weakness. The loss of Shreyas Iyer, one of India's most reliable batsmen, has left a void that the selection committee is scrambling to fill. Experts like Gautam Gambhir and Ajit Agarkar are leading the charge in identifying a suitable replacement.

Tilak Varma: The Emerging Contender

Tilak Varma, the young opener from Karnataka, has emerged as the top contender to fill the void left by Shreyas Iyer. Varma has been impressive in domestic cricket, showcasing his ability to anchor the innings and provide stability to the middle-order. His selection would be a significant boost for India's batting lineup.
