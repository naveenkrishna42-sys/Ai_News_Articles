Vinicius Jr signs six-year Real Madrid extension until 2032

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Vinicius Jr signs six-year contract extension at Real Madrid, keeping him at the club until 2032.
  • The announcement dashes Arsenal's hopes of signing the Brazilian winger.
  • Real Madrid had reportedly made an improved contract offer to secure his long-term future.

Vinicius Jr commits long-term future to Real Madrid

Real Madrid have officially announced that Vinicius Jr has signed a six-year contract extension, tying the Brazilian winger to the club until 2032. The announcement, made on Thursday, puts an end to months of speculation surrounding the 26-year-old's future at the Santiago Bernabeu.

The club's official website confirmed the renewal, stating simply: "Vini Jr. renewed until 2032." The deal represents a major statement of intent from the La Liga giants, who have moved decisively to secure one of their most valuable assets for the long term.

Arsenal's hopes dashed

The news will come as a significant blow to Arsenal, who had been strongly linked with a move for the Brazilian forward. Reports in recent weeks had suggested the Premier League club were monitoring the situation closely, hoping to tempt Vinicius away from Madrid with a lucrative offer.

However, those hopes have now been extinguished. The Guardian reported that Arsenal's interest had been "dashed" following the extension, while The Athletic had earlier indicated that Real Madrid had tabled an improved contract offer to fend off any potential suitors.

A cornerstone of Madrid's future

Vinicius has been a pivotal figure for Real Madrid since joining from Flamengo in 2018. His explosive pace, dribbling ability, and knack for scoring in big moments have made him a fan favourite and a central piece of the club's attacking setup.

The new deal underscores Madrid's commitment to building their next great era around the Brazilian. With the contract running until 2032, the club has effectively ensured that Vinicius will spend the prime of his career in the Spanish capital, provided he sees out the full duration of the agreement.

Financial terms of the deal have not been officially disclosed, though reports suggest the improved offer was necessary to reflect his status as one of the world's elite players.

Transfer saga finally closed

The extended contract brings closure to a transfer saga that had dragged on through the summer. Real Madrid's official announcement, first reported by Managing Madrid, came just hours after ESPN had noted the agreement, with the club's website providing the definitive confirmation.

For Madrid fans, the news is a cause for celebration. For the club's rivals, it serves as a reminder of their pulling power. The Brazilian's previous deal was due to expire in 2027, but that timeline has now been pushed back by five years.

Vinicius's decision to stay will also be seen as a signal of his faith in the project at Madrid, even as the club navigates a period of transition in other areas of the squad.

What happens next

All eyes will now turn to the official presentation, with the club expected to hold a formal event to mark the renewal in the coming days. Vinicius will be eager to get back on the pitch and justify the faith shown in him, with the new La Liga season looming on the horizon.

The real test, however, will come on the field. With his future settled, the pressure will be on Vinicius to deliver consistent performances and help Madrid compete for silverware both domestically and in Europe. The next chapter of his career at the Bernabeu promises to be closely watched.
