Galaxy S27 Ultra to Launch in Two Sizes

Samsung's upcoming Galaxy S27 Ultra is set to come in two sizes, according to new CAD models. The device, expected to be unveiled later this year, will reportedly offer a 6.8-inch and a 7.3-inch variant, catering to different user preferences.

New CAD Models Reveal Design Details

CAD models of the Galaxy S27 Ultra have surfaced online, providing a glimpse into the device's design. The models show a sleek, premium build with a flat-edge design, similar to its predecessor. The back panel is expected to feature a glass design, with a camera bump that houses the device's advanced camera setup.

Samsung's Camera Redesign Sparks Debate

Rumors suggest Samsung is redesigning the Galaxy S27 Ultra's camera system. The new setup is expected to include a 200MP primary sensor, a 12MP ultra-wide lens, and a 10MP telephoto lens. However, the inclusion of a periscope telephoto lens is yet to be confirmed. The redesign has sparked a debate among fans, with some praising the potential improvements and others expressing concerns about the increased complexity.

Galaxy S27 Rumors Talk About One UI 9.5, Galaxy AI 2.0, Satellite, NFC and More

Alongside the Galaxy S27 Ultra, rumors are swirling about the device's software features. The Galaxy S27 Ultra is expected to run on One UI 9.5, Samsung's custom skin for Android. The device will also feature Galaxy AI 2.0, Samsung's AI-powered assistant, which is expected to offer more advanced features. Additionally, the Galaxy S27 Ultra is rumored to support satellite communication, allowing users to make calls and send messages via satellite. The device is also expected to feature NFC, enabling users to make contactless payments and transfer files between devices.
