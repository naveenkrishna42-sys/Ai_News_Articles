iPhone 18 launch nears: Why Apple loyalty is getting costlier in India

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iPhone 18 series expected to launch soon; price increases reported.
  • Reports suggest significant price hikes, especially for Pro models in India.
  • Upcoming features may include design and hardware upgrades.
  • Apple's pricing strategy could impact consumer loyalty in India.

With the iPhone 18 series launch just weeks away, Apple loyalists in India are facing a bitter truth: staying with the brand is becoming significantly more expensive. Multiple reports, including from Moneycontrol and Forbes, indicate that the next-generation iPhones—particularly the Pro models—will carry heftier price tags than their predecessors. The buzz around the launch, expected in September, is already dominated by cost concerns rather than just camera specs.

A pricey upgrade cycle

According to industry trackers, the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max are set to see notable price increases in India. While Apple has not made any official announcement, analysts point to rising component costs and a weaker rupee against the dollar as key drivers. The base iPhone 18 models may also see a modest bump, but the steepest jump is likely reserved for the premium variants.

For Indian consumers, this is a departure from the recent trend where Apple offered aggressive discounts on older models during festive sales. The company has been trying to expand its user base in the country, but a higher entry price could test that ambition.

What the leaks suggest

Rumors about the iPhone 18 series have been swirling for months. Mashable reports that the lineup will include new color options and design tweaks, while Techgenyz highlights promising upgrades to the Pro models, possibly including enhanced camera sensors and a faster chip. However, none of these features are confirmed, and Apple has remained silent on the specifics.

The most concrete signal so far comes from a keynote update reported by Forbes, which hints at a September release date—consistent with Apple's usual schedule. But the excitement over new features is being overshadowed by the price question.

Loyalty under pressure

Apple's ecosystem is sticky—iMessage, iCloud, and seamless device integration make switching difficult. But there is a limit to what even the most devoted fans will pay. Social media discussions in India are already buzzing with complaints about the expected price hike, with some users suggesting they might hold onto their current devices for another year.

There is also the resale angle. iPhones hold their value well in India, which has historically justified the upfront cost. But if the new models are priced too high, even that calculus could shift. Industry watchers note that Apple's competitors, particularly Samsung and Google, are offering compelling alternatives at lower price points.

What this means for buyers

For those planning to upgrade, the advice is simple: wait for the official launch and compare prices across channels. Apple often introduces trade-in offers and bank discounts during the initial sale period, which can soften the blow. But with reports of a significant price jump, even those incentives may not be enough to keep the cost in check.

It is also worth noting that the price increase is not unique to India. Global reports suggest similar trends in other markets, though the impact is sharper here due to currency fluctuations and import duties.

As the launch date approaches, all eyes will be on Apple's pricing strategy. The company has a track record of testing premium price points, but the Indian market is price-sensitive. Whether Apple can maintain its premium image while keeping loyal customers happy remains to be seen. For now, the message is clear: the cost of being an Apple fan in India is going up, and buyers will need to decide if it is worth it.
