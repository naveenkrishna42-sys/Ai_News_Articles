Bihar BEd CET 2026: 10th Seat Allotment List Nears Release

The Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) is set to publish the 10th round seat allotment list for the Bachelor of Education Common Entrance Test (BEd CET) 2026. Candidates who registered for this round must check the official counselling portal for their allotment status, institute name and reporting deadline. The 10th round typically serves as a mop-up stage, filling vacancies left after earlier rounds in both government and private BEd colleges across Bihar.

This round matters for two kinds of aspirants: those who missed earlier allotments due to document delays, and those seeking upgrades into government colleges where annual fees are a fraction of private institute charges. Once the list is live, the confirmation window is usually short, often 48 to 72 hours, so candidates should keep scanned documents ready.

Bihar BEd CET 2026 Counselling: Key Dates and Process

BCECEB conducts the counselling online through its official admission portal. The sequence involves registration, choice filling, seat allotment, seat confirmation and physical reporting at the allotted institute. Candidates who accept a seat must pay the admission fee and complete document verification, failing which the allotment stands cancelled.

Documents Required for Verification

  • BEd CET 2026 admit card and rank card
  • Class 10 and 12 marksheets and passing certificates
  • Graduation marksheets and degree certificate (BA/BSc/BCom or equivalent)
  • Category certificate, if applicable, issued by a competent authority
  • Domicile certificate and Aadhaar-linked photo ID
  • Recent passport-size photographs and counselling fee receipt

Bihar BEd CET 2026 Cutoff: What to Expect

Cutoff marks are released category-wise and institute-wise. Government BEd colleges in Patna, Muzaffarpur and Gaya historically close at higher scores, while private colleges accept candidates across a wider score band. Fee Structure and Value Comparison

Government BEd colleges in Bihar charge roughly ₹3,000 to ₹8,000 per year, while private institutes charge ₹40,000 to ₹90,000 annually. Over a two-year programme, the cost gap can exceed ₹1.5 lakh, which explains why upgrade rounds see heavy competition for government seats. Candidates allotted a private college should weigh the fee against NCTE approval status, faculty strength and internship placement record before confirming.

Real-World Benchmarks for Applicants

  • Seat acceptance: Confirm within the stipulated window; late payment usually means forfeiture.
  • Upgrade option: Choosing upgrade keeps the current seat provisional until the next list is out.
  • Reporting: Physical reporting at the institute is mandatory; postal or email confirmation is not accepted.
  • Refund rules: Cancellation after the final round typically attracts fee deduction as per BCECEB norms.

Pros and Cons of the 10th Round Allotment

  • Pro: Last realistic chance to secure a BEd seat for the 2026-28 academic session.
  • Pro: Government seats occasionally open up when earlier allottees withdraw.
  • Con: Limited choice of institutes, mostly private colleges in distant districts.
  • Con: Very short confirmation and reporting windows leave little room for document delays.

Candidates should monitor the official BCECEB portal for the exact release time of the 10th seat allotment list, keep the counselling login credentials handy, and verify institute NCTE recognition before paying any fee.
