Garmin Enters Screenless Market

Garmin has launched the Cirqa Smart Band, a screenless fitness tracker that directly rivals Whoop. The new device is designed to track health and fitness without the need for a screen. Officials have not yet confirmed the full range of features, but it is known that the Cirqa Smart Band does not require a subscription.

Design and Features

The Cirqa Smart Band has a simple design, with a focus on functionality over visual display. The device is available in four colors, according to product images released by Garmin. The screenless design is a departure from traditional smartwatches and fitness trackers, which often feature large displays.

The Cirqa Smart Band is designed to track health and fitness metrics, although the exact details of its capabilities have not been fully disclosed. The device is likely to appeal to users who want a simple, low-maintenance fitness tracker that does not require constant interaction.

Competition with Whoop

The launch of the Cirqa Smart Band puts Garmin in direct competition with Whoop, a company known for its screenless fitness trackers. Whoop's devices have gained popularity among athletes and fitness enthusiasts, and Garmin is likely seeking to capture a share of this market. The fact that the Cirqa Smart Band does not require a subscription may be a key selling point, as some users may be deterred by the ongoing costs associated with Whoop's devices.

Market Impact

The launch of the Cirqa Smart Band is likely to have a significant impact on the fitness tracker market.
