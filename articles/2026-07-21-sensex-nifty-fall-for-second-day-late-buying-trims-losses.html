Sensex, Nifty fall for second day; late buying trims losses

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty declined for the second consecutive day.
  • Late buying in banking and IT stocks helped trim intraday losses.
  • Broader markets also witnessed selling pressure during the session.

Indian equity benchmarks ended in the red for the second straight session on Tuesday, as profit booking in early trade gave way to a partial recovery in the final hour. The Sensex closed 187 points lower at 81,122, while the Nifty slipped 54 points to settle at 24,678.

Intraday volatility was high. The Nifty had fallen over 150 points at one stage before late buying helped the index trim most of its losses. Traders said the recovery was led by short-covering in select heavyweight stocks.

Banking and IT stocks drag

Banking stocks were the biggest drag on the benchmarks. The Nifty Bank index fell nearly 0.6% during the session, with private lenders bearing the brunt of selling pressure. HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank were among the top contributors to the Sensex's decline.

Information technology stocks also stayed weak. The Nifty IT index lost 0.4% as investors remained cautious ahead of the upcoming quarterly results from major IT firms. Infosys and TCS saw modest declines.

Broader market under pressure

The broader market mirrored the weakness in benchmarks. The BSE Midcap index slipped 0.3%, while the Smallcap index ended flat. Analysts said the selling was broad-based, with more than two-thirds of BSE-listed stocks closing lower.

Auto and metal stocks also witnessed selling. Maruti Suzuki, Tata Steel and Hindalco were among the notable losers in the Nifty pack. On the flip side, select pharma and FMCG stocks managed to stay afloat, providing some support to the indices.

What drove the late recovery

Market participants attributed the late recovery to value buying by institutional investors. Domestic institutional investors were net buyers on Tuesday, while foreign portfolio investors remained net sellers. The buying emerged as the Nifty approached its key support level of 24,500, which traders said is a critical zone for short-term momentum.

Volatility is expected to remain elevated this week, with global cues and monthly derivatives expiry adding to the uncertainty. The rupee also weakened against the US dollar, ending at 83.45, which added to the caution among forex traders.

Investors will track global market trends and crude oil prices for further direction. The coming sessions could see range-bound movement as traders adjust positions ahead of the expiry.
