Sensex gains 152 pts, Nifty settles at 24,624 after RBI keeps rates unchanged

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex rose 152 points, Nifty settled at 24,624
  • RBI maintained status quo on repo rate
  • Realty and auto stocks among top gainers

Indian equity benchmarks ended Wednesday's session on a positive note, with the BSE Sensex gaining 152 points and the Nifty settling at 24,624, as the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) kept its key policy rates unchanged, aligning with market expectations.

The RBI's decision to maintain the status quo on the repo rate provided a sense of stability, though investor sentiment remained cautious amid global cues. The market's upward move was led by buying in realty and auto stocks, while some profit-taking was visible in IT and pharma counters.

RBI's status quo boosts sentiment

The central bank's monetary policy committee (MPC) chose to hold rates steady, a move that analysts said was widely anticipated. The decision comes against the backdrop of moderating inflation and a need to support economic growth.

Market participants took comfort in the RBI's stance, interpreting it as a signal that the central bank is in no hurry to tighten policy further. However, the commentary accompanying the decision was closely watched for any hints about the future trajectory of rates.

Sectoral performance mixed

On the sectoral front, the Nifty Realty index emerged as the top gainer, rising over a percent, followed by auto and FMCG stocks. Banking stocks also traded firm, with private lenders contributing to the index gains.

Conversely, IT and pharma shares witnessed mild selling pressure, capping the upside for the broader market. The broader market indices, including the BSE Midcap and Smallcap, ended marginally higher, reflecting a selective risk-on sentiment.

Broader market and global cues

Global markets were largely steady, with investors awaiting key economic data from the US later this week. Domestic institutional investors remained net buyers, while foreign institutional investors' activity was subdued.

Analysts noted that the market's focus would now shift to quarterly earnings and the progress of the monsoon, which could influence the RBI's future policy actions. The rupee traded in a narrow range against the US dollar.

What to watch next

Going ahead, investors will track global oil prices, the US Federal Reserve's stance, and any fresh domestic data points. The RBI's commentary in the coming weeks will also be crucial for market direction.

With the policy overhang out of the way, the market is likely to take cues from corporate earnings and global trends. Traders are advised to keep a close watch on the 24,500-24,800 range for the Nifty in the near term.
