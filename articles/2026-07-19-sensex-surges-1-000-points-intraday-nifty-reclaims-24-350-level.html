Sensex surges 1,000 points intraday, Nifty reclaims 24,350 level

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex jumped over 1,000 points during the trading session
  • Nifty crossed the 24,350 mark, indicating strong market momentum
  • Rally was attributed to buying in banking, IT, and auto stocks
  • Positive global cues and domestic economic data boosted investor sentiment

Market opens strong, indices hit day's high

Indian equity benchmarks opened sharply higher on Friday, with the BSE Sensex surging over 1,000 points in intraday trade. The NSE Nifty crossed the 24,350 level, reflecting broad-based buying across sectors.

The rally was led by gains in banking, information technology, and auto stocks. Market breadth was positive, with more than two-thirds of the stocks on the BSE trading in the green.

What fueled the surge

Analysts pointed to a mix of domestic and global factors. On the international front, Wall Street ended on a strong note overnight, and Asian markets traded higher. This provided a positive lead for Indian markets.

Domestically, fresh foreign portfolio investor (FPI) inflows added to the buying momentum. Data released earlier this week showed India's retail inflation remained within the Reserve Bank of India's comfort zone, boosting hopes of a rate cut later this year.

Sectoral performance

The Nifty Bank index rose over 1.5%, with heavyweights like HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank contributing significantly. The Nifty IT index also gained more than 1%, tracking positive cues from the US tech sector.

Auto stocks were in demand on expectations of healthy quarterly earnings. The Nifty Auto index climbed nearly 2% during the session.

Broader market participation

The midcap and smallcap indices outperformed the benchmarks, rising over 1.5% each. This suggests that the rally was not limited to large-caps but was broad-based.

Volatility, as measured by the India VIX, eased, indicating reduced investor anxiety. Traders covered short positions, which added to the upward momentum.

What traders and analysts say

Market participants said the breakout above key resistance levels triggered fresh buying. The Nifty's ability to hold above 24,300 in early trade encouraged bullish bets.

Technical analysts noted that the index has formed a strong support base around the 24,000 mark. A sustained move above 24,400 could open the door for a test of the 24,500-24,600 zone in the near term.

Volume data showed that both institutional and retail participation was robust. Options data indicated that maximum open interest has shifted higher, reflecting a change in sentiment.

Outlook for the session

While the market gave up some gains in the afternoon, it remained firmly in positive territory. Traders are now watching for any consolidation near the day's high.

The coming sessions will depend on global cues and earnings from index heavyweights. With the derivatives expiry next week, volatility is expected to remain elevated.
