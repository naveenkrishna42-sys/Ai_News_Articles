EAGLE Force raids Hyderabad pan shop for selling cigarettes to minors

The EAGLE Force of Hyderabad has recently conducted a raid on a pan shop in the city, resulting in the arrest of the owner and a worker for allegedly selling cigarettes to minors. The operation was carried out as part of a larger crackdown on the illegal sale of tobacco products to underage individuals.

Operation details and arrests

The raid was conducted on a pan shop located in the heart of Hyderabad. The EAGLE Force personnel, equipped with necessary legal documents, entered the premises and found the owner and a worker in the act of selling cigarettes to minors. The minors were observed to be under the age of 18, which is the legal age for purchasing tobacco products in India.

Seizure of evidence and impact

During the raid, the EAGLE Force seized a significant quantity of cigarettes and other evidence. The owner and the worker were arrested on the spot and are currently being interrogated. The operation has sent a strong message to the pan shop owners in Hyderabad that the sale of tobacco products to minors will not be tolerated.

Public awareness campaign

In the wake of this operation, the EAGLE Force has initiated a public awareness campaign to educate the youth about the harmful effects of smoking. The campaign aims to create a healthier society and reduce the number of young people who are tempted to take up smoking.

Impact on youth smoking habits in Hyderabad

The operation has had a significant impact on the youth smoking habits in Hyderabad. The arrest of the pan shop owner and worker has sent a clear message that the sale of tobacco products to minors is a serious offense. The public awareness campaign has also helped to create a more informed and aware youth population, which is crucial in the fight against smoking.
