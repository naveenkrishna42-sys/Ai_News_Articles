Awarapan 2 earns Rs 5.5 crore on Day 8

Awarapan 2 continues strong box office run

Emraan Hashmi's Awarapan 2 has added another Rs 5.5 crore to its box office collection on Day 8, continuing its strong run at the Indian box office. The film, which has now crossed the Rs 100 crore mark, is proving to be a significant hit for the actor and the production team.

Rajkumar Santoshi reacts to Batwara 1947's lower collections

Director Rajkumar Santoshi has expressed his satisfaction with the performance of Awarapan 2, stating that he will congratulate the team behind Batwara 1947 for its efforts, even though the film earned significantly less than Awarapan 2. Santoshi's comments highlight the competitive nature of the Indian film industry and the pressure on directors to deliver box office hits.

Shriya Saran's praise for Awarapan 2

Actor Shriya Saran has called Awarapan 2 one of Emraan Hashmi's best films, praising the film's performances and direction. Saran's endorsement adds to the film's growing reputation and could help attract more viewers to the cinema.

Writer Bilal Siddiqi's take on criticism

Writer Bilal Siddiqi has shared his thoughts on the criticism faced by Awarapan 2, stating that he finds learnings in every opinion, whether valid or invalid. Siddiqi's perspective offers a glimpse into the creative process behind the film and the challenges faced by its makers.

What happens next?

With Awarapan 2's strong box office performance, it is likely to continue its run at the Indian box office. The film's success could also pave the way for more collaborations between Emraan Hashmi and director Rajkumar Santoshi. Additionally, the film's critical acclaim and positive word-of-mouth could help it achieve further milestones in its theatrical run.
