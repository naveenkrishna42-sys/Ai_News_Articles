Sunita Ahuja’s Sharp Reaction

Sunita Ahuja, wife of veteran actor Govinda, has taken a veiled swipe at her husband’s new co-star Komal. In a social media post, she wrote, “Vinash Kaale, Vipreet Buddhi,” a Sanskrit proverb that roughly translates to “when destruction is near, wisdom takes a reverse turn.”

The post comes days after Govinda was photographed with Komal at Mumbai airport, fuelling speculation about their professional and personal equation. Sunita’s remark has added fresh fuel to the ongoing drama surrounding the actor’s personal life.
