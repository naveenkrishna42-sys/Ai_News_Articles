Sensex Gains 300 Points, Nifty Holds Above 24,200 as IT Rally Persists

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex climbed 300 points, Nifty held above 24,200
  • IT stocks continued their rally, driving market gains
  • Markets pared some intraday gains as trading progressed

Indian equity benchmarks closed higher on July 3, with the Sensex adding about 300 points and the Nifty settling above the 24,200 mark. Gains were led by a sustained rally in information technology stocks, though broader indices pared some of their intraday advances by the close.

IT Stocks Lead the Charge

The Nifty IT index remained in focus as heavyweight software exporters extended their recent upward move. Buying interest in large-cap technology names provided the market with a firm undertone, even as other sectors showed mixed trends.

Traders said the sustained momentum in IT stocks reflects improving global demand signals and a weaker rupee, which typically benefits export-oriented technology firms. The sector has been a key driver for the benchmark indices over the past several sessions.

Markets Pare Gains in Late Trade

After touching higher levels during the morning session, the indices gave up some ground in afternoon trade as profit-taking emerged in select banking and financial stocks. However, the losses in these sectors were not enough to offset the IT gains.

The Sensex, which had risen more than 350 points at one stage, closed with a gain of around 300 points. The Nifty, after crossing the 24,250 mark, settled just above 24,200, indicating some resistance at higher levels.

Broader Market and Sectoral Trends

Midcap and smallcap indices traded mixed, with selective buying seen in auto and pharma counters. Meanwhile, metal and energy stocks remained subdued, capping the overall upside.

Market breadth was slightly positive, with advances outpacing declines on the BSE. Foreign institutional investors have remained net buyers in recent sessions, providing additional support to the market sentiment.

What to Watch

Investors will now track global cues, particularly US Federal Reserve commentary and oil price movements, for further direction. The upcoming quarterly earnings season will also be a key catalyst for stock-specific action.

Analysts expect the market to consolidate around current levels in the near term, with IT stocks likely to remain in the spotlight as long as the global demand outlook stays favourable.
