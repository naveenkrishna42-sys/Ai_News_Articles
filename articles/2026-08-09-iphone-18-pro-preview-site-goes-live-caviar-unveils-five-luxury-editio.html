iPhone 18 Pro preview site goes live; Caviar unveils five luxury editions

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iPhone 18 Pro listed on preview website, hinting at imminent launch.
  • Caviar introduces five limited-edition iPhone 18 Pro designs.
  • Reports suggest September release driven by strong demand; A20 Pro chip expected.

Apple's next-generation iPhone 18 Pro has surfaced on a preview website, fueling speculation that the company is gearing up for an official announcement. The listing, spotted by Notebookcheck, comes weeks before the expected September launch, which reports say is being pulled forward by robust consumer demand.

In a parallel development, luxury accessories maker Caviar has unveiled five limited-edition iPhone 18 Pro models. The designs, known for their extravagant materials and steep price tags, target collectors who treat smartphones as status symbols rather than mere communication devices.

Preview site hints at imminent release

The preview website, though not officially affiliated with Apple, typically appears shortly before a new product cycle begins. It lists the iPhone 18 Pro with specifications that align with earlier leaks, including a titanium frame and an upgraded camera system. However, Apple has not confirmed any details, and the company traditionally remains silent until its own keynote.

Industry watchers interpret the timing as a sign that production has hit full stride. According to Forbes, the iPhone 18 Pro launch is likely to occur in September, with pre-orders opening within days of the announcement. The report cites supply chain sources indicating that Apple has increased component orders to meet anticipated demand, particularly in India and other emerging markets.

Caviar's five limited editions

Caviar, known for transforming iPhones into ornate pieces, has revealed five new models based on the iPhone 18 Pro. Each edition features distinct themes, ranging from gold-plated finishes to intricate engravings inspired by art and architecture. The company has not yet disclosed pricing, but previous Caviar editions have sold for lakhs of rupees, making them aspirational products for a niche clientele.

The move underscores a broader trend: luxury brands are increasingly collaborating with tech giants to create premium variants. For Caviar, the iPhone 18 Pro's rumoured design changes—including a possible buttonless frame—offer a fresh canvas for its artisans. The company has not commented on whether these editions will be available in India, but its global shipping policy suggests they will be.

Expected specs and India pricing

Leaks from TelecomTalk suggest the iPhone 18 Pro Max will feature the A20 Pro chip, a significant upgrade over the current A19 series. The chip is expected to deliver faster neural processing for on-device AI tasks, a key selling point for Apple. Camera improvements, including a periscope lens with higher optical zoom, are also rumoured.

The Economic Times has reported that the iPhone 18 Pro could start at around Rs 1,34,900 for the base variant, though official pricing will only be confirmed at launch. In India, Apple's recent price adjustments to MacBooks and iPads have raised concerns about similar hikes for iPhones. However, analysts believe that local manufacturing under the PLI scheme could offset import duties, keeping prices competitive.

What to watch

As September approaches, all eyes will be on Apple's official announcement, expected within the next few weeks. The company will likely confirm the iPhone 18 Pro's features, pricing, and availability during its annual keynote. For now, the preview site and Caviar's unveiling have set the stage for what promises to be a busy launch season.
