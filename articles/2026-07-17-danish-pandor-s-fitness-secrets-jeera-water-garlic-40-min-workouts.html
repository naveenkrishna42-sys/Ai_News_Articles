Danish Pandor's Fitness Secrets: Jeera Water, Garlic, 40-Min Workouts

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Health Original source ↗
Key points
  • Danish Pandor starts his day with jeera water and a raw garlic clove for digestion and immunity.
  • He follows a disciplined 40-minute workout routine combining cardio and strength training.
  • The actor avoids processed foods and focuses on home-cooked meals to maintain his physique.

Morning Ritual: Jeera Water and a Garlic Clove

For Dhurandhar star Danish Pandor, the day begins not with a cup of chai but with a glass of jeera water and a raw garlic clove. The actor swears by this simple combination for kickstarting his metabolism and boosting immunity.

Jeera water, made by soaking cumin seeds overnight, is known for aiding digestion. The raw garlic clove, though pungent, is believed to have antibacterial properties. Pandor has been following this routine for years and credits it for keeping him energetic through long shooting schedules.

40 Minutes of Focused Work

Pandor does not believe in spending hours at the gym. Instead, he dedicates exactly 40 minutes each day to a mix of cardio and strength training. "Consistency matters more than duration," he has said in interviews.

His workout includes bodyweight exercises like push-ups and squats, followed by light weight training. He also includes a short burst of high-intensity interval training to keep his heart rate up. The actor avoids overtraining, believing that rest days are essential for muscle recovery.

Diet: Home-Cooked and Simple

When it comes to food, Pandor prefers home-cooked meals over restaurant fare. His diet includes plenty of vegetables, dal, and roti. He avoids fried foods and sugary drinks.

The actor also drinks at least three litres of water throughout the day. He snacks on nuts and fruits when hungry between meals. "I don't follow any fancy diet plan. I just eat what my mother cooks, but in controlled portions," he says.

The Role of Discipline

According to Pandor, discipline is the key to maintaining his physique. He admits that staying fit while shooting for long hours is challenging, but he plans his meals and workouts in advance.

He also emphasizes the importance of sleep. The actor ensures he gets at least seven hours of rest each night, as he believes it helps his body recover from the daily grind.

What's Next for Danish Pandor?

With the success of Dhurandhar, Pandor is currently working on his next project, details of which are yet to be announced. He continues to stick to his fitness routine, hoping to inspire his fans to adopt a healthier lifestyle.
