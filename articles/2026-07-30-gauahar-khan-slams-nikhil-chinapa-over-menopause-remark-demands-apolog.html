Gauahar Khan Slams Nikhil Chinapa Over Menopause Remark, Demands Apology

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Gauahar Khan criticized Nikhil Chinapa for a comment about menopause and perimenopause.
  • She called the remark 'ridiculous' and demanded an apology from Chinapa.
  • Khan's brother-in-law, Awez Darbar, also slammed Chinapa, suggesting he needs a 'pause'.
  • Khan emphasized she had a baby nine months ago, highlighting the insensitivity of the remark.

Actress Gauahar Khan has publicly called out DJ and television host Nikhil Chinapa over a remark he made about menopause and perimenopause. In a sharp social media post, Khan demanded an apology, calling the comment 'ridiculous' and deeply insensitive.

The Remark That Sparked Outrage

The exact context of Chinapa's comment remains unclear, but Khan's response made her displeasure evident. She took to social media platform X (formerly Twitter) to express her anger.

'You owe women an apology. That is ridiculous,' Khan wrote, directly tagging Chinapa. Her post quickly gained traction, with thousands of users supporting her stance.

Family Joins the Fray

Khan's brother-in-law, choreographer Awez Darbar, also weighed in. In a pointed post, Darbar said, 'Aap jaise men ko pause hone ki jarurat hai' (Men like you need to be paused). The comment added a personal dimension to the backlash, amplifying the criticism against Chinapa.

A Personal Context

Khan, who gave birth to a son nine months ago, highlighted the timing of the remark. 'I had a baby 9 months ago. Menopause is a natural phase, not a joke,' she added in subsequent posts, underscoring why such comments hurt women, especially those navigating postpartum life.

Perimenopause and menopause are biological realities for millions of women, yet they remain taboo in public discourse. Khan's response has reignited conversations about respect and sensitivity around women's health issues.

Industry Reactions

Several celebrities and fans have rallied behind Khan. Many called Chinapa's remark 'tone-deaf' and 'outdated.' Neither Chinapa nor his representatives have issued a statement so far. The silence has only fueled the debate.

This is not the first time a public figure has faced backlash for comments on women's health. In recent years, similar remarks by comedians and influencers have sparked widespread criticism, leading to apologies and public awareness campaigns.

What Comes Next

As the online storm continues, all eyes are on Nikhil Chinapa for a response. Whether he will apologize or clarify his remarks remains uncertain. The episode serves as a reminder that casual remarks about women's bodies carry weight — and that public figures are increasingly being held accountable for them.
