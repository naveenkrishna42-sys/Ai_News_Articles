Hindu Group Issues Advisory to Mamdani Ahead of Bhagwat's NYC Event

A Hindu group has issued an advisory to Zohran Mamdani, reminding him of his 'sworn duty' ahead of RSS chief Mohan Bhagwat's upcoming event in New York City. The advisory comes amid growing concerns and recent developments related to Bhagwat's visit to the United States.

X Account of Event Organiser Suspended

The X account of AHEAD, the organiser of Bhagwat's event, was suspended ahead of the programme. This suspension has raised eyebrows and sparked discussions about the implications for free speech and the rights of organisations hosting events in the United States.

Panel Urges Revocation of Bhagwat's US Visa

A panel on religious freedom has urged the Trump government to revoke the US visa of RSS chief Mohan Bhagwat. The panel's recommendation highlights the growing concerns about Bhagwat's visit to the United States and the potential impact on religious freedom and interfaith relations.
