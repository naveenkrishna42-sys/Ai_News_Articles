Brazil to play Australia, India in first post-World Cup friendlies

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Brazil will play India in Kolkata in their first international break after the 2026 World Cup.
  • The five-time world champions will also face Australia in a separate friendly.
  • The match against India will be Brazil's first-ever visit to Kolkata for a friendly.
  • The All India Football Federation (AIFF) has confirmed the historic friendly.
  • The match is expected to be hosted at the iconic Salt Lake Stadium.

Brazil's post-World Cup schedule set

Brazil will play Australia and India in their first international matches after the 2026 FIFA World Cup. The five-time world champions have confirmed the fixtures for their first international break following the tournament.

The two matches are part of Brazil's preparations for the next cycle of international football. The exact dates are yet to be announced.

Historic Kolkata friendly confirmed

The All India Football Federation (AIFF) has confirmed that India will host Brazil in a friendly in Kolkata. The match will be a landmark event for Indian football.

This will be Brazil's first match on Indian soil. The iconic Salt Lake Stadium is expected to host the fixture.

Indian football fans have long awaited a clash with the five-time world champions. The match has been described by the AIFF as a date for the history books.

Details of the fixtures

Brazil will also play Australia as part of the same international break. The match against the Socceroos will be another high-profile friendly for the South American giants.

Both matches are expected to be played in September 2026, shortly after the conclusion of the World Cup. The Brazilian Football Confederation (CBF) has not yet released the full schedule.

The match against India will be a significant logistical undertaking. Kolkata's Salt Lake Stadium has a capacity of over 1.2 lakh spectators.

What this means for Indian football

Hosting Brazil is a major coup for the AIFF and Indian football. It provides a rare opportunity for the Indian national team to test itself against the best in the world.

India's current FIFA ranking is far below Brazil's. The match will be a massive learning experience for the players and coaching staff.

The financial and promotional benefits are also substantial. The match is expected to generate significant revenue and global attention for Indian football.

Brazil's World Cup 2026 performance

Brazil's performance at the 2026 World Cup will determine the context of these friendlies. If they perform well, the matches will carry added prestige.

If they underperform, the friendlies will be seen as a rebuilding exercise. Either way, the matches offer valuable game time for Brazil's squad.

The CBF has not indicated whether a full-strength squad will travel to India. However, even a second-string Brazil side would be a formidable opponent.

Australian and Indian fans will be watching closely. The matches are a rare chance to see Brazil play in their regions outside of major tournaments.

Officials have not yet confirmed ticket prices or broadcast details for the India match. Those announcements are expected in the coming weeks.
