Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta launch Open Secure AI Alliance for safety

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta, IBM, Cisco and 30+ firms launch Open Secure AI Alliance.
  • Alliance aims to set safety and security standards for open-weight AI models.
  • Move follows concerns over rogue AI models and cyber-security risks flagged by Anthropic CEO.
  • Alliance seen as a pushback against calls for stricter regulation of open-source AI.

Tech Giants Join Forces for AI Safety

Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta, and more than 30 other technology companies have announced the formation of the Open Secure AI Alliance. The group's goal is to establish common safety and security standards for open-weight artificial intelligence models.

The alliance includes major players like IBM and Cisco, and is being led by Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. The announcement was made public on Tuesday.

Why Now? The Rogue AI Threat

Huang cited the risk of rogue AI models causing severe damage as a key driver. He pointed to a recent incident where a hacked AI system allegedly compromised a multi-billion dollar company. "This is why we are bringing together the biggest names in tech," Huang said in a statement.

The move comes amid a heated debate on the safety of open-weight models. These AI systems, whose underlying code is publicly available, can be modified and deployed by anyone. Critics argue they pose a serious cyber-security risk.

The Anthropic CEO's Warning

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei has publicly disagreed with the push for open-weight models. He has warned that such systems could be weaponised by malicious actors, including state-sponsored groups. Amodei has specifically flagged risks related to China's rapid advances in AI development.

The Hindu and NDTV have reported that Amodei's stark warnings have added urgency to the industry's efforts. The new alliance is seen as a direct response to these concerns, aiming to build safeguards without halting innovation.

What the Alliance Will Do

The Open Secure AI Alliance will focus on creating a shared framework for testing and verifying the security of open-weight AI models. This includes developing tools to detect vulnerabilities and prevent misuse.

Members will collaborate on best practices for model deployment. The group expects to release its first set of guidelines within the next six months. Nvidia, Microsoft, and Meta have already committed significant engineering resources to the initiative.

A Shift in the Industry Debate

The formation of this alliance signals a shift. Until now, the debate over open-source AI has been polarised between advocates of complete openness and proponents of strict control. This group attempts to find a middle ground — keeping models open but with built-in safety measures.

Industry observers note that the alliance includes both hardware makers like Nvidia and software giants like Microsoft. This broad base suggests the initiative has strong backing across the tech ecosystem.

Details on how the alliance will enforce its standards are still being worked out. Officials from Nvidia have not yet confirmed whether non-compliance would lead to expulsion from the group.

What to watch next: The alliance is expected to hold its first public summit in early 2027. The key test will be whether its guidelines are adopted by smaller AI developers and open-source communities who value unrestricted access.
