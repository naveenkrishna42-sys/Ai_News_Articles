Ukraine drone strike kills 13 in Russia's Tatarstan, officials say

📅 2026-08-10 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • At least 13 killed, including a child, in drone strike on Nizhnekamsk
  • Officials say it is one of the deadliest Ukrainian attacks inside Russia in two years
  • Ukraine confirms it struck an oil refinery in Tatarstan

At least 13 people were killed, including a child, when Ukrainian drones struck the Russian city of Nizhnekamsk in the Tatarstan region, according to Russian officials. The attack, which targeted an oil refinery, is being described as one of the deadliest Ukrainian strikes inside Russia in two years.

The strikes hit early on Monday, with local authorities reporting multiple explosions near the refinery complex. Emergency services were deployed to the scene, and rescue operations continued through the morning.

Deadliest attack in two years

Russian officials said the death toll included a child, though details about the victims have not been fully released. The attack marks a significant escalation in the conflict, with the death toll exceeding that of previous Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian soil.

Regional authorities in Tatarstan have not yet confirmed the full extent of damage to the refinery, which is one of Russia's major fuel production facilities. The strike could have implications for fuel supplies in the region.

Ukraine confirms refinery strike

Ukraine has confirmed that it carried out strikes on the oil refinery in Tatarstan, saying the facility was a legitimate military target. Kyiv has increasingly targeted Russian energy infrastructure in recent months, aiming to disrupt fuel supplies that support Moscow's war effort.

The confirmation came through official channels, though Ukrainian authorities did not provide further details on the operation or the reported casualties.

Local response and international reaction

Russian officials have vowed to respond to the attack, with some lawmakers calling for increased air defense measures in the region. The Tatarstan government has declared a period of mourning for the victims, though official announcements are still pending.

International reactions have been muted so far, with no major statements from global leaders. The attack is likely to add further tension to an already volatile situation, as diplomatic efforts to end the conflict remain stalled.

Residents in Nizhnekamsk have been urged to stay indoors as investigations continue. The full casualty count may still rise, officials have warned, as search teams comb through the wreckage.

What to watch next

As Russia assesses the damage and considers its response, the international community will be watching closely for any escalation. More details on the victims and the refinery's status are expected in the coming hours.
