Sensex drops 650 pts from day's high, Nifty near 24,500: Key triggers

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex slips 650 points from intraday high, Nifty hovers near 24,500
  • Weak global cues, selling in financials and IT stocks drag indices
  • Investors await RBI policy outcome for near-term direction

Indian equity benchmarks gave up early gains and slipped sharply into the red on Wednesday, with the Sensex trading 650 points below its day's high and the Nifty hovering near the 24,500 mark. The sudden reversal caught many traders off guard, as the market had opened on a firm footing before losing momentum through the morning session.

At the time of writing, the Sensex was down around 210 points from its previous close, while the Nifty struggled to hold above the psychological 24,500 level. Volatility remained elevated as investors squared off positions ahead of the Reserve Bank of India's monetary policy decision due later this week.

Weak global cues weigh on sentiment

Global markets provided little support, with Asian peers trading mixed and US futures pointing to a subdued start. Persistent concerns over slowing economic growth and uncertainty around interest rate trajectories continued to weigh on risk appetite across the region.

Foreign institutional investors have been cautious in recent sessions, and their selling pressure in cash markets added to the domestic weakness. Analysts noted that any escalation in global trade tensions could further dampen sentiment in the near term.

Financials and IT stocks lead the decline

Selling pressure was visible across banking, financial services and information technology stocks, which together account for a significant weightage in the benchmark indices. Profit-taking in heavyweight names dragged the indices lower, with several frontline counters trading near their session lows.

Market participants pointed to profit booking after recent gains, as well as concerns over rich valuations in certain pockets. The broader market also witnessed selling, with midcap and smallcap indices trading in the red, reflecting weak breadth.

RBI policy outcome in focus

Traders are now awaiting the RBI's policy announcement, which is expected to provide clarity on the central bank's stance on interest rates and liquidity. The outcome is likely to influence market direction in the coming sessions, and any hawkish commentary could add to the downside pressure.

Meanwhile, the rupee's movement against the dollar and crude oil prices remain key monitorables for the market. A sharp move in either could alter the near-term trajectory for equities.

What to watch next

With the RBI decision just a day away, volatility is likely to remain elevated. Analysts advise caution and recommend focusing on stock-specific action rather than chasing the index in either direction.

Investors will also track global cues, particularly US inflation data and any fresh developments on the trade front, for further direction. The market's ability to hold the 24,500 level on the Nifty could determine whether the current pullback extends or attracts buying interest at lower levels.
