Spider-Man Brand New Day presales cross Rs 60 crore in India, target Endgame records

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Presales for Spider-Man: Brand New Day have exceeded Rs 60 crore in India.
  • Trade analysts predict a day one collection of Rs 45 crore, twice that of The Odyssey.
  • Ticket prices have soared past Rs 3,700 in some Indian markets.
  • Critics have hailed the film as Tom Holland's best Spider-Man outing.
  • The film is also performing strongly in China ahead of its release.

Presale frenzy hits new high

Spider-Man: Brand New Day has set the box office on fire before its first frame even rolls. Presales in India have crossed the Rs 60 crore mark, according to trade reports. The number has taken the industry by surprise, with many comparing the advance booking rush to the build-up for Avengers: Endgame.

Ticket prices in some premium multiplexes have shot past Rs 3,700. Fans are snapping up IMAX and 4DX shows days in advance. The demand has forced several chains to add extra screenings past midnight.

Day one estimates point to record opening

Trade analysts now predict a day one collection of Rs 45 crore for the Tom Holland starrer. That figure would be roughly double the opening day of The Odyssey, another major Hollywood release this season. If the estimates hold, Brand New Day will post one of the biggest single-day numbers ever for a Hollywood film in India.

The Rs 45 crore projection is not just optimism. Exhibitors across metros report near-full advance bookings for Friday shows. The film is expected to dominate the weekend with strong word-of-mouth already building.

Critics call it Tom Holland's best

Early reviews have been overwhelmingly positive. Critics have described Brand New Day as Tom Holland's strongest outing in the red-and-blue suit. One reviewer said they were 'shocked at how good this is'. Another called it a 'masterpiece of superhero storytelling'.

The film's emotional depth and action choreography have both drawn praise. Holland's performance is being singled out as more mature and layered than in previous Spider-Man films. The supporting cast has also received strong notices.

China adds to global momentum

Brand New Day is not just a monster in India. The film has opened strongly in China as well, with presales and early screenings showing robust numbers. That market alone could push the global opening weekend well past the billion-dollar conversation.

With India and China both firing, the film is on track to become one of the highest-grossing Hollywood releases of the year. The combination of fan loyalty, critical acclaim and massive screen count gives it a long runway.

What to watch next

All eyes are now on the actual opening day figures on Friday. If walk-in audiences match the presale frenzy, Brand New Day could very well challenge Avengers: Endgame's all-time records in India. The next 72 hours will tell if Spider-Man truly swings into history.
