One dead, five injured in Swiss rave shooting

A shooting at a rave party attended by 3,500 people in Switzerland has left one dead and five injured. The incident occurred at a nightclub in the city of Zurich, with reports indicating that several victims were shot. The gunman is still at large, and a manhunt is underway.

Police suspect terrorism motive

Swiss police have indicated that they are investigating the shooting with the suspicion that it may be terrorism-related. This suspicion has raised concerns among security officials and the public alike, as Switzerland is known for its strict security measures and low terrorism incidents.

Manhunt underway in Switzerland
