Ranbir Kapoor Reveals Final Face-Off With Yash In Ramayana Not Yet Shot

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ranbir Kapoor confirmed the final face-off with Yash in 'Ramayana' is yet to be shot.
  • Yash revealed he used large speakers to get into the character of Ravana.
  • The film created a buzz at San Diego Comic-Con 2026, with exclusive gold shankha gifts for attendees.

The much-anticipated Nitesh Tiwari directorial 'Ramayana' is still in the middle of production. Ranbir Kapoor, who plays Lord Ram, has revealed that the most crucial sequence of the film remains unfinished. The final confrontation between his character and Yash's Ravana has not yet been shot.

Speaking about the film's progress, Kapoor indicated that the team is saving the best for last. The actor's statement has only heightened curiosity around the scale and intensity of the climax.

Yash's Unique Preparation For Ravana

Yash, known for his towering screen presence in the 'KGF' franchise, has been equally candid about his process. The actor recently explained why he used large speakers while preparing for the role of the demon king.

According to Yash, the booming sound helped him channel the required energy and gravitas. He admitted that at one point, the volume got so loud that it 'spoiled the whole atmosphere' for those around him. The actor's dedication to embodying Ravana's complex persona has been a talking point among fans.

SDCC 2026 Creates Frenzy

The 'Ramayana' team made a splash at the San Diego Comic-Con 2026. A viral video from the event showed exclusive gold shankha (conch) gifts being handed out to attendees at the trailer launch. The opulent promotional item has sparked widespread discussion online.

Fans who attended the event shared unboxing videos, showcasing the stunning craftsmanship of the souvenir. The move underscores the film's ambitious marketing strategy, which aims to match the epic scale of the source material.

What To Expect From The Epic

Starring Ranbir Kapoor, Yash, and Sai Pallavi as Sita, 'Ramayana' is one of the most expensive Indian films ever produced. The project has been in the works for several years, with expectations running high for its visual effects and storytelling.

While the cast has remained tight-lipped about specific plot details, the recent updates confirm that the team is leaving no stone unturned. With the final face-off still pending, the production schedule suggests a release date further down the line.

As the shoot progresses, audiences can expect more glimpses into the making of this grand retelling. The film's promotional blitzkrieg is only just beginning.
