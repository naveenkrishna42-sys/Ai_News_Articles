Ranveer Singh: From Don 3 Backlash to Dhurandhar, a Career in Transition

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ranveer Singh's casting in Don 3 has sparked online backlash from fans of the original franchise.
  • His recent film Dhurandhar received mixed reviews and underperformed at the box office.
  • Singh's personal life, including his marriage to Deepika Padukone, remains a subject of public interest.
  • The actor is known for his energetic performances and unconventional fashion choices.

Bollywood star Ranveer Singh finds himself in the middle of two storms. One is the controversy surrounding his casting as the lead in the next installment of the Don franchise. The other is the tepid response to his latest film, Dhurandhar.

The actor, known for his high-voltage energy, is navigating a rare phase of public scrutiny. Both his professional choices and his personal brand are being tested.

The Don 3 Backlash

News of Singh stepping into the iconic role of Don, previously played by Shah Rukh Khan and before him, Amitabh Bachchan, was met with immediate resistance online. Fans of the franchise have questioned whether he fits the part.

Critics argue the role requires a suave, understated menace — a stark contrast to Singh's often flamboyant on-screen persona. The actor has not yet publicly addressed the backlash.

Dhurandhar's Lukewarm Reception

Singh's recent action drama Dhurandhar hit theatres to mixed reviews. While his performance was praised in parts, the film's script and direction drew criticism.

The box office numbers have been underwhelming for a star of his stature. Industry observers are watching to see if the film finds a second life on streaming platforms.

Beyond the Headlines: Wife, Image, and Facts

Away from work, Singh remains a fixture in tabloids. His marriage to actress Deepika Padukone is one of Bollywood's most talked-about relationships. The couple frequently makes public appearances together.

Singh's fashion choices — from floral suits to bold accessories — have made him a style icon for some and a subject of mockery for others. He is also known for his method-acting preparation, often staying in character during film shoots.

Born into a family with roots in the film and auto parts industries, Singh made his debut in 2010 with Band Baaja Baaraat. He has since delivered hits like Gully Boy and Padmaavat, earning multiple awards.

The Road Ahead

All eyes are now on Singh's next moves. He has several projects lined up, though official announcements remain pending.

How he handles the Don 3 controversy and whether Dhurandhar recovers its investment will shape the narrative around him in the coming months. The actor has often reinvented himself — this might be his toughest turnaround yet.
