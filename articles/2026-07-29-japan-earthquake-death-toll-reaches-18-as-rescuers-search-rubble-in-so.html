Japan Earthquake: Death Toll Reaches 18 as Rescuers Search Rubble in Southwest

📅 2026-07-29 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Death toll rises to 18 after a powerful earthquake in southwestern Japan.
  • More than 100 aftershocks have been recorded since the initial quake.
  • Rescue operations are ongoing as teams search through collapsed structures.

Death Toll Rises to 18

At least 18 people are confirmed dead after a powerful earthquake struck southwestern Japan, officials said on Wednesday. Rescue crews are racing against time, combing through the rubble of collapsed buildings in search of survivors.

The quake, which hit late Tuesday, caused widespread destruction in several towns. Authorities have not yet released a final casualty count, warning that the number could climb as search operations continue.

Over 100 Aftershocks Rattle the Region

Since the initial tremor, more than 100 aftershocks have been recorded, according to the Japan Meteorological Agency. Many of these tremors were strong enough to rattle nerves and complicate rescue efforts.

Residents have been advised to stay alert for further seismic activity. Officials are warning that the risk of landslides and further building collapses remains high.

Rescue Operations Underway

Emergency teams, including firefighters and soldiers, are working through debris in the hardest-hit areas. Heavy machinery has been deployed to lift concrete slabs and twisted metal, while sniffer dogs aid in the search for trapped victims.

Local hospitals are treating dozens of injured people. Medical staff have reported a range of injuries, from cuts and bruises to more serious trauma caused by falling structures.

Shopping Mall Disaster Adds to the Tragedy

In one devastating incident, a shopping mall partially collapsed following the quake. Reports indicate that a secondary blast occurred at the site, compounding the destruction. Details of what caused the explosion remain unclear.

Rescuers have been focusing efforts on this location, where several people are feared trapped. The mall was crowded at the time of the earthquake, though officials have not confirmed the number of casualties linked specifically to this event.

Government Response and Public Caution

Prime Minister's office has set up an emergency task force to coordinate relief and rescue operations. Authorities are urging people to avoid non-essential travel to the affected zones to keep roads clear for emergency vehicles.

Shelters have been opened for those displaced from their homes. Power and water supply have been disrupted in several areas, and restoration work is ongoing.

As the region endures continuous aftershocks, the focus remains on finding survivors. Officials have stated that the next 48 hours are critical for rescue efforts.
