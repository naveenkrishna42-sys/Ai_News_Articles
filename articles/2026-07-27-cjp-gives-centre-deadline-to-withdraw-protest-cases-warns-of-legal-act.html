CJP gives Centre deadline to withdraw protest cases, warns of legal action

📅 2026-07-27 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • CJP has issued an ultimatum to the Centre to withdraw cases against protesters.
  • The organisation warns of legal action and fresh protests if demands are not met.
  • Security remains tight in central Delhi amid the ongoing standoff.

The Coordination of Joint Protests (CJP) has given the central government a clear deadline to scrap all cases registered against protesters. The organisation has warned that failure to comply will lead to legal action and a resumption of demonstrations.

The CJP claims the government has breached an earlier agreement. The warning comes amid ongoing police action and heightened security in central Delhi.

Ultimatum Over Withdrawal of FIRs

The CJP has demanded the withdrawal of all First Information Reports (FIRs) filed against those who participated in recent protests. The organisation states that the government had previously promised to drop these cases.

“Withdraw cases or protests resume tomorrow,” a CJP representative said. The group has accused the Centre of breaking its word, calling the continued police action a violation of the deal struck earlier.

Legal Action and Renewed Protests

If the government does not meet the deadline, the CJP has threatened to move the courts. Legal recourse is being prepared, the organisation confirmed, alongside plans for fresh protests.

“The government is breaching the deal. We will protest again,” the CJP warned. The group has urged its supporters to be ready for a renewed agitation if the FIRs are not withdrawn within the stipulated time.

Security Tightened in Central Delhi

Authorities have not yet confirmed the exact number of cases involved or the specifics of the alleged agreement. Officials are tight-lipped about the next steps.

Security has been significantly tightened in central Delhi, particularly around key government buildings and protest sites. Police personnel have been deployed in large numbers to prevent any untoward incident. The situation remains tense as the deadline approaches.

What to Watch

The coming hours will determine whether the CJP’s ultimatum leads to a de-escalation or a fresh round of protests. All eyes are on the government’s response and the courts, should the CJP follow through on its legal threat.
