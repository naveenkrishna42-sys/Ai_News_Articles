SC Affirms Quashing Of FIR Against Naidu

📅 2026-08-14 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Supreme Court dismisses plea against Andhra HC order
  • FIR against CM Chandrababu Naidu and ex-minister quashed
  • Amaravati land pooling case not interfered with by SC

Supreme Court Decision

The Supreme Court has affirmed the quashing of an FIR against Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister Chandrababu Naidu and a former minister over the Amaravati land pooling case. The court dismissed a plea against the Andhra Pradesh High Court's order quashing the case.

Background of the Case

The case pertains to the land pooling scheme implemented for the development of Amaravati, the new capital of Andhra Pradesh. The scheme involved the acquisition of land from farmers, which was then pooled and redistributed. However, allegations of irregularities and corruption surfaced, leading to the registration of an FIR against CM Naidu and the former minister.

The Andhra Pradesh High Court had quashed the FIR, and the Supreme Court has now upheld this order. The apex court's decision implies that the criminal charges against CM Naidu and the former minister will not be pursued.

Reactions and Implications

The Supreme Court's decision is likely to have significant implications for the political landscape of Andhra Pradesh. The case had been a major point of contention between the ruling and opposition parties in the state. With the FIR quashed, CM Naidu and his government may breathe a sigh of relief.

Next Steps

While the Supreme Court's decision brings an end to the legal proceedings in this case, it remains to be seen how the development of Amaravati will proceed. The land pooling scheme has been a crucial aspect of the capital city's development, and any changes to the scheme may impact the project's timeline and budget.

Some of the key questions that arise from the Supreme Court's decision include:

  • How will the Andhra Pradesh government proceed with the development of Amaravati?
  • Will the quashing of the FIR have any impact on the opposition parties' stance on the issue?
  • What are the implications of the Supreme Court's decision for the farmers who had given up their land for the project?

The answers to these questions will become clearer in the coming days and weeks as the situation unfolds.
