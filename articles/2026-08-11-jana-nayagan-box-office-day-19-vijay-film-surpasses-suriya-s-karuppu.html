Jana Nayagan Box Office Day 19: Vijay Film Surpasses Suriya's Karuppu

Key points
  • Jana Nayagan overtakes Suriya's Karuppu as top Tamil grosser of 2026.
  • Film collects Rs 7.25 crore in third weekend, total reaches Rs 223 crore in 17 days.
  • Day 19 sees lowest single-day collection since release, but overall trend remains strong.

Jana Nayagan Overtakes Karuppu at the Box Office

Thalapathy Vijay's Jana Nayagan has edged past Suriya's Karuppu to become the highest-grossing Tamil film of 2026. The action-drama, co-starring Pooja Hegde, achieved the milestone in its third weekend, adding Rs 7.25 crore to its kitty.

The film's total now stands at Rs 223 crore gross in 17 days of release. Trade analysts point to strong urban turnout and repeat viewings as key drivers, though the lead over Karuppu is slim, keeping the race competitive.

Weekend Growth and Day 19 Dip

According to early estimates, Jana Nayagan recorded a 41% weekend growth compared to its second Friday, a healthy sign for a film in its third week. The jump was powered by holiday screenings and family audiences in Tamil Nadu and among the diaspora.

However, day 19 saw the film's lowest single-day collection since release, a pattern typical for big-ticket films post-festival rush. Despite the dip, the cumulative earnings remain robust, with the film holding steady in multiplexes and single screens alike.

Vijay's Star Power and Pooja Hegde's Role

Vijay, fresh off a pan-Indian release strategy, continues to draw crowds across centres. Pooja Hegde's presence has added to the film's appeal in urban belts, though her screen time remains limited compared to the male lead.

The film's music and action sequences have been widely discussed on social media, contributing to sustained buzz. Distributors in Tamil Nadu expect the film to run through the month, barring new competition from upcoming releases.

What Lies Ahead for the Box Office Race

With Karuppu still showing legs in select circuits, the gap between the two films could narrow or widen depending on weekday footfalls. Trade observers are watching whether Jana Nayagan can cross the Rs 250-crore mark in its lifetime run.

For now, Vijay's film enjoys the top slot, but the battle for 2026's biggest Tamil grosser is far from over. The coming week will determine if the momentum holds or fades as new releases hit screens.
