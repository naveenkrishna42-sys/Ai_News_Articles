India-Afghanistan T20I series confirmed, Delhi to host all three matches

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Delhi will host all three T20I matches between India and Afghanistan.
  • The series is scheduled before the Asian Games, serving as preparation for both teams.
  • BCCI and ACB have officially confirmed the dates after approval from the Indian board.

Series Finalised for Capital

The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) and the Afghanistan Cricket Board (ACB) have formally confirmed a three-match T20I series, with all games set to be played in Delhi. The announcement comes ahead of the Asian Games, giving both sides a competitive tune-up on Indian soil.

According to officials, the BCCI has greenlit the fixtures, and the ACB has accepted the proposed dates. The series will mark another chapter in the growing bilateral cricketing relationship between the two nations, which has seen Afghanistan play several home matches in India due to security concerns at home.

Delhi as the Venue

All three matches will be held at the Arun Jaitley Stadium, a venue that has historically hosted high-voltage encounters. The choice of Delhi underscores the capital's readiness to stage international cricket, with the stadium having undergone infrastructure upgrades in recent years.

For Afghanistan, playing in India is almost akin to a home away from home, given the passionate Afghan fan base in the country and the familiarity with Indian conditions. The series is expected to draw significant crowds, with fans from both communities expected to fill the stands.

Preparation for Asian Games

The series is strategically placed in the calendar to serve as preparation for the Asian Games, where both India and Afghanistan will field their T20I squads. For India, this is an opportunity to test bench strength and give younger players exposure to international pressure ahead of a multi-sport event.

Afghanistan, on the other hand, will look to fine-tune their combinations and build momentum. The team has shown rapid progress in white-ball cricket, and a competitive series against a top-tier side like India will be invaluable for their confidence.

Diplomatic and Sporting Significance

The confirmation of the series also carries diplomatic weight. Cricket has often served as a bridge between nations, and this bilateral engagement comes at a time when Afghanistan's sporting ties with several countries have faced logistical hurdles. The BCCI's willingness to host the series reinforces India's role as a cricketing ally.

Meanwhile, in a separate development, Bangladesh is racing to save their upcoming tour of India, with foreign ministers reportedly involved in talks. That situation remains fluid, but the India-Afghanistan series appears firmly on track.

Exact match timings and ticket details are yet to be announced, but the boards have indicated that the fixtures will be confirmed in the coming weeks. Both teams are expected to announce their squads closer to the series, with selectors likely to use the games as a final audition for the Asian Games.

As the cricketing calendar tightens, all eyes will be on Delhi in the lead-up to the series. Fans can expect a high-intensity contest that blends rivalry with camaraderie, and the outcomes could shape selections for the Asian Games squad.
