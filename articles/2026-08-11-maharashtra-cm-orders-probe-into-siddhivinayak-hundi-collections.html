Maharashtra CM orders probe into Siddhivinayak hundi collections

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • CM orders formal investigation into Siddhivinayak temple hundi collections
  • Move follows reports of discrepancies in donation counting and management
  • Temple trust yet to respond; devotees await transparency measures

Mumbai: The Maharashtra Chief Minister has ordered a formal investigation into the hundi collections at the iconic Siddhivinayak Temple in Prabhadevi, a move that could bring greater scrutiny to one of the country's richest religious institutions. The directive comes amid growing questions over the handling of donations offered by millions of devotees each year.

Officials familiar with the matter said the probe will examine the processes involved in counting, recording, and depositing the cash and valuables that pour into the temple's donation boxes. The exact scope of the investigation, however, has not been publicly detailed yet.

Why the probe was ordered

The decision follows reports of alleged discrepancies in the temple's hundi collection figures. Sources indicate that internal reviews had flagged inconsistencies between the amounts recorded and what was actually deposited, prompting the Chief Minister's office to step in.

While no formal complaint has been filed, the temple trust has been under pressure to ensure greater accountability. The Siddhivinayak Temple, dedicated to Lord Ganesha, attracts lakhs of visitors annually, and its hundi collections are believed to run into crores of rupees each year.

What the investigation will look at

The probe is expected to focus on the standard operating procedures for handling donations. This includes how the hundis are opened, the counting process, and the chain of custody until the money reaches the bank.

Investigators may also review CCTV footage and audit trails to identify any lapses. However, authorities have not confirmed whether any specific individuals are under suspicion at this stage.

The temple trust, which manages the shrine's daily operations, has not yet issued a formal statement. Efforts to reach its spokespersons were unsuccessful at the time of writing.

Broader implications for temple management

This is not the first time the Siddhivinayak Temple has faced questions over its finances. In recent years, activists and devotees have called for more transparent reporting of donations and expenses.

The investigation could set a precedent for other major temples in Maharashtra, many of which handle substantial hundi collections with varying degrees of oversight. If irregularities are found, it may lead to stricter regulations across the state.

The Chief Minister's office has indicated that the probe will be conducted expeditiously, though no timeline has been announced. The findings, once submitted, are likely to be made public, though officials have not confirmed this.

What happens next

All eyes will be on the temple trust's response in the coming days. Devotees, who place immense faith in the shrine, will be watching closely to see whether the investigation leads to concrete reforms.

The outcome could determine whether similar audits are ordered for other religious institutions in the state, making this a test case for financial accountability in India's temple ecosystem.
