Sensex jumps 350 points, Nifty ends above 24,630 on strong global cues

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex jumped over 350 points in today's session
  • Nifty50 ended above 24,630 mark
  • Gains driven by strong global cues and buying in heavyweight stocks

Mumbai: The Indian equity markets closed on a strong footing on Thursday, with the BSE Sensex surging over 350 points and the Nifty50 finishing above the 24,630 level. The rally was broad-based, with buying visible across sectors as investor sentiment got a boost from firm global cues.

The 30-share Sensex opened higher and maintained its momentum through the day, closing with gains of more than 350 points. The Nifty50, too, showed resilience, ending above the psychologically important 24,630 mark after touching an intraday high.

What drove the rally?

Market participants pointed to strong buying in heavyweight stocks, particularly in banking, IT, and auto sectors. Positive global signals, including steady Asian markets and a firm overnight close on Wall Street, helped improve risk appetite among domestic investors.

Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) were seen as net buyers in early trade, adding to the positive mood. Domestic institutional investors also remained active, providing support to the indices.

Sectoral performance

Most sectoral indices on the National Stock Exchange ended in the green. Banking stocks led the charge, with the Nifty Bank index gaining significantly. IT stocks also saw robust buying, while auto and FMCG shares contributed to the uptick.

Midcap and smallcap stocks also participated in the rally, reflecting broad-based buying interest. Analysts said the market's resilience was a positive sign, though they advised caution given global uncertainties.

Investor wealth grows

The rally added to investor wealth, with the total market capitalisation of BSE-listed companies rising sharply. According to provisional data, investor wealth increased by several lakh crore rupees during the session.

Meanwhile, the rupee held steady against the US dollar, providing further comfort to market participants. Bond yields remained range-bound, indicating stable expectations on interest rates.

Outlook

Going ahead, traders will watch for global cues, including US inflation data and central bank commentary. Domestic factors such as monsoon progress and corporate earnings will also guide market direction in the coming sessions.

Analysts suggest that the market may consolidate at higher levels, with stock-specific action likely to continue.
