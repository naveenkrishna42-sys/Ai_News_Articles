Bitcoin holds below $65k as US payrolls data awaited

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin remained little changed below $65,000 in early Friday trading.
  • Investors are focused on upcoming US payrolls data for Federal Reserve policy signals.
  • Crypto market sentiment is cautious ahead of the release.

Bitcoin hovered just below the $65,000 mark on Friday, with trading volumes thin as investors braced for the release of US payrolls data. The cryptocurrency has been rangebound for the past several sessions, unable to break decisively above the key psychological level.

At the time of writing, Bitcoin was trading at approximately $64,800, down marginally over the past 24 hours. The lack of directional momentum reflects a broader wait-and-see approach across risk assets, as traders position for the monthly jobs report due later in the day.

Payrolls data in focus

The US non-farm payrolls report is expected to offer fresh clues on the health of the labour market, which in turn could influence the Federal Reserve's interest rate trajectory. Strong job numbers could reinforce expectations of tighter monetary policy, while weak data might revive bets on rate cuts.

For Bitcoin, the stakes are high. The digital asset has shown sensitivity to macroeconomic data in recent months, especially to any signals that could alter liquidity conditions. A hotter-than-expected payrolls figure could push Bitcoin lower, while a softer print might provide the catalyst for a breakout above $65,000.

Market positioning and sentiment

Derivatives markets suggest traders are bracing for volatility. Open interest in Bitcoin futures remains elevated, and options markets are pricing in larger-than-usual swings following the data release. However, spot volumes have been subdued, indicating that many institutional players are sitting on the sidelines.

On-chain metrics paint a mixed picture. While long-term holders continue to accumulate, short-term traders appear hesitant, with exchange inflows and outflows showing no clear trend. This cautious stance is reflected in the broader crypto market, where major altcoins like Ethereum and Solana have also traded within narrow ranges.

Analyst views and resistance levels

Technical analysts point to $65,000 as a crucial resistance level for Bitcoin. A sustained move above this mark could open the door to further gains, with the next target around $67,000. On the downside, support is seen near $63,500, and a break below that could trigger a test of $62,000.

Some market participants argue that Bitcoin's recent consolidation is healthy, allowing the market to digest previous gains. Others caution that a disappointing payrolls report could still spark a sharp sell-off, given the elevated leverage in the system.

Broader crypto context

The crypto market has been trading in tandem with equities, with both asset classes reacting to the same macroeconomic drivers. The correlation between Bitcoin and the S&P 500 has hovered near multi-month highs, underscoring the influence of traditional finance on digital assets.

Regulatory headlines have also been muted, with no major developments in recent days. This has allowed traders to focus squarely on the macro calendar, with payrolls being the main event of the week.

As the release approaches, volatility is expected to pick up. The initial reaction may be sharp, but the direction of the move will likely depend on how the data compares to consensus forecasts. A significant miss or beat could set the tone for Bitcoin's trajectory into the weekend.
