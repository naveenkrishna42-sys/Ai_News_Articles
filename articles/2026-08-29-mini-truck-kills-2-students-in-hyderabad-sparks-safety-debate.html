Tragic Incident Leaves Two BTech Students Dead

In a shocking incident in Hyderabad, a mini truck rammed into a group of BTech students, resulting in the deaths of two and injuring several others. The accident occurred near a busy intersection, raising urgent concerns about road safety and the enforcement of traffic rules.

The incident has sparked a debate about the state of road safety in Hyderabad. Experts point to a lack of enforcement of traffic rules and regulations, which has led to a rise in accidents involving mini trucks and other commercial vehicles.
