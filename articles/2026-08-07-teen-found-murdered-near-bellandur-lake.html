Teen found murdered near Bellandur lake

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Body of a teenager discovered near Bellandur lake in Bengaluru
  • Police suspect murder and have registered a case
  • Investigation underway; no arrests reported yet

Bengaluru: The body of a teenager was found near Bellandur lake on Friday morning, sending shockwaves through the neighbourhood. Police said the victim, whose age is believed to be around 17, bore visible injury marks, indicating a brutal assault.

The discovery was made by passers-by who spotted the body near the lake's edge around 7 am. They immediately alerted the local police control room. A team from the Bellandur police station rushed to the spot and cordoned off the area.

Initial investigation

Senior officers, including the Deputy Commissioner of Police (South-East division), visited the site. The body has been shifted to a government hospital for post-mortem examination. Preliminary reports suggest the teen was strangled, though officials have not yet confirmed the exact cause of death.

Police have registered a case of murder under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code. The victim's identity has not been officially revealed, pending confirmation from relatives. Investigators are combing through CCTV footage from nearby establishments and questioning residents.

Community in shock

Residents of the area expressed alarm, noting that the lake, once infamous for its toxic froth, has become a popular spot for morning walkers. "This is terrifying. We never expected such an incident here," said a local shopkeeper, who did not wish to be named.

Authorities have appealed to the public for any information that could help identify the victim or the accused. A helpline number has been set up at the Bellandur police station.

Broader concerns

The incident has reignited concerns about safety in the city's peripheries. Bellandur, a bustling IT corridor suburb, has seen rapid urbanisation, but policing has often lagged behind. Activists have pointed to inadequate street lighting and surveillance in the area.

In recent months, Bengaluru has witnessed a spate of violent crimes, prompting the city police to increase night patrolling. However, Friday's murder suggests gaps remain. Police have assured swift action, with a special team formed to probe the case.

Next steps

The post-mortem report is expected within 24 hours, which could provide crucial clues. Police are also awaiting forensic analysis of evidence collected from the scene. No arrests have been made so far.

As the investigation unfolds, the focus will be on tracing the teenager's last movements. Officials have not ruled out the possibility of a personal motive or a targeted attack. The case is likely to be monitored closely by senior police officials in the coming days.
