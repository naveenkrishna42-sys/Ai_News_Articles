Nifty 50, Sensex Prediction Today: Indian Stock Market Outlook for 17 July

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Market likely to open flat with a positive bias due to mixed global signals.
  • Nifty 50 may find support at 24,400 and resistance at 24,700.
  • Sensex could trade in a range of 80,000 to 81,000.
  • IT and banking stocks may see volatility ahead of earnings.

Market Opening Expected to Be Flat

Indian equity benchmarks, the Nifty 50 and the Sensex, are likely to open on a flat note on 17 July. Analysts point to mixed cues from global markets and cautious investor sentiment.

The GIFT Nifty, which trades on the international exchange, was hovering near the 24,600 mark in early deals. This suggests a muted start for domestic indices. Traders are watching for triggers from quarterly earnings and macroeconomic data.

Key Levels to Watch for Nifty 50 and Sensex

Technical analysts have outlined crucial support and resistance levels for the Nifty 50. The index is expected to find support at 24,400. On the upside, resistance is seen at 24,700.

For the Sensex, the trading range is pegged between 80,000 and 81,000. A breakout above 81,000 could signal further gains. A fall below 80,000 may trigger selling pressure. Volume data from the previous session shows that put writers were active at lower levels, indicating support.

Sectoral Outlook: IT and Banking in Focus

The information technology sector is likely to see stock-specific action as companies continue to report quarterly results. Infosys and TCS will be closely tracked. Analysts say that deal wins and margin commentary will drive movement.

Banking stocks, particularly private sector lenders, may also be volatile. The Nifty Bank index is hovering near its resistance zone. HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank are key stocks to watch. Any sharp movement in these heavyweights will influence the broader indices.

Global Cues Provide Mixed Signals

Overnight, US markets ended on a mixed note. The Dow Jones closed with minor gains while the Nasdaq slipped on tech profit-taking. Asian markets this morning are trading with a negative bias, with Japan's Nikkei and Hong Kong's Hang Seng in the red.

Foreign portfolio investors were net sellers in the cash segment on 16 July, offloading shares worth about ₹1,200 crore. Domestic institutional buyers, however, provided support by purchasing shares worth ₹1,500 crore. This tug-of-war is likely to keep the market rangebound.

Oil prices remain stable, with Brent crude hovering near $85 per barrel. Any spike due to geopolitical tensions could hurt market sentiment. The rupee, meanwhile, is expected to trade in a narrow band against the US dollar.

What to Watch Today

Investors will track the release of WPI inflation data for June, scheduled later in the day. A higher reading could dampen hopes of a rate cut by the Reserve Bank of India. On the earnings front, several midcap and smallcap companies are set to report numbers.

Traders are advised to keep positions light and avoid aggressive bets. The derivatives data shows that the 24,500 strike has the highest open interest on the call side, while 24,400 has the highest on the put side. This range is likely to hold for the day.

The coming sessions will be crucial as more earnings data and global central bank decisions unfold. A decisive move above 24,700 could pave the way for a fresh rally, while a breakdown below 24,400 may lead to a correction.
