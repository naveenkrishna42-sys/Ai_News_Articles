Pant surpasses Dhoni in away Tests, sets new record

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pant surpasses MS Dhoni and Adam Gilchrist in away Tests
  • Pant becomes first Indian wicketkeeper with 2,500+ away Test runs
  • Pant sets new record for most runs by an Indian wicketkeeper in away Tests

Pant's Historic Feat

Rishabh Pant has etched his name in the annals of Indian cricket history by surpassing the legendary MS Dhoni and Adam Gilchrist in the tally of most runs scored by wicketkeepers in away Tests. This milestone was achieved during the second Test against Sri Lanka, where Pant not only scored a century but also became the first Indian wicketkeeper to amass 2,500 runs in away Tests.

Breaking Down the Records

Pant's achievement is not just a personal triumph but also a significant milestone for Indian cricket. Here's a breakdown of the records he has surpassed:

WicketkeeperRuns in Away Tests
Adam Gilchrist2,472
MS Dhoni2,443
Rishabh Pant2,500+

Pant's 105 runs in the second innings of the second Test against Sri Lanka were crucial in surpassing these records. This innings was not just about the runs but also about Pant's ability to anchor the innings and provide a solid foundation for the team.

Why This Matters

Pant's achievement is significant for several reasons. Firstly, it highlights his consistency and ability to perform under pressure, especially in away conditions. Secondly, it underscores the importance of wicketkeeping in the modern game, where the keeper is often required to contribute significantly with the bat.

From a team perspective, Pant's ability to score runs in away Tests is crucial for India's success in overseas tours. It provides a much-needed boost to the batting lineup and can turn the tide of a match. Pant's record also sets a new benchmark for future wicketkeepers, encouraging them to aim high and strive for excellence.

Expert Opinion

Cricket analyst and former player, Harsha Bhogle, has praised Pant's achievement. He believes that Pant's ability to perform in away Tests is a testament to his mental strength and resilience. Bhogle also highlighted that Pant's record is a significant milestone for Indian cricket and will inspire future generations of wicketkeepers.

Key Facts and Analysis

  • Pros: Pant's achievement is a significant milestone for Indian cricket and will inspire future generations of wicketkeepers. His ability to perform in away Tests is crucial for India's success in overseas tours.
  • Cons: However, Pant's record is not without its critics. Some analysts believe that the current Test format and scoring rates make it easier for batsmen to score runs, which could dilute the significance of such records.
  • Key Fact: Pant's record is a significant milestone for Indian cricket and will inspire future generations of wicketkeepers.
