Series Overview

India is set to tour Zimbabwe for a T20I series starting in late July 2026. The series will see Shreyas Iyer leading a relatively young Indian squad as they look to bounce back from recent setbacks. Zimbabwe, on the other hand, will aim to exploit India's vulnerabilities and make a mark on home soil.

The tour comes at a crucial juncture for both teams. India are searching for a winning combination ahead of major tournaments, while Zimbabwe are looking to rebuild and challenge a top-tier side.

Full Schedule and Match Timings

The exact dates and venues for the T20I matches are yet to be officially confirmed by the boards, but the series is expected to feature five matches. The first match is likely to be played around July 27, 2026, with subsequent games following every two to three days.

All matches are scheduled to be played at the Harare Sports Club. The timings are expected to be in the afternoon slot (local time), making it a prime-time watch in India.

Where to Watch: Live Streaming and Telecast

For Indian viewers, the series will be broadcast live on the Sports18 network. Live streaming will be available on the JioCinema app and website, ensuring fans can watch the action on their mobile devices.

International viewers can check local listings. The matches are also expected to be streamed on the official platforms of the Zimbabwe Cricket board for overseas audiences.

Team News and Key Players

Shreyas Iyer has been named captain for the series. He recently spoke about the importance of the tour, calling it a 'reality check' after the team's performances in Ireland and England. Iyer has backed young batter Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, stating that he must have rectified his recent mistakes.

Zimbabwe have recalled experienced players like Wesley Madhevere and Tanaka Nyamhuri for the series. The home side will be banking on their all-round capabilities to trouble the Indian batting lineup.

The Indian squad is expected to feature a mix of established names and fringe players looking to cement their spots. The series will be a key opportunity for the management to test bench strength.

What to Watch For

All eyes will be on how the Indian batting unit handles the Zimbabwean spin attack on slow pitches. For Zimbabwe, the challenge will be to contain an aggressive Indian top order and put pressure on the middle order.

The series is likely to be a closely contested affair, with both teams having points to prove. With the T20 World Cup on the horizon, every match counts for India's preparation.
