Piyush Goyal's FTA push: India's tech trade strategy

📅 2026-08-26 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • India to negotiate FTAs with 8-9 blocs, targeting 75% of global trade
  • Commerce minister to tweak rules within two months to address semiconductor and auto sector concerns
  • BIS to support high-tech industries, with potential currency and regulatory issues influencing short-term investment decisions

India's Commerce Minister Outlines Ambitious FTA Strategy

India's Commerce and Industry Minister, Piyush Goyal, has outlined a bold strategy to negotiate Free Trade Agreements (FTAs) with up to nine blocs, aiming to cover 75% of global trade. This move comes as a response to perceived threats from China and a desire to strengthen India's position in the global trade arena.

Why This Matters

The FTA strategy is a significant step towards diversifying India's trade partnerships and reducing its dependence on a single market. By negotiating with multiple blocs, India can access a wider range of products and services, thereby boosting its economic growth and creating jobs.

However, the success of this strategy hinges on India's ability to negotiate favorable terms and ensure that the benefits of these agreements are widely shared. There are concerns that some sectors, particularly high-tech industries, may not benefit equally, necessitating further policy adjustments.

Tweaking Rules to Boost Semiconductor and Auto Sectors

Goyal has announced that the government will tweak rules within two months to address concerns in the semiconductor and auto sectors. This move is crucial for India's ambitious plans to become a global hub for these industries, which are expected to drive significant economic growth in the coming years.

The proposed changes are likely to include measures to simplify regulatory processes, reduce import duties, and provide financial incentives to domestic manufacturers. These steps will not only boost domestic production but also enhance India's competitiveness in the global market.

BIS to Support High-Tech Industries

The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) will play a key role in supporting high-tech industries by ensuring that products meet international quality standards. This will help Indian companies compete more effectively in the global market and attract foreign investment.

However, there are potential challenges, including currency movements and regulatory issues, which could influence short-term investment decisions. The BIS will need to navigate these challenges carefully to ensure that India's high-tech industries can thrive.

Broader Impact and Expert Analysis

India's FTA strategy and policy adjustments are part of a broader effort to transform the country into a manufacturing and technology hub. These measures will not only boost economic growth but also create jobs and enhance India's global standing.

However, the success of these initiatives will depend on several factors, including the ability to negotiate favorable FTA terms, the effectiveness of policy adjustments, and the BIS's ability to support high-tech industries. Experts believe that India has a strong foundation to achieve these goals, but continued policy support and investment will be crucial.
