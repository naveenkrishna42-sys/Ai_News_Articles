Veteran actor Pradeep Rawat of 'Ghajini', 'Lagaan' fame dies at 74

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pradeep Rawat died at 74 after a battle with cancer, confirmed by his manager.
  • He was known for roles in 'Ghajini' and 'Lagaan'.
  • His funeral is scheduled for Wednesday in Mumbai.

Veteran actor Pradeep Rawat, known to millions for his menacing turn in 'Ghajini' and his memorable role in 'Lagaan', passed away on Tuesday. He was 74. His manager confirmed the news, stating that the actor had been battling cancer. The funeral is scheduled to take place on Wednesday, though the exact time and location have not yet been officially announced.

A career etched in memory

Rawat's filmography spanned decades and languages, making him a familiar face across Indian cinema. For many, he remains indelible as the ruthless villain in the 2008 blockbuster 'Ghajini', where he squared off against Aamir Khan. But his body of work went far beyond that single role.

In 'Lagaan', the 2001 Oscar-nominated epic, Rawat played a British army officer, adding to a film that became a landmark in Indian cinema. His ability to shift between villainous intensity and character-driven roles kept him in demand across Bollywood, Telugu, Tamil, and other industries.

More than a villain

While audiences often typecast him as the antagonist, Rawat's range was wider. He appeared in over a hundred films, including Telugu hits where he was a familiar presence. Colleagues remember him as a disciplined professional who brought a quiet gravitas to every set.

His passing has prompted an outpouring of tributes from fans and fellow actors on social media. Many have recalled his dialogue delivery and screen presence, noting how he could command a scene without raising his voice.

The final chapter

Details about his illness have been kept private, but his manager confirmed that cancer was the cause. The actor is survived by his family, who have requested privacy during this time. The film fraternity is expected to gather for the funeral on Wednesday in Mumbai.

Rawat's death marks the loss of yet another veteran from an era when character actors were the backbone of Indian cinema. His performances, especially in 'Ghajini' and 'Lagaan', will continue to be watched and admired for years to come.

As the industry mourns, fans are left to revisit his films and remember a talent that enriched every project he touched.
