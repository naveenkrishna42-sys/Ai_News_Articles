Rajahmundry Hit-and-Run: Medico Priyanka Dies, Police Seek Accused Custody

📅 2026-08-09 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Medical student Priyanka succumbed to injuries from a hit-and-run near a mall in Rajahmundry.
  • Police are set to seek custody of two accused in connection with the death.
  • Andhra Pradesh CM Chandrababu Naidu announced ₹20 lakh ex gratia to the victim's family.

Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh: A 22-year-old medical student, Priyanka, has died after being critically injured in a hit-and-run incident near a shopping mall in the city. The incident, which occurred recently, has sparked outrage and demands for justice from the victim's family and the local community.

Police have confirmed that two accused have been identified and are in custody. Authorities are now preparing to seek their remand for further interrogation, according to reports from local media.

The Incident and the Victim

Priyanka, a final-year MBBS student at a local medical college, was reportedly crossing the road near a mall when a speeding vehicle struck her. She was rushed to a nearby hospital with severe injuries but succumbed to them despite medical efforts.

The news of her death has sent shockwaves through the medical community and the region. Fellow students and faculty have expressed grief, with many calling for stricter action against reckless driving.

Police Action and Investigation

Rajahmundry police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code. The two accused, whose identities have not been officially released, were apprehended following a preliminary investigation. Police are expected to seek their custody to probe the circumstances leading to the incident.

Sources indicate that the accused may have been driving at high speed, and forensic evidence, including CCTV footage from the mall, is being examined. Officials have not yet confirmed whether the vehicle has been seized.

Family Demands Justice

Priyanka's family, who are said to be devastated, have demanded a thorough investigation and stringent punishment for those responsible. They have alleged that the accused may attempt to influence the probe, urging authorities to ensure a fair process.

Local residents and student groups have also staged protests near the incident site, demanding improved road safety measures and faster legal action. Police have assured the public that no leniency will be shown.

Government Response and Compensation

Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister Chandrababu Naidu has announced an ex gratia of ₹20 lakh to the victim's family. The announcement was made public through official channels, with the CM expressing condolences and promising support to the bereaved family.

The aid is intended to provide immediate financial relief, though the family has stated that their primary demand remains justice. Political leaders from various parties have also visited the family to offer their support.

Broader Concerns Over Road Safety

The incident has reignited concerns about road safety in Rajahmundry, where accidents involving speeding vehicles have been reported in the past. Activists have called for better traffic enforcement and the installation of speed cameras near busy commercial areas.

Medical students across the state have also voiced solidarity, organizing candlelight vigils in memory of Priyanka. Many have taken to social media to demand swift legal proceedings.

As the case progresses, the court will determine whether the accused are granted bail or remanded in judicial custody. Police have indicated that a chargesheet will be filed once the investigation is complete.

All eyes are now on the legal process, with the family and public expecting a conviction that serves as a deterrent. The Rajahmundry police have urged witnesses to come forward, promising protection and confidentiality.
