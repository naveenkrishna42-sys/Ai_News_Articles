A Grand Epic Takes Shape

The film is being directed by Nitesh Tiwari, known for his work on Dangal and Chhichhore. Produced by Namit Malhotra, the project has been in the making for years, with fans eagerly tracking every update. The casting itself has sparked massive buzz—Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram, Sai Pallavi as Goddess Sita, and Yash as Raavan.

What the Release Date Means

Releasing two days before Diwali is a strategic move. The festival period is traditionally a lucrative window for big-ticket releases in India, with families flocking to theatres during the holiday season. The date also allows the film to build word-of-mouth momentum going into the festival weekend.

While the exact date has not been officially confirmed in the public domain yet, reports suggest the film is slated for a 2027 release. This gives the team ample time to complete the ambitious production, which is believed to involve elaborate sets and cutting-edge technology.

Ramayana is planned as a multi-part series, with the first instalment expected to cover the early life of Lord Ram and his exile. Sai Pallavi, celebrated for her natural acting, is expected to bring grace to Sita, while Ranbir Kapoor's versatility makes him a compelling choice for Ram.
