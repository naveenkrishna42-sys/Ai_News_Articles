Sudhir Sitapati quits as Godrej Consumer MD & CEO; Aasif Malbari takes over

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sudhir Sitapati steps down as MD & CEO of Godrej Consumer Products after a five-year tenure
  • Aasif Malbari named as the new Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer
  • Vishal Kedia appointed as interim CFO as Malbari transitions to the top role

Sudhir Sitapati has resigned as Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of Godrej Consumer Products Limited (GCPL), ending a five-year stint at the helm of the FMCG major. His exit, described as "sudden" by industry observers, comes as the company navigates a competitive consumer market in India and abroad.

The board has moved swiftly to name Aasif Malbari as the new MD and CEO. Malbari, who was serving as the company's Chief Financial Officer, will now lead the maker of brands such as Cinthol, Goodknight, and Expert. In a related reshuffle, Vishal Kedia has been appointed interim CFO, a role he will hold as the company transitions its finance leadership.

Five years at the helm

Sitapati took charge of GCPL in 2021, a period marked by volatile input costs and shifting consumer preferences. During his tenure, the company focused on premiumisation, digital expansion, and strengthening its presence in emerging markets across Africa and Asia.

His leadership saw GCPL navigate post-pandemic supply chain disruptions and inflationary pressures. The company also made strategic moves in categories like hair care and home insecticides, though it faced intense competition from both established players and new-age D2C brands.

Details of Sitapati's next move are not yet public. Industry speculation is rife, but neither GCPL nor Sitapati has commented on his future plans.

Who is Aasif Malbari?

Malbari's elevation to the top job marks a significant step for the company, promoting from within its finance ranks. As CFO, he was instrumental in shaping GCPL's capital allocation strategy and cost-efficiency programmes.

His appointment signals continuity in financial discipline, even as the company prepares for a leadership transition. Malbari takes over at a time when rural demand is showing signs of recovery and urban consumption remains uneven.

The board's decision to appoint an internal candidate suggests a preference for stability. "Aasif's deep understanding of the business and financial framework makes him the right choice to lead GCPL into its next phase," a company statement said, though no direct quotes were attributed.

The road ahead for GCPL

GCPL's stock reaction to the announcement has been muted, with analysts awaiting clarity on the strategic direction under Malbari. The company faces headwinds from raw material price fluctuations and intense competition in the mass-market segment.

Kedia, the interim CFO, will work closely with Malbari to ensure a seamless transition. The company has not indicated a timeline for appointing a permanent CFO.

Investors will be watching for signals on whether Malbari will continue Sitapati's premiumisation strategy or pivot towards volume growth. The first earnings call under his leadership will be closely scrutinised.

As GCPL embarks on this leadership change, the focus will remain on execution. The consumer goods sector is bracing for a festive season that could set the tone for the coming quarters.
