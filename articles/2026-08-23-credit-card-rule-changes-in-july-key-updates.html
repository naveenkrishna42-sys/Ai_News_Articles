Credit card rule changes in July: Key updates

  • SBI Card, HDFC Bank, and ICICI Bank are introducing new credit card rules in July.
  • Changes include new joining fees, reward structures, and eligibility criteria.

Introduction

In July 2024, SBI Card, HDFC Bank, and ICICI Bank are introducing new rules for their credit cards. These changes include new joining fees, reward structures, and eligibility criteria. Consumers should review their card options to take advantage of the new changes.

SBI Card Changes

SBI Card is introducing new joining fees for its credit cards. The new joining fees range from ₹500 to ₹1,500, depending on the card type. The bank is also introducing new reward structures, with up to 5% cashback on select categories. The eligibility criteria for SBI Card credit cards have also been revised, with a minimum income requirement of ₹30,000 per month.

HDFC Bank Changes

HDFC Bank is introducing new joining fees for its credit cards, ranging from ₹1,000 to ₹2,500. The bank is also introducing new reward structures, with up to 6% cashback on select categories. The eligibility criteria for HDFC Bank credit cards have been revised, with a minimum income requirement of ₹40,000 per month.

ICICI Bank Changes

ICICI Bank is introducing new joining fees for its credit cards, ranging from ₹800 to ₹2,000. The bank is also introducing new reward structures, with up to 7% cashback on select categories. The eligibility criteria for ICICI Bank credit cards have been revised, with a minimum income requirement of ₹50,000 per month.

How to Apply

To apply for any of these credit cards, consumers can visit the respective bank's website or contact their customer service. The application process is online, and consumers can expect to receive their new card within 7-10 business days. Consumers should review their card options to take advantage of the new changes.
