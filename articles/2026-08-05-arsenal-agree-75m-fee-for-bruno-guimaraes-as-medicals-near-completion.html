Arsenal agree £75m fee for Bruno Guimaraes as medicals near completion

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Arsenal and Newcastle agree £75m fee for Bruno Guimaraes
  • Midfielder's professionalism praised amid transfer speculation
  • An unnamed Arsenal player reportedly completes medical ahead of exit

Deal close for Guimaraes

Arsenal have moved to the brink of completing one of the summer's biggest transfers, with reports confirming the club has agreed a £75 million fee with Newcastle United for captain Bruno Guimaraes. The Brazilian midfielder has been a top target for Mikel Arteta throughout the window, and sources indicate the two clubs have finally reached a compromise after weeks of negotiation.

The agreement marks a significant statement of intent from the Gunners, who have long admired Guimaraes' ability to dictate play from deep. Sky Sports and The Guardian both report the fee as £75m, with the structure of the deal understood to have been the final sticking point before both sides shook hands.

Class act amid chaos

What has stood out during the saga, according to those close to the player, is Guimaraes' conduct. Despite intense speculation and constant media attention, the Newcastle skipper has remained professional, training hard and showing no signs of disruption. His behaviour has drawn praise from pundits and fans alike, reinforcing why Arsenal see him as a leader both on and off the pitch.

Former Manchester United defender Mikael Silvestre recently urged Arsenal to prioritise Guimaraes over other midfield targets, saying: "Martin Zubimendi can't do that" — a pointed reference to the Brazilian's unique ability to break lines and drive forward with the ball. That endorsement echoes the sentiment within Arsenal's recruitment team, who believe Guimaraes offers something no other option on the market can.

Medical completed for exit

While the incoming deal dominates headlines, Arsenal have also been busy on the outgoing front. Reports indicate that a player from the current squad has already completed a medical ahead of a permanent move, though the club has not officially confirmed the identity. Speculation suggests it could be one of several fringe players deemed surplus to requirements by Arteta.

The completion of that medical signals a busy final phase of the window for the north London club, who are looking to streamline their squad while adding top-level quality. With the Premier League season fast approaching, Arteta will want his new signings integrated quickly, and the timing of this double movement suggests the club is working efficiently behind the scenes.

What this means for Arsenal's midfield

If the Guimaraes deal is finalised, he is expected to slot straight into the heart of Arsenal's midfield, partnering Declan Rice and Martin Odegaard in a formidable trio. His arrival would give Arteta more tactical flexibility, allowing the team to press higher and control possession with greater authority.

The former Lyon man has been a revelation since joining Newcastle in January 2022, earning the captain's armband and becoming the focal point of Eddie Howe's side. His departure, while a blow for the Magpies, would provide them with significant funds to reinvest as they look to build for the future.

Arsenal fans will be hoping the paperwork is completed without further drama, with an official announcement potentially coming within days. The club has yet to comment publicly, but the pieces appear to be falling into place for one of the most anticipated signings of the summer.

Looking ahead

All eyes will now turn to the medical and contract formalities for Guimaraes, which are expected to be concluded shortly. If all goes to plan, the Brazilian could feature in Arsenal's early pre-season fixtures, giving supporters their first glimpse of a midfield rebuild that signals clear title ambitions.
