CWG 2026: India's boxing gold rush powers 16-medal Saturday, fourth-place rise

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Seven boxing golds headlined India's 16-medal haul on Saturday at CWG 2026
  • Bhiwani's boxing hub contributed five golds, earning comparisons to 'mini Cuba'
  • The medal rush lifted India to fourth place in the overall standings
  • BFI president Ajay Singh called the boxers' performance 'exceptional' and 'exceeded expectations'

A Saturday to remember in Glasgow

India's Commonwealth Games campaign hit a spectacular high on Saturday as the boxing ring in Glasgow turned into a goldmine. The contingent bagged seven boxing golds, part of a 16-medal bonanza that propelled the country to fourth place in the overall standings.

The surge was led by a familiar source of strength — Bhiwani, Haryana's famed boxing factory, which delivered five of the seven golds. The town, long dubbed India's 'mini Cuba' for its relentless production of champions, once again proved why that label sticks.

Sakshi Chaudhary's forged triumph

Among the standout performances was Sakshi Chaudhary, who claimed gold in Glasgow. What made her victory remarkable was the road she travelled to get there — she had to beat world champions back home in India just to secure her spot in the squad. Her journey from domestic trials to the podium in Scotland underscores the depth of talent in Indian boxing.

Chaudhary's gold was not just a personal milestone but a statement about the fierce competition within the national camp. The selection process itself, it appears, was as tough as any international bout.

Jaismine's battle beyond the ring

Another emotional high came from Jaismine, a boxer who fought through a mystery illness to win her Commonwealth Games gold. A self-confessed MS Dhoni fan, she drew inspiration from the cricketer's calm under pressure, channelling it into her own gritty performance in Glasgow.

Details of the illness remain scarce, but her victory has resonated far beyond the boxing community. It is a reminder that athletes often fight battles invisible to the audience, and Jaismine's resilience has become a talking point on social media.

Bhiwani's golden legacy

The five golds from Bhiwani's boxers have reignited pride in a town that has consistently punched above its weight. From Vijender Singh's pioneering feats to the current crop, Bhiwani's small gyms and gritty training culture have produced a conveyor belt of medallists.

Saturday's haul strengthens the argument that grassroots boxing infrastructure in Haryana remains a model worth replicating across the country. The term 'mini Cuba' may be a nickname, but the results are anything but miniature.

Officials hail 'exceptional' performance

Boxing Federation of India president Ajay Singh did not hold back in his praise, calling the boxers' showing 'exceptional' and noting that they 'exceeded expectations'. His comments came as the federation celebrated a day that will be hard to top in any future Games.

With seven golds, India's boxing team has not only boosted the medal tally but also set a benchmark for the rest of the contingent. The overall fourth-place rise in the standings reflects a multi-sport effort, but boxing was clearly the day's headline act.

The focus now shifts to the remaining days of the Games. With boxing's marquee events done, attention turns to athletics, wrestling and other disciplines still in progress. Officials will be hoping the momentum carries through to the closing ceremony.

For the boxers, the journey back home will be one of celebration, but the real test lies ahead — building on this success for the next major championships. If Saturday is any indication, Indian boxing's upward trajectory shows no signs of slowing down.
