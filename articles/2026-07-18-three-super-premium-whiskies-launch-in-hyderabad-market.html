Three Super-Premium Whiskies Launch in Hyderabad Market

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Three super-premium whisky brands launched in Hyderabad.
  • The whiskies cater to the growing demand for luxury spirits.
  • Market analysts see steady growth in the premium segment.

Hyderabad's drink scene just got a little more sophisticated. Three new super-premium whiskies have hit shelves across the city, offering connoisseurs fresh options in the luxury spirits segment.

The arrivals were confirmed by local distributors, who said the whiskies target the growing number of consumers willing to spend more on high-end alcohol. The move comes as Hyderabad sees a steady uptick in demand for premium and super-premium spirits.

New Brands Enter the Mix

Each of the three whiskies brings a distinct profile. One is a single malt from Scotland, aged for over 18 years. Another is a blended Scotch known for its smooth finish. The third is a Japanese whisky that has won international awards.

Distributors declined to disclose exact retail prices, but industry sources peg them between Rs 5,000 and Rs 15,000 per bottle. The whiskies are now available at select liquor stores and high-end bars across the city.

Why Super-Premium?

The super-premium category typically includes whiskies aged for at least a decade and priced above Rs 5,000. Hyderabad has seen a growing appetite for such products, especially among young professionals and business owners.

Market watchers say the trend is not isolated to Hyderabad. Across India, the super-premium whisky segment has grown at over 15% annually for the last three years. The city's cosmopolitan culture and rising disposable incomes make it a prime market for luxury spirits.

Retailers Report Strong Interest

Retailers in upmarket areas like Banjara Hills and Jubilee Hills said they have already received inquiries from regular customers. One shop owner said the whiskies are likely to be popular as gifts and for special occasions.

Bars and restaurants are also placing orders. A manager at a five-star hotel in the city said they plan to feature the new whiskies in their cocktail menu. He added that the demand for single malts and premium blends has increased noticeably in the last two years.

What This Means for the Market

The entry of three new super-premium whiskies signals that global brands see Hyderabad as a viable market. It also points to a shift in consumer preferences from mass-market brands to more exclusive options.

Industry analysts note that the trend is likely to continue. As more people travel abroad and experience international drinking cultures, they bring back a taste for premium spirits. Hyderabad's growing expat community also contributes to the demand.

Officials from the Telangana Excise Department have not commented on the new launches. However, the state's liberal alcohol policy has made it easier for premium brands to enter the market.

The next few months will reveal whether these three whiskies find a loyal following. For now, Hyderabad's whisky lovers have more reasons to explore the city's expanding luxury spirits aisle.
