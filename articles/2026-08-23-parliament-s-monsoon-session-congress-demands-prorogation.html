Congress criticizes government for delaying prorogation of Parliament's Monsoon Session

Congress has expressed its dissatisfaction with the government's delay in proroguing Parliament's Monsoon Session, which adjourned on August 13. Ten days later, the session has not been formally closed, raising eyebrows among opposition parties.

Delimitation bill remains a key issue in Parliament

The delimitation bill, which aims to redraw parliamentary and assembly constituencies, has been a focal point of the ongoing session. Congress has questioned whether Home Minister Amit Shah is still seeking a two-thirds majority for the bill, despite the government's claim that it has already secured the necessary support.

Opposition parties demand transparency and accountability

Congress has called for transparency and accountability from the government, urging it to provide a clear timeline for the prorogation of the session. The party has also demanded that the government address the concerns of opposition parties regarding the delimitation bill.

Government defends its actions, insists on timely completion of legislative agenda

The government has defended its actions, stating that it is committed to completing the legislative agenda on time. Officials have not yet confirmed whether the session will be prorogued on the scheduled date or if there will be any further delays.

What happens next?

As the deadline for the prorogation of Parliament's Monsoon Session approaches, all eyes will be on the government to see if it will adhere to its commitment to close the session on time. Meanwhile, the delimitation bill will continue to be a key issue in Parliament, with opposition parties likely to intensify their efforts to secure the necessary support for the bill.
