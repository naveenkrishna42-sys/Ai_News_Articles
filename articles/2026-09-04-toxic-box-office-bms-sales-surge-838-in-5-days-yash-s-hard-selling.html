Box Office Surge: Toxic's Impressive 5-Day Performance

Toxic, the latest release from Yash Raj Films, has made a significant impact at the box office. On its fifth day, the film saw a staggering 838% jump in BookMyShow (BMS) sales from morning to noon. This surge is indicative of the film's strong appeal and the aggressive marketing strategies employed by Yash Raj Films.

Yash Raj Films' Aggressive Marketing Strategy

Yash Raj Films is known for its hard-selling approach, and Toxic is no exception. The film's marketing team is selling an impressive 143 tickets every single minute. This aggressive marketing strategy has helped the film to maintain a high occupancy rate and attract a large audience.

Toxic's Narrative and Performances

Toxic's narrative and performances have received mixed reviews. While some critics have praised the film's unique story and the performances of the lead actors, others have found the film to be predictable and lacking in depth. Despite the mixed reviews, the film's strong box office performance suggests that there is a significant audience for this type of content.
