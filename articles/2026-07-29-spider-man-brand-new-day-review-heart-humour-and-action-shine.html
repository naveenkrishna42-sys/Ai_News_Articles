Spider-Man: Brand New Day Review: Heart, Humour and Action Shine

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • The film is praised for balancing emotional depth with comedic moments.
  • Action sequences are described as some of the franchise's most inventive.
  • Critics call it one of the strongest entries in the Spider-Man series.

Early reviews for Spider-Man: Brand New Day are in, and the consensus is clear: this is one of the franchise's strongest outings. Critics are highlighting a sharp balance of heart, humour and action that makes the latest web-slinger adventure a crowd-pleaser.

A Fresh Start for the Wall-Crawler

The film picks up after the events of No Way Home, with Peter Parker navigating a world that no longer remembers him. Director Jon Watts, returning for his fourth Spider-Man film, leans into the emotional fallout while keeping the tone light when needed.

The result is a story that feels both grounded and epic. Reviewers note that the script avoids the usual origin-story beats, instead focusing on Peter's struggle to rebuild his life and relationships from scratch.

Action That Soars

Action sequences in Brand New Day are being called some of the most inventive in the series. Set pieces range from a dizzying chase through New York's skyline to a brutal underground fight that showcases Spider-Man's agility and wit.

The visual effects team has outdone themselves, with fluid web-slinging and seamless integration of practical stunts. One standout scene involves a fight on a moving subway train, which has drawn comparisons to the best moments from Spider-Man 2.

Tom Holland's Best Performance Yet

Tom Holland continues to grow into the role, delivering what many call his most mature performance as Peter Parker. The actor balances vulnerability with the wisecracking charm that defines the character.

Supporting cast members also shine. Zendaya returns as MJ, though her role is more subdued this time, reflecting the film's theme of loss. Jacob Batalon's Ned provides much of the comic relief, while a new villain brings a menacing physicality that raises the stakes.

Heart and Humour in Equal Measure

What sets Brand New Day apart from other superhero films is its willingness to slow down for character moments. A quiet scene between Peter and Aunt May, played by Marisa Tomei, has been singled out as one of the film's emotional highlights.

The humour, meanwhile, never feels forced. Quips land naturally, and the film avoids the trap of undercutting serious moments with jokes. This balance is what makes the movie work so well, according to critics.

The runtime is lean for a superhero film, clocking in at just over two hours. The pacing keeps the story moving without feeling rushed, allowing each emotional beat to land before the next action set piece kicks in.

Fans of the comics will find plenty of Easter eggs, including nods to classic storylines. But the film never relies on nostalgia alone, standing on its own as a satisfying chapter in the Spider-Man saga.

With Spider-Man: Brand New Day, the franchise has delivered a film that honours what came before while charting a bold new path. It is a reminder of why Spider-Man remains one of cinema's most beloved heroes.

The film is set for a wide release this Friday. Early box office projections suggest a strong opening weekend, with fans eager to see where the story goes next.
