Raisins: A natural remedy for constipation?

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Science Original source ↗
Key points
  • Raisins are a rich source of dietary fiber and natural sugars, which can aid digestion.
  • Clinical studies suggest raisins may help relieve constipation, but more research is needed.
  • Raisins are a convenient and affordable option for improving gut health.

Raisins and their potential benefits for digestion

Raisins, the dried form of grapes, have been a staple in diets worldwide for centuries. They are a rich source of dietary fiber, natural sugars, and antioxidants, making them a potential remedy for various digestive issues, including constipation.

Dietary fiber: The key to improved digestion

Dietary fiber, particularly soluble fiber, is known to improve digestion by adding bulk to stool and promoting regular bowel movements. Raisins contain about 2.5 grams of fiber per 100 grams, which is a significant portion of the daily recommended intake of 25-28 grams for adults.

Natural sugars and their role in digestion

Raisins also contain natural sugars, such as glucose and fructose, which can stimulate the release of digestive enzymes and improve gut motility. However, it's important to note that the sugar content in raisins is relatively low compared to other dried fruits.

Clinical studies and expert opinions

Several clinical studies have suggested that raisins may help relieve constipation. For instance, a study published in the Journal of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics found that a daily intake of 25 grams of raisins could significantly improve stool consistency and frequency in adults with constipation.

However, more research is needed to fully understand the mechanisms by which raisins improve digestion and to determine the optimal dosage for different individuals.

Pros and cons of using raisins for constipation

  • Pros: Raisins are a convenient, affordable, and natural remedy for constipation. They are also a good source of vitamins and minerals, such as vitamin K and potassium.
  • Cons: Raisins can be high in sugar and calories, which may not be suitable for people following a low-sugar or low-calorie diet. Additionally, some people may experience digestive discomfort or bloating after consuming raisins.

Conclusion

While raisins show promise as a natural remedy for constipation, more research is needed to fully understand their potential benefits and risks. In the meantime, incorporating raisins into a balanced diet, along with other fiber-rich foods and plenty of water, can help improve digestion and overall gut health.
