Rahul Steps In at the Toss

India's camp has remained tight-lipped about the specifics, only saying that Gill is being monitored by the medical staff.

This is not the first time Gill has faced an injury scare. His fitness has been a talking point in recent months, and any setback now would be particularly untimely given the importance of this Test series. The warm-up match was meant to be a final tune-up, not a source of new worries.

The medical team's assessment will be crucial in the coming hours. For now, the focus remains on the warm-up game, but the bigger picture is clearly on the series opener.
