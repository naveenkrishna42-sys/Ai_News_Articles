Introduction to India's Asian Games 2026 Performance

India's women's 4x400m relay team has made history by winning the gold medal at the Asian Games 2026, marking the country's sixth gold medal overall. This achievement is a testament to the team's hard work and dedication to their sport.

Team Members and Their Achievements

The team, comprising Kiran Pahal, Poovamma MR, Prachi, and Vithya Ramraj, delivered an outstanding performance, striking India's first athletics title at the Asian Games 2026. Their victory has been recognized and appreciated by the Prime Minister, who congratulated the team on their achievement.

Awards and Recognition

In recognition of their achievement, Vijay has announced a reward of ₹75 lakh for Vithya Ramraj, one of the team members. This reward is a gesture of appreciation for the team's hard work and dedication to their sport.

Men's 4x400m Relay Performance

Additionally, India's men's 4x400m relay team, led by Vishal TK, won the bronze medal, holding off Thailand in a closely contested race. This achievement demonstrates the team's skill and perseverance in the face of tough competition.
