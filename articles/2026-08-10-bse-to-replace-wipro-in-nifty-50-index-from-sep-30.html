BSE to Replace Wipro in Nifty 50 Index from Sep 30

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • BSE Ltd to enter Nifty 50, replacing Wipro effective Sep 30, 2026.
  • Move follows NSE Indices' semi-annual review based on six-month data.
  • Wipro's exit reflects its declining market cap and trading activity.

Index Rejig: BSE In, Wipro Out

In a significant reshuffle of India's benchmark equity index, the National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that BSE Ltd will replace IT major Wipro in the Nifty 50 index. The change will take effect from September 30, 2026, a move that underscores the shifting dynamics of India's corporate landscape.

The decision, based on NSE Indices' semi-annual review, reflects the six-month average free-float market capitalisation and trading activity of listed companies. BSE Ltd, the country's oldest stock exchange, has seen its stock price surge over the past year, driven by robust volumes in the derivatives segment and strong investor interest in its clearing and listing businesses.

Wipro, once a bellwether of India's software services industry, has been on a downward trajectory in recent years. The company's market valuation has lagged behind peers such as Infosys and TCS, and its weight in the index had been shrinking. Analysts say the exclusion is not surprising, given the sustained underperformance.

What This Means for Investors

Index funds and exchange-traded funds (ETFs) that track the Nifty 50 will need to rebalance their portfolios ahead of the effective date. This typically triggers buying in BSE shares and selling in Wipro, leading to temporary volatility in both counters. Market participants estimate that passive funds managing assets worth several lakh crore will adjust their holdings, potentially moving billions of rupees.

For Wipro, the exit from the Nifty 50 is a psychological blow. Being part of the benchmark index is seen as a marker of corporate standing, and its removal could reduce foreign institutional interest in the stock. On the other hand, BSE's inclusion is a validation of its growth story, which has been powered by the boom in derivatives trading and the success of its subsidiary, India International Exchange (INX).

Broader Market Implications

The reshuffle also highlights a structural shift in the Indian market. Traditional IT services companies are facing headwinds from global tech spending slowdowns and the rise of artificial intelligence, while exchanges and financial infrastructure firms are benefiting from increased market participation. The Nifty 50's composition has evolved over the years, with newer sectors like insurance, retail, and digital services gaining prominence.

NSE Indices periodically reviews the index constituents, using data from the preceding six months. The next review is expected in early 2027, and further changes are anticipated as market capitalisation patterns continue to evolve.

While the immediate focus is on the September 30 switch, market watchers will be keen to see how Wipro's stock performs post-exclusion. Historically, stocks removed from major indices often see a brief dip, but fundamentals eventually take over. For BSE, the inclusion is expected to boost its liquidity and attract long-term institutional capital.

As the effective date approaches, traders and investors will be watching the price movements of both stocks. The rebalancing is likely to create arbitrage opportunities, and active fund managers may use the event to adjust their portfolios.
