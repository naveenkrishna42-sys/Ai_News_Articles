The Odyssey Box Office: Nolan Film Nears Rs 25 Crore on Day 2 in India

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Early box office trends indicate The Odyssey is on track for a Rs 25 crore-plus day 2 in India.
  • The film has already recorded Nolan's best non-Dark Knight opening weekend globally, surpassing $120 million.
  • Reviews praise the film as a breathtaking epic that marks a new high for the director.

Strong Start Continues for Nolan's Epic

Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey is showing no signs of slowing down at the Indian box office. Early trends for the film's second day point to a collection of Rs 25 crore or more across the country.

This follows a robust opening day that has already set the stage for what is shaping up to be one of the director's biggest releases in the Indian market. The film's performance is being closely watched by trade analysts who see it as a sign of strong audience interest in the mythological epic.

Global Numbers Tell a Similar Story

The film's success is not limited to India. Internationally, The Odyssey has recorded Nolan's best opening weekend for a non-Batman film, crossing the $120 million mark at the global box office. This puts it ahead of the opening weekends of titles like Inception and Interstellar.

The strong worldwide response suggests the film's blend of Greek mythology and Nolan's signature visual storytelling has found a wide audience. Early estimates had already pegged the film for a strong debut, and the actual numbers are matching those projections.

Critical Reception and Viewer Experience

Reviews for The Odyssey have been overwhelmingly positive. Critics have described it as a breathtaking epic that explores themes of men, monsters and moral metamorphosis, with some calling it Nolan's best work yet.

However, a significant point of discussion among fans is the availability of the film in the 'real' IMAX format. As explained in a separate report, many viewers may not be watching the film in the full-frame IMAX experience that Nolan prefers, due to the limited number of compatible screens in India. This has not dampened enthusiasm, but it remains a talking point for cinema purists.

What to Watch For

The film's performance over the weekend will determine its long-term run in India. If the positive word-of-mouth continues, The Odyssey could easily cross the Rs 100 crore mark in its first week. All eyes are now on the Sunday numbers to see if the trend holds.
