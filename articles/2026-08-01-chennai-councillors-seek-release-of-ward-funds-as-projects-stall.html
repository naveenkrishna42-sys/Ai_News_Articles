Chennai councillors seek release of ward funds as projects stall

📅 2026-08-01 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Councillors demand release of pending ward development funds
  • Projects across Chennai remain stalled ahead of term expiry
  • Civic body yet to confirm timeline for fund disbursal

Chennai's councillors have demanded the immediate release of ward development funds, warning that dozens of local infrastructure projects have ground to a halt just months before the council's term ends. The plea comes amid growing frustration among elected representatives that money allocated for roads, streetlights, and drainage works is stuck in administrative limbo.

Funds stuck, work stalled

Councillors from multiple zones say the delay is crippling small-scale works that were approved earlier this year. In several wards, contractors have downed tools after payments for completed phases were not released, leaving half-finished stretches of pavement and open trenches.

“The money was sanctioned in the budget, but we are being told there is no cash flow,” one councillor said on condition of anonymity. “We cannot face voters with incomplete roads and broken drains.”

The ward development fund, a key mechanism for local infrastructure upkeep, is meant to be spent within the financial year. With the council term set to end, unspent amounts could lapse, prompting the urgency.

What the councillors are asking

In a formal representation to the Greater Chennai Corporation, councillors have sought a clear timeline for releasing the pending instalments. They have also demanded that projects already tendered be fast-tracked so that work can be completed before the election code of conduct kicks in.

Some have called for a special review meeting with the corporation's finance wing to resolve the bottleneck. Others want the funds to be transferred directly to ward-level committees to avoid further delays.

The demand has gained traction on social media, with residents in several neighbourhoods posting photos of incomplete works and tagging civic officials.

Corporation's response awaited

Officials at the Greater Chennai Corporation have not yet confirmed whether the funds will be released before the term ends. A senior official, speaking off the record, said the issue was “under examination” and that cash flow constraints had affected multiple departments this year.

The corporation has not issued a public statement on the matter. Councillors say they will escalate the issue to the state government if no action is taken within a week.

The development comes at a politically sensitive time, with all 200 wards in the city gearing up for elections. Councillors from both ruling and opposition parties have united on this demand, making it a rare show of consensus.

What to watch next

All eyes are now on the corporation's next council meeting, where the fund release is expected to be taken up. Whether the civic body can clear the pending amounts before the term concludes will determine if stalled projects see the light of day.
