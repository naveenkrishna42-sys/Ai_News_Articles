Android Circuit: Galaxy Z Fold8 Flex Titanium, Pixel 11 Leaks, OnePlus Exit

Samsung Flex Titanium: A New Era for Foldables

Samsung has officially unveiled Flex Titanium, a new display technology that will debut in the upcoming Galaxy Z Fold8. The company announced the innovation ahead of its Galaxy Unpacked event scheduled for July 22, 2026.

Flex Titanium is designed to advance foldable displays by using a titanium-based substrate, making the screen more resistant to creasing and impact. Samsung claims this addresses one of the biggest complaints among foldable users: durability.

The Flex Titanium display is expected to be thinner and lighter than previous generations, while maintaining the high brightness and colour accuracy that Samsung's flagship panels are known for. The move signals Samsung's continued investment in foldable form factors as the category matures.

Galaxy Z Fold8 Battery Life Gets a Big Boost

European Union regulatory filings have revealed that the Galaxy Z Fold8 series will see a significant improvement in battery life. According to listings from GSMArena, the devices have received higher energy efficiency ratings compared to their predecessors.

The exact battery capacity has not been confirmed by Samsung, but the EU ratings suggest the Fold8 will last longer on a single charge, likely due to a combination of a larger cell and more power-efficient chipset. This addresses another key pain point for foldable users, who often struggle with battery endurance due to the larger displays.

Samsung is expected to provide full specifications during the Unpacked event later this month.

Pixel 11 Leaks: The Glow-Up We've Been Waiting For?

Leaks surrounding Google's Pixel 11 have started to surface, and early reports hint at a major camera upgrade. Dubbed the 'glow-up' by industry watchers, the Pixel 11 is rumoured to feature a new multi-lens camera system with enhanced low-light performance and computational photography capabilities.

While Google has not confirmed any details, leaked renders suggest a redesigned camera bar and a larger primary sensor. The Pixel series has always been strong in photography, and this iteration appears to aim for the top spot in mobile imaging.

More concrete information is expected closer to the Pixel 11's launch, likely in late 2026.

OnePlus Exits the Stage

In a surprising development, OnePlus has reportedly decided to leave the flagship smartphone market. The company, which once prided itself on being a 'flagship killer', is said to be shifting its focus to mid-range and budget devices.

Industry analysts point to intense competition from Xiaomi, Vivo, and Samsung as reasons for OnePlus's retreat. The brand's recent flagship models failed to capture the same excitement as earlier releases, and sales have been declining.

OnePlus has not officially commented on the reports, but insiders suggest the company will continue to support existing devices while winding down its premium lineup. This marks the end of an era for a brand that helped popularise high-spec, affordable flagships in India and globally.

What's Next?

With Samsung and Google pushing the boundaries of hardware and AI, the Android ecosystem is evolving rapidly. Samsung's Galaxy Unpacked on July 22 will be the next major milestone, where the Galaxy Z Fold8 and other devices will be officially unveiled. All eyes are on whether Flex Titanium lives up to the hype.
