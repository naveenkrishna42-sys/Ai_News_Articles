Incident Reported at Popular Mumbai Eatery

A customer has filed a formal complaint against a Mumbai-based outlet of Jimis Burger following an alarming discovery. The customer alleged that a piece of glass was found inside a burger served at the establishment, raising serious concerns regarding food safety and quality control standards.

The incident, which was brought to light this week, has prompted local authorities to initiate an inquiry. While the restaurant chain is known for its popular burger offerings, this specific complaint has cast a shadow over its operational hygiene practices.

Police Intervention and Ongoing Investigation

Local law enforcement officials confirmed that a complaint has been lodged regarding the foreign object found in the food. Officers are currently reviewing the details provided by the customer to determine how a glass shard could have entered the preparation area.

The investigation aims to establish whether the contamination was an isolated incident or a failure in the outlet's standard operating procedures. Investigators have not yet confirmed if any staff members have been summoned for questioning or if a site inspection has been conducted at the premises.
