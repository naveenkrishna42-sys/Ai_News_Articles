Awarapan 2 Leads Batwara 1947 in Twitter Poll, Trade Predicts 2x Opening

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Twitter poll shows Awarapan 2 leading Batwara 1947 by 20%.
  • Trade analysts predict Awarapan 2 to open 2x higher than Batwara 1947.
  • Both films are set for release, with Emraan Hashmi and Sunny Deol in lead roles.

The clash between Emraan Hashmi's Awarapan 2 and Sunny Deol's Batwara 1947 is heating up, and early audience buzz suggests a clear winner. A recent Twitter poll conducted by a popular entertainment portal shows Hashmi's film leading by a significant 20% margin over Deol's period drama. The numbers, while not scientific, offer a snapshot of public anticipation ahead of the releases.

Poll Numbers and Audience Pulse

The poll, which ran over 24 hours, asked users which film they were most excited to watch. Awarapan 2 secured roughly 60% of the votes, while Batwara 1947 trailed at 40%. This gap, though modest, is being read by trade watchers as a reflection of the franchise pull that Hashmi enjoys, especially after the cult status of the original Awarapan (2007).

Sunny Deol's recent box office resurgence, following the massive success of Gadar 2, was expected to narrow the gap. However, the poll suggests that younger audiences may be more drawn to Hashmi's gritty romantic-action brand. "The Twitter poll is a good indicator of urban youth sentiment," noted a trade analyst, adding that it doesn't always translate to mass-market footfalls.

Trade Predictions: A Two-Fold Opening

Based on current buzz, advance bookings, and the poll results, trade circles are predicting that Awarapan 2 will open at least two times higher than Batwara 1947 on day one. If the trend holds, Hashmi's film could see a first-day collection in the range of ₹8-10 crore, while Deol's might settle around ₹4-5 crore.

This prediction is notable because Batwara 1947 carries the weight of a historical narrative and Deol's star power, which traditionally appeals to single-screen audiences. Yet, the current market seems to favor the sequel's blend of action and nostalgia, which has a broader appeal across multiplexes and single screens alike.

The Franchise Factor vs. Period Drama

Awarapan 2 benefits from a pre-existing fan base that has waited nearly two decades for a sequel. The film's music, led by a recreated version of the iconic title track, has already gone viral. In contrast, Batwara 1947 is a fresh property, though its Independence Day release date is strategically timed to tap into patriotic fervor.

Analysts caution that the Twitter poll, while indicative of online engagement, does not guarantee box office results. "Online polls often skew toward younger, more vocal fanbases," explained a Mumbai-based exhibitor. "The real test will be the opening weekend, especially in mass circuits where Deol has a stronghold."

What Lies Ahead for Both Films

With both films slated for release in the coming weeks, the promotional campaigns are in full swing. Hashmi has been touring cities for Awarapan 2, while Deol has leaned on his Gadar legacy to push Batwara 1947 as a family entertainer. The next few days will see final advance booking numbers, which will offer a clearer picture.

For now, all eyes are on the opening day figures. If Awarapan 2 indeed doubles Batwara 1947's collection, it will reinforce the sequel trend that has dominated Bollywood since 2023. Conversely, a strong word-of-mouth for the period drama could still upset early predictions. The box office, as always, will have the final say.
