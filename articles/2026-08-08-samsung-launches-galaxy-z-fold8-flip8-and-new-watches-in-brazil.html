Samsung launches Galaxy Z Fold8, Flip8, and new watches in Brazil

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung launched five new devices in Brazil: three foldables and two smartwatches.
  • The lineup includes the premium Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra alongside the standard Fold8 and Flip8.
  • New watches, Watch Ultra2 and Watch9, accompany the foldable launches.
  • The expansion comes as Apple is reportedly exploring foldable devices.

Samsung has officially expanded its foldable smartphone lineup in Brazil, introducing five new devices to the market. The launch includes the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Galaxy Z Fold8, Galaxy Z Flip8, along with the Galaxy Watch Ultra2 and Galaxy Watch9. The announcement was confirmed through Samsung's official channels on Friday.

New devices in the lineup

The Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra sits at the top of the range, offering a larger and more premium foldable experience. It joins the standard Galaxy Z Fold8, which continues the company's book-style foldable design, and the Galaxy Z Flip8, which retains the compact clamshell form factor.

The two smartwatches, Watch Ultra2 and Watch9, expand Samsung's wearable ecosystem in the region. While the company has not yet disclosed full specifications for the Brazilian market, the devices are expected to mirror global variants.

Market context and competition

The launch comes at a critical time for the foldable segment. Apple is reportedly exploring foldable devices, a move that could reshape the market. Samsung's aggressive expansion in Brazil appears aimed at consolidating its lead before potential new entrants arrive.

Industry observers have noted that Samsung's new design language is finding its audience. The devices have already gone on sale in India, according to The Times of India, suggesting a coordinated global rollout.

Brazil as a strategic market

Brazil represents a significant opportunity for Samsung, given its large consumer base and growing demand for premium smartphones. The company has invested heavily in local manufacturing and distribution to make devices more accessible.

Pricing and availability details for the Brazilian market have not been fully outlined yet. Samsung typically offers installment plans and trade-in programs in the region to ease adoption of high-end devices.

What this means for consumers

For Brazilian consumers, the expanded lineup means more choice in the foldable category. The Z Flip8 offers a more affordable entry point, while the Z Fold8 and Fold8 Ultra cater to productivity-focused users.

The Watch Ultra2 targets fitness enthusiasts, while the Watch9 serves as a general-purpose wearable. Together, they reinforce Samsung's ecosystem strategy, encouraging users to stay within its hardware family.

Analysts will be watching whether the new devices can sustain Samsung's momentum in Brazil, particularly as competitors like Motorola and Xiaomi strengthen their own portfolios.

As Apple circles the foldable market, Samsung's early mover advantage in regions like Brazil could prove decisive. The coming months will reveal how Brazilian consumers respond to the new lineup and whether Samsung can maintain its leadership as the foldable segment heats up.
