Ravi Kishan's Remark Sparks Political Debate

Ravi Kishan, the former Indian Premier League (IPL) player and current Member of Parliament (MP) from Gorakhpur, has made a remark comparing the city to Spain. His statement, 'Hamke lagaraha Spain' (We feel like Spain), has sparked a political debate in Uttar Pradesh.

Yogi Adityanath's Reaction

Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath has reacted to Ravi Kishan's remark. His reaction has been described as a mix of amusement and surprise, with some reports suggesting he couldn't stop smiling.

Congress' Political Reaction

The Congress party has also reacted to the remark. They have shared a video comparing Gorakhpur and Spain, highlighting the differences between the two places.
