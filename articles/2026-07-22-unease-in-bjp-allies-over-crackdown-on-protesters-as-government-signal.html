Unease in BJP, allies over crackdown on protesters as government signals outreach

📅 2026-07-22 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • BJP and its allies are uneasy over the police crackdown on CJP protesters in Delhi.
  • A 21-year-old woman injured in the protest has been taken off the ventilator and is stable.
  • Another 20-year-old protester remains on ventilator; one had a pellet in the eye.
  • Government signals possible outreach amid growing criticism.
  • Police action described as stampede-like by some reports.

NEW DELHI: The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) and its allies are showing signs of unease over the police crackdown on protesters near the Parliament complex. The protests were organised by the Confederation of Progressive Students (CJP) under the banner 'Sansad Chalo'.

The unease comes at a time when the government has signalled a potential outreach to address the situation. The move is seen as an attempt to calm frayed nerves within the ruling coalition.

Protesters' Injuries Raise Concerns

A 21-year-old woman who was critically injured during the protest has been taken off the ventilator at RML Hospital. Doctors have confirmed that she is stable now.

However, a 20-year-old protester remains on ventilator support. He was injured in what some reports describe as a stampede-like situation during the police action. Another protester suffered a pellet injury in the eye.

Medical teams at the hospital continue to monitor the condition of those injured. Officials have not yet released the names of the injured individuals.

Allies Express Discomfort

Sources within the National Democratic Alliance (NDA) indicate that some allies have expressed discomfort over the heavy-handed approach. The BJP's own rank and file are reportedly divided on the issue.

A senior leader from an allied party, speaking on condition of anonymity, said: "The way the situation was handled has created unnecessary trouble. We are not against maintaining law and order, but there needs to be a balance."

The BJP leadership has taken note of these concerns. Party managers are now working on damage control.

Government's Outreach Signal

In a shift from its earlier stance, the government has indicated willingness to engage with the protesting groups. Sources say backchannel communications have been initiated.

A senior government official said: "We are open to dialogue. The aim is to ensure that such incidents do not recur. The safety of protesters and the public is paramount."

The outreach is being read as an attempt to prevent the issue from snowballing into a larger political crisis. The opposition has already seized on the incident to attack the government.

Opposition leaders have demanded a statement in Parliament and a judicial probe into the crackdown. The government has not yet responded to these demands.

What happens next depends on how quickly the government can bring its allies on board and whether the injured protesters recover fully. The political fallout is still unfolding.
