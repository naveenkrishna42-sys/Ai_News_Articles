Kartik Aaryan's 'Captain India' Kicks Off London Shoot

Bollywood actor Kartik Aaryan is set to begin filming his upcoming project 'Captain India' in London on September 1. The film, directed by Shimit Amin, marks a significant milestone for the project, as it is expected to go on floors in London next week.

Shimit Amin Returns to Direct After 18 Years

Shimit Amin, known for his work on the film 'Rocket Singh' in 2008, is back behind the camera for 'Captain India'. This return to directing after a gap of 18 years is a significant development in the Indian film industry, as it showcases Amin's continued relevance and interest in the industry.
