BCCI Secures Rs 130 Cr 2‑Year Deal with Campa, SBI Life & ChatGPT

Deal Overview and Financial Upside

The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) has finalized a two‑year associate‑partner agreement worth over Rs 130 crore. The trio—Campa Cola, SBI Life Insurance and OpenAI’s ChatGPT—will support the India Home Cricket programme, which includes domestic leagues, women's cricket and grassroots initiatives.

At Rs 130 crore, the package is 44% larger than the previous cycle’s Rs 90 crore deal, signalling heightened sponsor confidence in cricket’s domestic reach.

Partner Contributions and Strategic Fit

1. Campa Cola – Refreshment Brand Revamp

Campa Cola, a legacy Indian soft‑drink, will leverage stadium signage, on‑field branding and limited‑edition packaging tied to match days. The brand aims to revive its market share by tapping into cricket’s massive viewership.

2. SBI Life – Financial Services Integration

SBI Life will embed insurance micro‑products within the BCCI app, offering match‑day coverage and retirement plans for players and fans. The partnership also includes co‑branded content on financial literacy.

3. ChatGPT – AI‑Powered Fan Interaction

OpenAI’s ChatGPT will be embedded in the official BCCI digital platforms, providing real‑time match queries, player stats and personalized content. The AI layer is expected to boost average session time by up to 15%.

Real‑World Buyer Implications

For advertisers, the deal expands inventory across traditional and digital touchpoints. Brands can now purchase combined packages that include stadium LED boards, in‑app AI prompts and co‑branded insurance offers, simplifying media planning.

From a pricing perspective, the Rs 130 crore figure translates to roughly Rs 5.4 crore per month, or about Rs 1.2 lakh per minute of live broadcast exposure, a premium justified by the integrated AI experience and cross‑category reach.

Performance Benchmarks and Value Justification

Early pilot tests of ChatGPT during the IPL indicated a 12% lift in fan engagement metrics, with query response times under two seconds. Campa Cola’s limited‑edition cans sold 8% more in stadium kiosks during the pilot phase, confirming the brand‑activation potential.

SBI Life reported a 6% increase in policy inquiries via the BCCI app within the first month, highlighting the efficacy of contextual insurance offers.

Key Takeaways for Stakeholders

  • Revenue boost: Rs 130 crore injects fresh capital into domestic cricket development.
  • Cross‑category synergy: Beverage, insurance and AI converge on a single platform.
  • Data advantage: ChatGPT’s analytics will inform future sponsorship pricing.
