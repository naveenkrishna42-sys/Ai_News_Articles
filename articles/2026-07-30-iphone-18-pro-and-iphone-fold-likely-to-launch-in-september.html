iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Fold Likely to Launch in September

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Fold expected to launch in September 2026.
  • Forbes reports spring delay may boost autumn sales of the Pro model.
  • Economic Times indicates iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max could cost up to Rs 26,000 more.
  • Moneycontrol confirms bigger battery and new camera for the Pro Max.
  • 9to5Mac suggests the iPhone 18 Pro is a compelling upgrade for many users.

Apple is reportedly gearing up for a major September 2026 launch event, with the iPhone 18 Pro and the company's first foldable iPhone, the iPhone Fold, expected to take centre stage. Industry sources suggest these devices will mark a significant shift in Apple's product strategy, combining iterative improvements with a new form factor.

Price Hikes and Market Strategy

The Economic Times has reported that the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max could see price increases of up to Rs 26,000 in India. This would position the Pro models at a significantly higher price point than their predecessors. The exact pricing structure has not been confirmed by Apple, but analysts expect the foldable model to command a premium over even the most expensive Pro Max variant.

Forbes has noted that a potential spring delay for standard iPhone 18 models might actually boost autumn sales of the Pro versions. The logic is that consumers who have been waiting for an upgrade will be more likely to opt for the higher-end Pro models when they launch in September. This strategy could help Apple maximise revenue per unit.

Hardware Upgrades and Features

Moneycontrol has highlighted that the iPhone 18 Pro Max is expected to feature a bigger battery and an all-new camera system. While specific specifications remain under wraps, leaks suggest a periscope lens with enhanced optical zoom will be a key selling point. The foldable iPhone, meanwhile, is rumoured to use a durable glass and metal hinge design, aiming to address durability concerns that have plagued other foldable devices.

9to5Mac has described the iPhone 18 Pro as a “no-brainer upgrade” for many users. The device is expected to include Apple's next-generation A18 Pro chip, improved thermal management, and a brighter, more power-efficient display. The combination of these upgrades could make it a compelling option for users of older iPhones.

What to Watch Next

All eyes are now on Apple's official announcement, expected in early September. The company will need to balance its premium pricing strategy with consumer demand, particularly in price-sensitive markets like India. The success of the iPhone Fold will also test whether Apple can replicate its smartphone dominance in the foldable segment, a space where competitors like Samsung have already established a strong foothold.
