15-Year-Old Sooryavanshi Jumps 230 Spots in ICC T20I Rankings

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Vaibhav Sooryavanshi rose 230 places in the ICC T20I batting rankings.
  • The 15-year-old's leap follows his performance in the Zimbabwe series.
  • Shubman Gill reclaimed the No. 1 spot in the ICC ODI batting rankings.
  • Joe Root returned to the top ten of the ODI batting rankings.
  • Rohit Sharma is closing in on the top three in ODI rankings.

Teen Sensation Sooryavanshi Makes Historic Leap

Indian cricket's youngest rising star, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, has stormed up the ICC T20I batting rankings. The 15-year-old jumped a staggering 230 places after his performances in the recent series against Zimbabwe.

The new rankings, released by the International Cricket Council, place Sooryavanshi among the most improved players in the format. His rapid rise has caught the attention of fans and analysts alike.

Sooryavanshi's leap is one of the largest single-rank jumps in recent T20I history. Officials have not yet confirmed his exact new ranking number, but the magnitude of the climb underscores his impact.

Gill Back on Top in ODI Rankings

Meanwhile, Shubman Gill has reclaimed the No. 1 spot in the ICC ODI batting rankings. The opener's consistent run of form in the 50-over format has pushed him past his rivals.

Gill's return to the summit marks a significant milestone in his career. He had previously held the top spot earlier this year before being overtaken.

Rohit Sharma is also closing in on the top three in the ODI rankings. The Indian captain's recent scores have moved him up the ladder, putting pressure on those above him.

Root Returns to Top Ten

England's Joe Root has made a comeback to the top ten of the ODI batting rankings. Root's resurgence in limited-overs cricket has been a talking point among selectors.

The rankings also saw movement among bowlers and all-rounders, though no other changes were as dramatic as Sooryavanshi's. The Zimbabwe series proved to be a launchpad for several young Indian players.

Sooryavanshi's performances included crucial knocks under pressure. His ability to handle international bowling at such a young age has drawn comparisons to earlier prodigies.

What This Means for Indian Cricket

The dual development of Sooryavanshi's rise and Gill's return to No. 1 bodes well for India's batting depth. The team now has a mix of youth and experience at the top of the order.

Selection headaches may arise as more players stake their claim. Sooryavanshi's rapid ascent could fast-track his inclusion in future ICC events.

The next update to the ICC rankings is expected after the upcoming bilateral series. All eyes will be on Sooryavanshi to see if he can hold onto his gains.
