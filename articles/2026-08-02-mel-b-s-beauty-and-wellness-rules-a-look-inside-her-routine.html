Mel B's Beauty and Wellness Rules: A Look Inside Her Routine

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Lifestyle Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mel B opened up about her beauty and wellness philosophy in a recent interview with The Handbook.
  • She emphasised the importance of consistency over complexity in skincare and fitness.
  • Her routine includes simple, accessible habits rather than expensive or elaborate treatments.

Mel B, the Spice Girls icon known for her fierce stage presence and unapologetic personality, has shared the beauty and wellness principles that keep her feeling her best. In a candid conversation with The Handbook, the 51-year-old singer and television personality revealed that her approach is far more grounded than one might expect from a global pop star.

Rather than chasing fleeting trends or elaborate multi-step rituals, Mel B's philosophy centres on consistency, self-care, and listening to her body. The rules she lives by are refreshingly simple, yet they reflect a discipline honed over decades in the public eye.

Skincare: Less Is More

Mel B's skincare routine is built on a few non-negotiable steps. She stresses the importance of cleansing and moisturising daily, regardless of how tired she might be after a long day or a late-night performance. "Your skin is the first thing people see," she told The Handbook, "so you have to treat it with respect."

She is a firm believer in sunscreen, applying it every morning even when she's not planning to spend time outdoors. For her, protection is the most effective anti-ageing strategy. She also avoids over-complicating things, preferring products that are gentle and effective over those with a long list of active ingredients she can't pronounce.

Hydration and Sleep: The Non-Negotiables

Water, she says, is her secret weapon. Mel B aims to drink at least two litres a day, and she credits this simple habit with keeping her skin plump and her energy levels stable. She admits it's not always easy, but she treats it as a daily target rather than an option.

Sleep is another pillar of her routine. The singer is candid about the fact that she doesn't get eight hours every night, but she prioritises rest whenever she can. "I'm not a robot," she joked, "but when I do sleep well, I see the difference in my face and my mood."

Fitness: Move Your Body, Your Way

Exercise for Mel B is not about punishment or achieving a specific body shape. It's about feeling strong and releasing stress. She mixes high-intensity workouts with gentler practices like yoga and walking, depending on how she feels on any given day. The key, she insists, is to find movement you enjoy, because that's what you'll stick with.

She also emphasizes the mental benefits of staying active. For her, a workout is as much for her head as it is for her body. "When I'm feeling overwhelmed, I go for a run or put on some music and dance," she said. "It resets everything."

Mindset and Self-Care: The Inner Work

Mel B is open about her journey with mental health, and she places as much importance on emotional wellness as on physical appearance. She practices daily affirmations and takes time each morning to set an intention for the day. This, she says, helps her stay grounded amid the chaos of her career.

She also advocates for regular "me time" — whether that's a long bath, reading a book, or simply sitting in silence with a cup of tea. It's a rule she insists on, even when her schedule is packed. "You can't pour from an empty cup," she noted.

Nutrition: Fuel, Not Fad

When it comes to food, Mel B avoids strict diets. She prefers a balanced approach: plenty of vegetables, lean proteins, and whole grains, with the occasional treat. She has learned that deprivation only leads to cravings, so she allows herself indulgences without guilt.

She also swears by starting her day with a warm glass of lemon water, a habit she says kick-starts her digestion and gives her a gentle energy boost. Beyond that, she listens to her body's cues. "If I'm hungry, I eat. If I'm tired, I rest," she explained. "It sounds simple, but it took me years to learn."

As Mel B continues to tour, appear on television, and manage her various projects, she remains committed to these rules. For her, beauty and wellness are not about perfection; they are about showing up for yourself every day.

Fans can expect Mel B to continue sharing her journey, both on stage and in interviews. With a memoir reportedly in the works and ongoing public appearances, her philosophy of simplicity and self-respect is likely to inspire many more women to adopt a kinder, more sustainable approach to their own routines.
