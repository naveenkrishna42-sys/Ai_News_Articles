Modi Launches Anti-Drug Campaign

📅 2026-08-03 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • PM Modi launches anti-drug campaign
  • Aim is to combat drug abuse among youth
  • Part of effort to connect with Gen 'Amrit'

Introduction to the Campaign

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has launched the 'Nasha Mukt Yuva for Viksit Bharat Sankalp Abhiyan', a nationwide campaign aimed at combating drug abuse among the younger generation, dubbed Gen 'Amrit'. This initiative is part of the government's efforts to connect with this demographic and address their concerns.

Objective of the Campaign

The objective of the 'Nasha Mukt Yuva for Viksit Bharat Sankalp Abhiyan' is to create awareness about the dangers of drug abuse and to encourage young people to refrain from using intoxicating substances. The campaign will also focus on providing support and rehabilitation to those affected by drug addiction.

Launch Event and Participation

The campaign was launched at the Sathya Sai school in Visakhapatnam, which was selected for this purpose. The event was attended by various dignitaries, including the Tamil Nadu Governor, who participated in the launch of the 'Intoxication-Free Youth' campaign. The Chief Minister also warned drug traffickers of tough legal action and bulldozer measures.

Government's Efforts to Connect with Youth

The launch of this campaign is seen as part of the government's efforts to connect with Gen 'Amrit' and address their concerns. The government has been using social media platforms, including Instagram, to reach out to the younger generation and promote its initiatives.

Officials have not yet confirmed the details of the campaign's implementation and the resources allocated for it. However, it is expected that the campaign will be implemented across the country, with the involvement of various stakeholders, including schools, colleges, and community organizations.

Next Steps

The government is expected to announce more details about the campaign in the coming days, including the allocation of funds and the role of various stakeholders. The success of the campaign will depend on the active participation of the youth and the effective implementation of the government's plans.
