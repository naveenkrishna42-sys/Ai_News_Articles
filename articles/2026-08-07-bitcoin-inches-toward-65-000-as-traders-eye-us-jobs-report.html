Bitcoin inches toward $65,000 as traders eye US jobs report

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin edged higher to around $65,000 ahead of the US non-farm payrolls report.
  • Traders are watching the jobs data for clues on the Federal Reserve's next rate move.
  • Crypto markets remained cautiously optimistic, with gains seen across major tokens.

Bitcoin climbs as markets hold breath for jobs report

Bitcoin nudged up to roughly $65,000 on Friday morning, as traders across the crypto market braced for the release of key US employment data. The world's largest cryptocurrency has been trading in a tight band over the past few days, with investors reluctant to make big bets before the numbers land.

The upcoming non-farm payrolls report is expected to offer fresh clues on the health of the US economy and, more importantly, the trajectory of interest rates. A stronger-than-expected jobs figure could prompt the Federal Reserve to keep rates higher for longer, a scenario that typically pressures risk assets like Bitcoin. Conversely, a weak print might revive hopes for rate cuts, giving digital currencies a boost.

Market watchers weigh rate cut odds

According to data from CME FedWatch, traders have priced in a roughly 50% chance of a rate cut in September, though the odds have fluctuated in recent weeks as economic indicators have come in mixed. The jobs report, scheduled for release later in the day, could tip the balance in either direction.

Bitcoin's recent resilience, despite lingering macroeconomic uncertainty, has surprised some analysts. The asset has held above the $60,000 mark for most of the past fortnight, supported by steady inflows into spot exchange-traded funds and growing institutional interest.

Altcoins follow Bitcoin's lead

Other major cryptocurrencies also traded in positive territory on Friday, with Ethereum, Solana, and Ripple's XRP recording modest gains of between 1% and 2% over the past 24 hours. The total crypto market capitalisation stood at around $2.4 trillion, according to CoinMarketCap data.

Trading volumes, however, remained subdued compared with recent peaks, suggesting that many participants are waiting on the sidelines. "The market is in a holding pattern," said one Mumbai-based crypto analyst, speaking on condition of anonymity. "Everyone is waiting for the jobs number to decide which way to lean."

India's crypto scene waits for policy clarity

In India, the crypto community continues to watch global developments with one eye on domestic regulatory discussions. The government has yet to finalise a comprehensive framework for digital assets, though industry insiders expect some clarity in the coming months. Indian exchanges have reported steady trading volumes, with Bitcoin and Ethereum dominating activity.

The Reserve Bank of India has maintained a cautious stance on cryptocurrencies, but recent statements from finance ministry officials have hinted at a possible consultative paper. For now, Indian traders are largely mirroring global sentiment, with Bitcoin's price action in rupee terms closely tracking international moves.

What happens next

All eyes will be on the US Bureau of Labor Statistics release at 8:30 am ET. A surprise in either direction could spark sharp volatility across crypto and traditional markets alike. Bitcoin's ability to hold above $65,000 will be a key test of bullish momentum in the short term.

Beyond the jobs report, traders will also monitor any commentary from Fed officials in the coming days, as well as ongoing developments in the ETF space. For now, the market remains cautiously optimistic, but the next few hours could set the tone for the week ahead.
