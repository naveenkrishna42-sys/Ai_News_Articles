Spider-Man: Brand New Day Box Office Day 5: Tom Holland's Film Sees First Major Dip in India

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Film grosses nearly Rs 350 crore in India in five days
  • First major drop in daily collections recorded on Tuesday
  • IMAX release expected to boost numbers further
  • Global opening weekend surpassed Avengers: Endgame

MUMBAI: Tom Holland's latest Spider-Man outing, 'Brand New Day', continues to swing high at the Indian box office, but the momentum has finally shown a crack. On its fifth day, the film witnessed its first significant drop in daily collections, yet the overall numbers remain staggering.

Trade estimates suggest the film has raced towards the Rs 350 crore gross mark in India alone, a figure that places it among the fastest to achieve the milestone in the country. The Tuesday slowdown, while expected after a record-breaking weekend, has not dampened industry optimism.

Record-Breaking Start

The film opened to unprecedented numbers across the country, shattering multiple records in its first three days. According to international reports, 'Brand New Day' accumulated $360 million globally in its opening weekend, surpassing the previous all-time high set by 'Avengers: Endgame'.

This achievement has been widely celebrated by fans and analysts alike, marking a new benchmark for superhero cinema. The Indian market contributed significantly to this global haul, with multiplexes and single screens reporting packed houses over the weekend.

Day 5 Dip and IMAX Boost

Despite the dip on Day 5, exhibitors remain confident about the film's longevity. The upcoming IMAX release is expected to give collections a fresh push, especially among audiences who prefer the premium large-format experience.

Industry insiders note that a mid-week drop is normal for big-ticket releases, and the real test will come with the second weekend. The addition of IMAX screens could help sustain momentum and attract repeat viewers.

Global Phenomenon

The film's performance has not just been limited to India. Reports from international trade publications highlight that 'Brand New Day' has broken multiple box office records worldwide, including the biggest opening weekend in history. This has solidified Tom Holland's position as a leading draw for the franchise.

Critics have also weighed in, with some praising the introduction of new characters and technological elements, while others have pointed out narrative flaws and potential future consequences that could shape the wider Marvel universe.

What Lies Ahead

As the film enters its second week, all eyes are on how it holds up against new releases and whether it can maintain its pace. The IMAX rollout is likely to provide a temporary spike, but sustained collections will depend on word-of-mouth and repeat viewings.

For now, 'Brand New Day' stands as a testament to the enduring appeal of Spider-Man in India, and the numbers suggest the web-slinger's journey is far from over.
