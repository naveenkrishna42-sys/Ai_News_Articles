Balakrishna undergoes knee surgery after muscle tear on 'NBK 111' set

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Actor Balakrishna sustained a left knee injury while filming an action sequence for 'NBK 111'.
  • Hospital confirmed a possible left knee muscle tear, later diagnosed as a torn quadriceps tendon.
  • He underwent a three-hour surgery at AIG Hospitals in Hyderabad.
  • Recovery timeline and impact on film schedule have not been officially announced.

Nandamuri Balakrishna was hospitalised in Hyderabad on Wednesday after suffering a knee injury on the set of his upcoming film, tentatively titled 'NBK 111'. The actor, who was shooting for an action sequence, was immediately rushed to AIG Hospitals, where doctors confirmed a possible left knee muscle tear.

According to hospital sources, the injury was later diagnosed as a torn quadriceps tendon. Balakrishna underwent a three-hour surgical procedure to repair the damage. The surgery, performed by a team of orthopaedic specialists, is said to have gone as planned.

Nature of the injury

The injury occurred during a physically demanding scene for the film, which is directed by Anil Ravipudi. The actor was performing a stunt when he felt a sharp pain in his left knee. Medical staff on set provided initial first aid before he was moved to the hospital for further evaluation.

Medical reports indicate that a quadriceps tendon tear is a serious injury that typically requires surgical intervention and a prolonged recovery period. The tendon connects the quadriceps muscle to the kneecap and is essential for straightening the leg.

Hospital statement and treatment

AIG Hospitals confirmed the surgery in a brief statement, noting that Balakrishna was in stable condition. “The actor sustained an injury to his left knee during a film shoot. After examination, a quadriceps tendon tear was identified, and he underwent surgery. He is currently under observation,” the statement read.

The hospital has not provided a specific timeline for his discharge or full recovery. Sources indicate that post-surgery rehabilitation will be critical, and the actor may require several weeks of physiotherapy before returning to normal activity.

Impact on film production

‘NBK 111’ is one of the most anticipated Telugu releases, with production underway on a tight schedule. The film also stars Sreeleela in a lead role. Industry insiders speculate that the shooting schedule may face delays, though the production house has not issued an official statement.

Balakrishna, known for his action-oriented roles, has previously dealt with injuries on set. His age and the nature of this injury could mean a longer break from physically demanding sequences. The director and producers are likely to assess the situation in the coming days.

Fans and colleagues from the film industry have taken to social media to wish the actor a speedy recovery. Several prominent figures from Telugu cinema have expressed their concern and support.

What to watch

The actor's recovery timeline will determine how soon filming can resume for 'NBK 111'. An official update from the production team regarding revised schedules and Balakrishna's health status is expected in the next few days.
