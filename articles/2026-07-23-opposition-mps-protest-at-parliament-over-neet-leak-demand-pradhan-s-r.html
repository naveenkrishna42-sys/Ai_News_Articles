Opposition MPs Protest at Parliament Over NEET Leak, Demand Pradhan's Resignation

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Opposition MPs protested at Parliament over the NEET exam leak, demanding Dharmendra Pradhan's resignation.
  • Government reached out to opposition for a debate on NEET, urging talks without preconditions.
  • MPs from both sides held a face-off outside Parliament with slogans and counter-protests.

Opposition MPs Rally at Parliament Over NEET Leak

Opposition MPs staged a protest at Parliament on Thursday over the NEET exam leak, demanding the resignation of Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan. The protest escalated as lawmakers gathered outside the Parliament House, with slogans echoing through the complex.

The incident marks the latest flashpoint in a growing controversy surrounding the medical entrance exam. Opposition leaders have alleged a systemic failure, holding the minister accountable for the breach that compromised the integrity of the examination process.

Government Extends Olive Branch for Debate

In response to the escalating standoff, the government reached out to opposition parties, urging them to participate in a debate on the NEET issue without preconditions. Sources within the ruling coalition indicated a willingness to address concerns but maintained that the debate should not be tied to demands for resignations.

The Times of India reported that the government's outreach was aimed at defusing tensions and ensuring parliamentary proceedings could continue smoothly. However, opposition leaders remained skeptical, insisting that a debate would be meaningless without accountability at the top.

Rule 267 and Parliamentary Tactics

The opposition has invoked Rule 267 in the Rajya Sabha, a provision that allows for the suspension of scheduled business to discuss an urgent matter. This move, as reported by The Hindu, is a procedural tactic to force a discussion on the NEET leak and Pradhan's role.

Government MPs countered by arguing that the demand for resignation was premature and that the issue should be investigated first. The face-off outside Parliament saw both sides exchanging slogans, with BJP MPs accusing the opposition of disrupting the session for political gain.

Face-Off Between Opposition and Ruling MPs

The protest turned into a direct confrontation as opposition and NDA MPs held a face-off outside Parliament House. Telegraph India reported that the atmosphere was charged, with counter-protests intensifying as each side tried to outshout the other.

BJP MPs accused the opposition of playing politics with a serious issue, while opposition leaders argued that the government was shielding Pradhan. The standoff highlighted the deep divisions over the handling of the NEET leak, which has sparked nationwide outrage among students and parents.

Officials have not yet confirmed the full extent of the leak or the number of candidates affected. Investigations are ongoing, but the political fallout has already disrupted parliamentary proceedings.

What Happens Next

The government has called for talks without preconditions, but the opposition remains firm on its demand for Pradhan's resignation. The next few days will determine whether a debate can proceed or if the standoff will continue to stall Parliament.
