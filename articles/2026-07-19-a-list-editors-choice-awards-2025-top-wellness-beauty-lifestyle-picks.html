St. Louis Magazine Reveals 2025 A-List Winners

St. Louis Magazine has announced its A-List Editors’ Choice Awards for 2025, spotlighting the best in wellness, beauty, and lifestyle across the region. The awards, now in their latest edition, aim to guide readers toward top-tier local businesses and services.

The editorial team curated the list after months of research and evaluation. Each winner reflects a blend of quality, innovation, and community impact, according to the magazine.

Wellness Category Highlights

In wellness, the editors recognized spas, fitness studios, and holistic health centers. One standout is a downtown yoga studio that offers trauma-informed classes, praised for its inclusive approach.

A local organic juice bar also made the cut, known for its cold-pressed blends and sustainable sourcing. The magazine noted that these choices cater to St. Louisans seeking mindful living.

Beauty Picks Spread Across Salons and Skincare

The beauty category features salons specializing in clean haircare and eco-friendly products. A botanical skincare line based in the Central West End earned top marks for its use of locally grown ingredients.

Editors also highlighted a makeup artist whose studio focuses on hypoallergenic brands. The selections aim to simplify choices for readers tired of overwhelming beauty aisles.

Lifestyle Favorites Include Dining and Home Goods

Lifestyle winners range from farm-to-table restaurants to boutique home decor stores. A family-run cafe in the Delmar Loop was applauded for its seasonal menu and community programs.

Another pick is a zero-waste shop in Clayton that sells reusable household items. The magazine stressed that these businesses represent the best of St. Louis living.

Each winner will be featured in the magazine's upcoming print edition. Readers can also find the full list online, along with editor notes on why each choice stands out.

The awards have become a trusted resource for locals and visitors alike. They reflect the city's growing emphasis on conscious consumerism and local entrepreneurship.

St. Louis Magazine plans to host a celebratory event for winners later this year. Details about the event are expected to be released in the coming weeks.
