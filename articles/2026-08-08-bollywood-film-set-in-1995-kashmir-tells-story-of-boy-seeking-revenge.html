A new Bollywood film is generating buzz for its gritty portrayal of 1995 Kashmir, a period marked by intense conflict. The film appears to use this historical backdrop to explore how personal grief can be weaponised, pushing ordinary individuals into extraordinary and often destructive paths.

For the film's makers, this period offers a potent canvas to examine themes of loss, radicalisation, and the cyclical nature of violence.
