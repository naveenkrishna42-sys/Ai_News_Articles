CNG prices surge in Delhi-NCR as LNG costs rise

📅 2026-08-29 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • CNG prices in Delhi-NCR increase by Rs 3.89/kg
  • Prices now near Rs 87/kg, up from Rs 84/kg
  • Rising LNG costs drive up CNG prices
  • Impact on auto fuel costs and consumer spending

CNG Prices Surge in Delhi-NCR

CNG prices in Delhi-NCR have seen a significant hike, increasing by Rs 3.89 per kilogram. This surge brings the price close to Rs 87/kg, a notable increase from the previous rate of Rs 84/kg. The rise is attributed to the increasing costs of LNG, the primary feedstock for CNG production.

Why the Increase?

The hike in CNG prices is primarily due to the rising costs of LNG. LNG, or Liquefied Natural Gas, is the primary raw material used in the production of CNG. As the global demand for LNG increases, so does its price, which in turn affects the cost of CNG. This is a classic example of the ripple effect of global commodity price fluctuations on local fuel costs.

Impact on Auto Fuel Costs

The increase in CNG prices is expected to have a significant impact on auto fuel costs. CNG is a popular choice for vehicles in Delhi-NCR, particularly in the public transport sector. A hike in CNG prices will inevitably lead to higher fuel costs for these vehicles, which in turn will be passed on to the consumers. This could potentially lead to a rise in the fare of public transport services, which could further strain the budget of the common man.

Broader Implications

This price hike is not just a local issue but has broader implications. It is a part of the larger trend of rising fuel costs globally. The increase in LNG prices is a reflection of the global energy market dynamics. As the world moves towards cleaner energy sources, the demand for LNG is expected to rise, which could potentially lead to further price hikes in the future.

Expert Opinions

Industry experts have reacted to the price hike with a mix of concern and caution. While they acknowledge the rise in LNG costs, they also point out that the increase in CNG prices is not as steep as expected. They believe that the government and the industry need to work together to ensure that the impact of the price hike is minimized. They also suggest that the government should consider introducing measures to promote the use of other cleaner fuels, thereby reducing the dependence on CNG.
