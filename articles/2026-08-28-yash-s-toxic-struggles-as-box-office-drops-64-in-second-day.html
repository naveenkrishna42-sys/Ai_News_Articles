Yash's Toxic Struggles as Box Office Drops 64% in Second Day

  • Film collects Rs 36.40 crore on Day 2, totaling Rs 190 crore worldwide
  • Industry experts attribute drop to high expectations and mixed reviews

Box Office Struggles: A 64% Drop

Yash's latest venture, 'Toxic', has hit a snag at the box office, with Day 2 collections showing a staggering 64% drop from the opening day. The film, which earned Rs 190 crore worldwide, including Rs 36.40 crore on its second day, is now under scrutiny for failing to meet initial expectations.

Industry Experts Weigh In

Industry experts have varying opinions on the film's performance. While some attribute the drop to the film's content, others point to the sheer volume of releases in the market. 'Toxic' is not the only film facing challenges; 'Dhurandhar 2' also saw a drop, indicating a potential market saturation.

Content and Audience Expectations

The film's content, which deals with themes of misogyny and toxicity, has sparked mixed reactions. While some viewers appreciate the bold subject matter, others feel the film lacks the necessary depth to captivate audiences. The film's success will largely depend on how well it performs in the long term, with word-of-mouth playing a crucial role.

Long-Term Viability

For 'Toxic' to become a success, it must navigate the challenges of a competitive market. The film's ability to generate buzz and maintain audience interest will be key. However, the initial drop suggests that the film may need to work harder to prove its worth.
