Mumbai Gets Travellator-Equipped Skywalk Linking Monorail, Metro 3 and Suburban Railway

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • A new skywalk with travellators will link Monorail, Metro 3, and suburban railway stations.
  • The project is designed to improve last-mile connectivity and reduce congestion.
  • Authorities expect the skywalk to benefit thousands of daily commuters.
  • Construction timelines and cost details have not been disclosed yet.

Mumbai's New Skywalk to Connect Key Transit Hubs

Mumbai is set to get a travellator-equipped skywalk that will link the Monorail, Metro 3, and the suburban railway network. The project, confirmed by officials, aims to improve last-mile connectivity for millions of daily commuters in the city.

The skywalk will be built near a major junction where these three modes of transport converge. Authorities say the travellators will make walking easier, especially during peak hours and in Mumbai's humid weather.

Easing Congestion in a Crowded City

Mumbai's local trains and metro systems carry over 7.5 million passengers daily. The new skywalk is expected to reduce crowding at existing foot overbridges and road crossings.

Commuters often struggle to switch between the Monorail, Metro 3, and suburban trains due to poor connections. The skywalk will provide a seamless, weather-protected pathway, cutting transfer time significantly.

Project Details and Timelines

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact cost or completion date of the project. However, they stated that the skywalk will be designed to handle heavy foot traffic and will include safety features like CCTV cameras and railings.

The travellators will be similar to those used in airports, moving at a steady speed to help commuters cover longer distances without fatigue. Maintenance and power supply arrangements are being planned to ensure round-the-clock operation.

Boosting Public Transport Usage

The skywalk is part of a larger push to make public transport more attractive in Mumbai. The city has been investing in metro lines, monorail extensions, and suburban train upgrades to reduce reliance on private vehicles.

Better last-mile connectivity is expected to increase ridership on all three systems. The project also aligns with the state government's focus on sustainable urban mobility.

Local residents and commuter groups have welcomed the announcement. Many hope the skywalk will be completed quickly, as current transfer options are often crowded and time-consuming.

Authorities are yet to release a detailed timeline or budget for the skywalk. They have said that tenders will be floated soon, and construction will begin after necessary approvals.

What to watch: The next few months should bring clarity on the project's cost, contractor, and deadline. Commuters will be watching closely to see if the skywalk delivers on its promise of seamless connectivity.
