Gill advised to pick 'nasty' Gurnoor Brar for Sri Lanka Tests: 'Brings X-factor'

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Shubman Gill advised to pick Gurnoor Brar for Sri Lanka Tests
  • Zaheer Khan says Brar brings 'X-factor' and has been fast-tracked rightly
  • Brar's 'nasty' pace and bounce could trouble Sri Lankan batters

As India gear up for the Test series in Sri Lanka, the debate over the final playing XI has taken a new turn. Shubman Gill, expected to lead the side in the absence of regular skipper Rohit Sharma, has been urged to hand a debut to young pacer Gurnoor Brar. The suggestion comes from former cricketers and analysts who believe the 22-year-old's raw pace and aggressive approach could be the difference-maker on Lankan tracks.

Zaheer Khan backs the young gun

Former India fast bowler Zaheer Khan has thrown his weight behind Brar, describing him as an 'exciting player' who has been rightly fast-tracked into the national setup. In an exclusive interaction, Zaheer said he expects a lot from the youngster, adding that Brar has the attributes to enjoy a 'good and lengthy career' at the highest level.

“He brings that X-factor,” Zaheer was quoted as saying. “It’s good that he has been fast-tracked. You need bowlers who can surprise the batter, and Gurnoor has that ability.”

The 'nasty' edge

What sets Brar apart, according to those who have seen him bowl in domestic cricket, is his 'nasty' streak. The young pacer is not just quick but also willing to use the short ball intelligently, mixing it with a sharp yorker. That combination, analysts argue, could be particularly effective in Sri Lanka, where pitches have historically offered some bounce early on before flattening out.

Farveez Maharoof, the former Sri Lankan all-rounder, has also weighed in, suggesting that Brar could be India's X-factor in the upcoming Tests. Maharoof’s comment adds weight to the growing chorus that Gill should not hesitate to include the debutant in the playing XI.

Gill’s captaincy challenge

For Gill, the Sri Lanka tour is set to be a significant test of his leadership skills. With senior players rested, the young captain will need to balance experience with fresh energy. Opting for Brar would be a bold statement, signalling that the team management is willing to back pace and aggression over conservative choices.

India’s pace attack for the series is expected to feature a mix of seasoned campaigners and newcomers. Brar’s inclusion would add a different dimension, giving the attack a sharp edge that could unsettle Sri Lanka’s top order. The team management has not yet confirmed the final XI, but the buzz around Brar is hard to ignore.

What lies ahead

The selectors and team management will take a final call closer to the first Test. With Gill at the helm, the decision on Brar could define the tone of the series. If given the nod, the young pacer will have a golden opportunity to prove that the hype around him is justified.

All eyes will be on the team announcement, with fans and experts alike eager to see whether Brar gets his maiden Test cap. The series is expected to be competitive, and India’s bowling combination could well be the deciding factor.
