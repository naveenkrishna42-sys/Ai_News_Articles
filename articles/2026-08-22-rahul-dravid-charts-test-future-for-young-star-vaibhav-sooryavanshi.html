Rahul Dravid charts Test future for young star Vaibhav Sooryavanshi

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Rahul Dravid advocates for multi-format development for young players.
  • Vaibhav Sooryavanshi is highlighted as a key prospect for Test cricket growth.
  • Dravid urges patience as Sooryavanshi transitions from T20s to red-ball cricket.

A Call for Versatility

Former India head coach Rahul Dravid has issued a clear directive regarding the future of Test cricket. As the sport grapples with the dominance of white-ball leagues, Dravid identifies the emergence of multi-format players as the primary solution for sustaining the traditional format's relevance.

Dravid specifically pointed to young talent Vaibhav Sooryavanshi as the type of player the system must nurture. According to Dravid, if Test cricket is to thrive, players with Sooryavanshi’s profile must transition effectively from high-impact T20 cricket into the rigours of the long game.

The Red-Ball Challenge

While Sooryavanshi has garnered significant attention for his explosive T20 performances, Dravid remains grounded about his current trajectory. The former batting legend noted that the youngster has not yet logged substantial experience in red-ball conditions, which demand a different technical and mental approach.

Transitioning from the brevity of T20s to the endurance required for Test cricket is a steep climb. Dravid suggests that the upcoming Duleep Trophy will be a critical testing ground for Sooryavanshi. It represents a move away from the 'T20 shadows' and an entry into a format where patience and defensive technique dictate success.

Managing Expectations

The transition process requires a measured strategy rather than immediate pressure. Dravid has called for patience from fans and selectors alike as the young batter navigates this shift. He argues that rushing a player into the Test arena without a solid foundation in first-class cricket could be counterproductive.

The Duleep Trophy will serve as an indicator of whether Sooryavanshi possesses the requisite discipline to handle the nuances of red-ball play. Dravid’s assessment is blunt: the talent is undeniable, but it requires refinement through the traditional domestic structure to become a viable asset for the national Test side.

  • Dravid emphasizes the importance of red-ball exposure for young cricketers.
  • The Duleep Trophy is identified as the primary platform for assessing readiness.
  • Balance between T20 impact and Test consistency remains the central goal.

All eyes will now shift to the Duleep Trophy, where Sooryavanshi’s performance will likely determine how quickly he integrates into the red-ball setup. His ability to adapt his game will be closely monitored by selectors and coaching staff throughout the domestic season.
