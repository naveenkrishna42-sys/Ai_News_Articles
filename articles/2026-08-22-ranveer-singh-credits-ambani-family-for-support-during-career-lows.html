Ranveer Singh Credits Ambani Family for Support During Career Lows

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ranveer Singh reflects on the difficult period in his career prior to the success of Dhurandhar: The Revenge.
  • The actor publicly acknowledged the support provided by the Ambani family during his professional lows.
  • Singh described the backing as a blessing, emphasizing the personal and professional impact of their faith in him.

A Candid Look at Professional Setbacks

Actor Ranveer Singh has spoken openly about the volatile nature of the film industry and the personal toll taken by his recent career fluctuations. In a series of recent interviews, the actor reflected on a challenging phase where several of his projects failed to resonate with audiences, leading to uncertainty about his trajectory.

Singh noted that while career ups and downs are inevitable in the entertainment business, navigating a public low point is rarely simple. He characterized this period as a test of resilience, admitting that the pressure to deliver a successful performance was significant.

The Role of the Ambani Family

During these testing times, the actor received unexpected support from the Ambani family. Singh credited their backing as a vital factor that helped him regain his footing, describing their encouragement as nothing short of a blessing.

He explained that having influential figures stand by him when his films were not performing well provided a necessary sense of stability. This support, he noted, went beyond simple professional association, offering him the confidence to focus on his work without the burden of constant external scrutiny.

Reflecting on Dhurandhar: The Revenge

The conversation inevitably shifted to the success of his latest project, Dhurandhar: The Revenge. Singh was candid about the production process, clarifying that the film was far from an easy venture. He described the behind-the-scenes efforts as intense, demanding both physical and emotional dedication from the entire crew.

The success of the film has served as a turning point for the actor. By acknowledging the struggles that preceded this hit, Singh has provided fans with a clearer understanding of the work ethic required to sustain a career in competitive cinema.

Looking Ahead

Industry analysts are now monitoring how this newfound momentum will influence Singh’s upcoming project choices. Whether this success leads to a shift in the scale of his future productions remains to be seen.
