Truck Collides with School Bus in Chennai

A truck collided with a school bus in Chennai, resulting in injuries to the driver and several students. The incident occurred near a busy intersection, causing significant disruption to traffic. Emergency services were quickly dispatched to the scene to provide assistance to the injured.

Injuries Reported

The driver of the truck and several students on the school bus sustained injuries. The exact number of injuries is not yet confirmed, but officials are working to assess the situation. The injured have been taken to nearby hospitals for treatment.

Investigation Underway

Authorities are investigating the incident to determine the cause of the collision. The Chennai Police have launched a probe to understand what led to the accident. Eyewitnesses have been interviewed, and the scene is being examined for any signs of negligence or mechanical failure.

Traffic Disruption

The collision caused significant disruption to traffic in the area. The road was temporarily closed to allow emergency vehicles to pass through.
