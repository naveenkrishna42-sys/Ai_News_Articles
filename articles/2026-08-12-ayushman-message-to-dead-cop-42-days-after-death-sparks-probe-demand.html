Ayushman message to dead cop 42 days after death sparks probe demand

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • A deceased Chhattisgarh constable's phone received an Ayushman Bharat message saying 'recovered' 42 days after his death.
  • The family has demanded a probe into how the portal showed him as recovering and returning home.
  • The incident has raised questions about the accuracy of the Ayushman Bharat database.

In a cruel twist of fate, the family of a Chhattisgarh police constable received a text message from the Ayushman Bharat health scheme on his phone — 42 days after he had died. The message, which read 'Happy you have recovered', was meant to celebrate a recovery that never happened. For the grieving family, it reopened wounds and raised uncomfortable questions.

The constable, whose identity has not been officially released, passed away over a month ago. Yet, the government health portal continued to show him as 'recovered' and 'returning home'. The family, already struggling with their loss, is now demanding a thorough investigation into how such a glaring error could occur.

The message that added to grief

The automated message from the Ayushman Bharat scheme arrived on the deceased constable's mobile phone, informing him of his recovery and return home. It was a routine notification, but for the family, it was a painful reminder of their loss. They have since approached authorities, seeking answers.

According to reports, the Ayushman portal displayed the constable's status as 'recovered' as late as 42 days after his death. The discrepancy has baffled officials, who have yet to explain how the system failed to update his records.

Family demands probe

The constable's kin have urged the administration to investigate the matter. They want to know who updated the portal and why no one noticed the error. The family has expressed concerns that such lapses could undermine the credibility of the health scheme, which is meant to serve the poor and vulnerable.

Local authorities have acknowledged the issue but have not yet provided a detailed response. Officials have not confirmed whether any disciplinary action is being considered against those responsible for the data entry.

Questions over data accuracy

The incident has highlighted systemic flaws in the Ayushman Bharat database. Health schemes across India rely heavily on digital records, and errors like this can cause distress to families already dealing with grief. Experts say this points to a lack of proper verification mechanisms.

In this case, the constable's treatment details, if any, are unknown. The hospital that may have treated him has not been named. The family's plea for a probe suggests they are not willing to let the matter rest.

What happens next

The district administration is expected to respond to the family's complaint in the coming days. Meanwhile, the state health department may review its data-updating processes to prevent similar incidents. The family, for now, seeks one thing: accountability.
