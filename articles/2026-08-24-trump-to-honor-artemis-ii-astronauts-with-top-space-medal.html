Trump to Award Top Space Medal to Artemis II Astronauts

US President Donald Trump is set to award the Congressional Space Medal of Honor to the four astronauts of NASA's Artemis II mission. This prestigious medal is the highest honor bestowed by the US government on space explorers. The ceremony will take place at the Johnson Space Center in Houston, marking a significant milestone in the nation's space exploration efforts.

Artemis II Crew: Pioneers of Lunar Exploration

The Artemis II crew consists of four astronauts who will embark on a historic journey around the Moon. This mission is a critical step in NASA's Artemis program, which aims to return humans to the lunar surface and eventually establish a sustainable presence on the Moon. The astronauts, whose identities have not been disclosed, will be the first humans to travel beyond low Earth orbit since the Apollo era.
