Tamil Nadu Assembly sees political tussle over corruption files

Tamil Nadu's political landscape is in turmoil as Udhayanidhi Stalin, leader of the DMK, and Aadhav Arjuna, Chief Minister Vijay Kanth's son, engage in a heated exchange over corruption-related files. The confrontation has intensified tensions in the state assembly, with both sides accusing each other of misleading the public.

Stalin challenges Vijay over corruption files

Udhayanidhi Stalin has taken a dig at Chief Minister Vijay Kanth, accusing him of treating the assembly like a movie set. Stalin's remarks come amid reports that the government has been withholding crucial files related to corruption cases. The DMK leader has called for these files to be made public, arguing that transparency is essential for the smooth functioning of democracy.

Arjuna rejects new cars for MLAs

Aadhav Arjuna, the son of Chief Minister Vijay Kanth, has rejected the government's decision to provide new cars for MLAs. Arjuna's stance has drawn criticism from the opposition, who see it as a sign of the government's inability to address the state's financial woes. The issue has further fueled the ongoing political rivalry between the DMK and the AIADMK.

Live telecast of Tamil Nadu Assembly proceedings

The Tamil Nadu Assembly is set to broadcast its proceedings live, a move that has been welcomed by the public. The live telecast aims to enhance transparency and accountability in the state's legislative body. However, the political tussle over corruption files and the new cars for MLAs issue has overshadowed the positive step towards greater transparency.
