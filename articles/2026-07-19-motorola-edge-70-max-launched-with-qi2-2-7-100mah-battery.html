Motorola Edge 70 Max Launched With Qi2.2, 7,100mAh Battery

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Motorola Edge 70 Max launched with a 7,100mAh battery and Qi2.2 wireless charging.
  • The phone supports 125W wired charging and 50W wireless charging.
  • Motorola promises three years of OS updates and four years of security patches.
  • The device lacks a dedicated telephoto camera, using a 200MP main sensor with crop zoom.
  • Pricing starts at ₹34,999, competing against the iQOO 15R.

Motorola has officially launched the Edge 70 Max in India, bringing a massive 7,100mAh battery and support for the new Qi2.2 wireless charging standard. The phone targets users who want all-day battery life and fast charging without compromise.

Battery and Charging: The Star of the Show

The Edge 70 Max's 7,100mAh cell is one of the largest in any mainstream smartphone today. Motorola claims it can deliver up to two days of heavy use on a single charge.

Wired charging goes up to 125W, while wireless charging hits 50W via the Qi2.2 standard. The latter is a significant upgrade, allowing faster wireless top-ups than most rivals. The phone also supports reverse wireless charging for accessories like earbuds.

Display and Performance

The phone features a 6.8-inch pOLED display with a 144Hz refresh rate and peak brightness of 1,800 nits. It uses a MediaTek Dimensity 8300 Ultra chipset, paired with up to 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage.

Motorola has not included expandable storage, but the base variant starts at 128GB. The phone runs Android 16 out of the box.

Camera Setup: No Telephoto, But a 200MP Main Sensor

The Edge 70 Max skips a dedicated telephoto lens. Instead, it relies on a 200MP primary sensor with pixel-binning for detail and crop zoom. An 8MP ultra-wide and a 2MP depth sensor round out the rear array.

This is a clear trade-off. Users who want optical zoom will need to look at competitors like the iQOO 15R, which offers a periscope lens. Motorola is betting that the high-resolution main sensor can handle most zoom needs through software cropping.

The front camera is a 32MP unit with autofocus, suitable for selfies and video calls.

Software Update Policy and Pricing

Motorola has issued an official statement on the Edge 70 Max's update policy. The phone will receive three years of major OS upgrades and four years of security patches. This is a step up from the company's previous two-year commitment for some models.

Pricing starts at ₹34,999 for the 8GB/128GB variant. The 12GB/256GB model costs ₹39,999. The phone goes on sale starting July 25 via Flipkart and Motorola's website.

The Edge 70 Max competes directly with the iQOO 15R, which offers a similar chipset and a telephoto camera but a smaller 5,500mAh battery. Buyers will have to choose between raw battery life and camera versatility.

Motorola's launch comes as the smartphone market sees increasing competition in the ₹30,000–₹40,000 segment. The company hopes the combination of a huge battery, fast wireless charging, and a solid update policy will sway buyers.

Sales numbers in the first few weeks will reveal whether the lack of a telephoto lens proves to be a dealbreaker for Indian consumers.
