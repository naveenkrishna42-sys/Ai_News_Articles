Mission 160kph: The making of Ashok Sharma, India's latest speed sensation

  • The pacer's journey from a small town to the international stage was driven by raw pace.

From raw talent to record speed

Ashok Sharma is not just another fast bowler. He is the latest Indian pace sensation who has been consistently touching the 160 kph mark, a milestone that was once considered rare for Indian quicks. His story is one of raw talent refined through relentless effort.

Sharma's journey began in a small town in Uttar Pradesh, where he first picked up a cricket ball. He had the natural ability to generate pace, but his action was raw. Local coaches noticed his potential and decided to focus on preserving his speed while improving his accuracy.

The making of a speedster

The transition from a local talent to a national prospect was not without challenges. Sharma's early days were marked by a lack of proper training facilities. He often bowled on concrete pitches with a tennis ball to avoid injuries.

His big break came when he was spotted by a former India fast bowler during a district-level tournament. Impressed by his pace, the mentor took him under his wing. Sharma's action was remodelled slightly to prevent stress fractures, a common issue with young fast bowlers.

Key milestones in his career

  • First clocked 150 kph at age 19 during a domestic T20 match.
  • Broke into the India A squad after a stellar Ranji Trophy season.
  • Touched 160 kph for the first time during a practice session ahead of an international series.

Coaches who worked with him say his strength lies in his repeatable action. He does not need to strain to generate pace; it comes naturally. This has allowed him to bowl long spells without losing speed.

The impact on Indian cricket

Sharma's rise has given Indian cricket a new weapon. Traditionally, India has relied on spinners and swing bowlers, but Sharma offers raw pace that can unsettle any batting lineup. His ability to bowl consistently above 155 kph makes him a rare asset.

Former players have hailed his emergence. Analysts note that his presence could change the dynamics of India's pace attack, especially in Test matches where bounce and pace are crucial. The team management is reportedly planning to manage his workload carefully to avoid burnout.

Sharma himself remains focused on the basics. He has stated in media interactions that his goal is to stay fit and keep improving. He does not want to be known only as a speedster but as a complete bowler.

What lies ahead

Sharma is expected to be part of India's upcoming international tours. The selectors are keen to give him exposure in overseas conditions. His ability to adapt to different pitches will be key to his long-term success.

For now, Ashok Sharma is living his dream. The journey from a small-town boy to India's speed sensation is a testament to his hard work and the support system that believed in his talent. The cricket world will be watching his next steps closely.
