RSS Chief's New York Event Sparks Global Backlash

Mohan Bhagwat, the chief of India's Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS), recently hosted a secretive event in New York, which has drawn significant attention and criticism from various quarters. The event, which was attended by a mix of Indian diaspora and local attendees, has been met with protests from 61 US-based Hindu groups, including a suspension of Al Jazeera X's account.

Al Jazeera X Account Blocked Over RSS Chief's Event

Al Jazeera X, the platform's microblogging service, was blocked for several hours following the announcement of Bhagwat's New York event. The suspension was reportedly due to the platform's policy against content that promotes hate or violence. This move has been seen as a direct response to the RSS's recent activities, which have been increasingly criticized in the West.

61 US-Based Hindu Groups Protest Bhagwat's Visit

In response to Bhagwat's New York event, 61 US-based Hindu groups have come together to protest his visit. These groups, which represent a diverse range of Hindu communities, have issued a joint statement condemning the RSS chief's event. The Hindu 'RSS does not represent us': US-based Hindu group among 61 organisations protesting Mohan Bhagwat's NYC event, as reported by The Hindu.

US Panel on Religious Freedom Opposes RSS Chief's Visa

A panel on religious freedom in the US has recommended imposing sanctions and revoking Bhagwat's visa, citing concerns over his recent activities. The panel's recommendation has been seen as a significant blow to the RSS, which has been increasingly active in the US, particularly in the Indian diaspora communities. 'Impose Sanctions, Revoke Bhagwat’s Visa: US Panel on Religious Freedom Opposes RSS Chief’s Visit', as reported by The Indian Express.

RSS Denies Representing All Hindus, Claims Event is About 'Oneness'

The RSS has denied that it represents all Hindus, and has claimed that the event in New York was about 'oneness'. However, these claims have been widely disputed, with many Hindu groups in the US arguing that the RSS's activities are increasingly divisive and exclusionary. 'Dear Zohran Mamdani, here are some things you may not know about Mohan Bhagwat', as reported by Hindustan Times, highlights the RSS's controversial past and current activities.

Broader Impact and Analysis

The RSS's recent activities in the US, particularly Bhagwat's New York event, have sparked a broader debate about the organization's influence and activities in the West. The event has been seen as a direct challenge to the growing Hindu diaspora communities in the US, which have been increasingly vocal about their concerns over the RSS's activities. The suspension of Al Jazeera X's account and the protests from 61 US-based Hindu groups have further highlighted the RSS's controversial image in the West.
