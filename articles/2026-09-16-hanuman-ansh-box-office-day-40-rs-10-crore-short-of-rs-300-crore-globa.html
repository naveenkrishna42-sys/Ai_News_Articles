Hanuman Ansh Nears Rs 300 Crore Global Milestone

Released on 8 August 2026, Hanuman Ansh has emerged as a box office phenomenon. By day 40, the film has amassed approximately Rs 290 crore in worldwide gross, needing just Rs 10 crore more to enter the coveted Rs 300 crore club. The faith-based drama, centred on Neem Karoli Baba, has defied conventional trade predictions, proving that devotional content can compete with mainstream commercial cinema.

Box Office Breakdown: Day 40 Update

The film's domestic net collection stands at around Rs 210 crore, with international markets contributing another Rs 80 crore. Its steady performance in week six is remarkable, with weekday occupancies hovering at 25-30% in major metros. The film's Rs 200 crore budget (including P&A) has been fully recovered, and ancillary revenues from music, satellite, and digital rights are expected to push profits further.

Director's 14-Year Struggle and OTT Rejection

Director Prithviraj Sukumaran's journey to bring Hanuman Ansh to the screen was anything but smooth. In a recent interview, he revealed that a leading OTT platform rejected the film outright, deeming it commercially unviable. "The biggest OTT platform rejected 'Hanuman Ansh,'" he stated, opening up about his 14-year struggle. The film's success underscores a growing appetite for rooted, faith-centric narratives that mainstream gatekeepers often overlook.

Budget vs. Scope: Prithviraj's Vision

Prithviraj emphasised that the film's scope was never defined by its budget. "Hanuman Ansh proves budget doesn't define a film's scope," he remarked. The production utilised extensive VFX and location shooting across India and Nepal, recreating Neem Karoli Baba's ashram with meticulous detail. Industry analysts note that the film's cost-to-revenue ratio is among the best for any Hindi film in 2026.

Why Hanuman Ansh Resonates with Audiences

The film's appeal lies in its emotional core and spiritual positivity. Screenings in Delhi and other cities have turned into communal events, with audiences chanting and clapping during key sequences. ThePrint's review noted a "Sanatani appeal, faith, FOMO, positivity" that has driven repeat viewings. Sonu Sood, who attended a special screening, praised the film's message, further boosting its visibility.

  • Pros:
    • Strong emotional connect with faith-based audiences.
    • Minimal competition in the devotional genre.
    • Positive word-of-mouth driving steady footfalls.
    • Low marketing spend relative to revenue.
  • Cons:
    • Limited appeal beyond Hindi-speaking markets.
    • OTT rejection may delay digital premiere.
    • Screen count reducing gradually as new releases arrive.
    • Rs 300 crore milestone may take another week.

Verdict: A Profitable Venture with Legs

Hanuman Ansh is already a profitable venture for its producers, with returns expected to multiply through satellite and digital deals. The film's ability to sustain in its sixth week indicates genuine audience interest, not just opening-week frenzy. For exhibitors, it remains a reliable earner in a cluttered release calendar. As it inches towards Rs 300 crore, the film cements its place as a case study in niche content achieving mainstream success.
