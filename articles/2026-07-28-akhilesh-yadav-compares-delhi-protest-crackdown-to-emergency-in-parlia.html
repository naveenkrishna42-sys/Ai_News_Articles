Akhilesh Yadav Compares Delhi Protest Crackdown to Emergency in Parliament

📅 2026-07-28 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Akhilesh Yadav likened the crackdown on Delhi protesters to the 1975-77 Emergency, highlighting tear gas use.
  • Priyanka Gandhi Vadra questioned the use of pellet guns on protesters, asking if they are terrorists.
  • The Opposition and Centre clashed in Lok Sabha over the anti-paper-leak Bill.
  • Priyanka Gandhi Vadra also targeted IIT Madras chief V Kamakoti, calling him a 'gau mutra' expert.

Opposition Slams Government Over Protest Crackdown

The Monsoon Session of Parliament witnessed sharp exchanges on Tuesday, with Samajwadi Party chief Akhilesh Yadav comparing the police action on protesters in Delhi to the Emergency of 1975-77. Yadav pointed to the use of tear gas against demonstrators as evidence of what he called a return to authoritarian tactics.

His remarks came amid ongoing debates over the government's handling of protests in the capital. The Samajwadi Party leader did not specify which protest he was referring to, but the context suggested recent agitations over various demands.

Priyanka Gandhi Questions Pellet Gun Use

Congress leader Priyanka Gandhi Vadra raised the issue of pellet guns being used on protesters. In a pointed query, she asked, "Are they terrorists?" This drew sharp reactions from Treasury benches.

She also targeted the government's approach to dissent, saying the Prime Minister needs to change the angle of his heart, not the angle of cameras. Her remarks were met with both support and criticism from different quarters of the House.

Clash Over Anti-Paper-Leak Bill

The Lok Sabha witnessed a heated clash between the Opposition and the Centre over the anti-paper-leak Bill. The legislation aims to prevent exam paper leaks and malpractices. Opposition leaders demanded that the government understand the pain of students affected by such leaks.

Members from both sides traded accusations, with the Centre asserting that the Bill is necessary to maintain the integrity of examinations. The Opposition argued that the Bill is being used to target students and protesters unfairly.

Priyanka Gandhi Takes on IIT Madras Chief

In a different context, Priyanka Gandhi Vadra also targeted IIT Madras director V Kamakoti, referring to him as a 'gau mutra' expert. This comment was seen as a dig at his views on traditional practices and cow urine.

Kamakoti has been a vocal proponent of integrating ancient Indian knowledge with modern science. His credentials include significant contributions to computing and Vedic studies, which have been both praised and criticized.

The monsoon session is expected to continue with more heated debates as the Opposition presses its demands for accountability over protest handling and other issues.
