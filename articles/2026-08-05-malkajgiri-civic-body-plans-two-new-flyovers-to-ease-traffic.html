Malkajgiri civic body plans two new flyovers to ease traffic

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Two new flyovers to be constructed by Malkajgiri Municipal Corporation
  • Move aimed at easing traffic congestion at busy junctions
  • Project part of broader infrastructure push in the region

Infrastructure push for Malkajgiri

The Malkajgiri Municipal Corporation has announced plans to construct two new flyovers in the region, a move expected to significantly ease traffic congestion at some of the busiest junctions. The decision comes after years of mounting commuter complaints about delays and bottlenecks during peak hours.

Officials said the flyovers are part of a larger infrastructure upgrade aimed at improving connectivity and reducing travel time for thousands of daily commuters. The exact locations and detailed project reports are yet to be made public, but the civic body has confirmed that feasibility studies are underway.

Why now?

Malkajgiri has witnessed rapid residential and commercial growth over the past decade, putting immense pressure on its road network. Local residents and commuters have long demanded grade-separated intersections to ease the daily crawl. The announcement follows sustained pressure from citizen groups and local elected representatives.

The corporation has not disclosed the estimated cost or the timeline for completion. However, similar projects in the Greater Hyderabad region have typically taken 18 to 24 months once construction begins. Officials have indicated that land acquisition is unlikely to be a major hurdle, as most of the proposed corridors are on existing road margins.

What to expect

Once completed, the flyovers are expected to decongest key corridors, reduce fuel consumption, and improve emergency vehicle access. The civic body has also promised to integrate pedestrian safety measures, including footpaths and signal-free crossings at strategic points.

Residents, however, are cautious. Past infrastructure projects in the region have faced delays due to utility shifting, monsoon disruptions, and contractor issues. The corporation has assured that it will expedite approvals and coordinate with utility departments to avoid prolonged disruption.

Next steps

The tendering process is expected to begin soon after the detailed project reports are finalised. Public consultations will be held to address concerns about traffic diversion during construction. The civic body has also said it will publish regular progress updates on its website.

For now, commuters will have to wait for the official blueprint. The announcement signals a positive step, but the real test will be in execution. All eyes will be on the municipal corporation to deliver on its promise without the usual delays.
