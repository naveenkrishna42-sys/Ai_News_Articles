Bitcoin Surges 12% in Two Days as Trump, Crypto Execs Push Clarity Act

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin surges 12% over two days amid renewed optimism for US crypto regulation
  • Trump and industry leaders launch coordinated push for the proposed Clarity Act
  • Market reacts positively to potential regulatory framework, pushing BTC past recent resistance levels
  • Analysts warn outcome remains uncertain as legislative process continues

Bitcoin Surges on Regulatory Optimism

Bitcoin climbed sharply over the past two days, with the world's largest cryptocurrency gaining 12% as news spread about a renewed push for federal crypto legislation. The rally reflects growing optimism that Washington may finally provide clearer rules for digital assets.

The surge comes as former President Donald Trump and several cryptocurrency executives launched what sources describe as a coordinated campaign to advance the proposed Clarity Act through Congress.

Clarity Act Gains Momentum

The Clarity Act, which has been circulating in legislative circles for several months, aims to establish a comprehensive federal framework for cryptocurrency regulation. Supporters say it would bring certainty to an industry that has operated under a patchwork of state-level rules and conflicting federal guidance.

Crypto industry leaders have increasingly lobbied for such legislation, arguing that clearer rules would unlock greater institutional investment and support wider adoption of digital assets.

Market Responds Positively

Bitcoin's two-day surge pushed the cryptocurrency past key resistance levels, with traders citing the Clarity Act news as a primary driver. Trading volumes picked up noticeably during the rally, suggesting renewed institutional interest.

The broader crypto market also benefited, with several major altcoins posting gains in line with Bitcoin's advance.

Political Dimension Remains Complex

The involvement of Trump in the push for crypto legislation marks a notable shift from his earlier skepticism about digital currencies. Industry observers note the former President's renewed engagement with the sector could help attract Republican support for the bill.

However, legislative experts caution that the path forward remains uncertain. Similar efforts have stalled in the past amid disagreements over consumer protections and the scope of federal versus state authority.

What Happens Next

Lawmakers are expected to discuss the Clarity Act in committee hearings over the coming weeks. Market participants will watch closely for signals about the bill's prospects. If recent momentum holds, Bitcoin could see further gains, though analysts warn that regulatory uncertainty may persist until concrete legislation emerges.
