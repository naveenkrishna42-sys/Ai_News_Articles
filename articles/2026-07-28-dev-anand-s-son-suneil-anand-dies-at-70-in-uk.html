Dev Anand's Son Suneil Anand Dies At 70 In UK

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Suneil Anand, son of Dev Anand and Kalpana Kartik, died at 70 in London.
  • Cause was a heart attack, confirmed by niece Gina Narang.
  • He was an actor and appeared in films like 'Anand Aur Anand' and 'Main Tera Dushman'.

Suneil Anand, the son of legendary Bollywood actor Dev Anand and actress Kalpana Kartik, has died at the age of 70. He passed away in London following a heart attack. His niece Gina Narang confirmed the news to Indian media outlets.

Suneil was born in 1956 to one of Hindi cinema's most celebrated couples. He chose to follow his parents into the film industry. Unlike his father, who achieved iconic status, Suneil's career remained modest.

A Brief Stint in Bollywood

He made his acting debut in 1982 with 'Anand Aur Anand', a film produced and directed by his father. The movie also featured his mother Kalpana Kartik. It was a family affair, but it failed to make a mark at the box office.

His only other notable appearance was in the 1988 film 'Main Tera Dushman'. He also worked as an assistant director on some of his father's later projects. After his acting career did not take off, Suneil moved to the United Kingdom.

Life Away From the Limelight

In London, he lived a quiet life away from the film industry. He rarely gave interviews. His death has now drawn attention back to the family's legacy.

Dev Anand passed away in 2011 at the age of 88. Kalpana Kartik, now in her 90s, is still alive. The family has not announced any funeral details yet.

Industry Reactions

News of Suneil's death has prompted tributes from fans and colleagues. Many recalled his connection to the golden era of Indian cinema. Social media has been filled with condolences to the family.

Film historian and writer Dilip Thakur said, "Suneil carried the weight of a famous surname. He tried his best, but the shadow of his father was too large." Others have noted his quiet dignity in stepping away from the public eye.

Suneil is survived by his mother Kalpana Kartik and other family members in India and abroad. The family has requested privacy during this time of mourning.

What happens next: The family is expected to hold a private ceremony in London. No plans for a memorial in India have been announced yet.
