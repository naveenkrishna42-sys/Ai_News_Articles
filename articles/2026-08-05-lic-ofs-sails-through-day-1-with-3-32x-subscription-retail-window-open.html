LIC OFS sails through Day 1 with 3.32x subscription; retail window opens tomorrow

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • LIC OFS subscribed 3.32 times on the first day of bidding
  • Government to exercise greenshoe option to sell additional shares
  • Retail investors' bidding window opens after institutional portion concludes

Strong start for LIC OFS

The government's offer for sale (OFS) in Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) got off to a brisk start, with the issue subscribed 3.32 times on the first day of bidding. The response from institutional investors was robust, prompting the government to confirm it will exercise the greenshoe option to sell additional shares beyond the base size.

The OFS, priced at ₹382 per share, allows the government to offload up to 6.5% of its stake in the country's largest insurer. At the upper end, this could add roughly ₹31,000 crore to the exchequer's disinvestment kitty, a key element of the Centre's fiscal math for the current financial year.

Retail participation takes centre stage

While institutional demand drove the early momentum, the retail portion is now the focus. The bidding window for retail investors opens on the next trading day, after the institutional segment concludes. Reports suggest retail interest has already been strong, with the retail portion subscribed 1.82 times the base size in early indications, though final figures will depend on the full bidding period.

The government has set aside a separate allocation for retail investors, who can bid at a discount to the floor price, a standard feature in OFS structures. This is aimed at widening the shareholder base and giving common investors a chance to own a piece of India's insurance behemoth.

Why the stake sale matters

This OFS is not just about numbers; it's a strategic move. The government is paring its holding in LIC to meet its disinvestment target, while also improving public float in the stock. LIC was listed in 2022 in what was then India's largest IPO, but the stock has struggled to sustain momentum, trading below its issue price for extended periods.

By selling at ₹382, the government is testing market appetite at a time when the broader equity market has shown resilience. The greenshoe option, if fully exercised, would mean the total sale could reach the full 6.5% stake, making this one of the largest OFS transactions in recent times.

What the subscription signals

The 3.32x subscription on Day 1, driven primarily by institutional buyers, signals confidence in LIC's fundamentals despite its stock price struggles. The insurer's massive market presence, steady premium income, and the government's implicit backing make it a staple for long-term investors.

For the government, a successful OFS is a win on two fronts: it brings in much-needed revenue and demonstrates that state-owned assets can be sold without disrupting markets. The partial retail take-up in the up-sized issue, as noted in some reports, suggests that individual investors remain selective but engaged.

Next steps for investors

Retail investors who missed the first day can participate when the retail window opens. The final subscription numbers, including the greenshoe allocation, are expected to be announced after the bidding closes. The government will then finalise the allotment, and shares are likely to be credited to demat accounts within a few days.

Market watchers will be keenly watching the final oversubscription ratio and the pricing discovered in the OFS, as it will set the tone for future disinvestment moves in other public sector companies. For now, the strong Day 1 response has put the spotlight firmly on how the retail segment responds in the coming session.
