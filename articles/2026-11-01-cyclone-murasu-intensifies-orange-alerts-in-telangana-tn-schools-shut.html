Cyclone Murasu Intensifies: Andhra Coast on Alert as Landfall Nears

Cyclone Murasu has rapidly intensified into a Severe Cyclonic Storm over the central Bay of Bengal, posing a significant threat to India’s eastern and southern coasts. Meteorological forecasts indicate the system is tracking west-northwestward and is highly likely to make landfall on the Andhra Pradesh coast around November 3, 2026. This escalation in intensity has triggered widespread alerts and preparedness measures across multiple states, demanding immediate attention from residents and authorities alike.

As Murasu gathers strength, its expansive cloud bands are already beginning to influence weather patterns, particularly across coastal regions. The rapid intensification underscores the dynamic nature of tropical cyclones and necessitates a proactive approach from communities in the projected path. TIVRA News urges all citizens in potentially affected areas to remain vigilant and prioritize safety based on official advisories.

Alert Levels and Regional Preparedness

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued a series of alerts reflecting the escalating threat. Six districts in Telangana have been placed under an Orange alert, indicating a high likelihood of heavy to very heavy rainfall, strong winds, and potential disruption to daily life. An additional five districts in Telangana are under a Yellow alert, signaling the need for continued awareness and preparedness for moderate to heavy rainfall.

In Tamil Nadu, authorities have preemptively announced school holidays in several districts, particularly in the northern regions, anticipating very heavy rainfall. This measure aims to ensure the safety of students and reduce road congestion during adverse weather. Southern Odisha is also on high alert, preparing for significant rainfall and strong winds as the cyclone's periphery extends its influence. Even Kolkata is bracing for rain as Murasu’s outer bands reach West Bengal.

Navigating the Storm: Practical Safety Guidelines

For residents in the affected states, understanding the implications of these alerts is crucial. An Orange alert signifies a call to 'Be Prepared,' indicating a high likelihood of significant weather events that could disrupt power, communication, and transportation. A Yellow alert means 'Be Aware,' suggesting conditions that could still cause moderate disruption but require less immediate action than an Orange alert.

As the cyclone approaches, state disaster response forces and emergency services are on high alert, coordinating efforts to minimize impact. Fishing communities have been advised to suspend operations and return to shore, while coastal authorities are monitoring for potential storm surges and inundation. Critical infrastructure teams are also preparing for possible power outages and communication breakdowns.

Key Real-World Highlights for Residents

  • Monitor Official Updates: Consistently follow bulletins from the India Meteorological Department (IMD) and local government authorities for the latest information and advisories.
  • Prepare an Emergency Kit: Assemble essential supplies including non-perishable food, drinking water, medicines, a first-aid kit, flashlights, extra batteries, and fully charged power banks.
  • Secure Your Home: Fasten loose objects outdoors, trim weak tree branches, and ensure windows and doors are secure to mitigate wind damage.
  • Avoid Unnecessary Travel: Refrain from traveling, especially to coastal or low-lying areas, during the storm. Stay indoors in a safe structure.
  • Follow Evacuation Orders: If authorities issue an evacuation order for your area, comply promptly and move to designated shelters.
  • Protect Valuables: Keep important documents and valuables in waterproof bags or elevated locations to protect them from potential flooding.
