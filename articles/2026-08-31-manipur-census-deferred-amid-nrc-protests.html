Manipur Census Deferred Amid NRC Protests

Key points

Government Defers Census in Manipur Amid NRC Protests

The Indian government has deferred the census operations in Manipur, following violent protests over the implementation of the National Register of Citizens (NRC). The decision comes after the Manipur High Court put a hold on census house-listing until October 12.

Violent Protests Over NRC Implementation

Protests have been ongoing in Manipur over the NRC implementation, with violent clashes turning the Torchlight vigil in Kakching into a scene of police firing tear gas. The unrest has raised concerns about the safety and conduct of the census in the state.

Manipur High Court Puts Census House-Listing on Hold

The Manipur High Court has put a hold on census house-listing until October 12, citing concerns over the NRC implementation. The court has also ordered a special hearing on NRC petitions amid the growing protests.

Broader Implications of the Census Deferral

The deferral of the census in Manipur has significant implications for the state and the country as a whole. The census is a crucial exercise for the government to understand the demographic profile of the population, which is used for various planning and development activities.

Impact on Manipur's Development Planning

The deferral of the census in Manipur will have a significant impact on the state's development planning. The census data is used by the government to allocate funds and resources for various development schemes. The deferral will delay the availability of this data, which could impact the state's development plans.

Concerns Over Census Safety and Conduct

The deferral of the census in Manipur has also raised concerns about the safety and conduct of the census in the state. The violent protests over the NRC implementation have raised questions about the government's ability to conduct the census in a safe and secure manner.
