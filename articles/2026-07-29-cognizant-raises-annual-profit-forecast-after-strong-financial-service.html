Cognizant raises annual profit forecast after strong financial services growth

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Cognizant raises FY26 EPS outlook after strong Q2 results.
  • Q2 revenue rose 4.5%, beating estimates, led by financial services.
  • AI-led deal momentum begins to cool, with cautious client spending.
  • Company's earnings missed estimates but revenue topped expectations.

Cognizant Lifts Annual Profit Forecast

Cognizant Technology Solutions has raised its full-year profit forecast after reporting a solid 4.5% jump in second-quarter revenue. The IT services giant credited strong growth in its financial services vertical for the upward revision.

The company now expects higher adjusted earnings per share for fiscal 2026, signaling confidence in its ability to navigate a mixed demand environment. The revised outlook comes as clients in banking and insurance continue to spend on digital transformation.

Q2 Performance: Revenue Up, Earnings Miss

Cognizant's second-quarter revenue topped analyst estimates, driven by sustained demand from financial services customers. However, the company's earnings per share missed expectations, reflecting higher investments and cost pressures.

Despite the earnings miss, investors focused on the revenue beat and the improved full-year guidance. Shares rose in after-hours trading as the market welcomed the stronger outlook.

AI Deal Momentum Begins to Cool

While Cognizant has aggressively pushed AI-led deals over the past year, recent commentary suggests the momentum is starting to ease. The company noted cautious client spending as enterprises take longer to commit to large-scale AI projects.

This cooling trend mirrors broader industry observations that generative AI adoption is moving from hype to more measured implementation. Cognizant's management indicated that clients are still exploring use cases but are being more deliberate with budgets.

Financial Services Leads the Charge

The financial services segment emerged as the standout performer in Q2, posting double-digit growth. Banks and insurers are accelerating their cloud migration and automation initiatives, benefiting Cognizant's consulting and implementation services.

Other verticals like healthcare and retail showed mixed results, with some clients deferring discretionary spending. The company's geographic mix also shifted, with North America seeing steady demand while Europe remained subdued.

Outlook and Key Metrics

Cognizant's revised FY26 EPS forecast reflects management's confidence in maintaining profitability despite a cautious demand environment. The company also flagged that client decision-making cycles are lengthening, particularly for large multi-year deals.

Revenue guidance for the current quarter came in slightly below some expectations, indicating that the path to recovery may be uneven. Analysts will watch for signs of sustained growth in financial services and any pickup in AI-led contracts.

Looking ahead, Cognizant faces the challenge of balancing investment in AI capabilities with margin discipline. The next few quarters will reveal whether the financial services momentum can offset weakness in other verticals and whether AI deals regain their pace.
