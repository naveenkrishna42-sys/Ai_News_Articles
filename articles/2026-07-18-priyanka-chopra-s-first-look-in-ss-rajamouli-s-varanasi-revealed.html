Priyanka Chopra's First Look in SS Rajamouli's 'Varanasi' Revealed

Key points
  • SS Rajamouli shared the first look of Priyanka Chopra from his upcoming film 'Varanasi'.
  • Priyanka plays a character named Mandakini, described as a blend of 'grace and fire'.
  • The film is a globe-trotting epic, marking Rajamouli's first project after 'RRR'.

First Glimpse of 'Varanasi'

Filmmaker SS Rajamouli has finally offered a glimpse of what fans have been waiting for. The 'RRR' director released the first look of Priyanka Chopra Jonas from his upcoming epic, 'Varanasi'. The image dropped on Saturday, sending social media into a frenzy.

Priyanka plays a character named Mandakini. In the still, she is seen in a traditional yet fierce avatar, adorned in rich silks and jewellery. The look blends regal poise with an undercurrent of intensity.

'Grace and Fire' – Rajamouli's Words

Rajamouli himself described the character as a mix of 'grace and fire'. The director shared the image with a caption that hinted at the scale of the project. 'Varanasi' is being touted as a globe-trotting spectacle, moving beyond the city it is named after.

This marks Priyanka's first collaboration with the acclaimed director. The actor, who celebrated her birthday recently, has been generating buzz with her international lineup. Fans were quick to draw comparisons, with many calling her look in 'Varanasi' superior to other recent period pieces.

Fan Reactions and Comparisons

Social media erupted with praise. Posts on X (formerly Twitter) compared her appearance to Deepika Padukone's look in 'Kalki 2898 AD'. One user wrote, 'Better than Deepika in Kalki 2898 AD', while others simply called it 'stunning'. The hashtag #Varanasi began trending shortly after the reveal.

Details about the plot remain scarce. Rajamouli has kept the script under wraps, only confirming that the film spans multiple continents. The scale is expected to match or exceed the director's previous blockbusters.

What We Know So Far

  • Priyanka Chopra Jonas plays Mandakini, a central character in the epic.
  • The film is directed by SS Rajamouli, known for 'RRR' and the 'Baahubali' series.
  • 'Varanasi' is expected to be a high-budget, international production.
  • No release date has been announced yet.

Looking Ahead

With the first look out, anticipation for 'Varanasi' is only building. Fans now await a teaser or a trailer, hoping for more clues about the story. Rajamouli is known for his grand reveals, so the next update could come at a major event or festival.

For now, the image of Mandakini has set the tone. The film promises to be a visual feast, and Priyanka's involvement adds star power that crosses borders.
