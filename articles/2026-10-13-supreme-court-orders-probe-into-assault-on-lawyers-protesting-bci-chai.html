Supreme Court Orders SIT Probe Into Assault on Lawyers

The Supreme Court of India has ordered a Special Investigation Team (SIT) to probe the alleged assault on lawyers who were protesting against the chairpersonship of Manan Mishra at the Bar Council of India (BCI). The incident, which occurred during a demonstration by legal practitioners, has raised serious concerns about the safety and independence of the legal fraternity.

Judicial Independence at Stake

During the hearing, a Bench of the Supreme Court expressed deep concern over any attempt to intimidate lawyers while they exercise their democratic right to protest. The Court emphasised that attacks on members of the legal profession, especially during peaceful demonstrations, strike at the very root of judicial independence.

Background of the Protest

The protest by lawyers was sparked by allegations of irregularities in the election process that led to the appointment of Manan Mishra as the chairperson of the BCI. Legal practitioners across the country had gathered to voice their dissent, demanding transparency and fairplay in the functioning of the apex body governing the legal profession.

Immediate Aftermath and Reactions

Reports indicate that several lawyers sustained minor injuries during the scuffle. Legal fraternity leaders have welcomed the Supreme Court's intervention, calling it a necessary step to uphold the rule of law. Bar Associations in various states have also condemned the incident and appealed for calm while seeking justice.

  • Swift Judicial Intervention: The Supreme Court acted promptly to ensure accountability.
  • Protection of Rights: Reinforces the right to peaceful protest for legal professionals.
  • Transparency Demanded: Calls for reform in BCI election procedures remain urgent.
  • National Concern: Incident has drawn criticism from legal bodies across India.

Way Forward

The SIT has been directed to submit its findings within a specified timeframe. The matter is likely to be listed for further hearings based on the progress of the investigation. Legal experts suggest that this case could set a precedent for safeguarding the independence of the bar and ensuring transparent governance of professional bodies.
