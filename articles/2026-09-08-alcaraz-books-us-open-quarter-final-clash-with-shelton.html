Alcaraz Powers Past Paul to Reach Flushing Meadows Quarter-Finals

Defending champion Carlos Alcaraz delivered another masterclass at Flushing Meadows, defeating American contender Tommy Paul in straight sets. The Spaniard showcased extraordinary athletic range and court coverage, prompting analysts to dub his current form 'super-human.' Alcaraz neutralized Paul’s aggressive baseline strategy by deploying deep defensive slices and sudden, explosive forehand winners.

This clinical performance underscores Alcaraz's hardcourt evolution. Rather than relying purely on raw power, the top seed dictated rallies through precise depth control and exceptional footwork. The victory secures his progression to the final eight of the 2026 US Open, keeping his title-defense campaign firmly on track.

The Ultimate Test: Alcaraz vs. Ben Shelton

The upcoming quarter-final pits Alcaraz against American sensation Ben Shelton, setting up one of the most anticipated matches of the tournament. Shelton’s explosive serve and relentless net-rushing style present a distinct tactical challenge. For sports bettors and commercial analysts tracking tournament viewership, this marquee fixture guarantees peak audience engagement and high-intensity tennis.

Shelton enters this clash with supreme confidence on home soil, backed by a raucous New York crowd. However, Alcaraz holds the tactical edge in multi-surface endurance and tactical adaptability. Their previous encounters underline a fierce athletic rivalry where baseline stamina often decides the outcome.
