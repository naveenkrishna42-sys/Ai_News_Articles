Slipper attack on Pappu Yadav: MP alleges murder plot, probe underway

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Analysis Original source ↗
Key points
  • Slipper hurled at Pappu Yadav, scuffle erupts at event
  • Yadav claims assassination plot; Opposition criticises police response
  • Attacker identified, no criminal record found; knife reportedly seized

What happened at the event

A scuffle broke out at a public event on Sunday after a man hurled a slipper at Member of Parliament Pappu Yadav. The incident, captured on video and widely shared, shows the attacker being overpowered by Yadav's security personnel and supporters. The MP later alleged that the attack was part of a larger conspiracy to kill him.

According to police sources, the man who threw the slipper has been identified. Preliminary checks show he has no criminal record, though reports suggest a knife was recovered from his possession. Officials have not yet confirmed the motive behind the attack.

The context: Ram temple skit controversy

The attack appears to be linked to a recent skit about the Ram temple that Yadav had reportedly been associated with. The New Indian Express reported that the slipper was thrown over this skit, which had already drawn criticism from certain groups. The MP, however, has framed the incident as a direct threat to his life, saying, "This is not just a slipper, it is a plot to kill me."

Yadav, a former Lok Sabha member from Bihar's Purnia constituency, has a history of political confrontations. He has been involved in several controversies over the years, often drawing sharp reactions from rivals and activists. Sunday's incident adds another layer to his already turbulent political career.

Political fallout and Opposition response

The Opposition has seized on the incident to criticise the police and the state administration. Leaders from various parties have questioned how the attacker managed to get so close to a sitting MP despite security arrangements. The Hindu reported that Opposition leaders are demanding a thorough inquiry and stricter security protocols for public representatives.

Some have also pointed to the knife allegedly found on the attacker as evidence that the incident was more than just a protest. "If it was merely a protest, why was a knife recovered?" asked a senior Opposition leader, who did not wish to be named. The police have not commented on the knife's origin or whether it was used in the scuffle.

What we know about the attacker

Police sources told ThePrint that the man has been identified and is being questioned. His background check has not revealed any prior criminal cases, which has puzzled investigators. They are now looking into his social media activity and personal connections to understand what prompted the attack.

The video footage shows the attacker being dragged away by security personnel, while Yadav's supporters can be seen pushing and shoving him. The MP himself appeared unharmed and continued to address the crowd after the incident. No official statement on the attacker's identity or age has been released so far.

What to watch next

The police are expected to file a formal case and may reveal more details about the attacker's interrogation in the coming days. Yadav's allegation of a murder plot will likely intensify political pressure, with demands for a high-level investigation growing louder. How the state government responds — and whether it tightens security for MPs — will be the key developments to follow.
