Inside Ranbir Kapoor and Alia Bhatt's Rs 250 Crore 'Krishna Raj' Bungalow in Mumbai

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ranbir Kapoor and Alia Bhatt have purchased the 'Krishna Raj' bungalow in Mumbai for Rs 250 crore.
  • The sea-facing property is located in the upscale Pali Hill area of Bandra.
  • The bungalow is named after Ranbir's late father, Rishi Kapoor, combining his parents' names.

Bollywood's power couple Ranbir Kapoor and Alia Bhatt have made headlines with their latest acquisition – a sprawling sea-facing bungalow in Mumbai worth Rs 250 crore. The property, named 'Krishna Raj', is located in the upscale Pali Hill area of Bandra, one of the city's most sought-after neighbourhoods.

The name holds deep personal significance. 'Krishna Raj' is a blend of the names of Ranbir's parents – his father, the late Rishi Kapoor, and his mother, Neetu Kapoor (born Neetu Singh). It is a tribute to the Kapoor family legacy, which has been intertwined with Mumbai's film history for decades.

A Sprawling Sea-Facing Estate

The bungalow sits on a prime plot of land that offers uninterrupted views of the Arabian Sea. Sources familiar with the property say it spans multiple floors, with extensive living spaces, private gardens, and a terrace that overlooks the coastline. The interiors are believed to blend modern luxury with vintage charm, reflecting the couple's personal tastes.

Real estate experts estimate the property's value at Rs 250 crore, making it one of the most expensive residential purchases in the city this year. The deal includes the land and the existing structure, which is being renovated to suit the couple's needs.

A Home for the Next Generation

Ranbir and Alia, who married in 2022 and welcomed their daughter Raha in 2023, are expected to move into 'Krishna Raj' with their extended family. Neetu Kapoor, who has been living in the Kapoor family home 'Krishna Raj' in Bandra for years, is likely to be a part of this new chapter.

For Ranbir, this bungalow represents more than just a home. It is a continuation of the Kapoor legacy. His grandfather, the legendary Raj Kapoor, had built the original 'Krishna Raj' bungalow decades ago, and the name has been passed down through generations.

Design and Amenities

While the couple has kept specific details of the interiors under wraps, industry insiders suggest the bungalow will feature state-of-the-art amenities. These include a home theatre, a gym, a swimming pool, and a dedicated space for their daughter. The design is said to prioritise privacy, with high walls and lush landscaping shielding the property from public view.

Architects working on the renovation have reportedly focused on sustainable features, including rainwater harvesting and solar panels. The couple is known to be environmentally conscious, and they have incorporated green elements into their other properties as well.

Neighbours include several Bollywood celebrities, adding to the area's prestige. The Kapoors have deep roots in this part of the city, and the new home keeps them close to their family and friends.

The Market and the Legacy

The Rs 250 crore price tag underscores the soaring value of prime real estate in Mumbai's Bandra area. Properties in Pali Hill, with their sea views and celebrity residents, command premium prices. For Ranbir and Alia, the investment is as much about sentimental value as it is about financial growth.

The Kapoor family has owned several properties in the neighbourhood over the years, including the iconic 'Krishna Raj' bungalow that was home to Raj Kapoor. Ranbir's decision to name his new home after his father is a nod to that heritage.

As the couple prepares to settle into their new home, fans and the film industry are watching closely. The bungalow is expected to become a new landmark in Bollywood's social circuit, hosting family gatherings and perhaps even film-related events in the future.

What remains to be seen is how the Kapoors will balance their private life with the inevitable public curiosity that comes with such a high-profile address. For now, the family is focusing on making 'Krishna Raj' a warm, welcoming space for their growing household.
