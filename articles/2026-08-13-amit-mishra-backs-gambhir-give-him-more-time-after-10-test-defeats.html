Amit Mishra backs Gambhir: 'Give him more time' after 10 Test defeats

📅 2026-08-13 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • India have lost 10 of 19 Tests under Gautam Gambhir's coaching tenure.
  • Amit Mishra has publicly called for giving Gambhir more time to turn things around.
  • India's World Test Championship final hopes are hanging by a thread.
  • BCCI is reportedly deliberating Gambhir's future, with the upcoming Sri Lanka series seen as crucial.

Pressure mounts on Gambhir

The numbers are stark. Ten defeats in 19 Tests. A World Test Championship (WTC) final spot slipping away. For most coaches, that record would invite severe scrutiny. Yet, former India spinner Amit Mishra believes the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) should hold its nerve.

Mishra has urged the board to give head coach Gautam Gambhir more time, arguing that constant chopping and changing will not solve India's problems. His comments come amid intense speculation over Gambhir's future, with several reports suggesting BCCI has already begun weighing its options.

A fragile WTC campaign

India's recent Test form has been erratic. A string of losses, both at home and overseas, has left the team in a precarious position in the WTC standings. With only a handful of matches remaining in the current cycle, every result now carries enormous weight.

The margin for error has evaporated. India need favourable results and, even then, may require other teams to slip up. The next assignment — a series against Sri Lanka — is being viewed as a potential turning point, but also as a possible final straw.

What Mishra is really saying

Mishra's intervention is notable. He is not in the current coaching setup, nor is he an outspoken critic. His call for patience suggests a section of the cricketing fraternity believes Gambhir deserves a longer runway, despite the poor results.

"You cannot judge a coach on one bad cycle," Mishra said, without specifying which matches he was referring to. He pointed to the need for continuity, especially with a young core still finding its feet in the longest format. "If you keep changing coaches every two years, players will never settle."

BCCI's dilemma

Behind the scenes, the BCCI is said to be divided. Some officials are leaning towards a change, citing the sheer number of defeats. Others, however, argue that Gambhir inherited a team in transition and that the results are not entirely his doing.

Reports suggest the board's final call may hinge on the Sri Lanka series. A clean sweep could buy Gambhir more time; another failure could make his position untenable. The selectors, too, are watching closely, aware that a coaching change mid-cycle could disrupt the team's preparation for the next WTC campaign.

The bigger picture

India's Test struggles are not solely a coaching problem. Batting collapses, inconsistent bowling, and questionable selections have all played their part. Gambhir, known for his aggressive and no-nonsense approach, has not been shy in backing his players, even when results have gone against them.

That loyalty, however, has not translated into wins. The dressing room remains united, but patience in Indian cricket is a finite resource. With fans and former players growing restless, the pressure on Gambhir will only intensify with each passing defeat.

What happens next is unclear. The BCCI has not made any official statement, and Gambhir himself has remained focused on the job at hand. The Sri Lanka series will likely provide the clearest indication of which way the wind is blowing.

For now, the cricketing world watches. If India falter again, the calls for a change will become impossible to ignore. If they bounce back, Mishra's plea for patience may well be vindicated. Either way, the next few weeks could define Gambhir's tenure.
