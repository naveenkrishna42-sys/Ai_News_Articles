Rahul captains India in warm-up as Gill nurses thumb injury

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • KL Rahul captains the touring Indian side in the warm-up game in Sri Lanka.
  • Shubman Gill is sidelined for Day 1 after taking a blow to his thumb.
  • India's bowlers found rhythm after a sluggish start in Colombo.
  • The injury raises fresh concerns ahead of the first Test against Sri Lanka.

Colombo, Aug 7: KL Rahul walked out for the toss in the tour game against a Sri Lanka Board President's XI, stepping in as captain with Shubman Gill ruled out of the opening day's play. The Indian team management confirmed that Gill took a blow on his right thumb during a practice session, a fresh injury worry with the first Test against Sri Lanka just days away.

The warm-up match, being played in Colombo, was expected to give the visitors valuable time in the middle before the series opener. Instead, it has turned into a test of the squad's depth, with Rahul taking charge and the team management keeping a close eye on Gill's recovery.

Gill's injury clouds build-up

Gill, who has been in decent form in recent outings, was struck on the thumb while facing throwdowns at the nets. The team physio attended to him immediately, but the decision was made to rest him for the first day of the tour game as a precautionary measure.

Team sources indicated that the injury is not believed to be serious, but with a Test series beginning soon, the management is unlikely to take any risks. Gill's availability for the remainder of the warm-up match and the first Test remains uncertain, pending further assessment.

Bowlers find their groove after slow start

On the field, India's bowlers had a mixed opening day. After a sluggish start, where the home side's top order looked comfortable, the visitors clawed back into the contest. The pacers found some movement with the new ball, while the spinners tightened the screws in the middle overs.

The performance will be a relief for the team management, who were keen to see the attack operate as a unit after a long break. The pitch in Colombo is expected to assist spin as the match progresses, giving the Indian tweakers a chance to bowl long spells.

Rahul's leadership in focus

With regular captain Rohit Sharma not part of the playing XI for this warm-up, Rahul's captaincy will be under the scanner. The Karnataka batter has led India in the past and is seen as a calm presence, but this match offers him a chance to set the tone for the series.

Rahul's own form with the bat will also be crucial. He has been opening in recent matches, and a solid knock here would ease any concerns about the top order. The team management will hope he can lead from the front.

What lies ahead

The first Test against Sri Lanka is scheduled to begin at the Galle International Stadium later this month. India will be eager to have Gill fit and available, given his importance at the top of the order. The team's medical staff will monitor his thumb overnight, with an update expected before the second day's play.

For now, the focus remains on the warm-up match, with India looking to fine-tune their combinations. Rahul's leadership and the bowlers' rhythm will be the key takeaways as the team builds towards the series opener.
