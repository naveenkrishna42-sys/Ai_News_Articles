Apple Opens iPhone 18 Pro Pre-Orders While Samsung Cuts Flagship Prices

Apple has opened pre-orders for the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, with India bookings starting September 12 and global availability following the company's usual September release rhythm. Simultaneously, Samsung has discounted the Galaxy Z Fold8 and the entire Galaxy S26 series, creating a rare overlap where two flagship ecosystems compete on price in the same buying window.

For buyers, this is not a routine refresh cycle. Apple has finally shipped a variable aperture main camera — a technology it has patented for years — while Samsung is using price aggression to defend its foldable and flagship volume ahead of the festive quarter. Here is what the deals actually mean in rupee and dollar terms.

iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max: What Buyers Actually Get

The headline upgrade is the variable aperture primary sensor on the Pro and Pro Max. Unlike a fixed f/1.78 lens, a variable aperture lets the camera physically stop down in bright light and open wide in low light, giving photographers genuine control over depth of field and exposure without software trickery. This is the first iPhone to offer it, and it directly addresses the one area where Pixel and Vivo flagships have led in recent years.

Expect ProMotion displays, an A20 Pro-class chipset, and Apple Intelligence features running on-device. Storage tiers and pricing are where buyers need to pay attention — Apple has historically raised Indian prices on Pro Max variants, and pre-order incentives usually favour the higher storage trims.

Samsung's Counter-Move: Z Fold8 and S26 Discounts

Samsung's discounts on the Galaxy Z Fold8 and Galaxy S26 series are a direct response to Apple's pre-order window. The Z Fold8 remains the most polished book-style foldable for productivity users, with improved crease management and multitasking software. The S26 series, meanwhile, targets buyers who want flagship cameras and battery life without foldable complexity.

In practical terms, a discounted Z Fold8 now sits closer to iPhone 18 Pro Max pricing, which changes the calculus for anyone comparing a foldable against a conventional flagship. If your priority is a large inner display for work, the Fold8's effective value has improved. If camera consistency and resale value matter more, the iPhone 18 Pro holds its edge.

Real-World Buyer Implications

  • Best time to buy: Pre-order windows usually carry bank cashback and exchange bonuses that disappear within two weeks.
  • Camera buyers: The iPhone 18 Pro's variable aperture is a genuine hardware advantage, not a marketing label.
  • Foldable buyers: The Z Fold8 discount narrows the price gap with slab flagships, improving its value proposition.
  • Upgrade timing: Moving from iPhone 14 Pro or Galaxy S22 delivers a noticeable jump; iPhone 16 Pro owners can wait.
  • India pricing: Expect Rs 1.35 lakh-plus for the Pro and Rs 1.55 lakh-plus for the Pro Max before exchange offers.

Pros and Cons at a Glance

  • Pros: First variable aperture iPhone camera; strong pre-order incentives; discounted Z Fold8 and S26 widen choice; both ecosystems now competitive on price.
  • Cons: Indian Pro Max pricing remains steep; foldable discounts may be short-lived; early software for new camera hardware often needs patches.

Verdict: Which Deal Deserves Your Money

Buy the iPhone 18 Pro or Pro Max if photography control and long-term resale value top your list — the variable aperture alone justifies the upgrade for serious shooters. Choose the discounted Galaxy Z Fold8 if you need a foldable workspace and the price cut brings it within budget. The Galaxy S26 series makes sense for users who want Samsung's ecosystem without paying foldable premiums.

Act during the pre-order and launch-discount window. These incentives are time-bound, and both Apple and Samsung historically tighten offers once initial stock clears.
