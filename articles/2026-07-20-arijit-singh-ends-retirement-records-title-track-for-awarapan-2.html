Arijit Singh ends retirement, records title track for Awarapan 2

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Arijit Singh has ended his retirement to record a song for Awarapan 2.
  • He has reunited with music composer Vishesh Bhatt for the title track 'Yeh Awarapan'.
  • The song is scheduled for release on July 21.

Arijit Singh is back. The singer, who had announced a break from playback singing earlier this year, has reportedly recorded a new song for Emraan Hashmi's upcoming film Awarapan 2.

The track is titled 'Yeh Awarapan'. It has been composed by Vishesh Bhatt, who also worked on the original 2007 film's music. The song is set to be released on July 21.

Comeback after retirement

Singh had announced his retirement from playback singing in early 2026, citing personal reasons. The decision had stunned the music industry and his fans. Since then, he had not released any new film songs.

News of his return first surfaced when reports suggested he had stepped into the recording studio for Bhatt. Sources close to the production confirmed the collaboration. "Arijit has recorded the title track. It's a powerful rendition," a source told Pinkvilla.

The singer was reportedly spotted at a Mumbai studio last week. Industry insiders described his return as a "beast out of his den".

Reunion with Vishesh Bhatt

Vishesh Bhatt composed the original Awarapan soundtrack. That album included the iconic 'Tera Mera Rishta' and 'Maula' sung by Arijit Singh. The singer's voice defined the film's emotional core.

For the sequel, Bhatt wanted Singh back. "The song needed his intensity. There was no other choice," a source said. The reunion marks the first time the duo has worked together since Singh's retirement.

The track 'Yeh Awarapan' is expected to retain the raw, emotional feel of the original film's music. It will be a solo by Singh.

Awarapan 2 and Emraan Hashmi

Awarapan 2 is directed by Bobby Khan. It stars Emraan Hashmi, reprising his role from the first film. The movie has been in production for over a year.

The original Awarapan gained a cult following over time. Its music, especially Singh's songs, played a large part in that. The sequel's team has been keen on recreating that magic.

Hashmi had earlier hinted at Singh's involvement in an interview. He had said, "Some voices are timeless. We wanted that voice for our film."

The song's release on July 21 will be followed by promotional activities. The film's release date has not been announced yet.
