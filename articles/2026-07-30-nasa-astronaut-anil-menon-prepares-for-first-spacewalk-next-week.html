NASA Astronaut Anil Menon Prepares for First Spacewalk Next Week

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Science Original source ↗
Key points
  • NASA astronaut Anil Menon will conduct his first spacewalk next week.
  • Menon revealed a behind-the-scenes ritual he follows before the mission.
  • NASA plans to cover three US spacewalks and host a preview news conference.

NASA astronaut Anil Menon is gearing up for a career milestone. The Indian-origin astronaut will perform his first spacewalk next week, marking a significant moment for the space agency and for India's space enthusiasts.

Menon, who has been with NASA for several years, shared a behind-the-scenes ritual he follows before stepping out into the vacuum of space. The video, released by the astronaut, offers a rare glimpse into the personal preparations that accompany the technical training.

The Ritual Before the Walk

In the video, Menon is seen going through his pre-spacewalk routine. He described it as a way to stay grounded and focused before the high-stakes mission. The ritual involves a mix of physical and mental exercises that help him maintain composure.

Menon did not elaborate on every detail of the routine but emphasised its importance. “It’s something I’ve developed over years of training. It keeps me centred,” he said in the clip. The video has since gone viral on social media, with many Indians expressing pride in his achievement.

Technical Preparations Underway

NASA has confirmed that Menon’s spacewalk is part of a series of three US spacewalks scheduled in the coming weeks. The agency will also host a preview news conference to brief the media on the objectives and procedures.

The spacewalks are aimed at upgrading the International Space Station’s systems. Menon will be accompanied by another NASA astronaut during the extravehicular activity. The duo will install new equipment and perform routine maintenance checks.

NASA officials have not disclosed the exact date of Menon’s first spacewalk, but reports indicate it will take place next week. The agency typically provides a live broadcast of such events, allowing the public to watch the astronauts work in microgravity.

A Historic Moment for India

Menon’s spacewalk is being closely watched in India. He is among a growing number of Indian-origin astronauts making their mark at NASA. His journey from a medical doctor to a NASA astronaut has inspired many young Indians to consider careers in space exploration.

The Indian space community has hailed the development. Several organisations have planned live screenings of the spacewalk. Social media platforms are abuzz with messages of support for the astronaut.

Menon, who was selected by NASA in 2021, completed his basic training and was assigned to the ISS mission last year. He holds a medical degree and has experience in emergency medicine, which proved useful during his training.

What Lies Ahead

Menon’s first spacewalk will test his skills in the harsh environment of space. The preparations, both technical and personal, reflect the seriousness of the task. NASA will provide updates as the date approaches.

For now, the astronaut is focused on the final checks. The world will be watching as he takes that historic step outside the station.
