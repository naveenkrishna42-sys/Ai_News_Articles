NIMS Hyderabad cuts deep brain stimulation surgery time with new technique

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Health Original source ↗
Key points
  • NIMS Hyderabad introduced a new technique for deep brain stimulation surgery.
  • The technique reduces surgery time significantly.
  • It improves precision and reduces complications for patients with movement disorders.

New technique cuts surgery time

Doctors at Nizam's Institute of Medical Sciences (NIMS) in Hyderabad have developed a new technique that reduces the time required for deep brain stimulation (DBS) surgery. The procedure, used to treat movement disorders like Parkinson's disease, typically takes several hours. The new method has brought it down substantially.

The team, led by neurosurgeons at the institute, refined the surgical approach to improve efficiency. Instead of multiple separate steps, they integrated key processes. This has reduced the overall duration without compromising accuracy.

What is deep brain stimulation?

Deep brain stimulation involves implanting electrodes in specific brain regions. These electrodes deliver electrical impulses to regulate abnormal activity. It is commonly used for patients with Parkinson's disease, essential tremor, and dystonia.

The surgery requires precise placement of the electrodes. Traditionally, it involves lengthy pre-operative planning and intraoperative adjustments. The new technique at NIMS streamlines these steps, leading to shorter surgery times.

Benefits for patients

Shorter surgery time means less time under anaesthesia for patients. This reduces the risk of complications. It also means faster recovery and shorter hospital stays.
