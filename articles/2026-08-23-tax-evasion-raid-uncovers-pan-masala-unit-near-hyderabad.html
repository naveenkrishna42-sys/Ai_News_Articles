Pan Masala Unit Busted Near Hyderabad

In a significant crackdown on tax evasion, authorities have busted a pan masala unit near Hyderabad, seizing goods worth Rs 160 crore. The operation, conducted by the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI), highlights the pervasive issue of tax evasion in the pan masala industry.

Scale of the Operation

The bust was conducted at a unit located in the outskirts of Hyderabad. Authorities found the unit to be operating under the guise of a legitimate business, but upon closer inspection, they discovered that the goods being smuggled were worth Rs 160 crore. The goods were found to be in violation of various tax laws and regulations.
