Supreme Court forms panel to probe student protests

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Supreme Court forms High-Powered Enquiry Committee
  • Committee led by ex-SC judge Subhash Reddy
  • Investigate violence during student protests
  • NEET protests in Delhi

Supreme Court forms panel to probe student protests

The Supreme Court has formed a five-member committee to investigate the violence during student protests in Delhi. The committee is led by former Supreme Court judge Subhash Reddy.

Committee formed to investigate violence

The High-Powered Enquiry Committee has been set up to probe the excessive use of force by police during the NEET protests. The committee will look into the events that led to the violence and the actions taken by the police.

Subhash Reddy to lead the committee

Subhash Reddy, a former judge of the Supreme Court, will lead the committee. He will be joined by four other members, including a retired IPS officer and a lawyer. The committee will have the authority to summon witnesses, examine evidence, and issue summons.

NEET protests in Delhi

The NEET protests in Delhi have been ongoing for several weeks. The students have been protesting against the proposed changes in the NEET exam. The protests have turned violent at times, with clashes between the students and the police.

What happens next?

The committee will now begin its investigation into the violence during the NEET protests. The committee's findings will be crucial in determining the actions taken by the police and the government. The committee's report will be submitted to the Supreme Court, which will then take appropriate action based on the findings.
