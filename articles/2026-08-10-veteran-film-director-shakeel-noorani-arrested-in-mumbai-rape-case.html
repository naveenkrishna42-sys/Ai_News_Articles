Veteran Film Director Shakeel Noorani Arrested in Mumbai Rape Case

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Shakeel Noorani, a 73-year-old film director, arrested in Mumbai.
  • Accused of raping a 33-year-old actress and threatening her with a video.
  • Police investigation underway; no official statement from the accused yet.

Arrest in Mumbai

Mumbai police have arrested veteran film director Shakeel Noorani, 73, in connection with the alleged rape of a 33-year-old actress. The arrest was made on Saturday, following a complaint filed by the woman at a police station in the city.

According to officials, the accused has been booked under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including those pertaining to rape, criminal intimidation, and outraging the modesty of a woman. Noorani was taken into custody after preliminary inquiries into the allegations.

The Allegations

The woman, an aspiring actress, has accused Noorani of sexually assaulting her under the pretext of offering a film role. In her complaint, she alleged that the director drugged her before the assault, and later threatened to leak a video of the incident if she refused to comply with his demands.

Police sources said the complainant claimed the accused had been harassing her for several months. The alleged incidents took place at a location in Mumbai, though specific details have not been disclosed by investigators.

Police Investigation Underway

Deputy Commissioner of Police (Zone VIII) Niyati Thakur confirmed the arrest, stating that a case has been registered and further investigation is in progress. "We have taken the accused into custody and are recording statements from all parties involved," Thakur said. The police are also examining the electronic devices of the accused, including his phone and laptop, as part of the probe.

Noorani, who has directed several films in the Hindi and regional language industries over the past four decades, has not yet issued a public statement. His legal team is expected to seek bail in the coming days, with the matter likely to be heard in a local court.

Industry Reaction

The arrest has sent ripples through the Mumbai film fraternity, where discussions about workplace safety and power dynamics have been ongoing. Several industry bodies have refrained from commenting officially, but sources say the case has revived concerns about the vulnerability of newcomers in the entertainment business.

This is not the first time the industry has faced such allegations, but each case brings renewed scrutiny to the mechanisms in place for women to report grievances. The police have assured that the investigation will be conducted fairly and expeditiously.

What's Next

The accused is currently in police custody and will be produced before a magistrate for further remand. The court will decide on bail after hearing arguments from both sides. Meanwhile, women's rights groups have called for a thorough probe and swift justice, as the case continues to unfold.
