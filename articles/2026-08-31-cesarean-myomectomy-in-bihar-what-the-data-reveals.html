Cesarean Myomectomy in Bihar: What the Data Reveals

📅 2026-08-31 📂 Health Original source ↗
Key points
  • 95% success rate for cesarean myomectomy in Bihar
  • Complication rates below national averages
  • Access to advanced gynecological care remains limited

High Success Rates in Bihar

A retrospective study at Patna's tertiary care center found cesarean myomectomy achieved a 95% success rate, significantly higher than national averages. The procedure, which combines cesarean delivery with myomectomy (removal of uterine fibroids) in a single operation, showed promise for improving outcomes in fibroid-related pregnancies.

Complication Rates Below National Averages

The study reported complication rates of 3.2%, well below the national average of 5.8%. This suggests that when performed by experienced teams in well-equipped facilities, cesarean myomectomy can be a safer option for high-risk pregnancies.

Access Remains a Challenge

While the study highlights the potential of cesarean myomectomy, it also underscores the broader issue of access to advanced gynecological care in Bihar. The study was conducted at a single tertiary care center, meaning results may not be representative of the entire state.

Broader Implications

The findings have significant implications for maternal health in India. By demonstrating the effectiveness of cesarean myomectomy, the study could encourage more widespread adoption of this procedure. However, it also serves as a reminder that infrastructure and training must be improved to ensure equitable access to quality care.
