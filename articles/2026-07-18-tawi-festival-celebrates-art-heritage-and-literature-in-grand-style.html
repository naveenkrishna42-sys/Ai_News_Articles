Tawi Festival Celebrates Art, Heritage, and Literature in Grand Style

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Culture & Arts Original source ↗
Key points

  • Attendees participated in heritage walks and literary sessions.

A Weekend of Culture and Ideas

The Tawi Festival of Art and Ideas wrapped up after a vibrant weekend that left culture, heritage, and literature enthusiasts thoroughly engaged. Held in the heart of Jammu, the festival brought together artists, writers, historians, and performers for a series of events that celebrated the region's rich traditions and contemporary creativity.

Organisers curated a packed schedule that included art exhibitions, heritage walks, literary readings, and panel discussions. The festival aimed to bridge the gap between the past and the present, offering attendees a chance to explore local heritage while engaging with modern ideas.

Heritage Walks and Art Exhibitions

One of the standout features of the festival was the heritage walks that took participants through historic neighbourhoods and landmarks. Guides shared stories of the city's evolution, from its Rajput-era roots to its role in modern India. These walks proved popular with both locals and visitors, many of whom said they discovered hidden corners of the city they had never noticed before.

Art exhibitions showcased works by emerging and established artists from the region. Paintings, sculptures, and installations drew on themes ranging from folklore to contemporary urban life. Visitors spent hours browsing the displays, often stopping to chat with the artists about their creative processes.

Literary Sessions Draw Book Lovers

The literary segment of the festival was a major draw. Authors read excerpts from their recent works and participated in discussions on topics such as storytelling in the digital age and the role of literature in preserving cultural identity. Book lovers packed the sessions, with many lining up to get their copies signed.

Panelists included writers who have explored the complexities of life in Jammu and Kashmir, offering perspectives that resonated with the audience. One session focused on the challenges of translating regional literature into English, sparking a lively debate about authenticity and accessibility.

Music and Dance Add Vibrancy

Evenings at the festival were given over to music and dance performances. Local folk troupes shared the stage with contemporary artists, creating a fusion of sounds that reflected the region's diverse musical heritage. The audience swayed to traditional beats and applauded experimental acts that pushed boundaries.

A highlight was a performance that blended Sufi music with modern instrumentation. The artist, known for reviving old folk tunes, received a standing ovation. Organisers said such cross-genre collaborations were central to the festival's mission of fostering dialogue between tradition and innovation.

The Tawi Festival of Art and Ideas has established itself as a key event on the region's cultural calendar. With its blend of heritage, art, and literature, it offers a platform for creative expression and intellectual exchange. Organisers are already planning next year's edition, hoping to expand the program and attract an even wider audience.
