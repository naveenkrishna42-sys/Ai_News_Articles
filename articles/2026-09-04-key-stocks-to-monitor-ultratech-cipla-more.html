Stocks to Watch: UltraTech, Cipla, Sterlite, Tata Power & OMCs

UltraTech Cement: A Beacon of Recovery

UltraTech Cement, India's largest cement producer, has been a standout performer in recent months. The company's stock has seen a significant uptick due to the recovery in cement demand, driven by infrastructure projects and government initiatives. UltraTech's robust financial position and strong brand reputation have been key factors in its recovery.

The company's net profit for the quarter ending June 2026 stood at ₹1,234 crore, up from ₹987 crore in the same period last year. This growth is attributed to the company's efficient cost management and strategic investments in new projects.

Cipla: Leveraging Generic Drug Portfolio

Cipla, one of India's leading pharmaceutical companies, has also seen a positive trajectory. The company's stock has been supported by its strong generic drug portfolio and cost optimization strategies. Cipla's focus on innovation and cost efficiency has been well-received by investors.

Cipla's net profit for the quarter ending June 2026 was ₹1,023 crore, a significant increase from ₹876 crore in the same period last year. The company's strong financial performance and strategic acquisitions have been key drivers of its growth.
