Canada's Trade War Gambit: A Lesson for India?

Canada's Foreign Minister, Mélanie Joly, recently made a bold move by publicly supporting India's stance against the US trade war. Her comments, which emphasized the importance of diversification and reducing reliance on a single market, have sparked discussions about India's own trade strategy. This move comes at a time when India is grappling with the fallout of the US-China trade war, which has led to a significant shift in global trade dynamics.

Why Canada's Move Matters

Canada's decision to publicly support India's position is a strategic move that could have significant implications for India's trade relations with the US. The US has been imposing tariffs on a wide range of Canadian goods, including steel, aluminum, and softwood lumber, in an effort to pressure Canada into accepting the US's terms for a new trade deal. Canada's defiance of the US's trade war tactics has been well thought out, and it has sent a strong message to the US that it is not willing to be bullied into accepting unfavorable terms.
