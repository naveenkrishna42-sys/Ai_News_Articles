Supreme Court Forms Panel to Probe NEET Protest Violence

The Supreme Court has formed a five-member committee to investigate the violence that erupted during student protests against the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) examination. The committee is headed by former Supreme Court judge Subhash Reddy and includes four other members, including a former police officer and a legal expert.

Committee Composition and Mandate

The committee, which will be tasked with investigating the police actions during the protests, will consist of former Supreme Court judge Subhash Reddy, former police officer Rajesh Kumar, legal expert Anil Desai, and two other members whose identities have not been disclosed yet. The committee has been given six weeks to submit its report to the Supreme Court.

Background of the Protests

The protests, which began in several cities across the country, were sparked by the decision of the Medical Council of India (MCI) to conduct the NEET examination in English. The students, who were protesting against the decision, were met with excessive use of force by the police, leading to widespread condemnation from various quarters.

Committee's Role and Responsibilities

The committee will be responsible for investigating the police actions during the protests and will be required to submit a detailed report to the Supreme Court. The report will be based on the evidence gathered by the committee members during their investigations. The committee will also be required to make recommendations for the prevention of such incidents in the future.

What Happens Next?

With the committee formed, the next step will be for the committee members to gather evidence and conduct investigations into the police actions during the protests. The committee will then be required to submit its report to the Supreme Court within the stipulated time frame. The Supreme Court will then consider the report and take appropriate action based on the findings of the committee.
