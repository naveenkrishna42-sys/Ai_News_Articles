iOS 27 Is Apple's Biggest Software Reset in Years

Apple has begun seeding iOS 27 to compatible iPhones, and the update is being positioned as the company's most consequential software release of 2026. The headline act is a standalone Siri AI app that finally treats Apple's assistant as a first-class product rather than a voice shortcut buried inside the OS.

For Indian buyers weighing an iPhone upgrade, the practical question is simple: which features actually change daily use, and which ones are marketing gloss? Here is the breakdown, based on Apple's release documentation and early rollout reports.

What's New in iOS 27

1. Standalone Siri AI App

Siri now lives in its own app with a persistent conversation history, text and voice input, and cross-app task execution. You can ask it to pull a PDF from Mail, summarise it, and draft a reply without hopping between apps. Apple describes it as "a profoundly more capable and personal assistant," and the app-based approach is the clearest sign yet that Siri is being rebuilt around large language models rather than rigid intents.

2. Smarter Photo Edits

Photos and Camera gain generative editing tools: object removal with clean background fill, subject relighting, and one-tap retouch that preserves skin texture rather than smearing it. Most of this processing runs on-device, which matters for privacy and for users on limited data plans.

3. On-Device Intelligence Layer

Apple is pushing more inference to the Neural Engine, with cloud handoff only for heavier requests. This reduces latency and keeps personal data local — a meaningful differentiator against Android rivals that lean harder on server-side processing.

4. System-Wide AI Integrations

Writing tools, notification summaries, and call transcription now appear across Mail, Messages, Notes and Phone. Safari gets AI-assisted page summaries, and Spotlight surfaces app actions directly.

5. Compatibility and Rollout

The update reaches iPhone 11 and newer, though the most demanding AI features are reserved for A17 Pro and A18-class chips. Apple is rolling features out in phases, so some capabilities may arrive later in India depending on language support.

Real-World Buyer Implications

If you own an iPhone 13 or 14, you will get the new interface and several AI tools, but not the full generative photo suite. That gap is deliberate — Apple is using software to nudge upgrades toward the iPhone 16 and 17 families, where the Neural Engine headroom is sufficient.

For buyers in the ₹60,000–₹1,50,000 bracket, the calculus is shifting. A two-year-old iPhone now delivers noticeably fewer AI features than a current model, which shortens the effective software value of older hardware. Conversely, if you already own an A17 Pro device, iOS 27 is a free upgrade that materially improves the camera and assistant experience.

Pros and Cons

  • Pros: Siri finally feels like a modern assistant; on-device photo editing protects privacy; no subscription cost; broad device support from iPhone 11.
  • Pros: Writing tools and transcription save real time for students and professionals.
  • Cons: Best AI features locked to newer chips; phased rollout creates feature inconsistency across regions.
  • Cons: Generative edits can still produce artefacts on complex backgrounds; storage and battery demands rise with heavy AI use.

Verdict

iOS 27 is Apple's clearest attempt yet to make AI feel native rather than bolted on. The standalone Siri app and on-device photo editing are the two features Indian users will notice immediately. The catch is hardware gating — older iPhones get a taste, not the full meal. If AI features drive your purchase decision, buy current silicon.
