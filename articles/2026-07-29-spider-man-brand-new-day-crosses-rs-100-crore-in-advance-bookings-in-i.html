Spider-Man: Brand New Day crosses Rs 100 crore in advance bookings in India

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Advance booking sales surpass Rs 100 crore in India.
  • Film poised for one of the biggest opening weekends globally.
  • China records biggest superhero opening in seven years.

Tom Holland's latest Spider-Man outing, 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day', has already made history before its official release. The film has crossed the Rs 100 crore mark in advance ticket sales in India, according to industry tracker Pinkvilla.

The number signals extraordinary demand. The previous Spider-Man films set high benchmarks, but this advance figure suggests a new peak for the franchise in the country.

Record-Breaking Advance Sales

Advance bookings for 'Brand New Day' opened to a frenzy. Fans snapped up tickets across multiplex chains and single screens, pushing the total past the Rs 100 crore milestone well before the first show.

Trade analysts attribute the rush to Holland's star power and the emotional stakes teased in the film's promotional material. The film reportedly confirms a heartbreaking reality for MJ without Peter Parker, adding to the anticipation.

Global Box Office Momentum

The Indian advance is part of a wider global wave. According to Boxoffice Pro, 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' could potentially score the second biggest opening weekend of all time. Only the record held by 'Avengers: Endgame' stands above it.

In China, the film has already swung to the biggest superhero opening in seven years, as reported by The Hollywood Reporter. The Chinese market response is particularly notable given the recent slowdown for Hollywood blockbusters there.

Industry observers point out that while superhero fatigue is a real concern for some franchises, Spider-Man remains immune. As one analyst put it, 'Superhero fatigue is real. Spider-Man fatigue is not.'

What This Means for the Franchise

The advance numbers suggest that audiences are deeply invested in Holland's iteration of the character. The 'Brand New Day' subtitle hints at a narrative reset, which has only stoked curiosity.

Pinkvilla's initial report indicates that the film's marketing campaign successfully balanced nostalgia with fresh stakes. The result is a pre-release momentum that rivals the biggest films in Indian cinema history.

Exhibitors are adding extra shows to meet demand. Some multiplexes have reported sold-out advance blocks for the opening weekend in major cities like Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru.

The China Factor

China's response is a critical piece of the puzzle. The seven-year record for a superhero opening there underscores the film's cross-cultural appeal. Hollywood studios have struggled to consistently break into the Chinese market recently, but Spider-Man has bucked the trend.

Local Chinese media noted that the film's visual effects and Holland's likability were key drivers. The story's emotional core also resonated with audiences who may not follow every Marvel plot twist.

Looking Ahead

All eyes are now on the film's opening day numbers. If the advance trend holds, 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' could redefine what a superhero film can earn in its first weekend. The industry will watch closely as the film hits screens globally.
