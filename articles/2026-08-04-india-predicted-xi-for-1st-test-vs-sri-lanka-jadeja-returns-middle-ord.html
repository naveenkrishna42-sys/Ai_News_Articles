India Predicted XI for 1st Test vs Sri Lanka: Jadeja Returns, Middle-Order Tussle

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ravindra Jadeja is set to make a return to the Test side for the series opener.
  • Sai Sudharsan and Devdutt Padikkal are in contention for a middle-order slot.
  • Pacer Auqib Nabi could be in line for a debut as India finalise their XI.

Jadeja's return bolsters spin attack

The Indian team management has a pleasant headache ahead of the first Test against Sri Lanka, with the return of Ravindra Jadeja set to add balance to the side. The all-rounder's inclusion is all but confirmed, giving the bowling attack a left-arm spin option and the batting order some much-needed depth.

Jadeja's presence allows India to field a more settled combination, especially if the pitch at the venue offers assistance to spinners. His ability to control the run rate and pick up crucial wickets in the middle overs makes him a vital cog in the team's plans.

Middle-order puzzle: Sudharsan vs Padikkal

The biggest selection debate revolves around the number three or four slot, with Sai Sudharsan and Devdutt Padikkal both staking strong claims. Sudharsan's impressive domestic season and his composure against pace bowling have made him a favourite among the think tank.

Padikkal, on the other hand, brings a left-handed option and has been in consistent form for India A. The team management is likely to take a call based on the match conditions and the overall balance of the side, with both players capable of anchoring the innings.

Auqib Nabi in line for a debut

Another interesting prospect is the potential inclusion of pacer Auqib Nabi, who has been turning heads with his pace and accuracy in domestic cricket. If the team opts for an extra seamer, Nabi could be handed a maiden Test cap, adding variety to the attack.

His ability to generate bounce and move the ball off the seam could prove handy on a track that might offer early assistance to the fast bowlers. The final call will depend on the overhead conditions and the composition of the Sri Lankan batting line-up.

Opening combination and wicketkeeper

At the top of the order, the pairing of Rohit Sharma and Yashasvi Jaiswal is expected to continue, given their success in recent series. The wicketkeeper's slot is likely to be filled by Rishabh Pant or Dhruv Jurel, depending on the team's preference for experience or youth.

The middle order will see Virat Kohli and Shubman Gill, with the latter expected to bat at number three. The lower middle order will be bolstered by the presence of Ravindra Jadeja, who can contribute valuable runs down the order.

Bowling attack: Spin or pace?

The final XI will hinge on the pitch at the venue, with the team management weighing the option of playing two spinners and two pacers, or three spinners if conditions are dry. Ravichandran Ashwin is a certainty, and with Jadeja in the mix, the spin department looks strong.

If the team opts for a third spinner, Kuldeep Yadav could come into the picture. Otherwise, the pace attack will likely feature Mohammed Siraj and one of Akash Deep or Auqib Nabi, depending on the conditions and the need for an extra seamer.

The team is expected to announce their playing XI on the morning of the match, with the management keeping their cards close to their chest. The final decision will be a balancing act between strengthening the batting and ensuring the bowling attack has enough teeth to take 20 wickets.

All eyes will be on the selection call, as India look to start the series on a winning note. The focus will be on getting the combination right, with the first Test set to set the tone for the rest of the series.
