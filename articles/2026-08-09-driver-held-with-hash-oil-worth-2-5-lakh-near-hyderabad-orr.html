Driver held with hash oil worth ₹2.5 lakh near Hyderabad ORR

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Driver arrested with hash oil valued at ₹2.5 lakh near Outer Ring Road
  • Police seized the contraband during a routine check
  • Investigation into the source and distribution network is ongoing

Hyderabad police arrested a driver with hash oil worth approximately ₹2.5 lakh near the Outer Ring Road (ORR) on Sunday. The accused was intercepted during a routine patrol, and a subsequent search led to the recovery of the contraband.

Arrest and seizure

The driver, whose identity has not been officially disclosed yet, was found in possession of a significant quantity of hash oil. Police officials said the seizure was made during a vehicle check in the early hours. The estimated street value of the oil is pegged at ₹2.5 lakh.

Hash oil, a concentrated cannabis extract, is considered a narcotic substance under Indian law. Its possession and distribution are prohibited under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985.

Police action

Following the recovery, the police registered a case against the driver and took him into custody. The vehicle used in the alleged transportation has also been seized as part of the investigation. Officials are now probing whether the accused was acting alone or as part of a larger network.

Sources indicate that the driver was possibly transporting the consignment for local distribution. However, no official confirmation has been made regarding the intended destination or the source of the hash oil.

Investigation underway

The police are questioning the accused to trace the supply chain. Efforts are on to identify any accomplices who may have been involved in the procurement or planned sale of the contraband. The authorities have not ruled out further arrests.

This is not an isolated incident. In recent months, Hyderabad police have intensified crackdowns on drug peddling, especially along major corridors like the ORR, which connects several key areas of the city and its outskirts.

Officials have urged the public to report any suspicious activity related to drug trafficking. They also reiterated that strict action would be taken against those found violating the NDPS Act.

The accused is expected to be produced before a local court, where the police will seek remand. Further details about the case, including the exact quantity of the hash oil and the accused's background, are awaited.

As the investigation progresses, more clarity is likely to emerge on the scale of the operation. For now, the focus remains on dismantling any potential network and preventing similar incidents in the future.
