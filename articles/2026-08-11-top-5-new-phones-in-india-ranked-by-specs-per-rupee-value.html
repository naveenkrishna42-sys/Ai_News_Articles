For budget-conscious buyers who want the most phone for their money, the Redmi Turbo 5 is the clear winner—flagship-level performance at a mid-range price.

If you prefer a near-premium experience with a better brand reputation and are willing to spend a bit more, the Motorola Edge 70 Pro is your best bet.

The OnePlus 15 suits buyers who value software updates and premium build over raw specs.
