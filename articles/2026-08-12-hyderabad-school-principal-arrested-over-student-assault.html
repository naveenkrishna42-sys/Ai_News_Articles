Arrest in Hyderabad

The principal of Sri Chaitanya School in Hyderabad was arrested on Wednesday in connection with an alleged assault on a student, police said. The arrest follows a complaint filed against the principal, whose name has not been officially released yet.

Officials confirmed that the principal was taken into custody after preliminary inquiries. The exact circumstances leading to the arrest are still being pieced together by investigators.

The case pertains to an incident involving a student at the school, though specific details about the nature of the assault have not been made public. Police have registered a case under relevant sections of the law, but the chargesheet is yet to be filed.

Sources indicated that the complaint was lodged by the student's family, prompting immediate police action. The school administration has not issued a formal statement so far.

Authorities are recording statements from staff, students, and witnesses to establish the sequence of events. The principal is expected to be produced before a local court for judicial remand, as per standard procedure in such matters.
