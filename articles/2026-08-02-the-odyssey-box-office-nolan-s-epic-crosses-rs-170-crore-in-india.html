The Odyssey Box Office: Nolan's Epic Crosses Rs 170 Crore in India

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The Odyssey collected Rs 170 crore in 16 days in India, a 75% jump on its third Saturday.
  • The film has become Christopher Nolan's highest-grossing release ever in India, surpassing 'Oppenheimer'.
  • The epic drama continues its strong run, with box office momentum persisting into the third week.
  • The film's success in India reflects a strong word-of-mouth and repeat viewership, even as it faces new releases.

A Record-Breaking Third Saturday

Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey continues its relentless march at the Indian box office. The epic drama witnessed a massive 75 per cent jump on its third Saturday, pushing its 16-day total past the Rs 170 crore mark. This surge on a typically slower weekend slot underscores the film's extraordinary hold over audiences.

The film's performance on its third Saturday is not just a blip; it is a testament to the sustained interest in Nolan's mythological retelling. Trade analysts point to strong word-of-mouth and repeat viewings as key drivers, with the film showing remarkable resilience even as new releases compete for screen space.

Beating Oppenheimer's Lifetime Run

The latest collections have cemented The Odyssey's status as Nolan's highest-grossing film ever in India. It surpassed the lifetime earnings of Oppenheimer within just 13 days of its release, a feat that took the previous record holder several weeks to achieve. The margin of victory is expected to grow significantly in the coming days.

This milestone is particularly notable given the differing scales of the two films. While Oppenheimer was a dialogue-driven historical drama, The Odyssey is a visual spectacle with grand set pieces, which appears to have resonated more deeply with Indian audiences. The film's performance in non-metro markets has been especially robust, a factor that has propelled its overall numbers.

India's Growing Appetite for Epic Cinema

The box office trajectory of The Odyssey reflects a broader trend in India, where audiences are increasingly embracing big-screen spectacles from global filmmakers. The film's success is not limited to the major metros; it has drawn crowds in smaller cities, a sign of the expanding reach of Hollywood releases in the country.

Industry observers note that the film's mythological source material has helped it connect with Indian viewers on a cultural level. The story of Odysseus, with its themes of journey and homecoming, has found resonance across diverse demographics, contributing to the film's wide appeal.

What Lies Ahead

With the third weekend expected to add another significant chunk to its total, The Odyssey is on course to challenge the Rs 200 crore club in India. The coming week will test its staying power as it faces the onslaught of new releases, but current trends suggest that Nolan's epic has the momentum to sail through.

All eyes are now on how the film performs on its fourth Friday, which will be a crucial indicator of its longevity. If the current pace holds, The Odyssey could end its theatrical run as one of the highest-grossing Hollywood films ever in India, a remarkable achievement for a director known for pushing cinematic boundaries.
