Smart Payment Strategies for Indian Bettors

Using credit cards for sports betting often triggers cash advance fees (2-3% upfront) and immediate interest, eroding your bankroll. Debit cards and ACH transfers are safer, but not all sportsbooks support them. Always check your card issuer’s policy—some Indian banks block gambling transactions entirely. For frequent bettors, maintaining a separate bank account for betting can simplify tracking and reduce fees.
