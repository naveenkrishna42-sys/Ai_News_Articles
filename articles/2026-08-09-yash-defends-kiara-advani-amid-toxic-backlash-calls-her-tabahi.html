Yash Defends Kiara Advani Amid Toxic Backlash, Calls Her 'Tabahi'

A Show of Support in Bengaluru

Kannada superstar Yash publicly came out in support of co-star Kiara Advani during the trailer launch of their upcoming film Toxic in Bengaluru. Addressing the gathering, Yash did not mince words when he described Kiara as 'tabahi' — a term that roughly translates to 'destruction' or 'devastation', clearly meant as a compliment to her screen presence and dedication.

The event, which was meant to unveil the film's trailer, turned into a moment of solidarity as Yash acknowledged the online negativity that has been swirling around Kiara in recent weeks. His remarks came amid reported trolling over a song from the movie, though the specifics of the controversy were not detailed at the event.

'Do Whatever You Believe In'

Yash's advice to Kiara was direct and personal. He told her not to get bothered by what people say, urging her to stay focused on her own convictions. "You don't care! Whatever you believe in, you should do," he said, according to reports from the launch.

His words were not just a casual pat on the back. Yash went on to highlight the effort Kiara had poured into the project, noting that she had worked hard on Toxic and had gone through a lot during the process. He described her as down-to-earth, saying she had fully surrendered to the film's vision — a quality he clearly admired.

Behind the Scenes of Toxic

The film, directed by Geetu Mohandas, has been one of the most anticipated releases in Indian cinema, largely due to the pairing of Yash and Kiara. The actor's comments shed light on the demanding nature of the shoot, suggesting that Kiara faced challenges that went beyond the usual rigours of filmmaking.

Yash's praise painted a picture of an actor who was fully committed to her craft. "Kiara was down to earth, surrendered to vision," he said, offering a glimpse into the working dynamic between the two leads. The remark also served as a subtle rebuke to those who have been critical of her work in the film.

Navigating Online Negativity

The entertainment industry has increasingly become a battleground for online opinions, and the Toxic team is no stranger to that reality. While the exact nature of the trolling was not elaborated on during the launch, Yash's intervention signals a growing trend of actors publicly addressing the toxicity of social media discourse.

By stepping in, Yash has positioned himself not just as a co-star but as a mentor figure, someone willing to shield a colleague from unwarranted criticism. His comments have resonated with fans, many of whom have taken to social media to echo his sentiments and show support for Kiara.

What Lies Ahead

As the trailer makes its rounds and the release date approaches, all eyes will be on how the film is received by audiences. The controversy surrounding the song may have generated buzz, but Yash's endorsement has added a layer of intrigue to the film's promotional campaign.
