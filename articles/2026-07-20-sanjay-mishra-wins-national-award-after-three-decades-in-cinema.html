Sanjay Mishra Wins National Award After Three Decades in Cinema

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sanjay Mishra wins National Film Award for Best Actor after 30-year career
  • The award recognizes his contribution to Indian cinema, known for roles in 'Masaan' and 'Newton'
  • Mishra's win highlights the recognition of character actors in the industry

Veteran actor Sanjay Mishra has finally received the National Film Award for Best Actor, three decades after he first stepped into the film industry. The announcement came on Monday, July 20, 2026, marking a milestone for the actor known for his powerful performances in films like 'Masaan' and 'Newton'.

Mishra, who has often played supporting roles, took home the top acting honor for his lead role in a yet-to-be-named film. The actor's journey from television comedy to critically acclaimed cinema has been long and winding.

From Comedy to Critical Acclaim

Sanjay Mishra started his career in the early 1990s with small roles in TV shows like 'Office Office'. He later moved to films, where he became known for his comic timing in movies like 'Dhamaal' and 'Golmaal' series.

But it was his turn to serious roles that brought him widespread recognition. His performance in 'Masaan' (2015) earned him critical praise, and his role in 'Newton' (2017) further cemented his reputation as a versatile actor.

A Win for Character Actors

The National Award comes as a recognition of Mishra's ability to elevate any character he plays. Industry insiders say this win is also a nod to the growing appreciation for character actors in Indian cinema.

Mishra has often spoken about the struggles of being typecast as a comedian. In recent years, he has deliberately chosen roles that showcase his range, from a grieving father in 'Aankhon Dekhi' to a quirky scientist in 'Bhooter Bhabishyat'.

Reactions and Tributes

News of the award sparked celebrations among fans and colleagues. Director Neeraj Ghaywan, who worked with Mishra in 'Masaan', called it a long-overdue recognition. "Sanjay ji has been the backbone of so many films," Ghaywan said. "This award is for every actor who keeps working without expecting fame."

On social media, fans posted clips of Mishra's most memorable scenes. Many pointed out that he has never chased stardom, choosing instead to focus on the craft.

What's Next for Sanjay Mishra

The actor has several projects lined up, including a crime drama and a family comedy. He is also set to appear in a web series directed by an acclaimed filmmaker.

With the National Award in hand, Mishra's career is entering a new phase. The recognition could open doors to more lead roles and bigger budgets. For now, the actor is said to be spending time with his family, soaking in the moment.

Industry watchers will be keen to see how this honor shapes his future choices. One thing is clear: Sanjay Mishra's best work may still be ahead of him.
