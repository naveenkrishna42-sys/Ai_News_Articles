Amazfit update adds new training features to eight smartwatches

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Update covers eight Amazfit watch models
  • Adds dozens of new workout modes including swimming and rowing
  • Improves map-related features for better navigation
  • Strengthens Amazfit's position against Garmin in fitness wearables

Amazfit has begun rolling out a significant software update that brings a host of new training features to eight of its smartwatch models. The move is being seen as a direct challenge to Garmin's dominance in the fitness wearables segment, with the update adding capabilities that were previously the preserve of more expensive rivals.

The update, which is being delivered over the air to compatible devices, introduces dozens of new workout modes. Among the most notable additions are specialised swimming and rowing tracking features, which allow users to capture detailed metrics during these activities. The inclusion of these modes is particularly significant, as they have long been a key selling point for Garmin's higher-end sports watches.

Expanded workout modes and enhanced mapping

Beyond swimming and rowing, the update broadens the range of available sports profiles, catering to a wider spectrum of fitness enthusiasts. From gym-based routines to outdoor adventures, the new modes are designed to provide more accurate data capture across different activities. This expansion effectively brings Amazfit's feature set closer to what users might expect from dedicated sports watches.

The update also improves map-related functionality. Users will now find enhanced navigation tools, making it easier to view routes and track progress during outdoor workouts. This is a crucial upgrade for runners and cyclists who rely on their watch to guide them through unfamiliar terrain. The improved mapping is expected to make Amazfit watches more self-reliant, reducing the need to pull out a phone mid-exercise.

Eight models in line for the update

While the company has not released the full list of compatible devices, it is known that the update covers eight different Amazfit watch models. Owners of these devices will receive a notification on their watch or companion app when the update is ready to install. As with most firmware updates, the rollout is likely to be phased, meaning some users may get access earlier than others.

The update is being delivered free of charge, which adds to the value proposition of Amazfit's hardware. The brand has long been recognised for offering strong specifications at competitive prices, and this software boost further enhances that reputation. It also serves as a reminder that firmware updates can significantly extend the usefulness of a device, often more so than hardware changes alone.

Raising the bar for budget fitness trackers

Industry observers have noted that this move puts pressure on Garmin, which typically charges a premium for advanced training features. By bringing similar capabilities to more affordable watches, Amazfit is blurring the line between budget and premium fitness wearables. The update effectively gives users access to pro-level tools without the pro-level price tag.

This is not the first time Amazfit has leveraged software updates to improve its devices. The company has a track record of adding new features post-launch, which helps maintain user engagement and loyalty. However, this particular update is one of the most substantial to date, both in terms of the number of new features and the range of devices covered.

For existing Amazfit owners, the update is a welcome bonus that enhances the functionality of their watches without any additional cost. For potential buyers, it makes the brand an even more attractive option in a crowded market. The combination of solid hardware, regular updates, and aggressive pricing is proving to be a winning formula.

Looking ahead, all eyes will be on how Garmin responds. The company has built its reputation on the depth and accuracy of its training metrics, and it may need to accelerate its own software development to stay ahead. For now, Amazfit users can enjoy a fresh batch of features that make their watches more capable than ever.

The rollout is expected to continue over the coming weeks, with all eight models likely to receive the update by the end of the month. Users are advised to ensure their watches are charged and connected to Wi-Fi to facilitate a smooth installation process.
