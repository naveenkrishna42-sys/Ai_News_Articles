Spider-Man: Brand New Day Nets $143M in Second Weekend, Third-Best Ever

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Second weekend gross of $143 million is the third-best in US box office history.
  • Film is performing strongly in India, closing in on Avengers: Endgame's record.
  • Reviews and interviews highlight James Norton's role in the new Spider-Man installment.

Marvel's latest web-slinger adventure, Spider-Man: Brand New Day, continues to defy gravity at the box office, swinging to a spectacular $143 million in its second weekend in the United States. The haul marks the third-best second weekend ever recorded domestically, according to industry reports. Only a handful of blockbusters have managed to pull in more cash in their sophomore frame.

The film's staying power has surprised even seasoned box office analysts. After a record-breaking debut, the drop-off in ticket sales has been remarkably shallow, a sign of strong word-of-mouth and repeat viewings. The numbers suggest that audiences are embracing this new chapter in the Spider-Man saga with unusual enthusiasm.

India Box Office Race

Across the globe, the film is posting equally impressive numbers. In India, Spider-Man: Brand New Day is racing towards the lifetime collections of Avengers: Endgame, a benchmark that seemed untouchable for any Hollywood release. Trade analysts note that the film's appeal in the Indian market has been bolstered by its blend of spectacle and emotional stakes.

The Hindi-dubbed version, in particular, has seen strong occupancy in multiplexes, with many shows running to packed houses even in week two. The film's momentum in India could push its global tally significantly higher over the coming weeks.

Critical Reception and James Norton

Amid the box office frenzy, reviews have been largely positive. Critics have singled out the performances, especially that of James Norton, whose role in the film has generated considerable buzz. In an interview with The Hindu, Norton discussed his approach to the character and the pressures of joining a franchise of this scale.

Norton's portrayal has been described as a standout, adding a layer of gravitas to the proceedings. The film's balance of high-octane action and character-driven moments has resonated with both critics and audiences, a combination that is often elusive in superhero cinema.

Life Lessons and Cultural Impact

The film's reach extends beyond the box office. A recent Rediff piece listed seven life lessons from Spider-Man: Brand New Day, a testament to its cultural footprint. From responsibility to resilience, the film's themes are striking a chord with viewers of all ages.

This cultural resonance is likely a key driver of the film's repeat business. Families are returning for second and third viewings, and the film's quotable lines are already becoming part of the pop culture lexicon.

What's Next

With the second weekend now in the books, all eyes are on how the film will perform in the coming weeks. The third weekend will be a crucial test of its legs, especially with new releases vying for audience attention. If the current trend holds, Spider-Man: Brand New Day could end its run as one of the highest-grossing films of all time, both in the US and globally.
