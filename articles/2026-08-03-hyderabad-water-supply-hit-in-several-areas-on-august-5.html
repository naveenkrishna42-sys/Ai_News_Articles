Hyderabad water supply hit in several areas on August 5

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Water supply will be disrupted in parts of Hyderabad on August 5.
  • The disruption is due to maintenance work by the water board.
  • Residents in affected areas are advised to store water in advance.

Hyderabad residents in several areas will face a disruption in water supply on August 5, according to an official announcement. The Hyderabad Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board (HMWS&SB) has scheduled maintenance work that will affect normal water distribution for the day.

Officials have not yet specified the exact list of neighbourhoods that will be impacted, but they have advised residents to store water in advance to avoid inconvenience. The maintenance is part of routine upkeep of pipelines and related infrastructure.

Reason for the disruption

The water board is carrying out essential repair and maintenance work on key pipelines. Such work is often required to ensure the long-term reliability of the supply system, but it temporarily interrupts service in the connected zones.

While the duration of the disruption has not been officially confirmed, similar maintenance tasks in the past have typically lasted for several hours during the day. The board has urged residents to cooperate and plan accordingly.

What residents should do

Those living in the affected areas are advised to store sufficient water for drinking and domestic use before August 5. The board has also requested residents to use water judiciously during the maintenance period.

For any urgent queries or assistance, residents can reach out to the HMWS&SB customer care helpline. However, the board has not yet released the specific contact numbers for this disruption, and details are expected to be shared closer to the date.

Past incidents and preparedness

In previous instances of scheduled maintenance, the water board has managed to restore supply by evening in most areas. However, some low-lying zones or areas at the end of the distribution network may experience delays in restoration.

Residents are encouraged to follow official social media channels and local announcements for real-time updates. The board has promised to keep the public informed about any changes to the schedule.

Looking ahead

The water supply is expected to normalise by the next day, August 6, once maintenance is complete. Residents are advised to stay alert for further notifications from the HMWS&SB regarding any extension of the work or additional areas affected.
