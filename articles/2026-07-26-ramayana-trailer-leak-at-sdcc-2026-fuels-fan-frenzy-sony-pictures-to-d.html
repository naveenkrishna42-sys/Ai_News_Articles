Ramayana Trailer Leak at SDCC 2026 Fuels Fan Frenzy, Sony Pictures to Distribute Globally

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • A video from San Diego Comic-Con 2026 claiming to show footage from the upcoming 'Ramayana' film has gone viral on social media.
  • Producer Namit Malhotra has officially announced Sony Pictures as the global distributor for the film.
  • The makers surprised SDCC attendees by distributing gold-coloured shankhas, sparking further excitement.

Viral Clip from SDCC 2026 Stirs Speculation

A video that appears to be from the set or trailer of the upcoming film 'Ramayana' has taken the internet by storm. The clip, which surfaced online during the San Diego Comic-Con 2026, shows glimpses of Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram, Yash as Ravana, and Sai Pallavi as Sita. Fans have been sharing it widely, demanding the makers release the official trailer immediately.

The three-minute video, shot on a mobile phone inside a packed convention hall, shows a large screen displaying what looks like a battle sequence. The production value and the scale of the visuals have impressed many, but the studio has not confirmed whether this is an officially sanctioned teaser or a leak.

Official Trailer Postponed, Sony Pictures Steps In

Adding to the intrigue, producer Namit Malhotra announced that the film's trailer, originally scheduled for a release during the convention, has been postponed. In a statement, Malhotra confirmed that Sony Pictures has been onboarded as the global distributor for 'Ramayana'. The move signals the film's ambition for a worldwide theatrical release, targeting both Indian and international audiences.

The decision to delay the trailer has only intensified curiosity. Industry insiders suggest the makers are waiting for a larger promotional window closer to the film's release date. Meanwhile, the leaked footage has already created a groundswell of online conversation.

Gold Shankha Surprise at Comic-Con

In a creative promotional stunt, the 'Ramayana' team surprised attendees at SDCC 2026 by distributing gold-coloured shankhas, or conch shells. The props, reminiscent of the divine conch blown by Lord Ram in the epic, were handed out at the Sony Pictures booth. Fans took to social media to share pictures of the shiny souvenirs, calling it a clever nod to the film's mythology.

This marketing move, combined with the leaked clip and the distribution announcement, has ensured that 'Ramayana' remains one of the most talked-about projects at the convention. Yash, who plays the ten-headed king Ravana, has been particularly vocal about his preparation. In a recent interview, he revealed why he "used to get big speakers" while rehearsing for the role, hinting at the intense physical and vocal demands of the character.

What to Watch Next

With the trailer postponed and a global distribution deal in place, the makers of 'Ramayana' are clearly playing a long game. The film, directed by Nitesh Tiwari, is expected to be one of the most expensive Indian productions ever. All eyes are now on the official trailer release, which is likely to happen in the coming weeks. Until then, fans will have to make do with the leaked clip and the gold shankhas.
