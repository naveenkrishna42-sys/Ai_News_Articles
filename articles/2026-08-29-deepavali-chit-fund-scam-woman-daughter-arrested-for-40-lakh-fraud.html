Woman and daughter arrested for ₹40 lakh fraud

In a recent development, a woman and her daughter have been arrested for cheating 350 people of ₹40 lakh through a Deepavali chit fund scheme in Chennai. The scam, which has come to light after a year-long investigation, highlights the persistent issue of chit fund frauds in India.

Chit fund scams: A persistent issue

Chit funds, which are legal in India, have been a source of financial scams for decades. According to reports, chit fund scams have cost Indians over ₹1,000 crore annually. This latest arrest underscores the need for stricter regulation and enforcement of existing laws.

How chit funds work

Chit funds operate on a simple principle: members contribute a fixed amount each month, and the fund is rotated among the members. The member who gets the fund at the end of the cycle receives an additional amount, which is the profit. However, the system can be easily manipulated, leading to scams.

The Deepavali chit fund scam

The woman and her daughter, who are the accused in this case, allegedly manipulated the chit fund system to siphon off the money. They are accused of cheating 350 people of ₹40 lakh. The victims, who had invested their hard-earned money in the chit fund, are now left with no recourse.

Legal and regulatory framework

The legal framework for chit funds in India is governed by the Chit Funds Act, 1982. The Act provides for the registration of chit funds and the appointment of a trustee. However, the Act has been criticized for being too lenient, leading to a proliferation of chit fund scams.

Victims demand stricter enforcement

The victims of the Deepavali chit fund scam have demanded stricter enforcement of the existing laws. They have also called for better awareness and education about the risks associated with chit funds. The latest arrest is a step in the right direction, but more needs to be done to prevent such scams in the future.

Pros and cons of chit funds

  • Pros: Chit funds provide a way for people to save and invest money. They are also a source of credit for the poor.
  • Cons: Chit funds are prone to manipulation and fraud. The system is also complex and difficult to understand.
