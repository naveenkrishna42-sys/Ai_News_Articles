Dehradun's Digital Elite: A New Ranking

Favikon, a platform that tracks social media influence, has released its ranking of the top 20 influencers in Dehradun for 2026. The list, published on February 28, 2025, offers a snapshot of who is shaping online conversations in the Himalayan foothill city.

The ranking spans creators across lifestyle, travel, food, and local culture. It includes names that have built substantial followings on Instagram, YouTube, and emerging platforms. The selection process weighed engagement rates, audience reach, and content authenticity.

Who Made the Cut

The top spots feature a mix of familiar faces and newer entrants. Several lifestyle bloggers who started during the pandemic have now crossed the lakh-follower mark. Travel vloggers documenting Uttarakhand's hidden trails also feature prominently.

Food reviewers who focus on Dehradun's street food scene—from Wala Tiba to Paltan Bazaar—have gained traction. A few creators in the sustainability and wellness space have also broken into the top 20, reflecting a shift in local audience interests.

Favikon did not disclose individual follower counts or specific engagement metrics for each influencer. But the company's methodology typically combines quantitative data with qualitative assessment of content quality.

Why Dehradun's Influencer Scene Matters

Dehradun has emerged as a digital content hub outside the metro cities. Its proximity to tourist destinations like Mussoorie and Rishikesh gives local creators a natural advantage in travel and adventure content. The city's growing startup ecosystem has also attracted young professionals who double as content creators.

The ranking underscores a broader trend: smaller cities are producing influencers with national reach. Brands are increasingly looking beyond Mumbai and Delhi for authentic regional voices. Dehradun's creators, with their local knowledge and relatable storytelling, fit that demand.

What the Ranking Doesn't Tell You

Favikon's list is a curated snapshot, not an exhaustive directory. Several well-known Dehradun influencers with large followings did not make the cut, possibly due to lower engagement in recent months. The ranking also excludes creators who focus on niche topics like education or tech reviews.

Critics argue that such lists can oversimplify influence. A high ranking does not always translate to monetisation or real-world impact. But for brands scouting talent, the list serves as a starting point.

Favikon has not clarified if the ranking will be updated annually. The company has not responded to requests for comment on the selection criteria.

The full list is available on Favikon's website. For creators in Dehradun, the ranking is both validation and motivation. The city's digital footprint is only expected to grow as more young residents turn content creation into a career.
