Poco X8 vanilla variant confirmed by HyperOS source code leak

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • HyperOS source code references a standard Poco X8 smartphone, alongside the previously rumoured X8 Pro.
  • The discovery comes from code analysis, though official specs or launch timeline are not yet confirmed.
  • Poco's strategy may now include a more affordable vanilla model to compete in the mid-range segment.

Poco fans who were worried the brand might skip a standard model for its next X-series phone can breathe easy. References to a vanilla Poco X8 have been spotted in HyperOS source code, indicating the device is very much in development.

The finding comes from code analysis shared by tipsters, revealing a model number that aligns with the Poco X8 lineup. This ends weeks of speculation that Poco might only launch an X8 Pro variant, leaving a gap in its mid-range portfolio.

What the source code reveals

The HyperOS source code—Xiaomi's custom Android skin—contains strings that point to a device codenamed internally as the Poco X8. This is separate from the X8 Pro, which had already been rumoured with higher-end specs.

Tipsters dug through the code and found references to display parameters, camera configurations, and chipset identifiers. While the exact specifications are not fully detailed, the presence of the vanilla model suggests Poco is planning a two-pronged launch.

Poco has not officially commented on the leak. The company typically refrains from confirming such details until closer to a launch event.

What to expect from the Poco X8

Based on previous generations, the vanilla Poco X8 is expected to be a more affordable alternative to the Pro model. The X7 series, for instance, offered a 6.67-inch AMOLED display and a MediaTek Dimensity chipset at a competitive price point.

For the X8, early speculation points to a similar mid-range focus—likely a Snapdragon or MediaTek processor, a 120Hz display, and a capable camera system. Battery capacity could be in the 5,000mAh range, with fast charging support.

The Pro variant, meanwhile, may pack a more powerful chipset, better cameras, and possibly a higher refresh rate display. Both models would run HyperOS out of the box, aligning with Xiaomi's push for a unified software experience.

Timeline and market context

No official launch date has been set for the Poco X8 series. However, given that the X7 launched in early 2025, a late 2026 or early 2027 release seems plausible.

The Indian market, where Poco has a strong following, will likely be a key launch region. The brand has traditionally targeted budget-conscious buyers with aggressive pricing, and the vanilla X8 could continue that trend.

Competition in the mid-range segment remains intense. Rivals like Realme, iQOO, and Samsung are all updating their offerings, making pricing and feature differentiation critical for Poco.

For now, buyers can expect more leaks and rumours as the launch window approaches. Poco's official confirmation will be the next major milestone to watch.
