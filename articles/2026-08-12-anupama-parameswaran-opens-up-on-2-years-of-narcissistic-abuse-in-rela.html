Anupama Parameswaran opens up on 2 years of narcissistic abuse in relationship

Actor Anupama Parameswaran has opened up about a painful chapter in her personal life, revealing that she endured narcissistic abuse for two years in a past relationship. In a recent interview, the Malayalam and Telugu star said her former partner wanted her to give up acting, a demand that left her feeling diminished.

“I was shrinking,” she said, describing the slow erosion of her confidence during that period. Her comments have sparked widespread conversation online, with many fans drawing connections to actor Dhruv Vikram, though she has not named anyone directly.

What Anupama Said

During the candid conversation, Parameswaran recounted how the relationship turned controlling. She said her ex-partner was not supportive of her career and increasingly tried to isolate her from her professional life.

“Imagine having Anniyan in your life,” she reportedly said, referencing the 2005 Tamil film about a man with multiple personalities. The analogy resonated with her audience, who saw it as a stark description of the emotional whiplash she experienced.

The actor did not specify when the relationship occurred or who the person was. She chose to keep the identity private, focusing instead on the broader message about recognising toxic dynamics.

Fan Reactions and Speculation

Within hours of the interview going viral, social media was abuzz with speculation. Many users pointed to Dhruv Vikram, son of actor Vikram, who was linked to Parameswaran in earlier reports. The two were rumoured to be dating around 2022, though neither confirmed it publicly.

Neither Parameswaran nor Vikram has addressed the speculation directly. The actor’s team has also not issued a statement clarifying the identity of the person in question.

Some fans have praised her for speaking out, saying it takes courage to discuss such experiences in an industry where personal lives are often scrutinised. Others have cautioned against jumping to conclusions without confirmation.

The Context of Narcissistic Abuse

Narcissistic abuse is a term used to describe a pattern of manipulation, control, and emotional degradation by someone with narcissistic traits. It often involves gaslighting, where the victim begins to doubt their own perceptions and worth.

Parameswaran’s description of “shrinking” fits a common experience among survivors, who frequently report feeling smaller, quieter, and less certain of themselves over time. She joins a growing list of Indian celebrities who have spoken about toxic relationships, helping to normalise conversations around mental health and emotional well-being.

The actor has not announced any legal action or further details about the relationship. She appears to have moved on, focusing on her career, which includes several upcoming projects in Telugu and Malayalam cinema.
