Rohit Sharma's automatic selection ends after Lord's ODI, BCCI sources confirm

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Rohit Sharma's automatic selection in ODIs has ended after the Lord's match against England
  • Reports suggest the BCCI and selectors have communicated a transition plan to the captain
  • Gautam Gambhir, the head coach, has been informed of the decision to look ahead

The era of Rohit Sharma as an automatic starter in India's ODI side is over. Multiple reports from The Times of India, The Indian Express, and NDTV Sports suggest that the Lord's ODI against England on July 17, 2026, could be the 39-year-old's final appearance in the format for India.

The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) and the national selection committee have conveyed to the captain that his place in the XI will no longer be guaranteed. Head coach Gautam Gambhir has also been informed of the decision.

Selection committee signals fresh start

According to sources, the selectors have decided to accelerate the transition in the 50-over format ahead of the next ODI World Cup cycle. Rohit, who led India to the 2023 World Cup final and the 2025 Champions Trophy title, has been India's most prolific white-ball batsman for over a decade.

But his form in ODIs has been inconsistent of late. The team management is now prioritising younger batsmen who can build towards the 2027 World Cup. The Lord's ODI is being viewed internally as a natural breakpoint.

Gautam Gambhir's role in the decision

Head coach Gautam Gambhir, who took over after the 2024 T20 World Cup, has been part of the discussions. Reports indicate he was briefed about the roadmap in a meeting with selectors and BCCI brass.

Gambhir has publicly backed Rohit in press conferences but has also emphasised the need to build depth. The coach's own relationship with the senior pro — both are from Mumbai and have played together for India — is believed to be professional but pragmatic.

What this means for India's ODI squad

With Rohit's automatic status removed, the door opens wider for Shubman Gill, Yashasvi Jaiswal, and Ruturaj Gaikwad to stake claims at the top of the order. KL Rahul remains a middle-order option, while Ishan Kishan could be recalled as a wicketkeeper-opener.

The selectors are also monitoring performances in domestic white-ball tournaments and the IPL. The message is clear: no player, regardless of stature, is beyond the churn of renewal.

Rohit's leadership remains a separate question. He continues as India's ODI and Test captain for now. But his playing future in the 50-over format is now firmly in the hands of the selection panel, match by match.

What happens next

The BCCI is expected to announce the squad for the next ODI series — against Australia in September — within two weeks. That squad will reveal whether the Lord's ODI was indeed Rohit's last or if he gets a reprieve. All signs point to a changing of the guard.
