Pearle Maaney loses 3 lakh Instagram followers after post on student protests triggers backlash

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pearle Maaney lost over 3 lakh Instagram followers after posting about student protests.
  • She faced backlash for allegedly disabling comments on the post.
  • The controversy also drew in filmmaker Sibi Malayil and influencer Mareena Michael Kurisingal.

Backlash over student protest post

Popular influencer and actress Pearle Maaney has lost over three lakh followers on Instagram after a post she made about ongoing student protests. The post, which stated 'I don't believe in violence,' did not sit well with a section of her audience. Many accused her of taking a vague stance on a sensitive issue.

Critics were quick to point out that she had disabled comments on the post. This move, according to several users, showed an unwillingness to engage in dialogue. The hashtag #PearleMaaney began trending on social media platforms soon after.

Industry figures weigh in

The controversy expanded beyond just one post. Filmmaker Sibi Malayil became a talking point after the internet rallied behind him. This followed Pearle Maaney's earlier remark calling him a 'failed filmmaker.' The comment, made in a different context, resurfaced as users scrutinised her past statements.

Influencer Mareena Michael Kurisingal also entered the fray. She thanked supporters after a previous interaction with Pearle Maaney was brought up. Pearle had reportedly told Mareena, 'I didn't even know you existed.' The remark, now circulating widely, added to the growing criticism.

The cost of a public stance

Losing over three lakh followers is a significant blow for any digital creator. For Pearle Maaney, who has built a sizable online presence over the years, the drop shows how quickly audience sentiment can shift. The incident underscores the risks influencers take when they comment on politically charged issues.

Neither Pearle Maaney nor her team have issued a follow-up statement. The post in question remains live, but with comments still disabled. Followers and critics alike are watching to see if she will address the backlash directly.

What comes next

With the student protests continuing to dominate headlines, the pressure on public figures to pick a side is unlikely to ease. Whether Pearle Maaney attempts damage control or lets the controversy fade remains to be seen. For now, her follower count and the online chatter tell their own story.
