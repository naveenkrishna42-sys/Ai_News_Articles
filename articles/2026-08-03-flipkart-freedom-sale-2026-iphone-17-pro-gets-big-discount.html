Flipkart Freedom Sale 2026: iPhone 17 Pro Gets Big Discount

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Flipkart Freedom Sale features significant discounts on iPhone 17 Pro
  • Amazon's Great Freedom Sale also runs this weekend with competing offers
  • Motorola Edge 70 Max and Edge 70 Pro prices revealed ahead of the sale

Flipkart has announced its Freedom Sale, bringing steep price cuts on premium smartphones, headlined by the iPhone 17 Pro. The e-commerce platform is looking to lure shoppers with significant discounts as the Independence Day shopping season kicks off.

The development comes just as Amazon gears up for its own Great Freedom Sale this weekend, setting the stage for a head-to-head battle between India's two largest online marketplaces.

iPhone 17 Pro Leads the Discounts

Flipkart's sale page highlights the iPhone 17 Pro as the marquee offer, with discounts that could shave a substantial amount off the premium handset's price. While the exact final pricing varies by variant and bank offers, the platform is marketing this as one of the deepest cuts on Apple's latest Pro model since its launch.

Shoppers can expect additional savings through exchange bonuses, card offers, and no-cost EMI schemes, which are standard fixtures during such festive sales. The move signals Flipkart's intent to corner the premium smartphone segment, a category where Apple has seen strong demand in India.

Competing Sales Heat Up the Weekend

Amazon is countering with its own Great Freedom Sale, which begins this weekend. The company has already teased deals on flagship devices, including Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra, the Galaxy S25 Ultra, and the OnePlus 15. Both platforms are vying for the same wallet, making this one of the most competitive sale windows of the year.

For consumers, the overlap means better bargains. But it also demands patience—prices on identical models can vary between the two platforms, and stock for high-demand items like the iPhone 17 Pro tends to run out quickly.

Motorola Enters the Fray

Motorola has also thrown its hat into the ring ahead of the Flipkart sale. The Moto Edge 70 Max is teased at Rs 49,999, while the Edge 70 Pro is expected to sell for Rs 36,999. These price points position the devices squarely against mid-range offerings from OnePlus and Samsung, giving shoppers more options beyond the flagship tier.

Motorola's strategy appears to be aggressive pricing combined with flagship-grade features, a formula that has worked for the brand in recent sale events. The discounts on these models are likely to be a key draw for budget-conscious buyers looking for premium specs.

What to Expect During the Sale

The sale period is expected to include daily flash deals, limited-time offers, and category-wide discounts across electronics, fashion, and home appliances. However, the biggest buzz remains around smartphones, where both Amazon and Flipkart are pulling out all stops.

Industry watchers note that such sales have become critical for brands to clear inventory and hit quarterly targets. For Flipkart, the Freedom Sale is also a test of its logistics and supply chain ahead of the much larger festive season sales in October.

Shoppers should keep an eye on bank-specific offers and early-bird discounts, which often deliver the steepest cuts. Those eyeing the iPhone 17 Pro would do well to compare prices across both platforms before hitting the checkout button.

As the weekend approaches, the battle lines are drawn. Amazon and Flipkart will both publish their full price lists shortly, and the first few hours of the sales typically see the best deals vanish. With Motorola adding mid-range firepower, this could be one of the most intense sale weekends of the year.

All eyes are now on the official sale start times and the final price tags, which will determine which platform wins the lion's share of festive spending.
