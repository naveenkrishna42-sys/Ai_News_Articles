iPhone 15 Price Outlook for Flipkart Big Billion Days 2026

The upcoming Flipkart Big Billion Days sale is rumoured to push the iPhone 15’s effective price down to ₹61,599 when combined with exchange bonuses and select bank discounts. This would represent a saving of roughly ₹18,000 off the device’s current market retail price (MRP) of about ₹79,900 for the 128 GB variant. Such a drop brings the iPhone 15 closer to the price band of many mid‑range Android flagships, making it a compelling option for users seeking Apple’s ecosystem without paying premium launch prices.

Base Price and Expected Discount

Apple’s official India pricing for the iPhone 15 series starts at ₹79,900 for the 128 GB model, ₹89,900 for 256 GB, and ₹1,09,900 for 512 GB. The rumoured sale price of ₹61,599 applies specifically to the base 128 GB configuration after factoring in Flipkart’s exchange offer (up to ₹15,000 depending on the old device) and an additional bank instant discount of around ₹3,000–₹4,000 from partner cards such as HDFC, SBI, or ICICI. The exact final amount will vary based on the condition and model of the phone being exchanged.

Exchange and Bank Offer Mechanics

Flipkart’s exchange programme evaluates the old device based on model, year, and physical condition, offering a credit that is applied directly at checkout. For a typical two‑year‑old Android flagship, the exchange value can reach ₹12,000–₹15,000, while older iPhones may fetch slightly less. Bank offers are usually instant discounts credited to the buyer’s account after the transaction, contingent on using a partnered credit or debit card for payment. Combining both can yield the headline figure, but buyers must ensure the exchange device meets Flipkart’s eligibility criteria to avoid last‑minute adjustments.

Real‑World Value Assessment

At an effective price of ₹61,599, the iPhone 15 competes directly with devices such as the Samsung Galaxy S23 FE, OnePlus 12, and Google Pixel 8a, all of which offer similar or superior hardware specifications at comparable costs. The iPhone’s advantages lie in its long‑term software support (up to six years of iOS updates), seamless integration with other Apple products, and a strong resale value. However, the absence of a high‑refresh‑rate display and the reliance on a single‑day battery life may be drawbacks for power users who prioritize gaming or extensive multimedia consumption.
