iQOO Neo 11 Ultra Specs Leak Ahead of August 18 Launch

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iQOO Neo 11 Ultra launch confirmed for August 18 in China.
  • Leaked specs include a 2K display and up to 10,000mAh battery.
  • iQOO Z11 series also expected to debut in India on August 20.

Just days before the official unveiling, the complete specification sheet of the iQOO Neo 11 Ultra has surfaced online, giving smartphone enthusiasts a detailed preview of what to expect. The device is slated to launch in China on August 18, and the leak covers everything from display quality to battery capacity.

Display and Design: A 2K Affair

According to the leaked details, the iQOO Neo 11 Ultra will sport a 2K resolution display, a feature that iQOO has already hinted at in its official teasers. The panel is expected to be a flat AMOLED screen with a high refresh rate, likely 144Hz, catering to gamers and media consumers alike.

The design language appears to follow iQOO's recent trend of combining performance with a sleek aesthetic. While the leak does not specify exact dimensions, the device is expected to feature a punch-hole camera setup and a glass-and-metal build, maintaining the premium feel of the Neo series.

Battery and Charging: The 10,000mAh Question

One of the most striking revelations is the battery capacity. Reports from 91mobiles suggest that the Neo 11 Ultra, along with the Z11S, could pack a massive 10,000mAh battery. If true, this would be a significant leap over current flagship standards, potentially offering two-day endurance under heavy use.

Charging speeds are also expected to be aggressive, with support for 120W or higher wired charging. This combination of a large cell and fast top-up could make the Neo 11 Ultra a standout choice for power users who are tired of carrying power banks.

Performance and Cameras

Under the hood, the iQOO Neo 11 Ultra is likely to be powered by the latest Snapdragon flagship chipset, possibly the Snapdragon 8 Gen 4. The leak indicates configurations with up to 16GB of RAM and 512GB of storage, ensuring smooth multitasking and ample space for apps and media.

On the imaging front, the device is expected to feature a triple-camera setup, headlined by a 50-megapixel primary sensor. While details on the secondary sensors remain scarce, iQOO has historically included a wide-angle and a macro or depth lens, so a similar arrangement is plausible.

Software and Additional Features

The Neo 11 Ultra will likely run on Android 16 with iQOO's custom FunTouch OS skin on top. Features such as an in-display fingerprint sensor, stereo speakers, and an X-axis linear motor for haptic feedback are expected to be part of the package, aligning with the brand's gaming-focused positioning.

Connectivity options should include 5G SA/NSA, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4, and an infrared blaster – a staple in many Chinese smartphones. The leak does not detail IP rating, so water resistance remains unconfirmed at this stage.

What About India?

While the August 18 launch is for the Chinese market, iQOO fans in India have their own reason to wait. The iQOO Z11 has been confirmed to debut in India on August 20, according to GSMArena. The Z11 series is expected to cater to the mid-range segment, offering a balanced mix of performance and affordability.

It remains unclear if the Neo 11 Ultra will make its way to India soon after, but given the brand's track record of bringing Neo models to the country, an announcement could follow in the coming weeks.

With the official launch just days away, iQOO is expected to reveal more details through its social media channels. The August 18 event will confirm whether the leaked specifications hold true, especially the ambitious battery figure that could redefine expectations for flagship smartphones.
