XRP lags as bitcoin leads crypto market rebound

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin's market bounce has not lifted XRP proportionally.
  • XRP's underperformance highlights shifting trader focus toward BTC.
  • Analysts are watching for catalysts that could revive XRP's price action.

Bitcoin's latest market bounce has once again widened the gap with XRP, leaving the third-largest cryptocurrency by market capitalisation trailing behind. While BTC has reclaimed key price levels and drawn renewed buying interest, XRP has struggled to keep pace, prompting traders to question whether the altcoin is losing its appeal in the current cycle.

The divergence became apparent over the past week as bitcoin surged ahead on strong spot demand and institutional inflows, while XRP remained subdued, moving sideways even as broader market sentiment turned positive. This pattern is not new; XRP has often lagged during bitcoin-led rallies, but the persistence of this trend is now drawing attention.

Bitcoin's dominance grows

Bitcoin's market dominance has climbed to its highest point in months, reflecting a flight to safety among crypto investors. The leading cryptocurrency has benefited from a mix of macroeconomic tailwinds, including easing inflation fears and growing expectations of a softer monetary policy stance from central banks.

In contrast, XRP has faced its own set of challenges. The ongoing legal tussle between Ripple Labs and the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) continues to cast a shadow over the token, even as the case has moved into a quieter phase. Regulatory uncertainty has made some institutional investors wary of building large positions in XRP, preferring the relative clarity that bitcoin offers.

Traders rotate into BTC

Market participants point to a clear rotation of capital into bitcoin at the expense of altcoins like XRP. Liquidity pools that once supported speculative bets on smaller tokens are now being redirected towards BTC, which is seen as a safer bet in an environment where risk appetite remains fragile.

Derivatives data also shows a shift in positioning. Open interest in XRP futures has remained flat, while bitcoin futures have seen a steady increase in volume and open interest, indicating that leveraged traders are doubling down on the top coin rather than spreading their bets across the broader market.

Is XRP being left behind permanently?

The question now is whether XRP's underperformance is a temporary blip or a sign of a deeper structural shift. Some analysts argue that XRP's utility in cross-border payments has not translated into sustained price momentum, especially when compared with newer platforms that offer faster and cheaper transactions.

Others believe that XRP could still catch up if the SEC case resolves favourably or if Ripple announces new partnerships that boost real-world adoption. However, without a clear catalyst, the token may continue to languish while bitcoin commands the spotlight.

What to watch next

For now, the market's focus remains firmly on bitcoin, with traders tracking its ability to hold recent gains. XRP's next move will likely depend on external factors—be it legal developments, regulatory clarity, or a shift in risk appetite that revives interest in altcoins.

As the week unfolds, all eyes will be on whether XRP can break out of its consolidative range or if it will continue to play second fiddle to bitcoin. The coming sessions could offer clues, but for the moment, XRP appears content to wait on the sidelines.
