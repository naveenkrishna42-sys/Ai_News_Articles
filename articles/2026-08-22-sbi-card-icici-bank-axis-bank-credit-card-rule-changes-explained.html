Introduction

SBI Card, ICICI Bank, and Axis Bank have recently announced changes to their credit card rules. These changes include new annual fees, adjusted reward rates, and updated cashback policies. Understanding these changes is crucial for existing cardholders and potential new applicants.

SBI Card Changes

Annual Fee Structure

SBI Card has introduced a new annual fee structure for its premium cards. The new fees are as follows:

  • SBI Card Premier: ₹5,000 annual fee
  • SBI Card Signature: ₹10,000 annual fee
  • SBI Card Platinum: ₹15,000 annual fee

The annual fees are waived for the first year for new cardholders.

Reward Structure

SBI Card has also adjusted its reward structure. The new reward rates are:

  • 5% cashback on fuel purchases
  • 3% cashback on groceries
  • 2% cashback on all other purchases

These rewards are valid for the first year and can be redeemed in the form of SBI Card points.

ICICI Bank Changes

Reward Rates

ICICI Bank has adjusted its reward rates. The new reward rates are:

  • 5% cashback on flights booked through ICICI Bank travel portals
  • 3% cashback on hotel bookings made through ICICI Bank
  • 2% cashback on all other purchases

The minimum spend required to avail these rewards has also been increased to ₹50,000 per quarter.

Foreign Transaction Fee

ICICI Bank has introduced a new foreign transaction fee of 3% for transactions made outside India. This fee is applicable on the amount converted to Indian Rupees.

Axis Bank Changes

Cashback Policy

Axis Bank has updated its cashback policy. The new cashback rates are:

  • 5% cashback on dining and entertainment
  • 3% cashback on fuel purchases
  • 2% cashback on all other purchases

The minimum spend required to avail these cashback benefits has been increased to ₹10,000 per quarter.

Welcome Offer

Axis Bank has also introduced a new welcome offer for new cardholders. The welcome offer includes:

  • 10% cashback on the first ₹5,000 spent within the first 30 days
  • 5% cashback on the next ₹10,000 spent within the first 60 days

How to Apply

To apply for any of these credit cards, visit the respective bank's website or contact their customer service. The application process is online and involves filling out an application form, submitting necessary documents, and undergoing a credit check. Once approved, the card will be delivered to your address.

Final Recommendation

These changes are beneficial for existing cardholders who can take advantage of the new reward structures and cashback policies. New applicants should consider the new annual fees and minimum spend requirements before applying. This guide should help you make an informed decision about your credit card.
