Shaik Rasheed, Sarfaraz Khan in race to replace injured Sai Sudharsan

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sai Sudharsan ruled out of Sri Lanka Test series due to injury
  • Shaik Rasheed and Sarfaraz Khan are leading contenders for replacement
  • Team India management yet to announce official replacement

The Indian cricket team's Test squad for the ongoing tour of Sri Lanka has been thrown into flux after young batter Sai Sudharsan was ruled out of the series due to injury. The left-hander, who was expected to play a key role in the middle order, will miss the remainder of the tour, dealing a blow to the visitors' plans.

According to multiple reports, the team management is now weighing two names as potential replacements: Shaik Rasheed and Sarfaraz Khan. Both players have been in impressive form in domestic cricket, and the selectors are believed to be zeroing in on one of them to fill the void left by Sudharsan.

Injury blow for India

Sudharsan's injury has been confirmed as a significant setback for India, with reports suggesting he has been ruled out of the Galle Test and is doubtful for the Colombo match as well. The exact nature of the injury has not been officially disclosed, but sources indicate it is serious enough to end his participation in the series.

The 24-year-old had been a regular feature in India's recent Test squads, and his absence leaves a gap in the batting lineup. The team had already been grappling with fitness concerns, and this latest development adds to the pressure on the management to find a suitable replacement quickly.

The contenders

Shaik Rasheed, a stylish right-handed batter from Andhra Pradesh, has been on the selectors' radar for some time. He has consistently scored runs in domestic competitions, including the Ranji Trophy, and is seen as a future star. His ability to play both spin and pace with equal ease makes him a strong candidate for subcontinent conditions.

Sarfaraz Khan, on the other hand, has been a domestic heavyweight for years, piling up runs at a staggering average in first-class cricket. Despite repeated calls for his inclusion in the national team, he has had limited opportunities at the international level. His recent performances in the Irani Cup and other tournaments have once again brought him into the spotlight.

Both players offer different strengths. Rasheed is younger and more versatile, while Sarfaraz brings experience and a proven record of converting starts into big scores. The team management is expected to make a decision in the coming days, keeping in mind the conditions in Sri Lanka and the balance of the squad.

What lies ahead

The selectors have not yet made an official announcement, and it remains unclear whether the replacement will be flown in immediately or later in the series. The team is currently preparing for the Galle Test, and the new addition is likely to join the squad ahead of the Colombo game.

With the series already underway, the choice of replacement will be crucial. India will want a player who can adapt quickly and contribute immediately, given the high stakes of the tour. The management's decision will also reflect their long-term vision, as both Rasheed and Sarfaraz are seen as potential mainstays in the Test side.

As the cricketing fraternity waits for clarity, the focus remains on the upcoming matches. The team will hope that whoever comes in can make an impact and help India secure a series win in Sri Lanka.
