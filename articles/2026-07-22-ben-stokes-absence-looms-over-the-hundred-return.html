Ben Stokes' Absence Looms Over The Hundred Return

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ben Stokes is not playing in The Hundred, choosing to play for Durham in the One-Day Cup instead.
  • Stokes scored a match-winning century for Durham, highlighting his form and commitment to county cricket.
  • His absence is seen as a significant blow to The Hundred's profile and marketing efforts.
  • The tournament returns amidst ongoing debates about its impact on traditional cricket formats.

A Champion's Choice

Ben Stokes is not at The Hundred. Days after announcing his retirement from one-day internationals, the former England captain walked out for Durham in the One-Day Cup and smashed a match-winning century. The timing could not be more pointed.

Stokes' decision to skip the ECB's flagship franchise tournament for a 50-over county fixture has sent a clear message. While The Hundred returns for its fifth edition, its most bankable star is playing elsewhere.

Durham Heroics Overshadow Launch

The left-hander's knock for Durham was vintage Stokes — aggressive, unorthodox and decisive. He paced his innings to anchor a chase, finishing unbeaten as Durham won comfortably. The scorecard will show a century. The subtext is more complex.

For the ECB, which has invested heavily in The Hundred as a vehicle to grow the game, Stokes' absence is a public relations problem. Promotional material featuring the all-rounder now feels dated. Broadcasters who built marketing campaigns around his name must adjust on the fly.

Why Stokes Skipped The Hundred

Stokes retired from ODI cricket last week, citing the physical toll of playing across formats. He remains available for Test matches and has not ruled out T20 cricket entirely. But his choice to play for Durham in the One-Day Cup — a competition The Hundred was partly designed to marginalise — suggests a deliberate stance.

There is no official word from Stokes or his management on why he is not playing in The Hundred. Sources close to the player indicate he values the traditional county game and wants to give back to Durham, the club that developed him. The ECB has not commented on his absence beyond noting that player availability is a matter of individual choice.

What This Means for The Hundred

The tournament begins its 2026 season with eight city-based franchises and a schedule packed with double-headers. Ticket sales are reported to be strong for the opening weekend. But the absence of England's most recognisable cricketer is a void that marketing cannot easily fill.

Other England stars, including Jos Buttler and Jofra Archer, are expected to feature. The ECB will hope their presence keeps the focus on the cricket. But Stokes' decision to play for Durham has reignited the debate about whether The Hundred is genuinely popular or simply mandated from above.

Looking Ahead

The Hundred runs through August. Stokes will continue playing for Durham in the One-Day Cup and is expected to be available for England's Test series in the autumn. His absence from the tournament will be felt every time a highlights package shows an empty space where he should be. The ECB will watch the viewing figures closely.
