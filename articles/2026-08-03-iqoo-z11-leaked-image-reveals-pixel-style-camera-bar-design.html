iQOO Z11 leaked image reveals Pixel-style camera bar design

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Leaked marketing image shows iQOO Z11 with a Pixel-inspired horizontal camera bar
  • The design marks a departure from iQOO's typical vertical camera module
  • iQOO CEO's teaser adds to speculation amid iQOO 16 cancellation rumours

A leaked marketing image of the upcoming iQOO Z11 has surfaced online, revealing a design that borrows heavily from Google's Pixel aesthetic. The render, shared by tipsters, shows a horizontal camera bar stretching across the back panel — a clear shift from the vertical camera modules that have become iQOO's signature.

The image, which appears to be official marketing material, suggests the Z11 will feature a 3D curved display and a redesigned rear panel. This is the first major visual leak for the device, which is expected to launch in India in the coming months.

Pixel-inspired camera bar

The most striking element in the leaked render is the camera housing. Instead of a square or rectangular bump, the Z11 sports a full-width horizontal strip, similar to the Pixel 9 series. The bar houses multiple camera sensors and an LED flash, arranged symmetrically for a clean, modern look.

This design choice is unusual for iQOO, which has traditionally favoured aggressive, gaming-oriented aesthetics. The shift towards a cleaner, more mainstream look could signal a change in strategy for the brand's Z-series, which has historically targeted budget and mid-range buyers.

Colour options and display

The leak also hints at at least two colour variants — a glossy black and a gradient blue. The 3D curved display, previously teased by iQOO executives, is expected to offer a high refresh rate, though exact specifications remain under wraps.

Industry insiders suggest the Z11 will pack a MediaTek Dimensity chip, likely the Dimensity 8300 or a similar mid-range processor. Battery capacity and charging speeds are still unconfirmed, but the Z-series has consistently offered large batteries with fast charging.

CEO teaser and iQOO 16 rumours

The leak comes days after iQOO CEO Nipun Marya teased the Z11 on social media, posting a cryptic image of a smartphone silhouette. That post fuelled speculation, especially as rumours swirl about the cancellation of the iQOO 16 flagship series.

Reports from TelecomTalk and Gadgets 360 suggest iQOO may be streamlining its portfolio, focusing on the Z-series and other mid-range devices. The cancellation of the iQOO 16, if true, would be a significant shift for the brand, which has competed aggressively in the premium segment with devices like the iQOO 12.

Neither iQOO nor its parent company Vivo has officially commented on the iQOO 16 rumours. The company has, however, confirmed that the Z11 will launch in India soon, with more details expected in the coming weeks.

What to expect at launch

While the leaked image gives us a clear look at the design, many questions remain. The exact camera specifications, battery size, and pricing are still unknown. Given the Z-series history, the Z11 is likely to be priced competitively, possibly under Rs 25,000, to take on rivals like the Redmi Note series and Realme numbers.

The Indian smartphone market has seen a surge in mid-range devices with premium features, and the Z11 seems poised to join that fray. With the Pixel-inspired design, iQOO is clearly aiming to stand out in a crowded segment.

As the launch date approaches, more leaks and official teasers are expected. iQOO has scheduled a reveal event for later this month, where the company is expected to confirm the Z11's specifications and India availability.
