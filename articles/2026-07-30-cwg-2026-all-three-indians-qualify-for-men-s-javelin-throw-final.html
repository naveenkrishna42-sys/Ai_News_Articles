CWG 2026: All three Indians qualify for men's javelin throw final

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Neeraj Chopra, Rohit Yadav and Yash Vir Singh all qualified for the javelin final at CWG 2026.
  • The event sets up a potential showdown between Chopra and Pakistan's Arshad Nadeem.
  • The final is expected to be held on the last day of athletics competition in Glasgow.

Three Indians in the final

India's javelin throwers made a clean sweep in the qualifying rounds at the Commonwealth Games in Glasgow. Neeraj Chopra, Rohit Yadav and Yash Vir Singh all secured their spots in the men's javelin throw final on Thursday.

The trio will now compete for medals in what is shaping up to be one of the most anticipated events of the Games. Chopra, the Olympic and world champion, led the Indian charge with a strong throw that comfortably met the automatic qualification mark.

Yadav and Singh also delivered consistent performances, ensuring that India will have three representatives in the final. That gives the country a significant advantage in team strategy and medal prospects.

Chopra vs Nadeem reloaded

All eyes are now on the potential rematch between Chopra and Pakistan's Arshad Nadeem. The two have built one of athletics' great rivalries over the past few years.

Nadeem, who also qualified for the final, pushed Chopra to the limit at the Tokyo Olympics and the World Championships. Their clashes have become box-office events for track and field fans across the subcontinent.

Both athletes have been in fine form this season. Chopra has consistently thrown beyond 88 metres, while Nadeem has shown he can match that distance on his day.

What to expect in the final

The men's javelin final is likely to be held on the last day of athletics competition. That timing often brings extra pressure and drama.

India's three-athlete presence could force other competitors to take risks early. Chopra's experience in big finals gives him an edge, but Yadav and Singh have proven they can step up under pressure.

The weather in Glasgow has been unpredictable, with wind and rain affecting some events. Officials will be monitoring conditions closely ahead of the final.

For India, a clean sweep of medals is not out of the question. But Nadeem and a strong field of Commonwealth throwers will have something to say about that.

All three Indians will now rest and prepare. The final is expected to draw a massive television audience back home, with millions tuning in to see if Chopra can add another gold to his collection.
