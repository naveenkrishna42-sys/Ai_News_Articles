Lupita Nyong'o Wears Sabyasachi Jewellery at The Odyssey Premiere

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Lupita Nyong'o wore Sabyasachi high jewellery at The Odyssey red carpet premiere.
  • The choice highlighted Indian craftsmanship on a global Hollywood platform.
  • Nyong'o has faced casting backlash for her role in the film, which co-star Jon Bernthal has publicly defended.
  • The look was widely celebrated as a tribute to Indian heritage.

A Hollywood Moment for Indian Craftsmanship

Lupita Nyong'o turned heads at the London premiere of The Odyssey with a look that celebrated Indian heritage. The Oscar-winning actress wore a custom Sabyasachi high jewellery set, bringing Indian craftsmanship to one of the year's biggest red carpets.

The choice was deliberate. Nyong'o's stylist worked with the Kolkata-based designer to create a look that fused modern Hollywood glamour with traditional Indian artistry. The jewellery featured uncut diamonds and intricate gold work, hallmarks of Sabyasachi's aesthetic.

More Than a Fashion Statement

Nyong'o's appearance comes amid a wider conversation about representation in Hollywood. The actress has faced online backlash over her casting in The Odyssey, with some questioning a Black actress playing a role in Greek mythology.

Her co-star Jon Bernthal has publicly defended her. "Lupita is one of the most talented actors of her generation," Bernthal told Yahoo Entertainment UK. "The idea that she has to defend her place in this film is absurd. Art is about imagination, not exclusion."

An Ongoing Debate on Casting

The backlash against Nyong'o is not new. Black actors in fantasy and historical films have often faced similar scrutiny. Slate Magazine recently argued that asking what mythological figures "really looked like" is the wrong question. The Root published a piece defending Black actors' right to exist in fantasy spaces without having to justify their casting.

Nyong'o has not directly addressed the criticism. Instead, she has let her work speak. Her performance in The Odyssey has drawn early praise from critics, with many noting her commanding presence in the epic film.

A Red Carpet With Purpose

The premiere itself was a spectacle. Nyong'o walked the carpet in a flowing gown that complemented the Sabyasachi jewellery. Indian fashion enthusiasts quickly took to social media, celebrating the moment as a win for desi design on a global stage.

Sabyasachi Mukherjee, the designer, has long been a favourite among Bollywood stars and global celebrities. His work often incorporates traditional Indian techniques with contemporary silhouettes. Nyong'o's choice signals a growing appetite for non-Western fashion in Hollywood.

For now, the conversation around The Odyssey remains divided between its artistic merits and the politics of its casting. What is clear is that Nyong'o's red carpet moment has given Indian heritage a spotlight that will not be forgotten soon.

What to watch: Whether Nyong'o addresses the casting backlash directly in future interviews, and how the film performs with global audiences when it releases widely this weekend.
