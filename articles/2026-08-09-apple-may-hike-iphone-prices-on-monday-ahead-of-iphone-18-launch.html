Apple may hike iPhone prices on Monday ahead of iPhone 18 launch

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • A new tip hints Apple may increase iPhone prices on Monday, ahead of the September launch.
  • The iPhone 18 Pro is expected to launch in September, with demand driving the schedule.
  • Reports suggest the iPhone 18 Pro Max could feature A20 Pro chip and camera upgrades.

Apple could be preparing to raise iPhone prices as early as Monday, according to a fresh tip that has set the tech world abuzz. The move, if confirmed, would come just weeks before the expected September launch of the iPhone 18 Pro series.

The tip, reported by Firstpost, suggests a price hike is imminent, though Apple has not officially confirmed any such decision. The timing is notable, as it would precede the company's annual flagship event, where the iPhone 18 Pro is widely expected to make its debut.

Why a price hike now?

Industry watchers point to a combination of factors that could justify an increase. Demand for the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro is reportedly strong, with sources indicating that Apple is banking on a robust upgrade cycle. This demand, coupled with rising component costs and supply chain pressures, may be pushing the Cupertino giant to adjust its pricing strategy.

Reports from Deccan Chronicle and TelecomTalk have also highlighted potential hardware upgrades, including a rumoured A20 Pro chip for the iPhone 18 Pro Max and significant camera improvements. Such additions could naturally lead to a higher price tag, though Apple has historically absorbed some costs to maintain market share.

Monday's move: what to watch

If the price hike does materialise on Monday, it would apply to existing models like the iPhone 17 series, making way for the premium positioning of the iPhone 18 lineup. This is a tactic Apple has used before, adjusting prices of older models to create a clear tier gap when new devices arrive.

For Indian consumers, the impact could be significant. With import duties and local taxes already inflating prices, any additional increase would make iPhones even more expensive in one of the world's fastest-growing smartphone markets. Analysts suggest a potential hike of a few thousand rupees could be on the cards, though exact figures remain speculative.

September launch on track

The iPhone 18 Pro is expected to launch in September, with Forbes reporting that demand is driving the release schedule. Apple typically unveils its new iPhones in the first half of September, with availability following a week or two later. This year, the company is reportedly aiming for a seamless rollout to capitalise on festive season sales in key markets like India.

Rumours also point to a redesigned camera system on the Pro Max model, possibly featuring a periscope lens for enhanced optical zoom. The A20 Pro chip is expected to deliver significant performance gains, focusing on on-device AI capabilities, which could be a major selling point.

What this means for buyers

For those planning to upgrade, the advice is to keep a close watch on Monday's announcement. If the hike is confirmed, buying an iPhone 17 before the price change could save money. However, waiting for the iPhone 18 might offer better long-term value, especially if the new features justify the premium.

Apple has not yet commented on the tip, and officials have not confirmed any pricing changes. The company's silence has only fuelled speculation, with tech enthusiasts and analysts alike parsing every signal for clues.

The coming week will be crucial, as any official announcement from Apple will set the tone for the September launch. If the price hike goes through, it could reshape consumer expectations and competitive dynamics in the premium smartphone segment.
