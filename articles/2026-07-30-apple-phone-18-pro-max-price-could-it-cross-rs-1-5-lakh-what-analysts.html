Apple Phone 18 Pro Max Price: Could It Cross Rs 1.5 Lakh? What Analysts Say

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Phone 18 Pro Max expected to start above Rs 1.5 lakh in India.
  • Price hike linked to new A20 chip, larger display, and titanium build.
  • Leaked specs suggest 6.9-inch screen, 48MP main camera, and 256GB base storage.

Price Predictions Point to a New High

Apple's next flagship, the Phone 18 Pro Max, is generating buzz even before its official launch. Tipsters and market analysts now estimate that the device could break the Rs 1.5 lakh barrier in India. That would make it the most expensive iPhone ever sold in the country.

Leaks from supply chain sources suggest the starting price could land between Rs 1.55 lakh and Rs 1.65 lakh. The current generation Phone 15 Pro Max starts at Rs 1.59 lakh in India, so a jump is not unexpected. Analysts at firms like IDC and Counterpoint point to rising component costs as the main driver.

What's Driving the Cost?

The rumored price hike is linked to several hardware upgrades. The Phone 18 Pro Max is expected to debut Apple's next-generation A20 chip, built on a more advanced 3-nanometer process. This alone pushes up manufacturing costs.

Display technology is another factor. Reports indicate the phone will feature a 6.9-inch LTPO OLED panel, slightly larger than the current 6.7-inch screen. The chassis is likely to use a stronger titanium alloy, replacing the stainless steel frame. These material changes add to the bill of materials.

Camera and Battery Upgrades

Camera improvements are also on the cards. The rear camera system is tipped to include a 48-megapixel main sensor with a larger aperture, plus a periscope lens for better optical zoom. The front-facing camera could get autofocus for the first time.

Battery capacity is expected to increase by around 10 percent, according to leaks from Chinese tipsters. Fast charging might finally go beyond 30W, though Apple has not confirmed this. The phone will run iOS 20 out of the box, with new AI-driven features for photography and productivity.

India Market Context

Apple has been aggressively pushing its Pro models in India. The company now assembles the iPhone 15 and 15 Plus locally, but the Pro and Pro Max units are still imported. This exposes them to import duties, which account for a significant chunk of the final price.

Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo has noted that Apple's premium pricing strategy is working in India. The country contributed around 5 percent of Apple's global revenue in 2025, up from 3 percent two years ago. A higher price tag could test how much Indian consumers are willing to spend on a single smartphone.

Rival flagships like the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra and the upcoming Google Pixel 10 Pro are priced well below Rs 1.5 lakh. Apple will need to justify the premium with exclusive features and ecosystem lock-in.

What to Watch Next

Apple is expected to unveil the Phone 18 series in September. The official India pricing will be announced shortly after. Whether the Pro Max crosses the Rs 1.5 lakh mark depends on rupee-dollar exchange rates and any last-minute changes in import duties. For now, the estimates are just that—estimates. The final number will only come from Cupertino.
