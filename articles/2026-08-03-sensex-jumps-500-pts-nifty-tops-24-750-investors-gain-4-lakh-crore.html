Sensex jumps 500 pts, Nifty tops 24,750; investors gain ₹4 lakh crore

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex jumped over 500 points, Nifty 50 closed above 24,750.
  • Investor wealth rose by ₹4 lakh crore in a single day.
  • The rally was driven by broad-based buying across sectors.

Indian equity markets closed sharply higher on Monday, with the Sensex surging over 500 points and the Nifty 50 comfortably settling above the 24,750 mark. The day's rally added a whopping ₹4 lakh crore to investor wealth, marking one of the strongest single-session gains in recent weeks.

The benchmark indices opened on a firm note and maintained momentum through the day, as buying activity spread across banking, IT, and auto stocks. Market participants said the positive close was driven by a mix of domestic and global cues, though specific triggers were not immediately confirmed by analysts.

Broad-based buying lifts indices

The Sensex, which tracks 30 of the largest listed companies, closed with a gain of over 500 points, while the Nifty 50 ended above the psychologically important 24,750 level. The rally was not limited to a few heavyweights—midcap and smallcap stocks also participated, reflecting a broad-based upswing.

Traders pointed to short-covering in index futures as a possible factor, but the sustained buying in cash market segments suggested genuine investor interest. The market breadth was positive, with advancing stocks comfortably outpacing decliners on both the BSE and NSE.

Investor wealth surges by ₹4 lakh crore

The sharp rally translated into a substantial increase in the market capitalisation of BSE-listed companies. In a single day, investor wealth swelled by approximately ₹4 lakh crore, a figure that underscores the scale of the day's gains.

This wealth creation comes after a period of consolidation, where indices had been range-bound. Monday's move signals a decisive shift in sentiment, though analysts cautioned that sustainability will depend on upcoming earnings and global cues.

Sectoral performance and key movers

Almost all sectoral indices ended in the green. Banking and financial services stocks were among the top contributors, with private sector lenders and public sector banks posting strong gains. IT stocks also rallied, helped by a weak dollar and hopes of steady client spending.

Auto stocks extended their recent uptrend, while metal and energy shares provided additional support. The volatility index, India VIX, cooled off during the session, indicating reduced fear among traders.

What to watch next

Market participants will now look to the next set of macroeconomic data and quarterly earnings from major companies to gauge whether the momentum can be sustained. Global factors, including US Federal Reserve policy signals and crude oil prices, will also remain in focus.

Analysts suggest that while the current rally is encouraging, investors should watch for any sharp reversal in foreign fund flows or unexpected domestic headwinds. The immediate resistance for the Nifty is seen near the 24,900–25,000 zone, with support at 24,500.
