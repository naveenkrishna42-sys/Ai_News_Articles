Key stocks to watch in September 2026

📅 2026-08-31 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • ITC's Q2 results and potential dividend impact
  • Happiest Minds' share price volatility and market sentiment
  • HDFC Bank's new loan growth targets and investor confidence
  • ONGC's exploration activities and energy sector outlook
  • Broader market trends affecting these stocks

ITC: A Dividend Dilemma

ITC, India's largest diversified business group, is set to report its Q2 results on September 1. Analysts are closely watching the company's net profit, which is expected to grow by 12% year-on-year to ₹14,000 crore. However, the real focus will be on the dividend payout. ITC has historically been a dividend stock, but the company's board is reportedly considering a special dividend of ₹5 per share. This would be the first such payout in over a decade. The market will be watching to see if this move is seen as a sign of confidence or a desperate attempt to boost shareholder value.

Happiest Minds: Share Price Volatility

Happiest Minds Technologies, a leading IT services company, has been a hot topic in the market. The company's share price has been volatile, with a recent 15% drop. This has raised concerns about the company's future prospects. Happiest Minds has been facing challenges in retaining talent and has been laying off employees. The market will be watching to see if the company can turn things around or if this is the beginning of a downward spiral.

HDFC Bank: Loan Growth Targets

HDFC Bank, India's largest private sector bank, has set ambitious loan growth targets for the fiscal year. The bank aims to grow its loan book by 15% year-on-year. This is a significant target, given the challenging economic environment. The market will be watching to see if the bank can achieve this target. If it does, it would be a positive sign for the banking sector. If it doesn't, it could signal trouble ahead.

ONGC: Exploration Activities

ONGC, India's largest oil and gas company, has been actively exploring new oil and gas fields. The company has recently discovered a significant oil field in the Krishna-Godavari basin. This discovery is expected to boost ONGC's production and revenue. The market will be watching to see if this discovery can offset the company's declining domestic production.

Broader Market Trends

The performance of these stocks is also influenced by broader market trends. The market is currently in a state of flux, with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) expected to cut interest rates in the near future. This could boost the performance of stocks like HDFC Bank, which are sensitive to interest rates. However, it could also lead to a correction in the market, which could impact stocks like Happiest Minds, which are already under pressure.
