Bitcoin Price Bounces Ahead of SpaceX IPO, Market Watchers Eye Rally

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin price rebounds ahead of SpaceX IPO
  • Market analysts attribute bounce to IPO anticipation and risk-on sentiment
  • Trading volumes increase as investors position for potential rally

Bitcoin Recovers Ground as IPO Buzz Grows

Bitcoin's price staged a notable recovery on Thursday, bouncing back from recent lows as excitement builds around SpaceX's initial public offering. The cryptocurrency, which had been trading in a tight range over the past week, saw a sharp uptick in value during early Asian trading hours.

Data from CoinDesk shows Bitcoin gained over 3 percent in the last 24 hours, briefly touching levels not seen since late May. The move comes as traders and investors digest news of SpaceX's planned stock market debut, an event that has captured the attention of both traditional and crypto markets.

SpaceX IPO Fuels Risk-On Sentiment

Market participants believe the SpaceX IPO is injecting a fresh wave of risk appetite into financial markets. The space exploration company founded by Elon Musk has long been a magnet for retail and institutional investors alike, and its listing is expected to be one of the largest in recent years.

Several analysts have pointed to the IPO as a catalyst for Bitcoin's bounce. The reasoning is simple: when high-profile public offerings generate excitement, they often lift the broader risk asset basket, including cryptocurrencies. This pattern has been observed during previous tech IPOs, where Bitcoin moved in tandem with equity market optimism.

Trading volumes on major exchanges have also picked up, suggesting that investors are positioning themselves ahead of the listing. Some are drawing parallels to the Dogecoin rally that followed Musk's earlier endorsements, though Bitcoin's move this time appears more grounded in market fundamentals.

Technical Indicators Show Short-Term Strength

From a technical perspective, Bitcoin's price action is showing signs of a short-term reversal. The cryptocurrency bounced off a key support level near $60,000, a zone that has held multiple times in recent weeks. The Relative Strength Index (RSI) has moved back above 50, indicating that buying pressure is returning.

Traders are watching the $65,000 mark as the next major resistance. A clean break above that level could open the door for a retest of the all-time high, though some caution that the rally may be fragile. The macroeconomic backdrop remains uncertain, with interest rate decisions and regulatory developments still in play.

Options market data shows an uptick in bullish bets for the coming weeks, with open interest concentrated at strike prices above $70,000. This suggests a segment of the market is betting on a sustained rally into the IPO date.

Not everyone is convinced, however. Some veteran traders warn that the bounce could be a dead cat bounce, especially if broader equity markets fail to hold their gains. The SpaceX IPO, while exciting, does not fundamentally change Bitcoin's supply-demand dynamics, and the crypto remains highly sensitive to macro news.

What to Watch Next

All eyes will be on the SpaceX IPO pricing and first-day trading performance. If the listing is well received, it could provide further tailwinds for Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies. Conversely, a weak debut could reverse the current momentum. The next few days will be critical in determining whether this bounce has legs or fizzles out.
