Delhi University under fire as exam schedules clash with teaching duties

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • Delhi University's exam schedule has clashed with regular teaching duties, sparking criticism from faculty.
  • Teachers are reporting logistical challenges and increased workload due to overlapping responsibilities.
  • Students have expressed concerns over disrupted learning and potential academic impact.
  • The university has been urged to reconsider the timetable to avoid further complications.

Faculty and students voice frustration over scheduling

Delhi University is facing growing backlash after its recently released exam schedule was found to directly clash with ongoing teaching duties. Faculty members have reported that the overlapping commitments are creating severe logistical problems, forcing them to juggle invigilation and classroom instruction simultaneously.

Teachers across multiple departments have expressed frustration, noting that the schedule leaves little room for adequate preparation or student interaction. Several have taken to social media and internal forums to highlight the impracticality of the arrangement, which they say undermines the quality of education.

Impact on academic routine

The clash has disrupted the normal flow of classes, with some lectures being cancelled or rescheduled at the last minute. Students, too, are feeling the strain, as they face confusion over which sessions to prioritise and worry about gaps in their learning.

One faculty member, speaking on condition of anonymity, described the situation as “untenable.” They added that the university’s administration had not consulted teachers before finalising the dates, leading to an avoidable crisis. The issue is particularly acute in departments with limited staff, where the same teachers are expected to handle both exam duties and regular classes.

University response and demands

Delhi University officials have acknowledged the complaints but have not yet announced any changes to the schedule. In an internal memo, the administration urged departments to “manage with available resources,” a suggestion that has only deepened the discontent.

Faculty associations have demanded an immediate revision of the timetable, warning of possible protests if their concerns are not addressed. They argue that the current arrangement not only affects teaching quality but also places undue stress on educators already dealing with heavy workloads.

Students have also weighed in, with some petitions circulating online calling for a more balanced academic calendar. Many say the clash is hampering their preparation for internal assessments and final exams, which are scheduled later in the semester.

Looking ahead

With the semester already underway, the clock is ticking for Delhi University to resolve the conflict. A meeting between faculty representatives and the administration is expected in the coming days, but no concrete timeline for a revised schedule has been announced yet.

Observers note that this is not the first time the university has faced criticism over exam scheduling, and the current backlash highlights a recurring failure to coordinate academic calendars. Whether the administration will act swiftly to restore normalcy remains to be seen.
