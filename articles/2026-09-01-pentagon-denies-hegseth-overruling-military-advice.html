Pentagon denies report of Hegseth overruling military advice

The Pentagon has played down a report suggesting that Pete Hegseth, the acting undersecretary of defense, has been regularly overruling military advice, particularly as tensions with Iran escalate. The report, which cites anonymous sources, claims that Hegseth has been dismissing recommendations from senior military officials, raising concerns about the chain of command and the potential for misguided decisions.

Defense Department spokesperson John Kirby stated that the report is inaccurate and that Hegseth has been working closely with military leaders to ensure that all advice is considered. Kirby emphasized that the Pentagon values the expertise of its military advisors and that Hegseth's actions are in line with the department's policies.

Trump and Netanyahu risk being played by Putin and Xi

As tensions in the Middle East continue to escalate, there are growing concerns that US President Donald Trump and Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu could be manipulated by their counterparts in Russia and China. Analysts warn that both Putin and Xi may be using the situation to advance their own agendas, potentially leading to further instability in the region.

According to a report in The Guardian, both Putin and Xi could benefit from a prolonged conflict in the Middle East, as it would divert attention from domestic issues and strengthen their positions on the global stage. The report suggests that both leaders are likely to continue their aggressive stance, regardless of the potential consequences.

Top US generals warn against escalation in Iran

Despite Trump's vow to escalate the conflict with Iran, top US generals have issued a stark warning against further military action. The generals, who spoke on the condition of anonymity, have expressed serious concerns about the potential for a full-blown war, which could have devastating consequences for both the US and its allies.

The generals argue that any escalation would not only risk further destabilizing the region but also could lead to unintended consequences, such as increased Iranian support from other countries and a broader involvement of US forces. The Pentagon has been working closely with the military to ensure that all options are considered, and to avoid any potential missteps.

Pentagon erupts over classified leak of Iran war dissent

The Pentagon has expressed its outrage over a classified leak of a report detailing dissent within the military over the US approach to the conflict with Iran. The leak, which was reported by The Hill, has raised serious concerns about the potential for further leaks and the security risks associated with classified information.

The Pentagon has launched an investigation into the leak, and has urged all personnel to be vigilant about protecting sensitive information. The department has emphasized that the leak could have serious consequences, not only for national security but also for the morale of the military.
