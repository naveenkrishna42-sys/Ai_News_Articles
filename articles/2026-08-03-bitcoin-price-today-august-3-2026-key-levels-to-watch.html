What's driving the market?

Several factors are influencing Bitcoin's price action. Macroeconomic data from major economies, including inflation figures and central bank policy expectations, remain key drivers. A stronger US dollar typically pressures risk assets like Bitcoin, while a weaker one often supports it.

On the regulatory front, news from various jurisdictions continues to shape sentiment. Any hint of stricter rules or, conversely, clearer frameworks can trigger sharp moves. Traders are also keeping an eye on institutional flows, with any signs of large-scale buying or selling adding to momentum.

Analysts point to a few price levels that could determine Bitcoin's next move. A break above a recent resistance zone could open the door to further upside, attracting fresh buyers. Conversely, losing a key support level might accelerate selling pressure, leading to a test of lower ranges.

Sentiment and positioning

Market sentiment appears mixed. Some investors remain bullish on Bitcoin's long-term prospects, citing adoption by institutions and growing use cases. Others are cautious, pointing to the potential for further downside if global risk appetite weakens.

Derivatives data shows that funding rates are near neutral, indicating that leveraged traders are not excessively positioned in either direction. This could mean the market is open to a sharp move once a catalyst emerges.

What to watch next
