Apple Music Subscription Prices Rise Across India

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple Music subscription prices have been raised across all plans in India.
  • The price hike also applies to Apple One bundles.
  • Apple cited rising licensing costs as the reason for the increase.

Apple has raised subscription prices for its Apple Music service across India, affecting individual, family, and student plans. The move comes as part of a broader revision of the company's pricing structure in the region.

New Pricing Details

The individual plan now costs ₹129 per month, up from ₹99. The family plan, which allows up to six members, has increased to ₹199 per month from ₹149. The student plan has been revised to ₹69 per month, compared to the earlier ₹49.

Apple One bundles, which combine multiple Apple services including Music, TV+, and iCloud storage, have also seen price adjustments. The individual Apple One plan now costs ₹249 per month, the family plan ₹299 per month, and the premier plan ₹399 per month.

Reason for the Price Hike

In a statement to subscribers, Apple attributed the price increase to rising licensing costs. The company said it needs to adjust prices to continue offering a vast library of songs and exclusive content.

The music streaming industry has seen similar moves globally, with rivals like Spotify and YouTube Music also revising their subscription fees in recent years. Licensing agreements with record labels and artists have become more expensive as competition for exclusive content intensifies.

Impact on Users

Existing subscribers will see the new prices reflected in their next billing cycle. Apple has notified users via email and in-app messages about the changes, giving them time to adjust their plans if needed.

For many users, the increase may prompt a review of their subscription choices. Students, in particular, face a 40% hike on their discounted plan, though it remains one of the more affordable options in the market.

Market Context

India is a key market for Apple, with the company aggressively expanding its services segment. The price hike comes amid a push to boost revenue from recurring subscriptions, which have become a major profit driver for the tech giant.

Competing services like Spotify, JioSaavn, and Gaana offer similar or lower pricing, making the streaming landscape highly price-sensitive. Apple's move could test user loyalty in a market where affordability often dictates choice.

Analysts note that while the increase is modest, it reflects broader trends in content costs. Licensing fees for music rights have been climbing globally, and streaming platforms are passing some of that burden to consumers.

What happens next: Subscribers will likely evaluate whether the enhanced features and integration with Apple's ecosystem justify the higher cost. The company may also introduce promotional offers or regional pricing adjustments to retain its user base in India.
