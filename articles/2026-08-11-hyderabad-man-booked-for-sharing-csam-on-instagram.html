Hyderabad man booked for sharing CSAM on Instagram

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • A man in Hyderabad has been booked for sharing CSAM on Instagram.
  • The case was registered under relevant sections of the law.
  • Investigation is ongoing to trace the origin and extent of the material.

Case registered after cyber tip-off

Hyderabad police have registered a case against a local man for allegedly sharing child sexual abuse material (CSAM) on the social media platform Instagram. The action follows a cyber tip-off that flagged the account for distributing explicit content involving minors.

Officials from the Hyderabad cyber crime wing confirmed that the accused, a resident of the city, has been identified. His name has not been released as the investigation is in its early stages.

Legal provisions invoked

The case has been filed under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act and relevant sections of the Information Technology Act. Police said the accused used his Instagram account to share the illegal content, which was detected during routine monitoring.

A senior officer said that the platform's reporting mechanisms helped flag the activity. "We received the tip and acted on it immediately," the officer said, while declining to provide further details.

Investigation underway

Police are now examining the accused's digital footprint, including his phone and social media activity, to trace the origin of the CSAM. They are also looking into whether the material was shared with others or if the accused was part of a larger network.

The cyber crime unit has sought details from Instagram's parent company, Meta, to obtain data linked to the account. Officials have not yet confirmed whether any victims have been identified in India.

Growing concern over online child safety

This case adds to a rising number of incidents where social media platforms are used to circulate illegal content. Law enforcement agencies across the country have been stepping up efforts to curb such activity, often relying on tips from tech companies and NGOs.

In recent months, several similar cases have been reported in Telangana, prompting authorities to issue advisories on reporting suspicious accounts. Cyber safety experts have also urged parents to monitor minors' online interactions closely.

Next steps

The accused is expected to be summoned for questioning in the coming days. Police are likely to seek his custody to extract details about the content's source and possible accomplices. A final chargesheet will be filed after the probe concludes.

As the investigation progresses, authorities are expected to coordinate with Meta for forensic analysis of the flagged account. The outcome could set a precedent for how such cases are handled in the state.
