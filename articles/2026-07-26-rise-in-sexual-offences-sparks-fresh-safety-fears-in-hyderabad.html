Rise in sexual offences sparks fresh safety fears in Hyderabad

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hyderabad reports a surge in sexual offences, alarming residents and activists.
  • Police have increased patrols and are using technology to track offenders.
  • Calls for faster trials and stricter implementation of laws grow louder.

Alarming numbers

Hyderabad has witnessed a sharp rise in reported sexual offences over the past few months, reigniting deep-seated concerns about women's safety in the city. Data from the Telangana police shows a significant increase in cases such as rape, molestation, and harassment, particularly in areas with dense populations and weak street lighting.

The numbers have triggered fresh protests from women's groups and residents, who accuse the authorities of failing to ensure a secure environment. Many have taken to social media to share their experiences, demanding immediate action from the state government.

Police response and measures

In response to the spike, the Hyderabad police commissioner has announced a series of steps aimed at curbing the trend. These include increasing the number of patrols in vulnerable zones, deploying more female officers, and installing CCTV cameras at strategic points across the city.

Officials have also begun using a mobile app that allows women to alert the nearest police station with a single tap. A senior officer told the media that a dedicated helpline is being set up to handle complaints swiftly. However, critics argue that these measures are reactive rather than preventive.

Community and activism

Local residents and student groups have organised candlelight vigils and public meetings to voice their anger. Activists say the surge in cases reflects a deeper societal failure, pointing to a lack of proper sex education and lenient punishment for offenders.

Some have called for the establishment of fast-track courts to ensure speedy justice. Others are pushing for better street infrastructure, including improved lighting and increased public transport frequency during late hours.

What next

Authorities have promised to release a detailed crime review report in the coming weeks. Meanwhile, the city's residents remain on edge, waiting to see if the police's new measures will translate into real safety on the streets.
