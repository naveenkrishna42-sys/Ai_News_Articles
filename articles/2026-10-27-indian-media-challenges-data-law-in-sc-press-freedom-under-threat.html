Indian Media Confronts Data Law in Supreme Court

In a pivotal development for India's media landscape, a consortium of major news organizations has moved the Supreme Court, challenging specific provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). These petitioners, including outlets associated with prominent industrial houses like Ambani and Adani, argue that the newly enacted law poses a significant threat to journalistic freedom and the crucial process of news gathering. The legal battle underscores a growing tension between individual privacy rights and the imperative for a free and robust press in a democratic society.

The petition highlights concerns that the DPDP Act, in its current form, could inadvertently criminalize legitimate journalistic activities, especially those involving the collection and processing of personal data for public interest stories. This challenge sets the stage for a critical judicial review, with far-reaching implications for how newsrooms operate and how citizens receive information.
