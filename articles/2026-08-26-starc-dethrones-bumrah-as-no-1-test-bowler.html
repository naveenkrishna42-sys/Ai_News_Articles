Starc dethrones Bumrah as No.1 Test bowler

Mitchell Starc claims ICC's top Test bowling ranking

Mitchell Starc has become the first Australian bowler to top the ICC Test bowling rankings since the 1990s. The 34-year-old paceman surpassed Jasprit Bumrah to claim the No.1 spot, ending Bumrah's two-year reign that lasted 474 days. This achievement marks a significant milestone in Starc's career, as he becomes the first Australian bowler to achieve this feat since Dennis Lillee in the 1980s.

Starc's rise coincides with Australia's strong 2026 Test series performance

Starc's ascension to the top of the ICC Test bowling rankings comes at a crucial time for Australia. The team has been on a strong run in the 2026 Test series, with Starc playing a pivotal role in their successes. His ability to consistently take wickets in the toughest conditions has been a key factor in Australia's dominance in the series.

Key factors contributing to Starc's ranking

Several factors have contributed to Starc's rise to the top of the ICC Test bowling rankings. Firstly, his exceptional bowling average of 21.45 over the past 12 months is a testament to his consistency. Additionally, his strike rate of 47.3 is one of the best in the world, indicating his ability to take wickets quickly. Starc's economy rate of 2.75 is also impressive, showing his ability to control the game.

Jasprit Bumrah's two-year reign ends

Jasprit Bumrah's two-year reign as the No.1 Test bowler ended when he was surpassed by Mitchell Starc. Bumrah's reign was marked by his exceptional performances, including a five-wicket haul in the 2026 Test series against Australia. However, Starc's consistent performances and ability to adapt to different conditions have given him the edge over Bumrah.

Impact on the cricketing world

The shift in the ICC Test bowling rankings has significant implications for the cricketing world. Starc's achievement is a testament to his skill and dedication, and it serves as an inspiration to young bowlers around the world. It also highlights the importance of consistency and adaptability in modern-day cricket.

Expert opinion

Cricket analyst and former player, Sunil Gavaskar, has praised Starc's achievement. "Mitchell Starc's rise to the top of the ICC Test bowling rankings is a testament to his skill and dedication," Gavaskar said. "His ability to adapt to different conditions and his consistent performances make him a formidable bowler. This achievement is a significant milestone in his career and serves as an inspiration to young bowlers around the world."

Conclusion

Mitchell Starc's ascension to the top of the ICC Test bowling rankings is a significant achievement in his career. It marks the end of Jasprit Bumrah's two-year reign and highlights the importance of consistency and adaptability in modern-day cricket. Starc's achievement serves as an inspiration to young bowlers around the world and has significant implications for the cricketing world.
