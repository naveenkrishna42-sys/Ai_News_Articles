Delhi Red Fort Blast Kills 13, Police Invoke Terror Law

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Crime & Law Original source ↗
Key points
  • Explosion near Red Fort in Delhi killed 13 people
  • Police invoked the Unlawful Activities Prevention Act (UAPA)
  • Investigation underway; no group has claimed responsibility

Blast Rocks Historic Red Fort Area

A powerful explosion near Delhi's iconic Red Fort killed at least 13 people on Tuesday, sending shockwaves through the national capital. The blast occurred in the morning hours, catching visitors and locals off guard.

Emergency services rushed to the scene. Ambulances and police teams cordoned off the area as crowds scattered in panic. Officials confirmed 13 deaths and several injuries, though exact numbers remain unverified.

Police Invoke Anti-Terror Law

Delhi Police have invoked the Unlawful Activities Prevention Act (UAPA), a stringent anti-terror law, in connection with the blast. The move signals that authorities are treating the explosion as a potential act of terrorism.

Senior police officials told reporters that investigations are in early stages. They have not yet named any suspects or identified the type of explosive used. Forensic teams are collecting evidence from the site.

No Claim of Responsibility Yet

As of now, no individual or group has claimed responsibility for the attack. Security agencies are scanning intelligence inputs to trace possible leads.

Multiple agencies, including the National Investigation Agency (NIA), are expected to join the probe. The area remains under heavy security as investigators piece together what happened.

Witnesses Describe Chaos

Eyewitnesses described scenes of chaos. One person said they heard a loud bang and saw smoke rising near the Red Fort's outer walls. Many visitors were tourists, including families.

Local hospitals declared an emergency. Injured persons were rushed for treatment. Doctors said some victims had severe injuries from shrapnel.

Political Reactions Pour In

Political leaders condemned the attack. Prime Minister Narendra Modi called for unity and assured the public of a thorough investigation. Opposition leaders demanded answers on security lapses.

Home Minister Amit Shah said the government would not spare those responsible. He directed officials to maintain vigilance across the country.

The Red Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, holds deep historical and national significance. It is the site of the prime minister's annual Independence Day address. Security around the monument is typically high, making Tuesday's breach a major concern.

What to watch: Investigators are expected to release more details in the coming hours, including the nature of the explosive and any potential leads. Security across Delhi's tourist spots has been tightened as a precaution.
