Hyderabad Traffic Diversions: What Commuters Need to Know for September 6

The Hyderabad City Police have issued a comprehensive traffic advisory ahead of the Chief Minister's public meeting and foot rally scheduled for Sunday, September 6, 2026. The diversions will impact key central zones of the city during the afternoon and evening hours, affecting lakhs of daily commuters, weekend travelers, and commercial transport operators across the Greater Hyderabad region.

Restrictions will be enforced in phases, beginning from mid-afternoon and extending into the night, with movement around the rally's starting point and concluding venue expected to remain heavily regulated. Residents and visitors are urged to plan journeys well in advance and use designated alternate corridors to avoid congestion on arterial roads.

Affected Routes and Key Diversion Points

The rally's route encompasses some of Hyderabad's busiest thoroughfares, with major restrictions around Tank Bund, Abids, Abkari Bhavan, and surrounding stretches connecting to the Lower Tank Bund-RTC Cross Road belt. These areas serve as critical connectors between Secunderabad, the Old City, and the central business district.

Commuters travelling from Secunderabad towards Mehdipatnam or Kukatpally will face restrictions near the rally corridor. Vehicles heading towards the Charminar and surrounding heritage zones may experience delays on parallel inner-ring routes. The Hyderabad Traffic Police have recommended diversion through the Outer Ring Road (ORR) and elevated corridors where feasible.

Timings, Restrictions, and Parking Advisory

Traffic curbs are expected to come into effect from approximately 3:00 PM onwards, intensifying between 4:00 PM and 8:00 PM as the foot rally gathers momentum. The meeting itself is likely to conclude late evening, after which normal traffic flow will resume in phases.

  • Parking restrictions: No-vehicle zones will be enforced within a 500-metre radius of the rally route.
  • Heavy vehicles: Trucks and commercial carriers may be restricted during peak rally hours.
  • Public transport: TSRTC bus routes servicing Abids, Tank Bund, and Sultan Bazar will be diverted; commuters should check updated route schedules.
  • Emergency services: Ambulance and emergency corridors will remain operational, with dedicated patrol units deployed to manage VIP movement.

Practical Impact on Daily Commuters

Sunday commuters planning visits to markets in Sultan Bazar, Begum Bazar, or weekend outings to Hussain Sagar and Lumbini Park should factor in extended travel times of 30 to 60 minutes. Office-goers working half-day shifts or attending late-evening commitments in the IT corridor (Gachibowli-Hitech City) should plan departures before 2:30 PM to bypass the worst of the restrictions.

Residents of surrounding colonies such as Narayanguda, Kachiguda, Basheerbagh, and Red Hills are advised to use colony-level internal roads where possible. Two-wheeler riders will have marginally better flexibility than four-wheelers, but parking near the rally site remains prohibited.

Travel Tips and Alternate Routes

  • Use the Inner Ring Road connecting Tarnaka, Uppal, and Habsiguda for east-west movement.
  • Take Raj Bhavan Road-Mehidipatnam corridor for south-bound travel instead of the central Tank Bund route.
  • Avoid the NTR Marg and Telugu Thalli flyover stretch during peak rally hours.
  • Check real-time updates via the official Hyderabad Traffic Police social media handles before setting out.

Summary: Plan Ahead for a Smooth Sunday

The Hyderabad Traffic Police have coordinated extensively with district administration units to ensure both public safety and minimal disruption. While diversions are unavoidable given the scale of the CM's public meeting and foot rally, adherence to the advisory and use of alternate corridors will help commuters navigate the city efficiently. Travellers should allocate extra buffer time, monitor live traffic feeds, and consider postponing non-essential travel through central Hyderabad until late Sunday night.
