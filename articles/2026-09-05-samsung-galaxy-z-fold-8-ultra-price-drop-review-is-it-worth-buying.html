Deal & Pricing Analysis

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra originally launched at £1,599 in the UK. A limited‑time promotion now offers a $300 saving, which translates to roughly £240 off, bringing the street price down to £1,359. This represents one of the deepest discounts seen on Samsung’s latest flagship foldable since its release, placing it competitively against the previous generation Z Fold 5 and the Google Pixel Fold. Historically, foldable prices have hovered above £1,400 even after several months, so this deal provides a notable value proposition for users seeking cutting‑edge flexibility without paying the full premium.

Real‑World Performance & Daily Use

In hand, the Z Fold 8 Ultra feels remarkably solid despite its slim profile. The hinge operates with a satisfying, almost silent snap, and the crease is visible only under direct lighting—a common trade‑off for foldables but less intrusive than on earlier generations. The main 7.6‑inch panel delivers vivid colours, deep blacks, and a buttery 120 Hz refresh rate that makes scrolling and gaming feel fluid. Multitasking is where the device truly shines: you can run up to three apps simultaneously in split‑screen or pop‑up view, and the S Pen (which slots neatly into the chassis) adds precision for note‑taking, sketching, or photo editing.

Battery endurance is respectable for a foldable of this size. With moderate use—mixing social media, video streaming, and occasional gaming—the phone comfortably lasts a full day, often ending with ~20 % charge left. Heavy users who push the main screen at max brightness for extended periods may need to top up by early evening, but the 25 W wired charger replenishes roughly 50 % in 30 minutes. Heat management is effective; the chipset stays warm under load but never uncomfortable to hold.

Competition & Market Alternatives

The closest rival in the same price bracket is the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 5, which currently sells for around £1,199. While the Z Fold 5 offers a very similar experience, the Z Fold 8 Ultra steps up with a newer Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chip, brighter main display, and improved S Pen latency. For those considering a different form factor, the Google Pixel Fold is priced near £1,599 and excels in computational photography but lacks the S Pen ecosystem and has a narrower outer screen, making one‑handed use less comfortable.

If raw camera versatility is the priority, the iPhone 15 Pro Max (though not a foldable) still leads in video and low‑light performance, but it sacrifices the multitasking advantages of a foldable design. Overall, the Z Fold 8 Ultra provides a more balanced flagship package for power users who value productivity alongside multimedia consumption.

Who Should Buy This Deal

This deal is ideal for professionals, creators, and tech enthusiasts who rely on multitasking, need a large canvas for drawing or document work, and appreciate having an S Pen always at hand. The discounted price makes the premium foldable experience accessible without waiting for a deeper price cut later in the year. Conversely, if you are primarily focused on camera performance, have a tight budget under £1,000, or prefer a more traditional slab phone, you may want to wait for a future generation or consider a non‑foldable flagship instead.

Final Buying Verdict

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra at £1,359 offers a compelling blend of cutting‑edge foldable technology, S Pen versatility, and strong day‑to‑day battery life, making it a worthwhile purchase for power users seeking a premium productivity tool. If you value multitasking, a large flexible screen, and stylus support, this deal is hard to beat; otherwise, waiting for a future price drop or considering a non‑foldable flagship may be more prudent.
