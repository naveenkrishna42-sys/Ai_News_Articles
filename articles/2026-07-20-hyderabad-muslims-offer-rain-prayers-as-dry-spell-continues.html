Hyderabad Muslims offer rain prayers as dry spell continues

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hundreds of Muslims in Hyderabad performed Salat al-Istisqa, the Islamic prayer for rain
  • The prayers come amid a prolonged dry spell affecting Telangana
  • Congregations were held at several mosques and open grounds across the city

Hundreds of Muslims in Hyderabad gathered on Sunday to offer Salat al-Istisqa, the Islamic prayer for rain, as the city continues to reel under a prolonged dry spell.

The special congregation was held at several mosques and open grounds across the city. Worshippers sought divine intervention for rainfall to ease the prevailing dry conditions.

Prayers held across the city

Large crowds turned up at locations including the historic Mecca Masjid and the Eidgah in Mir Alam area. Similar prayers were organised in other parts of the city and surrounding districts.

Men, women and children participated in the supplication. The faithful raised their hands in prayer, imploring for mercy from the heavens. Many had brought mats and water bottles, prepared for the outdoor congregation.

Local religious leaders led the prayers. In their sermons, they encouraged the community to repent and seek forgiveness, and to help those affected by the dry spell.

Dry spell grips Telangana

Telangana has been experiencing a significant rain deficit over the past few weeks. The state has received far less rainfall than normal for this period.

Farmers in several districts are worried about standing crops. The lack of rain has also led to water shortages in some urban areas, including parts of Hyderabad.

The India Meteorological Department has not yet forecast any substantial rainfall in the coming days.

Salat al-Istisqa is a traditional practice in Islam, performed during times of drought. The Prophet Muhammad is reported to have performed this prayer during a dry spell in Medina.

The practice is observed by Muslims in different parts of the world when faced with prolonged absence of rain.

Community response

Many of those who attended the prayers expressed hope that their supplication would be answered. “We have not seen such a dry spell in years. The crops are failing. We have no option but to turn to the Almighty,” said Mohammed Abdul Qadir, a resident of the old city.

Community organisations had spread the word about the prayer timings through mosque announcements and social media groups.

The local administration provided security and logistical support at the larger gathering points. Traffic was diverted in some areas to accommodate the crowds.

The Telangana government has said it is monitoring the situation and will take necessary steps to mitigate the impact of the dry spell.

Residents across the city now wait for the skies to open. The coming week’s weather forecast will be watched closely.
