Bombay Bar Association Demands Mishra's Resignation Over BCI Crisis

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bombay Bar Association criticizes Mishra's apology as belated.
  • Association demands Mishra's resignation as BCI chairman.
  • NALSAR standoff and BCI's crisis of authority cited as reasons.

Bombay Bar Association Criticizes Mishra's Apology

The Bombay Bar Association has strongly criticized Manan Kumar Mishra's apology, calling it belated and inadequate in addressing the ongoing crisis at the Bar Council of India (BCI). The association has demanded Mishra's immediate resignation from his position as BCI chairman.

NALSAR Standoff Highlights BCI's Crisis

The NALSAR standoff, a recent controversy involving the National Law School of India University (NLSIU), has further exacerbated the BCI's crisis of authority. The association has cited this incident as a significant factor in their demand for Mishra's resignation.

Legal Cockroaches to Protest Against Mishra

In response to the BCI's crisis, the All India Youth Legal Aid Association (AIYAA) has announced a protest against Mishra. The protest, scheduled for tomorrow, is expected to be attended by a large number of legal professionals, who have been referred to as 'legal cockroaches' by the Chief Justice of India (CJI).

CJP's Comment on the Protest

The Chief Justice of the Patna High Court, Justice Sanjay Karol, has expressed his support for the protest. He has stated that the protest is a necessary step to address the BCI's crisis and ensure the independence and integrity of the legal profession.
