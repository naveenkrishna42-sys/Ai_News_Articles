Haiwaan Box Office Day 2: Akshay-Saif Film Crosses Rs 5 Crore

Key points
  • Haiwaan has crossed the Rs 5 crore nett mark in India across its first two days, per trade estimates.
  • The Akshay Kumar-Saif Ali Khan creature comedy is running well below the opening weekend pace of Kumar's recent mid-budget hits.
  • Weekday holds from Monday onward will decide whether the film recovers its production and marketing spend.

Haiwaan, the creature comedy headlined by Akshay Kumar and Saif Ali Khan, has crossed the Rs 5 crore nett mark in India by the end of its second day, according to trade estimates. The two-day total places the film in modest territory for a star-driven Hindi release, and the number is now the single most scrutinised figure in the film's commercial story.

Haiwaan Day 2 Box Office: The Numbers So Far

Trade trackers pegged the day one nett collection in the Rs 2.5-3 crore range, with day two adding a similar figure to push the cumulative India nett past Rs 5 crore. Occupancy across multiplexes stayed strongest in the evening and night shows, while mass-market single screens in the Hindi belt reported softer footfalls.

These are trade estimates and not studio-audited figures. Final numbers typically firm up once distributors reconcile collections with exhibitors.

Why the Rs 5 Crore Mark Matters for Buyers of This Film

For exhibitors and distributors, the Rs 5 crore two-day figure is a signal about screen retention, not a verdict. A film that holds above Rs 2 crore nett on its first Monday can protect its show count through week one. A drop below that threshold usually triggers screen cuts by the second Friday, which caps the film's lifetime ceiling.

For audiences, the practical question is different. A creature comedy lives or dies on word of mouth. If the humour and VFX land with family audiences, weekend two can outperform weekend one. If the film is front-loaded by star fans alone, the second weekend will collapse.

The Budget and Recovery Arithmetic

Hindi creature comedies with two A-list leads typically carry a production and marketing outlay in the Rs 60-100 crore band, depending on VFX volume and promotional spend. Against that backdrop, a Rs 5 crore two-day start demands strong week-one multipliers and healthy ancillary revenue from satellite, digital and overseas rights.

Overseas collections, particularly from the Gulf, United Kingdom and North America, can add meaningful cushion. Gulf markets historically over-index for Akshay Kumar titles, and that trend is worth tracking when the first overseas weekend data arrives.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • Strongest segment: Multiplex evening and night shows in metros are driving the bulk of collections.
  • Weakest segment: Single-screen and mass-belt footfalls remain soft on early trends.
  • Star power is intact: The Akshay Kumar-Saif Ali Khan pairing is pulling opening-weekend audiences into theatres.
  • Screen count is the risk: A weak Monday hold could cost the film 20-30 per cent of its shows within a week.
  • Verdict pending: Two days is too small a sample to call a hit, average or flop.

What to Watch Next

The first Monday figure is the real test. A hold above Rs 2 crore nett keeps Haiwaan in the running for a respectable week-one total. A sharper fall points to a short theatrical run and a quick pivot to digital streaming, which has become the standard recovery route for mid-budget Hindi releases.

Buyers evaluating the film's commercial health should also track the second-weekend trend, overseas numbers and the eventual streaming sale. Those three data points, more than the day two headline, will determine whether Haiwaan ends up a profitable venture for its stakeholders.
