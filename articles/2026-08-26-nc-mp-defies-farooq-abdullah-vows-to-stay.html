NC MP defies Farooq Abdullah, vows to stay

📅 2026-08-26 📂 India Original source ↗

  • NC MP vows to stay in Delhi to support party's interests
  • Farooq Abdullah challenges Ruhullah Mehdi to resign

NC MP Rejects Resignation Demand

National Conference (NC) MP Ruhullah Mehdi has defied Farooq Abdullah's call for him to resign, stating he won't return to Srinagar empty-handed. This development has further deepened the rift within the party, which has been grappling with leadership disputes for some time.

Farooq Abdullah's Challenge

Farooq Abdullah, the party's president, has challenged Ruhullah Mehdi to resign from his position as an MP, citing concerns over the party's internal matters. Abdullah's demand has been seen as a direct attempt to assert his authority over the party's leadership.

Ruhullah Mehdi's Response

Ruhullah Mehdi has rejected Abdullah's demand, stating that he will not resign and will continue to serve as an MP. He has vowed to stay in Delhi to support the party's interests and ensure that the party's matters are handled in the best possible manner.

Broader Impact on NC

Ruhullah Mehdi's defiance of Farooq Abdullah's demand has been seen as a major development in the party's leadership dispute. It has further highlighted the deepening rift within the NC, which has been plagued by internal conflicts and power struggles for some time.

Expert Opinion

Political analyst Rajiv Kumar believes that Ruhullah Mehdi's stance is a calculated move to assert his authority and challenge Farooq Abdullah's leadership. Kumar adds that this development could have significant implications for the NC's future, as it could further weaken the party's unity and cohesion.

Key Facts

  • Ruhullah Mehdi is a senior NC leader and has been serving as an MP from the Srinagar constituency.
  • Farooq Abdullah is the current president of the NC and has been facing challenges to his leadership.
  • The NC has been grappling with leadership disputes and internal conflicts for some time.
