Ramlila Mahasangh demands pre-screening of Ranbir Kapoor's 'Ramayana', warns of protests

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ramlila Mahasangh demands a special pre-release screening of the Ranbir Kapoor-starrer 'Ramayana'.
  • The body says Kapoor's portrayal of Lord Rama 'lacks gravitas' and is not satisfied with the depiction.
  • The group has warned of protests if their demands are not met before the film's release.

Ramlila body ups ante over 'Ramayana'

The Ramlila Mahasangh, a body representing traditional Ramlila organisers across India, has struck a combative note against the makers of the upcoming film 'Ramayana', starring Ranbir Kapoor. The organisation has demanded a pre-release screening of the movie, saying it is not satisfied with the actor's portrayal of Lord Rama.

In a statement, the Mahasangh said Kapoor's depiction of the Hindu deity "lacks gravitas" and does not match the reverence expected of the character. The group has warned of protests if its demand for a special screening is not met before the film hits theatres.

Demand for transparency

The Mahasangh, which has been vocal about the representation of religious figures in popular media, insists that a pre-screening would allow community leaders to assess the film's content. They argue that any distortion of the epic's narrative or its central characters could hurt the sentiments of millions of devotees.

"We are not against the film, but we want to see it first. If the portrayal is not respectful, we will have no option but to protest," a representative said, without confirming specific dates or locations for any planned demonstrations.

Makers yet to respond

As of now, the makers of 'Ramayana'—which has been in production for months and features a star-studded cast—have not issued an official response to the Mahasangh's demand. Sources close to the production say the team is likely to evaluate the situation before deciding on a course of action.

The film, one of the most anticipated mythological dramas in recent years, has already faced scrutiny over its casting choices and visual effects. The latest controversy adds to the pressure on the production house, which is expected to unveil a trailer soon.

Precedents of religious scrutiny

This is not the first time a Bollywood film has faced such demands. In the past, movies like 'Padmaavat' and 'Oh My God' encountered protests from religious groups over content or casting. The Ramlila Mahasangh's move mirrors a pattern where community organisations seek early access to films with religious themes, citing the need to prevent offence.

For 'Ramayana', the stakes are higher given the epic's cultural centrality. The Mahasangh's warning could lead to coordinated protests in multiple cities, especially in the Hindi heartland, if the makers do not comply.

What lies ahead

The next few weeks will be crucial as the production team decides whether to accommodate the Mahasangh's request. A pre-screening could de-escalate tensions, but it also sets a precedent for other groups to make similar demands. Observers will watch if the makers engage in dialogue or take a firm stand to avoid delays.

For now, the ball is in the court of the film's producers. Their response will determine whether the release proceeds smoothly or faces the first of many hurdles.
