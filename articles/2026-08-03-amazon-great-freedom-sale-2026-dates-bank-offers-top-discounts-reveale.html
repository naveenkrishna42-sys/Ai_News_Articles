Amazon Great Freedom Sale 2026: Dates, bank offers, top discounts revealed

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Amazon Great Freedom Sale 2026 starts this weekend
  • Bank offers and discounts across phones, TVs, and fashion
  • Motorola and OnePlus also announce competing sales

Sale dates and early access

Amazon India is set to roll out its Great Freedom Sale 2026 this weekend, bringing a fresh wave of discounts across smartphones, televisions, fashion, and home appliances. The annual Independence Day-themed event is one of the biggest shopping festivals on the platform, and this year's edition is expected to draw millions of bargain hunters.

While the exact start date has not been officially confirmed by Amazon, reports indicate the sale will kick off on Saturday, with early access for Prime members likely a day in advance. Shoppers can look forward to steep price cuts, exchange bonuses, and no-cost EMI options across categories.

Bank offers and payment perks

As with previous editions, bank-linked discounts are expected to play a central role. HDFC Bank and SBI cardholders typically enjoy instant discounts of up to 10 per cent, and this year is likely to follow suit. Some reports suggest additional cashback on Amazon Pay UPI transactions, though the company has not yet detailed the full list of offers.

For big-ticket purchases like smartphones and televisions, customers can combine bank discounts with exchange offers to bring effective prices down significantly. No-cost EMI schemes are also expected on most products, making high-value items more accessible to a wider audience.

Top deals on smartphones and electronics

Smartphones are expected to be the star of the sale, with several brands launching Independence Day-specific offers. Motorola has already teased deals on its Edge series, with the Moto Edge 70 Max reportedly selling for Rs 49,999 and the Edge 70 Pro at Rs 36,999. These prices are likely to be available during the Amazon sale window.

OnePlus is also running its own Independence Day sale, offering discounts on smartphones, IoT devices, and accessories. The company is expected to bundle benefits like extended warranty and exchange bonuses on popular models such as the OnePlus Nord series and the flagship 13 series.

Televisions are another category to watch. Amazon typically slashes prices on 4K and smart TVs from brands like Samsung, LG, Sony, and Xiaomi during this period. Expect deep discounts on 55-inch and larger panels, especially during flash deals that refresh throughout the day.

Fashion, home, and other categories

Beyond electronics, the sale will cover fashion and home essentials. Apparel from top brands, footwear, watches, and accessories are likely to see discounts of 40-70 per cent. Home decor, kitchen appliances, and furniture are also part of the event, with bundle offers on multiple purchases.

Amazon has been aggressive in matching or undercutting rival Flipkart's Big Freedom Sale, which typically runs around the same time. This competitive tension benefits consumers, as both platforms push prices lower across overlapping categories.

What to watch for

As the sale approaches, expect Amazon to release a detailed schedule of flash deals and limited-time offers. Shoppers should keep an eye on the app's notification settings and wishlist items early to avoid missing out on the steepest discounts.

The coming days will reveal whether the rumoured bank tie-ups materialise and if any surprise launches or exclusive product variants are announced during the event. For now, the Great Freedom Sale 2026 looks set to deliver a weekend of heavy discounts across the board.
