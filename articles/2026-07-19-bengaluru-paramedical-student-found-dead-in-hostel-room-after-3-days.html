Bengaluru Paramedical Student Found Dead in Hostel Room After 3 Days

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 22-year-old paramedical student was found dead in her room in Bengaluru.
  • The body was recovered three days after she was last seen alive.
  • Police suspect suicide; further investigation is underway.

Bengaluru: The body of a 22-year-old paramedical student was discovered in her hostel room in Bengaluru, three days after she was last seen alive. The incident has sent shockwaves through the local community and raised questions about safety and mental health support for students.

Discovery After a Long Wait

The student, whose identity has not been officially released, was found in her room in a private hostel near the college campus. According to police sources, the body was in an advanced stage of decomposition, indicating that she had been dead for several days.

Her absence was noticed when she did not attend classes for two consecutive days. Concerned classmates and hostel staff tried to contact her, but her phone went unanswered. The door to her room was locked from the inside, prompting the authorities to break it open. The body was found on the third day.

Police Investigation and Suspicions

Senior police officials have reached the spot and are conducting a thorough investigation. Early indications suggest that the student may have died by suicide. No suicide note has been recovered from the scene yet.

"We are treating this as an unnatural death. The body has been sent for post-mortem, and we are waiting for the report. Our team is also speaking to her friends and family to understand if she was facing any stress or mental health issues," a police officer said on condition of anonymity.

Family and Community in Shock

The student's family has been informed and is en route to Bengaluru. They are in deep shock, as she was reportedly a bright student with a promising future in the paramedical field. She had been living in the hostel for the past year.

Fellow students have expressed grief and anger, demanding better mental health support systems in the hostel and college. Many have pointed out that the delay in discovering her body could have been avoided if regular attendance checks were more stringent.

Mental Health Concerns on Campus

This incident has once again brought to light the issue of student mental health in Indian educational institutions. Counsellors and experts emphasize the need for proactive measures, including regular check-ins and accessible counselling services.

College authorities have announced a temporary suspension of classes to allow students to grieve. They have also promised a review of hostel safety protocols and the implementation of a more robust system to track student attendance and well-being.

Police have registered a case of unnatural death and are investigating all angles. The post-mortem report is expected to provide clarity on the exact cause and time of death. Officials have not confirmed any foul play at this stage.

As the community reels from this tragedy, the focus now shifts to understanding what led to the student's untimely death and ensuring that such incidents can be prevented in the future. The investigation is ongoing, and more details are likely to emerge in the coming days.
