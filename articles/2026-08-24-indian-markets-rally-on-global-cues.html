Indian markets rally on global cues

📅 2026-08-24 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Indian equities open higher, Sensex gains over 200 points
  • Nifty hovers around 24,300, but shows signs of volatility
  • Analysts warn of a potential correction in the near term

Indian Markets Rally on Global Cues

Indian equities opened higher on Monday, tracking global cues, with the Sensex gaining over 200 points and the Nifty hovering around 24,300. This uptick comes as global markets show signs of recovery, with the US and European markets also posting gains. However, analysts warn that the rally may be short-lived, with potential volatility ahead.

Sensex and Nifty Performance

The Sensex, which had closed at 84,100 points on Friday, opened at 84,350 points, a gain of over 250 points. The Nifty, which had closed at 24,250 points, opened at 24,300 points, a gain of 50 points. While these gains are modest, they are significant given the market's recent volatility.

Analysts Warn of Volatility Ahead

Despite the initial rally, analysts are cautioning investors about the potential for a correction. "The market is showing signs of weakness, and we could see a pullback in the coming days," said a senior analyst at a leading brokerage firm. This caution is based on a number of factors, including rising interest rates, geopolitical tensions, and concerns about economic growth.

Rising Interest Rates

Rising interest rates are a major concern for investors, as they can lead to a decrease in stock prices. The Reserve Bank of India has been increasing interest rates in an effort to combat inflation, and this trend is likely to continue. This could lead to a decrease in the value of equities, despite the initial rally.

Geopolitical Tensions

Geopolitical tensions are also a major concern for investors. Recent events, such as the conflict in the Middle East, have led to concerns about the global economy. This uncertainty can lead to volatility in the markets, making it difficult for investors to make informed decisions.

Economic Growth Concerns

Concerns about economic growth are also a major concern for investors. The Indian economy has been growing at a steady pace, but there are concerns about the sustainability of this growth. If the economy slows down, it could lead to a decrease in the value of equities.
