Ryan ten Doeschate rejoins Knight Riders as Head of Cricket Strategy

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ryan ten Doeschate has been appointed Head of Cricket Strategy for the Knight Riders group.
  • He steps down from his role as assistant coach of the Indian cricket team.
  • The move marks his return to the franchise where he previously served as a player and coach.
  • He will oversee cricket operations across Knight Riders' franchises in multiple leagues.
  • The transition is effective immediately, with a focus on long-term team building.

Ryan ten Doeschate is back with the Knight Riders. The former Netherlands all-rounder has been appointed as the franchise's Head of Cricket Strategy, a move that ends his tenure as an assistant coach with the Indian cricket team.

The Knight Riders group, which owns franchises in the IPL, CPL, and other T20 leagues, confirmed the appointment on Wednesday. Ten Doeschate will oversee the cricketing vision and strategy across all Knight Riders teams.

Strategic shift for the franchise

Ten Doeschate's new role is a step up from his earlier association with the Knight Riders. He previously played for the Kolkata Knight Riders in the IPL and later served as an assistant coach for the franchise. His return is seen as a move to bring long-term stability to the team's talent pipeline and performance structure.

The franchise has been looking to consolidate its cricket operations across geographies. With ten Doeschate at the helm of strategy, the focus will be on scouting, player development, and in-game decision-making frameworks.

Departure from the Indian setup

Ten Doeschate stepped down as an assistant coach of the Indian national team, a role he took on in 2023. His work with the side, particularly during the 2023 ODI World Cup and subsequent tours, earned him praise from players and management alike.

Officials have not yet confirmed the reason for his departure, but sources indicate the Knight Riders role offered a broader, more long-term scope. The BCCI has not announced a replacement for ten Doeschate in the Indian coaching staff.

What the role entails

As Head of Cricket Strategy, ten Doeschate will work closely with the franchise's coaching staff and management. His responsibilities will include shaping the squad composition, identifying young talent, and formulating match-day tactics.

The role also involves coordinating with the analytics and scouting departments. The Knight Riders have invested heavily in data-driven decision-making, and ten Doeschate is expected to integrate these tools into the team's core strategies.

His experience of playing in multiple T20 leagues across the world makes him a natural fit for a franchise with a global footprint. The Knight Riders have teams in the IPL, CPL, and other leagues, and ten Doeschate will have a say in each of those setups.

His appointment underscores a broader trend of former international players moving into high-level administrative and strategic roles within franchise cricket.

The Knight Riders are yet to announce the start date for ten Doeschate's new role, but it is expected to be effective immediately. The franchise will be hoping that his return gives them a competitive edge in the upcoming auctions and tournaments.
