PM Modi's Central Asia Visit: A Strategic Move

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has embarked on a four-day visit to Uzbekistan and the Kyrgyz Republic, marking a significant milestone in India's foreign policy. The visit, which includes participation in the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) summit, underscores India's growing influence in Central Asia and its commitment to regional security and economic cooperation.

Focus on Regional Security

Central Asia has long been a focal point for India's foreign policy, with the region's strategic location and resources making it a key area of interest. PM Modi's visit aims to advance India's vision for a region free from terror, a priority that has been highlighted in recent times. The SCO summit, which will be held in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, is expected to be a key focus of the trip, with discussions on regional security and economic cooperation.

Economic Cooperation: A Key Priority

Economic cooperation is another key priority for PM Modi's visit. Central Asia is home to vast natural resources, and India is keen to strengthen its ties with the region in this area. The visit is expected to pave the way for increased investment and trade between India and Central Asia, with a particular focus on sectors such as energy, infrastructure, and agriculture.
