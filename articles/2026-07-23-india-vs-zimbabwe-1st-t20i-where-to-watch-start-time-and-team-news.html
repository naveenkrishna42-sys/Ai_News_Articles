India vs Zimbabwe 1st T20I: Where to watch, start time, and team news

  • India vs Zimbabwe 1st T20I starts at 4:30 PM IST on July 23, 2026.
  • Live streaming on JioCinema and Fancode; TV broadcast on Sports18.
  • Shreyas Iyer leads India after a 'reality check' in Ireland and England tours.
  • Zimbabwe hope to exploit India's search for a winning combination.

Match details and where to watch

The first T20I between India and Zimbabwe is set for Thursday, July 23, 2026, at the Harare Sports Club. The match will begin at 4:30 PM IST. For viewers in the United States, the start time is 7:00 AM ET. Fans in the UK can tune in at 12:00 PM BST, while Australian audiences will see the action at 9:00 PM AEST.

Indian viewers can watch the match live on the Sports18 network. Streaming options include JioCinema and Fancode. The series marks India's first white-ball assignment after a challenging away season.

India's search for form

India arrive in Zimbabwe after a difficult tour of Ireland and England. The team lost the T20I series in England and could not find a settled playing XI. Captain Shreyas Iyer acknowledged the setbacks, saying the fear of failure has no place in the current squad. The team management has been experimenting with combinations, but consistency has remained elusive.

Iyer himself is eager to put the recent disappointments behind him. He described the England tour as a 'reality check' and stressed the need for a positive mindset. The squad includes several young players looking to cement their spots ahead of future tournaments. Zimbabwe, on the other hand, will see this as an opportunity to test a transitional Indian side.

Zimbabwe's challenge

Zimbabwe have not faced India in a T20I since 2022. They have been preparing with a domestic T20 competition and are expected to field a balanced attack. The home side will rely on spin to trouble Indian batters on a slow Harare pitch. Captain Sikandar Raza will look to exploit any uncertainty in the Indian middle order.

The hosts have not announced their final eleven yet, but all-rounders and spinners are likely to feature prominently. Zimbabwe will aim to put pressure on India early and force mistakes.

Team news and likely lineups

India are expected to stick with Shreyas Iyer at number three. The batting order could see changes with Ruturaj Gaikwad and Yashasvi Jaiswal opening. In the bowling department, Arshdeep Singh and Ravi Bishnoi are likely to lead the attack. The management has hinted at giving game time to fringe players over the series.

Zimbabwe will miss some regulars due to injury, but have called up promising youngsters. The team is expected to include Blessing Muzarabani and Richard Ngarava in the pace attack. Wellington Masakadza may provide spin options in the middle overs.

What to watch for

The series offers India a chance to reset after a poor run. All eyes will be on how Shreyas Iyer handles captaincy pressure and whether the batting lineup can fire collectively. Zimbabwe will look to make a statement at home. The match is expected to be a tight contest on a surface that may slow down as the game progresses.
