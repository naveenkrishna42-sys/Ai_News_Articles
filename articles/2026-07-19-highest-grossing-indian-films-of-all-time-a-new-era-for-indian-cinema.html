Highest-Grossing Indian Films of All Time: A New Era for Indian Cinema

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Baahubali 2: The Conclusion remains the highest-grossing Indian film worldwide, with over ₹1,800 crore.
  • Dangal, starring Aamir Khan, had a massive global run, earning over ₹2,000 crore, aided by its success in China.
  • Films from multiple Indian languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and Malayalam, have achieved record-breaking box office numbers.

The Titans of the Box Office

Indian cinema has never been bigger. Over the past decade, a handful of films have shattered records and redefined what it means to be a blockbuster in the country. The list of highest-grossing Indian films is now a mix of spectacle, emotion, and cross-border appeal.

At the very top sits S. S. Rajamouli's Baahubali 2: The Conclusion. Released in 2017, the Telugu-language epic earned over ₹1,800 crore worldwide. It set a benchmark that few have come close to matching.

Close behind is Dangal, the Aamir Khan-starrer about wrestler Mahavir Singh Phogat. Its domestic earnings were strong, but its release in China pushed the film to over ₹2,000 crore globally. That success opened the door for other Indian films in the Chinese market.

The Pan-India Phenomenon

Rajamouli struck again with RRR in 2022. The film grossed over ₹1,200 crore worldwide, winning over audiences in India and abroad. Its soundtrack and action sequences became cultural touchstones.

Other films have also crossed the ₹1,000 crore mark. K.G.F: Chapter 2, starring Yash, brought Kannada cinema to the national stage. Pathaan, Shah Rukh Khan's comeback vehicle, collected over ₹1,050 crore in 2023. Jawan, also starring Khan, followed a similar trajectory.

Hindi films like Baahubali 2 (dubbed version), Dangal, and Sanju have performed well. But the list is increasingly dominated by Telugu and Tamil productions, reflecting a shift in audience taste.

Global Reach and the China Factor

The single biggest factor behind these record collections has been the Chinese market. Dangal earned nearly ₹1,300 crore in China alone. Baahubali 2 and RRR also saw significant returns from international markets, including the United States and the Middle East.

Indian films now consistently open in over 50 countries. The diaspora, along with growing interest from non-Indian audiences, has made global box office numbers a key metric for success.

Not all films have benefited equally. Some big-budget releases have failed to recover costs, while mid-budget films have punched above their weight. The trend shows that content, not just star power, drives ticket sales.

What the List Reveals

The highest-grossing Indian films list is dominated by a few directors and actors. Rajamouli has three films in the top ten. Aamir Khan and Shah Rukh Khan have multiple entries. South Indian stars like Prabhas, Yash, and Vijay have become national names.

Language barriers have broken down. Dubbed releases in Hindi have become standard for Telugu and Tamil films. This has expanded the potential audience for any single film to over 500 million people.

Inflation and rising ticket prices also play a role. A film earning ₹1,000 crore today would have been unthinkable a decade ago. But adjusted for inflation, some older hits like Sholay or Mughal-e-Azam would still rank high.

Box office numbers are often disputed. Producers may inflate figures, and tracking agencies use different methodologies. The exact ranking can vary depending on the source. But the overall picture is clear: Indian cinema is in a golden age of commercial success.

Industry analysts expect more records to fall. With rising incomes, more screens in small towns, and growing international interest, the next ₹2,000 crore film could be just a few years away. The race is on.
