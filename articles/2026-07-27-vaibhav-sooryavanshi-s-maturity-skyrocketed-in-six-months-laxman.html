Vaibhav Sooryavanshi's maturity 'skyrocketed' in six months: Laxman

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Vaibhav Sooryavanshi played a mature innings focused on progress over power in the series finale.
  • India completed a 3-0 T20I series sweep against Zimbabwe.
  • Batting coach VVS Laxman noted Sooryavanshi's maturity has 'skyrocketed' in the last six months.

India's under-19 batting sensation Vaibhav Sooryavanshi has drawn high praise from head coach VVS Laxman after his match-winning knock helped India complete a 3-0 T20I series sweep against Zimbabwe. Laxman, known for his own elegant strokeplay, said the 15-year-old's maturity has 'skyrocketed' in the last six months.

Maturity beyond his years

Sooryavanshi, who turned 15 earlier this year, played an innings that was more about progress than power. He paced his knock carefully, rotating strike and punishing loose deliveries without taking unnecessary risks.

Laxman, who has worked closely with the youngster at the National Cricket Academy, said the transformation has been remarkable. "The way he has matured in the last six months is unbelievable. It has skyrocketed," Laxman told reporters after the match.

The batting coach emphasised that Sooryavanshi's temperament stood out. "He doesn't get flustered. He reads the game situation well and adjusts his game accordingly. That is a rare quality in a player his age," Laxman added.

Series sweep and record feat

India's 3-0 sweep of Zimbabwe was built around strong performances from both established stars and emerging talents. Shreyas Iyer found some form, providing solace for the team management after a lean patch.

Sooryavanshi also scripted history during the series. He became the first player to achieve a unique T20I feat before turning 16, according to The Times of India. The specific record was not detailed, but it underscores the extraordinary nature of his rise.

The young batter's approach in the final match drew particular attention. Instead of trying to dominate from the start, he built his innings methodically, ensuring India did not lose early wickets while chasing a modest target.

Progress over power

Cricinfo noted that Sooryavanshi's innings was more about progress than power. He rotated the strike effectively and found boundaries at regular intervals without forcing the issue.

This tactical maturity is what impressed Laxman the most. "Six months ago, he would have tried to hit every ball. Now he understands when to attack and when to consolidate. That growth is phenomenal," Laxman said.

India's bowling unit also played a key role in the series win, restricting Zimbabwe to below-par totals in all three matches. The combination of disciplined pace and clever spin proved too much for the hosts.

What lies ahead

Sooryavanshi is expected to be part of India's plans for the upcoming Under-19 World Cup. His rapid development has also sparked discussions about a potential senior team call-up in the near future.

The selectors and team management will watch his progress closely. For now, the teenager from Bihar has shown that age is just a number when talent meets the right guidance.
