Redmi Note 17 India Launch Set for August 6

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Redmi Note 17 5G launch date confirmed for August 6 in India.
  • Full specifications of the Redmi 17 4G have leaked ahead of launch.
  • India price leak suggests the Redmi Note 17 will compete aggressively in the mid-range segment.
  • European prices for the Note 17, Note 17 Pro, and Note 17 Pro Max have reportedly leaked.

Redmi Note 17 India Launch Confirmed

Xiaomi has officially confirmed the launch date for its next-generation Redmi Note 17 series in India. The company will unveil the Redmi Note 17 5G on August 6, 2026.

The announcement comes via a teaser on Xiaomi India's social media channels. The event is expected to be a virtual launch, similar to previous Redmi Note series reveals.

Leaked Specifications and Price Details

Multiple leaks have surfaced in recent days, painting a detailed picture of what to expect. The Redmi 17 4G's full specifications have leaked via Gizmochina, suggesting a MediaTek Helio G-series chipset and a 5,000mAh battery with 33W fast charging.

For the Redmi Note 17 5G, leaks from Sportskeeda Tech point to an India price that could start around Rs 15,999. This would position it directly against competitors like the Realme Narzo 70 and the Samsung Galaxy M series.

The European prices for the entire lineup—including the Note 17, Note 17 Pro, and Note 17 Pro Max—have also reportedly leaked via Gadgets 360. The Pro variants are expected to feature higher-refresh-rate AMOLED displays and upgraded camera sensors.

Specs at a Glance

  • Redmi Note 17 5G: Expected 6.67-inch FHD+ display, Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset, 50MP primary camera, 5,000mAh battery.
  • Redmi 17 4G: Leaked with a MediaTek Helio G99, 90Hz display, and a 108MP main camera.
  • Pro and Pro Max variants: Could offer 120W fast charging and periscope telephoto lenses, according to European leaks.

Market Context and Competition

The Redmi Note series has been a mainstay in the Indian mid-range segment for years. The Note 17 arrives at a time when competition from Realme, iQOO, and Samsung is intensifying.

Xiaomi has been working to reclaim its market share after facing challenges from offline-focused brands. The Note 17 series is expected to be a key driver for the company's second-half sales.

FoneArena reports that the Redmi Note 17 5G will be available via Amazon India and Mi.com starting August 6. Flash sales are likely to follow within the same month.

What to Watch Next

All eyes will be on the pricing of the Pro and Pro Max variants when they launch globally. Xiaomi has not yet confirmed their India availability. The company is expected to reveal more details during the August 6 event.
