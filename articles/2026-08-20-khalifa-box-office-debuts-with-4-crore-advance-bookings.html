Khalifa box office debuts with 4 crore advance bookings

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Khalifa crosses 4 crore in worldwide advance bookings
  • Lags 27% behind Aadujeevitham in Kerala
  • Mixed reviews from critics and audience

Box Office Debut

Prithviraj Sukumaran's 'Khalifa' has made a strong start at the box office, crossing 4 crore in worldwide advance bookings. The film, which stars Sukumaran alongside Mohanlal, has generated significant interest from audiences across the country. However, it lags 27% behind the Malayalam film 'Aadujeevitham' in Kerala, which has been performing exceptionally well in the state.

Critical Reception

The film has received mixed reviews from critics and audience alike. Koimoi's review describes it as a shoddily written old-school smuggling drama, while The Hindu notes that there is 'no emotional connection' in the film. NDTV's review calls it a hollow spectacle that even Mohanlal can't save, while The Indian Express describes it as an uneven, average ride that has disappointed audiences.

Market Performance

Khalifa's box office performance has been strong in the initial days, with advance bookings crossing 4 crore. The film has been well-received in cities like Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore, where audiences have shown a keen interest in the film. However, its performance in Kerala has been disappointing, with the film lagging behind 'Aadujeevitham'.

Future Prospects

The film's box office performance in the coming days will be crucial in determining its overall success. With mixed reviews and a strong initial performance, Khalifa will need to perform well in the next few days to recover its losses and achieve a successful box office run.
