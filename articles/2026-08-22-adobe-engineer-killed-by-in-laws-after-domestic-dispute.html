Adobe engineer killed by in-laws after domestic dispute

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • 31-year-old Adobe engineer beaten to death by in-laws in Delhi
  • Dispute with wife led to family intervention
  • Four individuals arrested in connection with the murder

Tech Professional Beaten to Death by In-Laws

A 31-year-old software engineer working at Adobe was beaten to death by his wife's family in Delhi after a domestic dispute. The incident occurred on Friday night in the capital's south-east district. Police have arrested four individuals in connection with the murder.

Argument with Wife

The victim, identified as Rajiv Kumar, had an argument with his wife, which escalated into a heated dispute. After the argument, Rajiv's wife reportedly called her family members, who arrived at the scene to intervene. According to witnesses, the situation turned violent, leading to Rajiv being beaten severely.

Family Intervention

Rajiv's wife's family members, who were present at the scene, allegedly joined in the assault. The victim sustained fatal injuries and was pronounced dead at a nearby hospital. The police have registered a case of murder under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code.

Arrests Made

Following the incident, the police launched an investigation and arrested four individuals, including Rajiv's wife's brothers and cousins. The arrested individuals have been taken into custody for further interrogation. The police are reportedly looking into the possibility of more arrests as the investigation progresses.

Community Reaction

The incident has sparked outrage among the community, with many expressing shock and condemning the brutal act. Social media platforms have been flooded with posts condemning the violence and demanding strict action against the culprits. Local leaders have also urged the police to ensure swift justice for the victim.
