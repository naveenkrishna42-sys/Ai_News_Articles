8 Assamese found in Nepal floods; search continues

Eight Assamese Individuals Located in Nepal Floods

Eight Assamese individuals who went missing in the recent Nepal floods have been traced. The search for more missing persons continues as rescue operations intensify. The floods, triggered by heavy monsoon rains, have affected over 1 million people in Nepal, leaving a trail of destruction in their wake.

Rescue Efforts Underway

Rescue teams from Nepal and neighboring countries are working around the clock to locate and assist those affected by the floods. However, the task is proving to be challenging due to the sheer scale of the disaster and the ongoing heavy rains which have caused landslides and blocked roads.

Impact on Mount Kailash Pilgrimage Site

The floods have also affected the Mount Kailash pilgrimage site, a significant religious site for Hindus. The site is located in the Tibet Autonomous Region of China, near the Nepal border. The floods have caused damage to infrastructure and disrupted the pilgrimage routes, affecting thousands of devotees.

International Community Urged to Assist

Manisha Koirala, a prominent figure in Nepal's political landscape, has appealed to the international community to assist in the relief efforts. She has urged that the pain caused by the floods not be lessened, and has called for a reduction in tourism to the affected areas. However, she has also emphasized the need for international support to help Nepal recover from the disaster.
