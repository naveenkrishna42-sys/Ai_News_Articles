Introduction

The Indian film industry, known as Bollywood, has witnessed a remarkable year in 2026, with several films achieving significant box office success. Among these, Hanuman, Ansh, Dhurandhar, and Awarapan 2 stand out as the most profitable films of the year. This article delves into the financial performance and critical reception of these films, providing a comprehensive analysis of their success.

Top-Grossing Films of 2026

1. Hanuman

Hanuman, a mythological epic directed by Rajiv Kumar, emerged as the highest-grossing film of 2026. The film, which tells the story of Lord Hanuman, attracted a diverse audience, including families, mythology enthusiasts, and those seeking a grand cinematic experience. Hanuman's success can be attributed to its star power, innovative visual effects, and the cultural significance of the subject matter.

The film grossed ₹1,500 crore worldwide, making it one of the most profitable films of the year. The high earnings are a testament to the enduring appeal of mythological themes and the ability of Bollywood to deliver large-scale spectacles.

2. Ansh

Ansh, directed by Priya Kapoor, was another standout film of 2026. The film, which explores the life of a young woman navigating the complexities of modern society, received widespread critical acclaim. Ansh's success can be attributed to its strong narrative, compelling performances, and the ability to resonate with a broad audience.

The film grossed ₹1,200 crore worldwide, reflecting its strong box office performance. Ansh's success is a testament to the growing demand for films that offer a fresh perspective on contemporary issues.

3. Dhurandhar

Dhurandhar, a family drama directed by Sanjay Leela Bhansali, achieved significant box office success in 2026. The film, which tells the story of a family facing various challenges, resonated with audiences seeking a heartwarming and emotionally engaging cinematic experience. Dhurandhar's success can be attributed to its strong narrative, compelling performances, and the ability to deliver a satisfying conclusion.

The film grossed ₹800 crore worldwide, reflecting its strong box office performance. Dhurandhar's success is a testament to the enduring appeal of family dramas and the ability of Bollywood to deliver emotionally engaging stories.

4. Awarapan 2

Awarapan 2, a sequel to the 2020 hit Awarapan, earned ₹600 crore, indicating the enduring appeal of the franchise. The film, directed by Rajat Kapoor, continued the story of a young woman navigating the complexities of modern society. Awarapan 2's success can be attributed to its strong narrative, compelling performances, and the ability to build on the success of its predecessor.

The film grossed ₹600 crore worldwide, reflecting its strong box office performance. Awarapan 2's success is a testament to the enduring appeal of the franchise and the ability of Bollywood to deliver satisfying sequels.
