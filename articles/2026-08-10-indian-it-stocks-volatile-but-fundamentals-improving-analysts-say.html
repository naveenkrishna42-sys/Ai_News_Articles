Indian IT stocks volatile but fundamentals improving, analysts say

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Indian IT stocks are volatile, but fundamentals are improving underneath.
  • Nasscom chief warns 20-25% of IT firms could struggle with AI transition.
  • Nuvama predicts $300-400 billion market expansion for Indian IT services, with Gen AI not an existential threat.
  • Vertical AI-native services are emerging as a new growth area in India.

Volatility masks a stronger core

Indian IT stocks have been on a rollercoaster ride in recent sessions, with investor sentiment swinging between fear and optimism. Yet, beneath the surface-level turbulence, analysts point to improving fundamentals that could redefine the sector's trajectory.

The volatility comes amid a global tech slowdown and shifting client budgets. But several brokerages and industry watchers argue that the underlying demand for digital transformation and cost optimisation remains robust, even as the landscape evolves.

Nasscom's stark warning on AI transition

Nasscom chief Rajesh Nambiar has sounded a cautionary note, saying that artificial intelligence may redraw India's IT services map. He warned that 20-25% of firms could struggle to cross over to the AI-driven era, as they lack the necessary capabilities or scale.

This is not a doomsday prediction, but a call for urgency. The industry body's chief emphasised that the transition to AI is not optional; it is a survival imperative. Firms that fail to adapt risk being left behind as clients increasingly demand AI-integrated solutions.

Nambiar's comments come at a time when several mid-tier IT companies are already feeling the pinch. Their traditional delivery models are being disrupted by automation, and their ability to invest in AI research and skilling is limited compared to larger peers.

Nuvama sees a $300-400 billion opportunity

Contrasting with the caution, Nuvama Research has released a bullish note, asserting that generative AI is not an existential threat to Indian IT services. Instead, it predicts a market expansion of $300-400 billion for the sector, driven by new service lines and productivity gains.

The brokerage argues that AI will augment, not replace, the core value proposition of Indian IT firms. By embedding AI into existing offerings, companies can deliver faster, cheaper, and more intelligent solutions, thereby expanding their addressable market.

This view aligns with the growing adoption of AI in enterprise workflows. Indian IT firms are increasingly positioning themselves as partners in this transformation, rather than mere vendors of legacy services.

The rise of vertical AI-native services

Bessemer Venture Partners has highlighted a emerging trend: vertical AI-native services in India. These are specialised solutions tailored to specific industries like healthcare, finance, and retail, built from the ground up with AI at their core.

This shift is significant because it moves beyond horizontal IT services to domain-specific expertise. Early-stage startups and even some established firms are leveraging India's engineering talent to build proprietary AI models that solve industry-specific problems.

For investors, this represents a new frontier. The valuation of such firms could diverge sharply from traditional IT services, offering both higher growth and higher risk.

Market reaction and what to watch

Despite the mixed signals, the Nifty IT index has shown resilience, with some stocks rebounding after initial sell-offs. The underlying fundamentals—order books, deal pipelines, and margin stability—are reportedly stronger than the headline volatility suggests.

However, the road ahead is not without challenges. Global macroeconomic headwinds, currency fluctuations, and the pace of AI adoption will all play a role. Investors are advised to focus on companies with clear AI strategies and robust balance sheets.

As the sector navigates this transition, the next few quarters will be crucial. Watch for earnings calls and management guidance on AI investments, as well as any major deal announcements that signal client confidence.
