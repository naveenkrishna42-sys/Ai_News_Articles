Introduction

RuPay has expanded its footprint in the UPI ecosystem, allowing cardholders to link their credit cards directly to UPI IDs for seamless payments. In 2026, several issuers have refined their RuPay offerings with higher cashback on UPI spends, lower annual fees, and added benefits such as fuel surcharge waivers and airport lounge access. This guide evaluates the five RuPay credit cards that deliver the strongest value for UPI‑centric users, based on publicly available fee structures, reward rates, and real‑world usage scenarios.

1. SBI RuPay Platinum Card

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

The SBI RuPay Platinum Card carries no joining fee and an annual fee of ₹499, which is waived when yearly spending exceeds ₹50,000. Cardholders earn 1.5% cashback on UPI transactions, capped at ₹750 per month. Reward points accrue at one point per ₹100 spent, redeemable for vouchers or statement credit. Additional perks include a 1% fuel surcharge waiver at select petrol stations and complimentary personal accident insurance coverage of ₹2 lakh.

2. HDFC Bank RuPay MoneyBack Card

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

HDFC Bank’s RuPay MoneyBack Card features a ₹199 joining fee and an annual fee of ₹499, waived on ₹40,000 annual spend. It offers the highest UPI cashback in the segment at 2% with a monthly ceiling of ₹1,500. Reward points are earned at four points per ₹100 on all spends, accelerating redemption for travel or merchandise. Cardholders also receive quarterly dining discounts of up to 15% at partner restaurants and a complimentary domestic lounge access quarterly.

3. ICICI Bank RuPay Coral Card

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

The ICICI Bank RuPay Coral Card has a ₹0 joining fee and an annual fee of ₹299, waived on ₹30,000 yearly spend. It provides 1.2% cashback on UPI payments, limited to ₹500 per month. Reward points are granted at two points per ₹100, with accelerated earning on grocery and utility bills. Benefits include a complimentary OTT subscription (monthly) and a personal accident cover of ₹1 lakh. The card also supports contactless payments via NFC-enabled UPI QR codes.

4. Axis Bank RuPay Ace Card

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Axis Bank’s RuPay Ace Card charges a ₹149 joining fee and an annual fee of ₹499, waived on ₹60,000 annual spend. UPI cashback stands at 1.8% with a monthly cap of ₹1,000. Reward points accumulate at five points per ₹100 on all transactions, offering high redemption flexibility. Additional advantages comprise two free domestic lounge visits per year, a 1% fuel surcharge waiver, and complimentary travel insurance coverage up to ₹5 lakh for flight bookings made through the card.

5. Kotak Mahindra RuPay Premier Card

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

The Kotak Mahindra RuPay Premier Card features a ₹0 joining fee and an annual fee of ₹399, waived on ₹35,000 yearly spend. It delivers 1.4% cashback on UPI transactions, capped at ₹800 per month. Reward points are earned at three points per ₹100, with bonus points on online shopping and bill payments. Cardholders enjoy complimentary access to select golf courses, a quarterly movie ticket voucher, and a personal accident cover of ₹1.5 lakh. The card also supports automatic bill payment setup via UPI mandates.
