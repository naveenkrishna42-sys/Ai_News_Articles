The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered Goes Free

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered is set to become free to download starting September 2026. The game, which originally launched in 2015, has been remastered with improved graphics and gameplay mechanics. This move by CD Projekt Red aims to celebrate the game's 10th anniversary and attract new players.
