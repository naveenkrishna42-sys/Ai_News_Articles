Fire Breaks Out at Saudi Aramco Refinery in Saudi Arabia

RIYADH: A fire broke out on Sunday at a Saudi Aramco refinery, prompting an immediate response from emergency teams. The incident occurred at one of the state-owned oil giant's processing facilities, though the exact location has not been officially disclosed.

Videos and images circulating on social media showed thick plumes of black smoke rising from the site, drawing attention from local residents and international observers alike. Saudi Aramco, the world's largest oil producer, has yet to release an official statement detailing the cause of the blaze or the extent of the damage.

Emergency Response Underway

Civil defence units were dispatched to the refinery shortly after the fire was reported. Their priority has been to contain the flames and prevent them from spreading to adjacent storage tanks or production units. Witnesses described a heavy presence of fire trucks and ambulances at the scene.

Officials have not confirmed any casualties so far. Workers at the facility were reportedly evacuated as a precaution, but there has been no official word on their status. The company is expected to issue a formal update once the situation is stabilised.

No Immediate Impact on Output Confirmed

Analysts are closely watching whether the fire will affect Saudi Aramco's crude processing capacity. The kingdom is a key supplier to global energy markets, and any disruption could have ripple effects on oil prices. However, no official figures have been released regarding production losses.

Any major incident could invite scrutiny over safety protocols at these critical installations.
