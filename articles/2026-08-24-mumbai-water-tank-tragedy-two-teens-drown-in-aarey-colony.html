Tragic Incident at Aarey Colony Water Tank

Two teenage boys drowned in a water tank at Mumbai's Aarey Colony, a suburb known for its dense population and infrastructure challenges. The incident occurred on a rainy afternoon, when the water tank was reportedly overflowing due to heavy rains. The boys, aged 14 and 15, were reportedly playing near the tank when they fell in and could not swim to safety.
