Benchmark leak signals strong performance

A new Lenovo Legion gaming tablet featuring an OLED display has reportedly posted an impressive AnTuTu benchmark score, according to Notebookcheck. The score suggests the device is being tuned for high-end gaming performance, with expectations building around its official unveiling.

The tablet is widely believed to be the Lenovo Legion Y700 Infinite 5G, which is slated to launch in China on August 25. The device is expected to pack Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 system-on-chip, a processor designed to handle demanding games and multitasking with ease.

What the AnTuTu score means

AnTuTu is a popular benchmarking tool used to measure a device's overall performance, including CPU, GPU, memory, and user experience. A high score typically indicates that the hardware is capable of running graphics-intensive games and apps smoothly.

While the exact figure has not been officially confirmed by Lenovo, industry watchers note that the score places the tablet in the upper tier of Android devices. This would make it a strong contender for gamers who prefer a compact yet powerful slate.

Design and display upgrades

Earlier reports, including one from Mashable, have highlighted that the upcoming Lenovo Legion Y700 AI will feature a slimmer body and lighter design compared to its predecessor. The shift to an OLED panel is a notable upgrade, as it promises deeper blacks, better contrast, and more vibrant colours — key features for gaming visuals.

The tablet is expected to retain the Legion series' focus on portability, with a display size that balances screen real estate with ease of handling. The combination of OLED technology and a high-refresh-rate panel could make it a preferred choice for competitive gamers.

Broader Lenovo tablet push

This development comes as Lenovo appears to be doubling down on its tablet lineup. Reports from CNBC TV18 and other outlets have mentioned a 9-speaker tablet, a gaming slate with a 25% discount, and a discounted 140W power bank, indicating a wider strategy to capture different segments of the market.

The Legion brand, known for gaming laptops and accessories, is increasingly being leveraged for high-performance tablets. With the Y700 Infinite 5G, Lenovo is aiming to offer 5G connectivity alongside top-tier specs, making it a versatile option for on-the-go gaming and streaming.

What to expect at launch

Lenovo has not yet confirmed all specifications, including the exact AnTuTu score, battery capacity, or pricing for the Indian market. The August 25 launch in China will likely reveal full details, including availability in other regions.

For now, gamers and tech enthusiasts will be watching closely to see if the OLED Legion tablet lives up to its benchmark promise. The device could set a new standard for compact gaming tablets, but its real-world performance and software optimisation will be key factors in its success.
