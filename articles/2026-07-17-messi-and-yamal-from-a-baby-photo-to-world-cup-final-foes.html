Messi and Yamal: From a baby photo to World Cup final foes

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 2007 photo of Messi bathing a baby Yamal at Camp Nou has resurfaced ahead of the final.
  • Both players have reached the World Cup final after standout performances in their semi-finals.
  • The match marks a generational shift as Yamal, 19, faces Messi, 39, in his likely last World Cup.

Photo from 2007 resurfaces

A photograph taken nearly two decades ago at Camp Nou has become the defining image of this World Cup. In 2007, a young Lionel Messi was photographed bathing a baby Lamine Yamal during a charity calendar shoot for UNICEF. That baby is now 19 and set to face Messi in the FIFA World Cup 2026 final.

The image went viral after Yamal's emergence as a star for Spain. Fans on social media quickly connected the dots. Now, with both teams reaching the final, the photo has taken on an almost prophetic quality.

How they got here

Argentina reached the final after a tense semi-final win over Brazil. Messi, now 39, has been the team's creative engine throughout the tournament. He has scored four goals and provided three assists in six matches.

Spain, led by Yamal on the wing, defeated Germany in their semi-final. The teenager has been one of the breakout stars of the tournament. His dribbling, pace and vision have drawn comparisons to a young Messi himself.

Generational shift in focus

The final is being billed as a passing of the torch. Messi, in what is likely his last World Cup appearance, faces a player who was a baby when he was at his peak. Yamal has spoken about Messi as his idol, calling the encounter a dream.

Messi, for his part, has been diplomatic. He acknowledged the photo in a press conference, saying it was a nice coincidence. He added that Yamal has a bright future but that he wants to win the match.

What the final means

For Argentina, a win would make them back-to-back world champions. They won in 2022 in Qatar. For Spain, a victory would mark their second World Cup title, the first coming in 2010.

The match is also a clash of styles. Argentina relies on Messi's creativity and a solid defence. Spain plays a possession-based game, with Yamal providing width and unpredictability. Both teams have scored 11 goals so far in the tournament.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact start time for the final. It will be played at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey. The venue has a capacity of over 82,000 and is expected to be sold out.

What to watch: All eyes will be on the moment the two players shake hands before kickoff. The photo from 2007 will be shown on screen. How both men handle the narrative around it could shape the game.
