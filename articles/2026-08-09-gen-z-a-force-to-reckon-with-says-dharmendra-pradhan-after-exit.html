Gen Z a force to reckon with, says Dharmendra Pradhan after exit

📅 2026-08-09 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pradhan breaks silence on resignation, says Gen Z cannot be ignored
  • Alleges attempts to mislead young voters during student protests
  • Reacts to Naveen Patnaik's remarks, vows never to disgrace Odisha

New Delhi: Days after stepping down as Union Education Minister, Dharmendra Pradhan has broken his silence, describing Gen Z as an unstoppable force that politicians ignore at their own peril. In a series of media interactions, Pradhan addressed his resignation, the student protests that marked his tenure, and the political barbs traded with Odisha's former chief minister Naveen Patnaik.

'Some people tried to mislead Gen Z'

Pradhan said the youth, particularly those belonging to the Gen Z cohort, have emerged as a decisive factor in the country's political and social discourse. "Gen Z is a force that can't be ignored," he asserted, while cautioning that certain vested interests had attempted to steer their anger in a particular direction.

"Some people tried to mislead Gen Z," he said, referring to the recent student protests that rattled the education ministry. Pradhan suggested that the agitation was not entirely organic, with elements seeking to exploit genuine concerns for narrow political gains. He did not name any individual or organisation, but the remarks come amid intense speculation over the circumstances surrounding his exit.

Resignation and the road ahead

The former minister, however, remained tight-lipped about the specific reasons for his resignation, saying only that he acted in accordance with his conscience and the larger interest of the nation. Political observers note that his departure from the high-profile ministry was sudden, prompting questions about internal party dynamics and policy differences.

Pradhan's tenure as education minister was marked by several contentious decisions, including the implementation of new curriculum frameworks and the handling of examination reforms. While he defended his record, he acknowledged that communication gaps may have contributed to the unrest among students. "I have always believed in dialogue, but perhaps we could have done more to address the anxieties of the youth," he said.

War of words with Naveen Patnaik

The interview also saw Pradhan hit back at Naveen Patnaik, his former mentor turned political rival in Odisha. Patnaik had earlier made remarks that appeared to question Pradhan's loyalty to his home state. Responding sharply, Pradhan said, "I will never disgrace Odisha."

The exchange underscores the bitter political rivalry in Odisha, where Pradhan has emerged as a key BJP face against Patnaik's regional outfit. Pradhan's assertion was seen as an attempt to reaffirm his roots, even as he plays a larger role in national politics.

What lies ahead

As Pradhan transitions to a new role, all eyes are on his next assignment within the party or the government. His comments on Gen Z signal that he intends to stay relevant in the youth-centric political narrative, especially with elections looming in several states.

For now, the former minister's message is clear: the youth will shape India's future, and those who try to mislead them will be held accountable. Whether his resignation marks the end of a chapter or the beginning of a new political innings remains to be seen.
