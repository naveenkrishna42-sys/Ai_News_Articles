Controversial Answer Key Sparks Debate

Uttarakhand University's recent answer key has sparked a heated debate after declaring the crow as India's national bird. The decision has left many questioning the university's understanding of India's rich cultural heritage and national symbols.

Why the Controversy?

The declaration of the crow as India's national bird has raised eyebrows among experts and the public alike. While the crow is a common sight in urban areas, it is not widely recognized as a symbol of India's cultural heritage. The decision has been seen as a misrepresentation of the country's national identity.

Expert Opinions

Bird conservationist Dr. Anil Kapoor expressed his concerns, stating, "The crow is a common bird found in many parts of the world, and it is not unique to India. It is important to recognize and promote birds that are indigenous to our country."

Historian Dr. Priya Sharma added, "India has a rich cultural heritage, and it is crucial to promote our national symbols. The peacock, with its vibrant colors and graceful dance, is a perfect representation of India's cultural diversity."

Broader Implications

The debate over the crow as India's national bird highlights the importance of cultural heritage and national symbols. It is essential to recognize and promote the birds that are indigenous to our country and hold significant cultural and historical value.

Key Facts

  • The peacock, also known as the Indian peafowl, is widely recognized as India's national bird.
