East Bengal 4-1 Al-Arabi: Red and Gold Brigade storm into AFC Champions League Two group stage

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • East Bengal defeated Al-Arabi 4-1 in the AFC Champions League Two preliminary knockout.
  • The Red and Gold Brigade staged a brilliant comeback after conceding an early goal.
  • The win secures East Bengal's place in the group stage of the continental competition.

Historic Night in Kolkata

East Bengal produced a sensational second-half display to overpower Qatar's Al-Arabi 4-1 in the AFC Champions League Two preliminary knockout on Wednesday. The Red and Gold Brigade, trailing early, turned the match on its head with a flurry of goals that sent the home supporters into raptures.

The victory not only avenged the pre-match doubts but also secured a coveted spot in the group stage of Asia's second-tier club competition. For a club with such rich history, this marks a significant step forward in their continental journey.

Slow Start, Stunning Finish

Al-Arabi drew first blood, catching the East Bengal defence off guard with an early strike. The visitors looked composed, controlling possession and forcing the Indian side to chase the game.

But East Bengal, buoyed by the vociferous home crowd, refused to buckle. The equaliser came just before the hour mark, and from there, the floodgates opened. A quickfire double put the hosts in command, and a late fourth goal sealed a comprehensive win that few had predicted given the team's limited preparation.

Comeback Built on Grit

The Telegraph had earlier reported that East Bengal lacked enough alternatives in their squad, yet the team remained upbeat ahead of the clash. That optimism proved well-founded as the players dug deep, showing remarkable fitness and tactical discipline in the final half-hour.

The All India Football Federation hailed the result as a "sensational" comeback, with the ISL champions now turning their attention to the group stage. For a side that has often struggled to balance domestic and continental commitments, this performance signals a new-found maturity.

What This Means for Indian Football

East Bengal's progression is a boost for Indian club football, which has seen sporadic success in Asian competitions. The win over a Qatari side—teams from West Asia have traditionally dominated such matchups—adds credibility to the ISL's growing reputation.

The group stage draw will be keenly awaited, with East Bengal likely to face stronger opposition from across the continent. But for now, the Red and Gold faithful can savour a night that reaffirmed their club's place on the Asian map.

Looking Ahead

East Bengal will now prepare for the group stage draw, scheduled in the coming weeks. The team's management is expected to bolster the squad during the transfer window to cope with the demands of a continental campaign.

For the players, the immediate focus shifts to maintaining this momentum in the domestic league. But Wednesday's heroics will not be forgotten soon—this was a statement of intent.
