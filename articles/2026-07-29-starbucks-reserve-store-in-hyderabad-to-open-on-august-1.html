Starbucks Reserve store in Hyderabad to open on August 1

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • South India's first Starbucks Reserve store will open in Hyderabad on August 1.
  • The store will offer rare and exclusive coffee blends not available at regular outlets.
  • The move signals Starbucks' strategy to expand premium offerings in Indian markets.

Hyderabad gets a premium coffee destination

Starbucks is set to open South India's first Reserve store in Hyderabad on August 1. The announcement was made by the company on Wednesday, marking a significant step in its premium expansion strategy in the region.

The Reserve store will be located in the city's upscale Banjara Hills area. It will offer a curated menu featuring rare and exclusive coffee blends sourced from around the world.

What makes a Reserve store different

Starbucks Reserve stores are a premium format that focuses on small-lot, rare coffees and artisanal brewing methods. Unlike regular outlets, these stores often serve limited-edition single-origin beans and offer a more immersive coffee experience.

Baristas at Reserve stores are trained to use manual brewing techniques such as pour-over, siphon, and cold brew on tap. Customers can also purchase whole-bean coffee and merchandise unique to the Reserve line.

Expansion plans in South India

Hyderabad was chosen for the first Reserve store in South India due to its growing consumer base and demand for premium experiences. The company already operates several regular Starbucks outlets in the city.

Starbucks has been expanding its Reserve footprint globally, with stores in cities like Seattle, Shanghai, and Tokyo. The Hyderabad outlet will be the second Reserve store in India, after one in Mumbai.

Officials have not yet confirmed whether more Reserve stores will open in other South Indian cities. However, the company has indicated that the format is part of its long-term growth strategy in the country.

What to expect on opening day

The store will open its doors at 10 am on August 1. Early visitors can expect special promotions and a chance to sample exclusive Reserve blends.

Starbucks has not disclosed the exact investment or the number of employees at the new store. The outlet will have a seating capacity of around 50 guests.

The launch comes as the coffee chain continues to compete with local players and other international brands in India's rapidly growing café market.

Industry watchers will be keen to see how the Reserve format performs in Hyderabad, a city known for its tech-driven, young population with a taste for premium products.
