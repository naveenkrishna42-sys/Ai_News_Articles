Cyclone Akvan Crosses Odisha Coast Near Gopalpur, No Damage Reported

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Desk 📅 2026-11-21 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Cyclone Akvan crossed the Odisha coast near Gopalpur in Ganjam district and weakened rapidly after landfall, with no major damage or loss of life reported by the state government.
  • Rail and air travel took the biggest hit, with Vande Bharat services cancelled and IndiGo and Air India issuing passenger advisories for Bhubaneswar and Kolkata sectors.
  • Heavy rain shifted north-east into Srikakulam in Andhra Pradesh and parts of coastal West Bengal, where the Chief Minister reviewed district-level preparedness.
  • Pre-positioned NDRF and ODRAF teams plus early warnings kept casualty figures at zero, the clearest measure of how Odisha's disaster protocol performed this time.

Cyclone Akvan Crosses Coast Near Gopalpur

Cyclone Akvan crossed the Odisha coastline near Gopalpur in Ganjam district in the early hours of Friday, 20 November, before weakening steadily as it moved inland. The India Meteorological Department tracked the system as it lost strength over land, downgrading wind intensity through the morning.

Odisha's disaster management minister confirmed that no major damage to property or loss of life had been reported from the landfall zone. District collectors in Ganjam, Khordha, Puri and Jagatsinghpur were placed on alert through the night, with control rooms operating round the clock.

Rain Bands Shift Toward Andhra Pradesh

As the core weakened, the rain bands pushed north-east, bringing heavy showers to Srikakulam district in neighbouring Andhra Pradesh and to parts of coastal West Bengal. Local administrations in low-lying mandals were asked to keep relief shelters open through Friday.

West Bengal's Chief Minister reviewed district-level preparedness for the possible impact, focusing on coastal blocks in East Midnapore and South 24 Parganas, where embankment monitoring was stepped up.

Trains Cancelled and Flights Disrupted

Transport was the most visible casualty of the storm. Vande Bharat services on the Howrah–Bhubaneswar corridor were cancelled for the day, and several long-distance Express trains were diverted or short-terminated as a safety precaution.

IndiGo and Air India issued travel advisories allowing passengers flying to and from Bhubaneswar, Kolkata and Visakhapatnam to reschedule without penalty. Airport authorities regulated operations through the morning as crosswinds eased.

Why the Zero-Damage Headline Matters

Odisha's cyclone protocol has been rebuilt since the 1999 super cyclone, and the Akvan response shows the machinery working on a moderate system. Teams were pre-positioned before the storm arrived rather than after, and advisories reached fishermen and coastal residents a day ahead.

That sequencing matters commercially. Every cancelled Vande Bharat run and diverted flight carries a cost, but a single uncontrolled landfall in a densely populated coastal belt costs far more in rebuilding and lost tourism revenue along the Gopalpur–Puri stretch.

Real-World Highlights for Travellers and Residents

  • Rail passengers on the Howrah–Bhubaneswar route should confirm restoration timelines before booking same-day travel.
  • Airline waivers were time-bound; check the rebooking window before assuming a free date change.
  • Coastal residents should expect continued rain through Friday even as wind speeds drop.
  • Fishermen along the Ganjam and Srikakulam coast should stay off the water until official clearance.
  • Post-landfall road inspections usually clear within 24 to 48 hours unless waterlogging persists.

Odisha's relief machinery remains on standby, and district teams will assess crop and hut damage once the rain bands clear. For now, the storm has passed with the coastline intact.
