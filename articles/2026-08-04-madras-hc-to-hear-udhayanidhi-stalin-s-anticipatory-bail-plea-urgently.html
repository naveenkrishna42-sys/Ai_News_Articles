Madras HC to Hear Udhayanidhi Stalin's Anticipatory Bail Plea Urgently

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Madras High Court will hear Udhayanidhi Stalin's anticipatory bail plea on an urgent basis
  • Former DMK minister was detained in Vellore and nearby districts
  • BJP leaders Kangana Ranaut and Khushbu Sundar slammed his comments on Trisha

The Madras High Court on Tuesday agreed to urgently hear the anticipatory bail plea of former DMK minister Udhayanidhi Stalin, who was detained in Vellore and nearby districts. The development comes amid escalating political tension over his controversial remarks about actress Trisha.

Justice N. Anand Venkatesh reportedly agreed to take up the plea on an urgent basis, with the matter expected to be heard later in the day. Udhayanidhi, the son of Tamil Nadu Chief Minister M.K. Stalin, faces legal action following a complaint over his comments during a public event.

Arrest and Detention

Udhayanidhi was detained in Vellore and surrounding districts, with authorities moving swiftly after the complaint was registered. The exact nature of the charges has not been officially confirmed, but sources indicate the case stems from his remarks about the popular actress.

The detention has triggered immediate political reactions, with supporters of the DMK questioning the timing of the action. Opposition parties, meanwhile, have seized on the issue to criticise the former minister's conduct.

Political Reactions

BJP leader Khushbu Sundar was among the first to respond, taking a sharp swipe at Udhayanidhi. In a post on social media, she reportedly said, "If you're man enough..." — a remark aimed at his controversial statement about Trisha.

Bollywood actor and BJP MP Kangana Ranaut also weighed in, joining Sundar in supporting the arrest. Both leaders condemned what they described as "public display of filthy abuses" and "double-meaning obscene and vulgar jokes," saying such behaviour is not acceptable from a public figure.

The criticism from the BJP camp has been relentless, with the party using the incident to target the DMK government. The controversy has also sparked debate on social media, with hashtags trending on both sides of the political divide.

Legal Proceedings Ahead

The urgent hearing of the anticipatory bail plea is seen as a crucial step in determining Udhayanidhi's immediate fate. If granted bail, he would avoid arrest in connection with the case; if denied, he could face further detention.

Legal experts suggest the court's decision will hinge on the severity of the allegations and the evidence presented. The prosecution is expected to argue that the comments were deliberate and inflammatory, while the defence will likely contend that the remarks were taken out of context.

This is not the first time Udhayanidhi has faced controversy, but the legal escalation marks a significant turn in his political career. The case has also drawn attention to the broader issue of hate speech and the limits of free expression for public figures in India.

What to Watch

All eyes are now on the Madras High Court's hearing later today. The outcome will not only decide Udhayanidhi's immediate legal standing but could also shape the political narrative in Tamil Nadu in the coming weeks. Observers will be watching whether the court grants interim protection or allows the legal process to take its course.
