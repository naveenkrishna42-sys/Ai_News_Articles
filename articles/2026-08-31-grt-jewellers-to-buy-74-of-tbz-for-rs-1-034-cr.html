GRT Jewellers to Acquire 74.12% Stake in TBZ

GRT Jewellers is set to acquire a 74.12% stake in Tribhovandas Bhimji Zaveri (TBZ) for up to Rs 1,034 crore. This move marks a significant development in the Indian jewellery sector, with potential implications for market consolidation and strategic positioning.

Background and Context

Tribhovandas Bhimji Zaveri, founded in 1932, is one of India's oldest and most respected jewellery brands. GRT Jewellers, a leading jewellery retailer, is known for its extensive retail network and strong brand presence. The acquisition aims to strengthen GRT's market position and expand its product offerings.
