Punjab CM's wife files legal notice after NHRC probe

Punjab Chief Minister's wife has sent a legal notice to an ex-judge following the National Human Rights Commission's (NHRC) directive to the state government to probe a complaint against her. The complaint alleges that she sought money to suppress a rape case.

Background of the complaint

The complaint was filed by an ex-judge, who alleged that the Punjab CM's wife sought Rs 5 crore to suppress a rape case. The NHRC has asked the Punjab government to investigate the allegations.

Reaction from political parties

The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) has joined calls for the resignation of Punjab Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann over the allegations against his wife. The Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) MLA Kaur has alleged that the allegations are baseless and politically motivated.

NHRC's directive

The NHRC has issued a notice to the Punjab government asking it to investigate the allegations against the Punjab CM's wife. The notice comes after the NHRC received a complaint from an ex-judge alleging that the Punjab CM's wife sought money to suppress a rape case.

What happens next?

The Punjab government will now investigate the allegations against the Punjab CM's wife. The outcome of the investigation will determine the next course of action. The political parties will also continue to react to the allegations and the NHRC's directive. The ex-judge's legal notice to the Punjab CM's wife adds another layer of complexity to the ongoing controversy. The case will likely continue to make headlines as the investigation progresses and political parties express their views on the matter.
