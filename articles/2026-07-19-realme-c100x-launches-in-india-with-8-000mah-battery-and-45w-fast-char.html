Realme C100x Launches in India with 8,000mAh Battery and 45W Fast Charging

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Realme C100x features an 8,000mAh battery with 45W fast charging support.
  • Phone is powered by a MediaTek Dimensity 6300 processor and a 144Hz display.
  • Rugged build designed for durability; it also launched alongside the Narzo 100x 5G.

Realme has launched the C100x in India, a smartphone that prioritises battery life above almost everything else. The device packs an 8,000mAh battery — one of the largest in any mainstream phone in the country — and supports 45W fast charging.

The company claims this combination can keep the phone running for over two days on a single charge under normal use. For users who constantly hunt for a power outlet, this is a clear selling point.

Display and Performance

Despite its budget positioning, the C100x does not skimp on the screen. It features a 144Hz refresh rate display, a spec usually found on more expensive gaming phones. The high refresh rate makes scrolling and animations feel smoother.

Under the hood, the phone runs on a MediaTek Dimensity 6300 chipset. This processor supports 5G connectivity and is built on a 6nm process, which helps balance performance with power efficiency.

Rugged Build and Design

Realme has given the C100x a rugged build. The phone is designed to withstand drops, dust, and splashes better than standard glass-and-metal slabs. This makes it a strong contender for field workers, outdoor enthusiasts, or anyone prone to dropping their phone.

The exact IP rating or MIL-STD certification details have not been confirmed by the company yet. But early hands-on reports suggest a reinforced frame and textured back panel for extra grip.

Pricing and Availability

The Realme C100x has been priced aggressively. It starts at under ₹12,000 for the base variant, making it one of the most affordable phones with an 8,000mAh battery and a 144Hz display in India.

Sales begin this week via Realme's official website and major e-commerce platforms. The phone will be available in two colour options — a standard black and a more adventurous green.

Realme also launched the Narzo 100x 5G alongside the C100x. That model shares the same massive battery and display but targets a slightly different audience with its own design language.

Competition and Market Position

The C100x enters a crowded budget segment. Rivals like the Vivo T5 Lite offer similar battery specs but at a higher price point. The Realme phone's combination of large battery, fast charging, and high refresh rate screen gives it a clear edge on paper.

However, other aspects like the camera quality, software experience, and long-term durability will only become clear after detailed reviews are out.

The company is betting big on the battery-first approach. As 5G drains power faster than 4G, a phone that can last two days without a top-up could attract a lot of buyers who are tired of carrying power banks.

Sales numbers in the first few weeks will tell if this gamble pays off. Early indications suggest strong pre-order interest, particularly from users in smaller towns where reliable electricity is still a concern.
