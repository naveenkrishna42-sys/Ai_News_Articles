GTA VI Arrives After a 13-Year Gap

Rockstar Games has released Grand Theft Auto VI, ending a 13-year wait since Grand Theft Auto V launched in 2013. UK retailers confirmed launch stock this week, with gamers worldwide picking up copies across physical and digital stores.

The game returns to Vice City, expanded into a larger state loosely based on Florida, with a dual-protagonist structure and a heavier emphasis on character relationships than previous entries.

What Critics Are Saying

Early reviews have been broadly enthusiastic. The Guardian described it as "gaming's most depraved world" that is "now also its greatest love story," while Bloomberg called GTA VI "a masterpiece." CNN framed the question as whether the title was worth the 13-year wait, and reported that the critical consensus points to yes.

For buyers, that matters because launch-price games carry risk. A strong critical consensus reduces the chance of a day-one regret purchase, though individual tolerance for the series' mature satire remains a personal factor.

Buyer Checklist: Platforms, Pricing and Practicalities

Launch platforms centre on current-generation consoles, with PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S confirmed. A PC release window has not been firmly detailed, so PC players should avoid assuming day-one availability.

Pricing follows standard AAA patterns: expect a standard edition in the region of £70 / $69.99, with premium and special editions priced higher. Indian pricing typically lands in the ₹3,000-₹5,000 band for standard editions, though regional store pricing varies. Also check download size, storage space and whether your console has an internal SSD or requires an expansion card.

If you own a current-generation console and enjoy open-world action, GTA VI is the defining release of its cycle. Wait for post-launch patches and bundle deals if you are sensitive to price or storage, and verify your edition includes the content you actually want before checkout.
