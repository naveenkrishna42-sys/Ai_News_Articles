Bitcoin lags as global equities scale record highs: Crypto Markets Today

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Global equities reached record highs while Bitcoin and major altcoins lagged.
  • Crypto market cap remained flat, reflecting cautious investor appetite.
  • Analysts point to divergent risk sentiment between traditional and digital assets.

Global equity markets surged to unprecedented levels on Wednesday, but Bitcoin and the broader cryptocurrency market failed to join the rally, underscoring a growing divergence in risk appetite among investors.

While stock indices across major economies scaled new peaks, the price of Bitcoin remained range-bound, and most altcoins mirrored the sluggishness. The muted performance comes even as traditional markets bask in optimism driven by easing monetary policy expectations and robust corporate earnings.

Crypto's Stalled Momentum

Bitcoin, the largest cryptocurrency by market capitalisation, struggled to hold above key support levels, trading in a narrow band through the day. The broader digital asset market, tracked by major indices, showed negligible movement, with total market capitalisation hovering near recent levels.

Data from CoinDesk's market tracker revealed that most top-20 cryptocurrencies recorded marginal losses or gains, with no clear leader. Ethereum, the second-largest token, also failed to capitalise on the equity rally, reflecting a broader sense of caution among crypto traders.

Equities Race Ahead

In contrast, global equities enjoyed a stellar session. The MSCI World Index touched a fresh all-time high, while benchmarks in the US, Europe, and Asia posted strong gains. Investors piled into stocks, buoyed by hopes of interest rate cuts and better-than-expected earnings from technology and industrial giants.

The divergence between equities and crypto is notable, as the two asset classes have often moved in tandem in recent years, driven by similar liquidity conditions and risk-on sentiment. Wednesday's session suggests that institutional money is favouring traditional markets, at least for now.

What's Driving the Gap?

Market observers point to several factors that may explain crypto's underperformance. Regulatory uncertainty continues to weigh on sentiment, with policymakers in multiple jurisdictions yet to clarify rules for digital assets. Additionally, trading volumes in crypto markets have thinned, reducing the conviction behind price moves.

Some analysts argue that the absence of a fresh catalyst—such as a spot Bitcoin exchange-traded fund approval or a major institutional adoption announcement—has left the market without a clear narrative. Meanwhile, equities benefit from a steady stream of corporate earnings and macroeconomic data that reinforce growth expectations.

Bitcoin's correlation with the tech-heavy Nasdaq has also weakened in recent weeks, suggesting that crypto is no longer riding the same wave as high-growth stocks. This decoupling, while not unprecedented, adds a layer of complexity for traders who had grown accustomed to parallel moves.

Investor Sentiment Turns Cautious

On-chain metrics indicate that long-term holders are accumulating, but short-term traders appear hesitant to add positions. Funding rates on major derivatives exchanges remain subdued, pointing to a lack of leverage-driven speculation.

Stablecoin flows into exchanges have also slowed, a sign that fresh capital is not entering the crypto ecosystem at the pace seen earlier in the year. In contrast, equity markets are witnessing robust inflows, with mutual funds and ETFs reporting strong subscription numbers.

"The market is in a wait-and-see mode," said a crypto analyst quoted in the CoinDesk report, though no specific names were provided. "Equities have a clear catalyst—rate cuts—but crypto needs its own narrative."

What to Watch

As global equities continue to scale records, all eyes will be on whether Bitcoin can reclaim its correlation with risk assets or if it will chart an independent course. Key levels to watch include Bitcoin's recent support at around $60,000, with resistance near $65,000.

Investors will also monitor any regulatory developments, particularly in the United States and India, where policy clarity could reignite interest. For now, the crypto market remains in a holding pattern, awaiting a spark that could either reignite the bull run or extend the consolidation.

In the coming sessions, the focus will be on whether equity indices can sustain their record pace and whether crypto traders will finally join the party. Until then, the gap between Wall Street's exuberance and Crypto Street's caution is likely to persist.
