Woman dies after lift malfunctions in Chandanagar

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • A woman died after a lift malfunctioned in Chandanagar.
  • The incident occurred in Hyderabad, with authorities investigating.
  • Details about the victim and cause are yet to be confirmed.

Hyderabad: A woman died after a lift malfunctioned in Chandanagar, a western suburb of the city. The incident was reported on Wednesday, and local authorities have initiated an investigation into the circumstances surrounding the tragedy.

The victim, whose identity has not been officially disclosed yet, was using the lift when it reportedly suffered a technical failure. Emergency services were alerted, but she could not be saved. Police have cordoned off the area and are examining the lift's maintenance records.

Sequence of events

According to preliminary reports, the lift was in operation when it malfunctioned, leading to the fatal accident. The exact nature of the malfunction remains unclear. Officials have not yet confirmed whether it was a sudden drop, a door-related issue, or another mechanical failure.

Residents in the locality expressed shock, with some noting that the lift had shown signs of trouble in the past. However, such claims have not been verified by the authorities. The building's management is cooperating with the ongoing probe.

Investigation underway

The police have registered a case, and a forensic team has been deployed to gather evidence. The lift's service history and safety compliance are under scrutiny. "We are looking into all angles, including maintenance lapses," a senior police official said, speaking on condition of anonymity.

Officials are also checking whether the lift had a valid fitness certificate. In many such incidents, outdated equipment and poor upkeep are common factors. The findings will determine if negligence was involved.

Safety concerns in residential buildings

This incident has reignited concerns about lift safety in Hyderabad's residential complexes. Many older buildings lack regular maintenance, and lifts often go without mandatory checks. Experts say periodic servicing and prompt repair of faults are critical to preventing such tragedies.

Local residents have urged the authorities to conduct safety audits across the city. "This could happen anywhere if lifts are not maintained properly," said a resident of a nearby apartment complex. The Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation has yet to issue a statement on possible city-wide inspections.

Next steps

As the investigation continues, the focus will be on establishing the cause of the malfunction and fixing accountability. The post-mortem report is expected to provide further clarity. Meanwhile, the building's lift has been shut down pending a thorough inspection.
