PM Modi: Production-linked punishment held back India's growth

📅 2026-08-21 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • PM Modi criticizes production-linked punishment policies for hindering Indian industry growth.
  • India's political stability is seen as a bright spot for global growth, says PM Modi.
  • PM Modi announces reforms to boost manufacturing and investment in India.

PM Modi Criticizes Production-Linked Punishment Policies

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has criticized the era of production-linked punishment policies, which he says held back India's industrial growth. Speaking at the Economic Times World Leaders Forum 2026, Modi emphasized the need for a more supportive environment for businesses to thrive.

India's Political Stability Seen as Global Growth Bright Spot

Modi highlighted India's political stability as a key factor in its economic growth. He stated that the world sees India as a bright spot for growth, thanks to its stable political environment. This stability has attracted significant investment and boosted economic activities across various sectors.

Reforms to Boost Manufacturing and Investment

Modi announced a series of reforms aimed at boosting manufacturing and investment in India. These include the transition from Production-Linked Incentives (PLI) to Production-Linked Incentives (PLI) schemes, which are designed to provide targeted support to specific industries. The reforms are part of a broader effort to create a more favorable business environment.

Key Announcements

  • Transition from PLI to PLI schemes to provide targeted support to specific industries.
  • Reforms to boost manufacturing and investment in India.
  • Emphasis on political stability as a key factor in economic growth.

Modi's address at the Economic Times World Leaders Forum 2026 underscored India's commitment to economic reforms and its potential as a global growth leader. The reforms are expected to create a more supportive environment for businesses, attracting more investment and fostering economic growth.
