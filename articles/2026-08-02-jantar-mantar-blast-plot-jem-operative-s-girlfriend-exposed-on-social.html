Jantar Mantar Blast Plot: JeM Operative's Girlfriend Exposed on Social Media

📅 2026-08-02 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • West Bengal STF arrested a terror suspect planning an attack at Jantar Mantar in Delhi.
  • The suspect's girlfriend, a Jharkhand woman, was also arrested; her social media activity exposed the plot.
  • The module allegedly used 'motorcycle reels' and honey-trap tactics to target influential figures.

New Delhi: A terror plot targeting Jantar Mantar in the heart of the capital has been foiled with the arrest of a suspected Jaish-e-Mohammed (JeM) operative. The breakthrough came from an unlikely source: the social media activity of the suspect's girlfriend, according to officials from the West Bengal Special Task Force (STF).

The STF, which made the arrests, said the operative had plans to infiltrate a protest at Jantar Mantar, a site frequently used for public demonstrations. The specific nature of the planned attack has not been officially confirmed, but security agencies have heightened alert in the area.

Social Media Trail Leads to Arrest

Investigators revealed that the woman, a native of Jharkhand, was arrested after her online posts and messages helped trace the network. Her social media profiles allegedly showed connections to the operative and hinted at their activities. The trail, officials said, exposed the duo's movements and communications, allowing the STF to close in.

The suspect's girlfriend is believed to have played a key role in the module's operations. Reports suggest she was involved in what investigators describe as 'honey-trap missions'—using romantic or personal connections to gather intelligence on influential individuals. The Times of India has reported that the module used 'motorcycle reels' as a cover for reconnaissance, a tactic that has raised alarm among security agencies.

Honey-Trap Module Exposed

The arrests have unearthed a broader network allegedly linked to Pakistan's Inter-Services Intelligence (ISI). According to an exclusive report by News18, the JeM's Bengal honey-trap module was tracking VIPs across the country. The module's operations extended beyond the Jantar Mantar plot, with targets including government officials and military personnel.

The West Bengal STF has not revealed the full extent of the network, but sources indicate that the woman's role was central to these operations. Her ability to target influential figures, possibly through social media and personal interactions, has prompted a review of security protocols for public figures.

Jantar Mantar Plot Details

The Jantar Mantar plot, officials said, was at an early stage. The operative had allegedly planned to blend into a protest at the site, which often sees large crowds. The timing of the planned attack remains unclear, but the arrest has averted a potential security breach in Delhi.

The Hindu has reported that the STF's investigation is ongoing, with more arrests likely. The Jharkhand woman's interrogation is expected to reveal further details about the module's handlers and their objectives.

Security Implications

The case highlights the growing use of social media by terror groups to recruit and coordinate. The girlfriend's online presence, which inadvertently exposed the plot, also underscores the challenges of tracking such activities. Security agencies are now scrutinising similar patterns across states.

Officials have not yet confirmed the identities of the arrested individuals or their exact roles. The STF has urged the public to report suspicious online behaviour, but has stopped short of providing specific guidelines.

As the investigation unfolds, the focus will shift to dismantling the wider network. The arrest of the JeM operative and his girlfriend is a significant blow, but the threat of similar modules remains. Security forces are expected to increase surveillance at protest sites and public gatherings in the coming weeks.
