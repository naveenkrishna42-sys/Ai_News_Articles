Ex-Badrinath temple officer Rajendra Chauhan arrested in donation theft case

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Rajendra Chauhan, former Badrinath temple officer, arrested after SIT questioning.
  • CCTV evidence and panel recommendations include dress code and surveillance.
  • Temple treasurer also removed as probe into donation irregularities widens.

The Special Investigation Team (SIT) probing the donation theft at the Badrinath temple has arrested former temple officer Rajendra Chauhan. The arrest came after hours of grilling, during which investigators confronted him with evidence gathered from CCTV footage and financial records.

Chauhan, who served as the chief administrative officer of the Shri Badrinath Kedarnath Temple Committee (BKTC), was suspended earlier this year. The SIT took him into custody late on Thursday evening.

What the probe has uncovered so far

The investigation began after reports emerged of large-scale theft of donations meant for the temple. Devotees offer cash and valuables at the shrine, which are supposed to be accounted for and deposited in the temple treasury.

Sources say the SIT has found discrepancies in the records of donations collected between 2022 and 2025. The missing amount is believed to be several crores of rupees.

CCTV footage from the temple premises has been a key piece of evidence. It shows individuals handling donation boxes in ways that deviate from standard procedures. The SIT is also examining the movement of temple staff during peak pilgrimage seasons.

Panel suggests new rules for temple security

Meanwhile, a separate panel appointed to look into the theft has submitted interim recommendations. These include a strict dress code for temple staff and the installation of additional CCTV cameras at all entry and exit points, as well as near donation boxes.

The panel has also suggested that all donation boxes be sealed and opened only in the presence of multiple officials. A digital log of every opening and closing is being proposed to ensure transparency.

These recommendations come in the wake of the arrest and the removal of the temple treasurer. The BKTC has confirmed that the treasurer has been relieved of his duties pending a detailed inquiry.

Broader implications for temple management

The Badrinath shrine is one of the holiest in Hinduism and attracts lakhs of pilgrims every year. The scandal has raised questions about the management of temple funds and the accountability of officials appointed to oversee them.

The state government has promised a thorough investigation. The SIT has been given a free hand to question any current or former employee of the BKTC.

Chauhan is expected to be produced before a local court for remand. The SIT is likely to seek custody to question him further about the role of other officials.

The arrest marks a significant step in the probe. Investigators are now focusing on tracing the money trail and identifying anyone else who may have been involved.

The temple administration has announced that it will cooperate fully with the investigation. New standard operating procedures for handling donations are expected to be implemented before the next pilgrimage season begins.
