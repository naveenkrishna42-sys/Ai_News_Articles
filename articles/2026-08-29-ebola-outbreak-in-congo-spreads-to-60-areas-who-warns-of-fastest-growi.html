Ebola Outbreak Expands in Congo, WHO Declares Fastest-Growing Epidemic

The Ebola outbreak in eastern Congo has reached unprecedented proportions, with the disease spreading to two new health zones, bringing the total number of affected areas to 60. The World Health Organization (WHO) has warned that this is the fastest-growing Ebola epidemic in history, highlighting the urgent need for global intervention.

Spread of the Outbreak

The outbreak, which began in Bundibugyo district, has now spread to neighboring areas, including two new health zones. This expansion has raised concerns about the potential for further transmission and the need for a more robust response.

The organization has also begun vaccinations to combat the outbreak, recognizing the critical role of preventive measures in controlling the spread of the disease.
