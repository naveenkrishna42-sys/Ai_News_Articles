CCI Members Reject Lawn Bowling Club Takeover Plan

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • CCI members voted against a lawn bowling club takeover proposal
  • The decision leaves the club management in a difficult position
  • Future plans for the facility remain uncertain after the rejection

The Cricket Club of India (CCI) is facing an uncertain future after its members decisively voted down a proposal to hand over a section of the club to a lawn bowling operator. The move, which was pitched as a way to monetise underutilised space, has now left the management scrambling for alternatives.

The proposal, which had been in the works for months, sought to convert part of the club's sprawling premises into a dedicated lawn bowling facility. However, a significant section of the membership saw the plan as a dilution of the club's cricketing heritage, and the resistance proved too strong to overcome.

Members Speak Up

The voting, which concluded recently, saw a clear majority of members reject the takeover. Sources within the club indicate that the opposition was driven primarily by concerns over the loss of green space and the fear that commercial interests would overshadow the club's traditional sporting focus.

"The members have made their position very clear," said a club insider, speaking on condition of anonymity. "There was a strong sentiment that this was not the direction the CCI should be heading in." The rejection is a significant blow to the club's management, which had been banking on the proposal to generate much-needed revenue.

Financial Implications

The CCI, like many legacy sporting institutions in Mumbai, has been grappling with rising maintenance costs and the need to modernise its facilities. The lawn bowling proposal was seen as a low-effort, high-return venture that could have offset some of these expenses.

With the proposal now dead, the management is back to the drawing board. It is unclear what alternative plans are on the table, and officials have not yet confirmed what the next steps will be. The club will need to find a new way to balance its financial needs with the expectations of its members, who have shown they are unwilling to compromise on the club's core identity.

What's Next for the Club

The rejection has created a stalemate of sorts. The management cannot push the proposal through without member support, and the members have shown they are not in a mood to accept quick-fix commercial solutions. This could lead to a period of prolonged deliberation within the club's governing body.

For now, the focus will likely shift to more modest upgrades and a renewed emphasis on the club's primary sport. The lawn bowling chapter, at least for the foreseeable future, appears to be firmly closed.

All eyes will now be on the club's next general body meeting, where the management is expected to present alternative proposals. Whether those plans will find favour with the members remains to be seen.
