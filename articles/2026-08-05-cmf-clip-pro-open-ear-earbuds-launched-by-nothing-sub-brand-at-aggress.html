CMF Clip Pro Open-Ear Earbuds Launched by Nothing Sub-Brand at Aggressive Price

Key points
  • CMF Clip Pro marks the sub-brand's entry into open-ear earbuds
  • Smart Dial on the charging case adds functional control
  • Design follows Nothing's signature transparent, minimalist aesthetic
  • Launch draws mixed reactions over AI-generated promotional imagery

CMF Clip Pro: A New Era for Open-Ear Audio

Nothing's budget-focused sub-brand, CMF, has unveiled its first open-ear earbuds, the Clip Pro. The new audio wearable brings a fresh take on the open-ear category, combining a distinctive design with a price tag that undercuts most competitors.

The earbuds arrive at a time when open-ear audio is gaining traction among users who prefer situational awareness during workouts or commutes. CMF's entry into this segment signals an aggressive push to democratise features typically reserved for premium models.

Smart Dial: A Functional Twist

The most notable feature is the Smart Dial integrated into the charging case. This physical control allows users to adjust volume, skip tracks, or manage calls without touching the earbuds themselves. It is a practical addition that sets the Clip Pro apart from similar products in its price bracket.

The case itself follows Nothing's signature transparent design language, offering a glimpse of the internal components. This minimalist approach has become a hallmark of the brand, appealing to users who value aesthetics alongside functionality.

Design and Comfort

CMF has emphasised the open-ear form factor, which rests outside the ear canal rather than inserting into it. This design choice aims to provide all-day comfort while reducing the risk of ear fatigue during prolonged use. The clip mechanism ensures a secure fit, making the earbuds suitable for active lifestyles.

While official specifications remain limited, the Clip Pro is expected to deliver balanced audio with a focus on clarity rather than heavy bass. The open-ear design naturally prioritises ambient sound, catering to users who need to stay aware of their surroundings.

Controversy Over AI-Generated Imagery

The launch has not been without its share of backlash. Nothing faced criticism for using AI-generated people in promotional materials for the Clip Pro. Social media users and tech enthusiasts have expressed disappointment, arguing that the move undermines the brand's human-centric approach.

This controversy highlights a growing tension in the tech industry between cost-cutting measures and consumer expectations. While AI-generated imagery reduces production expenses, it often clashes with the authenticity that audiences demand from lifestyle brands.

Pricing and Availability

CMF has positioned the Clip Pro as an affordable option, though the exact price has not been officially confirmed at the time of writing. The sub-brand has consistently targeted the budget segment, and the Clip Pro is expected to follow that pattern, making premium features accessible to a wider audience.

Availability details are yet to be announced, but the earbuds are likely to launch in India and other key markets shortly after the global debut. Enthusiasts can expect a competitive price point that undercuts offerings from established players in the open-ear category.

What to Watch Next

As CMF expands its product ecosystem, the Clip Pro will test whether open-ear earbuds can win over mainstream consumers. The coming weeks will reveal how the brand navigates the backlash over AI imagery and whether the Smart Dial proves to be a genuine differentiator in everyday use. Tech observers will also be watching for detailed reviews that assess sound quality and battery life against the competition.
