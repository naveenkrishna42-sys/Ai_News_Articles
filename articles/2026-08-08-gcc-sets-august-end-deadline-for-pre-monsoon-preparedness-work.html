GCC sets August-end deadline for pre-monsoon preparedness work

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Greater Chennai Corporation sets August-end deadline for pre-monsoon works
  • Focus on desilting stormwater drains and repairing vulnerable infrastructure
  • Civic body aims to prevent waterlogging during the northeast monsoon

Deadline fixed for monsoon readiness

The Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC) has set August 31 as the deadline for completing all pre-monsoon preparedness work across the city. Officials said the focus is on desilting stormwater drains, clearing waterways, and repairing vulnerable infrastructure before the northeast monsoon arrives.

The directive comes as the civic body gears up for the rainy season, which typically brings heavy showers to Tamil Nadu between October and December. Past years have seen severe waterlogging in several neighbourhoods, prompting the GCC to start early this time.

Key tasks on the agenda

The preparedness drive includes desilting of major and minor drains, removal of encroachments in water channels, and maintenance of pumping stations. Officials have also been asked to identify flood-prone spots and deploy necessary equipment in advance.

Work orders have been issued to all zonal offices, with instructions to prioritise areas that witnessed stagnation last year. The GCC has also directed engineers to ensure that all stormwater drain inlets are functional and free of debris before the deadline.

Monitoring and accountability

Senior officials will review progress on a weekly basis, and zonal commissioners have been made responsible for meeting the timeline. The corporation has also urged residents to cooperate by not dumping waste into drains, which often leads to blockages during heavy rain.

According to the GCC, the desilting work is being carried out in phases, with core areas like T. Nagar, Mylapore, and parts of north Chennai receiving priority. The civic body has also planned to clean all water bodies under its jurisdiction before the monsoon sets in.

Infrastructure upgrades

In addition to desilting, the GCC is repairing worn-out culverts and replacing damaged drain covers. The work also includes deepening of canals and strengthening of embankments in low-lying areas.

Officials said that the monsoon preparedness is not just about cleaning but also about ensuring that the drainage network can handle intense rainfall. The corporation has been using mechanical equipment to speed up desilting in narrow streets where manual labour is difficult.

Coordination with other agencies

The GCC is coordinating with the Chennai Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board and the Tamil Nadu Generation and Distribution Corporation to clear utility lines that may obstruct drain flow. This inter-agency effort is aimed at avoiding last-minute glitches during the monsoon.

Residents in several localities have already reported seeing desilting work in progress. The civic body has appealed for public patience, noting that some roads may be temporarily affected as work is carried out.

What happens next

As the August-end deadline approaches, the GCC will conduct a final audit of all completed works. Any pending tasks will be fast-tracked in the first week of September, ahead of the monsoon's usual onset.

The corporation has said it will release a detailed report on the preparedness status after the deadline, which will help residents know what has been done in their areas. The coming weeks will show whether the civic body can meet its own target and reduce the risk of waterlogging this year.
