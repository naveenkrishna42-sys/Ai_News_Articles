Awarapan 2 Box Office: Momentum Slows After Strong Opening Week

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Awarapan 2 recorded a decline in ticket sales on its sixth day of release.
  • The film has successfully crossed the Rs 150-crore milestone in global box office collections.
  • Despite the mid-week slowdown, the movie maintains a significant lead over the competing title, Batwara 1947.

Mid-Week Dip at the Box Office

The highly anticipated sequel, Awarapan 2, experienced a noticeable decline in its box office performance on Wednesday. After maintaining a steady pace through the early part of the week, the film saw lower footfall on its sixth day in theatres.

Despite the dip, the production has established a strong financial footprint. Industry reports indicate that the film has successfully crossed the Rs 150-crore mark in global collections. This milestone underscores the sustained interest from audiences in the franchise, even as daily figures fluctuate.

Competitive Edge Over Batwara 1947

Market analysts are closely watching the performance of Awarapan 2 in relation to its primary competitor, Batwara 1947. Data suggests that the Emraan Hashmi and Disha Patani starrer is currently performing significantly better, with collections reportedly three times higher than the Sunny Deol-led partition drama.

This performance gap highlights a shift in current audience preferences during the ongoing theatrical window. While Batwara 1947 continues to run, the momentum appears to be heavily skewed in favor of the sequel.

Distribution Adjustments and Production Insights

Exhibitors have responded to the film's overall performance by adjusting show timings and allocations. Notably, Awarapan 2 has secured a higher number of shows for its seventh day compared to its opening day, signaling confidence from theatre owners despite the midweek slowdown.

Behind the scenes, the project took time to materialize. Vishesh Bhatt recently shared that Emraan Hashmi was not entirely convinced about the sequel when it was first proposed. The actor had expressed initial hesitations regarding the narrative direction before eventually committing to the project.

  • Global box office has surpassed Rs 150 crore.
  • Day 7 show counts have increased relative to the release date.
  • Outperforming Batwara 1947 by a wide margin in major territories.

The upcoming weekend will prove critical in determining whether the film can maintain its current lead or if the mid-week dip signals a plateau in its theatrical run.
