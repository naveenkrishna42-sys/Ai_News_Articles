The 1984 anti-Sikh riots were a series of violent incidents that occurred in Delhi and other parts of India. The riots were triggered by the assassination of Indira Gandhi, the then-Prime Minister of India, by her Sikh bodyguards. The riots resulted in the deaths of hundreds of people and caused extensive damage to property.
