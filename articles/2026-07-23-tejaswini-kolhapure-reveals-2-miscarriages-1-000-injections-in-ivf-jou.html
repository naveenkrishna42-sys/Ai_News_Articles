Tejaswini Kolhapure Reveals 2 Miscarriages, 1,000 Injections in IVF Journey

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tejaswini Kolhapure shared her IVF struggles, including two miscarriages and over 1,000 injections.
  • She spoke about the emotional and physical toll of the fertility treatment process.
  • The actor's candid revelation has sparked conversations about infertility and mental health in India.

Actor Tejaswini Kolhapure has opened up about her gruelling journey with in vitro fertilisation (IVF), revealing she endured over 1,000 injections and suffered two miscarriages. The candid disclosure came during a recent interview, where she detailed the physical and emotional toll of trying to conceive.

Kolhapure, known for her work in Marathi and Hindi cinema, did not hold back while describing the ordeal. She said the process left her feeling isolated and exhausted, but she chose to speak out to normalise conversations around infertility.

'A Thousand Injections'

The actor recounted the sheer number of medical interventions she underwent. 'I have taken more than 1,000 injections,' she said, adding that each cycle of treatment required daily shots of hormones to stimulate egg production.

She described the routine as 'relentless.' The injections, she explained, were not just painful but also came with side effects like bloating, mood swings, and fatigue. 'Your body is no longer your own,' she said.

Two Miscarriages and the Emotional Toll

Kolhapure also revealed she experienced two miscarriages during her IVF journey. She did not specify the timeline but said the losses were devastating. 'You build hope, and then it just crumbles,' she said, her voice heavy with emotion.

She spoke about the stigma attached to miscarriage in Indian society. 'People don't talk about it. You are expected to move on quietly,' she said. The actor stressed that sharing her story was a way to break that silence.

Support System and Coping

Kolhapure credited her husband and close family for standing by her during the toughest phases. 'Without them, I don't think I could have gone through it,' she said. She also acknowledged the role of her medical team, who guided her through each step.

But she was honest about the moments of doubt. 'There were days I wanted to give up. You question why this is happening to you,' she said. Therapy and open conversations, she added, helped her cope.

Broader Conversation on Fertility

The actor's revelation has struck a chord with many women in India, where infertility often remains a hushed topic. According to experts, one in six couples in India faces fertility issues, yet cultural taboos prevent many from seeking help or sharing their struggles.

Kolhapure's courage in speaking out is seen as a step towards normalising these discussions. 'If my story helps even one woman feel less alone, it's worth it,' she said.

She did not disclose whether she has had a successful pregnancy or plans to continue treatment. Officials close to the actor have not confirmed any further details.

What remains clear is that Kolhapure's raw account has added a powerful voice to the ongoing conversation about reproductive health, resilience, and the need for empathy.
