IAF officer arrested for leaking defence info, booked under Secrets Act

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • IAF Wing Commander arrested for allegedly leaking sensitive defence information.
  • Officials say the officer was lured on social media chats and was under active surveillance.
  • Booked under the Official Secrets Act; investigation ongoing.

Arrest and charges

An Indian Air Force officer has been arrested on charges of sharing classified defence information, with authorities booking him under the Official Secrets Act. The officer, a Wing Commander, was taken into custody after a joint operation involving military intelligence and civilian agencies.

Officials familiar with the case said the officer had been under active surveillance for some time before the arrest. The exact nature and extent of the information leaked have not been disclosed, but sources indicate it pertained to sensitive military operations and capabilities.

Social media trap

According to preliminary investigations, the officer was allegedly lured into a trap through social media chats. A woman, believed to be a handler for Pakistani intelligence, is said to have befriended him online. The conversations reportedly progressed from casual chats to video calls, during which the officer was gradually coaxed into sharing sensitive data.

Investigators have recovered digital evidence, including chat logs and call records, from the officer's devices. The handler's identity remains unknown, and authorities are working to trace the network involved.

Official response

The IAF has confirmed the arrest but has refrained from sharing specifics, citing the sensitivity of the case. A defence spokesperson said, "The officer is in custody and a court-martial is likely to follow, in addition to criminal proceedings under the Official Secrets Act."

This incident has reignited concerns about insider threats within the armed forces. Over the past few years, several cases of espionage have come to light, prompting the military to tighten its internal security protocols, including regular cyber audits and stricter vetting of personnel with access to classified information.

Investigation underway

The investigation is being handled by a multi-agency team, including the CBI and military intelligence. Officials are examining whether the officer acted alone or was part of a larger espionage ring. They are also checking his travel history and communication patterns over the past year.

Legal experts note that convictions under the Official Secrets Act can lead to severe punishment, including life imprisonment. The officer has not yet been formally charged in court, and his legal representation is yet to be announced.

The case underscores the growing challenge of digital espionage, where state actors increasingly use social engineering tactics to exploit individuals with access to sensitive data. Security agencies have urged personnel to report unsolicited contacts immediately.

As the probe continues, the focus will be on uncovering the full scope of the leak and ensuring that systemic gaps, if any, are addressed. The armed forces are expected to review their information-sharing protocols in the wake of this incident.
