Supreme Court Issues Stern Warning to Petitioners

The Supreme Court of India has issued a stern warning to petitioners involved in the Ram Mandir theft case, emphasizing that the case is purely criminal and should not be politicized. The court's intervention comes as a response to growing concerns about the potential for the case to be used for political gain.

Case is Purely Criminal, Not Political

The Supreme Court has made it clear that the case is purely criminal and should not be used as a platform for political rhetoric. The court has issued a series of orders, including the freezing of all further proceedings until the political aspect of the case is addressed.
