Hyderabad She Teams: A safe, confidential way for women to report harassment

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hyderabad She Teams provide a confidential helpline and mobile app for reporting harassment.
  • Women can file complaints anonymously or with identity protection.
  • The team ensures swift action, including verification and legal follow-up.
  • Officials stress the importance of documenting incidents with evidence.
  • The service is part of a broader effort to improve women's safety in public spaces.

Hyderabad’s She Teams have long been a critical resource for women facing harassment in public spaces. The unit, run by the Telangana police, operates on a simple premise: every complaint is treated with confidentiality and urgency. For many women, the fear of retaliation or social stigma often stops them from speaking up. She Teams aim to remove that barrier.

How the system works

Women can report harassment through multiple channels. The primary method is the dedicated helpline number, which is staffed by trained female officers. Calls are logged with a unique complaint ID, ensuring the complainant can track the case without revealing personal details.

There is also a mobile app — available on both Android and iOS — that allows users to file a report directly from their phone. The app uses end-to-end encryption, so the information shared stays secure. Women can attach photos, videos, or audio recordings as evidence.

Anonymity and identity protection

One of the most important features of the She Teams mechanism is the option to remain anonymous. A woman can file a complaint without giving her name, address, or phone number. If she chooses to share her identity, the police are bound by confidentiality rules. Her details are not shared with the accused or any third party.

Officials have stated that the team takes a victim-centric approach. The goal is to empower women to report without fear. The identity of the complainant is kept separate from the investigation file, and only the investigating officer has access.

What happens after a complaint is filed

Once a complaint is received, the She Teams verify the information. This can involve reviewing CCTV footage, speaking to witnesses, or conducting a field inquiry. If the evidence is sufficient, the police file a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code or the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, depending on the nature of the offence.

The team also offers counselling and legal guidance. In cases where the harassment is ongoing, they can arrange for a safety plan, including police patrolling near the complainant’s home or workplace. The entire process is designed to minimise further trauma.

Tips for women using the service

Police have shared practical advice for complainants. First, document the incident as soon as it happens. Note the time, location, and description of the accused. If possible, save any messages, call logs, or screenshots. Second, do not delete evidence, even if it feels minor. Third, call the helpline or use the app even if you are unsure — the officers are trained to assess and guide.

Women are also encouraged to share the complaint ID with a trusted friend or family member. This creates an additional layer of safety and ensures that someone else knows the case is being tracked.

Broader safety initiatives

The She Teams are part of a larger police strategy to tackle street harassment, stalking, and public nuisance. The unit conducts regular decoy operations at bus stops, railway stations, and markets to catch offenders. They also run awareness campaigns in colleges and workplaces.

Officials have noted that the number of complaints has risen steadily, which they interpret as a sign of growing trust in the system. The goal is not just to punish offenders but to create a deterrent effect in public spaces.

The next step for the Hyderabad police is to expand the She Teams’ digital footprint, including AI-based monitoring of complaints and faster response times. For now, the helpline and app remain the most direct and confidential routes for women seeking help.
