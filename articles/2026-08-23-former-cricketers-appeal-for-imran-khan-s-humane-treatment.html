Former cricketers appeal for Imran Khan's humane treatment

📅 2026-08-23 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • 21 former cricket captains write to Shehbaz Sharif, requesting humane treatment for Imran Khan
  • Imran Khan's party challenges hospital transfer in court, citing Supreme Court order violation
  • Pakistan ex-PM Imran Khan is being transported to a hospital from jail

Former cricket captains appeal for humane treatment for jailed ex-PM

Twenty-one former cricket captains, including Sunil Gavaskar and Kapil Dev, have written to Pakistan's Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, urging him to ensure humane treatment and proper medical care for former Prime Minister Imran Khan. The cricketers, who have been vocal about the political situation in Pakistan, have expressed concern over Khan's health and well-being while he is in custody.

Imran Khan's party challenges hospital transfer in court

Imran Khan's political party, Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI), has moved the Lahore High Court against the transfer of Khan to a hospital. The party argues that the Supreme Court's order, which bars Khan from leaving jail, has been violated. This legal move comes as Khan is being transported to a hospital from jail for medical treatment.

Imran Khan's health and well-being a cause of concern

The former Prime Minister's health has been a subject of concern for many, including his political allies and supporters. Khan, who is currently in custody, is being taken to a hospital for medical treatment. The former cricket captains have expressed their concern over Khan's health and well-being, urging the Pakistani government to ensure that he receives the best possible medical care.

Pakistan's political landscape and Imran Khan's role

Imran Khan's role in Pakistan's political landscape has been significant. As the former Prime Minister, he has been a key figure in the country's political arena. His current situation, with him in custody and being transported to a hospital, has raised questions about his health and well-being. The former cricket captains' appeal for his humane treatment and proper medical care reflects the concern and support that he enjoys from various quarters.
