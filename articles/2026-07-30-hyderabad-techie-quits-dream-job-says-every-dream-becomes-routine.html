Hyderabad Techie Quits Dream Job, Says Every Dream Becomes Routine

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • The techie resigned from a top IT firm, calling it a 'dream job' that lost its shine.
  • He posted about his decision on LinkedIn, sparking a debate on work-life balance.
  • His post received thousands of reactions, with many sharing similar experiences.

Walked Away from the Paycheck

A Hyderabad-based software engineer has quit his high-paying job at a top multinational IT firm, saying his 'dream role' had become just another routine. The techie, who worked for over six years at the company, shared his resignation story on LinkedIn, where it went viral.

In his post, he wrote: 'Every dream, when achieved, becomes the new normal. I walked away from mine today.' The post has since garnered thousands of likes, comments, and shares, with many netizens debating the price of ambition.

The Dream That Lost Its Shine

The techie, who requested anonymity, told Telangana Today that he had once idolised the company and the role. 'I worked hard to get here. But after two years, the excitement faded. The deadlines, the meetings, the targets — it all felt like a hamster wheel,' he said.

He added that the decision was not easy. 'My parents thought I was crazy. But I needed to break the cycle.' His story has resonated with many young professionals in Hyderabad's IT corridor, who often face similar burnout.

A Growing Trend in India's Tech Hub

HR experts in Hyderabad say such resignations are on the rise. 'We see more people quitting for mental peace or entrepreneurship. The 'dream job' concept is shifting,' said a senior HR consultant who works with several IT firms in the city.

The techie plans to take a break and travel before deciding his next move. 'I have saved enough for a year. I want to see what else I can do,' he said.

Social Media Reacts

The LinkedIn post triggered a flood of reactions. Some praised his courage, while others questioned his practicality. 'You are lucky to have that privilege. Not everyone can afford to walk away,' one user commented.

Another user wrote: 'I did the same last year. Best decision of my life.' The conversation has opened up discussions around mental health, financial planning, and the true meaning of success in the corporate world.

As for what comes next, the techie remains open-ended. 'I am not looking for another job right now. I want to find what truly makes me happy.'
