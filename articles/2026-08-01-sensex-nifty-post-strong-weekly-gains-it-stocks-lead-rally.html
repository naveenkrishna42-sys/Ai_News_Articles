Sensex, Nifty post strong weekly gains; IT stocks lead rally

📅 2026-08-01 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex rallied 2,035 points while Nifty50 gained 3% during the week.
  • Nifty IT index surged 7%, leading sectoral gains.
  • Bajaj twins among top gainers, boosting market sentiment.

Markets close the week on a strong note

Indian equity benchmarks wrapped up the week with solid gains, with the SENSEX rallying 2,035 points and the NIFTY50 climbing 3%. The rally was broad-based, but the standout performer was the NIFTY IT index, which jumped 7% during the week.

Market participants pointed to renewed buying interest in technology stocks, which had been under pressure in recent weeks. The sharp uptick in IT shares lifted sentiment across the board, helping benchmarks post their best weekly performance in months.

IT stocks lead the charge

The NIFTY IT index's 7% surge was the biggest sectoral mover of the week. Software exporters benefited from a weaker rupee and expectations of steady demand from key overseas markets. Large-cap IT names led the advance, with mid-cap tech stocks also joining the rally.

Analysts said the move reflected a mix of short-covering and fresh allocations by domestic institutional investors. The sustained buying in IT also helped offset concerns about elevated valuations in some other sectors.

Bajaj twins among top gainers

Bajaj Finance and Bajaj Finserv were among the top gainers on the benchmark indices, contributing significantly to the weekly advance. The two stocks rallied on the back of strong buying interest, though specific triggers were not immediately clear.

Their gains added heft to the financial services pack, which otherwise saw mixed moves during the week. The Bajaj twins' performance was notable given their weighting in the indices.

Broader market and sectoral trends

Aside from IT and the Bajaj group, other sectors such as auto and consumer durables also saw healthy gains. However, some defensive sectors lagged, as investors rotated into cyclical and growth-oriented names.

The broader market indices outperformed the benchmarks, with mid-cap and small-cap stocks attracting steady buying. Market breadth was positive, with more advances than declines on the BSE and NSE.

What to watch next week

With the strong close, traders will be watching for sustainability of the momentum in IT stocks and whether the broader rally extends. Global cues, including US Federal Reserve policy signals and crude oil prices, will also remain in focus.

Investors will also track foreign institutional investor flows, which have been volatile. Any sustained buying by FIIs could add further support to the market's upward move.
