DUSU Counting 2026: ABVP Dominance Under Duress, NSUI Mounts Challenge

The counting of votes for the Delhi University Students' Union (DUSU) elections 2026 is underway amid unprecedented security and a Delhi High Court ban on victory processions. Early trends show the Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP) leading in several key posts, but the National Students' Union of India (NSUI) is putting up a strong fight, particularly for the president's post. With over 1.5 lakh students casting their votes, the results will shape the political landscape of Delhi University for the coming year.

Early Trends: ABVP Ahead, NSUI Surges in Presidential Race

After the initial rounds of counting, ABVP has established a lead in the vice-president and secretary positions. However, the presidential race has seen a seesaw battle. NSUI's Vijay Meena led by over 400 votes after four rounds, but ABVP's Yash Dabas surged ahead after round 15. The counting process is expected to continue for several hours, with final results likely to be declared by evening.

The close contest reflects the intense campaigning by both parties, which focused on issues ranging from campus infrastructure to fee hikes. ABVP, riding on its organisational strength, claimed dominance in early trends, but NSUI's resilience in the presidential race indicates a tight fight.

High Court Ban on Victory Processions: A Game-Changer

In a significant development, the Delhi High Court banned victory processions after the DUSU poll results, warning of strict action against violators. This move comes in the wake of past incidents of violence and overcrowding during celebrations. The ban has forced both parties to plan low-key celebrations, with supporters gathering at counting centres in a subdued manner.

The court's intervention underscores the sensitivity surrounding student elections, which often serve as a barometer for national political trends. Security has been beefed up across the campus, with paramilitary forces deployed to maintain law and order.

Key Candidates and Their Prospects

The presidential race is the most closely watched, with NSUI's Vijay Meena and ABVP's Yash Dabas locked in a tight contest. Meena, a seasoned student leader, has focused on issues like hostel facilities and scholarship disbursement. Dabas, on the other hand, has emphasised campus safety and cultural events. The outcome will hinge on the support from various colleges, including those with a history of backing specific parties.

For the vice-president post, ABVP's candidate is leading comfortably, while the secretary race is also leaning towards ABVP. The joint secretary position remains contested, with NSUI hoping to secure at least one major post.

Voter Turnout and Security Arrangements

Over 1.5 lakh students voted in the DUSU elections, a turnout that reflects the high stakes involved. The polling was held across 52 colleges and faculties, with electronic voting machines used for the first time in some colleges. Security was tight, with CCTV cameras and metal detectors installed at counting centres.

The Delhi Police, along with university authorities, has set up a three-tier security ring around the counting venue. Entry is restricted to authorised personnel, and mobile phones are banned inside the counting hall to prevent any untoward incidents.

What the Results Mean for Students and National Politics

The DUSU election results are more than just a campus affair; they signal the mood of young voters in the national capital. A strong showing by ABVP would bolster the BJP's outreach to youth, while a comeback by NSUI would energise the Congress ahead of the 2027 assembly elections. For students, the new union will negotiate with the administration on issues like examination reforms, fee structures, and campus infrastructure.

The winning panel will also control the annual budget of the DUSU, which runs into several lakhs, and organise events like the famed DU fest. The office-bearers will serve a one-year term, during which they must balance student activism with administrative responsibilities.

Key Insights: Pros and Cons of the 2026 DUSU Elections

  • Pros:
    • High voter turnout indicates increased student engagement in campus politics.
    • Use of technology (EVMs) in some colleges streamlines the voting process.
    • Judicial oversight ensures a violence-free counting day.
  • Cons:
    • Ban on victory processions dampens the festive spirit of student politics.
    • Allegations of code violations and sporadic clashes during campaigning.
    • Counting delays could lead to anxiety and potential unrest.
