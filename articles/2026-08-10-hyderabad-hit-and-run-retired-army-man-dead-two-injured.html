Hyderabad hit-and-run: Retired army man dead, two injured

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Retired army man killed in hit-and-run in Hyderabad
  • Two others injured in the incident
  • Police investigation underway, accused at large

Hyderabad: A retired army man was killed and two others were injured in a hit-and-run incident reported in the city. The incident took place on Monday, according to police. The deceased has been identified as a retired personnel of the Indian Army, though his name has not been officially released yet.

The injured have been shifted to a nearby hospital and are stated to be out of danger. Police have registered a case and are investigating the circumstances leading to the accident. The vehicle involved in the incident is yet to be traced.

Accident details emerge

Preliminary investigation suggests that the victims were on the road when a speeding vehicle knocked them down. The driver allegedly fled the scene after the collision, leaving the injured behind. Locals who witnessed the incident rushed the victims to the hospital, where the retired army man was declared brought dead.

Police have not yet confirmed the exact spot of the accident or the time of the incident. Some reports indicate that the accident occurred in the early hours of Monday, but officials are yet to verify these details. A case of rash driving and causing death by negligence has been registered.

Police launch manhunt

Authorities have launched a search for the accused driver. CCTV footage from the area is being scanned to identify the vehicle. Police are also questioning eyewitnesses to reconstruct the sequence of events. A senior police officer said that all possible leads are being pursued, and the accused will be arrested soon.

Hit-and-run cases have been a recurring concern in Hyderabad, with several such incidents reported over the past year. In many instances, the accused are caught only after a lengthy investigation. This latest case has once again brought attention to road safety and the need for stricter enforcement of traffic rules.

Family, locals demand action

The family of the deceased has demanded strict action against the driver. They have urged the police to ensure that the culprit is brought to justice at the earliest. Local residents have also expressed shock over the incident and called for better safety measures on the roads.

The retired army man's body has been sent for post-mortem, and it is expected to be handed over to the family after the procedure. Police have assured the family of full support and a thorough investigation. The injured are being treated at a government hospital, and their condition is said to be stable.

What to watch

Police are expected to make a breakthrough in the case once the CCTV footage is fully analysed. The identification of the vehicle could lead to the arrest of the accused. Meanwhile, road safety activists are likely to use this incident to push for better traffic management and stricter penalties for hit-and-run offenders.
