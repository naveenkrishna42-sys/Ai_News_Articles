5 New Hindi Movies and OTT Series Releasing This Week (July 20-26)

Netflix Opens the Week with a Mystery Thriller

Netflix will premiere a new Hindi mystery thriller on July 20. The film follows a detective investigating a series of disappearances in a small town. Early reviews suggest a tightly woven plot with strong performances from the lead cast.

This release is expected to appeal to fans of slow-burn suspense. The streaming giant has not yet revealed the exact runtime or age certification for the film. Industry watchers are keen to see if it can replicate the success of recent Hindi originals on the platform.

Amazon Prime Video Drops a Romantic Drama

On July 22, Amazon Prime Video will debut a romantic drama set against the backdrop of a bustling Mumbai. The story revolves around two strangers whose lives intersect during a monsoon night. The film has been shot across multiple locations in the city, capturing its rain-soaked charm.

Both lead actors are known for their work in independent cinema. The streaming service has promoted the film as a heartfelt, modern love story. Pre-release buzz on social media indicates strong interest among younger audiences.

Disney+ Hotstar Offers a Comedy Series

A new Hindi comedy series lands on Disney+ Hotstar on July 23. It follows a family that inherits a chaotic roadside dhaba in Punjab. The show promises a mix of slapstick humour and warm family moments.

The series features a ensemble cast of television and film actors. Each episode runs around 30 minutes, making it a light watch for the weekend. The trailer has already generated positive reactions for its quirky characters and dialogue.

JioCinema Brings a Crime Drama

JioCinema will release a crime drama on July 24, focusing on a journalist uncovering a corporate conspiracy. The series is based on a true event from the early 2000s. It aims to blend investigative journalism with personal stakes.

The show has been shot across Delhi and Gurgaon. The producers have emphasised its research and authenticity in depicting newsroom dynamics. It is expected to attract viewers who enjoy political thrillers.

Zee5 Premieres a Family Drama

Rounding out the week, Zee5 will premiere a family drama on July 26. The story centres on three generations of a family reuniting after a long estrangement. Themes of forgiveness and tradition are central to the narrative.

The film features a veteran actor in a pivotal role. It has been directed by a filmmaker known for previous festival-circuit hits. The platform is banking on its emotional appeal to draw in family audiences during the holiday season.
