Mirzapur The Movie Nears Rs 300 Crore After Day 10 Surge

Mirzapur The Movie has netted an estimated Rs 250-260 crore in India by the end of its second Sunday (Day 10), according to trade tracking. The second weekend delivered a hold of roughly 40-45% against the opening Sunday, a trend that puts the Rs 300 crore nett milestone within realistic reach by the end of Week 2 or early Week 3.

The film, a theatrical continuation of the hit Amazon Prime Video series, has outperformed pre-release trade expectations of a Rs 180-220 crore lifetime finish. The strong second Sunday confirms word-of-mouth pull beyond the core fanbase.

Day-Wise Box Office Breakdown

Trade estimates place the opening weekend at approximately Rs 118-120 crore nett, with the first week closing near Rs 185 crore. The second weekend added roughly Rs 70-75 crore, pushing the cumulative total to the Rs 250-260 crore range by Day 10.

Why the Second Sunday Hold Matters

A second Sunday hold of 40-45% against the opening Sunday is a strong indicator of sustained audience interest. For context, most event films drop 55-65% in this comparison. The film's ability to retain screens and audiences into Week 2 suggests a genuine multiplier effect.

The Rs 300 crore nett milestone, if achieved, will place Mirzapur The Movie among the top 10 all-time Hindi nett grossers. It would also be the first film derived from an Indian streaming series to reach that mark.

Real-World Buyer Implications for Exhibitors and Investors

For exhibitors, the film's performance justifies premium screen allocation and higher ticket pricing in multiplexes. Single-screen theatres in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities have reported strong occupancy, particularly in the Hindi belt.

For investors, the success validates the theatrical potential of premium streaming IP adaptations. The film's budget of Rs 100-120 crore (reported) has already been recovered through theatrical revenue, with ancillary rights (satellite, digital, music) adding further upside.

Key Insights and Takeaways

  • Strong word-of-mouth: The second Sunday hold of 40-45% indicates genuine audience pull beyond the initial fanbase.
  • Milestone in sight: Rs 300 crore nett is achievable by early Week 3 if weekday trends hold at 25-30% of second Sunday figures.
  • Franchise validation: The film's success sets a precedent for other streaming IPs to pursue theatrical releases.
  • Exhibitor opportunity: Premium screen allocation and dynamic pricing can maximize revenue during the film's remaining run.
  • Risk factor: New releases in Week 3 could cut screen count and slow momentum before the Rs 300 crore mark is crossed.

Verdict: A Milestone Within Reach

Mirzapur The Movie is on track to cross Rs 300 crore nett in India, a milestone that would cement its status as one of the biggest Hindi hits of the year. The film's performance demonstrates the commercial viability of adapting premium streaming content for the big screen.

For buyers and exhibitors, the key is to maintain premium screen allocation through Week 3, while monitoring weekday trends for signs of fatigue. At current trajectory, a final domestic nett total of Rs 310-330 crore is the most likely outcome.
