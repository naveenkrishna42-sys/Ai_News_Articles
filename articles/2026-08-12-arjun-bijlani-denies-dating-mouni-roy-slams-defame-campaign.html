Arjun Bijlani denies dating Mouni Roy, slams 'defame' campaign

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Arjun Bijlani has denied dating Mouni Roy amid swirling rumours.
  • He says someone has been trying to defame her, including questioning her sexuality.
  • Bijlani urged people to stop spreading false stories about the actress.
  • He emphasised that both have their own families and personal lives.
  • The actor's remarks come after weeks of speculation on social media.

Arjun Bijlani has broken his silence on the rumoured link-up with actress Mouni Roy, calling out what he describes as a sustained attempt to defame her. The television actor, known for Naagin and Ishq Mein Marjawan, addressed the speculation in a recent interaction, asserting that he has a family and that Roy is also going through a difficult period in her personal life.

“I’ve a family and she’s also going through a lot. Someone has been trying to defame her,” Bijlani said, according to multiple reports. His statement comes amid weeks of online chatter that linked the two co-stars romantically, often without any evidence.

Sexuality question raised

What appears to have pushed Bijlani to speak out is not just the dating gossip but comments targeting Roy’s sexuality. Reports indicate that certain social media users and gossip pages have questioned the actress’s sexual orientation as part of the rumour mill. Bijlani categorically condemned these remarks.

“There were comments about her sexuality. That is completely unacceptable,” he said. The actor urged fans and media alike to refrain from speculating about someone’s personal life, especially when it involves baseless allegations that could harm a person’s reputation.

A pattern of false stories

This is not the first time Roy has been at the centre of unfounded gossip. The actress, who has starred in films like Gold and Made in China, has previously faced rumours about her private life. However, Bijlani’s public defence marks a rare instance of a male co-star stepping forward to shield a colleague from online vitriol.

Bijlani, who is married to wife Aashka Goradia, made it clear that his equation with Roy remains purely professional. The two have worked together in the past, including on the supernatural drama Naagin, which has a massive fan following. He said the rumours are not just untrue but also insensitive, given that Roy is already dealing with personal challenges.

Fan reactions and social media backlash

The actor’s comments have triggered a mixed response online. While many fans have applauded his chivalry and straightforwardness, others have questioned why such rumours gain traction in the first place. Some have pointed out that female celebrities in India often face disproportionate scrutiny about their personal lives, with little consequence for those who spread falsehoods.

Neither Bijlani nor Roy has filed any formal complaint or legal notice regarding the rumours. However, the actor’s strong words suggest that the issue may have crossed a line. He did not name any individual or entity behind the alleged defamation campaign, but hinted that it appears coordinated.

“Someone has been trying to defame her for a while now. It is sad that people are falling for it,” he added.

What lies ahead

As of now, Roy has not publicly reacted to Bijlani’s defence. The actress is reportedly focusing on her upcoming projects and has chosen to stay away from the noise. Industry insiders believe that the controversy will likely fizzle out unless new claims emerge.

For now, the ball is in the court of gossip mongers and social media users who have been amplifying the rumours. Bijlani’s statement serves as a reminder that celebrities are not immune to harm from baseless gossip. Whether this will prompt a larger conversation about online accountability remains to be seen.
