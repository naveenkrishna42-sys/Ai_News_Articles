Lenovo Googlebook leak reveals laptop and 2-in-1 tablet variants

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Leaked images reveal two Lenovo Googlebook devices: a full-size laptop and a 2-in-1 tablet.
  • The 2-in-1 tablet appears to have a detachable keyboard and kickstand, similar to Surface-style designs.
  • Lenovo has not officially confirmed the devices or their specifications yet.

A peek at Lenovo's Googlebook lineup

Fresh leaks have pulled back the curtain on Lenovo's upcoming Googlebook devices, showing a full-size laptop and a 2-in-1 tablet. The renders, shared by multiple tech outlets on Monday, give the clearest look yet at the hardware Lenovo has been quietly preparing.

The images suggest Lenovo is hedging its bets with two distinct form factors. One is a conventional clamshell laptop, while the other is a convertible tablet with a detachable keyboard — a design that puts it in direct competition with devices like Microsoft's Surface lineup.

The 15-inch laptop takes centre stage

According to the leaked material, the larger of the two devices is a 15-inch Googlebook. It appears to feature a slim bezel display, a full keyboard with a trackpad, and a hinge that allows the screen to lay flat. The overall look is clean and utilitarian, with a minimalist chassis that echoes Lenovo's recent design language.

The laptop seems aimed at users who want a straightforward, productivity-focused machine. There is no visible stylus slot or folding mechanism, suggesting Lenovo is keeping this model as a plain, no-frills workhorse.

The 2-in-1 tablet brings versatility

The second device is a smaller 2-in-1 tablet, complete with a detachable keyboard cover and a built-in kickstand. This form factor is a departure from the typical Android tablet, which usually relies on third-party accessories for typing.

Leaked renders show the tablet standing upright on its own, with the keyboard magnetically attached to the bottom edge. This setup allows it to switch between a laptop-style mode and a pure tablet mode, giving users more flexibility for media consumption, note-taking, or light productivity.

Notably, the tablet appears to have a uniform bezel around the display, which could house front-facing cameras and sensors. However, no official specifications — such as processor, RAM, or display resolution — have been confirmed so far.

What this means for the Googlebook brand

Googlebook is Google's push to create a more integrated Android experience on larger screens. The brand was first teased earlier this year, and these leaks suggest Lenovo is one of the first hardware partners to embrace it.

With two devices in the pipeline, Lenovo seems to be targeting different segments: the laptop for users who need a traditional computing experience, and the 2-in-1 for those who want portability without sacrificing productivity. The move could help Googlebook gain traction in a market currently dominated by Apple's iPad and Samsung's Galaxy Tab series.

Official word still pending

Lenovo has not officially confirmed the existence of these devices, nor have they shared any launch dates or pricing. The leaks, while detailed, should be treated with some caution until the company makes a formal announcement.

That said, the images align with earlier reports that Lenovo was working on multiple Googlebook models. If the leaks prove accurate, an official unveiling could come sooner rather than later, possibly at a major tech event later this year.

For now, enthusiasts will have to piece together the details from renders and rumour. As always, the final product may differ from what these leaks show, but one thing is clear — Lenovo is betting big on Googlebook, and the upcoming launch could reshape the Android tablet and laptop landscape.

In the coming weeks, watch for Lenovo to either confirm or deny these leaks, and for Google to provide more clarity on the software experience that will power these devices.
