Chennai businessman gets 5-year jail for drunk driving that killed boy

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Chennai businessman convicted in drunk driving death of a boy
  • Court awards five-year jail term
  • Case highlights road safety concerns in the city

A Chennai businessman has been sentenced to five years in rigorous imprisonment by a city court for a drunk driving incident that led to the death of a young boy. The verdict, delivered on Thursday, brings a measure of closure to a case that had drawn attention to the perils of driving under the influence.

The fatal night

The incident occurred when the accused, driving under the influence of alcohol, lost control of his vehicle and struck the boy, who was on the road. The impact proved fatal, and the child succumbed to his injuries at the scene or shortly after being rushed to a hospital.

Police had registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including those pertaining to causing death by negligence and rash driving. The accused was arrested soon after the incident and had been in judicial custody or out on bail during the trial.

Trial and conviction

During the trial, the prosecution presented evidence, including witness testimonies and medical reports, to establish the accused's culpability. The defence argued for leniency, citing the businessman's background and lack of prior offences, but the court was not persuaded.

The judge, in his order, observed that drunk driving is a grave offence that endangers public safety, and the loss of a young life cannot be trivialised. The sentence of five years is seen as a strong message against such behaviour.

Legal implications

The court also imposed a fine on the convict, the quantum of which has not been officially disclosed. The businessman has the option to appeal against the verdict in a higher court, which could prolong the legal process.

Legal experts note that the judgment aligns with recent judicial trends that call for stricter punishment in road accident cases involving intoxication. The case also underscores the need for rigorous enforcement of traffic laws in metropolitan cities like Chennai.

Road safety concerns

This incident has reignited discussions on road safety in Chennai, where accidents involving two-wheelers and pedestrians remain a concern. Civic groups have repeatedly urged the authorities to conduct regular checks for drunk driving, especially at night and during festive seasons.

The police have increased patrolling and random breathalyzer tests in recent months, but citizens argue that more needs to be done to deter offenders. The conviction in this case may serve as a deterrent, though its long-term impact remains to be seen.

While the verdict has brought justice for the victim's family, it also serves as a sombre reminder of the consequences of reckless behaviour behind the wheel. The family of the boy, who have endured a long legal battle, expressed relief at the outcome.

As the case moves to the appeal stage, the focus will be on whether the higher court upholds the sentence. For now, the judgment stands as a firm stance against drunk driving, and the authorities are expected to continue their efforts to make the city's roads safer.
