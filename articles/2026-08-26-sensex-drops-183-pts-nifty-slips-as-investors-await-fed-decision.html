Sensex drops 183 pts, Nifty slips as investors await Fed decision

📅 2026-08-26 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex drops 183 points, Nifty 50 slips 25 points
  • Investors await Fed rate decision for market direction
  • Key sectors like IT and banking under pressure

Sensex and Nifty Close With Losses

Indian stock markets ended Wednesday with losses, with the Sensex dropping 183 points and the Nifty 50 slipping 25 points. The market's decline comes as investors await a crucial decision from the Federal Reserve on interest rates.

Key Sectors Under Pressure

The IT sector, which has been a major driver of market growth, saw significant losses. The Nifty IT index dropped 2.5%, with key players like Infosys and TCS reporting lower-than-expected earnings. The banking sector also saw a decline, with the Nifty Bank index dropping 1.8%.

Investors Await Fed Decision

Investors are closely watching the Federal Reserve's decision on interest rates, which is expected to be announced on Wednesday. A rate hike could lead to a sell-off in the market, while a rate cut could lead to a rally. The market's reaction to the Fed decision will be crucial in determining the direction of the market in the coming weeks.

Market Sentiment and Future Outlook

Market sentiment is currently negative, with investors expressing concern over the global economic outlook. The market's performance in the coming weeks will be crucial in determining the overall health of the economy. Analysts are expecting a mixed performance, with some sectors likely to see a rally while others continue to face challenges.
