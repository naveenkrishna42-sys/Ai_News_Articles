Trump sees 'good chance' of progress in Iran talks as West Asia tensions ease

📅 2026-07-28 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Trump says US is talking to Tehran and sees a 'good chance' of progress.
  • The US President is set to meet Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu.
  • Trump warns Iran against delays, saying the US can 'beat them' if talks fail.
  • A lull in strikes provides a window for diplomacy in West Asia.
  • Trump threatens a return to 'strong military action' if negotiations collapse.

Diplomatic window opens amid lull

United States President Donald Trump has said there is a 'good chance' of progress in talks with Iran, as a relative calm settles over the West Asia conflict. Speaking to reporters, Trump confirmed the US was in direct communication with Tehran.

The development comes during a noticeable lull in the exchange of strikes that had escalated tensions in the region. Both sides appear to be holding fire, creating a narrow window for diplomatic engagement.

Trump to meet Netanyahu

The US President is set to meet Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu in the coming days. The timing of the meeting is significant, as it comes amid ongoing negotiations with Iran.

While Trump expressed optimism about the talks, he was quick to strike a stern tone. 'We are talking to them, but we can't bribe them,' he said, according to reports. The President warned that if diplomacy fails, the US would revert to 'strong military action'.

'Will beat them' warning

Trump's remarks to reporters included a direct warning to Tehran. 'If they think they can play games, they are wrong. We will beat them,' he said, as reported by The Times of India.

The statement underscores the high-stakes nature of the negotiations. The US administration is walking a tightrope, attempting to secure a diplomatic solution while keeping the threat of force on the table.

What is at stake

The current talks are being closely watched by global powers. A successful outcome could de-escalate one of the most volatile conflicts in the region.

However, the failure of diplomacy could lead to a renewed cycle of violence. The US has made it clear that it will not accept a prolonged stalemate, and military options remain on the table.

For now, the world waits to see if the 'good chance' Trump spoke of translates into a tangible agreement. The coming days, including the meeting with Netanyahu, will be critical in determining the next phase of this conflict.
