10-Day Temple Festival Begins in Jodhpur with BAPS Spiritual Guru

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Religion Original source ↗
Key points
  • The 10-day festival commenced at a temple in Jodhpur.
  • BAPS spiritual guru Mahant Swami Maharaj was present at the inauguration.
  • The event is expected to draw thousands of devotees over its duration.

A 10-day temple festival has begun in Jodhpur, drawing thousands of devotees. The event is being held in the presence of BAPS spiritual guru Mahant Swami Maharaj. He attended the opening ceremonies, marking the start of the religious celebrations.

Festival Inauguration

The festival kicked off on Sunday at a temple in the city. Organisers say it will feature daily rituals, discourses, and cultural programmes. Mahant Swami Maharaj led the initial prayers, setting a spiritual tone for the days ahead.

Devotees from across Rajasthan and other states have started arriving. Local authorities have made arrangements for crowd management and security. The temple premises have been decorated for the occasion.

Significance of the Event

This festival is part of a series of such events organised by the BAPS sect across the country. It aims to promote community bonding and spiritual learning. Mahant Swami Maharaj's presence is seen as a major draw for followers.

The guru is the current spiritual head of the Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha (BAPS). He has been travelling to various temples to bless such gatherings. His visits often attract large crowds seeking his blessings.

What to Expect

Over the next ten days, the temple will host multiple religious activities. These include bhajans, discourses on scriptures, and devotionals. Prasad distribution and community meals have also been planned.

Volunteers have been managing the flow of devotees. Medical and first-aid facilities are on standby given the expected turnout. The festival will conclude with a grand ceremony on the final day.

Officials have not yet released exact attendance figures. But they anticipate participation in the lakhs over the festival period. The temple trust has urged devotees to follow guidelines for a smooth experience.

This event highlights Jodhpur's role as a centre for religious gatherings. The city has a history of hosting large-scale spiritual events. The current festival adds to its calendar of such occasions.

Mahant Swami Maharaj's schedule includes visits to other temples in the region after this festival. His itinerary is closely watched by his followers. The next few days will see continued religious fervour in Jodhpur.
