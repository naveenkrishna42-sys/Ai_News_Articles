Why a Round‑The‑World Ticket Is the Smart Choice

Travel enthusiasts often chase the new 7 wonders—Petra, Machu Picchu, Christ the Redeemer, Great Wall, Taj Mahal, Colosseum, and Chichen Itza. Booking each leg separately inflates taxes, fuel surcharges, and change‑fee risk. A round‑the‑world (RTW) ticket consolidates these variables into a single fare, offering predictable pricing and a smoother itinerary.

Airline alliances such as Star Alliance and OneWorld design RTW fares with mileage caps, typically 30,000–40,000 miles per segment. When paired with high‑earning credit cards, the points required shrink dramatically, turning a multi‑thousand‑dollar adventure into a manageable redemption.

Practical Tips for Maximising Value

Start with a clear itinerary: map each wonder to the nearest hub airport and align segments with alliance mileage caps. Use the welcome bonus early, then funnel daily spend through Card C to keep the points pool growing.

Monitor promotional transfer bonuses—occasionally, partners offer 30% extra on transfers, shaving off 180,000 points from the total requirement.

Finally, book the RTW ticket at least six months ahead to lock in lower taxes and avoid peak‑season surcharges. With disciplined spending and strategic transfers, the new 7 wonders become an attainable reality on a single ticket.
