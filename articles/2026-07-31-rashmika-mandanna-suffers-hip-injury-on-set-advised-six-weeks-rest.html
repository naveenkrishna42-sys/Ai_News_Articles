Rashmika Mandanna suffers hip injury on set, advised six weeks rest

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Rashmika Mandanna has suffered a major hip injury on a film set.
  • Doctors have advised her to take six weeks of complete rest.
  • The incident highlights a worrying trend of injuries among Tollywood stars.
  • Production schedules of her upcoming films may be impacted.

Injury on Set

Actor Rashmika Mandanna has suffered a major hip injury while shooting for an upcoming film, according to reports. Doctors attending to her have advised a strict six-week rest period to ensure proper recovery. The exact nature of the accident or the specific project she was working on has not been officially confirmed by her team.

The injury comes at a time when several Tollywood stars have faced similar setbacks, raising questions about safety protocols on film sets. Mandanna, known for her roles in both Telugu and Hindi cinema, was reportedly in the middle of a demanding shoot schedule when the incident occurred.

Recovery and Impact

Medical experts suggest that a hip injury of this severity requires careful rehabilitation. The mandated six-week rest means Mandanna will have to step away from active shooting, potentially delaying multiple projects lined up for her. Sources close to the actor indicate that she is currently following her doctor's advice and is in good spirits.

The downtime could affect the release timelines of her upcoming films, though no official statements have been made by the production houses involved. In the past, similar injuries to lead actors have led to revised schedules and increased budgets, a concern that is now looming over Mandanna's current commitments.

Rising Injury Trend in Tollywood

Mandanna's injury adds to a growing list of Tollywood celebrities who have recently been sidelined due to accidents on set. Reports from industry insiders point to a pattern of rigorous action sequences and high-octane stunts being performed without adequate safety measures. This trend is taking a heavy toll not only on the stars' health but also on film budgets, as production halts lead to cost overruns.

Several industry experts have called for a review of safety standards, urging producers to invest in better equipment and trained stunt coordinators. While the allure of realistic action drives box office success, the physical risks to actors cannot be ignored, they argue.

What Happens Next

As Rashmika Mandanna begins her recovery period, fans and industry watchers will be keenly awaiting an official update from her team. The coming weeks will also reveal how her production houses adjust their shooting plans. For now, the priority remains her health, and a full recovery will be crucial before she returns to the big screen.
