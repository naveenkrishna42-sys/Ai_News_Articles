Iran Strikes Bahrain, Kuwait, Jordan After US Hits Nuclear Site

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Iran attacked military targets in Bahrain, Kuwait, and Jordan.
  • The strikes followed a US attack on an Iranian nuclear facility.
  • Iran has threatened to block the Strait of Hormuz to oil and gas shipments.

Retaliatory Strikes Across the Gulf

Iran launched a series of military strikes on targets in Bahrain, Kuwait, and Jordan early Monday. The attacks came hours after the United States confirmed it had hit an Iranian nuclear site.

Reports from the region indicate multiple projectiles were fired at military installations in the three countries. All three are key US allies in the region and host American military personnel.

US Confirms Nuclear Site Hit

The US military confirmed it completed its ninth consecutive night of strikes on Iran. President Donald Trump said the strikes were carried out in 'honour' of American soldiers killed in earlier engagements.

The attacks mark a significant escalation in the conflict. The targeting of a nuclear facility is widely seen as a red line that could trigger a broader war.

Strait of Hormuz Threat

In a dramatic response, Iran has vowed that not a 'single drop' of oil or gas will pass through the Strait of Hormuz. The narrow waterway is a critical chokepoint for global energy supplies.

Approximately 20% of the world's petroleum passes through the strait. Any disruption would send shockwaves through global energy markets and economies dependent on Gulf oil.

Casualties and Ground Reality

A third US service member has been killed in the conflict, according to CNN. Officials have not yet confirmed casualty figures from the strikes on Bahrain, Kuwait, and Jordan.

Air raid sirens were reported in several cities across the targeted countries. Civilian populations have been advised to stay indoors as the situation remains fluid.

What to Watch

The coming hours will be critical. The US has not indicated whether it will retaliate for the strikes on its allies, and Iran has not ruled out further attacks. The world is watching for any signs of a diplomatic off-ramp or further military escalation.
