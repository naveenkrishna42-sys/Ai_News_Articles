# 90 — TivraNews Advertising Policy

Advertising must complement the reading experience without degrading layout stability or content quality.

## 1. Clean Ad Experience
- Ads must never overlap primary navigation, article headlines, or editorial CTAs.
- Prevent Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) by maintaining fixed-dimension responsive ad containers.
- Never place deceptive ad units or disguised buttons that imitate system alerts.

## 2. Configuration & Ads.txt
- Maintain clean, authorized digital sellers lists in `/ads.txt` (Google AdSense publisher `pub-6206818645443465`).
