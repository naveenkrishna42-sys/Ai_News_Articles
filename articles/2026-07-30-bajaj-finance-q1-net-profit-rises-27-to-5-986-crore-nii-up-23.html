Bajaj Finance Q1 Net Profit Rises 27% to ₹5,986 Crore, NII Up 23%

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Consolidated net profit for the June quarter rose 27% year-on-year to ₹5,986 crore.
  • Revenue from operations stood at ₹23,165 crore, in line with market estimates.
  • Net interest income (NII) advanced 23%, supported by higher loan disbursements.
  • Standalone net profit for Q1FY27 increased 29% to ₹5,345.50 crore.
  • Analysts remain positive on Bajaj Housing Finance, with Motilal Oswal setting a target of ₹95.

Strong Profit Growth Driven by Loan Book Expansion

Bajaj Finance Ltd reported a consolidated net profit of ₹5,986 crore for the first quarter ended June 2026, a 27% increase from the same period last year. The result exceeded analyst expectations, supported by robust loan growth across its retail and commercial segments.

Revenue from operations came in at ₹23,165 crore, matching market estimates. The non-banking financial company (NBFC) has consistently delivered double-digit profit growth over the past several quarters, driven by expanding customer base and higher disbursements.

Net Interest Income and Asset Quality

Net interest income (NII), the difference between interest earned and interest paid, grew 23% year-on-year. This was underpinned by a steady net interest margin, which remained stable despite a competitive lending environment.

Asset quality indicators showed marginal improvement. Gross non-performing assets (NPAs) as a percentage of total loans stood lower than the previous quarter, indicating healthy recovery trends and prudent underwriting practices by the lender.

Standalone Performance and Housing Finance Subsidiary

On a standalone basis, Bajaj Finance reported a 29% jump in net profit to ₹5,345.50 crore for Q1FY27. The standalone numbers reflect the core lending operations before consolidation adjustments.

Bajaj Housing Finance, a key subsidiary, continues to attract analyst attention. Motilal Oswal has maintained a 'Neutral' rating on the housing finance arm with a target price of ₹95. The brokerage cited stable asset quality and growth prospects in the affordable housing segment as key factors.

The housing finance subsidiary has been expanding its presence in tier-2 and tier-3 cities, contributing to the overall growth of the Bajaj Finance group.

Market Reaction and Outlook

Shares of Bajaj Finance traded higher following the earnings announcement, reflecting investor confidence in the company's execution capabilities. The stock has been a consistent performer in the BFSI space over the past year.

The company's management has indicated a focus on deepening penetration in existing markets while maintaining credit discipline. With a strong capital base and diversified product suite, Bajaj Finance is well-positioned to capture incremental demand in the formal lending space.

Analysts will closely watch commentary on margin trajectory and loan book composition in the coming quarters, especially given the evolving interest rate environment and competitive pressures from banks and fintech players.
