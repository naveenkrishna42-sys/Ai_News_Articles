Spider-Man: Brand New Day Box Office: Tom Holland Film Shatters Records in India

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day grosses over Rs 455 crore in India, surpassing Avengers: Endgame
  • Film eyes third-best U.S. second weekend with $143M collection
  • Tom Holland starrer continues record-breaking run globally

A Record-Breaking Second Saturday

Spider-Man: Brand New Day has blown past expectations, exploding on its second Saturday at the box office. The Tom Holland starrer continues its relentless march, now setting its sights on Hollywood's all-time records in the United States.

According to box office tracking, the film is eyeing the third-best second weekend in U.S. history with a projected $143 million. This puts it in rarefied air, rubbing shoulders with the biggest blockbusters ever released.

India Embraces the Spider-Man Phenomenon

In India, the film has achieved a monumental feat. It has grossed over Rs 455 crore, surpassing the lifetime collections of Avengers: Endgame in the country. This is a seismic shift in Indian box office dynamics, where Marvel's biggest ensemble film previously reigned supreme.

The Telegraph India confirmed that Brand New Day has beaten Avengers: Endgame's collections, a testament to the massive fan following Tom Holland's iteration of Spider-Man enjoys in the subcontinent. The film's Hindi-dubbed version is believed to be a major driver of this unprecedented success.

A Global Juggernaut

The film's performance is not limited to India and the U.S. It has become a global juggernaut, with audiences flocking to theaters in droves. The buzz around the movie, fueled by strong word-of-mouth and positive reviews, has translated into sustained footfalls well beyond its opening weekend.

Trade analysts point to the film's emotional core and high-octane action as key reasons for its repeat viewing. The crossover appeal of the character, combined with Holland's charm, has created a perfect storm at the box office.

Reviews and Reactions

The critical reception has also been warm. The Hindu's First Day First Show review praised the film, while also featuring an interview with actor James Norton. This blend of critical and commercial success is rare for a superhero tentpole, but Brand New Day is managing to achieve both.

The film's ability to draw in non-traditional superhero audiences, including families and casual moviegoers, has been particularly notable. It has become a cultural event rather than just a movie release, with spoiler discussions dominating social media timelines.

What This Means for Hollywood

The record-breaking run has significant implications for Hollywood. It signals a strong return to theaters for event films, even in a post-pandemic landscape. The projected $143 million second weekend in the U.S. would be a clear sign that audiences are willing to show up for the right cinematic experience.

With such numbers, Spider-Man: Brand New Day is not just a hit; it's a phenomenon. It has redefined what a superhero film can achieve at the box office, both in India and globally.

The Road Ahead

As the film enters its third weekend, all eyes will be on whether it can sustain this momentum. With no major competition on the horizon, it is well-positioned to continue its record-breaking spree. The next milestone to watch will be its lifetime global gross, which could potentially challenge the all-time top spot.

For now, the box office belongs to Spider-Man. The question is not if it will break more records, but how many more it can shatter before its theatrical run concludes.
