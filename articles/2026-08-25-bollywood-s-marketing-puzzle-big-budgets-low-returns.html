Bollywood's marketing puzzle: Big budgets, low returns

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Marketing spend is high but returns are low
  • City tours may not be the most effective promotion tool
  • Industry needs to rethink marketing strategies

Marketing spend vs. box office performance

Bollywood's marketing spend is on the rise, but the returns on investment are not matching expectations. A recent report shows that a film can earn Rs. 21.89 crore without any promotions, but only Rs. 5.52 crore despite city tours. This raises questions about the effectiveness of current marketing strategies.

City tours: A double-edged sword

City tours are a popular promotion tool in the film industry. They involve actors visiting different cities to promote their films. However, the recent report suggests that city tours may not be as effective as other promotion tools. This could be due to the fact that city tours are more of a traditional marketing tool and may not reach the right audience.

The need for a rethink

The film industry needs to rethink its marketing strategies. The current strategies seem to be more about quantity than quality. The industry needs to focus on creating a strong brand and reaching the right audience. This could involve a mix of traditional and digital marketing tools.
