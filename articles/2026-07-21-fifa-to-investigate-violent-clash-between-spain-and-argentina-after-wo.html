FIFA to investigate violent clash between Spain and Argentina after World Cup final

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • FIFA has confirmed an investigation into a post-match brawl between Spain and Argentina players after the World Cup final.
  • Argentina midfielder Leandro Paredes is reported to have sparked the clash at the final whistle.
  • Players from both teams could face bans as FIFA reviews footage and match reports.

FIFA has launched an investigation into a violent on-pitch brawl that broke out between Spain and Argentina players immediately after the 2026 World Cup final. The incident, which unfolded at the final whistle, has drawn widespread condemnation and could result in significant bans for those involved.

The governing body confirmed on Monday that it is reviewing footage and match official reports from the final. The clash, described by several outlets as a 'total disgrace', marred what was supposed to be a crowning moment for one of the two footballing giants.

Paredes at the centre of the storm

According to multiple reports, Argentina midfielder Leandro Paredes sparked the brawl. The flashpoint occurred just after the referee blew the final whistle, with Paredes reportedly confronting Spanish players on the pitch.

Eyewitness accounts suggest the altercation quickly escalated. Punches were thrown, and several players from both sides had to be separated by security and coaching staff. The exact trigger for the confrontation remains unclear, but the scene has been widely circulated on social media.

Potential sanctions and fallout

FIFA's disciplinary committee will now examine the evidence. Players found guilty of violent conduct face multi-match suspensions that could extend into international breaks for the next year.

The incident has overshadowed the sporting outcome of the final. Argentina, who were seeking to defend their 2022 title, now find themselves under a cloud of scrutiny. Spanish football authorities are yet to comment on the actions of their own players, but have pledged full cooperation with the FIFA investigation.

The fallout has been immediate. Pundits and former players have labelled the scenes a 'disgrace' for the sport. The Spanish and Argentine national teams have not yet released official statements regarding the brawl.

What comes next

FIFA is expected to deliver its findings within the next two weeks. Both national federations will be required to submit their own reports. Until then, the focus remains on whether the governing body will make an example of those involved to deter future incidents at major tournaments.
