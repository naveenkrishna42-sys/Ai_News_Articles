The Visit That Sparked Speculation

Gaurav Khanna's recent visit to the sets of Lock Upp 2, where his ex-wife Akanksha Chamola is a contestant, has sparked considerable buzz and speculation. The television actor's appearance raised eyebrows, especially given the ongoing rumors about their divorce. Now, Akanksha herself has broken her silence, offering a peek into Gaurav's intentions and her perspective on the matter.

Akanksha's Side of the Story

In an exclusive interview with NDTV, Akanksha Chamola stated that Gaurav Khanna came to the Lock Upp set to clear his name and address the allegations made against him by his current girlfriend, Orry's, former partner, Karanvir Bohra. Akanksha revealed, "He wanted to set the record straight and show that he wasn't using me or manipulating me in any way."

Addressing Orry's Claims

Orry had previously claimed that Akanksha was using Gaurav Khanna's fame to gain visibility on Lock Upp. Akanksha hit back at these allegations, stating, "How can someone accuse me of using Gaurav when he has been the one supporting me throughout this journey?"

Gaurav's Stance on Accepting Akanksha's Bisexuality

During his visit, Gaurav Khanna also addressed his stance on accepting Akanksha's bisexuality. He told Akanksha, "I found it hot, and it's a part of who you are. I accept and respect that."
