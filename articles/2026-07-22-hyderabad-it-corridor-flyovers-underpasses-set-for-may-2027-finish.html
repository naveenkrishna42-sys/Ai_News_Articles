Hyderabad IT corridor flyovers, underpasses set for May 2027 finish

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Flyovers and underpasses in Hyderabad's IT corridor targeted for completion by May 2027.
  • Project aims to ease traffic congestion in the tech hub area.
  • Officials have not yet disclosed the total cost or detailed timeline.

Infrastructure push for tech hub

Hyderabad's IT corridor is set to get a major infrastructure upgrade, with flyovers and underpasses now targeted for completion by May 2027. The project aims to tackle the chronic traffic congestion that plagues the area, home to thousands of tech parks and offices.

Officials involved in the planning have confirmed the new deadline. They said the structures will be built along key stretches of the corridor to improve connectivity and reduce travel time for commuters. The exact number of flyovers and underpasses has not been disclosed yet.

Decongesting the IT corridor

The IT corridor, which includes areas like Hitech City, Madhapur, and Gachibowli, sees heavy traffic daily. Thousands of employees and residents rely on these roads. The new infrastructure is expected to provide smoother movement at major intersections and bottlenecks.

State government sources indicated that the project is part of a broader plan to modernise Hyderabad's road network. The focus is on creating grade separators that allow traffic to flow without signals at critical points. Work is likely to begin in phases over the coming months.

Funding and timeline

Details on the total cost of the project have not been made public. Officials have not confirmed whether central or state funds will be used, or if a public-private partnership model is being considered. The May 2027 deadline suggests a construction period of nearly three years from now.

Previous infrastructure projects in the city have faced delays due to land acquisition and utility shifting. It remains to be seen whether the current timeline will hold. Authorities have not yet issued a detailed schedule of milestones.

What to watch

With the target set, attention will now turn to the tendering process and contractor selection. Commuters will be watching for any disruption during construction. The success of this project could set a template for similar upgrades in other congested parts of Hyderabad.
