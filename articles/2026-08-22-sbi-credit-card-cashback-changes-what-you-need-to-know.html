SBI Credit Card Cashback Changes: What You Need to Know

From April 1, 2026, the State Bank of India (SBI) will introduce new cashback rates for its credit cards. This guide covers the new rates, eligibility criteria, and how to apply for the card to claim welcome bonuses.

New Cashback Rates

SBI will revise its cashback rates across various categories. Here are the new rates:

  • Fuel: 3% cashback on fuel purchases
  • Groceries: 2% cashback on grocery stores
  • Online Shopping: 1.5% cashback on online shopping platforms
  • Dining: 1% cashback on restaurant bills

The cashback will be credited to your SBI credit card account within 7-10 days of the transaction.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for the SBI credit card, you must meet the following criteria:

  • Indian citizen
  • Minimum age of 21 years
  • Valid PAN card
  • Minimum income of ₹30,000 per month

How to Apply

You can apply for the SBI credit card online through the SBI website or mobile app. Follow these steps:

  1. Visit the SBI website or open the SBI app
  2. Click on the "Credit Cards" section
  3. Select the SBI credit card you are interested in
  4. Fill in the required details and submit the application
  5. Upload the necessary documents, including your PAN card and income proof
  6. Wait for the approval, which usually takes 7-10 business days

Once approved, you will receive your SBI credit card, and you can start enjoying the new cashback rates.

Welcome Bonuses

SBI offers welcome bonuses for new cardholders. Here are the details:

  • Welcome cashback: 5% cashback on the first ₹20,000 spent within the first 90 days
  • Joining fee waiver: The annual fee is waived for the first year

To claim the welcome bonuses, make sure to activate your card and start using it within the first 90 days.
