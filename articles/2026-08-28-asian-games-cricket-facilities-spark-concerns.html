Asian Games cricket facilities spark concerns

Cricket Boards Express Growing Concerns Over Asian Games Facilities

As the 2026 Asian Games draw near, cricket boards from India and Pakistan are raising alarms over the quality of facilities prepared for the event. Reports suggest that the pitch at Korogi Athletic Park, one of the main venues, is causing unease among top teams, while the accommodation set up for the players has been described as 'tentative' at best.

Pitch Quality and Accommodation Issues Raise Doubts

The pitch at Korogi Athletic Park, which will host the cricket matches, has been questioned by several cricket experts. The surface is reportedly not up to the standard expected for international matches, leading to concerns about player safety and performance. Additionally, the accommodation facilities for the players have been criticized, with some players expressing dissatisfaction over the lack of proper infrastructure.

Indian Team Considers Second-String Option

In light of these concerns, the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) is reportedly considering the possibility of sending a second-string team to the Asian Games. This move would allow the BCCI to assess the quality of the facilities and ensure that the players are not put at a disadvantage. The decision has sparked a debate within the cricketing community, with some arguing that it is a necessary precaution, while others believe that the BCCI should not compromise on the quality of its team.

Broader Impact on Asian Games Cricket

The concerns over the Asian Games facilities come at a time when cricket is looking to regain its prominence in the Asian Games. The event is seen as a platform to showcase the sport on the continental stage, and the quality of the facilities will play a crucial role in determining the success of the cricket tournament. If the issues are not addressed, it could lead to a loss of interest from top teams and a decline in the standard of the competition.
