Cristiano Ronaldo likes Instagram post alleging FIFA helped Argentina reach World Cup final

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ronaldo liked an Instagram post claiming FIFA favoured Argentina in the World Cup.
  • The post alleged corruption to ensure Lionel Messi reached the final.
  • Argentina lost the 2026 World Cup final to Spain.
  • FIFA officials and fans have reacted strongly to the claim.
  • Ronaldo has not publicly commented on the like.

Cristiano Ronaldo has ignited a fresh storm online after his Instagram account liked a post alleging that FIFA helped Argentina reach the 2026 World Cup final. The post, which has since been widely shared, claimed that the global football body showed favouritism to ensure Lionel Messi played for the title.

The post that started it all

The controversial post suggested that FIFA wanted Argentina to win the World Cup and used its influence to get them past earlier rounds. It accused the organisation of corruption and bias, specifically pointing to decisions that benefited the South American side during the tournament.

Ronaldo's like on the post did not go unnoticed. Fans and football pundits quickly spotted the interaction, and screenshots began circulating on social media platforms within hours.

Argentina's final defeat adds fuel

The timing of the like is significant. Argentina lost the World Cup final to Spain, making the claim of FIFA favouritism even more contentious. Critics argue that if such bias existed, the outcome would have been different.

Supporters of the allegation point to several controversial refereeing calls in Argentina's matches. However, no official evidence has been presented to support the corruption theory.

Reactions from all sides

FIFA officials have dismissed the claim outright. A former World Cup official told the BBC, "I promise you there is no favouritism." The organisation has not issued a formal statement regarding Ronaldo's like.

Fans erupted on social media, with some calling Ronaldo a "cheater" for endorsing the post. Others defended him, saying a like does not necessarily mean agreement. The debate remains polarised.

Ronaldo's silence speaks volumes

As of now, Cristiano Ronaldo has not commented on the matter. His representatives have not responded to requests for clarification. The Portuguese star is known for his intense rivalry with Messi, and this incident has only deepened the narrative of tension between the two legends.

Whether the like was intentional or accidental is unclear. Instagram does not have a dislike button, and users sometimes tap accidentally while scrolling.

What comes next

The story is unlikely to fade quickly. With no statement from Ronaldo or FIFA, speculation will continue to grow. Fans will watch closely to see if Ronaldo addresses the controversy or lets it blow over.
