Jantar Mantar erupts in celebration after Dharmendra Pradhan resigns as Education Minister

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Dharmendra Pradhan resigned as Union Education Minister on the first day of student protests at Jantar Mantar.
  • Crowds at the protest site celebrated the resignation, with some chanting 'Now Modi should go'.
  • Pralhad Joshi has been appointed as the new Union Education Minister.

Jantar Mantar erupts in celebration

Delhi's Jantar Mantar, the traditional site for protests, witnessed scenes of celebration on Sunday after Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan submitted his resignation. The announcement came on the first day of a student-led protest, though the exact trigger for the resignation remains unclear.

Video footage from the site shows crowds cheering and clapping as news of the resignation spread. Some protesters were heard chanting “Now Modi should go,” according to eyewitness accounts. The atmosphere was one of jubilation, with many students and activists hugging each other.

Pradhan's resignation and political fallout

Dharmendra Pradhan offered to quit amid growing tensions over education policy. A BJP leader later claimed that Pradhan's resignation was linked to the student protest, but officials have not yet confirmed a direct connection. The resignation marks a significant shift in the Union Cabinet.

Pralhad Joshi has been appointed as the new Union Education Minister. Joshi, a seasoned BJP leader from Karnataka, previously held the portfolio of Parliamentary Affairs. His appointment signals a change in approach to the education sector, which has been a focal point of the government's agenda.

Reactions from celebrities and public figures

The resignation drew reactions from across the country. Bollywood actors Alia Bhatt and Ishaan Khatter, along with filmmaker Zoya Akhtar, responded to the news on social media. One post read, “Gen Z showed up,” highlighting the role of young protesters in the development.

Political analysts noted that control over education has been a key priority for the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS). The sacking of Pradhan is seen by some as a major setback for the organisation. However, the government has not commented on internal party dynamics.

What happens next

Pralhad Joshi will now oversee the Ministry of Education. His first task will be to address the concerns raised by student groups. The protests at Jantar Mantar are expected to continue, with organisers demanding broader reforms in education policy.

All eyes will be on Joshi's initial statements and actions. Whether the change in leadership will quell the unrest or fuel further demands remains to be seen.
