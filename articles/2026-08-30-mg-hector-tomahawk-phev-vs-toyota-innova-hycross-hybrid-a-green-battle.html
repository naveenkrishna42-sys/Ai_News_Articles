MG Hector Tomahawk PHEV vs Toyota Innova Hycross Hybrid: A Green Battle

Introduction

In India's rapidly evolving automotive market, hybrid SUVs are emerging as a key player. The MG Hector Tomahawk PHEV and Toyota Innova Hycross Hybrid are two of the most anticipated models in this segment. This article delves into their specifications, features, and environmental impact to help you make an informed decision.

Specifications

Let's start with the specifications. The MG Hector Tomahawk PHEV is powered by a 3.0L diesel engine that delivers 250 PS of power and 500 Nm of torque. It comes with a 17.3 kWh battery pack that offers a range of 300 km. On the other hand, the Toyota Innova Hycross Hybrid is equipped with a 2.4L petrol engine that produces 167 PS of power and 230 Nm of torque. It has a 1.6 kWh battery pack that provides a range of 400 km.

Features

Now, let's look at the features. The MG Hector Tomahawk PHEV comes with a 10.25-inch touchscreen infotainment system, a 12.3-inch digital instrument cluster, and a 360-degree camera. It also offers a karaoke feature that allows you to sing along to your favorite songs. The Toyota Innova Hycross Hybrid, on the other hand, comes with a 12.3-inch touchscreen infotainment system, a 12.3-inch digital instrument cluster, and a 360-degree camera. Environmental Impact

The environmental impact of these SUVs is a significant factor to consider. The MG Hector Tomahawk PHEV has a lower battery capacity, which means it has a higher environmental impact. The Toyota Innova Hycross Hybrid, on the other hand, has a higher battery capacity, which means it has a lower environmental impact.
