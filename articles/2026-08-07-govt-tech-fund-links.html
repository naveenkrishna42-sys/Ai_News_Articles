Government Tech Fund Allocation Raises Questions

The Indian government's Rs 1 lakh crore deep-tech financing initiative, aimed at boosting innovation, has allocated funds to private firms linked to the panel that selected them. This has sparked concerns about conflict of interest in the selection process.

Official reports have not yet confirmed the exact amount allocated to these firms, but sources indicate that the funds are part of the Rs 2,000 crore sanctioned under the scheme. The government has clarified that the selection process is transparent, but critics argue that the links between the firms and the selection panel are troubling.

Selection Process Under Scrutiny

The RDI scheme, launched by the government to encourage deep-tech innovation, has been dogged by controversy over the selection process. Parliament has been informed that there are concerns about conflict of interest in the year-old fund, with some firms linked to the selection panel receiving significant allocations.

Experts point out that the lack of transparency in the selection process has raised suspicions about the allocation of funds. While the government has called reports of conflict of interest misleading, officials have not yet provided a clear explanation of the selection criteria used to allocate funds to these firms.

Government Response

In response to the allegations, the government has stated that the selection process is fair and transparent. However, officials have not yet provided details about the measures in place to prevent conflict of interest. The government has sanctioned Rs 2,000 crore under the scheme, but the allocation of these funds has been criticized by some as being biased towards firms with links to the selection panel.

What's Next

As the controversy surrounding the government tech fund allocation continues to unfold, officials are likely to face increased scrutiny over the selection process and the allocation of funds. The government may need to provide more transparency about the selection criteria and the measures in place to prevent conflict of interest.
