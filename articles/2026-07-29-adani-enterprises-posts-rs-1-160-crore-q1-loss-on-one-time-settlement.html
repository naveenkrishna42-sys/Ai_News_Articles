Adani Enterprises posts Rs 1,160 crore Q1 loss on one-time settlement charge

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Adani Enterprises reported a net loss of Rs 1,160 crore for Q1 FY27, compared to a profit a year ago.
  • The loss was driven by a one-time settlement charge of Rs 2,644 crore.
  • Revenue surged 50% year-on-year, while EBITDA reached a record high of Rs 5,642 crore.

One-time charge drags bottomline into red

Adani Enterprises Ltd, the flagship firm of the Adani Group, reported a consolidated net loss of Rs 1,160 crore for the first quarter of FY27. This compares to a net profit of Rs 1,455 crore in the same period last year.

The swing to loss was largely due to a one-time settlement charge of Rs 2,644 crore. The company did not immediately disclose the nature of the settlement, but sources indicated it relates to a past regulatory or contractual dispute that has now been resolved. Analysts had expected the charge to weigh on earnings.

Revenue jumps 50%, EBITDA at record high

Despite the bottomline hit, operating performance remained strong. Revenue from operations rose 50% year-on-year, driven by robust performance across its emerging businesses, including airports, data centres and roads.

Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) touched a record high of Rs 5,642 crore. This marks the highest-ever quarterly EBITDA for the company, underscoring the underlying momentum in its core and new-economy assets. The EBITDA margin also improved significantly compared to the previous year.

Settlement charge clouds otherwise strong quarter

Excluding the one-time charge, the company would have reported a healthy profit. The management described the settlement as a one-off event that cleans up the balance sheet and removes legal overhang. Analysts are likely to focus on the core operating performance in their valuations.

The airports business, which now handles a significant share of India's air passenger traffic, continued to scale up. The data centre arm also added capacity during the quarter, in line with the group's expansion into digital infrastructure. The mining and logistics verticals saw steady demand from the industrial sector.

Market reaction and outlook

Shares of Adani Enterprises saw volatile trading in early deals on Thursday, as investors digested the loss figure against the strong operational numbers. The stock has been on a recovery path over the past year, driven by the group's deleveraging and capital raising efforts.

Adani Enterprises is the group's incubator for new businesses, and its performance is closely watched as a bellwether for the broader conglomerate. The company is expected to provide further details on the settlement charge and its impact on future earnings in the post-earnings conference call later today.

The next few quarters will show whether the company can sustain its revenue momentum and translate record EBITDA into consistent net profits, once the impact of the one-time charge fades.
