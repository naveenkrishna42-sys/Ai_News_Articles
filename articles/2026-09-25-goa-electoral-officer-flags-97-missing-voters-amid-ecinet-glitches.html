Goa Electoral Officer Flags 97 Missing Voters Amid ECINet Glitches

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Team 📅 2026-09-25 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Goa CEO sent eight emails to ECI in a week over missing electors.
  • ECINet’s decentralised design left field officials unable to fix the error.
  • Omission triggered partisan blame, with Congress demanding rollback of the summary revision exercise.

Overview of the Voter Roll Issue

Goa’s Chief Electoral Officer publicly disclosed that 97 eligible electors were absent from the latest electoral rolls. The officer described the situation as "Many Added" yet noted the absence persisted despite repeated attempts to rectify it. The disclosure came after a series of internal communications highlighted a breakdown in the voter‑list update process.

Background and Repeated ECI Outreach

Between 17 and 24 September 2026, the Goa CEO dispatched eight formal emails to the Election Commission of India seeking clarification and correction. Each email listed the specific polling stations where the 97 names were missing. Despite this urgency, the ECI’s response did not result in immediate inclusion of the omitted voters.

Technical Flaws in ECINet

The Election Commission’s ECINet platform, designed to decentralise voter‑list management, allows field officers to view data but restricts editing authority to central administrators. This separation meant that even after the Goa office identified the discrepancy, it could not directly amend the rolls. System logs showed that the error originated during a batch upload of summary revision data, where a validation rule inadvertently filtered out the 97 records.

Political Repercussions

The omission sparked a partisan exchange, with the Congress party accusing the BJP‑led state government of exploiting the error to influence upcoming local polls. Congress leaders demanded a rollback of the summary revision exercise and a parliamentary inquiry into ECINet’s reliability. BJP representatives countered that the issue was purely technical and urged calm while the ECI instituted a remedial drive.

Way Forward and Recommendations

Experts suggest three immediate steps: (1) grant limited edit rights to district officers for critical corrections, (2) introduce automated reconciliation checks after bulk uploads, and (3) establish a transparent audit trail for all changes made to voter lists. Implementing these measures could prevent similar oversights and restore public confidence in the electoral roll maintenance process.
