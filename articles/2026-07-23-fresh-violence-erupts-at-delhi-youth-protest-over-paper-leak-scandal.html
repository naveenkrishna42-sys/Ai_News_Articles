Fresh Violence Erupts at Delhi Youth Protest Over Paper Leak Scandal

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Fresh violence erupted at a youth protest in Delhi demanding the education minister's resignation.
  • The protest is linked to the ongoing NEET paper leak scandal that has caused political deadlock in Parliament.
  • Police used water cannons to disperse protesters; several injuries reported.
  • The opposition has hardened its stance, refusing a parliamentary discussion without the minister's resignation.
  • PM Modi has vowed to set up fast-track courts to handle paper leak cases.

Clashes at Delhi Protest

Fresh violence broke out on Thursday at a youth protest in Delhi demanding the resignation of the Union Education Minister over the alleged NEET paper leak. The demonstration, which began peacefully, turned chaotic as protesters clashed with police near the Jantar Mantar area.

Witnesses reported that a section of the crowd attempted to break through barricades, prompting police to use water cannons and mild force to disperse them. At least a dozen protesters and a few police personnel sustained minor injuries in the scuffles.

The protest was organised by a coalition of student groups who have been agitating for weeks over the irregularities in the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) results.

Political Fallout in Parliament

The violence on the streets mirrored the deadlock inside Parliament. The Monsoon Session remained paralysed for the fourth consecutive day on Thursday as opposition MPs moved adjournment motions demanding a discussion on the paper leak issue.

Opposition parties have hardened their stance, insisting that no debate can take place unless the Education Minister resigns. “No discussion without the Education Minister’s resignation,” a senior opposition leader said, echoing the demand of the protesting students.

The ruling party has so far refused to accept the resignation demand, calling it a politically motivated move to stall legislative business.

PM Modi's Pledge on Fast-Track Courts

Amid the escalating crisis, Prime Minister Narendra Modi addressed the nation on Wednesday, vowing to set up fast-track courts specifically to handle paper leak cases. “We will ensure strict punishment for those who play with the future of our youth,” the PM said.

The announcement comes as a direct response to the growing public anger over the NEET paper leak, which has allegedly affected thousands of students across the country. However, critics argue that the promise of fast-track courts does not address the immediate demand for accountability at the top.

Judicial Reaction and Ongoing Investigations

Meanwhile, the Chief Justice of India, in a separate event, reacted to the situation by reportedly saying, “Name is nothing,” in response to PM Modi's vow to punish offenders. The comment was made while a photograph of Dharmendra Pradhan, the current Education Minister, was being displayed, though the context remained unclear.

Investigations into the paper leak are ongoing, with multiple agencies looking into the role of coaching centres and middlemen. The Central Bureau of Investigation has so far arrested several individuals, but the exact scale of the leak remains under wraps.

Student leaders have rejected the government's assurances, calling for a transparent investigation and the immediate resignation of the minister. “We will not back down until justice is served,” a protest organiser said.

As the situation remains tense, all eyes are now on the government's next move. With Parliament stalled and protests showing no signs of abating, the coming days will determine whether the ruling party can break the political impasse or if the crisis will deepen further.
