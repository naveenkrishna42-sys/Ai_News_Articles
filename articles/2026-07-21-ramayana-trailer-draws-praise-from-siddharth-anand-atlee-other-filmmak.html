‘Ramayana’ Trailer Draws Praise From Siddharth Anand, Atlee, Other Filmmakers

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Siddharth Anand, Atlee, and others have lauded the trailer of Nitesh Tiwari's 'Ramayana'.
  • The film starring Ranbir Kapoor and Yash is reported to release on November 8.
  • Sunny Deol has been confirmed to play the role of Hanuman in the epic.

Trailer Launch Gets Industry Nod

The trailer for Nitesh Tiwari's ambitious 'Ramayana' has drawn strong reactions from fellow filmmakers. Siddharth Anand and Atlee are among those who have publicly praised the director's vision for the epic.

Details of their specific comments remain scarce, but the early buzz points to a high level of anticipation within the film industry. The trailer was unveiled at a launch event in Delhi, sparking immediate conversation online.

Release Date and Box Office Clash

The trailer has also confirmed the film's release date. 'Ramayana' is set to hit theatres on November 8. This puts it in direct competition with 'Godzilla: Minus Zero' for the Diwali window.

While official box office projections are not yet available, the clash of a large-scale mythological drama with a Hollywood monster movie is set to be a major test for the Indian film market. Both films are expected to command significant screen counts.

Full Cast Revealed

The complete cast of 'Ramayana' has now been unveiled. Sunny Deol will be playing the role of Hanuman, adding a major name to the already star-studded lineup. The casting choice has been widely discussed on social media.

Ranbir Kapoor leads the film as Lord Ram, while Yash, known for his work in Kannada cinema, plays a pivotal role. The film marks one of the most expensive and ambitious productions in Indian cinema history.

Online Hype and Eye Health?

A purported trailer of the film surfaced online shortly after the Delhi launch, though its authenticity was not immediately confirmed by the makers. The event also saw Ranbir Kapoor wearing sunglasses, which led to some media outlets linking it to monsoon eye care advice—a detail that has little to do with the film itself.

Nevertheless, the marketing machine for 'Ramayana' is in full swing. Fans are closely tracking every update, from casting announcements to promotional events. The production is backed by a major studio and is expected to have a wide release across multiple languages.

What remains to be seen is how the film's visual effects and storytelling will match the monumental scale of the epic. The team is reportedly working on a grand narrative that spans multiple kingdoms and characters.

As the November release approaches, more promotional material and a proper theatrical trailer are expected. The industry will be watching closely to see if 'Ramayana' can replicate the box office success of other recent mythological films.
