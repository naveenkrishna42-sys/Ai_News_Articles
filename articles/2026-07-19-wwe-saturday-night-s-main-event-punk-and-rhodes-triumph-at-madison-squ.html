WWE Saturday Night's Main Event: Punk and Rhodes triumph at Madison Square Garden

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • CM Punk and Cody Rhodes defeated Gunther and Sami Zayn in the main event
  • Event took place at Madison Square Garden on July 18, 2026
  • Full results from the show include multiple championship matches

WWE returned to Madison Square Garden on Saturday night for the latest edition of Saturday Night's Main Event, delivering a stacked card that culminated in a high-stakes tag team contest.

The main event saw CM Punk and Cody Rhodes team up to face the formidable duo of Gunther and Sami Zayn. The match had been heavily promoted in the weeks leading up to the show, with fans eager to see two of WWE's biggest babyfaces take on the dominant Intercontinental Champion and his volatile ally.

Main event delivers in New York

Punk and Rhodes controlled much of the early action, using their speed and technical prowess to keep Gunther grounded. The former world champions worked well as a unit, isolating Zayn and cutting off any attempt at a tag.

Gunther eventually got the hot tag and unleashed his signature hard-hitting offense, decimating both opponents with chops and power moves. Zayn played the role of the scrappy underdog, taking punishment before firing back with kicks and high-risk maneuvers.

The finish came after Zayn accidentally caught Gunther with a Helluva Kick while attempting to hit Rhodes. Punk capitalized, striking Zayn with a GTS for the decisive pinfall. The crowd erupted as Punk and Rhodes celebrated in the ring after the bout.

Full card results from MSG

Saturday Night's Main Event featured several other notable matches. The WWE Women's Championship was on the line as Bianca Belair defended against a determined Bayley, who had earned the opportunity after winning a number one contender's match on Raw.

In the tag team division, The Usos defeated The Judgment Day's Damian Priest and Finn Balor in a hard-fought contest that saw multiple near-falls and interference from both sides.

The show also included a segment featuring Drew McIntyre, who called out Seth Rollins for a potential future match. Rollins responded via video, setting the stage for what could be a major program in the coming months.

What's next for the winners

With this victory, Punk and Rhodes have cemented themselves as a formidable tag team, though both have their sights set on singles gold. Punk has been circling the World Heavyweight Championship, while Rhodes remains focused on unfinished business with Roman Reigns.

Gunther and Zayn, meanwhile, will likely look to regroup. The Intercontinental Champion has been on a dominant run, and one loss is unlikely to derail his momentum.

WWE has not yet announced the next edition of Saturday Night's Main Event, but the company is expected to continue the brand's revival with another event in the coming months.
