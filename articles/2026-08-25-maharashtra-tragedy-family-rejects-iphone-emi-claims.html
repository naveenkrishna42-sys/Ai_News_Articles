Family Rejects iPhone EMI Claims After Tragedy

In a shocking turn of events, the family of the victims from the recent Maharashtra cliff tragedy has dismissed claims that the incident was caused by electromagnetic interference (EMI) from iPhones. The family, led by the victims' relative, has stated that there is no evidence to support the conspiracy theory, and that the incident was a tragic accident.

Background of the Tragedy

The tragedy occurred on a popular tourist spot in Maharashtra, where a group of people were enjoying a day out. Suddenly, one of the victims, Kunal, fell from the cliff, and his parents, who were trying to save him, also fell. The incident left the entire group in shock and raised questions about the safety measures on the spot.

Family's Response to iPhone EMI Claims

The family's relative, who was present at the scene, has dismissed the iPhone EMI theory, stating that there was no evidence to support the claims. The relative has stated that the family is not aware of any iPhones being used in the area, and that the incident was a tragic accident.

Local Authorities' Investigation

Local authorities have launched an investigation into the incident, and have stated that they are looking into all possible causes. The authorities have also stated that they are working to ensure that safety measures are in place to prevent such incidents from happening in the future.

Broader Implications

The incident has raised questions about the safety measures on popular tourist spots in Maharashtra, and has sparked a debate about the role of technology in such incidents. The family's rejection of the iPhone EMI theory has also highlighted the importance of having evidence to support any claims, and the need to approach such incidents with an open mind.
