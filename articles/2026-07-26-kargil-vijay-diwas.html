Kargil Vijay Diwas: A Day of Remembrance

India is observing the 27th anniversary of the Kargil Vijay Diwas, a day that marks the country's victory over Pakistan in the Kargil war. The war, which took place in 1999, was a significant milestone in India's military history.

The Kargil war was a turning point in India's military doctrine, with the country learning valuable lessons from the conflict. According to former army chief General VP Malik, the Kargil lessons shaped Operation Sindoor, a subsequent military operation.

Lessons Learnt and Challenges Ahead

The Kargil war highlighted the importance of being prepared for proxy wars and unconventional threats. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh noted that the enemy's intentions remain unchanged, and proxy war is still a challenge that India faces. The country has been working to strengthen its military capabilities and counter these threats.

The anniversary events included a display of drone capabilities and a Shaurya Sandhya ceremony to honour the Kargil heroes. The events were attended by dignitaries, including Rajnath Singh, who paid tribute to the martyrs and hailed their bravery.

Honouring the Heroes

Rajnath Singh honoured the Kargil heroes in Ladakh on the anniversary of Operation Vijay. The event was a testament to the country's gratitude towards the soldiers who fought and sacrificed their lives in the war.

Legacy of the Kargil War

The Kargil war has left a lasting legacy on India's military doctrine and strategy. The country has been working to modernize its military and enhance its capabilities to counter emerging threats. The lessons learnt from the Kargil war continue to shape India's military approach and preparedness.
