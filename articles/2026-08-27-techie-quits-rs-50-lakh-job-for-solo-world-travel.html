Techie quits Rs 50 lakh job for solo world travel

📅 2026-08-27 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • High-paying job offer rejected, triggering career change
  • Solo travel across multiple continents planned
  • Financial independence and personal growth motivations

Career Shift Amid Economic Uncertainty

A 29-year-old software engineer has made headlines by quitting his Rs 50 lakh annual salary job to embark on a solo world tour. The decision comes amid reports of widespread job insecurity in the tech sector, with many companies downsizing or freezing hiring.

Motivations Behind the Decision

The engineer cited several reasons for his career change. First, he was left without any job offer after applying to several companies. Second, he wanted to experience new cultures and broaden his horizons. Third, he aims to achieve financial independence by working remotely during his travels.

Travel Plans and Financial Considerations

The engineer has already started his journey, visiting several countries in Asia and Europe. He plans to continue traveling for at least a year, working remotely from different locations. He has set aside a portion of his savings for this adventure, but he acknowledges that he will need to earn money during his travels to cover expenses.

Broader Implications for the Tech Industry

This decision sheds light on the growing trend of employees leaving high-paying jobs for personal fulfillment. It also highlights the challenges faced by the tech industry, which has been hit hard by economic downturns. Companies need to focus on employee well-being and job security to retain talent.

Expert Opinions and Analysis

Industry experts believe that this trend is likely to continue, with more employees prioritizing personal growth and work-life balance over career advancement. They also suggest that companies should consider flexible work arrangements and remote work options to attract and retain talent.
