Telangana Police Crack Principal Murder Case Using Lifestyle Clues

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Two Class 10 students arrested for killing school principal with 27 stab wounds during burglary
  • Police traced accused through Rs 1.51 lakh cash, an iPhone, and phone records showing a call to someone nicknamed 'babu'
  • The accused boys' lavish lifestyle, inconsistent with their background, raised suspicions during investigation

Two Students Arrested in Principal's Killing

Police in Telangana have arrested two Class 10 students for the murder of a school principal who was stabbed 27 times during a burglary at her residence in Kondamallepally. The investigation moved quickly once investigators focused on lifestyle inconsistencies among local youth.

The accused, both minors, have been remanded to judicial custody. Authorities have not disclosed their identities due to their age.

How the 'Babu' Call Exposed the Killers

Investigators found the principal's phone among the stolen items. Records showed her last incoming call was from a number registered to a local resident nicknamed 'babu'. This lead brought two Class 10 students into the investigation's focus.

Police subsequently recovered Rs 1.51 lakh in cash and an iPhone from the accused. The seizure raised immediate questions about how teenagers could afford such items.

Lifestyle Clues Proved Decisive

Sources close to the investigation said the accused had been living beyond their apparent means. Their spending patterns did not match family backgrounds, alerting both locals and police to their sudden wealth.

Detectives believe the burglary was premeditated. They allege the students targeted the principal's home knowing she kept valuables there. The attack occurred during evening hours.

School Community Shocked

Colleagues of the principal described her as well-respected within the school community. Education department officials have promised support for staff and students processing the trauma.

Campus counselling services have been arranged. Parents have been urged to monitor children's behaviour for signs of distress.

Investigation Continues

Police are examining whether others may have assisted the accused. Forensic teams completed evidence collection at the crime scene last week.

Authorities have not confirmed whether the stolen items have been fully recovered. The case is expected to move to juvenile court proceedings soon.
