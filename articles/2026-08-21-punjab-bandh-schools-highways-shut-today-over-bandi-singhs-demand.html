Punjab Bandh: Schools, Highways Shut Today Over Bandi Singhs Demand

📅 2026-08-21 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sikh groups have called for a seven-hour Punjab-wide bandh starting today.
  • Major highways and educational institutions remain closed across the state.
  • Security has been tightened at critical transit points, particularly near the Chandigarh-Mohali border.

Statewide Shutdown Disrupts Normal Life

Normal life across Punjab faces significant disruptions today as various Sikh organisations enforce a seven-hour bandh. The protest aims to push for the release of 'Bandi Singhs'—Sikh prisoners who supporters claim have completed their sentences but remain incarcerated.

Markets in several districts are observing a voluntary closure, while public transport services remain sparse. Highways connecting major towns have seen limited movement, with protesters gathering at key intersections to voice their demands.

Impact on Education and Transit

Educational institutions across the state have largely suspended operations for the day. Punjab University and other affiliated colleges have announced the postponement of scheduled examinations to prevent student inconvenience during the shutdown.

Commuters are advised to avoid non-essential travel. Bus services, both state-run and private, are operating at reduced capacity, leading to long wait times at major terminals. Many private operators opted to keep fleets off the roads to avoid potential damage amid the demonstrations.

Tightened Security Measures

Local authorities have deployed heavy security contingents at strategic locations to prevent any untoward incidents. Police presence is particularly visible at the Chandigarh-Mohali border, a frequent flashpoint for protests in the region.

The Qaumi Insaf Morcha, a key group behind the call for the bandh, has maintained that the demonstration will remain peaceful. However, district administrations have ordered police units to remain on high alert throughout the seven-hour window.

Protest Rationale

The core issue remains the long-standing demand for the release of Sikh prisoners. Supporters argue that these individuals have served their mandatory jail terms and that their continued detention is unjust.

While the state government has not yet issued a formal statement regarding the specific demands of the protesters today, the situation remains fluid. Law enforcement officials continue to monitor the ground situation as the protest progresses through the afternoon.

Authorities will evaluate the impact of the shutdown by evening to determine if additional security measures are required for the remainder of the day.
