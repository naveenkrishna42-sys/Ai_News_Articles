Taapsee Pannu’s Psychological Thriller Benchmark

Taapsee Pannu’s Gandhari, released in 2026, has set a new benchmark for psychological thrillers in Indian cinema. The film, directed by Vikram Bhatt, follows the story of a woman whose life unravels after a mysterious accident, revealing layers of deception and trauma. Pannu’s performance earned her critical acclaim and a National Film Award for Best Actress. Gandhari’s success has sparked interest in other thrillers featuring the actress, demonstrating her ability to command attention in suspenseful narratives.

Top 5 Indian Thrillers Starring Taapsee Pannu

1. The Silent Witness

Directed by Rakesh Omprakash Mehra, The Silent Witness is a legal thriller that explores the dark side of the justice system. Taapsee Pannu plays a defense attorney who takes on a high-profile case involving a controversial law. The film delves into themes of injustice and moral corruption, offering a gripping narrative that keeps viewers on the edge of their seats. The Silent Witness is a must-watch for fans of courtroom dramas and legal thrillers.

2. The Ghost in the Machine

Directed by Anurag Kashyap, The Ghost in the Machine is a science fiction thriller that blends psychological horror with futuristic technology. Taapsee Pannu stars as a scientist who discovers a way to communicate with the dead. The film explores themes of grief, technology, and the ethics of playing with life and death. The Ghost in the Machine is a visually stunning and intellectually engaging film that showcases Pannu’s range as an actress.

3. The Last Witness

Directed by Karan Johar, The Last Witness is a political thriller that examines the consequences of whistleblowing. Taapsee Pannu plays a journalist who uncovers a government conspiracy. The film is a high-stakes cat-and-mouse game between truth seekers and those who would silence them. The Last Witness is a compelling exploration of media ethics and the power of information in a democratic society.

4. The Shadow of the Past

Directed by Sanjay Leela Bhansali, The Shadow of the Past is a historical thriller that blends romance with suspense. Taapsee Pannu stars as a woman whose past love affair resurfaces during a royal wedding. The film is a tale of love, betrayal, and revenge, set against the backdrop of 19th-century India. The Shadow of the Past is a visually lavish and emotionally charged film that showcases Pannu’s ability to bring depth and nuance to her roles.

5. The Hunted

Directed by Zoya Akhtar, The Hunted is a crime thriller that explores the dark underbelly of Mumbai’s underworld. Taapsee Pannu plays a detective who teams up with a journalist to expose a corrupt politician. The film is a fast-paced and intense exploration of power, corruption, and the fight for justice. The Hunted is a gripping and thought-provoking film that showcases Pannu’s versatility as an actress.
