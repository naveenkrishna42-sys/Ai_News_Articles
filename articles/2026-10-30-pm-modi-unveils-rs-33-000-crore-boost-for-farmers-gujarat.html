PM Modi Kicks Off Two-Day Gujarat Visit with Major Financial Outlays

Prime Minister Narendra Modi commenced a significant two-day visit to Gujarat on October 31, 2026, marking a pivotal moment for both the nation's agricultural sector and the state's infrastructure development. The visit is highlighted by the release of the 24th installment of the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) scheme, amounting to a substantial Rs 19,000 crore. Concurrently, the Prime Minister is set to inaugurate and lay the foundation stone for development projects worth over Rs 14,000 crore across Gujarat, alongside the inauguration of Bharat Parv in Kevadia.

This comprehensive initiative underscores the government's dual focus on rural economic upliftment through direct financial support and accelerating urban and regional development via large-scale infrastructure investments. The combined financial commitment, exceeding Rs 33,000 crore, reflects a strategic push towards inclusive growth and national progress.

Direct Aid: The Rs 19,000 Crore PM-KISAN Disbursal

The release of Rs 19,000 crore under the PM-KISAN scheme represents a critical injection of funds directly into the bank accounts of millions of eligible farmers nationwide. This 24th installment continues the government's commitment to providing income support to small and marginal farming families, a cornerstone of its agricultural policy. The scheme's architecture, based on Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), ensures transparency and efficiency, circumventing intermediaries and delivering aid directly to beneficiaries.

PM-KISAN, launched in 2019, provides a financial benefit of Rs 6,000 per year, disbursed in three equal installments of Rs 2,000 every four months. This regular income support assists farmers in meeting various agricultural expenses, including purchasing seeds, fertilizers, and other inputs, thereby enhancing agricultural productivity and alleviating financial stress. The latest disbursal is expected to further bolster rural economies and empower farmers to sustain their livelihoods amidst evolving market dynamics.

Gujarat's Economic Impetus: Rs 14,000 Crore Development Projects

Beyond the national agricultural support, PM Modi's visit also focuses on substantial developmental strides within Gujarat. The inauguration and foundation stone laying for projects worth over Rs 14,000 crore, particularly in areas like Banaskantha, signal a robust commitment to enhancing the state's infrastructure and economic capabilities. These projects encompass a range of sectors, including road connectivity, irrigation, urban development, and social infrastructure, designed to create new opportunities and improve quality of life.

Such large-scale investments are crucial for driving economic growth, generating employment, and attracting further private investment. Improved infrastructure is a catalyst for industrial expansion, trade, and overall regional development, positioning Gujarat as a key contributor to India's economic trajectory. The strategic allocation of funds aims to address specific regional needs while contributing to the state's broader development vision.

Cultural Diplomacy: Bharat Parv in Kevadia

A significant cultural highlight of the visit is the inauguration of Bharat Parv in Kevadia. This national festival serves as a vibrant platform to showcase India's rich and diverse cultural heritage, art forms, and culinary traditions. Kevadia, already transformed into a major tourist and cultural destination centered around the Statue of Unity, provides an ideal backdrop for an event promoting national unity and celebrating India's achievements.

Bharat Parv aims to foster a deeper understanding and appreciation of India's composite culture among citizens and visitors alike. Such cultural initiatives play a vital role in nation-building, promoting social cohesion, and presenting India's soft power on a global stage. The event is expected to draw a large number of participants, further boosting tourism and local economies in the region.
