Nifty, Bank Nifty open flat as traders eye RBI policy cues

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty opened flat on Monday, Aug 3, 2026.
  • Traders stayed cautious ahead of RBI monetary policy decision later this week.
  • Banking stocks showed mixed trends, with private lenders slightly firmer.

Muted start for benchmarks

Indian equity benchmarks opened on a flat note on Monday, August 3, 2026, as investors refrained from making big bets ahead of the Reserve Bank of India's monetary policy announcement due later this week. The Nifty 50 hovered near its previous close, while the Bank Nifty showed limited movement in early trades.

Market participants said the mood was cautious, with global cues offering little direction. Asian markets were mixed in morning trade, and domestic indices mirrored that lack of conviction.

Banking stocks in focus

The Bank Nifty, which tracks the country's top lenders, saw a subdued opening. Private sector banks were slightly firmer, while public sector lenders remained under pressure. Traders noted that the upcoming policy decision has kept banking stocks range-bound, as investors await clarity on interest rate trajectory.

Broader markets, including midcap and smallcap indices, traded in a narrow band. Sectoral indices were mixed, with IT and pharma showing marginal gains, while metal and energy stocks slipped.

RBI policy the key trigger

The central bank's rate-setting panel is scheduled to meet later this week, and its decision is expected to set the tone for the market in the near term. Analysts believe that any hawkish surprise could weigh on banking stocks, while a status quo on rates may offer some support.

Foreign institutional investors have been net sellers in recent sessions, while domestic institutions have provided some buying support. This tug-of-war has kept the market in a tight range.

What to watch

Investors will keep a close eye on global crude oil prices and the movement of the rupee against the dollar. Any sharp move in either could influence market sentiment in the coming sessions.

The RBI's policy announcement is the next major event on the domestic calendar. Until then, analysts expect the market to remain in a wait-and-watch mode, with stock-specific action taking precedence.
