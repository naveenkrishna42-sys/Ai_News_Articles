Jharkhand HC halts cancellation of JPSC exam appointments

📅 2026-08-21 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jharkhand High Court stays cancellation of appointments made through JPSC exams 11-13
  • Affected candidates allege government betrayal and demand CBI probe
  • Petition filed in Supreme Court seeks investigation into exam irregularities

Jharkhand High Court Stays Cancellation of JPSC Exam Appointments

The Jharkhand High Court has issued a stay order halting the cancellation of appointments made through the 11th to 13th Jharkhand Public Service Commission (JPSC) exams. The order came in response to a petition filed by affected candidates who argue that their appointments were based on valid exam results.

Protests and Allegations

Job aspirants across the state have expressed their disappointment and frustration over the High Court's decision. They allege that the government's sudden move to cancel their appointments is a betrayal of their trust. Many have taken to social media to voice their concerns and demand a thorough investigation into the matter.

Government's Response

Official sources have not yet commented on the High Court's order or the allegations made by the candidates. However, it is understood that the government has been reviewing the situation and is likely to take further action in the coming days.

Supreme Court Intervention

A petition has been filed in the Supreme Court seeking a CBI probe into the irregularities surrounding the JPSC and JSSC exams. The petitioners argue that there have been serious lapses in the conduct of the exams and that the results cannot be relied upon. The Supreme Court is yet to take any action on the petition.

What Happens Next?

The situation in Jharkhand remains tense, with both the government and the affected candidates awaiting further developments. The High Court's order has sparked a new round of protests and demonstrations, and it is expected that the matter will continue to receive significant attention from the media and the public. The outcome of the Supreme Court's intervention will also play a crucial role in determining the future of the affected candidates' appointments.
