Bengaluru techie in coma after hit-and-run during bike taxi ride

📅 2026-08-01 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Techie critically injured in hit-and-run while on bike taxi
  • Accident occurred in Bengaluru; victim in coma
  • Police investigation underway to trace the fleeing vehicle

A young techie is battling for life in a Bengaluru hospital after a hit-and-run accident that occurred while he was riding a bike taxi. The incident has once again thrown the spotlight on road safety and the vulnerabilities of two-wheeler commuters in the city.

The Accident

The victim, whose identity has not been officially released, was travelling as a pillion on a bike taxi when an unidentified vehicle allegedly rammed into them and sped away. The impact threw both riders off the two-wheeler, leaving the techie with severe head injuries.

He was rushed to a nearby hospital by passers-by, where doctors reportedly placed him on ventilator support. He remains in a coma, with his condition stated to be critical. The bike taxi driver is said to have sustained minor injuries and is cooperating with the police.

Police Investigation Underway

Local police have registered a case of hit-and-run and are scanning CCTV footage from the area to trace the offending vehicle. Sources suggest that the accident occurred during late evening hours, when visibility is often reduced and traffic rules are frequently flouted.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact spot of the incident or the sequence of events leading up to the crash. A senior police officer said that teams have been deployed to identify the vehicle based on eyewitness accounts and camera records.

Rising Concerns Over Road Safety

This incident adds to a growing list of road accidents involving bike taxis in Bengaluru, a city that has seen a surge in app-based two-wheeler rides. While these services offer convenience, safety standards often come under scrutiny, especially during night rides.

Activists have repeatedly called for stricter enforcement of traffic norms and better infrastructure for two-wheeler riders. The lack of dedicated lanes and poor street lighting on several stretches remain persistent issues, making riders more susceptible to such mishaps.

What Happens Next

As the techie's family waits by his bedside, the police are expected to make a breakthrough in tracing the hit-and-run vehicle. The case also raises questions about accountability for bike taxi operators and whether more can be done to ensure passenger safety.

All eyes will be on the investigation's progress and the victim's recovery in the coming days. For now, the incident serves as a grim reminder of the risks that thousands of daily commuters take on Bengaluru's roads.
