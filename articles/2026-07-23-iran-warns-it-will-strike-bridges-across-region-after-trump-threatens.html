Iran Warns It Will Strike Bridges Across Region After Trump Threatens Power Plants

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Iran has warned it will strike bridges across the region in response to threats.
  • The warning comes after Trump threatened to target Iranian power plants.
  • Iran vows to continue its operations in the ongoing West Asia conflict.

Escalating Rhetoric: Tehran's Stern Warning

Iran has issued a stark warning, stating it will strike bridges across the region if its infrastructure is targeted. The announcement comes directly after former US President Donald Trump threatened to attack Iranian power plants.

Tehran's message was clear and immediate. Any aggression against its energy infrastructure would be met with a broad, regional response, targeting key logistical and transit points.

The Trigger: Trump's Threat to Power Plants

The latest escalation stems from Trump's explicit threat to bomb Iranian power plants. This statement has significantly raised the stakes in an already volatile confrontation between the two nations.

Iranian officials have interpreted Trump's words as a direct act of war. The response from Tehran indicates a strategy of asymmetric retaliation, aiming to disrupt regional stability rather than engage in a direct, symmetrical conflict.

Broader Conflict: West Asia War and Regional Fallout

This exchange is unfolding against the backdrop of a wider West Asia war. Iran has vowed to continue its strikes, signalling no intention of de-escalation despite increasing international pressure.

Fears are also growing over a potential incursion into Kuwait. Reports indicate Iran is prepared to bomb its own land as a defensive measure, highlighting the extreme measures being considered.

Mining the Strait of Hormuz

In a separate but related development, Iran has warned shipping companies that the southern route through the Strait of Hormuz has been mined. This move threatens a vital global energy chokepoint.

The warning to commercial vessels adds a dangerous new dimension to the conflict. A disruption to oil tanker traffic through the strait would have immediate and severe consequences for global energy markets.

A 9/11-Style Threat

Adding to the tension, Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) has issued a chilling threat to the United States. The IRGC warned of a 'national mourning in America,' invoking imagery reminiscent of the September 11 attacks.

This psychological warfare tactic is designed to instil fear and demonstrate the IRGC's reach. It represents a significant departure from standard military posturing and indicates a high level of hostility.

Officials have not yet confirmed the operational capability behind these threats. Analysts are closely watching for any tangible military movements in the Persian Gulf region.

The coming days will be critical. The world now watches to see if these threats translate into direct military action or if diplomatic channels can reopen before a wider regional war erupts.
