Woman falls from moving train in Chennai while resisting phone snatch bid

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 30-year-old woman fell from a moving suburban train near Chennai on Monday.
  • The incident occurred when she resisted a mobile phone snatcher.
  • She has been hospitalised with injuries; police have launched an investigation.

Incident on the move

A 30-year-old woman fell from a moving suburban train near Chennai on Monday evening while trying to fend off a mobile phone snatcher. The incident took place on a busy stretch of the Chennai suburban railway network.

Police said the woman was travelling alone in a ladies compartment when a man attempted to snatch her mobile phone. As she resisted, the snatcher pushed her, causing her to lose her balance and fall out of the moving train.

Immediate response

Passengers on the train pulled the emergency chain, stopping the train. Fellow commuters and railway staff rushed to help the woman, who was found lying near the tracks with injuries.

She was taken to a nearby government hospital, where doctors said her condition was stable. Officials have not yet disclosed the exact nature of her injuries.

Police investigation

Government Railway Police (GRP) have registered a case and are examining CCTV footage from the station and the train. A senior police officer said efforts are on to identify and apprehend the suspect.

"We are looking at footage from multiple angles. The suspect is believed to have fled the scene after the woman fell," a GRP official said. No arrests have been made so far.

Safety concerns resurface

The incident has once again raised questions about passenger safety on Chennai's suburban trains, especially for women travelling alone. Commuters have demanded better lighting, more CCTV cameras, and increased police presence inside compartments.

A local activist said, "Women should not have to fear for their safety while travelling on public transport. The authorities must act fast."

The railway administration has not yet issued a statement on the incident.

What happens next: Police are expected to release a suspect description once CCTV footage is analysed. The woman is likely to be discharged in a day or two, after which her statement will be recorded.
