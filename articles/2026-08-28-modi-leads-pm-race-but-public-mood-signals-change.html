Modi leads PM race, but public mood signals change

📅 2026-08-28 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Modi leads with 48.7% support, but voter sentiment indicates a potential change in leadership
  • Rahul Gandhi and Vijay Kanth are also prominent contenders

Modi Leads PM Polls, but Sentiment Signals Change

Narendra Modi has retained his position as the top choice for India's Prime Minister, according to the latest MOTN (Mood of the Nation) poll. With 48.7% support, Modi's lead is significant, but the poll also indicates a shift in voter sentiment, suggesting a potential change in leadership.

Rahul Gandhi and Vijay Kanth Among Top Contenders

Rahul Gandhi, the leader of the I.N.D.I.A. bloc, has been identified as the 2029 PM candidate by Tamil Nadu minister P Viswanathan. Meanwhile, Vijay Kanth, the Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, has been pitched as the next PM by his party, and he is among the top three choices in the PM polls. This indicates a strong opposition movement against Modi's leadership.

Political Parties Actively Campaigning

Political parties are actively campaigning to gain support for their candidates. Posters and rallies are playing a significant role in this process. For instance, posters in Tuticorin have been pitching Tamil Nadu CM Vijay as the next PM. This is an attempt to mobilize voters and create awareness about the candidates.

Expert Analysis and Critical Assessment

Political analysts believe that the shift in voter sentiment is a significant development. It indicates that a large section of the population is not satisfied with Modi's leadership and is looking for a change. However, Modi's strong lead in the PM polls suggests that he still has a significant following. The opposition parties are likely to intensify their campaigning efforts to gain more support.

Conclusion

The PM polls indicate a strong lead for Narendra Modi, but the shift in voter sentiment suggests a potential change in leadership. Political parties are actively campaigning to gain support for their candidates. The opposition movement is likely to intensify, and it will be interesting to see how this develops in the coming months.
