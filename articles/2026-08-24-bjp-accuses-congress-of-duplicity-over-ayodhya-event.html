BJP accuses Congress of 'duplicity' over Ayodhya event

📅 2026-08-24 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • BJP accuses Congress of insensitivity during holy month
  • Congress defends event, calls BJP's reaction 'overreaction'
  • Political row highlights tensions over religious symbols in politics

Political Row Erupts Over Ayodhya Event

Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath has accused the Congress-led opposition of displaying 'duplicity' by organizing a non-vegetarian event in Ayodhya during the holy month of Sawan. The BJP leader's remarks have sparked a political row, with Congress hitting back, calling the BJP's reaction an 'overreaction'.

BJP's Allegations

Adityanath, who is also the national president of the BJP, claimed that the Congress event violated religious sensitivities. He accused the opposition of serving fish, a non-vegetarian item, at the event, which he argued was an insult to the Sanatan Dharma, the traditional Hindu way of life.

Congress' Defense

Congress leader Priyanka Gandhi Vadra, who is also the national president of the Congress, has defended the event. She claimed that the event was a celebration of the local culture and that the fish served was part of a traditional dish. She also accused the BJP of trying to 'politicize' the event.

Broader Implications

The political row over the Ayodhya event highlights the tensions between the BJP and the Congress over religious symbols in politics. The BJP has been accused of trying to 'Hindutva' the Ayodhya issue, while the Congress has been accused of trying to 'secularize' it.

Expert Analysis

Political analyst Rajiv Kumar of the Centre for the Study of Developing Societies (CSDS) believes that the political row over the Ayodhya event is a reflection of the broader tensions between the BJP and the Congress over the role of religion in politics. He argues that the BJP's focus on religious symbols is a way to consolidate its support base, while the Congress' focus on secularism is a way to appeal to a broader cross-section of society.

Key Facts

  • Ayodhya is a city of great religious significance in India, with several important temples.
  • The month of Sawan is considered a particularly sacred time for Hindus.
  • The political row over the Ayodhya event has sparked a wider debate over the role of religion in politics.
