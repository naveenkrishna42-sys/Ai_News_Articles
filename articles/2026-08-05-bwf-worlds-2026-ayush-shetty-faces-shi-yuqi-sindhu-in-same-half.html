BWF Worlds 2026: Ayush Shetty faces Shi Yuqi, Sindhu in same half

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ayush Shetty to face world No. 1 Shi Yuqi in the first round of BWF World Championships.
  • PV Sindhu, seeded 9th, is placed in the same half as the top seed.
  • Satwiksairaj Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty are seeded 5th in men's doubles.

Draw throws up early test for Shetty

The BWF World Championships draw has handed young Indian shuttler Ayush Shetty a daunting opener. He will face top-ranked Shi Yuqi of China in the very first round — a match that pits a rising talent against the sport's current benchmark.

Shetty, who has been climbing the ranks steadily, now faces the toughest possible start. Shi Yuqi, the defending champion, has been in commanding form this season, and the draw offers little room for error.

This is the kind of test that can define a career early. A win would be seismic; a loss, however expected, would still sting given the stage.

Sindhu's path in the same half

Two-time champion PV Sindhu, seeded 9th, finds herself in the lower quarter of the top half — the same section as Shi Yuqi. While she avoided the very top seeds in her immediate draw, the road ahead is anything but smooth.

Sindhu has been chasing a third world title, a feat that would etch her name deeper into badminton history. Her seeding reflects her current ranking, but her pedigree on this stage remains unmatched among active players.

The draw means she could cross paths with the Chinese star in the later rounds. That potential showdown looms large, but Sindhu's immediate focus will be on navigating her early matches without hiccups.

Satwik-Chirag get 5th seeding

In men's doubles, Satwiksairaj Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty have been given the 5th seeding. The pair, often referred to as SatChi, have been India's most consistent doubles act in recent years.

Their seeding places them among the favourites, though the competition in the men's doubles draw is fierce. The top pairs from China, Indonesia and Malaysia will all be gunning for the title.

Satwik and Chirag have shown they can beat anyone on their day. But consistency over a week-long championship remains their biggest challenge.

What to watch out for

All eyes will be on Ayush Shetty's opening match — an early-round blockbuster that could set the tone for India's campaign. A strong showing, even in defeat, would signal his arrival on the biggest stage.

For Sindhu, every match from the quarterfinals onward will carry the weight of history. She has been here before, and knows exactly what it takes to go all the way.

The tournament promises high drama, and India's contingent — led by Sindhu and SatChi — will be hoping to convert promise into podium finishes.
