Roku hikes streaming device prices up to 50% on component crunch

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Roku has increased prices on its streaming hardware by up to 50%.
  • The price hike is attributed to global component shortages and rising manufacturing costs.
  • Some models, including popular streaming sticks, see increases of up to $50 or 60%.
  • The new prices are effective immediately, though some retailers may still have old stock at previous rates.

Roku, the company behind some of the most popular streaming devices in India and abroad, has quietly raised prices on its entire line of streaming hardware. The increases range from modest bumps to as much as 50% on certain models, and in some cases, prices have jumped by up to $50.

The move comes as a surprise to many consumers who have come to rely on Roku's affordable streaming sticks and set-top boxes. The company has confirmed the price adjustments, citing the ongoing global shortage of semiconductors and other electronic components.

Which devices are affected?

The price hike applies to Roku's entire streaming player lineup. Reports from multiple tech outlets, including Android Authority and Ars Technica, indicate that the Roku Express, Streaming Stick, and Streaming Stick+ have all seen significant price increases. The Roku Ultra, the company's premium offering, has also been affected.

According to The Verge, the price increase on some streaming sticks has gone up by as much as 60%. AOL.com reports that the company is blaming "component shortages" for the steep rise. The exact figures vary by model, but the most common increase cited is around 50%.

Why the sudden hike?

Roku is not alone in facing supply chain pressures. The global chip shortage, which began during the pandemic, has continued to disrupt manufacturing across the electronics industry. From automobiles to smartphones, companies have struggled to secure enough components to meet demand.

In a statement to media, Roku said the price increases are necessary to offset the higher costs it is paying for parts. The company noted that it has absorbed some of the costs internally, but the magnitude of the recent component price spikes has made a retail price adjustment unavoidable.

Limited-time old pricing?

Cord Cutters News reports that some retailers may still have Roku devices at the older, lower prices for a limited time. The report suggests that consumers who act quickly may be able to snag a device before the new pricing fully rolls out across all channels.

However, this window appears to be closing fast. Most major online retailers have already updated their listings to reflect the new prices. Roku's own website now shows the increased costs.

The impact is likely to be felt most by budget-conscious consumers who choose Roku for its affordability. With prices now closer to competitors like Amazon's Fire TV Stick or Google's Chromecast, Roku's value proposition has weakened.

Industry analysts are watching to see if other streaming hardware makers follow suit. So far, neither Amazon nor Google has announced similar broad price increases, but both have faced the same component shortages.

For now, Roku users in India—where the company has a growing presence—may also see price changes as local distributors adjust their margins. Roku has not issued a separate statement for the Indian market, but global pricing trends typically trickle down.

What happens next? Consumers looking for a new streaming device may want to compare prices across brands before making a purchase. The component shortage shows no signs of easing soon, which means hardware prices across the board could remain elevated for the rest of the year.
