Supreme Court Orders Withdrawal of FIRs Against Most CJP Protesters

The Supreme Court of India has quashed First Information Reports (FIRs) filed against most protesters associated with the Communist Party of India (Marxist) (CPI-M). The court's decision, delivered on September 1, 2026, has significant implications for the rights of protesters and the legal framework surrounding public demonstrations.

Hardened Criminals to Face Trial

However, the court has ruled that 'hardened criminals' who have a criminal antecedent will face trial. This distinction raises questions about the fairness and consistency of the legal process, as it suggests that certain individuals are exempt from the law.

CJP Leader Saurav Das Tells Supreme Court About Withdrawing September 5 Protest

CJP leader Saurav Das has informed the Supreme Court about the withdrawal of the September 5 protest march in Delhi. This decision comes after the Centre has acted to withdraw FIRs against the protesters. The protest, which was scheduled to take place in the national capital, was a significant event in the political calendar of India.

Centre Acts to Withdraw FIRs Against Protesters

The Centre has taken steps to withdraw FIRs against the protesters, which has been welcomed by the CJP leadership. This move is seen as a positive development, as it indicates a willingness on the part of the government to engage in dialogue with opposition parties.
