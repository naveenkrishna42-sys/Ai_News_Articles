SIR: Form-6, declaration forms given to all BLOs, says CEO

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • CEO confirms distribution of Form-6 and declaration forms to all BLOs in SIR
  • Move aims to streamline voter registration and correction process
  • Officials urge eligible citizens to verify and update their details

Forms distributed across SIR

The Chief Electoral Officer (CEO) of Karnataka has confirmed that Form-6 and declaration forms have been handed over to all Booth Level Officers (BLOs) in the SIR (Special Intensive Revision) areas. The announcement was made on Monday, marking a key step in the ongoing electoral roll revision exercise.

According to officials, the distribution is part of a broader effort to ensure every eligible voter can register or make corrections without hurdles. Form-6 is primarily used for new voter registration, while declaration forms cater to address changes and other updates.

What this means for voters

With forms now in place, BLOs are expected to conduct door-to-door campaigns to assist citizens, especially first-time voters and those who have recently relocated. The CEO's office has emphasised that no eligible person should be left out of the electoral roll.

Residents in SIR zones can approach their respective BLOs to obtain the forms or seek guidance on filling them. Officials have also urged people to carry necessary documents, such as proof of age and address, to avoid delays.

Officials urge timely verification

The electoral authorities have asked citizens to verify their names on the existing voter list and report discrepancies immediately. This includes checking for duplicate entries, incorrect spellings, or outdated addresses.

BLOs have been instructed to complete field verification within a stipulated timeframe and submit processed forms to the next level of the electoral machinery. The CEO's office has assured that adequate staff and resources have been deployed for the task.

While the exact number of BLOs in the SIR region has not been officially disclosed, the scale of the exercise suggests a significant push ahead of upcoming elections. Officials have not yet confirmed whether the revision will extend beyond the current SIR zones.

Next steps for citizens

Electoral officials expect the revision process to continue over the coming weeks, with periodic reviews to track progress. They have also set up helplines and designated offices to address public queries.

Citizens are advised to keep an eye on local announcements from the CEO's office and cooperate with BLOs during their visits. Timely submission of forms will help ensure that the final electoral roll is accurate and comprehensive.

Looking ahead, authorities are likely to release a schedule for the publication of the draft roll, followed by a window for claims and objections. Voters in SIR areas should treat this as an opportunity to secure their right to vote without last-minute complications.
