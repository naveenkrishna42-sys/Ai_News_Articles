10 must-visit Indian temples for spiritual seekers in 2025

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Religion Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kedarnath, Badrinath, and Rameswaram top the list for their religious significance and scenic locations.
  • Architectural marvels like Meenakshi Temple and Konark Sun Temple showcase India's ancient craftsmanship.
  • Tirupati Balaji and Golden Temple attract millions of devotees annually for their spiritual aura.

Sacred journeys waiting to be taken

India's temples are more than places of worship. They are living museums of art, history, and faith. For those planning a pilgrimage in 2025, the choices can be overwhelming. Here are ten temples that deserve a spot on your itinerary.

Kedarnath Temple, Uttarakhand

Perched in the Garhwal Himalayas, Kedarnath is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas. The temple opens for visitors only between April and November. The trek or helicopter ride to reach it is as rewarding as the darshan itself.

In 2023, over 10 lakh pilgrims visited Kedarnath, a number expected to rise in 2025. The temple's ancient stone structure has survived centuries of harsh weather.

Badrinath Temple, Uttarakhand

Badrinath, another of the Char Dham sites, sits along the Alaknanda River. The temple is dedicated to Lord Vishnu and remains open from April to November. The adjoining hot springs, Tapt Kund, are considered holy.

Pilgrims often combine Badrinath with Kedarnath for a complete Char Dham yatra. The 2025 season is expected to see improved road access and better facilities.

Rameswaram Temple, Tamil Nadu

Ramanathaswamy Temple in Rameswaram is famous for its long corridors and ornate pillars. It is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas and a key stop on the Char Dham circuit. The temple's 1,200 granite columns create a visual spectacle.

Devotees perform rituals at the temple's 22 theerthams (holy water bodies). The island town itself offers a peaceful coastal retreat.

Sri Venkateswara Temple, Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh

Tirupati Balaji is the richest and most visited temple in the world. Located on Tirumala hill, it draws over 2 crore pilgrims each year. The temple's architecture and the deity's adornments are legendary.

Online darshan booking and free meal services make the visit smoother. The 2025 calendar includes special festivals like Brahmotsavam in September.

Meenakshi Amman Temple, Madurai, Tamil Nadu

Meenakshi Temple is a marvel of Dravidian architecture. Its 14 gopurams (towers) are covered in thousands of colourful deities. The temple complex covers 14 acres and includes the famous Hall of Thousand Pillars.

Evening ceremonies at the temple's golden lotus tank are a sight to behold. Madurai's bustling markets and food stalls add to the experience.

Golden Temple, Amritsar, Punjab

Harmandir Sahib, or the Golden Temple, is the holiest shrine for Sikhs. The gold-plated structure sits in the middle of the Amrit Sarovar (holy tank). The temple is open 24 hours and offers free meals to over 1 lakh visitors daily.

The 2025 visit should include the early morning Palki Sahib ceremony. The atmosphere of devotion and community service is unmatched.

Jagannath Temple, Puri, Odisha

Puri's Jagannath Temple is famous for its annual Rath Yatra (chariot festival). The temple's main deities — Jagannath, Balabhadra, and Subhadra — are made of wood and replaced every 12 years. Non-Hindus cannot enter the main sanctum but can view from the roof.

The 2025 Rath Yatra is expected in July. Puri's beaches and seafood add to the pilgrimage.

Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Located on the banks of the Ganga, Kashi Vishwanath is one of the most revered Jyotirlingas. The temple was rebuilt in 1780 by Maharani Ahilyabai Holkar. The recent corridor project has improved access to the temple and the ghats.

Evening Ganga aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat is a spiritual highlight. Varanasi's narrow lanes and local cuisine are part of the experience.

Brihadeeswarar Temple, Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu

A UNESCO World Heritage site, this temple is a masterpiece of Chola architecture. Built in 1010 AD, its vimana (tower) rises 66 metres — one of the tallest in the world. The temple's granite blocks were brought from over 50 km away.

The shadow of the main shrine never falls on the ground, an architectural marvel. Thanjavur's art and silk weaving are worth exploring.

Konark Sun Temple, Odisha

Another UNESCO site, the Sun Temple at Konark is designed as a giant chariot of the sun god Surya. Built in the 13th century, it has 12 pairs of intricately carved wheels. The temple is a symbol of India's astronomical and artistic achievements.

Konark Dance Festival, held every December, brings classical dancers to this ancient stage. The nearby Chandrabhaga beach offers a quiet escape.

Plan your pilgrimage

Many of these temples offer online booking and accommodation. The best time to visit is between October and March, except for Himalayan temples which open only in summer. Local guides can enrich your understanding of each site's history and rituals.

For 2025, the Indian Railways and several state tourism boards have announced special pilgrimage packages. Check official websites for updated timings and entry rules.
