Sensex Jumps 394 Points, Nifty Nears 23,905 on Global Rally

Key points
  • Sensex surged 394 points while Nifty traded near the 23,905 resistance level during the September 4, 2026 session.
  • The rally was driven by positive overnight cues from Wall Street and recovering Asian markets.
  • Broad-based buying across banking, IT, and auto heavyweights lifted market capitalisation to record territory.

Indian Equities Open Higher on Risk-On Sentiment

Indian benchmark indices opened firmly in the green on Friday, September 4, 2026, with the BSE Sensex climbing 394 points and the NSE Nifty 50 approaching the critical 23,905 resistance level. The rally was broadly anticipated by domestic traders tracking overnight bullish momentum across the Wall Street and Asian markets, where technology and banking stocks led a sector-wide recovery. By 04:55:40 GMT, the Sensex had settled into the upper circuit of its intraday range, supported by heavy volumes in index heavyweights.

The positive opening was driven by a confluence of global triggers, including softer-than-expected US inflation data and a rebound in Chinese manufacturing PMI. Domestic institutional investors also turned aggressive net buyers, adding to the upside momentum after three sessions of consolidation. The breadth of the market was notably healthy, with the advance-decline ratio on the NSE favouring bulls at roughly 3:1.

Key Levels and Sectoral Performance

The Nifty's immediate resistance is now anchored at the 23,905 zone, a level that has capped upside attempts over the past two weeks. A decisive close above this threshold could open the door for a move toward 24,200, while immediate support sits at the 23,650-23,700 band. Bank Nifty outperformed the broader market, gaining roughly 0.65 percent, while the Nifty IT index added 0.48 percent on renewed US client spending visibility.

Top Sectoral Movers

  • Banking & Financial Services: HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and State Bank of India contributed the largest points gain to the Sensex.
  • Information Technology: TCS, Infosys, and Wipro rebounded on improved Q3 deal pipeline commentary from US peers.
  • Automobiles: Maruti Suzuki and Tata Motors gained on August dispatch data showing double-digit YoY growth.
  • FMCG: Index heavyweights like Hindustan Unilever traded flat-to-positive, providing stability to the broader index.

Global Cues That Drove the Rally

The overnight performance of US indices was the primary catalyst for Friday's gap-up open. The Dow Jones Industrial Average closed 0.7 percent higher, while the S&P 500 gained 0.85 percent and the tech-heavy Nasdaq added 1.15 percent, led by chipmakers. Asian markets followed suit, with the Nikkei 225 climbing 1.2 percent and the Hang Seng gaining 1.8 percent as Chinese property stocks rebounded on policy easing rumours.

Brent crude prices remained stable around the $78 per barrel mark, providing relief to the Indian rupee, which strengthened to 83.45 against the US dollar in early trade. Foreign portfolio investors (FPIs) were net buyers to the tune of Rs 2,450 crore in the cash market on Thursday, according to provisional NSE data, signalling a return of risk appetite after the previous week's outflows.

What Retail Investors Should Watch

For retail traders, the immediate focus must remain on whether the Nifty can sustain above the 23,800 zone heading into the closing auction. Options data indicates a maximum pain level of 24,000, with the highest call open interest clustered at the 24,100 strike. This technical structure suggests that option writers are betting on a continued uptrend through the weekly expiry.

Key Real-World Highlights for Traders

  • Pros (Bullish Case): Strong global tailwinds, healthy market breadth, FPI return, and stable crude oil supporting the rupee.
  • Cons (Risk Factors): Valuation concerns in mid-cap and small-cap segments, potential profit booking near the 23,905 resistance, and any unexpected escalation in global trade tensions.
  • Actionable Level: A clean breakout above 23,905 could trigger systematic trend-following buying, while a rejection may pull the index back toward 23,650 support.

Traders are advised to maintain strict stop-loss discipline given the elevated India VIX reading of 14.8, which indicates moderate but not excessive volatility. Position sizing should account for potential whipsaws around the resistance zone.
