GATE 2027: IIT Madras Announces Exam Dates, Syllabus Revision, New Paper

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • IIT Madras has announced GATE 2027 exam dates for February 2027.
  • The syllabus has been revised and a new paper introduced.
  • Online registrations are set to begin in September 2026.

The Indian Institute of Technology Madras has announced the schedule for the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) 2027. The exam will be held in February 2027, with online registrations starting in September 2026.

This year's edition brings a revised syllabus and the introduction of a new paper. The changes aim to align the test with evolving academic and industry requirements.

Syllabus Revision and New Paper

The GATE 2027 syllabus has been updated across multiple subjects. Candidates should check the official website for detailed changes. A new paper has been added, the specifics of which are available in the information brochure released by IIT Madras.

The introduction of a new paper expands the scope of the exam. It provides more options for aspirants from emerging engineering fields.

Exam Schedule and Registration

GATE 2027 will be conducted over multiple days in February 2027. The exact dates are available on the official GATE 2027 portal. Registrations will begin in September 2026 and close later that year.

IIT Madras, as the organising institute, has urged candidates to start preparing early. The revised syllabus means students must update their study materials.

What Candidates Need to Know

Candidates should visit the official GATE 2027 website for the latest updates. The information brochure contains the full syllabus, exam pattern, and eligibility criteria. Admit cards will be issued in January 2027.

The GATE score is used for admission to postgraduate programmes and recruitment by public sector undertakings. A higher score improves chances in both academic and professional pathways.

Preparation Tips

Students should focus on the revised syllabus and practice previous years' papers. The new paper requires additional study of the specific subject. Mock tests will be available on the official portal closer to the exam date.

IIT Madras has not yet announced any changes to the exam duration or number of questions. More details are expected in subsequent notifications.

All eyes will be on the registration numbers and the response to the new paper. The coming months will reveal how aspirants adapt to the changes.
