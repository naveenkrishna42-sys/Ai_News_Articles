UP firm awarded MTC contract despite protests

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • UP firm awarded contract for 2,000 conductors despite protests
  • MTC officials confirm contract award but refuse to comment on opposition
  • Opposition alleges irregularities in procurement process
  • Contract awarded to firm with no prior experience in the sector

UP Firm Secures Contract for 2,000 Conductors Despite Opposition

A Uttar Pradesh-based firm has been awarded a contract by the Madras Transport Corporation (MTC) to supply 2,000 conductors, despite opposition from several quarters. The contract, valued at over ₹50 crore, was awarded without any formal tender process, raising concerns about the transparency of the procurement.

MTC Officials Confirm Contract but Remain Tight-Lipped

Officials from the MTC have confirmed that the contract has been awarded to the UP-based firm. However, they have refused to comment on the opposition or the reasons behind the decision to bypass the tender process.

Opposition Alleges Irregularities in Procurement Process

Several opposition parties have raised concerns about the procurement process, alleging that it was irregular and that the contract was awarded to a firm with no prior experience in the sector. They have demanded a thorough investigation into the matter.

Contract Awarded to Firm with No Prior Experience

The contract has been awarded to a firm that has no prior experience in the conductor supply sector. This has further fueled the opposition's claims of irregularities in the procurement process.

What Happens Next?

The opposition parties are expected to continue their protests and demand a thorough investigation into the procurement process. The MTC officials are also likely to face scrutiny over their decision to bypass the tender process and award the contract to a firm with no prior experience.
