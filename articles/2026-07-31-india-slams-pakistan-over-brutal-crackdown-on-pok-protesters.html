India Slams Pakistan Over Brutal Crackdown on PoK Protesters

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • India condemns Pakistan's crackdown on protesters in Pakistan-occupied Kashmir
  • New Delhi asks global community to scrutinize Islamabad's actions
  • Protesters in PoK have been branded as enemies by Pakistan
  • India highlights the irony of Pak-trained Mujahideen targeting their own people

New Delhi has launched a sharp diplomatic offensive against Islamabad, condemning what it calls a 'ruthless' crackdown on peaceful protesters in Pakistan-occupied Kashmir (PoK). The Ministry of External Affairs issued a strongly worded statement, accusing Pakistan of turning its guns inward against its own citizens.

India's Stinging Rebuke

The statement from the Ministry of External Affairs did not mince words. It pointed out the stark irony that militants trained by Pakistan for use against India have now been deployed against the people of PoK. 'Pak-trained Mujahideen have turned their guns inwards,' the statement read, in a direct reference to the ongoing violence.

New Delhi has called for global scrutiny of Islamabad's actions, urging the international community to take note of the deteriorating human rights situation in the region. The crackdown, described as 'brutal' by Indian officials, has reportedly involved the use of force against civilians who were merely exercising their right to peaceful protest.

The PoK Crackdown

Tensions have been escalating in PoK as residents have taken to the streets to protest against the administration's policies. The protests, which began over local governance issues, have now come to represent broader discontent with the leadership in Islamabad.

Reports indicate that the security forces have been given a free hand to suppress the dissent, with protesters being branded as enemies of the state. This heavy-handed approach has drawn criticism not just from India but also from human rights organizations and observers within Pakistan itself.

India's Call for Global Attention

The Indian government has consistently raised the issue of human rights violations in PoK on international platforms. In this latest statement, New Delhi has reiterated its demand for the world to take a closer look at the situation, arguing that the people of PoK deserve the same rights and freedoms as any other citizen.

The Ministry of External Affairs highlighted that the current crackdown is a clear admission of the failure of Pakistan's governance in the region. It also underscored that the protestors are not terrorists but ordinary people demanding their basic rights, contrary to the narrative being pushed by Islamabad.

The Broader Implications

The development comes at a time when Pakistan is already under pressure from multiple fronts, including economic instability and political turmoil. The situation in PoK adds another layer of complexity to Pakistan's already strained relationship with the international community.

India has seized this opportunity to expose the hypocrisy of Pakistan's claims over Kashmir. The statement noted that Pakistan's pretence of being a champion of Kashmiri rights stands exposed when it uses brutal force against the very people it claims to protect.

What to Watch

As the international community reacts to India's call for scrutiny, all eyes will be on whether global bodies like the United Nations take up the matter. The coming weeks are likely to see heightened diplomatic activity, with India expected to raise the issue at various international forums. The situation on the ground in PoK remains volatile, and the world will be watching closely to see how Pakistan responds to growing international pressure.
