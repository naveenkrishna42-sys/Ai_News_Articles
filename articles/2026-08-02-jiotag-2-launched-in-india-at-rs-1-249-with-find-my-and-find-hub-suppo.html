JioTag 2 Launched in India at Rs 1,249 with Find My and Find Hub Support

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • JioTag 2 launched in India at Rs 1,249
  • Supports both Apple Find My and Google Find Hub networks
  • Features two-year battery life and cross-platform compatibility
  • Available for Android and iOS devices

Reliance Jio has expanded its smart device portfolio with the launch of the JioTag 2, a Bluetooth tracker priced at Rs 1,249 in India. The device, which succeeds the original JioTag, brings dual-network compatibility, working with both Apple's Find My and Google's Find Hub ecosystems.

The announcement was made on Sunday, positioning the tracker as a budget-friendly option in the growing segment of item-locating gadgets. At roughly $13, the JioTag 2 undercuts several international competitors while offering features that cater to both Android and iPhone users.

Cross-Platform Compatibility

The standout feature of the JioTag 2 is its ability to work seamlessly across two major tracking networks. iPhone users can locate their belongings through Apple's Find My app, while Android users can rely on Google's Find Hub. This dual support eliminates the platform lock-in that typically forces consumers to choose sides.

For a market like India, where both iOS and Android have significant user bases, this flexibility could prove decisive. Users no longer need to worry about whether their friends or family members can help locate a lost item, as the tracker pings whichever network is available nearby.

Battery Life and Design

Jio claims the JioTag 2 offers a two-year battery life, addressing one of the most common pain points with tracking devices. The extended battery means users can attach the tag to keys, bags, or luggage without frequent recharging or replacement.

While detailed specifications remain limited, the tracker follows the standard coin-sized form factor typical of Bluetooth tags. It is designed to be lightweight and durable, suitable for everyday carry. The device's compatibility with both major platforms suggests Jio is targeting a broad consumer base, from students to working professionals.

Market Context

The launch comes at a time when Bluetooth trackers are gaining traction in India, driven by increasing awareness about anti-theft and loss prevention. Jio's entry at Rs 1,249 positions it competitively against offerings from established brands, though the company has not yet confirmed any promotional pricing or bundled offers.

Telecom analysts note that Jio's existing ecosystem, including its digital services and retail network, could give the JioTag 2 a distribution advantage. The device is likely to be available through Jio's online and offline channels, though official availability dates have not been announced.

What to Watch

As JioTag 2 hits the market, the focus will be on real-world performance, particularly the reliability of the Find Hub network in India and how well the device integrates with third-party accessories. Consumers will also be watching for potential software updates that could add features over time. The tracker's success may prompt other Indian brands to accelerate their own smart accessory launches, making this a segment to track closely in the coming months.
