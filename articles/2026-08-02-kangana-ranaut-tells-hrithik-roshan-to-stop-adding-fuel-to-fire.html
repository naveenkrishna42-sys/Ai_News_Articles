A Public Spat Resurfaces

The long-simmering feud between Kangana Ranaut and Hrithik Roshan has flared up again, this time over a viral social media trend. Kangana has publicly told Hrithik to "stop adding fuel to fire" after his recent remarks, which she interpreted as teasing her. The exchange has reignited interest in their years-old legal and personal dispute.

Kangana's sharp retort came in response to what she perceived as Hrithik's attempt to mock her amid a trend where celebrities are reportedly apologising to former partners. While the trend has been circulating online, Hrithik's involvement appears to have touched a nerve.

What Did Kangana Say?

In her response, Kangana did not hold back. She urged Hrithik to stop "embarrassing your partner" and pointedly mentioned his girlfriend, Saba Azad. The remark has added a new layer to the public exchange, drawing Saba into a controversy that has largely centred on Kangana and Hrithik's fractured history.

Kangana's post was widely shared on social media, with fans and critics alike weighing in on the latest twist. The actress has been vocal over the years about her grievances with Hrithik, and this latest episode suggests that the wounds have not healed.

The context of the spat is a social media trend that has seen celebrities publicly apologise to people from their past. While the trend has been light-hearted for some, it has taken a more serious tone in this instance. Kangana appears to believe that Hrithik's participation was aimed at her, prompting her to respond with a warning.

Neither Kangana nor Hrithik has clarified the exact nature of the remarks that triggered this exchange. However, the actress's use of the phrase "stop adding fuel to fire" indicates that she sees the trend as an unnecessary provocation.
