Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP Prices Dip After Trump Speech

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin dropped over 4% to $58,200 after Trump's speech.
  • Ethereum fell 5.3% to $3,100, while XRP declined 6.1%.
  • Investors interpreted Trump's remarks as potentially hostile to crypto regulation.

Bitcoin, Ethereum, and XRP prices tumbled today after a speech by former US President Donald Trump sent shockwaves through the crypto market. The downturn erased billions in market value within hours, catching many traders off guard.

Trump’s Remarks Trigger Selloff

Speaking at a rally in Pennsylvania, Trump made statements that investors interpreted as a warning shot against digital assets. He did not mention cryptocurrencies by name but criticised “unregulated financial experiments” and called for stricter oversight of emerging technologies.

The comments came just as markets were bracing for potential policy shifts ahead of the 2026 midterm elections. Traders rushed to exit positions, flooding exchanges with sell orders.

Bitcoin Leads the Decline

Bitcoin, the largest cryptocurrency by market cap, fell more than 4% to trade near $58,200. The drop snapped a three-day winning streak and pushed the asset below its 50-day moving average.

Ethereum followed suit, sliding 5.3% to $3,100. XRP, the token associated with Ripple Labs, saw an even steeper decline of 6.1%, settling at $0.54.

Altcoins across the board bled red. Solana, Cardano, and Dogecoin each lost between 4% and 7% in the hours following the speech.

Liquidations Spike

Data from Coinglass showed over $320 million in leveraged positions were liquidated in the last 12 hours. Long traders bore the brunt, accounting for nearly 85% of the total liquidations.

Analysts pointed to thin liquidity and heightened geopolitical uncertainty as amplifying the selloff. “Markets were already fragile,” one trader noted on social media. “Trump’s speech was just the trigger.”

Market Reaction and Context

The broader financial markets also wobbled. US stock futures dipped slightly, though not as sharply as crypto. The dollar index edged higher, reflecting a flight to safety among traditional investors.

Trump has previously expressed skepticism about cryptocurrencies. In 2021, he called Bitcoin a “scam” and warned it could undermine the dollar. Today’s remarks revived those concerns, though his campaign later clarified he does not support an outright ban.

Industry observers cautioned against overreacting. “One speech doesn’t change fundamentals,” a blockchain analyst in Mumbai said. “But it shows how political noise still moves markets.”

What to Watch

All eyes now turn to the US Securities and Exchange Commission for any formal response. Crypto traders will also monitor the Federal Reserve’s next policy meeting, where rate decisions could further influence risk appetite.

For now, the market remains on edge. Whether this dip is a buying opportunity or the start of a deeper correction will depend on how regulators and politicians frame the debate in the coming weeks.
