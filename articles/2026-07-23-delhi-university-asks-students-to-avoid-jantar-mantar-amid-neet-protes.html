Delhi University asks students to avoid Jantar Mantar amid NEET protest

📅 2026-07-23 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Delhi University advisory tells students to avoid Jantar Mantar amid ongoing protests
  • Association of Indian Universities urges campuses to discourage NEET protest participation
  • DU colleges back minister Dharmendra Pradhan, while student forums support protesters

Delhi University has issued an advisory asking its students to steer clear of Jantar Mantar, the designated protest site in the capital, as demonstrations by NEET aspirants continue to intensify. The university urged students to remain on campus and not get drawn into the agitation.

The advisory comes amid growing pressure on educational institutions from the central government to contain the protests. The Common University Entrance Test (CUET) and NEET controversies have triggered widespread anger among students, with many alleging irregularities in the examination process.

AIU asks campuses to discourage protests

The Association of Indian Universities (AIU) has written to vice-chancellors across the country, asking them to counsel students to remain focused on their studies and not divert their energy into protests. The AIU's directive mirrors the government's stand that the examination process was fair and transparent.

The association has urged campuses to actively discourage students from joining the demonstrations at Jantar Mantar or any other location. This has drawn sharp criticism from student bodies, who accuse the administration of stifling dissent.

DU colleges back Pradhan, student forums support protesters

Several colleges under Delhi University have come out in support of Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan, who has defended the NEET and CUET processes. College administrations have been asked to maintain discipline and ensure that academic schedules are not disrupted.

However, student forums within the university have rallied behind the protesters. They argue that the concerns raised by NEET aspirants about question paper leaks and marking discrepancies are legitimate and need a thorough investigation. The divide between the administration and the student body has widened as the protests continue.

Security tightened at Jantar Mantar

Delhi Police has increased security at Jantar Mantar, where hundreds of NEET aspirants have been camping for days. The area has been cordoned off, and access is being strictly monitored. Authorities fear that the protests could escalate if more students from across the country join in.

The police have advised protesters to maintain peace and warned against any attempt to breach law and order. Several student leaders have been detained briefly over the past week for alleged violation of protest norms.

The government has maintained that the NEET results were declared after due process and that any allegations of irregularities are baseless. But protesters remain unconvinced, demanding a re-examination or a high-level probe into the matter.

With the university advisory and AIU directive in place, the coming days will test whether educational institutions can keep students away from the protest site or if the agitation will draw even more young people into its fold.
