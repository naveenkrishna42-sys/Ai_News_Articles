Google Assistant to Be Shut Down on Android Phones from September 4

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Google Assistant will be removed from Android phones and Wear OS devices on September 4.
  • Google has started emailing users about the shutdown and what comes next.
  • The move marks the end of a 10-year run for the voice assistant.

End of an Era: Google Assistant's Final Date

After a decade of service, Google has finally confirmed the exact date when Google Assistant will stop working on Android phones. The company has started sending emails to users, informing them that the assistant will be discontinued on September 4.

This news was first reported by Android Authority and later corroborated by The Verge, 9to5Google, and other outlets. The shutdown will affect all Android smartphones and Wear OS devices, marking a significant shift in how users interact with their devices.

What the Emails Say

Google's emails to users are part of a broader effort to prepare them for the transition. According to reports, the emails outline what will happen next and direct users to alternatives. The company has not yet specified the exact replacement features, but it is widely expected that Gemini will take over as the primary assistant on Android devices.

The emails are a clear signal that Google is moving on from its older assistant, which was first launched in 2016. Over the years, Google Assistant became a staple on Android phones, powering voice commands, smart home controls, and more.

Impact on Users and Devices

For millions of Android users, the shutdown means they will need to adapt to a new assistant or rely on other methods. Wear OS users will also lose Google Assistant on their smartwatches, which could affect hands-free functionality.

Google has not yet clarified whether existing devices will automatically switch to a new assistant or if users will need to manually enable it. The company has advised users to check their email for specific instructions.

Developers who have built skills or actions for Google Assistant will also need to update their services. It remains unclear how many third-party integrations will be affected.

Why Now?

The move comes as Google shifts its focus to Gemini, its AI-powered assistant that offers more advanced capabilities. Google has been gradually integrating Gemini into its ecosystem, and the decision to sunset Google Assistant aligns with this broader strategy.

Analysts note that Google's decision is not surprising, given the rapid advancement of AI technology. The company has been clear about its intention to replace older systems with more intelligent ones.

However, the timeline is aggressive. With just about a month to go, users and developers have limited time to prepare for the change.

What Happens Next

As September 4 approaches, expect more details from Google about the transition. The company may release a timeline for rolling out replacement features and could provide more guidance for users.

For now, Android users should check their inboxes and keep an eye on official announcements. The shift away from Google Assistant marks a new chapter for Android, one where AI takes the center stage.
