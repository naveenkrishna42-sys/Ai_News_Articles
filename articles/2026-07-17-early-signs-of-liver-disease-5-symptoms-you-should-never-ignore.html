Early signs of liver disease: 5 symptoms you should never ignore

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Health Original source ↗
Key points
  • Persistent fatigue and weakness are common early signs of liver dysfunction.
  • Changes in urine and stool colour can indicate liver issues.
  • Skin yellowing and itching are warning signs not to ignore.

The liver's silent cry for help

Your liver works around the clock — filtering toxins, producing bile, and aiding digestion. But when it starts failing, it often does so quietly. By the time obvious symptoms appear, the damage may already be advanced. Doctors say recognising early signals can make all the difference.

An AIIMS-trained physician recently highlighted five symptoms that people commonly brush aside as routine health problems. These signs, he warns, could indicate that the liver is under serious stress.

1. Unexplained fatigue that doesn't go away

Feeling tired after a long day is normal. But fatigue that persists even after rest — and has no clear cause — should raise a red flag. The liver, when damaged, struggles to flush out toxins from the blood. This buildup can leave you feeling drained, foggy, and low on energy.

Doctors say this is one of the earliest and most overlooked signs. If you are sleeping well yet waking up exhausted, it may be time to check your liver enzymes.

2. Changes in urine and stool colour

Your bathroom habits can reveal a lot about liver health. Dark, cola-coloured urine is a classic warning sign. It happens when bilirubin — a yellowish waste product — builds up in the blood because the liver cannot process it properly.

Similarly, pale or clay-coloured stools are not normal. They suggest that bile flow is blocked. Doctors advise seeking medical advice if these changes last more than a couple of days.

3. Yellowing of the skin or eyes

Jaundice is perhaps the most well-known liver symptom. Yet many people ignore a slight yellow tinge in the eyes or skin, mistaking it for tiredness or diet. In reality, it signals high bilirubin levels and liver dysfunction.

It can appear gradually. Family members or colleagues may notice it before you do. If you see a yellowish cast, do not delay getting a liver function test.

4. Persistent itching without a rash

Itching that does not go away — and has no visible rash or skin condition — can be another clue. When the liver is damaged, bile salts can accumulate under the skin, triggering intense itching. This symptom is often mistaken for allergies or dry weather.

Doctors say the itching tends to worsen at night. If antihistamines are not helping, the liver might be the real culprit.

5. Swelling in the belly or legs

Fluid retention is a later sign, but it can appear earlier in some cases. A bloated belly that feels hard to the touch, or swollen ankles and feet, may indicate that the liver is not producing enough protein to keep fluid in the blood vessels.

This condition, called ascites, requires immediate medical attention. It often points to advanced liver disease or cirrhosis.

What to watch for next

Experts stress that early detection can halt or slow liver damage. Simple blood tests — liver function tests and ultrasound scans — can catch problems before they become critical. If you notice any of these signs persistently, do not self-medicate. Consult a doctor for a proper evaluation.

With fatty liver disease on the rise in India, especially among young adults, awareness is the first line of defence. Catch it early, and the liver — one of the few organs capable of regeneration — may still have a chance to heal.
