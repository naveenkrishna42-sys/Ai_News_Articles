AB de Villiers sparks debate with Kohli-Tendulkar comparison

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • AB de Villiers claims Kohli performed better than Tendulkar in overseas Test matches.
  • The comparison drew immediate backlash from former Indian cricketers.
  • The debate highlights contrasting views on two of cricket's most iconic figures.

A Bold Assessment

Former South African captain AB de Villiers has ignited a fierce debate within the cricketing community. In recent comments, the batting legend suggested that Virat Kohli holds a superior record in overseas Test matches compared to the legendary Sachin Tendulkar.

De Villiers argued that Kohli has effectively raised the bar for Indian batters when playing outside the subcontinent. He highlighted Kohli's technical approach and his capacity to dominate in challenging conditions as evidence of his elevated status.

The Contested Comparison

The comparison centres on performance metrics in SENA nations—South Africa, England, New Zealand, and Australia. Proponents of the view point to Kohli’s involvement in 10 Test match victories in these regions as a defining factor.

However, the assertion has not gone unchallenged. Many cricket enthusiasts and experts maintain that comparing players across different eras is inherently difficult due to changes in pitch conditions, equipment, and bowling standards.

Public Backlash and Social Media Mockery

The sentiment expressed by de Villiers did not sit well with everyone. A former Indian cricketer reacted sharply to the assessment, using social media to mock the South African’s take with a blunt, sarcastic remark suggesting the opinion was far removed from reality.

The exchange quickly went viral, drawing thousands of reactions from fans across India. While some supported the statistical argument regarding Kohli’s overseas record, others defended Tendulkar’s longevity and his role in carrying the Indian batting lineup for over two decades.

  • Kohli's overseas Test performance remains a frequent topic of analysis.
  • Tendulkar’s career spanned 24 years, facing diverse bowling attacks.
  • The debate continues to divide opinion among cricket pundits.

As the conversation continues to gain momentum online, cricket boards and former players have yet to offer official commentary on these comparative claims. It remains to be seen whether these comments will influence future discussions regarding India's greatest Test batters.
