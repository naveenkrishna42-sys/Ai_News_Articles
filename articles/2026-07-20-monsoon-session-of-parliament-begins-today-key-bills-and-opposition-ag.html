Monsoon Session of Parliament Begins Today, Key Bills and Opposition Agenda in Focus

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Monsoon Session of Parliament commences today, July 20.
  • All-party meeting held ahead of session to discuss legislative agenda.
  • Wangchuk issue likely to dominate opposition's floor strategy.
  • Government to introduce several new bills during the session.

The Monsoon Session of Parliament begins today, with both Houses set to convene amid expectations of intense political showdowns. The session, which runs until mid-August, promises a packed legislative calendar and heated debates over key national issues.

All-Party Meet Sets the Tone

Ahead of the session, the government convened an all-party meeting to build consensus on the legislative agenda. Leaders from major political parties attended the meeting, where the ruling coalition outlined its priorities for the session.

According to sources, the government emphasised the need for smooth functioning and urged opposition parties to cooperate. However, the opposition has signalled that it will press for discussions on several contentious matters, potentially leading to disruptions.

Wangchuk Issue Likely to Dominate

The opposition is expected to corner the government over the Wangchuk issue, which has emerged as a flashpoint in recent weeks. Leaders from multiple parties have demanded a statement from the government and a detailed discussion in Parliament.

Political analysts say the issue could become a rallying point for the opposition, allowing them to coordinate their strategy across both Houses. The government, meanwhile, is likely to stick to its position, setting the stage for a bitter confrontation.

Several opposition MPs have already submitted notices for adjournment motions and discussions under rules that allow for debates without voting.

New Bills and Legislative Business

The government has listed several new bills for introduction during the session. These include legislation aimed at economic reforms, social welfare, and administrative changes. The exact number and text of the bills are yet to be officially released, but sources indicate that at least half a dozen major pieces of legislation are in the pipeline.

Some of these bills are expected to replace ordinances promulgated during the inter-session period. Others are entirely new proposals that the government hopes to pass with the support of its allies in the ruling coalition.

Opposition parties have already indicated that they will scrutinise each bill closely and may demand that certain bills be sent to parliamentary committees for detailed examination.

Potential Flashpoints Beyond Wangchuk

Apart from the Wangchuk issue, several other matters could spark trouble for the government. Inflation, unemployment, and the handling of natural disasters in various states are among the topics the opposition plans to raise.

Farmers' issues and the ongoing protests in parts of the country are also expected to feature prominently in debates. The government will need to navigate these challenges while ensuring that key legislative business is not stalled.

Both the Lok Sabha and the Rajya Sabha are likely to see frequent adjournments if the opposition decides to press its demands aggressively.

As the session opens, the big question is whether the government and opposition can find common ground, or whether the monsoon will bring more thunder than rain to Parliament. The first few days will set the tone for the weeks ahead.
