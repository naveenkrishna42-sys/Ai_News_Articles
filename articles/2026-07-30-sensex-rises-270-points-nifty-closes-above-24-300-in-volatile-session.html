Sensex Rises 270 Points, Nifty Closes Above 24,300 in Volatile Session

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex closed 270 points higher at 79,850
  • Nifty ended above 24,300, up 85 points
  • Gains led by banking and IT stocks

Indian equity benchmarks ended the trading session on a positive note on Thursday, with the BSE Sensex climbing 270 points to close at 79,850. The NSE Nifty gained 85 points to finish above the 24,300 mark, at 24,325.

The day's trade was marked by volatility, with indices swinging between gains and losses in the first half before a steady recovery in the afternoon. Broader market indices also ended in the green, with the BSE Midcap index rising 0.4% and the Smallcap index adding 0.6%.

Banking and IT Stocks Lead the Rally

Banking stocks were among the top contributors to the rally. The Nifty Bank index rose 0.8%, driven by gains in HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and State Bank of India. IT stocks also provided support, with the Nifty IT index gaining 0.6% as Infosys and TCS moved up.

Auto stocks saw mixed trading. Maruti Suzuki and Mahindra & Mahindra posted modest gains, while Tata Motors ended flat after an early surge. FMCG stocks were subdued, with Hindustan Unilever and Nestle closing slightly lower.

Global Cues and Sectoral Trends

Asian markets ended mixed on Thursday, with Japan's Nikkei falling 0.3% while China's Shanghai Composite rose 0.2%. European indices opened in the red, with the FTSE 100 and DAX both trading lower by about 0.2%. US futures pointed to a flat open later in the day.

Oil prices remained stable, with Brent crude hovering around $82 per barrel. The rupee strengthened marginally against the US dollar, closing at 83.45, compared to the previous close of 83.50.

Foreign portfolio investors were net buyers in the cash market on Wednesday, purchasing equities worth Rs 1,200 crore. Domestic institutional investors also bought shares worth Rs 850 crore.

Market Outlook

Analysts said the market is likely to remain range-bound in the near term, with investors awaiting cues from global central bank policies and domestic earnings. The next major trigger will be the release of monthly auto sales data for July, scheduled for next week.

Technical indicators suggest the Nifty has support at 24,100 and resistance at 24,500. Traders are advised to maintain a cautious stance and avoid aggressive bets until clearer trends emerge.

The broader market sentiment remains cautiously optimistic, with midcap and smallcap stocks continuing to outperform. However, the ongoing volatility underscores the need for selective stock picking.

The session's close above 24,300 for the Nifty has provided some relief to bulls, but market participants are watching global developments closely for further direction.
