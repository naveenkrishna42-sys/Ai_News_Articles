Sidhant Gupta Replaces Farhan Akhtar in Aamir Khan's Lalkaara

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sidhant Gupta replaces Farhan Akhtar in Aamir Khan's Lalkaara
  • Film directed by Ashutosh Gowariker; Farhan Akhtar has opted out
  • Vikrant Massey reportedly approached for a key role
  • Official confirmation from the makers is still awaited

Bollywood is abuzz with news that actor Sidhant Gupta has been roped in to replace Farhan Akhtar in Aamir Khan's upcoming sports drama Lalkaara. The film, directed by Ashutosh Gowariker, has been in the making for some time, and this casting change marks a significant shift in its trajectory.

Multiple reports, including from BizAsiaLive and The Times of India, suggest that Farhan Akhtar has opted out of the project. While the reasons for his exit have not been officially stated, speculation is rife. Sidhant Gupta, known for his work in web series and films, is now expected to take on the role that was initially earmarked for Akhtar.

A Casting Shuffle in the Making

The development comes as a surprise to many, given the strong association of both Aamir Khan and Ashutosh Gowariker with critically acclaimed cinema. Their previous collaboration, Lagaan, remains a landmark in Indian film history. Lalkaara was expected to recreate some of that magic, but the casting changes have added an element of unpredictability.

According to Bollywood Hungama, Vikrant Massey has also been approached for a key role in the film. Massey, who has carved a niche for himself with performances in films like 12th Fail, could bring a fresh dynamic to the narrative. However, no official confirmation has come from the makers or the actors involved.

Who is Sidhant Gupta?

Sidhant Gupta is not a new face in the industry, but this project could be a major turning point in his career. He has appeared in several Hindi web series and films, earning praise for his versatility. Taking over a role initially meant for Farhan Akhtar is no small task, given Akhtar's established screen presence and acting chops.

Gupta's casting suggests the filmmakers are looking at a younger, perhaps more agile performer for the part. Given that Lalkaara is a sports drama, physicality and intensity will likely be key requirements. Early reports indicate Gupta has already begun prep work for the role, although this has not been independently verified.

What We Know So Far

Details about the plot of Lalkaara remain under wraps. What is known is that Aamir Khan will be seen in a lead role, and Ashutosh Gowariker will direct. The film is expected to go on floors later this year, though schedules may shift given the recent casting changes.

Neither Aamir Khan's office nor Ashutosh Gowariker's team has issued a formal statement regarding the casting switch. Fans have taken to social media to express mixed reactions—some are excited about Sidhant Gupta's inclusion, while others remain curious about why Farhan Akhtar stepped away.

It is also unclear if the reported approach to Vikrant Massey is for a supporting part or a pivotal cameo. Given Massey's recent box office success, his involvement could add substantial weight to the project's ensemble cast.

As with most Bollywood productions, official announcements are expected once contracts are signed and paperwork is complete. Until then, the speculation will continue to simmer.

For now, all eyes are on the makers to clarify the final cast and the film's release timeline. If the reports hold, Lalkaara could emerge as one of the most talked-about sports dramas in recent memory, with a fresh face leading the charge alongside a veteran star.

What remains to be seen is how Sidhant Gupta rises to the occasion and whether Vikrant Massey officially boards the project. The coming weeks should bring more clarity as production gears up.
