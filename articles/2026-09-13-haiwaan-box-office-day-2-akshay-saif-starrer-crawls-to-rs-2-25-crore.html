Haiwaan Day 2 Box Office: A Sobering Reality Check for a Big-Budget Thriller

Akshay Kumar and Saif Ali Khan's much-anticipated thriller Haiwaan has posted a disappointing Rs 2.25 crore on its second day, according to early trade estimates. The Priyadarshan-directed film now stands at roughly Rs 8.60 crore nett over two days, falling well short of the Rs 10 crore mark that most trade analysts expected within the opening weekend. For a film carrying two A-list stars and a seasoned director, these numbers point to a clear underperformance at the Hindi box office.

The drop is especially telling because Day 2 usually benefits from weekend momentum. Instead, Haiwaan saw limited growth, suggesting weak word-of-mouth and a crowded release corridor that has split audience attention.

Why Haiwaan Is Struggling: Competition and Content

The film's troubles are twofold. First, the release window is brutal. Mirzapur The Movie has captured the youth and mass-market segment, while the animated Hanuman wave continues to draw families. That leaves Haiwaan fighting for a narrower slice of the audience, particularly in multiplexes where ticket prices are higher.

Second, critical reception has been mixed to negative. The Hindu described Saif Ali Khan as labouring in a "defanged" thriller, while other reviews pointed to a lack of tension and a predictable screenplay. For a genre film relying on suspense, weak reviews directly translate into reduced footfalls from Saturday evening onwards.

Real-World Buyer Implications

For exhibitors and distributors, Haiwaan is a cautionary tale about star power alone not guaranteeing returns. With a reported budget of Rs 90-100 crore, the film needs a theatrical total of roughly Rs 110-120 crore to break even after revenue sharing. At the current pace, even a Rs 30-35 crore lifetime finish looks optimistic, meaning significant losses for stakeholders.

What This Means for Akshay Kumar and Saif Ali Khan

Akshay Kumar has delivered a string of underperformers in recent years, and Haiwaan adds to that pattern. Saif Ali Khan, coming off the success of Vikram Vedha and the Sacred Games franchise, may find his theatrical pull questioned. Both actors still command OTT and satellite value, but the box office verdict is unforgiving.

Key Takeaways for Trade and Fans

  • Weak opening weekend: Rs 8.60 crore in two days is below par for a film with this cast and budget.
  • Competition is fierce: Mirzapur The Movie and Hanuman have eaten into the audience share.
  • Word-of-mouth is poor: Mixed reviews have capped growth, especially in urban centres.
  • Recovery unlikely: The film needs a miracle to recover its Rs 90-100 crore investment from theatrical alone.
  • OTT rescue possible: A digital premiere could still recoup some costs, but theatrical losses are mounting.

Final Analysis: A Missed Opportunity

Haiwaan had the ingredients of a commercial winner: two bankable stars, a veteran director, and a thriller premise. But box office results show that audiences are increasingly selective, and poor reviews spread faster than ever. Unless the film shows a dramatic jump on Day 3 and holds steady through the week, it will be remembered as one of 2026's notable disappointments.

For buyers and exhibitors, the lesson is clear: star-driven projects need strong content and a clear release strategy. Haiwaan had neither, and the numbers reflect that reality.
