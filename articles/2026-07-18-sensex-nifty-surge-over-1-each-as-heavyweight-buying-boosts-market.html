Sensex, Nifty surge over 1% each as heavyweight buying boosts market

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty gained over 1% each on Friday.
  • Buying across heavyweight stocks, including Reliance, HDFC, and ITC, drove the rally.
  • All sectoral indices closed in positive territory, with realty and metal stocks leading gains.

Indian equity benchmarks closed sharply higher on Friday, with the Sensex and Nifty each gaining over 1%, as aggressive buying across heavyweight stocks lifted market sentiment. The rally was broad-based, with all sectoral indices ending in the green.

Heavyweights lead the charge

The BSE Sensex surged more than 700 points, while the NSE Nifty crossed the key 24,800 mark during intraday trade. Traders pointed to strong buying in index heavyweights such as Reliance Industries, HDFC Bank, and ITC, which together contributed significantly to the day's gains.

Market participants said the move was driven by a mix of short covering and fresh long positions being built by institutional investors. Volumes were above average, indicating broad participation.

Sectoral rally broad-based

All 13 major sectoral indices on the NSE closed in positive territory. The Nifty Realty index jumped over 3%, making it the top gainer, followed by the Nifty Metal index, which rose nearly 2.5%. Banking stocks also saw strong interest, with the Nifty Bank index advancing over 1%.

Midcap and smallcap stocks outperformed the benchmarks, with the Nifty Midcap 100 index gaining 1.5% and the Nifty Smallcap 100 index rising 1.3%. Analysts said the rally was supported by improved global cues and expectations of steady domestic economic growth.

Market breadth remains strong

On the BSE, advancing stocks outnumbered declining ones by a ratio of nearly 2:1. Around 1,800 stocks advanced, while just over 900 declined. Over 250 stocks hit their 52-week highs during the session, reflecting bullish sentiment across the broader market.

Foreign portfolio investors were net buyers in the cash market, provisional data showed. Domestic institutional investors also turned buyers after a period of cautious activity.

What drove the buying?

Traders attributed the sharp move to a combination of factors, including short covering ahead of the weekly derivatives expiry and positive global market signals. Asian markets traded higher, with the Hang Seng and Nikkei both gaining, while US futures pointed to a firm start.

On the domestic front, expectations of a normal monsoon and steady corporate earnings added to the positive mood. However, analysts cautioned that the rally could face resistance at higher levels given stretched valuations in some pockets.

The market will now watch for cues from global central bank policies and the progress of the monsoon season in India. Any surprise on the inflation front or geopolitical developments could alter the near-term trajectory.
