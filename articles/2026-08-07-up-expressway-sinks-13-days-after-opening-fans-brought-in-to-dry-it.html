UP Expressway Sinks 13 Days After Opening; Fans Brought In To Dry It

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Toll suspended on Rs 4,200-crore Kanpur-Lucknow Expressway after a 300-metre stretch sank.
  • Electric fans were brought in to dry the affected section, drawing ridicule from opposition leaders.
  • Highway authority has initiated action against the contractor responsible for the project.

A Sinking Start for a Rs 4,200-Crore Showpiece

Thirteen days after opening to traffic, a stretch of the newly built Kanpur-Lucknow Expressway in Uttar Pradesh has developed a significant defect. A 300-metre section of the road has sunk, forcing authorities to suspend toll collection on the route.

The expressway, built at a cost of Rs 4,200 crore, was meant to be a flagship infrastructure project. Instead, it is now the site of an unusual repair operation involving large electric fans.

The 'Fan-tastic' Fix That Raised Eyebrows

Videos showing giant industrial fans placed on the sunken stretch of the expressway have gone viral on social media. The fans, apparently intended to dry out moisture beneath the road surface, have become a subject of widespread mockery and concern.

The visuals prompted sharp reactions from political rivals. Samajwadi Party leader Akhilesh Yadav took a swipe at the ruling dispensation, calling the scene a 'new wind of BJP progress'. The remark has added a political edge to what is already a major engineering embarrassment.

What Went Wrong?

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact cause of the slippage. Initial assessments suggest that water ingress may have weakened the sub-base, leading to the depression in the carriageway.

The decision to deploy electric fans is part of an interim measure to accelerate drying of the affected layers. However, experts question whether such an approach can provide a lasting solution for a high-speed corridor.

Toll Suspended, Contractor Under Scrutiny

The Uttar Pradesh Expressways Industrial Development Authority (UPEIDA) has suspended toll collection on the expressway until further notice. Commuters who have already paid for the journey will not be charged for the affected period.

More significantly, the highway authority has cracked down on the contractor responsible for the construction. The nature of the action taken has not been detailed, but it signals that the state government is treating the defect as a serious lapse in quality control.

A Question of Accountability

This is not the first time a newly inaugurated infrastructure project in India has faced such issues, but the speed of the failure here has raised questions about oversight. The expressway was inaugurated with much fanfare, yet within two weeks, a major section has become unusable.

The contractor's track record and the quality of materials used are likely to come under scrutiny in the coming days. UPEIDA has promised a thorough investigation into the matter.

What's Next for the Expressway

Repair work is underway, but authorities have not provided a timeline for when the expressway will be fully operational. The toll suspension will remain in effect until the road is deemed safe for high-speed traffic.

The coming weeks will be crucial. The investigation's findings will determine not only the fate of the contractor but also the credibility of the state's infrastructure push. For now, the giant fans remain a bizarre symbol of a project that promised speed but delivered a stumble.
