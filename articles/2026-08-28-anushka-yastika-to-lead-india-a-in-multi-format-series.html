India A Team Announced for Multi-Format Series

The Indian cricket board has announced the squad for the India A team, which will be led by Anushka Sharma and Yastika Bhatia in a multi-format series. The series includes Tests, One Day Internationals (ODIs), and Twenty20 (T20) matches, providing a comprehensive platform for the players to showcase their skills and prepare for international cricket.

Key Players and Their Roles

Anushka Sharma, a seasoned campaigner with over 150 international appearances, will lead the team in Tests. Her experience and leadership qualities make her a valuable asset to the squad. Yastika Bhatia, a promising young talent, will be the captain for the ODIs and T20s. Her aggressive batting style and wicket-keeping abilities have earned her a reputation as one of the most exciting players in Indian cricket.

Team Composition

The team includes a mix of experienced players and emerging talents. Some of the key players are:

  • Anushka Sharma (Captain, Tests)
  • Yastika Bhatia (Captain, ODIs and T20s)
  • Rohit Sharma (Vice-Captain, Tests)
  • Virat Kohli (Vice-Captain, ODIs and T20s)
  • Jasprit Bumrah (Bowling Coach)
  • Smriti Mandhana (Batting Coach)

Series Details

The multi-format series will be played over a period of three months, with matches scheduled in various domestic and international venues. The series aims to provide a platform for the players to gain experience and improve their skills, while also preparing them for future challenges.
