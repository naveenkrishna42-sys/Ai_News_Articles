Ramayana: Rise of a Legend Crosses ₹1,700 Cr Global Box Office

Key points
  • "Ramayana: Rise of a Legend" has grossed over ₹1,700 crore globally by its 19th day.
  • The film's domestic collection surpassed ₹1,000 crore, while overseas earnings are estimated around ₹700 crore, marking it as the highest-grossing Indian film internationally (excluding China).
  • Strategic implementation of cheaper ticket pricing on weekdays contributed to sustained audience footfall and boosted Tuesday's earnings.
  • The film has set new benchmarks, notably becoming the highest-grossing Indian film in the US market and outperforming competitors like 'Dhurandhar: The Revenge'.

"Ramayana: Rise of a Legend" Sets New Box Office Benchmarks

As of its 19th day in cinemas, the Ranbir Kapoor-starrer "Ramayana: Rise of a Legend" has officially surpassed the monumental ₹1,700 crore mark at the global box office. This unprecedented commercial triumph, confirmed by reports from The Indian Express and The Hindu on Tuesday, November 24, 2026, solidifies its position as one of Indian cinema's most significant financial successes. The film's sustained momentum, particularly notable with an uptick in Tuesday's earnings, points to a robust audience engagement strategy and widespread critical acclaim.

The domestic market has been a cornerstone of this success, with "Ramayana" eclipsing the ₹1,000 crore milestone within India. This remarkable figure reflects a deeply resonant narrative and stellar production quality that has captivated local audiences. Concurrently, its performance in international territories has been nothing short of historic, positioning it as the highest-grossing Indian film in overseas markets, notably excluding China, as highlighted by Telegraph India and The Times of India.

Strategic Pricing and Global Dominance

Audience-Centric Pricing Fuels Sustained Performance

A key factor in "Ramayana's" prolonged box office dominance has been a shrewd pricing strategy. Reports indicate that the implementation of cheaper tickets, particularly during weekdays, played a crucial role in maintaining audience footfall. This tactical move countered the typical weekday dip in collections, ensuring a consistent stream of viewers and directly contributing to the observed rise in Tuesday's earnings. Such dynamic pricing models optimize theatre occupancy and extend a film's commercial viability beyond the initial opening weekend.

The film's strategic market penetration also extends to specific international territories. "Ramayana: Rise of a Legend" has not only broken records but also established new benchmarks, notably becoming the highest-grossing Indian film in the United States box office. This achievement underscores its broad appeal and effective marketing to the Indian diaspora and cross-cultural audiences alike.
