India is developing a space-based early warning system to detect missile launches

India is building a network of satellites to spot missile launches before they become a threat. The system, which is still in the development stage, will complement India's existing radar-based warning system. The government has not yet confirmed the timeline for the system's deployment.

The system will provide a more comprehensive view of the region

The space-based early warning system will provide a more comprehensive view of the region, allowing India to detect missile launches from a greater distance. This will give the country more time to respond to potential threats. The system will also be able to detect missile launches that are not visible to radar-based systems.

India's existing radar-based warning system is limited in its ability to detect missile launches from a great distance. The space-based early warning system will help to overcome this limitation. The system will also be able to detect missile launches that are not visible to radar-based systems.

The government has not yet confirmed the timeline for the system's deployment. However, officials have indicated that the system is a priority for the government. The system is expected to be operational within the next few years.
