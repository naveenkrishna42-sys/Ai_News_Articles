Six Senses Vana: A Revived Ayurvedic Retreat in the Himalayan Foothills

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Lifestyle Original source ↗
Key points
  • Six Senses Vana has reopened in Dehradun after a comprehensive renovation.
  • The retreat merges ancient Ayurvedic practices with contemporary luxury amenities.
  • It is located in the Himalayan foothills, offering a serene environment for wellness.

A Himalayan Wellness Sanctuary Reopens

Six Senses Vana, a luxury wellness retreat nestled in the Himalayan foothills near Dehradun, has reopened after a significant renovation. The property, which originally debuted as Vana, was acquired by the Six Senses brand and has been transformed into a holistic health destination.

The retreat sits on 21 acres of forested land, offering guests a chance to disconnect from urban chaos. Its design draws from local architecture, using stone, wood, and natural materials to blend with the surroundings.

Ayurveda Meets Modern Luxury

The core of the experience at Six Senses Vana is its Ayurvedic programmes. Guests undergo personalised consultations with resident doctors who design treatments based on their dosha, or body type. Therapies include Panchakarma detox, Shirodhara, and herbal massages.

But the retreat does not stop at ancient wisdom. It offers modern wellness facilities such as a hydrotherapy pool, a steam room, and a fitness centre with yoga and meditation sessions. The kitchen serves a sattvic diet, using locally sourced organic ingredients.

Accommodation and Amenities

The property features 67 rooms and suites, each designed to maximise natural light and views of the Sal forest. Private gardens and outdoor showers are common in the upper categories. Rates start at around Rs 35,000 per night, inclusive of meals and wellness consultations.

An infinity pool overlooks the valley, and a spa with 17 treatment rooms offers a range of therapies. The retreat also has a library, a boutique, and spaces for group activities like cooking classes and nature walks.

Location and Accessibility

Six Senses Vana is about a 45-minute drive from Dehradun's Jolly Grant Airport. The nearest railway station is at Dehradun, about 35 kilometres away. The retreat can arrange transfers for guests arriving by air or rail.

The location in the Himalayan foothills provides a temperate climate for most of the year. Winters can be cold, but the retreat fires up its indoor spaces to keep guests warm during the December to February period.

The revived Six Senses Vana is now accepting bookings. The retreat aims to position itself as a leading destination for those seeking a serious wellness immersion rather than just a luxury holiday. Whether its mix of old and new will attract a steady stream of guests remains to be seen.
