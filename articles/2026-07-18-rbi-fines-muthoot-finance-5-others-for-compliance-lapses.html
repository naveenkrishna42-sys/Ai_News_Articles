RBI fines Muthoot Finance, 5 others for compliance lapses

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Banking Original source ↗
Key points
  • RBI slaps penalty on Muthoot Finance for non-compliance with KYC and loan norms
  • Five other entities also fined for similar violations
  • Penalties range from Rs 5 lakh to Rs 1 crore, imposed under Banking Regulation Act
  • Muthoot Finance did not respond to queries on the penalty

The Reserve Bank of India has imposed monetary penalties on Muthoot Finance and five other entities for violating regulatory norms. The central bank's action comes after inspections revealed lapses in know-your-customer (KYC) requirements and loan management guidelines.

Penalties and violations

Muthoot Finance, a major gold loan non-banking financial company (NBFC), has been fined Rs 50 lakh. The RBI cited non-compliance with directions on loan-to-value ratio and KYC norms. The company failed to adhere to prescribed limits on the amount it could lend against gold, the regulator said.

The other five firms include two NBFCs, two cooperative banks and one housing finance company. The penalties range from Rs 5 lakh to Rs 1 crore. The RBI did not name all the entities but said the fines were imposed after scrutiny of their compliance records.

Regulatory scrutiny on NBFCs

The RBI has been tightening oversight of NBFCs after several defaults in the sector. In recent months, it has conducted targeted inspections of gold loan companies and microfinance institutions. Muthoot Finance, which has a large network of branches across India, has faced similar penalties in the past.

Officials have not confirmed if the latest penalty is linked to any specific complaint. The RBI said the action is based on deficiencies in regulatory compliance and is not intended to pronounce on the validity of any transaction or agreement.

Market reaction and next steps

Shares of Muthoot Finance slipped marginally in afternoon trade on Friday after the news. Analysts said the penalty is unlikely to have a major impact on the company's operations. The company did not respond to queries from TIVRA News on the fine.

The RBI has given the affected entities 21 days to pay the penalty. They can also appeal against the order. The central bank has warned that further non-compliance could attract stricter action.

What to watch: Whether Muthoot Finance and other firms will challenge the penalties in court or take corrective measures to avoid future violations.
