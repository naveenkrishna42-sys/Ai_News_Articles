The discount offers significant savings for employees in Telangana, making EVs more accessible.

Telangana employees are in for a treat if they are considering an electric vehicle (EV). The state government has introduced a scheme offering up to a 20% discount on EVs, with the potential to save up to Rs 4 lakh. This discount is part of the government's efforts to promote the adoption of electric vehicles and reduce carbon emissions.

The discount is applicable to a wide range of electric vehicles, including popular models from brands like Tesla, Mahindra, and Tata. This makes it easier for employees to switch to an EV without breaking the bank. However, it's important to note that the discount is only available to employees of companies registered in Telangana. Additionally, the discount may vary depending on the specific model and brand of the EV.
