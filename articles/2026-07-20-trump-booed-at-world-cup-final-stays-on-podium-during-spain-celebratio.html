Trump Booed at World Cup Final, Stays on Podium During Spain Celebrations

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Donald Trump and Gianni Infantino were booed as they entered the field for the trophy ceremony.
  • Trump remained on the podium while the Spanish team celebrated their World Cup win.
  • The US president praised Lionel Messi's performance earlier in the tournament.

Booing Greets Trump and Infantino at Trophy Ceremony

The 2026 FIFA World Cup final in New York took an awkward turn on Sunday when US President Donald Trump and FIFA President Gianni Infantino were met with loud boos as they walked onto the field for the trophy presentation.

Spain had just defeated Argentina 3-1 at MetLife Stadium to claim their second World Cup title. But the focus quickly shifted to the reception the two leaders received from the crowd.

Reuters reported that the booing was audible and sustained as Trump and Infantino made their way to the podium. The US president appeared to ignore the jeers, waving to sections of the stadium.

Trump Refuses to Leave Podium During Spain's Celebrations

The most striking moment came after the trophy was handed to the Spanish captain. As the Spanish players began their victory celebrations, jumping and dancing in front of the main stand, Trump remained standing on the podium.

The Guardian described the scene as Trump attempting to 'crash' the Spanish celebrations. Photographs show the US president standing alone on the raised platform while the Spanish team hugged and sang a few feet away. FIFA officials eventually escorted him off the field.

Social media erupted with clips of the awkward encounter, with many users pointing out the contrast between the jubilant Spanish team and the isolated figure on the podium.

Trump's Praise for Messi Comes Back to Haunt Him

The incident has reignited talk of a 'Trump sports curse'. Earlier in the tournament, Trump had publicly praised Lionel Messi, calling his performance 'match-winning brilliance' in comments reported by The Times of India.

Messi's Argentina lost the final to Spain. USA Today noted that this loss, coming after Trump's comments, has led some fans to speculate whether the president's support is a jinx. However, no evidence supports this claim.

Trump has a history of attending major sporting events. His presence at the World Cup final was seen as a symbolic gesture, given the tournament was co-hosted by the United States, Canada, and Mexico.

Officials have not yet commented on whether the booing was politically motivated or part of the usual crowd banter during a high-stakes final.

What happens next: The Spanish team will return to Madrid for a victory parade. The White House has not issued a statement about Trump's experience at the match. FIFA is expected to review security protocols for future ceremonies.
