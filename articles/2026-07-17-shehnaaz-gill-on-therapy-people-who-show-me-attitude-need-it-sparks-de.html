Shehnaaz Gill on therapy: 'People who show me attitude need it'; sparks debate

  • Fans and critics are divided, with some supporting her stance and others calling it dismissive of mental health.

Shehnaaz Gill has sparked a fresh debate online after her sharp response to a question about therapy. The actress, known for her candid and often unfiltered remarks, was asked during a recent interaction whether she believes in therapy. Her reply was blunt: 'People who show me attitude need it.'

The comment, made in a light-hearted but firm tone, has split the internet. While many fans have backed her, calling it a confident take, others have criticised her for being dismissive of mental health care.

'People like me don't need it'

In the video that is now viral, Shehnaaz is heard saying that she doesn't need therapy. She added that those who show her 'attitude' are the ones who require it. The remark came across as a mix of humour and defiance.

The actress, who rose to fame after her stint on Bigg Boss, has often spoken about her strong personality. But this time, her words have touched a nerve. Mental health advocates online have pointed out that therapy is not about weakness or 'attitude', but about self-care.

Internet divided over her remark

Social media has been buzzing since the clip surfaced. Some users have praised Shehnaaz for being 'real' and refusing to conform. 'She knows her mind. Not everyone needs therapy,' one fan wrote on X.

Others were less impressed. 'This is a dangerous statement. Therapy is for everyone, not just those with 'attitude',' a critic posted. The debate has highlighted the ongoing stigma around mental health in India, especially among celebrities.

Fans come to her defence

Shehnaaz's loyal fan base, often called 'Sikhs', has rallied behind her. They argue that she was joking and that the comment is being taken out of context. Many have pointed out that she has supported mental health awareness in the past.

However, the video shows no visible cue that she was being sarcastic. The tone, as heard in multiple clips shared by news outlets, appears assertive. The question itself was straightforward: did she believe in therapy?

What happens next

Shehnaaz Gill has not issued any clarification or follow-up statement since the video went viral. The debate, however, is unlikely to die down soon. With mental health increasingly in the public eye, any remark from a star of her stature will be scrutinised. Whether she chooses to address the controversy or let it pass, the conversation around therapy in India has once again been pushed into the spotlight.
