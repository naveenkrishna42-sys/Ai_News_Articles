Eight-year-old dies of snakebite during power outage in Chennai

An eight-year-old child died in Chennai after being bitten by a snake during a power outage, in an incident that has shocked the local community and raised fresh concerns about safety during electricity cuts.

The child, whose identity has not been officially released, was at home when the incident occurred. The power outage, which reportedly affected the area, left the family in darkness, making it difficult to spot the snake in time.

Family members rushed the child to a nearby hospital after discovering the bite, but medical staff could not save the child. The exact time of the bite and the sequence of events leading up to the hospital dash have not been confirmed by authorities.

Power outage and snake activity

Power outages are not uncommon in parts of Chennai, particularly during the monsoon season or due to maintenance work. However, this tragedy underscores how a routine disruption can turn deadly, especially in residential areas where snakes may seek shelter during rains or when their natural habitats are disturbed.

Local residents have expressed grief and anger over the incident, with some questioning whether quicker response from electricity authorities could have prevented the tragedy. Officials have not yet commented on whether the outage was scheduled or unplanned.

The snake species involved has not been identified, and wildlife officials have not issued a statement. In many such cases, the snake is not captured, leaving the exact cause of death to be determined by medical reports.

Hospital response and family ordeal

The child was taken to a hospital, but the distance and the darkness may have delayed treatment. Antivenom availability and the time elapsed between the bite and medical intervention are critical factors in snakebite cases, though details of the treatment provided have not been made public.

Doctors at the hospital have not released a statement, and the child's family has not spoken to the media. The death has been reported to local police, who are expected to file a case of unnatural death.

Snakebite risks in urban areas

Chennai, like many Indian cities, has seen periodic reports of snake sightings in residential neighbourhoods, especially during the rainy season. Housing complexes near vacant plots, drainage lines, or greenery are more vulnerable, though this incident occurred during a power cut, which added to the risk.

Health experts often advise keeping a torch handy during outages and checking shoes and dark corners before moving around. But such precautions are not always possible, particularly in homes with young children.

The Tamil Nadu government has not yet responded to the incident. Local civic bodies may be asked to review safety protocols during power cuts, but no official announcement has been made.
