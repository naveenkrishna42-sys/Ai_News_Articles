Bengaluru GBA, ZP honored 181 students for their outstanding performance in the SSLC and PU exams at the 'Nammooru Hemme' event. The event, organized by the Bengaluru GBA and ZP, celebrated the achievements of students from over 50 schools across the city. The students were felicitated for their exceptional results, which reflect the hard work and dedication of both the students and their teachers.
