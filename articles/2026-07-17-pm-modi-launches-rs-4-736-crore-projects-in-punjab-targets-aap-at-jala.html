PM Modi launches Rs 4,736 crore projects in Punjab, targets AAP at Jalandhar rally

📅 2026-07-17 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • PM Modi launched development projects worth over ₹4,700 crore in Chandigarh, focusing on healthcare, education and road infrastructure.
  • At a rally in Jalandhar, Modi attacked the AAP government, calling it a 'Kattar Beimaan Party' over corruption and law and order.
  • Modi referenced the issue of drug abuse and Tahir Hussain in his speech, sharpening his attack on the state government.

PM Modi kicks off infrastructure push in Punjab

Prime Minister Narendra Modi launched a series of development projects worth over ₹4,700 crore in Chandigarh on Friday, giving a major push to rail and road infrastructure in Punjab. The projects span healthcare, education and road connectivity.

Modi laid foundation stones and dedicated several national highway projects to the nation. The move signals the Centre's focus on Punjab, even as the state heads into assembly elections.

'Even though BJP not in power here'

Speaking at a rally in Jalandhar, Modi acknowledged that the BJP is not in power in Punjab. But he made it clear that the central government will continue to invest in the state's development.

The Prime Minister said the projects are aimed at improving the lives of ordinary Punjabis. He stressed that infrastructure development should not be held hostage to politics.

Modi attacks AAP as 'Kattar Beimaan Party'

Modi trained his guns on the Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) government in Punjab. He called it a 'Kattar Beimaan Party', accusing it of failing on corruption and law and order.

The Prime Minister referenced the issue of drug abuse in the state, a persistent problem that has plagued Punjab for years. He also brought up the name of Tahir Hussain, the former AAP councillor from Delhi who was arrested in connection with the 2020 riots.

Modi argued that the AAP government has done little to tackle these issues. He contrasted the Centre's development push with what he described as the state government's inaction.

References to drugs and Tahir Hussain

Modi's speech in Jalandhar carried a sharp edge. He did not mince words when it came to the AAP's track record on law and order.

The mention of Tahir Hussain was significant. The former AAP councillor has been in the news for his alleged role in the Delhi riots. Modi used this to question the AAP's commitment to justice and security.

On drugs, Modi said the problem has destroyed families and youth in Punjab. He accused the state government of turning a blind eye to the menace.

What happens next

The projects launched by Modi are expected to be completed over the next few years. The political battle in Punjab, however, is far from over. With elections approaching, more such rallies and project launches are likely in the coming weeks.
