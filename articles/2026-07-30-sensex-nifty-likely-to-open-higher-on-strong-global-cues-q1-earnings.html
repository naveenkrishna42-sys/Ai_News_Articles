Sensex, Nifty Likely to Open Higher on Strong Global Cues, Q1 Earnings

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty are expected to extend their rally on the back of positive global market signals.
  • Q1 earnings reports from major companies will remain a key trigger for stock-specific movements.
  • Investors will also track global cues, including US and Asian market trends, for near-term direction.

Mumbai: Domestic equity benchmarks are expected to open higher on Friday, July 31, extending the recent rally. Sentiment is lifted by a firm handover from global markets and a steady flow of quarterly earnings.

Analysts expect the BSE Sensex and NSE Nifty 50 to start the session in positive territory. The focus will shift to stock-specific action as more companies report their June quarter results.

Global Cues Provide Tailwind

Asian markets traded higher in early deals, mirroring overnight gains on Wall Street. US indices closed at record levels on Thursday, driven by optimism over tech earnings and economic data.

This positive global backdrop is expected to support buying interest in Indian equities. Foreign portfolio investors have also remained net buyers in recent sessions, adding to the bullish undertone.

Q1 Earnings Season in Full Swing

The ongoing quarterly earnings season remains the primary domestic trigger. Several blue-chip companies are scheduled to announce their results later today, and analysts will parse these numbers for signs of demand recovery and margin trends.

Sectors such as banking, IT, and auto are likely to see heightened activity. Any positive surprises in earnings could fuel further upside in specific stocks, while misses may lead to profit booking.

Key Levels to Watch

Traders are watching the 24,800 level on the Nifty as immediate resistance. A decisive move above this could open the door for a test of the 25,000 mark. On the downside, the 24,600 zone is seen as strong support.

The Sensex has similar support and resistance bands. Volatility is expected to remain elevated due to the monthly derivatives expiry, which will keep traders on their toes.

What Investors Should Do

Market participants are advised to maintain a stock-specific approach rather than taking broad directional bets. With valuations at elevated levels, selective buying in quality names is recommended.

Analysts suggest booking partial profits in stocks that have run up sharply ahead of results. A disciplined stop-loss strategy is essential given the possibility of sharp intraday swings.

The coming session will also see data releases from the US and Europe, which could influence global risk appetite. Any negative surprise on the economic front may temper the bullish mood.

All eyes are now on how the market closes the week. A strong finish could set the stage for further gains in August, while a failure to hold key levels may trigger a short-term correction.
