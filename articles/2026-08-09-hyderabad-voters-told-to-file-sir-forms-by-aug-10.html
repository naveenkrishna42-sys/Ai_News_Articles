Hyderabad voters told to file SIR forms by Aug 10

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hyderabad electors must submit SIR forms by August 10 to update voter details.
  • Forms can be filed online via the NVSP portal or offline at designated centres.
  • Officials warn of disenfranchisement if forms are not submitted on time.

Deadline approaches for voter form submissions

Hyderabad's election authorities have issued a reminder to all electors to submit their Special Information Revision (SIR) forms before the August 10 deadline. The forms are part of an ongoing drive to update the electoral rolls ahead of upcoming elections.

Officials from the Hyderabad district election office said that voters who have changed their address, age, or other details must file the SIR form to ensure their names remain on the rolls. The deadline is strict, and no extensions are expected.

How to file the SIR form

Electors can submit the SIR form online through the National Voters' Services Portal (NVSP) or via the Election Commission's mobile app. Offline submission is also available at designated offices, including the district election office and local revenue offices.

Voters are advised to keep their EPIC number, Aadhaar card, and proof of address handy while filling the form. The process takes only a few minutes, but incomplete forms are likely to be rejected.

Common mistakes to avoid

Officials have noted that many forms are being returned due to mismatched details, such as incorrect spellings or outdated photographs. They urge electors to double-check all information before submission.

First-time applicants and those who have shifted residences within the city are particularly encouraged to apply early. The election office has set up help desks to assist with any queries.

Why this matters

A failure to submit the SIR form could result in a voter's name being removed from the electoral roll, effectively disenfranchising them for the next election cycle. With the state assembly elections expected in the coming months, the stakes are high.

The Election Commission has also launched a social media campaign to spread awareness, using local influencers to reach younger voters. However, officials say that the onus is on individual electors to act before the deadline.

What happens after submission

Once the form is submitted, it will be verified by the electoral registration officer. If approved, the voter's details will be updated in the rolls, and they will receive a confirmation via SMS or email.

For those who miss the deadline, the next window for corrections will only open after the final publication of the electoral roll, which may be too late for the upcoming polls.

Looking ahead

With just two days left, the district election office expects a surge in applications. Officials have assured that additional counters will be opened if needed, and the online portal will remain functional until midnight on August 10.

Voters are reminded to check their status on the NVSP portal after submission to confirm that their application has been received. The coming week will be crucial for ensuring a smooth and inclusive electoral process in Hyderabad.
