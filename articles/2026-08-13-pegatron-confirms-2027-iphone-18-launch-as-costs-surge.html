Pegatron confirms 2027 iPhone 18 launch as costs surge

📅 2026-08-13 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pegatron confirms iPhone 18 launch in 2027
  • iPhone 18 Pro BOM cost may surge nearly 40%, pressuring margins
  • Old and new iPhones could see price hikes up to Rs 10,000 in India

Pegatron confirms timeline

Apple's key manufacturing partner Pegatron has officially confirmed that the iPhone 18 will launch in 2027. The announcement, reported by AppleInsider, puts to rest months of speculation about the next major iPhone cycle. While Apple itself rarely comments on future products, supplier confirmations often serve as the most reliable signals of what is coming down the pipeline.

Pegatron, which assembles a significant share of iPhones at facilities across Asia, did not disclose specific production volumes or launch dates. However, the confirmation aligns with earlier reports that Apple is preparing a major hardware refresh for 2027, potentially marking the tenth anniversary of the iPhone X design language.

Cost pressures mount

TrendForce, a market research firm, has projected that the iPhone 18 Pro's bill of materials (BOM) cost could surge by nearly 40% compared to current models. This increase is attributed to rising component prices, including advanced chips, display panels, and camera modules. The research firm notes that Apple may have to sacrifice margins to sustain shipment targets in the face of these cost pressures.

The potential BOM surge has raised questions about Apple's pricing strategy. Historically, the company has absorbed some cost increases to maintain market share, but a 40% jump would be unprecedented. Analysts suggest that Apple could either pass the costs to consumers or trim profit margins, and the final decision will likely depend on demand forecasts closer to launch.

Price hike ripple effect

A report from 91mobiles indicates that both old and new iPhones could see price hikes of up to Rs 10,000 in India after the iPhone 18 series launch. This would affect not just the upcoming models but also existing inventory, a move that often follows major product refreshes in the Indian market. Import duties and currency fluctuations are also cited as contributing factors, though the report does not specify exact percentages.

For Indian consumers, a Rs 10,000 increase on the iPhone 17 lineup would push entry-level models further into premium territory. The iPhone 18 Pro, if priced similarly, could become one of the most expensive smartphones ever sold in the country.

Demand-driven launch schedule

Forbes reports that the iPhone 18 Pro release date is being driven by demand, with a September launch expected. This aligns with Apple's traditional release cycle, though the 2027 timing suggests the company is not rushing to beat competitors. Strong demand for the current iPhone 17 series appears to have given Apple breathing room to refine the next generation.

Supply chain sources suggest that Pegatron and other partners are already ramping up production capacity for the 2027 models. This includes investments in new assembly lines and testing facilities, indicating that Apple is planning for high-volume production from day one.

What to watch

Over the next year, all eyes will be on component suppliers and early leaks to gauge the final design and feature set of the iPhone 18. The key question remains whether Apple will raise prices to offset the BOM surge or absorb the costs to protect its market share. For Indian buyers, the possibility of a Rs 10,000 hike across the lineup adds another layer of uncertainty. As 2027 approaches, expect more clarity from both Apple and its suppliers on pricing and availability.
