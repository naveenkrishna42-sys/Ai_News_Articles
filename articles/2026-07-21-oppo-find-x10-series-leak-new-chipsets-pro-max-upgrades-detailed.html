OPPO Find X10 Series Leak: New Chipsets, Pro Max Upgrades Detailed

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • The OPPO Find X10 series will reportedly include a new Pro Max model with significant camera upgrades.
  • Leaks suggest the lineup may feature revised chipsets, possibly a MediaTek Dimensity or Qualcomm Snapdragon variant.
  • India launch remains uncertain, with some tipsters suggesting the series might skip the Indian market.

Revised Lineup and New Pro Max Model

The OPPO Find X10 series is shaping up to be a major departure from its predecessor. According to leaks from multiple tipsters, the lineup will see a revised structure, with the introduction of a 'Pro Max' variant sitting above the standard Pro model.

This Pro Max model is expected to pack the most significant hardware upgrades, especially in the camera department. Reports indicate it could be the first smartphone to feature a triple 200MP camera setup, a feat that would put it in a league of its own for mobile photography.

Processor and Chipset Options

Early leaks point to OPPO experimenting with different chipset configurations for the X10 series. While some variants may use a new MediaTek Dimensity flagship chip, others might stick with Qualcomm's latest Snapdragon platform. However, the exact chipset for each model has not been confirmed by the company.

Tipsters have also noted that the base model and the Pro model might see a split in processor choice, similar to what some competitors have done in the past. This would allow OPPO to target different price segments more effectively.

Uncertainty Around India Launch

One of the biggest question marks around the OPPO Find X10 series is its availability in India. A new tip from 91mobiles suggests that the OnePlus 16 and the upcoming OPPO flagship might not launch in the Indian market. This would be a significant shift, given OPPO's strong presence in the country.

However, other sources have not confirmed this. Sportskeeda has explored the possibility of a 'Find X10 Ultra' variant launching in India, but no official statement from OPPO has been made. The company has not responded to queries about the lineup's launch plans.

What to Watch For

As the launch window approaches, more concrete details about the OPPO Find X10 series are expected to surface. The key points to watch will be the final chipset specifications, the camera setup details for the Pro Max, and crucially, OPPO's official word on the India launch timeline.
