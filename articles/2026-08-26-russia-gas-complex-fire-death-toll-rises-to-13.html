Death toll rises to 13 in Russian gas complex fire

Russia's Amur Gas Chemical Complex, a key producer of propane, has seen its death toll rise to 13 following a devastating fire that has also injured 150 people. The incident has raised concerns about potential disruptions to the country's gas supply chain.

Plant's crucial role in Russia's gas supply

The Amur Gas Chemical Complex is responsible for producing 10% of Russia's propane, a vital component in the country's gas processing and distribution network. This fire has the potential to disrupt supply chains and affect the availability of propane across Russia.

Industry experts have warned that the incident could lead to price fluctuations in the global gas market, as the disruption in supply may prompt producers to increase prices to compensate for the shortage.

Global gas markets react to the incident

The fire at the Amur Gas Chemical Complex has sent shockwaves through the global gas market, with analysts predicting a short-term increase in propane prices. The incident has also raised concerns about the potential for further disruptions in the supply chain, which could have a domino effect on other gas-related industries.

In response to the incident, Russian authorities have launched an investigation to determine the cause of the fire and to assess the extent of the damage to the plant. Meanwhile, international organizations have called for increased safety measures and stricter regulations in the gas and chemical industries.

Key facts about the incident

  • Death toll: 13
  • Injured: 150
  • Propane production: 10% of Russia's total
  • Potential supply disruption: Short-term increase in propane prices
  • Investigation underway: Cause of the fire and extent of damage
