Bajaj's adventure bike launch confirmed for 2026

Bajaj Auto has confirmed the launch of its long-awaited adventure bike for late 2026. The company has not yet disclosed the exact model name or specifications, but industry sources suggest it will be positioned as a premium offering in the growing adventure bike segment.

Spy shots reveal new features and engine details

Latest spy shots of the upcoming Bajaj ADV bike show several new features, including a TFT display, multiple ride modes, and an air-cooled 250cc engine. The TFT display is expected to provide riders with essential information such as speed, fuel level, and gear position, while the ride modes will allow for customizable riding experiences.

TFT display for enhanced rider experience

The TFT display on the Bajaj ADV bike is a significant upgrade from traditional analog or digital displays. It is likely to be integrated into the bike's dashboard, providing riders with a clear and easy-to-read interface. The display will also support customization options, allowing riders to adjust the information shown based on their preferences.

Ride modes for versatile performance

The multiple ride modes on the Bajaj ADV bike are designed to cater to different riding conditions and preferences. These modes could include options for sporty riding, long-distance touring, and off-road adventures. The exact number and specifics of the ride modes have not been confirmed, but the inclusion of this feature suggests a focus on versatility and rider convenience.

250cc air-cooled engine for powerful performance

The 250cc air-cooled engine revealed in the spy shots is a notable specification for the upcoming Bajaj ADV bike. Air-cooled engines are known for their simplicity and reliability, which are desirable traits for adventure bikes. The 250cc displacement suggests a balance between performance and manageability, making it suitable for both urban and off-road riding.
