Zimbabwe recall Madhevere, Nyamhuri for T20I series against India

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Wessly Madhevere and Carl Nyamhuri have been recalled to Zimbabwe's T20I squad
  • The five-match series against India is set to begin next week
  • Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, reflecting on his early India career, spoke about handling ups and downs

Zimbabwe Cricket has named its squad for the upcoming five-match T20I series against India, bringing back experienced all-rounder Wessly Madhevere and pacer Carl Nyamhuri. The series is set to begin next week.

Madhevere, Nyamhuri return after extended breaks

Madhevere, 26, last played a T20I for Zimbabwe in early 2025. He offers a steady middle-order batting option and handy off-spin. Nyamhuri, a left-arm seamer, returns after missing the previous series due to form and fitness concerns.

Both players have been in consistent domestic form, which forced the selectors' hand. Zimbabwe's selection panel has stressed the need for experience against a strong Indian side, particularly on home pitches that are expected to assist spin.

Series holds significance for both sides

India, led by a new-look T20 captaincy group, is using this series to test bench strength ahead of the next World Cup cycle. For Zimbabwe, it is a rare opportunity to compete against a top-tier team in familiar conditions.

The hosts have struggled in recent bilateral series, losing to Bangladesh and Sri Lanka. The return of seasoned players is seen as a move to stabilise the batting order and add depth to the bowling attack.

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi eyes fresh start

Young Indian batter Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, who has had a difficult start to his international career, spoke about the challenges. "Chaar mahine mein kaafi ups and downs aaye," he said, referring to the fluctuations in his form and selection since his debut.

Sooryavanshi has been part of India's T20 setup but has not yet cemented his place. The Zimbabwe series offers him a chance to build a consistent run. He emphasised the importance of learning from failures and staying mentally strong.

Full squad and match schedule

Zimbabwe's full squad has not been officially confirmed by the board at the time of writing, but expected inclusions include captain Sikandar Raza, Sean Williams, and Blessing Muzarabani. The matches will be played across Harare and Bulawayo.

The Indian squad is likely to feature a mix of regulars and fringe players, with senior stars being rested. The series will be the first bilateral engagement between the two nations in over a year.

Zimbabwe will hope home conditions and the return of proven performers can help them register a rare series win against a full-member side.

All eyes will be on Madhevere and Nyamhuri to see if they can slot back into the XI and deliver under pressure.
