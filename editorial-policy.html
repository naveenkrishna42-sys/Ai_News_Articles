Editorial Policy

Sourcing

Stories on AI News Factory are based on publicly available reporting from established news outlets and wire services. Every article links to its original source in the byline area so readers can verify claims directly.

AI-assisted production

Articles are drafted with the assistance of AI language models and reviewed before publication. This is disclosed on every page footer and explained fully in our AI Content Disclaimer.

Independence

Editorial content is kept separate from advertising. Advertisers have no influence over which stories are covered or how they are written. Sponsored content, if ever published, will be clearly labeled as such.

Corrections policy

When an error is identified, we correct the article as soon as possible and, for significant factual errors, note the correction at the bottom of the affected article. Report an error via our Contact page.

Categories we cover

World, Tech, Business, Sports, and Health — chosen for broad public relevance rather than any political or commercial angle.
