The Odyssey first reviews: Nolan's epic debuts to 99% Rotten Tomatoes score, critics call it ‘film of the year

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Christopher Nolan's 'The Odyssey' premieres to a 99% Rotten Tomatoes score.
  • Critics call it 'film of the year' and praise its passionate storytelling.
  • The film is described as a technical and narrative triumph, with advice to arrive 30 minutes early.

Christopher Nolan’s latest film, The Odyssey, has arrived with thunderous critical acclaim. Early reviews from major outlets have pushed its Rotten Tomatoes score to an extraordinary 99%, with several critics already declaring it the film of the year.

The film, a sweeping epic based on Homer’s ancient Greek poem, marks Nolan’s first foray into mythological territory. It has drawn comparisons to his previous masterpieces like Interstellar and Inception, but many say it stands apart in ambition and emotional weight.

Critics praise the passion and scale

The New York Times called it “passion in every frame,” noting that Nolan has infused the epic with a deeply human touch. The review highlighted how the director balances grand spectacle with intimate character moments, a feat that has eluded many filmmakers adapting classical texts.

The New Yorker was equally effusive, running a review headlined “Christopher Nolan’s ‘The Odyssey’ Leaves the Gods in the Outtakes.” The magazine argued that Nolan has managed to create a film that feels both timeless and urgent, stripping away divine intervention to focus on human struggle and perseverance.

Technical mastery and audience advice

Roger Ebert’s review declared simply that “Christopher Nolan has done it again,” praising the director’s signature non-linear storytelling and jaw-dropping visual effects. The critic noted that the film’s sound design and score are among the best in recent memory, creating an immersive experience that demands to be seen on the biggest screen possible.

India Today ran a story advising audiences to “arrive at the theatre 30 minutes early” for The Odyssey. The reason, they explained, is that Nolan has included a pre-film sequence that sets up the narrative in a way that rewards early seating. Latecomers, they warned, risk missing crucial context for the film’s complex structure.

Box office expectations and reception

The 99% Rotten Tomatoes score places The Odyssey among the best-reviewed films of the year, alongside a handful of other critical darlings. Industry analysts expect the film to open strongly in India and globally, with advance bookings already surging in major metro cities.

Nolan’s fanbase in India is particularly passionate. The director’s previous film, Oppenheimer, enjoyed massive success in the country, and early social media buzz suggests The Odyssey could surpass those numbers. Trade experts estimate the film could earn upwards of Rs 50 crore in its opening weekend in the domestic market alone.

What the early reviews highlight

Critics have pointed out that the film’s runtime, reportedly over three hours, never feels bloated. The pacing is described as relentless, with each scene building toward a climax that has left several reviewers visibly moved. The cast, featuring a mix of Hollywood A-listers and rising stars, has drawn unanimous praise for their performances.

The cinematography, handled by Nolan’s longtime collaborator Hoyte van Hoytema, is being called some of the best work of his career. The film’s use of IMAX cameras and practical effects has been widely celebrated, with one critic noting that “every frame could be a painting.”

As the first wave of reviews settles, the conversation is already shifting to awards season. Many believe The Odyssey will be a strong contender at the Oscars, particularly in categories like Best Director, Best Cinematography, and Best Picture.

Nolan has yet to comment on the reviews, but the near-unanimous praise suggests he may have delivered his most accomplished work yet. Fans across India are now counting down the days until the film’s theatrical release, eager to see if the epic lives up to the hype.
