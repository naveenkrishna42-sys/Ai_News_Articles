Selectors Set to Drop Rohit Sharma After Lord's ODI, Report Claims

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Selectors reportedly plan to move on from Rohit Sharma after the Lord's ODI.
  • The decision signals a youth-focused transition for the 2027 World Cup.
  • Rohit's recent form and age are factors, though his quality is undisputed.

The writing appears to be on the wall for Rohit Sharma's ODI career. Multiple media reports indicate that the national selectors are unlikely to pick the veteran opener after the ongoing Lord's ODI against England. The decision, if confirmed, would mark a significant shift in India's white-ball strategy.

Transition Talk Intensifies

The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) and the selection committee, led by Ajit Agarkar, are reportedly looking to build a younger squad for the 2027 ODI World Cup. Rohit, who turns 39 next year, is seen as a short-term option in a long-term plan. Head coach Gautam Gambhir is believed to share this vision.

Reports from multiple outlets, including The Indian Express and NDTV Sports, suggest the Lord's match could be Rohit's final appearance in blue. The selectors have not made any official announcement, but the signals are increasingly clear.

Form and Fitness Under Scrutiny

Rohit's recent performances have not helped his case. While his talent remains undeniable, the runs have dried up in crucial series. The pressure has been mounting, with Telegraph India noting that the selectors are signalling an ODI transition.

Despite the speculation, batting coach Sitanshu Kotak has backed Rohit. Speaking to Cricbuzz, Kotak said, “Rohit is too good a player to feel pressure.” The comment underlines the respect Rohit commands within the dressing room, but selection decisions are rarely based on sentiment alone.

Agarkar and Gambhir's Stance

Reports have also revealed the stance of chief selector Ajit Agarkar and head coach Gautam Gambhir regarding Virat Kohli. While Kohli's place appears secure for now, the focus is clearly on blooding younger talent. The selectors are believed to be keen on giving extended runs to players like Shubman Gill and Yashasvi Jaiswal as the core of the next World Cup squad.

Rohit's omission would leave a massive void in experience. He has been India's most successful captain in the format and a prolific run-scorer. However, the selectors seem to have decided that the future cannot wait.

What This Means for India's ODI Future

The move would accelerate the transition that began after the 2023 World Cup. With the 2027 tournament in South Africa, the team management wants a squad that peaks together. Players in their late 30s, regardless of past achievements, are being phased out.

Rohit's legacy is secure. But in professional sport, the game moves on. If the reports are accurate, the Lord's ODI will be remembered as the day the selectors chose tomorrow over today.

What happens next? The selectors are expected to announce the squad for the next ODI series in the coming days. That announcement will confirm whether Rohit Sharma's long and decorated ODI career has indeed reached its final chapter.
