Sensex Today: Markets Rally as US Fed Delivers Expected Rate Hike

The Sensex opened on a firm note and jumped over 150 points in early trade on Thursday, tracking global cues after the US Federal Reserve raised its benchmark interest rate by 25 basis points. The Fed's decision, widely anticipated by Dalal Street, saw the US central bank reiterate its commitment to bringing inflation down to its 2% target.

At 9:30 AM IST, the 30-share BSE Sensex was trading near the 62,000 mark, while the Nifty50 held above 18,500. Banking heavyweights ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank, and Axis Bank contributed the bulk of the index gains, while IT majors Infosys and TCS followed closely. The broader market participation remained healthy, with midcap and smallcap indices trading in the green.

Why the US Fed Rate Hike Matters for Indian Equities

1. The 'Higher for Longer' Narrative

The Fed's latest hike signals that US interest rates will stay elevated for longer than markets had hoped. For Indian equities, this means foreign portfolio investors (FPIs) may continue to find US bonds more attractive, potentially curbing fresh inflows into emerging markets like India.

2. Rupee Pressure and Imported Inflation

A stronger dollar typically weakens the rupee, making imports costlier. India's crude oil import bill is directly impacted, which in turn affects inflation and the RBI's rate stance. A weaker rupee also pressures IT companies' margins in the short term, though it boosts their export realisations.

3. Sector Rotation

Rate hikes tend to favour financials in the near term as net interest margins improve. However, rate-sensitive sectors like realty, auto, and consumer durables could face headwinds if borrowing costs rise further. Investors should watch the RBI's next policy meeting for domestic cues.

What Should Retail Investors Do Now?

  • Pros: Banking stocks offer value with improving margins; IT stocks benefit from rupee depreciation; long-term SIPs remain the safest way to average out volatility.
  • Cons: FPI outflows may continue; elevated crude prices could stoke inflation; rate-sensitive sectors face earnings pressure.
  • Key Watch Levels: Nifty support at 18,300; Sensex support at 61,500. A break below these could trigger further correction.
  • Strategy: Avoid leveraged positions; stagger purchases in large-cap banks and IT; keep 10-15% cash for dips.

Global Cues and Road Ahead

Global markets are pricing in one more Fed hike before a potential pause. However, the timing depends on US inflation and jobs data. Back home, the RBI has already signalled a data-dependent approach. Investors should track quarterly earnings, GST collections, and monsoon progress for domestic direction.

While the 150-point Sensex rally is encouraging, it's not a signal to go all-in. Volatility is likely to persist. Focus on fundamentally strong companies with low debt and consistent cash flows. For long-term investors, market corrections are opportunities to accumulate quality stocks, not reasons to panic.
