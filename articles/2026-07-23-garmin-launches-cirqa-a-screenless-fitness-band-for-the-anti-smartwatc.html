Garmin Launches Cirqa: A Screenless Fitness Band for the Anti-Smartwatch Era

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Garmin Cirqa is a screenless fitness tracker that uses LED lights and vibration instead of a display.
  • The device offers a subscription-free experience, focusing on core activity and sleep tracking.
  • Cirqa targets users seeking a distraction-free fitness device, moving away from smartwatch overload.
  • The band syncs with a smartphone app for detailed data analysis, keeping the wrist experience minimal.

No Screen, No Distractions

Garmin has taken a sharp turn away from the crowded smartwatch market. The company launched the Cirqa, a fitness band with no screen. It is designed for people who want to track their activity without the constant buzz of notifications.

The Cirqa uses a series of LED lights and haptic vibrations to communicate with the user. Instead of glancing at a display, you feel a pulse or see a light flash. It signals the start of a workout, a goal reached, or an incoming call.

Subscription-Free Tracking

Garmin is making a bold promise with the Cirqa. The device comes with no subscription fee. All core features—step counting, sleep tracking, heart rate monitoring—are available without a monthly payment.

This sets it apart from some competitors who lock advanced metrics behind a paywall. Garmin is betting that users will pay a premium for the hardware upfront in exchange for a clean, uninterrupted experience.

How It Works

The band relies on a companion app on your smartphone. The Cirqa collects raw data on your wrist, but the detailed analysis happens in the app. Users can check their sleep cycles, heart rate trends, and activity history on their phone.

On the wrist, the experience is intentionally minimal. A single button controls the device. Tapping it cycles through different modes—activity, sleep, and a 'do not disturb' setting. The LED array on the front of the band changes colour and pattern to convey information.

Targeting the Anti-Smartwatch Crowd

The Cirqa is not for everyone. It is a niche product aimed at people who feel overwhelmed by smartwatches. These users want the health benefits of a tracker but resent the constant pull of a screen.

Garmin is betting that a growing number of people are ready to disconnect. The company calls this the 'anti-smartwatch era'. The Cirqa is a direct response to that sentiment.

Comparisons and Reception

Early comparisons place the Cirqa alongside other screenless trackers, such as the Whoop Strap and the Oura Ring. However, the Garmin device is a wristband, not a ring, and it offers a more rugged design typical of the brand.

Some early reviews have noted that while the core features are free, certain advanced insights may require a subscription. Garmin has not yet confirmed details on any potential premium tier. The company is expected to clarify the full feature set closer to the shipping date.

The device is now available for pre-order. Garmin has not disclosed the exact release date for general retail.

What to watch: How Garmin balances the 'no subscription' promise with the potential for a paid tier. The success of the Cirqa will depend on whether consumers are willing to trade screen convenience for a cleaner, more mindful fitness experience.
