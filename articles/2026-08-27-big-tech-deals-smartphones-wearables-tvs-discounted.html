Big Tech Deals: Smartphones, Wearables & TVs Discounted

📅 2026-08-27 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Galaxy S26 Ultra now Rs 90,999 with Rs 30,000 iPhone 15 exchange
  • Moto Tag 2 now as low as Rs 1,375
  • TCL 2026 Google AI TVs up to Rs 64,000 off
  • Samsung S25 Ultra now under Rs 80,000, but with conditions

Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra: The Best Deal in Town

The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra has seen a significant price drop, now available at Rs 90,999 in India. This price includes a Rs 30,000 discount on exchange of an iPhone 15. The device boasts a 200MP primary camera, 12GB RAM, and a 5,000mAh battery, making it a top contender in the flagship smartphone market.

This deal is particularly attractive for those looking to upgrade from an iPhone, as the exchange offer significantly reduces the cost of the new device. However, it's important to note that the price is valid only for a limited time and may vary based on stock availability.

Motorola Moto Tag 2: Affordable Wearable

Motorola has also announced a price drop on its Moto Tag 2, now available from Rs 1,375. This wearable, which includes a 1000mAh battery and IP68 water resistance, is a great option for those looking for a budget-friendly smart tag.

The Moto Tag 2 is ideal for those who frequently misplace their keys or wallets. Its compact size and robust build make it a practical choice. However, it's important to note that the price drop is only available for a limited time and may not include all color options.

TCL 2026 Google AI TVs: Smart Savings

TCL has also announced significant discounts on its 2026 Google AI TVs, with prices up to Rs 64,000 off. These TVs come with Google's latest AI features, including voice control, smart home integration, and personalized content recommendations.

This deal is particularly attractive for those looking to upgrade their home entertainment system. However, it's important to note that the discounts are only available for a limited time and may vary based on the model and size of the TV.

Samsung S25 Ultra: The Catch

Samsung's S25 Ultra is now available under Rs 80,000, but there is a catch. The price is valid only for those who purchase the device through Samsung's online store and agree to a two-year contract.

This deal is particularly attractive for those looking to get a flagship device at a discounted price. However, it's important to note that the contract may not be suitable for those who prefer to purchase their devices outright.
