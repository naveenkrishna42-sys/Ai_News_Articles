Lionel Scaloni issues apology to Argentina fans after 2026 World Cup exit

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Lionel Scaloni released a lengthy statement apologising for Argentina's failure at the 2026 World Cup
  • The coach defended his players against criticism, calling detractors 'people who don't know us'
  • Speculation over Scaloni's future has intensified, with some calling for his resignation

Scaloni breaks silence after early exit

Lionel Scaloni has apologised to Argentina supporters following the national team's disappointing campaign at the 2026 FIFA World Cup. The head coach released a lengthy statement on Monday, addressing the team's failure to defend their title.

Argentina, who won the 2022 World Cup in Qatar, were eliminated earlier than expected in the 2026 tournament. The loss has sparked intense debate in Argentine football circles.

Scaloni's statement was his first detailed public reaction since the team's exit. He expressed regret but also defended the squad's effort and character.

'People who don't know us'

In the statement, Scaloni hit back at critics. He said those questioning the team's commitment were 'people who don't know us'. The coach insisted the players gave everything on the pitch.

Scaloni acknowledged the high expectations placed on the team after their 2022 triumph. He said the weight of being world champions made the defeat harder to process.

The 48-year-old thanked fans for their support during his tenure. He did not, however, address his own future directly.

Future uncertain amidst calls for change

Reports have emerged of a movement in Argentina to prevent Scaloni from leaving. Some sections of the media have referred to him as 'the maker of glory', referencing his role in the 2022 victory.

But the early World Cup exit has also led to calls for a change in leadership. Critics argue the team lacked tactical flexibility when it mattered most.

Scaloni has been in charge since 2018. He led Argentina to Copa America glory in 2021 and the World Cup in 2022. His contract situation remains unclear.

What comes next for La Albiceleste

The Argentine Football Association has not yet issued a formal statement on Scaloni's future. Sources suggest internal discussions are ongoing.

Scaloni's apology may soothe some fans, but the disappointment of an early World Cup exit will take time to fade. Argentina's next competitive fixtures are not until the next international break.

The coming weeks will reveal whether Scaloni stays on to rebuild or steps aside for a new era.
