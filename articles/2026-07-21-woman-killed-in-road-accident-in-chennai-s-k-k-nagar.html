Woman killed in road accident in Chennai's K.K. Nagar

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Victim, 52, was riding a scooter when a car hit her
  • Accident occurred near K.K. Nagar bus stop on Sunday afternoon
  • Driver of the car has been detained by police

Accident near K.K. Nagar bus stop

A woman was killed in a road accident in K.K. Nagar on Sunday afternoon. The victim, identified as 52-year-old local resident, was riding a scooter when a speeding car hit her near the K.K. Nagar bus stop.

Witnesses told police the car was travelling at high speed. The impact threw the woman off her two-wheeler. She suffered severe head injuries and died on the spot.

Driver detained by police

Police reached the spot soon after the accident. They have detained the car driver. Officials are questioning him to establish the exact sequence of events.

Traffic on the road was disrupted for about an hour as police cleared the scene and recorded statements from bystanders. The victim's body has been sent to a government hospital for post-mortem examination.

Rising road fatalities in Chennai

Road accidents remain a serious concern in Chennai. According to traffic police data, over 500 people died in road accidents in the city last year. Two-wheeler riders form a large chunk of these casualties.

In this case, police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code. Investigations are ongoing. Officials have not yet confirmed if the driver was under the influence of alcohol.

The woman's family has been informed. They are expected to claim the body after the post-mortem is completed. Neighbours described her as a homemaker who often ran errands on her scooter in the afternoon.

Authorities are reviewing CCTV footage from nearby shops to reconstruct the accident. They are also checking if the car had any prior traffic violations.

Police have urged motorists, especially two-wheeler riders, to wear helmets and follow speed limits. They also advise drivers to be cautious near bus stops and pedestrian crossings.

The case is likely to be heard in a local court once the investigation concludes. What happens next: Police will file a chargesheet after completing the probe, and the driver may face charges of reckless driving causing death.
