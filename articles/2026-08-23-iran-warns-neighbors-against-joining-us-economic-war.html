Iran warns neighbors against joining US economic war

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Iran's foreign minister dismisses new US economic sanctions threats
  • Iran warns neighbors against joining US 'economic war'
  • Tehran says it needs to move beyond 'no war, no peace' situation

Iran's foreign minister dismisses new US economic sanctions threats

Iran's Foreign Minister Hossein Araghchi has dismissed the latest US threats of economic sanctions against Tehran, calling them 'unconstructive' and 'counterproductive'. Araghchi's remarks come as the US continues to ramp up pressure on Iran, with new sanctions expected to be announced soon.

Iran warns neighbors against joining US 'economic war'

In a stern warning to its neighbors, Iran's security chief has threatened to retaliate against any country that joins the US in its 'economic war' against Tehran. The warning comes as the US prepares to impose new sanctions on Iran, targeting its financial and trade sectors.

Tehran says it needs to move beyond 'no war, no peace' situation

Iran's government has expressed a desire to move beyond the current 'no war, no peace' situation, with officials suggesting that a diplomatic solution to the ongoing tensions with the US is the best way forward. However, the warning from Iran's security chief suggests that any move by a neighboring country to join the US in its economic sanctions could further escalate tensions.

US continues to ramp up pressure on Iran

The US has been ramping up pressure on Iran, with new sanctions expected to be announced soon. The sanctions are expected to target Iran's financial and trade sectors, with the aim of crippling its economy and forcing it to negotiate a diplomatic solution to the ongoing tensions.
