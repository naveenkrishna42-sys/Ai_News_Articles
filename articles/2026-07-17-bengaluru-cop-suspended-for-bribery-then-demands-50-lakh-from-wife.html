Bengaluru Cop Suspended for Bribery, Then Demands ₹50 Lakh from Wife

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Head constable Shivakumar was suspended for accepting a bribe.
  • He allegedly demanded ₹50 lakh from his wife, threatening to harm her.
  • A case has been registered at the Halasuru Gate police station.

A Bengaluru police officer already under suspension for bribery has landed in fresh trouble. Head constable Shivakumar, attached to the Halasuru Gate police station, now faces a case for allegedly demanding ₹50 lakh from his wife. The development has put the department under scrutiny once again.

The New Allegation

According to a complaint filed by his wife, Shivakumar called her up and demanded the money. He threatened to harm her and her family members if she failed to pay up. The woman, whose name has not been disclosed, approached the police on Wednesday.

Officials at the Halasuru Gate police station confirmed that a case has been registered under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code. The officer has not been arrested yet, but an investigation is underway.

Previous Suspension

Shivakumar was suspended earlier this year after he was caught on camera accepting a bribe. The incident was reported by a local resident who claimed the officer demanded money to settle a complaint. The video went viral, forcing the department to act.

The suspension was ordered by the Deputy Commissioner of Police. Since then, Shivakumar has been under departmental inquiry. The fresh case is likely to complicate matters further for the officer.

Department's Response

Senior police officials have said they are taking the new complaint seriously. “We will investigate the matter and take appropriate action,” a senior officer told reporters. The department has also assured the woman of security.

The case has drawn attention to the conduct of personnel within the force. The Bengaluru police have faced criticism in the past over allegations of corruption and misconduct.

What Happens Next

The investigation into the fresh demand for money is in its early stages. Police are likely to question the officer and his wife to verify the claims. If found guilty, Shivakumar could face additional charges and a longer suspension.

The department is also reviewing the officer's service record. Sources indicate that disciplinary proceedings may be expedited given the nature of the allegations. The outcome will be closely watched as it could set a precedent for handling such cases within the force.
