LPU's Global Recognition in Innovation

Lovely Professional University (LPU) has secured the 78th spot in the World University Ranking for Innovation 2026, according to a report by Careers360. This places the university fourth among Indian institutions, underscoring its steady rise in the global academic arena.

The ranking, which evaluates universities on their innovation output, patent filings, and research collaborations, reflects LPU's efforts to foster a culture of creativity and problem-solving among its students and faculty.

What Drives LPU's Innovation Ranking?

LPU's climb in the rankings is attributed to its robust research infrastructure and industry partnerships. The university has consistently invested in modern labs, incubation centres, and interdisciplinary projects, enabling students to work on real-world challenges.

Officials at LPU have not yet released a detailed breakdown of the metrics behind this year's ranking, but the achievement signals a broader trend of Indian universities gaining visibility in global innovation indices.
