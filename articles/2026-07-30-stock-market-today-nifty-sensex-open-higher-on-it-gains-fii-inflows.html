Stock Market Today: Nifty, Sensex Open Higher on IT Gains, FII Inflows

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nifty and Sensex opened in positive territory, with Nifty near 24,250.
  • IT stocks like Infosys and HUL surged, while Adani Ports fell.
  • Falling oil prices and foreign fund inflows boosted market mood.

Indian stock markets started the trading session on a positive note on July 30, with benchmark indices gaining ground. The Nifty 50 hovered near the 24,250 mark, while the Sensex traded flat to marginally higher. Optimism was driven by gains in information technology stocks and sustained foreign fund inflows.

IT stocks lead the rally

Shares of information technology firms were among the top gainers. Infosys jumped 4 per cent in early trade, lifting the broader IT index. Hindustan Unilever (HUL) also surged 5 per cent, adding to the positive momentum. The rally in these heavyweights helped offset losses in other sectors.

Jio Financial Services was another notable gainer, rising 5 per cent. However, not all sectors shared the cheer. Adani Ports fell 3 per cent, dragging down the otherwise upbeat mood. Realty stocks also came under pressure, with the sector index trading in the red.

Global cues and oil prices support sentiment

Positive cues from Wall Street overnight set a favourable tone for Asian markets. Indian indices tracked this optimism. Additionally, a decline in global crude oil prices provided relief to import-dependent India. Lower oil prices help reduce the country's import bill and ease inflationary pressures.

Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) remained net buyers in the cash market, adding to the buying momentum from the previous sessions. Their continued inflows have been a key support for the market in recent weeks.

What to watch today

Traders are keeping an eye on earnings announcements from several large-cap companies due later this week. The direction of foreign fund flows and any fresh triggers from global central bank commentary will also influence market movement.

Volatility is expected to remain as the session progresses, with sectoral rotation likely. The performance of banking and auto stocks will be closely watched for any shift in investor preference.

Markets are likely to remain range-bound in the near term, with the Nifty holding above the 24,000 level. Any sharp move will depend on corporate earnings results and global risk appetite.
