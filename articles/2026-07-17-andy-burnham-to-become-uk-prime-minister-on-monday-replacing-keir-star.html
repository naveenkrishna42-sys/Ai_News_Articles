Andy Burnham to become UK Prime Minister on Monday, replacing Keir Starmer

📅 2026-07-17 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Andy Burnham will become UK Prime Minister on Monday
  • He takes over from Keir Starmer after being declared Labour leader
  • Burnham pledges to be 'a leader for the North, South, East and West'

New Labour leader set to take office

Andy Burnham, the mayor of Greater Manchester, is poised to become Britain's next prime minister. He will take over from Keir Starmer on Monday after being declared the new leader of the Labour Party.

The announcement marks a significant shift in UK politics. Burnham, a figure long associated with the north of England, now steps onto the national stage.

Promises to 'bring hope back'

Speaking after the confirmation, Burnham said he intends to 'bring hope back' to the country. He stressed his commitment to governing for every region.

'I will be a leader for the North, South, East and West,' Burnham told reporters. His rise comes after a period of internal debate within the Labour Party over its direction and leadership.

From mayor to prime minister

Burnham's journey from regional mayor to the highest office in the land is unusual in British politics. He has served as the Mayor of Greater Manchester since 2017, building a reputation as a pragmatic and vocal advocate for northern England.

His leadership bid reportedly focused on unifying a party that has faced divisions in recent years. The transition from Starmer to Burnham is expected to be smooth, with the handover scheduled for Monday.

What this means for India and the UK

Burnham's ascension comes at a time of shifting global alliances. India's relationship with the UK has been under focus, with trade talks and diaspora ties remaining key points of engagement.

Observers will watch for any changes in policy direction. Burnham has previously spoken about strengthening regional ties and may prioritise closer cooperation with Commonwealth nations.

Reactions and next steps

Political analysts have noted the speed of the transition. Starmer's exit was not widely expected, and Burnham's coronation as party leader signals a desire for a fresh start.

The Labour Party is expected to confirm a full cabinet in the coming days. Burnham's team has indicated he will focus on domestic issues like housing and public services, but foreign policy will remain a priority.

Details of the handover ceremony are yet to be announced. Officials have not confirmed whether King Charles III will formally appoint Burnham on Monday.

What to watch: Burnham's first speech as prime minister and any immediate policy announcements on trade and international partnerships.
