Nepal floods: 13 bodies recovered from UP's Gandak river

Nepal's recent floods have once again brought to the forefront the issue of cross-border recovery and flood management. The latest development is the recovery of 13 bodies from the Gandak river in Uttar Pradesh's Gorakhpur district. These bodies are believed to be from Nepal, raising questions about the effectiveness of cross-border recovery mechanisms.

Questions raised about cross-border recovery and flood management

The recovery of 13 bodies from the Gandak river has raised serious questions about the effectiveness of cross-border recovery and flood management. The fact that the bodies were found in UP's Gandak river, which is a transboundary river with Nepal, suggests that there may be gaps in the coordination between the two countries.

Moreover, the recovery process itself has been a source of controversy. The bodies were found in the river, and it took several days for them to be identified and handed over to Nepal. This delay has raised questions about the efficiency of the recovery process and the need for better coordination between the two countries.

Nepal floods revive Bihar's annual flooding nightmare

Nepal's recent floods have also revived Bihar's annual flooding nightmare. Bihar, which is also prone to floods, has been facing the issue of flooding for several years. The recent floods in Nepal have once again highlighted the need for better flood management and disaster preparedness in the region.

Experts have pointed out that the flooding in Nepal and Bihar is a result of a combination of factors, including heavy rainfall, poor drainage systems, and lack of disaster preparedness. The recent floods have once again highlighted the need for better flood management and disaster preparedness in the region.
