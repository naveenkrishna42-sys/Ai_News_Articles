Sreejesh's Scathing Critique

Former Indian hockey goalkeeper Sreejesh has launched a blistering attack on the country's hockey management, questioning whether anyone truly cares about the sport after India's disappointing World Cup exit. Sreejesh, who has been vocal about the state of Indian hockey, has called for accountability from the national team's management, accusing them of failing to deliver on the promise of a World Cup victory.

Sreejesh's question, 'Does anyone actually care?', resonates deeply with many fans and analysts. The World Cup exit has had a significant impact on Indian hockey.
