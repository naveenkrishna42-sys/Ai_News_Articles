Bengaluru police arrest rowdy-sheeter with 24 criminal cases

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • A rowdy-sheeter with 24 criminal cases was arrested in Bengaluru
  • The arrest was made by the city police following a tip-off
  • The accused is known to be involved in multiple offences across the city

The Bengaluru police have arrested a notorious rowdy-sheeter with as many as 24 criminal cases registered against him. The arrest was made on Thursday evening following a tip-off received by the city crime branch officials.

The accused, identified as a habitual offender, has been on the radar of law enforcement for a long time. His criminal record spans multiple offences, including violent crimes and property-related cases.

Arrest details

Acting on specific intelligence, a police team laid a trap and apprehended the suspect from a location in the city. Sources said the accused was trying to evade arrest by frequently changing his hideouts.

The police have not yet disclosed the exact spot of the arrest or the details of the operation. Officials said the man was taken into custody without any resistance.

Criminal history

The rowdy-sheeter has 24 pending cases against him, which include charges of assault, extortion, and theft. Police records show that he has been arrested multiple times in the past but continued to be involved in illegal activities.

He was also reportedly involved in a recent incident of gang rivalry that had created tension in a particular locality. However, officials have not confirmed whether the latest arrest is linked to that incident.

Further investigation underway

The accused is currently being questioned by the investigating team. Police are verifying his involvement in several unsolved cases in the city.

A senior officer said the team is also looking into whether the accused had any associates who helped him evade arrest. The investigation is in its early stages.

Legal proceedings

The accused will be produced before a local court, where the police will seek his custody for further interrogation. Given the number of cases, the prosecution is expected to argue for strict action.

The arrest comes as part of a broader crackdown on repeat offenders in the city. Police have stepped up surveillance on known rowdy-sheeters and are tracking their movements closely.

Authorities have urged the public to report any suspicious activity in their neighbourhoods. The police have assured that such operations will continue in the coming weeks to bring down the crime rate.

The next step in the process will be the court hearing, where the accused's remand will be decided. The police are also expected to file a detailed charge sheet in the cases where evidence has been collected.
