Google Pixel 11 to drop 128GB base storage, standardise at 256GB

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pixel 11 base storage expected to start at 256GB, skipping 128GB options.
  • Move aligns Pixel with flagship rivals amid rising storage demands.
  • Official confirmation awaited at Made by Google event on August 12.
  • Pixel Watch 5 and Pixel 11 Pro Fold also expected at the launch.

Google is reportedly resetting the storage baseline for its next flagship lineup. The Pixel 11 series, expected to be unveiled at the Made by Google event on August 12, will likely do away with 128GB base variants entirely, according to industry reports. Instead, the entry-level model is said to start at 256GB, a shift that could redefine what buyers expect from a standard Pixel.

Why 128GB is being phased out

The move comes as apps, photos, and video files grow heavier each year. A 128GB phone, once considered generous, now fills up quickly with high-resolution media and large game installs. Google's decision to standardise at 256GB mirrors a broader industry trend, where rivals like Samsung have already pushed base storage higher on premium devices.

For Indian consumers, this could be a significant upgrade. Many users rely on their phones as primary cameras and entertainment hubs. With 8K video recording and advanced AI features becoming standard, the extra space is no longer a luxury but a practical necessity. However, the change may also nudge prices upward, though Google has not confirmed any cost adjustments.

What else to expect from the Pixel 11 series

Alongside the storage overhaul, the Pixel 11 lineup is rumoured to include the Pixel 11 Pro Fold, a successor to the foldable Pixel 9 Pro Fold. Leaks suggest improved hinges, a brighter main display, and better camera hardware on the foldable. The Pixel Watch 5 is also expected to debut, with possible upgrades to battery life and health tracking sensors.

Earlier reports from 9to5Google indicate the Pixel 11 HiLight feature will support a specific set of colours, though details remain scarce. The HiLight tool, which lets users isolate and recolour objects in photos, appears to be getting a wider palette in the new generation.

Comparing with the competition

The Pixel 11 will face stiff competition from the Samsung Galaxy S26, which is also slated for release around the same time. A price guide from TechRepublic suggests both devices will be positioned aggressively in the Asia-Pacific market, including India. Storage upgrades could become a key differentiator in this rivalry.

Samsung has offered 256GB base storage on its Ultra models for years, but the standard S-series has often started at 128GB. If Google commits to 256GB across the board, it could pressure Samsung to follow suit on all variants, benefiting consumers in the long run.

Implications for Indian buyers

In India, where the Pixel has a smaller but loyal following, the storage bump could make the device more appealing to power users. Local pricing will be crucial, as the Pixel has historically struggled to compete on value against Chinese brands offering similar specs at lower prices. A 256GB base model, if priced smartly, might tilt the scales.

That said, analysts caution that the lack of a 128GB option could price out budget-conscious buyers. Google may need to offer attractive exchange deals or EMI schemes to maintain momentum in the sub-Rs 60,000 segment.

What happens next

All will become clear at the Made by Google event on August 12, where the company is expected to officially unveil the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro Fold, and Pixel Watch 5. The event will also likely showcase new Gemini AI features, which have become a central selling point for Pixel devices. Until then, details remain speculative, but the storage shift appears increasingly certain. Watch for official pricing and India availability in the days following the launch.
