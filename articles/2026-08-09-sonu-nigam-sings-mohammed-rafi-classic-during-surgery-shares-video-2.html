Sonu Nigam sings Mohammed Rafi classic during surgery, shares video

Playback singer Sonu Nigam has turned an unlikely setting into a stage. The veteran artist reportedly performed Mohammed Rafi's timeless classic 'Suhani Raat Dhal Chuki' while undergoing surgery, and shared a video of the moment that has since gone viral.

The clip, which emerged online, shows Nigam rendering the song even as medical staff work around him. According to reports, the singer was in the operation theatre when he decided to break into the old Hindi film favourite. The video has been widely shared across social media platforms, with fans expressing a mix of amusement and admiration for the singer's dedication to his craft.

The organic moment in the OT

Nigam, known for his versatility and vocal range, later spoke about the experience. In an interaction with Hindustan Times, he said the singing "just happened organically" in the operation theatre. The singer did not elaborate on the nature of the surgery or the medical reasons behind it, and his team has not released an official statement on the procedure.

The choice of song itself is significant. 'Suhani Raat Dhal Chuki' is a composition from the 1949 film 'Dulari', famously sung by Mohammed Rafi. The melancholic yet soothing melody has remained a favourite among Rafi enthusiasts for decades. Nigam, who has often cited Rafi as a major influence, appears to have paid an impromptu tribute to the legendary singer in an unusual setting.
