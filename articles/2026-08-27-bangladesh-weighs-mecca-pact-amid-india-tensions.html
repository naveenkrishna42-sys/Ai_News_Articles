Bangladesh's Mecca Pact Consideration

Bangladesh is reportedly considering joining the Mecca Joint Defence Agreement (Pact), a military alliance between Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and Pakistan. This move comes amid growing regional security concerns and tensions with India.
