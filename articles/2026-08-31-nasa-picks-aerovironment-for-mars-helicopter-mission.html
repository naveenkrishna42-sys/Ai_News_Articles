NASA's 2028 Mars mission gets a nuclear-powered upgrade

NASA has awarded AeroVironment a contract to build three autonomous helicopters for its SkyFall mission to Mars in 2028. The helicopters will be nuclear-powered and will search for signs of life under the Martian surface. This is a significant upgrade from the previous Mars missions, which relied on solar-powered rovers.

AeroVironment stock gains as NASA taps the company for the Mars mission

AeroVironment stock has gained as NASA has tapped the company to build the helicopters for the Mars mission. The company's stock has risen by 5% since the announcement was made. This is a testament to the company's expertise in building autonomous helicopters and its ability to deliver on complex projects.

What is SkyFall? NASA's 2028 Mars Mission

SkyFall is NASA's 2028 Mars mission, which will be the first mission to search for signs of life under the Martian surface. The mission will be supported by three autonomous helicopters, which will be built by AeroVironment. The helicopters will be nuclear-powered and will be able to fly in the thin Martian atmosphere.

What will the helicopters be looking for under the Martian surface?

The helicopters will be looking for signs of past or present life under the Martian surface. They will be equipped with advanced sensors and cameras to collect data and images. The data collected will be transmitted back to Earth for analysis. This will be a significant step forward in our understanding of the Red Planet.
