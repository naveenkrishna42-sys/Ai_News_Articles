vivo S2 launch set for Aug 6; Galaxy S27 Pro battery details leak

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • vivo S2 full specs leak ahead of August 6 India launch
  • Galaxy S27 Pro and Ultra battery capacities reportedly revealed
  • vivo S2 FE tipped to replace Pro model in S-series lineup

vivo S2 confirmed for India launch

vivo has officially confirmed that the S2 will launch in India on August 6. The company's newsroom issued a statement, with details authored by Paigham Danish, confirming the premium phone's return to the S-series after a gap.

The confirmation comes amid a flurry of leaks and reports that have already laid out the device's full specification sheet. A hands-on image circulating online shows the S2 with a curved display and OriginOS 6 running out of the box.

Full specs leak ahead of debut

According to reports aggregated by GSMArena, the vivo S2 will pack a high-refresh-rate AMOLED panel with curved edges. The handset is expected to be powered by a mid-to-upper tier chipset, paired with up to 12GB of RAM and 256GB of internal storage.

The camera setup is said to include a 50-megapixel primary sensor, an ultra-wide shooter, and a dedicated depth lens. A 5,000mAh battery with 80W fast charging is also tipped, though vivo has not officially confirmed these figures.

Industry watchers note that the S2's return signals vivo's intent to strengthen its position in the premium mid-range segment in India, a space dominated by OnePlus and Samsung's A-series.

Galaxy S27 Pro and Ultra battery capacities leak

In parallel, Samsung's upcoming Galaxy S27 Pro and S27 Ultra have seen their battery capacities surface online. The Pro model is said to carry a 5,000mAh cell, while the Ultra could go up to 5,500mAh — a marginal bump over its predecessor.

Leaks suggest the S27 Ultra will also support faster wired charging, though exact wattage remains unconfirmed. Samsung has not commented on the reports.

S2 FE tipped to replace Pro

Separately, 91mobiles reports that vivo is working on an S2 FE (Fan Edition) model, which could eventually replace the Pro variant in the S-series lineup. The FE is expected to offer a similar design but with slightly toned-down internals to hit a lower price point.

If the tip holds, vivo would follow a strategy similar to Samsung's, offering a more affordable version of its flagship mid-ranger without compromising on key features like display and battery.

Week 31 roundup

This week also saw other developments across the mobile industry, including new software updates and regional launches. However, the spotlight remains on the August 6 vivo S2 unveiling and the growing buzz around Samsung's next flagship series.

Both companies are expected to reveal more details in the coming weeks as they prepare for the festive season in India, a critical period for smartphone sales.

With the S2 launch just days away, all eyes are on vivo's pricing strategy. The company is likely to position the device aggressively to challenge rivals in the Rs 30,000-40,000 bracket. For Samsung, the S27 series is expected to debut early next year, and more leaks are likely to surface as the launch approaches.
