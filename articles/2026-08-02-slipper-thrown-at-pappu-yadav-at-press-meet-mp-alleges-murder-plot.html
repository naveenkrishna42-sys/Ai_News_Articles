Slipper thrown at Pappu Yadav at press meet, MP alleges murder plot

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pappu Yadav was attacked with a slipper during a press conference.
  • A scuffle erupted after the incident; a knife was recovered from the assailant.
  • Yadav claims he has been receiving threats and alleges a plot to kill him.
  • The attack is linked to his recent remarks on Ram temple donation theft.

NEW DELHI: A routine press conference turned chaotic on Sunday when a slipper was hurled at Purnia MP Pappu Yadav, sparking a scuffle that left his security team scrambling. The incident, which occurred in the national capital, has escalated into a fresh political controversy, with the MP claiming it was part of a larger conspiracy to kill him.

Video footage from the scene shows a man lunging towards Yadav before being intercepted by guards. In the melee that followed, a knife was recovered from the alleged attacker, according to eyewitnesses and preliminary reports.

What triggered the attack

The attack comes days after Yadav made controversial remarks about the theft of donations at the Ram temple. The MP had defended a skit on the issue, which drew sharp criticism from various quarters. Police sources suggest the assailant may have been motivated by these comments, though no official statement has been released yet.

Yadav, known for his combative style, was addressing reporters when the incident unfolded. The MP sustained no injuries but was visibly shaken. His supporters quickly surrounded him, and the accused was detained by security personnel.

Yadav alleges conspiracy

Speaking to reporters after the incident, Yadav claimed he had been receiving threats for weeks. "This was not a random act. They threw a slipper and carried a knife. This is a plot to kill me," he said.

The MP did not specify who "they" were, but hinted at political rivals. He has been a vocal critic of the ruling dispensation at the Centre, often targeting Prime Minister Narendra Modi and the BJP over policy issues.

Police investigation underway

Delhi Police have registered a case and are questioning the accused. Officials have not yet confirmed the identity of the attacker or his motives. "We are looking into all angles, including the possibility of a premeditated assault," a senior officer said on condition of anonymity.

The recovery of a knife from the spot has raised the gravity of the incident. Legal experts point out that carrying a sharp weapon to a public event could indicate intent, though the final determination rests with the investigation.

Political reactions

The incident has drawn condemnation from various political leaders, with many calling for a thorough probe. Some opposition figures have drawn parallels to past attacks on politicians, urging authorities to take security threats seriously.

Yadav's supporters have demanded action against the accused, while his detractors argue that his provocative remarks may have contributed to the hostile atmosphere. The MP, however, remains defiant, stating he will not be silenced.

As the investigation proceeds, the focus will be on verifying Yadav's claim of a plot and ensuring the safety of public figures amid rising political tensions. The accused is expected to be produced in court soon, where further details may emerge.
