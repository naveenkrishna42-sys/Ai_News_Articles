Centre forms high-level committee to resolve Gorkha issue

The Centre has announced the formation of a high-level committee to finalise a permanent political solution to the Gorkha issue. The committee was formed after a meeting led by Home Minister Amit Shah, which also reviewed the security situation along the Siliguri Corridor.

Amit Shah reviews Siliguri Corridor security, pushes for 'triangular grid'

During the meeting, Amit Shah reviewed the security arrangements along the Siliguri Corridor, which connects Siliguri in West Bengal to Sevoke in Nepal. Shah pushed for the implementation of a 'triangular grid' security system, which involves deploying security personnel in a triangular pattern to cover all possible entry points.

Centre threatens action against West Bengal over land transfer in Darjeeling

The Centre has also threatened to take action against the West Bengal government over the transfer of land in the Darjeeling hills to non-Gorkha communities. The Centre has been pushing for the transfer of land to Gorkha communities, which form a significant portion of the population in the region.

Centre pushes for permanent political settlement for Bengal's Darjeeling Hills

Shah has vowed to push for a permanent political settlement for the Darjeeling hills, which is a major tourist destination and a significant part of the cultural heritage of the Gorkha community. The Centre has been pushing for the formation of a separate state for the Gorkha community, which would include the Darjeeling hills.

What happens next?

The formation of the high-level committee is a significant step towards finding a permanent political solution to the Gorkha issue. The committee will be tasked with finalising a solution that addresses the concerns of both the Gorkha community and the rest of the population in the region. The Centre will be closely monitoring the progress of the committee and will take necessary action if the solution does not address the concerns of the Gorkha community.
