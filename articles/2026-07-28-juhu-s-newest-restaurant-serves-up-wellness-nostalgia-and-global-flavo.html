Juhu’s newest restaurant serves up wellness, nostalgia and global flavours

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Lifestyle Original source ↗
Key points
  • New Juhu restaurant blends wellness, nostalgia and global cuisine
  • Menu features dishes with functional ingredients and traditional Indian flavours
  • Cocktail menu includes twists on classics using local and seasonal produce

Mumbai’s dining scene has a new address that is turning heads in Juhu. The restaurant, which opened its doors recently, is being talked about for its unusual mix of wellness, nostalgia and global flavours — all paired with cocktails that come with a twist.

Wellness on a plate

The menu leans into functional eating without sacrificing taste. Dishes are built around ingredients like millets, probiotics and seasonal vegetables. Think fermented rice bowls, quinoa upma and broths infused with turmeric and ginger.

Each section of the menu is designed to offer something that feels both nourishing and indulgent. The kitchen avoids labelling itself as strictly health food — instead, it presents wellness as a natural part of the dining experience.

Nostalgic flavours, modern execution

Nostalgia runs deep in the food here. The chef has drawn from regional Indian comfort food — from Bengali-style posto preparations to Maharashtrian varan bhaat reinterpreted as a risotto. These dishes are familiar but elevated.

Regulars have pointed out that the flavours evoke memories of home-cooked meals. Yet the presentation and technique are firmly contemporary. It’s a balancing act that seems to be working with early diners.

Global influences and a clever bar

The global section of the menu offers small plates inspired by Southeast Asian, Mediterranean and Latin American cuisines. Think miso-glazed eggplant, harissa-spiced lamb sliders and ceviche with kokum.

The cocktail list is equally ambitious. Bartenders have created drinks that use ingredients like curry leaf syrup, tamarind foam and smoked chaat masala. Classics are reworked — a negroni gets a hint of cardamom, while a mojito uses raw mango.

Ambience and location

The restaurant is located in a quiet lane off Juhu’s main stretch. The interiors are minimalist with warm wood tones and open shelving stacked with spice jars. There is an outdoor seating area with planters and low lighting.

Given its location and concept, the restaurant is expected to draw both local residents and visitors looking for a meal that is different from the usual fine-dine or fast-casual options in the area.

What remains to be seen is whether this blend of health, nostalgia and global flair can sustain interest beyond the initial buzz. Early reviews suggest the kitchen is consistent, but Mumbai’s restaurant-goers are known to move on quickly.
