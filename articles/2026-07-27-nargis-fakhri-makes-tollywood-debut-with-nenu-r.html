Nargis Fakhri makes Tollywood debut with 'Nenu R'

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nargis Fakhri, famous for 'Rockstar', makes her Tollywood debut with 'Nenu R'.
  • Director of the film expressed that he specifically wanted Fakhri for the role.
  • The film marks Fakhri's first foray into Telugu cinema after a break from Bollywood.

A New Chapter in Telugu Cinema

Bollywood actress Nargis Fakhri, best known for her role in the 2011 musical drama 'Rockstar', is set to make her debut in Tollywood. The actress will star in the upcoming Telugu film tentatively titled 'Nenu R'. The project marks her first foray into the South Indian film industry.

The film's director confirmed that Fakhri was his first choice for the role. 'I wanted Nargis Fakhri to be a part of this film,' he said, adding that her presence brings a unique dynamic to the story. The team has not yet revealed the full cast or plot details.

From Bollywood to Tollywood

Fakhri shot to fame with 'Rockstar', opposite Ranbir Kapoor, and later appeared in films like 'Madras Cafe' and 'Housefull 2'. However, her Bollywood career slowed down in recent years, with her last major release being the 2020 film 'Torbaaz'.

The move to Tollywood is seen as a strategic step to revive her acting career. Industry insiders suggest that the Telugu film industry has been welcoming to Bollywood actors looking for new opportunities. Fakhri is expected to bring her experience and screen presence to the project.

What We Know About 'Nenu R'

Details about 'Nenu R' remain sparse. The film is currently in pre-production, with shooting expected to begin later this year. The director has not disclosed the genre or other cast members, but sources indicate it will be a mainstream commercial entertainer.

Fakhri's inclusion has generated buzz among fans and industry watchers. Her ability to perform in Telugu is still unconfirmed, but the team is likely to use dubbing or train her for the role. The film's title, 'Nenu R', translates to 'I will come' in Telugu, hinting at a narrative centered around arrival or return.

Challenges and Opportunities

For Fakhri, entering Tollywood comes with its own set of challenges. Language barriers, cultural differences, and a distinct style of filmmaking are factors she will need to adapt to. However, her previous work in Hindi cinema and her experience with diverse roles could work in her favor.

The Telugu film market has seen a surge in pan-Indian releases, making it an attractive destination for actors from other industries. Fakhri's debut could open doors for more cross-industry collaborations. Producers are betting on her star power to draw audiences.

The official announcement of 'Nenu R' is expected in the coming weeks. Fans will be watching closely to see if Fakhri can recreate the magic of her 'Rockstar' days in a new language and format.
