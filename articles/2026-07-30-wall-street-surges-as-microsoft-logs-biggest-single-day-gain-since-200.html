Wall Street surges as Microsoft logs biggest single-day gain since 2008

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Microsoft shares posted their largest single-day jump since 2008 after strong Azure cloud growth
  • Nasdaq composite surged, leading a broad tech rally; Dow and S&P 500 also rebounded
  • Nvidia, Amazon, and other AI-linked stocks rose as Microsoft’s earnings boosted sector sentiment
  • Bond yields climbed as investors priced in stronger economic data and corporate performance

Wall Street bounces back on tech rally

Wall Street staged a powerful comeback on Thursday, with the Nasdaq composite leading the charge. The Dow Jones Industrial Average and the S&P 500 also rebounded sharply, reversing the previous session’s steep losses. Investors piled into technology stocks after Microsoft reported robust quarterly results that exceeded expectations.

The rally was broad but tech-heavy. Microsoft’s shares soared, recording their biggest single-day percentage gain since the financial crisis of 2008. The surge added hundreds of billions to the company’s market capitalisation and lifted the entire sector.

Microsoft’s Azure growth fuels optimism

Microsoft’s cloud division, Azure, posted growth numbers that surprised even the most bullish analysts. Revenue from the unit jumped by a double-digit percentage, driven by enterprise adoption of AI tools and cloud infrastructure. The company’s earnings report, released after the bell on Wednesday, painted a picture of accelerating demand for its AI-powered services.

Investors took the results as a signal that the artificial intelligence boom is not just hype — it is translating into real revenue. Microsoft’s commentary about sustained capital expenditure on AI data centres further reinforced confidence in the sector’s trajectory.

AI stocks ride the wave

The optimism spread quickly to other names in the AI ecosystem. Nvidia, the chipmaker whose processors power many AI workloads, rose sharply. Amazon, which competes with Microsoft in the cloud space through AWS, also saw gains. Nebius, a lesser-known AI infrastructure firm, joined the rally as investors hunted for related plays.

Several analysts upgraded their price targets for Microsoft and its peers, citing the durable growth of cloud and AI. The broader tech-heavy Nasdaq composite ended the session up by more than 2%, recovering a significant chunk of the ground lost in Wednesday’s sell-off.

Bond yields rise as risk appetite returns

Treasury yields moved higher on Thursday, reflecting a shift away from safe-haven assets. The yield on the 10-year note climbed as traders priced in stronger corporate earnings and resilient economic data. Higher yields typically weigh on growth stocks, but the tech rally proved resilient in the face of rising rates.

Some market participants cautioned that the bond market’s reaction could signal lingering inflation concerns. However, the dominant narrative for the day was corporate earnings, not macro fears.

What to watch next

Investors now turn their attention to earnings reports from Apple and Amazon, due next week. Their results will test whether the AI-driven rally has legs beyond Microsoft. Traders will also monitor the Federal Reserve’s next policy meeting for any clues on interest rate direction.
