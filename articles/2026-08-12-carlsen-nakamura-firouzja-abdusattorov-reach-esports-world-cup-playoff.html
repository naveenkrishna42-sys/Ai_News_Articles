The Esports World Cup chess section is heating up in Riyadh, with the sport's biggest names securing their spots in the playoff stage. World No. 1 Magnus Carlsen, alongside Hikaru Nakamura, Alireza Firouzja, and Nodirbek Abdusattorov, have all advanced, setting the stage for a high-stakes knockout phase.

India's Arjun Erigaisi delivered a standout performance, defeating compatriot Nihal Sarin in a gripping all-Indian encounter. The match drew significant attention, as both players are among the country's brightest young talents. Erigaisi's win, however, was followed by a semifinal defeat, ending his run in the tournament.

Nihal Sarin's exit in the opening round was a disappointment for fans, but the clash itself highlighted the depth of Indian chess. Another Indian player, Aravindh, saw his campaign come to an end on day one, as qualifying spots grew scarce.

Qualifiers and Early Exits

Andrey Esipenko and Wei Yi were among the first to book their places in the group stage, demonstrating solid form under pressure.
