Xiaomi 18 Series: Three of Four Leica Models to Launch Globally, Tip Claims

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tip claims three of four Xiaomi 18 series models will get a global launch.
  • Xiaomi 18 Pro, tipped for global release, may not remain a China exclusive.
  • One model, possibly the 18 Ultra, might have a rear OLED screen.
  • All four models are expected to feature Leica-branded cameras.

A fresh wave of leaks suggests Xiaomi is expanding its global ambitions for the upcoming 18 series. According to tipsters tracking the company's release plans, three of the four expected Leica-branded flagship models will launch outside China. This marks a shift from previous years, when the company reserved its most advanced hardware for the domestic market.

The Global Lineup: Three Models Confirmed

The Xiaomi 18 Pro is now tipped for a global rollout. GSMArena.com reports that the device, previously thought to be a China exclusive, will join the standard Xiaomi 18 and a third, as-yet-unnamed model. The fourth device in the series, likely the 18 Ultra, may remain restricted to China or launch later in select markets.

Sources from ximitime.com indicate the series will include four distinct variants. Each is expected to feature a Leica-tuned camera system, continuing Xiaomi's partnership with the German optics maker. The global models will likely share core hardware, though regional software customisations are expected.

A Surprise in the Works: Rear OLED Display

Android Authority reports that one of the global models could feature a secondary OLED screen on the rear. This unique design element, seen in earlier concept phones, would allow users to view notifications, take selfies using the main camera, or display always-on information without waking the main display.

The feature has been rumoured for Xiaomi's flagship line before, but this is the first strong indication it could make it to a production model. If confirmed, it would set the Xiaomi 18 apart from rivals like the Samsung Galaxy S26 and OnePlus 13, neither of which offer a rear screen.

Specs and Surprises: What We Know So Far

The Xiaomi 18 Pro is expected to pack a Snapdragon 8 Gen 4 chipset, up to 16GB of RAM, and a 5,000mAh battery with 120W wired charging. The camera system is tipped to include a 1-inch type primary sensor, a periscope telephoto lens, and an ultrawide shooter, all co-engineered with Leica.

Xiaomi has not yet confirmed any of these details. The company typically announces its flagship series in December, with a global launch following in early 2026. The tip suggests that at least three models will be available outside China within weeks of the domestic launch.

What This Means for Xiaomi's Global Strategy

If the tip holds true, it signals a more aggressive global push from Xiaomi. The company has traditionally launched its Ultra models only in China, citing limited demand for premium devices abroad. By releasing the 18 Pro globally, Xiaomi is betting that international buyers are ready for a true camera flagship at a premium price.

The move also puts pressure on competitors. With Leica branding, a rear OLED screen, and top-tier specs, the Xiaomi 18 series could carve out a niche among photography enthusiasts who want an alternative to the Pixel 10 Pro or iPhone 17 Pro Max.

Xiaomi is expected to officially unveil the 18 series in China by late 2025. The global launch event, if separate, would likely follow in early 2026. Until then, expect more leaks to confirm or contradict these claims.
