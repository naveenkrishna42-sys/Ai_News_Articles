Apple 2026 Back to School offer: Free AirPods, Apple Pencil with Mac, iPad in India

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple has started its 2026 Back to School education discount offer in India.
  • Students buying a MacBook Air or MacBook Pro get free AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation.
  • Purchases of iPad Pro or iPad Air come with a free Apple Pencil Pro.
  • The deal is available for students, parents of students, and faculty through the Apple Education Store.
  • Apple also offers gift cards with certain Mac and iPad purchases, as per GSMArena.

Apple has officially launched its 2026 Back to School promotion in India, offering students and educators free accessories with the purchase of select Mac and iPad models. The annual deal, which typically runs through the summer months, is now live on Apple's Education Store.

What's on offer?

Under the current promotion, students who buy a MacBook Air or MacBook Pro — whether powered by Apple Silicon M4 or M5 chips — will receive a free pair of AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation. The standalone retail price of these earphones in India is around Rs 5,000.

For those purchasing an iPad Pro or iPad Air, Apple is bundling a free Apple Pencil Pro. The stylus, which supports squeeze gestures and Find My integration, typically costs upwards of Rs 10,900.

According to GSMArena, Apple is also offering gift cards to customers who buy a Mac or iPad during the promotional period, though the exact value of the gift card has not been disclosed by Apple India.

Who is eligible?

The offer is available to current and newly admitted university students, their parents, and faculty members at all levels — from school to university. Apple verifies eligibility through its Education Store portal, where users need to confirm their academic status.

This year's deal closely mirrors Apple's global Back to School strategy, with the key difference being that Indian customers get the AirPods 4 ANC variant instead of the standard AirPods or a gift card alternative offered in some regions.

How to claim the offer

Eligible buyers need to visit Apple's official Education Store website in India, select a qualifying Mac or iPad, and proceed to checkout. The free accessory is automatically added to the cart. Apple does not allow swapping the AirPods for an Apple Pencil or vice versa — the accessory is tied to the device category.

The promotion is valid on purchases made directly from Apple, and not from third-party retailers or e-commerce platforms. Apple has not specified an end date for the Indian offer, but global Back to School promotions typically run until late September.

Context and competition

Apple's Back to School drive comes at a time when the Indian premium laptop and tablet market is heating up. Rivals like Samsung and Lenovo have launched their own student discount programs, often bundling productivity software or extended warranties.

While Apple does not offer deep discounts on its hardware, the free accessory bundle effectively lowers the total cost of ownership. For a student buying a MacBook Air M4, the free AirPods 4 ANC represent a saving of about Rs 5,000 — roughly 5% of the laptop's price.

Industry analysts point out that the promotion is designed to lock students into the Apple ecosystem early. Once a student owns a Mac, iPad, and AirPods, switching to a competing platform becomes less likely.

Apple has not yet confirmed whether the gift card offer, reported by GSMArena, applies to all qualifying purchases or only specific configurations. Buyers are advised to check the Education Store for the exact terms before placing an order.

The Back to School promotion is expected to run through the next two months. Students planning to buy a new Mac or iPad for the upcoming academic year may want to act before Apple pulls the deal without prior notice.
