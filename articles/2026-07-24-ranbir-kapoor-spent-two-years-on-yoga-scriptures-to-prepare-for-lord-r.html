Ranbir Kapoor Spent Two Years on Yoga, Scriptures to Prepare for Lord Ram Role

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ranbir Kapoor practiced yoga and meditation for two years to prepare for Lord Ram's role.
  • The actor read scriptures to understand Lord Ram's philosophy.
  • Kapoor admitted to experiencing doubt and fear before taking on the iconic character.
  • He said Ramayana will be his daughter Raha's first movie.

Two-Year Prep: Yoga and Scriptures

Ranbir Kapoor has revealed that he dedicated two years to yoga and meditation to prepare for his role as Lord Ram in Nitesh Tiwari's upcoming film Ramayana. The actor said the practice helped him get closer to the philosophy of the deity.

Speaking about his preparation, Kapoor shared that he also read religious scriptures to understand the character's essence. The role, one of the most anticipated in Indian cinema, required deep mental and spiritual groundwork.

Fear and Doubt Before Playing Lord Rama

In a recent interview, Kapoor admitted to experiencing significant doubt and fear before taking on the part. "Will I be able to do this?" he recalled asking himself, acknowledging the weight of portraying a figure revered by millions.

The actor described the role as a responsibility rather than just a performance. He said the initial hesitation was natural given the cultural and religious significance of Lord Ram in Indian society.

Ramayana Will Be Raha's First Film

Kapoor also shared a personal connection to the project. He said Ramayana will be the first movie his daughter Raha watches in a theatre. The actor, who shares Raha with wife Alia Bhatt, expressed excitement about introducing her to cinema through this film.

He added that the epic story holds a special place in many Indian households, and he hopes the film does justice to the source material.

Massive Expectations for the Epic

Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana is one of the most ambitious projects in Bollywood history. The film features a star-studded cast, including Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram, and is expected to be released in multiple parts.

Reports suggest the production team has focused heavily on authenticity, from set design to costumes. Kapoor's extensive preparation aligns with the director's vision of creating a faithful yet cinematic adaptation.

The film's first look is yet to be officially released, but anticipation continues to build among audiences and industry insiders alike.

What to Watch

The makers are expected to unveil the first teaser or poster in the coming months. With Kapoor's preparation now public, fans will be watching closely to see how his portrayal of Lord Ram takes shape on screen.
