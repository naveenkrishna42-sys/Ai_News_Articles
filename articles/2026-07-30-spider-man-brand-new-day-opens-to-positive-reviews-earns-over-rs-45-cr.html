Spider-Man Brand New Day Opens to Positive Reviews, Earns Over Rs 45 Crore in India

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The film has earned over Rs 45 crore in India on its opening day.
  • Critics have praised it as the best MCU Spider-Man movie yet.
  • Directed by Destin Daniel Cretton, the film is described as a soulful reinvention of the character.

Tom Holland's latest outing as the web-slinger has swung into theatres with a bang. Spider-Man: Brand New Day has opened to overwhelmingly positive reviews from critics and audiences alike, earning over Rs 45 crore in India on its first day.

Box Office Triumph

The film collected more than Rs 45 crore nett in India on Thursday, according to early estimates. This marks one of the strongest opening days for a Hollywood superhero film in the country this year.

Industry trackers attribute the strong start to the massive fan following of Tom Holland and the buzz generated by the film's fresh narrative direction. The previous Spider-Man film, No Way Home, had set a high bar, and Brand New Day appears to have sustained that momentum.

Critical Acclaim

Reviewers have been effusive in their praise. The Indian Express called it a film where 'Tom Holland finally swings free', highlighting the actor's evolved portrayal of Peter Parker. The Hindu noted that both the actor and the character 'have come of age'.

Telegraph India joined the chorus, while The Hollywood Reporter declared it the 'Best MCU Spider-Man Movie Yet'. Critics have singled out director Destin Daniel Cretton's vision as a 'soulful reinvention' of the franchise, giving it a distinct emotional core.

A Global Phenomenon

The film's success is not limited to India. According to The Hollywood Reporter, Spider-Man: Brand New Day has swung to the biggest superhero opening in China in seven years. This suggests the film is resonating strongly with audiences worldwide.

The Chinese box office has been a crucial market for Hollywood tentpoles, and this performance indicates that the appeal of Spider-Man remains universal. The film's blend of action, heart, and humour seems to have struck a chord across cultures.

What This Means for the Franchise

With such a strong start, Spider-Man: Brand New Day is poised to become one of the biggest hits of the year. The film marks a new chapter for Peter Parker, free from the shadow of previous storylines.

Tom Holland has indicated this is a fresh beginning, and the audience response suggests they are ready for it. The film's success also reaffirms the star power of Holland, who has become synonymous with the character for a generation.

All eyes are now on the weekend numbers. If the current trend holds, the film is expected to cross the Rs 100 crore mark in India within its first three days. The coming days will reveal whether this Spider-Man can spin a new box office record.
