Maharashtra Governor, CM Lead Kargil Vijay Diwas Commemoration

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Governor and CM paid tribute at the Kargil War Memorial in Mumbai.
  • Ceremony included wreath-laying and a two-minute silence for the fallen.
  • Event marked the 27th anniversary of India's victory in the Kargil War.

Mumbai: Maharashtra Governor C P Radhakrishnan and Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis led the state's commemoration of the 27th Kargil Vijay Diwas on Monday. The ceremony was held at the Kargil War Memorial in the city. Officials from the state government and the armed forces were present.

Wreath-Laying and Tributes

The Governor and the Chief Minister laid wreaths at the memorial. They observed a two-minute silence in honor of the soldiers who lost their lives during the 1999 conflict. The event was solemn and brief.

Several veterans and families of the fallen were in attendance. The state government had organised the event to mark the anniversary. Kargil Vijay Diwas is observed annually on July 26.

Honoring the Brave

Governor Radhakrishnan spoke about the sacrifice of the armed forces. He said the nation owes a debt to its soldiers. The Chief Minister echoed these sentiments, calling the day a reminder of India's unity.

The commemoration included a guard of honour by personnel of the Maharashtra Police and the Indian Army. School children were also present, holding flags and singing patriotic songs. The mood was one of quiet pride.

27th Anniversary of Victory

This year marks the 27th anniversary of India's victory in the Kargil War. The conflict, fought in the high altitudes of Jammu and Kashmir, saw the Indian Army push back Pakistani intruders. The war ended with India regaining control of key peaks.

More than 500 Indian soldiers were killed in the operation. Their sacrifice is remembered every year on this date. The Maharashtra government has been observing the day with official events since the war ended.

Officials confirmed that similar ceremonies were held across the state in district headquarters. The focus remained on the courage of the troops who fought in extreme conditions.

What to watch: The state government is expected to release a list of welfare measures for veterans and families of martyrs in the coming weeks.
