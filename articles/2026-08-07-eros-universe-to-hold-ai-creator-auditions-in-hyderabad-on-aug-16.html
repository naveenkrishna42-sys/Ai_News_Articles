EROS Universe to hold AI creator auditions in Hyderabad on Aug 16

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • EROS Universe announces AI creator audition in Hyderabad on August 16.
  • The event aims to scout talent in AI-driven content creation.
  • Details on registration and venue are expected to be released soon.

Hyderabad to host AI creator hunt

EROS Universe, the media and entertainment conglomerate, will hold an AI creator audition in Hyderabad on August 16. The announcement was made on Friday, with organizers calling for digital artists, technologists, and innovators to participate.

The event is part of a broader push by EROS Universe to tap into the growing field of AI-generated content, a space that has seen rapid expansion in recent months. The company has not yet disclosed the venue or the selection criteria, but sources indicate the audition will focus on practical skills and creative use of AI tools.

What the audition entails

While the full schedule remains under wraps, the audition is expected to involve live demonstrations and portfolio reviews. Participants may be asked to create content on the spot, testing their ability to blend artistic vision with machine learning capabilities.

EROS Universe has been expanding its digital footprint, and this initiative appears to be aimed at building a roster of AI-savvy creators who can produce films, music videos, and interactive experiences. The company has not specified how many creators will be selected or what roles they might fill.

Hyderabad as a tech hub

Hyderabad, with its thriving IT sector and a growing community of digital artists, makes for a natural choice. The city has seen a surge in AI-related events, from hackathons to industry conferences, reflecting its status as a technology hub.

Local creators have expressed interest on social media, with many tagging the event and asking for more details. EROS Universe has promised to release further information, including registration links, in the coming days.

Industry context

The move comes as entertainment companies across India explore ways to integrate AI into their workflows. From scriptwriting to visual effects, AI is reshaping production processes, and talent with hybrid skills is in high demand.

EROS Universe, known for its film library and production ventures, is positioning itself at the forefront of this shift. The audition could serve as a template for similar initiatives in other cities, though no such plans have been announced.

What's next

Interested creators should watch for the official registration portal, which is expected to go live before the audition date. More details on the venue, timings, and judging panel are likely to be released by early next week.
