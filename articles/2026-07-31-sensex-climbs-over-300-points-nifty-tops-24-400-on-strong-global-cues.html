Sensex climbs over 300 points, Nifty tops 24,400 on strong global cues

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex climbed over 300 points in early trade on July 31.
  • Nifty crossed 24,400, supported by gains in banking and auto stocks.
  • Bajaj twins, Adani Ports, and M&M were among the top gainers.

Indian equity benchmarks opened on a strong note on Friday, July 31, 2026, with the BSE Sensex climbing over 300 points and the Nifty50 breaching the 24,400 mark in early trade. The rally was driven by firm global cues, as Asian markets traded higher and US futures pointed to a positive start.

The Sensex was trading at around 80,000 levels (implied), up 320 points, while the Nifty was hovering near 24,420, up about 90 points from the previous close. Market breadth was positive, with advances outpacing declines on the BSE.

Top gainers and sectors

Among the top gainers on the Nifty were the Bajaj twins – Bajaj Finance and Bajaj Finserv – along with Adani Ports and Mahindra & Mahindra (M&M). These stocks contributed significantly to the index gains, reflecting broad-based buying interest.

Sectorally, auto, banking, and infrastructure stocks led the charge. The Nifty Auto index rose over 0.5%, while the Nifty Bank was up nearly 0.4%. Metals and IT stocks also traded higher, though gains were modest.

Global cues supportive

The positive sentiment in domestic markets mirrored strength in global equities. Asian shares climbed as investors digested a batch of upbeat economic data from the US and Europe. Overnight, Wall Street ended higher, with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq posting gains, supported by strong corporate earnings and expectations of a softer monetary policy stance.

Meanwhile, the rupee opened stronger against the US dollar in early trade, tracking the positive risk appetite. Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) have been net buyers in the cash market over the past few sessions, adding to the optimism.

Market breadth and volatility

The India VIX, a measure of market volatility, eased slightly, indicating reduced anxiety among traders. On the BSE, more than 1,500 stocks advanced while around 800 declined in early trade, pointing to a broad-based rally.

Traders noted that the Nifty's ability to hold above the 24,400 level could trigger further short covering. The index has been consolidating in a range over the past week, and a decisive move above this threshold may open the door for a test of higher levels.

What to watch

Investors will keep an eye on the US Federal Reserve's policy meeting next week, along with domestic corporate earnings and the progress of the monsoon. The market will also track the movement of crude oil prices, which have remained volatile.

In the near term, the direction of global cues and FII flows will remain key. If the Nifty sustains above 24,400, analysts expect the momentum to continue, but caution remains over potential profit-booking at higher levels.

As the session progresses, traders will watch for any sharp moves in the second half, especially ahead of the weekend. The undertone remains positive, but volatility is likely to persist given the global uncertainties.
