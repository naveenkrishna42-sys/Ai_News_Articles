Sensex ends higher after CAS; Nifty closes below 24,600

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Markets ended higher after Cabinet Committee on Security cleared the CAS proposal.
  • Nifty closed below the 24,600 mark, while Sensex posted gains.
  • Investors await further policy cues and global market trends.

Markets Close Higher After Cabinet Nod

Indian equity benchmarks finished Monday's session in the green, as investor sentiment got a boost from the Cabinet Committee on Security's approval of the proposed CAS (Combat Air System) project. The Sensex climbed during the day, while the Nifty managed to hold gains but closed just below the key 24,600 level.

Traders said the decision brought some clarity on defence spending, which has been a major theme for the market in recent weeks. The benchmark indices opened on a firm note and maintained momentum through the afternoon, though profit-taking at higher levels trimmed some gains towards the close.

Nifty's Narrow Miss

The Nifty50 index ended the session a shade below the psychologically important 24,600 mark, after briefly trading above it during the day. This level has emerged as a crucial resistance zone in recent sessions, and market participants will be watching whether the index can sustain a breakout in the coming days.

The broader market showed mixed trends, with midcap and smallcap stocks putting up a varied show. Sectoral indices were largely positive, with defence-related stocks among the prominent gainers following the CAS announcement.

What Drove the Rally?

Apart from the CAS approval, buying was seen in select heavyweight stocks, which helped lift the overall market. However, gains were capped by concerns over global cues, including crude oil prices and the movement of foreign institutional flows.

According to analysts, the market is currently in a consolidation phase, with investors awaiting fresh triggers. The CAS decision provided a short-term push, but the sustainability of the rally remains uncertain.

Investor Sentiment and Outlook

Market breadth was slightly positive, with more stocks advancing than declining on the BSE. The rupee's movement against the dollar also remained in focus, as a weaker currency could impact foreign inflows.

Experts suggest that domestic institutional investors have been supporting the market, but sustained buying from foreign portfolio investors is needed for a decisive move above the 24,600 level on the Nifty.

Going forward, traders will be closely tracking global market trends, upcoming economic data, and any further policy announcements from the government. The CAS project's detailed contours, including its timeline and budget allocation, are expected to be revealed in the coming weeks, which could influence defence-related stocks further.

For now, the immediate support for the Nifty is seen around the 24,300-24,400 zone, while resistance remains at 24,600-24,700. A breakout above this range could open the door for fresh upside, but until then, the market is likely to remain range-bound.
