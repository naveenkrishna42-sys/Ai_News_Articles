Congress ministers threaten to quit if Rahul not PM candidate

Congress ministers have threatened to quit the Vijay Cabinet if Rahul Gandhi is not accepted as the party's Prime Minister candidate in the 2029 general elections. This move comes as a significant challenge to the current government and could have far-reaching implications for the political landscape.

Modi remains India's top choice for Prime Minister

Despite the Congress ministers' threats, a recent survey shows that Narendra Modi remains India's top choice for Prime Minister. This indicates that Modi's popularity continues to be strong, despite the political turmoil.

TVK not ruling out Vijay as PM candidate in 2029

The Congress president, Mallikarjun Kharge, has not ruled out Vijay as a potential PM candidate in the 2029 general elections. This could potentially ease the tensions within the party and prevent a complete split.

Congress minister threatens to quit TVK cabinet if Rahul is not accepted as PM candidate

A Congress minister has threatened to quit the TVK cabinet if Rahul Gandhi is not accepted as the party's PM candidate. This could further escalate the political tensions within the Congress party.

Broader Implications

The political turmoil within the Congress party could have significant implications for the upcoming general elections. It could lead to a weaker opposition, which could benefit the current government. However, it could also lead to a more fragmented opposition, which could make it easier for the opposition to form a coalition government.
