Mac Allister's father reveals Messi's tunnel talk before World Cup final

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mac Allister Sr. disclosed the message his son sent about Messi's pre-final talk.
  • Messi's 'forget about everything' speech has gone viral, fueling debate.
  • A video from the tunnel has emerged, prompting mixed interpretations.

In a revelation that has added fresh layers to one of football's most talked-about moments, the father of Argentina midfielder Alexis Mac Allister has shared what his son told him about Lionel Messi's dressing-room speech before the 2026 FIFA World Cup final.

Speaking to media, Mac Allister Sr. said, 'I sent a message to Alexis. He told me, "Dad, Messi gave a speech. He said forget about everything."' The anecdote, first reported by The Times of India, has since been picked up by outlets including MSN, NDTV Sports and Euronews.

Messi's 'forget about everything' speech

The Argentine captain's pre-final talk has become a subject of intense speculation. According to multiple accounts, Messi told his teammates to block out external noise and focus solely on the match. The phrase 'forget about everything' has been widely quoted.

NDTV Sports reported that a teammate described the speech as pivotal. 'It was simple but powerful. He made us believe we could do it,' the unnamed player said. The exact wording and context, however, remain unclear.

Tunnel video fuels debate

A video of the Argentina team in the tunnel before the final has added to the mystery. Euronews noted that the clip 'fuels speculation' about what was said. Some observers interpret it as evidence of a morale-boosting moment, while others read deeper meaning into the players' expressions.

MARCA, however, offered a different take. The Spanish outlet suggested the video 'buries the conspiracy theories about the defeat against Spain,' referencing rumours that Argentina's loss was influenced by factors beyond the pitch. It remains unclear which match the tunnel footage is from.

What actually happened in the dressing room?

Officials have not confirmed the full content of Messi's speech. The Argentina Football Association has not issued a statement. The only firsthand accounts come from players and their families, like the Mac Allisters.

Mac Allister Sr.'s disclosure adds a personal dimension. 'Alexis said the whole team was emotional,' he recounted. 'It wasn't just tactics. It was about heart.' The comment underscores the intense psychological stakes of a World Cup final.

The match itself ended in a loss for Argentina against Spain, a result that surprised many. Conspiracy theories online have ranged from tactical errors to external interference, but no evidence supports them.

What remains clear is that Messi's leadership in the dressing room continues to fascinate fans and analysts alike. The combination of Mac Allister's account, the viral video and the team's performance creates a story that is as much about human emotion as about sport.

As the dust settles, attention is turning to what Messi himself might say. So far, he has not commented publicly. Whether the full story will emerge—or remain a legend of the tunnel—is something only time will tell.
