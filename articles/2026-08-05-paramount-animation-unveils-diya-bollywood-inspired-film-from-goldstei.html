Paramount Animation Unveils Diya, Bollywood-Inspired Film From Goldstein and Daley

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Paramount Animation is developing an animated Bollywood-inspired film titled Diya.
  • The project is being helmed by Jonathan Goldstein and John Francis Daley, known for Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves.
  • Specific plot details and release dates have not yet been announced.
  • The film marks a notable expansion of Hollywood animation into Indian cultural narratives.

Paramount Animation has set its sights on India with an ambitious new project. The studio is developing Diya, an animated film inspired by the vibrancy of Bollywood, with Jonathan Goldstein and John Francis Daley attached to direct.

The announcement, first reported by The Hollywood Reporter, signals a bold move by the studio to tap into one of the world's largest and fastest-growing entertainment markets. While specifics remain scarce, the project is already generating significant buzz within the industry.

A Creative Duo at the Helm

Goldstein and Daley are no strangers to high-stakes filmmaking. The duo previously directed Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves, which earned praise for its blend of humour, heart, and spectacle. Their knack for balancing character-driven storytelling with large-scale action makes them a fitting choice for a Bollywood-inspired animated feature.

The pair have also built a reputation as versatile writers, with credits ranging from Horrible Bosses to Spider-Man: Homecoming. Their ability to infuse stories with wit and emotional depth will likely be crucial in translating the larger-than-life essence of Bollywood to animation.

What We Know About Diya

Details about the film's plot remain tightly under wraps. The title, Diya, which translates to 'lamp' or 'light' in Hindi, hints at themes of hope, celebration, and perhaps a protagonist who brings brightness into the world. But official synopses have not yet been released.

What is clear is the intent: to create an animated feature that captures the musical, colourful, and emotionally charged storytelling of Bollywood cinema. This is not merely a project with an Indian setting, but one that aims to embody the spirit of a genre that has captivated audiences globally for decades.

A Strategic Move for Paramount

For Paramount Animation, Diya represents more than just another release. It is a calculated step towards diversifying its slate and appealing to a global audience. With India's box office growing steadily, a film that authentically celebrates its culture could find substantial traction both domestically and internationally.

The studio has been looking to expand its footprint beyond traditional Western narratives. Earlier initiatives have included partnerships and projects aimed at international markets, though none have been as direct in their embrace of a specific cinematic tradition as Diya.

Why Bollywood and Animation Are a Natural Fit

Bollywood films are known for their song-and-dance sequences, dramatic emotions, and larger-than-life characters—elements that animation can amplify in spectacular ways. The medium offers limitless possibilities for visual grandeur, from elaborate musical numbers to fantastical settings, making it an ideal match for the genre's exuberance.

Industry observers have noted that animated musicals have a strong track record in Hollywood, from Disney classics to recent hits like Encanto. Diya could well follow in that tradition, but with a distinctly Indian flavour, offering something fresh to audiences weary of conventional animated fare.

What's Next

As of now, there is no confirmed release date, casting news, or further creative details. Production is presumably in early stages, with Goldstein and Daley likely developing the script and visual style alongside the animation team at Paramount.

All eyes will be on the studio in the coming months for casting announcements, a first-look concept art reveal, or a teaser trailer. The success of Diya could open the floodgates for more culturally specific animated films from major Hollywood studios, making it a project worth watching closely.
