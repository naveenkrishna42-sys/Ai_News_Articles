Uneven utility patches cause discomfort and safety concerns for motorists

Motorists in Tambaram are facing discomfort and safety concerns due to uneven utility patches on the city's roads. The patches, which are meant to provide a smooth surface for vehicles, have instead created bumps and dips, making the roads rough and uncomfortable to drive on.

Why this matters

The issue of uneven utility patches is not new, but it has become more prevalent in recent months. The problem is not only causing discomfort for motorists but also posing a safety risk. Some drivers have reported accidents due to the uneven surfaces, which can cause vehicles to lose control and skid.

The problem is particularly severe in Tambaram, a bustling locality in Chennai, where the roads are heavily used by both private and public vehicles. The issue is likely to worsen in the coming months, especially during the monsoon season, when the roads are more prone to potholes and cracks.

What authorities are doing

Local authorities have been urged to take immediate action to address the issue. Officials have acknowledged the problem and have promised to repair the uneven patches as soon as possible. However, motorists are skeptical about the speed and effectiveness of the repairs.

The authorities have also been asked to ensure that the utility patches are properly maintained and that the roads are regularly inspected to prevent such issues in the future.
