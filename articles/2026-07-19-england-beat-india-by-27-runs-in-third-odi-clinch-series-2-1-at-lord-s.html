England beat India by 27 runs in third ODI, clinch series 2-1 at Lord's

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ben Duckett scored 176 off 138 balls, Joe Root made 114 as England posted 387/5.
  • England broke a 51-year-old Lord's record for highest ODI total at the venue.
  • India managed 360/7 in 50 overs, losing the deciding match by 27 runs and the series 2-1.

England defeated India by 27 runs in the third and deciding ODI at Lord's on Sunday, winning the three-match series 2-1. Chasing a daunting target of 388, India finished at 360 for seven in their 50 overs.

Duckett and Root power England to 387

Ben Duckett hammered a magnificent 176 off 138 balls, his second ODI hundred. Joe Root backed him with a measured 114, his 17th ODI century. The duo added 241 runs for the second wicket, setting the platform for England's imposing total of 387 for five.

England's innings broke a 51-year-old record at Lord's. Their total of 387 is now the highest ODI score at the historic ground, surpassing the previous best of 334 set by India against England in 1975.

India's chase falters despite Kohli's fight

India's reply began steadily, but wickets fell at regular intervals. Virat Kohli top-scored with 78 off 72 balls, holding the innings together. However, the required rate kept climbing, and India never managed a sustained partnership to threaten England's target.

Shubman Gill made 47, and Hardik Pandya chipped in with 44. But no other batter crossed fifty. The lower order swung hard in the final overs — KL Rahul smashed a quick 35 not out — but the asking rate proved too steep.

Brydon Carse was the pick of England's bowlers, taking three wickets. Adil Rashid and Liam Livingstone each picked up one, keeping India's scoring in check during the middle overs.

Series result and what it means

With this win, England took the series 2-1 after India had won the opening ODI. The result gives England momentum ahead of their upcoming white-ball assignments.

India will now shift focus to the T20I series against England, which begins on July 22 at the same venue. The squad is expected to see several changes, with rested players returning.
