Bank of India FD Rates: How Senior Citizens Can Earn 7.45% Interest

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Credit Cards & Cashback Original source ↗
Key points
  • Senior citizens can now earn up to 7.45% interest on select FD tenures.
  • Higher rates apply to specific investment durations; check the bank's latest slabs.
  • Investors can apply for these FDs online via BOI's mobile app or internet banking portal.

Updated Interest Rates for Senior Citizens

Bank of India (BOI) has officially adjusted its fixed deposit (FD) interest rates, creating a competitive window for conservative savers. Senior citizens stand to benefit most, with the highest available rate now touching 7.45% on specific tenures. These hikes are part of the bank's strategy to attract retail deposits while offering higher yield potential to its senior customer base.

The interest rates are not uniform across all time frames. You must review the bank's latest interest rate chart, as rates vary significantly based on whether you choose a short-term, medium-term, or long-term deposit. The 7.45% rate is reserved for select tenures, and opting for a duration outside these specific bands will result in a lower yield. Always verify the maturity period before locking in your funds.

Eligibility and Account Features

To qualify for the senior citizen rate, you must be 60 years of age or older. You will need to provide valid age proof, such as an Aadhaar card, PAN card, or a government-issued birth certificate, during the account opening process. The FD account can be opened as a single holder or a joint account, provided the first applicant meets the age criteria.

These deposits offer the safety of a public sector bank, making them a preferred choice for individuals looking to preserve capital while earning a steady income. Interest is typically compounded quarterly, which helps in maximizing your returns over the tenure of the deposit. However, ensure you understand the penalty clauses for premature withdrawals, as these can reduce your overall earnings.

How to Open Your FD Online

Applying for a new FD is straightforward if you already have an existing savings account with Bank of India. You do not need to visit a physical branch to secure these rates. Log in to the BOI internet banking portal or use the official mobile application to access the 'Fixed Deposit' section.

  • Select the 'Open New FD' option from the main dashboard.
  • Enter the principal amount you intend to invest.
  • Choose the tenure that qualifies for the 7.45% interest rate.
  • Review the interest payout frequency—you can opt for monthly, quarterly, or cumulative interest payouts.
  • Confirm the details and authenticate the transaction using your secure MPIN or transaction password.

Once the transaction is successful, the FD receipt will be available for download through the portal. If you are a new customer, you will need to complete the e-KYC process through the bank’s website or visit a branch to initiate the relationship. Ensure your PAN is linked to your account to avoid higher TDS deductions on interest income.

Final Recommendation

This fixed deposit offering is ideal for retirees or senior citizens who prioritize guaranteed, risk-free returns over the market volatility associated with equity investments. If you have a lump sum sitting in a low-interest savings account, moving it into these specific BOI tenures will significantly improve your passive income stream without exposing your capital to market risks.
