myHyundai App Now Integrates 30,000+ EV Charging Points Across India

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • myHyundai app now provides access to over 30,000 EV charging points across the country.
  • Hyundai claims this is India's largest EV charging ecosystem accessible through a single app.
  • The company targets establishing 600 public EV charging stations by 2030.
  • Hyundai Motor India inaugurated its 61st public EV charging station in Telangana.
  • A new electric SUV from Hyundai is expected to launch this fiscal year.

Hyundai Motor India has significantly expanded its digital EV charging network. The myHyundai app now offers access to over 30,000 charging points across the country, the company announced on Monday.

This integration creates what Hyundai calls India's largest EV charging ecosystem accessible through a single mobile application. Owners can locate, navigate to, and pay for charging sessions directly through the app.

Massive Network, Single Platform

The 30,000-plus charging points span multiple operators and locations. The move eliminates the need for drivers to juggle multiple apps or memberships for different charging networks.

Hyundai has been steadily expanding its own public charging infrastructure. The company recently inaugurated its 61st public EV charging station in Telangana, marking continued physical expansion alongside the digital push.

Ambitious Targets Ahead

Hyundai has set a target of establishing 600 public EV charging stations across India by 2030. This would represent a tenfold increase from current numbers over the next four years.

The company is also preparing to launch a new electric SUV within this fiscal year. Details about the model remain under wraps, but it will join Hyundai's existing EV lineup in India, which includes the Kona Electric and Ioniq 5.

Industry Context

India's EV charging infrastructure has been a major barrier to adoption. Limited public charging availability has deterred potential buyers, particularly those without dedicated home charging setups.

Automakers are increasingly investing in charging networks to address this gap. Hyundai's approach combines its own branded stations with partnerships that aggregate third-party chargers onto a single platform.

What This Means for Owners

For current Hyundai EV owners, the update is automatic through the myHyundai app. New features include real-time charger availability, pricing transparency, and route planning that accounts for charging stops.

The app also supports remote monitoring of charging sessions. Users can start and stop charging, track energy consumption, and receive notifications when charging is complete.

Hyundai has not disclosed how many of the 30,000 points are its own versus partner-operated. Officials have not yet confirmed the full list of partner networks included in the aggregation.

The company's push comes as competition in India's EV market intensifies. Tata Motors, MG Motor, and Mahindra are all expanding their charging ecosystems and launching new electric models.

With a new SUV on the horizon and charging infrastructure growing, Hyundai is positioning itself for a larger share of India's evolving electric vehicle market.
