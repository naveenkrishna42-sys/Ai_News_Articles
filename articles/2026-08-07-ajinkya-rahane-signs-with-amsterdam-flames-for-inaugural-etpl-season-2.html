Ajinkya Rahane signs with Amsterdam Flames for inaugural ETPL season

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ajinkya Rahane has signed with Amsterdam Flames for the inaugural ETPL season.
  • This is Rahane's first overseas franchise commitment after retiring from international cricket.
  • The ETPL is a new European T20 league featuring city-based teams across the continent.

Rahane's new chapter begins in Europe

Ajinkya Rahane, one of India's most dependable Test batters of the modern era, has signed with Amsterdam Flames for the inaugural season of the European T20 Premier League (ETPL). The move comes shortly after his retirement from international cricket, marking a fresh chapter in a career that spanned over a decade.

The Amsterdam Flames confirmed the signing on Friday, though financial details of the contract have not been disclosed. Rahane is expected to bring leadership and experience to a side that will feature a mix of domestic European players and overseas stars.

From India's middle order to European greens

Rahane's international career was defined by resilience. He played 85 Tests, 90 ODIs, and 20 T20Is for India, with his standout moment coming during the 2020-21 Border-Gavaskar Trophy in Australia, where he led a depleted side to a historic series win. His 27 Test centuries and 5,077 runs in the format placed him among India's most consistent overseas performers.

The BCCI has since paid tribute to Rahane's contributions, acknowledging his role in shaping Indian cricket's away dominance. Speaking to the board, Rahane revealed that the decision to join the ETPL was influenced by his desire to stay active in the game while exploring new challenges outside the traditional cricket circuit.

Why the ETPL, and why now?

In an interview with India Today, Rahane explained that the ETPL's growing reputation and its focus on nurturing young talent drew him to the league. He also hinted that the timing felt right, saying that the transition from international cricket to franchise leagues was a natural progression for players of his generation.

The Times of India reported that Rahane had been weighing offers from multiple leagues, including the IPL and county cricket, before settling on the ETPL. The Amsterdam Flames, a newly formed franchise, are believed to have offered him a mentorship role alongside playing duties.

Rahane also spoke candidly about the toughest phase of his career, revealing that he spent nearly two years as India's 12th man. "I was India's 12th man for two years," he said, referring to a period when he was consistently in the squad but rarely in the playing XI. That experience, he added, taught him patience and mental toughness, qualities he now hopes to pass on to younger teammates.

What the ETPL means for cricket's global expansion

The ETPL is part of a broader push to expand cricket's footprint in Europe, a region traditionally dominated by football. With franchises based in cities like Amsterdam, London, and Berlin, the league aims to tap into new audiences and provide a platform for players from non-traditional cricketing nations.

Rahane's signing is a significant boost for the league's credibility. His reputation as a calm, composed leader could help attract other high-profile players in future seasons. The Amsterdam Flames are expected to announce their full squad in the coming weeks, with the tournament slated to begin later this year.

For Rahane, this is more than just a playing stint. It is an opportunity to reinvent himself as a mentor and ambassador for the sport in a new market. His experience in Australian and English conditions will be invaluable on European pitches, which are still unfamiliar to many international cricketers.

Looking ahead

Rahane will join the Amsterdam Flames for pre-season training in September, with the ETPL's opening match expected in October. The league's organisers will be watching closely to see whether his presence can draw Indian fans and global attention to a tournament still finding its feet.
