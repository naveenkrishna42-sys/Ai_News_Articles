Sensex surges 750 points, Nifty50 nears 24,000 on global cues, buybacks

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex surged over 750 points in Monday's trade
  • Nifty50 ended the session just below the 24,000 mark
  • Global market strength and buyback announcements fueled the rally

Indian equity benchmarks staged a strong rally on Monday, with the BSE Sensex climbing over 750 points and the Nifty50 closing just shy of the key 24,000 level. The sharp uptick came on the back of positive global cues and a flurry of corporate buyback announcements that boosted investor sentiment.

Global markets provide tailwind

Asian and European markets traded firmly in the green, lifting domestic indices from the open. Wall Street's strong close on Friday also set a positive tone for emerging markets like India.

Foreign portfolio investors turned net buyers in early trade, adding to the momentum. Analysts pointed to easing fears of aggressive rate hikes by the US Federal Reserve as a key factor behind the global risk-on mood.

Corporate actions drive buying

Several blue-chip companies announced share buyback programmes over the past week, which provided a floor under stock prices. Heavyweights in banking, IT and auto sectors saw sustained buying interest.

Reliance Industries, HDFC Bank and Infosys were among the top contributors to the Sensex gains. Market breadth remained positive, with over two-thirds of Nifty50 stocks ending in the green.

Technical levels in focus

The Nifty50 closed at 23,982, just 18 points shy of the psychologically important 24,000 mark. Traders said the index faces resistance at the 24,050-24,100 zone, while support has shifted higher to 23,750.

Volatility indicators eased, with India VIX dropping nearly 4% during the session. This suggests traders are pricing in lower near-term risk.

Broader market participation

Midcap and smallcap indices also rose between 0.8% and 1.2%, tracking the benchmarks. The BSE Midcap index hit a fresh record high during intraday trade.

However, market veterans cautioned that the rally remains narrow. Only a handful of sectors — banking, IT and energy — drove the bulk of the gains, while pharma and FMCG stocks lagged.

What to watch next

Investors will now eye macroeconomic data due later this week, including India's July manufacturing PMI and US jobs numbers. Any surprise in these releases could determine whether the indices sustain above current levels or face a pullback. The 24,000 mark on Nifty remains the immediate target for bulls.
