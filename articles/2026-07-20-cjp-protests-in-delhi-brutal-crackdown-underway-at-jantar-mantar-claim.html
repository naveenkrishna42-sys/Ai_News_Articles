CJP Protests in Delhi: 'Brutal Crackdown' Underway at Jantar Mantar, Claims Group

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • CJP claims police are conducting a 'brutal crackdown' on protesters at Jantar Mantar.
  • A viral video shows a protester standing firm during the police action.
  • The group has made three demands to the government, including the release of Sonam Wangchuk and compensation for NEET deaths.

New Delhi: Tensions ran high at Jantar Mantar on Monday as the 'Cockroach Janta Party' (CJP) accused Delhi Police of launching a 'brutal crackdown' against its protesters. The group, which had been demonstrating at the historic site, claimed that authorities were using excessive force to disperse the crowd.

In a statement, the CJP alleged that police personnel were beating protesters and making arbitrary arrests. Officials from the Delhi Police have not yet confirmed the nature of the action or the number of detentions made.

Viral Video Shows Protester Standing Firm

A video clip from the protest site has gone viral on social media. It shows a male protester standing his ground as a line of police personnel advance, shouting 'Maaro sir' (Hit him, sir). The protester does not retreat, even as officers close in. The incident has sparked widespread debate online, with many users condemning the police action.

The authenticity of the video has not been independently verified by TIVRA News. However, it has been widely shared across platforms, drawing attention to the ongoing standoff between the group and law enforcement.

CJP's Three Demands to the Government

The CJP has outlined three specific demands as the basis for their protest. First, they are demanding the immediate release of activist Sonam Wangchuk, who they say has been detained. Second, they are calling for the removal of Union Minister Dharmendra Pradhan from his post. Third, the group is seeking Rs 1 crore in compensation for each of the students who died in connection with the NEET examination irregularities.

The group had earlier announced their plan to march to Parliament. It remains unclear if they were able to proceed with that plan amid the police action at Jantar Mantar.

Political Reactions and Broader Context

The protest comes on the same day that Prime Minister Narendra Modi appealed for a smooth monsoon session of Parliament. Opposition parties, however, forced multiple adjournments, citing various issues including the treatment of protesters. The CJP's demonstration adds to the charged political atmosphere in the capital.

Activist Sonam Wangchuk has been a prominent figure in recent protests. The demand for his release has become a rallying point for several opposition groups. The call for Minister Pradhan's removal and the compensation for NEET deaths have further complicated the government's handling of the situation.

As the day progressed, reports indicated that some protesters had been taken into custody. The exact number of detainees and the status of the protest's leadership remain unclear.

What to watch: The Delhi Police are expected to issue an official statement regarding the nature of the operation. The CJP has indicated they will continue their agitation, raising the possibility of further confrontations in the coming days.
