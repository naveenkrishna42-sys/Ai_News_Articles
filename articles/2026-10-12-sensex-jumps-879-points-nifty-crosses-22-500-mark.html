Sensex Jumps 879 Points, Nifty Crosses 22,500 Mark

Market Overview: Strong Close for Benchmark Indices

The BSE Sensex rallied 879 points to close above 78,000, while the NSE Nifty crossed the 22,500 mark, marking a robust end to the trading session. The upside was led by gains in FMCG, auto, and financial stocks, indicating broad-based buying interest among investors.

Key Movers: ITC and Eicher Motors Shine

Among the top contributors to the Sensex rally, ITC and Eicher Motors emerged as standout performers, each surging nearly 4%. ITC’s gains were driven by continued momentum in its consumer goods business, while Eicher Motors benefited from sustained demand for Royal Enfield motorcycles.

ITC: FMCG Momentum Drives Rally

ITC closed 4.1% higher, supported by strong volume and positive sentiment around its diversified portfolio. The stock has been on an upward trajectory over the past month, reflecting investor confidence in its non-cigarette businesses, particularly foods and personal care.

Eicher Motors: Auto Demand Keeps Gauges Upbeat

Eicher Motors ended the day up 3.9%, tracking gains in auto stocks amid healthy retail demand. Royal Enfield’s consistent performance in the premium biking segment has kept the investor focus on the company’s growth prospects.

Market Breadth and Technical Outlook

The advance-decline ratio remained positive throughout the session, with over 1,800 stocks rising against 1,100 declining on the NSE. This suggests healthy participation across market caps, reinforcing the strength of the ongoing rally.

Support and Resistance Levels to Watch

For the Nifty, immediate support is placed around 22,350, while resistance could cap gains near 22,700. On the Sensex, the next key level to watch is 78,500 on the upside and 77,600 on the downside.

The strong showing across mid and small-cap indices further buoyed investor morale. With global cues remaining stable and domestic consumption showing resilience, equities appear well-positioned for continued upside, barring any major macroeconomic disruption.
