Bajaj Finance, Finserv Shares Fall on RBI Draft Proposal

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Banking Original source ↗
Key points
  • RBI draft proposal triggers sell-off in Bajaj Finance and Bajaj Finserv shares.
  • The proposal raises concerns over regulatory compliance and business models.
  • Market analysts watch for further clarity from the central bank.

Shares of Bajaj Finance and Bajaj Finserv took a sharp hit on Friday, tumbling as much as 8% in early trade, after the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) released a draft proposal that spooked investors. The draft, which seeks to tighten norms around certain lending practices, has raised questions about the future profitability of the country's largest non-banking financial companies (NBFCs).

The sell-off wiped out nearly ₹30,000 crore in combined market capitalisation within hours, reflecting the sensitivity of the market to any regulatory tightening. By mid-morning, Bajaj Finance was trading at ₹6,120, down 7.5%, while Bajaj Finserv slipped 6.8% to ₹1,740 on the BSE.

What's in the RBI Draft Proposal?

While the RBI has not officially published the full text, sources suggest the draft focuses on capping the amount NBFCs can lend against certain collateral, particularly gold and unlisted securities. The central bank is reportedly concerned about the rapid growth in these segments and the potential systemic risk they pose.

For Bajaj Finance, which has a significant retail lending portfolio, the proposal could directly impact its loan growth trajectory. Analysts at domestic brokerages have already started trimming their earnings estimates, citing the possible compression in net interest margins.

"The RBI is signalling that it wants NBFCs to be more prudent," said a banking sector analyst on condition of anonymity. "But the market is reacting as if this is a done deal, which is why we are seeing this panic selling."

Investor Sentiment Turns Cautious

The decline in Bajaj Finance and Bajaj Finserv also dragged down the broader financial indices, with the Nifty Bank falling 1.2% in tandem. Other NBFCs, including Muthoot Finance and Cholamandalam Investment, also witnessed selling pressure, though their losses were relatively muted.

The market's reaction underscores a growing wariness among investors about regulatory overreach. Over the past year, the RBI has taken several steps to tighten oversight of the shadow banking sector, including increasing risk weights on unsecured personal loans.

"This is not the first time the RBI has moved to curb excesses," noted a fund manager at a Mumbai-based asset management company. "But the severity of the market reaction suggests that investors are pricing in a worst-case scenario."

What This Means for Bajaj Finance

Bajaj Finance has been a market darling for years, thanks to its strong growth in consumer durables financing and digital lending. However, the proposed norms could force the company to recalibrate its product mix, potentially slowing down its expansion in high-yield segments.

The company's management has not yet issued an official statement, but sources indicate that they are studying the draft and will engage with the RBI during the consultation period. Historically, the central bank has been open to feedback, and there is a possibility that some of the more stringent provisions could be softened.

Still, the uncertainty is likely to keep the stock volatile in the near term. Options data suggests that traders are betting on further downside, with put volumes at their highest in three months.

Market Outlook and Expert Views

Most analysts believe that the fundamental strength of Bajaj Finance remains intact, but they caution that the regulatory environment is becoming less favourable. "The company has navigated regulatory changes before," said a research head at a foreign brokerage. "But the cost of compliance is going up, and that will inevitably impact returns."

The RBI has invited comments on the draft proposal until the end of September, after which it will finalise the norms. Until then, investors are likely to remain on edge, and any further clarity from the central bank will be closely watched.

For now, the immediate focus is on whether the RBI will provide clarifications in its upcoming monetary policy meeting, scheduled for next month. Until then, the market's mood towards NBFCs remains cautious, and Bajaj Finance's stock is likely to mirror that sentiment.
