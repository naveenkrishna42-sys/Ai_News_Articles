Spider-Man: Brand New Day Sets $42M Tuesday Record, Grosses $1.1B Globally

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day earned $42 million on Tuesday, a new U.S. record.
  • The film's global gross has reached $1.1 billion, the highest of any movie this year.
  • It surpassed Avengers: Endgame's opening weekend record with $360 million domestically.

A Record-Breaking Tuesday

Spider-Man: Brand New Day has swung past another milestone, setting a new U.S. box office record for a Tuesday with $42 million in ticket sales. The figure, reported by industry tracker Deadline, pushes the film's global total past $1.1 billion, making it the highest-grossing picture worldwide so far this year.

The Tuesday haul is particularly striking because it is not a holiday or a traditional moviegoing night. It underscores the sustained momentum of a film that has dominated conversations since its release, drawing repeat viewers and casual fans alike.

Biggest Opening Weekend Ever

The latest Spider-Man installment has already claimed the title of the biggest opening weekend in box office history, earning $360 million domestically. That performance eclipsed the previous record held by Avengers: Endgame, a benchmark that had stood for years.

Industry analysts had predicted a strong debut, but the scale of the response has exceeded expectations. The film's blend of spectacle and emotional stakes appears to have resonated widely, pulling in audiences across age groups and demographics.

Topping Toy Story 5

With the $1.1 billion global gross, Brand New Day has also overtaken Toy Story 5 to become the year's highest-grossing film. The animated sequel had held the top spot for several months, but Spider-Man's rapid accumulation has now pushed it aside.

The gap is expected to widen further as the film continues its run in international markets, where it has shown no signs of slowing down. Several major territories have yet to fully exhaust their theatrical windows.

Industry Reactions and Rivalries

The success has not gone unnoticed by peers in the industry. The Russo Brothers, who directed Avengers: Doomsday, publicly congratulated the Spider-Man team on their box office achievement. Their message, shared on social media, acknowledged the film's remarkable performance.

Such gestures are rare in a competitive landscape, but they reflect a sense of camaraderie among filmmakers who understand the challenges of delivering a blockbuster in today's market. The goodwill also hints at potential collaborations or crossovers down the line.

What's Driving the Success

While the film's plot details remain under wraps, the buzz has centered on its fresh take on the beloved character. Early reviews have praised the direction, performances, and visual effects, with many calling it a return to form for the franchise.

Box office trackers note that the film has benefited from strong word-of-mouth and a lack of major competition in recent weeks. The release timing, positioned in a relatively quiet period, has allowed it to dominate screens without fragmentation.

What to Watch Next

As Brand New Day continues its theatrical run, all eyes will be on whether it can sustain its pace and challenge the all-time global records held by films like Avatar and Avengers: Endgame. The coming weeks will also bring new releases that could test its longevity, but for now, Spider-Man remains the undisputed king of the box office.

Industry observers will be tracking its performance through the next few weekends, particularly as school holidays begin in several markets. Whether it eventually crosses the $2 billion mark is a question that only time, and audiences, will answer.
