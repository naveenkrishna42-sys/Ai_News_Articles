South Indian OTT Releases This Week: Gatta Kusthi 2, Balan: The Boy and More

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Gatta Kusthi 2, a Tamil comedy sequel, streams on Netflix from July 31.
  • Malayalam film Balan: The Boy, a coming-of-age drama, premieres on Amazon Prime Video.
  • Telugu thriller 'Rudhram' and Kannada horror 'Nagarahavu' also debut this week.

Gatta Kusthi 2: A Sequel with a Twist

The wait for Tamil comedy fans is over. 'Gatta Kusthi 2' drops on Netflix this Friday, July 31. The film picks up where the 2023 original left off, continuing the story of a small-town wrestler with big dreams.

Director R. Kannan returns with a mix of slapstick and emotional beats. The cast includes Vishnu Vishal and Aishwarya Rajesh reprising their roles. Early buzz suggests the sequel leans harder into family drama than its predecessor.

Balan: The Boy — A Quiet Debut

Malayalam cinema offers a quieter alternative this week. 'Balan: The Boy' arrives on Amazon Prime Video on July 30. The film follows a young boy navigating loss and friendship in a coastal village in Kerala.

Director Anjali Menon, known for 'Ustaad Hotel', takes a minimalist approach here. The film has no major stars, relying instead on its script and a child actor's performance. Critics who caught it at the International Film Festival of Kerala praised its restraint.

Other Notable Releases

Telugu audiences get 'Rudhram', a psychological thriller starring Naveen Chandra. It streams on Disney+ Hotstar from August 1. The film deals with a serial killer's cat-and-mouse game with a police officer.

Kannada horror 'Nagarahavu' lands on Zee5 on July 31. Directed by debutant Pavan Kumar, it draws on local folklore about a shape-shifting spirit. The film has been delayed by a year due to post-production issues.

What to Watch This Weekend

If you prefer action-comedy, 'Gatta Kusthi 2' is the safest bet. For those seeking emotional depth, 'Balan: The Boy' offers a slow-burn experience. 'Rudhram' works for thriller enthusiasts, while 'Nagarahavu' caters to horror buffs who enjoy regional mythology.

OTT platforms continue to expand their South Indian language libraries. Netflix and Amazon Prime Video have both committed to more original productions in Tamil and Malayalam over the next quarter.

Viewers should check regional availability, as some titles may have geo-restrictions. Subtitles are available for all releases mentioned here.
