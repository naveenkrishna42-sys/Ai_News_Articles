Urvashi Cinema: A 55-year chapter ends in Bengaluru

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Urvashi Cinema, Bengaluru's oldest cinema, closes after 55 years.
  • The closure has sparked debates on preserving historic cinemas.
  • The building will be redeveloped into a luxury apartment complex.

End of an Era

Bengaluru's oldest cinema, Urvashi, has closed its doors after a remarkable 55-year run. The iconic theatre, which has been a staple of the city's film culture, has finally shut down, leaving a void in the hearts of cinephiles and a sense of nostalgia for the past.

Debates on Preservation

The closure of Urvashi has sparked debates among historians, architects, and cinephiles about the preservation of historic cinemas. While some argue that the building is too old and in need of significant repairs, others believe that it holds cultural significance and should be preserved.

Cultural Significance

Urvashi Cinema has been a witness to numerous milestones in Bengaluru's film history. It has hosted premieres of classic films, screening of international films, and even served as a venue for live performances. The theatre has been a cultural hub, bringing people together through the magic of cinema.

Economic Impact

The closure of Urvashi has also raised questions about the economic impact on the local community. The theatre was a significant employer in the area and its closure will have a ripple effect on the local economy. The redevelopment of the building into a luxury apartment complex will bring new opportunities, but it will also mean the loss of a piece of Bengaluru's history.

Future of Bengaluru's Cinemas

The closure of Urvashi is a reminder of the challenges faced by historic cinemas in the modern world. With the rise of digital streaming and multiplexes, many historic cinemas are struggling to keep up. The future of Bengaluru's cinemas will depend on the city's ability to balance the preservation of its cultural heritage with the need for modern amenities.
