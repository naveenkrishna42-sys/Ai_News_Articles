Aayush Sharma: Salman Khan's Panvel farmhouse has a 'bhai ka bulaawa' rule

Key points
  • Salman Khan reportedly spends most of his time at his Panvel farmhouse.
  • Aayush Sharma says guests can only leave when Salman gives permission.
  • The actor's retreat has become a preferred spot for family and close friends.

Actor Aayush Sharma has offered a rare glimpse into Salman Khan's private life, revealing that the Bollywood superstar spends the bulk of his time at his sprawling Panvel farmhouse. The disclosure came during a recent media interaction where Aayush, married to Salman's sister Arpita Khan, described the unique protocol that governs visits to the property.

'Bhai ka bulaawa' and the unwritten rule

Aayush explained that visits to the farmhouse operate on Salman's terms. "You go there, but you leave only when bhai wishes," he said, adding that the superstar's schedule dictates everything. The actor used the phrase "bhai ka bulaawa aata hai" to describe the informal summons that brings family and friends to the retreat.

Once there, guests quickly learn the house rule: departure is not a personal decision. According to Aayush, Salman's authority over the farmhouse is absolute, and no one leaves without his approval. The system, he suggested, is both a sign of respect and a reflection of Salman's commanding presence.

A retreat from the spotlight

The Panvel farmhouse, located on the outskirts of Mumbai, has long been Salman's preferred sanctuary. Sources close to the actor say he values the privacy and open space it offers, far from the chaos of the city. The property features extensive grounds, a swimming pool, and areas for his pets, including his beloved dogs.

Friends and family members have often described the farmhouse as Salman's true home. The actor reportedly spends weekends and off-days there, hosting close friends and relatives in a relaxed setting. Aayush's comments confirm that the farmhouse is not merely a weekend getaway but Salman's primary residence for much of the week.

Family dynamics and the 'bhai' culture

The disclosure also highlights the centrality of Salman Khan within his extended family. As the eldest son and the most famous member of the Khan family, his preferences often set the agenda. Aayush, who married into the family in 2014, has spoken before about the adjustments required when navigating life around Salman.

"He is a very generous person, but he runs things his way," Aayush had said in a previous interview. The farmhouse rule, he indicated, is not about control but about Salman's desire to have everyone together on his terms. The 'bhai ka bulaawa' culture, as Aayush called it, is a fixture of family gatherings.

Life at the farmhouse

Those who have visited the Panvel property describe a laid-back atmosphere where Salman is often found tending to his animals or working on his fitness. Meals are communal, and conversations stretch late into the night. Aayush said that the farmhouse has become a core part of the family's bonding ritual.

The revelation has sparked curiosity among fans about Salman's off-screen life. While the actor maintains a busy schedule with film shoots and endorsements, the farmhouse remains his personal retreat. Aayush's candid remarks offer a rare window into how Salman Khan, one of India's biggest stars, chooses to spend his time away from the cameras.

What remains to be seen is whether any guest has ever managed to leave the farmhouse without waiting for 'bhai ka bulaawa' to end. For now, Aayush's account suggests that the rule holds firm.
