Two dead, 50 rescued in Noida village building fire; landlord arrested

📅 2026-07-17 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Two people died and around 50 were rescued after a fire in a building in Noida's Mamura village.
  • The landlord has been arrested; police suspect the fire was caused by charging an electric vehicle inside the premises.
  • Narrow lanes in the village hampered firefighting efforts, delaying the response.

Fire in Mamura village claims two lives

A fire that broke out in a multi-storey building in Noida's Mamura village on Wednesday morning killed two people and led to the rescue of around 50 others. The blaze, which started on the ground floor, quickly spread to the upper floors due to the presence of combustible materials.

Firefighters and local police reached the spot after neighbours raised an alarm. Rescue operations continued for several hours as teams worked to evacuate residents trapped inside the building.

Landlord arrested; EV charging suspected

Police have arrested the landlord of the building. Preliminary investigations suggest the fire was triggered by an electric vehicle being charged inside the premises using a regular household extension board.

Charging EVs from standard power sockets and extension boards has become a known fire hazard in many parts of Noida and Delhi-NCR. Officials said such setups lack the necessary safety mechanisms and can easily overheat, especially in congested buildings.

Narrow lanes delayed firefighting

Firefighting efforts were significantly hampered by the narrow lanes in Mamura village. Fire tenders could not reach the building directly, forcing crews to run hoses over long distances. This delayed the response and allowed the fire to intensify.

Residents said they have repeatedly raised concerns about the lack of access for emergency vehicles in the densely packed colony. Local authorities have not yet commented on any measures to address the issue.

Friend recalls victim's last moments

A friend of one of the victims, a 28-year-old software engineer, said she received a call from him moments before the fire engulfed his room. He reportedly told her he was trapped and asked her to inform his family. His body was recovered later; it was burnt beyond recognition. A locket he always wore was the only item that helped identify him.

The other victim has been identified as a 35-year-old daily wage labourer who lived on the ground floor. Their families have been informed, and post-mortem examinations are underway.

What happens next

The arrested landlord is expected to be produced in court on Thursday. Police said they are investigating whether the building had the required fire safety clearances. Meanwhile, the Noida Authority is likely to issue fresh advisories on EV charging in residential areas, though concrete action on ground remains to be seen.
