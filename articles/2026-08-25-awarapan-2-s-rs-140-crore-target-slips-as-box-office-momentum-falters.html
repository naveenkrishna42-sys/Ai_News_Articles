Awarapan 2's Rs 140 crore target slips as box office momentum falters

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Awarapan 2 fails to meet Rs 140 crore India net target on Day 11
  • Global ambitions now face scrutiny after domestic performance
  • Comparison with Emraan Hashmi's previous films reveals mixed results

Box Office Struggles

Emraan Hashmi and Disha Patani's 'Awarapan 2' has hit a snag in its box office journey. After an impressive start, the film failed to meet its Rs 140 crore India net target on Day 11, falling short by a whisker. This marks a significant deviation from the Rs 200 crore global target the film was initially aiming for.

The film's performance has been mixed, with some sources suggesting it may not be able to cross the Rs 200 crore mark globally. This is a stark contrast to Emraan Hashmi's previous films like 'Dhurandhar' and 'Border 2', which had a more consistent box office performance.

Comparative Analysis

When compared to 'Batwara 1947', another Emraan Hashmi film, 'Awarapan 2' has had a less impressive run. While 'Batwara 1947' crossed the Rs 50 crore mark, 'Awarapan 2' is yet to reach even half of that figure. This raises questions about the film's marketability and audience appeal.

The film's performance on Day 11 is a significant setback. It's a clear indication that the film's box office momentum is faltering. The film's ability to sustain its initial momentum will be crucial in determining its ultimate box office performance.

Expert Opinion

Industry experts are divided in their opinions about 'Awarapan 2'. While some believe that the film's performance is a reflection of the current market conditions, others suggest that the film's marketing and promotion strategies may need a relook.

One expert, who asked not to be named, suggested that the film's target audience may not have been clearly defined. This could have led to a lack of focus in the marketing campaigns, which in turn has affected the film's box office performance.

Broader Impact

The film's performance has broader implications for the Indian film industry. It's a reminder that box office success is not guaranteed, even for films with a strong star cast and a reputed director.

Moreover, the film's performance could also impact the careers of Emraan Hashmi and Disha Patani. Both actors have been in the industry for a while and have a significant fan following. However, a film's box office performance is a crucial factor in determining an actor's career trajectory.

Conclusion

In conclusion, 'Awarapan 2' has had a mixed box office run. While it has had a decent start, it's yet to meet its initial targets. The film's ability to sustain its momentum will be crucial in determining its ultimate box office performance. Moreover, the film's performance has broader implications for the Indian film industry and the careers of Emraan Hashmi and Disha Patani.
