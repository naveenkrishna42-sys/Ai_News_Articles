WhatsApp Media Sharing Hit by Global Outage, Users Report Stuck Photos and Videos

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Users globally report problems sending and receiving photos and videos on WhatsApp.
  • The issue appears widespread, affecting users across multiple countries.
  • Meta has not yet confirmed the cause or provided a resolution timeline.
  • This is the latest in a series of technical glitches for the messaging platform.

WhatsApp users across the globe are facing significant issues with media sharing, with multiple reports emerging on Monday of photos and videos getting stuck or failing to send. The problem, which appears to be widespread, has disrupted communication for millions who rely on the Meta-owned platform for daily messaging.

Global Disruption Reported

According to reports from various news outlets, including Telangana Today and The Economic Times, users have been unable to share multimedia content since early Monday morning. The issue is not limited to one region, with complaints flooding in from different parts of the world, indicating a global outage rather than a localized network problem.

On social media platforms like X (formerly Twitter), users have been quick to vent their frustration. Many have reported that while text messages are going through, images and videos are either stuck at a “sending” status or fail to download entirely. This has caused particular inconvenience for businesses and families who depend on WhatsApp for sharing important documents and memories.

Recurring Technical Glitches

This is not the first time WhatsApp has faced such challenges. The platform has experienced several outages over the past year, raising questions about the reliability of its infrastructure. While the current issue seems focused on media sharing, it follows a pattern of intermittent technical problems that have plagued the service recently.

In July, a similar incident occurred where users were unable to send or receive media files for several hours. Meta, the parent company, had then attributed the issue to a server configuration error. However, the company has not yet commented on the current situation, leaving users in the dark about the cause and expected resolution time.

Impact on Users

The timing of this outage is particularly disruptive, coming on a Monday when many professionals rely on WhatsApp for work-related communication. Freelancers, small business owners, and journalists are among those who have reported significant delays in their workflows due to the media-sharing failure.

Some users have resorted to alternative platforms such as Telegram and Signal to share files, while others have reverted to email for urgent documents. The lack of an official update from Meta has only added to the uncertainty, with many speculating whether the issue is due to a hack, a technical upgrade gone wrong, or a simple server overload.

No Official Word Yet

As of now, Meta has not issued any public statement regarding the outage. WhatsApp’s official status page, which is typically updated during such incidents, has also remained silent, further frustrating users who are seeking clarity. Officials have not yet confirmed the root cause, nor have they provided an estimated time for when services will be fully restored.

In the past, Meta has usually resolved such issues within a few hours, but the company’s silence this time has been unusual. Some experts suggest that the problem might be more complex than a simple glitch, possibly involving the platform’s encryption protocols or content delivery networks.

What to Watch

Users are advised to keep an eye on WhatsApp’s official social media handles for updates. If the issue persists beyond a few hours, it could signal a deeper problem that might require a more extensive fix. For now, the best course of action is patience, as the company works behind the scenes to restore normalcy.

The coming hours will be crucial in determining whether this is a short-lived disruption or a more prolonged outage. Historically, WhatsApp has bounced back quickly, but the lack of communication from Meta is a concern that users and industry watchers will be closely monitoring.
