Spider-Man: Brand New Day Crosses $1.67 Billion Globally, Sets India Records

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The film is the second-fastest to reach $1 billion overseas.
  • Global box office stands at $1.67 billion.
  • It has grossed over Rs 455 crore in India, beating Avengers: Endgame.
  • The film had a strong U.S. second weekend with $145 million.

Marvel's latest web-slinger adventure, Spider-Man: Brand New Day, has swung past yet another milestone. The Tom Holland-starrer has become the second-fastest movie ever to cross $1 billion at the overseas box office, and its global tally now stands at a staggering $1.67 billion.

The film's overseas run has been nothing short of spectacular, with markets across Asia, Europe, and Latin America contributing heavily. According to trade analysts, the movie achieved the $1 billion overseas mark in record time, trailing only Avengers: Endgame in the speed-to-milestone race.

A Record-Breaking Run in India

India has emerged as a major stronghold for the film. Spider-Man: Brand New Day has grossed over Rs 455 crore in the domestic market, surpassing the lifetime collections of Avengers: Endgame. This makes it the highest-grossing Hollywood film ever released in India, a title previously held by the Russo brothers' epic.

The film's performance in India is particularly notable given the competitive landscape. It has outpaced several big-ticket Bollywood releases during its run, and exhibitors report strong repeat viewership, especially in premium formats like IMAX and 4DX.

Box Office Dominance Continues

The film's global momentum shows no signs of slowing. In its second weekend in the United States, Spider-Man: Brand New Day collected $145 million, marking the third-best second weekend in U.S. box office history. This strong hold indicates that the film is enjoying positive word-of-mouth and high audience satisfaction.

Industry observers note that the film's blend of multiverse elements, emotional stakes, and fan-favourite characters has struck a chord with audiences worldwide. The overseas contribution now accounts for more than 60% of its total gross, underscoring the franchise's global appeal.

What This Means for the Franchise

The success of Brand New Day reinforces the enduring popularity of Spider-Man as a character, and Tom Holland's portrayal in particular. With the film still running in several markets, including Japan and China, the final global tally could climb even higher.

For Marvel Studios and Sony Pictures, the numbers are a clear signal that the Spider-Man franchise remains one of the most bankable properties in cinema. The film's performance in India, a market that has historically been challenging for Hollywood, is being closely studied by studios planning future releases.

As the theatrical run continues, trade experts will be watching to see if Brand New Day can eventually challenge the $2 billion mark, a feat achieved by only a handful of films in history. For now, the web-slinger is enjoying a remarkable victory lap.
