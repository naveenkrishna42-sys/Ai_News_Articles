BJP leader falls into open drain in Bengaluru, files police complaint against Minister and MLA

BJP leader falls into open drain in Bengaluru

A BJP leader in Bengaluru has filed a police complaint against a local Minister and an MLA after he fell into an uncovered drain. The incident took place in the city, highlighting the persistent problem of open drains in many parts of Bengaluru.

The leader, who has not been named in the complaint, was reportedly walking when he stepped into the drain. He sustained minor injuries. The complaint alleges negligence on the part of the authorities responsible for maintaining civic infrastructure.

Police complaint names Minister and MLA

The complaint specifically names a state Minister and a local MLA. The BJP leader claims that the elected representatives have failed to ensure basic safety measures like covering drains. He is seeking action against them for dereliction of duty.

Police officials have confirmed receiving the complaint. They said they are looking into the matter. No FIR has been registered yet. The Minister and the MLA have not responded to the allegations so far.

Civic infrastructure under scrutiny

This incident has once again brought attention to Bengaluru's crumbling civic infrastructure. Open drains, potholes, and poor lighting are common complaints in many wards. Residents often blame local politicians for ignoring these issues.

The BJP leader's fall is the latest in a series of such accidents. In the past, several citizens have been injured or killed after falling into uncovered drains. The city's rapid urbanisation has outpaced its infrastructure development.

Political blame game begins

The opposition has seized on the incident to attack the ruling party. BJP leaders have demanded a thorough investigation and immediate action to cover all open drains. They argue that the safety of citizens is being compromised.

The ruling party, meanwhile, has downplayed the incident. Some leaders have called it a political stunt. They say the BJP leader could have avoided the drain if he had been careful. The debate is expected to continue in the coming days.

What happens next depends on the police investigation. The complaint will likely be scrutinised for legal validity. Meanwhile, residents of Bengaluru will be watching to see if any concrete action is taken to fix the drains.
