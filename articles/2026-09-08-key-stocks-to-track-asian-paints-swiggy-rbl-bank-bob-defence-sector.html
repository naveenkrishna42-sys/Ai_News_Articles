Market Focus: Top Stocks to Watch in September 2026

As Indian markets navigate post-rate cut dynamics and evolving consumer preferences, a select group of stocks has emerged as key indicators of sectoral health and investor sentiment. From paint manufacturers to food delivery platforms and banks, these stocks reflect critical trends in housing, retail, banking, and defence. This analysis breaks down the performance drivers, technical benchmarks, and investment implications for Asian Paints, Swiggy, RBL Bank, Bank of Baroda (BoB), and the defence sector.

1. Asian Paints: Leading Paint Manufacturer's Performance Drivers

Asian Paints, India's largest paint company, continues to benefit from strong demand in the housing and infrastructure segments. The company's recent price hikes and expanded product portfolio have supported revenue growth, with Q2 2026 reporting a 12% YoY increase in net sales. The order book, currently at ₹2,500 crore, indicates robust future orders, particularly from residential projects and commercial buildings. Market share remains above 40%, with a focus on premium paints and eco-friendly products.

2. Swiggy: Food Delivery Leader's Growth Trajectory

Swiggy, the dominant food delivery platform, is diversifying beyond meals to capture broader consumer needs. The company's foray into grocery and pharmacy delivery, alongside expansion into international markets (e.g., Singapore, Malaysia), has boosted user base growth. Q2 2026 saw a 15% increase in active users, with subscription models contributing to higher recurring revenue. However, profitability remains a challenge, with operating losses widening to ₹150 crore due to increased competition and logistics costs.

3. RBL Bank: Retail Banking Expansion and Digital Transformation

RBL Bank has emerged as a retail banking leader, driven by aggressive loan growth and digital innovation. The bank's NBFC exposure has declined as it focuses on core retail loans, with personal loans growing 18% YoY. Digital banking initiatives, including the 'RBL Mobile Banking' app, have improved customer engagement, with over 5 million active users. The bank's P/E ratio of 12x is lower than peers, indicating undervaluation amid strong growth prospects.

4. BoB (Bank of Baroda): Regional Bank's Strategic Moves

Bank of Baroda (BoB) is strengthening its position through asset quality improvement and branch modernization. The bank has reduced non-performing assets (NPAs) by 8% YoY, with a focus on bad loan resolution. Digital services, such as mobile banking and internet banking, have increased transaction volumes by 25%. The bank's market cap of ₹1.2 lakh crore and P/E of 8x make it an attractive option for value investors, though regional exposure limits its growth potential.

5. Defence Sector: Government Procurement and Modernization

The defence sector is gaining momentum from government initiatives to modernize the armed forces and boost FDI. Recent procurement orders, including for fighter jets and naval vessels, have increased order books for domestic manufacturers. Companies like Larsen & Toubro (L&T) and Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL) have reported strong orders, with L&T's order book at ₹1.5 lakh crore. The government's 35% FDI cap increase in defence manufacturing has attracted foreign investors, supporting long-term growth.
