Diamond jewellery worth Rs 3 crore stolen from train in Chennai

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • A passenger reported a bag with diamond jewellery worth Rs 3 crore missing from a train at Chennai's MGR Central station.
  • The incident occurred between July 25 and July 26 on the Chennai-bound train.
  • Chennai Railway Police have registered a case and are examining CCTV footage.

Jewellery bag vanishes from train compartment

A passenger travelling to Chennai has reported that a bag containing diamond jewellery worth Rs 3 crore went missing from his train compartment. The incident came to light on Sunday morning at MGR Central railway station in Chennai.

The passenger, a jeweller, had boarded the train from a station in the interior of Tamil Nadu. He placed the bag on the berth above his seat and fell asleep. When he woke up as the train approached Chennai, the bag was gone.

How the theft unfolded

The jeweller realised the bag was missing around 5 am on Sunday, just minutes before the train pulled into Platform 8 at MGR Central. He immediately alerted the train's ticket examiner and the Railway Protection Force personnel stationed at the platform.

Officials said the bag contained multiple pieces of diamond-studded jewellery, including necklaces, earrings and bangles. The total value has been estimated at Rs 3 crore. The passenger was transporting the items from a jewellery showroom in a smaller town to his shop in Chennai.

Investigation underway

The Chennai Railway Police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code. A team has been formed to investigate the theft. Police are scanning CCTV footage from the train's coaches and the station platforms.

Sources said the train had made three scheduled stops between the passenger's boarding point and Chennai. Investigators are questioning passengers who were in the same compartment. They are also checking records of individuals who may have boarded or alighted during the journey.

Railway police have advised passengers to keep valuables securely on them and to avoid leaving bags unattended, especially during overnight journeys. They have also urged anyone with information about the theft to come forward.

The jeweller is cooperating with the police. Officials have not yet confirmed whether any arrests have been made. The investigation is ongoing.

Authorities are now working to trace the bag and the jewellery. The outcome of the CCTV analysis is expected to provide key leads in the coming days.
