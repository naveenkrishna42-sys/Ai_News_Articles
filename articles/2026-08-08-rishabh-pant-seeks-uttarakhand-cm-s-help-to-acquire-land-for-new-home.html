Rishabh Pant seeks Uttarakhand CM's help to acquire land for new home

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Rishabh Pant made a late-night social media appeal to CM Dhami for land acquisition help
  • The wicketkeeper-batter said he has been trying to buy land in Uttarakhand for three years
  • Pant's cryptic post has gone viral, drawing widespread reactions from fans and officials

In an unusual late-night appeal, Indian cricketer Rishabh Pant has reached out to Uttarakhand Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami, seeking help to acquire land in the state. The wicketkeeper-batter, known for his explosive batting, took to social media after midnight on Saturday with a direct request that has since gone viral.

"Please help me in land acquisition," Pant wrote, addressing the chief minister directly. The post, which came at around 1:30 am, was accompanied by a cryptic note that suggested the cricketer's patience was wearing thin after what he described as a three-year struggle to secure a plot.

A three-year wait

Pant, who hails from Roorkee in Uttarakhand, has been vocal about his desire to build a home in his home state. In his post, the 28-year-old said he had been trying to get land for years but had not succeeded. "It's been 3 years, didn't get any land," he wrote, appealing to Dhami to intervene personally.

The cricketer's frustration was palpable in the message, which he framed as a request for a "gift" from the chief minister. While the exact location of the proposed land purchase remains unclear, Pant's emotional appeal suggests this is more than a casual inquiry—it is a plea rooted in his connection to the state.

Viral reaction

Within hours, the post had spread across social media platforms, with fans, political observers, and cricket enthusiasts weighing in. Many expressed sympathy for Pant, noting that even a high-profile athlete like him faces bureaucratic hurdles when trying to buy land in India. Others speculated about the reasons behind the delay, though no official explanation has emerged.

The timing of the appeal is notable. Pant is currently preparing for a busy international season, and his off-field concerns have now taken a public turn. The cricketer, who has been based in Mumbai and Delhi for much of his professional career, has often spoken about his desire to return to his roots.

Political response

As of now, there has been no formal response from the Chief Minister's office. Officials in Dehradun have not confirmed whether Dhami has seen the post or whether any action has been initiated. The silence from the state government has only added to the buzz, with many waiting to see how the administration reacts to a plea from one of India's most popular cricketers.

This is not the first time Pant has shown his attachment to Uttarakhand. He has been involved in local initiatives and has frequently spoken about the state's role in his upbringing. However, this public appeal marks a shift in approach—moving from private negotiations to an open request for state intervention.

What to watch

All eyes are now on the Uttarakhand government's next move. If Dhami responds favourably, it could set a precedent for how the state handles land requests from public figures. For Pant, the outcome will determine whether he can finally build the home he has been dreaming of in the hills he calls his own.
