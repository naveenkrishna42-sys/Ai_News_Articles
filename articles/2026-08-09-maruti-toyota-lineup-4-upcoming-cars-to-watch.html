Maruti, Toyota Lineup: 4 Upcoming Cars to Watch

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Maruti Baleno facelift and new SUV derivatives are in the pipeline
  • Toyota is readying an all-new Fortuner and hybrid powertrains
  • Maruti's flexible production lines will underpin the new models

Maruti Suzuki and its partner Toyota are gearing up for a busy phase in the Indian market, with at least four new models lined up for launch over the next couple of years. From a refreshed Baleno hatchback to an all-electric multi-purpose vehicle, the two automakers are looking to strengthen their presence across segments.

Baleno Facelift Leads the Charge

The Maruti Baleno, a mainstay in the premium hatchback segment, is set to receive a mid-cycle facelift. Spy shots and industry chatter suggest the updated model will feature sharper styling cues and a tweaked cabin. The powertrain lineup is expected to remain largely unchanged, with the 1.2-litre petrol engine continuing to do duty.

Maruti has been steadily updating its lineup to meet stricter emission norms, and the Baleno facelift will likely incorporate some efficiency tweaks. The car is expected to retain its position as a volume driver for the company, competing against the Hyundai i20 and Tata Altroz.

Electric MPV on the Horizon

The partnership between Maruti and Toyota is also bearing fruit in the electric vehicle space. A new electric MPV, codenamed YMC, is in the works and is expected to debut within the next few years. This model will be built on a dedicated EV platform and is likely to be sold under both brands, with minor cosmetic differences.

Maruti has been cautious in its EV approach, but the YMC MPV marks a significant step. It will join the recently launched e-Vitara in the company's electric portfolio. The MPV is expected to target family buyers who are looking for a practical, zero-emission vehicle.

SUV Expansion and Hybrid Push

Beyond the Baleno and the EV, Maruti is reportedly working on new SUV derivatives. The Fronx, which has been a strong seller, is likely to receive a hybrid version. This would align with Maruti's strategy of offering more fuel-efficient options in the compact SUV space, where competition is intense.

Toyota, on its part, is preparing an all-new Fortuner. The flagship SUV has been a favourite among buyers seeking a rugged, road-ready vehicle, and the next generation is expected to bring updated styling and more technology. Toyota is also likely to offer a strong-hybrid powertrain option, given the increasing demand for fuel-efficient large SUVs.

Flexible Production Lines

Maruti's chairman, RC Bhargava, recently indicated that the company is making all its new production lines flexible. This means the same assembly line can produce different models, including petrol, hybrid, and electric vehicles. This approach is expected to reduce costs and allow Maruti to respond quickly to market shifts.

The flexible lines will be crucial as Maruti and Toyota introduce a mix of powertrains across their shared models. It also positions the companies to scale up EV production without heavy new investments. The move reflects a broader industry trend toward adaptable manufacturing.

What to Expect Next

While official timelines are yet to be announced, the Baleno facelift is expected first, possibly within the next year. The electric MPV and new Fortuner are likely to follow in the subsequent years. Maruti and Toyota are keeping details under wraps, but the pipeline clearly signals a focus on electrification and SUV versatility.

Industry watchers will be keen to see how these launches fare in a market that is rapidly evolving, with buyers increasingly prioritising safety, efficiency, and technology. The coming months should bring more clarity on launch dates and pricing.
