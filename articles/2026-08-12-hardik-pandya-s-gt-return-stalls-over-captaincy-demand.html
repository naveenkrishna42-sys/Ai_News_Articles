Hardik Pandya's GT return stalls over captaincy demand

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hardik Pandya has expressed desire to return to Gujarat Titans
  • His captaincy demand is the sticking point in negotiations
  • KKR and Mumbai Indians also in talks for the all-rounder

Mumbai, Aug 12: Hardik Pandya's much-speculated return to Gujarat Titans has hit a wall, with the franchise refusing to entertain the all-rounder's demand for captaincy. Sources close to the development confirmed that while Pandya is keen on a comeback to the Ahmedabad-based side, the rider attached to his homecoming has stalled talks.

The development comes ahead of the IPL 2027 mega auction, where player movements are expected to dominate the build-up. Pandya, who led Gujarat Titans to the title in their debut season in 2022, left the franchise in 2024 to join Mumbai Indians. His subsequent stint with MI, where he took over the captaincy from Rohit Sharma, was marred by fan backlash and inconsistent performances.

Captaincy as a deal-breaker

Gujarat Titans, who have since moved on with Shubman Gill as skipper, are unwilling to revisit the leadership question. The franchise's stance is clear: they want Pandya as a player, not as captain. This has created an impasse, with both sides refusing to budge.

Pandya, for his part, is believed to be keen on a role that reinstates him as a central figure. Having led India to the T20 World Cup title in 2024, the all-rounder sees captaincy as a natural extension of his career. But franchises are wary of the baggage that comes with his leadership, especially after the mixed results at Mumbai Indians.

Multiple suitors in the fray

While the GT talks remain stuck, other franchises have entered the fray. Kolkata Knight Riders are reportedly leading the chase for Pandya's services, with the franchise looking to bolster their middle order and add a seam-bowling option. KKR's interest is not new; they have long been admirers of Pandya's match-winning abilities.

Mumbai Indians, meanwhile, are undecided on whether to part ways with the star all-rounder. The franchise has been weighing the pros and cons of retaining him, given the financial implications and team balance. A trade involving Harshit Rana and Rinku Singh has been suggested by some quarters, with Ravichandran Ashwin floating the idea on his YouTube channel. However, no formal proposal has been made yet.

What lies ahead

With the IPL governing council yet to announce the final auction date, teams are in no hurry to finalise trades. The coming weeks are expected to see intense negotiations as franchises look to strengthen their squads. For Pandya, the situation is delicate — his value as a player remains high, but his leadership aspirations could limit his options.

The coming days will be crucial in determining whether the trade materialises or fizzles out. For now, the ball is in Gujarat Titans' court — and they have made their position clear. Whether Pandya is willing to compromise on his captaincy demand remains to be seen.
