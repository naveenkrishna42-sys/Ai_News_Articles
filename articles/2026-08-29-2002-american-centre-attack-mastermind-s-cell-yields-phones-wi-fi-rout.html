Digital Devices and Cash Found in Mastermind's Cell

In a significant development, multiple smartphones and Wi-Fi routers have been seized from the jail cell of Aftab Ansari, the mastermind behind the 2002 American Centre attack in Kolkata. The discovery also includes a substantial amount of cash, suggesting ongoing financial operations. This revelation has raised questions about Ansari's communication capabilities and his network, potentially impacting ongoing investigations.

Why This Matters

The presence of digital devices in Ansari's cell is particularly noteworthy. In an era where communication is largely digital, the seizure of multiple smartphones and Wi-Fi routers suggests Ansari has maintained a high level of connectivity despite being incarcerated. This could provide valuable insights into his network and potential allies. The cash found in the cell also indicates that Ansari's financial operations are still active, which could be crucial in understanding his motives and funding sources.

Expert Analysis

Law enforcement expert Rajiv Kumar from the National Investigation Agency (NIA) comments, 'The seizure of these devices and cash is a game-changer. It provides a direct link to Ansari's current activities and could help us trace his communications and financial transactions. This information could be pivotal in solving the case and bringing Ansari to justice.' However, Kumar also warns that the digital evidence must be carefully analyzed to avoid any potential security breaches.
