Apple may hike iPhone prices in India from tomorrow: Report

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple likely to raise iPhone prices in India from tomorrow
  • Price revision attributed to higher component costs and import duties
  • Move comes ahead of expected September iPhone 18 series launch

Apple may be set to increase the prices of its iPhones in India from tomorrow, according to a report. The potential price revision comes amid rising component costs and changes in import duties, though the company has not yet made an official announcement.

The report, citing industry sources, suggests that the price hike could apply across multiple iPhone models currently sold in the country. However, the exact percentage of the increase and the specific models affected remain unclear. Apple has not responded to requests for comment.

Why the hike now?

The reported price revision is being attributed to a combination of factors. Global supply chain pressures have pushed up the cost of key components like semiconductors and displays. Additionally, recent adjustments in India's import duty structure on electronics have added to the financial burden on manufacturers.

For a company like Apple, which positions its products in the premium segment, even a small price change can have a significant impact on consumer demand. The Indian smartphone market, however, has shown resilience, with premium devices gaining traction among buyers in metro cities and beyond.

Ahead of the iPhone 18 launch

The reported price hike comes at a strategic moment. Apple is expected to launch its iPhone 18 series in September, with demand reportedly driving the release timeline. If the price increase takes effect now, it could set a new baseline for the upcoming models.

Analysts note that Apple often adjusts prices in India to account for currency fluctuations and tax changes. The company has also been ramping up local manufacturing through partners like Foxconn and Wistron, which helps offset some costs. Yet, a full pass-through of cost increases to consumers seems likely if the report is accurate.

What this means for buyers

For Indian consumers, a price hike would make iPhones more expensive just ahead of the festive season, a period when many typically upgrade their devices. Those planning to buy an iPhone in the coming weeks may want to act quickly before the new prices take effect.

However, it is worth noting that Apple occasionally offers trade-in deals and financing options that can soften the blow. The company's retail presence in India, including its online store and physical outlets, may also run promotions to ease the transition.

The bigger picture

This is not the first time Apple has raised prices in India. Similar moves have been made in the past due to duty changes or currency depreciation. The key difference this time is the timing—just weeks before a major product launch.

Industry watchers will be closely monitoring whether the price revision is a one-off adjustment or a signal of a broader pricing strategy. Apple has been expanding its India operations, and the country is seen as a key growth market for the company.

If the hike is confirmed, it could influence consumer sentiment and impact sales in the short term. But Apple's loyal customer base has historically absorbed price increases without a significant dip in demand.

As of now, the official word from Apple is awaited. The company typically announces price changes through its website and retail channels. Until then, buyers and analysts are left to speculate on the extent of the increase and its long-term implications.

The coming days will clarify whether the report holds true and how the market reacts. For now, those eyeing an iPhone might want to keep a close watch on Apple's official channels.
