Spider-Man: Brand New Day Shatters India Box Office Records on Opening Weekend

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Record-breaking $360M global opening weekend
  • Biggest opening weekend in India, surpassing Avengers: Endgame
  • Mixed to positive reviews from critics and audiences in Kolkata

MUMBAI: The web-slinger has spun a new record in India. Spider-Man: Brand New Day has swung to the biggest opening weekend in the country's box office history, eclipsing the previous benchmark set by Avengers: Endgame. The film's debut has sent ripples through the exhibition industry, with multiplex chains and single-screen theatres reporting packed houses across major metros.

A Historic Debut

According to early estimates, the film has collected a staggering $360 million globally in its opening weekend, making it the second-biggest worldwide launch ever. In India, the response has been nothing short of phenomenal, with the film rewriting records in Hindi, English, Tamil, and Telugu versions.

Trade analysts point to a combination of factors driving the frenzy: the return of beloved characters, a fresh narrative direction, and a marketing blitz that left no screen untouched. The previous record-holder, Avengers: Endgame, had held the top spot for years, but Brand New Day has now claimed the crown.

What's New in the Web-Slinger's World

The film introduces a host of new characters, new flaws, and new technology, setting up brain-exploding future consequences that have fans buzzing. Early reviews from international critics have hailed it as a bold reinvention, with The Hollywood Reporter calling it the 'Spidey Surprise' of the year.

In Kolkata, audiences have offered a mixed but largely positive verdict. Telegraph India's street interviews revealed a range of opinions, from 'best Spider-Man film ever' to cautious praise for its ambitious plot. The consensus, however, is that the film delivers on spectacle and emotional stakes.

Fan Reactions and Spoiler Debates

Social media is already flooded with spoiler discussions, as fans dissect the film's final act and its implications for the wider Marvel universe. The Guardian has even published a dedicated spoiler-filled analysis, urging viewers to watch before reading.

For Indian audiences, the local connect has been a major draw. Special screenings in IMAX and 4DX formats have seen ticket prices soar, yet demand remains unmatched. Exhibitors in Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru have added midnight shows to accommodate the rush.

Global Box Office Domination

Globally, the film's $360 million opening trails only Avengers: Endgame, which holds the all-time record. The performance has exceeded pre-release projections by a wide margin, catching even the most optimistic trade pundits off guard.

The film's success is a shot in the arm for the theatrical exhibition sector, which has been grappling with streaming competition. Industry watchers note that a release of this scale reaffirms the big-screen experience's enduring appeal, particularly in markets like India where cinema remains a cultural cornerstone.

What to Watch Next

With such a monumental start, all eyes are now on the film's second-weekend hold and its long-term run in India. The real test will be whether it can sustain momentum through the coming weeks, especially with new releases slated for the festive season.

For now, Spider-Man: Brand New Day has firmly established itself as a box office juggernaut, and the industry is watching closely to see if it can spin an even bigger web.
