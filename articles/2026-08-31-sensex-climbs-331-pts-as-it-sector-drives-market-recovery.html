Sensex climbs 331 pts as IT sector drives market recovery

📅 2026-08-31 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex gains 331 points, closing at 78,456
  • IT sector leads market recovery with 10% gains
  • Nifty 50 rises 68 points, closing at 22,123
  • Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) buy shares worth ₹1,200 crore

Stock markets recover as IT sector drives rebound

Indian stock markets saw a significant rebound on Friday, ending a two-day losing streak. The Sensex, India's benchmark index, climbed 331 points to close at 78,456, marking a 0.4% increase. The Nifty 50, another key index, gained 68 points, closing at 22,123, a 0.3% rise. This recovery was driven by strong buying in the IT sector, which saw a 10% increase in its index.

Key drivers of the market recovery

The IT sector's performance was the primary driver of the market recovery. Several IT companies reported strong quarterly results, boosted by increased demand for their services. Companies like TCS, Infosys, and Wipro saw significant gains, with their stocks rising between 5% and 10%. This performance was driven by a surge in demand for digital transformation services, particularly in the healthcare and retail sectors.

Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) play a crucial role

Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) also played a crucial role in the market recovery. FIIs bought shares worth ₹1,200 crore, injecting fresh capital into the market. This buying spree was driven by the positive outlook for the IT sector, which is expected to continue growing in the coming quarters. The increased demand for IT services, particularly in the cloud computing and AI sectors, is expected to drive further growth in the IT sector.

Broader market impact and expert analysis

The market recovery is a welcome relief for investors who had been on edge following the two-day slump. Analysts attribute the recovery to a combination of strong corporate earnings and positive FII inflows. However, they caution that the market remains volatile and is subject to sudden swings. The IT sector's performance is expected to continue driving the market in the coming weeks, but investors should remain vigilant and diversify their portfolios to mitigate risks.
