Victory Cinema in Bengaluru refuses to screen 'Jana Nayagan' amidst distribution row

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Victory Cinema in Bengaluru has declined to screen the movie 'Jana Nayagan'.
  • The decision stems from a distribution dispute between the theatre and the film's distributors.
  • The film's release in other theatres across the city remains unaffected for now.

Bengaluru's iconic Victory Cinema has decided not to screen the upcoming film Jana Nayagan, citing an ongoing dispute with distributors. The theatre management confirmed the move on Friday, stating that commercial terms could not be finalised.

This decision comes just days before the film's scheduled release. Victory Cinema, a single-screen landmark in the city, has a history of hosting major Kannada and other language releases. The current standoff has left moviegoers in the area surprised.

Theatre management cites unresolved terms

Sources close to the theatre said the disagreement revolves around revenue sharing and screening conditions. Victory Cinema's management has not publicly detailed the exact points of contention, but indicated they were not agreeable to the terms proposed by the distributors.

The management stated in a brief note that they value their audience but could not proceed under the current arrangement. They did not specify whether the dispute was with the film's production house or a local distribution agent.

Film's release elsewhere remains on track

Despite the setback at Victory, Jana Nayagan is still set to open in multiple other theatres across Bengaluru and Karnataka. Distributors have not yet commented on the Victory Cinema situation, nor have they indicated any broader impact on the film's release plan.

Industry observers note that such disputes are not uncommon in the film trade, especially for highly anticipated releases. Victory Cinema has previously been involved in similar standoffs, though most were resolved before opening day.

Audience reaction and alternatives

Regular patrons of Victory Cinema expressed disappointment on social media. Many had hoped to watch the film at the historic venue. The theatre has not announced any alternative programming for the dates Jana Nayagan was expected to run.

Other single-screen theatres and multiplex chains in Bengaluru are expected to screen the film as planned. Bookings for those shows remain open. Officials at Victory said they would update the public if the situation changes.

What happens next depends on whether the two sides return to the negotiating table. With the release date approaching, both the theatre and the distributors face pressure to resolve the deadlock or risk losing business.
