Sensex, Nifty Likely to Extend Rally Next Week; RBI Policy, Q1 Results in Focus

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • RBI monetary policy decision due next week will be a major trigger.
  • Q1 earnings from major companies will continue to influence market sentiment.
  • Global cues, including US Fed signals and crude oil prices, will also be watched.
  • Analysts expect the rally to continue if key support levels hold.

Market Rally Likely to Continue

Indian equity benchmarks, the Sensex and the Nifty, are expected to extend their upward journey in the week starting August 3. The market has been on a firm footing, and the momentum could persist, driven by a host of domestic and global factors. Investors will closely track the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) monetary policy decision, the ongoing Q1 earnings season, and global cues.

After a strong performance in the previous week, the indices are hovering near their record highs. The undertone remains bullish, with buying interest visible across most sectors. However, volatility cannot be ruled out, especially ahead of the RBI policy announcement.

RBI Policy: The Key Event

The RBI's bi-monthly monetary policy review is scheduled for next week, and it will be the biggest trigger for the market. While the central bank is widely expected to keep interest rates unchanged, the commentary on inflation and growth will be scrutinised. Any hawkish surprise could dampen sentiment, while a dovish stance may fuel the rally further.

Market participants will also watch for any signals on liquidity management and forward guidance. The central bank's approach to managing inflation while supporting growth remains a delicate balancing act, and its words will be parsed carefully by traders.

Q1 Earnings and Global Cues

The earnings season continues to be a major driver, with a slew of companies set to report their June quarter numbers. IT, banking, and auto stocks will be in focus as their results will provide insights into demand trends and margin pressures. Strong numbers could lift sectoral indices, while disappointments may lead to profit booking.

On the global front, investors will monitor developments in the US-China trade talks, movements in crude oil prices, and any new data points from the US economy. A firm trend in global markets, especially in the US and Asia, would provide additional support to domestic equities.

Technical Outlook

From a technical perspective, the Nifty has formed a strong base near the 24,800 level. Analysts believe that as long as the index holds above this support, the bullish momentum is likely to continue. On the upside, the next resistance is seen around the 25,200-25,300 zone.

The broader market is also showing resilience, with midcap and smallcap stocks participating in the rally. However, valuations remain a concern for some, and selective buying is advised.

What Investors Should Watch

Apart from the RBI policy and earnings, the movement of the rupee and foreign institutional investor (FII) flows will also be important. Sustained FII buying has been a key pillar of the recent rally, and any reversal could trigger a correction.

In the coming week, market participants will also react to any fresh macroeconomic data releases. The overall mood is cautious optimism, but traders should remain alert to global shocks or unexpected policy commentary.

Going ahead, the direction of the market will largely depend on the RBI's stance and the quality of earnings. A dovish policy and robust results could push indices to new highs, while any negative surprise may lead to a near-term pullback.
