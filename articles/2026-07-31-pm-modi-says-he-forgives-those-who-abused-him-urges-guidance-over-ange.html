PM Modi says he forgives those who abused him, urges guidance over anger

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • PM Modi says he forgives those who abused him and his mother.
  • He urged people to guide the misguided, saying abuses solve nothing.
  • Congress reacts with a dig, claiming Modi has forgiven the country after being asked to apologise.

PM Modi's message of forgiveness

Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Friday said he wants to forgive those who abused him, responding to recent incidents of students using foul language against him at Jantar Mantar. Speaking at an event, Modi said, "I want to forgive those who abused me," and added that the abuse directed at him and his mother does not deter him from his duties.

The Prime Minister's remarks come amid a controversy over a video showing some students allegedly abusing him. Modi said that instead of responding with anger, the focus should be on guiding the misguided youth. "Our job is to guide them," he said, emphasising that abuses never solve any problem.

A call to guide the misguided

Modi urged citizens to take a constructive approach towards those who resort to such language. He said that the youth involved may be unaware of the consequences of their actions, and it is the responsibility of society to bring them back on the right path. The Prime Minister's appeal was seen as an attempt to de-escalate tensions after the incident drew sharp reactions from his supporters.

The remarks also carried a personal tone, as Modi referred to the abuse directed at his mother. He said that despite the personal attacks, his commitment to the nation remains unchanged. "I forgive them," he repeated, stressing that holding grudges would not serve the country's interests.

Political reactions

The Congress party was quick to respond, taking a swipe at the Prime Minister. In a post on social media, the party said, "The country is asking him to apologise, but he has forgiven the country." The comment appeared to reference ongoing demands for an apology from the Prime Minister over various issues, including the Jantar Mantar incident.

Political analysts noted that the exchange reflects the charged atmosphere ahead of upcoming elections. The BJP, however, has rallied behind Modi, with party leaders praising his magnanimity. Several supporters have lauded the Prime Minister for turning the other cheek, framing it as a sign of strength rather than weakness.

Context of the Jantar Mantar incident

The controversy began when a video surfaced showing students at Jantar Mantar in Delhi using abusive language against the Prime Minister. The clip went viral, triggering widespread condemnation from BJP leaders and supporters. Police have been looking into the matter, though no formal arrests have been reported yet.

The incident has also sparked a debate on freedom of expression and the limits of political dissent. While some have defended the students' right to protest, others have called for action against those involved. The Prime Minister's latest remarks are seen as an attempt to cool down the discourse.

Looking ahead

As the political slugfest continues, all eyes will be on how the administration handles the investigation into the video. The Prime Minister's message of forgiveness may set the tone for his party's campaign rhetoric in the coming weeks, with leaders likely to highlight his statesmanlike approach. Whether the Opposition will shift its strategy remains to be seen.
