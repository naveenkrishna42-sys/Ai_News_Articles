FIFA bans Paredes for 10 matches over World Cup final scuffle

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • FIFA imposes a 10-match ban on Leandro Paredes for his part in the World Cup final scuffle.
  • Argentina faces additional penalties, including a fine, for the brawl and the Falkland Islands banner incident.
  • Paredes' ban is the most severe punishment handed out in the aftermath of the World Cup final.

FIFA imposes 10-match ban on Leandro Paredes

FIFA has announced a 10-match ban for Argentina's Leandro Paredes, following his involvement in the scuffle during the World Cup final. The ban is the most severe punishment handed out in the aftermath of the match, which ended in controversy.

Argentina faces additional penalties

Alongside Paredes' ban, Argentina has been fined by FIFA for the brawl and for the Falkland Islands banner incident. The team's officials have been ordered to attend a hearing, with further sanctions possible.

Paredes' role in the brawl

Paredes was involved in the post-match scuffle, which saw both teams engage in a physical altercation. Despite the severity of his involvement, Paredes has avoided the most severe sanctions, which could have included a longer ban or even a lifetime ban.

FIFA's response to the incident

FIFA's disciplinary committee has stated that the incident was a serious breach of the sport's code of conduct. The committee has also expressed concern over the lack of respect shown by both teams and their supporters.

What happens next?

Argentina's football authorities are expected to appeal the decision, arguing that the ban is too severe and that Paredes' involvement was not as significant as it has been portrayed. Meanwhile, FIFA is likely to monitor the situation closely, with further action possible if the incident resurfaces.
