London Spirit Register First Win in Women's Hundred With Thrilling Victory Over Trent Rockets

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • London Spirit chased down 127 with two balls to spare.
  • Deandra Dottin scored an unbeaten 37 and took two wickets.
  • The result lifted Spirit from the bottom of the points table.

London Spirit finally have points on the board. In a tense encounter at Trent Bridge, the Spirit chased down 127 with two balls to spare, beating Trent Rockets by six wickets in the Women's Hundred.

The win, their first in three matches, came after back-to-back losses to start the campaign. For the Rockets, it was a second defeat in three games.

Dottin Leads the Charge With Bat and Ball

Deandra Dottin was the difference. The West Indian all-rounder first picked up two wickets with her medium pace, finishing with figures of 2 for 17 from 15 balls. She then anchored the chase with an unbeaten 37 off 26 deliveries.

Dottin struck two fours and two sixes, holding the innings together as wickets fell around her. She found able support from skipper Heather Knight, who made a composed 28 off 23 balls.

The pair added 48 runs for the fourth wicket, taking the game deep. With 15 needed off the final 10 balls, Dottin hit a six and a four off the last two deliveries of Alana King's set to seal the win.

Rockets Innings Built on Sciver-Brunt

Earlier, Trent Rockets posted 126 for 5 from their 100 balls. Nat Sciver-Brunt top-scored with 40 off 35, striking four boundaries. She shared a 51-run stand with captain Meg Lanning, who made 28 off 22.

London Spirit's bowlers kept the scoring in check through the middle overs. Dottin's double strike removed Lanning and then Rachel Priest in quick succession, slowing the Rockets' momentum. Sarah Glenn also bowled a tight spell, conceding just 18 runs from her 20 balls.

Despite a late flurry from Alana King, who hit an unbeaten 18 off 10, the Rockets fell short of a daunting total. The Spirit's chase never looked entirely comfortable, but they got over the line when it mattered.

Points Table Shuffle

The result lifted London Spirit to fifth on the eight-team table, with two points from three matches. Trent Rockets slipped to fourth, level on four points with the top three but with a lower net run rate.

Birmingham Phoenix and Manchester Super Giants, who play later on Sunday, have the chance to leapfrog both sides. The competition remains tight, with only one team — Northern Superchargers — yet to register a win.

Spirit will next face Oval Invincibles at Lord's. Rockets travel to Headingley to take on Northern Superchargers. Both matches are scheduled for later this week.
