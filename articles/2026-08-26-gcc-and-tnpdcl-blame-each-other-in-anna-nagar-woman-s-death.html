TNPDCL and GCC blame each other in Anna Nagar woman's death

Chennai's infrastructure agencies, the Tamil Nadu Power Distribution Corporation Limited (TNPDCL) and the Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC), are locked in a blame game over a woman's death in Anna Nagar. The incident has raised serious questions about accountability and the safety of Chennai's infrastructure.

TNPDCL claims GCC's illegal construction caused the incident

TNPDCL has accused GCC of illegal construction in the area, which they claim caused the incident. They argue that the construction work was not properly supervised and safety measures were not in place. The corporation has demanded GCC to take responsibility for the incident.

GCC alleges TNPDCL's negligence in maintaining infrastructure

On the other hand, GCC has alleged TNPDCL's negligence in maintaining the infrastructure. They claim that the corporation has failed to address the issues in the area, leading to the incident. GCC has demanded TNPDCL to take responsibility for the incident.
