Apple raises subscription prices ahead of iPhone 18 Pro launch

📅 2026-08-29 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple TV and Apple One prices increased in US
  • Hike comes ahead of September 9 iPhone launch event
  • Apple's move signals potential inflation in streaming services
  • Apple One now costs $16.95/month for individual plan
  • Apple TV+ now costs $6.99/month for standard plan

Apple's Latest Price Hike Signals Streaming Service Inflation

Apple has announced price hikes for its Apple TV and Apple One subscriptions, effective immediately. The move comes ahead of the September 9 iPhone launch event, where the tech giant is expected to unveil the iPhone 18 Pro. This price increase signals a trend of inflation in the streaming service industry, which is likely to impact consumers and competitors alike.

New Pricing Structure for Apple TV and Apple One

Apple TV, the streaming service that includes Apple TV+, Apple Music, and iCloud+, has seen its price increase to $6.99 per month for the standard plan. The premium plan, which offers ad-free music and higher-quality video, now costs $14.95 per month. Apple One, Apple's all-in-one subscription service, has also seen a price hike. The individual plan, which includes Apple Music, Apple TV+, Apple Arcade, and iCloud+, now costs $16.95 per month. The family plan, which covers up to six people, now costs $24.95 per month.

Why Apple's Price Hike Matters

The price hike by Apple is not an isolated incident. It is part of a broader trend of inflation in the streaming service industry. According to a report by the GSMA, the global streaming services market is expected to grow at a CAGR of 12.3% from 2023 to 2030. This growth is driven by the increasing demand for digital content and the rise of OTT platforms. However, the increasing competition and the need to invest in content and technology are also contributing factors.

Impact on Consumers and Competitors

The price hike by Apple is likely to impact consumers, who may have to spend more to access the same services. It is also likely to impact competitors, who may need to adjust their pricing strategies to remain competitive. For instance, Netflix and Amazon Prime Video have also seen a rise in subscription prices in recent years. This trend is likely to continue as the streaming service industry matures and competition intensifies.

Apple's Strategy and Future Prospects

Apple's price hike is part of its broader strategy to increase revenue and invest in its services. The tech giant has been aggressively expanding its services portfolio, and the price hike is a reflection of its confidence in the market. However, it is also a reminder of the challenges that the streaming service industry faces, including the need to balance between profitability and consumer affordability.
