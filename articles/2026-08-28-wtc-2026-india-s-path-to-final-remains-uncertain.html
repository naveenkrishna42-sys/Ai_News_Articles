India's WTC 2026 hopes hang in the balance

The World Test Championship 2026 points table has shifted significantly after India's second Test against Sri Lanka ended in a draw. The result leaves India's path to the final uncertain, with their points table position slipping and their rivals closing in.

Points table analysis

The draw means India have picked up just 12 points from their two Tests, leaving them in third place. Behind them are England, who have 24 points from two Tests, and Australia, who have 20 points from three Tests.

Sri Lanka's performance raises questions

Sri Lanka's draw with India has been a surprise, with their performance raising questions about their consistency. Despite their strong batting lineup, Sri Lanka have struggled to take wickets, which has cost them dearly in Tests.

Sonal Dinusha, Sri Lanka's methodical gem, has been a standout performer. However, the team's inability to take wickets in pressure situations has been a major concern.

Follow-on strategy debated

The decision to follow-on in the second Test has been debated extensively. Shubman Gill, India's captain, has defended the decision, arguing that it was the right call given the conditions and Sri Lanka's batting lineup.

However, some experts have questioned the decision, arguing that it put India under unnecessary pressure. The debate highlights the complexity of modern Test cricket, where every decision can have a significant impact on the outcome of the match.

India's path to final becomes even tougher

The draw means India's path to the WTC final has become even tougher. With England and Australia both ahead of them in the points table, India will need to win their remaining Tests to have any chance of reaching the final.

However, with Sri Lanka's performance raising questions about their consistency, India's task is made even more difficult. The team will need to address their own issues, particularly their inability to take wickets in pressure situations, if they are to have any chance of reaching the final.
