Bitcoin Surges Past $65,000 Again; Here’s What Drove the Rally

📅 2026-07-16 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin crossed $65,000 for the first time in weeks, driven by a mix of institutional accumulation and positive regulatory signals.
  • The rally was supported by a weaker US dollar and expectations of a more favourable crypto policy environment.
  • Analysts caution that resistance near $68,000 could trigger profit-taking, though momentum remains strong.

Bitcoin has clawed its way back above the $65,000 mark, a level it had not held since late June. The world's largest cryptocurrency touched an intraday high of $65,420 on Wednesday, marking a recovery of nearly 12% from its recent lows near $58,000.

What Pushed Bitcoin Back Above $65,000

The latest leg up appears to have multiple drivers. Market participants pointed to a fresh wave of institutional buying, with several large asset managers adding to their crypto holdings. Data from CoinShares showed digital asset investment products recorded inflows of over $200 million in the past week, the largest such figure in a month.

A softer US dollar also played a role. The dollar index slipped after weaker-than-expected retail sales data, making dollar-denominated assets like Bitcoin relatively more attractive. Meanwhile, comments from a Federal Reserve official hinting at a possible pause in rate hikes further boosted risk appetite across markets.

Regulatory Clouds Lift Slightly

On the regulatory front, there were signs that the worst of the crackdown might be behind the market. A US court ruling earlier this week dismissed a lawsuit against a major exchange, which traders interpreted as a positive signal for the broader crypto industry. In India, the government's decision to defer a contentious taxation proposal for virtual digital assets also helped sentiment.

However, experts cautioned that the regulatory picture remains mixed. The Securities and Exchange Board of India has yet to clarify its stance on crypto trading platforms, leaving some uncertainty for domestic investors.

Technical Factors at Play

From a chart perspective, Bitcoin's move above $65,000 broke through a key resistance level that had capped price action for nearly three weeks. Traders noted that a short squeeze — where traders who had bet against Bitcoin were forced to cover their positions — amplified the rally. Open interest in Bitcoin futures rose sharply, indicating fresh long positions entering the market.

But the climb was not without volatility. The asset briefly dipped to $64,200 before recovering, a sign that selling pressure remains near psychological round numbers.

What to Watch Now

For now, the market is watching whether Bitcoin can hold above $65,000 and mount a challenge of the next resistance zone near $68,000. A failure to sustain these levels could see the price retreat toward the $62,000 support area. Trading volumes will be key — if they taper off, the rally may lose steam.

The broader crypto market has taken its cue from Bitcoin. Ethereum rose past $3,500, while smaller tokens also posted gains. The total cryptocurrency market capitalisation climbed back above $2.5 trillion.

Investors are now awaiting the release of US inflation data next week, which could either reinforce the current risk-on mood or trigger another round of selling. For the moment, Bitcoin has reclaimed its perch above $65,000 — but the road ahead is unlikely to be a straight line.
