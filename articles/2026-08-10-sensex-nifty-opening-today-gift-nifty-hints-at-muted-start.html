Sensex, Nifty Opening Today: GIFT Nifty Hints at Muted Start

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • GIFT Nifty trends suggest a soft opening for Indian equities.
  • Global markets remain cautious ahead of key economic data.
  • Investors eye domestic inflation numbers due later this week.

Mumbai: Indian equity benchmarks are expected to open on a cautious note on Monday, with GIFT Nifty futures pointing to a muted start for the Sensex and the Nifty. The SGX Nifty, now traded as GIFT Nifty on the International Exchange in Gujarat, was hovering around the flat line in early morning deals, indicating that the domestic market may open without any strong directional bias.

Market participants are treading carefully as global cues remain mixed. While US indices closed marginally higher in the previous session, Asian markets were trading with modest gains on Monday morning. However, concerns over interest rate trajectories and slowing global growth continue to weigh on investor sentiment.

Global Cues and Their Impact

Overseas signals have been largely responsible for the cautious tone ahead of the opening bell. The US Federal Reserve's stance on rates remains a key overhang, with investors parsing every piece of economic data for clues on the central bank's next move. Meanwhile, a rebound in oil prices has added to inflationary worries, which could influence the Reserve Bank of India's policy thinking in the coming months.

Back home, foreign institutional investors have been net sellers in recent sessions, adding pressure on the market. Domestic institutional investors, however, have provided some support by buying on dips. The tug-of-war between these two groups has kept the market rangebound over the past few days.

Technical Levels to Watch

For the Nifty, immediate support is seen around the 24,800 level, while resistance is placed near 25,100. A breakout on either side could set the tone for the week. The Sensex, on the other hand, has been hovering close to the 81,500 mark, with traders keeping a close eye on banking and IT stocks for direction.

Banking stocks, which have a heavy weightage in the indices, are expected to remain in focus. Any positive news on the credit growth front or a dip in bond yields could lift the sector. IT stocks, meanwhile, are likely to react to movements in the rupee and global tech sentiment.

Key Events This Week

The week ahead is packed with important domestic and global data points. On the home front, retail inflation data for July is due later this week, and any upside surprise could renew fears of a prolonged rate pause. On the global side, US inflation numbers and remarks from Federal Reserve officials will be closely tracked.

Quarterly earnings season is also drawing to a close, with a few mid-cap companies still slated to report. Investors will be scanning these results for any signs of demand weakness or margin pressures that could impact valuations.

What Should Investors Do?

Market experts suggest that traders should avoid taking aggressive positions until the direction becomes clearer. Given the lack of fresh triggers, volatility is likely to remain elevated. For long-term investors, any sharp dip could present a buying opportunity, but stock selection remains key.

Analysts also advise keeping a close watch on the rupee's movement against the dollar. A weaker rupee could hurt foreign flows and put pressure on the market. Conversely, stability in the currency could help attract overseas money back into Indian equities.

As the trading day unfolds, the initial hour will be crucial in determining the market's trajectory. If the indices manage to hold above key support levels, a recovery could be on the cards. However, a break below those levels could trigger further selling.

Going ahead, the focus will shift to the inflation data and global central bank commentary. The market's reaction to these events will likely dictate the trend for the rest of the week.
