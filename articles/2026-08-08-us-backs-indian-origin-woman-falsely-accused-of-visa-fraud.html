US Backs Indian-Origin Woman Falsely Accused of Visa Fraud

📅 2026-08-08 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sadhana Gollapudi, an Indian-origin engineer and US citizen, was falsely accused of visa fraud after a dancing video went viral.
  • US authorities have clarified her naturalised citizen status, countering online misinformation.
  • The episode underscores growing anti-immigrant sentiment and the spread of unverified claims on social media.

Viral Video Sparks Baseless Allegations

A seemingly innocuous dancing video has thrust an Indian-origin engineer in the United States into the centre of a social media firestorm. Sadhana Gollapudi, a Telugu woman living in the US, found herself falsely accused of visa fraud after the clip circulated widely online. The allegations, spread by anti-immigration vigilantes, quickly gained traction, prompting a strong rebuttal from her attorney and, notably, from US authorities.

Gollapudi, who has been described as a naturalised citizen, became the target of a coordinated online campaign. Her legal representative stepped in to clarify her status, stating unequivocally that she is a US citizen. The accusation of visa fraud, which often carries severe legal consequences, has been dismissed as baseless by those familiar with her case.

US Authorities Step In

The response from Washington has been clear. Officials have backed Gollapudi, confirming her status as a naturalised citizen and implicitly rejecting the online claims. This intervention marks a rare instance where the US government has publicly addressed misinformation targeting an individual immigrant, even one who has already acquired citizenship.

The case has drawn attention to the growing phenomenon of social media users acting as self-appointed visa police. In recent months, several Indian-origin professionals in the US have faced similar online scrutiny, often based on flimsy or fabricated evidence. This incident, however, stands out because of the explicit support from US authorities, which has helped counter the narrative pushed by online detractors.

Context of Anti-Immigrant Sentiment

The episode unfolds against a backdrop of heightened anti-immigrant rhetoric in parts of the US political discourse. While legal immigration and visa processes remain tightly regulated, vigilante online groups have taken it upon themselves to police these rules, frequently targeting individuals from South Asian communities. Gollapudi's case is a stark example of how a personal video can be weaponised to spread falsehoods about someone's legal standing.

Her attorney has been vocal in defending her, emphasising that the allegations are not only false but also damaging to her reputation and sense of security. The lawyer's statement, widely reported by Indian media outlets including The Times of India and Deccan Chronicle, has helped clarify the facts, yet the original misinformation continues to circulate in some corners of the internet.

Broader Implications for Social Media Accountability

This incident raises uncomfortable questions about the power of social media to shape public perception and even influence legal outcomes. For Gollapudi, the ordeal has been a personal nightmare, though she has largely stayed out of the public eye since the controversy erupted. Her case has become a talking point in discussions about the safety and rights of immigrants in the US, particularly those from India who constitute a significant portion of the skilled workforce.

Media coverage, including reports from NDTV and Firstpost, has highlighted the absurdity of the situation, noting that a simple dance video was twisted into a false narrative of criminality. The speed at which the allegations spread underscores the challenges of combating misinformation in an era where viral content often outpaces factual corrections.

What Happens Next

As the dust settles, Gollapudi's legal team has indicated that no further action is planned against the online accusers, although they reserve the right to revisit the matter. The case serves as a cautionary tale for both immigrants and social media users. For now, US authorities have made their position clear, but the broader issue of online vigilantism remains unresolved. Observers will be watching whether this incident prompts platforms to take stronger action against such targeted harassment, or if it fades into the growing list of unpunished digital witch hunts.
