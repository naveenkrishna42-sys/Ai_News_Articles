AB de Villiers Faces Backlash Over Kohli-Tendulkar Comparison

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • AB de Villiers claimed Virat Kohli outperformed Sachin Tendulkar in overseas Test matches.
  • The statement triggered immediate backlash from fans and former cricketers.
  • Critics mocked the South African legend for his assessment of the two Indian batting icons.

A Bold Assessment

Former South African captain AB de Villiers has found himself at the center of a heated debate following his recent comments comparing Indian batting legends Sachin Tendulkar and Virat Kohli. During a discussion on his social media platforms, de Villiers stated that Kohli possessed a superior record in overseas Test conditions compared to his predecessor.

De Villiers, who shared a long tenure with Kohli at the Royal Challengers Bengaluru, argued that Kohli effectively raised the bar for Indian batters when playing abroad. He suggested that Tendulkar, while a titan of the game, did not exhibit the same level of dominance in foreign conditions as the modern-day stalwart.

Public Reaction and Social Media Outcry

The remarks were met with swift and sharp criticism from the cricketing fraternity and fans alike. Many supporters of Tendulkar pointed to the era-specific challenges faced by the 'Little Master,' noting the quality of bowling attacks he encountered throughout his 24-year career.

Social media users were particularly unforgiving, with some mocking the former Proteas star for his perspective. One former Indian cricketer even quipped that de Villiers might be under the influence of substances, using the phrase "pass on the weed" to dismiss the validity of the comparison.

Comparing Eras and Conditions

The debate highlights the difficulty of comparing players across different generations. Tendulkar played the majority of his career during a time when defensive technique was the primary currency, facing legendary fast bowlers on pitches that often lacked the protection of modern regulations.

Conversely, Kohli has thrived in an era where aggressive batting and high-strike rates are prioritized. While Kohli’s achievements in SENA countries—South Africa, England, New Zealand, and Australia—are statistically significant, analysts remain divided on whether these numbers can be directly measured against Tendulkar’s longevity and consistency.

The Statistical Divide

De Villiers maintained that Kohli’s intensity and ability to adapt to bouncy or seaming conditions set him apart. He noted that the current Indian star’s approach to pressure was a defining factor in his success away from home. Despite these observations, the consensus among traditionalists remains that Tendulkar’s 100 international centuries and overall impact on the sport place him in a unique category.

The backlash underscores the deep-seated reverence for Tendulkar among Indian cricket fans. While de Villiers’ admiration for his former teammate is well-documented, his attempt to quantify their relative greatness has clearly struck a nerve with those who view the comparison as fundamentally flawed.

Cricket experts are expected to continue dissecting the technical merits of the two batters in upcoming panel discussions. Public discourse on the matter shows no signs of cooling down as fans continue to defend the legacies of both icons.
