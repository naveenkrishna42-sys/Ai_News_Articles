Sunrisers Leeds thrash MI London in Women's Hundred 2026 opener

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sunrisers Leeds won by 8 wickets against MI London.
  • The 2026 season features new team names and owners after significant investment.
  • JioStar has secured exclusive India broadcasting rights for the tournament.

Dominant start for Sunrisers Leeds

Sunrisers Leeds announced their arrival in the new-look Women's Hundred with a commanding eight-wicket victory over MI London in the tournament opener on Tuesday. Chasing a modest target, the Leeds batters made short work of the chase, reaching the mark with more than 20 balls to spare.

The win marks a fresh chapter for the franchise, which now operates under new ownership and a rebranded identity. The 2026 season is the first under a significantly revamped structure, with private investors taking control of all eight teams.

New era, new names

The Women's Hundred has undergone a major transformation this year. Each team now carries a city-based name and a private owner. MI London, for instance, is backed by the Mumbai Indians group, while Sunrisers Leeds shares its parent company with the Sunrisers Hyderabad IPL franchise.

Budgets have risen sharply. Reports suggest each team now has a significantly larger salary cap, allowing franchises to retain and attract top international talent. The league has moved away from its original eight-team, city-less model to a more traditional franchise setup.

Indian stars in focus

The tournament features several prominent Indian cricketers. Smriti Mandhana, Jemimah Rodrigues, Deepti Sharma and Richa Ghosh are among those participating. Their presence adds to the growing global appeal of the competition, which now attracts a wider television audience in India.

JioStar, the joint venture between Reliance Industries and Disney, has acquired exclusive broadcasting rights for the Indian market. The deal underscores the increasing commercial value of women's franchise cricket in the subcontinent.

What the win means

For Sunrisers Leeds, the opening victory sets a positive tone. The team will look to build momentum as the league stage progresses. MI London, meanwhile, will need to regroup quickly after a below-par performance with both bat and ball.

The 2026 season runs through late July and August, with matches scheduled across major English cricket grounds. The final is set for late August at Lord's.

The coming weeks will show whether the new financial muscle translates into a higher quality of cricket. Early signs suggest the gap between teams may have narrowed, but Sunrisers Leeds have sent an early message to the rest of the league.
