Bitcoin Holds Above $64,000 as Market Eyes Fed Rate Decision

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin rose above $64,000 ahead of the Federal Reserve's rate decision.
  • The cryptocurrency shrugged off weakness in AI-led tech stocks and ETF outflows.
  • Market uncertainty persists due to Fed policy and crypto bill delays.
  • Analysts suggest a bottom may be forming despite recent sell-offs.

Bitcoin Climbs Past $64,000

Bitcoin rose above the $64,000 mark on Wednesday, trading steadily as investors shifted focus to the US Federal Reserve's upcoming rate decision. The cryptocurrency's resilience came despite a broader sell-off in AI-led tech stocks and ongoing concerns over exchange-traded fund (ETF) outflows.

At the time of reporting, Bitcoin hovered near $64,200, recovering from earlier dips that had pushed it close to $63,000. The move higher reflects cautious optimism in the crypto market, even as traditional equities faced pressure.

Fed Decision in Focus

The Federal Reserve's interest rate decision, due later today, remains the key event for global markets. Traders are pricing in a potential rate cut, which could boost risk assets like Bitcoin. However, uncertainty about the pace of easing has kept many investors on edge.

Bitcoin's recent price action suggests it is decoupling from tech stocks, which have been dragged down by weakness in AI-related shares. This divergence has fueled speculation that crypto may be finding its own footing amid macroeconomic headwinds.

ETF Outflows and Liquidations

Despite the uptick, data shows significant headwinds. Outflows from spot Bitcoin ETFs have accelerated, with investors pulling capital in recent sessions. Additionally, over $510 million in crypto long positions were liquidated in the past 24 hours, according to CoinGlass data, adding to market jitters.

The sell-offs have been exacerbated by delays in US crypto legislation, which have dampened sentiment. The proposed digital asset bill, seen as a potential catalyst for institutional adoption, remains stalled in Congress.

Analysts See Bottom Forming

Some analysts argue the worst may be over. Reports from Hindustan Times and FXEmpire indicate that Bitcoin is struggling against gold, which has outperformed the cryptocurrency as a haven asset. However, technical indicators suggest a bottom could be forming near $63,000.

“Bitcoin is showing signs of accumulation at these levels,” one market observer noted. “The Fed decision could be the trigger for a breakout or a deeper correction.”

Gold vs. Bitcoin

The comparison with gold remains a key theme. While Bitcoin has failed to match gold's rally, its ability to hold above $64,000 amid tech weakness is seen as a positive sign. Gold prices have surged to record highs this month, reinforcing its status as a safe haven.

For Bitcoin to reclaim its narrative as digital gold, it would need to break above resistance levels near $66,000 and sustain momentum. The Fed's tone on inflation and economic growth will be critical in shaping that path.

What to watch: The Federal Reserve's rate decision and accompanying press conference later today. A dovish outcome could propel Bitcoin toward $66,000, while hawkish remarks might trigger a retest of $63,000 support.
