Rathee's Viral Comment After Release

After being released from Delhi detention, YouTuber Dhruv Rathee made headlines with his remark: 'My score 1, PM score 0,' referring to Prime Minister Narendra Modi's first press conference. The statement quickly trended across social media platforms, drawing reactions from supporters and critics alike.

Context of Detention and Protest

Rathee was detained on October 10 during a protest in Delhi. He was released the following day, describing the situation as 'emergency-like.' In videos posted after his release, he criticized the police action, saying, 'Don’t scare a person so much that all the fear vanishes.' His comments have added fuel to ongoing debates about press freedom and civil liberties in the country.

Political Reactions and Online Buzz

The YouTuber’s remarks have sparked a flurry of responses online. While some praised his boldness, others questioned the appropriateness of comparing himself to the Prime Minister. Rathee also clarified that he is not affiliated with the so-called 'Cockroach Janta Party,' distancing himself from any controversial groups.

Background and Public Profile

Dhruv Rathee is a well-known content creator with a significant following on YouTube. His videos often focus on politics, social issues, and policy analysis. With over several million subscribers, his opinions carry weight among younger demographics and urban audiences.

Previous Controversies

This is not the first time Rathee has found himself in the spotlight for his critical views. He has previously faced scrutiny for his content, including fact-checking claims made by public figures and engaging in debates on national issues. His detainment during the recent protest marks another chapter in his increasingly visible role in India’s digital discourse.

Implications for Digital Expression

Rathee’s case underscores the growing tension between digital expression and state authority in India. As social media continues to shape public opinion, incidents like these raise questions about the boundaries of dissent and the treatment of content creators who challenge authority.
