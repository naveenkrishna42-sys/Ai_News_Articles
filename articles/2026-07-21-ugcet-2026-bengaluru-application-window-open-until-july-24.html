UGCET 2026: Bengaluru Application Window Open Until July 24

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • UGCET application deadline in Bengaluru is July 24, 2026
  • Online portal open for undergraduate engineering course applications
  • No official confirmation on fee extension or late fee window

The Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) has kept the application window for the Undergraduate Common Entrance Test (UGCET) 2026 open in Bengaluru until July 24. Candidates seeking admission to engineering and other undergraduate courses across the state must submit their forms before the deadline.

Application Process and Eligibility

Applicants can register online through the official KEA website. The test is mandatory for admission to government and private engineering colleges in Karnataka. Candidates who have passed or are appearing for their 12th standard board exams with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as core subjects are eligible.

The application fee varies based on category. General category candidates pay Rs. 500, while SC, ST, and OBC applicants pay Rs. 250. The fee can be paid online through net banking, credit card, or debit card.

No Extension Announced Yet

KEA officials have not announced any extension beyond July 24. In previous years, the authority has sometimes provided a late fee window after the initial deadline, but no such details have been confirmed for 2026.

Candidates are advised to complete the application process well before the closing date to avoid last-minute technical glitches. The KEA website has faced heavy traffic in the past, causing delays during peak hours.

Documents Required

  • Scanned photograph and signature in prescribed format
  • 12th standard marks card or hall ticket
  • Category certificate (if applicable)
  • Aadhaar card or other government ID

Applicants must ensure all documents are clear and legible. Incorrect uploads can lead to rejection of the application.

Exam Schedule and Pattern

The UGCET 2026 exam is expected to be held in August or September, though KEA has not released the exact dates yet. The test consists of multiple-choice questions from Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Each subject carries 60 marks, totaling 180 marks.

There is no negative marking for wrong answers. The exam duration is three hours. Rank lists will be prepared based on the combined performance in the entrance test and the 12th standard board marks, as per the state's reservation policy.

What Happens Next

KEA will release the admit cards for registered candidates a few weeks after the application window closes. The authority is expected to publish the exam timetable by early August. Candidates should regularly check the official website for updates on hall tickets and exam dates.
