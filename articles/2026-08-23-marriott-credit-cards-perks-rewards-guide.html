Marriott Credit Cards: Perks & Rewards Guide

Marriott credit cards offer a range of benefits and perks for frequent travelers. These cards provide free nights at Marriott properties, elite status, and dining benefits. The annual fees vary, but the rewards and perks make them a worthwhile investment for those who travel frequently.

Full Features & Standout Perks

Marriott credit cards offer several standout perks. Cardholders can enjoy free nights at Marriott properties, which can be redeemed for stays at over 30,000 hotels worldwide. The cards also offer elite status, which provides access to exclusive lounges and priority check-in. Additionally, cardholders can enjoy dining benefits, such as free breakfast at participating Marriott hotels.

Joining/Annual Fees or Premium Structures

The annual fees for Marriott credit cards range from ₹2,500 to ₹10,000. The fees vary depending on the card type and the benefits offered. For example, the Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant® American Express® Card has an annual fee of ₹10,000, while the Marriott Bonvoy Boundless® World Mastercard® has an annual fee of ₹2,500.

Exact Reward/Cashback Rates Across Shopping Categories

Marriott credit cards offer cashback rewards on various shopping categories. The cashback rates vary depending on the card type. For example, the Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant® American Express® Card offers 10% cashback on flights booked through the Amex Travel portal, up to ₹50,000 per year. The card also offers 5% cashback on dining and 3% cashback on all other purchases.

Lounge Access or Tax-Saving Benefits

Marriott credit cards provide access to exclusive lounges at participating Marriott properties. Cardholders can enjoy complimentary lounge access, which includes food, beverages, and Wi-Fi. Additionally, Marriott credit cards are eligible for 80C tax savings under certain conditions. The tax savings can be claimed on the annual fees paid for the credit card.

Eligibility Criteria

Marriott credit cards are available to individuals who meet certain eligibility criteria. The criteria vary depending on the card type. For example, the Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant® American Express® Card is available to individuals with a good credit history and a minimum annual income of ₹5 lakh. The card also requires a minimum credit limit of ₹5 lakh.

How to Apply Online to Claim Special Welcome Rewards or Joining Cashback Bonuses

To apply for a Marriott credit card and claim special welcome rewards or joining cashback bonuses, follow these steps:

  • Visit the official website of the bank or credit card issuer.
  • Fill out the online application form with the required details.
  • Submit the application form and wait for approval.
  • Once approved, receive the credit card and activate it.
  • Use the credit card to make purchases and redeem the welcome rewards or joining cashback bonuses.
