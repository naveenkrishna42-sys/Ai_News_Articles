Suvendu Adhikari Accuses Abhishek Banerjee of Funding Chandranath Rath Murder

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Team 📅 2026-09-16 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Adhikari alleges a ₹2 crore payout by the Bhaipo gang to kill Chandranath Rath.
  • He asserts TMC MP Abhishek Banerjee sanctioned the payment, citing intercepted communications.
  • West Bengal Police continue investigation; no charges filed against the named individuals.
  • The accusation intensifies BJP‑TMC rivalry ahead of 2026 by‑polls in Nandigram.
  • Independent verification of the alleged financial transfer remains unavailable.

Background of the Chandranath Rath Case

Chandranath Rath, a community activist from Nandigram, was found dead in early 2024 under circumstances that prompted a homicide investigation. Rath had been involved in land‑rights campaigns and was known to have interacted with various local political groups. His death attracted media attention due to the alleged links between his activism and ongoing disputes over land acquisition in the district.

Allegations Made by Suvendu Adhikari

Claim of Payment to Contract Killers

During a press conference on 14 September 2026, BJP leader Suvendu Adhikari stated that a group referred to as the "Bhaipo gang" had transferred ₹2 crore to contract killers to carry out the murder of Chandranath Rath. He named Trinamool Congress MP Abhishek Banerjee as the individual who allegedly authorised the payment.

Nature of the Evidence Presented

Adhikari said his claim is based on intercepted communications and financial trails that investigators have reportedly examined. He did not release the alleged documents publicly, noting they are part of an ongoing police dossier. As of the date of reporting, no independent verification of the ₹2 crore transfer has been made available to the public or the judiciary.

Profile of the Alleged Bhaipo Gang

Police records show the Bhaipo gang has been linked to extortion and intimidation activities in East Midnapore over the last ten years. The group’s name appears in several FIRs related to land disputes and illegal mining, though no senior member has been convicted in a court of law. Analysts observe that such syndicates sometimes offer their services to political actors seeking to settle scores or influence outcomes.

Political and Legal Implications

Effect on West Bengal Politics

The accusation intensifies the already strained relationship between the BJP and the Trinamool Congress ahead of the 2026 by‑elections. Political commentators suggest that allegations of contract killing can sway voter sentiment, particularly in constituencies like Nandigram where Rath’s murder remains a salient issue for residents.

Status of Judicial Proceedings

As of the reporting date, the West Bengal Police have not filed any charges against Abhishek Banerjee, the Bhaipo gang, or any other individual in connection with Rath’s killing. The case remains under investigation, and any future legal action would require corroborating evidence that satisfies the burden of proof required under Indian criminal law.

Key Insights

  • The allegation relies on claimed financial and communication evidence that has not been independently verified.
  • If substantiated, the case would indicate a serious breach of electoral law and raise concerns about the use of contract violence in West Bengal politics.
  • The absence of publicly available proof leaves the claim contested and may affect the credibility of the accuser.
  • Political fallout could influence voter perceptions in the upcoming by‑polls, especially in districts affected by Rath’s activism.
  • Continued police scrutiny is essential to determine whether any illicit financial transactions linked to the murder actually occurred.
