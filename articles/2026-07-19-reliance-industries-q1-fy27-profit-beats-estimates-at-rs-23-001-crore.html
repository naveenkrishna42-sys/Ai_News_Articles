Reliance Industries Q1 FY27: Profit Beats Estimates at Rs 23,001 Crore on Strong Refining, Jio Growth

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • RIL Q1 net profit at Rs 23,001 crore, surpassing analyst estimates
  • Strong operating performance across refining, telecom (Jio), and retail segments
  • Revenue rose 25% year-on-year; EBITDA hit a record quarterly high

Reliance Industries Ltd (RIL) has kicked off the financial year 2026-27 on a solid footing, reporting a consolidated net profit of Rs 23,001 crore for the first quarter. The figure comfortably beat market estimates, driven by strong showings in its core refining business and continued momentum in telecom and retail.

The conglomerate's revenue for the quarter ending June 30 surged 25% year-on-year, while EBITDA touched an all-time quarterly record. Analysts had expected a more modest profit, but a sharp recovery in refining margins and aggressive subscriber additions by Reliance Jio pushed earnings higher.

Refining Margins Boost Core Earnings

RIL's petrochemicals and refining division, which forms the bulk of its revenue, reported improved margins as global crude prices stabilised. The company's Singapore-based complex refinery margins widened, allowing it to capture higher value from processing cheaper crude grades.

Operating income from the refining segment rose sharply compared to the same quarter last year, offsetting weakness in petrochemical spreads. The company has been investing in upgrading its refining capacity to produce more diesel and jet fuel, which fetched premium prices during the quarter.

Jio and Retail Keep Growth Engine Running

Reliance Jio continued its aggressive push into smaller cities and rural areas, adding millions of new subscribers. The telecom arm's average revenue per user (ARPU) held steady, even as the company rolled out more affordable data plans to capture price-sensitive customers.

Jio's network expansion and the launch of new digital services, including cloud and enterprise offerings, contributed to a healthy rise in segment revenue. The retail division also posted double-digit growth, driven by both grocery and fashion segments, as consumer spending remained resilient.

Market Reacts Positively to Earnings Surprise

The results, announced after market hours on Friday, sent RIL shares up in early trade on Monday. Investors cheered the better-than-expected profit and the record EBITDA, which underscored the conglomerate's ability to deliver across diverse businesses.

Analysts at several brokerages raised their price targets for RIL, citing strong execution and the potential for further margin improvement. The company's debt levels remained manageable, with cash flows from operations comfortably covering capital expenditure commitments.

RIL's chairman, Mukesh Ambani, highlighted the group's focus on growth and operational efficiency in a statement accompanying the results. He noted that the company is on track to meet its guided targets for the full year.

What to Watch in the Coming Quarters

Investors will now focus on RIL's plans for its new energy business, including solar and green hydrogen projects. The company has committed significant capital to these ventures, and any signs of progress could further boost sentiment.

Additionally, the trajectory of global oil prices and telecom tariff hikes will be key swing factors for RIL's earnings momentum in the remainder of FY27.
