Shoriful Islam matches Starc's six-for in Mackay Test

Bangladesh pacer Shoriful Islam has made history by taking six wickets on Day 1 of the Mackay Test, matching Australia's Mitchell Starc. Shoriful's six-for was achieved despite him playing through pain, which he attributed to a muscle injury. The Bangladesh team is now in a spot after the first day of the Test, with Australia leading the series 1-0.

Starc and Shoriful shine as pacers rule Day 1

Starc and Shoriful's performances on Day 1 of the Mackay Test have set the tone for the match. Starc took six wickets, including the crucial wicket of Bangladesh's captain, Shakib Al Hasan. Shoriful's six wickets came at the expense of 143 runs, with him bowling 25 overs. The duo's performances have put Bangladesh in a difficult position, with the team struggling to keep up with the Australian pace attack.
