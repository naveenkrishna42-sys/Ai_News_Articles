RSS-backed group protests Bhagwat's NYC event

📅 2026-08-29 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • 61 US-based organizations protest Bhagwat's NYC event
  • RSS's influence questioned by protesting groups
  • X account blocking controversy adds to tensions

Protest Erupts Over Bhagwat's US Event

Sixty-one US-based organizations have joined forces to protest Mohan Bhagwat's recent event in New York City. The groups, representing a diverse range of Hindu communities and advocacy organizations, have raised concerns over what they perceive as the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS)'s undue influence on Bhagwat's activities abroad.

RSS Influence Questioned

The protesting organizations argue that Bhagwat's event, while marketed as a gathering of Hindu leaders and scholars, is heavily influenced by the RSS's ideology and practices. They contend that this influence extends beyond India's borders, raising questions about the organization's global reach and impact.

X Account Blocking Controversy

The protesting groups have also cited the recent blocking of the event organizers' X account as a point of contention. The account, which had been a platform for promoting Bhagwat's event, was blocked for several hours, leading to widespread speculation and concern among the organizers and attendees. The sudden blocking and subsequent restoration have added to the tensions surrounding the event.

Event Organizers' Response

The organizers of Bhagwat's event have maintained that the X account blocking was an isolated incident and that the platform's terms of service were not violated. They have also emphasized that the event was a platform for open dialogue and debate, and that it was not intended to promote any particular ideology or agenda.

Broader Implications

The protest and the subsequent controversy have raised important questions about the role of Hindu organizations and leaders in the US and beyond. The event's organizers and attendees have been subjected to scrutiny and criticism, with some questioning the motives and intentions behind the event. The broader implications of these developments are likely to have a significant impact on the Hindu community in the US and on the global perception of Hindu organizations and their activities.
