Tragic End to Alleged Harassment

A 22-year-old woman in Bengaluru has reportedly taken her own life, citing harassment and blackmail by an ex-classmate. The incident has sent shockwaves through the city, highlighting the growing issue of cyber harassment in India. The victim's suicide note, which she reportedly left behind, has become a focal point of the investigation.

Suicide Note Details

The suicide note, which was found near the scene, reportedly contains detailed allegations against the accused. The woman is said to have accused her ex-classmate of sending explicit videos and threatening her via WhatsApp. The note also mentions that the accused had been harassing her for months.

Investigation Underway

Police have registered a case against the accused based on the suicide note. The investigation is underway, and the accused has been booked under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code. The victim's family is said to be distraught and has requested privacy during this difficult time.

Broader Implications

This incident is a stark reminder of the challenges faced by women in the digital age.
