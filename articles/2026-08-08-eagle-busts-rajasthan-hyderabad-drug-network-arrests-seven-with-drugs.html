EAGLE busts Rajasthan-Hyderabad drug network, arrests seven with drugs worth ₹12.65 lakh

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • EAGLE arrested seven people in Hyderabad for drug trafficking
  • Opium and mephedrone worth ₹12.65 lakh were seized
  • The network reportedly had links to Rajasthan
  • Investigation is ongoing to trace other operatives

EAGLE strikes in Hyderabad

Telangana's Elite Anti-Gang Enforcement and Liaison (EAGLE) unit has dismantled a drug trafficking network operating between Rajasthan and Hyderabad. In a coordinated operation, the team arrested seven persons and seized opium and mephedrone valued at ₹12.65 lakh.

The arrests follow weeks of surveillance and intelligence gathering. Officials said the gang was active in supplying synthetic and semi-synthetic drugs to the city's suburbs.

Details of the seizure

The contraband includes a significant quantity of opium and mephedrone, a synthetic stimulant often abused as a recreational drug. The total street value is estimated at ₹12.65 lakh, though the exact weight of the seized material has not been disclosed.

According to sources, the drugs were being transported from Rajasthan, a state that has emerged as a major transit point for narcotics in northern India. The accused were allegedly coordinating supply chains through couriers and direct deliveries.

How the operation unfolded

EAGLE teams, acting on a tip-off, conducted simultaneous raids at multiple locations in Hyderabad. The suspects were taken into custody without any incident. Their identities have not been officially released, but police sources indicate they are in their twenties and thirties.

Investigators are now probing how the network sourced its supply and whether it had links to larger syndicates. A senior officer said that the arrested individuals are being questioned to map the entire distribution chain.

Rising concern over synthetic drugs

Mephedrone, also known as M-Kat or bath salts, has seen a sharp rise in seizures across Indian cities in recent years. Its low cost and easy availability have made it a popular choice among young users, prompting stricter enforcement.

Telangana has ramped up anti-drug operations, with EAGLE playing a key role in cracking down on inter-state networks. The agency has previously busted several such gangs, but officials admit that the supply routes keep evolving.

What comes next

The seven accused will be produced before a local court, where the police are expected to seek their remand for further interrogation. The focus now shifts to identifying other members of the network, including those based in Rajasthan.

Officials have not ruled out more arrests as the investigation deepens. For now, this operation sends a clear message to traffickers that inter-state drug corridors are under close watch.
