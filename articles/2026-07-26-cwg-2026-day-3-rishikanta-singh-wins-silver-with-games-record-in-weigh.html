Rishikanta Singh makes history with silver and Games record

India's medal count at the Commonwealth Games 2026 in Glasgow received a significant boost on Day 3. Weightlifter Chanambam Rishikanta Singh clinched the silver medal in his category. He did not just settle for second place — he also set a new Commonwealth Games record in the process.

This performance marks a personal milestone for the athlete. The record-breaking lift has put the Indian contingent in a strong position early in the competition. Officials have confirmed the medal, though full details of the lifts are still being compiled.

Indian swimmers make a splash in the pool

In the aquatic centre, the men's 4x200m freestyle relay team gave fans reason to cheer. The quartet powered through their heat to secure a spot in the final. This is a notable achievement for a squad that has been rebuilding its depth in recent years.

The relay final is expected to be fiercely competitive. Australia and England traditionally dominate the distance events. India's entry into the final gives the team a chance to fight for a podium finish.

India's medal hopes and key results from Day 3
