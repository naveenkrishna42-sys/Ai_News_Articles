Accidental Firing at Varanasi Airport

Two people were injured after a passenger's gun went off at the Varanasi airport. The incident occurred during a security check when the passenger was asked to prove that their gun was not loaded.

According to airport staff, the passenger claimed that the gun was not loaded, but it accidentally fired, injuring two staff members. The injured staff members are undergoing treatment.

Investigation Underway

Officials have not yet confirmed the identity of the passenger or the circumstances surrounding the incident. An investigation is currently underway to determine the cause of the accidental firing.

The incident has raised concerns about airport security and the protocols in place for handling firearms. Authorities are reviewing the incident to identify any lapses in security and to prevent such incidents in the future.

Security Protocols

Airport security protocols require passengers to declare any firearms they are carrying and to prove that they are not loaded. The passenger in question had declared the gun, but the accident occurred during the security check.
