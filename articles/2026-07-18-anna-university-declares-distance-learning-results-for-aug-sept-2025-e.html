Anna University declares distance learning results for Aug-Sept 2025 exams

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • Anna University declared distance learning results for August-September 2025 exams.
  • Students can access marksheets on the official website using registration number and date of birth.
  • The university has not yet announced revaluation or supplementary exam dates.

Anna University has declared the results for its distance learning programmes for the August-September 2025 examination session. The announcement was made on Tuesday, March 10, 2026, bringing an end to weeks of waiting for thousands of students enrolled in various undergraduate and postgraduate courses through the university's Directorate of Distance Education.

The results are now available on the official website, and students are advised to download their marksheets at the earliest.

How to check the results

Students can access their individual scorecards by visiting the official result portal at coe1.annauniv.edu. On the homepage, they need to click on the link that reads 'Results for Distance Education Programmes – August/September 2025'.

After that, candidates must enter their registration number and date of birth in the required fields. Once the details are submitted correctly, the marksheet will appear on the screen. Students are advised to download and take a printout for future reference.

The university has not provided a separate login for distance learners, so students must use the same credentials they used during the examination process.

What the results contain

The marksheet displays the marks obtained in each subject, the total marks, and the overall grade or percentage. It also shows the student's name, registration number, programme name, and semester details.

Students who have cleared all subjects will see their final grade. Those who have failed in one or more papers will find a 'fail' status against those subjects. The university has not yet announced the schedule for revaluation or supplementary examinations for distance learners.

Officials have not confirmed whether revaluation applications will be accepted online or offline. Students are expected to check the official website regularly for updates on this front.

Key details to note

  • The results are only for distance education programmes, not for regular or evening college courses.
  • Students must verify all personal details on the marksheet. Any discrepancy should be reported to the university's examination section immediately.
  • The university has not issued any physical mark sheets yet. The digital copy is considered official for now.

Anna University's distance education directorate offers a range of programmes including B.Sc, B.Com, B.A, M.Sc, M.Com, and M.A in various specialisations. The August-September 2025 examination session covered all semesters and years for these courses.

Students who have completed their final semester exams can expect their degree certificates to be processed in the coming months. The university typically issues provisional certificates within 60 to 90 days after the result declaration, but no specific timeline has been announced for this batch.

For now, students are advised to keep checking the official website for any further announcements regarding revaluation, supplementary exams, and certificate distribution.
