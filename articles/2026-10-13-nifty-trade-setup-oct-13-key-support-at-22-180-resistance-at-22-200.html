Nifty Trade Setup Oct 13: Key Support at 22,180, Resistance at 22,200

  • Bank Nifty and broader market remain structurally weak despite intraday bounces
  • Trent, Infosys and ICICI Bank among key stocks to watch for short-term trades

Technical Read

Nifty 50 closed Monday's session near 22,280, holding just above the 22,200 psychological level but well below the 22,180 immediate support zone that now acts as a swing pivot. The index has retraced roughly 4.5% from its recent peak, and unless bulls reclaim 22,200 with sustained volume, the path of least resistance remains downward toward 22,180 and then 21,900.

Immediate Levels

  • Support: 22,180 (swing low), 21,900 (prior breakout)
  • Resistance: 22,200 (psychological), 22,450 (20-DMA cluster)
  • Breakout trigger: Sustained close above 22,500 opens 22,700–22,850

Borrowing costs for Nifty futures remain elevated at 9.2% annualized, indicating limited speculative appetite for directional long positions. IV for Nifty 50 options expiring Oct 16 is pricing in a 1.1% move, suggesting traders expect continued two-way action rather than a clean directional breakout.

Bank Nifty and Sectoral Watch

Bank Nifty tested 47,200 support and bounced weakly to 47,600, reflecting continued stress in PSU banks after recent underperformance. ICICI Bank and Kotak Mahindra are showing relative strength, but the broader banking pack remains pressured below 47,500.

Stocks to Watch

  • Trent: Testing support at 950; a bounce above 975 targets 1,010
  • Infosys: Holding 1,490 support; break below opens 1,465
  • ICICI Bank: Key support at 1,015; reclaim of 1,035 signals stability

Foreign institutional investors sold another ₹1,280 crore of cash-market equities last week, taking their year-to-date outflow to ₹18,400 crore, while domestic mutual funds bought ₹890 crore, per BSE data. This divergence keeps the market vulnerable to short-covering rallies that fail at lower highs.

Trading Plan for Oct 13

Given the weak structure, the recommended bias is cautious. Traders should look for intraday long opportunities only above 22,250 with a stop below 22,150, targeting 22,450. On the downside, a sustained break below 22,180 opens 21,900 and 21,750, with 21,500 as the next major floor.

Key Takeaways

  • Weak technical structure favors range-bound or short-bias trades until 22,200 is reclaimed decisively
  • 22,180 is the critical support; a break targets 21,900 and 21,750
  • Banks and financials underperform, adding downside pressure on the index
  • Wait for confirmation above 22,500 before considering fresh long positions
