Karnataka Man Kills Wife, Daughters Before Suicide in Mysuru

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Family of four found dead in Hunsur, Mysuru district.
  • Police suspect businessman killed wife and two daughters before suicide.
  • Investigation underway; motive not yet confirmed.

In a tragic incident in Karnataka's Mysuru district, a family of four was found dead in their home in Hunsur on Tuesday. Police say the father, a local businessman, allegedly killed his wife and two daughters before dying by suicide.

Bodies Discovered in Hunsur Home

Authorities were alerted to the scene after neighbours reported unusual silence from the house. When police arrived, they found the bodies of the businessman, his wife, and their two daughters. Senior officers confirmed that no signs of forced entry were visible, pointing to a domestic incident.

The victims have been identified as the businessman, his wife, and their two young daughters. Their exact ages have not been released by officials.

Police Suspect Murder-Suicide

Preliminary investigations suggest the man killed his wife and children before taking his own life. A senior police officer stated that a note was recovered from the scene, but its contents have not been disclosed. "We are examining all angles, including financial stress and family disputes," the officer said.

Forensic teams are gathering evidence from the house. Autopsies are scheduled to determine the exact cause of death for each victim.

Community in Shock

The incident has sent shockwaves through the quiet neighbourhood in Hunsur, a town known for its proximity to Mysuru city. Locals described the family as well-respected and said they had not noticed any signs of trouble. "They seemed like a happy family. We are stunned," a neighbour told reporters.

Police have urged the public not to speculate until the investigation is complete. They are also counselling family members and neighbours to help them cope with the tragedy.

What Happens Next

The investigation will focus on the recovered note and the family's financial and personal history. Officials have not yet confirmed a motive. A detailed forensic report is expected in the coming days, which may provide more clarity on the sequence of events.
