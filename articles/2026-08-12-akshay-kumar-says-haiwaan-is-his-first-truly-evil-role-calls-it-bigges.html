Akshay Kumar says 'Haiwaan' is his first truly evil role, calls it biggest challenge

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Akshay Kumar calls 'Haiwaan' his first truly evil role
  • The film marks his reunion with director Priyadarshan after years
  • Teaser shows Akshay as sinister force, Saif Ali Khan as blind man
  • Fans have reacted strongly to the dark, chilling tone of the teaser

Mumbai: Akshay Kumar has done action, comedy, drama and even the occasional anti-hero. But he says he has never been truly evil on screen — until now. The actor has opened up about his role in Priyadarshan's upcoming film Haiwaan, calling it his first outright villainous part and, by his own admission, his biggest challenge yet.

The revelation came as the film's teaser dropped, sending fans into a frenzy. The clip pits Akshay against Saif Ali Khan, who plays a blind man caught in a deadly game of hide-and-seek. The tone is dark, the stakes are personal, and the two stars are clearly operating in a space neither has fully explored before.

A departure from the usual

In a recent interaction, Akshay explained why Haiwaan feels different from anything he has done. He said the script demanded a level of menace he had never tapped into. "This is not the typical villain with a backstory and a sad childhood," he was quoted as saying. "This is pure evil — and that scared me."

He admitted that playing a character with no redeeming qualities was unsettling. "I kept asking Priyadarshan sir, 'Are you sure you want me to do this?' He just smiled and said, 'That's the point.'"

The actor, known for his khiladi-era bravado and later his patriotic dramas, said the role stripped him of every crutch he usually relies on. "No punchlines, no heroics, no saving the day. Just a man who is the problem."

Reuniting with Priyadarshan

Haiwaan also marks a reunion between Akshay and Priyadarshan, a director who has helmed some of the actor's most beloved comedies like Hera Pheri, Garam Masala and Bhool Bhulaiyaa. But this time, the duo has traded laughter for dread.

Priyadarshan, known for his visual flair, seems to have crafted a thriller that leans into atmospheric horror. The teaser shows shadowy corridors, flickering lights and a relentless predator. Saif's character, a blind man, appears to be the prey — but the clip hints that the game may not be as one-sided as it seems.

Fans have already begun speculating about the plot. Some believe Haiwaan could be a survival thriller, while others think it might have a supernatural twist. The makers have remained tight-lipped, only promising a "chilling experience" in the weeks ahead.

Saif Ali Khan's intriguing presence

Saif Ali Khan, who has been on a roll with performances in Sacred Games and Tandav, brings a quiet intensity to the teaser. His portrayal of a blind man is already being praised for its vulnerability. The contrast between his fragility and Akshay's menace creates a palpable tension.

The teaser's highlight is a face-off scene where the two actors exchange no dialogue but convey everything through their eyes — or, in Saif's case, the lack of them. This is a far cry from the light-hearted banter they shared in films like Main Khiladi Tu Anari.

Industry insiders say the film has been in the making for over a year, with heavy emphasis on practical effects and minimal CGI. Priyadarshan reportedly wanted the horror to feel grounded, relying on performance and sound design rather than jump scares.

What this means for Akshay's career

Akshay Kumar's recent box-office run has been mixed, with several films underperforming. His decision to take on a role that defies his brand is being seen as a bold move. Trade analysts suggest that if Haiwaan works, it could open a new chapter for the actor, allowing him to explore darker, more complex characters.

For now, the teaser has done its job — it has people talking. The film's release date has not been announced yet, but the buzz is unmistakable. Fans are already debating whether Akshay can pull off a role that even he admits frightened him.

The coming weeks will reveal more about the film's story and its eventual release. But one thing is clear: Akshay Kumar is no longer playing it safe. And that, in itself, is the biggest twist of all.
