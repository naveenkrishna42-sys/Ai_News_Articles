Boxer's tale tickles Prime Minister

Prime Minister Narendra Modi was left in splits when Indian boxer Narender Berwal recounted a peculiar conversation he had with a Pakistani opponent during the World Military Games. The anecdote, which has since gone viral, centred on the pugilist's admission that he had developed a strong dislike for the name Narender.

Speaking at an event where Modi interacted with athletes, Berwal narrated how the Pakistani boxer, after their bout, confessed: "Mujhe Narendra naam se nafrat ho gayi hai" (I have come to hate the name Narender). The reason, as Berwal explained, was the Indian's dominance in the ring, which had made the name a sore point for his rival.
