Apple Pay Goes Live in India with Axis Bank Partnership

Apple Pay has officially entered the Indian market through a tie‑up with Axis Bank. The service allows users to make contactless payments at NFC‑enabled terminals using their iPhone or Apple Watch. No separate fee is charged for using Apple Pay; costs are limited to the fees already associated with the linked Axis Bank card.

How Apple Pay Works with Axis Bank Cards

When a card is added to Apple Pay, the actual card number is never stored on the device or Apple’s servers. Instead, a unique Device Account Number is created, encrypted and stored in the Secure Element. During a transaction, a dynamic cryptogram is generated, ensuring that the merchant receives only a tokenized value.

Axis Bank Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards are eligible for tokenization. The process is handled through the Axis Bank mobile app or the Wallet app on Apple devices. Once verified, the card appears in Wallet and can be used instantly for in‑store, in‑app, and online purchases.

Key Benefits for Axis Bank Cardholders

Cardholders retain all existing benefits of their Axis Bank cards, including reward points, cashback categories, complimentary lounge access, and insurance coverage. Since Apple Pay does not impose an additional surcharge, the effective cost of transactions remains unchanged.

For frequent travelers, the ability to pay with a tap at international terminals that support Apple Pay adds convenience without needing to carry physical cards. The service also supports transit systems in select cities where NFC readers are deployed.

Security and Transaction Limits

Apple Pay’s security model relies on tokenization and biometric authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode). Even if the device is lost, the token cannot be used without the owner’s biometric verification.

Transaction limits are dictated by the underlying Axis Bank card. For credit cards, the limit equals the card’s credit limit; for debit cards, it is bounded by the available account balance. Apple Pay does not impose its own daily caps, unlike some UPI‑based wallets.

Eligibility and How to Set Up

To use Apple Pay, customers must own an iPhone 8 or later, or an Apple Watch Series 3 or later, running iOS 15 or watchOS 8 or newer. An active Axis Bank Visa or Mastercard credit or debit card is required. Non‑resident Indian (NRI) accounts are currently not supported.

Setup steps: open the Wallet app, tap the plus sign, select “Debit or Credit Card”, follow the prompts to enter card details, and complete verification via OTP sent to the registered mobile number. Once verified, the card is ready for contactless payments.
