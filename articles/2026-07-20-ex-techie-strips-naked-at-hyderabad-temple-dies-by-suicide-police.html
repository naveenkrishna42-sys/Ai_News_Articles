Ex-Techie Strips Naked at Hyderabad Temple, Dies by Suicide: Police

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 32-year-old former techie stripped naked inside a Hyderabad temple.
  • He was taken to a hospital for a medical check-up after the incident.
  • Later, he died by suicide at an undisclosed location.
  • Police are investigating possible mental health issues.

Incident at the Temple

A 32-year-old former software engineer allegedly stripped naked inside a prominent temple in Hyderabad on Saturday. Witnesses said the man entered the sanctum sanctorum and removed his clothes, causing panic among devotees.

Temple security personnel quickly intervened and detained him. They handed him over to the local police, who arrived at the scene shortly after.

Hospital Visit and Release

Police took the man to a nearby government hospital for a medical evaluation. Officers said he appeared disoriented but did not show signs of aggression. Doctors examined him and found no immediate physical ailment.

After a few hours, the hospital discharged him. Police say they planned to question him further but allowed him to leave as he seemed stable. The exact reason for his behavior remains unclear.

Suicide and Investigation

Later on Saturday evening, the man died by suicide at an undisclosed location. Police have not revealed the method or exact timing of the death. A case has been registered under relevant sections of the law.

Investigators are looking into his background. Preliminary inquiries suggest he had been working as a software engineer but quit his job a few months ago. Neighbors described him as quiet and reserved.

Police are also checking his phone records and speaking with family members to understand if he was under any stress or suffering from mental health issues. No suicide note has been found so far.

Mental Health Angle

This incident has raised concerns about mental health awareness in the tech community. Hyderabad's IT corridor has seen several cases of professionals struggling with work pressure and isolation.

Counsellors and activists have urged companies to provide better support systems. The police have not confirmed whether the man was undergoing any treatment.

Officials said they are waiting for post-mortem reports and will share more details once the investigation progresses. The temple has resumed its regular activities.

What happens next: Police will submit a detailed report to the magistrate, and the man's family is expected to record their statements in the coming days.
