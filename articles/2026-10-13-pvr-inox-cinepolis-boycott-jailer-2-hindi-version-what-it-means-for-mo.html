PVR-INOX, Cinepolis Boycott Jailer 2 Hindi Version: What It Means for Moviegoers

Multiplex Chains Skip Hindi Release Amid Dispute

Leading multiplex chains PVR-INOX and Cinepolis have announced they will not screen the Hindi-dubbed version of Jailer 2, starring Rajinikanth, when it releases on October 15, 2026. The decision comes after weeks of failed negotiations over revenue-sharing terms between the film's distributors and theater operators.

Why the Boycott?

Industry sources indicate that tensions arose over the percentage of box office collections allocated to exhibitors. While the exact figures remain undisclosed, such disputes are common during high-profile pan-India releases where distribution rights vary significantly across regions.

  • Revenue Sharing: Multiplexes typically demand higher shares for star-driven films like Jailer 2.
  • North Market Impact: Hindi-speaking audiences form a large part of the film’s expected earnings.
  • Precedent Concerns: Other producers may follow suit if initial returns fall short.

Film Still Releasing in Four South Languages

Despite the setback with its Hindi version, Jailer 2 is confirmed for release in Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, and Kannada. Advance bookings have already crossed ₹20 crore globally, indicating strong anticipation among core South Indian audiences.

THEATRICAL DETAILS

The film's release strategy was designed to maximize reach across linguistic boundaries. However, excluding the Hindi version limits access for millions of potential viewers in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, and other Hindi-dominant states.

OTT Plans Offer Backup Distribution

If theatrical performance suffers due to limited screen availability, digital streaming might salvage some losses. Reports suggest that the Hindi version of Jailer 2 could go online four weeks after its theatrical debut, possibly on an unnamed OTT platform.

Real-World Implications for Fans

For fans hoping to catch Jailer 2 in theaters, options outside PVR-INOX and Cinepolis venues remain available. Independent theaters and single-screen halls in select cities may pick up the Hindi print, though coverage will be uneven.

  • Limited Reach: Fewer screens reduce overall visibility and word-of-mouth momentum.
  • Regional Divide: Audience fragmentation affects pan-India collection potential.
  • Alternative Viewing: Theater-goers must seek partner platforms or wait for streaming.

Industry Reaction Remains Cautious

No official statements have been issued by either party involved—Rajinikanth’s production house or the affected multiplex chains. Analysts expect further developments before the scheduled release date, especially given the star’s massive fanbase and the film's pre-release hype.
