Best Diwali Cashback Credit Cards in India

Diwali is the perfect time to shop for festive essentials and gifts. To make your shopping experience even more rewarding, consider applying for one of the best Diwali cashback credit cards offered by Indian banks. These cards offer attractive cashback rates, discounts, and other benefits to help you save money while shopping for Diwali.

Top 5 Diwali Cashback Credit Cards

1. HDFC Bank Diners Club Privilege Card

The HDFC Bank Diners Club Privilege Card is one of the most popular Diwali cashback cards in India. It offers a flat 10% cashback on all online shopping, up to Rs. 1,500 per month. Additionally, the card provides access to airport lounges, concierge services, and exclusive discounts on travel and dining.

2. ICICI Bank Select Credit Card

The card also provides access to airport lounges, concierge services, and exclusive discounts on travel and dining. Joining and Annual Fees

Most of the Diwali cashback credit cards mentioned above do not charge any joining or annual fees. However, it is essential to check the terms and conditions of each card before applying.

Eligibility Criteria and How to Apply Online

To apply for any of the Diwali cashback credit cards, you need to meet the eligibility criteria set by the bank. Generally, you need to have a valid credit score and income proof. You can apply for these cards online by visiting the bank's website or by using the bank's mobile app. Once you have submitted your application, the bank will review it and approve or reject it based on your eligibility.

Final Recommendation

If you are a frequent online shopper and looking for a credit card that offers attractive cashback rates and other benefits, then the Diwali cashback credit cards mentioned above are a great option.
