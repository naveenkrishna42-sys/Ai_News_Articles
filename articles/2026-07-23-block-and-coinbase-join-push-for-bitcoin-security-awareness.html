Block and Coinbase Join Push for Bitcoin Security Awareness

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Block and Coinbase announce collaboration to promote Bitcoin security best practices.
  • Initiative includes educational resources and tools for safer cryptocurrency storage.
  • Move aims to counter rising crypto scams and user errors leading to losses.

Block and Coinbase have announced a joint initiative aimed at bolstering Bitcoin security awareness among users. The two companies, both major players in the cryptocurrency space, are pooling resources to educate the public on how to protect their digital assets.

The collaboration comes at a time when crypto adoption is growing rapidly, but so are incidents of theft, phishing, and user error. Industry data shows that billions of dollars in Bitcoin have been lost or stolen over the past few years, often due to weak security practices or reliance on unverified third-party services.

Focus on Education and Best Practices

The initiative will focus on creating easy-to-understand guides and tools that help users secure their Bitcoin holdings. Topics covered include using hardware wallets, enabling two-factor authentication, and recognizing common scam tactics.

Block, founded by Jack Dorsey, has long been a proponent of Bitcoin and has integrated crypto features into its Cash App. Coinbase, one of the largest cryptocurrency exchanges in the United States, has also emphasized security as a core part of its service. Both companies say the effort is not about promoting their own products but about raising the overall security standard in the ecosystem.

Industry-Wide Challenge

The push comes amid a broader industry challenge: making self-custody of Bitcoin accessible without compromising safety. While many exchanges offer custodial services, a growing number of users prefer to hold their own private keys. This shift, however, places the burden of security directly on individuals.

Scammers have also become more sophisticated. Phishing attacks, fake wallet apps, and social engineering schemes are common. Even experienced users have fallen victim to hacks that exploit vulnerabilities in software or human error. Block and Coinbase aim to address these pain points through clear, actionable advice.

Part of a Larger Trend

This is not the first time crypto companies have come together for security education. In the past, industry groups have launched similar campaigns, but the involvement of two such high-profile firms signals a more coordinated effort. The initiative is expected to include webinars, online resources, and possibly in-person workshops in select cities.

Both companies have also committed to sharing threat intelligence with each other and with law enforcement agencies. The goal is to create a feedback loop that helps identify new attack vectors and respond quickly.

What to Watch

The initiative is in its early stages, and details on specific timelines and deliverables are expected in the coming weeks. For now, the message from Block and Coinbase is clear: as Bitcoin becomes more mainstream, security cannot be an afterthought. Users who adopt best practices will be better protected, and the industry as a whole stands to benefit from fewer high-profile losses.
