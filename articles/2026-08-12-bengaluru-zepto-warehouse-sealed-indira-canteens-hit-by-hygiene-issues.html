Bengaluru: Zepto warehouse sealed, Indira Canteens hit by hygiene issues

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • A Zepto warehouse in Bengaluru was sealed due to unhygienic conditions.
  • Rats and worms were discovered in Indira Canteens, raising food safety concerns.
  • Officials have not yet confirmed the full extent of the actions taken.

Zepto Warehouse Sealed

Bengaluru's food safety inspectors have sealed a Zepto warehouse after finding unhygienic conditions at the facility. The action comes as part of a broader crackdown on food handling practices in the city's rapidly growing quick-commerce sector.

The warehouse, whose exact location has not been disclosed, was found to be in violation of basic sanitation norms. Officials have not yet confirmed the specific violations, but the sealing indicates serious lapses in storage and maintenance standards.

Indira Canteens Under Scrutiny

In a separate but equally concerning development, inspections at Indira Canteens — the city's subsidised food outlets — have revealed the presence of rats and worms. The discovery has raised alarms about the quality of food served to thousands of daily visitors who rely on these canteens for affordable meals.

Health officials have not yet detailed which specific canteens were affected or the exact nature of the contamination. However, the findings have prompted immediate corrective measures, though authorities are yet to confirm whether any canteens have been temporarily shut down.

Public Health Concerns

The twin incidents have reignited debates about food safety oversight in Bengaluru. While Zepto operates as a private quick-commerce player, Indira Canteens are a government initiative, making the hygiene lapses particularly embarrassing for the city administration.

Residents and consumer groups have expressed outrage on social media, demanding strict action and transparent reporting. The Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) has not yet issued an official statement on the timeline for reopening the sealed warehouse or rectifying issues at the canteens.

Broader Implications

These incidents underscore the challenges of regulating a booming food delivery and public catering ecosystem. With quick-commerce companies expanding rapidly across Indian cities, the need for robust inspection mechanisms has never been more critical.

Experts point out that while such crackdowns are welcome, they must be backed by sustained monitoring rather than one-off raids. The state's food safety department has been understaffed, and officials have not yet confirmed whether additional inspections are planned across other warehouses and canteens.

What Happens Next

Authorities are expected to release a detailed report on the findings in the coming days. The Zepto warehouse will likely remain sealed until it meets all hygiene standards, while Indira Canteens face a similar review process.

For now, Bengaluru residents are left waiting for clarity on how these issues will be resolved, and whether stricter penalties will deter future lapses. The city's food safety apparatus is under the spotlight, and the coming weeks will reveal whether these actions lead to lasting change.
