Guru Purnima celebrated at Flushing Swaminarayan Temple

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Religion Original source ↗
Key points
  • Guru Purnima observed at Flushing Swaminarayan Temple with special ceremonies.
  • Devotees offered prayers and listened to discourses on the guru-shishya tradition.
  • Cultural programs and community feasts marked the celebration.

Devotees gather for Guru Purnima at Flushing Temple

The Swaminarayan Temple in Flushing, New York, was filled with devotees on Wednesday as the community observed Guru Purnima. The festival, which honours spiritual teachers, saw a day-long programme of prayers, discourses and cultural performances.

Hundreds of people attended the event. Many arrived early in the morning to participate in the rituals. The temple premises were decorated with flowers and lights.

Ceremonies and rituals

The celebrations began with a havan, a sacred fire ceremony. Priests chanted Vedic mantras as devotees offered ghee and grains into the fire. This was followed by a special abhishek, or ritual bathing, of the temple's murtis.

Devotees then offered puja to their gurus. They sought blessings for spiritual progress. Temple volunteers distributed prasad, sanctified food, to everyone present.

Discourses on guru-shishya parampara

Senior swamis from the BAPS Swaminarayan Sanstha delivered discourses. They spoke about the importance of the guru in Hindu tradition. The talks focused on the guru-shishya parampara, the lineage of teacher and disciple.

One swami narrated stories from the life of Bhagwan Swaminarayan. He highlighted how the guru guides disciples on the path of dharma. Devotees listened attentively, many taking notes.

Cultural programs and community feast

In the afternoon, children and youth presented cultural programs. They performed devotional songs and dances. A skit depicted the life of a saint, drawing applause from the audience.

The day concluded with a community feast. Volunteers served a vegetarian meal to all attendees. Families sat together, sharing food and conversation. Temple officials said the event strengthened community bonds.

Guru Purnima is observed on the full moon day of the Hindu month of Ashadha. It is a day to express gratitude to teachers and spiritual guides. The Flushing temple has been celebrating the festival for over a decade.

Organisers said they plan to hold similar events for upcoming festivals. The temple will next celebrate Janmashtami, the birth of Lord Krishna, in August.
