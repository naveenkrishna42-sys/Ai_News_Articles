Sensex drops over 300 pts, Nifty tests 24,450 as FMCG stocks drag

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex fell over 300 points in early trade
  • Nifty50 slipped below 24,450 level
  • FMCG stocks among major drags

Indian stock markets opened lower on Tuesday, extending losses from the previous session, as investor sentiment remained cautious amid mixed global cues. The BSE Sensex dropped over 300 points in early trade, while the Nifty50 slipped below the 24,450 mark, testing key support levels.

Market opens in red

The 30-share Sensex was trading at around 79,800 levels, down 0.4 per cent from Monday's close. The broader Nifty50 hovered near 24,440, with selling pressure visible across most sectors.

Market breadth was negative, with more than two stocks declining for every one advancing on the BSE. Volatility index India VIX rose marginally, indicating nervousness among traders.

FMCG stocks lead decline

Fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) stocks were the biggest drag on the indices, with major players like Hindustan Unilever, ITC, and Nestle India trading in the red. Analysts attributed the weakness to concerns over rural demand and rising input costs.

Other sectors, including banking, auto, and metals, also witnessed selling pressure. However, IT stocks showed some resilience, with select counters trading in the green.

Global cues weigh

Asian markets were mixed in early trade, with Japan's Nikkei and China's Shanghai Composite trading lower, while Hong Kong's Hang Seng showed marginal gains. US futures pointed to a flat opening, offering little direction to domestic traders.

Investors are also awaiting key economic data due later this week, including inflation figures and industrial production numbers, which could influence the Reserve Bank of India's monetary policy stance.

What to watch

Market participants will closely track the movement of the rupee against the dollar, as well as foreign institutional investor (FII) flows, which have been volatile in recent weeks. The Nifty's ability to hold above the 24,400 level will be crucial in determining the short-term trend.

With no major domestic triggers scheduled for the day, analysts expect the market to remain range-bound, with stock-specific action driven by corporate announcements and sectoral news.
