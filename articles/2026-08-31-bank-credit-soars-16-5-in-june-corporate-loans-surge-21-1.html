Bank credit soars 16.5% in June, corporate loans surge 21.1%

📅 2026-08-31 📂 Banking Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bank credit growth surged 16.5% in June, driven by corporate loans
  • Corporate loans rose 21.1% in June, while non-food credit grew 19.1% in July
  • Economic expansion and government spending likely fueling the growth
  • RBI's monetary policy stance remains a key factor in credit growth

Bank credit growth accelerates in June, corporate loans lead the charge

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has released data showing a significant surge in bank credit growth in June, with corporate loans leading the charge. Bank credit expanded by 16.5% in June, marking the highest growth rate since December 2022. This growth was primarily driven by a 21.1% increase in corporate loans, which accounted for 42% of the total credit growth.

Non-food credit growth continues in July

While June saw a slowdown in bank credit growth, July data indicates a continued expansion in non-food credit. Non-food credit, which includes loans for housing, vehicles, and other consumer durables, grew by 19.1% in July. This growth is a positive sign for the economy, as it indicates increased consumer spending and investment.

Economic expansion and government spending likely fueling the growth

The surge in bank credit growth can be attributed to several factors. Economic expansion, particularly in the manufacturing and services sectors, has led to increased demand for loans. Additionally, the government's spending plans, including infrastructure development and social sector initiatives, are likely to boost credit demand.

RBI's monetary policy stance remains a key factor in credit growth

The RBI's monetary policy stance also plays a crucial role in bank credit growth. The central bank's decision to keep interest rates unchanged in the recent policy review has provided a stable and supportive environment for credit growth. However, the RBI has also emphasized the need for banks to maintain prudential standards and manage risks effectively.
