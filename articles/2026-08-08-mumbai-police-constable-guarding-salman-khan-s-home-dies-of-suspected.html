Mumbai Police Constable Guarding Salman Khan's Home Dies of Suspected Heart Attack

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Constable collapsed outside Salman Khan's Bandra residence
  • Deployed as part of security amid death threats to actor
  • Cause of death suspected heart attack; official confirmation awaited

A Mumbai Police constable deployed at Bollywood actor Salman Khan's Bandra residence died on Saturday morning after collapsing outside the building. The officer, part of the security detail assigned to the actor amid ongoing death threats, is suspected to have suffered a heart attack, according to reports.

The incident occurred at Galaxy Apartment, where the constable was on duty. He was rushed to a nearby hospital but was declared dead on arrival. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact cause of death, though preliminary reports point to a cardiac arrest.

Security Detail Under Strain

The constable was one of several personnel deployed to guard the actor's residence following threats to his life. Salman Khan has been under heightened security cover for months, with authorities treating the threats seriously. The deployment includes local police and, at times, central forces, depending on the threat level.

This tragedy highlights the physical and mental toll such extended duties take on security personnel. The deceased, whose name has not been officially released, was reportedly a former Army serviceman, adding to the sense of loss among colleagues who knew him as a dedicated officer.

Immediate Response and Investigation

Police officials at the Bandra station have registered a case of accidental death and are conducting an inquiry. A senior officer said the constable had been on duty earlier in the morning and had not complained of any discomfort. Colleagues noticed him collapse suddenly, and efforts to revive him at the spot failed.

The body has been sent for post-mortem at a civic hospital. A detailed medical report is expected to confirm whether a heart attack was indeed the cause. The police department has assured full support to the constable's family, though specific details of compensation or assistance have not been announced.

Background of Threats

Salman Khan has faced multiple threats over the past year, leading to a significant increase in his security cover. In 2025, the actor received threatening messages, allegedly from gangsters, which prompted the Mumbai Police to upgrade his protection. The security arrangement outside his residence includes armed guards, metal detectors, and regular patrols.

Despite these measures, the risks remain. The death of a security personnel underscores the dangers faced by those tasked with protecting high-profile figures. The actor has not yet commented publicly on the incident, and his team has remained silent.

Broader Implications

This incident has sparked discussions about the welfare of police personnel on long-term protective duty. Experts point out that extended hours, stress, and irregular schedules can lead to health complications. The Maharashtra Police have faced criticism in the past for inadequate medical checks for such personnel.

The constable's death is a grim reminder of the human cost behind celebrity security. While the focus often remains on the star, the officers who stand guard through the night are equally vulnerable. Their service, often unnoticed, is now in the spotlight for the wrong reasons.

As the investigation continues, the police are likely to review the health and deployment protocols for personnel assigned to high-risk duties. The next of kin of the deceased have been informed, and funeral arrangements are expected to be made with full police honours.

What to watch: The post-mortem report will clarify the cause of death, and the Mumbai Police may announce revised duty schedules or health screenings for personnel on extended security assignments.
