DLF Sells Gurugram Penthouse For Rs 271 Crore In Record Deal

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • DLF has sold a penthouse at The Dahlias in Gurugram for Rs 271 crore, the most expensive single-unit residential deal in the area.
  • The deal works out to about Rs 2.6 lakh per square foot, reflecting the premium attached to ultra-luxury properties.
  • The buyer is entrepreneur Manav Sardana, highlighting the growing demand among India's ultra-rich for high-end real estate.

In what is being described as a record transaction for the National Capital Region's luxury property market, real estate giant DLF has sold a penthouse at its The Dahlias project in Gurugram for Rs 271 crore. The deal, which translates to roughly Rs 2.6 lakh per square foot, marks the most expensive single-unit residential sale in the region, according to multiple reports.

The buyer has been identified as Manav Sardana, an entrepreneur. The transaction underscores a broader trend of India's wealthiest individuals pouring money into ultra-luxury homes, with prices that rival or exceed the cost of private jets.

The Dahlias And The Record Deal

The Dahlias is DLF's flagship super-luxury residential project located in Gurugram. The penthouse sold is a sprawling unit that commands a premium view and comes with the kind of bespoke fittings that define the top end of India's real estate market.

At Rs 271 crore, the price eclipses previous benchmarks for single-apartment deals in the region. Industry trackers noted that the per-square-foot rate of approximately Rs 2.6 lakh sets a new high watermark for Gurugram, a market that has seen significant price appreciation in the luxury segment over the past few years.

Who Is Manav Sardana?

Manav Sardana, the entrepreneur who purchased the penthouse, has not made any public statement about the acquisition. Details about his business interests remain largely private, though his entry into this league of buyers places him among a select group of ultra-high-net-worth individuals investing in trophy real estate assets.

The identity of the buyer was confirmed through multiple reports citing sources close to the transaction. Neither DLF nor Sardana has officially commented on the deal so far.

The Surge In Ultra-Luxury Buying

The sale is part of a larger pattern. Across India's major cities, particularly Delhi-NCR and Mumbai, the demand for ultra-luxury residences has surged. Properties priced between Rs 60 crore and Rs 600 crore are increasingly finding takers among the country's business elite, professionals, and inheritors of family wealth.

For many of these buyers, a high-end apartment is not just a home but a statement of success and a store of value. The per-square-foot pricing in such projects often exceeds the cost of a high-end car or even a small aircraft, making them among the most expensive residential spaces in the country.

What This Means For The Market

Real estate analysts view the DLF deal as a bullish signal for the luxury segment. It shows that demand at the very top of the pyramid remains robust, even as interest rates and overall property prices have climbed. Developers with premium land banks in locations like Gurugram's Golf Course Road extension are expected to benefit from this trend.

The transaction also highlights the growing concentration of wealth in India, with a small but expanding cohort able to spend lavishly on homes. While the broader housing market continues to cater to mid-income buyers, the ultra-luxury niche is performing strongly on the back of a few high-value deals.

What To Watch Next

As the record sinks in, the focus will shift to whether other developers can match or surpass this benchmark. DLF itself has more inventory in The Dahlias and similar projects. The question is whether the demand for such pricey units will sustain, or if this remains an outlier in an otherwise cautious market. Industry watchers will also be keen to see if Sardana or DLF break their silence on the transaction in the coming days.
