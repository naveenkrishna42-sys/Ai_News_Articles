Private Credit: A Growing Trend

Private credit is a form of financing that is provided by non-bank financial institutions, such as private equity firms, hedge funds, and asset managers. It is a growing trend in India's financial sector, driven by the need for alternative financing methods that are not subject to the same regulations and restrictions as traditional banking loans.
