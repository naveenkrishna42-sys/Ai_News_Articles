China worker's 200 squats punishment leads to muscle breakdown

📅 2026-08-27 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Employee suffered muscle breakdown after 200 squats punishment
  • Manager refused to cover hospital bills, worsening the situation
  • Incident raises concerns about excessive workplace discipline in China

Harsh Discipline Leads to Severe Consequences

A 32-year-old employee in China has suffered severe muscle damage after her manager ordered her to do 200 squats for missing work targets. The incident has sparked concerns about the extent of workplace discipline in China, where physical punishments are reportedly common.

Employee's Condition and Manager's Response

The employee, who wishes to remain anonymous, developed a muscle breakdown in her legs, requiring hospitalization. The manager refused to cover her hospital bills, leaving the employee to bear the financial burden. This response has been widely criticized, with many seeing it as a violation of basic human rights.

Broader Implications of Workplace Discipline

The incident highlights the broader issue of excessive workplace discipline in China. While physical punishments are not uncommon, they often lead to severe health consequences. This case serves as a stark reminder of the need for more humane and effective workplace management practices.

Expert Opinions and Analysis

Human rights experts have condemned the incident, calling it a clear violation of the employee's rights. They argue that physical punishments should be replaced with more effective and humane disciplinary measures. The incident also raises questions about the effectiveness of such punishments in improving workplace performance.
