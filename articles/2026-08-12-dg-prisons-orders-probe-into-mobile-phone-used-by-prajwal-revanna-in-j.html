DG Prisons orders probe into mobile phone used by Prajwal Revanna in jail

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • DG Prisons has ordered a probe into the source of a mobile phone found with Prajwal Revanna in Bengaluru jail.
  • The phone's recovery has raised questions about prison security and visitor protocols.
  • Officials have not yet disclosed how the device was smuggled in or who provided it.

The Director General of Prisons has ordered an inquiry into how a mobile phone ended up in the hands of Prajwal Revanna inside a Bengaluru jail. The former MP is currently in judicial custody in connection with an alleged sexual harassment case.

The probe will trace the origin of the device and identify the channel through which it entered the high-security facility. Officials say the phone was recovered during a routine check, though the exact date of recovery has not been officially confirmed.

Security breach under scrutiny

The incident has put the spotlight on prison security protocols in Karnataka. Mobile phones are strictly prohibited inside jail premises, and their presence raises serious questions about the effectiveness of existing checks.

A senior prison official, speaking on condition of anonymity, said the inquiry will cover all possible entry points, including visitor visits, staff access, and supplies. The findings are expected to determine whether any personnel were complicit in the breach.

Prajwal Revanna's custody

Prajwal Revanna, a former Hassan MP, was arrested earlier this year after multiple complaints of sexual harassment were filed against him. He has been in judicial custody since then, with his bail pleas repeatedly rejected by courts.

His legal team has maintained that the allegations are politically motivated. However, the recovery of the mobile phone could complicate his legal position, as it may be seen as a violation of prison rules.

Inquiry process

The DG Prisons has tasked a senior officer to lead the investigation. The officer will examine call logs, message histories, and contact lists on the recovered device to trace its usage and possible links to the outside world.

Officials have not yet confirmed whether the phone was used for any unlawful activity. The investigation will also look into whether similar devices have been smuggled into other prisons in the state.

Prison department sources indicate that routine checks have been intensified across Bengaluru jails following the incident. The department is also reviewing visitor entry protocols to prevent future lapses.

This is not the first time a mobile phone has been recovered inside a Karnataka prison. Similar incidents have been reported in other districts, prompting calls for stricter surveillance and the use of signal-jamming technology.

The inquiry report is expected to be submitted within a week. Depending on its findings, the department may initiate disciplinary action against any staff found negligent.

The case has also reignited public debate on the privileges available to high-profile prisoners. While authorities maintain that all inmates are treated equally, critics argue that such incidents reveal gaps in the system.

As the investigation progresses, all eyes will be on the prison department's next steps. Whether the probe leads to systemic reforms or remains a one-off inquiry is yet to be seen.
