Virat Kohli calls Ajinkya Rahane his favourite Test batting partner after retirement

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Virat Kohli calls Ajinkya Rahane his 'favourite Test batting partner'
  • Sachin Tendulkar praises Rahane's composure and aggression
  • BCCI and former coach Pravin Amre also pay tribute to Rahane's career

Virat Kohli has described Ajinkya Rahane as his 'favourite Test batting partner', following the veteran batter's retirement from international cricket. The tribute came as the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) and several former teammates acknowledged Rahane's distinguished career.

Kohli's tribute to a trusted partner

Kohli, who shared the dressing room with Rahane for over a decade, did not hold back in his praise. He referred to Rahane as his go-to partner in the longest format of the game.

"My favourite Test batting partner," Kohli said, reflecting on the countless partnerships they built in the middle. The two batsmen often anchored India's innings together, especially in challenging overseas conditions.

Tendulkar highlights a unique quality

Sachin Tendulkar also weighed in, offering a nuanced take on Rahane's style. "You showed that composure isn't the opposite of aggression," Tendulkar said in his heartfelt tribute.

Tendulkar's comment underlined how Rahane's calm demeanour on the field was often misinterpreted. The former captain argued that Rahane's patience was a form of strength, not a lack of intent.

A career defined by grit

Rahane retires with over 5,000 Test runs to his name, including 12 centuries. His most memorable knock remains the 112 he scored at Lord's in 2014, a innings that helped India secure a famous win.

Former coach Pravin Amre called Rahane a 'silent warrior'. "He never sought the limelight, but he was always there when the team needed him," Amre told ThePrint.

BCCI acknowledges contributions

The BCCI released a statement paying tribute to Rahane's international career, which spanned more than a decade. The board highlighted his role in India's Test series win in Australia in 2020-21, where Rahane captained the side to a historic victory.

Rahane's ability to perform under pressure, especially in overseas conditions, made him a vital cog in India's Test setup. His leadership during the Australia tour, after Kohli's paternity leave, earned him widespread acclaim.

What comes next for Rahane

Rahane has not announced any immediate plans post-retirement, but he is expected to continue playing domestic cricket and possibly take up a mentoring role. The cricketing world will watch closely to see if he transitions into coaching or commentary.
