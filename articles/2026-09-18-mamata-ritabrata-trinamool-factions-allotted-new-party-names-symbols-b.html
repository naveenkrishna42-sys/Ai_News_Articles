Google Pixel 10 Pro Fold: The Complete Review

The Pixel 10 Pro Fold arrives at a critical moment for Google's hardware ambitions. After two generations of foldable experiments, Google has refined its approach with the Tensor G5 chip, a redesigned hinge mechanism, and a refined camera system that finally feels competitive with the best foldables on the market. But at $1,799, the stakes are high, and the competition from Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold 7 and OnePlus Open 2 has never been fiercer.

Design and Build Quality

Google has trimmed the Pixel 10 Pro Fold's chassis to 10.8mm when folded and 5.1mm when open, making it noticeably thinner than its predecessor. The new "fluid friction" hinge allows the device to hold any angle between 20 and 145 degrees, and Google claims a 400,000-fold lifespan. The outer display measures 6.4 inches with a 120Hz refresh rate, while the inner 8.1-inch LTPO panel now hits 2,400 nits peak brightness.

The aluminum frame has been reinforced with a new alloy that Google says reduces weight by 12 grams while improving rigidity. The IPX8 rating remains, though dust resistance is still absent — a lingering weakness compared to Samsung's IP48 rating on the Z Fold 7.

Performance: Tensor G5 Tested

The Tensor G5 is Google's first chip fabricated on TSMC's 3nm process, and the difference is tangible. In our benchmark suite, the Pixel 10 Pro Fold scored 1,847 in single-core Geekbench 6 and 4,923 in multi-core — roughly 28% faster than the Pixel 9 Pro Fold. More importantly, sustained performance has improved dramatically; after 30 minutes of gaming, the device retained 82% of its peak performance, versus just 61% on the previous generation.

Thermal management remains a concern during extended 4K video recording, but for everyday multitasking, split-screen apps, and AI features, the G5 delivers smooth, consistent results.

Camera System

Google has equipped the Pixel 10 Pro Fold with a 50MP main sensor, a 48MP ultrawide, and a 10.8MP telephoto with 5x optical zoom. The inner display houses a 10MP selfie camera, while the outer display uses a 12MP shooter. Computational photography remains Google's strong suit — Night Sight on the foldable now processes images 40% faster thanks to the G5's improved TPU.

Video quality has also improved, with 8K30 recording and new AI-powered stabilization that rivals Apple's Action Mode. However, the telephoto lens struggles in low light compared to the Galaxy Z Fold 7's 10MP 3x and periscope setup.

Software and AI Features

Android 16 ships with seven years of OS and security updates. Gemini Nano runs entirely on-device, powering features like call screening, live translation, and the new "Split Screen Assistant" that can summarize content across two apps simultaneously. Google's "Add Me" feature now works in foldable mode, letting users insert themselves into group photos using the outer display as a viewfinder.

Battery Life and Charging

The 5,100mAh battery delivers a full day of mixed use, with our standard rundown test lasting 11 hours and 20 minutes. Charging remains modest: 30W wired and 15W wireless, meaning a full charge takes about 95 minutes.

Analysis: Where Google Wins and Where It Doesn't

Google's foldable finally feels like a finished product rather than a beta test. The Tensor G5 closes much of the performance gap, the hinge is more durable, and the AI software integration is unmatched. However, charging speeds remain embarrassingly slow compared to the OnePlus Open 2, and the lack of dust resistance is a real durability concern for a device that will spend most of its life in pockets and bags.

At $1,799, the Pixel 10 Pro Fold undercuts Samsung's Z Fold 7 by $100 while offering better software support and cleaner AI features. But buyers who prioritize raw performance, faster charging, or brighter displays may find the OnePlus Open 2 a more compelling value proposition.

Key Takeaways

  • Best-in-class AI: On-device Gemini Nano and seven years of updates make this the most future-proof foldable.
  • Improved performance: The Tensor G5 delivers a 28% CPU uplift and dramatically better sustained performance.
  • Camera compromise: Solid overall, but the telephoto lags behind Samsung's dual-telephoto setup.
  • Slow charging: 30W wired is well behind the competition, and a full charge takes over 90 minutes.
  • No dust resistance: IPX8 is good for water, but the missing dust rating is a durability gap.
  • Verdict: The Pixel 10 Pro Fold is Google's most polished foldable yet and a worthy alternative to the Galaxy Z Fold 7 — provided you can live with its charging and durability limitations.
