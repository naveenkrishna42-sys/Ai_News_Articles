CWG 2026 Day 1: India Shocks in Lawn Bowls, Doping Case Mars Start

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Indian lawn bowlers, including World No. 21 Putul, defeat higher-ranked opponents in Glasgow.
  • Rupa Rani Tirkey and Pinki also secure wins, marking a strong start for the team.
  • A doping case involving an Indian athlete has emerged, casting a shadow over the opening day.

Lawn Bowls Delivers Early Upsets

India's lawn bowls contingent produced a series of stunning results on the opening day of the Commonwealth Games 2026 in Glasgow. The team, often considered underdogs on the global stage, turned heads with disciplined performances against higher-ranked opponents.

In a major upset, World No. 21 Putul defeated the reigning outdoor singles champion, a result that has sent ripples through the tournament. The victory marks a significant milestone for Indian bowls, a sport where the country has historically struggled for recognition.

Rupa Rani Tirkey and Pinki Shine

Alongside Putul, Rupa Rani Tirkey and Pinki also registered wins, showcasing the depth of talent in the squad. Their victories came in tightly contested matches, with both athletes displaying composure under pressure.

The performances have boosted India's medal hopes in a discipline that rarely features in the country's medal tally. The team's preparation, including months of training and participation in international events, appears to be paying off.

Doping Setback Hits Indian Camp

However, the day was not without controversy. Reports emerged that an Indian athlete has been provisionally suspended after failing a doping test. Officials have not yet confirmed the athlete's identity or the substance involved, but the development has cast a shadow over the camp.

The Commonwealth Games Federation has launched an investigation. The athlete has the right to request a B-sample analysis. This is the second such case involving India at a major multi-sport event this year, raising questions about the effectiveness of the country's anti-doping protocols.

Navneet: The Pilot on Unpaid Leave

Among the notable stories from the Indian contingent is Navneet, a pilot who is competing in lawn bowls while on unpaid leave. His journey from the cockpit to the greens has drawn attention, highlighting the diverse backgrounds of athletes at the Games.

Navneet's participation underscores the sacrifices many athletes make to represent the country. His story is one of several that have emerged from the Indian camp, adding a human element to the competitive narrative.

Other Day 1 Highlights

Beyond lawn bowls, India's campaign saw mixed results across other sports. While some athletes advanced in their respective events, others faced early exits. The boxing and badminton contingents are yet to begin their campaigns.

Medal hopes remain high in weightlifting and shooting, though both events are scheduled for later in the week. The athletics team is also preparing for track and field events starting from Day 3.

What to watch: The Indian camp will be focused on damage control regarding the doping case, while lawn bowlers aim to build on their early momentum. Results from the women's hockey team's opening match are expected later today.
