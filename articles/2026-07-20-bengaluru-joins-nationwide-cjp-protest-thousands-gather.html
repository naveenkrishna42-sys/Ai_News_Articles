Bengaluru joins nationwide CJP protest, thousands gather

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Thousands joined the protest in Bengaluru as part of a nationwide movement against the CJP.
  • Demonstrators marched through key parts of the city, voicing demands for justice.
  • Police maintained a presence but no incidents of violence were reported.

Massive turnout in Bengaluru

Thousands of people in Bengaluru took to the streets on Monday as part of a nationwide protest against the CJP. The demonstration, which began in the morning, saw crowds gather at multiple locations across the city before converging at a central venue.

Protesters carried banners and placards, shouting slogans demanding accountability and justice. The mood was charged but largely peaceful, with participants from diverse backgrounds joining the march.

Route and police presence

The march began at Town Hall and proceeded through M.G. Road before reaching Vidhana Soudha. Traffic was diverted on several major roads as the crowd swelled through the day.

Police personnel were deployed in large numbers to manage the situation. No untoward incidents were reported, and the administration confirmed that the protest remained peaceful throughout.

Demands of the protesters

Protesters listed several demands, including a judicial inquiry into the CJP's actions and immediate policy changes. Many carried handwritten notes listing their grievances against the body.

Speakers at the rally urged the government to take the demands seriously. Organisers claimed the turnout was one of the largest in the city in recent years.

Nationwide resonance

The Bengaluru protest was part of a larger movement that saw demonstrations in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, and Chennai. In each city, crowds of similar size gathered to voice their opposition to the CJP.

Social media was flooded with images and videos from the protests, with hashtags trending nationally. Organisers said the movement would continue until their demands were met.

Officials have not yet commented on the protests. The government is expected to issue a statement in the coming days.

What happens next

Protest leaders have announced plans for a larger rally next week if the government does not respond. The coming days will test whether the administration engages with the demands or the movement intensifies.
