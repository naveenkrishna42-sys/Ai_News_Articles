MakeMyTrip ICICI Credit Card: Full Features & Perks

The MakeMyTrip ICICI Credit Card is designed for frequent travelers, offering generous cashback rewards and exclusive benefits. Cardholders can enjoy 5% cashback on travel bookings made through MakeMyTrip, including flights, hotels, and holiday packages. Additionally, there's a 10% cashback on domestic flights booked through the same platform. This card also provides access to free hotel stays at select properties, making it a valuable option for those who travel frequently.

Joining & Annual Fees

The MakeMyTrip ICICI Credit Card has no joining fee, making it an attractive option for new cardholders. However, there is an annual fee of ₹500, which is standard for most credit cards. This fee is deducted annually and helps maintain the card's benefits and services.

Cashback & Reward Rates

Cardholders can earn 5% cashback on travel bookings made through MakeMyTrip. This includes flights, hotels, and holiday packages. Additionally, there's a 10% cashback on domestic flights booked through the same platform. These rewards are credited to the cardholder's account on a quarterly basis. For other spending categories, the cashback rate is 1%, which is typical for most credit cards.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for the MakeMyTrip ICICI Credit Card, applicants must be Indian citizens aged 21 years or older. They must also have a valid government-issued ID, such as a PAN card or Aadhaar card. The card is available to individuals with a good credit history and a stable income. Applicants must meet ICICI Bank's credit card eligibility criteria, which may include a minimum income requirement.

How to Apply Online

Applying for the MakeMyTrip ICICI Credit Card online is a straightforward process. Visit the ICICI Bank website or use the ICICI Bank mobile app to start the application. Fill in the required personal and financial details, upload the necessary documents, and submit the application. Once approved, the card will be delivered to the applicant's address. Cardholders can also enjoy special welcome rewards and joining cashback bonuses by completing the application process quickly and accurately.
