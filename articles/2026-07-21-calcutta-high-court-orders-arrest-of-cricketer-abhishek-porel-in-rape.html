Calcutta High Court Orders Arrest of Cricketer Abhishek Porel in Rape Case

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Calcutta High Court ordered the immediate arrest of cricketer Abhishek Porel.
  • The case involves allegations of rape on the promise of marriage and blackmail.
  • Police have been directed to seize Porel's electronic devices as part of the investigation.

The Calcutta High Court on Monday ordered the immediate arrest of Delhi Capitals cricketer Abhishek Porel in connection with a rape case. The court also directed the police to seize his electronic devices, including mobile phones and laptops, as part of the investigation.

Allegations of Rape and Blackmail

The case against Porel was filed by a woman who accused him of rape on the promise of marriage. She further alleged that the cricketer blackmailed her using explicit videos and photographs. The woman claimed that Porel had assured her of marriage but later refused to honour the commitment.

According to the complaint, the alleged incidents took place over several months during which Porel is said to have maintained a relationship with the woman under false pretences. The accused has denied the allegations through his legal counsel.

Court's Directive to Police

Justice Jay Sengupta of the Calcutta High Court, while hearing the matter, issued a clear directive to the West Bengal Police to trace and apprehend Porel. The court expressed concern over the delay in the investigation and ordered the police to act promptly.

The investigating officer has been instructed to seize all electronic devices belonging to the cricketer. The court noted that such devices could contain crucial evidence related to the blackmail allegations. The police have also been asked to file a compliance report within a week.

Porel, a young wicketkeeper-batsman, has played for the Delhi Capitals in the Indian Premier League and has also represented Bengal in domestic cricket. The development comes as a shock to the cricketing fraternity, with many awaiting further details of the case.

Legal Proceedings and Next Steps

The case was initially lodged at a police station in Kolkata before being escalated to the High Court. The woman's legal team argued that despite repeated complaints, the police had not taken effective action against Porel. This prompted the court to intervene and issue the arrest order.

Porel's lawyers have not yet made a public statement regarding the court's order. However, sources close to the cricketer indicate that they may challenge the directive in a higher court.

The police are now expected to launch a manhunt for the cricketer, who is believed to be currently in an undisclosed location. Authorities have been directed to coordinate with other states to ensure his arrest.

As the case unfolds, all eyes will be on how the investigation proceeds and whether the electronic devices yield evidence that corroborates the allegations. The court has made it clear that it will monitor the progress closely.
