Dilip Joshi shuts down Taarak Mehta exit rumours, calls for fact-checking

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Dilip Joshi has publicly denied rumours of his exit from Taarak Mehta Ka Ooltah Chashmah.
  • He criticised media houses for publishing 'fabricated stories' without verification.
  • Joshi urged fans and reporters to 'verify the facts' before sharing such reports.
  • The denial comes on the show's 18th anniversary, a milestone for the long-running sitcom.
  • The actor remains committed to his role as Jethalal, with no official confirmation of any cast changes.

Joshi breaks silence on exit rumours

Dilip Joshi, the actor who plays Jethalal in the long-running sitcom Taarak Mehta Ka Ooltah Chashmah, has finally addressed swirling rumours about his departure from the show. In a statement to the media on Friday, he dismissed the reports as baseless and accused certain outlets of publishing 'fabricated stories'.

Joshi said he was disappointed by the lack of fact-checking. 'People publish fabricated stories. Please verify the facts before publishing,' he urged, making it clear he had no plans to leave the iconic role he has played for nearly two decades.

18th anniversary milestone

The actor's clarification came on a significant day for the show — its 18th anniversary. The sitcom, which airs on Sony SAB, has been a staple of Indian television since 2008. Joshi's character, the bumbling yet lovable Jethalal Champaklal Gada, remains the heart of the series.

Industry insiders noted that the timing of the rumours was odd, given the anniversary celebrations. Joshi's swift rebuttal suggests he is keen to put an end to speculation that could unsettle the show's loyal fanbase.

Media houses called out

Joshi specifically called out media organisations that had run with the story without cross-checking. The Times of India and IBTimes India had both reported that Joshi was 'quitting' the show, citing unnamed sources. Moneycontrol.com also carried a version of the story, linking his alleged exit to the show's anniversary.

In his statement, Joshi said it was 'disappointing' that journalists did not reach out to him or his team for confirmation. The actor has rarely commented on rumours in the past, making this public intervention notable.

No official word on cast changes

Neither the show's production house, Neela Film Productions, nor Sony SAB has issued any official statement regarding cast changes. Joshi's denial stands as the only official word on the matter. The actor remains under contract and continues to shoot for upcoming episodes.

Fans had taken to social media in recent weeks, expressing concern after several unconfirmed reports claimed Joshi was considering a break. Some speculated that the show might undergo a revamp after 18 years, but no such plans have been announced.

What happens next

With Joshi's denial, the rumour is expected to die down. The actor has made it clear he intends to continue as Jethalal. However, the episode has highlighted the damage that unverified reporting can do, especially for a show that relies on audience goodwill. For now, viewers can expect business as usual in Gokuldham society.
