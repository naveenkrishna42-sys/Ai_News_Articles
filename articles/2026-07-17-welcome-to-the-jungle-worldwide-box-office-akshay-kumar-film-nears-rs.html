Welcome To The Jungle Worldwide Box Office: Akshay Kumar Film Nears Rs 195 Crore

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Worldwide collection stands at Rs 194.5 crore after 21 days
  • Film has entered Akshay Kumar's top 10 highest-grossing movies
  • Week 3 added Rs 6.50 crore, competing with Dhamaal 4
  • Lifetime target set at Rs 130 crore in India, Rs 200 crore worldwide

Slow But Steady: The Jungle Run Continues

Akshay Kumar's Welcome To The Jungle is proving that slow and steady can still win the race at the box office. In its third week, the comedy sequel added Rs 6.50 crore to its worldwide kitty, pushing the total to Rs 194.5 crore after 21 days.

The film is now inches away from the Rs 195 crore milestone. Trade analysts expect it to cross that mark by the end of the weekend. The movie has already entered Akshay Kumar's top 10 highest-grossing films globally.

Week 3 Performance and Competition

During its third week, Welcome To The Jungle faced stiff competition from the newly released Dhamaal 4. Despite the clash, the film managed to hold its ground in multiplexes and single screens alike.

Single-screen audiences, in particular, have shown strong loyalty to the franchise. The film's comic timing and ensemble cast — which includes Raveena Tandon, Lara Dutta, and Paresh Rawal — have worked well in mass-market circuits.

India vs Overseas Numbers

In India, the film's net collection stands at approximately Rs 115 crore after three weeks. The overseas market has contributed roughly Rs 79.5 crore, with strong showings in the Gulf, North America, and the UK.

The domestic lifetime target is now pegged at Rs 130 crore. If the film sustains its current momentum, a Rs 200 crore worldwide finish is within reach. That would make it one of Akshay Kumar's biggest solo hits in recent years.

How It Compares to Akshay's Past Hits

With Rs 194.5 crore worldwide, Welcome To The Jungle has already surpassed films like Bhool Bhulaiyaa 2 (Rs 185 crore) and Mission Mangal (Rs 190 crore) in global earnings. It is now chasing the Rs 200 crore club, which includes titles like Housefull 4 and Good Newwz.

The film's performance is notable given that it released without a holiday advantage and faced mixed reviews. Industry insiders attribute its longevity to repeat viewership and the franchise's nostalgic pull.

What Lies Ahead

The coming week will be crucial. With no major Hindi releases until the next weekend, Welcome To The Jungle has a clear window to add another Rs 5-7 crore to its total. The Rs 200 crore milestone now looks like a realistic target.

All eyes will be on whether the film can maintain its hold in the fourth week. If it does, Akshay Kumar will have another feather in his cap — and a solid entry in his top 5 highest-grossing films.
