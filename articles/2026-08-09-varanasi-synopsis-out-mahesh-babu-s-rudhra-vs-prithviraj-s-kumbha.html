Varanasi Synopsis Out: Mahesh Babu's Rudhra vs Prithviraj's Kumbha

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • SS Rajamouli unveiled the official synopsis of 'Varanasi' on Mahesh Babu's birthday.
  • Prithviraj Sukumaran plays Kumbha, a tech genius who is the antagonist to Mahesh Babu's Rudhra.
  • The film will be shot entirely in IMAX format, a first for Indian cinema.

Rajamouli's 'Varanasi' Plot Takes Shape

Filmmaker SS Rajamouli has finally pulled back the curtain on his much-anticipated project 'Varanasi', revealing the official synopsis on the occasion of Mahesh Babu's birthday. The announcement has sent waves of excitement through the Indian film industry, confirming the film's central conflict: Mahesh Babu's fierce Rudhra versus Prithviraj Sukumaran's tech genius, Kumbha.

The synopsis paints a picture of a clash that is both physical and intellectual. Rudhra, as the name suggests, embodies raw, untamed power. Kumbha, on the other hand, represents a different kind of threat—one born from technological mastery. Their confrontation forms the core of this ambitious pan-India project.

Prithviraj Sukumaran's Kumbha: A New Kind of Villain

Prithviraj Sukumaran, known for his versatility in Malayalam and Tamil cinema, steps into the role of Kumbha. The synopsis describes him as a tech genius, a character type that is a departure from the conventional mythological or action-heavy villains Rajamouli has previously crafted.

This suggests a more contemporary, cerebral antagonist. The dynamic between Rudhra's primal ferocity and Kumbha's calculated intelligence could offer a fresh narrative tension. Prithviraj's casting has been praised by fans, who are eager to see him pit his acting prowess against Mahesh Babu's star power.

Birthday Stills and a Fierce First Look

Along with the synopsis, Rajamouli also released new stills of Mahesh Babu as Rudhra. The visuals showcase the actor in a rugged, intense avatar, perfectly complementing the title 'Varanasi'. Rajamouli shared the stills with the caption, 'Fierce isn't his only shade', hinting that Rudhra possesses emotional depth beyond his formidable exterior.

The birthday reveal has triggered a massive response on social media, with hashtags like #Varanasi and #MaheshBabu trending. However, not all reactions were positive. Some internet users compared a set of pictures showing Mahesh Babu lounging in Africa to a 'Mountain Dew ad', questioning the aesthetic choices. Despite the mixed reactions, the buzz around the project remains unprecedented.

Shot Entirely on IMAX: A Cinematic First

In a separate announcement, Rajamouli confirmed that 'Varanasi' will be shot entirely on IMAX cameras. This marks a historic first for Indian cinema, as no major production has attempted a full-length IMAX shoot before. The decision underscores the director's ambition to deliver a visually spectacular experience.

IMAX cameras are known for their superior resolution and dynamic range, which could bring the ancient city of Varanasi to life in stunning detail. The scale of the project is expected to be massive, with production likely to span multiple countries and involve a large technical crew. The use of IMAX throughout, rather than select sequences, signals Rajamouli's confidence in the story's visual and narrative scope.

As 'Varanasi' moves into production, the industry will be watching closely. With a star-studded cast, a visionary director, and a groundbreaking technical approach, the film is already being touted as a milestone for Indian cinema. The clash between Rudhra and Kumbha is not just a personal battle, but a test of two opposing worldviews—one rooted in tradition and power, the other in innovation and intellect.

The coming months will likely bring more casting announcements and production updates. Fans will be keen to see how Rajamouli balances the epic scale with the intimate character drama promised by the synopsis. The film's progress will be a key story to track as it heads toward its release.
