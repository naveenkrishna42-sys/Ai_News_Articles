Google sends invites for Pixel 11 series launch in India

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Google has dispatched media invitations for the Pixel 11 series launch event in India.
  • The event is expected to showcase the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, and Pixel Fold.
  • Leaked Amazon listings have allegedly revealed full US pricing and specifications for the lineup.

Google sends out Pixel 11 launch invites in India

Google has begun sending invitations to media for the launch event of its Pixel 11 series in India. The event is expected to take place in the coming weeks, though the company has not yet disclosed the exact date or venue.

The invites arrive amid a flurry of leaks and speculation about the upcoming devices. The Pixel 11 lineup is anticipated to include the standard Pixel 11, the Pixel 11 Pro, and a new foldable model, possibly called the Pixel Fold.

Leaked listings hint at specs and pricing

Earlier this week, several Amazon listings for the Pixel 11 series appeared online before being taken down. These listings allegedly revealed the full US pricing and detailed specifications for the three models.

According to reports from Android Authority, the leaked pages showed storage, RAM, display sizes, and battery capacities. The Pixel 11 is said to start at $799, the Pixel 11 Pro at $999, and the Pixel Fold at $1,799. Google has not confirmed any of these figures.

Design and colour options surface

A gallery published by 9to5Google showcases what are believed to be the official colour options for the Pixel 11 series. The images show a refreshed design language, with subtle changes to the camera bar and rear panel.

The Pixel 11 appears in a new mint green shade, while the Pro variant is shown in a champagne gold finish. The foldable model, if launched, is expected to come in a matte black and a silver option.

What sets the Pixel 11 apart

PhoneArena argues that the Pixel 11 will not try to compete directly with the iPhone 18 or the Galaxy S27 on raw specs. Instead, the outlet claims that Google's strength lies in software optimisation and camera processing.

This approach, often referred to as Google's 'superpower', has historically allowed Pixel devices to deliver a smooth user experience and exceptional photography despite using less powerful hardware than rivals.

Market expectations in India

India remains a key market for Google's Pixel lineup. The company has expanded its retail presence and after-sales support in the country over the past two years, aiming to capture a larger share of the premium smartphone segment.

Industry analysts expect the Pixel 11 series to launch in India at competitive prices, possibly with introductory offers or bundled services like Google One subscriptions. Official pricing and availability details are expected at the event.

All eyes are now on Google's official announcement. The company is expected to confirm the event date soon, and more details on the devices are likely to emerge in the days leading up to the launch.
