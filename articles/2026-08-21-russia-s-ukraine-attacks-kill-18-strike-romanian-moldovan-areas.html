Russia's Ukraine attacks kill 18, strike Romanian, Moldovan areas

📅 2026-08-21 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • At least 18 people killed in Ukraine and neighboring countries
  • Russian strikes on Kyiv and adjacent regions
  • Ukrainian leader calls for international intervention

Russian airstrikes kill 17 in Kyiv, extend to neighboring countries

Russian forces launched a series of airstrikes across Ukraine, killing at least 17 people in Kyiv and injuring dozens more. The attacks also spilled over into Romanian and Moldovan territory, with officials confirming damage but no immediate casualties reported there.

Ukrainian leader calls for global response

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has urged the international community to respond to Russia's overnight strike. He emphasized the need for collective action to support Ukraine's defense against what he described as a coordinated and brutal assault.

Missile and drone barrage targets Kyiv

Reports indicate that the Russian attacks consisted of a barrage of missiles and drones targeting Kyiv. The scale of the assault suggests a deliberate effort to weaken Ukraine's ability to defend itself, with experts noting the increasing sophistication of Russia's military tactics.

Damage and casualties reported in neighboring countries

While the primary focus of the Russian strikes was on Kyiv, damage was also reported in neighboring Romanian and Moldovan regions. Local authorities are assessing the extent of the destruction and any potential casualties, though initial reports suggest the attacks were less severe in these areas.

What happens next?

As the situation continues to unfold, the international community will be closely watching for any further developments. The Ukrainian government is expected to provide more details on the scope of the attacks and the response from its military forces. Meanwhile, Russia is likely to maintain its position, possibly escalating its military actions in the coming days.
