Disha Patani's Rs 6 Crore 'Awarapan 2' Pay: A Milestone in Bollywood's Star Economy

Disha Patani has added another feather to her cap with her Rs 6 crore paycheck for the upcoming 'Awarapan 2'. This marks a significant milestone in her career, reflecting her growing influence and marketability in the Indian film industry.

While her movie earnings are a significant part of her net worth, Disha Patani's brand endorsements have played a crucial role in boosting her financial status. Her association with top brands has not only increased her visibility but also added to her personal wealth.
