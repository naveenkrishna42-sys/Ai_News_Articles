Activist Devendra Mahto's Plea

Activist Devendra Mahto has sought the civil surgeon's nod to return to the protest site. The move comes amid ongoing unrest over recruitment exam irregularities in India. Mahto's plea is currently being considered by the authorities.

Background of the Protest

The protest, which has been ongoing for some time, is a response to the perceived irregularities in the recruitment system. The issue has sparked widespread outrage among the youth, with many taking to the streets to demand action. The Congress has defended the Jharkhand government's handling of the situation, accusing the BJP of shielding the 'exam mafia'.

Other news outlets have reported on related issues, including leaked papers and lost years in India's recruitment system. A video has also been circulating, which some claim shows a prominent figure with the Jharkhand Congress President, although this has been debunked as false.

Recruitment Exam Irregularities

The recruitment exam irregularities have led to a fire at a protest site, highlighting the frustration and anger among the youth. The issue has been the subject of editorials, with some calling for urgent action to address the problem of jobless youth in India.

Political Fallout

The Congress has defended the Jharkhand government's actions, saying that no force was used against the protesters. The party has accused the BJP of shielding the 'exam mafia', which is allegedly behind the recruitment exam irregularities. The BJP has not commented on the issue.
