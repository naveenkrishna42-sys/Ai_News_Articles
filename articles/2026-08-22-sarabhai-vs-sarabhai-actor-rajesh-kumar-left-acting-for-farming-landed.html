Sarabhai vs Sarabhai Actor Rajesh Kumar Left Acting for Farming, Landed in Rs 2 Crore Debt

Key points

  • He accumulated Rs 2 crore in debt due to farming setbacks

From Television Fame to Financial Struggle

Rajesh Kumar, best known for his role as Sahil Sarabhai in the beloved sitcom 'Sarabhai vs Sarabhai', found himself at a crossroads that would alter his life's trajectory. The actor, who won hearts with his comic timing, made a dramatic shift away from the film and television industry.

The decision came at the advice of spiritual leader Jaggi Vasudev, popularly known as Sadhguru. Following this counsel, Rajesh Kumar stepped away from acting and pursued farming, a move that surprised many in the entertainment industry.

The Financial Toll of a Career Change

The transition proved costly. Sources indicate that Rajesh Kumar accumulated a debt of Rs 2 crore following his foray into farming. The agricultural venture, which he took up after leaving his acting career, encountered multiple challenges that resulted in significant financial losses.

The actor, who had built a loyal fanbase through his work in television, found himself navigating unfamiliar territory in rural settings. The series of setbacks that followed his farming efforts contributed to the mounting debt.

Finding Meaning in the Struggle

Despite the financial difficulties, Rajesh Kumar has maintained a philosophical outlook. He has spoken about his journey, referring to his current situation as 'not a failure.' This perspective suggests the actor has found personal growth amid the economic challenges.

His supporters have rallied around him as he continues to work on projects including 'Saiyaara.' The actor's ability to remain positive despite substantial debt has drawn admiration from those who follow his career.

A Cautionary Tale for Industry Professionals

Rajesh Kumar's experience highlights the risks actors face when making dramatic career changes, particularly into sectors far removed from their expertise. The Rs 2 crore debt represents a significant burden that many in the entertainment field would find difficult to recover from.

His journey from a popular television face to a struggling farmer serves as a reminder of the importance of careful financial planning when leaving a stable career, regardless of the source of the advice prompting such a decision.

Looking Ahead

Rajesh Kumar continues to work on 'Saiyaara' while managing his financial obligations. Industry observers will be watching to see whether he can leverage his remaining connections in the entertainment world to recover from this setback.
