Bandhan Bank FD Rates: Earn Up to 7.95% Interest

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Credit Cards & Cashback Original source ↗
Key points
  • Maximum interest rate of 7.95% available for specific tenures.
  • Higher rates for senior citizens across various deposit terms.
  • Flexible tenure options ranging from 7 days to 10 years.

Understanding Bandhan Bank's Latest FD Offerings

Bandhan Bank has adjusted its fixed deposit (FD) interest rates, positioning itself competitively in the current retail banking market. Investors can now secure returns of up to 7.95% per annum depending on the tenure chosen. This rate is particularly attractive for conservative savers looking to lock in yields amidst fluctuating market conditions.

These rates apply to domestic retail fixed deposits. The bank offers a wide range of tenures, starting from just 7 days and extending up to 10 years. By diversifying your deposits across different time horizons, you can maintain liquidity while capturing the higher end of the interest rate spectrum.

Interest Rates and Senior Citizen Perks

The headline rate of 7.95% is generally reserved for specific tenure buckets. Senior citizens typically receive an additional interest rate benefit of 0.50% to 0.75% over the standard rates offered to general customers. This premium makes Bandhan Bank a strong consideration for retirees seeking a steady monthly or quarterly income stream through FD interest payouts.

Interest can be compounded quarterly or paid out monthly, depending on the specific deposit scheme you choose. Selecting the monthly payout option is an effective way to generate a predictable cash flow for household expenses. Always verify the specific tenure bucket that qualifies for the peak 7.95% rate, as these change periodically based on the bank's asset-liability management strategy.

Eligibility and Application Process

Resident individuals, Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs), and sole proprietorship firms are eligible to open these accounts. You will need your PAN card and Aadhaar card to complete the Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements. If you already hold a savings account with Bandhan Bank, the process is streamlined significantly through their net banking portal.

To apply online, follow these steps:

  • Log in to the Bandhan Bank mobile app or internet banking portal.
  • Navigate to the 'Invest' or 'Fixed Deposit' section in the main menu.
  • Enter the amount you wish to invest and select your preferred tenure.
  • Review the interest rate displayed for your selected tenure and submit the application.
  • The funds will be debited from your linked savings account instantly, and your digital FD advice will be generated.

Strategic Considerations for Savers

Before locking your capital, assess your immediate liquidity needs. Fixed deposits are subject to premature withdrawal penalties, which can reduce your effective yield. If you anticipate needing cash within the next six months, consider splitting your corpus into multiple smaller FDs. This 'laddering' strategy ensures that a portion of your money matures every few months, providing both interest income and access to funds.

Bandhan Bank also offers nomination facilities, which are essential for estate planning. Ensure you register a nominee during the digital account opening process to avoid legal hurdles for your beneficiaries. Tax on FD interest is deducted at source (TDS) if your annual interest income exceeds the prescribed limits set by the Income Tax Department. You may submit Form 15G or 15H if your total income is below the taxable threshold.

This fixed deposit offer is best suited for risk-averse savers who prioritize capital protection and guaranteed returns over market-linked instruments. It is an ideal parking spot for emergency funds or short-term savings goals where you cannot afford any loss of principal.
