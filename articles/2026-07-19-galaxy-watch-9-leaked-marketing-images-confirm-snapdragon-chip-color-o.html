Galaxy Watch 9 leaked marketing images confirm Snapdragon chip, color options

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Leaked images show Galaxy Watch 9 in multiple color options.
  • Device confirmed to run on Snapdragon Wear Elite chipset.
  • Marketing materials hint at AI-powered health features.
  • Expected launch at Samsung Unpacked event.
  • Leak sourced from SamMobile and corroborated by multiple tech outlets.

Samsung's Galaxy Watch 9 has surfaced in a series of leaked marketing images, giving potential buyers their clearest look yet at the upcoming smartwatch. The images, first reported by SamMobile and later corroborated by 9to5Google and PhoneArena, show the device in several color variants and confirm a major chip upgrade.

The leaks come just weeks before Samsung's Unpacked event, where the company is expected to unveil its next generation of wearables alongside the Galaxy Z Fold 6 and Z Flip 6.

Snapdragon Wear Elite confirmed

The most significant takeaway from the marketing materials is the processor. The Galaxy Watch 9 will be 'Powered by Snapdragon Wear Elite,' according to the leaked images. This marks a shift from Samsung's previous reliance on its own Exynos chips, which powered the Galaxy Watch 6 and Watch 7 series.

Qualcomm's Snapdragon Wear Elite platform is designed to deliver better performance and power efficiency. For users, this could translate to smoother app navigation and longer battery life between charges.

Color options and design

Marketing images show the Galaxy Watch 9 in at least four finishes: a dark graphite, a silver variant, a rose gold option, and a cream or beige shade. The renders indicate a familiar circular dial with a rotating bezel, though the bezel appears slightly thinner than previous models.

The watch bands shown in the leaks are color-matched to the case, a trend Samsung has followed in recent flagship watches. The overall silhouette remains consistent with the Galaxy Watch 8, suggesting Samsung is focusing on internal upgrades rather than a radical redesign.

AI-powered health features take centre stage

Samsung's own newsroom has been teasing what it calls an 'AI-powered health companion on your wrist.' The leaked materials reinforce this messaging, with slogans emphasising personalised wellness tracking and smarter coaching.

Details on specific sensors remain scarce, but the Snapdragon Wear Elite chip includes an upgraded neural processing unit. This could enable on-device AI for sleep analysis, stress detection, and workout form feedback without needing a phone connection.

Launch timeline and what to expect

Samsung is expected to unveil the Galaxy Watch 9 at its Unpacked event later this month. Alongside the watch, the company is likely to announce the Galaxy Watch 9 Pro or Classic variant, though the leaked images only show the standard model.

Pricing for the Galaxy Watch 9 has not been confirmed, but industry watchers expect it to remain in line with the Watch 8's launch price, starting around Rs 30,000 for the base model. Pre-orders could open immediately after the event.

With the Snapdragon Wear Elite chip and a renewed focus on AI health features, Samsung is positioning the Galaxy Watch 9 as a direct competitor to the Apple Watch Series 10. All eyes will be on Unpacked to see if the leaked specs hold up to real-world performance.
