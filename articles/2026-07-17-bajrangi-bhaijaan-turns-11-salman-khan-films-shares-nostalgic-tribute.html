Bajrangi Bhaijaan Turns 11: Salman Khan Films Shares Nostalgic Tribute

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Salman Khan Films shared a nostalgic video on social media to mark 11 years of Bajrangi Bhaijaan.
  • The post highlights key emotional moments from the film, including the iconic 'Bhangda Paale' sequence.
  • Fans and industry colleagues have flooded the post with tributes, recalling the film's cross-border theme.
  • Bajrangi Bhaijaan, directed by Kabir Khan, remains one of Salman Khan's most acclaimed and commercially successful films.
  • The film's storyline about reuniting a mute Pakistani girl with her family continues to resonate with audiences.

Eleven years of Pavan and Munni

Salman Khan Films on Friday took a trip down memory lane, sharing a nostalgic tribute as Kabir Khan's Bajrangi Bhaijaan completed 11 years since its release. The production house posted a video montage on social media, featuring key moments from the film that turned into cultural touchstones. The clip includes the now-iconic scene where Salman's character, Pavan Chaturvedi, dances to 'Bhangda Paale' with Harshaali Malhotra's Shahida, and the emotional climax at the Wagah border.

The post simply read, '11 years of love, laughter, and the bond that crossed borders. Thank you for keeping Bajrangi Bhaijaan in your hearts.' Within hours, the post garnered lakhs of likes and thousands of comments from fans who called the film 'timeless' and 'a true masterpiece'.

A film that defied box office norms

Released in 2015, Bajrangi Bhaijaan was not just a commercial juggernaut but also a critical darling. It earned over Rs 900 crore worldwide, becoming one of the highest-grossing Indian films at the time. The film's premise — a devout Hindu man helping a mute Pakistani girl reunite with her family — struck a chord across the border too. It was a rare Bollywood film that performed strongly in both India and Pakistan, despite the tense political climate.

Trade analysts note that the film's success redefined the template for Salman Khan's Eid releases. It proved that a story-driven narrative, paired with the star's mass appeal, could outperform the high-octane action films he was known for. The film also earned Salman his first National Film Award nomination for Best Actor.

Kabir Khan's directorial triumph

Director Kabir Khan, who had previously worked with Salman on Ek Tha Tiger, crafted a film that balanced humour, emotion, and social commentary. The screenplay, co-written by Khan and Parveez Shaikh, avoided jingoism and instead focused on human connections. The supporting cast — including Nawazuddin Siddiqui as a Pakistani journalist, Kareena Kapoor Khan as Pavan's love interest, and Harshaali Malhotra in her debut role — received widespread acclaim.

The film's music, composed by the trio of Shankar-Ehsaan-Loy, also became a chartbuster. Songs like 'Selfie Le Le Re' and 'Bhar Do Jholi Meri' remain staples at weddings and religious events, adding to the film's lasting cultural footprint.

Fan nostalgia and industry echoes

On the 11th anniversary, fans shared their own stories of how the film moved them. Many recalled watching it with family, while others highlighted the scene at the Wagah border — where Pavan carries Shahida across the gate — as one of the most powerful in modern Indian cinema. Several actors and filmmakers also joined the tribute, with Kabir Khan reposting the video and writing, 'Grateful for the journey. This one will always be special.'

The film's legacy has also inspired conversations around cross-border storytelling. In an era of heightened nationalism, Bajrangi Bhaijaan remains a rare example of cinema that builds bridges rather than walls. Analysts believe it paved the way for later films like Mulk and Article 15, which tackled social issues with nuance.

As the anniversary celebrations continue, fans are already speculating about a sequel. While neither Salman nor Kabir has confirmed any plans, the enduring love for the film suggests that audiences would welcome a return to Pavan and Munni's world.
