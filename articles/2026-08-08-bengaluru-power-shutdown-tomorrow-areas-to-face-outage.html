Bengaluru power shutdown tomorrow: Areas to face outage

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Power disruption scheduled in multiple Bengaluru areas tomorrow
  • Outage due to maintenance work by electricity utility
  • Residents advised to plan accordingly

Several parts of Bengaluru will experience power disruption tomorrow as the electricity utility carries out scheduled maintenance work. The outage is expected to affect residential and commercial pockets across the city, with officials urging residents to plan their day accordingly.

Areas affected

The utility has not yet released the complete list of affected localities, but sources indicate that multiple zones will be impacted during the maintenance window. Residents in the affected belts should expect supply to be interrupted for a few hours, though exact timings are yet to be confirmed.

Officials said the shutdown is part of routine upkeep aimed at strengthening the local power infrastructure. Such exercises are carried out periodically to ensure grid stability and prevent unplanned failures during peak demand.

What residents should know

Those living or working in the affected areas are advised to keep essential electronic devices charged and store water in advance, as pumping systems may not function during the outage. Businesses that depend on continuous power, such as IT offices and medical facilities, have been asked to make necessary arrangements.

The utility has not specified whether the disruption will affect the entire day or only a few hours. Commuters and office-goers are encouraged to check official channels for updates before stepping out.

Previous maintenance patterns

Bengaluru has witnessed similar scheduled shutdowns in the past, with the utility typically notifying residents a day in advance. While such outages are usually brief, delays in restoration have occasionally been reported, drawing complaints from citizens.

Officials have assured that crews will be deployed to complete the work swiftly and restore supply at the earliest. They have also requested cooperation from the public during the maintenance period.

How to stay updated

Residents can reach out to the utility's customer care helpline for area-specific details. Local ward offices and apartment associations are also expected to circulate notices once the final schedule is confirmed.

The utility has not yet confirmed whether the disruption will be staggered across zones or simultaneous. More clarity is expected later today.

Tomorrow's shutdown comes amid growing demand for electricity in the city, and officials have reiterated that such maintenance is essential to avoid larger breakdowns in the long run.

As Bengaluru continues to expand, infrastructure upkeep remains a key priority for the utility. Residents are advised to stay informed and make the necessary preparations to minimise inconvenience.

The exact list of affected areas and timings is expected to be released shortly, and updates will follow as officials confirm details.
