Who Should Buy This Deal

Travelers planning a Vietnam itinerary who want to save on mid‑range activities—such as cooking classes, cultural shows, or day trips—will find this promotion especially useful, as the 5% can reach the ₹288 cap without needing a large upfront spend. Families booking multiple tickets for the same attraction can also benefit, as the discount applies to the total cart value. Conversely, users seeking luxury or high‑ticket items (e.g., private yacht charters or multi‑day luxury tours) may see the savings capped at ₹288, making the proportional benefit smaller; in those cases, waiting for a site‑wide sale or a higher‑percentage coupon could yield better value.
