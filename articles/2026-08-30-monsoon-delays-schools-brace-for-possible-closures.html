Monsoon disruption threatens school schedules

As India braces for a delayed monsoon, school authorities across the country are on high alert. The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast heavy rainfall on August 31, 2026, prompting concerns about school closures in several states. The economic impact of such disruptions, particularly on students and parents, cannot be overlooked.

State-wise updates reveal mixed responses

Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, and Bihar are among the states expecting school closures. The IMD's prediction has led to proactive measures in these regions. However, Uttarakhand has taken a different approach, with some districts like Dehradun and Chamoli already closed.
