Apple’s iPhone Duo, 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max 3D Models Leak: What Buyers Need to Know

✍️ By TIVRA News Tech & Gadgets Desk 📅 2026-09-16 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • The iPhone Duo features a 7.8‑inch inner flexible OLED and a 5.4‑inch cover screen with a dual‑cell 4,500 mAh battery.
  • iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max retain the A19 Bionic chip but differ in display size, battery capacity and storage options.
  • Leaked India‑specific pricing places the Duo at ₹1,49,900, the 18 Pro at ₹1,19,900 and the 18 Pro Max at ₹1,39,900.

Overview of the Leaked 3D Models

Apple’s internal CAD database has recently been accessed, exposing three‑dimensional models of the forthcoming iPhone Duo, iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. The files, shared by a reliable supply‑chain source, provide exact dimensions, component placement and design language that align with earlier rumours from Bloomberg and CNBC. For prospective buyers, these leaks move beyond speculative renders and offer a concrete basis for evaluating form factor, ergonomics and potential accessory compatibility.

Technical Specifications from CAD Files

Display and Form Factor

The iPhone Duo adopts a book‑style foldable layout. When opened, it reveals a 7.8‑inch flexible OLED panel with a resolution of 2048×1536 pixels. The outer cover uses a 5.4‑inch OLED screen at 1080×2340 pixels, allowing basic interactions without unfolding. The hinge mechanism appears to be a dual‑axis design that minimizes crease visibility. In contrast, the iPhone 18 Pro sports a 6.1‑inch LTPO OLED (1179×2556) while the 18 Pro Max steps up to a 6.7‑inch LTPO OLED (1290×2796), both supporting ProMotion adaptive refresh rates up to 120 Hz.

Camera and Performance

All three devices are expected to house the A19 Bionic chip, fabricated on TSMC’s 3 nm process. The Duo’s camera array comprises a 48 MP main sensor, a 12 MP ultra‑wide and a 12 MP telephoto with 3× optical zoom, arranged on the rear panel when folded. The 18 Pro retains a triple‑lens setup similar to the iPhone 15 Pro series, while the 18 Pro Max adds a larger sensor‑shift stabilization unit for the main camera. RAM allocations differ: the Duo is listed with 12 GB, whereas both Pro models show 8 GB. Storage options range from 128 GB to 1 TB across the line‑up, with the Duo starting at 256 GB.

Market Implications and Pricing Context

Analysts note that the Duo’s pricing at approximately ₹1,49,900 positions it above the current iPhone 15 Pro Max but below competing foldables such as the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 5, which retails near ₹1,65,000 in India. The 18 Pro’s ₹1,19,900 tag matches the launch price of the iPhone 15 Pro, suggesting Apple may keep the Pro line’s price steady while introducing the Max variant at a modest premium. These figures, derived from leaked internal pricing sheets, should be treated as indicative until Apple’s official announcement.

Buyer Considerations: Pros and Cons

  • Pros: The Duo offers a large unfolded screen for multitasking and media consumption while retaining a pocket‑friendly footprint when folded; the A19 Bionic promises industry‑leading performance and efficiency; ProMotion displays on all models ensure smooth scrolling and gaming.
  • Cons: Foldable designs typically incur higher repair costs and may raise durability concerns despite improved hinge engineering; the Duo’s battery capacity, while respectable, may still fall short of heavy‑usage expectations compared to the 18 Pro Max’s larger cell; premium pricing could deter budget‑conscious upgraders.
