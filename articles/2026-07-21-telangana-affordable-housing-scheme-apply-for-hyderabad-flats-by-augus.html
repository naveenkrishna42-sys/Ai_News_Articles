Telangana affordable housing scheme: Apply for Hyderabad flats by August 10

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Application window for affordable flats in Hyderabad is now active.
  • Deadline for submitting applications is August 10.
  • Scheme is part of Telangana government's housing initiative for low-income groups.

Telangana opens applications for affordable housing in Hyderabad

The Telangana government has formally opened the application window for its affordable flat scheme in Hyderabad. Eligible residents can now apply for the flats, which are part of a larger push to provide housing for lower-income groups in the state capital. The last date to submit applications is August 10.

Officials have not yet released the exact number of flats available or the specific locations within Hyderabad. The scheme is expected to cover multiple areas across the city, targeting families that meet the income criteria set by the state housing department.

Who can apply

The scheme is designed for low and middle-income households. Applicants must meet specific annual income thresholds, which the government has detailed in the official guidelines. Preference is likely given to residents who have been living in Hyderabad for a minimum period, though full eligibility criteria are still being circulated.

Interested individuals can apply online through the official housing department portal. The application form requires basic personal details, income proof, and address verification. No offline applications are being accepted.

Scheme background and timeline

This affordable housing initiative is part of the Telangana government's broader commitment to provide shelter for all. The state has been working on multiple housing projects across Hyderabad, with a focus on reducing the urban housing shortage. The current application window is one of several phases planned for the year.

After the August 10 deadline, authorities will begin the verification process. Successful applicants will be notified through the same online portal. The government has not yet announced when the flats will be allotted or when possession will be given.

What applicants should know

Applicants are advised to keep all necessary documents ready before starting the application. Incomplete or incorrect submissions are likely to be rejected. The government has not specified a fee for applying, but applicants should check the portal for any charges.

Officials have warned against fraud. The entire application process is online, and no middlemen or agents are authorised to help with applications. Any claims of guaranteed allotment should be reported to the housing department.

The response from the public has been significant since the window opened. Many residents see this as a rare opportunity to own a home in Hyderabad at a subsidised rate. The government expects a high volume of applications before the deadline.

The next steps will involve scrutiny of applications and a lottery-based allocation system if the number of applicants exceeds available flats. The Telangana housing department is expected to release a timeline for these processes after the application window closes.
