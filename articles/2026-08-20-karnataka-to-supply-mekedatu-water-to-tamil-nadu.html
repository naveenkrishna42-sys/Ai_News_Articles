Karnataka to supply Mekedatu water to Tamil Nadu

Karnataka Chief Minister D K Shivakumar has announced that water from the newly constructed Mekedatu dam will be supplied to Tamil Nadu, except for 5 TMC (thousand million cubic feet) of water that will be reserved for drinking purposes in the state. The announcement was made during a meeting with Union Home Minister Amit Shah.

Karnataka ready to supply all water from Mullaperiyar dam to Tamil Nadu

Karnataka's Minister for Water Resources, V D Satheesan, has stated that the state is ready to supply all the water from the Mullaperiyar dam to Tamil Nadu. The Mullaperiyar dam is a major source of water for Tamil Nadu, and the decision is expected to further strain the already tense Cauvery river dispute between the two states.

Tamil Nadu CM Vijay discusses delimitation, NEET and Cauvery issues with Amit Shah

Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Vijay discussed issues related to delimitation, the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET), and the Cauvery river dispute with Union Home Minister Amit Shah. The meeting is expected to have far-reaching implications for the future of the Cauvery river dispute and other issues affecting the southern states.

Southern Zonal Council meeting: T'gana Dy CM seeks protection of state's rights in Krishna waters

During a meeting of the Southern Zonal Council, the Deputy Chief Minister of Telangana sought the protection of the state's rights in the Krishna river waters. The meeting is expected to discuss various issues affecting the southern states, including water disputes and economic cooperation.

Southern economy could reach $10 trillion by 2047, says Naidu, seeks more cooperation

Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N Chandrababu Naidu has stated that the southern economy could reach $10 trillion by 2047. He urged for more cooperation among the southern states to achieve this goal. The statement is expected to spark discussions on economic cooperation and development in the region.
