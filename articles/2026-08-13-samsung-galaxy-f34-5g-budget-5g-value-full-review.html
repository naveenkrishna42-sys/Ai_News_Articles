Samsung Galaxy F34 5G: Budget 5G Value? Full Review

📅 2026-08-13 📂 Gadget Comparisons Original source ↗
Key points
  • Launched in India at a competitive sub-Rs 20,000 price point.
  • Features a 120Hz AMOLED display, a standout spec in this segment.
  • 6000mAh battery provides exceptional longevity.
  • Exynos 1280 performance is adequate for daily use but not for heavy gaming.
  • Camera is decent in daylight but below par in low light.

The Galaxy F34 5G has just launched in India, marking Samsung's latest salvo in the competitive budget 5G segment. With a focus on display quality and battery life, it's a timely contender for shoppers seeking a reliable daily driver without breaking the bank.

What reviewers generally say: Early reviews generally praise the F34 5G for its standout 120Hz AMOLED display and long-lasting battery, but some critics note the Exynos 1280 chipset is only average for the price, and low-light camera performance is a weak point.

Pros
  • 120Hz Super AMOLED display is rare at this price point, offering smooth scrolling and vibrant colors.
  • 6000mAh battery easily lasts a full day and a half for most users, reducing charging anxiety.
  • Clean and bloat-free One UI experience with a promise of two major Android updates.
Cons
  • Exynos 1280 processor is not as powerful as competing Snapdragon chips, so heavy gaming may stutter.
  • Camera struggles in low light, producing soft and noisy images compared to rivals like the Redmi Note 12 series.
  • Plastic build feels less premium, and there's no official IP rating for water resistance.

The Samsung Galaxy F34 5G is a compelling choice if your priorities are a buttery-smooth display and marathon battery life on a budget. For everyday tasks, social media, and media consumption, it delivers excellent value for its price tier.

However, if you're a mobile gamer or a photography enthusiast, you'll find better performance and camera quality from competitors like the Redmi Note 12 or iQOO Z7. The F34 5G is best for students, first-time smartphone buyers, or anyone who values screen quality and endurance over raw speed.
