Israel believes Iran moved nuclear centrifuges to Pickaxe Mountain site

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Israel believes Iran relocated nuclear centrifuges to a secret underground facility at Pickaxe Mountain.
  • The move is seen as an attempt to shield sensitive nuclear equipment from potential airstrikes.
  • The report comes amid heightened US-Iran tensions, with Trump stating the US has no interest in talks unless Tehran engages meaningfully.

Secret transfer to fortified mountain facility

Israel has assessed that Iran moved a significant number of uranium-enrichment centrifuges to a deep underground facility inside Pickaxe Mountain, according to a report by The Wall Street Journal. The intelligence suggests Tehran is trying to shield its nuclear equipment from potential military strikes.

The mountain site, located in central Iran, is believed to be fortified against aerial bombardment. Israeli officials have not disclosed how they obtained this information. The transfer likely occurred over several weeks, using covert logistics to avoid detection.

Iran has not commented on the report. Its nuclear program has long been a source of friction with Western nations.

US-Iran tensions escalate

The developments come as US President Donald Trump said Iran is 'desperate' for negotiations but that Washington has no interest unless Tehran engages 'meaningfully'. The remarks were made during a briefing on the ongoing US-Iran tensions, which have seen heightened military posturing in the region.

Trump's administration has maintained a policy of maximum pressure on Iran, including sanctions and threats of military action. The latest intelligence could further complicate any potential diplomatic openings.

Iran has repeatedly denied seeking nuclear weapons, insisting its program is for peaceful purposes. However, its enrichment activities have exceeded limits set by the 2015 nuclear deal, which the US abandoned in 2018.

Strategic implications for the region

The relocation of centrifuges to Pickaxe Mountain represents a tactical shift for Iran. The site's depth and geological features make it harder to destroy with conventional bombs. This could limit the effectiveness of any preemptive Israeli or American strikes.

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has long warned about Iran's nuclear ambitions. In recent speeches, he has called for a 'credible military threat' to deter Tehran. The new intelligence may reinforce Israel's push for more aggressive action.

Experts caution that the move could also be a signal of Iran's willingness to escalate, rather than negotiate. The standoff remains one of the most volatile flashpoints in the Middle East.

What to watch

The IAEA is expected to release its next quarterly report on Iran's nuclear activities in the coming weeks. Any confirmation of the centrifuge transfer could trigger fresh diplomatic efforts or further sanctions. The world will be watching for Iran's next move.
