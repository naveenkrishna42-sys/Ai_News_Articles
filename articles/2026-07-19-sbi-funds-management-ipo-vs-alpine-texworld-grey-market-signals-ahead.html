SBI Funds Management IPO vs Alpine Texworld: Grey Market Signals Ahead of Listings

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • SBI Funds Management IPO is the fourth-most-bid IPO in India, drawing $31 billion in bids.
  • Grey market premium for SBI Funds Management IPO is strong, indicating high listing gains.
  • Alpine Texworld IPO has seen muted grey market activity, suggesting a subdued listing.
  • SBI Funds Management IPO allotment status will be available soon; investors can check on BSE and registrar site.
  • Multiple IPOs are opening for subscription next week, keeping investors busy.

The grey market is sending mixed signals for two major IPOs scheduled for listing this week. SBI Funds Management IPO, which drew a staggering $31 billion in bids — making it the fourth-most-bid IPO in India — is commanding a healthy grey market premium (GMP). In contrast, the Alpine Texworld IPO has seen muted interest in the unofficial market.

SBI Funds Management IPO: Strong Grey Market Demand

SBI Funds Management IPO, the asset management arm of State Bank of India, saw overwhelming demand during its subscription period. The issue was subscribed multiple times across all investor categories, with qualified institutional buyers leading the charge.

In the grey market, the stock is trading at a significant premium to its issue price. While the exact GMP fluctuates daily, market sources indicate that the premium has remained in the double digits, reflecting strong investor confidence. The IPO's massive $31 billion bid amount — the fourth-highest in Indian IPO history — has set the stage for a strong listing.

Investors who applied for the IPO are now awaiting the allotment status. The allotment is expected to be finalised soon, and applicants can check the status on the BSE website or the registrar's portal. The listing is scheduled for later this week.

Alpine Texworld IPO: Muted Grey Market Activity

Alpine Texworld IPO, a textile company, has not generated the same level of excitement. The grey market premium for this issue remains negligible, indicating a likely flat or subdued listing.

The company's IPO was smaller in size compared to SBI Funds Management, and the response from investors was modest. Market experts attribute the lack of grey market buzz to the company's niche sector and relatively lower growth visibility. Retail investors, in particular, have shown limited interest.

Alpine Texworld's allotment status will also be released alongside SBI Funds Management. Given the current GMP, analysts expect the listing to be close to the issue price, with limited upside on day one.

What the Grey Market Signals Mean

The grey market premium is an unofficial indicator of investor sentiment ahead of listing. A high GMP suggests strong demand and potential listing gains, while a low or negative GMP indicates weak interest.

For SBI Funds Management, the strong GMP aligns with the IPO's massive subscription numbers. The company's brand value, parent backing from SBI, and the growing asset management industry in India have all contributed to the positive sentiment.

For Alpine Texworld, the muted GMP reflects the challenges in the textile sector and the company's smaller scale. Investors who have been allotted shares may see minimal gains or even a slight loss on listing, depending on market conditions.

What to Watch Next

With SBI Funds Management and Alpine Texworld set to list this week, all eyes will be on the listing day performance. The SBI Funds Management IPO is expected to deliver strong returns, while Alpine Texworld may struggle to maintain its issue price.

Meanwhile, the IPO calendar remains busy. Several companies, including those in the fintech and manufacturing sectors, are opening for subscription next week. Investors should watch for the allotment status and listing dates of both IPOs to plan their next moves. The grey market will continue to provide real-time cues on sentiment.
