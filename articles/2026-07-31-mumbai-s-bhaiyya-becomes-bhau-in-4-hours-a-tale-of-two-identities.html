The Curious Case of a Name Change

In a city where a person's identity can shift as quickly as the local trains, a peculiar incident has caught the attention of Mumbaikars. A man, initially addressed as 'Bhaiyya'—a term often used for north Indians—was suddenly called 'Bhau', the Marathi equivalent for brother, within a span of just four hours. The transformation, which took place in a crowded neighbourhood, has left many amused and intrigued.

The incident unfolded on a typical weekday, when the man, whose name has not been revealed, was first approached by a street vendor. The vendor, assuming the man was from Uttar Pradesh or Bihar, greeted him with a warm 'Bhaiyya'. Hours later, the same man was addressed as 'Bhau' by a local shopkeeper, who presumed he was a Maharashtrian.

What Triggered the Shift?

According to eyewitnesses, the change occurred after the man engaged in a lengthy conversation in fluent Marathi with a group of locals. His impeccable accent and knowledge of local customs convinced them he was a native. This linguistic switch, though subtle, was enough to alter how he was perceived.

Social observers point out that such incidents are not uncommon in Mumbai, a melting pot of cultures.
