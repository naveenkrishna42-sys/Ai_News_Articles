Chembur tree-fall: Clean chit to BMC departments, contractors face action

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • BMC gives clean chit to Garden and Roads departments for Chembur tree-fall.
  • Contractors responsible for tree maintenance to be penalised.
  • Incident had resulted in casualties and raised safety questions.

BMC Clears Its Departments

The Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) has given a clean chit to its Garden and Roads departments in connection with the Chembur tree-fall incident. A preliminary inquiry found no negligence on the part of these civic wings.

The incident, which occurred earlier this month, had resulted in casualties and sparked outrage among residents. Many had questioned the maintenance of trees along city roads.

Contractors to Face Penalties

While the municipal departments have been absolved, the BMC has decided to penalise the contractors who were responsible for tree maintenance. Officials said the contractors failed to ensure the health and stability of the tree that collapsed.

The exact nature of the penalty has not been disclosed. Sources indicate it could involve financial fines and possible blacklisting from future civic contracts.

Inquiry Details

The BMC's internal probe examined maintenance records and inspection logs. It concluded that the Garden department had flagged the tree as a potential risk, but the contractor did not act on the warning in time.

The Roads department was also given a clean chit, as it was not directly involved in tree upkeep. The civic body has now asked all contractors to submit fresh health reports for trees in their zones.

Residents Express Mixed Reactions

Local residents have welcomed the decision to penalise contractors but remain wary. Some said the clean chit to departments could lead to a lack of accountability in future incidents.

Others argued that the BMC should have taken stricter action against its own officials to ensure better oversight. The incident had led to protests in Chembur, with demands for a thorough audit of all trees in the area.

What Happens Next

The BMC is expected to issue a formal order detailing the penalties for the contractors in the coming days. It has also announced a city-wide tree census to identify vulnerable trees before the monsoon season.
