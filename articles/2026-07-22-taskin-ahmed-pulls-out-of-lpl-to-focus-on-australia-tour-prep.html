Taskin Ahmed pulls out of LPL to focus on Australia tour prep

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Taskin Ahmed has withdrawn from the Lanka Premier League.
  • The decision is to focus on preparation for Bangladesh's tour of Australia.
  • The tour includes Test and ODI matches later this year.

Bangladesh fast bowler Taskin Ahmed has withdrawn from the upcoming Lanka Premier League (LPL) as he shifts focus entirely to the national team's tour of Australia later this year. The decision, confirmed by sources close to the player, underscores the importance of the assignment Down Under.

Priority shift for the pacer

Taskin, known for his pace and bounce, was expected to feature in the LPL's next season. However, he has opted out to undergo specific training and conditioning programmes tailored for Australian conditions. The move suggests a deliberate plan by both the player and the Bangladesh Cricket Board (BCB) to maximise his effectiveness on fast, bouncy tracks.

The tour of Australia is a major challenge for Bangladesh. Taskin's presence will be critical, especially in the Test series where pace bowlers traditionally hold the key.

Preparing for pace and bounce

Australian pitches offer pace and carry that can trouble subcontinental bowlers. Taskin's decision to skip the LPL gives him time to work on his fitness, line, and length for those conditions. He is expected to bowl long spells in simulated match scenarios during this preparatory phase.

The BCB has not officially commented on the withdrawal, but it is understood that the board supports the pacer's choice. Team management believes that a well-prepared Taskin could be a decisive factor in Australia.

What this means for LPL

Taskin's absence is a blow for the LPL franchise that had signed him. Teams will now have to look for a replacement overseas or promote a local talent. The LPL has gained reputation as a competitive T20 league, but player pullouts ahead of international commitments are not uncommon.

For Bangladesh, this represents a long-term investment. A fully prepared Taskin could trouble Australian batters with his short-pitched deliveries and yorkers.

Bangladesh's tour of Australia

The tour is scheduled to include two Test matches and three One-Day Internationals. Exact dates are yet to be announced. Bangladesh have historically struggled in Australia, but the team is aiming to improve on past performances.

Taskin has been one of Bangladesh's most improved bowlers in recent years. His ability to generate extra bounce makes him a weapon in any conditions, but especially on Australian soil.

The team will depart for Australia a few weeks before the first Test to acclimatise. Taskin's preparation is part of that larger strategy.

What to watch next

All eyes will be on Taskin's fitness and form in the lead-up matches. The BCB is expected to announce a preparatory camp shortly. Whether his gamble of skipping league cricket pays off will be known only when he bowls his first ball in Australia.
