Nifty, Sensex Face Sixth Day of Losses: US Fed, Trade War in Focus

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nifty and Sensex have fallen for five consecutive sessions, losing over 2.3% weekly.
  • Five key factors to watch: US Fed decision, trade tariffs, West Asia tensions, Q1 earnings, and FII outflows.
  • Midcap and smallcap indices have also declined for two straight weeks but outperformed benchmarks.

Markets Brace for Sixth Straight Fall

Indian equity benchmarks, the Nifty and Sensex, are staring at a potential sixth consecutive session of declines on Monday. Both indices have already lost over 2.3 percent on a weekly basis, driven by global inflation fears and renewed trade war anxieties.

The Nifty closed below the key 23,800 level on Friday, a threshold many traders view as critical support. The broader market mood remains cautious, with midcap and smallcap indices extending their losing streak for a second straight week. However, they have outperformed the benchmarks during this period.

Five Factors That Could Drive Markets This Week

Investors are watching five major triggers that could determine the direction of domestic equities over the next few days.

1. US Federal Reserve Policy Stance: The US Fed's commentary on interest rates remains the single biggest global cue. Any hawkish signal could strengthen the dollar and trigger further outflows from emerging markets like India.

2. US Trade Tariffs: Fresh US trade tariffs have already rattled global supply chains. The impact on Indian IT, pharma, and auto firms is being closely monitored by analysts.

3. West Asia Tensions: Escalating conflict in the Gulf region has pushed oil prices higher. India, being a net importer of crude, faces inflationary pressure and a widening current account deficit.

4. Quarterly Earnings Season: A mixed bag of Q1 results from Indian corporates has failed to inspire confidence. Investors are now looking for forward guidance from management teams.

5. Foreign Institutional Investor (FII) Flows: FIIs have been net sellers in recent sessions. A reversal in this trend will be crucial for any sustained recovery in the market.

Nifty Technical Levels Under Pressure

Technical analysts point out that the Nifty's close below 23,800 is a bearish signal. The next support is seen at 23,500, while any bounce could face resistance at the 24,000 mark.

Trading volumes have been elevated, indicating panic selling in some pockets. Options data suggests that put writers are active at lower levels, but call writing at higher strikes caps upside potential.

Broader Market Showing Relative Strength

Despite the benchmark indices bleeding, midcap and smallcap segments have shown resilience. The BSE Midcap index fell 1.8 percent last week, while the BSE Smallcap index slipped 1.5 percent — both less than the Sensex's 2.3 percent drop.

This divergence suggests that domestic retail and institutional investors are still selectively buying into smaller names, even as foreign money exits largecaps.

Market participants will watch Monday's session closely. If the indices fail to hold the 23,500 level on the Nifty, a deeper correction could be on the cards. All eyes remain on the US Fed and global developments for the near-term direction.
