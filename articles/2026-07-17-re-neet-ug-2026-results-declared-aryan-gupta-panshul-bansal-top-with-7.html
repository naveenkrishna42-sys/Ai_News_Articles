Re-NEET UG 2026 Results Declared; Aryan Gupta, Panshul Bansal Top With 715 Marks

📅 2026-07-17 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Re-NEET UG 2026 results declared on neet.nta.nic.in
  • Aryan Gupta (Punjab) and Panshul Bansal (Haryana) top with 715 marks
  • 11.21 lakh candidates qualify; 138 candidates score 690 or above
  • Women outperform men in the re-test

Results Announced After Re-Test

The National Testing Agency (NTA) declared the Re-NEET UG 2026 results on Thursday, July 16. Candidates can check their scores on the official website, neet.nta.nic.in. The re-test was conducted after the original exam faced allegations of irregularities.

Two candidates have topped the examination with identical scores. Aryan Gupta from Punjab and Panshul Bansal from Haryana each secured 715 marks out of 720. Their achievement places them at the top of the merit list for the country's largest medical entrance exam.

Qualifying Numbers and Top Scorers

A total of 11.21 lakh candidates have qualified in the re-test. This is a significant number, reflecting the scale of the examination. Among the top performers, 138 candidates scored 690 marks or above, indicating a highly competitive field.

The NTA has not yet released detailed category-wise cut-offs. However, the high number of top scorers suggests that the cut-off marks for admission to MBBS, BDS, and other medical courses will be elevated this year. Officials are expected to publish the final merit lists in the coming weeks.

Women Outperform Men in Re-Test

In a notable trend, women candidates have outshone their male counterparts in the Re-NEET UG 2026. The overall pass percentage among female candidates is higher than that of male candidates. This continues a pattern seen in recent years, where women have consistently performed better in medical entrance exams.

The exact gender-wise breakdown of qualifying candidates has not been released. However, preliminary data from the NTA indicates that women have secured a larger share of the top ranks as well. This is likely to influence the counselling process and seat allocation.

What Candidates Should Do Next

Candidates who have qualified should download their scorecards from the official website. The scorecard is essential for the counselling process. The NTA will soon announce the schedule for the choice-filling and seat allocation rounds.

Those who have not qualified or are unsatisfied with their scores have no recourse for re-evaluation. The NTA has stated that the re-test results are final. Candidates are advised to check the website regularly for updates on counselling.

Medical colleges across the country will begin the admission process based on these results. The counselling will be conducted by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) for All India Quota seats and by respective state authorities for state quota seats.

The next step for qualified candidates is to register for counselling. They must ensure that all their documents are in order. The competition is expected to be intense, especially for seats in top government medical colleges.
