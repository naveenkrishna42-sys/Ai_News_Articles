IIT Delhi Plainclothes Cop: Students Cry Foul Over Campus Entry During Turbulent Protests

Students Challenge Police Presence on Autonomous Campus

A plainclothes police officer entering IIT Delhi during campus protests has sparked outrage among students who question whether law enforcement had proper authorization to be inside the autonomous institution. Video footage of the officer circulated widely among students, raising concerns about the expanding role of external authorities in campus affairs.

The incident occurred amid heightened tensions following the death of student Rishikesh. Fellow students allege academic pressure contributed to the tragedy, prompting demands for systemic reforms from institute administration.

Director Accepts Accountability But Defends Institute

Ashutosh Ranka, Director of IIT Delhi, addressed the controversy directly, stating he is accountable and responsible for the situation. However, Ranka rejected calls for faculty suspensions, arguing that punitive action against individual teachers would not address root causes.

Students have demanded accountability from faculty members they believe contributed to a toxic academic environment. The Director's refusal to suspend staff has only intensified their frustration.

Mental Health Measures Fall Short, Critics Say

The institute has published a list of measures intended to reduce academic stress, including counseling services and workload adjustments. Yet student activists argue these steps are reactive rather than preventive.

"Announcing reforms after a death is too late," said one graduate student who requested anonymity. "We need structural changes, not press releases."

Critical Questions About Police Access

The presence of a plainclothes officer without apparent university escort raises legal and procedural questions. Educational institutions with autonomous status typically maintain control over external access to campus grounds. Students are now demanding clarity on what protocols authorize police entry.

The video footage has become evidence in ongoing discussions about whether proper procedures were followed. University administration has not confirmed whether they requested police assistance.

Broader Implications for Campus Autonomy

This incident fits a pattern of increasing external intervention in university affairs across India. When law enforcement enters campuses without transparent authorization, it undermines the independence that statutory institutions require to function effectively.

The protest movement at IIT Delhi reflects growing student frustration nationwide with institutional responses to mental health crises. Critics say universities often prioritize reputation over genuine welfare of their community members.

What Comes Next

Students have called for an independent inquiry into both the circumstances of Rishikesh's death and the unauthorized police presence. Whether the institute will agree to external oversight remains uncertain.

The Director's public acknowledgment of responsibility marks a shift in tone, but concrete action will determine whether this moment leads to lasting change or simply another controversy that fades from public attention.
