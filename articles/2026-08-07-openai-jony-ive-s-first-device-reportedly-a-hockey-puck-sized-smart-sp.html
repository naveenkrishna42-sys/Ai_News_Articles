OpenAI, Jony Ive's first device reportedly a hockey puck-sized smart speaker

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • OpenAI's first device with Jony Ive is reportedly a smart speaker
  • Device is hockey puck-sized and priced between $300 and $400
  • Design said to feature a 'living' aesthetic, no official confirmation yet

OpenAI's first hardware with Jony Ive takes shape

OpenAI's much-anticipated first hardware device, developed in collaboration with legendary designer Jony Ive, is reportedly a smart speaker. According to multiple reports, the device is roughly the size of a hockey puck and will carry a price tag between $300 and $400.

The gadget marks a significant step for OpenAI as it moves from software into physical products. Bloomberg, The Verge, and TechCrunch have all cited sources familiar with the project, painting a picture of a compact, AI-first device that could redefine how users interact with ChatGPT in their daily lives.

A 'living' design philosophy

Reports suggest the speaker will feature what insiders describe as a 'living' design. This implies a form factor that adapts or responds to user presence and context, though specific details remain under wraps. The hockey puck shape is a departure from traditional smart speakers, which are often cylindrical or fabric-covered boxes.

The device is expected to rely heavily on ChatGPT's capabilities, potentially offering more advanced conversational interactions than existing assistants like Amazon's Alexa or Google Assistant. Analysts believe the pricing positions it as a premium product, competing directly with high-end smart speakers and possibly Apple's HomePod line.

Jony Ive's role and design legacy

Jony Ive, the former Apple design chief known for the iPhone and MacBook aesthetics, joined forces with OpenAI in 2024. His design studio, LoveFrom, has been working on this project, aiming to create hardware that feels intuitive and human. Ive's involvement has fueled expectations for a device that prioritises minimalism and user experience over raw specs.

The collaboration was first rumoured after Ive stepped back from Apple, with OpenAI CEO Sam Altman confirming the partnership in interviews. The project reportedly began with a focus on how AI could be integrated into everyday objects, and the smart speaker appears to be the first tangible outcome.

Market context and competition

If the reports hold, OpenAI will enter a crowded market dominated by Amazon, Google, and Apple. However, the company's AI prowess could give it an edge. Unlike existing assistants that rely on pre-programmed responses, ChatGPT-based speakers could offer open-ended dialogue, task execution, and even emotional support.

Pricing between $300 and $400 is ambitious. Most smart speakers sell for under $100, with premium models like the HomePod at $299. OpenAI will need to justify the premium with a clearly superior experience. Early testers, according to reports, have praised the device's natural voice interaction and ability to handle complex queries.

Uncertainties remain

Neither OpenAI nor Jony Ive's LoveFrom studio has officially confirmed the device. The reports, while sourced, could change before launch. The project has faced delays, typical for hardware ventures, and no release date has been announced.

Industry watchers are also curious about the device's ecosystem. Will it require a ChatGPT subscription? How will it handle privacy? These questions remain unanswered. OpenAI has not responded to requests for comment, and Ive's studio has maintained silence on the matter.

What to watch next

All eyes will be on OpenAI's upcoming developer conference, where an official reveal is possible. If the device launches as reported, it could signal a new era for AI hardware, blending the conversational power of ChatGPT with the design sensibility of one of the world's most celebrated product designers.
