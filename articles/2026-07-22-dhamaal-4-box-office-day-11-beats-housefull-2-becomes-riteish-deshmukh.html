Dhamaal 4 Box Office Day 11: Beats Housefull 2, Becomes Riteish Deshmukh's 5th Highest-Grossing Film

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Dhamaal 4 collected Rs 3.25 crore on its second Monday, taking its India net to Rs 125 crore.
  • The film has surpassed Housefull 2's lifetime collections.
  • It is now the 5th highest-grossing film in Riteish Deshmukh's career.
  • The comedy franchise is on track to become the highest-grossing entry in the Dhamaal series.

Ajay Devgn's comedy caper Dhamaal 4 continues to hold steady at the box office in its second week. The film collected approximately Rs 3.25 crore on its 11th day (second Monday), pushing its India net collection past the Rs 125 crore mark.

According to early estimates, the film has now crossed the Rs 174 crore mark in worldwide gross collections within 11 days. This performance has allowed it to leapfrog over the lifetime collections of Housefull 2.

A New Milestone for Riteish Deshmukh

The film's strong run has placed it as the fifth highest-grossing film in Riteish Deshmukh's career. The actor, known for his comic timing, now has five films in the Rs 100-crore-plus club.

Dhamaal 4 joins the ranks of his biggest hits, though the top spots remain occupied by films like Total Dhamaal and his other multi-starrer blockbusters. The current run indicates the film could climb higher on that list in the coming days.

Beating the Competition

The film is also showing resilience against new releases. Despite competition from The Odyssey, which has been drawing audiences to theatres, Dhamaal 4 has managed to hold its ground.

Trade analysts point out that the film's family-friendly humour and the star power of the ensemble cast—including Ajay Devgn, Riteish Deshmukh, Sanjay Dutt, Arshad Warsi, and Javed Jaffrey—are keeping the footfalls steady even on weekdays.

Franchise Record in Sight

With the current momentum, Dhamaal 4 is on track to become the highest-grossing film in the Dhamaal franchise. The previous record was held by Total Dhamaal (2019), which had collected around Rs 153 crore net in India.

Given that the film has already crossed Rs 125 crore and is showing minimal drops on weekdays, it is expected to surpass that milestone by the end of its third weekend. The film's overseas collections have also been robust, contributing to the overall worldwide tally.

The coming weekend will be crucial. If Dhamaal 4 maintains its current pace, it will comfortably become the biggest hit of the franchise, proving once again that the comedy genre remains a reliable draw for Indian audiences.
