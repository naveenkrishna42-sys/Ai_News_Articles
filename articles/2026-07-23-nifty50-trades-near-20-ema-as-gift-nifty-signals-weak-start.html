NIFTY50 Trades Near 20 EMA as GIFT Nifty Signals Weak Start

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • GIFT Nifty points to a gap-down opening for Indian equity markets on Wednesday.
  • Rising crude oil prices and escalating Middle East tensions are weighing on sentiment.
  • The NIFTY50's 20-day exponential moving average (20 EMA) is a crucial support level to watch.
  • Asian shares traded mixed, adding to the cautious mood ahead of domestic trading.

GIFT Nifty Signals Negative Start

Indian stock markets are set for a weak opening on Wednesday, with GIFT Nifty indicating a gap-down start. The derivative contract traded lower, pointing to selling pressure at the open for the NIFTY50 and Sensex.

Traders are bracing for a volatile session as global cues remain mixed. Asian shares traded with a negative bias, and overnight losses on Wall Street added to the cautious sentiment.

Crude Oil and Geopolitical Tensions Weigh

Rising crude oil prices have emerged as a fresh headwind for Indian equities. The uptick in oil comes amid renewed Middle East tensions, which have kept investors on edge.

Higher crude prices typically hurt India's trade balance and inflation outlook. This has led to a cautious stance among domestic institutional investors, who are watching the geopolitical developments closely.

Key Support: The 20 EMA

Technical analysts are focused on the NIFTY50's 20-day exponential moving average (20 EMA). This level has acted as a short-term support in recent sessions.

A decisive break below the 20 EMA could trigger further selling. However, a bounce from this level would signal that bulls are still in control. Market participants are watching for the index to defend this mark in early trade.

What to Watch on Wednesday

Besides the 20 EMA, traders will track the performance of banking and energy stocks. The Nifty Bank index has been volatile, and any sharp moves could set the tone for the broader market.

Foreign institutional investor (FII) flows will also be in focus. After recent bouts of selling, any change in FII stance could provide direction.

On the macro front, no major domestic data is scheduled for release, so global cues and commodity prices will dictate intraday moves.

The coming session will test whether the NIFTY50 can hold its short-term support amid multiple headwinds. A close above the 20 EMA would bring some relief, while a breakdown could open the door to lower levels in the days ahead.
