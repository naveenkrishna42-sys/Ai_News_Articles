Q1 Results: Wipro PAT Flat at ₹3,352 Cr, Tech Mahindra Profit Rises, BHEL in Black

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Wipro's Q1 net profit flat at ₹3,352 crore; legal and professional expenses rose 67% to ₹316 crore.
  • Tech Mahindra and BHEL reported increased profits; BHEL posted ₹382 crore net profit.
  • Polycab, ITC Hotels, Jio Financial posted higher profits; CEAT's profit declined.

Wipro's Flat Quarter and Rising Costs

Wipro reported a consolidated net profit of ₹3,352 crore for the first quarter of FY27, unchanged from the same period last year. The IT services giant's revenue remained under pressure, even as it logged strong deal wins in artificial intelligence and other digital segments.

However, the company's legal and professional expenses surged 67% year-on-year to ₹316 crore, raising questions about cost control. Analysts pointed out that while deal momentum remains robust, margin compression and a muted revenue outlook have weighed on investor sentiment.

Tech Mahindra and BHEL Post Gains

Tech Mahindra bucked the trend with a rise in quarterly profit, driven by efficiency measures and growth in its enterprise business. The company did not provide segment-wise breakdowns, but management attributed the performance to improved operational leverage.

State-run BHEL reported a net profit of ₹382 crore for the quarter, compared to a loss in the year-ago period. The turnaround was supported by higher execution of power sector orders and a reduction in finance costs. The company's order book remains strong, though officials have not yet confirmed specific project timelines.

Consumer and Auto Sector Results Mixed

Polycab India posted a higher profit, helped by robust cable and wire demand. ITC Hotels also reported improved earnings, benefiting from a rise in business travel and wedding season bookings. Jio Financial Services continued its growth trajectory with a higher quarterly bottom line.

In contrast, tyre maker CEAT saw its profit decline, hurt by rising raw material costs and subdued replacement demand. The company's management flagged continued volatility in rubber prices as a key risk for the coming quarters.

Outlook and Market Reaction

Investors are now watching for commentary from Wipro's leadership on the near-term demand environment, especially in the BFSI and retail verticals. The company's muted guidance for the current quarter has already led to some selling pressure in early trade.

Analysts expect the divergence in Q1 performance across sectors to continue, with IT firms facing headwinds from global uncertainty while domestic-focused companies benefit from steady consumption. The next batch of earnings from banking and auto firms will offer further clues on the health of the broader economy.
