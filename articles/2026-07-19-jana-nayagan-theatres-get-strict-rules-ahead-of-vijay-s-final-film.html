Jana Nayagan: Theatres get strict rules ahead of Vijay's final film

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Strict instructions issued to theatres screening 'Jana Nayagan'
  • Advance bookings in Tamil Nadu opened with shows selling out at Rs 2,500
  • Film marks Vijay's farewell from cinema ahead of political career

Chennai, July 19: Theatres across Tamil Nadu have received strict instructions ahead of the release of 'Jana Nayagan', the final film starring actor and Chief Minister Vijay. The Tamil Nadu Film Exhibitors Association issued a set of guidelines aimed at ensuring smooth screenings as advance bookings opened to massive demand.

Sources in the trade said shows in several multiplexes and single screens are selling out within hours. Some premium tickets are being sold at Rs 2,500, reflecting the frenzy around Vijay's farewell from cinema.

Strict rules for theatres

Exhibitors have been asked to adhere to a code of conduct during screenings. The instructions include ensuring no disruptions during the film, managing crowd flow outside theatres, and coordinating with local police for security.

Individual theatres have been warned against unauthorised recording or streaming of the movie. Officials said strict action will be taken against any violation.

Advance bookings break records

Advance bookings for 'Jana Nayagan' opened across India on Saturday. In Tamil Nadu, several shows were sold out within minutes. Theatres in Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai and Tiruchirappalli reported the highest demand.

Premium screens in the city have priced tickets between Rs 1,500 and Rs 2,500. Many fans queued up online and at box offices to secure seats for the opening weekend.

Impact on other Tamil releases

The release of 'Jana Nayagan' has forced several other Tamil films to reconsider their release plans. Producers of at least half a dozen films have postponed their releases to avoid a clash at the box office.

Trade analysts said the Vijay factor has created an unprecedented window. No film is expected to compete directly with 'Jana Nayagan' during the first two weeks of its run.

Knitwear units work overtime

In Tiruppur, knitwear units are working overtime to produce merchandise for the film. T-shirts, caps and banners featuring Vijay's image and film logo are being churned out in large quantities.

Unit owners said orders have come from fan clubs across the state. Some units have been operating double shifts since last week to meet the demand.

Emotional father-daughter track released

The makers released a new song from the film on Saturday. Titled 'Adiye En Poonthene', the track focuses on the father-daughter relationship in the story. Fans have praised the emotional composition and visuals.

The song has trended on social media platforms since its release. Music analysts say it could become one of the biggest hits from the album.

With the film's release just days away, all eyes are on the final box office numbers. The industry will also closely watch how Vijay transitions from cinema to full-time politics after this farewell film.
