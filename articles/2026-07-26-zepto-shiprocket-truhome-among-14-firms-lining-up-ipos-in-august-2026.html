Zepto, Shiprocket, Truhome among 14 firms lining up IPOs in August 2026

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • At least 14 companies are planning to launch IPOs in August 2026.
  • The total fundraising target is estimated to exceed Rs 25,000 crore.
  • High-profile names include Zepto, Shiprocket, and Truhome Finance.

The Indian primary market is bracing for a busy August 2026. At least 14 companies, including quick-commerce major Zepto, logistics platform Shiprocket, and housing finance firm Truhome, are set to launch their initial public offerings next month.

According to reports, these firms together aim to raise over Rs 25,000 crore from the public markets. The line-up signals a revival in IPO activity after a relatively subdued period in the first half of the year.

Big names lead the charge

Zepto, the Mumbai-based quick-commerce startup, is expected to be the most watched IPO of the month. The company, which delivers groceries in 10 minutes, has been preparing for a public listing for months. Its debut will test investor appetite for loss-making but high-growth tech firms.

Shiprocket, a logistics aggregator serving e-commerce sellers, is also on the block. The company has built a network of courier partners and claims to process millions of shipments monthly. Truhome Finance, a non-banking financial company focused on affordable housing loans, rounds out the top tier of issuers.

A dozen more in the pipeline

Beyond the headline names, over a dozen smaller firms are expected to hit the market. These include businesses from sectors such as manufacturing, healthcare, and financial services. The exact calendar of issues is still being firmed up, with draft documents already filed with the Securities and Exchange Board of India.

Investment bankers are optimistic. They point to strong domestic mutual fund inflows and a stable regulatory environment as tailwinds. However, global uncertainty—sparked by geopolitical tensions and potential interest rate moves—remains a risk factor.

Market sentiment and valuation concerns

The IPO pipeline comes at a time when the secondary market has shown mixed signals. The benchmark Nifty 50 has been volatile, with foreign institutional investors pulling out funds in recent weeks. Analysts caution that high valuations could be a sticking point for some issues.

Zepto, for instance, is reportedly seeking a valuation of over $3 billion. That would make it one of the most richly valued new-age tech listings in India. Investors will be scrutinising its path to profitability closely.

Shiprocket and Truhome are also expected to command premium pricing. Their success will depend on how well they can convince institutional buyers of their growth prospects and margins.

What retail investors should watch

Retail participation in IPOs has been strong in 2026, with several issues seeing oversubscription of 10-20 times. For August, the sheer number of offerings could fragment demand. Experts advise investors to focus on business fundamentals rather than hype.

Key factors include revenue growth, profitability timeline, and the use of IPO proceeds. Companies that clearly outline expansion plans and debt reduction are likely to fare better.

The month of August will also see a few offers for sale by existing investors. This could provide an exit opportunity for early backers, especially in the startup space.

With 14 firms lining up, the coming weeks will be a litmus test for the Indian IPO market. A strong debut by Zepto and other marquee names could pave the way for even more listings later in the year. The market will be watching the subscription numbers and listing-day gains closely.
