Amit Shah's helicopter diverted to IAF station due to heavy rain

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Amit Shah's helicopter diverted to IAF station due to heavy rain
  • Incident occurred near Chennai on Sunday
  • Officials confirmed the diversion; minister later reached destination

Union Home Minister Amit Shah's helicopter was diverted to an Indian Air Force (IAF) station near Chennai on Sunday due to heavy rain in the region. The diversion, confirmed by officials, came as inclement weather disrupted the scheduled travel plan. The minister was en route to an event when the pilot decided to change course for safety.

Diversion due to weather

Heavy downpour and low visibility made it difficult for the helicopter to continue its original flight path. The pilot, following standard protocol, opted to land at the nearest IAF facility. Sources said the decision was taken to ensure the safety of all on board.

The IAF station, located on the outskirts of Chennai, received the helicopter without any untoward incident. Ground staff were alerted in advance, and the landing was smooth. Officials did not specify how long the minister stayed at the station before resuming his journey.

Minister's schedule affected

The diversion caused a brief delay in Amit Shah's itinerary for the day. He was scheduled to participate in a series of programmes in and around Chennai. According to officials, the minister eventually reached his destination after the weather cleared.

Details of the event he was heading to have not been officially disclosed. However, such diversions are not uncommon in the region during the monsoon season, when sudden showers can disrupt air travel.

Security and protocol

The diversion was handled with full security protocol, with local police and IAF personnel coordinating closely. The helicopter was kept under tight vigil during its brief halt. No security breach was reported.

This incident highlights the logistical challenges faced by VVIP movements during the monsoon. Authorities often keep alternative landing sites on standby, and this diversion was part of that preparedness.

What's next

Officials have not confirmed whether Amit Shah's remaining engagements for the day proceeded as planned. The weather in Chennai remains unpredictable, and further travel adjustments cannot be ruled out. The minister's office is expected to release a detailed schedule update shortly.
