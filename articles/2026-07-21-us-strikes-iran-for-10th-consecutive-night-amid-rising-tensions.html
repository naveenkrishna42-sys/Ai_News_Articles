US Strikes Iran For 10th Consecutive Night Amid Rising Tensions

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • US has carried out airstrikes on Iran for the 10th night in a row.
  • Tehran has launched attacks on Kuwait, widening the conflict.
  • President Trump warned of strong retaliation following the deaths of US service members.

Washington has launched a fresh round of airstrikes against Iran, marking the 10th consecutive night of bombing. The strikes come as President Donald Trump warned of strong retaliation for the deaths of US service members, according to reports from CNN and the BBC.

The sustained campaign has drawn global attention, with Iranian forces responding by hitting targets in Kuwait. The escalation signals a significant expansion of hostilities beyond the direct US-Iran confrontation.

10 Nights of Bombing

The US military has maintained a relentless pace, striking what it describes as Iranian military infrastructure and assets. The operation, now in its tenth night, is one of the longest sustained bombing campaigns since the US-led invasion of Iraq in 2003.

Pentagon officials have not specified the exact targets or confirmed the number of sorties flown. However, reports from Al Jazeera indicate that the strikes have been concentrated in southern and central Iran.

Iran Strikes Kuwait

Tehran has retaliated by launching attacks on Kuwait, according to Al Jazeera. The nature and scale of these attacks are still emerging, but they mark a dangerous new phase in the conflict, dragging a Gulf neighbour into the fray.

Kuwaiti authorities have not yet issued an official statement on the extent of damage or casualties. The move suggests Iran is aiming to disrupt US allies in the region and widen the theatre of war.

Trump's Warning

President Trump, speaking from the White House, warned that the US would respond forcefully to any further attacks that harm American personnel. His comments came after reports of US service members being killed in previous Iranian strikes.

The White House has framed the bombing campaign as a necessary measure to protect US interests and deter future aggression. Critics, however, have questioned the strategy, pointing to the risk of a protracted regional war.

Regional Fallout

The conflict has put Iran's Gulf neighbours in a precarious position. The Hindu reported that Iran has hit out at these nations, accusing them of aiding the US campaign. This has raised fears of a broader confrontation that could destabilise the entire region.

Diplomatic efforts have so far failed to secure a ceasefire. The United Nations has called for restraint, but neither side has shown willingness to de-escalate. Oil prices have surged, and global markets remain volatile.

As the bombing enters its 11th night, the world watches for signs of either a breakthrough or a deeper spiral into war. The next few days will determine whether this conflict remains contained or spirals into a wider regional crisis.
