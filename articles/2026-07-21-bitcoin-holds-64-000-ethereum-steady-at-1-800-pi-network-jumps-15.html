Bitcoin and Ethereum Hold Key Levels

The cryptocurrency market opened the week on a mixed note. Bitcoin is hovering above the $64,000 mark, maintaining its footing after a period of price swings. Ethereum, the second-largest digital asset, is holding steady at around $1,800. Both assets have seen relatively low volatility in the past 24 hours.

Traders are watching these levels closely. A break above $65,000 for Bitcoin could signal further upside, while a dip below $63,000 might invite fresh selling pressure. Ethereum's $1,800 support has held firm, but the asset has struggled to push higher in recent sessions.

Pi Network Surges 15%

Pi Network has emerged as the standout performer among altcoins. The token surged 15% in the last 24 hours, catching the attention of traders. The rally comes amid growing community activity and speculation around upcoming network developments.

Pi Network, which allows users to mine coins via mobile phones, has a large user base in India and other emerging markets. The price jump has pushed its market cap higher, though liquidity remains thin compared to larger cryptocurrencies.

Pump.fun Rebounds After Dip

Pump.fun, a platform known for launching meme coins, has rebounded after a recent decline. The recovery has added to the positive sentiment in the meme coin sector, which often sees sharp moves driven by retail interest.

Analysts caution that such tokens remain highly speculative. The rebound may be short-lived if broader market conditions turn bearish. Still, the bounce has provided a temporary boost to risk appetite among crypto traders.

Market Outlook

The overall crypto market remains choppy. Bitcoin dominance has edged slightly higher, indicating investors are favoring the largest asset for safety. Altcoins like Pi Network and Pump.fun are drawing attention, but their gains are not broad-based.

Regulatory news and macroeconomic data will likely influence the next move. Investors are watching for any signals from the US Federal Reserve and upcoming blockchain events.

What to watch next: Bitcoin's ability to hold above $64,000 and Ethereum's reaction to the $1,800 level will set the tone for the week. Pi Network's rally could continue if trading volumes pick up, but corrections are equally possible.
