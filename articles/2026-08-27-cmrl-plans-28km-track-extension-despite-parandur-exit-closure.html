CMRL Plans 28km Track Extension Despite Parandur Exit Closure

Chennai Metro Rail Ltd (CMRL) has announced plans to extend its metro network by 28 kilometers despite the closure of the Parandur exit. The proposed extension aims to connect Chennai to Kanchipuram via Chengalpattu, a route that has been under consideration for several years.

Project Details and Timeline

The project, which is expected to cost around ₹1,000 crore, is expected to be completed by 2030. The extension will include the construction of 28 kilometers of new track, along with 12 new stations. The project will be funded by the government and CMRL.

Despite the announcement, there are concerns about the feasibility of the project. The route passes through densely populated areas, which could pose challenges in terms of land acquisition and environmental impact. Additionally, the cost of the project is expected to be high, which could raise questions about the affordability of the metro system.
