Why “missing casting” matters: beyond star power

In Indian cinema, casting is rarely just about fit. It’s also about financing, distribution expectations, and the kind of opening-week turnout a film can reliably target. Amitabh Bachchan’s presence typically changes how a script is read by audiences: viewers expect scale, moral clarity, and dialogue delivery that lands like a verdict.

When he isn’t part of the final cast, the film’s tone can shift subtly. A story that might have become a courtroom-style drama can tilt toward romance, satire, or action depending on who becomes the focal point. For filmmakers, not getting a particular actor can force a faster rework of characterization, pacing, and even marketing copy.
