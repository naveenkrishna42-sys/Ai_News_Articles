Trump Media posts $238m loss as crypto holdings tumble

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Trump Media & Technology Group reported a $238 million net loss for the second quarter.
  • The loss is largely attributed to a sharp decline in the value of its cryptocurrency holdings.
  • Traffic to Truth Social has fallen significantly during the summer months.
  • The company has announced a new turnaround effort to address its financial challenges.

Losses mount as crypto bet sours

Trump Media & Technology Group (TMTG), the parent company of Truth Social, has reported a staggering $238 million net loss for the second quarter of the fiscal year. The figure marks one of the largest quarterly losses in the company's history and underscores the volatility of its business model, which has become increasingly tied to cryptocurrency markets.

The loss comes as the value of the company's digital asset holdings, primarily Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies, experienced a sharp downturn during the period. TMTG had earlier pivoted towards crypto as a growth avenue, but the market's recent turbulence has exposed the fragility of that strategy.

Truth Social traffic takes a summer slide

Adding to the company's woes, traffic to Truth Social has reportedly declined sharply this summer. According to data from web analytics firms, user engagement on the platform has dropped significantly, raising questions about its long-term viability as a competitor to mainstream social media networks.

The decline in traffic is a worrying sign for TMTG, which has struggled to monetize its user base. Advertising revenue, a key income stream for social platforms, has remained thin, and the shrinking audience could further deter potential advertisers.

Turnaround plan announced amid scrutiny

In response to the mounting losses, TMTG has unveiled a new turnaround effort, though details remain sparse. The company's management has hinted at cost-cutting measures and a renewed focus on core operations, but analysts are skeptical about the feasibility of such plans given the current trajectory.

The announcement has done little to reassure investors, with TMTG's stock price remaining volatile in after-hours trading. The company, which went public to much fanfare, has consistently struggled to meet market expectations, and this latest report is likely to intensify calls for greater transparency and accountability.

Regulatory and market implications

The losses also come at a time of heightened regulatory scrutiny over cryptocurrency investments by publicly traded companies. TMTG's heavy reliance on digital assets has drawn criticism from governance experts, who warn that such exposure can lead to unpredictable swings in financial performance.

Meanwhile, the broader market for social media stocks has been under pressure, with investors favoring platforms with more robust revenue diversification. TMTG's inability to diversify beyond its niche user base remains a fundamental concern.

What lies ahead

As the company navigates this turbulent phase, all eyes will be on its next moves. Will the turnaround plan yield tangible results, or will further losses erode investor confidence? With the crypto market showing no signs of stabilizing and user engagement waning, TMTG faces an uphill battle to regain its footing.

Observers will be watching closely for any regulatory actions or strategic shifts that could alter the company's fortunes in the coming months.
