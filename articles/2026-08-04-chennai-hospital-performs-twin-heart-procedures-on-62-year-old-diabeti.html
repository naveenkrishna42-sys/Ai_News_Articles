Chennai hospital performs twin heart procedures on 62-year-old diabetic man

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • 62-year-old diabetic man underwent two heart procedures in one sitting
  • Procedures included angioplasty and a valve replacement
  • Performed at a private hospital in Chennai
  • Patient's condition was complex due to diabetes

Chennai: In a rare medical feat, a private hospital in Chennai has successfully performed two distinct heart procedures on a 62-year-old diabetic man in a single session. The patient, whose identity has not been disclosed, underwent both angioplasty and a heart valve replacement during one operation, a complex undertaking given his pre-existing condition.

A complex case, a combined approach

The man, who had been suffering from diabetes, presented with multiple cardiac issues that required immediate intervention. Doctors at the hospital decided to tackle both problems together rather than risk a second procedure later. This approach reduces the number of times a patient must be anaesthetised and shortens overall recovery time.

While angioplasty clears blocked arteries, the valve replacement addressed a separate malfunctioning heart valve. Combining these procedures is technically demanding, as it requires careful coordination and precise timing. The fact that the patient was diabetic added another layer of complexity, as such patients often have weaker blood vessels and slower healing.

How the twin procedures were performed

The surgical team first performed angioplasty, inserting a stent to open a narrowed coronary artery. Once that was completed and the blood flow restored, they proceeded to the valve replacement. The entire operation was carried out in a single sitting, under one anaesthesia.

The hospital has not yet released the exact duration of the surgery or the specific type of valve used. However, doctors have confirmed that the patient is currently stable and under observation in the intensive care unit. The success of this combined procedure highlights the advanced cardiac care now available in Chennai.

Risks and recovery for diabetic patients

Diabetes is a known risk factor for heart disease, and managing such patients during major cardiac surgery is a challenge. High blood sugar levels can impair wound healing and increase the risk of infection. In this case, the medical team had to carefully regulate the patient's glucose levels before, during, and after the procedure.

The patient's recovery will be closely monitored over the coming days. Doctors will watch for any signs of complications, such as bleeding or infection, and manage his diabetes aggressively to ensure a smooth recovery. The hospital has stated that the man is expected to be discharged in about a week, though this has not been officially confirmed.

What this means for cardiac care

This case is significant because it demonstrates that even high-risk patients can undergo complex, combined cardiac procedures successfully. It also reflects a growing trend in Indian hospitals towards minimally invasive and multi-step surgeries performed in one go, which can be more cost-effective and less stressful for patients.

Medical experts not involved in the case have noted that such combined procedures require a highly skilled team and advanced equipment. They also caution that not every patient is a candidate for this approach, and decisions must be made on a case-by-case basis.

As the patient continues his recovery, the hospital is expected to release more details about the procedure and the patient's progress. This case will likely be discussed in medical circles as an example of the possibilities in modern cardiac surgery.
