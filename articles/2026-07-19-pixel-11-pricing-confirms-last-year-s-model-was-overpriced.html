Pixel 11 pricing confirms last year's model was overpriced

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pixel 11 series pricing is notably lower than Pixel 10 at launch, validating earlier speculation about overpricing.
  • Leaked Amazon listings reveal full US pricing and specs for the Pixel 11 lineup.
  • Google has confirmed the Pixel 11 launch event for August 12 in India, with invites going out.
  • The new series is expected to offer better value with improved storage, RAM, and display options.

Pixel 11 pricing: A correction in strategy?

Google's upcoming Pixel 11 series is making headlines, and not just for its rumored specs. The pricing leaks suggest a significant shift from last year's Pixel 10 lineup, confirming what many analysts had suspected: the 2025 models were priced too high.

Leaked Amazon listings in the US have spilled full pricing and specifications for the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, and Pixel 11 Pro XL. The base model starts at a lower price point than the Pixel 10 did at launch, a move that could help Google claw back market share in the premium segment.

What the leaked listings reveal

According to Android Authority, deleted Amazon listings show the Pixel 11 with 128GB storage starting at $699, down from the Pixel 10's $799 launch price. The Pro models also see cuts, with the Pixel 11 Pro priced at $899 and the Pro XL at $999.

Specifications include upgraded displays with higher refresh rates, improved camera sensors, and larger batteries. RAM options start at 8GB for the base model and go up to 16GB for the Pro XL. Storage tops out at 512GB, a first for the Pixel line.

India launch confirmed for August 12

Google has started sending invites for the Pixel 11 series launch event in India, set for August 12. The event, dubbed "Pixel Glow," will take place in multiple cities. This marks the second year Google is holding a dedicated India launch, signaling the market's importance.

Indian pricing is expected to be announced at the event. Given the US price cuts, analysts expect a similar reduction in India, where the Pixel 10 was seen as expensive against rivals from Samsung and OnePlus.

The Pixel 10 overpricing theory gains traction

Last year, critics argued that the Pixel 10's premium pricing was hard to justify, especially with competition offering similar specs at lower costs. The Pixel 11's price point validates that view, as Google appears to be recalibrating its strategy.

Android Police notes that the new pricing proves "what I suspected about last year's model." The sentiment is echoed by Droid Life, which highlights that the Pixel 11 offers better value with more RAM and storage at a lower cost.

Google has not officially commented on the pricing strategy shift. However, the message from the leaked listings is clear: the company is listening to consumer feedback.

What to watch next

The August 12 launch will reveal final pricing for India and other markets. Pre-orders are expected to open immediately after the event, with shipping starting by late August. The real test will be whether the lower prices translate to higher sales, especially against Apple's iPhone 17 series launching in September.
