India's Test advantage grows with Padikkal's century

Devdutt Padikkal's second Test century has put India in a strong position against Sri Lanka, capping off a hard-fought day that showcased the team's batting depth and adaptability. The series balance now shifts with India leading by one Test, setting the stage for a crucial third Test.

Padikkal's masterclass highlights India's batting strength

Padikkal's 117-run innings was a masterclass in Test cricket, demonstrating India's ability to build innings in challenging conditions. His century came in the second session of Day 2, after India had survived a shaky start on Day 1. The right-hander's ability to anchor the innings and accelerate when needed was crucial for India's total of 350 for 6 declared.

Sri Lanka's bowling attack struggles to contain India's top order

Sri Lanka's bowling attack, led by Dushmantha Chameera and Kasun Rajitha, struggled to contain India's top order. The Indian batsmen, including Padikkal, Rohit Sharma, and Shubman Gill, showed their ability to adapt to different conditions and exploit weaknesses in the Sri Lankan bowling. The Indian bowlers, led by Ravichandran Ashwin and Ravindra Jadeja, also played a crucial role in restricting Sri Lanka to 200 for 8.

Series balance shifts with India now leading by one Test

Padikkal's century has shifted the balance of the series, with India now leading by one Test. The third Test, to be held in Galle, is crucial for both teams. India will look to build on their momentum, while Sri Lanka will aim to bounce back and level the series.
