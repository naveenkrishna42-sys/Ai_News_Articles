Banaswadi Railway Land Encroachment Sparks Row in Bengaluru

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Railway land in Banaswadi, Bengaluru, has been allegedly encroached upon by private parties.
  • Illegal structures and commercial activities have been reported on the land.
  • Residents have raised concerns, leading to a dispute with authorities.
  • Officials have not yet confirmed the extent of the encroachment.

BENGALURU: A patch of railway land in Banaswadi, in the northeastern part of the city, has become the centre of a fresh controversy. Residents have alleged that the land, meant for railway use, has been encroached upon and is being used for illegal commercial activities.

Allegations of Encroachment

Locals claim that over the past few months, unauthorised structures have come up on the land. These include small shops and temporary shelters, which are now operating without any clear permission from the railways or the civic body.

“The land was vacant for years. Suddenly, we saw construction activity. No one from the railway department came to stop it,” said a resident who did not wish to be named. The encroachment has also led to garbage dumping and parking issues in the area.

Residents Raise Concerns

Residents have approached the local corporator and the railway authorities, demanding action. They have pointed out that the land is prime property and should be used for public benefit, such as a park or a parking lot, rather than being allowed to be misused.

“We have filed a complaint. But nothing has been done so far. The illegal occupants seem to be operating openly,” another resident told reporters. The issue has also been raised on social media, with photos of the encroached land being shared widely.

Railway Officials Yet to Respond

Railway officials have not yet confirmed the exact extent of the encroachment. Sources said a survey of the land is being conducted to verify the claims. The land in question falls under the jurisdiction of the South Western Railway zone.

“We are aware of the complaints. A team has been sent to inspect the site. Action will be taken based on the report,” a railway official said, requesting anonymity. However, no timeline for the report or action has been provided.

The Banaswadi area has seen rapid urbanisation in recent years, with several residential layouts coming up. Railway land in the vicinity has often been a target for encroachers, according to local activists. Previous attempts by the railways to clear such land have faced legal hurdles.

What Next?

Residents have warned of a protest if the authorities do not act soon. The matter is likely to be raised in the next ward committee meeting. The railway department is expected to release its survey findings in the coming weeks. Whether the encroachment will be cleared or will lead to a legal battle remains to be seen.
