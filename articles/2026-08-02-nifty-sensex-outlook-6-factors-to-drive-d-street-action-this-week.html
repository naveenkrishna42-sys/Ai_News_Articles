Nifty, Sensex outlook: 6 factors to drive D-Street action this week

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • RBI's monetary policy decision will be the key domestic trigger for markets.
  • Q1 earnings season continues with results from major companies.
  • Geopolitical tensions in the Middle East and global cues will influence sentiment.
  • Foreign institutional investor flows and crude oil prices will also be monitored.

Indian equity benchmarks ended last week on a firm footing, but the road ahead is fraught with crosscurrents. As traders gear up for Monday's session, the spotlight shifts to a clutch of domestic and global factors that could determine whether the Nifty and Sensex can build on their gains or cede ground.

From the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) interest rate decision to the ongoing US-Iran conflict, the week promises plenty of triggers. Here are six key factors that are likely to drive Dalal Street action.

RBI policy: The pivotal event

The RBI's monetary policy committee (MPC) will announce its bi-monthly decision this week. With inflation showing signs of cooling but growth still fragile, the central bank faces a delicate balancing act.

Markets will parse the accompanying commentary for clues on the future rate trajectory. A status quo is largely priced in, but any hawkish or dovish surprise could spark sharp moves across rate-sensitive sectors like banking, auto and real estate.

Q1 earnings season: Results take centre stage

Corporate earnings for the April-June quarter are in full swing, and this week brings results from several heavyweights. Investors will scrutinise management commentary on demand, margins and outlook amid an uneven consumption recovery.

The quality of earnings, rather than just the headline numbers, will be the key differentiator. Any downgrades to guidance could weigh on individual stocks and drag sectoral indices.

Middle East tensions: A geopolitical overhang

The ongoing US-Iran conflict continues to cast a shadow over global markets. Any escalation in hostilities could push crude oil prices higher, stoking inflationary fears and denting risk appetite.

Indian markets, being net importers of oil, remain particularly sensitive to spikes in crude. A de-escalation, on the other hand, could provide a much-needed breather and fuel a relief rally.

Global cues and FII flows

Overseas signals, including the US Federal Reserve's rate path and economic data releases, will influence sentiment. Global equity trends and the dollar index will also dictate foreign institutional investor (FII) flows.

After a volatile few months, FII buying or selling will be closely watched. Sustained inflows could provide support to the indices, while outflows may cap upside.

Crude oil and currency watch

Brent crude's trajectory remains a crucial monitorable. A sustained rise above key levels could hurt India's fiscal math and corporate margins, while also pressuring the rupee.

The rupee's movement against the dollar will be important for import-heavy sectors and for FII flows. A stable currency would bolster investor confidence.

Technical levels and derivatives data

On the charts, the Nifty has reclaimed some crucial moving averages, but the 25,000-25,200 zone is seen as a stiff resistance. On the downside, support is placed around 24,700-24,500.

Options data suggests that call writers have built positions at higher strikes, capping rallies, while put writing at lower levels offers a floor. Traders will watch these levels for directional cues throughout the week.

With a dense calendar of events, volatility is likely to remain elevated. The RBI's decision and the geopolitical situation will be the primary market movers, and any unexpected development could quickly alter the course.

For investors, the week calls for caution and a focus on stock-specific opportunities rather than broad index bets. The coming sessions will reveal whether the bulls can sustain their momentum or whether bears regain control.
