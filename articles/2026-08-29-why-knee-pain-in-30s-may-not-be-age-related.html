Knee Pain in 30s: What Doctors Are Saying

Knee pain in young adults is often dismissed as just a part of aging, but doctors are now revealing that it may have nothing to do with age. According to spine surgeon Dr Rajesh Verma, pain in the 30s can be a sign of early aging, but it can also be caused by a variety of other factors.

Common Causes of Knee Pain in 30s

Knee pain in the 30s is not always a sign of aging. It can be caused by a variety of injuries, conditions, and lifestyle factors. Some of the most common causes include:

  • Early knee arthritis
  • Sports injuries
  • Poor biomechanics
  • Obesity
  • Lack of physical activity

Early knee arthritis, also known as osteoarthritis, is a common cause of knee pain in young adults. It is caused by the wearing down of the cartilage in the knee joint. Sports injuries, such as ACL tears, can also cause knee pain in the 30s. Poor biomechanics, or the way the body moves, can also contribute to knee pain.

How to Prevent and Manage Knee Pain

There are several ways to prevent and manage knee pain in the 30s. Lifestyle changes, such as weight loss and strength training, can help prevent knee pain. Medications, such as pain relievers and anti-inflammatory drugs, can also help manage knee pain. In some cases, surgery may be necessary to treat knee pain.
