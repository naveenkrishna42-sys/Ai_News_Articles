Abhishek Sharma's 30-Ball T20I Hundred: Fastest by a Full Member Batter

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Team 📅 2026-09-17 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Abhishek Sharma reached his hundred in just 30 balls, the fastest in T20 internationals by a batter from a Test-playing nation.
  • He broke Rohit Sharma's previous record of 35 balls and equalled Virat Kohli's record for the fastest T20I century by an Indian.
  • The innings helped India complete a 3-0 series sweep over Afghanistan, underlining India's bench strength ahead of future ICC events.
  • The milestone places Abhishek in an elite group of power-hitters and signals a shift towards hyper-aggressive T20 batting.

A Record-Breaking Blitz

Abhishek Sharma etched his name into the record books with a breathtaking 30-ball century against Afghanistan, the fastest T20 international hundred by a batter from a Test-playing nation. The left-hander's onslaught eclipsed Rohit Sharma's previous mark of 35 balls, set in 2017 against Sri Lanka, and equalled Virat Kohli's record for the fastest T20I ton by an Indian. Abhishek's innings powered India to a series sweep, sealing a 3-0 victory.

Innings Breakdown and Key Stats

Opening the batting, Abhishek wasted no time, reaching his fifty in just 15 balls before accelerating to triple figures off the 30th delivery. His knock included nine fours and eight sixes, showcasing a combination of timing and brute power. The strike rate of 333.33 during the century stands among the highest for any T20I hundred.

Context and Comparison

While Chris Gayle holds the overall fastest T20I century (21 balls, for West Indies against South Africa in 2016), Abhishek's feat is the quickest among batters from Full Member nations. Gayle's record came in a high-scoring affair, but Abhishek's innings was equally destructive, dismantling a quality Afghan attack featuring Rashid Khan and Mujeeb Ur Rahman. His 30-ball ton is also the second-fastest in all T20Is, behind only Gayle.

Abhishek's rise has been meteoric. The 23-year-old had already impressed in the IPL for Sunrisers Hyderabad, and this innings cements his place as a key member of India's T20I setup. With the next T20 World Cup on the horizon, his form provides selectors with a welcome headache.

Match Summary and Series Sweep

Chasing 173, India lost early wickets but Abhishek's blitz ensured there were no further hiccups. He added a quickfire 50-run partnership with Rinku Singh, and India cruised home with 27 balls to spare. The win completed a 3-0 series whitewash, following victories in the first two T20Is. Afghanistan, despite a fighting fifty from Ibrahim Zadran, were outclassed.

Key Takeaways

  • Historic Milestone: Abhishek's 30-ball hundred is the fastest by a Full Member batter, breaking Rohit Sharma's record.
  • Elite Company: He now shares the record for the fastest T20I century by an Indian with Virat Kohli.
  • Series Dominance: India completed a 3-0 sweep, reinforcing their depth in T20 cricket.
  • Future Star: Abhishek's explosive batting makes him a strong contender for the upcoming T20 World Cup.

What This Means for Indian Cricket

Abhishek's innings is a testament to India's evolving T20 philosophy—fearless cricket from ball one. With established stars rested, the young brigade seized the opportunity. Coach Rahul Dravid has emphasised flexibility and aggression, and Abhishek embodies that approach. His ability to score rapidly against both pace and spin makes him a valuable asset on flat decks and turning tracks alike.

As the cricketing world marvels at his achievement, the focus now shifts to whether Abhishek can sustain this form. For now, he has announced himself on the global stage in spectacular fashion.
