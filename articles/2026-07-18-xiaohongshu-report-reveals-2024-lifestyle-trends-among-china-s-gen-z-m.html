Xiaohongshu Report Reveals 2024 Lifestyle Trends Among China's Gen Z, Millennials

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Lifestyle Original source ↗
Key points
  • Xiaohongshu's 2024 report shows Gen Z and millennials prioritising experiences over material goods.
  • Travel trends include 'revenge travel' and offbeat destinations, with a focus on budget-friendly trips.
  • Digital detox and mindful consumption are gaining traction among younger Chinese demographics.

A new report from Xiaohongshu, the Chinese social commerce platform often compared to Instagram, offers a detailed look into how the country's Gen Z and millennials are living, spending, and thinking in 2024. The findings paint a picture of a generation that is redefining success, prioritising experiences over possessions, and seeking balance in a hyper-digital world.

Experiences Over Things: The New Status Symbols

The report indicates a major shift away from conspicuous consumption. Instead of luxury handbags or the latest gadgets, status for this demographic now comes from unique experiences. Concert tickets, niche travel destinations, and culinary adventures are the new markers of social standing.

This trend is visible on Xiaohongshu itself, where posts about a weekend hiking trip or a pop-up art exhibition often generate more engagement than those showcasing designer goods. The platform has become a key hub for people to share and discover these experiences, creating a feedback loop that drives the trend further.

The Rise of 'Revenge Travel' and Offbeat Destinations

Travel remains a top priority, but the nature of it has changed. The report notes a continuation of 'revenge travel', a phenomenon where people make up for lost time after pandemic restrictions. However, the focus is now on value and authenticity.

Younger travellers are increasingly choosing lesser-known cities and rural areas over crowded tourist hotspots. They are looking for local food, homestays, and activities that offer a genuine connection to a place. Budget-friendly travel hacks and itineraries are some of the most popular content categories on the platform.

Digital Detox and Mindful Consumption

Paradoxically for a generation born into the internet, there is a growing desire for a digital detox. The report highlights a significant interest in activities that promote mental well-being, such as meditation, journaling, and spending time in nature.

This is not about rejecting technology entirely, but about using it more intentionally. Users are sharing tips on how to reduce screen time, curate their social media feeds, and find offline hobbies. Mindful consumption extends to shopping as well, with a preference for sustainable, second-hand, or locally-made products.

The 'Ping-Pong' Economy and Side Hustle Culture

Financial pragmatism is another key theme. The report describes a 'ping-pong' economy where young people move between spending on experiences and saving aggressively. They are highly price-sensitive and actively seek deals, discounts, and group-buying opportunities.

This financial savvy is paired with a strong side-hustle culture. Many Gen Z and millennials are using Xiaohongshu itself to start small businesses, from selling handmade crafts to offering personal styling advice. The platform's e-commerce features make it easy to turn a hobby into a source of income.

Redefining Success and Community

The traditional markers of success—a high-paying corporate job, a house, a car—are being questioned. The report shows that many young Chinese now define success by personal fulfilment, work-life balance, and the strength of their social connections.

Online communities play a crucial role here. Xiaohongshu serves as a space where people find like-minded individuals who share their niche interests, whether it is urban gardening, vintage clothing, or board games. These communities provide validation and support, replacing some of the social functions that were once filled by physical neighbourhoods or workplaces.

Looking ahead, these trends are likely to deepen. As the economic environment remains uncertain, the focus on value, experience, and well-being will continue to shape consumer behaviour. Brands and platforms will need to adapt to a generation that is more discerning, digital-native, and driven by purpose than any before it.
