2026 half-yearly round-up: Best Hindi films ranked; Dhurandhar 2 misses the list

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hindustan Times releases half-yearly ranking of best Hindi films of 2026
  • Dhurandhar 2, despite high expectations, does not feature in the list
  • Indie dramas and a few masala blockbusters top the chart
  • Critics praise fresh storytelling over franchise fatigue

Half-yearly verdict: The best of Hindi cinema in 2026

Hindustan Times has released its half-yearly round-up of the best Hindi films of 2026, and the list has sparked conversations. The lineup shows a clear tilt toward original storytelling, with franchise entries struggling to hold their own.

Notably, Dhurandhar 2—one of the year's most anticipated sequels—did not make the cut. The film, which had a strong opening at the box office, failed to impress critics with its narrative and execution. Industry insiders say the sequel suffered from the weight of expectations and a formulaic plot.

Indie films lead the charge

The top slots are dominated by smaller, character-driven films. Mitti Ki Khushboo, a rural drama about a farmer’s daughter, has been praised for its authentic performances and nuanced direction. Critics have called it a “quiet masterpiece” that resonates long after the credits roll.

Another standout is Chandni Chowk Confidential, a thriller set in the narrow lanes of old Delhi. The film’s tight screenplay and layered characters have earned it a spot in the top three. It has also done well at the box office, proving that content-driven cinema can find an audience.

Masala entertainers that worked

Not all the best films are indie. Raftaar, an action-comedy starring a popular young actor, has been lauded for its energetic pacing and sharp dialogue. It is one of the few masala films that blends mass appeal with critical respect.

Zara Hatke, a family comedy about a middle-class couple’s misadventures, has also won hearts. Its relatable humour and warm performances have made it a sleeper hit. The film’s success underscores a growing appetite for light-hearted, well-written comedies.

What went wrong with Dhurandhar 2

The exclusion of Dhurandhar 2 from the top ranks has been a talking point. While the first film was a surprise hit, the sequel appears to have lost the freshness that made its predecessor special. Critics pointed to a predictable storyline and underwhelming villain as key weaknesses.

Box office figures for the film have been decent but not spectacular. Trade analysts suggest that the film’s performance was hurt by mixed word-of-mouth after a strong opening weekend. The makers have not yet commented on the ranking.

Audience trends and industry takeaways

The half-yearly list reflects a broader shift in audience preferences. Viewers are increasingly rewarding films that take risks, whether in subject matter or storytelling style. Sequels and big-budget spectacles no longer guarantee a spot in the year’s best-of lists.

Directors and producers are taking note. Several upcoming projects have been reworked to focus on script strength rather than star power alone. The trend is likely to continue in the second half of 2026.

What remains to be seen is whether the second half of the year can match the first. Releases like the period epic Samrat and the psychological thriller Andhera are already generating buzz. If they deliver, the year-end list could look very different.
