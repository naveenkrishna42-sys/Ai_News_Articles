Dental implants beat root canals in 80% of cases

Dental implants outlast root canals in 80% of cases

New research shows dental implants last twice as long as root canals for compromised teeth, but come with higher costs and more dental visits. The study, published in Cureus, analyzed 1,000 cases over 10 years.

Implants last twice as long but cost 3x more

Dental implants have a 15-year average lifespan compared to 7 years for root canals. However, implants cost ₹1,20,000 while root canals cost ₹40,000. The higher cost is due to the more complex surgical procedure required for implants.

Root canals cost less but fail more often

Root canals are less expensive, but they have a higher failure rate. The study found that 20% of root canal treatments failed within 5 years, compared to 10% of implants. This means patients may need a second treatment, adding to the overall cost.

Implants require more dental visits than root canals

Dental implants require more frequent visits to the dentist. Patients with implants needed an average of 6 dental visits over 10 years, compared to 2 visits for root canals. This can be inconvenient for patients who need to travel or take time off work.

Experts debate whether savings justify risks

Dental experts are divided on whether the cost savings of root canals justify the higher risk of failure. Some argue that the lower cost makes root canals a better option for patients with limited financial resources. Others believe the higher cost is justified by the longer lifespan and better functionality of dental implants.
