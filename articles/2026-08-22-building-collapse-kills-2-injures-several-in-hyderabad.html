Two killed, several injured as 7-storey building collapses in Hyderabad

Hyderabad, August 22, 2026 - A 7-storey under-construction building collapsed in Kondapur, Hyderabad, killing two people and injuring several others. The building collapsed onto another apartment, exacerbating the situation. Emergency services and rescue teams have been rushed to the scene to assist in the rescue and relief operations.

Emergency services and rescue teams at the scene

Emergency services, including fire brigade, police, and medical teams, have been deployed to the site. Rescue operations are underway to locate and extricate any trapped individuals. The injured have been rushed to nearby hospitals for treatment. Authorities have cordoned off the area to ensure safety and prevent any further accidents.

Authorities investigating cause of the collapse

Officials have not yet confirmed the cause of the collapse. However, they are investigating whether structural issues or other factors contributed to the incident. The building was under construction, and safety measures were reportedly not fully implemented. Authorities are working to ensure the safety of the surrounding areas and to prevent any further incidents.

Community and neighbours affected

The collapse has affected the local community and neighbours. Many residents have expressed concern and shock over the incident. The Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority (HMDA) has been informed about the incident, and they are coordinating with local authorities to address the situation. The community is coming together to support the affected families and provide assistance during this difficult time.

What happens next

As investigations continue, authorities will likely focus on determining the cause of the collapse and ensuring the safety of the surrounding areas. The HMDA and other relevant agencies will work to address any structural or safety concerns that may have contributed to the incident. The community will continue to support the affected families and provide assistance during this challenging time. The incident serves as a reminder of the importance of strict adherence to safety regulations in construction projects.
