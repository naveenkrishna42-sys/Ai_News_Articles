Ram Kapoor reveals he helped his father plan his death on Lock Upp

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ram Kapoor said he helped his terminally ill father plan his death.
  • The actor broke down while recounting the experience on Lock Upp 2.
  • Kapoor revealed his mother and sister do not speak to him over the decision.

Mumbai: Actor Ram Kapoor made a startling and emotional revelation on the reality show Lock Upp 2. He said he helped his terminally ill father plan his own death.

The confession came during a conversation with host Kangana Ranaut. Kapoor broke down as he described his father's final days. The segment has since gone viral.

A painful decision

Kapoor said his father was suffering from a terminal illness. The actor claimed his father was in immense pain and had lost the will to live. "He asked me for help. He wanted to end his suffering," Kapoor said on the show.

According to Kapoor, he agreed to assist his father. He described the act as an act of love, not abandonment. "I helped him plan his death," Kapoor stated, his voice cracking with emotion.

Family fallout

The actor also revealed that his decision created a permanent rift in his family. He claimed his mother and sister stopped speaking to him after the incident. "They don't talk to me. It's been years," he said.

Kapoor did not provide further details about the specific circumstances. He did not specify when the event took place. The show's producers have not commented on the revelation.

Legal and ethical questions

The actor's admission has raised questions. Assisted suicide is illegal in India under Section 309 of the Indian Penal Code. Active euthanasia is also not permitted under Indian law.

Legal experts say such a public confession could invite scrutiny. However, no police complaint has been filed so far. Kapoor's statement remains a personal disclosure on a reality show, not a legal deposition.

What happens next

Ram Kapoor has not made any further statements since the episode aired. His representatives have not responded to requests for comment. It remains to be seen whether the revelation will have any legal or professional repercussions for the actor.
