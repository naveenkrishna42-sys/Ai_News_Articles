Sensex 'Flash Crash' Raises F&O Market Concerns

📅 2026-08-28 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex surged from Rs 1 lakh to Rs 44 lakh in 5 minutes during closing auction
  • Sebi's CAS and F&O curbs are still under implementation
  • Experts debate whether the 'Flash Crash' was a one-off event or a sign of deeper issues

Sensex 'Flash Crash' During Closing Auction

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) Sensex index experienced a dramatic surge from Rs 1 lakh to Rs 44 lakh in just five minutes during the closing auction session on a futures and options (F&O) expiry day. This sudden and sharp increase in the index value, often referred to as a 'Flash Crash', has sparked widespread discussion and concern among traders and market regulators.

Why This Matters

The 'Flash Crash' event is not just a one-off anomaly. It raises serious questions about the stability and resilience of India's financial markets, particularly during high-volatility periods like F&O expiry days. The sudden and extreme price movements can lead to significant financial losses for investors, especially those who are not well-versed in the intricacies of the stock market.

Sebi's CAS and F&O Curbs

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (Sebi) has been working on implementing the Circuit Breaker and Surveillance Mechanism (CAS) and curbs on F&O trading to prevent such extreme market movements. However, the progress of these measures is still a work in progress, as evidenced by the recent 'Flash Crash' event.

Expert Opinions and Analysis

Experts in the financial sector have varying opinions on whether the 'Flash Crash' was a one-off event or a sign of deeper issues in the market. Some believe that the event was a result of a combination of factors, including high volatility, lack of liquidity, and the absence of effective market surveillance mechanisms. Others argue that the event highlights the need for more robust and proactive measures to prevent such extreme market movements.

Pros and Cons of the 'Flash Crash' Event

  • Pros:
    • Brought attention to the need for stronger market surveillance mechanisms
    • Highlighted the potential risks associated with F&O trading
  • Cons:
    • Led to significant financial losses for investors
    • Raised concerns about the stability of India's financial markets

In conclusion, the 'Flash Crash' event during the Sensex closing auction has raised serious concerns about the stability and resilience of India's financial markets. It has also highlighted the need for more robust and proactive measures to prevent such extreme market movements. The implementation of Sebi's CAS and F&O curbs is a step in the right direction, but more needs to be done to ensure the stability of India's financial markets.
