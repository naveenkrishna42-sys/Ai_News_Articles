Mithoon on Ve Junoon success: Reuniting with Vishesh Films feels like coming home

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mithoon expresses joy over the positive response to Ve Junoon
  • He describes reuniting with Vishesh Films as a homecoming
  • The composer reflects on the creative synergy with the banner

Mithoon, the acclaimed music composer, is basking in the success of his latest track Ve Junoon. The song, which marks his return to Vishesh Films after a gap, has struck a chord with audiences. In a recent interaction, Mithoon shared his thoughts on the overwhelming response and what the collaboration means to him.

A creative reunion

Mithoon did not hold back his emotions when discussing his return to Vishesh Films, the iconic production house founded by Mahesh Bhatt and Mukesh Bhatt. "Reuniting with Vishesh Films feels like coming home," he said. The composer has a long history with the banner, having created some of his most memorable work for their films.

He credited the team's trust and creative freedom for making the experience special. "They understand music and let the artist breathe. That is rare," he added.

Response to Ve Junoon

The track Ve Junoon has been trending since its release. Mithoon acknowledged the love pouring in from fans and critics alike. "The response has been beyond expectations. It feels great when people connect with your work," he said.

The composer noted that the song's energy and intensity have resonated with listeners. Ve Junoon brings together powerful vocals and a stirring composition, a signature Mithoon style. He thanked the listeners for embracing the track so warmly.

Looking back at the journey

Mithoon's association with Vishesh Films dates back over a decade. He has delivered several hit numbers for the banner, including tracks from Aashiqui 2 and Hamari Adhuri Kahani. Each collaboration, he said, has been a learning experience.

"Every time I work with them, I grow as a musician. They push me to explore new boundaries," Mithoon explained. The composer believes that this synergy is what makes their projects stand out.

What makes Ve Junoon special

Mithoon described Ve Junoon as a track that captures raw emotion and passion. He spent months fine-tuning the composition to ensure it matched the film's vision. "The song had to reflect the protagonist's journey. It needed to be intense yet melodic," he said.

He also praised the vocalists who brought the song to life. Their rendition, he said, added layers of depth to the music. The result is a track that feels both contemporary and rooted in tradition.

Fans have already started speculating about more collaborations between Mithoon and Vishesh Films. While the composer did not confirm any upcoming projects, he left the door open. "I am always willing to work with them. It feels like family," he said.

The music industry has taken note of this reunion. Many believe that Mithoon's return to Vishesh Films signals a new chapter for both the composer and the banner. With Ve Junoon already a hit, expectations are high for what comes next.

Mithoon continues to work on multiple projects, but his heart remains with the music. As he put it, "At the end of the day, it is all about the melody. If the melody connects, everything else falls into place."

What lies ahead for Mithoon and Vishesh Films? Industry insiders are watching closely, but the composer is keeping details under wraps. For now, he is enjoying the moment and the love for Ve Junoon.
