2026 Mercedes-Maybach GLS 680 Facelift Debuts With Upgrades

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Upgraded 4.0-litre V8 engine with 557 bhp and 770 Nm torque
  • Revised front grille, new wheel designs, and updated lighting
  • Enhanced MBUX infotainment with Maybach-specific graphics
  • India launch expected later this year, prices likely above Rs 3.5 crore

Mercedes-Maybach has taken the wraps off the updated GLS 680, bringing a fresh wave of luxury and performance to its flagship SUV. The 2026 facelift arrives with a sharper design, a more powerful V8 engine, and a host of technology upgrades aimed at keeping it ahead in the ultra-luxury segment.

Engine and Performance Boost

Under the bonnet, the GLS 680 now packs a 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 that churns out 557 bhp and 770 Nm of peak torque. That is a noticeable bump over the outgoing model, which produced 550 bhp and 730 Nm.

The mild-hybrid system has been retained. A 48-volt integrated starter-generator adds a temporary 20 bhp boost during hard acceleration. Mercedes claims the SUV can sprint from 0 to 100 km/h in just 4.6 seconds. Top speed remains electronically limited to 250 km/h.

Exterior and Interior Changes

The facelift brings a redesigned front grille with vertical chrome slats, a hallmark of Maybach design. The bumper has been reshaped with larger air intakes, and the headlamps now feature updated LED graphics. New 21- and 22-inch wheel options are available, including exclusive Maybach multi-spoke designs.

At the rear, the taillights have been tweaked with a more intricate light signature. The dual exhaust tips remain, now integrated into a revised rear bumper.

Inside, the cabin gets new colour and trim combinations. The MBUX infotainment system has been upgraded with Maybach-specific displays and animations. Rear passengers get reclining seats with massage, heating, and ventilation functions. A new fragrance diffuser and upgraded Burmester 3D surround sound system are also part of the package.

Suspension and Ride Quality

The GLS 680 continues with the E-Active Body Control suspension, which uses a 48-volt system to counteract body roll and pitch. It can also scan the road ahead with a camera to adjust damping in real time. The result is a ride that remains plush even on broken tarmac.

Mercedes has also recalibrated the air suspension for better comfort at low speeds and improved stability during highway cruising.

India Launch and Pricing

Mercedes-Benz India is expected to bring the facelifted GLS 680 to the local market within the next few months. The current model retails at around Rs 3.5 crore (ex-showroom). The updated version will likely see a modest price hike given the added equipment and engine upgrades.

The SUV will compete directly with the Bentley Bentayga and the Range Rover SV Autobiography in the ultra-luxury SUV space. Deliveries of the updated model have already begun in select global markets.

The facelift ensures the Maybach GLS retains its position as a symbol of automotive opulence. With more power and an even more pampered cabin, it continues to target buyers who demand the finest in motoring. Watch for an official India announcement in the coming weeks.
