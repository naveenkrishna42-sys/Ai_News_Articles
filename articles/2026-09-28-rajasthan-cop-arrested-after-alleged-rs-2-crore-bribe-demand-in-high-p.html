Rajasthan Cop Arrested After Alleged Rs 2 Crore Bribe Demand in High-Profile Case

The Allegation: A ₹2 Crore Bribe Demand

A senior officer of Rajasthan’s Security Operations Group (SOG) was accused of demanding a ₹2 crore bribe to halt proceedings in a case related to the February suicide of a university lecturer. The allegation surfaced through whistleblowers and informal reporting networks before the Anti‑Corruption Bureau (ACB) took formal action.

Scope of the Demand

The officer allegedly promised that the complaint would be dropped, the file sealed, and any public inquiries muted if ₹2 crore were paid. The sum is a staggering amount for the alleged bribe, equivalent to 8 months of the average annual salary of an Indian bureaucrat.

Arrest and Investigation: ACB’s Move

On 27 September 2026, the Rajasthan ACB executed a warrant, arresting the officer at his residence. The Act was accompanied by the arrest of a third‑party middleman who reportedly acted as the financier and liaison between the accused and purported payers. An additional Superintendent of Police was also detained for tampering with evidence.

Legal Charges and Process

The officer faces charges under the Prevention of Corruption Act for demanding a bribe and under Section 120B of the IPC for criminal conspiracy. The middleman is charged under Sections 420 (cheating) and 201 (abetting). The case is currently in the preliminary inquiry stage, with a mandatory provisional detention of 60 days as per Section 482 IPC.

Immediate Consequences and Potential Verdict

Charges filed against the officer carry a maximum penalty of 10 years’ imprisonment and a fine up to the amount of the bribe demanded. Should the court confirm the allegations, he will face both criminal liability and a possible downgrading of his rank, as stipulated by the Police Act, 1861.

Impact on the Original Case

Once the officer is removed from handling the lawsuit, investigators of the original death complaint have a clear path to pursue the case without undue influence, eliminating the risk of obstruction and ensuring procedural integrity.

Long‑Term Impact on Law‑Enforcement Culture

The arrest signals an active stance by state authorities against uneven influence. However, the fact that a high‑ranking officer could leverage a multi‑crore bribe risk exposes systemic weaknesses. Law‑enforcement agencies will need to strengthen monitoring, enforce stricter asset‑and‑income disclosure mechanisms, and introduce biometric checks to verify officer conduct independent of political intervention.

Public Perception and Trust

Transparency in the investigation and timely court rulings will be essential to restore public faith. Any perception of selective enforcement could exacerbate societal cynicism, while a swift, fair verdict could serve as a deterrent for future malingering.

Key Insights and Takeaways

  • Pro: Demonstrates accountability; government action against corruption.
  • Pro: Potential restoration of public trust.
  • Cons: Possible erosion of morale in police ranks if perceived as punitive.
  • Cons: Requires sustained reforms to prevent recurrence.
