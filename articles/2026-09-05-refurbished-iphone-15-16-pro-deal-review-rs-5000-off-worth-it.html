Hands-on testing of the iPhone 15 Pro and 16 Pro platforms confirms flagship-class responsiveness across messaging, multitasking, and AAA gaming titles. The A17 Pro and A18 Pro silicon easily handle console-grade workloads such as Resident Evil Village and Assassin's Creed Mirage, with thermal headroom that holds up under sustained load thanks to the titanium thermal architecture.

A unit at 90 percent health will deliver roughly 90 percent of original endurance, meaning the iPhone 16 Pro still comfortably survives a heavy 14-hour day, while the iPhone 15 Pro may require a top-up by evening under intensive use. Competition & Market Alternatives

The refurbished iPhone 15 Pro at this price tier competes directly with brand-new mid-range Android flagships such as the Samsung Galaxy S24 (Rs. 59,999) and OnePlus 12 (Rs. 64,999). The Cashify refurb remains the most affordable route into the A18 Pro generation.
