Cheap AI visuals hurt Vijay's Rs 500 crore farewell film Jana Nayagan

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jana Nayagan, starring Thalapathy Vijay, is a Rs 500 crore farewell film facing backlash for poor AI-generated visuals.
  • Director H Vinoth defends the film, urging critics not to judge it by box office numbers alone.
  • The film crossed Rs 150 crore in India but saw a big Monday drop, earning only Rs 10.15 crore on day 5.
  • AI visuals are undermining the film's quality, leading to mixed reviews and a roadblock in Chennai.
  • Despite criticism, the film remains a strong performer at the box office, with fans showing support.

Thalapathy Vijay's highly anticipated farewell film, Jana Nayagan, has hit a rough patch. The film, made on a staggering Rs 500 crore budget, is facing harsh criticism for its subpar AI-generated visuals. Fans and critics alike have pointed out that the cheap AI effects are undermining the overall quality of the movie.

Director H Vinoth defends the film

Director H Vinoth has come out in defence of Jana Nayagan. In a recent statement, he urged critics not to judge the film solely by its box office performance. 'Don't judge Jana Nayagan by box office,' Vinoth said, emphasising that the film has more to offer than just numbers.

Despite the director's plea, the AI visuals have become a major talking point. Many have noted that the effects look rushed and unpolished, which is surprising for a film of this scale. The criticism has been particularly harsh in Chennai, where the film has hit a roadblock in terms of audience reception.

Box office performance

On the financial front, Jana Nayagan has had a mixed run. The film crossed the Rs 150 crore mark in India, a significant achievement by any measure. However, the momentum seems to be slowing down. On day 5, the film saw a big Monday drop, earning only Rs 10.15 crore.

The drop is a concern for the makers, especially given the film's massive budget. While the opening weekend was strong, the weekday numbers suggest that word-of-mouth might not be as positive as expected. The AI visuals are likely a contributing factor to this trend.

AI visuals: A double-edged sword

The use of AI in filmmaking is not new, but Jana Nayagan has shown how it can backfire. The cheap AI visuals have been described as a distraction, taking away from the film's narrative and performances. This has led to mixed reviews, with some calling it a missed opportunity for Vijay's farewell project.

Fans remain loyal, but even they have expressed disappointment. Social media is flooded with comparisons to other big-budget films that have used AI more effectively. The backlash has forced the team to reconsider their approach, though no official changes have been announced.

What happens next for Jana Nayagan will depend on how the makers address the criticism. Whether they rework the visuals or let the box office speak for itself, the coming weeks will be crucial for Vijay's farewell film.
