Mumbai Mayor allocates Rs 25 lakh for social media engagement

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Budget of Rs 25 lakh allocated for social media promotion.
  • The initiative aims to increase the reach of mayoral activities online.
  • The expenditure has drawn scrutiny regarding the necessity of paid engagement.

Public funds for digital visibility

The office of the Mumbai Mayor has moved to allocate Rs 25 lakh for a social media promotion campaign. This expenditure is earmarked for increasing visibility and engagement on various digital platforms, according to reports.

The move aims to ensure that the civic body’s outreach efforts reach a wider audience. Officials have indicated that the budget covers the cost of boosting posts and managing digital presence to keep citizens informed about ongoing municipal projects.

The debate over expenditure

Critics have raised questions about whether public funds should be used to purchase 'likes' and digital interactions. The decision to spend a significant sum on virtual metrics has triggered a conversation about the prioritization of municipal spending.

While the administration maintains that digital communication is vital in modern governance, the scale of the allocation has surprised some observers. The debate centers on whether such funds might have been better utilized for tangible infrastructure or civic amenities.

Transparency in digital outreach

The municipal office is yet to release a detailed breakdown of how the Rs 25 lakh will be distributed across specific platforms. Transparency advocates are calling for clear documentation on how these digital agencies are selected and what metrics constitute success for this investment.

Historically, civic bodies have relied on traditional media and public notices to disseminate information. This shift toward high-budget digital marketing represents a distinct change in the municipal communication strategy.

  • Budget allocation: Rs 25 lakh
  • Primary objective: Enhanced public engagement
  • Platform focus: Social media outreach

The administration is expected to face further questioning in the coming days as opposition members seek clarity on the procurement process. Observers will be watching to see if the municipal corporation provides a detailed audit of the campaign's impact once the funds are deployed.
