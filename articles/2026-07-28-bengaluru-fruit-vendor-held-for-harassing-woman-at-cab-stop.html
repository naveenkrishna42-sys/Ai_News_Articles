Bengaluru fruit vendor held for harassing woman at cab stop

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗

A 28-year-old fruit vendor was arrested in Bengaluru late Tuesday night for allegedly sexually harassing a woman who was waiting for a cab. The incident took place around 11 pm near a junction in the city's eastern suburb, police said.

Woman waiting for cab was targeted

The woman, a resident of the area, was standing at the junction waiting for a cab. The accused, who operates a fruit stall nearby, approached her. He allegedly touched her inappropriately and made lewd comments before trying to flee.

The woman immediately alerted her husband, who was on the phone with her at the time. The husband rushed to the spot and, with the help of bystanders, chased and caught the accused. Locals held him until police arrived.

Accused arrested, booked under IPC

Police reached the scene shortly after being called. They arrested the man and took him to the local police station. He has been booked under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including 354 (assault or criminal force to woman with intent to outrage her modesty) and 509 (word, gesture or act intended to insult the modesty of a woman).

Police said the accused has been operating the fruit stall in the area for over a year. He has no prior criminal record. Officials have not confirmed if he was under the influence of alcohol at the time of the incident.

Safety concerns in public spaces

The incident has once again highlighted safety concerns for women in public spaces in Bengaluru. Late-night commutes, especially for women waiting alone at cab stops or bus stands, remain a challenge despite increased police patrolling and CCTV coverage in many areas.

Local residents have demanded stricter action against repeat offenders and better lighting at junctions. Some have also called for more frequent police checks on street vendors operating near transit points late at night.

The woman has given her statement to the police. The accused is currently in custody and will be produced before a magistrate on Wednesday.

Police are reviewing CCTV footage from the junction to corroborate the woman's account. They have also urged citizens to immediately report any such incidents to the nearest police station or dial the women's helpline number.
