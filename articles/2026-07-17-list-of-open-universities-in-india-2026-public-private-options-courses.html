List of Open Universities in India 2026: Public & Private Options, Courses & Fees

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • Open universities in India offer flexible learning options through distance education.
  • 2026 list includes both public (state-run) and private open universities.
  • Courses range from undergraduate to postgraduate and diploma programs.
  • Fee structures vary significantly between public and private institutions.

For students and working professionals seeking flexible higher education, open universities in India remain a viable option. The year 2026 sees a mix of public and private institutions offering a range of courses through distance learning. Shiksha has compiled a detailed list covering these universities, their programmes, and fee structures.

Public Open Universities in India 2026

Public open universities are established by state or central governments. They typically offer lower fees compared to private counterparts. The Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU) remains the largest and most prominent, with a presence across the country.

State-level open universities like the Uttar Pradesh Rajarshi Tandon Open University (UPRTOU), the Karnataka State Open University (KSOU), and the Madhya Pradesh Bhoj (Open) University also continue to operate. These institutions provide undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma courses in arts, science, commerce, and vocational studies.

Fees for public open universities are generally affordable. For instance, a bachelor's degree programme at IGNOU can cost between Rs 5,000 and Rs 15,000 per year, depending on the subject. Master's programmes may range from Rs 6,000 to Rs 20,000 annually.

Private Open Universities in India 2026

Private open universities have gained traction in recent years, especially in states like Rajasthan, Maharashtra, and Gujarat. These institutions often offer more specialised courses and modern infrastructure for distance learners.

Notable private open universities include the Symbiosis Centre for Distance Learning (SCDL), the Amity University Distance Education, and the Sikkim Manipal University Distance Education. They provide courses in management, information technology, healthcare, and liberal arts.

Fees for private open universities are higher. An MBA programme can cost between Rs 50,000 and Rs 1.5 lakh for the entire duration. Undergraduate degrees may range from Rs 20,000 to Rs 60,000 per year.

Courses Offered by Open Universities

Open universities in India offer a wide array of programmes. Undergraduate degrees include BA, B.Sc, B.Com, BBA, and BCA. At the postgraduate level, MA, M.Sc, M.Com, MBA, and MCA are common.

Diploma and certificate courses are also available in fields like journalism, tourism, computer applications, and environmental studies. Many institutions now offer skill-based programmes aligned with industry needs.

Students should note that course availability can vary by university. The University Grants Commission (UGC) has recognised most open universities for distance education, but applicants must verify the accreditation status of their chosen institution.

Fee Structure and Affordability

Fee structures differ widely. Public open universities charge minimal fees, making education accessible to a larger population. For example, a BA programme at a state open university may cost less than Rs 10,000 for the entire course.

Private open universities, while more expensive, often provide instalment payment options and scholarships. Some institutions also offer online learning materials, which can reduce additional costs.

Students are advised to compare fees across universities before enrolling. Hidden costs, such as examination fees and study material charges, should also be factored in.

As of 2026, the list of open universities continues to expand, reflecting the growing demand for flexible education. Prospective students should research thoroughly and choose a programme that aligns with their career goals and budget.
