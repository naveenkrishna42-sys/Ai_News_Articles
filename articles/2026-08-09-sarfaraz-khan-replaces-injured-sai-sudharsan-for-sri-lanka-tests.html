Sarfaraz Khan replaces injured Sai Sudharsan for Sri Lanka Tests

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sai Sudharsan ruled out of Sri Lanka Test series due to injury
  • Sarfaraz Khan named as his replacement in India squad
  • Shaik Rasheed was also a contender for the spot

Sudharsan's setback opens door for Sarfaraz

The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) has confirmed that top-order batter Sai Sudharsan will miss the upcoming Test series against Sri Lanka due to injury. In his place, Mumbai's Sarfaraz Khan has been drafted into the squad.

The selection panel moved swiftly after Sudharsan's fitness concerns came to light. The left-handed batter, who had been in impressive form, has been ruled out of the two-match series.

Race for the replacement slot

Reports had earlier suggested that Shaik Rasheed and Sarfaraz Khan were the two frontrunners for the vacant spot. The selectors have now opted for the latter, rewarding Sarfaraz's consistent run in domestic cricket and his recent international exposure.

Sarfaraz, known for his solid technique and ability to score big in pressure situations, adds depth to the middle order. His inclusion gives the team management a flexible option, particularly against Sri Lanka's spin-heavy attack.

Fitness concerns prompt cautious approach

The decision to rule out Sudharsan comes amid broader concerns over player workload and injury management. The team management has adopted a cautious stance ahead of a busy international calendar.

Sudharsan's absence is a blow to India's batting reserves, but the squad remains strong. The selectors have shown they are willing to make tough calls to ensure players are fully fit for long series.

What Sarfaraz brings to the side

Sarfaraz has been a consistent performer in first-class cricket, with a string of high scores across seasons. His ability to play spin well makes him a valuable asset on Sri Lankan pitches.

He has previously represented India in limited-overs formats and has been knocking on the Test door for some time. This opportunity could be his chance to cement a place in the longest format.

The team will now finalise its preparations with Sarfaraz in the mix. The first Test is expected to test India's batting depth, and the new addition will be keen to make an impact.

Looking ahead

The series against Sri Lanka promises to be a competitive contest, and India will rely on a balanced squad to secure a win. All eyes will be on Sarfaraz to see if he can translate his domestic form into international success.
