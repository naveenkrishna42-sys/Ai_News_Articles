Bank of America Rewards Program Overview 2026

Bank of America’s rewards program centers on a set of credit cards that earn cash back or points on everyday spending. The program is tightly linked to the bank’s Preferred Rewards tier system, which can amplify earnings for customers who maintain qualifying balances across checking, savings, or investment accounts. Understanding the specific cards, their fee structures, and how rewards translate into value helps buyers select a product that matches their spending habits and financial goals.

Core Rewards Cards and Their Terms

The Bank of America rewards lineup features three primary cards. Each card differs in annual fee, earning rates, and redemption flexibility, allowing consumers to align the product with their primary spending categories.

How Preferred Rewards Boosts Earnings

Bank of America’s Preferred Rewards program has three tiers—Gold, Platinum, and Platinum Honors—based on the combined average monthly balance in linked Bank of America accounts. Qualifying customers receive a rewards booster of 25% (Gold), 50% (Platinum), or 75% (Platinum Honors) on the base earning rate of their credit card. For example, a Platinum Honors holder using the Customized Cash Rewards card would earn up to 5.1% cash back in the chosen 3% category (3% × 1.75). This boost applies to all eligible purchases and can significantly increase the effective return on everyday spending.

Redeeming Points and Cash Back

Rewards from Bank of America cards can be redeemed in several ways. Cash back from the Customized Cash Rewards card may be deposited as a statement credit, transferred to a linked Bank of America checking or savings account, or converted into gift cards. Points from the Premium Rewards and Travel Rewards cards are similarly convertible to statement credit or gift cards at a 1:100 point‑to‑dollar ratio. Travel Rewards points also provide enhanced value when booked through the Bank of America Travel Center, where each point is worth approximately $0.01 toward flights, hotels, car rentals, and cruise purchases. Redemptions are processed online via the Bank of America website or mobile app, with no minimum threshold for cash‑back transfers.

Who Should Consider These Cards

The Customized Cash Rewards card suits consumers who want no‑fee cash back and can rotate their 3% category to match seasonal spending (e.g., gas in summer, online shopping during holidays). The Premium Rewards card appeals to frequent travelers and diners who value a flat 2 points per $1 on those categories and are comfortable paying the $95 annual fee for the higher earn rate. The Travel Rewards card is ideal for individuals who prefer a simple, flat‑rate points card with no annual fee and who plan to redeem points through the Bank of America travel portal. In all cases, maintaining a qualifying balance to reach a Preferred Rewards tier can materially improve the effective return, making the cards more attractive for existing Bank of America customers with substantial deposits.
