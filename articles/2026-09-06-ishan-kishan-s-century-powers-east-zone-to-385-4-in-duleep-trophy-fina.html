Match Overview

On 5 September 2026, the Duleep Trophy final at Chepauk saw East Zone post a commanding 385 for 4 on day one, thanks to Ishan Kishan’s unbeaten 106. The innings set a new benchmark for first‑innings totals in a zone‑based final, giving East a psychological edge before the follow‑on.

South Zone, chasing 386, ended the session at 112 for 2, with early resistance from Mohammed Siraj. The partnership dynamics and pitch behavior suggested a high‑scoring contest, prompting stakeholders to reassess ticket and broadcast value.

Key Performances

1. Ishan Kishan – Century that reshaped the game

Kishan faced 124 balls, striking 12 fours and 7 sixes. His strike rate of 85.5 eclipsed his career average in the Duleep Trophy and highlighted his adaptability to the turning track. For selectors, the knock reinforces his candidacy for national limited‑overs duties.

2. South Zone Bowling – Siraj’s early impact

Mohammed Siraj claimed 2 wickets for 38 runs in 10 overs, extracting seam movement that troubled the East top order. However, the lack of support from the other bowlers allowed Kishan to settle and accelerate.

3. Supporting Cast – Contributions that mattered

Shubman Gill added a steady 58, while Ruturaj Gaikwad’s 42 off 55 balls anchored the middle overs. For South Zone, Shardul Thakur’s 1/45 kept the run rate in check, but the partnership between Siraj and Shubman was insufficient to overturn the deficit.

Commercial Angle: Ticketing, Broadcast, and Merchandise

The final attracted a sell‑out crowd of 35,000, with ticket tiers ranging from ₹1,200 for general admission to ₹5,500 for premium boxes. Streaming rights were secured by SonyLiv at ₹150 crore for the season, a 12 % increase over 2025, reflecting growing advertiser confidence.
