Sunrisers Leeds Start Hundred Campaign with Win, Litchfield Stars

Litchfield Leads the Charge

Phoebe Litchfield announced her arrival in The Hundred 2026 with a match-winning knock as Sunrisers Leeds defeated Trent Rockets in their opening fixture. The Australian left-hander looked in complete control, timing the ball cleanly under pressure.

Her innings came at a crucial time, steadying the Sunrisers' chase after early wickets. Litchfield's composure against both pace and spin stood out on a pitch that offered some assistance to the bowlers.

Sunrisers Chase Down Target

Chasing a competitive total set by Trent Rockets, Sunrisers Leeds lost quick wickets at the top. But Litchfield found able support from the middle order, rotating the strike and finding boundaries when needed.

The Rockets bowlers kept it tight in the middle overs, but Litchfield's ability to pierce the field and run hard between wickets kept the required rate in check. She finished unbeaten as the Sunrisers crossed the line with deliveries to spare.

Indian Stars in Focus

The Hundred 2026 features several Indian women's cricketers, including Smriti Mandhana, Jemimah Rodrigues, Deepti Sharma, and Richa Ghosh. Their performances will be closely watched by fans back home, especially with the Women's T20 World Cup on the horizon.

Mandhana, playing for a different franchise, will be key. Deepti Sharma's all-round abilities and Rodrigues' explosive batting make them vital cogs in their respective teams. Ghosh's wicketkeeping and lower-order hitting add depth.

Broadcast Boost for The Hundred

In a significant development for Indian viewers, JioStar has acquired exclusive rights to broadcast The Hundred 2026 in India. The deal ensures that fans can watch all matches live on television and digital platforms.

This move is expected to boost the tournament's popularity in India, a key cricket market. The involvement of top Indian players and the unique format of The Hundred have already generated considerable interest.

The Hundred 2026 continues with more group-stage matches. Teams will look to build momentum early in the competition. The tournament's fast-paced nature means every game is critical for qualification.

All eyes will now be on the upcoming fixtures, particularly those featuring the Indian stars. Their form and fitness will be under the scanner as they prepare for international assignments later this year.
