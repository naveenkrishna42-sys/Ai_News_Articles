Jharkhand CID raids 18 locations in JPSC exam irregularities case

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jharkhand CID raids 18 locations in JPSC exam irregularities case
  • Former JPSC chief Khiangte questioned for seven hours
  • Accused govt staffer cleared BARC, CRPF; protests demand retired judge probe
  • Cockroach Janta Party extends support to student protesters

Ranchi, August 3: The Jharkhand Criminal Investigation Department (CID) carried out raids at 18 locations on Monday as part of its widening probe into the JPSC exam irregularities case. The action comes amid mounting pressure from job aspirants who have been protesting in Ranchi, demanding a fair investigation into alleged paper leaks.

The CID's搜查 spanned multiple districts, though officials have not yet disclosed the specific addresses or what materials were seized. The raids mark an escalation in the investigation, which has already seen former Jharkhand Public Service Commission (JPSC) chief Khiangte questioned for seven hours.

Former JPSC chief grilled for seven hours

Khiangte, who headed the commission during the period under scrutiny, faced intense questioning by CID officials earlier this week. Sources said the former chief was asked about the examination process, handling of question papers, and the chain of custody before papers reached centres.

The questioning comes as part of a broader effort by the CID to trace how question papers were allegedly leaked and sold to candidates. Khiangte's role, if any, in the irregularities has not been officially confirmed, and no charges have been framed against him so far.

Accused govt staffer was a competitive exam whiz

In a curious twist, the CID has said that a Jharkhand government staffer accused in the case is a competitive exam enthusiast who had cleared exams conducted by BARC and CRPF. The revelation has added a layer of complexity to the investigation, with officials suggesting the accused may have used his expertise to facilitate the leak.

The staffer's knowledge of examination procedures is believed to have aided the alleged racket. Investigators are now examining his communication records and financial transactions to identify other individuals involved in the conspiracy.

Protests continue; demand for retired judge probe

Meanwhile, job aspirants in Ranchi have intensified their protests, demanding that a retired judge from outside Jharkhand lead the investigation. The protesters argue that an external probe would ensure impartiality, given the involvement of state government employees in the case.

The protests have drawn support from an unlikely quarter — the Cockroach Janta Party, a fringe political outfit, which has extended its support to the students. The party's backing, while symbolic, has helped keep the issue in the public eye.

What's at stake

The JPSC exam irregularities case has reignited concerns about the integrity of recruitment processes in the state. With thousands of aspirants affected, the CID's investigation is being closely watched by both the public and political circles.

The raids and the questioning of key officials suggest that the CID is leaving no stone unturned. However, the demand for an external probe remains a sticking point, and the government has not yet responded to it.

As the investigation progresses, all eyes will be on the CID's next steps — and whether it can deliver a clean report that restores faith in the examination system.
