Hyderabad Man Stabbed in US, MBT Appeals for Family Aid

Key points
  • A Hyderabad native was stabbed in the US.
  • The Majlis Bachao Tehreek (MBT) is seeking community support for the victim's family.
  • Details of the incident remain scarce as officials have not yet released a full statement.

A man from Hyderabad was stabbed in the United States, prompting the Majlis Bachao Tehreek (MBT) to call for community support for his family. The incident has sent ripples through the local community in Hyderabad, where the victim's relatives reside.

The MBT, a political party active in the Old City area, has taken up the cause. Party leaders have urged citizens to contribute to a relief fund for the family, who are grappling with the sudden tragedy. The precise location and circumstances of the stabbing in the US have not been officially confirmed.

MBT Steps In

MBT leaders held a press conference on Wednesday to announce their appeal. They described the victim as a hardworking individual who had moved to the US for better opportunities. The party is coordinating with community groups to gather donations.

“We cannot bring him back, but we must stand with his family in this hour of need,” a party spokesperson said. The MBT has set up a helpline number for those wishing to offer financial or logistical assistance.

Family in Distress

The victim’s family in Hyderabad has been left in shock. Neighbours and relatives have gathered at their home to offer condolences. The family has requested privacy as they await more details from US authorities.

Officials at the US consulate in Hyderabad have not yet issued a statement on the case. The Indian embassy in Washington is believed to be in touch with local law enforcement agencies.

What We Know So Far

Details remain sketchy. The stabbing reportedly occurred earlier this week. The victim’s identity has not been publicly released pending notification of next of kin. Local police in the US are investigating the incident.

It is unclear whether the attack was random or targeted. Authorities have not confirmed any arrests. The MBT has urged the government to expedite the process of repatriating the body if needed.

Community Reaction

The incident has sparked concern among the Telugu diaspora in the US. Many have taken to social media to express grief and offer help. Community organisations in the US are also mobilising to assist the family.

“This is a tragic reminder of the risks our youth face abroad,” said a local community leader in Hyderabad. “We must ensure the family gets all possible support.”

The MBT has promised to follow up with authorities on both sides of the ocean. The party plans to submit a memorandum to the Telangana government seeking state assistance for the family.

More details are expected in the coming days as investigations proceed.
