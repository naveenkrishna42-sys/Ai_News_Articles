7.4-Magnitude Earthquake Kills 20 in Colombia and Ecuador

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • At least 20 deaths reported after a 7.4-magnitude earthquake hits Colombia and Ecuador
  • A hospital in Cali, Colombia, was severely damaged in the quake
  • Rescue operations are underway as the situation is described as 'critical'

Quake Strikes Near Bogota

A powerful 7.4-magnitude earthquake rocked Colombia and neighboring Ecuador on Monday, killing at least 20 people and sending terrified residents into the streets. The epicenter was located near Bogota, the Colombian capital, according to initial reports.

The tremor struck at midday, catching many off guard. Buildings swayed for nearly a minute in the capital, and residents rushed out of offices and homes as alarms blared.

Hospital in Cali Hit Hard

In Cali, Colombia's third-largest city, a hospital was directly hit by the quake, causing significant structural damage. Emergency crews are working to evacuate patients and staff from the facility, though the full extent of casualties there has not yet been confirmed.

Local authorities have described the situation as "critical," with rescue teams racing against time to locate survivors in the rubble. Power outages and disrupted communication lines are hampering relief efforts in several affected areas.

Regional Impact and Response

The earthquake was also felt strongly in parts of Ecuador, particularly in border provinces. Officials in both countries have activated emergency protocols, but the scale of the disaster is still unfolding.

Colombia's disaster management agency has deployed search-and-rescue teams and medical aid to the worst-hit zones. The military has been put on standby to assist in relief operations.

International Reactions

News of the disaster has drawn reactions from around the world. Real Madrid, the Spanish football club, issued an official statement expressing its shock and solidarity with those affected, a nod to the disaster's global resonance.

Meanwhile, international aid agencies have offered support, though formal requests for assistance have yet to be made by either government.

What to Watch

As aftershocks continue to rattle the region, the death toll is expected to rise. Authorities are urging residents to stay clear of damaged structures and remain vigilant. The coming hours will be critical for rescue efforts, with the focus on reaching those still trapped under debris.
