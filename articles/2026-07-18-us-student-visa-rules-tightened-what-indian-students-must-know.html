US Student Visa Rules Tightened: What Indian Students Must Know

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • The US has imposed a fixed duration of stay for international students, replacing earlier flexible extensions.
  • PhD students and researchers face particular uncertainty over completing programs within the new 4-year limit.
  • India has stated it will engage with US authorities to address difficulties for Indian students.

The United States has tightened visa regulations for foreign students, introducing a fixed duration of stay that replaces the earlier system where students could remain as long as they made academic progress. The move, confirmed by multiple sources including Reuters and AP News, has sparked concern among Indian students — the second-largest group of international students in America.

Under the revised policy, student visa holders will now be granted a specific period of stay tied to their program's expected completion time, rather than an open-ended 'duration of status' that allowed extensions as long as the student remained enrolled. This change brings student visas in line with other non-immigrant visa categories.

What the New Rules Mean for Students

The most significant impact falls on PhD candidates and long-term researchers. Many doctoral programs in the US take five to seven years to complete, but the new rules reportedly cap the stay at four years for most students. Experts have asked how PhD students will finish their coursework and research within this compressed timeline.

"It's over for international researchers in the US," one expert told The Times of India, reflecting a sentiment that the policy could deter top talent from pursuing advanced degrees in America. The regulations also affect journalists and exchange visitors, tightening the overall framework for non-immigrant entry.

India's Response

India has taken note of the changes. The Ministry of External Affairs stated it will engage with US authorities to minimise difficulties for Indian students. "To minimise difficulties, India will engage with the US on revised visa policies," an official said, as reported by NDTV.

Indian students constitute a significant portion of the over one million international students in the US, contributing billions to the American economy annually. Any disruption in their ability to complete degrees could have ripple effects on both countries' academic and professional ecosystems.

Broader Context

The tightening aligns with the Trump administration's broader push to restrict immigration and ensure visa compliance. In recent months, the US has also increased scrutiny of student visa renewals and introduced stricter documentation requirements. The changes are part of a pattern of policy shifts that began after the previous administration's attempts to limit remote learning for international students during the pandemic.

Universities and student groups have criticised the move, arguing that it undermines academic freedom and the global nature of research. Many institutions are expected to file legal challenges or seek waivers for doctoral candidates.

Indian students planning to apply for US universities will now need to factor in the new time limits. Short-term programs — such as one-year master's degrees — may see less impact, but those pursuing long-term research face the greatest uncertainty.

The Indian government is expected to raise the issue in upcoming bilateral talks with the US. Whether Washington will offer exemptions or transitional arrangements for existing students remains unclear.
