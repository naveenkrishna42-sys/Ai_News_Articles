Jharkhand job exam protests intensify; ex-JPSC chief arrested

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Protests intensify over alleged irregularities in Jharkhand job exams
  • A student protester was injured in a police lathicharge
  • CID arrests former JPSC chairperson in connection with the recruitment scam
  • Political rivalry adds to pressure on the Soren government

Ranchi is on edge. What began as a simmering anger over alleged corruption in state recruitment exams has boiled over into sustained protests, with police action and political point-scoring now part of the daily rhythm on the streets.

The immediate trigger was the use of force. A student protester was injured in a police lathicharge, reportedly sustaining a head injury. The incident, captured on camera and shared widely, has added fuel to an already volatile movement.

Allegations of Corruption in Recruitment Exams

At the heart of the unrest are allegations of irregularities in examinations conducted by the Jharkhand Public Service Commission (JPSC). Protesters have been demanding a thorough investigation and action against those responsible for what they describe as a systemic scam that has denied deserving candidates their due.

These are not isolated complaints. The protests echo similar movements in neighbouring states, where youth have taken to the streets over paper leaks and manipulation in government job recruitment. The demand is straightforward: a fair and transparent process for public employment.

Police Action and Protesters' Response

Videos from the protests show a now-familiar scene in Indian cities: water cannons being turned on demonstrators, and protesters, in some cases, dancing in defiance as the water sprays over them. The contrast between the festive defiance and the serious undercurrent of anger has been striking.

The lathicharge, however, has raised the stakes. Reports of the injured student, with blood on his head, have become a rallying point for the demonstrators. Police have defended their actions as necessary to control a crowd that was turning violent, but protesters remain unconvinced.

Arrest of Former JPSC Chairperson

In a significant development, the Jharkhand Criminal Investigation Department (CID) has arrested the former chairperson of the JPSC in connection with the alleged irregularities in the recruitment exams. This arrest marks a concrete step in the official investigation, providing some ammunition to those who have long claimed that the rot runs deep.

The CID's move signals that the state government is under pressure to show results. However, critics argue that the arrest is too little, too late, and question whether the investigation will reach the higher echelons of power where the real decisions were made.

Political Tensions in Ranchi

The protests have also exposed deep political fissures. Rival parties in Ranchi are using the unrest to corner the Hemant Soren-led government. Opposition leaders have been quick to blame the administration for the alleged corruption and the heavy-handed police response, while the ruling side has accused them of politicising a serious issue.

The result is a divided city. While the protesters remain camped out, demanding justice, political workers from both sides are attempting to claim the narrative. This has kept the state government on edge, with the administration walking a tightrope between maintaining order and addressing legitimate grievances.

As the standoff continues, all eyes will be on the CID investigation and the government's next move. The arrest of the former JPSC chief is a start, but for the young protesters on the streets of Ranchi, it is only the beginning. They want a clean system, and they have made it clear they are not going anywhere until they see it.
