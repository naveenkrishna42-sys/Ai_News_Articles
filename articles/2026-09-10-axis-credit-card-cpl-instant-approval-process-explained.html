Eligibility Criteria

Before applying for the Axis Credit Card CPL, applicants must meet specific eligibility requirements set by Axis Bank. These criteria ensure that the card is issued to individuals with the financial capacity to manage credit responsibly.

  • Age: Primary applicant must be between 18 and 70 years old
  • Income: Minimum net monthly income of ₹25,000 for salaried individuals; ₹3 lakh annual income for self-employed professionals
  • Employment Status: Salaried employees with minimum 6 months at current employer, or self-employed with 2 years of business continuity
  • Credit Score: CIBIL score of 700 or above recommended for instant approval
  • Residential Status: Indian resident with valid KYC documents
  • Existing Relationship: Existing Axis Bank customers may receive pre-approved offers with relaxed criteria

Required Documents

The documentation process is streamlined for digital applications, though physical verification may be required in certain cases.

Identity Proof (Any One)

  • PAN Card (mandatory)

  • Voter ID Card
  • Driving License

Address Proof (Any One)

Income Proof

Rewards Program Deep Dive

The rewards structure is a critical differentiator for the CPL variant. Understanding the earning and redemption mechanics maximizes cardholder value.

Earning Rates

  • Base Earn Rate: 1 EDGE Reward point per ₹200 spent on all categories
  • Accelerated Categories: 5x points on dining, groceries, and fuel (capped at ₹1,000 points/month per category)
  • Online Spends: 2x points on e-commerce, food delivery, and streaming subscriptions
  • Milestone Benefits: 2,500 bonus points on annual spend of ₹2 lakh; 5,000 points on ₹4 lakh
  • Welcome Bonus: 1,000 EDGE points on first transaction within 30 days

Redemption Options

  • Statement Credit: 1 point = ₹0.30 (minimum 1,000 points)
  • Vouchers: Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, BigBasket at 1 point = ₹0.25
  • Air Miles: Transfer to airline partners at 1 point = 0.5 miles (varies by partner)
  • Merchandise: Catalog redemption at 1 point = ₹0.20 value
  • Fuel Surcharge Waiver: 1% waiver on fuel spends (max ₹400/month, min ₹400 transaction)

Points Validity

EDGE Reward points expire after 36 months from the date of accrual. No auto-extension on card renewal. Points are forfeited on card closure or upgrade/downgrade.
