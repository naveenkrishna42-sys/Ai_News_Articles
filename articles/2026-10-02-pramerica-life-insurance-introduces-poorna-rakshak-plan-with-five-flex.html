Overview of Pramerica Poorna Rakshak

Pramerica Life Insurance has launched Poorna Rakshak, a combined protection and savings solution designed to address multiple financial needs throughout a policyholder’s life. The product integrates a base life cover with optional riders that can be tailored to individual risk profiles and goals. By offering five distinct ways to plan, the insurer aims to simplify decision‑making for buyers who would otherwise need separate policies for death, illness, accident, premium waiver and savings.

Five Planning Options

1. Life Cover Option

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

The core component provides a death benefit payable to nominees upon the insured’s demise. Sum assured can be selected from ₹5 lakh to ₹50 lakh, with the amount influencing the base premium. This option ensures financial stability for dependents in the event of the policyholder’s untimely death.

2. Critical Illness Rider

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Policyholders may add a critical illness rider that pays a lump sum upon diagnosis of covered conditions such as cancer, heart attack, stroke or kidney failure. The rider sum assured typically ranges from ₹2 lakh to ₹20 lakh. Activation of this benefit does not affect the base life cover, allowing simultaneous claims if needed.

3. Accidental Death Benefit

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

An accidental death rider doubles or triples the base sum assured if the insured dies due to an accident. The multiplier is chosen at policy inception and remains fixed throughout the term. This feature is particularly relevant for individuals with high exposure to travel or occupational hazards.

4. Waiver of Premium

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

The waiver of premium rider ensures that future premiums are waived if the policyholder suffers a total and permanent disability or is diagnosed with a specified critical illness. The policy continues to provide all benefits without further payment, safeguarding coverage during periods of reduced income.

5. Savings Component (Money‑back)

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Poorna Rakshak includes a money‑back feature that returns a portion of the premiums as survival benefits at predetermined intervals (e.g., 20% after 5 years, 30% after 10 years, and the remaining 50% plus accrued bonuses at maturity). This element provides liquidity and helps meet mid‑term financial goals such as education or home purchase.

Premium Structure and Eligibility

Premiums are calculated based on age, gender, smoking status, sum assured and selected riders. For a 30‑year‑old non‑smoking male opting for ₹10 lakh life cover with no riders, the annual premium starts at approximately ₹4,500. Adding a critical illness rider of ₹5 lakh raises the premium to around ₹7,200 per year, while the accidental death benefit adds roughly ₹800 annually. Eligibility for the base cover spans ages 18 to 55; most riders are available up to age 50. Policy terms can be set between 10 and 30 years, allowing alignment with long‑term obligations.

Value Proposition for Buyers

From a buyer’s perspective, Poorna Rakshak offers convenience by consolidating multiple protections under a single policy, reducing administrative overhead and the risk of coverage gaps. The money‑back component addresses the common criticism that traditional term plans lack savings, providing a forced‑savings mechanism with guaranteed returns. Tax advantages under Section 80C for premiums paid and Section 10(10D) for maturity proceeds further enhance post‑tax yields. However, the bundled nature means that opting for all riders can push the annual premium above ₹15,000 for higher sum assured levels, which may strain budgets for younger buyers. Additionally, early surrender incurs charges that can erode the savings component, making the plan less suitable for those needing short‑term liquidity.

  • Flexible term and sum assured options suit varied financial horizons.
