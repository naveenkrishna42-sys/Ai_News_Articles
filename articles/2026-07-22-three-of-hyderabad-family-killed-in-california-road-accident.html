Three of Hyderabad family killed in California road accident

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Three members of a family from Hyderabad died in a road accident in California.
  • The deceased have been identified by family sources; officials are yet to release names.
  • The Indian consulate in San Francisco has been alerted and is assisting with formalities.
  • Telangana government officials are coordinating with US authorities for repatriation of bodies.

Fatal crash in California

Three members of a family from Hyderabad died in a road accident in California, officials confirmed on Tuesday. The mishap occurred on a highway in the state, though exact location and time have not been officially released.

Family sources identified the deceased as a couple and their young daughter. They were reportedly residents of the US for several years but maintained close ties with relatives in Hyderabad.

Consulate alerted

The Indian consulate in San Francisco has been informed about the incident. Consular officials are in touch with local law enforcement and the family's relatives to provide necessary assistance.

Telangana government authorities have also reached out to the US administration. Officials are working on documentation required for the repatriation of the bodies to India.

Relatives in shock

Relatives in Hyderabad are in deep shock. Neighbours and family friends have gathered at their residence in the city's Banjara Hills area. Many recalled the family's recent visit to India a few months ago.

The family had reportedly moved to the US over a decade ago. The couple was well-known in the local Telugu community in California.

Authorities have not yet confirmed whether any other passengers were injured in the crash. An investigation into the cause of the accident is underway.

Local police have said they are examining factors including speed and road conditions at the time of the crash. No arrests have been reported in connection with the incident.

Telangana's Non-Resident Indian cell has been activated to provide support to the bereaved family. The state government has assured all possible help for the swift repatriation of the remains.

The Indian Ministry of External Affairs is also monitoring the situation. Officials have advised the family to complete all formalities with the consulate at the earliest.

What next

The bodies are expected to be flown to Hyderabad within the next few days, pending clearance from US authorities. The family is awaiting final reports from the coroner's office before making travel arrangements.
