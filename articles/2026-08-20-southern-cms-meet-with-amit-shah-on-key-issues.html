Southern CMs meet with Amit Shah on key national issues

Chief Ministers of Southern states, including Tamil Nadu's M.K. Stalin, Karnataka's Siddaramaiah, and Andhra Pradesh's Y.S. Jaganmohan Reddy, met with Union Home Minister Amit Shah on Thursday to discuss critical national issues. The meeting, held in Delhi, aimed to address concerns and find common ground on matters affecting the region.

NEET, delimitation, and Cauvery river dispute take center stage

Among the key issues discussed were the NEET examination, delimitation of parliamentary and assembly constituencies, and the Cauvery river dispute. The CMs raised their respective states' concerns and sought a unified approach from the central government.

TN CM emphasizes cooperative federalism and strong states

Tamil Nadu Chief Minister M.K. Stalin highlighted the importance of cooperative federalism and stressed that strong states make for a stronger India. He emphasized the need for a balanced approach to address the issues discussed, ensuring that the interests of all states are considered.

DKS highlights delimitation concerns, Vijay-Satheesan interaction focuses on Cauvery river dispute

Devaraj Kumar Swamy, a prominent leader from Tamil Nadu, raised concerns about delimitation and sought clarity on the process. Meanwhile, the interaction between M.K. Stalin and Siddaramaiah focused on the Cauvery river dispute, with both CMs expressing their states' perspectives on the issue.

Southern Zonal Council meet sees CMs interact on sidelines

On the sidelines of the Southern Zonal Council meet, the CMs engaged in discussions with each other, exchanging views on various regional issues. The meet provided a platform for the CMs to interact and find common ground on matters affecting the Southern states.

The meeting between the Southern CMs and Amit Shah underscores the importance of addressing key national issues through a unified approach. As the discussions continue, the focus will be on finding mutually acceptable solutions that benefit all states and the nation as a whole.
