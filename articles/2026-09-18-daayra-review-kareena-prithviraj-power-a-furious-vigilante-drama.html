Daayra Review: A Furious Vigilante Drama Posing As A Police Procedural

Meghna Gulzar's Daayra arrives with the promise of a gritty police procedural, but within the first thirty minutes, it sheds that skin to reveal a furious vigilante drama. The film, starring Kareena Kapoor and Prithvaj Sukumaran, is a study in rage and retribution, wrapped in the guise of a cop investigation. It's a bold, if uneven, swing that demands attention.

The Premise: When Justice Fails, Fury Takes Over

The story follows a police officer (Kareena Kapoor) investigating a series of brutal killings that mirror the methods of a notorious vigilante. As the body count rises, the line between law enforcement and extrajudicial justice blurs. Prithviraj Sukumaran plays a key figure in this cat-and-mouse game, delivering a performance that is both menacing and magnetic. The film's core question—can a system heal itself, or does it need an outside hand?—is posed with unflinching intensity.

Performance Breakdown: Kareena and Prithviraj at Their Peak

Kareena Kapoor delivers one of her most layered performances. Her cop is feminine, wears lipstick, and isn't 'manly'—a deliberate choice that subverts the typical Bollywood heroine. She brings a quiet ferocity to the role, though the script doesn't always know what to do with her. Prithviraj Sukumaran, meanwhile, impresses with a restrained yet powerful turn. His chemistry with Kareena crackles, even when the narrative stumbles.

Pros and Cons: What Works, What Doesn't

  • Pros:
    • Kareena Kapoor's nuanced portrayal of a feminine cop breaks stereotypes.
    • Prithviraj Sukumaran delivers a career-best performance.
    • Meghna Gulzar's direction is inspired by hope and outrage, giving the film emotional heft.
    • The vigilante angle provides thrilling, unpredictable sequences.
  • Cons:
    • The film struggles to balance its dual identity—procedural vs. vigilante—leading to tonal shifts.
    • Kareena's character is underutilized in the second half.
    • Some plot points feel rushed, undermining the impact of key revelations.

Box Office and Audience Reception

Early trends suggest Daayra has opened to a strong response in urban centres, with multiplex audiences appreciating its bold themes. However, the film's dark tone and complex narrative may limit its appeal in mass circuits. With a reported budget of approximately ₹85 crore (including marketing), the film needs a solid run to recover costs. Given the star power and director's pedigree, a lifetime collection of ₹120-140 crore seems achievable, but word-of-mouth will be crucial.

Final Verdict: A Flawed but Ferocious Watch

Daayra is not a perfect film, but it is an important one. It challenges the conventions of the police procedural and offers a platform for two powerhouse performers to shine. Meghna Gulzar's conviction is evident in every frame, even when the script falters. For viewers seeking a gritty, thought-provoking drama that refuses to play safe, Daayra is worth the ticket. It may not fully reconcile its dual nature, but its fury is unforgettable.
