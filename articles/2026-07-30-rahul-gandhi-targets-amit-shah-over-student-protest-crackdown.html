Rahul Gandhi Targets Amit Shah Over Student Protest Crackdown

📅 2026-07-30 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Rahul Gandhi demanded PM Modi sack Amit Shah over alleged police brutality against student protesters.
  • Gandhi questioned Shah's absence from Parliament, calling it a sign of guilt.
  • BJP MP Anurag Thakur moved a privilege notice against Rahul Gandhi over his remarks in the Lok Sabha.

Rahul Gandhi has turned up the heat on the Narendra Modi government, directly targeting Home Minister Amit Shah over the police response to recent student protests. The Leader of the Opposition demanded Prime Minister Modi sack Shah, accusing the Home Minister of overseeing a brutal crackdown on young demonstrators.

Speaking to reporters, Gandhi said Shah's continued absence from Parliament during the ongoing session 'reeks of guilt'. The Congress leader did not mince words, calling the police action an assault on democratic rights.

Privilege Notice in Lok Sabha

The BJP hit back within hours. Party MP Anurag Thakur moved a privilege notice against Rahul Gandhi in the Lok Sabha, citing his remarks as a breach of parliamentary conduct. The notice accuses Gandhi of making false and inflammatory statements that bring disrepute to the House.

Thakur argued that Gandhi's comments were aimed at undermining the government and the office of the Home Minister. The Speaker is yet to rule on the notice.

Student Protests and Police Action

The trigger for this political clash is a series of student protests that turned violent earlier this week. Videos and photographs circulating on social media show police using batons and water cannons against unarmed students. The exact number of injured is not yet known, but local hospital sources said dozens have been treated for injuries.

The protests began over a proposed change in university funding rules, which students say will make higher education unaffordable for the poor. The government has defended the police action, calling it necessary to maintain public order.

Amit Shah's Absence Questioned

Rahul Gandhi seized on Amit Shah's absence from Parliament during this crisis. The Home Minister has not been seen in the Lok Sabha for three consecutive days. Gandhi said this is an abdication of responsibility.

'If the Home Minister has nothing to hide, why is he running away from answering questions in the House?' Gandhi asked. He demanded a full statement from Shah on the floor of Parliament.

The BJP has dismissed the charge, saying Shah is attending to pre-scheduled constituency work. The party accused Gandhi of playing politics with a law-and-order situation.

What Comes Next

The privilege motion against Rahul Gandhi will be examined by the Lok Sabha's privileges committee. If upheld, it could lead to a reprimand or suspension. Meanwhile, the student protests show no signs of abating, with more demonstrations planned for the weekend. The political fallout from this clash between the government and the opposition is far from over.
