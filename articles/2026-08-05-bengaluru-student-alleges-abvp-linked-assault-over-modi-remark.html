Bengaluru student alleges ABVP-linked assault over Modi remark

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Student alleges assault by ABVP members after questioning PM Modi
  • Incident reported in Bengaluru; police investigation underway
  • ABVP has not yet responded to the allegations

A student in Bengaluru has lodged a complaint alleging he was assaulted by members of the Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP) after he questioned Prime Minister Narendra Modi's leadership. The incident, which took place on the city's university campus, has sparked concerns about freedom of expression on educational premises.

The student, whose identity has not been officially disclosed, claimed that the attackers confronted him with the words, "You dare demand Modi's resignation?" before physically assaulting him. He has since filed a formal complaint with the local police, who have initiated an inquiry into the matter.

Details of the alleged assault

According to the student's statement, the incident occurred during a discussion session on campus, where political topics were being debated. The student reportedly expressed his opinion on the current government's policies, which led to a heated exchange with ABVP members present at the scene.

The complainant alleges that the situation escalated quickly, with the accused individuals using abusive language and then resorting to physical force. He sustained minor injuries, though medical reports have not been made public yet. The police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including those pertaining to assault and intimidation.

Political reactions and campus tensions

The incident has drawn sharp reactions from student bodies and political groups. While some have condemned the alleged use of violence to suppress dissent, others have urged restraint and called for a fair investigation. Campus politics in Bengaluru has often been polarised, with ABVP being a prominent student organisation affiliated with the Bharatiya Janata Party.

ABVP office-bearers have not yet issued an official response to the allegations. However, sources within the organisation suggest they may hold a press conference to present their version of events. The university administration has also been silent so far, though they are expected to cooperate with the police probe.

Legal and procedural aspects

The police have registered a First Information Report (FIR) based on the student's complaint. They are currently reviewing CCTV footage from the campus and have recorded statements from witnesses who were present during the altercation. A senior police official said that the investigation is in its early stages, and no arrests have been made as of yet.

Legal experts point out that the case hinges on establishing the sequence of events and the intent of the accused. The student's lawyer has demanded a thorough and unbiased inquiry, emphasising that the right to dissent is constitutionally protected. Authorities have assured that they will take appropriate action based on evidence collected.

Broader implications for free speech

This incident has reignited debates about the limits of political discourse on university campuses across India. Several student organisations have expressed solidarity with the complainant, organising small protests and vigils. They argue that such incidents create a chilling effect on freedom of expression, particularly for students who hold dissenting views.

Civil liberties groups have also weighed in, urging the police to act without fear or favour. They have called for a transparent process to ensure that justice is delivered, regardless of the political affiliations of those involved. The case is being closely monitored by both local media and national observers.

What happens next

The police are expected to complete their preliminary investigation in the coming days, which will determine whether charges will be pressed against the accused. Meanwhile, the university has announced an internal review of campus security protocols to prevent similar incidents in the future. The student community awaits further developments, hoping for a resolution that upholds democratic values and the rule of law.
