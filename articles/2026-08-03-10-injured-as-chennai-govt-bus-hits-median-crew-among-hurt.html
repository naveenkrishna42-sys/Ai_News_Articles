10 Injured as Chennai Govt Bus Hits Median, Crew Among Hurt

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ten people, including bus crew, were injured when a government bus hit a median in Chennai.
  • The incident occurred on Friday and the injured were taken to a nearby hospital.
  • Police are investigating the cause of the accident.

Ten people, including the crew, were injured when a government bus crashed into a median in Chennai on Friday. The accident took place in the afternoon, and the bus was travelling on a busy city route when the driver lost control.

The impact of the collision left the front of the bus damaged, with several passengers sustaining injuries. Emergency services were quick to reach the spot, and the injured were rushed to a nearby government hospital. Their condition is reported to be stable.

Accident Details Emerge

According to preliminary information, the bus was moving at a moderate speed when it suddenly veered off course and hit the median. The exact cause of the loss of control is not yet known. Police officials have said that they are examining the possibility of a mechanical failure or driver error.

Witnesses at the scene said the bus swerved sharply before hitting the divider, causing panic among the passengers. Some of the injured were seated near the front of the bus and bore the brunt of the impact.

Injured Rushed to Hospital

The injured include both passengers and crew members. They were taken to the Rajiv Gandhi Government General Hospital for treatment. Hospital sources said that most of the injuries are minor, with a few cases of fractures and bruises. No fatalities have been reported.

Officials from the transport department have reached the hospital to gather details and ensure that the injured receive proper care. The bus has been taken to the depot for inspection.

Investigation Underway

The city police have registered a case and are investigating the incident. They are checking the bus's maintenance records and the driver's statement to determine what went wrong. A senior police officer said that the driver, who was among the injured, will be questioned once he is fit to give a statement.

This is the latest in a series of minor accidents involving government buses in the city. Transport authorities have been urged to conduct regular safety checks on the fleet.

What to Watch

The police report and the bus inspection report are expected in the coming days. These will provide clarity on whether negligence or a technical snag led to the accident. Commuters will be watching for any action the transport department takes to prevent similar incidents in the future.
