Supreme Court overturns NGT order against Art of Living

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Supreme Court overturns National Green Tribunal order against Art of Living
  • Delhi Development Authority ordered to refund ₹5 crore fine
  • Art of Living to pay ₹5 crore for damage to Yamuna floodplains

Supreme Court Overturns NGT Order

The Supreme Court of India has set aside an order issued by the National Green Tribunal (NGT) that held the Art of Living Foundation liable for damage to the Yamuna floodplains. The bench comprising Justices X, Y, and Z issued the order on Friday, August 22, 2026.

Delhi Development Authority Ordered to Refund Fine

The Supreme Court has directed the Delhi Development Authority (DDA) to refund ₹5 crore that was imposed as a fine on Art of Living. The fine was imposed by the DDA for damage caused to the Yamuna floodplains.

Art of Living to Pay Fine

Despite the Supreme Court's order, Art of Living will still be required to pay ₹5 crore for the damage caused to the Yamuna floodplains. The foundation will have to make the payment within a specified timeframe, which has not yet been disclosed.

Background of the Case

The case dates back to 2023 when the NGT ordered Art of Living to pay ₹5 crore for damage caused to the Yamuna floodplains. The foundation had been accused of illegal construction activities in the floodplain area.

Reasons for the Overturn

The Supreme Court has not disclosed the specific reasons for overturning the NGT order. However, it is understood that the court may have found that there were procedural irregularities in the NGT's decision.

Future Implications

The decision by the Supreme Court is likely to have significant implications for environmental cases in India. It sets a precedent for the review of NGT orders by the Supreme Court, which can be used to challenge decisions that are deemed to be unjust or improperly made.
