Chinese Cyber Group Deploys AI for Large-Scale Server Attacks

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Cybercrime group UAT-10147 has integrated AI into its attack operations.
  • The campaign targets approximately 170,000 servers worldwide.
  • Attackers are employing advanced methods including Linux rootkits and BYOVD.

A New Era of Automated Cyber Warfare

A Chinese-speaking cybercrime syndicate, known in security circles as UAT-10147, has significantly increased its operational efficiency by integrating agentic AI into its attack framework. This development marks a shift in how threat actors manage post-compromise operations, allowing for rapid, automated exploitation of system vulnerabilities.

Security researchers at Cisco Talos and other industry bodies have tracked the group's activities, confirming that the campaign has impacted roughly 1.7 lakh servers. By leveraging AI, the group has streamlined the complex process of identifying and breaching vulnerable infrastructure.

Technical Sophistication and AI Integration

The group’s methodology goes beyond basic scripting. Reports indicate that UAT-10147 is using DeepSeek AI to automate the discovery and exploitation of system weaknesses. Once a server is breached, the AI assists in maintaining control and executing further commands without constant human intervention.

In addition to AI, the attackers are deploying a Linux rootkit to secure persistent access to compromised machines. They are also utilizing a "Bring Your Own Vulnerable Driver" (BYOVD) technique, which involves loading legitimate but vulnerable drivers to bypass security protections on host systems.

The Scale of the Threat

The sheer volume of targeted infrastructure highlights the risks posed by AI-driven automation. With 1.7 lakh servers currently under the group's radar, the potential for widespread data theft and system disruption is substantial. Security analysts are currently working to map the extent of the damage caused by these incursions.

This campaign appears to be part of a broader trend where traditional hacking groups adopt emerging technologies to scale their operations. While the specific objectives of UAT-10147 remain under investigation, the technical footprint of their tools suggests a highly organized and well-resourced operation.

Defensive Measures and Industry Response

  • Security teams are urged to audit server logs for unusual automated traffic patterns.
  • Patching vulnerabilities remains the most effective defense against the initial compromise.
  • Endpoint detection systems should be configured to flag unauthorized driver loads associated with BYOVD attacks.

Cybersecurity experts are continuing to monitor UAT-10147 for changes in their command-and-control infrastructure. Companies are advised to strengthen their perimeter security and implement strict access controls to mitigate the risk of automated exploitation.

Industry observers expect further disclosures regarding the specific AI models used by the group as forensic analysis of the compromised servers continues.
