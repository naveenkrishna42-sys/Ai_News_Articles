Three newborns die in NICU fire at Amravati government hospital

Amravati, Maharashtra - Three newborns died in a fire that broke out in the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) of a government hospital on Monday. The incident has sparked outrage and raised serious questions about safety protocols and staff training in the healthcare sector.

No fire audit lapse reported by officials

Officials from the health department have stated that there was no fire audit lapse in the hospital. They have assured that the necessary measures were in place to prevent such incidents. However, the parents of the deceased infants have described seeing their babies covered in ashes, which has further intensified the outrage.

Government pledges Rs 5 lakh aid for victims' kin

The state government has pledged Rs 5 lakh in aid for the families of the victims. This amount is expected to provide some relief to the grieving families. However, many are demanding a thorough investigation into the incident and stricter penalties for those responsible.

Parents describe seeing infants covered in ashes

Parents of the deceased infants have described the horrifying scene of their babies covered in ashes. They have expressed their shock and disbelief at the turn of events. The incident has highlighted the need for better safety measures in hospitals, especially in the NICU where newborns are the most vulnerable.

Minister defends staff efforts, Opposition calls for statewide fire audit

A minister from the state government has defended the efforts of the hospital staff, stating that they acted quickly to save the infants. However, the Opposition has called for a statewide fire audit, demanding stricter safety measures and penalties for those responsible. The incident has once again brought to light the need for better training and resources in the healthcare sector.
