10 Most Anticipated Bollywood Films: From Ramayana to King

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • IMDb has listed the ten most anticipated upcoming Bollywood films.
  • The list includes major productions like Ramayana, King, and Jee Le Zaraa.
  • Projects feature top stars such as Shah Rukh Khan, Ranbir Kapoor, and Alia Bhatt.

IMDb has released its list of the ten most awaited Bollywood films that are currently in various stages of production. The lineup spans mythological epics, action thrillers, and family dramas, featuring some of the industry's biggest stars.

From a big-budget adaptation of the Ramayana to Shah Rukh Khan's next project, here are the films generating the most buzz.

The Epic Scale of Ramayana

Topping the list is the ambitious multi-part adaptation of the Ramayana, directed by Nitesh Tiwari. The film is expected to be one of the most expensive Indian productions ever made.

Ranbir Kapoor will play Lord Ram, while Sai Pallavi has been cast as Sita. The project also features Sunny Deol as Hanuman and Yash as Raavan. Visual effects and scale are reportedly being handled by international teams.

Shah Rukh Khan Returns with King

Shah Rukh Khan will headline King, directed by Sujoy Ghosh. The film is described as an action thriller, with Khan playing a don-like figure mentoring a young woman.

Reports suggest the female lead may be played by Suhana Khan, though the team has not confirmed this. The film began principal photography earlier this year and is scheduled for a 2026 release.

Other Major Titles on the List

The list also includes Jee Le Zaraa, directed by Farhan Akhtar. The road-trip film stars Priyanka Chopra Jonas, Alia Bhatt, and Katrina Kaif. It marks the return of Akhtar to directing after a long gap.

Another notable entry is War 2, starring Hrithik Roshan and Jr NTR. The spy universe sequel is directed by Ayan Mukerji and is expected to be a major action spectacle. Fans are also waiting for Tiger vs Pathaan, which will bring together Salman Khan and Shah Rukh Khan. Yash Raj Films has not announced a release date yet.

Genre Diversity in the Pipeline

The list shows a mix of genres. There is Housefull 5, the next installment in the comedy franchise. The film will feature Akshay Kumar and a large ensemble cast.

On the darker side, there is Animal Park, the sequel to the 2023 blockbuster Animal. Ranbir Kapoor will reprise his role, and the film is expected to be even more violent. Director Sandeep Reddy Vanga is currently writing the script.

Also on the list is The Intern remake, starring Deepika Padukone and Amitabh Bachchan. The film adapts the 2015 Hollywood original for Indian audiences. Finally, Hera Pheri 3 is confirmed with the original trio of Akshay Kumar, Suniel Shetty, and Paresh Rawal.

What to Watch For

Most of these films are expected to release between late 2026 and 2027. Production schedules and release dates may shift as post-production work continues. Industry watchers will be keen to see which of these projects meets its deadline first.
