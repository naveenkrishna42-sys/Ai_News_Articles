Maritime Emergency Near Odisha Coast

A cargo vessel carrying 24 crew members sank in the Bay of Bengal on Saturday, triggering a massive search-and-rescue operation. The ship, which was flying a Panama flag, went down approximately 200 nautical miles off the coast of Paradip, Odisha.

Initial reports indicate the vessel was en route to China when it encountered distress. The exact cause of the sinking remains under investigation as maritime authorities work to secure the area.

Indian Coast Guard Mobilises Assets

The Indian Coast Guard (ICG) confirmed its involvement shortly after receiving distress signals. Several ships have been deployed to the site to locate the crew and monitor the wreckage.

Operations are currently being conducted under challenging conditions at sea. The ICG is coordinating with local port authorities and nearby merchant vessels to bolster the search effort.

Scope of the Rescue Operation

Authorities have not yet released details regarding the identity or nationality of the 24 crew members. The scale of the search covers a significant stretch of the coastline, given the distance of the sinking from the shore.

  • Vessel registry: Panama
  • Incident location: 200 nautical miles off Paradip
  • Crew capacity: 24 individuals

Emergency responders are prioritising the survival of the crew amidst the vast area of the Bay of Bengal. Officials are maintaining communication with relevant international maritime bodies to ensure all protocols are followed during the recovery process.

Next Steps in the Investigation

The maritime community is waiting for updates on the status of the missing personnel. Further information will be released as the ICG concludes its initial sweep of the crash site and provides clarity on the circumstances surrounding the vessel's sinking.
