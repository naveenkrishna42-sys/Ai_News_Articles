A Packed Week for South Indian Cinema

This week offers a diverse slate of South Indian films, hitting both theatres and OTT platforms. From a family drama set in a village to a psychological thriller, there is something for every kind of viewer.

The lineup includes Balan: The Boy, Srinivasa Mangapuram, Pluto, and Unmadham. Each film brings a distinct flavour, promising to keep audiences engaged across languages and genres.

Balan: The Boy – A Tale of Innocence and Struggle

Directed by K. S. Sivakumar, Balan: The Boy is a Tamil drama that revolves around a young boy navigating a harsh world. The film explores themes of poverty and resilience.

It releases in theatres on July 30. Early reviews have praised the child actor's performance and the film's raw, emotional storytelling.

Srinivasa Mangapuram – Family Drama with a Divine Twist

This Telugu film, directed by M. Mohan Krishna, centres on a family in the titular village. The story weaves local traditions with a conflict that threatens to tear the family apart.

Srinivasa Mangapuram is set for a theatrical release on July 31. The film is expected to appeal to audiences who enjoy rooted, emotion-driven narratives.

Pluto – A Dark Thriller on OTT

Directed by Aneesh Krishna, Pluto is a Malayalam psychological thriller that follows a man haunted by his past. The film has generated buzz for its atmospheric tension and gripping plot.

It streams on Disney+ Hotstar from July 30. The OTT release makes it accessible to a wider audience beyond Kerala.

Unmadham – Comedy and Chaos

This Telugu comedy, directed by V. K. Naresh, revolves around a group of friends caught in a series of misunderstandings. The film relies on slapstick and situational humour.

Unmadham releases in theatres on July 31. It is positioned as a light-hearted option for the weekend crowd.

With this mix of releases, viewers have plenty of choices across platforms. The coming days will reveal which film manages to capture the audience's attention and box office momentum.
