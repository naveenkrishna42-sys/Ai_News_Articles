Hyderabad: Two dead as car rams flyover pillar in Gachibowli

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Two persons killed in a car crash against a flyover pillar in Gachibowli.
  • The incident occurred late Saturday in Hyderabad's IT corridor.
  • Police have launched an investigation; identities not yet released.

Two people were killed early Saturday after their car slammed into a flyover pillar in Gachibowli, one of Hyderabad's busiest IT corridors. The accident took place under the flyover near the Gachibowli junction, a spot that sees heavy traffic even at night.

Police said the vehicle was travelling at high speed when it lost control and hit the concrete pillar. The impact was severe, leaving the car mangled and the occupants trapped. Rescue teams arrived after local residents alerted authorities.

The deceased were declared dead on the spot by medical personnel. Their identities have not been officially released, and police are in the process of notifying family members. Investigators are looking into whether overspeeding or driver fatigue played a role, though nothing has been confirmed yet.

Accident spot known for risks

Gachibowli has seen multiple accidents in recent years, particularly on stretches where flyover pillars are close to the road. The junction connects major tech parks and residential areas, and late-night speeding is a recurring concern for local commuters.

Traffic police have previously installed speed breakers and signboards in the area, but residents say enforcement remains patchy. "Every few months there is a crash here," said a shopkeeper near the spot, who did not want to be named. "The pillars are right there, and people drive as if the road is empty."

Investigation underway

Police have registered a case and are reviewing CCTV footage from nearby cameras to reconstruct the sequence of events. They are also checking whether the car's airbags deployed and if the driver had a valid licence.

Officials have not yet confirmed the speed at which the vehicle was moving, nor have they ruled out mechanical failure. A forensic team has been called in to examine the wreckage.

Road safety concerns persist

The incident has reignited calls for better road design in Gachibowli. Activists have long demanded crash barriers around flyover pillars and better lighting on service roads. The Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation has not announced any immediate measures following the crash.

Local councillors are expected to raise the issue in the next ward meeting. Meanwhile, police have urged drivers to exercise caution, especially during late hours when traffic is sparse and speeds tend to rise.

As the investigation continues, the focus will be on determining the exact cause of the crash and whether any preventive steps can be taken to avoid a repeat. The stretch remains open to traffic, and authorities have not indicated any changes to the road layout as of now.
