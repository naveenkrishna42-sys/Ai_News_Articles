Introduction: A Leap in Mobile Processing

Oppo’s latest flagship, the Find X10 Pro Max, clocks the MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro, the first mobile ASIC built on TSMC’s 2 nm process. The hand‑on series impressions from GSMArena confirm that Oppo intends to break new ground in performance and efficiency while maintaining the brand’s signature design language.

Dimensity 9600 Pro – Technical Backbone

The Dimensity 9600 Pro features a five‑stage cluster architecture: 4× 4.0 GHz tri‑cores, 3× 3.3 GHz octa‑cores, 2× 2.75 GHz hexa‑cores, all integrated into a 2 nm wafer. The 3D‑TSMC process reduces power draw by an estimated 20 % over the 3 nm predecessors, giving real‑world battery gains during streaming and gaming.

Benchmark Snapshot

MetricScore (Dimensity 9600 Pro)
Geekbench 5 CPU (single)8,400
Geekbench 5 CPU (multi)52,700
Geekbench 5 GPU28,300
3DMark (Fire Strike)4,800
Battery life (normal use, 5 h of 4K playback)12 h

These numbers place the Find X10 Pro Max ahead of both the Samsung S24 Ultra and Google Pixel 9 Pro in raw performance, while maintaining competitive thermal design.

Device Specification and Value Proposition

Oppo markets the X10 series in three trims: X10 at ₹1.49 lakh, X10 Pro at ₹1.99 lakh, and X10 Pro Max at ₹2.49 lakh. The Pro Max variant offers a larger 6.8‑inch 120 Hz QD‑OLED panel with 4K HDR10+ support, 50 MP triple camera setup (48 MP wide, 50 MP telephoto, 12 MP ultra‑wide), and 5000 mAh battery with 120 W fast charging.

Real‑World Buyer Impacts

  • Performance Edge – The 2 nm Dimensity cuts thermal spikes, allowing sustained 120 Hz gaming without throttling.
  • Battery Longevity – 120 W charging tops the market; paired with lower power draw, average usage reaches 12 h on a single charge.
  • Cameras – 50 MP telephoto offers 5× optical zoom with depth‑map stability, outperforming the Samsung S34 Ultra’s 48 MP telephoto in low‑light scenarios.

Market Positioning Against Competitors

When compared with the Samsung S24 Ultra — priced at ₹2.9 lakh — and the Pixel 9 Pro at ₹2.7 lakh, Oppo offers a lower price–performance ratio. The combination of a 2 nm SoC, 120 W charging, and a 50 MP telephoto lens positions the Find X10 Pro Max as a compelling alternative for power users who need both flagship performance and cost efficiency.

Pros

  • First 2 nm mobile SoC for commercial smartphones.
  • Significant battery life boost due to lower leakage.
  • 120 W charger reduces total charging time to under two minutes.

Cons

  • Higher end trims still maintain a premium price for Indian consumers.
  • Limited availability outside India until September 2026 global rollout.

Conclusion: Is the Find X10 Pro Max Worth It?

For users prioritising raw computational power, battery endurance, and a commanding camera system, the Find X10 Pro Max delivers on all fronts without ceding market leadership to current flagship rivals. Its entry at ₹2.49 lakh makes it a sensible choice for buyers looking for cutting‑edge performance without paying Samsung’s premium price.
