Alzarri Joseph Declined Selection for Pakistan Tests, Says Daren Sammy

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Alzarri Joseph declined selection for West Indies' Test series against Pakistan, says coach Daren Sammy.
  • The decision was reportedly due to personal reasons, though no official statement from Joseph has been made.
  • Uncapped spinner Joshua Bishop has been added to the 15-man squad for the two-match series.
  • Sammy stressed the importance of building on recent winning momentum in Test cricket.

Sammy Confirms Joseph's Decision

West Indies head coach Daren Sammy has confirmed that fast bowler Alzarri Joseph declined selection for the upcoming two-Test series against Pakistan. Sammy revealed the development during a press conference, stating the 27-year-old pacer communicated his unavailability due to personal reasons.

The decision leaves a significant gap in the West Indies bowling attack. Joseph has been a key figure in the Test side, known for his pace and ability to extract bounce.

Uncapped Bishop Gets the Nod

Cricket West Indies has named uncapped spinner Joshua Bishop as Joseph's replacement in the 15-member squad. Bishop, a left-arm orthodox bowler, has been a consistent performer in domestic cricket and recently impressed in the West Indies 'A' tour matches.

The selection comes as West Indies aim to maintain momentum after a promising Test series earlier this year. Sammy emphasized the need to build on that winning feeling.

Sammy Wants to Build on Winning Momentum

Speaking to the media, Sammy stressed that the team's focus remains on sustained progress. "We have spoken about building on our winning momentum in Test cricket," the coach said. "Every player in this squad has earned their place, and we back them to perform against a strong Pakistan side."

Pakistan, known for their spin-friendly conditions, could pose a challenge for the West Indies batters. The inclusion of Bishop adds variety to the bowling attack, which already features experienced pacers and spinners.

Joseph's Absence Raises Questions

Alzarri Joseph's declining selection has sparked discussion among fans and analysts. The pacer has not publicly detailed his reasons, leading to speculation about his future in Test cricket. However, Sammy did not indicate any rift or long-term concerns regarding the player.

Joseph last played a Test match against Bangladesh in June 2025, where he picked up four wickets. His absence for the Pakistan series means West Indies will rely on other fast bowlers like Jayden Seales and Kemar Roach to lead the attack.

What's Next?

The first Test between West Indies and Pakistan is scheduled to begin on August 7 in Kingston. The team will hope to carry forward their recent form while managing the absence of a key fast bowler. All eyes will be on Joshua Bishop to see if he can make an impact on debut.
