Anahat Singh becomes first Indian to win World Junior Squash Championship

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Anahat Singh won the World Junior Squash Championship, a first for India.
  • The Prime Minister congratulated her on the historic achievement.
  • Singh's victory breaks a long-standing barrier for Indian squash.

Anahat Singh has made history. The young squash player from India has become the first Indian to win the World Junior Squash Championship. The achievement, reported by The Hindu and other major outlets, marks a milestone for Indian squash on the global stage.

The Prime Minister was quick to congratulate Singh on her historic feat. In a statement, he praised her dedication and the hard work that led to this monumental win. The victory has sparked celebrations across the country, with many hailing her as a future star of the sport.

Breaking the 'Curse'

For years, Indian junior squash players have come close but failed to win the world title. Singh's win breaks what some in the squash community had called a 'curse'. She managed to overcome the pressure and deliver when it mattered most.

Her journey to the title was not easy. She faced tough opponents from traditional squash powerhouses like Egypt and Malaysia. But her skill and composure under pressure set her apart. She played a fearless brand of squash that left her rivals struggling to keep up.

A Historic Moment for Indian Squash

Singh's victory is the biggest achievement for Indian squash since the rise of players like Joshna Chinappa and Saurav Ghosal. While they have won medals at the Asian Games and Commonwealth Games, a world junior title was missing from the cabinet. Singh has now filled that gap.

The win is expected to inspire a new generation of squash players in India. The sport has been growing steadily in the country, with more academies and coaching facilities coming up. Singh's success could accelerate that growth.

'It Means the World'

In her first reaction after the win, Singh said, "It means the world to me." She dedicated the victory to her family and coaches who have supported her throughout her career. Her humility and grace in the moment of triumph won her many admirers.

Singh's technique and fitness were praised by experts. She showed remarkable stamina and shot selection throughout the tournament. Her ability to read the game and adapt to different opponents was a key factor in her success.

What Next for the Champion?

The win has put Singh in the spotlight. She will now be expected to carry this form into senior competitions. The transition from junior to senior squash is a big step, but her potential is clear.

With the Asian Games and World Championships on the horizon, Singh's journey is far from over. She has set a benchmark for herself, and the squash world will be watching her closely.
