Arijit Singh's 'Yeh Awarapan' from Awarapan 2 released

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Arijit Singh has sung the new version of 'Yeh Awarapan' for Awarapan 2.
  • The song is a recreated version of the original 2007 track from the first film.
  • The music video features lead actors from the sequel.

The first song from the much-anticipated sequel Awarapan 2 has finally dropped. Titled 'Yeh Awarapan', the track is sung by Arijit Singh and was released by the film's music label on Tuesday.

The song is a reimagined version of the original 2007 hit from the first Awarapan film. That earlier track, sung by Shivaji Bose, had become a cult favourite among fans of melancholic Hindi film music.

New voice, same soul

Arijit Singh's rendition retains the emotional core of the original while bringing his signature depth to the composition. The music is composed by Mithoon, who also helmed the first film's soundtrack.

The lyrics, penned by Sayeed Quadri, echo the themes of isolation and longing that defined the original. The new arrangement weaves in modern instrumentation without losing the raw, acoustic feel of the first version.

In the music video, the lead pair—played by Aditya Roy Kapur and Mrunal Thakur—are seen in a series of moody, rain-soaked sequences. The visuals complement the song's brooding tone.

A sequel 19 years in the making

Awarapan 2 is directed by Mohit Suri, who also directed the original. The film has been in development for several years and is one of the most awaited Bollywood sequels.

The original 2007 film starred Emraan Hashmi and Shriya Saran. The sequel features a fresh cast but retains the same narrative universe. The story is expected to follow a similar arc of love, loss, and redemption.

Produced by Vishesh Films, the sequel has generated considerable buzz since its announcement. The release of the first song is expected to build further momentum ahead of the film's theatrical release.

What to watch next

The makers are expected to release the film's trailer in the coming weeks. Awarapan 2 is slated for a late 2026 release. Whether the sequel can match the emotional impact of the original remains to be seen.
