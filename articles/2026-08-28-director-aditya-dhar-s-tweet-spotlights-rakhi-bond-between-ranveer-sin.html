Director Aditya Dhar's Tweet Spotlights Rakhi Bond Between Ranveer Singh's On-Screen Siblings

Aditya Dhar's Heartfelt Rakhi Tweet

Filmmaker Aditya Dhar, known for his 2019 blockbuster 'Uri: The Surgical Strike', took to Twitter on Raksha Bandhan to share an emotional still from his upcoming film. The picture, featuring lead actor Ranveer Singh with his on-screen sister, has sparked a wave of excitement and anticipation among fans.

Ranveer Singh's On-Screen Bond

In the still, Singh is seen applying 'tilak' on his sister's forehead, a traditional Hindu ritual often performed by brothers on their sisters during Rakhi. The image encapsulates the spirit of Raksha Bandhan, a festival celebrating the love and protection between brothers and sisters.

Dhar's Directorial Choices

Dhar's decision to share this particular still on Rakhi is noteworthy. It not only underscores the importance of the occasion but also hints at the emphasis his film places on sibling relationships. The tweet reads, "#RakshaBandhan special! When brothers become protectors... Coming Soon.", suggesting a narrative centered around familial bonds.

The yet-untitled film, produced by Ronnie Screwvala, also stars Shraddha Kapoor and is expected to release later this year.

Reaction and Analysis

Dhar's tweet has been widely praised, with many appreciating the emotional depth of the still. Film analyst, Anupama Chopra, weighed in, saying, "Aditya Dhar has a knack for capturing the essence of relationships. This still promises an engaging narrative."
