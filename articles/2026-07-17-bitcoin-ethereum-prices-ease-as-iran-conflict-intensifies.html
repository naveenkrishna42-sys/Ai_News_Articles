Bitcoin, Ethereum prices ease as Iran conflict intensifies

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin and Ethereum prices declined on Friday amid rising geopolitical tensions in Iran.
  • The conflict escalation prompted a broader market shift toward traditional safe-haven assets like gold.
  • Analysts note that crypto's correlation with risk assets remains strong during crises.
  • Trading volumes surged as uncertainty gripped global markets.

Prices dip as Iran tensions spike

Bitcoin and Ethereum prices softened on Friday, July 17, as escalating conflict in Iran rattled global markets. The downturn marks a reversal from recent gains, with investors moving toward traditional safe-haven assets like gold and the US dollar.

Bitcoin fell below $60,000 during Asian trading hours, while Ethereum dropped around 4% to trade near $3,200. The declines come as reports of heightened military activity in the Strait of Hormuz fueled fears of supply disruptions and broader regional instability.

Risk-off sentiment grips crypto

The geopolitical shock triggered a broad sell-off across risk assets. Equities in Asia and Europe also slid, while crude oil prices jumped on supply concerns. Crypto markets, often touted as hedges against turmoil, showed a strong correlation with traditional risk instruments.

“When geopolitical risk spikes, crypto behaves more like a risk-on asset than a safe haven,” said a market analyst. “We are seeing that play out today. The move into gold and the dollar is clear.”

Data from CoinMarketCap showed total crypto market capitalisation shedding about $80 billion in the past 24 hours. Trading volumes surged, with exchanges reporting higher activity from both retail and institutional players.

Gold outperforms, dollar strengthens

Gold prices climbed above $2,450 per ounce, while the US dollar index rose 0.5% against a basket of currencies. The shift underscores investor preference for assets with long-established safe-haven status during periods of extreme uncertainty.

Bitcoin’s price action has often diverged from gold in recent months, but Friday’s move aligned with the broader flight to quality. Ethereum, which had been riding momentum from recent network upgrades, could not escape the downdraft.

What to watch next

Market participants are now focused on diplomatic channels and any statements from Iran or regional powers. A de-escalation could trigger a swift rebound in crypto prices, while further conflict may deepen the sell-off.

Investors are also watching for any regulatory responses or emergency measures from central banks. The coming days will test whether crypto can reclaim its narrative as a non-correlated asset class or remain tethered to traditional risk markets.
