The Odyssey India Box Office: Nolan's Epic Crosses Rs 150 Crore Mark

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The Odyssey grosses over Rs 150 crore in India by day 19
  • It is Nolan's first film to achieve this milestone in the country
  • Film ranks 8th highest-grossing Hollywood release in India
  • Global collections cross $900 million in third weekend

Christopher Nolan's mythological epic The Odyssey has sailed past another milestone in India. On its 19th day in theatres, the film grossed over Rs 150 crore nett, becoming the director's first movie to achieve this feat in the domestic market. The steady run shows no signs of slowing down, with collections on the third Tuesday remaining robust.

A Record-Breaking Feat for Nolan in India

This is uncharted territory for Nolan in India. None of his earlier releases — from Inception to Oppenheimer — had crossed the Rs 150 crore mark domestically. The Odyssey has now done so with ease, riding on strong word-of-mouth and repeat viewings.

The film's performance places it as the 8th highest-grossing Hollywood film ever in India. It now sits comfortably among the top ten, a list dominated by Marvel and Avatar titles.

Global Box Office Momentum

Overseas, the film continues its march. It crossed $900 million globally during its third weekend, according to trade reports. The mythological retelling of Homer's epic has resonated with audiences worldwide, and India has emerged as a key contributor.

The domestic run has been remarkably consistent. Even on weekdays, the film has held its ground, with the third Tuesday's numbers staying close to the previous day's collections. Trade analysts attribute this to the film's visual spectacle and Nolan's loyal fan base in the country.

What's Driving the Numbers?

The film's release in multiple formats — including IMAX and 70mm — has been a major draw. Premium screens have reported high occupancy, especially in metropolitan cities. The epic scale of the production, combined with a star-studded cast, has generated sustained interest.

Moreover, the lack of major Bollywood releases in the same window has allowed The Odyssey to dominate the box office without significant competition. The film's extended run is expected to continue for several more weeks.

Comparing with Previous Nolan Hits

For context, Oppenheimer had collected around Rs 130 crore in its entire run in India. The Odyssey has surpassed that in less than three weeks. The film's collection trajectory suggests it could end its run in the Rs 200 crore range, a figure that would place it among the top five Hollywood films in India.

Interestingly, Oppenheimer is set to re-release in theatres later this month, which could create an interesting box office dynamic. However, with The Odyssey still going strong, the re-release will likely cater to a niche audience of Nolan completists.

Looking Ahead

As the film enters its fourth week, the focus will be on how it holds up against new releases. The upcoming weekend will be crucial in determining whether it can sustain its momentum. If current trends continue, The Odyssey is poised to become Nolan's highest-grossing film in India by a significant margin.
