Trump bans foreign gear in US energy grid

📅 2026-08-27 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Trump signs order banning foreign equipment from US energy grid
  • Order targets Chinese and Russian suppliers
  • Market reactions vary among affected companies

Trump's Energy Grid Order Sparks Global Market Reaction

President Donald Trump has signed an executive order banning certain foreign equipment from the US energy grid, targeting Chinese and Russian suppliers. This move has sent shockwaves through global markets, with shares of affected companies seeing significant fluctuations.

Why This Matters

The order is part of Trump's broader strategy to reduce reliance on foreign technology in critical infrastructure. However, industry experts warn that the ban may have unintended consequences, including job losses and increased costs for US energy providers.

Targeted Suppliers and Market Reactions

The order specifically mentions Chinese and Russian suppliers, including CG Power, GE Vernova, and Hitachi Energy. Shares of these companies have surged up to 4% in global markets, reflecting investor concerns and the potential impact on their business operations.

Expert Opinions and Criticisms

Industry experts have criticized the order, arguing that it contradicts real industrial needs and could lead to higher costs and job losses. They point out that many US energy providers already use a mix of domestic and foreign equipment, and a sudden ban could disrupt supply chains and increase costs.

  • Pros:
    • Reduces reliance on foreign technology in critical infrastructure
    • Potential for increased domestic production and job creation
  • Cons:
    • Could lead to higher costs for US energy providers
    • May disrupt supply chains and increase costs
    • Could result in job losses in affected industries

Broader Impact on US Energy Sector

The order is expected to have a significant impact on the US energy sector, potentially leading to higher costs for consumers and increased reliance on domestic production. However, it also presents an opportunity for US companies to expand their market share and create new jobs in the energy sector.
