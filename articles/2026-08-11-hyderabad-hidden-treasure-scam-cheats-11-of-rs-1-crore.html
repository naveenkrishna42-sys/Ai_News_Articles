Hyderabad 'Hidden Treasure' Scam Cheats 11 of Rs 1 Crore

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Eleven people were cheated of Rs 1 crore in Hyderabad.
  • The accused promised the victims a hidden treasure in exchange for money.
  • A case has been registered and an investigation is underway.

In a classic case of greed meeting gullibility, 11 people in Hyderabad have been cheated of approximately Rs 1 crore by a gang that promised them a share of a 'hidden treasure'. The incident, reported on Tuesday, has prompted a police investigation into the elaborate con that played on the victims' hopes of overnight riches.

The Promise of Easy Wealth

The accused allegedly spun a tale of a hidden treasure, likely buried or stashed away, and convinced the victims that they needed funds to retrieve it. In return, the victims were promised a substantial cut of the supposed fortune.

Over time, the victims paid up increasingly large sums, believing the treasure would soon be unearthed. Such 'treasure hunt' scams are not new to the region, but this one appears to have been executed with enough detail and persuasion to rope in over a dozen people.

How the Fraud Unfolded

While the exact details of the conversations remain under wraps, police sources indicate that the gang used a mix of secrecy and urgency to keep the victims in the dark. Each victim was likely approached separately, with the promise of a share, and asked to contribute money for 'processing fees', 'equipment', or 'bribes' to officials.

The amounts paid by each individual are yet to be disclosed, but in total, the gang is believed to have collected around Rs 1 crore. The scam came to light when one or more victims realised they had been duped and approached the police, leading to the registration of a case.

Police Investigation Underway

Hyderabad police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code for cheating and criminal conspiracy. Officers are now trying to trace the accused, who are believed to be part of a well-organised gang that may have operated in other cities as well.

Officials have not yet confirmed the identities of the accused or the victims, but they have urged citizens to be wary of anyone promising quick riches from hidden treasures or similar schemes.

A Familiar Trap, New Victims

This incident serves as a reminder that age-old con tricks continue to find fresh targets. The lure of easy money often clouds judgment, and the accused here appear to have exploited exactly that weakness.

In recent years, Hyderabad has seen a few such cases, but the scale of this one—both in terms of the number of victims and the amount involved—makes it stand out. Cybercrime and economic offences wings are often called in for such investigations, though in this case, the local police are handling the matter.

What to Watch

The police are likely to release more details as the investigation progresses, including the modus operandi and whether the gang has struck elsewhere. For now, they have advised the public to report any suspicious offers to the police before parting with their money.

As the probe continues, the focus will be on recovering the cheated amount and ensuring that the accused are brought to book. The case also underscores the need for greater public awareness about such confidence tricks that promise hidden treasures but deliver only losses.
