Jasmine Sandlas mobbed at Dehradun concert, alleged groping caught on video

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jasmine Sandlas was performing in Dehradun when a video showed her being mobbed and allegedly groped.
  • The video has gone viral on social media, with fans and celebrities condemning the incident.
  • Authorities have not yet confirmed any arrests or an official complaint from the singer.

Viral video shows distressing scene

A concert by singer Jasmine Sandlas, known for the hit track Dhurandhar, took an ugly turn in Dehradun on Saturday night. A video now circulating widely on social media shows the singer being mobbed by a section of the crowd on stage. In the clip, several men can be seen crowding around her, with some allegedly touching her inappropriately.

The footage has triggered a wave of anger online. Fans and fellow artists have called the incident a clear case of harassment and demanded strict action against those involved.

Outpouring of support online

Social media platforms were flooded with messages of support for Sandlas. Many users used hashtags like #JusticeForJasmine and #RespectWomen to voice their outrage. The video has been shared thousands of times, with many pointing out the lack of security at the event.

Several celebrities also weighed in. While no official statement has been issued by Sandlas herself yet, sources close to the singer suggest she was shaken by the experience. The organisers of the Dehradun concert have not commented on the incident so far.

What the video shows

The clip, filmed by an audience member, shows Sandlas performing when a group of men rush the stage. She is seen trying to move away, but the crowd continues to press in. The singer appears distressed and is eventually escorted off stage by a few staff members.

It remains unclear whether security personnel were present at the venue. The Dehradun police have said they are looking into the matter, but no formal complaint has been registered yet.

Pattern of concern at live events

This incident adds to a growing list of cases where female performers have faced harassment during live shows in India. In recent years, several singers and dancers have spoken out about being groped or mobbed on stage, often blaming poor security arrangements.

Experts say that while event organisers are legally responsible for ensuring the safety of performers, enforcement remains weak. Many have called for stricter protocols, including designated barriers and trained security personnel at all live events.

What happens next

Jasmine Sandlas has not yet released a public statement, but her team is reportedly reviewing legal options. The Dehradun police have said they will act if a complaint is filed. For now, the video continues to fuel a national conversation about the safety of women artists on stage.
