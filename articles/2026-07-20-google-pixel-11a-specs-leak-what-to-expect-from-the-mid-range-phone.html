Google Pixel 11a Specs Leak: What to Expect From the Mid-Range Phone

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pixel 11a leaked specs suggest a Tensor G6 chip, 8GB RAM, and 6.2-inch display.
  • Reported pricing in the US is around $499, with 128GB base storage.
  • Google has confirmed a Pixel 11 series launch event for August 12 in India.

Pixel 11a Leak Reveals Key Specs

New leaks have surfaced detailing the specifications of the upcoming Google Pixel 11a. The mid-range phone is expected to be the most affordable entry in the Pixel 11 series.

According to reports from Android Authority and Droid Life, the Pixel 11a will run on Google's Tensor G6 processor. It is tipped to come with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage as the base configuration.

The device is said to feature a 6.2-inch OLED display with a 90Hz refresh rate. This would mark a significant upgrade over the previous A-series models, which had 60Hz panels.

Camera and Battery Details

The leak suggests a dual rear camera setup on the Pixel 11a. The primary sensor is expected to be a 64-megapixel shooter, paired with a 12-megapixel ultra-wide lens.

Battery capacity is reported at 4,500mAh, with support for 30W wired charging. Wireless charging is also expected to be included, a feature that was absent on the Pixel 10a.

Google has yet to officially confirm any of these specifications. However, the consistency across multiple leaks suggests the company is finalising the hardware for production.

Pricing and Launch Timeline

Deleted Amazon listings, spotted by Android Authority, reportedly showed the Pixel 11a priced at $499 in the US. This would place it in direct competition with devices like the Samsung Galaxy A56 and OnePlus Nord 5.

In India, the phone could be priced around ₹38,000 based on currency conversion and local taxes. Google has already sent invites for a Pixel 11 series launch event in India on August 12.

The event is likely to unveil the entire Pixel 11 lineup, including the standard Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, and the Pixel 11a. The company has teased the event with the tagline "Pixel Glow."

What This Means for the A-Series

The Pixel 11a appears to be a significant step up for Google's budget line. The inclusion of a higher refresh rate display and wireless charging could help it stand out in a crowded mid-range market.

However, the rumoured $499 price tag is higher than the Pixel 10a's launch price of $449. This could limit its appeal among price-sensitive buyers who expect the A-series to be a true budget option.

Competitors like Xiaomi and Realme offer similar specs at lower prices in India. Google will need to rely on its camera software and clean Android experience to justify the premium.

Officials have not yet confirmed the leaked details. The August 12 event should provide clarity on the final specifications and pricing for the Indian market.
