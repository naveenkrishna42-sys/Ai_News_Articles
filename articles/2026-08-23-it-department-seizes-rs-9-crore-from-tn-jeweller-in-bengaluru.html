IT Department Seizes Rs 9 Crore from TN Jeweller in Bengaluru

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Income Tax officials seized Rs 9 crore in cash from a luxury SUV.
  • The operation took place on the busy Hosur Road corridor in Bengaluru.
  • The seized funds are reportedly linked to a jewellery business based in Tamil Nadu.

Major Cash Seizure on Bengaluru Outskirts

Income Tax department officials conducted a significant operation on Hosur Road, intercepting a luxury SUV transporting a massive sum of unaccounted cash. The raid resulted in the seizure of Rs 9 crore, which authorities believe is connected to a prominent jewellery business operating out of Tamil Nadu.

The vehicle was flagged down during routine surveillance aimed at tracking illegal financial transactions. Officials moved quickly to secure the cash, which was found hidden inside the vehicle, and subsequently took custody of the money for further verification.

Operation Details and Financial Scrutiny

Preliminary reports indicate that the cash was being transported across state lines when the IT team intervened. While the identity of the jeweller remains under official wraps, investigators are currently cross-referencing the seized currency with the business's recent tax filings and transaction logs.

The seizure highlights the ongoing efforts of the tax department to clamp down on the movement of illicit funds between industrial hubs. Hosur Road, a critical transit artery connecting Bengaluru to Tamil Nadu, has frequently been a point of interest for enforcement agencies monitoring inter-state cargo and private vehicle movement.

Next Steps for Enforcement Agencies

  • Detailed audit of the business's financial records to trace the source of the funds.
  • Verification of whether the cash was intended for legitimate business expansion or tax evasion.
  • Potential follow-up raids on premises linked to the jeweller in Tamil Nadu.

The Income Tax department has not yet disclosed the full list of individuals detained or questioned in connection with the vehicle. The seized cash is being held in a secure facility while the investigation into the origins of the money continues.

Authorities are expected to provide a formal update on the legal proceedings once the initial reconciliation of the seized funds is complete.
