★ 4.2 / 5.0 Value Rating

The BOB Card Etihad offers strong value for frequent flyers with accelerated reward points on Etihad Airways and a generous welcome bonus, though the annual fee from the second year and limited category bonuses for non-travel spend slightly reduce its overall appeal.

Deal & Pricing Analysis

The BOB Card Etihad credit card is currently available with a first-year fee waiver, making it an attractive proposition for new applicants. The card typically carries an annual fee of ₹999, which is waived for the first year as part of this limited-period offer. Additionally, the card offers a welcome bonus of 15,000 Etihad Guest miles upon spending ₹50,000 within the first 90 days of card issuance. This bonus alone can be worth approximately ₹15,000 when redeemed for Etihad Airways flights, effectively offsetting the annual fee for several years. The ongoing rewards structure includes 6 Etihad Guest miles per ₹200 spent on Etihad Airways, 3 miles per ₹200 on international spends, and 1 mile per ₹200 on other categories. There is also a 1% fuel surcharge waiver at petrol pumps across India. Given the current promotional offer, the card provides excellent value for frequent travelers, especially those who fly with Etihad Airways or its partners.

Real-World Performance & Daily Use

In daily use, the BOB Card Etihad shines for those who frequently travel internationally or patronize Etihad Airways. The accelerated reward rate on Etihad spends is among the highest in the Indian market, making it a no-brainer for loyal Etihad flyers. The card also offers complimentary access to airport lounges, though the number of free visits is limited to two per year for domestic lounges and two for international lounges, which is modest compared to premium travel cards. The card's acceptance is widespread, and the bank's app provides a seamless experience for tracking rewards and managing transactions. However, the foreign transaction fee of 3.5% is on the higher side, which can eat into the value of international rewards if not offset by the accelerated earning. Customer service is responsive, and the instant approval process online is smooth, with most applicants receiving a decision within minutes. The card's design is sleek, and the Etihad Guest miles can be redeemed for flights, upgrades, and other rewards on Etihad and partner airlines.

Competition & Market Alternatives

When compared to other travel credit cards in the Indian market, the BOB Card Etihad holds its own. The HDFC Bank Diners Club Black offers a higher reward rate on travel and dining but comes with a significantly higher annual fee of ₹10,000. The Axis Bank Vistara Signature card provides similar benefits for Vistara flyers but lacks the Etihad partnership. For those who prefer cashback over travel rewards, the SBI Card SimplyCLICK offers 10x rewards on online spends but no airline-specific benefits. The BOB Card Etihad's main advantage is its low annual fee and the generous welcome bonus, making it accessible to a wider audience. However, if you are not a frequent Etihad flyer, the rewards may not be as lucrative as other cards that offer more flexible redemption options.

Who Should Buy This Deal

This card is ideal for frequent flyers who travel with Etihad Airways at least a few times a year, as the accelerated rewards and welcome bonus can translate into significant savings on flights. It is also suitable for those who spend heavily on international transactions and can benefit from the 3 miles per ₹200 rate. If you are a casual traveler or prefer cashback over miles, you might want to consider other options. Additionally, if you cannot meet the minimum spend requirement for the welcome bonus, the card's value diminishes. Overall, the BOB Card Etihad is a solid choice for Etihad loyalists and those who can maximize its travel rewards.

Final Buying Verdict

The BOB Card Etihad is a compelling choice for frequent Etihad Airways flyers, offering accelerated rewards and a substantial welcome bonus that can offset the annual fee. With the first-year fee waiver, it's a low-risk entry into travel rewards. However, if you don't fly Etihad regularly or prefer cashback, other cards may offer better overall value.
