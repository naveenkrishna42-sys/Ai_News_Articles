Sensex Falls 430 Points, Nifty Below 24,600 as Financial Stocks Drag

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex fell over 430 points, Nifty slipped below 24,600
  • Financial stocks led the decline
  • Markets extend losses for second consecutive session

Indian equity markets extended their losing streak on Friday, with the Sensex plunging over 430 points and the Nifty slipping below the 24,600 mark. The sell-off was led by financial stocks, which dragged the benchmarks lower amid persistent concerns over valuations and global cues.

Financials Lead the Decline

Banking and financial services shares were the primary culprits behind the market's slide. Heavyweights in the sector witnessed significant selling pressure, pulling the indices down. The Nifty Bank index, a key barometer for the sector, was among the worst performers, reflecting investor caution.

Analysts pointed to profit-booking after recent gains, coupled with lingering worries about elevated interest rates and their impact on lending growth. The sector, which has a heavy weightage in the benchmarks, has been under scrutiny as investors reassess earnings expectations.

Broader Market Under Pressure

The broader market also felt the heat, with midcap and smallcap indices trading in the red. The decline was widespread, with most sectoral indices on the National Stock Exchange recording losses. Only a few defensive pockets, such as IT and pharma, managed to stay afloat, offering limited support.

Market breadth was negative, with more stocks declining than advancing on both the BSE and NSE. This indicated that the selling pressure was not confined to large caps but permeated across the market spectrum.

Investor Sentiment and Global Cues

Investor sentiment remained fragile, mirroring weak global signals. Asian markets traded mixed, while US futures pointed to a subdued opening, adding to the cautious mood. Domestic institutional investors were net sellers, while foreign institutional investors' activity remained a key monitorable.

Experts noted that the market is in a consolidation phase after a strong run-up. "The correction is healthy, but the lack of fresh triggers is keeping investors on edge," said a market strategist. The upcoming earnings season and global central bank policies are expected to dictate the near-term trajectory.

What's Next for the Markets

Traders are now eyeing the next set of economic data and corporate results for direction. The Reserve Bank of India's monetary policy stance and US Federal Reserve signals will be crucial in shaping sentiment. A sustained recovery in financial stocks is essential for the benchmarks to reclaim higher levels.

Technically, the Nifty has immediate support at the 24,500 zone, and a breach could trigger further downside. On the upside, the 24,800-25,000 range remains a strong resistance. Market participants are advised to stay cautious and watch for global cues, as volatility is likely to persist in the near term.
