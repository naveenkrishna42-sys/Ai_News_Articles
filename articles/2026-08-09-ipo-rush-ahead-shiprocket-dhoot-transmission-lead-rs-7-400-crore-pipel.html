IPO Rush Ahead: Shiprocket, Dhoot Transmission Lead Rs 7,400 Crore Pipeline

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nine companies to launch IPOs next week, targeting over Rs 7,400 crore collectively
  • Shiprocket, Dhoot Transmission, and Milky Mist are among the prominent issuers
  • The rush signals sustained investor appetite despite broader market volatility

The primary market is bracing for a packed week as nine companies, including logistics major Shiprocket and dairy player Milky Mist, are set to launch their initial public offerings (IPOs). Together, these issues are expected to raise over Rs 7,400 crore, according to reports collated from multiple financial news outlets.

The list also features Dhoot Transmission, a Pune-based manufacturer of wires and cables, which has drawn significant attention from retail and institutional investors alike. The sheer number of launches in a single week underscores the continued momentum in India's capital markets, even as global headwinds persist.

Who's In The Pipeline?

Among the most anticipated is the Shiprocket IPO, which aims to capitalise on the booming e-commerce logistics space. The company, backed by marquee investors, is looking to tap public markets to fuel expansion and reduce debt.

Milky Mist, known for its dairy products, is another notable entrant. The company's IPO is expected to attract investors keen on the consumer staples segment, which has shown resilience in recent quarters.

Dhoot Transmission, which supplies components to the automotive and electrical sectors, rounds out the top-tier issuers. Its offering is seen as a bet on infrastructure and manufacturing growth.

What's Driving The Surge?

Market observers point to a combination of factors behind the clustering of IPOs. Robust domestic liquidity, improved corporate earnings, and a regulatory framework that has streamlined public issues are all playing a role.

Additionally, the success of recent listings has emboldened companies to accelerate their plans. Many of these firms are in sectors—logistics, FMCG, and auto components—that have benefited from post-pandemic demand shifts.

However, not all issues are expected to sail through smoothly. Some analysts caution that valuations in certain segments have become stretched, and investors may turn selective.

Retail Participation Key

Retail investors are likely to play a crucial role in the success of these offerings. With several IPOs priced in the affordable bracket, the response from small investors could determine whether the issues are oversubscribed or face tepid demand.

Financial advisors recommend that investors study each company's financials, business model, and use of proceeds before applying. The grey market premium, often a gauge of sentiment, has been mixed so far for the upcoming issues.

Regulators have also been watchful. The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has recently tightened disclosure norms, which could impact how companies price their offers.

What To Watch

The coming week will test the market's appetite for new paper, especially in a climate of fluctuating global cues. If the issues sail through, it could encourage more companies to file draft papers in the months ahead.

For now, investors and analysts alike will be tracking subscription numbers, listing day performance, and any last-minute regulatory changes. The outcome could set the tone for the next wave of public offerings in the second half of the fiscal year.
