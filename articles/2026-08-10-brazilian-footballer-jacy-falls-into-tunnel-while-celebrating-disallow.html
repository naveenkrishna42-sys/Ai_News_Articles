Brazilian footballer Jacy falls into tunnel while celebrating disallowed goal

A Brazilian footballer's moment of joy turned into a painful ordeal when he fell into an underground tunnel while celebrating a goal. The incident, which was captured on camera, has since gone viral on social media.

Jacy had every reason to celebrate. He had just found the back of the net in what appeared to be a crucial moment in the match. The adrenaline got the better of him as he sprinted towards the sideline, leaping over the hoardings with arms outstretched.

What he did not anticipate was the gap beyond the boards. The underground tunnel, typically used by players and staff to reach the dressing rooms, was wide open. He vanished from view almost instantly, leaving teammates and fans stunned.

Viral video and reaction
