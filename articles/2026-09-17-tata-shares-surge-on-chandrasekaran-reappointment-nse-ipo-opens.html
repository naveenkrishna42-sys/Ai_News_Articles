Tata Shares Surge on Chandrasekaran Reappointment, NSE IPO Opens

Key points
  • Tata group shares rose up to 3% after reports of N Chandrasekaran's reappointment as chairman for a second term.
  • NSE IPO opened for subscription, aiming to raise over ₹10,000 crore at a valuation of about ₹1.5 lakh crore.
  • Analysts expect the positive momentum to continue in Tata stocks, while the NSE IPO may see strong listing gains.

Tata Group Stocks Rally on Chandrasekaran Reappointment

Tata group shares advanced in early trade on September 17, 2026, following reports that N Chandrasekaran will be reappointed as chairman for a second five-year term. The stock of Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) gained 2.5%, while Tata Motors and Tata Steel rose over 3% each. The reappointment, expected to be formalised at the board meeting later this week, provides continuity to the conglomerate's turnaround strategy.

Chandrasekaran, who took over in 2017, has streamlined operations, reduced debt, and focused on digital transformation. Investors view his extension as a vote of confidence in the group's long-term vision, especially amid global economic uncertainties.

Market Reaction and Key Movers

The BSE Sensex was up 450 points, or 0.6%, at 75,300, while the Nifty 50 gained 0.7% to 22,800. Among Tata stocks, Tata Consumer Products rose 2.8%, and Titan Company added 1.9%. Analysts attribute the rally to renewed foreign institutional interest and expectations of improved capital allocation.

NSE IPO Opens: Subscription Details and Valuation

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) opened its initial public offering (IPO) for subscription today, marking one of the largest issues in India's capital markets. The IPO aims to raise over ₹10,000 crore through an offer for sale by existing shareholders, including several banks and private equity firms. The price band is set at ₹1,450–₹1,525 per share, valuing the exchange at approximately ₹1.5 lakh crore.

The issue closes on September 20, 2026. Anchor investors have already committed ₹3,000 crore, indicating strong institutional demand. Retail investors can bid for a minimum of one lot (10 shares).

Pros and Cons for Investors

  • Pros:
  • NSE is India's largest stock exchange by trading volume, with a near-monopoly in derivatives.
  • Strong financials: FY26 revenue up 18% YoY, net profit margin of 55%.
  • Listing gains likely, given the scarcity premium and robust secondary market.
  • Cons:
  • Regulatory risks, including SEBI's ongoing probe into co-location case.
  • High valuation leaves little room for error.
  • Competition from BSE and new entrants like NSE's own platform.

Broader Market Outlook and Expert Take

Market experts believe the Tata rally and NSE IPO will boost overall sentiment. "The reappointment removes uncertainty, and NSE's listing will deepen India's capital markets," said an analyst at a Mumbai-based brokerage. However, they caution that global headwinds, including US Fed policy and oil prices, could cap upside.

For long-term investors, Tata stocks offer stable growth, while the NSE IPO suits those with a higher risk appetite. Both events underscore India's growing economic clout.
