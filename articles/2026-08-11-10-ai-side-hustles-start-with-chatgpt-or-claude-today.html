10 AI Side Hustles: Start With ChatGPT or Claude Today

📅 2026-08-11 📂 AI Tips & Tools Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pick a side hustle that uses AI for output, not just ideas.
  • Validate demand before building a full service.
  • Use ChatGPT or Claude to automate the repetitive parts, not the client relationship.

Why This List Matters Now

Forbes recently published a list of 10 AI-powered side hustles built around ChatGPT or Claude. The ideas range from content editing to chatbot setup for small shops. Most require no coding. You can start with a free account and a few hours a week.

The key is not to treat these tools as magic. Treat them as a junior assistant that works fast but needs supervision. Your edge is your judgment, your niche knowledge, and your ability to deliver a finished product.

Step 1: Choose One Hustle, Not Three

Pick one from the Forbes list that matches a skill you already have. If you write well, try AI-assisted blog posts or email newsletters. If you know local businesses, try setting up AI chatbots for customer queries. If you edit videos, use Claude to generate captions and timestamps.

Do not spread yourself thin. A single focused service will earn more than five half-baked offerings. Write down your pick and your target customer.

Step 2: Validate the Demand Before You Build

Post in local Facebook groups or LinkedIn. Ask: "If I offered [service] for [price], would anyone pay?" Get at least three positive replies or pre-orders before you invest time.

Check competing rates. Search Fiverr and Upwork for similar gigs. Note the price range and what clients complain about. That gap is your entry point.

Step 3: Set Up Your AI Workflow

Create a dedicated folder for each client. Use a consistent prompt template for every task. For ChatGPT or Claude, write a system prompt that includes the client's brand voice, tone, and do-not-say words.

Here is a concrete 5-step workflow you can copy:

  • 1. Write a project brief in your own words: what the deliverable is, who reads it, what action they should take.
  • 2. Feed the brief to the AI with a specific instruction, e.g., "Draft a 500-word product description for a handloom saree shop, tone friendly and premium."
  • 3. Ask the AI to output three variations. Do not accept the first draft.
  • 4. Edit the best variation yourself. Add local details, prices, or anecdotes the AI cannot know.
  • 5. Run a final check: read it aloud, verify facts, and remove any generic phrases.

This workflow takes 30–60 minutes per deliverable. Charge for the value, not the time.

Step 4: Package It as a Subscription or Project

One-off gigs are exhausting. Offer a monthly package: 4 blog posts, 8 social captions, or 1 chatbot update. Use a simple Google Sheet to track deliverables and deadlines.

For chatbot setup, charge a setup fee plus a monthly maintenance fee. Most small businesses in India prefer a flat rate under ₹10,000 per month. That is your sweet spot.

Step 5: Automate the Repetitive, Humanise the Rest

Use ChatGPT or Claude to draft invoices, write follow-up emails, and summarise client feedback. But never let the AI write your proposal or handle a complaint. Clients can smell automation.

Your personal touch is what separates you from a free bot. Reply to messages within a few hours, deliver early, and ask for a testimonial after each project.

When This Works — And When It Doesn't

This approach works when you have a clear niche, a paying client, and a willingness to edit AI output. It fails when you expect the tool to replace thinking or when you copy-paste generic text. Use it as a lever, not a crutch.
