Pakistan on emergency footing after BLA ambush kills 45 soldiers in Balochistan

📅 2026-07-17 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • 45 Pakistani soldiers killed in BLA ambush in Balochistan, the fourth attack this month
  • Pakistan declares emergency: hospitals on alert, helicopters deployed, highways closed
  • BLA claims responsibility for the Mastung ambush, exposing tactical vulnerabilities
  • At least 3 killed and 29 injured in a separate attack on a military convoy in SW Pakistan
  • Five civilians also killed in the ambush on security forces, officials confirm

Emergency declared after deadliest attack in weeks

Pakistan has been pushed into an emergency footing after an ambush by the Baloch Liberation Army (BLA) killed at least 45 soldiers in Balochistan province. The attack, which took place near Mastung, is the fourth such assault on security forces this month alone.

Authorities have placed hospitals in the region on high alert, mobilised military helicopters, and closed key highways. The scale of the response underscores the severity of the incident, which officials describe as one of the deadliest single attacks on the army in recent years.

BLA claims responsibility, exposes tactical gaps

The BLA has claimed responsibility for the Mastung ambush, saying it targeted a convoy of security personnel. In a statement, the group said the operation was carried out by its suicide bombing unit, the Majeed Brigade, and involved a roadside bombing followed by a suicide attack.

News18, citing exclusive details, reported that the ambush exposed significant tactical frailties in Pakistan's counter-insurgency operations in Balochistan. The attackers appeared to have precise knowledge of the convoy's route and timing, raising questions about intelligence failures.

At least 3 people were killed and 29 injured in a separate attack on a military convoy in southwestern Pakistan, according to Xinhua. The Arab News, quoting an official, confirmed that five civilians also died in the ambush on the security forces convoy.

Highways closed, helicopters deployed

The Pakistani government has declared a state of emergency in the affected areas. All major highways leading in and out of Mastung and surrounding districts have been closed to civilian traffic. Military helicopters are conducting aerial surveillance and casualty evacuation operations.

Hospitals in Quetta and Mastung have been put on high alert, with medical teams instructed to prepare for mass casualties. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of injured, but local sources suggest the figure could be higher than initially reported.

Fourth attack in a month signals escalating insurgency

This ambush marks the fourth major attack by the BLA in July alone, signalling an escalation in the group's insurgency against the Pakistani state. The BLA has been fighting for the independence of Balochistan, a resource-rich but impoverished province, for decades.

The Pakistani military has launched several operations in the region over the years, but the latest attacks suggest the group retains the capacity to strike with precision and lethality. Analysts say the BLA's use of suicide bombers and improvised explosive devices is becoming more sophisticated.

ThePrint reported that 45 soldiers were killed in this attack, making it the deadliest single incident in Balochistan this year. The BLA claimed responsibility for the attack in a statement sent to media outlets.

What happens next

The Pakistani military is expected to launch a large-scale retaliatory operation in Balochistan in the coming days. Security forces will likely intensify patrols and intelligence-gathering in Mastung and other districts. The civilian death toll may rise as authorities complete their assessments. The BLA's ability to execute such a coordinated attack will remain a key concern for Islamabad.
