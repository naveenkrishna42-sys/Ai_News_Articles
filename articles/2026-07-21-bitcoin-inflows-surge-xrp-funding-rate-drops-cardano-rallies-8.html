Bitcoin Inflows Surge, XRP Funding Rate Drops, Cardano Rallies 8%

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin recorded significant inflows, indicating renewed investor interest.
  • XRP funding rate plunged into negative territory, suggesting bearish sentiment.
  • Cardano surged 8%, outperforming major cryptocurrencies in the last 24 hours.

Bitcoin Inflows Signal Renewed Investor Interest

Bitcoin, the world's largest cryptocurrency by market capitalisation, has recorded notable inflows in the latest trading session. Data from multiple exchanges suggest that investors are moving funds into Bitcoin, a trend often interpreted as a bullish signal.

The inflows come amid a broader market recovery, with Bitcoin holding steady above recent support levels. Analysts are watching closely to see if this momentum can sustain, given the volatile nature of the asset class.

XRP Funding Rate Turns Negative

In contrast to Bitcoin's strength, XRP's funding rate has plunged sharply. The funding rate, a key metric for perpetual futures contracts, fell into negative territory for the first time in weeks. This indicates that short sellers are dominating the market for XRP.

A negative funding rate means traders betting against XRP are paying those with long positions. While this can sometimes precede a price squeeze, it currently reflects bearish sentiment around the token. The exact reasons for the shift remain unclear, though broader market dynamics and regulatory news are often cited.

Cardano Jumps 8% in 24 Hours

Cardano was the standout performer among major cryptocurrencies, surging 8% in the last 24 hours. The rally pushed ADA past a key resistance level, drawing attention from traders and analysts alike.

The jump appears to be driven by a combination of technical buying and positive network developments. Cardano's development team has been active with upgrades, though no specific catalyst was confirmed at the time of writing. The move outpaced gains in Bitcoin and Ethereum, which were relatively flat during the same period.

Market Overview and What to Watch

The broader cryptocurrency market is showing mixed signals. While Bitcoin inflows and Cardano's rally suggest pockets of strength, XRP's funding rate drop highlights the persistent caution among traders. Regulatory developments in major economies continue to weigh on sentiment, particularly for tokens like XRP that have faced legal scrutiny.

Volume across exchanges has been moderate, with no extreme volatility spikes. Traders are advised to monitor funding rates and inflow data closely, as these metrics often precede larger moves. The coming days will test whether the current trends are sustainable or merely a short-term reaction.

Looking ahead, attention is on the upcoming Federal Reserve meeting and its potential impact on risk assets. Crypto markets remain sensitive to macroeconomic cues, and any shift in interest rate expectations could trigger significant price action.
