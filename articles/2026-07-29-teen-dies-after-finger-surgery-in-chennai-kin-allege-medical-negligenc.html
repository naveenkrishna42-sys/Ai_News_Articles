Teen dies after finger surgery in Chennai; kin allege medical negligence

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 16-year-old boy died after undergoing surgery for a finger injury at a private hospital in Chennai.
  • The family alleges medical negligence, claiming the boy's condition deteriorated rapidly post-surgery.
  • Chennai police have registered a case under relevant sections and are investigating the hospital's procedures.

A 16-year-old boy died earlier this week after undergoing surgery for a finger injury at a private hospital in Chennai. His family has alleged medical negligence and lodged a complaint with the local police. The incident has sparked concern among residents and renewed scrutiny on surgical protocols in smaller healthcare facilities.

The Surgery and Sudden Deterioration

The teenager had sustained a minor cut on his finger, which required a routine surgical procedure. The operation took place at a private clinic in the Perambur area. According to family members, the boy was initially stable after the surgery. But within hours, his condition worsened rapidly.

“He was fine when we brought him home. Then he started vomiting and complained of severe pain. By the time we reached the hospital again, it was too late,” a relative told reporters. The hospital staff attempted resuscitation, but the boy was declared dead shortly after arrival.

Family Alleges Negligence

The boy’s parents have accused the doctors of failing to administer proper post-operative care. They claim that the hospital did not monitor the patient adequately and discharged him prematurely. “No one checked his vitals before sending him home. They just gave some painkillers and said everything was fine,” the father said.

A formal complaint was filed at the Perambur police station on Tuesday. The police have registered a case under Section 304A of the Indian Penal Code (causing death by negligence) and are investigating. Hospital authorities have not issued a public statement so far.

Police Investigation Underway

Police officials said they have seized medical records and are questioning the doctors and nursing staff involved. A senior officer stated that the exact cause of death will be determined by the post-mortem report, which is awaited. “We are looking into whether the recommended protocols were followed. If negligence is proven, strict action will be taken,” the officer said.

Medical experts say that while finger surgeries are generally low-risk, complications such as infection or adverse reactions to anaesthesia can occur. In this case, investigators are also examining the type of anaesthesia used and the sterile conditions during the procedure.

Broader Concerns

The incident has reignited debates about the quality of care in smaller nursing homes and clinics across Chennai. Residents in the area expressed shock that a simple procedure could lead to a fatality. “We trust these doctors with our lives. When something like this happens, it shakes that trust,” a local resident said.

Medical negligence cases have been on the rise in Tamil Nadu, with patient safety advocates calling for stricter regulatory oversight. The state health department has not yet commented on this specific case.

The family is now awaiting the post-mortem results and has demanded a thorough inquiry. They have also sought compensation, though legal proceedings are in the early stages. The hospital premises were reportedly calm on Wednesday, with routine operations continuing.

What comes next hinges on the autopsy report and the police investigation. If evidence of negligence is found, the doctors involved could face legal action and the hospital may face a suspension of its license. The case is being closely watched by patient rights groups in the city.
