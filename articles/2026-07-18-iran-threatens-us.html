Iran Escalates Rhetoric Against US

Iran has unveiled a new billboard in Tehran's Enghelab Square, showing Donald Trump lying in a coffin. The billboard features a slogan that reads 'We Kill Trump', escalating the rhetoric against the US. This move comes after a wave of US strikes, which have heightened tensions between the two nations.

Billboard and Mural Campaign

The billboard is part of a larger campaign that includes murals depicting Trump and Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu drowning in a 'sea of revenge'. These visuals are a stark representation of the deepening rift between Iran and the US. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact message Iran intends to convey with these billboards and murals.
