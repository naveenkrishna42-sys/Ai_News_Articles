Jaffna Kings franchise agreement terminated mid-season in LPL

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jaffna Kings franchise agreement terminated mid-season by Sri Lanka Cricket
  • Co-owner Manjot Kalra arrested in Sri Lanka in bribery probe
  • Team will continue in Lanka Premier League under SLC operational control

In a dramatic mid-season development, Sri Lanka Cricket (SLC) has terminated the franchise agreement of Jaffna Kings in the Lanka Premier League (LPL) following the arrest of co-owner Manjot Kalra on bribery and corruption charges. The decision was announced on Thursday, leaving the tournament's defending champions under the direct operational control of the cricket board.

Manjot Kalra, the former India Under-19 World Cup-winning hero from 2018, was arrested in Sri Lanka earlier this week. Reports from local media indicate he is being investigated for his alleged involvement in a bribery probe linked to the LPL. Kalra, who also played alongside Shubman Gill in that victorious U-19 squad, had become a co-owner of the Jaffna Kings franchise in recent years.

Franchise Agreement Scrapped Mid-Tournament

SLC moved swiftly to sever ties with the franchise ownership group. The termination of the franchise agreement means the existing management structure is dissolved with immediate effect. However, the league has stressed that the team itself will continue to participate in the ongoing LPL season without any disruption.

"The Lanka Premier League will continue with the Jaffna Kings team, which will now be operated directly by SLC," an official statement from the board read. The decision ensures that players, support staff, and match schedules remain unaffected by the off-field turmoil.

Team Operations Under SLC Control

With the franchise owners removed, SLC has taken over the day-to-day running of the team. This includes managing player contracts, logistics, and match-day operations for the remainder of the tournament. The board has assured that all existing player agreements will be honoured.

The development has sent ripples through the LPL, which has been trying to establish itself as a credible T20 league. The Jaffna Kings are one of the league's most successful franchises, having won multiple titles. Their campaign this season now continues under a cloud of uncertainty regarding future ownership.

Manjot Kalra's Fall from Grace

Manjot Kalra's arrest marks a stunning fall for the cricketer-turned-businessman. He was a hero of India's 2018 U-19 World Cup victory, scoring a match-winning century in the final against Australia. After failing to break into the senior national team, Kalra transitioned into franchise ownership in Sri Lanka.

His arrest has raised questions about the governance and vetting processes within the LPL. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact nature of the bribery allegations, but sources indicate the probe involves financial irregularities related to player selection and contract awards.

The Jaffna Kings franchise agreement termination is a clear signal from SLC that it will not tolerate corruption within its premier domestic competition. The board has not commented on whether it will seek new owners for the franchise after the season ends.

What happens next for the Jaffna Kings beyond the current season remains unclear. SLC has not announced a timeline for finding new ownership or whether the franchise will be put up for auction. For now, the team's focus remains on the field, but the off-field drama is far from over.
