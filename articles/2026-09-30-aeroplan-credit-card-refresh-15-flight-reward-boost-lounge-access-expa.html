Overview of the Aeroplan Benefits Overhaul

Aeroplan, Air Canada's loyalty program, has announced a refresh of its affiliated credit card benefits effective September 2026. The update introduces a 15% discount on flight redemptions and expands lounge access for cardholders. This analysis breaks down the practical implications for prospective buyers, covering earning structures, fees, and eligibility criteria.

What the 15% Flight Reward Discount Means

The new benefit allows cardholders to receive a 15% discount when using Aeroplan points to book flights through the Aeroplan website or mobile app. For example, a reward ticket that normally costs 60,000 points would now require only 51,000 points. This effectively increases the value of each point by roughly 0.18 cents, assuming a baseline valuation of 1.2 cents per point. The discount applies to both Economy and Premium cabin redemptions, but does not extend to partner airline awards or cash‑plus‑points bookings.

Because the discount is applied at the point of redemption, it does not affect the earning rate on everyday purchases. Cardholders who regularly redeem for Air Canada flights will see a noticeable reduction in the points needed for a given itinerary. The benefit is automatically applied when logging into the Aeroplan portal; no promo code or registration step is required.

Expanded Lounge Access Details

Under the revised program, eligible Aeroplan credit cards now provide up to four complimentary visits per year to Air Canada Maple Leaf Lounges, in addition to existing Priority Pass selections where applicable. The lounge visits are valid for domestic and international departures from Canadian airports. Cardholders must present the associated credit card and a same‑day boarding pass to gain entry. Access is not transferable and does not include guest privileges unless the card specifically offers them.

Each visit grants access to standard lounge amenities such as complimentary snacks, beverages, Wi‑Fi, and workspaces. The four‑visit cap resets on the cardholder’s anniversary date, not the calendar year. Travelers who exceed the allotted visits can purchase day passes at the published lounge rate or rely on any complementary Priority Pass lounges included with the card.

Fee Structure and Eligibility Criteria

Most Aeroplan‑branded cards carry an annual fee ranging from CAD 120 to CAD 150, with no separate joining fee. Income requirements typically start at a personal gross income of CAD 60,000 or a household income of CAD 100,000, depending on the issuer. A minimum credit score in the good to excellent range (usually 660+ on the Equifax scale) is expected. Foreign transaction fees are generally set at 2.5% on purchases made outside Canada.
