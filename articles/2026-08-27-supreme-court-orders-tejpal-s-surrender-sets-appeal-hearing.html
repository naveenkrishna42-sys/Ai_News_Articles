Supreme Court Rejects Plea, Sets Surrender Deadline

The Supreme Court has rejected Tarun Tejpal's plea to postpone his surrender, directing the former Tehelka editor to appear before the court within two weeks. This decision comes as Tejpal seeks to challenge his conviction in a 2013 rape case. The court has made it clear that the appeal hearing will follow after his surrender.

Background of the Case

Tarun Tejpal, a prominent journalist and former editor of Tehelka, was convicted in a 2013 rape case. The case has been under scrutiny for several years, with Tejpal maintaining his innocence throughout.
