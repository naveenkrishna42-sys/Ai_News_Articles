East Zone Edge South Zone as Kishan and Mohan Post Crucial Fifties in Duleep Trophy Final

Match Overview

The Duleep Trophy final on 8 September 2026 saw East Zone chase a modest 258 against South Zone at the Eden Gardens. After a disciplined first‑innings spell, South Zone posted 257/9, thanks to a resilient 48 from their skipper and a quick‑fire 55 from the middle order.

East Zone began their chase with confidence, but early wickets put them under pressure. A partnership between Ishan Kishan (57) and Mohan (62) steadied the innings, ultimately delivering a 12‑run win.

Key Performances

Batting Highlights

Kishan, known for his aggressive T20 style, tempered his approach, rotating the strike and anchoring the chase. Mohan, a rising talent from the domestic circuit, displayed composure, striking 8 fours and a six.

South Zone's bowlers, led by veteran pacer Rajesh Sharma (3/45), kept the run rate in check, but the East Zone middle order's partnership proved decisive.

Bowling Impact

East Zone's spin duo, Arjun Singh (2/38) and Rahul Desai (2/41), restricted South Zone's lower order, while fast bowler Vikram Patel claimed crucial wickets at the death, ending with figures of 4/52.

Implications for Player Futures

The performance cements Kishan's case for a red‑ball comeback, adding depth to India's limited‑overs pool. Mohan's innings signals his readiness for higher honors, potentially earning a spot in the India A squad.

South Zone's captain, despite the loss, showcased leadership that could translate into future leadership roles at the national level.

Looking Ahead

With the domestic season winding down, selectors will weigh these performances heavily for upcoming tours and IPL auctions. East Zone's victory also highlights the growing competitiveness of zonal cricket, promising tighter contests in future editions.
