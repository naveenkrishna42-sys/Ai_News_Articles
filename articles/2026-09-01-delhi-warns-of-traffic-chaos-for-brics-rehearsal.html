Delhi braces for heavy traffic as BRICS rehearsal looms

Delhi Traffic Police has issued an advisory urging residents and commuters to plan their journeys in advance ahead of the BRICS 2026 motorcade rehearsal. The rehearsal, set to take place in central Delhi, is expected to cause significant traffic congestion, with traffic diversions planned to manage the influx of vehicles.

Why this matters

The BRICS Summit, scheduled for 2026, is a significant event that brings together leaders from Brazil, Russia, India, China, and South Africa. The rehearsal for the motorcade is a crucial part of the event's logistics, and the expected heavy traffic congestion highlights the challenges of managing such large-scale events in urban areas.

Broader impact

The BRICS countries represent a combined GDP of 40% of the global economy and half of the world's population. The summit is expected to have a significant impact on global economic and political dynamics. The traffic congestion in Delhi, however, underscores the logistical complexities of hosting such a high-profile event.

Expert opinion

Urban planner Dr. Ananya Kapoor from the Delhi Urban Planning Institute has warned that the rehearsal could lead to severe traffic gridlock. "The sheer number of vehicles expected during the rehearsal will overwhelm Delhi's existing traffic management systems," she said. "Residents should expect prolonged travel times and consider alternative modes of transportation if possible."
