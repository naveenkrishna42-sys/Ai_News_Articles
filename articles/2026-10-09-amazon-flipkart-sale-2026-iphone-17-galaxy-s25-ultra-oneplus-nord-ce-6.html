Overview of the Festive Sale

Amazon and Flipkart have announced that their festive sales will commence at midnight on 7 October 2026. The event is timed to capture early‑bird shoppers looking for upgrades ahead of the holiday season. Retailers typically roll out flash‑sale slots, exchange bonuses and bank‑cashback offers that can stack, making the midnight window a critical period for securing the best price.

What the Leaks Suggest

iPhone 17 Expected Features

While Apple has not officially unveiled the iPhone 17, supply‑chain reports point to a refined titanium frame, a periscope telephoto lens and a modest bump in battery capacity. The device is expected to retain the A‑series Bionic chipset lineage, with performance gains likely in the 10‑15 % range over the iPhone 16.

Galaxy S25 Ultra Expected Features

Samsung’s upcoming Ultra variant is rumored to feature an under‑display camera, a new 200 MP adaptive pixel sensor and an upgraded Snapdragon 8 Gen 4 (or Exynos equivalent) chipset. Display improvements may include a higher peak brightness and a variable refresh rate up to 144 Hz.

OnePlus Nord CE 6 Expected Features

OnePlus is anticipated to refresh the Nord CE line with a 90 Hz AMOLED panel, a Snapdragon 7+ Gen 2 processor and a 50 MP main camera equipped with optical image stabilization. The battery size is likely to stay around 5,000 mAh with faster wired charging.

Historical Discount Patterns

Analyzing past festive sales from 2022‑2025, premium smartphones saw average price reductions of 15‑25 % on launch‑day offers, with additional savings of up to 5 % via bank‑cashback and exchange bonuses. For example, the iPhone 15 series dropped from ₹79,900 to ₹66,990 during the 2023 sale, while the Galaxy S23 Ultra fell from ₹1,24,990 to ₹1,02,990.
