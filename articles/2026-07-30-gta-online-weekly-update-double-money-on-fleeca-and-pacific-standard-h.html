GTA Online Weekly Update: Double Money on Fleeca and Pacific Standard Heists

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Double GTA$ and RP on The Fleeca Heist and The Pacific Standard Heist Finale
  • Summer Heist Event introduces new bonuses and discounts
  • New Pegassi Ignus Pursuit law enforcement vehicle added

Double Money on Classic Heists

Rockstar Games has rolled out its latest weekly update for GTA Online, offering players double GTA$ and RP on two fan-favourite heists. The Fleeca Heist and The Pacific Standard Heist Finale are the focus this week, part of the ongoing Summer Heist Event.

Players looking for a quick score can run The Fleeca Heist, a two-player job that serves as an entry point to the game's heist system. The Pacific Standard Heist Finale, meanwhile, offers a larger payout for teams willing to coordinate a bank robbery in Los Santos.

New Law Enforcement Vehicle Arrives

Rockstar has also introduced the Pegassi Ignus Pursuit, a new police supercar now available in the game. This law enforcement vehicle joins the roster of emergency response cars, giving players another option for roleplay or pursuit missions.

The Ignus Pursuit is modelled after real-world hypercars and comes with high-speed handling. It can be purchased from Warstock Cache & Carry, though prices have not been officially listed in the update notes.

5X Race Payouts and Bonuses

Beyond heists, this week's update brings 5X GTA$ and RP on select race modes. The exact list of races included has not been detailed, but players can expect boosted payouts on several featured series.

Discounts are also live across a range of vehicles, properties, and weapons. Rockstar has not disclosed the full list, but dataminers have spotted price reductions on several high-end cars and aircraft.

Bug Fixes and Stability Updates

Rockstar has pushed a separate patch to address glitches tied to the recent Kortz Center Heist update. The fix targets issues that caused mission freezes and payout errors.

Players on both console and PC should see the update applied automatically. The developer has not released detailed patch notes, but community reports suggest the fix resolves the most common crashes.

The Summer Heist Event will run until next Thursday. Rockstar typically refreshes bonuses and discounts every seven days, so players have until then to claim double rewards on the highlighted heists.
