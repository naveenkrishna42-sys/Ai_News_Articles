Anna Hazare's Health Crisis

Social activist Anna Hazare has been admitted to the intensive care unit (ICU) of a Mumbai hospital after his health deteriorated due to a viral infection and severe dehydration. The 72-year-old activist, known for his role in the 2011 anti-corruption movement, was shifted from Delhi to Mumbai for better medical care.

Medical Condition and Treatment

Hospital authorities have described Hazare's condition as critical. He is receiving intensive medical treatment to combat the viral infection and rehydrate his body. The exact nature of the virus has not been disclosed, but the severity of his condition has prompted a rapid transfer to Mumbai.
