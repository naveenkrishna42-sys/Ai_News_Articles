IIT Madras to Live Stream 63rd Convocation Ceremony

Key points
  • IIT Madras is holding its 63rd convocation ceremony.

IIT Madras Set for 63rd Convocation

The Indian Institute of Technology Madras will hold its 63rd convocation ceremony on July 15, 2026. The institute has announced that the event will be live streamed for graduates and their families.

This allows alumni across the country and abroad to participate virtually. The decision reflects the institute's push toward digital accessibility.

How to Watch the Live Stream

Graduates can access the live stream through the official IIT Madras website. The link is available on the institute's homepage under the convocation section.

Viewers do not need special software. A stable internet connection and a browser are sufficient. The stream will begin at the scheduled time of the ceremony.

Details for Graduates and Families

Students who completed their degrees in the last academic year are eligible to attend. The convocation marks the formal conferral of degrees for undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes.

Families are encouraged to watch the live stream if they cannot attend in person. The institute has not yet confirmed the number of graduates participating.

Institutional Significance

The 63rd convocation is a milestone for IIT Madras, one of India's premier engineering institutes. The ceremony celebrates academic achievement and research output.

IIT Madras has consistently ranked among the top engineering colleges in the country. Its convocation events typically feature speeches from distinguished guests and award presentations.

What to Watch For

Officials have not released the full schedule or list of awardees. The live stream will include the convocation address and the degree distribution.

Graduates should check the institute's website for updates on timing and technical requirements. The live stream will be archived after the event.

IIT Madras continues to adapt to changing times by making its ceremonies accessible online. The 63rd convocation will be a moment of pride for the graduating class and their families.
