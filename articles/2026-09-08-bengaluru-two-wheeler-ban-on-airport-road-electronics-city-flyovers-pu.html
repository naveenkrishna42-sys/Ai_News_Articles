Bengaluru: Two-Wheeler Ban on Airport Road, Electronics City Flyovers Put on Hold

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Team 📅 2026-09-08 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bengaluru BDA has deferred the proposed two-wheeler ban on Airport Road and Electronics City flyovers for 3 months, pushing the original September 15 2026 effective date to December 2026.
  • The hold follows backlash from 2.3 lakh daily two-wheeler users, including low-income workers and delivery executives, who cited unaffordable alternative transport options and added commute times of 45-60 minutes.
  • The BDA will conduct a 3-month public consultation and feasibility study,

Market Position and Competition

The QuantumX enters a competitive landscape dominated by established players like Intel and AMD. While its price-to-performance ratio is favorable, it lags behind in single-core performance when measured against the latest Intel offerings. However, its unique integration of AI acceleration and energy efficiency features positions it as a niche leader in specialized workloads. AMD’s Ryzen 9 7950X remains a formidable competitor in the multi-core space, though it lacks the QuantumX’s AI-centric optimizations.
