Apple Back to School 2026: Gift cards with MacBook, iPad purchases

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple's Back to School 2026 promotion offers gift cards with purchase of eligible MacBook and iPad models.
  • Students and educators in India get free AirPods with Mac purchase, and Apple Pencil with iPad.
  • The offer is available for a limited period through Apple's official channels.
  • Eligible models include MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iPad Pro and iPad Air.

Apple has launched its annual Back to School promotion for 2026 in India, offering gift cards to students and educators purchasing select MacBook and iPad models. The promotion, which runs for a limited period, is aimed at making Apple devices more accessible for the academic year.

What the offer includes

Under the scheme, customers who buy a MacBook Air or MacBook Pro will receive a gift card. Similarly, those purchasing an iPad Air or iPad Pro are eligible for a gift card. The exact value of the gift cards has not been specified by Apple in the initial announcement, but reports from multiple sources indicate that the offer also includes free AirPods with eligible Mac purchases and a free Apple Pencil with compatible iPad models.

This is not a flat discount on the device itself. Instead, the gift card can be used for future purchases on Apple's online or offline stores, including accessories, software, or services. The company typically targets the promotion at students, parents of students, and faculty members at educational institutions.

Eligibility and availability

The promotion is available in India, as confirmed by reports from The Indian Express and Hindustan Times. Apple has not officially disclosed the exact end date for the offer, but previous Back to School promotions have typically run from mid-July through September or early October. Verification of educational status may be required at the time of purchase, either via Apple's education store portal or in physical retail locations.

The offer is limited to specific models. The eligible devices include the MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iPad Air, and iPad Pro. Older models or refurbished units may not qualify. Apple has also not clarified whether the offer applies to purchases made through authorised third-party resellers.

Context and comparison

Apple's Back to School promotion is a recurring annual event, but the format has changed over the years. In 2024, the company offered free AirPods with Mac purchases and a gift card with iPad purchases. The 2026 version appears to follow a similar structure, with the addition of gift cards for both product lines. Competitors like Samsung and Lenovo also run student discount programmes, but Apple's offer is notable for bundling high-value accessories.

The promotion comes at a time when Apple is seeing strong demand in India for its higher-end laptops and tablets. The Indian education market has been a key growth area for the company, which has been expanding its retail presence and online education store in the country.

What to watch

Students and educators looking to take advantage of the offer should verify eligibility criteria and check Apple's official education store for the most accurate terms. The gift card amount and specific model availability may vary by region. Apple is expected to keep the promotion running through the start of the academic year, but no official end date has been confirmed yet.
