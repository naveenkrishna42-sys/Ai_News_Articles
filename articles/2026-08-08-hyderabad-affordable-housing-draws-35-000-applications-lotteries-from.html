Hyderabad affordable housing draws 35,000 applications; lotteries from Aug 20

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Over 35,000 applications received for affordable flats in Hyderabad
  • Application deadline is August 10
  • Lotteries for allocation to begin from August 20

Hyderabad's affordable housing scheme has drawn a strong response, with over 35,000 applications already submitted for the limited number of flats on offer. The sheer volume underscores the pressing demand for budget homes in the city, where property prices have climbed steadily over the past few years.

The Telangana government's initiative, aimed at middle and lower-income groups, has clearly struck a chord. Officials said the application window remains open until August 10, and those who have not yet applied can still do so before the deadline. After that, the allotment process will be set in motion.

Lottery process to begin soon

Once the application window closes, the authorities will move quickly to the next stage. Lotteries for the allocation of flats are scheduled to start from August 20. This randomised draw is expected to bring transparency to the process, ensuring that all eligible applicants get a fair chance.

The housing department has not yet disclosed the exact number of flats available in this phase, but sources indicate that the response has far exceeded the inventory. Officials have urged applicants to keep their registration details handy and verify their eligibility before the deadline.

Why the rush for affordable homes

Hyderabad has seen rapid urbanisation, with IT corridors and industrial hubs attracting a steady influx of professionals. Rental costs in many parts of the city have risen sharply, pushing many families to seek ownership options within a manageable budget.

The scheme offers flats at rates significantly below the prevailing market prices, making them an attractive proposition for first-time buyers. For many, this is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to own a home in a city where even modest apartments can cost several lakhs of rupees.

Eligibility and documentation

Applicants need to meet specific income and residential criteria, and the authorities have been strict about verifying documents. In the past, similar schemes have seen a number of applications rejected at the scrutiny stage due to incomplete paperwork or ineligibility.

Officials have advised applicants to ensure that all required documents, including income certificates, address proof, and Aadhaar details, are in order before the deadline. Any discrepancy could lead to disqualification at the lottery stage.

What happens after the lottery

Successful candidates will be notified through official channels, and a schedule for document verification and payment will follow. The entire process is expected to be completed over the next few months, with possession of flats likely to be handed over in phases.

For those who do not make it in this round, the waiting list could offer a second chance, as some applicants may drop out during verification or fail to complete payments on time.

The response to this scheme is a clear indicator of the housing shortage in Hyderabad. The government may need to launch more such projects to keep up with demand. For now, all eyes are on the August 20 lottery, which will decide the fate of tens of thousands of hopeful applicants.
