Opening and Closing Highlights

The Jagran Film Festival has officially announced the two films that will anchor this year’s edition. Organisers confirmed that the Jaaved Jaaferi starrer Shikayatein will serve as the opening film, setting the stage for the festival's programming. The event will conclude with the screening of Lost & Found Kumbh.

These selections represent the festival's focus on blending established talent with compelling narratives. By positioning these specific titles at the start and end of the schedule, the festival aims to draw significant attention to its diverse slate of screenings.

A Focus on Indian Storytelling

The Jagran Film Festival has long served as a platform for filmmakers to showcase regional and national cinema to a broader audience. The inclusion of Shikayatein, featuring veteran actor Jaaved Jaaferi, is expected to be a major draw for attendees. The film joins a long line of high-profile opening titles in the festival's history.

On the other end of the schedule, Lost & Found Kumbh will provide the final act of the event. Organisers have curated this selection to reflect themes that resonate with domestic audiences.
