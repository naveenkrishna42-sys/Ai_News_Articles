# 80 — TivraNews SEO & Google News Policy

Optimize for editorial value, user trust, and sustainable search ranking over mass programmatic volume.

## 1. Google News Compliance
- Ensure every article carries:
  - Canonical URL
  - Visible publication and update timestamps
  - Editorial author attribution (TIVRA News Editorial Team)
  - Clear primary source citations with outbound attribution links
  - Valid `NewsArticle` schema JSON-LD with structured headline, author, and date metadata

## 2. Category Routing & Technical SEO
- Maintain dynamic category routing so that all slugs (`credit-cards-cashback`, `product-deals-offers`, `gadget-comparisons`, `ai-tips-tools`) render populated article grids without empty state errors.
- Ensure `robots.txt` and `sitemap-index.xml` remain accessible and valid for major search engine crawlers (Googlebot, Googlebot-News, Bingbot).
