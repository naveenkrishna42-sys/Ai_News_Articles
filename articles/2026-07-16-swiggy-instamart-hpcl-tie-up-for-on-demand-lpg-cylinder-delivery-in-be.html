Swiggy Instamart, HPCL tie up for on-demand LPG cylinder delivery in Bengaluru

📅 2026-07-16 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Swiggy Instamart has partnered with HPCL to offer on-demand LPG cylinder delivery in Bengaluru.
  • The service allows users to order cylinders through the Swiggy app for rapid home delivery.
  • The pilot launch aims to address common refill delays and improve convenience for city residents.

Swiggy Instamart has tied up with Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) to deliver LPG cylinders on-demand in Bengaluru. The partnership aims to give households a faster alternative to the traditional refill booking system. Users can now order a cylinder through the Swiggy app, much like they would order groceries.

The service is being rolled out in select areas of the city as a pilot. HPCL will supply the cylinders, while Swiggy Instamart handles the last-mile delivery. This is the first time an Indian quick-commerce platform has ventured into kitchen fuel delivery.

How the service works

Customers need to open the Swiggy app and search for 'LPG cylinder' within the Instamart section. The platform will show available HPCL cylinders for delivery. The order is fulfilled from a nearby Instamart dark store that stocks the cylinders.

Delivery is promised within minutes, similar to other Instamart orders. The cylinder is delivered to the doorstep, and the user pays online. HPCL will manage the safety checks and cylinder certification before handing them over to Swiggy's delivery partners.

Why this matters for Bengaluru residents

Bengaluru households often complain about delays in LPG refill delivery. Traditional booking through distributors can take days. This tie-up cuts that wait time drastically.

The partnership also taps into the growing quick-commerce habit in the city. Swiggy Instamart already delivers vegetables, snacks, and electronics in under 10 minutes. Adding cylinders to that list is a logical next step for the platform.

An HPCL official said the collaboration will help the company reach more urban consumers who prefer digital-first solutions. The company did not specify how many cylinders would be stocked per store or how the pricing compares to standard subsidised rates.

Safety and regulatory aspects

Transporting LPG cylinders comes with strict safety norms. Both companies have confirmed that delivery personnel will undergo training on handling pressurised gas containers. The cylinders will be packed in secure, tamper-proof bags.

Regulatory approvals for this model have been obtained from the relevant petroleum and explosives authorities. Officials have not yet confirmed if the service will be extended to other cities after the Bengaluru trial.

Swiggy Instamart has not disclosed the exact delivery fee or minimum order value for the cylinder service. The company said pricing will be competitive with market rates.

The pilot is limited to non-subsidised cylinders initially. Users will need to provide their existing consumer number or a valid ID for the transaction.

What to watch: If the Bengaluru pilot succeeds, similar partnerships could be replicated in other metros where quick-commerce networks are dense. The model may also open the door for other utilities like water cans or battery packs to be delivered on-demand.
