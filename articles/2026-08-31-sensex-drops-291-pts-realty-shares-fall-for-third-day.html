Sensex Plunges 291 Points Amid Market Turmoil

The Bombay Stock Exchange's Sensex dropped 291 points on Monday, August 31, 2026, marking the third consecutive day of decline. The Nifty 50 index also fell, closing at 24,536 points, down 64 points. The market's sharp decline has raised concerns about the economic outlook and investor sentiment.

Realty Sector Faces Third Consecutive Decline

Realty shares continued their downward trend, marking the third consecutive day of decline. The sector's performance has been particularly weak, with major players like DLF, Godrej Properties, and Oberoi Realty seeing significant drops in their stock prices. The decline in realty shares is attributed to concerns about the housing market slowdown and increased supply in the market.

Economic Uncertainty and Global Concerns

The market's decline is partly attributed to economic uncertainty and global concerns. The Federal Reserve's interest rate hikes and the ongoing geopolitical tensions have raised fears about a potential recession. Investors are also cautious about the impact of the upcoming general elections on the economic policy landscape.

Sectoral Performance and Market Sentiment

The market saw mixed performance across sectors. While the IT and pharma sectors saw gains, the banking and auto sectors faced declines. The market sentiment remains cautious, with investors awaiting clearer policy signals from the government and the central bank.

Expert Opinion and Analysis

Market experts suggest that the current decline is a correction rather than a bear market. They advise investors to stay patient and focus on long-term investments. The realty sector's decline is seen as a temporary setback, with experts predicting a recovery in the coming months.

However, the global market volatility and economic uncertainty pose significant challenges. Investors are urged to diversify their portfolios and stay informed about the latest market developments.
