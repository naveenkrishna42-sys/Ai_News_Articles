Migrant Worker Killed, Another Injured in Terror Attack in J&K's Kulgam

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Two migrant labourers were fired upon by terrorists in Kulgam district of Jammu and Kashmir.
  • One worker died on the spot, while another sustained injuries and was rushed to a hospital.
  • Security forces have cordoned off the area and launched a search operation to track down the attackers.

Attack on Labourers in South Kashmir

A migrant worker was killed and another injured in a terrorist attack in Jammu and Kashmir's Kulgam district on Friday. The victims, both non-local labourers, were fired upon by terrorists in the southern Kashmir region, officials said.

The incident took place in the evening hours, triggering immediate security response. Police have cordoned off the area and launched a search operation to apprehend the attackers.

Victims and Immediate Response

One of the labourers succumbed to gunshot wounds on the spot, while the second was rushed to a nearby medical facility for treatment. The condition of the injured worker was stated to be stable, though officials have not yet released the identities of the deceased or the wounded.

Authorities have not confirmed details about the workers' native places, but such attacks in the past have targeted labourers from other states working in the valley. The area has seen a history of violence against migrant workers, raising concerns about security for the non-local workforce.

Security Operation Underway

After the firing, security forces swiftly moved into the area. A joint team of police and paramilitary forces has been conducting searches in the surrounding villages. The operation is aimed at tracking down the terrorists who fled after the attack.

Officials have not yet made any arrests in connection with the incident. The region has been on edge in recent months, with periodic attacks on civilians and security personnel.

Background of Violence

Kulgam, located in south Kashmir, has been a hotspot for militant activity. Non-local labourers, often engaged in construction and other manual work, have been frequently targeted by terrorists in the valley. Such attacks have led to periodic exoduses of migrant workers, disrupting local economies.

In the past, authorities have tightened security around labour camps and provided escorts for workers in high-risk areas. However, Friday's attack underscores the persistent threat faced by this vulnerable group.

The administration has not yet issued an official statement on the incident, but local officials are expected to review security arrangements for migrant workers in the district. Police have appealed to the public to remain vigilant and report any suspicious activity.

What to Watch

Security forces are continuing their search operation, and more details on the injured worker's condition and the identity of the deceased are expected to be released soon. The attack is likely to prompt fresh measures to protect migrant labourers in the region, with the administration under pressure to ensure their safety.
