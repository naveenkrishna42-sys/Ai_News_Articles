Jantar Mantar Protest Updates

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has released an Instagram reel thanking people for their 'insightful' suggestions on the ongoing Jantar Mantar protest. The reel is the latest development in the students' stir, which has been gaining momentum over the past few days.

The protest has been led by the students, who have been demanding certain changes from the government. The Centre has been engaging in talks with the students, with the third round of talks scheduled to take place tomorrow.

CJP's Demands

The Chief Justice of India (CJP) has given a 48-hour deadline for the government to meet the demands of the students. The CJP has stated that the demands are non-negotiable and that nothing less will work.

The CJP had met with ministers, including Dharmendra Pradhan, to discuss the demands of the students. However, the meeting did not yield any results, with the CJP reiterating the deadline for the government to act.
