Jana Nayagan Box Office Day 18: Vijay Film Sees 41.2% Jump, Adds Rs 3.60 Crore

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Day 18 collections jump 41.2% to Rs 3.60 crore
  • Film nears Suriya's 'Karuppu' record, with Rs 320 crore total
  • Vijay-Pooja Hegde starrer leads over 'Karuppu' in recent days

Vijay's 'Jana Nayagan' Surges on Day 18

Thalapathy Vijay's action-drama 'Jana Nayagan' continues its robust run at the box office, recording a significant spike on its 18th day. The film added Rs 3.60 crore on Monday, a jump of 41.2% from the previous day's collections. The surge suggests strong word-of-mouth and steady footfalls even in its third week, a rarity for big-ticket releases.

The film, co-starring Pooja Hegde, has now crossed the Rs 320 crore mark worldwide. Trade analysts point out that the movie is just Rs 2.11 crore away from surpassing Suriya's 'Karuppu' to become the highest-grossing Tamil film of 2026. The milestone, if achieved, would mark a significant feather in Vijay's cap, especially as he transitions away from active politics-tinged roles.

How the Numbers Stack Up

Day 18's collection of Rs 3.60 crore is notable for a film that has been in theatres for nearly three weeks. The 41.2% jump indicates that weekend momentum has spilled into the week, with family audiences and repeat viewers contributing to the tally. In comparison, 'Karuppu' had a stronger opening but has seen a steeper decline in recent days.

Industry insiders note that 'Jana Nayagan' benefits from a festive period and limited competition from new releases. The film's Telugu and Hindi dubbed versions have also added to its overall numbers, though the Tamil version remains the primary driver.

Race Against 'Karuppu' Intensifies

The box office battle between 'Jana Nayagan' and 'Karuppu' has been closely watched. While 'Karuppu' led initially, 'Jana Nayagan' has steadily closed the gap. On day 19, reports suggest the Vijay-starrer has taken a clear lead, with early estimates showing it ahead by a small margin. The final verdict on which film ends up as the year's top grosser will depend on the coming weekend's performance.

For Vijay, who has a massive fan following across Tamil Nadu and beyond, the film's success reinforces his box office pull. For Pooja Hegde, the film adds another hit to her filmography, though her role has been more limited in screen time.

What's Next for the Film

With the Rs 320 crore mark crossed, the film's team is now eyeing the Rs 350 crore milestone. The next few days, including the upcoming weekend, will be crucial. If the trend continues, 'Jana Nayagan' could end its theatrical run as one of the highest-grossing Tamil films ever.

Officials from the production house have not yet confirmed any plans for an extended cut or a digital release date. Typically, big hits stream on OTT platforms after 45-50 days, so a mid-September premiere is possible.

All eyes are now on the day 19 and 20 numbers to see if the 41.2% jump is a one-off or the beginning of a sustained second wind.
