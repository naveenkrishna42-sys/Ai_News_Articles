J&K power tariffs hiked by 7%

📅 2026-08-23 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • 7% hike in electricity tariffs approved by J&K government
  • Political parties and public express outrage over tariff increase
  • New rates to apply from September 1, 2026
  • AIP leader calls tariff hike unjustified, seeks relief

Government Approves 7% Hike in Electricity Tariffs

The Jammu & Kashmir government has approved a 7% increase in electricity tariffs, effective from September 1, 2026. The decision has sparked widespread outrage among consumers and political parties in the region.

Public and Political Reaction

Residents across Jammu & Kashmir have expressed anger over the tariff hike, with many calling it unjustified and a burden on their already strained finances. Political parties in the region, including the AIP leader, have called for relief measures, citing the economic challenges faced by the population.

Rising Kashmir Parties Oppose Hike

Several political parties in Jammu & Kashmir have come together to oppose the tariff increase. They argue that the hike will exacerbate the financial difficulties faced by the people and hinder economic growth in the region.

AIP Leader Seeks Relief

The leader of the AIP has called the tariff hike unjustified and has sought relief measures from the government. He has emphasized the need for a balanced approach to ensure that the increase does not disproportionately affect the common people.

What Happens Next?

As the new tariffs come into effect, the government will need to address the concerns raised by the public and political parties. It is crucial that relief measures are implemented to mitigate the impact of the hike on consumers. The government should also engage in dialogue with stakeholders to find a sustainable solution that benefits both the consumers and the region's economy.
