Ram Charan visits Coimbatore temple with wife Upasana after wrist surgery

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ram Charan underwent successful wrist surgery in Coimbatore after an injury on the sets of 'Peddi'.
  • He visited a temple with wife Upasana Konidela following the operation.
  • Doctors have advised rehabilitation before he returns to work.
  • The actor has since returned to Hyderabad.

Telugu cinema star Ram Charan stepped out for a spiritual visit with his wife Upasana Konidela in Coimbatore, days after undergoing wrist surgery. The actor had sustained an injury while filming for his upcoming project, tentatively titled 'Peddi'.

Photographs from the temple visit show the couple at a local Ayyappa shrine. Charan's wrist was seen bandaged, a visible reminder of the recent procedure. The visit marked his first public appearance since the operation.

Surgery and recovery

Charan was admitted to a private hospital in Coimbatore for the surgery last week. Medical sources confirmed the procedure was successful. The injury occurred during an action sequence on the sets of 'Peddi', which is being directed by Buchi Babu Sana.

Doctors have advised the actor to undergo a period of rehabilitation before resuming shoot schedules. Production on 'Peddi' has been adjusted to accommodate his recovery timeline.

Temple visit with Upasana

The couple's visit to the Ayyappa temple in Coimbatore was brief but significant. Upasana, who has been by Charan's side throughout the hospital stay, was seen in traditional attire. The actor offered prayers seeking a speedy recovery and strength for the coming months.

Fans and well-wishers greeted the pair outside the temple. Security personnel maintained a tight cordon to allow the family to complete their darshan without disruption.

Return to Hyderabad

After a few days of post-operative observation in Coimbatore, Ram Charan returned to Hyderabad earlier this week. He is expected to continue his rehabilitation under the supervision of his medical team at home.

The actor's team has not released an official statement regarding a revised shooting schedule. Industry insiders suggest that the production unit is exploring options to film scenes that do not require Charan's physical involvement until he recovers fully.

'Peddi' is one of the most anticipated Telugu releases of the year. The film also stars Janhvi Kapoor and features music by A.R. Rahman. With Charan's injury, the team is proceeding cautiously to ensure the actor's health remains the priority.

Charan's last outing, 'Game Changer', released earlier this year and performed well at the box office. Expectations for 'Peddi' are high, given the combination of the star with director Buchi Babu Sana, who previously delivered the hit 'Uppena'.

As the actor focuses on recovery, fans are keeping a close watch on updates. The next few weeks will be critical in determining when the star can return to full-fledged action on set.
