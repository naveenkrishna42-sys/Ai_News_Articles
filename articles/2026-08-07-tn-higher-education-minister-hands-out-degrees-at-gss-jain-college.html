TN Higher Education Minister hands out degrees at GSS Jain College

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Minister distributed degree certificates to graduating students at GSS Jain College
  • Emphasised the role of education in nation-building and skill development
  • Event saw participation of college management, faculty, and students

Chennai: The Higher Education Minister on Wednesday presented degree certificates to graduating students at GSS Jain College, marking a significant milestone for the institution's latest batch of alumni. The ceremony, held on the college campus, saw the Minister personally hand over certificates to students across various disciplines, underscoring the government's commitment to recognising academic achievement.

Minister's address to graduates

In his address, the Minister congratulated the students on their hard work and perseverance, noting that their accomplishments were a testament to their dedication. He urged the graduates to carry forward the values of knowledge and integrity as they step into the professional world, emphasising that education is not merely a pathway to employment but a foundation for responsible citizenship.

The Minister also highlighted the state government's ongoing efforts to enhance higher education infrastructure and promote skill-based learning. He called upon the students to actively contribute to the nation's growth, particularly in sectors such as technology, research, and social development, where the demand for skilled professionals continues to rise.

Institution's role in shaping careers

GSS Jain College, known for its focus on holistic education, has consistently produced graduates who excel in various fields. The institution's management, faculty, and staff were present at the ceremony, taking pride in the achievements of their students. College authorities expressed gratitude to the Minister for taking time to grace the occasion, describing it as an inspiration for the students.

The event also featured cultural performances and a formal procession of graduates, adding a festive touch to the proceedings. Parents and family members of the graduates attended the ceremony, cheering on their loved ones as they received their certificates.

Focus on skill development

During his speech, the Minister reiterated the importance of aligning academic curricula with industry requirements. He noted that the state is working towards creating an ecosystem where students are not only academically proficient but also equipped with practical skills. This, he said, would enable them to compete globally and contribute meaningfully to the economy.

The Minister's remarks come at a time when higher education institutions across the country are re-evaluating their teaching methods to better prepare students for a rapidly changing job market. His emphasis on skill development resonated with the audience, many of whom are preparing to enter competitive sectors.

A moment of pride for the college

For GSS Jain College, the ceremony was a moment of collective pride. The institution has been steadily growing its reputation for academic excellence and community engagement. The presence of a state minister at the convocation is seen as a recognition of the college's contributions to the educational landscape in Chennai.

Students who received their degrees expressed mixed feelings of joy and nostalgia, with many looking forward to the next chapter of their lives. Some shared plans to pursue higher studies, while others are set to begin their professional careers in various organisations.

As the ceremony concluded, the Minister posed for photographs with the graduates and their families, adding a personal touch to the event. The college management announced that it would continue to support its alumni through mentorship programmes and placement assistance.

Moving forward, the focus will be on how these graduates leverage their education to make a difference in society, and how institutions like GSS Jain College adapt to the evolving demands of higher education.
