OpenAI cuts AI access for Cursor, deepening Musk-Altman feud

📅 2026-08-29 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • OpenAI to cut off AI models for SpaceX-owned Cursor
  • Escalating feud between Musk and Altman
  • Impact on AI model availability and competition

OpenAI's Decision to Disconnect Cursor

OpenAI has announced it will cut off AI models for Cursor, a coding tool owned by SpaceX. This decision marks a significant escalation in the ongoing feud between OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman, and Elon Musk, the CEO of SpaceX and Tesla. The move comes after OpenAI acquired Cursor in a bid to compete with Musk's ventures.

Background of the Feud

The rivalry between Altman and Musk has been brewing for some time. It began with Altman's decision to step down as CEO of OpenAI, a position he had held since the organization's inception. Musk, who was a co-founder of OpenAI, saw this as a betrayal and has been critical of Altman's leadership ever since.

Impact on AI Model Availability

The decision to disconnect Cursor raises concerns about the availability of AI models for developers. Cursor is a popular coding tool, and its users will now have to look for alternative solutions. This could potentially slow down the development of AI-powered applications, impacting the broader tech industry.

Broader Implications

The feud between Altman and Musk is not just about OpenAI and SpaceX. It has broader implications for the AI industry. OpenAI is a pioneer in the field, and its decisions can significantly impact the development and adoption of AI technologies. The disconnect with Cursor could potentially open up opportunities for other AI companies to fill the void left by OpenAI.

Expert Opinion

Michael Truell, who was named one of the 100 Most Influential People in AI 2026 by Time Magazine, has commented on the situation. He believes that the feud between Altman and Musk is a sign of the growing competition in the AI industry. He also believes that the disconnect with Cursor could potentially lead to a more fragmented AI market, with different companies offering different solutions.

Key Points

  • OpenAI's decision to disconnect Cursor is a significant escalation in the feud between Altman and Musk.
  • The move could potentially slow down the development of AI-powered applications.
  • The feud has broader implications for the AI industry, potentially leading to a more fragmented market.
