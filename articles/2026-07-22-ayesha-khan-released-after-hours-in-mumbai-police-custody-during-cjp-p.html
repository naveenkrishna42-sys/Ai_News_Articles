Ayesha Khan released after hours in Mumbai Police custody during CJP protest

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Actor Ayesha Khan was detained by Mumbai Police during a protest related to the Common University Entrance Test (CJP).
  • She was held for several hours before being released, and described the experience as traumatic.
  • Fellow actor Uorfi Javed claimed she narrowly avoided detention, stating people want to protest peacefully.

Actor Ayesha Khan, known for her role in the film Dhurandhar, was released late Wednesday evening after spending several hours in Mumbai Police custody. She was detained earlier in the day while participating in a protest related to the Common University Entrance Test (CJP).

The protest, which saw a large turnout of students and young actors, has been ongoing across several cities. Khan's detention drew immediate attention on social media, with fans and colleagues expressing concern.

Detention and release

Police officials confirmed that Khan was taken into custody from the protest site in the Bandra area around noon. She was held at a local police station for questioning before being released without any charges being filed.

In a brief statement after her release, Khan said, "My hands are literally shivering. I was kept for hours without being told why. It was a frightening experience." She added that she was simply exercising her right to peaceful protest.

Uorfi Javed's narrow escape

Fellow actor Uorfi Javed, who was also present at the protest, claimed she nearly got detained by the police. In a social media post, she wrote, "Log peaceful protest karna chah rahe hain..." (People want to protest peacefully).

Javed said she managed to leave the area just before the police began making arrests. Her post has garnered thousands of reactions, with many users questioning the police's handling of the demonstration.

Broader context of the protests

The CJP protests have been a recurring flashpoint in Indian cities over the past few weeks. Students and activists are demanding changes to the exam's format and alleged irregularities in its conduct.

Several prominent figures from the entertainment industry have voiced support for the protesters. Actor Vijay, who released a statement earlier on Wednesday, called for a transparent investigation into the matter. His comments have added to the pressure on authorities.

Neither the Mumbai Police nor the state government has issued an official statement regarding the detention of Khan or other protesters. The lack of clarity has fueled further speculation online.

As of Wednesday evening, no formal complaint has been filed by either side. Khan has not indicated whether she plans to take legal action against the police.

What happens next remains uncertain. The protest movement shows no signs of abating, and more celebrities may join the fray. All eyes are now on the government's response to the growing chorus of dissent.
