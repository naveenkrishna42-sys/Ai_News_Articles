Hyderabad faces 24-hour water cut on July 27

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Water supply in Hyderabad will be disrupted for 24 hours on July 27.
  • The Hyderabad Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board (HMWSSB) announced the disruption.
  • The cut is due to maintenance work on a key pipeline at the Krishna Drinking Water Supply Scheme.
  • All zones under the Krishna scheme, including parts of the old city and new areas, will be affected.
  • Residents are advised to store sufficient water in advance.

Hyderabad residents should brace for a 24-hour water supply disruption on Monday, July 27. The Hyderabad Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board (HMWSSB) announced the scheduled cut, citing essential maintenance work on a major pipeline at the Krishna Drinking Water Supply Scheme.

Which areas will be affected?

The disruption will impact all zones receiving water from the Krishna scheme. This covers large parts of the city, including areas in the old city and newer suburbs. Officials have not released a full list of specific localities but said the cut will be widespread.

The HMWSSB clarified that the maintenance is critical to ensure the long-term reliability of the supply system. The work will involve repairs and upgrades to a key section of the pipeline network.

What residents should do

The board has urged residents to store sufficient water for their needs before the disruption begins. Tanker services will be available for essential services like hospitals, but the general public is advised to make advance arrangements.

In a statement, the HMWSSB said it will try to restore supply as soon as the work is completed. However, they cautioned that restoration in some high-lying areas might take additional time due to the nature of the network.

Frequency of such cuts

Water supply disruptions in Hyderabad often occur due to maintenance or emergency repairs. The Krishna scheme is a vital source, and such periodic work is considered necessary to prevent larger failures. The board did not specify if similar cuts are planned in the coming weeks.

Residents in affected zones have been advised to check the HMWSSB's official channels for updates. The board has not yet confirmed if the schedule will change based on the progress of the work.

Officials expect the maintenance to be completed within the 24-hour window, and normal supply should resume by Tuesday morning.
