Sachin Tendulkar Fielded for Pakistan Before India Debut

A Teenage Tendulkar in Pakistan Colours

Long before he tormented Pakistani bowlers with his straight drives, a 14-year-old Sachin Tendulkar was on the field wearing their colours. In 1987, two years before his iconic India debut, Tendulkar was a ball boy during a Test match in Karachi. It was a moment that has since become a fascinating footnote in cricket history.

India was touring Pakistan, and young Sachin, who was already making waves in Mumbai's cricketing circles, was part of the support staff for the match. He was there to fetch balls, not to face them. But for a teenager who dreamt of playing for India, being on the same ground as legends like Imran Khan and Sunil Gavaskar was a dream in itself.

The Irony of the Rivalry

Given the intense rivalry that would define his career, the image of Tendulkar in a Pakistan jersey is almost surreal. The same player who would later become the highest run-scorer in Tests and ODIs, and who would be the backbone of India's batting against Pakistan, was once a ball boy for the opposition. It was a role reversal that even Tendulkar himself has acknowledged with a smile.

This incident, revealed in various interviews and biographies, offers a glimpse into his humility. He was not a prodigy who was thrust into the limelight; he was a hardworking kid who was willing to do the grunt work to be close to the game he loved. That Karachi Test was part of his education, a stepping stone to greatness.

From Ball Boy to Batsman

Within two years, Tendulkar would make his Test debut against Pakistan in 1989, at the age of 16. The boy who once fetched balls for them was now facing them with a bat in hand. The transition was swift, but the foundation was laid in those early years, including that stint in Karachi.

The story also resonates with fans because it underscores how far he came. From a ball boy to a legend, Tendulkar's journey is a testament to his dedication. In a sport where rivalries are fierce, this anecdote humanises the players and reminds us that even legends have humble beginnings.

A Rare Anecdote in Cricket Lore

Such anecdotes are rare in cricket, where the focus is usually on statistics and match-winning performances. Looking Ahead

As cricket continues to evolve, stories like this one keep the spirit of the game alive. They remind us of the journey, not just the destination. Tendulkar's rise from that Karachi ground to the pinnacle of cricket remains an inspiration, and this anecdote will likely be retold for generations.
