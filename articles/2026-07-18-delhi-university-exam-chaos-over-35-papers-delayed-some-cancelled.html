Delhi University Exam Chaos: Over 35 Papers Delayed, Some Cancelled

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • Over 35 examination papers at Delhi University have been delayed or cancelled
  • Students report confusion and frustration over last-minute schedule changes
  • University officials cite administrative issues but details remain unclear

Delhi University is grappling with a major examination crisis. Over 35 papers have been delayed and some have been cancelled outright, leaving thousands of students in limbo. The chaos has sparked widespread frustration across the campus, with many demanding immediate answers from the administration.

What Triggered the Disruption

The trouble began earlier this week when the university released a revised exam timetable. Students who had prepared for their exams found the schedule changed at the last minute. Reports from several colleges indicate that some papers were postponed without prior notice.

In at least a handful of cases, exams were cancelled just hours before they were scheduled to begin. This has disrupted the academic calendar and left students unsure about when they will be tested. The university has not yet provided a detailed explanation for the abrupt changes.

Student Anger and Confusion

Students across multiple faculties have expressed their anger. Many took to social media to vent their frustration, sharing screenshots of the revised timetables and cancelled exams. Some reported traveling long distances to campus only to find the exam hall empty.

"We were told the paper was postponed just 12 hours before the exam," said a student from the School of Open Learning, speaking on condition of anonymity. "I had studied all night. Now I don't know when it will happen." Another student from North Campus said the uncertainty is affecting mental health and preparations for other papers.

Administration's Response

University officials have acknowledged the delays but have been vague about the reasons. A spokesperson said the university is "reviewing the schedule" and that affected papers will be rescheduled shortly. No specific timeline has been given for when the exams will be held.

Sources within the administration suggest that logistical issues and shortage of staff may have contributed to the problem. However, officials have not confirmed these details. The university has promised to issue a fresh timetable in the coming days.

Impact on Students' Academic Plans

The delays are causing more than just inconvenience. Many students are worried about the impact on their overall academic performance. Some are scheduled to graduate this semester and fear that the delays could affect their degree completion.

For students applying for higher studies or jobs, the uncertainty around exam dates is a significant setback. The university has not indicated whether it will offer any compensation or alternative arrangements for those affected.

What Happens Next

The university is expected to release a revised schedule within this week. Students are advised to check their college notice boards and the official DU website for updates. For now, the administration faces mounting pressure to resolve the crisis and restore normalcy. The coming days will reveal whether the university can bring order to the chaos or if more disruptions lie ahead.
