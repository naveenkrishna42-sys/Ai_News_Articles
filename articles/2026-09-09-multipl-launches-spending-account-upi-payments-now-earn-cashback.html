What is Multipl’s Spending Account?

Multipl, the fintech that pioneered instant credit‑card approvals, has expanded its product suite with a ‘Spending Account’. The offering works like a hybrid between a traditional credit card and a digital wallet, allowing users to make UPI payments while earning cash‑back rewards.

How the Cashback Engine Works

When a user links their bank‑linked UPI ID to the Spending Account, every transaction is routed through Multipl’s backend. The platform applies a tiered cash‑back rate based on merchant category and spend volume. For example, grocery purchases at partnered supermarkets earn 5% back, while utility bill payments fetch 2%.

Key Mechanics

  • Cash‑back is credited instantly to the account’s balance, not as points.
  • Users can withdraw the balance to any linked bank account or use it for future UPI payments.
  • No credit‑check is required; the account operates on a spend‑and‑pay‑later model with a 30‑day grace period.

Real‑World Value for Indian Consumers

India’s UPI ecosystem processes over 10 billion transactions a month, yet most reward programs are tied to card usage. Multipl’s move bridges that gap, giving everyday spenders—who often prefer UPI over cards—a direct incentive.

For a typical household spending ₹25,000 per month on groceries, utilities, and transport, the potential cash‑back could range between ₹500 and ₹1,250, effectively reducing the net outflow by 2‑5%.

Pricing, Fees, and Transparency

Multipl charges a modest ₹99 monthly service fee after the introductory free period. Late‑payment penalties are capped at 2.5% per month, aligning with RBI guidelines for non‑card credit products.

The platform also offers a premium tier—Spending Plus—for ₹499 per month, unlocking higher cash‑back caps (up to ₹5,000) and exclusive merchant partnerships.

Pros & Cons

  • Pros:
    • Instant cash‑back on UPI, eliminating point conversion delays.
    • No credit‑score impact for account activation.
    • Zero annual fee for the base tier.
  • Cons:
    • Cash‑back caps may limit high‑spend users.
    • Monthly service fee after the first year.
    • Limited merchant network for the highest‑rate categories.

Bottom Line for Buyers

Multipl’s Spending Account is a compelling addition for Indian consumers who live on UPI but crave reward incentives. The zero‑fee entry point and transparent cash‑back structure make it a low‑risk experiment, while the premium tier offers a clear upgrade path for power users.

Buyers should evaluate their monthly spend patterns: if most purchases fall within the partnered categories, the account can easily offset the ₹99 service charge. Otherwise, the free tier still provides a modest return without any long‑term commitment.
