Hyderabad Traffic Police announce Bonalu procession diversions

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bonalu processions prompt traffic diversions in Hyderabad
  • Police announce alternate routes for commuters
  • Advisory issued to ease congestion during festival

The Hyderabad Traffic Police have announced a series of traffic diversions across the city to facilitate the smooth conduct of Bonalu processions. The festival, marked by vibrant religious processions, draws thousands of devotees to temples, particularly in the Old City and Secunderabad.

In an official advisory, the police have identified key routes that will be closed or altered during the procession hours. Commuters have been urged to cooperate and plan their journeys in advance to avoid inconvenience.

Key diversions and affected routes

According to the advisory, several major roads and intersections will be barricaded to allow the processions to pass without disruption. The affected stretches include areas around prominent temples where Bonalu celebrations are concentrated.

Traffic will be regulated at these points, and vehicles will be redirected to alternate routes. The police have also arranged for additional personnel to manage the expected surge in pedestrian and vehicular movement.

Alternate routes for commuters

Motorists have been advised to use designated alternate corridors to reach their destinations. Specific diversions have been planned for heavy vehicles, which will be restricted from entering the core procession areas.

Local residents and shopkeepers along the procession routes have been asked to cooperate with traffic personnel and follow the guidelines issued by the police.

Festival background and scale

Bonalu is an annual festival dedicated to Goddess Mahakali, celebrated with fervour in Hyderabad and other parts of Telangana. The processions involve large gatherings, traditional music, and offerings, making crowd management a priority for the authorities.

This year, the celebrations are expected to attract significant participation, prompting the police to issue the diversions well in advance. The advisory aims to minimise disruption while ensuring the safety of all attendees.

Public advisory and emergency contacts

The Hyderabad Traffic Police have urged commuters to follow the instructions of traffic personnel at all times. They have also advised against using mobile phones while driving and to maintain patience during the diversions.

For real-time updates, the police have directed citizens to their official social media handles. Emergency contact numbers have been shared for those requiring assistance during the processions.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact duration of the diversions, but they are expected to remain in place until the processions conclude. Commuters are encouraged to check local updates before stepping out.

The diversions come as part of broader efforts to balance religious traditions with urban mobility. As the festival progresses, the police will assess the situation and make adjustments if necessary.

Looking ahead, similar advisories are likely for the remaining days of Bonalu, and the public is advised to stay informed through official channels.
