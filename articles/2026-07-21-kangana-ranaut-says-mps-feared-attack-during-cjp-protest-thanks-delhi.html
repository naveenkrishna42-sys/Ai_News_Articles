Kangana Ranaut says MPs feared attack during CJP protest, thanks Delhi Police

📅 2026-07-21 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kangana Ranaut claimed MPs feared being attacked by protesters from the CJP march near Parliament.
  • She thanked the Delhi Police for providing security and shielding lawmakers.
  • Separately, Hema Malini dismissed the protest as ineffective and refused to comment on a resignation demand.

Kangana describes fear inside Parliament

BJP MP Kangana Ranaut has said that lawmakers inside Parliament were scared of a possible attack from protesters during the recent CJP march. Speaking to reporters, she claimed the mood was tense. "Shayad attack kar denge," she said, describing the fear that many MPs felt.

She thanked the Delhi Police for their swift action. According to her, the force formed a protective shield around the Parliament building. This prevented what she called a potential mob attack.

Police response gets praise

Ranaut specifically lauded the Delhi Police for standing firm. She said their presence ensured that no MP was harmed. The actor-turned-politician emphasised that the police acted as a barrier between the protesters and the lawmakers inside.

Her comments come amid heightened security in the national capital. The CJP protest had drawn thousands to the streets near Parliament. Authorities had deployed additional forces to maintain order.

Hema Malini offers a different take

Fellow BJP MP Hema Malini struck a different note. She dismissed the protest as futile. "Protest karne se toh kuch kaam nahi hoga," she said, implying that demonstrations rarely yield results.

When asked about the demand for Union Minister Dharmendra Pradhan's resignation, she refused to comment. Malini did not elaborate on the reasons for her silence. Her remarks stand in contrast to Ranaut's more vocal stance on the protest.

Context of the CJP protest

The CJP march was organised to press for specific demands. While the exact nature of the protest has been reported, the details remain contested. Supporters claim it was a peaceful assembly, while critics point to instances of disruption.

Security agencies had been on alert for days. The protest coincided with a Parliament session, which added to the tension. Lawmakers from various parties have since commented on the events, with some criticising the government's handling of the situation.

What comes next

The Delhi Police are expected to review the security arrangements for future protests. Meanwhile, the CJP has indicated that more demonstrations may follow. The government is yet to issue an official statement on the demands raised during the march.
