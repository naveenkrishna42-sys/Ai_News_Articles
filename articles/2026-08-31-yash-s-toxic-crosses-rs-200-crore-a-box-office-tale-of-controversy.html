Yash's 'Toxic' crosses Rs 200 crore mark in India

Yash's latest film, 'Toxic', starring Nayanthara and Kiara Advani, has crossed the Rs 200 crore mark at the Indian box office. The film, a crime thriller, has been a subject of controversy due to its content and portrayal of women. Despite the backlash, the film has managed to attract a significant audience, contributing to its box office success.

Box office success amid a trend of downfall

The box office success of 'Toxic' is significant, especially considering the trend of downfall in Yash's career. The film has managed to cross the Rs 200 crore mark, which is a testament to the power of Yash's star power and the film's marketing strategy. However, the film's success is also a subject of debate, with many questioning the impact of Yash's star power on the film's box office performance.

Controversy over the film's content and portrayal of women

The film 'Toxic' has been a subject of controversy due to its content and portrayal of women. The film's storyline and the way it portrays women have been criticized by many, with some calling it 'toxic' and 'misogynistic'. Despite the backlash, the film has managed to attract a significant audience, contributing to its box office success. The controversy surrounding the film has also sparked a debate on the representation of women in the Indian film industry.

Rishab Shetty's praise for Yash

Director Rishab Shetty has praised Yash for his performance in 'Toxic', calling it 'truly impressive'. Shetty's praise for Yash is significant, especially considering the controversy surrounding the film. Shetty's support for Yash can be seen as a vote of confidence in Yash's acting skills and his ability to deliver a powerful performance despite the film's contentious nature.

Box office collection timeline
