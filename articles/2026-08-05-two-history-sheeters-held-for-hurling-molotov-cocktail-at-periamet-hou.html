Two history-sheeters held for hurling molotov cocktail at Periamet house

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Two history-sheeters arrested for throwing a molotov cocktail at a house in Periamet, Chennai.
  • The incident occurred recently; police have launched an investigation.
  • No injuries or damage reported; motive yet to be established.

Arrests in Periamet attack

Chennai police have arrested two history-sheeters in connection with a late-night attack on a house in Periamet, where a molotov cocktail was thrown at the premises. The accused, whose names have not been officially released, were taken into custody following a swift investigation by the local police.

The incident, which took place under the jurisdiction of the Periamet police station, came to light after residents reported hearing a loud explosion and seeing flames near the entrance of the house. Police personnel, who arrived at the spot, found remnants of a glass bottle and burnt debris, confirming the use of an incendiary device.

Police probe underway

According to a senior police officer, the accused have a prior criminal record, with multiple cases registered against them at various police stations in the city. The officer said that the duo was arrested based on technical surveillance and intelligence inputs, and that they were being questioned to ascertain the motive behind the attack.

Preliminary inquiries suggest that the attack may be linked to a personal feud or an extortion attempt, though the police have not confirmed any specific reason. Officials have not yet established whether the house was targeted specifically or if the accused mistook the location.

No injuries reported

Fortunately, no injuries were reported in the incident, as the occupants of the house were present at the time but managed to escape unharmed. The fire caused by the molotov cocktail was quickly doused by locals, preventing any significant damage to property. Police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including attempt to murder and causing mischief by fire or explosive substance.

The accused have been remanded in judicial custody and are currently lodged at the Chennai Central Prison. Further interrogation is underway to identify any other individuals involved in the planning or execution of the attack.

History-sheeter crackdown

The arrests come as part of a broader crackdown by the Greater Chennai Police on habitual offenders in the city. In recent months, the police have intensified surveillance on history-sheeters, who are individuals with a documented history of criminal activity, often involved in violent crimes. This incident highlights the ongoing efforts to curb such activities in densely populated areas like Periamet, a bustling neighbourhood known for its textile markets and residential blocks.

Local residents have expressed relief at the swift action taken by the police, though some have called for increased patrolling in the area to prevent such incidents in the future. The police have assured the public that they are taking all necessary measures to ensure safety and order.

As the investigation continues, the focus remains on unravelling the exact circumstances that led to the attack. Whether this was an isolated incident or part of a larger pattern will become clearer as more details emerge from the interrogation of the accused.

The case is expected to be heard in a Chennai court later this week, where the police will seek further remand or chargesheet based on evidence collected. In the meantime, the authorities have urged citizens to report any suspicious activity to the nearest police station, emphasising the importance of community vigilance in maintaining law and order.
