Producer Ashwin Varde accuses Paresh Rawal of trying to steal OMG 2 from Akshay Kumar

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Producer Ashwin Varde accuses Paresh Rawal of being 'unprofessional'.
  • Varde claims Rawal tried to 'steal' OMG 2 from Akshay Kumar.
  • The dispute centres on who originated the film's concept.

War of words erupts over OMG 2 credit

The simmering dispute over who deserves credit for the 2023 hit OMG 2 has exploded into a public spat. Producer Ashwin Varde has fired back at veteran actor Paresh Rawal, accusing him of being 'unprofessional' and attempting to hijack the project from its star, Akshay Kumar.

Varde's sharp retort comes days after Rawal claimed the film was his original idea. The actor had alleged that Kumar and director Amit Rai did not give him his due credit for the concept.

Varde calls Rawal's claims 'shocking'

In a statement to the press, Varde did not mince words. He said Rawal's version of events was 'shocking' and accused the actor of trying to rewrite history. According to Varde, Rawal's behaviour on the project was far from collaborative.

“Paresh Rawal tried to steal OMG 2 from Akshay Kumar. He was unprofessional,” Varde alleged. The producer did not provide specific evidence but insisted that the film's success belonged squarely to Kumar and the director.

Rawal's original claim sparks controversy

The controversy began when Rawal, in a recent interview, stated that the concept for OMG 2 was his brainchild. He claimed he had developed the idea and brought it to Kumar, only to be sidelined when the film went into production.

Rawal's remarks triggered a wave of speculation in Bollywood circles. Some industry insiders backed the actor, while others questioned his narrative. The film's director, Amit Rai, also responded, backing Varde's version of events.

Rai said Rawal's claims were baseless and that the film's story was a collaborative effort. He added that the actor's contribution, while valuable, did not amount to authorship of the project.

What really happened behind the scenes?

Sources close to the production paint a messy picture. They say Rawal was initially enthusiastic about the film but later grew unhappy with his role. Tensions reportedly escalated when the script was finalised without his input.

Varde's accusation that Rawal tried to 'steal' the film suggests a deeper rift. The producer implied that the actor attempted to take control of the project, potentially sidelining Kumar and Rai. Neither Rawal nor his representatives have responded to the latest allegations.

The public feud is a rare glimpse into the behind-the-scenes power struggles that often shape big-budget Bollywood films. OMG 2, a sequel to the 2012 hit OMG – Oh My God!, was a commercial success, grossing over Rs 200 crore worldwide. Its themes of faith and rationalism struck a chord with audiences.

What happens next?

The ball is now in Paresh Rawal's court. He has not yet commented on Varde's counterattack. Industry watchers expect the actor to either issue a clarification or let the matter drop. For now, the credit for OMG 2 remains a contested subject in Bollywood.
