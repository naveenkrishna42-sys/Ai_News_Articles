US strikes southern Iran, Tehran retaliates against Gulf nations: Latest updates

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • US airstrikes hit civilian infrastructure in southern Iran, including bridges, airport, and railway station.
  • Iran retaliates by targeting Gulf countries, demonstrating capability despite ongoing US attacks.
  • India confirms Shahid Beheshti terminal at Chabahar port remains undamaged after strikes.
  • Chabahar port appears to have sustained damage from the US strikes.
  • The situation remains fluid with no official confirmation of casualties or full extent of damage.

US airstrikes target southern Iran

The United States launched a series of airstrikes on southern Iran on Friday, targeting civilian infrastructure in a significant escalation of hostilities. Reports from the region indicate that bridges, an airport, and a railway station were among the sites hit.

The strikes appear to have damaged Chabahar port, a key strategic asset on Iran's southeastern coast. The port is a critical hub for regional trade, including for India's connectivity projects with Afghanistan and Central Asia. The full extent of the damage is still being assessed.

Iran retaliates against Gulf countries

In response, Iran has struck targets in Gulf countries, demonstrating its ability to project power despite the sustained US assault. The nature of the Iranian retaliation remains unclear, but initial reports suggest it has caused disruptions across the region.

Analysts note that Tehran's ability to inflict damage on neighbouring states, even under heavy attack, underscores the resilience of its military capabilities. The Gulf states affected have not yet issued official statements on the scale of the damage or casualties.

Chabahar port: India's assessment

India has confirmed that the Shahid Beheshti terminal at Chabahar port remains unscathed after the US strike. The terminal, operated by India Ports Global Limited, is a key component of India's strategic interests in the region, providing an alternative trade route to Afghanistan and Central Asia, bypassing Pakistan.

New Delhi has not yet commented on the broader implications of the attack on the port's operations. However, the statement suggests that Indian assets at the facility were not directly hit. The status of other parts of the port complex remains uncertain.

Broader implications and uncertainty

The US strikes on civilian infrastructure mark a new phase in the conflict, raising concerns about a wider regional war. The targeting of airports, bridges, and railway stations is likely to disrupt civilian life and supply chains in southern Iran.

International reactions have been muted so far, with no major powers calling for an immediate ceasefire. The United Nations has not yet convened an emergency session to address the escalating violence.

Officials have not confirmed casualty figures or the full list of damaged sites. Independent verification remains difficult due to the volatile security situation on the ground.

What to watch

The coming hours will be critical as both sides assess the damage and decide on next moves. The risk of further escalation remains high, with potential ripple effects across the Gulf and beyond. India's position on the Chabahar port's future will be closely watched, as will any response from Gulf states hit by Iranian retaliation.
