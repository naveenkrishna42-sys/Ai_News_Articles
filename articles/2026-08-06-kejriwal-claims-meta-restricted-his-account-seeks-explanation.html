Kejriwal claims Meta restricted his account; seeks explanation

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kejriwal claims his account was restricted without explanation.
  • He publicly questioned Meta's decision and timing.
  • He urged Meta not to succumb to political pressure.
  • Meta has not yet issued an official response.
  • The incident has sparked debate on platform neutrality.

Account restricted, no reasons given

Arvind Kejriwal, the former Chief Minister of Delhi and national convenor of the Aam Aadmi Party, has alleged that Meta restricted his social media account in India without providing any explanation. In a series of posts, Kejriwal claimed that the restriction came without prior notice or a clear rationale, leaving him and his team in the dark about what triggered the action.

The politician took to other platforms to voice his concern, saying, "No explanation given." He said the move was puzzling, especially since his account had been active and compliant with platform guidelines.

Questions for Meta

Kejriwal directly questioned Meta's decision-making process, asking why his account was singled out. He also raised the possibility of external pressure, urging the tech giant not to bow to the Prime Minister or any political authority.

"Don't bow down to the Prime Minister," he said, in a pointed appeal to the company's leadership. His remarks have intensified the ongoing debate about the neutrality of social media platforms in India, particularly when it comes to political figures from the opposition.

Political reactions and silence from Meta

While Kejriwal's statements have drawn reactions from his supporters and political allies, Meta has not yet issued a formal response. The company typically cites privacy and policy reasons for not commenting on individual account actions, but the lack of clarity has only fuelled speculation.

Some political observers have noted that this is not the first time a prominent opposition leader has alleged bias on social media platforms. However, Kejriwal's direct appeal to Meta's global leadership adds a new dimension to the ongoing tussle between technology companies and political entities in the country.

What this means for platform accountability

The incident raises fresh questions about how Meta enforces its community standards in India, a key market with over 300 million users. Critics argue that the lack of transparency in account restrictions can be weaponised to silence dissent, while supporters of the government have dismissed the allegations as politically motivated.

Kejriwal has not indicated whether he plans to take legal action or appeal the decision through Meta's internal review process. His office has said that they are exploring all options to seek a clear explanation.

What happens next

As the controversy gains traction, all eyes are on Meta's next move. If the company responds, it could set a precedent for how similar cases are handled in the future. For now, Kejriwal's followers and political rivals will be watching closely to see if the platform offers any clarity or sticks to its usual silence.
