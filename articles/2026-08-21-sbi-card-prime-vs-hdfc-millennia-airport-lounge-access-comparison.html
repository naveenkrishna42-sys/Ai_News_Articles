SBI Card Prime vs HDFC Millennia: Airport Lounge Access Comparison

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Credit Cards & Cashback Original source ↗
Key points
  • SBI Card Prime offers international lounge access via Priority Pass.
  • HDFC Millennia focuses on domestic cashback and convenience.
  • Compare annual fees and reward structures before applying.

Comparing Airport Lounge Access

Airport lounge access has become a primary driver for credit card selection among frequent travelers. Both the SBI Card Prime and HDFC Millennia offer distinct approaches to this perk. The SBI Card Prime is positioned as a travel-oriented premium card, whereas the HDFC Millennia targets the digital-savvy shopper who occasionally travels.

The SBI Card Prime provides four complimentary international lounge visits annually through a Priority Pass membership. Additionally, it offers eight complimentary domestic lounge visits each year. This makes it a stronger contender for those who travel abroad or frequent major airports within India.

Conversely, the HDFC Millennia card focuses on domestic travel. It provides eight complimentary domestic lounge visits per year, capped at two visits per quarter. If your travel is strictly limited to domestic flights within India, the HDFC Millennia covers your needs without the added cost of a premium travel card.

Rewards and Cashback Structures

Earning potential differs significantly between these two products. The HDFC Millennia is a cashback-heavy card designed for online shopping. It offers 5% cashback on top e-commerce platforms like Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra. Users receive 1% cashback on all other spends, excluding fuel, making it a reliable daily driver for retail expenses.

The SBI Card Prime operates on a reward point system rather than direct cashback. You earn 10 reward points per ₹100 spent on dining, groceries, and movies. For other retail purchases, the rate is 2 reward points per ₹100. These points can be redeemed against statement balances or for gifts, though the effective return rate depends on your redemption strategy.

Fees and Eligibility

Understanding the cost of ownership is vital. The SBI Card Prime carries an annual fee of ₹2,999 plus taxes. However, this fee is often waived if your annual spend exceeds ₹3 lakh in the previous year. It is best suited for individuals with a steady income who can meet this spending threshold to offset the fee.

The HDFC Millennia is more accessible, often issued as a First Year Free card or with a nominal annual fee of ₹1,000. This fee is waived if you spend ₹1 lakh or more in the preceding year. Eligibility for the Millennia is generally broader, requiring a lower minimum monthly income compared to the Prime variant.

Application and Final Verdict

You can apply for both cards through the official websites of SBI Card and HDFC Bank. Ensure you have your PAN card, Aadhaar number, and recent salary slips or income tax returns ready for digital verification. Using the bank’s mobile app or official portal often results in a faster approval process.

The SBI Card Prime is the superior choice for frequent flyers who value international lounge access and high rewards on dining and lifestyle spends. The HDFC Millennia is the practical choice for young professionals and digital shoppers who want consistent cashback on online purchases and sufficient domestic lounge access without a high annual fee.
