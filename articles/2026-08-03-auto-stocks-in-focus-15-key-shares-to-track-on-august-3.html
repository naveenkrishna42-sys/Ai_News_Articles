Auto Stocks Lead the Spotlight

Tata Motors: PV and TMCV in Focus

Tata Motors is drawing particular attention, with its passenger vehicle (PV) and Tata Motors Commercial Vehicles (TMCV) divisions being closely monitored. The company's recent sales trends and market share movements are likely to influence its stock price trajectory.

Analysts note that Tata Motors' performance in the domestic market has been a mixed bag, with PV demand showing resilience while commercial vehicles face headwinds. The stock's movement today could set the tone for the broader auto pack.

Maruti Suzuki and Mahindra & Mahindra

Maruti Suzuki, the country's largest carmaker, remains a bellwether for the sector. Its monthly sales numbers and any updates on new launches are expected to drive trading interest. Meanwhile, Mahindra & Mahindra's SUV lineup and tractor business are being watched for sustained growth signals.

Both companies have been aggressive in expanding their product portfolios, and any positive news could lift their shares. Market participants are also eyeing their export numbers, which have shown volatility in recent months.

Two-Wheeler Majors: TVS Motor and Bajaj Auto

TVS Motor and Bajaj Auto are the other key names on the list. The two-wheeler segment has been recovering steadily, and both firms are benefiting from improved rural demand. Bajaj Auto's export-heavy business model adds another layer of complexity, given global economic conditions.

TVS Motor, on its part, has been gaining traction in the premium motorcycle space. Investors will be looking for any commentary on electric vehicle plans, which remain a hot topic across the industry.
