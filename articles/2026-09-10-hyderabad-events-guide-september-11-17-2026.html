Hyderabad's September 2026 Event Calendar

Hyderabad, the vibrant capital of Telangana, is set to host a plethora of events between September 11 and September 17, 2026. These events span across various sectors, including culture, technology, and business, offering a rich tapestry of experiences for both locals and visitors.

Cultural Events

Cultural events form a significant part of Hyderabad's September calendar. The Hyderabad International Film Festival, scheduled from September 11 to September 15, is a highlight. This festival showcases a diverse range of films from around the world, providing a platform for filmmakers and audiences alike. The festival also includes masterclasses, panel discussions, and networking opportunities.

The Hyderabad Heritage Festival, held on September 16, is another cultural event worth mentioning. This festival celebrates Hyderabad's rich history and heritage. Visitors can explore the city's historical sites, attend cultural performances, and indulge in traditional cuisine. The festival also features workshops and exhibitions that delve into Hyderabad's cultural heritage.

Tech and Business Events

The Tech Summit 2026, scheduled for September 12-13, is a significant event for the tech and business community. This summit brings together industry leaders, startups, and investors to discuss the latest trends and innovations in the tech world. The event includes keynote speeches, panel discussions, and networking sessions.

The Hyderabad Business Forum, held on September 17, is another event that catters to the business community. This forum provides a platform for businesses to connect, collaborate, and explore new opportunities. The event includes workshops, seminars, and networking sessions.

Food and Lifestyle Events

The Food Festival, scheduled for September 14-15, is a celebration of Hyderabad's culinary delights. This festival offers a chance to indulge in a variety of cuisines from around the world. Visitors can enjoy food stalls, cooking demonstrations, and food workshops. The festival also features live music and cultural performances.

The Hyderabad Lifestyle Expo, held on September 16, is an event that caters to the lifestyle and wellness community. This expo showcases the latest trends and innovations in the lifestyle and wellness sector. Visitors can explore various stalls, attend workshops, and engage in activities that promote a healthy lifestyle.

Key Insights

  • Cultural Events: Hyderabad's cultural events offer a unique opportunity to explore the city's rich history and heritage. The Hyderabad International Film Festival and the Hyderabad Heritage Festival are must-attend events for culture enthusiasts.
  • Tech and Business Events: The Tech Summit 2026 and the Hyderabad Business Forum provide valuable networking opportunities for professionals in the tech and business sectors. These events are ideal for industry leaders, startups, and investors.
  • Food and Lifestyle Events: The Food Festival and the Hyderabad Lifestyle Expo cater to foodies and wellness enthusiasts. These events offer a chance to indulge in delicious cuisines, explore the latest trends in lifestyle and wellness, and engage in activities that promote a healthy lifestyle.
