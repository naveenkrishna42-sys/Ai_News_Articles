Ramayana Trailer to Release at Brahma Muhurat on July 30

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The 'Ramayana' trailer will be released on July 30 during Brahma Muhurat, a pre-dawn auspicious period.
  • Producer Namit Malhotra made the announcement, linking the timing to the epic's spiritual significance.
  • The trailer will also debut with screenings of 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' in theatres.
  • Fans have reacted with excitement on social media, praising the decision to choose an auspicious time.

Producer Namit Malhotra has announced that the much-anticipated trailer for the upcoming film 'Ramayana' will be released on July 30 during Brahma Muhurat. This pre-dawn period, which typically occurs about 90 minutes before sunrise, is considered one of the most spiritually charged times of the day in Hindu tradition.

The decision to drop the trailer at such an hour has sparked widespread interest. Malhotra shared the news on social media, stating that the timing was chosen to align with the epic's sacred themes. The film, which has been in production for several years, is expected to be a grand retelling of the ancient Sanskrit epic.

What is Brahma Muhurat?

Brahma Muhurat, also known as the "Creator's Hour," is a 48-minute period that begins roughly 1 hour and 36 minutes before sunrise. Hindu scriptures describe it as an ideal time for meditation, prayer, and starting new ventures. The air is said to be calm and charged with positive energy, making it a window for heightened spiritual awareness.

Astrologers and priests often recommend Brahma Muhurat for important life events. By choosing this window, the filmmakers are tapping into a deep cultural belief that actions performed during this hour carry special blessings. For a film based on Lord Ram's journey, the timing adds a layer of authenticity and reverence.

The Trailer's Theatrical Debut

According to reports, the 'Ramayana' trailer will not only be released online at that early hour but will also be screened in theatres. It is set to debut alongside the release of 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day', a move that ensures maximum audience reach. This dual-platform strategy suggests the producers are confident about creating a major event around the trailer launch.

Industry insiders note that attaching a trailer to a blockbuster Hollywood film is a tried-and-tested method. It guarantees that millions of viewers will see it on the big screen. The combination of a spiritual release time and a commercial theatrical debut is unusual but could generate significant buzz.

Fan Reactions and Expectations

Social media has been buzzing since the announcement. Many fans have expressed appreciation for the cultural sensitivity shown by the filmmakers. "Finally, a producer who respects our traditions," one user wrote on X. Others have joked about waking up at dawn to catch the first glimpse of the trailer.

The film has faced delays and speculation over its cast and release date. While official details remain under wraps, the trailer is expected to offer the first real look at the visual effects and star cast. Namit Malhotra, known for his work on 'Brahmastra', has promised a visually stunning spectacle.

What This Means for the Film

Choosing Brahma Muhurat for a trailer launch is a marketing masterstroke. It sets the film apart from standard Bollywood promotions and reinforces its connection to Indian mythology. The move also signals that the producers are treating the project with a sense of sacred duty, not just commercial intent.

However, some critics have questioned whether the early hour will limit the trailer's initial viewership. The producers are likely banking on the novelty factor and the subsequent media coverage to drive views throughout the day. With the 'Spider-Man' tie-in, they are covering all bases.

As the July 30 release approaches, all eyes will be on the trailer itself. If the visuals live up to the hype, the film could become one of the biggest releases in Indian cinema. For now, audiences are setting their alarms for a very early morning.
