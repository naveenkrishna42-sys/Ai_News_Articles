9 killed in fresh strikes as Russia-Ukraine war intensifies

Nine people have been killed in fresh strikes between Russia and Ukraine, a day after a deadly drone attack on a shopping centre in Moscow. The attacks have escalated tensions between the two nations, with casualties mounting and the mood darkening.

Russian missiles rain down on Kyiv

As Russian missiles rain down on Kyiv, the situation on the ground has become increasingly dire. The Ukrainian capital has been a focal point for Russian military operations, with the city suffering significant damage and loss of life.

Moscow's deadly shopping centre attack

On August 20, a drone strike by the Russian Federation on a shopping complex in Kyiv resulted in the deaths of 16 people and injuries to 42 others. This attack has further escalated the conflict, with both sides accusing each other of targeting civilian areas.

Casualties mount as tensions rise

Russian and Ukrainian attacks have killed a total of 9 people, with the situation on the ground becoming increasingly volatile. The international community has called for a ceasefire, but efforts to de-escalate the conflict have so far been unsuccessful.

What happens next?

The situation in Ukraine and Russia remains fluid, with both sides likely to continue their military operations. The international community will be watching closely to see if any progress can be made towards a ceasefire or diplomatic resolution.
