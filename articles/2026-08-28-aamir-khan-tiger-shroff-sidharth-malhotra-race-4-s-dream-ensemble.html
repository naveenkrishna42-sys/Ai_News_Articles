Blockbuster Ensemble in Talks for Race 4

Ramesh Taurani, producer of the successful 'Race' franchise, is reportedly in negotiations with a power-packed trio - Aamir Khan, Tiger Shroff, and Sidharth Malhotra - for 'Race 4'. The news, first broken by Pinkvilla, has since been echoed by multiple sources including The Times of India and Inshorts.

Industry Reactions and Implications

Trade analysts are buzzing with optimism. 'If this casting happens,' says Taran Adarsh, 'it will be the biggest ensemble of 2027.' However, some are cautious. 'It depends on the script and direction,' says Komal Nahta. 'star power alone doesn't guarantee a hit.'

Meanwhile, 'Race 4' is rumored to go on floors in mid-2027. Fans eagerly await official announcements from Taurani and the cast.
