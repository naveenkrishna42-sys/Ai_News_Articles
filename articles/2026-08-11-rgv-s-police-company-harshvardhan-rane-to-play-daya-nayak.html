RGV's Police Company: Harshvardhan Rane to Play Daya Nayak

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ram Gopal Varma announces new film 'Police Company'
  • Harshvardhan Rane to play former Mumbai encounter specialist Daya Nayak
  • Film based on Nayak's extraordinary life and career

RGV's Next Crime Thriller

Filmmaker Ram Gopal Varma has announced his next project, a crime thriller titled Police Company, with actor Harshvardhan Rane in the lead role. The film will be based on the life of former Mumbai encounter specialist Daya Nayak.

Varma, known for his gritty portrayals of the underworld and law enforcement in films like Satya and Company, is returning to familiar territory. The announcement has generated considerable buzz in the industry, given the director's track record with police dramas.

Who is Daya Nayak?

Daya Nayak was a celebrated officer in the Mumbai Police, credited with over 80 encounters during his career in the 1990s and early 2000s. He was a key figure in the fight against the city's organised crime syndicates.

His life has been marked by both professional acclaim and personal controversy. Nayak faced suspension and legal troubles later in his career, including a case of alleged disproportionate assets, which he has consistently denied. The film is expected to explore his extraordinary life, including his rise and subsequent challenges.

Harshvardhan Rane's Role

Harshvardhan Rane, known for his performances in films like Sanam Teri Kasam and the series Bekaaboo, will step into the shoes of Nayak. The casting suggests a departure from the typical portrayal of encounter specialists, with Rane's intensity likely to bring a new dimension to the role.

Rane has not yet commented publicly on the announcement. However, industry insiders suggest that the actor underwent extensive preparation for the role, including studying Nayak's mannerisms and the operational details of Mumbai Police's encounter units.

Varma's Return to Form

For Varma, Police Company marks a return to the genre that made him a household name. His earlier works, particularly Company and Ab Tak Chhappan, were praised for their raw, realistic depiction of crime and policing.

The director has been quoted in earlier interviews saying that Nayak's story has never been fully told, and he believes it deserves a cinematic treatment. The film's title, Police Company, hints at a focus on the collective operations of the police force, with Nayak as the central figure.

Production Details

While the announcement has been made, official details regarding the production schedule, other cast members, and the release date are yet to be disclosed. The film is expected to go on floors later this year, though no official confirmation has been provided.

Given Varma's reputation for mounting projects quickly, fans may not have to wait long for updates. The director has a history of releasing promotional material early in the production cycle, which could give audiences a first look at Rane in the role of Nayak soon.

As the project moves forward, all eyes will be on how Varma balances the factual account of Nayak's life with the dramatic liberties expected in a feature film.
