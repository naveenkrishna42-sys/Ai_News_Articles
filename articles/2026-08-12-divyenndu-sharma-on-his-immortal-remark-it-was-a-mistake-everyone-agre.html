Divyenndu Sharma on his 'immortal' remark: 'It was a mistake, everyone agrees'

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Divyenndu Sharma clarifies his 'immortal hero' statement was a mistake
  • He says the remark was taken out of context but admits it was poorly worded
  • The actor discusses the pressure of living up to the 'hero' image in Bollywood

The remark that sparked debate

Bollywood actor Divyenndu Sharma has finally broken his silence on the comment that had the internet talking. In a recent interview, the 'Mirzapur' star addressed his now-viral statement, 'I am a Hindi film hero, I am immortal', which drew both mockery and criticism from fans and industry watchers.

Sharma, known for his roles in 'Chhichhore' and 'Mulk', acknowledged that the phrasing was unfortunate. He admitted that the statement, made during a promotional event, came out wrong and has since become a talking point for all the wrong reasons.

'Everyone agrees it was a mistake'

Speaking candidly, the actor said that even those close to him have pointed out the blunder. 'Everyone agrees it was a mistake,' he said, adding that he understands why the words were picked apart. The actor clarified that his intention was to speak about the larger-than-life perception of Hindi film heroes, not to make a personal claim of invincibility.

He explained that the comment was part of a longer conversation about the unrealistic standards set by commercial cinema. However, he conceded that the soundbite, when isolated, came across as arrogant and out of touch with the ground reality of the film industry.

The pressure of the 'hero' tag

In the same conversation, Sharma reflected on the burden that comes with being labelled a hero in Hindi cinema. He noted that the industry often expects actors to maintain a certain image, which can lead to statements that are easily misconstrued. 'The word 'immortal' was a slip, not a belief,' he said, stressing that he has always seen himself as a working actor rather than a larger-than-life figure.

The actor, who has often played supporting roles with critical acclaim, said he prefers to stay grounded. He pointed out that his filmography includes characters that are flawed and human, which is why the 'immortal' comment felt particularly out of character for him.

Fan reactions and industry response

Social media had a field day after the original clip surfaced, with many users creating memes and jokes around the 'immortal' line. Some, however, defended the actor, saying the comment was taken out of context. Industry insiders suggest that the controversy has not affected his upcoming projects, but it has certainly added to the chatter around his public persona.

Sharma, who is currently awaiting the release of his next film, said he has learned from the episode. He emphasised that actors must be careful with their words, especially in an age where every statement is recorded and amplified.

What lies ahead

As the dust settles on this controversy, Divyenndu Sharma is expected to focus on his professional commitments. The actor has several projects in the pipeline, and his team is reportedly working on damage control to steer the narrative back to his work. Whether this episode will fade into Bollywood trivia or linger as a cautionary tale remains to be seen, but Sharma seems keen to move past it.
