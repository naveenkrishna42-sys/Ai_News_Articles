SML Mahindra Shares Surge 20% After M&M Truck, Bus Division Sale

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • SML Mahindra shares surged 20% hitting the upper circuit limit
  • Mahindra & Mahindra to sell its truck and bus division to SML Mahindra for Rs 525 crore
  • The deal aims to consolidate Mahindra's commercial vehicle operations under one entity

Shares Hit Upper Circuit

SML Mahindra shares surged 20% in early trade on Wednesday, hitting the upper circuit limit. The sharp rally came after parent Mahindra & Mahindra (M&M) announced the sale of its truck and bus division to SML Mahindra for Rs 525 crore.

The stock was locked at its daily price band, with buyers queued up but no sellers willing to offload shares. The move signals strong investor optimism about the restructuring plan.

Deal Details

Under the transaction, M&M will transfer its entire truck and bus manufacturing business to SML Mahindra, a subsidiary. The consideration of Rs 525 crore will be settled through a combination of cash and stock.

The deal is part of a broader strategy to streamline operations. Mahindra has been looking to consolidate its commercial vehicle portfolio under a single entity to improve efficiency and reduce overlap.

SML Mahindra, formerly known as Swaraj Mazda, already manufactures buses and trucks. The acquisition will give it access to M&M's product line, dealer network, and customer base in the medium and heavy commercial vehicle segment.

Strategic Rationale

Analysts say the move allows M&M to focus on its core businesses while giving SML Mahindra a more comprehensive product range. The truck and bus division has been a small part of M&M's overall revenue, and the sale frees up capital for other priorities.

For SML Mahindra, the acquisition is transformative. It will become the sole entity for Mahindra's commercial vehicle business in India, competing directly with Tata Motors and Ashok Leyland.

The transaction is expected to close in the coming months, subject to regulatory and shareholder approvals. M&M has indicated that employees of the truck and bus division will be transferred to SML Mahindra on similar terms.

Market Reaction

Investors cheered the clarity on the restructuring. SML Mahindra shares have been volatile in recent months, but the deal provides a clear growth trajectory.

The broader market also responded positively, with M&M shares trading higher. Analysts have upgraded their outlook on both stocks, citing the potential for improved margins and market share.

Some experts caution that integration risks remain. Combining two manufacturing operations and dealer networks will require careful execution. However, the long-term benefits are expected to outweigh the short-term challenges.

What Lies Ahead

The completion of the deal will mark a new chapter for SML Mahindra. The company is expected to announce a detailed integration plan in the coming weeks, including timelines for product launches and capacity expansion. All eyes will be on how quickly it can capture market share in India's competitive commercial vehicle space.
