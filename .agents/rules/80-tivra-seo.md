# TivraNews SEO Policy

Optimize for useful search visibility and high user satisfaction, not mere article volume.

## Every Article Must Have:
- Unique descriptive title under 70 characters
- Useful, readable summary (140-160 chars)
- Canonical URL matching site structure
- Valid NewsArticle & Product structured data (Schema.org)
- Accurate published_time metadata
- High-resolution verified image with alt text
- Clear attribution link to the original reporting source
