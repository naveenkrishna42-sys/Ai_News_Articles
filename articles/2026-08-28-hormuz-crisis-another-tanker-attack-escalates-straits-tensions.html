Hormuz Crisis: Another Tanker Attack Escalates Straits Tensions

📅 2026-08-28 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Third tanker attack in Straits of Hormuz
  • Heightened tensions between Iran and US
  • India, Qatar express concern, seek de-escalation

Another Tanker Attack in Hormuz

The tension in the Straits of Hormuz has ratcheted up another notch with the third attack on a tanker in less than a month. This time, a British-owned tanker, Beringer, was struck by an unknown projectile while transiting through the strategic waterway, according to the UK Maritime Agency. The attack caused a fire but no reports of casualties or oil spills.

Escalating Tensions

The incident comes amidst heightened tensions between Iran and the United States. While Iran denies involvement, the US blames it for previous attacks, further straining relations. Iran has vowed retaliation for US economic sanctions, which have crippled its oil exports.

Global Reaction

India, the world's third-largest oil importer, has expressed concern over the attacks, urging all parties to exercise restraint. Qatar, which shares a border with Iran, also sought an immediate de-escalation, stating that it is committed to diplomatic efforts to preserve regional security.

Crude Oil Prices Soar

The attacks have sent crude oil prices soaring, with Brent crude futures hitting their highest level since May 2022. The Straits of Hormuz are a crucial chokepoint for global oil supplies, with about 30% of all crude oil and nearly a third of liquefied natural gas passing through it each day.
