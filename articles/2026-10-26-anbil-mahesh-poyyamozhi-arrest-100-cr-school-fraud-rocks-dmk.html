Former DMK Minister Anbil Mahesh Poyyamozhi Arrested in ₹100 Crore School Fraud

In a significant development shaking Tamil Nadu's political landscape, former DMK Minister Anbil Mahesh Poyyamozhi was arrested on Monday, October 26, 2026, in connection with an alleged ₹100-crore private schools fraud case. The arrest follows weeks of intense legal scrutiny and a Madras High Court decision to deny his anticipatory bail plea, emphasizing the necessity of an unhindered investigation into the multi-layered corruption allegations. This high-profile arrest sends a clear signal about intensified efforts to tackle corruption, irrespective of political affiliations.

Poyyamozhi, who previously held a prominent portfolio within the DMK administration, faces accusations of orchestrating a large-scale bribery scheme. The alleged fraud involved illicit payments and kickbacks for granting official approvals and recognition to numerous private educational institutions across the state. Such a scandal not only tarnishes individual reputations but also raises serious questions about the integrity of regulatory processes within the crucial education sector.

The Allegations: A Web of Corruption in School Approvals

The core of the ₹100-crore fraud lies in the alleged manipulation of the approval process for private schools. Investigations suggest that a network facilitated payments from school managements seeking clearances, affiliations, and other regulatory nods from government departments. These approvals are critical for a school's operation, impacting everything from curriculum delivery to student admissions and infrastructure standards.

Sources indicate that the alleged scheme bypassed established protocols, compromising the very benchmarks designed to ensure quality and safety in educational institutions. The financial implications, estimated at ₹100 crore, highlight the extensive reach and systematic nature of the alleged corruption. For parents and students, such malpractices undermine trust in the education system and raise concerns about the legitimacy of institutions operating under potentially compromised approvals.

Legal Scrutiny and High Court Intervention

The legal trajectory leading to Poyyamozhi's arrest saw the Madras High Court play a pivotal role. The court's denial of anticipatory bail was a critical juncture, with judges observing that granting pre-arrest bail in multi-layered corruption cases could significantly thwart ongoing investigations. This judicial stance underscores the judiciary's commitment to allowing enforcement agencies full scope to unearth evidence and establish facts without interference.

The High Court's decision reflects a broader legal principle: the need to preserve the integrity of the investigative process, particularly in cases involving public figures and substantial financial irregularities. For citizens, this ensures that due process is followed, and no individual is above the law, irrespective of their past or current political standing.

Political Fallout and Reactions

The arrest has predictably ignited a political firestorm in Tamil Nadu. Chief Minister MK Stalin, while not directly commenting on the specifics of the case against his former cabinet colleague, has reportedly emphasized the government's commitment to transparency and accountability. However, opposition parties have seized upon the incident, launching sharp attacks against the ruling DMK.

The Thanthai Periyar Dravida Kazhagam (TVK), for instance, was quick to label the alleged actions an 'evil force,' targeting the DMK over the former minister's arrest. These reactions highlight the volatile political environment and the immediate implications such high-profile arrests have on party image and electoral prospects. The incident is likely to become a significant talking point in upcoming political discourses, shaping public perception of governance and corruption.

The Road Ahead: Investigation and Justice

With Anbil Mahesh Poyyamozhi now in custody, the investigation is expected to intensify. Enforcement agencies will focus on tracing the financial trail, identifying other potential beneficiaries or facilitators, and gathering concrete evidence to build a robust case. This involves scrutinizing financial records, official documents, and potentially interrogating other individuals linked to the alleged scheme.

The outcome of this case holds significant implications for governance in Tamil Nadu. It will test the state's resolve in combating corruption at higher echelons and reinforce the importance of transparent and accountable public service. For the education sector, it presents an opportunity to review and strengthen regulatory mechanisms, ensuring that approvals are granted based on merit and adherence to standards, rather than illicit financial transactions.
