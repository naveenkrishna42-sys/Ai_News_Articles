1. Respect for intellectual property

AI News Factory summarizes and links to publicly available news reporting. We respect the intellectual property rights of others and expect the same from anyone submitting content on our behalf. If you believe content on this site infringes your copyright, you can request its removal as described below.

2. How to file a takedown request

Send a written notice to our contact email (see Contact page) that includes:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  • The exact URL(s) on this site of the material you claim is infringing.
  • Your name, address, phone number, and email address.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

3. Our process

We review valid takedown notices and, where warranted, remove or disable access to the identified material promptly — generally within 5 business days. We may also correct or remove content that turns out to be inaccurately attributed even outside a formal notice.

4. Counter-notices

If you believe content was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notice through the same contact channel identifying the removed material and explaining why it should be restored.

5. Repeat infringement

Content flagged through repeated, substantiated takedown requests is subject to permanent removal and, where relevant, review of the workflow that produced it.

6. Contact

Submit copyright notices via our Contact page.
