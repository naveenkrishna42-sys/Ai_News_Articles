Sensex, Nifty end flat amid mixed global cues

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex closes 600 points higher
  • Nifty settles above 24,200
  • Mixed global cues influence market sentiment

Stock Markets End Flat Amid Mixed Global Cues

Indian stock markets closed flat on Tuesday, with the Sensex and Nifty settling in a narrow range amid mixed global cues. The Sensex gained 44 points to close at 82,444, while the Nifty rose above 24,230 to end at 24,235. The benchmark indices held near the flatline in afternoon trade, with investors awaiting further cues on the global economic outlook.

Factors Influencing Market Sentiment

Several factors influenced market sentiment on Tuesday. The Sensex closed 600 points higher, with gains in the FMCG and auto sectors driving the rally. The Nifty snapped a seven-day fall to settle above 24,200, reflecting investor optimism ahead of the upcoming quarterly results season.

Global Cues Remain Mixed

Global cues remained mixed, with the US stock markets showing signs of weakness, while European markets ended higher. The dollar index fell to a three-week low, while gold prices rose to a one-month high, reflecting investor risk appetite.

Sectoral Performance

Sectoral performance varied on Tuesday. The FMCG and auto sectors led the gains, with the Sensex closing 600 points higher. The Nifty settled above 24,200, reflecting investor optimism ahead of the upcoming quarterly results season.

What to Watch Next

Investors will be closely watching the upcoming quarterly results season, with several key companies expected to report earnings in the coming weeks. The global economic outlook will also be a key focus, with investors awaiting further cues on the US Federal Reserve's monetary policy.
