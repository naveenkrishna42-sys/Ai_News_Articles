Gold and silver ease as CPI risk steadies yields and dollar

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Gold and silver prices eased on Tuesday as traders positioned ahead of the US CPI report.
  • US Treasury yields and the dollar steadied, reducing immediate pressure on bullion.
  • Gold had risen for three straight sessions before the pullback, supported by safe-haven demand.
  • Markets await inflation data for clues on the Federal Reserve's next policy move.

Precious metals take a breather

Gold and silver prices slipped on Tuesday as market participants turned cautious ahead of the key US inflation report. The pullback comes after gold's three-day winning streak, during which it climbed to near two-month highs.

Spot gold eased as US Treasury yields and the dollar steadied, removing some of the tailwinds that had lifted bullion in recent sessions. Silver followed suit, giving back some of its recent gains as traders locked in profits.

CPI in focus

The spotlight is firmly on the upcoming US Consumer Price Index (CPI) data, which will provide fresh clues on the Federal Reserve's interest rate trajectory. A hotter-than-expected print could strengthen the case for higher-for-longer rates, weighing on non-yielding assets like gold.

Conversely, a cooler inflation reading might revive hopes of policy easing, potentially reigniting buying interest in precious metals. Until the numbers land, analysts expect range-bound trading.

Yields and dollar steady

US Treasury yields held their ground on Tuesday, while the dollar index remained firm, capping any upside in bullion. The steadiness in yields and the greenback reflects a market that is waiting for direction rather than taking decisive positions.

Gold's recent resilience, however, points to underlying support from geopolitical tensions and persistent central bank buying. The metal has held a bullish structure despite pressure from real yields, according to technical analysts.

Oil rally adds a twist

Crude oil prices extended their rally, adding a complicating factor for inflation watchers. Higher energy costs could feed into broader price pressures, potentially influencing the Fed's calculus.

For gold, rising oil prices often signal inflationary pressures, which can be a double-edged sword—boosting safe-haven demand while also prompting tighter monetary policy.

Market outlook

Traders are now eyeing the CPI release for the next big move in precious metals. A clear break above recent highs could attract fresh buying, while a disappointing print might trigger profit-taking.

In the near term, gold is likely to remain sensitive to inflation data and central bank signals. Silver, with its dual industrial and monetary appeal, could see sharper swings depending on the economic outlook.

As always, volatility is expected around the data release, and market participants should brace for quick shifts in sentiment.
