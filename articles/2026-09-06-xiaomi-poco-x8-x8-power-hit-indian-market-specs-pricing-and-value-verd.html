Xiaomi Poco X8 & X8 Power Hit Indian Market: Specs, Pricing and Value Verdict

✍️ By TIVRA News Tech & Gadgets Desk 📅 2026-09-06 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Xiaomi offers two mid‑range models under ₹40,000, each with distinct battery capacities.
  • Both phones use Snapdragon 7+ Gen 2, delivering smooth performance for everyday tasks and gaming.
  • The X8 Power’s 10,000 mAh battery sets a new benchmark for endurance in its price bracket.

Launch overview and market positioning

Xiaomi’s Poco sub‑brand expands its Indian portfolio with the Poco X8 and X8 Power, slated for sale from 11 September. Priced at ₹29,999 and ₹34,999 respectively, the duo targets consumers seeking flagship‑level performance without premium pricing. Their entry coincides with a crowded mid‑range field where Realme, OnePlus and Samsung vie for attention.

Design, display and build quality

Both models share a 6.78‑inch AMOLED panel with a 120 Hz refresh rate, offering vibrant colours and fluid scrolling. The X8 carries a 6.55 mm thickness, while the X8 Power adds a marginal 0.2 mm to accommodate the larger battery. Gorilla Glass 5 protects the front, and a matte polycarbonate back gives a premium feel without adding weight.

Performance engine and battery endurance

Under the hood sits Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 7+ Gen 2, paired with 8 GB RAM and UFS 3.1 storage. This combination handles multitasking, 5G connectivity and mid‑range gaming with ease. The standout difference lies in battery size: the X8 houses a 5,000 mAh cell, whereas the X8 Power jumps to 10,000 mAh, promising up to two days of mixed‑use on a single charge.

Real‑world battery test

Independent testing shows the X8 Power delivering 21 hours of video playback, compared with 12 hours on the X8. Fast‑charging support is 67 W for both, refilling the 10,000 mAh pack to 70 % in just 30 minutes, a practical advantage for heavy users.

Camera suite and imaging performance

The X8 features a quad‑camera array: 108 MP primary, 8 MP ultra‑wide, 2 MP macro and 2 MP depth sensor. The X8 Power swaps the macro for a 5 MP telephoto lens, adding 2× optical zoom. Both devices record 4K video at 30 fps and benefit from AI scene detection. In daylight, the 108 MP sensor resolves fine detail; low‑light results are acceptable but lag behind premium competitors.

Pricing, value and competition

At ₹29,999, the Poco X8 undercuts many 5G rivals while delivering flagship‑class display and chipset. The X8 Power, at ₹34,999, justifies its premium with the massive battery and added telephoto lens. Compared with the Realme P4s 5G (₹27,999) and OnePlus Nord CE6 (₹32,999), Poco’s offering provides a superior refresh‑rate display and a more future‑proof chipset.

Pros & Cons

  • Pros: 120 Hz AMOLED, Snapdragon 7+ Gen 2, 67 W fast charging, 10,000 mAh battery (Power), competitive pricing.
  • Cons: Macro sensor on X8 is low‑resolution, telephoto on Power limited to 2× optical zoom, no wireless charging.

Buyer implications

For users who prioritise screen fluidity and gaming, both models deliver a premium feel at sub‑₹35,000 cost. Those with heavy media consumption or long commutes will find the X8 Power’s endurance a decisive factor. If photography is a primary need, the 108 MP main sensor offers solid results, but buyers seeking a dedicated macro lens may look elsewhere.

Overall, Xiaomi’s dual launch strengthens its foothold in India’s mid‑range segment, offering clear trade‑offs between price, battery life and camera versatility.
