Bigg Boss 20 teaser out: Salman Khan hints at Karan-Arjun twist

Key points
  • Bigg Boss 20 will premiere on September 6, with Salman Khan returning as host.
  • The first teaser features a cryptic Karan Arjun hint, leading to fan speculation about Shah Rukh Khan's involvement.
  • Neither Salman nor the makers have confirmed any co-host, leaving fans guessing.
  • The promo has sparked massive buzz on social media with fans linking the hint to the 1995 film.

Salman Khan returns for Bigg Boss 20

The wait is finally over for reality TV fans. Salman Khan is back as the host of Bigg Boss, and the show's 20th season is set to premiere on September 6. The first teaser dropped on Tuesday, and it has already set the internet abuzz.

In the promo, Salman appears in his trademark style, but it is his words that have everyone talking. He drops a cryptic reference to the 1995 blockbuster Karan Arjun, a film that starred him alongside Shah Rukh Khan. The line, delivered with a knowing smile, has fans decoding every frame.

The Karan Arjun hint that has fans guessing

The teaser shows Salman in a dramatic setup, with the Karan Arjun reference thrown in almost casually. For those who remember the film, the phrase is iconic — it was a tale of reincarnation and brotherhood. But why bring it up now?

Fans have taken to social media with theories. The most popular one? Shah Rukh Khan might co-host a segment or even appear as a special guest. The idea of the two superstars sharing screen space on Bigg Boss has sent excitement levels soaring. Neither Salman nor the makers have confirmed anything yet, but the speculation is only growing.

What the teaser reveals

The promo also gives a glimpse of the show's new look. The set appears bigger, bolder, and more glitzy than before — fitting for a milestone season. Salman, dressed in his usual sharp avatar, walks through the familiar corridors, but the energy feels different this time.

There is no confirmation on the contestant list yet. However, rumours are already swirling about who might enter the house. Past seasons have seen a mix of television actors, film personalities, and social media influencers, and season 20 is expected to follow suit.

Why the Karan Arjun reference matters

The Karan Arjun connection is not just about nostalgia. It is a clever marketing move that taps into the enduring popularity of the Salman-Shah Rukh pairing. The two have not shared a full-fledged film in decades, and any hint of collaboration is enough to break the internet.

Bigg Boss has always thrived on drama and surprise. A co-host of Shah Rukh's stature would be a massive coup. But even if it is just a promotional gimmick, the teaser has already achieved its goal — it has got people talking.

What to expect from the premiere

The September 6 premiere will likely be a grand affair. Salman's entry, the first look of the house, and the reveal of contestants are all expected to be part of the opening episode. The makers have kept details under wraps, but the teaser sets the tone for a season that promises high voltage entertainment.

For now, fans will have to wait and watch whether the Karan Arjun hint leads to something bigger. The show's official social media handles are likely to release more promos in the coming weeks, each possibly carrying more clues.
