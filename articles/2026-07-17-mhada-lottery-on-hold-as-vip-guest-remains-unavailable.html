MHADA lottery on hold as VIP guest remains unavailable

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • MHADA's housing lottery for over 2,000 homes in Mumbai has been postponed indefinitely.
  • The delay is due to the unavailability of a VIP guest who was to preside over the draw event.
  • Thousands of applicants, who had been awaiting results for months, now face further uncertainty.

Lottery for 2,000 homes stalled

The Maharashtra Housing and Area Development Authority (MHADA) has postponed its much-awaited housing lottery for over 2,000 homes across Mumbai. The draw, originally scheduled for earlier this month, has been put on hold indefinitely due to the unavailability of a VIP guest who was to preside over the event.

According to sources within MHADA, the lottery is typically conducted in the presence of a senior government functionary. For this round, the authority had sought a date from a high-profile minister, but scheduling conflicts have forced a delay. Officials have not yet confirmed who the VIP is or when the event might be rescheduled.

Applicants face fresh uncertainty

The postponement has left thousands of applicants in limbo. Many had been waiting for months after submitting their applications and deposits. For a city where the median price of a one-bedroom apartment in the suburbs exceeds ₹1 crore, MHADA’s lottery offers flats at rates that are often a fraction of the market value.

“I’ve been checking the website every day,” said a middle-aged applicant from Andheri, who did not wish to be named. “We were told the draw would be in June, then July. Now there’s no date at all.” The lottery includes units in prime locations such as Wadala, Powai, and Dahisar, making it one of the most competitive housing schemes in the state.

MHADA’s protocol and public pressure

MHADA has a long-standing practice of having a political figure or senior bureaucrat conduct the draw. This protocol, meant to add transparency and gravitas to the process, has now become a bottleneck. Critics argue that the delay underscores a system overly reliant on VIP presence. “If a lottery can be held online for a crore rupees, why can’t it be done without a minister?” asked a housing rights activist in a social media post.

MHADA officials have stated that the lottery will be rescheduled once a suitable date is available for the dignitary. They have assured applicants that their applications and deposits remain valid. However, no timeline has been provided for the rescheduled event.

In the past, such delays have triggered protests and legal petitions. In 2023, a similar postponement of a MHADA lottery in Pune led to a public outcry and eventual intervention by the state housing department.

What happens next

With no fresh date in sight, applicants and real estate watchers will be closely watching the next move. MHADA is expected to announce the rescheduled draw once the VIP confirms availability. Until then, thousands of hopeful homebuyers remain in a state of anxious waiting.
