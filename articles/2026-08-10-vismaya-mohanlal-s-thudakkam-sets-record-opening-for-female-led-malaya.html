Vismaya Mohanlal's 'Thudakkam' Sets Record Opening for Female-Led Malayalam Film

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Thudakkam records highest worldwide opening weekend for a female-led Malayalam film
  • Film outperforms Kalyani Priyadarshan's Lokah at the box office
  • Sunday collections saw an 18.1% rise, earning Rs 4.25 crore
  • Vismaya Mohanlal's martial arts performance in the revenge thriller draws praise

A Historic Opening for Malayalam Cinema

Vismaya Mohanlal's action drama Thudakkam has stormed the box office, registering the highest worldwide opening for a female-led Malayalam film. The movie has surpassed the previous record held by Kalyani Priyadarshan's Lokah, marking a significant milestone for women-led cinema in the Malayalam industry.

The film, which features Vismaya in the lead role, has been generating buzz since its announcement. Daughter of legendary actor Mohanlal, Vismaya has stepped into the limelight with a performance that critics and audiences are calling a game-changer.

Box Office Numbers Speak Volumes

Trade reports indicate that Thudakkam collected Rs 4.25 crore on Sunday alone, a sharp 18.1 percent rise from the previous day. The weekend total has pushed the film to a record-breaking position, outperforming Lokah in its opening days.

The film's worldwide collection has crossed expectations, with strong contributions from Kerala, the Gulf region, and other international markets. Distributors are optimistic about the film's staying power, given the positive word-of-mouth and repeat viewings.

Martial Arts and a Revenge Thriller

Reviews have highlighted Vismaya's martial arts prowess as a key highlight. The Hindu's review notes that her skills inject life into what is otherwise a typical revenge thriller narrative. The action sequences, choreographed with precision, have been praised for their authenticity and intensity.

The film follows a familiar template—wronged protagonist seeking vengeance—but Vismaya's screen presence elevates the material. Critics say her physicality and commitment to the role set her apart from contemporaries, giving the film an edge at the box office.

Comparisons with Lokah and Female-Led Films

The comparison with Lokah was inevitable. Kalyani Priyadarshan's film, which released earlier this year, had set a benchmark for female-led Malayalam films. Thudakkam has now reset that benchmark, signaling a shift in audience appetite for women in action-oriented roles.

Industry observers see this as a positive trend. Malayalam cinema has traditionally been male-dominated, but recent successes suggest a growing acceptance of female-led narratives. The record opening of Thudakkam could encourage producers to back more such projects.

What Lies Ahead

As the film enters its first week, the focus will be on sustaining momentum. The upcoming weekend will be crucial in determining whether Thudakkam can maintain its lead and set a long-term record for female-led Malayalam films.

For Vismaya, the film marks a promising debut as a leading lady. With the box office numbers backing her, she appears poised for a strong career trajectory. The industry will be watching closely to see how this success shapes her next moves.
