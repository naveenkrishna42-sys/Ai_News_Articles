Rajiv Thakur's Father Dies; Krushna Abhishek Confirms, Indore Show Postponed

Key points
  • Rajiv Thakur's father has died, as confirmed by comedian Krushna Abhishek.
  • The duo's scheduled show in Indore was postponed due to the tragedy.
  • Organisers have apologised to ticket holders; further details are awaited.

Comedian Rajiv Thakur, known for his work on The Kapil Sharma Show, is in mourning after the death of his father. The news was confirmed by fellow comedian and frequent collaborator Krushna Abhishek, who shared the information with media outlets on Sunday.

Thakur, a popular face on Indian television, had been scheduled to perform in Indore alongside Krushna Abhishek. That event has now been postponed following the sudden development.

Confirmed by Krushna Abhishek

Krushna Abhishek, who has often shared the stage with Thakur in live shows and television appearances, broke the news to the press. He expressed grief over the loss and asked for privacy for the family during this difficult time.

While the exact cause and timing of the death have not been officially disclosed, several entertainment news portals, including NDTV and The Indian Express, have reported the confirmation from Abhishek. The comedian has not yet made a public statement on his social media handles.

Indore Show Postponed

The scheduled event in Indore, which was set to feature both Thakur and Abhishek, has been called off. Organisers of the show issued a statement apologising to ticket holders, citing the personal loss in the comedian's family as the reason for the postponement.

According to reports, the organisers are expected to announce a new date for the performance. Attendees have been urged to hold on to their tickets, as the fresh schedule will be communicated soon.

Fans and colleagues express condolences

News of the demise has prompted an outpouring of support from fans and colleagues in the entertainment industry. Many took to social media to offer their condolences to Thakur, who is widely recognised for his comic timing and energetic stage presence.

Rajiv Thakur rose to prominence with his appearances on The Kapil Sharma Show, where his sketches and characters earned him a loyal following. He has also been a part of stand-up comedy circuits and live tours across the country.

The comedian had been active professionally, with several stage shows lined up in different cities. The Indore event was one among those, and it remains unclear whether other upcoming appearances will be affected.

As of now, no further details about the funeral or last rites have been made public. The family has requested privacy, and fans have been advised to await official communication from Thakur's team.

The sudden loss comes as a shock to those who have followed Thakur's career, and the coming days are likely to see tributes from the comedy fraternity. For now, the focus remains on supporting the comedian and his family through this personal tragedy.

Organisers of the Indore show have promised to share updates on the rescheduled date shortly, and fans are hopeful that the event will be held once Thakur is ready to return to the stage.
