Goa BJP MLA and Former Minister Jennifer Monserrate Dies at 56

📅 2026-07-19 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jennifer Monserrate, Goa BJP MLA and former minister, died at 56 after a prolonged illness.
  • She represented the Taleigao constituency and served as a minister in the state government.
  • Her last rites will be held on July 20 as per her wishes.
  • Residents of Taleigao and Ponda will have to wait for the general election to elect their representatives.

Goa Bharatiya Janata Party MLA and former state minister Jennifer Monserrate passed away on Sunday at the age of 56. She had been battling a prolonged illness, though officials have not yet disclosed the specific cause of death.

A Career in Public Service

Monserrate was a prominent figure in Goa politics, representing the Taleigao constituency. She had served as a minister in the state government, holding portfolios that included women and child development. Her tenure was marked by her work on local infrastructure and social welfare schemes.

She joined the BJP after a stint with the Congress party, a move that reflected the shifting alliances in Goa's political landscape. Her colleagues remembered her as a dedicated public servant who worked tirelessly for her constituents.

Last Rites and Constituency Impact

According to reports, Monserrate's last rites will be held on July 20, as per her wishes. The funeral arrangements are being made by her family, with party leaders expected to attend.

Her death leaves the Taleigao seat vacant. The Election Commission has not yet announced a by-election schedule. Residents of Taleigao and the nearby Ponda constituency will have to wait until the next general election to elect their representatives, according to officials.

Political Reactions

Goa Chief Minister Pramod Sawant expressed condolences, calling Monserrate's passing a significant loss for the state. BJP leaders across the state have paid tribute, highlighting her contributions to the party and the people of Goa.

Opposition leaders also offered their condolences, acknowledging her work across party lines. The legislative assembly observed a moment of silence in her memory.

Her Legacy

Monserrate's political journey spanned over a decade. She was known for her accessibility and her focus on grassroots issues. Her work in the Taleigao constituency included pushing for better roads, water supply, and educational facilities.

Her death marks the end of an era for local politics in the region. The BJP will now need to field a candidate for the upcoming general election to retain the seat.

For now, the people of Taleigao mourn a leader who served them for years. The funeral on Monday will be a final farewell to a woman who shaped Goa's political discourse.
