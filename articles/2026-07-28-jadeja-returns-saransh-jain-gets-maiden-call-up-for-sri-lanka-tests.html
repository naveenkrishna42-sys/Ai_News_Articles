Jadeja returns, Saransh Jain gets maiden call-up for Sri Lanka Tests

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ravindra Jadeja returns to India's Test squad after injury layoff.
  • Saransh Jain, 33, earns first Test call-up for Sri Lanka series.
  • BCCI announced squad for the Tour of Sri Lanka on Tuesday.

Jadeja returns to red-ball setup

Ravindra Jadeja is back in India's Test squad. The all-rounder has recovered from his knee injury and will travel to Sri Lanka for the two-match series. His return adds depth to both batting and bowling.

Jadeja last played a Test in early 2025. His absence was felt during the home series against Bangladesh. The team management is now confident about his fitness for the tour.

Saransh Jain: a late bloomer gets his chance

The biggest surprise in the squad is 33-year-old Saransh Jain. The left-arm spinner has been a consistent performer in domestic cricket for over a decade. He has taken 420 first-class wickets at an average of 24.5.

Jain's call-up comes after a strong Ranji Trophy season. He picked up 45 wickets in 8 matches for Madhya Pradesh. The selectors believe his experience will be crucial on Sri Lankan pitches that traditionally assist spin.

This is the first time Jain has been named in any senior national squad. He has previously played for India A and in the IPL but never got a Test cap. At 33, he is one of the oldest debutants in recent Indian cricket history.

Squad composition and selection rationale

The BCCI announced the squad on Tuesday. The selection committee led by Ajit Agarkar retained most players from the previous Test series. There are no major omissions.

Rohit Sharma will captain the side. Virat Kohli, Shubman Gill, and Rishabh Pant are also included. The pace attack features Jasprit Bumrah, Mohammed Siraj, and Prasidh Krishna.

The spin department now has Ravichandran Ashwin, Jadeja, and Jain. Axar Patel has been left out due to a niggle. The team management is likely to play three spinners in Sri Lanka, given the dust bowls expected in Galle and Colombo.

Kuldeep Yadav is also not in the squad. The selectors have opted for a left-arm spin option in Jain over the left-arm wrist spinner. This suggests they want control and experience rather than variety.

What this means for India's Test future

The return of Jadeja strengthens the lower middle order. He can bat at number seven and bowl long spells. His presence allows India to play an extra batter or pacer depending on conditions.

Saransh Jain's selection signals that domestic performers are being rewarded. The selectors have ignored age and looked purely at numbers. This could open the door for other experienced domestic players who have been overlooked.

India will play two Tests in Sri Lanka starting in mid-August. Both matches are part of the World Test Championship cycle. India currently sits second in the standings behind Australia.

Winning the series in Sri Lanka will be critical for India's chances of reaching the WTC final. The team will leave for Colombo in the first week of August for a preparatory camp.
