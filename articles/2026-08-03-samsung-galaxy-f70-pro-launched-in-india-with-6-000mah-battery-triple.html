Samsung Galaxy F70 Pro launched in India with 6,000mAh battery, triple cameras

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Galaxy F70 Pro features a 6.7-inch FHD+ 120Hz AMOLED display
  • Powered by Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 with a 6,000mAh battery
  • Triple camera setup and six OS upgrades promised
  • Launch price under Rs 30,000 in India

Samsung has officially taken the wraps off the Galaxy F70 Pro in India, a new mid-range 5G smartphone that brings a hefty battery and a promise of long software support. The device, which joins the company’s popular F-series lineup, is aimed at buyers who want endurance without stretching their budget beyond Rs 30,000.

The announcement was confirmed through multiple tech publications on Monday, with the phone now listed on Samsung India’s website. The Galaxy F70 Pro is positioned as a value-for-money offering, stacking a large AMOLED panel with a capable chipset and a battery that should easily last a full day of heavy use.

Display and performance

The Galaxy F70 Pro sports a 6.7-inch FHD+ AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate, ensuring smooth scrolling and vibrant colours. The screen is expected to handle outdoor visibility well, though official brightness figures have not been disclosed yet.

Under the hood, the phone runs on Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 chipset, a processor built for efficient mid-range performance. It should handle everyday tasks, streaming, and light gaming without breaking a sweat. The device ships with 5G support, aligning with the growing demand for future-proof connectivity in this segment.

Camera and battery

Photography duties are handled by a triple camera setup on the back, though Samsung has not yet detailed the exact megapixel counts for each sensor. Early reports suggest a primary sensor paired with ultra-wide and macro lenses, covering the standard versatility expected at this price point.

The headline feature, however, is the 6,000mAh battery. This is a significant upgrade over many rivals in the sub-Rs 30,000 bracket, which typically pack 5,000mAh cells. While fast charging speeds have not been officially confirmed, the large capacity alone should appeal to users who are tired of carrying power banks.

Software and availability

Samsung is promising six OS upgrades for the Galaxy F70 Pro, a move that puts it ahead of most competitors in this price range. This means the phone will receive Android updates well into the future, making it a solid long-term investment for budget-conscious buyers.

The device is available for purchase in India starting today, with pricing set under Rs 30,000. Samsung has not yet clarified whether the phone will launch in other markets, but the F-series has traditionally been India-focused.

What to watch

With the Galaxy F70 Pro now official, attention will shift to real-world battery tests and camera reviews. The phone faces stiff competition from the likes of the Galaxy M47 and other Snapdragon-powered rivals, so how it performs in daily use will determine its success. Samsung is expected to roll out the device in phases across retail and online platforms, and buyers should watch for early offers and bank discounts.
