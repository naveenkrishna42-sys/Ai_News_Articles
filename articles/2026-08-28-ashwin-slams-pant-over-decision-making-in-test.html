Ashwin's blunt warning to Pant

Ravichandran Ashwin, India's premier spinner, has issued a stark warning to Rishabh Pant, urging him to be more cautious with his decision-making in Test cricket. In a recent interview, Ashwin said, 'It takes only one moment of foolishness to lose a Test.'

Criticism of Pant's decision-making

Ashwin's comments come as a surprise, given Pant's recent performances in Test cricket. However, Ashwin has been critical of Pant's decision-making in the past, questioning his ability to handle the pressure of the game. Ashwin said, 'My expectation from Rishabh Pant hasn't been fulfilled.'

Ashwin's concerns about Pant's future

Ashwin's comments suggest that he may not be ready for ODI and T20I sides due to his decision-making issues. Ashwin said, 'Don't give me once in a generation argument.' This is a serious warning from Ashwin, who has been a key player in India's Test cricket for over a decade.

Ashwin's interview with Sportstar

In an interview with Sportstar, Ashwin was asked about Pant's decision-making. Ashwin said, 'I have been critical of Rishabh Pant's decision-making in the past. I think he needs to be more cautious with his decisions.'

Ashwin's interview with NDTV Sports

In an interview with NDTV Sports, Ashwin was asked about Pant's decision-making. Ashwin said, 'I think Rishabh Pant needs to be more cautious with his decisions. He needs to understand that Test cricket is fragile and can be lost with one moment of foolishness.'

In an interview with The Indian Express, Ashwin was asked about Pant's decision-making. Ashwin said, 'I think Rishabh Pant needs to be more cautious with his decisions. He needs to understand that Test cricket is fragile and can be lost with one moment of foolishness.'
