Cookie Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that let a website remember information about your visit. This page explains what we use them for on AI News Factory, in addition to the broader data practices covered in our Privacy Policy.

2. Categories of cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary: required for basic site functionality (e.g. remembering a cookie-consent choice). These can't be switched off.
  • Analytics: help us understand which articles and categories are read, so we know what to keep publishing.
  • Advertising: set by third-party ad networks (including Google AdSense) to serve and measure ads, and in some cases to personalize them based on browsing activity across sites.

3. Third-party advertising cookies

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies — including the DoubleClick DART cookie — to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Opt out of personalized Google ads at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of participating third-party vendors' use of cookies at the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.
  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings; blocking all cookies may affect site functionality.

4. Cookie consent

Visitors in regions covered by GDPR/ePrivacy or similar consent requirements are shown a cookie-consent banner on first visit. Choices are remembered for future visits on the same device/browser.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our advertising or analytics setup changes. Check back periodically for updates.
