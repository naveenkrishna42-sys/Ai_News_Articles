Director Madhur Bhandarkar calls Hanuman Ansh a 'case study' for Bollywood

Director Madhur Bhandarkar has hailed the film Hanuman Ansh as a 'case study' for the Indian film industry, praising its cinematic magic and box office success. The film, which tells the story of a young boy who discovers he is the reincarnation of Lord Hanuman, has been a sleeper hit, earning ₹40 crore in just 24 days despite a slow start.

Box office success and critical acclaim

Hanuman Ansh has been a surprise hit at the box office, crossing the ₹50 crore mark in just 25 days. The film's success can be attributed to its unique storyline, engaging performances, and stunning visuals. Critics have also praised the film for its message of hope and inspiration, with many hailing it as a must-watch for all age groups.
