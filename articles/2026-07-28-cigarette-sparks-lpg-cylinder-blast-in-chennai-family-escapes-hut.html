Cigarette Sparks LPG Cylinder Blast in Chennai, Family Escapes Hut

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Blast occurred when a lit cigarette ignited leaking LPG gas.
  • Family of four escaped the hut moments before the explosion.
  • No injuries reported; hut was completely destroyed.

Blast Rocks Residential Area in Chennai

A lit cigarette sparked a massive LPG cylinder blast in a Chennai hut on Tuesday, leaving a family of four shaken but unhurt. The incident occurred in the early hours near the city's outskirts, where the family was asleep.

The explosion destroyed the makeshift hut completely. Neighbors rushed to the spot after hearing a loud bang, only to find the structure reduced to rubble. Fire services arrived within minutes and doused the flames.

How the Accident Happened

According to preliminary police reports, the family had been using the LPG cylinder for cooking. A leak likely went unnoticed. One of the family members lit a cigarette, igniting the gas that had accumulated inside the hut.

The resulting blast tore through the walls and roof. The family managed to scramble out seconds before the explosion, avoiding serious injury. Officials have not confirmed if the cylinder was properly maintained or if safety norms were followed.

No Injuries, But Narrow Escape

All four occupants—two adults and two children—escaped with minor bruises. They were taken to a nearby hospital for observation and later discharged. The hut, made of corrugated sheets and wood, was reduced to ashes.

Locals described the blast as terrifying. “We heard a deafening sound. The whole area shook,” said a neighbor. The family now has no shelter and is staying with relatives until authorities provide assistance.

Investigation Underway

Chennai police have registered a case and are investigating the cause of the gas leak. Fire department officials suspect a faulty regulator or hose. They have warned residents to check LPG equipment regularly.

This incident highlights the dangers of using LPG cylinders in poorly ventilated spaces. Authorities are urging people to avoid smoking near gas cylinders and to ensure proper maintenance.

What happens next: The family will receive temporary relief from the local corporation. The investigation report is expected within a week, which may lead to stricter safety advisories for hut dwellers.
