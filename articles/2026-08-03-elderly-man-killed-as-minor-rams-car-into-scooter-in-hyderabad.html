Elderly man killed as minor rams car into scooter in Hyderabad

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 70-year-old man was killed when a minor allegedly driving a car rammed into his scooter in Karmika Nagar, Hyderabad.
  • The incident occurred on Sunday; the victim was rushed to a hospital but succumbed to injuries.
  • Police have registered a case and are investigating; the minor's identity has not been officially confirmed.

Hyderabad: A 70-year-old man died on Sunday after a car allegedly driven by a minor rammed into his scooter in Karmika Nagar. The collision took place in the afternoon, leaving the elderly rider critically injured. He was rushed to a nearby hospital, where doctors declared him brought dead.

Accident details

The victim, whose name has not been officially released, was riding his scooter when the car, reportedly driven by a 17-year-old, hit him from behind. The impact threw the man off his vehicle. Bystanders gathered quickly and alerted the police, who reached the spot and shifted the body for post-mortem.

Initial reports suggest the minor was driving at a high speed, but investigators are yet to confirm this. The car was seized by the police. The minor, who is said to be a resident of the area, was detained for questioning.

Police action

The Karmika Nagar police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including causing death by negligence. Since the accused is a minor, the Juvenile Justice Act may apply. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact charges or whether the minor will be produced before the Juvenile Justice Board.

Traffic police are also examining CCTV footage from nearby locations to piece together the sequence of events. A forensic team has been called in to inspect the vehicles involved.

Rising concern over underage driving

This incident has revived concerns about minors driving vehicles on city roads. Hyderabad has seen several such cases in recent years, and police have repeatedly warned parents against allowing children to drive without a licence. In many instances, minors involved in accidents are let off with a warning, but fatal cases often lead to stricter action.

According to the Motor Vehicles Act, a minor caught driving can face a fine of up to Rs 25,000 and three years of imprisonment for the guardian. However, enforcement remains patchy, and such incidents continue to occur.

What happens next

The police are expected to file a detailed report after the post-mortem and forensic analysis. The minor's family has not issued a statement. Meanwhile, the victim's family is yet to receive any compensation, though officials say they may be eligible for financial aid under the state's road accident victim scheme.

Local residents have demanded stricter patrolling in Karmika Nagar, which has seen a rise in traffic violations. The investigation is ongoing, and further details are awaited.
