Chennai's rainwater harvesting campaign to begin in September

📅 2026-08-29 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Chennai to audit rainwater harvesting structures from September
  • City to launch citywide campaign to improve water storage and management
  • Rainwater harvesting can help Chennai address its water scarcity issues

Chennai's Rainwater Harvesting Campaign

Chennai, a city known for its water scarcity, is set to launch a citywide rainwater harvesting campaign from September. The campaign aims to improve the city's water storage and management capabilities, which have been severely impacted by the lack of rain in recent years.

Audit of Existing Structures

Before the campaign begins, the city will conduct an audit of all existing rainwater harvesting structures. This audit will help identify the structures that are functioning well and those that need repair or replacement. The audit will also help the city understand the current state of rainwater harvesting in Chennai and the potential for improvement.

Citywide Campaign

The citywide campaign will involve various stakeholders, including residents, government agencies, and private organizations. The campaign will focus on improving the design and maintenance of rainwater harvesting structures, as well as promoting public awareness about the importance of rainwater harvesting.

Benefits of Rainwater Harvesting

Rainwater harvesting can help Chennai address its water scarcity issues. It can also help reduce the city's dependence on groundwater, which is being depleted at an alarming rate. Additionally, rainwater harvesting can help improve the city's water quality, as it is naturally filtered through the soil.

Challenges and Considerations

While rainwater harvesting is a promising solution for Chennai's water scarcity issues, there are also challenges and considerations. One of the main challenges is the lack of rain in recent years, which has made it difficult for residents to collect and store rainwater. Additionally, there is a need for proper planning and implementation to ensure that the campaign is successful.
