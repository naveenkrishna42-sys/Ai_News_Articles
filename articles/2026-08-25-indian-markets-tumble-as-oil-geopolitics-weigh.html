Stock Market LIVE: Sensex drops 100 pts, Nifty around 24,150

Indian equities opened lower on Tuesday, August 25, 2026, as global market sentiment turned negative. The Sensex dropped 100 points, while the Nifty ended around 24,150. Hindustan Copper shares fell 7%, a significant decline for the copper giant.

Market Decline Amid Elevated Crude Oil Prices

The market decline is primarily attributed to elevated crude oil prices. The global oil benchmark, Brent crude, is trading above $90 per barrel, a level not seen since 2014. This has led to a sharp increase in fuel prices, which has a direct impact on the economy and consumer sentiment.

Escalating Geopolitical Tensions

Geopolitical tensions are also playing a significant role in the market decline. The ongoing conflict in the Middle East, coupled with rising tensions between major powers, has led to increased volatility in global markets. This uncertainty is making investors cautious, leading to a sell-off in equities.

D-Street Braces for Weak Opening on Negative GIFT Nifty Cues

Domestic markets are also reacting to negative cues from the GIFT Nifty. The GIFT Nifty, which is a benchmark for the Indian stock market, has been under pressure, leading to a weak opening for Indian equities. This is a clear indication of the global market sentiment affecting the domestic market.
