Real-World Performance & Daily Use

In practical application, the 85-inch LG QNED Mini-LED TV delivers an outstanding visual experience. The QNED technology, combining Quantum Dot and NanoCell with Mini-LED backlighting, results in incredibly vibrant colors, deep blacks, and exceptional contrast. The precision dimming zones effectively minimize blooming, ensuring that bright objects against dark backgrounds appear sharp and well-defined. The native 120Hz refresh rate, coupled with the α9 GenX AI Processor, provides buttery-smooth motion, making it ideal for fast-paced sports and gaming. Input lag is commendably low, and the inclusion of HDMI 2.1 ports ensures full compatibility with current-generation gaming consoles, supporting 4K at 120Hz with VRR and ALLM.

The webOS 23 smart platform is intuitive and responsive, offering quick access to a vast array of streaming services and smart home controls via ThinQ AI. Navigation is fluid, and app loading times are minimal. Build quality is robust, featuring a sleek, minimalist design with thin bezels that maximize screen real estate. The included Magic Remote enhances the user experience with its point-and-click functionality and voice control. While power consumption is higher than standard LED TVs due to its size and advanced backlighting, the immersive viewing experience it provides easily justifies this trade-off for dedicated home theater enthusiasts.
