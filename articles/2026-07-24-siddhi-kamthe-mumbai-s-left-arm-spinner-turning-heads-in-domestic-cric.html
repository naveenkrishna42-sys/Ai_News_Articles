Consistent performances earn notice

In recent domestic tournaments, Kamthe has been a key figure for her team. She has delivered economical spells and broken partnerships when her captain needed it most.

Calm under pressure

One quality that stands out about Kamthe is her temperament. Even when the match is tight, she holds her nerve. This has made her a reliable option in the middle overs.

What sets her apart
