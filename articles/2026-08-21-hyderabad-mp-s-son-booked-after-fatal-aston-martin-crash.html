Hyderabad: MP’s son booked after fatal Aston Martin crash

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • A woman was killed in a road accident involving an Aston Martin near Inorbit Mall.
  • Police have registered a case against the son of an Andhra Pradesh Rajya Sabha MP.
  • Authorities are currently investigating the circumstances of the collision.

Fatal Collision Near Inorbit Mall

A high-profile road accident in Hyderabad’s IT corridor has claimed the life of a woman after she was struck by a luxury vehicle. The incident occurred near the busy Inorbit Mall, a major landmark in the city’s Madhapur area.

Police have confirmed that the vehicle involved in the crash is an Aston Martin. The collision resulted in immediate, fatal injuries to the victim.

Legal Action Initiated

Local authorities have registered a case in connection with the incident. The investigation has drawn significant attention as the driver has been identified as the son of a sitting Rajya Sabha Member of Parliament from Andhra Pradesh.

The police have not yet released the specific charges pressed against the individual, but standard protocols for fatal road accidents are currently being followed. Officials are in the process of gathering evidence from the scene, including potential CCTV footage from the surrounding commercial establishments.

Investigation Procedures

The investigation is now focused on reconstructing the events leading up to the impact. Officers are questioning witnesses who were present in the area at the time of the collision to determine the speed of the vehicle and the traffic conditions at that hour.

Forensic teams have examined the luxury car to assess technical data that might explain the circumstances of the accident. The identity of the deceased is being verified, and their family has been notified of the tragedy.

  • The vehicle involved is a high-end Aston Martin.
  • The incident took place in the vicinity of Inorbit Mall.
  • Legal proceedings are underway against the driver.

The police department is expected to release a detailed statement once the preliminary investigation is completed. The vehicle has been impounded as part of the evidence-gathering process.

Observers are now awaiting the outcome of the forensic analysis and the formal charges that will be presented before the local magistrate as the legal process moves forward.
