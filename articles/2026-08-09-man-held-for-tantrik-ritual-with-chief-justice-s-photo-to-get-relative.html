Man Held for Tantrik Ritual With Chief Justice's Photo to Get Relative Bail

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • The man was arrested in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh
  • He performed a ritual at a cremation ground using the Chief Justice's photo
  • The act was intended to influence a bail decision for a relative

In a bizarre incident that has left authorities in Chhattisgarh stunned, a man was arrested in Bilaspur for allegedly performing a tantrik ritual using a photograph of the High Court Chief Justice. The ritual was reportedly aimed at securing bail for a relative facing legal trouble.

Police said the man was found at a cremation ground, where he had set up the photograph and was conducting the ceremony. The act came to light after locals noticed the suspicious activity and alerted the authorities.

Ritual at Cremation Ground

The accused, whose identity has not been officially confirmed, was taken into custody on Saturday. According to initial reports, he had placed the Chief Justice's photo at the cremation ground and performed rituals that are traditionally associated with tantric practices.

Sources said the man believed that by performing the ritual, he could influence the judicial process and secure the release of his relative, who is currently in judicial custody. The police have not yet disclosed the nature of the case against the relative.

The use of a cremation ground in such practices is not uncommon in some parts of the country, where superstition often takes precedence over legal recourse. However, this instance has drawn particular attention because it involved the photograph of a sitting judge.

Police Action and Investigation

The Bilaspur police have registered a case against the man under relevant sections of the law. A senior police officer, speaking on condition of anonymity, said that the man was questioned and later released on bail, though the investigation is ongoing.

"We have taken the matter seriously. While the ritual itself may not have any legal standing, the act of using a judge's photograph in such a manner could be seen as an attempt to interfere with the course of justice," the officer said.

The officer added that the police are also examining whether any other individuals were involved in the planning or execution of the ritual. The photograph used in the ceremony has been seized as evidence.

Superstition vs. Legal Process

This incident highlights the deep-rooted superstitions that persist in some sections of society, even when dealing with formal institutions like the judiciary. Legal experts point out that such actions have no bearing on the outcome of a case, which is decided solely on the merits of evidence and law.

"It is unfortunate that people resort to such measures instead of trusting the judicial system. The courts function independently and are not swayed by external influences, whether they are threats or rituals," said a local lawyer who preferred not to be named.

Cases of individuals attempting to use occult practices to influence outcomes are not new in India. In the past, there have been reports of people performing rituals to get jobs, win elections, or even secure admissions. However, targeting a high-ranking judicial officer is rare and has raised eyebrows across the state.

What's Next

Police officials have indicated that they will conduct a thorough investigation to determine if there was any larger conspiracy behind the act. Meanwhile, the man has been released on bail, and the case is set to be heard in court in the coming weeks.

The Chhattisgarh High Court has not issued any official statement on the matter, and it remains to be seen whether the judiciary will take any cognizance of the incident. For now, the focus remains on ensuring that such acts do not undermine public confidence in the legal system.
