Delhi's Iconic Roshanara Club Reopens After Two-Year Closure

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Lifestyle Original source ↗
Key points
  • The 103-year-old heritage club has resumed operations following a two-year shutdown.
  • Renovations focus on upgrading sports infrastructure and introducing new wellness zones.
  • The club remains a significant landmark in North Delhi's social and historical sphere.

A New Chapter for a Heritage Landmark

The Roshanara Club, one of Delhi’s oldest and most prominent social institutions, has officially reopened its gates. The century-old establishment, which marks 103 years of history this year, resumed operations after remaining shut for the past two years.

Situated in the heart of North Delhi, the club has long been a hub for the city’s elite and sports enthusiasts. The recent reopening marks the end of a long period of uncertainty for its members and the local community.

Modern Upgrades Meet Heritage

Management has focused on a major overhaul during the closure. The facility now boasts revamped sports infrastructure designed to meet contemporary standards while maintaining the club's colonial-era charm.

The upgrades include:

  • Completely renovated sports courts and specialized training areas.
  • New wellness zones catering to fitness and relaxation.
  • Refurbished communal spaces intended for social gatherings.

These changes are part of a broader effort to modernize the club's offerings. By integrating wellness-focused amenities, the management aims to attract a younger demographic while serving its existing long-term members.

The Legacy of Roshanara

Established in the early 20th century, the Roshanara Club is renowned for its lush green surroundings and historical significance. It has served as a center for cricket and other outdoor activities for decades, often hosting prominent figures from Delhi’s social circles.

The two-year closure was a subject of much discussion among residents of the capital, many of whom have deep personal ties to the venue. The return of the club is expected to revitalize the social calendar of North Delhi, restoring a space that has been central to the city's identity for over a century.

Looking Ahead

As the club welcomes members back, attention will shift toward the long-term maintenance of the new facilities. Future updates on membership programs and event schedules are expected to be released by the administration in the coming weeks.
