Sensex surges 400 pts, Nifty tops 24,600 after RBI policy; auto stocks lead

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex gains 400 points, Nifty tops 24,600 after RBI policy announcement.
  • Nifty Auto index hits a fresh all-time high, leading sectoral gains.
  • Market rally broad-based; investors eye RBI stance and global cues.

RBI policy sparks rally

Indian stock markets surged on Wednesday, with the Sensex climbing over 400 points and the Nifty crossing the 24,600 mark, as investors cheered the Reserve Bank of India's policy announcement. The rally was led by auto stocks, with the Nifty Auto index touching a new lifetime high.

The central bank's decision, widely anticipated by the market, provided a fresh impetus to risk-taking. Broader indices also traded higher, reflecting a positive sentiment across sectors.

Auto index at record peak

The Nifty Auto index's climb to a record high stood out, driven by strong buying in key auto counters. Dealers and analysts attributed the move to improved demand outlook and policy clarity.

While specific stock-level moves were not immediately detailed, the sector's outperformance signaled confidence among investors in the auto industry's growth trajectory.

Market breadth and sectoral trends

Gains were not limited to autos; banking and financial stocks also contributed to the Sensex's rise, tracking the RBI's stance. Midcap and smallcap indices mirrored the upward trend, indicating broad-based buying.

Market participants noted that the RBI's commentary on growth and inflation was balanced, which helped calm nerves. Volatility, as measured by India VIX, remained subdued during the session.

What's driving the sentiment

Global cues remained supportive, with Asian markets trading mixed but stable. Foreign institutional investors' activity, along with domestic buying, has been a key driver in recent sessions.

Analysts pointed out that the RBI's policy continuity has reinforced expectations of a stable interest rate environment, which bodes well for equities. However, they advised caution, as global headwinds persist.

Looking ahead

Investors will now parse the RBI's detailed policy statement for future guidance. The market's next trigger could be global inflation data and quarterly earnings from index heavyweights.

Any surprise in the RBI's tone, especially regarding liquidity management, could influence near-term moves. For now, the bulls seem firmly in control, with the Nifty eyeing further upside.
