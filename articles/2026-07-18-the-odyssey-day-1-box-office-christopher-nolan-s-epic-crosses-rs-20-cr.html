The Odyssey Day 1 Box Office: Christopher Nolan's Epic Crosses Rs 20 Crore In India

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The Odyssey collected more than Rs 20 crore nett in India on day one.
  • The film is Christopher Nolan's first period epic, based on Homer's ancient Greek poem.
  • Day one figures position it as one of the year's biggest Hollywood openers in India.

Christopher Nolan's latest film, The Odyssey, has made a thunderous entry at the Indian box office. The epic period drama, based on Homer's ancient Greek poem, collected over Rs 20 crore nett on its opening day across the country.

The figure puts the film in the league of the biggest Hollywood openers in India this year. Trade analysts had predicted a strong start, but the actual numbers have exceeded expectations for a non-franchise, non-superhero film.

A Return to Form for Nolan

Nolan has long been a favourite among Indian audiences. Films like Interstellar, Inception, and Tenet have enjoyed loyal followings here. But The Odyssey marks his first foray into ancient mythology, shot with his signature practical effects and large-format cameras.

The film reportedly used IMAX cameras extensively, though reports from The Hindu suggest that many Indian theatres are not equipped to project the film in its true IMAX format. That did not stop audiences from turning up in large numbers.

Mixed Reactions from Critics

Critical response has been divided. While NDTV called the film 'a home run', Time Magazine described it as 'just another reason for despair'. The Financial Times praised Nolan for 'rowing against the tide' to bring a Hollywood blockbuster home. The Wire noted the director's ambition in tackling an ancient text.

Audience scores on booking platforms remain high, with many praising the visual spectacle and Hans Zimmer's score. Some have questioned the film's pacing and length, which runs close to three hours.

What This Means for the Box Office

The day one collection of over Rs 20 crore puts The Odyssey on track for a strong weekend. If word-of-mouth holds, the film could cross Rs 100 crore in its lifetime run in India. That would make it one of Nolan's highest-grossing films in the country.

However, competition from local releases in the coming weeks could slow its momentum. The film's long runtime also limits the number of daily shows.

The opening weekend numbers will be crucial. Trade insiders are watching to see if the film can sustain its initial burst or if it will follow the pattern of other recent Hollywood epics that faded after a strong start.
