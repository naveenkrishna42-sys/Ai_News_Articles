Explore India's Prominent Ram Temples This Ram Navami 2025

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Religion Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ram Navami 2025 is a key time to visit Ram temples across India.
  • Ayodhya's new Ram Mandir is a major attraction this year.
  • Other significant temples include Rameswaram, Bhadrachalam, and Sitamarhi.
  • Devotees can plan pilgrimages to multiple states for the festival.

Ram Navami, the festival celebrating the birth of Lord Ram, draws millions of devotees to temples across India each year. In 2025, the occasion holds special significance with the newly constructed Ram Mandir in Ayodhya now open to the public. For those planning a pilgrimage, several temples stand out as essential visits during this period.

Ayodhya: The New Ram Mandir Takes Center Stage

The Ram Mandir in Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh, is the focal point of this year's celebrations. Consecrated in January 2024, the temple complex has become a major destination for devotees. On Ram Navami, special rituals and processions are expected to draw large crowds. The temple authorities have made arrangements for darshan and other ceremonies, though officials have not yet confirmed exact crowd estimates for the day.

Ayodhya itself is steeped in Ramayana lore, with several other sites like Hanuman Garhi and Kanak Bhawan also attracting visitors. The city's ghats along the Sarayu River offer a serene setting for evening aartis.

Rameswaram: The Southern Gateway

In Tamil Nadu, the Ramanathaswamy Temple in Rameswaram is another key destination. According to legend, this is where Lord Ram worshipped Shiva after building the bridge to Lanka. The temple is known for its long corridors and 22 theerthams (sacred water bodies). On Ram Navami, devotees perform special abhishekams and offer prayers. The temple is also a key stop on the Char Dham pilgrimage circuit.

Bhadrachalam and Sitamarhi: Regional Celebrations

In Telangana, the Bhadrachalam Temple dedicated to Lord Ram sees grand celebrations. The temple's deity, known as Sita Ramachandra, is adorned with special ornaments during the festival. The town is located on the banks of the Godavari River, adding to its spiritual atmosphere. Officials expect a surge in footfall from Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

In Bihar, the Sitamarhi temple marks the birthplace of Goddess Sita. While it is primarily associated with the goddess, the temple is a significant stop for Ram Navami pilgrims. The town also has other temples linked to the Ramayana, making it a complete pilgrimage experience.

Other Notable Temples

Devotees can also visit the Ram Raja Temple in Orchha, Madhya Pradesh, where Lord Ram is worshipped as a king. The temple's architecture blends Rajput and Mughal styles. In Karnataka, the Kodandarama Temple in Bangalore and the Ramachandra Temple in Hampi offer quieter alternatives for those seeking a less crowded experience.

In Maharashtra, the Ram Temple in Dehu near Pune is popular among followers of the saint Tukaram. The temple's location along the Indrayani River provides a calm backdrop for prayers.

Travelers should book accommodations well in advance, as hotels and guesthouses near major temples fill up quickly during Ram Navami. Local authorities often arrange special transport services for the festival.

As Ram Navami 2025 approaches, these temples are preparing to welcome thousands of devotees. The festival offers a chance to explore India's diverse cultural and spiritual heritage, with each temple providing a unique glimpse into the Ramayana tradition.
