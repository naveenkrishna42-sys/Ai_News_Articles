Kala Hiran makers challenge Delhi HC order in Supreme Court

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Producers of Kala Hiran challenge Delhi HC order in Supreme Court
  • Delhi HC ordered removal of movie's teaser due to 'objectionable content'
  • Supreme Court to hear the petition on the matter

The Legal Battle Over Kala Hiran's Teaser

The producers of the upcoming Bollywood film, Kala Hiran, have filed a petition in the Supreme Court of India against the Delhi High Court's order to remove the movie's teaser. The Delhi High Court had earlier directed the removal of the teaser due to its 'objectionable content'.

Background of the Case

The controversy surrounding Kala Hiran's teaser began when it was posted on various social media platforms. The teaser, which has been widely shared, has been accused of depicting 'objectionable content' by some sections of society. The Delhi High Court was approached by these sections, leading to the order to remove the teaser.

Supreme Court's Role

The Supreme Court will now hear the petition filed by the producers of Kala Hiran. The court will decide whether the Delhi High Court's order to remove the teaser is justified. The producers argue that the teaser is a part of the promotional campaign for the movie and should not be removed without a proper legal process.

Implications of the Case

The outcome of this case could set a precedent for similar cases in the future. It will also determine the balance between the freedom of expression and the sensitivity of certain topics. The case will be closely watched by the film industry and the legal fraternity.
