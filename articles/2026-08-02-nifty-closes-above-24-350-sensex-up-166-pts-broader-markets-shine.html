Nifty closes above 24,350, Sensex up 166 pts; broader markets shine

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nifty settled above 24,350, gaining for the session
  • Sensex rose 166 points, led by banking and IT stocks
  • Broader markets outperformed benchmarks, with midcap and smallcap indices posting stronger gains

Indian equity benchmarks closed higher on Friday, extending the week's gains, as the Nifty settled above the 24,350 mark and the Sensex advanced 166 points. The session saw broad-based buying, with the broader markets outperforming the frontline indices.

Market breadth and sectoral trends

The Nifty ended the day at 24,352.40, up 0.45 per cent, while the Sensex closed at 79,802.62, a gain of 166 points. The midcap and smallcap indices rose over 0.7 per cent each, signalling robust investor appetite beyond the large-cap names.

Sectorally, banking and financial services led the charge, with the Nifty Bank index climbing 0.6 per cent. IT stocks also contributed, while metal and pharma counters saw mixed movement.

Top movers and laggards

Among the top gainers on the Nifty were Bajaj Finserv, Hindalco, and Tata Motors, each rising over 1.5 per cent. On the losing side, Sun Pharma, NTPC, and Asian Paints slipped, capping the index's upside.

Broader market participation was healthy, with advancing shares outpacing decliners on the BSE. Market breadth was positive, with nearly 2,400 stocks advancing against about 1,100 declining.

What drove the sentiment

Positive global cues, particularly from the US and European markets, provided a tailwind. Investors also took comfort from declining crude oil prices, which eased concerns over inflation and its impact on domestic consumption.

Foreign institutional investors were net buyers in the cash market, while domestic institutions also supported the rally. Provisional data from exchanges showed FII inflows of around Rs 1,200 crore, while DIIs bought equities worth Rs 850 crore.

Risks and watch-outs

Despite the positive close, analysts remain cautious about elevated valuations in select pockets. The upcoming release of the monthly auto sales data and the US jobs report could influence market direction in the near term.

Volatility index India VIX eased 2 per cent, indicating a slight cooling of investor anxiety. However, the impending monsoon session of Parliament and any unexpected geopolitical developments remain key monitorables.

Market participants are also tracking the progress of the monsoon, which has been uneven so far, as it could impact rural demand and inflation expectations. A firm monsoon would support agricultural output and consumer spending, aiding broader market sentiment.

Outlook for the coming sessions

Analysts expect the market to consolidate in the near term, with Nifty likely to find support around the 24,000-24,100 zone. A sustained close above 24,500 would open the door for fresh highs, they add.

Traders are advised to watch global cues and sectoral rotation. With earnings season largely behind, the focus shifts to macro data and policy signals from the Reserve Bank of India's next meeting.

The coming week will see the release of manufacturing and services PMI data, which will provide further clarity on the domestic economic momentum. Investors will also parse the US non-farm payrolls for clues on the Federal Reserve's rate trajectory.
