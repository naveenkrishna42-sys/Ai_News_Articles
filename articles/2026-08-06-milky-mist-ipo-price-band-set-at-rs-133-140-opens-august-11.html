Milky Mist IPO price band set at Rs 133-140, opens August 11

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Price band fixed at Rs 133-140 per share
  • Issue opens August 11, targets Rs 1,553 crore
  • Valuation pegged at Rs 10,778 crore (about $1.13 billion)
  • Temasek-backed dairy firm enters public markets
  • IPO seen as a trimmed offering amid market conditions

Issue Details and Timeline

Dairy major Milky Mist has set the price band for its initial public offering at Rs 133-140 per share, with the issue opening on August 11. The company aims to raise Rs 1,553 crore through the offer, which will close for subscription later in the week.

At the upper end of the price band, the company is valued at Rs 10,778 crore, roughly translating to $1.13 billion. The IPO is backed by Temasek, the Singaporean state investment firm, which holds a significant stake in the dairy player.

The issue comprises a fresh issue of equity shares along with an offer for sale by existing shareholders. Proceeds from the fresh issue are earmarked for expansion of manufacturing capacity, debt repayment, and general corporate purposes.

Company Profile and Market Position

Milky Mist is one of India's leading dairy product manufacturers, known for its paneer, curd, butter, and cheese products. The company has built a strong distribution network across southern and western India, with a growing presence in other regions.

The dairy sector in India remains highly fragmented, with organized players accounting for a fraction of the overall market. Milky Mist has carved out a niche in value-added dairy products, which command higher margins compared to liquid milk.

Its product portfolio includes over 100 SKUs, and the company has been expanding its cold chain infrastructure to support distribution. The IPO comes at a time when consumer staples companies are witnessing steady demand, though input costs for dairy remain volatile.

IPO Structure and Investor Interest

Market observers note that the company has trimmed its IPO size from earlier expectations, reflecting cautious sentiment in the primary market. The offer includes a reservation for qualified institutional buyers, non-institutional investors, and retail investors, with the latter category getting up to 35% of the net offer.

Anchor investors are expected to be roped in a day before the issue opens, a standard practice for large IPOs. The company's book-running lead managers will handle the issue, with shares likely to list on both BSE and NSE.

Analysts will be watching the subscription numbers closely, especially from retail investors, given the price band's affordability. The company's valuation of about 28-30 times its annualized earnings is seen as competitive compared to listed peers in the dairy space.

Market Context and Outlook

The IPO pipeline has been active in 2026, with several consumer and food companies tapping the market. Milky Mist's offering is among the more closely watched, given its brand recall and Temasek's backing.

Investors are also assessing the company's ability to sustain growth amid rising competition from both organized players like Amul and regional dairies. The company has reported consistent revenue growth over the past three fiscals, though profit margins have been under pressure due to higher milk procurement costs.

Post-listing, the stock's performance will hinge on quarterly results and management commentary on capacity expansion. The dairy major's ability to scale its value-added product segment will be a key metric for long-term investors.

What to Watch Next

The subscription window will open on August 11, and the final allotment is expected later in the month. The listing date will be announced once the issue closes, with market participants keen to see the debut premium. Updates on anchor investor participation and over-subscription numbers will provide early signals on demand.
