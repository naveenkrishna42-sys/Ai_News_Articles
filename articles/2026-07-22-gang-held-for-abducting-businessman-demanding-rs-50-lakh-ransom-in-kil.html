Gang held for abducting businessman, demanding Rs 50 lakh ransom in Kilpauk

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • A gang kidnapped a businessman from Kilpauk, Chennai, and demanded Rs 50 lakh ransom.
  • Police arrested the gang members and rescued the victim unharmed.
  • The incident highlights ongoing concerns about safety in the city.

Gang Apprehended for Businessman Abduction

Chennai police have arrested a gang for allegedly abducting a businessman from the Kilpauk area and demanding a ransom of Rs 50 lakh. The victim was rescued unharmed, officials confirmed on Wednesday.

The incident came to light after the businessman's family received a call demanding the hefty sum for his safe release. They immediately alerted the police, who launched an investigation.

How the Arrest Was Made

Acting on a tip-off, a special police team tracked down the suspects to a hideout on the outskirts of the city. The gang was taken into custody without any resistance, and the victim was found safe at the location.

Police sources said the accused had planned the abduction meticulously, targeting the businessman after monitoring his movements for several days. They had demanded the ransom in cash, threatening harm if the family approached the authorities.

Details of the Accused

The arrested individuals have been identified as residents of Chennai and nearby areas. Their ages and specific roles in the crime are under investigation. Police are also probing whether they have a prior criminal record.

Authorities have not yet disclosed the exact amount of ransom that was paid, if any, or the full extent of the gang's operations. Investigations are ongoing to determine if they were involved in similar crimes in the past.

Victim's Condition and Family Statement

The businessman, whose identity has been withheld for privacy reasons, was reported to be in good health. He was reunited with his family late Tuesday night after being rescued.

Family members expressed relief at his safe return and thanked the police for their swift action. They declined to provide further details, citing the ongoing investigation.

Police Response and Safety Concerns

Chennai police commissioner has assured the public that such crimes will be dealt with firmly. A senior officer stated that patrolling in commercial areas has been increased to prevent similar incidents.

The case has once again raised questions about the safety of businessmen in the city. Local trade associations have called for better security measures, including more CCTV cameras and quicker police response times.

What to Watch

The accused are expected to be produced in court later this week for remand. Police are expected to seek custody to interrogate them further and uncover any larger conspiracy.
