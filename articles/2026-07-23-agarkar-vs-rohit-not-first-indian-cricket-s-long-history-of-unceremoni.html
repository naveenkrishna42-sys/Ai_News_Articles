Agarkar vs Rohit not first: Indian cricket's long history of unceremonious exits

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Reports of a rift between chief selector Ajit Agarkar and captain Rohit Sharma have surfaced.
  • Indian cricket has a history of unceremonious exits for iconic players like Sourav Ganguly and Virat Kohli.
  • The pattern raises questions about how the BCCI manages transitions and player departures.

A New Chapter in an Old Story

The reported tension between chief selector Ajit Agarkar and captain Rohit Sharma is the latest in a long line of uncomfortable exits in Indian cricket. According to multiple media reports, the two have not been on the same page over team selection and captaincy plans. Sources suggest the disagreement runs deeper than usual.

This is not an isolated incident. Indian cricket has a rich history of its biggest stars leaving the stage under a cloud. The pattern is consistent: a legend is eased out, often without a proper farewell.

From Ganguly to Kohli: A Familiar Pattern

Sourav Ganguly's exit in 2005 was perhaps the most dramatic. The then-captain was dropped from the team after a public spat with coach Greg Chappell. Ganguly was at his peak as a batsman, but the selectors pulled the plug. He never captained India again.

Virat Kohli's removal from ODI captaincy in late 2021 was equally abrupt. The BCCI announced the change without prior consultation with Kohli. He later revealed in a press conference that he was informed just an hour before the selection meeting. The public fallout was immense.

Sachin Tendulkar's retirement in 2013 was more graceful, but even he faced hints from the board about stepping down. The message was clear: the selectors, not the player, often decide when a career ends.

What Makes Agarkar vs Rohit Different?

The current situation is unique because Agarkar is a former teammate of Rohit. Both played together in the Indian team. This adds a layer of personal history to the professional friction.

Reports indicate that Agarkar has been pushing for a younger captaincy candidate, while Rohit wants to continue until the 2027 World Cup. The selectors have not publicly commented, but the rumour mill is in overdrive.

R Ashwin, the veteran spinner, recently made a telling statement. He said, "There was fire," referring to the retirement chatter around Rohit. The comment suggests that the pressure on the captain is real.

The Unfinished Business Factor

Rohit Sharma still has unfinished business, according to several former players. He led India to the 2024 T20 World Cup title, but the ODI World Cup eluded him in 2023. Many believe he deserves a proper send-off.

A former India World Cup winner recently dared the selectors to drop Rohit. "Himmat hai toh karo," he said in an interview, challenging the board to take the call. The remark underscores the emotional weight of the decision.

The BCCI has not confirmed any rift. But the silence from the board room is telling. In the past, such silence has preceded a sudden announcement.

For now, the team is preparing for the upcoming series. Rohit remains the captain. But the whispers are getting louder. If history is any guide, the next move will come sooner rather than later.
