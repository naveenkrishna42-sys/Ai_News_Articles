Realme P4s vs iQOO Z11: 2026 Budget Smartphone Showdown

Display and Performance: A Battle of Refresh Rates

The Realme P4s and iQOO Z11 are both mid-range smartphones that offer a high refresh rate display. The Realme P4s has a 144Hz AMOLED display, while the iQOO Z11 has a 120Hz AMOLED display. The higher refresh rate on the Realme P4s means it's better suited for gaming and other high-performance tasks.

In terms of performance, the Realme P4s has a dual chipset, while the iQOO Z11 has a single chipset. The dual chipset on the Realme P4s allows it to handle more demanding tasks more efficiently.

Battery Life: Powering Through the Day

The Realme P4s has an 8,000 mAh battery, while the iQOO Z11 has a 5,000 mAh battery. The larger battery on the Realme P4s means it can last longer between charges. This is a significant advantage, especially for those who use their smartphones heavily throughout the day.

Camera Capabilities: Capturing Moments in Style

Both smartphones come with a 50MP primary camera and a 16MP front camera. The Realme P4s has a slightly larger sensor on its primary camera, which can result in better low-light performance. The iQOO Z11, on the other hand, has a more advanced camera system, which can result in better image quality in well-lit conditions.

Value for Money: Which Smartphone Offers the Best Deal?

When it comes to value for money, the Realme P4s and iQOO Z11 are both strong contenders. The Realme P4s offers a larger battery and a higher refresh rate display, while the iQOO Z11 has a more advanced camera system and a single chipset that is more power-efficient.

Ultimately, the best smartphone for you will depend on your individual needs and preferences. If you prioritize battery life and a high refresh rate display, the Realme P4s is the better choice. If you prioritize camera performance and power efficiency, the iQOO Z11 is the better choice.
