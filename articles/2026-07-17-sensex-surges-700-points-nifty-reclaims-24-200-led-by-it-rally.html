Sensex Surges 700 Points, Nifty Reclaims 24,200 Led by IT Rally

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex surged nearly 700 points, Nifty reclaimed 24,200 level.
  • IT stocks led rally with HCL Tech, Tech Mahindra, and Jio Financial among top gainers.
  • RIL also contributed to gains, boosting market sentiment.
  • Broader market participation was strong, with most sectoral indices in green.

Indian equity benchmarks opened Friday on a strong footing and extended gains through the day, with the Sensex surging nearly 700 points and the Nifty reclaiming the 24,200 mark. The rally was broad-based, led by information technology stocks and supported by index heavyweights like Reliance Industries.

IT Stocks Lead Charge

The Nifty IT index was the top sectoral gainer, rising over 3 percent. HCL Technologies was among the frontrunners, surging more than 5 percent after the company reported strong quarterly earnings and raised its revenue guidance. Tech Mahindra and Infosys also gained over 3 percent each.

Jio Financial Services, the newly listed entity, was another major contributor, jumping nearly 8 percent in early trade. The stock has been volatile since its listing but found strong buying interest today.

Reliance Industries Adds Muscle

Reliance Industries (RIL) rose nearly 2 percent, providing significant support to the benchmarks. The oil-to-telecom conglomerate's stock has been in focus amid expectations of strong performance from its retail and digital services arms. Analysts also cited positive global cues and easing crude oil prices as tailwinds for RIL.

The broader market mirrored the rally, with the BSE Midcap and Smallcap indices gaining over 1 percent each. More than 2,000 stocks advanced on the BSE, compared to around 800 declines, indicating broad participation.

Global Cues and Sectoral Performance

Positive global cues also aided sentiment. Overnight gains on Wall Street, driven by strong US economic data and easing inflation concerns, provided a supportive backdrop. Asian markets traded higher, with Japan's Nikkei and Hong Kong's Hang Seng both up over 1 percent.

Among other sectoral indices, banking stocks were mixed. The Nifty Bank index was up 0.5 percent, with HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank trading in the green. However, public sector banks lagged, with SBI and PNB seeing marginal declines. Auto stocks also saw buying interest, with Maruti Suzuki and Tata Motors gaining over 1 percent each.

Technical Outlook and Market View

From a technical perspective, the Nifty's move above 24,200 is seen as a bullish signal. Analysts noted that the index has formed a strong bullish candle on the daily charts, indicating sustained buying momentum. The immediate resistance is now seen at 24,350, while support is at 24,000.

Market participants will now watch for further triggers, including corporate earnings announcements and global macroeconomic data. The rally in IT stocks suggests that the sector is regaining favour after a period of underperformance.

Looking ahead, investors will closely monitor the upcoming US Federal Reserve meeting and domestic inflation data for further direction. The Nifty's ability to hold above 24,200 will be key for sustaining the upward momentum in the near term.
