Atif Aslam on India ban: 'I miss you guys', fans listen over VPN

  • He notes fans are still listening to his music over VPN

Pakistani playback singer Atif Aslam has broken his silence on the decade-long ban that has kept him from performing in India. In a recent interaction, the singer expressed affection for his Indian fanbase, saying, "I miss you guys," while also acknowledging the unexpected silver lining of the restriction.

The ban, which has been in place for ten years, has been a topic of speculation and debate among fans on both sides of the border. Atif's latest comments, reported by The Times of India and other outlets, come as a candid reflection on how the professional setback reshaped his perspective.

A Message of Gratitude

In a surprising twist, Atif Aslam thanked those responsible for the ban, suggesting that the restrictions pushed him to explore new creative and professional avenues. "Thanks to those who banned me," he said, crediting the forced break for helping him discover aspects of his craft he might otherwise have overlooked.

The singer, known for hits like 'Tera Hone Laga Hoon' and 'Pehli Nazar Mein', clarified that he does not miss working in India. This statement, while seemingly contradictory to his earlier expression of missing fans, underscores a nuanced stance: a separation between the industry's political barriers and the emotional connection with listeners.

Fans Over VPN

Atif Aslam was quick to point out that despite the geographical and political restrictions, his music continues to reach Indian audiences through digital means. "Fans are listening over VPN," he remarked, highlighting the loopholes fans use to access his work. This admission reflects the persistent demand for his music in India, where his songs remain popular on streaming platforms and social media.

The singer's comments have sparked reactions online, with many fans expressing hope that the ban might eventually be lifted. However, Atif's tone suggested a resigned acceptance of the current situation, even as he acknowledged the workaround.

Context of the Ban

The ban on Pakistani artists in India has been in effect since 2016, following heightened political tensions between the two nations. Atif Aslam, who enjoyed massive popularity in Bollywood, was among the most affected artists. His last major Indian release was the title track for 'Ae Dil Hai Mushkil', which received widespread acclaim despite the restrictions.

Over the years, several Pakistani artists have voiced their displeasure over the ban, but Atif's latest statement stands out for its mix of defiance and warmth. By thanking those who banned him, he turns the narrative on its head, suggesting that the restriction may have inadvertently benefited his career.

While his music continues to thrive in Pakistan and other international markets, the Indian audience remains a significant part of his fanbase.
