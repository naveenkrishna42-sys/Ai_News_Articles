Supreme Court Verdict

The Supreme Court has quashed the FIRs against comedian Samay Raina and four other comedians over their remarks about disabled persons. The court has dismissed the criminal proceedings against them, stating that their comments did not constitute a criminal offence.

The FIRs were filed against the comedians for allegedly making insensitive remarks about disabled persons. However, the Supreme Court has appreciated the efforts of the comedians, describing them as 'very bright youngsters'. The court's decision is seen as a significant move in protecting the freedom of speech and expression in the country.
