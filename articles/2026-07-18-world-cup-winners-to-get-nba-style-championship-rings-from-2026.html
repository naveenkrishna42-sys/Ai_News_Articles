World Cup winners to get NBA-style championship rings from 2026

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • FIFA will award championship rings to World Cup winners starting 2026.
  • The rings are inspired by the NBA tradition, marking a cultural shift.
  • The 2026 World Cup final will be held in the US, with Donald Trump expected to attend.

FIFA has announced that from the 2026 World Cup onwards, the winning team will receive US-style championship rings, a tradition borrowed from the NBA. The decision was confirmed by the global football body on Friday, marking a significant departure from the sport’s long-standing trophy and medal ceremonies.

NBA inspiration for football

The rings are similar to those awarded to winners in American professional basketball. Each player and coach is expected to receive a custom-made ring, often featuring the team’s logo, the tournament year, and the player’s name. FIFA officials said the move is part of a broader effort to “celebrate the champions in a unique way” and to align with the host nation’s sporting culture.

The 2026 World Cup will be co-hosted by the United States, Canada, and Mexico, with the final scheduled to take place at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey. This is the first time the tournament will be staged across three countries.

Donald Trump to attend final

US President Donald Trump is expected to attend the final, a source familiar with the planning told Al Jazeera. Neither the White House nor FIFA has officially confirmed his presence. Trump has previously expressed interest in attending major sporting events during his term.

The decision to introduce rings comes amid growing commercial ties between FIFA and US-based sponsors. Critics argue the move prioritises spectacle over tradition. Supporters say it adds a new layer of prestige for players who have already won the sport’s ultimate prize.

Reaction from players and fans

Reactions have been mixed. Some former World Cup winners questioned whether rings could ever match the emotional weight of lifting the trophy. Others welcomed the change, pointing out that medals are often lost or forgotten, while rings are worn and displayed.

FIFA has not yet revealed the design or estimated cost of the rings. A tender process for manufacturers is expected to begin later this year. The rings will be in addition to the traditional gold medals and the original FIFA World Cup Trophy, which remains the primary prize.

The 2026 tournament will feature 48 teams for the first time, up from 32. The expanded format has already sparked debate over scheduling and player workload.

What happens next

FIFA will release more details about the ring design and selection process in the coming months. The 2026 World Cup is set to begin in June 2026, with the final taking place on July 19. The rings will be presented immediately after the trophy ceremony.
