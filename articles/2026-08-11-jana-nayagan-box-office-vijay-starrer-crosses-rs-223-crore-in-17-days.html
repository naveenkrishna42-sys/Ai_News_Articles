Jana Nayagan Box Office: Vijay Starrer Crosses Rs 223 Crore in 17 Days

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jana Nayagan collected Rs 7.25 crore in its third weekend, pushing the 17-day total to Rs 223 crore.
  • The film leads over Suriya's Karuppu, becoming the highest-grossing Tamil film of 2026 so far.
  • Weekend growth of 41% indicates strong hold in Tamil Nadu and overseas markets.

Third Weekend Steady Despite Dip

Thalapathy Vijay's Jana Nayagan continues its box office march, adding Rs 7.25 crore in its third weekend. The film's 17-day domestic total now stands at Rs 223 crore, according to early trade estimates.

The third weekend numbers mark a predictable slowdown from the opening frenzy, but the drop is shallower than many expected. Trade analysts point to strong word-of-mouth in urban centres and repeat viewings from Vijay's fan base.

Outpacing Karuppu in 2026 Race

The film has taken a clear lead over Suriya's Karuppu in the battle for the top Tamil grosser of 2026. Industry trackers say Jana Nayagan has now surpassed Karuppu's lifetime collections, making it the highest-grossing Tamil film of the year so far.

That position matters more than ever, with both films releasing within weeks of each other. The gap widened after Jana Nayagan recorded 41% weekend growth over its second Friday-to-Sunday period, a sign of sustained audience interest.

What's Driving the Numbers

Pooja Hegde's presence and a commercially packaged script have helped the film hold screens across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and overseas circuits. The overseas market, in particular, has contributed a significant chunk of the total gross.

Distributors note that the film's advance bookings for the fourth weekend remain healthy, especially in multiplexes. Single-screen occupancy has dipped slightly, but the overall trend points to a smooth run until the next big release arrives.

Context: Vijay's Final Stretch

This is among the last few releases featuring Vijay before his planned break from acting to focus on politics. That backdrop has added an emotional pull for audiences, with many treating the theatrical run as a farewell event.

While reviews have been mixed, the film's box office resilience suggests that star power, not critical consensus, is driving footfalls. The makers have not yet announced any official worldwide gross figure for day 19, but trade estimates suggest the film is nearing Rs 320 crore globally.

What to Watch Next

All eyes are now on the fourth weekend, where the film will face competition from a new Telugu release. Whether Jana Nayagan can sustain its lead and cross the Rs 250 crore domestic mark will determine its final standing in Tamil cinema's all-time list.
