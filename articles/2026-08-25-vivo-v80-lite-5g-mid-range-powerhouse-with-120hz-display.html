Vivo V80 Lite 5G: Specs and Features

Vivo has launched its latest mid-range smartphone, the V80 Lite 5G, in India. The device comes with a 120Hz curved AMOLED display, a 7,050mAh battery, and a 50MP dual rear camera setup. The phone is powered by the Snapdragon 7 Gen 1 chipset and runs on Android 14 with Vivo's custom FunTouch OS 14.

Display and Performance

The V80 Lite 5G features a 6.78-inch Full HD+ AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate and a 1,000 nits peak brightness. The display is curved on the sides, which should provide an immersive viewing experience. The phone is powered by the Snapdragon 7 Gen 1 chipset, which is expected to deliver good performance for mid-range devices.

Camera Setup

The V80 Lite 5G comes with a 50MP primary rear camera, an 8MP ultra-wide-angle camera, and a 2MP macro camera. The phone also features a 32MP front camera for selfies and video calls. The camera setup should be capable of capturing good-quality images in most lighting conditions.

Battery and Charging

The V80 Lite 5G packs a 7,050mAh battery, which should provide good battery life for a mid-range device. The phone supports 44W fast charging, which should allow for quick charging. However, it's worth noting that some markets may get a version of the V80 Lite 5G with a 10,000mAh battery, such as the one launching in Malaysia on 3 September.

Market Variants and Global Launch

It's interesting to note that the battery size of the V80 Lite 5G varies by market. While the Indian version comes with a 7,050mAh battery, the Malaysian version is expected to have a 10,000mAh battery. This suggests that Vivo is tailoring its devices to meet the specific needs of different markets.

Conclusion

The Vivo V80 Lite 5G is a solid mid-range smartphone that offers a good balance of features and performance. The 120Hz display, 50MP cameras, and 44W fast charging make it a compelling option for users looking for a feature-packed device. However, the varying battery sizes across markets and the lack of an IP rating could be points of consideration for some users.
