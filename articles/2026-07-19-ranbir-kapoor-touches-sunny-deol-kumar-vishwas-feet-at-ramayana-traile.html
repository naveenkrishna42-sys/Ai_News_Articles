Ranbir Kapoor touches Sunny Deol, Kumar Vishwas' feet at Ramayana trailer launch

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ranbir Kapoor touched the feet of Sunny Deol and Kumar Vishwas at the Pratham Sankalp event for Ramayana.
  • Arun Govil, who played Lord Ram in the TV series, confirmed he will play Raja Dasharath in Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana.
  • Ranbir expressed his desire to carry forward the legacy of Arun Govil as Lord Ram.

Mumbai: A moment of humility and tradition unfolded at the trailer launch of the upcoming film 'Ramayana' in Delhi. Actor Ranbir Kapoor, who plays Lord Ram in the Nitesh Tiwari-directed epic, was seen touching the feet of veteran actor Sunny Deol and poet-politician Kumar Vishwas at the Pratham Sankalp event.

The gesture drew applause from the audience, marking a significant moment of respect from the younger star towards the seasoned artistes. Deol, known for his role in the 'Ramayana' TV series, and Vishwas, a well-known figure in Indian poetry and politics, were present at the event to support the film.

Arun Govil's return to the Ramayana universe

In a separate development, actor Arun Govil, who played Lord Ram in the iconic television series, has confirmed his involvement in the film. Speaking at the trailer launch, Govil revealed that he will essay the role of Raja Dasharath, Lord Ram's father.

"I kept thinking about it on the sets," Govil said, reflecting on the transition from playing Lord Ram to now portraying his father. He expressed confidence in Ranbir Kapoor's ability to carry forward the legacy of the character. Govil's casting has been widely appreciated by fans of the original series.

Ranbir Kapoor's vision for Lord Ram

At the event, Ranbir Kapoor spoke about his approach to the role. He acknowledged the responsibility of portraying a deity revered by millions and expressed his hope to take forward the legacy set by Arun Govil. "It is a huge honour to step into the shoes of Lord Ram after Arun Govil ji," Kapoor said.

The actor also praised the film's director, Nitesh Tiwari, for his vision and commitment to staying true to the epic's essence. Kapoor's performance has been one of the most anticipated aspects of the film.

What to watch next

The film 'Ramayana' is currently in production, with the makers yet to announce an official release date. Industry insiders suggest that the film's first look and teaser may be unveiled in the coming months.
