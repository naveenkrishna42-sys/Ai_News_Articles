Card Features and Benefits

The Samsung-Axis Visa card comes with several features and benefits designed to enhance the user experience. Here are some of the key highlights:

Cashback Offers

The most notable feature of this card is the 10% cashback on Samsung products and services. This includes purchases made through Samsung's official website, authorized retailers, and Samsung Experience Stores. The cashback is credited to the user's account on a quarterly basis, providing a steady stream of rewards.

For high spenders, the card offers an elite rewards program. Users can earn additional rewards points on every purchase, which can be redeemed for a variety of benefits, including discounts on Samsung products, freebies, and more.
