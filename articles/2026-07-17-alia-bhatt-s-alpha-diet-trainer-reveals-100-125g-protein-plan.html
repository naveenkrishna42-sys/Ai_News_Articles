Alia Bhatt's 'Alpha' diet: Trainer reveals 100-125g protein plan

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Alia Bhatt consumes 100-125g protein daily for muscle strength
  • Trainer Karan Sawhney emphasizes protein-rich foods like eggs, chicken, fish, and paneer
  • Diet is part of 'Alpha' training to build lean muscle and endurance

Alia Bhatt’s fitness transformation for her upcoming film ‘Alpha’ has sparked curiosity, with her celebrity trainer Karan Sawhney now revealing the details of her high-protein diet. The actress reportedly consumes 100 to 125 grams of protein every day to support her intensive strength training regimen.

Trainer breaks down the protein plan

Sawhney, who has been guiding Bhatt through her preparation, shared that the diet is built around lean protein sources. Eggs, chicken, fish, paneer, and plant-based options like lentils and tofu form the core of her meals. The goal is to repair muscle tissue and sustain energy for gruelling workout sessions.

The trainer explained that the high-protein intake is paired with strength training exercises, including weighted squats, deadlifts, and resistance band work. Bhatt has also incorporated a variation of squat jumps called the ‘resistance twist,’ which adds a rotational element to the exercise.

Why 100-125g of protein matters

For a woman of Bhatt’s build, 100-125g of protein is significantly higher than the average recommended dietary allowance. Nutrition experts say such levels are typical for athletes or actors undergoing body recomposition for a role. The protein helps in reducing body fat while preserving muscle mass, a key requirement for the physically demanding stunts in ‘Alpha’.

Sawhney emphasised that the diet is not about starvation but about strategic eating. He has reportedly divided Bhatt’s meals into five to six small portions a day, ensuring a steady supply of amino acids to the muscles. Carbohydrates and healthy fats are not eliminated but are timed around workouts.

What the ‘Alpha’ training involves

The training protocol, called ‘Alpha,’ is designed to build functional strength and explosive power. It combines compound lifts with plyometric moves. Bhatt has been seen performing deadlifts with heavy weights and resistance band exercises that target the glutes and core.

Her trainer has also shared videos of her doing squat jumps with a resistance band, adding a twist at the peak of the jump. This move, he says, engages the obliques and improves rotational stability, which is crucial for action sequences.

Comparisons with other transformations

Reports have drawn parallels between Bhatt’s diet and that of other actors who have undergone similar transformations. Sharvari Wagh, who is also working on a physically demanding role, has been following a comparable high-protein regimen. Fitness experts note that such diets require careful monitoring to avoid kidney strain, and trainers usually adjust protein sources based on individual tolerance.

Sawhney has not disclosed the exact caloric intake or supplement usage, but he has confirmed that whole foods are prioritised over powders. He has also stressed the importance of hydration and sleep in the recovery process.

Alia Bhatt’s upcoming film ‘Alpha’ is expected to showcase her new physique and action skills. The production team has not yet announced a release date.

Fans and fitness enthusiasts are now watching to see if the actress will maintain the regimen post-release or shift to a maintenance plan. Sawhney has hinted that the diet will evolve once the shooting wraps, but the focus on strength will remain.
