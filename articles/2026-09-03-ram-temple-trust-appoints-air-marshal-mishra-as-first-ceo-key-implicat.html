Background and Context

On September 2, 2026, the Ram Temple Trust announced the appointment of Air Marshal Jeetendra Mishra (Retd.) as its first Chief Executive Officer. This appointment marks a significant milestone in the governance and management of the Ram Mandir project in Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh. The trust, which was established to oversee the construction and development of the temple, has now taken a crucial step towards ensuring the project's timely completion and adherence to its vision.

Air Marshal Jeetendra Mishra (Retd.)

Air Marshal Jeetendra Mishra (Retd.) is a seasoned military officer with a distinguished career spanning over three decades. He retired from the Indian Air Force in 2024 after serving in various high-profile positions, including Commandant of the Indian Air Force Academy and Director of the National Institute of Military Orthopedics and Traumatology. His military background brings a unique blend of strategic planning, project management, and leadership skills to the Ram Temple Trust.

As the first CEO of the Ram Temple Trust, Air Marshal Mishra will be responsible for overseeing the construction and development of the Ram Mandir. His key responsibilities will include:
