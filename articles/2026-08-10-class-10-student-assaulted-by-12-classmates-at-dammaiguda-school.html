Class 10 student assaulted by 12 classmates at Dammaiguda school

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • A Class 10 student was allegedly assaulted by 12 classmates at a private school in Dammaiguda.
  • The victim's family lodged a complaint with the local police, who have registered a case.
  • School authorities have not yet issued a public statement; investigation is ongoing.

Incident at Dammaiguda school

A Class 10 student was allegedly assaulted by 12 of his peers inside a private school in Dammaiguda on Monday. The incident has sparked outrage among parents and locals, who gathered outside the school demanding action.

According to police, the victim was attacked by his classmates during school hours. The reason for the assault is not yet clear, and officials have not confirmed the sequence of events leading up to the incident.

Police complaint and FIR

The victim's family lodged a complaint at the local police station later in the day. Based on the complaint, police registered a case against 12 students under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code and the Juvenile Justice Act, as the accused are minors.

A senior police officer said that a probe has been initiated and statements of the victim, witnesses, and school staff are being recorded. The accused students have been identified, but no arrests have been made so far, given their age.

School response and parent protests

Parents of other students staged a protest outside the school, demanding strict action against the accused and better safety measures on campus. The school management has yet to release an official statement, but sources say a disciplinary committee has been formed to look into the matter.

Local education department officials have also been alerted. They are expected to visit the school in the coming days to review the situation and ensure compliance with safety norms for students.

Legal and psychological implications

Since all those involved are minors, the case will be handled by the Juvenile Justice Board. Legal experts point out that while the juvenile system focuses on reform, repeat offences or severe injuries could lead to stricter measures.

The victim is reported to be in stable condition and receiving medical care. Counselors have been suggested to help him cope with the trauma, though the school has not yet confirmed whether such support has been arranged.

This incident adds to growing concerns about bullying in schools across Telangana. In recent years, several cases of student-on-student violence have been reported, prompting the state government to issue guidelines on anti-bullying measures, but implementation remains uneven.

What next

Police are expected to submit a preliminary report to the Juvenile Justice Board within a week. The school's disciplinary action and the board's decision on the accused will be key to watch, as parents demand a safe environment for all students.
