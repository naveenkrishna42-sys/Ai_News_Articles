Russia's Central Bank Proposes Crypto Trading Framework

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Central bank proposes framework for public trading of major cryptocurrencies
  • Move signals a shift from previous restrictive stance on crypto
  • Regulated exchanges would facilitate trading under strict oversight

Russia's central bank has proposed a framework that would permit the public trading of major cryptocurrencies, a move that signals a notable shift in the country's approach to digital assets. The proposal, reported by The Moscow Times, outlines a pathway for regulated exchanges to facilitate trading in what the bank deems established and widely recognised cryptocurrencies.

A Change in Stance

The central bank, which has historically maintained a cautious and often restrictive position on cryptocurrencies, appears to be recalibrating its strategy. The proposed framework is designed to bring crypto trading out of the grey market and onto officially sanctioned platforms, where transactions could be monitored and controlled.

This marks a departure from previous years when the bank advocated for a near-total ban on crypto operations. The shift suggests that Russian financial authorities are now looking to integrate digital assets into the formal economy, albeit under strict regulatory supervision.

Key Elements of the Proposal

Under the proposed rules, only certain major cryptocurrencies—likely those with substantial market capitalisation and trading volumes—would be eligible for public trading. The framework would require trading to occur through licensed exchanges that adhere to rigorous compliance standards, including anti-money laundering (AML) and know-your-customer (KYC) protocols.

The central bank has not disclosed the specific list of cryptocurrencies that would qualify, nor has it set a timeline for implementation. Officials have not yet confirmed how the framework would interact with existing crypto-related laws, which include restrictions on using digital currencies as payment.

Context and Implications

The proposal comes amid ongoing global debates over cryptocurrency regulation. In Russia, where crypto mining and trading have flourished in recent years despite legal ambiguities, this move could provide clarity for market participants. It may also serve to channel tax revenues from crypto activities that currently operate outside official oversight.

Industry observers note that the framework, if adopted, could boost confidence among Russian investors who have been wary of legal repercussions. However, the central bank's insistence on tight controls suggests that the emphasis remains on risk mitigation rather than unfettered adoption.

What to Watch

The proposal is now subject to public consultation and likely legislative review. The central bank's next steps will be closely watched, particularly regarding which cryptocurrencies make the cut and how the exchanges will be regulated. The outcome could set a precedent for other countries exploring similar regulated crypto markets.
