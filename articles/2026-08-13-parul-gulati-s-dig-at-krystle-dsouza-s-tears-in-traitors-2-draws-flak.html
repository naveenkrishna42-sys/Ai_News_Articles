Parul Gulati's Dig at Krystle Dsouza's Tears in Traitors 2 Draws Flak

  • Parul Gulati mocked Krystle Dsouza's emotional moment in The Traitors 2
  • Gulati's comment 'Man She Is A TV ACTRESS' drew widespread criticism
  • Netizens slammed Gulati for belittling a fellow contestant's emotions

The Traitors 2 has barely begun, but the drama has already spilled beyond the castle walls. A comment by actress Parul Gulati about fellow contestant Krystle Dsouza has ignited a social media firestorm, with netizens calling out what they see as unnecessary mockery.

What Parul Said

During an intense moment in the show, Krystle Dsouza was seen breaking down in tears. Parul Gulati, watching the scene unfold, reportedly quipped: 'Man She Is A TV ACTRESS..' — a remark that seemed to dismiss Dsouza's emotional display as performative.

The comment, which surfaced online, did not sit well with viewers. Many felt Gulati was belittling Dsouza's genuine feelings, reducing a vulnerable moment to a mere acting gig.

Netizens React

Social media platforms quickly filled with criticism. Users accused Gulati of being insensitive and condescending. One tweet read: "Being a TV actress doesn't make her emotions fake. This is exactly the kind of elitism that needs to stop." Another wrote: "Parul should know better. Crying on a reality show isn't a crime."

The backlash has been swift and sharp, with many demanding an apology from Gulati. Some, however, defended her, arguing that reality shows often involve exaggerated reactions for camera time.

The Traitors 2: High Emotions, High Stakes

The second season of The Traitors has already promised a rollercoaster of emotions. The trailer shows accusations, breakdowns, and deception taking centre stage. Mallika Sherawat, Krystle D'Souza, Shweta Tiwari, and Rida Tharana are seen crying amid palace politics, setting the tone for a tense season.

Adding to the drama, Shweta Tiwari made a explosive claim on the show, revealing that she caught her ex-husbands Raja Chaudhary and Abhinav Kohli cheating on her. Rhea Chakraborty has also issued a 'Bhed Chaal' warning, hinting at betrayal and shifting alliances.

The Bigger Picture

This incident highlights a recurring issue in reality TV: the fine line between playful banter and outright disrespect. While contestants are often pushed to their limits, public perception can turn quickly when comments appear to cross the line.

For Gulati, the fallout could have professional implications. Brands and producers are watching how she handles the controversy. A public apology might help, but the damage to her image may already be done.

As the season progresses, all eyes will be on how Gulati and Dsouza navigate their relationship on screen. Will this incident define their time on the show, or will it blow over? Viewers will be watching closely.
