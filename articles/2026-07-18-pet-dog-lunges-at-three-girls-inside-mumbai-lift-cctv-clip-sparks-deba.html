Pet dog lunges at three girls inside Mumbai lift; CCTV clip sparks debate

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Three girls were inside a lift when a pet dog lunged at them in a Mumbai residential building.
  • The incident was captured on CCTV and has gone viral on social media.
  • The dog's owner was reportedly present during the incident; no injuries have been reported.

A CCTV video from a residential building in Mumbai has gone viral, showing a pet dog lunging at three girls inside a lift. The incident, which took place on Saturday, has sparked widespread discussion online about pet behaviour and safety in shared spaces.

The footage, which has been circulating on social media platforms, shows the three girls entering the lift. As the doors close, a dog, which appears to be a large breed, suddenly jumps towards them. The girls are seen recoiling and trying to shield themselves. The dog's owner is reportedly seen in the lift with the pet at the time of the incident.

How the incident unfolded

According to the CCTV clip, the girls were on their way down when the dog lunged. The brief video does not show any physical contact between the dog and the girls, but the sudden movement caused visible distress. The lift doors open shortly after, and the girls are seen stepping out quickly.

Residents of the building, who spoke to local media on condition of anonymity, said the dog is known in the complex. They added that such incidents have raised concerns among other residents about safety, especially for children and elderly people using the lifts.

Viral video and public reaction

The video has been shared widely on Twitter, Instagram and Facebook. Many users have criticised the dog's owner for not keeping the pet on a leash or under control in a confined space like a lift. Others have pointed out that dogs can get anxious in lifts, and owners must take precautions.

Some commenters have also highlighted the need for building societies to frame clear rules for pets in common areas. The incident has once again brought up the debate on pet ownership in high-rise apartments in Mumbai, where space is often limited.

What authorities have said

Local police have not registered any complaint in connection with the incident so far. Officials from the building's managing committee have not issued any official statement. The dog's owner has not been identified publicly, and it is unclear if the girls sustained any injuries. No medical reports have been filed.

Animal welfare activists in the city have urged people not to vilify the dog. They said the incident likely resulted from the animal being startled or anxious, and not from aggression. They recommend that pet owners train their dogs to be calm in lifts and always use a leash or muzzle if needed.

What happens next

Residents of the building are expected to hold a meeting to discuss safety protocols for pets in common areas. The society may issue guidelines on using lifts with pets. Meanwhile, the viral video continues to fuel conversations online about responsible pet ownership in urban India.
