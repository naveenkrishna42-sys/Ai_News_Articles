Education Minister Resigns Amid Protests

India's Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan has resigned, marking a significant victory for the youth-led 'Cockroach' protests. The protests, which began recently, were sparked by concerns over the government's education policies. According to reports, Pradhan had offered to resign on the first day of the protests, but it was only after sustained pressure that the government agreed to the demonstrators' demands.

Protests Gain Momentum

The 'Cockroach' protests, which have been led by young people, have gained significant momentum in recent days. The demonstrations have been marked by widespread participation, with protesters demanding changes to the government's education policies. The protests have also had a significant political impact, with some observers suggesting that they have damaged the image of Prime Minister Narendra Modi.
