Deal & Pricing Analysis

Asus India has rolled out a launch-period protection promotion for its new Vivobook lineup, and the headline number is hard to ignore: Rs. 99 for a 2-year warranty extension that stacks into 3 years of total additional coverage. The offer is tied to the new Vivobook launch on 16th September and remains valid through 20th September 2026, according to Asus India official offers. Importantly, this is not a discount on the laptop itself. The Rs. 99 is an add-on cost for the extended protection plan, which means the value proposition depends on how much you value post-warranty repair security. For a mainstream Vivobook that typically sells between Rs. 40,000 and Rs. 80,000 depending on configuration, a three-year protection package for under a hundred rupees is a negligible fraction of the purchase price. Historically, extended warranties from major PC brands cost several thousand rupees for comparable coverage, so this is a genuine launch-window incentive rather than a routine bundle. Buyers should confirm the exact terms at checkout, because the offer is time-bound and tied to new purchases during the launch period.

Real-World Performance & Daily Use

In hands-on use, the Vivobook line is built for students, office workers, and home users who need a dependable everyday machine rather than a gaming or creator powerhouse. The keyboards offer comfortable 1.4mm travel, and the anti-glare FHD panels are serviceable for document work, browsing, and video calls, though they are not color-critical displays. Build quality is mixed: entry configurations use durable polycarbonate, while select models add an aluminum lid that improves rigidity and premium feel. Battery endurance typically lands between 6 and 8 hours of mixed productivity, which covers a full campus or workday if you are not pushing the CPU continuously. The 720p webcam is adequate for meetings but not impressive in low light. Thermals are managed conservatively, so the fans stay quiet under light loads, but sustained heavy tasks will cause some throttling. The real story here is the protection plan: a 3-year coverage window means accidental damage or hardware failure in years two and three is far less financially painful, which matters greatly for a laptop that may be carried daily.

Competition & Market Alternatives

The Vivobook competes directly with the HP Pavilion and Lenovo IdeaPad Slim series in the same price tier. HP's Pavilion often matches the Vivobook on display quality and offers similar battery life, but its extended warranty packages usually cost more. Lenovo's IdeaPad Slim tends to have slightly better keyboard ergonomics and comparable performance, yet its standard warranty is one year unless you pay extra. Where Asus pulls ahead with this promotion is total cost of ownership: a Rs. 99 add-on for three years of protection undercuts the typical Rs. 3,000–Rs. 6,000 extended warranty pricing from rivals. If you are comparing purely on hardware specs, the three brands are close; if you factor in long-term peace of mind, the Asus launch offer is the standout.

Who Should Buy This Deal

This offer is best for students, first-time laptop buyers, and families purchasing a machine that will see daily transport and heavy use. If you are already set on a new Vivobook, adding the Rs. 99 protection plan is a no-brainer. However, if you are still undecided on the laptop itself, do not let the cheap warranty rush you—the core hardware still needs to fit your performance needs. Buyers who prefer premium build, high-refresh displays, or dedicated graphics should look at higher tiers or wait for a configuration that matches their workload. For everyone else, the combination of a new Vivobook and three years of coverage for under a hundred rupees is one of the more sensible launch promotions of the season.
