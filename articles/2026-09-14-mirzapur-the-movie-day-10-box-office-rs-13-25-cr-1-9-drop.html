Mirzapur: The Movie Day 10 Box Office — Rs 13.25 Crore and a 1.9% Slide

Mirzapur: The Movie added Rs 13.25 crore on its tenth day in theatres, registering a 1.9% drop from day nine. The near-flat holdover is significant: films that shed under 5% at this stage usually retain a meaningful share of screens into the third week. For a franchise making the jump from streaming to a theatrical release, that stability matters more than a spike.

Pankaj Tripathi returns as Kaleen Bhaiya, the role that turned a regional crime drama into a national property. The question buyers and exhibitors are asking is simpler: does a Prime Video franchise convert into sustained footfalls when audiences are used to watching it at home for the price of a subscription?

Why a 1.9% Drop Is Actually Good News

Weekday declines of 30-50% are routine for mid-budget Hindi releases. A 1.9% dip on day 10 suggests either a strong evening-to-night occupancy pattern or limited new competition drawing audiences away. Both favour the film's second-weekend collections.

Screen Count and Show Allocation

Exhibitors typically reassess show counts after the second Monday. A hold this tight gives distributors leverage to protect prime-time slots. If screens hold, the film's lifetime total gets a real boost from the third weekend, when family and group bookings rise.

Pricing and Ticket Yield

Multiplex average ticket prices in metros range from Rs 250 to Rs 450 for a film of this scale. Single-screen and tier-2 markets pull that average down considerably. That mix explains why a Rs 13.25 crore day does not translate into uniform occupancy across India.

Budget Context and Recovery Math

The Mirzapur film carries a larger production value than a standard season of the series — bigger sets, theatrical-grade action, and a wider marketing spend. Recovery depends on the split between theatrical, satellite, and retained streaming rights. For buyers, the practical benchmark is whether the film clears its print-and-advertising cost within the first two weeks. A day 10 hold of this kind keeps that target within reach.

Real-World Highlights for Buyers and Exhibitors

  • Day 10 at Rs 13.25 crore with only a 1.9% slide signals genuine second-week legs, not a front-loaded opening.
  • Pankaj Tripathi's star pull is confirmed in theatrical terms, not just streaming watch-time metrics.
  • Screen retention after the second Monday will decide whether the film crosses its next collection milestone.
  • Franchise fans who already watched the series on OTT remain the core risk — repeat theatrical conversion is unproven.
  • Tier-2 and tier-3 centres are likely carrying a disproportionate share of the daily total.

Verdict

Mirzapur: The Movie is holding better than most trade projections expected for a streaming-origin property. The Rs 13.25 crore day 10 figure, with a 1.9% drop, gives the film a credible path through week two. Watch the second Friday number closely — that is where theatrical verdicts are actually settled.
