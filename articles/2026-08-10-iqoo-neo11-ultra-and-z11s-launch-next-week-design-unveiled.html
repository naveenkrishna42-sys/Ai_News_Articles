iQOO Neo11 Ultra and Z11s launch next week, design unveiled

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iQOO Neo11 Ultra and Z11s launch confirmed for next week
  • Design revealed shows 2K display and large camera module
  • Z11s to feature 3D curved AMOLED panel with 144Hz refresh rate
  • Reports suggest 10,000mAh battery in one of the models

Chinese smartphone maker iQOO has confirmed the launch of its next-generation devices, the Neo11 Ultra and Z11s, scheduled for next week. The company has also revealed the design of both handsets, giving consumers a first look at what to expect from the upcoming lineup.

Design and display details

The Neo11 Ultra will feature a 2K resolution display, a specification that iQOO has officially confirmed ahead of the launch. The panel is expected to deliver sharp visuals, catering to users who prioritise media consumption and gaming on their handsets.

Meanwhile, the Z11s is set to arrive with a 3D curved AMOLED display offering a 144Hz refresh rate. This specification, revealed ahead of the India launch, positions the device as a contender in the mid-range segment where smooth scrolling and responsive touch are key selling points.

Battery and performance expectations

Reports circulating ahead of the launch suggest that one of the upcoming iQOO devices could pack a massive 10,000mAh battery. If accurate, this would be a significant departure from the typical 5,000mAh cells found in most modern smartphones, potentially offering multi-day usage on a single charge.

The company has not yet detailed the processor or camera specifications for either model. However, given iQOO's history of equipping its devices with capable chipsets and versatile camera systems, expectations remain high among enthusiasts.

India launch and availability

The Z11s has been specifically highlighted for its India launch, with the event reportedly scheduled for August 20. The 3D curved AMOLED panel and 144Hz refresh rate are expected to be headline features for the Indian market, where iQOO has built a strong following among younger consumers.

Details on pricing and availability in other regions have not been confirmed. iQOO typically adopts an aggressive pricing strategy, and the upcoming devices are likely to follow suit, though official numbers will only be revealed at the launch event.

What the design suggests

The revealed design shows a refined aesthetic with a prominent camera module, a trend seen across the industry. The Neo11 Ultra appears to carry a dual-tone finish, while the Z11s opts for a cleaner, more minimal look.

Both devices are expected to run on the latest version of Android with iQOO's custom skin on top, offering features tailored to performance and customisation. The company has not yet commented on software update commitments.

With the launch just days away, iQOO is expected to release additional teasers highlighting camera capabilities, charging speeds, and other key specifications. Consumers interested in the devices will have to wait for the official unveiling to get the full picture.

Analysts will be watching to see how these new models compete with offerings from rivals like OnePlus and Xiaomi in the same price brackets. The next week's event will clarify whether iQOO can maintain its momentum in a crowded smartphone market.
