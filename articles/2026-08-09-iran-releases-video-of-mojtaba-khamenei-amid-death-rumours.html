Iran releases video of Mojtaba Khamenei amid death rumours

📅 2026-08-09 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • State media released surprise video of Mojtaba Khamenei
  • Footage follows Israeli reports of his death and ill-health
  • Iranian president reportedly met him in darkness, unsure it was him

Surprise footage released

Iran's state media has released a surprise video of Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei, in what appears to be a concerted effort to counter swirling reports about his health. The footage, aired on Sunday, comes days after Israeli media claimed the 70-year-old cleric had died or was critically ill.

The video shows a man resembling Khamenei in a dimly lit room, speaking in measured tones. State outlets presented it as recent, though no timestamp or context was provided. Officials have not yet confirmed when or where the footage was recorded.

Conflicting reports fuel scepticism

The release has done little to quell speculation. A report citing an unnamed source claimed Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian met Khamenei in near-total darkness and was not convinced the person he saw was actually the Supreme Leader. The presidency has not commented on the account.

Israeli intelligence assessments have, for weeks, suggested Khamenei's health was deteriorating rapidly. Some regional analysts say Tehran's uncharacteristically quick response to those reports indicates deeper anxiety inside the establishment.

What the video shows

The broadcast clip is short and lacks the usual production polish of official state media releases. Khamenei appears gaunt, and his voice is noticeably weaker than in previous public addresses. Supporters have pointed to the video as proof he is alive, while critics note it raises more questions than it answers.

Independent verification is impossible. Iranian state media has a long history of controlling the Supreme Leader's image, and any footage is carefully curated. The ambiguity has left both domestic audiences and foreign observers parsing every frame.

Regional and global stakes

The uncertainty comes at a volatile moment. Iran is engaged in a shadow war with Israel, negotiations over its nuclear programme remain stalled, and domestic dissent simmers. A leadership transition, should one occur, would have profound consequences across the Middle East.

Khamenei's successor is not publicly designated, and a power struggle within the clerical establishment is widely anticipated. The video release may be aimed at projecting stability, but it has inadvertently highlighted the fragility of the current situation.

What to watch

Expect further statements from Iranian officials in the coming days, likely with more footage or a public appearance. Whether Khamenei addresses the nation directly, and under what conditions, will be the clearest signal of his actual state. Until then, the regime's denials will continue to be met with scepticism.
