Hyderabad gas agencies see rush as eKYC deadline nears

Hyderabad's gas agencies are experiencing a surge in foot traffic as the August 23 deadline for eKYC (electronic Know Your Customer) for LPG connections approaches. With the government's push to digitise LPG distribution, many people are rushing to complete their eKYC before the deadline.

Long queues at gas agencies

People are lining up at gas agencies across Hyderabad, with some queues stretching for over a kilometre. The rush has led to long waits, with some people spending hours in line. Officials at the agencies have advised people to come early to avoid the crowds.

eKYC process explained

The eKYC process involves submitting personal details and biometric information online. Once completed, people can apply for an LPG connection or refill their existing cylinder. The process is designed to be quick and convenient, but the rush has made it challenging for some.

Government's push for digitisation

The government has been pushing for the digitisation of LPG distribution to improve efficiency and transparency. The eKYC process is a key part of this initiative, and the August 23 deadline is a crucial step in achieving this goal. However, the rush has highlighted the need for better planning and communication.

What happens next?

The government is expected to announce further measures to streamline the eKYC process and address the challenges faced by people during the rush. Agencies are also likely to implement additional measures to manage the crowds and ensure a smooth eKYC process for all.
