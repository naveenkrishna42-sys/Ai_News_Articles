All-Party Meet: MPs Push for Debates on NEET Leak, Delimitation, Temple Theft, E20

📅 2026-07-20 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • MPs from multiple parties sought dedicated debates on the NEET paper leak, delimitation, temple thefts, and E20 ethanol blending policy.
  • The Opposition staged a walkout after TMC rebels Kakoli Ghosh Dastidar and Sudip Bandyopadhyay attended the meeting as NCPI MPs.
  • Kakoli Ghosh Dastidar announced her entry into the NDA, claiming 20 Mamata Banerjee rebels are joining her.

Cross-party Demands for Key Debates

An all-party meeting convened by the government on Sunday saw members of Parliament pushing for structured debates on four pressing national issues. The agenda items raised by MPs included the recent NEET paper leak, the contentious delimitation exercise, a spate of temple thefts across the country, and the government's E20 ethanol blending policy.

Sources present at the meeting said the demand for these debates cut across party lines, with several MPs arguing that the issues required urgent parliamentary attention. The government has not yet confirmed the schedule for these discussions.

Opposition Walkout Over TMC 'Traitors'

The all-party meeting was disrupted when Opposition MPs staged a walkout. The protest was triggered by the presence of two former Trinamool Congress (TMC) heavyweights — Kakoli Ghosh Dastidar and Sudip Bandyopadhyay — who attended the meeting as MPs of the newly formed National Coalition for Progressive India (NCPI).

Opposition leaders accused the duo of being 'traitors' for aligning with the ruling coalition. The walkout underscored the deep political rift in West Bengal, where the TMC has been losing key figures to the NDA since last year's assembly elections.

Kakoli Ghosh Dastidar's NDA Entry

Shortly after the meeting, Kakoli Ghosh Dastidar made a public announcement. She declared that she was formally joining the National Democratic Alliance (NDA) and claimed that at least 20 other Mamata Banerjee loyalists had rebelled and were prepared to join her. "We intend to work with PM Modi," she said, signalling a major realignment of political loyalties in the state.

The TMC has not yet officially responded to these claims. However, party insiders suggest the leadership is attempting to consolidate its remaining base.

NEET Leak and Delimitation Dominate Talk

The NEET paper leak, which affected thousands of medical aspirants this year, was a key point of contention. MPs demanded a full-fledged investigation and a debate on the floor of the House to ensure accountability. The government has maintained that a probe is underway and that those responsible will be punished.

Similarly, the delimitation exercise — aimed at redrawing parliamentary constituencies — has triggered fears among southern states about losing representation. MPs from Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and Karnataka insisted on a detailed debate before any final decision is taken.

Temple Thefts and E20 Fuel Policy

The issue of temple thefts, particularly in southern India, where historical idols and gold ornaments have been stolen from several prominent shrines, also featured prominently. MPs called for better security and a central law to protect temple assets.

On the E20 fuel policy — which mandates 20% ethanol blending in petrol by 2025 — MPs from sugarcane-growing states supported the move, while others raised concerns about food security and water usage.

What to watch: The government is expected to announce the schedule for these debates in the next few days. The political fallout from the TMC rebellion and the NCPI's emergence will continue to shape the dynamics in Parliament.
