Monsoon Session: 5 New Bills, 2 Pending, No Delimitation Law

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Centre has listed five new bills for the upcoming Monsoon Session of Parliament.
  • The new bills include a law to protect Vande Mataram and an amendment to the FCRA.
  • Two pending bills from earlier sessions are also on the agenda.
  • Delimitation legislation is not mentioned in the government's list.
  • Opposition leader Kharge has demanded an all-party meeting on delimitation.

The government has finalised its legislative agenda for the upcoming Monsoon Session of Parliament. Five new bills are set to be introduced, along with two pending pieces of legislation. The list, made public on Thursday, makes no mention of any bill related to delimitation.

Five New Bills on the Agenda

The five new bills cover a range of subjects. One of the most prominent is a proposed law to penalise those who insult or block the singing of Vande Mataram. The bill aims to give the national song statutory protection, making disrespect a punishable offence.

Another key legislation is an amendment to the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, or FCRA. The government is expected to tighten rules for non-governmental organisations receiving foreign funds. The exact provisions of the amendment have not been made public yet.

A bill to overhaul the higher education system is also on the list. This legislation is expected to introduce changes to the regulatory framework for universities and colleges. The government says it will streamline processes and improve quality.

Pending Bills and Other Matters

Two bills that were introduced in previous sessions but could not be passed will also be taken up. These include a bill related to the prevention of corruption and another on the regulation of medical devices. The government hopes to secure passage for these in the current session.

The session is also expected to see discussions on several other issues. These include the situation in Manipur, price rise, and unemployment. The opposition is likely to raise these matters aggressively.

Delimitation: A Missing Piece

Notably absent from the government's listed agenda is any bill on delimitation. The opposition, led by Rajya Sabha LoP Mallikarjun Kharge, has been pressing for a discussion. Kharge has written to Prime Minister Narendra Modi, demanding an all-party meeting on the issue.

Delimitation involves redrawing constituency boundaries based on the latest census. Southern states have expressed concern that they could lose parliamentary seats if delimitation is based on population. The government has not yet indicated how it plans to address these concerns.

What to Watch

The Monsoon Session is expected to be stormy. The opposition is united on several issues and plans to corner the government. The passage of the Vande Mataram Bill and the FCRA amendment will be closely watched. Whether the government brings up the delimitation issue later remains to be seen.

The session is likely to begin in the third week of July. The exact dates are yet to be announced. All eyes will be on Parliament as the government seeks to push through its legislative agenda amid expected opposition protests.
