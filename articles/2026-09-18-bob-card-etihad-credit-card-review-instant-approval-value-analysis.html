The BOB Card Etihad offers strong value for frequent Etihad flyers with accelerated reward points and travel benefits, but the annual fee after the first year and limited redemption options for non-flyers slightly reduce its overall appeal.

Deal & Pricing Analysis

The BOB Card Etihad credit card is currently available with a compelling welcome offer: new applicants receive 15,000 Etihad Guest miles upon card activation and payment of the joining fee. The card carries a joining fee of ₹1,999, but this is effectively waived as you receive Etihad Guest miles worth approximately ₹3,000. Additionally, the annual fee for the first year is waived, making it an attractive proposition for frequent flyers. The card offers accelerated reward points on Etihad Airways spends (6 Etihad Guest miles per ₹100) and on international spends (3 miles per ₹100). For a limited time, the bank is also offering instant approval for eligible customers who apply online, with no additional documentation required for pre-approved offers. This deal is particularly valuable for those who travel with Etihad Airways or its partners, as the miles can be redeemed for flight upgrades, tickets, and other rewards.

Real-World Performance & Daily Use

In daily use, the BOB Card Etihad performs well for frequent travelers. The accelerated reward rate on Etihad spends is among the best in the market, allowing users to quickly accumulate miles for their next trip. The card also offers 3 miles per ₹100 on international spends, which is competitive. However, for domestic spends outside of Etihad, the reward rate is a modest 1 mile per ₹100, which is lower than some general travel cards. The card provides complimentary lounge access, which adds significant value for frequent flyers. The application process is straightforward, and instant approval is available for eligible customers, with the card dispatched within a few working days. The bank's customer service is responsive, and the Etihad Guest program offers a wide range of redemption options, including flights, upgrades, and merchandise. However, the redemption process can be cumbersome, and miles may expire if not used within a certain period.

Competition & Market Alternatives

Compared to other travel credit cards in the same tier, the BOB Card Etihad holds its own. For instance, the HDFC Bank Diners Club Black offers similar travel benefits but with a higher annual fee and more comprehensive reward structure. The Axis Bank Vistara Signature card provides accelerated rewards on Vistara spends but may not appeal to Etihad flyers. The BOB Card Etihad's key advantage is its partnership with Etihad Guest, making it a niche product for loyal Etihad customers. However, for those who prefer a more general travel card, the SBI Card Elite offers a broader range of benefits, including higher reward rates on dining and groceries. The BOB Card Etihad's annual fee of ₹1,999 is reasonable, but it may not be waived in subsequent years unless you meet spend thresholds.

Who Should Buy This Deal

This card is ideal for frequent flyers who regularly travel with Etihad Airways or its partner airlines. If you can maximize the accelerated reward points on Etihad spends and utilize the lounge access and travel insurance, the card offers excellent value. It is also suitable for those who spend significantly on international transactions. However, if you are not a frequent Etihad flyer or prefer cashback over travel rewards, this card may not be the best fit. Additionally, if you are looking for a card with no annual fee, you might want to consider other options. Overall, the BOB Card Etihad is a solid choice for its target audience, especially with the current welcome bonus and instant approval offer.

Final Buying Verdict

The BOB Card Etihad is a compelling choice for frequent Etihad flyers, offering accelerated rewards and valuable travel benefits. With the current welcome bonus and instant approval, it's worth applying if you can maximize the miles. However, if you prefer cashback or don't fly Etihad often, consider other cards.
