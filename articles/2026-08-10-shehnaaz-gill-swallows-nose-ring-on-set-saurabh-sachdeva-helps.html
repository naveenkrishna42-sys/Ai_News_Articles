Shehnaaz Gill swallows nose ring on set; Saurabh Sachdeva helps

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Shehnaaz Gill accidentally swallowed her nose ring during a shoot.
  • Actor Saurabh Sachdeva rushed to help her on set.
  • A video of the moment has gone viral, worrying fans.
  • Gill later shared a BTS moment, playing down the incident.

Mumbai: Actress Shehnaaz Gill gave her fans a scare on Monday when she accidentally swallowed her nose ring while shooting an action-packed sequence. The incident, which occurred on the sets of her upcoming project, was captured on camera and has since gone viral on social media.

In the footage, Gill is seen running at full speed when the jewellery piece comes loose and slips down her throat. The actress, known for her spontaneity, momentarily froze before signalling for help. Co-actor Saurabh Sachdeva, who was nearby, immediately rushed to her side to assist.

What happened on set

The video shows the crew rushing towards the actress as she tries to clear her throat. Sachdeva, who was in costume, is seen patting her back and checking if she was okay. Fellow cast members and crew were visibly concerned, with one person handing her a glass of water.

According to sources on set, the scene required Gill to sprint across a courtyard, and the sudden movement likely dislodged the nose pin. The shoot was paused for a few minutes as the team ensured she was comfortable before resuming work.

Fans worried, actress plays it cool

Once the clip surfaced online, fans flooded social media with worried messages. Many expressed relief that the actress was unharmed, while others joked about the unusual mishap. One user wrote, "Only Shehnaaz can turn a nose ring into a snack and still look cute." Another commented, "Thank God she is safe. That could have been dangerous."

Gill, however, brushed off the scare. In a BTS post shared later, she laughed about the incident and assured fans she was fine. "What a day! Accidentally ate my nose ring while running. Guess it wanted to be part of my diet," she wrote, adding that she had not felt any discomfort since.

Doctors weigh in

While the actress has downplayed the matter, medical experts suggest that swallowing small jewellery pieces can pose risks. "Most small objects pass through the digestive tract naturally, but sharp edges can cause internal injuries," a city-based ENT specialist noted. "If there is pain or difficulty swallowing, immediate medical attention is required."

Fortunately for Gill, no such complications have been reported. The production team confirmed that the actress resumed shooting within an hour and completed the day's schedule without further incident.

Who is Saurabh Sachdeva

Sachdeva, who plays a key role in the project, has been praised by fans for his quick response. The actor, known for his work in web series and films, was seen comforting Gill until she gave a thumbs-up to the crew. His gesture did not go unnoticed, with many netizens lauding his presence of mind.

The incident has added to the buzz around the project, which is already generating significant curiosity among Gill's massive fan following. Neither the actress nor the production house has officially commented on the details of the film or its release date.

As for the nose ring, Gill later joked that she would "wait for it to come out naturally" rather than visit a doctor. Whether she actually follows through remains to be seen, but for now, the actress appears to be in good spirits and back to her usual energetic self.

Fans will be hoping that the only thing the actress swallows next is a hit at the box office. The team is expected to wrap up the current schedule by the end of the month, with more updates likely to emerge soon.
