'Dhamaal 4' Box Office Day 11: Ajay Devgn's Film Nears Rs 130 Crore Mark

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • 'Dhamaal 4' earned Rs 3.2 crore on its second Monday (Day 11).
  • Total domestic collection now stands at approximately Rs 127 crore.
  • Film needs around Rs 3 crore more to cross the Rs 130 crore mark.
  • Weekend numbers were strong, but weekday occupancy has dipped.
  • The comedy franchise continues to perform well in multiplexes.

Ajay Devgn's comedy caper 'Dhamaal 4' is holding steady at the box office. On Day 11, the film added an estimated Rs 3.2 crore to its kitty. This takes its total domestic collection to around Rs 127 crore.

The movie is now within striking distance of the Rs 130 crore milestone. Trade analysts are watching closely to see if it can cross that mark by the end of its second week.

Weekday Slowdown Expected

The film saw a predictable dip in footfalls on Monday. The second Monday collection is about 60% lower than its opening day figures. However, this is standard for a comedy film after a strong first weekend.

Occupancy in morning and afternoon shows was moderate. Evening and night shows in major metros pulled in better numbers. The film's family-friendly content continues to drive ticket sales in multiplexes.

Competition and Screen Count

'Dhamaal 4' is currently the only major Hindi comedy release in theatres. It faces no direct competition from other new films this week. This has helped it retain a decent number of screens.

Screen count has been reduced slightly in single-screen theatres. But multiplex chains have kept the film on enough screens to sustain its run. The next big release is still a week away, giving 'Dhamaal 4' some breathing room.

What the Numbers Mean

Crossing Rs 130 crore would make 'Dhamaal 4' one of the higher-grossing comedies of the year. For a franchise film that relies on slapstick and ensemble casting, these numbers are solid.

Ajay Devgn's star power and the familiarity of the 'Dhamaal' brand are clearly working. The film's budget was reportedly controlled, so even at this pace, it is on track to be a profitable venture.

Officials from the production house have not yet confirmed the exact Day 11 figures. The final numbers will be released later in the day by trade analysts.

What to Watch For

The next few days will be crucial. If 'Dhamaal 4' can maintain a Rs 2-3 crore daily run through the week, it will hit Rs 130 crore by Thursday. A strong third weekend could then push it towards Rs 140 crore.
