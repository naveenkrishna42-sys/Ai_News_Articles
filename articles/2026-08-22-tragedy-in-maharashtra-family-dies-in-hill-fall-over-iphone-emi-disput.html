Tragedy in Maharashtra: Family dies in hill fall over iPhone EMI dispute

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • A teenager and his parents died after falling from a hill in Maharashtra.
  • The family was involved in a dispute over an iPhone EMI payment.
  • Authorities are investigating the circumstances surrounding the tragedy.

Tragic End to a Dispute

A teenager and his parents died in a tragic accident in Maharashtra's Raigad district on Saturday. The family was involved in a dispute over an iPhone EMI payment, which reportedly escalated to the point where the teenager felt his only option was to end his life.

Final Moments of the Tragedy

The incident occurred at around 3 pm, when the teenager and his parents were on their way to meet a local sarpanch (village head) to resolve the dispute. The sarpanch, who was trying to mediate the situation, offered the iPhone as a settlement. However, the teenager reportedly refused and said that death was the only solution now.

Desperate Attempts to Save the Teenager

Despite the sarpanch's efforts, the teenager's condition worsened, and he began to plead and prostrate himself, begging for his life. His parents, who were trying to save him, also fell from the hill, resulting in their deaths.

Investigation Underway

Police have registered a case and are investigating the circumstances surrounding the tragedy. The family's condition at the time of the incident is not yet known, but it is believed that the teenager's mental state was severely affected by the dispute.

What Happens Next?

As the investigation progresses, it is crucial to understand the root cause of the dispute and the circumstances that led to the tragic incident. This will not only provide closure to the grieving family but also serve as a lesson for others to resolve their disputes peacefully and without resorting to extreme measures.
