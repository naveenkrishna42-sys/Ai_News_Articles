Chelsea, Aston Villa reach verbal agreement for £117m Morgan Rogers transfer: Report

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Chelsea and Aston Villa have reached a verbal agreement for Morgan Rogers' transfer.
  • The reported fee is £117 million, making it a record deal for the club.
  • Personal terms and a medical examination are the next steps to finalize the move.

Verbal Agreement Reached for Star Midfielder

Chelsea and Aston Villa have reportedly reached a verbal agreement for the transfer of midfielder Morgan Rogers. The deal, valued at £117 million, would see the 23-year-old move to Stamford Bridge in what is being described as one of the most expensive transfers in Premier League history.

Multiple sources, including The Athletic and ESPN, have confirmed that the two clubs have settled on the fee. The agreement is said to have been reached late on Saturday, with both parties now focusing on the finer details of the contract.

Record-Breaking Fee for Villa's Star

The £117 million price tag would make Rogers the most expensive signing in Chelsea's history, surpassing the previous record set for Enzo Fernandez. It also marks a significant profit for Aston Villa, who signed the player from Middlesbrough for a reported £15 million just two years ago.

Rogers' performances for Aston Villa this season, including a standout role in their Champions League campaign, have made him one of the most sought-after young talents in Europe. His ability to play as an attacking midfielder or winger has attracted interest from several top clubs.

Personal Terms and Medical Pending

While the clubs have agreed on the transfer fee, the move is not yet complete. Chelsea must now negotiate personal terms with Rogers and his representatives. A medical examination is also expected to be scheduled in the coming days.

Sources close to the player suggest that Rogers is keen on the move. The opportunity to play regular football at a club with Chelsea's stature is believed to be a significant draw for the England international.

Impact on Both Squads

For Chelsea, the signing would add a dynamic and versatile attacker to their squad, bolstering their options for the upcoming season. Manager Enzo Maresca has been looking to strengthen the team's creativity in the final third, and Rogers fits that profile perfectly.

Aston Villa, on the other hand, will lose a key player. The club has already begun identifying potential replacements, with reports suggesting they are targeting several midfielders across Europe. The substantial fee will give them significant financial firepower in the transfer market.

What to Watch Next

The next few days will be crucial for the deal. If personal terms are agreed and the medical is passed without issue, the transfer could be officially announced by the end of next week. Fans on both sides will be watching closely for any developments.
