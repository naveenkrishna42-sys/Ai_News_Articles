AI Portfolio Builders: The Good, The Bad, The Ugly

When I tasked three leading AI tools—Gemini, Claude, and ChatGPT—with building my portfolio, the results were as varied as their approaches. Only one delivered a portfolio that truly represented my skills and personality. This isn't just a test of AI capabilities; it's a reflection on how well these tools understand and adapt to human needs.

Why the Disparity?

The differences weren't just in the final product. Each AI approached the task differently, reflecting their unique strengths and weaknesses. Gemini focused heavily on technical specifications, while Claude leaned into creative storytelling. ChatGPT, however, balanced both, creating a portfolio that felt personal yet professional.

Technical vs. Creative Balance

Gemini's portfolio was technically sound, with precise details about my projects. But it lacked the human touch, making it feel more like a resume than a portfolio. Claude's portfolio was visually stunning and creative, but the technical details were sometimes off. ChatGPT struck the right balance, ensuring both the technical accuracy and creative flair were present.

User Experience and Customization

User experience was another key factor. ChatGPT allowed for easy customization, letting me tweak the design and content to fit my preferences. Gemini and Claude, while impressive, had more rigid structures, making it harder to personalize the output.

Broader Implications

This test highlights the challenges and opportunities in AI-assisted content creation. While AI tools are becoming increasingly sophisticated, they still struggle with nuanced human needs. The best AI tools are those that can balance technical proficiency with creative understanding and user-friendly customization.

Key Takeaways

  • AI tools vary significantly in their ability to handle complex, creative tasks like portfolio building.
  • User experience and customization are crucial factors in the success of AI-assisted content creation.
  • Balancing technical accuracy with creative flair is key to creating content that resonates with users.
