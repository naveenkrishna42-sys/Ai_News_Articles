Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, Dogecoin Stay Flat as Iran Tensions Weigh on Crypto Markets

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP and Dogecoin remained flat over the weekend as geopolitical tensions with Iran spooked investors.
  • A market analyst says Bitcoin needs to break above a specific resistance level to ignite a strong upward move.
  • Traders are watching for a clear catalyst as crypto markets consolidate in a narrow range.

Major cryptocurrencies are trading in a tight range on Monday, with Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP and Dogecoin showing little movement as geopolitical tensions with Iran keep risk assets on edge.

Bitcoin hovered near $64,000, barely changed from the weekend close. Ethereum held around $3,450, while XRP and Dogecoin also remained flat. The lack of direction comes as traders digest reports of heightened military posturing between the U.S. and Iran.

Analyst Flags Key Bitcoin Level

One crypto analyst has identified a specific price level that could act as a trigger for Bitcoin's next major move. According to the analyst, a break above $66,500 would likely ignite a 'significant rally' that could send prices to new highs.

"The market is coiling," the analyst said. "If Bitcoin can reclaim $66,500 with volume, we could see a rapid move toward $70,000." The level has acted as resistance since early July.

Traders are watching for a breakout as Bitcoin's 50-day moving average converges with the current price. A decisive move above that zone would signal a shift in momentum.

Geopolitical Uncertainty Weighs on Sentiment

The flat trading comes as news of escalating tensions between Iran and the West dominated headlines over the weekend. Reports of increased naval deployments in the Persian Gulf have pushed crude oil prices higher, while equity markets also struggled for direction.

Cryptocurrencies have historically moved in sympathy with risk assets during geopolitical shocks. The current sideways action suggests traders are waiting for clarity before placing large bets.

"Markets hate uncertainty," a Mumbai-based trader said. "Until there is a clear resolution or escalation, expect more of the same."

Altcoins Fail to Break Out

Ethereum, the second-largest cryptocurrency by market cap, has been stuck below $3,500 since late last week. XRP and Dogecoin have also failed to generate any meaningful momentum.

Trading volumes across major exchanges remain below the 30-day average, indicating a lack of conviction among buyers and sellers alike. Analysts say a catalyst is needed to break the current stalemate.

Some traders are looking ahead to potential regulatory developments in the U.S. and India. The Indian government is expected to release a discussion paper on crypto regulation in the coming weeks, which could provide direction for the market.

For now, the crypto market remains in a wait-and-watch mode. Bitcoin's ability to hold above $63,000 will be key to preventing a deeper correction. A break below that level could see intensified selling.

What to watch this week: Bitcoin's price action at the $66,500 resistance level, and any fresh headlines from the Iran situation that could shift risk appetite.
