iPhone 17 Pro at Rs. 69,990, Pro Max Rs. 74,990 as iPhone 18 Pre-Bookings Open

✍️ By TIVRA News Tech & Gadgets Desk 📅 2026-09-14 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iPhone 17 Pro falls to Rs. 69,990 and iPhone 17 Pro Max to Rs. 74,990, a discount of roughly 22-26 percent off launch pricing.
  • Croma has opened iPhone 18 pre-bookings, signalling a September 2026 India launch window and triggering the current clearance cycle.
  • Buyers upgrading from iPhone 14 or older get the strongest value; iPhone 15 and 16 owners should weigh the newer chip, modem and camera gains against a Rs. 15,000-25,000 premium.

Apple's India pricing has shifted sharply. The iPhone 17 Pro is now listed at Rs. 69,990, while the iPhone 17 Pro Max sits at Rs. 74,990. Simultaneously, Croma has opened pre-bookings for the iPhone 18 series, confirming that the next generation is close enough to pull current flagships into deep clearance. For buyers who do not need the newest silicon, this is the most favourable entry point into Apple's Pro tier in three years.

What the New Prices Actually Mean

The iPhone 17 Pro launched in September 2025 at Rs. 1,34,900 for the 256GB variant. A drop to Rs. 69,990 is a reduction of nearly Rs. 65,000, or roughly 48 percent, in under twelve months. The Pro Max, which started at Rs. 1,44,900, now sits at Rs. 74,990. These are not token festival discounts; they are structural price corrections driven by incoming iPhone 18 inventory.

Should You Buy the iPhone 17 Pro or Wait for iPhone 18?

The decision rests on three variables: budget, upgrade cycle, and tolerance for launch pricing. Buyers on an iPhone 14 or older will notice a substantial jump in camera quality, display brightness, thermal behaviour and battery endurance. At Rs. 69,990, the iPhone 17 Pro undercuts several Android flagships with comparable optics.

Buyers already on an iPhone 15 Pro or 16 Pro should be more cautious. The iPhone 18 series is expected to bring a newer A-series chip, revised modem hardware and incremental camera upgrades. That premium will likely start above Rs. 1,30,000 at launch in India, which is Rs. 60,000 more than the current iPhone 17 Pro price.

Real-World Performance Benchmarks

Sustained gaming and 4K ProRes recording remain the clearest differentiators. The A19 Pro handles extended 4K 60fps capture without the aggressive thermal throttling seen on earlier generations. For photographers, the 48MP triple array holds detail well in low light, and ProRAW files remain manageable on 256GB storage for most users.

Value Justification at Rs. 69,990

At this price, the iPhone 17 Pro competes directly with upper-mid Android flagships rather than Apple's own Pro Max. Buyers get a 120Hz LTPO display, titanium-class chassis, USB-C with fast transfer, and a software support window that typically extends six to seven years. That longevity is the strongest argument for buying now rather than waiting.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • Pro: Rs. 69,990 for a current-generation Pro iPhone is historically low in India.
  • Pro: Pro Max at Rs. 74,990 offers the larger 6.9-inch display and bigger battery for only Rs. 5,000 more.
  • Pro: Croma pre-bookings confirm iPhone 18 is imminent, so stock on iPhone 17 units will thin out quickly.
  • Con: iPhone 18 will bring a newer chip and modem, making the 17 Pro a generation behind within weeks.
  • Con: Resale value on the iPhone 17 Pro will fall faster once the iPhone 18 ships.
  • Con: Deep discounts are usually limited to specific storage tiers and colours; verify variant availability before ordering.

Buying Advice

If you are upgrading from an iPhone 13, 14 or any older device, the iPhone 17 Pro Max at Rs. 74,990 is the better pick because the Rs. 5,000 gap buys a larger display and longer battery life. If you already own an iPhone 15 Pro or newer, waiting for the iPhone 18 makes more sense despite the higher launch price. Buyers who pre-book the iPhone 18 at Croma should confirm delivery timelines, exchange values and bank cashback terms in writing, since launch-window offers vary by city and retailer.
