Rukmini Maitra has been a prominent figure in the Bengali film industry for several years. Known for her versatile acting skills and ability to portray a wide range of characters, Maitra has established herself as one of the most talented actresses in the region. Her Hindi debut is highly anticipated, as it offers her the opportunity to work with some of the biggest names in the industry.
