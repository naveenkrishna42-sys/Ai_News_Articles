Borderlands 4 Sees Player Resurgence on Steam

Borderlands 4 has experienced a dramatic resurgence on Steam, driven by a free-to-play weekend and the release of new downloadable content. According to SteamDB, concurrent players peaked at over 250,000 within 24 hours of the promotions, a 300% increase from the previous week. The free weekend, which runs until September 14, 2026, grants full access to the base game, while the DLC adds new character FL4K, Story Pack 2: Loveless the Hacker, and Bounty Pack 5.

What’s Included in the Free Weekend and DLC?

The free-to-play weekend allows newcomers to experience the full base game, including all core missions and multiplayer modes. Progress carries over upon purchase. Simultaneously, Gearbox Software released three paid DLCs: FL4K, a beastmaster character with three unique action skills; Story Pack 2: Loveless the Hacker, which introduces a new narrative arc and a hacker-themed vault hunter; and Bounty Pack 5, offering new missions and cosmetic items.

FL4K: The Beastmaster Returns

FL4K specialises in summoning pets and dealing critical hits. Their skill trees—Hunter, Master, and Stalker—cater to sniper, pet-centric, and stealth playstyles. For players seeking a fresh solo or co-op experience, FL4K offers high replayability.

Story Pack 2: Loveless the Hacker

This story expansion adds a new planet, multiple main missions, and side quests focused on hacking and infiltration. It introduces Loveless, a hacker character with a unique skill set, and expands the game’s lore. The pack is designed for level 50+ characters, with enemy scaling to match.

Pros and Cons

  • Pros:
    • Free weekend lowers barrier to entry, allowing full game trial.
    • DLC adds significant content: new character, story, and missions.
    • Season pass offers 30% savings over individual purchases.
    • Progress carries over, encouraging conversion to full purchase.
  • Cons:
    • DLC pricing may seem steep for casual players.

Buyer’s Guide: Is It Worth It?

For newcomers, the free weekend is an ideal test. If you enjoy the core loop, purchasing the base game at 50% off (₹1,749) is a steal. For veterans, the DLCs offer substantial content. FL4K adds a new way to play, while Story Pack 2 extends the narrative. The season pass at ₹1,299 provides the best value for all three DLCs, saving ₹299 compared to buying separately (total ₹1,597).

System requirements remain unchanged: minimum GTX 1060, recommended RTX 2070, 16 GB RAM, and 80 GB SSD. The game runs at 60 FPS on recommended settings at 1080p.

Final Verdict

Borderlands 4’s resurgence demonstrates the power of free weekends combined with compelling DLC. The player surge signals strong interest, and the pricing structure offers options for all budgets. Whether you’re a lapsed player or a newcomer, this weekend is the perfect time to jump in.
