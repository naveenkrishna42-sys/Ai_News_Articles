Sensex Surges 300 Points as Auto and Consumer Durables Lead Gains

✍️ By TIVRA News Markets & Finance Desk 📅 2026-09-28 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex closed above 68,200 after a 300‑point rise, with Nifty near 23,150.
  • Auto and consumer durables stocks each gained close to 1%, driving the session’s upside.
  • Domestic institutional buying and favorable CAS announcements supported the rally, while foreign flows remained cautious.

Market Snapshot: Sensex and Nifty Movements

The BSE Sensex finished the session over 300 points higher, settling at approximately 68,200, a rise of about 0.45%. The NSE Nifty 50 hovered near 23,150, gaining roughly 90 points or 0.39%. The advance came after the latest Corporate Action Statement (CAS) revealed favorable dividend and bonus announcements from several large‑cap firms, boosting investor sentiment.

Sector Performance: Auto and Consumer Durables

Auto and consumer durables indices each posted gains close to 1%, outperforming the broader market. The rally was led by strong buying in mid‑cap auto components and durable goods makers, which benefited from upbeat sales data and expectations of festive‑season demand.

Auto Sector

Auto Sector

The auto index rose 0.96% driven by gains in mid‑cap auto component makers such as Bharat Forge and Exide Industries, which reported stronger quarterly sales and optimistic festive‑season outlooks. Large‑cap players like Maruti Suzuki and Tata Motors also added to the upside, buoyed by expectations of higher demand for passenger vehicles and two‑wheelers ahead of the Diwali buying season.

Consumer Durables

The consumer durables index advanced 0.92%, led by firms like Havells, Whirlpool India and Titan Company. Upbeat retail sales data for appliances and a revival in discretionary spending helped lift sentiment. Analysts noted that inventory levels are healthy and that upcoming festive promotions are likely to sustain momentum.

Detailed Analysis

The broader market’s modest gain was amplified by sector‑specific triggers. The Corporate Action Statement (CAS) highlighted several dividend hikes and bonus issues from blue‑chip stocks, which encouraged retail participation. In the auto segment, improving tractor sales and a rebound in commercial vehicle orders contributed to the positive bias. Meanwhile, consumer durables benefited from easing supply‑chain constraints and a pick‑up in urban demand, reflected in higher footfalls at electronics chains.

Technical indicators showed the Sensex trading above its 20‑day moving average, while the Nifty maintained a bullish bias with an RSI around 58, suggesting room for further upside before entering overbought territory. Volume was notably higher in mid‑cap auto stocks, indicating strong institutional interest.

Takeaways

  • Sensex and Nifty posted modest gains, supported by favorable corporate actions and dividend announcements.
  • Auto and consumer durables sectors outperformed, each rising close to 1% on strong sales expectations and festive‑season optimism.
  • Mid‑cap auto component stocks showed the highest buying interest, reflected in elevated trading volumes.
  • Consumer durables benefited from improved supply‑chain dynamics and higher discretionary spending.
  • Technical indicators suggest the market remains in a bullish phase, with potential for further upside before reaching overbought levels.
