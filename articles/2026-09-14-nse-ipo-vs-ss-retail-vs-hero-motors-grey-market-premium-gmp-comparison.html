NSE IPO vs SS Retail vs Hero Motors: Grey Market Premium (GMP) Comparison 2026

✍️ By TIVRA News Markets & Finance Desk 📅 2026-09-14 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • NSE IPO commands the strongest grey market premium, reportedly in the 55-65% band, making it the headline grey market winner among the three.
  • Hero Motors IPO is a pure-play EV and components proxy with modest GMP, so listing-day gains may be far smaller than NSE's.
  • SS Retail IPO is a smaller issue with the thinnest analyst coverage, and grey market quotes there are the least reliable for price discovery.
  • GMP is an unregulated, unofficial indicator that collapses quickly; it should never replace reading the RHP, valuation and promoter track record.

Three issues are dominating the primary market conversation this fortnight: the NSE IPO, the SS Retail IPO and the Hero Motors IPO. Grey market premium (GMP) has become the default scoreboard for retail applicants, but the numbers demand scrutiny. GMP is an unofficial, unregulated quote from unregistered dealers. It is a sentiment gauge, not a valuation. Here is what the data actually shows.

Grey Market Premium Snapshot

GMP moves daily and can swing 20-30% within a week of listing. The table below reflects the pattern reported across market trackers in mid-September 2026. Treat these as indicative bands, not fixed prices.

Why NSE Is the Grey Market Winner

NSE is not just another listing. It is the exchange that most Indian equity investors use daily, which creates an unusual emotional and structural pull. Reports indicate that SBI, GIC and Bank of Baroda have trimmed their stake-sale plans, reducing free float and tightening supply. A smaller float against massive retail demand is precisely the combination that inflates GMP.

There is a second layer. NSE cannot trade on its own platform immediately after listing, a governance point flagged by market commentators. That delay does not affect the grey market, but it does affect how institutional investors model liquidity and index inclusion timelines. Buyers should separate the listing pop from the long-term earnings story.

Hero Motors IPO: EV Optionality, Not a Sure Thing

Hero Motors offers exposure to electric mobility and auto components, a sector with genuine tailwinds but brutal competition. A GMP of 8-14% suggests the market expects a decent but not spectacular debut. Compare that with the euphoria around NSE, and the risk-reward profile is completely different. Allotment-day selling pressure in mid-cap EV names has historically been sharp.

SS Retail IPO: Small Issue, Bigger Risk

SS Retail is the smallest of the three and the least covered. GMP in the 5-12% range sounds attractive in percentage terms, but small-cap grey market quotes are thinly traded and can vanish on listing day. The float is small, so a single large seller can move the price disproportionately.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • NSE dominates GMP because of brand familiarity, restricted float and pent-up demand after a sluggish IPO season.
  • Hero Motors is a sector bet, not a momentum bet; the EV theme needs volume growth to justify premium valuations.
  • SS Retail carries the highest data risk, with fewer analyst estimates and unreliable grey market pricing.
  • Stake-sale cuts by large NSE shareholders reduce supply, which mechanically supports GMP but also signals caution among existing holders.
  • Listing-day gains often undershoot peak GMP, a pattern repeated across recent mainboard issues.

What Retail Buyers Should Actually Do

Use GMP to understand crowd sentiment, then ignore it for allocation decisions. Check the red herring prospectus for promoter shareholding changes, use of proceeds and related-party transactions. For NSE, the critical question is whether the exchange can grow revenue beyond transaction fees. For Hero Motors, it is whether EV volumes and margins hold through the festive quarter. For SS Retail, it is whether the business can scale without diluting margins.

Apply in lots you can hold through volatility. If you are borrowing to apply, the maths rarely works once GMP cools. The grey market rewards patience, not leverage.
