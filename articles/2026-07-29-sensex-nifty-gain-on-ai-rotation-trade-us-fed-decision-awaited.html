Sensex, Nifty Gain on AI Rotation Trade; US Fed Decision Awaited

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty closed higher on Wednesday, buoyed by buying in AI-linked stocks.
  • The AI rotation trade saw money move from traditional IT services to firms with direct exposure to artificial intelligence.
  • Investors are awaiting the US Federal Reserve's interest rate decision, due later this week.
  • Broader market indices also traded in the green, reflecting positive sentiment.
  • Sectoral indices showed mixed trends, with realty and metal stocks among the gainers.

Indian stock markets ended Wednesday's session in the green, with the benchmark Sensex and Nifty indices advancing on the back of a sharp rotation into artificial intelligence-focused stocks. The move comes as global investors reposition portfolios ahead of the US Federal Reserve's crucial interest rate decision, expected later this week.

AI Rotation Trade Fuels Rally

The so-called 'AI rotation trade' was the dominant theme of the day. Money moved out of traditional information technology giants and into companies with more direct exposure to the artificial intelligence boom. This shift lifted several mid-cap and large-cap stocks that have been making inroads into AI and machine learning.

Analysts attribute the move to growing conviction that AI will be the primary growth driver for the next several years. “Investors are now looking beyond the usual IT names and focusing on firms that are actually building AI products or integrating them deeply into their services,” a market observer said.

US Fed in Focus

While domestic cues were positive, the overarching sentiment remained cautious. All eyes are on the US Federal Reserve, which is widely expected to announce a rate decision on Thursday. Markets are pricing in a high probability of a pause, but the commentary accompanying the decision will be scrutinised for clues on future policy moves.

A dovish stance could further fuel risk appetite, while any hawkish surprise may trigger a sell-off. “The Fed decision is the single biggest event for global markets this week. Everyone is in a wait-and-watch mode until then,” a fund manager said.

Broader Market and Sectoral Action

The broader market mirrored the positive trend, with the BSE Midcap and Smallcap indices also closing in the green. Sectorally, the action was mixed. Realty and metal stocks were among the top gainers, while the Nifty IT index ended flat to negative as the rotation trade played out.

Pharma and FMCG stocks also saw some buying interest, though gains were modest. The volatility index, India VIX, inched lower, indicating some easing of anxiety among traders.

What to Watch

Market participants will now turn their focus to the US Fed's statement and press conference. The domestic market's trajectory in the near term hinges on the global interest rate outlook and the sustainability of the AI-led rally. Any shift in global risk sentiment could quickly alter the current positive bias.
