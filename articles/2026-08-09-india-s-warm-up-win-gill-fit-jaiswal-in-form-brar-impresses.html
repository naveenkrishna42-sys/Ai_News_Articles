India's preparation for the Sri Lanka Test series received a significant boost as the team secured a clinical victory in their warm-up fixture against a Sri Lanka Cricket XI. More than the result, however, it was the checklist of concerns that the management wanted addressed, and on that front, the signs were largely encouraging.

Captain's fitness reassures the camp

The most pressing question heading into the match revolved around skipper Shubman Gill's fitness. The captain was under observation after a minor niggle, and his participation in the warm-up was seen as the final test. As it turned out, Gill came through the match without any discomfort, batting in the middle order and spending valuable time in the middle.

His presence in the XI for the opening Test now appears all but certain. The team management would be breathing a sigh of relief, as losing their captain on the eve of a crucial series would have been a significant blow to their plans.

Jaiswal finds his rhythm

At the top of the order, Yashasvi Jaiswal was another man with a point to prove. The young opener has been working on specific aspects of his game, and the warm-up provided the perfect platform to translate that preparation into match practice. He looked fluent during his stay at the crease, getting his eye in and playing his natural attacking game.

While the exact score from the innings is yet to be officially detailed in full, reports from the ground indicate that Jaiswal's timing was immaculate. For a batter who thrives on confidence, this knock will do him a world of good as he gears up for the spin challenge that Sri Lanka is expected to pose.

Brar and Padikkal stake their claims

The most pleasant surprise of the day came from Gurnoor Brar. The young pacer, who has been on the selectors' radar for a while, made a strong impression with the ball. His pace and accuracy troubled the SLC XI batters, and his performance has certainly given the think tank something to ponder ahead of the final XI selection.

In the batting department, Devdutt Padikkal emerged as the standout performer. The left-hander, known for his elegant strokeplay, made a compelling case for the No. 3 spot. With the team management looking for a stable option at that position, Padikkal's knock in the warm-up could well have tilted the scales in his favour.

What this means for the series

The warm-up fixture was always going to be about process over outcome for India. With the captain fit, the opener in form, and the new-ball bowlers hitting the right lengths, the visitors have covered most of their bases. The one remaining question surrounds the final composition of the playing XI, particularly how they balance the pace and spin attack.

The team will now shift focus to the first Test, where conditions in Sri Lanka are expected to offer significant turn. India's batting depth, combined with a versatile bowling unit, makes them favourites on paper, but the hosts will be no pushovers in their own backyard.

All eyes will now be on the official team announcement, with the management set to make some tough calls based on the performances from this warm-up game.
