Air India Pilots Face Dope Tests

📅 2026-08-13 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • All Air India pilots to undergo dope tests
  • Tests to start from August 13
  • Move comes after a captain tested positive for marijuana

Air India's New Directive

Air India has announced that it will conduct dope tests for all its pilots, starting from August 13. This move comes after a captain was found positive for marijuana. The airline will undertake a full screening of its pilots for prohibited substances and medications.

Incident That Led to the Decision

The decision to conduct dope tests was made after an incident involving an Air India flight from Phuket to Delhi. Although officials have not yet confirmed the details of the incident, it is reported that the flight experienced turbulence and logged several warnings in a minute.

Pilot Screening Process

The screening process will be mandatory for all Air India pilots. The tests will be conducted to ensure that pilots are not under the influence of any prohibited substances while operating flights. This move is expected to enhance the safety of Air India's operations.

Reaction from Other Sources

Other news sources have also reported on the incident and the subsequent decision to conduct dope tests. According to reports, the screening process will start from August 13 and will be applicable to all Air India pilots.

There have been reports of a turbulent flight from Phuket to Delhi, which logged 9 warnings in a minute due to hydraulic pressure and autopilot disconnect issues. However, officials have not yet confirmed the details of this incident.

Next Steps

The move to conduct dope tests for all Air India pilots is a significant step towards ensuring the safety of the airline's operations. With the screening process set to start from August 13, all eyes will be on how the airline implements this new directive.

As the situation develops, it will be interesting to see how Air India's pilots respond to the mandatory screening process. The airline's actions will be closely watched by regulatory bodies and the public alike.
