Yash and Ladies to Launch Toxic Trailer Today

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Yash and the film's leading ladies will launch the 'Toxic' trailer together.
  • Directed by Geetu Mohandas, the film is described as a dark gangster drama.
  • Early reviews call the trailer a chaotic, violent blend of action and romance.

Bengaluru, Aug 8: The much-anticipated trailer of Toxic, starring Yash, is set to drop today, and the actor will not be doing it alone. The team has announced that Yash will be joined by the film's leading ladies for the trailer launch, an event that has already generated significant buzz among fans and industry watchers.

A Fairytale for Grown-Ups

Directed by Geetu Mohandas, Toxic has been described by the makers as a 'fairytale for grown-ups', a phrase that has intrigued audiences since the film's announcement. The trailer, which released to a mixed but largely positive reception, promises a heady mix of raw action and intense romance, set against a backdrop of crime and violence.

Early reviews of the trailer from major publications have been swift. The Times of India noted that Yash "unleashes raw action and romance" in the dark gangster drama, while The Hindu highlighted how the actor "blends melodrama and mayhem" in what they call a violent actioner.

Violence, Tears, and a Dual Role

NDTV's take was particularly striking, describing the trailer as "Yash vs Yash in a film soaked in blood and tears." This hints at a possible dual role for the actor, a trope that has often worked well in Indian cinema. The Indian Express echoed the sentiment, calling the film "as chaotic and dark as it gets."

The trailer offers glimpses of a world that is both brutal and emotionally charged. Yash, known for his pan-Indian appeal after the KGF franchise, appears to be pushing himself into newer, more complex territory under Mohandas' direction. The film marks a significant departure from his previous blockbuster outings.

The Women of Toxic

The decision to have Yash launch the trailer alongside the film's female cast is a deliberate move by the makers. It signals that the women in Toxic are not merely supporting players but central to the narrative. While the full cast list has not been officially confirmed beyond the leads, the presence of multiple actresses at the launch indicates layered female characters.

Geetu Mohandas, an acclaimed filmmaker known for her work in Malayalam cinema and the international festival circuit, brings a distinct sensibility to this project. Her previous directorial ventures have been praised for their nuanced storytelling and strong character arcs, particularly for women.

What to Expect Next

With the trailer now out, the promotional machinery is expected to go into overdrive. The film's release date has not been announced yet, but industry insiders suggest a major festive window could be targeted. The combination of Yash's star power, Mohandas' directorial vision, and the intriguing premise makes Toxic one of the most closely watched projects in Indian cinema.

Fans are now waiting for the full trailer to dissect every frame, and for the makers to announce a release date. The coming weeks will reveal how this dark, violent fairytale is positioned in the crowded box office calendar.
