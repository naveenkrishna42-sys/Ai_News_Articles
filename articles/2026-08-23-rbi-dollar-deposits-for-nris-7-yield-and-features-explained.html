RBI Dollar Deposits for NRIs: 7% Yield and Features Explained

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Credit Cards & Cashback Original source ↗
Key points
  • Up to 7% interest rate on specific NRI dollar-denominated deposits.
  • Available for Non-Resident Indians seeking stable, high-yield foreign currency accounts.
  • Repatriation benefits and tax considerations for foreign currency holdings.

Understanding the New RBI Dollar Deposit Initiative

The Reserve Bank of India has signaled a shift in its foreign currency deposit policy for Non-Resident Indians (NRIs). By offering interest rates scaling up to 7% on select dollar deposits, the central bank aims to bolster foreign exchange reserves while providing an attractive investment avenue for the diaspora. These deposits are primarily managed through authorized dealer banks operating within India.

Unlike standard rupee-denominated savings accounts, these dollar deposits protect the investor from currency depreciation risk. By keeping the principal and interest in USD, NRIs can hedge against the volatility of the Indian Rupee. Banks are currently in the process of updating their FCNR (Foreign Currency Non-Resident) account offerings to reflect these competitive yield benchmarks.

Key Features and Interest Structures

The 7% headline rate is typically applicable to long-tenure fixed deposits, usually spanning three to five years. Shorter tenures often attract lower rates, reflecting the prevailing interest rate environment in global markets. These deposits are fully repatriable, meaning both the principal amount and the accrued interest can be transferred back to the investor’s home country without significant regulatory hurdles.

  • Currency Protection: Principal and interest are maintained in foreign currency, eliminating conversion losses.
  • Repatriation: Funds are freely repatriable, offering liquidity for global financial commitments.
  • Taxation: Interest earned on FCNR deposits is generally exempt from income tax in India for non-residents.

Eligibility and Documentation

To qualify for these high-yield deposits, individuals must hold a valid NRI status under FEMA (Foreign Exchange Management Act) guidelines. You must possess a valid Indian passport or evidence of Indian origin, such as a PIO or OCI card. Banks require basic KYC documentation, including a copy of your passport, valid visa or residence permit, and proof of overseas address.

Existing account holders may be able to upgrade their current FCNR accounts to these new, higher-yielding schemes through their net banking portals. If you do not have an existing relationship with an Indian bank, you must initiate the application via the bank’s official NRI services website. Verification often involves a video KYC process or in-person attestation at an Indian consulate, depending on the bank’s specific internal policy.

How to Apply Online

Start by visiting the official website of your preferred Indian bank and navigating to the 'NRI Services' or 'Foreign Currency Deposits' section. Compare the 'Annual Percentage Yield' (APY) across different tenures before selecting your deposit term. Most banks provide an online application form that allows you to upload documents digitally, significantly reducing the turnaround time for account activation.

Ensure you select the 'Auto-renewal' option if you intend to keep your funds locked for the long term. This ensures your deposit continues to earn interest without requiring manual intervention upon maturity. If you require specific tax certificates for your country of residence, confirm that the bank provides digital access to these documents through their mobile app or web portal.

This investment opportunity is best suited for NRIs looking for a safe, low-risk instrument to park their excess foreign currency. If your goal is capital preservation combined with a stable return that outperforms standard international savings rates, these RBI-backed deposits provide a compelling alternative to traditional offshore banking.
