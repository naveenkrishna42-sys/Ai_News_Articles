Best travel credit cards: Atlas vs TravelOne vs Regalia Gold vs Diners Club

Introduction

Choosing the right travel credit card can significantly enhance your travel experience. Here's a detailed comparison of four popular options: Axis Atlas, HSBC TravelOne, HDFC Regalia Gold, and Diners Club.

Axis Atlas

Features and Perks

Axis Atlas offers a generous welcome bonus of 10,000 bonus miles on spending ₹50,000 within the first 90 days. It also provides complimentary airport lounge access at over 1,000 airports worldwide. The card comes with a host of benefits including travel insurance, rental car benefits, and concierge services.

Joining and Annual Fees

The joining fee for Axis Atlas is ₹5,000, and the annual fee is ₹1,000. These fees are waived for the first year.

Reward Rates

Axis Atlas offers 1.5 miles per ₹100 spent on travel bookings and 1 mile per ₹100 on all other purchases. Miles can be redeemed for flights, hotel stays, and more.

Lounge Access

The card provides complimentary access to over 1,000 airport lounges worldwide, including those in India.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for Axis Atlas, you need a net annual income of ₹12 lakh or more. You must also be a resident of India.

How to Apply

You can apply for Axis Atlas online through the Axis Bank website. The application process is straightforward and can be completed in a few minutes. Once approved, you will receive the welcome bonus and other benefits.
