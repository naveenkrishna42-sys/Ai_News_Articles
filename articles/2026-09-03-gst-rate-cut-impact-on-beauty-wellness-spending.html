Introduction

The recent reduction in Goods and Services Tax (GST) rates on beauty and wellness products is expected to significantly impact consumer spending patterns in India. This article explores the potential implications of this policy change on the beauty and wellness market, providing a detailed analysis of how consumers can leverage this change to enhance their personal care routines.

Understanding the GST Rate Cut

The GST rate cut on beauty and wellness products, from 18% to 12%, is a strategic move by the government to boost consumer spending in these sectors. This reduction is part of a broader effort to stimulate economic growth and increase disposable income. The beauty and wellness sector, which includes products like cosmetics, skincare, haircare, and wellness supplements, is expected to benefit significantly from this change.

Impact on Consumer Spending

With the GST rate cut, consumers can expect to see a notable reduction in the overall cost of beauty and wellness products.
