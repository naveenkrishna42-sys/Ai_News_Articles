Yash says loud music on Ramayana set would ‘spoil the atmosphere’

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Yash said he used to get big speakers and play Lord Shiva songs loudly on the Ramayana set.
  • He joked that his loud music would 'spoil the atmosphere' between takes.
  • The actor also spoke about the 'extra pressure' of playing Ravana after iconic portrayals.
  • Ranbir Kapoor revealed that he and Yash have not yet shot their Ram-Ravana face-off scene.

Kannada superstar Yash, who is set to play Ravana in Nitesh Tiwari's upcoming epic Ramayana, has revealed that his habit of playing loud music on set often disrupted the mood during filming. In a recent interview, the KGF star joked that he would 'spoil the atmosphere' by cranking up the volume between shots.

'I used to get big speakers'

Yash recounted how he would bring his own sound system to the sets. “I used to get big speakers. I would play Lord Shiva songs very loudly. We were shooting in a very calm, peaceful environment, and I would just spoil the whole atmosphere,” he said, laughing.

The actor explained that the music helped him get into character, though it may not have been appreciated by everyone present. “For me, it was about feeling the energy. But I can imagine what the others must have thought,” he added.

Pressure of playing Ravana

Yash also opened up about the weight of taking on the role of the ten-headed demon king. He admitted that the 'extra pressure' came from having to match the iconic portrayals that have come before. “You cannot escape the comparisons. There have been such powerful versions of Ravana in Indian cinema and television. That is a burden you carry,” he said.

The actor emphasised that he chose to interpret the character in his own way rather than trying to replicate past performances. “I decided to not look at what others have done. I had to find my own Ravana, my own truth. Otherwise, the pressure would have been unbearable,” Yash stated.

Face-off yet to be filmed

Meanwhile, Ranbir Kapoor, who plays Lord Ram in the film, revealed that he and Yash have not yet shot their climactic face-off. In a separate interaction, Kapoor said, “We haven't shot the Ram-Ravana encounter yet. We needed somebody with his aura for that role, and Yash brings exactly that.”

Kapoor described the anticipation surrounding their first scene together. “It will be a special day when we finally stand opposite each other. That scene is the emotional core of the film,” he added.

The film, directed by Nitesh Tiwari, is one of the most ambitious projects in Indian cinema, with a reported budget exceeding ₹500 crore. It also stars Alia Bhatt as Sita and is expected to release in multiple languages.

As production continues, fans are waiting to see how Yash's Ravana will compare to the legendary performances of the past. The actor's candid admission about his music habit has only added to the curiosity around his interpretation.

What remains to be seen is whether the on-set atmosphere will survive Yash's next playlist — and how the final confrontation between Ram and Ravana will shape up when the cameras finally roll on that pivotal scene.
