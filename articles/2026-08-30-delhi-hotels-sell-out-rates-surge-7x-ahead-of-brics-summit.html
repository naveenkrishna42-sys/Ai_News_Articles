Delhi hotels sell out, rates surge 7x ahead of BRICS Summit

📅 2026-08-30 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Luxury hotels in Delhi are sold out ahead of the BRICS Summit.
  • Room tariffs have surged to ₹1.5 lakh per night, a 7x increase.
  • The Summit will be held at Bharat Mandapam, further straining hotel capacity.

Hotel Inventory Lockout

Luxury hotels in Delhi are facing a severe room crunch ahead of the BRICS Summit, with most properties sold out. The Summit, scheduled to be held at Bharat Mandapam, is expected to attract a large number of delegates, government officials, and media personnel, putting immense pressure on the city's hospitality sector.

Price Surge

Room tariffs have soared to ₹1.5 lakh per night, a significant increase from the usual rates. This price hike is a direct result of the high demand and limited supply. The surge in prices is not only affecting local travelers but also international guests, who are finding it difficult to secure accommodations at reasonable rates.

Impact on Travel Plans

The sudden surge in prices and the lack of availability have led to a scramble among travelers to book rooms in advance. Many are being forced to consider alternative options, such as staying in nearby cities or opting for less luxurious accommodations. This situation is likely to have a ripple effect on the local economy, as businesses that rely on tourism may see a decline in foot traffic.

Expert Analysis

Industry experts suggest that the situation is a classic case of supply and demand. With the BRICS Summit being a high-profile event, it's not surprising that hotels are taking advantage of the situation to charge premium rates. However, they should also consider the potential backlash from travelers who are being priced out of the market.
