Shivangi Joshi's Lock Upp ouster sparks 'Ektaa Kapoor' resurrection jokes online

Eviction that sparked a meme fest

Shivangi Joshi's exit from Lock Upp 2 has left the internet in splits, but not for the reasons one might expect. After Shreya Kalra used her special power to eliminate the popular actress, fans quickly drew parallels to the dramatic twists typical of producer Ektaa Kapoor's television universe. The joke? In Kapoor's shows, no one stays dead—or evicted—for long.

Social media erupted with memes suggesting Joshi's ouster is just a temporary plot twist. "The dead are never dead in Ektaa Kapoor shows," one user quipped, referencing the producer's history of bringing characters back from the dead in serials like Kumkum Bhagya and Naagin. The hashtag #LockUpp2 began trending within hours of the episode airing.

Harshad Chopda's sacrifice divides fans

Adding fuel to the fire, Harshad Chopda reportedly sacrificed his finale spot to save Joshi during a task, only for her to be eliminated moments later by Kalra's special power. This sequence left viewers stunned and sharply divided. Some praised Chopda's gesture as noble, while others accused him and Joshi of emotional manipulation.

Critics on platforms like X (formerly Twitter) called the move "calculated drama" aimed at gaining sympathy votes. "Harshad and Shivangi are playing the emotional card to the gallery," wrote one netizen. However, loyal fans defended the duo, insisting their bond was genuine and the sacrifice was a selfless act of friendship.

Shreya Kalra's power play

Shreya Kalra, who had earned the special power through a prior task, used it decisively to eliminate Joshi, shocking inmates and audiences alike. The move was strategic, as Joshi was seen as a strong contender for the trophy. Kalra's bold decision has since made her a polarising figure in the show's fanbase, with some calling her a game-changer and others labelling her a villain.

Meanwhile, Chopda became the first finalist of the season, cementing his place in the finale. His journey has been marked by highs and lows, but this latest twist has put him squarely in the spotlight, with many wondering if his alliance with Joshi will cost him the title.

Ektaa Kapoor's resurrection trope takes centrestage

Ektaa Kapoor, known for her larger-than-life storytelling, has built a career on improbable comebacks. From Kasautii Zindagii Kay to Naagin, her characters have famously returned from the dead, and fans are now joking that Joshi's eviction is merely a prelude to a dramatic re-entry. "Watch out, Lock Upp might pull a Naagin and bring Shivangi back with a twist," another user tweeted.

The meme wave reflects a deeper cultural familiarity with Kapoor's signature style, where plot twists often defy logic but keep audiences hooked. Whether Joshi actually returns remains to be seen, but the internet is already scripting her comeback script.

What's next for Lock Upp 2?

With Chopda confirmed as the first finalist, the competition is heating up. The remaining contestants face tougher tasks and unpredictable eliminations, and the production team is likely to capitalise on the buzz generated by Joshi's exit. Will there be a wildcard entry, or will the finale proceed without her? Fans are keeping a close watch.
