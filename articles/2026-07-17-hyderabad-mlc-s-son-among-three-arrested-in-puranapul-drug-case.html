Hyderabad: MLC's son among three arrested in Puranapul drug case

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Three individuals arrested in Puranapul drug case, including son of a Telangana MLC
  • Police recovered suspected narcotics from the accused during a raid
  • Investigation underway to trace the supply chain and possible links to larger networks

Arrests in Puranapul drug bust

Hyderabad police have arrested three men, including the son of a sitting MLC from Telangana, in connection with a drug case in the Puranapul area. The arrests were made late Wednesday evening following a tip-off about alleged drug peddling in the locality.

Officials said the accused were caught with a quantity of suspected narcotics. The exact nature and weight of the substance are yet to be confirmed by forensic tests. Police have registered a case under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act.

MLC's son among accused

The involvement of the MLC's son has drawn significant attention. Sources said the legislator, whose identity has not been officially disclosed, is a member of the Telangana Legislative Council. The arrested individual is reportedly in his late twenties.

Police have not commented on whether the MLC was aware of his son's activities. The other two accused are local residents with prior criminal records, according to preliminary inquiries. Officials are verifying their backgrounds.

Investigation and legal proceedings

The accused were produced before a magistrate on Thursday and remanded to judicial custody. Police are now probing the source of the drugs and whether the trio was part of a larger supply ring in the old city area.

Raids are being conducted at multiple locations in Hyderabad. Investigators are also checking call records and financial transactions to establish links with known peddlers. No further arrests have been made so far, but more detentions are expected.

The case has sparked political reactions, with opposition parties demanding a thorough probe and questioning the MLC's role. The ruling party has not issued an official statement yet. Police said the investigation will follow the evidence without any interference.

Authorities have urged the public to report any suspicious activities related to drug abuse. The Hyderabad police commissioner has directed the narcotics wing to step up patrolling in vulnerable zones.

What comes next hinges on forensic reports and custodial interrogation. The police are expected to file a detailed chargesheet within the statutory period. The focus remains on dismantling the supply chain that feeds drug abuse in the city.
