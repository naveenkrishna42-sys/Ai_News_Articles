Diljit Dosanjh backs CJP student protest after lathi-charge, says 'will be called anti-national again'

📅 2026-07-21 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Diljit Dosanjh expressed solidarity with protesting students of the CJP, anticipating being labelled anti-national.
  • Riteish Deshmukh and Genelia Deshmukh urged the government to listen and empower the youth.
  • Singer Arijit Singh warned the police action against students 'will be remembered'.

Punjabi singer and actor Diljit Dosanjh has come out in strong support of students protesting under the banner of the Communist Party of India (Marxist) [CPI(M)]-affiliated student wing, the CJP, after they faced a lathi-charge. Dosanjh took to social media to criticise the police action, adding that he expects to be called 'anti-national' again for his stance.

The protest, dubbed 'Sansad Chalo', saw students marching towards Parliament to press for their demands. Police intervention led to a lathi-charge, which has drawn sharp reactions from several public figures across the entertainment industry.

Dosanjh's blunt warning

In his post, Dosanjh did not mince words. He said that by backing the students, he was aware of the backlash that would follow. “I will be called anti-national again,” he wrote, referencing past instances where his comments on social issues drew criticism from certain quarters.

He urged the government to listen to the demands of the young protesters instead of using force. His statement resonated with thousands of his followers, who shared and commented in support of the students.

Other celebrities join the chorus

Dosanjh is not alone in this. Actor-politician Riteish Deshmukh and his wife Genelia Deshmukh also stood with the CJP's 'Sansad Chalo' protest. In a joint statement, the couple said that the voices of the youth deserve to be heard. “Let us listen, support, and empower them,” they posted, urging a peaceful resolution.

Singer Arijit Singh, known for his reclusive public persona, also broke his silence. He criticised the police action in a pointed remark: “Yaad rakha jayega” (It will be remembered). The cryptic yet powerful message from one of India's most popular playback singers added significant weight to the growing support for the student agitators.

Political reactions and context

The CJP's protest comes amid a backdrop of rising student unrest over issues including alleged fee hikes, hostel conditions, and demands for more employment opportunities. The lathi-charge has reignited debate about the use of force against peaceful demonstrators.

While the government has not officially commented on the celebrity statements, supporters of the ruling party have accused the stars of being misled by opposition narratives. Social media is divided, with hashtags both condemning and defending the police action trending on Indian Twitter.

This is not the first time Bollywood and music industry figures have waded into political waters. Dosanjh, in particular, has a history of speaking out on farmer rights and other issues, often drawing both praise and criticism for his outspokenness.

What comes next is unclear. The CJP has vowed to continue its agitation until their demands are met. With influential voices like Dosanjh, the Deshmukhs and Arijit Singh amplifying the students' cause, the pressure on authorities to de-escalate and negotiate is only likely to grow.
