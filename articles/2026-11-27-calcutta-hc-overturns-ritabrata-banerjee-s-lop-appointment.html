Calcutta HC Overturns Ritabrata Banerjee’s LoP Appointment

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Desk 📅 2026-11-27 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Court rules the appointment violated assembly rules and impartiality standards.
  • TMC must reconvene to name a new LoP and chief whip before the next session.
  • The decision may shift power dynamics ahead of the 2027 state elections.

Background: The LoP Controversy in Bengal

West Bengal’s 294‑member Legislative Assembly requires a Leader of Opposition (LoP) once a party or coalition commands at least 10% of seats, i.e., 30 members. After the 2026 assembly election, the Trinamool Congress (TMC) retained a dominant 213 seats, while the opposition bloc—comprising the BJP, CPI(M) and allied independents—held 81 seats. In October 2026, the TMC‑aligned speaker appointed Ritabrata Banerjee, a former TMC rebel, as LoP, a move that immediately raised procedural eyebrows.

Ritabrata, who had defected to the BJP earlier in the year, was not a member of the officially recognised opposition alliance. Critics argued that his appointment bypassed the statutory requirement that the LoP be chosen from the largest opposition party, not an individual nominated by the ruling house.

Court’s Ruling and Legal Reasoning

Key Findings of the Calcutta High Court

On 27 Nov 2026, a three‑judge bench delivered a unanimous verdict, quashing the appointment and directing the speaker to re‑evaluate the LoP selection process. The court anchored its decision on two pillars:

  • Statutory breach: The West Bengal Assembly Rules (2020 amendment) stipulate that the LoP must be elected by opposition members, not appointed by the speaker.
  • Impartiality violation: Evidence showed the speaker consulted exclusively with TMC officials, undermining the neutral role mandated by Article 194 of the Constitution.

The judgment also ordered the immediate reinstatement of the opposition’s chief whip, whose removal had been part of the same procedural package.

Political Implications for West Bengal

Impact on Opposition Cohesion

The ruling party now faces pressure to honour the court’s directive before the next assembly session in February 2027. Failure to do so could invite contempt proceedings and further erode TMC’s image of democratic governance.

For the opposition, the decision restores a legitimate platform to question government policy, especially on contentious issues like land acquisition and industrial licensing. The BJP, which currently holds 45 seats, is poised to claim the LoP slot, strengthening its legislative visibility.

Strategic Calculus Ahead of 2027 Elections

Analysts note that the episode may recalibrate voter sentiment. A transparent LoP selection could bolster opposition credibility, while any perceived manipulation by TMC might fuel anti‑incumbency narratives.

Moreover, the court’s assertive stance signals a judiciary willing to enforce procedural safeguards, a factor that parties will weigh in their campaign strategies.
