Samsung boosts Galaxy Z Fold8 output by 1 million amid record demand

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung increases Galaxy Z Fold8 production target by 1 million units for this year
  • Galaxy Z Series sets record for highest ever pre-orders, according to Samsung
  • New foldables include India-developed Pay Now feature
  • Analysts say Fold8's polished design is driving mainstream adoption

Samsung is ramping up production of its Galaxy Z Fold8 by an additional 1 million units this year, responding to an unexpected surge in demand for its latest foldable smartphones. The move, reported by Digitimes and corroborated by industry trackers, signals that the company's bet on foldables is paying off more aggressively than projected.

The production increase comes as Samsung announced that its Galaxy Z Series has recorded the highest ever pre-order numbers in the company's history. While the firm has not disclosed exact figures, the milestone underscores a shift in consumer preference toward premium, multi-tasking devices.

Why the Fold8 is clicking with buyers

The Galaxy Z Fold8 has been widely praised by reviewers, with The Economic Times noting that "the Fold finally feels like a complete product." Early adopters, who were previously hesitant about durability and software optimisation, are now embracing the device as a daily driver.

Industry analysts point to improved hinge mechanics, better battery life, and a more seamless app experience as key factors driving the upgrade cycle. The Fold8's thinner profile and reduced weight have also addressed long-standing ergonomic complaints.

India's role in the foldable push

In a significant nod to the Indian market, Samsung has added an India-developed Pay Now feature to its new foldables. The functionality, reported by Telangana Today, allows users to initiate UPI payments directly from the cover screen without unlocking the device—a feature tailored to India's digital payments ecosystem.

Local manufacturing and software customisation are becoming central to Samsung's India strategy, which now accounts for a growing share of its global foldable shipments. The company has been expanding its R&D presence in Bengaluru and Noida, with the Pay Now feature developed by engineers in India.

Supply chain adjustments

The additional 1 million units will require Samsung to adjust component orders with its display and chipset suppliers. Sources indicate that the company has already placed rush orders for ultra-thin glass and flexible OLED panels, which remain the most constrained parts in foldable production.

Analysts estimate that the increased target could push Samsung's total foldable shipments for 2026 to over 20 million units, up from earlier forecasts of around 18 million. The company is also reportedly evaluating a similar production boost for its clamshell Galaxy Z Flip series, though no official announcement has been made.

Competition is heating up as Chinese manufacturers like Xiaomi and Honor prepare their own foldable launches, but Samsung's early lead in brand trust and ecosystem integration gives it a distinct advantage in premium segments.

What's next

Samsung is expected to officially confirm the revised production target in its next earnings call, likely in October. The company will also unveil the global rollout schedule for the Pay Now feature, which is currently exclusive to India-bound units.

With demand outstripping supply in several markets, consumers may face extended wait times for the Fold8 in the coming weeks. The real test will be whether Samsung can sustain this momentum beyond the initial launch wave and convert new foldable users into long-term loyalists.
