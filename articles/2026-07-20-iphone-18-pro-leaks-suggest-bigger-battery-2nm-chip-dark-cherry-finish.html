iPhone 18 Pro Leaks Suggest Bigger Battery, 2nm Chip, Dark Cherry Finish

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iPhone 18 Pro expected to get a 2nm processor for better performance and efficiency.
  • A larger battery is rumored, alongside a new Dark Cherry finish.
  • iPhone 18 Pro Max may include a variable aperture camera.
  • Apple's historical launch timeline suggests a September 2026 unveiling.

New leaks surrounding Apple's upcoming iPhone 18 Pro suggest the company is preparing significant hardware upgrades, including a 2nm chip, a larger battery, and a fresh Dark Cherry colour option. The rumours come as analysts look to Apple's 15-year launch timeline, which points to a September release window.

2nm Chip and Battery Boost

The most notable rumour is a shift to a 2nm processor. This would mark a generational leap from the current 3nm chips, promising better performance and improved power efficiency. A more efficient chip would also support a rumoured larger battery, addressing one of the most common user complaints.

Industry insiders say the battery capacity could increase by several hundred milliamp-hours. While Apple rarely specifies battery size in mAh, the upgrade is expected to deliver noticeably longer usage between charges.

New Dark Cherry Finish and Camera Upgrades

The iPhone 18 Pro is also tipped to arrive in a new Dark Cherry finish. This deep red hue would join the existing lineup of Pro colours, offering a more premium look. The colour leak comes from multiple sources, suggesting Apple is expanding its palette for the high-end models.

Meanwhile, the iPhone 18 Pro Max is rumoured to feature a variable aperture camera. This would allow the lens to physically adjust the amount of light entering the sensor, giving users more control over depth of field and low-light performance. Such a feature has been available on some Android flagships but would be a first for an iPhone.

Release Date Clues from Apple's History

Forbes recently highlighted that Apple's 15-year event timeline points to a specific date in September for the iPhone 18 Pro launch. Historically, the company has held its flagship iPhone events in the first half of September, with few exceptions.

If Apple follows its usual pattern, pre-orders could open the following Friday, with shipments starting a week later. Indian consumers should expect a launch in the same timeframe, though pricing and availability may vary by region.

These leaks, while unconfirmed by Apple, align with the company's pattern of incremental but meaningful upgrades. The 2nm chip would be a headline feature, while the larger battery and new colour aim to keep the Pro line competitive.

What to watch next: As September approaches, more concrete details should emerge from supply chain reports and regulatory filings. Apple typically keeps its cards close until the official event, so the next few weeks will be crucial for confirming these rumours.
