Sunny Deol and Rajkumar Santoshi reunite for new action film

After the disappointing performance of 'Batwara 1947', Sunny Deol and Rajkumar Santoshi are set to reunite for a new action film titled 'Ghatak Ghus Ke Maarega'. The film is not a sequel to 'Batwara 1947', but rather a new project that will see the duo return to their iconic roles from 'Banaras'.

Roles and production details

The film will feature Sunny Deol as the 'Ghatak' character, a role he made famous in the 'Banaras' series. Rajkumar Santoshi will also reprise his role from the series. Production has already begun, with an official announcement expected soon. The film is being produced by the same team that worked on 'Banaras', ensuring a familiar and engaging experience for fans of the series.
