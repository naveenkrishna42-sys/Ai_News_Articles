Treesa-Gayatri clinch historic World Championship medal for India

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Treesa Jolly and Gayatri Gopichand reach BWF World Championships semi-finals.
  • First Indian women's doubles pair to win a medal at the event in 15 years.
  • The duo stunned a top-tier Chinese pair to confirm the podium finish.

A Long-Awaited Breakthrough

Indian badminton has achieved a significant milestone as the women’s doubles pair of Treesa Jolly and Gayatri Gopichand secured a medal at the ongoing BWF World Championships. Their victory in the quarter-finals marks the first time an Indian women’s doubles duo has reached the podium in 15 years.

The last time India celebrated such success in this category was in 2011, when Jwala Gutta and Ashwini Ponnappa achieved a breakthrough performance. Since then, the path to a medal in the women’s doubles discipline has remained elusive for Indian shuttlers.

Overcoming the Chinese Challenge

Jolly and Gopichand delivered a composed performance to defeat their Chinese opponents in a high-stakes encounter. The intensity of the match tested their defensive coordination and offensive transitions throughout the court.

By overcoming a formidable Chinese pair, the duo demonstrated the tactical growth they have undergone over the past few seasons. Coaches and analysts have noted the pair’s improved consistency in rallies, which proved vital during the final exchanges of the match.

Building on Rising Momentum

  • The duo’s progression to the semi-finals guarantees at least a bronze medal for India.
  • Their performance highlights the strengthening depth of the Indian badminton contingent on the international circuit.
  • The win serves as a testament to the training regimes implemented at the national academies.

The pair has consistently climbed the world rankings, proving that their recent success is not an isolated event. Their ability to maintain composure under pressure against established global powers suggests they have bridged the technical gap that previously hampered Indian doubles teams.

Looking Ahead

With the semi-final berth confirmed, the focus now shifts to the penultimate round of the tournament. The pair must recalibrate their strategy as they prepare to face their next set of opponents in the quest to change the colour of their guaranteed medal.
