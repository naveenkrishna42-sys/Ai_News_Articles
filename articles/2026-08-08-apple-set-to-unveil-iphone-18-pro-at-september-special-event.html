Apple Set to Unveil iPhone 18 Pro at September Special Event

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple is expected to hold its iPhone 18 Pro special event in September, according to recent reports.
  • A leaked internal memo suggests the company is planning the iPhone 18 Pro and a new iPhone Ultra model.
  • Retail employees are reportedly participating in a lottery system to work during the launch period.
  • The iPhone 18 Pro is slated for an India launch next month, with 12 new features expected to be highlighted.

Apple is reportedly preparing to unveil its next-generation iPhone 18 Pro at a special event next month, according to multiple reports and a leaked internal memo. The tech giant is also said to be working on a new iPhone Ultra model, signaling a broader expansion of its flagship lineup.

September Event Expected

Recent reports from Forbes and Bloomberg suggest that Apple is gearing up for its traditional September launch event, during which it is expected to introduce the iPhone 18 Pro. The event is likely to be held in the second week of September, though Apple has not yet confirmed the exact date.

The memo, leaked to PhoneArena, outlines Apple's plans for the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Ultra, indicating that the company is preparing for a significant product cycle. While details about the event's location and format remain unclear, insiders suggest it will follow the company's usual pattern of a pre-recorded keynote followed by hands-on media sessions.

Retail Employee Lottery

Bloomberg reports that Apple has begun a retail employee lottery to determine which staff members will work during the launch period. This internal process is a common practice at Apple stores ahead of major product releases, ensuring that stores are adequately staffed for the expected rush of customers.

The lottery system is seen as a sign that Apple is expecting high demand for the iPhone 18 Pro and possibly the iPhone Ultra. Retail employees selected through the lottery will likely receive special training on the new devices and their features.

India Launch Expected Next Month

According to India Today, the iPhone 18 Pro is slated for an India launch next month. The report lists 12 reasons why users should consider upgrading from the iPhone 17 Pro, highlighting significant improvements in camera technology, battery life, and processing power.

MacRumors has also reported on the 12 new features expected in the iPhone 18 Pro, which include enhanced display technology, a more powerful chip, and improved AI capabilities. These features are likely to be central to Apple's marketing campaign in India, where the premium smartphone market is growing rapidly.

What to Expect

While Apple has not officially confirmed details about the iPhone 18 Pro or the iPhone Ultra, the leaked memo and retail preparations suggest that the company is on track for a major announcement. The event is expected to showcase the company's latest innovations, with a focus on performance and user experience.

Analysts believe that the iPhone Ultra, if launched, could be positioned as a premium tier above the Pro models, potentially featuring advanced camera systems and a larger display. However, official specifications and pricing have not yet been disclosed.

As the September event approaches, more leaks and reports are likely to emerge, providing further clarity on Apple's plans. The company is also expected to release iOS updates alongside the new hardware, which could include new features for existing iPhone users.

Looking Ahead

With the event expected within weeks, all eyes will be on Apple's official invitation, which typically reveals the date and theme of the launch. The company is also expected to announce availability dates and pre-order details for India and other key markets. For now, consumers and analysts alike await the official confirmation from Cupertino.
