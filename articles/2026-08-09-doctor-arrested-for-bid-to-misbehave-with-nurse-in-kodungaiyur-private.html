Doctor arrested for bid to misbehave with nurse in Kodungaiyur private hospital

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Doctor arrested in Kodungaiyur, Chennai
  • Incident occurred at a private hospital
  • Nurse filed complaint; police registered case

Arrest in Kodungaiyur

A doctor was arrested on Saturday for allegedly attempting to misbehave with a nurse at a private hospital in Kodungaiyur, a northern suburb of Chennai. The police acted swiftly after the nurse lodged a complaint, and the accused was taken into custody.

The incident took place inside the hospital premises, though officials have not yet disclosed the exact sequence of events. Sources said the nurse reported the matter to the hospital administration, which then alerted the local police.

Police action

The Kodungaiyur police registered a case based on the nurse's complaint and arrested the doctor. He was later produced before a magistrate and remanded in judicial custody, according to a senior police officer familiar with the investigation.

The doctor, whose name has not been officially released, is believed to be attached to the same hospital. The police are examining CCTV footage and recording statements from other staff members to build the case.

Context of workplace safety

The arrest comes amid growing scrutiny of safety for healthcare workers, especially women, in Tamil Nadu. Recent incidents of violence and harassment in hospitals have prompted calls for stricter enforcement and better reporting mechanisms.

Nurses' associations in the state have repeatedly demanded zero tolerance for such behaviour. In this instance, the quick response by the police has been noted, though some activists say systemic issues remain unaddressed.

Legal provisions invoked

The case has been filed under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including those dealing with assault or criminal force on a woman with intent to outrage her modesty. Additional charges may be added as the investigation progresses.

Police have not ruled out questioning other hospital staff to ascertain whether similar incidents occurred earlier. The hospital management has not issued a public statement so far.

What to watch

The investigation is at an early stage, and the police are expected to file a chargesheet soon. The case also highlights the need for robust internal complaints committees in private hospitals, a point likely to be raised in the coming days by healthcare worker unions.
