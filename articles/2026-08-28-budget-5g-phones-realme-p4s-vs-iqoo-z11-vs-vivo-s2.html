Budget 5G smartphones: A battle of performance and value

India's smartphone market is flooded with budget 5G options, making it hard to choose the best device. Three recent launches - Realme P4S, iQOO Z11, and Vivo S2 - are vying for attention in the sub-20,000 rupee range. We compare their specs, performance, and value to help you make an informed decision.

Realme P4S: The powerhouse with a premium look

The Realme P4S stands out with its 144Hz AMOLED display, which is rare in this price segment. It's powered by a dual chipset configuration - a MediaTek Dimensity 7200 Ultra for gaming and a MediaTek Dimensity 7200 for everyday tasks. The 8,000mAh battery is a significant upgrade over competitors, offering all-day battery life even with heavy usage.

The P4S's dual chipset setup is a game-changer, allowing for seamless multitasking and improved gaming performance. However, its price tag of 19,999 rupees might be a concern for some buyers.

iQOO Z11: The value-packed performer

The iQOO Z11 offers a more balanced package at 17,999 rupees. Its 120Hz AMOLED display and 5,000mAh battery are impressive for the price, and the MediaTek Dimensity 7200 processor ensures smooth performance.

iQOO's software optimizations, particularly for gaming, give it an edge over competitors. The Z11 also includes a 50MP main camera, which is decent for the segment.

Vivo S2: The budget-friendly option

The Vivo S2 is the most affordable option at 18,999 rupees. Its 90Hz AMOLED display and 5,000mAh battery are solid, but the processor is the same as the iQOO Z11. The S2's software experience is also less polished compared to iQOO's.

Which one to buy?

  • Choose Realme P4S if you want the best display and battery life, and don't mind paying extra.
  • Choose iQOO Z11 if you want the best value for money and excellent gaming performance.
  • Choose Vivo S2 if you want a budget-friendly option with decent performance.

All three phones offer 5G connectivity, ensuring you're future-proofed. The choice ultimately depends on your priorities - whether it's display quality, battery life, or gaming performance.
