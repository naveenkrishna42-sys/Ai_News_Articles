Market Outlook for Monday, 24 August 2026

Indian stock markets are set to open on Monday, 24 August 2026, with investors closely monitoring cues from the US, South Korea, and Taiwan. The Sensex and Nifty are expected to be influenced by key economic events and policy decisions in these regions.

US Federal Reserve Meeting

The Federal Reserve's policy meeting will be a focal point for market watchers. The central bank is expected to announce its monetary policy decision, which could have significant implications for global markets, including India. Investors are particularly interested in the Fed's stance on interest rates and the timing of any potential rate cuts.

South Korea's Economic Data

South Korea's economic data, including GDP growth and industrial output, will also be under scrutiny. Strong economic performance in South Korea can boost investor confidence and potentially drive up stock prices in India. Conversely, weak data could lead to a more cautious market outlook.

Taiwan's Semiconductor Exports

Taiwan's semiconductor exports are a key area of interest for global markets. The island's advanced semiconductor manufacturing capabilities have made it a critical player in the global tech supply chain. Any developments in Taiwan's semiconductor industry could have ripple effects on the Indian stock market.

Investor Sentiment and Market Trends

Overall investor sentiment will play a crucial role in shaping the market outlook for Monday. Positive cues from the US, South Korea, and Taiwan could lead to a bullish market, while negative developments may result in a more cautious approach. Traders are advised to keep a close eye on these key indicators to make informed investment decisions.
