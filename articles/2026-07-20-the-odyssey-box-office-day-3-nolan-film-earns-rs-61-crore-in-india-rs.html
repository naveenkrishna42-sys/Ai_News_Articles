The Odyssey Box Office Day 3: Nolan Film Earns Rs 61 Crore in India, Rs 2,540 Crore Globally

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • The Odyssey earned Rs 61 crore in India on day 3, taking its domestic total past Rs 150 crore.
  • Globally, the film has collected an estimated Rs 2,540 crore (approx. $305 million) in three days.
  • The film is on track to break multiple all-time box office records in India and worldwide.

Christopher Nolan's latest epic, The Odyssey, has stormed the global box office in its opening weekend. The film earned an estimated Rs 61 crore in India on its third day, according to early trade estimates. This brings its three-day domestic total to a formidable Rs 156 crore.

Worldwide, the numbers are even more staggering. The film has collected approximately Rs 2,540 crore (around $305 million) globally in just three days. The figure positions the film as one of the fastest to cross the Rs 2,500 crore mark in cinema history.

India Performance

The Indian box office has been a key driver for the film's success. After earning Rs 48 crore on its opening day and Rs 47 crore on Saturday, the Sunday numbers showed strong hold, especially in multiplexes in major cities. Hindi belt centres contributed nearly 45% of the domestic total.

Trade analysts attribute the strong performance to Nolan's dedicated fan base in India and the film's visual spectacle, which has drawn repeat viewers. The English and Hindi versions have both performed well, with dubbed versions in Tamil, Telugu, and Kannada also contributing significantly.

Global Domination

Internationally, The Odyssey has performed strongly across all major markets. The United States and China have been the top contributors, with the film earning over $100 million in each territory. Europe, particularly the UK and Germany, has also shown robust numbers.

IMAX screens worldwide have reported near-capacity shows, with many shows sold out for the entire weekend. The film's 70mm prints have been a major draw for cinephiles, adding to the premium pricing that has boosted overall collections.

Record-Breaking Trajectory

With a reported production budget of over Rs 1,500 crore, The Odyssey is one of the most expensive films ever made. The opening weekend numbers suggest it is on track to become one of the highest-grossing films of all time. Industry experts predict the film could cross the Rs 5,000 crore mark globally by the end of its second weekend.

In India, the film is already the highest-grossing Hollywood release of the year. It has surpassed the lifetime collections of recent blockbusters like Oppenheimer and Dune: Part Two in just three days. The film's performance in non-metro cities has been particularly encouraging for distributors.

The coming week will be crucial as the film faces its first Monday test. However, with strong word-of-mouth and positive reviews, trade sources expect minimal drop-offs. The film's long run will depend on sustaining interest through the week and into the second weekend.
