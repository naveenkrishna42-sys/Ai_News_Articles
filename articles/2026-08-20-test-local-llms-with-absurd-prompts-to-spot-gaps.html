Test local LLMs with absurd prompts to spot gaps

📅 2026-08-20 📂 AI Tips & Tools Original source ↗
Key points
  • Identify the top local LLMs for comparison
  • Create absurd prompts to test model capabilities
  • Analyze responses to spot gaps in performance

Step 1: Identify the Top Local LLMs

Start by listing the top local LLMs available for testing. This could include models like LLaMA, Mistral, or others that are widely used in your region. Ensure you have access to these models through their respective platforms or APIs.

Step 2: Create Absurd Prompts

Design prompts that are intentionally absurd or complex. These prompts should test the limits of the models' understanding and creativity. For example, you could ask the models to write a poem about a talking banana or to explain the concept of a time-traveling cat.

Example Prompts

  • Write a short story about a robot who dreams of being human.
  • Explain the theory of relativity in the context of a baking competition.
  • Describe a day in the life of a sentient cloud.

Step 3: Test the Models

Input your absurd prompts into each of the local LLMs and note the responses. Compare these responses with the outputs from ChatGPT and Claude for the same prompts. Pay attention to the coherence, creativity, and accuracy of the responses.

Step 4: Analyze the Responses

Analyze the responses to identify any gaps in performance. Look for patterns in how the models handle absurd prompts. For example, some models might struggle with maintaining coherence in complex scenarios, while others might excel in creative writing tasks.

Step 5: Document the Findings

Document your findings in a report. Include the prompts used, the responses from each model, and a detailed analysis of the performance gaps. This report can help you understand the strengths and weaknesses of each model and guide your decision-making in choosing the right model for your specific needs.
