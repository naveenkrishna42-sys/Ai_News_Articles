Kargil Vijay Diwas: President Murmu, PM Modi lead nation in paying homage to 1999 war martyrs

📅 2026-07-26 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • President Murmu and PM Modi led tributes at the Kargil War Memorial on the 27th anniversary of Operation Vijay.
  • Defence Minister Rajnath Singh stated India would only talk to Pakistan about PoK, rejecting broader dialogue.
  • A Kargil martyr's family said they were still awaiting promises made 26 years after his sacrifice.
  • Governor Arlekar and political leaders also paid tributes to soldiers across the country.

Nation Remembers Its Bravehearts

President Droupadi Murmu and Prime Minister Narendra Modi led the country in paying homage to the soldiers who laid down their lives during the 1999 Kargil War. The 27th anniversary of 'Operation Vijay' was marked with solemn ceremonies at the Kargil War Memorial in Dras and across the nation.

The President and the Prime Minister laid wreaths at the memorial, bowing their heads in silence. Senior military officials and families of the fallen soldiers were also present at the high-altitude venue.

Political Leaders Join Tributes

Governor Arlekar and leaders from various political parties paid their respects to the soldiers. In separate events, they highlighted the bravery and sacrifice of the armed forces during the conflict.

The Eastern Naval Command (ENC) in Visakhapatnam also held a ceremony to honour the war heroes. Naval personnel paid tribute, underscoring the unity of the three defence forces in remembering the Kargil victory.

Defence Minister Sticks to PoK Stance

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh made a firm statement on the ongoing tensions with Pakistan. Speaking on the sidelines of the Vijay Diwas events, he said, 'India will talk to Pakistan only on PoK.' He rejected any possibility of broader dialogue, reiterating the government's position on Pakistan-occupied Kashmir.

His remarks come at a time when diplomatic relations between the two neighbours remain frozen. The minister's words were seen as a clear signal that New Delhi would not engage in talks unless the core issue of PoK is addressed.

Families of Martyrs Voice Grievances

Not all stories on the day were about official ceremonies. A family of a Kargil martyr told reporters they were 'still awaiting' promises made to them 26 years after their loved one's sacrifice. The family, speaking to The New Indian Express, did not specify the exact nature of the unfulfilled promises but expressed deep disappointment.

Their statement served as a stark reminder that for many families, the war never truly ended. The emotional toll and the struggle for recognition continue long after the national spotlight moves on.

What to watch: The government's response to the families' grievances and any diplomatic moves regarding the PoK statement from the Defence Minister will be closely observed in the coming weeks.
