Sensex Likely to Open Flat; Asian Markets Mixed

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • GIFT Nifty trends suggest a flat start for Sensex and Nifty.
  • Asian shares traded mixed in early morning deals.
  • Investors eye global cues and domestic macros for direction.

Indian equity benchmarks are likely to open on a flat note on Wednesday, mirroring the cautious mood across Asian markets. The SGX Nifty, an early indicator of the domestic market's opening, traded near the previous close, suggesting a muted start for the Sensex and the Nifty 50.

Asian Markets Show Mixed Trends

Across the region, Asian shares traded mixed in early deals. While some indices saw mild gains, others slipped into the red as investors weighed fresh economic data and lingering global growth concerns. Japan's Nikkei edged slightly higher, while China's Shanghai Composite and Hong Kong's Hang Seng showed subdued movements.

The mixed tone in Asia follows a lackluster session on Wall Street overnight, where major indices closed with modest changes. US futures remained range-bound, offering little direction to global risk appetite.

Domestic Cues and Investor Sentiment

Back home, market participants will keep a close watch on the movement of foreign institutional investors (FIIs) and domestic institutional investors (DIIs). Recent sessions have seen volatile flows, with overseas investors turning net sellers in certain pockets.

Oil prices also remain a key monitorable, as any sharp spike could impact India's import bill and inflation trajectory. Brent crude hovered near recent levels, with traders assessing supply dynamics and demand outlook.

On the corporate front, a slew of quarterly earnings releases are scheduled through the day, which could add stock-specific volatility. Analysts expect the broader market to remain range-bound until clearer triggers emerge.

Technical Outlook and Key Levels

From a technical standpoint, the Nifty 50 has been consolidating in a tight band over the past few sessions. Immediate support is seen near the 24,000 mark, while resistance is placed around 24,300 levels. A breakout on either side could set the tone for the near term.

The Sensex, similarly, is likely to find support in the 78,500-78,800 zone, with upside capped near 79,200 levels. Traders are advised to keep an eye on global cues and any sudden news flow that could trigger sharp moves.

What to Watch Next

Market participants will now await the release of key economic data points, including inflation numbers and industrial production figures, due later this week. A surprise in either direction could influence the Reserve Bank of India's policy stance and, in turn, market sentiment.

Additionally, the movement of the rupee against the dollar and the trajectory of global bond yields will be closely tracked. For now, the mood remains cautious, and a clear directional bias is likely only after fresh domestic or global catalysts emerge.
