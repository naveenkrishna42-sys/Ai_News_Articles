Bindyarani Devi wins bronze, India takes fifth weightlifting medal at Commonwealth Games

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bindyarani Devi won bronze in the women's 58kg weightlifting event at the Commonwealth Games in Glasgow.
  • India's weightlifting medal count has reached five, including one silver and four bronze.
  • Nigerian weightlifter Lawal shattered Commonwealth Games records in the same event to win gold.
  • The Indian team continues to perform strongly, with athletes advancing in other sports.

Bindyarani Devi secures bronze for India

Indian weightlifter Bindyarani Devi claimed a bronze medal in the women's 58kg category at the Commonwealth Games in Glasgow on Monday. Her lift of 198kg total was enough to put her on the podium, adding to India's growing medal tally in the sport.

This is the fifth weightlifting medal for India at the ongoing Games. The Indian contingent has now won one silver and four bronze medals in weightlifting alone.

Nigerian record-breaker dominates

The gold in the women's 58kg event went to Nigeria's Lawal, who shattered multiple Commonwealth Games records with her performance. She lifted 105kg in snatch and 135kg in clean and jerk, setting new benchmarks in both lifts and the total.

Lawal's total of 240kg was far ahead of the rest of the field. The silver medal went to another Nigerian lifter, while Bindyarani held off competition from lifters from Canada and Australia to secure bronze.

India's weightlifting campaign

Bindyarani's bronze follows earlier medals from Indian lifters in the women's 49kg, women's 55kg, and men's events. The team has shown depth across weight categories, with several first-time Commonwealth Games participants making the podium.

Coaches and support staff have credited systematic training and improved facilities for the consistent medal flow. India's weightlifting federation has focused on building a strong bench for international competitions.

Other Indian athletes advance

Beyond weightlifting, Indian athletes have been making progress in multiple disciplines at the Glasgow Games. Several track and field athletes have qualified for their event finals, while boxers and shooters have also advanced in their draws.

The badminton and wrestling teams are expected to add more medals in the coming days. India's overall medal count is projected to rise as the Games enter their second week.

What happens next

Bindyarani and the rest of the weightlifting team will return to India after the Games conclude. The focus will now shift to the remaining weight categories, where Indian lifters are still in contention. The Commonwealth Games federation will announce the final medal standings on August 2.
