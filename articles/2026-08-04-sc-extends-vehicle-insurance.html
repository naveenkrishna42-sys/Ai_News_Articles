SC Extends Vehicle Insurance

📅 2026-08-04 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Third party insurance for cars increased to 4 years
  • Two-wheelers insurance increased to 6 years
  • No insurance, no fuel pilot project proposed

Supreme Court Directs Increase in Vehicle Insurance

The Supreme Court has directed an increase in the third party insurance period for cars to 4 years and for two-wheelers to 6 years. This move aims to reduce the number of uninsured vehicles on the road. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact date of implementation for this new rule.

Uninsured Vehicles a Concern

According to reports, there are approximately 16.54 crore uninsured vehicles in the country. The Supreme Court has expressed concern over this issue, citing the risks and financial burdens it poses to victims of accidents involving these vehicles. To address this, the court has proposed a pilot project linking fuel supply with vehicle insurance.

No Insurance, No Fuel Pilot Project

The proposed pilot project, 'no insurance, no fuel', aims to ensure that only insured vehicles are able to purchase fuel. This initiative is expected to encourage vehicle owners to purchase insurance, thereby reducing the number of uninsured vehicles on the road. The government has been directed to prepare a plan for the implementation of this project.

Technical Road Safety Overhaul

In addition to the insurance extension and the 'no insurance, no fuel' project, the Supreme Court has also sought a technology-driven overhaul of road safety in the country. The details of this overhaul have not been confirmed, but it is expected to involve the use of technology to improve road safety and reduce accidents.

The Supreme Court's directives are expected to have a significant impact on the vehicle insurance industry and road safety in the country. Vehicle owners and insurance companies will need to adapt to the new rules and regulations.

Next Steps

The government will now need to prepare a plan for the implementation of the 'no insurance, no fuel' pilot project and the extension of the vehicle insurance period. The Supreme Court will likely monitor the progress of these initiatives and provide further directives as necessary.
