Messi uncertain over future after father's death

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Messi has said he is not certain to play 'much longer' following his father's death
  • Inter Miami coach has given no timetable for the Argentine's return
  • Cristiano Ronaldo has publicly sent a message of support to his longtime rival

No Timetable for Return

Lionel Messi has cast doubt over how much longer he will continue playing professional football, following the recent death of his father, Jorge. The Argentine superstar's remarks have sent ripples through the sporting world, with fans and pundits alike trying to read between the lines.

Inter Miami's coach has confirmed that there is no timetable for Messi's return to the pitch. The club is giving the 39-year-old all the space he needs to grieve, but the uncertainty has left many wondering what the future holds for one of the game's greatest icons.

'I Don't Know How to Carry On'

In a heartbreaking letter addressed to his father, Messi reportedly wrote, 'I don't know how to carry on.' The emotional weight of those words has resonated deeply with fans across the globe, who have flooded social media with messages of support.

The bond between Messi and his father was well-documented, with Jorge playing a pivotal role in his son's career from a young age. From the streets of Rosario to the grandest stages of world football, Jorge was a constant presence, and his loss has left a void that may prove difficult to fill.

Ronaldo's Message of Support

In a rare show of cross-rival solidarity, Cristiano Ronaldo has sent a public message to Messi, saying, 'Leo, stay strong.' The two have dominated football for nearly two decades, but this moment has transcended their on-pitch rivalry.

Ronaldo's gesture has been widely praised, with many noting that it speaks to the shared humanity of even the fiercest competitors. The football community has rallied around Messi, with tributes pouring in from former teammates, clubs, and governing bodies.

What This Means for Football

Messi's comments have inevitably sparked speculation about his retirement timeline. While he has not made any formal announcement, the uncertainty alone is enough to raise questions about his participation in upcoming fixtures and tournaments.

For Inter Miami, the situation is delicate. The club has built its recent success around Messi's presence, both on and off the field. His absence, whether temporary or permanent, would be a significant blow to their ambitions in the MLS and beyond.

For Argentina, the national team will be watching closely. Messi has been the heart and soul of the side for years, and any prolonged absence would force the coaching staff to rethink their strategies, particularly with major international competitions on the horizon.

The Road Ahead

At this point, all eyes are on Messi as he navigates this deeply personal loss. Neither he nor his representatives have offered any concrete details about his future, and Inter Miami has been careful not to speculate.

The coming weeks will likely bring more clarity, but for now, the football world can only wait and hope that one of its brightest stars finds the strength to carry on, in whatever capacity he chooses.
