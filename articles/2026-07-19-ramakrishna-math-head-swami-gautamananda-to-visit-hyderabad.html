Ramakrishna Math head Swami Gautamananda to visit Hyderabad

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Swami Gautamananda, the global head of Ramakrishna Math and Mission, is visiting Hyderabad.
  • The visit is scheduled from July 22 to July 25, 2026.
  • Events include public talks, meditation sessions, and meetings with devotees.
  • The Hyderabad Ramakrishna Math centre is hosting the visit.

Swami Gautamananda, the global head of the Ramakrishna Math and Ramakrishna Mission, will be in Hyderabad for a four-day visit starting July 22, 2026. The trip is expected to draw thousands of devotees and spiritual seekers from across the region.

Schedule and events

During his stay, the Math president will lead a series of public programmes at the Ramakrishna Math centre in Hyderabad. These include discourses on Vedanta philosophy, meditation sessions, and question-and-answer interactions with followers.

Devotees will get the chance to attend a special satsang, or spiritual gathering, on the evening of July 23. The Math has not yet released the full schedule, but organisers say the events will be open to all without any registration fee.

Who is Swami Gautamananda?

Swami Gautamananda has been the president of the Ramakrishna Math and Ramakrishna Mission since 2018. He is a senior monk who joined the order in the 1960s and has served in various capacities, including as the head of the Delhi centre. His visits to cities often draw large crowds, and his talks focus on practical spirituality and self-development.

The Math and Mission, founded by Swami Vivekananda in 1897, run hundreds of educational, healthcare, and relief projects across India and abroad. The Hyderabad centre itself runs a school, a library, and free medical services.

Preparations under way

The local Math centre has begun preparations for the visit. Volunteers are being trained to manage crowds and ensure smooth conduct of events. Security arrangements are being coordinated with the Hyderabad police.

Officials at the Math expect a significant turnout, especially on the weekend of July 24 and 25. The centre has advised devotees to arrive early for the main sessions as seating will be limited.

Significance for Hyderabad

This is the first visit by the Ramakrishna Math president to Hyderabad in several years. The city has a strong Ramakrishna-Vivekananda following, with multiple study circles and youth groups active in the area. Many local spiritual organisations have welcomed the visit, saying it will give a boost to the city's spiritual climate.

The Math has not confirmed any private meetings with political or business leaders during the visit. The focus, organisers say, will remain on public spiritual programmes.

What to watch: The Math is expected to release the detailed itinerary and timings in the coming days. Devotees are advised to follow the Hyderabad centre's official social media handles for updates.
