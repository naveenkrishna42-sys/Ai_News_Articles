Real-World Performance & Daily Use

In real-world testing, the Libas perfume opens with a burst of fresh, floral notes that gradually settle into a warm, slightly woody base. Given the price, the performance exceeds expectations, though those with dry skin may need to moisturize beforehand to enhance longevity.
