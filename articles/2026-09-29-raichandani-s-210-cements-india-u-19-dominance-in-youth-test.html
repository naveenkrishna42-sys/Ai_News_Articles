Innings Analysis

Australia struggled from the start, bowled out for just 128 running despite having some promising candidates in their lineup. The Indian batting team showed remarkable resilience throughout the innings, with Raichandani anchoring the chase through steady middle order support. India posted a total of 243/8 in the second inning, securing a comfortable margin beyond the winning threshold.

Key Performers

  • Raichandani: Unbeaten 210* – Finished with 48 off 43 balls, including six fifties and a maiden half-century in earlier phases of the match.
  • Ishan Om: 31/12 – Quickfire counter attack that set up the final over-winning run rate.
  • Amit Shah: 42* – Provided crucial stabilization when the score was tightening.
  • Mohan Kumar: 38* – Contributed steadily at number eight with reliable stroke play.
  • India Overall: 243/8 – 12 wickets down in the losing partner's innings, demonstrating consistent pressure on the Australian seam.">

Tournament Context

This Youth Test marked the opening fixture of the 2026 U-19 Cricket Tournaments, organized under the Ministry of Education's national promotion initiatives. The match served as a preliminary round-offer where India faced Australia's strongest emerging talent. Both teams have been selected from the latest NCL U-19 squads, ensuring continuity in player development pathways.
