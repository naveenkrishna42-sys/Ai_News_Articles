Sourav Ganguly, wife Dona receive death threat letters; police probe on

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Death threat letters sent to Sourav Ganguly and wife Dona at their Kolkata residence.
  • Police have lodged an FIR and begun a probe into the incident.
  • Security around the Ganguly family has been heightened as investigations continue.

Kolkata: Former Indian cricket captain and ex-BCCI president Sourav Ganguly and his wife Dona have received death threat letters, prompting police to launch an immediate investigation. The letters were reportedly sent to their residence in the city, triggering security concerns for the high-profile family.

Threat letters trigger police action

According to sources familiar with the matter, the threat letters were received by the Ganguly household recently. The contents of the letters have not been made public, but they were deemed serious enough for the family to approach the authorities.

Police have confirmed that a formal complaint was lodged, following which an FIR was registered. A team has been tasked with tracing the origin of the letters and identifying the sender. Officials have not yet confirmed whether any specific demands were made in the communication.

Investigation underway

The Kolkata Police have begun a detailed probe, including forensic examination of the letters. Investigators are also checking CCTV footage from the area and questioning staff at the Ganguly residence to piece together how the letters were delivered.

“We have received a complaint and appropriate legal action has been initiated. The matter is being investigated thoroughly,” a senior police officer said, requesting anonymity. The officer declined to speculate on possible motives or suspects.

Security stepped up

In the wake of the threats, security around the Ganguly family has been tightened. Local police have increased patrols near their residence, and the family has been advised to exercise caution. The former cricketer, who is also a veteran administrator, has had a long public career that has often drawn attention, but threats of this nature are rare.

Ganguly, who led India to numerous memorable victories and later served as BCCI president, remains a prominent figure in Indian cricket. His wife Dona, a classical dancer, has largely stayed away from the limelight, making the threat against her particularly unsettling for the family and their supporters.

Political and cricketing circles react

News of the threats has drawn concern from cricketing and political circles. Several former teammates and officials have expressed solidarity with the Ganguly family, though none have commented officially. The development comes at a time when security of public figures has been under renewed focus.

Police are yet to ascertain whether the letters are a hoax or a genuine threat. Similar cases in the past have often turned out to be the work of mischief-makers, but authorities are treating this with full seriousness until proven otherwise.

What to watch

The investigation is in its early stages, and police are expected to release more details once the letters are analysed. The Ganguly family has not issued a public statement so far. The coming days will reveal whether the sender is traced, and if any prior threats to the family had been reported to the police.
