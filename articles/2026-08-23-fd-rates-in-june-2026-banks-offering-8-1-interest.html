Understanding Fixed Deposits in June 2026

Fixed deposits (FDs) are a popular investment option in India, offering a safe and secure way to park your money for a fixed tenure. In June 2026, several banks are offering an attractive interest rate of 8.1% on FDs. This guide will help you understand the features, benefits, and how to apply for these FDs.

Banks Offering 8.1% Interest Rate on Fixed Deposits

Several banks in India are offering a 8.1% interest rate on fixed deposits in June 2026. Some of the banks that are offering this rate include:

  • State Bank of India (SBI)
  • Punjab National Bank (PNB)
  • Bank of Baroda (BoB)
  • Canara Bank
  • Union Bank of India

Features and Benefits of Fixed Deposits

Fixed deposits come with several features and benefits that make them an attractive investment option. Some of the key features include:

  • Safe and secure investment option
  • Attractive interest rates
  • Flexibility in tenure
  • Tax benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act

To maximize your returns, it's important to choose the right tenure and amount for your FD. You can use online calculators to determine the best combination of tenure and amount based on your financial goals.

Eligibility Criteria for Fixed Deposits

To apply for a fixed deposit, you need to meet certain eligibility criteria. These criteria include:

  • Minimum deposit amount: Most banks require a minimum deposit amount of Rs. 1,000
  • Maximum deposit amount: There is usually no upper limit on the deposit amount
  • Tenure: You can choose a tenure ranging from 7 days to 10 years

It's important to note that the interest rate on FDs may vary based on the tenure and amount of the deposit. Therefore, it's essential to compare the interest rates offered by different banks before making a decision.

How to Apply for Fixed Deposits

Applying for a fixed deposit is a straightforward process. You can apply for an FD online, offline, or through a mobile banking app. Here are the steps to apply for an FD online:

  1. Visit the website of the bank offering the FD
  2. Click on the 'Fixed Deposits' or 'Investments' tab
  3. Select the 'Open Fixed Deposit' option
  4. Fill in the required details, including the amount, tenure, and interest rate
  5. Review the details and submit the application
  6. Pay the deposit amount and confirm the transaction

Once your application is approved, you will receive a confirmation message or email. You can then start earning interest on your FD.
