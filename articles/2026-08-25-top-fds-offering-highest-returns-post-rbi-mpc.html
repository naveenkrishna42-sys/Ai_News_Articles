Impact of RBI's Monetary Policy on Fixed Deposit Interest Rates

The recent monetary policy meeting of the RBI has had a significant impact on fixed deposit interest rates. The RBI has raised the repo rate and reverse repo rate, which has led to an increase in the interest rates offered by financial institutions. This move is expected to control inflation and stabilize the economy.
