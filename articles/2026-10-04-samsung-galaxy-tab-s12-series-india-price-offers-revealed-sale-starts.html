Samsung Galaxy Tab S12 Series Launch in India

Samsung has confirmed the Indian launch of the Galaxy Tab S12 family, comprising the Tab S12+ and the flagship Tab S12 Ultra. The announcement clarifies the price point, sale date and promotional bundle that will accompany the rollout from 7 October 2026. The move targets professionals and students seeking a large‑screen Android tablet with productivity‑focused accessories.

Design and Display

Both models retain the slim metal chassis introduced with the previous S‑series tablets. The Ultra variant features a 12.4‑inch Super AMOLED panel with a resolution of 2800×1752 pixels and a 120 Hz adaptive refresh rate. The S12+ offers a slightly smaller 12.0‑inch LCD with similar colour accuracy but a standard 60 Hz refresh. Thin bezels and an S Pen slot on the top edge improve ergonomics for note‑taking and sketching.

Performance and Productivity Features

Under the hood, the Tab S12 Ultra is powered by Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 platform paired with up to 12 GB LPDDR5 RAM and storage options of 256 GB or 512 GB UFS 4.0. The S12+ uses the Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 with 8 GB/12 GB RAM configurations. Both tablets run Android 14 with One UI 6.1, offering DeX mode, multi‑active window and improved S Pen latency (<9 ms). Battery capacity is rated at 10,090 mAh, promising up to 14 hours of mixed usage.

Pricing and Availability

The Tab S12 Ultra Wi‑Fi model is priced at Rs 1,29,999, while the LTE variant carries a modest premium. The Tab S12+ starts at a lower tier, though exact figures were not disclosed in the source snippet. Sales commence on 7 October 2026 via Samsung’s online store, retail partners and major e‑commerce platforms. Early buyers can pre‑order from 3 October.

Offers and Value Proposition

Samsung’s launch bundle includes an instant bank discount of up to Rs 10,000 for select credit cards, no‑cost EMI tenures ranging from 3 to 24 months and an exchange bonus that can reach Rs 15,000 when trading in an older tablet. These incentives effectively lower the out‑of‑pocket expense, positioning the Ultra as a competitive alternative to premium laptops in the same price band.

Pros and Cons

  • Pros: Large 120 Hz AMOLED display, strong Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 performance, S Pen with low latency, DeX productivity suite.
  • Cons: High starting price, weight (~600 g) may feel bulky for prolonged handheld use, limited app optimisation for tablet‑specific Android features.
