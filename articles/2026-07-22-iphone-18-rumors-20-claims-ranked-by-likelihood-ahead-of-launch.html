iPhone 18 Rumors: 20 Claims Ranked by Likelihood Ahead of Launch

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • 20 iPhone 18 claims analyzed for credibility.
  • Price hike of up to $300 for Pro models is considered likely.
  • Variable aperture camera sensor rumored for iPhone 18 Pro Max.
  • Launch expected in two months; only 'richie rich' may need to wait.

20 Rumors, One Reality Check

Two months before Apple's expected iPhone 18 launch, the rumor mill is in overdrive. MacRumors has compiled 20 claims about the upcoming device and ranked them by likelihood. Some predictions seem solid. Others are speculative at best.

The list covers everything from camera upgrades to price jumps. If you are thinking about upgrading, it helps to separate likely changes from wishful thinking.

Price Hike: Likely and Painful

One of the most discussed rumors is a significant price increase. The Times of India reports that Apple may add up to $300 to the cost of the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max. That would push the top-end model well past the $1,500 mark in some markets.

Industry analysts point to rising component costs and inflation as reasons. If true, the iPhone 18 Pro could become the most expensive mainstream smartphone Apple has ever sold. India Today quipped that only the 'richie rich' should bother waiting for the Pro models.

Camera Upgrade: Variable Aperture Returns

TelecomTalk reports that the iPhone 18 Pro Max will feature an upgraded camera sensor with variable aperture. This technology allows the lens to physically open and close, giving photographers better control over depth of field and low-light performance.

Apple last used variable aperture in the iPhone 14 Pro's main camera. Bringing it back — possibly for the ultrawide or telephoto lens — would be a meaningful upgrade for serious mobile photographers. The rumor ranks high on the likelihood scale.

Design and Other Expected Changes

Deccan Herald lists key upgrades expected in the iPhone 18 Pro: a faster A19 chip, improved battery life, and possibly a titanium frame. These are iterative improvements, not revolutionary ones.

Other claims on the MacRumors list include under-display Face ID, a periscope zoom lens, and a solid-state button design. These are less certain. Apple has tested such features before but has not always shipped them.

What to Watch

The iPhone 18 lineup is expected in September 2026. Apple has not confirmed any details. Until the official announcement, treat every rumor with caution. The only thing that seems certain is that the Pro models will cost more — and that buyers will have to decide if the upgrades are worth the premium.
