A Cinematic Tribute to the Nation

As India prepares to celebrate its 77th Republic Day on January 26, 2026, many will be looking for ways to mark the occasion at home. For cinephiles, there is no better way than revisiting some of Bollywood's most stirring patriotic dramas.

IMDb has curated a list of five films that celebrate valour, sacrifice, and national pride. From recent blockbusters to critically acclaimed war epics, these movies offer a mix of high-octane action and emotional depth.

Uri: The Surgical Strike — A Modern Classic

Leading the list is the 2019 film Uri: The Surgical Strike, directed by Aditya Dhar. The movie dramatises the Indian Army's retaliatory strikes against terror launch pads in Pakistan-occupied Kashmir following the 2016 Uri attack.

With Vicky Kaushal in the lead role, the film was a massive commercial success. Its iconic dialogue, 'How's the josh?', became a nationwide catchphrase, cementing its place in popular culture. The film's realistic action sequences and gripping narrative earned it multiple National Film Awards.

Fighter — Soaring High with the IAF

Another recent addition to the patriotic genre is Fighter, released in 2024. Starring Hrithik Roshan and Deepika Padukone, this aerial action thriller showcases the Indian Air Force's daring missions.

The film's spectacular dogfight sequences and patriotic fervour made it a box-office hit. It blends personal drama with national duty, offering a fresh take on modern warfare. For those who enjoy high-stakes action, Fighter is a compelling watch.

Other Gems in the List

Beyond these two heavyweights, IMDb's list includes other films that have left an indelible mark on audiences. While the exact titles were not disclosed in the initial report, the selection is expected to feature a blend of classic and contemporary cinema.

Films like Border, Lakshya, or Raazi could easily find a place, given their enduring popularity. Each of these movies approaches patriotism differently, focusing on soldiers, spies, or ordinary citizens in extraordinary circumstances.

Why These Films Resonate

Patriotic films have long been a staple of Bollywood, evolving from the jingoistic dramas of the 1960s to the nuanced, technically polished productions of today. The current crop reflects a shift towards realism, with filmmakers investing heavily in research and production design.

Audiences today demand authenticity, and movies like Uri and Fighter deliver that by depicting real events or plausible scenarios with military precision. This blend of entertainment and education makes them ideal for Republic Day viewing.
