CJP activists attacked in Rajasthan during school inspection

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • CJP activists attacked during school inspection in Rajasthan village
  • Arvind Kejriwal and Ashutosh Ranka blame BJP workers for the assault
  • CJP leader Dipke issues 48-hour deadline for action after the clash

Volunteers of the Common Civilian Party (CJP) were allegedly assaulted in a Rajasthan village on Friday while attempting to inspect a local school. The incident, which took place during what the party described as a routine inspection, has triggered sharp political reactions and a fresh war of words between the CJP and the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP).

What happened?

The CJP team arrived at the village as part of its ongoing school inspection campaign. According to party workers, they were met with hostility from a group of locals, allegedly linked to the BJP, who opposed their presence. The situation quickly escalated into a physical altercation, leaving several activists injured.

Local police have registered a case, though officials have not yet confirmed the exact sequence of events or the number of people involved. The CJP has alleged that the attack was premeditated and aimed at derailing their campaign, which has been critical of the state of government schools in Rajasthan.

Political fallout

The attack has drawn sharp condemnation from CJP leaders. Party convenor Arvind Kejriwal lashed out at the BJP, asking, “What’s going on in the country?” He accused the ruling party of orchestrating violence against his volunteers and demanded a thorough investigation.

Senior CJP leader Ashutosh Ranka went further, directly blaming BJP workers for the assault. “This is a deliberate attempt to stop us from exposing the sorry state of education in Rajasthan. The BJP is scared of the truth,” he said. The BJP has not yet issued a formal response to the allegations.

Broader campaign, rising tension

The CJP’s school inspection drive has been a flashpoint in Rajasthan for weeks. The party has been highlighting poor infrastructure in government schools, including one instance where classes were reportedly held in a buffalo shed. The campaign has gained traction on social media, embarrassing the state government.

In a related development, CJP leader Dipke has given authorities a 48-hour deadline to act on the complaints filed by the injured volunteers. He warned that if no action is taken, the party would escalate its protest, possibly moving from Jantar Mantar to Jaipur. “We will not be silenced,” Dipke said.

What next?

With the 48-hour deadline ticking, all eyes are on the Rajasthan Police and the state administration. The CJP has said it will decide its next course of action based on the police response. Meanwhile, the party is expected to release a detailed report on the attack, including video evidence, to bolster its demand for a probe.

The incident has added to the charged political atmosphere in the state, with the CJP vowing to continue its inspections despite the risks. Whether the authorities can defuse the tension before the deadline remains to be seen.
