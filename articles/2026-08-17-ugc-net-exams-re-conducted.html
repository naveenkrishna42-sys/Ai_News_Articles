UGC-NET Exams Re-Conducted

NTA Admits Errors in UGC-NET Exams

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has admitted to errors in the UGC-NET exams for English, Sociology, and Commerce. Following complaints from candidates, the NTA has decided to re-conduct the exams for these three subjects.

According to reports, the errors included typos and incorrect questions. The NTA has ordered a fresh exam for the affected subjects after an audit found question paper errors.

Errors in Question Papers

The errors in the question papers were significant, with typos and incorrect questions affecting the exams. For example, 'Putzer for Ritzer, Parsow for Parsons' were some of the typos found in the question papers.

The NTA has released provisional answer keys for 84 subjects of the UGC-NET June 2026 exam. However, the errors in the question papers for English, Sociology, and Commerce have led to the re-exams being scheduled.

Next Steps

Candidates who appeared for the exams in these three subjects will be notified about the re-exam dates. The NTA has not yet confirmed the dates for the re-exams, but they are expected to be announced soon.

Audit Finds Question Paper Errors

The audit conducted by the NTA found significant errors in the question papers for English, Sociology, and Commerce. The errors were found to be serious enough to affect the outcome of the exams, leading to the decision to re-conduct the exams.

The NTA has taken steps to ensure that such errors do not occur in the future. The re-exams will be conducted to ensure that the candidates are given a fair chance to appear for the exams without any errors affecting their performance.

The candidates who appeared for the exams in these three subjects can expect an update on the re-exam dates soon. The NTA will notify the candidates about the dates and other details of the re-exams.
