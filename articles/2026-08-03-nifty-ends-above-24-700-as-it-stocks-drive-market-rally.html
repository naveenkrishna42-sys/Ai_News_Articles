Nifty Ends Above 24,700 as IT Stocks Drive Market Rally

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nifty closes above 24,700 for the first time, led by IT stocks.
  • IT index outperforms on strong earnings outlook and global demand.
  • Broader market gains but sectoral rotation keeps volatility in check.
  • Analysts advise caution amid rich valuations and global cues.

Mumbai: The Nifty 50 index closed above the 24,700 mark on Monday, powered by a sharp rally in information technology stocks. The benchmark settled at 24,712.35, up 1.2 per cent, as investors piled into IT heavyweights on expectations of robust earnings and steady global demand.

The rally was broad-based but led by the IT sector, which gained over 2.5 per cent. Infosys, TCS, and HCL Technologies were among the top contributors, with each rising between 2 and 3 per cent. The surge came after a series of positive broker notes highlighted improving deal pipelines and stable client spending in the US and Europe.

IT Stocks Outperform Amid Global Optimism

Software exporters took the lead as the rupee held steady and US tech peers posted strong quarterly numbers. The Nifty IT index hit a fresh 52-week high, reflecting renewed confidence in the sector's growth trajectory.

Market participants said the rally was driven by expectations that IT firms will report healthy order books in the upcoming earnings season. "The sentiment is clearly positive for IT, with clients resuming discretionary spending," said a senior analyst at a domestic brokerage, requesting anonymity.

Broader Indices Follow Suit

The Sensex also climbed 1.1 per cent to close at 80,890.15, while the Nifty Midcap 100 and Smallcap 100 indices rose 0.8 per cent and 0.6 per cent, respectively. However, sectoral moves were mixed, with FMCG and pharma stocks lagging the broader market.

Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) were net buyers in the cash market, pumping in over Rs 3,200 crore, according to provisional data. Domestic institutional investors (DIIs) too remained supportive, though their buying was comparatively modest.

Volatility Index Eases

India VIX, the volatility gauge, slipped 3.5 per cent to 12.8, indicating a calmer market stance. Traders said the drop reflected reduced anxiety ahead of key domestic inflation data due later this week.

Despite the strong close, analysts cautioned that valuations remain stretched in several pockets. The Nifty's price-to-earnings ratio is hovering near 22 times, above its long-term average. "One good day doesn't change the underlying picture. Investors should stay selective," noted a fund manager at a mid-sized asset management company.

What to Watch Next

Market participants will now keep a close eye on the release of India's consumer price index (CPI) data, scheduled for Wednesday. A softer print could fuel hopes of rate cuts, while a surprise uptick might trigger profit-booking.

Global cues, particularly the US Federal Reserve's stance on interest rates, will also dictate near-term direction. For now, the momentum is with the bulls, but the sustainability of the rally will depend on earnings delivery and macroeconomic data.
