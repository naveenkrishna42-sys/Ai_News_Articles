How Rs 40,000 found in washroom unravelled the Ayodhya Ram Temple donation theft

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • A cleaner found Rs 40,000 in a washroom at the Ram Temple complex, triggering a probe.
  • Police traced the cash to a donation box theft, leading to the arrest of temple staff.
  • The investigation has uncovered a larger racket, with key documents recovered from an accused's rented house.
  • A detailed report on the theft is ready and will be submitted to authorities soon.

Chance Discovery in Washroom

A routine cleaning session at the Ram Temple in Ayodhya took an unexpected turn when a worker found Rs 40,000 in cash inside a washroom. The discovery, made earlier this month, seemed odd — why would anyone leave that much money in a toilet?

The temple management alerted the police, who began looking into the matter. What started as a minor enquiry soon snowballed into a full-fledged investigation, revealing a carefully executed donation theft operation.

How the Probe Unfolded

Police reviewed CCTV footage and questioned temple employees. They found that the cash had been taken from a donation box. The suspect, a temple staffer, had allegedly stashed the money in the washroom to retrieve it later, but was caught off guard by the cleaner's discovery.

Investigators arrested the employee, and during interrogation, he reportedly named others involved. The police have since recovered key documents from a rented house linked to one of the accused. These papers are believed to contain details of multiple thefts over several months.

Scope of the Theft

The Ram Temple, a major pilgrimage site, attracts lakhs of devotees who donate generously. Officials suspect that the theft may have been going on for a while, with the accused siphoning off cash from donation boxes. The exact amount stolen is yet to be determined.

Sources told NDTV that a detailed report on the theft is ready and will be submitted to the authorities anytime now. The report is expected to outline the modus operandi and the total financial loss.

Political Reactions

The incident has also drawn political attention. The Congress party cited the theft to question the transparency of the temple's administration. In Parliament, Congress leaders demanded that Prime Minister Narendra Modi break his silence on the floor of the House, pointing to his earlier announcement about the Ram Temple trust.

The ruling party has not yet responded to these demands. The temple trust, meanwhile, has said it is cooperating with the police and has tightened security around donation boxes.

What Comes Next

The police are expected to file a chargesheet soon, based on the recovered documents and witness statements. The accused remain in custody, and further arrests cannot be ruled out as the investigation widens.

For now, the temple management is reviewing its internal processes to prevent such incidents in the future. The focus remains on ensuring that devotees' offerings are handled with the utmost security and transparency.
