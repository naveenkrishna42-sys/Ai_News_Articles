Bengaluru STP project delayed by three years, completion uncertain

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • The STP project was originally scheduled for completion three years ago but is still ongoing.
  • Residents report untreated sewage flowing into local water bodies.
  • Officials cite funding and logistical issues for the delay.

A sewage treatment plant (STP) in Bengaluru has missed its original deadline by three years, with no clear end in sight. The project, intended to treat waste from several residential areas, was supposed to be operational by 2023. It remains incomplete.

Residents raise alarm over untreated waste

Locals living near the site say the delay has led to untreated sewage seeping into stormwater drains and nearby lakes. "The smell is unbearable during the rains," a resident told Bangalore Mirror. The situation has worsened during the monsoon, with complaints of water contamination.

Environmental activists have flagged the issue, noting that the incomplete plant poses a health risk. "We have been raising this for months. The authorities need to act," said a local activist.

Officials blame funding and logistics

Sources in the Bengaluru Water Supply and Sewerage Board (BWSSB) said the project faced multiple hurdles. "There were issues with land acquisition and contractor payments. We are working to resolve them," an official said. The board did not provide a revised completion date.

The project was part of a larger plan to upgrade the city's sewage network. Bengaluru generates over 1,400 million litres of sewage daily, but treatment capacity remains inadequate.

What the delay means for the city

Environmental experts warn that the delay could have long-term consequences. Untreated sewage entering water bodies contributes to algal blooms and kills aquatic life. Bellandur Lake, a notorious example, has seen toxic froth due to untreated waste.

Residents want the BWSSB to set a clear timeline. "We are tired of promises. We need action," a local community leader said.

The project's cost has also escalated due to the delay. Original estimates were in crores, but officials have not confirmed the current figure.

Next steps under scrutiny

The BWSSB has not announced any fresh deadlines. The matter is expected to be raised in the next council meeting. Until then, residents remain in limbo.
