Deepika Padukone Confirms No Brahmastra 2 Role: Franchise Troubles Mount

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Deepika Padukone appeared only in a cameo in the 2022 original, not the sequel
  • Jio Studios has reportedly opted out of Brahmastra 2, raising questions about the project's viability
  • The sequel faces mounting production and casting challenges after the original's mixed box office performance

End of the Rumor Trail

After weeks of speculation, Deepika Padukone's spokesperson has firmly shut down any possibility of the actor appearing in Brahmastra 2. In a clear statement, her team confirmed that "all recent reports are untrue" regarding her involvement in the sequel to the 2022 sci-fi spectacle. This marks a definitive end to rumors that had gained traction across entertainment portals and social media.

What the Original Role Actually Was

It is worth clarifying what Padukone's actual contribution to the franchise has been. In Brahmastra: Part One - Shiva, released in September 2022, she played a cameo as a scientist named Junali. Her screen time was limited, and the role was designed primarily to support her real-life husband Ranbir Kapoor, who starred as the protagonist Shiva. The cameo did not set up any narrative thread that would necessitate her return in a sequel.

The Bigger Picture: A Franchise Struggling to Take Off

The denial arrives at a precarious moment for the Brahmastra franchise. Multiple reports indicate that Jio Studios, which was initially attached as a co-producer, has exited the project. This departure raises serious questions about funding, production timeline, and overall feasibility of a sequel that has already faced significant budget overruns.

The original film's performance offers context. Despite a massive budget reportedly exceeding ₹410 crore, the film earned approximately ₹430 crore worldwide. While this was not a flop by conventional standards, it fell short of the blockbuster expectations set by its ambitious scale and star-studded cast. For a sequel to justify its existence, producers need cost-cutting measures—and losing Padukone, who commands a substantial acting fee, may actually align with those goals.

Why This Matters for the Franchise's Future

The absence of Padukone is unlikely to doom Brahmastra 2, but it strips away a potential drawing point. The franchise's survival depends on whether filmmakers can deliver a compelling story at a fraction of the original's cost. Director Ayan Mukerji and the team behind Dharma Productions must now convince audiences that the universe they built is worth revisiting, even without the glamour of Bollywood's highest-paid actress.

Assessing the Claims

  • The spokesperson's statement is categorical and leaves no room for reinterpretation
  • Jio Studios' exit, while not officially confirmed, has been reported by credible outlets
  • The sequel's development remains shrouded in uncertainty with no announced release date
  • The original film's financial performance was underwhelming relative to its massive budget

The Brahmastra franchise now faces a crossroads. Without a clear production timeline, studio backing, or star confirmation beyond Kapoor, the sequel exists more as a promise than a certainty. Whether Mukerji can resurrect this ambitious mythological universe from development limbo remains to be seen.
