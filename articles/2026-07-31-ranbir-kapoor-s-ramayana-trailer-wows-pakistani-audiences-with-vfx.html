Cross-border praise for the epic spectacle

The trailer for Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana, starring Ranbir Kapoor as Rama and Yash as Ravana, has sparked widespread admiration among Pakistani audiences, with many lauding the film's visual effects and grandeur.
