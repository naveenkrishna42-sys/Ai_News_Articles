Odisha Temples Overflow as Ram Navami 2026 Draws Massive Crowds

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Religion Original source ↗
Key points
  • Lakhs of devotees visited temples across Odisha for Ram Navami 2026
  • Special rituals and security measures were implemented at major shrines
  • The festival marks the birth of Lord Rama and is celebrated with great fervour

Odisha witnessed an extraordinary display of faith on Friday as Ram Navami 2026 drew lakhs of devotees to temples across the state. From early morning, queues snaked outside major shrines as the faithful gathered to mark the birth of Lord Rama.

Massive Turnout at Key Temples

The spiritual fervour was palpable, particularly at the Ram Mandir in Bhubaneswar and the Jagannath Temple in Puri. Devotees, many walking barefoot and carrying offerings, waited for hours to catch a glimpse of the deity.

Local authorities estimated the crowd at several lakhs across the state. Temple trusts reported that the numbers this year exceeded those of previous years, reflecting a surge in religious enthusiasm.

Special Rituals and Celebrations

Temples conducted elaborate rituals, including the recitation of the Ramayana and special bhajans. In many places, processions carried idols of Lord Rama through the streets, accompanied by chants and traditional music.

At the Sri Ram Temple in Cuttack, priests performed a grand abhishekam using milk, honey, and holy water. The ceremony drew a packed congregation, with many devotees offering prayers for peace and prosperity.

Volunteer groups distributed prasadam—sweet rice, fruits, and laddoos—to the crowds. The atmosphere remained orderly despite the large numbers, thanks to meticulous planning.

Security and Logistical Arrangements

State police deployed additional forces at all major temples to manage the crowds. Barricades, watchtowers, and CCTV cameras were installed at strategic points. Officials confirmed that no untoward incidents were reported.

The Bhubaneswar Municipal Corporation set up temporary water stations and medical booths along the routes. Traffic diversions were put in place to ease congestion, and special buses ran from outskirts to temple areas.

"We had coordinated with temple committees weeks in advance," a senior police officer said. "The goal was to ensure devotees could worship without inconvenience."

Cultural Programs Across Districts

Beyond the temples, several districts organised Ram Leela performances and cultural events. In Puri, a 10-hour marathon recitation of the Ramcharitmanas drew thousands. Similar events were held in Sambalpur and Rourkela.

Merchants reported brisk sales of flowers, garlands, and religious paraphernalia. Markets near temple towns stayed open late, catering to the festive demand.

The state government had earlier declared a public holiday for Ram Navami, allowing families to participate in the celebrations.

As night fell, the temples remained lit and active. Devotees continued to pour in, determined to offer their prayers before the day ended.

Looking ahead, temple authorities are now preparing for the upcoming Akshaya Tritiya festival, expected to draw even larger crowds in the coming weeks.
