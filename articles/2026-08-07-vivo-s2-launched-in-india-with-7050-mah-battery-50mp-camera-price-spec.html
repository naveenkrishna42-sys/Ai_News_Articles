Vivo has officially launched the S2 in India, bringing a massive 7050 mAh battery and a 50MP camera to the premium segment. The device, which had its full specifications and pricing leaked ahead of the launch, is now available for buyers looking for a feature-packed upper mid-range smartphone.

Price and availability in India

The Vivo S2 is positioned for premium segment buyers, though the company has not yet revealed the exact pricing structure for all variants. Industry trackers suggest it will compete directly with the Motorola Edge 70 Pro, which has been a strong contender in the same bracket.

Early reports indicate that the phone will be available across major online and offline retail channels. Vivo is expected to announce the full price list and availability details shortly, but the device has already generated significant buzz among enthusiasts.

Battery and charging

The headline feature of the Vivo S2 is its 7050 mAh battery, a capacity that is rare in this segment. This is a significant upgrade over most rivals, which typically offer batteries in the 5000-6000 mAh range.

With such a large cell, users can expect all-day usage on a single charge, and likely even longer for moderate use. However, the company has not yet confirmed the charging speed or whether a charger is included in the box, though leaks suggest support for fast charging.

Camera and display

The Vivo S2 comes with a 50MP primary camera, which should deliver sharp and detailed images in various lighting conditions. Vivo has not disclosed the full camera specifications, including the ultrawide and macro sensors, but the primary shooter is expected to be the main highlight.

The device also features a curved display, a design element that was once reserved for flagship models. This marks a return of the familiar S-series design language, blending aesthetics with functionality. The display is likely to offer a high refresh rate, though the exact panel details have not been officially confirmed.

Comparison with Motorola Edge 70 Pro

Early comparisons between the Vivo S2 and the Motorola Edge 70 Pro suggest a close contest. The Edge 70 Pro has been praised for its near-stock Android experience and solid performance, while the Vivo S2 counters with its larger battery and curved display.

Both phones target the same audience, but the Vivo S2's battery capacity could be the deciding factor for power users. The camera setup and software experience will also play a crucial role in how the two devices stack up in real-world usage.
