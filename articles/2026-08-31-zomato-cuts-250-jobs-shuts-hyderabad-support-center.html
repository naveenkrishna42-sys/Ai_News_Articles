Zomato cuts 250 jobs, shuts Hyderabad support center

📅 2026-08-31 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Zomato lays off 250 employees, shuts Hyderabad customer support center
  • Company offers four months' pay and ex-gratia to affected workers
  • Layoffs come amid broader industry challenges and cost-cutting measures

Zomato's Cost-Cutting Measures

Food delivery giant Zomato has announced layoffs of 250 employees, including the closure of its customer support center in Hyderabad. The move comes as the company seeks to streamline operations and reduce costs in the face of increasing competition and economic pressures.

Financial Compensation for Affected Employees

The company has offered four months' pay and ex-gratia to the affected employees. This financial support aims to provide a safety net for those who have been impacted by the restructuring. However, the exact amount of ex-gratia has not been disclosed, leaving many employees uncertain about their future.

Industry Context and Broader Impact

The layoffs at Zomato are part of a broader trend in the food delivery industry, where companies are facing increased competition and rising operational costs. This has led to a wave of cost-cutting measures, including layoffs and the closure of support centers. The closure of the Hyderabad customer support center is particularly notable, as it highlights the company's commitment to centralizing operations and reducing overhead costs.

Expert Analysis and Criticism

Industry experts have expressed concern about the layoffs, arguing that they could have a negative impact on customer service and employee morale. They also question whether the cost-cutting measures will be sufficient to address the company's financial challenges. Some analysts believe that Zomato may need to reconsider its strategy and focus on innovation and customer retention to remain competitive in the market.

Key Takeaways and Future Outlook

The layoffs at Zomato serve as a reminder of the challenges facing the food delivery industry. As companies continue to grapple with rising costs and increased competition, it is essential for them to find ways to innovate and adapt to changing consumer preferences. The future of Zomato and other food delivery companies will depend on their ability to navigate these challenges and emerge stronger.
