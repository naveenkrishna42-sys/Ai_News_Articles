Kangana Ranaut Hits Back At Trolls: 'Don't Mislead The Youth'

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kangana Ranaut reacted strongly to trolls criticising her movie choices.
  • She warned against misleading the youth with false narratives.
  • The actress emphasised the need for responsible discourse on social media.

Actor and Member of Parliament Kangana Ranaut has fired back at online critics who have been targeting her over her recent film roles. In a sharp response, she accused the trolls of attempting to distort her work and warned them against misleading the youth.

Taking to social media, Ranaut did not hold back, stating that her body of work speaks for itself and that those attacking her are driven by malice rather than genuine critique. “Don’t mislead the youth,” she wrote, addressing the wave of negativity that has followed her latest releases.

A Career Under Scrutiny

The backlash appears to stem from a section of social media users who have questioned the consistency and intent behind Ranaut's choice of scripts. Some have accused her of prioritising propaganda over art, a charge she has consistently rejected.

Ranaut, known for her outspoken nature, has often found herself at the centre of political and cultural debates. Her films, frequently centred on nationalist themes and historical figures, have drawn both praise and criticism.

Defending Artistic Freedom

In her latest statement, the actor asserted that every film she has chosen has been driven by a sense of purpose. She maintained that her roles reflect her convictions and that no amount of online harassment would force her to alter her path.

She also called out what she described as a coordinated effort to tarnish her reputation. The actor urged her followers to see through the noise and focus on the substance of her work rather than the chatter surrounding it.

Reaction From Fans and Critics

Her remarks have sparked a fresh round of debate on social media, with supporters rallying behind her and detractors doubling down on their criticism. The exchange highlights the increasingly polarised environment in which public figures operate, especially those with strong political affiliations.

This is not the first time Ranaut has clashed with trolls. Over the years, she has frequently used her platforms to respond to detractors, often turning the spotlight back on their motives.

The Larger Conversation

The episode also raises questions about the nature of online discourse in India, where celebrities are often subjected to intense scrutiny. While some argue that public figures must be open to criticism, others believe the line between critique and harassment has become blurred.

Ranaut’s response underscores a growing trend among celebrities to challenge online abuse rather than ignore it. Whether this leads to more responsible engagement or further polarisation remains to be seen.

As the debate continues, the actor has made it clear that she will not be silenced. Her message to the trolls was simple: engage with the work, not the noise. For now, her supporters are watching closely to see how the conversation evolves.
