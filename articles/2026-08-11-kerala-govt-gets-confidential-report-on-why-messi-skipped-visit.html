Kerala govt gets 'confidential' report on why Messi skipped visit

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sports department submits 'highly confidential' report to Kerala government on Messi's no-show
  • Muslim Youth League leader P.K. Firoz seeks VACB enquiry into the 'Messi affair'
  • Official inquiry report calls the Kerala Messi match plan a 'massive scam'

Thiruvananthapuram: The Kerala sports department has finally told the state government why football icon Lionel Messi never set foot in the state, and it has done so in a manner that has raised more questions than answers. The report, marked 'highly confidential', was submitted to the government amid growing political heat over the failed attempt to bring the Argentine legend to Kerala.

The development comes days after an official inquiry report described the entire Messi match plan as a massive scam. The state government, which had earlier projected the visit as a major sporting coup, is now facing demands for a thorough probe into what went wrong.

The confidential report

According to sources, the sports department's report details the sequence of events, communications, and financial transactions related to the proposed Messi visit. However, the 'highly confidential' tag has ensured that the contents remain under wraps for now.

Officials have not yet confirmed what the report says or why it needed such strict secrecy. The government is yet to make a statement on whether the report will be made public or tabled in the assembly.

Political heat and demand for probe

Muslim Youth League leader P.K. Firoz has now stepped up pressure, seeking a Vigilance and Anti-Corruption Bureau (VACB) enquiry into what he calls the 'Messi affair'. Firoz's demand adds to a chorus of opposition voices alleging that public money and government machinery were misused in the pursuit of a dream that never materialised.

The official inquiry report, which has already been made public, does not mince words. It calls the Kerala Messi match plan a massive scam, pointing to critical lapses in the previous government's proceedings. The sports department has now flagged these lapses in its confidential submission, though the specifics remain unclear.

What went wrong?

Questions have been raised about how a plan to host Messi, arguably the most sought-after footballer of his generation, fell apart so spectacularly. Reports suggest that negotiations were handled without proper due diligence, and that promises were made without concrete agreements in place.

Former chief minister Pinarayi Vijayan's role has also come under scrutiny, with some media reports asking whether he 'red carded' the country's laws to lure Messi to Kerala. The opposition has alleged that rules were bent to facilitate the visit, though no formal charges have been made.

The road ahead

The VACB will now have to decide whether to take up the enquiry sought by Firoz. The state government, meanwhile, is expected to respond to the confidential report in the coming days.

All eyes are now on whether the government will declassify the report or continue to keep the details under lock and key. The political fallout of the 'Messi affair' is far from over, and the coming weeks will reveal whether this was a case of mismanagement, misplaced ambition, or something more serious.
