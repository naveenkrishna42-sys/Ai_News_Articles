SBI Q1 net profit up 10% to Rs 21,121 crore, beats estimates; stock jumps 3%

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • SBI's Q1 net profit rose 10% year-on-year to Rs 21,121 crore, surpassing analyst expectations.
  • Healthy loan growth drove the earnings beat, boosting investor sentiment.
  • Shares of India's largest public sector bank jumped over 3% following the results announcement.

State Bank of India (SBI) opened its FY27 earnings season with a solid beat. The country's largest public sector lender reported a 10% year-on-year rise in net profit to Rs 21,121 crore for the April-June quarter, surpassing street estimates. Investors responded swiftly, pushing the stock up over 3% in early trade.

The profit growth was underpinned by healthy loan demand, a key indicator that the bank's core lending engine remains robust despite a competitive market. The results come at a time when the banking sector has been navigating tight margins and shifting deposit dynamics.

What drove the earnings beat

Analysts had broadly expected a modest profit increase, with some flagging concerns over net interest income (NII) pressure and elevated deposit costs. SBI's actual numbers, however, painted a more resilient picture. The bank's loan book expansion appears to have offset any margin compression, allowing profitability to scale higher.

The management's commentary on asset quality and provision coverage will be closely parsed in the post-earnings call. Market participants are also watching for guidance on deposit growth, which has been a sector-wide challenge as customers shift toward higher-yielding instruments.

Stock market reaction

SBI shares climbed more than 3% in morning deals on Friday, reflecting the positive surprise in the numbers. The rally also lifted sentiment in the broader public sector banking pack, with investors reading the result as a sign of underlying strength in the state-owned banking cohort.

The stock's move underscores the market's appetite for banks that can deliver growth without compromising on asset quality. SBI has consistently outperformed its peers on loan growth, and Friday's numbers reinforce that narrative.

Context and outlook

The Q1 performance arrives against the backdrop of a dynamic interest rate cycle. With the Reserve Bank of India's policy stance evolving, banks have had to balance lending growth with margin preservation. SBI's ability to beat estimates despite these headwinds signals operational resilience.

Looking ahead, investor focus will shift to the management's full-year guidance, particularly on credit growth targets and the trajectory of net interest margins. The bank's deposit mobilisation strategy will also be under the lens, given the industry-wide competition for low-cost funds.

For now, the market has taken the numbers in its stride. The coming weeks will reveal whether this momentum can be sustained as the bank navigates the remainder of the fiscal year.
