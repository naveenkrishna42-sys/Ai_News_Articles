Kangana Ranaut Calls Naseeruddin Shah 'Fox' in Heated Spat Over Remark

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kangana Ranaut called Naseeruddin Shah a 'lomdi' (fox) after his 'dog with a bone' remark on the Jharkhand protest.
  • Piyush Mishra also slammed the veteran actor, escalating the war of words.
  • Ranaut questioned Shah's loyalty, saying he fights for a neighbouring country despite living in India.

The war of words between Bollywood veterans and the current political class has taken a sharp turn. Actor-politician Kangana Ranaut has responded to Naseeruddin Shah's recent 'dog with a bone' comment, calling him a 'lomdi' (fox). The exchange comes amid an ongoing debate over the Jharkhand student protest.

Ranaut's retort was sharp and personal. She questioned Shah's allegiance, saying in Hindi, "Woh iss desh ke hai lekin ladte padosi desh ke liye hai" (He belongs to this country but fights for a neighbouring one). The remark has added fuel to a controversy that has been simmering for days.

The 'Dog with a Bone' Comment

The spat began when Naseeruddin Shah made a remark that was widely interpreted as critical of the government's handling of the Jharkhand protest. He reportedly used the phrase 'dog with a bone' to describe the situation, a comment that did not sit well with many.

While the veteran actor is known for his outspoken views, this particular comment struck a nerve. It drew an immediate reaction from several quarters, including the ruling party's supporters and now, prominent figures from the film industry.

Piyush Mishra Joins the Fray

Adding his voice to the chorus, actor and poet Piyush Mishra also slammed Shah. He echoed Ranaut's sentiment, calling Shah a 'fox' and questioning his intent. Mishra's intervention has turned the exchange into a broader cultural clash, with artists taking sides.

The timing is significant. The Jharkhand protest has become a flashpoint, drawing reactions from celebrities, politicians, and the public. The debate has moved beyond the protest itself to questions of patriotism and loyalty, with Ranaut's latest jibe crystallising that shift.

An Ongoing Feud

This is not the first time Kangana Ranaut and Naseeruddin Shah have been at odds. Their differing political views have often put them on opposite sides of public debates. However, this latest exchange is notable for its personal tone.

Ranaut, who has been a vocal critic of what she calls 'liberal hypocrisy', seems to be using this moment to sharpen her attack. By framing Shah's comment as anti-national, she is tapping into a familiar narrative that has defined much of the recent cultural discourse.

What Lies Ahead

As the controversy continues to trend, neither side shows signs of backing down. Shah has not yet responded to the 'fox' jibe, and it remains to be seen whether he will. The episode has also reignited discussions about the role of artists in political debates.

The coming days are likely to see more reactions from both camps. With the Jharkhand protest still unresolved, this cultural skirmish may well be a preview of a longer, more contentious battle over free speech and patriotism in Indian public life.
