Amarnath Yatra Suspended From Today Amid Heavy Rain Forecast

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Amarnath Yatra suspended from July 19 due to heavy rain warning
  • Vaishno Devi and Shiv Khori pilgrimages also temporarily halted
  • Ganderbal administration issues weather advisory for residents and pilgrims

Yatra Suspended Amid Heavy Rain Warning

The annual Amarnath Yatra has been suspended from today, July 19, following a forecast of heavy rainfall in the region. Officials took the decision late on Saturday to ensure the safety of pilgrims and security personnel.

Alongside the Amarnath Yatra, the Vaishno Devi and Shiv Khori pilgrimages have also been temporarily halted. The move comes as the India Meteorological Department issued a warning for widespread rain and thunderstorms across Jammu and Kashmir over the next 48 hours.

Weather Advisory Issued

The Ganderbal administration has issued a detailed weather advisory for residents, tourists, and Amarnath pilgrims. It advises against non-essential travel and urges people to stay indoors or in safe shelters during the period of heavy rain.

Authorities have cautioned that landslides and flash floods are possible in vulnerable areas along the yatra route. The administration has also directed all relief and rescue teams to remain on standby.

Impact on Pilgrims

Thousands of pilgrims who were either en route or waiting at base camps have been affected by the suspension. Officials have arranged for temporary accommodation and medical aid at camps in Baltal and Pahalgam.

Pilgrims have been asked to cooperate with security personnel and follow all advisories. The suspension will remain in effect until weather conditions improve and the route is declared safe for movement.

Safety First

This is not the first time the yatra has been halted due to adverse weather. In previous years, similar precautions have been taken to prevent accidents and loss of life in the mountainous terrain.

The decision to suspend all three pilgrimages simultaneously reflects the severity of the forecast. Officials have not yet confirmed when the yatra will resume, stating that a review will be conducted once the weather clears.

Rescue teams and medical personnel have been deployed at key points along the route. The administration has also set up control rooms to coordinate response efforts and provide real-time updates to pilgrims and their families.

What to Watch

The weather is expected to improve by Tuesday, after which a reassessment will be made. Pilgrims are advised to stay in touch with official channels for updates on the resumption of the yatra.
