Prabhadevi Party Brawl: Police Probe Conflicting Claims

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • A brawl broke out at a party in Prabhadevi late Monday night.
  • Police are investigating conflicting claims from two groups about who started the fight.
  • No arrests have been made so far; CCTV footage is being reviewed.
  • Minor injuries were reported, with one person treated at a local hospital.

Midnight Chaos at Prabhadevi Gathering

A late-night party in Prabhadevi turned violent on Monday, leaving police with two contradictory versions of what happened. The brawl, which erupted around 11:30 pm at a residential flat near the Prabhadevi temple, drew neighbours and security personnel to the scene.

Officers from the Prabhadevi police station reached the spot within minutes. They found a group of people arguing loudly outside the flat. A few people had minor injuries, and one person was taken to a nearby hospital for treatment. He was discharged after first aid.

Two Versions, One Fight

The first group claims the trouble started when some guests made inappropriate comments to a woman at the party. They say when the woman's friends objected, the accused group got aggressive and started throwing punches. Bottles and furniture were used in the scuffle, according to this account.

The second group tells a different story. They allege the fight began after a dispute over a parking spot. They claim the other side was drunk and picked a fight over a minor bump between two vehicles. This group insists they were merely defending themselves when the other party turned physical.

Police officials have not confirmed which narrative is accurate. They are recording statements from both parties and from neutral witnesses.

No Arrests Yet, CCTV Under Scrutiny

No arrests have been made in the case so far. Senior inspector Rajesh Patil of Prabhadevi police station said the investigation is in its early stages. "We are verifying the claims made by both sides. Our teams are collecting CCTV footage from the building and nearby establishments," Patil told reporters on Tuesday morning.

Police are also checking call records and mobile phone data to establish the sequence of events. They have not ruled out the possibility of adding charges of assault or rioting if evidence points to a larger conspiracy. The flat where the party was held belongs to a local businessman, who was not present at the time of the incident.

Neighbors Complain of Noise, Safety Concerns

Residents of the building, who spoke on condition of anonymity, said such parties have become a nuisance. "Music blares till 2 am often. This was bound to happen sooner or later," one resident said. Another neighbour said they had complained to the society management multiple times about late-night gatherings in the flat.

Police have urged residents to report any suspicious activity. They have also asked the building society to tighten security and ensure that parties are registered with the local beat officer.

Investigators are expected to submit a preliminary report to senior officers by Thursday. The case will likely be transferred to a special team if the probe reveals a pattern of such incidents in the area.
