The wait is finally over for fans of Westeros. The Season 3 finale of 'House of the Dragon' lands this weekend, bringing the latest chapter of the Targaryen civil war to a close. If you are planning to watch the finale as it drops, here is everything you need to know about the release schedule and where to stream it.

When and where to watch the finale

The final episode of Season 3, Episode 8, is set to premiere on Sunday night in the United States. For viewers in India, that translates to an early Monday morning release. The show airs on HBO in the US and is available for streaming on Max. Indian audiences can watch the episode on JioCinema, which holds the streaming rights for the franchise in the region.

As per the standard release pattern, the episode will be available at the same global time. That means you will need to adjust your alarm clocks if you want to avoid spoilers on social media. The exact time may vary depending on your time zone, so it is advisable to check your local listing.

The buildup has been intense, with the directors teasing major dragon-on-dragon action in the closing episodes.

According to reports, the showrunners have promised several surprises involving the dragons.
