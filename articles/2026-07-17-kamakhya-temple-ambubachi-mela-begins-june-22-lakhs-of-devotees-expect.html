Kamakhya Temple Ambubachi Mela Begins June 22; Lakhs of Devotees Expected

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Religion Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ambubachi Mela at Kamakhya Temple starts June 22, runs for four days.
  • Thousands of devotees and sadhus are expected to attend.
  • Temple remains closed for three days during the ritual period, reopens on the fourth day.

The Kamakhya Temple in Guwahati, Assam, will observe the annual Ambubachi Mela from June 22. The four-day festival is one of the most important events at the ancient Shakti Peetha, drawing thousands of devotees and sadhus from across the country.

Rituals and significance

Ambubachi Mela marks the annual menstruation of Goddess Kamakhya. According to tradition, the temple remains closed for three days during this period. Devotees believe the goddess goes through her annual cycle, and the earth becomes impure for these three days.

The temple reopens on the fourth day, when special rituals are performed. This reopening is a major draw for pilgrims who queue for hours to offer prayers. The four-day event is capped by a grand celebration of the goddess's fertility and creative power.

Massive gathering expected

Officials at the Kamakhya Temple have made arrangements to handle the large influx of pilgrims. Thousands of devotees, including many sadhus and ascetics, are expected to arrive from different parts of India. The state government and district administration are coordinating security and logistics.

Special trains and buses have been arranged for the event. Temporary shelters, water points, and medical camps are being set up along the temple premises and the Nilachal Hill. Authorities advise devotees to plan their travel in advance and follow crowd management guidelines.

Security and infrastructure

Guwahati Police have deployed additional personnel for the mela. CCTV cameras and control rooms are being installed to monitor the crowd. The district administration has also issued advisories on traffic diversions and parking.

Devotees are urged to avoid carrying large bags or valuables. The temple trust has requested everyone to cooperate with security checks at entry points. Emergency services, including ambulances and fire tenders, will be on standby.

Historical and cultural context

Ambubachi Mela has been celebrated for centuries. The Kamakhya Temple, located atop Nilachal Hill, is one of the 51 Shakti Peethas. It holds immense significance for Tantric traditions and Hindu devotees alike.

The mela is also a platform for cultural exchange. Sadhus from various sects gather here, and many perform rituals and discourses. The event is a unique blend of spirituality, tradition, and community.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of expected visitors. However, based on previous years, the attendance is likely to cross several lakhs over the four days.

What to watch

The reopening of the temple on the fourth day will be the highlight. Authorities will closely monitor the crowd during the final puja and darshan. Devotees are advised to stay updated through official channels for any changes in schedule or guidelines.
