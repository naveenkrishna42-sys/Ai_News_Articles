Meta Apologises For PM Post Error

Meta has told a parliamentary panel that it is ready to apologise for an error related to a post by Prime Minister Narendra Modi. The company's representatives appeared before the panel to discuss the issue and algorithmic bias on their platform.

Grilled On Algorithmic Bias

The panel grilled Meta's representatives on algorithmic bias, with some members expressing concern over the company's content control and moderation policies. The discussion also touched on foreign links and the need for social media regulation.

According to reports, the panel chief stated that Mark Zuckerberg, Meta's CEO, will have to apologise for the error related to the PM's video removal. The company's apology was deemed insufficient by the panel, which called for more action to be taken.

Social Media Regulation

Executives from Google, Meta, and X also appeared before the parliamentary panel to discuss social media regulation. The panel's discussions with the companies are part of a broader effort to regulate social media platforms in India.

Next Steps

The parliamentary panel's discussions with Meta and other social media companies are likely to continue in the coming days. The panel may also summon other companies to discuss social media regulation and algorithmic bias.

Other news outlets have reported on the story, with NDTV stating that the panel chief said Mark Zuckerberg will have to apologise, and ThePrint reporting that the panel discussed foreign links and content control.

Panel's Demands

The parliamentary panel has made it clear that it expects more from Meta and other social media companies in terms of regulating their platforms and addressing concerns over algorithmic bias. The panel's demands are likely to have significant implications for the social media industry in India.

Officials have not yet confirmed what specific actions Meta will take to address the panel's concerns, but the company's apology is seen as a first step towards resolving the issue.
