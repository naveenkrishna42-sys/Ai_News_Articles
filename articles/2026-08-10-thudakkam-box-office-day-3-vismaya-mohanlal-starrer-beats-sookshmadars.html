Thudakkam Box Office Day 3: Vismaya Mohanlal Starrer Beats Sookshmadarshini, Scores 2nd Best Female-Led Openin

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Thudakkam earned Rs 4.25 crore on Sunday, up 18.1% from Saturday.
  • The film beat Sookshmadarshini to record the 2nd biggest opening weekend for a female-led Malayalam film.
  • Vismaya Mohanlal's martial arts performance has been praised by critics.
  • The film's 3-day total now stands at approximately Rs 10.2 crore.

Strong Sunday Push for Thudakkam

Vismaya Mohanlal's action thriller Thudakkam continued its impressive run at the box office, collecting Rs 4.25 crore on Sunday. The third-day numbers mark an 18.1 percent jump from Saturday's collection, giving the film a strong weekend finish.

The film's three-day total now stands at approximately Rs 10.2 crore. Trade analysts note that the Sunday spike is particularly encouraging, as it suggests positive word-of-mouth is driving audience footfall beyond the opening-day curiosity factor.

Beating Sookshmadarshini's Record

Thudakkam has now surpassed the opening weekend figures of Sookshmadarshini, which had earlier set a high benchmark for female-led Malayalam films. The new release now holds the second-biggest opening weekend for a female-led film in Malayalam cinema.

The record for the biggest opening weekend in this category still belongs to Kalyani Priyadarshan's Lokah. Industry observers had been speculating whether Thudakkam would topple that record, but the film fell short of the mark even as it secured a strong second position.

Martial Arts Action Wins Over Critics

Reviews for Thudakkam have been largely positive, with critics singling out Vismaya Mohanlal's martial arts sequences. The Hindu's review noted that the actress's combat skills "inject life into a typical revenge thriller," suggesting that the action choreography elevates an otherwise familiar narrative.

The film, which follows a revenge plot, has been described as a genre piece that benefits from its lead's physical commitment to the role. Vismaya, the daughter of Malayalam superstar Mohanlal, has been undergoing intensive training for the part, and that preparation appears to have paid off on screen.

Box Office Momentum and Competition

The film's performance is notable given the competitive landscape at the Malayalam box office. With multiple releases vying for audience attention, Thudakkam's ability to hold its screens and show steady growth through the weekend signals strong audience engagement.

The 18.1 percent rise from Saturday to Sunday is a healthy indicator, as most films see a more modest increase or even a dip on the final day of the weekend. The sustained momentum suggests the film could post respectable numbers in its first full week.

What to Watch Next

The coming week will be crucial for Thudakkam as it faces the Monday test. The film's weekday performance will determine whether it can maintain its box office trajectory or if the opening weekend spike was driven primarily by advance bookings and fan enthusiasm.

Trade observers will also be watching whether the film can sustain its lead over Sookshmadarshini's lifetime collections, as the comparison now shifts from opening weekend numbers to overall box office staying power.
