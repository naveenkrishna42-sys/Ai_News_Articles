Hyderabad man arrested for selling banned oxytocin to dairy farmers

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • A man was arrested in Hyderabad for selling oxytocin injections.
  • Oxytocin is banned for use in dairy animals due to health risks.
  • The drug is often misused to artificially increase milk production in cattle.

A man has been arrested in Hyderabad for allegedly selling oxytocin injections, a drug banned for use in dairy animals. The arrest was made by local law enforcement following a tip-off about the illegal trade. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact quantity of the drug seized.

What is Oxytocin and Why is it Banned?

Oxytocin is a hormone that is naturally produced in the body. In the dairy industry, it has been misused to artificially stimulate milk let-down in cattle. This practice forces animals to release milk that would otherwise be reserved for their calves.

The drug has been banned for use in dairy animals in India due to serious health concerns. Consuming milk from animals treated with oxytocin can lead to hormonal imbalances in humans. It can also cause side effects such as headaches, drowsiness, and even long-term reproductive issues.

The Arrest and Investigation

The accused was reportedly operating from a location in Hyderabad, supplying the banned injections to dairy farmers in the region. The arrest came after a coordinated effort between local police and drug control authorities.

Officials are now investigating the source of the supply chain. They are looking into whether the accused was part of a larger network involved in the illegal distribution of the drug. The man has been booked under relevant sections of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act.

Illegal Trade in Dairy Drugs

This is not an isolated incident. The illegal sale of oxytocin has been a persistent problem in several states across India. Despite the ban, black market sales continue because some farmers seek quick ways to boost milk production.

Authorities have repeatedly warned that such practices are not only illegal but also harmful to animal welfare and public health. The misuse of oxytocin can cause pain and distress to dairy animals, as it forces them to release milk unnaturally.

The Telangana police have urged the public to report any suspicious sales of such drugs. They have also advised dairy farmers to follow ethical and legal practices for milk production.

What Happens Next

The accused is expected to be produced in court. Investigations will continue to trace the drug's origin and identify other possible buyers. Authorities are likely to step up surveillance on the illegal trade of veterinary drugs.
