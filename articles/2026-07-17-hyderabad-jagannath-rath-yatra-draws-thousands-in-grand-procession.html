Hyderabad Jagannath Rath Yatra draws thousands in grand procession

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Grand procession held in Hyderabad for Jagannath Rath Yatra
  • Thousands of devotees participated, pulling chariots through city streets
  • Event featured traditional music, dance, and religious fervour

Hyderabad's streets come alive with Rath Yatra fervour

The city of Hyderabad witnessed a spectacular display of devotion on Thursday as thousands gathered for the annual Jagannath Rath Yatra. The grand procession, a vibrant affair, saw devotees pulling elaborately decorated chariots through the main thoroughfares, filling the air with chants and hymns.

The event, organised by local temples and cultural bodies, began early in the morning. Participants, many dressed in traditional attire, joined the procession that wound its way through key neighbourhoods, drawing onlookers from balconies and street corners.

Chanting, dancing, and communal harmony

The atmosphere was electric. Devotees danced to the beat of drums and cymbals as the chariots, carrying idols of Lord Jagannath, Balabhadra, and Subhadra, moved at a slow, ceremonial pace. The route was lined with people offering flowers and prayers.

“This is a day of joy and togetherness,” said a volunteer involved in the arrangements. “People from all communities come to participate. It shows the spirit of Hyderabad.” The procession also featured cultural performances, including traditional Odissi dances and folk songs, adding to the festive mood.

Elaborate arrangements ensure smooth conduct

The organisers had made extensive arrangements to ensure the event ran without a hitch. Temporary stages were set up at key points for cultural programmes. Volunteers distributed food and water to participants along the route.

Police personnel were deployed in large numbers to manage the crowds and traffic. Authorities had issued a traffic advisory earlier in the week, rerouting vehicles to avoid congestion. Officials confirmed that no untoward incidents were reported during the event.

A tradition rooted in faith

The Jagannath Rath Yatra, originally from Puri in Odisha, has been celebrated in Hyderabad for decades. The city’s large Odia community, along with devotees from other regions, has kept the tradition alive with enthusiasm. The chariots, built specially for the occasion, were a centrepiece of the procession.

For many, the yatra is not just a religious event but a cultural bridge. “It connects us to our roots,” said a participant who has been attending for over 20 years. “Every year, it feels fresh. The energy is unmatched.”

Looking ahead, the chariots will remain on display for public viewing over the next few days. The festival concludes with a ceremonial return procession, scheduled for later this week.
