Market Pulse: Sep 9 Session Snapshot

On September 9, 2026, the BSE Sensex traded near 81,200 with a modest 0.15% gain, while the NSE Nifty 50 held steady above 24,800. Global cues were mixed after the U.S. ISM services PMI came in at 52.3, slightly above estimates. Crude oil settled at $62 per barrel, and gold edged lower to ₹74,000 per kg. Traders should focus on intraday setups rather than positional longs until volatility eases.

7 Stocks to Buy Today: Tactical Picks

1. Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)

Entry: ₵3,480 | Stop-Loss: ₵3,390 | Target: ₵3,560

TCS is showing relative strength amid IT sector consolidation. A break above ₵3,500 could push it toward ₵3,650. Recommended for intraday traders only.

2. HDFC Bank

Entry: ₵1,660 | Stop-Loss: ₵1,620 | Target: ₵1,730

HDFC Bank found support near ₵1,630 and is consolidating. A positive candlestick close above ₵1,680 confirms momentum. Ideal for 15-30 min trades.

3. Reliance Industries

Entry: ₵1,390 | Stop-Loss: ₵1,340 | Target: ₵1,450

Reliance is trading in a narrow range between ₵1,370-1,410. A breakout from this range offers a quick 40-point move. Monitor volume spikes near support.

4. Infosys

Entry: ₵1,520 | Stop-Loss: ₵1,480 | Target: ₵1,580

Infosys is bouncing off its 20-day EMA. Momentum indicators suggest a short-term upmove. Suitable for quick scalps near ₵1,500 levels.

5. ICICI Bank

Entry: ₵1,085 | Stop-Loss: ₵1,060 | Target: ₵1,120

ICICI Bank is consolidating above its support zone. A sustained move above ₵1,095 could trigger short-covering. Use tight SL for risk control.

6. Kotak Mahindra Bank

Entry: ₵1,820 | Stop-Loss: ₵1,780 | Target: ₵1,880

Kotak is showing bullish divergence on the daily chart. A breakout from the descending triangle pattern may yield 60 points. Best for swing trades.

7. Axis Bank

Entry: ₵1,160 | Stop-Loss: ₵1,120 | Target: ₵1,210

Axis Bank is rebounding from its key support. RSI indicates strengthening momentum. Avoid trading if Nifty opens below 24,750.
