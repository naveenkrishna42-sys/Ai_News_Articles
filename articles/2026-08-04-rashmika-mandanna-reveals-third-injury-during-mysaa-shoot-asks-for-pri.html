Rashmika Mandanna reveals third injury during 'Mysaa' shoot, asks for privacy

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Rashmika Mandanna sustained a hip tendon injury during the shoot of 'Mysaa'.
  • This is her third injury in recent months, leading to a six-week recovery period.
  • She penned a long note urging fans for privacy and self-care.
  • Doctors have advised rest, and she will resume shooting after recovery.

Rashmika Mandanna has finally broken her silence after sustaining yet another injury on the sets of her upcoming film Mysaa. The actor, who has been juggling multiple projects, took to social media to share an emotional note, revealing that she had been hoping to keep the incident under wraps.

“Was really hoping no one will know…” she wrote, addressing fans directly. The note came after reports surfaced about a “freak accident” on set that has now put her out of action for six weeks.

A third setback in a row

This marks the third injury for the 29-year-old actor in recent months, a pattern that has raised concerns among her fanbase. While the earlier injuries were relatively minor, this time the damage is more serious — one of her hip tendons detached during a stunt or physically demanding sequence on the Mysaa set.

In her note, Rashmika admitted she had been pushing herself too hard. “I should treat my body like a human, not a machine,” she wrote, reflecting on the toll that relentless shooting schedules have taken on her health.

Recovery and road ahead

Doctors have advised the actor to take complete rest for at least six weeks. This means she will have to pause shooting for Mysaa, which was reportedly in an advanced stage of production. The team is expected to adjust schedules to accommodate her recovery.

Rashmika’s note also carried a plea for privacy, asking fans and media not to speculate or worry excessively. She assured everyone that she is in good hands and will return to work soon, stronger than before.

Fans rally behind the actor

Within hours of her post, social media was flooded with messages of support. Hashtags like #GetWellSoonRashmika trended on X (formerly Twitter), with fans sharing clips of her previous interviews where she spoke about handling stress and physical exhaustion.

The outpouring reflects the strong bond Rashmika shares with her audience, who have followed her journey from a small-town girl to one of India’s most sought-after stars. Many also pointed out that her candid admission about treating herself as a machine resonates with countless young professionals facing burnout.

What this means for 'Mysaa'

Mysaa, which has been generating buzz due to its high-octane action sequences, will now see a delayed release. While the makers have not issued an official statement, industry insiders suggest that the team may use the downtime to complete other portions of the film that do not require Rashmika’s presence.

The actor’s upcoming slate also includes a pan-India project with a major Telugu director, and her recovery timeline will be crucial for those schedules as well. For now, the focus remains on her healing.

As Rashmika takes this forced break, fans will be watching closely for updates on her health. The actor has promised to keep everyone posted, but for now, she is prioritising rest — a rare pause in a career that has been racing at breakneck speed.
