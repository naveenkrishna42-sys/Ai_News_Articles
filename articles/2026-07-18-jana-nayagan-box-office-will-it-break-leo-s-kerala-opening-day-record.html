Jana Nayagan Box Office: Will It Break Leo's Kerala Opening Day Record?

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jana Nayagan is Thalapathy Vijay's next big release, generating massive advance bookings.
  • The film aims to surpass the opening day collection record in Kerala held by Vijay's own film, Leo.
  • A distribution dispute has led to Victory Cinema in Bengaluru declining to screen the film.
  • The CBFC requested changes to an Ambedkar-related scene, which the producer has addressed.

The hype around Thalapathy Vijay's upcoming film, Jana Nayagan, is reaching a fever pitch. With just days left for the release, the big question on everyone's mind is whether it can break the opening day record in Kerala set by his own film, Leo.

Early tracking suggests the film is on a strong trajectory. Advance bookings in Kerala have been robust, especially in the major centres of Thiruvananthapuram, Kochi, and Kozhikode. Industry trackers are watching the numbers closely.

The Leo Benchmark in Kerala

Leo had set a high bar when it released in October 2023. The film collected an estimated Rs 8.5 crore on its opening day in Kerala, a record for any film in the state. For Jana Nayagan to beat that, it needs near-perfect occupancy across all shows on day one.

Distributors in Kerala are confident. They point to the sustained demand for Vijay's films in the region and the positive buzz around the film's music and promotional material. However, they also caution that a lot depends on the film's reviews on release day.

Hurdles Off the Screen

Despite the strong buzz, the film has hit a roadblock in Bengaluru. Victory Cinema, a prominent single-screen theatre in the city, has declined to screen Jana Nayagan amid a distribution dispute. The specifics of the dispute have not been officially disclosed, but sources indicate it involves revenue-sharing terms.

This is a setback for the film's Karnataka release. The loss of a key single-screen theatre in a major market like Bengaluru could impact the film's overall opening weekend numbers in the state. Negotiations are reportedly ongoing to resolve the issue.

Price Cap and CBFC Changes

In Tamil Nadu, the government has capped ticket prices. An exclusive report confirmed that tickets for Jana Nayagan will not be sold above Rs 190. This is standard procedure for big releases in the state but ensures the film remains accessible to a wider audience.

Another controversy has been addressed by the film's producer. The Central Board of Film Certification (CBFC) had requested a change in a scene involving Ambedkar and the Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) party symbol. The producer confirmed the change was made, stating it was a minor adjustment to avoid any potential misinterpretation.

The Father-Daughter Track

Amidst the box office projections and distribution issues, the film's music has won hearts. Vijay has dropped an emotional father-daughter track titled 'Adiye En Poonthene'. The song, which features Vijay and a young actress, has been trending online and is expected to be a major emotional highlight of the film.

The track's release has further boosted the film's pre-release buzz. It showcases a softer side of the action star, which fans are eagerly anticipating.

All eyes are now on the advance booking numbers for the first weekend. Whether Jana Nayagan can topple Leo's record will be known by Sunday morning.
