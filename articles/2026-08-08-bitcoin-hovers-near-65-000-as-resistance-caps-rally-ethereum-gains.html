Bitcoin hovers near $65,000 as resistance caps rally; Ethereum gains

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin is hovering near $65,000, with resistance capping further upside.
  • Ethereum is showing stronger momentum compared to Bitcoin.
  • Traders are watching key levels as market sentiment remains mixed.

Bitcoin stuck below $65,000 ceiling

Bitcoin continues to hover near the $65,000 mark, with sellers defending a key resistance zone that has capped upside moves over the past several sessions. The cryptocurrency has repeatedly tested this level but has failed to break through, leaving traders to weigh the next possible direction.

Market participants point to the resistance as a psychological barrier as much as a technical one. A decisive close above this level could open the door to fresh highs, but until then, the range-bound action persists.

Ethereum shows relative strength

While Bitcoin struggles at resistance, Ethereum has been gaining momentum, outperforming the larger cryptocurrency in recent trading. The shift suggests some capital rotation into the second-largest digital asset, even as overall market volumes remain moderate.

Analysts note that Ethereum's recent moves have been supported by steady network activity, though no single catalyst has been identified. The divergence between the two assets is a point of focus for traders tracking relative strength.

Resistance levels in focus

For Bitcoin, the $65,000 area has become the line in the sand. Sellers have stepped in each time the price approaches, and buyers have so far lacked the conviction to push through. Below, support is seen near the $62,000-$63,000 range, which has held firm during dips.

Technical charts show a tightening range, often a precursor to a breakout. But with no clear fundamental trigger, the market appears to be waiting for direction rather than forcing the issue.

Market sentiment remains cautious

Overall sentiment in the crypto market is cautious but not fearful. Trading volumes have not surged, suggesting that neither bulls nor bears are fully committing. The absence of major headlines, regulatory or otherwise, has left the market to trade on technicals.

Global macroeconomic factors, including interest rate expectations and risk appetite, continue to play a background role. Any shift in these broader conditions could quickly alter the current equilibrium.

What to watch next: whether Bitcoin can close above $65,000 on sustained volume, and if Ethereum can extend its lead. A breakout in either direction could set the tone for the coming weeks, but until then, the sideways grind looks set to continue.
