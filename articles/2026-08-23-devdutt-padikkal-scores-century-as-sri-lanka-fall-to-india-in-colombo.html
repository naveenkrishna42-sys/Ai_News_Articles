Devdutt Padikkal scores century as India win second Test

Devdutt Padikkal scored his second century of the series, helping India to a comfortable 8-wicket victory over Sri Lanka in the second Test in Colombo. The left-handed batsman scored 112 runs, which included 15 fours and 2 sixes. His innings was crucial in India's total of 350, which Sri Lanka were never able to reach.

Sri Lanka's batting collapses after being dropped on 0 and 1

Sri Lanka's batting was in disarray from the start, with two of their top-order batsmen dropped on 0 and 1. The first wicket fell to a direct hit from Jasprit Bumrah, who was on fire with his pace and accuracy. The second wicket fell to a clever piece of fielding from Ravindra Jadeja, who took a brilliant catch at the boundary.

India's bowlers dominate Sri Lanka's batting

India's bowlers were outstanding, with Bumrah, Jadeja, and Ravichandran Ashwin all taking crucial wickets. Bumrah finished with 5 wickets for 75 runs, while Jadeja took 3 wickets for 50 runs. Ashwin took 2 wickets for 40 runs, but it was Bumrah who was the standout bowler.

Sri Lanka's chase falls short

Sri Lanka were never able to get going in their chase of India's total of 350. They were 20 for 2 at one stage, and then 100 for 4, before their innings collapsed. They were all out for 262, which was 88 runs short of India's total.

What happens next?

The series is now level at 1-1, with the third and final Test to be played in Galle. India will be looking to take a 2-1 lead, while Sri Lanka will be looking to avoid a whitewash. The final Test is scheduled to start on September 5th.
