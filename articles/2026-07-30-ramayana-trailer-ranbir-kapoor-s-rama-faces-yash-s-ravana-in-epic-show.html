'Ramayana' trailer: Ranbir Kapoor's Rama faces Yash's Ravana in epic showdown

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • The trailer for Nitesh Tiwari's 'Ramayana' was released, featuring Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Rama and Yash as Ravana.
  • Sony Pictures has come on board as the global distributor for the film.
  • Fans are asking about the absence of Hanuman in the trailer.

The wait is over. The trailer for Nitesh Tiwari's ambitious epic 'Ramayana' has finally arrived, giving audiences their first look at Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Rama and Yash as the ten-headed king Ravana.

Released in the pre-dawn hours — a time considered auspicious in Hindu tradition — the trailer sets the stage for a clash between righteousness and ambition. The visual grandeur is unmistakable, with sprawling sets and intricate costumes that bring the ancient epic to life.

A clash of titans

Ranbir Kapoor's Rama is serene, measured, and embodies dharma. His posture and expressions suggest a man carrying the weight of duty. In stark contrast, Yash's Ravana is theatrical and menacing — a king who believes his power is absolute.

The trailer focuses heavily on the personal conflict between these two figures. It is not merely a battle of armies but a philosophical war between order and chaos. The brief glimpses of combat suggest a film that will deliver both spectacle and substance.

Global ambitions

Sony Pictures has signed on as the global distributor for 'Ramayana'. This is a significant move. It means the film will get a wide release in international markets, including North America, Europe, and parts of Asia where Indian cinema has a growing footprint.

The production, backed by DNEG's visual effects expertise, is clearly aiming for a scale that matches Hollywood epics. The trailer's shots of Lanka — a golden city of towering spires — and the dense forests of Dandaka suggest significant investment in world-building.

The Hanuman question

One notable absence in the trailer is Hanuman. Fans were quick to point out that the monkey god, played by an as-yet-unannounced actor, did not appear in the footage. This has sparked speculation about whether the character will have a reduced role or is being saved for a later reveal.

The makers have not confirmed the casting for Hanuman. Given the epic's narrative, the character's entry typically marks a turning point. It is possible the trailer is deliberately holding back to maintain suspense.

What is clear is that 'Ramayana' is being positioned as a major event film. The response on social media has been electric. Comparisons to other mythological retellings are inevitable, but this version seems intent on carving its own identity.

The film is expected to release in multiple languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, and Kannada. A release date has not been officially announced, but industry insiders suggest a 2026 Diwali window is likely.

All eyes are now on the next trailer or poster drop. The team will need to address the Hanuman question soon, as fan curiosity is only growing.
