The Xiaomi 17T is set to launch in India soon, aiming to shake up the premium mid-range segment. Its most obvious rival is the OnePlus 13R, a phone that has dominated this price bracket with its clean software and robust hardware. Here's how the two stack up on paper before the Xiaomi lands.

The OnePlus 13R offers a proven flagship-grade experience with a reliable Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chip and a large battery, while the Xiaomi 17T is expected to match it with newer hardware and potentially aggressive pricing. The Xiaomi 17T's real-world performance and camera quality are still unverified, making the OnePlus 13R the safer pick today.

Choose the Xiaomi 17T if you want the latest processor, a potentially better primary camera, and are willing to take a chance on early software kinks for a likely lower launch price. Choose the OnePlus 13R if you value proven reliability, cleaner software, faster updates, and a well-rounded camera system that's already tested—it's the safer buy for most people right now.
