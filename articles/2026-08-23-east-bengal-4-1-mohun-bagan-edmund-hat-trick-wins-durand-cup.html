East Bengal 4-1 Mohun Bagan: Edmund hat-trick wins Durand Cup

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Edmund scored a first-half hat-trick as East Bengal defeated Mohun Bagan 4-1 in the Durand Cup final.
  • East Bengal's four-goal first-half blitz sealed the trophy, marking a dominant derby performance.
  • Mohun Bagan had earlier reached the final via a penalty shootout win over NorthEast United.

Derby dominance in the final

East Bengal produced a stunning first-half display to crush Mohun Bagan 4-1 in the Durand Cup final, with Edmund scoring a hat-trick that set the Salt Lake stadium alight. The Kolkata derby, always high on emotion, turned into a one-sided affair as the red-and-gold brigade ran riot before the break.

The four-goal blitz inside the opening 45 minutes effectively sealed the contest, leaving Mohun Bagan chasing shadows. By the time the second half began, the result seemed a foregone conclusion, though the Mariners did pull one back to restore some pride.

Edmund's night to remember

Edmund was the architect of East Bengal's triumph, his first-half treble dismantling the Mohun Bagan defence with clinical precision. Each goal came at a crucial juncture, silencing the maroon-and-green supporters and energising the red-and-gold faithful packed into the stands.

His hat-trick will be remembered as one of the finest individual performances in a Durand Cup final, a display of sharp movement and ruthless finishing. The forward's exploits ensured East Bengal's name would be etched on the trophy once again, ending years of waiting in this storied competition.

How the final unfolded

East Bengal came out with intent, pressing high and forcing errors from Mohun Bagan's backline. The early breakthrough set the tone, and the goals kept flowing as the half progressed, leaving the Mariners shell-shocked.

Mohun Bagan, who had reached the final after a tense penalty shootout victory over holders NorthEast United, struggled to find their rhythm. Their response in the second half was spirited but insufficient, as East Bengal managed the game with composure to see out a memorable victory.

A season-defining statement

For East Bengal, this Durand Cup triumph is more than just silverware; it is a statement of intent ahead of the upcoming season. Winning a derby final in such emphatic fashion will do wonders for the squad's confidence and endear them further to their passionate supporters.

Mohun Bagan, meanwhile, will look to regroup quickly, knowing that the derby defeat stings but the season is far from over. The rivalry remains as fierce as ever, and this result only adds another chapter to one of Indian football's most celebrated fixtures.

What next for both sides

Attention will now shift to the league season, where both clubs will aim to build on their Durand Cup campaigns. East Bengal will carry immense momentum, while Mohun Bagan will be eager to bounce back and prove this final was an aberration.

With the trophy secured, the focus moves to consistency over a long domestic calendar. Fans of both clubs will watch closely to see how their teams respond when the real grind begins.
