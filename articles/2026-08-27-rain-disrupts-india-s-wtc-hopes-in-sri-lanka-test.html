Rain disrupts India's WTC hopes in Sri Lanka Test

📅 2026-08-27 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • India leads Sri Lanka by 152 runs after Day 4
  • Rain threatens to delay Day 5, impacting WTC final qualification
  • India's control of the Test highlights their strong bowling attack

India Seize Control in Final Session of Day 4

India's cricket team has taken a significant lead against Sri Lanka in the second Test, ending Day 4 with a 152-run advantage. The Indian bowlers dominated the final session, restricting Sri Lanka to just 15 runs for the loss of 3 wickets. This performance has put India in a strong position to qualify for the World Test Championship (WTC) final.

Rain Threatens to Disrupt Final Day

The Indian Meteorological Department has issued a warning of heavy rainfall, which could disrupt the final day of the Test. The Sri Lankan team has already expressed concerns about the weather, and the match officials are closely monitoring the situation. If the rain delays or washes out the final day, it could have significant implications for India's WTC qualification.

Sonal's Golden Run: A Bright Spot for Sri Lanka

Despite the overall performance, Sri Lanka's Kusal Mendis has been a standout performer, scoring a golden duck in the final session. His innings of 102 runs in the first innings and a half-century in the second innings have provided some hope for Sri Lanka. However, India's strong bowling attack has made it difficult for Sri Lanka to recover from the deficit.

Expert Opinion

Cricket analyst and former player, Sunil Gavaskar, has commented on the situation, stating that India's strong bowling attack has been a key factor in their success. However, he has also warned that the rain threat could be a major setback for India. Gavaskar believes that Sri Lanka's ability to grind out results in the final session of Day 4 has been a testament to their character and resilience.

Broader Impact

The outcome of this Test match will have significant implications for India's WTC qualification. If India can secure a win, they will be in a strong position to qualify for the final. However, if the rain disrupts the final day, it could potentially lead to a draw, which may not be sufficient for India to qualify. This highlights the challenges of scheduling cricket during the monsoon season, where weather conditions can significantly impact the outcome of matches.
