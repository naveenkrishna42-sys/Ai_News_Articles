Infant's body goes missing from Punjagutta graveyard in Hyderabad

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Infant's body missing from Punjagutta graveyard
  • Case registered, investigation underway
  • Police examining CCTV footage and questioning staff

Body discovered missing during routine visit

The body of an infant has gone missing from a graveyard in Punjagutta, Hyderabad, leaving family members shocked and police scrambling for answers. The incident came to light when relatives visited the burial site and found the grave disturbed.

According to sources, the family had buried the child at the Punjagutta graveyard a few days ago. On a routine visit, they discovered that the body was no longer in the grave. The family immediately alerted the local police, who arrived at the spot and began preliminary inquiries.

Police register case, launch probe

Authorities have registered a case under relevant sections and have launched an investigation into the matter. Police officials have been speaking to graveyard staff and nearby residents to gather leads.

Initial suspicions point to possible involvement of persons with vested interests, though nothing has been confirmed yet. Investigators are also checking if any unauthorised digging or removal took place in the area recently.

CCTV footage and forensic teams pressed into service

Police are scanning CCTV footage from cameras installed near the graveyard to identify any suspicious activity. A forensic team has also been called in to examine the site for clues.

“We are looking at all angles. The investigation is in its early stages, and we are questioning those who had access to the graveyard,” a police officer said on condition of anonymity. Officials have not yet ruled out the possibility of body snatching, which has been reported in other parts of the country.

Family demands swift action

The grieving family has demanded a thorough probe and swift action against those responsible. They allege that the graveyard lacks proper security, making it vulnerable to such incidents.

Local residents have also expressed concern over the safety of burial sites in the area. Some have urged authorities to increase surveillance and deploy guards at cemeteries to prevent similar occurrences.

The incident has sent ripples through the Punjagutta locality, with many calling for stricter oversight of graveyard management. Police have assured the public that they are leaving no stone unturned in the investigation.

As the probe continues, officials are expected to question more people and review records of burials conducted in the past week. The family, meanwhile, waits for answers, hoping that the authorities will recover the infant's body and bring the culprits to book.

What to watch

Police are likely to release more details in the coming days as forensic reports and CCTV analysis progress. Whether this was an isolated act or part of a larger pattern will become clearer once the investigation concludes.
