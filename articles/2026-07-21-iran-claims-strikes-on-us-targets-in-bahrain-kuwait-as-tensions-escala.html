Iran Claims Strikes on US Targets in Bahrain, Kuwait as Tensions Escalate

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Iran's Revolutionary Guards claim to have struck US military targets in Bahrain and Kuwait
  • US launches fresh strikes on Iran amid escalating conflict
  • Trump warns of retaliation for deaths of US soldiers

Guards Announce Strikes on US Positions

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) has claimed responsibility for striking American military targets in Bahrain and Kuwait, marking a significant escalation in the ongoing West Asia conflict. The announcement was made through state-aligned media channels on Tuesday, with the Guards asserting that the operations were in retaliation for recent US actions in the region.

The specific nature of the targets and the extent of any damage have not been independently verified. Officials in Bahrain and Kuwait have not yet commented on the claims.

US Intensifies Air Campaign Against Iran

The Iranian claims come as the United States has launched fresh airstrikes on Iranian positions. President Donald Trump has warned of severe retaliation for the deaths of American soldiers in recent clashes, though the precise number of casualties has not been disclosed by the Pentagon.

The strikes represent the latest phase of a conflict that has rapidly expanded across the region. US Central Command has confirmed ongoing operations but declined to provide operational details, citing security concerns.

Intelligence Assessments Cast Doubt on Strategy

Despite the military escalation, multiple intelligence reports suggest that the current US bombing campaign is unlikely to alter Iran's strategic calculus. Analysts have pointed to Tehran's history of absorbing punitive strikes while pursuing its regional objectives through proxies and asymmetric warfare.

The assessment raises questions about the long-term efficacy of the US approach, as both sides appear locked in a cycle of attack and retaliation without clear off-ramps for de-escalation.

Regional Fallout and Gulf Neighbours

Iran has also directed criticism at its Gulf neighbours, accusing them of facilitating US military operations. The rhetoric has heightened fears that the conflict could draw in other regional players, potentially destabilising the broader Gulf region.

Bahrain and Kuwait, both hosts to US military facilities, find themselves in a precarious position. Their governments have historically balanced close security ties with Washington against the need to manage relations with Tehran.

What to Watch

With both sides showing no signs of backing down, the immediate focus will be on whether Iran follows through with further strikes and how the US responds. Diplomatic channels remain largely silent, and the risk of a broader regional war continues to grow.
