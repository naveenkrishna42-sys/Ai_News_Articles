Mumbai Local Tragedy: Man Pushed to Death, Accused Blames Crow

📅 2026-08-20 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 62-year-old specially abled passenger died after being pushed from a moving train.
  • The incident occurred between Bandra and Mahim stations on the Mumbai local network.
  • The accused has provided a bizarre justification, claiming he was chasing a bird.
  • Government Railway Police have initiated a formal investigation into the death.

A Fatal Commute

A routine train journey turned fatal on Thursday when a 62-year-old specially abled man was pushed from a moving Mumbai local train. The incident, which occurred between Bandra and Mahim stations, has sparked outrage among regular commuters and raised fresh questions regarding passenger safety on the suburban rail network.

Witnesses reported an altercation breaking out inside the crowded compartment before the victim was shoved toward the door. The victim fell from the moving train and succumbed to his injuries shortly after the impact. His identity has not been officially released pending notification of his family.

A Bizarre Defense

Authorities took the primary suspect into custody shortly after the incident was reported. During initial questioning by the Government Railway Police (GRP), the accused offered an unusual explanation for his actions. He claimed that he was not intentionally attacking the victim, but was instead attempting to chase a crow that had entered the carriage.

Police officials remain skeptical of this account. Investigators are currently cross-referencing the suspect's statement with testimonies from other passengers present in the coach. The GRP has confirmed that a formal probe is underway to determine the exact sequence of events leading to the fatal fall.

Safety Concerns on Local Trains

The incident highlights the persistent dangers faced by commuters on the Mumbai local lines. Every day, lakhs of people rely on the rail network, often traveling in dangerously overcrowded conditions. Activists have long demanded better surveillance and increased security personnel presence inside compartments to prevent such altercations.

Railway officials stated that they are reviewing footage from station platforms and looking for additional witnesses. The accused remains in police custody as the investigation proceeds. The GRP is expected to file a comprehensive report once the forensic analysis and witness statements are concluded.

Legal experts suggest that the accused will likely face charges related to culpable homicide. The court will determine whether the bizarre "crow" defense holds any weight during the upcoming trial proceedings.

The GRP is now awaiting post-mortem reports to confirm the specific cause of death. Further developments are expected as the police finalize their charge sheet in the coming weeks.
