Ashwin suggests Mumbai Indians demand Rana, Rinku in Hardik trade

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ashwin suggests MI demand Rana and Rinku in a Hardik Pandya trade with KKR
  • Speculation grows over Hardik's future ahead of IPL 2027 mega auction
  • CSK and KKR emerge as potential destinations for the Indian all-rounder

The Indian Premier League's trade window is still months away, but speculation over Hardik Pandya's future is already generating heat. Ravichandran Ashwin, the veteran spinner-turned-analyst, has weighed in with a specific suggestion for Mumbai Indians if they decide to part ways with their captain.

Speaking on his YouTube show, Ashwin proposed that Mumbai Indians should demand Harshit Rana and Rinku Singh from Kolkata Knight Riders in exchange for Hardik. The suggestion comes amid growing reports that the all-rounder could be traded ahead of the IPL 2027 season.

Why KKR makes sense for Hardik

Kolkata Knight Riders have emerged as one of the frontrunners to secure Hardik's services. The franchise has a history of reviving careers and building balanced squads around match-winners. With the mega auction approaching, KKR could see Hardik as the perfect centrepiece for their future core.

Hardik's ability to contribute with both bat and ball makes him an attractive proposition for any franchise. His leadership experience, including a title-winning stint with Gujarat Titans, adds further value. KKR's current setup, which boasts young Indian talent, could benefit immensely from his presence.

Ashwin's proposed deal

In his analysis, Ashwin argued that Mumbai Indians should not let Hardik go cheaply. He specifically named Rana and Rinku as the players MI should demand in return. Both have been instrumental in KKR's recent success, with Rana emerging as a reliable pace option and Rinku establishing himself as a finisher.

The suggestion reflects the changing dynamics of IPL trades, where franchises increasingly look for ready-made replacements rather than future picks. Ashwin's logic appears simple: if MI lose a proven all-rounder, they must gain two players who can fill multiple roles.

CSK remain in the mix

Chennai Super Kings have also been linked with Hardik in recent reports. The franchise's track record of getting the best out of experienced players makes them a natural fit. However, CSK's squad composition, which already includes several all-rounders, could complicate any potential deal.

Ashwin himself acknowledged that CSK remains a possible destination, though he did not elaborate on what MI should demand in that scenario. The uncertainty around the mega auction rules adds another layer of complexity to any trade discussions.

What Mumbai Indians stand to gain

For Mumbai Indians, trading Hardik would be a significant decision. The franchise invested heavily in him as captain, and his departure would mark the end of a turbulent era. However, acquiring Rana and Rinku would give MI a young Indian core to build around for the next cycle.

Rana's pace and accuracy make him a valuable asset in the middle overs, while Rinku's finishing skills have drawn comparisons to some of the best in the business. Both players are in their mid-20s and could serve MI for years to come.

As the IPL 2027 mega auction approaches, trade talks are expected to intensify. Franchises will look to reshape their squads, and Hardik's name will likely dominate discussions. Whether MI ultimately agree to Ashwin's suggested terms or explore other options remains to be seen.

The coming weeks will be crucial as teams finalise their retention lists and trade strategies. For now, all eyes are on the franchise owners and their willingness to strike a deal that could reshape the IPL's competitive balance.
