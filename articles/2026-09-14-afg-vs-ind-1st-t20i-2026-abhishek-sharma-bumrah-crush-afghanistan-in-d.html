AFG vs IND 1st T20I 2026: Abhishek Sharma Blazes as India Cruise in Delhi

India took a 1-0 lead in the three-match T20I series against Afghanistan with a seven-wicket win at the Arun Jaitley Stadium in Delhi, chasing 155 with 19 balls to spare. The night belonged to Abhishek Sharma, whose 50 off 27 balls turned a modest target into a no-contest, and to Jasprit Bumrah, who marked his return to international cricket with figures of 2 for 12 from four overs.

Afghanistan won the toss and chose to bat on a surface with even bounce. The decision backfired almost immediately as Arshdeep Singh and Bumrah reduced them to 58 for 6 inside 10 overs, before Azmatullah Omarzai's unbeaten 58 off 34 balls dragged the total to 154 for 8.

How the Match Was Won: Powerplay Bowling, Then Abhishek's Assault

New-ball spell that decided the contest

Arshdeep Singh set the tone with a double-wicket first over, removing Rahmanullah Gurbaz and Sediqullah Atal. Bumrah, playing his first T20I since the 2024 World Cup cycle, operated on a hard length and conceded just 12 runs while dismissing Ibrahim Zadran and Gulbadin Naib. Afghanistan's middle order had no answer to the change of pace and the Delhi surface's extra carry.

Abhishek Sharma's 27-ball fifty

Chasing 155, India lost Shubman Gill early to Fazalhaq Farooqi, but Abhishek Sharma took the game away in the powerplay. He struck five fours and three sixes, repeatedly making room to leg-side bowlers and using the short square boundary to good effect. By the time he fell for 50, India were 96 for 2 in the 11th over and the required rate had dipped below six.

Real-World Takeaways for Fans and Fantasy Buyers

  • Bumrah's fitness is the headline: His four-over spell at 3.00 an over, with no visible discomfort, settles India's Asian Games bowling plans.
  • Abhishek Sharma's T20I ceiling is rising: A 185-strike-rate fifty against a decent Afghanistan attack strengthens his case as a first-choice opener.
  • Afghanistan's top order remains fragile: Losing 4 wickets for 29 runs in the powerplay is a recurring pattern on good batting decks; Omarzai's rescue act masked the collapse.
  • Delhi pitch offered value: 154 was at least 25 runs short; teams batting first here must target 175-plus.
  • Fantasy impact: Arshdeep and Bumrah were the highest-point bowlers; Abhishek was the top batting pick by a distance.

What This Means for the Series and the Asian Games Build-Up

India's selection hinted at their Asian Games template: two frontline pacers, two wrist-spinners, and a batting order with multiple all-round options. Afghanistan, meanwhile, must find a way to protect their top order against the moving ball or risk a 3-0 sweep. The second T20I in Mohali on 15 September offers them a chance to regroup; India will be tempted to give fringe players a run with the series lead secured.

For buyers of cricket content and streaming packages, this match delivered exactly what a bilateral T20I should: a genuine contest for 15 overs, a marquee fast bowler's return, and a young Indian batter announcing himself. The series continues with Afghanistan's pride and India's experimentation both on the line.
