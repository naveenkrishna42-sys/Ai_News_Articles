iPhone 18 Pro: 5 big upgrades leaked ahead of Apple's next launch

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Leaked details point to five major upgrades for the iPhone 18 Pro.
  • A spring launch delay is expected to boost autumn sales of the Pro model.
  • Apple is reportedly looking to cut costs on the iPhone 18 Pro Max display.
  • The iPhone 18 Pro Max may launch in India with a higher price tag.
  • Loyal Apple customers face rising costs as the new series nears launch.

Five Leaked Upgrades for the iPhone 18 Pro

Leaks and reports are piling up ahead of Apple's next flagship launch. The iPhone 18 Pro is expected to arrive with at least five major upgrades, according to industry watchers and supply chain sources.

Firstpost reports that the new model will feature a significantly improved camera system. A larger battery is also on the cards, potentially offering a full day of heavy use. The display is rumoured to get a brightness boost and a new anti-reflective coating.

Performance upgrades include a faster A-series chip and an increase in base storage. The design may see subtle refinements, though Apple is not expected to overhaul the look entirely.

Spring Delay Could Boost Autumn Sales

Forbes has reported that Apple is planning a spring launch delay for the iPhone 18 series. This unusual move is expected to shift consumer demand towards the autumn release of the iPhone 18 Pro.

By staggering the launch, Apple could avoid cannibalising sales of its own mid-year products. The delay may also allow the company to clear inventory of older models before the Pro arrives.

Analysts believe the strategy could result in a strong sales quarter for the Pro model, as buyers who held off in spring will be ready to upgrade by autumn.

Cost-Cutting on the Pro Max Display

GSMArena.com reports that Apple wants to save money on the iPhone 18 Pro Max's screen. The company is reportedly considering a cheaper display panel supplier for the largest model.

This cost-cutting move may not affect display quality, but it signals Apple's push to maintain margins amid rising component prices. Consumers may see no difference in brightness or colour accuracy.

The standard Pro model is expected to retain its current display supplier, ensuring top-tier performance for the smaller flagship.

India Launch Details and Rising Prices

Moneycontrol.com has outlined expected details for the iPhone 18 Pro Max launch in India. The device is likely to arrive with a bigger battery and a new camera system, as rumoured.

Price in India is expected to rise again, continuing a trend seen with recent models. The premium segment in India has grown, and Apple is betting that loyal customers will pay more for the latest features.

The Times of India notes that as the iPhone 18 series launch nears, the cost of staying loyal to Apple is rising. Entry-level models are also seeing price hikes, making the ecosystem more expensive for Indian buyers.

What to Watch Next

Apple is expected to officially announce the iPhone 18 series in the coming months. The company has not confirmed any of the leaked details. Watch for a possible spring event for the base models, followed by a larger autumn launch for the Pro line.
