Divya Mittal, a UP IAS officer, resigns after 13 years in service

Divya Mittal, a graduate of IIT and IIM, has resigned from the Indian Administrative Service (IAS) after 13 years. Mittal, who joined the IAS in 2013, cited political pressures and personal reasons for her decision. Her resignation has sparked discussions about career choices in the IAS, a service known for its prestige and challenges.

Mittal's journey: From London to IAS to resignation

Mittal, who had lived in London before joining the IAS, made headlines when she brought piped water to a village in Uttar Pradesh after 75 years of independence. This act of service was seen as a symbol of her commitment to public service. However, her decision to resign has raised questions about the pressures she faced and the reasons behind her departure.

Why Mittal resigned: Political pressures and personal reasons

Mittal has been quoted as saying that she will speak about her reasons for resigning when the time is right. However, there have been speculations about political pressures and personal reasons influencing her decision. The IAS is known for its rigorous training and high expectations, and Mittal's resignation adds to the ongoing discussions about the challenges faced by officers in the service.

Impact of Mittal's resignation on the IAS

Mittal's resignation has raised questions about the career choices and the pressures faced by IAS officers. The IAS is a coveted service, and officers are expected to serve for a long period. Mittal's decision to resign after 13 years has sparked discussions about the challenges and the reasons behind such decisions. The IAS is known for its prestige and the opportunities it offers, but it also comes with its own set of challenges and pressures.

Expert opinion: Understanding the IAS and its officers

Experts in the field of public administration have weighed in on Mittal's resignation. They have highlighted the rigorous training and the high expectations placed on IAS officers.
