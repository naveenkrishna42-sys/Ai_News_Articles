Lenovo Legion Tab Gen 5 review: Compact gaming tablet packs a punch

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Legion Tab Gen 5 review highlights strong performance in a small form factor.
  • Lenovo Legion Y700 Infinite 5G confirmed with 50MP camera, 6.5mm thin body, and 2.89mm bezels.
  • Lenovo expands tablet lineup with a 9-speaker model and discounts on gaming slate and 140W power bank.

A pocket-sized powerhouse

Lenovo's Legion Tab Gen 5 has landed, and early reviews are pointing to one thing: this compact tablet punches well above its weight. The device, which continues the Legion line's focus on gaming performance, is being praised for its ability to handle demanding titles without breaking a sweat, all while keeping the footprint small enough to slip into a backpack or even a large pocket.

The review from Mashable highlights the tablet's "stellar processing" capabilities, noting that Lenovo has managed to squeeze flagship-level performance into a chassis that feels surprisingly light and portable. For gamers who have long complained that powerful tablets are too bulky, the Legion Tab Gen 5 appears to offer a solution that doesn't compromise on speed.

Y700 Infinite 5G: A new benchmark for slimness

Meanwhile, The Tech Outlook has confirmed details about the upcoming Lenovo Legion Y700 Infinite 5G, a variant that takes the design philosophy even further. The device is now confirmed to feature a 50MP rear camera, a 6.5mm thin body, and ultra-slim 2.89mm bezels. These specifications suggest Lenovo is not just chasing performance but also aesthetics and build quality.

The ultra-thin profile is particularly notable. At 6.5mm, the Y700 Infinite 5G would be among the slimmest gaming tablets on the market, a feat that requires careful thermal management to prevent overheating during extended gaming sessions. The 2.89mm bezels, meanwhile, offer an immersive viewing experience without the need for a notch or punch-hole camera.

Lenovo goes all-in on tablets and power accessories

Beyond the Legion Tab Gen 5 and Y700 Infinite 5G, Lenovo is clearly betting big on the tablet segment. A report from Finance Biggo reveals that the company is expanding its lineup with a 9-speaker beast of a tablet, designed for media consumption and immersive audio. This move signals that Lenovo is not just targeting gamers but also entertainment enthusiasts who want a portable device that can double as a mini home theatre.

In addition to new hardware, Lenovo is sweetening the deal with discounts on its existing gaming slate, offering 25% off, and a 140W power bank at a reduced price. These promotions are likely aimed at clearing inventory ahead of new launches while also building a loyal ecosystem of accessories for the Legion brand.

Slimmer, lighter, and smarter: The Y700 AI

CNBC TV18 has also revealed details about the Lenovo Legion Y700 AI, which takes the slimness and weight reduction even further. The Y700 AI is described as having a slimmer body and lighter design compared to its predecessors, with additional features that leverage artificial intelligence for enhanced gaming performance and battery management.

Gizmochina's teaser coverage of the Legion Y700 Infinity adds to the picture, showcasing symmetrical bezels and a design that emphasises both elegance and ergonomics. The teaser images suggest Lenovo is paying close attention to the little details—like bezel symmetry—that often go unnoticed but contribute significantly to the overall user experience.

What to watch next

With the Legion Tab Gen 5 already impressing reviewers and the Y700 Infinite 5G and Y700 AI on the horizon, Lenovo is positioning itself as a serious contender in the compact gaming tablet space. The company's aggressive push into thinner designs, higher-resolution cameras, and AI-driven features suggests that the next few months will be crucial for the Legion brand.

Consumers can expect official launch dates and pricing for the Y700 Infinite 5G and Y700 AI in the coming weeks. Whether these devices will live up to the hype generated by their spec sheets remains to be seen, but if the Legion Tab Gen 5's early reviews are any indication, Lenovo is on the right track.
