Rival Protests Outside Parliament Over Jharkhand, Rahul Gandhi

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • NDA MPs protested against police action in Jharkhand, shouting slogans against Rahul Gandhi.
  • Congress and Opposition MPs staged a counter-protest targeting Amit Shah and the Ram Temple issue.
  • BJP leader Dharmendra Pradhan called Rahul Gandhi a 'habitual liar' and accused Congress of 'selective democracy'.

Parliament Sees Dueling Demonstrations

New Delhi witnessed a dramatic political clash on Tuesday as rival protests unfolded outside Parliament, with ruling NDA MPs and Opposition leaders trading barbs over Jharkhand, Rahul Gandhi, and the Ram Temple. The dueling demonstrations underscored the deepening political chasm ahead of the winter session.

BJP and NDA lawmakers gathered first, shouting slogans against Rahul Gandhi over the police action in Jharkhand, where protesters had clashed with authorities. The ruling side accused the Congress leader of instigating unrest, while counter-protests by Opposition MPs quickly followed, targeting Union Home Minister Amit Shah and the ruling party's stance on the Ram Temple.

Jharkhand Police Action Sparks Political Firestorm

The protests were triggered by the police crackdown on protesters in Jharkhand, an incident that has become a flashpoint between the Centre and the state government. NDA MPs alleged that Rahul Gandhi's remarks had encouraged the protesters, leading to the violence, and demanded an apology from the Congress.

Congress and its allies, however, dismissed the charges, insisting the BJP was deflecting from the real issues. "The police action was brutal and unconstitutional," one Opposition MP said, requesting anonymity. "Instead of answering for that, they are targeting Rahul Gandhi."

Pradhan's Sharp Attack on Gandhi

Union Minister Dharmendra Pradhan escalated the war of words, calling Rahul Gandhi a 'habitual liar' and accusing the Congress of practicing 'selective democracy'. "They speak of democracy only when it suits them," Pradhan told reporters outside Parliament. "Their silence on the Ram Temple and their support for those who disrupt peace exposes their double standards."

Pradhan's remarks were met with sharp retorts from Congress leaders, who pointed to the government's handling of the Ram Temple issue and its alleged attempts to polarise voters. "The BJP cannot hide behind temple politics forever," a Congress spokesperson said. "The people of Jharkhand deserve justice, not slogans."

Counter-Protests Target Amit Shah

As the NDA MPs chanted 'Shame, Rahul Gandhi', Opposition leaders, including Priyanka Gandhi, staged a counter-demonstration, raising slogans against Amit Shah and the Ram Temple. The visual of two protest camps facing each other—separated by barricades—captured the fractious mood of the nation's political class.

Priyanka Gandhi's presence added to the charged atmosphere, with her party colleagues accusing the BJP of using state machinery to silence dissent. The Congress also sought to link the Ram Temple to the Jharkhand issue, arguing that the BJP was prioritising its Hindutva agenda over the rights of tribal communities.

Political Theatre or Deeper Divide?

Political analysts noted that Tuesday's protests were as much about optics as substance. Both sides sought to frame the narrative ahead of upcoming elections in several states, with the Jharkhand police action and the Ram Temple serving as convenient rallying points.

"This is classic political theatre," said a Delhi-based political commentator. "But it also reflects the hardening of positions. The BJP is determined to paint Rahul Gandhi as an outsider who incites violence, while the Congress is equally set on portraying the government as authoritarian."

What Happens Next

With Parliament set to continue its session, more confrontations are likely as both sides press their advantage. The government is expected to face questions on the Jharkhand police action, while the Opposition may push for a discussion on the Ram Temple. All eyes will be on whether the two sides can find common ground or if the protests will only deepen the deadlock.
