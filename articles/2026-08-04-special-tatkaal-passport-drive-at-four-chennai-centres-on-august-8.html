Special Tatkaal passport drive at four Chennai centres on August 8

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Four passport centres in Chennai will host a special Tatkaal drive on August 8.
  • The drive aims to expedite passport issuance for applicants with urgent travel requirements.
  • Applicants are advised to book appointments in advance through the official portal.

Chennai residents with urgent travel plans can breathe easier. The Regional Passport Office is organising a special Tatkaal drive at four passport seva kendras in the city on August 8. The initiative is designed to fast-track passport applications for those who need documents in a hurry.

Four centres, one day

The special drive will be held at designated Passport Seva Kendras across Chennai. While the Regional Passport Office has not disclosed the exact addresses, officials confirmed that all four centres will operate dedicated counters for Tatkaal applicants on that day.

Tatkaal is a premium service that ensures passport issuance within one to three working days, subject to police verification. It is meant for emergencies such as medical treatment abroad, higher education admissions, or overseas employment.

How to apply

Applicants must first register on the Passport Seva portal and fill the application form under the Tatkaal category. After payment of the applicable fees, they can schedule an appointment at one of the four participating centres. Walk-ins are unlikely to be entertained, so booking in advance is strongly advised.

Documents required include proof of address, date of birth, and identity. For Tatkaal, applicants also need to provide a valid reason for urgency. Officials have urged people to carry self-attested copies of all documents to avoid last-minute hiccups.

What to expect on the day

The drive will run during regular office hours. Staff have been instructed to process applications swiftly, with a target of clearing as many cases as possible by the end of the day. However, officials caution that police verification is a separate step and may take additional time in some cases.

Applicants who already have appointments for regular passport services are not affected by this drive. The special counters are exclusively for Tatkaal requests.

Why this matters

Passport delays have been a recurring grievance among Chennai residents, particularly during peak travel seasons. This targeted drive is a step towards clearing the backlog and addressing urgent cases. It also aligns with the central government's push to digitise and streamline passport services.

In recent months, the Regional Passport Office has introduced several measures to reduce waiting times, including expanded appointment slots and weekend drives. August 8 will be the latest in this series of initiatives.

Officials have not yet confirmed whether similar drives will be held in other cities or on a recurring basis. Applicants are advised to monitor the official Passport Seva portal for updates.

The success of this drive could set a template for future expedited services. For now, those with pressing travel plans should mark August 8 on their calendars and complete the online formalities well in advance.
