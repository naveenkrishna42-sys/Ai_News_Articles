Federal Scapia Credit Card: Features & Benefits Explained

The Federal Scapia Credit Card stands as a premium offering designed for the discerning Indian traveller. This co-branded partnership between Federal Bank and Scapia delivers unmatched travel-centric rewards alongside comprehensive lifestyle benefits. The card targets salaried professionals and business individuals seeking premium experiences without excessive annual fees.

Key features include 2.5 lakh reward points annually on spending up to ₹5 lakh, 12 complimentary domestic lounge accesses, and exclusive airport transfer services. Cardholders enjoy priority check-in facilities at major Indian airports and discounted hotel bookings through partner networks. The reward redemption system offers flexible options including flight tickets, hotel stays, merchandise and voucher redemptions at favourable conversion rates.

Reward Structure & Travel Benefits

Scapia's reward engine operates on a tiered accumulation system where members earn points at accelerated rates on travel bookings and lifestyle spends. Domestic flight bookings yield 8 reward points per ₹100 spent while international travel generates 10 points per ₹100. Hotel stays across partner properties provide 6 points per ₹100, creating substantial value for frequent travellers.

Additional perks include 12 complimentary domestic lounge visits annually at over 1000 locations, priority security clearance services, and dedicated customer support lines. Cardholders gain access to exclusive airport lounges including Plaza Premium, DG World and Air India Lounges across major Indian airports and select international terminals.

Eligibility Criteria & Application Process

Prospective applicants must meet specific eligibility benchmarks to qualify for Federal Scapia Credit Card. Minimum monthly income requirement stands at ₹15,000 for salaried individuals and ₹25,000 for freelancers and business professionals. Age criteria mandate minimum 21 years and maximum 70 years at card validity expiry.

The online application process begins through the official Federal Bank website or Scapia mobile application. Applicants must upload essential documents including KYC verification, income proof, address proof and recent photograph. Processing typically completes within 24-48 hours for qualified candidates meeting all verification requirements.

Fee Structure & Value Justification

The annual fee of ₹5,000 presents compelling value proposition given the cumulative benefits package. Considering average lounge access value at ₹2,500 per visit, travel booking rewards estimated at ₹15,000-30,000 annually, plus lifestyle redemption opportunities, the net benefit significantly exceeds the cost structure.

First-year applicants enjoy fee waiver eligibility upon spending ₹2 lakhs within initial 90 days, effectively making the first year complimentary. Replacement card fees apply at ₹500 for lost or damaged cards, while cash advance transactions attract 2.5% processing fee plus standard interest rates from transaction date.

Maximizing Card Benefits & Strategic Usage

Savvy cardholders optimise rewards through strategic spending patterns and timing. Concentrating travel bookings, utility payments and major purchases during promotional bonus periods maximises point accumulation efficiency. Leveraging partner hotel chains and airline alliances ensures optimal reward conversion rates.

International travellers should utilise the card for foreign currency transactions to harness favourable exchange rates and avoid additional markup charges. The dedicated concierge service assists with complex travel arrangements, restaurant reservations and exclusive experience bookings across metros.
