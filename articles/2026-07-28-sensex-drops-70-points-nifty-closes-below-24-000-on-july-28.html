Sensex Drops 70 Points, Nifty Closes Below 24,000 on July 28

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex declined by 70 points on Tuesday.
  • Nifty closed below the crucial 24,000 level.
  • Markets ended in the red after a volatile trading session.

Benchmarks End Lower After Choppy Session

Indian stock markets closed in negative territory on Tuesday, July 28, dragged by selling in select heavyweights. The BSE Sensex slipped 70 points, while the NSE Nifty ended the day below the psychologically important 24,000 mark.

Traders described the session as range-bound, with the indices swinging between gains and losses before settling lower. The Nifty’s close under 24,000 is being watched closely by market participants as a potential near-term resistance level.

Broader Markets Mixed

The broader market showed a mixed trend. While some midcap and smallcap stocks managed to buck the trend, the overall sentiment remained cautious. Sectoral indices were split, with IT and pharma stocks witnessing some buying interest, while banking and auto names faced pressure.

Analysts pointed to a lack of fresh triggers as a reason for the subdued movement. Global cues remained mixed, with Asian markets trading without a clear direction.

What Weighed on Sentiment

Profit booking in recent outperformers and uncertainty ahead of key economic data releases contributed to the decline. Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) were net sellers in the cash market, adding to the downward bias.

Domestic institutional investors (DIIs) provided some support, but it was not enough to lift the benchmarks into positive territory. The volatility index, India VIX, inched up marginally, reflecting some nervousness among traders.

Stocks in Focus

Among individual stocks, index heavyweight Reliance Industries ended with losses, putting pressure on the Sensex. Banking stocks including HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank also traded lower, contributing to the Nifty’s fall below 24,000.

On the other hand, select IT stocks such as Infosys and TCS managed to close in the green, offering some relief to the markets. Analysts expect the trend to remain stock-specific in the near term.

What to Watch

Traders will now focus on upcoming quarterly earnings from major companies and global macroeconomic data. The movement of the rupee against the dollar and crude oil prices will also be key factors to monitor in the coming sessions.
