CWG 2026 Day 6: Harjinder Kaur wins silver; three boxers guarantee medals

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Harjinder Kaur lifts 227kg total to win silver in women's 69kg weightlifting, India's seventh medal in the sport at the 2026 Games.
  • Boxers Preeti (women's 54kg), Priya (women's 57kg), and Jadumani Singh (men's 63.5kg) all reached their respective semifinals, ensuring at least bronze medals.
  • Day 6 saw India add to its medal tally with strong performances across weightlifting and boxing events in Glasgow.

Harjinder Kaur secures silver in weightlifting

Indian weightlifter Harjinder Kaur delivered a commanding performance in the women's 69kg category at the 2026 Commonwealth Games in Glasgow on Tuesday. She lifted a combined total of 227kg to clinch the silver medal, marking India's seventh weightlifting medal of the ongoing Games.

Harjinder, who had won bronze at the previous edition, upgraded the colour of her medal with a superlative show. “No pain, no gain,” she said after the event, reflecting on her rigorous preparation. Her effort in the clean and jerk segment was particularly impressive, sealing the second spot on the podium.

The weightlifting contingent has been a consistent medal provider for India in Glasgow. With Harjinder's silver, the count now stands at two golds, three silvers, and two bronzes from the lifting platform.

Boxers Preeti, Priya, and Jadumani guarantee medals

India's boxing campaign gathered momentum on Day 6 as three pugilists assured themselves of at least bronze medals. Preeti (women's 54kg), Priya (women's 57kg), and Jadumani Singh (men's 63.5kg) all won their quarterfinal bouts, booking semifinal spots.

Since the Commonwealth Games awards bronze to both losing semifinalists, each boxer is now certain to leave Glasgow with a medal. Their performances have lifted expectations for the Indian boxing team, which has faced stiff competition from host nation Scotland and other powerhouses like England and Australia.

Preeti dominated her bout with sharp combinations, while Priya showed remarkable composure against a taller opponent. Jadumani, competing in the men's lightweight division, used his reach and footwork to outpoint his rival.

What to watch next

The semifinals for all three boxers are scheduled later this week. For Harjinder, the focus will shift to recovering from the exertion and preparing for possible future international events. India's medal tally is expected to grow as athletics and other events get underway in the coming days.
