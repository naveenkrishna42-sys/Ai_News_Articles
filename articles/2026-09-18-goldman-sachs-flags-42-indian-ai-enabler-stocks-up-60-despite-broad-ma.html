Goldman Sachs Flags 42 Indian AI‑Enabler Stocks Up 60% Despite Broad Market Slide

✍️ By TIVRA News Markets & Finance Desk 📅 2026-09-18 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Goldman Sachs identifies 42 AI‑enabler stocks delivering roughly 60% YTD gains amid a 12% Nifty decline.
  • The basket is split across AI infrastructure, data‑centre plays and 5G‑linked companies, each benefiting from policy tailwinds and capex cycles.
  • Investors gain sector exposure but face valuation stretch, concentration risk and dependence on global tech spending.

Goldman Sachs’ AI‑Enabler Spotlight

Goldman Sachs has pointed out a discreet rally within India’s equity market: 42 stocks classified as AI‑enablers have risen approximately 60% year‑to‑date, even as the broader Nifty 50 index has slipped about 12%. This divergence suggests that thematic strength in artificial‑intelligence related infrastructure is outperforming the market’s overall risk‑off sentiment.

The observation comes amid a wave of launches and commentary focused on AI‑driven growth. NSE Indices introduced the Nifty AI Catalysts Index in September 2026, aiming to capture the performance of companies directly involved in AI hardware, software enablement and adjacent telecom upgrades. Goldman’s note frames the 42‑stock basket as a leading indicator of where capital is flowing despite headline index weakness.

Breakdown of the 42 Stocks

AI Infrastructure

The largest cluster within the basket comprises firms that supply chips, servers and networking gear optimised for AI workloads. These companies have benefited from a surge in data‑centre capex as both domestic hyperscalers and global cloud providers expand their Indian footprints. Revenue visibility has improved, supporting the roughly 60% price appreciation attributed to this segment.

Data Centre Exposure

A second group includes pure‑play data‑centre operators and real‑estate trusts that have seen higher utilisation rates and rental yields. Government incentives for digital infrastructure, coupled with rising demand for AI‑training clusters, have driven occupancy levels above pre‑pandemic peaks. Analysts note that lease‑up cycles in this segment typically lag hardware demand by six to twelve months, providing a runway for further growth.

5G‑Linked Plays

The final segment links AI enablers to the ongoing 5G rollout. Telecom equipment makers and system integrators that supply edge‑computing nodes and private‑network solutions have captured orders from manufacturing and logistics firms seeking low‑latency AI inference. The synergy between 5G rollout schedules and AI workload placement has contributed to the overall basket’s performance.

Investment Implications and Risks

For investors, the 42‑stock grouping offers a concentrated way to gain exposure to AI‑related capex without betting on individual names. The thematic tilt can diversify a portfolio that is otherwise weighted toward traditional sectors such as banking or consumer goods. However, the group’s valuation metrics have risen in tandem with prices, prompting caution about potential mean‑reversion if global tech spending slows.

Key risks include sector concentration: a downturn in AI hardware orders or a delay in data‑centre projects could affect a large portion of the basket simultaneously. Additionally, the performance of these stocks is closely linked to global supply‑chain health and the timing of capex cycles in North America and Europe, which remain uncertain amid macro‑economic headwinds.

Outlook for 2026 and Beyond

Goldman Sachs maintains that AI‑infrastructure stocks in India could continue to outperform through 2026, citing an estimated 60% upside for the sector on a year‑over‑year basis. The bank’s view hinges on sustained government support for digitalisation, continued expansion of hyperscale campuses and the maturation of 5G networks that enable edge AI applications.

Looking ahead, market participants will watch quarterly order books, capacity utilisation reports and any shifts in global AI investment trends. Should the macro environment stabilise, the thematic rally may extend beyond the current 42‑stock universe, potentially prompting broader index providers to expand AI‑focused benchmarks.
