Hockey India president Dilip Tirkey questions saffron jersey approval

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hockey India president Dilip Tirkey has sought clarification on who approved the controversial jersey change.
  • Officials claim they were informed about the saffron jersey switch only at the last minute.
  • Political parties have criticised the move, calling it an unnecessary politicisation of sports.

Hockey India president Dilip Tirkey has demanded to know who authorised the team's switch to saffron jerseys, a decision that has triggered a political storm and exposed deep divisions within the sport's governing body.

Tirkey, a former captain of the Indian men's hockey team, said officials were kept in the dark until the eleventh hour, raising questions about transparency in the organisation's decision-making process. His remarks come amid growing criticism from political quarters and former players over the sudden colour change.

Officials kept in the dark

According to sources within Hockey India, the move to adopt saffron jerseys was communicated to senior officials only shortly before its public unveiling. Tirkey, who took over as president in 2024, is believed to have been briefed after the fact, leaving him to scramble for answers.

"Who approved it?" Tirkey is reported to have asked, reflecting the frustration of several office-bearers who felt bypassed in the process. The lack of prior consultation has led to allegations of unilateral decision-making, further straining relations within the federation.

Political reactions intensify

The jersey change has not gone unnoticed in the political arena. The Congress party has accused the ruling BJP and its ideological parent, the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS), of using sports as a platform for their political agenda. In a statement, the party demanded a rollback of the colour change, asserting that "sports shouldn't be a political experiment."

Separately, commentators have pointed to the saffron jersey as the latest in a series of cultural symbols being reimagined under the current dispensation. TheWire.in described it as the "BJP-RSS's latest saffron project," while The New Indian Express noted that the controversy has revealed a significant chasm in Hockey India's leadership.

A deepening rift

Insiders suggest the jersey issue is only the tip of the iceberg. Long-simmering differences between Tirkey and other senior officials have reportedly come to the fore, with the federation now split over how to handle the fallout. Some members are pushing for a reversal, while others see the change as a deliberate attempt to align the sport with a particular cultural identity.

Former players have also weighed in, with many expressing concern that the focus should remain on performance rather than symbolism. The Indian men's team, currently preparing for upcoming international fixtures, is said to be unfazed by the controversy, though the distraction is unwelcome.

What happens next

Hockey India is expected to convene an emergency meeting in the coming days to address the issue. Tirkey has indicated that he will seek a full explanation from those responsible for the decision, and a formal statement is likely once the internal review is complete.

The episode serves as a reminder that in Indian sports, the jersey is never just a jersey. As the debate rages on, all eyes will be on whether the federation can restore unity and refocus on the game itself.
