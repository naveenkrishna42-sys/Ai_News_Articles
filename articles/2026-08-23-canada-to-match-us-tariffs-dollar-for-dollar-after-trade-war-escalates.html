Canada to match US tariffs 'dollar for dollar' after trade war escalates

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • US imposes 50% tariffs on Canadian goods
  • Canada to match tariffs 'dollar for dollar'
  • Trade talks collapse after US and Canada fail to reach agreement

US Imposes 50% Tariffs on Canadian Goods

In a significant escalation of trade tensions, the United States has imposed 50% tariffs on a list of Canadian goods, including aluminum, steel, and seafood. The move comes after months of failed negotiations between the two countries over trade barriers and market access.

Canada Vows to Match Tariffs 'Dollar for Dollar'

Canada's Finance Minister, Bill Carney, has announced that the country will retaliate with 'dollar for dollar' tariffs on US goods. Carney described Trump's new tariffs as a 'miscalculation' and called for a more constructive approach to resolving the ongoing trade dispute.

Trade Talks Collapse

The imposition of tariffs marks the collapse of trade talks between the US and Canada, which had been ongoing for several months. Both countries had been negotiating to address concerns over trade barriers and market access, but the talks broke down after the US announced its new tariffs.

Trade War Escalates

The escalation of tariffs comes as the US and Canada continue to engage in a broader trade war with other countries. The US has already imposed tariffs on steel and aluminum imports from several countries, including Canada, Mexico, and the European Union. Canada's retaliatory tariffs are expected to further escalate the trade tensions.

Impact on Trade and Economy

The imposition of tariffs is expected to have significant implications for trade and the economy. The US and Canada are two of the world's largest economies, and any disruption to trade between the two countries could have far-reaching consequences. The impact of the new tariffs on specific industries and sectors will depend on the specific goods that are affected.

What Happens Next?

As the trade war between the US and Canada continues to escalate, it is important to monitor the developments closely. The imposition of tariffs could lead to further retaliatory measures from other countries, and could also have implications for global trade and the economy. The ongoing negotiations between the US and Canada will be closely watched, as both countries seek to find a resolution to the trade dispute.
