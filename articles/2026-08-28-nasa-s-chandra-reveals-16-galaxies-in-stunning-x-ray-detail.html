NASA has released a new gallery of 16 galaxies captured by the Chandra X-ray Observatory. The images, which combine data from Chandra, the James Webb Space Telescope, and the Hubble Space Telescope, offer a detailed look at cosmic phenomena like supermassive black holes and star formation.

The Significance of the New Gallery

The new gallery is significant because it provides a unique perspective on the universe. X-ray observations, which are invisible to the human eye, reveal aspects of galaxies that are not visible in other wavelengths. This includes the presence of supermassive black holes, the hot gas that surrounds them, and the regions where new stars are being formed.

Key Features of the Gallery

The gallery includes a diverse range of galaxies, from spiral to elliptical, and from nearby to distant.
