Telangana Flood Crisis: 11 Rescue Personnel Missing After Boat Capsizes in Khammam

A grave situation has unfolded in Telangana's Khammam district, where eleven personnel from the State Disaster Response Force (SDRF) and Fire Services are feared missing. Their rescue boats reportedly capsized in the heavily swollen waters of Peddacheruvu, a significant lake in the region. The incident occurred during an ongoing operation to assist residents affected by relentless floodwaters that have inundated large parts of the state.

This tragic development highlights the extreme perils faced by emergency responders as they navigate treacherous conditions to save lives. The search and rescue efforts are now intensified, with additional resources being mobilised to locate the missing personnel.

The Incident: A Perilous Rescue Attempt

The incident took place on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, when two boats carrying the rescue teams encountered severe turbulence in the swollen Peddacheruvu. Heavy rainfall over the past few days has led to alarming water levels, turning what would typically be a routine operation into a high-stakes endeavour. Witnesses reported seeing the boats overturn, plunging the personnel into the turbulent waters.

These responders were engaged in evacuating marooned individuals and providing critical aid, a testament to their dedication in the face of widespread devastation. The sudden capsizing has sent shockwaves through the emergency services community and the state administration.

Multi-Agency Response and Escalation

Immediately following the incident, local authorities launched an extensive search operation. The complexity of the task, however, necessitated further assistance. Subsequently, teams from the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) have been deployed to Khammam to bolster the ongoing efforts.

NDRF personnel, equipped with specialized gear and trained for challenging aquatic environments, are now working alongside local SDRF and Fire Services units. The focus remains squarely on locating the missing eleven, navigating through strong currents and debris, which significantly complicates the search.

Telangana's Ongoing Flood Challenge

This incident is set against the backdrop of a severe flood situation gripping Telangana. Persistent heavy monsoon rains have led to overflowing rivers, reservoirs, and lakes, causing widespread disruption, displacement, and infrastructure damage. Several districts have reported extensive waterlogging, cutting off communities and posing immense challenges for relief operations.

Emergency services across the state are stretched thin, working around the clock to mitigate the impact of the floods. The capsizing in Khammam serves as a stark reminder of the inherent dangers and sacrifices made by those at the forefront of disaster response.

Understanding the Risks for First Responders

Personnel from SDRF, NDRF, and Fire Services undergo rigorous training to prepare for such extreme conditions. Their expertise includes swift water rescue, boat handling in adverse weather, and disaster management protocols. However, natural calamities, particularly flash floods and rapidly rising water bodies, can present unpredictable and overwhelming forces.

The equipment used, while robust, has limitations against nature's fury. Such incidents underscore the critical need for continuous safety assessments, advanced warning systems, and robust operational planning to protect the very individuals who protect communities.
