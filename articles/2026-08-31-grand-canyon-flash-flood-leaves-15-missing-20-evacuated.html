Flash Flood Devastates Grand Canyon

A sudden flash flood in the Grand Canyon has left at least 15 people missing and over 20 evacuated. The National Park Service (NPS) has confirmed one fatality, with authorities warning of ongoing risks. The flood, which struck without warning, sent a bunkhouse and boulders rushing downstream, causing significant destruction.

Evacuations and Ongoing Risks

Emergency services have been working tirelessly to evacuate residents and visitors from the affected areas. The Phantom Ranch and North Kaibab Trail have been closed due to the flash flooding, further complicating the situation. Authorities are urging caution, as the threat of further flooding remains.

Environmental experts warn that flash floods in the Grand Canyon are becoming more frequent due to climate change. The increased rainfall and melting snow contribute to the heightened risk.
