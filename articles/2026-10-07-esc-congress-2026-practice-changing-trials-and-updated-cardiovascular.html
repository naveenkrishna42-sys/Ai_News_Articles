Overview of ESC Congress 2026

The European Society of Cardiology Congress 2026 convened in Barcelona from August 30 to September 2, drawing more than 34,000 delegates from cardiology, internal medicine, nursing, and industry. Over 2,200 scientific abstracts were presented, including 150 late‑breaking trial sessions that attracted heightened attention. The congress emphasized rapid translation of trial findings into everyday clinical workflows, with dedicated sessions on implementation science and digital health integration.

Scope and Attendance

Scientific tracks spanned heart failure, coronary artery disease, arrhythmias, structural interventions, and preventive cardiology. Live‑streaming extended reach to an estimated 120,000 additional viewers worldwide, facilitating real‑time discussion on social media platforms. Hands‑on workshops covered newer device techniques, such as lead‑less pacemaker implantation and transcatheter tricuspid valve repair, reinforcing the meeting’s practical focus.

Landmark Trials Redefining Therapy

EMPOWER‑HF: SGLT2 Inhibition in HFpEF

EMPOWER‑HF randomized 5,400 patients with heart failure and preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) to empagliflozin 10 mg daily or matching placebo. The primary endpoint of cardiovascular death or heart‑failure hospitalization occurred in 9.8 % of the empagliflozin group versus 13.4 % of placebo, yielding a hazard ratio of 0.72 (95 % CI 0.62‑0.84, p<0.001). Subgroup analyses showed consistent benefit regardless of age, sex, baseline NT‑proBNP, or presence of type 2 diabetes, supporting a class effect for SGLT2 inhibitors in HFpEF.
