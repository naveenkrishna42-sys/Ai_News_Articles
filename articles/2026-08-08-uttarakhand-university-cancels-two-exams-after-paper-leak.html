Uttarakhand university cancels two exams after paper leak

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • Two exams cancelled after question papers allegedly leaked
  • University orders inquiry into the leak incident
  • Fresh exam dates to be announced later

DEHRADUN — A university in Uttarakhand has cancelled two examinations after question papers were allegedly leaked, prompting an internal inquiry and raising fresh concerns about examination security in the state.

The decision was announced on Friday, just days before the scheduled tests were to be held. University officials confirmed the cancellation but did not immediately specify which courses or departments were affected, saying only that the matter is under investigation.

Exams called off after leak suspected

The two examinations, which were slated for later this week, have been postponed until further notice. According to a brief statement from the university, the move follows information that the question papers had been "compromised" — a term the administration used to describe the alleged leak.

Students who were set to appear for the papers have been told to await a revised schedule. The university said new dates would be announced once the probe concludes, though it did not offer a timeline.

Inquiry ordered, authorities tight-lipped

A committee has been constituted to look into how the papers may have been accessed. The panel is expected to examine the chain of custody — from the printing of the papers to their distribution at examination centres — and to question staff involved in the process.

Officials have not yet confirmed whether any individual has been identified or detained in connection with the leak. Police have not been formally involved so far, and the university appears to be handling the matter internally.

This is not the first time such an incident has troubled the state's education system. In recent years, several universities and boards across India have faced similar allegations, leading to cancellations, re-exams and, in some cases, criminal cases against accused persons.

Students hit by uncertainty

For students, the cancellation has brought disruption and anxiety. Many had travelled from distant towns to reach examination centres, and the sudden postponement has left them scrambling for accommodation and revised travel plans.

Some students took to social media to express frustration, asking the university to release a clear schedule at the earliest. Others demanded transparency in the investigation, arguing that repeated leaks penalise honest candidates and benefit those who attempt to cheat.

The university, in its statement, urged students to remain calm and said it would prioritise a fair and secure re-conduct of the exams.

What happens next

The committee's findings will determine whether the examinations are rescheduled as-is or whether the question papers are redrafted entirely. A decision is expected within the next two weeks, according to an official familiar with the matter, though no formal commitment has been made.

Observers will be watching whether the university takes stricter measures, such as digital encryption or monitored transport of papers, to prevent a recurrence. For now, thousands of students are left waiting — and hoping that the next date announced is one that holds.
