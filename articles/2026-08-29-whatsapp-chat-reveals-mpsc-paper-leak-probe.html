WhatsApp Chat Exposes MPSC Paper-Leak Scandal

A leaked WhatsApp chat has blown open a major probe into the alleged leakage of Maharashtra Public Service Commission (MPSC) examination papers. The chat, which surfaced on social media, reveals a Rs 50 lakh bribe paid to an unknown suspect. This development has sent shockwaves through the exam community and raised serious questions about the integrity of government exams.

Key Suspects Identified

The leaked chat has identified several key suspects, including a former MPSC official and a private coaching institute owner. The former official is alleged to have leaked the papers to the coaching institute owner in exchange for the bribe. The coaching institute owner, in turn, is alleged to have distributed the papers to students.

MPSC to Investigate Further

The MPSC has confirmed that it is investigating the allegations based on the leaked chat. The commission has issued a statement urging all candidates to remain calm and not to spread rumors. However, the exposure of the chat has raised serious concerns about the security of government exams.

Broader Implications

The MPSC paper-leak scandal has broader implications for the integrity of government exams. It raises serious questions about the security measures in place to prevent such leaks. The scandal also highlights the need for stricter regulations and penalties for those involved in such activities.
