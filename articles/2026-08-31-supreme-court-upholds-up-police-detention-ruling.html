Supreme Court upholds Allahabad HC's decision

The Supreme Court has stayed the Allahabad High Court's ₹10 lakh compensation order for a man who was illegally detained by Uttar Pradesh police. The top court's decision upholds the Allahabad HC's judgment but stays the compensation order due to procedural flaws.

Procedural flaws in the case

The Supreme Court's decision to stay the compensation order was based on procedural flaws in the case. The Allahabad HC had ordered ₹10 lakh in compensation for the man, who was illegally detained for 12 hours by UP police. However, the top court found that the lower court had not followed the proper procedure for awarding compensation in such cases.

Expert opinion on the case
