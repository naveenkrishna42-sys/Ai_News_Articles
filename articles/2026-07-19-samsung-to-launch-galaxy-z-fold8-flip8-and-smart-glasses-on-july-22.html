Samsung to launch Galaxy Z Fold8, Flip8, and smart glasses on July 22

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung will launch the Galaxy Z Fold8, Z Flip8, and a new smart glasses product on July 22.
  • BTS member J-Hope has teased the Galaxy Z Fold8 ahead of the official event.
  • The company has debuted Flex Titanium display technology for the foldable lineup.
  • Leaks indicate a Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra variant may also be introduced.

Samsung is gearing up for its next Unpacked event on July 22, where the company is expected to unveil a new generation of foldable smartphones and its first-ever smart glasses. The lineup will likely include the Galaxy Z Fold8, Galaxy Z Flip8, and possibly a higher-end Fold8 Ultra model.

J-Hope teases the Galaxy Z Fold8

BTS member J-Hope has given fans an early look at the upcoming Galaxy Z Fold8. In a social media post, the K-pop star was seen holding the device, sparking excitement across tech and entertainment circles. Samsung has a history of collaborating with BTS for product promotions, and this teaser fits that pattern.

The brief glimpse has fueled speculation about the Fold8's design and camera layout, though official specifications remain under wraps. Samsung has not commented on the teaser.

Flex Titanium: A new display technology

Samsung has officially introduced Flex Titanium, a new display technology designed to enhance durability and flexibility in foldable phones. According to the company's global newsroom, the material aims to reduce crease visibility and improve resistance to daily wear and tear.

The technology is expected to debut on the Galaxy Z Fold8 and possibly the Fold8 Ultra. Analysts see this as Samsung's answer to growing competition from Chinese rivals like Huawei and Xiaomi, who have also pushed foldable innovation.

The Flex Titanium panel is said to use a titanium-based substrate, making it lighter and stronger than previous iterations. Early reviews from tech blogs suggest the crease is significantly less noticeable than on the Galaxy Z Fold5 or Fold6.

Leaked specs point to a Fold8 Ultra

Leaks from multiple sources, including The Indian Express, suggest Samsung may launch a Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra alongside the standard Fold8. The Ultra variant is rumored to feature a larger main display, upgraded camera sensors, and support for the S Pen.

Both the Fold8 and Flip8 are expected to run on the Exynos 2500 or Snapdragon 8 Gen 4 chipset, depending on the market. Battery sizes and charging speeds remain unconfirmed, but leaks point to incremental improvements over the previous generation.

The smart glasses product, which has been in development for years, is expected to be a lightweight augmented reality wearable. Samsung has not released details about its specs or pricing.

What happens next

All eyes are on Samsung's July 22 Unpacked event. The company will need to deliver on both hardware and software to justify premium pricing in a slowing smartphone market. With competition heating up, the success of the Fold8 lineup and the smart glasses could define Samsung's strategy for the rest of the decade.
