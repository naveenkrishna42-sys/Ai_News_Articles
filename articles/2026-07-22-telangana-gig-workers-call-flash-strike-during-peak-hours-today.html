Telangana Gig Workers Call Flash Strike During Peak Hours Today

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Flash strike called during peak hours in Telangana
  • Workers demand higher pay and improved conditions
  • Disruptions expected in delivery and ride-hailing services

Gig workers in Telangana have announced a flash strike during peak hours today, causing widespread disruption to delivery and ride-hailing services across Hyderabad.

The surprise action, called by worker collectives, aims to pressure platforms on wage revisions and better working conditions. Commuters and customers face delays as drivers and delivery partners down tools.

Strike Details and Timing

The strike began early this morning and is expected to stretch through lunch and evening rush hours. Workers have gathered at key hubs across the city, refusing to accept orders or ferry passengers.

Organisers said the flash strike was a response to stalled negotiations with major aggregators. They accused platforms of ignoring long-standing demands for a minimum fare hike and transparent pay structures.

Worker Demands

At the heart of the protest are demands for a base fare increase of at least 20 per cent. Workers also want a cap on commission deductions and compensation for fuel costs.

“We are not being paid enough to cover our expenses,” said a rider who did not want to be named. “The companies make profits while we struggle.”

Safety concerns, including insurance coverage and grievance redressal, are also on the table. Many workers claim they face arbitrary deactivation from apps without explanation.

Impact on Services

Ride-hailing apps showed surge pricing on most routes in Hyderabad by late morning. Food delivery times have doubled in several areas, with many orders cancelled due to lack of drivers.

Commuters took to social media to express frustration. Some supported the workers, while others demanded alternative arrangements from platforms.

“I had to wait 40 minutes for a cab,” said Priya Sharma, a software professional. “I understand their issues, but this is inconvenient.”

Platforms have not yet issued a detailed statement. Sources say they are monitoring the situation and trying to minimise disruption through incentives for non-striking workers.

Previous Tensions

This is not the first such action in Telangana. Gig workers have held sporadic protests over the past year, but this flash strike is among the most coordinated.

Labour rights groups have called for a regulatory framework to protect gig workers. The Telangana government has set up a committee to study the issue, but concrete steps remain pending.

Officials have not commented on today's strike. The state labour department is expected to hold talks with all parties in the coming days.

Workers have warned of more frequent flash strikes if their demands are not met. They plan to escalate the protest if no progress is made by next week.

What happens next depends on whether platforms come to the table. For now, Hyderabad's streets are quieter, and many orders remain undelivered.
