iPhone 18 design overhaul tipped, more RAM for iPhone 18e

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iPhone 18 said to feature a surprising design upgrade
  • iPhone 18e to receive more RAM, improving performance
  • Reports also hint at price hikes and higher production costs
  • Pro models may launch in September due to strong demand

Design shift in the works

Apple's next-generation iPhone 18 is reportedly set to bring a design overhaul that has caught the attention of industry watchers. The change, described as surprising, could mark one of the most noticeable visual updates to the base model in years. While specifics remain under wraps, the shift is expected to distinguish the upcoming series from its predecessor.

According to a report by Notebookcheck, the design upgrade is part of a broader refresh that Apple is preparing for its 2026 lineup. The company has historically reserved major design changes for Pro models, but the new leak suggests the standard iPhone 18 will also get a fresh look this time around.

Memory boost for the iPhone 18e

Alongside the design change, the iPhone 18e is said to receive a bump in RAM. The entry-level model, which has often been seen as a budget-friendly option, could see improved multitasking and smoother performance with the added memory. Apple has not officially confirmed the specifications, but the rumour aligns with the company's pattern of incrementally upgrading its more affordable devices.

The additional RAM is expected to benefit users who rely on their phones for productivity and gaming. It also suggests that Apple is keen to keep the iPhone 18e relevant in a competitive mid-range segment, where Android rivals often offer higher memory at lower price points.

Pricing and production cost concerns

Separate reports have indicated that the iPhone 18 series could see a price hike of up to Rs 10,000 in India after launch. The increase, if implemented, would affect both old and new models, potentially impacting buyer sentiment in one of Apple's key growth markets. A report from 91mobiles.com has highlighted this possibility, though Apple has not commented on the matter.

Adding to the speculation, TrendForce has estimated that the iPhone 18 Pro's bill of materials could surge nearly 40 per cent. The rise in component costs is attributed to advanced chipsets, display technology, and other premium parts. Analysts suggest that Apple may have to sacrifice some profit margins to sustain shipment volumes, especially in price-sensitive markets like India.

Pro model launch timeline

Amid these developments, Forbes has reported that the iPhone 18 Pro could launch in September, driven by strong consumer demand. The timeline aligns with Apple's traditional release schedule, though supply chain constraints could still cause delays. The Pro models are expected to feature significant camera and performance upgrades, making them a focus for early adopters.

For Indian consumers, the upcoming series has generated considerable buzz. India Today has listed nine reasons to wait for the iPhone 18 Pro despite the rumoured price hike. These include expected improvements in battery life, display quality, and the new design language.

What to watch next

As the launch window approaches, more leaks and official confirmations are likely to surface. Apple's September event, if it happens as reported, will reveal the full extent of the design changes and the RAM specifications. Until then, potential buyers will have to weigh the rumoured upgrades against the possible price increases.
