Convocation cancelled amid student protests

The National Law School of India University (NLSIU) convocation was abruptly cancelled on August 27, 2026, following student protests. The decision came after a meeting between students and the Chief Justice of India (CJI), which ended with students rejecting the CJI's demands.

CJI meeting and rejected demands

Students had convened a meeting with the CJI to discuss their grievances, which included demands for better infrastructure, faculty support, and improved academic standards. The meeting, which lasted several hours, ended with the CJI presenting his proposals. However, students rejected these proposals, citing insufficient improvements and lack of transparency in the decision-making process.

Broader implications for academic freedom and student rights

The cancellation of the NLSIU convocation has significant implications for academic freedom and student rights. It highlights the growing tension between students and the university administration, as well as the broader issue of student activism in India. The event underscores the need for better communication and dialogue between students, faculty, and university authorities.
