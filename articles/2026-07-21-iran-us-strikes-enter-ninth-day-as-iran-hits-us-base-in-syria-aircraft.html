Iran-US Strikes Enter Ninth Day as Iran Hits US Base in Syria, Aircraft in Jordan

📅 2026-07-21 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Iran-US military strikes continue for the ninth day, with no signs of de-escalation.
  • Missiles hit a US base in Syria and a US aircraft in Jordan, with IRGC claiming 'dozens killed'.
  • Iran's IRGC also targeted a US base in Kuwait and one in Qatar, calling it punishment for aggression.
  • Iran released exclusive video of ballistic missiles hitting US bases in the Gulf.

The military confrontation between Iran and the United States has entered its ninth day, with fresh attacks reported on Monday. Missiles struck a US base in Syria and a US aircraft stationed in Jordan, according to initial reports.

The escalation comes as Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) declared a 'full-scale war' against American forces in the region. The IRGC also claimed that Jordan helped target US forces, a charge that has not been independently verified.

Missiles Hit US Base in Kuwait

In a separate incident, missiles hit a US base in Kuwait. The scale of damage and casualties from that attack remains unclear. US Central Command has not yet issued a statement on the attack in Kuwait.

Iran's IRGC said the strike on the US base in Qatar was meant to 'punish the aggressor'. The IRGC released an exclusive video showing ballistic missiles hitting US bases in the Gulf, which was broadcast by Iranian state media.

IRGC Claims Jordan Helped Target US Forces

The IRGC alleged that Jordanian authorities assisted in identifying targets for the US military. The claim, reported by the ANI news agency, suggests that Iranian intelligence believes Jordan is actively cooperating with American operations. Jordan has not yet responded to the allegation.

Reports from the region indicate that 'dozens' of US personnel have been killed in the attacks on aircraft in Jordan. The exact number of casualties has not been confirmed by US officials.

Iran Releases Video of Ballistic Missile Strikes

Iran released an exclusive video showing ballistic missiles hitting US bases in the Gulf, according to a report by Firstpost America. The footage, which has been widely circulated on social media, is part of Iran's information warfare campaign.

The video shows multiple missiles striking what Iran claims are US military installations. Analysts say the footage is intended to boost morale at home and project strength abroad. The veracity of the footage has not been independently confirmed.

Iran's declaration of 'full-scale war' marks a significant rhetorical escalation. The phrase was used by an IRGC commander in a statement broadcast on state television.

The international community has called for restraint. The United Nations Security Council has scheduled an emergency meeting to discuss the situation. Diplomatic channels remain open, but no ceasefire talks have been publicly announced.

The coming days will be critical. The US is expected to respond to the attacks on its bases and aircraft. Iran has warned of further strikes if its conditions are not met.
