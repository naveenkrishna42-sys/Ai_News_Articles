Farah Khan's Praise for Gaurav Khanna

Farah Khan, the renowned filmmaker and choreographer, has praised Gaurav Khanna, the actor and entrepreneur, after Akanksha Chamola's divorce announcement. Farah Khan, known for her work in Bollywood, has called Gaurav Khanna a rare husband, highlighting his qualities and the impact he has had on Akanksha Chamola's life.

Akanksha Chamola's Responses

Akanksha Chamola, the ex-wife of Gaurav Khanna, has responded to various rumors and claims in the media. She has addressed the pregnancy rumors, stating that she is not pregnant and that she is focused on her career and personal life. Akanksha Chamola has also responded to Orry's claim that she is milking Gaurav Khanna's popularity, calling it a baseless allegation and expressing her gratitude to the audience for their support.

Gaurav Khanna's Parents' Reactions

Gaurav Khanna's parents have reacted to Akanksha Chamola's revelation about her bisexuality. They have expressed their support for Akanksha Chamola and have encouraged her to live her life according to her own choices. Gaurav Khanna's parents have also praised Gaurav Khanna for his understanding and support during this challenging time.
