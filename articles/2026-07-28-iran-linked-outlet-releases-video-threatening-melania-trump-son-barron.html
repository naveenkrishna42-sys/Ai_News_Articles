Iran-Linked Outlet Releases Video Threatening Melania Trump, Son Barron

📅 2026-07-28 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tasnim, an IRGC-linked outlet, shared a video titled 'Where to Kill Melania Trump'.
  • The video also issues threats against Barron Trump, the former president's son.
  • US security agencies are reviewing the threat; no official comment from Iran yet.

Video Threat Targets Former First Lady

A media outlet affiliated with Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) has published a video explicitly calling for harm to Melania Trump, the wife of former US President Donald Trump. The video, shared by Tasnim, is titled 'Where to Kill Melania Trump' and instructs supporters on how to carry out an attack.

The footage does not stop at the former first lady. It also includes a direct warning against Barron Trump, the couple's youngest son. The content has drawn immediate condemnation from US officials and security experts.

Security Concerns Escalate

The Trump family has been under heightened security since Donald Trump left office. This latest threat marks a significant escalation in rhetoric from Iranian state-affiliated media. US Secret Service and the Department of Homeland Security are reviewing the video to assess its credibility and potential operational intent.

No specific plot has been confirmed, but the explicit nature of the call to action has raised alarms. Federal law enforcement agencies routinely monitor such propaganda for signs of real-world planning.

Context of US-Iran Tensions

The threat comes against a backdrop of enduring hostility between Washington and Tehran. The Trump administration's 2020 assassination of Qasem Soleimani, the head of the IRGC's Quds Force, remains a flashpoint. Iranian officials have repeatedly vowed revenge, though they have not claimed direct involvement in this video.

Tasnim is known to operate with the blessing of the IRGC. Analysts say such content is often a mix of psychological warfare and genuine incitement, making it difficult to dismiss entirely.

Reaction from US Officials

Former President Donald Trump has not publicly commented on the video. However, his campaign and family office are understood to have been briefed. Security around Mar-a-Lago, the Trump family residence in Florida, has been stepped up in recent days.

Critics argue that the Biden administration must respond firmly to deter further threats. There is growing pressure on the State Department to designate more Iranian entities as terrorist organizations.

What Happens Next

US intelligence agencies are expected to release a formal assessment of the threat level in the coming days. The UN Security Council may also take up the matter if the video is deemed a violation of international norms on incitement to violence.
