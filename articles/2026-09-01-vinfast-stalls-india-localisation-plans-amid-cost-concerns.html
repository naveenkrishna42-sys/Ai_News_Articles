Vietnamese EV maker pauses India localisation amid cost concerns

VinFast, the Vietnamese electric vehicle (EV) manufacturer, has reportedly paused its plans to localise the assembly of its VF3, VF6, and VF7 models in India. The company has denied claims of a complete production halt, stating that it is instead reassessing costs and supply chain logistics. This move comes as VinFast continues to pursue India-specific EV development and has received approval for Phase 2 of its Thoothukudi plant.

Company denies production halt claims

VinFast has strongly refuted reports of a shutdown in India, emphasizing its commitment to local manufacturing. The company has stated that the VF3, VF6, and VF7 assembly lines will continue to operate, with adjustments being made to improve efficiency and reduce costs. This reassessment is expected to lead to a more competitive product offering in the Indian market.

Broader strategic shifts in India's EV market

The pause in localisation plans reflects broader strategic shifts in India's EV market. As the country's EV industry grows, manufacturers are increasingly focusing on cost optimization and supply chain resilience. VinFast's move is likely driven by the need to align with these evolving market dynamics, ensuring its products remain competitive in a rapidly changing landscape.
