MP's son charged after fatal Aston Martin crash in Hyderabad

📅 2026-08-20 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Aston Martin crash kills woman in Hyderabad
  • MP's son arrested in fatal accident death case
  • Woman identified as Mall employee
  • Police investigation ongoing

Fatal Crash Involving Luxury Car

An Aston Martin, allegedly driven by a speeding vehicle, fatally struck a woman near Inorbit Mall in Hyderabad, Andhra Pradesh. The incident occurred on a busy street, causing widespread panic and immediate police response. The victim, identified as a Mall employee, was pronounced dead at the scene. The car involved in the crash has been impounded for further investigation.

Arrest of MP's Son

Lingamaneni Sanjush, son of a Rajya Sabha Member of Parliament from Andhra Pradesh, has been arrested in connection with the fatal accident. Sanjush is accused of driving the Aston Martin at excessive speeds, leading to the tragic incident. Police have booked him under relevant sections of the Motor Vehicles Act and the Indian Penal Code.

Police Investigation

Police are currently investigating the circumstances surrounding the crash. Eyewitnesses have reported that the Aston Martin was driving at high speeds when it allegedly lost control and struck the woman. The car's speed, as well as the driver's actions, are under scrutiny. The police have not yet confirmed the exact speed of the car or the driver's license status.

Public Reaction

The incident has sparked public outrage and condemnation, with many expressing shock and sadness over the tragic loss of life. Social media platforms are abuzz with discussions and tributes to the victim. The incident has also raised questions about road safety and the need for stricter enforcement of traffic rules.

Next Steps

As the investigation progresses, further details are expected to emerge regarding the circumstances of the crash and the actions of the accused driver. The public will be closely watching the outcome of the legal proceedings and the measures taken to prevent such incidents in the future.
