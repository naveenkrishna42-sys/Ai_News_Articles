Mourinho's Netflix doc: Ferguson succession, Abramovich 'revenge' and more

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mourinho agreed to take over from Sir Alex Ferguson at Manchester United.
  • The documentary details Mourinho's 'revenge' on Roman Abramovich, including ignoring a yacht SOS.
  • Mourinho claims he and his teams were 'being manipulated' during his career.
  • The docuseries has drawn mixed reviews, with some critics questioning his openness.

Jose Mourinho has never been one to shy away from a headline, but his new Netflix documentary has delivered a treasure trove of them. The series, which charts the career of one of football's most polarising figures, has already sparked widespread discussion for its candid revelations. From a secret agreement to succeed Sir Alex Ferguson at Manchester United to a simmering feud with Roman Abramovich, the documentary offers an unprecedented look into the mind of 'The Special One'.

The Ferguson succession plan

Perhaps the most explosive claim in the documentary centres on Mourinho's move to Manchester United. The Portuguese manager reveals that he had agreed to succeed Sir Alex Ferguson at Old Trafford, a plan that was reportedly in place well before the Scot's retirement in 2013. Mourinho suggests the arrangement was a long-standing understanding, though the timing of Ferguson's departure ultimately altered the dynamics.

The revelation adds a new layer to the narrative of United's post-Ferguson struggles. Mourinho eventually took charge in 2016, but by then the club had already gone through David Moyes and Louis van Gaal. The documentary implies that the delay may have impacted the project's success, with Mourinho hinting that the club he eventually inherited was not the one he had originally agreed to take over.

'Revenge' on Roman Abramovich

The documentary also revisits Mourinho's turbulent relationship with former Chelsea owner Roman Abramovich. One of the most talked-about anecdotes involves an incident where Mourinho allegedly ignored a distress call from Abramovich's yacht. The manager frames this as a deliberate act of defiance, a form of quiet 'revenge' for what he perceived as a lack of support during his first spell at Stamford Bridge.

Mourinho does not hold back in describing the dynamics of his time at Chelsea. He suggests that the constant scrutiny and interference from the club's hierarchy took a toll, and that his relationship with Abramovich was far more complex than the public knew. The anecdote has been seized upon by fans and pundits alike as evidence of Mourinho's fiercely independent streak.

'We were being manipulated'

Beyond the specific incidents, the documentary paints a broader picture of Mourinho's career as a battle against unseen forces. He claims that he and his teams were 'being manipulated' at various points, though he stops short of naming specific individuals or institutions. The assertion is classic Mourinho – part siege mentality, part genuine grievance, and wholly compelling.

This narrative thread runs through the series, offering context for some of his most infamous outbursts. Whether it is his touchline antics or his pointed comments at press conferences, Mourinho frames his behaviour as a response to a hostile environment. Critics, however, have questioned whether this framing is entirely accurate, with some suggesting the documentary allows him to rewrite history.

Mixed reviews and a reluctant subject

The documentary has received a mixed critical response. Some have praised it for its candour, while others have noted a certain reluctance on Mourinho's part to fully open up. One review in The Guardian questioned why he agreed to star in the series if he was not willing to be more forthcoming, suggesting that the documentary may raise more questions than it answers.

Despite the reservations, the series has clearly struck a chord with audiences. It has reignited debates about Mourinho's legacy and his place in football history, with fans and pundits dissecting every revelation. The documentary's timing, released as Mourinho continues to manage at the top level, adds an extra layer of intrigue.

As the series continues to generate headlines, one question remains: what will Mourinho do next? The documentary hints at unfinished business, and with his career far from over, it seems the story of 'The Special One' is still being written.
