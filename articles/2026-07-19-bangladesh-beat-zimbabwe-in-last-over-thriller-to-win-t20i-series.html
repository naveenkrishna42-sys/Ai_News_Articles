Bangladesh beat Zimbabwe in last-over thriller to win T20I series

  • Bangladesh won the third T20I against Zimbabwe in a last-over thriller to take the series 2-1.
  • Tanzid Hasan remained unbeaten on 66 to guide the chase under pressure.
  • The series win provides a morale boost after a forgettable tour overall.

Bangladesh ended their difficult tour of Zimbabwe on a high note, winning the third T20I in a dramatic last-over finish to clinch the series 2-1. The visitors held their nerve in a tense chase, with opener Tanzid Hasan anchoring the innings with an unbeaten 66.

Nail-biting finish in Harare

Chasing a competitive target set by Zimbabwe, Bangladesh needed a steady hand. Tanzid provided exactly that. He paced his innings brilliantly, rotating strike and finding the boundary when needed.

The match went down to the final over. Bangladesh needed a handful of runs with wickets in hand. The crowd in Harare was on its feet as the equation came down to the last few balls.

The Bangladeshi camp erupted when the winning runs were scored. It was a fitting end to a contest that had swung one way and then the other.

Tanzid Hasan's crucial knock

The left-handed opener was the star of the show. His 66 not out came at a brisk pace and under immense pressure. He didn't panic when wickets fell around him, keeping his focus on the target.

Youngster Tanzid has shown promise in the past. This innings, however, will be remembered for its composure in a series-deciding game. He was well-supported by the lower middle order, who chipped in with useful runs.

Series win masks tour struggles

This T20I series victory is a silver lining on an otherwise forgettable tour for Bangladesh. The visitors had struggled in the preceding Test and ODI legs of the itinerary.

Bowling and batting inconsistencies had plagued the team throughout. Questions were being raised about the squad's form and morale. This win, however, provides a much-needed boost heading home.

The team management will hope this momentum can be carried forward into future assignments. Winning a series, even against Zimbabwe, is never easy in away conditions.

What Zimbabwe will rue

Zimbabwe will look back at this match with some regret. They had Bangladesh on the ropes at various points but could not deliver the final blow. Fielding lapses and a wayward final over cost them dear.

The home side's batting, however, showed significant improvement compared to earlier matches in the series. They posted a total that should have been defendable. But their bowlers could not contain Tanzid when it mattered most.

For Zimbabwe, the search for consistency continues. They have shown flashes of brilliance but have not been able to string together complete performances.

Series summary and takeaways

Bangladesh won the three-match T20I series 2-1. After losing the first game, they bounced back strongly to win the next two. The turnaround shows character in the squad.

The focus now shifts back home for both teams. Bangladesh will look to build on this win. Zimbabwe will go back to the drawing board to address their shortcomings.

This victory might not erase the memories of a tough tour, but it offers a positive ending. The players can return with some pride and a trophy in hand.
