What reviewers generally say: Generally well-regarded for class-leading active noise cancellation and rich sound profile, though criticized for the bulky charging case and lack of water resistance.

Pros
  • Exceptional active noise cancellation performance for the segment
  • High-fidelity sound reproduction with QN1e processor
  • Stable connectivity with reliable touch controls
Cons
  • Charging case is significantly bulkier than modern alternatives
  • Lacks an official IP rating for water or sweat resistance
  • No support for LDAC or multi-point pairing

The Sony WF-1000XM3 is a compelling choice for users who prioritize sound quality and noise isolation above all else. If you are a commuter or frequent traveler who needs to block out ambient noise, these remain highly effective.

However, skip these if you need gym-ready earbuds, as the lack of water resistance is a major drawback. Additionally, if you prioritize portability, the large footprint of the charging case may feel outdated compared to newer, more compact TWS options currently on the market.
