Hyderabad court sentences man to 10 years for raping minor

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Man convicted for raping a 12-year-old girl in Hyderabad
  • Court sentenced him to 10 years of rigorous imprisonment
  • Case was heard under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act

Verdict Delivered

A Hyderabad court on Friday sentenced a man to 10 years of rigorous imprisonment for raping a 12-year-old girl. The conviction was pronounced after a fast-track trial, with the court also imposing a fine on the convict.

The identity of the accused has not been disclosed by the authorities. Officials confirmed that the case was registered under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, which mandates stringent punishment for sexual offences against minors.

Details of the Case

According to the prosecution, the incident came to light after the victim's family filed a complaint. The girl was a minor at the time of the offence, and the trial was conducted in a special court designated for POCSO cases.

The court found the accused guilty based on the evidence presented, including the victim's testimony. The quantum of punishment was announced after hearing arguments from both the prosecution and the defence.

Legal Proceedings and Sentencing

The judge, while pronouncing the sentence, observed that crimes against children are of a grave nature and warrant strict action. The 10-year rigorous imprisonment term is in line with the minimum punishment prescribed under the POCSO Act for such offences.

In addition to the jail term, the court also directed the convict to pay a fine. The amount of the fine has not been made public. The trial was completed within a relatively short period, reflecting the judiciary's focus on speedy disposal of cases involving minors.

Response and Next Steps

Legal experts say the verdict sends a strong signal that offences against children will not be tolerated. The victim's family has expressed satisfaction with the court's decision.

The convict has the option to appeal against the judgment in a higher court. Meanwhile, child rights activists have urged the authorities to ensure that the victim receives proper counselling and support to recover from the trauma.

As the case concludes, the focus now shifts to the implementation of the sentence and the rehabilitation of the victim. The police have stated that they will continue to work on similar cases to ensure swift justice for minors.
