Chennai's old-world transport jolts commuters

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Traditional transport methods persist in Chennai
  • Carts drawn by oxen and horses are a common sight
  • Modern alternatives are available but not widely used

Traditional transport persists in Chennai

Despite the city's rapid modernization, carts drawn by oxen and horses are still a common sight in Chennai. Commuters can often be seen jolted about on these traditional vehicles, which travel along the city's streets and bylanes.

Why traditional transport remains

Several factors contribute to the continued use of these traditional carts. For one, they are a cost-effective mode of transport for many residents, especially in areas where modern infrastructure is lacking. Additionally, some commuters prefer the experience of riding in these carts, finding it nostalgic or simply more enjoyable than other options.

Modern alternatives exist but are not widely used

While Chennai has seen significant improvements in its transport infrastructure, traditional carts still hold a significant place in the city's transportation landscape. Public transport options such as buses and metro trains are available, but they may not be accessible to everyone, particularly in rural or less developed areas.

Challenges and concerns

The use of traditional carts also poses certain challenges and concerns. Safety is a major issue, as these vehicles are not equipped with modern safety features. Additionally, the use of traditional carts can contribute to environmental degradation, as they rely on animal power and may produce more emissions than modern vehicles.

Looking ahead

As Chennai continues to grow and develop, it is likely that the use of traditional carts will decline. However, these vehicles will likely remain a part of the city's cultural heritage for some time to come.
