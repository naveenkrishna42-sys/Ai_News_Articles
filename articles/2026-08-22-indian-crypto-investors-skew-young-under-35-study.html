Indian crypto investors skew young, under 35: study

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • 75% of Indian crypto investors are under 35
  • Younger investors show higher interest in crypto
  • CoinSwitch study highlights demographic trends in crypto investment

Younger investors dominate Indian crypto market

Nearly three-quarters of Indian crypto investors are under 35, according to a recent study by CoinSwitch. The report suggests that younger investors are more inclined towards cryptocurrencies, reflecting a broader trend in the country's financial landscape.

Key findings from the CoinSwitch study

The study, which surveyed a large sample of Indian crypto investors, found that the majority of participants were aged between 18 and 34. This demographic group showed a higher interest in cryptocurrencies, with many citing factors such as financial freedom, investment opportunities, and the potential for high returns.

Interestingly, the study also revealed that younger investors were more likely to be familiar with blockchain technology and its underlying principles. This suggests that education and awareness play a significant role in shaping the crypto investment landscape in India.

Demographic trends in crypto investment

The findings of the CoinSwitch study align with broader demographic trends in the Indian crypto market. Younger investors are often more tech-savvy and open to new financial products, making them a key target for crypto platforms and exchanges.

However, the study also highlighted some challenges faced by younger investors, such as a lack of regulatory clarity and the potential for scams and fraud. These issues are crucial considerations as the crypto market continues to evolve and grow in India.

What does this mean for the future of crypto in India?

The dominance of younger investors in the Indian crypto market suggests that the sector is likely to continue growing and evolving. As this demographic group becomes more financially literate and confident, they are likely to drive further adoption and innovation in the crypto space.

However, it is important for regulators and industry players to address the challenges and concerns raised by the study. By doing so, they can help ensure that the crypto market in India continues to thrive and benefit all stakeholders.
