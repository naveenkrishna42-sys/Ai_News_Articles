Akshay Oberoi's ₹3 Crore Farmhouse: A Sanctuary from Mumbai's Chaos

Akshay Oberoi, known for his roles in Bollywood, has recently acquired a ₹3 crore farmhouse in Karjat, Maharashtra. The property, designed to be a sanctuary away from the chaos of Mumbai, has been making waves in the entertainment industry. The farmhouse, which took 9 months to design, is a testament to Oberoi's love for nature and peace.

Stunning Interiors and Lush Views

The farmhouse features stunning interiors that blend modern design with natural elements. The interiors are designed to provide a comfortable and cosy atmosphere, making it a perfect retreat for Oberoi. The farmhouse is surrounded by lush greenery, offering breathtaking views that provide a sense of tranquillity.

Cosy Spaces and Modern Amenities

The farmhouse includes various cosy spaces, such as a living room, dining area, and bedroom, all designed to provide a comfortable stay. The modern amenities, including a swimming pool and a gym, add to the overall appeal of the property. The farmhouse is also equipped with advanced security systems to ensure the safety of the residents.
