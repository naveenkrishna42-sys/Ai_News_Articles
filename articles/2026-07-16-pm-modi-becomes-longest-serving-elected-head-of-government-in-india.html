PM Modi Becomes Longest-Serving Elected Head of Government in India

📅 2026-07-16 📂 Politics Original source ↗
Key points
  • PM Modi surpasses Manmohan Singh's tenure record of 10 years, 4 months, 17 days.
  • He is now the longest-serving elected head of government in India's history.
  • The milestone was achieved on July 16, 2026.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has crossed a historic milestone. On Thursday, July 16, 2026, he became the longest-serving elected head of government in India's history.

He surpassed the previous record held by Dr. Manmohan Singh, who served as Prime Minister from 2004 to 2014. Dr. Singh's tenure lasted 10 years, 4 months, and 17 days.

Record-Breaking Tenure

PM Modi first took oath as Prime Minister on May 26, 2014. His second term began on May 30, 2019. The third term started on June 9, 2024.

By crossing Dr. Singh's mark, PM Modi has now served longer than any other elected head of government since independence. The milestone was noted by officials at the Prime Minister's Office.

Context of the Achievement

The record applies specifically to elected heads of government. India's longest-serving Prime Minister overall remains Jawaharlal Nehru, who served for 16 years and 286 days from 1947 to 1964. However, Nehru's tenure began before the first general elections in 1952.

PM Modi's record is based on continuous service after being elected by the Lok Sabha. His tenure includes the abrogation of Article 370 in 2019, the COVID-19 pandemic response, and the launch of several flagship schemes.

Political Reactions

Leaders from the Bharatiya Janata Party celebrated the achievement. Party spokespersons called it a testament to public trust in the Prime Minister's leadership.

Opposition leaders have not yet made official statements. Analysts note that the milestone comes amid ongoing debates over economic performance and democratic freedoms.

What the Record Means

Experts point out that longevity alone does not define success. But the record underscores PM Modi's electoral dominance since 2014. He led the BJP to two consecutive majorities in 2014 and 2019, and a coalition victory in 2024.

His government has focused on infrastructure, digital governance, and national security. Critics argue that his tenure has also seen a rise in polarisation and challenges to institutional independence.

PM Modi's office has not issued a formal statement on the milestone. However, social media was abuzz with posts from supporters and political analysts marking the date.

Looking Ahead

The Prime Minister's current term runs until 2029. If the government completes its full term, PM Modi will further extend his record. The next general election is scheduled for 2029.

For now, the milestone places PM Modi in a unique spot in India's political history. His government is expected to continue its focus on development, with an eye on the next electoral test.
