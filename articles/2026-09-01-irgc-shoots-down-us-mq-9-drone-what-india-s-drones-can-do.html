Iran's IRGC downs US MQ-9 drone

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) has shot down a US MQ-9 Reaper drone over the Strait of Hormuz. The incident occurred moments ago, with the drone crashing into the clear waters of the Persian Gulf. This is not the first time the US has lost a drone in the region, but it is a significant escalation.

India's MQ-9 drones: What can they do?

India operates MQ-9 drones, but they have different capabilities compared to the US MQ-9 Reaper. The Indian drones are primarily used for intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) missions. They are not equipped with the same advanced weapons as the US drones.
