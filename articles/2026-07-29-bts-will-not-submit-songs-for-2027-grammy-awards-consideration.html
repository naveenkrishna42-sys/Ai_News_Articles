BTS Will Not Submit Songs for 2027 Grammy Awards Consideration

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • BTS will not submit any songs for the 2027 Grammy Awards.
  • The decision comes after the Recording Academy introduced a new 'Best Asian Pop Performance' category.
  • BTS's 'ARIRANG' album project will not be entered for consideration.

BTS Opts Out of 2027 Grammy Race

BTS has confirmed they will not submit any songs for consideration at the 2027 Grammy Awards. This includes tracks from their widely anticipated 'ARIRANG' album project.

The decision was reported by multiple outlets, including Billboard and Variety, on Wednesday. It marks the first time the group has collectively stepped back from Grammy contention since their rise to global prominence.

New Category Sparks Controversy

The move comes a month after the Recording Academy announced a new award: 'Best Asian Pop Performance.' Industry observers saw this as a direct response to the growing influence of K-pop and other Asian genres.

Critics argued the category could ghettoise Asian artists. BTS's decision appears to be a pointed rejection of this framing. The group has not issued a formal statement, but sources close to the band indicate they view the category as reductive.

ARIRANG Project Shelved From Grammys

The 'ARIRANG' project, a deeply personal album weaving traditional Korean folk elements with modern pop, was seen as a strong contender. Fans had expected the group to push for nominations in major categories like Album of the Year or Best Pop Duo/Group Performance.

By withholding the project, BTS is making a clear statement. They are refusing to be funnelled into a single, newly created category. The move echoes past criticisms by artists who feel genre-specific awards limit recognition.

Industry insiders say the decision could have a ripple effect. Other major K-pop acts may follow suit. The Recording Academy has not yet responded to the news.

What This Means for the Grammys

The 2027 Grammys will now proceed without the biggest musical act of the decade. BTS's absence from the submission pool is a significant blow to the awards show's viewership and global relevance.

It also raises questions about the Academy's strategy. Introducing a category meant to include Asian pop has instead led to its biggest star walking away. The Academy may face pressure to reconsider how it categorises international music.

For now, BTS fans are left speculating about the group's next move. A world tour or independent award show could be on the horizon. What is clear is that BTS is charting its own path, far from the Recording Academy's definitions.
