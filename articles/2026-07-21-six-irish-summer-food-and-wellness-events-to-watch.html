Six Irish Summer Food and Wellness Events to Watch

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Lifestyle Original source ↗
Key points
  • Six events listed include a farmers market, a wellness retreat, and a food festival.
  • Events span from June to August across various Irish locations.
  • Focus on local produce, mindfulness, and outdoor activities.

Summer Lineup of Irish Food and Wellness Events

Ireland’s summer calendar is filling up with events that blend food, wellness, and outdoor living. From farm-to-table markets to yoga retreats, these six gatherings offer something for everyone looking to recharge and eat well.

The events, reported by The Gloss Magazine, take place between June and August. They highlight local produce, mindfulness practices, and community connections.

1. The Cork Summer Farmers Market

Held every Saturday in June, this market in Cork city centre features over 50 vendors. You’ll find fresh vegetables, artisan cheeses, and baked goods.

Local chefs also host cooking demonstrations. The market runs from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. and is free to enter.

2. West Coast Wellness Retreat

This three-day retreat in County Clare combines yoga, meditation, and plant-based meals. Organisers say the focus is on “slow living and mindful eating.”

Accommodation is in eco-friendly cabins. The retreat costs €400 per person, including all meals and sessions.

3. Galway Food Festival

Galway’s annual food festival returns in July with a focus on seafood. The event includes oyster shucking competitions and a seafood trail along the coast.

There’s also a “Meet the Producer” tent where visitors can talk to local farmers and fishermen.

4. Dublin Urban Garden Tour

On a Saturday in August, Dublin opens several community gardens to the public. The tour includes rooftop gardens, school plots, and a permaculture site.

Volunteers offer workshops on composting and growing herbs. The tour is self-guided and free.

5. Sligo Sound Bath and Brunch

A Sunday morning event in Sligo combines a sound bath session with a healthy brunch. The sound bath uses singing bowls and gongs to promote relaxation.

Brunch includes smoothie bowls, avocado toast, and locally roasted coffee. Tickets are €35.

6. Kerry Foraging Walk

In August, a foraging expert leads a walk along the Kerry coastline. Participants learn to identify wild herbs, seaweed, and edible mushrooms.

The walk ends with a tasting session. Organisers advise wearing sturdy shoes and bringing a basket.

What to Expect

Most events are outdoor, so weather may affect schedules. Organisers recommend booking in advance as spaces are limited.

Some events, like the foraging walk, have age restrictions for safety. Check individual websites for details.

These events reflect a growing interest in sustainable eating and wellness in Ireland. Whether you’re a local or a visitor, they offer a chance to explore the country’s food culture.

More events may be added as summer progresses. Keep an eye on local listings for updates.
