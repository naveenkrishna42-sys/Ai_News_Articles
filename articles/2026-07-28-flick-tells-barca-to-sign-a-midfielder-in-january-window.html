Flick tells Barca to sign a midfielder in January window

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Manager Hansi Flick has requested a midfield signing in January.
  • The request comes amid concerns over squad depth in midfield.
  • Barcelona's board is reportedly evaluating targets for the winter window.

Barcelona manager Hansi Flick has formally asked the club's hierarchy to reinforce the midfield in the upcoming January transfer window, according to a report from Barca Universal. The German coach believes the current squad lacks sufficient depth in the centre of the park to compete on multiple fronts this season.

Flick's request signals growing unease in the technical staff about the team's ability to maintain intensity in La Liga and the Champions League. While the club's attack and defence have drawn praise, the midfield has been a persistent area of concern since the start of the campaign.

Why the midfield needs fresh legs

Barcelona's midfield options have thinned out due to a combination of injuries and inconsistent form. Key players have struggled to stay fit, leaving Flick with limited rotation choices in high-stakes matches.

The manager is said to be looking for a player who can slot into the starting eleven immediately rather than a developmental prospect. The club's scouting team has already begun drawing up a shortlist of affordable targets ahead of the winter window.

Financial constraints remain a hurdle

Barcelona's well-documented financial restrictions continue to shape their transfer strategy. Even with Flick's request, the board will likely need to offload players or structure a deal creatively to comply with La Liga's salary cap rules.

Loan moves with an option to buy are being considered as a viable route. The club has used such mechanisms in recent windows to bring in talent without triggering long-term financial commitments.

Flick has held multiple meetings with sporting director Deco in recent weeks to discuss the squad's needs. Sources close to the club indicate that the midfield position is now the top priority for January.

Flick's track record with mid-season signings

The 60-year-old manager has a history of pushing for winter reinforcements when he spots a weakness. During his time at Bayern Munich, he successfully integrated mid-season arrivals who made an immediate impact.

At Barcelona, Flick has so far relied on the existing squad but has grown concerned about fatigue as the season progresses. The team faces a congested fixture list in the second half of the campaign, and the manager wants more reliable options off the bench.

No specific names have been confirmed yet, though several midfielders across Europe are being monitored. The club is expected to move quickly once the window opens to avoid a late scramble.

What happens next

Barcelona's board will now assess the market and present Flick with a list of feasible targets before the end of the year. The coming weeks will determine whether the club can grant the manager's wish or if financial realities force a compromise.
