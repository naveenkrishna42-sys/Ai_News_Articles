Venezuela's 100-year oil concessions: A gamble with U.S. energy security

Venezuela's interim President has announced that the U.S. energy deal will last 25 years, but the country has granted U.S.-backed oil firm NABEP 100-year concessions for 17 oil fields. This discrepancy raises questions about the sustainability of the deal and its impact on U.S. energy security.

Trump's 'cheap gas miracle': A bold claim, but is it feasible?

Donald Trump's claim of a 'cheap gas miracle' via the Venezuela oil deal is a bold one. However, experts are skeptical about the feasibility of such a claim, given the long-term concessions granted to NABEP and the potential impact on Venezuela's oil industry.

Firms including Chevron, ONGC, GE Vernova on track to sign final pacts in Venezuela

Several firms, including Chevron, ONGC, and GE Vernova, are on track to sign final pacts in Venezuela. This move is seen as a significant step towards securing U.S. energy interests in the country, but it also raises questions about the potential impact on Venezuela's oil industry and the broader energy market.

Alejandro Betancourt: The man who would be Trump's 'viceroy' in Venezuela

Alejandro Betancourt, a key figure in the U.S. energy deal, is being referred to as Trump's 'viceroy' in Venezuela. His role in the deal is significant, but his background and experience raise questions about his ability to navigate the complex political landscape of Venezuela.

The broader impact of the Venezuela oil deal

The Venezuela oil deal has significant implications for the broader energy market. The long-term concessions granted to NABEP and the potential impact on Venezuela's oil industry are likely to have a significant impact on global oil prices and energy security.
