Former Cricket Captains Appeal for Imran Khan's Release

In a significant development, former cricket captains Javed Miandad and Pat Cummins have publicly appealed for the release of Imran Khan from detention. Miandad, who is known for his emotional appeals, broke down during an interview, emphasizing that Imran Khan is a national hero. Cummins has also supported the plea, highlighting the importance of Imran Khan's medical treatment.

Why This Matters

The appeal by Miandad and Cummins comes at a time when Imran Khan is facing serious health issues. The former captains have urged the Pakistani government to ensure that Imran Khan receives the necessary medical treatment and is released from detention. This appeal is significant as it brings attention to the plight of a national hero who is currently in a vulnerable position.
