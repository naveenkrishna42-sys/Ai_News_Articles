Search-and-Rescue Operation Underway

Search efforts resumed on Saturday for the 22 crew members of a Panama-flagged cargo ship that sank in the Bay of Bengal off the coast of Odisha. The Indian Coast Guard (ICG) has deployed three ships to assist in the search-and-rescue operation.

Vessel Details

The Singapore-bound cargo ship, with a crew of 24, sank in the early hours of Saturday. The exact cause of the sinking is yet to be determined. Officials have not confirmed any survivors or casualties.

Coast Guard Deployment

The ICG has mobilized its resources to locate the missing vessel and crew. Three ships have been deployed to assist in the search operation. The search area is expected to be vast, given the depth of the sea and the size of the vessel.

International Response

Panamanian authorities have been alerted to the incident. The Panama Maritime Authority has been informed about the sinking and is likely to play a role in the search-and-rescue operation. The International Maritime Organization (IMO) has also been notified.

Next Steps

The search operation is expected to continue for several days. The weather conditions in the Bay of Bengal are likely to impact the search efforts. The Coast Guard and other agencies are working closely with each other to ensure that all possible measures are taken to locate the missing crew members.
