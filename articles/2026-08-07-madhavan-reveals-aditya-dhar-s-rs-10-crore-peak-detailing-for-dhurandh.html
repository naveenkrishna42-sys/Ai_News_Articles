Madhavan reveals Aditya Dhar's Rs 10 crore 'peak detailing' for Dhurandhar

  • He praised Aditya Dhar's obsessive perfectionism

R Madhavan has opened up about the extraordinary lengths director Aditya Dhar went to for his upcoming film Dhurandhar. In a recent interview, the actor revealed that Dhar spent a staggering Rs 10 crore extra on what he called 'peak detailing' — a figure that left even Madhavan stunned.

“He is insane,” Madhavan said, describing the director's perfectionism. The actor's candid remarks have sparked curiosity about the scale of the production and what audiences can expect from the film.

The Rs 10 crore question

Madhavan disclosed that the additional expenditure was not for new sequences or star cast, but purely for enhancing the minutiae of the film — from set design to visual texture. “Before the release, I would have questioned, who is going to watch?” he admitted, reflecting on his initial skepticism about the investment.

The actor's comments suggest that Dhar's obsession with detail is both a creative strength and a financial gamble. While such spending is rare in Indian cinema, it underscores the director's reputation for meticulous filmmaking.

A director known for precision

Aditya Dhar, who rose to fame with the 2019 blockbuster Uri: The Surgical Strike, has built a name for himself as a filmmaker who leaves no stone unturned. His previous work was praised for its technical polish, and Dhurandhar seems to be an extension of that ethos.

Madhavan's revelation offers a rare glimpse into Dhar's working style. The actor's tone was a mix of admiration and disbelief, suggesting that the director's methods are as intense as they are effective.

What this means for the film

With Rs 10 crore spent purely on detailing, Dhurandhar is shaping up to be a visually ambitious project. While the exact nature of these details remains under wraps, industry insiders speculate that the film could feature elaborate period sets or high-end VFX work.

Madhavan's comments also hint at the film's scale. “He spent it without flinching,” the actor noted, adding that Dhar's conviction in the project was unwavering.

Fans are now eagerly awaiting the first trailer to see if the investment pays off. The film's production team has remained tight-lipped about the plot, but the buzz around the budget is already generating significant interest.

The road ahead

As Dhurandhar gears up for its release, all eyes will be on whether the detailing translates into box office success. Madhavan's revelation has set the stage for a high-stakes premiere, with audiences curious to judge if Dhar's 'insane' perfectionism was worth every rupee.
