Bombay High Court Mandates CBI Probe into Disha Salian's Death; None Treated as Accused Yet

📅 2026-09-02 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bombay High Court orders CBI probe
  • No one to be treated as accused yet
  • Petitioner alleges political pressure

CBI Probe into Disha Salian's Death

The Bombay High Court, in a significant development, has directed the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) to probe the death of Disha Salian, former celebrity manager and friend of late actor Sushant Singh Rajput. The court, led by Justice S S Shinde, issued this order on September 1, 2026, stating that no one should be treated as an accused without sufficient evidence.

Petitioner's Allegations

Disha's father, Satish Salian, had approached the court alleging that there was political pressure to shield certain individuals involved in the case. He contended that the Mumbai Police, NCB, and the CBI had failed to conduct a fair investigation.

Court's Observations

The court noted that the investigating agencies had not conducted a fair and thorough probe. It observed that the case involved serious allegations and hence, required a fair, impartial, and independent investigation.

Key Facts and Analysis

  • Pros of CBI Probe: The CBI, with its national jurisdiction and experience in handling high-profile cases, can ensure a fair, unbiased, and thorough investigation.
  • Cons of CBI Probe: The CBI's reputation has been marred by controversies. There are concerns that the case may get politicized, with the central agency intervening.
  • The court's directive that no one should be treated as accused without sufficient evidence is significant. It ensures that the investigation remains fair, protecting both the accused and the victim's family from media trials and political pressures.
  • The case has been closely watched due to its connection to the Sushant Singh Rajput case and the alleged involvement of prominent personalities. The court's decision is a step towards ensuring justice and transparency.

The impact of this decision will be keenly watched. It could set a precedent for how high-profile cases are investigated and handled by the judicial system. The court's emphasis on fairness and impartiality is a crucial step towards ensuring that justice prevails.
