Indus Tower, Tata Chemicals Q1 Results Lead Market Buzz

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Indus Tower Q1 net profit up 18% YoY, revenue at Rs 8,200 crore.
  • Tata Chemicals Q1 net profit down 12%, revenue flat.
  • Brokerages remain bullish on Indus Tower, cautious on Tata Chemicals.

Indus Tower Beats Estimates on Strong Tenancy Growth

Indus Tower reported its Q1 FY27 results today, posting a consolidated net profit of Rs 1,850 crore, up 18% year-on-year. Revenue from operations stood at Rs 8,200 crore, a 14% increase from the same quarter last year.

The company attributed the growth to a 7% rise in tower tenancies and steady rental income. EBITDA margin improved to 52.3%, driven by operating leverage and cost control measures.

Analysts had expected a net profit of around Rs 1,700 crore. The beat was largely on account of higher-than-anticipated tenancy additions in rural markets.

Tata Chemicals Faces Margin Squeeze

Tata Chemicals reported a mixed set of numbers. Net profit fell 12% to Rs 520 crore, while revenue remained almost flat at Rs 4,100 crore compared to the year-ago period.

The company's soda ash division saw stable demand, but lower realisations in the US and European markets weighed on margins. EBITDA margin contracted to 18.5% from 21.2% last year.

Management highlighted that input costs remain elevated, particularly for energy and raw materials. The domestic fertiliser business, however, performed better on the back of a good monsoon.

Market Movers: What Drove the Buzz

Shares of Indus Tower rose 3.5% in early trade following the results, before closing 2.1% higher. Tata Chemicals stock slipped 1.8% as investors digested the margin decline.

Other buzzing stocks today included Reliance Industries, which gained on news of a new retail partnership, and HDFC Bank, which was flat ahead of its board meeting on fund-raising plans.

Brokerages maintained a 'buy' rating on Indus Tower, citing strong cash flows and a healthy order pipeline. For Tata Chemicals, most analysts have a 'hold' stance, waiting for a recovery in global soda ash prices.

The broader market was mixed, with the Nifty 50 closing 0.2% lower and the BSE Sensex down 0.1%. IT and pharma stocks saw profit-booking after recent gains.

What to Watch Next

All eyes now turn to the RBI's monetary policy decision next week, which could set the tone for rate-sensitive sectors. Indus Tower's capex plans and Tata Chemicals' cost reduction measures will be closely tracked by investors in the coming quarters.
