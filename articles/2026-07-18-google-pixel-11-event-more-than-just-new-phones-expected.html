Google Pixel 11 event: More than just new phones expected

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Google has released its first Pixel 11 teaser video featuring 'Pixel Glow'.
  • Leaked Amazon listings have revealed US pricing and full specifications for the Pixel 11 series.
  • Renders show the Pixel 11 in three new colours, sparking design speculation.
  • Industry watchers hope the event will include surprises beyond the phones, such as new wearables or AI features.

Google's Pixel 11 launch event is just around the corner, and the rumour mill is running at full speed. The company has already released its first official teaser video, showing off what it calls 'Pixel Glow' — a new lighting effect that will likely feature on the upcoming devices.

But as a flurry of leaks and listings hit the internet, many are hoping Google has more in store than just a new smartphone. With competitors pushing boundaries in foldables and AI, the bar for this year's Pixel event is higher than ever.

Pixel 11 leaks: Pricing, specs, and colours

Leaked Amazon listings have spilled the beans on US pricing and full specs for the Pixel 11 series. According to reports, the lineup will include multiple models with varying storage and RAM configurations. Displays and battery sizes have also been detailed, though Google has not officially confirmed any of these numbers.

New renders from GSMArena show the Pixel 11 in three colours, giving us a clear look at the design language Google is going for this year. The images suggest a refined aesthetic, though not a radical departure from the Pixel 10.

Android Police notes that while the leaks are comprehensive, they don't capture the full picture. 'More deleted Pixel 11 Amazon listings reveal full US pricing and specs,' the outlet reported, hinting that retailers are jumping the gun.

Teasers and the 'Pixel Glow'

Google's own teaser video has generated buzz for its 'Pixel Glow' feature. The short clip shows light emanating from the device, possibly signalling a new notification LED or an always-on display trick. 9to5Google called the teaser a 'first look' at what Google plans to highlight at the event.

But the question remains: will the glowing effect be more than just eye candy? Some speculate it could tie into Google's broader AI ambitions, perhaps as a visual cue for Assistant or new health features.

Will Google surprise us?

The headline from Android Police — 'I hope Google's Pixel 11 event surprises us with more than just new phones' — captures a growing sentiment. Pixel launches have become predictable: a new phone, better camera, and the latest Android version. This year, fans want more.

Rumours of a Pixel Watch 3 or a next-gen Pixel Tablet have not been confirmed. Nor has any major AI-powered feature that goes beyond what Gemini already offers. Google has been tight-lipped, and the teaser didn't reveal much beyond the glow.

Droid Life's spec breakdown suggests the hardware is solid, but in a market where Samsung and Apple are pushing foldables and spatial computing, hardware alone may not be enough.

What to watch: The event is expected to take place in the coming weeks. If Google unveils a surprise product — be it a new wearable, a foldable Pixel, or a bold AI feature — it could shift the narrative. If not, the Pixel 11 may just be another very good phone in a sea of very good phones. All eyes are on the stage.
