A Third Instalment That Plays It Safer

Lust Stories 3 arrives on Netflix with four new short films, continuing the anthology format that began in 2018. The franchise built its reputation on frank, adult storytelling from some of India's most respected directors. This time, the tone shifts noticeably toward comedy and light mischief rather than raw provocation.

The film is available only on Netflix, so there is no theatrical ticket to buy. Your real cost is the monthly subscription, which ranges from the mobile-only tier to the premium 4K plan. For viewers already subscribed, the marginal cost of watching is effectively zero.

Segment-by-Segment Breakdown

1. Vishal Bhardwaj's Chapter

Bhardwaj anchors the anthology with the most literary segment. Reporting confirms the story draws from a Bashir Badr couplet and Anais Nin's erotica, and Bhardwaj has spoken about the challenge of filming desire without objectifying his lead. The result is a layered, dialogue-heavy short that rewards patient viewers.

2. Konkona Sen Sharma's Chapter

Sen Sharma returns to the franchise with a segment that trades intensity for warmth. Her writing leans on everyday domestic tension, a strength she has shown across her acting and directing work.

3. Neha Sharma's Chapter

This segment brings generational conflict into the bedroom-adjacent conversation, using humour to defuse awkwardness. It is the lightest chapter of the four.

4. The Fourth Segment

The remaining short rounds out the anthology with a comedic framing, reinforcing the collection's overall softer register compared with the first two films.

Real-World Viewing Highlights

  • Bhardwaj's segment is the standout for viewers who value writing over shock value.
  • The comedic tilt makes this the most family-adjacent chapter of the franchise, though it remains adult content.
  • Four short films mean flexible viewing; you can watch one segment per sitting.
  • No theatrical release means no travel or ticket spend, only your existing subscription.
  • Fans of the earlier films may find the reduced intensity a letdown.

Is It Worth Your Subscription Time?

For Netflix subscribers, Lust Stories 3 offers roughly two hours of varied adult storytelling at no extra charge. The anthology format works in its favour: uneven segments still deliver at least one strong chapter. If you value director-driven storytelling and can accept a gentler, funnier register, this third outing justifies an evening. If you expect the sting of the original, temper that expectation.
