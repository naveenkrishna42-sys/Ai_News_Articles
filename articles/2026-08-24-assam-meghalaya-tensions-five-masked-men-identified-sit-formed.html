Five masked persons linked to Assam-Meghalaya tensions identified

Recent developments in the Assam-Meghalaya tensions have seen the identification of five masked persons linked to the incident. The Special Investigation Team (SIT) has been formed to probe the matter further. This move comes as a response to the ongoing protests and tensions between the two states.

Meghalaya taxi bodies call off protests after government talks

The Meghalaya taxi bodies have called off their protests after holding talks with the Meghalaya government. The government has assured the taxi operators of steps towards a lasting solution. This development has brought some relief to the situation, but the underlying issues remain unresolved.

Different resolution timelines emerge after Aug-23 talks

After the Aug-23 talks, different resolution timelines have emerged. This has added to the complexity of the situation and has raised questions about the effectiveness of the ongoing negotiations. The lack of a clear resolution timeline has left the issue hanging in the balance.

Meghalaya government holds talks with protesting taxi operators, assures lasting solution

The Meghalaya government has held talks with the protesting taxi operators and has assured them of a lasting solution. This move has been seen as a positive step towards resolving the issue. However, the specifics of the solution have not been disclosed, leaving the taxi operators in a state of uncertainty.
