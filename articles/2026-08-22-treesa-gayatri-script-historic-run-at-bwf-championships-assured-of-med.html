Treesa-Gayatri script historic run at BWF Championships, assured of medal

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Treesa Jolly and Gayatri Gopichand reach BWF World Championships semifinals
  • Pair becomes first Indian women's doubles team to guarantee a medal at the championships
  • Gayatri Gopichand is daughter of former chief national coach Pullela Gopichand
  • Historic achievement comes after an emotional moment during their quarterfinal victory
  • Coach B Sumeeth Reddy had earlier spoken about the pressure of the Gopichand legacy

Historic semifinal finish for Indian pair

Treesa Jolly and Gayatri Gopichand have become the first Indian women's doubles pair to guarantee a medal at the BWF World Championships. The duo secured their place in the semifinals with a commanding performance, etching their names in Indian badminton history.

The achievement marks a significant milestone for Indian badminton, a sport that has seen tremendous growth thanks to the efforts of coaches and players over the past two decades.

Emotional quarterfinal triumph

The moment of victory brought raw emotions to the fore. As the final shuttlecock landed, Treesa was overcome with feeling, tears streaming down her face. It was her partner Gayatri who stepped forward to comfort the emotional Treesa, with the two sharing a warm embrace on court.

The gesture captured the bond between the two players, who have worked together to reach this pinnacle of the sport. Their complementary styles and mutual support have been evident throughout the tournament.

Carrying the Gopichand legacy

Gayatri Gopichand brings a famous surname to the partnership. She is the daughter of Pullela Gopichand, the former chief national coach whose pedigree has shaped countless champions in Indian badminton. The weight of that legacy has been a topic of discussion leading up to the championships.

Coach B Sumeeth Reddy had earlier addressed the challenges of representing such a distinguished family name in international competition. "Being Pullela Gopichand's daughter is a big baggage," Reddy had noted, acknowledging the expectations that come with the connection.

What the medal means for Indian badminton

Prior to this achievement, India had never had a women's doubles pair reach the medal rounds at the World Championships. The sport has traditionally been dominated by singles players in the country, withPV Sindhu and Saina Nehwal leading the charge in their categories.

This result signals the emergence of a strong women's doubles program, potentially opening doors for more investment and attention to the discipline in the years ahead.

Road ahead

The Indian pair will now turn their attention to the semifinal clash, where they face stiff competition from higher-ranked opponents. Officials have not yet confirmed their next opponents as of reporting time.

Regardless of the outcome in the semifinals, Treesa and Gayatri have already accomplished something remarkable. The badminton world will be watching closely to see if they can push further and potentially claim gold.
