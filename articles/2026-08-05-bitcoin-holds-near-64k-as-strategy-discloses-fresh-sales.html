Bitcoin Holds Near $64K as Strategy Discloses Fresh Sales

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin continues to trade in a narrow band near $64,000.
  • Strategy has disclosed further sales, adding to recent market pressure.
  • Traders remain cautious as the cryptocurrency shows limited directional momentum.

Bitcoin remained locked in a tight trading range around the $64,000 mark on Tuesday, with investors digesting the latest disclosure from Strategy, the software firm formerly known as MicroStrategy, which revealed additional sales of its holdings.

The cryptocurrency has struggled to break out of its recent consolidation pattern, hovering near the psychologically important level as market participants weigh a mix of macroeconomic signals and corporate moves.

Strategy's latest move

Strategy, one of the largest corporate holders of Bitcoin, disclosed further sales of its digital assets, according to a regulatory filing. The company, which has been a vocal proponent of Bitcoin as a treasury reserve asset, has been actively managing its portfolio in recent weeks.

The disclosure adds to a series of transactions that have kept the market on edge, as traders monitor whether the firm is trimming positions for liquidity reasons or as part of a broader strategic shift. The company has not yet detailed the exact size of the latest sale or the average price realised.

Market mood: cautious but steady

Bitcoin's price action on Tuesday reflected a broader sense of caution among traders. After a volatile start to the week, the digital asset has settled into a narrow band, with buyers and sellers seemingly unwilling to commit in either direction.

Volume has been moderate, and options markets suggest that traders are bracing for potential swings without a clear catalyst to trigger a decisive move. Some analysts point to upcoming macroeconomic data as a possible trigger, while others note that the current range could persist for some time.

Corporate holders under the lens

The disclosure from Strategy has reignited debate about the role of corporate treasuries in the crypto market. While some companies have embraced Bitcoin as a hedge against inflation, others have been more cautious, and the recent sales have raised questions about the sustainability of such strategies.

Regulatory scrutiny has also been a factor, with policymakers in several jurisdictions examining the implications of corporate crypto holdings. For now, the market appears to be taking the news in stride, with Bitcoin's price showing little reaction beyond a modest dip earlier in the session.

What to watch

Investors will be keeping a close eye on any further disclosures from Strategy and other major holders, as well as upcoming economic indicators that could influence risk appetite. A clear break above or below the current range could set the tone for the next phase of trading.

For now, Bitcoin remains in a holding pattern, with the market waiting for a fresh catalyst to spark the next move.
