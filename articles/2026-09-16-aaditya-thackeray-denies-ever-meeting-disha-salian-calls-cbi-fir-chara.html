Aaditya Thackeray Responds to CBI FIR in Disha Salian Case

Shiv Sena (UBT) leader Aaditya Thackeray has publicly addressed the recent CBI FIR linked to the death of Disha Salian, former manager of late actor Sushant Singh Rajput. In a statement, Thackeray asserted he had never met Salian and described the FIR as a deliberate attempt at character assassination. His response comes amid escalating political tension in Maharashtra.

Political Reactions and Implications

Shiv Sena (UBT)'s Stand

Aaditya Thackeray's denial aligns with his party's stance that the CBI action is politically motivated. Shiv Sena (UBT) leaders have questioned the timing, suggesting it aims to divert attention from governance issues. The party views this as a vendetta against Thackeray's campaign against the Fadnavis government.

BJP and NCP Responses

Deputy Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis stated it is improper to engage in speculation, emphasizing that the CBI is an independent body. However, BJP leaders have indirectly supported the probe, highlighting the need for justice. NCP's Nawab Malik linked the FIR to Thackeray's criticism of the government, calling it a diversionary tactic. These reactions underscore the charged political atmosphere.

Background of the Disha Salian Case

Disha Salian died on June 8, 2020, after falling from a high-rise in Mumbai. Initially deemed accidental, the case was closed, but renewed interest followed Sushant Singh Rajput's death. The CBI's fresh FIR cites new evidence and witnesses, though specifics remain undisclosed. The case has become a political flashpoint, with various parties trading accusations.

Real-World Implications

  • Political Polarization: The FIR deepens divisions, with Shiv Sena (UBT) and NCP alleging misuse of agencies, while BJP calls for impartial probe.
  • Legal Scrutiny: The CBI must substantiate claims, as courts may question delayed action.
  • Public Perception: Voters may view this as political drama, impacting trust in institutions.
  • Electoral Impact: With elections approaching, parties will leverage the issue to galvanize support.

Conclusion

The CBI FIR in the Disha Salian case has reignited a controversial chapter in Maharashtra politics. Aaditya Thackeray's strong denial sets the stage for a fierce political battle. As the investigation proceeds, its handling will be closely watched for signs of impartiality. For now, the case remains a potent tool in the arsenal of political rhetoric.
