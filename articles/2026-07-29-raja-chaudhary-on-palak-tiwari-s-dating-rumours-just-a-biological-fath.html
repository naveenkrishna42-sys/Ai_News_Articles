Raja Chaudhary on Palak Tiwari's dating rumours: 'Just a biological father, don't care'

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Raja Chaudhary dismissed Palak Tiwari's dating rumours with Ibrahim Ali Khan, saying he is 'just a biological father'.
  • He alleged that Palak walked out of his birthday party within 10 minutes after he made a remark.
  • Raja also claimed he blocked Palak on social media and renewed allegations against Shweta Tiwari.

Raja Chaudhary has broken his silence on the swirling dating rumours linking his daughter, actress Palak Tiwari, with Ibrahim Ali Khan, son of Bollywood stars Saif Ali Khan and Amrita Singh. The response was blunt and dismissive.

Speaking to the media, Chaudhary said, “I am just a biological father. I don't care.” The remark has set off a fresh wave of conversation around the already strained relationship between the father and daughter.

Fresh allegations against Shweta Tiwari

This is not the first time Raja Chaudhary has made headlines for his family disputes. He previously alleged that his ex-wife, actress Shweta Tiwari, had an affair with her Kasautii Zindagii Kay co-star Cezanne Khan during their marriage. He also claimed he was not allowed to meet Palak after the separation.

In his latest round of interviews, Chaudhary has renewed those allegations. He claimed that Shweta Tiwari has consistently kept him away from their daughter's life, a charge the actress has denied in the past.

Birthday party incident and social media block

Raja Chaudhary also shared a specific incident that he says illustrates the distance between him and Palak. He claimed that on his birthday, Palak arrived at his party but left within 10 minutes after he made a remark.

“She came, and in 10 minutes she was gone. Left after I said something,” he told reporters. Chaudhary added that he has since blocked Palak on social media, a move he described as necessary given their lack of communication.

“I blocked her. There is no point,” he said.

Dating rumours with Ibrahim Ali Khan

The dating rumours between Palak Tiwari and Ibrahim Ali Khan have been doing the rounds for months. Neither Palak nor Ibrahim has confirmed the relationship. The two have been spotted together at several events, but both have maintained silence on the subject.

Raja Chaudhary's dismissive response suggests he has no interest in engaging with that narrative. By calling himself only a “biological father,” he has effectively signalled that he has no role or stake in Palak's personal choices.

What happens next

Palak Tiwari has not reacted to her father's latest comments. The actress is currently focused on her acting career and upcoming projects. It remains to be seen whether this fresh round of statements will lead to any direct response from Palak or Shweta Tiwari.
