Bitcoin, Ethereum Gain; XRP, Dogecoin Slide Amid Crypto Act Standoff

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin and Ethereum posted gains, while XRP and Dogecoin declined.
  • The moves come amid a standoff over the proposed Crypto Act in the U.S. Congress.
  • An analyst has identified a potential bottom signal for Bitcoin.

Bitcoin and Ethereum moved higher on Thursday, while XRP and Dogecoin slipped, as traders weighed the latest developments in the U.S. legislative standoff over the proposed Crypto Act. The mixed performance underscores the market's sensitivity to regulatory headlines, with investors parsing every signal from Washington.

Mixed Signals in the Market

Bitcoin, the world's largest cryptocurrency by market capitalization, rose during the session, extending its recent recovery from a sharp pullback earlier this week. Ethereum followed suit, adding to gains as investors rotated into the two most established digital assets. In contrast, XRP and Dogecoin both declined, reflecting a cautious mood among holders of these altcoins.

Market participants said the divergence highlights a flight to relative safety within the crypto space. "When regulatory uncertainty spikes, traders tend to trim riskier positions and stick with the majors," noted one Mumbai-based crypto analyst, who asked not to be named.

Legislative Hurdles

The price action comes against the backdrop of an ongoing impasse in the U.S. Congress over the Crypto Act, a bill that seeks to establish a clear regulatory framework for digital assets. Lawmakers remain divided on key provisions, including how to classify certain tokens and which agency should oversee spot markets. The standoff has left the industry in limbo, with no clear timeline for a vote.

Observers say the prolonged uncertainty is weighing on sentiment across the sector. "Every delay in the legislative process adds a layer of risk premium to crypto prices," said a Delhi-based blockchain consultant. "Until there's clarity, we're likely to see choppy, headline-driven trading."

Analyst Flags Potential Bottom Signal

Amid the volatility, one analyst has flagged a potential bottom signal for Bitcoin. The analyst, whose work has been shared widely on social media, pointed to a technical pattern that has historically preceded recoveries. The signal, based on a combination of moving averages and trading volume, suggests that selling pressure may be exhausting.

If the pattern holds, Bitcoin could be poised for a sustained rebound, though the analyst cautioned that external factors—particularly the legislative standoff—could still derail the rally. "Technical signals are useful, but they're not a crystal ball," the analyst wrote in a note. "The macro backdrop remains the key variable."

Altcoins Under Pressure

XRP and Dogecoin, meanwhile, continue to face headwinds. XRP's decline comes amid ongoing legal and regulatory scrutiny, while Dogecoin's slide reflects waning retail interest after a period of heightened speculative activity. Both tokens have underperformed the broader market over the past week, according to data from CoinGecko.

For Indian investors, the moves serve as a reminder of the asset class's inherent volatility. Crypto trading volumes in India have picked up in recent months, but many participants remain wary of sudden swings. "The market can turn on a dime," said a Bengaluru-based trader. "You have to stay nimble."

What's Next

All eyes now turn to the next round of negotiations in Washington. A breakthrough on the Crypto Act could inject fresh optimism into the market, while further delays may prolong the current uncertainty. Traders are also watching for any signals from the Federal Reserve, which could influence risk appetite across asset classes. For now, the crypto market remains in a wait-and-see mode, with the coming days likely to set the tone for the rest of the month.
