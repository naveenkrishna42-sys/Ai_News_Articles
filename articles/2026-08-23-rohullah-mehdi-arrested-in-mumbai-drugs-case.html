Bollywood Actor Arrested In Mumbai Drug Case

Bollywood actor Rohullah Mehdi has been arrested in Mumbai in connection with an alleged social media drug network case. Police seized 28 grams of MD worth Rs 5.60 lakh during the arrest. Mehdi is a co-star of Shahid Kapoor's upcoming film 'Deva'.

Background And Implications

Mehdi's arrest comes at a time when the entertainment industry is already grappling with issues of substance abuse. This case highlights the need for stricter enforcement of anti-drug laws and increased awareness about the dangers of substance abuse.

Key Details Of The Case

The case is being investigated under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. Police have alleged that Mehdi was involved in a social media drug network, which is a relatively new phenomenon in India. This case could set a precedent for future investigations into online drug networks.
