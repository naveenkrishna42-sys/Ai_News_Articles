Kajal Aggarwal: Salman Khan made me wait longest in Bollywood

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kajal Aggarwal says Salman Khan kept her waiting the longest on set.
  • She describes Bollywood as having a culture of chronic lateness.
  • Aggarwal praises South Indian film industries for being disciplined and punctual.

Actress Kajal Aggarwal, who has worked extensively in both Bollywood and South Indian cinema, has drawn a sharp contrast between the two industries. In a recent interview, she singled out superstar Salman Khan as the actor who made her wait the longest on set.

Aggarwal, known for her roles in Telugu, Tamil, and Hindi films, did not mince words when discussing Bollywood's work culture. She described the Hindi film industry as lacking basic punctuality.

Salman Khan's sets, a test of patience

“Salman Khan made me wait the longest,” Aggarwal said, without offering further details about the specific film or schedule. Her comment points to a broader pattern she observed while working in Mumbai.

The actress did not elaborate on the duration of the wait, but her statement has sparked discussion among fans and industry insiders. Many have pointed out that Bollywood's relaxed approach to time is often joked about but rarely called out publicly.

Bollywood's time problem

Aggarwal went beyond naming one star. She said Bollywood, as a whole, operates with little regard for schedules. “Nothing is on time in Bollywood,” she stated, contrasting it with the discipline she experienced in the South.

Her remarks highlight a long-standing perception. South Indian film industries, particularly Telugu and Tamil cinema, are known for their efficient shoot schedules and professional timeliness. Actors and crews often work within tight deadlines, finishing shoots on schedule.

“The South is much more disciplined,” Aggarwal said. “People respect time. They ensure that things start and end as planned.”

A career across languages

Kajal Aggarwal began her career in Telugu cinema before moving to Tamil and Hindi films. She has starred in hits like Magadheera, Thuppakki, and Singham. Her Bollywood debut came with Singham in 2011.

Having worked on both sides, she has a unique vantage point. Her comments are not the first from a star who has worked across industries. Several actors have previously noted the difference in work ethics between Bollywood and regional film industries.

Aggarwal did not name any other Bollywood star who kept her waiting. She also did not say whether she plans to work less in Mumbai as a result of her experience.

What comes next

Kajal Aggarwal has several projects lined up in Telugu and Tamil cinema. She has not announced any new Bollywood film. Her frank remarks may add to the ongoing conversation about professionalism and punctuality in the Hindi film industry.
