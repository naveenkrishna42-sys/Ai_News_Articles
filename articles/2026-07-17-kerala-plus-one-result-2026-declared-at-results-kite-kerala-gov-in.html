Kerala DHSE Announces Plus One Scores

The Directorate of Higher Secondary Education (DHSE) in Kerala released the Plus One (Class 11) result for the academic year 2025-26 on Friday, July 17. The much-awaited scorecard is now available on the official portal, results.kite.kerala.gov.in.

Students who appeared for the examinations can check their results using their roll number and date of birth. The announcement was made at 3 PM, as confirmed by multiple sources including the state education board.

How to Check the Plus One Result 2026

Candidates can follow a simple process to view their marks. Visit the official website results.kite.kerala.gov.in or the alternate portal results.hse.kerala.gov.in. Click on the link for 'Plus One Result 2026'. Enter your roll number and date of birth. The scorecard will appear on the screen.

Students are advised to download and print a copy of the result for future reference. The portal may experience heavy traffic, so patience is recommended.

What the Result Includes

The Plus One scorecard displays subject-wise marks, total marks obtained, and the overall grade. It also indicates whether the student has passed or needs to appear for compartment exams. The examination was conducted earlier this year across various centers in the state.

School-wise results are also expected to be published on the same portal. Teachers and administrators can access these to compile performance reports for their institutions.

Previous Year Trends and This Year's Expectations

In previous years, the overall pass percentage for Kerala Plus One has been high, often exceeding 80%. The state consistently ranks among the top in the country for higher secondary education. Officials have not yet released the overall pass percentage for 2026. The exact number of students who appeared for the exam is also awaited.

Students who are unsatisfied with their results can apply for revaluation or re-totaling through the official channels once the board releases the schedule for such requests.

What Happens Next

The next step for students is to prepare for the Plus Two (Class 12) curriculum. Academic sessions for the new year are expected to begin shortly. The board will also release the schedule for supplementary exams for those who could not clear all subjects.

For the latest updates, students should keep checking the official DHSE Kerala website and their respective school notices.
