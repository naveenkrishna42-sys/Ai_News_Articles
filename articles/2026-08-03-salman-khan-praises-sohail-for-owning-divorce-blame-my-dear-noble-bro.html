Salman Khan praises Sohail for owning divorce blame: 'My dear noble bro'

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Salman Khan publicly reacted to Sohail Khan accepting blame for the divorce with Seema Sajdeh
  • He called Sohail a 'dear noble bro' in a social media post
  • The comment comes amid ongoing public discussion about the couple's separation

Mumbai: Bollywood superstar Salman Khan has broken his silence on brother Sohail Khan's recent statement taking full responsibility for his divorce from Seema Sajdeh. In a rare public display of familial support, Salman took to social media to praise his younger brother, calling him a 'dear noble bro'.

The comment has set off a fresh wave of speculation about the Khan family's dynamics, even as Sohail and Seema continue to navigate their separation in the public eye.

A brother's backing

Salman's reaction came shortly after Sohail, in an interview, acknowledged that the failure of his marriage was on him. The 'Tiger' star, known for his often guarded personal life, did not hold back in his appreciation.

"My dear noble bro," Salman wrote, attaching the post to Sohail's remarks. The brevity of the message did not dilute its weight, coming from a star who rarely comments on family matters in such direct terms.

Fans were quick to flood the comments section, with many praising the bond between the two brothers. Others saw it as a subtle endorsement of Sohail's stance on the divorce.

The context of the split

Sohail and Seema, who married in 1998, announced their separation in 2022. They have two sons, Nirvaan and Yohan, and have continued to co-parent amicably since.

In his recent interview, Sohail said he did not want to point fingers and chose to take the blame himself. "I take responsibility for what went wrong," he said, adding that he had no regrets about the marriage.

Seema, meanwhile, has moved on and is now seen as a popular figure on reality television. Her own comments about the divorce have been measured, though she has hinted at the emotional toll it took.

Questions over 'Still listening to Seema?'

Salman's post also sparked a playful yet pointed query among fans and gossip columns: was he hinting at Seema's influence? The Indian Express reported the line, 'Still listening to Seema?', as part of the online chatter around the post.

Neither Salman nor Sohail has clarified the intent behind the comment. But the phrasing suggests a light-hearted jab, possibly referencing Seema's reported role in family decisions during the marriage.

Social media users have been divided, with some defending Seema's right to speak her truth, while others see Salman's remark as a brother closing ranks.

A family in the spotlight

The Khans have long been one of Bollywood's most watched families, and their personal lives often become public property. Salman himself has had his share of scrutiny, from his 2018 hit-and-run case to his jail time in 2006.

In a separate recent comment, Salman spoke about his jail experience, describing cramped conditions with 60-70 people in a small area and a single bathroom. That rare reflection gave fans a glimpse into a difficult chapter, one that he said changed his perspective.

Against that backdrop, his support for Sohail carries added weight. It signals solidarity, but also a willingness to engage with the narrative around the divorce.

What lies ahead

Sohail is currently busy with production work, while Seema continues her innings on television. Neither has indicated any desire for a formal reconciliation, and both have stressed their commitment to their children.

As the dust settles on Salman's comment, the focus now shifts to how Seema might respond, if at all. For now, the Khan brothers have made their stance clear, and the internet is watching.
