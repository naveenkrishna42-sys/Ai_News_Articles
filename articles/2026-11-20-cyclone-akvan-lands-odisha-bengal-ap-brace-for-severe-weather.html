Cyclone Akvan Lands: Odisha, Bengal, AP Brace for Severe Weather

Cyclone Akvan Makes Landfall: Initial Impacts and Trajectory

Cyclone Akvan has commenced its landfall process along the Odisha coast as of Friday morning, November 20, 2026. TIVRA News correspondents report severe weather conditions, including exceptionally rough seas and sustained strong winds, are now impacting the region. This highly anticipated cyclonic storm threatens to bring widespread disruption across India's eastern seaboard.

Landfall Confirmation and Immediate Conditions

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) confirmed Akvan's landfall, with initial reports indicating the core of the cyclone crossing near a coastal point in Odisha. Coastal districts are experiencing significant turbulence, with trees uprooted and power supply disruptions beginning in isolated pockets. Residents in low-lying areas have reported the onset of heavy downpours accompanying the intensifying winds.

Meteorological Overview and Warning Status

Meteorological data indicates Cyclone Akvan is a severe cyclonic storm, characterized by sustained wind speeds estimated between 100-120 kmph, with gusts potentially reaching up to 135 kmph during landfall. Its trajectory suggests a gradual weakening post-landfall as it moves inland, but the immediate threat of heavy rainfall and high winds remains. An Orange Alert has been issued for coastal West Bengal, while Odisha and northern Andhra Pradesh remain under high alert for extreme weather conditions.

Widespread Disruption Across Coastal Regions

The impact of Cyclone Akvan is not confined to a single state, with significant weather events now unfolding across Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, and West Bengal. Authorities are monitoring the situation closely, advising citizens to remain indoors and follow safety protocols.

Odisha: The Epicenter of Impact

Odisha's coastal districts are bearing the brunt of Akvan's ferocity. Beyond the immediate wind and rain, the state's legislative assembly has been adjourned until November 23 morning, underscoring the severity of the situation and the need for focused disaster management. Essential services are on high alert, with emergency response teams prepositioned across vulnerable areas.

Andhra Pradesh: Srikakulam Bears the Brunt

Further south, parts of Andhra Pradesh, particularly the Srikakulam district, are experiencing continuous heavy rain. Low-lying areas in Ichchapuram and Sompeta have reported significant inundation, prompting local authorities to initiate relief operations and assess damage to infrastructure. Fishing communities along the coast had been evacuated as a precautionary measure.

West Bengal: Preparations and Rain

West Bengal, though not directly in the path of landfall, is also feeling the effects of Cyclone Akvan. Kolkata has been experiencing continuous rain, contributing to waterlogging in several parts of the city. The Orange Alert in the state signifies the potential for moderate to heavy rainfall and strong winds, urging residents to exercise caution and avoid unnecessary travel.

Preparedness and Response Efforts

State and central disaster management agencies have activated comprehensive response protocols to mitigate the impact of Cyclone Akvan. These efforts prioritize human safety and rapid relief.

Government and Disaster Management Actions

Thousands of people from vulnerable coastal areas across Odisha and Andhra Pradesh have been evacuated to designated cyclone shelters. Teams from the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) and State Disaster Response Force (SDRF) have been deployed in critical locations, equipped with rescue apparatus. Relief camps are operational, providing food, water, and medical assistance to evacuees.

Public Advisory and Safety Measures

Authorities reiterate strict advisories for the public: remain indoors, avoid coastal areas, refrain from venturing out during strong winds, and secure loose objects around homes. Fishermen have been strictly prohibited from venturing into the sea. Continuous updates are being broadcast through official channels, urging adherence to all safety guidelines.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • Cyclone Akvan officially made landfall along the Odisha coast, initiating a period of severe weather.
  • Coastal districts in Odisha, Srikakulam in Andhra Pradesh, and Kolkata in West Bengal are experiencing direct impacts.
  • Widespread heavy rainfall, strong winds, and rough sea conditions are prevalent across the affected regions.
  • Thousands evacuated; NDRF and SDRF teams actively deployed for rescue and relief operations.
  • Odisha Assembly adjourned, reflecting the gravity of the cyclonic event and its disruption to normal functions.
