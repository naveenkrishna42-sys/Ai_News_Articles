Bigg Boss 20: A new look for a milestone season

The iconic reality show Bigg Boss is set to return for its 20th season, and this time, it brings a fresh visual identity. The show, hosted by Bollywood superstar Salman Khan, will debut an all-new eye logo, marking two decades of drama, alliances, and controversies. The Times of India confirmed the development on Tuesday.

The new logo replaces the familiar eye design that has been a staple of the franchise since its inception. While the core concept of the show—contestants living in a house under constant surveillance—remains unchanged, the updated branding signals a new chapter for the long-running series.

JioStar expands the Bigg Boss universe

In a significant move, JioStar, the media conglomerate behind the Hindi version, has announced plans to launch six Bigg Boss editions across different languages and platforms. This expansion aims to tap into regional audiences, replicating the success of the Hindi flagship. The company also plans to release a franchise coffee table book, chronicling the show's most memorable moments and contestants over the years.

Details about the regional editions remain under wraps, but sources indicate that Bengali, Marathi, and Tamil versions are among the priorities. The coffee table book is expected to feature exclusive photographs and interviews with past winners and hosts.

Probable contestants and regional spinoffs

Speculation about the contestant list for Bigg Boss 20 is already rife. Names like Yogesh Rawat, Akanksha Choudhary, and Ruru Thakur have surfaced on entertainment portals, with reports suggesting they might enter the house based on fan demand. However, the official lineup has not been announced, and the channel has not confirmed any names.

In a related development, former Indian cricket captain Sourav Ganguly is rumored to host Bigg Boss Bangla 3. Pinkvilla reported the buzz, though no official confirmation has come from the network. If true, Ganguly would join a growing list of celebrities who have hosted regional versions of the franchise.

What to watch next

Fans can expect an official announcement regarding the premiere date and full contestant list in the coming weeks. With the new logo and expanded franchise plans, Bigg Boss 20 promises to be a season of reinvention. Whether the show can recapture its original spark remains to be seen.
