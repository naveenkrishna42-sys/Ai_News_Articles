Delhi Police to Register FIR Over Pellet Gun Injuries From July 20 CJP Protest

Police Action Follows Gandhi's Protest

Delhi Police will register a First Information Report (FIR) regarding pellet injuries sustained by young protesters during a Citizens for Justice and Peace (CJP) demonstration on July 20, according to sources familiar with the matter. The decision comes hours after Congress leader Rahul Gandhi sat on a dharna outside a police station, demanding action against those responsible for the injuries.

The July 20 protest saw security forces use pellet guns to disperse crowds, resulting in injuries to several participants. Details about the number of injured or the specific circumstances leading to the use of force have not yet been confirmed by officials.

Congress Leader's Stand

Rahul Gandhi's dharna marked a notable escalation in the opposition's approach to police conduct during protests. The Congress leader positioned himself outside the police station for several hours, calling for accountability over the pellet gun injuries.

His protest drew immediate reactions from rival political parties. The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) dismissed the demonstration as political theatre, with party spokespersons describing it as a coordinated effort to generate headlines rather than address genuine grievances.

Political Reactions

The BJP's response centered on the term "Camerajeevi," suggesting the protest was stage-managed for media coverage. Party leaders argued that such demonstrations undermine legitimate law enforcement while serving opposition political interests.

Political analysts note the incident reflects a broader shift in opposition strategy. Gandhi's street-level protest represents a more confrontational approach compared to traditional parliamentary methods of holding the government accountable.

Investigation Parameters

The FIR will reportedly cover the circumstances of the July 20 protest and the subsequent use of pellet guns by security personnel. Police officials have indicated the investigation will examine whether existing protocols were followed during the demonstration.

Civil rights organisations have called for transparency regarding the incident. They argue that pellet gun use against protesters requires thorough documentation and independent oversight.

What Comes Next

The registration of the FIR sets the stage for a formal investigation into the July 20 incident. Observers will watch whether the inquiry progresses to charges or remains limited to documentation. The political fallout from this episode is expected to persist, with both sides likely to maintain their respective positions as the matter moves forward through legal channels.
