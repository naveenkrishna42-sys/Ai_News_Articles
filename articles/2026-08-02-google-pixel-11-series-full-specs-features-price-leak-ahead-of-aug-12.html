Google Pixel 11 Series: Full Specs, Features, Price Leak Ahead of Aug 12 Launch

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pixel 11 series launch confirmed for August 12, with full specs and pricing leaked.
  • Leaks suggest larger batteries across models, but entry-level price sees a hike.
  • Google confirms price increase and focuses on RAM efficiency in Android.
  • Pixel 11 Pro Fold design renders surface, showing a refined hinge and slimmer profile.

Pixel 11 Series: What the Leaks Reveal

With the Google Pixel 11 series set to launch on August 12, a flood of leaks has now painted a near-complete picture of what to expect. From full specifications to pricing details, the upcoming lineup—likely including the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold—has been the subject of intense speculation. Recent reports from multiple tech outlets have converged on key details, giving consumers a clear preview before the official reveal.

The most significant change appears to be in battery capacity. Leaks suggest that Google is bumping up battery sizes across the board, addressing one of the most persistent criticisms of previous Pixel models. However, this improvement comes with a trade-off: the entry-level price is expected to rise, though the 256GB storage variants may actually cost less than their predecessors.

Pricing and Storage: A Mixed Bag

According to leaks, the Pixel 11 series will see a price increase at the base level. This aligns with Google's own confirmation that the Pixel 11 will cost more than the Pixel 10. The company has not provided specific figures, but industry insiders suggest the hike could be in the range of Rs 5,000 to Rs 10,000, depending on the model.

Interestingly, the 256GB variants are expected to be cheaper than before, possibly due to falling memory prices. This could make higher-storage models more attractive to buyers who previously opted for the base configuration. The 128GB models, however, may see a steeper increase, pushing consumers toward the larger storage options.

RAM Efficiency: Google's New Focus

In a rare pre-launch statement, Google confirmed the price increase and revealed a 'dedicated' effort to make Android more RAM-efficient. This is a notable shift, as it suggests the company is looking to optimise software rather than simply adding more RAM to hardware. The move could allow the Pixel 11 series to deliver smoother performance with lower memory usage, potentially extending battery life and reducing costs.

This focus on RAM efficiency is particularly relevant for the Pixel 11 Pro Fold, which is expected to feature a larger display and multitasking capabilities. Leaked renders show a redesigned hinge and a slimmer profile, hinting at a more polished foldable experience. The device is likely to benefit from the software optimisations, making it a more viable competitor in the foldable market.

Design and Display: Incremental Upgrades

The Pixel 11 Pro Fold's design has been a hot topic, with renders surfacing online. The device appears to have a more refined look, with thinner bezels and a more durable hinge mechanism. The main Pixel 11 and Pro models are expected to retain the signature camera bar design, but with improved sensors and processing capabilities.

Display specs remain largely under wraps, but leaks point to higher refresh rates and improved brightness levels across the lineup. The Pro models may also feature LTPO panels for better power efficiency, complementing the larger batteries.

What to Expect at Launch

As the August 12 launch date approaches, more details are likely to emerge. Google has a history of underpromising and overdelivering, but the confirmed price hike means the company will need to justify the increase with tangible improvements. The RAM-efficient Android effort could be a game-changer, especially if it results in noticeable performance gains.

Consumers in India, a key market for Google, will be watching closely. The Pixel series has gained a loyal following here, and the pricing strategy will play a crucial role in its success. With the 256GB variants becoming more affordable, Google may be aiming to capture a larger share of the premium smartphone segment.

All eyes are now on August 12, when Google will formally unveil the Pixel 11 series. Until then, expect more leaks and speculation to fill the void, as the tech community eagerly awaits the next chapter in Pixel history.
