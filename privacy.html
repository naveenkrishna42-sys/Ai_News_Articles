Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how TIVRA News ("we," "us," "our," or "https://tivranews.com") collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

  • Log & Device Data: Pages visited, referring URLs, browser version, operating system, IP address, approximate geolocation, and timestamps collected automatically via server logs and analytics.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: Small text files placed on your device to remember user preferences, measure website traffic, and deliver personalized content and advertisements.
  • Push Notification Tokens: If you opt-in to Web Push Notifications via OneSignal, an anonymous device subscription token is stored to deliver news and deal alerts. No personally identifiable information (PII) is required.
  • Information You Provide: Inquiries, feedback, or correction requests submitted via our Contact page.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, maintain, and optimize the performance of TIVRA News.
  • To deliver verified news coverage, curated product comparisons, and deal alerts.
  • To serve personalized or contextual advertisements through authorized ad networks.
  • To prevent fraudulent activity, security vulnerabilities, and unauthorized access.

4. Advertising & Monetization Partners

We work with trusted third-party advertising and monetization partners to support our free news publishing:

  • Google AdSense & DoubleClick: Google uses cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and other websites across the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings.
  • Ezoic Inc.: We partner with Ezoic to analyze site usage and optimize ad placement. Ezoic and its partners may store and access cookies on your device. For details on Ezoic's data practices, please visit Ezoic Privacy Policy.
  • Affiliate Networks (Cuelinks & Amazon Associates): TIVRA News participates in affiliate marketing programs. When you click on an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. These links place tracking cookies to verify conversions. See our Affiliate Disclosure for full details.

5. Web Push Notifications (OneSignal)

With your explicit consent, we use OneSignal to send web push notifications to your browser. You can unsubscribe at any time in your browser settings under "Site Settings" › "Notifications" › "Block."

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your geographic location, you may have specific privacy rights regarding your data:

  • European Economic Area (GDPR): You have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, data portability, and restriction of processing of your personal data.
  • California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know what personal data is collected, request deletion, and opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Opt-Out of Interest-Based Advertising: You may also opt-out via the Digital Advertising Alliance or Your Online Choices (EU).

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

TIVRA News is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy. Changes are effective immediately upon posting. Your continued use of the site signifies acceptance of any revisions.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy-related requests, please reach out via our Contact Us page.
