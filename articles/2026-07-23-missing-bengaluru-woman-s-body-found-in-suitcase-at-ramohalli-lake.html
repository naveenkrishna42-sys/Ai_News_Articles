Missing Bengaluru woman's body found in suitcase at Ramohalli Lake

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Body found inside a suitcase floating in Ramohalli Lake.
  • Victim is a 61-year-old woman who had been reported missing.
  • Police investigating the case; no arrests announced yet.

Bengaluru: The body of a 61-year-old woman, who had been reported missing for several days, was recovered from a suitcase floating in Ramohalli Lake on Wednesday morning. Police were alerted by locals who spotted the suitcase in the water and alerted authorities.

Discovery at Ramohalli Lake

Personnel from the Kengeri police station reached the spot after receiving a call around 8 am. The suitcase was pulled out of the lake and opened in the presence of senior officers. The body was in an advanced state of decomposition, making immediate identification difficult.

Police are verifying if the suitcase matches the description of any missing persons case in the area. The victim is believed to have been killed elsewhere and then dumped in the lake.

Victim identified as missing woman

Preliminary investigations suggest the deceased is a 61-year-old woman from a nearby locality who had been missing for about a week. Her family had filed a missing complaint with the local police.

Senior police officials said the body will be sent to a government hospital for post-mortem to establish the exact cause of death. The investigation is underway to identify the person or persons responsible.

Police probe under way

Forensic teams have collected samples from the spot. The suitcase has been seized as evidence. Cops are checking CCTV footage from the lake's vicinity and questioning residents.

No arrests have been made so far. Sources said the police are looking into possible motives, including property dispute or personal enmity.

The area around Ramohalli Lake has seen rapid urbanisation, but the spot where the suitcase was found remains relatively secluded. Locals said they did not notice any suspicious activity in the past few days.

Authorities have urged anyone with information to come forward. The investigation is ongoing, and more details are expected after the post-mortem report arrives.
