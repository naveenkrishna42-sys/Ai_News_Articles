Major Revamp of Cooke Town Parks

The once-neglected parks of Cooke Town, Bengaluru, are undergoing a significant transformation. The city's civic body has allocated substantial funds for a comprehensive overhaul, focusing on greenery, infrastructure, and community engagement. This initiative marks a pivotal moment for the neighborhood, which has long struggled with urban decay.

Focus on Green Spaces

The revamp includes planting native species, creating walking trails, and installing modern amenities. The goal is to enhance biodiversity and provide residents with better recreational spaces. This shift is part of Bengaluru's broader strategy to combat urban heat islands and improve air quality.

Community Engagement

Local residents are actively involved in the project. Workshops and awareness campaigns have been organized to educate the community about sustainable practices. This collaborative approach ensures that the parks meet the needs and preferences of the people who will use them.

Broader Impact

The transformation of Cooke Town's parks is expected to have a ripple effect across the city. It sets a precedent for other neglected areas and demonstrates the potential of community-driven urban development. The project also highlights the importance of investing in green spaces in densely populated cities.

Key Details and Analysis

According to urban planners, the revamp is a multi-phase project expected to be completed by the end of 2027. The total budget allocated is approximately ₹5 crore, which includes costs for new plants, walkways, and community facilities.
