Chaitra Navratri 2025: Must-Visit Durga Temples Across India

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Religion Original source ↗
Key points
  • Chaitra Navratri 2025 begins in late March, marking a period of worship for Goddess Durga.
  • Several ancient and powerful Durga temples across India attract devotees during this time.
  • Temples like Vaishno Devi, Kamakhya, and Dakshineswar Kali are among the top destinations.

As Chaitra Navratri 2025 approaches, millions of devotees across India prepare to observe nine days of fasting, prayer, and celebration dedicated to Goddess Durga. This spring festival, which typically falls in March or April, is considered one of the most auspicious times to seek the goddess's blessings. For those planning a pilgrimage, several temples across the country offer a deeply spiritual experience.

Vaishno Devi Temple, Jammu and Kashmir

Perched in the Trikuta Mountains, the Vaishno Devi shrine is one of the most visited pilgrimage sites in India. Devotees undertake a 12-kilometer trek from Katra to reach the cave temple, where the goddess is represented by three naturally formed rock formations known as pindies. During Navratri, the temple sees a surge in footfall, with special queues and arrangements made for the crowds. The journey itself is considered an act of devotion.

The temple complex operates round the clock, and authorities often extend darshan hours during the festival. Many pilgrims choose to walk through the night, chanting hymns and carrying flags. The atmosphere is charged with faith.

Kamakhya Temple, Assam

Situated on the Nilachal Hill in Guwahati, the Kamakhya Temple is a major Shakti Peetha. It is dedicated to the goddess in her form as Kamakhya, the embodiment of desire and fertility. The temple's unique sanctum does not house an idol but a yoni-shaped stone, representing the goddess's creative power. Navratri is a particularly potent time here, with elaborate rituals and animal sacrifices.

During the festival, the temple is adorned with flowers and lights. Thousands of devotees gather to offer prayers, especially on the final day of Ram Navami. The nearby Umananda Temple on the Brahmaputra River is also worth visiting.

Dakshineswar Kali Temple, West Bengal

Located on the eastern bank of the Hooghly River near Kolkata, the Dakshineswar Kali Temple is a historic shrine associated with the 19th-century saint Ramakrishna. The main temple houses Goddess Kali in her form as Bhavatarini. During Navratri, the temple witnesses special pujas and bhajans. The atmosphere is especially vibrant during the Bengali festival of Durga Puja, which coincides with Navratri in some regions.

Devotees can also visit the nearby Belur Math, the headquarters of the Ramakrishna Mission. The temple complex includes 12 smaller shrines dedicated to Shiva.

Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Uttar Pradesh

While primarily dedicated to Lord Shiva, the Kashi Vishwanath Temple in Varanasi holds special significance during Navratri. The temple complex includes a shrine to Goddess Annapurna, an incarnation of Durga. Devotees often offer prayers to both deities during the nine days. The city itself, considered the spiritual capital of India, is a major hub for pilgrims during this period.

Varanasi's ghats, especially the Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat, draw large crowds. The narrow lanes around the temple are filled with vendors selling prasad and religious items. Authorities advise planning visits early in the morning to avoid long queues.

Mata Vaishno Devi, Jammu and Kashmir (Reiteration for completeness)

This temple is often listed separately due to its distinct pilgrimage. The trek is manageable for most age groups, with pony and helicopter services available. The cave temple's natural formations are a source of wonder for many. Devotees typically register online for entry slots to manage the crowd.

During Navratri, the temple trust organizes special events like cultural performances and free meals. The nearby Bhairon Temple, a short walk from the main shrine, is also part of the pilgrimage.

What to Watch Next

With Chaitra Navratri 2025 just around the corner, devotees should check temple websites for updated darshan timings and special arrangements. Many temples now offer online booking for VIP passes. Plan your travel early to avoid last-minute rush.
