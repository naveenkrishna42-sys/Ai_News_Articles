Hetmyer, Rutherford steer West Indies to consolation win in tense chase

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • West Indies won the fifth ODI by two wickets, ending the series 3-2 in New Zealand's favour.
  • Shimron Hetmyer and Sherfane Rutherford anchored a tense chase after early wickets.
  • New Zealand had earlier posted a competitive total after being put in to bat.

Shimron Hetmyer and Sherfane Rutherford produced a gritty partnership under pressure as West Indies beat New Zealand by two wickets in the fifth and final ODI on Wednesday. The win gave the hosts a consolation victory, though New Zealand walked away with the series 3-2.

West Indies captain Shai Hope won the toss and opted to bowl first, a decision that brought Hetmyer back into the playing eleven. The move paid off as the left-hander played a crucial role in the tense chase.

New Zealand set a competitive target

Put in to bat, New Zealand's top order laid a solid foundation. The visitors managed to post a challenging total, though regular wickets in the middle overs prevented them from accelerating further. The bowlers kept the scoring in check during the death overs, leaving West Indies with a stiff ask.

West Indies' bowling unit shared the workload, with no bowler taking more than two wickets. The team's fielding was sharp, cutting off boundaries and effecting a run-out that stalled New Zealand's momentum.

Hetmyer, Rutherford hold nerve in chase

The home side's chase got off to a shaky start. Openers fell early, and the middle order struggled to build partnerships. At one stage, West Indies looked in danger of collapsing to a heavy defeat.

Hetmyer walked in at a tricky moment and combined caution with aggression. He found an able partner in Rutherford, who played the anchor role. The duo added vital runs, rotating the strike and punishing loose deliveries. Their stand brought the required rate under control.

Both batters kept their composure when the required runs came down to single digits. Even as wickets tumbled at the other end, Hetmyer and Rutherford ensured the finish line was crossed. The winning runs came with two wickets in hand, sparking celebrations in the stands.

Series outcome

New Zealand had already sealed the series with a 3-1 lead going into this match. The visitors dominated the earlier games with consistent batting and disciplined bowling. For West Indies, this win provides a much-needed morale boost after a string of losses.

The two teams will now shift focus to the T20I series, which is scheduled to begin later this week.

Officials have not yet announced the squads for the T20I matches. The selectors are likely to consider the performances from this ODI series before finalising the lineup.
