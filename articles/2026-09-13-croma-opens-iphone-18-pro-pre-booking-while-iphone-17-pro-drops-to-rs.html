Croma's Dual iPhone Strategy: Pre-Book the New, Discount the Old

Croma has opened pre-bookings for Apple's iPhone 18 Pro lineup while running aggressive promotional pricing on the outgoing iPhone 17 Pro series. The retailer's iPhone 17 Pro listing now sits at Rs 69,990, bundled with an exchange bonus, a free protective case, and a 10% instant discount on select cards. This twin-track approach lets Croma capture early adopters at full price while clearing existing inventory through value-led offers.

The timing matters for Indian buyers. Apple's India pricing continues to carry a significant premium over US rates, with the flagship duo costing nearly Rs 1 lakh more here after duties and GST. That gap reshapes the upgrade decision: pay full freight for the newest silicon, or take a discounted previous-generation flagship that still delivers flagship-grade performance.

The table reflects confirmed retail data points: Croma's advertised iPhone 17 Pro price, its bundle structure, and the widely reported India-US pricing differential. iPhone 18 Pro final India pricing remains unannounced, so buyers should treat pre-booking amounts as indicative rather than final.

What the iPhone 17 Pro Discount Really Means for Buyers

A Rs 69,990 iPhone 17 Pro with exchange bonus and card discount is a genuine value play, not a token markdown. The effective out-of-pocket cost can fall further if a buyer trades in a two-to-three-year-old device. For users upgrading from an iPhone 13 or 14, the 17 Pro still offers ProMotion display, a triple-camera system, and Apple Intelligence features that remain current.

The counter-argument is software longevity. Apple typically supports iPhones for five to six years of major iOS updates. Buying the 17 Pro in late 2026 shortens that runway by roughly a year compared with the 18 Pro, a real consideration for buyers who keep phones for the long haul.

India Pricing Pressure and the Refurbished Shift

India's iPhone premium is structural, not seasonal. Import duties and GST push landed costs well above US retail, and the flagship duo's near-Rs 1 lakh gap is the clearest evidence. That pressure is already steering cost-conscious buyers toward certified refurbished iPhones, a segment Indian retailers and online marketplaces are expanding rapidly.

For Croma, the pre-booking push and the 17 Pro discount serve two different wallets: the buyer who wants the newest camera and chip, and the buyer who wants maximum flagship per rupee. Both are rational, but they carry different depreciation curves.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • iPhone 17 Pro at Rs 69,990 with exchange bonus and 10% card discount is among the lowest effective flagship prices in recent Croma cycles.
  • Free case bundling adds roughly Rs 3,000-5,000 in practical value for buyers who would otherwise purchase one separately.
  • iPhone 18 Pro pre-booking secures early allocation, but final India pricing is unconfirmed and could rise with duty revisions.
  • India-US price gap of nearly Rs 1 lakh makes refurbished and previous-generation flagships increasingly attractive.
  • Software support window favours the 18 Pro for buyers planning to keep the device beyond four years.

Buying Verdict: Which iPhone Should You Book?

Choose the iPhone 17 Pro if you want flagship cameras, ProMotion, and Apple Intelligence at the lowest effective price, and you upgrade every two to three years. Choose the iPhone 18 Pro pre-booking if you want the longest software runway, the newest silicon, and you accept unconfirmed launch pricing. Either way, confirm exchange eligibility and card discount terms at checkout, because those two levers decide whether the Croma offer is genuinely the best deal in the Indian market.
