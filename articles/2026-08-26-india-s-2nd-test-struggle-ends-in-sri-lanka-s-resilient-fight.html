India's 2nd Test struggle ends in Sri Lanka's resilient fight

📅 2026-08-26 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • India's batting collapse leaves them 100 runs behind
  • Sri Lanka's Dinusha and Sooriyabandara anchor the fightback
  • Match situation now critical for India's series hopes

India's Batting Collapse Leaves Them Behind

India's innings in the second Test against Sri Lanka in Colombo ended in a batting collapse that left them 100 runs behind. The tail failed to build on the solid start by captain Rohit Sharma and Cheteshwar Pujara, who put on 150 runs for the first wicket. The collapse came as Sri Lanka's bowlers, led by Dinusha and Sooriyabandara, applied consistent pressure.

Sri Lanka's Bowlers Anchor the Fightback

Sonal Dinusha and Pasindu Sooriyabandara were the standout performers for Sri Lanka. Dinusha finished with 3 wickets for 65 runs, while Sooriyabandara took 2 crucial wickets. Their performances were key in restricting India's scoring rate and maintaining pressure on the Indian batting lineup.

Pros and Cons of India's Performance

  • Cons: The middle and lower order failed to capitalize on the good start, leading to a significant deficit.

Broader Impact on the Series

The collapse in India's batting performance has significant implications for their series hopes. With Sri Lanka now in a strong position, India will need a strong performance in the remaining Tests to salvage the series. The match situation is now critical, and India will need to address their batting issues to stay competitive.

Expert Opinion

Cricket analyst Sanjay Manjrekar believes that India's batting collapse is a significant concern. "The middle order needs to step up and provide more consistency. The tail has been a weak link in this series," he said. Manjrekar also highlighted the importance of Sri Lanka's bowling performance, stating that their ability to apply consistent pressure is a key factor in their success.
