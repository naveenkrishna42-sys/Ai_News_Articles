Kolkata Police file FIR against Mamata Banerjee

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Team 📅 2026-08-30 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kolkata Police file FIR against Mamata Banerjee for violating Calcutta High Court order
  • Mamata Banerjee was attending a TMC student wing event when the FIR was filed
  • The Calcutta High Court had ordered restrictions on public gatherings and political meetings

Background to the Case

On August 25, 2026, the Calcutta High Court issued an order restricting public gatherings and political meetings in Kolkata due to concerns over the spread of a contagious disease. The order was in response to a public interest litigation (PIL) filed by a group of citizens.

Incident and FIR Filing

On August 28, 2026, Mamata Banerjee attended a TMC student wing event at the Salt Lake Stadium. The event was held in defiance of the Calcutta High Court order, which had prohibited large public gatherings.

Following the event, Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari instructed Kolkata Police to file a First Information Report (FIR) against Mamata Banerjee for violating the Calcutta High Court order. The FIR was filed on August 29, 2026.

Legal and Political Implications

The filing of the FIR has raised questions about the balance between public health measures and political freedoms. Legal experts argue that while the Calcutta High Court order was issued to prevent the spread of the disease, the FIR against Mamata Banerjee raises concerns about the misuse of legal processes for political purposes.

Political analysts suggest that the FIR could have broader implications for the political landscape in West Bengal. It could be seen as an attempt to intimidate political opponents and suppress dissent, which could further polarize the political environment.

Public Reaction and Analysis

The public reaction to the FIR has been mixed. While some supporters of Mamata Banerjee have defended her actions, others have criticized the move as an overreach of the police and legal system.

Legal experts have also raised concerns about the potential for abuse of power. They argue that the FIR could set a dangerous precedent, where political leaders are held accountable for attending public events, even if those events are not illegal.

Conclusion

In conclusion, the filing of the FIR against Mamata Banerjee for violating the Calcutta High Court order is a complex issue with significant legal and political implications. It highlights the delicate balance between public health measures and political freedoms, and raises concerns about the potential for abuse of power.
