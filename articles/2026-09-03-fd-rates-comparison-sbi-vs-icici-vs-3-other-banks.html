Fixed Deposit Rates in 2026: A Comprehensive Comparison

Fixed deposits (FDs) remain a cornerstone of savings and investment strategies in India, offering attractive interest rates and liquidity. In 2026, several banks have adjusted their FD rates, reflecting changes in the economic landscape. This article provides an in-depth comparison of FD rates across State Bank of India (SBI), ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, and Punjab National Bank (PNB).

SBI: Leading the Pack with Highest FD Rates

State Bank of India (SBI) continues to be a top choice for investors seeking high FD rates. The bank offers competitive interest rates across various tenures, making it a preferred option for both short-term and long-term investments.

Key Features of SBI Fixed Deposits

  • Competitive interest rates ranging from 6.5% to 7.25% per annum
  • Flexible tenure options from 7 days to 10 years
  • Senior citizen benefits with additional 0.50% interest rate
  • Nominee facility for added security

ICICI Bank: Competitive Rates with Flexible Options

ICICI Bank has also maintained a strong presence in the FD market, offering attractive rates and flexible investment options. The bank's FD rates are comparable to SBI, making it a viable alternative for investors.

Key Features of ICICI Bank Fixed Deposits

  • Interest rates ranging from 6.4% to 7.1% per annum
  • Tenure options from 7 days to 5 years
  • Senior citizen benefits with additional 0.50% interest rate
  • Online account opening and FD management

HDFC Bank and Axis Bank: Attractive Rates for Senior Citizens

HDFC Bank and Axis Bank have gained popularity among senior citizens due to their attractive FD rates and additional benefits. These banks offer higher interest rates for senior citizens, making them a preferred choice for this demographic.

Key Features of HDFC Bank Fixed Deposits

  • Interest rates ranging from 6.3% to 7.0% per annum
  • Tenure options from 7 days to 5 years
  • Senior citizen benefits with additional 0.75% interest rate
  • Online FD renewal and maturity options

Key Features of Axis Bank Fixed Deposits

  • Interest rates ranging from 6.2% to 6.9% per annum
  • Tenure options from 7 days to 5 years
  • Senior citizen benefits with additional 0.75% interest rate
  • Online account opening and FD management

PNB: Competitive Rates with Lower Tenure Options

Punjab National Bank (PNB) offers competitive FD rates, though its tenure options are relatively lower compared to other banks. The bank's FD rates are still attractive, making it a viable option for investors.

Key Features of PNB Fixed Deposits

  • Interest rates ranging from 6.1% to 6.8% per annum
  • Tenure options from 7 days to 3 years
  • Senior citizen benefits with additional 0.50% interest rate
  • Online account opening and FD management

Conclusion

In 2026, SBI, ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, and PNB continue to offer competitive FD rates, catering to diverse investor needs. SBI leads the pack with the highest rates, while ICICI Bank provides flexible options. HDFC Bank and Axis Bank offer attractive rates for senior citizens, and PNB provides competitive rates with lower tenure options. Investors should carefully evaluate their financial goals and risk tolerance before choosing the right FD option.
