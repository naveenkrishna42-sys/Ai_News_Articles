Subhash Chandra Accuses Mukesh Ambani of Media Campaign

Subhash Chandra, the founder of Zee Entertainment Enterprises, has accused Mukesh Ambani, the chairman of Reliance Industries, of waging a media campaign against him. Chandra claims that Ambani is using various media outlets to spread misinformation and damage his reputation.

Chandra's accusations come amid a long-standing dispute over a ₹22,000 crore loan that he claims Ambani is trying to recover through legal means. The controversy has been brewing for several years, with both parties accusing each other of various irregularities.

Reliance Denies Allegations, Calls Them Baseless

Reliance Industries has strongly denied Subhash Chandra's allegations, calling them baseless and an attempt to divert attention from the legal proceedings. The company has stated that it is committed to resolving the dispute through the courts and has no intention of harming Chandra's reputation.

Reliance has also accused Chandra of making false claims and spreading misinformation, which it claims is an attempt to create a negative narrative around the company. The company has stated that it will take all necessary legal actions to protect its interests and reputation.

Controversy Over ₹22,000 Crore Loan Dispute

The controversy over the ₹22,000 crore loan dispute between Subhash Chandra and Mukesh Ambani has been a long-standing issue. The loan was allegedly granted by Reliance Industries to Chandra's company, Zee Entertainment Enterprises, for the acquisition of a stake in Reliance Communications.

The dispute has been ongoing for several years, with both parties accusing each other of various irregularities. Chandra claims that the loan was granted under false pretenses and that Reliance is trying to recover the amount through legal means. Reliance, on the other hand, claims that the loan was granted under normal business practices and that Chandra is trying to create a negative narrative around the company.
