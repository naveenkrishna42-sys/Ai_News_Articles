Hyderabad: Balloon gas cylinder blast near GSM mall injures four

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Explosion occurred near GSM mall in Hyderabad on Saturday afternoon.
  • Four people were injured and have been shifted to a hospital.
  • Fire and police teams reached the spot; probe into cause is ongoing.

Blast Reported in Busy Commercial Area

A gas cylinder used for filling balloons exploded near the GSM mall in Hyderabad on Saturday afternoon, leaving four people injured. The incident took place in a bustling stretch, prompting an immediate response from local authorities.

According to initial reports, the cylinder burst while being handled for balloon inflation, a common sight in commercial zones. The force of the blast caused panic among shoppers and passersby, though the exact sequence of events is still being pieced together by investigators.

Injured Shifted to Hospital

The four injured individuals were rushed to a nearby hospital, where their condition is stated to be stable. Medical staff have not yet provided a detailed health bulletin, but sources indicate that none of the injuries are life-threatening.

Police and fire tenders reached the spot shortly after the explosion. The area was cordoned off for some time to ensure safety and to allow forensic teams to examine the debris.

Investigation Underway

Authorities have launched an inquiry to determine whether the blast was due to a faulty cylinder, mishandling, or a lapse in safety norms. Balloon gas cylinders, typically filled with helium or hydrogen, can be volatile if damaged or exposed to heat.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact cause or the type of gas involved. Vendors in the vicinity have been asked to exercise caution while handling such equipment.

This is not the first such incident in Hyderabad, where small-scale cylinder explosions have occasionally been reported in market areas. However, Saturday's blast did not cause any major property damage, barring minor impact on nearby stalls.

Safety Concerns for Street Vendors

The incident has renewed focus on the unregulated use of gas cylinders by balloon sellers, who often operate without proper training or safety gear. City police have in the past issued advisories on handling compressed gas, but enforcement remains uneven.

Local traders have urged the municipal authorities to conduct periodic safety checks in commercial hubs. They also want awareness drives for vendors who routinely deal with flammable materials.

As the investigation progresses, officials are expected to file a detailed report on the blast. The injured are likely to be discharged within a day, pending medical observation.

What to Watch

Authorities are expected to release a formal statement on the cause of the explosion in the coming days. The incident may also prompt stricter checks on gas cylinder usage across the city's markets.
