Bitcoin Hits One-Month High of $65,400 Amid Renewed Buying

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin reached $65,400, a one-month high.
  • The rally was fueled by increased buying activity.
  • Analysts cite favorable macroeconomic cues and institutional interest.

Bitcoin Breaks Past $65,400 Mark

Bitcoin climbed to $65,400 on Monday, marking its highest price in a month. The digital asset gained over 4% in the last 24 hours, snapping a weeks-long consolidation phase.

Traders pointed to a sudden surge in spot buying on major exchanges as the primary catalyst. Volume on platforms like Binance and Coinbase jumped sharply during the Asian trading session.

What Drove the Rally?

Market participants attributed the move to a combination of factors. Renewed optimism around a potential spot Bitcoin ETF approval in the U.S. has been building. Comments from regulatory officials last week hinted at progress.

Macroeconomic conditions also played a role. The dollar index softened, making risk assets like crypto more attractive. Bitcoin's correlation with tech stocks has strengthened in recent weeks.

Institutional Interest Resurfaces

Data from on-chain analytics firm CoinDesk shows that wallet addresses holding at least 1,000 BTC have been accumulating steadily over the past fortnight. This pattern often precedes significant price moves.

Large block trades were reported on over-the-counter desks, suggesting institutional players are re-entering the market. One trader described the buying as “aggressive but measured.”

Retail sentiment has also improved. Social media chatter around Bitcoin turned positive for the first time in three weeks, according to sentiment trackers.

Resistance and Next Targets

The $65,400 level is a key resistance zone. If Bitcoin holds above this, the next target is $68,000, a price not seen since early June. A failure to sustain could see a pullback to $62,000.

Options market data indicates bullish positioning for the July expiry. The put-call ratio has dropped, signaling that traders expect further upside. However, volatility remains a risk, with sudden profit-taking possible.

Some analysts caution that the rally lacks broad-based altcoin support. Ethereum and other major tokens have not kept pace, which could indicate a narrow rally.

What to watch: Bitcoin's ability to close above $65,400 in the next 24 hours will be critical. Traders are eyeing U.S. Federal Reserve commentary later this week for cues on interest rates, which could influence risk appetite.
