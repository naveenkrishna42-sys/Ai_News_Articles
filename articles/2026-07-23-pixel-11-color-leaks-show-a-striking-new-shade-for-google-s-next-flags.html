Pixel 11 color leaks show a striking new shade for Google's next flagship

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Leaked images show the Pixel 11 series in multiple frame colors, with one new shade described as particularly eye-catching.
  • The Pixel 11 is expected to feature an improved face unlock system.
  • Google has reportedly started sending invites for the Pixel 11 launch event in India.

A Fresh Coat of Paint

Leaked images of Google's upcoming Pixel 11 series have surfaced, giving us a clear look at the colour options the company is preparing. According to Android Authority, the renders show a lineup of frame colours, and one new shade is being described as particularly eye-catching.

The leak aligns with reports from Droid Life, which noted that Google is experimenting with fun frame colours to differentiate the Pixel 11 from its predecessors. The exact name of the standout colour has not been confirmed, but early descriptions suggest a vibrant, saturated tone that could appeal to users looking for something beyond the usual black, white, or pastel options.

Hardware and Feature Rumours

Beyond the colours, 9to5Google reports that the Pixel 11 will come with an improved face unlock system. This upgrade is expected to address speed and low-light performance, bringing it closer to the reliability of Apple's Face ID. The same report indicates that the Pixel 11a will use the Tensor G6 chip, suggesting Google is planning a staggered rollout for its mid-range and flagship devices.

GSMArena.com has noted that Google has begun sending out invites for the Pixel 11 series launch event in India, indicating that the company is preparing for a global release. The event is expected to take place in the coming months, though an exact date has not been officially announced.

What We Know So Far

Nokiamob has compiled a summary of everything known about the Pixel 11 series so far. The lineup is expected to include the standard Pixel 11, a Pro variant, and the Pixel 11a. Camera improvements, including a new sensor and software enhancements, are also rumoured, though Google has not confirmed any specifics.

The leaked images suggest that Google is betting on bold design choices to attract buyers in a crowded smartphone market. The new colour option, in particular, seems designed to stand out on shelves and in online listings.

As with all leaks, these details should be treated with caution until Google makes an official announcement. The company has a history of making last-minute changes to hardware and software before launch.

What to Watch Next

All eyes will be on Google's official launch event, expected later this year. The Pixel 11 series will need to compete with the iPhone 17 and Samsung Galaxy S26, making the final colour choices and feature set critical to its success.
