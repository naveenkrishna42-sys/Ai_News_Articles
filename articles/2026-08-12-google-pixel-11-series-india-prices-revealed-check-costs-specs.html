Google Pixel 11 series India prices revealed: Check costs, specs

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pixel 11 series launched in India with Tensor G6 processor
  • Google raised Pixel prices by $100 due to rising memory costs
  • Made by Google 2026 event also unveiled Pixel Watch 5 and new AI features

Pixel 11 series pricing in India

Google has finally revealed how much the Pixel 11 series will cost in India. The new lineup includes the standard Pixel 11, the Pixel 11 Pro, and the top-end Pixel 11 Pro Fold, all powered by the new Tensor G6 processor.

As reported by GSMArena.com and The Hindu, the Pixel 11 Pro Fold has been launched in India with the Tensor G6 chip. The pricing details come amid a broader trend of rising component costs, particularly memory chips, which has forced Google to increase Pixel prices by $100 across markets.

The exact rupee prices for each model have not been fully disclosed in the initial announcements. However, industry observers expect the Pixel 11 range to sit at a premium, given the global price hike and the inclusion of the latest Tensor silicon.

Why prices went up

Investing.com India reported that Google has raised Pixel prices by $100 as memory costs climb. This is a significant shift for the company, which has traditionally priced its flagship phones aggressively to compete with Samsung and Apple.

The increase is attributed to the rising cost of RAM and storage components, a global issue affecting many smartphone makers. For Indian consumers, this means the Pixel 11 series will likely cost more than the previous generation, though Google may offer exchange bonuses or EMI options to soften the blow.

Analysts note that the memory price surge is unlikely to ease soon, which could keep smartphone prices elevated through 2027. Google has not commented on whether further price adjustments are planned.

What the Pixel 11 series offers

The Pixel 11 series is being marketed as "your most personal Pixel yet," according to blog.google. The devices leverage the Tensor G6 processor to deliver enhanced on-device AI capabilities, improved camera processing, and better battery efficiency.

The Pixel 11 Pro Fold, in particular, brings a foldable form factor with a large inner display, aiming to compete with Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold series. Google has also integrated deeper AI features across the lineup, including real-time translation, advanced photo editing, and proactive assistant functions.

All models are expected to run the latest version of Android, with seven years of software updates promised, extending the lifespan of the devices.

Pixel Watch 5 and more at Made by Google 2026

The 'Made by Google 2026' event, as covered by The Times of India, was a packed affair. Alongside the Pixel 11 phones, Google unveiled the Pixel Watch 5 and a host of new AI features across its ecosystem.

The Pixel Watch 5 is expected to offer improved health tracking and deeper integration with the Pixel phones. The event also showcased new AI tools that span Google's apps and services, reinforcing the company's push to make AI the core of its hardware experience.

While the Pixel 11 series availability in India is confirmed, exact sale dates and carrier partnerships are yet to be announced. Google is likely to open pre-orders soon, with deliveries expected in the coming weeks.

Consumers looking to buy the Pixel 11 series should keep an eye on Google's India online store and major e-commerce platforms for launch offers. The price hike may be a sticking point for some, but the new Tensor G6 features could justify the premium for many enthusiasts.

The coming weeks will reveal how the Indian market responds to the new pricing, and whether Google's AI-first approach gives it an edge in the competitive premium segment.
