Hockey India's saffron jersey sparks controversy; Tirkey says not consulted

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hockey India introduced a saffron jersey, replacing the iconic blue, ahead of a major tournament.
  • Dilip Tirkey, Hockey India president, said the decision was taken without his prior knowledge.
  • Critics question the rationale, citing India's successful history in blue and the move's political overtones.

Hockey India's decision to don a saffron jersey has sparked a controversy that refuses to die down. The change, announced ahead of a key international assignment, replaces the iconic blue jersey that has been synonymous with Indian hockey's greatest triumphs. But what has added fuel to the fire is the revelation that the country's hockey chief, Dilip Tirkey, was not even consulted before the switch.

Tirkey's bombshell

Tirkey, a legend of the sport and current president of Hockey India, told the executive board that the jersey change was taken without his prior knowledge. This admission has raised questions about the decision-making process within the national federation. If the president of the body is kept in the dark, who exactly is calling the shots?

The timing of the announcement has also invited scrutiny. The saffron jersey is being introduced just as the team gears up for a major tournament, a period when most federations prefer stability over experimentation. For a team that has historically performed well in blue, the sudden shift has left players and fans alike puzzled.

The blue legacy

India's blue hockey jersey is not just a piece of clothing; it is a badge of honour. From the golden era of the 1970s and 1980s to the recent resurgence in international hockey, blue has been the colour of Indian success. Critics argue that changing it for a turf is a sham explanation, as the colour of a jersey has little to do with on-field performance.

Social media has been abuzz with fans pointing out that Pakistan, India's arch-rivals, have won twice while wearing green, while India has triumphed in blue. The implication is clear: why fix what isn't broken? The backlash, led largely by Gen Z fans, has been vocal and unrelenting. Yet, the federation appears unmoved.

A political undertone?

The saffron colour, often associated with the ruling party's ideological hue, has inevitably drawn political interpretations. While Hockey India has not officially linked the jersey to any political symbolism, the optics are hard to ignore. In a country where sport and politics often intersect, this move has been seen by many as an attempt to align the sport with a larger cultural narrative.

However, the federation has maintained that the decision is purely commercial and aimed at refreshing the team's image. Officials have not yet confirmed whether the saffron jersey will be a permanent change or a one-off experiment for the upcoming tournament.

What lies ahead

As the debate rages on, the focus will inevitably shift to the team's performance on the field. If the players deliver results, the jersey colour may fade into the background. But if the team stumbles, the saffron switch could become a lasting symbol of poor judgment.

For now, all eyes are on Hockey India's next move. Will Tirkey's statement force a rethink, or will the federation dig in its heels? The coming weeks will reveal whether this is a temporary storm or a permanent scar on the sport's legacy.
