Woman hurls ink-like liquid at Abhijeet Dipke during CJP protest at Jantar Mantar

📅 2026-07-18 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • A woman threw an ink-like liquid at Abhijeet Dipke at Jantar Mantar
  • The incident took place during a protest by the CJP (Citizens for Justice and Peace) group
  • Police have not yet made any arrests or confirmed the nature of the liquid

A woman threw an ink-like liquid at Abhijeet Dipke during a protest organised by the Citizens for Justice and Peace (CJP) at Jantar Mantar in New Delhi on Saturday. The incident took place in the morning, shortly after the demonstration began.

Dipke, who was present at the protest site, was approached by the woman who then hurled a dark, ink-coloured substance at him. Eyewitnesses said the liquid splashed onto his face and clothes. Security personnel present at the venue quickly intervened and detained the woman.

Protest and disruption

The CJP has been holding protests at Jantar Mantar for the past several weeks, demanding justice in a series of cases. The group has been vocal about issues related to police brutality and judicial accountability. Saturday's protest saw a turnout of around 200 people.

Witnesses said the woman was among the protesters but had not been seen before. She was not carrying any banner or placard. After throwing the liquid, she reportedly shouted slogans before being taken away by security. The nature of the liquid has not been confirmed by authorities.

Police response and investigation

Delhi Police officers present at the scene detained the woman and took her to the nearby Connaught Place police station for questioning. A case has been registered under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code for assault and causing public nuisance.

Police sources said the woman's identity and motive are being investigated. They have not ruled out the possibility of a personal grudge. Dipke did not sustain any visible injuries and declined to comment to the media. Police are also examining CCTV footage from the area.

Unconfirmed reports

Some media reports suggest the liquid might have been ink mixed with water. However, official confirmation is pending. Police said forensic analysis will be conducted if required. The CJP has distanced itself from the incident, stating the woman was not a member of their group.

Context of the protests

The CJP protests have been largely peaceful so far. The group has been demanding a judicial inquiry into the deaths of several individuals allegedly in police custody. The government has not responded to their demands. Saturday's incident marks the first reported violence at these protests.

Dipke is known to be a supporter of the ruling party and has been a vocal critic of the CJP. He was at Jantar Mantar to counter-protest, according to sources. His presence at the site had been announced on social media earlier this week.

Police have increased security at the protest venue. They have advised both groups to maintain peace. No further incidents have been reported after the woman was taken away.

The investigation is ongoing. Police are expected to provide more details after questioning the woman. The court will likely hear the matter in the coming days.
