KSRTC bus overturns near Bidadi; driver, conductor killed

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • The accident occurred near Bidadi on Saturday afternoon
  • Driver and conductor died on the spot
  • Several passengers injured and hospitalised
  • KSRTC officials and police are investigating
  • Cause of the overturn not yet confirmed

Two KSRTC employees were killed and several passengers injured when a Kozhikode-Bengaluru bus overturned near Bidadi on Saturday afternoon. The bus, operated by the Kerala State Road Transport Corporation, was on its regular inter-state route when the driver lost control and the vehicle flipped onto its side.

The deceased have been identified as the driver and the conductor, both attached to the Kozhikode depot. Their names have not been officially released yet, pending family notification. Police said the duo died on the spot, while the injured were rushed to a nearby government hospital.

Accident spot and rescue

The incident took place on the busy Bengaluru-Mysuru highway corridor, close to Bidadi, around 30 km from the city. Local residents and passersby were the first to respond, pulling passengers out of the mangled bus before emergency services arrived.

Fire tenders and ambulances reached the spot within minutes. Police cordoned off the area to clear the wreckage and restore traffic, which was disrupted for nearly two hours. The bus, carrying roughly 40 passengers, was heading to Bengaluru when the mishap occurred.

Passengers injured

According to preliminary reports, at least a dozen passengers sustained injuries, some of them serious. They have been admitted to hospitals in Bidadi and Bengaluru for treatment. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of injured or their current condition.

KSRTC officials from both Karnataka and Kerala have been informed. A senior KSRTC officer said the corporation will extend full support to the families of the deceased and cover the medical expenses of the injured.

Investigation underway

Police have registered a case of rash driving and negligence, though the cause of the overturn remains unclear. Preliminary assessments suggest the driver may have lost control while negotiating a curve, but investigators are also checking for mechanical failure or a possible tyre burst.

“We are examining the bus and speaking to eyewitnesses. Nothing can be confirmed until the probe is complete,” a police officer said. The driver's record and the vehicle's maintenance logs are also being reviewed.

Safety questions resurface

The accident has once again raised concerns about the safety of inter-state bus travel, especially on the Kozhikode-Bengaluru route, which is popular among migrant workers and students. KSRTC has a relatively good safety record, but this incident highlights the risks on long-distance routes.

Transport experts say driver fatigue and poor road conditions are common factors in such accidents. The highway near Bidadi has seen multiple crashes in the past, and local authorities have been urged to improve signage and lighting.

As the investigation continues, KSRTC has promised a detailed report. The corporation is expected to conduct an internal inquiry and may review driver duty schedules to prevent similar incidents.
