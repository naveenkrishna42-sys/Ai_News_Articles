Bitcoin dips to $64.2k as Hormuz hopes fade, Strategy sells more coins

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin slipped to $64,200 as geopolitical optimism faded.
  • Strategy sold more coins, adding to supply pressure.
  • Market focus shifts to Fed signals and ETF flows.

Bitcoin slips as Hormuz optimism cools

Bitcoin traded lower on Tuesday, easing to $64,200, as hopes for a quick de-escalation in the Strait of Hormuz faded. The digital asset had rallied in recent sessions on speculation that tensions in the region might ease, but those bets unravelled as no concrete diplomatic breakthrough emerged.

Traders who had piled into risk assets on the back of a potential ceasefire narrative began unwinding positions, dragging Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies down. The pullback underscores how sensitive crypto prices remain to geopolitical headlines, even as institutional interest deepens.

Strategy's latest coin sale adds pressure

Adding to the downward pressure, Strategy—the software firm formerly known as MicroStrategy—sold more coins, according to market data. The company, which has been one of the largest corporate holders of Bitcoin, has periodically trimmed its holdings, and the latest sale has injected fresh supply into an already cautious market.

Investors are watching Strategy's moves closely, as its treasury operations have often influenced Bitcoin's price direction. The sale comes at a time when liquidity is thin, amplifying the impact of any large transaction on the spot market.

What's driving the market now?

Beyond geopolitics, traders are also eyeing macroeconomic cues. With the US Federal Reserve's policy path still uncertain, any shift in rate expectations could sway risk assets, including crypto. A stronger dollar or higher bond yields typically weigh on Bitcoin, and today's move reflects that dynamic.

On-chain data shows that exchange inflows have ticked up, suggesting some holders are looking to lock in profits or cut losses. Meanwhile, spot Bitcoin ETFs have seen mixed flows, with no clear trend emerging in recent sessions.

Looking ahead

The next few days could set the tone for Bitcoin's near-term trajectory. Key levels to watch include $63,500 as immediate support, with a break below that opening the door to further downside. On the upside, reclaiming $65,000 would signal renewed buying interest.

Geopolitical developments in the Middle East and any fresh statements from Strategy's management will likely dictate sentiment. For now, the market remains in a wait-and-see mode, with traders bracing for volatility in either direction.
