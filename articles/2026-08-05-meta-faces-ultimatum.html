Meta Faces Ultimatum

📅 2026-08-05 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Meta given ultimatum by parliament panel
  • Content moderation practices under scrutiny
  • Meeting with IT ministry officials scheduled

Parliament Panel Issues Ultimatum to Meta

A parliament panel has given Meta an ultimatum: apologise over a video related to the Prime Minister or lose safe harbour protection. The panel's move comes amid a row over Meta's content moderation practices. Officials have not yet confirmed the specifics of the video in question.

Content Moderation Under Scrutiny

Meta's content moderation practices have been under scrutiny, with the BJP and opposition parties sparring over the issue at a panel meeting. The parliament panel has summoned Meta's public policy head again to discuss the matter. The centre has also summoned Meta's global team on August 5-6 to raise concerns over child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and artificial intelligence (AI).

A meeting between Meta and IT ministry officials is scheduled for August 5-6, where issues such as CSAM, deepfakes, and verified account safeguards will be on the agenda. The meeting is expected to be crucial in resolving the ongoing row over Meta's content moderation practices.

Upcoming Meeting with IT Ministry Officials

The meeting between Meta and IT ministry officials will be closely watched, as it may have significant implications for Meta's operations in India. The company may be required to implement stricter content moderation policies to avoid losing its safe harbour protection. Officials have not yet confirmed the specifics of the meeting or the potential outcomes.

Implications of the Row

The row over Meta's content moderation practices has sparked a wider debate over social media regulation in India. The government has been pushing for stricter regulations to curb the spread of misinformation and harmful content on social media platforms. The outcome of the meeting between Meta and IT ministry officials may have significant implications for the future of social media regulation in India.
