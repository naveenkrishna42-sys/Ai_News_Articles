Sangeetha withdraws divorce case against Tamil Nadu CM Vijay

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sangeetha withdrew her divorce petition against CM Vijay
  • Chengalpattu court closed the case after a 15-minute hearing
  • She retains the right to file a fresh petition in the future

Case Withdrawn After Brief Hearing

Chengalpattu: In a significant development, Sangeetha, wife of Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Vijay, on Friday withdrew her divorce petition before the Chengalpattu court. The case was closed after a brief 15-minute hearing, bringing temporary relief to the chief minister.

The withdrawal comes months after Sangeetha had filed for divorce, a move that had drawn considerable public and media attention given Vijay's political stature. The court has officially recorded the withdrawal and closed the proceedings.

Right to File Fresh Petition Retained

According to the order, Sangeetha retains the legal right to file a fresh divorce petition at a later date. This means the matter is not fully settled and could resurface depending on future circumstances.

Legal experts note that such a withdrawal without prejudice is a common procedural step, allowing the petitioner to approach the court again if needed. The specific reasons for the withdrawal have not been disclosed by either party.

CM's Office Maintains Silence

The Chief Minister's office has not issued any official statement on the development. Vijay, who has been leading the state government since 2026, has largely kept his personal life away from public discourse.

Reports suggest that the hearing was brief, with Sangeetha's counsel stating her intention to withdraw the petition. The court accepted the submission and disposed of the matter.

Background of the Case

The divorce petition had been filed earlier, and its progress had been closely followed by political circles and the media. The case had also sparked discussions about the personal challenges faced by public figures in Tamil Nadu.

Neither Sangeetha nor her legal representatives have commented on the withdrawal or future plans. The development comes at a time when Vijay's administration is dealing with multiple policy initiatives and administrative responsibilities.

What Happens Next

With the case closed for now, the immediate legal pressure on the chief minister has eased. However, the retained right to file a fresh petition means the matter could return to court, keeping the personal saga open-ended. Observers will watch for any further legal moves by Sangeetha or statements from the CM's camp.
