Salman Khan Tops the Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 Guest List

Salman Khan joined his family at brother Sohail Khan's Mumbai residence for Ganesh Chaturthi 2026, a visual confirmed by NDTV's video report. The actor's presence at the Khan household marked one of the festival's most tracked celebrity sightings this season, arriving amid a wave of Bollywood Ganpati welcomes across the city.

The Khan family has observed Ganesh Chaturthi with consistent devotion for years, and Salman's appearance at Sohail's home underlines how the festival functions as a family anchor for one of Indian cinema's most prominent clans. For fans and media alike, the video offered a rare, unguarded look at the superstar away from film promotions and brand commitments.

Bollywood's 2026 Ganpati Roll Call

This year's celebrations spread across Mumbai's celebrity map. Shilpa Shetty, Ankita Lokhande and Sunita Ahuja welcomed Bappa, as reported by The Indian Express. The Times of India confirmed that Akshay Kumar, Kajol, Kangana Ranaut, Kareena Kapoor and Sharvari shared festive wishes, while Ananya Panday hosted Ganpati at home with her family, per Telangana Today. Sonu Sood also featured among the stars welcoming the deity.

The breadth of participation matters commercially. Celebrity Ganpati posts generate millions of impressions within 24 hours, feeding a content cycle that benefits photographers, event planners, decorators and digital marketing teams. Brands tracking celebrity social media value treat September as a peak engagement window, second only to the Diwali season.

Why Celebrity Ganpati Coverage Commands Attention

Ganesh Chaturthi sits at the intersection of faith, family and fame in Mumbai. When a star like Salman Khan steps into a sibling's home for aarti, the image carries authenticity that scripted publicity cannot manufacture. Audiences respond to that sincerity, which is precisely why news outlets prioritised the Sohail Khan residence visuals over generic festival greetings.

For the film industry, the festival also serves a practical networking function. Directors, producers and financiers gather at celebrity pandals and private celebrations, often ahead of festive-season release calendars. Coverage of these events keeps stars visible during production gaps, maintaining audience recall between projects.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • Salman Khan's appearance at Sohail Khan's home generated the day's most-shared celebrity Ganpati visual.
  • Participation spanned generations, from veterans like Kajol and Akshay Kumar to younger stars like Ananya Panday and Sharvari.
  • Multiple outlets—NDTV, The Indian Express, The Times of India and Telangana Today—independently verified the breadth of celebrations.
  • Home celebrations, not just public pandals, dominated this year's celebrity coverage.
  • Festival content delivers a measurable spike in digital engagement and brand interest every September.

Buyer's Lens: What Festival Content Is Worth

Media buyers and brand managers should note the pattern. Celebrity Ganpati content performs strongly for lifestyle, fashion, jewellery and food categories, where festive associations translate directly into purchase intent. Sponsorship slots around such coverage, or influencer tie-ins with celebrity-adjacent events, typically command premium rates during the festival week.

However, brands chasing virality must verify authenticity. Audiences quickly detect staged devotion, and backlash can outweigh reach. The most durable campaigns align with genuine family traditions, exactly the format seen at the Khan residence this year.

As Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 progresses, expect further celebrity sightings and expanded coverage. The Salman Khan video sets the benchmark: personal, verifiable and rooted in family—qualities that keep Bollywood's festival reporting relevant long after the aarti ends.
