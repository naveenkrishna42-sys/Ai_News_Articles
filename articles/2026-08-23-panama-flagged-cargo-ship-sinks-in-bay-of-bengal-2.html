Panama-flagged cargo ship sinks in Bay of Bengal

A Panama-flagged cargo ship carrying 24 crew members has sunk in the Bay of Bengal, approximately 240 nautical miles off the Paradip coast in Odisha. The incident occurred on Sunday, with two crew members rescued, while 22 remain missing. The search operation is ongoing with international assistance.

Search operation intensifies

The search for the 22 missing crew members has intensified over the past 24 hours. Officials have confirmed that no casualties have been reported yet, but the situation remains critical. The shipping minister has expressed concern over the incident, stating that the search operation is being conducted with the help of international agencies.

International assistance mobilized

International assistance has been mobilized to aid in the search and rescue operation. Various countries have offered their support, including search and rescue teams, ships, and aircraft. The Indian Coast Guard and the Odisha state government are leading the operation, with coordination from the Ministry of Shipping.

Shipping minister confirms incident

The shipping minister has confirmed the incident, stating that the cause of the sinking is still under investigation. The minister has assured the public that all necessary measures are being taken to ensure the safety of the missing crew members. The incident has raised concerns about maritime safety and the need for stricter regulations.

What happens next

The search operation will continue for the next few days, with a focus on locating the missing crew members. The cause of the sinking will be thoroughly investigated, and measures will be taken to prevent such incidents in the future. The international community will play a crucial role in the search and rescue operation, highlighting the importance of global cooperation in maritime safety.
