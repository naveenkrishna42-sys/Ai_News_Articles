Nvidia boosts markets, but caution prevails

📅 2026-08-27 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty open higher, driven by Nvidia gains
  • Caution prevails amid broader economic concerns
  • Nvidia's performance masks underlying market risks

Nvidia's gains drive Indian markets higher

The Sensex and Nifty opened modestly higher on Thursday, with Nvidia's strong performance driving gains in the technology sector. Nvidia's stock surged after the company reported better-than-expected earnings, boosting investor sentiment. However, the broader market remains cautious, with concerns over economic growth and inflation weighing on sentiment.

Caution prevails amid broader economic concerns

While Nvidia's gains have lifted the market, caution remains prevalent. Investors are wary of the broader economic landscape, with concerns over global growth and inflation persisting. The Reserve Bank of India's recent monetary policy decisions have also added to the uncertainty, with markets awaiting further clarity on interest rate policies.

Nvidia's performance masks underlying market risks

Nvidia's strong performance has masked some of the underlying risks in the market. The company's earnings growth has been driven by strong demand for its AI chips, which are in high demand across various sectors. However, the broader market remains vulnerable to economic headwinds, with concerns over global growth and inflation persisting.

Market performance

The Sensex opened higher by 120 points, while the Nifty gained 30 points. The technology sector was the biggest gainer, with stocks like Infosys and TCS also showing gains. However, the banking and financial services sector remained under pressure, with stocks like ICICI Bank and HDFC Bank showing losses.
