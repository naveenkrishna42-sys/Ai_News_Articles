Q1 Results LIVE: NTPC, Shriram Finance, SBI Life, BoB, Others Report Earnings on July 24

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • NTPC, Shriram Finance, SBI Life, Hindustan Zinc, Lodha, Bank of Baroda, CG Power, REC among companies reporting Q1 results on July 24.
  • Tata Consumer Products reported a 28.4% rise in consolidated PAT. ACC saw a 61.5% decline in PAT.
  • Shriram Finance, Hindustan Zinc, SBI Life, SBI Cards, and Laurus Labs posted a rise in Q1 profit. Jindal Steel profit slumped.
  • Bank of Baroda net interest income rose; Bank of India asset quality showed improvement.

Earnings Day: A Heavy Slate of Q1 Reports

July 24 turned out to be a packed day for corporate earnings in India. A host of blue-chip companies across sectors — from power and banking to consumer goods and metals — released their results for the quarter ended June 2025.

Among the major names reporting were NTPC, Shriram Finance, SBI Life Insurance, Hindustan Zinc, Lodha (Macrotech Developers), Bank of Baroda, CG Power, and REC. The day also saw results from SBI Cards, Laurus Labs, SAIL, KFin Technologies, and others.

Tata Consumer and ACC: A Mixed Bag

Tata Consumer Products reported a solid performance. The company's consolidated net profit rose 28.4% compared to the same quarter last year. This growth came on the back of strong demand in its packaged beverages and foods business.

In contrast, ACC, the cement giant, had a tough quarter. The company's profit after tax declined sharply by 61.5%. Industry watchers pointed to higher input costs and pricing pressure in the cement sector as possible reasons.

Banking and Finance: Divergent Trends

Shriram Finance, a leading non-banking financial company, posted a rise in its quarterly profit. The company continued to see healthy growth in its loan portfolio, particularly in the commercial vehicle and small business segments.

Bank of Baroda reported an increase in its net interest income — a key metric for banks. The public sector lender's asset quality metrics remained stable. Bank of India also showed improvement in its asset quality, with gross non-performing assets trending lower.

SBI Life Insurance and SBI Cards both reported higher profits for the quarter. SBI Life benefited from strong new business premiums, while SBI Cards saw an uptick in spending and receivables.

Metals and Power: Mixed Signals

Jindal Steel and Power saw its profits slump during the quarter. The steel maker faced headwinds from lower global prices and higher raw material costs. Hindustan Zinc, on the other hand, reported a rise in profit, aided by higher zinc and lead prices.

NTPC, the country's largest power generator, also reported its numbers on Thursday. The state-owned giant's results were closely watched given the ongoing summer demand for electricity. REC, the power sector financier, also announced its earnings.

Other Notable Results

Laurus Labs, the pharmaceutical and biotechnology company, posted a rise in quarterly profit. The company has been benefiting from a strong pipeline of contract development and manufacturing orders.

Lodha (Macrotech Developers) reported its numbers, reflecting the continued momentum in the Indian real estate sector. CG Power, the electrical equipment manufacturer, also announced its Q1 performance.

KFin Technologies, a player in the financial services technology space, was among the other companies that reported on July 24.

The earnings season remains in full swing. Next week, markets will be watching heavyweights like Larsen & Toubro, Bharat Electronics Limited, Coal India, and Adani Enterprises as they are scheduled to report their quarterly numbers.
