GST Inspector Held After Alleged Workplace Harassment in Chennai

A Chennai-based Goods and Services Tax (GST) inspector was arrested on Thursday evening following allegations of harassing a female colleague, according to the Nungambakkam police. The arrest comes amid increasing scrutiny over workplace misconduct within government establishments across Tamil Nadu.

Details of the Complaint and Arrest

Police sources said the complaint was filed earlier this week by the victim, who works under the same department in the city. Based on her statement, the accused was taken into custody and produced before a local magistrate, who remanded him in judicial custody.

The investigation revealed a pattern of repeated inappropriate behaviour, including verbal misconduct and unwelcome advances during office hours. No physical altercation was reported, but colleagues confirmed that the environment had become increasingly tense prior to the complaint being escalated.

Broader Implications for Public Sector Workplaces

This incident adds to a growing list of misconduct cases emerging from government offices in Chennai and other metropolitan areas. Earlier this year, several departments underwent internal reviews following public complaints about lack of accountability and inadequate redressal mechanisms.

Officials familiar with the matter stated that while punitive action is being considered, the focus is also shifting towards strengthening internal reporting channels and sensitisation programmes for staff.

Administrative Response and Next Steps

The Commercial Tax Department, under which the GST inspector served, has initiated a departmental inquiry. Senior officials confirmed that the case will also be reviewed by the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) for disciplinary action.

In a statement released late evening, the department emphasised its zero-tolerance policy towards harassment and vowed to ensure safe and respectful workplaces for all employees. The case is now being monitored by higher authorities in both state and central offices.
