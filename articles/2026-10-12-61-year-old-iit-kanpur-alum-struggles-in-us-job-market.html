Stranded Abroad: The Plight of a Seasoned IITian

An IIT Kanpur alumnus, aged 61, has been jobless for over two years in the United States despite possessing two decades of professional experience. His story underscores growing concerns over age discrimination, visa limitations, and the precarious nature of late-career immigration for highly skilled Indian professionals.

Background and Circumstances

The individual, who relocated to the U.S. seeking better opportunities, now finds himself unable to secure employment commensurate with his qualifications. With dwindling savings and mounting living costs—especially rent in cities like Silicon Valley—he launched a GoFundMe campaign to sustain basic needs.

Challenges Facing Older Immigrant Professionals

For veterans in tech, especially those beyond their prime working years, re-entering the workforce post-layoff or after visa shifts becomes increasingly difficult. Employers often perceive older candidates as costly or less adaptable compared to younger counterparts commanding lower salaries.

  • Age Discrimination: Despite equal-opportunity laws, subtle biases persist in hiring practices favoring youth.
  • Visa Constraints: H-1B holders nearing green card stages face instability without employer sponsorship.
  • Skill Obsolescence: Rapid technological shifts demand continuous upskilling rarely pursued mid-career.
  • Cost of Living: Housing prices in major hubs make survival tough during extended unemployment periods.

Implications for Future Migrants

This case serves as a cautionary tale for aspiring Indian immigrants considering long-term settlement abroad. Families must evaluate contingency plans including repatriation strategies amid uncertain global economies and shifting immigration policies.

Policy and Community Response

While individual stories gain traction online, systemic change remains elusive. Advocacy groups argue stronger protections are needed for older immigrant workers, alongside mentorship programs aiding career pivots within India’s burgeoning startup ecosystem.
