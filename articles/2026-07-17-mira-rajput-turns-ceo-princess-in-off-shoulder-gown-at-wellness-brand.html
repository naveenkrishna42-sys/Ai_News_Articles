Mira Rajput Kapoor stepped into the spotlight on Saturday evening, not as a celebrity spouse but as an entrepreneur. She launched her own wellness brand at a curated event in Mumbai, marking her formal entry into the lifestyle and health space.

The launch drew a select gathering of family, friends and industry insiders. Among those present was her husband, actor Shahid Kapoor, who cheered from the sidelines as Mira took the stage to introduce her new venture.
