IDFC FIRST Bank has extended zero forex markup benefits to select credit cards, a structural shift for Indian travellers who have historically absorbed 2% to 3.5% in foreign transaction fees on every overseas swipe. For a family spending ₹3 lakh abroad, that mark-up alone costs ₹6,000 to ₹10,500—money that never converts into rewards, miles or savings. The bank's move puts it directly against premium travel cards from HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, ICICI Bank and SBI Card.

Why Zero Forex Markup Actually Matters

Forex markup is applied to the converted rupee value of any transaction settled in a foreign currency—hotel bills, airline tickets, overseas POS terminals and international e-commerce. On a ₹2 lakh annual overseas spend, a 3.5% markup costs ₹7,000; at zero markup, that amount stays with you. The benefit compounds for frequent travellers, NRI-linked families and professionals billing clients in USD or EUR.

The catch is eligibility. Zero markup usually rides on premium or co-branded variants with joining fees, minimum spend thresholds or specific reward structures. Compare the total cost of ownership, not just the headline fee waiver.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • Zero forex markup delivers the biggest absolute savings on high-value transactions like flights, hotels and overseas medical bills.
  • Premium travel cards often justify their fees through lounge access, milestone vouchers and transferable reward points—not just markup.
  • Always check whether the zero-markup benefit is capped, variant-specific or tied to a minimum annual spend.
  • ATM withdrawals abroad typically attract separate charges even on zero-markup cards.
  • Dynamic currency conversion at overseas terminals can override card benefits—always pay in the local currency.

Buyer's Verdict

For pure forex savings, IDFC FIRST's zero markup is the most direct win available to Indian travellers. For reward maximisation, Axis Atlas and HDFC Infinia remain stronger long-term plays. Match the card to your annual overseas spend: below ₹2 lakh, a zero-markup card wins outright; above ₹8-10 lakh, a premium rewards card with transfer partners usually delivers more value despite the markup.
