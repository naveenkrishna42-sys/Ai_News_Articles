Sharvari, Vedang Raina Reunite for Second Imtiaz Ali Film: Report

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sharvari Wagh and Vedang Raina to reunite for a new Imtiaz Ali film, sources claim.
  • The duo previously worked together on the unreleased project 'Main Wapas Aaunga'.
  • Imtiaz Ali's upcoming film is reportedly in its pre-production phase.

Bollywood's newest on-screen pair, Sharvari Wagh and Vedang Raina, are reportedly teaming up again. After working together on the upcoming film Main Wapas Aaunga, the two actors have been approached for another project directed by Imtiaz Ali, according to a report by NDTV.

The news, which broke on Wednesday, suggests that the filmmaker is keen to capitalise on the chemistry between the young stars. Neither Sharvari nor Vedang has officially confirmed the development, but industry insiders indicate discussions are at an advanced stage.

From 'Main Wapas Aaunga' to a New Collaboration

Sharvari and Vedang first came together for Main Wapas Aaunga, a film that has been generating buzz since its announcement. That project, also directed by Imtiaz Ali, remains unreleased, with no confirmed theatrical date yet.

Now, barely months after that shoot wrapped, the director is reportedly keen to bring the duo back. The new film is said to be a distinct project, not a sequel or spin-off. It is understood to be in early pre-production stages, with a script that has been in development for some time.

Sharvari's Rising Trajectory

Sharvari Wagh has been on a steady rise since her debut. She made an impact with Bunty Aur Babli 2 and later starred in the OTT hit The Fabulous Lives of Bollywood Wives. Her role in the web series Mismatched earned her a wide fanbase.

She has also been part of the Yash Raj Films spy universe, appearing in Pathaan in a cameo. A second film with Imtiaz Ali would further solidify her position as one of the most sought-after young actresses in the industry.

Vedang Raina's Breakout Run

Vedang Raina made his debut with the Netflix film The Archies, directed by Zoya Akhtar. Though the film received mixed reviews, his performance was widely praised. He followed it up with a role in Jigra, alongside Alia Bhatt.

His pairing with Sharvari in Main Wapas Aaunga was seen as a fresh casting choice. A second collaboration with Imtiaz Ali would give him a strong foothold in the industry, especially since Ali is known for launching and reviving careers with his unique storytelling.

What Imtiaz Ali Brings to the Table

Imtiaz Ali is known for films like Jab We Met, Rockstar, and Tamasha. His films often explore complex relationships and self-discovery, set against visually rich backdrops. He has a knack for extracting nuanced performances from his actors.

If the report holds true, this new film could be yet another character-driven love story. The director has been relatively quiet since the release of Amar Singh Chamkila in 2024, and fans are eager to see what he does next.

Official confirmation from the production house is awaited. For now, the buzz around this potential reunion is enough to keep cinephiles watching closely.
