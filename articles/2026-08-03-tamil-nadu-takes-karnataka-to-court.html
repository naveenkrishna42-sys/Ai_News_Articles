Tamil Nadu Takes Karnataka to Court

📅 2026-08-03 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tamil Nadu files petition in Supreme Court
  • Karnataka increases water release from Kabini dam
  • Farmers in Tamil Nadu stage protests over water scarcity

Cauvery Water Dispute Escalates

Tamil Nadu has moved the Supreme Court against Karnataka in the long-standing Cauvery water row. The petition seeks directions to Karnataka to release the stipulated amount of water to Tamil Nadu. The move comes amidst growing tensions between the two states over the sharing of the river's waters.

Background of the Dispute

The Cauvery river flows through both Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, and the two states have been locked in a dispute over the sharing of its waters for decades. The dispute has led to violent protests and clashes between the two states in the past. Recently, Karnataka increased the release of water from the Kabini dam, but Tamil Nadu says it is not enough.

Protests in Tamil Nadu

Farmers in Tamil Nadu have been staging protests over the water scarcity, with some even burying themselves in sand to draw attention to their plight. The protests have been peaceful so far, but the situation remains tense. The farmers are hoping for a sustained release of water from Karnataka to save their crops.

Political Developments

Meanwhile, Vijay, a prominent political leader, has deferred his visit to Karnataka after an appeal from Shivakumar, a Karnataka politician, to avoid escalating the tensions. The visit was scheduled to take place amidst the ongoing dispute, but has been put off for now.

According to reports, the Cauvery water is expected to reach Tamil Nadu by Tuesday, bringing relief to the farmers. However, the long-term solution to the dispute remains elusive, and the Supreme Court's decision will be closely watched.

What's Next

The Supreme Court is expected to hear Tamil Nadu's petition soon, and its decision will have significant implications for the dispute. The court's ruling will determine the course of the dispute and whether Karnataka will be directed to release more water to Tamil Nadu. The situation will continue to unfold in the coming days, with the farmers and politicians in both states waiting with bated breath for the outcome.
