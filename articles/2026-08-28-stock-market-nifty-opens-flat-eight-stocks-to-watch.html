Nifty Opens Flat, Market Hints at Negative Start

The Nifty 50 index opened flat on Friday, August 28, 2026, hinting at a potential negative start to the trading day. This follows a mixed performance in the previous trading session, with the index ending slightly lower. The market sentiment is currently bearish, with analysts cautioning about the possibility of further declines.

Analysts Recommend Eight Day Trading Stocks

Analysts at TradingView have recommended eight stocks for day trading on Friday, August 28, 2026. These stocks are considered to have high volatility and liquidity, making them suitable for short-term trading strategies. The recommended stocks are:

  • Reliance Industries
  • Tata Consultancy Services
  • HDFC Bank
  • Infosys
  • ICICI Bank
  • Bharti Airtel
  • Hindustan Unilever
  • State Bank of India

These stocks are expected to show significant price movements during the day, providing opportunities for traders to capitalize on intraday price fluctuations.

Broader Market Impact and Expert Opinion

The flat start to the Nifty index suggests that the market is in a state of uncertainty. This could be due to a variety of factors, including global economic conditions, geopolitical tensions, and domestic policy changes. Analysts are closely monitoring these factors to gauge the market's direction.

Expert opinion suggests that the market may continue to show volatility in the coming days. Traders are advised to stay cautious and use risk management strategies to mitigate potential losses.
