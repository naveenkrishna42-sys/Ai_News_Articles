Govinda’s Manager Questions Divorce Drama as Sunita Ahuja Withdraws Plea

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sunita Ahuja has formally withdrawn her divorce plea against actor Govinda.
  • Allegations surfaced regarding an alleged past relationship between Govinda and co-star Komal Rani.
  • The actor’s manager has publicly questioned the timing and motives behind the filing and subsequent withdrawal.

A Swift Withdrawal Ends Legal Speculation

Sunita Ahuja has formally withdrawn the divorce petition filed against her husband, Bollywood actor Govinda. The move comes amid a flurry of public allegations and media scrutiny that have dominated entertainment headlines over the past few days.

The withdrawal of the legal plea serves as an abrupt conclusion to what was shaping up to be a high-profile courtroom battle. However, the resolution of the legal matter has done little to quiet the surrounding chatter regarding the couple's personal life.

Manager Expresses Disappointment

Govinda’s long-time manager has voiced strong disapproval regarding how the situation was handled publicly. Speaking on the developments, the manager stated that the entire episode has left him deeply saddened.

The manager raised pointed questions regarding why such a sensitive domestic matter was brought to the public eye in the first place. He suggested that the airing of these grievances has caused unnecessary distress to those involved.

Allegations of Past Affairs

The controversy gained significant traction after claims surfaced that Govinda had once planned to marry his former co-star, Komal Rani. These allegations were reportedly discussed during a phone conversation between Sunita Ahuja and celebrity personality Rakhi Sawant.

The circulation of these claims has drawn various reactions from within the film industry. While some have sought to distance themselves from the drama, others have offered public commentary on the nature of celebrity marriages and the pressures that come with them.

Public and Industry Reaction

The narrative has prompted a wider discussion on how personal disputes involving public figures are managed. Industry peers, including Poonam Pandey, have weighed in on the character of the actor, attempting to temper the intensity of the affair allegations being leveled against him.

Despite the legal withdrawal, the public discourse remains active. The manager’s frustration reflects a growing sentiment that the private details of the couple's history were exploited for public consumption. Officials have not provided further details regarding the specific reasons behind the initial filing or the subsequent decision to drop the case.

Observers will now watch to see if the couple issues a joint statement to address the speculation or if they will continue to maintain silence to protect their remaining privacy.
