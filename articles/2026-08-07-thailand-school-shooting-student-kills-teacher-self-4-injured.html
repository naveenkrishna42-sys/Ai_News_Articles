Thailand School Shooting: Student Kills Teacher, Self; 4 Injured

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • A student opened fire at a school in Thailand, killing a teacher and injuring four others.
  • The shooter died by suicide after the attack.
  • Earlier reports indicate the suspect may have killed his grandparents before the school shooting.

Attack at Thai School Leaves Teacher Dead

A teacher was killed and four others were injured in a shooting at a school in Thailand on Friday, according to initial reports. The attacker, identified as a student at the institution, turned the weapon on himself and died at the scene.

The incident unfolded in the morning hours, sending waves of panic through the campus. Local authorities have cordoned off the area and launched an investigation into the sequence of events.

Suspect's Family Members Also Targeted

Early indications suggest the violence may have begun before the student reached the school. Multiple news agencies, citing police sources, have reported that the suspect's grandparents were also shot and killed. This would bring the total death toll to at least eight, including the shooter.

The details of the family killings remain murky, with officials yet to confirm the exact circumstances. Investigators are piecing together the timeline of the shooter's movements on the day of the attack.

Students Describe Chaos and Fear

Video footage circulating on social media shows students ducking under desks as gunshots ring out in the background. The harrowing clips capture the terror experienced by those inside the school during the rampage.

Witnesses described a sudden burst of gunfire that shattered the morning calm. Students and staff scrambled for cover, with many barricading themselves in classrooms until police arrived.

Police Investigation Underway

Thai police have not yet released a formal statement detailing the motive behind the attack. However, they have confirmed that the shooter acted alone. The weapon used in the incident has been recovered from the scene.

Authorities are also examining the shooter's background, including any history of mental health issues or prior grievances. The school has been closed for the day, and counseling services are being arranged for students and staff affected by the tragedy.

Rare Incident in Thailand

School shootings are relatively rare in Thailand, though the country has seen sporadic incidents of gun violence in public spaces. This attack has nonetheless reignited concerns about gun control and school safety in the kingdom.

Thai officials have expressed condolences to the families of the victims. The prime minister has been briefed on the situation, and an official statement is expected in the coming hours.

As the investigation continues, the focus will be on understanding what drove a student to commit such an act. The local community is in mourning, and questions are being raised about the adequacy of security measures in schools across the country.

The coming days will likely see calls for stricter background checks and better mental health support for students. For now, the immediate priority is to support the injured and the families of those who lost their lives in this senseless act of violence.
