Apple Stock Downgraded to Hold on iPhone 18 Pro Price Hike Concerns

📅 2026-08-01 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple's stock rating cut to 'hold' amid concerns over iPhone 18 Pro pricing.
  • Fresh leaks suggest a significant price jump for the Pro models at September launch.
  • Rumored features include a redesigned camera system and possible foldable debut.

Apple's Rating Slips to 'Hold'

Apple Inc. has been downgraded to a 'hold' rating by analysts, a move that comes as fresh reports warn of a substantial price increase for the upcoming iPhone 18 Pro. The cautious stance reflects growing unease about consumer appetite in key markets like India, where premium smartphone buyers are particularly price-sensitive.

The downgrade, reported by Yahoo Finance UK, arrives ahead of the expected September launch event. With inflation still pinching household budgets, a higher price tag on the flagship device could test the loyalty of even the most dedicated Apple enthusiasts.

iPhone 18 Pro Price Rumours Intensify

Fresh details have surfaced online suggesting the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max will carry heftier price tags than their predecessors. India Today reports that new price specifics have emerged just weeks before the official unveiling, though Apple has not confirmed any figures. Industry watchers speculate the increase could be anywhere from 10-15% in some regions, potentially pushing the Pro model past the Rs 1.5 lakh mark in India.

This would mark the third consecutive year of price hikes for the Pro lineup. Analysts point to rising component costs and the addition of advanced features as key drivers, but the move carries risk. "The Indian consumer is value-conscious," said a Mumbai-based tech analyst. "A steep hike without a compelling reason could push buyers toward older models or Android rivals."

What's Driving the Cost?

The rumored price bump is attributed to a suite of upgrades. Reports from NDTV highlight five features that appear credible, including a redesigned periscope camera system for improved optical zoom and a new titanium frame. Mashable adds that the iPhone 18 series is also expected to debut with a more powerful A19 Pro chip, built on a smaller 2-nanometer process, which is costlier to manufacture.

  • Revamped camera module with advanced zoom capabilities.
  • New titanium body for durability and lighter weight.
  • Possible introduction of a foldable iPhone variant alongside the 18 series.

The foldable iPhone debut, long rumored, has been a point of speculation. Moneycontrol notes that a foldable model could launch as early as 2026, potentially alongside the iPhone 18 lineup, but it is likely to command a premium far above even the Pro Max.

Market Reaction and Consumer Sentiment

The downgrade comes as Apple's stock has been volatile, with investors weighing supply chain constraints against strong services revenue growth. In India, Apple has seen record sales in recent years, particularly in the premium segment, but a price hike could slow that momentum. The company has also expanded local manufacturing to mitigate import duties, yet the Pro models are still largely imported, making them vulnerable to currency fluctuations.

Social media chatter in India reflects mixed feelings. Some users welcome the rumored camera upgrades, while others express frustration over the escalating cost of entry into the iPhone ecosystem. "At this rate, the iPhone will soon cost as much as a small car," one user posted on X, formerly Twitter.

What to Watch Next

All eyes are now on Apple's September event, expected to be held in the second week of the month. The company will need to justify the price hike with tangible improvements, or risk alienating its core user base. Investors, meanwhile, will scrutinize pre-order numbers in the weeks following the announcement to gauge whether the 'hold' rating was premature or prescient.
