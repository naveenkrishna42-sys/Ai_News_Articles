OnePlus N6x launch set for July 31, design and colour options revealed

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • OnePlus N6x will launch in India on July 31.
  • Teaser images show a flat display and a redesigned rectangular camera island.
  • Multiple colour options, including a green shade, have been revealed.
  • The design marks a significant departure from the OnePlus N6.
  • The phone is expected to feature 5G connectivity.

Official Launch Date and Teaser Campaign

OnePlus has confirmed that its upcoming N6x smartphone will launch in India on July 31. The company began teasing the device earlier this week, sharing official renders that reveal the phone's design and colour options for the first time.

The teaser campaign signals that the N6x will not be a minor refresh. Instead, it introduces a visual language that clearly sets it apart from the existing OnePlus N6.

Flat Display and Redesigned Camera Island

Official images show the N6x with a flat display, a departure from the curved screens seen on some recent OnePlus models. The front panel houses a centrally placed punch-hole cutout for the selfie camera.

The rear of the device features a rectangular camera module, a shift from the square or vertically aligned designs used on earlier N-series phones. The module protrudes slightly and houses multiple sensors and an LED flash.

The overall silhouette appears more squared off, with flat side rails that suggest a modern, industrial look. Volume and power buttons are placed on the right edge.

Colour Options and Finish

OnePlus has teased at least two colour variants of the N6x. A green shade, likely carrying a matte or frosted finish, has been featured prominently. A darker, possibly black or charcoal variant has also been shown.

The company has not yet confirmed the official colour names or whether additional options will be available at launch. Based on the teasers, the finishes appear to resist fingerprints, a common complaint with glossy glass backs.

What to Expect from the N6x

The 'x' suffix typically indicates a mid-cycle upgrade for OnePlus. However, the design overhaul suggests the N6x could bring more than just a spec bump. The phone is expected to support 5G connectivity, a standard feature in this price segment.

Details about the processor, display specifications, and battery capacity remain unconfirmed. OnePlus is expected to reveal full specifications closer to the launch date.

The N6x will likely slot below the flagship OnePlus 13 series in the company's lineup, targeting the upper mid-range segment where competition from Xiaomi, Realme, and Samsung is intense.

Industry watchers will be keen to see how OnePlus prices the device. The N6 was launched at around Rs 25,000, and the N6x is expected to stay in a similar bracket.

With the July 31 date locked in, OnePlus will likely ramp up its teaser campaign over the coming days, revealing more features and marketing messages.
