Apple's foldable iPhone could disrupt smartphone industry

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Foldable smartphone shipments expected to double to 36 million units by 2028
  • Apple's foldable iPhone could disrupt the smartphone industry
  • Challenges remain in terms of durability, battery life, and software optimization

Foldable smartphones on the rise

Foldable smartphones are set to become a significant part of the global smartphone market. According to Omdia, foldable smartphone shipments are expected to double to 36 million units by 2028. This growth is driven by the increasing demand for more compact and versatile devices.

Apple's foldable iPhone: A game-changer?

Apple's entry into the foldable smartphone market could be a game-changer. The company's premium brand and ecosystem could attract a new segment of users who value innovation and design. However, Apple will need to address several challenges to succeed in this market.

Challenges for Apple

Apple will need to focus on durability, battery life, and software optimization. The foldable form factor is still relatively new, and users are not yet familiar with its quirks. Apple will need to ensure that its foldable iPhone is as reliable and user-friendly as its traditional models.

Additionally, Apple will need to differentiate its foldable iPhone from other competitors. The market is already crowded with devices from Samsung, Google, and others. Apple will need to offer unique features and a seamless user experience to stand out.

Broader impact on the smartphone industry

The success of Apple's foldable iPhone could have a significant impact on the smartphone industry. It could accelerate the adoption of foldable technology and push other companies to innovate. However, it could also lead to increased competition and price wars.

For consumers, foldable smartphones could offer a more versatile and compact device. However, they may also come with a higher price tag. Consumers will need to weigh the benefits against the costs before making a purchase.

Expert opinions and analysis

According to CNET, book-style foldables are winning over traditional flip phones. The book-style design offers a larger screen and a more stable form factor. Apple could benefit from this trend by adopting a book-style design for its foldable iPhone.

NBC News reports that foldable phones are entering a new and expensive era. The technology is still evolving, and users may need to pay a premium for the latest models. Apple will need to balance innovation with affordability to succeed in this market.
