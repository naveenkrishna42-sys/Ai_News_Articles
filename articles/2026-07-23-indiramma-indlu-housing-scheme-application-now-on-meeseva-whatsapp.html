Indiramma Indlu housing scheme application now on MeeSeva WhatsApp

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Indiramma Indlu application added to MeeSeva WhatsApp service.
  • Residents can now apply for housing scheme through a familiar platform.
  • Telangana government expands digital access to welfare schemes.
  • The move aims to reduce paperwork and improve convenience.
  • Service is part of broader MeeSeva digital initiative.

HYDERABAD: The Telangana government has made the application for the Indiramma Indlu housing scheme available on the MeeSeva WhatsApp service. This is part of a push to bring welfare schemes closer to residents through digital platforms they already use.

Launched earlier this year, the Indiramma Indlu scheme aims to provide housing to eligible families across the state. The move to add it to MeeSeva WhatsApp is expected to simplify the application process, cutting down on the need for physical visits to government offices.

How the service works

Residents can now access the application form by sending a message to the MeeSeva WhatsApp number. The bot will guide users through the steps, allowing them to fill in details and upload necessary documents directly from their phones.

Officials say this reduces the time and effort involved. The platform also allows users to track the status of their application, bringing transparency to the process.

Expanding digital access

The MeeSeva WhatsApp service already handles several other government services, from certificate applications to bill payments. Adding the Indiramma Indlu scheme to this list is part of a broader strategy to digitise public welfare delivery.

State IT officials noted that WhatsApp has a large user base in Telangana, making it a practical channel for reaching rural and urban populations alike. The government has not yet disclosed the number of applications received since the feature went live.

What the scheme offers

Under Indiramma Indlu, eligible beneficiaries receive financial assistance to construct or purchase a house. The scheme targets families living in kutcha houses or those without any shelter.

Priority is given to scheduled castes, scheduled tribes, backward classes, and other economically weaker sections. The government has allocated a significant budget for the scheme in the current financial year, though exact figures were not provided in the announcement.

Officials have urged residents to use the WhatsApp service to avoid middlemen and potential fraud. They also warned against sharing personal information with unauthorised agents claiming to facilitate the application.

Looking ahead

The government plans to add more welfare schemes to the MeeSeva WhatsApp platform in the coming months. Residents can expect updates on other housing and social security programmes to be integrated soon.
