Technical Specifications and Real‑World Performance

The charger features two USB‑C Power Delivery 3.0 ports and one USB‑A port supporting Qualcomm Quick Charge 3.0. Total output is capped at 30 W, which the internal power‑management IC distributes dynamically based on connected devices. When a single USB‑C port is used, it can deliver up to 30 W (5 V/3 A, 9 V/3 A, 12 V/2.5 A, 15 V/2 A, 20 V/1.5 A). With two devices plugged in, the output splits—for example, 15 W each on the USB‑C ports or 10 W on USB‑C and 5 W on USB‑A. Thermal testing shows the unit stays below 45 °C under continuous 30 W load, thanks to its gallium‑nitride (GaN)‑derived efficiency design.

Compatibility

Both USB‑C ports support the full PD 3.0 protocol, enabling fast charging for recent iPhones (8 V/1.5 A‑style), Android flagships, iPad Pro, and MacBook Air. The USB‑A port can quick‑charge older Android devices and accessories like Bluetooth headsets. The charger’s input accepts 100‑240 V AC at 50‑60 Hz, making it suitable for international travel with a simple plug adapter.
