Market Overview for October 1, 2026

On the morning of October 1, 2026, the Sensex opened around 66,200 points while the Nifty 50 hovered near 19,750. Early trade reflected a mixed sentiment as global cues leaned slightly positive but domestic investors awaited clarity on upcoming policy minutes. Trading volumes were moderate, indicating a wait‑and‑watch stance among institutional participants.

Gift Nifty as a Leading Indicator

The SGX Nifty (Gift Nifty) futures contract traded at 19,780, a premium of roughly 30 points over the spot Nifty 50. This suggested that overseas investors expected a modest upside for the Indian index once the local market opened. Analysts noted that the Gift Nifty often mirrors overnight developments in Asian markets and US futures, making it a useful short‑term gauge.

US Inflation Data and Its Ripple Effect

The US Bureau of Labor Statistics released the September CPI figure, showing a year‑on‑year increase of 3.2%, down from 3.7% in August. Core inflation, excluding food and energy, eased to 4.0%. The softer print reduced fears of an aggressive Federal Reserve rate hike, lifting sentiment in global equity markets. For India, the data lessened the likelihood of sudden capital outflows driven by US rate expectations.

Oil Prices and Their Impact on Inflation

Brent crude settled at $84.10 per barrel after a session of range‑bound trading. OPEC+ maintained its current output policy, while US shale production showed modest growth. The price level kept imported inflation pressures in check, supporting the RBI’s stance of holding the repo rate at 6.5% for the time being. Analysts warned that any geopolitical shock could quickly shift the balance.

Domestic Earnings and Policy Outlook

Several large‑cap companies posted Q2 FY27 results that were broadly in line with estimates, with IT and pharmaceutical sectors showing modest upside. The RBI’s upcoming monetary policy meeting, scheduled for later in the month, remained a focal point for market participants. Forward guidance from the central bank would likely dictate the near‑term trajectory of bond yields and equity valuations.

Technical Levels to Watch

The Nifty 50 faced immediate resistance at 19,850 and support at 19,600. A break above the resistance could open the path toward the 20,000‑point psychological barrier, while a slip below support might trigger profit‑booking toward 19,400. Traders were advised to monitor volume spikes around these levels for confirmation of directional moves.
