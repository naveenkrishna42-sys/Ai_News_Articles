India Warns Pak on Kargil Diwas

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Rajnath Singh warns Pakistan on Kargil Diwas
  • No talks with Pakistan except on PoK
  • India and Pakistan tensions rise on Kargil Diwas

Kargil Diwas Warning

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh issued a sharp warning to Pakistan on Kargil Diwas, stating that any future aggression would invite a response beyond imagination. Singh also made it clear that India will not engage in talks with Pakistan except on the issue of Pakistan-occupied Kashmir (PoK).

No Talks Except on PoK

Singh's statement comes on the 27th anniversary of the Kargil War, which was fought between India and Pakistan in 1999. The defence minister's comments are seen as a strong message to Pakistan, which has been accused of supporting radicalisation and terrorism in the region. India has consistently maintained that it will not engage in talks with Pakistan unless the neighbouring country takes concrete steps to stop supporting terrorism.

Comparing India and Pakistan

Rajnath Singh also highlighted the difference between India and Pakistan, saying that while India is making data centres, Pakistan is creating radicalisation centres. This comparison is seen as a reflection of the two countries' priorities and values. Meanwhile, Prime Minister Narendra Modi paid tribute to the martyrs of the Kargil War, saying that the morale of Indian soldiers was greater than any challenge they faced during the conflict.

Tributes and Warnings

Other leaders, including Shivakumar, also paid tribute to the martyrs on Kargil Vijay Diwas. The day is observed to commemorate the victory of Indian forces in the Kargil War. As tensions between India and Pakistan continue to rise, the warnings issued by Rajnath Singh are seen as a clear message to the neighbouring country that India will not hesitate to take strong action if provoked.

Looking ahead, the situation between India and Pakistan is likely to remain tense, with both countries watching each other's moves closely. The international community will be watching the developments between the two nuclear-armed nations with great interest, as any escalation in tensions could have far-reaching consequences for regional and global security.
