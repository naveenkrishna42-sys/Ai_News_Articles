PM Modi's 137th Mann Ki Baat: A Vision for India's Future

Prime Minister Narendra Modi's 137th Mann Ki Baat radio address was a mix of urgent appeals and aspirational visions. The address, which aired on Sunday, August 30, 2026, covered a range of topics from public health to economic empowerment and space exploration.

Drug-free India by 2047: A National Call to Action

One of the most pressing issues Modi addressed was the scourge of drug abuse in India. He called for a drug-free India by 2047, emphasizing the need for a collective effort to combat this menace. 'Make India drug-free' was his clarion call to the nation. This is not just a health issue, but a societal one, with far-reaching implications for India's future.

The PM's appeal was not just a moral one, but also a practical one. He highlighted the economic burden of drug abuse, which is estimated to be in the range of 3-4% of India's GDP. This is a significant figure, and it underscores the need for urgent action.

Opportunities for Indian Women in the Space Industry

Modi also highlighted the opportunities for Indian women in the space industry. He mentioned the success of the Chandrayaan-3 mission, which was a significant achievement for India. He urged young Indian women to join the space industry, which is a rapidly growing field.

This is a significant development, as it reflects a broader trend of increased participation of women in the Indian space industry. The success of the Chandrayaan-3 mission is a testament to the potential of Indian women in the field of space exploration.

The PM SVANidhi Scheme: Empowering Women Entrepreneurs

Modi also highlighted the success of the PM SVANidhi scheme, which is providing 'new strength' to 35 lakh women entrepreneurs. This scheme, which was launched in 2020, provides collateral-free loans to women entrepreneurs. It has been a significant success, with the number of women entrepreneurs benefiting from the scheme increasing rapidly.
