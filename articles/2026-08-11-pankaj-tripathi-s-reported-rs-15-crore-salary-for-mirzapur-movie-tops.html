Pankaj Tripathi's Reported Rs 15 Crore Salary for Mirzapur Movie Tops Cast

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pankaj Tripathi's reported salary for Mirzapur The Movie is Rs 15 crore.
  • The figure surpasses what Ali Fazal and Divyenndu Sharma are said to earn.
  • The film's release date and official budget remain unconfirmed.

MUMBAI: The much-anticipated big-screen adaptation of the hit web series Mirzapur is already making headlines, and this time it's about money. According to a report by News18, Pankaj Tripathi, who plays the feared Kaleen Bhaiya, is reportedly taking home a hefty Rs 15 crore for the film. That figure reportedly puts him ahead of his co-stars Ali Fazal and Divyenndu Sharma in the pay pecking order.

The reported salary is a significant jump from what actors typically earn in streaming projects. It also signals the massive commercial expectations riding on the film, which is being produced by Excel Entertainment. While the production house has not officially commented on the numbers, the buzz around the pay cheques has added to the excitement among fans.

A Pay Scale That Reflects Stardom

Tripathi's reported fee is not just about his role in the series. Over the years, he has become one of the most dependable actors in Hindi cinema, with a string of hits across genres. His portrayal of the ruthless yet oddly philosophical Kaleen Bhaiya in the Amazon Prime Video series earned him a massive fan following, making him a bankable name for the film spin-off.

The reported Rs 15 crore fee places him in the league of top-tier character actors who command premium prices. It also underscores how streaming success has translated into theatrical clout for actors who were once considered supporting players. The film's makers are betting on his star power to draw audiences to theatres, a gamble that seems well-calculated given his track record.

Co-Stars and Their Reported Pay

Ali Fazal, who plays the ambitious Guddu Pandit, and Divyenndu Sharma, who portrays the volatile Phoolchand Tripathi, are reportedly earning less than Tripathi. While the exact figures for Fazal and Divyenndu have not been disclosed, industry insiders suggest that the gap is significant. This has sparked discussions about how the film's budget is being allocated.

The trio has been central to the show's success, and their on-screen dynamics are a major draw. However, the reported disparity in their salaries has led to chatter about contractual negotiations and star billing. Neither actor nor the production team has issued a statement clarifying the numbers, leaving room for speculation.

Why the Film Is a Big Deal

Mirzapur, which premiered in 2018, became a cultural phenomenon with its gritty storytelling and memorable dialogues. The series has run for three seasons, and the announcement of a feature film took fans by surprise. The movie is expected to continue the saga of power, revenge, and betrayal in the lawless town of Mirzapur.

The reported salaries reflect the franchise's immense popularity. A film starring the original cast, with Tripathi leading the charge, is likely to attract both loyal viewers and new audiences. The makers have not yet announced a release date, but production is believed to be underway in various locations across Uttar Pradesh.

Industry Reactions and Speculation

Trade analysts see Tripathi's reported fee as a positive sign for the film's scale. It suggests that the producers are sparing no expense to deliver a theatrical experience that matches the show's grandeur. However, some have questioned whether the budget allocation is skewed, given that the film's success will depend on the entire ensemble.

Fans, meanwhile, are more focused on what the movie will bring to the table. The series ended on a cliffhanger, and the film is expected to resolve several plot threads. With Tripathi reportedly being the highest-paid actor in the project, all eyes will be on his performance and the film's box-office performance.

As the project moves forward, more details about the cast, crew, and release plans are expected to emerge. For now, the reported salary figures have set the stage for what promises to be one of the most talked-about film releases in Indian cinema.
