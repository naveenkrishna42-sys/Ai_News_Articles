IIT Madras directs student body to delete pro-protest Instagram video

📅 2026-07-23 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • IIT Madras directed a student group to delete an Instagram video supporting Jantar Mantar protests.
  • The student body alleges pressure from the administration to remove the post and drop protest activities.
  • The institute has not officially commented on the specific reasons for the request.

Institute cites policy breach in video takedown order

Chennai: The Indian Institute of Technology Madras has asked a student body to remove an Instagram video that expressed support for the ongoing protests at Jantar Mantar in New Delhi. The video, posted on the group’s official account, showed solidarity with the demonstrators.

Student representatives confirmed receiving a formal communication from the institute’s administration directing them to delete the post. The directive cited violation of institutional policies regarding social media use by recognised student organisations.

Student body claims pressure to drop protest stance

In response, the student body alleged that the administration pressured them not just to remove the video but also to refrain from any further protest-related activities. “We were told to delete the post and drop the protest,” a member said on condition of anonymity.

The students claim the move stifles their right to express solidarity with a legitimate cause. The Jantar Mantar protests, which have drawn participants from across the country, centre on demands related to reservation policies and educational reforms.

Administration yet to offer detailed explanation

IIT Madras has not issued an official statement detailing why the video was deemed objectionable. Sources within the institute suggest the administration views the post as a potential violation of guidelines that prohibit student bodies from taking political stances.

The lack of clarity has led to speculation among students and faculty. Some argue the directive is an overreach, while others maintain that student groups must adhere to the code of conduct governing their activities on campus.

Broader context of campus and protest dynamics

The incident comes amid heightened sensitivity around student activism in Indian higher education institutions. Several universities have faced similar controversies in recent months over social media posts supporting or opposing various social movements.

IIT Madras, like other premier institutes, has its own set of rules for student bodies. These typically require prior approval for any public communication that could be seen as representing the institute’s official position. The student group’s video did not carry such a disclaimer.

Legal and procedural questions remain

Legal experts point out that while institutes can regulate official channels, the line between permissible expression and policy violation is often blurred. The student body is reportedly considering a formal appeal to the institute’s grievance committee.

No further action has been taken against the students as of now. The video remains unavailable on the group’s page, though it has been circulated widely on other platforms.

The coming weeks will likely see the institute clarify its social media policy for student organisations. Whether the student body pursues the matter through official channels or escalates it to a public debate remains to be seen.
