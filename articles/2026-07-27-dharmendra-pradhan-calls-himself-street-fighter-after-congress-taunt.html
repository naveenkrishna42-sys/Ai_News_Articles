Dharmendra Pradhan Calls Himself 'Street Fighter' After Congress Taunt

📅 2026-07-27 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Dharmendra Pradhan responded to a Congress MP's 'it happens' remark with 'I'm a street fighter'.
  • The exchange took place in Parliament after Pradhan's resignation and re-induction into the cabinet.
  • BJP MPs gave Pradhan a rousing welcome, while Congress leaders expressed anger over the episode.

Pradhan's Fiery Retort

Union Minister Dharmendra Pradhan on Monday described himself as a 'street fighter' after a Congress MP made a dismissive remark about his recent political turmoil. The exchange took place in Parliament as Pradhan returned to the Lok Sabha following his brief resignation and re-induction into the Union Cabinet.

A Congress MP reportedly said 'it happens' in a casual tone when Pradhan's name was mentioned. The minister did not let the comment pass. 'I'm a street fighter,' Pradhan shot back, drawing cheers from BJP benches.

Raucous Welcome in Parliament

The former education minister received a thunderous welcome from BJP MPs as he entered the House. Slogans of 'Dharmendra Pradhan Zindabad' echoed through the chamber, with party members thumping desks in support. The scene stood in stark contrast to the Congress benches, where several MPs were visibly fuming.

Opposition leaders questioned the circumstances around Pradhan's resignation and subsequent return. They argued the episode raised serious questions about the government's accountability. Congress MPs accused the ruling party of treating constitutional positions with casualness.

Background of the Resignation

Pradhan resigned from the cabinet last week under a cloud of controversy. While the government maintained it was a routine administrative reshuffle, critics pointed to mounting pressure over policy decisions during his tenure as education minister. The minister was later re-inducted into the cabinet, retaining his portfolio.

The BJP leadership has stood firmly behind Pradhan. Party MPs have portrayed him as a victim of political vendetta. The Congress, however, has demanded a full explanation, calling the entire affair a 'cover-up'.

Mixed Reactions Across Aisle

Several BJP MPs welcomed Pradhan back with warm gestures. 'He is a grassroots leader who understands the pulse of the nation,' said a senior BJP MP on condition of anonymity. Meanwhile, a Congress MP countered, 'This is not about street fighting. This is about the integrity of the government.'

Pradhan himself has remained defiant. In brief remarks to reporters, he said he would continue to serve the people with full dedication. His 'street fighter' comment has already become a talking point on social media, with supporters and detractors debating its appropriateness.

What Comes Next

Pradhan is expected to make a detailed statement in Parliament later this week. The opposition is likely to press for a debate on the circumstances of his resignation and re-induction. All eyes are on how the government navigates this political flashpoint in the coming days.
