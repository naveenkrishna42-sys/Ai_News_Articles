Penguin India denies publishing Sonia Gandhi memoir

📅 2026-08-28 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Penguin Random House India denies publishing Sonia Gandhi's memoir
  • Controversy surrounds claims of biased content targeting PM Modi, RSS, China
  • Publisher insists on editorial independence and fact-checking process

Publisher Denies Publishing Memoir

Penguin Random House India has firmly denied any involvement in publishing Sonia Gandhi's memoir, stating that the book was not published by their company. The controversy surrounding the memoir, which reportedly contains critical remarks about Prime Minister Narendra Modi, the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS), and China, has sparked widespread debate.

Controversy Over Content

The memoir has been the subject of intense scrutiny, with claims that it contains biased and defamatory content. The publisher has maintained that the book underwent a thorough fact-checking process and that the content reflects the author's personal views rather than the publisher's stance.

Publisher's Stand

In a statement, Penguin Random House India emphasized its commitment to editorial independence and the importance of fact-checking. The publisher has stated that it does not endorse the content of the memoir and has no control over the author's personal views. The company has also denied any involvement in the editing or publishing process of the book.

Broader Impact

The controversy over the memoir has raised questions about the role of publishers in the dissemination of information and the potential for bias in published content. The incident has also highlighted the need for a robust fact-checking process to ensure the accuracy and fairness of published material.

  • Controversy surrounds claims of biased content targeting PM Modi, RSS, China
  • Publisher insists on editorial independence and fact-checking process
