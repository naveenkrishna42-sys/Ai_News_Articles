NEET Paper Leak: Govt Readies for Parliament Discussion on Day 3

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kiren Rijiju confirms government ready for discussion on NEET-UG paper leak
  • KC Venugopal moves adjournment motion in Lok Sabha, seeks Pradhan's statement
  • Opposition plans to protest and demand resignation of Dharmendra Pradhan

Government Signals Openness to Debate

On the third day of the Parliament Monsoon Session, the government indicated it is prepared to discuss the NEET-UG paper leak issue. Union Minister Kiren Rijiju stated that the government is ready for a discussion. Sources also confirmed that the Centre is open to a debate, with the Prime Minister reportedly calling the paper leak a 'ghor paap' (heinous sin).

The move comes amid escalating pressure from the Opposition, which has been demanding a thorough probe and accountability. The government's willingness to engage in a structured discussion marks a shift from earlier resistance.

Opposition Pushes for Adjournment Motion

Congress leader KC Venugopal moved an adjournment motion in the Lok Sabha, seeking a statement from Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan on the paper leaks. The Opposition has been relentless in its demand for Pradhan's resignation, blaming his ministry for the security lapse that compromised the integrity of the medical entrance exam.

The adjournment motion is a procedural tool to force a debate on an urgent matter. The Opposition's strategy appears to be aimed at cornering the government on the issue, which has sparked outrage among students and parents across the country.

Protest Plans and Political Fallout

The Opposition is expected to stage a protest at Parliament, demanding Pradhan's resignation. The paper leak, which came to light earlier this year, has raised serious questions about the examination process and the government's ability to conduct fair entrance tests.

The issue has become a major flashpoint in the session, with both houses likely to witness disruptions. The government, while agreeing to a discussion, has not yet indicated any change in its stance on Pradhan's resignation. Officials have not confirmed whether the minister himself will respond in the House.

The NEET-UG scandal has had a cascading effect, with the Supreme Court also taking cognizance of the matter. The court has sought a detailed report from the Central Bureau of Investigation, which is probing the leak.

What Happens Next

The Lok Sabha is expected to take up the adjournment motion later in the day. The government's response and the Opposition's next steps will determine whether the session can proceed with its scheduled legislative business. All eyes are on the floor of the House as the debate unfolds.
