Bigg Boss 20: Salman Khan's show to premiere on September 6, teaser out

Key points
  • Bigg Boss 20 will premiere on September 6, 2026, with Salman Khan returning as host.
  • The first teaser, unveiled by Salman Khan, hints at a 'Karan-Arjun' angle, leading to fan speculation about Shah Rukh Khan's possible involvement.

A landmark season takes shape

The countdown for Bigg Boss 20 has officially begun. Salman Khan, the face of the reality juggernaut, unveiled the first teaser on Tuesday, confirming that the show will premiere on September 6. This season is being billed as a landmark one, and the early buzz suggests the makers are pulling out all stops.

The teaser itself is classic Bigg Boss — dramatic lighting, the iconic house, and Salman in his element. But it is the hint of a 'Karan-Arjun' twist that has set social media abuzz. The reference, a nod to the 1997 blockbuster starring Salman and Shah Rukh Khan, has fans wondering if the latter might make a special appearance or even co-host.

The Karan-Arjun speculation

Indian Express reported that the teaser has sparked intense speculation. Fans are dissecting every frame, trying to decipher if the 'Karan-Arjun' angle is a metaphor for two contestants, a theme for the season, or something more direct — like an SRK collaboration. Neither Salman nor the channel has confirmed any details, leaving room for rampant guessing.

SRK and Salman's on-screen chemistry in Karan Arjun remains legendary. If the show does bring them together, it would be a reunion that could break the internet. However, sources close to the production have remained tight-lipped, and officials have not yet confirmed any guest appearances.

Fan demand: Yogesh and Akanksha

Meanwhile, a parallel conversation is trending. Fans are vocally demanding that Yogesh and Akanksha, popular figures from the reality TV circuit, be included in the lineup. Their chemistry and individual fan bases have made them a favourite talking point ahead of the premiere.

The demand has grown loud enough that it is trending on social media platforms. Whether the casting team has taken note is unclear, but the enthusiasm underscores the anticipation for this season. Bigg Boss has always thrived on a mix of known faces and fresh personalities, and the audience is clearly invested in who walks through the doors this time.

What to expect from Bigg Boss 20

With the premiere less than a month away, the promotional machinery is expected to kick into high gear. The show, which has run for nearly two decades, has reinvented itself with each season — new twists, tougher tasks, and ever-evolving drama. Season 20 is expected to be no different, and if the initial teaser is anything to go by, the makers are aiming for spectacle.

Salman Khan's return as host was never in doubt. His presence has been a constant since season 4, and his opening monologue, often a blend of wit and warning, is a ritual viewers look forward to. The teaser also hints at a grander set design, though the network has not shared official images yet.

The coming weeks will reveal more about the contestants, the house layout, and any special surprises. Fans are already counting days to September 6, and the speculation around the 'Karan-Arjun' twist will only grow until the premiere night. All eyes are now on the makers to see if they turn the rumour into reality.
