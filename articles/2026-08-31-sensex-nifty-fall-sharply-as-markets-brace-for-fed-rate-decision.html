Indian Stock Markets Open Lower Amid Global Volatility

Indian stock markets opened lower on Monday, with the Sensex declining 226 points to 77,028 and the Nifty dropping 120 points to 24,053. The early declines were driven by global cues, as investors awaited the Federal Reserve's rate decision later in the day.

Global Cues Dominate Early Trading

The early declines in Indian markets were influenced by global trends, particularly the ongoing volatility in Asian markets. Investors were also watching closely for any developments related to the Fed's monetary policy, which is expected to have significant implications for global and domestic markets.

Sectoral Analysis Shows Tech and Financials Leading Declines

Sectoral analysis revealed that the technology and financial sectors were leading the declines. The Nifty IT index fell by 1.5%, while the Nifty Bank index declined by 1.2%. The decline in the financial sector was partly attributed to concerns over rising interest rates and their potential impact on banking stocks.
