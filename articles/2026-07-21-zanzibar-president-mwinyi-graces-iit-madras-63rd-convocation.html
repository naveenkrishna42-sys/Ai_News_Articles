Zanzibar President Mwinyi graces IIT Madras 63rd convocation

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Dr. Hussein Ali Mwinyi, President of Zanzibar, attended IIT Madras' 63rd convocation as chief guest.
  • He conferred degrees upon graduating students and delivered the convocation address.
  • The event highlighted growing academic ties between India and Zanzibar.

Zanzibar leader at IIT Madras

His Excellency Dr. Hussein Ali Mwinyi, President of Zanzibar and Chairman of the Revolutionary Council, graced the 63rd convocation of the Indian Institute of Technology Madras. He was the chief guest at the ceremony held on the institute’s campus.

Dr. Mwinyi conferred degrees upon the graduating batch of 2026. The event marked a significant moment for the institute, which has a growing international profile. The President's presence underscored the deepening ties between India and Zanzibar in education and technology.

Convocation highlights

The ceremony saw thousands of students receive their degrees across undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes. Gold medals were awarded to top performers in various disciplines. The institute's director presented the annual report, detailing research achievements and new initiatives.

Dr. Mwinyi addressed the gathering, congratulating the graduates on their hard work. He spoke about the role of technology in shaping the future of Africa and India. The President also highlighted opportunities for collaboration between IIT Madras and Zanzibari institutions.

Strengthening India-Africa ties

The visit of the Zanzibar President is part of a broader push for academic exchanges between India and African nations. IIT Madras has been actively engaging with partners in Africa through research projects and student exchanges. The convocation served as a platform to showcase these efforts.

Officials from both sides expressed optimism about future cooperation. The institute has previously hosted delegations from several African countries. The presence of a head of state at the convocation signals the high priority placed on these relationships.

The 63rd convocation of IIT Madras will be remembered for its diplomatic significance. As graduates step out into the world, the ties forged during this event may open new doors for collaboration. What remains to be seen is how quickly the proposed academic partnerships between IIT Madras and Zanzibar take concrete shape.
