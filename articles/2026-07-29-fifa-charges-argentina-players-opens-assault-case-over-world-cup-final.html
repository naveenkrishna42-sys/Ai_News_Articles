FIFA Charges Argentina Players, Opens Assault Case Over World Cup Final

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • FIFA opens disciplinary proceedings against Argentina players over World Cup final incidents
  • Leandro Paredes faces three assault charges related to post-match clashes
  • Argentina also charged over 'Las Malvinas' banner displayed during celebrations

FIFA has opened disciplinary proceedings against three Argentina players following the chaotic scenes that marred the end of the 2022 World Cup final in Qatar. The world football body confirmed the move on Wednesday, with midfielder Leandro Paredes facing three separate assault charges.

Assault charges against Paredes

Leandro Paredes, who came on as a substitute during the final against France, now faces three counts of assault. The charges stem from incidents that erupted after Argentina's penalty shootout victory at Lusail Stadium.

FIFA's disciplinary committee has not released the full details of the alleged assaults. But reports suggest the charges relate to physical confrontations involving Paredes with opposition players and staff in the immediate aftermath of the final whistle.

The match, which Argentina won 4-2 on penalties after a 3-3 draw, saw tempers flare both on the pitch and in the tunnel area. Television footage from the night showed Paredes involved in several heated exchanges.

Falklands banner controversy

Beyond the assault charges, FIFA has also charged the Argentine Football Association over a banner displayed by players during their victory celebrations. The banner referenced the Falkland Islands, which Argentina calls Las Malvinas.

The display has stirred political tensions. The islands remain a sensitive subject between Argentina and the United Kingdom, which controls the territory. FIFA's disciplinary code prohibits political statements at official events.

The charge adds a layer of complexity to the case. FIFA is treating the banner as a separate breach alongside the physical altercations involving the players.

Disciplinary proceedings underway

FIFA has not named the other two players facing charges. The governing body said only that disciplinary proceedings have been opened against three members of Argentina's squad.

The players have been given a deadline to respond to the allegations. FIFA's disciplinary committee will then review the evidence before issuing any sanctions. Potential penalties range from fines to multi-match suspensions.

Argentina's World Cup triumph has been overshadowed by these developments. The team's conduct during and after the final has drawn criticism from several quarters, including former players and officials.

FIFA's decision to pursue the case comes after a lengthy review of match reports and video footage. The governing body has faced pressure to act decisively to maintain discipline at international tournaments.

What happens next depends on the players' responses and the disciplinary committee's findings. If found guilty, the Argentina players could face bans from future international matches. FIFA is expected to announce a verdict in the coming weeks.
