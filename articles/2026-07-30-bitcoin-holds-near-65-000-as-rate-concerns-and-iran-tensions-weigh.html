Bitcoin Holds Near $65,000 as Rate Concerns and Iran Tensions Weigh

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin is hovering near the $65,000 mark, showing marginal gains amid mixed signals from global markets.
  • Investors are balancing expectations of a Federal Reserve rate cut against heightened geopolitical tensions involving Iran.
  • All eyes are on Strategy's upcoming quarterly earnings report, which could influence crypto market direction.

Bitcoin edged up on Thursday, trading just below the $65,000 level, as the cryptocurrency market remained caught between two powerful forces: the prospect of lower interest rates and escalating geopolitical uncertainty linked to Iran.

The world's largest digital asset was last seen changing hands near $64,800, a modest gain of about 0.5% over the previous 24 hours. The move came after a period of sideways action, with traders reluctant to place big bets ahead of a crucial earnings report from Strategy, the corporate bitcoin holder formerly known as MicroStrategy.

Rate Cut Expectations Offer Support

Bitcoin's recent resilience has been partly underpinned by growing expectations that the Federal Reserve will begin cutting interest rates later this year. Lower rates tend to weaken the US dollar and boost demand for alternative assets like cryptocurrencies.

Markets are currently pricing in a roughly 70% chance of a quarter-point cut at the Fed's September meeting, according to CME Group data. That sentiment has helped Bitcoin recover from a dip below $62,000 earlier this month.

However, the optimism around monetary policy is being tempered by fresh worries about stability in the Middle East. Reports of heightened military activity involving Iran have pushed investors toward traditional safe havens like gold, while keeping risk assets including crypto in check.

Geopolitical Jitters Cap Gains

The Iran factor has added a layer of unpredictability to an already cautious market. Traders are warily watching for any escalation that could trigger a broader sell-off across risk assets.

Historically, Bitcoin has not always behaved like a classic risk-on asset during geopolitical shocks. In some past instances, it has dropped sharply alongside equities; in others, it has held steady as investors sought alternatives to fiat currencies in regions under stress. The current environment appears to be testing that dual nature.

Analysts note that while the immediate impact of Iran-related headlines has been muted, a major escalation could quickly change the calculus. For now, the market is in a holding pattern.

Strategy Earnings in Focus

The spotlight this week is firmly on Strategy, the software company turned bitcoin treasury play. The firm, which holds more than 200,000 bitcoins on its balance sheet, is set to report quarterly earnings after the closing bell.

Investors will be scrutinising not just the company's core software revenue but also any updates on its bitcoin buying strategy. Strategy has been one of the most vocal corporate advocates for bitcoin, and its continued accumulation has been a key factor in the asset's price floor.

A strong earnings beat or a fresh announcement of additional bitcoin purchases could provide the spark needed to push prices above $65,000. Conversely, any disappointment could see the cryptocurrency test support at lower levels.

Trading volumes have been relatively subdued in the run-up to the report, suggesting that many market participants are waiting on the sidelines for a clearer signal.

What to watch next: Strategy's earnings call later today, and any fresh headlines out of Iran over the weekend. Either could break Bitcoin out of its current range.
