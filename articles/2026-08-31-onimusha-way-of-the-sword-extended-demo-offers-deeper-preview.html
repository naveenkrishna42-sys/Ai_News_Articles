Onimusha: Way of the Sword - Extended Demo Offers Deeper Preview

Capcom's Onimusha Resurgence

The wait is over for fans of the Onimusha series, as Capcom has announced an extended demo for Onimusha: Way of the Sword, arriving on Sept 15. This follows the initial demo's positive reception and aims to provide a more comprehensive preview before the game's Oct 25 release.

Combat: A Soulslike Standout

Critics have commended the game's combat, which has been successfully adapted to fit the modern Soulslike era. The challenging yet satisfying gameplay mechanism is a standout feature, according to reviewers from IGN and Wccftech.

Formulaic Gameplay: A Double-Edged Sword

While critics are generally positive about the combat, some have noted a lack of novelty and repetition in the gameplay. CNET and VICE reviewers pointed out that the endless fights and formulaic level design might tire players, despite the engaging mechanics.

Extended Demo: What to Expect

The extended demo is an opportunity for players to experience more of the game's systems and story before making a purchase decision. It will be available on all platforms - PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.

Deluxe Edition: A Closer Look

The Onimusha: Way of the Sword Deluxe Edition has been detailed, offering bonus content such as an artbook, soundtrack, and in-game items. The table below summarizes the deluxe edition bonuses:

BonusDescription
ArtbookA digital artbook featuring concept art and designs from the game.
SoundtrackA collection of tracks composed by legendary musician Yugo Kanno.
