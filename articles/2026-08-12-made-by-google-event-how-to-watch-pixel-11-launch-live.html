Made by Google event: How to watch Pixel 11 launch live

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Made by Google event scheduled for today, August 12, 2026.
  • Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold expected.
  • Leaks suggest a 10.8MP telephoto camera and new HiLight color options.
  • Indian pricing details may be revealed during the event.

When and where to watch

Google's annual hardware showcase, the Made by Google event, is scheduled for today, August 12, 2026. The company will stream the event live on its official YouTube channel and the Google Store website. The keynote is expected to begin at 10:00 AM PT (10:30 PM IST), though viewers should check local listings for exact timings.

For those in India, the stream will be accessible on the same platforms. Google typically provides a dedicated landing page for the event, offering a countdown timer and direct link to the live feed. Viewers can also follow along on Google's official social media handles for real-time updates.

What to expect from the Pixel 11 lineup

The event is widely expected to feature four new models: the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. This mirrors last year's structure, with a standard model, two pro-tier variants, and a foldable.

Leaks and reports from multiple tech outlets suggest significant camera upgrades. One notable claim is a 10.8MP telephoto lens, which could improve zoom capabilities. However, the same leaks indicate that Google may have dropped the RGB notification LED, a feature that some users had hoped would return.

HiLight color options

Google Contacts app has reportedly revealed the color options for the Pixel 11 HiLight, a feature that might be tied to the camera or display. While specific shades have not been confirmed, the leak points to a fresh palette for the new devices. This has generated curiosity among Pixel enthusiasts who value distinctive finishes.

Pricing hints for India

A separate report from Gizbot claims that Google Pixel 11 series pricing in India has been revealed hours before the launch. The report suggests possible price hikes across the lineup, citing rising component costs and import duties. However, official pricing will only be confirmed during the event.

Historically, Google has positioned Pixel devices in the premium segment in India, competing directly with Samsung's Galaxy S series and Apple's iPhone. If the leaked prices hold, the Pixel 11 series may see a modest increase over the Pixel 10 lineup.

What else could be announced

Beyond the Pixel 11 phones, the Made by Google event is likely to feature updates on the Tensor chip, which powers the devices. Google may also showcase new features in Android 16, including AI-driven tools and enhanced integration with the Google ecosystem.

Rumors of a new Pixel Watch or Pixel Buds have also surfaced, though these remain unconfirmed. Google has used past events to refresh its accessories lineup, so a surprise announcement cannot be ruled out.

As the event unfolds, expect detailed hands-on coverage and benchmark tests from tech reviewers. For now, the live stream is the best way to catch all announcements as they happen.

Following the event, Google will likely roll out pre-orders in select markets, with India expected to be among the first to receive the devices. The official pricing and availability for the Indian market should be clarified during the keynote.
