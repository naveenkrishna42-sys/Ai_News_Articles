Xiaomi Expands Indian Power Bank Lineup with Three New Models

Xiaomi has launched a trio of power banks in India aimed at catering to different user needs. The new lineup includes the ultra-slim Xiaomi Ultra Slim, the feature-packed Xiaomi Power Bank 20000, and the budget-friendly Xiaomi Power Bank 10000. In this article, we'll delve into the key specifications, prices, and practical implications of each model.

1. Xiaomi Ultra Slim: Pint-Sized Power

The Xiaomi Ultra Slim is the standout offering in terms of portability. It packs a 5000mAh battery but weighs a mere 98 grams, making it incredibly lightweight. Powering up from empty takes around 3.5 hours via USB-C. 2. Xiaomi Power Bank 20000: High-Capacity Beast

The Xiaomi Power Bank 20000 is designed for users who need ample power on the go. It features a large 20000mAh lithium-ion battery, allowing for multiple full phone charges. The device supports 165W fast charging, refilling the battery in just 1.7 hours. 3. Xiaomi Power Bank 10000: Affordable Power

The Xiaomi Power Bank 10000 is an affordable option for those requiring additional power without breaking the bank. Its 10000mAh battery provides around two full phone charges.
