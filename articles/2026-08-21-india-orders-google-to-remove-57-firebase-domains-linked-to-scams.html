Indian Government Orders Google to Remove 57 Firebase Domains

The Indian government has issued an order to Google to remove 57 Firebase domains that were being used in cyber scams. The order comes after a pattern of misuse of Google Firebase by criminals was spotted.

Pattern of Misuse of Google Firebase

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of accounts involved, but the pattern of misuse of Google Firebase by criminals has been a concern for some time. The domains were being used to impersonate bank websites in cyber scams, which has been a growing problem in India.

Impact on Cyber Scams

The removal of these domains is expected to have a significant impact on cyber scams in India. The domains were being used to trick users into entering their personal and financial information, which is then used by criminals for fraudulent activities.

Government's Role in Cracking Down on Cyber Scams

The Indian government has been increasingly focusing on cracking down on cyber scams in recent times. This order is a part of the government's efforts to create a safer digital environment for its citizens.

What Happens Next?

Google is expected to comply with the order and remove the specified Firebase domains. The Indian government will continue to monitor the situation and take necessary actions to prevent the misuse of such platforms in the future.
