Spider-Man: Brand New Day swings to Rs 125 crore in India in 2 days

📅 2026-08-01 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day collects Rs 125 crore in India in two days.
  • Film sets U.S. records with $167 million opening day and $72 million in previews.
  • Tom Holland starrer beats Avengers: Endgame's preview record.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day, starring Tom Holland, has swung to a spectacular Rs 125 crore at the Indian box office in just two days. The film, which released on Friday, has taken the country by storm, with collections showing no signs of slowing down.

Trade analysts suggest the movie is on a record-breaking spree, with Day 2 figures touching Rs 131 crore and counting, according to early estimates. The numbers have surpassed all expectations, making it one of the fastest-grossing Hollywood releases in India.

A Record-Breaking Start in the U.S.

The film's success isn't limited to India. In the United States, Spider-Man: Brand New Day has set a new benchmark with a staggering $167 million opening day. This includes $72 million from previews alone, beating the previous record held by Avengers: Endgame.

Industry experts are calling this a historic moment for superhero cinema. The film's ability to draw audiences back to theatres in such massive numbers signals a strong revival for the box office.

What's Driving the Hype?

Tom Holland's portrayal of Peter Parker continues to resonate with fans, and this installment adds a fresh layer to the character's journey. Reviews have praised the film for addressing young people's anxieties within the usual superhero spectacle, giving it a deeper emotional core.

The marketing campaign, coupled with positive word-of-mouth, has clearly worked. Audiences are flocking to screenings, with many theatres reporting sold-out shows across major cities like Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru.

India's Love for Spider-Man

Spider-Man has always had a special place in Indian audiences' hearts, and Brand New Day proves that connection remains strong. The film's Hindi-dubbed version is performing particularly well in non-metro regions, contributing significantly to the overall collection.

Distributors are optimistic about the film's long run, especially with the upcoming weekend expected to add another substantial chunk to its total. The current trajectory suggests the film could cross the Rs 200 crore mark in India within its first week.

Comparisons and Milestones

To put things in perspective, Spider-Man: Brand New Day is already outpacing several big-ticket releases from the past year. Its opening day collection in India alone has surpassed the lifetime earnings of many mid-sized Bollywood films.

Globally, the film is on course to join the exclusive billion-dollar club, a feat achieved by only a handful of superhero movies. The momentum shows no signs of slowing, with international markets also reporting robust numbers.

Officials have not yet confirmed the final worldwide figures, but early estimates are nothing short of phenomenal. The film's success is a testament to the enduring appeal of Spider-Man and Tom Holland's star power.

As the weekend progresses, all eyes will be on whether Spider-Man: Brand New Day can maintain its blistering pace. With no major Hollywood competition in the coming weeks, the film is expected to dominate the box office for some time.

For now, fans and trade watchers alike are celebrating what could be one of the biggest blockbusters of the year, both in India and globally.
