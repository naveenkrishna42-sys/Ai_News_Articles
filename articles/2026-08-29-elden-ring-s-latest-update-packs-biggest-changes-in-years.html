Elden Ring's Latest Update Packs Biggest Changes in Years

Elden Ring's latest update, Patch 1.17, has been revealed to bring the biggest changes to the game in years. The update introduces a host of new features and content, including new weapons, armor sets, NPC invasions, and horse armor. Additionally, the upcoming DLC is set to bring back a beloved character from Dark Souls 2.

New Weapons and Armor Sets

One of the most significant additions in Patch 1.17 is the introduction of new weapons and armor sets. These new items are designed to provide players with fresh options for combat and exploration. The new weapons include a variety of swords, axes, and other melee weapons, each with unique abilities and stats. The new armor sets offer a range of defensive options, from heavy armor for tanking to light armor for agility.

NPC Invasions and Horse Armor

Patch 1.17 also introduces NPC invasions, which add a new layer of challenge and excitement to the game. These invasions involve groups of non-player characters attacking the player, forcing them to defend themselves or flee. The NPC invasions are designed to be both challenging and rewarding, offering players the chance to earn valuable loot and experience.

Additionally, the update introduces horse armor, which allows players to customize their mounts with a variety of armor sets. This feature adds a new level of customization to the game, allowing players to create unique and personalized characters.
