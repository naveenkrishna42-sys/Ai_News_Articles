Suriya at 51: Rare Photos Trace His Rise in Tamil Cinema

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Suriya celebrates his 51st birthday on July 23, 2026.
  • He made his acting debut in 1997 with 'Nerukku Ner'.
  • Suriya is known for films like 'Ghajini', 'Singam', and 'Soorarai Pottru'.
  • He also runs the production house 2D Entertainment.
  • Rare photos from his early career and family life have surfaced.

From Debut to Superstardom

Suriya, one of Tamil cinema's most bankable stars, turns 51 today. Born Saravanan Sivakumar on July 23, 1975, in Chennai, he stepped into the film industry in 1997 with Vasanth's 'Nerukku Ner'. The film did not set the box office on fire, but it marked the arrival of a new talent.

His breakthrough came with the 2001 romantic drama 'Nandha', directed by Bala. This film earned him his first Filmfare Award for Best Actor – Tamil. It was a turning point that set him on a path to stardom.

The 'Ghajini' and 'Singam' Effect

Suriya's career peaked in the mid-2000s. The 2005 blockbuster 'Ghajini', directed by A.R. Murugadoss, remains a landmark in Indian cinema. It was later remade in Hindi with Aamir Khan. Suriya's portrayal of a man with short-term memory loss was widely praised.

The 'Singam' franchise, starting in 2010, cemented his image as an action hero. His role as the fiery police officer Durai Singam became a pop culture phenomenon. The trilogy earned over Rs 400 crore globally.

Venturing into Production

In 2016, Suriya and his brother Karthi launched 2D Entertainment. The production house has backed critically acclaimed films like 'Soorarai Pottru' (2020), which won several National Awards. The film, based on the life of Air Deccan founder G.R. Gopinath, showcased Suriya's commitment to meaningful cinema.

His recent projects include 'Vikram' (2022), a multi-starrer directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj, which became one of the highest-grossing Tamil films of all time. He is currently shooting for 'Rolex', the spin-off from the 'Vikram' universe.

Rare Photos Surface Online

On his birthday, fans and film archives have shared rare photos of Suriya. These include candid shots from his early modeling days, behind-the-scenes images from 'Nandha', and family pictures with his wife, actress Jyothika, and their two children.

One photo shows a young Suriya on the set of 'Nerukku Ner' with director Vasanth. Another captures him receiving his first Filmfare award. These images offer a nostalgic glimpse into his journey over three decades.

Suriya is also known for his philanthropic work. He runs the Agaram Foundation, which provides educational scholarships to underprivileged students. His off-screen contributions have earned him widespread respect.

As he steps into his 52nd year, Suriya remains a dominant force in Tamil cinema. His upcoming slate includes 'Rolex' and a biopic on the late Tamil writer and activist Periyar. Fans are waiting for official announcements.
