Supreme Court reviews NTA's NEET-UG exam security

📅 2026-08-20 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Supreme Court questions NTA's capacity for NEET-UG exams
  • Centre lists security measures before the court
  • National medical body raises concerns about exam leaks
  • Supreme Court advises meaningful continuity in NTA reforms

Supreme Court questions NTA's capacity for NEET-UG exams

The Supreme Court has questioned the National Testing Agency's (NTA) capacity to handle the NEET-UG exams, suggesting a more robust system like the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC). The court expressed concerns about the NTA's ability to manage the exams effectively and ensure their integrity.

Centre lists security measures before the court

The Centre has listed several security measures in place for the NEET-UG exams before the Supreme Court. These measures include a foolproof system for question paper distribution, strict monitoring of exam centres, and the use of advanced technology to prevent leaks. The Centre argued that these measures make it difficult for any leaks to occur.

National medical body raises concerns about exam leaks

The National Medical Commission (NMC) has raised concerns about the adequacy of the NTA's measures to curb exam paper leaks. The NMC questioned the effectiveness of the security measures and expressed fears about the impact of leaks on the integrity of the NEET-UG exams.

Supreme Court advises meaningful continuity in NTA reforms

The Supreme Court has advised the NTA to focus on meaningful continuity in its reforms rather than committee-hopping. The court emphasized the need for a stable and dedicated body to manage the NEET-UG exams and ensure their integrity. The NTA has been criticized for frequent changes in its leadership and structure, which the court believes have hindered its ability to handle the exams effectively.
