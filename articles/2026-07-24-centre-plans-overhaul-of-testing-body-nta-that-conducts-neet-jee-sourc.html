Centre Plans 'Overhaul' Of Testing Body NTA That Conducts NEET, JEE: Sources

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Centre plans comprehensive overhaul of NTA following exam irregularities.
  • 47 NTA officials have been sacked, with some facing potential criminal action.
  • Over a third of NTA posts are vacant, according to the Minister of State for Education.
  • Sources indicate the overhaul is in response to widespread student protests.

The Union government is preparing for a sweeping overhaul of the National Testing Agency (NTA), the body responsible for conducting major entrance exams like NEET and JEE. Sources within the Education Ministry have confirmed to NDTV that a comprehensive restructuring is on the cards.

Why the Overhaul Now?

The move comes amid growing allegations of irregularities and a series of high-profile exam paper leaks. The situation escalated earlier this year when thousands of students took to the streets in cities across India, protesting delays and suspected fraud in the exam process.

According to multiple media reports, the government has already initiated a massive cleanup within the NTA. In a significant development, 47 NTA officials have been sacked. The Times of India reported that this is part of a big overhaul triggered by the protests.

Criminal Action for Some Officials

The shake-up is not limited to termination of services. Sources indicate that some of the sacked officials may face criminal proceedings. The Indian Express noted that the action is being taken amid widespread student protests demanding accountability.

The Hindustan Times confirmed the figure of 47 sacked officials, adding that more cleanup measures are expected to follow in the coming weeks.

The Vacancy Problem

The NTA has been operating with a severely depleted workforce for some time. The Hindu reported that Minister of State for Education, while speaking in Parliament, revealed that as many as 15 out of 39 sanctioned posts in the National Testing Agency are currently vacant.

This chronic understaffing is seen as a key factor that has allowed systemic issues to fester. With nearly 40% of top positions lying empty, the agency's ability to conduct foolproof exams for millions of students has been questioned.

What the Overhaul Entails

While the exact blueprint of the restructuring is still being finalised, sources say the government is looking at both administrative and technological fixes. Key areas being examined include tightening the question paper printing and distribution process, and strengthening the digital infrastructure used for online tests.

The government is also likely to revisit the appointment and tenure of top NTA officials to ensure greater accountability. The focus is on creating a system that can handle the scale of NEET and JEE without compromising on security or fairness.

The coming weeks will be crucial, as the Education Ministry is expected to announce a detailed plan to restore confidence in the examination system.
