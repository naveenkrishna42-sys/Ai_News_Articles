Nifty below 24,650 as market snaps four-day gaining streak

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nifty closed below 24,650, ending a four-day winning run
  • RBI policy announcement awaited; market participants cautious
  • Broader indices also declined, tracking weak global cues

Market snaps winning streak

Indian equity benchmarks ended lower on Tuesday, with the Nifty closing below the 24,650 mark, snapping a four-day gaining streak. The Sensex also declined in tandem, as investors chose to book profits ahead of the Reserve Bank of India's monetary policy decision due later this week.

The pullback was broad-based, with selling pressure visible across most sectors. Market participants said the recent run-up had left valuations stretched, and the pause was not unexpected.

RBI policy in spotlight

All eyes are now on the RBI's policy announcement, which is expected to set the tone for the near-term trajectory of the market. Economists and traders are divided on whether the central bank will hold rates or signal a shift, given the conflicting signals on inflation and growth.

A status quo is widely anticipated, but the commentary accompanying the decision will be key. Any hawkish surprise could trigger further profit-taking, while a dovish tilt may revive buying interest.

Broader market under pressure

The midcap and smallcap indices also ended lower, mirroring the weakness in the largecaps. The advance-decline ratio was clearly in favour of declines, indicating that the selling was not limited to a few heavyweight stocks.

Foreign institutional investors have been net buyers in recent sessions, but domestic institutions have turned cautious. The volatility index, a measure of market fear, edged up slightly, suggesting that traders are bracing for movement in either direction.

What to watch

With the RBI policy out of the way, the market will shift focus to global cues, including the trajectory of US bond yields and crude oil prices. The ongoing earnings season will also provide stock-specific triggers.

For now, the immediate support for the Nifty is seen around the 24,400-24,500 zone. A decisive break below that could open up further downside, while a recovery above 24,800 would signal renewed strength.

Market participants are advised to stay nimble and avoid aggressive positions until the policy clarity emerges. The coming sessions are likely to be data-heavy, and volatility may remain elevated.
