FedEx CEO Raj Subramaniam landed first job after taking roommate’s abandoned interview

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Raj Subramaniam became FedEx CEO after a career that started with a roommate’s missed interview.
  • He attended the interview in place of his roommate and got the job.
  • The story is often cited as an example of the power of saying yes to unexpected chances.

A missed interview that changed everything

Raj Subramaniam, the CEO of FedEx, did not land his first job through a carefully planned career move. It happened because his roommate decided to skip an interview.

Subramaniam, who now leads one of the world’s largest logistics companies, has often recounted the story. He was in his early twenties, studying in the United States, when his roommate was scheduled for a job interview. The roommate backed out at the last minute. Subramaniam decided to go in his place.

He got the job. That role set him on a path that would eventually lead to the top office at FedEx.

Saying yes when it matters

The story has been widely shared in business circles as an example of the power of seizing opportunity. Subramaniam himself has described it as a lesson in saying yes, even when the situation is uncertain.

At 63, he has spoken about how that single decision shaped his career. He did not have a detailed plan. He simply took a chance that was not meant for him.

Industry watchers note that the anecdote also highlights a willingness to step into unfamiliar situations, a trait that has served him well in the fast-changing logistics sector.

From that first job to the corner office

Subramaniam joined FedEx in 1991 and worked his way up through several roles. He became CEO in 2022, succeeding founder Fred Smith. His tenure has been marked by efforts to adapt FedEx to shifts in global trade and e-commerce.

The company has faced pressure from rising costs, changing consumer habits, and competition from rivals like Amazon. Under Subramaniam, FedEx has pushed for greater efficiency and a stronger focus on digital solutions.

His career trajectory, from a last-minute interview to leading a multinational corporation, is often held up as a reminder that big careers can start with small, unplanned decisions.

What to watch next

Subramaniam continues to steer FedEx through a volatile global cargo market. The company is expected to announce further restructuring plans in the coming quarters as it adjusts to shifting trade patterns. Whether his trademark willingness to say yes will extend to new ventures remains to be seen.
