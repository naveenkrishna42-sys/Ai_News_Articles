Bitcoin climbs back toward $64,000 after AI shock and bill uncertainty

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin rebounds toward $64,000 after a sharp slide triggered by AI sector shock and uncertainty over India's cryptocurrency bill.
  • Market sentiment remains cautious as traders weigh the impact of AI developments on risk assets.
  • Regulatory clarity in India continues to be a key factor for crypto markets, with no final decision on the bill yet.

Bitcoin is staging a recovery, climbing back toward the $64,000 mark after a sharp decline that rattled crypto markets. The slide was triggered by a combination of an artificial intelligence-driven market shock and renewed doubts over India's long-awaited cryptocurrency bill.

The world's largest digital asset had dropped sharply earlier this week, falling below $60,000 at one point. Traders and investors were spooked by a sudden sell-off in AI-linked stocks, which spilled over into crypto markets. The fear was that a broader tech downturn could sap risk appetite across the board.

AI shock rattles risk assets

The AI shock originated from a surprise earnings miss by a major US tech firm, which raised concerns about overvaluation in the sector. That nervousness quickly spread, hitting everything from equities to digital currencies.

Bitcoin, often correlated with tech stocks during periods of market stress, bore the brunt of the sell-off. Analysts noted that the cryptocurrency's recent rally had been partly fuelled by optimism around AI and blockchain convergence, making it vulnerable to such a shakeout.

“When AI takes a hit, crypto feels it too,” one market observer said. “The two sectors have become intertwined in the eyes of many investors.”

India's crypto bill casts a shadow

Adding to the pressure was the lingering uncertainty over India's proposed cryptocurrency legislation. The bill, which has been in the works for months, was expected to provide a clear regulatory framework. But recent signals from New Delhi suggest that the government may take a stricter stance than previously anticipated.

Reports indicate that the bill could include provisions that restrict trading and holding of private cryptocurrencies, potentially imposing heavy penalties for non-compliance. That has left the Indian crypto community on edge, with many fearing a clampdown similar to China's 2021 ban.

“The lack of clarity is the biggest problem,” a Delhi-based crypto trader said. “People are holding back, waiting to see what the government actually does.”

Officials have not yet confirmed the final shape of the bill, and discussions are believed to be ongoing. The government has repeatedly stated that it wants to regulate, not ban, cryptocurrencies. But the market remains skeptical.

Recovery under way, but caution remains

Despite the recent turbulence, Bitcoin has managed to claw back much of its losses. The recovery was driven by bargain buying and a partial rebound in AI stocks. However, trading volumes remain subdued, suggesting that many investors are still sitting on the sidelines.

Technical analysts point to the $64,000 level as a key resistance point. A sustained move above that could signal further upside, but failure to hold could see the price slip back toward the $60,000 support zone.

Market participants are now closely watching two things: the trajectory of AI-related stocks and any fresh developments on the Indian crypto bill. Both factors are likely to determine Bitcoin's direction in the coming days.

What happens next depends largely on regulatory clarity in India and whether the AI sector stabilises. Until then, the crypto market remains in a wait-and-watch mode, with traders bracing for more volatility.
