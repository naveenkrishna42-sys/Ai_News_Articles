Mumbai highrise fire: 2 dead, 4 injured in Vile Parle blaze on 11th floor

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Fire broke out on the 11th floor of a residential highrise in Vile Parle, Mumbai.
  • Two people died—a 2.5-year-old infant and a 23-year-old woman; four others were injured.
  • Rescue operations helped evacuate residents, including a child, as fire teams battled the blaze.

A massive fire tore through the 11th floor of a residential highrise in Mumbai's Vile Parle area on Tuesday, leaving two people dead and four others injured. Among the deceased were a 2.5-year-old infant and a 23-year-old woman, according to officials familiar with the rescue operation.

Blaze breaks out in afternoon hours

The fire was reported in the afternoon, with thick smoke billowing from the upper floors of the building. Fire tenders and emergency response teams were rushed to the spot, and a rescue operation was launched immediately to evacuate trapped residents.

Visuals from the scene showed flames licking the exterior of the building as firefighters worked to douse the blaze. The cause of the fire has not yet been confirmed, and authorities are likely to launch a probe once the situation is brought under control.

Rescue efforts amid chaos

As the fire spread, several residents on the 11th floor found themselves trapped. Rescue teams managed to pull out at least four people, including a child, from the affected floor. The injured have been shifted to a nearby hospital, though their condition is not yet known.

Officials said the rescue operation was challenging due to the height of the building and the intensity of the flames. Firefighters used aerial ladders and other equipment to reach the upper floors, while residents on lower levels were evacuated as a precaution.

Infant and woman among victims

The death of the infant and the young woman has sent shockwaves through the locality. Neighbours and local residents gathered near the building as news of the tragedy spread. The identities of the deceased have not been officially released pending family notification.

Four other people were injured in the incident and are receiving medical care. The exact nature of their injuries is still unclear, but officials have said they are stable.

Building safety under scrutiny

The incident has reignited concerns about fire safety in Mumbai's residential highrises. Many older buildings in the city lack adequate fire escapes or modern suppression systems, making such blazes particularly deadly. However, officials have not yet commented on whether the Vile Parle building had the required safety clearances.

Local authorities have promised a thorough investigation into the cause of the fire and the response time of emergency services. Residents of the building are likely to be temporarily relocated while structural assessments are carried out.

What to watch next

As the investigation unfolds, the focus will shift to the condition of the injured and any lapses in fire safety protocols. The Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation is expected to release an official statement in the coming hours, and a detailed inquiry report may determine whether the building complied with fire norms.
