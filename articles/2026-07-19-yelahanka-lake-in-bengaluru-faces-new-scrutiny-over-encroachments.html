Yelahanka Lake in Bengaluru Faces New Scrutiny Over Encroachments

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Yelahanka lake in Bengaluru is under renewed examination by civic authorities.
  • Allegations of encroachment and pollution have prompted a fresh investigation.
  • Officials are reviewing land records and assessing the lake's condition.

Bengaluru's Yelahanka lake is once again under the spotlight. Civic authorities have initiated a fresh round of scrutiny over allegations of encroachment and pollution. The move comes after complaints from local residents and environmental activists.

What Prompted the Scrutiny?

Residents near the lake have reported construction activity along its periphery. They claim this has reduced the water body's size. Environmental groups have also flagged untreated sewage entering the lake.

Officials from the Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) have begun inspecting the site. They are reviewing land records to determine the extent of any illegal occupation.

Authorities Examine Land Records

BBMP teams have started a survey of the lake's boundary. They are comparing current physical markers with official documents. This is to identify any structures that have come up on lake bed land.

A senior BBMP official said the exercise is aimed at restoring the lake to its original dimensions. The official added that action will be taken against any encroachment found.

Pollution Concerns Resurface

Beyond encroachment, the lake's water quality is a concern. Local activists say sewage and industrial waste have been flowing into the lake. This has led to algal blooms and a foul smell in the area.

Residents have demanded immediate steps to stop the inflow of pollutants. They want the lake to be cleaned and maintained as a public recreational space.

Previous Restoration Efforts

Yelahanka lake has seen restoration projects in the past. In 2020, a desilting and beautification drive was undertaken. However, activists claim those efforts were not sustained.

The current scrutiny is seen as a chance to address long-standing issues. But residents remain skeptical about follow-through.

Officials have not yet confirmed a timeline for the completion of the survey or any subsequent action. They said the findings will determine the next steps.

What to watch: BBMP is expected to release a preliminary report on the encroachment survey within two weeks. The report will likely outline the number of illegal structures and the area of lake bed encroached.
