Panama-flagged cargo ship sinks off Odisha coast

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Panama-flagged cargo ship sinks off Odisha coast
  • 24 crew members aboard the vessel
  • Search-and-rescue operation underway
  • No immediate reports of casualties

Vessel Sinks 200 Nautical Miles Off Paradip Port

A Panama-flagged cargo ship carrying 24 crew members has sunk in the Bay of Bengal off Odisha's Paradip port. The incident occurred approximately 200 nautical miles from the port, with no immediate reports of casualties. The ship was en route to Singapore when it encountered difficulties and sank.

Search-and-Rescue Operation Underway

The Indian Coast Guard (ICG) has deployed ships for a search-and-rescue operation following the sinking of the vessel. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact cause of the incident. The ICG is coordinating with other agencies to ensure the safety of the crew members aboard the sunken ship.

No Immediate Reports of Casualties

As of now, there are no immediate reports of casualties or injuries among the 24 crew members on board the vessel. The ICG is working to locate the crew and provide assistance if needed. The search-and-rescue operation is expected to continue for several hours as conditions in the Bay of Bengal remain challenging.

Investigation into the Incident

Officials are investigating the circumstances surrounding the sinking of the Panama-flagged cargo ship. The exact cause of the incident is still under review, and authorities are working to gather more information to determine what happened. The ICG and other relevant agencies are collaborating to ensure a thorough investigation into the matter.
