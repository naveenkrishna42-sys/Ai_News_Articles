Rahul Gandhi Demands FIR Over 'Purification' Ritual

Rahul Gandhi has called on Prime Minister Narendra Modi to order a First Information Report (FIR) under the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act over a 'purification' ritual performed during a rally by Congress leader Mallikarjun Kharge in Uttarakhand. The incident has sparked a controversy and raised questions about the practice of untouchability in the political arena.

Chirag Paswan Criticizes Rahul Gandhi's Remarks

Chirag Paswan, a prominent leader from the Rashtriya Janata Dal, has criticized Rahul Gandhi's remarks on caste discrimination. Paswan accused Rahul Gandhi of using the issue to divert attention from other matters and questioned his motives behind the controversy. The exchange between the two leaders has added fuel to the ongoing debate.

BJP Members Accused of Practicing Untouchability

Rahul Gandhi has accused BJP members of practicing untouchability openly, citing the 'purification' ritual as an example. He argued that such practices are a violation of the law and a disgrace to the principles of equality and justice. The allegations have sparked a heated debate and raised concerns about the state of democracy in the country.

Rahul Gandhi Seeks Justice for Kharge

Rahul Gandhi has demanded justice for Mallikarjun Kharge, who was reportedly subjected to the 'purification' ritual during his rally in Uttarakhand. He has called for a thorough investigation into the matter and urged the authorities to take strict action against those responsible. The Congress leader's appeal for justice has drawn widespread support from various sections of society.
