Bitcoin Inflows Rise, Ravencoin Hits Record Low, Cardano Whales Sell

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin recorded significant inflows, signaling renewed investor interest.
  • Ravencoin plunged to an all-time low amid market pressure.
  • Cardano whales are selling, raising concerns about further downside.

The cryptocurrency market presented a mixed picture on Wednesday, with Bitcoin attracting fresh capital while altcoins struggled under persistent selling pressure. Data tracked by Analytics Insight shows a notable divergence in investor behaviour across major digital assets.

Bitcoin sees renewed inflows

Bitcoin, the world's largest cryptocurrency by market capitalisation, recorded substantial inflows over the past 24 hours. The movement suggests that institutional and retail investors are once again accumulating the asset, possibly viewing recent price dips as buying opportunities.

While the exact figures were not disclosed, the trend aligns with a broader pattern of Bitcoin recovering faster than its peers during periods of market uncertainty. Analysts note that such inflows often precede price stabilisation, though they stop short of predicting a sustained rally.

The inflows come after weeks of volatility, during which Bitcoin's price swung sharply in response to macroeconomic data and regulatory headlines. Market watchers will be keen to see whether this momentum can be maintained through the rest of the week.

Ravencoin plummets to record low

In stark contrast, Ravencoin (RVN) fell to an all-time low on Wednesday, extending a prolonged downtrend that has erased most of its value over the past year. The asset, which once enjoyed a loyal following among miners and retail traders, has struggled to regain traction amid declining network activity and waning interest.

The token's slide reflects broader weakness in the proof-of-work mining sector, where rising energy costs and hardware expenses have squeezed margins. Ravencoin's utility, primarily focused on asset tokenisation, has also failed to attract meaningful adoption beyond its core community.

Traders are now watching key support levels, though many remain cautious about calling a bottom. "The trend is clearly bearish," one analyst noted, "and without a fundamental catalyst, further downside cannot be ruled out."

Cardano whales offload holdings

Cardano (ADA) faced additional pressure as data revealed that large holders, commonly referred to as whales, have been reducing their positions. Whale activity is often seen as a leading indicator, and the selling has raised concerns among retail investors who follow these moves.

The sell-off comes despite Cardano's ongoing network upgrades and a generally active development community. However, the token's price has remained range-bound for months, and whales may be reallocating capital to assets with clearer short-term momentum.

On-chain metrics show a steady decline in the number of addresses holding significant ADA amounts, though the total supply remains unchanged. Whether this distribution shift will lead to a price correction or simply a consolidation phase is still unclear.

Market outlook

The divergence between Bitcoin and altcoins highlights a growing preference for established assets during uncertain times. While Bitcoin's inflows suggest cautious optimism, the struggles of Ravencoin and Cardano indicate that not all cryptocurrencies are benefiting equally.

Regulatory developments, particularly in the United States and India, continue to weigh on sentiment. Investors are also keeping an eye on upcoming Federal Reserve decisions, which could influence risk appetite across all markets.

As the week progresses, the focus will remain on whether Bitcoin can hold its gains and whether altcoin selling pressure will intensify. For now, the market appears to be in a wait-and-see mode, with traders bracing for potential volatility.
