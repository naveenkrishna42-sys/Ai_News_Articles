NDTV Profit Business Leadership Awards 2026 Honor Falguni Nayar, SBI, Tata Electronics

  • Nykaa founder Falguni Nayar awarded Trailblazer of the Year.
  • State Bank of India wins PSU Financial Powerhouse of the Year.
  • Tata Electronics' Randhir Thakur receives India's Atmanirbharta Champion honour.

A Night of Recognition for Corporate Excellence

The NDTV Profit Business Leadership Awards 2026 celebrated the country's top corporate performers and innovators in a ceremony held recently. The event recognised leaders and companies that have driven growth, championed self-reliance, and set benchmarks across sectors.

From retail to banking and manufacturing, the awards spanned a wide spectrum of the Indian economy. The winners were chosen for their strategic vision, operational excellence, and contribution to nation-building.

Falguni Nayar: Trailblazer of the Year

Nykaa founder Falguni Nayar was awarded the Trailblazer of the Year honour. The recognition underscores her role in transforming India's beauty and personal care e-commerce space.

Nayar built Nykaa from a startup into a publicly listed company, challenging established players and creating a new market category. The award acknowledges her entrepreneurial journey and impact on the retail landscape.

SBI Named PSU Financial Powerhouse

The State Bank of India (SBI) won the PSU Financial Powerhouse of the Year award. The country's largest lender was recognised for its robust financial performance, digital transformation initiatives, and expanded reach.

SBI has been at the forefront of banking modernisation, from mobile banking to financial inclusion programs. The award highlights its role as a pillar of the public sector banking system.

Tata Electronics' Atmanirbharta Champion

Tata Electronics' Randhir Thakur received the India's Atmanirbharta Champion honour. The award celebrates the company's efforts in boosting domestic manufacturing, particularly in electronics and semiconductors.

Tata Electronics has been a key player in the government's push for self-reliance in high-tech manufacturing. The recognition underscores the firm's progress in building a local supply chain for critical components.

Other nominees and winners across categories included leaders from various industries, reflecting the breadth of India's corporate growth. The event also featured discussions on economic trends and future business strategies.

What to Watch Next

The winners and nominees will now be closely watched for their next moves in an evolving economic environment. Industry observers will track how these leaders build on their momentum in the coming quarters.
