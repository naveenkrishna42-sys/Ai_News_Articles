Tharoor slams Parliament building as 'soulless'

  • Calls it a 'soulless conference centre'

Congress leader criticises new Parliament building

Congress leader Shashi Tharoor has expressed his disappointment with the new Parliament building, calling it a 'soulless conference centre' and lacking in the traditional charm and intimacy of its predecessors.

Lacks intimacy, traditional charm

Tharoor, who has been a vocal critic of the new building, has pointed out that the modern aesthetic of the new Parliament does not resonate with the historical and cultural significance that the building has always held.

The new Parliament building, which was inaugurated in 2023, is a stark contrast to the traditional and ornate architecture of the previous buildings. Tharoor believes that the new building lacks the soul and spirit that the previous buildings had, which made them a symbol of democracy and national unity.

Highlights modern aesthetic over historical significance

Tharoor has also criticized the new building for its focus on modern aesthetics and functionality, which he believes overshadows the historical and cultural significance of the building.

He has pointed out that the new building lacks the intricate carvings and detailed designs that were a hallmark of the previous buildings. Tharoor believes that these elements were not just decorative but also held significant historical and cultural value.

Calls for preservation of historical buildings

Tharoor has called for the preservation of historical buildings and the incorporation of modern technology in a way that does not compromise the historical and cultural significance of these buildings.

He believes that the new Parliament building should have been a blend of the old and the new, preserving the historical and cultural significance of the building while incorporating modern technology and functionality.
