Thailand school shooting: six killed, 15 wounded, police say

📅 2026-08-07 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • At least six people killed, 15 wounded in a school shooting in Thailand, police said.
  • The teen suspect reportedly killed his grandparents before attacking the school.
  • Witness videos show students ducking under desks as gunfire rang out.
  • Authorities have not yet confirmed the motive or the suspect's condition.

Attack on school leaves six dead

At least six people were killed and 15 others wounded in a shooting at a school in Thailand on Friday, according to police. The attack, which unfolded in a province still being identified by authorities, has sent shockwaves through the country, where mass shootings are rare but not unprecedented.

Police said the suspect, a teenager, opened fire at the school after allegedly shooting his grandparents at their home. The sequence of events, from the first shots to the final moments, remains under investigation, but officials have confirmed the death toll and the number of injured.

Teen suspect's rampage

Reports from local media and international outlets, including CNN and The Guardian, indicate that the teen suspect killed his grandparents before turning his weapon on schoolmates and teachers. The exact relationship between the suspect and the victims has not been officially confirmed, but police have described the attack as deliberate.

Videos shared on social media, later picked up by The Hindu, show terrified students crouching under desks as gunshots echo through classrooms. One clip, verified by news agencies, captures the chaos and fear that gripped the school during the assault.

Casualties and response

The death toll stands at six, with 15 wounded, according to police. The injured have been rushed to nearby hospitals, and their conditions range from stable to critical, though officials have not released a detailed breakdown. The suspect's own fate is unclear; some reports suggest he was subdued by authorities, but this has not been officially confirmed.

Emergency services and police cordoned off the school grounds within minutes of the first reports. Parents gathered outside, desperate for news of their children, as officers worked to secure the area and gather evidence.

Gun violence in Thailand

Thailand has seen sporadic mass shootings in recent years, including a 2020 attack on a mall in Nakhon Ratchasima that killed 29 people, and a 2022 daycare centre massacre that claimed 36 lives, most of them children. While civilian gun ownership is relatively high for the region, school shootings remain rare, making Friday's incident particularly alarming.

The country's gun laws allow for legal ownership of firearms, but illegal weapons are also widespread. Following the daycare tragedy, authorities pledged stricter enforcement, but progress has been slow. Friday's attack is likely to reignite calls for reform, though experts warn that cultural and regulatory hurdles remain significant.

What to watch

Investigators are expected to release more details about the suspect's background, including his mental health and possible motives, in the coming days. The condition of the wounded will also be closely monitored, as the death toll could rise. For now, the focus remains on the victims and their families, as Thailand grapples with another tragic episode of gun violence.
