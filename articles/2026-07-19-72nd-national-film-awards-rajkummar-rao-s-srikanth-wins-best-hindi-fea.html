72nd National Film Awards: Rajkummar Rao’s ‘Srikanth’ Wins Best Hindi Feature Film

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Rajkummar Rao’s ‘Srikanth’ wins Best Hindi Feature Film at the 72nd National Film Awards.
  • The film is a biopic on visually impaired industrialist Srikanth Bolla.
  • The award was announced on Saturday by the Directorate of Film Festivals.
  • This is the first National Film Award for Rajkummar Rao as a producer.

The 72nd National Film Awards announced on Saturday recognised Rajkummar Rao’s production Srikanth as the Best Hindi Feature Film. The biopic, which traces the life of visually impaired industrialist Srikanth Bolla, also won the award for Best Screenplay.

A Biopic of Resilience

Directed by Tushar Hiranandani, Srikanth tells the story of Bolla, who despite being blind from birth, went on to become the first international visually impaired student at MIT and later founded Bollant Industries, a Hyderabad-based packaging company. The film traces his battles against prejudice and systemic barriers in education and business.

Rajkummar Rao played the lead role and also co-produced the film under his banner. In a statement, he called the award “a tribute to the indomitable spirit of Srikanth Bolla.”

Recognition Beyond Acting

This is the first National Film Award for Rajkummar Rao as a producer. The actor, who has previously won a National Award for his performance in Shahid (2013), now adds a production honour to his shelf. The Best Screenplay award went to writer-director Tushar Hiranandani and co-writer Jagdeep Sidhu.

The film released in theatres earlier this year to positive reviews, with critics praising Rao’s understated performance and the film’s refusal to turn disability into melodrama.

Other Major Winners

The 72nd National Film Awards also saw wins for other films across categories. While the full list is yet to be officially released, early announcements confirm that Srikanth was among the top Hindi entries. The awards are decided by a jury appointed by the Directorate of Film Festivals, and this year’s panel included filmmakers and critics from across the country.

The ceremony for the 72nd National Film Awards is expected to be held later this year in New Delhi, where winners will receive their Swarna Kamal and Rajat Kamal trophies.

The win for Srikanth underscores a growing trend of biographical films being recognised at the national level. In recent years, the awards have honoured stories rooted in real-life struggles, from Uri: The Surgical Strike to Bhonsle.

With the National Film Awards now in its seventh decade, the recognition for Srikanth also highlights the increasing role of actors as producers backing content-driven cinema. Rajkummar Rao, who has often chosen unconventional roles, now has a production credit that matches his acting pedigree.

The full list of winners across all categories is expected to be published by the Directorate of Film Festivals in the coming days. Film enthusiasts will be watching to see which regional films and technical categories also received honours.
