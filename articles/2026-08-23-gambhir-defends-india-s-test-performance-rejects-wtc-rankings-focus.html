Gambhir dismisses critics, defends India's Test performance

Indian cricket coach Gautam Gambhir has fired back at critics of the team's Test performance, declaring that India is not struggling and that the focus should be on immediate series wins rather than the World Test Championship (WTC) rankings.

In a recent interview, Gambhir stated, 'My job is not to get views and likes. It's to win matches and make the team better.' He emphasized that India's recent performances, including two home whitewashes, prove that the team is not struggling.

India's recent Test success

India has won five of its last six Test matches, including two home series whitewashes. This recent success has led to a resurgence in the team's confidence and performance.

The team's success is a testament to Gambhir's coaching and the players' hard work and dedication. The team's focus is now on winning the upcoming Test series, rather than the WTC rankings.

Gambhir's vision for future Test series

Gambhir has expressed a preference for a three-match Test series format for future contests. He believes that this format would provide a better balance between the team's preparation and the intensity of the competition.

Gambhir's vision for the future of Test cricket is one of increased competition and intensity. He believes that a three-match series format would help achieve this goal.

Focus on immediate series wins

Gambhir has made it clear that the team's focus is on winning the upcoming Test series, rather than the WTC rankings. He believes that the team's recent success has proven that it is capable of competing at the highest level.

The team's focus is now on building on this recent success and continuing to improve. Gambhir is confident that the team is capable of achieving great things in the future.
