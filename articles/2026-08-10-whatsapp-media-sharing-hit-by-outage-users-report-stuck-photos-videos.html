WhatsApp media sharing hit by outage; users report stuck photos, videos

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Users across India report problems sending photos, videos and other media on WhatsApp
  • The issue appears to affect users globally, with reports from multiple countries
  • Meta has not yet confirmed the cause or provided an official statement
  • Downdetector and social media platforms show a spike in outage complaints
  • No word on when the service will be fully restored

Media messages stuck across India

WhatsApp users across India reported difficulty sending photos, videos and other media files on Monday morning, with many saying the messages remained stuck at 'sending' status for prolonged periods. Complaints began surfacing on social media platforms around 6 am IST, with users in major cities like Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru and Hyderabad flagging the issue.

The problem appears to be widespread, affecting both Android and iOS devices. Text messages and voice calls seemed to work for most users, but any attempt to attach media triggered delays or outright failure. Several users took to X (formerly Twitter) to express frustration, with hashtags like #WhatsAppDown trending in India.

Global outage reports emerge

While the initial reports centred on India, monitoring platforms and news outlets soon picked up similar complaints from other parts of the world. Telangana Today and India.com reported that WhatsApp users globally were facing issues sending videos and photos. The Economic Times noted that the outage was not limited to one region, suggesting a broader technical problem rather than a localised network issue.

Downdetector, a popular outage-tracking website, showed a sharp spike in complaints around the same time, with a majority of reports pointing to media upload failures. However, the exact scope of the outage remains unclear, as WhatsApp parent company Meta has not yet released an official statement or acknowledged the issue on its status page.

No official word from Meta

As of this report, Meta has not confirmed the cause of the disruption. The company typically communicates service issues through its official channels, but no such update has been posted yet. This silence has left users guessing whether the problem stems from server overload, a software glitch, or something else entirely.

For many users, the outage is more than an inconvenience. In India, WhatsApp is a critical communication tool for personal chats, business transactions and even official announcements. Small businesses that rely on the app for customer orders and payments have been particularly affected, with some reporting delays in responding to clients.

Workarounds and user frustration

Some users have resorted to sending media as documents or compressing files before sharing, but these workarounds have had mixed results. Others have tried switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data, restarting the app, or clearing cache—none of which have provided a consistent fix, according to social media posts.

This is not the first time WhatsApp has faced such an issue. Similar outages have occurred in the past, often resolved within a few hours. However, the lack of communication from Meta has added to the anxiety, especially for users who depend on the platform for time-sensitive work.

What to watch next

Users are advised to keep an eye on Meta's official status page and social media handles for updates. If the outage persists beyond a few hours, it could impact business communications across the country. Historically, WhatsApp has restored service quickly, but until Meta confirms the issue, affected users may need to rely on alternative messaging apps or phone calls.

The next few hours will be crucial in determining whether this is a brief glitch or a more prolonged disruption. For now, patience seems to be the only option for millions of users waiting to hit 'send' on their media files.
