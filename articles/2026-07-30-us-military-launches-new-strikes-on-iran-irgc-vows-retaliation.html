US military launches new strikes on Iran, IRGC vows retaliation

📅 2026-07-30 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • US military launched new airstrikes on multiple targets inside Iran.
  • Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) has vowed retaliation.
  • The US also reported intercepting Iranian ballistic missiles aimed at its forces in the region.
  • The strikes mark a significant widening of the ongoing conflict in West Asia.

The United States military has carried out a fresh wave of airstrikes inside Iran, hitting multiple targets, according to reports confirmed by The Hindu and other international outlets. The strikes mark a major escalation in the widening West Asia conflict, with Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) already vowing a firm response.

What We Know About the Latest Strikes

The US military operation, launched late on Wednesday or early Thursday (local time), struck several facilities within Iranian territory. While the precise locations and nature of the targets have not been officially detailed by the Pentagon, the strikes represent the most direct American action against Iran since the conflict's recent intensification.

Al Jazeera News reported that the US hit “multiple targets,” with the IRGC quickly issuing a statement pledging retaliation. The scale of damage and any potential casualties remain unconfirmed, as independent access to the affected areas is restricted.

Iranian Ballistic Missiles Intercepted

In a related development, the US military confirmed it intercepted Iranian ballistic missiles that were fired at American forces stationed in the Middle East. Telegraph India reported the interception, though the exact location and number of missiles neutralised have not been disclosed.

This incident underscores the heightened state of alert across the region. American air defence systems have been on high alert, and the downing of the missiles suggests a direct attempt by Iran to strike at US assets, possibly in response to earlier strikes or as a preemptive move.

Context and Escalation

The latest US strikes come amid a rapidly deteriorating security situation across West Asia. What began as a concentrated conflict has now expanded to include direct exchanges between the US and Iran. The IRGC's pledge of retaliation raises the spectre of a broader, more protracted war.

Bloomberg.com described the situation as a “widening conflict,” noting that the strikes are part of a larger pattern of military action that has drawn in regional powers. The US has consistently maintained its right to self-defence, while Iran frames the strikes as an act of aggression.

News On AIR reported on Thursday morning that the situation remains fluid, with diplomatic channels under immense strain. Several nations have called for restraint, but no formal ceasefire or de-escalation talks have been announced.

Key Developments at a Glance

  • The US launched new airstrikes on multiple targets inside Iran.
  • The IRGC has publicly pledged to retaliate for the attacks.
  • US forces intercepted Iranian ballistic missiles aimed at their positions.
  • The conflict continues to widen, with no immediate end in sight.
