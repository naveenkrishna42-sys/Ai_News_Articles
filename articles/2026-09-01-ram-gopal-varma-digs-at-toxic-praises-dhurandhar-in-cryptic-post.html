Ram Gopal Varma digs at Toxic, praises Dhurandhar in cryptic post

Key points

  • Director criticizes expensive films, saying they fail to make characters feel real

Director's Critique

Bollywood director Ram Gopal Varma has posted a cryptic message on social media that appears to target his recent film Toxic while praising his earlier work Dhurandhar. In the post, Varma criticizes what he calls 'most expensive films' for failing to make characters feel real.

Background on Toxic

Toxic, released in 2023, is a crime thriller starring Ajay Devgn and Isha Sharvani. The film has been criticized for its plot and character development, with many viewers and critics finding it to be a disappointment. The film's high production value and expensive cast have also come under scrutiny.

Dhurandhar Praise

Dhurandhar, released in 2016, is a political drama starring Ajay Devgn and Tabu. The film was a critical and commercial success, praised for its strong performances and realistic portrayal of political corruption. Varma's praise for Dhurandhar suggests that he believes the film succeeded where Toxic failed.

Broader Industry Implications

Varma's critique highlights a broader issue in Bollywood, where expensive films often struggle to make characters feel real. This can be attributed to a focus on special effects and stunts over character development. The industry's increasing reliance on expensive films can also lead to financial risks, as seen with Toxic's disappointing performance.

Expert Opinion

Film critic and analyst Rajeev Masand says, 'Varma's critique is well-founded. Expensive films often prioritize spectacle over substance, leading to characters that feel one-dimensional. This trend is not unique to Bollywood but is a global issue in the film industry.'
