India vs Brazil confirmed: Kolkata to host blockbuster friendly

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • India will host five-time world champions Brazil in a football friendly in Kolkata.
  • The match is scheduled for the first international break after the 2026 FIFA World Cup.
  • Brazil will also play Australia during the same window.
  • The fixture is seen as a landmark event for Indian football.

Historic Clash in Kolkata

The All India Football Federation (AIFF) has confirmed that the Indian men's football team will host five-time world champions Brazil in a friendly match in Kolkata. The fixture is set to take place during the first international break after the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

The announcement was made on Thursday, with the AIFF describing the event as a 'date for history books'. Kolkata, long considered the heartland of Indian football, will host the game at the iconic Salt Lake Stadium.

Brazil's Tour Plans

Brazil, currently ranked among the top teams globally, will play two matches during that international window. Apart from facing India, the Seleção are also scheduled to play Australia, as reported by multiple sources.

This marks the first time Brazil will play in India. The match presents a massive opportunity for the Indian team to test themselves against one of the most celebrated football nations in history.

Significance for Indian Football

For Indian football, hosting Brazil is a monumental step. It brings the world's most famous footballing nation to a country where the sport's popularity is surging, driven by the Indian Super League and growing grassroots interest.

The AIFF has not yet announced the exact date or ticket pricing for the match. However, officials confirmed that the game will be part of the FIFA international match calendar, ensuring both teams can field their strongest available squads.

Reactions and Preparations

Football fans in Kolkata and across India have reacted with excitement to the news. Social media was abuzz with the announcement, with many calling it a dream fixture. The AIFF president stated that the federation is working to ensure a world-class event.

Brazil's football confederation (CBF) has acknowledged the fixture, with their schedule indicating a stop in India as part of their post-World Cup tour. The match will serve as preparation for both teams as they look toward future qualifiers and tournaments.

Authorities in Kolkata are expected to coordinate closely with the AIFF for security and logistics. The Salt Lake Stadium, with a capacity of over 85,000, is likely to be packed for the encounter.

The Indian team has been on an upward trajectory in recent years, but facing Brazil will be their toughest test yet. The match offers invaluable exposure for players and could boost India's standing in world football.

The exact date and time of the match are expected to be confirmed in the coming weeks. All eyes will be on Kolkata as it prepares to welcome the five-time world champions for what promises to be a landmark event in Indian sports history.
