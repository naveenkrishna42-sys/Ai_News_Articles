Indian Stock Market Ends Flat; Nifty 50 Holds Key Support

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nifty 50 closed near 24,850, holding the crucial 24,800 support level.
  • Bank Nifty underperformed, falling 0.3% on profit booking in private lenders.
  • IT and pharma stocks saw mild buying, offering some support to the broader market.
  • Market breadth was slightly negative, with 1,480 advances against 1,620 declines on the BSE.
  • Volatility index India VIX eased 2% to 13.5, indicating reduced fear among traders.

Indian equity benchmarks ended the session on a flat note on Friday, as the Nifty 50 managed to hold above the key 24,800 support level. The index closed at 24,853, down just 12 points from the previous close. The BSE Sensex also drifted lower, ending at 81,234, a marginal loss of 45 points.

Mixed Global Cues Keep Traders on Edge

Global markets offered no clear direction. Asian peers traded mixed, while US futures pointed to a subdued open later in the day. The lack of fresh domestic triggers meant that traders were largely focused on stock-specific moves.

Foreign institutional investors remained net sellers for the third consecutive session, pulling out Rs 1,200 crore from cash markets. Domestic institutions, however, provided some cushion with net buying of Rs 850 crore.

Bank Nifty Under Pressure; IT Stocks Offer Relief

The Bank Nifty underperformed the broader market, slipping 0.3% to close at 51,120. Profit booking in private sector lenders like HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank weighed on the index. HDFC Bank fell 0.8% after reporting a slight dip in net interest margin in its quarterly update.

On the other hand, IT stocks saw mild buying interest. Infosys and TCS each gained around 0.5% as the rupee remained stable against the dollar. Pharma stocks also edged higher, with Sun Pharma rising 1.2% on renewed buying in defensive sectors.

Midcaps and Smallcaps Show Mixed Trends

The broader market showed a mixed picture. The BSE Midcap index inched up 0.1%, while the Smallcap index was flat. Market breadth remained slightly negative, with 1,480 stocks advancing and 1,620 declining on the BSE.

Auto stocks were a drag, with Maruti Suzuki falling 0.6% on concerns over slowing demand in the entry-level segment. Metal stocks also softened, tracking lower global commodity prices.

The India VIX, a gauge of market fear, eased 2% to 13.5, suggesting that traders are not expecting sharp swings in the near term.

What to Watch Next Week

Looking ahead, traders will keep an eye on the monthly derivatives expiry next Thursday. The Nifty 50 has strong support at 24,700, while resistance is seen at 25,000.

Global cues, especially the US Federal Reserve's commentary on interest rates, will also influence sentiment. Domestic macroeconomic data, including infrastructure output for June, is due later in the week.
