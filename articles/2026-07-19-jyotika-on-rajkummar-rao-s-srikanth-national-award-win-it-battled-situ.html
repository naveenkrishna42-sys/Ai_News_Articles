Jyotika on Rajkummar Rao's 'Srikanth' National Award win: 'It battled situations'

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jyotika reacts to 'Srikanth' winning Best Hindi Feature Film at the 72nd National Film Awards.
  • She highlights the film's journey, calling it a battle against situations.
  • Director Tushar Hiranandani, Alaya F, and the team express gratitude for the win.

Jyotika's reaction to 'Srikanth' National Award win

Actor Jyotika has joined the chorus of voices celebrating Rajkummar Rao-starrer 'Srikanth' for winning the Best Hindi Feature Film at the 72nd National Film Awards. In a statement, she said the film 'battled situations' to achieve this recognition.

Jyotika, who has worked closely with Rao in the past, did not elaborate on specific challenges the production faced. But her words pointed to the film's underdog journey, mirroring the real-life story of visually impaired industrialist Srikanth Bolla that inspired it.

The actor's reaction comes as the film industry showers praise on the biographical drama, which released in 2024 to critical acclaim.

Director Tushar Hiranandani on the win

'Srikanth' director Tushar Hiranandani said the National Award win 'feels justified'. Speaking to The Times of India, he spoke about the collective effort that went into making the film.

Hiranandani, known for his work on earlier hits, said the award validates the team's commitment to telling an honest story. He did not share specific details about the film's budget or box office performance, focusing instead on the emotional payoff of the recognition.

The director also thanked Rajkummar Rao for his portrayal of Bolla, calling it a career-defining performance.

Alaya F and team express gratitude

Actor Alaya F, who played a key role in 'Srikanth', celebrated the win by saying 'honest stories always find their place'. Her comment, shared on social media, echoed the film's central theme of perseverance.

The broader 'Srikanth' team, including producers and technicians, expressed gratitude through various media outlets. Cinema Express reported that the cast and crew are planning a small celebration to mark the achievement.

Rajkummar Rao, who has not yet made a detailed public statement, is expected to address the win soon. The film also won praise for its sensitive handling of disability and entrepreneurship.

Impact of the National Award win

The 72nd National Film Awards ceremony, held earlier this week, saw 'Srikanth' take the top Hindi feature prize. The win has reignited interest in the film, which had a modest theatrical run.

Industry observers note that the award could boost the film's streaming numbers and give Rao's career further momentum. The actor has been on a strong run with varied roles in recent years.

For now, the 'Srikanth' team is basking in the glory of a national honour that many believe was well-deserved.
