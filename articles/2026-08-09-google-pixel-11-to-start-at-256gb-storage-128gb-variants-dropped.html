Google Pixel 11 to start at 256GB storage, 128GB variants dropped

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pixel 11 base storage reportedly moves to 256GB, ending 128GB variants
  • Launch event 'Made by Google 2026' scheduled for August 12, hosted by Trevor Noah
  • Pixel 11 series, Watch 5, and color options for HiLight feature are expected

Storage shift confirmed in leaks

Google is reportedly doing away with 128GB storage on its next flagship. According to leaks, the Pixel 11 series will adopt 256GB as the new base storage, a first for the company's mainline phones.

This means buyers in India and elsewhere will no longer see entry-level 128GB models. The move aligns Pixel with rivals like Samsung, who have already standardised higher storage on premium devices.

What the leaks suggest

Industry trackers, including GSMArena, have noted the absence of 128GB variants in early Pixel 11 listings. The shift is expected across both standard and Pro models, though Google has not officially confirmed specifications.

For Indian consumers, this could push up the starting price, but it also removes the need to juggle cloud storage for heavy users. The 256GB base is likely to be paired with 12GB or 16GB RAM, depending on the model.

Launch event details emerge

The 'Made by Google 2026' event is set for August 12, with a lineup that includes comedian Trevor Noah as host and cricketer Shubman Gill. The event will showcase the Pixel 11 series, the Pixel Watch 5, and possibly new colorways for the Pixel HiLight feature.

TechRepublic has already published a price guide comparing the Pixel 11 with the Samsung Galaxy S26 ahead of the launch. Google Contacts has also revealed which colors the HiLight feature will support, offering a glimpse into software tweaks.

Watch 5 alongside phones

Alongside the Pixel 11, the Pixel Watch 5 is expected to debut with minor hardware upgrades. Leaks suggest improved battery life and new fitness tracking modes, though details remain thin.

For Indian buyers, the Pixel Watch 5's price will be a key factor. The previous generation saw aggressive pricing during festive sales, and analysts expect similar positioning this year.

What to watch next

All eyes are on August 12 when Google makes the official announcement. Confirmations on storage, pricing for India, and availability dates will likely follow shortly after the event.

Until then, the 256GB base storage rumour remains just that—a rumour. But if true, it marks a significant shift in Google's budget-to-premium strategy, one that could influence other Android makers in the coming months.
