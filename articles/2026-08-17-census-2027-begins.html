Census 2027 Begins

📅 2026-08-17 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Census 2027 begins today with 40 questions
  • Caste query included for first time since Independence
  • Personal data safety concerns raised by experts

Population Count Begins

The population count for Census 2027 has begun today, with over 40 questions being asked to gather data on the country's population. This census is significant as it includes a caste query for the first time since India's Independence.

According to officials, no private data is being sought, and ID details will only be provided if they are available. The exercise aims to collect accurate data on the country's population.

Personal Data Safety Concerns

However, experts have raised concerns over the collection of personal data, including Aadhaar and mobile numbers. They question the need for such data and the potential risks to individual privacy.

The issue of data safety has sparked debate, with some arguing that the collection of such data is essential for planning and development purposes. Others, however, are skeptical about the government's ability to protect sensitive information.

Census Methodology

The census will be conducted through a combination of traditional and modern methods, including self-enumeration in some areas. In snowbound areas of Jammu and Kashmir, and in Ladakh, self-enumeration will begin on Monday.

Preparations and Controversies

Preparations for the census have been underway for several months, with officials working to ensure a smooth and accurate data collection process. However, the inclusion of the caste query has sparked controversy, with some politicians calling for official caste lists to avoid confusion.

Anbumani Ramadoss, a politician, has written to MPs expressing his concerns over the issue and seeking clarity on the matter. The debate surrounding the census highlights the complexities and challenges involved in collecting data on a large and diverse population.

Way Forward

As the census progresses, officials will face the challenge of collecting accurate data while addressing concerns over personal data safety. The outcome of the census will have significant implications for policy-making and development planning in the country.
