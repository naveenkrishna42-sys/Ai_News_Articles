Slipper hurled at Pappu Yadav during press meet over Ram temple skit; knife seized

📅 2026-08-02 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Slipper thrown at Pappu Yadav during a press conference in Patna.
  • A knife was recovered from the attacker, who has been identified with no prior criminal record.
  • Yadav claims he has been receiving threats since a skit on Ram temple donation theft.
  • Police sources confirm the attacker's identity; investigation underway.

Bihar politician Pappu Yadav was targeted during a press conference on Sunday when an unidentified man hurled a slipper at him. The incident occurred as Yadav was defending a controversial skit about the theft of donations at a Ram temple. The assailant was swiftly overpowered by security personnel, and a knife was recovered from his possession.

The attack took place in Patna, where Yadav had called the media to address the backlash over his remarks. The skit, which allegedly referenced the misappropriation of funds at a temple, had drawn sharp criticism from political rivals and religious groups. Yadav, however, maintained that his comments were taken out of context.

What we know about the attacker

Police sources have identified the man who threw the slipper, stating that he has no prior criminal record. The recovery of a knife from the attacker has raised concerns about the severity of the incident, though officials have not yet confirmed whether the weapon was intended for use.

"The individual has been taken into custody and is being questioned," a police official said, requesting anonymity. "We are verifying his background and the motive behind the attack." The swift response of Yadav's security team prevented any physical harm, but the incident has underscored the heightened tensions surrounding the politician.

Yadav claims ongoing threats

Speaking to reporters after the attack, Yadav said he had been receiving threats for some time. "I have been getting threat calls and messages ever since the skit controversy began," he claimed. "This attack is a result of that hostility." Yadav, a former MP known for his outspoken style, did not elaborate on the nature of the threats but urged authorities to investigate.

The politician's remarks had sparked a debate, with some accusing him of disrespecting religious sentiments. Others defended his right to free speech, arguing that the skit was a satirical commentary on corruption. The mixed reactions have made the issue a flashpoint in the state's political discourse.

Political fallout and reactions

The incident has drawn condemnation from across the political spectrum, with leaders expressing concern over the safety of public figures. "Violence has no place in democracy," said a state minister, who declined to be named. "We condemn this attack and assure strict action against the perpetrator."

Yadav's supporters have called for a thorough investigation, alleging that the attack was premeditated. The recovery of the knife, they argue, suggests the assailant intended to cause serious harm. Police, however, have urged calm, stating that the investigation is in its early stages.

Security concerns and next steps

The incident has raised questions about the adequacy of security arrangements for politicians during public events. While Yadav was provided with a personal security detail, the attacker managed to get close enough to throw a slipper, prompting calls for stricter protocols.

Authorities are now examining CCTV footage from the venue and recording statements from witnesses. The attacker's mobile phone and other belongings have been seized for forensic analysis. Officials have not yet confirmed whether any charges beyond assault will be pressed, but legal experts suggest that the knife could lead to more serious charges.

As the investigation unfolds, the focus will remain on ensuring the safety of public figures and addressing the polarising rhetoric that has accompanied the controversy. The incident serves as a reminder of the fragile line between free expression and public sentiment in India's charged political environment.

Moving forward, all eyes will be on the police investigation and the potential legal consequences for the attacker, as well as any further statements from Yadav. The episode is likely to intensify the debate over the skit and the broader issue of political accountability.
