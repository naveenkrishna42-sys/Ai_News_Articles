Anil Menon Prepares for First Spacewalk at International Space Station

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Science Original source ↗
Key points
  • NASA astronaut Anil Menon is preparing for his first spacewalk on the International Space Station.
  • The spacewalk is scheduled for next week and will involve maintenance tasks.
  • NASA will cover the event and host a preview news conference.
  • Menon has shared details of a behind-the-scenes ritual before the historic walk.

A Historic Step for Anil Menon

NASA astronaut Anil Menon is gearing up for his first spacewalk at the International Space Station (ISS). The milestone event is scheduled for next week, marking a significant moment in his career. Menon, who is of Indian origin, will step out of the station to perform critical maintenance tasks.

His preparations have been intense. The astronaut has been training for months to handle the challenges of floating in the vacuum of space. NASA officials confirmed that the spacewalk will be part of a series of three planned US spacewalks.

Behind-the-Scenes Ritual

Menon recently revealed a personal ritual he follows before such a high-stakes mission. In a video shared online, he described his routine, which includes checking equipment multiple times and a moment of quiet reflection. “It’s about staying calm and focused,” he said in the clip.

The ritual has drawn attention on social media, with many praising his discipline. Colleagues at NASA have also noted his meticulous approach. The space agency has not disclosed the exact timing of the spacewalk but said it will be covered live.

NASA Coverage and Preview

NASA will host a preview news conference ahead of the spacewalk. The agency plans to brief reporters on the objectives and safety measures. The spacewalk itself will be streamed on NASA’s official channels, allowing the public to watch Menon’s historic first step.

The Indian diaspora has been following Menon’s journey closely. His achievement is seen as a source of pride, especially in a year when India’s own space programme has made strides. Menon’s work on the ISS underscores the collaborative nature of space exploration.

Maintenance Tasks Planned

The spacewalk will focus on upgrading and repairing external equipment on the ISS. Astronauts will replace components and check systems to ensure the station’s smooth operation. Such tasks are routine but require extreme precision.

Menon will be accompanied by an experienced crew member during the spacewalk. Safety tethers and protocols will be in place to prevent any mishaps. The entire operation is expected to last several hours.

What Lies Ahead

After his first spacewalk, Menon is likely to take on more responsibilities aboard the ISS. The mission will add to his growing list of achievements. For now, all eyes are on next week’s event.

NASA will release more details in the coming days. The spacewalk promises to be a defining moment for Menon and a testament to years of preparation. The world will be watching as he takes that first step into the void.
