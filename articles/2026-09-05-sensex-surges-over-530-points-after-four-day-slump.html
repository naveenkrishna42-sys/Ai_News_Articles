Market Rebounds After Four-Day Decline

Indian stock markets opened sharply higher on Fri, 04 Sep 2026, with the Sensex climbing over 530 points in early trade. The move breaks a four-session losing streak that had weighed on investor sentiment and benchmark valuations. Early buying was broad based, reflecting a pause after consecutive declines.

The rebound comes after sustained selling pressure in the previous week that pushed benchmarks lower on profit booking. A sharp intraday recovery offers short-term relief to equity holders who absorbed losses across four sessions. Volume patterns in the opening minutes will determine whether the move sustains through the session.

What Drove The Early Session Turnaround

Buying Interest Returns After Capitulation

Four days of declines typically create oversold conditions that attract value buyers. Early trade on 4 Sep 2026 shows that class of buyers stepping in at lower levels. The Sensex gain of over 530 points signals conviction rather than a muted technical bounce.

Sentiment Shift In Early Trade

Market participants often reassess risk after a multi-day correction. The change in tone is visible in index futures and cash market momentum. A rebound of this magnitude in the first hour suggests fresh orders rather than short covering alone.

Technical Levels And Buyer Implications

For traders, a 530-plus point move resets intraday reference points and changes stop-loss calculations. Retail investors who paused purchases during the slump now face a decision on entry timing versus waiting for confirmation. Institutional desks will monitor whether gains hold above the prior day’s high to validate continuation.

Pricing context matters for portfolio rebalancing. A sharp rebound can improve advance-decline ratios and improve risk-reward for selective buying. Buyers should match position size to volatility and avoid chasing the open without defined exit levels.
