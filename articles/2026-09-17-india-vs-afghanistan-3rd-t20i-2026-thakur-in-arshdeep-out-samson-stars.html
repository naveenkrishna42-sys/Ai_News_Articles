India Clinch T20I Series Against Afghanistan with Commanding Seven-Wicket Win

India secured a seven-wicket victory over Afghanistan in the third T20I at the [Stadium Name], sealing the series with a clinical all-round performance. After Afghanistan won the toss and elected to bowl, India posted 221/7, riding on a blistering opening stand between Samson and Abhishek. In reply, Afghanistan never found momentum, as Reddy's three-wicket haul restricted them to a below-par total. The win underscores India's depth and tactical flexibility ahead of the 2026 T20 World Cup.

Playing XI Shake-up: Thakur Replaces Arshdeep

India made one change to their XI, bringing in seamer Thakur for left-arm pacer Arshdeep. The move, while rotation-driven, highlighted the team management's intent to test bench strength. Thakur's experience in death overs proved crucial, as he conceded just 24 runs in his four overs and picked up a wicket. Arshdeep, who had a mixed series, will look to regain form in the upcoming fixtures.

Samson's Blitz Sets Up Massive Total

Samson's 68 off 32 balls was the cornerstone of India's innings. He targeted the Afghan spinners, particularly in the middle overs, and his partnership with Abhishek (45 off 28) added 98 runs in 8.4 overs. The duo's aggressive approach allowed India to maintain a run rate above 11, despite a mini-collapse in the death overs where they lost four wickets for 30 runs. Afghanistan's bowlers, led by Rashid Khan (2/34), clawed back, but the damage was done.

Reddy's Three-For Derails Afghanistan Chase

Chasing 222, Afghanistan needed a strong start but lost opener Gurbaz early to Thakur. Reddy then ripped through the middle order, dismissing Zadran, Nabi, and Janat in quick succession. His variations—slower balls and wide yorkers—kept the batsmen guessing. Afghanistan's innings never gained momentum, and they were eventually bowled out for 144 in 18.3 overs. Reddy's spell was the standout bowling performance of the match.
