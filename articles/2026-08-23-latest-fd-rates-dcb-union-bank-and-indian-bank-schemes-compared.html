Latest FD Rates: DCB, Union Bank, and Indian Bank Schemes Compared

Key points
  • DCB Bank leads with a competitive 7.50% interest rate.
  • Union Bank and Indian Bank offer stable rates at 6.55% and 6.65%.
  • Compare tenure-based returns to maximize your fixed income.

Understanding New Fixed Deposit Rates

As of August 2026, several Indian banks have adjusted their Fixed Deposit (FD) interest rates to attract retail depositors. DCB Bank currently stands out with an offer of 7.50%, making it a primary contender for individuals looking to park their savings for higher yields. Meanwhile, public sector lenders Union Bank and Indian Bank have pegged their rates at 6.55% and 6.65% respectively.

Fixed deposits remain a cornerstone of financial planning for conservative investors in India. These instruments provide guaranteed returns, shielding your capital from market volatility. Choosing the right bank depends on your liquidity needs and the duration for which you can lock away your funds.

Breakdown of Bank Offers

DCB Bank: The High-Yield Option

DCB Bank's 7.50% rate is particularly attractive for those seeking to beat inflation. This rate usually applies to specific tenures, often ranging from 18 to 36 months. Investors should verify the exact tenure requirements on the bank's portal, as rates fluctuate based on the duration of the deposit.

Union Bank and Indian Bank: Public Sector Stability

Union Bank of India offers a return of 6.55%, providing a secure avenue for risk-averse savers. Similarly, Indian Bank is offering 6.65%. While these rates are lower than private sector peers like DCB, they are often perceived as safer due to the government backing of these institutions. These banks frequently offer additional interest rate hikes of 0.50% for senior citizens, which can significantly boost the effective yield over a five-year period.

Eligibility and Application Process

To open an FD account at these banks, you must be a resident Indian individual or a Hindu Undivided Family (HUF). You will need your PAN card and Aadhaar card to complete the Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements. If you already hold a savings account with the respective bank, the process is streamlined via mobile banking apps or net banking portals.

  • Log in to your bank’s official website or mobile application.
  • Navigate to the 'Investments' or 'Fixed Deposit' section.
  • Select the tenure and amount you wish to deposit.
  • Review the interest rate and maturity value displayed on the screen.
  • Confirm the transaction to generate an e-receipt.

Tax Implications and Strategy

Interest earned on FDs is fully taxable according to your income tax slab. If the interest income exceeds ₹40,000 in a financial year (or ₹50,000 for senior citizens), the bank will deduct Tax Deducted at Source (TDS). You can submit Form 15G or 15H to avoid TDS if your total annual income falls below the taxable limit.

Consider a laddering strategy to manage liquidity. Instead of putting your entire corpus into one long-term deposit, split the amount into multiple FDs with staggered maturity dates. This approach ensures that you have access to cash periodically without breaking a high-interest deposit prematurely and incurring penalties.

Final Recommendation: DCB Bank is ideal for investors prioritizing maximum yield for medium-term goals. If you prefer the security of a public sector bank for long-term wealth preservation, Indian Bank’s 6.65% rate offers a reliable balance of safety and return. Always check the specific 'bulk deposit' vs 'retail deposit' rates, as these can vary significantly.
