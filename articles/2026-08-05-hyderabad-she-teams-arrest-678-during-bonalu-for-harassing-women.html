Hyderabad SHE Teams arrest 678 during Bonalu for harassing women

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • 678 arrested for harassment during Bonalu
  • SHE Teams deployed across key locations
  • Focus on crowded areas and transport hubs

Hyderabad's SHE Teams have arrested 678 individuals for harassing women during the Bonalu festivities, a crackdown that underscores the city's commitment to women's safety during large public gatherings. The arrests were made over the course of the festival, which saw massive crowds thronging temples and streets across the city.

Operation details

The SHE Teams, a special police unit dedicated to addressing crimes against women, were deployed in strength during the festival period. Officers were stationed at key locations, including temple premises, busy market areas, and public transport hubs, where harassment incidents are most likely to occur.

According to officials, the arrests were made for offences ranging from eve-teasing to more serious forms of harassment. The accused were booked under relevant sections of the law, and many were produced before courts immediately after their arrest.

Focus on preventive action

The drive was not just about making arrests but also about preventing incidents before they could escalate. Plainclothes officers were deployed among the crowds to spot and intercept potential offenders. This proactive approach appears to have paid off, with the number of arrests reflecting a strong deterrent message.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact breakdown of cases, but sources indicate that a significant number of arrests were made near major Bonalu pandals and along processional routes. The presence of visible police patrols, along with undercover teams, created a heightened sense of security for women attending the festivities.

Public response and awareness

The crackdown has been widely appreciated by women's groups and the general public. Many have taken to social media to commend the police for their swift action. However, some have also pointed out that such drives need to be sustained beyond the festival period to ensure lasting safety for women in the city.

The SHE Teams have been active in Hyderabad for several years, conducting regular operations in schools, colleges, and public spaces. During Bonalu, their efforts were intensified given the large gatherings and the increased risk of harassment in crowded environments.

Legal proceedings and next steps

All 678 arrested individuals are being processed under the law, with many facing charges that could lead to imprisonment or fines. The police have urged victims to come forward and report any incidents, assuring them of confidentiality and support.

Authorities have also advised women to use the SHE Teams' helpline and mobile app, which allow for quick reporting of harassment. These tools have been instrumental in enabling swift police response during the festival.

As the festival season winds down, the focus now shifts to maintaining the momentum of this crackdown. Police have indicated that similar operations will continue in the coming weeks, particularly in areas with high footfall such as shopping districts and transit points.

Moving forward, the success of this drive will likely shape future policing strategies for major festivals in Hyderabad. The message from the police is clear: harassment of women will not be tolerated, and the law will be enforced with full vigour.
