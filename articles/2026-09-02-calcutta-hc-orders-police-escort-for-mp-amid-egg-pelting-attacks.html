Calcutta High Court Orders Police Escort for TMC MP Amid Protests

The Calcutta High Court has directed the police to provide security cover for Trinamool Congress MP Mahua Moitra after she faced protests during her constituency visit in Nadia, West Bengal. The court's directive comes amid a wave of egg-pelting attacks on political leaders in the state.

MP Faces 'Go Back' Chants and Egg-Pelting

Moitra, a prominent leader in the Trinamool Congress, was met with 'go back' chants and egg-pelting during her visit to her constituency. The protests, which lasted for several hours, raised concerns about the safety of political leaders in the state. Moitra, who is known for her strong political stance, accused her opponents of poor work and corruption.
