Upper Subansiri steps up immunisation drive

📅 2026-08-28 📂 Health Original source ↗
Key points
  • Low vaccination coverage remains a persistent challenge
  • HPV vaccine rollout faces logistical hurdles in remote regions
  • Community engagement and healthcare worker training are critical

Persistent Vaccination Gaps

Upper Subansiri District in Arunachal Pradesh is stepping up efforts to boost routine immunisation and introduce HPV vaccination, but persistent gaps in coverage remain. The district's recent task force meeting highlighted that despite these initiatives, only 45% of children receive the full course of routine vaccines, far below the national target of 90%. The HPV vaccine, which is new to the district, faces additional challenges due to its remote and rugged terrain.

HPV Vaccine Rollout Faces Hurdles

The introduction of HPV vaccination marks a significant step in preventing cervical cancer, but the rollout has been slower than expected. The district's healthcare infrastructure is not yet fully equipped to handle the vaccine's storage requirements, and supply chain issues have delayed distribution. Dr. Anil Kumar, the district's health officer, noted that they are working with state and central agencies to address these logistical challenges.

Community Engagement Critical

Community engagement is proving to be a key factor in the success of these vaccination drives. Local leaders and community health workers are being trained to educate parents about the importance of these vaccines. However, cultural and linguistic barriers pose significant challenges. The district's diverse population includes numerous tribes with their own languages and traditions, making it difficult to reach everyone effectively.

Way Forward

To improve vaccination rates, the district is focusing on several strategies. These include increasing the number of healthcare workers, improving infrastructure, and enhancing community engagement. The district is also exploring the use of mobile vaccination units to reach remote areas. However, these efforts will require sustained funding and political will, as the district's limited resources and remote location make it difficult to achieve the national targets.
