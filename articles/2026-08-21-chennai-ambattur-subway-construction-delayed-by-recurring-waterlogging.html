Chennai: Ambattur subway construction delayed by recurring waterlogging

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Southern Railway conducted a site inspection at the Ambattur subway.
  • Officials cited recurring waterlogging as the main reason for the project delay.
  • The subway project aims to improve transit flow in the growing Chennai suburb.

Inspection at the Site

Senior officials from the Southern Railway visited the Ambattur subway construction site on Friday to evaluate the progress of the long-pending infrastructure project. The visit comes amidst increasing pressure from residents and commuters who face daily traffic bottlenecks in the area.

The inspection team focused on assessing the current structural status of the subway. They reviewed the engineering challenges that have hindered the completion of the work, which is intended to streamline movement between key points in the suburb.

Waterlogging Stalls Progress

During the review, officials identified recurring waterlogging as the primary impediment to the construction schedule. The site, situated in a low-lying zone, often collects stagnant water following rain, which disrupts earthwork and compromises the stability of the foundation layers.

Contractors have struggled to keep the work area dry, leading to frequent interruptions. The railway department noted that specialized pumping machinery has been deployed, yet the volume of water remains a significant concern for the engineering teams on the ground.

Addressing Public Concerns

The Ambattur subway is a critical project for the local transportation network. Residents have frequently expressed frustration over the extended timelines and the lack of a clear completion date. Traffic congestion around the work site has become a persistent issue during peak hours.

Railway authorities have not yet confirmed a revised timeline for the project's completion. They emphasized that the priority remains securing the site against seasonal water ingress to ensure the structural integrity of the subway once finished.

  • Engineers are evaluating additional drainage solutions.
  • Increased monitoring of the construction site has been ordered.
  • Traffic management plans remain in place to assist commuters.

The Southern Railway is expected to coordinate with local municipal bodies to address the underlying drainage issues in the vicinity of the subway. Whether these interventions will accelerate the construction pace in the coming months remains to be seen.
