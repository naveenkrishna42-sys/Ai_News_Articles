Fixed Deposit Interest Rate Landscape

Fixed deposits remain a cornerstone of conservative financial planning for Indian households. As of July 2026, both Canara Bank and Union Bank of India have adjusted their interest rate structures to remain competitive among public sector undertakings (PSUs). Choosing between these two lenders requires a careful look at your specific investment horizon and liquidity needs.

Canara Bank currently offers tiered interest structures that favor medium-term deposits ranging from one to three years. Union Bank of India frequently updates its "special tenure" rates, which often provide a marginal spread over standard card rates. You should verify the current daily rates on the official bank websites before finalizing your decision, as these figures fluctuate based on RBI liquidity guidelines.

Key Features and Reward Structures

Both banks offer standard features such as cumulative and non-cumulative interest payout options. Senior citizens typically receive an additional 0.50% interest over the general public rate. If you are planning for tax efficiency, both banks provide Tax Saver Fixed Deposits, which allow you to claim deductions under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. These deposits come with a mandatory lock-in period of five years.

  • Premature Withdrawal: Both institutions apply a penalty on the interest rate if you break the deposit before maturity. This is usually between 0.50% and 1.00% depending on the amount.
  • Auto-Renewal: Digital platforms now allow you to set auto-renewal instructions, ensuring your principal and earned interest are reinvested without manual intervention.
  • Loan Against FD: You can avail of an overdraft facility against your deposit, usually up to 90% of the principal amount, without needing to liquidate the investment.

Eligibility and Online Application Process

To open an FD account, you must hold a valid savings account with the respective bank. Resident Indians, Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs), and trusts are eligible to invest. You will need your PAN card, Aadhaar number, and updated KYC documents to proceed.

Applying online is the most efficient route. Log into your internet banking portal or use the mobile app of the bank. Navigate to the 'Deposits' or 'Investments' section and select 'Open Fixed Deposit'. Input the principal amount, tenure, and payout frequency. Ensure you select the 'Nomination' facility during this process to avoid legal hurdles for your beneficiaries. Upon successful submission, the bank will generate an e-receipt, which serves as your proof of investment.

Maximizing Your Returns

Before locking your capital, assess your cash flow requirements. If you anticipate needing funds in the next twelve months, opt for a shorter tenure or ladder your deposits. Laddering involves splitting your corpus into multiple FDs with staggered maturity dates. This strategy provides liquidity while allowing you to benefit from potential interest rate hikes in the future.

For investors who prioritize stability and government-backed security, these PSU banks are optimal choices. Canara Bank generally performs well for depositors seeking reliable, long-term wealth preservation. Union Bank of India is often preferred by those looking for slightly higher short-term promotional rates. Review the 'Special Tenure' offerings on each bank's portal, as these specific schemes often offer the highest yield for a fixed window of time.
