# TivraNews Content Integrity

TivraNews contains news, finance, business, technology, product and affiliate content.
The highest priority is factual accuracy, source attribution, and user trust.

## Source-First Policy
For current or time-sensitive information:
1. Identify the original/authoritative source.
2. Verify the relevant facts.
3. Record the source URL and source name.
4. Record publication/update date.
5. Never invent facts to fill missing information.

Do not fabricate:
- Prices, discounts, or coupon terms
- Product specifications
- Financial rates (FD, Savings, Loans, EMI)
- Vacancies, company announcements, or regulatory mandates
- Statistics, casualty figures, or quotes

## Financial & Banking Content
For banking, loans, credit cards, insurance, investments, FD rates, RBI, taxation:
- Do not publish unverified rates, fees, benefits, or eligibility.
- Clearly distinguish official facts from editorial comparisons.
- Never use placeholder bank names ("Bank A", "Bank B"). Use official institutions.

## Placeholder Protection
Never publish:
- "Bank A", "Bank B", "Company X"
- Dummy prices or fake URLs
- Incomplete tables or placeholder text
