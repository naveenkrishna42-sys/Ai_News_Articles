The Odyssey Crosses Rs 100 Crore In India, Nolan's Second Century

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The Odyssey crossed Rs 100 crore net in India on Day 9.
  • It is the first Hollywood film of 2026 to achieve this milestone in India.
  • This is Christopher Nolan's second film to enter the Rs 100 crore club in India after Oppenheimer.

Nolan's Epic Sails Past Rs 100 Crore Mark

Christopher Nolan's 'The Odyssey' has become the first Hollywood film of 2026 to enter the Rs 100 crore club in India. The epic mythological drama achieved the milestone on its ninth day of release, according to early box office estimates.

This marks Nolan's second film to cross the coveted mark in the country after 'Oppenheimer', which had collected over Rs 150 crore nett in India in 2023. The director's latest offering has now set a new benchmark for Hollywood releases in the Indian market this year.

Strong Second Weekend Performance

The film earned an estimated $84 million globally in its second weekend, setting a new record for a Christopher Nolan film. Industry analysts attribute the sustained momentum to strong word-of-mouth and repeat viewership.

In India, the film has maintained steady occupancy across major metro cities and multiplex chains. The Hindi-dubbed version has particularly performed well in non-metro markets, expanding the film's reach beyond traditional English-language audiences.

India Box Office Breakdown

Trade sources indicate that 'The Odyssey' collected approximately Rs 12-14 crore nett in India on its second Saturday, pushing its nine-day total past the Rs 100 crore milestone. The film's strong hold in its second week suggests a healthy lifetime run ahead.

Weekend numbers from key circuits like Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, and Bengaluru have remained robust, with evening shows continuing to see high occupancy. The film's premium formats, including IMAX and 4DX, have contributed significantly to the overall collection.

Homer's Epic Gets Modern Revival

Nolan's adaptation of Homer's ancient Greek poem has sparked renewed interest in classical literature. Bookstores across India have reported a surge in sales of 'The Odyssey' and other works by Homer since the film's release.

Educational institutions and book clubs have organised special screenings and discussions, blending cinematic experience with literary analysis. The film's success has also prompted publishers to release new editions of the original text with film tie-in covers.

Critics have praised Nolan's visual storytelling, with many noting that the film makes the 3,000-year-old epic accessible to modern audiences without diluting its thematic depth. The director's signature non-linear narrative style finds a natural home in the episodic structure of Homer's original work.

What's Next for the Epic

With the Rs 100 crore milestone achieved in just nine days, trade analysts expect 'The Odyssey' to challenge the lifetime collections of 'Oppenheimer' in India. The coming week will determine if the film can sustain its momentum through the weekdays and into the third weekend, especially with new releases slated for the following Friday.
