Anurag Kumar appointed Delhi Police chief, replaces Satish Golchha

📅 2026-07-17 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Anurag Kumar, a 1994-batch IPS officer, appointed Delhi Police Commissioner
  • He replaces Satish Golchha; the change was announced by the Centre
  • Kumar previously served as the head of an Indian spy agency

The Centre on Friday appointed Anurag Kumar as the new Commissioner of Delhi Police. He replaces Satish Golchha, who has been removed from the post.

Kumar is a 1994-batch IPS officer. He previously served as the chief of a prominent Indian spy agency. His appointment marks a significant change in leadership for the national capital's police force.

Who is Anurag Kumar?

Anurag Kumar is a seasoned officer with decades of experience in internal security and intelligence. His last posting was as the head of an external intelligence agency, a role that placed him at the centre of India's national security apparatus.

Before that, he served in various capacities across the country. His portfolio includes stints in counter-terrorism and law and order. Colleagues describe him as a low-profile but operationally sharp officer.

Why was Satish Golchha removed?

The government has not officially stated the reasons for Satish Golchha's removal. Speculation in bureaucratic circles points to recent law and order challenges in the capital.

Delhi has seen a series of protests and security breaches in recent months. Sources say the Centre wanted a change in leadership to address these concerns more aggressively. Golchha's tenure lasted less than two years.

What changes are expected under Kumar?

Kumar takes charge at a time when Delhi faces multiple policing challenges. These range from traffic management to communal harmony and VIP security.

His experience in intelligence is expected to bring a sharper focus on preventive policing. Officers in the force expect a tightening of surveillance and a more data-driven approach to crime control.

The new commissioner is also likely to review the current deployment of personnel. A senior officer, speaking on condition of anonymity, said: "He is known for his meticulous planning. The force should expect some structural changes."

Political reactions

The appointment has drawn mixed reactions from political parties. The ruling party at the Centre welcomed the move, calling it a step towards a more secure Delhi.

The opposition has questioned the timing of the change. Some leaders have demanded a detailed explanation for Golchha's removal. Others have pointed out that frequent changes in top police posts can affect long-term strategy.

Neither the Ministry of Home Affairs nor the Delhi Police has issued a detailed statement on the transition. The official handover is expected to take place in the next 24 hours.

Anurag Kumar's tenure will be closely watched. His first major test will be managing the upcoming festival season, when the city sees a surge in crowds and potential security threats.
