Yami Gautam Dhar's Nayyi Navelli to Release in October

A New Fantasy Venture

Amazon MGM Studios and Colour Yellow Productions have confirmed that their upcoming project, Nayyi Navelli, will hit theatres this October. The film stars Yami Gautam Dhar in a narrative described as a fantasy family-drama.

The production marks a significant collaboration between the global studio and Aanand L. Rai’s banner. While plot specifics remain under wraps, the film is expected to combine elements of traditional family storytelling with fantasy tropes.

High-Stakes October Competition

The announcement of the October release window brings the film into direct competition with major marquee projects. Market reports indicate that Nayyi Navelli will go head-to-head with Jailer 2, headlined by Rajinikanth.

Such a clash typically signals a busy festive period for exhibitors. Industry observers are monitoring how the screens will be divided between these two high-profile releases as the festive season approaches.

Social Media Buzz

The film has already generated significant online engagement following the release of its first promotional poster. Aditya Dhar, husband of the lead actor, added to the conversation by sharing the poster on social media with a lighthearted comment questioning what he had married into.

Yami Gautam Dhar responded to the post, maintaining the playful tone regarding her character in the film. This public interaction has helped keep the title trending among fans as the marketing campaign begins to ramp up.
