Cyberabad Police Urge Staggered Departures as Heavy Rain Looms

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Cyberabad Traffic Police issued advisory for staggered early office departures.
  • Heavy rain forecast prompts work-from-home recommendations.
  • Citizens urged to avoid waterlogging-prone areas and check traffic updates.

Police Issue Advisory Ahead of Heavy Rain

The Cyberabad Traffic Police have advised offices in the region to implement staggered early departures for employees on Monday, as the Indian Meteorological Department forecasts heavy rain in Hyderabad and surrounding areas. The advisory, released early Monday morning, aims to reduce traffic congestion and ensure commuter safety during the expected downpour.

Officials from the Cyberabad Traffic Police noted that the staggered timing would help spread out the rush hour traffic, preventing bottlenecks and allowing emergency services to move freely. They also recommended that companies consider allowing employees to work from home where possible, to minimize the number of vehicles on the road.

Safety Tips for Commuters

In a series of posts on social media, the police urged citizens to avoid waterlogged areas and underpasses, and to park vehicles in elevated spots to prevent damage from flooding. They also advised commuters to check live traffic updates before stepping out, and to use alternative routes if their usual path is prone to waterlogging.

The police have deployed additional personnel at key junctions and vulnerable points to manage traffic and assist motorists. They also cautioned drivers to maintain safe distances and reduce speed during the rain, as visibility may be low.

Work-From-Home Encouraged

The advisory specifically asked IT companies and other large offices in the Cyberabad area, which includes major tech hubs like Hitech City and Gachibowli, to be flexible with employee schedules. Many firms have already responded positively, with several sending internal memos allowing staff to leave early or work remotely for the day.

This is not the first time such advisories have been issued during monsoon season in Hyderabad, which often experiences flash floods and traffic chaos during heavy rain. The local administration has been working on improving drainage, but residents remain wary of sudden waterlogging.

What Citizens Should Do

Residents are advised to stay indoors unless absolutely necessary, and to keep emergency numbers handy. The police have also set up a dedicated helpline for traffic-related queries and assistance during the rain.

Those who must travel are urged to use public transport where possible, and to avoid driving through flooded streets. The police have warned that vehicles may be towed if parked in no-parking zones that obstruct emergency vehicles.

The rain is expected to continue through the evening, with the possibility of thunderstorms. Officials have not yet confirmed any damage or incidents related to the weather, but teams are on standby for quick response.

As the city braces for the downpour, the key takeaway is to plan ahead, stay informed, and prioritize safety over schedules.
