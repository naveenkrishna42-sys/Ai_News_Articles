HMRL to upgrade 10 high-traffic Hyderabad Metro stations

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • HMRL will upgrade 10 high-traffic metro stations across Hyderabad.
  • Upgrades include wider platforms, better signage and improved lighting.
  • Work is expected to start in August 2026 and be completed in phases.

Hyderabad Metro Rail Limited (HMRL) has announced a plan to upgrade 10 metro stations that see the highest footfall across the network. The move aims to ease congestion during peak hours and improve the overall commuter experience.

Officials identified the stations based on daily passenger data. The selected stations include Ameerpet, Miyapur, LB Nagar, Kukatpally, KPHB Colony, Erragadda, Bharathi Nagar, SR Nagar, Panjagutta and Paradise. These stations collectively handle a significant portion of the city's metro traffic.

What the upgrades will cover

The renovation will focus on three key areas: passenger flow, comfort and safety. HMRL plans to widen ticketing and entry areas at several stations to reduce bottlenecks during rush hours.

Better signage and digital information boards will be installed to help passengers navigate more easily. The stations will also get improved lighting, especially in underpasses and parking lots. Additional escalators and elevators are part of the plan for stations like Ameerpet and Miyapur, which handle heavy inter-change traffic.

Focus on accessibility and amenities

HMRL has said that upgrades will also make stations more accessible for elderly passengers and persons with disabilities. This includes wider gates and tactile paving. Waiting areas will be expanded, and more seating will be added on platforms.

Dustbins and water dispensers will be placed at more locations. The aim is to make the stations cleaner and more comfortable for daily commuters who spend significant time inside the network. HMRL expects these upgrades to cut down the average time a passenger spends inside a station by at least 10-15%.

Timeline and execution

Work is expected to begin in August 2026. HMRL will carry out the upgrades in phases to minimise disruption to regular services. Each station will be taken up one at a time, with night shifts and weekend closures used for major construction work.

A senior HMRL official said the agency has already prepared detailed project reports for all 10 stations. Contractors will be finalised within the next few weeks. The entire upgrade is expected to be completed within 12 to 18 months, depending on the complexity of work at each site.

The Hyderabad Metro, which began operations in 2017, now carries over 4.5 lakh passengers daily. The network has expanded to cover 70 km across three corridors. The 10 stations selected for upgrade account for nearly 40% of the total daily ridership, according to HMRL data.

Commuters have often complained about overcrowding and long queues at these stations, especially during morning and evening peak hours. The upgrade is seen as a direct response to those grievances.

HMRL will share more details on the specific changes planned for each station in the coming weeks. Commuters can expect clearer communication on diversions and service changes before construction begins.
