Supreme Court may force UP to buy Banke Bihari temple land

📅 2026-08-26 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Supreme Court may intervene in land acquisition dispute for Banke Bihari temple
  • Court orders all donations must go directly to temple treasury
  • Bar and Bench says no impediment to temple redevelopment will be tolerated

Supreme Court May Force Uttar Pradesh Government To Acquire Land For Banke Bihari Temple

The Supreme Court has indicated it may intervene in the land acquisition dispute for the Banke Bihari temple in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. The court's hint comes as the Uttar Pradesh government has been unable to acquire the necessary land for the temple's redevelopment.

This is not the first time the court has intervened in the temple's affairs. In 2023, the court ordered that all donations to the temple must go directly to the temple treasury, with no siphoning of funds allowed.

All Donations Must Go Directly To Temple Treasury

The Supreme Court has made it clear that all donations to the Banke Bihari temple must go directly to the temple treasury. This order was given in response to reports of funds being siphoned off for other purposes.

This is a significant development, as it ensures that the temple's funds are used for their intended purpose. It also sends a strong message to those who might be tempted to misuse the temple's resources.

No Impediment To Temple Redevelopment Will Be Tolerated

The Bar and Bench of the Supreme Court has made it clear that no impediment to the Banke Bihari temple's redevelopment will be tolerated. This includes the land acquisition dispute.

This is a strong statement from the court, and it is likely to put pressure on the Uttar Pradesh government to resolve the land acquisition issue as quickly as possible.

What This Means For Banke Bihari Temple

The Supreme Court's intervention in the land acquisition dispute for the Banke Bihari temple is a significant development. It shows that the court is willing to take a strong stance in protecting the temple's interests.

However, it remains to be seen what impact this will have on the land acquisition process. The Uttar Pradesh government will need to act quickly to resolve the issue, as the court's intervention could put pressure on them to do so.
