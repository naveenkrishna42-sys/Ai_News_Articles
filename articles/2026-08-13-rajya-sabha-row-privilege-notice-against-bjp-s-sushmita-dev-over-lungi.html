Rajya Sabha row: Privilege notice against BJP's Sushmita Dev over 'lungiwala' remark

📅 2026-08-13 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • John Brittas filed privilege notice against Sushmita Dev
  • Rajya Sabha witnessed uproar over 'lungiwala' remark
  • House was adjourned amid the controversy
  • Counter-reactions over 'dhoti' followed on social media

Uproar in the Upper House

The Rajya Sabha witnessed unprecedented scenes on Wednesday as CPI(M) MP John Brittas announced he had filed a privilege notice against BJP MP Sushmita Dev over her alleged 'lungiwala' remark. The comment, made during a debate, triggered sharp protests from opposition benches, forcing the Chair to adjourn the House briefly.

Brittas, who represents Kerala, termed the remark "highly derogatory" and said it insulted the dignity of the House and its members. He accused Dev of making personal attacks instead of sticking to substantive issues, alleging a pattern of such behaviour from ruling party members.

What sparked the row

The exact context of the 'lungiwala' remark remains unclear, but sources suggest it was made during a heated exchange on a policy matter. The word, referring to a person wearing a lungi, was perceived as a casteist and regional slur aimed at Brittas, who hails from Kerala where the garment is commonly worn.

Opposition MPs, including those from the Congress, DMK and TMC, rallied behind Brittas, demanding that Dev apologise unconditionally. Some even staged a walkout, chanting slogans against the BJP, which the Chair struggled to control.

Political reactions and 'dhoti' counter

Following the uproar, social media was abuzz with reactions, with several users drawing parallels to the 'dhoti' remarks made against Tamil Nadu politicians in the past. Hashtags like #RespectLungi and #ShameOnBJP trended briefly, with netizens condemning the alleged slur.

Meanwhile, BJP leaders defended Dev, claiming her remark was taken out of context. They accused the opposition of deliberately disrupting the House over a trivial matter, pointing to pending legislative business. Dev herself remained defiant, stating she would respond in detail after reviewing the proceedings.

Privilege notice details

Brittas's privilege notice, submitted to the Rajya Sabha Secretariat, seeks action against Dev for allegedly breaching parliamentary privilege by using unparliamentary language. Under the rules, a privilege motion can be moved if a member's remarks are deemed to obstruct the House's functioning or lower its dignity.

Rajya Sabha Chairman Jagdeep Dhankhar has not yet ruled on the admissibility of the notice. Sources say the matter could be referred to the Committee of Privileges for further examination, a process that may take weeks.

Previous instances

This is not the first time the Upper House has seen such rows. In recent years, several privilege notices have been filed against MPs for personal remarks, though most were resolved through apologies or withdrawals. However, the frequency of such incidents has raised concerns about declining decorum in Parliament.

Political analysts note that the incident reflects the deep polarisation in the current political climate, with regional attire becoming a flashpoint for identity politics. The lungi, a common garment in southern India, has often been stereotyped in northern discourse, making the remark particularly sensitive.

What next

The Rajya Sabha is likely to take up the matter when it reconvenes, with opposition MPs demanding a statement from Dev. The privilege committee's decision, if referred, will be crucial in determining whether the BJP MP faces any sanction.

Observers will be watching whether the government chooses to defuse the tension or allow the issue to escalate, as the winter session has a packed agenda. For now, the 'lungiwala' remark has added another flashpoint to an already contentious parliamentary session, with both sides digging in for a prolonged standoff.
