Infosys, IndiGo, Cipla Among Major Firms Reporting Q1 Results on July 23

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Infosys, IndiGo, Cipla, Meesho, Vishal Mega Mart, Go Digit, PVR Inox, Mphasis to report Q1 results on July 23
  • Infosys ADRs fell over 4% after Q1 revenue guidance cut overshadowed profit growth
  • Ashiss Kumar Dash appointed as Infosys CEO-designate, set to take over from current MD & CEO
  • GIFT Nifty traded 0.4% lower ahead of the earnings, indicating cautious market sentiment

Mumbai: A slew of major Indian companies across sectors are set to announce their financial results for the April-June quarter on July 23. The list includes Infosys, IndiGo, Cipla, Meesho, Vishal Mega Mart, Go Digit, PVR Inox, and Mphasis, among others. Investors are bracing for a busy day as these earnings will offer a snapshot of corporate health in the first quarter of the financial year.

Infosys in focus after ADR drop

All eyes will be on Infosys, the country's second-largest IT services exporter, which reports its Q1 numbers later today. The company's American depositary receipts (ADRs) fell over 4% in overnight trading on Wednesday. The decline came after the company's revenue guidance for the quarter was reportedly cut, overshadowing a rise in net profit.

Infosys had earlier this week appointed Ashiss Kumar Dash as its new managing director and chief executive officer-designate. Dash, an IIT Kharagpur alumnus with three decades of experience at Infosys, will take over the top role from the current MD & CEO. The leadership transition adds another layer of interest to the earnings announcement.

Market sentiment cautious

Ahead of the earnings rush, GIFT Nifty traded 0.4% lower, signaling cautious sentiment among traders. The benchmark index's movement is often seen as an early indicator of how the broader market might react to the day's corporate announcements. Analysts will parse Infosys's commentary on client spending, deal pipeline, and demand outlook for the rest of the year.

IndiGo, the country's largest airline by market share, will also report its Q1 numbers. The aviation sector has benefited from strong domestic travel demand, but rising fuel costs and capacity constraints remain headwinds. Cipla's earnings will be watched for updates on its US generic pipeline and domestic formulation sales.

E-commerce and retail in focus

E-commerce platform Meesho and hypermarket chain Vishal Mega Mart are among the companies reporting results. Both firms operate in highly competitive segments and their numbers will shed light on consumer spending patterns in smaller cities and towns. Meesho has been expanding its seller base while Vishal Mega Mart has focused on value retail.

Insurance firm Go Digit and multiplex operator PVR Inox are also on the list. Go Digit, which went public last year, will be watched for premium growth and loss ratios. PVR Inox's performance will reflect the recovery in footfalls at movie theatres, especially after a strong lineup of film releases in the quarter.

IT services firm Mphasis rounds out the key names. Its results will be compared against Infosys's performance and broader trends in the technology sector.

The market will react to these numbers in the coming sessions. Investors will look for signs of margin improvement, demand resilience, and management guidance to adjust their portfolios.
