Indian benchmark indices opened on a flat note on Thursday, 13 August, as investors weighed mixed global cues against a resilient domestic earnings season. The Nifty and the Sensex traded within a narrow band in early deals, with buying interest visible in select midcap and specialty chemical names.

Market breadth remained slightly positive, though traders chose to stay cautious ahead of key economic data due later in the week. Volatility, as measured by India VIX, stayed subdued, indicating that participants are not expecting a sharp directional move in either direction.

Aarti Industries: The stock to buy today

Among individual picks, BusinessLine has flagged Aarti Industries as the stock to buy for today's session. The specialty chemicals manufacturer has been on a steady uptrend, supported by robust order flows and a favourable demand environment in agrochemical and pharmaceutical intermediates.

Technical charts suggest the stock is trading above its key moving averages, with momentum indicators pointing to a continuation of the upmove. Analysts believe the current consolidation phase offers a fresh entry opportunity for short-to-medium term traders.

What's driving the bullish view

The company's focus on backward integration and capacity expansion has improved its margin profile over recent quarters. With global supply chains diversifying away from China, Indian specialty chemical players like Aarti Industries are seen as key beneficiaries of the 'China plus one' sourcing strategy.

Moreover, the management's guidance on volume growth and new product launches has kept investor sentiment positive. Brokerages have maintained a 'buy' stance, citing a comfortable valuation relative to its growth trajectory.

Broader market cues to watch

Across the Street, attention is on the upcoming inflation print and monsoon progress, which could influence the RBI's rate path. Foreign institutional investors have turned net buyers in recent sessions, providing some support to largecaps.
