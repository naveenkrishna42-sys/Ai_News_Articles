Key Features and Benefits

When choosing a credit card for large purchases, consider the following key features and benefits:

  • High credit limit: A high credit limit allows you to make large purchases without worrying about exceeding your limit.

  • Insurance benefits: Credit cards often include insurance benefits, such as travel insurance or purchase protection, which can provide peace of mind.
