Indian Army to receive indigenous AK-203 rifles by 2027

📅 2026-08-25 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Indian Army to receive 100% indigenous AK-203 'Sher' rifles by April 2027
  • 10 prototype rifles to start trials in a month
  • Indo-Russian Rifles Private Limited (IRRPL) to manufacture the rifles
  • AK-203 is a modernized version of the AK-47
  • India aims to reduce reliance on foreign imports

Indian Army to receive indigenous AK-203 rifles by 2027

The Indian Army is set to receive 100% indigenous AK-203 'Sher' rifles by April 2027. This marks a significant milestone in India's journey towards self-reliance in defense manufacturing. The rifles will be manufactured by Indo-Russian Rifles Private Limited (IRRPL), a joint venture between the Indian government and Russian defense company Kalashnikov Concern.

10 prototype rifles to start trials

Trials for the AK-203 rifles will begin in a month with 10 prototype rifles. These trials will be conducted by the Indian Army to assess the performance, reliability, and effectiveness of the rifles. The results of these trials will be crucial in determining the future of the AK-203 in the Indian Armed Forces.

Indo-Russian Rifles Private Limited (IRRPL) to manufacture the rifles

IRRPL is responsible for the manufacturing of the AK-203 rifles. The company has been working on the project for several years, and the successful completion of the trials will pave the way for mass production. This will not only reduce India's reliance on foreign imports but also create job opportunities in the defense sector.

AK-203 is a modernized version of the AK-47

The AK-203 is a modernized version of the AK-47, a rifle that has been in service with the Indian Army for decades. The AK-203 features several improvements, including a new barrel, gas system, and trigger mechanism. These upgrades make the rifle more accurate, reliable, and durable, making it a suitable replacement for the older AK-47 rifles.

India aims to reduce reliance on foreign imports

India's decision to manufacture its own AK-203 rifles is part of a broader strategy to reduce reliance on foreign imports. The country has been working towards achieving self-reliance in defense manufacturing, and the AK-203 project is a significant step in that direction. By manufacturing its own rifles, India can ensure a steady supply of weapons and reduce its vulnerability to supply chain disruptions.
