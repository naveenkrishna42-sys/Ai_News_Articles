Toxic's Rapid Box Office Boom Hides Underlying Backlash

📅 2026-08-27 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Toxic's record Day 2 collections
  • BookMyShow rating disappearance
  • Critics' backlash due to misogynistic themes
  • Box office versus critical reception divergence

The Box Office Success Story of Toxic

Just two days into its release, Kannada superstar Yash's 'Toxic' has become a box office phenomenon. The film, also starring Bollywood actress Kiara Advani, raked in over Rs 36 crore on its second day, taking its India net total to Rs 137 crore and worldwide gross to a staggering Rs 190 crore. This success has been hailed as a further testament to Yash's star power and the regional film industry's growing clout.

Vanishing BookMyShow Rating: A Cautionary Sign

A closer look, however, reveals a worrying trend. The film's rating on BookMyShow, India's leading movie ticketing platform, has mysteriously disappeared. While the average rating still stands at 3.8, the number of reviews has plummeted from over 10,000 to a mere 1,000 in just a few days. This sudden vanishing act, amidst the film's astronomical box office success, raises eyebrows.

Critical Acclaim or Backlash?

Toxic's impressive box office numbers stand in stark contrast to its critical reception. While the film is being widely praised for its technical prowess and Yash's performance, it is also drawing severe flak for its treatment of women. Several publications, including The News Minute and NDTV, have published opinion pieces lambasting the film's misogynistic themes and toxic masculinity.

Toxic's Key Facts and Criticisms

  • Released in five languages, Toxic has broken multiple opening weekend records.
  • The film's unique selling point is Yash's character, who transforms from a 'zero' to a powerful figure.
  • However, critics argue that the film's portrayal of women as objects of revenge and sexual gratification is unacceptable.
  • The film's defense that it only mimics societal norms does little to assuage critics.

Toxic's Metacritic score of 40 and BookMyShow rating of 3.8, despite a high IMDb rating of 7.5, indicate a critical divide. The film's box office numbers, however, tell a different story, proving that commercial success can often overshadow critical consensus.

What the Backlash Means

The backlash against Toxic underscores a growing trend in Indian cinema. Audiences are increasingly willing to overlook problematic content if the film delivers on action, drama, or star power. However, the critical community is pushing back, demanding more progressive storytelling. This tug-of-war reflects the changing Indian society, where audiences want escapism but also expect films to reflect their growing social awareness.

Toxic's success, despite its contentious themes, signals a complex landscape. Film industries worldwide grapple with this dichotomy - box office success versus critical acclaim. As Indian cinema continues to grow, it will be interesting to see how these two metrics intersect and diverge in the future.
