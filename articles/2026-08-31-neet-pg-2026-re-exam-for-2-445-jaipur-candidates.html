NEET PG 2026: Re-exam for 2,445 Jaipur candidates

NEET PG 2026 re-exam dates have been announced for 2,445 candidates whose original exam in Jaipur was disrupted by technical glitches and protests. The Press Information Bureau (PIB) has dismissed reports of paper leaks, stating that the re-exam is necessary to ensure fairness.

Tech glitches and protests disrupt Jaipur exam

The original NEET PG exam in Jaipur was marred by repeated computer crashes, leading to chaos and protests. Over 2,000 candidates were affected, with some reporting that their answers were not recorded properly. The PIB has acknowledged the technical issues but maintains that they were not due to any malpractice.

Hyderabad centre reports high attendance

In contrast, the NEET PG exam in Hyderabad reported a high attendance of 97.47%. This highlights the variability in exam conditions across different centres, which is a concern for the fairness of the exam.

Re-exam dates yet to be announced for other affected centres

The re-exam dates for the 2,445 Jaipur candidates have been announced, but no such dates have been announced for other affected centres. This raises questions about the consistency of the exam process and the fairness of the re-exam process.

Broader impact on NEET PG 2026

The NEET PG 2026 exam has been plagued by technical issues and protests, which have raised concerns about the fairness and reliability of the exam. The PIB's dismissal of paper leak claims and the announcement of re-exam dates for only some candidates are likely to further fuel these concerns. It is crucial that the exam authorities address these issues transparently and ensure that all candidates have a fair chance to participate in the exam.
