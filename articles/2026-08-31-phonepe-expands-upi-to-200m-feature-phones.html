PhonePe expands UPI to 200M+ feature phones

📅 2026-08-31 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • PhonePe now offers UPI payments on feature phones without internet
  • Service targets over 200 million feature phone users in India
  • UPI 123Pay uses USSD technology for offline transactions
  • Service aims to bridge the digital divide in India's financial ecosystem

PhonePe's Bold Move to Reach India's Unbanked

PhonePe, India's leading digital payments platform, has launched UPI 123Pay, a service that brings UPI payments to over 200 million feature phone users without requiring internet connectivity. This move is a significant step towards financial inclusion, as it targets the vast population that remains excluded from the formal banking system.

How UPI 123Pay Works

UPI 123Pay uses USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data) technology, which allows users to make UPI payments via their feature phones. The service is available in multiple Indian languages, making it accessible to a diverse user base. Users can initiate a transaction by dialing a USSD code, entering the recipient's UPI ID, and confirming the transaction.

Key Features of UPI 123Pay

  • No internet connectivity required
  • Available in multiple Indian languages
  • Simple USSD-based interface
  • Integration with existing UPI ecosystem

The Broader Impact of UPI 123Pay

The launch of UPI 123Pay is expected to have a profound impact on India's financial ecosystem. It will enable over 200 million feature phone users to participate in the digital payments revolution, thereby boosting economic activity and financial inclusion. The service will also help in reducing the digital divide, as it caters to the needs of the unbanked and underbanked population.

Challenges and Considerations

While the launch of UPI 123Pay is a significant step towards financial inclusion, it also presents certain challenges. The service relies on USSD technology, which has certain limitations, such as slower transaction speeds and a less user-friendly interface compared to internet-based services. Additionally, the service's reach is limited to areas with good mobile network coverage.
