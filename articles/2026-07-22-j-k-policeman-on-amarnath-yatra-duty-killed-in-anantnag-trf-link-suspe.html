J&K policeman on Amarnath Yatra duty killed in Anantnag; TRF link suspected

📅 2026-07-22 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • A policeman was shot dead at close range in Anantnag while deployed for Amarnath Yatra security.
  • Police suspect the attacker is a new recruit of the banned Lashkar-e-Taiba front, The Resistance Front (TRF).
  • Over 2,000 people have been detained across Jammu and Kashmir in connection with the attack.
  • Jammu and Kashmir L-G condemned the attack, saying the 'heinous act will not go unpunished'.

A Jammu and Kashmir policeman was shot dead at close range in Anantnag district on Wednesday while deployed for Amarnath Yatra security duties. Officials suspect the attacker is a fresh recruit of The Resistance Front (TRF), a proxy outfit of Lashkar-e-Taiba.

The attack took place in the south Kashmir district. The officer was critically injured and later succumbed to his injuries. The police have not officially named the deceased or disclosed his rank.

Security forces launch massive crackdown

Following the killing, security forces launched a widespread cordon-and-search operation across the region. Over 2,000 people have been detained for questioning, according to official sources. Multiple police and paramilitary teams are combing through villages and urban pockets in Anantnag and adjoining districts.

The crackdown is one of the largest in recent months. Authorities are trying to trace the attacker and determine if he had local logistical support.

L-G condemns attack, vows action

Jammu and Kashmir Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha condemned the attack in a post on social media. He said, "This heinous act will not go unpunished." Sinha directed security agencies to take swift action against those responsible.

The L-G's office said the administration is committed to ensuring the safety of Amarnath pilgrims and security personnel. The annual yatra is currently underway, with thousands of devotees travelling to the cave shrine in the Himalayas.

Suspected TRF recruit behind the killing

Preliminary investigations suggest the attacker is a newly radicalised recruit of The Resistance Front. TRF has emerged as a major militant group in Kashmir over the past few years, often targeting security forces and local police. The group is considered a front for Pakistan-based Lashkar-e-Taiba.

Police sources said the attacker fired at the policeman from close range before fleeing the scene. No group has claimed responsibility so far. Investigators are scanning call detail records and social media activity to track the suspect.

The killing has heightened security concerns along the yatra route. Additional forces have been deployed at checkpoints and sensitive locations in Anantnag.

What happens next: The police are expected to release more details about the slain officer and the suspect once the investigation progresses. The massive detention drive is likely to continue over the next few days as security forces try to prevent any further attacks during the ongoing yatra.
