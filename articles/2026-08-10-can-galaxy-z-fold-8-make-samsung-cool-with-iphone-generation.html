Can Galaxy Z Fold 8 make Samsung cool with iPhone generation?

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung launches Galaxy Z Fold8, Fold8 Ultra, Z Flip8, Watch Ultra2, and Watch9
  • New foldables aim to attract younger, iPhone-centric consumers in India
  • Early discounts on Z Flip8 and other models signal competitive pricing strategy

Samsung has officially launched its latest foldable lineup in India, betting big on the Galaxy Z Fold8 series and the Z Flip8 to win over a generation that has grown up with iPhones. The new devices, along with the Galaxy Watch Ultra2 and Watch9, went on sale across the country, with early discounts already available on the Z Flip8.

The question now is whether these foldables can do what previous Galaxy phones could not — make Samsung the default choice for young, style-conscious consumers who have long seen Apple as the trendsetter.

A pocket-friendly pitch

The Galaxy Z Fold8 has been described in early reviews as the 'pocket phone' that actually fits your jeans. That is a pointed shift from earlier foldables, which were often criticized for their bulk and weight. Samsung appears to have listened, slimming down the device and making it more practical for daily use.

The Z Flip8, meanwhile, leans into compactness and style. It is positioned as a fashion-forward device, with a clamshell design that appeals to users who want something different from the rectangular slab that dominates the market.

This is a direct challenge to the iPhone, which has remained visually consistent for years. Samsung is hoping that the novelty of a folding screen — paired with a form factor that slips easily into a pocket — will be enough to pull younger buyers away from Apple's ecosystem.

Pricing and early deals

Samsung is not waiting for demand to build. Deals are already live, with the Z Flip8 getting a discount out of the gate. The Z Fold8 and Z Fold8 Ultra are also on sale, according to reports from GSMArena.

This aggressive pricing strategy is notable in a market like India, where smartphone buyers are highly price-sensitive. By offering early discounts, Samsung is trying to lower the entry barrier for foldables, which have traditionally been seen as premium, niche devices.

The company is also bundling its new watches into the ecosystem. The Galaxy Watch Ultra2 and Watch9 are designed to work seamlessly with the new phones, creating a connected experience that rivals Apple's Watch and iPhone integration.

The Indian market test

India is a critical battleground for Samsung. The country has one of the largest smartphone user bases in the world, and it is also a market where Apple has been gaining ground steadily. Samsung's new foldables are not just gadgets; they are a statement of intent.

Early reviews in Indian media have been largely positive, with The Indian Express praising the Z Fold8's practicality. But whether that translates into sustained sales remains to be seen. Foldables still carry a premium price tag, and durability concerns linger in the minds of many buyers.

Analysts will be watching how the Z Flip8 performs, particularly among younger consumers. If it catches on, it could signal a shift in how this demographic views Samsung — not as a budget alternative, but as a brand that offers something genuinely fresh.

What to watch next

Sales data over the next few months will reveal whether Samsung's bet pays off. The company is also expected to push its foldables aggressively during the upcoming festive season in India, a period that typically drives high-volume purchases. If the Z Fold8 and Z Flip8 can hold their own against the iPhone, Samsung may finally crack the code with the generation that grew up on Apple.
