Uttarakhand Technical University cancels two semester exams after paper leak

📅 2026-08-13 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • UTU cancels two semester exams after alleged paper leak
  • University cites integrity concerns and student interests
  • Fresh dates to be announced; probe underway

DEHRADUN: Uttarakhand Technical University (UTU) has cancelled two semester examinations following an alleged paper leak, leaving thousands of students in limbo. The university announced the decision late on Wednesday, citing the need to uphold academic integrity.

Officials said the cancellation applies to specific papers across multiple courses, though the exact number of affected students has not been disclosed. The move comes after the university received reports that question papers had been circulated on social media before the scheduled exams.

Integrity Over Convenience

In an official statement, the university said the decision was taken in the interest of fairness. "We cannot allow a breach of confidentiality to compromise the evaluation process," a spokesperson said. "The cancellation is a necessary step to protect the credibility of our examinations."

The university has not specified which exams were cancelled, but sources suggest the affected papers are from the B.Tech and MBA programmes. Students, many of whom had prepared for months, expressed frustration at the last-minute disruption.

Students Left in the Lurch

Reactions from students have been mixed. Some welcomed the decision, arguing that a fair exam is better than a compromised one. Others, however, said the cancellation has disrupted their academic calendar and career plans.

  • Students demand clarity on rescheduled dates
  • Many rely on results for internships and job placements
  • University urges patience, promises timely updates

"We were told just a day before the exam," said a third-year B.Tech student, requesting anonymity. "I had prepared for weeks. Now I have no idea when the next exam will be."

Probe Underway

The university has formed a committee to investigate the leak. Officials have not yet confirmed how the paper was compromised or whether any arrests have been made. Local police have been alerted, and the university is working with cyber cell teams to trace the source of the leak.

This is not the first such incident in the state. In recent years, Uttarakhand has seen sporadic cases of question paper leaks in technical and medical exams, prompting authorities to tighten security protocols.

What Happens Next

The university has assured students that fresh exam dates will be announced within a fortnight. Officials said the new schedule will be published on the official website and through college notice boards. Students are advised to keep checking for updates.

Meanwhile, the committee's findings could lead to stricter measures, including digital watermarking of question papers and enhanced surveillance during printing and transport. The university has also urged students to report any suspicious online activity related to exam papers.

For now, students must wait. The university has promised a transparent process, but the disruption has already taken a toll. With the academic year at stake, all eyes are on the investigation and the announcement of fresh dates.
