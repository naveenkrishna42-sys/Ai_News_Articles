Ranbir Kapoor and Yash Face Off in 'Ramayana' Trailer; Hanuman Missing

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The trailer for Nitesh Tiwari's 'Ramayana' was released, featuring Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram and Yash as Ravan.
  • Ranbir Kapoor also plays a double role as Parashurama, which the internet is calling a scene-stealer.
  • Fans on social media are questioning the absence of Hanuman from the trailer, sparking widespread speculation.

Epic showdown unveiled

The much-anticipated trailer for Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana hit the internet on Thursday, offering the first real look at its star-studded cast. Ranbir Kapoor steps into the role of Lord Ram, while Kannada superstar Yash portrays Ravan. The trailer sets up a classic confrontation between dharma and adharma.

Kapoor's Ram is calm and composed, a stark contrast to Yash's menacing, larger-than-life Ravan. The visual effects and grand sets have drawn immediate praise. The trailer focuses heavily on the emotional weight of the exile, the abduction of Sita, and the build-up to war.

A double role for Ranbir Kapoor

One of the biggest surprises in the trailer is Ranbir Kapoor's appearance as Parashurama, the axe-wielding avatar of Vishnu. This double role has generated significant buzz online. Reports from NDTV suggest that the internet is already calling the Parashurama sequence a scene that 'will eat'.

The decision to have Kapoor play two distinct characters in the same film is a bold creative choice. It ties the narrative threads of different yugas together, hinting at a deeper mythological connection. Fans are eager to see how Tiwari balances these dual portrayals within the larger story.

The missing vanara

While the trailer was widely celebrated, a glaring omission did not go unnoticed. Hanuman, arguably one of the most beloved characters in the epic, is nowhere to be seen. Social media platforms, particularly X (formerly Twitter), were flooded with one question: 'Where is Hanuman?'

According to reports from The Indian Express and Telegraph India, fans are both excited and anxious. The character's absence from the trailer has led to speculation about the casting. Some believe the makers are saving the reveal for a later date, while others worry the character's role may have been reduced.

Yash's Ravan wins hearts

Despite the questions, the clear winner of the trailer is Yash as Ravan. His portrayal of the ten-headed king of Lanka has been described as 'powerful' and 'intimidating'. Rediff noted that while Ranbir wins as Ram, Yash's Ravan is a scene-stealer.

The actor, known for the KGF franchise, brings a raw intensity to the role. His dialogue delivery and physical presence in the trailer suggest a layered antagonist, not just a one-dimensional villain. This has raised expectations for the film's treatment of the complex character.

The film also features a supporting cast that includes names like Sunny Deol, Lara Dutta, and Vijay Sethupathi in key roles. Production values look top-notch, with elaborate costumes and VFX-heavy battle sequences.

What comes next is the marketing rollout for the film's scheduled release. The makers are expected to release a second trailer or a character-specific teaser for Hanuman in the coming weeks to address fan curiosity. All eyes are now on the final cut.
