RBI's draft on revolving credit sparks concerns in banking sector

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has released a draft on revolving credit, which could potentially impact lending practices on Unified Payments Interface (UPI) and flexi loans, according to the Managing Director of Piramal Finance. This draft has raised concerns among industry experts and could lead to significant changes in how banks operate.

Potential impact on UPI and flexi loans

UPI has become a popular mode of payment in India, with millions of users transacting daily. The RBI's draft on revolving credit could potentially affect the way banks offer UPI-based lending products. Similarly, flexi loans, which are short-term loans with flexible repayment options, could also be impacted. Banks may need to reassess their lending strategies to comply with the new regulations.

Piramal Finance MD warns of changes in lending practices

The Managing Director of Piramal Finance has expressed concerns about the potential impact of the RBI's draft on revolving credit. He has warned that banks may need to make significant changes in their lending practices to ensure compliance with the new regulations. This could lead to a slowdown in the growth of UPI and flexi loans in the short term.

Other lending products could also be affected

The impact of the RBI's draft on revolving credit is not limited to UPI and flexi loans. Other lending products, such as personal loans and credit cards, could also be affected. Banks may need to review their risk assessment models and lending policies to ensure compliance with the new regulations. This could lead to a more cautious approach to lending in the banking sector.

What happens next?

As the RBI's draft on revolving credit gains traction, banks and financial institutions will need to closely monitor the developments. They will need to reassess their lending strategies and ensure compliance with the new regulations. The banking sector will be closely watching the RBI's next steps, as the draft could potentially reshape the lending landscape in India.
