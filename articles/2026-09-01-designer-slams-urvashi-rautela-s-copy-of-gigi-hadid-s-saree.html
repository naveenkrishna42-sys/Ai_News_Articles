Designer Sandeep Khosla Reacts to Urvashi Rautela's 'Copy' Claim

Fashion designer Sandeep Khosla has reacted to Urvashi Rautela's 'gandi copy' claim over Gigi Hadid's NMACC saree look from the 2023 NMACC opening in Mumbai. Khosla, who is known for his innovative designs, has called out Rautela for allegedly copying his work.

Background of the Controversy

The controversy began when Urvashi Rautela wore a saree look that closely resembled the design created by Gigi Hadid for the NMACC opening. Rautela, who is known for her bold fashion choices, had claimed that the design was a 'gandi copy' of Hadid's look.

Sandeep Khosla's Response

Sandeep Khosla, who was present at the NMACC opening, has reacted to Rautela's claim by stating that the design was not a copy but rather an original creation. Khosla has also questioned the authenticity of Rautela's claim, stating that she should have approached him for permission to use the design.

Importance of Originality in Fashion

The controversy highlights the importance of originality in fashion. Fashion designers must be cautious of plagiarism and copyright issues, as it can lead to legal complications and damage their reputation. The incident also raises questions about the ethics of fashion and the need for designers to respect each other's work.

Conclusion

In conclusion, the controversy between Sandeep Khosla and Urvashi Rautela over the NMACC saree look serves as a reminder of the importance of originality in fashion. Fashion designers must be cautious of plagiarism and copyright issues, and should approach each other's work with respect and caution.
