Andy Flower rules himself out of England head coach race

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Andy Flower confirms he will not apply for the England Test head coach position.
  • Flower says he is happy with his current franchise roles at RCB and London Spirit.
  • The England job opened after Brendon McCullum stepped down recently.

Andy Flower has taken his name out of the race to become England's next Test head coach. The former Zimbabwe captain, who previously coached England to Ashes glory, said he will not pursue the role that opened up after Brendon McCullum stepped down.

Flower is currently head coach of Royal Challengers Bengaluru in the IPL and London Spirit in The Hundred. He made it clear he would rather stay with those teams than return to international cricket.

Happy in franchise cricket

Speaking to Cricbuzz, Flower said he is enjoying his current assignments and is not looking for a change. "I am happy in franchise cricket. I am not chasing the England job," he stated.

The 57-year-old has been with RCB since 2024 and took over London Spirit last year. His decision ends speculation that he might return to the England setup, where he served as head coach from 2009 to 2014.

McCullum steps down

Flower's announcement comes days after Brendon McCullum resigned as England's Test coach. The New Zealander had been in charge since 2022 and led the team through a period of aggressive cricket under captain Ben Stokes.

McCullum's tenure saw England win 18 of 32 Tests. But a string of losses in the recent home series against Australia and India prompted him to step aside. The England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB) is now searching for a replacement.

Other names in the fray

Several candidates are being linked with the job. Former England captain Andrew Strauss is seen as a frontrunner, while current batting coach Marcus Trescothick is also in the mix. Flower was considered a strong candidate given his past success with the team.

During his first stint, Flower guided England to three Ashes series wins and the world number one ranking in Tests. But he has not coached at international level since leaving the role after a 5-0 Ashes loss in 2013-14.

Franchise focus pays off

Flower has rebuilt his career in franchise leagues. He coached Multan Sultans to two Pakistan Super League titles and took St Kitts and Nevis Patriots to a Caribbean Premier League crown. At RCB, he has turned the team into consistent playoff contenders.

London Spirit, under his guidance, finished third in The Hundred last season. Flower said he wants to build on that momentum. "We have good squads and good owners. I want to see these projects through," he added.

The ECB is expected to announce its new head coach in the coming weeks. Flower's decision means the board will have to look elsewhere for a candidate who can continue McCullum's attacking template or chart a new direction.
