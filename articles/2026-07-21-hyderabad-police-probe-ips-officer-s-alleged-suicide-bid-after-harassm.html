Hyderabad police probe IPS officer’s alleged suicide bid after harassment case

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • An IPS officer in Hyderabad allegedly attempted suicide following a harassment case filed against him.
  • Police have launched an investigation and are examining the officer's hospital records and statements.
  • The harassment case and the officer's condition are under official scrutiny.
  • No formal complaint has been filed yet by the officer's family or colleagues.

Hyderabad police have launched an investigation into claims that a serving IPS officer attempted suicide after a harassment case was registered against him. The incident has sent ripples through the state's police establishment, with senior officials closely monitoring the developments.

The officer, whose identity has not been officially disclosed, is currently under medical care. Police sources confirmed that they are verifying the sequence of events that led to the alleged attempt. No official statement has been released by the Telangana Police headquarters yet.

Harassment case details emerge

The harassment case against the officer was filed earlier this month. Sources indicate that the complainant is a woman who had worked with the officer in a professional capacity. The nature of the allegations has not been made public.

The case was reportedly at an early stage of inquiry when the alleged suicide bid occurred. Investigators are now trying to establish if the two events are directly linked. Records from the police station where the harassment complaint was lodged have been secured for review.

Medical and legal proceedings underway

The officer is being treated at a private hospital in Hyderabad. Doctors attending to him have not issued any public statement regarding his condition. Police officials have spoken to the medical team to gather a preliminary assessment.

A team of officers from the Hyderabad Police Commissionerate is handling the probe. They are recording statements from the officer's family, colleagues, and the complainant. No arrests have been made in connection with the matter so far.

Departmental response and internal review

Senior IPS officers in the state have reportedly held internal discussions about the case. The Telangana Police is known for its strict code of conduct, and such incidents are taken seriously. The department is expected to initiate a parallel internal inquiry once the criminal investigation progresses.

Police sources added that the officer's service record is being examined. Any past complaints or disciplinary actions against him will be part of the review. The department has not confirmed if the officer has been placed under suspension.

The harassment case is being handled by a separate team of investigators to avoid any conflict of interest. The complainant has been assured of a fair and transparent inquiry. Police have urged the media not to speculate on the officer's identity or the details of the case until the investigation is complete.

What lies ahead

The investigation will determine if the officer's alleged suicide bid was a direct consequence of the harassment case or if other factors were involved. The police are expected to submit a preliminary report to senior officials within a week. The outcome could have significant implications for both the officer's career and the handling of harassment complaints within the force.
