Sameera Reddy Opens Up About Postpartum Depression, Menopause And Past Body Shaming

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sameera Reddy revealed she weighed 100 kg after her second pregnancy and struggled with postpartum depression.
  • She said she was made 'three shades lighter' for her 2002 debut film opposite Sohail Khan.
  • The actress discussed the emotional toll of menopause and rebuilding self-worth after a breakup.

Sameera Reddy has opened up about the physical and emotional struggles she faced after motherhood, including weighing 100 kg and battling postpartum depression. In a series of recent interviews, the actress also revisited the colour bias she endured early in her Bollywood career.

Weight gain and postpartum depression

Speaking candidly about her health journey, Reddy said she weighed 100 kg after her second pregnancy. The weight gain, combined with hormonal changes, triggered a deep sense of loss and depression.

"I didn't recognise myself in the mirror," she recalled. The actress described how the pressure to bounce back to a pre-pregnancy body added to her mental strain. She said the postpartum period left her feeling isolated and overwhelmed.

Menopause and self-worth

Reddy also spoke about navigating menopause, another phase that brought significant physical and emotional changes. She admitted the transition was tough, especially in an industry that often equates a woman's worth with her appearance.

"Menopause hit me hard," she said. The actress stressed the importance of talking openly about such experiences to break the stigma around ageing and female health.

Colour bias in her debut film

In separate interviews, Reddy revisited the discrimination she faced during the making of her debut Bollywood film in 2002, which co-starred Sohail Khan. She alleged that the film's team made her skin appear "two to three shades lighter" on screen.

"I was made three shades lighter," she said, recalling how makeup artists and lighting technicians worked to alter her natural complexion. The actress also revealed she was asked to wear padded bras to conform to a certain body image.

Reddy said the experience left her feeling insecure about her looks early in her career. She noted that such practices were common at the time, reflecting deep-seated colourism in the entertainment industry.

Rebuilding after a breakup

The actress also touched upon how a personal breakup forced her to confront her self-worth. She described the period as a turning point that pushed her to prioritise her mental health and family.

"I had to learn to love myself again," she said. Reddy credited therapy and the support of her husband for helping her navigate the lows.

A broader conversation

Reddy's frank admissions have sparked discussions about the pressures women face—both in Bollywood and beyond—to conform to narrow beauty standards. Her story adds to the growing chorus of voices demanding more realistic representations of motherhood, ageing, and body image.

She urged other women to seek help and not suffer in silence. "Your worth is not defined by your weight or your skin colour," she said.

Sameera Reddy is currently active on social media, where she often shares unfiltered glimpses of her life as a mother and wife. She continues to advocate for body positivity and mental health awareness.
