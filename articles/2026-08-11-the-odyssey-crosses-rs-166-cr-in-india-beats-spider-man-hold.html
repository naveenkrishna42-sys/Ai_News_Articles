The Odyssey Crosses Rs 166 Cr in India, Beats Spider-Man Hold

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The Odyssey earns Rs 166.89 crore in India over 24 days.
  • IMAX contributes Rs 74.21 crore, a record for Nolan.
  • Film surpasses $1.1 billion globally, Nolan's highest grosser.
  • Sustains box office momentum despite Spider-Man: Brand New Day release.

Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey continues its remarkable run at the Indian box office, collecting Rs 166.89 crore in 24 days. The film has shown exceptional resilience, holding its ground even as Spider-Man: Brand New Day entered theatres in the same window.

What stands out is the massive contribution from IMAX screens. The premium format has generated Rs 74.21 crore of the total, underlining the audience's appetite for the big-screen spectacle that Nolan is known for.

Box Office Breakdown

The film's 24-day total of Rs 166.89 crore places it among the top Hollywood releases in India this year. Trade analysts point to the sustained week-on-week collections, which have barely dipped despite new competition.

IMAX's share of nearly 44 percent is unprecedented for a Hollywood title in the Indian market. This reflects both the film's visual scale and the growing preference among Indian audiences for premium formats.

Global Milestones

Internationally, The Odyssey has crossed the $1.1 billion mark at the worldwide box office. This makes it the highest-grossing film of Nolan's career, surpassing the records set by his earlier blockbusters.

The film has also become the highest-grossing IMAX release ever, a testament to its success in large-format screens across the globe. Industry observers note that the combination of Nolan's storytelling and the epic scale of the Greek myth has resonated strongly with audiences.

India-Specific Trends

In India, the film has performed particularly well in metropolitan centres, where IMAX screens are concentrated. Cities like Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru have contributed significantly to the IMAX haul.

The hold against Spider-Man: Brand New Day is noteworthy. Usually, a big-ticket superhero release cannibalises the collections of existing films, but The Odyssey has managed to retain a substantial portion of its screen count and audience.

What This Means for Nolan

For Nolan, this success reinforces his box-office credibility beyond the Batman franchise. His previous original and historical dramas have performed well, but The Odyssey has taken his commercial appeal to a new level.

The film's performance also signals a shift in audience preferences, with a clear willingness to pay a premium for an immersive theatrical experience. This bodes well for future large-format releases.

As the film enters its fourth week, the focus now shifts to its long-term sustainability. With no major Hollywood release scheduled for the next couple of weeks, The Odyssey could continue its steady accumulation.

Watch for whether it can maintain its IMAX dominance and inch closer to the Rs 200 crore mark in India, a feat achieved by only a handful of Hollywood films in the country.
