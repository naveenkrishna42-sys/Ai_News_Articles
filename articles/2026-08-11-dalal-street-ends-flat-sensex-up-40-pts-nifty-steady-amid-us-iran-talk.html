Dalal Street ends flat: Sensex up 40 pts, Nifty steady amid US-Iran talks

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • BSE Sensex closed 40 points higher, NSE Nifty50 nearly unchanged.
  • Trading was lacklustre with US-Iran negotiations and US inflation data in focus.
  • CAS (Customs Automated System) updates also drew investor attention.
  • Analysts expect market direction to hinge on global cues and upcoming data.

Flat close after a lacklustre session

Dalal Street ended Monday’s session on a flat note, with the BSE Sensex managing a modest gain of 40 points while the NSE Nifty50 closed virtually unchanged. The muted performance reflected a day of cautious trading, as investors held back amid a lack of strong domestic triggers.

Trading remained range-bound through the day, with benchmark indices oscillating within a narrow band. Market participants described the session as “lacklustre,” with volumes staying thin and sectoral moves largely subdued.

US-Iran negotiations weigh on sentiment

Global cues played a key role in shaping Monday’s mood. Ongoing US-Iran negotiations kept geopolitical risk on the radar, prompting traders to adopt a wait-and-watch approach. Any potential breakthrough or escalation in these talks could influence crude oil prices, which in turn has a direct bearing on India’s import bill and inflation outlook.

While there was no major headline development during the session, the very presence of the talks added a layer of uncertainty. Analysts noted that a clearer direction may emerge only once concrete outcomes from the negotiations become public.

US inflation data and CAS in focus

Investors also kept an eye on upcoming US inflation figures, scheduled for release later this week. A stronger-than-expected reading could alter expectations around the US Federal Reserve’s rate trajectory, affecting capital flows into emerging markets like India.

On the domestic front, developments around the Customs Automated System (CAS) drew attention. The system, which streamlines customs clearance processes, is being closely monitored by trade and industry circles for its potential impact on logistics and compliance costs.

What lies ahead

With the earnings season largely behind, market direction is likely to hinge on global macros, geopolitical developments, and the US inflation print. Until then, traders may continue to navigate a choppy, range-bound market.

Analysts suggest that any decisive move will require fresh triggers, either from the US-Iran front or from central bank commentary. For now, the undertone remains cautious, and participants are advised to stay nimble.
