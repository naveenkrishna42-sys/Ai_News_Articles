Salman Khan, one of Bollywood's most guarded stars, has opened up about a chapter of his life he seldom discusses — his time in jail. In a recent interaction, the actor offered a stark description of the conditions, saying, "We had bars, in a small area there were 60-70 people."

The comment, brief as it was, marks one of the few instances where Khan has publicly reflected on his incarceration. Known for keeping his personal struggles away from the limelight, the actor's candour has sparked fresh interest among fans and media alike.

Khan's remark surfaced during a promotional event, though details of the exact setting remain unclear. The actor did not elaborate further, but his words painted a vivid picture of overcrowding and confinement — a stark contrast to the glitz and space he commands on screen.

This is not the first time the star has touched upon the subject, but such admissions are rare. Over the years, Khan has often deflected questions about his legal troubles, preferring to focus on his work and family.
