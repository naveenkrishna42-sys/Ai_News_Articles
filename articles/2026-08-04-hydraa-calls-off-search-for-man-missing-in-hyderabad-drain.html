HYDRAA calls off search for man missing in Hyderabad drain

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • HYDRAA has ended the search for a man who fell into a stormwater drain in Hyderabad.
  • The operation involved multiple teams and was called off after exhaustive efforts.
  • The man's whereabouts remain unknown; officials have not confirmed further details.

Search called off after extensive operation

The Hyderabad Disaster Response and Asset Protection Agency (HYDRAA) has officially ended its search for a man who went missing after falling into a stormwater drain in the city. The decision was confirmed on Monday, bringing a close to a multi-day operation that involved several rescue teams.

According to officials, the man slipped into the drain during recent heavy rains, which had turned the waterway into a fast-moving channel. Rescue divers and local authorities scoured the drain and its outlets, but no trace of the individual was found.

Challenges faced by rescue teams

The search was hampered by the drain's complex network and the strong flow of water, which made it difficult for divers to navigate. HYDRAA personnel worked in shifts, using specialised equipment to probe sections of the drain that were accessible.

Despite these efforts, the operation was called off after it became clear that further search would not yield results. Officials have not yet confirmed whether the man's body has been recovered or if the case will be reopened under different circumstances.

Background of the incident

The incident occurred in a part of Hyderabad that is prone to waterlogging during the monsoon. Stormwater drains in the city have been a recurring concern, with residents often flagging inadequate maintenance and safety measures.

Local authorities have not released the man's identity, pending notification of his family. The police have registered a case and are investigating the circumstances leading to the fall.

Response from authorities

HYDRAA officials have stated that the decision to end the search was taken after exhausting all available options. They emphasised that the agency's priority remains public safety and that steps are being taken to prevent such incidents in the future.

Meanwhile, the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation has been urged to inspect nearby drains and install safety barriers, although no official announcement has been made yet.

As the monsoon continues, residents are advised to stay away from open drains and report any hazards to local authorities. The search may be over, but questions about drain safety in the city are likely to persist.
