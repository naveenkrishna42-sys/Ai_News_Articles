Tom Lee Names Two Cryptocurrencies Set for Major Gains

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tom Lee forecasts significant upside for Bitcoin and Ethereum.
  • Institutional adoption and clear regulations are cited as catalysts.
  • Lee expects Bitcoin to reach $150,000 and Ethereum to hit $10,000 in the next 12-18 months.

Fundstrat's Tom Lee Backs Bitcoin and Ethereum for Big Moves

Tom Lee, the co-founder of Fundstrat Global Advisors, has identified two cryptocurrencies he believes are poised for massive gains. The veteran analyst, known for his bullish calls on digital assets, named Bitcoin and Ethereum as his top picks in a recent interview.

Lee's outlook comes amid a broader recovery in crypto markets, with both coins trading significantly higher from their lows earlier this year. He pointed to growing institutional interest and clearer regulatory frameworks in key economies as primary catalysts.

Why Bitcoin Is Lee's Top Pick

Lee expects Bitcoin to reach $150,000 within the next 12 to 18 months. He argues that the flagship cryptocurrency is increasingly being viewed as a digital store of value, similar to gold but with better portability and divisibility.

The analyst highlighted that major corporations and even some governments are now holding Bitcoin on their balance sheets. This institutional adoption, he says, provides a strong floor for prices and reduces volatility over time.

Lee also noted that the upcoming Bitcoin halving event, expected in 2028, will further constrain supply. Historically, such halvings have preceded significant price rallies.

Ethereum's Path to $10,000

For Ethereum, Lee set a price target of $10,000 over the same period. He believes the network's transition to proof-of-stake has made it more scalable and energy-efficient, attracting developers building decentralized finance (DeFi) and non-fungible token (NFT) applications.

He pointed to the growing use of Ethereum for real-world applications, from supply chain tracking to digital identity. Lee said that as more enterprises deploy smart contracts on the network, demand for Ether will rise.

The analyst also cited the potential for a spot Ethereum exchange-traded fund (ETF) in the United States. Approval of such a product, he argued, could unlock billions of dollars in institutional capital.

Regulatory Clarity as a Key Catalyst

Lee emphasized that clearer regulations in the US and Europe are reducing uncertainty for investors. He noted that recent court rulings have classified Bitcoin and Ethereum as commodities rather than securities, giving them a clearer legal status.

This clarity, he said, allows traditional asset managers to allocate funds to these assets without fear of legal repercussions. Several major banks have already started offering crypto custody services to their clients.

Lee also pointed to favorable tax treatment in some jurisdictions, which he believes will encourage more retail and institutional participation.

Risks and Challenges Remain

While Lee is optimistic, he acknowledged that cryptocurrencies remain volatile assets. He warned that sudden regulatory crackdowns, security breaches, or macroeconomic shocks could derail his forecasts.

The analyst advised investors to hold a diversified portfolio and not allocate more than they can afford to lose to digital assets. He said that while the long-term trend is upward, short-term corrections are inevitable.

Lee also noted that competition from other blockchain networks, such as Solana and Cardano, could challenge Ethereum's dominance. However, he believes Ethereum's first-mover advantage and developer community give it a strong edge.

Tom Lee's predictions add to a growing chorus of analysts who see a bright future for Bitcoin and Ethereum. Whether these targets materialize will depend on how quickly the broader financial system embraces digital assets. Investors will be watching key milestones, including ETF approvals and institutional adoption rates, in the months ahead.
