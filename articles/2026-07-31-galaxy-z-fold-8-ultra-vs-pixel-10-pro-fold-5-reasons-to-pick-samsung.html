Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra vs Pixel 10 Pro Fold: 5 reasons to pick Samsung

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung's Z Fold 8 Ultra offers superior multitasking and S Pen support compared to the Pixel 10 Pro Fold.
  • Foldable durability and hinge design remain Samsung's stronghold, with the Ultra featuring an upgraded Armor Aluminum frame.
  • The Ultra's brighter, more adaptive display and faster charging give it an edge over Google's offering.
  • Samsung's One UI 8.0 brings enhanced AI features and better app continuity for foldable screens.
  • Pre-orders in India crossed 2.71 lakh units within 72 hours, signaling strong consumer interest.

The Foldable Battle Heats Up

The race for foldable supremacy has taken a new turn. Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Google's Pixel 10 Pro Fold are the two most anticipated devices of the year. Both bring cutting-edge technology, but they cater to slightly different needs.

For anyone weighing the two, the choice often comes down to specific priorities. Based on hands-on impressions and early reviews, here are five reasons the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra edges out its Pixel counterpart.

1. Multitasking That Actually Works

Samsung has spent years refining its foldable software, and it shows. The Z Fold 8 Ultra runs One UI 8.0, which brings a redesigned taskbar and improved drag-and-drop. You can run up to four apps simultaneously without the interface feeling cluttered.

Google's Pixel 10 Pro Fold, meanwhile, relies on Android's native foldable optimizations. While functional, it lacks the depth of Samsung's app continuity and window management. For power users who juggle emails, spreadsheets, and video calls, the Ultra is the more capable tool.

2. S Pen Support Is a Game Changer

The Ultra comes with built-in S Pen support, a feature the Pixel 10 Pro Fold simply doesn't offer. Whether you're sketching, annotating documents, or taking handwritten notes, the S Pen adds a layer of productivity that's hard to replicate.

Google has kept its foldable stylus-free, betting that most users won't miss it. But for professionals and creatives, the ability to write directly on the main display is a significant advantage. Samsung's display also includes a dedicated layer to protect against scratches from the S Pen, a thoughtful touch.

3. Durability and Hinge Design

Samsung's hinge mechanism has improved with each generation. The Z Fold 8 Ultra features an upgraded Armor Aluminum frame and a new hinge that reduces the crease further. It feels solid in the hand, and the fold mechanism is smooth yet sturdy.

Google's hinge is decent, but early reviews note a slightly more visible crease and less reassuring build tolerance. The Ultra also has an IP48 rating, which means it can withstand dust particles larger than 1mm, a first for foldables. That's a practical win for anyone who carries their phone to the beach or a dusty job site.

4. Brighter Display and Faster Charging

The Ultra's main display hits a peak brightness of 3,000 nits, making it one of the brightest panels on any phone. That's a noticeable difference when using the device outdoors or under direct sunlight. The Pixel 10 Pro Fold, while no slouch, tops out lower.

Charging is another area where Samsung pulls ahead. The Ultra supports 45W wired charging, compared to the Pixel's 30W. That means fewer minutes plugged in and more time on the go. Both support wireless charging, but the Samsung is faster on that front as well.

5. The AI and Software Ecosystem

Samsung's Galaxy AI has matured significantly. The Z Fold 8 Ultra leverages the new Snapdragon chip's on-device AI for real-time translation, summarization, and photo editing. These tools are integrated deeply into One UI, making them feel like a natural part of the experience.

Google's AI is smart, but it's often tied to cloud services. Samsung's approach is more offline-friendly and privacy-conscious. For frequent travelers or those in areas with patchy connectivity, the Ultra's on-device AI is a clear plus.

Pre-Order Numbers Tell the Story

The market seems to agree. Reports indicate that the Galaxy Z Fold 8 series, including the Ultra, crossed 2.71 lakh pre-orders in India within just 72 hours of launch. SamMobile notes that the standard Z Fold 8 may have even overtaken the Ultra in pre-orders, suggesting strong demand across the lineup.

Google hasn't released comparable numbers, but the buzz around the Pixel 10 Pro Fold is more muted. Early adopters are clearly voting with their wallets, and Samsung's established brand trust in the foldable segment plays a big role.

All this said, the Pixel 10 Pro Fold has its own strengths—cleaner software, a more compact design, and a competitive camera system. But for those who want the most versatile and durable foldable on the market, the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra makes a compelling case.

As both devices hit shelves in the coming weeks, the real test will be long-term reliability and software updates. Samsung has a three-year lead in foldable engineering, and the Ultra shows it. Whether Google can close that gap by next year remains to be seen.
