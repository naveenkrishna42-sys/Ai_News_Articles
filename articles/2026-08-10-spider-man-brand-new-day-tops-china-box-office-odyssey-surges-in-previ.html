Spider-Man: Brand New Day Tops China Box Office; Odyssey Surges in Previews

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day retains top spot at China box office
  • Once Upon a Time in the Middle East and The Odyssey surge in previews
  • Spider-Man crosses Rs 500 crore in India, earns $1.67 billion worldwide in 10 days

Marvel's latest web-slinger adventure continues to dominate the global box office. 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' has retained its crown in China, holding the No. 1 position for another weekend. The film's staying power in the world's second-largest movie market shows no signs of slowing down.

Meanwhile, two major releases are making waves in previews. 'Once Upon a Time in the Middle East' and Christopher Nolan's 'The Odyssey' have both posted impressive early numbers, setting the stage for a fierce box office battle in the coming days.

Spider-Man's Global Dominance

The numbers are staggering. 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' has earned USD 1.67 billion worldwide in just 10 days of release. That places it among the fastest films to cross the billion-dollar mark in recent memory. The film's performance in India has been particularly noteworthy, becoming the first Hollywood film to cross the Rs 500-crore mark in the country.

China remains a crucial territory for Hollywood releases, and the film's continued hold on the top spot there underscores its broad appeal. Industry analysts point to strong word-of-mouth and repeat viewings as key drivers behind the sustained momentum.

Previews Signal Strong Competition

'Once Upon a Time in the Middle East' has emerged as a surprise contender in previews. The film, which blends historical drama with contemporary storytelling, has resonated with Chinese audiences. Early screening numbers suggest it could pose a serious challenge to Spider-Man's reign.

Christopher Nolan's 'The Odyssey' is also generating significant buzz. The epic adaptation has set a new box office record for the director, marking his strongest preview performance to date. Nolan's track record in China, coupled with the film's ambitious scale, has made it one of the most anticipated releases of the year.

What This Means for the Market

The Chinese box office has shown remarkable resilience this year, with a diverse slate of films drawing audiences back to theatres. The success of both Hollywood blockbusters and local productions points to a healthy recovery in the sector. Distributors are closely watching the weekend numbers to gauge whether the current momentum can be sustained.

For 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day', the question now is how long it can hold off the challengers. With 'The Odyssey' and 'Once Upon a Time in the Middle East' expanding their screen counts, the competition is set to intensify.

India's Growing Appetite for Hollywood

Spider-Man's record-breaking run in India highlights a broader trend. Hollywood films have been increasingly finding success in the Indian market, with dubbed versions in multiple languages expanding their reach. The Rs 500-crore milestone is a testament to the franchise's popularity and the effectiveness of local marketing strategies.

Distributors in India are now looking at the upcoming releases with renewed optimism. The success of 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' has set a benchmark that future Hollywood releases will aim to surpass.

As the weekend approaches, all eyes will be on the box office numbers. Will Spider-Man continue its reign, or will 'The Odyssey' and 'Once Upon a Time in the Middle East' dethrone the web-slinger? The coming days will provide the answer, with industry watchers predicting a closely contested race.
