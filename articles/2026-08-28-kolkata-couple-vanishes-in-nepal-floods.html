Kolkata couple vanishes in Nepal floods

📅 2026-08-28 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kolkata couple among missing in Nepal floods
  • Flash floods disrupt Kailash Yatra pilgrimage
  • Authorities warn of increasing travel risks in Himalayas

Missing Couple's Journey Cut Short by Floods

A couple from New Town, Kolkata, who had saved for two years to embark on a spiritual journey, have vanished in Nepal. The incident has raised concerns about the safety of pilgrims traveling to the Himalayas during the monsoon season.

Kailash Yatra Disrupted by Unprecedented Floods

The Kailash Yatra, a significant pilgrimage route in Nepal, has been severely affected by unprecedented flash floods. The floods have not only disrupted the journey of the missing couple but also posed a threat to the safety of other pilgrims.

Rising Concerns About Travel Safety

With the increasing frequency of such natural disasters, authorities have issued warnings about the rising risks associated with travel to the Himalayas. The incident has sparked a debate about the need for better infrastructure and disaster management plans in the region.

Broader Impact on Tourism and Economy

The disappearance of the couple has also highlighted the broader impact of such incidents on the tourism and economy of the region. The Himalayas are a major tourist destination, attracting pilgrims and adventure seekers from across the globe.

Need for Improved Infrastructure and Disaster Management

Experts suggest that the incident underscores the need for improved infrastructure and better disaster management strategies in the Himalayan region. This includes the construction of more robust bridges, the establishment of early warning systems, and the provision of adequate rescue resources.
