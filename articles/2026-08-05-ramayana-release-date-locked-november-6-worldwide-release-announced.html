Ramayana release date locked: November 6 worldwide release announced

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ramayana will release worldwide on November 6.
  • The film, produced by Namit Malhotra, stars Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram.
  • Costume backlash and comparisons to Adipurush and old Ramayan have sparked debate.

Release date confirmed

The wait for one of the most anticipated Indian films of the decade is finally over. Namit Malhotra's magnum opus, Ramayana, will hit theatres worldwide on November 6. The announcement was made exclusively by Bollywood Hungama, putting an end to months of speculation.

The film, which brings together a stellar cast led by Ranbir Kapoor, has been in production for years. With a budget that reportedly rivals big-ticket Hollywood productions, the epic is expected to be a visual spectacle. However, the road to release has not been without its share of turbulence.

Costume backlash and online memes

Even before its release, Ramayana has found itself at the centre of online debates. Social media users have resurfaced memes from Adipurush, the 2023 Prabhas-starrer that faced heavy criticism for its visual effects and dialogues. The comparison comes as Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana faces its own costume backlash, with netizens questioning the authenticity and design of the outfits worn by the lead actors.

This is not the first time a modern retelling of the epic has faced scrutiny. Ramanand Sagar's grandson recently remarked that Ranbir Kapoor carries a lot of baggage after his role in Animal, adding that surpassing the old Ramayan is a huge task. Ameesha Patel, who starred in the 2008 film Tulsidas, also reacted to the trailer, noting that surpassing the classic series is no small feat.

High expectations and comparisons

The legacy of Ramanand Sagar's Ramayan, which aired on Doordarshan in the late 1980s, looms large over any new adaptation. For generations of Indians, that series remains the definitive visual representation of the epic. The challenge for Tiwari and Malhotra is to offer something fresh while respecting the cultural weight of the source material.

Adding to the pressure is the recent failure of Adipurush, which was criticised for its subpar VFX and modernised dialogues. The makers of Ramayana have promised a more faithful and grandiose treatment, but the audience remains sceptical. The costume backlash, in particular, has raised questions about whether the film can strike the right balance between tradition and cinematic appeal.

What to expect from the film

Ramayana is being produced on a scale never before attempted in Indian cinema. With Namit Malhotra, known for his work on Hollywood blockbusters, at the helm of production, the film aims to set new benchmarks in visual storytelling. The casting of Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram has been widely discussed, with many eager to see how the actor, known for his gritty roles, brings the Maryada Purushottam to life.

The film's release on November 6 will be a global event, with plans for a simultaneous worldwide rollout. Trade analysts are already predicting a record-breaking opening, though much will depend on the final trailer and word-of-mouth. The makers are expected to ramp up promotions in the coming months, with a full-scale marketing campaign likely to begin after the monsoon season.

As the release date draws closer, the debate around Ramayana will only intensify. Whether it emerges as a landmark in Indian cinema or becomes another cautionary tale remains to be seen. For now, all eyes are on November 6, when the epic finally reaches the big screen.
