GIFT Nifty Up 100 Points, Sensex Jumps 650 as Oil Prices Crash on US-Iran War

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • GIFT Nifty rises 100 points in early trade, most Asian markets also advance.
  • Brent crude oil falls below $91 per barrel, dropping 7% amid US-Iran war.
  • Sensex jumps 650 points, crude-sensitive stocks like JK Tyre and Asian Paints gain up to 5%.
  • Investors eye Fed policy, earnings season and global cues for market direction.

Markets Open Strong on Oil Price Crash

Indian stock markets surged in early trade on Monday as benchmark indices rallied sharply, driven by a massive drop in global crude oil prices. The GIFT Nifty, trading on the NSE International Exchange, rose 100 points, signaling a strong start for the domestic equity market.

The BSE Sensex jumped over 650 points in the opening session, while the Nifty 50 also traded firmly in the green. Most Asian markets mirrored the positive trend, advancing in tandem with Indian indices.

Brent Crude Below $91 as Geopolitical Tensions Escalate

The key catalyst for today's rally was a sharp decline in oil prices. Brent crude futures fell below $91 per barrel, a drop of nearly 7%, as the ongoing US-Iran war continued to disrupt global supply chains but also sparked fears of demand destruction.

Traders and analysts attributed the price crash to expectations that the conflict could lead to a broader economic slowdown, reducing fuel demand. Additionally, reports of potential diplomatic backchannels to de-escalate the situation added downward pressure on oil prices.

Crude-Sensitive Stocks Rally Up to 5%

The sharp fall in crude prices provided a major boost to sectors heavily dependent on oil. Shares of JK Tyre, Asian Paints, and other crude-sensitive companies gained up to 5% in early trade.

Auto stocks, paint manufacturers, and tyre makers — all of which have crude oil as a key raw material input — saw strong buying interest as lower input costs are expected to improve their margins. Analysts noted that a sustained decline in oil prices could provide significant relief to these sectors in the coming quarters.

Investors Eye Fed, Earnings Season and Global Cues

Market participants are now closely watching the US Federal Reserve's upcoming policy decision, as well as the ongoing corporate earnings season in India. Global cues, including the trajectory of the US dollar and bond yields, remain in focus.

The Fed's stance on interest rates will be critical for emerging markets like India. A dovish tone could further boost investor sentiment, while any hawkish surprise may temper the current optimism. Domestic earnings reports from key sectors will also guide stock-specific movements.

With the US-Iran situation still fluid, volatility is expected to remain high. Traders are advised to keep a close watch on crude price movements and geopolitical developments for near-term direction.
