India's rocket launches costliest among major space powers: study

India's Rocket Launches Costliest Among Major Space Powers

India's rocket launches are the most expensive among major space powers, according to a recent study by Cambridge University. The study reveals that India spends significantly more on rocket launches compared to the US, despite recent successes in the space sector.

Skyroot's Success Does Not Lower Costs

Despite Skyroot Aerospace's recent success in launching a rocket into space, the overall cost of India's rocket launches remains high. The study shows that India's space ambitions come with a hefty price tag, four times more than what the US spends on similar missions.

US Dominates Global Payload Market

The US sent over 80% of the global payload in 2025, according to the study. This dominance is largely due to the US's significant investment in space technology and infrastructure. India, on the other hand, has been making strides in the space sector but still lags behind in terms of launch costs.

India's Space Ambitions Face High Costs

India's space ambitions face over 4x higher rocket launch costs than the US, according to the study. This high cost is a significant challenge for India's space program, which aims to compete with other major space powers. The study suggests that India needs to find ways to reduce the cost of its rocket launches to keep up with the global space race.

What Happens Next

India's space program will need to focus on reducing the cost of rocket launches to remain competitive in the global space market. The recent success of Skyroot Aerospace is a positive sign, but it is not enough to change the overall high cost of India's rocket launches. The study's findings will likely prompt India to look for new technologies and strategies to lower the cost of its space missions. The global space market is expected to grow significantly in the coming years, and India will need to adapt to stay relevant in this rapidly evolving industry.>
