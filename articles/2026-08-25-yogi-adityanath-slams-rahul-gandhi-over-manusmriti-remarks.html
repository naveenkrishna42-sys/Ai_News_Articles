Manusmriti: A reflection of India's rich cultural heritage

The Manusmriti, a Hindu legal text, is considered a reflection of India's rich cultural heritage. The text, which dates back to the 2nd century CE, outlines the rules and regulations for Hindu society, including those related to marriage, divorce, and inheritance.
