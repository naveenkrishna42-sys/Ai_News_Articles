Mumbai: birth, death, marriage certificates in 3 days after registration

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Certificates to be issued within three days of registration
  • Move aims to ease bureaucratic delays for residents
  • Applies to all three certificate types in Mumbai

Faster certificates for Mumbai residents

Mumbaikars will no longer have to wait weeks for official documents. The Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) has announced that birth, death, and marriage certificates will now be issued within three days of registration. The directive, reported by The Hindu, is expected to bring relief to thousands of residents who often face delays in obtaining these essential papers.

The new timeline applies across all municipal wards in the city. Officials said the move is part of an ongoing effort to streamline civic services and reduce the administrative burden on citizens.

How the new process works

Under the revised system, the clock starts ticking once a registration is completed. For births and deaths, hospitals and nursing homes typically register the event; for marriages, couples or their representatives file the paperwork. After that, the civic body will process and issue the certificate within 72 hours.

Previously, the process could take anywhere from a week to several weeks, depending on the ward and the volume of applications. The new timeline is expected to standardise the experience across the city.

Why this matters

Birth, death, and marriage certificates are required for a range of purposes — from school admissions and passport applications to property inheritance claims and government scheme enrolment. Delays in obtaining these documents often lead to missed deadlines and added stress for families.

By cutting the waiting period, the BMC aims to reduce the need for follow-up visits and repeated queries. Residents will also be able to track the status of their applications online, although officials have not yet confirmed if the tracking facility will be available immediately.

What residents should know

The three-day service will be applicable to all new registrations. For older cases that are already pending, the civic body has not specified a separate timeline. Residents are advised to ensure that all required documents are submitted correctly at the time of registration to avoid any processing hiccups.

Officials have also indicated that the move is part of a broader digital push by the BMC. While the current announcement covers certificates, similar fast-track measures may be extended to other civic services in the coming months.

For now, Mumbaikars can expect quicker access to documents that are often taken for granted but can make a significant difference in daily life. The civic body is expected to release a detailed circular with operational guidelines in the next few days.
