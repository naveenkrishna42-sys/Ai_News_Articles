Senior Citizen FDs: Compare 5-year rates up to 8.05%

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Credit Cards & Cashback Original source ↗
Key points
  • 5-year senior citizen FDs offer interest rates up to 8.05%
  • Compare rates across SBI, PNB, HDFC Bank and other banks
  • Senior citizen FDs are tax-free under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act

Understanding Senior Citizen Fixed Deposits

Senior citizen fixed deposits (FDs) are a popular investment option for individuals aged 60 years and above. These FDs offer tax-free interest income under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, making them an attractive option for retirees looking to grow their savings.

Interest Rates and Comparisons

Interest rates for 5-year senior citizen FDs vary across different banks. As of June 2026, the highest interest rate offered is 8.05%, by State Bank of India (SBI). Other banks offering competitive rates include Punjab National Bank (PNB) and HDFC Bank.

Here is a comparison of 5-year senior citizen FD interest rates across different banks:

  • State Bank of India (SBI): 8.05%
  • Punjab National Bank (PNB): 7.95%
  • HDFC Bank: 7.85%
  • ICICI Bank: 7.75%
  • Axis Bank: 7.65%

Eligibility Criteria

To open a senior citizen FD, you must be at least 60 years old. The minimum deposit amount varies across banks, typically ranging from ₹50,000 to ₹1 lakh. The maximum deposit amount is usually ₹2 crore.

How to Open a Senior Citizen FD

Opening a senior citizen FD is a straightforward process. You can visit your bank's branch, fill out the FD application form, and submit the required documents. Alternatively, you can open an FD online through your bank's website or mobile app.

When opening an FD online, you will need to provide your personal details, including your name, age, and address. You will also need to choose the deposit amount, the tenure, and the interest rate. Once you have filled out the form, you can submit it and receive your FD receipt.

Tax Benefits

Senior citizen FDs offer tax-free interest income under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. This means that the interest earned on your FD is exempt from tax, allowing you to keep more of your hard-earned money.

To claim the tax benefits, you will need to provide your FD receipt to your accountant or tax consultant. They will then deduct the interest income from your taxable income, reducing your tax liability.

Final Recommendation

Senior citizen FDs are a great investment option for retirees looking to grow their savings. With interest rates up to 8.05% and tax-free benefits, these FDs offer a high return on investment. To get the best rate, compare the interest rates offered by different banks. Once you have chosen a bank, visit their branch or open an FD online to start growing your savings.
