PM Modi’s Central Asia Visit: Terror-Free Vision

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has embarked on a 4-day visit to Uzbekistan and the Kyrgyz Republic, a move that underscores India’s commitment to advancing its vision of a terror-free region. This visit comes ahead of the upcoming Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Summit, where India will be seeking to strengthen its regional security architecture.

Why Central Asia Matters for India

Central Asia is a strategic region for India, both in terms of security and economic interests. The region is a key transit route for India’s trade with Europe and Central Asia, and it is also a vital area for counter-terrorism efforts. India’s Central Asia policy is a crucial part of its broader strategy to ensure regional stability and prosperity.

India’s Vision for a Terror-Free Region

India’s vision for a terror-free region is rooted in its commitment to multilateral cooperation and dialogue. PM Modi’s visit to Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan is a testament to India’s efforts to build a consensus on counter-terrorism issues and to promote regional stability. The SCO Summit, which will be held in Uzbekistan, is a significant opportunity for India to advance its vision and to strengthen its ties with its Central Asian partners.

Key Highlights of PM Modi’s Visit

During his visit, PM Modi will hold bilateral meetings with his counterparts from Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan, and he will also participate in the SCO Summit. The focus of these meetings will be on counter-terrorism, regional security, and economic cooperation. PM Modi will also have the opportunity to discuss India’s vision for a terror-free region and to seek support for his initiatives from his Central Asian partners.
