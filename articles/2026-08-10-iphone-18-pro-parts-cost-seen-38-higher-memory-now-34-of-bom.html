iPhone 18 Pro parts cost seen 38% higher, memory now 34% of BOM

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • TrendForce estimates 256GB iPhone 18 Pro BOM ~38% above iPhone 17 Pro
  • Memory components now account for 34% of the total bill of materials
  • Rising costs may force Apple to sacrifice margins to sustain shipments

Apple's upcoming iPhone 18 Pro could cost significantly more to build than its predecessor, according to a new analysis from TrendForce. The research firm estimates that the bill of materials (BOM) for the 256GB variant of the iPhone 18 Pro is running roughly 38% higher than that of the iPhone 17 Pro.

The sharp increase is driven largely by memory components, which now account for 34% of the total BOM. That is a substantial jump from previous generations, where memory typically made up a smaller slice of the overall cost.

Memory costs spiral

TrendForce's findings point to a broader trend across the smartphone industry: memory prices have climbed steadily over the past year, and Apple is not immune. The company is reportedly using higher-capacity, higher-performance storage and RAM in the iPhone 18 Pro, pushing up component costs.

Analysts note that this is not just about the base storage tier. The 256GB model, which is expected to be a popular option for Indian buyers, is seeing the steepest cost increase. Lower-tier variants may see comparatively smaller bumps, but the overall trend is unmistakable.

Apple's margin squeeze

The rising BOM could put pressure on Apple's gross margins, traditionally among the healthiest in the hardware business. TrendForce suggests that Apple may have to absorb some of these costs rather than pass them entirely to consumers, especially in price-sensitive markets like India.

"Apple may sacrifice margins to sustain shipment volumes," the firm said in its report. This is a notable shift for a company that has historically maintained premium pricing without compromising profitability.

There is also speculation that Apple could increase iPhone 17 prices ahead of the iPhone 18 Pro launch, a move that would help offset the higher cost of the newer model. However, no official confirmation has been made.

Release timeline and India launch

Despite the cost pressures, the iPhone 18 Pro is expected to launch on schedule. Reports indicate that strong demand is driving a September release, with the device likely to debut alongside other new Apple products.

In India, Apple is reportedly planning to launch up to nine devices, including the iPhone 18 Pro, MacBook Pro with the M6 chip, and others. The exact date has not been announced, but industry watchers expect an announcement within weeks.

What to watch

As the launch approaches, all eyes will be on the final pricing of the iPhone 18 Pro in India. If Apple decides to pass on some of the BOM increase, Indian consumers could see a noticeable price hike. Alternatively, Apple may choose to absorb the costs to maintain its competitive position in the premium segment.

TrendForce's analysis also raises broader questions about the sustainability of rising component costs across the smartphone industry. For now, Apple appears to be navigating the challenge carefully, balancing cost pressures with the need to keep its flagship lineup attractive.
