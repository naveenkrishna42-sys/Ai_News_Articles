KU exams delayed as floods disrupt Nepal education

📅 2026-08-27 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kathmandu University postpones exams due to floods
  • New exam dates yet to be announced
  • Impact on students and institutions significant

Exams Postponed Amid Flooding Crisis

Kathmandu University has announced the postponement of its upcoming exams due to the severe flooding in Nepal. The university, one of the country's most prestigious institutions, has not yet announced new exam dates, leaving students and faculty in limbo.

Broader Impact on Education

The postponement of exams is not an isolated incident. Across Nepal, schools and universities have been severely affected by the floods. According to the Ministry of Education, over 500 schools have been damaged, and thousands of students have been displaced.

Challenges for Students and Institutions

For students, the postponement of exams means a delay in their academic progress. Many students are already struggling to cope with the disruption to their education. The university has assured students that they will be provided with additional study materials and resources to help them catch up.

For institutions, the situation is equally challenging. The damage to infrastructure and the displacement of students have put a significant strain on the resources of schools and universities. The government has pledged to provide financial assistance to affected institutions, but the full extent of the damage is yet to be assessed.

Expert Opinion

Dr. Rajendra Prasad, a professor at Kathmandu University, has expressed concern about the long-term impact of the floods on education. He warns that the postponement of exams could lead to a significant drop in academic standards if students are unable to make up for lost time.

He also highlights the need for better infrastructure and disaster preparedness. "We need to invest in robust infrastructure that can withstand natural disasters," he says. "We also need to have a comprehensive disaster management plan in place to ensure that students and faculty are safe during such crises."

Conclusion

The postponement of exams at Kathmandu University is a stark reminder of the impact of natural disasters on education. It highlights the need for better infrastructure, disaster preparedness, and support for students and institutions during such crises. As the situation unfolds, it will be crucial to monitor the progress and ensure that the academic year is not derailed permanently.
