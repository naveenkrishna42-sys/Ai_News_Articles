Galaxy Z Fold8, iPhone 17 Pro see big price cuts in new deals

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Galaxy Z Fold8 and Z Fold8 Ultra receive notable price cuts
  • iPhone 17 Pro and Pro Max also see reduced prices in latest deals
  • Foldable market heats up as Apple prepares potential entry

Discounts hit flagship foldables and iPhones

Retailers have slashed prices on Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold8 series and Apple's iPhone 17 Pro models, offering some of the steepest discounts seen this year. The deals, spotted by GSMArena, cover both the standard Galaxy Z Fold8 and the premium Z Fold8 Ultra, alongside the iPhone 17 Pro and Pro Max.

While exact price cuts vary by region and seller, the markdowns come at a time when consumers are holding onto devices longer. The move signals intense competition among brands to clear inventory ahead of newer launches.

Why the Fold8 series is turning heads

The Galaxy Z Fold8, which has been praised in reviews as a top-tier gaming device and tablet replacement, is now more accessible. Reviewers at SamMobile called it the best gaming device and tablet they've used, highlighting its large foldable display and improved multitasking.

The Z Fold8 Ultra, meanwhile, has received strong reviews for its camera system and build quality. The discount brings the Ultra closer to the price of traditional flagship phones, potentially tempting users who were hesitant about foldables.

Foldables: mature but still niche

Industry observers note that foldables have matured significantly, but sales growth has slowed as the novelty fades. A recent commentary from The Verge suggested that foldables are now 'sort of boring' — and that could be good news for Apple, which has yet to enter the segment.

India Today has speculated whether Apple will finally embrace a foldable design, with the Galaxy Z Fold8 being seen as a benchmark for what a future iPhone Fold might need to match.

iPhone 17 Pro discounts add pressure

The iPhone 17 Pro and Pro Max are also part of the current deals, a rare occurrence so soon after launch. Apple typically maintains strict pricing, but retailers often offer their own incentives to move units.

The discounts on both Android and iOS flagships suggest a broader trend: consumers are waiting longer between upgrades, forcing brands to sweeten the pot. With festive season sales approaching in India, further price drops are likely.

What this means for buyers

For shoppers, this is an opportune moment to pick up a premium device at a lower cost. The Galaxy Z Fold8 series, in particular, offers a unique form factor that has matured into a reliable daily driver.

Analysts will be watching whether these discounts boost foldable adoption or merely shift sales from other premium models. The coming months will also reveal if Apple responds with its own foldable, which could reshape the market.

As the year progresses, expect more aggressive pricing and possible trade-in bonuses as brands fight for a shrinking pool of upgraders.
