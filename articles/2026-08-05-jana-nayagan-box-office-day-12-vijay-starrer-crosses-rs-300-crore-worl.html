Jana Nayagan Box Office Day 12: Vijay Starrer Crosses Rs 300 Crore Worldwide

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The film crossed Rs 300 crore worldwide on its 12th day.
  • Day 11 saw a 37.2% jump in collections, nearing the milestone.
  • It grossed Rs 210 crore in India within 11 days.
  • The movie is now competing with Suriya's 'Karup' at the box office.

Rs 300 Crore Milestone Achieved

Thalapathy Vijay and Pooja Hegde's 'Jana Nayagan' has crossed the Rs 300 crore mark at the worldwide box office on its 12th day of release. The film, which opened to massive numbers, continues to show remarkable resilience even in its second week.

Trade analysts note that the movie's performance on the second Monday was particularly strong, defying the usual post-weekend slump. The film's steady run has cemented its position as one of the biggest grossers of the year.

Day 11 Jump Sets the Pace

On day 11, the film recorded a substantial 37.2% jump in collections, bringing it tantalizingly close to the Rs 300 crore mark. This surge was driven by strong occupancy in urban centres and single-screen theatres alike.

According to early estimates, the movie grossed around Rs 210 crore in India within its first 11 days. The overseas markets have also contributed significantly, with major collections coming from the US, UAE, and Southeast Asia.

Second Monday Hold

The film's hold on the second Monday has been described as "impressive" by exhibitors. Despite facing competition from new releases, 'Jana Nayagan' managed to retain a significant share of screens and audiences.

This sustained performance suggests strong word-of-mouth and repeat viewings, particularly among Vijay's dedicated fan base. The film's musical numbers and action sequences have been widely praised.

Competition and Context

'Jana Nayagan' is now in a direct box office battle with Suriya's 'Karup', which released around the same time. While 'Karup' has had a decent run, it has been overshadowed by the sheer scale of Vijay's starrer.

Industry observers are watching closely to see whether 'Jana Nayagan' can maintain its momentum into the third week. The film's trajectory will also depend on upcoming releases and festival holidays.

With the Rs 300 crore milestone now crossed, the focus shifts to whether the film can sustain its pace and set new benchmarks. The coming week will be crucial in determining its final box office verdict.
