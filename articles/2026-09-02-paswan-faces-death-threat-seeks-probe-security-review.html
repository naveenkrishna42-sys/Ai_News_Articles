Minister Receives Anonymous Death Threat

Union minister Chirag Paswan has received an anonymous letter warning that 'hardcore terrorists' will kill him. The letter was delivered to his residence in Uttar Pradesh, where Paswan is currently staying. This comes as a significant escalation in the security threats faced by Indian politicians in recent years.

Paswan Seeks Home Ministry Probe

Paswan has reportedly sought a probe from the Home Ministry into the matter. He has also requested for enhanced security measures at his residence. The minister's office has denied any political motive behind the threat, stating that the letter was received by his personal security team.

Security Tightened at Residence

In response to the threat, security has been tightened at Paswan's residence. Additional security personnel have been deployed, and the area has been cordoned off. The minister's office has assured the public that all necessary measures are being taken to ensure his safety.

Letter Warns of Attack by 'Hardcore Terrorists'

The letter, which was written in English, warns that 'hardcore terrorists' will kill Paswan. The letter does not provide any specific details about the attackers or the timing of the attack. This has raised concerns among security experts about the potential for a targeted attack on a high-profile politician.

Paswan's Office Denies Political Motive
