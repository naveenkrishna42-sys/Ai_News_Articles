Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8, Flip8 pre-orders surpass targets in Europe

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Galaxy Z Fold8 and Flip8 surpass Samsung's pre-order targets in Europe.
  • European consumers show strong interest in the new foldable devices.
  • Pre-orders jump 30% from last year, with records broken in US and Korea.

Samsung's latest foldable smartphones, the Galaxy Z Fold8 and Galaxy Z Flip8, have exceeded the company's pre-order targets across Europe, according to a report from GSMArena. The strong showing suggests that consumer appetite for foldable devices remains robust, even as the broader smartphone market faces headwinds.

The news comes as Samsung faces stiff competition from rivals like Google and Motorola, who have also doubled down on foldable form factors. But early indicators suggest Samsung's iterative refinements—slimmer hinges, brighter displays, and improved battery life—are resonating with buyers who want a premium device without compromising on durability.

European demand beats expectations

While Samsung did not disclose exact figures, the company confirmed that pre-orders for both the Fold8 and Flip8 have surpassed internal projections in key European markets. This marks a notable achievement, as Europe has traditionally been a tougher sell for foldables due to higher price points and lingering concerns about long-term reliability.

Industry analysts point to several factors driving this surge. The Fold8's refined multitasking features, like enhanced app continuity and a more seamless cover screen, have made it a more practical daily driver. Meanwhile, the Flip8's compact design and improved camera system have attracted a younger demographic looking for a fashion-forward yet functional device.

Global momentum builds

The European success story mirrors broader global trends. Samsung has reported a 30% jump in pre-orders compared to last year's Fold and Flip models, with particularly strong demand in the United States and South Korea. In the US, carrier promotions and trade-in deals have helped lower the entry barrier, while in Korea, Samsung's home turf, the devices have broken previous pre-order records.

This momentum is critical for Samsung, which has positioned foldables as its flagship differentiator in the premium segment. The company is betting that these devices will eventually replace traditional slab phones as the default choice for power users.

What's driving the appeal?

Reviewers have praised the Fold8 for its comfortable, practical form factor that works well for both productivity and entertainment. The cover screen is more usable than ever, reducing the need to unfold the device for quick tasks. The Flip8, on the other hand, has been lauded for its portability and the ability to use the exterior display for notifications, selfies, and even payments.

Battery life, a common pain point in earlier foldables, has also seen meaningful improvements. Both devices now last a full day under moderate to heavy use, thanks to more efficient processors and better power management software.

Still, challenges remain. The high cost of foldables continues to be a barrier for many consumers, and repair costs for damaged screens can be steep. But with each generation, Samsung is narrowing the gap between foldables and traditional flagships in terms of price and durability.

Looking ahead

As Samsung ramps up production to meet demand, the company is expected to announce official sales figures in the coming weeks. The real test will come in the months ahead, as early adopters begin to share their long-term experiences with the devices.

With the holiday season approaching, Samsung will be hoping that the pre-order buzz translates into sustained sales. If the early indicators are anything to go by, foldables have finally moved from niche novelty to mainstream contender.
