Mumbai University Postpones July 6 Exams Amid Heavy Rain, Fresh Dates Soon

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • All exams scheduled for July 6 have been postponed due to heavy rainfall in Mumbai.
  • The university said revised dates will be announced soon on its official channels.
  • Students have been asked to monitor the university website and notice boards for updates.

Mumbai University on Monday postponed all examinations scheduled for July 6, citing heavy rainfall lashing the city. The decision, announced through an official circular, affects all undergraduate and postgraduate exams that were to be held on the day. The university said fresh dates would be released shortly, though it did not specify a timeline.

The move comes as the India Meteorological Department (IMD) had forecast intense to very heavy rainfall for Mumbai and adjoining regions over the next 24 hours. Several parts of the city have already reported waterlogging, with low-lying areas facing the brunt. Commuters struggled through flooded streets in the morning, and trains ran behind schedule on both the Central and Western lines.

Students Advised to Stay Alert

In an official notice, the university urged students to keep checking its website and other official platforms for updates on the revised timetable. It also asked examination centres to remain prepared for the rescheduled dates. "All concerned are requested to take note and act accordingly," the circular read.

This is not the first time the university has had to postpone exams during the monsoon. In previous years, similar weather-related disruptions have led to rescheduling, with the university typically releasing a revised calendar within a few days. Students, many of whom had travelled from distant suburbs to reach exam centres, expressed mixed reactions. Some welcomed the decision, citing safety concerns, while others worried about the impact on their preparation schedules.

Mumbai's Monsoon Woes

The city has been witnessing relentless rain since Saturday, with several weather stations recording over 200 mm of rainfall in 24 hours. The civic body has deployed pumps and rescue teams in vulnerable spots, but waterlogging has still disrupted road and rail traffic. Schools and colleges in some areas had already declared holidays on Monday.

The IMD has issued an orange alert for Mumbai and neighbouring districts, predicting very heavy rain at isolated places. The weather office has also warned of high tides, which could worsen flooding in coastal areas. Authorities have advised residents to avoid unnecessary travel and stay away from the shoreline.

What Happens Next

The university is expected to announce the revised exam schedule within the next few days. Students are advised to keep their admit cards and ID proofs ready, as the fresh dates could be announced at short notice. Officials have not yet confirmed whether all postponed papers will be clubbed together or spread over multiple days.

Meanwhile, the city continues to brace for more rain through the week. The monsoon session of the state legislature, too, has been affected, with several meetings postponed. For now, students and staff alike will be watching the skies and the university's official channels for the next update.
