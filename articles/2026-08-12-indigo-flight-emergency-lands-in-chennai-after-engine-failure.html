IndiGo flight emergency lands in Chennai after engine failure

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • IndiGo flight from Kolkata to Chennai declared full emergency after left engine failure
  • Aircraft landed safely; all passengers and crew reported safe
  • Technical snag and oil leak reported; investigation underway
  • Chennai airport authorities activated emergency protocols
  • Flight operations temporarily affected; normalcy restored later

An IndiGo flight operating between Kolkata and Chennai made an emergency landing at Chennai airport on Wednesday after the aircraft's left engine failed mid-flight. The incident triggered a full emergency declaration at the airport, with authorities scrambling to ensure the safety of all onboard.

The flight, which had departed from Kolkata, encountered the technical snag while approaching Chennai. According to initial reports, the left engine suffered a failure, leading to an oil leak that prompted the pilots to declare an emergency and seek immediate landing clearance.

Emergency protocols activated

Chennai airport authorities were put on high alert as soon as the distress signal was received. A full emergency was declared, which involves mobilising fire tenders, ambulances, and rescue teams to the runway as a precautionary measure.

The aircraft landed safely on the runway, and all passengers and crew members were reported safe. No injuries have been reported so far, though officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of passengers on board.

Technical snag and oil leak

Preliminary investigations suggest that the engine failure was accompanied by an oil leak, which may have been a contributing factor. Aviation experts say such incidents, while rare, are taken extremely seriously, and the aircraft will undergo a thorough inspection before being cleared for future operations.

IndiGo, in a statement, confirmed the incident and said that the aircraft landed safely following standard operating procedures. The airline has not yet disclosed the aircraft's registration number or the exact time of the incident.

Passenger experience and response

Passengers on board described moments of tension as the crew instructed them to brace for landing. However, the pilots managed to bring the aircraft down smoothly, averting a potential crisis. Many passengers were seen disembarking normally, though some appeared visibly shaken.

The airline has arranged for alternative travel arrangements for affected passengers, though details of these arrangements have not been fully disclosed. IndiGo has also announced that it will cooperate fully with aviation regulators in the investigation.

Broader context: aviation safety in India

This incident comes amid heightened scrutiny of aviation safety in India, with regulators emphasising the need for rigorous maintenance and operational checks. While engine failures are not uncommon in global aviation, each incident is treated as a serious event requiring a detailed probe.

Chennai airport, one of the busiest in southern India, has seen a steady increase in air traffic, making such emergencies particularly challenging for ground staff. The swift response on Wednesday, however, highlighted the preparedness of the airport's emergency services.

The Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) is likely to launch an inquiry into the incident, though no official announcement has been made yet. The aircraft, an Airbus A320neo, is among the most widely used in IndiGo's fleet.

What to watch next

Aviation regulators will now focus on determining the root cause of the engine failure, with the aircraft's maintenance logs and flight data recorders expected to be examined closely. Passengers and industry observers will also be keen to see if any directives are issued regarding the specific engine type involved.
