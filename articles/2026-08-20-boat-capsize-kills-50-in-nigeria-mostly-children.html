Boat capsize kills 50 in Nigeria, mostly children

📅 2026-08-20 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • At least 50 killed, mostly children, in boat capsize in Nigeria
  • Boat was carrying farm workers from Niger to Nigeria
  • Rescue operations ongoing, several missing
  • Official cause of capsize not yet confirmed

Boat carrying farm workers capsizes in Nigeria

A boat carrying farm workers from Niger capsized in the Niger River, resulting in at least 50 deaths, mostly children. The incident occurred in the northwestern part of Nigeria, with rescue operations underway. The exact cause of the capsize is not yet confirmed, but officials suspect overloading.

Rescue operations ongoing

Rescue teams are working to pull survivors from the river, but several people are still missing. The Nigerian military and local authorities have been deployed to assist in the rescue efforts. The boat was reportedly overloaded, with more people on board than it was designed to carry.

Most victims are children

Most of the victims are children, with officials reporting that at least 40 of the deceased are under the age of 18. The incident has raised concerns about the safety of farm workers and the conditions under which they travel. Human rights groups have called for stricter regulations on boat safety and overloading.

Official cause of capsize not yet confirmed

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact cause of the capsize. However, they suspect that overloading was a significant factor. The boat was reportedly carrying a large number of people, including farm workers and their families. The incident has highlighted the need for better safety measures and regulations to protect the lives of those traveling by boat.

What happens next?

Rescue operations will continue, with a focus on locating and rescuing the missing individuals. Authorities are also expected to investigate the incident to determine the exact cause of the capsize. Human rights groups and advocacy organizations may also press for stricter regulations on boat safety and overloading to prevent similar incidents in the future.
