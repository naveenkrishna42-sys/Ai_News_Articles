F&O traders pay Rs 25,000 crore in fees despite losses

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Retail traders paid Rs 25,000 crore in transaction costs in FY26.
  • Nearly 90% of F&O traders incurred losses in FY26.
  • The number of derivatives traders fell for the first time in four years.

Retail traders pay Rs 25,000 crore in F&O trading fees

Retail traders incurred Rs 25,000 crore in transaction costs in FY26, despite posting losses of Rs 91,685 crore, according to data from the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI). The high fees, coupled with significant losses, highlight the challenges faced by retail investors in the futures and options (F&O) market.

Nearly 90% of F&O traders lost money in FY26

SEBI's study revealed that nearly 90% of F&O traders incurred losses in FY26. The total losses amounted to Rs 91,685 crore, even as participation in the market fell. This trend indicates a significant shift in the F&O market, with more traders experiencing losses than gains.

Derivatives trader base falls for first time in four years

The number of derivatives traders decreased for the first time in four years in FY26, according to SEBI. This decline in participation, combined with the high transaction costs, suggests a cooling off in retail interest in F&O trading. The regulatory body has not yet provided a detailed analysis of the reasons behind this decline.

Young and low-income traders bear the brunt

SEBI's study showed that 88.5% of traders aged under 30 incurred losses, with most coming from low-income groups. This demographic trend indicates that younger and less affluent investors are more likely to be affected by the high fees and losses in the F&O market. The study did not provide specific figures on the income levels of the traders.

What happens next?

The decline in participation and the high transaction costs are likely to prompt regulatory scrutiny and potential reforms in the F&O market. SEBI may need to address the issues faced by retail traders, including the high fees and the high proportion of losses. Additionally, the market may see further shifts in investor behavior, with a possible increase in cautious approaches to F&O trading.
