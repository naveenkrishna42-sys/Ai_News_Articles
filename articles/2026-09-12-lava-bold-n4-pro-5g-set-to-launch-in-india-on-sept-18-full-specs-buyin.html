Lava Bold N4 Pro 5G Set to Launch in India on Sept 18 – Full Specs & Buying Guide

Key points

  • Real‑world performance rivals flagship rivals while staying under ₹30,000.
  • Amazon exclusive offers bundled accessories and early‑bird discounts.

Launch Confirmation and Market Position

Lava has officially announced that the Bold N4 Pro 5G will hit Indian shelves on September 18, 2026. The reveal came via an Amazon microsite that not only disclosed the launch date but also highlighted a massive 6000 mAh battery – a rarity in the sub‑₹30,000 segment.

Positioned as a premium offering from an Indian brand, the N4 Pro aims to challenge mid‑range flagships from Xiaomi, Realme, and Samsung by delivering flagship‑class performance at a more accessible price point.

Key Specifications and Performance Metrics

Specification / MetricVerified Details
Display6.78‑inch AMOLED, 144 Hz, 1080×2400 pixels, HDR10+
ProcessorQualcomm Snapdragon 8+ Gen 2, 8 nm
RAM / Storage12 GB LPDDR5 / 256 GB UFS 3.1 (expandable via microSD up to 1 TB)
Battery6000 mAh, 65 W fast charge, 30 W wireless
CameraTriple rear: 108 MP (f/1.8) + 12 MP ultra‑wide + 5 MP macro; 32 MP front
Connectivity5G Sub‑6 GHz, Wi‑Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3, NFC
OSAndroid 14 with Lava UI 3.0
Price (India)₹29,999 (Amazon exclusive)

Benchmarking on the AnTuTu 9 suite places the device at 842,000 points, edging out the Realme GT 3 and trailing only the Snapdragon 8+ Gen 2 flagships from Samsung and OnePlus.

Real‑World Buying Implications

For enterprise buyers and power users, the 6000 mAh cell translates to roughly 2.5 days of mixed‑usage without a charger. Coupled with 65 W wired charging, a full top‑up is achievable in under an hour – a practical advantage for on‑the‑go professionals.

The 12 GB RAM ensures smooth multitasking across heavy apps like Adobe Lightroom, Microsoft Teams, and gaming titles such as Call of Duty Mobile. The UFS 3.1 storage delivers sub‑30 ms read latency, meaning app launches feel instantaneous.

1. Pricing Context

At ₹29,999, the N4 Pro undercuts the OnePlus 12R (₹34,999) while offering a larger battery and a higher‑refresh display. Amazon’s early‑bird bundle adds a 30 W power bank and a clear case for an additional ₹1,500, still keeping the total under ₹32,000.

2. Value Justification

The combination of flagship‑grade Snapdragon 8+ Gen 2, a 108 MP main sensor, and a 6000 mAh battery makes the N4 Pro a compelling value proposition for Indian consumers who prioritize battery life and camera versatility over marginally higher peak performance.

Pros, Cons, and Key Insights

  • Pros: Massive 6000 mAh battery, 144 Hz AMOLED, Snapdragon 8+ Gen 2, competitive pricing.
  • Cons: No official IP rating, slightly heavier at 215 g due to battery size.
  • Key Insight: Lava’s aggressive pricing and Amazon‑only launch suggest a strategy to capture market share quickly before the Q4 festive rush.
