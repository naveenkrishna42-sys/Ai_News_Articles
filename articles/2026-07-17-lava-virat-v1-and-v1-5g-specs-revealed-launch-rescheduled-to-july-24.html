Lava Virat V1 and V1 5G Specs Revealed, Launch Rescheduled to July 24

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Lava Virat V1 and Virat V1 5G launch rescheduled to July 24.
  • Smartphones to be sold exclusively on Flipkart as an online-first series.
  • Specs include 6.75-inch 120Hz display and 6000mAh battery for the 5G variant.

Lava Reschedules Virat Series Launch

Homegrown smartphone maker Lava has announced a new launch date for its upcoming 'Virat' series. The Virat V1 and Virat V1 5G will now debut in India on July 24. The company had earlier planned a different timeline but has now rescheduled the event.

The new lineup will be sold exclusively through Flipkart. This marks Lava's latest push into the online-first segment, a strategy many Indian brands are adopting to reach a wider digital audience.

Key Specifications Revealed

According to reports, the Virat V1 5G will feature a 6.75-inch display with a 120Hz refresh rate. This is a significant upgrade for a budget-oriented smartphone, promising smoother scrolling and better gaming visuals.

Battery life appears to be a strong point. The 5G variant is expected to pack a 6,000mAh battery. Details on the standard Virat V1's battery are still awaited, but it is likely to offer a similar or slightly smaller capacity.

The exact processor details have not been officially confirmed yet. However, industry sources suggest the 5G model will be powered by a mid-range chip capable of handling 5G bands. The standard Virat V1 is expected to feature a 4G-only processor to keep costs down.

Partnership with Flipkart

Lava has partnered with Flipkart to launch this series. The collaboration is aimed at making the phones accessible to a large online customer base. Flipkart will handle exclusive sales and likely offer launch-day discounts or bank offers.

This is not the first time Lava has tied up with an e-commerce platform. The company has previously used online channels to release limited-edition devices. The Virat series, however, seems to be a more concerted effort to build a dedicated online brand.

What to Expect on Launch Day

Pricing remains under wraps. Analysts expect the Virat V1 to be priced competitively, possibly under Rs 10,000. The 5G variant could fall in the Rs 12,000 to Rs 15,000 range, depending on the final specifications.

Both phones are expected to run a near-stock version of Android. Lava has historically favoured a clean user interface with minimal bloatware, a feature that has appealed to budget-conscious buyers.

With the launch just a week away, more details are likely to surface. Official product pages on Flipkart are expected to go live soon, confirming the complete spec sheet and final pricing.
