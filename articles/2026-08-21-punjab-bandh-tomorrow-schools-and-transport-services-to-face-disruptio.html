Punjab Bandh Tomorrow: Schools and Transport Services to Face Disruptions

📅 2026-08-21 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Qaumi Insaf Morcha calls for state-wide protest demanding the release of Bandi Singhs.
  • Private schools shift to online mode; exam schedules face potential postponements.
  • Security heightened across the Tricity area to manage potential highway blockades.

Call for Statewide Shutdown

Normal life in Punjab is expected to remain disrupted tomorrow following a call for a statewide bandh issued by the Qaumi Insaf Morcha. The organization is staging the protest to demand the release of 'Bandi Singhs'—individuals they describe as Sikh prisoners who have completed their sentences.

Authorities have put the state administration on high alert as the call for the shutdown gains momentum. Local police forces are coordinating with district officials to prevent any untoward incidents during the demonstrations.

Impact on Educational Institutions

Educational institutions across the state are bracing for the impact of the protest. Several private schools have already announced a shift to online classes to ensure student safety and avoid disruptions to the academic calendar.

Higher education institutions are also reviewing their schedules. Panjab University and affiliated colleges are currently assessing whether upcoming examinations need to be postponed. Students have been advised to monitor official portals for updates regarding their specific exam centers.

Transport and Commercial Disruptions

Commuters are likely to face significant hurdles tomorrow, particularly on major national highways. The protest organizers have indicated that highway blockades are part of their strategy, which could stall the movement of heavy vehicles and public transport for several hours.

Local markets in several districts are expected to remain closed as shopkeepers weigh the risks of operating during the bandh. While the call for the shutdown is widespread, the extent of compliance remains to be seen in urban centers versus rural belts. Businesses are opting for caution, with many opting to keep shutters down to avoid potential losses or damage.

Security Measures in Tricity

The Tricity region, comprising Chandigarh, Mohali, and Panchkula, remains a focal point for security preparations. Police checkpoints have been intensified at entry and exit points to monitor the movement of protesters and maintain order.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact duration of the blockades or the specific routes that will face the heaviest restrictions. Residents are encouraged to plan their travel well in advance and avoid non-essential trips to areas known for high protest activity.

State authorities are expected to hold further meetings this evening to decide if additional security deployments are required to manage the situation on the ground tomorrow.
