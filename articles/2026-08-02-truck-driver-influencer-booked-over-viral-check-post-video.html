Truck driver influencer booked over viral check post video

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • A truck driver influencer was booked after a video showing a 'sting operation' at a Tamil Nadu check post went viral.
  • The clip allegedly captured irregularities during vehicle inspections, prompting police action.
  • Officials have not yet confirmed the full sequence of events; an investigation is underway.

A truck driver who doubled as a social media influencer has been booked by police in Tamil Nadu after a video described as a 'sting operation' at a check post went viral, triggering a flurry of reactions online and an official response from authorities.

The clip, widely shared across platforms, purportedly shows the influencer confronting officials at a state check post, capturing exchanges that appear to point to lapses in the inspection process. Within hours, the footage had drawn thousands of comments, with many users demanding a thorough probe.

What the video shows

The viral footage, shot on a mobile phone, shows the influencer engaging with personnel at the check post, claiming that vehicles were being waved through without proper scrutiny. In one segment, he alleges that cash changed hands, though this has not been independently verified.

Police have not yet confirmed the authenticity of the full video or the specific allegations made in it. However, they have registered a case against the influencer, citing the manner in which the operation was conducted.

Officials said the booking was made after a preliminary review of the footage and a complaint from the check post staff, who accused the influencer of obstructing their duties.

Police response and legal action

According to sources, the case has been filed under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including charges related to obstructing a public servant in the discharge of duties. The influencer, whose identity has not been officially released, is yet to be arrested.

Police said they are examining the video frame by frame to ascertain whether any procedural violations occurred. They have also sought the influencer's cooperation in the inquiry.

"We are looking into all angles," a senior officer told reporters, without elaborating. "The video is part of the evidence, but we need to verify the circumstances under which it was shot."

Mixed reactions online

The incident has split public opinion. Some social media users have hailed the influencer as a whistleblower, saying such stings are necessary to expose corruption at road check posts. Others have criticised the move, arguing that self-styled vigilantes can disrupt lawful procedures.

Several transport associations have weighed in, calling for a transparent inquiry. They have also urged authorities to address the underlying issues that prompt drivers to record such interactions.

This is not the first time a citizen's video has led to police action in Tamil Nadu. In recent years, several clips of alleged misconduct by officials have surfaced online, prompting departmental reviews.

What to watch next

As the investigation progresses, the focus will be on whether the influencer's claims hold up under scrutiny. The transport department is expected to issue a detailed statement on the check post's functioning, and the outcome could set a precedent for how such viral exposes are handled in the state.
