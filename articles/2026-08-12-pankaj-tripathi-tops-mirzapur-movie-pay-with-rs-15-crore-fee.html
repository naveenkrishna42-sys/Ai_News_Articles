Pankaj Tripathi tops Mirzapur movie pay with Rs 15 crore fee

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pankaj Tripathi's fee for Mirzapur: The Movie is Rs 15 crore, highest in the series.
  • The film is a big-screen continuation of the popular Amazon Prime Video series.
  • Other cast members' salaries have not been officially disclosed.

Mirzapur goes big screen with record payday

Pankaj Tripathi, the actor synonymous with the ruthless Kaleen Bhaiya, is set to earn a whopping Rs 15 crore for his role in the upcoming Mirzapur: The Movie. This makes him the highest-paid actor in the franchise's history, according to a report by The Times of India.

The fee marks a significant jump from his television earnings, reflecting the film's anticipated scale and the character's central place in the story. Tripathi's portrayal of the aging mafia patriarch has been a fan favourite since the show premiered on Amazon Prime Video in 2018.

A franchise that refuses to fade

The announcement comes as the makers gear up for a theatrical release, a move that shifts the gritty, gun-laden drama from the small screen to multiplexes. The original series, created by Puneet Krishna and Karan Anshuman, built a massive following across India with its dark humour, violent power struggles, and memorable one-liners.

While Tripathi's fee has been widely reported, the salaries of other key cast members, including Ali Fazal, Shweta Tripathi Sharma, and Rasika Dugal, remain undisclosed. Industry insiders suggest that the budget for the film adaptation is significantly higher than the show's per-season cost, though official figures have not been confirmed.

Why the Rs 15 crore figure matters

For context, a Rs 15 crore fee places Tripathi in the upper echelon of character actors in Hindi cinema, though still below the top-tier stars who command Rs 50 crore or more. The number signals that OTT-to-film transitions are becoming lucrative for actors who built their fame on streaming platforms.

It also underscores the commercial confidence in the Mirzapur brand. The show's third season, which released earlier this year, broke viewership records for Amazon Prime Video, and the film is expected to capitalise on that momentum.

What the film promises

Details about the plot remain tightly under wraps. Reports suggest that the movie will pick up after the events of season three, with Kaleen Bhaiya's empire facing new threats from within and outside Mirzapur. The film is expected to feature the trademark violence and sharp dialogue that made the series a cultural phenomenon.

Fans have speculated about the return of several characters, including some who were presumed dead. The makers have kept casting announcements minimal, fuelling further curiosity.

As the release date approaches, more information about the cast, crew, and promotional plans is likely to emerge. For now, the Rs 15 crore fee has set a benchmark, and it will be interesting to see if other actors in the ensemble negotiate similar deals as the franchise expands.
