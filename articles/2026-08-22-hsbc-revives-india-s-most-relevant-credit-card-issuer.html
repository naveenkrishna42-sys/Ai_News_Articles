HSBC Credit Cards: Features & Perks

HSBC is making a comeback in India’s credit card market with its latest offerings. The HSBC Cashback Credit Card and HSBC Frequent Flyer Credit Card are designed to cater to different needs of Indian consumers.

HSBC Cashback Credit Card

The HSBC Cashback Credit Card offers a generous 5X rewards on fuel purchases, 2X on groceries, and 1.5X on dining. This card also provides 12 free lounge visits per year, which is a significant perk for frequent travelers.

HSBC Frequent Flyer Credit Card

The HSBC Frequent Flyer Credit Card is tailored for those who travel frequently. It offers 5X rewards on fuel, 2X on groceries, and 1.5X on dining. This card also includes 12 free lounge visits per year and a complimentary Priority Pass membership.

Joining & Annual Fees

Both HSBC credit cards come with no joining fee. The annual fee for the HSBC Cashback Credit Card is ₹500, while the HSBC Frequent Flyer Credit Card has an annual fee of ₹1,000.

Reward & Cashback Rates

The reward and cashback rates are consistent across both cards. Consumers can earn 5X rewards on fuel, 2X on groceries, and 1.5X on dining. These rewards can be redeemed for statement credits or transferred to the HSBC Frequent Flyer program.

Lounge Access & Additional Benefits

Both cards offer 12 free lounge visits per year. The HSBC Frequent Flyer Credit Card also includes a complimentary Priority Pass membership, which provides access to over 1,000 lounges worldwide.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for the HSBC credit cards, applicants must be salaried individuals with a minimum salary of ₹30,000 per month. They must also have a valid Indian bank account and a valid government-issued ID.

How to Apply Online

To apply for the HSBC credit cards online, visit the HSBC website or download the HSBC mobile app. Fill in the application form with the required details and submit it. Once approved, you can claim your welcome rewards and joining cashback bonuses.

Final Recommendation

HSBC’s credit cards are ideal for individuals who frequently travel and make significant fuel and grocery purchases. The generous reward rates and lounge access make these cards a valuable addition to any consumer’s financial toolkit.
