CM Vijay Meets Amit Shah: NEET, Delimitation Top Tamil Nadu Agenda

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tamil Nadu CM Vijay met Union Home Minister Amit Shah to discuss state-specific grievances.
  • Key issues raised include the NEET exam controversy, delimitation concerns, and water sharing.
  • The meeting took place during the Southern Zonal Council session involving multiple southern states.

High-Stakes Dialogue in the South

Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Vijay held a significant meeting with Union Home Minister Amit Shah on Thursday. The interaction took place on the sidelines of the Southern Zonal Council meeting, providing a platform for the state government to present its primary concerns to the central leadership.

The agenda focused on three critical issues that have remained at the forefront of Tamil Nadu's political discourse. These include the contentious National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET), the looming process of delimitation, and the ongoing Cauvery water-sharing dispute.

The NEET and Delimitation Concerns

The state government has long expressed reservations regarding the NEET examination, citing its impact on students from diverse socio-economic backgrounds. CM Vijay reiterated these concerns during the discussion, highlighting the state's preference for an alternative admission process that aligns with local educational policies.

Parallel to this, the issue of delimitation of constituencies took center stage. Tamil Nadu, like several other southern states, remains apprehensive about the potential political consequences of a new delimitation exercise. There are concerns that states with lower population growth rates could see their political representation in Parliament reduced, a point the Chief Minister emphasized during the deliberations.

Water Security and Regional Interests

The Cauvery water-sharing dispute also formed a core component of the discussions. With water security remaining a persistent challenge, the state government urged the Centre to intervene in ensuring consistent water release as per established agreements.

The Southern Zonal Council meeting also served as a venue for other states to voice their grievances. Telangana’s Deputy Chief Minister used the forum to demand the protection of the state’s rights regarding the allocation of Krishna river waters. The presence of multiple state leaders allowed for a broader assessment of the regional challenges facing the southern corridor.

Official Response and Expectations

While the meeting concluded with the submission of the state's formal requests, central authorities have not yet released a detailed response regarding the specific outcomes of the discussions. The Home Minister’s office is expected to review the points raised by the Tamil Nadu government in the coming weeks.

Observers are now watching for any potential policy shifts or administrative follow-ups from the Ministry of Home Affairs regarding the state's specific demands.
