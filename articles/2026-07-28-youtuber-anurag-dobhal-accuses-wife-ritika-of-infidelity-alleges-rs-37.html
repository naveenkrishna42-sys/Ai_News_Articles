YouTuber Anurag Dobhal Accuses Wife Ritika of Infidelity, Alleges Rs 37 Lakh Demand to See Son

YouTuber Anurag Dobhal has made serious allegations against his wife, Ritika Chauhan, accusing her of infidelity during her pregnancy. In a series of social media posts, Dobhal claimed that Ritika cheated on him with her best friend, and that she later demanded Rs 37 lakh to allow him to meet their child.

The Allegations

Dobhal took to his social media handles to share what he described as 'proof' of the affair. He alleged that Ritika was involved with her best friend while she was pregnant, a claim that has now gone viral across multiple platforms.

He wrote, 'My only mistake was that I loved too much.' The YouTuber did not provide specific dates or locations of the alleged incidents, but said he had evidence to back his claims.

Rs 37 Lakh Demand

One of the most startling allegations Dobhal made was that Ritika demanded Rs 37 lakh from him as a condition to meet their son. He did not clarify whether this demand was made before or after the child's birth, or if any payment was made.

News outlets including NDTV, Hindustan Times, India Today, and News18 have reported on the story, but none have independently verified Dobhal's claims or the authenticity of the 'proof' he shared.

Background of the Couple

Anurag Dobhal and Ritika Chauhan are known figures in the Indian YouTube and social media space. The couple has often shared glimpses of their personal life with their followers, making this public fallout a matter of intense interest among their audience.

Neither Ritika Chauhan nor her representatives have responded to the allegations publicly so far. Attempts to reach her for comment have not yielded any reaction.

What Comes Next

As the story continues to trend on social media, the focus now shifts to any legal or official response from Ritika Chauhan. Dobhal has indicated he may reveal more details in the coming days, but no timeline has been given.

Observers are watching to see if this personal dispute moves into the legal domain, especially given the financial and custodial implications of the allegations.
