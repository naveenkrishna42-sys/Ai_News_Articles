iQoo tablet with new Snapdragon chipset tipped to debut alongside iQoo 16

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • A new iQoo tablet with Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chipset has been tipped.
  • The tablet is expected to debut alongside the iQoo 16 series.
  • Separate leaks also suggest the iQoo 16, Neo 12, and Z12 Turbo may pack up to 10,000mAh batteries.

Chinese smartphone brand iQoo appears to be expanding its tablet lineup. A new leak suggests the company is working on a compact tablet powered by Qualcomm's upcoming Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chipset. The device is tipped to launch alongside the iQoo 16 series later this year.

Compact tablet with flagship silicon

According to tipsters, the iQoo tablet will feature a relatively compact form factor. While specific display dimensions remain unconfirmed, the device is expected to target users who prefer smaller tablets for portability.

The Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chipset is Qualcomm's next-generation flagship processor. If the leak holds, the tablet will be among the first devices to ship with this silicon, offering top-tier performance for gaming and multitasking.

Launch timeline and iQoo 16 series

The tablet is expected to debut alongside the iQoo 16 series. The iQoo 16 lineup is rumoured to include the standard iQoo 16, iQoo 16 Pro, and possibly a Neo 12 or Z12 Turbo variant.

Separate leaks from 91mobiles and GSMArena indicate that the iQoo 16 series phones could pack batteries as large as 10,000mAh. The iQoo Z12 Turbo is also said to bring the Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset to the mid-range segment, blurring the line between flagship and budget performance.

Battery and camera details

Details about the iQoo 16's camera system have also surfaced. The phone is tipped to feature a upgraded primary sensor, though specific megapixel counts remain under wraps. The larger battery capacities suggest iQoo is prioritising endurance, likely to support the power-hungry new chipsets.

The iQoo Neo 12 and Z12 series are also expected to benefit from bigger batteries. This aligns with a broader industry trend where brands are packing 6,000mAh to 7,000mAh cells even in mid-range devices.

What to expect from the iQoo tablet

iQoo's tablet strategy so far has focused on performance-first devices. The company's previous tablet, the iQoo Pad, offered a MediaTek Dimensity 9000+ chipset and a 12.1-inch display. The new compact model could be a smaller, more portable alternative with similar power.

It remains unclear whether the tablet will support stylus input or include a high-refresh-rate display. Given the flagship chipset, a 120Hz or 144Hz panel is plausible.

Officials have not yet confirmed any details about the device. The leaks come from tipsters on Weibo and Chinese forums, which have a mixed track record. iQoo typically announces its flagship phones in the second half of the year, so an official unveiling may still be months away.

For now, users can expect a compact tablet with cutting-edge performance, launching alongside one of iQoo's most anticipated phone series. Watch for more concrete specifications as the launch window approaches.
