Chennai police constable found dead at St. Thomas Mount residence

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Constable found dead at his residence in St. Thomas Mount
  • Police have registered a case and are investigating
  • Cause of death yet to be confirmed

A police constable was found dead at his residence in St. Thomas Mount on Saturday morning, triggering an investigation by local authorities. The deceased, identified as a 45-year-old man attached to a city police unit, was discovered by family members in his house around 7 am.

Discovery and Initial Response

According to preliminary reports, the constable's relatives grew concerned when he did not respond to calls and went to check on him. They found him lying unconscious in a room and immediately alerted neighbours, who informed the local police station.

Upon arrival, officers from the St. Thomas Mount police station declared him dead at the scene. The body has been sent to a government hospital for post-mortem examination to ascertain the exact cause of death.

Investigation Underway

Police have registered a case under suspicious death and have begun questioning family members and neighbours. Initial inquiries suggest no forced entry or signs of struggle, but officials have not ruled out any angle.

A senior officer told reporters that the constable had been on leave for the past two days. His colleagues described him as a quiet and diligent officer, and they expressed shock at the sudden demise.

Family in Mourning

The constable is survived by his wife and two teenage children. The family has been inconsolable, and relatives have gathered at the house, with several colleagues from the police department visiting to offer condolences.

Authorities have assured a thorough investigation, and a forensic team has examined the premises. The post-mortem report is expected to provide clarity on whether the death was due to natural causes or any foul play.

Previous Incidents and Context

Deaths of police personnel in their residences are rare but not unheard of in the city. In recent years, cases of cardiac arrest and other health-related issues have been reported among officers, often attributed to stress and irregular duty hours.

However, police have not yet linked this incident to any such pattern. They are awaiting the medical report before drawing any conclusions.

What Happens Next

The post-mortem report is expected within a day, after which the police will decide on further action. The constable's last rites will be performed with full police honours once the body is released by the hospital.

Authorities have urged the public not to speculate, stating that all facts will emerge from the investigation.
