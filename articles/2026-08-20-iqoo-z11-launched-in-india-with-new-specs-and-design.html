iQOO Z11 launched in India with new specs and design

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iQOO Z11 launched in India with 7050mAh battery and 144Hz AMOLED display
  • iQOO Z11 features AI features and different design from previous models
  • iQOO Buds TWS launched under Rs 2,000 with up to 50 hours battery life and AI Call Noise Cancelling

iQOO Z11 Launched in India

iQOO, a subsidiary of BBK Electronics, has launched its latest smartphone, the iQOO Z11, in India. The device boasts a 7050mAh battery and a 144Hz AMOLED display, making it a strong contender in the premium segment.

Key Features of iQOO Z11

The iQOO Z11 comes with a 6.78-inch 144Hz AMOLED display, offering vibrant colors and smooth visuals. The device is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset, ensuring top-notch performance. It features a triple-camera setup on the rear, including a 50MP main sensor, and a 16MP front-facing camera for selfies.

The standout feature of the iQOO Z11 is its massive 7050mAh battery, which supports 120W fast charging. The device also includes various AI features, such as AI Call Noise Cancelling and AI Portrait Mode, enhancing the user experience.

iQOO Buds TWS Launched

Alongside the iQOO Z11, the company has also launched the iQOO Buds TWS earbuds. Priced under Rs 2,000, these earbuds offer a compelling feature set for the price.

Key Features of iQOO Buds

The iQOO Buds TWS feature a 10mm dynamic driver for rich audio quality. They come with up to 50 hours of battery life, including 10 hours of playtime on a single charge. The earbuds also include AI Call Noise Cancelling, which helps in reducing background noise during calls.

Both the iQOO Z11 and the iQOO Buds TWS are designed to cater to the needs of premium segment buyers in India. The devices are expected to compete with other flagship smartphones and earbuds in the market.
