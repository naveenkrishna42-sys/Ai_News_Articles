Air India turbulence: Misinformation spreads as pilot drug test awaits confirmation

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Social media misinformation about the Air India turbulence incident is spreading rapidly.
  • The pilot's psychoactive substance test is not negative but requires a confirmatory test, officials say.
  • Experts advise waiting for official confirmation before drawing conclusions.

Misinformation spreads after turbulence incident

Within hours of the Air India Phuket-Delhi flight encountering severe turbulence, social media platforms were flooded with unverified claims and speculative posts. The incident, which caused injuries to passengers and crew, has become a breeding ground for misinformation, with several posts falsely asserting that the pilot was under the influence of substances at the time of the incident.

According to officials familiar with the matter, the captain of the flight has been suspended pending further investigation. However, authorities have clarified that the initial screening test for psychoactive substances was 'not negative' and has been sent for a confirmatory test. This crucial detail, often missing from viral posts, has led to widespread confusion and premature conclusions.

Official statements and expert caution

The government, in a statement, said the captain's screening test result was 'not negative' and has been forwarded for a confirmatory analysis. This is a standard procedure in such cases, as screening tests can sometimes produce false positives. Experts emphasize that the confirmatory test, which is more rigorous and accurate, will determine the final outcome.

"Screening tests are preliminary and not definitive. It is reckless to assume guilt based on an incomplete process," said a senior aviation safety expert, requesting anonymity. "The public should wait for the confirmatory test results before any judgment is made."

The airline has not yet issued a detailed statement on the matter, but sources indicate that the pilot has been relieved of duties pending the investigation's outcome.

Social media's role in amplifying rumors

The rapid spread of misinformation has raised concerns among aviation professionals and fact-checkers. Unverified posts on platforms like X (formerly Twitter) and Facebook have claimed everything from the pilot's immediate dismissal to fabricated cockpit conversations. These posts often lack context and ignore the procedural steps involved in such investigations.

Experts note that the public's demand for quick answers often clashes with the need for thorough, evidence-based inquiry. "In high-profile incidents, there is a tendency to jump to conclusions. But aviation investigations are meticulous and time-consuming," said another analyst. "Misinformation not only harms the individuals involved but also undermines trust in the system."

What the investigation involves

In cases involving alleged substance use, the standard protocol includes an initial screening test followed by a confirmatory test, usually conducted by a specialized laboratory. The confirmatory test uses advanced techniques like gas chromatography-mass spectrometry to rule out false positives. Results can take several days to a week.

The Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) is overseeing the investigation into the turbulence incident itself, which includes analyzing flight data and cockpit voice recorders. The pilot's test results are just one aspect of the broader inquiry.

Experts urge patience and verification

Aviation experts and media watchdogs are urging the public to rely on official sources and verified reporting. They recommend cross-checking information before sharing it on social media, as false narratives can have serious consequences for the individuals involved and for public confidence in air travel.

The incident has also reignited discussions about passenger safety and crew training in extreme weather conditions. Turbulence, while common, can be severe and unpredictable, and airlines continuously update their protocols to handle such scenarios.

As the confirmatory test result is awaited, the focus remains on ensuring a fair and transparent investigation. The suspended pilot, whose identity has not been officially released, awaits the outcome that will determine the next steps.

The coming days are critical as the confirmatory test results are expected to be released, which will clarify the situation. Meanwhile, authorities are likely to issue further updates on the turbulence investigation and any regulatory actions that may follow.
