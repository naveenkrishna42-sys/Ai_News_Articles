Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 pre-orders jump 30% year-on-year

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pre-orders for Galaxy Z Fold8 are 30% higher than last year's foldable lineup.
  • Samsung launched Z Fold8, Z Fold8 Ultra, Z Flip8, Watch Ultra2, and Watch9.
  • New design and consumer interest in foldables fuel demand amid Apple competition.

Samsung says pre-orders for its new Galaxy Z Fold8 are running 30 percent ahead of last year's foldable devices. The company made the announcement shortly after officially unveiling the Z Fold8, Z Fold8 Ultra, Z Flip8, Watch Ultra2, and Watch9. The numbers suggest that consumer appetite for foldable phones is growing, even as Apple prepares to enter the segment.

Strong demand for the new foldable lineup

The 30 percent increase in pre-orders is a clear signal that Samsung's latest foldables have struck a chord. The company has not disclosed absolute figures, but the comparative growth points to a healthy uptake. Industry watchers attribute this to the refined design and the introduction of the premium Ultra variant, which offers a distinct option for power users.

Samsung's foldable strategy appears to be paying off. The new devices build on the company's experience in the category, addressing earlier concerns about durability and hinge mechanics. The result is a more polished product that appeals to both early adopters and mainstream buyers.

What's new in the Z Fold8 series

The Z Fold8 and Z Fold8 Ultra share the core folding design but differ in specs. The Ultra model targets enthusiasts with upgraded cameras and display capabilities. Both phones feature a slimmer profile and improved multitasking software, making them more practical for daily use.

The Z Flip8 continues the clamshell format, focusing on portability and style. Alongside the phones, Samsung refreshed its wearable lineup with the Watch Ultra2 and Watch9, expanding its ecosystem. The combined launch underscores Samsung's push to keep its lead in the foldable market.

Apple's looming entry adds pressure

Apple is reportedly working on its own foldable device, which could reshape the competitive landscape. Samsung's early lead in the segment gives it an advantage, but the market is likely to get more crowded. The strong pre-order numbers suggest Samsung is not resting on its laurels.

Analysts note that Samsung's aggressive pricing and trade-in offers have helped drive early demand. The company is also leveraging its retail and online channels to reach a wider audience. Whether this momentum holds beyond the pre-order phase remains to be seen.

What this means for the foldable market

The 30 percent growth in pre-orders is a positive indicator for the broader foldable category. It suggests that consumers are increasingly comfortable with the form factor and willing to pay a premium for it. Samsung's ability to iterate quickly and offer multiple variants gives it flexibility in catering to different buyer preferences.

Competitors like Google and Motorola are also expanding their foldable portfolios, but Samsung's scale and brand recognition remain key strengths. The company's investment in foldable technology over the past several years is starting to yield tangible returns.

As the pre-order period winds down, Samsung will be watching whether sales meet the elevated expectations. The company has not yet shared shipment targets for the new devices. Early indicators are encouraging, but the real test will come when the phones reach stores and face full market scrutiny.

Watch for Samsung's next move in the coming weeks, including how it positions the Z Fold8 against Apple's anticipated foldable. The competition is heating up, and consumers stand to benefit from the innovation.
