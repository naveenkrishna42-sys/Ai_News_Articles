Kangana Ranaut challenges CJP to fight elections, backs elected government's authority

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kangana Ranaut dared the CJP to contest elections instead of protesting.
  • She said an elected government has the right to make decisions without being arm-twisted.
  • Hema Malini also commented, calling for peaceful dialogue and questioning what the protests would achieve.

BJP MP and actor Kangana Ranaut has taken a strong stand against the ongoing protests by the Confederation of Journalists and Professionals (CJP) near Parliament. She dared the protesting body to fight elections instead of trying to dictate terms through demonstrations.

'Don't arm-twist the government'

Speaking to reporters, Ranaut said that an elected government has the right to make decisions. She criticised the CJP for trying to pressure the administration through street protests.

"If you want to bring change, contest elections. Don't arm-twist the government. Protests should not decide who remains in office or what policies are made," she said. Her remarks come as the CJP continues its protest march in the capital, demanding changes in media regulation and press freedom.

Hema Malini echoes similar sentiments

Veteran actor and BJP MP Hema Malini also reacted to the CJP protest. She called for peaceful dialogue and questioned the effectiveness of the demonstrations.

"Protest karne se koi kaam nahi hoga (nothing will be achieved by protesting)," Malini said. She urged the protesters to engage in constructive talks with the government rather than holding marches that disrupt public life.

What will they gain?

Malini added that she does not understand what the CJP hopes to achieve through the protest. She stressed that dialogue is the only way forward in a democracy.

"What will they gain by blocking roads and creating chaos? The government is always ready to listen if there is a genuine issue," she said.

Background of the protest

The CJP has been organising protests near Parliament for several days. The group comprises journalists and media professionals demanding greater transparency and freedom in news reporting. They have accused the current government of muzzling the press and pushing a partisan narrative.

However, the BJP MPs have dismissed these allegations. They maintain that the government respects press freedom but will not tolerate attempts to undermine its authority.

Political reactions

Opposition parties have criticised the BJP MPs for their remarks. They say that the right to protest is fundamental to democracy and cannot be dismissed as arm-twisting.

Congress leaders accused the ruling party of being intolerant towards dissent. They pointed out that the CJP represents genuine concerns of journalists across the country.

Officials have not yet confirmed any plans to meet the CJP delegation. The protest is expected to continue for the next few days.

What happens next will depend on whether the government agrees to a dialogue or the CJP decides to escalate its agitation. Both sides appear unwilling to back down for now.
