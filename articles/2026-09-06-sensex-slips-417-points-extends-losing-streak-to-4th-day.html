Market Snapshot: Fourth Consecutive Session Of Losses

Sensex extended its losing run to four straight sessions on 3 September 2026, slipping 417 points in intraday trade. The decline marks the longest negative streak in recent weeks and reflects broad-based caution across domestic equities. Traders cited external headwinds and sector-specific weakness as immediate triggers for the pullback.

The broader market underperformed benchmark indices, with selling pressure visible across mid and small caps. Volume remained firm, indicating active participation rather than a low-interest drift. Participants are now watching support zones closely for signs of stabilization.

Drivers Behind The Sell-Off

Rising Crude Oil Adds Cost Pressure

Crude oil prices rose again, reviving concerns over imported inflation and widening trade deficit. Higher energy costs weigh directly on fuel retailers, airlines and logistics firms, and indirectly on consumer discretionary demand. For India, which imports more than 80% of its oil needs, sustained crude gains can erode earnings visibility for rate-sensitive sectors.

IT Sector Leads The Decline

Information technology stocks underperformed the broader market, leading the Sensex lower. Weakness was driven by renewed worries over global demand slowdown and margin pressures from wage inflation and currency volatility. Large-cap IT names bore the brunt of profit booking after recent outperformance.

Technical Context And Investor Implications

Four consecutive down days increase psychological resistance for bulls and invite defensive repositioning. Investors with short-term exposure are reassessing risk, while long-term buyers look for entry points near key support. The 417-point drop narrows recent gains and tests momentum built over the prior upcycle.

Portfolio managers are emphasizing quality names with stable cash flows and limited oil exposure. Sector rotation is likely to favor domestic consumption and capital goods over export-linked IT and oil-sensitive industries. Cash management and selective addition remain prudent until trend clarity returns.
