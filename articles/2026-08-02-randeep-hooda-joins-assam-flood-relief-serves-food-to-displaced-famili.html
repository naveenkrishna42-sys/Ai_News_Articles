Randeep Hooda joins Assam flood relief, serves food to displaced families

📅 2026-08-02 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Randeep Hooda participated in flood relief in Assam's Sivasagar district.
  • He served food to displaced families at a langar and called it a duty.
  • At least 82 people have died due to the floods in Assam.
  • He appealed to the public for support for flood-hit families.

Actor on ground in Sivasagar

Bollywood actor Randeep Hooda has joined relief operations in flood-hit Assam, personally serving meals to displaced families at a langar in Sivasagar district. The actor, known for his roles in films like Sarbjit and Kedarnath, was seen in the affected region on Saturday, drawing attention to the ongoing crisis in the northeastern state.

Hooda, who has been vocal about social causes in the past, shared images and updates from the ground, urging people across the country to extend support. "It is our duty," he said, as he helped distribute food to those forced out of their homes by the deluge.

82 deaths, thousands displaced

The floods in Assam have claimed at least 82 lives so far, according to official reports. Several districts remain submerged, with thousands of families taking shelter in relief camps and makeshift arrangements. Sivasagar, where Hooda carried out relief work, is among the worst-affected areas.

The situation has overwhelmed local administration and volunteer groups, who have been working round the clock to provide essentials such as food, drinking water and medical aid. The arrival of a public figure like Hooda has brought additional attention to the scale of the disaster.

Appeal for support

In his interactions, the actor emphasised the need for sustained help, not just immediate relief. "The road to recovery is long," he noted, as he called upon citizens and organisations to contribute in whatever way they can — be it supplies, funds or volunteer time.

Hooda's presence at the langar, where he personally served food to the displaced, was seen as a gesture of solidarity. Local volunteers and officials appreciated the actor's hands-on approach, which went beyond a mere visit.

Celebrity involvement in disaster relief

Several Bollywood personalities have stepped forward during natural disasters in India, using their reach to mobilise support. Hooda's effort in Assam follows a similar pattern seen during floods in Kerala, Cyclone Fani in Odisha and the COVID-19 pandemic, when actors contributed both on ground and through fundraising.

However, relief workers point out that sustained attention is crucial. As floodwaters recede, the focus often shifts, leaving affected families to deal with damaged homes, lost livelihoods and health risks. Hooda's appeal aims to counter this trend, at least for Assam.

What happens next

With the monsoon still active, more rainfall is expected in the region, which could worsen the situation. Relief teams remain on high alert, and Hooda has indicated he will continue to highlight the needs of the affected communities in the coming days.

Efforts are also underway to assess the full extent of damage once waters recede fully. For now, the immediate priority is ensuring that every displaced family has access to food, shelter and medical care. The actor's call to action may help keep the spotlight on Assam when it matters most.
