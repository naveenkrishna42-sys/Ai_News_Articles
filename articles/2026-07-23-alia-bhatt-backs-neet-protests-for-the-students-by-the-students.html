Alia Bhatt backs NEET protests: 'For the students, by the students'

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Alia Bhatt publicly supports student protests over the NEET paper leak.
  • Several Bollywood celebrities, including Varun Dhawan and Kareena Kapoor Khan, have voiced solidarity.
  • Kusha Kapila noted that movements don't have to be perfect to be meaningful.

Alia Bhatt joins chorus of support

Bollywood actor Alia Bhatt has publicly backed the ongoing student protests against the NEET paper leak. Taking to social media, she described the movement as one that is 'for the students, by the students'.

Her statement comes as a growing number of film industry figures express solidarity with young aspirants demanding accountability. The protests have spread across several cities, with students alleging that a leak in the medical entrance exam has compromised their futures.

Industry voices grow louder

Alia Bhatt is not alone. Twinkle Khanna, Kareena Kapoor Khan, Varun Dhawan, and Sara Ali Khan have also come out in support. The Times of India quoted them as saying they are 'proud to be in a country where young people have the courage to dissent'.

Earlier, actors Salman Khan and R. Madhavan had also backed the protests, with NDTV noting that the movement has gained significant traction among public figures. The support has been framed not as a political stance but as a moral one, centred on fairness and merit.

Kusha Kapila on imperfect movements

Digital creator and actor Kusha Kapila added a nuanced perspective in The Indian Express. She argued that 'movements don't have to be perfect' to be valid or effective. Her comment resonated with many who see the protests as a raw, organic expression of student anger rather than a polished campaign.

The Bollywood amplification of the protest, now being called the CJP (Common Joint Protest) by some outlets, reflects a broader cultural shift. Celebrities are increasingly using their platforms to comment on systemic issues, even when those issues are not directly tied to the entertainment industry.

What the protests are about

The NEET paper leak allegations have sparked nationwide outrage. Students claim that the leak, which reportedly occurred before the exam date, gave unfair advantages to some candidates. The controversy has led to calls for a re-examination and a thorough investigation.

While the government has formed a committee to look into the matter, protesters remain unsatisfied. They argue that without concrete action, the integrity of the entire medical entrance process remains in doubt. The celebrity endorsements have given the movement greater visibility, though critics question whether such support translates into real change.

Officials have not yet confirmed the full extent of the leak or named those responsible. The investigation is ongoing.

What to watch next

With more actors likely to speak out, the pressure on authorities to respond transparently is increasing. The coming weeks will show whether this celebrity-backed movement can force a policy shift or a re-examination schedule from the examination board.
