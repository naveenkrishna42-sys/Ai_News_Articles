Bitcoin, Ethereum Rise as Investor Risk Appetite Returns on July 21

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin and Ethereum posted gains on July 21, 2026, as risk appetite returned to markets.
  • The rally comes amid easing macroeconomic concerns and increased institutional interest.
  • Analysts caution that volatility remains high and the rally may be tested by upcoming economic data.

Bitcoin and Ethereum led a broader crypto market rally on Tuesday, July 21, 2026, as investors appeared ready to take on more risk. The uptick reversed some of the losses seen in recent weeks.

Bitcoin and Ethereum Lead the Charge

Bitcoin climbed past the $68,000 mark during Asian trading hours, adding nearly 4% in 24 hours. Ethereum followed closely, rising above $3,500 for the first time in a fortnight.

The gains were broad-based. Most top-20 cryptocurrencies by market capitalisation traded in the green. Solana, Cardano, and Polygon each rose between 3% and 6%.

What's Driving the Rally

Market participants pointed to a shift in sentiment. Fears of aggressive central bank tightening have eased after recent data showed inflation cooling in major economies. That has encouraged flows into risk assets, including crypto.

Institutional activity also provided a boost. Data from CoinShares showed digital asset investment products recorded net inflows of $245 million last week, the highest in over a month. Bitcoin products accounted for the bulk of those inflows.

Traders also cited a relatively calm regulatory environment in the United States and Europe. No major enforcement actions or policy surprises have emerged in recent days.

Options Markets Signal Cautious Optimism

Derivatives data suggests traders are positioning for further upside but without excessive leverage. The put-call ratio on Bitcoin options has fallen, indicating more bullish bets. However, open interest remains below levels seen in early 2026, suggesting the rally is still tentative.

Funding rates on perpetual futures have turned positive but are not yet at levels that typically signal overheating. That leaves room for additional gains, analysts said.

Caution Remains

Not everyone is convinced the rally will hold. Some analysts warn that the move is driven by short-covering and algorithmic trading rather than genuine spot demand. Volume on major exchanges has not picked up significantly.

Macroeconomic risks have not disappeared. The US Federal Reserve is still expected to hold rates steady at its next meeting, and any hawkish surprise could reverse the risk-on mood.

Regulatory uncertainty in India also looms. The government has not yet clarified its stance on crypto taxation or a potential ban on private digital assets. That has kept many domestic retail investors on the sidelines.

The coming days will test whether this rally has legs. Key US economic data, including GDP and jobless claims, are due later this week. A strong print could reignite inflation fears. For now, crypto markets are enjoying a moment of calm. Whether it lasts depends on how much risk investors are truly willing to take.
