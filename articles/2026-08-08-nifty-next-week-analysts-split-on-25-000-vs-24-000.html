Nifty next week: Analysts split on 25,000 vs 24,000

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Analysts see Nifty rangebound between 24,000 and 25,000, with a breakout likely next week.
  • Global cues, FII flows, and domestic macro data will be crucial triggers.
  • Support is seen at 24,000-24,200, while resistance is at 24,800-25,000.

Analysts split on Nifty's next move

As the weekly derivative expiry fades and the market digests a fresh set of domestic and global cues, the question on every trader's mind is simple: will the Nifty scale 25,000 or slip below 24,000? Analysts are divided, and the next five sessions could set the tone for the rest of August.

Technical charts suggest the index is in a tight consolidation phase. After a volatile week, the Nifty has been oscillating in a 400-point band, with neither bulls nor bears gaining decisive ground. The lack of a clear trigger has kept participants on edge.

Key levels to watch

On the upside, 24,800-25,000 remains a stiff resistance zone. A sustained close above 24,800 could open the doors for a quick move towards the psychological 25,000 mark. However, analysts caution that the index has failed at this level twice in the past month.

On the downside, 24,200-24,000 is the critical support. A break below 24,000 could trigger a sharp sell-off, dragging the index towards 23,700-23,500. The options data also points to strong put writing at 24,000, which may act as a floor in the near term.

What's driving the uncertainty?

Global cues remain mixed. The US Federal Reserve's stance on interest rates, crude oil prices, and the dollar index are all influencing sentiment. Domestic institutional flows have been robust, but foreign investors have turned cautious, and their activity in the coming days will be crucial.

On the macro front, traders will watch out for inflation data and any surprise from the government's borrowing programme. A spike in bond yields could pressure equities, especially rate-sensitive sectors like banking and realty.

What should traders do?

Market veterans suggest staying nimble. For positional traders, a close above 24,800 on a weekly basis would be a buy signal, with a stop-loss at 24,400. On the flip side, a decisive break below 24,000 could be used to initiate shorts, targeting 23,700.

Options traders are likely to focus on the 24,500 strike, which has seen heavy activity. Volatility, as measured by India VIX, has inched up, indicating that a sharp move is expected sooner rather than later.

The road ahead

The coming week is packed with events. Apart from the monthly expiry, the market will react to any fresh foreign fund flows and the progress of the monsoon, which remains a key driver for rural demand. The earnings season is largely over, so the focus will shift to macro data and global headlines.

Analysts remain optimistic about the medium-term structure but warn that a break on either side could lead to a directional move of 500-700 points. For now, the index is at a crossroads, and the next few sessions will likely decide its fate for the month.

As always, a disciplined approach with defined risk is the best strategy in such uncertain times. The market rewards patience, and those who wait for a clear breakout may find better risk-reward opportunities.
