Lava Virat V1 Pro 5G: Budget 5G smartphone with 6,000 mAh battery

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Lava Virat V1 Pro 5G launched in India with 120Hz display, 6,000 mAh battery, and low price.
  • The smartphone features a 50MP primary camera and runs on a bloatware-free UI.
  • Lava aims to compete in the budget 5G smartphone segment with this launch.

Budget 5G smartphone launched in India

Lava, a leading Indian smartphone brand, has launched the Virat V1 Pro 5G in India. The smartphone is priced competitively and aims to cater to the growing demand for budget 5G devices. The Virat V1 Pro 5G features a 120Hz display, a 6,000 mAh battery, and a 50MP primary camera. The device runs on a bloatware-free UI, which is a significant selling point for users looking for a clean and efficient user experience.

Key Specifications and Features

The Lava Virat V1 Pro 5G comes with a 6.78-inch Full HD+ display with a 120Hz refresh rate, ensuring smooth and responsive visuals. The smartphone is powered by an octa-core processor and offers up to 6GB of RAM and 128GB of internal storage. The device features a 50MP primary camera, an 8MP ultra-wide camera, and a 2MP macro camera for versatile photography options. The smartphone also includes a 16MP front camera for high-quality selfies.

Competitive Pricing and Availability

Lava has priced the Virat V1 Pro 5G competitively, making it an attractive option for budget-conscious consumers. The smartphone is available in two color variants: Glowing Green and Glowing Blue. The device will be available for purchase starting from August 25, 2026, through both offline and online retail channels.

Market Impact and Competition

The launch of the Lava Virat V1 Pro 5G is expected to have a significant impact on the Indian smartphone market. The device aims to compete with other budget 5G smartphones from brands like Samsung, Realme, and Redmi. With its competitive pricing and feature-rich specifications, the Virat V1 Pro 5G is poised to attract a wide range of consumers looking for an affordable 5G smartphone.

Expert Opinion and Analysis

Industry experts have praised Lava for its strategic move into the budget 5G smartphone segment. The company's decision to launch a bloatware-free UI and offer a 6,000 mAh battery in this price range is a bold move that could potentially disrupt the market. However, the success of the Virat V1 Pro 5G will ultimately depend on its performance, build quality, and customer reviews.
