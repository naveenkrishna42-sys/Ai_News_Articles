US imposes 50% tariffs on $20 bn Canadian goods

The United States has imposed 50% tariffs on $20 billion worth of Canadian products, including aluminum, steel, and dairy products. This move comes after talks between the US and Canada collapsed, with the US rejecting Canada's latest offer.

Canada says it will retaliate

Canada has announced that it will retaliate against the US tariffs, but has not yet specified what products will be targeted. Prime Minister Justin Carney has called Trump's tariffs a 'miscalculation' and has warned that the trade war could have serious economic consequences for both countries.

Trade talks collapse after US rejects Canada's offer

Trade talks between the US and Canada collapsed after the US rejected Canada's latest offer, which included increased access to the US market for Canadian products. The US has accused Canada of not doing enough to protect American jobs and has threatened to impose further tariffs if Canada does not agree to its demands.

Carney stands up to Trump in trade war despite the risks

Prime Minister Carney has been defiant in his response to Trump's tariffs, calling them a 'miscalculation' and warning that the trade war could have serious economic consequences. However, Carney has also acknowledged the risks of escalating tensions between the two countries.

What happens next?

The next few weeks will be crucial in determining the outcome of the trade war between the US and Canada. Both countries will be closely monitoring each other's moves and will be looking for opportunities to negotiate a resolution. In the meantime, businesses on both sides of the border will be closely watching the situation and preparing for the potential economic impact of the tariffs.
