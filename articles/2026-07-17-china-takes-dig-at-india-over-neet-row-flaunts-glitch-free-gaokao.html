China Takes Dig at India Over NEET Row, Flaunts Glitch-Free Gaokao

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • Chinese embassy posts on X comparing NEET chaos with China's Gaokao, calling it 'efficient and fair'.
  • Gaokao, China's college entrance exam, concluded without reported glitches this year.
  • Opposition parties in India demand a thorough investigation into the NEET paper leak.
  • Government says it has taken strict action and is working to prevent future incidents.

The Chinese embassy in India has taken a swipe at New Delhi over the ongoing controversy surrounding the NEET exam, posting on social media that China's Gaokao — its own college entrance test — concluded without any glitches this year.

The post on X, formerly Twitter, came as India grapples with allegations of a paper leak in the NEET-UG exam, which has sparked massive protests and a political firestorm. The embassy's message was simple: China's Gaokao is "efficient and fair."

Gaokao: China's High-Stakes Exam

The Gaokao, China's national college entrance exam, is widely considered one of the toughest and most consequential exams in the world. Held over two days, it determines admission to the country's top universities.

This year, the exam was conducted without any reported irregularities. The Chinese embassy's post highlighted this contrast, implying that while India struggles with exam security, China has its system under control.

The post did not directly mention the NEET row but was widely interpreted as a dig at India's handling of the situation.

NEET Row: Protests and Political Fallout

In India, the NEET paper leak has led to widespread protests by students and opposition parties. Thousands of students have taken to the streets, demanding a fresh exam and a thorough investigation into the leak.

The opposition has accused the government of failing to protect the integrity of the exam. Leaders from the Congress party and other groups have called for the resignation of the Education Minister.

The government has responded by stating that it has taken strict action against those responsible and is working to prevent future incidents. However, critics argue that the measures are not enough.

What Happened in the NEET Paper Leak?

Details of the leak remain murky. Officials have not yet confirmed how the question paper was accessed or who was involved. Several arrests have been made, but the full extent of the breach is still under investigation.

The National Testing Agency, which conducts NEET, has faced intense scrutiny over its security protocols. The agency has maintained that it is cooperating with the investigation.

The controversy has also raised broader questions about the fairness of high-stakes exams in India, where a single test can determine a student's future career.

Political Reactions and Next Steps

Political parties have seized on the issue, with the ruling BJP facing sharp criticism. The opposition has demanded a joint parliamentary committee probe into the matter.

The government has so far resisted this demand, insisting that the existing investigation is sufficient. The Supreme Court is also hearing petitions related to the leak.

The Chinese embassy's post has added a new dimension to the debate, with some calling it an unnecessary provocation and others using it to highlight India's systemic failures.

The immediate focus remains on the students, many of whom are demanding a re-exam. The government is expected to announce its decision on this in the coming days.

For now, the NEET row shows no signs of abating, and the political battle over exam integrity is likely to continue.
