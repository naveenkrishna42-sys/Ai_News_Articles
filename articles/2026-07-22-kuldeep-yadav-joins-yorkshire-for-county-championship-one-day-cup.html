Kuldeep Yadav Joins Yorkshire for County Championship, One-Day Cup

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kuldeep Yadav will play for Yorkshire in the County Championship and One-Day Cup.
  • This is his first county stint in England.
  • The move comes after he was not selected for India's ODI series against England.

Indian left-arm wristspinner Kuldeep Yadav has signed a deal with Yorkshire County Cricket Club, marking his first stint in English county cricket. The 28-year-old will feature in both the County Championship and the One-Day Cup.

The move comes after a period of limited game time for Kuldeep in the Indian setup. He was notably overlooked for the recent ODI series against England, prompting speculation about his immediate international future.

Yorkshire's Spin Need

Yorkshire have been on the lookout for a frontline spinner to add variety to their attack. The club's director of cricket, Darren Gough, confirmed the signing, expressing confidence in Kuldeep's ability to make an impact across formats.

"Kuldeep is a world-class talent with a proven track record in international cricket. His wristspin gives us a different dimension, especially in the longer format where we need wickets in the middle overs," Gough said in a statement.

County Championship Focus

Kuldeep is expected to be available for a chunk of the County Championship season, which runs through the summer. The competition's red-ball format suits his style—he relies on flight, drift, and sharp turn to outfox batters.

His inclusion could be a boost for Yorkshire's promotion push in Division Two. The side have struggled for consistency this season, with their spin department lacking bite in recent matches.

One-Day Cup Role

Kuldeep will also turn out for Yorkshire in the One-Day Cup, the domestic 50-over competition. His ability to pick wickets in the middle overs makes him a valuable asset in limited-overs cricket, even though he has not played an ODI since late 2025.

His last appearance for India was in a T20I against Australia earlier this year, where he returned figures of 1 for 32. Since then, he has been working on his fitness and variations at the National Cricket Academy in Bengaluru.

What This Means for Kuldeep

The county stint offers Kuldeep a chance to regain form and rhythm away from the intense scrutiny of the Indian domestic circuit. Regular game time in English conditions could sharpen his skills ahead of potential international recalls.

England's pitches often assist spin, especially as the season progresses, and Kuldeep will be eager to make a strong impression. His success in county cricket could also open doors for other Indian spinners looking for overseas exposure.

Yorkshire fans will be watching closely. The last Indian spinner to represent the club was Murali Kartik in 2007, who took 35 wickets in 11 first-class matches.

Kuldeep is expected to join the Yorkshire squad in early August, subject to visa clearance and NOC from the Board of Control for Cricket in India. The club has not specified a timeline for his debut.
