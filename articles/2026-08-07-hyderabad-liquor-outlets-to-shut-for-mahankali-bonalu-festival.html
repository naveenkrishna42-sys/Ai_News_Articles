Hyderabad liquor outlets to shut for Mahankali Bonalu festival

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • All liquor shops and bars in Hyderabad will remain closed on August 14 for Mahankali Bonalu
  • The decision aims to maintain law and order during the festival procession
  • Police will deploy additional forces and make traffic diversions for the event

Dry day declared for festival

The Hyderabad police have announced that all liquor outlets across the city will remain shut on August 14 in view of the Mahankali Bonalu festival. The move is part of the administration's efforts to ensure peaceful celebrations and avoid any untoward incidents during the large public gatherings.

Officials said the order applies to all licensed wine shops, bars, and pubs operating within the city limits. The closure will be in effect for the entire day, with strict instructions to owners to comply. Violations will invite penalties under the relevant excise and police regulations.

Bonalu fervour grips the city

Bonalu is a traditional festival dedicated to Goddess Mahankali, celebrated with great enthusiasm in Hyderabad and neighbouring regions. Thousands of devotees participate in processions, offering prayers and carrying pots decorated with neem leaves and turmeric. The festival is marked by rhythmic drum beats and colourful attire, drawing crowds to temples and street corners.

This year, the Mahankali temple in Secunderabad is expected to witness a heavy turnout. The police have been coordinating with temple authorities and local volunteers to manage the flow of devotees smoothly.

Security and traffic arrangements

In addition to the liquor ban, the police have chalked out a comprehensive security plan. Additional personnel will be deployed along the procession routes and at sensitive points. Senior officers will monitor the situation through CCTV cameras and drone surveillance.

Traffic diversions will be put in place from early morning, and commuters have been advised to plan their journeys accordingly. Parking restrictions will also be imposed near major temples and gathering spots. The police have urged the public to cooperate and follow the instructions of officials on duty.

Previous precedent and public response

The practice of closing liquor outlets during major festivals is not new in the state. Similar measures have been implemented in the past for Bonalu and other religious events to maintain decorum. While the decision has generally been accepted by the public, some business owners have expressed concerns over the loss of revenue.

However, the police maintain that the move is essential for public safety. They have also warned against the sale of illicit liquor during the period and asked citizens to report any suspicious activity.

What to expect next

As the festival approaches, more detailed guidelines regarding the procession timings and route maps are expected to be released. The police are likely to issue a public advisory with helpline numbers and emergency contacts. Residents are advised to stay updated through official channels.

The closure order is applicable for one day, and normal business hours will resume the following day. All eyes will be on the smooth conduct of the festivities, with the administration leaving no stone unturned to ensure a safe and joyous Bonalu.
