Gandhinagar Crane Collapse: Rescue Underway at Kudasan Site

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Team 📅 2026-09-09 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • A crane collision at an under-construction site in Kudasan, Gandhinagar, triggered a major structural slab collapse.
  • Early emergency response teams have successfully rescued multiple workers, though over a dozen remain trapped.
  • State and local disaster management units have deployed heavy machinery and canine squads to fast-track rescue efforts.

Emergency Response Deployed Following Kudasan Structural Failure

A major industrial accident occurred in Gandhinagar when a heavy-duty crane impacted an under-construction building in the Kudasan locality. The collision triggered the sudden collapse of a massive iron and concrete ceiling slab, plunging the active job site into disarray. Local authorities confirmed that a multi-agency rescue operation is currently underway to locate and secure workers trapped beneath the heavy debris.

Initial reports from the site indicate that at least 15 individuals were unaccounted for immediately following the incident. Emergency responders, including the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) and local fire services, were dispatched rapidly. Specialized heavy equipment and debris-clearing vehicles are being utilized to lift massive concrete slabs without jeopardizing the structural integrity of the remaining framework.

Key Real-World Highlights and Site Safety Concerns

Accidents of this magnitude underscore the critical importance of rigorous safety protocols and machinery operation clearances on high-rise and commercial construction sites. Investigative teams are expected to review crane operator visibility, load limits, and site safety compliance.

  • Rapid Mobilization: Local police and emergency services established a secure perimeter within minutes of the accident to facilitate ambulance access.
  • Complex Extrication: Rescuers face significant hurdles dealing with tangled rebar and unstable concrete slabs overhead.
  • Regulatory Scrutiny: District authorities have ordered a temporary halt on site operations pending a full safety audit and structural inspection.
  • Medical Triage: Mobile medical units are stationed on-site to provide immediate trauma care to rescued workers before hospital transport.

Accountability and Next Steps for Regulators

District administration officials have stated that strict legal action will be pursued if contractor negligence or safety standard violations are identified during the inquiry. As the rescue operations continue into the night, the primary objective remains the safe extraction of all remaining trapped personnel. Further updates regarding structural stability assessments will be released by municipal authorities once the immediate rescue phase concludes.
