iQOO Z11 Display Confirmed: 6.83-Inch Curved AMOLED, Slim Bezels

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iQOO Z11 will feature a 6.83-inch curved AMOLED display with a slim frame
  • India launch confirmed for August 20
  • Device to be powered by MediaTek Dimensity 7500 Turbo chipset
  • Leaked marketing image suggests a Pixel-like camera bar design

Display Details Officially Teased

iQOO has officially confirmed the display specifications for its upcoming Z11 smartphone, revealing a 6.83-inch curved AMOLED panel housed within an unusually slim frame. The company shared the details through its official channels ahead of the device's India launch, which is scheduled for August 20.

The curved screen is expected to offer an immersive viewing experience, though the company has not yet disclosed the refresh rate or peak brightness figures. Industry watchers anticipate a 120Hz panel, but iQOO has remained tight-lipped on those specifics.

Slim Frame Design

In its teaser, iQOO highlighted the Z11's narrow bezels and slim profile, claiming it will be one of the most compact-feeling devices in its segment despite the large display. The company has not provided exact dimensions, but early renders suggest a premium build with a glass sandwich design.

The curved edges are expected to enhance grip and reduce visual bulk, a trend that has gained traction among mid-range and flagship devices in India over the past year.

Chipset and Performance Leaks

Beyond the display, iQOO has confirmed that the Z11 will be powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 7500 Turbo chipset. This processor is designed for upper-mid-range performance, offering a balance between power efficiency and gaming capability.

The chipset details were teased alongside colourway options, though the company has not yet revealed RAM or storage configurations. Given the Z-series' history, buyers can expect multiple variants, likely starting at 8GB RAM and 128GB storage.

Design Rumours and Leaks

A leaked marketing image circulating online suggests the Z11 will feature a Pixel-like camera bar on the rear, a departure from iQOO's earlier square or rectangular camera modules. The image, reported by GSMArena, shows a horizontal camera strip that houses multiple sensors, giving the phone a distinct look.

This design shift could help the Z11 stand out in a crowded market where most budget and mid-range devices look alike. However, iQOO has not officially commented on the leaked image, and the final design may vary.

India Launch and Market Context

The Z11's India launch on August 20 comes at a time when iQOO is also dealing with rumours about the cancellation of its iQOO 16 flagship. The company's CEO recently teased the Z11 amid these reports, suggesting that the brand is focusing its efforts on the Z-series to maintain momentum in the competitive Indian smartphone market.

Pricing has not been announced, but analysts expect the Z11 to be positioned between ₹25,000 and ₹30,000, directly competing with the likes of the OnePlus Nord series and the Xiaomi Mi 11 Lite lineup.

The device is also expected to include a large battery, fast charging, and a capable camera setup, though official specifications for those components are still pending.

What to Watch Next

As the August 20 launch approaches, iQOO is likely to release more teasers revealing camera details, battery capacity, and pricing. The company's decision to confirm the display and chipset early suggests it is confident in the Z11's appeal to performance-focused buyers.

Keep an eye on official iQOO India channels for the final specifications and availability details. The Z11 could be a key player in the upcoming festive season sales, and its success may shape iQOO's strategy for the rest of 2026.
