H1N1 cases surge in Delhi; health ministry lists symptoms

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Health Original source ↗
Key points
  • Delhi records 1,777 H1N1 cases this year
  • Health ministry lists symptoms to watch for H1N1
  • JP Nadda reviews treatment facilities amid influenza spikes

Delhi reports record H1N1 cases

Delhi has logged more than 1,700 cases of H1N1, also known as swine flu, this year. The surge in infections comes amid a rise in seasonal influenza cases, officials said. Health authorities are closely monitoring the situation, with the health ministry listing symptoms to watch for.

Health ministry lists symptoms to monitor

The health ministry has advised citizens to monitor for key symptoms of H1N1, including fever, cough, sore throat, body aches, headache, chills, and fatigue. These symptoms can indicate the presence of the virus, and individuals experiencing them should seek medical attention.

JP Nadda reviews treatment facilities

Delhi Health Minister JP Nadda has reviewed the treatment facilities to ensure they are prepared to handle the influx of H1N1 cases. He has instructed hospitals to stock up on necessary medications and to be ready to provide timely treatment to patients.

Doctors report rising cases

Doctors in Delhi have reported a surge in H1N1 cases, with many patients presenting with symptoms similar to those of seasonal flu. However, they have noted that some patients are experiencing more severe symptoms, which has prompted health authorities to take the situation seriously.

Public health measures in place

Public health measures are in place to prevent the spread of H1N1, including hand hygiene, respiratory etiquette, and vaccination. Health officials are urging the public to stay informed and to take necessary precautions to protect themselves and others from the virus.
