APD's 'Yes To Access' reveals Bengaluru's accessibility gaps

📅 2026-08-19 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗

  • APD's 'Yes To Access' initiative highlights accessibility gaps across Bengaluru.
  • The initiative includes a comprehensive report on accessibility issues in Bengaluru.
  • The report includes recommendations for improving accessibility in Bengaluru.

APD's 'Yes To Access' initiative launched

The Accessibility Partnership of Delhi (APD) has launched 'Yes To Access', a comprehensive initiative to highlight accessibility gaps across Bengaluru. The initiative includes a detailed report on accessibility issues in the city, along with recommendations for improvement.

Comprehensive report on accessibility issues

The report, which is based on extensive research and field studies, identifies several key areas where Bengaluru falls short in terms of accessibility. These include public transportation, pedestrian pathways, public buildings, and digital platforms.

Public transportation

The report highlights the lack of accessibility features in public transportation, such as ramps, elevators, and braille signage. Many buses and trains do not have these features, making it difficult for people with disabilities to travel.

Pedestrian pathways

Pedestrian pathways in Bengaluru are often not accessible to people with disabilities. Many sidewalks lack ramps, curbs are not properly marked, and there is a lack of tactile paving for the visually impaired.

Public buildings

Public buildings in Bengaluru, such as government offices and hospitals, often lack accessibility features. Many buildings do not have ramps, elevators, or accessible toilets, making it difficult for people with disabilities to access these facilities.

Digital platforms

The report also highlights the lack of accessibility features in digital platforms. Many websites and apps are not designed to be accessible to people with disabilities, making it difficult for them to access information and services online.

Recommendations for improvement

The report includes several recommendations for improving accessibility in Bengaluru. These include the implementation of a city-wide accessibility audit, the provision of funding for accessibility improvements, and the development of a city-wide accessibility policy.

What happens next?

The 'Yes To Access' initiative is expected to spark a broader conversation about accessibility in Bengaluru. The report and recommendations will be shared with relevant authorities and stakeholders, who are expected to take appropriate action to address the identified gaps.
