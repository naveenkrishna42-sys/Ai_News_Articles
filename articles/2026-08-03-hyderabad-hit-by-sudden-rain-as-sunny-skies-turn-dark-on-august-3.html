Hyderabad hit by sudden rain as sunny skies turn dark on August 3

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sudden rain lashed parts of Hyderabad on August 3, turning sunny skies dark.
  • The downpour caught residents and commuters off guard, causing traffic snarls.
  • Weather officials have not yet confirmed the duration or intensity of the spell.

Hyderabad residents experienced a dramatic weather shift on Monday, August 3, as sunny skies gave way to dark clouds and a sudden spell of rain lashed parts of the city. The unseasonal downpour, which struck without much warning, caught many commuters and locals off guard.

The change was swift. Morning brightness turned into overcast conditions within hours, and by late morning, several neighbourhoods reported steady rainfall. The suddenness of the weather took many by surprise, with social media quickly filling with posts about the unexpected turn.

Traffic disrupted as rain hits key areas

The rain disrupted traffic in several parts of the city, particularly on busy arterial roads. Commuters faced delays as waterlogging was reported in low-lying stretches. Auto and cab services were in high demand as people sought shelter or tried to reach their destinations.

Local reports suggest that areas such as Begumpet, Ameerpet, and parts of Secunderabad received moderate to heavy showers. However, the exact spread and intensity of the rainfall across the city have not yet been fully confirmed by officials.

Weather officials yet to comment

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has not yet issued an official statement on the sudden spell. Officials have not confirmed whether this was a localised weather event or part of a broader system affecting the region. Residents are advised to stay updated through official channels for any alerts.

This is not the first time Hyderabad has witnessed abrupt weather changes. The city, known for its hot and dry climate, has seen similar sudden showers in recent years, often linked to convective activity or upper-air circulation.

Residents caught in the open

Many residents were caught in the open when the rain began. Office-goers, students, and street vendors had to scramble for cover as the intensity picked up within minutes. Some reported that the rain lasted for a short while, while others said it continued for over an hour.

The sudden change also affected outdoor activities. Parks and open-air markets saw fewer people as the weather turned unwelcoming. In some areas, the rain brought a pleasant drop in temperature, offering relief from the usual August humidity.

What to watch

As the day progresses, the key question is whether more showers are expected in the coming hours. Weather models and local forecasts will be closely watched for updates. For now, residents are advised to carry umbrellas and plan their commutes accordingly, given the unpredictability of the weather.
