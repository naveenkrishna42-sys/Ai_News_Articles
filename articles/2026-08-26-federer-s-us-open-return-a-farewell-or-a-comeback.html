Federer's US Open return: A farewell or a comeback?

📅 2026-08-26 📂 Sports Original source ↗

  • Federer's emotional US Open exhibition marks his return to Flushing Meadows after a 12-year absence.
  • He rules out a Williams-like return to professional tennis, focusing instead on legacy and farewell.
  • The ATP Tour and fans debate whether Federer's return is a farewell or a comeback.

Federer's Emotional Return to US Open

Roger Federer, the Swiss tennis legend, made an emotional return to the US Open on August 25, 2026, playing an exhibition match against a team of former stars including John McEnroe, Andre Agassi, and Andy Roddick. The match, held at Flushing Meadows, marked Federer's first appearance at the tournament since 2014, a 12-year hiatus that has been widely discussed in the tennis world.

Rules Out Williams-Like Return

Despite the emotional return, Federer has ruled out a return to professional tennis, stating, 'No, not at all,' when asked if he would consider a Williams-like return. This statement has sparked debates among fans and experts, with some believing it to be a farewell and others seeing it as a strategic move to focus on his legacy.

Federer's Legacy and Future

Federer's decision to focus on his legacy rather than a return to professional tennis has been met with both praise and criticism. While some appreciate his decision to prioritize his family and other interests, others believe he could have continued to inspire a new generation of players.
