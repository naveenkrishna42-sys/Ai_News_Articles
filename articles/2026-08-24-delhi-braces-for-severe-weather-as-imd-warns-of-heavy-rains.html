Delhi braces for severe weather as IMD warns of heavy rains

📅 2026-08-24 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • IMD issues red and orange alerts for heavy rains in Delhi-NCR
  • Heavy rains cause waterlogging, traffic chaos, and power outages
  • Delhi records maximum temperature of 33.9°C despite cloudy skies

IMD Issues Red Alert for Delhi-NCR Amid Severe Weather

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued red alerts for several districts in Delhi-NCR, warning of extremely heavy rainfall in the next 24 hours. This is the first red alert for the region since the monsoon season began, indicating the severity of the weather conditions. Residents are advised to stay indoors and avoid unnecessary travel.

Heavy Rains Cause Waterlogging and Traffic Chaos

Delhi-NCR has been lashed by heavy rains, leading to waterlogging in low-lying areas and traffic snarls across the city. The Delhi Police have reported numerous accidents due to slippery roads, and emergency services are on high alert. The situation has been exacerbated by the sudden downpour, which caught many off guard.

Temperature and Weather Conditions

Despite the heavy rains, Delhi recorded a maximum temperature of 33.9°C on Monday, which is slightly above normal for this time of the year. The IMD has forecast cloudy skies with light rain for the next few days, indicating that the weather conditions are likely to remain unstable.

Impact on Daily Life and Infrastructure

The heavy rains have disrupted daily life in Delhi-NCR, with schools and offices closed in some areas. Power outages have also been reported, affecting the daily routines of residents. The Delhi Metro and other public transport services have been running at reduced capacity, causing inconvenience to commuters.

Preparedness and Response

The Delhi government has activated its disaster response protocol, with officials urging residents to stay updated through official channels. The National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) and other agencies are on standby to provide assistance if needed. Residents are advised to have emergency supplies on hand and to follow safety guidelines issued by the authorities.

Broader Implications and Long-Term Outlook

The heavy rains in Delhi-NCR highlight the challenges posed by climate change, which is expected to increase the frequency and intensity of extreme weather events. The IMD's red alert underscores the need for robust infrastructure and preparedness measures to mitigate the impact of such events. The event also serves as a reminder of the importance of sustainable urban planning to ensure resilience against climate-related disasters.
