Samsung Galaxy Unpacked July 2026: How to watch Z Fold 8, Z Flip 8 launch live

  • Samsung's Galaxy Unpacked event is scheduled for July 2026.

  • The event can be watched live via Samsung's official website and YouTube channel.

Samsung is gearing up for its next big Galaxy Unpacked event, slated for July 2026. The company is expected to pull the wraps off its latest foldable smartphones, including the Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Galaxy Z Flip 8. Alongside these devices, Samsung will likely showcase upgrades to its Galaxy AI suite and new Flex Titanium technology aimed at making foldable displays more durable.

When and where to watch the event

The Galaxy Unpacked live stream will begin at 10 AM ET on the scheduled date. Samsung will broadcast the event on its official website and YouTube channel. For those in India, the event will air in the evening, making it convenient for viewers to tune in after work hours.

Tech enthusiasts can also follow live coverage on major gadget blogs and news portals. Android Police, GSMArena, and Techgenyz are among the outlets that will provide minute-by-minute updates. Samsung has not yet confirmed the exact date in July, but speculation points to the middle of the month.

What to expect: Z Fold 8 and Z Flip 8

The Galaxy Z Fold 8 is rumored to feature a slimmer hinge and a lighter build. Reports suggest Samsung will introduce Flex Titanium technology to the foldable lineup, a material that promises better resistance to scratches and impacts. The Z Flip 8, on the other hand, may come with a larger cover display and improved camera sensors.

Both devices are expected to run on the latest Qualcomm Snapdragon chipset. Samsung is also likely to increase the battery capacity on the Fold 8 to address one of the key complaints from previous generations. The company has not confirmed any specifications, so details remain in the realm of leaks and speculation.

Galaxy AI and software upgrades

Samsung's Galaxy AI features will likely get a significant update at this Unpacked event. The company has been pushing AI-powered tools for photo editing, translation, and productivity. The new foldables could debut exclusive AI modes that take advantage of the larger folding screens.

One UI, Samsung's custom Android skin, is also expected to receive a version bump. The software may include better multitasking gestures and app continuity between the cover and main display. Samsung has been teasing these upgrades in recent press releases, but full details will only emerge during the live event.

Samsung's challenge in a growing foldable market

Samsung essentially created the foldable smartphone category with the original Galaxy Fold. But competition is heating up. Chinese manufacturers like Huawei, Oppo, and Xiaomi have launched foldables with thinner designs and lower price points. Samsung now faces the task of defending its lead.

The Times of India noted that the company must innovate rapidly to stay ahead. The July Unpacked event is Samsung's chance to prove that its foldables remain the gold standard. Analysts will be watching closely for pricing and availability details, especially in key markets like India where premium phone sales are growing.

The event is also expected to feature new Galaxy Watch models and possibly a new Galaxy Tab. Samsung has not confirmed these products, but leaks from supply chain sources point to a busy launch lineup.

As the date approaches, more concrete information will surface. Samsung typically sends out media invites two weeks before the event. Until then, the tech world waits for what could be the most important Unpacked of the year.
