Weekend Guide: Things to Do in Bengaluru This July

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bangalore Mirror has released a curated list of activities for Bengaluru residents this weekend.
  • Options range from heritage food walks to contemporary art exhibitions and nature trails.
  • The guide emphasises local experiences, including indie music gigs and pop-up markets.
  • Many events are free or low-cost, catering to families, couples, and solo visitors.

Bangalore Mirror has released its latest weekend guide for Bengaluru, offering residents and visitors a curated list of activities to beat the July chill. The guide, published Wednesday, covers everything from heritage food walks to contemporary art exhibitions, all within the city limits.

With the monsoon season in full swing, the recommendations lean heavily on indoor cultural events and covered outdoor spaces. Organisers have planned events across multiple neighbourhoods, from Indiranagar to Basavanagudi.

Food and Heritage Walks

The guide highlights a guided food walk through the city's oldest markets, including KR Market and Russell Market. Participants can sample local delicacies like benne dosa and filter coffee while learning about the area's colonial history.

Another recommended activity is a heritage walk around the Bangalore Palace grounds, which includes a visit to the palace museum. Tickets for the walk are priced at Rs 500 per person, with discounts for students and senior citizens.

Art and Music

Art enthusiasts can visit a group exhibition at the National Gallery of Modern Art titled 'Monsoon Musings,' featuring works by emerging Indian painters. The show runs through Sunday, with free entry for all visitors.

For music lovers, indie bands are performing at several venues across the city. The popular venue Fandom at Gilly's Redefined in Koramangala is hosting a night of original Kannada rock on Saturday.

Nature and Outdoors

Those seeking fresh air can head to the Lalbagh Botanical Garden for a guided nature trail. The garden's lake is currently full, making it an ideal spot for birdwatching. Entry costs Rs 20 for adults.

Another option is the Nandi Hills sunrise trek, which has been rescheduled to a later start time due to cloud cover. The trek is organised by a local adventure group and costs Rs 300 per person, including transport from the city centre.

Pop-Up Markets

Weekend shoppers can explore a pop-up market at the Sheraton Grand Bangalore Hotel in Brigade Gateway. The market features over 50 stalls selling handmade crafts, organic produce, and artisanal food items. It is open from 10 am to 8 pm on both Saturday and Sunday.

Organisers have confirmed that all vendors follow safety and hygiene protocols. Entry is free, but visitors are encouraged to bring reusable bags.

As the monsoon continues, many events may shift indoors or get rescheduled. Check individual event pages on the Bangalore Mirror website for real-time updates before stepping out.
