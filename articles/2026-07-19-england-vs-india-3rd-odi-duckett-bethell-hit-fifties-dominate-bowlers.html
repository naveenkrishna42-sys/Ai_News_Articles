England vs India 3rd ODI: Duckett, Bethell Hit Fifties, Dominate Bowlers at Lord's

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ben Duckett and Jacob Bethell scored aggressive fifties against India in the 3rd ODI at Lord's.
  • The duo put on a record 192-run partnership for England.
  • India's bowlers struggled to contain the run flow, looking clueless.
  • England posted a strong total, putting India under pressure.

England's openers Ben Duckett and Jacob Bethell tore into the Indian bowling attack at Lord's on Sunday, leaving the visitors clueless in the third ODI. The duo smashed quick fifties, putting on a record 192-run stand that has put England firmly in the driver's seat.

India's bowlers had no answers as Duckett and Bethell found the boundary with ease. The pair targeted every bowler, with the short boundaries at Lord's aiding their aggressive approach.

Record Partnership Rocks India

The 192-run partnership is now the highest for England against India in ODIs at Lord's. Duckett, in particular, was severe on anything short, pulling and cutting with authority. Bethell matched him stroke for stroke, driving beautifully through the covers.

India's decision to bowl first backfired as the English openers seized the initiative from the very first over. The pitch offered little assistance to the seamers, and the spinners were treated with similar disdain.

Indian Bowlers Struggle for Breakthrough

Mohammed Shami and Jasprit Bumrah, India's most experienced bowlers, were taken for runs. Shami's length was too short, allowing Duckett to rock back and punch through the off-side. Bumrah, usually so precise, sprayed the ball down the leg side on multiple occasions.

Kuldeep Yadav, brought into the attack to provide control, was met with aggressive intent. Bethell danced down the track to hit him over long-on for a massive six. The spin option failed to stem the flow of runs.

India's fielding also let them down. A sharp chance was put down in the deep, and there were overthrows in the middle overs. The usual energy in the field was missing as England's openers kept piling on the runs.

Duckett and Bethell Make History

The stand between Duckett and Bethell has now entered the record books. They are the first English pair to put on 150 or more for the first wicket against India at Lord's. Their aggressive running between the wickets added to the pressure on India.

Duckett brought up his fifty off just 48 balls, while Bethell reached his milestone in 56 deliveries. Both batsmen showed excellent temperament, mixing caution with aggression at the historic venue.

India's bowling attack, which had been so effective in the first two ODIs, looked completely out of sorts. The lack of a fifth bowler also hurt them, as they had to rely on part-timers to complete the overs.

With England posting a formidable total, India now face a stiff chase. The required run rate is already above six an over, and the pitch is expected to slow down in the second half.

The match is far from over, but England hold all the momentum. India's batting lineup, which has been inconsistent throughout the series, will need to produce something special to chase down this target at Lord's.
