Use ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude as Your Personal Finance Assistant: Exact Prompts

📅 2026-08-13 📂 AI Tips & Tools Original source ↗
Key points
  • Set up a secure context for financial queries
  • Use specific prompts for budgeting, expense analysis, and investment research
  • Cross-check AI advice with official sources before acting

Why AI for Personal Finance?

AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude can help you track spending, build budgets, and even explain complex financial terms. They are not certified advisors, but they can save hours of manual spreadsheet work. The trick is to give them clear, structured prompts.

This guide walks you through three practical tasks: creating a budget, analysing your expenses, and researching investment options. Each step includes a ready-to-use prompt you can copy and paste.

Step 1: Set Up Your AI Session Securely

Before sharing any financial data, take two precautions. First, avoid pasting bank statements or account numbers. Use rounded figures and category totals instead. Second, check your chat history settings — turn off data saving if your tool offers it.

For ChatGPT, click your profile icon and select 'Data controls' to disable chat history. Gemini has a similar toggle under 'Activity and history'. Claude allows you to start a temporary chat that won't be saved.

Once your session is private, you are ready to begin.

Step 2: Build a Monthly Budget with a Precise Prompt

Start with a simple, structured prompt that asks for a budget plan based on your income and fixed costs. The more specific you are, the better the output.

Copy this prompt into any of the three tools:

Prompt for budget creation: "My monthly income after tax is ₹85,000. My fixed expenses are rent ₹25,000, utilities ₹5,000, transport ₹3,000, and loan EMI ₹8,000. I want to save at least ₹15,000 per month. Create a 50/30/20 budget plan and show me how much I can spend on discretionary items. Also suggest three areas where I can cut costs."

Run this in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude separately and compare the suggestions. You will notice each tool gives slightly different advice — that is fine. Use the best ideas from each.

Step 3: Analyse Your Spending Patterns

To understand where your money actually goes, feed a summary of your last month's expenses. Do not share full account details — just category totals.

Prompt for expense analysis: "Here is my spending last month: groceries ₹12,000, dining out ₹8,500, entertainment ₹4,000, shopping ₹6,200, subscriptions ₹2,800, and miscellaneous ₹3,500. My total income was ₹85,000. Identify any red flags, suggest a realistic budget for each category, and tell me which three categories I should reduce first."

This prompt works well with all three tools. Gemini tends to give more visual breakdowns if you ask it to "format the answer as a table". Claude often provides a detailed narrative explanation. ChatGPT usually balances both.

Step 4: Ask for Investment Research (with a Warning)

AI can help you understand mutual funds, stocks, or fixed deposits, but it cannot give personalised financial advice. Use it for general education and comparison.

Prompt for investment research: "Explain the difference between large-cap and mid-cap mutual funds in simple terms. Compare the risk and return profile of each. Also list three questions I should ask a SEBI-registered financial advisor before investing."

This prompt avoids asking for specific buy or sell recommendations, which AI models are not equipped to give reliably. If you want to check a specific fund's performance, ask for "the general criteria to evaluate a mutual fund's past performance" rather than "is Fund X good?".

Always cross-check any facts the AI provides against official sources like SEBI's website or your broker's research reports.

Step 5: Create a Debt Repayment Plan

If you have multiple debts, AI can help you prioritise. Use this prompt:

Prompt for debt payoff: "I have three debts: credit card ₹40,000 at 36% annual interest, personal loan ₹1,20,000 at 14%, and a car loan ₹2,50,000 at 9%. My available monthly payment is ₹25,000. Should I use the avalanche method or the snowball method? Show me a month-by-month plan for the first six months."

The tools will compute the interest savings and give you a sequence. This is one of the most practical uses of AI in personal finance.

When to Use This Technique

This technique works best for budgeting, expense review, and learning about financial concepts. It is not a substitute for a licensed advisor, especially for tax planning or large investments. Use AI to organise your numbers and generate ideas, but always verify with official documents before making any financial decision.
