Elderly Woman Killed as Cop's Son Drunk-Drives Mercedes in Delhi

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 70-year-old woman was killed in a head-on collision in Delhi.
  • The Mercedes was allegedly driven by a Haryana police officer's son, reportedly drunk.
  • A beer bottle was found inside the Mercedes after the crash.
  • The incident occurred late on Friday night; the accused has been detained.

Fatal Crash in Delhi

A 70-year-old woman was killed late Friday night when a Mercedes, allegedly driven by the son of a Haryana police officer, crashed head-on into her WagonR in Delhi. The collision occurred in a residential area, leaving the elderly bystander dead on the spot.

Police have detained the accused, whose identity has not been officially released. The Mercedes was reportedly being driven at high speed when it veered into the wrong lane, smashing into the oncoming WagonR. The impact was severe enough to crumple both vehicles.

Beer Bottle Found at Scene

Investigators recovered a beer bottle from inside the Mercedes, raising immediate suspicion of drunk driving. The accused has been taken for medical examination to confirm alcohol consumption, though test results are pending.

Preliminary reports suggest the driver was intoxicated at the time of the crash. A senior police official said, "We have registered a case and are conducting a thorough probe. The accused is in custody." The family of the deceased has been informed, and post-mortem procedures are underway.

Victim and Family

The victim, a 70-year-old woman, was a resident of the area and was reportedly standing near her car when the collision occurred. Her family members, who were nearby, rushed to the spot but she had already succumbed to her injuries.

Local residents gathered at the scene, expressing outrage over the incident. "She was a grandmother, just standing there. This is murder," one neighbour said. Police have assured swift action, but the accused's family background has added to public anger.

Legal Proceedings

Authorities have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including causing death by negligence and rash driving. Additional charges of drunk driving may be added once medical reports confirm the same.

The accused, a young man in his twenties, has been taken into police custody. His father, a serving Haryana police officer, has not yet issued a public statement. The Mercedes has been seized for forensic examination, and the traffic police are reconstructing the sequence of events.

What Happens Next

The accused will be produced before a magistrate on Saturday, where police are likely to seek custodial remand. The medical report on blood alcohol levels will be crucial in determining the severity of charges. The case has reignited discussions on drunk-driving penalties in Delhi, with activists calling for stricter enforcement. The probe continues, and further arrests are unlikely unless evidence points to other involvement.
