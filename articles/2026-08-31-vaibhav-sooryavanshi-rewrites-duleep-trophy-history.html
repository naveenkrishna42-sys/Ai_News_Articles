Vaibhav Sooryavanshi writes his name in Duleep Trophy history

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, a 15-year-old cricket prodigy, has etched his name in the annals of the Duleep Trophy by becoming the youngest-ever centurion in the tournament's history. His innings of 112 runs not only set a new record but also guided the North Zone to a resounding victory in the semi-final.

North Zone's innings

The North Zone posted a formidable total of 461 runs in their first innings, with Vaibhav Sooryavanshi playing a pivotal role. His innings, laced with 12 boundaries and 2 sixes, was a testament to his maturity beyond his years. The North Zone declared their innings at 461/6, setting a target of 446 runs for the Central Zone.

Central Zone's collapse

The Central Zone's innings was a spectacle of bowling excellence. Abhijit Sarkar and Mukesh Kumar, the bowling duo, bowled with precision and control. Sarkar's 3/58 and Kumar's 4/67 were the highlights of the innings. The Central Zone was all out for 266 in 68 overs, conceding a lead of 195 runs.

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi's record-breaking innings

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi's innings was not just a personal achievement but also a record-breaker. He became the youngest-ever centurion in the Duleep Trophy, surpassing the previous record held by a player who was 17 years old. His innings was a blend of aggression and composure, which is a hallmark of a true leader.

Impact of the match

The match had a significant impact on the Duleep Trophy. It not only set a new record but also showcased the talent that India has to offer. The match was a testament to the fact that age is just a number in cricket. Vaibhav Sooryavanshi's innings will be remembered for years to come and will inspire a new generation of cricketers.
