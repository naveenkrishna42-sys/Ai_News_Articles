Maitreyi Ramakrishnan Returns to Netflix in Bollywood Dance Film 'Best of the Best'

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Maitreyi Ramakrishnan returns to Netflix in a new original film titled 'Best of the Best'.
  • The movie is described as a Bollywood dance competition film.
  • A first-look image from the project has been released.
  • Ramakrishnan rose to fame playing Devi Vishwakumar in the comedy series 'Never Have I Ever'.

Maitreyi Ramakrishnan is heading back to Netflix. The 'Never Have I Ever' star has signed on for a new original film titled 'Best of the Best'. The project is described as a Bollywood dance competition movie.

The streamer has released a first-look image from the film. It shows Ramakrishnan in a vibrant dance costume, surrounded by other dancers in a brightly lit studio. Details about her character remain under wraps, but the image suggests she will be at the centre of the competitive dance world.

From Devi to the Dance Floor

Ramakrishnan became a household name playing Devi Vishwakumar in Mindy Kaling and Lang Fisher's coming-of-age comedy 'Never Have I Ever'. The series ran for four seasons on Netflix and earned Ramakrishnan widespread critical praise. She was celebrated for bringing depth and humour to a rare lead role for a Tamil-Canadian actress.

'Best of the Best' marks her first major lead role in a feature film since the show ended. It also keeps her partnership with Netflix alive. The streaming platform has bet heavily on star-driven original movies that tap into global pop culture, and this project appears to fit that strategy.

Bollywood Meets Netflix's Global Ambitions

The film leans into the spectacle of Bollywood-style dance numbers. While not a traditional Hindi-language production, 'Best of the Best' will feature high-energy choreography and music inspired by Indian cinema. Netflix has previously found success with similar cross-cultural projects, including the wedding comedy 'Love Hard' and the action film 'The Gray Man', which featured Bollywood elements.

No director or full cast list has been announced yet. The production is still in early stages. Given Ramakrishnan's popularity and Netflix's track record with dance films, the project is already generating buzz among fans of the genre and of the actress.

What Comes Next

Filming is expected to begin later this year. An official release date has not been set. For now, the first look offers a glimpse of what promises to be a colourful and energetic addition to Netflix's growing library of original movies.
