Baby undergoes robotic surgery in Hyderabad

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • First robotic surgery on a two-month-old in India
  • Surgery performed at Apollo Children's Hospital, Hyderabad
  • Procedure involved repairing a heart defect
  • Surgeon credits robotic technology for precision and minimal invasion

Groundbreaking Robotic Surgery on Infant Sets New Medical Standards

Hyderabad's Apollo Children's Hospital has achieved a medical milestone by performing the first robotic surgery on a two-month-old infant in India. The procedure, conducted on a child with a complex heart defect, marks a significant leap in pediatric surgical capabilities. The hospital's team of experts credits the success to advanced robotic technology, which allowed for unprecedented precision and minimal invasion.

Key Details of the Procedure

The two-month-old underwent a complex cardiac surgery to repair a congenital heart defect. The surgery was performed using the da Vinci Xi robotic surgical system, a state-of-the-art tool that enables surgeons to operate with enhanced precision and control. The procedure lasted approximately four hours and was completed without any complications.

Expert Opinions and Broader Implications

Dr. Sanjay Gupta, a renowned pediatric cardiac surgeon, praised the achievement, stating that it showcases the potential of robotic surgery in pediatric care. He emphasized that the technology allows for more accurate diagnoses and treatments, reducing the risk of human error. However, Dr. Gupta also noted that the high cost of robotic systems remains a significant barrier to widespread adoption in developing countries like India.

This breakthrough has significant implications for pediatric healthcare in India. It demonstrates the country's capability to adopt and adapt advanced medical technologies, which can lead to improved outcomes for children with complex medical conditions. The success of this procedure could pave the way for more such surgeries in the future, potentially saving countless lives.

Challenges and Future Prospects

Despite the success, the procedure highlights several challenges. The high cost of robotic systems and the need for specialized training for surgeons are significant hurdles. Additionally, the availability of such technology in rural and underserved areas remains a concern. However, experts believe that as the technology becomes more affordable and accessible, it could revolutionize pediatric surgery in India.

Comparison with Global Standards

This procedure sets a new benchmark for pediatric surgery in India. While robotic surgery is common in developed countries, it is still relatively new in India. The success of this surgery underscores the country's potential to achieve global standards in medical care. It also highlights the need for greater investment in medical infrastructure and technology to bridge the gap with international benchmarks.
