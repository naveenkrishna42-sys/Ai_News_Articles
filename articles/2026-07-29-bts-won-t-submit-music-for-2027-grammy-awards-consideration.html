BTS Won’t Submit Music for 2027 Grammy Awards Consideration

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • BTS has decided not to submit any music for the 2027 Grammy Awards.
  • The decision comes shortly after the Recording Academy introduced a 'Best Asian Pop Music' category.
  • Reports indicate the move is seen as a protest against the new category's framing.

K-Pop Titans Step Back From Grammys

BTS will not submit any music for consideration at the 2027 Grammy Awards. The decision was confirmed by multiple reports on Wednesday, ending weeks of speculation within the music industry.

The announcement arrives just a month after the Recording Academy introduced a new category titled 'Best Asian Pop Music'. The category was widely seen as an attempt to formally recognise the growing global influence of K-pop and other Asian pop genres.

However, BTS' decision signals a clear rejection of that move. Sources close to the group indicated that the members viewed the category as a form of segregation rather than inclusion.

A Protest Against Segregation

According to reports from Variety and BBC, BTS' withdrawal is being interpreted as a protest. The group is said to believe that the new category boxes Asian artists into a separate lane, rather than allowing them to compete in the main categories like Pop, Album of the Year, or Record of the Year.

This is not the first time BTS has expressed discomfort with how their music is categorised. In past years, the group had submitted their work for general field categories. They earned a nomination for Best Pop Duo/Group Performance in 2021 for 'Dynamite', but have never won a Grammy despite multiple nominations and record-breaking commercial success.

The Telegraph India reported that the group's stance is a direct reaction to the new category's introduction, calling it a 'protest' against the framing of Asian pop music as a niche subgenre.

Industry Reactions and Fallout

The Recording Academy has not yet issued an official statement regarding BTS' decision. However, industry insiders suggest the move could prompt other major K-pop acts to reconsider their participation.

BTS remains one of the best-selling musical acts in the world. Their decision to withhold submissions removes a major commercial draw from the Grammy nominations process. The move also places the Academy under scrutiny for how it handles non-English and non-Western music.

The Korea JoongAng Daily noted that the group's action is unprecedented in scale. While individual artists have boycotted award shows before, a group of BTS' stature stepping away from the Grammys entirely sends a strong message to the global music establishment.

What This Means for the 2027 Awards

The 2027 Grammy Awards will now proceed without the possibility of a BTS nomination in any category, including the new Asian pop slot. This could impact viewership and global engagement with the ceremony, especially in Asian markets where BTS commands a massive fanbase.

Fans have rallied behind the group's decision on social media, with hashtags trending in support of the boycott. However, some critics argue that the move isolates BTS from the very platform that could amplify Asian representation on a global stage.

What remains clear is that BTS has drawn a line. The group is choosing to define their legacy on their own terms, rather than accept a category they feel diminishes their artistry.

All eyes will now be on the Recording Academy's response and whether it revisits the category structure before the next awards cycle.
