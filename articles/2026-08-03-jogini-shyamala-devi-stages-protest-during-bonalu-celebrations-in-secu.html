Jogini Shyamala Devi stages protest during Bonalu celebrations in Secunderabad

Protest during festivities

Bonalu celebrations in Secunderabad took an unexpected turn on Monday when Jogini Shyamala Devi staged a protest during the ongoing festivities. The demonstration, which unfolded amid the religious fervour of the annual event, drew the attention of devotees and authorities alike.

Details of the protest remain sketchy, with officials yet to confirm the exact location and sequence of events. Eyewitnesses suggest the protest was peaceful, though it briefly disrupted the flow of celebrations near one of the key procession routes.

Who is Jogini Shyamala Devi?

Jogini Shyamala Devi is known locally for her activism, though her specific demands during this protest have not been officially disclosed. The term 'Jogini' refers to a traditional practice in parts of Telangana where women are dedicated to local deities, often facing social and economic marginalisation.

Devi has reportedly been vocal about issues affecting women in similar circumstances, and her protest appears to be linked to long-standing grievances. However, no formal statement has been issued by her or her supporters so far.

Bonalu celebrations continue

The Bonalu festival, dedicated to Goddess Mahakali, is a major event in Hyderabad and Secunderabad, marked by colourful processions, traditional music, and large crowds. Monday's celebrations were part of the ongoing festivities, which typically span several weeks during the monsoon season.

Despite the protest, the celebrations continued without major disruption, according to local reports. Police presence was heightened in the area, though no incidents of violence or confrontation were reported.

Authorities yet to respond

Officials from the Secunderabad police and the local administration have not yet commented on the protest. It remains unclear whether any formal complaint was filed or if negotiations are underway with the protesters.

Such demonstrations during public festivals are not uncommon in the region, as activists often use high-visibility events to press their demands. The response from authorities, if any, is awaited.

What to watch

The coming days will reveal whether the protest leads to any official dialogue or action. With Bonalu celebrations set to continue, further demonstrations cannot be ruled out. Observers will be watching for any statement from Jogini Shyamala Devi or the district administration regarding the issues raised.
