15 Bangladeshis Held in Special Drive Against Illegal Immigrants in Bengaluru

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Fifteen Bangladeshi nationals arrested in Bengaluru during a special drive.
  • Action part of a broader crackdown on illegal immigrants in the city.
  • Police investigating possible links to larger networks; further details awaited.

Bengaluru police have detained 15 Bangladeshi nationals as part of a special drive targeting illegal immigrants in the city. The operation, conducted over recent days, reflects an intensified focus on undocumented residents, with authorities citing concerns over documentation and potential security risks.

Details of the Drive

The arrests were made across multiple locations in Bengaluru, though officials have not yet disclosed specific areas or the exact duration of the operation. Sources suggest the drive was planned following tip-offs and routine checks that flagged individuals unable to produce valid identification or residence permits.

Each detainee is being questioned to ascertain their entry into India and length of stay. Police have said that preliminary inquiries indicate some had been living in the city for months, possibly working in informal sectors without proper papers.

Legal Proceedings Underway

The detained individuals are currently in custody, with authorities preparing cases under relevant provisions of the Foreigners Act and the Passport (Entry into India) Act. Legal experts note that such cases typically involve verification of nationality and potential deportation proceedings, though timelines can vary.

Officials have not confirmed whether the group will face additional charges or if any will be released pending further investigation. The Bangladeshi High Commission has not yet issued a public statement on the detentions.

Broader Crackdown Context

This drive is part of a wider effort by Bengaluru police and central agencies to identify and act against illegal immigrants, particularly those from neighbouring countries. Similar operations have been reported in other major Indian cities in recent months, with authorities emphasising the need to tighten border controls and internal monitoring.

Police have indicated that the current focus is on dismantling any local networks that may have facilitated the entry or stay of these individuals. Investigators are also checking if the detainees have any links to organised rackets that forge documents or arrange illegal crossings.

What Happens Next

Officials are expected to complete verification and file a chargesheet in the coming weeks. The fate of the 15 will depend on legal outcomes, with deportation likely if their Bangladeshi citizenship is confirmed. Watch for updates on any larger network arrests or policy shifts in Bengaluru's approach to undocumented migrants.
