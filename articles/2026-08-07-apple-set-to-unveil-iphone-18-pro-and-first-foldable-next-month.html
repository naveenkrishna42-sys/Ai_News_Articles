Apple set to unveil iPhone 18 Pro and first foldable next month

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple's next iPhone launch is expected in September, with the iPhone 18 Pro lineup and a foldable iPhone tipped for unveiling.
  • Retail employees in some regions have been informed of a lottery system for store launch duties, a common practice for major product releases.
  • Reports suggest the iPhone 18 Pro Max will arrive in a new Dark Cherry finish, among other colour options.
  • Apple's event is also expected to feature at least five new devices, per industry speculation.

Apple is gearing up for what could be its most significant September event in years, with reports indicating the company will unveil the iPhone 18 Pro lineup alongside its first-ever foldable iPhone. The announcement is expected within the next month, according to multiple industry sources.

Bloomberg has reported that Apple has already begun internal preparations, including a lottery system for retail employees to determine who will work at store launch events. This practice is typically reserved for major product debuts, signalling that the upcoming launch is being treated as a high-priority occasion.

iPhone 18 Pro: What's Expected

Tech analysts and leakers have pointed to several upgrades for the iPhone 18 Pro models. While Apple has not confirmed any specifications, reports from Techgenyz and other outlets suggest improvements in camera technology, battery life, and processing power. The Pro Max variant is also tipped to introduce a new Dark Cherry colour finish, a departure from the usual black, white, and natural titanium shades.

The colour leak, reported by Moneycontrol, has generated considerable buzz among Apple enthusiasts. If accurate, the Dark Cherry option would mark a bold aesthetic shift for the company's premium lineup, which has largely stuck to muted tones in recent years.

The Foldable iPhone Arrives

The most anticipated reveal, however, is the foldable iPhone. This would mark Apple's entry into a segment that Samsung and other Android manufacturers have dominated for years. Rumours suggest the device will feature a book-style foldable design, similar to Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold series, though Apple is expected to bring its own approach to durability and software optimisation.

Analysts have long speculated about Apple's foldable plans, with delays attributed to concerns over display technology and hinge reliability. If the device does launch next month, it will be a direct challenge to rivals in a category that has seen steady growth but remains niche compared to traditional smartphones.

Five Devices Likely at the Event

According to Deccan Herald, Apple's September event could see the launch of at least five new products. Beyond the iPhone 18 Pro and foldable, the company is expected to refresh its standard iPhone models, possibly introduce a new Apple Watch, and update its AirPods lineup. The exact list remains unconfirmed, but the breadth of expected launches underscores the scale of this year's event.

Retail staff in India and other markets have reportedly been told to prepare for a busy launch period, though Apple has not issued any official statement about the event date. The company typically sends invitations to media two weeks before its September keynote, which means an announcement could come as soon as late August.

What to Watch For

As the launch approaches, investors and consumers will be watching for details on pricing, especially for the foldable iPhone, which is expected to command a premium over existing models. Battery life and durability of the foldable screen will also be key talking points, given past criticism of foldable devices in general.

Apple's ability to manage supply chains will be another factor. With global smartphone demand showing signs of recovery, a successful launch could provide a significant boost to the company's fiscal year. For now, the tech giant remains tight-lipped, but the next few weeks are likely to bring more leaks and official confirmations.

The September event, if it proceeds as expected, will be a defining moment for Apple in 2026.
