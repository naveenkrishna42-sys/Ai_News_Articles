Bitcoin drops to $64K, Ethereum under $1,900 as crypto market swings

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin slipped to $64,000, extending recent losses.
  • Ethereum traded below $1,900, reflecting broad market weakness.
  • Volatility driven by macroeconomic factors and investor caution.

Market Takes a Hit

Bitcoin tumbled to $64,000 on Tuesday, while Ethereum dropped below $1,900, as the cryptocurrency market reeled under fresh volatility. The sell-off wiped out gains from the past week, leaving traders scrambling to reassess their positions.

The decline comes after a period of relative calm, during which Bitcoin had hovered near $70,000. Now, the largest digital asset is trading at levels not seen in weeks, and Ethereum is struggling to hold key support.

What's Behind the Slide

Market participants point to a mix of factors, including rising Treasury yields and a stronger US dollar, which typically weigh on risk assets like cryptocurrencies. Geopolitical tensions and uncertainty over central bank policies have also added to the jitters.

Regulatory headlines, too, have kept investors on edge. While no major announcement has been confirmed, traders are bracing for potential policy shifts in key markets.

Liquidations and Leverage

The sharp moves have triggered a wave of liquidations across derivatives exchanges. Data from tracking platforms suggests that leveraged long positions were hit hardest, as prices broke below key technical levels.

Such cascading liquidations often amplify downward moves, creating a feedback loop that spooks retail and institutional players alike. Analysts warn that if Bitcoin fails to hold $64,000, the next support could be near $60,000.

Investor Sentiment

Sentiment has turned cautious, with fear and greed indices sliding toward 'fear' territory. Some traders see this as a buying opportunity, while others prefer to wait for clearer signals.

In India, where crypto trading volumes have picked up in recent months, exchanges report higher-than-usual activity. Local investors are closely monitoring global cues, given that domestic regulations remain in flux.

What to Watch Next

All eyes are on the upcoming US inflation data, which could shape the Federal Reserve's rate path. A hotter-than-expected print may push Bitcoin lower, while a cooler number could spark a relief rally.

For now, the market remains in a holding pattern, with volatility likely to persist. Traders should brace for more swings as the week unfolds.
