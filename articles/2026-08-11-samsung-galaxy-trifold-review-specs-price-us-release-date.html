Samsung Galaxy TriFold Review: Specs, Price, US Release Date

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Gadget Comparisons Original source ↗
Key points
  • US release date is expected in early 2026, but no exact date announced.
  • Unfolded display is the largest on any mainstream phone at ~10 inches.
  • Weight and durability remain the biggest trade-offs.
  • Price is expected to be over $2,000, but not yet confirmed.

This is the first mainstream tri-fold phone from Samsung, and it's generating buzz ahead of its US release. With the device now being reviewed in the US, it's the moment to decide if the future of foldables is worth the leap.

What reviewers generally say: Early US reviews are mixed but generally positive, praising the engineering and display size while criticizing the high price and durability concerns common to tri-fold designs.

Pros
  • Massive 10-inch unfolded display for productivity and media consumption
  • Premium titanium build with IP48 water resistance
  • Powerful Snapdragon 8 Gen 4 chipset handles multitasking with ease
Cons
  • Extremely heavy at ~260g, making one-handed use nearly impossible
  • Crease is still visible on both folds, and durability of the inner screen is a long-term unknown
  • Battery life is average at best, struggling to last a full day with heavy use

The Samsung Galaxy TriFold is a marvel of engineering, but it's not for everyone. If you're a power user who craves a tablet in your pocket and can afford the premium price, this is the ultimate productivity tool. However, if you value portability, battery life, or durability over screen size, you're better off with a regular foldable like the Galaxy Z Fold or a traditional slab phone. The TriFold is a statement piece, not a daily driver for the masses.
