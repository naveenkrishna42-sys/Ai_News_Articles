Google Pixel 11a specs leak online ahead of expected launch

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Leaked specs show Pixel 11a with 6.1-inch 90Hz OLED display, Tensor G5 processor, and 5,000 mAh battery.
  • The phone is expected to feature 8GB RAM and 128GB base storage, with a 50MP main camera.
  • Google has started sending invites for the Pixel 11 series launch event in India, with the 11a likely to be a key offering.

Leaked specifications surface

A detailed specifications sheet for the Google Pixel 11a has appeared online, giving potential buyers a clear picture of what to expect from the budget-friendly device. The leak, reported by GSMArena, comes just days before Google is expected to unveil its Pixel 11 series in India.

According to the leaked information, the Pixel 11a will feature a 6.1-inch OLED display with a 90Hz refresh rate. This marks a notable upgrade over its predecessor, which offered a 60Hz panel. The screen is expected to reach a peak brightness of 2,000 nits, making it usable under direct sunlight.

Tensor G5 chip and battery upgrades

Under the hood, the Pixel 11a is said to run on Google's in-house Tensor G5 processor. This custom chip is designed to handle AI tasks and photography processing with greater efficiency than previous generations. The phone will reportedly come with 8GB of RAM and two storage options: 128GB and 256GB.

Battery life appears to be a key focus. The leak indicates a 5,000 mAh battery, which would be a significant jump from the 4,492 mAh cell in the Pixel 8a. This could give the 11a an edge in endurance, especially when paired with the power-efficient Tensor G5.

Camera and design expectations

The camera setup is rumoured to include a 50-megapixel primary sensor alongside a 13-megapixel ultra-wide lens. Google's computational photography has long been a selling point for its Pixel lineup, and the 11a is expected to benefit from the same software magic. A 13-megapixel front-facing camera is also listed.

In terms of build, the Pixel 11a is likely to retain the familiar Pixel design language — a clean back panel with a horizontal camera bar. The phone is expected to be IP68 rated for dust and water resistance, a feature that has become standard on higher-end Pixel models.

Pricing and launch timeline

Google has reportedly started sending media invites for a Pixel 11 series launch event in India. While the exact date remains unconfirmed, the event is expected to happen within the next few weeks. The Pixel 11a will likely be positioned as the most affordable entry point into the Pixel ecosystem.

Pricing details have not been officially confirmed. However, earlier Amazon listings that appeared briefly suggested the base model could start around ₹45,000. That would put it in direct competition with devices like the OnePlus Nord series and the Samsung Galaxy A55.

The Pixel 11a is also expected to ship with Android 16 out of the box, with Google promising seven years of OS and security updates. This long-term software support remains a major differentiator in the mid-range segment.

As with any pre-release leak, this information should be treated with caution until Google makes an official announcement. The company has not commented on the leaked specifications.

All eyes are now on Google's upcoming launch event in India, where the full Pixel 11 series is expected to be unveiled. If the leaks hold true, the Pixel 11a could be one of the most compelling budget smartphones of the year.
