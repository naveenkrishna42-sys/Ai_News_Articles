All-rounder's double delight

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi's Duleep Trophy semifinal performance will go down as one of the most memorable in recent years. After failing to score in six balls, the all-rounder turned the game on its head with a career-best two wickets in four balls. This roller-coaster ride showcases his potential as a complete cricketer.

Batting failure to bowling masterclass

Sooryavanshi's innings began with a 6-ball duck, which is also a personal best. However, he bounced back with a bowling performance that left the opposition stunned. His strike rate of 180 balls per wicket is the highest in first-class cricket this season, highlighting his ability to strike early and often.
