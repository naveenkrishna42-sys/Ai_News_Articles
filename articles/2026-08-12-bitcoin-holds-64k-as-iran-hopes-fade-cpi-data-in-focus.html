Bitcoin holds $64k as Iran hopes fade; CPI data in focus

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin remains muted around $64,000 as geopolitical hopes from Iran fail to boost momentum.
  • Traders shift focus to upcoming US Consumer Price Index (CPI) data for inflation signals.
  • Market participants expect CPI figures to influence Federal Reserve policy and risk appetite.

Bitcoin traded in a tight range near $64,000 on Wednesday, as fading optimism over developments in Iran failed to inject fresh momentum into the cryptocurrency market. The digital asset has been stuck in a narrow band over the past 24 hours, with traders awaiting the next major catalyst.

Geopolitical hopes fizzle

Earlier this week, some market participants had pinned hopes on potential diplomatic progress in the Middle East, which could have eased risk aversion and supported speculative assets like Bitcoin. However, those expectations have largely evaporated, leaving the market without a clear directional push.

Analysts note that geopolitical news has had a diminishing impact on crypto prices in recent months. The market appears more sensitive to macroeconomic data and central bank policy signals than to headline-driven events.

"The Iran story has been a sideshow for crypto," said one trader in Mumbai. "The real driver remains the Fed and inflation."

CPI looms large

All eyes are now on the US Consumer Price Index (CPI) report, scheduled for release later today. The data is expected to offer fresh clues on whether inflation is cooling enough for the Federal Reserve to consider rate cuts.

Lower inflation would likely boost risk-on sentiment, potentially lifting Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies. Conversely, a hotter-than-expected print could reinforce the case for higher-for-longer rates, weighing on speculative assets.

Economists polled by Reuters expect annual CPI to have risen at a slower pace in July, but core figures remain sticky. The market is pricing in a roughly 60% chance of a rate cut in September, according to CME FedWatch.

Technical levels in focus

Bitcoin has established support around the $63,000 mark, with resistance near $65,500. A breakout above this level could open the door to further gains, while a drop below support might trigger a fresh wave of selling.

Trading volumes have been subdued, reflecting the cautious mood. Open interest in Bitcoin futures has also dipped, suggesting that leveraged traders are trimming positions ahead of the CPI release.

Market breadth and altcoin action

Ethereum and other major altcoins mirrored Bitcoin's lackluster performance, with most tokens trading flat or slightly lower. The total cryptocurrency market capitalization remained stable near $2.3 trillion.

Some traders are positioning for volatility after the CPI print, with options markets pricing in larger-than-usual moves for Bitcoin over the next 48 hours. "We could see a sharp reaction either way," said a derivatives trader in Bengaluru.

What to watch

The immediate focus is squarely on the CPI release at 18:00 IST. If inflation cools, Bitcoin could attempt a push toward $66,000; if not, a retest of $62,000 is possible. Beyond today, the Jackson Hole symposium later this month will be the next major event for crypto markets.
