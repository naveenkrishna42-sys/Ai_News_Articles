Incident Overview

A 21‑year‑old student from Guwahati was discovered dead near railway tracks in Srikakulam district, Andhra Pradesh, following a series of alarming calls and a video sent from a train compartment. The student, who had been experiencing distress over drunken men in the coach, reportedly texted her family and filmed the scene before her disappearance.

Immediate Response and Police Actions

Local railways authorities first responded to the calls, but the student's body was later found by a train guard while inspecting the track area. The Andhra Police launched a joint enquiry with the Indian Railway Safety Organisation (IRSO) and the Central Railway Police (CRP). The investigation centres on verifying the presence of the alleged drunken men, determining if there were any threats, and ruling out foul play.

Implications for Railway Safety and Student Welfare

Railways have long advocated safety trainings and vigilance programmes, especially for passengers. This incident illustrates a potential lapse in monitoring passenger behaviour in long‑haul coaches. Active measures such as on‑board CCTV scrutiny, real‑time passenger reporting apps, and better train staff presence can mitigate such risks. Additionally, universities, especially in Assam, need to reinforce travel safety guidelines for student travel, including emergency contacts and real‑time tracking systems.

Stakeholder Reactions and Calls for Action

Parliamentary representatives from Assam’s Lok Sabha have demanded a thorough review of railway security protocols. Student associations at Guwahati University issued a statement urging authorities to investigate the alleged assault and improve safety signage. Railway Chief Minister and state authorities have pledged a comprehensive audit of coach safety standards, water trust and a mandatory installation of in‑coach surveillance cameras.
