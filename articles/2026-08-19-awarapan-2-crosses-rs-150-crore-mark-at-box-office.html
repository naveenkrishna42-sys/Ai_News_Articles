Emraan Hashmi's film Awarapan 2 has crossed the Rs 150 crore mark at the box office, making it his first solo Rs 100-crore hit. The film, which released to mixed reviews, has been a surprise hit, thanks to its appeal to Gen Z audiences.

Emraan Hashmi delivers first solo Rs 100-crore hit

Emraan Hashmi, known for his collaborations with Salman Khan, has finally delivered his first solo Rs 100-crore hit with Awarapan 2. The film, directed by Vishesh Bhatt, has been a commercial success, crossing the Rs 150 crore mark in just five days.

Film's success attributed to Gen Z appeal and reclaimed box office failure of first film

The success of Awarapan 2 can be attributed to its appeal to Gen Z audiences, who have been the driving force behind the film's success. Vishesh Bhatt, the director of the film, has also stated that he wanted to reclaim the box office failure of the first Awarapan film.
