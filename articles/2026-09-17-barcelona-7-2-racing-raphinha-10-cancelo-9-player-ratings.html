Barcelona 7-2 Racing Santander: Player Ratings & Match Analysis

Barcelona kicked off their 2026/27 La Liga campaign with a resounding 7-2 victory over Racing Santander at the Estadi Olímpic Lluís Companys. Raphinha stole the show with a stunning hat-trick, while João Cancelo impressed with a goal and an assist. Hansi Flick's men sent a clear message to their title rivals with a ruthless attacking display.

Our expert player ratings reflect a team in sync, with standout performances across the board. Below, we break down the key contributions, tactical nuances, and what this result means for Barcelona's season.

Player Ratings: Raphinha Shines, Cancelo Impresses

Raphinha's perfect 10 is a testament to his clinical finishing and creative spark. The Brazilian winger tormented the Racing defence all evening, completing 5 dribbles and creating 3 chances. Cancelo's 9 highlights his dual threat: he completed 4 tackles and contributed a goal and an assist from right-back.

Other notable ratings include Pedri (8) for his midfield control, and Robert Lewandowski (8) for his brace. The defence had a few shaky moments, but the overall performance was dominant.

Tactical Breakdown: Flick's High Press Pays Off

Hansi Flick's signature high-pressing system was on full display. Barcelona suffocated Racing's build-up, forcing 12 turnovers in the attacking third. The quick transitions, led by Pedri and Frenkie de Jong, consistently found Raphinha and Cancelo in space.

Racing Santander, promoted this season, struggled to cope with the intensity. Their two goals came from set-pieces, exposing Barça's occasional lapse in concentration. However, the attacking fluidity more than compensated.

What This Means for Barcelona's Season

Starting with a seven-goal haul is a statement of intent. Flick has instilled a winning mentality early, and the squad depth looks promising. However, defensive frailties against set-pieces could be a concern against tougher opponents.

For Racing, this match serves as a harsh lesson on the demands of La Liga. They showed glimpses of quality but were outclassed physically and tactically.

Pros & Cons: Key Insights

  • Pros:
    • Raphinha's world-class form continues from last season.
    • Cancelo's attacking output from right-back is a huge asset.
    • Flick's tactics are already yielding results.
    • Youngsters like Lamine Yamal got valuable minutes.
  • Cons:
    • Defensive concentration lapses led to two soft goals.
    • Over-reliance on Raphinha's brilliance could be risky.
    • Racing's high line was easily exploited, but better teams won't be as naive.

Conclusion: A Promising Start

Barcelona's 7-2 win is a perfect opening day result. Raphinha and Cancelo were exceptional, and Flick's impact is clear. While defensive issues remain, the attacking firepower suggests a thrilling season ahead. Next up, Barça face a tougher test against Atlético Madrid, where consistency will be key.

For Racing Santander, it's back to the drawing board as they aim to survive in the top flight.
