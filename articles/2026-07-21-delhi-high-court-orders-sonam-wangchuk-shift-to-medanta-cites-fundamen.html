Delhi High Court Orders Sonam Wangchuk Shift to Medanta, Cites Fundamental Rights

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Delhi High Court orders immediate transfer of Sonam Wangchuk to Medanta Hospital.
  • Court says shifting to a hospital of choice ensures protection of fundamental rights.
  • Order comes after appeal against refusal to move Wangchuk to a private facility.

The Delhi High Court on Tuesday ordered the immediate transfer of climate activist Sonam Wangchuk to Medanta Hospital, a private medical facility of his choice. The bench ruled that accommodating his request would safeguard his fundamental rights, rejecting earlier objections from authorities.

Court's Ruling on Hospital Transfer

Justice Prathiba M. Singh, hearing the matter, stated that the state cannot ignore the concerns of a patient regarding medical care. “Shifting Sonam Wangchuk to a hospital of his choice will ensure protection of his fundamental rights,” the court observed.

The order came in response to an appeal filed by Wangchuk challenging the refusal of authorities to move him from a government hospital to a private one. The court directed the hospital administration to facilitate the transfer without delay.

Background of the Case

Sonam Wangchuk, known for his activism on environmental and social issues in Ladakh, had been admitted to a government facility. His legal team argued that the lack of specialised care at the current hospital was affecting his health.

The Delhi High Court noted that while the state has a duty to provide medical care, the patient's choice must be respected unless there are compelling reasons to deny it. No such reasons were presented in this case.

Legal Implications

Advocates for Wangchuk welcomed the ruling, describing it as a reaffirmation of individual autonomy in medical decisions. The court's observation that fundamental rights are at stake sets a precedent for similar disputes between patients and authorities.

Officials from the Delhi government have not yet commented on whether they will appeal the decision. The hospital has been asked to provide a report on Wangchuk's condition within 24 hours of his admission.

Reactions and Next Steps

Supporters of the activist gathered outside the court, expressing relief at the verdict. Wangchuk's family members said they hoped the transfer would lead to better treatment and recovery.

The court has scheduled the next hearing for next week to review progress. Until then, Medanta Hospital has been directed to ensure all necessary medical protocols are followed.

What remains to be seen is whether this order will lead to broader changes in how courts handle medical care disputes involving activists or prisoners. For now, Wangchuk's transfer is expected to take place within hours.
