NASA Astronaut Anil Menon to Undertake First Spacewalk Next Week

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Science Original source ↗
Key points
  • NASA astronaut Anil Menon will conduct his first spacewalk next week from the International Space Station.
  • The spacewalk is part of a series of three US spacewalks to upgrade the station's solar arrays.
  • NASA will host a preview news conference before the spacewalks and provide live coverage.
  • Menon has shared a behind-the-scenes ritual he follows before his historic first spacewalk.

Historic Moment for Indian-Origin Astronaut

NASA astronaut Anil Menon is set to make history next week when he steps out of the International Space Station for his maiden spacewalk. Menon, who was born in India and later moved to the United States, will become one of the few astronauts of Indian origin to perform a spacewalk.

The spacewalk is scheduled as part of a series of three US spacewalks aimed at upgrading the ISS's solar arrays. NASA has announced it will provide live coverage of the event and host a preview news conference ahead of the spacewalks.

Upgrading the Space Station's Power System

The primary objective of the spacewalks is to install new solar arrays that will boost the station's power generation capacity. These upgrades are critical for supporting ongoing research and future missions.

Menon and his fellow astronauts have been preparing extensively for the complex task. The solar array upgrade is a multi-step operation that requires precise coordination between the spacewalkers and ground control teams.

Behind-the-Scenes Ritual Revealed

In a video shared ahead of the mission, Menon revealed a personal ritual he follows before every spacewalk. The astronaut described a quiet moment of reflection and focus, a routine he developed during his training at NASA.

Menon's journey to this point has been remarkable. Selected by NASA in 2021, he completed over two years of rigorous training before being assigned to the ISS expedition. His background as a physician and former flight surgeon has been invaluable for the mission.

Coverage and Next Steps

NASA will broadcast the spacewalk live on its official channels, with experts providing commentary and analysis. The preview news conference, scheduled for later this week, will offer more details about the timeline and specific tasks.

For Menon, this spacewalk represents the culmination of years of hard work and determination. It also serves as an inspiration for aspiring astronauts in India and around the world.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact date and time for the spacewalk, but updates are expected during the preview conference. The mission is proceeding as planned, with all systems on the ISS operating normally.

The spacewalks are expected to continue over the coming weeks, with Menon likely participating in more than one. As the solar array upgrade progresses, the station's power capabilities will be significantly enhanced, paving the way for expanded scientific experiments.
