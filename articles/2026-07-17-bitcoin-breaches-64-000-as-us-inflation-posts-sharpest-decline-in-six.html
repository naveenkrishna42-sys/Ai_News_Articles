Bitcoin Breaches $64,000 as US Inflation Posts Sharpest Decline in Six Years

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin price rose above $64,000 following the release of US inflation data.
  • Annual inflation slowed to its lowest level in six years, marking a sharp deceleration.
  • The data has fueled speculation about a potential pause or reversal in the Fed's rate hiking cycle.
  • Crypto markets saw broad gains, with Bitcoin leading the rally among major digital assets.
  • Analysts caution that volatility could persist as markets digest the implications for monetary policy.

Bitcoin Crosses $64,000 After Inflation Data

Bitcoin surged past the $64,000 mark on Thursday, rallying sharply after the United States reported its biggest annual slowdown in inflation in six years. The digital asset, which had traded in a narrow range for weeks, broke above resistance levels as traders reacted to the macroeconomic development.

The latest Consumer Price Index (CPI) data showed inflation easing more than expected, raising hopes that the Federal Reserve may soon soften its aggressive monetary tightening stance. The news triggered a broad-based risk-on move across financial markets, with crypto assets leading the charge.

Inflation Slowdown Sparks Fed Policy Bets

According to the Bureau of Labor Statistics, annual inflation slowed to its lowest level since 2020. The decline was the steepest year-over-year drop in six years, catching many economists by surprise. The data suggests that the cumulative effect of the Fed's rate hikes is finally cooling price pressures.

Market participants interpreted the report as a signal that the central bank may pause or even begin reversing its rate increases later this year. This outlook is particularly bullish for assets like Bitcoin, which have historically benefited from a loose monetary environment.

Crypto Market Gains Momentum

Bitcoin's rally lifted the broader crypto market. Ethereum, Solana, and other major altcoins also posted gains, though Bitcoin remained the primary focus. The total crypto market capitalisation rose by several percentage points in the hours following the inflation release.

Trading volumes on major exchanges spiked, indicating renewed interest from both retail and institutional investors. Some analysts pointed to the breakout as a technical signal that could attract further buying in the coming days.

Analysts Eye Next Resistance Levels

With Bitcoin now above $64,000, traders are watching the next key resistance zone near $66,000. A sustained move above that level could open the path toward the all-time high of around $69,000. However, some caution that the rally may be overextended in the short term.

"The inflation number was a clear catalyst, but we've seen sharp reversals before," one market observer noted. "The Fed's next move is still uncertain, and any hawkish commentary could quickly reverse these gains."

The rally also comes amid ongoing regulatory scrutiny in several jurisdictions. While the inflation data dominated headlines, policy developments remain a key risk factor for the sector.

What to Watch Next

All eyes are now on the Federal Reserve's next policy meeting in late July. If the inflation trend continues, the central bank may signal a shift in stance. For now, Bitcoin has reclaimed a crucial psychological level, but sustaining the rally will depend on whether the broader macroeconomic environment remains favourable for risk assets.
