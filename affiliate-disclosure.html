Affiliate & Partner Disclosure

Transparency and reader trust are the foundational principles of TIVRA News.

In compliance with FTC guidelines, Google Publisher policies, and affiliate network standards, this page provides a full disclosure of how commercial and affiliate relationships operate on TIVRA News.

1. Amazon Associates Program

TIVRA News is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.in, Amazon.com, and affiliated regional marketplaces. As an Amazon Associate, TIVRA News earns from qualifying purchases.

2. Cuelinks & Merchant Partner Networks

We partner with Cuelinks and verified multi-merchant affiliate networks to provide direct shopping and booking links to major online retailers and service providers, including Flipkart, Reliance Digital, Croma, Ajio, Tata CLiQ, Myntra, FirstCry, MakeMyTrip, 1mg, and certified financial institutions. When you click on a partner link and complete a purchase or application, we may receive a commission at zero additional cost to you.

3. How We Mark Commercial Links

Every commercial or affiliate link on TIVRA News is clearly labelled (Paid link) or identified with store attribution. Furthermore, all outgoing commercial links carry the rel="nofollow sponsored noopener noreferrer" attributes, adhering strictly to search engine guidelines.

4. Editorial Independence & Quality Commitment

  • Unbiased Coverage: Products, deals, and financial topics are chosen strictly based on news relevance, reader utility, and verified market trends — never by commission rates.
  • Objective Analysis: Pros, cons, ratings, and buying advice are authored independently. We do not accept paid reviews or sponsored favourable verdicts.
  • Price & Stock Disclaimers: Prices, interest rates, discounts, and availability reflect verified reporting at publication time and may fluctuate. Please confirm the final terms on the merchant's official portal before purchasing.

5. Contact & Corrections

If you have any questions regarding our affiliate disclosures or spot an outdated link, please reach out directly via our Contact Us page or review our Editorial Policy.
