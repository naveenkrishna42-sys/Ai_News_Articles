Why Vijay's Jana Nayagan Tickets Capped at Rs 190 in Tamil Nadu

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tamil Nadu theatres cap Jana Nayagan tickets at Rs 190 per Vijay's directive.
  • Bengaluru sees 6 am shows and advance booking rush for the film.
  • Jana Nayagan joins elite list with 8,000 screens, alongside Pushpa 2 and Jawan.

Tamil superstar Vijay's upcoming film 'Jana Nayagan' has a strict pricing cap in Tamil Nadu — no ticket will be sold for more than Rs 190. The decision, confirmed by theatre owners and distributors, comes directly from the actor himself.

Sources close to the production told NDTV that Vijay insisted on keeping tickets affordable for his fans. The move is seen as a populist gesture ahead of the film's release, especially since the actor has been increasingly active in public and political life.

Bengaluru Sees Pre-Dawn Rush for Shows

The pricing cap hasn't dampened demand. In Bengaluru, several theatres have scheduled 6 am shows to accommodate the surge in advance bookings. Fans queued up online and at counters within hours of the booking window opening.

This early morning slot is usually reserved for only the biggest releases. Industry observers noted that such a rush indicates the film is poised for a massive opening weekend across Karnataka as well.

8,000 Screens Across India

'Jana Nayagan' is releasing on over 8,000 screens nationwide, placing it in an elite club alongside 'Pushpa 2: The Rule' and 'Jawan'. The wide release reflects the confidence distributors have in the film's box office potential.

In Tamil Nadu alone, more than 2,500 screens will show the film. The state's multiplexes and single-screen theatres have agreed to the Rs 190 cap, though some have privately expressed concerns about revenue loss on premium formats.

Film Tweaked After CBFC Battle

The movie underwent re-editing after a prolonged tussle with the Central Board of Film Certification (CBFC). The producer confirmed that new songs have been added and the title card featuring 'CM Vijay' — a reference to the actor's political ambitions — has been retained after negotiations.

This suggests the CBFC had objected to certain political undertones in the film. The final cut has now been certified with a U/A rating, clearing the way for the release without further delays.

Lyrical Video and Father-Daughter Theme

The lyrical video for 'Adiye En Poonthene' was released this week, showing Vijay and actress Mamitha Baiju in emotional father-daughter scenes. The song has already trended on YouTube, racking up millions of views within hours.

The track highlights the film's central relationship, which producers say is the emotional core of the story. Early reviews from private screenings have praised the chemistry between the lead pair.

Meanwhile, a controversy brews in Bengaluru. Victory Cinema, a landmark single-screen theatre in the city, has declined to screen 'Jana Nayagan' amid a dispute with distributors over revenue-sharing terms. The theatre management cited 'unreasonable demands' from the film's distribution arm.

Industry insiders say this is an isolated incident, but it underscores the often-fractious relationship between exhibitors and distributors in the South Indian film market. Other Bengaluru theatres have confirmed they will screen the film as scheduled.

As the March 28 release date approaches, all eyes are on the advance booking numbers. If the current trend holds, 'Jana Nayagan' could break opening-day records in Tamil Nadu and Karnataka. Whether the Rs 190 cap will be extended to other states remains unclear, but for now, Tamil Nadu fans have a reason to cheer.
