Hyderabad newlywed dies by suicide; family alleges dowry harassment

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Woman, married less than a year, died by suicide at her residence in Hyderabad.
  • Family alleges dowry harassment and mental torture by in-laws.
  • Police have filed a case under relevant sections; investigation underway.

Incident reported in Hyderabad

A 23-year-old newlywed woman died by suicide at her residence in Hyderabad on Tuesday, prompting her family to allege harassment by her in-laws over dowry demands. The incident came to light when police received information about the woman's death and rushed to the spot.

According to preliminary reports, the woman was married less than a year ago. Her family members, who arrived at the scene, accused her husband and in-laws of subjecting her to constant mental and physical torture for additional dowry.

Family alleges dowry harassment

The woman's family said she had complained to them multiple times about being harassed for not bringing enough dowry. They alleged that despite several attempts to resolve the matter through family discussions, the harassment continued.

Police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including those related to dowry death and cruelty by husband or relatives. The body has been sent for post-mortem, and a detailed investigation has been initiated.

Police probe underway

Investigating officers said they are recording statements from the woman's family and neighbours to ascertain the circumstances leading to the suicide. They are also examining the woman's phone and other electronic devices to gather evidence.

"We have taken the allegations seriously and are verifying all claims," a police official said, asking not to be named. "The accused have not been arrested yet, but we are questioning them as part of the inquiry."

The in-laws have denied the allegations, claiming the woman was depressed and under stress for other reasons. However, police sources indicated that preliminary evidence suggests a history of domestic discord.

Rising concern over domestic harassment

This incident adds to a disturbing pattern of suicides among young married women in Telangana, often linked to dowry demands. Social activists have repeatedly called for stricter enforcement of anti-dowry laws and better counselling for families.

In recent months, several similar cases have been reported across the state, prompting authorities to set up special cells to handle such complaints. Yet, many cases go unreported or are settled within families, leaving victims without recourse.

Experts say that while legal measures exist, societal pressure and stigma often prevent women from seeking help. They urge families to be more supportive and to report harassment at the earliest.

What happens next

The police are expected to file a chargesheet after the post-mortem report and forensic evidence are received. The accused may face arrest if investigations confirm the family's allegations, and the case will likely be tried in a fast-track court if charges are framed.
