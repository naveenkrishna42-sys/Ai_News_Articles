PM Modi Meets CWG 2026 Medal Winners, Praises Lovlina and Mirabai

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • PM Modi met India's Commonwealth Games 2026 medal winners in New Delhi.
  • He praised Lovlina Borgohain for objecting to a distorted India map.
  • PM Modi fed laddus to weightlifter Mirabai Chanu during the interaction.
  • The meeting comes after India's podium finish at the Glasgow CWG 2026.

A Warm Welcome for the Champions

Prime Minister Narendra Modi met India's medal winners from the Commonwealth Games 2026 in New Delhi on Sunday, celebrating their achievements at the Glasgow event. The interaction, held at his official residence, was a mix of personal warmth and national pride.

The athletes, who returned with a strong medal haul, were greeted individually by the Prime Minister. According to officials, the meeting was scheduled to acknowledge their hard work and dedication on the international stage.

Lovlina's Stand on the Distorted Map

During the interaction, PM Modi specifically lauded boxer Lovlina Borgohain for her objection to a distorted map of India displayed at the Games. The Prime Minister reportedly praised her for standing up for the nation's territorial integrity.

Lovlina's act, which drew attention on social media, was highlighted by the PM as an example of an athlete's responsibility beyond sport. She had raised the issue when she noticed the incorrect representation at a venue or event in Glasgow.

Sweet Gesture for Mirabai

In a light-hearted moment, PM Modi was seen feeding laddus to weightlifter Mirabai Chanu, a gesture that quickly went viral. The act underscored the personal rapport the Prime Minister shares with the athletes.

Mirabai, a seasoned campaigner who has brought laurels to India in multiple international events, was among the medal winners present. The Prime Minister's gesture was widely appreciated as a sign of respect for her contributions to Indian sports.

A Strong Showing at Glasgow

India's performance at the Glasgow Commonwealth Games was notable, with athletes excelling across disciplines. The medal winners included debutants and veterans, reflecting the depth of talent being nurtured in the country.

According to reports from The Hindu, India finished on the podium with a creditable medal tally. The exact count of medals has not been officially re-confirmed by the Prime Minister's Office in this interaction, but the overall performance has been termed impressive by sports analysts.

Message of Inspiration

Times of India reported that PM Modi delivered a heartfelt message to the CWG heroes, remarking that 'from every drop of tears, gold medals are dripping'. This was seen as a tribute to the sacrifices and struggles behind each victory.

The Prime Minister encouraged the athletes to continue striving for excellence, drawing inspiration from their own journeys. He also stressed the importance of sports in building a strong and confident nation.

Telegraph India noted that the PM's interaction was part of a series of engagements with sportspersons following major international events. The government has consistently backed athletes through initiatives like the Target Olympic Podium (TOP) scheme.

What Lies Ahead

The meeting sets the tone for upcoming international competitions, including the Asian Games and the Olympics. With the Commonwealth Games behind them, the focus now shifts to sustained preparation and support for these athletes.

Officials have indicated that more structured training and funding will be directed towards medal prospects. The interaction serves as a morale booster as India aims for a stronger show on the global sporting stage in the years to come.
