Rahul Gandhi's NEET Strategy Faces Crucial Test in Jharkhand

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Rahul Gandhi's support for Jharkhand students' NEET demands is seen as a major political move against the Hemant Soren government.
  • BJP has withdrawn support to the Soren government, demanding students' demands be met, and challenged Gandhi to discuss in Parliament.
  • Nadda accuses Gandhi of double standards and disrupting Parliament over the issue.
  • The protest has become a flashpoint between Congress and BJP ahead of elections.

A Political Flashpoint in Jharkhand

The ongoing students' protest over NEET-related issues in Jharkhand has turned into a high-stakes political confrontation. Rahul Gandhi's decision to back the students' demands has put his party's strategy under the spotlight, with the BJP now challenging him to take the matter to Parliament.

The protest, which has seen thousands of students demanding changes to the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) process, has become a rallying point for opposition parties. Gandhi's active support has intensified the pressure on the Hemant Soren-led government, a key ally of the Congress in the state.

BJP's Counter-Move and Soren Government's Predicament

The BJP has escalated the situation by announcing the withdrawal of support to the Soren government unless the students' demands are met. This puts the ruling alliance in a difficult spot, as it navigates between addressing the protests and managing political fallout.

BJP president J.P. Nadda has thrown down the gauntlet, daring Rahul Gandhi to come for a discussion in Parliament. "Will be given a fitting reply," Nadda said, as he accused Gandhi of double standards on the Jharkhand students' protest. The BJP's move is seen as an attempt to corner the Congress and its allies on a sensitive issue that has mobilised young voters.

Gandhi's NEET Playbook Under Scrutiny

Rahul Gandhi's engagement with the students' cause is not new. His "NEET playbook"—a strategy of aligning with student grievances against competitive exam irregularities—has been a recurring theme in his political outreach. However, Jharkhand presents a unique challenge: the Congress is a coalition partner in the state government, and Gandhi's support for the protesters directly criticises his own ally's administration.

This dual role has invited criticism from the BJP, which accuses Gandhi of irresponsibility and deliberately disrupting Parliament. The opposition leader, however, has framed the issue as a matter of justice for students, arguing that the government must act on their demands. The standoff has also exposed tensions within the INDIA bloc, as the Congress and its allies grapple with aligning their national and state-level positions.

Parliament Disruption and Political Fallout

The issue has spilled into Parliament, where disruptions have stalled proceedings. Nadda's sharp response to Gandhi's remarks indicates the BJP's intent to use the protest as a wedge issue. "Rahul Gandhi is behaving irresponsibly, deliberately disrupting Parliament," Nadda said, signalling a combative session ahead.

For the Congress, the challenge lies in sustaining its credibility with the student base while maintaining coalition harmony in Jharkhand. The BJP, on the other hand, sees an opportunity to project itself as the true champion of student interests, even as it faces questions about its own handling of education policies.

What to Watch Next

The coming days will be crucial as both sides dig in. The Soren government's response to the students' demands will likely determine whether the protest escalates or de-escalates. Parliament is set to witness further clashes, with the BJP demanding a structured discussion and the Congress pushing for immediate action. The political stakes in Jharkhand, a state with significant tribal and youth populations, are high, and the outcome could shape the narrative in the run-up to the next elections.
