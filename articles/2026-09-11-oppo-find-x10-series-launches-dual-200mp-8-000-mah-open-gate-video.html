Oppo Find X10 Series: Launch Timeline and India Positioning

Oppo has confirmed the Find X10 series launch for September 2026, with pre-orders already live in China and global availability expected within weeks. The lineup spans three SKUs: the standard Find X10, the flagship Find X10 Pro Max, and a new value-focused Find X10 E. Indian pricing is projected between ₹65,000–₹85,000 for the Pro Max, placing it against the Vivo X200 Pro and Xiaomi 15 Ultra. Oppo's Hasselblad partnership enters its fourth generation, now backed by a dedicated imaging NPU for real-time RAW processing.

Camera Architecture: Dual 200MP Sensors and Open Gate Readout

The Pro Max debuts a dual 200MP configuration: a 1/1.4-inch 200MP main camera (f/1.65, 23mm equivalent) with 17 EV dynamic range and a 200MP periscope telephoto (CIPA 7.5 rating, 100mm equivalent). The main sensor uses 16-in-1 pixel binning for 12.5MP output with 2.24µm effective pixels. Crucially, the Pro Max supports Open Gate readout—utilising the full 4:3 sensor area for 4K/120fps video without cropping, a first for Oppo. The standard X10 drops the 200MP telephoto for a 50MP 3x module, while the X10 E uses a 50MP main with OIS but no periscope.

Battery and Charging: 8,000 mAh Silicon-Carbon Pack

All three models pack an 8,000 mAh silicon-carbon anode battery, delivering roughly 25% higher energy density than graphite equivalents. Oppo claims 1.8 days of mixed usage and 12 hours screen-on-time. Wired charging peaks at 100W (0–100% in 26 minutes), while 50W wireless and 10W reverse wireless are standard across the range. The battery retains 80% capacity after 1,600 cycles—double the industry average—thanks to Oppo's Battery Health Engine and adaptive charging algorithms that learn user routines.

Verdict: Should You Pre-Order?

The Pro Max is a genuine imaging benchmark—Open Gate video and dual 200MP sensors justify its premium for creators. The standard X10 offers 90% of the photo experience for ₹15,000–₹20,000 less, making it the value sweet spot. The X10 E exists solely to hit a sub-₹60,000 price; its compromised ultrawide and missing telephoto make it hard to recommend over a discounted Find X8 Pro. Wait for retail units to verify thermal throttling under sustained 4K/120fps recording before committing.

  • Pro: Industry-leading 8,000 mAh battery with 1,600-cycle longevity
  • Pro: Open Gate 4K/120fps video on Pro Max eliminates crop penalty
  • Pro: Hasselblad colour science now includes multi-frame RAW fusion
  • Con: Pro Max weight exceeds 235g; one-handed use is impractical
  • Con: X10 E uses mid-range Dimensity 8350 at near-flagship price
  • Con: No microSD slot or 3.5mm jack on any variant
