Besant Nagar Church Festival: Traffic chaos looms

📅 2026-08-29 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Traffic diversions and parking arrangements announced for Besant Nagar Annai Velankanni Church festival
  • Authorities expect high footfall due to the festival
  • Concerns raised over traffic congestion and parking availability
  • Local residents and businesses affected by the festival preparations

Traffic Diversions and Parking Arrangements Announced

Chennai city authorities have announced traffic diversions and parking arrangements for the upcoming Annai Velankanni Church festival in Besant Nagar. The festival, which is expected to attract a large number of devotees, has prompted the authorities to take preemptive measures to manage the expected traffic congestion.

High Footfall Expected

With the festival expected to draw thousands of devotees, the authorities are bracing for a significant increase in foot traffic in the area. The church, located in the heart of Besant Nagar, is a popular pilgrimage site, and the festival is a major event that draws devotees from all over the city and beyond.

Concerns Over Traffic Congestion

While the traffic diversions and parking arrangements are a welcome step, concerns have been raised over the effectiveness of these measures. With the festival expected to coincide with the peak of the monsoon season, the already waterlogged roads in the city could exacerbate the traffic congestion. Additionally, the limited parking facilities in the area could lead to long queues and frustration among the devotees.

Local Residents and Businesses Affected

The festival preparations have also affected local residents and businesses in the area. Many residents have reported disruptions to their daily routines due to the construction work and traffic diversions. Businesses, on the other hand, have expressed concerns over the loss of revenue due to the reduced foot traffic in the area.

Broader Impact on Chennai

The festival and the associated traffic management measures highlight the broader challenges faced by Chennai in managing large-scale events. With the city's infrastructure struggling to keep pace with its growing population, the authorities need to explore innovative solutions to manage such events effectively. This includes investing in better public transport, improving road infrastructure, and enhancing parking facilities.
