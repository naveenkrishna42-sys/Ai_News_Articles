Intesa Sanpaolo cuts Bitcoin ETF stake 94%, triples Ether ETF in Q2

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Intesa Sanpaolo reduced its IBIT stake by 94% in the second quarter.
  • The bank tripled its Ether ETF holdings during the same period.
  • The moves came as crypto prices slumped in Q2 2026.

Bank's Crypto Pivot

Italy's largest bank, Intesa Sanpaolo, made a striking shift in its cryptocurrency exposure during the second quarter of 2026. Regulatory filings show the bank slashed its stake in the iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) by a massive 94%, while simultaneously tripling its holdings in Ether-based exchange-traded funds.

The moves come against a backdrop of declining crypto prices, suggesting a strategic rebalancing rather than a wholesale exit from digital assets. The bank's actions reflect a nuanced approach to a volatile market segment.

Details of the Filings

According to the latest 13F filing with the US Securities and Exchange Commission, Intesa Sanpaolo's IBIT position was reduced to a fraction of its previous size. In contrast, the bank increased its Ether ETF holdings threefold, signaling a preference for the second-largest cryptocurrency over Bitcoin during the downturn.

Neither the bank nor BlackRock, which manages IBIT, has issued an official statement explaining the rationale behind the trades. The filings, however, provide a rare glimpse into how a major European financial institution is navigating the crypto winter.

Market Context

The second quarter of 2026 saw crypto markets under pressure, with Bitcoin and Ether both experiencing significant drawdowns. Intesa Sanpaolo's decision to trim Bitcoin exposure while boosting Ether suggests a belief that Ethereum's ecosystem offers better long-term prospects, or at least a more favorable risk-reward profile at current valuations.

Analysts note that institutional investors often use such periods to rebalance portfolios, and this move could be part of a broader strategy to diversify within the asset class. The timing, during a slump, also hints at opportunistic buying in Ether at lower prices.

Implications for Institutional Adoption

Intesa Sanpaolo's actions underscore the growing, if cautious, institutional engagement with crypto ETFs. While the bank reduced its Bitcoin stake, its simultaneous increase in Ether holdings demonstrates that it remains committed to the sector, albeit with a different allocation.

This development may influence other European banks to reconsider their own crypto strategies, particularly as ETF products gain regulatory clarity. However, the volatility of digital assets continues to be a deterrent for many traditional financial institutions.

What remains to be seen is whether this represents a temporary tactical shift or a longer-term change in Intesa Sanpaolo's crypto thesis. With markets still uncertain, observers will watch for further filings in the coming quarters to gauge the bank's next move.
