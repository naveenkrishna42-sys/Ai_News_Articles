Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 display module gets 10% thinner with Flex Titanium

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung reduced the Z Fold8 display module thickness by 10% using Flex Titanium.
  • The new material allows for a slimmer, lighter foldable phone without sacrificing strength.
  • The Z Fold8 series is expected to launch alongside the Z Flip8 in India soon.

Slimmer Fold, Stronger Build

Samsung has managed to shave off 10% of the display module thickness on its upcoming Galaxy Z Fold8. The company achieved this using a new material it calls 'Flex Titanium'. The move is aimed at making the foldable phone thinner and lighter while retaining the structural integrity users expect from a premium device.

The reduction in thickness is not just about aesthetics. A slimmer display module allows for a more compact form factor when folded, and reduces overall weight. This addresses a common complaint among foldable phone users — bulkiness.

What is Flex Titanium?

Flex Titanium is a proprietary alloy that Samsung developed specifically for the Z Fold8's display assembly. It is lighter than traditional stainless steel but offers comparable rigidity. The material also resists scratches and dents better than earlier generations of foldable phone frames.

Industry analysts note that this innovation could set a new benchmark for foldable displays. Competing brands have been experimenting with titanium alloys in their flagship models, but Samsung's integration of the material directly into the display module is a first.

Broader Z Fold8 Series Details

Beyond the display module, the Galaxy Z Fold8 series is expected to include multiple variants. Reports suggest a base Z Fold8, a premium Z Fold8 Ultra, and the clamshell Z Flip8. All models are likely to feature improved hinges and water resistance ratings.

Samsung has already begun teasing the 'Passport' design language for the Fold8, which refers to a wider, more square-shaped cover display. Bloomberg has described this as the next big smartphone trend. The design aims to make the outer screen more usable without unfolding the device.

Availability in India

Reliance Digital has confirmed it will stock the Galaxy Z Fold8 series and Z Flip8 across its stores in India. The retail chain made the announcement earlier this week, suggesting a launch is imminent. Pricing details have not been officially disclosed, but the Z Fold8 is expected to be priced in line with its predecessor, around the ₹1.5 lakh mark.

Samsung's global launch event is expected within the next few weeks. The company is positioning the Fold8 as a device that combines the productivity of a tablet with the portability of a phone. Battery capacity leaks for the Galaxy S27 Pro and S27 Ultra have also surfaced, but those models are likely to launch later in the year.

With the Z Fold8, Samsung is betting that a thinner, lighter foldable will finally convince mainstream users to switch. The next few months will show whether the market agrees.
