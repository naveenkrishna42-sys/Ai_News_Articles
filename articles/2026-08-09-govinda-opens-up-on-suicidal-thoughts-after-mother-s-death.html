Govinda Opens Up on Suicidal Thoughts After Mother's Death

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Govinda revealed he felt suicidal after his mother Nirmala Devi's demise.
  • He walked into the Narmada River, hoping to meet her again.
  • The actor said he realised he had to live for his kids and pulled back.
  • The revelation offers a rare, personal glimpse into the star's grief.

Mumbai: In a deeply personal revelation, actor Govinda has spoken about the overwhelming grief that followed his mother Nirmala Devi's death, admitting that he walked into the Narmada River with suicidal thoughts.

The veteran star, known for his comic timing and dance moves, opened up about the darkest chapter of his life, recalling the moment he felt he could not carry on without his mother.

A Mother's Loss

Nirmala Devi, a renowned classical and folk singer, passed away in 2018. For Govinda, who shared an exceptionally close bond with her, the loss was devastating. In a recent interview, he described the period as one of tremendous pain, where the weight of grief clouded his judgement.

He recounted that in that state of mind, he found himself at the Narmada River. The actor said he walked into the waters, feeling a pull towards what he thought would be a reunion with his mother.

Walking Into the Narmada

Govinda told reporters that he “felt kind of suicidal” in the aftermath of her death. He described the act not as a planned decision but as an impulsive surrender to sorrow. “I thought I’d meet her again,” he said, explaining the emotional logic that drove him towards the river.

The incident, which he has now chosen to share publicly, sheds light on the fragile mental state that can follow the loss of a loved one. The actor did not elaborate on the exact circumstances or how he was stopped, but his words carry the weight of a man who has stared into the abyss and pulled back.

Turning Point: His Children

The turning point, Govinda said, came when he realised that his responsibilities were not over. “Soon realised I had to live for my kids,” he recalled. That thought—simple yet profound—pulled him back from the edge and gave him a reason to endure.

The actor, who has often spoken about his family values, credited his children for giving him the strength to move forward. In the years since, he has continued to work in films and television, though he has also faced health and career challenges.

A Stark Reminder

Govinda's admission is a stark reminder that grief can strike anyone, regardless of fame or fortune. His willingness to talk about such a vulnerable moment may help normalise conversations around mental health, especially in an industry where personal struggles are often hidden behind the glare of the spotlight.

Mental health experts have long stressed the importance of seeking help during bereavement. While Govinda did not mention professional counselling, his story underscores the need for support systems for those dealing with loss.

As the star continues his journey, his candid confession adds a new layer to his public persona—one that is not just about laughter on screen, but about resilience in real life.

Looking ahead, Govinda's revelation is likely to spark wider discussion about how public figures cope with personal tragedies. His story may encourage others who are silently suffering to speak up and seek help, knowing that even the brightest stars can face dark nights.
