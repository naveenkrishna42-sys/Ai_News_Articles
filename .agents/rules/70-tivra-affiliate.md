# TivraNews Affiliate Governance

Affiliate links are controlled commercial assets.
Never invent, alter, or fabricate affiliate URLs.

## Approved Affiliate Networks Only
Affiliate links may originate only from configured/approved networks:
- Amazon Associates (tag: sirmohana-21)
- Cuelinks (cid: 310115)
- Verified direct partner programs

## Strict Contextual Matching
Affiliate offers must match the article's user intent:
- Fixed Deposit (FD) / Banking articles -> Compare verified bank FD rates & savings accounts
- Credit Card articles -> Relevant cashback & reward cards
- Gadget / Electronics articles -> Amazon & Flipkart price comparison
- Fashion & Apparel -> Ajio & Myntra verified sales
- B2B Software & HR -> Rise Works global payroll
- Healthcare / Diagnostics -> 1mg verified lab tests
- Travel / Booking -> MakeMyTrip flights & hotels

Never place unrelated commercial offers merely to generate clicks (e.g. Never put B2B SaaS on an FD article).

## Disclosure
Commercial/affiliate relationships must be disclosed clearly with (Paid link) and rel="nofollow sponsored noopener noreferrer".
