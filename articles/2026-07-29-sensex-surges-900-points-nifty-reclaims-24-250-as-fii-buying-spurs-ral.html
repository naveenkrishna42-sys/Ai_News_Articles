Sensex Surges 900 Points, Nifty Reclaims 24,250 as FII Buying Spurs Rally

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex surged 900 points, Nifty crossed 24,250 on July 29.
  • FII buying was the primary driver of the rally.
  • Market sentiment improved across the board.

Markets Open Strong, Extend Gains Through Session

Indian equity benchmarks staged a powerful rally on Wednesday, July 29, with the BSE Sensex vaulting over 900 points and the NSE Nifty reclaiming the crucial 24,250 mark. The buying momentum was broad-based, lifting almost every sector into the green.

Traders said the surge was triggered by sustained foreign institutional investor (FII) inflows. After weeks of cautious positioning, FIIs turned aggressive buyers, pushing heavyweight stocks higher from the opening bell.

FII Buying Lifts Heavyweights

Banking, IT, and auto stocks led the charge. HDFC Bank, Reliance Industries, and Infosys were among the top contributors to the Sensex gain. The Nifty Bank index also jumped sharply, reflecting renewed confidence in the financial sector.

Market participants attributed the FII buying to improving global cues and expectations of steady domestic economic growth. Analysts noted that foreign funds had been underweight on Indian equities in recent months and were now covering positions.

Broader Market Sees Positive Breadth

The rally was not limited to large-caps. The BSE Midcap and Smallcap indices also climbed, indicating widespread investor optimism. On the BSE, advancing stocks outnumbered decliners by a ratio of nearly 3:1.

Volatility, as measured by the India VIX, cooled down, suggesting a decline in near-term anxiety among traders. Market observers said the day's move had broken the range-bound action seen over the past week.

While the exact triggers for the FII buying spree remain debated, many analysts pointed to stable crude oil prices and a slightly weaker dollar as supportive factors. The rupee also strengthened against the greenback, adding to the positive sentiment.

What Lies Ahead

Investors will now watch for the monthly FII flow data and any commentary from the Reserve Bank of India in the coming weeks. The sustainability of this rally depends on whether foreign buying continues or remains a one-day phenomenon.
