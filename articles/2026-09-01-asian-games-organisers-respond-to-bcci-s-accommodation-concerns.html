The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) has expressed concerns about the accommodation facilities for athletes at the upcoming Asian Games. The BCCI's president, Sourav Ganguly, has stated that the facilities are not up to the standard expected for such a prestigious event. This has raised questions about the credibility of the Asian Games and the ability of the organisers to deliver a top-tier event.

Organisers Issue Statement Addressing BCCI's Concerns

In response to the BCCI's concerns, the Asian Games organisers have issued a statement addressing the issues raised. The statement acknowledges the concerns and assures that efforts are being made to improve the facilities. However, the BCCI's president has stated that the assurances are not enough and that the facilities need to be upgraded immediately.
