Bitcoin falls below $63,000, hits two-week low on weak Coinbase results

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin dropped below $63,000, its lowest level in two weeks.
  • Disappointing Coinbase earnings contributed to the decline.
  • Overall market optimism appears to be fading among investors.

Bitcoin slid below the $63,000 mark on Friday, touching a two-week low as a combination of weak earnings from major exchange Coinbase and a broader cooling of risk appetite weighed on the cryptocurrency market. The pullback underscores just how quickly sentiment can shift in digital assets, where traders had been riding a wave of optimism just days earlier.

The world's largest cryptocurrency by market value has now given back a chunk of the gains it had accumulated over the past fortnight. While the drop is not steep in percentage terms, the psychological significance of losing the $63,000 level has not been lost on market participants.

Coinbase earnings disappoint

Coinbase, the largest crypto exchange in the United States, reported quarterly results that fell short of analyst expectations. The weak numbers reignited concerns about trading volumes and revenue growth, which have been under pressure as retail participation in crypto markets fluctuates.

Investors often treat Coinbase's performance as a proxy for the health of the broader crypto ecosystem. When the exchange struggles, it tends to dampen enthusiasm across the sector, and Friday's price action reflected that dynamic.

Fading momentum

The broader market had been showing signs of fatigue even before the Coinbase report. Over the past several sessions, momentum had stalled, with buyers failing to push prices decisively higher. The latest slide suggests that the bullish narrative that had supported prices is losing traction.

Trading desks noted that volumes have been thinner than usual, a sign that many investors are sitting on the sidelines awaiting clearer direction. The lack of fresh catalysts, combined with macroeconomic uncertainties, has left the market vulnerable to sudden moves.

What this means for traders

For short-term traders, the breach of $63,000 is a technical signal that could invite further selling. Support levels below that mark are likely to be tested if the downtrend persists. However, some analysts caution against reading too much into a single day's move, noting that bitcoin has a history of sharp reversals.

Long-term holders, meanwhile, appear largely unmoved. On-chain data suggests that wallets with significant bitcoin holdings have not been dumping their positions in a panic, which could indicate that the current decline is more about speculative positioning than fundamental shifts.

Global cues and risk sentiment

The crypto market is not operating in a vacuum. Global equity markets have also been choppy, and any signs of stress in traditional finance tend to spill over into digital assets. With central banks around the world signalling a cautious approach to policy, riskier assets like bitcoin often bear the brunt of investor caution.

The coming days will be crucial. If bitcoin manages to reclaim the $63,000 level quickly, the recent slide could be brushed off as a minor correction. But if selling pressure intensifies, the next leg down could bring the $60,000 mark into focus.

For now, traders are watching key support zones and awaiting any fresh news that could shift the narrative. The market remains highly reactive, and even a single headline can change the course of the day.

What happens next will depend largely on whether institutional investors step in to buy the dip or choose to stay on the sidelines. With no major macroeconomic data due in the immediate session, technical levels and sentiment will likely dictate the short-term path.
