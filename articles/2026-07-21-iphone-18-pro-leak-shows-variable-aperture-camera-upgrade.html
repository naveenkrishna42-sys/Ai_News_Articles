iPhone 18 Pro leak shows variable aperture camera upgrade

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • New video analysis suggests variable aperture is the biggest upgrade for the iPhone 18 Pro.
  • Leaks indicate a 2nm A20 Pro chip, larger battery, and a Dark Cherry colour option.
  • Expected launch in September 2026 with prices reportedly starting at $1,499 in the US.

Variable aperture takes centre stage

A fresh wave of leaks has focused on what could be the most significant hardware change in Apple's upcoming iPhone 18 Pro series. A new video analysis from Notebookcheck zeroes in on the variable aperture camera system, describing it as the generation's defining feature.

Tipsters predict the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max will ship with a mechanically adjustable aperture on the main lens. This would allow the camera to let in more light in dim settings or reduce light intake for sharper, better-exposed shots in bright conditions. The feature is common in professional cameras but has been absent from recent iPhones.

Other rumoured upgrades

91mobiles has reported that the iPhone 18 Pro Max could see the biggest camera overhaul, with the variable aperture being the headline improvement. The leak suggests Apple is finally moving beyond fixed apertures to give users more creative control over depth of field and exposure.

Separately, The Times of India reports the iPhone 18 Pro is expected to launch in September 2026. The device is also tipped to feature a smaller Dynamic Island, a 2nm A20 Pro chip, and a larger battery. The A20 Pro, built on TSMC's 2nm process, would mark a major leap in performance and efficiency over the current 3nm chips.

A new Dark Cherry colour option has also been floated, potentially joining the existing lineup of premium finishes.

Pricing expectations

The Economic Times has cited pricing estimates that place the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max at up to $1,499 in the US. That would mark a noticeable increase from the current generation, though official pricing remains unconfirmed.

Apple typically unveils its Pro models in September, and the company has not yet commented on any of these leaks. As always, specifications and features could change before the final product is announced.

Industry watchers will be looking for confirmation of the variable aperture and 2nm chip in the coming months. If the leaks hold, the iPhone 18 Pro could be one of the most technically ambitious iPhones in years.
