Amgen to set up Science and Innovation Centre at Genome Valley

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Amgen to establish a Science and Innovation Centre in Hyderabad's Genome Valley.
  • The facility aims to enhance research and development in biotechnology.
  • Hyderabad's biotech ecosystem continues to attract global pharma players.

Global biotech major Amgen is set to establish a new Science and Innovation Centre in Hyderabad's Genome Valley, marking another feather in the cap for the city's life sciences cluster. The announcement, made on Tuesday, underscores the growing importance of India as a hub for cutting-edge research and development.

Strategic Expansion in India

The new centre will focus on advancing scientific research and fostering innovation in the biotechnology space. Amgen's decision to choose Hyderabad comes amid a broader trend of multinational pharmaceutical companies deepening their footprint in India, leveraging the country's talent pool and cost advantages.

The facility is expected to create significant employment opportunities for scientists and researchers, further strengthening the region's reputation as a premier destination for biotech investment. Genome Valley, already home to over 200 life sciences companies, will now add Amgen to its list of prominent occupants.

Boosting Hyderabad's Biotech Ecosystem

Industry experts view this development as a positive signal for Hyderabad's ambition to become a global biotech innovation hub. The presence of Amgen, one of the world's largest independent biotechnology companies, is likely to attract more investments and collaborations in the sector.

The city has been steadily building its capabilities in drug discovery, clinical research, and manufacturing, with both state and central governments offering supportive policies. Amgen's entry is expected to accelerate knowledge transfer and skill development in the region.

Officials have not yet disclosed the exact investment size or the number of jobs that will be created, but the move is widely seen as a vote of confidence in India's biotech landscape.

Implications for the Life Sciences Sector

Amgen's latest initiative aligns with its global strategy to tap into emerging markets and diversify its research capabilities. The company already has a presence in India through its operations in Bengaluru, and this new centre in Hyderabad will complement those efforts.

The development also comes at a time when India is pushing to enhance its share of the global biotech market, which is projected to grow significantly in the coming years. With investments from players like Amgen, the country is well-positioned to play a larger role in the global pharmaceutical supply chain.

What Lies Ahead

As Amgen moves forward with its plans, all eyes will be on the timelines and specific research areas the centre will pursue. The company is expected to release more details in the coming months, including collaboration opportunities with academic institutions and startups.

For Hyderabad, the announcement adds momentum to its journey toward becoming a top-tier life sciences destination, and the ripple effects are likely to be felt across the industry.

The coming months will reveal how Amgen's centre takes shape and what it means for the region's biotech ambitions.
