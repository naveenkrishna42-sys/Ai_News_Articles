Canada May Rename Trump Avenue to 'Taco Avenue'

Ottawa's city council is considering renaming a street named after former US President Donald Trump to 'Taco Avenue' amid diplomatic tensions over Lake Ontario. The move comes as Canada seeks to distance itself from Trump's policies and the ongoing row over the lake's waters.

Diplomatic Tensions Over Lake Ontario

The renaming of the street is part of a broader effort by Ottawa to address the diplomatic tensions that have arisen over the sharing of Lake Ontario's waters. Canada has accused the US of not adequately addressing the issue of algal blooms in the lake, which have been linked to agricultural runoff from the US.
