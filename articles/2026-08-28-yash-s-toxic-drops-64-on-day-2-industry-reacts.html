Yash's 'Toxic' Struggles to Retain Audience on Day 2

Yash's latest film 'Toxic', starring Kiara Advani and Nayanthara, saw a significant 64% drop in box office collection on Day 2, according to industry reports. This drop has sparked discussions among industry experts about the appeal of the thriller-horror genre and the expectations of the audience.

Understanding the Drop

The drop in collection can be attributed to several factors. Firstly, the audience's initial excitement for the film might have worn off, leading to a decline in attendance. Secondly, the marketing strategy might not have been as effective as expected, failing to create a sustained buzz around the film.

Industry Experts Weigh In

Industry experts have varying opinions on the film's performance. Some believe that the thriller-horror genre is not as widely appealing as other genres, and that the audience's initial curiosity might have been the main driving force behind the film's success on Day 1. Others argue that the film's narrative and performances need to be more compelling to retain the audience's interest.
