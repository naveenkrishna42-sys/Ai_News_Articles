Nepal Flash Floods Leave 61 Pilgrims Missing

Nepal has been hit by severe flash floods, leaving 61 pilgrims, including a Mumbai doctor and his wife, missing. The floods, which struck on August 29, have caused widespread destruction, with reports of landslides and river overflows. The missing pilgrims are part of a group of 169 people who were on a pilgrimage to Kailash Mansarovar.

Safety of 102 Pilgrims Confirmed

Good news came on August 30 when 102 pilgrims from Maharashtra returned safely from Nepal. They had been stranded in Kathmandu after the floods. The group, which included 102 Nagpur pilgrims, was saved by local authorities and returned to Mumbai on August 30. Their safe return has brought relief to their families and friends.

Missing Pilgrims: A Closer Look

The missing pilgrims include a Mumbai doctor and his wife, who were part of a group of 69 people from Maharashtra who crossed the China-Nepal border into Nepal. The group was expected to return to Mumbai on August 31. The missing pilgrims are believed to be in the remote areas of Nepal, where the floods have caused significant damage.
