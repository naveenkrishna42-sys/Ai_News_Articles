Vijay's 'Jana Nayagan' Breaks Advance Booking Records, Sells 6.45 Lakh Tickets

Advance Bookings Surge for Vijay's 'Jana Nayagan'

Vijay's upcoming political action drama 'Jana Nayagan' is already setting the box office on fire, even before its release. Advance ticket sales have crossed a staggering 6.45 lakh tickets, signaling massive fan anticipation across Tamil Nadu and beyond.

Industry trackers report that the film is on track to break several opening-day records. The advance booking numbers reflect the star power of Vijay, who is widely regarded as one of the most bankable actors in Indian cinema today.

A Political Action Drama with an Ensemble Cast

Directed by H. Vinoth, 'Jana Nayagan' features a star-studded cast including Vijay, Pooja Hegde, Bobby Deol, Prakash Raj, and Mamitha Baiju. The film is being marketed as a high-stakes political thriller, with Vijay playing a character navigating the murky world of power and corruption.

Given Vijay's own rumored political ambitions, the film's theme has generated additional buzz. Fans are already drawing parallels between the on-screen narrative and real-life political scenarios in Tamil Nadu.

Director Responds to Controversies

In recent weeks, the film has been dogged by controversy. Director H. Vinoth was forced to issue a statement denying that any scenes were shot during a stampede that occurred in Karur during a promotional event. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of injuries from that incident.

Furthermore, Vinoth responded to allegations that the film was deliberately leaked to boost Vijay's political image ahead of the Tamil Nadu assembly polls. Calling the rumors baseless, he stated that the leak was an act of piracy and that the team was working with authorities to track down the source.

What to Watch Next

With advance bookings already setting records, all eyes are now on the opening weekend numbers. The film's political undertones and Vijay's potential entry into electoral politics will keep the conversation alive long after the credits roll.
