Television actor Rupali Ganguly has found herself at the centre of a social media storm after her comment on an incident involving Prime Minister Narendra Modi's photograph drew sharp criticism. The actor, known for her role in the popular show Anupamaa, reacted to a video showing a minor allegedly hitting the PM's picture with a slipper, and her choice of words has not gone down well with netizens.

The remark that sparked outrage

In response to the video, Ganguly reportedly said she wished the Prime Minister were a dictator, a statement that many interpreted as endorsing authoritarian measures. The comment quickly spread across platforms, drawing a mix of shock, anger, and sarcasm.

While the actor has not yet issued a formal clarification, the backlash has been swift. Several users pointed out the irony of her statement, given that her on-screen persona 'Monisha' from the sitcom Sarabhai vs Sarabhai is often remembered for her quirky, privileged demeanour.

Internet schools 'Monisha beta'

Social media was flooded with reactions, with many addressing her by her fictional character's name. One user wrote, "Monisha, you were stupid all along," while others questioned her understanding of democracy. The hashtag trended briefly as debates erupted over the limits of free speech and the responsibility of public figures.

Some defended her, arguing that her remark was made in the heat of the moment, prompted by anger over the disrespect shown to the PM's photo. But the majority of responses appeared to condemn the sentiment, with many calling it an attack on democratic values.

Context of the incident

The original video that triggered Ganguly's reaction reportedly shows a minor abusing a photograph of PM Modi. The incident itself has already sparked political reactions, with leaders from various parties condemning the act. However, the focus has now shifted to the actor's controversial response.

Details about the minor and the circumstances surrounding the video remain unclear, and authorities have not yet commented on any action taken. The incident adds to a growing list of online controversies involving celebrities reacting to political events.
