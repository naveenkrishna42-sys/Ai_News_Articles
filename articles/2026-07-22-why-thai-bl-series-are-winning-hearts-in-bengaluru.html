Why Thai BL series are winning hearts in Bengaluru

Key points
  • Bengaluru has become a key destination for Thai BL actors, with fan meets and events drawing large crowds.
  • Fans attribute the genre's appeal to its emotional depth, representation, and break from traditional Indian romance narratives.
  • The trend reflects a growing cross-cultural exchange between India and Thailand through pop culture.

Thai BLs find a home in Bengaluru

Bengaluru has quietly become a hub for Thai Boys' Love (BL) series. Thai actors now regularly include the city in their promotional tours. Recent fan meets have drawn hundreds of young viewers, many of whom queue for hours to meet their favourite stars.

The phenomenon is not just about celebrity worship. It reflects a deeper shift in how young Indians consume romance and representation.

Why the genre clicks

Fans of Thai BL dramas say the shows offer something they rarely see in mainstream Indian content. "The stories are slow, emotional, and focused on consent and communication," says a 22-year-old fan from Koramangala. "It's not about drama for drama's sake."

The genre typically follows romantic relationships between men. But its appeal has spread far beyond LGBTQ+ audiences. Many viewers say they enjoy the aesthetic, the soundtracks, and the actors' chemistry.

Another fan, a college student from Indiranagar, points to the lack of stereotypes. "In Indian shows, gay characters are often comic relief or tragic. Here, they are just people falling in love."

Cross-cultural pull

Thai BL series have also introduced fans to Thai culture. Phrases like "Sawadee Krap" (hello) are now common in fan circles. Some fans have started learning Thai online. Food, travel, and language vlogs by Thai actors get thousands of views from Bengaluru.

Event organisers have taken note. Multiple fan meetings have been held in the city over the past year. Tickets often sell out within hours. Local businesses, from cafes to merchandise stalls, have also benefited from the trend.

Representation and relatability

For many fans, the appeal is deeply personal. "I never saw love stories that felt like mine until I watched Thai BLs," says a 25-year-old software engineer. "It made me feel less alone."

Parents, too, are slowly becoming aware. Some fans say they now watch episodes with their families. The shows' focus on family acceptance in later seasons has helped start conversations at home.

Critics of the genre argue that the shows often feature unrealistic beauty standards and formulaic plots. Fans acknowledge the flaws but say the emotional core keeps them invested.

Streaming platforms have responded by adding more BL titles to their Indian libraries. Netflix, Viki, and YouTube now host dozens of series with subtitles. The availability has made the genre more accessible than ever.

What lies ahead

With Bengaluru firmly on the map for Thai actors, more events and collaborations are expected. Fans are already speculating about which star will visit next. The city's embrace of Thai BLs shows no signs of cooling down.
