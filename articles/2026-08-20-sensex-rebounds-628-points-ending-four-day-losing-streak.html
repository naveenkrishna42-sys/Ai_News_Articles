Sensex Rebounds 628 Points, Ending Four-Day Losing Streak

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex gained 628 points, breaking a four-day slide.
  • Nifty 50 index settled at 24,232.
  • Market sentiment shifted after a prolonged period of volatility.

Markets Stage Recovery

Indian equity benchmarks registered a sharp recovery on Thursday, snapping a four-day losing streak that had weighed heavily on investor sentiment. The BSE Sensex surged 628 points, providing a much-needed boost to the domestic market participants who had faced persistent selling pressure over the previous week.

The Nifty 50 index mirrored this positive momentum, settling at 24,232. Traders welcomed the reversal after the index struggled through a seven-day losing streak, which marked its longest period of decline in nearly 11 months.

Sectoral Performance and Momentum

Early trade set a constructive tone for the session, with the Sensex climbing 504 points to reach 77,416. The Nifty 50 also showed early strength, recording a 118-point gain to hit 24,198 during the opening hours. This early buying interest eventually sustained throughout the trading day, preventing a late-session reversal.

Market analysts are now evaluating whether this rally signals a definitive bottom or if it represents a temporary respite within a volatile environment. The broader market breadth remained supportive, indicating that the gains were not limited to a few heavyweights but were spread across multiple sectors.

Investor Sentiment Shifts

The recent slide in stock prices had sparked concerns regarding the near-term stability of the Indian bourses. By breaking the streak today, indices have managed to regain some of the lost ground that had eroded valuations over the past week.

Foreign and domestic institutional activity remains a primary focal point for participants. While the current jump brings relief to portfolios, market observers emphasize that the sustainability of these gains depends on sustained buying pressure in the coming sessions.

  • Sensex closing gain: 628 points.
  • Nifty 50 closing level: 24,232.
  • Market context: Reversal of a 7-day Nifty losing streak.

Market participants will closely monitor trading volumes and global cues tomorrow to determine if this upward momentum holds or faces renewed selling pressure at higher levels.
