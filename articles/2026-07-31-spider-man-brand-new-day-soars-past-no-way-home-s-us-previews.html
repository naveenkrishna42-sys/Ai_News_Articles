Spider-Man: Brand New Day Soars Past No Way Home's US Previews

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • US Thursday previews surpass No Way Home's $50 million benchmark
  • India opening day haul of Rs 72 crore sets new Hollywood record
  • Tom Holland's film receives critical praise for its reinvention

Marvel's latest web-slinger adventure is proving to be a box office phenomenon. Spider-Man: Brand New Day has swung past expectations, with Thursday previews in the United States outpacing the $50 million earned by its predecessor, Spider-Man: No Way Home, according to early estimates.

The film, directed by Destin Daniel Cretton, has generated significant buzz not just for its commercial performance but also for its creative direction. Critics have hailed it as a soulful reinvention that finally allows Tom Holland's Peter Parker to break free from the shadows of his predecessors.

A Historic Opening in India

The film's impact has been particularly pronounced in India, where it has created history. The MCU release amassed a staggering Rs 72 crore on its opening day, marking the biggest opening day haul for any Hollywood release in the Indian market.

This achievement surpasses the previous record held by Avengers: Endgame, a film that had enjoyed unprecedented popularity in the country. The milestone underscores the growing appetite for superhero cinema among Indian audiences and the expanding footprint of Hollywood releases in the region.

Critical Acclaim and Creative Shift

Beyond the numbers, Brand New Day is drawing attention for its tonal shift. Reviews have praised Cretton's direction for bringing a fresh perspective to the franchise, with The Hindu noting that Holland finally gets to "swing free" in a story that balances emotional depth with high-octane action.

The film's narrative reportedly distances itself from the multiverse complexities of No Way Home, opting for a more grounded approach. This creative decision appears to have resonated with both critics and audiences, who have embraced the character's evolution.

Box Office Trajectory

Industry analysts are closely watching the film's weekend performance, with projections suggesting a robust debut. The strong Thursday preview numbers indicate solid word-of-mouth and repeat viewing potential, factors that could propel the film to record-breaking totals.

While official worldwide figures are yet to be consolidated, early indications suggest Brand New Day is on track to become one of the highest-grossing entries in the Spider-Man franchise. The film's performance in international markets, particularly in Asia, will be crucial in determining its long-term box office viability.

What Lies Ahead

As the weekend unfolds, all eyes will be on whether the film can sustain its momentum and topple more records. With positive reviews and strong audience turnout, Spider-Man: Brand New Day appears poised for a historic theatrical run.

The coming days will reveal if it can match or exceed the lifetime grosses of its predecessors, and how the film's success shapes the future direction of the MCU's Spider-Man storyline.
