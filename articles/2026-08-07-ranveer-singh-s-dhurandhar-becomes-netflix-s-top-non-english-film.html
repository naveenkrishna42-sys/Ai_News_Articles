Ranveer Singh's Dhurandhar Becomes Netflix's Top Non-English Film

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Netflix co-CEO Ted Sarandos confirmed Dhurandhar as the platform's most-watched non-English film worldwide.
  • The Ranveer Singh-starrer has surpassed all previous non-English films in viewership within weeks of release.
  • The achievement underscores the growing global appeal of Indian cinema on streaming platforms.

A Historic Feat for Indian Cinema

Ranveer Singh's latest blockbuster, Dhurandhar, has officially become the most-watched non-English film in Netflix history. The announcement came from none other than Ted Sarandos, co-CEO of Netflix, during a recent industry event. Sarandos revealed that the film's global viewership numbers have eclipsed every other non-English title on the platform since its debut.

The revelation has sent ripples through the entertainment world. For an Indian film to top this chart is unprecedented. It marks a clear shift in how global audiences consume content from the subcontinent.

The Ranveer Singh Effect

Ranveer Singh, known for his electric screen presence and versatility, has delivered yet another powerhouse performance in Dhurandhar. The film, which blends high-octane action with a gripping narrative, has resonated with viewers far beyond India's borders. Trade analysts point to Singh's international fan base and the film's universal themes as key drivers of its success.

The film's release strategy also played a crucial role. Netflix rolled it out simultaneously across multiple regions, ensuring maximum reach. Within weeks, it climbed to the top of the charts in several countries, including markets where Indian films traditionally struggle to find traction.

Numbers That Speak Volumes

While Netflix has not released official viewership figures, industry insiders estimate Dhurandhar has been streamed by tens of millions of accounts worldwide. The film's performance has surpassed previous record-holders, including several high-profile European and South Korean productions. Sarandos's statement confirms that the film's cumulative viewing hours have crossed the threshold to claim the top spot.

This milestone is particularly significant given the stiff competition. The non-English film category on Netflix has seen fierce contenders in recent years. Yet Dhurandhar's rapid ascent suggests a changing tide in global streaming preferences.

What This Means for Bollywood

The achievement signals a new era for Indian cinema on the global stage. Streaming platforms have already been investing heavily in Indian content, and this success is likely to accelerate that trend. Producers and studios may now prioritise stories with universal appeal, backed by stars like Ranveer Singh who can draw international audiences.

It also validates Netflix's strategy of backing big-ticket Indian films. The platform has been aggressive in acquiring and producing local content, and Dhurandhar is a testament to that approach. For Bollywood, this is a moment of pride—a mainstream entertainer leading the world in streaming viewership.

Reactions and Responses

The film's team has been celebrating the news. Ranveer Singh took to social media to thank fans, calling the achievement a dream come true. Trade pundits and film critics have also weighed in, noting that the film's success could redefine how Indian films are marketed globally.

Meanwhile, Netflix is expected to leverage this momentum with more Indian originals in the pipeline. The platform has already announced several high-profile projects featuring top Bollywood talent.

The Road Ahead

As Dhurandhar continues its record-breaking run, all eyes will be on how long it holds the top spot. Upcoming releases from other countries may challenge its position, but for now, Indian cinema stands tall. The film's success is a clear indicator that language is no longer a barrier when storytelling is compelling.

For now, Ranveer Singh and his team can savour a historic win that has put Indian cinema at the forefront of global entertainment.
