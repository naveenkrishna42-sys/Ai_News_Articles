Protest Erupts in Jharkhand

Students in Jharkhand are protesting against the state government over a recruitment exam row. The protest turned violent when police used water cannons on the students who were trying to march to the Jharkhand Assembly.

The students, who are job aspirants, are demanding justice in the Jharkhand Public Service Commission (JPSC) and Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) recruitment exams. The exams have been marred by controversy, leading to widespread protests across the state.

Tensions Rise Between Police and Protesters

Tensions rose between the police and protesters near the Vidhan Sabha, with four people reported injured. The police have been trying to disperse the crowd, but the protesters are refusing to back down.

The protest has been called a ‘day of revolution’ by some leaders, with many rival parties coming together to support the students. The protest is putting pressure on the Soren government, which is already facing criticism over the recruitment exam row.

Political Implications

The protest has significant political implications, with many parties trying to capitalize on the situation. The divided opposition is trying to unite against the government, which is facing criticism over its handling of the recruitment exams.

Protesters Demand Justice

The protesters are demanding justice and are refusing to back down until their demands are met. The situation is tense, with many fearing that the protest could turn violent again.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of people injured in the protest, but reports suggest that several people have been hurt. The police are trying to maintain law and order, but the situation remains volatile.
