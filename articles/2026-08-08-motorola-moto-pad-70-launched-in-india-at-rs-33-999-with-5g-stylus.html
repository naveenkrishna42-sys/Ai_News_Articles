Motorola Moto Pad 70 launched in India at Rs 33,999 with 5G, stylus

Key points
  • Moto Pad 70 launched in India at Rs 33,999
  • Features 12.1-inch 90Hz display and 10,200mAh battery
  • Comes with bundled Moto Pen and AI-powered writing tools
  • Supports 5G connectivity

Motorola expands tablet lineup with Moto Pad 70

Motorola has officially launched the Moto Pad 70 in India, marking the company's latest push into the premium tablet segment. Priced at Rs 33,999, the device brings a 12.1-inch display with 90Hz refresh rate, 5G support, and a hefty 10,200mAh battery.

The announcement was made on Saturday, and the tablet is positioned as a productivity-focused companion, with a bundled Moto Pen stylus included in the box. The move signals Motorola's intent to compete directly with other Android tablet makers in the mid-to-upper price bracket.

Display and performance details

The Moto Pad 70 sports a 12.1-inch screen that refreshes at 90Hz, a spec that should appeal to users who consume media or work with stylus input. The company has not yet detailed the processor or RAM options, but the inclusion of 5G suggests a chipset capable of handling modern connectivity demands.

Battery life appears to be a key selling point, with a 10,200mAh cell that should easily last a full day of mixed use. Charging speeds and exact endurance figures have not been confirmed by Motorola at launch, though the large capacity is likely to be a draw for students and professionals on the move.

AI tools and stylus integration

One of the standout features is the integration of AI-powered writing tools, as highlighted in early reports. The bundled Moto Pen is designed to work seamlessly with these tools, offering low-latency input for note-taking and sketching. Motorola has positioned the Moto Pad 70 as a device that bridges the gap between a tablet and a laptop replacement, though it remains to be seen how the software ecosystem supports that ambition.

The stylus is included at no extra cost, a move that adds tangible value at the Rs 33,999 price point. Rivals often sell styluses separately, so this bundling could sway buyers who prioritise out-of-the-box usability.

Pricing and availability in India

The tablet is priced at Rs 33,999, placing it in a competitive zone against offerings from Xiaomi, Samsung, and OnePlus. Motorola has not yet specified exact sale dates or retail partners, but the device is expected to be available through major online and offline channels shortly.

Industry observers will watch whether the 5G capability and AI features justify the premium over Wi-Fi-only tablets in the same range. For now, the Moto Pad 70 appears to be a solid all-rounder, though detailed reviews will be needed to assess real-world performance.

What to watch next

Motorola is likely to reveal more specs, including processor details and software update commitments, in the coming days. Buyers should also keep an eye on introductory offers or bank discounts that could lower the effective price, as is common in the Indian tablet market.
