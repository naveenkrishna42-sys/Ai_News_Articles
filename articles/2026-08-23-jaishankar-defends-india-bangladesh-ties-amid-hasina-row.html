Jaishankar defends India-Bangladesh ties amid Hasina row

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • EAM Jaishankar says India expects mutual benefit from Bangladesh ties
  • Rashtrapati Bhavan withdraws notice for Bangladesh PM visit
  • Jaishankar calls Pakistan's actions 'systemic terrorism'

EAM Jaishankar says India expects mutual benefit from Bangladesh ties

External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar has said that India expects its ties with Bangladesh to serve mutual interest, amid uncertainty over Bangladesh Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina's visit to India. The Rashtrapati Bhavan had earlier recalled a notice for Hasina's visit, following her government's decision to expel Indian diplomats from Bangladesh.

In a statement, Jaishankar said, 'We are not expecting our neighbours to do only what's in our interest. We expect our ties to serve mutual benefit and mutual interest.'

Rashtrapati Bhavan withdraws notice for Bangladesh PM visit

The Rashtrapati Bhavan has withdrawn the notice for Bangladesh Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina's visit to India, following the government's decision to expel Indian diplomats from Bangladesh. The notice had been issued in the wake of the diplomatic row between the two countries.

Sources close to the matter said that the decision to withdraw the notice was taken to de-escalate the situation and to maintain the diplomatic ties between the two countries.

Jaishankar calls Pakistan's actions 'systemic terrorism'

In his statement, Jaishankar also called Pakistan's actions 'systemic terrorism', referring to the recent attacks on Indian soil. He said that Pakistan's actions were a threat to regional peace and stability.

Jaishankar's comments come at a time when India is grappling with a surge in cross-border terrorism from Pakistan. The EAM's remarks are likely to further strain the already tense relations between India and Pakistan.

What happens next?

With the diplomatic row between India and Bangladesh still unresolved, it remains to be seen how the situation will unfold. The withdrawal of the notice for Hasina's visit suggests a possible de-escalation, but the expulsion of Indian diplomats from Bangladesh has raised serious concerns about the future of the bilateral ties. Meanwhile, Jaishankar's comments on Pakistan's actions are likely to further strain the relations between the two countries.
