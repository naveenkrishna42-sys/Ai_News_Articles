3 students dead as train hits school van in Bengal's Murshidabad

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • The accident occurred when a school van was hit by a train at an unmanned level crossing in Murshidabad.
  • Three students were killed on the spot, while four others sustained critical injuries.
  • Local residents and police reached the spot for rescue; an investigation is underway.

Train Collision Kills Three Students in Murshidabad

At least three students were killed and four others critically injured on Friday morning when a train rammed into a school van in West Bengal's Murshidabad district. The accident took place at an unmanned level crossing near the Sagardighi area, local police officials said.

The van was carrying children to a local school when the speeding train struck it. The impact threw the vehicle off the tracks, leaving it mangled. Two of the deceased were students, while the third victim is yet to be formally identified. Officials have not confirmed the ages or names of the victims pending family notification.

Rescue and Hospitalisation

Local residents rushed to the spot moments after the crash and helped pull children out of the wreckage. Police and emergency teams arrived soon after. The injured were taken to a nearby hospital, where doctors described four of them as critical. The condition of the driver of the van is also under assessment.

Authorities have cordoned off the site. Traffic on the rail line was briefly halted but later resumed. Railway officials are conducting an inspection of the level crossing, which lacked any barrier or warning system.

Questions Over Unmanned Crossings

The incident has once again raised concerns about safety at unmanned railway crossings in rural Bengal. Activists have long demanded that such crossings be either manned or replaced with bridges or underpasses. The Murshidabad district administration has faced criticism for failing to act on earlier complaints about the spot.

The state's transport department has ordered an inquiry into the accident. The local police have also registered a case of negligence. The school van was reportedly a private vehicle, and its fitness and route permits are being examined.

Political Reactions and Compensation

West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee expressed grief over the deaths and announced an ex-gratia of Rs 2 lakh each to the families of the deceased. The opposition BJP demanded stricter enforcement of safety norms at level crossings and accused the state government of negligence.

Meanwhile, local education officials have suspended classes at the school that the children attended. Counsellors have been deployed to support grieving students and staff. The school management has not yet issued a statement.

The Railway Ministry has said it will provide compensation to the families under its standard accident relief norms. However, details of the payout have not been released.

Investigators are now examining whether the van driver ignored warning signs or if the train crew failed to sound the horn. The engine driver and guard of the train are being questioned.

As the families wait for answers, the debate over the safety of thousands of children who travel on such routes every day remains unresolved. Officials are expected to submit a preliminary report within 48 hours.
