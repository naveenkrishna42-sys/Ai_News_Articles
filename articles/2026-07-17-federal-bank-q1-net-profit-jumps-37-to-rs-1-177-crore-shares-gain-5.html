Federal Bank Q1 Net Profit Jumps 37% to Rs 1,177 Crore, Shares Gain 5%

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Net profit rose 37% year-on-year to Rs 1,177 crore for the June quarter.
  • Strong core income and healthy asset quality underpinned the performance.
  • Shares of the private lender climbed 5% following the earnings announcement.

Federal Bank has reported a 37% year-on-year jump in its net profit for the first quarter of the current financial year, coming in at Rs 1,177 crore. The strong bottom-line growth was driven by a rise in core income and stable asset quality metrics.

Shares of the private sector lender rose as much as 5% in morning trade on Friday after the numbers were released, reflecting investor optimism about the bank's earnings trajectory.

Core Income Drives Bottom Line

The bank's net interest income (NII), the difference between interest earned and interest paid, grew substantially during the quarter. While the exact NII figure was not disclosed in the initial statement, the lender attributed the profit surge to robust core performance.

Other income streams also contributed positively, helping the bank post a net profit of Rs 1,177 crore, up from Rs 859 crore in the same period last year.

Asset Quality Holds Steady

Federal Bank managed to keep its asset quality under control during the quarter. Gross non-performing assets (NPAs) as a percentage of total advances remained at manageable levels, though the exact ratios were not immediately available.

The bank has been focusing on maintaining a healthy loan book and has not reported any major stress in its corporate or retail portfolios. Provisions for bad loans were also kept in check, which aided profitability.

Market Reaction and Outlook

Following the earnings announcement, Federal Bank's stock gained over 5% on the BSE, outperforming the broader market. Analysts said the earnings beat expectations on both net profit and margin fronts.

The bank is also said to be preparing for a potential shift in the Reserve Bank of India's norms on loan-loss provisioning, though no specific details have been shared. Fedbank Financial, a subsidiary, meanwhile, reported a 52.5% jump in Q1 profit to Rs 114.38 crore and reiterated its target of reaching 200 branches by the end of FY27.

Investors will now watch for the bank's net interest margin trajectory and loan growth guidance in the coming quarters, as well as any regulatory impact from the anticipated RBI directive on provisioning.
