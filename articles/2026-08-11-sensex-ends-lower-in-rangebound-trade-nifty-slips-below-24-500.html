Sensex ends lower in rangebound trade; Nifty slips below 24,500

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty ended lower after a volatile, rangebound session.
  • Nifty closed below the 24,500 level.
  • Markets lacked directional cues amid mixed global signals.

Indian equity benchmarks closed in the red on Tuesday after a largely rangebound session, as investors grappled with mixed global cues and a lack of fresh domestic triggers. The Nifty ended below the 24,500 mark, while the Sensex gave up early gains to finish lower.

Rangebound trade caps upside

The session saw the indices oscillate within a narrow band, with buying interest emerging at lower levels but profit-taking capping any sustained rally. Volatility remained subdued, reflecting caution among traders ahead of key economic data points due later this week.

Market participants noted that the absence of any major domestic catalyst kept the momentum in check. Global cues were mixed, with Asian peers trading unevenly while European markets opened on a tentative note.

Sectoral performance mixed

On the sectoral front, buying was seen in select IT and pharma stocks, while metal and oil & gas names witnessed selling pressure. The broader indices — the BSE Midcap and Smallcap — also ended marginally lower, in line with the benchmarks.

Analysts said the market is in a consolidation phase after last week's rally, and a clear direction may emerge only after fresh triggers, such as domestic inflation data or global central bank commentary.

Nifty holds key support

Despite the decline, the Nifty managed to hold above its immediate support zone, which traders are closely watching. Any decisive break below this level could invite further selling, while a rebound above 24,600 would signal renewed strength.

Foreign institutional investors remained net sellers in the cash market, while domestic institutional investors provided some support. The rupee traded flat against the US dollar.

What to watch next

Traders will now keep an eye on the upcoming US inflation print and domestic macroeconomic data for directional cues. Earnings announcements from a few large-cap companies later this week could also influence market sentiment.

Until then, analysts expect the market to remain rangebound, with stock-specific action taking precedence over index-level moves.
