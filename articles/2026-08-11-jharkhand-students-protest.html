Jharkhand Students' Protest Gains Momentum

Students in Jharkhand have been protesting against police action, and the situation has taken a turn with the detention of ABVP workers. The workers were detained during a march to the Assembly, where they were demanding action against the police.

The protest has gained support from various quarters, with Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal extending his support to the students. The protest has also led to a bandh being enforced in the state by the BJP.

Clash with Police

There was a clash between the ABVP workers and the police during the march to the Assembly. The police had to detain the workers to prevent the situation from escalating. The exact number of workers detained has not been confirmed by officials.

The protest is against the police action against job aspirants in the state. The students are demanding that the government take action against the police and provide jobs to the aspirants. The situation remains tense, with the bandh affecting normal life in the state.

Political Reactions

The protest has also led to a war of words between the BJP and the Congress. The BJP has asked the Congress to take a stand on the issue, while the Congress has not yet responded. The CJP has hailed the protest spearhead, Devendra Nath Mahto, as a leader who is fighting for the rights of the students.

What Next?

The situation in Jharkhand remains tense, and it is unclear how the protest will unfold. The government has not yet responded to the demands of the students, and the bandh is expected to continue until the demands are met.

Officials have not yet confirmed what action will be taken against the police, or when the jobs will be provided to the aspirants. The students have vowed to continue the protest until their demands are met.
