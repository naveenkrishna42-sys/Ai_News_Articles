Cookie Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that let a website remember information about your visit.

Cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: required for basic site function.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand which articles and categories readers engage with.
  • Advertising cookies: set by Google AdSense and other ad partners to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads based on your browsing across sites.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect site functionality and will typically switch ads to non-personalized. To manage ad personalization specifically, visit Google Ads Settings or the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.
