Why TAP Air Portugal Miles&Go Deserves Your Attention in 2026

TAP Air Portugal's Miles&Go programme has quietly become one of the most underrated frequent flyer schemes for Indian travellers. With Portugal emerging as a popular gateway to Europe, the Americas and Africa, TAP's Lisbon hub offers convenient connections from Delhi and Mumbai. Unlike many programmes that devalue miles overnight, Miles&Go maintains a stable award chart and generous earning opportunities. This guide breaks down the 14 most effective ways to accumulate miles in 2026, with practical benchmarks and pricing context.

Earning Through Credit Cards and Transfers

1. Co-Branded TAP Credit Cards

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

TAP does not issue co-branded cards in India, but international cards like the TAP Miles&Go Mastercard from Banco Português de Gestão offer up to 2 miles per euro on TAP purchases. For Indian residents, this is less relevant unless you hold a Portuguese bank account. Instead, focus on transferable currencies.

2. American Express Membership Rewards

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Amex India allows transfers to TAP Miles&Go at a 1:1 ratio. With cards like the Platinum Charge Card earning 1 point per ₹40 spent, you effectively earn 1 mile per ₹40. During transfer bonuses (often 20-30%), this improves to 1 mile per ₹30. This is the fastest route for high spenders.

3. Marriott Bonvoy Transfers

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Marriott Bonvoy points transfer to TAP at 3:1, with a 5,000-mile bonus for every 60,000 points transferred. That translates to 25,000 miles per 60,000 points, or 1 mile per 2.4 points. Given Marriott points are valued at ₹0.80 each, you're effectively buying miles at ₹1.92—a fair deal for business class redemptions.

4. HSBC and Axis Bank Transfer Partners

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

HSBC India's travel programme and Axis Bank's Travel Edge portal both list TAP Miles&Go as a transfer partner. Axis offers 1:1 for some cards, while HSBC is 2:1. Always check current ratios, as they change quarterly.

Earning Through Flying and Partners

5. Fly TAP Air Portugal

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Earning rates depend on fare class and distance. A Delhi-Lisbon round trip in Discounted Economy earns 50% of miles flown (approx. 6,000 miles), while Business earns 200% (approx. 24,000 miles). TAP's status tiers add bonuses up to 100%.

6. Star Alliance Partner Flights

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Miles&Go members earn miles on all Star Alliance carriers, including Air India, United, Lufthansa and Singapore Airlines. The earning rate varies by partner and fare class, but generally you get 25-100% of miles flown. This is a major advantage for Indian flyers who frequently use Air India.

7. Hotel Stays

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Earn miles with hotel partners like Accor, IHG and Hilton. Typically, you earn 2-5 miles per USD spent, or you can convert hotel points to miles. For example, Accor's ALL points convert at 2:1 to TAP miles.

8. Car Rentals

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Partners like Hertz, Avis and Sixt offer 500-1,000 miles per rental. Always provide your Miles&Go number at booking.

9. Shopping Portals

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

TAP's online shopping portal (Miles&Go Shopping) offers up to 10 miles per euro at retailers like Apple, Nike and Booking.com. Indian users can access some international retailers, but shipping restrictions apply.

10. Dining Programmes

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

In Portugal, the Miles&Go Dining programme lets you earn miles at participating restaurants. Outside Portugal, this is limited.

11. Fuel and Everyday Spends

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Through partners like Galp (Portugal), you earn miles on fuel. In India, no such direct partnership exists, but you can use transferable credit card points.

12. Buy Miles

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

TAP periodically sells miles with up to 100% bonus. At 1.5 euro cents per mile (approx. ₹1.35), buying during a bonus can be cheaper than revenue tickets for premium cabins. However, avoid speculative buying.

13. Refer-a-Friend

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Refer friends to Miles&Go and earn 500 miles per successful referral, up to 5,000 miles annually. The friend must take a qualifying flight.

14. Surveys and Promotions

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

TAP runs periodic surveys and challenges through its app, offering 100-1,000 miles for completion. These are easy wins but require active monitoring.

Pros and Cons of Earning Miles&Go Miles

  • Pros: Stable award chart, Star Alliance access, generous transfer bonuses, no expiry for active members, low fuel surcharges on TAP redemptions.
  • Cons: Limited Indian co-branded cards, fewer transfer partners than KrisFlyer or Asia Miles, award availability can be sparse during peak season, call centre support not 24/7 for India.

Verdict: Is It Worth Your Effort?

For Indian travellers who fly to Europe or the Americas via Lisbon, Miles&Go offers strong value. The ability to transfer Amex points at 1:1 and redeem for TAP's excellent business class (lie-flat seats, Portuguese cuisine) makes it a compelling alternative to more crowded programmes. Focus on credit card transfers and Star Alliance flights to maximise earnings. Avoid buying miles unless you have a specific redemption in mind. With a bit of planning, 100,000 miles—enough for a round-trip business class to Europe—is achievable within a year for a moderate spender.
