Omar Abdullah Corrects JP Nadda Over J&K Paper Leak Date

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Omar Abdullah accused JP Nadda of providing the wrong date for a JKSSB paper leak.
  • Abdullah clarified the examination took place during the Lieutenant Governor's administration, not the Congress era.
  • The exchange has sparked a political row, with the opposition accusing the BJP of misleading the public.

Jammu and Kashmir Chief Minister Omar Abdullah has publicly corrected Union Minister JP Nadda over the timeline of a recent paper leak involving the Jammu and Kashmir Services Selection Board (JKSSB). The confrontation unfolded on social media and in subsequent press briefings, with Abdullah asserting that Nadda had 'got the date wrong'.

The dispute centres on a leaked examination paper that Nadda had attributed to the previous Congress-led government. Abdullah countered this claim, stating that the exam in question was conducted under the current Lieutenant Governor's administration, well after the dissolution of the state's legislative assembly.

Political Crossfire Over Exam Irregularity

Omar Abdullah did not mince words in his response. 'You got the date wrong. The paper leak happened during L-G rule, not during the Congress era,' he said, directly addressing Nadda. The Chief Minister accused the BJP of attempting to rewrite history to score political points.

The National Conference leader's remarks have been echoed by other opposition parties, who have termed Nadda's original statement a 'deliberate attempt to deceive people'. The Congress party has demanded a formal apology from the Union Minister.

BJP Hits Back at Abdullah

The BJP has not taken the criticism lightly. Party spokesperson Pradeep Bhandari fired back at Abdullah, accusing him of lying about the chronology of events. 'You are lying,' Bhandari said, insisting that the roots of the paper leak scandal trace back to administrative failures during the Congress-NC coalition.

Bhandari's defence of Nadda has only deepened the political divide. The BJP maintains that the systemic issues leading to such leaks were present long before the current administration took over. However, they have not provided concrete evidence to link the specific 2024 exam leak to the earlier period.

What Actually Happened

The JKSSB paper leak pertains to an examination for subordinate posts that was held in early 2024. Investigations by the Jammu and Kashmir Police have already led to several arrests, including that of a board official. The inquiry is ongoing, and authorities have not yet confirmed the full extent of the leak.

Officials have not confirmed whether any political figures are directly implicated. The focus remains on the administrative lapses that allowed the breach to occur.

The controversy has now become a flashpoint in the ongoing election campaign in Jammu and Kashmir. Both the BJP and the opposition are using the incident to question each other's integrity and governance record.

What to watch: The Election Commission is expected to announce a formal investigation into all paper leaks in the Union Territory. Whether this probe will assign political responsibility remains an open question.
