Standard Chartered sees losing crypto coin turning around on tokenization wave

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Standard Chartered has identified a losing crypto coin that it believes will turn around.
  • The bank attributes the potential recovery to the growing wave of asset tokenization.
  • Tokenization involves representing real-world assets as digital tokens on a blockchain.
  • The forecast comes amid broader market uncertainty for many digital currencies.

A contrarian call on a beaten-down token

Standard Chartered has thrown its weight behind a crypto coin that has been losing ground, predicting a turnaround driven by the accelerating tokenization of real-world assets. The bank's analysts see the current slump as temporary, with the underlying technology poised for broader adoption.

The coin in question has struggled against a backdrop of market volatility and waning retail interest. Yet, the lender's forecast suggests that the shift toward digitizing assets like real estate, bonds, and commodities could breathe new life into its ecosystem.

Tokenization as the catalyst

Tokenization, the process of converting physical and traditional financial assets into blockchain-based digital tokens, has been gaining traction among institutional players. Standard Chartered believes this wave will drive utility and demand for the coin, which has so far lagged its peers.

The bank's analysts point to increasing pilot projects and partnerships in the space. They argue that as more assets get tokenized, the coin could become a key settlement layer, reversing its fortunes.

Market context and skepticism

Not all market watchers are convinced. Some traders note that tokenization is still in its infancy, with regulatory hurdles and liquidity concerns unresolved. The coin's recent price action reflects that skepticism, with declines outpacing the broader market.

Still, Standard Chartered's track record on crypto calls has drawn attention. The bank has previously made bold predictions on bitcoin and ether, some of which have played out. This latest forecast is being watched closely by institutional investors.

What it means for investors

For Indian investors, the news adds another layer to the ongoing debate about crypto's viability. While the market remains risky, the involvement of a major global bank signals that digital assets are not fading into irrelevance.

Analysts advise caution, noting that even a successful turnaround may take time. The coin's fundamentals, including its development activity and network usage, will be key to validating the bank's optimism.

Standard Chartered has not released specific price targets, but the report underscores a growing institutional interest in tokenization as a use case that could outlast the current bearish cycle.

Looking ahead

The coming months will reveal whether this contrarian bet pays off. Watch for regulatory updates on tokenization and any major partnerships involving the coin's network, as these could be early signals of a recovery.
