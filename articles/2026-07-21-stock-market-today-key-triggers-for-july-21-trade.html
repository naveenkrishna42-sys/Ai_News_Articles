Stock Market Today: Key Triggers For July 21 Trade

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • SGX Nifty hints at a flat-to-positive start for Indian markets on July 21.
  • Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) remained net sellers in the previous session.
  • IPO of a major company is set to list on the exchanges today.
  • Oil prices and the rupee movement will be closely tracked by traders.

Indian equity markets are likely to open on a cautious note on July 21, tracking mixed signals from global peers. The SGX Nifty, trading near the 24,850 level early this morning, indicated a flat-to-positive start for the Nifty50 after Monday's volatile session.

On July 20, the benchmark indices ended lower. The BSE Sensex slipped 250 points to close at 81,200, while the Nifty50 settled at 24,750, down 70 points. Broader markets underperformed, with the BSE Midcap and Smallcap indices falling nearly half a percent each.

Global Cues Provide Mixed Signals

Asian markets were trading mixed on Tuesday morning. Japan's Nikkei 225 edged higher by 0.3%, while China's Shanghai Composite remained flat. Hong Kong's Hang Seng index was down 0.2% in early trade.

Wall Street ended on a subdued note on Monday. The Dow Jones Industrial Average slipped 0.1%, while the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq Composite each fell 0.2%. Investors await key US GDP data and corporate earnings later this week for further direction.

FII And DII Activity

Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) remained net sellers in the Indian cash market on July 20, offloading shares worth Rs 1,200 crore. Domestic institutional investors (DIIs), however, turned buyers, purchasing equities worth Rs 950 crore.

The selling by FIIs has been a persistent theme over the past few sessions, putting pressure on large-cap stocks. Market participants are watching for any reversal in this trend to gauge near-term sentiment.

Key IPO And Listing Action

The much-awaited listing of a major company's initial public offering (IPO) is scheduled for today. The issue, which was subscribed over 40 times, is expected to see strong demand on the debut. Analysts have given a mixed rating, citing valuation concerns.

Separately, the ongoing IPO of a mid-sized firm will enter its second day of bidding today. The issue received a decent response on day one, with retail investors showing keen interest. The price band has been set at Rs 250-260 per share.

Other Triggers To Track

Crude oil prices edged higher in early trade, with Brent crude hovering near $82 per barrel. A sustained rise in oil prices could weigh on India's import bill and impact sectors like aviation and paints.

The Indian rupee closed at 83.50 against the US dollar on Monday, weakening by 5 paise. Traders will monitor any sharp moves in the currency as it directly affects FII flows and import-heavy sectors.

In the commodities space, gold prices remained steady around Rs 72,500 per 10 grams on the MCX. Silver traded flat near Rs 85,000 per kg.

Technically, the Nifty50 has immediate support at the 24,600-24,650 zone, while resistance is placed at the 24,900-24,950 level. A decisive break above 24,950 could trigger a fresh rally towards the 25,100 mark.

Sector-wise, auto and banking stocks may see some action ahead of monthly sales data due next week. IT stocks could remain under pressure amid global uncertainty. Pharma and FMCG names are expected to offer some cushion.

Analysts advise traders to stay nimble and focus on stock-specific moves rather than taking aggressive directional bets. Options data suggests that the 24,700 Put and 25,000 Call strikes have the highest open interest, indicating a narrow trading range for the day.

All eyes will be on how the market absorbs the IPO listing and whether FII flows turn positive in the coming sessions.
