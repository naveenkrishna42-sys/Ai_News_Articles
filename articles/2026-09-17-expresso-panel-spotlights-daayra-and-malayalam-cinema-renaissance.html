The Expresso Conversation: A Panels Lineup and Theme

The recent Expresso session gathered Prithviraj Sukumaran, Kareena Kapoor, and Meghna Gulzar to dissect the remarkable performance of the Hindi-language cop-thriller *Daayra*.
Prithviraj Sukumaran** brings his extensive experience in action and crime roles, offering perspective on star-driven narratives. Kareena Kapoor** appears as *Daayra*'s lead, making a notable crossover into police-oriented cinema.
Meghna Gulzar**, behind the camera, provides unique insight into Malayalam cinema's critical qualities. This tripartite dialogue underscores the importance of regional talent entering mainstream markets and vice versa.

Daayra: A Critical Success in the Action Genre

*Daayra*, released in late September 2026, has been lauded for its gritty portrayal of moral ambiguity in law enforcement.
Kareena Kapoor** delivers a nuanced performance that balances vulnerability with determination. Prithviraj Sukumaran** shines as the seasoned detective, earning praise for his gravitas and emotional restraint.
Meghna Gulzar** crafts a complex female protagonist whose journey challenges traditional gender stereotypes. The film received mixed-to-positive reviews, with critics noting its direction and pacing as areas for improvement.

Detailed Analysis

The conversation highlighted how *Daayra* bridges regional sensibilities with pan‑Indian appeal. Prithviraj Sukumaran noted that the film’s tight screenplay allows the lead characters to inhabit morally grey zones without resorting to caricature. Kareena Kapoor emphasized the preparation she underwent to portray a police officer grappling with personal loss, stating that the role demanded both physical training and emotional introspection. Meghna Gulzar discussed her approach to visual storytelling, explaining that she chose a muted colour palette to reflect the film’s themes of duty and disillusionment.

Narrative Structure

The plot unfolds in three acts: the inciting incident that throws the protagonists into a high‑stakes investigation, a mid‑point twist that reveals systemic corruption, and a climax that forces the leads to confront their own convictions. Critics praised the non‑linear flashbacks that gradually uncover the protagonist’s backstory, though some felt the pacing lagged in the second act.

Performance Highlights

Kapoor’s portrayal balances vulnerability with steely resolve, particularly in interrogation scenes where her restraint speaks louder than dialogue. Sukumaran’s veteran detective brings a gravitas that anchors the film’s action sequences, while Gulzar’s direction ensures that each performance serves the larger moral inquiry rather than standing alone as a showcase.

Direction and Cinematography

Gulzar employed handheld cameras during chase sequences to impart immediacy, juxtaposed with steady, wide‑angle shots during moments of introspection. The sound design uses sparse, ambient tones to heighten tension, avoiding a reliance on a heavy musical score. This stylistic choice reinforces the film’s grounded realism.

Key Takeaways

  • *Daayra* demonstrates that strong regional talent can successfully headline mainstream Hindi cinema without diluting artistic integrity.
  • The film’s blend of gritty realism and character‑driven narrative offers a template for future action‑thrillers seeking critical acclaim.
  • Financially, the movie has surpassed its budget by nearly 150 %, driven by robust theatrical performance and lucrative ancillary rights.
  • Prithviraj Sukumaran’s crossover role reinforces the growing acceptance of South Indian actors in Bollywood‑led projects.
  • Kareena Kapoor’s venture into police‑centric storytelling marks a notable shift in her career trajectory, opening doors for more genre‑diverse offerings.
  • Meghna Gulzar’s direction underscores the importance of a unified visual language that supports thematic depth over stylistic excess.
