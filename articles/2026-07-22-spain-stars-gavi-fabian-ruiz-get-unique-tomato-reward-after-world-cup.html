Spain Stars Gavi, Fabian Ruiz Get Unique Tomato Reward After World Cup Win

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Gavi and Fabian Ruiz, part of Spain's World Cup-winning squad, received a unique hometown reward.
  • The reward involved tomatoes equal to the players' body weight, a local tradition in their Spanish town.
  • The celebration highlights the deep connection between the players and their community.

Tomatoes Replace Medals in Spanish Town's World Cup Tribute

When Spain won the World Cup, most of the country celebrated with parades and trophies. But for two of the team's stars, the reward came in a more literal, edible form: tomatoes.

Midfielders Gavi and Fabian Ruiz were honoured by their hometown with a tradition that has caught attention globally. Instead of medals or cash, they received tomatoes equal to their body weight.

How the Tradition Works

The ceremony took place in the players' local town, where community leaders presented the duo with crates piled high with ripe tomatoes. The amount matched each player's weight exactly.

For Gavi, who weighs around 68 kg, that meant 68 kg of tomatoes. Fabian Ruiz, slightly heavier at 74 kg, received a corresponding 74 kg. The gesture, while unusual, is rooted in local agricultural pride.

Video footage shared on social media shows the two players smiling as they pose beside the towering stacks of red produce. Neither player appeared to expect the tribute, but both took it in stride.

Why Tomatoes, Not Trophies?

The town is known for its tomato harvest, and the tradition is a way to celebrate local heroes using what the region produces best. Officials explained that the gift symbolises the sweat and hard work both players put into their careers, mirroring the labour of farmers.

"These boys made us proud on the world stage. We wanted to give them something from the heart, something that represents us," a local organiser told reporters. The tomatoes were not just symbolic—they were also meant for the players to share with their families.

The reward comes as part of a broader celebration that included a small parade and a civic reception. However, the tomato presentation became the most talked-about moment.

Reactions from Fans and Online

The unusual reward quickly went viral. Fans on social media called it "wholesome" and "unexpectedly charming." Some joked that the players would need to open a salsa business, while others contrasted the gift with the lavish bonuses often given to top athletes.

Neither Gavi nor Fabian Ruiz has publicly complained about the gift. In fact, both players are known for their grounded personalities, often returning to their hometowns during breaks. The tomato reward fits their humble image.

Spanish media covered the event with a mix of amusement and pride. One outlet noted that the tradition highlights how smaller communities celebrate success in their own way, without copying the grand gestures of big cities.

What It Says About Modern Football Celebrations

The tomato reward stands out in an era where World Cup winners often receive luxury cars, sponsorship deals, or massive cash prizes. Here, the focus was on community and agriculture.

It also reinforces the idea that for many players, hometown recognition matters as much as international fame. Gavi and Fabian Ruiz came through the Spanish youth system and have remained connected to their roots.

As for the tomatoes, local reports suggest the players donated some of them to nearby food banks. No official confirmation has been made, but if true, it adds another layer to the story.

For now, the two midfielders are back with their clubs, preparing for the new season. But they carry with them a unique memory—and possibly a lot of tomato sauce recipes.
