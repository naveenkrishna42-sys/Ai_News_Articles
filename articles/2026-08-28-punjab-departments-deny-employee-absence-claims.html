Punjab departments deny employee absence claims

📅 2026-08-28 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Punjab government denies claims of unauthorised absence
  • Government officials cite ongoing dialogue and strike standoff
  • Functioning of government schools disrupted due to employee absence
  • Civil hospital OPD closure causes hassle for patients

Punjab Government Denies Claims of Unauthorised Absence

Punjab government departments have rejected allegations of unauthorised absence among employees following the Maha Hartal on August 27, 2026. The government has cited ongoing dialogue and strike standoff as the reason for the absence, which they claim is a legitimate form of protest.

Ongoing Dialogue and Strike Standoff

Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann has stated that dialogue and strike cannot go together, emphasizing the need for a peaceful resolution to the ongoing standoff. The government has ordered action against striking staff, citing the standoff as a potential hindrance to the upcoming 2027 polls.

Impact on Government Services

The functioning of government schools has been disrupted due to the absence of employees, causing inconvenience to students and parents. Additionally, the closure of Outpatient Departments (OPDs) at civil hospitals has caused hassle for patients, with some having to reschedule their appointments.

Expert Opinion

Political analyst Rajiv Kumar from the Institute for Conflict Management and Peace Studies in Punjab believes that the government's decision to order action against striking staff is a calculated move to prevent any potential disruptions ahead of the 2027 polls. He warns that such actions could escalate the situation and lead to further unrest.

Broader Impact

The ongoing standoff and the resulting disruption in government services have broader implications for the state of Punjab. The disruption in education and healthcare services could have long-term consequences for the state's development and growth. Additionally, the escalation of the situation could lead to further political instability and unrest.

Conclusion

The Punjab government's decision to reject claims of unauthorised absence and order action against striking staff is a significant development in the ongoing standoff. The impact on government services and the broader implications for the state's development and growth cannot be overlooked. It remains to be seen how the situation unfolds and what measures will be taken to resolve the ongoing standoff peacefully.
