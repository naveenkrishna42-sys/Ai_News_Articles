CBFC clears Awarapan 2 with U/A 16+ and minimal cuts; runtime revealed

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • CBFC has passed Awarapan 2 with a U/A 16+ certificate after 9 edits.
  • The film's runtime and the nature of modifications have been revealed.
  • Emraan Hashmi has denied any delay, confirming the release is on track.

The Central Board of Film Certification (CBFC) has cleared Emraan Hashmi's much-anticipated Awarapan 2 with a U/A 16+ certificate, following a modest round of edits. The certification process, which has been a talking point among fans, concluded with the board mandating only nine modifications — a relatively light touch for a film that carries the weight of its 2007 predecessor.

According to details emerging from the certification process, the film's runtime stands at a crisp two hours and ten minutes. The nine edits are understood to be minor in nature, likely addressing language and a few intense sequences, though the board's specific reasoning for each cut has not been officially detailed. The U/A 16+ tag means viewers under 16 will need parental guidance, a standard classification for films with mature themes.

What the cuts entail

The exact nature of the nine modifications has not been made public by the CBFC, but industry insiders suggest they are limited to trimming a few dialogues and shortening some violent passages. This is a far cry from the heavy scissors that often accompany action thrillers, and it signals that the filmmakers have largely got what they wanted.

Bollywood Hungama, which first reported the certification details, noted that the cuts are minimal and do not affect the film's core narrative. The production team is said to be relieved that the film escaped with its integrity intact, allowing director Mohit Suri's vision to reach audiences without significant compromise.

Emraan Hashmi addresses delay rumours

Amid the certification news, Emraan Hashmi has broken his silence on swirling release date rumours. Speculation had been rife that Awarapan 2 might be pushed back, but the actor has categorically denied any such delay. In a recent interaction, he assured fans that the film is on schedule, putting to rest concerns that had been building on social media.

The actor's comments come at a time when the film's marketing is expected to kick into high gear. With the CBFC clearance now in hand, the team can proceed with finalising promotional material and theatrical bookings without further hiccups.

The prequel twist and Disha Patani's role

Adding to the intrigue, reports have surfaced that Awarapan is set to return to theatres 'as a prequel', a narrative choice that has piqued curiosity. Emraan Hashmi has also teased Disha Patani's secret skill in the film, though he has been characteristically tight-lipped about specifics. These revelations have only amplified the buzz around the project.

Patani, who joins the franchise in a pivotal role, is expected to bring a fresh dynamic to the story. Her pairing with Hashmi, a veteran of the genre, is being touted as one of the film's major attractions.

What this means for the release

With the CBFC certificate in hand and the runtime locked, the path is clear for Awarapan 2 to hit screens as planned. The minimal cuts suggest that the film's gritty tone has been preserved, which should please fans of the original. The U/A 16+ rating also allows for a wider audience than an 'A' certificate would, potentially boosting its box office prospects.

The film's release strategy will now hinge on the final marketing push, with the team expected to unveil a trailer and promotional songs in the coming weeks. Trade analysts will be watching closely to see how the sequel performs against a crowded release calendar.

As the release date approaches, all eyes will be on how the film's prequel angle is woven into the narrative, and whether it can recapture the magic of the original. For now, the certification news has removed a major hurdle, and the team can focus on what matters most — delivering a compelling cinematic experience.
