India urges global action on Pakistan's 'ruthless' PoK crackdown

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • India condemns Pakistan's crackdown on peaceful protesters in PoK as 'ruthless'.
  • New Delhi calls for global scrutiny of Islamabad's actions in the region.
  • Allegations that 'Pak-trained Mujahideen have turned their guns inwards'.
  • Protesters have been branded 'enemies' by Pakistani authorities.

India's sharp rebuke

New Delhi has issued a strong condemnation of Pakistan's handling of protests in Pakistan-occupied Kashmir (PoK), calling the crackdown on civilians 'ruthless' and demanding international attention. The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) on Friday accused Islamabad of using excessive force against peaceful demonstrators, a move that has escalated tensions between the two nuclear-armed neighbours.

The statement from India comes amid reports of widespread arrests and violence in the region, where locals have been protesting against alleged administrative and political injustices. India has consistently maintained that PoK is an integral part of Indian territory, and New Delhi's latest remarks seek to spotlight what it describes as Islamabad's 'double standards' on self-determination.

'Mujahideen turned their guns inwards'

In a pointed attack, India alleged that 'Pak-trained Mujahideen have turned their guns inwards', suggesting that elements once used by Pakistan to foment unrest in Kashmir are now being deployed against its own citizens. The phrase, used by Indian officials, underscores a narrative that Islamabad's policies are backfiring, with violence once directed at India now being used to suppress dissent within PoK.

Officials in New Delhi have not provided specific evidence to support this claim, but they have urged the global community to scrutinise Pakistan's actions. The Indian government has called for an independent inquiry into the crackdown, arguing that the treatment of protesters in PoK is a matter of international concern, not just a bilateral issue.

Protesters branded 'enemies'

Reports from the region indicate that Pakistani authorities have labelled protesters as 'enemies of the state', a characterisation that India says is part of a broader pattern of repression. The MEA's statement highlighted the irony of Pakistan's rhetoric on Kashmir while it suppresses similar demands for rights in PoK.

Local sources, though difficult to verify independently, have described a heavy security presence in towns across PoK, with curfews imposed and communication lines disrupted. The Indian government has expressed solidarity with the protesters, framing their struggle as part of the larger Kashmir issue that the United Nations has long recognised.

Global community urged to act

India has not specified what form of global scrutiny it expects, but diplomatic observers suggest New Delhi may push for discussions at international forums, including the UN. The timing of the statement, ahead of any potential bilateral engagement, signals that India is unwilling to let the matter rest quietly.

Pakistan has not yet responded officially to India's latest salvo. However, Islamabad has historically dismissed Indian statements on PoK as interference in its internal affairs, a position that is likely to be reiterated in the coming days. The lack of a direct line of communication between the two countries complicates any immediate de-escalation.

Regional implications

The crackdown in PoK has drawn attention not just from India but also from analysts who study the region's fragile politics. The situation threatens to destabilise an already volatile area, with potential spillover effects on the wider Kashmir conflict. India's insistence on global scrutiny could also strain Pakistan's diplomatic standing among nations that have traditionally backed its position on Kashmir.

Meanwhile, human rights organisations have echoed concerns about the use of force against civilians. While no official casualty figures have been released, unconfirmed reports suggest injuries among protesters have occurred. The lack of verified data makes it difficult to assess the full scale of the crackdown, but the international community's response will be closely watched.

What to watch

As the situation develops, all eyes will be on Pakistan's next move and whether it heeds calls for restraint. India's push for global scrutiny is unlikely to yield immediate results, but it has succeeded in placing PoK back on the international agenda. The coming weeks will reveal if this diplomatic offensive translates into concrete action or remains a rhetorical standoff.
