AWS Sets Up Builder Lofts in Hyderabad, Berlin and Sao Paulo

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • AWS announces new Builder Lofts in Hyderabad, Berlin and Sao Paulo
  • Builder Lofts offer collaborative spaces for developers and startups
  • Hyderabad emerges as a key destination for global tech investments
  • Expansion reflects growing demand for cloud skills worldwide

AWS Expands Developer Spaces to Three Continents

Amazon Web Services (AWS) has announced plans to establish Builder Lofts in Hyderabad, Berlin and Sao Paulo, marking a significant expansion of its developer engagement initiative. The cloud computing giant confirmed the three-city rollout without specifying exact timelines for each location.

Builder Lofts are collaborative workspaces designed to support developers, startups and technology enthusiasts. They offer access to AWS resources, training programmes and networking opportunities.

Hyderabad's Growing Tech Stature

Hyderabad's inclusion in the list highlights the city's emergence as a major technology hub in India. The city already hosts major IT companies and has seen substantial investments in cloud infrastructure over recent years.

Local technology leaders have welcomed the development, noting that such initiatives help bridge the gap between theoretical learning and practical cloud adoption. The facility is expected to serve the growing community of developers in Telangana and neighbouring states.

Global Developer Strategy

Alongside Hyderabad, AWS has chosen Berlin and Sao Paulo for the expansion. Berlin represents Europe's thriving startup ecosystem, while Sao Paulo anchors AWS's presence in Latin America. The selection of cities across three continents suggests a deliberate strategy to support developer communities in emerging tech markets.

Builder Lofts typically provide co-working spaces, technical workshops and access to AWS experts. They serve as community hubs where developers can collaborate on projects and build cloud skills.

Implications for Local Ecosystems

Technology observers note that such initiatives complement government efforts to promote digital skills. Hyderabad's IT corridor has been expanding steadily, with multiple global companies establishing or expanding their India operations in the city.

The arrival of a Builder Loft could accelerate cloud adoption among small and medium enterprises in the region. These businesses often face challenges in accessing training and technical guidance for cloud technologies.

What Lies Ahead

AWS has not yet announced specific dates for the opening of each Builder Loft or the programmes they will offer. The company is expected to share more details in the coming months as planning progresses. Industry watchers will be tracking whether Hyderabad receives its facility ahead of or alongside the other two cities.
