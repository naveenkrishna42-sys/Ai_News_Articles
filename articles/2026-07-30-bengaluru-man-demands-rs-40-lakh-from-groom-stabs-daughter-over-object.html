Bengaluru Man Demands Rs 40 Lakh From Groom, Stabs Daughter Over Objection

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • The accused is a 55-year-old man in Bengaluru.
  • He allegedly demanded Rs 40 lakh from his daughter's fiancé.
  • When she objected, he stabbed her multiple times.
  • The woman is hospitalised; police have arrested the father.

A 55-year-old man in Bengaluru has been arrested for allegedly stabbing his 28-year-old daughter multiple times after she opposed his demand for Rs 40 lakh from her would-be husband. The incident, which took place on Wednesday evening, has sent shockwaves through the city's residential neighbourhoods.

Argument Over Dowry Escalates

Police sources said the father had been pressuring his daughter for months to extract a large sum from her fiancé's family. The daughter, a software professional, had repeatedly refused, calling the demand illegal and unethical. On Wednesday, the argument turned violent.

Witnesses told investigators that the father picked up a kitchen knife during a heated exchange. The daughter tried to calm him down, but he allegedly stabbed her in the chest and abdomen. Neighbours heard her screams and rushed her to a nearby hospital.

Victim in Stable Condition

Doctors at the hospital said the woman underwent emergency surgery and is now in stable condition. They added that she suffered deep wounds but is expected to recover fully. Her fiancé has visited her and is cooperating with the police.

Police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including attempt to murder. The accused has been remanded to judicial custody. Officials said they are also probing whether the father had a history of domestic violence.

Dowry Demand a Red Flag

The case has reignited conversations about dowry, a practice that remains illegal in India but persists in many communities. Activists pointed out that the demand for Rs 40 lakh is unusually high, even by local standards. The woman's fiancé had earlier expressed willingness to marry her without any dowry, but the father insisted.

“This is a clear case of greed and patriarchal control,” said a local women's rights activist who works with the police. “The daughter stood up for her rights, and she paid the price. We need stricter enforcement of anti-dowry laws.”

Police Investigation Underway

The police have recorded statements from the victim, her fiancé, and neighbours. They are also checking if the father had made similar demands in the past. The accused, who works as a private contractor, has no prior criminal record.

Senior officers said they are taking the case seriously. “We are gathering all evidence and will ensure a strong chargesheet,” a police spokesperson said. “Dowry harassment remains a crime, and we will not tolerate any violence against women.”

The victim's mother has been staying with relatives since the incident. She told reporters she was unaware of the extent of her husband's demands and expressed shock at the attack.

What Happens Next

The accused will be produced in court for a routine remand hearing on Monday. The victim is expected to be discharged from the hospital in a week. Police are also considering counselling for the family to prevent further trauma.
