Poise Under Pressure: Kaziah Liz Mejo Crowned Miss Universe India 2026

In a landmark victory, Kaziah Liz Mejo, an Indian-Omanese Muslim, was crowned Miss Universe India 2026, making history as the first contestant of her background to win the prestigious title. The 23-year-old, who hails from Abu Dhabi, is an alumna of St. Joseph's School Abu Dhabi. Her win has been met with widespread celebrations and pride, not just in the UAE, but also back home in India.

Mejo's journey to the crown was not without its challenges. She had earlier faced criticism for her decision to wear a hijab during the pageant, But the young trailblazer stood her ground, advocating for her right to express her religious identity. Her resilience and poise under pressure have been widely praised, with many lauding her as a symbol of diversity and inclusion.

St. Joseph's School Abu Dhabi Proclaims Pride in Alumna's Achievement

The news of Mejo's victory has brought immense joy to her alma mater, St. Joseph's School Abu Dhabi. The school took to social media to express its pride, posting a congratulatory message that read, "We are incredibly proud of our former student, Kaziah Liz Mejo, for being crowned Miss Universe India 2026. Her achievement is a testament to her hard work, dedication, and resilience."

Controversy Over Judging Criteria and Representation

While Mejo's win has been celebrated by many, it has also sparked controversy.
