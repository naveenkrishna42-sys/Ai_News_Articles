Ali Fazal explains Mirzapur's crossover with the movie

Actor Ali Fazal has clarified how the Mirzapur series connects to the upcoming film, urging fans to watch only the first six episodes of the series.

Fans advised to watch only the first six episodes of the series

Fazal, who plays the role of Kaleen Bhaiya in the series, has stated that the story of the film begins from episode six. He advised fans to watch the series up to episode six to fully understand the connection between the series and the film.

The story of the film begins from episode six of the series

Fazal explained that the first five episodes of the series set the stage for the film, introducing key characters and establishing the backdrop of the story. From episode six onwards, the narrative shifts to the film, with new characters and plotlines introduced.

Fans react to Fazal's clarification

Fans of the series have reacted positively to Fazal's clarification, with many expressing excitement about the upcoming film. Some have also praised the series for its complex storytelling and character development.

What happens next?

With the release of the Mirzapur film fast approaching, fans are eagerly awaiting Fazal's clarification on the series-movie connection. The film is expected to build on the rich tapestry of characters and storylines introduced in the series, promising a thrilling and engaging cinematic experience. As the countdown to the film's release begins, the anticipation among fans continues to grow, with many eagerly awaiting the opportunity to witness the culmination of the Mirzapur saga on the big screen.
