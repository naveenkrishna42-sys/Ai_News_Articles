Thailand school shooting: Teen kills 7 near Bangkok, officials say

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • At least seven people killed, more than 30 injured in shooting at a high school and home outside Bangkok
  • Teenage suspect reportedly shot himself after the rampage
  • Officials have not yet confirmed a motive or the suspect's identity
  • Video showed students ducking under desks as gunshots rang out
  • Investigation underway; authorities have not released further details

Attack unfolds across two locations

A teenage student opened fire at a high school and a nearby home outside Bangkok on Friday, killing at least seven people and wounding more than 30, according to officials. The suspect, a student at the school, later shot himself, authorities said.

The incident began at a residence before moving to the school grounds, though the exact sequence of events remains unclear. Police have cordoned off both sites, and emergency responders were seen transporting the injured to local hospitals.

Officials have not yet confirmed the suspect's identity or a possible motive. The attack has sent shockwaves through the community, a short drive from Thailand's capital.

Students describe terror in classrooms

Video footage circulating online showed students crouching under desks as loud gunshots echoed through hallways. In one clip, a teacher can be heard urging pupils to stay low and remain silent.

Witnesses described chaos as students fled the premises, with some climbing over fences to escape. Local media reported that the gunman was armed with a handgun, though police have not officially confirmed the weapon used.

The death toll of at least seven includes victims at both the home and the school, according to officials. More than 30 others were injured, with several in critical condition at nearby medical facilities.

Officials respond, investigation begins

Thailand's national police chief has arrived at the scene to oversee the investigation. Authorities are examining the suspect's background, including any history of disciplinary issues or mental health concerns, though no findings have been made public.

School shootings are rare in Thailand, making this attack particularly jarring for the nation. The last major incident of this kind occurred in 2022, when a former police officer killed dozens in a daycare centre in the northeastern province of Nong Bua Lamphu.

Government spokespersons have offered condolences to the families of the victims but have not yet announced policy responses. Questions are already emerging about school security protocols and access to firearms.

What happens next

Police are expected to release more details in the coming hours, including the suspect's name and the full list of casualties. The injured remain the immediate priority, with hospitals on alert for additional admissions.

Investigators will also look into how the teenager obtained the weapon and whether any warning signs were missed. For now, the community near Bangkok is left to grapple with a tragedy that has upended a quiet school day.
