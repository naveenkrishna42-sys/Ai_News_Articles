Overview of Medigap in 2026

Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policies continue to fill the gaps left by Original Medicare, covering deductibles, copayments and foreign travel emergency costs. In 2026 the standardised plans (A through N) remain unchanged, but insurers adjust pricing, underwriting rules and added services annually. Buyers should focus on premium stability, financial strength and any value‑added benefits when comparing carriers.

Top Medigap Providers Evaluated

1. UnitedHealthcare

UnitedHealthcare leads the market with the lowest average monthly premium for Plan G, reported at approximately $152 in 2026. The carrier holds an AM Best A+ (Superior) rating and offers a nationwide network of providers. Additional perks include a 24/7 nurse line and access to UnitedHealthcare’s wellness portal at no extra cost.

2. Humana

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Humana’s Plan G averages $158 per month, positioning it just above UnitedHealthcare. The company also maintains an AM Best A+ rating and provides a fitness program called SilverSneakers included with most Medigap policies. Humana’s underwriting is slightly more lenient for applicants with controlled chronic conditions.

3. Mutual of Omaha

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Mutual of Omaha offers Plan G at an average of $165 per month and carries an AM Best A (Excellent) rating. The insurer distinguishes itself with a return‑of‑premium rider available on select policies and a robust customer service reputation reflected in low complaint ratios with state insurance departments.

4. Cigna

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Cigna’s Plan G premium averages $170 per month, backed by an AM Best A rating. Cigna adds a telehealth allowance of up to 12 virtual visits per year and a prescription discount program that can reduce out‑of‑pocket drug costs for Medigap holders.

5. Blue Cross Blue Shield (State Affiliates)

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Blue Cross Blue Shield operates through independent state affiliates, resulting in premium variation. In 2026, the average Plan G premium across reporting affiliates ranges from $160 in the Midwest to $190 in the Northeast. Most affiliates hold an AM Best A or A+ rating and offer localised wellness initiatives, such as community health screenings.

Pros and Cons of Leading Medigap Carriers

  • UnitedHealthcare – lowest premiums, strong national network, limited state‑specific perks.
  • Humana – competitive pricing, included fitness program, slightly higher premium than UnitedHealthcare.
  • Mutual of Omaha – excellent customer service, optional return‑of‑premium rider, marginally higher cost.
  • Cigna – robust telehealth benefits, solid financial rating, premium at the upper‑mid tier.
  • Blue Cross Blue Shield – highly localised service, variable pricing requires state‑level research.
