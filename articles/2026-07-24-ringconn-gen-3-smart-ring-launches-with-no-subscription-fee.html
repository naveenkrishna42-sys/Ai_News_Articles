RingConn Gen 3 Smart Ring Launches with No Subscription Fee

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • RingConn Gen 3 offers all health tracking features without a monthly subscription.
  • The ring includes upgraded sensors for sleep, heart rate, and stress monitoring.
  • Battery life is improved over the previous generation, lasting up to a week on a charge.

RingConn Gen 3 Breaks the Subscription Mold

RingConn has officially launched the Gen 3 smart ring, and its biggest selling point is clear: no monthly fee. While competitors like Oura charge a subscription to unlock detailed health insights, RingConn is keeping all features free. The move could shift the smart ring market, which has increasingly leaned on recurring revenue models.

The Gen 3 arrives as the smart ring category heats up. Samsung recently entered the space with the Galaxy Ring, and Amazfit has been expanding its lineup. But RingConn is betting that buyers want a one-time purchase without worrying about a recurring bill.

What's New in the Gen 3

The RingConn Gen 3 packs upgraded sensors for sleep tracking, heart rate monitoring, stress detection, and blood oxygen measurement. The company claims improved accuracy compared to the Gen 2, with better algorithms for sleep stage analysis. The ring also tracks activity and step count throughout the day.

Battery life has been extended to around seven days on a single charge, up from roughly five days on the previous model. The charging case provides additional top-ups when you are on the go. The ring is water-resistant up to 100 metres, making it suitable for swimming and showering.

Design and Fit

The Gen 3 retains the lightweight titanium build of its predecessor, available in several finishes including black, silver, and rose gold. It comes in sizes 5 to 13. The ring is thinner than many rivals, which should help with comfort during sleep.

RingConn has not yet confirmed pricing for the Indian market, but the global price is expected to remain competitive with the Gen 2, which retailed around Rs 25,000. The company sells directly through its website and select e-commerce platforms.

Software Without a Paywall

The companion app displays all health metrics without locking anything behind a paywall. Users get daily readiness scores, sleep quality breakdowns, stress trends, and HRV analysis. The app also offers guided breathing exercises and activity summaries.

RingConn is positioning this as a long-term advantage. While other brands may offer a free period followed by a subscription, the Gen 3 promises no such surprises. The app will continue to receive updates without additional cost.

How It Stacks Up

The Oura Ring Gen 3 still leads in sleep tracking accuracy and has a larger body of research backing its algorithms. But Oura charges about $6 a month (around Rs 500) for detailed insights after a free trial. The Galaxy Ring requires a Samsung phone for full functionality and also pushes users toward Samsung Health's premium features.

For buyers who want a simple, no-strings-attached health tracker, the RingConn Gen 3 is a strong contender. The lack of subscription is a clear differentiator, especially for price-conscious users in India.

What to Watch

RingConn will likely face supply constraints in the early weeks, as demand for subscription-free smart rings has been high. The company is expected to begin shipping in India by late August. Reviews from early testers will reveal whether the sensor accuracy holds up against pricier rivals.
