Onam Sadhya 2026: Top places in Mumbai and Delhi NCR to feast

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Restaurants in Mumbai and Delhi NCR are offering special Onam Sadhya menus for the festival.
  • Traditional Kerala-style meals are served on banana leaves with multiple vegetarian courses.
  • Advance reservations are recommended as seats fill quickly during the Onam week.

A Festival of Feasts

Onam, the harvest festival of Kerala, is a time of colour, dance, and above all, food. For Malayalis living in Mumbai and Delhi NCR, the festival brings a wave of nostalgia, and the quest for an authentic Sadhya becomes a priority. This year, several restaurants and catering services in both cities are rolling out elaborate spreads to capture the spirit of Thiruvonam.

The Sadhya, a traditional vegetarian meal served on a banana leaf, is more than just lunch. It is a ritual of flavours—sweet, sour, salty, and spicy—that unfolds in a specific order. For those away from home, these curated experiences offer a taste of Kerala's culinary heritage without the need to travel south.

Mumbai's Malayali Table

In Mumbai, the demand for Onam Sadhya has grown steadily over the years. Restaurants across the city, from Fort to Bandra, are preparing multi-course meals that include classics like avial, thoran, sambar, and the ever-popular payasam. Many of these establishments source ingredients like coconut, curry leaves, and raw bananas directly from Kerala to maintain authenticity.

Some venues are going a step further by recreating the full traditional setting—banana leaves, seated dining, and even the customary lighting of lamps. Food bloggers and community groups have already started sharing lists of recommended spots, with many suggesting that early bookings are essential. Last year, several popular outlets were fully reserved days in advance.

Delhi NCR's Growing Appetite

The national capital region has also seen a rise in South Indian fine dining options, and Onam is now a key date on their calendar. Hotels in Delhi and Gurugram are hosting special Sadhya brunches, while standalone Kerala-style restaurants are offering limited-period menus. These are not just meals; they are cultural events, often accompanied by traditional music or performances.

For those who prefer to celebrate at home, several cloud kitchens and caterers in Noida and Ghaziabad are delivering Sadhya boxes. These come with pre-arranged banana leaves and portioned dishes, making it easier for families to host their own Onam lunch. The price points vary, but most packages are designed for groups of four to six people.

What to Expect on the Leaf

A typical Sadhya includes over 20 items, though some restaurants offer a condensed version. Rice is the centrepiece, served with parippu (dal), sambar, rasam, and buttermilk. Side dishes range from the crunchy pachadi to the spicy kalan, and the meal ends with multiple varieties of payasam, a sweet pudding made with milk, jaggery, and either rice or lentils.

Purists insist that the meal must be eaten with the right hand, and that no cutlery should touch the banana leaf. While not all restaurants enforce this, many encourage diners to follow the traditional method for the full experience. The order of serving is also significant, with each dish placed at a specific position on the leaf.

Plan Ahead for a Seamless Feast

Given the popularity of these special menus, experts advise confirming details in advance. Check whether the restaurant offers a fixed-price thali or à la carte options, and clarify if taxes and service charges are included. Some venues also offer Jain versions, which omit onion and garlic, so it is worth asking if that is a requirement.

For those looking for a more private celebration, several chefs in both cities are offering home-chef services, where a professional travels to your residence and sets up the entire Sadhya. This is a growing trend, especially among younger Malayalis who want the tradition but lack the time to cook.

As Onam approaches, the culinary buzz is only expected to grow. Whether you choose a grand hotel spread or a humble local eatery, the essence of the festival lies in the sharing of food. With reservations opening soon, the wise move is to book your spot now to avoid disappointment.

The coming weeks will see more restaurants announce their Onam specials, so keep an eye on local listings and social media pages for the latest updates.
