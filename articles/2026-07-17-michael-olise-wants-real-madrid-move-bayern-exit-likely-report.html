Michael Olise wants Real Madrid move, Bayern exit likely: report

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Michael Olise has reportedly communicated his final decision to join Real Madrid this summer.
  • Bayern Munich may be forced to sell the winger, who has been linked with Liverpool as a contingency.
  • Real Madrid's interest has been consistent, with Olise preferring a move to the Santiago Bernabéu.

Michael Olise has reportedly told Bayern Munich he wants to leave the club this summer, with Real Madrid his preferred destination. The winger's decision has sent ripples through the transfer market, as European giants scramble for his signature.

Olise's final decision

According to reports from Managing Madrid and Goal.com, the 24-year-old has communicated his final decision to Bayern Munich. Olise wants to join Real Madrid, the club he has reportedly favoured for some time.

The French-born winger, who also qualifies to represent England and Algeria, moved to Bayern from Crystal Palace in 2024. His time in the Bundesliga has been marked by flashes of brilliance, but he now appears ready for a new challenge.

Bayern Munich's dilemma

Bayern Munich are now faced with a difficult situation. Olise's desire to leave could force the club to enter negotiations, despite his contract running until 2029.

A former Bayern player has thrown shade at Bundesliga clubs over the transfer sweepstakes, suggesting the league cannot compete with Real Madrid's pull. The comment has added fuel to the ongoing speculation around Olise's future.

Liverpool's contingency plan

Liverpool have been monitoring the situation closely. Reports from the Liverpool Echo suggest the Merseyside club have a contingency plan in place if Olise moves to Madrid.

The Reds are also reportedly ready to move for Brazil's Rayan as an alternative. This indicates Liverpool are prepared for a summer without Olise, should Real Madrid seal the deal.

What Olise brings to the table

Olise is known for his creativity, dribbling, and ability to play across the frontline. At Crystal Palace, he was a key figure before his move to Germany.

His style of play suits Real Madrid's attacking philosophy. The club's need for a dynamic winger has been evident, and Olise fits that profile.

Bayern Munich have not yet issued an official statement on the matter. The coming weeks will be crucial as the transfer window heats up.

All eyes are on Real Madrid and Bayern Munich as they navigate this potential transfer. If Olise gets his wish, the Santiago Bernabéu could be his new home before the season starts.
