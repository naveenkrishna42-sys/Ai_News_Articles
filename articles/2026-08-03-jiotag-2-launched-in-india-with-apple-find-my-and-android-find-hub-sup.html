JioTag 2 Launched in India with Apple Find My and Android Find Hub Support

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • JioTag 2 is priced at Rs 1,249 in India.
  • Supports both Apple Find My and Android Find Hub networks.
  • Battery life is claimed to last up to two years.

Reliance Jio has launched the JioTag 2 in India, a Bluetooth tracker that works with both Apple's Find My network and Google's Find Hub for Android devices. Priced at Rs 1,249, the device is aimed at users who want to keep track of their belongings — keys, wallets, bags, or even luggage — without being tied to a single smartphone ecosystem.

The tracker is now available in the Indian market, and its dual compatibility sets it apart from many rivals that typically support only one platform. With Apple's Find My integration, iPhone users can locate their tagged items through the Find My app, while Android users can rely on the Find Hub network, which is Google's equivalent service.

Price and Availability

JioTag 2 comes with a price tag of Rs 1,249, which roughly translates to about $13 in international terms. This positions it as an affordable option in the growing segment of Bluetooth trackers in India. The device is expected to be sold through Jio's online and offline retail channels, though the company has not yet detailed all available purchase points.

The launch comes at a time when smart trackers are gaining traction among Indian consumers, especially those who frequently travel or misplace everyday items. The sub-Rs 1,500 price bracket makes it a competitive choice against offerings from global brands.

Battery and Build

One of the standout features of the JioTag 2 is its battery life. The company claims the tracker can last up to two years on a single charge, which is notable for a device of this size. This long battery life reduces the hassle of frequent recharging, a common pain point with many smart trackers.

The device is designed to be compact and lightweight, making it easy to attach to various personal items. However, Jio has not yet disclosed the exact dimensions or weight, nor has it confirmed whether the battery is replaceable or built-in. More details are expected in the coming weeks.

Platform Compatibility

The JioTag 2's support for both Apple Find My and Android Find Hub is its key selling point. For iPhone users, the tracker will appear in the Find My app, allowing them to see its last known location on a map. Android users, on the other hand, can use the Find Hub app or the built-in Find My Device feature to track their items.

This cross-platform approach is uncommon in the budget tracker segment, where most devices are optimised for one ecosystem. It also means that a household with mixed phone usage — some family members on iPhones, others on Android — can all use the same tracker without compatibility issues.

What to Expect Next

While the JioTag 2 is now launched, the company has not yet announced any bundled accessories or additional variants. Industry watchers will be keen to see if Jio follows up with a pro version or a multi-pack offering, which is common in this category. For now, the tracker is available at a competitive price, and its dual-network support could make it a popular choice among Indian consumers looking for a reliable, budget-friendly tracking solution.

As the device reaches retail shelves, user reviews will likely shed more light on its real-world performance, especially the accuracy of its location tracking and the effectiveness of the Find Hub integration. The coming months will reveal whether the JioTag 2 can carve out a niche in a market that is slowly warming up to smart trackers.
