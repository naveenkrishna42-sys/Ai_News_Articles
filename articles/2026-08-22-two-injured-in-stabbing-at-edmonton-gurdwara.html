Two Injured in Stabbing at Edmonton Gurdwara

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 27-year-old suspect is in police custody following an attack at Nanaksar Gurdwara.
  • Two victims sustained injuries during the incident.
  • Canadian Prime Minister Carney has publicly condemned the violence.
  • The suspect had reportedly been released from prison just one day before the event.

Violent Incident at Nanaksar Gurdwara

Two people are recovering following a stabbing attack at the Nanaksar Gurdwara in Edmonton, Canada. Local law enforcement confirmed that officers responded to reports of a violent altercation at the temple premises early Saturday morning.

Emergency services arrived at the scene shortly after the call to provide medical assistance to the injured victims. While details regarding the severity of their wounds remain limited, authorities have confirmed that the individuals are currently receiving necessary care.

Suspect Apprehended by Authorities

Police have identified and detained a 27-year-old male suspect in connection with the stabbing. Preliminary investigations suggest the man had been released from jail only one day prior to the assault.

Officers secured the perimeter of the gurdwara to conduct a thorough forensic investigation. The site remains a focal point for local law enforcement as they piece together the sequence of events that led to the breach of security at the religious institution.

Official Response and Condemnation

The attack has drawn swift condemnation from the highest levels of government. Prime Minister Carney described the incident as an appalling act of violence, emphasizing that such conduct has no place in Canadian society.

Community leaders and various Sikh organizations have also expressed deep concern regarding the safety of the congregation. They are calling for increased vigilance and support for those affected by the trauma of the morning's events.

Ongoing Security Concerns

  • Police are continuing to interview witnesses present at the scene.
  • The motive behind the attack is currently under official review.
  • Heightened security measures are expected to remain in place around the facility for the coming days.

The investigation is currently active as officials work to determine the specific circumstances surrounding the suspect's movements following his recent release from custody. Further updates are expected as the police complete their initial report.
