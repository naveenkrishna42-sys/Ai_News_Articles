Senate Republicans Unveil Revised CLARITY Act for Crypto Oversight

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • The revised CLARITY Act proposes new definitions for digital assets and securities.
  • The bill aims to establish clearer jurisdiction between the SEC and CFTC.
  • Industry stakeholders are reviewing the updated text for its potential impact on crypto markets.

Revised Crypto Bill Enters Senate Discussion

Senate Republicans have released an updated version of the CLARITY Act, a bill designed to establish a regulatory framework for digital assets. The revised text aims to address earlier criticisms and provide clearer guidelines for the crypto industry.

The legislation, first introduced in a previous session, has been reworked to better define what constitutes a digital asset and when it should be treated as a security. Lawmakers behind the bill say the goal is to reduce regulatory confusion that has hampered innovation.

Key Provisions in the Updated Text

The updated CLARITY Act proposes a more detailed classification system for cryptocurrencies. It draws a line between digital commodities, which would fall under the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), and digital securities, which would be overseen by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).

The bill also includes provisions for market registration and disclosure requirements for crypto exchanges. These measures are intended to protect retail investors while allowing the industry room to grow.

Senate aides familiar with the drafting process say the revised language incorporates feedback from both industry groups and regulatory agencies. The goal is to strike a balance between consumer protection and fostering a competitive digital asset market.

Industry Reaction and Next Steps

Reactions from the crypto sector have been mixed. Some industry advocates have praised the bill for attempting to create legal certainty, especially around the question of whether a token is a security. Others have expressed caution, noting that the devil is in the details.

One point of contention remains the bill's treatment of decentralised finance (DeFi) protocols. Critics argue that the current draft may impose overly burdensome compliance requirements on projects that lack a central operator.

The bill has been referred to the Senate Banking Committee, where hearings are expected in the coming weeks. Its path to passage remains uncertain, given the divided nature of the current Congress.

What to Watch

Market observers are closely tracking the bill's progress through committee. Any significant amendments could shift the regulatory landscape for cryptocurrencies in the United States. The outcome of the Senate Banking Committee vote will be the next major milestone.
