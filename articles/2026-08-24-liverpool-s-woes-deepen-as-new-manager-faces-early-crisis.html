Liverpool's early-season struggles raise concerns about new manager

Liverpool's season opener against Newcastle has left fans and pundits alike questioning the effectiveness of new manager Andoni Iraola. The 2-2 draw saw key players Florian Wirtz and Alexander Isak struggle, with both failing to make a significant impact on the game. This performance has raised serious doubts about whether Iraola can turn around Liverpool's fortunes.

Key players fail to deliver, highlighting deeper issues

Wirtz, Liverpool's star signing from Borussia Dortmund, was largely ineffective, failing to influence the game in either half. Isak, who joined from Newcastle last summer, also struggled to make an impact, with his lack of involvement in the attacking play a significant concern. These performances suggest that Liverpool's problems may not be solely down to personnel but could be indicative of a more systemic issue within the team.

Managerial challenges and defensive vulnerabilities

Iraola's early struggles are not just about individual performances but also about the team's overall approach. Comparisons with previous Liverpool managers, particularly Arne Slot, have been inevitable. Slot's tenure was marked by a similar struggle to find consistency and form. The pressure on Iraola is immense, and the early-season results suggest that he may face a tough challenge to prove his worth. Looking ahead: What does this mean for Liverpool's season?
