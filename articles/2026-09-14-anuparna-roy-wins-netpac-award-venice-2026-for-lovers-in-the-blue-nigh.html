Overview of the NETPAC Award and Its Significance

The NETPAC Award, presented by the Network for the Promotion of Asian Cinema, honours films that exhibit strong artistic vision and cultural resonance within the Asian continent. At the Venice Film Festival, this prize is regarded as a marker of quality that often leads to increased festival circulation and distribution interest. The 2026 edition featured a competitive slate of Asian entries, with the award drawing attention to works that balance local storytelling with broader human themes.

About Lovers in the Blue Night

Lovers in the Blue Night is Anuparna Roy’s second feature film, following a debut that garnered national praise for its authentic depiction of village life. The narrative centres on two strangers who meet during an overnight train journey across rural Bengal. As the train moves through darkened landscapes, their conversation unfolds, revealing personal aspirations, societal expectations, and a quiet yearning for connection. The film was shot on location in small towns, railway platforms, and fields, relying heavily on natural light to capture the muted hues of twilight.

Anuparna Roy’s Journey from Purulia to Venice

Born in Purulia, West Bengal, Roy began her career assisting established directors before creating a series of short films that circulated in Indian festival circuits. Her debut feature received multiple national awards and was noted for its sincere portrayal of everyday rhythms. With Lovers in the Blue Night, she expanded her scope while retaining the rooted sensibility that defines her work, securing a place in the Venice Orizzonti section and ultimately winning the NETPAC prize.

Technical and Artistic Merit

The film’s cinematography employs long takes and minimal cutting, allowing viewers to inhabit the characters’ silences and the rhythm of the train’s motion. Sound design focuses on ambient elements—train wheels, distant murmurs, night insects—creating an immersive soundscape without an overpowering musical score. Roy’s direction encourages restrained performances, letting subtle gestures and facial expressions convey emotional depth.
