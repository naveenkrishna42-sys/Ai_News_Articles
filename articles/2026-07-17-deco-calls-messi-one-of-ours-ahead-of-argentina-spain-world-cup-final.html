Deco calls Messi 'one of ours' ahead of Argentina-Spain World Cup final

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Barcelona director Deco hailed Lionel Messi ahead of the World Cup final, calling him 'one of ours'.
  • President Joan Laporta called Messi's 2026 World Cup campaign 'extraordinary' and praised Lamine Yamal as the future.
  • The final pits Argentina's Messi against Spain's Yamal, both linked to Barcelona's legacy.
  • Laporta expressed excitement about seeing the two Barcelona heroes face off.
  • No official comment from Laporta on reported tensions between him and Messi.

Barcelona director Deco has heaped praise on Lionel Messi ahead of the 2026 World Cup final between Argentina and Spain, saying the club's all-time great 'will always be one of ours'. The comment, reported by Barca Universal, comes as the football world braces for a showdown that pits Messi's Argentina against a Spanish side featuring Barcelona's teenage sensation Lamine Yamal.

Deco: Messi's legacy is Barcelona's legacy

Deco, who played alongside Messi at Barcelona and now serves as the club's sporting director, did not hold back. 'He will always be one of ours,' he said, referring to the Argentine captain. 'What he has done for this club and for football is unmatched.'

Messi left Barcelona in 2021 under financial strain and later joined Inter Miami. But the bond remains strong. Deco's remarks underline the club's efforts to keep ties warm even as Messi's career winds down in Major League Soccer.

Laporta: 'Messi's World Cup is extraordinary'

Barcelona president Joan Laporta also weighed in, calling Messi's 2026 campaign 'extraordinary'. Speaking to World Soccer Talk, Laporta said, 'Messi's World Cup is extraordinary. He has shown once again why he is the best in history.'

Laporta's praise comes amid reported tensions between him and Messi over the player's departure. The president did not address those reports directly. Instead, he focused on the final itself.

Lamine Yamal: The future meets the past

The final is not just about Messi. Spain's 19-year-old winger Lamine Yamal has taken the tournament by storm. Laporta called him 'the future' of Barcelona in comments reported by Goal.com. 'Lionel Messi is the past, Lamine Yamal is the future,' Laporta said. 'We are excited to see our heroes go head-to-head.'

Yamal broke into Barcelona's first team at 16 and has become a key player for both club and country. He will face Messi for the first time in a competitive match on the biggest stage.

What to watch

The final kicks off on Sunday. It will be Messi's second World Cup final after winning in 2022. For Yamal, it is a chance to etch his name alongside the legend he grew up watching. Barcelona fans will be torn. The club's past and future are on opposite sides of the pitch.
