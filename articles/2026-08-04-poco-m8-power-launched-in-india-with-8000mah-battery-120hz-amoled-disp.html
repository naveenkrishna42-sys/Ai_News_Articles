POCO M8 Power launched in India with 8000mAh battery, 120Hz AMOLED display

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • POCO M8 Power 5G launched in India with an 8000mAh battery.
  • Features a 6.99-inch FHD+ 120Hz OLED display and Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 chipset.
  • Device aims to address battery anxiety with extended endurance.

Chinese smartphone maker POCO has officially launched the M8 Power 5G in India, a device that squarely targets battery-anxious users. The headline feature is a massive 8,000mAh cell, a figure that dwarfs most current mid-range offerings. The company pairs this with a large 6.99-inch FHD+ OLED display that refreshes at 120Hz, aiming to deliver a smooth viewing experience without constant charging.

The launch comes at a time when the Indian budget-to-midrange segment is fiercely competitive. Brands are increasingly differentiating on battery size and display quality rather than just processor specs. POCO's latest move signals that endurance has become a key battleground.

Battery and Charging: The Core Proposition

The 8,000mAh battery is the undisputed hero of the M8 Power. This capacity is significantly higher than the 5,000mAh cells commonly found in most smartphones today. For heavy users who stream video, game, or use navigation extensively, this could translate into two full days of usage on a single charge.

While the company has not yet detailed the exact charging speed, the focus is clearly on longevity rather than rapid top-ups. This approach may appeal to users who prioritise all-day reliability over quick recharge times. The trade-off is likely a thicker and heavier device, a compromise many buyers may accept for the extended endurance.

Display and Performance: Built for Media Consumption

The 6.99-inch FHD+ OLED panel with a 120Hz refresh rate is designed for immersive media consumption. The large screen size is ideal for watching movies, browsing social feeds, or playing casual games. The high refresh rate ensures smooth scrolling and fluid animations, a feature once reserved for flagship devices.

Under the hood, the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 chipset powers the device. This processor is positioned as an efficient, entry-level 5G solution. It should handle day-to-day tasks and moderate gaming without strain, though it is not aimed at intensive, high-end gaming. The combination of a big screen and a massive battery suggests POCO is targeting users who are on their phones for long stretches.

Market Positioning and Competition

POCO's launch strategy appears to be focused on carving a niche for the 'battery king' label. Reviews and early coverage, such as that from MensXP, have already begun questioning whether the device is truly a battery champion or just hype. This indicates that the company's marketing will heavily lean on endurance as the primary selling point.

The M8 Power enters a crowded field where rivals like the POCO M8 Pro 5G already exist. The new model's differentiation lies in its sheer battery capacity and screen size. It will compete with similarly priced devices from Realme, Redmi, and Samsung, all of which offer their own interpretations of value.

What to Watch

The success of the M8 Power will depend on real-world battery tests and how well the Snapdragon 4 Gen 4 manages efficiency. Consumers will also be watching the device's weight and thickness, as the large battery inevitably adds bulk. POCO will need to prove that the trade-offs are worth it for the target audience.

Going forward, the key question is whether other manufacturers will follow suit with even larger batteries. If the M8 Power performs well in reviews, we could see a trend toward 8,000mAh becoming a new standard in the budget segment. For now, POCO has made a bold statement, and the market's response will determine if this is a lasting shift or a passing fad.
