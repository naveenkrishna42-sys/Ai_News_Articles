Sensex, Nifty Open Flat as IT and Metal Gains Offset Financial Weakness

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty opened flat on Tuesday.
  • Mixed global cues influenced market sentiment.
  • IT and metal stocks supported the indices.
  • Financial stocks were a drag on the market.

Indian benchmark indices opened flat on Tuesday, July 21, as mixed signals from global markets kept investors cautious. The BSE Sensex and the NSE Nifty 50 hovered near the previous session's close in early trade.

While IT and metal stocks lent support, weakness in financials prevented any significant upward momentum. Traders pointed to a lack of strong domestic triggers, leaving the market to take cues from overseas trends.

Global Cues Remain Mixed

Asian markets traded in a narrow range on Tuesday, mirroring the subdued sentiment from Wall Street overnight. The lack of a clear direction globally meant that Indian indices struggled to find a firm footing.

Investors are awaiting further clarity on interest rate trajectories from major central banks. The cautious tone was evident as volumes remained moderate in the opening hour.

IT and Metal Stocks Provide Support

Information technology stocks were among the top gainers in early trade. The sector, which has been volatile in recent weeks, saw renewed buying interest. Analysts attribute this to bargain hunting after a recent dip in some IT counters.

Metal stocks also traded higher, supported by stable commodity prices. Gains in these heavyweight sectors helped offset the drag from other parts of the market.

Financials Weigh on Benchmarks

On the downside, the financial sector was the biggest drag on the indices. Banking stocks, both public and private sector, traded with losses. The Nifty Bank index was down in early trade, pulling the broader market lower.

Investors appeared to be booking profits in financials after a recent run-up. The sector has been a key driver of the market rally in the past few months, making it susceptible to profit-taking.

Other sectors like auto and FMCG were also mixed, with no clear trend emerging. The overall market breadth was neutral, with an equal number of stocks advancing and declining on the BSE.

Market participants will now watch for any domestic or global triggers that could provide direction. The lack of major economic data or corporate earnings announcements this week means the market may continue to take cues from global peers and sector-specific news.
