August 2026 financial deadlines: ITR, RBI policy, bank charges, Tatkal

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Banking Original source ↗
Key points
  • Income tax return (ITR) filing deadline for most taxpayers is August 31, 2026.
  • RBI's monetary policy committee (MPC) meeting scheduled in August; repo rate decision awaited.
  • New bank charges on savings accounts and Tatkal train ticket booking rules come into effect this month.

ITR filing deadline: August 31

The Income Tax Department has set August 31, 2026 as the deadline for filing income tax returns (ITRs) for the financial year 2025-26. This applies to individual taxpayers and most other entities not requiring audit. Missing the date attracts a late fee under Section 234F, up to Rs 5,000, besides interest on any outstanding tax.

Tax experts advise early filing to avoid last-minute glitches on the e-filing portal. Those with audit requirements have a later deadline, typically October 31. Ensure all Form 16s, bank interest certificates, and capital gains statements are handy before starting the return.

RBI monetary policy: Repo rate watch

The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) is scheduled to meet in August 2026. Markets will closely watch the repo rate decision, which influences lending rates for home, auto, and personal loans. The central bank has been balancing inflation control with growth support.

Any change in the policy rate will have a direct impact on EMI outflows for borrowers. Banks typically revise their external benchmark lending rates (EBLR) soon after the RBI announcement. Home loan borrowers should monitor their bank's communication for potential changes in monthly instalments.

Bank charges: New rules from August

Several banks have announced revised service charges effective from August 1, 2026. These include changes in savings account maintenance fees, ATM transaction charges beyond free limits, and charges for branch transactions. Customers are advised to check their bank's updated schedule on the official website or mobile app.

Digital transactions, including UPI and NEFT, remain free for most retail customers. However, cash deposits and withdrawals beyond specified thresholds in non-home branches may attract higher fees. Maintaining the minimum balance in savings accounts is crucial to avoid penalties.

Tatkal booking: Revised timings and rules

Indian Railways has introduced changes to the Tatkal quota booking system, effective August 2026. The booking window for Tatkal tickets has been revised. Earlier, Tatkal bookings opened at 10 am for AC classes and 11 am for non-AC classes. The new schedule is expected to streamline the process and reduce server load.

Passengers are advised to check the revised timings on the IRCTC website before planning their travel. The railway ministry has also updated cancellation rules for Tatkal tickets. Refunds for confirmed Tatkal tickets are now subject to higher deductions compared to regular tickets.

Other financial reminders for August

August also marks the deadline for filing revised or belated returns for the previous assessment year, if any. Senior citizens with interest income above the basic exemption limit must ensure advance tax payments are made by August 15, 2026. Quarterly GST returns for the June quarter are due by August 20 for most filers.

Equity investors should track the ex-dividend dates and record dates for upcoming corporate actions. The market regulator has mandated stricter disclosure norms for listed entities from this month. Mutual fund investors can review their portfolios ahead of the September quarter.

With multiple deadlines converging this month, financial planners recommend maintaining a checklist. Setting reminders for tax payments, bank charges, and booking windows can help avoid unnecessary penalties. The RBI's policy stance will likely set the tone for borrowing costs in the coming months.
