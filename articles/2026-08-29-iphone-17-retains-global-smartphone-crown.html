iPhone 17 retains global smartphone crown

📅 2026-08-29 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iPhone 17 remains world's best-selling smartphone in Q2 2026
  • Top 10 smartphones contribute 26% of global sales
  • Samsung S26 Ultra enters top 4, Apple may skip successor launch

iPhone 17 maintains global smartphone leadership

Apple's iPhone 17 has retained its position as the world's best-selling smartphone in the second quarter of 2026, according to reports from Counterpoint Research and other industry sources. This marks the third consecutive quarter of leadership for the iPhone 17, solidifying Apple's dominance in the smartphone market.

Market share and competition

The top 10 smartphones, including the iPhone 17 and Samsung's S26 Ultra, accounted for 26% of the global smartphone market in Q2 2026. The iPhone 17's strong performance is attributed to its consistent innovation, ecosystem integration, and brand loyalty. Samsung's S26 Ultra has entered the top 4, indicating a significant rise in its market share.

Broader industry implications

The continued dominance of the iPhone 17 and the rise of the Samsung S26 Ultra have significant implications for the broader smartphone industry. Apple's ability to maintain its leadership position despite increasing competition from Samsung and other brands highlights the importance of innovation, brand loyalty, and ecosystem integration. The rise of the Samsung S26 Ultra suggests that Samsung is making significant strides in catching up to Apple's market dominance.

Future outlook for Apple

Reports suggest that Apple may not launch a successor to its most-selling iPhone of the year 2026. This could be due to a variety of factors, including the high cost of developing new hardware, the saturation of the market, and the focus on other product lines. If Apple does not launch a new iPhone, it could lead to a shift in market dynamics, with other brands potentially gaining more market share.
