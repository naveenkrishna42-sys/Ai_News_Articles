Pakistan end three-year away Test drought with eight-wicket win over West Indies

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pakistan's first away Test win in three years, ending an eight-match losing streak
  • Eight-wicket victory over West Indies in the second Test levels the series
  • Win improves Pakistan's position in the World Test Championship standings

Historic breakthrough in the Caribbean

Pakistan ended a three-year wait for an away Test victory on Wednesday, dismantling the West Indies by eight wickets in the second Test to draw the series. The win in the Caribbean also snapped an eight-match overseas losing streak that had weighed heavily on Babar Azam's side.

The result marks a significant turnaround for a team that had struggled to convert promising positions into victories on foreign soil. Pakistan's last away Test win had come in August 2023, and the drought had become a talking point in cricketing circles.

Bowlers set up the chase

Pakistan's bowlers did the heavy lifting, restricting the West Indies to a modest total in the second innings. The visitors then chased down the target with ease, losing just two wickets in the process. The clinical nature of the chase underscored the team's intent to put the losing streak behind them.

Babar Azam, leading from the front, marshalled his resources astutely. The captain's calm demeanour in the field translated into disciplined bowling spells and sharp fielding, keeping the pressure on the home side throughout.

WTC standings get a boost

The victory also had a tangible impact on the World Test Championship points table. Pakistan's win lifted them in the standings, providing a much-needed boost to their campaign. While the full implications of the result are yet to be calculated, the points gained will be crucial in the context of a tightly contested championship cycle.

For the West Indies, the series draw will feel like a missed opportunity after they had won the opening Test. The home side fought hard but could not sustain the intensity required to overcome a determined Pakistan unit.

A series of positives for Pakistan

Beyond the result, Pakistan will take several positives from the match. The performance of the pace attack, which exploited helpful conditions, and the resilience shown by the batting order in the chase are likely to please the team management. The win also provides a psychological edge as Pakistan look ahead to future assignments.

Players will now regroup and shift focus to upcoming fixtures, with the selectors likely to take confidence from the squad's ability to close out a game under pressure.

What lies ahead

Pakistan's next Test assignment will be watched closely, with the team aiming to build on this momentum. The focus will be on consistency, particularly in overseas conditions, as the WTC cycle progresses.
