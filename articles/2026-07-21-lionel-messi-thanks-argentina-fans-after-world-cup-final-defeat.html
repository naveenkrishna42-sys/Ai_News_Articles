Lionel Messi thanks Argentina fans after World Cup final defeat

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Messi thanks Argentina fans for support after World Cup final loss to Spain.
  • Argentina lost 3-1 to Spain in the 2026 World Cup final.
  • Messi says the 'wound will take time to heal'.
  • Spain celebrated their World Cup triumph with an open-top bus parade in Madrid.

Messi breaks silence after defeat

Lionel Messi has thanked Argentina's fans for their unwavering support after the national team's heartbreaking loss to Spain in the 2026 FIFA World Cup final. The captain took to social media late Monday evening, saying the 'wound will take time to heal'.

Argentina went down 3-1 to a dominant Spanish side at the tournament's showpiece event. The defeat shattered the nation's dream of back-to-back World Cup titles after their triumph in Qatar 2022.

A bitter end to a golden run

Spain outclassed Argentina on the night. Their structured, possession-based game plan left La Albiceleste chasing shadows for large stretches. The Spanish midfield smothered Argentina's creative outlets, leaving Messi isolated and frustrated.

This was widely expected to be Messi's final World Cup appearance. The 39-year-old had carried the hopes of a football-obsessed nation on his shoulders. His tearful image at the final whistle has become the defining photograph of the tournament.

Fans rally behind their captain

Argentine fans, many of whom had travelled thousands of miles to support the team, responded with an outpouring of love and gratitude for their captain. Social media has been flooded with messages thanking Messi for his service to the national team.

In Buenos Aires, thousands gathered at the Obelisco to console each other. While there was palpable disappointment, the mood was largely one of appreciation. 'We love you, Leo. You gave us everything,' read one banner held aloft in the crowd.

Spain celebrates a historic triumph

Meanwhile, Spain celebrated their fourth World Cup title with an open-top bus parade through the streets of Madrid. Hundreds of thousands of fans lined the route, waving red and yellow flags and cheering their heroes.

Coach Luis de la Fuente has been hailed as a tactical genius. His team's collective brilliance, built on a foundation of youth and technical skill, overwhelmed every opponent they faced in the knockout stages. 'Method over madness,' one Indian sports daily summed it up.

What happens next

Messi has not indicated whether he will continue playing international football. The Argentine Football Association is expected to hold a press conference in the coming days to discuss the team's future direction. For now, a nation mourns, but its gratitude for its greatest son remains undimmed.
