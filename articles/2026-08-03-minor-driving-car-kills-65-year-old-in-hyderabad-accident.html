Minor driving car kills 65-year-old in Hyderabad accident

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 65-year-old man was killed after a minor crashed a car into his scooty in Hyderabad.
  • The incident occurred recently, and police have registered a case.
  • Further details about the minor and the accident are awaited.

Hyderabad: A 65-year-old man was killed on Monday after a car driven by a minor crashed into his scooty, police said. The incident took place in the city, though the exact location has not been officially confirmed.

The victim, whose identity has not been released pending family notification, was on his two-wheeler when the car, allegedly driven by a teenager, rammed into him. The impact was severe, and the man died on the spot or shortly after being rushed to a hospital, according to preliminary reports.

Police investigation underway

Local police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code and the Motor Vehicles Act. The minor driver has been detained, but officials have not yet disclosed the age or other details, as juvenile procedures apply.

Sources said the car was being driven in a rash manner, though this has not been officially confirmed. Authorities are examining CCTV footage from the area to piece together the sequence of events.

Rising concern over underage driving

The incident has once again highlighted the issue of minors driving vehicles on city roads. Hyderabad has witnessed several such cases in recent years, often leading to tragic outcomes. Traffic police have repeatedly urged parents to ensure their children do not operate vehicles without a valid licence.

Under the Motor Vehicles Act, a minor caught driving can face penalties under Section 199A, which includes a fine of up to Rs 25,000 and possible imprisonment for the guardian. However, enforcement remains a challenge, and accidents continue to occur.

Legal process and next steps

The minor will be produced before the Juvenile Justice Board, where the case will be heard. The victim's family has been informed, and post-mortem procedures are likely to be completed soon.

Police are also verifying the ownership of the car and whether the minor had access to it without consent. Depending on the findings, additional charges could be added.

As the investigation progresses, officials are expected to release more details, including the exact spot of the crash and the sequence of events leading up to it.

What to watch: The Juvenile Justice Board's ruling on the minor's fate and any action against the vehicle's owner or the minor's guardians could set a precedent for how such cases are handled in the city.
