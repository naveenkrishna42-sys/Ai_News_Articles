Nawazuddin Siddiqui on Kahaani's surprise success at Bollywood Hungama Style Icons

A Throwback to Kahaani's Quiet Beginning

At the Bollywood Hungama Style Icons Summit and Awards 2026, Nawazuddin Siddiqui took a walk down memory lane. The actor recalled how Sujoy Ghosh's 2012 thriller Kahaani crept up on audiences and the box office alike.

"For the first three-four days, hardly anyone knew it had released," Siddiqui said on stage, drawing laughter and nods from the audience. The film, starring Vidya Balan as a pregnant woman searching for her missing husband in Kolkata, went on to become a sleeper hit.

An Unexpected Success Story

Made on a modest budget, Kahaani earned over Rs 60 crore at the domestic box office, a remarkable feat for a female-led thriller in 2012. The movie's success was not just commercial but critical, with Balan's performance receiving widespread praise.

For Siddiqui, who played the investigating officer, the film was a significant milestone in his journey from theatre and small roles to mainstream recognition. The actor, known for his roles in Gangs of Wasseypur and The Lunchbox, has often credited Kahaani for giving him a wider audience.

Style Icons Summit: Celebrating Cinema and Fashion

The Bollywood Hungama Style Icons Summit and Awards is an annual event that recognises the intersection of cinema and style. This year's edition brought together actors, directors, and fashion designers to discuss trends and celebrate achievements in the entertainment industry.

Siddiqui's anecdote was part of a candid conversation about his career choices and the unpredictable nature of film success. He also touched upon how word-of-mouth publicity has become even more crucial in the age of social media and OTT platforms.

The Changing Face of Film Releases

Reflecting on the current landscape, Siddiqui noted that while Kahaani took time to find its audience, today's films often get only a weekend to prove themselves. The actor emphasised that content-driven cinema has found new life on streaming platforms, allowing slower burn films to reach viewers.

He also praised the increasing acceptance of regional stories and unconventional narratives in mainstream Bollywood, a trend that films like Kahaani helped set in motion.

An Evening of Recognition

The event also saw several stars receiving awards for their contributions to cinema and style. However, it was Siddiqui's trip down memory lane that became one of the evening's most talked-about moments.

His recollection served as a reminder that sometimes the most memorable films are the ones that arrive without fanfare. For the audience, it was a rare glimpse into the uncertainty that even the most celebrated actors face before a film's release.

As for what's next, Siddiqui is said to be working on multiple projects across streaming platforms and theatrical releases. The coming months will show whether his upcoming films can replicate the quiet magic of Kahaani.
