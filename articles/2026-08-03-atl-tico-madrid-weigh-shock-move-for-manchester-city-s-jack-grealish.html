Atlético Madrid weigh shock move for Manchester City's Jack Grealish

Key points
  • Atlético Madrid are weighing a move for Jack Grealish
  • The Spanish club are considering a summer bid for the Manchester City winger
  • Grealish's future at City has been the subject of speculation

Atletico Madrid are weighing up a surprise move for Manchester City winger Jack Grealish, according to reports circulating in the European football press. The Spanish club are considering a bid for the England international as they look to strengthen their attacking options ahead of the new season.

Grealish, who joined City from Aston Villa in a record-breaking £100 million deal in 2021, has struggled to hold down a regular starting spot under Pep Guardiola. Despite winning multiple trophies at the Etihad, the 30-year-old's playing time has been limited in recent campaigns.

Why Atletico are interested

Diego Simeone's side are believed to be in the market for a creative wide player, and Grealish's ability to draw fouls, retain possession and unlock defences fits the profile Atletico are looking for. The Spanish club have traditionally favoured a direct, physical style, but they have shown a willingness to adapt in recent transfer windows.

Grealish's experience at the highest level, including Champions League and Premier League titles, would bring a winning mentality to a squad that has been rebuilt around younger talents. Atletico finished last season with a top-four finish and are eager to close the gap on Real Madrid and Barcelona.

City's stance and financial reality

Manchester City are not actively looking to sell Grealish, but they may be open to listening to offers if the fee is right. The Premier League champions have been prudent in the transfer market this summer, focusing on outgoings before making new signings. Grealish's wages, reportedly in the region of £300,000 a week, could complicate any potential deal.

Atletico would need to offload players to fund a move, and they have several squad members who could be sold to raise funds. The La Liga side have also been linked with a number of other attacking targets, including Athletic Club's Nico Williams, but Grealish's availability may tempt them into a move.

Other rumours in the transfer mill

The transfer gossip mill has been buzzing with other stories as well. Arsenal's pursuit of Real Madrid's Vinícius Júnior continues to dominate headlines, though the Brazilian forward is reportedly set to resume contract talks with the Spanish giants. The Gunners have also been linked with a host of other players, with Mikel Arteta hinting at more arrivals before the window closes.

Meanwhile, the agency representing Vinícius and his teammate Doumbia has issued an angry statement regarding speculation over their clients' futures. The statement did not provide specifics but expressed frustration at what it called "irresponsible rumours" circulating in the media.

What this means for Grealish

For Grealish, a move to Atletico Madrid would represent a fresh chapter in his career. After three seasons at City, the winger has won every major domestic trophy but has often been seen as a peripheral figure in big matches. A switch to Spain could offer him a more central role and a chance to prove his doubters wrong.

However, any transfer would depend on City's valuation and Grealish's own willingness to leave. The player has repeatedly stated his happiness at the club, but the prospect of regular football and a new challenge may be tempting.

Officials at both clubs have not yet confirmed any negotiations, and the coming weeks will determine whether this rumour gains traction or fades away like so many others in the summer window.

With the transfer deadline still a few weeks away, expect more twists and turns in this saga. Atletico's interest may be genuine, but a deal remains far from done.
