Bengaluru Park Bill: Environmentalists, BJP MPs voice concerns over land takeover

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Environmentalists and BJP MPs express concerns over the Park Bill.
  • The bill aims to facilitate the takeover of Lalbagh land for Tunnel Road expansion.
  • The move has raised questions about the balance between urban development and environmental preservation.

Environmentalists and BJP MPs raise concerns over Park Bill

Environmentalists and BJP MPs in Bengaluru have expressed concerns over the recent Park Bill, which aims to facilitate the takeover of land in Lalbagh for the expansion of Tunnel Road. The bill has sparked a debate about the balance between urban development and environmental preservation.

Lalbagh land takeover for Tunnel Road expansion

The Park Bill, introduced by the city administration, is intended to streamline the process of acquiring land in Lalbagh for the expansion of Tunnel Road. This move is part of the city's ongoing efforts to improve its infrastructure and facilitate smoother traffic flow.

Concerns over ecological balance

Environmentalists have raised concerns that the takeover of land in Lalbagh could have a significant impact on the area's ecological balance. Lalbagh is known for its rich biodiversity and is home to several protected species. The potential loss of this land could have far-reaching consequences for the city's environment.

BJP MPs voice opposition

Several BJP MPs from Bengaluru have also voiced their opposition to the Park Bill. They argue that the takeover of land in Lalbagh could lead to the displacement of local communities and the destruction of historical and cultural sites. The MPs have called for a more comprehensive approach to urban development that takes into account the needs of all stakeholders.

What happens next?

The Park Bill will now go through the legislative process, and its final outcome will depend on the votes cast by the city's lawmakers. Environmentalists and BJP MPs will continue to advocate for their respective positions, and the public will have an opportunity to voice their opinions on the matter.
