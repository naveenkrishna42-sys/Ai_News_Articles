Justin Langer frontrunner for England Test coach role; no Indian candidates in race

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Justin Langer is the frontrunner for the England Test coach position.
  • Ravi Shastri and Rahul Dravid are not being considered for the role.
  • Andy Flower has ruled himself out of the running, opening the door for other candidates.

Langer leads the pack for England's top job

Justin Langer has emerged as the frontrunner for the England Test team's head coach position. The former Australia opener is being strongly considered by the England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB) to replace Brendon McCullum.

McCullum was recently sacked as Test coach after a string of poor results. The ECB is now looking to appoint a successor before the next international series.

Langer's name has gained traction in recent weeks. His previous stint as Australia's head coach, which included an Ashes win and a T20 World Cup triumph, makes him a compelling candidate.

No Indian candidates in the mix

Ravi Shastri and Rahul Dravid are not in the race for the England Test coach job. This puts to rest any speculation about the two former India head coaches taking up the role.

Shastri, who coached India from 2017 to 2021, has not been approached by the ECB. Dravid, who stepped down as India's head coach after the 2025 World Cup, is also not being considered.

Both remain highly respected figures in world cricket, but their current commitments and priorities lie elsewhere.

Flower rules himself out

Andy Flower, a former England head coach, has ruled himself out of the running. Flower led England to the top of the ICC Test rankings and a historic Ashes win in 2010-11.

He had been tipped as a potential candidate, but his decision to withdraw narrows the field. Flower's stock remains high, but he has chosen not to pursue a return to the high-pressure role.

McCullum's shadow lingers

Brendon McCullum has expressed confidence he can work with the new Test coach. Despite his sacking, McCullum remains involved in the white-ball setup and has pledged to be around for The Hundred tournament.

His relationship with the incoming coach will be crucial. The ECB wants a smooth transition, and McCullum's willingness to cooperate is a positive sign.

Langer's personality is a contrast to McCullum's. Langer is intense and disciplined, while McCullum is more laid-back. How they mesh will be a key factor in England's future strategy.

What happens next

The ECB is expected to announce the new Test coach within the next three weeks. Langer remains the clear favourite, but no final decision has been made.

Other candidates may yet emerge. For now, all eyes are on Langer and whether he will take up the challenge of rebuilding England's Test side.
