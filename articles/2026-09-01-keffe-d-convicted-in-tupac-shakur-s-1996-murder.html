On September 7, 1996, Tupac Shakur was shot multiple times outside the Las Vegas nightclub where he was performing. The shooting was part of a larger feud between Tupac and the East Coast rap group, the Notorious B.I.G. and his associates. Keffe D, a former member of the Crips gang, was accused of being the mastermind behind the murder.

Legal Battle: The Road to Conviction

The case against Keffe D was complex and involved a significant amount of evidence. The prosecution argued that Davis was motivated by a desire to protect his own interests and those of his gang.
