Tehseen Poonawalla detained before E20 protest

Tehseen Poonawalla Detained Before E20 Protest

Tehseen Poonawalla, the chairman of the Sugar Industry Association of India, was detained by police on Saturday morning ahead of an E20 protest in Delhi. The protest is aimed at opposing a recent hike in sugar excise duty.

Sugarcane Juice Offered to Police

During the detention, Poonawalla offered sugarcane juice to the police officers present. He joked that the sugarcane juice would soon become a luxury item, hinting at the potential impact of the sugar excise duty hike on the industry.

Protests Over Sugar Excise Duty Hike

The E20 protest is part of a broader movement by sugar industry leaders and farmers to protest the government's decision to increase sugar excise duty. The hike is expected to significantly impact the profitability of the sugar industry, which has been struggling with low sugar prices in recent years.

Police Response

Police have not yet commented on the reason for Poonawalla's detention. However, they have confirmed that he was taken into custody for further investigation. The sugar industry association has called for his immediate release, stating that his detention is an attempt to suppress the protest movement.

What Happens Next?

The sugar industry is closely watching the outcome of the E20 protest and the subsequent actions by the government. The industry leaders are expected to intensify their protests and strike calls if their demands are not met. The government, on the other hand, is likely to defend its decision to increase sugar excise duty, citing the need to balance the country's fiscal deficit.
