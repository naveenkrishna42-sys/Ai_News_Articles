U.S. panel urges sanctions on RSS members over Bhagwat visit

  • Hindu American Foundation issues security advisory ahead of RSS event

U.S. Religious Freedom Panel Takes Strong Stance

The U.S. Commission on International Religious Freedom (USCIRF) has urged sanctions on members of the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) following the visit of its chief, Mohan Bhagwat, to New York. This move comes as part of the panel's ongoing efforts to address concerns about religious freedom violations globally.

This is not the first time the USCIRF has taken such a stance. In the past, it has urged sanctions on various groups, including the Chinese Communist Party and the Taliban, for their alleged human rights abuses. The panel's latest move is a significant escalation, given the RSS's influence in India and its global activities.

Security Advisories and Protests

The Hindu American Foundation (HAF), a prominent Hindu organization in the U.S., has issued a security advisory ahead of Bhagwat's event. The advisory urges members to 'stay vigilant' and reminds them of their 'sworn duty' to ensure the safety of all attendees.

However, the advisory has not dampened the spirit of protests. Several groups have expressed their objection to Bhagwat's visit, with some calling for a boycott of the event. The RSS, in response, has urged its followers to 'be civil' and respect the rights of all attendees.

Delhi Confidential: Zohran Mamdani's Objection

Zohran Mamdani, a prominent Indian-American lawyer and civil rights activist, has publicly objected to Bhagwat's visit. Mamdani, who has a long history of advocating for minority rights in the U.S., has expressed concerns about the potential for violence and has urged the U.S. government to take a stronger stance against the RSS.

Mamdani's objections have not gone unnoticed. Shivraj Singh Chouhan, the Chief Minister of Madhya Pradesh and a key RSS ally, has expressed his support for Bhagwat's visit. Chouhan's stance is a stark contrast to Mamdani's and highlights the deep political divide between the RSS and its critics.

Broader Implications

The RSS's visit to the U.S. has significant implications for India-U.S. relations. The RSS is a major political force in India, with a strong influence over the country's political and social landscape. Its activities in the U.S. are a cause for concern for many, given the potential for conflict and the erosion of religious freedom.

However, the RSS's visit is not without its supporters. Many Hindus in the U.S. view Bhagwat's visit as an opportunity to strengthen ties between India and the U.S. and to promote Hindu culture and values. This divide of opinion highlights the complex nature of religious and political issues in the U.S. and India.
