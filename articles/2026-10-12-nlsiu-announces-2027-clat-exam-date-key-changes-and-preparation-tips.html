NLSIU Kolkata Sets the Timeline for CLAT 2027

The National Law School of India University (NLSIU) Kolkata has officially announced the examination date for the Common Law Admission Test (CLAT) 2027. As the conducting authority for this year's cycle, NLSIU has set the stage for thousands of aspirants aiming to secure a seat in India's premier National Law Universities (NLUs). While the exact date is expected to fall between mid-December 2026 and early January 2027, candidates are advised to regularly check the official website clat.nls.ac.in for final confirmation and important updates.

Why This Announcement Matters

The CLAT notification typically drops a few weeks before the exam, outlining crucial details like eligibility, pattern changes, and application deadlines. For 2027, students should anticipate minor adjustments in section-wise weightage or the introduction of new question types, especially in legal reasoning and logical reasoning sections.

Who Can Appear for CLAT 2027

Eligibility Criteria Explained

To qualify, candidates must have passed the Senior Secondary (10+2) examination or an equivalent qualification with a minimum of 45% marks (40% for candidates belonging to reserved categories like SC, ST, OBC, and PwD). There is no upper age limit, making it accessible to a wide demographic of law aspirants.

Application Process Overview

The online application window usually opens in August and closes in October. The current fee stands at ₹4,000 for general category applicants and ₹3,500 for SC/ST/PwD candidates. Applications are submitted exclusively through the official CLAT portal, which also hosts mock tests and study material.

Understanding the Exam Pattern and Syllabus

Section-Wise Breakdown

CLAT is a pen-and-paper based test comprising 150 multiple-choice questions divided into five sections:

SectionNumber of QuestionsMarks per Question
English Language301
General Knowledge & Current Affairs301
Legal Aptitude351
Logical Reasoning301
Quantitative Techniques251

Each correct answer awards one mark, while 0.25 marks are deducted for every incorrect response. The total duration of the exam is two hours.

Syllabus Highlights

The syllabus spans core subjects like torts, contracts, constitutional law, and criminal law under Legal Aptitude. General Knowledge includes current events, static GK, and legal awareness. Quantitative Techniques focus on basic math, graphs, and data interpretation relevant to law studies.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • Negative Marking: Every unanswered or wrongly answered question leads to a 0.25 mark deduction, encouraging strategic answering.
  • NLUs Participating: Over 22 National Law Universities accept CLAT scores, offering more than 1,800 law seats annually.
  • Preparation Window: With nearly four months left post-announcement, serious aspirants should begin solving previous year papers and taking timed mock tests.
  • Reserved Category Relaxation: A 5% relaxation in cutoff percentile is granted for SC/ST/PwD candidates in individual NLUs.

Final Thoughts

With NLSIU at the helm, CLAT 2027 promises to maintain its reputation as the most competitive law entrance exam in India. Aspirants must align their preparation with the latest trends, focus on speed and accuracy, and leverage official resources to maximize their chances of securing a top-ranking NLU seat.
