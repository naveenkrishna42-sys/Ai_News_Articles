SC orders AG, SG oversight in BCI polls amid tenure row

Supreme Court mandates AG, SG oversight in BCI policy decisions

The Supreme Court has ordered the Attorney General and Solicitor General to be consulted in every policy decision of the Bar Council of India (BCI), citing concerns over the democratic deficit in the tenure of its current chairperson, Manan Mishra. This decision comes amid a row over Mishra's long tenure as BCI chief.

Strict timelines set for BCI polls, questioning Manan Mishra's tenure

The Supreme Court has also set strict timelines for the upcoming BCI polls, questioning the validity of Mishra's tenure. The court has directed the BCI to hold the polls within a specified period, and has also ordered a review of Mishra's tenure.

BCI chief's tenure: SC says AG, SG 'shall be associated with every policy decision'

The Supreme Court has ruled that the AG and SG shall be associated with every policy decision made by the BCI, stating that this is necessary to ensure that the BCI's decisions are made in a democratic manner. This decision is seen as a major step towards ensuring transparency and accountability in the BCI's decision-making process.
