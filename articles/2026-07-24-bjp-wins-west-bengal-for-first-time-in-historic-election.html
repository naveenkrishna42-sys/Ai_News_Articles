BJP wins West Bengal for first time in historic election

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Politics Original source ↗
Key points
  • BJP secures majority in West Bengal assembly for first time
  • Mamata Banerjee's Trinamool Congress concedes defeat after three terms
  • Election seen as major boost for Modi ahead of 2029 national polls

Historic victory in Kolkata

Narendra Modi's Bharatiya Janata Party has won the West Bengal assembly election for the first time in the state's history. The victory ends Mamata Banerjee's three-term rule and marks a tectonic shift in eastern Indian politics.

Results declared on Monday showed the BJP crossing the majority mark in the 294-seat assembly. The Trinamool Congress, which had governed since 2011, conceded defeat. Party leaders said they accepted the mandate.

Campaign turned on development and identity

The BJP ran a high-voltage campaign centred on the themes of development and cultural identity. Modi held multiple rallies across the state, promising jobs, infrastructure and a crackdown on corruption.

Banerjee campaigned on her welfare schemes and local governance record. But analysts say anti-incumbency and factionalism within the TMC hurt her prospects. The BJP also made inroads among traditionally non-aligned voter groups.

What this means for national politics

The win gives the BJP its first foothold in a state it had long viewed as a tough frontier. West Bengal sends 42 MPs to the Lok Sabha, and the party hopes this victory will boost its tally in the 2029 general election.

Political observers say the result weakens the opposition alliance. Banerjee was seen as a key figure in any anti-Modi coalition. Her defeat removes a major challenger from the national stage.

Modi called the victory a win for the people of Bengal. In a tweet, he said the state had chosen a path of progress and peace.

Reaction from the ground

BJP supporters celebrated in Kolkata's streets, waving party flags and bursting firecrackers. In rural areas, long queues formed outside party offices as workers distributed sweets.

Trinamool Congress leaders said they would sit in opposition and rebuild. Banerjee herself has not made a public statement since the results. Party workers are expected to meet later this week to chart a course.

Election Commission officials said the voting was largely peaceful, though isolated incidents of violence were reported in a few constituencies. Security has been tightened in sensitive areas.

All eyes are now on the swearing-in ceremony. The BJP is expected to announce its chief ministerial candidate in the coming days. The new government will have to hit the ground running on law and order, economic revival and contentious land issues.
