OMR residents demand urgent repair of dilapidated foot overbridges

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Foot overbridges on OMR are in a dilapidated state, with broken steps and missing railings.
  • Residents say the structures are unsafe, especially for children and the elderly.
  • Commuters are forced to cross the busy road at grade, risking accidents.
  • Local authorities have been petitioned but repairs have not been carried out.

Residents along Chennai’s Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR) are demanding that the civic authorities immediately repair several foot overbridges that have fallen into a state of severe disrepair. The bridges, meant to help pedestrians cross the busy arterial road safely, now pose risks themselves.

Many of the overbridges have broken steps, missing handrails, and poor lighting. Residents say the structures have not been maintained for years and are now barely usable.

Safety hazard for pedestrians

Commuters, especially women, children, and senior citizens, say they are forced to take their chances crossing the road at grade, dodging speeding vehicles. “It’s a death wish using that bridge. The steps are crumbling and there’s no light at night,” said a resident who uses the stretch daily.

Parents of schoolchildren in the area have also expressed concern. Several schools are located along OMR, and students rely on these bridges to get to bus stops safely.

Broken promises, no action

Residents say they have repeatedly approached the Greater Chennai Corporation and local elected representatives with complaints. While officials have acknowledged the problem, no repair work has been carried out so far.

“We have been told that a tender will be floated, but nothing happens. It’s been months,” said a local resident welfare association member. The lack of action has led to frustration among the community.

Patchwork repairs not enough

Some residents point out that even when minor repairs are done, they are shoddy and do not last. “They just patch a few steps here and there. The entire structure needs a thorough overhaul,” another resident added.

The OMR stretch sees heavy traffic throughout the day, and a well-maintained pedestrian infrastructure is critical. Residents say the current situation is an accident waiting to happen.

What needs to be done

Residents have demanded a comprehensive audit of all foot overbridges on OMR, followed by immediate structural repairs. They also want better lighting and regular maintenance schedules.

Some have suggested that the corporation install elevators or ramps at key locations to make the bridges accessible for the elderly and differently-abled. The current stair-only design is a barrier for many.

The Greater Chennai Corporation has not yet issued a statement on the timeline for repairs. Residents are hoping that the renewed attention will finally spur action before someone gets seriously hurt.
