India Slams Pakistan Over Crackdown on PoK Protesters

📅 2026-07-31 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • India slams Pakistan for branding protesters as 'enemies' and cracking down on civilians in PoK.
  • New Delhi calls for international scrutiny of the 'ruthless' crackdown in Pakistan-occupied Kashmir.
  • The action has drawn sharp reactions from Indian officials, escalating tensions between the two neighbours.

New Delhi's Strong Condemnation

India has sharply criticised Pakistan for its handling of protests in Pakistan-occupied Kashmir (PoK), calling the crackdown on civilians 'ruthless' and accusing Islamabad of treating its own people as enemies. The Ministry of External Affairs issued a strong statement on Friday, demanding global attention to what it termed a 'brutal suppression' of peaceful demonstrations.

The Indian government's remarks come amid reports of escalating violence in the region, where Pakistani security forces have been deployed to quell unrest. Official sources in New Delhi expressed deep concern over the situation, noting that the treatment of civilians in PoK reflects a broader pattern of disregard for human rights.

'Pak-Trained Mujahideen Turn Guns Inwards'

In a pointed attack, Indian officials referenced the irony of Pakistan's long-standing policy of training and arming militants. 'The so-called Pak-trained Mujahideen have turned their guns inwards,' a senior diplomat said, alluding to reports that armed groups are now being used against local protesters in PoK.

This statement underscores a recurring theme in India's diplomatic messaging: that Pakistan's support for cross-border terrorism has ultimately destabilised its own territory. The Indian establishment has consistently argued that the people of PoK are suffering under a regime that denies them basic freedoms and democratic rights.

Demand for Global Scrutiny

India has called on the international community to examine the situation in PoK more closely, urging countries and multilateral bodies to take note of the 'ruthless' measures employed by Pakistani authorities. The MEA's statement highlighted the need for transparency and accountability in how Islamabad handles civilian dissent.

This is not the first time India has raised the PoK issue on the world stage, but the latest escalation has prompted a more urgent tone. Diplomatic observers suggest that New Delhi is seeking to internationalise the matter, potentially raising it at the United Nations and other forums in the coming weeks.

Background of Unrest in PoK

The protests in PoK are believed to stem from long-standing grievances over governance, economic neglect, and political marginalisation. Local residents have reportedly been demanding better infrastructure, employment opportunities, and greater political autonomy, but their voices have been met with force.

Analysts point out that Pakistan's administration in the region has often been marked by heavy-handed tactics, which have historically fuelled resentment among the local population. The current crackdown appears to have intensified tensions, with reports of arrests, curfews, and clashes between security forces and demonstrators.

While exact casualty figures remain unverified, eyewitness accounts and local media reports suggest a significant number of injuries and detentions. Pakistani officials have not yet confirmed the scale of the operation, though they have defended their actions as necessary for maintaining law and order.

Regional Implications

The developments in PoK carry significant implications for regional stability. India's strong reaction is likely to further strain already fragile India-Pakistan relations, which have seen little progress on key issues including cross-border terrorism and the Kashmir dispute.

Observers note that the situation could also impact Pakistan's internal dynamics, as the use of force against civilians may deepen political fractures within the country's leadership. Some analysts have speculated that the crackdown is linked to internal power struggles, though this remains unconfirmed.

For now, New Delhi has made its position clear: the world must not turn a blind eye to the suffering of civilians in PoK. The Indian government has vowed to continue raising the issue at every appropriate platform, ensuring that the voices of those affected are not silenced.

What to Watch Next

As the international community reacts to India's call for scrutiny, all eyes will be on Pakistan's response and whether it will allow independent observers to assess the ground situation. The coming days are likely to see further diplomatic exchanges, with India expected to press its case in multilateral forums.
