Amit Shah tables bill to protect Vande Mataram in Rajya Sabha

📅 2026-07-21 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Amit Shah tabled a bill to protect Vande Mataram in Rajya Sabha.
  • Opposition MPs protested over a lathi charge against student protesters in Delhi.
  • Rajya Sabha was adjourned till 2 pm due to the uproar.

Day 2 of Monsoon Session Begins with Controversy

The second day of the Parliament monsoon session kicked off on Tuesday with the Rajya Sabha witnessing heated exchanges over police action against student protesters. The opposition demanded an explanation from the government after a lathi charge was carried out on students demonstrating in Delhi.

Amid the chaos, Union Home Minister Amit Shah is expected to table a bill aimed at protecting the national song 'Vande Mataram'. The proposed legislation seeks to prevent any disrespect or dishonour to the song, which holds a revered place in Indian culture.

Opposition Protests Over Student Lathi Charge

The opposition, led by Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge, vocally criticised the government over the police action. "They are beating students," Kharge said, slamming the lathi charge against protesters from the Centre for Justice and Peace (CJP) in the national capital.

The uproar forced the Rajya Sabha chair to adjourn the House till 2 pm. MPs from various opposition parties staged a walkout, demanding a full-fledged debate on the issue. The government, however, maintained that the police action was necessary to maintain law and order.

PM Modi Speaks on Youth Aspirations

Meanwhile, Prime Minister Narendra Modi addressed the nation, focusing on the aspirations of the youth. "Youth full of aspirations, want country to move forward," PM Modi said, emphasising the need to channel their energy towards nation-building. He did not directly comment on the ongoing protests or the Vande Mataram bill.

The prime minister's speech was seen as an attempt to steer the narrative towards development, even as the opposition continued to press its demands for a parliamentary committee to investigate the police action.

Vande Mataram Bill: Key Provisions

The bill introduced by Amit Shah aims to criminalise any act that shows disrespect to Vande Mataram, including altering its lyrics or tune without authorisation. The proposed law also prescribes penalties for anyone who prevents others from singing the national song.

Sources within the home ministry confirmed that the bill has been in the works for months and is part of the government's efforts to uphold national symbols. The opposition has questioned the timing of the bill, calling it a diversion from pressing issues like unemployment and inflation.

As the session progresses, all eyes will be on whether the government can push through the bill amid the ongoing political storm. The monsoon session is expected to last three weeks, with several key pieces of legislation lined up for debate.
