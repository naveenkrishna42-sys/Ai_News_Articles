Argentina coach snaps at media over 'internal rift' questions after Messi video

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Argentina coach Lionel Scaloni reportedly jumped out of his car to confront reporters
  • Media questions centered on an alleged 'internal rift' sparked by a viral Lionel Messi video
  • The incident occurred after Argentina's loss in a major final, with Messi's pre-match speech under scrutiny

Argentina's head coach Lionel Scaloni lost his cool with journalists on Wednesday, reportedly jumping out of his car to confront them over questions about an alleged rift within the squad. The flashpoint comes after a video of captain Lionel Messi delivering a pre-final team talk went viral, triggering intense speculation.

Scaloni's heated exchange with reporters

According to witnesses, Scaloni was leaving a team facility when reporters surrounded his vehicle, asking about a supposed internal divide in the dressing room. The coach abruptly stopped the car, stepped out and snapped at the journalists, demanding to know the source of their claims.

Videos circulating on social media show Scaloni gesticulating forcefully, though his exact words are unclear. Local media reports describe his tone as frustrated and dismissive.

Messi's viral video under the microscope

The controversy stems from a clip of Messi addressing his teammates before the final. In the video, the captain is heard telling the squad to 'forget everything' — a phrase that some interpreted as a sign of discord or last-minute panic.

Midfielder Rodrigo De Paul has since blasted what he called 'absurd conspiracy theories' surrounding the speech. 'People are inventing problems where none exist,' De Paul told reporters. 'We are united.'

Meanwhile, Alexis Mac Allister's father has also weighed in, revealing what he claims is the true context of Messi's talk. Speaking to Argentine media, he insisted the speech was standard motivational fare and not a response to any internal crisis.

A team under pressure

The Argentine squad has been under intense scrutiny since their defeat in the final. Questions about team morale, tactical decisions and Messi's role have dominated headlines. Scaloni's outburst suggests the pressure is taking a toll on the coaching staff as well.

Argentina's football federation has not officially commented on the incident. However, sources close to the team say Scaloni remains fully in control of the dressing room and that the media is blowing minor disagreements out of proportion.

What comes next

With speculation showing no signs of dying down, all eyes will be on Scaloni's next press conference. Whether he addresses the rift allegations directly or tries to move the conversation back to football remains to be seen. For now, Argentina's camp is anything but quiet.
