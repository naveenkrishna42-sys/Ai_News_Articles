Yash Defends Kiara Advani Amid Toxic Song Backlash, Calls Her 'Tabahi'

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Yash calls Kiara Advani 'tabahi' and praises her hard work on 'Toxic'.
  • He advises her to ignore the controversy and follow her own convictions.
  • Kiara remained grounded and fully committed to the film's vision despite the backlash.

Bengaluru, August 9: Kannada superstar Yash has stepped forward to defend Bollywood actress Kiara Advani amid the online storm surrounding her song 'Toxic'. In a recent interaction, Yash reportedly referred to Kiara as 'tabahi' (destruction), lauding her performance and urging her not to let the negativity affect her.

'Whatever you believe in, do that. Don't get bothered by what others say,' Yash is said to have told Kiara, offering a strong word of support during a turbulent phase for the actress.

A Song Under Fire

'Toxic', a track from an upcoming project, has sparked significant trolling on social media. Critics and netizens have targeted Kiara's portrayal, with some questioning her dance moves and expressions. The backlash grew loud enough to catch the attention of industry insiders, prompting Yash to speak out in her defence.

The controversy, however, has not dampened the actress's spirit, according to those close to the production. Kiara, known for her roles in 'Kabir Singh' and the 'Bhool Bhulaiyaa' franchise, reportedly invested considerable effort into perfecting the performance.

'She Surrendered to the Vision'

Yash, who has himself faced scrutiny over his choices in the past, emphasised the hard work Kiara put into the song. Sources indicate he described her as 'down to earth' and someone who 'surrendered to the vision' of the director. This commitment, he suggested, is what makes the criticism unfair.

The 'KGF' star's remarks carry weight in the industry, given his own journey from television to pan-India stardom. His public backing is being seen as a rare gesture of solidarity between two film industries that often operate in silos.

A History of Trolling

Online trolling of film songs is not new, but the intensity around 'Toxic' has been notable. Several celebrities have faced similar backlash in recent years, with many choosing to stay silent. Yash's decision to address it directly has sparked conversations about how actors handle public pressure.

Kiara has not yet publicly responded to Yash's comments. Neither she nor her team has issued an official statement regarding the controversy or the support she has received.

What's Next for Kiara

Despite the noise, the actress remains focused on her upcoming releases. Industry trackers believe the controversy is unlikely to impact her career trajectory, given her track record at the box office. The song itself continues to trend, with mixed reactions.

As the debate over 'Toxic' rages on, all eyes will be on how Kiara navigates the coming weeks and whether she addresses the criticism directly. For now, Yash's words offer a reminder that in the world of entertainment, conviction often matters more than commentary.
