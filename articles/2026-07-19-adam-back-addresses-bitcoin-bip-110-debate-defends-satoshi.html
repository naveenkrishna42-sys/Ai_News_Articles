Adam Back Addresses Bitcoin BIP-110 Debate, Defends Satoshi

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Adam Back comments on the BIP-110 controversy surrounding Bitcoin's development.
  • Back defends Satoshi Nakamoto's original design decisions.
  • The debate highlights tensions between preserving Bitcoin's core principles and implementing upgrades.

Back Weighs In on BIP-110

Adam Back, the CEO of Blockstream and a prominent figure in the cryptocurrency space, has broken his silence on the ongoing Bitcoin BIP-110 controversy. In a recent statement, Back addressed the technical debate surrounding the proposed upgrade.

BIP-110, a Bitcoin Improvement Proposal, has sparked heated discussions within the developer community. Critics argue it deviates from the original vision laid out by Bitcoin's pseudonymous creator, Satoshi Nakamoto.

Defending Satoshi's Legacy

Back was unequivocal in his defence of Satoshi's work. He stated that Satoshi was not, as some have suggested, lacking in foresight. “Satoshi was not retarded,” Back said, pushing back against detractors who claim the original design is flawed.

The comment comes amid growing tensions between those who want to keep Bitcoin's code unchanged and those pushing for more aggressive updates. Back's stance aligns him firmly with the conservative camp.

The Core of the Controversy

At its heart, the BIP-110 debate is about scaling and security. Proponents argue the change is necessary to handle increasing transaction volumes. Opponents, including Back, see it as an unnecessary risk to the network's stability.

The proposal has not yet been activated. Its fate rests on community consensus, which remains deeply divided. Developers have not set a firm timeline for a decision.

A Divided Community

Back's remarks have drawn sharp reactions. Supporters applaud his defence of Bitcoin's foundational principles. Critics accuse him of stifling innovation.

The incident underscores a recurring pattern in Bitcoin's history: ideological battles between pragmatists and purists. Each camp claims to have the network's best interests at heart.

No official vote or poll has been conducted to measure community sentiment. The debate continues on mailing lists and social media platforms.

What Comes Next

For now, BIP-110 remains in limbo. Developers are expected to release a revised proposal in the coming weeks. Whether it gains enough support to move forward is uncertain.

The Bitcoin ecosystem will be watching closely. The outcome could set a precedent for how future upgrades are handled.
