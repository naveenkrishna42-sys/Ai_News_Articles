Tambaram to get bamboo park at Rs 91 lakh

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tambaram municipality sanctions Rs 91 lakh for a new bamboo park
  • Park aims to increase green cover and provide recreational space
  • Project part of broader environmental push in Chennai suburbs

Tambaram is set to add a new green lung with the municipality approving a bamboo park project at an estimated cost of Rs 91 lakh. The initiative is part of efforts to expand green cover in the rapidly growing Chennai suburb, which has seen significant urbanisation in recent years.

The park will be developed on municipal land, with plantation of multiple bamboo species. Officials said the project will not only enhance the aesthetic appeal of the area but also serve as a natural carbon sink, contributing to the local environment.

Project details and scope

The Rs 91 lakh budget covers land preparation, sapling procurement, pathways, seating arrangements, and basic amenities for visitors. The municipality has already floated tenders and expects work to commence within the next few months.

Bamboo was chosen for its fast growth and low maintenance, making it an ideal choice for urban green spaces. The park is expected to become a popular spot for morning walkers and families, offering a tranquil escape from the bustling city life.

Environmental benefits

Bamboo plants are known to absorb more carbon dioxide and release more oxygen compared to many other trees. The park will also support local biodiversity, providing habitat for birds and small animals.

Municipal officials said the project aligns with the state government's push for sustainable urban development. Similar green initiatives have been taken up in other parts of Chennai, including tree parks and vertical gardens, as the city grapples with rising temperatures and air pollution.

Residents' expectations

Local residents have welcomed the decision, noting that the area lacks sufficient recreational spaces. Many hope the park will be maintained well and remain accessible to the public.

Some have also suggested that the municipality include educational signage about bamboo's uses and benefits, making the park an informative destination for school children.

The exact location of the park has not been officially disclosed yet, but sources indicate it will be in a densely populated residential zone to maximise reach.

Way forward

Once completed, the bamboo park will add to Tambaram's civic infrastructure, which has been undergoing upgrades in roads, drainage, and street lighting. The municipality is also planning to develop other green spaces in the coming years.

The project's success will depend on timely execution and community involvement. Residents and environmental groups are urged to participate in its upkeep.

As the work gets underway, all eyes will be on the municipality to deliver a well-designed park that truly benefits the community and the environment.
