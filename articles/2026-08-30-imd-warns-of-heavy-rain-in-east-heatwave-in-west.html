IMD warns of heavy rain in East, heatwave in West

📅 2026-08-30 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • IMD predicts heavy rain in Mumbai, Odisha, while Delhi faces heatwave
  • Monsoon shifts eastward, affecting Bihar, NE states
  • Very heavy rain warning for Arunachal, Bengal, Meghalaya, Assam

IMD forecasts heavy rain in Mumbai, Odisha

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued a weather forecast predicting heavy rain in Mumbai and Odisha, while Delhi is expected to experience a heatwave. This shift in weather patterns is a result of the monsoon's eastward progression, affecting various states across India.

Monsoon's eastward shift

The monsoon has been moving eastward, bringing heavy rainfall to the eastern states. Mumbai, which has been experiencing heavy rainfall, is expected to continue receiving rain. Odisha, on the other hand, is expected to face very heavy rain, with the IMD warning of extremely heavy rain in some areas.

Heatwave in Delhi

While the eastern states are bracing for heavy rain, Delhi is expected to turn hotter. The IMD has issued a heatwave warning for the national capital, with temperatures expected to rise significantly. This is a stark contrast to the heavy rain being experienced in other parts of the country.

Impact on agriculture

The heavy rain in the eastern states is expected to have a significant impact on agriculture. The rain is crucial for the kharif crops, which are sown in the monsoon season. However, the heavy rain can also lead to flooding, which can damage crops and infrastructure. The IMD's warnings are crucial for farmers and authorities to take necessary precautions.

Very heavy rain warning for Arunachal, Bengal, Meghalaya, Assam

The IMD has issued a very heavy rain warning for Arunachal Pradesh, West Bengal, Meghalaya, and Assam. These states are expected to receive heavy rainfall, which can lead to flooding and landslides. The IMD's warnings are crucial for authorities to take necessary precautions to protect lives and property.

Forecast for Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, NE states

The IMD has also forecast heavy rainfall over Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, and the North Eastern States till Saturday. These states are expected to receive heavy rainfall, which can have a significant impact on agriculture and infrastructure. The IMD's warnings are crucial for authorities to take necessary precautions.
