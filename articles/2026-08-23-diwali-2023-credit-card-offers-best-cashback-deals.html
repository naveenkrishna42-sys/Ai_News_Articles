Diwali Offers 2023: Best Credit Card Deals

Diwali, the festival of lights, is also a time for shopping and spending. To make the most of your spending, several banks in India have announced special offers for the Diwali season. Here are the best credit card deals from SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Kotak Mahindra Bank.

SBI Credit Card Offers

10% Cashback on Fuel Purchases

SBI offers 10% cashback on fuel purchases made using their credit cards. This offer is valid for all SBI credit card holders and is applicable on both petrol and diesel purchases. The cashback is credited to your card account within 24 hours of the transaction.

5% Cashback on Groceries

SBI also offers 5% cashback on grocery purchases made using their credit cards. This offer is valid for all SBI credit card holders and is applicable on purchases made at supermarkets, hypermarkets, and online grocery stores. The cashback is credited to your card account within 24 hours of the transaction.

HDFC Bank offers 5% cashback on dining and entertainment purchases made using their credit cards. This offer is valid for all HDFC Bank credit card holders and is applicable on purchases made at restaurants, bars, and entertainment venues. 3% Cashback on Online Shopping

HDFC Bank also offers 3% cashback on online shopping purchases made using their credit cards. This offer is valid for all HDFC Bank credit card holders and is applicable on purchases made at online shopping websites. The cashback is credited to your card account within 24 hours of the transaction.

How to Apply for a Credit Card

To apply for a credit card, you need to visit the official website of the bank and fill out the online application form. You will need to provide your personal and financial details, as well as your credit score. Once your application is approved, you will receive your credit card in the mail.

To claim the Diwali offers, you need to make purchases using your credit card during the offer period. The cashback will be credited to your card account within 24 hours of the transaction. You can then redeem the cashback for future purchases or withdraw it as cash.
