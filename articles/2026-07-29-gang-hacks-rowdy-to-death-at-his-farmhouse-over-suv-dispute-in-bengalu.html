Gang hacks rowdy to death at his farmhouse over SUV dispute in Bengaluru

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • A gang hacked a rowdy to death at his farmhouse near Bengaluru.
  • The murder is believed to have stemmed from a dispute over an SUV.
  • Police have registered a case and are investigating the incident.

A gang allegedly hacked a rowdy to death at his farmhouse on the outskirts of Bengaluru late Monday night. The murder is believed to be the culmination of a dispute over an SUV.

The victim, identified as a local rowdy, was attacked by a group of men who barged into his farmhouse. The assailants used sharp-edged weapons, leaving him with grievous injuries. He died on the spot.

Dispute over an SUV

Preliminary investigations suggest that the attack was triggered by a long-standing feud over an SUV. The exact nature of the dispute remains unclear, but police sources indicate that it involved ownership or usage rights of the vehicle.

Neighbors reported hearing loud arguments and screams from the farmhouse around midnight. By the time police arrived, the attackers had fled the scene.

Police investigation underway

Bengaluru police have registered a case of murder and are on the lookout for the suspects. Forensic teams have collected evidence from the farmhouse, including blood samples and possible fingerprints.

Officials are also questioning the victim's associates and family members to establish a clear motive. They have not yet confirmed the exact sequence of events or the number of attackers involved.

Previous criminal record

The victim had a history of criminal activity, with multiple cases registered against him at various police stations in the city. Police are exploring whether the murder was an act of revenge or a result of a turf war.

Sources say the victim was known to be involved in illegal activities, and the SUV dispute may have been the final straw in a series of escalating conflicts.

Authorities have increased patrolling in the area to prevent any further violence. They have also urged local residents to come forward with any information that could help in the investigation.

The coming days will likely see more arrests as police track down the suspects. The case highlights the persistent problem of gang violence in Bengaluru's outskirts, where land and vehicle disputes often turn deadly.
