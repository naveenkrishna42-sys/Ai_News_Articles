Crane Collapse Injures 8 During Mumbai Eviction

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Eight people injured in Mumbai crane collapse during eviction
  • Authorities investigating cause of the accident
  • Eviction operation ongoing, residents displaced

Crane Collapse During Eviction Operation Leaves 8 Injured

Eight people were injured on Friday when a crane collapsed during an eviction operation in Mumbai. The incident occurred in the city's central business district, leaving residents displaced and authorities scrambling to assess the situation.

Mumbai Police and Fire Services Responding

Mumbai Police and fire services were immediately dispatched to the scene. The police have launched an investigation to determine the cause of the crane collapse. Witnesses reported that the crane was being used to demolish a building that was part of an ongoing eviction operation.

Eviction Operation Ongoing, Residents Displaced

The eviction operation was part of a larger urban redevelopment project. Residents of the affected buildings have been displaced and are currently being accommodated in nearby shelters. The local government has assured residents that efforts are being made to relocate them to safer and more suitable housing.

No Immediate Cause Confirmed

Officials have not yet confirmed the immediate cause of the crane collapse. They are reviewing CCTV footage and interviewing witnesses to gather more information. The incident has raised concerns about safety standards in construction and demolition operations in the city.

What Happens Next?

The Mumbai Police will continue their investigation to determine the cause of the crane collapse. Authorities will also ensure the safety of the displaced residents. The local government is expected to provide updates on the redevelopment project and the status of the eviction operation.
