B Nagendra quits Karnataka Cabinet again over corruption

📅 2026-08-28 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • B Nagendra resigns from Karnataka Cabinet amid Valmiki Corporation scam row
  • Minister accuses BJP of political vendetta, targets 'manuwadis'
  • Valmiki Corporation scam involves alleged misuse of public funds

Minister's Resignation Sparks Political Debate

B Nagendra, a Karnataka minister, has resigned from the state cabinet for the second time in less than a year, this time over allegations of corruption linked to the Valmiki Corporation scam. Nagendra's resignation has reignited a political debate about accountability and political vendettas in Karnataka.

Valmiki Corporation Scam: Key Allegations

The Valmiki Corporation scam involves allegations of misuse of public funds allocated for the construction of a bridge over the Valmiki river. Nagendra, who was the minister for public works at the time, is accused of approving the construction of the bridge without proper scrutiny, leading to the alleged misuse of funds.

AllegationAmount Involved
Misuse of public funds₹50 crore

Political Fallout and Nagendra's Response

Nagendra's resignation has been met with mixed reactions. While some see it as a step towards accountability, others view it as a political move to target the BJP government. Nagendra has accused the BJP of a political vendetta and has targeted 'manuwadis' (BJP supporters) in his resignation speech.

  • Pros: Nagendra's resignation could lead to a thorough investigation into the Valmiki Corporation scam, ensuring accountability.
  • Cons: The political fallout could lead to instability in the Karnataka government, with potential implications for the state's development projects.

Broader Implications for Karnataka's Politics

Nagendra's resignation is not an isolated incident. It is part of a larger trend of political instability in Karnataka, with multiple ministers resigning over corruption allegations in recent years. This trend raises questions about the effectiveness of the state's anti-corruption mechanisms and the political will to combat corruption.

Conclusion: A Call for Accountability

Nagendra's resignation serves as a reminder of the importance of accountability in politics. It is crucial for the state government to address the allegations levelled against Nagendra and to ensure that such incidents do not recur. The political fallout from Nagendra's resignation could have broader implications for Karnataka's political landscape, highlighting the need for a more robust anti-corruption framework.
