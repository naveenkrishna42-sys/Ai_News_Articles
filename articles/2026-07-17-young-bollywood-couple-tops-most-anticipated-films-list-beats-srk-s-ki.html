A young Bollywood couple has quietly overtaken the biggest names in the industry. Their upcoming films now sit at the top of India's most anticipated movies list, according to a recent survey. Shah Rukh Khan's much-awaited film King did not claim the top spot. The superstar's project, which has been in development for months, landed lower on the list. This comes as a surprise to many, given Khan's massive fan following and box office track record.

This trend suggests a generational shift in Indian cinema. Older superstars still command loyalty, but newer faces are building their own fan bases. The couple's rise is a clear indicator of this changing dynamic.
