Xiaomi Redmi Note 17 Euro Prices Leak Ahead of India Launch

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Euro pricing for the Redmi Note 17 and Note 17 Pro (Max) has been leaked online.
  • The India launch of the Redmi Note 17 5G is confirmed for August 6, 2026.
  • The leaked Euro prices suggest a potential price hike compared to previous models.

Just days before the official India launch, leaked European prices for Xiaomi's upcoming Redmi Note 17 and Redmi Note 17 Pro (Max) have surfaced online. The information, first reported by Notebookcheck, hints at a significant jump in pricing compared to the previous generation. The leak comes as Xiaomi has confirmed the Redmi Note 17 5G will launch in India on August 6.

Leaked Euro Prices

According to the leak, the base Redmi Note 17 is expected to carry a Euro price tag of around €299. The Redmi Note 17 Pro is pegged at approximately €399. The top-end Redmi Note 17 Pro (Max) could cost as much as €499.

These figures represent a noticeable increase over the launch prices of the Redmi Note 16 series in Europe. While direct conversions to Indian rupees are not straightforward due to taxes and market positioning, the leaked Euro prices suggest the Indian variants could also see a price hike. Xiaomi officials have not yet confirmed the final India pricing.

India Launch Confirmed

Xiaomi India has officially confirmed the launch of the Redmi Note 17 5G on August 6, 2026. Teasers have been circulating on social media, highlighting the device's 5G capabilities and a new design language. The company is expected to reveal full specifications and availability details at the event.

The launch is particularly significant for the Indian market, where the Redmi Note series has been a consistent top-seller in the budget and mid-range segments. With the new model, Xiaomi is likely aiming to fend off competition from rivals like Realme, Samsung, and OnePlus.

What to Expect

While official specifications remain under wraps, leaks and rumors suggest the Redmi Note 17 series will feature improved cameras, faster processors, and larger batteries. The Pro (Max) variant is expected to be the flagship of the lineup, possibly offering a 200MP main camera and a high-refresh-rate AMOLED display.

Industry analysts are watching closely to see if Xiaomi can maintain its aggressive pricing strategy in the face of rising component costs. The leaked Euro prices have raised concerns that the Indian prices may also be higher than the Redmi Note 16 series, potentially impacting sales in the price-sensitive market.

Xiaomi will officially announce the Indian pricing and availability on August 6. The company is also expected to detail the device's network band support and software update policy. All eyes will be on the final price tag to see if the Redmi Note 17 can continue the series' legacy of offering flagship-like features at a competitive price.
