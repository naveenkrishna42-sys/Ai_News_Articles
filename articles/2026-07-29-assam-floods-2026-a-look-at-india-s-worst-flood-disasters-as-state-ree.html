Assam's Flood Crisis: Toll Rises to 75

Assam continues to grapple with devastating floods as the death toll climbs to 75. Over 3.32 lakh people remain in distress, with relief operations underway across multiple districts.

Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma flagged off a Rs 16 crore relief fund on Wednesday to accelerate rebuilding efforts. The state government has prioritised restoring connectivity and providing essential supplies to marooned areas.

Survivors Recall Harrowing Escapes in Sivasagar

In Sivasagar, survivors have recounted terrifying experiences as floodwaters rose rapidly. Many lost homes and belongings, facing a long road to recovery.

Local relief teams report that thousands have been moved to temporary shelters. Officials warn that water-borne diseases remain a major concern in the coming weeks.

Bollywood Star Alia Bhatt Calls for Better Preparedness

Bollywood actor Alia Bhatt urged her fans to support flood relief efforts in Assam. She expressed dismay that the annual disaster still catches authorities off guard.

Bhatt's appeal has sparked discussions online about the need for long-term mitigation strategies. Many social media users echoed her call for proactive measures rather than reactive relief.

India's Worst Flood Disasters in History

India has witnessed several catastrophic floods. The 2013 Uttarakhand floods, triggered by cloudbursts and glacial lake outbursts, killed over 5,000 people. The 2018 Kerala floods claimed 483 lives and caused massive economic damage.

The 2019 Bihar floods, affecting over 15 million people, and the 2005 Maharashtra floods, which killed 1,200 in Mumbai, remain among the most severe. Assam itself has a history of recurrent flooding, with the Brahmaputra River swelling almost every monsoon.

Climate experts warn that extreme weather events are becoming more frequent. Poor urban planning and deforestation have worsened the impact in many regions.

What Happens Next

The Assam government has deployed additional teams for rescue and relief. Meteorologists predict more rainfall in the coming days, prolonging the crisis. Officials are monitoring river levels closely, with evacuation plans on standby for vulnerable areas.
