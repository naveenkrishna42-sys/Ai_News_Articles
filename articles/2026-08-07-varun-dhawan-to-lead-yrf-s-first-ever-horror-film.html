Varun Dhawan to Lead YRF's First-Ever Horror Film

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Varun Dhawan will star in YRF's first horror film.
  • The project marks a genre shift for the banner known for romances and action.
  • Official confirmation from the studio is awaited.

Bollywood Hungama has learned exclusively that Varun Dhawan is set to headline Yash Raj Films' first-ever horror movie. The project marks a significant departure for the banner, which has long been synonymous with romance, family dramas, and high-octane action spectacles.

Details about the film's plot, director, and co-stars remain tightly under wraps. Sources suggest the studio has been quietly developing the project for some time, and Dhawan was their first and primary choice for the lead role.

YRF's Genre Evolution

Yash Raj Films has built its reputation on iconic love stories like Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge and blockbuster action franchises such as Tiger and War. A horror entry is uncharted territory for the studio, which has rarely ventured into supernatural or thriller genres in its five-decade history.

This move signals a strategic pivot as the studio looks to diversify its slate amid changing audience tastes. Horror has emerged as a lucrative genre in Indian cinema, with recent hits proving that well-made scary films can draw crowds across demographics.

Varun Dhawan's Career Trajectory

For Dhawan, the project offers a chance to stretch beyond his established persona of the boy-next-door entertainer. The actor has previously dipped into darker roles with films like Badlapur and October, earning critical praise for his dramatic range.

His upcoming slate already includes a mix of action and comedy, but this horror venture could redefine his positioning in the industry. The actor's box-office pull combined with a fresh genre could prove to be a winning combination.

What We Know So Far

Neither Yash Raj Films nor Varun Dhawan's team has issued an official statement yet. The studio is known for keeping its projects under strict confidentiality until formal announcements are made.

Industry insiders speculate that the film might be a supernatural thriller rather than a gore-heavy slasher, given YRF's family-friendly brand image. However, no plot details or production timelines have been confirmed at this stage.

Why This Matters

YRF's entry into horror could open the floodgates for other mainstream banners to experiment with the genre. It also gives Dhawan, who has been consistently delivering hits, a chance to lead a project that breaks the mould of typical Bollywood hero vehicles.

The film's casting and crew announcements will be keenly watched in the coming weeks. If successful, this could also pave the way for more unconventional storytelling from the studio.

As the industry awaits official confirmation, all eyes are on YRF's next move. The project is expected to be formally unveiled once the script and principal crew are locked.

For now, fans and trade watchers can only speculate about what this horror collaboration will look like. The coming months will likely bring more clarity on the film's direction and release plans.
