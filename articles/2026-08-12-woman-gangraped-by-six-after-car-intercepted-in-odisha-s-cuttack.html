Woman Gangraped by Six After Car Intercepted in Odisha's Cuttack

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Incident occurred in Cuttack, Odisha, when six men intercepted a car carrying a woman and her male friend.
  • The woman was gangraped and her friend was assaulted by the accused.
  • Police have registered a case and investigations are underway.
  • The exact location and sequence of events are yet to be officially confirmed.

In a disturbing incident that has sent shockwaves through Odisha, a woman was allegedly gangraped by six men after they intercepted the car she was travelling in with a male friend. The incident took place in the Cuttack district, police said on Wednesday.

The Attack

The woman and her friend were out for a drive when their vehicle was stopped by the accused. According to preliminary reports, the men pulled the duo out of the car, assaulted the man, and then took turns raping the woman. The accused fled the spot after the crime, leaving the victims traumatised.

Local residents who noticed the commotion alerted the police. The woman was later taken to a hospital for medical examination, while her friend received treatment for injuries sustained during the assault.

Police Response

Officials have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including those dealing with rape and assault. A senior police officer said teams have been formed to trace the accused. "We are examining CCTV footage from nearby areas and questioning locals to identify the culprits," the officer said, without disclosing further details.

The exact spot where the incident occurred has not been officially confirmed, though reports suggest it happened on a stretch of road on the outskirts of Cuttack city. Police have not yet commented on whether the car was forced to stop or was blocked by the accused.

Investigation Underway

Forensic teams have been deployed to collect evidence from the scene. The victim's statement has been recorded, and her family has been informed. Authorities have assured that all necessary measures are being taken to ensure a speedy trial.

This case adds to a worrying trend of violent crimes against women in the state. In recent months, Odisha has witnessed several high-profile sexual assault cases, prompting calls for stricter policing and faster legal action.

Community Outrage

News of the incident spread quickly on social media, with many demanding justice for the victim. Local women's groups have planned protests outside the police station, urging authorities to arrest the accused at the earliest.

The district administration has promised full support to the victim, including counselling and legal aid. "We stand with the survivor and will ensure that the guilty are punished," a district official said.

As the investigation progresses, police are expected to release more details, including the possible identities of the accused. The car used in the incident has been seized for examination.

Authorities have urged the public to come forward with any information that could help identify the six men. They have also appealed for calm, stating that action will be taken against those found guilty.

The case is being monitored by senior officials, and a special investigation team may be constituted if required. The victim remains under medical supervision, and her condition is stated to be stable.

All eyes are now on the police to deliver swift justice in a case that has once again highlighted the safety challenges faced by women in the region.
