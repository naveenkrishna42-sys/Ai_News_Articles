Godzilla vs. Kong Actor Kaylee Hottle Dies in Car Crash at 18

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points

  • She was known for playing Jia in Godzilla vs. Kong.
  • Tributes led by Millie Bobby Brown have poured in.
  • Hottle came from a 'generationally deaf' family.
  • Details of the crash remain unconfirmed by officials.

Young Star Dies in Tragic Accident

Kaylee Hottle, the 18-year-old actress best known for her role as Jia in the 2021 blockbuster Godzilla vs. Kong, has died in a car crash in the United States. The news was confirmed by multiple reports on Wednesday, though authorities have not yet released official details about the accident.

Hottle, who was deaf, played a pivotal role in the MonsterVerse film as the last surviving member of a native tribe on Skull Island. Her performance earned widespread acclaim for bringing emotional depth to the action-packed franchise.

Tributes Pour In From Co-Stars

Millie Bobby Brown, who starred alongside Hottle in Godzilla vs. Kong, led the wave of tributes. The Stranger Things actor described herself as 'devastated' in a social media post, remembering Hottle's warmth and talent. Other cast members and fans have shared similar messages of grief and support.

The BBC and CNN also reported on the tragedy, highlighting Hottle's impact as a young deaf actor in Hollywood. Her death has sparked an outpouring of condolences from the entertainment industry and beyond.

From Deaf Community to Global Fame

Hottle came from a 'generationally deaf' family, as noted by the Hindustan Times. Her parents, Joshua and Ketsi Carlson, have been central figures in the deaf community. Kaylee's rise to fame was seen as a breakthrough for representation, as she used American Sign Language in her role and advocated for deaf awareness.

Before Godzilla vs. Kong, Hottle had limited acting experience, but her natural screen presence and chemistry with co-stars like Alexander Skarsgård won her the part. The film grossed over $470 crore worldwide, making her a recognizable face to millions.

What Happens Next

Details surrounding the car crash remain sparse. Officials have not disclosed the location, time, or circumstances of the accident. Family representatives have requested privacy during this difficult time. Fans and colleagues await further information as investigations continue.
