Trump Booed at World Cup Final as He Refuses to Leave Podium

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Trump and Gianni Infantino were booed by the crowd as they entered the field for the trophy ceremony.
  • Trump arrived at the stadium via a dramatic Marine One helicopter landing.
  • The former US president refused to leave the podium even as Spain lifted the World Cup trophy.
  • Argentina's loss to Spain has reignited talk of a 'Trump sports curse' after Messi's earlier comments.

The 2026 FIFA World Cup final in New York took an unexpected turn when former US President Donald Trump made a dramatic entrance via Marine One, only to be met with loud boos from the crowd. The arrival, captured on live television, showed the helicopter landing directly on the pitch before Trump emerged to join FIFA President Gianni Infantino for the trophy ceremony.

The boos grew louder as Trump and Infantino walked onto the field. Spectators, many waving Spanish and Argentine flags, made their displeasure clear. The reception stood in stark contrast to the usual pomp of a World Cup final.

Trump Refuses to Leave Podium

The situation escalated after Spain defeated Argentina to claim the title. As the Spanish team prepared to lift the trophy, Trump remained on the podium, refusing to step aside. Video footage showed him lingering even as Infantino gestured for him to move.

Broadcasters captured the awkward moment when Spanish players appeared to wait for Trump to vacate the stage. The former president eventually stepped back, but only after several seconds of visible hesitation. Social media erupted with clips of the incident, drawing sharp reactions from users across the globe.

Argentina Loss Reignites 'Trump Curse' Talk

Argentina's loss in the final has revived speculation about a so-called 'Trump sports curse.' The theory, which gained traction after Trump's previous appearances at sporting events coincided with defeats for teams linked to him, resurfaced following comments by Lionel Messi earlier in the tournament.

Messi had reportedly made remarks about Trump's presence during the World Cup. While the exact nature of those comments remains unclear, fans and analysts have pointed to a pattern. USA Today noted that the idea of a 'Trump curse' has become a recurring topic among sports commentators.

Reactions from Officials and Fans

Neither Trump nor Infantino have publicly commented on the booing or the podium incident. FIFA officials have not confirmed whether the former president's presence was officially requested. The White House did not issue a statement.

On social media, fans expressed a mix of amusement and outrage. Some called Trump's behavior disrespectful to the Spanish team, while others defended his right to attend the event. The incident has overshadowed what was otherwise a tightly contested final.

What Happens Next

With the World Cup now concluded, attention is likely to shift to how FIFA handles the controversy. No official inquiry has been announced, but the governing body may review protocols for future ceremonies. Trump's team has not indicated whether he plans to attend other international sporting events.
