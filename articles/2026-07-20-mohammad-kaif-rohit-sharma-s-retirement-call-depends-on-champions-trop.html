Mohammad Kaif: Rohit Sharma's retirement call depends on Champions Trophy

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mohammad Kaif links Rohit Sharma's retirement to Champions Trophy 2025
  • Rohit Sharma scored a resilient fifty at Lord's, ending criticism
  • India captain remains focused on 2027 World Cup, not immediate retirement

Former India cricketer Mohammad Kaif has made a bold prediction about Rohit Sharma's international future, stating that the captain's retirement hinges on the upcoming Champions Trophy in 2025. Kaif's comments come after Rohit silenced critics with a gritty fifty at Lord's, his favourite overseas venue.

Kaif's take on Rohit's timeline

Speaking to the media, Kaif said that Rohit Sharma's retirement will be decided based on his performance in the Champions Trophy. If the India skipper leads the team to victory or scores heavily, he could extend his career till the 2027 World Cup.

"Rohit is a big-match player. The Champions Trophy in 2025 will be a defining moment. If he performs well, there's no reason he can't continue till the next ODI World Cup," Kaif stated. The former batter stressed that age is not a factor given Rohit's fitness and form.

Lord's knock silences the noise

Rohit Sharma's half-century at Lord's was a statement innings. Coming into the match under pressure after a lean run, he weathered early swing and counter-attacked to steady India's innings.

"If there's no noise, there's no fun," Rohit said after the innings, brushing aside criticism. His knock at the historic ground, where he has a strong record, reaffirmed his ability to perform under scrutiny.

The India captain has often spoken about ignoring external chatter. His latest performance at Lord's followed a familiar pattern — using the occasion to remind everyone of his class.

No immediate retirement plans

Despite speculation, Rohit Sharma has not indicated any immediate retirement. Multiple reports, including from Al Jazeera, suggest he has his sights set on the 2027 World Cup.

"India's Rohit Sharma ends retirement talk as World Cup 2027 still in sight," the report noted, quoting team sources. The 38-year-old remains one of India's most important batsmen across formats.

His recent form in whites and blue has been inconsistent, but the Lord's innings provided a timely boost. Selectors are likely to persist with him for the Champions Trophy, given his leadership and experience.

What the critics say

While Kaif's statement is supportive, not everyone agrees. Some analysts argue that Rohit's place should be based on sustained performance, not just one innings. They point to his dip in average over the last 12 months.

However, the captain's ability to rise on big occasions — like the World Cup semi-final and now Lord's — keeps his detractors at bay. The Champions Trophy will be the ultimate test of his longevity.

For now, Rohit Sharma is focused on the next series, not retirement. "Picture abhi baaki hai," he seemed to say with his bat at Lord's, keeping his own English tradition alive.

The coming months will reveal whether Kaif's prediction holds. All eyes will be on the Champions Trophy, where Rohit Sharma's future may indeed be decided.
