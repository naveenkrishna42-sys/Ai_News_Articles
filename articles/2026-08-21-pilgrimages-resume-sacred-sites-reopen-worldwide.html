Pilgrimages Resume: Sacred Sites Reopen Worldwide

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Sacred Places Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pilgrimage sites worldwide are reopening after pandemic restrictions
  • Key sites include Mecca, Jerusalem, Lourdes, and Varanasi
  • Practical visiting tips include best times to visit and nearby attractions

Pilgrimages Resume Worldwide

After years of restrictions due to the pandemic, religious pilgrimages are back in full swing. Pilgrims from around the world are once again visiting sacred sites, adhering to health and safety guidelines set by local authorities.

Key Pilgrimage Sites

Mecca, Saudi Arabia

The Hajj, the annual pilgrimage to Mecca, is one of the most significant events in the Islamic calendar. Pilgrims perform rituals at the Kaaba, the most sacred site in Islam. The Hajj is typically held in the last month of the Islamic lunar calendar, Dhu al-Hijjah.

Jerusalem, Israel/Palestine

Jerusalem is a holy city for three major Abrahamic religions: Judaism, Christianity, and Islam. Key sites include the Western Wall, the Church of the Holy Sepulchre, and the Dome of the Rock. Pilgrims from all three faiths visit these sites, each holding them in high reverence.

Lourdes, France

Lourdes is a significant pilgrimage site for Catholics, known for the reputed healing of the Virgin Mary's apparitions. Pilgrims visit the Basilica of the Sacred Heart, where the Virgin Mary is said to have appeared. The site is open year-round, with a peak season during the summer months.

Varanasi, India

Varanasi, one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world, is a major pilgrimage site for Hindus. The city is sacred to Lord Shiva, and pilgrims visit the Kashi Vishwanath Temple, one of the twelve Jyotirlingas, or divine abodes of Shiva. The best time to visit is during the winter months, from October to March.

Practical Visiting Information

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit pilgrimage sites varies depending on the site. For Mecca, the Hajj is held in Dhu al-Hijjah. For Jerusalem, the site is open year-round, with peak visitation during religious festivals. For Lourdes, the summer months are the busiest. For Varanasi, the winter months from October to March are ideal.

How to Reach

Access to pilgrimage sites can vary. Mecca is only accessible to pilgrims during the Hajj. Jerusalem can be reached via air, with Ben Gurion Airport being the main international airport. Lourdes is accessible by train from Paris. Varanasi has its own airport and is well-connected by rail and road.

Nearby Attractions

Visitors to pilgrimage sites can combine their trips with nearby attractions. In Jerusalem, the Sea of Galilee and the Dead Sea offer scenic and religiously significant sites. In Lourdes, the nearby Pyrenees Mountains provide hiking opportunities. In Varanasi, the nearby city of Ayodhya is significant for Hindus, as it is believed to be the birthplace of Lord Rama.
