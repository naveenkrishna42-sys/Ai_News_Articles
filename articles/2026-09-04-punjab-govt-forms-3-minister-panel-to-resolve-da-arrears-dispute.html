Background to the DA Arrears Dispute

The DA arrears dispute in Punjab has been a long-standing issue, with employees demanding their due Dearness Allowance (DA) benefits for the past few years. The dispute has been exacerbated by the recent announcement of a mass leave by employees on September 8, 2026. The Punjab government has been under pressure to address this issue promptly to avoid any escalation of the situation.

Government's Response

In response to the escalating situation, the Punjab government has formed a three-minister panel to hold talks with the employees. The panel is expected to address the concerns of the employees and find a mutually acceptable solution to the DA arrears dispute.

The government has also announced that it will restore the Old Pension Scheme (OPS) and clear the DA dues of the employees. This move is expected to appease the employees and defuse the situation.

Employee Protests and Concessions

Employee protests have been a common feature of the Punjab government's tenure. Despite the protests, the government has been able to make some concessions to the employees. However, these concessions have not been enough to completely satisfy the employees, leading to the ongoing dispute.

Harpal Singh Cheema, a prominent leader in the opposition, has claimed that Punjab gives the highest salaries in the country. However, this claim has been disputed by the government, which has stated that the salaries are competitive and in line with the market rates.
