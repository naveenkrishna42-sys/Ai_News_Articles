Bitcoin holds above $64,000 as markets await Fed rate decision

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin is holding steady above the $64,000 mark.
  • The cryptocurrency is shrugging off weakness in AI and technology stocks.
  • Traders are focused on the upcoming Federal Reserve decision on interest rates.

Bitcoin steady above $64k

Bitcoin is trading above the $64,000 level on Wednesday, holding its ground even as technology stocks face pressure. The world's largest cryptocurrency has shown little reaction to a sell-off in AI-related shares, instead keeping its focus on the US Federal Reserve's upcoming interest rate decision.

At the time of writing, Bitcoin was hovering near $64,200, having recovered from a brief dip below $63,800 earlier in the session. The move comes as broader markets remain cautious ahead of the Fed's policy announcement later today.

Tech weakness fails to drag crypto down

Stock markets in the US and Asia have seen a pullback in AI and tech heavyweights, triggered by concerns over valuations and regulatory signals. But Bitcoin has largely shrugged off that weakness, suggesting a decoupling from the traditional tech trade.

Analysts point to growing institutional interest and a maturing market structure as reasons behind Bitcoin's relative resilience. The cryptocurrency has been trading in a narrow range for the past week, with volatility compressing ahead of the Fed event.

What the Fed decision means for Bitcoin

The Federal Reserve is widely expected to hold interest rates steady, but traders are watching for any signals on the timing of future cuts. A dovish tone could weaken the US dollar and boost demand for alternative assets like Bitcoin.

On the other hand, a hawkish surprise might trigger a risk-off move across markets. However, with Bitcoin already holding above the psychologically important $64,000 level, many traders see limited downside in the near term.

Options market data shows that open interest has risen for both call and put strikes near the current price, indicating that traders are positioning for a breakout rather than a sharp drop.

Key levels to watch

  • Support at $63,500 and $62,800.
  • Resistance at $65,000 and $66,200.

Trading volumes remain moderate, with spot inflows picking up slightly in the past 24 hours. The broader crypto market has also been steady, with Ethereum and other major altcoins trading flat to slightly higher.

The Fed decision due later today will likely set the tone for Bitcoin's next move in the short term.
