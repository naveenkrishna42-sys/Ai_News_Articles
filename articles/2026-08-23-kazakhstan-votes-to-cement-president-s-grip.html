Kazakhstan votes to cement president's grip

📅 2026-08-23 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kazakhstan holds parliamentary election expected to cement president's grip
  • International observers monitor the election process
  • Kazakhstan's future after the elections: Russia, EU or China

Kazakhstan begins parliamentary elections

Kazakhstan has begun its parliamentary elections, with voters casting their ballots to elect members of the country's Majilis, the lower house of parliament. The election is expected to further solidify the grip of President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev, who has been in power since 2019.

International observers monitor the process

International observers, including representatives from the Organization for Security and Co-operation in Europe (OSCE), are monitoring the election process to ensure it is free and fair. The observers have delivered an assessment of the country's first Kurultai vote, which is a traditional gathering of elders and leaders in Kazakh society.

Kazakhstan's future after the elections

The outcome of the elections will have significant implications for Kazakhstan's future, with the country potentially aligning itself more closely with Russia, the European Union, or China. The election results will also shape the country's domestic political landscape and its relationship with neighboring countries.

Kazakhstan prepares for a cosmetic election

Analysts suggest that the election may be more of a cosmetic exercise, with the outcome already determined by the government. This is due to the lack of opposition parties and the restrictions on political activity in the country.

What happens next

The results of the elections will be announced in the coming days, with the new Majilis members expected to be sworn in shortly after. The new parliament will then begin its work, with the potential to pass new laws and policies that will shape the country's future. International observers will continue to monitor the political landscape in Kazakhstan, ensuring that the election results are accepted and that the country's democratic processes are maintained.
