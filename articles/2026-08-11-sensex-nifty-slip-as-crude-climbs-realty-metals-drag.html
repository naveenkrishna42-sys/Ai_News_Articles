Sensex, Nifty Slip as Crude Climbs; Realty & Metals Drag

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty declined in morning trade on Tuesday.
  • Rising crude oil prices dampened investor sentiment.
  • Realty and metal stocks were the top drags on the indices.

Indian equity benchmarks traded lower on Tuesday morning, with the Sensex and Nifty slipping as a climb in global crude oil prices weighed on investor sentiment. The decline was led by losses in realty and metal stocks, which dragged the indices from their recent highs.

Crude Oil Pressure Mounts

Brent crude prices extended their upward move, stoking concerns about inflationary pressures and their impact on India's import bill. A sustained rise in oil prices can widen the current account deficit and put pressure on the rupee, which typically unnerves equity markets.

Analysts said the spike in crude was triggered by supply-side worries, though specific triggers were not immediately clear. The rise comes at a time when global markets are already grappling with uncertainty over interest rates and growth prospects.

Realty and Metals Bear the Brunt

Realty stocks were among the worst hit, with the sector index falling sharply in early trade. Metal shares also traded weak, tracking a dip in global commodity prices and profit-booking after a recent run-up.

The broader market mirrored the weakness, with midcap and smallcap indices also trading in the red. However, losses were largely contained, suggesting that investors are not in a panic mode but are trimming positions in sectors that are sensitive to input costs and global cues.

Market Breadth and Sectoral Trends

On the National Stock Exchange, the Nifty was down around half a percent, while the BSE Sensex slipped by a similar margin. Among sectoral indices, realty and metals led the decline, while defensive sectors like IT and FMCG showed relative resilience.

Market participants noted that the decline is part of a broader consolidation phase, with the indices having run up significantly over the past few weeks. The current pullback is seen as a healthy correction, though sustained crude price hikes could trigger deeper cuts.

What to Watch

Investors will now keep a close eye on crude oil trajectories and any domestic or global policy signals. The direction of foreign institutional flows and quarterly earnings updates will also play a role in determining near-term market direction.

With no major domestic triggers due in the immediate session, the market's movement is likely to be dictated by global cues and commodity prices. Traders are advised to stay cautious until clarity emerges on the crude front.
