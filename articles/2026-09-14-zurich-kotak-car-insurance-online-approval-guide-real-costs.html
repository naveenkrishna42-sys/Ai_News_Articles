What 'Instant Approval' Actually Means for Zurich Kotak Car Insurance

Zurich Kotak General Insurance markets a fully digital car insurance journey: enter your registration number, verify details, pay, and receive a policy document by email within minutes. That is a real convenience, but buyers must separate policy issuance from claim approval. No insurer approves a claim before an accident happens.

The 'instant' label refers to the proposal and issuance pipeline, not underwriting of claims. For a new car or a renewal with no break-in, the system auto-issues a cover note. For older vehicles, modified cars, or those with a claims history, the portal may still route the file to a human underwriter.

Claim Settlement: The Metric That Outweighs Every Discount

Before buying, check the insurer's most recent public claim settlement ratio and average settlement turnaround, published in IRDAI annual reports and the insurer's own disclosures. A 2-minute purchase is worthless if a claim drags for weeks. Ask specifically about cashless approval at your preferred garage and the documents needed for reimbursement claims.

For a renewal, compare three quotes with identical IDV and add-ons. Third-party premiums are fixed by IRDAI, so any 'discount' there is misleading. Real savings come from own-damage pricing, NCB, and rider selection — not from the speed of the checkout page.
