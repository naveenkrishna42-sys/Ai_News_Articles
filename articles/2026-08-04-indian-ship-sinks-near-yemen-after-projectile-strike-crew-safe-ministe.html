Indian ship sinks near Yemen after projectile strike, crew safe: minister

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Indian vessel sank after being hit by a projectile near Yemeni waters.
  • All seafarers on board are safe, the Indian shipping minister said.
  • No details on the ship's name or crew size have been officially released yet.

An Indian vessel has sunk near Yemeni waters after being struck by a projectile, India's shipping minister said on Tuesday. The minister confirmed that all seafarers aboard are safe, though he did not provide further details about the crew or the ship's identity.

Incident details remain sparse

The attack took place in waters off Yemen, an area that has seen heightened maritime tensions in recent months. The minister's statement, brief and to the point, offered no specifics on the type of projectile involved or the exact location of the sinking.

Officials have not yet confirmed the name of the vessel, its cargo, or the number of people on board. Shipping industry sources were not immediately available for comment.

Regional context

The Red Sea and Gulf of Aden have become flashpoints for naval incidents, with several commercial ships reporting attacks. The region's strategic chokepoints are vital for global trade, and any disruption draws immediate attention from international maritime agencies.

India has maintained a naval presence in the area to protect its commercial interests and ensure safe passage for its vessels. The minister's reassurance of crew safety will likely be followed by a broader review of security protocols for Indian ships transiting these waters.

Response and rescue efforts

Rescue operations were reportedly initiated swiftly, though the minister did not elaborate on the nature of the rescue or whether naval assets were involved. It remains unclear if the crew has been brought to shore or is still being assisted at sea.

The Indian government has not issued a formal statement beyond the minister's remarks. The Ministry of External Affairs and the Indian Navy have not commented on the incident as of this report.

What to watch

Further details are expected to emerge in the coming hours, including the ship's registration and the crew's nationality. The incident could prompt renewed calls for enhanced maritime security in the region, and India's response may set the tone for how it handles similar threats in the future.
