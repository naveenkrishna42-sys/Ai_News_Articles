FIIs near record lows, Jio BlackRock sees potential rebound

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • FIIs are near historical lows, with inflows showing signs of recovery
  • FIIs may be tactical, not structural, according to HSBC MF's Venugopal Manghat
  • FIIs are flocking to financials and extending buying spree in IT sector
  • Jio BlackRock flags potential contrarian setup for Indian equities

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) Near Historical Lows

Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) in India are near historical lows, according to recent reports. This has sparked interest from market analysts and investors alike, with Jio BlackRock flagging a potential contrarian setup for Indian equities.

Signs of Recovery in FII Inflows and Valuations

The inflows of FIIs and the valuations of Indian equities have shown signs of recovery, according to a report by BusinessLine. This recovery could be a sign of a potential rebound in the Indian equity market.

FPIs' Return to India May Be Tactical, Not Structural

Venugopal Manghat, Managing Director and Chief Investment Officer of HSBC Mutual Fund, has suggested that the return of foreign portfolio investors (FPIs) to India may be tactical, not structural. This means that the FPIs may be looking to take advantage of the current market conditions, rather than making a long-term investment.

FIIs Flocking to Financials and Extending Buying Spree in IT Sector

FIIs have been flocking to the financial sector, with a significant increase in buying spree. Additionally, FIIs have been extending their buying spree in the IT sector, according to a report by Rediff MC. This could be a sign of increased confidence in these sectors.

Jio BlackRock Flags Potential Contrarian Setup for Indian Equities

Jio BlackRock has flagged a potential contrarian setup for Indian equities. This could be a sign of a potential rebound in the Indian equity market, with investors looking to take advantage of the current market conditions.
