CJP Gives Centre Ultimatum: Withdraw Cases or Face Protests Again Tomorrow

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • CJP has issued a warning to the Centre to withdraw cases or face protests from tomorrow.
  • The party claims the government is breaching a prior agreement reached during earlier talks.
  • Security remains tight in central Delhi ahead of the potential new protests.

CJP Issues Fresh Ultimatum to Centre

The Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) has given the Centre a clear warning: withdraw all cases against protesters or face renewed demonstrations from Tuesday. The group has accused the government of breaching a prior agreement that was meant to de-escalate tensions.

In a statement released on Monday, CJP leaders said they were left with no choice but to resume protests if their demands are not met. The warning comes amid an ongoing police crackdown in several states.

Allegations of a Broken Deal

CJP has flagged the detention of its supporters in multiple states, claiming the government is not honouring its earlier assurance. The party says a deal was struck during previous rounds of talks, but authorities have continued to file FIRs and make arrests.

“The government is breaching the deal. If cases are not withdrawn, we will protest again,” a senior CJP functionary said. The group has demanded the immediate release of all detained protesters and the dropping of all charges.

Security Tightened in Central Delhi

Authorities have not taken any chances. Security remains tight in central Delhi, with police barricades and personnel deployed in large numbers around key government buildings and protest sites. Officials are bracing for a possible surge in agitation if the CJP follows through on its threat.

The Delhi Police have not yet issued an official statement on the CJP’s latest warning. The force has been under pressure to maintain order while also handling the legal aspects of the ongoing cases.

What Triggered the Standoff?

The CJP’s protest movement had seen a temporary lull after what both sides described as a breakthrough in talks. However, the group now claims that the government’s actions on the ground do not match its words. The arrest of several key activists in recent days appears to have reignited the confrontation.

The Centre has not publicly responded to the CJP’s latest ultimatum. Sources indicate that internal discussions are underway to assess the situation and decide on a course of action.

With the deadline set for Tuesday morning, all eyes are on Delhi. The coming hours will determine whether the standoff escalates into a fresh wave of protests or whether the government moves to defuse the situation by withdrawing cases.
