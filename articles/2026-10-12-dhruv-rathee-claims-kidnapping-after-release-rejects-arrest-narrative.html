Dhruv Rathee Claims 'Kidnapping' After Release, Rejects Arrest Narrative

Rathee's Post-Release Statement Sparks Legal Controversy

In a viral social media post hours after his release, YouTuber Dhruv Rathee rejected the government's narrative, stating he was not formally arrested or detained. Instead, he described his experience as being 'kidnapped' by unknown individuals posing as law enforcement officers.

Rathee's statement, shared across multiple platforms, claimed he was forcibly taken from his residence in South Delhi and held incommunicado for several hours. He further alleged that his Indian passport was seized during the incident, which occurred just hours before a scheduled protest related to the Chief Justice of India's office.

Official Police Response Contradicts Claims

Delhi Police issued a formal statement denying Rathee's allegations, asserting that his detention was both lawful and procedurally correct. Senior police officials confirmed that Rathee was questioned as part of a routine investigation into alleged violations of Section 144 restrictions in the national capital.

The police clarified that Rathee was released on personal bond with directions to maintain peace and not disturb public order. They also confirmed that no passport was seized from him, though his laptop and mobile devices were temporarily confiscated for forensic examination.

Legal Experts Weigh In on 'Kidnapping' Assertion

Constitutional lawyer Sanjay Hegde called Rathee's characterization of events 'legally unsustainable,' noting that the remedies under Article 226 were available but not pursued. 'If there was indeed a kidnapping, the appropriate recourse would be a writ petition. The fact that no such legal action has been initiated raises questions about the veracity of these claims,' Hegde told reporters.

Political Fallout and Public Reaction

The controversy has drawn sharp reactions across the political spectrum. While opposition parties have expressed support for Rathee, calling the incident 'undemocratic,' ruling party leaders have dismissed the allegations as an attempt to malign security forces.

Social media campaigns #JusticeForRathee and #ExposeTheTruth have trended for over 12 hours, collectively generating over 2.3 million posts according to platform analytics firm Croww.

Timeline of Events Leading to Detention

According to available information, Rathee was taken into custody around 10:30 AM after police received intelligence about his participation in an unauthorized gathering near Jantar Mantar. He was questioned for approximately four hours before being released.

Fact-Checking the Passport Claim

Rathee's assertion about his passport being seized has been independently verified as inaccurate. Government sources confirmed that his passport remains with him, and no travel restriction orders were issued. The confusion may stem from standard protocol during questioning where travel documents are sometimes photographed for record purposes.
