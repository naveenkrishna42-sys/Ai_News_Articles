Vivo S2 Teaser Image Leaks, Key Specs Surface Ahead of India Launch

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • A leaked teaser image shows the Vivo S2 design with a large camera module.
  • The phone is tipped to launch in India in early August after a seven-year gap for the S-series.
  • Expected price is under Rs 50,000, targeting the premium mid-range segment.

Leaked Teaser Reveals Design

A new teaser image of the upcoming Vivo S2 has surfaced online, giving a first look at the phone’s design. The image, shared by tipsters, shows a large rectangular camera module on the rear, housing multiple sensors and an LED flash.

According to reports, the Vivo S2 will feature a curved display with a hole-punch cutout for the front camera. The phone appears to have a glass back and a metal frame, consistent with premium mid-range offerings.

Key Specifications Emerge

Alongside the teaser, key specifications of the Vivo S2 have also leaked. The phone is expected to be powered by a MediaTek Dimensity 7200 chipset, paired with up to 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage.

The display is tipped to be a 6.7-inch AMOLED panel with a 120Hz refresh rate. On the camera front, the Vivo S2 could sport a 50-megapixel primary sensor, an 8-megapixel ultrawide lens, and a 2-megapixel macro camera. A 32-megapixel front camera is expected for selfies.

Battery and Charging Details

The device is likely to pack a 4,500mAh battery with support for 66W fast charging. The phone is also expected to run Android 14 with Funtouch OS 14 out of the box.

Earlier reports had suggested a launch in early August, with pricing under Rs 50,000 in India. However, Vivo has not officially confirmed any details yet.

Return of the S-Series After Seven Years

The Vivo S2 marks the return of the S-series in India after nearly seven years. The company had previously launched the Vivo S1 in 2019, but the series was discontinued after that. The S2 is expected to revive the lineup with a focus on design and camera performance.

The phone will compete with other premium mid-range devices such as the OnePlus Nord 4, Nothing Phone (3), and the iQoo Neo 9 Pro. The exact launch date and availability details are expected to be announced in the coming weeks.

What to Watch For

With the teaser and specs now out in the open, all eyes are on Vivo for an official confirmation. The company is expected to begin sending media invites soon, which should clarify the launch timeline and pricing for the Indian market.
