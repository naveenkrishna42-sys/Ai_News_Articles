Realme 16x 5G Debuts in India with 7,000mAh Battery, 144Hz Display

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Realme 16x 5G launched in India with a 7,000mAh battery and 144Hz display.
  • The phone is powered by MediaTek Dimensity 6300 chipset.
  • Expected to be priced under Rs 26,000, competing in the budget 5G segment.

Realme expands 5G lineup with battery-focused 16x

Realme has officially launched the 16x 5G in India, bringing a 7,000mAh battery and a 144Hz display to the budget segment. The device, which was teased for weeks, is positioned as a mid-range contender with a clear emphasis on endurance and smooth scrolling.

The phone arrives as an upgrade to the Realme 15x, though the company has not yet detailed full pricing or availability. Reports suggest a significant price hike over its predecessor, with analysts expecting the 16x to sit under the Rs 26,000 mark.

Battery and display take centre stage

The 7,000mAh cell is the headline feature, a rarity in this price bracket. Realme claims the capacity is paired with optimisations that should stretch usage to two days for moderate users. The 144Hz refresh rate on the display is another standout, promising fluid animations for gaming and social media browsing.

While the screen size and resolution have not been officially confirmed, leaked specifications point to a large LCD panel with a punch-hole cutout. The 144Hz mode is likely adaptive, letting the phone drop to lower rates when static to conserve power.

Dimensity 6300 under the hood

Powering the 16x 5G is MediaTek's Dimensity 6300 chipset, a 6nm processor designed for entry-level 5G devices. It is paired with multiple RAM and storage configurations, though the exact variants for India remain unannounced.

Early reviews indicate the chip handles everyday tasks with ease, but heavy gaming may push the thermal limits. The phone runs on Realme UI, based on a recent Android version, with the company's usual suite of customisation options.

Camera and software details remain under wraps

Realme has stayed quiet on the camera setup. Based on leaks, the 16x 5G could feature a dual rear camera system led by a 50-megapixel primary sensor, supplemented by a depth or macro lens. A 16-megapixel front camera is expected for selfies.

Software updates are another open question. The brand has not specified how many OS upgrades or security patches the device will receive, a factor that often influences long-term value in this segment.

What to expect at launch

The company is expected to reveal final pricing and sale dates in the coming days. With competitors like the Redmi Note series and POCO X lineup in the same range, the 16x 5G will need to leverage its battery and display credentials to stand out.

Watch for early flash sales and introductory bank offers, which could temporarily lower the effective price. Analysts will also be tracking whether the 7,000mAh cell sets a new benchmark for budget 5G phones in India.
