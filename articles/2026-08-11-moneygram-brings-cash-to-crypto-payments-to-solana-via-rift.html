MoneyGram Brings Cash-to-Crypto Payments to Solana via Rift

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • MoneyGram integrates with Solana through Rift to enable cash-to-crypto transactions.
  • The service targets users without traditional banking, allowing cash deposits for crypto purchases.
  • This marks a significant step in bridging fiat and digital currencies in emerging markets.

MoneyGram and Rift: A New Bridge to Crypto

MoneyGram has announced a strategic partnership with Rift, a payments infrastructure firm, to enable cash-to-crypto transactions directly on the Solana blockchain. The move allows users to convert physical cash into digital assets at MoneyGram locations worldwide, tapping into a vast network of retail agents.

This collaboration is aimed at simplifying crypto adoption for individuals who rely on cash, particularly in regions where banking access is limited. By integrating Solana's high-speed, low-cost network, the service promises near-instant settlements, a marked improvement over traditional banking transfers.

How the Service Works

Users can walk into any participating MoneyGram outlet, hand over cash, and receive the equivalent value in a supported cryptocurrency, which is then credited to their Solana wallet. The process is designed to be as straightforward as a standard money transfer, eliminating the need for a bank account or credit card.

Rift's technology handles the backend, ensuring compliance with anti-money laundering (AML) regulations and real-time conversion rates. The companies have not yet disclosed which specific tokens will be supported at launch, but Solana's native SOL is expected to be among the initial options.

Expanding Financial Inclusion

MoneyGram has been steadily venturing into the crypto space, having previously experimented with stablecoin services and partnerships with other blockchain networks. This new initiative with Rift is a significant step forward, as it directly addresses the cash-dependent population that forms a large part of its customer base.

According to industry analysts, the move could accelerate crypto adoption in developing economies, where remittances and cash-based transactions dominate. By leveraging MoneyGram's existing infrastructure across over 200 countries, the service could bring millions of unbanked individuals into the digital asset ecosystem.

Regulatory and Market Implications

The announcement comes amid growing regulatory scrutiny of crypto services worldwide. MoneyGram has stated that it will operate in full compliance with local laws in each jurisdiction, a factor that may limit availability in certain markets initially.

Solana's price saw a modest uptick following the news, reflecting investor optimism about real-world use cases for the blockchain. The partnership is seen as a validation of Solana's scalability, which has often been questioned during periods of network congestion.

Observers note that this could pressure other money transfer giants, such as Western Union, to accelerate their own crypto strategies. However, no official comments have been made by competitors so far.

What's Next for Cash-to-Crypto?

The service is expected to roll out in select markets within the coming months, with a broader expansion planned thereafter. MoneyGram and Rift are likely to announce specific launch dates and supported cryptocurrencies soon.

For now, the partnership signals a growing trend: bridging the gap between traditional finance and decentralized digital assets. As more companies follow suit, cash-to-crypto may become as commonplace as sending a wire transfer.
