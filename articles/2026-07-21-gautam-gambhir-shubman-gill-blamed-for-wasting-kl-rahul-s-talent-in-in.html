Gautam Gambhir, Shubman Gill blamed for wasting KL Rahul's talent in India ODI setup

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • KL Rahul's inconsistent position in the batting order is under scrutiny after India's ODI series loss to England.
  • Former cricketers and commentators have blamed head coach Gautam Gambhir and captain Shubman Gill for 'wasting' a genuine batter.
  • Comparisons are being drawn to the handling of Sanju Samson and Vaibhav Suryavanshi, suggesting a pattern of mismanagement.

Frustration boils over after England ODI series loss

Criticism is mounting against the Indian team management, specifically head coach Gautam Gambhir and captain Shubman Gill, for the way they have handled KL Rahul in the recent ODI series against England.

Following India's series defeat, several former cricketers and analysts have voiced their frustration, accusing the duo of 'absolutely wasting' a batsman with 'unreal numbers'. The core of the complaint is Rahul's constantly shifting role in the batting order.

'Just like you screwed up Vaibhav Suryavanshi'

The criticism has been sharp and pointed. One prominent comment compared Rahul's situation directly to that of young opener Vaibhav Suryavanshi, who was dropped after a brief run last year. The remark, widely shared on social media, stated: "Just like you screwed up Vaibhav Suryavanshi, you are now wasting KL Rahul."

Another commentator drew a parallel to wicketkeeper-batter Sanju Samson, who has also struggled to find a consistent place in the XI. The argument is that Gambhir and Gill are repeating a pattern of mismanagement that has already cost India talented players.

Rahul's 'unreal numbers' vs. inconsistent role

KL Rahul's statistics make the situation harder to defend. In ODIs, he averages over 45 and has a strike rate of 88, with seven centuries and 14 fifties. These are numbers that would secure a fixed spot for most players in world cricket.

Yet, in the England series, Rahul was shuffled between the middle order and the top, never given a clear mandate. This lack of clarity, critics argue, is sapping his confidence and preventing him from playing his natural game.

Online hate and bullying called out

The debate has also highlighted the darker side of fan reaction. Rahul, who has faced online abuse in the past, was again the target of targeted trolling after the series loss. A tweet from a verified user went viral: "Who gets the blame? It's KL Rahul again."

Multiple sports journalists have called out the 'online hate and bullying' directed at the batter, pointing out that the real problem lies with team selection and strategy, not with Rahul's effort or talent.

What happens next for Rahul and the team

The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) and the team management are under pressure to clarify Rahul's role before the next international series. With the Champions Trophy scheduled for next year, India cannot afford to waste a batsman of his calibre.

Whether Gambhir and Gill will stick with their current approach or make a course correction will be the key question in the coming weeks.
