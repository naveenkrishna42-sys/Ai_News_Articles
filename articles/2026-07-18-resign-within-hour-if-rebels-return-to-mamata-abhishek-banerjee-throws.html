Resign within hour if rebels return to Mamata: Abhishek Banerjee throws down challenge

📅 2026-07-18 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Abhishek Banerjee challenges TMC rebels to return to Mamata Banerjee
  • Offers to resign from his party position within one hour if they do so
  • Statement seen as attempt to consolidate party ahead of elections

Blunt challenge to dissenters

Trinamool Congress national general secretary Abhishek Banerjee has thrown a sharp challenge to rebel party leaders. He said he will resign from his post within an hour if they return to the party fold under Mamata Banerjee's leadership.

The statement, made on Saturday, signals an escalation in the TMC's internal dynamics. It comes amid reports of factionalism and dissident voices within the West Bengal ruling party.

What Abhishek said

Addressing party workers, Abhishek Banerjee dared the rebels to come back. He offered to step down from his organisational role immediately if they pledge allegiance to Mamata Banerjee, who is widely referred to as 'Didi' within the party.

“If any rebel leader returns to Didi, I will resign from my party post within one hour,” he said. The remark was met with applause from loyalist supporters present at the event.

Banerjee did not name any specific individuals. But political observers note that the challenge is aimed at leaders who have either left the party or are seen as working against its interests from within.

Context of the challenge

The TMC has faced several exits in recent months. Some former MPs and MLAs have either joined the BJP or floated their own political outfits. The party also saw a section of leaders express dissatisfaction over organisational matters.

By offering to resign, Abhishek Banerjee is attempting to call the bluff of dissenters. He is essentially asking them to either return and prove their commitment or stop complaining about the current leadership.

Party insiders say the move is also an effort to project unity ahead of upcoming local body elections in West Bengal. The TMC wants to avoid any public display of infighting that could hurt its electoral prospects.

Reactions from political circles

Opposition parties in the state were quick to react. BJP leaders termed the statement a “publicity stunt” and accused the TMC of trying to cover up internal contradictions. Left Front leaders said it reflected the “growing panic” within the ruling party.

Within the TMC, the challenge has been received positively by loyalists. Several district-level leaders expressed confidence that the party would remain united under Mamata Banerjee's command. However, some independent political analysts say the dare could backfire if no rebel leader takes up the offer.

What happens next

The ball is now in the court of the rebel leaders. It remains to be seen whether any of them will respond to Abhishek Banerjee's offer. Political watchers will be closely watching for any movement in the TMC's internal equations in the coming days.
