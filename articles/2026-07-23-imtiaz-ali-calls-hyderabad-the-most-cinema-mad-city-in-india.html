Imtiaz Ali Calls Hyderabad the Most Cinema-Mad City in India

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Imtiaz Ali called Hyderabad the craziest city about cinema
  • He praised the energy and passion of Hyderabad's film audience
  • The filmmaker spoke during a recent event in the city

Acclaimed filmmaker Imtiaz Ali has declared Hyderabad as the most cinema-obsessed city in India. The director, known for films like Jab We Met and Rockstar, made the remark during a recent interaction in the city.

“People in Hyderabad are the craziest about cinema,” Ali said. He was speaking at an event where he discussed his journey and the evolving film culture in the country.

Unmatched Passion for Films

Ali highlighted the unique energy that Hyderabad’s audience brings to the theatres. He noted that the city’s film lovers engage with movies on a deeper level than most other places.

“The enthusiasm here is infectious,” he added. The filmmaker did not elaborate on specific examples but emphasised the palpable love for storytelling among Hyderabadi audiences.

Why Hyderabad Stands Out

Hyderabad has long been a hub for both Telugu and Hindi cinema. The city’s multiplexes and single-screen theatres often see packed houses for a wide range of films.

Ali’s comments come as no surprise to local film buffs. Many believe the city’s diverse cultural fabric makes it a fertile ground for cinematic appreciation.

A Filmmaker’s Perspective

Imtiaz Ali is known for his nuanced portrayals of love and relationships. His films often resonate with audiences across India, but he finds something special in Hyderabad.

The director did not compare Hyderabad to other film-loving cities like Mumbai or Chennai. Instead, he focused on the raw, unfiltered passion he observed among Hyderabadi moviegoers.

Officials at the event confirmed that Ali spoke extemporaneously. His remarks were met with applause from the audience, many of whom are avid followers of his work.

What This Means for Local Cinema

Ali’s statement is likely to boost the morale of Hyderabad’s film community. Local directors and producers often struggle for recognition in a market dominated by Bollywood and the Telugu film industry.

The comment also underscores the growing importance of regional audiences in shaping national film trends. Hyderabad, with its blend of tradition and modernity, is increasingly seen as a trendsetter.

Exact details of the event where Ali spoke have not been disclosed. The filmmaker is currently working on his next project, details of which remain under wraps.

For now, Hyderabad’s film lovers can take pride in being called the “craziest” by one of India’s most respected directors. The city’s love for cinema shows no signs of waning.

Ali is expected to return to Hyderabad for the promotion of his upcoming film. Whether he will repeat his praise remains to be seen.
