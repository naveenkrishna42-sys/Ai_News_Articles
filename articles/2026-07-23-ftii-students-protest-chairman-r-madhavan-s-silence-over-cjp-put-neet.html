FTII Students Protest Chairman R Madhavan's Silence Over CJP, Put 'NEET For Sale' Posters On His Door

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • FTII students posted 'NEET For Sale' and 'Gayab' posters on chairman R Madhavan's office door.
  • The protest targeted Madhavan's initial silence on the CJP paper leak controversy and student protests.
  • Madhavan later broke his silence, issuing a statement supporting the demand for a fair system.
  • The action comes amid a nationwide debate over NEET paper leaks and student safety.

Posters Go Up At FTII

Students at the Film and Television Institute of India (FTII) in Pune targeted chairman R Madhavan on Wednesday. They pasted posters on his office door that read 'NEET For Sale' and 'Gayab', mocking his silence over protests related to the Common Judiciary Process (CJP) paper leak.

The posters were a direct response to the actor's failure to comment on the ongoing student agitation. The CJP paper leak has sparked nationwide outrage, with students demanding justice for a victim who died by suicide.

Silence Broken

For days, R Madhavan remained quiet as the protests grew louder. Students at FTII, known for their activism, decided to turn the spotlight on their own chairman. The 'Gayab' poster was a reference to his perceived absence from the conversation.

Madhavan eventually broke his silence. In a statement, he backed the student protests, saying, 'Every student deserves a system that is fair.' The comment came after he was widely trolled on social media for his delayed response.

What The Posters Said

The posters were placed prominently on Madhavan's office door. One read 'NEET For Sale', a sarcastic jab at the alleged corruption in the exam system. Another simply said 'Gayab', meaning 'missing' or 'absent'.

The students said they were holding him accountable as the chairman of a premier institution. They argued that silence in the face of injustice is complicity.

Context Of The Protests

The CJP paper leak has been a flashpoint across India. Protests have erupted in multiple states, with students demanding stricter laws and accountability. The death of a student linked to the scandal has only intensified the anger.

FTII students have a history of political activism. In the past, they have protested against administrative decisions and national issues. This time, they chose to target a well-known figure in the entertainment industry who also heads their institute.

Madhavan, who took over as FTII chairman in 2023, has faced criticism before for his handling of campus issues. The latest protests mark a fresh challenge to his leadership.

Reaction And Next Steps

Social media has been divided over the incident. Some have praised the students for their boldness, while others have questioned the method of protest. Madhavan has not directly addressed the posters but his statement on the CJP issue suggests a shift in stance.

Officials at FTII have not commented on whether the posters have been removed or if any action will be taken against the students. The institute's administration is yet to release an official statement.

What remains to be seen is whether Madhavan will engage further with the students or if the protest will simmer down. With the CJP controversy still unresolved, the spotlight on FTII and its chairman is unlikely to fade soon.
