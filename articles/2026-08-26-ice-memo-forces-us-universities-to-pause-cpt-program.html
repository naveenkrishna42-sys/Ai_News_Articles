ICE Memo Forces Universities to Pause CPT Program

The U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) has issued a new memo, compelling universities to pause the Curricular Practical Training (CPT) program. This development has left Indian students enrolled in CPT programs in a state of uncertainty.

Impact on Indian Students

Indian students who are currently on CPT are at risk of losing their status if they do not have proper authorization. The new memo requires universities to verify the eligibility of each student before authorizing CPT. This means that Indian students who have been working on CPT for a while may now face difficulties in continuing their work.

Universities Must Verify Eligibility

Universities are now required to verify the eligibility of each student before authorizing CPT. This means that students must make an official request for authorization. The new policy has led to a significant number of universities pausing the CPT program, leaving students in a state of uncertainty.

American Students Also Affected

American students are also affected by the new policy. They will now have to complete training before they can participate in the CPT program. This means that American students who have been working on CPT for a while may now face difficulties in continuing their work.

Conclusion

The new memo issued by ICE has forced universities to pause the CPT program. This has left Indian students on CPT in a state of uncertainty. The new policy requires universities to verify the eligibility of each student before authorizing CPT. This means that Indian students who have been working on CPT for a while may now face difficulties in continuing their work. American students are also affected by the new policy, and they will now have to complete training before they can participate in the CPT program.
