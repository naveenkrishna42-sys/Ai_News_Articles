Mother’s Day 2026: 7 Bollywood Films To Watch With Your Mom

  • Movies span genres from drama to comedy, highlighting maternal love.

Celebrating Mother’s Day with Cinema

Mother’s Day 2026 is here, and what better way to honour the bond than with a movie marathon? Bollywood has a rich history of films that centre on mothers—their sacrifices, joys, and unwavering love. Whether you’re looking for a tearjerker or a light-hearted comedy, these seven films offer something for every mood.

From classic black-and-white dramas to contemporary family sagas, the list spans decades. Each film brings a unique perspective on motherhood, making them perfect for a shared viewing experience.

1. Mother India (1957)

No list of mother-centric Bollywood films is complete without this landmark movie. Nargis plays Radha, a poor village woman who fights against all odds to raise her sons. The film won India’s first Oscar nomination for Best Foreign Language Film.

Its themes of resilience and sacrifice remain timeless. Watching it with your mom can spark conversations about strength and tradition.

2. Piku (2015)

This Amitabh Bachchan-Deepika Padukone starrer is a modern classic. It explores the relationship between a father and daughter, but the mother’s presence is felt throughout. The film’s focus on family, ageing, and everyday quirks makes it a warm, relatable watch.

Piku’s mother, played by Moushumi Chatterjee, adds layers of humour and tenderness. It’s a film that celebrates the small, messy moments of family life.

3. Kahaani (2012)

Vidya Balan delivers a powerhouse performance as a pregnant woman searching for her missing husband in Kolkata. The film redefines the mother archetype—showing her as intelligent, determined, and fiercely independent.

It’s a thriller that keeps you on edge while highlighting a mother’s love and grit. Perfect for moms who enjoy suspense.

4. English Vinglish (2012)

Sridevi plays Shashi, a homemaker who learns English to gain respect from her family. The film is a gentle reminder that mothers are often underestimated. Her journey to self-confidence is inspiring and funny.

This movie resonates with many Indian mothers who balance tradition with modern aspirations. It’s a feel-good pick for the day.

5. Mili (1975)

Jaya Bhaduri plays Mili, a cheerful young woman battling a terminal illness, while her mother quietly supports her. The film captures the silent sacrifices mothers make. It’s a tearjerker but leaves you with a sense of hope.

The relationship between Mili and her mother is understated yet powerful. A must-watch for those who appreciate emotional depth.

6. Neerja (2016)

This biopic of Neerja Bhanot, a flight attendant who saved passengers during a hijack, features a strong mother-daughter dynamic. Neerja’s mother, played by Shabana Azmi, is a pillar of strength and courage.

The film celebrates bravery and the values instilled by a mother. It’s an inspiring choice for Mother’s Day.

7. Dil Dhadakne Do (2015)

This ensemble film about a dysfunctional family on a cruise includes a poignant mother-son story. Anil Kapoor and Shefali Shah play parents with complex relationships. The mother, played by Shah, is both loving and flawed.

The movie balances humour and emotion, making it a great pick for a family movie night. It reminds us that mothers are human too.

These films, available on various streaming platforms, offer a mix of nostalgia and contemporary storytelling. They celebrate the many faces of motherhood—from traditional to modern, from quiet strength to loud laughter.

This Mother’s Day, grab some popcorn, settle on the couch, and let these stories bring you closer. After all, the best gift is time spent together.
