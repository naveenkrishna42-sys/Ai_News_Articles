Apple Launches iPhone, iPad and Mac Leasing Program in the US

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple launches device leasing program in the US in partnership with Klarna.
  • iPhones, iPads and Macs available for lease with monthly payments starting at $17.99.
  • Program called Apple Upgrade, offering flexibility to upgrade devices annually.

Apple Shifts from Ownership to Leasing

Apple has launched a device leasing program in the United States, allowing customers to rent iPhones, iPads and Macs instead of buying them outright. The initiative, called Apple Upgrade, is part of a partnership with the Swedish fintech company Klarna.

Under the program, customers can lease a new iPhone for as low as $17.99 per month. The monthly fee varies depending on the device model and configuration chosen.

How the Apple Upgrade Program Works

The leasing model offers users the flexibility to upgrade their devices every year. At the end of the lease term, customers can either return the device, continue paying monthly, or upgrade to a newer model.

Apple has not disclosed the full terms of the lease agreement, including any down payment requirements or interest rates. However, Klarna’s involvement suggests the program may include a buy-now-pay-later structure.

Strategic Shift in Hardware Sales

This move marks a significant shift for Apple, which has traditionally relied on outright sales and carrier financing. Leasing could make Apple’s premium-priced devices more accessible to a broader customer base.

The program is currently available only in the United States. Apple has not announced plans to expand the leasing option to other markets, including India.

Competition and Consumer Impact

Several smartphone makers already offer leasing or subscription models. Samsung and Google have similar programs in select markets. Apple’s entry could intensify competition in the device financing space.

For consumers, the leasing option eliminates the need for a large upfront payment. However, customers will not own the device unless they choose to buy it at the end of the lease term.

Apple has not detailed how the program handles accidental damage or loss. It is unclear whether AppleCare+ will be mandatory or included in the monthly fee.

What to Watch Next

Industry analysts will watch for customer adoption rates and whether Apple extends the leasing program to other countries. The partnership with Klarna may also signal deeper collaboration between the two companies in financial services.
