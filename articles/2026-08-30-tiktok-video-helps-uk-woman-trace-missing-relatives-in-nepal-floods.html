Tulshi Patel, a UK-based Odia woman, is on a desperate mission to find her mother and aunt in Nepal after they went missing during the devastating floods. Patel, who is originally from Odisha, India, used a TikTok video posted by her mother to trace their last known location.

The video, which was posted before the floods, shows Patel's mother and aunt in a familiar location. The video, which has been shared widely on social media, has become a crucial clue in the search operation.
