PSBB Group of Schools marks 69th anniversary with art-centric celebrations

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • PSBB Group of Schools celebrated 69 years of educational excellence in Chennai
  • The anniversary festivities were themed around art, creativity, and student expression
  • Events included performances, exhibitions, and activities highlighting artistic talents

CHENNAI: The PSBB Group of Schools marked its 69th foundation day with a celebration that put creativity at the centre stage. The anniversary, held across its campuses, brought together students, teachers, and alumni in a showcase that revolved entirely around the arts.

The theme, 'Art flowing in the AIR', set the tone for a day that turned classrooms and corridors into galleries and stages. From visual installations to live performances, the event underlined the school's long-standing emphasis on holistic education.

A tribute to artistic expression

Students from various age groups participated in a range of activities, including painting, sculpture, music, and dance. The corridors of the school displayed colourful artwork, reflecting months of preparation. Teachers guided students in curating exhibits that spanned traditional Indian art forms as well as contemporary styles.

The celebrations were not merely about display. Workshops and interactive sessions allowed younger students to engage with senior artists and alumni who returned to share their experiences. This intergenerational exchange formed a key part of the day's programme.

Performances that captured attention

The main auditorium hosted a series of performances that drew applause from a packed audience. Classical dance recitals, a school band's rendition of popular numbers, and a short theatre piece on the life of a fictional artist were among the highlights. The students' energy was palpable, and their discipline on stage reflected the training they receive throughout the academic year.

One of the most talked-about segments was a collaborative mural, where students from different houses painted a single large canvas over several hours. The finished work, depicting the journey of a child from curiosity to mastery, was unveiled by the school's principal during the closing ceremony.

Alumni and community connect

The anniversary also served as a reunion of sorts. Several former students, now professionals in fields ranging from design to engineering, were invited to speak about how their PSBB education shaped their careers. Their talks touched on the importance of creativity in problem-solving, a message that resonated with both students and parents in attendance.

Parents who visited the campus expressed appreciation for the school's effort to balance academics with extracurricular pursuits. Many noted that such events help children discover talents they might otherwise overlook in a routine school schedule.

Looking back, moving forward

PSBB Group of Schools, which began its journey nearly seven decades ago, has grown into one of Chennai's most respected educational institutions. The 69th anniversary was a reminder of its founder's vision — that education must nurture the mind as well as the soul.

The event concluded with a vote of thanks and a pledge by students to carry the spirit of creativity into their daily lives. School officials said the response this year has encouraged them to consider making the arts an even more prominent part of the curriculum in the coming academic session.

As the institution inches towards its 70th year, the focus will likely remain on innovation in teaching methods while preserving the cultural ethos that has defined it for generations. Plans for a larger, week-long celebration next year are already being discussed.
