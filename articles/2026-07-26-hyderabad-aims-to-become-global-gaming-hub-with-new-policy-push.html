Hyderabad aims to become global gaming hub with new policy push

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hyderabad is developing a dedicated policy to boost the gaming and esports sector.
  • The city aims to attract major gaming companies and create thousands of jobs.
  • Officials are focusing on infrastructure, talent development, and investment incentives.

Hyderabad is making a serious bid to put itself on the world map of gaming and esports. The Telangana government is crafting a dedicated policy to transform the city into a global hub for game development, animation, and competitive gaming.

Officials say the move is aimed at capitalising on the rapid growth of the gaming industry in India and abroad. The city already has a strong base of IT and tech talent, which the government believes can be leveraged to build a thriving gaming ecosystem.

Policy to focus on infrastructure and incentives

The proposed gaming policy is expected to offer a mix of financial incentives, infrastructure support, and regulatory ease. State officials are studying successful models from cities like Seoul, Montreal, and Shanghai to design a framework that works for Hyderabad.

Key components under discussion include setting up dedicated gaming zones, providing tax breaks for new studios, and creating a single-window clearance system for approvals. The government is also looking at ways to encourage local startups and indie developers.

“We want to create an environment where global gaming companies see Hyderabad as a natural destination for their operations,” a senior official from the IT department said.

Job creation and talent pipeline

One of the primary goals of the policy is job creation. The gaming industry requires a mix of programmers, designers, artists, and marketers. Hyderabad’s existing pool of engineering graduates and creative professionals gives it a head start.

The government is also planning to partner with universities and private institutes to offer specialised courses in game design, 3D modelling, and esports management. This is intended to build a steady pipeline of skilled workers for the industry.

Industry experts estimate that a focused push could create tens of thousands of direct and indirect jobs in the city over the next five years. The state is also keen on attracting major esports tournaments and events to Hyderabad.

Competition with other Indian cities

Hyderabad is not alone in chasing this dream. Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Pune have also been making moves to attract gaming companies. However, officials believe Hyderabad can stand out by offering lower operational costs and a more proactive government approach.

The city already hosts several large gaming studios, including international firms that have set up development centres here. The new policy aims to build on this base and encourage more companies to set up shop.

“Hyderabad has the potential to be a top-five global gaming destination if we play our cards right,” a local industry representative said.

Focus on esports and live events

Esports is a major part of the plan. The government is exploring the creation of a dedicated esports arena that can host national and international tournaments. This would draw players, sponsors, and audiences to the city.

Live gaming events are seen as a way to build a community and give the city a visible presence in the global gaming calendar. Officials are also looking at tax incentives for event organisers.

The policy is expected to be finalised and announced within the next few months. The state is currently in talks with industry bodies and potential investors to fine-tune the details.

What happens next will depend on how quickly the government can roll out the policy and how effectively it can market Hyderabad to the global gaming community.
