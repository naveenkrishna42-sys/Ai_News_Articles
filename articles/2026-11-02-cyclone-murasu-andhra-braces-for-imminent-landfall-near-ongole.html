Cyclone Murasu: Andhra Braces for Imminent Landfall Near Ongole

Coastal Andhra Pradesh is on high alert as Cyclone Murasu intensifies, with landfall projected for approximately 4 AM on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The severe cyclonic storm is expected to cross the coast near Ongole in Prakasam district, bringing with it destructive winds and torrential rainfall. Authorities have initiated extensive preparedness measures, including the evacuation of vulnerable populations, to mitigate potential devastation.

The Impending Landfall and Meteorological Threat

Cyclone Murasu has steadily gained strength over the Bay of Bengal, posing a significant threat to the Andhra Pradesh coastline. Meteorological forecasts indicate that the storm will make landfall with sustained wind speeds reaching up to 120 kmph, capable of causing widespread damage to infrastructure and communication networks.

Path, Intensity, and Wind Velocity

The storm's current trajectory places Ongole at the direct path of impact. Residents in Prakasam district and surrounding coastal areas are advised to brace for extreme weather conditions. The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued red alerts, emphasizing the severity of the approaching cyclone and its potential for rapid intensification just before landfall.

Associated Hazards: Rain and Storm Surge

Beyond high winds, Cyclone Murasu is expected to trigger heavy to very heavy rainfall across coastal Andhra Pradesh. Northern Tamil Nadu has already begun experiencing significant rainfall, indicating the expansive reach of the storm's outer bands. A storm surge, potentially several feet high, remains a critical concern for low-lying coastal communities, exacerbating the risk of flooding and inundation.

Coordinated Response: Evacuation and Preparedness

State and national disaster response agencies have been mobilized, implementing a multi-pronged strategy to ensure public safety and minimize casualties. The focus remains on proactive evacuations and establishing robust relief mechanisms.

Prakasam District on High Alert

Prakasam district, identified as the primary impact zone, has been placed on high alert. District administrations are working round the clock to shift vulnerable groups, particularly those residing in temporary structures and fishing villages along the coast, to designated relief camps. These camps are equipped with essential supplies, medical facilities, and sanitation arrangements to accommodate evacuees safely.

Multi-Agency Deployment and Political Oversight

Teams from the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) are strategically deployed across critical locations, ready for search and rescue operations. State disaster management authorities, local police, and volunteers are coordinating efforts to ensure smooth evacuations and public awareness. Senior political figures, including Pawan Kalyan, have urged officials to maintain heightened vigilance, particularly along the Nellore coast, emphasizing the need for swift and effective response mechanisms.

Broader Impact and Public Safety Advisories

The ripple effects of Cyclone Murasu extend beyond the immediate landfall zone, affecting travel and necessitating public caution across the broader region.

Travel Disruptions and Regional Effects

In anticipation of the severe weather, at least 19 flights have been cancelled across affected regions, disrupting air travel schedules. Road and rail networks are also expected to face significant disruptions as the storm progresses. Coastal states are on standby, preparing for potential impacts even if they are not directly in the storm's path, highlighting the need for regional preparedness.

Essential Safety Measures for Residents

Authorities advise residents in affected areas to remain indoors, secure their homes, and avoid coastal activities. It is crucial to monitor official advisories from disaster management agencies and local authorities. Emergency kits, including food, water, medications, and power banks, are recommended. Residents should also be prepared for potential power outages and communication disruptions in the aftermath of the storm.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • Prakasam district is under high alert, with extensive evacuations of vulnerable populations underway.
  • Wind speeds up to 120 kmph and heavy rainfall are expected, posing significant risks.
  • NDRF teams are on standby for search and rescue operations.
  • At least 19 flights have been cancelled, impacting regional travel.
  • Heavy rains have already commenced in northern Tamil Nadu, indicating a wider affected zone.
