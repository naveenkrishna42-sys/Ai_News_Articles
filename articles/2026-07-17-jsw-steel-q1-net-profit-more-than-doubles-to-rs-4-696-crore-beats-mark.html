JSW Steel Q1 net profit more than doubles to Rs 4,696 crore, beats market estimates

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Net profit jumped to Rs 4,696 crore, more than double YoY
  • EBITDA rose 24% in the June quarter
  • Revenue growth driven by higher sales volumes and better realisations

JSW Steel has kicked off the new fiscal year on a strong note. The company reported a consolidated net profit of Rs 4,696 crore for the first quarter of FY27, more than double the profit recorded in the same period last year. The figure comfortably exceeded analyst estimates, sending a positive signal about demand recovery in the domestic steel market.

Profit surge beats expectations

The Mumbai-headquartered steelmaker posted a net profit of Rs 4,696 crore for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. This marks a significant improvement over the corresponding quarter of the previous fiscal, when the company had reported a lower base. Market analysts had expected a profit in the range of Rs 4,000-4,200 crore, making the actual result a clear beat.

Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortisation (EBITDA) jumped 24% year-on-year, reflecting better operational efficiency and cost control. The company's margins also improved during the quarter, helped by stable input costs and higher realisations.

Revenue growth driven by volumes

The strong bottom line was backed by a healthy top-line performance. JSW Steel's revenue from operations grew on the back of higher sales volumes. The company managed to push through more steel in both domestic and export markets, benefiting from robust demand from infrastructure and automotive sectors.

While the company did not break down segment-wise revenue in detail, the overall trend indicates that the Indian steel industry is riding a wave of government-led capital expenditure and private sector investment. JSW Steel, being one of the largest private producers, is a direct beneficiary of this cycle.

Cost management aids margins

Steelmakers globally have been grappling with volatile raw material prices. However, JSW Steel appears to have navigated these challenges effectively. The company's focus on operational efficiencies and a favorable product mix helped cushion the impact of any cost pressures.

The 24% jump in EBITDA underscores that the company not only grew sales but also protected its profitability. This is a key metric that investors track closely, as it shows the underlying health of the business beyond just the headline profit number.

Outlook for the rest of FY27

The management is expected to provide more details on demand trends and capacity utilisation in the coming days. With the government's continued push on infrastructure projects and a revival in the real estate sector, the outlook for domestic steel consumption remains positive.

JSW Steel's performance in Q1 sets a strong benchmark for the rest of the fiscal year. Investors and analysts will now watch for commentary on pricing power, export opportunities, and any impact from global trade policies. The company's ability to sustain this momentum will be key in the quarters ahead.
