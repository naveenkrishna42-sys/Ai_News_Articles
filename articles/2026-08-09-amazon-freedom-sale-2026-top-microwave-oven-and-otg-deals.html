Amazon Freedom Sale 2026: Top Microwave Oven and OTG Deals

Key points
  • Amazon Freedom Sale 2026 offers significant discounts on microwave ovens and OTGs.
  • Top brands like Samsung, LG, and Bajaj are part of the sale.

Sale Highlights for Kitchen Appliances

Amazon's Freedom Sale 2026 is underway, and among the many categories seeing heavy discounts, microwave ovens and OTGs (oven toaster grillers) are drawing particular attention. The sale, timed around India's Independence Day, brings price cuts on models from top brands, making it a good window for upgrading kitchen gear.

Shoppers can find deals on both basic solo microwaves for reheating and defrosting, and convection models that support baking and grilling. The discounts vary by brand and model, with some products seeing reductions of thousands of rupees.

What to Look For in a Microwave

For most households, a solo microwave with a capacity of 20 litres or more is sufficient for everyday tasks. These models are typically the most affordable in the sale. If you bake regularly, a convection microwave with a larger capacity, around 28-32 litres, offers more flexibility.

Stainless steel interiors are a practical choice for easy cleaning and durability. Some models also include pre-set menus for Indian cooking, which can simplify operations for less tech-savvy users.

OTG Options for Baking Enthusiasts

OTGs remain popular among those who prefer traditional baking without the complexity of microwave functions. These appliances heat evenly and are ideal for cakes, cookies, and roasted dishes. In the Freedom Sale, OTGs from brands like Bajaj and Morphy Richards are available with discounts on their standard 30-litre and 50-litre variants.

When choosing an OTG, consider the wattage and the availability of accessories like baking trays and tongs. Some models also feature a rotisserie function, which expands the range of dishes you can prepare.

Brands and Availability

Major brands participating in the sale include Samsung, LG, IFB, and Bajaj. While the discounts are attractive, availability may vary across regions and stock levels. Some deals are time-sensitive, so early browsing is advisable.

Amazon has also bundled offers with select bank cards, providing additional cashback or no-cost EMI options on higher-priced models. These can bring down the effective cost further, especially for premium convection microwaves.

How to Make the Most of the Sale

Compare prices across models before making a decision. The same capacity microwave can differ significantly in price based on brand and features. Look for customer reviews to gauge heating consistency and durability, which are common pain points.

Also, check the warranty terms. Most brands offer a standard one-year warranty on the product and a longer period on the magnetron, which is the core component. Confirming these details at the time of purchase can save trouble later.

If you are replacing an old appliance, some sellers on Amazon also offer exchange bonuses, reducing the final price further. This is worth exploring if you have an old microwave or OTG lying around.

The sale is expected to continue for a few more days, with new deals being added periodically. For those who miss out on a particular model, it may return during upcoming festive sales, though prices are unlikely to be as low.

Keep an eye on lightning deals, which offer steep discounts but for limited quantities. Setting up deal alerts on the app can help you catch these before they sell out.
