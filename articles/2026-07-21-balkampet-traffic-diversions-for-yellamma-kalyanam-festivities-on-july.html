Balkampet Traffic Diversions for Yellamma Kalyanam Festivities on July 21-22

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Traffic diversions in Balkampet on July 21 and 22 for Yellamma Kalyanam and Rathotsavam.
  • Diversions begin at 4 PM each day; roads near Yellamma Temple blocked.
  • Alternate routes via Balkampet Road and Ameenpur Road advised.

Traffic Advisory for Balkampet Festivities

The Hyderabad traffic police have issued a traffic diversion plan for Balkampet on July 21 and 22, in view of the Yellamma Kalyanam and Rathotsavam celebrations. The festival attracts large crowds, and authorities aim to ensure smooth movement of devotees and vehicles.

Diversions will be in effect from 4 PM each day until the conclusion of the events. Commuters are advised to plan their travel accordingly.

Roads Affected and Diversion Plan

Vehicles will not be allowed on the stretch from Yellamma Temple to Balkampet crossroads during the festival hours. Traffic will be diverted at key points to avoid congestion.

Motorists coming from the Ameenpur side will be diverted towards the old Mumbai highway. Those travelling from the Balkampet side will be directed towards the Ameenpur Road.

Alternate Routes for Commuters

Authorities have suggested alternate routes for those needing to cross the area. Commuters can use the road via Kukatpally or the inner ring road to bypass the festival zone.

Local residents and emergency vehicles will be allowed passage with proper identification. Police personnel will be stationed at diversion points to assist the public.

Public Advisory

The traffic police have urged commuters to cooperate and follow the directions of officers on the ground. They recommend using public transport or carpooling to reduce vehicle density near the temple.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact end time for the diversions on each day. The restrictions will be lifted once the procession and rituals conclude.

Devotees attending the Yellamma Kalyanam and Rathotsavam are advised to arrive early and park in designated areas. Parking will be available at specified lots near the temple.

The police have also warned against parking on main roads. Violators may face fines or towing.

This festival is an annual event in Balkampet, drawing thousands of participants. The Rathotsavam procession typically involves a chariot carrying the deity through the streets.

Commuters are advised to check for real-time updates from the Hyderabad traffic police on social media. The diversions are expected to cause some delays, especially during evening peak hours.

Local businesses in the area have been informed about the road closures. They have been asked to plan their logistics accordingly.
