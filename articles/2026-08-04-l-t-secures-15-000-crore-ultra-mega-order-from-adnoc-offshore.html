L&T secures ₹15,000 crore 'ultra-mega' order from ADNOC Offshore

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • L&T bags an 'ultra-mega' order worth over ₹15,000 crore from ADNOC Offshore.
  • The contract covers offshore engineering, procurement, and construction work.
  • L&T shares rose following the announcement, reflecting investor confidence.

Mumbai: L&T lands massive offshore deal

Larsen & Toubro (L&T) has clinched an 'ultra-mega' order from ADNOC Offshore, valued at over ₹15,000 crore. The engineering and construction giant announced the contract win on Tuesday, marking one of its largest recent overseas orders.

The scope of work spans offshore platforms, pipelines, and associated facilities for ADNOC Offshore, a subsidiary of the Abu Dhabi National Oil Company. L&T's hydrocarbon division will execute the project, which is slated for completion over the next few years.

What the order means for L&T

This order bolsters L&T's already robust order book, which stood at record levels in the last fiscal year. The company has been aggressively expanding its presence in the Middle East, particularly in the UAE and Saudi Arabia, as energy majors ramp up capacity.

For ADNOC Offshore, the contract is part of a broader strategy to boost crude production capacity to 5 million barrels per day by 2027. The award underscores L&T's competitive edge in offshore engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) services.

Market reaction

Investors responded positively, with L&T shares rising in early trade on Tuesday. The stock has been a consistent outperformer in the capital goods space, supported by a strong pipeline of domestic and international orders.

Brokerages have largely maintained a positive stance on the stock, citing the company's execution capabilities and diversified portfolio. The ADNOC order is expected to contribute meaningfully to revenue visibility over the medium term.

Strategic significance

This win comes amid intensifying competition in the global offshore EPC market, where players from South Korea, China, and Europe vie for lucrative contracts. L&T's ability to secure such a large order reflects its technical expertise and cost competitiveness.

The company has previously executed several projects for ADNOC, including offshore installations and pipeline networks. This repeat business is a testament to L&T's reliability in handling complex, large-scale energy infrastructure.

What to watch next

Analysts will be tracking L&T's execution timeline for this project and its impact on margins, given the high capital intensity of offshore work. The company is also expected to pursue further opportunities in the region as Gulf nations accelerate energy transition investments.

With the order book at a comfortable cushion, L&T's focus shifts to timely delivery and cost management. The market will also look for updates on other mega tenders in the pipeline, particularly in the renewable and green hydrogen segments.
