The 83-Year-Old's Workout Secret

Amitabh Bachchan's trainer has revealed what the actor's fitness routine looks like at 83. The regimen, according to sources, is built around consistency rather than intensity.

Bachchan, who has been a fitness icon for decades, follows a tailored programme. It includes light cardio, stretching, and functional exercises designed to maintain mobility and strength.

The trainer emphasised that the routine is adapted to Bachchan's age and recovery needs. The focus is on staying active without risking injury.

Health Clarifications After Concern

This revelation comes after a period of public concern over Bachchan's health. A blog post by the actor had sparked rumours about a surgery.

Bachchan clarified that he did not undergo any surgery. He said the post was misunderstood. The actor stated that his post-surgery homecoming is the toughest phase, referencing an earlier procedure that was not new.

Several outlets, including NDTV and Rediff, reported the clarification. Bachchan denied being hospitalised, calling the speculation a misunderstanding.

What the Routine Includes

Details of the workout remain sparse, but the trainer shared that the routine is not about heavy weights or high-impact exercises. It involves controlled movements, breathing exercises, and short sessions.

The aim is to ensure Bachchan can continue his demanding work schedule. The actor has several film projects lined up, and his fitness is key to his professional commitments.

The trainer noted that Bachchan's discipline is remarkable. He sticks to the routine even when travelling or working late.

Background of the Health Scare

The recent health scare began when a blog post by Bachchan mentioned a difficult recovery. Some interpreted this as a sign of a new surgery.

Bachchan quickly took to social media to set the record straight. He said the post referred to an old surgery and that he was fine.

Telegraph India and MSN also covered the clarification. The actor stressed that he was not hospitalised and that his health was stable.
