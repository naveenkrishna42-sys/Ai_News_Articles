East Bengal secures record-equalling 17th Durand Cup title

East Bengal has added another chapter to its illustrious history by clinching the Durand Cup for the 17th time. The Red and Gold Brigade secured a 4-1 victory over Mohun Bagan in the final held in Kolkata. This achievement not only cements East Bengal's status as the most successful club in the Durand Cup but also highlights the team's dominance in Indian football.

Key moments in the final

Habas' tactical acumen was on full display as he orchestrated a comprehensive 4-1 victory over Mohun Bagan. Edmund's hat-trick was the standout performance, with his clinical finishing and ability to find the back of the net proving decisive. The victory was a testament to East Bengal's attacking prowess and Habas' strategic brilliance.
