Telangana Ganesh Pandals Get Free Power: What It Means for Organisers

  • Telangana has put arrangements in place to supply free electricity to Ganesh pandals across the state, with the Hyderabad region a key focus.
  • Free power directly cuts a major cost line for pandal committees, which typically spend heavily on generators and diesel during the festival.
  • Organisers must still meet safety, metering and load-declaration norms to qualify; unauthorised connections remain a disconnection risk.

Telangana has moved to supply free electricity to Ganesh pandals across the state, with arrangements now in place ahead of the festival period. For pandal committees in Hyderabad and the wider state, this removes one of the heaviest line items in their budget: powering lighting, sound systems, decorative installations and cooling equipment for days on end.

The Hindu reported the development on 13 September 2026, confirming that the supply arrangements are ready. The practical details matter more than the announcement — eligibility, load limits, metering and safety compliance decide whether a committee actually benefits.

What the Free Power Arrangement Covers

Free supply applies to electricity drawn for pandal operations, typically routed through a temporary connection sanctioned by the distribution company. This covers illumination, public address systems, fans, and in many cases the refrigeration needed for prasadam and water distribution.

Committees that would otherwise run diesel generator sets stand to gain the most. Diesel costs during a 7-10 day festival can run into several tens of thousands of rupees for a mid-sized pandal, and well over a lakh for large installations in crowded parts of Hyderabad.

The arithmetic is simple. A committee running a 15 kVA diesel generator for eight hours a day over ten days spends close to ₹1.5-2 lakh on fuel alone, before maintenance and operator charges. Free grid supply wipes out that expense.

Pros and Real-World Highlights

  • Direct cost relief: eliminates diesel and generator rental spending for eligible committees.
  • Cleaner festival operations: grid power cuts local emissions and noise compared to generator sets.
  • Better safety oversight: sanctioned connections bring wiring under utility inspection, reducing fire risk in dense pandal structures.
  • Predictable budgeting: committees can redirect savings to decoration, prasadam and cultural programmes.
  • Conditional benefit: unauthorised hook-ups still attract penalties and disconnection.

What Organisers Must Do

Committees need to apply through the local distribution company with details of the pandal location, expected load and duration. Wiring must meet safety norms, and the connection point should be accessible for inspection. Load declarations that understate actual draw risk tripping and mid-festival disruption.

For large pandals in Hyderabad's older quarters, where narrow lanes and dense crowds complicate cabling, early coordination with the utility matters. Committees that finalise their sanctioned load well before installation day avoid last-minute scrambles.

Buyer and Committee Takeaway

Free power is a genuine financial lever, not a token gesture. A committee that qualifies saves enough to fund an entire additional day of festivities or upgrade its lighting and sound setup. The catch is procedural: valid application, honest load declaration and compliant wiring.

Committees comparing options should treat free grid supply as the default choice and keep a generator only as emergency backup for critical loads. That combination balances cost, safety and uninterrupted celebration.
