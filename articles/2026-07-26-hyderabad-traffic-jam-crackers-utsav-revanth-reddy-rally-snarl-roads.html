Hyderabad traffic jam: Crackers Utsav, Revanth Reddy rally snarl roads

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Massive traffic jams hit Hyderabad on Saturday due to two simultaneous events.
  • The Crackers Utsav and a public rally by Chief Minister Revanth Reddy clogged major intersections.
  • Commuters reported delays of over two hours on key routes like Begumpet and Ameerpet.
  • Police diverted traffic but struggled to manage the combined crowd and vehicle load.
  • Officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of vehicles or people affected.

Hyderabad ground to a halt on Saturday as a massive traffic jam gripped the city. Two events—the Crackers Utsav and a public rally by Chief Minister Revanth Reddy—clashed, leaving commuters stranded for hours.

Key roads from Begumpet to Ameerpet turned into parking lots. Vehicles crawled, and many drivers abandoned their cars to walk. The situation worsened by evening as offices closed and festival-goers hit the streets.

Crackers Utsav draws crowds, blocks arteries

The Crackers Utsav, a popular pre-Diwali event, saw thousands of families and vendors gather at designated zones. Organizers had set up stalls and entertainment, but the influx of cars and buses overwhelmed narrow roads.

Traffic police had deployed extra personnel, but they could not prevent bottlenecks at key junctions like Paradise Circle and Punjagutta. Many commuters reported waiting over two hours to cross a single intersection.

Revanth Reddy rally adds to chaos

Simultaneously, Chief Minister Revanth Reddy addressed a public meeting at Nizam College grounds. Supporters poured in from surrounding districts, adding to the vehicle density. The rally itself was peaceful, but the logistics of parking and crowd movement snarled traffic for kilometers.

Police diverted traffic from the area, but the diversions pushed vehicles onto already clogged alternate routes. Commuters heading towards Secunderabad and Hi-Tech City faced the worst delays.

Commuters vent frustration

Social media lit up with complaints. Many users posted photos of unmoving traffic and empty fuel tanks. Auto-rickshaw drivers refused trips, and cab fares surged due to high demand.

One commuter, a software professional traveling from Gachibowli to Kukatpally, said he spent three hours on the road. "I left office at 5 PM and reached home at 8 PM. Usually it takes 40 minutes," he told reporters.

Police officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of vehicles affected or the total delay caused. They advised commuters to plan alternate routes and avoid the area until late night.

What happens next

Authorities are reviewing the traffic management plan for future events. With Diwali and election season approaching, similar congestion could repeat. Officials may stagger timings of public gatherings to prevent another gridlock.
