Toxic’s Day‑15 Box‑Office Plunge: 20% Drop Raises Alarm

Key points

  • The film’s earnings are now below the industry break‑even threshold of ₹12 crore.
  • Distributors and theatre owners face reduced returns, forcing renegotiations on future slate deals.

Box‑Office Trajectory of “Toxic”

Yash’s latest multi‑star venture opened with a modest ₹8.5 crore net on day 1, far short of the ₹15 crore opening benchmark set by his previous blockbuster ‘Kavach’. The initial buzz waned as mixed reviews highlighted a disjointed screenplay, leading to a steady slide in footfall.

By day 10, cumulative net earnings hovered around ₹45 crore, still above the ₹40 crore mark but well below the ₹55 crore target projected by trade analysts. The latest report from the Times of India shows a 20 % drop on day 15, taking the net to ₹5.8 crore for that single day and pushing the total to roughly ₹58 crore.

Implications for Stakeholders

For distributors, the 20 % slump translates into a direct hit on their margin calculations. The industry’s break‑even point for a film of this scale sits at roughly ₹12 crore net per day; ‘Toxic’ is operating at less than half that, forcing distributors to renegotiate revenue shares or seek ancillary monetisation such as OTT rights.

Theatre owners, especially in Tier‑2 and Tier‑3 cities, are witnessing lower seat‑occupancy rates, prompting them to replace ‘Toxic’ with regional releases that promise higher per‑screen yields. This shift could affect the film’s remaining run length, potentially curtailing its total gross by another ₹5‑7 crore.

What’s Next for “Toxic”?

Producers have hinted at an accelerated OTT launch within the next 30 days, a strategy that may recover a portion of the lost revenue through licensing fees estimated at ₹10‑12 crore. Simultaneously, a targeted promotional push focusing on Kiara Advani’s fan base is planned to revive interest in the remaining urban screens.

Analysts advise investors to monitor the film’s per‑screen average over the next week; a rebound above ₹1.2 crore could stabilize the downtrend, while continued decline may trigger a full theatrical pull‑out.

Key Real‑World Highlights

  • Day‑15 net fell 20 % to ₹5.8 crore, extending a two‑week decline.
  • Screen count dropped by 450 venues since opening, reflecting weak demand.
  • Break‑even threshold of ₹12 crore per day remains unmet, pressuring distributors.
  • Potential OTT deal could salvage up to ₹12 crore in ancillary revenue.
  • Targeted star‑centric marketing may offer a modest uplift in Tier‑1 markets.
