Millennials, Gen Z shift spending to food, wellness during pandemic

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Lifestyle Original source ↗
Key points
  • Millennials and Gen Z spend more on food and wellness during pandemic
  • Healthcare and fitness services see increased demand
  • E-commerce platforms witness surge in orders for health products

Millennials and Gen Z shift spending priorities during pandemic

Millennials and Gen Z in India are altering their spending habits during the COVID-19 pandemic, with a greater emphasis on food and wellness. According to recent data, these younger demographics are allocating more of their budgets to food and health-related services.

Healthcare and fitness services see increased demand

Healthcare and fitness services have witnessed a surge in demand as people prioritize their well-being. Gyms and yoga studios have seen an increase in memberships, while online health consultations have also seen a rise. People are more conscious about their health and are willing to invest in services that help them maintain a healthy lifestyle.

E-commerce platforms witness surge in orders for health products

E-commerce platforms have also seen a significant increase in orders for health-related products. From protein supplements to home workout equipment, people are buying more health products than ever before. The pandemic has made people more aware of the importance of health and they are willing to spend more on products that help them stay fit and healthy.

Food delivery services see increased demand

Food delivery services have also seen a surge in demand. People are more health-conscious and are looking for healthier options. This has led to an increase in the number of people ordering healthy meals and snacks. The pandemic has also made people more aware of the importance of nutrition and they are willing to spend more on food that is good for their health.

Conclusion

The pandemic has forced Millennials and Gen Z to rethink their spending priorities. They are now spending more on food and wellness, indicating a shift in their values and priorities. This trend is likely to continue as people become more health-conscious and prioritize their well-being.
