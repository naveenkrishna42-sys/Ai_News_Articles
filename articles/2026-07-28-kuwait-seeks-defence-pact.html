Kuwait Seeks Defence Pact

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kuwait seeks defence pact with Pakistan
  • No Pakistani troops to be sent to Kuwait
  • Deal may include intel sharing and military cooperation

Defence Pact Consideration

Kuwait is exploring the possibility of a Saudi-style defence pact with Pakistan. The move comes as Kuwait faces pressure from Iran. Pakistan and Saudi Arabia have a long-standing defence cooperation agreement, which includes intelligence sharing and military training.

Islamabad has ruled out the possibility of sending Pakistani troops to Kuwait. The decision is likely to be welcomed by Pakistan's military, which is already engaged in various operations within the country.

Potential Terms of the Deal

The potential defence pact between Kuwait and Pakistan may include provisions for intelligence sharing, military training, and cooperation in counter-terrorism efforts. The deal could also lead to increased economic cooperation between the two countries.

Visit by Kuwaiti Foreign Minister

The Kuwaiti foreign minister is scheduled to visit Pakistan from July 28 to 29. The visit is expected to focus on strengthening bilateral ties and discussing the potential defence pact. Pakistan's military strategy and its implications for regional security are likely to be key topics of discussion.

Regional Implications

The potential defence pact between Kuwait and Pakistan could have significant implications for regional security. It may lead to increased cooperation between Pakistan and other Gulf countries, potentially altering the regional balance of power. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact terms of the proposed agreement.

A deal between Kuwait and Pakistan could also impact India's relations with both countries. India has traditionally maintained good relations with Kuwait and has been seeking to strengthen ties with Pakistan.

Next Steps

The visit by the Kuwaiti foreign minister to Pakistan will be closely watched. The outcome of the discussions is expected to provide clarity on the potential defence pact and its implications for regional security.

What happens next will depend on the progress made during the visit. If an agreement is reached, it could lead to a significant shift in regional dynamics, with potential implications for India and other countries in the region.
