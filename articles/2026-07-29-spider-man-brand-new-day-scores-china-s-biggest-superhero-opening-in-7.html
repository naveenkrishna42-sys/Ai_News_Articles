Spider-Man: Brand New Day Scores China's Biggest Superhero Opening in 7 Years

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tom Holland's Spider-Man: Brand New Day opens to China's biggest superhero debut in seven years.
  • Film earns over ₹400 crore globally in opening weekend, with strong Indian box office projections.
  • Critics praise the film as a mature, grounded take on the superhero genre.

Tom Holland's latest outing as the web-slinger has swung into Chinese theatres with a record-breaking opening. 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' has recorded the biggest superhero opening in China in seven years, marking a significant rebound for Hollywood in the world's second-largest film market.

Record-Breaking Numbers in China

The film earned an estimated ₹320 crore in its opening weekend in China alone, according to industry tracker Artisan Gateway. This surpasses the opening of 'Avengers: Endgame' in 2019 and sets a new benchmark for the superhero genre in the region.

Industry analysts attribute the success to a combination of Tom Holland's enduring popularity and the film's grounded narrative. 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' reportedly connects with Chinese audiences who have shown signs of superhero fatigue with other franchises.

Global Box Office Momentum

Worldwide, the film has crossed the ₹400 crore mark in its debut weekend, with strong performances in North America and Europe. In India, trade experts project the film could open twice as big as Christopher Nolan's 'The Odyssey', which released earlier this year.

Box Office India estimates suggest 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' could earn over ₹50 crore nett in its opening weekend in India, driven by advance bookings and strong word-of-mouth. The film is releasing in English, Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu to maximise reach.

Critical Acclaim for Mature Storytelling

Critics have praised the film for taking a more mature approach to the character. Rotten Tomatoes' first reviews describe it as 'the most grounded, mature Spider-Man story yet'. The South China Morning Post calls it a 'mature Marvel triumph'.

The film reportedly deals with Peter Parker navigating life after the multiverse events of 'No Way Home'. Early reviews highlight Holland's performance as more nuanced and emotionally resonant than previous entries.

Box Office Implications for Hollywood in China

The strong opening is a bright spot for Hollywood studios struggling to regain traction in China. The market has become increasingly unpredictable, with local films dominating the box office in recent years.

'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' proves that superhero films with strong storytelling can still draw massive crowds. The film's success could encourage more Hollywood productions to tailor content for Chinese audiences.

All eyes are now on the film's second weekend performance. If it holds well, 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' could become one of the highest-grossing Hollywood films in China since the pandemic.
