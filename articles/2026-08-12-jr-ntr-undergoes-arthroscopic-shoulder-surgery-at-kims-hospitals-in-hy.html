Surgery Details

Arthroscopic shoulder surgery involves small incisions and a camera-guided scope to repair damaged tissue. It is commonly performed for rotator cuff tears, labral injuries, or recurring dislocations. The procedure typically allows for faster recovery compared to open surgery.
