FCNR(B) inflows exceed RBI's estimates, boosting India's reserves

Foreign Currency Non-Resident (B) deposits, a key source of forex inflows, have crossed the $100 billion mark, far exceeding the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) projection of $80 billion. This surge in inflows is a significant development for India's foreign exchange reserves, which have been a critical component of the country's economic stability.

RBI's forex swap facility draws over $136 billion

The RBI's forex swap facility has mobilized over $136 billion, with more than $127 billion coming from FCNR (B) deposits. This indicates a robust demand for foreign currency from Indian non-resident depositors, who are likely to be investing in India's financial markets and assets.

Banks mobilize USD 127.23 billion in deposits from Indian diaspora

Banks have mobilized USD 127.23 billion in deposits from the Indian diaspora, highlighting the significant role of the Indian diaspora in India's forex inflows. This is a testament to the strong ties between India and its diaspora, which have been instrumental in boosting India's foreign exchange reserves.

ICICI Bank garners $17.88 billion in FCNR-B deposits

ICICI Bank has garned $17.88 billion in FCNR-B deposits, underscoring the trust that Indian non-resident depositors have in the bank. This is a significant achievement for ICICI Bank, which has been at the forefront of India's financial services sector.

India draws $136.38 billion in forex inflows, boosting reserves

India has drawn $136.38 billion in forex inflows, boosting its reserves. This is a significant development for India's economy, as it indicates a strong demand for foreign currency from investors around the world. The surge in forex inflows is likely to have a positive impact on India's economic growth and stability.
