T-Series Wins Ramayana Music Rights for Record Rs 75 Crore

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • T-Series bags music rights for Ramayana Part 1 and 2.
  • Deal valued at Rs 75 crore, a record for Indian cinema music rights.
  • Sony Music had offered Rs 70 crore; makers had sought Rs 100 crore.
  • Trailer of the film is set to launch on July 30.

In what is being called the biggest music rights deal in Indian cinema history, T-Series has secured the audio rights for both parts of the upcoming Ramayana film series. The deal, worth Rs 75 crore, surpasses previous records and underscores the massive commercial expectations riding on the mythological epic.

The two-part film stars Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram and Kannada superstar Yash as Ravana. The project has been one of the most anticipated in Bollywood, with its scale and casting drawing comparisons to international blockbusters.

Fierce Bidding War for the Epic

The music rights for Ramayana were not handed over easily. Industry insiders reveal that Sony Music had put forward a competing offer of Rs 70 crore. The makers, however, were holding out for a higher valuation, initially seeking Rs 100 crore.

T-Series eventually clinched the deal at Rs 75 crore. The agreement covers the music rights for both Part 1 and Part 2, making it a comprehensive package that includes the film's soundtrack and background score.

This figure eclipses the previous record held by Pushpa 2: The Rule, whose music rights were sold for a reported Rs 60 crore. The Ramayana deal now sets a new benchmark in the industry.

Why the Music Rights Are So Valuable

Music rights have become a major revenue stream for film studios, especially for films with high repeat value. T-Series, already the largest music label in India, is betting on the film's soundtrack to generate massive royalties from streaming platforms, public performances, and digital downloads.

The Ramayana is not just a film; it is a cultural property. The story is known across the subcontinent, and its songs are expected to have a long shelf life. This makes the music rights a high-stakes asset.

The deal was struck ahead of the film's trailer launch, which is scheduled for July 30. The trailer is expected to give audiences the first real glimpse of the film's visual grandeur and musical tone.

Cast and Crew Behind the Mega Project

Directed by Nitesh Tiwari, the Ramayana series has been in development for years. Ranbir Kapoor steps into the role of Lord Ram, while Yash plays the antagonist Ravana. The film also features a sprawling ensemble cast, though full details have been kept under wraps.

The project is being produced by Madhu Mantena and Namit Malhotra, among others. With a reported budget that rivals the biggest Hollywood productions, the two-part film is being filmed simultaneously to ensure continuity.

The music is composed by a team of leading musicians, though the official composer list is yet to be announced. Given the budget and the cultural weight of the story, the soundtrack is expected to blend traditional instruments with modern orchestration.

What This Means for T-Series and the Industry

For T-Series, this acquisition solidifies its dominance in the music rights market. The label has been aggressive in recent years, outbidding rivals for major film soundtracks. The Ramayana deal signals that the company is willing to bet big on content with long-term value.

The Rs 75 crore figure also indicates that the film's producers are confident about the project's box office potential. Music rights deals are often seen as a barometer of a film's pre-release buzz. In this case, the numbers suggest extraordinary expectations.

Industry analysts will be watching to see if the record is broken again soon. With several big-budget films in the pipeline, the arms race for music rights appears far from over.

The trailer on July 30 will be the next major milestone. All eyes are now on how the first look is received by audiences and whether it justifies the record-breaking bet on its music.
