Army Major Killed, 2 Lieutenants Injured in Jaisalmer Camel Collision

📅 2026-07-31 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Major Hamza killed in SUV-camel collision near Jaisalmer Military Station
  • He had been married just a month ago
  • Two lieutenant-rank officers injured; vehicle overturned five times
  • Army authorities have launched an investigation

Jaisalmer, July 31: A tragic road accident near the Jaisalmer Military Station has claimed the life of an Army Major, who had been married only a month ago. The officer, identified as Major Hamza, was killed on the spot when his SUV collided with a camel and overturned multiple times. Two lieutenant-rank officers travelling with him sustained injuries and have been hospitalised.

The Accident

The incident occurred late on Thursday night when the SUV, reportedly on a routine movement, was heading towards the military station. A camel suddenly crossed the road, leaving the driver with little time to react. The vehicle hit the animal and flipped over five times, according to initial reports.

Emergency response teams rushed to the scene after receiving information. Major Hamza was declared dead on arrival at the nearest medical facility. The two injured lieutenants are said to be in stable condition and are receiving treatment at a military hospital.

A Life Cut Short

Major Hamza's death has sent shockwaves through his unit and family. He had tied the knot just a month ago, and colleagues described him as a dedicated officer with a promising career. News of his demise has left his young bride and immediate family devastated.

Defence sources have confirmed the identity of the deceased but have not yet released further personal details. The Army has extended its deepest condolences to the family and has assured full support during this difficult time.

Investigation Underway

Local police and military authorities have launched a joint investigation into the accident. Preliminary findings suggest that the camel's sudden appearance on the highway was the primary cause, but officials are also examining whether speed or road conditions played a role.

Camel collisions are not uncommon on highways in Rajasthan, especially near desert regions where animals roam freely. However, such incidents involving military vehicles highlight the need for better road safety measures and animal control in these areas.

Road Safety Concerns

This incident has reignited concerns about the safety of highways in the region. Several stretches near Jaisalmer lack proper fencing or warning signs for wildlife crossings. Local residents have often complained about stray camels and cattle posing risks to motorists.

The Army has said it will review its protocols for night-time vehicle movements in such terrains. Meanwhile, the injured lieutenants are expected to recover fully, and a court of inquiry has been ordered to ascertain the exact circumstances of the accident.

As the military community mourns the loss of Major Hamza, questions linger over preventive measures. The coming days will likely see enhanced patrolling and awareness campaigns in the area to prevent similar tragedies.
