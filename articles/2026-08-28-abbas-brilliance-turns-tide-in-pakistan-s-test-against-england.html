Abbas' brilliance turns tide in Pakistan's Test against England

In a rain-hit first day of the second men's cricket Test between England and Pakistan at Lord's, Pakistan's bowlers dominated the English side, with Abbas' predictable brilliance lifting team spirits amid gloom.

Rain-hit first day leaves both teams in a precarious position

The first day of the match was marred by rain, which led to a shortened day of play. Both teams struggled to gain an upper hand, with England taking the first innings to close at 261/6, 261 runs behind Pakistan's first innings total of 522/8 declared.

Abbas' predictable brilliance lifts Pakistan's spirits

In the second innings, Pakistan's bowlers, led by Abbas, dominated the English batsmen. Abbas, known for his consistent performances, bowled with precision and took 4 wickets for 62 runs. His predictable brilliance, which has been a hallmark of his career, lifted Pakistan's spirits and gave them a much-needed boost in the match.

England's bowlers struggle to contain Pakistan's batsmen
