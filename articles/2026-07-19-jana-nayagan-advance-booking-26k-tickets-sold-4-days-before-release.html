Jana Nayagan Advance Booking: 26K Tickets Sold 4 Days Before Release

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jana Nayagan has sold 26,200 tickets in advance four days before release
  • Tamil Nadu theatres receive strict instructions ahead of Vijay's farewell film
  • Tiruppur knitwear units working overtime to produce merchandise for the film

Thalapathy Vijay's final film 'Jana Nayagan' is already making waves at the box office, with advance bookings crossing 26,200 tickets four days before its release. The numbers, reported on Sunday, indicate a solid start for what is being billed as the actor's farewell movie.

Advance Booking Numbers Look Promising

According to early trends, the film has sold over 26,000 tickets across major chains, including PVR, INOX, and Cinepolis. Industry trackers expect this number to climb sharply as the release date approaches.

Typically, advance sales for big-ticket Tamil films see a surge 48 to 72 hours before release. With four days still to go, the current pace suggests 'Jana Nayagan' could register one of the highest opening day collections of the year.

Theatres Given Strict Guidelines

The Times of India reported that authorities have issued strict instructions to theatres ahead of the film's release. Tamil Nadu cinema owners have been directed to ensure security and manage crowd flow, given the massive fan following Vijay commands.

Sources indicate that additional screenings may be added in multiplexes and single screens to accommodate demand. Distributors are coordinating with theatre chains to avoid any last-minute glitches.

Tiruppur Units Work Overtime for Merchandise

In a unique development, knitwear units in Tiruppur are reportedly working overtime to produce merchandise for 'Jana Nayagan'. ThePrint reported that these factories are churning out custom apparel and accessories tied to the film's release.

This marks one of the few instances where the textile hub has been directly involved in movie merchandise production. The scale of orders suggests high expectations from the film's marketing team.

Competing Films Rethink Release Plans

Several Tamil films originally scheduled for release around the same date have been pushed back. The Indian Express noted that producers are reconsidering their plans to avoid clashing with 'Jana Nayagan'.

This is a common pattern for Vijay's releases, given his unmatched box office pull in Tamil Nadu. Smaller films typically prefer to release a week before or after his movies to capture audience attention without direct competition.

Meanwhile, the emotional father-daughter track 'Adiye En Poonthene' from the film has already gone viral. The song, released earlier this week, has been trending on music streaming platforms.

With just four days to go, the buzz around 'Jana Nayagan' shows no signs of slowing down. All eyes are now on July 23, when the film finally hits screens and Vijay bids farewell to his acting career.
