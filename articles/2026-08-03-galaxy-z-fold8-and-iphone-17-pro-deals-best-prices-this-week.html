Retailers across major e-commerce platforms and offline stores are offering discounts that shave thousands off the MRP. The deals come as brands look to clear inventory ahead of the upcoming festive season, a period when smartphone sales typically surge.

Galaxy Z Fold8 Series: A Foldable Bargain

Samsung's latest foldables — the Galaxy Z Fold8 and the pricier Z Fold8 Ultra — are at the centre of this week's promotions. The standard Fold8 is available for up to ₹12,000 off, while the Ultra variant sees a similar markdown, bringing its effective price down considerably.

Reviewers at GSMArena have praised the Z Fold8 Ultra for its refined hinge mechanism and improved crease visibility, calling it "the most polished foldable yet." The device's large 7.6-inch inner display and upgraded camera system are key talking points, but the price has often been a barrier. This week's deal changes that equation.

Additionally, exchange bonuses are sweetening the pot. If you're trading in an older smartphone, you can stack an extra ₹5,000 to ₹8,000 off, depending on the model and condition. Some retailers are also bundling free accessories like the S Pen and a protective case.

iPhone 17 Pro and Pro Max: Apple's Premium Push

Apple isn't sitting idle either. The iPhone 17 Pro and Pro Max are seeing discounts of up to ₹8,000 on select online platforms. This is notable because Apple typically keeps its pricing rigid, especially for Pro models in the first year of release.

The iPhone 17 Pro, launched late last year, has been a strong seller thanks to its A19 Pro chip and improved battery life. But with the festive season approaching, retailers are keen to move units. Bank offers, including no-cost EMI and cashback on premium credit cards, are also available, effectively reducing the out-of-pocket cost further.

Industry watchers note that these discounts are likely a strategic move. With Samsung's foldables gaining traction and OnePlus and Xiaomi chipping away at the premium segment, Apple is ensuring its Pro lineup stays competitive on value.

For consumers, the practical takeaway is simple: both devices are excellent, but the discounts make them more tempting than usual. If you're leaning toward a foldable, the Z Fold8 series offers a unique experience that no slab phone can match. If you prefer iOS, the iPhone 17 Pro's camera and ecosystem remain class-leading.
