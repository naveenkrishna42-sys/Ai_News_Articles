Sensex Surges 500 Points, Nifty Crosses 23,900 on US-Iran Détente

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex surged over 500 points in early trade, Nifty crossed 23,900.
  • Gains driven by easing US-Iran tensions and positive global cues.
  • Broader market indices, sectoral indices all in the green.
  • Investor sentiment improved on hopes of de-escalation in West Asia.
  • Analysts caution that volatility may persist until a formal accord is signed.

Benchmarks Open Strong on Global Cues

Indian stock markets opened sharply higher on Monday, with the BSE Sensex jumping over 500 points and the Nifty50 reclaiming the 23,900 mark. The rally was driven by a broad-based improvement in global risk appetite after the United States and Iran signalled a de-escalation of hostilities in West Asia.

The Sensex climbed 512 points to 78,534.68 in early trade. The Nifty rose 148 points to 23,918.90. All sectoral indices traded in the green, led by banking, auto and IT stocks.

US-Iran Détente Fuels Risk-On Mood

Investors cheered reports that diplomatic channels between Washington and Tehran had reopened, reducing the likelihood of a direct military confrontation. Oil prices eased on the news, providing relief to import-dependent economies like India.

Brent crude futures fell below $80 a barrel in Asian trade, down from recent highs. A sustained drop in oil prices helps contain India's import bill and supports corporate margins, especially in the aviation, paints and FMCG sectors.

Broader Markets and Sectoral Performance

The broader market mirrored the benchmarks' strength. The BSE Midcap index rose 0.8% and the Smallcap index added 1.1%.

Among sectoral indices, Nifty Bank gained 1.2%, led by ICICI Bank and HDFC Bank. Nifty Auto added 0.9% on hopes of lower input costs. Nifty IT rose 0.7% as global sentiment improved.

Metal stocks also rallied on expectations of stronger demand from a peaceful West Asia. Hindalco and Tata Steel rose 1.5% each.

What Traders Are Watching

Market participants will closely monitor any official statements from Washington or Tehran that could confirm or reverse the current détente. Options data suggest that the Nifty may face resistance at 24,200, while 23,700 serves as immediate support.

Foreign portfolio investors turned net buyers in early trade, adding to the positive momentum. Domestic institutional investors also remained active on the buy side.

Analysts at domestic brokerages have upgraded their near-term outlook on Indian equities, citing the easing of geopolitical risk and stable domestic macros. However, they caution that the situation remains fluid and any fresh provocation could quickly reverse the gains.

The rupee strengthened marginally against the dollar, trading at 83.45, supported by the equity inflows and softer crude prices.

For now, the market's focus remains on the diplomatic track. If the US-Iran talks progress further, analysts expect the Nifty to test the 24,500 level in the coming weeks. If talks stall, the recent lows near 23,500 could be revisited.
