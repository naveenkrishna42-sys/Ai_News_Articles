Trinamool Factions, Congress Plan Show of Strength in Kolkata on Martyrs’ Day

📅 2026-07-21 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Trinamool Congress factions and Congress are organizing separate Martyrs' Day programmes in Kolkata.
  • Calcutta High Court has directed police to protect the TMC's venue and sought a report on alleged vandalism.
  • Kolkata Police have made special traffic arrangements to manage three simultaneous events.

Kolkata is bracing for a high-stakes political showdown this Martyrs’ Day, with multiple factions of the Trinamool Congress and the Congress party planning separate rallies. The events, scheduled for the same day, have prompted the Calcutta High Court to step in, directing the police to secure the Trinamool Congress’s designated venue.

Parallel Rallies and Political Tensions

The Trinamool Congress is split between the faction led by Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee and a rebel group. Both are determined to hold their own programmes, each aiming to project strength. The Congress party is also organising a separate event, adding a third dimension to the day’s political activity.

This clustering of rallies has raised concerns about potential clashes. The rival groups have been trading barbs in the lead-up to the day, with Mamata Banerjee and the rebels accusing each other of undermining party discipline and democratic processes.

Court Intervention and Vandalism Reports

The Calcutta High Court has directed the Kolkata Police to provide adequate protection to the Trinamool Congress’s official Martyrs’ Day venue. The court also sought a detailed report on allegations that the stage at the venue was vandalised. The incident prompted Mamata Banerjee to stay guard overnight, a move that underscored the high emotions surrounding the event.

The court’s order aims to prevent any untoward incidents and ensure that the rival groups can hold their programmes without disruption. Police have been instructed to maintain a strict vigil and take preventive action if needed.

Traffic and Security Arrangements

Kolkata Police have released a detailed traffic management plan to handle the three simultaneous events. Several key routes in the city are expected to be closed or diverted, and commuters have been advised to plan their journeys accordingly. The police have deployed additional personnel to manage crowds and prevent any law and order breakdown.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of attendees expected at each rally. However, given the political significance of the day, large turnouts are anticipated. Security has been tightened around all venues, with special focus on the Trinamool Congress’s main programme.

The coming hours will reveal whether the parallel rallies remain peaceful or escalate into confrontation. All eyes are on the police’s ability to maintain order as political rivals test their strength on the streets of Kolkata.
