US Surgeon Shares 5 Heart-Health Habits After 8,000 Surgeries

A US cardiothoracic surgeon with 25 years' experience has shared 5 key habits for maintaining heart health. The surgeon, who has performed over 8,000 surgeries, draws from his extensive experience to offer practical advice. His recommendations are based on a combination of medical knowledge and personal experience.

Regular Exercise is Non-Negotiable

The surgeon emphasizes the importance of regular physical activity. He suggests at least 30 minutes of moderate exercise, such as brisk walking, five days a week. Regular exercise helps maintain a healthy heart rate and improves overall cardiovascular health.

Maintain a Healthy Weight

Weight management is another critical aspect of heart health. The surgeon advises avoiding excessive weight gain and maintaining a balanced diet. He suggests tracking daily calorie intake and staying hydrated to support heart health.

Avoid Smoking and Excessive Alcohol Consumption

The surgeon strongly advises against smoking and excessive alcohol consumption. Smoking damages blood vessels and increases the risk of heart disease, while excessive alcohol can lead to high blood pressure and other cardiovascular issues.

Monitor Blood Pressure and Cholesterol Levels

Regular monitoring of blood pressure and cholesterol levels is essential for heart health. The surgeon recommends regular check-ups with a healthcare provider to ensure these levels remain within a healthy range. Early detection of any issues can prevent serious complications.

Prioritize Mental Health

Mental health plays a significant role in heart health. The surgeon highlights the importance of managing stress and avoiding loneliness. He suggests incorporating relaxation techniques, such as meditation and deep breathing, into daily routines to support overall well-being.
