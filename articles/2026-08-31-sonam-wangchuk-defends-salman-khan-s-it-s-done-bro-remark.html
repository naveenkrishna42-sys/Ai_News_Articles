Sonam Wangchuk defends Salman Khan's controversial remark

Actor Sonam Wangchuk has reacted to Salman Khan's controversial remark, saying that everyone has their own compulsions. The remark, made during a podcast interview, sparked a debate on the pressure faced by actors in Bollywood.

The remark and the backlash

During a recent episode of Prakhar Gupta's podcast, Salman Khan was asked about his upcoming projects. He replied, 'Sonam, it's done bro,' referring to his collaboration with Wangchuk. The remark was perceived as dismissive and insensitive, given the significant amount of time and effort that goes into filmmaking.

The backlash was immediate, with many fans and industry insiders expressing their disapproval. Some even questioned the actor's professionalism and commitment to his craft.

Wangchuk's response and the need for understanding

Wangchuk, in her response, emphasized the importance of understanding and empathy in the entertainment industry. She said, 'Majbooriyan hoti hongi sabki,' meaning that everyone has their own compulsions. This response was seen as a call for a more compassionate approach towards the challenges faced by actors.

Wangchuk's response highlights the need for a more nuanced understanding of the pressures and responsibilities that actors face. It also underscores the importance of respect and consideration in the industry, where collaboration and mutual respect are crucial for the success of any project.
