Bumrah, Pandya Injury Updates: BCCI Secretary to Visit CoE Amid Crisis

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • BCCI secretary Devajit Saikia to visit the Centre of Excellence to review the injury situation.
  • Saikia is scheduled to meet with VVS Laxman, head of cricket at the CoE.
  • Concerns persist over the fitness of Jasprit Bumrah and Hardik Pandya.

Injury Crisis Takes Centre Stage

The Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) has moved to address a growing injury crisis that has put key players, including Jasprit Bumrah and Hardik Pandya, under the scanner. According to reports, BCCI secretary Devajit Saikia will visit the Centre of Excellence (CoE) in Bengaluru to take stock of the situation.

Saikia is expected to meet with VVS Laxman, who heads the CoE, to discuss the rehabilitation and recovery of several players. The visit signals the board's concern over the recurring fitness issues that have plagued the Indian team in recent months.

Bumrah's Recovery Timeline Questioned

Jasprit Bumrah's fitness remains a major talking point. A former pacer has reportedly advised the speedster to take a 6-12 month break to ensure complete recovery from his recurring back injury. This has raised questions about how long an athlete of Bumrah's calibre would need to return to peak condition.

Medical experts suggest that recovery timelines for fast bowlers with stress fractures can vary significantly, depending on the severity of the injury and the rehabilitation process. The BCCI has not yet confirmed any official timeline for Bumrah's return.

Hardik Pandya's Fitness Woes

Hardik Pandya, the all-rounder, also finds himself in the spotlight as concerns over his workload management persist. Pandya has had multiple injury setbacks in the past, and his current status remains unclear. The BCCI's decision to involve the CoE in his recovery plan indicates a more structured approach to his fitness.

The team management has been cautious with Pandya, but the lack of clarity on his availability has added to the uncertainty ahead of upcoming international fixtures.

What's Going Wrong at the CoE?

The CoE, formerly the National Cricket Academy, is tasked with ensuring player fitness and rehabilitation. However, the recent spate of injuries has raised questions about the effectiveness of the programmes in place. Saikia's visit is seen as an attempt to identify systemic issues and streamline processes.

According to a report in The Hindu, the BCCI secretary's meeting with VVS Laxman will focus on the injury crisis and the measures needed to prevent further breakdowns. The board is under pressure to ensure that key players are fit for major tournaments.

Looking Ahead

While the BCCI has not issued an official statement, the visit by Saikia is a clear indication that the board is taking the matter seriously. The meeting with Laxman could lead to changes in how player workloads are managed at the CoE.

For now, fans and selectors alike will be watching closely for updates on Bumrah and Pandya's recovery. The coming weeks could be crucial in determining the team's composition for the next series.
