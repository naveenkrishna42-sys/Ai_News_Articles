Salman Khan’s lean look in new video worries fans; actor says he’s fine

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • A video of Salman Khan’s latest public appearance shows him looking noticeably leaner.
  • Fans expressed concern on social media, with many asking if the actor is unwell.
  • Salman Khan responded to the speculation, saying 'Aap logon ki tabiyat kaisi hai' and indicating he is fine.

New video fuels health speculation

A fresh video of Bollywood star Salman Khan has set social media abuzz with concern. In the clip, captured during a recent public appearance in Mumbai, the actor is seen looking significantly leaner than his usual self. Fans were quick to notice the change, with many taking to platforms like X (formerly Twitter) to ask: “What happened to him?” and “Is he unwell?”

The video, widely shared by entertainment portals, shows Khan walking with a visibly slimmer frame. While he appeared to be moving normally, the weight loss prompted an outpouring of worried messages. Some fans speculated that the actor might be dealing with an undisclosed health issue.

Fans react with concern

Social media timelines were flooded with comments. “He looks sick. I hope he is okay,” wrote one user. Another posted, “Salman bhai, please take care of your health. We are worried.” The trend quickly gained traction, with many demanding an official update from the actor or his team.

This is not the first time Khan’s appearance has sparked speculation. Last year, a similar wave of concern arose when he was spotted with a changed physique during a film shoot. However, the actor had then dismissed the rumours, attributing the transformation to his fitness routine.

Khan responds with a question

In what appears to be a direct response to the chatter, Salman Khan shared a new photoshoot on his social media. Posing in casual wear, the actor displayed his lean look and asked fans: “Aap logon ki tabiyat kaisi hai” (How is your health?). The post seemed to be a lighthearted way of addressing the speculation.

Close sources told media outlets that Khan is “perfectly fine” and has been focusing on a stricter diet and workout regimen for an upcoming film role. They added that the actor is known for drastic physical transformations for his projects and this is likely part of that preparation.

No official statement yet

Despite the buzz, neither Salman Khan nor his representatives have issued a formal statement regarding his health. The actor’s team has not confirmed if the weight loss is due to a film or any other reason.

In the absence of clarity, fans remain divided. While some accept the fitness-related explanation, others continue to express concern, urging the star to prioritise his well-being over professional commitments.

What to watch for

As the speculation simmers, all eyes are on Salman Khan’s next public appearance or social media post for any further indication of his health. If the weight loss is indeed for a film, an official announcement about his upcoming project could put the rumours to rest. Until then, fans and media alike will be watching closely.
