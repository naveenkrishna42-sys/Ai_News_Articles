US Open 2026 Final: Zverev vs Shelton – A Clash of Styles

Arthur Ashe Stadium hosts a blockbuster US Open 2026 men's singles final on Sunday, as fourth seed Alexander Zverev meets American sensation Ben Shelton. The matchup pits Zverev's metronomic baseline game against Shelton's explosive lefty serve and fearless net rushes. With a maiden Grand Slam title on the line for both, expect fireworks under the New York lights.

Head-to-Head: All Square at 1-1

Zverev and Shelton have split their two previous meetings, each winning on their favoured surface. Zverev claimed a four-set victory on the clay of Roland Garros in 2025, while Shelton edged a tight three-setter on the fast hard courts of Cincinnati in 2024. This final will be their first Grand Slam encounter, adding intrigue to an already compelling rivalry.

Match Time and Live Streaming in India

The final is scheduled for Sunday, September 13, 2026, at 2:00 PM local time (EDT). For Indian fans, that translates to 11:30 PM IST. Live coverage will be available on Sony Sports Network (Sony Ten 2 HD and Sony Ten 3 HD) with English and Hindi commentary. Streaming is available on SonyLIV via subscription. Live scores and updates can be followed on the official US Open website and app.

Player Profiles and Path to the Final

Alexander Zverev has been in imperious form, dropping just one set en route to the final. His serve has been unbroken in his last three matches, and his backhand down-the-line remains one of the most potent weapons in the game. The German is seeking his first Grand Slam title after several near-misses, including a heartbreaking five-set loss in the 2024 US Open final.

Ben Shelton, the 23-year-old lefty, has captured the imagination of the home crowd with his thunderous 144 mph serve and acrobatic volleys. He defeated Frances Tiafoe in a thrilling five-set semifinal, showcasing remarkable composure under pressure. Shelton's aggressive return game and improved backhand have made him a serious contender.

Key Insights: Pros and Cons

  • Pros for Zverev: Superior baseline consistency, elite return of serve, and big-match experience. His ability to extend rallies could neutralise Shelton's power.
  • Pros for Shelton: Left-handed serve with extreme pace and spin, aggressive net play, and massive crowd support. He thrives on quick points and can dictate with his forehand.
  • Cons for Zverev: Occasional double-fault issues under pressure and a tendency to play passively on crucial points.
  • Cons for Shelton: Inconsistent backhand and limited experience in Grand Slam finals. If his serve percentage drops, Zverev will pounce.

Buyer's Guide: What to Watch For

For Indian tennis fans planning to stay up, the match offers a rare chance to witness two distinct playing styles. Zverev's methodical approach against Shelton's high-octane attack will likely hinge on first-serve percentage and break-point conversions. Expect a tight contest, possibly decided in four or five sets.
