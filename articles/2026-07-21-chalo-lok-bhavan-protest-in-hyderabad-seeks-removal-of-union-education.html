Chalo Lok Bhavan protest in Hyderabad seeks removal of Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Protesters gathered under the 'Chalo Lok Bhavan' banner in Hyderabad.
  • The demonstration specifically called for the removal of Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan.
  • Participants cited policy issues as the reason for their demand.

A protest named 'Chalo Lok Bhavan' was held in Hyderabad on Tuesday, with demonstrators marching to the state government's administrative headquarters. The crowd demanded the removal of Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan from his post.

Why the protest

The protesters, who gathered in significant numbers, voiced strong opposition to the minister's policies. They argued that recent decisions by the Union Ministry of Education have adversely affected students and educators across the country.

While specific policy points were not detailed in the initial reports, the chants and placards made the central demand clear: Pradhan must go. The demonstration was organised by a coalition of student groups and civil society members.

March to Lok Bhavan

The march began at a designated assembly point in the city and proceeded towards Lok Bhavan, the seat of the Telangana state government. Traffic on key routes was disrupted for a few hours as the procession moved through the streets.

Police personnel were deployed in large numbers to manage the crowd. No untoward incidents were reported, and the protest remained peaceful throughout. The organisers had obtained prior permission for the rally.

Demands presented

A memorandum listing the demands was submitted to state authorities. The primary demand was the immediate removal of Dharmendra Pradhan as Union Education Minister. The protesters also called for a rollback of certain educational reforms they termed as harmful.

Representatives of the protesting groups stated that they would continue their agitation until the central government addressed their concerns. They hinted at larger protests in the coming weeks if no action was taken.

Political reactions

Political parties in Telangana have reacted differently to the protest. Ruling party leaders condemned the demonstration, calling it a politically motivated stunt. Opposition leaders, however, expressed support for the protesters' cause.

The Union Education Minister's office has not yet issued a statement regarding the protest. Officials in New Delhi are believed to be monitoring the situation.

What happens next will depend on how the government responds to the growing discontent. The protesters have vowed to escalate their campaign if their demands are not met.
