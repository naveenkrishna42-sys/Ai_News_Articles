Bitcoin holds above $64k as markets eye Hormuz deal

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin remains rangebound above $64,000, showing limited volatility.
  • Traders focus on geopolitical developments, particularly a possible Hormuz deal.
  • Market participants await further cues on global risk sentiment.

Bitcoin held steady above the $64,000 mark on Wednesday, with traders keeping a close watch on geopolitical headlines, particularly around a potential agreement concerning the Strait of Hormuz. The cryptocurrency has remained in a tight band over the past 24 hours, reflecting a broader pause in risk appetite across global markets.

The lack of decisive movement suggests that investors are waiting for clearer signals—both from the geopolitical front and from macroeconomic data—before committing to directional bets. At the time of writing, bitcoin was hovering near the lower end of its recent range, having failed to break out despite occasional buying pressure.

Hormuz deal in focus

The prospect of a diplomatic resolution involving the Strait of Hormuz, a critical chokepoint for global oil shipments, has emerged as a key factor influencing market sentiment. Any easing of tensions in the region could bolster risk-on flows, which typically support assets like bitcoin. Conversely, a breakdown in talks might spur safe-haven demand for traditional stores of value.

However, details of the potential deal remain sketchy, and officials have not yet confirmed any formal agreement. This ambiguity has left traders cautious, with many opting to reduce leverage and hold positions rather than chase momentum.

Market conditions and trading activity

Volatility has contracted noticeably, with bitcoin's intraday swings narrowing to a few hundred dollars. Derivatives data shows a decline in open interest, suggesting that speculative activity has cooled. Funding rates across major exchanges have stabilised near neutral, indicating that neither longs nor shorts are dominating the market.

Spot volumes, too, have dipped compared with the previous week, underscoring the wait-and-see mood. Some analysts attribute the quiet tape to the summer lull, while others point to the absence of fresh catalysts. The crypto market is now closely tracking traditional assets, particularly equities and oil, for direction.

Technical levels and what to watch

From a technical perspective, bitcoin continues to find support above the $64,000 zone, a level that has held firm over the past several sessions. On the upside, resistance is visible near $66,000, where sellers have repeatedly emerged. A break beyond that could open the door to a test of recent highs, but momentum indicators remain mixed.

On the downside, a sustained move below $64,000 would likely trigger stop-losses and could accelerate selling. Traders are also monitoring the broader macroeconomic calendar, including upcoming US inflation data and remarks from Federal Reserve officials, which could influence risk sentiment across all asset classes.

Geopolitics and crypto's reaction

The crypto market has historically shown sensitivity to geopolitical developments, with tensions often leading to short-term volatility. The Hormuz situation is particularly relevant because of its potential impact on energy prices, which in turn affect inflation expectations and central bank policies. A peaceful resolution could ease inflationary pressures, potentially paving the way for a more accommodative monetary stance—a scenario that tends to favour risk assets like bitcoin.

Still, the market's reaction so far has been muted, suggesting that investors are sceptical about the likelihood of a breakthrough or are already pricing in a positive outcome. The coming days are likely to provide more clarity, especially if official statements emerge from involved parties.

For now, bitcoin remains rangebound, and traders appear content to wait. The path of least resistance will depend on how the Hormuz narrative unfolds and whether broader risk appetite returns. A decisive move above or below the current range could set the tone for the next phase of trading.

Looking ahead, the focus shifts to any official confirmation of the deal and its potential ripple effects on global markets. Until then, expect choppy but contained price action, with bitcoin likely to remain tethered to the $64,000–$66,000 band.
