India's Best Superhero Movies: A Look at the Genre's Finest

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • The list includes iconic films like 'Krrish' and 'Mard Ko Dard Nahi Hota'.
  • It showcases performances by Hrithik Roshan, Rajkummar Rao, and others.
  • The article emphasizes the genre's evolution in Indian cinema.

Indian cinema has embraced the superhero genre with a distinct flavour, blending mythology, drama, and action. A recent feature by Rediff has highlighted some of the best superhero movies to come out of the country, celebrating the actors and directors who brought these larger-than-life characters to the screen.

The list, compiled by Rediff, spans several decades and includes films that have become cultural landmarks. From the groundbreaking 'Krrish' to the irreverent 'Mard Ko Dard Nahi Hota', the selection showcases the diversity and ambition of Indian superhero storytelling.

The Iconic 'Krrish' and Its Legacy

Topping many lists is 'Krrish', Hrithik Roshan's 2006 blockbuster. The film, directed by Rakesh Roshan, redefined the superhero genre in Bollywood. Roshan's portrayal of the masked vigilante, alongside his secret identity as Krishna, won over audiences and critics alike.

The movie's success spawned a franchise, including a sequel and a recent third installment. 'Krrish' is often credited with setting a new benchmark for visual effects and action choreography in Indian cinema.

Unique Takes on the Superhero Formula

The list also celebrates films that subvert traditional superhero tropes. 'Mard Ko Dard Nahi Hota' (2019) is a quirky, martial arts comedy about a young man who cannot feel pain. Starring Abhimanyu Dassani and Gulshan Devaiah, the film was praised for its fresh approach and energetic action sequences.

Another notable entry is 'Makkhi' (2012), the Telugu film directed by S.S. Rajamouli. The story of a man reincarnated as a housefly seeking revenge is as inventive as it sounds. The film's visual effects and emotional core made it a critical and commercial success.

Superheroes from Regional Cinema

The Rediff feature does not limit itself to Bollywood. It includes Tamil and Telugu superhero films that have made a significant impact. 'Mersal' (2017), starring Thalapathy Vijay, combines social commentary with superhero action. The film's portrayal of a vigilante doctor fighting corruption struck a chord with audiences.

Similarly, 'Eega' (the original Telugu version of 'Makkhi') is highlighted for its originality and technical brilliance. These films demonstrate that the superhero genre thrives across India's diverse film industries.

Performances That Defined the Genre

Beyond the spectacle, the list acknowledges performances that gave the genre emotional weight. Hrithik Roshan's dual role in 'Krrish' is often cited as a masterclass in balancing vulnerability and heroism. Meanwhile, Rajkummar Rao's deadpan delivery in 'Mard Ko Dard Nahi Hota' added a layer of humour that set the film apart.

Other actors like Akshay Kumar in 'Ra.One' and Shah Rukh Khan in 'Koi... Mil Gaya' also feature, though the latter is more science fiction than superhero. The article notes that these performances helped ground fantastical stories in relatable human experiences.

As Indian cinema continues to evolve, the superhero genre shows no signs of slowing down. With upcoming projects like 'Brahmastra' and 'Adipurush' pushing boundaries, the future looks bright for fans of caped crusaders and masked avengers.
