Apple regains top spot as world’s most valuable company, dethrones Nvidia

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple dethroned Nvidia to become the world’s most valuable company.
  • Nvidia’s shares fell 3.7%, contributing to the shift in market cap rankings.
  • The two companies have been vying for the top spot in recent weeks.

Apple has once again claimed the title of the world’s most valuable company, edging past chipmaker Nvidia in a closely watched battle for market capitalisation supremacy. The shift comes as Nvidia’s shares dipped 3.7%, allowing the iPhone maker to retake the top spot it had lost earlier this year.

How Apple reclaimed the crown

Market watchers said the change was driven by a modest rally in Apple’s stock and a sharp reversal in Nvidia’s recent run. Nvidia had surged ahead on the back of the artificial intelligence boom, with its chips powering the majority of data centre workloads. But a 3.7% slide in its share price on Thursday was enough to tip the scales back in Apple’s favour.

Apple’s market capitalisation crossed the $3.3 trillion mark, analysts estimated, though exact figures fluctuated during trading hours. The company has not released any major product announcement in recent days, suggesting the move was more about market dynamics than company-specific news.

Nvidia’s dip: profit-taking or correction?

Nvidia’s stock had more than tripled over the past year, making it a favourite among growth investors. The 3.7% drop is being viewed by some analysts as routine profit-taking after a prolonged rally. Others pointed to rising competition in the AI chip space, with companies like AMD and Intel launching new products.

“Nvidia’s valuation has been stretched for a while,” said a market analyst who spoke on condition of anonymity. “A pullback was expected. Whether this is the start of a deeper correction remains to be seen.”

The chipmaker still commands a market cap of over $3.1 lakh crore, keeping it well ahead of other tech giants like Microsoft and Amazon.

Apple vs Nvidia: a tale of two markets

The rivalry between Apple and Nvidia reflects a broader battle between two pillars of the tech economy: consumer hardware and advanced computing. Apple’s strength lies in its ecosystem of iPhones, iPads, and services, which generate steady revenue and high margins. Nvidia, by contrast, is at the heart of the AI revolution, supplying the chips that train and run large language models.

Both companies have seen their market caps rise sharply in 2026. Apple benefited from strong iPhone 17 sales and growth in its services segment. Nvidia rode the AI wave, with revenue from data centre chips more than doubling year-on-year.

The title of world’s most valuable company has changed hands several times in recent months. Microsoft held the top spot briefly earlier this year before Nvidia surged ahead. Now, Apple has reclaimed its familiar position, though analysts expect the race to remain tight.

What this means for investors

For investors, the back-and-forth is a reminder of how quickly market sentiment can shift. Apple is often seen as a safer bet, with a loyal customer base and a massive cash reserve. Nvidia, while more volatile, offers exposure to what many consider the defining technology trend of the decade.

“It’s not about picking a winner,” said a portfolio manager at a Mumbai-based fund. “Both are great companies. The question is what fits your risk appetite.”

The two companies are also increasingly interconnected. Apple uses Nvidia chips in some of its products, and both have a stake in the future of augmented reality and autonomous systems.

All eyes will be on Nvidia’s next earnings report, due in a few weeks, for clues on whether the dip is temporary or signals a longer shift in investor sentiment.
