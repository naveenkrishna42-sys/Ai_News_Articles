A Sharp Reaction at the Airport

When photographs of Govinda walking through the airport with his rumoured girlfriend Komal surfaced online, the internet did what it does best — it erupted. But the most stinging response came from someone much closer to the actor: his wife, Sunita Ahuja.

In a candid reaction, Sunita did not hold back. She said the actor "has lost his mind," and went on to quote the Hindi proverb "Vinaash kaale vipreet buddhi" — a sharp remark suggesting that when destruction is near, a person's judgement turns upside down.

The photos, which showed Govinda and Komal together at the airport, have reignited a public spat that had been simmering for weeks. Sunita's comments have now added fresh fuel to a controversy that shows no signs of cooling down.

Who Is Komal?

Komal Rani Swarnkar, the woman at the centre of the storm, is not just a rumoured romantic interest. She is also Govinda's co-actor, a fact that has made the situation even more complicated for the family.

Reports suggest that Komal has been spotted with Govinda on multiple occasions, and their professional association has now spilled into personal territory — at least in the public eye. Neither Govinda nor Komal has issued a detailed statement about the nature of their relationship.

For now, the focus remains on the actor's wife and her sharply worded reaction, which has been widely shared and discussed across social media platforms.

A History of Tension

This is not the first time Sunita has spoken out about her husband's alleged infidelity. Earlier, she had made cheating allegations against Govinda, and the couple's marital discord has played out in full view of the media.

Govinda, for his part, has defended himself in his own characteristic style. In a recent interview, he said, "She hurls abuses and I take it," and questioned why she bad-mouths him for his success. His remarks suggested a man trying to brush off the allegations, but Sunita's latest comments indicate that the wounds are far from healed.

The back-and-forth has turned a private family matter into a very public spectacle, with fans and critics alike taking sides.
