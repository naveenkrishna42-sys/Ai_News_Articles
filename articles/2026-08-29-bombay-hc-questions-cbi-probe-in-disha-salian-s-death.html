Bombay High Court Reserves Order on CBI Probe Plea

The Bombay High Court has reserved its order on a plea seeking a CBI probe into the death of celebrity manager Disha Salian. The court's decision comes amid a flurry of legal and media activity surrounding the case, which has raised serious questions about the circumstances of her death.

Questions Raised About Suicide Claim

Lawyers and family members have raised doubts about the suicide claim in Disha Salian's death. The body was found 10 feet away from the suicide note, which has led to speculation about the authenticity of the claim. The Bombay High Court has called the ADR (Alternative Dispute Resolution) inquiry into the case 'futile' and has expressed concerns about the probe.

Political Rivals Accused of Using Disha Salian's Father as a Tool

Disha Salian's father, a prominent figure in the political sphere, has been accused of being used as a tool by political rivals. Aaditya Thackeray's lawyer has made these allegations, which have added a layer of political intrigue to the case. The Bombay High Court has been asked to consider these allegations in its order.
