Redmi Note 17 Series Launch, OnePlus Exits EU and North America: Week 29 Recap

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Redmi Note 17 and 17 Pro announced with 8,000mAh and 9,000mAh batteries respectively.
  • OnePlus confirms it will withdraw from European and North American markets.
  • POCO M8 Power expected to launch in India as a rebranded Redmi Note 17.

Redmi Note 17 Series Goes Official

Xiaomi has taken the wraps off its latest mid-range lineup, the Redmi Note 17 and Redmi Note 17 Pro. The devices were officially announced this week, drawing attention for their unusually large battery capacities.

The standard Redmi Note 17 packs an 8,000mAh battery. The Pro model pushes that further to 9,000mAh. Both phones are aimed at users who prioritise battery life above all else.

The standard model features a 7-inch display. Xiaomi has not yet confirmed detailed specifications for either device, including processor details or camera configurations.

OnePlus Exits Europe and North America

In a major strategic shift, OnePlus has confirmed it will exit the European and North American markets. The company made the announcement during Week 29, surprising many industry watchers.

The move marks the end of OnePlus's presence in two of its key regions. The brand had built a loyal following in both markets since its inception. Officials have not yet explained the reasoning behind the withdrawal or provided a timeline for the phase-out.

Existing customers in those regions will need to rely on existing service centres and warranty support. OnePlus has not detailed how it will handle after-sales service post-exit.

POCO M8 Power Tipped for India Launch

Meanwhile, speculation is building around the POCO M8 Power. According to reports, the device is expected to launch in India as a rebranded Redmi Note 17.

The POCO M8 Power is tipped to feature the same 8,000mAh battery as the standard Redmi Note 17. This would make it one of the largest battery capacities ever seen in a smartphone in the Indian market.

Leaks suggest the phone could debut under the POCO brand within weeks. Xiaomi has not confirmed these plans.

What to Watch Next

The Redmi Note 17 series will likely arrive in India soon, though Xiaomi has not set a date. OnePlus's exit will reshape the competitive dynamics in Europe and North America, leaving room for rivals like Samsung and Google to capture its user base. Industry watchers are also tracking whether the POCO M8 Power will indeed launch as a rebranded Redmi Note 17 in India, potentially offering a massive battery at a competitive price point.
