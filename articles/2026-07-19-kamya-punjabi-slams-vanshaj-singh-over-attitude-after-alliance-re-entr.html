Kamya Punjabi Slams Vanshaj Singh Over Attitude After Alliance Re-Entry

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kamya Punjabi took a dig at Vanshaj Singh for his perceived arrogant behaviour after returning to the show.
  • The comment follows a physical altercation between Kushal Tandon and Vanshaj Singh that left Sohail Khan upset.
  • Kunal Kemmu had earlier scolded Kushal Tandon for pushing Nikhil Chinapa, leading to a breakdown.

Kamya Punjabi Calls Out Vanshaj Singh

Kamya Punjabi has taken a sharp dig at Vanshaj Singh, criticising his attitude after he re-entered the reality show Alliance. The actress did not mince words as she called out what she described as a dismissive and arrogant demeanour.

Sources close to the set confirm that Punjabi made the remark during a group conversation. She reportedly pointed out that Singh's behaviour had changed noticeably after his return. Other contestants nodded in agreement.

Tensions Boil Over Between Kushal Tandon and Vanshaj Singh

The comment comes amid a charged atmosphere inside the house. A heated physical fight between Kushal Tandon and Vanshaj Singh has left actor Sohail Khan visibly fuming. Khan, who was present during the altercation, expressed his frustration.

“Fail to understand what is happening,” Khan was overheard saying. The fight reportedly started over a minor disagreement but quickly escalated. Producers intervened to separate the two.

Kunal Kemmu Scolds Kushal Tandon After Push Incident

In a separate but related incident, actor Kunal Kemmu scolded Kushal Tandon for pushing fellow contestant Nikhil Chinapa. The reprimand was sharp and public.

“Allowed nahi hai,” Kemmu told Tandon firmly. The warning came after Tandon lost his temper during a task. Witnesses say the push was more aggressive than what is normally tolerated on the show.

Kushal Tandon Breaks Down After Confrontation

The scolding visibly shook Kushal Tandon. He broke down shortly after the confrontation, crying alone in a corner of the house. Several contestants tried to calm him down.

Netizens have reacted strongly to the viral video of the incident. Some called it a case of the “bully getting bullied”. Others expressed sympathy for Tandon, saying the pressure of the show was taking a toll.

The show's producers have not yet issued a formal statement on the recent string of incidents. However, sources indicate that stricter guidelines may be enforced going forward.

All eyes are now on how the contestants will navigate the remaining weeks of the season. The growing hostility suggests more confrontations could follow.
