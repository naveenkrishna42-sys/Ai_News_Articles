Centre Denies Clearance for Telangana CM's US Trip

The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) has rejected the request for political clearance for Telangana Chief Minister Revanth Reddy's upcoming visit to the United States. The decision comes as a surprise to Reddy, who had been planning the trip for some time.

MEA Cites 'Programme Not Appropriate'

Officials from the MEA have cited 'programme not appropriate' as the reason for the rejection. However, they have not provided any further details on what exactly was deemed inappropriate about Reddy's programme. This lack of clarity has only fueled more speculation and controversy around the decision.

Political Row Over 'Political Clearance'

The rejection of Reddy's US visit has sparked a political row over the 'political clearance' requirement for chief ministers' foreign trips. The BJP has cited procedural and regulatory factors as the reason for the denial, while Reddy has called for a rethink of the system, urging officials to think beyond 'politics and elections'.

What is 'Political Clearance'?

The 'political clearance' requirement for chief ministers' foreign trips is a relatively new development. It is a process that involves the approval of the government for a chief minister's foreign visit, ensuring that the trip is in line with the government's foreign policy and does not cause any diplomatic issues. However, the exact process and the criteria for approval are not clearly defined, leading to confusion and controversy.

Reddy's Reaction

Revanth Reddy has expressed his disappointment over the decision. He has called for a rethink of the 'political clearance' system, urging officials to think beyond 'politics and elections'. He has also vowed to continue his diplomatic efforts, regardless of the government's decision.
