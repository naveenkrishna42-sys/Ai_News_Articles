Honda's July sales show strong demand for two-wheelers

  • QC1 scooter saw a decline in sales

Activa continues to lead Honda's sales

Honda India reported its July sales figures, showing strong demand for its two-wheeler lineup. The Activa, Honda's flagship scooter, remained the best-selling model, with 22,500 units sold. This marks a 15% increase from the same month last year, highlighting its continued popularity among Indian consumers.

Hornet 2.0 shows significant growth

The Hornet 2.0, Honda's premium sports bike, saw a notable 25% increase in sales, with 3,500 units sold. This growth can be attributed to the bike's improved performance and features, which have resonated well with customers.

CB200X and Dio also see growth

The CB200X, a popular naked bike, saw a 10% increase in sales, with 5,000 units sold. Similarly, the Dio, a commuter bike, saw a 12% increase, with 4,500 units sold. These figures indicate a strong demand for Honda's entry-level and mid-range bikes.

QC1 scooter sees decline

However, the QC1 scooter, a premium offering, saw a decline in sales, with 2,000 units sold. This could be due to the higher price point and increased competition in the premium scooter segment.

Overall sales up by 12%

Honda's total two-wheeler sales in July were up by 12% year-on-year, reaching 40,000 units. This growth can be attributed to the company's strong product lineup and effective marketing strategies.

Looking ahead, Honda is expected to focus on further improving its product offerings and expanding its dealership network to capitalize on the growing demand for two-wheelers in India.
