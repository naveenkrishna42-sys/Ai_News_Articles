Singer Jasmine Sandlas allegedly groped at Dehradun concert, video sparks outrage

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jasmine Sandlas was performing in Dehradun when she was allegedly groped by a mob.
  • A video of the incident has gone viral, showing the singer being surrounded on stage.
  • The incident has sparked widespread outrage and calls for better security at concerts.

Viral video shows singer being mobbed on stage

A disturbing video has gone viral on social media showing Punjabi and Bollywood singer Jasmine Sandlas being mobbed and allegedly groped by a crowd during a concert in Dehradun. The incident took place on Saturday evening at a live event, where the 'Dhurandhar' hitmaker was performing.

In the clip, Sandlas is seen surrounded by a large group of people on stage, with many reaching out to touch her. The singer appears visibly distressed and attempts to move away from the crowd, but the mob continues to press in on her. Security personnel are seen struggling to control the situation.

Outrage on social media

The video has sparked widespread outrage online, with fans and celebrities condemning the incident. Many have called for stricter security measures at live events and demanded action against those responsible. The hashtag #JusticeForJasmineSandlas has been trending on X, formerly Twitter.

Several public figures have also expressed their solidarity with the singer. Actor and singer Diljit Dosanjh tweeted, "This is absolutely unacceptable. Artists deserve respect and safety. Hoping the authorities take strict action." Others have pointed out that such incidents highlight the persistent issue of safety for women in public spaces.

No official statement yet from singer or authorities

As of now, Jasmine Sandlas has not released any official statement regarding the incident. The local police have also not confirmed whether any complaint has been filed or if an investigation has been launched. Officials at the venue have remained unavailable for comment.

The organisers of the concert have not yet responded to requests for clarification on security arrangements. The lack of an immediate response has added to the public's frustration, with many demanding a thorough inquiry.

What happens next

It remains to be seen whether the Dehradun police will register an FIR in the matter. With pressure mounting on social media, the authorities are expected to issue a statement soon. The incident has once again raised questions about the safety protocols at live events in India.
