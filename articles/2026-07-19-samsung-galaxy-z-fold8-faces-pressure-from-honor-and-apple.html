Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Faces Pressure From Honor and Apple

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Honor's passport-style foldable is gaining traction as a design alternative.
  • Apple's rumored foldable iPhone is pressuring Samsung to innovate faster.
  • Samsung is pushing AI features across three foldables to stay ahead.
  • The foldable market is heating up as more brands enter the mainstream.

A New Challenger in the Foldable Arena

Samsung's dominance in the foldable phone market is facing its toughest test yet. The upcoming Galaxy Z Fold8 is caught between two powerful forces: Honor's innovative 'passport-style' design and Apple's long-rumored entry into the foldable segment.

Honor has been making waves with its unique approach to foldables. The company's passport-style foldable, which opens like a book with a wider aspect ratio, has been praised for its practicality. Industry watchers say this design could set a new standard.

Apple's Shadow Looms Large

Apple has reportedly changed Samsung's folding phone plans. Sources suggest that the Cupertino giant is working on a foldable iPhone that could debut as early as next year. This has forced Samsung to rethink its strategy for the Z Fold8.

Did the foldable iPhone knock the Galaxy Z Fold8 out even before Samsung's big premiere? That's the question on everyone's mind. Samsung has traditionally led the foldable market, but Apple's entry could shift consumer preferences overnight.

Samsung Pushes AI to Stay Ahead

In response, Samsung is doubling down on artificial intelligence. The company plans to launch three foldable devices with advanced AI features before Apple enters the market. These features include on-device AI for photo editing, real-time translation, and productivity tools.

Samsung's strategy is clear: use AI to differentiate its foldables from the competition. The company believes that intelligent software can give it an edge over both Honor's design and Apple's ecosystem.

The Mainstream Moment for Foldables

Foldable phones are finally going mainstream. What was once a niche category is now attracting major players. Samsung, Honor, and Apple are all betting big on the form factor, but each has a different vision for the future.

Analysts say the key to success is finding the right balance between design, durability, and price. Samsung has the experience, but Honor has the innovation. Apple has the brand loyalty. The next year will be critical.

As the competition heats up, consumers will be the ultimate winners. More choices mean better devices at lower prices. But for Samsung, the pressure is on to deliver a Z Fold8 that can hold its own against both Honor and Apple.

What happens next? Watch for Samsung's official unveiling of the Galaxy Z Fold8 later this year. The company's AI push and design changes will reveal just how much Apple's shadow has influenced its plans.
