Deal & Pricing Analysis

Air India's partnership with IHG (InterContinental Hotels Group) is a strategic move that allows Flying Returns members to earn Maharaja Points on eligible hotel stays across IHG's portfolio, which includes brands like InterContinental, Crowne Plaza, Holiday Inn, and Six Senses. Unlike a typical discount deal, this is an earning opportunity that adds value to your travel spend. The current offer allows members to earn up to 1,000 Maharaja Points per stay, depending on the hotel brand and room rate. This is not a limited-time sale but an ongoing partnership, making it a consistent way to accumulate points for future award flights. When you consider that a one-way domestic Air India flight can be booked for as low as 6,000 Maharaja Points, a few hotel stays can quickly translate into a free ticket. The real value here is the flexibility it offers: you can choose to earn Maharaja Points instead of IHG One Rewards points, or vice versa, depending on your travel goals. For frequent flyers who already stay at IHG properties, this is essentially free points on top of your regular hotel stay, with no additional cost.

Real-World Performance & Daily Use

In practice, this partnership is seamless for travelers who already stay at IHG properties. You simply link your Flying Returns and IHG One Rewards accounts, and then choose which program you want to earn points in at check-in. The earning is automatic, and points typically post within a few days of checkout. The build quality of this partnership is solid—both programs are well-established, and the integration is smooth. However, the daily use reveals a few nuances. For instance, the earning rate of up to 1,000 points per stay is not uniform; budget brands like Holiday Inn Express may earn fewer points than premium ones like InterContinental. Also, you must ensure you book at an eligible rate—deeply discounted rates often do not qualify. Battery endurance, in a metaphorical sense, is strong: the partnership is ongoing, so you can accumulate points over multiple stays without worrying about a deadline. The real-world benefit is most pronounced for business travelers who frequently stay at IHG properties and also fly Air India domestically. For leisure travelers who stay at hotels only a few times a year, the points accumulation may be slower, but it still adds up over time.

Competition & Market Alternatives

The Air India IHG partnership competes with similar airline-hotel alliances, such as the one between Marriott Bonvoy and United Airlines, or Accor and Air France-KLM. In the Indian context, the most direct rival is the partnership between ITC Hotels and Air India, which allows members to earn Maharaja Points on ITC stays. However, IHG has a much larger global footprint than ITC, making it a better option for international travelers. Another alternative is using a co-branded credit card, like the HDFC Bank InterMiles card, which offers more flexible earning on all spends, not just hotel stays. But for those who prefer to stay at IHG properties, this partnership offers a more targeted earning opportunity. Compared to the Marriott-United tie-up, which often offers higher earning rates, the Air India IHG partnership is more modest but still valuable for domestic flyers who want to avoid the hassle of transferring points across programs.

Who Should Buy This Deal

This partnership is ideal for frequent business travelers who regularly stay at IHG hotels and fly Air India. If you are a member of both programs, you can maximize your benefits by choosing to earn Maharaja Points on stays that you would otherwise not earn anything extra on. It is also a great option for travelers who are close to a Maharaja Points redemption and need a small top-up. However, if you are a leisure traveler who stays at hotels only occasionally, or if you prefer other hotel chains like Marriott or Accor, this partnership may not be your best bet. In that case, you might be better off with a flexible travel rewards credit card that allows you to earn points on all your spending. Overall, this is a no-cost way to enhance your travel rewards, provided you are already loyal to IHG and Air India.

Key Highlights & Tradeoffs

  • ✅ Seamless earning on hotel stays without extra cost
  • ✅ Access to IHG's vast global portfolio of hotels
  • ✅ Flexibility to choose between Maharaja Points and IHG One Rewards points
  • ✅ Ongoing partnership, not a limited-time offer
  • ⚠️ Earning rates are modest compared to some co-branded credit cards
  • ⚠️ Eligible rates exclude many discounted bookings, limiting earning opportunities

Final Buying Verdict

The Air India IHG partnership is a valuable addition for frequent travelers who already use IHG hotels and fly Air India. It offers a straightforward way to earn Maharaja Points without extra spending, but it may not be the most rewarding option for occasional travelers or those loyal to other hotel chains. We recommend it for business travelers looking to maximize their travel rewards.
