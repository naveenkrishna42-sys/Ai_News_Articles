JD Vance calls PM Modi, discusses deepening India-US strategic ties

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • US Vice President JD Vance initiated a phone call with PM Modi.
  • The leaders discussed ways to deepen India-US strategic partnership.
  • Vance congratulated Modi on recent developments, with talks focusing on trade, defence, and tech.
  • The call signals renewed momentum in bilateral ties.

US Vice President JD Vance on Saturday telephoned Prime Minister Narendra Modi, in a call that signals a fresh push to deepen the India-US strategic partnership. The conversation, confirmed by officials on both sides, covered a range of bilateral issues, with an emphasis on expanding cooperation in trade, defence, and emerging technologies.

Focus on Strategic Convergence

The call comes at a time when both nations are recalibrating their foreign policy priorities. Vance and Modi discussed ways to align their visions on regional security and economic resilience, according to a statement from the Prime Minister's Office.

Officials familiar with the conversation said the two leaders reviewed ongoing initiatives under the India-US Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership. They also touched upon collaborative efforts in the Indo-Pacific, where both countries have sought to counterbalance China's growing assertiveness.

Congratulatory Note and Personal Touch

During the call, Vance also reportedly congratulated Modi on India's recent achievements, though the specifics were not officially disclosed. Some reports indicate that the Vice President extended warm wishes on behalf of the US administration, adding a personal touch to the diplomatic exchange.

The conversation was described as cordial and productive, with both sides expressing a commitment to sustaining the momentum in bilateral ties. It also comes weeks after senior officials from both countries met in Washington to chart a roadmap for the next phase of cooperation.

Trade, Tech, and Defence on the Table

Sources indicate that trade remained a key talking point, with both leaders exploring ways to reduce barriers and boost investment flows. The US has been keen to diversify its supply chains, and India has emerged as a preferred partner in sectors like semiconductors, pharmaceuticals, and clean energy.

Defence cooperation also featured prominently, with discussions on joint military exercises and technology sharing. The two countries have expanded their defence ties significantly over the past decade, and this call is expected to build on that foundation.

Broader Geopolitical Context

The call arrives amid a complex global environment, with ongoing conflicts and shifting alliances. Both nations have stressed the importance of a rules-based international order, and this dialogue is seen as a step toward reinforcing that shared stance.

Analysts note that the timing is significant, as India navigates its relationships with multiple global powers. The US, for its part, has been actively courting New Delhi as a key partner in the region.

What to Watch

Observers will be watching for follow-up visits or ministerial-level meetings in the coming months. The two governments are expected to announce concrete outcomes from this call, possibly during upcoming multilateral summits.

For now, the conversation underscores a mutual desire to keep the partnership moving forward, even as both capitals juggle domestic and international priorities.
