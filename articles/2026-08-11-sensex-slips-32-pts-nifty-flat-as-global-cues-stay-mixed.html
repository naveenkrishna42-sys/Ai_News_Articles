Sensex slips 32 pts, Nifty flat as global cues stay mixed

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex closed 32 points lower, Nifty nearly unchanged.
  • Markets took cues from mixed global trends and sector-specific moves.
  • Broader market showed resilience despite weak overnight cues.

Indian equity benchmarks ended Tuesday's session with marginal losses, as the BSE Sensex slipped 32 points while the NSE Nifty remained virtually flat. The subdued close reflected a day of cautious trading, with investors weighing mixed signals from global markets.

Market closes with thin losses

The Sensex settled at its closing level after shedding 32 points, while the Nifty managed to hold its ground, ending barely changed from the previous close. The narrow range of movement suggested a lack of strong directional conviction among traders.

Dealers said volumes were moderate, with participants preferring to wait for fresh triggers. The lack of any major domestic macroeconomic data point kept the market in a tight band through the day.

Sectoral performance mixed

Buying was visible in select pockets, while profit-taking emerged in others. Banking and financial stocks saw some pressure, whereas IT and pharma counters witnessed selective buying interest.

Midcap and smallcap indices outperformed their largecap peers, indicating that broader market sentiment remained intact. However, the overall tone was cautious as overseas cues offered little clarity.

Global backdrop remains uncertain

Asian markets ended on a mixed note, with some indices closing in the green while others lagged. European bourses opened without a clear trend, adding to the uncertainty that kept domestic investors on edge.

Crude oil prices and the movement of the rupee against the dollar were closely monitored, though neither showed any dramatic shift during the session. Foreign institutional investors remained net buyers in recent sessions, but their pace has slowed.

What to watch now

Traders will now turn their attention to upcoming global cues and any fresh domestic triggers. The absence of major events on the calendar suggests that volatility could remain low in the near term.

Market participants will also keep an eye on the next set of macroeconomic data releases and any commentary from the central bank. Until then, the benchmark indices are likely to consolidate within a narrow range.
