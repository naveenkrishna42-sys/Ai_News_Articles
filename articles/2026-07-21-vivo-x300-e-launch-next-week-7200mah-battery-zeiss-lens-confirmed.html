Vivo X300 E Launch Next Week: 7200mAh Battery, Zeiss Lens Confirmed

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Vivo X300 E launch date confirmed for next week.
  • Device to feature a massive 7,200mAh battery.
  • Zeiss Telephoto lens included in the camera setup.
  • Pre-order offers and key features revealed.

Launch Date and Availability

Vivo has confirmed the launch date for its upcoming X300 E smartphone. The device is set to debut next week, with pre-orders already opening up in select markets. Reports from CNBC TV18 and Gadgets 360 indicate that the phone will first launch in China, with a global rollout expected shortly after.

Pre-order offers have been revealed, though exact pricing details remain under wraps. Industry watchers expect the X300 E to slot into the upper mid-range segment, competing with devices from OnePlus and Xiaomi.

Massive Battery and Charging

The biggest talking point around the Vivo X300 E is its battery. The phone will pack a 7,200mAh cell, a significant jump over the previous generation. This places it among the largest batteries in any mainstream smartphone today.

Charging specifications have not been officially confirmed, but leaks suggest support for fast wired charging. The large battery capacity is aimed at heavy users who need the phone to last through a full day of intensive use.

Camera and Optics

Vivo is bringing its partnership with Zeiss to the X300 E. The phone will feature a Zeiss Telephoto lens, a first for the E-series. This should allow for improved optical zoom and portrait photography.

The primary camera sensor details are yet to be disclosed. However, the inclusion of Zeiss optics signals that Vivo is positioning the X300 E as a capable imaging device, not just a battery powerhouse.

Design and Other Specs

Early renders show a sleek design with a punch-hole display. The X300 E is expected to feature a high-refresh-rate AMOLED screen. Processor details are unconfirmed, but speculation points to a MediaTek Dimensity chipset.

RAM and storage variants will likely range from 8GB to 12GB, and 128GB to 256GB respectively. The phone will run on Android 14 with Vivo's Funtouch OS on top.

What to Watch

With the launch just days away, all eyes are on the final pricing. If Vivo prices the X300 E aggressively, it could disrupt the mid-premium segment in India. The official unveiling next week will clear up remaining questions on processor, camera specs, and local availability.
