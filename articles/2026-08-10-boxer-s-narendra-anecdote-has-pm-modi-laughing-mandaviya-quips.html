An anecdote that tickled the PM

Prime Minister Narendra Modi was in for a light moment on Monday when Indian boxer Narender Berwal recounted a tale from Pakistan during an interaction. The heavyweight pugilist, who has represented India at multiple international events, narrated how a Pakistani boxer once told him he hated the name 'Narendra'. The remark left PM Modi in splits, and the exchange quickly became the talking point of the day.

Berwal, who goes by the ring name 'Narender', explained that the Pakistani boxer had made the comment during a bout, ostensibly in reference to the Indian PM. The story, shared at an event attended by top athletes, drew laughter from the room, including the Prime Minister himself.

Mandaviya's sharp retort

Reacting to the anecdote, Union Sports Minister Mansukh Mandaviya turned the remark into a spirited defence of the PM. "Narendra, the name feared in Pakistan," he said, drawing applause from the audience. The minister's comment underscored the sentiment that the name 'Narendra' carries weight beyond India's borders, a reference to Modi's strong stance on national security.

Mandaviya's quip was widely shared on social media, with many users lauding the minister's wit. The sports minister was present at the event, which was part of a series of interactions between the PM and athletes preparing for upcoming international competitions.

The revelation was met with laughter, and PM Modi reportedly responded with a grin, appreciating the lighter side of the rivalry.
