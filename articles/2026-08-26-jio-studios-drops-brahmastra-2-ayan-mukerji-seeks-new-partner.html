Jio Studios Drops Brahmastra 2

Jio Studios has pulled out of Brahmastra 2, a high-profile film starring Ranbir Kapoor, Deepika Padukone, and Alia Bhatt. The decision comes as a surprise, given the film's star power and the studio's recent investments in Bollywood. The reasons behind the withdrawal are not yet clear, but industry insiders suggest it could be due to financial constraints or strategic shifts.

Ayan Mukerji Begins Hunt for New Studio Partner
