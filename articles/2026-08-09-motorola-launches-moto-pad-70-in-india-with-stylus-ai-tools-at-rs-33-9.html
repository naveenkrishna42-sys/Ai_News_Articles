Motorola launches Moto Pad 70 in India with stylus, AI tools at Rs 33,999

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Moto Pad 70 launched in India at Rs 33,999 with 5G support.
  • Tablet features a 12.1-inch 90Hz display and 10,200mAh battery.
  • Bundled stylus and AI-powered writing tools are key highlights.
  • A separate Moto Pad 70 Groove variant includes nine JBL speakers.

Moto Pad 70 arrives in India

Motorola has expanded its tablet portfolio in India with the launch of the Moto Pad 70. The device was announced on Saturday, bringing a 12.1-inch display with 90Hz refresh rate, 5G connectivity, and a hefty 10,200mAh battery to the mid-range segment.

The tablet is priced at Rs 33,999 and comes with a stylus bundled in the box. Motorola is positioning the Moto Pad 70 as a productivity-focused device, with AI-powered writing tools integrated into the software.

Display and performance

The 12.1-inch panel offers a smooth 90Hz refresh rate, which should appeal to users who consume media or take notes. The large screen real estate, combined with the bundled stylus, makes it a contender for students and professionals who prefer handwritten notes over typing.

Under the hood, the tablet supports 5G networks, ensuring faster download speeds and lower latency for cloud-based tasks. The 10,200mAh battery is among the larger cells seen in this price bracket, promising extended usage on a single charge.

AI-powered writing tools

Motorola has packed the Moto Pad 70 with AI-driven features aimed at improving writing productivity. These tools can assist with note-taking, summarising, and even drafting text, making the stylus more than just a drawing accessory.

The company has not disclosed the full list of AI capabilities or whether they will receive future updates. However, the integration suggests Motorola is betting on software intelligence to differentiate its tablet in a crowded market.

Audio-focused Groove variant

Alongside the standard Moto Pad 70, Motorola has teased a special edition called the Moto Pad 70 Groove. This variant stands out with nine JBL speakers, a configuration that is rare in tablets. The audio setup is likely aimed at media enthusiasts who want a more immersive sound experience without external speakers.

Pricing and availability for the Groove model have not been announced yet. Motorola has only confirmed its existence, leaving room for speculation about a potential premium price tag.

Market positioning and competition

The Moto Pad 70 enters a segment dominated by Xiaomi, Samsung, and Lenovo. At Rs 33,999, it sits in the upper mid-range tier, where buyers expect a balance of performance, display quality, and battery life.

The bundled stylus and AI tools give it an edge over rivals that charge extra for accessories. However, the success will depend on how well the software performs in real-world usage and whether the battery lives up to its 10,200mAh claim.

Motorola's move also signals a renewed focus on tablets, a category the company has dabbled in but not aggressively pursued in recent years. With 5G and AI as selling points, the Moto Pad 70 could attract early adopters looking for a future-proof device.

What remains to be seen is the availability of the Groove variant and its pricing. Motorola is expected to reveal more details in the coming weeks, along with retail availability across online and offline channels.
