Air India Phuket-Delhi Flight Hit by Severe Turbulence, 17 Injured

📅 2026-08-05 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Air India flight from Phuket to Delhi dropped 300 feet mid-air due to severe turbulence.
  • At least 17 people were injured in the incident.
  • Passengers described the ordeal as the 'worst two hours' of their lives.
  • An investigation has been launched into the sudden altitude loss.
  • The cabin suffered damage, with broken panels and scattered luggage.

An Air India flight traveling from Phuket to Delhi turned into a nightmare for passengers on Wednesday when the aircraft plunged 300 feet mid-air after being hit by severe turbulence. The sudden drop left 17 people injured and the cabin in disarray, with broken panels and luggage strewn across the aisles.

Passengers onboard described the harrowing experience as the 'worst two hours' of their lives. Many feared the worst as the plane lurched violently, sending unsecured items flying and triggering screams throughout the cabin.

Terror at 30,000 Feet

The incident occurred when the Airbus, carrying hundreds of passengers, encountered what aviation experts describe as clear-air turbulence — a phenomenon that strikes without warning. Within seconds, the aircraft lost significant altitude, catching both crew and passengers off guard.

One passenger recounted the moment the plane dropped, saying, 'We thought we would die. It felt like a free fall, and nobody knew what was happening.' Another added that the crew struggled to maintain order as overhead bins burst open and debris rained down.

Injuries and Immediate Response

At least 17 people sustained injuries during the incident, though officials have not yet confirmed the severity of these injuries. Cabin crew, trained for such emergencies, moved swiftly to assist the wounded and reassure panicked travelers.

The flight continued to Delhi and landed safely, where medical teams were deployed to treat the injured. Passengers were later deplaned, with many requiring additional support to recover from the shock.

Investigation Underway

Following the incident, a formal probe has been launched to determine the exact cause of the sudden altitude loss. Aviation regulators and Air India officials are expected to scrutinize flight data and weather reports to piece together what happened.

The airline has not yet issued a detailed statement, but sources indicate that preliminary assessments point to unexpected weather conditions along the route. Investigators will also review whether the crew had adequate warning to prepare for the turbulence.

Rising Concerns Over Air Turbulence

This incident adds to a growing list of turbulence-related scares in recent years, as climate patterns shift and air travel increases. While turbulence is a routine part of flying, severe cases can lead to injuries and structural damage, as seen here.

Aviation safety experts emphasize that such events, while alarming, are rarely fatal. However, they stress the importance of keeping seatbelts fastened at all times, as turbulence can strike with little notice.

Passengers on this ill-fated flight are likely to carry the memory of those two hours for a long time. One traveler summed up the collective sentiment: 'It was the worst two hours of my life. We are lucky to be alive.'

What Happens Next

The investigation will now focus on analyzing cockpit voice recorders and flight data to provide a clearer picture of the incident. Air India is expected to release a formal report in the coming weeks, while affected passengers may seek compensation for their ordeal.

For now, the focus remains on the injured and their recovery, as well as ensuring that such incidents are better anticipated in the future. Aviation authorities will likely review protocols for turbulence forecasting to prevent a repeat of this terrifying experience.
