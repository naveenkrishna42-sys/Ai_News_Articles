14th train for Yellow Line reaches Namma Metro Bengaluru, one more pending

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • The 14th train for the Yellow Line has been delivered to Namma Metro in Bengaluru.
  • Only one more train delivery is pending to complete the full fleet order.
  • The Yellow Line is a crucial new corridor expected to ease congestion in the city.

New train arrives for Bengaluru metro's Yellow Line

Bengaluru's Namma Metro has taken delivery of the 14th train set for its upcoming Yellow Line. The train arrived at the depot earlier this week, officials confirmed.

This marks a significant step forward for the line, which will connect RV Road to Bommasandra. With this delivery, only one more train remains to be handed over to complete the full order.

What the Yellow Line will offer

The Yellow Line is expected to be a vital addition to Bengaluru's metro network. Stretching nearly 19 kilometres, it will link the southern suburbs to the city's core.

Once operational, the line will provide a faster commute for thousands of daily passengers. It will also connect with the existing Purple and Green lines at key interchange stations.

Delivery status and next steps

Namma Metro had originally ordered a total of 15 trains for the Yellow Line. Each train consists of six coaches, designed for high-capacity travel.

The 14th train has now been delivered and is undergoing standard checks. The 15th and final train is expected to arrive in the coming weeks, according to sources familiar with the process.

Testing and launch timeline

Work on the Yellow Line has been progressing steadily. Trial runs have already been conducted on several sections of the track.

Officials have not yet announced a firm date for passenger services. However, with the fleet nearly complete, the line is one step closer to opening.

The Yellow Line is being built with modern signalling and safety systems. It will also feature elevated and underground stations along its route.

For Bengaluru, this line represents another push towards a more robust public transport system. The city's rapid growth has made metro expansion a priority for the state government.

The next few months will be critical as the final train arrives and integration tests continue.
