Farmers' Rally in Mumbai: Delegation Meets Ministers Over Demands

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Farmers from across Maharashtra held a rally in Mumbai.
  • A delegation met with state ministers to discuss key demands.
  • Demands include loan waivers, MSP guarantees, and better crop insurance.

Thousands of farmers from across Maharashtra converged on Mumbai on Wednesday, staging a large rally to press for their outstanding demands. The protest, organized by various farmer unions, saw participants marching through key arterial roads before gathering at Azad Maidan.

Delegation Holds Talks with Ministers

A delegation of farmer leaders was granted a meeting with senior state ministers in the afternoon. The talks, which lasted over two hours, focused on the core issues that have been simmering for months.

Sources present at the meeting indicated that the farmers' representatives submitted a detailed memorandum outlining their demands. The ministers assured the delegation that their concerns would be examined sympathetically.

Key Demands Raised by Farmers

The rally's primary demands centered around a complete waiver of farm loans, a legal guarantee for Minimum Support Price (MSP) for all crops, and an overhaul of the crop insurance scheme. Farmers argued that existing compensation mechanisms were inadequate and delayed.

Another significant demand was for the implementation of the Swaminathan Commission's recommendations in full. Protesters held placards reading 'Loan Waiver Now' and 'Save Our Farms'.

The state government has not yet issued a formal statement on the outcome of the meeting. Officials said the demands would be reviewed and a response would be communicated in due course.

Traffic Disruptions Across the City

The massive gathering caused significant traffic disruptions in south Mumbai. Commuters faced delays on key routes leading to Azad Maidan and the nearby Mantralaya. The police had diverted traffic and deployed additional personnel to manage the crowd.

Local businesses in the area reported lower footfall as many offices advised employees to work from home. The rally remained peaceful throughout the day, with no untoward incidents reported.

The farmer unions have stated that the protest will continue until their demands are met. They have announced plans for further agitations if the government does not respond positively within a week.

What Happens Next

The delegation is expected to hold a follow-up meeting with the Chief Minister's office next week. The next round of talks will determine whether the farmers will escalate their protest or call it off.
