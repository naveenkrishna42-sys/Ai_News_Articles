Arsenal's Lewis-Skelly Offered to Chelsea, Man United in Shock Twist

Key points
  • Arsenal have offered Myles Lewis-Skelly to Chelsea and Manchester United as a potential left-back solution.
  • Chelsea have made an enquiry for the teenager, while Manchester United also hold long-term interest.
  • The surprise twist comes amid reports linking Reiss Nelson with a move to West Ham.

Arsenal's teenage sensation Myles Lewis-Skelly has been offered to Premier League rivals Chelsea and Manchester United in a move that has sent shockwaves through the transfer window. The 19-year-old, who broke into the Gunners' first team last season, is now at the centre of a tug-of-war between England's biggest clubs.

According to multiple reports, Arsenal have made the highly-rated defender available, with both London and Manchester clubs alerted to his availability. The development marks a dramatic shift from earlier in the summer, when the club insisted the youngster was not for sale at any price.

Left-Back Solution

Manchester United are reportedly viewing Lewis-Skelly as a potential long-term answer to their left-back troubles. The Red Devils have struggled for consistency in that position since the departure of Luke Shaw's injury-plagued campaigns, and the Arsenal starlet's versatility makes him an attractive option.

Chelsea, meanwhile, have already made an enquiry for the player, according to Sky Sports. The Blues are in the market for defensive reinforcements and see Lewis-Skelly as a player who could slot straight into their starting XI. Their interest is not new — the west London club have tracked the youngster for some time.

Surprise Twist in Stance

The Gunners' willingness to listen to offers represents a significant reversal. Earlier this summer, Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta spoke glowingly about Lewis-Skelly's potential and hinted he would play a key role in the coming season. Yet behind the scenes, the club's hierarchy appears to have changed its tune.

Sources suggest Arsenal are open to a sale if the right offer comes in, though they are unlikely to accept anything below their valuation. The club may also prefer a loan move with an obligation to buy, a structure that would allow them to recoup a significant fee while ensuring the player's development continues.

Nelson's Potential Exit

In related news, Reiss Nelson could be heading to West Ham. The winger, who has struggled for regular game time at the Emirates, is reportedly a target for the Hammers as David Moyes looks to bolster his attacking options. A move would see Nelson reunite with former Arsenal teammate Declan Rice, though no formal bid has been lodged yet.

Arsenal's transfer activity this summer has been focused on trimming their squad and raising funds for further reinforcements. The potential sales of Lewis-Skelly and Nelson would free up both wages and space in a squad that has grown bloated in recent windows.

For Lewis-Skelly, the decision will come down to playing time. At Arsenal, he faces stiff competition from established stars, while both Chelsea and Manchester United can offer more immediate first-team opportunities. The teenager's representatives are said to be weighing up all options, with a decision expected before the transfer window closes.

Neither Chelsea nor Manchester United have commented publicly on the reports, and Arsenal have remained tight-lipped. Officials have not yet confirmed whether formal offers have been submitted, though enquiries are understood to have been made.

The coming days will be crucial in determining where Lewis-Skelly's future lies. With the new Premier League season just weeks away, all three clubs will want a resolution sooner rather than later. Arsenal fans, who have already seen several academy products leave in recent years, will be watching nervously as one of their brightest prospects could be the next to walk out the door.
