Ola Electric Q1 net loss narrows to Rs 336 crore, revenue down 45%

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Net loss narrowed to Rs 336 crore in Q1 FY27
  • Revenue declined 45% year-on-year to Rs 455 crore
  • EV scooter orders jumped, helping improve market share

Loss narrows but top line shrinks

Ola Electric Mobility reported a net loss of Rs 336 crore for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027, a narrower deficit compared to the same period last year. The improvement, however, came alongside a sharp 45 percent year-on-year drop in revenue, which fell to Rs 455 crore.

The Bengaluru-based electric vehicle maker's numbers reflect a mixed quarter. While cost controls and operational efficiencies appear to be kicking in, the steep revenue decline signals continued demand pressure in the electric two-wheeler segment.

Orders climb, market share recovers

According to BusinessLine, the company's loss narrowed as revenue and market share improved. The Finimize report noted that Ola Electric trimmed losses as EV scooter orders jumped, indicating a revival in consumer interest.

The company has been ramping up its scooter portfolio and expanding service networks to win back customers. Industry watchers say the order book momentum in the June quarter may have helped stabilise its position in a competitive market dominated by players like TVS Motor and Bajaj Auto.

Revenue fall raises questions

The 45 percent drop in revenue to Rs 455 crore is stark. Analysts had expected a softer decline, given the company's aggressive discounting and festive-season pushes earlier in the year. Ola Electric has not yet detailed the reasons for the revenue dip, but factors could include lower average selling prices, reduced government incentives, or a shift in product mix towards entry-level scooters.

The company's earnings call, scheduled later today, is likely to address these concerns. Investors will be keen to hear management's outlook on demand sustainability and margin recovery in the coming quarters.

Share price and investor sentiment

Shares of Ola Electric have been volatile in recent months, tracking the company's financial performance and broader EV sector trends. The Q1 results come at a time when the company is also focusing on expanding its charging infrastructure and battery technology.

NDTV Profit had earlier flagged the earnings call and dividend announcement as key events to watch. No dividend has been declared for the quarter, consistent with the company's reinvestment phase.

What to watch next

Investors will now focus on the management's guidance for Q2, particularly on how the company plans to arrest the revenue decline while continuing to narrow losses. With festive season demand expected to pick up from October, Ola Electric's ability to convert order momentum into sustained revenue growth will be the key test in the months ahead.
