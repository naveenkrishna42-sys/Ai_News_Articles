Farhan Akhtar on Mirzapur film: First instinct was to take him to a psychiatrist

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Farhan Akhtar said his first instinct was to take the character to a psychiatrist
  • The Mirzapur film adaptation has been in demand from fans, according to Akhtar
  • Pankaj Tripathi reportedly charges Rs 15 crore, becoming the highest-paid cast member

A Shocking First Reaction

Farhan Akhtar has opened up about the making of Mirzapur The Movie, revealing that his initial reaction to one of the characters was far from cinematic. The actor-producer admitted that his first instinct was to take the character to a psychiatrist.

Speaking about the creative process behind the much-anticipated film, Akhtar said the character's complexity was so overwhelming that it seemed beyond the realm of normal storytelling. The remark offers a rare glimpse into how the team approached the dark, violent world of Mirzapur.

Fans Drove the Film

The transition from web series to film was not a spontaneous decision. Akhtar noted that fans had demanded this adaptation for a long time, and the team listened. "Fans have demanded this," he said, explaining why the story of Mirzapur made the leap to the big screen.

The original series, which became a cultural phenomenon, built a massive following across India. Its gritty narrative and memorable characters, including Kaleen Bhaiya and Guddu, found resonance with audiences who craved more of the bloody turf wars in the heartland.

Trailer Hints at Power Shift

The recently released trailer of Mirzapur The Movie sets the stage for a dramatic power struggle. Ravi Kishan's character is seen eyeing Kaleen Bhaiya's throne, while Munna and Guddu bring the 'bhaukaal' that fans have come to expect. The trailer promises high-octane action and intense drama, staying true to the series' tone.

With the film's release approaching, the buzz is palpable. The trailer's reception suggests that the movie will not just be an extension of the series but a cinematic event in its own right.

Big Budgets, Bigger Stakes

Reports have surfaced about the cast fees, and they are staggering. Pankaj Tripathi, who plays the iconic Kaleen Bhaiya, is reportedly charging Rs 15 crore, making him the highest-paid actor in the film. This dwarfs the remunerations of co-stars Ali Fazal, Divyenndu Sharma, and Shweta Tripathi, according to a Times of India report.

While official confirmations are still pending, the numbers reflect the star power that the cast commands. Tripathi's fee, if accurate, marks a significant milestone for a character actor who has become a household name through shows like Mirzapur.

What to Expect

As the release date draws closer, more details about the film's plot and characters are likely to emerge. The team has kept specifics under wraps, but the trailer has already set expectations high.

The coming weeks will determine whether Mirzapur The Movie can replicate the success of the series on the big screen. With Akhtar's candid revelations and a stellar cast, all eyes are on this high-stakes adaptation.

Watch for the film's release and the inevitable comparisons to the series that started it all.
