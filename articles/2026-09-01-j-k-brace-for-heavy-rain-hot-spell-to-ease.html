J&K Brace for Heavy Rain, Hot Spell to Ease

📅 2026-09-01 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Heavy rain and thunderstorms expected in J&K over next 48 hours
  • Temperatures to gradually fall after mid-September
  • J&K records zero heatwave days in August despite national heatwave

Heavy Rain and Thunderstorms Loom Over Jammu & Kashmir

The Meteorological Department (MeT) has issued warnings of heavy rain and thunderstorms across Jammu & Kashmir over the next 24-48 hours. This comes after a period of intense heat and humidity that has gripped the region. The MeT Director has urged residents to stay prepared for potential disruptions, including flooding and landslides.

Temperatures to Ease After Mid-September

While the immediate forecast calls for heavy rain, the MeT has also indicated that temperatures are expected to gradually fall after mid-September. This is a welcome relief for residents who have been enduring hot and humid conditions. The easing of temperatures is part of a broader trend observed in the region.

J&K Records Zero Heatwave Days in August

Despite the national heatwave affecting much of the country, Jammu & Kashmir has recorded zero heatwave days in August. This is a notable achievement, given the region's vulnerability to extreme heat. The absence of heatwaves in J&K can be attributed to its high altitude and mountainous terrain, which help to moderate temperatures.

Broader Impact on J&K

The forecast has significant implications for Jammu & Kashmir. The heavy rain and thunderstorms can help in replenishing water sources, which have been under strain due to the heat. However, the potential for flooding and landslides underscores the need for preparedness and caution.

The gradual cooling after mid-September is also a positive development. It can help in preparing for the winter season and can also have a positive impact on agriculture. However, the sudden temperature changes can be challenging for residents and farmers alike.

In conclusion, while the forecast of heavy rain and thunderstorms is a welcome relief from the heat, it also comes with its own set of challenges. The gradual cooling after mid-September is a positive sign, but the sudden temperature changes can be disruptive. Residents are urged to stay informed and prepared.
