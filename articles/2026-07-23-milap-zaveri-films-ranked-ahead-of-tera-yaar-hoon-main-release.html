Milap Zaveri Films Ranked Ahead of Tera Yaar Hoon Main Release

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Milap Zaveri's filmography includes Marjaavaan, Satyameva Jayate, and Mastizaade.
  • His movies often feature action, drama, and masala entertainment.
  • Tera Yaar Hoon Main is his upcoming project, generating anticipation among fans.

With Milap Zaveri gearing up for his next directorial venture, Tera Yaar Hoon Main, fans and critics are revisiting his body of work. Zaveri has carved a niche in Bollywood with his brand of massy, action-packed entertainers. From his debut to his biggest hits, here's a look at how his films stack up against each other.

Marjaavaan: A Stylish Action Drama

Released in 2019, Marjaavaan starring Sidharth Malhotra, Riteish Deshmukh, and Tara Sutaria was Zaveri's second directorial outing. The film revolved around a love story set against a backdrop of gang wars and revenge.

Critics praised its slick visuals and Riteish Deshmukh's menacing performance as the antagonist. However, the predictable plot and over-the-top action sequences drew mixed reactions. Despite this, the film performed decently at the box office, cementing Zaveri's reputation for masala cinema.

Satyameva Jayate: A Blockbuster Debut

Zaveri's directorial debut in 2018, Satyameva Jayate, starring John Abraham, was a vigilante action thriller. The film tackled corruption in the police force and became a commercial success, grossing over Rs 100 crore worldwide.

Its high-octane action and patriotic themes resonated with audiences. While critics noted its formulaic approach, the film's box office numbers spoke for themselves. This film remains Zaveri's most successful project to date.

Mastizaade: A Comedy That Missed the Mark

Before venturing into action, Zaveri co-wrote and directed Mastizaade (2016), a sex comedy starring Tusshar Kapoor, Sunny Leone, and Vir Das. The film aimed for laughs but was panned for its crude humour and weak script.

It failed to connect with audiences and critics alike, making it Zaveri's lowest-rated film. The experience likely shaped his shift towards the action genre, where he found more success.

Other Notable Works

Zaveri also wrote films like Shootout at Wadala (2013) and Hate Story 2 (2014), which displayed his flair for gritty narratives. These projects, though not directed by him, influenced his filmmaking style.

His penchant for dramatic dialogues and larger-than-life characters is evident across his filmography. Each film, regardless of its reception, reflects his commitment to commercial cinema.

What to Expect from Tera Yaar Hoon Main

As Zaveri prepares for the release of Tera Yaar Hoon Main, speculation is rife about its tone and scale. Early reports suggest it will stay true to his strengths—action, emotion, and masala entertainment.

Details about the cast and plot remain under wraps. But given his track record, the film is likely to target a wide audience seeking escapist cinema. Whether it will surpass Satyameva Jayate's success remains to be seen.
