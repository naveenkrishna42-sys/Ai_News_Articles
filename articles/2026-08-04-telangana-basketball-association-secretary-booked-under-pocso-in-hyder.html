Case Registered in Madhapur

The Madhapur police have registered a case against the secretary of the Telangana Basketball Association under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act. The registration took place on Tuesday, according to official sources.

The police have not yet disclosed the nature of the allegations or the circumstances that led to the filing of the complaint. Officials said the case was booked based on a formal complaint received by the station.

The accused is the secretary of the state-level basketball association, a position that involves overseeing administrative and organisational duties. His identity has not been officially released to the media, and attempts to reach him for comment were unsuccessful.

Sources indicate that the complaint was filed recently, and the police are treating the matter with sensitivity given the nature of the law involved. The POCSO Act provides for stringent penalties, and cases under it are typically investigated by specialised units.

Under the POCSO Act, the identity of the victim is protected, and reporting restrictions are in place. The police have not divulged any details that could reveal the complainant's identity or the specifics of the alleged offence.
