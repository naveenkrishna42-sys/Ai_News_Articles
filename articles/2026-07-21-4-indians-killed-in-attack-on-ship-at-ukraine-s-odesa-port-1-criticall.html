4 Indians killed in attack on ship at Ukraine's Odesa port, 1 critically injured

  • India's embassy in Ukraine is coordinating with local authorities for repatriation and medical evacuation of the injured sailor.

Attack on vessel at Odesa port kills four Indian nationals

Four Indian crew members were killed and one more is critically injured after a missile strike hit a commercial ship anchored at Ukraine's Odesa port. The attack, reported late Monday evening, has sent shockwaves through the Indian diplomatic and maritime community.

The vessel was docked at the port when it came under fire. Local Ukrainian authorities have attributed the strike to Russian forces, though Moscow has not officially commented on the incident.

Indian officials confirmed that the deceased were part of the ship's crew. The critically injured sailor has been rushed to a nearby hospital and is receiving emergency medical care.

Diplomatic response and repatriation efforts

India's Ministry of External Affairs issued a statement expressing profound grief over the loss of lives. The government has directed its embassy in Kyiv to extend all possible assistance to the families of the deceased and to coordinate with Ukrainian authorities.

Officials are working to identify the victims and bring their bodies back to India. The process is expected to be complicated by the ongoing conflict and the need for clearances from multiple agencies.

The injured crew member's medical evacuation is also being prioritised. Indian diplomats are in constant touch with the hospital and port authorities to ensure he receives the best possible care.

Global reaction and security concerns

The attack has drawn widespread condemnation from international observers. It highlights the persistent danger to civilian shipping in the Black Sea, even as both sides in the conflict trade blame for such incidents.

United Nations officials have called for an immediate investigation. The strike on a commercial vessel at a major grain export hub raises serious questions about the safety of maritime corridors that are vital for global food supplies.

India, which has consistently called for a diplomatic resolution to the Russia-Ukraine war, has now been directly impacted by its fallout. The tragedy is likely to further strain diplomatic ties and prompt a review of safety protocols for Indian seafarers in conflict zones.

What happens next

The Indian government is expected to press for a thorough investigation into the attack. Repatriation of the bodies and medical evacuation of the injured sailor will be the immediate focus in the coming days. The incident will also likely feature in upcoming bilateral talks between India and Russia.
