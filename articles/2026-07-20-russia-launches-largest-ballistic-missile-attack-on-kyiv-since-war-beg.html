Russia launches largest ballistic missile attack on Kyiv since war began

📅 2026-07-20 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Russia launched its largest ballistic missile attack on Kyiv since the start of the full-scale war.
  • At least 8 people were killed, and 13 others wounded in the strikes across Ukraine.
  • Ukrainian air defenses intercepted several missiles, but debris caused casualties in the capital.
  • The attack involved multiple ballistic missiles, overwhelming air defense systems in some areas.

Russia launched its largest ballistic missile attack on Kyiv since the start of the full-scale invasion, Ukrainian officials confirmed on Sunday. The strikes killed at least eight people and wounded 13 others across the country, with the capital bearing the brunt of the assault.

The attack targeted multiple regions, but Kyiv saw the most intense barrage. Air raid sirens blared for hours as residents rushed to shelters. Debris from intercepted missiles fell on residential areas, causing casualties and damage.

Massive barrage overwhelms defenses

Ukrainian air force officials reported that Russia fired dozens of ballistic missiles at Kyiv and other cities. Ballistic missiles travel at high speeds and are harder to shoot down than cruise missiles. Air defense systems managed to intercept several, but not all.

Casualties and damage mount

In Kyiv, at least one person was killed and 13 wounded, according to city officials. The dead included a civilian caught in the open during the attack. Rescue teams searched through rubble for survivors.

Elsewhere in Ukraine, strikes hit infrastructure and residential areas. The full extent of damage is still being assessed. Power outages were reported in some districts, though officials have not confirmed the scale.

International reaction

Ukraine's allies condemned the attack. Western leaders reiterated their support, vowing to continue military aid. The United Nations called for an immediate de-escalation, but no ceasefire is in sight.

Russia has not commented on the specific strike. Moscow maintains that its targeting is limited to military infrastructure, but Kyiv and international bodies accuse it of indiscriminate attacks on civilians.

This assault is the largest ballistic missile strike on Kyiv since the war began in February 2022. Previous attacks involved cruise missiles and drones, but Sunday's barrage marks an escalation in the type of weaponry used against the capital.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said in a statement that the attack shows Russia's intent to terrorize civilians. He called for more advanced air defense systems from partners to protect cities.

Residents in Kyiv described the fear of not knowing when the next wave would come. The attack lasted several hours, leaving many shaken. Schools and offices remained closed as the city assessed the damage.

The coming days will likely see more diplomatic pressure on Russia, but on the ground, Ukrainian forces brace for further strikes. Kyiv's air defenses, though bolstered by Western supplies, face a relentless barrage of missiles.
