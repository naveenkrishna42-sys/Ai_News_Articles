Cristiano Ronaldo marries Georgina Rodriguez in intimate Portugal ceremony

Key points
  • Ronaldo and Rodriguez married in an intimate ceremony in Portugal
  • The couple has been together for 10 years and shares two children
  • Wedding guest list remains private; reports of star players attending unconfirmed

Cristiano Ronaldo and Georgina Rodriguez have officially tied the knot. The couple exchanged vows in an intimate ceremony in Portugal, bringing a decade-long love story to a new chapter. The news, confirmed by multiple outlets on Wednesday, ends years of speculation about when the football superstar would make his relationship official.

A decade in the making

Ronaldo and Rodriguez first met in 2016, when she was working as a sales assistant at a Gucci store in Madrid. Their relationship quickly became public, and Rodriguez has since been a constant presence at Ronaldo's side, from his trophy-laden years at Real Madrid to his current stint in Saudi Arabia.

The couple shares two children, Alana Martina and a son born in 2022. Ronaldo also has three older children, whom Rodriguez has helped raise. Over the years, she has often spoken about the challenges of blending their large family, but the pair have consistently presented a united front.

An intimate affair

Details of the wedding remain scarce. Reports describe the ceremony as a private, low-key event, in keeping with the couple's preference for shielding their personal life from the spotlight. The venue and exact date have not been officially confirmed, though local Portuguese media suggest the ceremony took place at one of Ronaldo's properties in the country.

A guest list has not been released. Social media has been abuzz with speculation, with some claiming that football luminaries such as Lionel Messi, Kylian Mbappé, and Erling Haaland were in attendance. However, fact-checkers have found no evidence to support these claims, and The Times of India has flagged such reports as unverified.

From partner to wife

For years, Rodriguez was referred to as Ronaldo's girlfriend, a title she has often brushed aside. In interviews, she has emphasised that their bond goes beyond labels. "We are a family," she said in a 2023 documentary, echoing a sentiment she has repeated often. Now, that family has a formal seal.

The wedding marks a milestone for Ronaldo, who at 41 continues to play at the highest level. Known for his discipline and focus, he has often credited Rodriguez with providing stability off the pitch. Her role in managing their household and supporting his career has been well-documented, and friends of the couple say the timing felt right.

What comes next

As the couple settles into married life, fans will be watching to see if Rodriguez takes on a more prominent public role. She already runs her own fashion and beauty ventures, and a wedding could amplify her profile further. For Ronaldo, the focus remains on the pitch, with another season ahead.

Neither Ronaldo nor Rodriguez has made a public statement about the wedding yet. But the news has already sparked celebrations among fans worldwide, many of whom have followed their journey from the beginning. The couple's next move—whether a honeymoon, a family gathering, or simply a quiet return to routine—will likely be kept as private as the ceremony itself.
