Vivo S2 India Launch Teased: Expected Specs, Price, and Release Date

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Vivo S2 launch teased in India; official confirmation follows multiple leaks.
  • Expected to feature Snapdragon 8 Gen 5, triple-camera setup, and premium design.
  • Price likely in the ₹30,000-₹40,000 range; release expected in August 2026.

Official Teaser Confirms Vivo S2 India Launch

Vivo has officially teased the Vivo S2 in India, ending weeks of speculation. The company shared a brief promotional video on social media, hinting at a premium device with a sleek design.

The teaser shows a slim profile and a large camera module. This aligns with recent leaks that suggested the S series would make a comeback after a hiatus.

Industry trackers had spotted dummy units and live images online. The official confirmation now sets the stage for a launch event expected within the next two weeks.

Expected Specifications and Features

Leaks point to the Vivo S2 being powered by the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset. This would place it in the upper mid-range segment, competing with devices like the OnePlus Nord 5 and the Realme GT 7.

The phone is expected to sport a 6.7-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate. A 50-megapixel primary camera, paired with an ultra-wide and a macro lens, is rumored for the triple-camera setup on the back.

Reports from 91mobiles and Techgenyz have shown the dummy unit revealing a glass back, an in-display fingerprint sensor, and a 5,000mAh battery with 66W fast charging. The device is likely to run Funtouch OS based on Android 15.

Design and Build

The leaked live images suggest a curved-edge display and a punch-hole selfie camera. The rear panel has a gradient finish, which has become a signature for Vivo's S series.

The volume rocker and power button are placed on the right edge. The USB-C port and speaker grille sit at the bottom. A 3.5mm headphone jack may be absent, following current industry trends.

Expected Price and Release Date in India

The Vivo S2 is expected to be priced between ₹30,000 and ₹40,000 in India. This would position it as a premium mid-range offering.

Multiple sources suggest an August 2026 launch. The teaser video did not specify a date, but industry insiders expect the official unveiling in the first week of August.

Pre-orders may start shortly after the announcement. Vivo is likely to offer bank discounts and exchange offers to attract early buyers.

Watch for the official launch details in the coming days. The Vivo S2 will aim to revive the S series as a strong contender in the competitive Indian smartphone market.
