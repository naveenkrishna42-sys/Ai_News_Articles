Deal & Pricing Analysis

AU Bank has rolled out a limited-period offer for its Credit Card CPL (Consumer Preferred Lifestyle), waiving the joining fee entirely. Typically, the card carries a joining fee of ₹499, but under this promotion, new applicants pay nothing upfront. The annual fee of ₹499 is also waived for the first year, making it effectively free for the initial 12 months. This pricing strategy positions the card as an accessible entry point into AU Bank's credit ecosystem, especially for those with limited credit history. The card is aimed at millennials and first-time credit card users, offering a straightforward rewards structure without complex tiers. While the ongoing rewards are not industry-leading, the zero-cost entry and instant approval process make it a low-risk option for building credit. The offer is available through AU Bank's official website and select partner channels, with instant approval for eligible applicants based on digital verification.

Real-World Performance & Daily Use

In daily use, the AU Bank Credit Card CPL performs adequately for routine spending. The 2% cashback on online transactions is credited as reward points, which can be redeemed against statement balance or catalog purchases. However, the redemption value is 1 point = ₹0.25, effectively making the online cashback 0.5%—a significant caveat. Offline spends earn 1% as points, translating to 0.25% actual value. This is below average compared to cards offering direct cashback. The card shines in its digital onboarding: applicants with a clean credit profile can get instant approval and a virtual card within minutes. The physical card arrives in 5-7 business days. The AU Bank mobile app is functional, allowing users to track spends, redeem points, and manage EMIs. Customer service is responsive but not 24/7. The card lacks premium perks like airport lounge access or movie discounts, so it's best for everyday, low-value transactions. Fuel surcharge waiver is a nice touch, but capped at ₹500 per month. Overall, it's a basic card that does the job without frills.

Competition & Market Alternatives

Compared to the Amazon Pay ICICI Credit Card, which offers 5% cashback for Prime members on Amazon and 2% on other online spends with no joining fee, the AU Bank CPL falls short in rewards. The Amazon Pay card also has no annual fee and provides direct cashback, not points. Another competitor is the Flipkart Axis Bank Credit Card, which gives 5% cashback on Flipkart and 4% on preferred merchants, with a ₹500 annual fee (waived on spends). Both alternatives offer superior value for online shoppers. However, AU Bank CPL's instant approval and acceptance for those with limited credit history give it an edge for credit builders. If you have a good credit score, you're better off with the Amazon Pay or Flipkart cards. If you're new to credit, AU Bank CPL is a decent starting point.

Who Should Buy This Deal

This card is ideal for individuals with little to no credit history who want to build credit without paying fees. The instant approval and zero joining fee make it a low-risk entry. It's also suitable for those who prefer a simple rewards structure and don't mind the lower redemption value. However, if you're an avid online shopper with a good credit score, you should skip this and opt for cashback-focused cards like Amazon Pay ICICI or Flipkart Axis. Additionally, if you frequently travel or dine out, this card's lack of premium benefits will disappoint. Consider waiting if you can qualify for better cards; otherwise, grab this deal while the fee waiver lasts.

The AU Bank Credit Card CPL is a solid choice for credit builders seeking a no-cost entry card with instant approval. However, its modest rewards and lack of premium perks mean it's not for everyone. If you have a good credit score, explore better cashback cards; otherwise, this limited-period offer is worth grabbing.
