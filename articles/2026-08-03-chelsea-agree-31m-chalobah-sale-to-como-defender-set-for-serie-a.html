Chelsea agree £31m Chalobah sale to Como; defender set for Serie A

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Chelsea and Como have reached a verbal agreement worth £31 million for Trevoh Chalobah.
  • Chalobah has agreed to a long-term deal with the Serie A club, ending his stint at Stamford Bridge.
  • The move is expected to provide regular first-team football and European competition for the England defender.

London, Aug 3: Chelsea have reached a verbal agreement to sell defender Trevoh Chalobah to Serie A side Como for a fee in the region of £31 million, with the England international reportedly accepting a long-term contract at the Italian club.

The deal, first reported by The Sun and subsequently confirmed by multiple outlets including BBC Sport, marks the end of Chalobah's long association with the west London club. The 27-year-old came through Chelsea's academy and has made over 80 first-team appearances, but has often found himself on the fringes of the starting XI in recent seasons.

Como's ambitious project under Fabregas

Como, coached by former Arsenal and Chelsea midfielder Cesc Fabregas, have been among the most active clubs in the European transfer market this summer. The club's ambition is clear: to establish themselves as a competitive force in Serie A and push for European qualification.

Chalobah is expected to play a key role in Fabregas's defensive setup, bringing Premier League experience and physicality to a side that has been rebuilding rapidly since their promotion to the top flight.

Why Chalobah is leaving Chelsea

Despite being a homegrown talent and a fan favourite at Stamford Bridge, Chalobah has struggled to secure a regular starting spot under successive managers. With the arrival of new signings and the emergence of younger defenders, his game time has been limited, prompting the club to consider offers.

The £31m fee represents a significant profit for Chelsea, who have been active in balancing their books under the Premier League's financial regulations. The sale also frees up a spot in the squad for potential reinforcements before the transfer window closes.

What Chalobah brings to Como

Chalobah is known for his versatility, capable of playing as a centre-back or in a defensive midfield role. His pace and reading of the game make him well-suited to the demands of Italian football, where tactical discipline is paramount.

His experience in the Premier League and European competitions will be invaluable to a Como side that is still finding its feet at this level. The chance to play regular first-team football and compete in European competition is believed to have been a major factor in his decision to move.

What happens next

The verbal agreement is expected to be formalised in the coming days, with a medical and personal terms likely to be completed before the end of the week. Chelsea fans will be watching closely to see if the funds from this sale are reinvested in new signings before the transfer deadline.

For Chalobah, the move represents a fresh start in a new league, and the opportunity to prove himself as a mainstay in a team that is on the rise. All eyes will be on Como's next few fixtures to see how quickly he adapts to life in Serie A.
