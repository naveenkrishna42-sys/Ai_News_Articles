Mumbai Police crack interstate cyber fraud ring

Mumbai Police dismantle ₹25.45 crore cyber fraud ring

Mumbai Police have dismantled a large-scale cyber fraud ring operating across multiple states, with an estimated worth of ₹25.45 crore. The operation, codenamed 'Operation Mule', resulted in the arrest of 12 suspects.

Operation details

The fraud ring used 'mule' accounts to launder money, a common method in cyber crimes where unsuspecting individuals are tricked into transferring funds. The operation involved extensive coordination between multiple states, with suspects operating from Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, and Hyderabad.

Key suspects

Among the arrested suspects are four individuals from Mumbai, three from Delhi, two from Bangalore, and three from Hyderabad. All suspects are currently in police custody and are being interrogated.

Investigation progress

Police have seized multiple devices, including laptops, smartphones, and external hard drives, during the operation. These devices are being examined for further evidence. The investigation is ongoing, and more arrests are expected.

Officials have not yet confirmed the identities of the masterminds behind the operation, but sources suggest that the ring had been active for over a year.

Impact on cyber security

The busting of this cyber fraud ring is a significant blow to the growing trend of interstate cyber crimes in India. It highlights the need for stronger cooperation between law enforcement agencies across states to combat such crimes.

What happens next? Authorities will likely focus on tracing the funds and identifying the masterminds behind the operation. The case could also set a precedent for future investigations into interstate cyber crimes. Watch for updates on the investigation and any further arrests.
