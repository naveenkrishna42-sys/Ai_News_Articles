History-sheeter hacked to death by gang in Washermenpet, 4 detained

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Victim, 28, was a history-sheeter with multiple cases against him.
  • Attack happened near Washermenpet on Sunday night; victim died on spot.
  • Four suspects detained; police probe motive and gang links.

Attack in Washermenpet

A 28-year-old history-sheeter was hacked to death by a gang in Chennai's Washermenpet locality on Sunday night. The victim, whose name has not been officially released, was attacked near a street corner around 9 pm. He died on the spot.

Police said the assailants arrived on motorcycles and attacked the man with sharp weapons. They fled the scene within minutes. Passersby alerted the local police, who reached the spot and sent the body for post-mortem.

Victim's criminal background

The deceased had multiple criminal cases registered against him, including charges of robbery, assault, and extortion. He was a known history-sheeter in the Washermenpet and surrounding areas. Police sources said he had been released on bail a few months ago.

Investigators are probing whether the murder was a result of a gang rivalry or a personal vendetta. The area has seen sporadic violence in the past due to turf wars between local gangs.

Four persons detained

Police have detained four persons for questioning in connection with the murder. The suspects are being interrogated at the Washermenpet police station. Officials have not yet confirmed their exact role in the attack.

A search is on for other suspects. Police have also recovered some weapons from the spot, which have been sent for forensic analysis.

Investigation underway

A special team has been formed to investigate the case. Police are scanning CCTV footage from nearby shops and streets to trace the assailants. They are also gathering details about the victim's recent movements and associates.

Senior police officials visited the crime scene on Monday morning. They have asked the team to expedite the probe and bring the accused to book.

What to watch: The detained persons may be formally arrested once their role in the crime is established. Police are also checking if the attack was planned or a sudden flare-up.
