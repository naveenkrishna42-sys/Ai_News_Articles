Joe Root played a quintessentially classy knock of 99 not out to steer England to a tense victory against India, leveling the ODI series. The innings, which fell just one run short of a century, was a masterclass in pacing a chase under pressure.

Kohli, who had held the record for several years, was powerless to prevent Root from reaching the landmark during the chase. The achievement adds another feather to Root's already decorated cap.
