Ozon warehouse hit in drone attack in Russia

📅 2026-08-23 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ozon warehouse in Orenburg region hit by drone attack
  • Governor confirms damage to the warehouse
  • Ukraine claims responsibility for the attack
  • Ozon reports no immediate impact on operations

Drone attack hits Ozon warehouse in Russia

Russian online retailer Ozon has confirmed that a warehouse in the Orenburg region was hit by a drone attack. Governor of the region, Sergey Gorokhov, has stated that the attack caused significant damage to the facility. The attack is believed to be the latest in a series of strikes by Ukrainian forces targeting Russian infrastructure.

Governor confirms damage

Gorokhov has provided details about the attack, stating that the drone struck the Ozon warehouse during the night. The facility is used for storage and distribution of goods. The governor has emphasized that the attack is a deliberate act aimed at disrupting Russian operations. He has urged authorities to take immediate action to address the situation.

Ukraine claims responsibility

Ukraine has claimed responsibility for the attack on the Ozon warehouse. The Ukrainian military has stated that the drone strike was part of ongoing operations to target Russian infrastructure. The attack is seen as a response to recent Russian actions in Ukraine. Ukrainian officials have praised the strike as a significant blow to Russian operations.

Ozon reports no immediate impact on operations

Ozon has released a statement confirming that the attack did not have an immediate impact on its operations. The company has stated that it is working to assess the full extent of the damage and to ensure that its operations continue as normal. Ozon has also expressed its concern for the safety of its employees and has urged them to follow safety protocols.

What happens next

The attack on the Ozon warehouse is likely to escalate tensions between Russia and Ukraine. Both sides are expected to continue targeting each other's infrastructure, with the potential for further strikes in the coming days. The incident also raises questions about the vulnerability of Russian supply chains and the effectiveness of Ukrainian drone strikes. Authorities in Russia are expected to take measures to strengthen security and to investigate the attack.
