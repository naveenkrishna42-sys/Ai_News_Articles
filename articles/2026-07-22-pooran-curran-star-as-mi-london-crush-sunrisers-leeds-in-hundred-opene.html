Pooran, Curran star as MI London crush Sunrisers Leeds in Hundred opener

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • MI London beat Sunrisers Leeds comfortably in the opening match of the Men's Hundred 2026.
  • Nicholas Pooran and Sam Curran starred with the bat, anchoring the chase.
  • The win marks a strong start for MI London in their campaign.

MI London make a statement on opening night

MI London launched their Men's Hundred 2026 campaign with a commanding victory over Sunrisers Leeds on Wednesday. The hosts chased down the target with ease, thanks to explosive batting from Nicholas Pooran and Sam Curran.

The win at Lord's set the tone for the season. The team, backed by the Mumbai Indians franchise, looked sharp in all departments.

Pooran and Curran take charge

Chasing a competitive total, MI London lost an early wicket. But Pooran walked in and took control from the first ball. He smashed boundaries at will, putting the bowlers under immediate pressure.

Sam Curran joined him and played the perfect supporting role. The pair added a rapid partnership that took the game away from Sunrisers Leeds. Pooran brought up his fifty in style, while Curran accelerated in the death overs.

The required rate never climbed beyond comfortable. MI London reached the target with several balls to spare, much to the delight of the home crowd.

Sunrisers Leeds fight but fall short

Earlier, Sunrisers Leeds posted a total that looked defendable at the halfway stage. Their innings had moments of promise but lacked the final push needed to set a daunting target.

MI London's bowlers kept picking wickets at regular intervals. They never allowed the Sunrisers batsmen to build a match-winning partnership. The fielding was sharp, with a couple of crucial run-outs adding to the pressure.

Despite a late flourish, Sunrisers Leeds ended up 20-25 runs short of what they would have aimed for. The pitch played true, but their bowlers could not find the right lines and lengths to defend it.

What this means for the tournament

MI London will take confidence from this all-round performance. Their batting depth and bowling firepower make them early contenders. Sunrisers Leeds have areas to address before their next fixture.

The Men's Hundred is still wide open. But MI London have sent a clear message to the rest of the league.

Both teams will now look ahead to their upcoming matches. MI London travel to face Birmingham Phoenix next. Sunrisers Leeds return home to host Oval Invincibles.
