Cockroach Party Threatens New Nationwide Protest Over Student Crackdown

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Cockroach Party threatens to restart nationwide protest if agitators face action following Supreme Court order.
  • Party accuses BJP-ruled states of weaponising the court order against student protesters.
  • CJP demands a Parliament debate on police action during the July 20 protest, citing a breach of promise.

Fresh Stir Threat Over Student Crackdown

India's Cockroach Party has threatened to launch a fresh nationwide agitation if student protesters face punitive action following a recent Supreme Court order. The party, known for its anti-establishment stance, accused the government of breaking its word.

The warning came two days after the court issued its directive. The Cockroach Party's leader, often referred to as the CJP, said any move to target students would be seen as a betrayal of assurances given earlier. The party has demanded that the government not use the court order as a weapon.

Two Warnings in as Many Days

The threat marks the second warning from the party in two days. On Tuesday, the CJP had already signalled that a fresh stir was on the cards if the authorities did not back off. The party is particularly aggrieved by what it calls a coordinated crackdown in states ruled by the BJP.

Reports from multiple states indicate that police have been filing cases against students who took part in the July 20 protests. The Cockroach Party claims this violates a tacit understanding that the protesters would not be pursued once the court became involved.

Demand for Parliamentary Debate

The CJP has now called for a Parliament debate on the police action during the July 20 protest. The party argues that the way the protest was handled raises serious questions about democratic freedoms and the rule of law.

The July 20 protest saw large crowds in several cities. While the event was largely peaceful, there were reports of lathi charges and arrests in some BJP-ruled states. The Cockroach Party says this was a breach of promise by the government, which had earlier assured that students would not be targeted.

Accusations of Weaponising Court Order

The party's central accusation is that the BJP-led governments are weaponising the Supreme Court order. The CJP said the order was never meant to be used to harass students, but that is exactly what is happening on the ground.

Independent observers have noted that the court's directive was broadly worded, leaving room for interpretation. The Cockroach Party insists that any action against student protesters now would be a clear violation of the spirit of the court's order and would trigger a fresh round of nationwide agitation.

What happens next depends on how the government and the state administrations respond. The Cockroach Party has given no deadline for its threatened stir, but the CJP has made it clear that the party is ready to act if students are targeted.
