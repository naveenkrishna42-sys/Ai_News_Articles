Jared Leto denies sexual assault allegations after BBC report

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Four women have accused Jared Leto of criminal sexual conduct when they were teenagers, per a BBC report.
  • Leto has publicly denied all allegations through his representatives.
  • The accusations have been covered by international media outlets including Reuters, The Guardian, and The Hindu.

Allegations surface in BBC investigation

Hollywood actor Jared Leto is facing fresh allegations of sexual misconduct. Four women have accused the 54-year-old star of criminal sexual conduct, claiming the incidents occurred when they were teenagers.

The accusations were published in a BBC report that has drawn widespread attention. Leto, known for his Oscar-winning role in 'Dallas Buyers Club' and as the frontman of the band Thirty Seconds to Mars, has denied all claims through his legal team.

Details of the accusations

The women have not been named in the reports. They allege that Leto engaged in inappropriate and non-consensual sexual behaviour with them during a period when they were under the legal age of consent. Specific dates and locations have not been disclosed in the available material.

Representatives for Leto issued a statement rejecting the allegations as 'categorically false'. They said the actor intends to defend himself vigorously against what they describe as baseless claims.

Wider media coverage

The story has been picked up by multiple major news organisations. Reuters, The Guardian, and The Hindu have all reported on the allegations. The Guardian's coverage described a documentary titled 'Jared Leto: Hollywood’s Dark Secret' as containing 'utterly disturbing' accounts of abuse.

The Times of India also reported on the story, citing a former colleague of Leto who described his behaviour during a Thirty Seconds to Mars tour as 'horrible' and 'uncomfortable'. The colleague reportedly said there was 'no soul behind his eyes'.

Leto's career and previous controversies

Jared Leto has been a prominent figure in Hollywood for decades. He won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for his role in 'Dallas Buyers Club' in 2014. He is also the lead singer of the rock band Thirty Seconds to Mars, which he formed with his brother Shannon Leto in 1998.

The actor has faced scrutiny in the past over his behaviour on film sets and during promotional tours. However, he has not previously faced such direct criminal allegations of this nature.

What happens next

Authorities have not yet commented on whether any investigation has been launched. Legal representatives for Leto have indicated they will fight the allegations in court if necessary. The BBC report has reignited discussions about accountability in the entertainment industry, but no formal charges have been filed as of now.

The coming weeks will likely see further statements from both sides. Observers are watching to see if any law enforcement agencies take up the case.
