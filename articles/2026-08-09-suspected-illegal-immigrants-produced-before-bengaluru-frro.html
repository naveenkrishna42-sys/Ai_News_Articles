Bengaluru's Foreigners Regional Registration Office (FRRO) saw a fresh development on Sunday as a group of suspected illegal immigrants was produced before the authorities. The move comes amid increased scrutiny of foreign nationals residing in the city without valid documentation.

Legal Framework and Procedures

Under Indian law, foreign nationals must register with the FRRO if they intend to stay beyond a specified period. Failure to do so can lead to penalties, detention, or deportation. The Foreigners Act, 1946, empowers authorities to take action against those found staying illegally.

When suspected illegal immigrants are produced before the FRRO, the office typically verifies their passports, visas, and other documents. If irregularities surface, the case may be referred for further investigation or prosecution. In many instances, individuals are held at detention centres until their status is resolved.

Bengaluru has seen such cases before. In recent years, authorities have periodically detained foreign nationals found overstaying or working without proper permits. The city's cosmopolitan character and large expatriate community make it a focal point for immigration enforcement.
