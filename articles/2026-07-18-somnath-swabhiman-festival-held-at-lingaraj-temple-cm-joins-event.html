Somnath Swabhiman Festival Held At Lingaraj Temple; CM Joins Event

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Religion Original source ↗
Key points
  • Somnath Swabhiman Festival celebrated at Lingaraj Temple in Bhubaneswar.
  • Odisha Chief Minister attended the event in Ekamra Kshetra.
  • Festival promotes cultural heritage, unity, and spiritual harmony.
  • Event featured traditional rituals, performances, and community participation.

The Somnath Swabhiman Festival brought together devotees and dignitaries at the historic Lingaraj Temple in Bhubaneswar on Tuesday. Odisha Chief Minister attended the event, held in the sacred precincts of Ekamra Kshetra, underscoring the region's deep-rooted spiritual and cultural traditions.

Celebrating Heritage And Unity

The festival, named after the revered Somnath Jyotirlinga, symbolises a broader message of cultural pride and solidarity. Organisers highlighted the event as a platform to showcase Odisha's rich temple traditions and the enduring legacy of Lord Shiva.

Chief Minister's presence lent significance to the occasion, with officials noting his emphasis on preserving ancient rituals while encouraging community participation. The event drew large crowds, including local residents and pilgrims from nearby districts.

Rituals And Performances

Traditional ceremonies marked the day, starting with early morning prayers and offerings at the 11th-century Lingaraj Temple. Priests performed special aartis and chanted Vedic hymns, creating an atmosphere of devotion.

Cultural programmes followed, featuring Odissi dance and folk music performances. Artists from across the state presented pieces that narrate tales from the Puranas, focusing on Lord Shiva's cosmic dance and his role as the destroyer of evil.

Volunteers distributed prasad and organised community meals, reinforcing the festival's inclusive spirit. Local artisans set up stalls displaying handicrafts and religious artefacts, adding a vibrant market feel to the proceedings.

Political And Social Significance

The festival also carried political undertones, with the Chief Minister using the platform to appeal for communal harmony. In his address, he called for unity among all sections of society, drawing parallels between the Somnath temple's historical resilience and Odisha's cultural endurance.

Opposition leaders criticised the event as a publicity exercise, but organisers dismissed the charge, insisting the focus remained on faith and heritage. Security was tight, with police deployed in strength to manage the large turnout.

Authorities reported no untoward incidents, and the event concluded peacefully by evening. Officials have not yet confirmed attendance figures, but estimates suggest several thousand people participated.

What To Watch

The festival is expected to become an annual fixture on Bhubaneswar's cultural calendar. Observers will watch if the government announces more funds for temple infrastructure or heritage projects in Ekamra Kshetra in the coming months.
