The Arrival of a Super Mega

The Pokémon GO Ultra Unlock event has finally brought Mega Starmie into the raid rotation. While the starfish Pokémon has always had a cult following, its Mega Evolution raises a key question: does it carve a niche in the current raid meta, or is it just another dex filler? Let's break down the numbers and its practical applications.

Mega Starmie boosts its already good Attack stat to a respectable 261, while its Defense climbs to 205. With a max CP of 4,456, it is not a glass cannon, but it is far from a bulky tank. Its Water/Psychic dual typing gives it a unique set of resistances and weaknesses that define its usability.

Typing Synergy and Movepool

Mega Starmie's dual typing is its biggest advantage. It resists Water, Ice, Psychic, and Steel, making it a decent anchor in certain matchups. More importantly, its coverage allows it to hit Fighting and Poison types for super-effective damage with both STAB moves. This is particularly useful in raids against Pokémon like Machamp or Muk.

However, the Dark and Ghost weaknesses are crippling. In a meta where Darkrai and Gengar are common, Starmie will fold quickly. Its movepool includes Water Gun and Fast Attack, with Hydro Pump and Psychic as charge moves, which gives it the necessary tools. But the lack of a faster charge move like Surf hurts its consistency.

  • Pros: Dual STAB coverage, good Defense for a Mega, boosts candy for Water and Psychic types.
  • Cons: Outclassed by existing Megas, suffers from common weaknesses, no exclusive move.
  • Key Fact: Shiny Starmie is available during the Raid Day, a strong incentive for collectors.

Raid Day Value and Verdict

The Super Mega Raid Day itself is a boon for trainers. With five free raid passes, increased shiny odds, and bonus candy, it is an efficient way to build a team or farm XL candies. Even if Mega Starmie isn't the best attacker, the event is worth participating in for the rewards alone.

In the long run, Mega Starmie will find a place on niche teams, especially if you lack a Primal Kyogre or Mega Swampert. Its Psychic coverage adds versatility, but it won't top any damage charts. For players who value type coverage over raw power, it's a fine addition. For the meta-chasers, save your passes for the next big thing.
