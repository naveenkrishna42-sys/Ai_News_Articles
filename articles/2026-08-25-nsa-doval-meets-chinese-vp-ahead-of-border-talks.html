India-China Diplomatic Engagement Intensifies

National Security Advisor (NSA) Ajit Doval's meeting with Chinese Vice-President Han Zheng marks a significant escalation in diplomatic efforts ahead of high-stakes boundary talks. This move comes as reports suggest Chinese President Xi Jinping may visit India for the BRICS Summit 2026, marking his first trip to India in seven years.

Xi Jinping's Potential Visit

According to multiple sources, Xi Jinping is likely to visit India next month for the BRICS Summit. This visit would be a historic moment, as it would be the first time Xi has traveled to India since 2019. The timing of this visit coincides with the upcoming boundary talks, adding an extra layer of complexity to the diplomatic negotiations.

India's Stance on Boundary Talks

The Indo-China boundary talks are expected to be a critical moment in the ongoing standoff between the two nations. Reports suggest that India may be willing to give up its long-standing position on an all-sector settlement with China. This shift in India's stance could have significant implications for the future of the relationship between the two countries.
