Chennai to Get Moderate Rain and Thunderstorm Today

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Moderate rain and thunderstorm expected in Chennai
  • Weather department issues alert for the day
  • Residents advised to take precautions

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast moderate rain with thunderstorms in Chennai for Sunday, August 2. The city, which has been experiencing humid conditions over the past week, is expected to get some respite as cloud activity intensifies over the coastal region.

Weather Forecast Details

According to the IMD's regional centre in Chennai, the sky is likely to remain generally cloudy with moderate rain accompanied by thunderstorm and lightning in some areas. The maximum temperature is expected to hover around 35 degrees Celsius, while the minimum will settle near 27 degrees.

Officials said the rainfall could be sporadic but intense at times, particularly during the afternoon and evening hours. Winds are likely to blow at speeds of up to 40 kmph in gusts during the thunderstorm activity.

Impact on Daily Life

The sudden weather change may cause waterlogging in low-lying areas and disrupt traffic during peak hours. Commuters have been advised to plan their travel accordingly and avoid parking vehicles under trees or near hoardings.

Fishermen along the Chennai coast have been warned to exercise caution while venturing into the sea, as squally weather could prevail in the coastal waters.

Precautionary Measures

The Greater Chennai Corporation has said it is prepared to handle any rain-related issues, with pumps and motor teams on standby in vulnerable localities. Residents in flood-prone areas have been urged to report waterlogging or fallen trees to the civic body's helpline.

Health officials have also cautioned against stepping out unnecessarily during the thunderstorm, and advised people to stay indoors and keep windows closed.

While the rain is unlikely to cause major disruption, authorities are monitoring the situation closely. The IMD will issue further updates if the intensity of the weather system increases.

The city is expected to see similar conditions over the next 24 to 48 hours, with weather models indicating the possibility of continued showers. Residents are advised to stay updated through official channels.
