England Beat France 6-4 in 10-Goal Thriller, Saka Hat-Trick Seals Third Place

Ten Goals, End-to-End Action

The 2026 World Cup bronze medal match turned into a classic. England edged out France 6-4 in a game that saw relentless attacking play from both sides. Thomas Tuchel's men held their nerve in a chaotic second half to secure third place.

Neither team showed any signs of playing for a consolation prize. From the first whistle, the tempo was electric. Defenders were stretched, and goalkeepers were kept busy. The final scoreline reflected the open, high-risk football on display.

Saka's Hat-Trick Heroics

Bukayo Saka was the star of the show. The Arsenal winger scored three goals, his first hat-trick for England in any competition. His pace and finishing were too sharp for the French backline.

Saka's first goal came from a swift counter-attack. His second was a composed finish inside the box. He completed his treble with a powerful strike that left the French goalkeeper with no chance. The 24-year-old has now cemented his place as one of England's most reliable players on the biggest stage.

Mbappe Breaks All-Time Record

Kylian Mbappe provided the highlight for France. He scored twice in the match, taking his all-time World Cup goal tally to a record-breaking number. The precise figure was not immediately confirmed, but multiple reports from the stadium indicated he surpassed the previous benchmark held by Miroslav Klose.

Despite Mbappe's individual brilliance, France could not contain England's attack. The French defence conceded six goals, their highest in a World Cup match in decades. Coach Didier Deschamps will have questions to answer about his team's defensive structure after such a chaotic performance.

Bellingham's Stunner Seals the Win

Jude Bellingham scored what many called the moment of the day. With the score tight late in the game, the midfielder unleashed an absurd long-range strike that flew into the top corner. The goal was described by commentators as a thing of beauty, a fitting way to seal such a wild contest.

The assist for Bellingham's goal came from a quick throw-in, catching the French defence off guard. It was a moment of individual genius that decided a game full of collective chaos. England's bench erupted, knowing the third-place finish was secured.

What Happens Next

England will return home with a bronze medal and a growing reputation as a team that can score freely under Thomas Tuchel. France, despite the loss, will focus on Mbappe's historic achievement. The World Cup now moves to its final, with both teams already looking ahead to the next cycle of international football.
