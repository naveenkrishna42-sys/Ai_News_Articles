Sonos Ace Ultra headphones revealed in FCC filing, AI features expected

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • FCC filing reveals Sonos Ace Ultra over-ear headphones
  • Expected to rival Apple's AirPods Max with voice assistant support
  • Broad push into AI features for Sonos products

FCC filing hints at imminent launch

Sonos appears to be gearing up for a major product launch. The company's upcoming Ace Ultra over-ear headphones have surfaced in a Federal Communications Commission (FCC) filing, a strong signal that a release is drawing near.

The filing, spotted by Engadget, lists the new audio hardware but doesn't reveal much in the way of specifications. Still, the paperwork is a mandatory step before any wireless device can be sold in the United States, and its timing suggests Sonos is in the final stages of preparation.

Voice assistant and AI ambitions

Reports from Bloomberg and MacRumors indicate the Ace Ultra will be positioned as a direct rival to Apple's AirPods Max. A key feature will be voice assistant support, though it remains unclear whether Sonos will integrate an existing assistant or develop its own.

Beyond the headphones, Sonos is reportedly planning a broad push into artificial intelligence. The company has been quiet on specifics, but sources suggest AI could play a role in audio tuning, user personalisation, and even content discovery across its speakers and soundbars.

Building on the original Ace

The original Sonos Ace headphones launched last year to decent reviews, praised for their comfort and sound quality but criticised for a lack of standout features at a premium price. The Ace Ultra appears designed to address those gaps with smarter functionality and deeper integration into the Sonos ecosystem.

A key question is pricing. The first-generation Ace retailed at Rs 54,900 in India, putting it in direct competition with the AirPods Max. If the Ultra version adds AI capabilities and an upgraded design, analysts expect the price to climb further, though Sonos has not confirmed any figures.

What to watch

Sonos has not issued an official statement about the Ace Ultra, and the company's plans could still change. The FCC filing is a strong hint, but it doesn't guarantee an immediate launch.

Industry watchers will be looking for an official announcement in the coming weeks, possibly alongside more details on Sonos's AI strategy. For now, audiophiles and tech enthusiasts will have to wait to see if the Ace Ultra lives up to the hype—and whether it can finally give Apple's premium headphones a serious challenger.
