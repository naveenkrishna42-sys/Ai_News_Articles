Hyderabad artist's viral video draws global support for temple painting

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Artist's Instagram video of a temple painting goes viral
  • Global audience offers support and appreciation
  • Hyderabad-based artist gains international recognition

A Hyderabad-based artist has found himself at the centre of an unexpected wave of international attention after a short Instagram video featuring his temple painting went viral. The clip, which captures the intricate detailing of the artwork, has resonated with viewers far beyond India, drawing messages of support and admiration from across the globe.

From local studio to global feed

The video, posted on the artist's personal Instagram account, shows the painting in progress — layers of colour, fine brushwork, and the gradual emergence of a temple facade. Within days, the reel amassed thousands of shares and comments, many from users in countries where Indian temple art is less familiar.

What began as a routine post soon turned into a conversation starter. Art enthusiasts, diaspora communities, and even casual scrollers found themselves drawn to the visual narrative of devotion and craft.

An outpouring of appreciation

Comments on the post range from simple praise to detailed questions about technique and symbolism. Several users have reportedly offered to purchase prints or commission similar works, though the artist has not yet confirmed any commercial plans.

The global response has surprised the artist, who is accustomed to a more localised audience. In interviews following the viral surge, he has expressed gratitude for the recognition, noting that the support validates the hours of labour invested in each piece.

The art of temple painting

Temple painting, as practised in Hyderabad and other parts of South India, is a specialised form that combines religious iconography with regional aesthetic traditions. Artists often train for years to master the precise proportions, colour palettes, and symbolic elements required for such works.

The viral video has inadvertently spotlighted this craft, introducing it to viewers who may never have encountered it otherwise. Art critics and cultural observers have pointed out that such moments of digital virality can serve as a bridge between traditional art forms and modern audiences.

What lies ahead

While the immediate buzz may fade, the artist's growing online following suggests a lasting impact. He has hinted at sharing more behind-the-scenes content and possibly conducting virtual walkthroughs of his studio.

For now, the temple painting remains a work in progress, but its journey from a Hyderabad studio to screens worldwide is a reminder of how art can transcend borders. Whether this leads to exhibitions, commissions, or simply a wider appreciation for the form, the coming weeks will reveal the true extent of this viral moment's reach.
