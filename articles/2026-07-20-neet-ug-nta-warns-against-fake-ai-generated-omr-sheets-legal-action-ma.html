NEET-UG: NTA warns against fake, AI-generated OMR sheets; legal action may follow

📅 2026-07-20 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • NTA has issued a stern warning against the use of forged or AI-generated OMR sheets by NEET-UG candidates.
  • The warning comes amid growing allegations of OMR tampering and score discrepancies reported by aspirants.
  • Legal action is threatened against those found circulating or using fake answer sheets.

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has issued a strong advisory to NEET-UG aspirants, warning against the circulation and use of fake or AI-generated OMR sheets. The agency has made it clear that any candidate found involved in such practices could face legal consequences.

The warning comes at a time when the medical entrance exam is already under scrutiny over allegations of result discrepancies and OMR tampering. Several students have reported massive gaps between their expected and actual scores, triggering protests and political backlash.

NTA's firm stance on fake OMR sheets

In a public notice, the NTA stated that it has come to its notice that certain unscrupulous elements are creating and circulating forged OMR answer sheets using artificial intelligence tools. These sheets are being used to mislead students and create confusion about the examination process.

The agency has warned that strict action, including lodging of criminal complaints, will be taken against anyone found fabricating or distributing such documents. It has also urged candidates to rely only on official channels for their results and admit cards.

OMR tampering allegations surface

Separately, an NTA probe has revealed possible tampering of OMR sheets after several NEET aspirants alleged huge gaps between their expected and actual marks. In one instance from Beed, Maharashtra, a student who expected 702 marks received only 87, sparking outrage.

These discrepancies have led to demands for a thorough investigation. The NTA has not yet commented on the specific findings of its probe but has assured that any irregularities will be dealt with strictly.

Political reactions intensify

Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal has raised concerns over the re-NEET result discrepancies and targeted the central government over the handling of the exam. He also slammed the NTA for what he described as threatening students instead of addressing their genuine grievances.

His remarks add to the growing chorus of criticism from opposition parties, who have accused the NTA of mismanagement and lack of transparency. The controversy has put the agency under pressure to ensure fairness and credibility in the examination process.

The NTA has advised students to remain vigilant and report any suspicious communications or documents. It has also reiterated that the official results uploaded on its website are the only authentic records of a candidate's performance.

As the agency moves to tighten security and verification protocols, candidates are being urged to cooperate and refrain from sharing unverified information. The coming days are likely to see more clarity on the probe findings and any corrective measures the NTA may announce.
