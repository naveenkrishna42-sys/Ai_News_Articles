Salman Khan breaks silence with cryptic post

Bollywood superstar Salman Khan has finally addressed swirling rumours about his health with a cryptic post on social media. The actor took to Instagram on Monday morning, sharing a brief but pointed message that left fans both relieved and amused.

The post, which appeared without any accompanying image, simply read: “Aap logon ki tabiyat…”. The incomplete sentence immediately sparked a wave of reactions, with fans interpreting it as Salman’s way of turning the tables on those speculating about his well-being.

Fans flood comments with get-well wishes

Within minutes of the post going live, the comment section was flooded with messages from concerned fans. Thousands of followers poured in with prayers and good wishes, urging the actor to take care of himself.

Many fans recalled similar instances in the past when the Dabangg star had used social media to cheekily respond to gossip. The overwhelming response highlighted the deep emotional connection Salman shares with his fanbase, who have stood by him through various controversies and health scares over the years.

A cheeky reply that won hearts

According to reports, Salman’s team later clarified that the actor is in good health and the rumours were baseless. The cryptic post was his way of poking fun at the speculation without making a formal statement.

“It’s classic Salman. He never does what people expect. Instead of getting defensive, he just made everyone realise how quickly they jump to conclusions,” a source close to the actor told Hindustan Times. The cheeky reply has since gone viral, with fans and fellow celebrities alike applauding the actor’s wit.

Rumours had spread like wildfire

The health rumours began circulating over the weekend after a few unverified social media accounts claimed that Salman had been admitted to a hospital in Mumbai. The news spread rapidly, prompting fans to trend hashtags like #GetWellSoonSalmanKhan on Twitter.

However, no official confirmation came from the actor’s family or management until Monday morning. The lack of immediate clarification only fuelled further speculation, with some outlets even reporting that the actor had postponed his upcoming film schedule.

What happens next?

With the cryptic post, Salman has effectively shut down the rumours without engaging in a lengthy clarification. Fans are now eagerly awaiting his next move, perhaps a more direct update or a glimpse of his upcoming projects.

For now, the actor’s social media timeline remains a mix of humour and mystery—exactly the way his fans love it.
