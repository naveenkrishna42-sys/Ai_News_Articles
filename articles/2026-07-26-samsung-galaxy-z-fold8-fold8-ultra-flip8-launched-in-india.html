Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8, Fold8 Ultra, Flip8 launched in India

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung launched Galaxy Z Fold8, Fold8 Ultra, and Z Flip8 in India.
  • Reliance Digital is stocking the new foldables in stores nationwide.
  • The Week 30 review highlights a poll asking readers which model they prefer.

Samsung Unfolds Its Latest Lineup

Samsung launched its next-generation foldable smartphones — the Galaxy Z Fold8, Z Fold8 Ultra, and Z Flip8 — this week in India. The company says the devices are designed to suit different lifestyles, from productivity-focused users to those who want a compact, stylish device.

The launch is part of Samsung's ongoing push to make foldables mainstream. The new models come with improvements in durability, display quality, and camera performance compared to their predecessors.

Reliance Digital Stocks the New Foldables

Reliance Digital has announced that it is bringing the Galaxy Z Fold8 series and Z Flip8 to its stores across India. Customers can walk into retail outlets to see the devices in person and purchase them directly.

The retailer's pan-India presence means the phones will be available in major cities and smaller towns alike. Samsung has not yet confirmed specific pricing or availability details for the Fold8 Ultra separately.

What's New in the Fold8 and Flip8

The Galaxy Z Fold8 and Fold8 Ultra are expected to feature larger, more durable screens with improved crease technology. The Z Flip8, meanwhile, targets users who want a compact foldable that fits easily in a pocket.

Samsung has positioned the new devices under the tagline "Foldables, Perfected for Every Way of Living." The company is betting on the devices' versatility to attract both early adopters and mainstream buyers.

Industry Response and Weekly Poll

GSMArena.com is running a weekly poll asking readers: "Will you buy a Galaxy Z Fold8, Z Fold8 Ultra, or a Z Flip8?" The poll results will give an early indication of consumer interest in the new models.

Other phones launched in India this week include the iQOO Z11 Lite, but the Samsung foldables have dominated the conversation. The phones are expected to compete directly with offerings from OnePlus, Xiaomi, and Google in the premium segment.

The launch comes at a time when the Indian smartphone market is seeing increased demand for premium devices. Samsung's foldables are priced at the higher end of the spectrum, but the company is hoping that incremental upgrades will convince existing users to upgrade.

Analysts say the success of the Fold8 series will depend on how well Samsung addresses durability concerns and how competitive the pricing is in the Indian market. Samsung has not yet shared sales targets for the new devices.

What to Watch Next

Samsung is expected to begin taking pre-orders for the Galaxy Z Fold8 and Flip8 in the coming days. The company will likely announce pricing and availability for the Fold8 Ultra at a later date. Consumers can expect to see the phones in Reliance Digital stores starting this week.
