Saba Pataudi Clears Air Over Kareena Kapoor Pregnancy Rumours

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Saba Pataudi confirms Kareena Kapoor is not expecting a third child.
  • Viral social media speculation stemmed from a recent video clip.
  • Pataudi clarifies the visual effect was merely a bad camera angle.

Setting the Record Straight

Social media speculation regarding Kareena Kapoor Khan’s personal life reached a fever pitch this week after a video clip went viral. Fans and followers began questioning whether the actor was expecting her third child with husband Saif Ali Khan.

The rumours gained significant traction across various digital platforms, prompting family members to address the situation directly. Saba Pataudi, the sister of Saif Ali Khan, has stepped in to dismiss the claims, putting an end to the ongoing public discussion.

The 'Bad Angle' Explanation

Saba Pataudi took to her social media channels to clarify the situation for concerned observers. She explicitly stated that the perceived baby bump in the viral footage was nothing more than an optical illusion caused by the lighting and camera positioning.

“It is just a bad angle,” Pataudi stated, effectively quelling the chatter. Reports from industry insiders have further supported this account, with some sources jokingly suggesting that the appearance was simply the result of a hearty meal, specifically pasta.

Family Dynamics and Public Scrutiny

This is not the first time the Pataudi family has had to address speculative reports regarding their private lives. As public figures, the family frequently deals with intense media scrutiny, especially concerning their living arrangements and family expansion.

Beyond clarifying the rumours, Pataudi used the platform to pivot the conversation toward her own family aspirations. She mentioned that she has been encouraging her sister, Soha Ali Khan, to consider expanding her own family, shifting the focus away from her sister-in-law.

Managing Viral Misinformation

The incident highlights the speed at which unverified information travels on digital platforms. A single video, often captured without context, is frequently enough to spark widespread speculation before official representatives have an opportunity to comment.

By responding directly, Pataudi has provided a definitive conclusion to the speculation. For now, the family continues to focus on their professional commitments and private lives away from the public gaze.

Observers are now looking ahead to the actor's upcoming professional projects, as the focus shifts back to her work in the film industry.
