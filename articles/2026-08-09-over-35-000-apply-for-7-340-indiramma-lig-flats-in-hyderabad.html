Over 35,000 apply for 7,340 Indiramma LIG flats in Hyderabad

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Over 35,000 applications received for 7,340 Indiramma LIG flats in Hyderabad
  • Scheme targets low-income group families with affordable housing options
  • Officials will verify applications before conducting a draw for allotment

Hyderabad: The state government's ambitious Indiramma housing scheme has drawn an overwhelming response in Hyderabad, with over 35,000 applications flooding in for just 7,340 Low Income Group (LIG) flats. The numbers underline the acute demand for affordable housing in the city, where property prices have been climbing steadily.

The Telangana government had invited applications earlier this year for the LIG category flats being developed under the Indiramma scheme. As the deadline closed, officials were left processing a pile of more than 35,000 forms, translating to nearly five applicants for every available flat.

What are Indiramma LIG flats?

The Indiramma housing programme is a flagship initiative of the Telangana government, aimed at providing pucca houses to eligible families across the state. The LIG category specifically targets households with an annual income between Rs 1.2 lakh and Rs 3 lakh, offering them flats at subsidised rates.

In Hyderabad, the 7,340 flats are spread across various locations, with basic amenities like water supply, drainage, and internal roads. The government has fixed the cost of these flats at rates significantly lower than the prevailing market prices, making them a lucrative option for many families.

Application process and eligibility

Applicants were required to submit their forms online as well as offline, with supporting documents such as income certificates, Aadhaar cards, and residential proof. The response has been particularly strong from the city's peripheral areas, where land values have appreciated in recent years but housing remains scarce for lower-income groups.

Officials said the scrutiny of applications would now begin, with a focus on weeding out ineligible candidates. Those who fail to meet the income criteria or have previously availed of housing benefits under other schemes are likely to be rejected.

Next steps: Draw and allotment

Once the verification process is complete, the government will conduct a draw of lots to select the beneficiaries. The draw is expected to be held in the coming weeks, with the entire process being transparent and conducted in the presence of officials and applicants.

Selected candidates will be allotted flats based on their preferences and availability. Payments can be made in instalments, making it easier for families to afford the homes. The government has promised to hand over possession within a stipulated time frame after the allotment.

Demand outstrips supply

The huge gap between the number of applications and the available flats has once again highlighted the housing shortage in Hyderabad. Real estate experts say the city's rapid urbanisation, coupled with rising construction costs, has pushed many low-income families to the margins, forcing them to rely on government schemes.

While the Indiramma scheme has provided some relief, the sheer number of applicants suggests the need for more such projects. The state government has indicated that it is working on expanding the programme, with additional phases likely in the near future.

As the scrutiny process gets underway, all eyes will be on the draw, which will decide the fate of thousands of hopeful families. For now, the message from the housing department is clear: patience is key, and the process will be fair.
