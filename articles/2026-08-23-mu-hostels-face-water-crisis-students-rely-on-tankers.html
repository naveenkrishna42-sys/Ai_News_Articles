MU hostels face water crisis, students rely on tankers

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mumbai University hostels facing severe water shortage
  • Students reliant on tankers for water supply
  • University administration working to resolve the issue

Water Crisis Hits Mumbai University Hostels

Mumbai University (MU) hostels are facing a severe water shortage, forcing students to rely on tankers for their basic needs. The crisis has left many students without access to clean water, affecting their daily routines and studies.

Students Struggle Without Water Supply

Residents of MU hostels have reported that water supply has been disrupted, leaving them with no choice but to depend on tankers for their water needs. This has created a significant hardship for students who are already under immense pressure due to their academic responsibilities.

University Administration Responds

The university administration has acknowledged the issue and is working to resolve it. Officials have stated that they are in the process of addressing the water shortage and ensuring that a steady supply of water is restored to the hostels as soon as possible.

Impact on Daily Life

The water crisis has had a profound impact on the daily lives of MU students. Many have reported difficulties in maintaining personal hygiene and preparing meals. The situation has also affected their ability to study and concentrate, as they are constantly worried about their water supply.

Looking Ahead

As the situation continues to unfold, it is crucial for the university administration to take immediate action to resolve the water shortage. Students are hoping for a swift resolution to this crisis, so that they can return to normalcy and focus on their studies without the added stress of water shortages.
