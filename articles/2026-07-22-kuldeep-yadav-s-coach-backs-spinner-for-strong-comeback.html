Kuldeep Yadav's Coach Backs Spinner for Strong Comeback

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kuldeep Yadav's coach is confident about his comeback despite recent ODI snub.
  • Ravichandran Ashwin questioned Gautam Gambhir and team management over Kuldeep's handling.
  • Gambhir's decisions face heat as critics call for clarity on selection logic.

Kuldeep Yadav's childhood coach has expressed strong confidence in the spinner's ability to bounce back after a period of inconsistency in the Indian cricket team. The chinaman bowler was notably left out of the playing XI for recent ODIs, sparking widespread debate over his handling by the current team management.

Sources close to the bowler suggest he remains focused on his skills and has been working on variations in the nets. The coach, who has known Kuldeep since his early days, believes the setback is temporary and that the spinner will reclaim his spot soon.

Ashwin's sharp criticism of team management

Veteran off-spinner Ravichandran Ashwin did not mince words when asked about Kuldeep's omission. He stated he has 'absolutely zero confidence' in the way Gautam Gambhir and the team management have handled the situation. Ashwin's comments have added fuel to a growing chorus of criticism.

Ashwin went a step further, reportedly urging Kuldeep to consider moving on from the ODI setup after the England snub. His remarks highlight a deep frustration among former players over what they see as inconsistent selection policies.

Gambhir's decisions under scrutiny

Head coach Gautam Gambhir's choices have come under intense scrutiny. A Rediff report questioned 'What Logic Is There?' in the current selection approach, pointing to a lack of clarity in team composition. The criticism centers on the management's preference for bits-and-pieces allrounders over specialist bowlers like Kuldeep.

This isn't the first time Gambhir's methods have faced pushback. Critics argue that sidelining a wicket-taking option like Kuldeep in conditions that assist spin is a strategic misstep. The team management, however, has not publicly explained the rationale behind the exclusion.

The fallacy of bits-and-pieces players

A Cricinfo analysis recently tackled the issue, calling out the 'fallacy of bits-and-pieces allrounders.' The piece argued that relying on players who are neither top-order batsmen nor frontline bowlers can weaken the team's core strength. For Kuldeep, this trend has meant fewer opportunities despite a proven track record in ODIs.

Kuldeep's recent performances in domestic cricket and the IPL have been encouraging, but the national selectors have not shown the same faith. His coach remains adamant that the bowler's class is undiminished and that patience will pay off.

The coming months will be crucial for Kuldeep Yadav. With a packed international calendar ahead, selectors will have to decide whether to persist with the current combination or bring back a proven match-winner. For now, the spinner's camp is quietly preparing for the next chance.
