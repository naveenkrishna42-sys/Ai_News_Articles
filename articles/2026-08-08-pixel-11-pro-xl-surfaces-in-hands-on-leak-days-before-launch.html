Pixel 11 Pro XL surfaces in hands-on leak days before launch

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pixel 11 Pro XL appears in hands-on leak ahead of August 12 launch
  • Leak confirms 'Glow' feature is officially called 'HiLight'
  • Google's event lineup includes Trevor Noah and Shubman Gill
  • APAC pricing guide compares Pixel 11 with Galaxy S26

Just days before Google's 'Made by Google 2026' event, the Pixel 11 Pro XL has surfaced in a hands-on leak, giving enthusiasts an early look at the flagship device. The images, shared by 9to5Google, showcase the phone's design and confirm a key feature that had been speculated for months.

Leaked hands-on reveals design and 'HiLight' feature

The leaked gallery provides a clear view of the Pixel 11 Pro XL's build, including its camera bar and display bezels. More importantly, it confirms that the rumoured 'Glow' feature will officially be called 'HiLight'. According to the leak, HiLight will let users highlight subjects or objects in photos and videos, with effects that can be adjusted after capture.

This is a significant addition to Google's computational photography toolkit. The feature is expected to work seamlessly with the Tensor chip inside the device, which has been tuned for on-device AI processing.

Pricing and regional positioning

Alongside the leak, an APAC price guide has surfaced, comparing the Pixel 11 series with Samsung's Galaxy S26 lineup. The guide, published by TechRepublic, suggests Google is positioning the Pixel aggressively in markets like India, where pricing often decides the winner. While exact rupee figures haven't been confirmed, industry watchers expect the Pixel 11 Pro XL to sit in the premium segment, competing directly with Samsung's top-tier offerings.

Google has not officially commented on the leak or the pricing details. The company typically stays silent on such reports until the formal announcement.

Launch event details emerge

The Times of India has reported that the 'Made by Google 2026' event will be hosted by comedian Trevor Noah, with cricketer Shubman Gill also appearing on the lineup. This mix of global and Indian personalities suggests Google is aiming for a broad appeal, particularly in the South Asian market.

The event is scheduled for August 12, and the company is expected to unveil the entire Pixel 11 series, including the standard model, the Pro, and the Pro XL. Accessories like the Pixel Watch and Buds could also make an appearance.

What the leak means for buyers

For prospective buyers, the leaked images offer a realistic preview of what to expect. The design appears consistent with previous renders, and the HiLight feature adds a tangible reason to upgrade for photography enthusiasts. However, as with any leak, details could change before the official reveal.

Analysts note that Google's focus on AI-driven features like HiLight could give it an edge over competitors, but the final verdict will depend on real-world performance and battery life, which remain unverified.

What to watch next

All eyes are now on August 12. The official unveiling will confirm pricing, availability in India, and whether HiLight lives up to its billing. For now, the leak has set the stage for what promises to be a closely watched launch.
