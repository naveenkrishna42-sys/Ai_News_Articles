Emraan Hashmi denies Mehmood role in R.D. Burman biopic

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Multiple reports linked Emraan Hashmi to the role of legendary comedian Mehmood in the R.D. Burman biopic.
  • Hashmi has publicly denied being approached for the film.
  • Other reports suggest Akshay Kumar may play Rajesh Khanna in the same project.
  • Farhan Akhtar and Neeraj Pandey are reportedly backing the ambitious biopic.

Bollywood is abuzz with speculation over who will star in the upcoming R.D. Burman biopic, but one name has already been pulled out of the hat. Reports on Monday claimed that Emraan Hashmi had been roped in to play the legendary comedian Mehmood in the film, which is being backed by Farhan Akhtar and directed by Neeraj Pandey.

Within hours, however, the actor himself stepped in to shut down the chatter. In a statement to a leading news outlet, Hashmi said he had not been approached for any such role. The denial puts a question mark over the casting news that had spread across entertainment portals through the day.

Conflicting reports over casting

The initial scoop, attributed to sources, suggested that Hashmi would step into the shoes of Mehmood, the iconic actor-comedian known for his impeccable comic timing and memorable performances in films like Padosan and Gumnaam. Mehmood, who passed away in 2004, remains a towering figure in Hindi cinema, and portraying him on screen would be a demanding task for any actor.

Adding to the confusion, another report from a film trade website claimed that Akshay Kumar has been signed to play Rajesh Khanna in the same biopic. If true, the film would feature two of Bollywood's most bankable stars in roles that require serious craft. Neither Kumar nor the filmmakers have commented on this particular piece of news so far.

Farhan Akhtar and Neeraj Pandey's ambitious project

The R.D. Burman biopic has been in the news for months, with Farhan Akhtar producing and Neeraj Pandey directing. The duo previously collaborated on the 2008 film Karthik Calling Karthik, and their reunion has generated considerable interest in the industry. R.D. Burman, often called Pancham Da, revolutionised Bollywood music with his innovative compositions, and a film on his life is expected to be a major event.

The project's ambition is clear — it aims to chronicle the life of a man whose music defined generations. From the jazzy beats of O Haseena Zulfon Wali to the melancholic melodies of Musafir Hoon Yaaron, Burman's genius remains unmatched. The biopic will need to capture both his professional brilliance and his personal struggles, including his turbulent relationship with singer Asha Bhosle.

Mehmood's legacy in question

Mehmood was more than just a comedian; he was a complete performer who could sing, dance, and deliver dialogues with a flair that few could match. His versatility made him a favourite among directors, and he appeared in over 300 films. Choosing the right actor to portray him is critical, and the speculation around Hashmi's casting suggests the filmmakers are looking for someone with a similar edge.

Hashmi, known for his intense performances in films like Murder and Once Upon a Time in Mumbaai, has also shown comic ability in recent projects. However, his denial raises questions about whether the reports were premature or entirely off the mark.

Industry insiders say casting announcements for such high-profile biopics often go through multiple iterations before being finalised. With several names floating around, the makers may be keeping their cards close to their chest. Neither Farhan Akhtar nor Neeraj Pandey has issued an official statement, and fans are left to speculate.

As the buzz continues, one thing is clear: the R.D. Burman biopic is shaping up to be one of the most anticipated projects in Hindi cinema. The combination of a legendary composer, a celebrated director, and a producer with a keen eye for storytelling is enough to generate excitement.

What remains to be seen is whether the final cast will match the rumours or surprise everyone with fresh choices. Until an official announcement is made, the speculation will only grow. For now, audiences will have to wait and watch which actor takes on the challenging task of bringing Mehmood and possibly Rajesh Khanna back to the silver screen.
