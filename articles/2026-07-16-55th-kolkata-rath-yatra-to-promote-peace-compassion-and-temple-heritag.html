55th Kolkata Rath Yatra to Promote Peace, Compassion and Temple Heritage

Key points
  • The 55th edition of the Kolkata Rath Yatra will focus on peace and compassion.
  • The event will also celebrate India's rich temple heritage.
  • Organisers expect thousands of devotees and tourists to participate.

Kolkata Prepares for 55th Rath Yatra

Kolkata is gearing up for the 55th edition of its iconic Rath Yatra, scheduled to roll through the city's streets next week. Organisers have announced that this year's festival will carry a special emphasis on universal peace, compassion, and India's temple heritage.

The annual chariot procession, which draws thousands of devotees and tourists, is one of the oldest such events in eastern India. The 2026 edition promises to be the largest yet, with preparations underway across multiple locations.

Universal Message Takes Centre Stage

This year's theme centres on spreading a message of peace and compassion that transcends religious boundaries. Organisers believe the Rath Yatra, rooted in the Jagannath tradition, offers a powerful platform to promote harmony in a divided world.

"The Rath Yatra has always been about bringing people together," said a spokesperson. "This year, we want to highlight how the festival's core values resonate with everyone, regardless of faith."

The procession will feature three towering chariots, each meticulously decorated with traditional motifs and colours. Devotees will pull the chariots along the designated route, chanting hymns and offering prayers.

Showcasing Temple Heritage

Beyond the religious aspect, the event will also shine a spotlight on India's temple architecture and cultural legacy. Organisers have arranged exhibitions and cultural performances that trace the evolution of temple art and rituals.

"Our temple heritage is a treasure trove of history and spirituality," the spokesperson added. "We want visitors to experience that richness firsthand."

The festival will include guided tours of local temples and displays of traditional crafts. Artisans from across the state have been invited to demonstrate their skills, from stone carving to textile weaving.

Logistics and Security

Authorities have put in place extensive security arrangements for the event. Multiple police teams will monitor the route, and barricades have been set up to manage the crowds. Traffic diversions will be in effect from early morning.

Medical teams and ambulances will be stationed at key points. Volunteers from local organisations will assist with crowd management and first aid.

The Kolkata Municipal Corporation has deployed additional sanitation workers to keep the area clean. Portable toilets and drinking water stations have been installed along the route.

Devotees Eagerly Await the Festival

For many in Kolkata, the Rath Yatra is a cherished tradition that marks the beginning of the monsoon season. Families prepare special offerings and dress in new clothes for the occasion.

"I have been coming to this yatra for over 30 years," said a local resident. "It is a time of joy and devotion. This year's theme of peace is very relevant."

Tourists from other states and countries have also started arriving. Hotels in central Kolkata are reporting high occupancy rates for the festival weekend.

The 55th Kolkata Rath Yatra is set to be a memorable event, blending faith, culture, and a call for harmony. As the city prepares to welcome the chariots, the hope is that the festival's message of peace will echo far beyond the procession route.
