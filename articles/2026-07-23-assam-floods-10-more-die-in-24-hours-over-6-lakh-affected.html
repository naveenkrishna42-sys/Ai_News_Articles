Assam Floods: 10 More Die in 24 Hours, Over 6 Lakh Affected

📅 2026-07-23 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • 10 more deaths in 24 hours, total toll reaches 50.
  • Over 6 lakh people affected across 872 submerged villages.
  • Gaurav Gogoi alleges severe delay in relief material delivery.
  • IMD debunks cloudburst claims, highlights infrastructure gaps.
  • Several states under red, orange, yellow rain alerts; landslides worsen Himalayan crisis.

Death Toll Rises, Relief Efforts Under Scrutiny

The flood situation in Assam continues to worsen. At least 10 more people have died in the last 24 hours, pushing the official death toll to 50. Over 6 lakh people are now affected across the state, with 872 villages completely submerged.

The disaster has triggered sharp political reactions. MP Gaurav Gogoi alleged a “severe delay” in the delivery of relief materials to the flood-hit population. His comments come as rescue teams struggle to reach stranded families in several remote areas.

Infrastructure Gaps Exposed

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has debunked claims that cloudbursts caused the deluge. Instead, officials point to prolonged heavy rainfall and weak infrastructure as the primary reasons for the scale of the disaster. The floods have exposed gaps in drainage systems and embankment maintenance across the Brahmaputra valley.

Landslides in the Himalayan foothills have compounded the crisis. Several states, including Assam, are under red, orange and yellow rain alerts. The situation remains critical in districts such as Majuli, Jorhat and Barpeta.

Missing Persons and Rescue Operations

Over 100 people are reported missing, according to The Times of India. Rescue teams from the National Disaster Response Force and the State Disaster Response Force are conducting operations, but bad weather is hampering progress. Many villagers have taken shelter on higher ground and in relief camps.

The Assam government has not yet confirmed the exact number of missing persons. Officials say they are prioritising rescue and relief over verification at this stage.

Political Fallout and Criticism

Gogoi’s allegations have added to the political heat. The opposition has demanded a thorough review of the state’s disaster preparedness. The ruling party has defended its response, stating that logistics in such extreme weather are challenging.

ThePrint reports that the floods have affected more than 6 lakh people, with many areas still cut off from road connectivity. Relief materials, including food packets and drinking water, are being airlifted to the worst-hit zones.

Weather Forecast and Next Steps

The IMD has warned that more rain is expected over the next 48 hours. Red alerts remain in place for several districts. Officials are on high alert for further breaches of embankments and rising river levels.

The coming days will test the state’s ability to manage a worsening humanitarian crisis. Rescue teams are racing against time as rivers continue to swell.
