5 credit cards to maximise GST 2.0 savings

Top 5 credit cards to maximise GST 2.0 savings

With the introduction of GST 2.0, consumers can now claim refunds on GST paid on their purchases. Here are the top 5 credit cards that offer high cashback on GST refunds, travel, and dining.

1. HDFC Bank GST Refund Card

The HDFC Bank GST Refund Card offers a flat 10% cashback on GST refunds. It also provides 5% cashback on fuel, groceries, and dining. The card has an annual fee of ₹500 and is available for a joining fee of ₹1,500.

2. ICICI Bank GST Refund Card

The ICICI Bank GST Refund Card offers a flat 8% cashback on GST refunds. It also provides 4% cashback on travel and 3% cashback on dining. The card has an annual fee of ₹499 and is available for a joining fee of ₹1,000.

3. Axis Bank GST Refund Card

The Axis Bank GST Refund Card offers a flat 7% cashback on GST refunds. It also provides 5% cashback on fuel and 3% cashback on dining. The card has an annual fee of ₹500 and is available for a joining fee of ₹1,500.

4. SBI Card GST Refund Card

The SBI Card GST Refund Card offers a flat 6% cashback on GST refunds. It also provides 4% cashback on travel and 2% cashback on dining. The card has an annual fee of ₹499 and is available for a joining fee of ₹1,000.

5. Kotak Mahindra Bank GST Refund Card

The Kotak Mahindra Bank GST Refund Card offers a flat 5% cashback on GST refunds. It also provides 3% cashback on travel and 2% cashback on dining. The card has an annual fee of ₹500 and is available for a joining fee of ₹1,500.

How to apply for special welcome offers and joining cashback bonuses

To apply for any of these credit cards, you need to meet the eligibility criteria and provide the necessary documents. The eligibility criteria vary for each card, but generally, you need to be an Indian citizen, have a valid ID proof, and earn a minimum income of ₹30,000 per month.

Once you have met the eligibility criteria, you can apply for the card online through the bank's website or mobile app. You will need to fill in your personal and financial details, and upload the required documents. After the application is approved, you will receive the card in the mail.

Most of these cards offer special welcome offers and joining cashback bonuses. For example, the HDFC Bank GST Refund Card offers a welcome cashback of ₹1,000 on your first purchase, and the ICICI Bank GST Refund Card offers a joining cashback bonus of ₹500.

Final Recommendation

If you frequently claim GST refunds and make purchases in the fuel, groceries, dining, and travel categories, these credit cards can help you maximise your savings. The HDFC Bank GST Refund Card offers the highest cashback on GST refunds, while the ICICI Bank GST Refund Card offers the highest cashback on travel. Choose the card that best suits your spending habits and eligibility criteria.
