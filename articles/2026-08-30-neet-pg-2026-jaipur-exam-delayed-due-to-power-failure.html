NEET-PG 2026: Jaipur exam delayed due to power failure

📅 2026-08-30 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Exam rescheduled due to power outage at Jaipur centre
  • Concerns raised about exam integrity and candidate rights
  • Broader impact on NEET-PG 2026 exam logistics and scheduling

Exam Rescheduled After Power Outage

The National Eligibility cum Entrance Test for Postgraduate (NEET-PG) 2026 exam scheduled for August 30 at the Jaipur centre has been rescheduled to September 5 due to a power outage. The outage, which affected the exam's server infrastructure, was reported around 9:30 AM local time, forcing authorities to halt the examination.

Concerns Over Exam Integrity

Candidates and experts have raised concerns about the impact of the rescheduling on exam integrity. The power outage disrupted the exam for approximately two hours, raising questions about the reliability of the examination process. Some candidates have expressed frustration and demanded explanations from the conducting authorities.

Candidate Protests and Demands

Protests erupted among candidates at the Jaipur centre, with some demanding refunds or re-examination opportunities. The disruption has also highlighted the need for robust backup systems to prevent such incidents in the future. Experts suggest that such disruptions can affect the fairness of the exam and the confidence of candidates in the examination process.

Broader Impact on Exam Logistics

The rescheduling of the Jaipur centre exam has broader implications for the NEET-PG 2026 exam logistics. The delay could affect the overall timeline of the examination, potentially leading to further disruptions if other centres face similar issues. This incident underscores the importance of thorough planning and contingency measures to ensure a smooth examination process.

Preparations at Other Centres

Meanwhile, preparations are underway at other centres, including Coimbatore, where the NEET-PG exam is scheduled to be held at 18 centres on August 30. Authorities in Andaman and Nicobar have also made necessary arrangements to ensure a smooth examination process. However, the Jaipur incident serves as a cautionary tale for all centres involved in the examination.
