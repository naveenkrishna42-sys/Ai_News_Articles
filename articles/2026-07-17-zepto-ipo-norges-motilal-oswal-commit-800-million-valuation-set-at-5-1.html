Zepto IPO: Norges, Motilal Oswal commit $800 million; valuation set at $5.1 billion

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Norges Bank Investment Management and Motilal Oswal are among anchor investors in Zepto's $800 million IPO.
  • The IPO values Zepto at $5.1 billion post-money, a significant drop from its earlier $7 billion valuation.
  • Zepto has reportedly reduced its IPO size by 20% amid shifting market conditions.
  • Sources indicate the company may now be valued between $3.5-4 billion, well below expectations.

Zepto's initial public offering has secured commitments from heavyweight institutional investors including Norway's Norges Bank Investment Management and domestic asset manager Motilal Oswal, according to sources familiar with the matter. The quick-commerce company is targeting to raise $800 million through the issue, which would value the firm at approximately $5.1 billion on a post-money basis.

The development comes as Zepto navigates a sharply recalibrated valuation environment. At its peak in late 2024, the company was valued at $7 billion. The proposed IPO valuation of $5.1 billion represents a decline of over 25% from that high, reflecting the broader recalibration in the quick-commerce space and investor caution around loss-making technology companies.

Anchor investors and structure

Norges, which manages Norway's sovereign wealth fund, and Motilal Oswal are expected to form the core of the anchor book for the IPO. The company is likely to allocate a significant portion of the issue to institutional investors, with the remainder reserved for retail and high-net-worth individuals.

Bankers working on the IPO have indicated that the anchor portion will be fully subscribed, providing a strong signal to the broader market. The $800 million target includes both fresh issue of shares and an offer-for-sale component by existing investors.

Valuation gap widens

The $5.1 billion post-money valuation is sharply lower than the $7 billion that Zepto commanded in its last private fundraise. Multiple reports, including from Bloomberg and The Economic Times, have suggested that Zepto may face an even steeper valuation cut in the public market. Some sources have indicated the company could be valued at $3.5-4 billion, nearly half its peak private valuation.

The gap between private and public market valuations has become a defining theme of India's IPO pipeline. Quick-commerce firms, which expanded aggressively during the pandemic on venture capital dollars, are now being measured against profitability metrics by public market investors. Zepto's path to profitability remains a key question for analysts.

IPO size trimmed

Zepto has reportedly reduced its IPO size by about 20% from earlier plans. The company was initially looking to raise $1 billion but has scaled down to $800 million in response to feedback from institutional investors. The move mirrors a broader trend of Indian startups moderating their IPO ambitions as market conditions remain choppy.

The company's decision to lower the quantum of the issue also signals a willingness to compromise on valuation to ensure a successful listing. Zepto's management has been in roadshows over the past month, meeting potential investors across Mumbai, Singapore, and the United States.

Competitive landscape

Zepto operates in the hyper-competitive Indian quick-commerce market, where it competes with Zomato-owned Blinkit, Swiggy's Instamart, and Tata-backed BigBasket. The sector has seen intense price wars and high cash burn as companies vie for market share in India's top cities.

Blinkit, which is part of publicly listed Zomato, has been profitable at the unit level for several quarters. Swiggy, which listed in 2024, has also shown improving margins. Zepto's IPO will be closely watched as a barometer for investor appetite for pure-play quick-commerce models that are yet to demonstrate consistent profitability.

What to watch now: The final price band for the IPO is expected to be announced in the coming days. Subscription data from anchor investors will set the tone for retail participation. All eyes will be on whether Zepto can sustain its $5.1 billion valuation through the listing day or if the grey market is already pricing in a deeper discount.
