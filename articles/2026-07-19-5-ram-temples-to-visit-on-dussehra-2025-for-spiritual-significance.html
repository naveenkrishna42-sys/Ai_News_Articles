5 Ram Temples to Visit on Dussehra 2025 for Spiritual Significance

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Religion Original source ↗
Key points
  • Dussehra 2025 is an auspicious day to visit Ram temples, marking Lord Ram's triumph over Ravana.
  • The list includes Ayodhya's Ram Janmabhoomi, Bhadrachalam, Rameswaram, Kodandarama Temple, and Ramaswamy Temple.
  • Each temple has unique mythological and historical connections to Lord Ram's life.

Dussehra and the Ram Temples

Dussehra, the festival celebrating Lord Ram's victory over the demon king Ravana, holds deep religious significance for Hindus across India. For devotees, visiting a Ram temple on this day is a way to honour the deity and seek blessings. As Dussehra 2025 approaches, many pilgrims are planning their travel to sacred sites associated with Lord Ram.

Outlook India has compiled a list of five must-visit Ram temples that stand out for their historical, mythological, and spiritual importance. These temples are spread across the country, offering a blend of devotion and heritage.

Ayodhya's Ram Janmabhoomi Temple

Ayodhya, the birthplace of Lord Ram, tops the list. The Ram Janmabhoomi temple in Uttar Pradesh is a modern marvel of architecture and faith. Inaugurated in January 2024, it draws millions of devotees annually.

On Dussehra, the temple sees special rituals and a grand aarti. The city itself is decorated with lights and flowers, creating a festive atmosphere. Pilgrims can also visit nearby ghats along the Sarayu River for a complete spiritual experience.

Bhadrachalam Temple, Telangana

Located on the banks of the Godavari River, the Bhadrachalam Temple is dedicated to Lord Ram as Sita Ram. According to legend, it is the site where Lord Ram stayed during his exile. The temple is known for its intricate carvings and the annual Kalyanotsavam, a reenactment of Ram and Sita's wedding.

Dussehra celebrations here include special poojas and processions. The temple's serene location amidst lush greenery adds to its appeal for devotees seeking peace and devotion.

Rameswaram Temple, Tamil Nadu

Rameswaram's Ramanathaswamy Temple is one of the 12 Jyotirlingas and holds immense significance in the Ramayana. It is believed that Lord Ram worshipped Lord Shiva here to atone for sins after killing Ravana. The temple's long corridors and ornate pillars are architectural wonders.

On Dussehra, devotees take a holy dip in the Agni Theertham, the sea near the temple, before offering prayers. The island town also has several other Ram-related sites, making it a key pilgrimage destination.

Kodandarama Temple, Karnataka

The Kodandarama Temple in Hampi, Karnataka, is dedicated to Lord Ram in his archer form. The temple's name comes from the bow (Kodanda) he holds. It is part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Hampi, known for its Vijayanagara Empire ruins.

Dussehra here combines religious rituals with a chance to explore history. The temple's location near the Tungabhadra River and the surrounding boulder-strewn landscape offers a unique backdrop for prayer and reflection.

Ramaswamy Temple, Tamil Nadu

In Kumbakonam, Tamil Nadu, the Ramaswamy Temple is a 16th-century marvel known for its frescoes depicting scenes from the Ramayana. The temple's sanctum houses idols of Lord Ram, Sita, Lakshman, and Hanuman in a single shrine, a rare feature.

During Dussehra, special discourses and cultural programs are held. The temple's detailed paintings and architecture make it a favorite among art and history enthusiasts, apart from devotees.

Each of these temples offers a distinct experience tied to the Ramayana. Whether through mythology, architecture, or natural setting, they provide a way for pilgrims to connect with the spirit of Dussehra.

What to Watch

As Dussehra 2025 nears, local authorities are expected to announce crowd management measures and special train or bus services to these temples. Pilgrims should check official websites for updated timings and entry protocols before planning their visit.
