Bodhi Greens Launches All-Vegan Wellness Retreat in Dharamshala

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Lifestyle Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bodhi Greens has launched an all-vegan wellness retreat in Dharamshala.
  • The retreat focuses on plant-based dining, yoga, and Himalayan nature experiences.
  • It caters to travellers seeking holistic health and sustainable tourism.

A New Kind of Himalayan Escape

Nestled in the pine-clad hills of Dharamshala, Bodhi Greens has thrown open its doors to travellers seeking a different kind of mountain holiday. The new all-vegan wellness retreat combines plant-based living with the stillness of the Himalayas, offering guests a chance to reset both body and mind.

The retreat is designed for those who want more than a scenic getaway. It promises a structured yet flexible schedule of yoga sessions, guided meditations, and nature walks, all anchored by a kitchen that serves exclusively vegan meals.

Plant-Based Dining at the Core

Food is central to the Bodhi Greens experience. Every meal is prepared from locally sourced vegetables, grains, and spices, with menus that change daily to reflect seasonal produce. The chefs avoid all animal products, including dairy and honey, ensuring that every plate aligns with the retreat's ethical and health-focused philosophy.

Guests can expect hearty North Indian dishes, light continental options, and herbal teas brewed from Himalayan herbs. The kitchen also accommodates gluten-free and raw-food preferences, making the retreat accessible to a wide range of dietary needs.

Wellness Beyond the Mat

While yoga and meditation form the backbone of the daily routine, the retreat encourages guests to explore the surrounding landscape. Morning hikes to nearby viewpoints, visits to local monasteries, and quiet corners for reading or journaling are all part of the offering.

The property itself is built with sustainability in mind, using natural materials and solar power where possible. This eco-conscious approach extends to waste management and water conservation, reflecting a commitment to leaving a minimal footprint on the fragile mountain environment.

Who Is It For?

The retreat caters to solo travellers, couples, and small groups. It is particularly suited to first-time vegans curious about plant-based living, as well as seasoned practitioners looking for a supportive environment. The staff includes trained yoga instructors and wellness guides who are available for one-on-one consultations.

Pricing is positioned as mid-range, with packages covering accommodation, meals, and daily activities. Guests can choose from private rooms or shared dormitories, depending on their budget and preference for solitude.

Dharamshala's Growing Wellness Scene

Dharamshala has long been a magnet for spiritual seekers, thanks to its Tibetan community and the presence of the Dalai Lama. Bodhi Greens adds a contemporary, health-focused layer to this tradition, appealing to a younger, more eco-conscious demographic.

Local tourism officials have noted a steady rise in wellness-related travel to the region, and Bodhi Greens appears well-positioned to tap into this trend. The retreat has already received bookings for the spring season, with several weeks sold out in advance.

As the retreat gains visibility through social media and word of mouth, it could set a benchmark for vegan hospitality in the Indian Himalayas. The team behind Bodhi Greens plans to introduce week-long detox programmes and seasonal wellness workshops later this year.
