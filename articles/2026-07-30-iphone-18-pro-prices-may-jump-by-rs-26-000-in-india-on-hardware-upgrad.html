iPhone 18 Pro prices may jump by Rs 26,000 in India on hardware upgrades

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max could cost up to Rs 26,000 more than current models
  • Price hike linked to major hardware upgrades including A20 chip and camera enhancements
  • Spring delay in iPhone 18 release expected to boost autumn sales of Pro models
  • Larger battery and new camera features rumored for Pro Max variant

Apple's next Pro iPhones could burn a bigger hole in your pocket

Apple is reportedly preparing to hike prices on its upcoming iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max by as much as Rs 26,000 in India. The increase would mark one of the steepest generational price jumps for the company's flagship devices.

The price revision is tied to significant hardware upgrades that Apple is planning for the Pro line. Sources tracking Apple's supply chain indicate the company is investing heavily in new components for the 2026 models.

What's driving the costlier Pro models

Reports point to a next-generation A20 chipset as the primary cost driver. The chip is expected to be built on an advanced fabrication process, delivering substantial performance and efficiency gains over the A19 chip in current iPhones.

Camera hardware is also getting a major overhaul. The Pro Max variant is rumored to feature a periscope lens with enhanced optical zoom, along with upgraded sensors across all rear cameras. A bigger battery is also being tested for the larger model.

Industry analysts believe these component upgrades, combined with rising raw material costs, have forced Apple to reassess its pricing strategy for India.

Spring delay could shift sales pattern

Separate reports from Forbes suggest Apple may delay the standard iPhone 18 launch to spring 2026, pushing the Pro models to an autumn release window. The staggered timeline is expected to boost Pro sales as consumers who wait longer may opt for the higher-end devices.

Apple has not confirmed any launch dates. Historically, the company has unveiled new iPhones in September, but supply chain constraints and component shortages have occasionally disrupted that schedule.

What Indian buyers should expect

If the price hike materializes, the iPhone 18 Pro could start at well over Rs 1.50 lakh in India, while the Pro Max may cross the Rs 1.80 lakh mark. Current Pro models begin at around Rs 1.34 lakh.

Economic Times and Moneycontrol reports corroborate the upward pricing trend, with both outlets citing internal supply chain estimates. The final pricing will depend on import duties, currency fluctuations, and local taxes, which often push Apple's India prices higher than US rates.

The standard iPhone 18 and iPhone 18 Plus are expected to see smaller price increases, if any, as they will carry fewer hardware changes.

What to watch

Official confirmation on pricing and specifications is expected closer to the launch window, likely in late 2026. Until then, buyers will have to weigh whether the rumored upgrades justify the steep premium Apple appears to be preparing for its next-generation Pro models.
