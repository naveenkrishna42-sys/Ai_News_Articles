2026 Maruti Baleno Facelift: What Changed

The 2026 Maruti Baleno facelift was officially launched at a starting price of Rs 6.10 lakh (ex-showroom), reinforcing Maruti Suzuki's position in the premium hatchback segment. With design updates, feature upgrades, and improved efficiency, the refreshed model aims to counter rivals like the Hyundai i20 and Honda Amaze while retaining its signature comfort and space.

1. Exterior Design Overhaul

The facelift introduces a more sculpted front fascia with a revised grille, restyled LED headlamps, and integrated LED DRLs. The bumper design is sharper with black cladding accents, giving the car a sportier stance. New alloy wheel designs and revised taillights complete the visual upgrade.

2. Interior Comfort and Tech Upgrades

Inside, the cabin sees material upgrades with soft-touch inserts on higher trims. The infotainment system now supports wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, paired with a 9-inch touchscreen. A new 7-inch digital driver display is available, enhancing the cockpit experience.

3. Safety and Driver Assistance

Maruti has bumped up safety across trims, with dual airbags, ABS, and ISOFIX anchors standard. Higher variants get additional features like rear parking sensors, a rear camera, and automatic headlamps. The car continues to run on the lightweight HEARTECT platform for structural rigidity.

4. Engine and Performance

The 1.2-litre K-series petrol engine remains, delivering 88.5 bhp and 112 Nm of torque. Paired with a 5-speed manual or CVT automatic, the engine offers refined performance with ARAI-claimed fuel efficiency of 22.35 kmpl for manual and 20.32 kmpl for automatic variants.

5. Pricing and Trim Strategy

Launched across four trims—Sigma, Delta, Beta, and Alpha—the Baleno facelift offers competitive value. The Delta variant, positioned as the sweet spot, includes features like keyless entry, rear AC vents, and a fast-charging USB port. Resale and service network support remain key selling points for Maruti buyers.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • Value for Money: Base variant undercuts competitors like Hyundai i20 by over Rs 1 lakh.
  • Efficiency: Class-leading fuel economy makes it ideal for daily commuting.
  • After-Sales: Maruti's extensive service network ensures low maintenance costs.
  • Feature Gap: Lacks ventilated seats and wireless charging even on top trims.
  • Performance: Engine feels adequate but lacks punch under hard acceleration.

Should You Buy the Facelift?

The 2026 Baleno facelift strengthens an already solid offering. With a competitive starting price, updated tech, and strong efficiency figures, it remains a practical choice for families and first-time buyers upgrading from entry-level cars. However, those seeking more premium interiors or advanced driver aids may still lean toward the i20 or the upcoming Nissan Kicks.
