Nepal floods: Death toll rises to 788 as rescue efforts intensify

Nepal's Flood Disaster Worsens as Death Toll Climbs

Nepal's death toll from recent floods has risen to 788, with rescue efforts intensifying to find the 900 still missing. The disaster, triggered by heavy monsoon rains, has exposed the vulnerability of the Himalayan region to climate change.

Rescue Operations Underway in Hydel Tunnel

Rescue teams are drilling through debris to reach trapped workers at a hydel tunnel. One tunnel head has been located, but the exact number of survivors remains uncertain. The operation is complex due to the unstable nature of the debris.

Indian Tourists Among the Missing and Rescued

Among the missing are 149 Indians, with 500 others safe in China. A driver's quick thinking helped save tourists traveling towards Rasuwa for a Mansarovar trek, highlighting the importance of preparedness in disaster-prone areas.

U.N. Climate Chief Highlights Himalayas' Fragility

The U.N. climate chief has emphasized the fragility of the Hindu Kush Himalayas, stating that the disaster exposes the urgent need for climate adaptation and disaster preparedness. The region's unique geography makes it particularly vulnerable to such events.
