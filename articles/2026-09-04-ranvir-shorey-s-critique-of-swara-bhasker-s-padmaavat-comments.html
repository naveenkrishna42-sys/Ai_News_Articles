Ranvir Shorey's Critique of Swara Bhasker's 'Padmaavat' Comments

Actor Ranvir Shorey has criticized Swara Bhasker for her comments on the jauhar scene in the film 'Padmaavat'. Shorey, known for his sensitive portrayal of women in films, has expressed his disapproval of Bhasker's comparison of the jauhar to rape survivors.

Swara Bhasker's Defense

Swara Bhasker, a well-known writer and critic, has defended her stance on the jauhar scene. She argues that the scene is a part of history and should be respected. Bhasker believes that even Rani Padmini would agree with the jauhar, as it was a matter of honor and duty.

BJP's Attack on Swara Bhasker

The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) has attacked Swara Bhasker, accusing her of disrespecting women. The party has argued that Bhasker's comments are insensitive and disrespectful to women who have faced real-life trauma.

Padmaavat Writer's Defense

The writer of 'Padmaavat' has defended the jauhar scene, arguing that it is a necessary part of the story. The writer believes that the scene is a matter of historical accuracy and should be respected. The writer has also argued that some people are looking for 'negativity' in the film.

Did Swara Bhasker Call Rani Padmavati A Coward?

There has been speculation about whether Swara Bhasker called Rani Padmavati a coward. However, Bhasker has clarified that she did not mean to imply that Rani Padmavati was a coward. She believes that the jauhar was a matter of honor and duty.
