About TIVRA News

TIVRA News is an independently run news aggregation and summarization site. We use AI-assisted tools to gather, summarize, and publish news stories across World, Tech, Business, Sports, and Health, drawing on publicly available reporting from established news sources.

How our content is produced

Our editorial workflow combines automated drafting with human review and publishing decisions. Stories are generated with the help of AI language models based on original source reporting, then formatted and published to this site. We link back to original sources wherever possible so readers can verify details independently.

Our goal

We aim to give readers a fast, easy-to-scan overview of current events. We are not a substitute for original investigative journalism — we encourage readers to follow the source links on every article to read full, original reporting.

Editorial standards

  • Every article links to its original source.
  • We correct factual errors promptly when they're brought to our attention.
  • We do not knowingly publish fabricated events or quotes.
