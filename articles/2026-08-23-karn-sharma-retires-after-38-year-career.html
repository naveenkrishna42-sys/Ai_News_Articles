Karn Sharma Calls Time on Cricket Career

Karn Sharma, a name synonymous with Indian cricket, has announced his retirement from all formats of the game. The 38-year-old cricketer made the announcement on Sunday, bringing an end to a career that spanned nearly two decades.

International and Domestic Career

Sharma began his cricketing journey in 2005, making his debut for the Indian national team in 2008. He represented India in Tests, One Day Internationals (ODIs), and Twenty20 Internationals (T20Is), showcasing his skills across all formats.

On the domestic front, Sharma played for various Indian Premier League (IPL) teams, most notably the Mumbai Indians, where he won four IPL titles. His leadership and batting prowess made him a fan favorite and a respected figure in the cricketing world.

Tributes and Praise

Rinku Singh, a teammate and fellow cricketer, has praised Sharma's career. 'Everything has been great in Bhaiya's career,' Singh said, highlighting Sharma's contributions to the sport.

Sharma's retirement comes after a distinguished career marked by numerous achievements and milestones. He played his last match in 2026, leaving behind a legacy that will be remembered by fans and fellow cricketers alike.

Reflections on a Long Career

Reflecting on his career, Sharma has expressed mixed emotions. 'There is no point in playing anymore,' he said, acknowledging the physical toll of the game. However, he also expressed gratitude for the opportunities he had and the memories he created over the years.

Sharma's retirement marks the end of an era in Indian cricket. His journey from a young debutant to a seasoned veteran, and ultimately to a retired player, is a testament to his dedication and passion for the sport.
