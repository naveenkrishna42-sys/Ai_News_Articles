Infantino calls emergency FIFA staff meeting amid growing pressure

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Gianni Infantino has called an emergency FIFA staff meeting as pressure on his presidency intensifies.
  • Football legend Luis Figo has publicly called for Infantino to resign.
  • Questions loom over FIFA's shelved Forward Enterprise programme and executive scrutiny.
  • The crisis meeting comes amid wider calls for governance reforms within world football's governing body.

Emergency Meeting Called

FIFA president Gianni Infantino has summoned staff to an emergency meeting, according to reports, as pressure mounts on his leadership. The development, first reported by ESPN, signals a leadership under siege as questions swirl around key FIFA executives and shelved projects.

The meeting comes at a fraught moment for the world football governing body, with several threads of scrutiny converging on Infantino's tenure. While FIFA has not officially commented on the agenda, sources suggest the gathering is aimed at addressing internal disquiet and plotting a response to escalating external criticism.

Figo Demands Resignation

The pressure intensified this week when Portuguese football legend Luís Figo publicly joined calls for Infantino to step down. The former Barcelona and Real Madrid winger, a Ballon d'Or winner, said the beleaguered FIFA president 'must go', adding a powerful voice to a growing chorus of dissent.

Figo's intervention carries weight. As one of the most recognisable figures in the modern game, his call for resignation amplifies the narrative that Infantino's position is becoming untenable. The Brazilian-born administrator has faced repeated questions over governance, transparency, and the direction of FIFA's commercial ventures.

Forward Enterprise Under Scrutiny

Central to the current turmoil is the shelved FIFA Forward Enterprise programme, a project that has drawn sharp criticism from various quarters. The initiative, once touted as a transformative digital platform for football development, was quietly put on hold, raising questions about financial oversight and strategic missteps.

The Hindu has reported on the programme's suspension, highlighting it as a key point of contention. While exact reasons for the shelving remain unclear, the episode has handed ammunition to Infantino's detractors, who argue it exemplifies a pattern of opaque decision-making at the top of world football.

Executive Tensions and Key Questions

BBC reporting has flagged 'key questions' facing Infantino as he contends with FIFA's executive committee. The relationship between the president and senior executives appears increasingly strained, with internal debates over reform and accountability coming to the fore.

Observers note that the emergency staff meeting is a defensive manoeuvre, an attempt to project unity while critics circle. Yet the optics are challenging: a president in crisis mode, rallying employees as prominent voices in the sport call for his head, and as legacy projects stall under a cloud.

What Happens Next

All eyes now turn to how FIFA's leadership navigates this turbulent period. Infantino's next moves will be scrutinised for signs of concession or defiance—whether he addresses the Forward Enterprise questions head-on or seeks to weather the storm.

With Figo's call echoing across the football world and executives reportedly restless, the coming weeks could prove decisive for the future of FIFA's presidency. Whether this emergency meeting stabilises Infantino's position or accelerates his downfall remains to be seen.
