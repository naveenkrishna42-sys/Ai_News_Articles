Next-gen foldable iPhone to get larger displays, new leaks suggest

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Third-generation foldable iPhone said to feature larger displays
  • New leaks suggest silver and dark blue color options
  • Apple's foldable roadmap reportedly includes multiple future models

Fresh leaks surrounding Apple's upcoming foldable iPhone suggest the third-generation device will arrive with noticeably larger displays than its predecessors. The reports, which emerged from multiple sources in the tech ecosystem, point to a significant screen size bump aimed at keeping pace with rivals like Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold series.

The development comes as Apple continues to fine-tune its foldable strategy, with the company reportedly planning a slate of devices over the coming years. Industry watchers have long speculated about Apple's entry into the foldable segment, and these latest leaks offer the clearest picture yet of what the Cupertino giant has in store.

What the leaks reveal

According to accounts from GSMArena.com and other outlets tracking the foldable iPhone, the third-generation model will feature larger displays on both the cover screen and the main foldable panel. While exact dimensions have not been officially confirmed, the reports indicate a design shift that would bring the device closer to the screen real estate offered by Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold 8.

The leaks also point to new colour options, with silver and dark blue reportedly in the pipeline. These finishes would mark a departure from the more conservative palette Apple has typically used for its flagship devices, suggesting the company is looking to make a visual statement with its foldable entry.

Apple's foldable roadmap takes shape

Beyond the immediate third-generation model, reports indicate Apple has a broader foldable roadmap in place. Firstpost and other publications have noted that the company is planning multiple future models, though specific timelines and specifications remain unclear. Apple has not commented publicly on any of these developments.

The strategic direction appears to be one of iteration rather than revolution. Apple's approach to the foldable form factor has been marked by caution, with the company reportedly taking time to address durability and hinge mechanics before committing to a full-scale launch. The third-generation device, if these leaks hold, would represent a maturing of that effort.

Comparing with the competition

The foldable smartphone market has been dominated by Samsung, whose Galaxy Z Fold series has set the benchmark for large-screen foldables. A recent comparison between the iPhone Ultra and Galaxy Z Fold 8 highlighted key differences in display size, weight, and multitasking capabilities, with Samsung currently holding an edge in several areas.

Apple's entry into this space has been anticipated for years, but the company's deliberate pace has frustrated some enthusiasts. The new leaks suggest that Apple is not merely looking to match competitors but to leapfrog them with a more refined product, albeit one that arrives later than many would like.

Colour and design signals

The reported silver and dark blue finishes are notable for more than just aesthetics. They indicate Apple is treating the foldable as a premium flagship rather than an experimental product. The colour choices also align with trends in the broader smartphone market, where matte and metallic finishes have gained traction.

Gadget Hacks first reported the colour leak, and the information has since been picked up by other outlets. As with all pre-release rumours, the details remain unverified until Apple makes an official announcement.

Analysts have pointed out that Apple's decision to expand display sizes could be a response to user feedback on earlier foldable prototypes. Larger screens would improve the device's usability for productivity tasks, a key selling point for foldables. However, some commentators have questioned whether bigger displays solve the core challenges of the form factor, such as weight and crease visibility.

For now, the focus remains on what Apple will deliver with its third-generation foldable. The company has a history of refining technologies over successive iterations, and the larger displays reportedly in the works suggest a continued commitment to improving the foldable experience.

As the leaks continue to trickle out, the tech community will be watching closely for any official word from Apple. The company typically remains silent on such rumours until a formal launch event, and there is no indication that this cycle will be any different.

The coming months should bring more clarity on the foldable iPhone's specifications, pricing, and availability. For consumers weighing a switch to foldables, the choice between waiting for Apple's offering and opting for an existing device like the Galaxy Z Fold 8 is becoming more nuanced with each new leak.

Whether the third-generation foldable iPhone lives up to expectations will depend on how well Apple integrates larger displays with its software ecosystem. The company has a track record of delivering polished products, but the foldable segment presents unique challenges that even the most experienced hardware makers have struggled to fully overcome.
