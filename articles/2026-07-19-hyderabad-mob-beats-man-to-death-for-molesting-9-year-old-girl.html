Hyderabad mob beats man to death for molesting 9-year-old girl

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • A man was lynched by a mob in Hyderabad for allegedly molesting a 9-year-old girl.
  • The incident occurred late Friday evening in the Old City area.
  • Police have filed a case and are probing the mob violence.
  • The victim's family has demanded strict action against the perpetrators.

Mob lynching in Old City

A 35-year-old man was beaten to death by a mob in Hyderabad's Old City area on Friday evening after he allegedly molested a 9-year-old girl. The incident took place near a busy marketplace, where locals caught the man in the act.

Witnesses said the accused was seen dragging the child into a narrow lane. When the girl screamed, nearby residents rushed to the spot. They caught hold of the man and began beating him with sticks and rods.

Police arrive late

Police reached the spot after receiving a call from a local shopkeeper. By then, the man had already been severely beaten. He was rushed to a nearby hospital, where doctors declared him brought dead.

Officials said the mob had dispersed by the time police arrived. No arrests have been made in connection with the lynching yet. An investigation is underway to identify those involved.

Victim's condition

The 9-year-old girl was taken to a hospital for medical examination. Doctors said she was in shock but physically stable. Her family has lodged a complaint against the deceased man for molestation.

Police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code for molestation and also for the mob lynching. They are scanning CCTV footage from the area to identify the attackers.

Community reaction

The incident has sparked debate in the locality. Some residents justified the mob action, saying they were angry about the crime. Others condemned the lynching, urging the law to take its own course.

Local leaders have appealed for calm. They said no one should take the law into their own hands. The police have increased patrolling in the area to prevent any further violence.

What happens next: Police will file a chargesheet against the accused molester. They are also likely to arrest those involved in the lynching soon. The case is expected to be heard in court in the coming weeks.
