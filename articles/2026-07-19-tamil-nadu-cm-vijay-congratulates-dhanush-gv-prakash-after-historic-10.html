Tamil Nadu CM Vijay Congratulates Dhanush, GV Prakash After Historic 10 National Awards

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tamil cinema won 10 awards at the 72nd National Film Awards, the highest for any language
  • Chief Minister Vijay personally congratulated Dhanush and GV Prakash for their wins
  • Rajkumar Periyasamy dedicated his award to Major Mukund Varadarajan's family
  • Dhanush called his first National Award as director 'truly humbling' and 'unforgettable'

Tamil Cinema Sweeps National Film Awards

Tamil cinema has made history at the 72nd National Film Awards, winning 10 trophies — the highest tally for any language this year. Chief Minister Vijay took time to personally congratulate the winners, including actor-director Dhanush and music composer GV Prakash, for putting the state on the national stage.

The chief minister's office released a statement praising the filmmakers for their dedication and artistry. Vijay highlighted that the haul reflects the depth and diversity of Tamil cinema today. Industry insiders have called it a landmark moment for Kollywood.

Dhanush and GV Prakash Lead the Winners

Dhanush, who won his first National Award as a director for the film 'Raayan', said the recognition was unforgettable. "I'm truly humbled," he told reporters, adding that the award belongs to the entire team that worked tirelessly on the project. 'Raayan' also picked up several other honours, cementing its place as one of the year's most acclaimed Tamil films.

GV Prakash was recognised for his musical score in a feature film. The composer, who has been a mainstay in Tamil cinema for over a decade, thanked the chief minister and his fans for their continued support. He described the award as validation for experimental soundtracks.

'Amaran' and Rajkumar Periyasamy's Tribute

The war biopic 'Amaran' also won big, bringing home multiple awards including Best Feature Film. Director Rajkumar Periyasamy, in his acceptance speech, dedicated his National Award to the family of Major Mukund Varadarajan, the real-life hero on whom the film is based. Major Varadarajan was posthumously awarded the Ashoka Chakra for his bravery in Jammu and Kashmir.

Periyasamy said the film was a small tribute to the soldier's sacrifice. He added that the award would not have been possible without the cooperation of the Varadarajan family, who shared personal stories to make the portrayal authentic.

A Record-Breaking Year for Kollywood

Tamil cinema's 10 awards this year include categories spanning acting, direction, music, and regional storytelling. The previous best for the industry was six awards in a single year. The jump to double digits has sparked celebrations across the film fraternity in Chennai and beyond.

Several winners have already begun receiving congratulatory calls from politicians and celebrities. Social media is abuzz with clips from the award ceremony, with hashtags like #ProudTamilCinema trending locally.

All eyes in the industry now turn to the 73rd National Film Awards. With this year's momentum, several Tamil productions already in the pipeline are expected to be strong contenders next time around.
