Mirzapur The Movie Day 6 Box Office: On Track for ₹150 Crore

Key points
  • Film crosses ₹150 crore in six days, outpacing most 2026 releases.
  • Per‑screen average and occupancy remain above 70% across metros and tier‑2 cities.
  • Success forces producers to rethink franchise budgeting and distribution models.

Opening Box Office Overview

Ali Fazal’s "Mirzapur The Movie" delivered a ₹150 crore gross by the end of day 6, confirming its status as the year’s most resilient Hindi‑language release. The film opened on 4,200 screens nationwide, with a strong push in Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, and the Hindi‑belt, regions that traditionally drive the franchise’s core audience.

Day‑by‑Day Performance

Day 1 recorded a ₹32 crore gross, a respectable start for a non‑festival release. Day 2 saw a modest 12% dip, typical of weekend dynamics, but day 3 rebounded with a 9% uplift as word‑of‑mouth spread on social platforms. The weekday surge on day 4‑5 was driven by repeat viewings, especially in tier‑2 markets where occupancy hit 78%.

Key Metrics and Pricing Context

Per‑screen average (PSA) has hovered around ₹35 lakhs, far exceeding the industry median of ₹22 lakhs for comparable budgets. Ticket pricing remained stable at ₹250‑₹350, indicating that the collection surge is rooted in volume rather than premium pricing.

Buyer Implications for Distributors

For regional distributors, the film’s sustained occupancy translates into lower risk on future franchise deals. The PSA suggests that a ₹10‑₹12 crore minimum guarantee can be justified for similar genre projects, provided they secure at least 3,000 screens in core markets.

Strategic Takeaways for Producers

Producers can leverage the data to negotiate higher satellite and OTT rights. Early‑window streaming deals for a film of this calibre could command upwards of ₹30 crore, given the strong theatrical run. Additionally, the success reinforces the viability of mid‑budget, high‑concept action dramas over star‑driven extravaganzas.

Pros & Cons / Key Real‑World Highlights

  • Pros: Consistent PSA, strong repeat business, robust tier‑2 performance.
  • Cons: Limited overseas footprint; North‑American and European markets contributed less than 2% of total gross.
  • Key Insight: Audience loyalty to the original series drives higher-than‑expected theater dwell time.
