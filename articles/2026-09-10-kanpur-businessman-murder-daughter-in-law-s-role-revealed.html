Background of the Incident

The recent murder of a prominent businessman in Kanpur has sent shockwaves through the city. The victim, identified as Mr. Rajesh Kumar, was stabbed 26 times in a planned attack. The case has raised questions about the role of his daughter-in-law, Ms. Priya Sharma, in the murder plot.

Investigation and Evidence

Investigators have uncovered a series of events that led to the murder. CCTV footage from a nearby restaurant shows Ms. Priya Sharma partying with friends the night of the murder. WhatsApp chats between Ms. Priya Sharma and her ex-employee, Mr. Anil Verma, reveal a detailed plan to eliminate Mr. Rajesh Kumar.

WhatsApp Chats

The WhatsApp chats between Ms. Priya Sharma and Mr. Anil Verma provide crucial evidence of the murder plot. Ms. Priya Sharma allegedly sent a message to Mr. Anil Verma, 'You are smart, are you in relationship?' which Mr. Anil Verma interpreted as a signal to proceed with the murder. The chats also reveal that Ms. Priya Sharma had hired Mr. Anil Verma to kill Mr. Rajesh Kumar for a sum of ₹6 lakh.

CCTV Footage

CCTV footage from a nearby restaurant shows Ms. Priya Sharma partying with friends the night of the murder. The footage also shows Mr. Anil Verma entering the restaurant shortly before the murder. The CCTV footage provides a clear timeline of the events that led to the murder.

Role of the Daughter-In-Law

Ms. Priya Sharma's role in the murder plot has been a subject of intense scrutiny. The investigation has revealed that Ms. Priya Sharma had a long-standing feud with her father-in-law, Mr. Rajesh Kumar. The feud allegedly stemmed from a financial dispute that Ms. Priya Sharma believed Mr. Rajesh Kumar had not resolved to her satisfaction.

Motive for the Murder

The motive for the murder is believed to be financial. Ms. Priya Sharma allegedly believed that Mr. Rajesh Kumar had not resolved a financial dispute to her satisfaction. The investigation has revealed that Mr. Rajesh Kumar had recently made a significant financial contribution to a charity that Ms. Priya Sharma was involved with. This financial contribution is believed to have triggered the feud and ultimately the murder.

Legal Proceedings

Ms. Priya Sharma has been arrested and is currently in custody. The legal proceedings are expected to take several months to conclude. The case has raised important questions about the role of technology in facilitating criminal activities and the need for stricter laws to prevent such activities.

  • The motive for the murder is believed to be financial.
