BJP Worker's SIR Form Scandal Sparks BLO Suspensions

Five BLOs (Booth Level Officers) have been suspended after a BJP worker was found photocopying 3,404 SIR (Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan) forms in Navi Mumbai. The SIR forms are used to register voters, and their unauthorized photocopying is a serious violation of the Election Commission's guidelines. The incident has raised questions about the integrity of the BJP's voter registration process.
