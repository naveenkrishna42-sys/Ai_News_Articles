Policeman killed in terror attack in Anantnag, Jammu and Kashmir

📅 2026-07-22 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Policeman Aashiq Hussain Qureshi killed in Anantnag, J&K.
  • CCTV footage shows terrorist shooting him at point-blank range.
  • First terror attack in the region since the Pahalgam killings.
  • Security forces have launched a cordon-and-search operation.

A policeman was killed in a suspected terror attack in the Anantnag district of Jammu and Kashmir on Wednesday, officials said. The incident has sparked a security alert across the region, with forces launching a search operation to track down the attackers.

The deceased officer has been identified as Aashiq Hussain Qureshi, a police constable who was part of a security detail. The attack took place when a group of suspected terrorists opened fire on a police party in the southern Kashmir district.

CCTV Captures Point-Blank Shooting

CCTV footage from the scene shows a terrorist approaching Qureshi and shooting him at point-blank range. The chilling visuals have been shared widely, underscoring the brazen nature of the assault. The officer was critically injured and later succumbed to his wounds at a hospital.

Sources said the terrorists fired multiple rounds before fleeing the area. Local residents reported hearing gunshots around midday, triggering panic in the otherwise busy locality.

First Major Attack Since Pahalgam Killings

This is the first terror-related fatality in Jammu and Kashmir since the Pahalgam killings earlier this year. That incident had sent shockwaves through the region, leading to a heightened security posture. Wednesday's attack has raised fresh concerns about the security of security personnel and civilians alike.

The Amarnath Yatra, an annual pilgrimage that draws lakhs of devotees, was already under high security. Qureshi was among the personnel deployed for yatra security duties. Officials have not yet confirmed if the attack was linked to the yatra or was a standalone incident.

Security Forces Launch Search Operation

Soon after the attack, security forces cordoned off the area and launched a combing operation. Teams of the Jammu and Kashmir Police, along with the Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), are searching nearby forests and villages for the assailants.

“We have inputs that the terrorists may still be hiding in the vicinity. All exit points have been sealed,” a senior police officer said. The operation is expected to continue through the night.

The attack comes at a time when the region has seen a relative lull in violence. Analysts say such incidents serve as a grim reminder of the persistent threat posed by militant groups operating in the valley.

Political Reactions Pour In

Political leaders have condemned the attack. Former chief ministers and local representatives took to social media to express grief and demand action. “This cowardly act will not deter our resolve to maintain peace,” one leader said.

The family of the slain constable has been informed. They are expected to receive compensation and other benefits as per government norms.

Authorities are yet to release further details about the number of attackers or the group behind the assault. Investigations are ongoing, with the CCTV footage being a key piece of evidence.

What to watch: Security forces are likely to intensify patrolling along the Amarnath Yatra route in the coming days. The identification and capture of the assailants will be a priority for the police.
