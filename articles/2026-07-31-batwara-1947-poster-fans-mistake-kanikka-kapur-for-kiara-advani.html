The first-look poster of the upcoming film Batwara 1947 has set social media abuzz for an unexpected reason. Fans scrolling through their feeds were convinced they spotted Kiara Advani in the frame. A closer look, however, revealed it was debutante Kanikka Kapur, whose striking resemblance to the established star has left many doing a double-take.

The poster, which features Karan Deol alongside Kapur, has triggered a wave of comments across platforms. Several users asked outright if the film included Kiara Advani, while others marvelled at the uncanny similarity. The confusion has turned into a talking point, giving the period drama an unexpected dose of viral attention.
