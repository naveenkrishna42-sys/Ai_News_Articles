Background: The Kashmiri Pandit Community

The Kashmiri Pandit community, numbering around 7,000, has a rich history in Jammu and Kashmir. They have been a significant part of the region's cultural and economic landscape. However, in recent years, they have faced increasing threats and violence, primarily from extremist groups.

Doxxing As A Weapon

Doxxing, the act of publicly releasing someone's personal information, has been used as a weapon in this campaign. The LeT's proxy group has been targeting the Kashmiri Pandits, threatening them and their families. This has led to a significant amount of fear and anxiety within the community.

Political Fallout

The doxxing campaign has led to a significant political fallout. Farooq Abdullah, the President of the National Conference, has questioned the threats to the Kashmiri Pandit employees and called it a 'conspiracy'. The BJP has also demanded a probe into the NC's role in the violence. This has led to a significant amount of political tension in the region.

Security Implications

The doxxing campaign has significant security implications. The threats to the Kashmiri Pandits have led to a significant amount of fear and anxiety within the community. This has led to a significant amount of internal displacement and a loss of confidence in the government's ability to protect them.

Conclusion

The doxxing campaign by LeT's proxy group is a significant threat to the Kashmiri Pandit community. It has led to political fallout and security implications. The government needs to take significant steps to protect the community and ensure that justice is served.
