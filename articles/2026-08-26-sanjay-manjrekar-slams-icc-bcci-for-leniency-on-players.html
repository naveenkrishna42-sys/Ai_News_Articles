Sanjay Manjrekar Criticizes ICC and BCCI for Leniency on Players

Renowned cricket commentator Sanjay Manjrekar has taken a strong stance against the International Cricket Council (ICC) and the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) for their leniency towards players involved in on-field clashes. Manjrekar believes that the recent incidents involving players like Vaibhav and Jaiswal highlight the need for stricter punishment and a more robust code of conduct.

ICC Imposes Sanctions, but Manjrekar Believes Punishment Should Be Stricter

The ICC has imposed sanctions on Jaiswal and Fernando, but Manjrekar feels that the punishment is not sufficient. He argues that the ICC and BCCI should not let players involved in such incidents 'get away' with just a warning or a fine. Manjrekar believes that the punishment should be stricter to serve as a deterrent for other players.

Manjrekar Calls for a Stricter Code of Conduct

Manjrekar believes that the ICC and BCCI should introduce a stricter code of conduct to maintain the integrity of the sport. He argues that the current code of conduct is not effective in dealing with on-field clashes and other misconduct. Manjrekar believes that the code of conduct should be more stringent to ensure that players are held accountable for their actions.

Manjrekar's Views on Recent On-Field Clashes

Manjrekar has been critical of the recent on-field clashes involving players like Vaibhav and Jaiswal. He believes that such incidents are not good for the sport and can have a negative impact on the game. Manjrekar argues that players should be more respectful and should not resort to physical altercations on the field.

Conclusion

Sanjay Manjrekar's criticism of the ICC and BCCI for their leniency towards players involved in on-field clashes highlights the need for a stricter code of conduct. Manjrekar believes that the punishment should be stricter to serve as a deterrent for other players. He also calls for a more robust code of conduct to maintain the integrity of the sport.
