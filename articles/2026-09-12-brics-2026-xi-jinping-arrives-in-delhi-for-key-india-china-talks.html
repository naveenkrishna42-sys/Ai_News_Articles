Xi Jinping Lands in Delhi for BRICS 2026 Summit

High-Level Security Measures for a High-Profile Visit

Chinese President Xi Jinping arrived in Delhi on Saturday, September 12, 2026, marking the beginning of a tightly scheduled 24-hour visit centered around the BRICS Summit. The visit comes at a time of cautious diplomatic engagement, with both nations aiming to balance strategic rivalry with cooperative frameworks. A security detail of over 15,000 personnel has been deployed to ensure the safety of the visiting dignitary, underscoring the sensitivity and significance of the trip.

Agenda Set: Bilateral Talks and Trade Cooperation

During his stay, President Xi is scheduled to hold bilateral talks with Prime Minister Narendra Modi, focusing on trade relations, border stability, and multilateral collaboration within BRICS. The Indian government has emphasized its push for expanded trade cooperation, even as it remains wary of China's growing economic influence in the region. Both sides are expected to address longstanding issues, including the trade deficit and infrastructure investment concerns.

Diplomatic and Economic Stakes

Flights Resume After Six Years

In a move signaling improved connectivity, China Southern Airlines is set to resume direct flights between Guangzhou and Delhi, marking the first such service in six years. The resumption of flights is expected to boost tourism, business travel, and cultural exchanges between the two nations. Industry experts estimate that the route could facilitate over 1.5 lakh passengers annually once fully operational.

Balancing Cooperation and Competition

While the BRICS platform provides a multilateral setting for engagement, India continues to navigate its complex relationship with China. New Delhi is seeking greater trade reciprocity and market access for its exports, while expressing concerns over China’s dominance in critical sectors like telecommunications and renewable energy. The Modi government's strategic autonomy approach remains key as it engages Beijing on equal terms.

Key Takeaways from Xi’s Visit

  • Security Overhaul: A 15,000-strong security force underscores the high stakes of the visit.
  • Trade Talks: Both nations are likely to push for balanced trade growth and dispute resolution.
  • Connectivity Boost: Resumption of direct flights after six years marks a step toward normalization.
  • Strategic Dialogue: Border stability and regional cooperation will be central themes in Modi-Xi talks.

Looking Beyond the Summit

The outcome of this visit will likely influence the trajectory of India-China relations in the coming months, especially in the context of BRICS expansion and global governance reforms. With both countries vying for influence across South Asia and beyond, the Delhi leg of Xi’s tour will be closely watched by regional stakeholders.
