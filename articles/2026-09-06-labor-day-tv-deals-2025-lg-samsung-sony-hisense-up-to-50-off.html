Deal & Pricing Analysis

Labor Day weekend marks one of the last major TV sales events before the fall sports and holiday season, and retailers are clearing inventory with discounts reaching up to 50% on select LG, Samsung, Sony and Hisense models. The deepest cuts are concentrated on last-year flagship OLED and QLED sizes, particularly 65-inch and 75-inch variants that typically carry the highest MSRPs. CNN tracked offers across major retailers showing LG OLED C4 and C3 series dropping into the mid $1,000s, Samsung QN90D and QN85D QLED sets falling below $1,200 for 65-inch, Sony Bravia 7 and X90L models with 30 to 45% reductions, and Hisense U7K and U8K Mini-LED sets approaching sub $800 pricing. Price history shows these levels match or beat Prime Day lows, with the added benefit of broader brand coverage. Stock is limited on top-tier sizes and bundles with soundbars or extended warranties are being used to sweeten the value. Shoppers should compare model years carefully, as discounts are strongest on 2023 and 2024 panels while 2025 flagships see more modest 15 to 25% cuts.

Real-World Performance & Daily Use

In daily use, LG OLED models deliver the best contrast and viewing angles for dark room movie watching, with near instant response times that benefit gaming at 120Hz with VRR support. Samsung QN90D offers brighter highlights and excellent anti-reflection for living rooms with windows, plus robust upscaling for streaming content. Sony Bravia 7 balances color accuracy and motion handling, making it a strong all-rounder for sports and film with its XR processing and Acoustic Surface Audio that uses the screen as a speaker. Hisense U7K provides the highest refresh rate and gaming features at the lowest price point, though peak brightness and local dimming precision trail the premium sets. Build quality is solid across the board, with LG and Samsung using more premium stands and finishes, while Hisense keeps weight and cost down. Smart platforms are mature, with webOS and Tizen offering the most app support, Google TV providing the best search integration, and VIDAA being fast but with a smaller app catalog.

Competition & Market Alternatives

Outside the Labor Day quartet, TCL QM8 and Vizio OLED remain price competitive in the sub $1,000 range, but lack the brand service network and panel consistency of LG, Samsung and Sony. For buyers prioritizing pure picture quality, the LG OLED C4 is hard to beat under $1,500, while Samsung QN90D wins for bright room performance. Sony Bravia 7 sits between them with superior processing and audio integration. Hisense U7K and U8K undercut rivals on price per inch, making them ideal for large 75 to 85-inch purchases where budget is constrained. If you can wait, Black Friday typically brings further cuts on 2024 models, but Labor Day offers the best selection before inventory shrinks.

Who Should Buy This Deal

This sale is best for shoppers ready to upgrade to a 65-inch or larger premium TV before football season and the holidays. Home theater enthusiasts should target LG OLED C4 or Sony Bravia 7 for contrast and color accuracy. Bright room households and gamers will benefit most from Samsung QN90D or Hisense U7K with high refresh rates and low input lag. Budget conscious buyers seeking maximum screen size should focus on Hisense Mini-LED deals. Those needing the absolute latest 2025 models with MLA OLED or 8K should wait, as discounts here are modest and stock is limited. Act quickly on specific sizes, as the deepest 40 to 50% cuts are on limited quantities and sell out fast during Labor Day weekend.
