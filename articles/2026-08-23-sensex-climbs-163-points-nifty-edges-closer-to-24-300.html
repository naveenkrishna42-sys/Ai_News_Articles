Stock markets rally as Sensex gains 163 points

Indian stock markets opened higher on Friday, with the Sensex gaining 163.35 points. The Nifty 50 index was near 24,300, marking a significant climb from its previous close. The rally was broad-based, with gains across sectors, led by the top gainer, Eternal.

Eternal leads the charge

Eternal, a leading player in the consumer goods sector, was the top gainer, surging over 5% in early trading. The company's strong earnings report and bullish outlook on the future have contributed to its impressive performance.

Sector-wise performance

The IT sector was also a key contributor to the market's rally, with several blue-chip companies posting gains. The banking and financial services sector also performed well, with stocks of major lenders rising. The auto sector, however, lagged behind, with stocks of major players declining.

Market sentiment remains positive

Market sentiment remained positive, with investors optimistic about the economic outlook. The Reserve Bank of India's recent policy statement, which indicated a cautious approach to interest rate hikes, has also contributed to the positive sentiment. However, there are concerns about the global economic situation, which could impact the market in the coming weeks.

What to watch next

Traders will be closely watching the upcoming economic data releases, including the consumer price index and industrial production figures. The Federal Reserve's policy meeting next week is also expected to have a significant impact on the market. Investors will also be keen to see how the global economic situation evolves in the coming weeks.
