Madhavan on 'G.D.N', India's unsung heroes and why 'Run' changed his career

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Madhavan studied rare 16mm footage and a 2-hour audiobook to portray G.D. Naidu.
  • The film 'G.D.N' releases on August 7, spotlighting Naidu's unsung contributions.
  • Madhavan credits 'Run' as a pivotal career moment that changed his path.
  • Vinay Rai's look as Bhupendra in the film has been revealed by the makers.

A Methodical Transformation

R. Madhavan doesn't do things by halves. For his upcoming film 'G.D.N', the actor immersed himself in the world of Gopalaswami Doraiswami Naidu, the man often called the 'Edison of India'. The preparation was intense: he listened to a two-hour audiobook on Naidu's life and pored over rare 16mm footage to capture the inventor's essence.

This wasn't just about getting the mannerisms right. Madhavan wanted to understand the mind of a man who built India's first indigenous motor car and founded the first electric railway in the country. The result, he says, is a film that honours a forgotten pioneer.

Why Naidu's Story Matters Now

G.D. Naidu was a polymath—an inventor, an industrialist, and a visionary who believed in self-reliance long before it became a buzzword. Yet, his name is barely known outside Tamil Nadu. Madhavan believes that telling such stories is essential, especially for a generation that often looks West for inspiration.

'We have our own heroes, people who achieved extraordinary things with limited resources,' the actor said in a recent interview. The film, which releases on August 7, aims to correct that oversight. It is a passion project for Madhavan, who has also co-produced the venture to ensure Naidu's legacy reaches a wider audience.

The 'Run' Effect

In the same conversation, Madhavan reflected on how the 2002 film 'Run' changed his career. The film, a Tamil action-drama, was a turning point. Before 'Run', he was seen as a romantic hero; after it, he was taken seriously as an actor who could carry a film on his own.

'Run taught me that versatility is survival,' he said, recalling how the film's success gave him the confidence to experiment with roles. It also paved the way for his later forays into Bollywood and web series, where he continued to choose unconventional projects.

A Supporting Cast of Intrigue

Meanwhile, the makers of 'G.D.N' have unveiled the look of actor Vinay Rai, who plays Bhupendra in the film. The character is expected to be a significant presence, possibly a rival or a collaborator in Naidu's journey. The reveal has generated curiosity among fans, who are eager to see how the two actors share screen space.

Details about Bhupendra's role remain under wraps, but the filmmakers have hinted that the character is pivotal to the plot. With Madhavan's meticulous preparation and a strong supporting cast, 'G.D.N' is shaping up to be a cinematic tribute that could spark renewed interest in India's industrial history.

What to Watch For

As the release date approaches, audiences will be watching to see if 'G.D.N' translates Naidu's complex legacy into compelling cinema. The film's success could also open doors for more biopics on unsung Indian achievers. For Madhavan, this is not just another release—it's a statement about the stories we choose to tell.
