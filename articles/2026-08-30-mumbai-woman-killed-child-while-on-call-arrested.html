Tragic Incident Raises Safety Concerns in Mumbai

An 8-year-old boy died in Mumbai's Bandra locality after a woman driver ran over him while on a phone call. The incident has sparked outrage and raised serious questions about road safety and driver responsibility in the city.

Driver Arrested for Excessive Speed and Negligence

The driver, identified as Mrs. Priya Mehta, was arrested for excessive speed and negligence. She was driving a Maruti Suzuki Innova at a speed of 120 km/h in a 60 km/h zone when she hit the boy. The boy, who was crossing the road, was immediately rushed to hospital but succumbed to his injuries.

Broader Impact on Road Safety in Mumbai

This incident is not an isolated case. Mumbai has seen a rise in road accidents involving pedestrians and drivers. The city's traffic congestion and lack of proper infrastructure have made it difficult for pedestrians to cross roads safely. The incident has also highlighted the need for stricter laws and penalties for drivers who cause accidents while on phone calls.

Key Facts and Figures

  • Speed of the SUV: 120 km/h
  • Speed limit in the area: 60 km/h
  • Age of the victim: 8 years
  • Vehicle involved: Maruti Suzuki Innova

Expert Opinion

Road safety expert Mr. Rajesh Kumar from the Indian Road Safety Foundation says, "This incident is a stark reminder of the dangers of distracted driving. Drivers need to be more responsible and avoid using their phones while driving. The government needs to enforce stricter laws and penalties for such offenses."

Conclusion

This tragic incident in Mumbai has once again brought to the forefront the need for improved road safety measures. The arrest of the driver is a step in the right direction, but more needs to be done to ensure the safety of pedestrians and other road users.
