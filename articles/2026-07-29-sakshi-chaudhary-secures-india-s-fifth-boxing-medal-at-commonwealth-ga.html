Sakshi Chaudhary secures India's fifth boxing medal at Commonwealth Games 2026

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sakshi Chaudhary has assured India a boxing medal at the 2026 Commonwealth Games.
  • She is the fifth Indian boxer to guarantee a podium finish at the event.
  • The Indian boxing contingent has dominated, ensuring eight medals overall.
  • Shot putters Tajinderpal Singh Toor and Gill have advanced to the final.

Indian boxer Sakshi Chaudhary has assured the country its fifth boxing medal at the 2026 Commonwealth Games. Her dominant performance in the ring has added to what is shaping up to be a stellar campaign for India's pugilists.

Chaudhary's victory in the quarterfinals guaranteed her at least a bronze medal. The exact nature of the medal — gold, silver, or bronze — will be decided in her upcoming semifinal bout.

Dominance in the ring

Chaudhary's assured performance is part of a larger story of Indian boxing success at these Games. According to reports, Indian boxers have collectively guaranteed eight medals for the country. This includes the five assured by women boxers.

The Indian boxing contingent has been in formidable form, with several pugilists advancing deep into their respective weight categories. The team's preparation and strategy appear to have paid off handsomely.

Arundhati Choudhary's dramatic weight cut

In a related development, fellow boxer Arundhati Choudhary also secured a medal. Reports indicate she lost 2kg in a single day to make weight for her category. The drastic measure underscores the intense discipline and sacrifice required at this level.

Choudhary's medal assurance adds to the growing tally. Her achievement, coupled with Chaudhary's, highlights the depth of talent in Indian women's boxing.

Beyond the boxing ring

The success is not limited to boxing. Shot putters Tajinderpal Singh Toor and Gill have both qualified for the final in their event. Their progress adds to India's medal hopes in athletics.

Toor, a Commonwealth Games gold medalist from the previous edition, will be looking to defend his title. Gill, meanwhile, will aim to make his mark on the big stage.

India's overall performance at the 2026 Commonwealth Games has been impressive across multiple disciplines. The boxing team's near-certain medal haul is a significant highlight.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of medals India is projected to win. However, the early returns suggest a strong showing.

What remains to be seen is whether Chaudhary and her teammates can convert these assured medals into gold. The semifinals will be the next challenge. All eyes will be on the ring as India's boxers aim for the top step of the podium.
