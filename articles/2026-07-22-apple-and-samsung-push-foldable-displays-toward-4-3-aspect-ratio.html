Apple and Samsung Push Foldable Displays Toward 4:3 Aspect Ratio

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Foldable smartphones are moving toward 4:3 aspect ratio displays, driven by Apple and Samsung.
  • Samsung has introduced Flex Titanium technology to advance foldable display durability.
  • The Galaxy Z Fold 8 leak shows a wider display ahead of its launch.
  • Samsung built the foldable market but now faces increased competition.

Shift in Display Design

Apple and Samsung are steering the foldable smartphone market toward 4:3 aspect ratio displays, according to industry sources. The move signals a new design cycle as both tech giants aim to enhance user experience and app compatibility on foldable screens.

The 4:3 ratio, long associated with tablets and laptop screens, offers a more square-like canvas. This could make multitasking and media consumption more natural on foldable devices, which have traditionally used taller, narrower displays.

Samsung's Flex Titanium Technology

Samsung has introduced Flex Titanium technology to advance its foldable displays. The new material aims to improve durability and reduce crease visibility, two longstanding pain points for foldable users.

The technology is expected to debut in upcoming Galaxy Z Fold models. Samsung's move comes as it works to defend its lead in the foldable segment, which it effectively created with the original Galaxy Fold in 2019.

Galaxy Z Fold 8 Leak Hints at Wider Display

A leak of the Galaxy Z Fold 8 suggests a wider display before its official launch. The device is rumored to feature a larger cover screen and a more expansive inner display, aligning with the 4:3 trend.

Samsung's Galaxy Unpacked event for July 2026 is expected to showcase the new device. The company has not confirmed specifications, but the leak points to a significant redesign aimed at improving one-handed usability.

Apple's Foldable Plans

Apple is reportedly working on its own foldable device, with early prototypes using 4:3 displays. The company is said to be testing multiple form factors, including a foldable iPhone and a larger foldable iPad.

Apple's entry into the foldable market could accelerate adoption of the 4:3 ratio. The company's influence on app developers may push more software to support the format, benefiting the entire ecosystem.

Market Competition Intensifies

Samsung built the foldable smartphone market, but it now faces growing competition from Chinese brands like Huawei, Oppo, and Xiaomi. These rivals have launched devices with innovative hinge designs and competitive pricing.

The shift to 4:3 displays could be a key differentiator in the next wave of foldables. As the market matures, consumers will likely see more choices in form factors and price points.

Industry analysts expect the foldable segment to grow significantly in the coming years. Both Apple and Samsung are betting on improved display technology and software optimization to win over mainstream users.

What to watch: The Galaxy Unpacked event in July 2026 will reveal Samsung's next-generation foldable. Apple's first foldable device is expected to launch in 2027 or 2028, depending on development progress.
