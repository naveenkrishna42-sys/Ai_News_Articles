Volkswagen's Tax Case Returns to Bombay High Court

The Bombay High Court has released the Volkswagen tax case, sending it back to the court for re-hearing after 1.5 years. This development has significant implications for the Indian auto industry and tax enforcement mechanisms.

Background of the Case

The case revolves around Volkswagen's alleged under-declaration of import duties on vehicles imported into India. The company had initially challenged the $1.4 billion tax demand, which was reserved by a bench 1.5 years ago.

The outcome of this case will have broader implications for the Indian auto industry. It will set a precedent for how tax enforcement is carried out and may influence future import duties and tax policies.
