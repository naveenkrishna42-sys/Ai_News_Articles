Chase Aeroplan Card Launch: 115,000 Bonus Points Available

Chase has introduced a new Air Canada Aeroplan co-branded credit card offering substantial bonus incentives for new applicants. The promotion grants up to 115,000 Aeroplan points for those meeting a $75,000 spending threshold within the first year of account opening. This represents one of the most generous transferable point offers currently available in the Canadian market for frequent flyers targeting long-haul international travel.

Bonus Structure and Spending Requirements

The 115,000-point bonus breaks down into 75,000 points for meeting the minimum spend, plus an additional 40,000 points for spending $75,000 in the first year. Points post within 6-8 weeks of meeting requirements, subject to typical verification processes. The offer appears time-limited, with Chase indicating availability through specific promotional windows rather than as a permanent welcome bonus.

Primary Earnings Rate

Beyond the welcome bonus, cardholders earn 1.5 Aeroplan points per dollar spent on eligible travel and dining purchases, including flights, hotels, car rentals, and restaurant bills globally. All other purchases earn one point per dollar. No foreign transaction fees apply, making it attractive for travelers making international purchases.

Annual Fee and Additional Benefits

The card carries a $99 annual fee, waived for the first year. Additional perks include free checked bags for the primary cardholder and up to four companions on Air Canada and Air Canada Rouge flights. Travel insurance coverage includes trip cancellation/interruption, emergency medical, and baggage delay protection.

Comparison to Competing Aeroplan Cards

Compared to other Aeroplan-issued cards, this Chase offering surpasses the standard TD Aeroplan card's 35,000-point welcome bonus but falls short of American Express Aeroplan cards that sometimes offer 100,000+ points with higher spending thresholds. However, Chase's lower annual fee after the first year ($99 vs. $120-$150 for premium Amex versions) creates long-term value for moderate spenders who prioritize basic flight benefits over elite status acceleration.

Redemption Value and Point Transfer

Aeroplan points typically value at approximately 1.2-1.5 cents each when booking Air Canada flights, meaning the 115,000 bonus could translate to $1,380-$1,725 in flight value. Points transfer to multiple airline partners including Emirates Skywards, Singapore KrisFlyer, and Etihad Guest, expanding redemption flexibility beyond Air Canada's network. Point charts have remained relatively stable compared to recent devaluations in other programs.

Practical Application for Travelers

For Canadian residents planning significant international travel, particularly with Air Canada or its alliance partners, the math favors this card. Spending $75,000 annually through the card generates 112,500 points ($75,000 × 1.5 points), exceeding the bonus threshold. Combined with the welcome offer, total first-year value reaches approximately 227,500 points—enough for two economy round-trip transatlantic flights or one business-class segment on many routes.

Application Considerations

Eligible applicants must be 18+, have valid Canadian mailing address, and meet Chase's standard credit approval criteria. The card is available exclusively through Chase's website or select branch locations. Given the promotional nature, applicants should verify current terms directly with Chase before applying, as offers frequently change based on regulatory and competitive factors.
