Affiliate Disclosure

TIVRA News participates in affiliate marketing programs, including the Amazon Associates Program, Cuelinks, and verified merchant partner programs.

As an Amazon Associate, TIVRA News earns from qualifying purchases. Some of our gadget, electronics, lifestyle, and deal articles also include affiliate links through Cuelinks and official merchant networks (such as Amazon, Flipkart, Reliance Digital, Croma, Ajio, and Tata CLiQ). If you buy a product or sign up for a service after clicking one of these links, we may receive a small commission or referral compensation at no extra cost to you.

How we mark commercial and affiliate links

Every commercial link on this site is explicitly designated as a (paid link) or partner referral box and carries search-engine compliant attributes (rel="nofollow sponsored noopener noreferrer") so users and search engines clearly understand the commercial nature of the link.

Editorial Independence

  • Content selection is driven strictly by editorial news value, consumer utility, and factual product announcements—never by commission rates.
  • Comparisons, reviews, and specifications are never skewed or reordered to favor a higher-paying affiliate partner.
  • We maintain strict separation between our editorial news coverage and commercial deal widgets.

About prices and availability

We do not display live prices or stock levels. Any price mentioned in an article reflects what was reported at the time of writing and can change at any time. The price and availability shown on Amazon at the moment of purchase are the ones that apply.

Accuracy and corrections

Our team carefully curates gadget articles, but specifications can occasionally contain errors. Where a specification could not be confirmed, we leave it blank rather than guess. Please verify any detail that matters to your buying decision on the manufacturer's own listing before purchasing, and see our Editorial Policy for how we handle corrections. If you spot a mistake, tell us via the Contact page.
