Sandeep Reddy Vanga's Brother Alleges Deepika Padukone Leaked Spirit Details

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pranay Reddy Vanga alleges Deepika Padukone demanded a 10% share of the film's profits.
  • Reports suggest the actor was dropped from the project following allegations of leaking script details.
  • Clarifications were issued regarding actor Don Lee's involvement in the production.

Allegations Surface Over Spirit Casting

The upcoming film Spirit, directed by Sandeep Reddy Vanga, has become the center of a fresh controversy. Pranay Reddy Vanga, the director's brother, has publicly addressed the reasons behind the reported exit of Deepika Padukone from the project.

According to statements made by Pranay, the decision to part ways with the lead actor was not based on a single factor. Instead, he cited a combination of professional disagreements and internal concerns regarding the management of sensitive project information.

Profit Sharing Demands and Security Breaches

The primary point of contention appears to be the financial terms discussed during the pre-production phase. Pranay alleged that Padukone demanded a 10% share of the film's total profits, a condition that the production team found unsustainable.

Beyond financial expectations, more serious allegations have emerged regarding the integrity of the production. Pranay claimed that details concerning the plot and specific scenes of Spirit were leaked to external parties. These unauthorized disclosures reportedly compromised the production's security protocols.

Addressing Casting Rumors

The discourse surrounding Spirit has also touched upon international casting rumors. For several months, social media speculation linked South Korean actor Don Lee to the film.

Pranay Reddy Vanga explicitly addressed these rumors to manage public expectations. He confirmed that Don Lee is not part of the cast for Spirit. By clarifying this, the production house aims to shift the focus back to the official development phase of the project.

Production Status and Industry Impact

The film has generated significant anticipation due to Sandeep Reddy Vanga’s previous directorial successes. However, high-profile casting changes often invite public scrutiny and debate within the Indian film industry.

  • The demand for a 10% profit share was cited as a major hurdle.
  • Leaked script details allegedly led to a breakdown in professional trust.
  • The production team maintains that official updates will be provided in due course.

Industry analysts are now waiting to see how these developments affect the production timeline. Observers will monitor the official announcement of the final cast as the team moves forward with filming.
