Real-World Performance & Daily Use

The NYX Buttermelt Blush has garnered a cult following for its intensely pigmented, buttery formula that blends seamlessly into the skin. In real-world use, a little goes a long way—a single tap of the brush picks up enough product for one cheek, so the 6.5g pan will last months even with daily application. The texture is finely milled, avoiding the chalkiness or patchiness often found in drugstore blushes. It sits comfortably on the skin without emphasizing texture or pores, making it suitable for both dry and oily skin types. The finish is predominantly matte with a subtle satin sheen that mimics a natural flush. Wear time is impressive: on normal to dry skin, it lasts 7–8 hours before fading, while oily skin may see slight fading after 5–6 hours, especially in humid conditions. The packaging is sleek and compact, with a clear lid that makes shade identification easy. However, the compact does not include a mirror or brush, which is typical for the price point. The blush is buildable, allowing for a sheer wash or a bold pop of color. It layers well over foundation and powder without disturbing the base. One minor drawback is that the formula can kick up a bit of powder when tapped, so a light hand is recommended. Overall, the Buttermelt Blush performs like a high-end product at a fraction of the cost.

Competition & Market Alternatives

In the same price tier, the NYX Buttermelt Blush competes with the Maybelline Fit Me Blush (₹500–₹600) and the L'Oréal Paris True Match Blush (₹700–₹800). The Maybelline Fit Me offers a natural, sheer finish but lacks the intense pigmentation and longevity of the Buttermelt. The L'Oréal True Match provides a similar satin finish but has a drier texture that can be harder to blend. The NYX Buttermelt stands out for its buttery softness and high impact, making it a favorite among makeup enthusiasts. Compared to high-end blushes like NARS (₹2,500+), the Buttermelt delivers comparable pigment and wear at a fraction of the price. However, if you prefer a cream or liquid blush, the NYX Sweet Cheeks Creamy Powder Blush (₹700) is a good alternative, though it offers a different finish. For those on a tighter budget, the Swiss Beauty Blush (₹300) is a decent option but lacks the same blendability and shade range. Overall, the Buttermelt Blush is the best-in-class for its price, especially with the current discount.
