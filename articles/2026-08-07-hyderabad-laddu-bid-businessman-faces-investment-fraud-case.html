Hyderabad: Laddu Bid Businessman Faces Investment Fraud Case

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hyderabad police have registered an investment fraud case against a prominent businessman.
  • The individual was previously in the news for bidding a record amount for a Ganesh Laddu.
  • The case pertains to alleged irregularities in investment schemes, with no official comments yet.

Case Registered Against Prominent Bidder

Hyderabad police have registered a case of investment fraud against a city-based businessman who made headlines last year for his record-breaking bid for a Ganesh Laddu. The development comes after multiple complaints were filed by investors alleging irregularities in the businessman's investment schemes.

According to sources, the case was registered at a local police station on Thursday night. The complainant has accused the businessman of failing to return principal amounts and promised returns on investments, leaving several investors in the lurch. Police have not yet named the accused formally in a chargesheet, and officials have not confirmed the exact number of complainants or the total amount involved.

From Laddu Glory to Legal Trouble

The businessman shot to public attention in 2025 when he bid a staggering sum for a Ganesh Laddu at a prominent temple in Hyderabad, a gesture widely covered by local media. That act of devotion made him a household name in the city, but the current fraud case paints a different picture.

Details of the alleged fraud are still emerging. Initial reports suggest that the businessman collected money from individuals and small-scale investors under the promise of high returns, reportedly through chit-fund-like arrangements. When investors began seeking their money, repayments allegedly stalled, triggering the complaints.

Investors Left in the Dark

Several investors have reportedly approached the police over the last few weeks, but the case was formally registered only recently. A senior police officer, speaking on condition of anonymity, said that the investigation is at a preliminary stage and the businessman's financial records are being scrutinised.

“We are looking into the transactions and the trail of funds. Nothing can be said conclusively until the verification is complete,” the officer said. The businessman has not yet issued a public statement, and attempts to reach his office for comment were unsuccessful.

Legal Proceedings Ahead

Legal experts note that cases of this nature often involve multiple hearings and detailed forensic audits. The police are expected to summon the businessman for questioning in the coming days. If the allegations are proven, he could face charges under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code relating to cheating and criminal breach of trust.

The development has surprised many in Hyderabad, where the businessman is still remembered for his grand temple gesture. However, police have urged the public to treat the case on its merits and not jump to conclusions before the investigation concludes.

What to Watch

The next step will be the businessman's appearance before the investigating officer, which is likely to happen soon. His financial disclosures and the statements of investors will be critical in determining whether the case holds ground. For now, the man once celebrated for his generosity faces an uncertain legal journey.
