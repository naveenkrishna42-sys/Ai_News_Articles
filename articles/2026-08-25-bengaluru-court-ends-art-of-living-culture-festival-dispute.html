Bengaluru court ends Art of Living culture festival dispute

Supreme Court resolves Bengaluru cultural festival dispute

The Supreme Court of India has delivered a landmark verdict in the ongoing dispute over the Art of Living's annual culture festival in Bengaluru. The apex court has upheld the Karnataka government's decision to grant permits for the festival, effectively ending a months-long legal battle.

Background of the dispute

The row began when the Art of Living Foundation applied for permits to organize its 12-day culture festival in Bengaluru. The state government, however, denied the permits citing concerns over the festival's scale and potential impact on the city's infrastructure. The foundation then approached the high court, which subsequently referred the matter to the Supreme Court.

Key arguments and court's reasoning

The Supreme Court heard arguments from both sides, with the Art of Living Foundation contending that the festival promotes cultural exchange and spiritual values, while the state government argued that the festival's scale posed significant challenges to city management. The apex court, in its verdict, noted that the state government's decision to grant permits was based on a proper assessment of the festival's impact on Bengaluru's infrastructure and public services.

Broader implications

The Supreme Court's verdict has significant implications for the cultural and event management landscape in Karnataka. It sets a precedent for future cultural festival permit applications, emphasizing the need for a balanced approach that considers both the cultural value and the practical challenges posed by such events. The verdict also highlights the importance of transparent and accountable decision-making in the management of public spaces and services.

Future outlook

With the dispute resolved, the Art of Living Foundation can now proceed with its annual culture festival in Bengaluru. The festival, which is expected to attract thousands of visitors, will not only showcase the rich cultural heritage of India but also provide a platform for cultural exchange and understanding. The event is likely to boost tourism and cultural tourism in the city, contributing to its economic growth and development.
