TNHB's Rs 11K offer sparks land dispute in Chennai's IT corridor

📅 2026-08-27 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • TNHB offers Rs 11K for land in Chennai's IT corridor
  • Landowners demand land back, citing property rights
  • Dispute highlights challenges in urban land management

TNHB's Rs 11K Offer

Tamil Nadu Housing Board (TNHB) has offered Rs 11,000 for a piece of land located in Chennai's IT corridor. The land, spanning 100 square meters, is currently owned by a local resident. The offer comes as part of the board's efforts to acquire land for affordable housing projects in the area.

Landowners' Demand

The landowners, who have lived on the property for over 20 years, are demanding that the land be returned to them. They argue that the offer is significantly lower than the market value of the land and that it does not reflect the true worth of their property.

Legal experts suggest that the landowners may have a strong case, as the offer does not provide adequate compensation for the land. They advise the landowners to consult with a lawyer to understand their rights and options.

Broader Implications

The dispute highlights the challenges faced by landowners in urban areas, where land is often acquired for development projects. It also raises questions about the fairness of the compensation offered by the government.

Urban planners argue that such land acquisitions are necessary for the development of the city. However, they also acknowledge that there is a need for a more transparent and fair process for compensating landowners.
