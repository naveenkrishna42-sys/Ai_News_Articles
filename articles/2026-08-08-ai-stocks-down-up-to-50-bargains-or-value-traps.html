AI stocks down up to 50%: Bargains or value traps?

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Several AI stocks have dropped up to 50% from their peaks, raising questions about their true value.
  • Analysts are split: some see long-term growth potential, others warn of overvaluation and weak fundamentals.
  • Kellton Tech Solutions faces bearish momentum after a technical downgrade, while a microcap AI stock trades near its 52-week low.

Sharp Declines Spark Debate

The artificial intelligence rally that once seemed unstoppable has hit a rough patch. Several AI-focused stocks have fallen by as much as 50% from their highs, leaving investors to question whether these are rare buying opportunities or traps that could sink further.

Market watchers point to a mix of factors behind the sell-off: profit-taking after a long run, concerns about sky-high valuations, and growing caution about how quickly AI adoption will translate into earnings. The result is a polarised market where optimism meets deep scepticism.

The Case for Bargains

For some analysts, the sharp correction has created compelling entry points. They argue that the long-term demand for AI infrastructure, software, and services remains intact, and that companies with solid balance sheets and real revenue growth are now trading at more reasonable multiples.

One notable example is Kellton Tech Solutions Ltd, which has seen its share price slide amid bearish momentum. A recent technical downgrade by MarketsMojo has added to the negative sentiment, but some investors believe the company's fundamentals may be stronger than the market suggests.

The Value Trap Warning

Others caution that a falling stock price does not automatically mean a stock is cheap. Classic value traps occur when a company's decline is driven by deteriorating business fundamentals, not just market sentiment.

In the AI sector, this risk is amplified by intense competition, high research costs, and the uncertainty around which technologies will ultimately dominate. A stock trading near its 52-week low, such as a hidden microcap AI stock highlighted by Trade Brains, could be a sign of trouble rather than an opportunity.

Technical Signals and Investor Sentiment

Technical indicators are also flashing warnings. Kellton Tech's downgrade reflects bearish momentum, suggesting that selling pressure may not yet be exhausted. Similarly, the broader AI segment has seen volatility as traders react to news about falling AI prices—one report notes a 280-fold drop in certain AI costs, which could squeeze margins for firms that rely on proprietary models.

Investors are now torn between catching falling knives and waiting for clearer signs of a bottom. The divergence in opinions is evident in the range of headlines, from 'Hidden Bargains' to 'Classic Value Traps'—the same stock can be seen as both, depending on one's horizon and risk appetite.

What to Watch Next

As earnings season approaches, the key will be whether these beaten-down companies can show tangible progress in revenue and profitability. A miss could confirm the value-trap thesis, while a beat might reignite interest.

For now, the advice from many market veterans is to focus on fundamentals rather than price alone. The AI story is far from over, but the days of indiscriminate buying are likely behind us.
