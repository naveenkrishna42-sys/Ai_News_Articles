Uttarakhand on High Alert as Heavy Rain Warning Issued for 24 Hours

📅 2026-08-06 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Heavy rain warning issued for Uttarakhand for next 24 hours
  • Landslides have cut off villages and damaged a government residential unit
  • Schools and Anganwadi centres closed in Dehradun as precaution
  • Waterlogging reported in Doon after heavy afternoon shower, later cleared

State on Edge as Met Issues Warning

Uttarakhand has been put on high alert after the meteorological department issued a heavy rainfall warning for the next 24 hours. The alert covers several districts, with authorities bracing for potential flash floods, landslides, and disruption to daily life. The state administration has asked district magistrates to remain vigilant and coordinate relief efforts.

Heavy rains have already begun to take a toll. Landslides triggered by the downpour have cut off several villages, leaving residents stranded and hampering access to essential supplies. In one incident, a boulder crashed into a government residential unit, causing damage but no immediate reports of casualties. Officials have not yet confirmed the full extent of the damage.

Schools Shut, Daily Life Disrupted

In Dehradun, schools and Anganwadi centres will remain closed today as a precautionary measure. The district administration announced the closure late last night, citing safety concerns for students and staff. Parents have been advised to keep children indoors and avoid unnecessary travel.

The downpour also led to waterlogging in several parts of Doon city after a heavy afternoon shower. Roads were temporarily submerged, causing traffic snarls and inconvenience to commuters. However, as the rain eased, the water receded and roads were cleared soon after, according to local reports.

Landslides and Infrastructure Concerns

The hilly terrain of Uttarakhand remains particularly vulnerable during such weather events. Landslides have been reported in multiple locations, with debris blocking roads and disrupting connectivity. The boulder crash into the government residential unit underscores the risk posed to infrastructure in landslide-prone areas.

State authorities are working to clear blocked routes and restore normalcy. The State Disaster Response Force (SDRF) and local teams have been deployed to assist in rescue and relief operations. Villages that have been cut off are being prioritised, though officials have not yet provided a timeline for when connectivity will be fully restored.

Weather Forecast and Preparedness

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has predicted continued heavy rainfall over the next 24 hours, with isolated very heavy showers possible in some areas. Residents in low-lying and landslide-prone regions have been advised to stay alert and follow safety guidelines.

Local authorities have set up control rooms to monitor the situation and respond to emergencies. People have been urged to avoid venturing near rivers, streams, and slopes during the alert period. The administration is also keeping a close watch on river levels to prevent any flood-like situation.

Looking Ahead

With the rain warning in force, the next 24 hours remain critical for Uttarakhand. Officials have said that the situation will be reviewed as the weather evolves, and further precautionary measures may be taken if needed. For now, the focus remains on ensuring safety and minimising the impact of the downpour on vulnerable communities.

As the state hunkers down, all eyes will be on the weather update due later today. The administration has assured that timely action will be taken to address any emerging challenges, but residents have been warned to remain cautious until the alert is lifted.
