Surrey Groundsman Placed on Leave After ECB Row

Lee Fortis, the head groundsman at Surrey County Cricket Club, has been placed on leave after a fresh controversy involving an England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB) employee. Fortis, who previously clashed with former Indian cricketer Gautam Gambhir, is now embroiled in a new row that has led to his suspension.

Background: Previous Clash with Gambhir

Fortis first came to public attention when he had a heated exchange with Gambhir during a match. The incident was widely reported and sparked a debate about the behavior of groundsmen in cricket. Fortis has since been a subject of interest and controversy, with his actions often drawing scrutiny.

Fresh Controversy with ECB Employee

The latest controversy involves Fortis and an ECB employee. The exact nature of the dispute is not clear, but it has reportedly upset the ECB, leading to Fortis being placed on leave. The ECB has not provided detailed information about the incident, but it is clear that the dispute has had serious consequences for Fortis.

Impact on Surrey County Cricket Club

The placement of Fortis on leave is a significant development for Surrey County Cricket Club. Fortis has been a key figure in the club's ground operations, and his suspension will have a notable impact on the team's preparations for upcoming matches. The club has not commented on the incident, but it is likely that the situation will be closely watched by both the club and the ECB.
