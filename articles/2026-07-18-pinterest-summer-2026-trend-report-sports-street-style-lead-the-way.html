Pinterest Summer 2026 Trend Report: Sports, street style lead the way

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Lifestyle Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sporty street style dominates with blends of athletic and casual wear.
  • Bold colors and oversized silhouettes are key trends for the season.
  • Practical fashion like utility vests and cargo pants sees a surge in interest.

Pinterest unveils Summer 2026 trends

Pinterest has released its Summer 2026 Trend Report, offering a glimpse into what users across the globe are planning to wear. The report, based on search data from the platform’s millions of users, points to a clear direction: sporty street style is taking over.

The findings show a major shift towards blending athletic wear with everyday fashion. Searches for 'sporty street style outfits' have climbed by 340% year-on-year. Users are looking for ways to combine comfort with bold looks.

Sporty meets street

The line between gym wear and casual dressing is blurring. Searches for 'track pants with heels' are up 210%. 'Bike shorts with blazers' has seen a 180% increase. People want clothes that move with them but still look put-together.

Footwear trends echo this. Chunky sneakers remain popular, but 'slip-on sport sandals' are seeing a 150% rise in searches. The focus is on practicality without sacrificing style.

Bold colours and oversized fits

Neon and electric shades are making a strong comeback. Searches for 'neon green outfits' and 'electric blue streetwear' have jumped by 290% and 260% respectively. The palette is unapologetically bright.

Oversized silhouettes are another key theme. 'Oversized cargo pants' and 'baggy denim jackets' are trending. The relaxed fit is not just about comfort; it is a statement. Users want volume and freedom in their clothing.

Practical fashion in focus

Utility is at the heart of many searches. 'Utility vests' and 'cargo skirts' have seen a 220% spike. Pockets, straps, and multi-functional pieces are in demand. The look is functional but fashionable.

Accessories are also getting the utility treatment. 'Belt bags' and 'crossbody phone cases' are popular. People want to keep their hands free while staying on trend.

Layering remains important. 'Mesh tops under t-shirts' and 'sheer dresses over denim' are trending searches. The idea is to mix textures and create depth in outfits.

The report also highlights a growing interest in sustainable fashion. Searches for 'thrifted sportswear' and 'upcycled streetwear' have increased by 180% and 160% respectively. Users are looking for ways to build a sporty wardrobe without buying new.

Gen Z and millennial users are driving these trends. They are using Pinterest to plan outfits that blend nostalgia with modern twists. 90s and early 2000s influences are clear in the data.

Pinterest’s trend report is based on search behaviour from its community. The platform notes that these trends are early signals of what will be seen on the streets and in stores this summer.

As summer 2026 approaches, retailers and designers will be watching these trends closely. The message from users is clear: sporty, bold, and practical fashion is the way forward.
