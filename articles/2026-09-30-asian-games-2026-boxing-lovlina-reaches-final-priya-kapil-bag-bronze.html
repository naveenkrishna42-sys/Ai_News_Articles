Lovlina Borgohain Sets Sights on Asian Games Gold

Olympic medallist Lovlina Borgohain delivered a commanding tactical performance to advance to the women's 75kg final at the Asian Games 2026. Facing stiff continental opposition, the seasoned Indian pugilist controlled the pace from the opening exchange, relying on her superior reach and calculated counter-punching. The victory guarantees Borgohain at least a silver medal and places her in prime contention to script history with a continental gold.

Borgohain's semi-final victory highlighted her technical maturity under pressure. By controlling distance with stiff lead jabs and maintaining tight defensive guards against incoming hooks, she blunted her opponent's aggression across all three rounds. The judges awarded her a decisive split-to-unanimous margin, confirming her trajectory toward the tournament's top podium spot.

Podium Finishes: Priya Ghanghas and Kapil Pokhariya Secure Bronze

While Borgohain pushed forward into the gold medal match, young compatriots Priya Ghanghas and Kapil Pokhariya concluded their respective campaigns with bronze medals. Both boxers reached the semi-final stage after navigating challenging preliminary brackets, showing remarkable poise against seasoned regional contenders.

Priya Ghanghas displayed relentless offensive drive in her semi-final contest. Facing an aggressive counter-puncher, Priya initiated high-tempo combinations and forced her opponent against the ropes. Despite absorbing clean combinations in the second round, she maintained consistent forward pressure. Although the final decision narrowly favored her rival, the performance secured an invaluable international bronze medal for the young prodigy.

In the men's division, Kapil Pokhariya produced a spirited effort in his penultimate bout. Confronting a technically sharp opponent known for lateral footwork, Kapil relied on body work and power hooks to disrupt his opponent's rhythm. While points edged away in the closing exchanges, Pokhariya's podium finish reinforces the depth of talent emerging from the national camp.
