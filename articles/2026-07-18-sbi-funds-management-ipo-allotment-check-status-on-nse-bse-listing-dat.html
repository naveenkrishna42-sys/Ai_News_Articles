SBI Funds Management IPO allotment: Check status on NSE, BSE; listing date here

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • SBI Funds Management IPO allotment status can be checked on NSE, BSE, and KFin Technologies websites.
  • The IPO was subscribed 41.66 times, with QIBs leading at 140.11 times.
  • Total bids worth Rs 3 lakh crore were received, making it India's fourth-most-bid IPO.
  • Listing date and time are yet to be officially announced by the exchanges.

Investors who applied for the SBI Funds Management Initial Public Offering (IPO) can now check the allotment status online. The allotment process has been completed, and the basis of allotment is expected to be finalised shortly. Those who bid for the shares can verify their allotment status on the websites of the National Stock Exchange (NSE), BSE, and the registrar, KFin Technologies.

How to check SBI Funds Management IPO allotment status

To check the allotment status on the NSE, visit the NSE website and navigate to the 'IPO Allotment Status' section. Select 'SBI Funds Management' from the dropdown menu and enter your application number or PAN. The system will display whether you have been allotted shares.

On the BSE website, go to the 'Investors' section and click on 'IPO Allotment Status'. Choose the issue name, enter your PAN or application number, and submit. The status will appear on the screen.

Alternatively, you can check directly on KFin Technologies' portal. Visit their IPO allotment page, select the SBI Funds Management issue, and provide your PAN or application number. The registrar will show the allotment details.

Subscription and demand details

The SBI Funds Management IPO witnessed exceptional demand. The issue was subscribed 41.66 times overall. Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) led the charge with a subscription of 140.11 times their reserved portion. Non-Institutional Investors also showed strong interest, subscribing 30 times the shares set aside for them.

Retail investors subscribed 6.5 times the portion reserved for them. The total bids received amounted to approximately Rs 3 lakh crore, making this the fourth-most-bid IPO in Indian history. The strong response reflects investor confidence in the asset management company's market position and growth prospects.

Listing date and time

The listing date for SBI Funds Management shares has not been officially announced yet. Typically, shares are listed on the stock exchanges within three to seven working days after the allotment is finalised. The company is expected to list on both the BSE and NSE.

Exchanges will announce the exact listing date and time once the allotment process is complete. Investors should check the official websites of the exchanges or the company for updates. The listing day will see the shares begin trading in the cash market segment.

Key details of the IPO

  • Total issue size: The IPO raised significant capital, though the exact amount has not been specified.
  • Price band: The price band was set at a range that attracted strong demand from all investor categories.
  • Lead managers: The issue was managed by leading investment banks.
  • Registrar: KFin Technologies is handling the allotment and refund process.

The SBI Funds Management IPO's success underscores the robust interest in India's asset management sector. The company manages a large portfolio of mutual funds and is one of the largest asset managers in the country.

Investors who have not yet checked their allotment status can do so now. Those who have been allotted shares should ensure their demat accounts are active to receive the shares. For those who did not receive allotment, refunds will be processed by the registrar.

The listing will be closely watched by market participants. The stock's performance on the debut day will indicate the market's valuation of the company and the overall sentiment for new-age financial services firms.
