Hyderabad school fires teacher over religious homework for Class 2 student

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Teacher at a private school in Hyderabad sacked for assigning religious homework to a Class 2 student.
  • Homework required the child to write a phrase from a specific religion multiple times.
  • School management acted after parents complained; teacher's identity not disclosed.

Hyderabad: A private school in the city has terminated a teacher after a Class 2 student was allegedly given homework that required writing a religious phrase. The incident has sparked a debate about the boundaries of religious instruction in secular educational institutions.

School officials confirmed that the teacher was asked to leave with immediate effect after parents of the seven-year-old student complained to the management. The homework, which involved writing a phrase from a specific religion repeatedly, was deemed inappropriate for a child of that age in a school setting.

What the homework contained

According to sources familiar with the matter, the worksheet instructed the student to write the phrase 'Allah is the only God' 10 times. The child's parents, who follow a different faith, were taken aback when they saw the assignment brought home from school.

They approached the school principal the next morning, expressing their concern that such an exercise violated the secular ethos of the school and could confuse young children. The parents did not demand the teacher's dismissal but wanted the school to ensure no such homework was given again.

School's response

The school management acted swiftly. Within 48 hours of receiving the complaint, the teacher was called for a meeting and subsequently issued a termination letter. A school spokesperson said, "We have a zero-tolerance policy regarding the imposition of any religious beliefs on students. The teacher's actions were a clear violation of our code of conduct."

The school also issued a circular to all staff, reiterating that homework and classroom activities must remain strictly secular. Teachers have been reminded not to introduce religious content unless it is part of the prescribed curriculum for comparative religion studies at higher grades.

Teacher's side

The teacher, whose name has not been disclosed, is believed to have argued that the homework was intended as a cultural or moral lesson rather than a religious one. However, the school management did not accept this explanation.

Local teacher unions have expressed concern over the dismissal, with some calling it disproportionate. One union leader said, "A warning or a transfer might have been more appropriate. Sacking a teacher over one worksheet sets a harsh precedent." The school has not responded to this criticism.

Broader context

This incident comes amid periodic debates in India about the role of religion in schools. While the Constitution guarantees secular education, private schools often have varying policies. In Hyderabad, a city with a diverse population, schools typically maintain strict neutrality in religious matters.

Experts in education law note that teachers can face disciplinary action for violating school policies, but the severity depends on the institution's rules. There is no central law that specifically bans religious homework, but most schools include such prohibitions in their employee handbooks.

The school has not confirmed whether the teacher will appeal the decision. Meanwhile, the student has been moved to another section and is attending classes normally. The management has also offered counselling if needed, though the parents have not requested it.

What happens next: The teacher is expected to approach the school management for a review of the termination. If that fails, legal recourse or intervention by the state education department remains a possibility.
