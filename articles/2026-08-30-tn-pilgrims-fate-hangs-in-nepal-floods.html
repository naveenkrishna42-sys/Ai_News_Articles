TN pilgrims' fate hangs in Nepal floods

📅 2026-08-30 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • 100 Tamil Nadu pilgrims missing in Nepal floods
  • 21 rescued, 49 feared on China side
  • Sarees saved lives of 21 rescued pilgrims
  • Minister Rajmohan calls for continued search efforts

Tamil Nadu pilgrims' disappearance raises concerns

As the monsoon rains continue to wreak havoc in Nepal, the disappearance of 100 Tamil Nadu pilgrims has raised alarm among the state's administration and the public. The missing pilgrims, who were part of a group traveling to the Muktinath Temple, are believed to have been caught in the floods that have affected the region. The Tamil Nadu government has urged the Nepalese authorities to expedite the search and rescue operations.

Rescue efforts underway

Despite the challenges posed by the ongoing floods, rescue efforts are underway. A team of Tamil Nadu officials, including the state's home minister Rajmohan, has been in Nepal to coordinate with the local authorities. So far, 21 pilgrims have been rescued and are safe. They were able to cling on to their saarees, which became a lifeline in the floods. The minister has expressed his gratitude to the Nepalese authorities for their assistance in the rescue operations.

Concerns over missing pilgrims

The disappearance of 49 pilgrims, who were on the China side of the border, has raised concerns. The Chennai-based tour operator, who organized the trip, has confirmed that the group was on the China side when the disaster struck. The tour operator has urged the Tamil Nadu government to take immediate action to locate the missing pilgrims. The state government has promised to provide all necessary assistance to the families of the missing pilgrims.

Impact on Tamil Nadu

The disappearance of the pilgrims has had a significant impact on Tamil Nadu. The state government has been criticized for not being more proactive in ensuring the safety of the pilgrims. The families of the missing pilgrims are understandably anxious and have been demanding answers from the authorities. The incident has also highlighted the need for better coordination between the Indian and Nepalese authorities in times of crisis.

Broader implications

The incident has broader implications for the region. The floods in Nepal have affected thousands of people, and the disappearance of the Tamil Nadu pilgrims has added to the concerns. The incident has also raised questions about the safety of pilgrims traveling to Nepal. The Tamil Nadu government has been urged to review its policies and procedures for pilgrimage tours to ensure the safety of the pilgrims.
