Apple September 2026 event: iPhone 18, watches, AirPods expected

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple's September 2026 event is expected to unveil the iPhone 18 series, including Pro models and possibly a foldable iPhone.
  • New Apple Watch models and AirPods are also anticipated at the event, with five devices likely to launch.
  • Analysts forecast a potential price increase for iPhone 18 Pro in India by up to Rs 27,000.
  • The event is expected to take place next month, with several new products beyond the iPhone.

Apple's September 2026 event: What's on the horizon

Apple is gearing up for its traditional September event, and this year's lineup appears more packed than usual. According to reports from Notebookcheck and other outlets, the company is expected to unveil the iPhone 18 series, new Apple Watches, AirPods, and possibly a foldable iPhone. The event is likely to take place next month, though Apple has not yet confirmed the exact date.

Industry watchers are predicting a significant overhaul of the iPhone lineup, with the standard iPhone 18 and iPhone 18 Pro models expected to lead the charge. A foldable iPhone has been the subject of speculation for years, and multiple reports suggest it could finally make its debut at this event. If true, this would mark Apple's first major form-factor change since the iPhone X.

iPhone 18 Pro price in India: A possible hike

One of the most talked-about aspects of the upcoming launch is the pricing. Moneycontrol reports that the iPhone 18 Pro price in India may increase by up to Rs 27,000, according to a revised forecast by an analyst. This would be a substantial jump from the current generation, potentially pushing the Pro model into a new price bracket.

The price hike, if it materialises, could be attributed to rising component costs, advanced chip manufacturing, and the inclusion of new features like a larger display or improved camera system. Apple has not commented on the reports, and the final pricing will only be confirmed at the event. For Indian consumers, this could mean a tough decision between upgrading to the latest Pro model or sticking with older versions.

Six new products to watch

The Indian Express has highlighted six new Apple products to keep an eye out for. Apart from the iPhone 18 series, the list includes next-generation Apple Watches, which are expected to come in multiple variants, and new AirPods models. There are also rumours of an updated iPad or MacBook, though these are less certain.

Reports suggest that five devices are likely to launch next month, according to Deccan Herald. This aligns with Apple's recent strategy of releasing multiple products simultaneously to maximise impact. The Apple Watch, in particular, is expected to see a design refresh, possibly with a thinner profile and new health sensors.

Foldable iPhone: The big question

The possibility of a foldable iPhone has generated the most buzz. Deccan Chronicle reports that Apple could launch the iPhone 18 Pro alongside a foldable iPhone, with the latter possibly being announced as a separate product line. If launched, it would compete directly with Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold series and other foldables in the market.

However, details about the foldable's specifications remain scarce. Analysts are divided on whether Apple will release it this year or wait until 2027. What is clear is that Apple has been working on foldable display technology for years, and a launch in 2026 would not be a surprise.

What to expect next

Apple is expected to send out official invitations for the September event in the coming weeks. The exact date and product lineup will be confirmed then. For now, consumers and analysts alike are watching closely, with the iPhone 18 Pro's pricing and the foldable's debut being the key storylines to follow.
