NEET UG Re-Exam Results Out

NEET UG Re-Exam Results Announced

The National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) UG re-exam results are out, with 11.21 lakh candidates qualifying for admission to undergraduate medical courses. The results were declared recently, bringing relief to students who appeared for the re-exam. Women have outshone men in the results, with a higher number of female candidates qualifying.

Top Performers

Punjab's Aryan Gupta and Haryana's Panshul Bansal have emerged as the toppers in the NEET UG re-exam. The two students have secured the top ranks in the exam, followed by other candidates from different parts of the country. The results have also seen a strong performance from students from Telangana, with eight students from the state featuring in the top 138.

Telangana's Sahyu has secured an All India Rank (AIR) of 13, highlighting the state's dominance in the exam. The strong performance of students from Telangana is a testament to the state's thriving education system and the hard work put in by the students.

Results and Rankings

Candidates can check their results online by visiting the official website of the National Testing Agency (NTA). The website provides a direct link to check the results, and candidates can access their scorecards by logging in with their registration number and password. The results have been declared for all candidates who appeared for the re-exam, and the rankings have been prepared based on the scores obtained by the candidates.

What's Next?

Now that the results are out, candidates who have qualified will be eligible to participate in the counselling process for admission to undergraduate medical courses. The counselling process is expected to begin soon, and candidates will be able to apply for seats in medical colleges across the country. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact dates for the counselling process, but candidates are advised to keep an eye on the official website for updates.
