Man hacked to death in public view in Hyderabad's Saidabad

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Incident occurred in Saidabad, Hyderabad, in full public view.
  • Victim died on the spot after being attacked with sharp weapons.
  • Police have registered a case and are investigating the motive.

Hyderabad: In a brazen daylight attack, a man was hacked to death in full public view in the Saidabad area on Tuesday morning. The incident has sent shockwaves through the locality, with residents grappling with the violence that unfolded on their streets.

Police said the victim, whose identity has not been officially released yet, was attacked with sharp weapons by a group of assailants. The attack was so swift and brutal that the man succumbed to his injuries on the spot, before any help could reach him.

Attack in a crowded area

The assault took place in a busy part of Saidabad, a densely populated neighbourhood in the Old City. Eyewitnesses described a scene of chaos, with people scattering in panic as the attackers struck. Despite the presence of passers-by, no one could intervene in time to save the victim.

Local residents said such an audacious act of violence in broad daylight was unprecedented for the area. "We have seen petty fights before, but this was something else entirely. They came prepared and left no chance," said a shopkeeper who witnessed the incident from a distance.

Police investigation underway

Following the attack, personnel from the Saidabad police station rushed to the spot. The body was recovered and sent for post-mortem examination. A case of murder has been registered, and teams have been formed to trace the attackers.

Preliminary inquiries suggest the attack may have been the result of an old rivalry, but police have not confirmed any motive yet. Officials said they are scanning CCTV footage from nearby establishments and questioning witnesses to piece together the sequence of events.

"We are investigating all angles. The accused will be identified and arrested soon," a senior police officer said, requesting anonymity as the investigation is ongoing.

Residents demand action

The brazen nature of the killing has sparked concern among local residents, who are demanding better policing and quicker action. Many have expressed fear that such incidents could become more frequent if the perpetrators are not brought to justice swiftly.

Community leaders have urged the police to increase patrolling in the area, especially during peak hours. "People are scared to step out. We need assurance that our streets are safe," said a resident welfare association member.

Police have assured the public that additional forces will be deployed in the area as a precautionary measure. They have also appealed to anyone with information about the attack to come forward.

As the investigation progresses, all eyes are on the police to deliver a breakthrough. The coming days will reveal whether this was a targeted killing or a symptom of deeper unrest in the locality.
