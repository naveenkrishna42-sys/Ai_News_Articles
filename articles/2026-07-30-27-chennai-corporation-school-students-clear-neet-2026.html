27 Chennai Corporation School Students Clear NEET 2026

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • 27 students from Chennai Corporation-run schools cleared NEET 2026.
  • The results were announced on Wednesday by the National Testing Agency.
  • Officials say this is the highest number of successful candidates from the civic body's schools in recent years.

Breakthrough for Civic School Students

Twenty-seven students from Chennai Corporation-run schools have cleared the National Eligibility-cum-Entrance Test (NEET) this year, officials said on Thursday. The results, announced by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on Wednesday, mark a significant achievement for the city's public school system.

This is the highest number of successful candidates from Corporation schools in recent memory. Last year, 18 students had qualified. The steady rise points to focused academic interventions by the civic body.

How They Prepared

Corporation officials attributed the success to a dedicated bridge course and coaching programme. Students from Classes 11 and 12 were given extra classes in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology after school hours.

Teachers identified potential medical aspirants early and provided them with study material and mock tests. The civic body also tied up with NGOs to offer free online coaching modules.

Students Speak

Many of the successful candidates come from modest backgrounds. A student from a school in North Chennai said she studied for four hours daily after regular classes. Another aspirant, whose father is an auto-rickshaw driver, credited his teachers for staying back late to clear doubts.

The students now face the counselling process, which begins next month. They will compete for seats in government medical colleges across Tamil Nadu.

What This Means for the System

Chennai Corporation runs over 250 schools, mostly in underserved neighbourhoods. The NEET results are seen as a validation of recent reforms, including teacher training and lab upgrades.

Education activists caution that one good year does not solve systemic problems. Infrastructure gaps and teacher vacancies remain challenges. But officials hope the momentum will attract more students to the science stream.

The state government has promised to increase funding for Corporation schools. A new science block is planned at three high-performing campuses next year.

Looking Ahead

The counselling process for NEET 2026 will determine where these 27 students finally end up. For now, the Corporation school system is celebrating a rare moment of recognition on a national stage.
