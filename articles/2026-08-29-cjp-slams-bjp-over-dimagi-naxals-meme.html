CJP Criticizes BJP's 'Dimagi Naxals' Meme

Chief Justice of India (CJP) has slammed the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) for its 'Dimagi Naxals' meme featuring a scene from the Kannada film 'KGF'. The meme, which depicts a group of people resembling Naxalites, has sparked controversy for its perceived call for mass genocide.

BJP's Satirical Social Media Battle

The BJP's use of satire in the social media battle against the Congress has been a subject of debate. While the party has been known for its use of humor in political campaigns, the 'Dimagi Naxals' meme has been criticized for its potential to incite violence.

RSS Weekly Expands 'Dimagi Naxal' Label

The Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) weekly has expanded the 'dimagi Naxal' label to include NGOs. This move has been seen as an attempt to broaden the base of the party's political opposition.

CJP's Comments Highlight Need for Responsible Use of Satire

CJP's comments on the 'Dimagi Naxals' meme highlight the need for responsible use of satire in political campaigns. The Chief Justice has called on the political parties to ensure that their use of humor does not cross the line into incitement.
