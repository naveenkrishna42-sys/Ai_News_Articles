Pros
  • Delivers smartwatch-level features like heart rate, SpO2, GPS, and sleep tracking at fitness band pricing
  • Larger display than predecessors improves readability and touch interaction
  • Solid battery life reduces charging frequency compared to full smartwatches
  • Samsung ecosystem integration for users with Galaxy devices
Cons
  • Limited to Samsung's own app ecosystem — fewer third-party apps than Wear OS alternatives
  • Lacks voice assistant and advanced smartwatch capabilities like LTE connectivity
  • Basic notification interactions compared to higher-end smartwatches
  • Plastic build prioritizes affordability over premium feel

The Samsung Galaxy Fit 3 is worth buying if you want smartwatch-level health tracking and notifications without spending smartwatch money — particularly strong for fitness-focused users already in the Samsung ecosystem. However, if you need robust third-party app support, LTE connectivity, or a premium build, skip this and spend more on a full smartwatch.
