Rajiv Rahadari owners to strike on Aug 11 over land acquisition

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hunger strike scheduled for August 11 over land acquisition for Rajiv Rahadari elevated corridor
  • Property owners demand fair compensation and transparent process
  • Protest follows months of unresolved negotiations with authorities

Protest planned over corridor land acquisition

Property owners along the Rajiv Rahadari corridor in Hyderabad have announced a hunger strike on August 11, protesting the land acquisition process for the upcoming elevated corridor project. The decision comes after months of talks with the authorities failed to resolve their concerns over compensation and rehabilitation.

The protestors, who own land and structures along the stretch, say they have been left with no choice but to escalate their agitation. They allege that the acquisition process has been opaque, with several owners yet to receive formal notices or clarity on the extent of land being taken over.

Key demands of the agitators

The primary demand is a fair and transparent compensation package, indexed to current market rates. Many owners also want the authorities to provide alternative land parcels or built-up space in lieu of the acquired property, especially for those whose livelihoods depend on the roadside establishments.

Another sticking point is the timeline of the project, which the owners say has not been clearly communicated. They fear that prolonged uncertainty will further depress property values and disrupt their businesses even before acquisition notices are served.

Government's position so far

Officials from the Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority (HMDA) and the revenue department have held multiple rounds of discussions with the owners, but a breakthrough has remained elusive. The authorities have maintained that the land is required for a public infrastructure project of significant importance, and that compensation will be determined as per the provisions of the land acquisition act.

However, the owners argue that the rates offered in the initial assessments are far below the prevailing market value in the corridor, which has seen rapid commercial development in recent years. They have also pointed out that several small businesses along the route will be rendered unviable if the acquisition goes through without proper mitigation measures.

What the hunger strike entails

The protest is scheduled to begin at 10 am on August 11 at a designated spot along the corridor. The organisers have said that the strike will continue until the authorities respond with a concrete offer or a written assurance to revisit the compensation formula.

Local residents' associations and traders' bodies have extended their support to the agitation, though they have stopped short of calling for a bandh or road blockade. The police have been informed about the protest, and permission is being sought as per the standard procedure for such demonstrations.

The Rajiv Rahadari elevated corridor is a key infrastructure project aimed at decongesting one of Hyderabad's busiest arterial roads. The project involves widening of the existing carriageway and construction of elevated sections at major junctions, which requires acquisition of land parcels on both sides of the road.

Looking ahead

With the hunger strike just two days away, all eyes are on the district administration and the HMDA to see if a last-minute negotiation can avert the protest. If the strike goes ahead, it could set the stage for a longer confrontation between the property owners and the government, potentially delaying the project's construction timeline.

The coming week will be crucial in determining whether the authorities choose to engage with the owners' demands or stick to their earlier stance. For now, the property owners have made it clear that they are prepared to sustain the agitation until their concerns are addressed.
