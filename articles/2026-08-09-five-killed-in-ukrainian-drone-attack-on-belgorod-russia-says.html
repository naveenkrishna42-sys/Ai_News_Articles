Five killed in Ukrainian drone attack on Belgorod, Russia says

📅 2026-08-09 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Five people were killed in a Ukrainian drone attack on Belgorod, according to Russian authorities.
  • Overnight strikes between Russia and Ukraine left at least seven dead and many injured.
  • Russia says its air defenses destroyed 285 Ukrainian drones over a 12-hour period.

Russian authorities say five people were killed in a Ukrainian drone attack on the border city of Belgorod, part of a fresh wave of overnight strikes that have left at least seven dead across both countries.

The attack on Belgorod, which lies roughly 40 km from the Ukrainian border, was reported by regional officials on Sunday. They said several residential buildings were hit, and many people were injured, though exact figures have not yet been confirmed.

Overnight exchange of strikes

The Belgorod incident was one of several reported overnight. In Ukraine, officials said Russian strikes killed at least two people and wounded several others. Local authorities described damage to civilian infrastructure, but details remain sketchy.

Both sides routinely deny targeting civilians, yet the toll from these exchanges continues to mount. The attacks come amid an ongoing escalation in cross-border strikes that have become a near-daily feature of the conflict.

Russia claims massive drone interception

Meanwhile, Russia's defence ministry said its air defences destroyed 285 Ukrainian drones over a 12-hour period. That figure, if accurate, would mark one of the largest single-day interceptions reported in recent months.

The ministry did not specify how many drones were intercepted over which regions, but such claims are common in official Russian statements. Independent verification of these numbers is not possible.

Ukraine has not commented on the scale of its drone operations. Its military typically confirms strikes only after they are reported by Russian sources.

Civilian toll rises

The overnight violence brings the total reported deaths from the latest round of strikes to seven, with many more wounded. Hospitals in Belgorod said they were treating casualties, but did not provide a breakdown.

In recent weeks, both Russia and Ukraine have intensified long-range attacks, targeting energy infrastructure, military sites, and border towns. The strikes have displaced thousands and put renewed pressure on emergency services.

International observers have repeatedly called for restraint, but there is little sign of de-escalation. The United Nations has not yet issued a statement on this latest incident.

What to watch

In the coming days, expect further confirmation of casualty figures and possible retaliatory strikes. The pattern of overnight attacks shows no sign of abating, and diplomatic efforts remain stalled.
