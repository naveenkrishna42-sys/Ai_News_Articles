Batwara 1947: Sunny Deol's Rs 60 crore fee, Preity Zinta's pay revealed

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sunny Deol's reported remuneration for Batwara 1947 is Rs 60 crore
  • Preity Zinta is reportedly paid Rs 10 crore for her role
  • The film is set for release on August 14
  • Deol and Zinta have been promoting the film, including meeting UP CM Yogi Adityanath

The upcoming period drama Batwara 1947 has been making headlines for its subject matter, but now the reported pay cheques of its lead actors are doing the rounds. According to industry sources cited by Times of India, Sunny Deol is taking home a whopping Rs 60 crore for the film, while his co-star Preity Zinta is reportedly earning Rs 10 crore.

The figures, if accurate, place Deol among the highest-paid actors in the country for a single project. Neither the actors nor the film's production house has officially confirmed the numbers, but the reports have already sparked conversations about star remuneration in Bollywood.

A return to a pivotal chapter

Batwara 1947 revisits the Partition of India, a theme that resonates deeply with audiences across the subcontinent. The film is scheduled to hit theatres on August 14, a date steeped in historical significance.

Deol, known for his action-packed roles, has been vocal about the film's emotional core. He recently urged the youth to watch the movie, stating that "nothing is greater than humanity" — a message that cuts through the historical trauma of Partition.

Preity Zinta's political interactions

Preity Zinta, who has been away from the big screen for several years, is making a notable comeback with this project. Ahead of the release, she met Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath, describing the interaction as warm and praising his passion for books.

The meeting was part of a broader promotional campaign that has seen the film's team engage with political and cultural figures. Sunny Deol and his son Karan Deol also visited the Partition Museum in Amritsar, a move that underscores the film's historical grounding.

Star power and box office expectations

The reported salary disparity between Deol and Zinta has raised eyebrows, but industry observers point out that Deol's box office track record in recent years has been exceptional. His last few releases have performed strongly, and his fan base remains loyal, especially in the Hindi heartland.

Zinta's Rs 10 crore fee, while significantly lower, still reflects her star status in an industry where female actors often earn a fraction of their male counterparts. The film's producers have not commented on the pay structure, and it remains to be seen if the reported figures will be corroborated.

The road to release

With less than a week to go for the release, the promotional machinery is in full swing. The film's team is banking on the emotional weight of the Partition story, combined with Deol's star power, to draw audiences to theatres.

Early buzz suggests that Batwara 1947 could be a significant commercial and critical player in the Independence Day weekend slot. However, the film faces competition from other releases, and its performance will depend on word-of-mouth.

As the release date approaches, all eyes will be on the box office collections and whether the reported salaries translate into returns for the producers. The film's reception will also signal whether audiences are ready for a Partition drama in an era dominated by spectacle-driven cinema.
