Bishnoi Gang Link Suspected in Threat to Aamir Khan

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mumbai Police investigating threat to Aamir Khan
  • Possible Bishnoi gang link under scrutiny
  • Security for the actor has been heightened

MUMBAI: The threat against Bollywood actor Aamir Khan has taken a sharper edge, with investigators now probing a possible link to the Bishnoi gang. The development comes as Mumbai Police intensify their inquiry into the menace that has put the actor's security on high alert.

Sources familiar with the matter said the gang, known for its reach and previous high-profile threats, has emerged as a key line of investigation. However, officials have not yet confirmed any direct connection, and the probe remains in its early stages.

Threat Assessment and Security Measures

The actor's security cover was enhanced after the threat came to light, with additional personnel deployed at his residence and public appearances being reviewed. A senior police officer, speaking on condition of anonymity, said the threat is being treated with utmost seriousness.

"We are examining all angles, and the Bishnoi link is one of them," the officer said. "The safety of the individual is our priority, and we are taking all necessary precautions."

The development has sent ripples through the film industry, where security concerns for leading figures have grown in recent years. Aamir Khan, known for his critically acclaimed films, has a massive fan following, but his outspoken views on social issues have occasionally drawn ire.

Bishnoi Gang's Notorious Reputation

The Bishnoi gang, believed to be headed by jailed gangster Lawrence Bishnoi, has been linked to several high-profile crimes, including the murder of Punjabi singer Sidhu Moosewala. The gang has also been accused of targeting individuals in the entertainment and political spheres.

Police records show the gang operates with a network spanning multiple states, often using contract killers and extortion to assert its influence. Any confirmed link to the threat against Aamir Khan would mark a significant escalation in its activities.

Investigators are now scrutinising the origin of the threat, including any digital footprint or intercepted communication. They are also looking into whether the threat is a standalone act or part of a larger pattern.

Industry Reaction and Precedents

The news has revived memories of past threats against Bollywood celebrities, many of which were traced to extortion rackets with links to organised crime. In several cases, the threats were issued to demand money, though the motive in the current instance remains unclear.

Film industry insiders have expressed concern, though most declined to comment on record. "It is a worrying trend," said a trade analyst, who did not wish to be named. "When a star of Aamir's stature is threatened, it affects the entire fraternity."

Police have not disclosed the exact nature of the threat — whether it was made over phone, social media, or through a third party. They have also not revealed if any arrests have been made, but confirmed that the investigation is progressing.

The actor's team has issued no official statement, and his upcoming projects are believed to be proceeding as scheduled, though security logistics are being reassessed.

What Lies Ahead

The coming days are likely to see more clarity on the Bishnoi link, as police await forensic and technical reports. The Mumbai Police's Special Branch is coordinating with agencies in other states to track any leads.

Observers will be watching whether the threat leads to a broader crackdown on the gang's network, and how the film industry responds to the heightened security climate. For now, Aamir Khan's safety remains the immediate concern, with the investigation moving on multiple fronts.
