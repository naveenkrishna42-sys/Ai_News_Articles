Man critically injured as scooter hits parked trolley auto in Amberpet

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • A man was critically injured in Amberpet when his scooter hit a parked trolley auto.
  • The accident occurred in the Amberpet area of Hyderabad.
  • The victim was rushed to a hospital for treatment; police are investigating.

Scooter crashes into parked vehicle in Amberpet

A 35-year-old man was critically injured on Tuesday afternoon after his scooter collided with a parked trolley auto in the Amberpet area of Hyderabad. The incident occurred around 2:30 pm on a busy stretch of road near the Amberpet police station.

According to local residents, the man was riding his scooter at a moderate speed when he lost control and crashed into the stationary vehicle. The impact was severe, and the man was thrown off his scooter, sustaining serious head and chest injuries.

Victim rushed to hospital

Passersby immediately alerted the police and an ambulance. The man was rushed to a nearby government hospital, where doctors described his condition as critical. He is currently undergoing treatment in the intensive care unit.

Police officials have not yet released the victim's identity, pending family notification. They are investigating the exact cause of the accident, including whether speed or a mechanical failure played a role.

Parked trolley auto raises safety questions

The trolley auto, a common sight in Hyderabad for transporting goods, was parked on the side of the road at the time of the crash. While it is unclear if the vehicle was legally parked, the incident has sparked concerns among local residents about the hazards posed by poorly parked vehicles along busy roads.

Residents told TIVRA News that the stretch of road near Amberpet police station often has vehicles parked haphazardly, forcing riders to swerve into traffic. The police have not yet filed a formal report on the incident.

Authorities have not confirmed whether any charges will be filed against the owner of the trolley auto. The investigation is ongoing.

What happens next

Police are expected to release the victim's name once his family is notified. They are also reviewing CCTV footage from the area to determine the sequence of events. The incident could lead to a broader review of parking regulations in the Amberpet area.
