Hyderabad Ring Rail: North DPR ready, South alignment nears completion

📅 2026-08-30 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • DPR completed for Hyderabad Ring Rail's north section
  • South alignment in final stages of DPR
  • Project aims to reduce congestion, improve connectivity

DPR Milestone Achieved for Hyderabad Ring Rail

Kishan Reddy, Minister for Urban Development and Housing, has confirmed that the Detailed Project Report (DPR) for the north section of the Hyderabad Ring Rail project is ready. The DPR for the south alignment is in the final stages of completion. This is a significant milestone for the project, which aims to transform Hyderabad's urban mobility landscape.

Project Overview

The Hyderabad Ring Rail project is a crucial infrastructure initiative aimed at reducing congestion and improving connectivity in the city. The project involves the construction of a 100-kilometer ring rail around Hyderabad, with the north and south sections being the first phases. The DPR is a comprehensive document that outlines the technical and financial aspects of the project.

Key Details and Timeline

The DPR for the north section has been completed, and the project is expected to start soon. The south alignment is in the final stages of DPR completion, with construction expected to begin shortly after. The project is expected to be completed in phases, with the entire ring rail expected to be operational by 2030.
