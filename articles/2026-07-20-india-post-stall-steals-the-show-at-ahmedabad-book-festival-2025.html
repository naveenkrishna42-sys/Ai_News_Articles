India Post Stall Steals the Show at Ahmedabad Book Festival 2025

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Culture & Arts Original source ↗
Key points
  • India Post's stall was the main attraction at the Ahmedabad International Book Festival 2025.
  • The stall featured rare stamps, commemorative covers, and philatelic displays.
  • Visitors showed keen interest in stamp collection and postal heritage.

Postal Heritage Shines at Book Festival

India Post’s stall became the biggest draw at the Ahmedabad International Book Festival 2025. The event, held in the city, saw thousands of visitors flock to the postal department’s exhibit over the course of the festival.

The stall showcased a rich collection of vintage stamps, special commemorative covers, and thematic philatelic displays. Many visitors, from schoolchildren to senior citizens, stopped to browse and ask questions.

Rare Stamps and Collectibles on Display

Among the highlights were first-day covers and stamps depicting India’s cultural and historical milestones. Officials from India Post were on hand to explain the significance of each item. The stall also offered limited-edition stamps and souvenir sheets for sale.

Collectors and hobbyists found the display particularly engaging. Several people purchased stamp albums and starter kits, with many expressing interest in taking up philately as a pastime.

Interactive Activities Draw Crowds

Beyond the exhibits, the stall featured interactive sessions. Postal staff conducted brief demonstrations on how to preserve stamps and build collections. Children were given activity sheets and small prizes for participating in quizzes about Indian postal history.

The department also set up a temporary counter where visitors could mail postcards with a special festival cancellation stamp. This hands-on experience proved to be a major crowd-puller, with long queues forming throughout the day.

Promoting Philately Among Youth

India Post has been making concerted efforts to revive interest in stamp collection among younger generations. The Ahmedabad festival stall was part of that broader push. Officials noted that many young visitors expressed surprise at the variety and artistry of Indian stamps.

“We wanted to show that stamps are not just for mailing letters,” a postal official at the stall said. “They are tiny windows into our history, art, and achievements.” The response, according to the department, exceeded expectations.

Book Festival Witnesses Strong Footfall

The Ahmedabad International Book Festival 2025, organized by the National Book Trust and local authorities, attracted publishers, authors, and readers from across the country. India Post’s participation added a unique dimension to the literary event.

Other stalls at the festival included major publishing houses, regional language booksellers, and digital learning platforms. Yet, the postal department’s exhibit stood out for its nostalgic appeal and hands-on engagement.

The festival concluded on a high note, with India Post reporting that it had sold out several of its limited-edition items by the final day.

What’s Next for India Post’s Outreach

Encouraged by the response, India Post is likely to replicate similar stalls at other literary and cultural festivals across the country. The department is also planning to launch a mobile philatelic van to reach smaller towns and rural areas. The aim is to make stamp collection accessible to a wider audience.
