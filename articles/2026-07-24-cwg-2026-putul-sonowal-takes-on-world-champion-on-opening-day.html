CWG 2026: Putul Sonowal takes on world champion on opening day

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Putul Sonowal competes against the reigning world bowls champion on Day 1.
  • The opening ceremony in Glasgow features King Charles declaring the Games open.
  • Mirabai Chanu and Lovlina Borgohain lead India in the Parade of Nations.

Putul Sonowal's tough start in bowls

Indian lawn bowls player Putul Sonowal will face the reigning world champion in her opening match at the Commonwealth Games 2026 on Thursday, July 23. The draw pits her against one of the most decorated players in the sport from the opening round.

Sonowal has been training in Glasgow for the past week, acclimatising to the greens. The women's singles event is expected to be fiercely competitive, with several Commonwealth medallists in the field.

Officials from the Indian contingent have not commented on her chances but noted she has prepared well. A win would put her in contention for a quarterfinal berth.

Opening ceremony marks official start

The Commonwealth Games 2026 will be formally declared open by King Charles III at the opening ceremony in Glasgow on Wednesday evening. The ceremony, held at the city's Scotstoun Stadium, will feature cultural performances and the Parade of Nations.

India's flag bearers for the ceremony are weightlifter Mirabai Chanu and boxer Lovlina Borgohain. Both are Olympic medallists and carry strong medal hopes for the country.

The Parade of Nations will see the Indian contingent march in traditional attire. The ceremony is expected to last around two hours.

Mirabai Chanu targets Asian Games

While the Commonwealth Games are the immediate focus, Mirabai Chanu and her coach have made it clear that the Asian Games remain the bigger goal for the year. Speaking before the opening ceremony, they outlined a long-term plan that prioritises performance in Hangzhou later this season.

Chanu, a gold medallist at the 2022 Commonwealth Games, is competing in the 49kg category in Glasgow. Her coach said the team is using the CWG as a stepping stone, not the final destination.

This approach means Chanu may not attempt her heaviest lifts in the early rounds. Fans will be watching closely to see how she balances immediate success with future ambitions.

India's medal prospects on Day 1

Beyond bowls and weightlifting, India has several athletes in action on the opening day of competition. Boxers, badminton players and shooters will also begin their campaigns.

Lovlina Borgohain, who leads the boxing contingent, has a first-round bye. She will step into the ring later this week.

Shooting events, reintroduced after a gap, will see Indian marksmen in qualifying rounds. The country has traditionally done well in the sport at the Commonwealth Games.

The full schedule of medal events on July 24 will feature finals in swimming, cycling and gymnastics. India is not expected to contend for medals in those disciplines on the first full day of competition.

What to watch next

All eyes will be on Putul Sonowal's match and Mirabai Chanu's weightlifting performance on Thursday. Results from the opening rounds will set the tone for the Indian contingent's campaign in Glasgow.
