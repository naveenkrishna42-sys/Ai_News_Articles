Spider-Man: Brand New Day sells 5.80 lakh tickets in national chains for Day 1

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day sells 5.80 lakh tickets in national chains for Day 1 advance bookings
  • Tom Holland returns as Peter Parker in Destin Daniel Cretton's film
  • Early reviews call it the best MCU Spider-Man movie yet

Spider-Man: Brand New Day is off to a flying start at the Indian box office. The Tom Holland starrer has sold 5.80 lakh tickets across national chains for its opening day, according to final advance booking figures.

The numbers put the film in a strong position for its release. Industry trackers expect the movie to post impressive numbers over the weekend.

Advance booking numbers

The 5.80 lakh ticket figure covers major multiplex chains across India. Single-screen theatres and regional chains are not included in this count, meaning the actual advance sales could be higher.

This marks one of the strongest pre-sales for a Hollywood release in India this year. The film has seen particular demand in metro cities like Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru.

Early critical response

Reviews for Brand New Day have been overwhelmingly positive. The Hindu called it a "soulful reinvention" while Rediff described it as the "best MCU Spider-Man movie yet."

Telegraph India noted that Holland and his character Peter Parker "have come of age" in this instalment. Critic consensus points to director Destin Daniel Cretton bringing a fresh approach to the franchise.

The film picks up after the events of Spider-Man: No Way Home. Peter Parker faces new challenges while navigating life as a superhero without the support system he once had.

Box office expectations

Trade analysts are watching the opening weekend numbers closely. The strong advance booking suggests the film could challenge previous Spider-Man records in India.

No Way Home had set the bar high with its India collections. Early indicators suggest Brand New Day could come close to matching that performance, driven by positive word-of-mouth and Holland's star power.

The film is releasing in multiple languages including English, Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu. This wide release strategy has helped it reach audiences beyond the traditional metros.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day hit theatres on July 31, 2026. The box office numbers over the next few days will confirm whether the advance booking momentum translates into sustained collections.
