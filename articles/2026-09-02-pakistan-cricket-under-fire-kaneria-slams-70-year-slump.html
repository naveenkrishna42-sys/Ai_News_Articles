Danish Kaneria slams Pakistan cricket after Lord's loss

Former Pakistan cricketer Danish Kaneria has lambasted the country's cricket team after their humiliating defeat at Lord's. Kaneria, who played for Pakistan in the 1990s, called the team's performance a 'shame after 70 years'.

Kaneria's comments come as Pakistan faces a crisis in cricket, with the team struggling to perform at the highest level. The team's recent defeat at Lord's, where they were bowled out for just 33 runs, has further highlighted the problems facing Pakistan cricket.

Pakistan's cricket crisis

The defeat at Lord's is the latest in a series of poor performances by the Pakistan cricket team. The team has failed to qualify for the ICC Cricket World Cup in the last two editions, and their performance in the ICC Champions Trophy was also disappointing.

The team's problems are not just limited to their performance on the field. There are also concerns about the team's selection and management. The current captain, Babar Azam, has been criticized for his leadership, and there are questions about the team's coaching and training.

Experts and players highlight the need for change

Former Pakistan cricketer and commentator Sarfraz Ahmed has also criticized the team's performance, blaming a lack of time spent in the middle. Ahmed, who played for Pakistan in the 2000s, has called for a change in the team's approach, with more emphasis on building partnerships and rotating the strike.

Other experts and players have also called for change in Pakistan cricket. Former Pakistan captain Wasim Akram has criticized the team's selection, with Akram arguing that the team needs to be more aggressive in its approach. Akram has also called for a change in the team's coaching, with Akram arguing that the team needs a more experienced and knowledgeable coach.

Pakistan cricket is facing a crisis, with the team struggling to perform at the highest level and facing concerns about its selection, coaching, and leadership.
