Pakistan rebuilds JeM HQ in Bahawalpur

📅 2026-08-23 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • JeM's Bahawalpur terror base, destroyed in India's Operation Sindoor, is fully operational again
  • Pakistan has rebuilt the base 15 months after India's strikes
  • India has not commented on the development

JeM's Bahawalpur terror base fully operational again

15 months after India's Operation Sindoor destroyed Jaish-e-Mohammed's (JeM) terror base in Bahawalpur, Pakistan has rebuilt the facility, making it fully operational again. Reports suggest that the base is now in its original condition, with all the necessary infrastructure in place.

India's Operation Sindoor and the destruction of the base

Operation Sindoor was a military operation conducted by India in 2024, targeting JeM's terror infrastructure in Pakistan. The operation resulted in the destruction of several JeM facilities, including the Bahawalpur terror base. The operation was a significant blow to JeM, which had been a major player in the region's terror landscape.

Pakistan's response to India's strikes

Pakistan has not commented on the development of the Bahawalpur terror base. However, it is not uncommon for Pakistan to rebuild terror infrastructure destroyed by India. In the past, Pakistan has been accused of providing safe haven to terror groups and rebuilding their infrastructure.

India's stance on the matter

India has not commented on the development. However, the Indian government has been vocal about its stance against terror groups operating from Pakistan. The government has been pushing for a stronger response from Pakistan to the terror attacks in India.

What happens next?

The development of the Bahawalpur terror base is a significant one, and it is likely to be watched closely by both India and Pakistan. The Indian government is likely to continue its efforts to counter terror groups operating from Pakistan. At the same time, Pakistan is likely to continue its efforts to provide safe haven to terror groups and rebuild their infrastructure.
