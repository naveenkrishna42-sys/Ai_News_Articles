Mirabai Chanu wins India's first gold at Commonwealth Games 2026

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mirabai Chanu won India's first gold at the 2026 Commonwealth Games in Glasgow.
  • She completed a historic hat-trick of gold medals in the event.
  • India added three more medals on Day 4, including silvers from Rishikanta and Muthupandi.

Mirabai Chanu powered India to its first gold medal at the 2026 Commonwealth Games in Glasgow, delivering a record-breaking performance in weightlifting. The 31-year-old star lifted a combined total that shattered the Games record, securing her third consecutive Commonwealth gold. She had previously won gold at Gold Coast 2018 and Birmingham 2022.

The lift came after months of intense preparation and rehabilitation from a lingering back injury. Chanu, who also won a silver medal at the Tokyo Olympics, told NDTV exclusively after the event: "Tears were for everything I went through."

Hat-trick in Glasgow

Chanu's victory on Day 4 of the Games marked a historic milestone. She is now the first Indian weightlifter to win three gold medals at the Commonwealth Games. Her opening lifts in the snatch and clean-and-jerk categories were flawless, drawing loud cheers from the Indian contingent in the stands.

Officials confirmed that her final lift was a new Games record, though exact figures were not immediately disclosed. The Indian weightlifting federation praised her discipline and resilience, calling her performance "inspirational for the next generation."

Other medal wins boost India's tally

India added three more medals on the same day. Weightlifters Rishikanta and Muthupandi each won silver medals in their respective weight categories, continuing the country's strong showing in the sport. The pair delivered consistent lifts, fending off tough competition from Nigeria and Malaysia.

These victories pushed India's overall medal count to five, with two golds and three silvers. The boxing and shooting teams are also expected to add to the tally in the coming days.

Emotional return to form

Chanu's journey to Glasgow was not easy. She underwent extensive physiotherapy after a disc bulge in her lower back threatened her career. Many doubted whether she could replicate her past success.

Speaking after the medal ceremony, Chanu said the tears were not just for the gold but for the months of pain and doubt. "This one is special," she added, clutching the medal. Her coach credited her mental toughness and rigorous training regimen.

What's next for Indian weightlifting

The Commonwealth Games continue through the week, with Indian athletes competing in athletics, wrestling, and badminton. Chanu's performance has set a high benchmark for the rest of the contingent.

Officials are now looking ahead to the 2028 Olympics, where Chanu is expected to make a strong bid for gold. The federation plans to support her with advanced training facilities and nutrition programs.
