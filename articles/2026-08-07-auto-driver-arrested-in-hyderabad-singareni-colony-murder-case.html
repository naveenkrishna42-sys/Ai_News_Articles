Auto Driver Arrested in Hyderabad Singareni Colony Murder Case

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Saidabad police arrest an auto-rickshaw driver in connection with a murder in Singareni Colony.
  • The arrest follows an investigation by the Saidabad police team.
  • Further details about the motive and evidence are awaited.

Hyderabad: The Saidabad police have arrested an auto-rickshaw driver in connection with the murder of a man in Singareni Colony. The arrest comes days after the victim's body was found under circumstances that had sparked tension in the area.

Police said the accused, whose identity has not been officially disclosed, was taken into custody on Thursday night. He was produced before a local court on Friday and remanded to judicial custody.

Sequence of Events

The murder came to light when locals spotted the body in Singareni Colony and alerted the police. Initial investigations suggested a possible personal rivalry, but the police did not rule out other angles.

A senior officer from the Saidabad station said the accused was traced based on technical evidence and witness statements. "We have gathered sufficient material to establish his involvement," the officer said, requesting anonymity as the probe is still underway.

Evidence and Confession

During interrogation, the accused reportedly admitted to his role in the crime. Police have recovered the weapon used in the attack, though they have not yet disclosed its nature.

The auto-rickshaw allegedly used by the accused during the incident has also been seized. Investigators are verifying whether the accused acted alone or with accomplices.

Community Concerns

The incident had briefly caused unease among residents of Singareni Colony, with many demanding swift action. Local leaders have welcomed the arrest but urged the police to ensure a thorough investigation.

Police have increased patrolling in the area as a precautionary measure. They have also appealed to the public not to spread rumours about the case.

Legal Proceedings

The accused has been booked under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including charges of murder. Further legal steps will follow as the investigation progresses.

Confirming the development, a police press note stated, "The accused has been arrested and remanded. A detailed investigation is in progress."

Officials have not yet confirmed the motive behind the murder, but they indicated that a dispute over money is one of the lines of inquiry being pursued.

The case is likely to be taken up for hearing in the local court in the coming days, where the police will seek extended custody for further questioning. Meanwhile, the family of the victim has been informed of the arrest.

The investigation now shifts to establishing the exact sequence of events that led to the murder and ensuring that all those involved are brought to book.
