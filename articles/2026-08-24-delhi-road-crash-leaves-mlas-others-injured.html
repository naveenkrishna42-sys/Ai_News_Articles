Two MLAs among six injured in Delhi road accident

Two MLAs were among six people injured in a road accident in Delhi's ITO area on Sunday. The incident occurred when a car jumped the divider, crashed into an SUV, and exploded due to a CNG cylinder. The injured include Abhay Patil, an MLA from the Aam Aadmi Party, whose condition is stable.

What happened?

The accident occurred on a busy road in Delhi's ITO area. A car, which had a CNG cylinder, jumped the divider and crashed into an SUV. The impact caused the CNG cylinder to explode, adding to the severity of the accident. Six people were injured in the incident, including two MLAs.

Who were the injured?

The injured include two MLAs, Abhay Patil and another MLA whose name has not been disclosed. The other injured are four civilians. Abhay Patil, who is an MLA from the Aam Aadmi Party, is in stable condition. The other injured are also reported to be in stable condition.

Why does this matter?

This incident highlights the dangers of driving on Delhi's roads. The accident occurred on a busy road, and the driver of the car jumped the divider, which is a clear violation of traffic rules. The explosion of the CNG cylinder added to the severity of the accident, causing multiple injuries.

What is being done?

The Delhi Police have launched an investigation into the incident. The driver of the car has been arrested, and the CNG cylinder has been recovered. The injured have been taken to the hospital, where they are receiving treatment. The Delhi Police are also advising drivers to follow traffic rules and be cautious while driving on the roads.
