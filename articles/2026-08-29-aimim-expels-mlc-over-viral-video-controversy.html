AIMIM expels MLC over viral video controversy

📅 2026-08-29 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • AIMIM expels MLC Mirza Rahmath Baig over controversial video
  • BJP criticizes expulsion as 'not justice'
  • MLC alleges deepfake threat, approaches cybercrime police
  • Video's origin and authenticity remain unverified

Controversy Erupts Over AIMIM's Leadership Decision

All India Majlis-e-Ittehadul Muslimeen (AIMIM) has expelled its MLA Mirza Rahmath Baig from the party, citing a controversial video that allegedly shows him in a compromising position. The decision has sparked widespread criticism and raised questions about the authenticity of the video.

BJP Criticizes Expulsion as 'Not Justice'

The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) has criticized AIMIM's decision to expel Baig, calling it 'not justice'. BJP leader Asaduddin Owaisi, who is also the AIMIM president, has been vocal in his support for Baig, calling the expulsion a 'political conspiracy'.

MLC Alleges Deepfake Threat, Approaches Cybercrime Police

Baig, who is also a member of the Legislative Council (MLC), has alleged that the video is a deepfake and has approached the cybercrime police for assistance. He has also threatened legal action against those responsible for the video's circulation.

Video's Origin and Authenticity Remain Unverified

The origin and authenticity of the video remain unverified, with no concrete evidence presented to support the allegations against Baig. This has led to speculation and conspiracy theories, with some questioning the motives behind the expulsion.
