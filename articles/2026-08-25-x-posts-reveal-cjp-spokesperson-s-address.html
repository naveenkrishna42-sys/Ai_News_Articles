CJP Spokesperson Files Privacy Suit After X Posts Reveal Address

Saurav Das, spokesperson for the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP), has moved the Delhi High Court over the public disclosure of his address on social media platform X. The court has summoned Abhijit Iyer-Mitra, along with other media portals, to appear before it in the matter.

Court Declines Interim Relief, Notes Posts Already Taken Down

The Delhi High Court has declined to grant interim relief to Saurav Das in his privacy suit, noting that the posts revealing his address have already been taken down by X. The court has, however, directed the parties to appear before it on a specified date to present their arguments.
