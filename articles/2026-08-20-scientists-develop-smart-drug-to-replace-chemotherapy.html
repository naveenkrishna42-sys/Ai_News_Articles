Scientists develop smart drug to replace chemotherapy

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Health Original source ↗
Key points
  • RK-251 is a smart drug that activates primarily in cancer cells
  • Developed by researchers from IASST and IIT-Guwahati
  • Could potentially replace chemotherapy for certain types of cancer

Indian scientists have developed a new 'smart' drug, RK-251, that could replace chemotherapy for certain types of cancer. The drug, which activates primarily in cancer cells, has been created by researchers from the Institute of Advanced Studies in Science and Technology (IASST) and the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) in Guwahati.

How RK-251 Works

The drug is designed to target tumour cells while sparing healthy cells. It works by activating in response to specific conditions within cancer cells, such as low oxygen levels or high acidity. This targeted approach could potentially reduce the side effects associated with traditional chemotherapy.

Potential Benefits

RK-251 could be particularly beneficial for patients with aggressive breast cancer. The drug's ability to specifically target cancer cells could lead to more effective treatment outcomes while minimizing damage to healthy tissues.

Future Developments

The development of RK-251 is a significant step forward in the field of cancer treatment. However, further clinical trials are needed to fully understand its efficacy and safety. The Department of Science and Technology (DST) has announced the new drug, which could potentially replace chemotherapy for certain types of cancer.

As research into smart drugs continues, it is likely that we will see more such innovative treatments emerge in the future. This could mark a significant shift in the way cancer is treated, with a greater focus on targeting specific cells and minimizing damage to healthy tissues.
