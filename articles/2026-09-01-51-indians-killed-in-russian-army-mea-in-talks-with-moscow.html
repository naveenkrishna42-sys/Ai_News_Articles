51 Indian nationals killed in Russian Army: What we know

51 Indian nationals serving in the Russian Army have been killed in action, according to official sources. The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) has confirmed the deaths, stating that 139 Indian nationals have been discharged from service. The MEA has also indicated that talks are ongoing with the Russian government regarding the situation.

Broader Implications for India-Russia Relations

The deaths of Indian nationals in the Russian Army have significant implications for India-Russia relations. The Russian government has been accused of using Indian nationals as cheap labor in the war against Ukraine. The Indian government has been vocal about its concerns, and the ongoing talks with the Russian government are a sign of the seriousness of the situation.
