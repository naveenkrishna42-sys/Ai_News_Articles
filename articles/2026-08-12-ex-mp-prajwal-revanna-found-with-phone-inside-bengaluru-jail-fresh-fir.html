Ex-MP Prajwal Revanna Found With Phone Inside Bengaluru Jail, Fresh FIR Filed

📅 2026-08-12 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Prajwal Revanna, serving life term in a rape case, was found with a phone and pen drive inside Bengaluru prison.
  • The Central Crime Branch (CCB) has registered a fresh FIR under the Prisons Act.
  • This is not the first such incident involving Revanna inside the jail.

Bengaluru: Former Hassan MP Prajwal Revanna, who is serving a life term in a rape case, has landed in fresh trouble after prison officials found a mobile phone and a pen drive in his possession inside the Bengaluru prison. The discovery has triggered a new case against the disgraced politician, who has been in custody since his arrest in connection with multiple sexual assault allegations.

Phone and Pen Drive Seized

According to prison sources, the electronic items were recovered during a routine check of Revanna's cell. The officials immediately seized the phone and the pen drive and informed the Central Crime Branch (CCB), which has now registered a fresh FIR under the relevant sections of the Prisons Act.

The CCB has taken over the investigation. It is not yet clear how the former MP managed to get the phone inside the high-security facility, or how long he had been using it. Officials have not ruled out the involvement of prison staff in facilitating the breach.

Fresh FIR Under Prisons Act

The new case has been filed under the Karnataka Prisons Act, which strictly prohibits inmates from possessing any electronic communication devices. The law also prescribes punishment for anyone found aiding such violations.

This is not the first time Revanna has been embroiled in a controversy while in custody. Earlier, there were reports of him allegedly using mobile phones to coordinate with associates, though those claims were not formally confirmed. The latest seizure, however, has put the prison administration under scrutiny.

Background of the Case

Revanna, a former MP from Hassan, was arrested in 2024 after multiple women accused him of sexual assault. He was convicted and sentenced to life imprisonment in a high-profile case that drew nationwide attention.

The case had already damaged the political standing of the Revanna family, which has been influential in Karnataka's old Mysuru region. The former MP's father, H.D. Revanna, is a senior leader of the Janata Dal (Secular), and his uncle, H.D. Kumaraswamy, has served as Chief Minister of the state.

Investigation Underway

The CCB is now examining the contents of the seized phone and pen drive to determine if they contain any incriminating material. Investigators are also questioning prison staff to trace how the items entered the facility.

Security protocols in Bengaluru's central prison are expected to be reviewed in the wake of the incident. A senior prison official said that an internal inquiry has been ordered to identify any lapses.

The fresh FIR adds to the legal troubles of Revanna, who is already facing multiple cases. His lawyers have not yet commented on the latest development.

What Happens Next?

The CCB will submit its findings to the court, which may decide on further action against Revanna. The prison department is also likely to face questions from the Karnataka High Court over the security breach.

For now, the former MP remains in custody, and the investigation into how he obtained the phone is ongoing.
