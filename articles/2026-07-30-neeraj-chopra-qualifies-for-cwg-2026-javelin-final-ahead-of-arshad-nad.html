Neeraj Chopra qualifies for CWG 2026 javelin final ahead of Arshad Nadeem

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Neeraj Chopra qualified for the men's javelin throw final at the 2026 Commonwealth Games.
  • Chopra threw ahead of Pakistan's Arshad Nadeem in the qualification round.
  • Indian throwers Rohit Yadav and Yashvir Singh also made the cut for the final.

Olympic and world champion Neeraj Chopra has booked his spot in the men's javelin throw final at the 2026 Commonwealth Games, outqualifying his long-time rival Arshad Nadeem of Pakistan.

The Indian star produced a strong throw in the qualification round on Thursday, finishing ahead of Nadeem. Two other Indian throwers, Rohit Yadav and Yashvir Singh, also advanced to the final.

Chopra leads Indian charge

Chopra's performance in the qualifiers was clinical. He did not need to push to his absolute limit, but his throw was enough to top the group and send a message to the competition.

Arshad Nadeem, the reigning Olympic silver medallist, also qualified comfortably. The Pakistan athlete has pushed Chopra hard in recent years, including at the Tokyo Olympics and the 2023 World Championships.

Rohit and Yashvir join the fray

India's depth in the event continues to grow. Rohit Yadav and Yashvir Singh both secured their places in the 12-man final, giving the country three representatives in the medal round.

Rohit has been a consistent performer on the international circuit, while Yashvir has shown rapid improvement. Their presence adds tactical depth for the Indian camp.

Stage set for another epic showdown

The final, scheduled for later this week, is expected to be a high-voltage contest between Chopra and Nadeem. The two have developed one of athletics' most compelling rivalries.

Chopra holds the edge in major championships, but Nadeem has repeatedly broken the 90-metre barrier, including a national record throw at the 2023 World Championships where Chopra won gold.

What to watch for

All eyes will be on Chopra's form and whether Nadeem can finally topple him on a global stage. The Indian throwers' collective strength could also play a role in pressure management.

With qualification done, the focus now shifts to the final. The men's javelin throw final will be a must-watch event at the Commonwealth Games.
