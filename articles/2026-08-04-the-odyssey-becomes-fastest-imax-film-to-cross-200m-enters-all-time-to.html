The Odyssey Becomes Fastest IMAX Film to Cross $200M, Enters All-Time Top 3

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The Odyssey is the fastest IMAX release to cross $200 million worldwide.
  • The film has overtaken Avengers: Endgame to enter the all-time top 3 IMAX grossers.
  • India's IMAX box office saw its highest-grossing release with The Odyssey.
  • A US 70mm cinema reported multi-week extensions due to overwhelming demand.

A Record-Breaking Run

Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey has stormed past yet another milestone, becoming the fastest IMAX release ever to cross the $200 million mark globally. The film, which has been dominating box office conversations since its release, achieved this feat in record time, surpassing the previous benchmark set by Avengers: Endgame.

With this surge, The Odyssey has now broken into the all-time top three IMAX grossers, overtaking the Marvel epic and cementing its place among the biggest theatrical spectacles ever mounted. The numbers reflect not just Nolan's draw but the growing appetite for premium large-format experiences among audiences worldwide.

India's IMAX Milestone

In India, the film has set a new benchmark of its own. The Odyssey has become the highest-grossing IMAX release in the country's history, a significant achievement given the rapid expansion of IMAX screens in Indian metros over the past few years. The response from Indian audiences, known for their enthusiasm for Hollywood spectacles, has been overwhelming.

Industry trackers note that the film's performance in India has contributed substantially to its global IMAX haul, with several shows selling out weeks in advance. The combination of Nolan's storytelling and the immersive IMAX format has clearly struck a chord with Indian cinephiles.

Global IMAX Dominance

Globally, The Odyssey has sold an estimated $52 million in IMAX tickets alone, a figure that underscores the film's appeal in premium formats. According to reports, this has also had a ripple effect on the stock market, with IMAX shares hitting a new high on Wall Street, as investors bet on the continued success of large-format cinema.

The film's performance has been particularly strong in 70mm screenings. A prominent US theatre, Alex Theatre, has topped all 70mm cinemas presenting The Odyssey in the United States, leading to a multi-week extension of its screenings. This is a rare feat in an era where digital projection dominates, and it speaks to the filmmaker's insistence on celluloid.

What This Means for Theatrical Cinema

The success of The Odyssey in IMAX and 70mm formats is being closely watched by industry analysts. At a time when streaming services and AI-generated content are reshaping entertainment, the film's theatrical dominance suggests that audiences still crave the shared, larger-than-life experience that only cinemas can provide.

Nolan has long been a champion of the theatrical window, and this release reinforces his belief that the big screen remains the ultimate venue for storytelling. The film's box office performance is likely to influence how studios approach future big-budget releases, with an increased focus on premium formats.

As The Odyssey continues its theatrical run, all eyes will be on whether it can sustain this momentum and eventually challenge the all-time IMAX record. With extensions already in place and demand showing no signs of slowing, the film is well positioned to keep climbing the charts in the weeks ahead.
