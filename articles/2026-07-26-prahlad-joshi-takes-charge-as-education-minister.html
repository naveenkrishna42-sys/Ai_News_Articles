New Education Minister

Prahlad Joshi has taken charge as the new Education Minister of India, replacing Dharmendra Pradhan who resigned from the position. This change comes amidst widespread youth unrest in the country. Joshi, who has been assigned this new role, stated that education is a new subject for him.

Background

Dharmendra Pradhan's resignation was seen as a significant development, especially given the current youth unrest in India. The government's decision to appoint a new minister is aimed at building trust and addressing the concerns of the youth. Pradhan's resignation was followed by protests at Jantar Mantar, highlighting the discontent among the youth.
