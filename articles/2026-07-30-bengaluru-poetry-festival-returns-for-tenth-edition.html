A Decade of Verse

The Bengaluru Poetry Festival is gearing up for its tenth edition this year. The event, which has grown into a major fixture on India's literary calendar, will return to the city that has nurtured it for a decade.

Organisers have confirmed the milestone edition but are yet to announce specific dates and venues. The festival has traditionally drawn poets, spoken-word artists, and audiences from across the country and abroad.

From Small Gathering to City Landmark

What began as a modest gathering has expanded into a multi-day affair featuring readings, workshops, and panel discussions. The festival has hosted some of the most recognised names in contemporary Indian poetry.

Its longevity is a testament to Bengaluru's appetite for literary culture. The city, known for its tech industry and cosmopolitan character, has consistently supported the arts in ways that smaller metros have not.

What to Expect This Year

While the full lineup remains under wraps, past editions have included bilingual sessions, open mic nights, and performances that blend poetry with music. The organisers are expected to retain that mix for the tenth edition.

Details about ticketing and participating poets are likely to be released in the coming weeks. The festival has typically drawn several thousand attendees over its duration.

The Broader Literary Scene

Bengaluru's poetry festival is part of a wider resurgence of interest in the written and spoken word in India. Similar events in other cities have also seen growing attendance in recent years.

For now, the focus remains on the tenth edition. Organisers have indicated they intend to make it a landmark event, though specifics remain scarce.

What happens next: The festival's official dates and venue are expected to be announced within the next month. Those interested in attending should keep an eye on the organisers' official channels for updates.
