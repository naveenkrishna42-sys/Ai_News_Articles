Northants edge Somerset in thriller to reach T20 Blast final

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Northamptonshire won a close T20 Blast semi-final against Somerset at Edgbaston.
  • Captain David Willey played a key role with a gritty defensive performance.
  • Northants will now contest the final on the same day in Birmingham.

Northamptonshire are through to the final of the 2026 Vitality T20 Blast after edging past Somerset in a tense semi-final at Edgbaston on Saturday. The match went down to the wire, with captain David Willey holding his nerve to guide his side home.

The victory sets up a title decider later on the same day in Birmingham. For Somerset, it is another near-miss in a competition they have won only once.

Willey holds the innings together

Chasing a modest target, Northants found themselves in early trouble. Wickets fell at regular intervals, and the required run rate began to climb.

David Willey anchored the chase with a scrappy but vital knock. He rotated the strike and found the boundary when needed, ensuring his team never fell too far behind.

The captain remained unbeaten at the end, leading his side to victory with just a few balls to spare. It was not a polished innings, but it was effective under pressure.

Somerset fight hard but fall short

Somerset had earlier posted a competitive total on a pitch that offered help to the bowlers. Their innings was built around a solid middle-order contribution, but they lost momentum in the final overs.

In the field, Somerset bowlers struck regularly and kept the game alive until the final over. A few dropped catches and missed run-out chances proved costly in the end.

They will rue those missed opportunities. In a knockout game, margins are thin, and Somerset have once again been left to wonder what might have been.

Finals Day drama continues

Edgbaston has seen its share of T20 Blast drama over the years. This semi-final added another chapter to that history.

The pitch offered assistance to both pace and spin, making stroke play difficult. Batters had to work hard for their runs, and the low-scoring contest kept the crowd on edge throughout.

Northants will now prepare for the final later in the evening. They will face the winner of the other semi-final, which was also scheduled for the same day.

The T20 Blast final is set to begin at Edgbaston later tonight. Northamptonshire will hope to carry their winning momentum into the title match.
