CWG 2026: Neeraj Chopra, Rohit, Yashvir advance to javelin throw final

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Neeraj Chopra advanced to the javelin throw final at CWG 2026.
  • Indian teammates Rohit and Yashvir also qualified for the final.
  • Chopra faced tricky wind conditions during qualification.
  • The final is expected to feature a showdown with Pakistan's Arshad Nadeem.

Chopra leads Indian charge into javelin final

India's Neeraj Chopra booked his spot in the javelin throw final at the 2026 Commonwealth Games on Thursday, overcoming gusty winds at the stadium. The Olympic champion delivered a steady performance in qualification, ensuring he remains on course to defend his Commonwealth title.

Chopra's teammates Rohit and Yashvir also made the cut, giving India a strong presence in the final. The trio will now prepare for what promises to be a high-stakes showdown for medals.

Windy conditions test throwers

Reports from the venue noted tricky crosswinds that disrupted several athletes' rhythm. Chopra adjusted his approach mid-competition, a sign of his experience in handling adverse conditions. Coaches later said his technique held up well under pressure.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact distances thrown by all qualifiers, but Chopra's mark was enough to advance comfortably. The focus now shifts to the final, where consistency will be key.

Rivalry with Arshad Nadeem set to resume

The final is likely to feature another chapter in the Chopra-Arshad Nadeem rivalry. Pakistan's Nadeem, a silver medallist at the Tokyo Olympics, also qualified. Their previous encounters have produced some of the most thrilling moments in world athletics.

Sri Lanka's Tharanga Pathirage, another strong contender, will join the field. The competition is expected to be intense, with all three athletes capable of throws over 85 metres.

Indian team depth on display

Rohit and Yashvir's qualification highlights India's growing depth in javelin throw. Both athletes have shown steady improvement in recent months, and their presence adds strategic options for the team. A clean sweep on the podium is not out of the question if they perform to their potential.

The javelin final is scheduled for later this week. Fans will be watching closely to see if Chopra can add another gold to his collection, which already includes Olympic and world championship titles.

What to watch next

All eyes will be on the final, where Chopra will aim to overcome Nadeem and Pathirage. A win would cement his legacy as one of the greatest javelin throwers in Commonwealth Games history.
