Jammu and Kashmir Flash Floods: 11 Dead, Several Missing After Cloudburst

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • At least 11 dead, several missing in flash floods and cloudburst in J&K's Poonch district.
  • Rescue operations underway, with NDRF and local teams searching for missing persons.
  • CM Omar Abdullah cut short a Delhi visit to chair a review meeting in Jammu.
  • A man lost eight family members after stepping out to move his SUV during the cloudburst.

At least 11 people have died and several others are missing after flash floods and a cloudburst struck Jammu and Kashmir's Poonch district on Sunday. Rescue operations are underway as authorities scramble to reach affected areas.

The heavy downpour triggered sudden flooding and landslides, sweeping away homes and vehicles. Officials fear the death toll could rise as search teams comb through debris.

Cloudburst Triggers Devastation in Poonch

The cloudburst hit the remote hilly areas of Poonch late on Sunday evening, catching residents off guard. Water and mud cascaded down slopes, burying houses and blocking roads.

In one tragic incident, a local man told reporters he stepped out to move his SUV to safety. When he returned minutes later, eight members of his family were trapped under a wall of mud. Rescue teams have not yet confirmed if any survivors were pulled from that spot.

Rescue Operations Underway

Personnel from the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF), along with state police and local volunteers, are working in challenging terrain. Heavy machinery has been deployed to clear blocked roads and reach isolated hamlets.

Army units stationed in the region have also joined the search. Officials have not yet confirmed the total number of missing persons, but initial reports suggest at least a dozen people remain unaccounted for.

Political Leadership Responds

Chief Minister Omar Abdullah cut short his visit to Delhi and returned to Jammu on Sunday night. He chaired an emergency meeting with senior officials to review the situation and coordinate relief efforts.

The Chief Minister directed the administration to ensure speedy rescue operations and provide immediate medical aid to the injured. He also announced ex-gratia compensation for the families of the deceased, though the amount has not been disclosed.

Weather Warning for Western Himalayas

The India Meteorological Department has issued an alert for the Western Himalayas, warning of dangerous rain spells in the coming week. Several states, including Uttarakhand and Himachal Pradesh, are on high alert for similar flash flood events.

Authorities in Jammu and Kashmir have advised residents in vulnerable areas to stay indoors and avoid riverbanks. The weather office has forecast more heavy rainfall over the next 48 hours, which could hamper rescue efforts.

What to watch: Rescue teams will continue searches through Monday, with the weather playing a decisive role. Officials are expected to release an updated casualty and missing persons count later today.
