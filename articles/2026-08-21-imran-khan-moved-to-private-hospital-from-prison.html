Imran Khan moved to private hospital from prison

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Supreme Court orders transfer of Imran Khan to private hospital
  • Imran Khan's health deteriorates in prison
  • Public reaction to the court order varies

Supreme Court Orders Transfer to Private Hospital

The Pakistani Supreme Court has ordered the transfer of jailed former Prime Minister Imran Khan to a private hospital, citing concerns over his deteriorating health. The decision comes after a petition was filed by Imran Khan's supporters, who argued that prison conditions were not suitable for his medical treatment.

Imran Khan's Health Deteriorates in Prison

Imran Khan has been in prison since April 2023, following his conviction on corruption charges. His health has been a significant concern, with reports indicating that he has been suffering from various ailments, including diabetes and high blood pressure. The transfer to a private hospital is expected to provide him with better medical care.

Public Reaction to the Court Order

The public reaction to the court order has been mixed. While some have praised the decision, arguing that it is necessary for Imran Khan's health, others have criticized it, calling it a petty and shameful move. The transfer has also sparked debates about the conditions of prisons in Pakistan and the need for better medical facilities for inmates.

Imran Khan's Supporters

Imran Khan's supporters have welcomed the court order, arguing that it is a step in the right direction. They have expressed hope that the transfer will allow Imran Khan to receive the medical treatment he needs. However, they have also raised concerns about the safety of Imran Khan during the transfer process.

Critics of the Court Order

Critics of the court order have argued that it is a political move, aimed at undermining Imran Khan's political career. They have questioned the need for the transfer and have expressed concern about the conditions of the private hospital. The critics have also raised questions about the transparency of the court process and the fairness of the decision.

What Happens Next?

The transfer of Imran Khan to a private hospital is expected to be a significant development in his case. It remains to be seen how his health will improve and whether he will be able to continue his political activities. The public reaction to the court order is likely to continue, with debates about the conditions of prisons in Pakistan and the need for better medical facilities for inmates.
