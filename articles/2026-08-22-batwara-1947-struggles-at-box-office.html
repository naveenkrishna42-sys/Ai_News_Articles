Batwara 1947 struggles at box office

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Batwara 1947 earns Rs 1.35 cr on Day 7, sinking further at the box office
  • Awarapan 2 continues to lead the charts
  • Rajkumar Santoshi denies making a propaganda film, blames Sunny Deol's Muslim character for backlash

Batwara 1947 Struggles at Box Office

Sunny Deol's Batwara 1947 has earned Rs 1.35 crore on its seventh day, continuing its downward trend at the box office. The film, which was released on August 14, has yet to cross the Rs 50 crore mark worldwide. Despite its initial buzz, the film has failed to attract a significant audience, leading to a poor box office performance.

Awarapan 2 Leads the Charts

Awarapan 2, another film released recently, continues to lead the charts. The film has been performing well at the box office, earning a significant amount of money. The success of Awarapan 2 can be attributed to its strong marketing and the popularity of its lead actors.

Rajkumar Santoshi Denies Propaganda Charges

Rajkumar Santoshi, the director of Batwara 1947, has denied making a propaganda film. He has stated that the film is a historical drama and not a political statement. Santoshi has blamed the poor reception of the film on the portrayal of a Muslim character by Sunny Deol. He believes that the character has put off some viewers, leading to a lack of interest in the film.

Box Office Performance and Future Prospects

The poor box office performance of Batwara 1947 raises questions about the future of the film. The film has not been able to attract a significant audience, and its earnings are not expected to improve. However, the film's performance on the digital platform and its streaming potential cannot be ignored. It will be interesting to see how the film performs in the long run.
