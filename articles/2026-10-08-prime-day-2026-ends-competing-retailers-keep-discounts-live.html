Prime Day 2026 Concludes: What Shoppers Missed

Amazon’s Prime Day 2026 wrapped up on the night of October 8, delivering the usual surge of flash deals across electronics, home goods and fashion. While the event recorded record‑breaking turnover, many shoppers reported missing out on limited‑stock items due to rapid sell‑outs. The aftermath leaves a gap that competing retailers are eager to fill with their own promotional windows.

Competing Retailers Extend the Sale Momentum

Flipkart launched its Big Saving Days on October 9, running through October 15, while Reliance Digital’s Diwali Dash and Croma’s Festive Offer both began on October 10 and continue until the end of the month. These campaigns mirror Prime Day’s discount depth but add region‑specific incentives such as bank cashback, no‑cost EMI and exchange bonuses.

Electronics: TVs, Laptops and Smartphones

In the TV segment, Flipkart is offering the Samsung Q80B 55‑inch 4K QLED at ₹55,990, down from the usual ₹69,990—a 20 % reduction. Reliance Digital lists the LG UR80 50‑inch 4K LED for ₹38,490, a ₹6,500 cut from the MRP. Both retailers include a one‑year extended warranty at no extra cost.

Laptop deals focus on mid‑range productivity machines. The Dell XPS 13 (2024 model) with Intel Core i5‑1340P appears on Croma for ₹89,990 versus ₹1,09,990 list price, accompanied by a ₹5,000 bank cashback offer. Flipkart’s listing of the Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 (AMD Ryzen 7 7730U) shows a price of ₹56,490 after a ₹13,500 discount.

Smartphone promotions centre on last‑generation flagships. The Apple iPhone 15 (128 GB) is available on Reliance Digital for ₹69,990, down from ₹79,900, with a ₹4,000 exchange bonus. Flipkart lists the Samsung Galaxy S23 (256 GB) at ₹58,990, a ₹11,000 reduction from its launch price, plus a no‑cost EMI option for ten months.

Home & Kitchen Appliances

Refrigerator deals are prominent this week. The Whirlpool 265 L Frost‑Free Double Door (2024 model) sits at ₹22,990 on Croma, a ₹4,000 saving, and includes a free water purifier cartridge. Reliance Digital offers the LG 260 L Smart Inverter Refrigerator for ₹24,490, down ₹3,500, with a five‑year compressor warranty.

In the kitchen segment, mixer‑grinder bundles are attracting attention. Flipkart’s Philips HL7701/00 750 W Mixer Grinder comes with a two‑jar set for ₹3,490, a ₹1,000 discount, and a ₹500 bank cashback. The same retailer lists the Prestige Omega Deluxe Plus 5‑Jar Mixer Grinder at ₹4,990, down from ₹6,490.

Fashion and Personal Care

Fashion retailers are leveraging the post‑Prime Day lull to clear summer stock. Myntra’s End of Reason Sale, running from October 12 to October 20, offers up to 60 % off on branded apparel. A representative example is the Levi’s 501 Original Fit Jeans, priced at ₹2,990 against a MRP of ₹4,990.

Personal‑care bundles are also prominent. Nykaa’s Beauty Bonanza provides a ₹1,499 kit containing a moisturiser, serum and sunscreen from the Lotus Herbals line, a 30 % saving versus buying each item separately. The kit includes free shipping and a ₹200 Nykaa cashback voucher for future purchases.

What Buyers Should Prioritise

Given the overlapping timelines, shoppers can maximise savings by stacking retailer‑specific bank offers with exchange bonuses. For electronics, checking the warranty extension terms is essential, as some retailers add coverage only when purchased via their EMI plans. In home appliances, verifying the after‑sales service network in your city avoids future inconvenience.

Fashion and personal‑care deals benefit from early‑bird access; many sites provide an additional 5 % discount for app‑only users during the first 48 hours of the sale. Keeping an eye on stock‑level indicators helps avoid the disappointment of sold‑out sizes, especially in high‑demand categories like denim and activewear.
