Bangladesh, India race to save cricket tour amid political tensions

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Foreign ministers of India and Bangladesh are in talks to save the cricket tour.
  • Bangladesh Cricket Board (BCB) has confirmed dates for the Delhi T20Is after BCCI approval.
  • Afghanistan's confirmed T20I series in September adds scheduling pressure.
  • Diplomatic tensions have cast a cloud over the bilateral series.

NEW DELHI: In a bid to rescue the cricketing ties between India and Bangladesh, diplomatic channels are working overtime as political turmoil threatens to derail the much-anticipated tour. Foreign ministers of both nations are reportedly involved in high-level discussions to ensure the series goes ahead as planned.

The development comes amidst growing uncertainty over India's tour of Bangladesh, with the Afghanistan Cricket Board's confirmation of a T20I series in September further complicating the schedule. Sources indicate that the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) has given a green light for the Delhi T20Is, with the Bangladesh Cricket Board (BCB) confirming the dates.

Political backdrop casts a long shadow

The political climate between the two neighbours has been fraught in recent weeks, with diplomatic tensions casting a cloud over the bilateral engagement. While neither government has officially commented on the specifics of the talks, the involvement of foreign ministers underscores the significance of the tour beyond just sport.

Cricket has historically been a bridge between India and Bangladesh, fostering goodwill and people-to-people connections. The current uncertainty has left fans on both sides of the border anxious, with social media buzzing with speculation about the fate of the series.

Scheduling puzzle and BCCI's balancing act

The confirmation of Afghanistan's T20I series in September has added a layer of complexity to an already tight international calendar. The BCCI, known for its meticulous planning, is now faced with the challenge of accommodating both series without compromising player workload or broadcast commitments.

According to Cricbuzz, the ACB has confirmed dates for their T20I assignment, which directly overlaps with the proposed window for the Bangladesh tour. This has prompted the BCCI to accelerate its decision-making process, with the Delhi T20Is being a key component of the bilateral series.

The BCB, for its part, remains "hopeful" about the tour proceeding. Board officials have expressed optimism that the diplomatic efforts will yield a positive outcome, allowing the series to be played as originally scheduled.

What's at stake

For Bangladesh, hosting India is not just about cricket; it is a matter of national pride and economic benefit. The tour brings in significant revenue from broadcasting rights, sponsorships, and tourism. For India, it is an opportunity to strengthen ties with a neighbour that has often looked up to its cricketing prowess.

The series also holds importance for the global cricket calendar, with both teams looking to fine-tune their preparations ahead of major tournaments. A cancellation would not only disrupt schedules but also send a negative signal about the state of bilateral relations.

The road ahead

As the foreign ministers continue their talks, the cricketing boards are preparing for multiple scenarios. The BCCI has reportedly kept contingency plans ready, including the possibility of rescheduling the tour to a later date or moving some matches to neutral venues.

The coming days are crucial. With the Afghanistan series looming and the international calendar packed, a final decision is expected soon. For now, fans can only wait and hope that the spirit of cricket prevails over political differences.
