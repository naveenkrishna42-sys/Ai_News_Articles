Akshay Kumar to play Rajesh Khanna in R D Burman biopic by Farhan Akhtar

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Akshay Kumar to play Rajesh Khanna in R D Burman biopic
  • Farhan Akhtar produces; Neeraj Pandey directs
  • Emraan Hashmi denies reports of playing Mehmood

Mumbai: A biopic on legendary composer R D Burman has been in the works for some time, and now reports suggest that Akshay Kumar is set to step into the shoes of Rajesh Khanna, the superstar who defined an era of Hindi cinema. The film, backed by Farhan Akhtar and directed by Neeraj Pandey, has been the subject of intense speculation in the industry.

The casting buzz

According to a report by Bollywood Hungama, Kumar will essay the role of Rajesh Khanna, whose on-screen chemistry with R D Burman's music created timeless hits. Farhan Akhtar is expected to play the composer himself, often referred to as Pancham Da. The pairing of Akhtar and Kumar, both known for their versatility, has already generated significant interest among fans and trade analysts.

This would mark a reunion of sorts for the two actors, who have previously shared screen space in projects like Zindagi Na Milegi Dobara, though in a different capacity. The biopic is reportedly in its pre-production stage, with casting for other key roles still underway.

Emraan Hashmi's denial

Amid the casting rumours, reports had also suggested that Emraan Hashmi would play comedian-actor Mehmood, a frequent collaborator of R D Burman. However, Hashmi has dismissed these claims outright. Speaking to Telegraph India, the actor said he is not part of the project, putting an end to the speculation.

Social media, particularly in Pakistan, has been abuzz with reactions to Hashmi's denial. Some users have even questioned whether the actor could have done justice to Mehmood's iconic comic timing, though such commentary remains largely speculative.

The legacy of R D Burman

R D Burman, who revolutionised Hindi film music with his innovative use of Western instruments and Indian melodies, remains a towering figure in Indian cinema. His collaborations with Rajesh Khanna produced some of the most memorable songs of the 1970s, including numbers from films like Aradhana and Kati Patang. A biopic on his life has been a long-cherished dream for many in the industry.

Neeraj Pandey, known for his meticulous storytelling in films like Special 26 and Baby, is expected to bring a grounded approach to the narrative. Farhan Akhtar, who has both directed and acted in critically acclaimed films, will produce the project under his banner, Excel Entertainment.

What lies ahead

Neither Akshay Kumar nor the filmmakers have officially confirmed the casting as of yet. Trade insiders suggest that an announcement could come soon, once contracts are finalised and schedules are locked. The film is expected to go on floors later this year, with a release likely in 2027.

As the project moves forward, audiences will be keen to see how the makers balance the personal and professional lives of R D Burman, including his tumultuous relationship with singer Asha Bhosle. For now, the film remains one of the most anticipated biopics in Hindi cinema.

With casting still taking shape, the coming weeks will likely bring more clarity on who else joins the ensemble. The focus remains on getting the details right for a story that spans decades of musical evolution.
