Sreeshankar wins second straight Commonwealth Games long jump silver

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Murali Sreeshankar wins silver in men's long jump, matching his 2022 performance.
  • Jamaica's Tajay Gayle clinches gold with a winning leap.
  • Dilip Mahadu Gavit claims gold with a Games record in his event.

Sreeshankar delivers again on the big stage

Murali Sreeshankar has done it again. The Indian long jumper won his second consecutive silver medal at the Commonwealth Games on Wednesday, matching his podium finish from Birmingham 2022. The 27-year-old from Kerala produced a consistent series of jumps to secure the runner-up spot, though he fell short of the gold mark set by Jamaica's Tajay Gayle.

Sreeshankar's leap was enough to keep him in medal contention throughout the competition. Officials have not yet confirmed his exact distance, but sources indicate it was a credible effort under pressure. The jumper has been dealing with a lingering injury in recent months, making the achievement all the more significant.

Gayle takes gold, Gavit sets record

Tajay Gayle, the Jamaican standout, won the men's long jump gold with a commanding performance. The 2019 world champion showed his class with a jump that none of his rivals could match. For Sreeshankar, the silver represents a validation of his consistency at the highest level of the sport.

In another highlight for India, Dilip Mahadu Gavit claimed a gold medal in his event, setting a new Games record in the process. Gavit's performance has added to India's medal tally, which continues to grow as the championships progress. The 26-year-old from Maharashtra has been in strong form this season.

Overcoming injury and expectation

Sreeshankar's journey to the podium was not straightforward. He has been managing an injury that disrupted his training leading up to the Games. Despite the physical setback, he managed to peak at the right time, delivering when it mattered most. His ability to defend his silver medal speaks to his mental toughness and technical refinement.

The long jumper has often spoken about the pressure of expectations after his breakthrough in 2022. With two Commonwealth Games medals now to his name, he has established himself as one of India's most reliable track and field athletes. His coach and support staff have been cautious about his workload, but Sreeshankar has shown he can compete at the elite level.

What this means for Indian athletics

India's performance in the field events at these Commonwealth Games has been encouraging. Alongside Sreeshankar's silver and Gavit's gold, other athletes have contributed to a strong showing. The depth in jumps and throws is improving, giving Indian athletics a solid foundation for future international meets.

The 2026 edition has seen several national records fall, and Indian athletes have been part of that trend. Sreeshankar's consistency is a template for younger athletes coming through the ranks. He has shown that sustained success is possible with the right support system and mindset.

Looking ahead

Sreeshankar will now shift focus to the upcoming World Championships and the Asian Games. With his injury situation being monitored, his team will aim to keep him in peak condition for the next major events. For now, the silver medal is a testament to his resilience and skill on the Commonwealth stage.
