Mumbai University postpones exams amid heavy rain, fresh dates soon

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mumbai University postponed all exams scheduled for Monday due to heavy rain.
  • Waterlogging and flooding across the city made travel to exam centres unsafe.
  • Fresh examination dates will be announced once weather conditions improve.

Exams called off as rain batters Mumbai

Mumbai University has postponed all examinations scheduled for Monday, July 6, after heavy rain and waterlogging paralysed large parts of the city. The decision was announced in a late-night circular on Sunday, citing safety concerns for students and staff.

Several low-lying areas in Mumbai reported knee-deep water by early Monday morning. The India Meteorological Department (IMD) had issued a red alert for the city, predicting extremely heavy rainfall over the next 48 hours. Authorities have advised residents to stay indoors unless absolutely necessary.

Students advised to stay safe

The university said fresh dates for the postponed exams will be announced in due course. Students have been asked to keep checking the official Mumbai University website for updates. No further details on the revised schedule have been released yet.

Many students had already reached exam centres or were stuck on waterlogged roads when the postponement was announced. Social media was flooded with pleas for help and updates on safe routes. Local authorities have deployed rescue teams in the worst-hit areas.

What triggered the decision

Officials said the decision was taken after reviewing the city's weather forecast and ground reports from various exam centres. With several roads impassable and public transport running at a crawl, holding exams would have put lives at risk.

This is not the first time Mumbai University has had to reschedule exams due to monsoon fury. In previous years too, the city's infrastructure has struggled to cope with heavy downpours, leading to similar disruptions in academic calendars.

Broader impact on city life

Besides the university exams, schools and colleges across Mumbai remained shut on Monday. Many offices asked employees to work from home. Train services on the Central and Harbour lines were affected, with some services suspended. BEST buses were running on limited routes.

The Mumbai civic body has set up control rooms to handle flood-related emergencies. Residents have been urged to avoid wading through flooded streets and to stay away from dangerous structures.

The IMD has forecast that rainfall intensity is likely to reduce from Tuesday evening. However, the city remains on high alert.

Mumbai University will announce the revised exam schedule as soon as weather conditions stabilise. Students are advised to stay tuned to official channels for the latest information.
