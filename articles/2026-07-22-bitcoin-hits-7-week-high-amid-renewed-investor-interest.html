Bitcoin Hits 7-Week High Amid Renewed Investor Interest

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin reached its highest price in seven weeks, crossing key technical resistance.
  • The rally was attributed to increased spot market buying and positive sentiment around potential ETF approvals.
  • Other cryptocurrencies, including Ethereum and Solana, also posted gains.
  • Analysts caution that the move could face resistance near the $30,000 mark.

Bitcoin Breaks Out of Recent Range

Bitcoin climbed to a seven-week high on Wednesday, breaking out of a narrow trading range that had persisted for much of the month. The world's largest cryptocurrency rose past $28,000 during the afternoon session, its highest level since early June.

Trading volumes picked up sharply, with data from CoinMarketCap showing a 40% increase in spot market activity over the past 24 hours. The move caught many short sellers off guard, triggering liquidations worth over $100 million across crypto derivatives exchanges.

What Drove the Rally

Market participants pointed to several factors behind the sudden uptick. Renewed institutional interest was cited by multiple analysts, with reports of large block trades on Coinbase and Binance suggesting accumulation by big buyers.

Sentiment also got a boost from ongoing speculation around a spot Bitcoin ETF approval in the United States. While the Securities and Exchange Commission has yet to make a final decision, recent filings and comments from key officials have kept hopes alive.

Altcoins Follow Suit

Ethereum, the second-largest cryptocurrency by market cap, rose 5% to trade near $1,850. Solana jumped 8%, outperforming the broader market. The rally was broad-based, with most tokens in the top 100 by market cap trading in the green.

DeFi tokens and meme coins also saw gains, though the moves were more muted compared to the leaders. Analysts noted that the rally appeared driven more by spot buying than leveraged speculation, which could lend it more durability.

Resistance Ahead

The $30,000 level now looms as a key psychological resistance for Bitcoin. The cryptocurrency has not closed above that mark since mid-May. Technical analysts say a decisive break above that level could open the door to a test of $32,000.

However, some caution that the move may be running ahead of fundamentals. The macroeconomic backdrop remains uncertain, with the Federal Reserve's interest rate decisions still a wild card for risk assets. Regulatory clarity around crypto in the US and Europe is also pending.

For now, traders are watching to see if Bitcoin can hold above $28,000 in the coming sessions. A failure to do so could see prices slip back into the familiar $25,000-$27,000 range.

What to watch: All eyes are on the SEC's next move regarding spot Bitcoin ETF applications. A green light could provide the catalyst for a sustained rally, while a rejection could dampen sentiment and trigger a pullback.
