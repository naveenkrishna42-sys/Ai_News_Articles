Mohamed Salah agrees €10m-a-year Beşiktaş deal: Report

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mohamed Salah reportedly agrees to a €10m-a-year contract with Beşiktaş.
  • The deal follows his emotional exit from Liverpool this summer.
  • Beşiktaş is set to become Salah's next club, pending official confirmation.

Mohamed Salah has reportedly agreed a three-year contract with Turkish Süper Lig side Beşiktaş, according to multiple reports. The deal is said to be worth €10 million a year, a significant sum but a fraction of his previous earnings at Liverpool.

Salah left Liverpool at the end of his contract this summer after an emotional farewell. The Egyptian forward, who turns 34 this year, becomes a free agent for the first time since his move to Anfield in 2017.

Beşiktaş emerges as frontrunner

Beşiktaş have been linked with Salah for weeks. The Istanbul club sees him as a marquee signing to boost their profile and challenge for the league title. Reports from Turkish media suggest the player's agent has already held talks with club officials.

The move would reunite Salah with a league he knows well. He played for Turkish side Alanyaspor on loan in 2015 before moving to Italy and then England.

Salary details and contract terms

The reported €10m-a-year deal would make Salah one of the highest-paid players in Turkey. However, it is a steep drop from his Liverpool wages, which were around £350,000 a week.

Beşiktaş are believed to have offered a three-year contract with performance bonuses. The club has not confirmed any figures, but sources close to the negotiations say a verbal agreement is in place.

Liverpool exit and emotional farewell

Salah's departure from Liverpool was confirmed earlier this summer after eight trophy-laden years. He scored over 200 goals for the club and won every major honour, including the Premier League and Champions League.

In his final message to fans, Salah thanked the club and supporters for their unwavering support. He said the decision to leave was personal and that he would always hold Liverpool close to his heart.

What this means for Beşiktaş

If the deal goes through, Beşiktaş will add a global superstar to their squad. Salah's arrival would bring massive commercial attention and on-field quality to the Turk Telecom Stadium.

The club has been active in the transfer market this summer. They have already signed several players to strengthen their squad for the upcoming season. Salah would be their biggest signing in years.

Officials have not yet confirmed the deal. The transfer window in Turkey remains open until mid-September, giving both parties time to finalise the paperwork.

What to watch: Official confirmation is expected in the coming days. Salah's medical tests and formal signing could happen as early as next week. Beşiktaş fans are waiting for the club to announce the arrival of their new hero.
