Deepika Padukone and Ranveer Singh's Temple Visit Ignites Baby No. 2 Speculation

Bollywood power couple Deepika Padukone and Ranveer Singh were spotted at Mumbai's renowned Siddhivinayak Temple on Saturday, setting social media abuzz with rumours that they are expecting their second child. The visit, which took place early in the morning, saw the couple seeking blessings amid tight security. Fans quickly interpreted the temple run as a sign of another pregnancy, especially given the couple's previous visit to the same temple before welcoming their daughter, Dua, in September 2024.

While neither Deepika nor Ranveer has officially confirmed any pregnancy news, the speculation gained traction after several media outlets reported the couple's temple visit with headlines suggesting 'Baby No. 2 coming soon'. The couple, known for keeping their personal life private, has not addressed the rumours yet.

Why Siddhivinayak Temple Holds Special Significance

The Siddhivinayak Temple, dedicated to Lord Ganesha, is one of Mumbai's most visited religious sites. It holds particular significance for Bollywood celebrities, many of whom visit before major life events. For Deepika and Ranveer, this temple has been a recurring destination—they visited before their wedding in 2018 and again during Deepika's first pregnancy. This pattern has led fans to believe that history is repeating itself.

In Indian culture, seeking blessings at temples before or during pregnancy is a common tradition. The couple's choice to visit together, dressed in traditional attire, further fueled the narrative. Deepika was seen wearing a loose-fitting kurta, which some fans interpreted as a subtle hint, though no official statement has been made.

Fan Reactions and Social Media Buzz

As soon as pictures and videos from the temple visit surfaced online, Twitter and Instagram exploded with reactions. Hashtags like #DeepVeer and #BabyNo2 trended within hours. Many fans expressed joy and sent congratulatory messages, while others urged the media to respect the couple's privacy until an official announcement.

One fan tweeted, "If Deepika and Ranveer are really expecting again, I'm so happy for them! Dua will be the best big sister." Another wrote, "Let's not assume. They went to temple, that's it. Stop speculating." The mixed reactions highlight the intense public interest in the couple's personal life.

What We Know About Deepika and Ranveer's Family

Deepika Padukone and Ranveer Singh tied the knot in November 2018 at Lake Como, Italy. After five years of marriage, they announced their first pregnancy in February 2024 and welcomed daughter Dua on September 8, 2024. The couple has since been vocal about the joys and challenges of parenthood, often sharing glimpses of their daughter on social media while keeping her face hidden.

If the rumours are true, this would be their second child. However, given their past pattern of announcing pregnancies only after the first trimester, an official confirmation may take time. Fans are advised to wait for an official statement from the couple.

Key Takeaways for Fans and Media

  • Respect Privacy: The couple has not confirmed any pregnancy. Speculation should not be treated as fact.
  • Cultural Context: Temple visits are common for blessings, not necessarily pregnancy announcements.
  • Past Pattern: Deepika and Ranveer announced their first pregnancy only after three months, so any news may come later.
  • Fan Excitement: Social media is buzzing, but official word is awaited.
