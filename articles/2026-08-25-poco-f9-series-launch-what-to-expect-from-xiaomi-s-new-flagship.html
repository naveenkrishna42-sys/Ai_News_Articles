Poco F9 series launch: What to expect from Xiaomi's new flagship

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Poco F9 series global launch confirmed for September 1
  • New design and colors revealed for Poco F9 Pro and F9 Ultra
  • Poco F9 Pro spotted in NBTC certification, revealing key specs

Poco F9 series global launch confirmed

Xiaomi's Poco sub-brand has confirmed the global launch of its F9 series smartphones, set to hit the market on September 1. The Poco F9 Pro and F9 Ultra are expected to be the flagship models in the series, promising top-tier performance and innovative features.

Design and colors revealed

The Poco F9 series has been revealed to feature a new design language, with a focus on sleek aesthetics and premium build quality. The colors for the F9 Pro and F9 Ultra have also been teased, with options including Aurora Green, Black, and White. These colors are expected to give the devices a modern and stylish look.

Poco F9 Pro spotted in NBTC certification

The Poco F9 Pro has been spotted in NBTC certification, revealing some key specifications. The device is expected to feature a 6.7-inch AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate, ensuring smooth and vibrant visuals. The F9 Pro is also rumored to be powered by the latest Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset, promising top-tier performance.

The launch of the Poco F9 series is significant for several reasons. Firstly, it marks Xiaomi's entry into the premium smartphone segment, competing directly with other flagship devices. Secondly, the new design and colors are expected to set a new standard for the brand, appealing to a wider audience. Lastly, the specifications revealed suggest that the Poco F9 series will offer top-tier performance and innovative features, making it a strong contender in the market.

Conclusion
