Vivo S2 5G launched in India with 7,050mAh battery, Dimensity 7360-Turbo

Key points
  • Vivo S2 5G launched in India after six-year hiatus of S-series
  • Packs a 7,050mAh battery and MediaTek Dimensity 7360-Turbo chipset
  • Positioned as a premium mid-range smartphone with 5G support

Vivo resurrects S-series in India

Vivo has officially launched the S2 5G in India, marking the return of its S-series after a six-year gap. The new smartphone brings a hefty 7,050mAh battery and MediaTek's Dimensity 7360-Turbo chipset, signalling a clear push into the premium mid-range segment.

The device was announced through Vivo's India newsroom, and early coverage from tech publications confirms the company is aiming to carve out a niche with battery-first design and performance-focused internals.

Price and availability in India

Vivo has not yet disclosed the full pricing structure for all variants, but the S2 5G is expected to be positioned aggressively. Given the specifications on offer, analysts suggest the phone could slot into the ₹25,000–₹35,000 bracket, though official confirmation is still awaited.

Availability details remain thin. Vivo typically rolls out its premium devices through Flipkart, Amazon, and its own e-store, and the S2 is likely to follow a similar route. Buyers should wait for the company's next announcement for exact dates and offers.

Key specifications at a glance

  • Display: Likely a high-refresh-rate AMOLED panel, though exact size and resolution are unconfirmed
  • Processor: MediaTek Dimensity 7360-Turbo, built for 5G and sustained performance
  • Battery: 7,050mAh, among the largest in its segment
  • Charging: Fast charging support expected, but wattage not officially detailed
  • Camera: Multi-lens rear setup, with details pending official spec sheet
  • Software: Funtouch OS based on Android, version yet to be confirmed

The highlight is clearly the battery. A 7,050mAh cell is rare in this price band, and Vivo appears to be banking on endurance as a key differentiator. The Dimensity 7360-Turbo, meanwhile, is a newer chipset that should handle daily tasks and gaming with ease.

Why the S-series return matters

The S-series was last seen in India around 2020, when the original Vivo S1 made waves with its pop-up camera. Six years later, the lineup is back with a completely different identity — no gimmicks, just raw battery and processing muscle.

This launch also comes at a time when the Indian smartphone market is seeing intense competition in the mid-range, with brands like iQOO, OnePlus, and Realme fighting for attention. Vivo's move suggests it wants a slice of that pie without compromising on its core strengths.

Industry watchers note that the S2's success will depend on how well Vivo prices it against rivals that already offer 5G, big batteries, and fast charging. The company has a reputation for solid build quality and camera tuning, which could tilt the scales in its favour.

What to watch next

Vivo is expected to reveal full specifications, retail pricing, and sale dates in the coming days. Early buyers should also keep an eye on introductory offers and exchange deals, which often sweeten the deal during launch week. The S2 5G's real test will be in how it stacks up in real-world battery tests and daily performance — and that verdict will come soon enough.
