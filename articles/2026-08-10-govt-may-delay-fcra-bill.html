Govt May Delay FCRA Bill

📅 2026-08-10 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Government may delay FCRA bill to woo Opposition parties
  • Christian bodies and Mizoram CM appeal to Home Minister
  • Opposition parties to protest FCRA bill if introduced in Parliament

Government's Strategy

The government may be adopting a slow approach on the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act (FCRA) to gain support from Opposition parties like the DMK and NCP for the delimitation process. This move is seen as an attempt to break the logjam in Parliament, where the Opposition has been protesting against the bill.

The Centre's strategy is crucial as it needs the support of these parties to push through the delimitation process, which is necessary for the redistribution of parliamentary and assembly seats.

Opposition's Stance

Opposition parties have made it clear that they will protest the FCRA bill if it is introduced in Parliament. KC Venugopal, a senior leader, has stated that the Opposition will not support the bill, citing concerns over its impact on non-governmental organizations (NGOs) and civil society groups.

Appeals to Home Minister

Christian bodies and the Mizoram Chief Minister have made synchronous social media appeals to the Home Minister to roll back the FCRA Bill. They have raised concerns over the bill's provisions, which they claim will affect the functioning of NGOs and civil society groups.

Monsoon Session

The Monsoon Session of Parliament has been facing a washout due to the Centre's struggles to break the Opposition logjam. The government has been trying to introduce the FCRA bill, but the Opposition has been resisting it, leading to disruptions in the House.
