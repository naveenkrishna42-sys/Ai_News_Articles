Understanding Transfer Bonuses

What Are Transfer Bonuses?

Transfer bonuses are promotions offered by credit card issuers that allow cardholders to earn additional points or miles when they transfer their existing rewards to a new card. These bonuses can significantly boost the value of your rewards, making them a crucial tool for maximizing your credit card benefits.

How Transfer Bonuses Work

Transfer bonuses typically work by offering a certain number of bonus points or miles for every point or mile you transfer from your old card to your new card. For example, a 40% transfer bonus means you get 40% more points or miles in addition to the points you transfer.

How to Get the Most Out of Transfer Bonuses

Choosing the Right Card

When choosing a card with a transfer bonus, it's important to consider your spending habits and travel preferences. For example, if you frequently fly with a particular airline, you might want to choose a card that offers bonus points or miles for that airline.

Timing Your Transfers

Transfer bonuses are often time-sensitive, so it's important to transfer your points or miles as soon as possible to take advantage of the bonus. Keep an eye on the expiration dates of your rewards and transfer them before they expire.

Combining Bonuses

Some credit card issuers offer additional bonuses when you combine transfer bonuses with other promotions. For example, you might be able to get a bonus on top of the transfer bonus if you meet certain spending requirements. Be sure to check with your credit card issuer to see if there are any additional bonuses available.
