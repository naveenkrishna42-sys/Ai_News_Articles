Indian cargo ship sinks off Yemen after attack; crew of 14 safe

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Indian cargo vessel sank after an attack in Yemeni waters.
  • All 14 crew members on board were rescued safely.
  • The incident has raised concerns about maritime security in the region.

An Indian cargo vessel has sunk in Yemeni waters after coming under attack, but all 14 crew members on board have been rescued, according to officials. The incident occurred on Tuesday, triggering a swift response from maritime authorities.

The ship, whose name has not been officially released, went down following the assault. Details of the attack remain unclear, and officials have not yet confirmed the nature of the strike or the vessel's cargo.

Rescue operation successful

All 14 crew members were pulled to safety, a senior official confirmed. The rescue operation was completed without any reported injuries, though the crew's current location and condition have not been detailed.

The Indian government has been alerted to the incident, and the Ministry of External Affairs is said to be monitoring the situation. No official statement has been issued yet.

Rising threats in the Red Sea

The attack adds to a string of maritime incidents in the region, where commercial shipping has faced heightened risks in recent months. The waters off Yemen have seen increased naval activity and sporadic assaults on vessels, prompting warnings to shipping companies.

Indian authorities have not indicated whether the attack was linked to any specific group. Regional security analysts point to ongoing tensions in the area, but no group has claimed responsibility so far.

Safety protocols under scrutiny

The sinking raises questions about the adequacy of safety measures for Indian merchant vessels operating in conflict-prone zones. Shipping industry experts have repeatedly called for enhanced escorts and real-time tracking systems.

Naval sources suggest that Indian warships deployed in the region were involved in the rescue coordination. The exact timeline of the attack and rescue has not been made public.

Meanwhile, the crew's families have been informed, and arrangements are being made for their repatriation. Officials have assured that all necessary consular support will be provided.

What to watch next

Investigations into the attack are expected to begin soon, with focus on the vessel's route and any prior threat warnings. The incident is likely to be raised in maritime security forums, as India and other nations assess the safety of shipping lanes near Yemen.

Further updates from the Indian government and shipping authorities are awaited, including the vessel's name and the crew's formal statements.
