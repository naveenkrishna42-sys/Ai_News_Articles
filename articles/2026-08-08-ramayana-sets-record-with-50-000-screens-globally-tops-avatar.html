Ramayana sets record with 50,000+ screens globally, tops Avatar

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ramayana gets 50,000+ screens worldwide, a new record for an Indian film
  • Overtakes Avatar in release scale, marking a global milestone
  • Signal of Indian cinema's expanding international footprint

The Indian epic 'Ramayana' has stormed into global cinema history, securing the largest worldwide release ever for an Indian film. With over 50,000 screens across the planet, the movie has overtaken James Cameron's 'Avatar' in terms of sheer scale, a feat that underscores the growing heft of Indian cinema on the international stage.

Trade analysts are calling this a watershed moment. Not long ago, an Indian film releasing in 10,000 screens was considered a triumph. Now, 'Ramayana' has multiplied that figure fivefold, and the numbers are still climbing as distributors in newer territories confirm additional screens.

A Global Rollout Like No Other

The release strategy for 'Ramayana' has been nothing short of aggressive. From North America to Southeast Asia, from the Gulf to Europe, the film has been slotted into prime multiplexes and single-screen theatres alike. In several international markets, it has taken over IMAX and premium large-format screens, a rarity for any non-Hollywood production.

Industry insiders point out that the film's mythological subject matter has a universal appeal, transcending language and cultural barriers. The makers have dubbed the film in multiple languages, including English, Spanish, and Mandarin, to cater to a truly global audience.

Avatar Comparison and Box Office Implications

The comparison with 'Avatar' is more than symbolic. 'Avatar' had set a benchmark for worldwide releases, with around 40,000 screens at its peak. 'Ramayana' has now crossed that mark, a first for an Indian film. While screen count does not guarantee box office returns, it does indicate the level of confidence that exhibitors have in the film's drawing power.

Early estimates suggest that 'Ramayana' is off to a flying start, with packed shows in major metros and strong advance bookings in overseas circuits. The film's opening weekend numbers are expected to be historic, potentially rewriting the record books for Indian cinema.

What This Means for Indian Cinema

This milestone is not just about one film; it is a statement about the evolving perception of Indian movies worldwide. For decades, Indian cinema was seen as a niche market, confined to diaspora audiences. That narrative is now changing, with mainstream audiences in non-traditional markets showing genuine interest in Indian storytelling.

Producers and directors are taking note. The success of 'Ramayana' is likely to encourage more ambitious, high-budget productions that can compete on a global scale. It also puts pressure on the exhibition sector to upgrade infrastructure and screen quality to match Hollywood standards.

Challenges Ahead

However, a wide release is only the first step. The film must sustain its momentum over the coming weeks to truly cement its legacy. Competition from other big-ticket releases, both Hollywood and regional Indian films, could pose a challenge. Additionally, piracy remains a concern, as large releases often become targets for illegal streaming within hours of their debut.

The makers have not yet disclosed the exact production budget, but reports suggest it is one of the most expensive Indian films ever made. The stakes are high, and the coming weeks will reveal whether the gamble pays off.

Looking Forward

As 'Ramayana' continues its global march, all eyes will be on its box office collections and audience response in the second weekend. If the film holds steady, it could set a new benchmark for Indian films in overseas markets, paving the way for more such ambitious projects. For now, the record stands: 50,000 screens and counting, a number that will be hard to beat.
