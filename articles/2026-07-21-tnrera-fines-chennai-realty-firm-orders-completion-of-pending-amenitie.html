TNRERA fines Chennai realty firm, orders completion of pending amenities

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • TNRERA imposed a fine on a Chennai real estate firm for not providing promised amenities.
  • The developer has been directed to complete the pending amenities within a stipulated timeframe.
  • The order comes after homebuyers filed complaints about incomplete facilities in the project.

The Tamil Nadu Real Estate Regulatory Authority (TNRERA) has imposed a financial penalty on a Chennai-based real estate developer for failing to deliver amenities promised to homebuyers. The regulator has also directed the firm to complete the pending facilities within a set deadline.

Homebuyers flagged missing facilities

Several homebuyers had approached TNRERA with complaints that the developer had not provided key amenities such as a clubhouse, children's play area, and landscaped gardens. These were part of the project's approved plan and sales agreement.

The buyers argued that despite paying for these facilities in their apartment costs, the developer had abandoned the work midway. They sought intervention to get what they were promised.

Regulator's order and fine

After hearing both sides, TNRERA found the developer in violation of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act. The authority imposed a monetary penalty on the firm, though the exact amount has not been disclosed in the order.

TNRERA has directed the developer to complete the pending amenities within a specified period. Failure to comply could lead to further action, including revocation of the project's registration.

The regulator also warned that any delay beyond the deadline would attract additional penalties.

Impact on homebuyers

Homebuyers who have been waiting for years for the promised facilities expressed relief at the order. Many had moved into the apartments but found the complex incomplete.

Legal experts say this order reinforces the authority of RERA to hold developers accountable. It also sends a signal to other builders that non-compliance will not be tolerated.

The developer has not issued a public statement on the order. It remains unclear if the firm plans to challenge the TNRERA decision in a higher court.

What happens next

TNRERA will monitor the developer's progress on the amenities. If the builder fails to meet the deadline, the regulator could initiate further proceedings, including cancelling the project's registration and imposing heavier fines.
