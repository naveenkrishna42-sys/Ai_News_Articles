Hindustan Zinc stake sale: Govt eyes Rs 5,000 crore

📅 2026-08-24 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hindustan Zinc shares jump 7% in two sessions
  • Government eyes Rs 5,000 crore from stake sale
  • OFS expected in two weeks

Hindustan Zinc Shares Surge on Stake Sale Rumors

Hindustan Zinc shares have surged by 7% over two sessions, with the stock hitting a high of Rs 236. This rally coincides with reports that the government is planning a stake sale in the company, potentially worth Rs 5,000 crore. The market is reacting to the possibility of an Offer for Sale (OFS) by the government, which is expected to be launched in the next two weeks.

Government's Stake Sale Strategy

The government's decision to sell a stake in Hindustan Zinc is part of a broader strategy to raise funds and reduce its debt. The company, a unit of Vedanta Resources, has been underperforming in recent times, and the government sees this stake sale as a way to unlock value and improve the company's financial health.

The government's target for the proposed OFS is 1.5%, which is among the four PSUs in FY27. This stake sale is expected to be a part of the government's efforts to monetize its holdings in strategic sectors and generate revenue to fund its various initiatives.

Market Reaction and Analyst Views

Analysts have been closely watching the developments at Hindustan Zinc. HSBC, in a recent report, highlighted why the company has been underperforming and why it could potentially rise by 33% in the future. The report suggested that the company's focus on cost-cutting and efficiency improvements could drive its growth in the long term.

The market reaction to the stake sale rumors has been positive, with Hindustan Zinc shares rising for the third consecutive session. Investors are hopeful that the government's intervention will lead to a turnaround in the company's performance and drive its stock price higher.
