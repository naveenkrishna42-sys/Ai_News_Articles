Kangana Ranaut vs CJP: Actor hits back after 'Generation Gutter' remark sparks row

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kangana Ranaut has responded sharply to CJP leader Saurav Das's criticism of her 'Generation Gutter' remark targeting Gen Z protesters.
  • Rakhi Sawant has slammed Kangana, calling her 'gutter mouth' and 'witch' over the same issue.
  • CJP had earlier accused Kangana of doing little for the country and claimed even the BJP does not take her seriously.

The war of words between actor Kangana Ranaut and the Chandigarh Joint Forum (CJP) intensified on Wednesday, with Kangana firing back at CJP leader Saurav Das after he condemned her recent 'Generation Gutter' remark.

Das had earlier called out Kangana for her comments targeting Gen Z protesters, accusing her of speaking from a place of privilege. He also took a swipe at the actor, claiming that even her own party, the BJP, does not take her seriously.

Rakhi Sawant joins the fray

Adding fuel to the fire, entertainer Rakhi Sawant also weighed in, launching a blistering attack on Kangana. Sawant referred to Kangana as a 'gutter mouth' and a 'witch', directly opposing the actor's characterisation of the younger generation.

Sawant's comments come amid growing backlash against Kangana for her remarks, which many have interpreted as an insult to young activists involved in recent protests.

The 'Generation Gutter' controversy

The controversy began when Kangana, in a social media post, referred to the current generation of protesters as 'Generation Gutter'. She accused them of spreading 'filth' and questioned, 'who is birthing them?'

The comment drew immediate ire from CJP and other youth organisations. Saurav Das responded by stating that Gen Z has done more for the country than Kangana ever has, and accused her of being disconnected from ground realities.

Kangana hits back

Kangana did not take the criticism lying down. In her latest retort, she targeted Das personally, though the exact content of her response has not been fully detailed in available reports. Sources suggest she questioned his credentials and accused him of being part of a coordinated attack against her.

The actor has a history of engaging in political and social commentary, often drawing sharp reactions from both supporters and detractors. Her remarks have previously landed her in legal and public relations trouble.

Political undertones

The spat has also taken a political turn, with Das claiming that even the BJP, the party Kangana is associated with, does not take her seriously. Kangana has dismissed this, maintaining that her views are independent and not dictated by any political affiliation.

The debate highlights the growing divide between established figures and the younger generation, which has become increasingly vocal on issues of governance and social justice.

What remains to be seen is whether this war of words will escalate further or fizzle out, as both sides continue to trade barbs on social media.
