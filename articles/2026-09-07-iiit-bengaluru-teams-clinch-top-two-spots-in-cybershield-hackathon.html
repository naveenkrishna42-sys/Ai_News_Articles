CyberShield Hackathon: A Brief Overview

Organised by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) in partnership with leading security firms, the CyberShield Hackathon ran from 12‑18 August 2026. Over 1,200 participants from 85 Indian colleges tackled real‑world attack scenarios ranging from ransomware containment to AI‑driven phishing detection.

Prize money totaled INR 30 lakh, with additional mentorship and incubation opportunities for the top three teams. The event’s rigorous judging panel included senior engineers from Palo Alto Networks, KPMG, and the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT‑IN).

IIIT Bengaluru’s Winning Teams

1. Team "Sentinel" – First Place

Comprising final‑year students Arjun Rao, Priya Nair and Karan Mehta, Team Sentinel built a hybrid AI‑ML framework that identifies zero‑day exploits in containerized environments within seconds. Their prototype integrated OpenTelemetry logs with a lightweight neural net, achieving a 94% detection rate on the test suite.

2. Team "Aegis" – Runner‑up

Team Aegis, led by Ananya Sharma, focused on cloud‑native threat hunting. Their solution leveraged server‑less functions to correlate IAM anomalies across multi‑cloud accounts, cutting incident response time by 68% compared to industry baselines.

Technical Merit and Real‑World Implications

Both solutions addressed gaps that enterprises face daily. Sentinel’s rapid exploit detection aligns with the growing need for edge‑centric security in 5G deployments, while Aegis’s IAM analytics cater to the surge in hybrid cloud adoption among Indian SMEs.

Industry observers note that the prototypes are ready for pilot testing. Several startups approached the teams for joint development, offering seed funding ranging from INR 5‑10 lakh.
