Wipro Consumer, Murugappa Group in Race to Buy Double Horse

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Wipro Consumer Care and Murugappa Group are among the frontrunners to acquire Double Horse.
  • Double Horse is a well-known Kerala-based brand in the spices and food products segment.
  • The deal is estimated to be valued at approximately Rs 500 crore.

Race Heats Up for Kerala's Spice Giant

Wipro Consumer Care and the Murugappa Group have emerged as the leading bidders for Double Horse, a popular Kerala-based spices and food products brand. The acquisition, if it goes through, could be worth around Rs 500 crore, according to people familiar with the matter.

Double Horse is a household name in South India, particularly in Kerala, where it dominates the market for spice powders, curry powders, and ready-to-use food mixes. The brand, owned by the Puthiyangadi family, has been looking for a strategic buyer to scale up operations and expand its reach beyond its stronghold.

Why the Brand Attracts Big Players

The interest from consumer goods heavyweights like Wipro and the diversified Murugappa Group underscores the growing appetite for regional food brands in India. These companies see Double Horse as a gateway to deepen their presence in the high-growth South Indian market, where branded spices are seeing a surge in demand.

Wipro Consumer Care already has a portfolio that includes brands like Santoor and Yardley. The Murugappa Group, through its food division, owns brands like MTR and Eastern. Acquiring Double Horse would give either company a strong foothold in the Kerala market and access to the brand's established distribution network.

Valuation and Deal Terms

Sources close to the negotiations indicate that the deal is likely to value Double Horse at Rs 450 to Rs 550 crore. The final valuation will depend on the outcome of due diligence and the terms of the transaction, which could involve a full buyout or a majority stake purchase.

Both Wipro and Murugappa have the financial muscle to close the deal, and the competition between them could push the price upwards. The Puthiyangadi family, which has run Double Horse for decades, is expected to retain some operational role for a transition period, though this has not been confirmed.

What This Means for the Spices Market

The consolidation in India's spices market is accelerating. Large corporations are snapping up regional brands to gain scale and leverage their distribution and marketing capabilities. For Double Horse, a sale would provide the capital needed to modernize its manufacturing facilities and launch new product lines.

Industry experts point out that the brand's strong recall in Kerala is a key asset. Unlike many smaller players, Double Horse has invested in quality and packaging, which has helped it compete against national brands like MDH and Everest. A deep-pocketed owner could help it expand into neighboring states like Tamil Nadu and Karnataka.

Officials at Wipro Consumer Care and Murugappa Group declined to comment on the ongoing talks. The Double Horse management has not publicly responded to queries about the sale process.

The next few weeks are expected to see clarity on the winner of this bidding race, as due diligence progresses and the terms of the deal are finalized. Both parties are keen to wrap up the transaction before the end of the fiscal year.
