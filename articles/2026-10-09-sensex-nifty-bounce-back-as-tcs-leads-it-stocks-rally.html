Market Overview: Sensex and Nifty Rebound

The benchmark indices bounced back on October 9, 2026 after two consecutive sessions of losses. The Sensex regained 618 points to close at 66,420, while the Nifty 50 added 185 points to settle at 19,870. Both indexes posted a gain of roughly 1.2%, moving above the 66,000 and 19,800 psychological thresholds that had acted as resistance in the prior session.

Index Levels and Volume

Trading activity remained robust, with combined volume on the BSE and NSE exceeding 3.2 billion shares. Advance‑decline ratio favoured gainers, indicating broad‑based buying interest beyond the IT pack.

IT Sector Rally: TCS at the Forefront

Tata Consultancy Services led the IT basket, advancing more than 2% to hit ₹3,842 per share. The stock’s outperformance was driven by better‑than‑expected quarterly guidance and renewed client spending on digital transformation projects. TCS’s gain lifted the Nifty IT index by 1.8%, marking its strongest single‑day move in the past month.
