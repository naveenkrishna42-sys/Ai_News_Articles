Delhi Police Denies Pellet Gun Use Amidst Mounting Medical Evidence

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Delhi Police denies using pellet guns against protesters.
  • Hospital reports and medical papers show pellet wounds on victims.
  • Family of a third victim alleges excessive force during treatment.

Police Statement on Pellet Guns

Delhi Police have categorically stated that no pellet guns were fired during the recent student protests in the capital. The assertion comes amidst growing scrutiny of law enforcement tactics used to disperse crowds.

Officials maintain that standard crowd control measures were employed, denying the use of non-lethal ammunition that has been controversial in the past.

Medical Evidence Contradicts Claims

However, multiple media reports present a conflicting picture. The Times of India has published an exclusive report based on medical papers and wound photographs, claiming pellet guns were indeed used against protesters in Delhi. India Today has also reported that hospital records show pellet wounds on a journalist covering the student protests.

These reports directly challenge the police version of events, suggesting that the injuries sustained by protesters are consistent with pellet gun impact.

Family Alleges Brutality

Adding to the controversy, the family of a third Delhi victim with pellet injuries has spoken out. Speaking to Alt News, the family alleged that doctors did not treat the student as a patient but as a terrorist. The statement has raised serious questions about the conduct of medical staff in handling protest-related casualties.

The family's account echoes concerns raised by Article-14, which reported that police used 'brute force', including pellet guns and lathis fitted with nails, against protesters.

Official Denials and Demands for Inquiry

Despite the mounting evidence, the Delhi Police has not revised its position. The denial has been met with demands for an independent inquiry into the events. Human rights groups and opposition parties have called for a transparent investigation into the use of force.

Authorities have not yet confirmed whether any internal probe has been initiated into the allegations.

The conflicting narratives between official statements and documented medical evidence have created a tense atmosphere. The next steps will likely involve a closer examination of hospital records and police logs to establish the facts.
