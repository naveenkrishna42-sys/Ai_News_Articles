Centre First Offered to Change Dharmendra Pradhan's Portfolio, CJP Rejected: Sources

📅 2026-07-28 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • The Centre first offered to change Dharmendra Pradhan's portfolio instead of demanding his resignation.
  • The CJP rejected the offer, leading to Pradhan's eventual resignation.
  • Pradhan's resignation has been met with both opposition criticism and CJP celebrations.

The Union government initially offered to change Dharmendra Pradhan's portfolio instead of seeking his resignation, sources have told NDTV. The proposal was rejected by the Caste Justice Party (CJP), which had been demanding his removal from the cabinet.

Portfolio Change Offer Rejected

According to sources familiar with the developments, the Centre approached the CJP leadership with a proposal to reassign Pradhan to a different ministry. The offer was aimed at de-escalating the political crisis that had erupted over Pradhan's handling of his previous portfolio.

The CJP leadership, however, rejected the proposal outright. They insisted on Pradhan's complete removal from the Union Council of Ministers, sources said.

Resignation and Aftermath

Following the CJP's refusal, Pradhan submitted his resignation to Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Monday. The resignation was accepted, ending his tenure in the cabinet.

The development has triggered a war of words between the BJP and opposition parties. The opposition has attacked the BJP for what it called a 'garlanded as if he won a war' felicitation of Pradhan after his resignation.

The Times of India reported that the event saw Pradhan being greeted with garlands and applause, drawing sharp criticism from opposition leaders who termed it 'insensitive' and 'out of touch'.

CJP Protesters Targeted

Meanwhile, reports have emerged of CJP protesters being targeted after Pradhan's exit. India Today reported that protesters were tracked, attacked, and humiliated in the aftermath of the resignation.

The reports have raised concerns about the safety of political activists and the state of democratic dissent in the country. Human rights groups have called for an investigation into the incidents.

Inside the Decision

The News Minute's analysis of the events leading to Pradhan's resignation described it as 'both a win and a warning' for the government. The publication noted that while the government managed to placate the CJP, the episode has exposed fissures within the ruling coalition.

Experts suggest that the resignation could set a precedent for how the government handles similar demands from its allies in the future.

What happens next will depend on how the BJP manages its relationship with the CJP and whether the opposition can capitalise on the political fallout.
