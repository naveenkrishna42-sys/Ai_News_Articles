Lava Cuts Agni 4, Bold N2 Prices During Amazon Great Freedom Festival Sale

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Lava has announced price cuts on Agni 4 and Bold N2 series during Amazon Great Freedom Festival Sale.
  • The discounts are part of Amazon's larger sale event, which also features offers on other smartphones and gadgets.
  • The move is expected to intensify competition in the budget and mid-range smartphone segment in India.

Lava joins Amazon's festive sale with price cuts

Lava International has slashed prices on its Agni 4 and Bold N2 series as part of the Amazon Great Freedom Festival Sale, which went live on Friday. The company is looking to attract buyers during one of the biggest online shopping events of the year.

The discounted models include the Agni 4, a mid-range 5G smartphone, and the Bold N2 series, which targets the budget segment. While the exact new prices were not immediately disclosed, the sale is expected to run for several days, giving consumers a window to grab the deals.

What the sale offers

Amazon's Great Freedom Festival Sale, now in its 2026 edition, is offering up to 80 per cent off on top products across categories. Smartphones, tablets, headphones, and smart home devices are among the key items on discount.

Other brands, including Dreame, Sennheiser, and Nothing, have also announced offers on their products during the sale. The event has become a major shopping occasion for Indian consumers, with e-commerce platforms competing aggressively on pricing.

Competition heats up in the smartphone market

Lava's move comes as the Indian smartphone market sees intense competition, particularly in the sub-Rs 20,000 segment. The Agni 4, launched earlier this year, was positioned as a feature-rich 5G option, while the Bold N2 series targets first-time smartphone users.

Price reductions during festive sales have become a standard strategy for brands to clear inventory and boost volumes. For Lava, which has been working to regain market share against Chinese rivals, such offers are crucial.

Other big-ticket discounts

Apart from Lava, Amazon has dropped prices on flagship devices like the Galaxy Fold 8, OnePlus 15, and certain iPhone models, as noted by tech media. The sale also includes offers on accessories and smart home gadgets.

Consumers can expect additional bank discounts and exchange offers, which could further lower effective prices. However, availability of specific models may vary by region.

What to watch

Shoppers should keep an eye on Lava's official store on Amazon for the updated price list of the Agni 4 and Bold N2 series. The sale is likely to continue for a few more days, and stock may run out quickly for popular variants.

With the festive season approaching, more such offers are expected from other brands as well. The coming weeks will show whether these price cuts translate into higher sales for Lava in a crowded market.
