Prolonged laptop use linked to spine problems: Doctor warns

An orthopedic surgeon has raised concerns over the increasing number of patients suffering from spine problems due to prolonged laptop use. The surgeon, who spoke to TIVRA News, explained that the constant looking down at screens can lead to various spinal issues, including back pain and poor posture.

Women and children at higher risk

The surgeon noted that women and children are at a higher risk of developing these problems. Women, who often have longer work hours, and children, who spend a significant amount of time on screens, are more prone to these issues. The surgeon suggested that these groups should take preventive measures to avoid these problems.

Preventive measures

The surgeon recommended several preventive measures to mitigate the risks. These include taking regular breaks from the laptop, maintaining good posture while using the laptop, and using ergonomic furniture. The surgeon also advised against sitting in one position for long periods and suggested using a standing desk or taking short walks during breaks.

Doctors raise concern over screen time

Doctors across the country have raised concerns over the increasing screen time, especially among children. They have advised parents to limit their children's screen time and encourage them to engage in physical activities. The doctors also suggested that parents should ensure that their children have a balanced diet and get adequate sleep.

Tech industry responds

The tech industry has also responded to these concerns. Several companies have started producing ergonomic laptops and other accessories that can help reduce the risk of spine problems. Some companies have also introduced features like blue light filters and adjustable stands to make laptops more comfortable to use.
