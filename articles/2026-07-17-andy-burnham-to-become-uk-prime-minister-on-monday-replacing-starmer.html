Andy Burnham to become UK Prime Minister on Monday, replacing Starmer

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Andy Burnham confirmed as leader of UK's governing Labour Party
  • He will take over as Prime Minister from Keir Starmer on Monday
  • Burnham has promised 'big change' but offered few details on delivery

LONDON — Andy Burnham is set to become the next Prime Minister of the United Kingdom on Monday, taking over from Keir Starmer after being confirmed as the leader of the governing Labour Party.

The development was confirmed by multiple British media outlets on Friday, with Burnham now designated as the country’s PM-in-waiting.

Burnham confirmed as Labour leader

Burnham’s elevation comes after a leadership contest within the Labour Party. He has served as the Mayor of Greater Manchester since 2017 and was a prominent figure in the party’s previous governments under Tony Blair and Gordon Brown.

His victory marks a significant shift in the party’s direction. Starmer, who led Labour to a landslide general election victory in 2024, has been Prime Minister for just over two years.

Reports suggest internal party pressure and declining poll numbers may have hastened the transition. No official reason for Starmer’s departure has been given.

'I have a plan,' says Burnham

In his first remarks as PM-designate, Burnham told Sky News: "I have a plan." He struck a confident tone, promising to deliver change for the British public.

Speaking to the BBC, Burnham said he would bring "big change" to the country. However, he offered few clues on how exactly he intends to deliver on that promise, leaving many questions unanswered about his policy agenda.

The Guardian reported that Burnham is expected to announce key cabinet appointments over the weekend, ahead of Monday’s formal handover at Downing Street.

What to expect on Monday

The transition will follow the standard procedure. Burnham will travel to Buckingham Palace to be formally appointed by the monarch. He will then deliver his first speech as Prime Minister from outside 10 Downing Street.

Starmer is expected to tender his resignation to the King earlier in the day. No details have emerged yet on Starmer’s future plans.

Reactions and road ahead

Political analysts in the UK have noted that Burnham inherits a party that, while still in government, faces challenges on the economy, public services, and internal unity. His tenure as Greater Manchester mayor has been praised for his handling of transport and housing issues, but national leadership is a different scale.

The Conservative Party, now in opposition, has already begun attacking Burnham as "more of the same." The Liberal Democrats and other smaller parties have called for a general election, though one is not due until 2029 under UK law.

Burnham’s first major test will come later this month when he faces Prime Minister’s Questions in the House of Commons for the first time.

What happens next: All eyes are on Monday’s handover and the composition of Burnham’s first cabinet, which is expected to signal his immediate priorities.
