R Madhavan on Vijay as CM, says no political ambitions for himself

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • R Madhavan says he addresses actor Vijay as 'CM Vijay' following his election victory.
  • The actor confirmed he has no political ambitions despite Vijay's success.
  • Madhavan spoke about this while promoting his upcoming film 'Dhurandhar'.

Actor R Madhavan has opened up about his friend and fellow actor Vijay's transition from cinema to politics, revealing a small but telling change in how he addresses him now.

Speaking to the media, Madhavan said he now calls Vijay 'CM Vijay' — a mark of respect for the latter's recent electoral victory in Tamil Nadu. The two have shared a long-standing friendship in the film industry, but the dynamics have shifted since Vijay took the political plunge.

'Friend' becomes 'CM'

Madhavan was candid about the adjustment. "I have to call him CM Vijay now," he said, acknowledging the new reality. The remark came during a promotional interaction for his upcoming film 'Dhurandhar'.

The actor did not elaborate on how their personal equation has evolved, but his tone suggested a mix of pride and playful formality. Vijay, who led his party to a decisive win in the state assembly polls, has been formally sworn in as Chief Minister.

No political ambitions for Madhavan

When asked whether Vijay's success has inspired him to consider a similar path, Madhavan was unequivocal. He confirmed he has no political ambitions, drawing a clear line between his friend's journey and his own.

"I don't see myself in politics," he said, without offering further reasoning. The statement puts to rest speculation that the success of actors-turned-politicians might lure more film personalities into the fray.

Madhavan's response comes at a time when several actors across Indian film industries are being courted by political parties. However, he appears to be firmly focused on his craft.

Focus on 'Dhurandhar'

The actor is currently busy promoting 'Dhurandhar', which is set to release soon. The film has been generating buzz, though Madhavan's comments on Vijay have dominated recent headlines.

He did not mix his professional commitments with political commentary, keeping the conversation largely on his work. When pressed about Vijay's performance as Chief Minister, Madhavan chose to stay non-committal, saying only that he wishes his friend well.

Observers note that Madhavan's stance is a contrast to some of his contemporaries who have openly flirted with political ideas. But the actor seems content with his current trajectory.

Industry reactions

The film fraternity has been watching Vijay's political career with interest, and Madhavan's remarks add to the ongoing discourse. Some see his clarity as a refreshing change in an environment where many keep their options open.

Madhavan did not comment on whether he would campaign for Vijay in future elections, nor did he address any specific policies of the new government. His silence on those matters was notable.

For now, the actor appears eager to move on from political questions and return to the subject of his film.

As Vijay settles into his new role, Madhavan's next steps will be watched closely — but his latest statement makes it clear that politics is unlikely to be among them. Fans can expect him to remain in the entertainment space for the foreseeable future.
