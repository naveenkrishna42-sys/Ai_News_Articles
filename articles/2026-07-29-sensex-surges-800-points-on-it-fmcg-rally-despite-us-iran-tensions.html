Sensex Surges 800 Points on IT, FMCG Rally Despite US-Iran Tensions

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex surged nearly 900 points in intraday trade, Nifty climbed above 24,250.
  • IT and FMCG stocks led the rally, with Adani Ports also contributing to gains.
  • The upmove defied fresh geopolitical tensions between the US and Iran.
  • Broader market sentiment remained positive despite caution ahead of Fed policy decisions.

Indian stock markets staged a strong rally on Wednesday, with the Sensex jumping nearly 900 points in intraday trade. The Nifty crossed the 24,250 mark, driven by a broad-based rally in IT and FMCG stocks.

This upmove came even as fresh US-Iran tensions threatened to destabilise global crude oil supplies. Traders shrugged off geopolitical worries, focusing instead on domestic fundamentals and corporate earnings.

IT and FMCG Stocks Lead the Charge

Shares of information technology companies surged, pushing the Nifty IT index higher by over 2 per cent. Heavyweights like Infosys, TCS, and HCL Technologies were among the top gainers.

FMCG stocks also saw strong buying interest. Hindustan Unilever and Nestlé India gained as investors bet on steady demand in rural and urban markets. The rally was broad-based, with 40 of the 50 Nifty stocks trading in the green.

Geopolitical Tensions Take a Back Seat

Markets opened strong despite reports of heightened US-Iran tensions. Oil prices edged higher in global markets, but Indian traders appeared undeterred.

Analysts noted that the domestic market's resilience reflected strong institutional flows and improving corporate earnings outlook. Foreign portfolio investors have been net buyers in recent sessions, adding to the positive sentiment.

Adani Ports and Other Movers

Adani Ports was among the top Nifty gainers, rising over 3 per cent after the company reported strong operational numbers. The stock has been on an uptrend, supported by cargo volume growth.

Banking stocks also contributed to the rally, with HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank gaining about 1 per cent each. However, public sector banks lagged as investors remained cautious about asset quality.

Midcap and smallcap indices outperformed the benchmarks, rising over 1.5 per cent each. The broader market participation indicated strong risk appetite among retail and institutional investors.

Some profit booking emerged in the afternoon, trimming gains slightly. The Sensex was last seen trading 800 points higher, while the Nifty held above 24,200.

Market breadth remained positive, with over 2,000 stocks advancing on the BSE. The rally was supported by a weaker rupee, which boosted export-oriented IT companies.

Investors are now watching for the US Federal Reserve's policy decision due later this week. Any hawkish stance could trigger volatility in emerging markets.
