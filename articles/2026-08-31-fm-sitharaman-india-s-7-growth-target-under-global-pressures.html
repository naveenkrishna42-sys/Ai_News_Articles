FM Sitharaman: India's 7% growth target under global pressures

📅 2026-08-31 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • India's economy expected to grow 7% in FY 2026-27 despite global challenges
  • Domestic demand and infrastructure projects to drive growth
  • Global uncertainties may impact growth but India's resilience is expected

Government Maintains 7% Growth Forecast Despite Global Headwinds

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman has reaffirmed India's economic growth target of 7% for the fiscal year 2026-27, despite growing global uncertainties. This forecast comes as the country continues to navigate challenges like the US-Iran war and other geopolitical tensions that could impact global trade and investment.

Domestic Demand and Infrastructure to Drive Growth

Sitharaman emphasized that India's domestic demand and infrastructure projects will be the key drivers of economic growth. The government has been aggressively investing in infrastructure, with significant projects in roads, railways, and digital connectivity.

The recent data shows that India's economy grew between 7-7.5% in the April-June quarter, with SBI research expecting a growth rate of 8%. This growth is expected to continue, supported by strong domestic demand and robust infrastructure development.

Global Uncertainties May Impact Growth

However, global uncertainties pose significant challenges to India's economic growth. The US-Iran war and other geopolitical tensions could impact global trade and investment, which in turn could affect India's exports and foreign direct investment.

Despite these challenges, Sitharaman expressed confidence in India's ability to sustain its growth trajectory. The government has been taking measures to mitigate the impact of global uncertainties, such as diversifying trade routes and strengthening supply chains.

India's Economic Resilience

India's economic resilience is a key factor in maintaining the 7% growth forecast. The country has a diverse economy with strengths in manufacturing, services, and agriculture. This diversity helps to cushion the impact of global shocks and ensures that the economy remains stable.

The government has also been focusing on improving the business environment, with initiatives like the production-linked incentive scheme and the ease of doing business reforms. These measures are expected to boost investment and further drive economic growth.
