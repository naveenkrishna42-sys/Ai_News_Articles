Sensex surges 750 points, Nifty crosses 24,250; here's what drove the rally

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex surged over 750 points in Friday's trade.
  • Nifty50 index crossed the 24,250 mark during the session.
  • Rally driven by positive global cues and strong buying in banking and IT stocks.

Markets open strong, extend gains through the day

Indian equity benchmarks opened sharply higher on Friday and maintained momentum through the session. The BSE Sensex climbed more than 750 points, while the NSE Nifty50 breached the 24,250 level for the first time in recent weeks.

Broader market indices also traded in the green, reflecting broad-based buying interest. The rally was led by banking, financial services, and information technology stocks.

Global cues provide tailwind

Positive signals from global markets set the tone early. Overnight gains on Wall Street, driven by easing concerns over US interest rate hikes, boosted investor sentiment across Asia. European markets also opened higher, adding to the optimism.

Analysts pointed to a softer-than-expected US inflation reading earlier this week as a key catalyst. This has raised hopes that the Federal Reserve may pause or slow its rate tightening cycle, which typically benefits emerging markets like India.

FII buying returns, domestic support remains

Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) have turned net buyers in the cash market over the past few sessions. Provisional data from the exchanges showed continued FII purchases on Friday, reversing a trend of outflows seen earlier this month.

Domestic institutional investors (DIIs) also remained active, providing additional support. The combination of FII and DII buying created a strong upward push for frontline indices.

Banking and IT stocks lead the charge

The banking sector was among the top contributors to the rally. Heavyweights like HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and State Bank of India recorded solid gains. The Nifty Bank index rose over 2 percent during the day.

IT stocks also attracted strong buying interest, with Infosys, TCS, and Wipro trading higher. The sector has been buoyed by expectations of steady demand from US clients and a weaker rupee, which boosts export earnings.

Technical factors and market breadth

Technical analysts noted that the Nifty50 broke above key resistance levels, triggering short-covering by traders. The index had been consolidating in a range for several sessions before Friday's breakout.

Market breadth was positive, with more than two-thirds of stocks on the BSE advancing. The rally was not limited to large-caps; mid-cap and small-cap indices also gained, indicating broad participation.

Volatility, as measured by the India VIX, eased during the session, reflecting reduced anxiety among traders. A lower VIX typically supports further upside in the market.

Investors will now watch for any fresh domestic or global triggers next week. The focus is likely to remain on corporate earnings, monsoon progress, and cues from global central banks.
