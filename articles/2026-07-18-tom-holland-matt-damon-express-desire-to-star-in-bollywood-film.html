Tom Holland, Matt Damon express desire to star in Bollywood film

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tom Holland and Matt Damon expressed admiration for Indian cinema.
  • Both actors said they would love to star in a Bollywood film.
  • Their remarks come amid growing global interest in Indian films.

Hollywood stars open up about Indian cinema

Tom Holland and Matt Damon have joined a growing list of international actors expressing admiration for Indian cinema. In a recent conversation, both stars shared their thoughts on Bollywood and revealed a strong desire to work in the industry.

Damon, known for films like Good Will Hunting and the Bourne series, said he would jump at the opportunity to star in a Bollywood production. Holland, best known for playing Spider-Man, echoed the sentiment, calling Indian films vibrant and emotionally powerful.

What they said about Bollywood

During the interaction, Damon reportedly said that Indian cinema has a unique energy and storytelling style that appeals to global audiences. He added that he has watched several Indian films and finds them deeply engaging.

Holland, on his part, said he is fascinated by the scale and passion of Bollywood movies. “I would jump at the chance to be part of one,” he said, according to the source.

The actors did not specify any particular director or project they are eyeing. However, their remarks have sparked speculation about possible cross-continental collaborations.

Growing global interest in Indian films

Hollywood stars showing interest in Indian cinema is not new. In recent years, actors like Will Smith, Chris Pratt, and Zendaya have praised Bollywood films. Some, like Priyanka Chopra and Deepika Padukone, have successfully worked in both industries.

Holland and Damon’s comments come at a time when Indian films are gaining more international recognition. Movies like RRR and Pathaan have drawn global audiences, and streaming platforms have made Indian content accessible worldwide.

Industry insiders say such endorsements from major Hollywood stars could open doors for more collaborations. However, no official announcements have been made regarding any projects involving Holland or Damon in Bollywood.

What this means for fans

For Indian audiences, the idea of seeing Tom Holland or Matt Damon in a Bollywood film is exciting. Both actors have massive fan followings in India, and their involvement could bring more international attention to the industry.

Bollywood producers have not yet commented on the remarks. But given the stars’ enthusiasm, it would not be surprising if offers come their way soon.

Whether this leads to actual casting remains to be seen. For now, fans can only wait and hope that Hollywood’s friendly overtures turn into something real on screen.
