SS Rajamouli Shares New Priyanka Chopra Images From 'Varanasi' on Her Birthday

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • SS Rajamouli shared new images of Priyanka Chopra Jonas from his film 'Varanasi' on her birthday.
  • Rajamouli described Chopra as having 'grace' and 'fire' in the post.
  • The film also stars Mahesh Babu and Prithviraj Sukumaran.
  • Chopra's look from the film has generated significant buzz online.

Birthday Surprise From Rajamouli

Filmmaker S.S. Rajamouli marked Priyanka Chopra Jonas' birthday on Saturday by releasing fresh images from his upcoming film 'Varanasi'. The director took to social media to share the stills, offering fans a new look at the actor in what is being described as one of his most ambitious projects.

Rajamouli's post described Chopra as embodying both 'grace' and 'fire'. The choice of words has sparked speculation about the character she will play in the film, though the director has kept plot details closely guarded.

A High-Stakes Collaboration

'Varanasi' brings together a formidable cast. Alongside Chopra, the film stars Telugu superstar Mahesh Babu and Malayalam actor Prithviraj Sukumaran. Industry insiders have called this Rajamouli's biggest gamble yet, given the scale of production and the ensemble cast drawn from across Indian cinema.

Reports suggest that Chopra's role required extensive preparation, though the actress has not publicly detailed her process. The images released show her in a striking, culturally rich aesthetic, hinting at a narrative deeply rooted in Indian traditions.

Social Media Buzz

The birthday post was quickly picked up by fans and media outlets. Mahesh Babu and Prithviraj Sukumaran also joined in wishing Chopra, amplifying the excitement around the project. The hashtag #Varanasi trended briefly on Indian Twitter as fans dissected the new images.

Chopra herself responded to the post, thanking Rajamouli and the team. The actor has been away from Indian film sets for several years, making her return in a Rajamouli film a major event for the industry.

What We Know About 'Varanasi'

Rajamouli has not revealed a release date for 'Varanasi'. The film is believed to be in advanced stages of production. Given the director's track record with 'Baahubali' and 'RRR', expectations are sky-high.

The title itself suggests the holy city of Varanasi could play a central role in the story, though no official synopsis has been released. The images released so far show Chopra in what appears to be traditional attire, set against ancient architecture.

Industry watchers are keenly waiting for a teaser or trailer. Rajamouli is known for keeping plot details under wraps until close to release, building anticipation through carefully timed reveals.

The coming weeks are likely to bring more updates from the 'Varanasi' team, as the film moves toward a potential 2027 release.
