History-sheeter shot in leg during escape attempt in Chennai

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Chennai police shot a history-sheeter in the leg during an alleged escape attempt.
  • The accused has multiple criminal cases registered against him.
  • He was taken to a government hospital for treatment, and a probe is underway.

Incident at a busy junction

Chennai police shot a history-sheeter in the leg on Monday after he allegedly tried to flee from custody. The incident occurred at a busy junction in the city, causing a brief commotion among commuters.

The accused, identified as a man with multiple criminal cases, was being escorted by a police team when he attempted to escape. Officials said he was shot in the leg after he ignored repeated warnings to stop.

Police version of events

According to police sources, the history-sheeter was being taken for a routine procedure when he suddenly broke free and ran. Officers chased him and fired a warning shot, but he did not stop. The second shot hit him in the leg, bringing him down.

“He has a long record of violent crimes and was considered a high-risk detainee,” a police officer said, requesting anonymity. “Our team acted to prevent him from escaping and endangering the public.”

Hospitalisation and legal process

The accused was immediately rushed to a government hospital, where doctors confirmed a bullet wound in his leg. His condition is stated to be stable. Police have registered a case and are investigating the circumstances of the escape attempt.

Senior officers have ordered a departmental inquiry into the shooting, standard procedure in such cases. The history-sheeter will be produced before a court once he is discharged.

History-sheeter’s criminal record

The man has been involved in several cases, including robbery, assault and illegal possession of weapons. He was arrested earlier this year and had been in custody since. His name appears in the police records as a habitual offender.

Residents in the area where the incident took place reported hearing gunshots. Many gathered at the spot after the police cordoned off the area. No bystander was injured.

Police have not yet released the man’s name, pending family notification.

What happens next

The departmental inquiry will determine if the officers followed protocol. The accused will remain under police guard at the hospital. A magisterial probe into the shooting may also be ordered, depending on the findings of the preliminary investigation.
