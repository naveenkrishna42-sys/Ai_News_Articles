Ashmita Chaliha wins Korea Masters; Basant bags U20 high jump silver

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ashmita Chaliha defeated China's Han Qianxi to win the Korea Masters women's singles title.
  • This is Chaliha's maiden BWF World Tour title.
  • Basant secured a silver medal in high jump at the World U20 Championships.

Historic win for Indian badminton

India's Ashmita Chaliha claimed her first BWF World Tour title at the Korea Masters on Sunday, defeating China's Han Qianxi in the women's singles final. The victory marks a significant milestone in the young shuttler's career, as she capped off a strong week in the Korean city with a composed performance under pressure.

Chaliha, who had reached her maiden World Tour final just a day earlier, carried that momentum into the title clash. She handled the Chinese opponent's pace well, mixing aggressive smashes with clever drops to keep the rallies in her control. The win adds to a growing list of Indian success on the international circuit this season.

Basant shines at World U20 Championships

Meanwhile, in the athletics arena, India's Basant secured a silver medal in the high jump at the World U20 Championships. The young jumper cleared a credible height to finish second on the podium, marking one of India's best results in the event at this level.

Basant's consistency throughout the competition stood out, as he cleared early heights without fuss before engaging in a tense battle for the top spot. Though the gold remained elusive, the silver is a strong statement of intent for the upcoming senior competitions.

Mixed day for Indian sports

Sunday's results brought a double dose of cheer for Indian sports fans. While badminton celebrated a new champion, athletics added another medal to the country's junior tally. The dual success highlights the depth emerging in both disciplines.

For Chaliha, the Korea Masters title will likely boost her ranking and confidence ahead of a busy season. For Basant, the World U20 silver provides a springboard for future international exposure. Coaches and selectors will be keen to see how both athletes build on these achievements.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact margin of victory in the badminton final or the precise height cleared by Basant. However, the results have already been widely reported by Indian media, including ESPN and The Times of India.

What lies ahead

Attention now shifts to the upcoming tournaments on the BWF calendar and the remainder of the athletics season. Chaliha will look to convert this maiden title into consistent performances, while Basant aims to push higher in domestic and international meets.

The coming weeks will reveal whether these breakthroughs translate into sustained success. For now, Indian sports can savour a day of two podium finishes.
