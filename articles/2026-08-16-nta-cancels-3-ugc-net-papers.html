NTA Cancels 3 UGC-NET Papers

📅 2026-08-16 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • NTA cancels 3 UGC-NET papers due to errors
  • Re-exams to be held for English, Sociology, and Commerce
  • Answer keys for 84 subjects released

NTA Admits Errors, Orders Fresh NET for 3 Subjects

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has cancelled three University Grants Commission National Eligibility Test (UGC-NET) papers due to errors in the question papers. The agency has admitted to mistakes and ordered a fresh exam for the affected subjects.

According to reports, several typos and errors were found in the question papers, including 'Putzer for Ritzer' and 'Parsow for Parsons'. The errors prompted the NTA to cancel the exams for English, Sociology, and Commerce.

Re-Exam Dates Announced

The NTA has announced the dates for the re-exams, which will be held for the three subjects. The agency has also released the answer keys for 84 subjects.

The decision to cancel the exams and hold re-exams has been taken after complaints were received from candidates about the errors in the question papers. The NTA has taken responsibility for the mistakes and has assured that the re-exams will be conducted fairly.

What Went Wrong

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact nature of the errors, but it is believed that a combination of human and technical errors led to the mistakes in the question papers. The NTA has said that it will conduct an inquiry into the matter and take steps to prevent such errors in the future.

Next Steps

Candidates who appeared for the cancelled exams will be notified about the re-exam dates and other details. The NTA has advised candidates to check their website for updates on the re-exams.

What to Watch

The NTA will conduct the re-exams for the three subjects in the coming days. Candidates who appeared for the cancelled exams will be eagerly waiting for the results, which will be declared after the re-exams are held.
