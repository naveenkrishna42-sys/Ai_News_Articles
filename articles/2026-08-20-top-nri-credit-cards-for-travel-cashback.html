Best NRI Credit Cards for Travel and Cashback

If you're an NRI looking for credit cards that offer travel rewards and cashback, you're in the right place. We've compiled a list of the best NRI credit cards that can help you save money and enjoy your travels.

Full Features and Standout Perks

The best NRI credit cards come with a range of features and perks designed to enhance your travel experience and save you money. These can include:

  • Air miles and hotel points
  • Cashback on international purchases
  • Lounge access at airports
  • Insurance coverage for travel emergencies
  • Discounts on travel-related services

Joining and Annual Fees

Most NRI credit cards have joining fees that range from Rs. 500 to Rs. 5,000. Annual fees vary, with some cards waiving the annual fee for the first year. It's important to compare the fees and benefits to find the best deal for your needs.

Reward and Cashback Rates

Reward and cashback rates vary depending on the card and the type of purchase. For example, some cards offer 5% cashback on international flights, while others offer 2% cashback on all purchases. Make sure to check the reward rates before applying for a card.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for an NRI credit card, you typically need to have a valid passport, a bank account, and a steady source of income. Some banks may also require proof of address and employment. Make sure to check the eligibility criteria before applying for a card.

How to Apply Online

Applying for an NRI credit card online is quick and easy. You can typically apply through the bank's website or mobile app. You'll need to provide some personal information, such as your name, address, and passport details. Once your application is approved, you'll receive your card in the mail.

Special Welcome Rewards

Many NRI credit cards offer special welcome rewards to new cardholders. These rewards can include cashback bonuses, air miles, or hotel points. Make sure to check the welcome rewards before applying for a card.

Final Recommendation

If you're an NRI looking for a credit card that offers travel rewards and cashback, consider applying for one of the best NRI credit cards. Make sure to compare the fees, rewards, and eligibility criteria to find the best deal for your needs. With the right card, you can save money and enjoy your travels.
