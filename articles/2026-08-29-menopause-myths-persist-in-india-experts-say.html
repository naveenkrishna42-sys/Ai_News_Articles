Menopause Myths Persist in India, Experts Say

Menopause, a natural biological process marking the end of menstruation, is often misunderstood in India. A recent survey by ThisIsMenopause reveals that 82% of women associate stress and burnout with menopause, not the condition itself. Experts have debunked common myths, emphasizing the need for medical care.

Common Myths About Menopause

Several myths surround menopause, with many women overlooking it as a medical issue. Here are some of the most common myths:

  • Myth: Menopause is just a natural part of aging and doesn't require medical attention.
  • Myth: Menopause symptoms are just a normal part of aging and can be ignored.
  • Myth: Menopause is only about hot flashes and mood swings.

Expert Opinion

Dr. Priya Sharma, a senior gynecologist at Apollo Hospitals, says, 'Menopause is a medical condition that requires attention. It's not just about hot flashes and mood swings. Women often ignore symptoms like fatigue, sleep disturbances, and weight gain, thinking they are just a part of aging.'

Dr. Anjali Mehta, a reproductive endocrinologist at Max Healthcare, adds, 'Women need to understand that menopause is a medical issue that requires medical care. They should not ignore symptoms and seek medical attention when they notice any changes.'

Survey Findings

A recent survey by ThisIsMenopause reports that for 82% of women, stress and burnout were the first answers when asked about menopause. Only 18% of women associated menopause with the condition itself.

Conclusion

Menopause is a medical condition that requires attention. Women need to understand that menopause symptoms are not just a normal part of aging and can cause significant health issues.
