Mumbai crime branch arrests 12 in ₹500 crore international cyber fraud ring

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • 12 international gang members arrested by Mumbai crime branch
  • Case involves alleged cyber fraud amounting to ₹500 crore
  • Arrests made following a coordinated operation by the crime branch

The Mumbai crime branch has arrested 12 members of an international gang in connection with a cyber fraud case involving an estimated ₹500 crore. The arrests were made on Friday, following a coordinated operation that officials say dismantled a network operating across multiple jurisdictions.

According to preliminary information, the gang is suspected of orchestrating large-scale digital frauds, targeting victims through deceptive online schemes. The exact modus operandi of the racket has not been fully disclosed, but sources indicate that the accused used sophisticated methods to siphon off funds over an extended period.

Coordinated operation leads to arrests

The crime branch executed the arrests after weeks of surveillance and intelligence gathering. Teams were deployed at several locations simultaneously, leading to the apprehension of the accused, who are believed to have been operating from different parts of the country and abroad.

Officials have not yet confirmed the nationalities of all those arrested, but the term “international gang” suggests involvement of foreign nationals or links to overseas networks. The investigation is being handled by a specialised unit of the crime branch, which has been probing the case for several months.

Scale of the fraud

The ₹500 crore figure, if substantiated, would place this among the larger cyber fraud cases tackled by Mumbai police in recent years. Investigators are now tracing the flow of funds, which are suspected to have been routed through multiple bank accounts and digital wallets to obscure their trail.

Documents and electronic devices have been seized from the accused, and a forensic analysis is underway. The police are also working with financial institutions and cyber security experts to map the full extent of the fraudulent activity.

Victims and recovery efforts

Details about the number of victims have not been released so far. However, in cases of this magnitude, the affected individuals often span multiple cities and may include both retail investors and businesses. The crime branch is expected to issue a public notice urging potential victims to come forward.

Recovery of the defrauded amount remains a challenging task, as funds in such operations are frequently moved to offshore accounts or converted into cryptocurrencies. Investigators are coordinating with agencies in other countries to freeze assets linked to the gang.

Previous cases and pattern

Mumbai has witnessed a rise in cyber fraud cases in recent years, with gangs often operating from call centres or using phishing and investment scam templates. This particular case stands out due to the alleged international dimension and the large sum involved.

The arrested individuals are expected to be produced before a local court, where the police will seek their custody for further interrogation. Legal experts note that cases of this nature require careful handling of digital evidence to ensure conviction.

As the investigation progresses, the crime branch is likely to uncover more details about the gang's network, including potential links to other unsolved frauds. The focus now shifts to the court proceedings and the subsequent disclosure of evidence in the coming weeks.
