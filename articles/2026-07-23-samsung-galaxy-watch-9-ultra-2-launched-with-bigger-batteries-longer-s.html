Samsung Galaxy Watch 9, Ultra 2 launched with bigger batteries, longer support

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung launched Galaxy Watch 9 and Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 on Wednesday.
  • Both watches feature larger batteries for improved battery life.
  • Software support has been extended significantly for both models.
  • Health tracking and durability remain key selling points.

New Galaxy Watches arrive with battery focus

Samsung has officially launched the Galaxy Watch 9 and the Galaxy Watch Ultra 2, putting battery life at the centre of the upgrade. The new smartwatches were unveiled on Wednesday and are already generating buzz for their larger cells and extended software support promises.

The company is clearly responding to a persistent user complaint: smartwatches that need daily charging. With these two models, Samsung is betting that bigger batteries and better power management will be enough to win over both fitness enthusiasts and casual users.

Galaxy Watch Ultra 2: built for the outdoors

The Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 is the more rugged of the two. It retains the chunky, titanium-clad design of its predecessor but now packs a noticeably larger battery. Samsung claims the watch can last several days on a single charge under moderate use, though real-world tests will be needed to verify that.

This model targets athletes and outdoor adventurers. It includes a new multi-band GPS system for more accurate tracking in remote areas and an improved altimeter. The watch is also water-resistant to 100 metres and meets military-grade durability standards.

Galaxy Watch 9: refined daily driver

The standard Galaxy Watch 9 takes a more mainstream approach. It is slimmer and lighter than the Ultra 2, with a circular AMOLED display that is bright and sharp. The battery here is also larger than the Watch 8, though Samsung has not released exact capacity figures yet.

Samsung has focused on health sensors for this model. The Watch 9 includes an updated BioActive sensor that can measure blood oxygen, heart rate, and stress levels more accurately. Sleep tracking has also been improved with new algorithms that break down sleep stages in more detail.

Software support extended to seven years

One of the biggest announcements alongside the hardware is the extended software support. Samsung has confirmed that both the Galaxy Watch 9 and the Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 will receive seven years of software updates. This matches the commitment the company recently made for its flagship smartphones.

This is a significant move in the smartwatch market, where most manufacturers offer three to four years of updates at best. Longer support means these watches will stay secure and compatible with new phones for years, which could justify the premium price tag for many buyers.

Pricing and availability

Samsung has not yet announced India pricing, but global prices are expected to start around Rs 35,000 for the Galaxy Watch 9 and go up to Rs 55,000 for the Ultra 2. Pre-orders open this week, with general sales starting from August.

The watches will run Wear OS 5 with Samsung's One UI Watch 6 skin. They are compatible with both Android and iOS devices, though some features will be limited on iPhones.

Industry watchers will be looking closely at how these new batteries perform in daily use. If Samsung has cracked the battery life problem without sacrificing features, the Galaxy Watch 9 and Ultra 2 could set a new standard for smartwatches in 2026.
