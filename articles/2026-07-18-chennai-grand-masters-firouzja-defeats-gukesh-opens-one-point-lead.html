Chennai Grand Masters: Firouzja Defeats Gukesh, Opens One-Point Lead

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Alireza Firouzja defeated world champion D Gukesh to take the sole lead at the Chennai Grand Masters.
  • Firouzja now has 3.5 points, one point ahead of Arjun Erigaisi.
  • Arjun Erigaisi won his game in 43 moves to move into second place.

Firouzja Takes Commanding Lead

Alireza Firouzja delivered a decisive blow to world champion D Gukesh in Round 4 of the 4th Quantbox Chennai Grandmasters 2026 on Friday. The French grandmaster now sits alone at the top of the standings with 3.5 points, one clear of the chasing pack.

The victory marks a significant turnaround for Firouzja, who has been competing after recovering from an injury setback. His aggressive play against Gukesh proved too sharp for the Indian world champion to handle.

Gukesh Struggles to Find Counterplay

Playing with the white pieces, Gukesh opted for the Queen’s Gambit. Firouzja responded with the Ragozin Defense, a line that has seen a resurgence in top-level chess. The Frenchman equalised comfortably out of the opening.

As the game progressed into the middlegame, Firouzja began to outmanoeuvre Gukesh. The world champion appeared to misjudge the tactical nuances of the position. By move 30, Firouzja had secured a healthy advantage.

Gukesh tried to complicate matters, but Firouzja calculated precisely. He converted the full point without giving his opponent any realistic chances for counterplay. The loss drops Gukesh to 2 points out of 4.

Erigaisi Fires Back with a Masterclass

Arjun Erigaisi kept pace with the leaders by winning his game in 43 moves. The Indian grandmaster produced a clinical performance to dispatch his opponent and move into sole second place with 2.5 points.

Erigaisi’s victory was described as a masterclass by observers. He controlled the centre, built pressure methodically, and struck at the right moment. His form will be crucial as the tournament enters its second half.

The win places Erigaisi as the nearest challenger to Firouzja. With three rounds remaining, the race for the title is far from decided.

Tournament Context and Stakes

The Chennai Grand Masters is a closed round-robin event featuring some of the world’s strongest players. It serves as a key preparation event for upcoming elite competitions. The field includes world champion Gukesh, Firouzja, Erigaisi, and several other top grandmasters.

Firouzja’s performance so far has been impressive, especially considering his recent injury. He has shown resilience and sharp tactical vision. His one-point lead gives him breathing room, but the tournament is still wide open.

Gukesh will need to recover quickly if he hopes to challenge for the title. A loss to his main rival puts him in a difficult position. He will be looking to bounce back in the next round.

What to Watch Next

Round 5 will be critical. Firouzja faces a tough test against another strong contender, while Erigaisi will aim to close the gap. Gukesh will be desperate to return to winning ways. The standings could shift dramatically in just one day of play.
