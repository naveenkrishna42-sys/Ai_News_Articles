Hyderabad: Dutch Woman Alleges Harassment by Boyfriend's Roommate

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • A Dutch woman lodged a complaint at a Hyderabad police station against her boyfriend's roommate.
  • The allegations include sexual harassment, stalking, and inappropriate advances over a period.
  • Police have registered a case and are investigating the matter.

Complaint Filed in Hyderabad

A Dutch national living in Hyderabad has filed a sexual harassment complaint against her boyfriend's roommate. The woman approached the police on Sunday, alleging repeated inappropriate behaviour by the accused over the past few weeks.

According to the complaint, the accused allegedly made unwelcome advances and engaged in stalking. The woman stated that despite her objections, the behaviour continued, causing her distress and fear for her safety.

Details of the Allegations

The woman, who has been residing in the city for work, told police that the accused would often be present in common areas of the shared apartment and would make lewd comments. She also alleged that he would follow her when she left the apartment and try to initiate unwanted conversations.

The complaint mentions that the woman had previously complained to her boyfriend, who confronted the roommate. However, the situation escalated rather than improved, leading her to seek legal recourse.

Police Response and Investigation

Based on the complaint, the police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including those dealing with sexual harassment and stalking. An investigation has been initiated.

“We have taken the complaint seriously and are recording the statements of all parties involved. The accused will be summoned for questioning,” a police official said. Authorities have not yet made any arrests, pending further inquiry.

Broader Context of Safety Concerns

This case highlights ongoing concerns about the safety of women, including foreign nationals, in shared living spaces in Indian cities. Hyderabad, a major IT hub, has a significant expatriate population, and such incidents often draw attention to the need for stricter enforcement of laws protecting women.

Legal experts point out that while the law provides for stringent action in cases of sexual harassment, the onus is often on the victim to report and follow up on complaints. The police have assured that the investigation will be conducted fairly and expeditiously.

What happens next: The police will continue their probe, and the accused is expected to be called for questioning in the coming days. Further legal action will depend on the evidence gathered during the investigation.
