PSU Bank FD Rates Stay Below 7%: What Buyers Need to Know

Public sector bank fixed deposit rates remain capped below the 7% mark for general citizens across most standard tenors. Business Today's comparison of SBI, PNB, Bank of Baroda and Bank of India confirms that the best headline rate among these four sits at 6.80% p.a., offered by SBI on its 399-day Amrit Kalash scheme. For a saver parking ₹5 lakh, that translates to roughly ₹37,200 in interest over the full term before tax.

The gap matters. Private banks and small finance banks routinely offer 7.25% to 8.25% on comparable tenors, so the PSU premium now runs on safety and liquidity rather than yield.

Practical Buyer Implications

  • Tax drag is real: At 6.80%, a saver in the 30% slab nets roughly 4.76% post-tax. That is below current CPI inflation, so FDs are a capital-protection tool, not a wealth-creation one.
  • Senior citizens should book the longest acceptable tenor: The 0.50% extra compounds meaningfully. A ₹10 lakh deposit for 399 days at 7.30% earns about ₹79,900.
  • Deposit insurance covers ₹5 lakh per bank per depositor: Splitting across SBI, PNB, BoB and Bank of India keeps every rupee insured.
  • Ladder the maturities: Splitting into 6-month, 12-month and 24-month buckets preserves liquidity and lets you reprice if rates rise.
  • Compare against alternatives: Small finance banks and NBFCs offer 7.25%-8.25%, but carry higher credit risk and lower liquidity.

Verdict

SBI offers the best headline PSU FD rate at 6.80%, with Bank of Baroda close behind at 6.60%. If you are a senior citizen, SBI's 7.30% for 399 days is the single best PSU deal on the board. For general citizens, no PSU bank crosses 7%, so treat these deposits as a safety anchor within a diversified portfolio rather than a yield play.
