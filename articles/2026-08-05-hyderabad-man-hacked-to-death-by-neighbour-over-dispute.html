Hyderabad man hacked to death by neighbour over dispute

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Victim attacked with sharp weapons by neighbour
  • Incident took place in Hyderabad on Tuesday
  • Accused detained; police investigation underway
  • Motive not yet officially confirmed

Hyderabad: A 35-year-old man was hacked to death by his neighbour in what police suspect was a premeditated attack. The incident took place on Tuesday evening in a residential locality, sending shockwaves through the community.

The victim, whose identity has not been officially released, was attacked with sharp weapons while he was near his home. The accused, also a neighbour, fled the spot immediately after the assault but was later detained by the police.

Attack details

According to preliminary reports, the two men had a history of animosity, though the exact reason for the escalation remains unclear. Witnesses said the accused arrived on the scene with a weapon and attacked the victim without warning, leaving him grievously injured.

Local residents rushed the victim to a nearby hospital, where doctors declared him brought dead. The police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code and have launched a detailed probe.

Police response

Senior police officials visited the spot and assured residents of swift action. "The accused has been taken into custody, and we are verifying the sequence of events," said a police officer familiar with the investigation, requesting anonymity as he is not authorised to speak to the media.

The weapon used in the attack has been recovered, and forensic teams are examining the evidence. Police are also recording statements from family members and neighbours to establish the motive.

Community in shock

The incident has left the locality rattled, with many residents expressing concern over the rising frequency of such violent altercations. "We never imagined something like this could happen here. It's terrifying," said a neighbour who did not wish to be named.

Authorities have urged residents to remain calm and have increased patrolling in the area as a precautionary measure. The victim's family has been informed, and the body has been sent for post-mortem.

What to watch

Police are expected to produce the accused before a local court within 24 hours. Sources indicate that a prior dispute over property may have triggered the attack, though this has not been confirmed. Further details are awaited as the investigation progresses.
