Kerala Universities to Conduct Exams in Malayalam for All Courses

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kerala universities to offer Malayalam as medium for all exam papers
  • Move aims to boost regional language use in higher education
  • Students can opt for Malayalam in theory exams across courses

In a significant move to promote regional language in higher education, Kerala has decided to allow university students to write examinations in Malayalam for all courses. The decision, announced by the state's higher education department, will cover undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across all universities in the state.

What the Decision Entails

Students can now choose to write theory exam papers in Malayalam instead of English. The option applies to all subjects, including science, commerce, and humanities. Authorities said the move aims to remove language barriers that often hinder students from performing to their full potential.

The directive will be implemented from the next academic semester. Universities have been asked to prepare question papers in both English and Malayalam. Students who opt for Malayalam will have their answer scripts evaluated by examiners proficient in the language.

Why This Matters

Kerala has one of the highest literacy rates in India, but many students struggle with English-medium examinations. Educationists have long argued that this puts first-generation learners and those from rural backgrounds at a disadvantage.

Officials believe the policy will boost confidence among students and reduce exam-related stress. It also aligns with the National Education Policy 2020, which encourages the use of mother tongue in teaching and assessment.

Implementation Challenges

While the decision has been welcomed, questions remain about execution. Universities will need to translate question papers, train evaluators, and ensure uniformity in grading. Technical and medical courses may pose additional challenges due to specialised terminology.

The state's higher education council is working on a standard glossary of Malayalam equivalents for technical terms. This is expected to be ready before the new system rolls out.

Reactions and Next Steps

Student groups have largely supported the move. Many said it will level the playing field for those who are not comfortable with English. Teachers' associations have also endorsed the decision but called for adequate preparation time.

The government has directed all universities to submit their implementation plans within a month. A coordination committee will be set up to monitor progress and address issues.

The first exams under the new policy are expected to be held in mid-2025. If successful, the model could be extended to other states as well.
