HDFC Bank board fines CEO, CFO Rs 1 lakh each for 'business overreach'

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • HDFC Bank's board imposed a Rs 1 lakh penalty each on CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan, CFO Srinivasan Vaidyanathan and a group head.
  • The penalties were for 'business overreach' in connection with the MSRDC deposit pricing case.
  • The board found no improper motive in the deposit pricing review but still penalised the executives.

MUMBAI: HDFC Bank's board has penalised its managing director and chief executive officer Sashidhar Jagdishan, chief financial officer Srinivasan Vaidyanathan, and a group head, imposing a fine of Rs 1 lakh each. The action comes after an internal review into the bank's dealings with the Maharashtra State Road Development Corporation (MSRDC).

The board cited 'business overreach' as the reason for the penalty. The development was first reported by multiple news outlets on Monday.

MSRDC deposit case under scrutiny

The penalties stem from a review of how the bank handled deposits from MSRDC. Sources familiar with the matter said the review focused on pricing and the terms under which these deposits were accepted.

While the board concluded that there was no improper motive in the deposit pricing process, it found that certain actions constituted an overreach. This led to the decision to fine the three senior executives.

Board finds no improper motive

According to Reuters, HDFC Bank's board stated that it found no evidence of any wrongdoing in terms of intent. The review appears to have been a thorough examination of internal processes.

Despite clearing the executives of any malicious intent, the board still deemed the penalties necessary. The fines of Rs 1 lakh each are symbolic but carry significant weight given the stature of the individuals involved.

What happens next

The bank has not issued a detailed public statement beyond confirming the penalty. Investors and analysts are watching closely to see if this leads to any changes in the bank's internal controls or senior management structure.

HDFC Bank shares traded flat in a broadly positive market on Monday. The episode underscores the intense regulatory and board-level scrutiny that top executives at India's largest private sector lender face.

The MSRDC case is now effectively closed from the board's perspective, but it serves as a reminder of the fine line between aggressive business growth and overreach in India's banking sector.
