Yash's 'Toxic' faces box office slump, hits Rs. 153 cr in 2 days

📅 2026-08-28 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Yash's 'Toxic' crosses Rs. 155 cr in India in 3 days
  • Box office collections decline on Day 3
  • Mixed reviews and controversies impact film's performance

Box Office Performance: A Mixed Bag

Yash's latest film 'Toxic', starring Nayanthara and Kiara Advani, has crossed the Rs. 155 crore mark in India in just three days. However, the film's box office collections have started to decline on the third day, indicating a potential slowdown in its performance.

Critical Reception: A Clash of Visions

The film has received mixed reviews from critics. While some have praised Yash's performance and the film's ambitious vision, others have criticized it for being a misfire, with a clash of visions between Yash and director Geetu Mohandas.

Controversies and Public Reception

The film has also been embroiled in controversies, with some critics accusing it of promoting toxic behavior and misogyny. This has led to a divided public reception, with some viewers supporting the film's message, while others are critical of it.

Broader Impact: The Toxic Men Syndrome

The film's release coincides with a broader discussion about the 'toxic men syndrome', where society is more forgiving of toxic behavior in men compared to women. The film's controversies have added to this ongoing debate.
