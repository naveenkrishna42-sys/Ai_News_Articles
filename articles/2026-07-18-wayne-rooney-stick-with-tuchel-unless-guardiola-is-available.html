Wayne Rooney: Stick with Tuchel unless Guardiola is available

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Wayne Rooney urges FA to keep Thomas Tuchel as England manager, but makes exception for Pep Guardiola.
  • Rooney praises Tuchel's tactical acumen but acknowledges Guardiola as a transformative figure.
  • England's World Cup exit has sparked debate over Tuchel's future, with Guardiola reportedly open to a return.

Rooney's Verdict on Tuchel

Wayne Rooney has weighed in on the debate surrounding Thomas Tuchel's future as England manager. The former England captain believes the Football Association should stick with the German coach, but only if Pep Guardiola is not available to take over.

Speaking after England's early exit from the 2026 World Cup, Rooney said Tuchel has done enough to keep his job. He pointed to the manager's tactical discipline and his ability to get the best out of a talented squad.

The Guardiola Factor

Rooney, however, made a notable exception. “Unless Pep Guardiola is available, I think we stick with Thomas,” he told reporters. The comment comes after reports that Guardiola, who left Manchester City at the end of last season, has held private talks about returning to management.

Guardiola has been linked with the England job before. Sources indicate he is open to a role with the national team, though nothing has been confirmed. Rooney described Guardiola as a “once-in-a-generation” coach who could transform England’s style of play.

Pressure on Tuchel After World Cup Exit

Tuchel's position has come under scrutiny since England crashed out of the World Cup in the round of 16. Critics have called his tenure a “disgrace,” with one Premier League manager reportedly ready to leave his club to replace him.

The FA has not made any public statements about Tuchel's future. The German tactician took over in 2025 and has a contract until 2028. His record in competitive matches has been mixed, though he led England to a strong showing in the Nations League.

What Rooney's Comments Mean

Rooney's endorsement of Tuchel carries weight. As one of England's greatest ever players, his opinion reflects a section of the football community that believes stability is key. He argued that sacking Tuchel without a clear upgrade would be a mistake.

“The players respect him. He's a top-level coach. But if the best manager in the world is free, you have to consider it,” Rooney said.

The FA now faces a tough decision. They can back Tuchel and risk more disappointment, or pursue Guardiola and reshape the team's identity. Both options come with risks.

Guardiola's availability is uncertain. He has been linked with several top clubs since leaving City, and a return to club football remains possible. The FA would need to move quickly if they want to secure him.

For now, Tuchel remains in charge. England's next fixtures are in September for the European Championship qualifiers. The pressure will only intensify if results do not improve.

What to Watch

The FA is expected to make a decision on Tuchel's future before the end of the month. Guardiola's next move will be a major factor in whether England's managerial situation changes.
