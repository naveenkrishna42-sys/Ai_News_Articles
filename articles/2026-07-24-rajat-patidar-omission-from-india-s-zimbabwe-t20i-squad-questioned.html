Rajat Patidar Omission From India's Zimbabwe T20I Squad Questioned

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Former India cricketer Sadagoppan Ramesh questioned Rajat Patidar's exclusion from the T20I squad for Zimbabwe.
  • Ramesh noted that no player from two-time IPL champions Royal Challengers Bengaluru was selected.
  • Patidar was a key performer for RCB in IPL 2026, especially in the playoffs.
  • The squad selection has sparked debate about consistency and regional representation.

Former Cricketer Questions Patidar's Snub

Former India batter Sadagoppan Ramesh has publicly questioned the national selectors over the omission of Rajat Patidar from India's T20I squad for the upcoming tour of Zimbabwe. Ramesh's remarks came shortly after the squad announcement, which left out several notable performers from the recently concluded IPL season.

Speaking to reporters, Ramesh pointedly asked, “Where is Rajat Patidar?” He highlighted that Patidar's consistent performances for Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB) in IPL 2026, particularly in high-pressure playoff matches, merited a national call-up.

RCB's Absence in the Squad

Ramesh did not stop at Patidar's exclusion. He noted a broader pattern in the selection. “Not a single player selected from two-time champions RCB,” he said, slamming what he termed as selection hypocrisy. RCB have won the IPL title twice in recent years, yet their players found no place in the squad for the Zimbabwe series.

Patidar was RCB's second-highest run-scorer this season, amassing 463 runs in 14 matches at a strike rate of 158. His unbeaten 78 in the Eliminator was widely praised. Critics argue that ignoring such form sends a confusing message to domestic performers.

Selection Consistency Under Fire

The selection committee, led by Ajit Agarkar, has defended the squad as a blend of youth and experience, tailored for Zimbabwe conditions. However, questions persist. Patidar, 29, has played 2 T20Is for India but has not featured since 2023. His domestic T20 numbers remain strong.

“If you reward IPL performances, then Patidar should be on the plane. If you are looking at future prospects, he is still young enough,” Ramesh argued. The debate underscores a recurring tension in Indian cricket: how to balance current form with long-term planning.

What's Next for Patidar?

Patidar himself has not commented publicly on the snub. He is expected to play in the upcoming Duleep Trophy. With a busy international calendar ahead, including a home T20 series against Australia later this year, the door is not shut.

The Zimbabwe tour begins on July 10. The squad will be under scrutiny, and any failure in the top order could revive calls for Patidar's inclusion in subsequent series.
