Hyderabad to distribute 1 lakh free clay Ganesh idols

📅 2026-08-26 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • GHMC to distribute 1 lakh clay Ganesh idols free of cost
  • Initiative aims to reduce plastic waste during Ganesh Chaturthi
  • Clay idols are more eco-friendly and biodegradable
  • Limited to residents of Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation limits

GHMC Launches Initiative to Distribute Free Clay Ganesh Idols

The Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) has announced a new initiative to distribute one lakh clay Ganesh idols free of cost to residents during the upcoming Ganesh Chaturthi festival. This move comes as part of the city's efforts to promote eco-friendly practices and reduce plastic waste.

Why This Matters

The initiative is a significant step towards addressing the environmental impact of traditional plastic Ganesh idols. Plastic waste poses a serious threat to wildlife and ecosystems, and this initiative aims to mitigate that impact. By promoting clay idols, the GHMC is encouraging a shift towards more sustainable materials.

Eco-Friendly Benefits of Clay Idols

Clay idols are more eco-friendly compared to their plastic counterparts. They are biodegradable and do not contribute to the growing problem of plastic pollution. Additionally, clay is a natural material that is less harmful to the environment. This initiative aligns with the global trend of moving towards sustainable and eco-friendly materials.

Broader Impact and Future Prospects

This initiative by the GHMC sets a precedent for other cities to follow suit. By leading the way in promoting eco-friendly practices, Hyderabad can inspire national and international efforts towards sustainability. The success of this initiative will depend on community engagement and support, as well as the scalability of the project.
