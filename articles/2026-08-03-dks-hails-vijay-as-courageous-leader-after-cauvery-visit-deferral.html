DKS hails Vijay as courageous leader after Cauvery visit deferral

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • DKS calls Vijay courageous after TN CM defers Bengaluru visit
  • Deferral follows Supreme Court petition by Tamil Nadu over Cauvery water
  • Shivakumar had appealed to Vijay to postpone visit amid tensions

Karnataka Deputy Chief Minister D K Shivakumar on Sunday praised Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Vijay as a "courageous leader" after the latter agreed to defer his scheduled visit to Bengaluru, a decision that comes against the backdrop of an escalating Cauvery water dispute between the two states.

Shivakumar's remarks came hours after Vijay, who also heads the Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), accepted the Karnataka leader's appeal to postpone the visit. The visit was planned amid rising tensions over the sharing of Cauvery river water, a decades-old issue that has repeatedly flared up between the neighbouring states.

Appeal and acceptance

Shivakumar had publicly urged Vijay to reconsider his travel plans, citing the sensitivity of the situation. The deputy chief minister's appeal was rooted in concerns that the visit could inflame passions on both sides of the border, especially after Tamil Nadu moved the Supreme Court against Karnataka over the water row.

Vijay's decision to defer was seen as a gesture of restraint. "Vijay is a courageous leader," Shivakumar told reporters. "He understood the gravity of the situation and acted in the interest of peace."

Legal escalation

The backdrop to the political manoeuvring is a fresh legal battle. Tamil Nadu has approached the Supreme Court, seeking intervention in the Cauvery water dispute with Karnataka. The move marks another chapter in a long-running conflict that has seen multiple judicial orders, protests, and political standoffs over the years.

The court petition, filed by the Tamil Nadu government, is believed to press for a directive on water release from Karnataka's reservoirs. Officials in both states have remained tight-lipped about the specifics of the filing, but sources indicate the matter could come up for hearing in the coming weeks.

Political undercurrents

The deferral has also exposed differing positions within Tamil Nadu's political landscape. Reports suggest that the TVK and the Congress are not on the same page regarding the Cauvery issue, a divergence that could have implications for opposition unity in the state.

While Vijay's party has sought to project a firm stance on the water dispute, the Congress, which leads the opposition alliance in Tamil Nadu, appears to be treading more cautiously. This difference in approach has not gone unnoticed in political circles, with some analysts pointing to it as a potential flashpoint in the run-up to future elections.

What lies ahead

For now, the deferral has eased immediate tensions, but the underlying dispute remains unresolved. The Supreme Court's response to Tamil Nadu's petition will be crucial in shaping the next phase of the conflict.

Shivakumar's olive branch, meanwhile, has opened a window for dialogue. Whether that window widens into a sustained engagement or slams shut with the next court order is the question both states are watching closely.
