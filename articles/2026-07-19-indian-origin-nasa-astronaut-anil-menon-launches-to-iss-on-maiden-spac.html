A Historic Launch for Anil Menon

Anil Menon, a NASA astronaut of Indian origin, has launched to the International Space Station (ISS) on his first-ever spaceflight mission. The launch, which took place on July 18, 2026, has been widely covered by outlets including NASA, Ars Technica, and The Hindu.

Menon's journey to space marks a significant milestone for the astronaut, who was selected by NASA in 2021. He joins a small group of Indian-origin astronauts who have travelled to orbit.

Mission Details and Crew

The mission saw Menon and two other astronauts reach the ISS successfully, as confirmed by NASA's official coverage. The launch was part of NASA's ongoing commercial crew program, which uses private spacecraft to transport astronauts.

Specific details about the duration of the mission and the experiments planned have not been fully disclosed yet. Officials have not confirmed the exact timeline for Menon's return to Earth.

Coverage Across Media

Menon's flight underscores the growing diversity within the astronaut corps. His Indian heritage has been a point of pride for many in India and the diaspora, as it highlights the global nature of space exploration.

The mission also demonstrates the reliability of commercial spacecraft for regular crew rotations to the ISS. This is part of a broader trend where private companies are playing a larger role in human spaceflight.
