Goa casino attack: Director escapes gunfire, police hunt suspects

📅 2026-08-29 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Casino director shot at, escapes unhurt
  • Police hunt two armed suspects
  • CM terms incident 'first in tenure'
  • Congress slams govt on law and order
  • Security guard also targeted in attack

Casino director escapes gunfire in Goa

In a shocking incident, the director of a prominent Goa casino group escaped unharmed after being shot at by two armed men in Porvorim. The attack took place on a busy weekend, raising concerns about security in the state. The incident has sparked a widespread hunt for the suspects by the Goa police.

CCTV footage reveals attackers' flight

CCTV footage from the casino shows the two attackers fleeing the scene immediately after the shooting. The footage, which has been released by the police, shows the attackers running away in a panic. The quick action of the security personnel and the casino staff likely prevented a worse outcome.

CM terms incident 'first in tenure'

The Chief Minister of Goa has termed the incident as the 'first in his eight-year tenure'. This statement has been criticized by the opposition, who have accused the government of failing to maintain law and order in the state. The incident has also raised questions about the security measures in place at the casino.

Congress slams govt on law and order

The Congress party has strongly condemned the incident, calling it a 'violation of public peace'. The party has accused the government of not taking adequate measures to prevent such incidents. The opposition has also demanded a thorough investigation into the incident.

Security guard also targeted

In addition to the casino director, a security guard was also targeted in the attack. The guard was also shot at but escaped unhurt. The incident has once again highlighted the need for better security measures at public places, especially in tourist-heavy areas like Goa.
