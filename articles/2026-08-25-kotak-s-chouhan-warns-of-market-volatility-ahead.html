Kotak's Chouhan warns of market volatility ahead

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Global risks resurface, impacting Indian markets
  • Kotak Securities signals caution with market volatility warning
  • Chouhan advises investors to stay vigilant amid economic uncertainties

Global Risks Resurface, Impacting Indian Markets

Shrikant Chouhan, head of equity research at Kotak Securities, has issued a stark warning about the resurgence of global risks that could significantly impact Indian markets. In a recent report, Chouhan highlighted the potential for a sharp correction in the Indian equity market, citing a combination of factors including geopolitical tensions, economic slowdowns, and rising inflation.

Kotak Securities Signals Caution with Market Volatility Warning

Kotak Securities has advised investors to brace for a volatile market environment, with Chouhan emphasizing the need for a cautious approach. The research firm has revised its market outlook, downgrading its expectations for the upcoming quarter. This move comes amid a backdrop of rising global uncertainty, with investors closely watching developments in the United States and Europe.

Chouhan Advises Investors to Stay Vigilant Amid Economic Uncertainties

In an interview with Business Standard, Chouhan stressed the importance of staying vigilant amid the current economic uncertainties. He advised investors to diversify their portfolios and consider hedging strategies to mitigate potential risks. Chouhan also cautioned against making impulsive decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.
