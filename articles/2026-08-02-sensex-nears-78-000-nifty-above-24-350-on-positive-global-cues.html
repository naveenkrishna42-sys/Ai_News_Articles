Sensex nears 78,000, Nifty above 24,350 on positive global cues

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex traded near 78,000, Nifty above 24,350 on July 31
  • Positive global cues and sectoral buying drive market momentum
  • Broader market indices also showed strength in early trade

Indian equity benchmarks opened higher on Friday, July 31, with the Sensex hovering near the 78,000 mark and the Nifty trading above 24,350. The uptick came on the back of positive global cues, as investors shrugged off overnight concerns and focused on fresh buying in heavyweight stocks.

Market opens with strong momentum

The BSE Sensex climbed over 300 points in early trade, inching closer to the psychological 78,000 level. Simultaneously, the NSE Nifty reclaimed and held above 24,350, driven by gains in banking, IT, and auto stocks. Market participants said the tone was buoyant right from the opening bell, with breadth favouring advances.

Global markets provided a supportive backdrop. Asian peers traded higher, while US futures pointed to a steady start, easing fears of any immediate slowdown. Analysts noted that the absence of major domestic triggers allowed global sentiment to take the lead.

Sectoral action and key movers

Among sectoral indices, banking and financial services were the top contributors, with private lenders and PSU banks witnessing steady buying. IT stocks also gained, tracking strength in global technology shares. Auto and FMCG counters added to the positive mood, while pharma and metal stocks showed mixed trends.

Broader indices outperformed the benchmarks, with the BSE Midcap and Smallcap indices trading higher. This indicated that retail and institutional investors were both participating in the rally, a sign of broad-based confidence.

What is driving the rally?

Market analysts attributed the upswing to several factors. First, global risk appetite improved after key economic data from the US came in line with expectations. Second, foreign institutional investors (FIIs) have been net buyers in recent sessions, adding to domestic liquidity. Third, expectations of steady corporate earnings growth have kept valuations supported.

However, some experts cautioned that the market could face resistance at higher levels, given that valuations are not cheap. They advised investors to remain selective and focus on quality stocks with strong fundamentals.

Investor sentiment and outlook

Traders are now watching the Reserve Bank of India's policy stance and any fresh cues from the global commodity market. The rupee's movement against the dollar will also be closely monitored, as a stable currency supports foreign flows.

Volatility, as measured by India VIX, remained range-bound, suggesting that investors are not overly anxious. Yet, any sudden geopolitical development or unexpected data release could alter the course quickly.

As the session progresses, the key question is whether the indices can sustain these levels. Market participants will look for sustained buying in the afternoon trade and any commentary from policymakers.

In the coming days, the focus shifts to monthly auto sales data and the services PMI, which could provide fresh direction. A breakout above 24,400 on the Nifty could open the door for further upside, while a fall below 24,200 may trigger profit-booking.
