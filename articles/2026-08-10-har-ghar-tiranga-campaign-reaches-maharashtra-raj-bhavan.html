Har Ghar Tiranga campaign reaches Maharashtra Raj Bhavan

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Har Ghar Tiranga event held at Lok Bhavan, the Maharashtra Governor's residence, on August 9, 2026.
  • The campaign aims to encourage citizens to hoist the national flag at their homes for Independence Day.
  • Official communication from Raj Bhavan Maharashtra confirms the event, with details still emerging.

Mumbai, August 10: The Maharashtra Governor's official residence, Lok Bhavan, played host to the Har Ghar Tiranga campaign on Sunday, August 9, 2026. The event, part of the nationwide initiative ahead of Independence Day, saw the Tricolour take centre stage at the state's most prominent address.

Raj Bhavan Maharashtra confirmed the development through its official communication channel on Monday. The brief announcement, dated 09.08.2026, simply stated: "Har Ghar Tiranga in Lok Bhavan." Further details about the programme's format or participants have not been disclosed yet.

What is Har Ghar Tiranga?

The Har Ghar Tiranga campaign is a central government-led movement that urges every citizen to hoist the national flag at their homes between August 13 and 15. Launched in 2022, it aims to instil a sense of patriotism and connect Indians with the tricolour's symbolism.

By holding the event at Lok Bhavan, the Governor's office has signalled its endorsement of the campaign. The choice of venue is significant: Lok Bhavan is not just a residence but a working hub for the state's constitutional head.

Significance for Maharashtra

Maharashtra has been an active participant in the Har Ghar Tiranga drive since its inception. Municipal corporations, schools, and housing societies across the state typically organise flag-hoisting ceremonies and awareness rallies during the campaign window.

This year, the Raj Bhavan's involvement adds a layer of official gravitas. It also sets an example for other government buildings in Mumbai and beyond to follow suit, observers noted.

What to expect next

The state government is likely to release more details about the Independence Day celebrations in the coming days. Citizens can also expect increased availability of flags at local markets and designated distribution points.

As August 15 approaches, similar events are anticipated at other official residences and public institutions across Maharashtra. The Raj Bhavan's move may well prompt a ripple effect, encouraging greater public participation in the flag-hoisting initiative.
