Mumbai: Actress Urmila Matondkar has set social media abuzz with a cryptic post about loyalty, coming days after her ex-husband Mohsin Akhtar shared pictures from his second marriage reception. The timing of the post has led to a flurry of speculation among fans and followers.

Matondkar, who married Kashmir-based businessman Mohsin Akhtar in 2016 and separated in 2019, took to her Instagram stories late Tuesday night with a message that did not name anyone but appeared loaded with meaning. The post, now widely circulated, has drawn reactions from netizens who were quick to connect the dots.

A message on love and loyalty

The actress's note spoke about the importance of loyalty in relationships, using phrases that many interpreted as a veiled reference to her past marriage. While she did not tag anyone or provide context, the emotional tone of the words stood out.

Sources close to the actress say she has been in a reflective mood lately, though they declined to comment on whether the post was directed at anyone specific. Matondkar has largely stayed away from discussing her personal life publicly since the separation.

Mohsin Akhtar's wedding photos go viral

Days before Matondkar's post, Akhtar had shared a series of pictures from his reception with his new wife, Nidhaa Bhatt. The images, which showed the couple smiling together, were accompanied by a caption expressing gratitude to God for bringing Bhatt into his life.

The photos, posted on his Instagram account, quickly gained traction online. Reports suggest the wedding took place a few months ago, but the reception pictures were shared only recently, drawing fresh attention to the former couple's separate journeys.

Netizens draw their own conclusions

Social media users were not slow to link the two events. Within hours of Matondkar's post, comments began pouring in, with many interpreting her words as a response to Akhtar's public display of happiness. Some praised her for taking the high road, while others urged her to ignore online chatter.
