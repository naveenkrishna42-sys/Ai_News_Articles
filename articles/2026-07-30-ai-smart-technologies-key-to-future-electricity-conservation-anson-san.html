AI Smart Technologies Key to Future Electricity Conservation: Anson Sando

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Anson Sando emphasized AI's role in future electricity conservation.
  • Smart technologies can optimize energy usage and reduce waste.
  • The remarks were made at a conference in Chennai.
  • Sando highlighted the potential for AI to transform energy management.

Anson Sando, a noted technology expert, has said that smart technologies powered by artificial intelligence will be instrumental in conserving electricity in the years ahead. He made the remarks during a discussion on energy efficiency held in Chennai.

AI-Driven Solutions for Energy Management

Sando explained that AI can analyse consumption patterns in real-time. This allows systems to automatically adjust lighting, cooling, and other electrical loads based on actual need.

Such intelligent grids can predict peak demand periods and allocate resources more efficiently. This reduces both wastage and strain on the power infrastructure.

Smart Homes and Industries

At the household level, smart meters and AI-enabled appliances can learn user behaviour. They can switch off devices when not in use or schedule high-energy tasks for off-peak hours.

For industries, Sando noted that AI could optimise manufacturing processes. It can identify inefficiencies in machinery and suggest maintenance schedules to prevent energy loss.

These technologies are not a distant dream. Many are already being piloted in urban centres across India.

The Chennai Context

The event in Chennai brought together policymakers and tech innovators. Sando's comments come amid growing concerns about rising electricity demand in Tamil Nadu.

Officials at the event acknowledged that traditional conservation methods are no longer enough. They expressed interest in adopting AI-based solutions to manage the grid better.

Sando urged the government to incentivise the adoption of smart technologies. He said that initial investments would pay off through long-term savings in power costs.

Challenges Ahead

However, Sando also cautioned about challenges. Data privacy and the need for robust cybersecurity frameworks are critical for widespread AI deployment.

He stressed that public awareness campaigns would be essential. People need to understand how smart technologies work and trust them with their energy data.

The integration of AI into existing power systems will require significant upgrades. But Sando believes the benefits far outweigh the hurdles.

As Chennai and other Indian cities grow, efficient electricity use becomes non-negotiable. AI offers a practical path forward, turning data into actionable savings.

What to watch: The Tamil Nadu government is expected to announce pilot projects for AI-based energy management in select residential and commercial zones within the next quarter.
