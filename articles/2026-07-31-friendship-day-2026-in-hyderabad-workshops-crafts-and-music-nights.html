Friendship Day 2026 in Hyderabad: workshops, crafts and music nights

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Multiple venues across Hyderabad hosting Friendship Day events
  • Events include DIY workshops, craft sessions, and live music nights
  • Organisers expect large turnout as city embraces the celebration

Hyderabad is set to celebrate Friendship Day with a mix of creative workshops, hands-on craft sessions, and live music nights spread across the city. From the bustling hubs of Banjara Hills to the quieter corners of the Old City, organisers are pulling together events that cater to groups of friends looking to make the day memorable.

Craft and DIY workshops take centre stage

Several venues are hosting craft workshops where participants can create personalised gifts — from handmade bracelets to customised photo frames. These sessions are designed for small groups, encouraging collaboration and a bit of friendly competition.

One organiser told Telangana Today that the idea is to move beyond the usual dinner-and-movie routine. "People want to make something with their hands, something they can take home," he said. The workshops are expected to run through the afternoon, with slots booked in advance.

Music nights promise a lively evening

As the sun sets, the focus shifts to music. Multiple live gigs are planned across the city, featuring local indie bands and acoustic duos. Some venues are offering open-mic segments, giving friends a chance to dedicate songs to each other on stage.

Entry to most music nights is free, though a few premium events come with a cover charge. Organisers have not yet confirmed the full lineup, but early announcements have generated buzz on social media.

Community events and family-friendly options

Not all events are aimed at young adults. Some community centres are organising family-friendly gatherings, including storytelling sessions and board game afternoons. These are designed for friends of all ages, from schoolchildren to senior citizens.

Local libraries and cultural associations are also joining in, with poetry readings and group discussions on the theme of friendship. The response, according to one coordinator, has been "overwhelming", with registrations closing early for several sessions.

How to plan your day

For those looking to pack in multiple events, the city's Metro and app-based cabs make it easy to hop between venues. Most events are clustered in the central and western parts of Hyderabad, reducing travel time.

Organisers advise arriving early, especially for the free workshops, as seating is limited. Some venues are also offering combo passes that cover both a workshop and a music night at a discounted rate.

A growing tradition

Friendship Day has been gaining momentum in Hyderabad over the past few years, with more venues and businesses joining the celebration. This year's lineup reflects a shift towards experiential events, where the focus is on participation rather than passive consumption.

While the day is not a public holiday, many workplaces and colleges are expected to be quieter as groups take time off to celebrate together. Retailers, too, are cashing in, with special discount offers on gifts and dining deals across the city.

As the city prepares for the celebrations, all eyes are on the weather. The monsoon has been sporadic, and organisers are keeping a close watch. Most events are planned for indoor venues, but a few open-air music nights may be rescheduled if rain plays spoilsport.
