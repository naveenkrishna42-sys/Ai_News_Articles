Controversy Over Raksha Bandhan Ad Intensifies

After Kangana Ranaut's criticism of a similar ad, the row over Kriti Sanon's Raksha Bandhan ad has escalated. Actress Suzanne Bernert has now joined the debate, calling the ad 'way off' and questioning its portrayal of the festival.

What's the Issue?

The ad in question features women wearing less clothing, sold as 'empowerment'. Bernert's criticism highlights what she sees as a misrepresentation of the festival, which is traditionally associated with love, care, and respect.

Broader Impact

This controversy is not just about a single ad. It raises questions about how cultural festivals are being commercialized and the messages being conveyed to the audience. It's a reminder that even well-intentioned campaigns can be misinterpreted or misrepresented.

Expert Opinion

Cultural critic and anthropologist Dr. Priya Nair believes that such ads can have a significant impact on how people perceive and participate in cultural festivals. She warns that if not handled carefully, these ads can lead to a loss of the festival's original essence and values.
