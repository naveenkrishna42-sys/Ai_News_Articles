SEBI’s CAS System: A New Challenge for Traders

India’s stock markets have been experiencing significant volatility since the implementation of the Continuous Auction System (CAS) by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI). Traders are struggling to adapt to the new system, which has led to wild swings in the Sensex and Nifty indices. This has raised concerns about the effectiveness of the CAS system and its impact on market stability.

Understanding the CAS System

The CAS system, introduced by SEBI in August 2026, aims to create a more continuous and transparent trading environment for investors. Under the new system, trading occurs in a single continuous session from 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM, with the last 15 minutes of the session designated as the closing auction. This is a significant change from the previous system, which had separate opening and closing auctions.

Traders’ Struggles with CAS

Traders have expressed their frustrations with the CAS system, citing difficulties in executing trades and managing risks. The new system has led to a significant increase in the number of trades executed in the closing auction, which has caused volatility in the market. Traders are also struggling to adapt to the new system, which has led to a decrease in overall trading volumes.

Expert Opinions

Experts have offered varying opinions on the CAS system. Some, like the trading head of Equirus Group, believe that the system is a step in the right direction but that the timing of its implementation may not have been optimal. Others, like UK Sinha, former chairman of SEBI, believe that SEBI could have been better prepared for the transition to the new system.

The Impact of CAS on Market Stability
