Spider-Man: Brand New Day Eyes Record-Breaking Opening; Aims to Beat Avengers: Endgame

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day is expected to have the highest opening day for a Hollywood film in India.
  • Early estimates suggest it could beat the record set by Avengers: Endgame.
  • The film has received positive reviews, with critics praising Tom Holland's performance.
  • It has already achieved China's biggest superhero opening in seven years.

The buzz around Spider-Man: Brand New Day is reaching a fever pitch. Early box office estimates suggest Tom Holland's latest outing as Peter Parker is on track to smash records. The film is aiming for the biggest opening day for a Hollywood release in India, with projections that could see it surpass Avengers: Endgame.

If the numbers hold, this would be a historic moment for the Indian box office. No Hollywood film has come close to Endgame since its release in 2019. Trade analysts are watching the advance booking figures closely.

Positive Reviews Fuel the Frenzy

Critical reception has been overwhelmingly positive. The Hindu called it a 'soulful reinvention,' noting that Tom Holland finally swings free under director Destin Daniel Cretton. Telegraph India echoed the sentiment, stating that both Holland and Peter Parker have come of age.

These reviews are driving word-of-mouth. Audiences who were unsure about another Spider-Man film are now lining up for tickets. The film's emotional depth and fresh direction are being cited as key draws.

Global Momentum Builds

The film isn't just dominating in India. The Hollywood Reporter confirmed that Spider-Man: Brand New Day has already scored China's biggest superhero opening in seven years. That is a massive win for the studio, given the Chinese market's recent unpredictability for Hollywood content.

This global momentum is feeding into the Indian release. Fans are eager to see what the fuss is about. Early morning shows and IMAX screenings are reportedly filling up fast in major metro cities.

Industry Expectations and Comparisons

The comparison to Avengers: Endgame is no small feat. That film collected over Rs 50 crore on its opening day in India. Early estimates for Brand New Day suggest it could cross that mark, setting a new benchmark for Hollywood in the country.

Distributors are betting big. Some have even increased screen counts to accommodate the demand. The film's release strategy appears to be paying off, with multiplex chains reporting strong pre-sales across premium formats.

What happens next will depend on walk-in audiences over the weekend. If the early estimates hold, Tom Holland will have a new box office crown. All eyes are now on Friday's final numbers.
