BCCI finally considers India star for Afghanistan series

After five years of ignoring him, the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) has reportedly 'discussed' including a key player in India's upcoming T20I series against Afghanistan. This move comes as a significant shift in the BCCI's approach, which had previously overlooked the player's potential.

Key players likely to make a comeback

According to various sources, Nitish Rana and Krunal Pandya are likely to be included in the Afghanistan T20I series. Rana has been receiving fitness clearance certificates and is on track for a comeback, while Pandya's performance in the IPL has been a significant factor in his consideration.

Jasprit Bumrah's recovery on track but return delayed

Jasprit Bumrah's recovery is on track, but his return to the Indian team is delayed. Bumrah has been a key player for India, and his absence has been felt in the team's performance. The BCCI's decision to consider including other players in the Afghanistan series indicates a strategic approach to manage the team's resources effectively.

India's all-round boost ahead of Afghanistan T20Is

India's all-round boost ahead of the Afghanistan T20Is is a significant development. The inclusion of key players like Nitish Rana and Krunal Pandya is expected to strengthen the team's performance. The BCCI's decision to consider including these players indicates a strategic approach to manage the team's resources effectively.
