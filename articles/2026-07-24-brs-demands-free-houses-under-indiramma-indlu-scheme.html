BRS Demands Free Houses Under Indiramma Indlu Scheme

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • BRS demands free house allotment under Indiramma Indlu scheme
  • Party criticizes Congress government's proposed payment model
  • Scheme aims to provide housing to poor in Telangana

BRS Stakes Claim on Housing Scheme

The Bharat Rashtra Samithi (BRS) has demanded that houses under the Indiramma Indlu scheme be allotted free of cost to beneficiaries. The party's demand comes as a direct challenge to the Congress government's proposed model, which reportedly includes a payment component from recipients.

Senior BRS leaders argued that the scheme, named after former Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu's mother, was originally envisioned as a fully subsidised housing programme. They accused the current administration of deviating from this core promise.

Political Battle Over Welfare

The Indiramma Indlu scheme is a flagship housing initiative of the Telangana government, aimed at providing pucca houses to the poor. The BRS contends that making beneficiaries pay even a nominal amount would defeat the purpose of the scheme.

"This is a betrayal of the poor," a BRS spokesperson said. "The Congress government must honour the commitment of free houses, as we had promised during our tenure." The party has threatened to launch an agitation if the demand is not met.

The Congress government, however, has maintained that the scheme is sustainable only with a contribution from beneficiaries. Officials argue that a token payment ensures ownership and prevents misuse.

What is Indiramma Indlu?

Indiramma Indlu is a housing scheme launched by the Telangana government to provide financial assistance for construction of houses to poor families. The scheme was initially introduced by the BRS government and later continued by the Congress administration.

The scheme targets beneficiaries identified through the socio-economic and caste census, offering financial aid for house construction. The current controversy centres on whether the aid will be a full grant or a subsidised loan.

Opposition Ramps Up Pressure

The BRS has decided to take the issue to the streets. Party workers are planning protests across the state to demand free allotment of houses. The opposition sees this as a key issue to mobilise support ahead of local body elections.

Political analysts note that housing schemes are a major vote-getter in Telangana, especially among rural and urban poor. Both parties are keen to claim credit for welfare measures.

The state government has not yet responded officially to the BRS's demand. Officials say the final modalities of the scheme are still being worked out and an announcement is expected soon.

With the demand for free houses gaining traction, the Congress government faces a tough test of its commitment to welfare. The upcoming weeks will reveal whether it will bend to opposition pressure or stick to its financial prudence argument.
