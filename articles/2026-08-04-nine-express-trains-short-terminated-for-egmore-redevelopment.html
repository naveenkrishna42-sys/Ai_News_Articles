Nine Express trains short-terminated for Egmore redevelopment

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nine Express trains will be short-terminated at Chennai Beach and Tambaram.
  • Move facilitates Egmore station redevelopment work.
  • Revised schedule to be announced by Southern Railway.

Redevelopment drive prompts train schedule changes

In a move to expedite the redevelopment of Chennai Egmore railway station, Southern Railway has decided to short-terminate nine Express trains. The affected services will now end their runs at Chennai Beach and Tambaram stations instead of Egmore, officials said on Tuesday.

The decision comes as part of the station's modernisation project, which requires platform and track blockages for construction activity. By diverting these trains, the railway aims to free up capacity at Egmore and ensure the redevelopment work proceeds without hindrance.

Which trains are affected?

While the full list of affected trains is yet to be officially released, railway sources indicated that both originating and terminating services at Egmore will be impacted. The short-termination will apply to select Express trains that currently use Egmore as their terminal point.

Passengers boarding or alighting at Egmore for these nine services will need to make alternative arrangements. The railway has not yet confirmed whether substitute bus services will be provided between Egmore and the revised terminal stations.

Redevelopment project scope

The Egmore station redevelopment is part of a broader initiative to upgrade major railway terminals across the country. The project includes modernising passenger amenities, improving circulation areas, and upgrading platform infrastructure to handle increased traffic in the coming years.

Chennai Egmore, one of the city's busiest stations, handles a significant number of trains to southern Tamil Nadu and Kerala. The redevelopment is expected to transform the station into a world-class facility, but the interim period will involve temporary disruptions for passengers.

What passengers should know

Southern Railway has advised passengers to check the revised schedule before planning their journeys. The changes will come into effect from a date that is yet to be announced, and the railway has promised to publicise the revised timings well in advance.

Regular commuters and long-distance travellers alike are urged to stay informed through official railway announcements. The short-termination arrangement will remain in place until the redevelopment work at Egmore is completed.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact duration of the disruption, but similar redevelopment projects at other stations have typically taken several months to a year to complete.

Looking ahead

Passengers can expect a detailed circular from Southern Railway in the coming days, listing the affected train numbers and revised arrival and departure times. The railway is likely to also announce alternative arrangements, including additional stops at Chennai Beach and Tambaram for these services.

As the redevelopment gathers pace, travellers should monitor official updates to avoid last-minute inconvenience at the station.
