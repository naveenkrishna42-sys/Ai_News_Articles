Crane collapse at Mumbai Port leaves workers injured

📅 2026-08-31 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Crane collapse at Mumbai Port injures two workers
  • Fire breaks out after collapse, causing further damage
  • Safety concerns raised amid rising incidents in the port

Crane Collapse at Mumbai Port

Two operators were injured when a crane collapsed and caught fire at the Mumbai Port on Monday. The incident occurred around 9:30 AM, disrupting operations at the port. The crane, which was being used to load goods onto a ship, collapsed suddenly, causing the fire. The operators were rushed to a nearby hospital for treatment.

Fire and Damage

The fire, which broke out after the collapse, spread quickly due to the flammable materials in the vicinity. Firefighters were called in to contain the blaze, which took several hours to extinguish. The damage caused by the fire and the collapse is estimated to be in the range of several crores.

Safety Concerns

This incident has raised serious safety concerns at the Mumbai Port. The port authority has been criticized for not ensuring adequate safety measures for the cranes. The incident is the latest in a series of accidents that have occurred at the port in the past few months.

Investigation Underway

An investigation is underway to determine the cause of the crane collapse and the fire. The port authority has promised to take stringent action against the persons responsible for the incident. The incident has also highlighted the need for stricter safety regulations at the port.

Impact on Port Operations

The incident has caused significant disruption to the port operations. Several ships are waiting to be unloaded, and the delay is expected to cause a ripple effect on the supply chain. The port authority has assured the public that it is taking all necessary steps to restore normalcy as soon as possible.
