Ramayana Part 1 With Ranbir Kapoor, Yash Tops IMDb India Movies List

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • IMDb India releases its annual Most Anticipated Movies list for 2026
  • 'Ramayana Part 1' starring Ranbir Kapoor and Yash takes the top spot
  • The list includes other major Hindi and regional language films

IMDb India has released its annual Most Anticipated Movies list for 2026, and topping the charts is Nitesh Tiwari's 'Ramayana Part 1'. The epic fantasy, starring Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram and Kannada superstar Yash as Raavan, has generated enormous buzz ahead of its release.

What makes the list

The rankings are based on the page views and interest metrics of IMDb users over the past year. 'Ramayana Part 1' leads a pack of high-profile Indian films set to hit theatres next year.

Other films in the top ten include Shah Rukh Khan's yet-untitled next project, a big-budget action thriller directed by Siddharth Anand. Also featuring are Alia Bhatt's 'Queen of Hearts', a period drama, and 'Jungle Cry', a survival thriller starring Hrithik Roshan.

Star power and scale

Ranbir Kapoor's casting as Lord Ram was announced last year, with Yash — fresh off the 'KGF' franchise — playing the antagonist. The film is produced by Madhu Mantena and is expected to be one of the most expensive Indian productions ever, with extensive VFX and a sprawling cast.

The makers have kept details about the plot and other cast members under wraps. However, industry insiders confirm that the film will cover the early life of Lord Ram, his exile, and the events leading up to the battle with Raavan.

Regional language entries

The list also reflects the growing pan-India appeal of regional cinema. Malayalam superstar Mohanlal's 'Roudram' and Tamil star Vijay's 'Thalapathy 67' have secured spots in the top 20. Both films are expected to release in multiple languages.

Rajinikanth's 'Coolie', directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj, also made the cut. The film marks the first collaboration between the veteran actor and the young director known for 'Master' and 'Leo'.

Anticipation and expectations

IMDb India's list has become a reliable indicator of audience interest. Last year's top-ranked film, 'Jawan', went on to become one of the highest-grossing Hindi films of all time. Industry observers are watching closely to see if 'Ramayana Part 1' can replicate that success.

The film's combination of mythology, star power, and cutting-edge technology has struck a chord with viewers. Social media conversations around the project have been intense, with fans debating everything from casting choices to the visual effects team.

The full list of IMDb India's Most Anticipated Movies for 2026 is available on the platform's website. The rankings will be updated quarterly as more films announce their release dates.

Shooting for 'Ramayana Part 1' is expected to wrap up by late 2026. The film is slated for a multi-language release across India and international markets. Whether it lives up to the hype will be known once it hits the big screen.
