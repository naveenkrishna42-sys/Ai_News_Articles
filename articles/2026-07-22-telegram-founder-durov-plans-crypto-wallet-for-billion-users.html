Telegram Founder Durov Plans Crypto Wallet for Billion Users

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Telegram founder Pavel Durov is planning to offer a crypto wallet to the platform's billion users.
  • The move aims to integrate blockchain technology directly into Telegram's messaging interface.
  • Durov's vision could significantly expand cryptocurrency adoption among mainstream users.

Telegram founder Pavel Durov has set his sights on a massive new goal: giving the messaging app's one billion users a built-in crypto wallet. The plan, which was reported by Decrypt, signals a fresh push to bring blockchain technology to the mainstream through one of the world's most popular communication platforms.

Durov's vision would effectively embed a digital wallet within Telegram, allowing users to send, receive, and store cryptocurrencies as easily as they send a text message. The move could dramatically expand the user base for digital currencies, tapping into Telegram's vast global audience.

Why Telegram is doubling down on crypto

The messaging giant has a long history with blockchain. It previously launched the Telegram Open Network (TON) and the Gram token, though those efforts faced regulatory hurdles. Now, with a renewed focus and a billion users, Durov appears ready to try again.

Integrating a wallet directly into the app removes a key barrier for many users: the need to download separate, often confusing, crypto applications. For Telegram, it also creates new revenue opportunities and strengthens user lock-in, as people may be less likely to leave a platform that also holds their digital assets.

How the wallet might work

While specific technical details remain scarce, the wallet is expected to be custodial in nature, meaning Telegram would hold the private keys on behalf of users. This approach prioritises ease of use over full decentralisation, making it accessible to non-technical users.

Sources suggest the wallet could support major cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ether, as well as TON tokens. The integration may also allow for peer-to-peer payments within chats, essentially turning Telegram into a payment app similar to WeChat in China or WhatsApp Pay in India.

Challenges ahead for Durov's plan

Rolling out a wallet to a billion users is a Herculean task. Regulatory hurdles are the biggest obstacle, especially in countries with strict cryptocurrency laws. India, for example, has imposed heavy taxes on crypto transactions, while China has banned them outright.

Security is another major concern. A high-profile hack or technical flaw in a wallet used by a billion people could have devastating consequences. Telegram will need to invest heavily in security infrastructure and user education to prevent fraud and loss of funds.

Durov has not yet announced a timeline for the rollout. Industry watchers expect a phased launch, likely starting in regions with friendly crypto regulations before expanding globally.

What this means for crypto adoption

If successful, Durov's plan could be a watershed moment for cryptocurrency. Bringing a wallet to a billion users overnight would dwarf the current user base of any existing crypto exchange or wallet provider. It would introduce digital assets to millions who have never used them before.

Critics, however, point out that sheer user numbers do not guarantee adoption. Many of Telegram's users are in countries with low banking penetration, which could be a positive for financial inclusion. But others are in regions where crypto is viewed with suspicion.

The coming months will show whether Durov can turn this ambitious vision into reality. For now, the crypto world is watching closely to see if Telegram can deliver a wallet that is both secure and simple enough for a billion people to use.
