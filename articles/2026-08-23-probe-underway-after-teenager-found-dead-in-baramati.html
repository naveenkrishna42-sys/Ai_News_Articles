Probe underway after teenager found dead in Baramati

Tragedy in Baramati

A somber atmosphere has gripped Baramati following the discovery of a 14-year-old girl’s body near her residence. The victim is reported to be the daughter of a long-time personal assistant to NCP (SP) MLA Rohit Pawar.

Local authorities were alerted to the situation earlier today. Police officials have cordoned off the area to collect evidence and speak with those who last saw the teenager.

Investigation into the incident

Preliminary reports indicate that the teenager left her home following a disagreement with her family members. Her absence prompted an immediate search by family and local residents, which ended when her body was discovered in the vicinity of their home.

Baramati police have registered a case and are currently treating the death as a matter of urgent investigation. Senior officers visited the site to supervise the gathering of evidence and forensic analysis.

Seeking answers

Investigators are now reconstructing the teenager's final movements. They are examining CCTV footage from the surrounding neighborhood and questioning family members about the nature of the reported dispute.

While initial findings point to a domestic incident, officials have not yet confirmed the exact cause of death. A post-mortem report is expected to provide clarity on the timeline of the tragedy.

Political and community reaction

MLA Rohit Pawar has been informed of the incident, which has left the local community in shock. As a high-profile political figure in the region, his close association with the family has brought significant public attention to the case.

Police have appealed to the public to refrain from circulating unverified information while the investigation is ongoing. They maintain that all leads are being explored to determine if any foul play was involved.

The police are expected to release a formal statement once the preliminary post-mortem results are available to investigators.
