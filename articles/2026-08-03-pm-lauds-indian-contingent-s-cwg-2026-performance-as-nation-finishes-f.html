PM lauds Indian contingent's CWG 2026 performance as nation finishes fourth

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • India finished fourth at CWG 2026 with 39 medals
  • Women athletes led India's medal tally, earning the moniker 'Nari Shakti'
  • PM's message highlights pride in contingent's achievement
  • India's all-time CWG medal count stands at 602, fourth in standings

NEW DELHI: Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Monday expressed pride in the Indian contingent's performance at the Commonwealth Games 2026 in Glasgow, as the country finished fourth in the overall standings with a haul of 39 medals. The PM's message came a day after the Games concluded, with women athletes emerging as the standout contributors to India's medal tally.

In a statement released by the Press Information Bureau, the Prime Minister praised the athletes for their dedication and sportsmanship, saying their performances have inspired millions of young Indians. He specifically highlighted the role of women athletes, whose dominance earned the contingent the moniker "Nari Shakti" in media reports.

Women lead India's charge in Glasgow

The Times of India reported that women athletes were the driving force behind India's golden run at the Glasgow Games. Their contributions spanned multiple disciplines, including weightlifting, boxing, and athletics, helping the nation secure its highest finishes in several events.

India's final tally included gold, silver, and bronze medals across a range of sports, with weightlifting emerging as the most successful discipline in the country's CWG history. The sport has now contributed the largest share of India's all-time Commonwealth Games medals.

India's CWG legacy: 602 medals and counting

With the Glasgow edition concluded, India's cumulative medal count at the Commonwealth Games now stands at 602, placing the nation fourth in the all-time standings. This legacy, built over decades, reflects the steady growth of Indian sport on the international stage.

According to Telegraph India, the team's performance in Glasgow has sparked discussions about whether this momentum can translate into success at the LA Olympics in 2028. Analysts point to the depth of talent in women's events and the strengthening of grassroots programmes as positive indicators.

Broader context: A week of global events

The CWG 2026 results came amid a week marked by other significant global developments. Europe continued to grapple with raging wildfires, while a devastating earthquake in Japan saw its death toll rise to 28, as reported by The Hindu in its weekly chart roundup. These events underscored the challenges facing nations even as sporting triumphs provided moments of collective pride.

For India, the Glasgow Games have reinforced the message that consistent investment in sports infrastructure and athlete support can yield tangible results. The PM's acknowledgment of the contingent's effort is likely to spur further government initiatives aimed at nurturing talent ahead of future international competitions.

What to watch next

As the dust settles on Glasgow, attention will now turn to how the Indian Olympic Association plans to build on this performance. With the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics on the horizon, the focus will be on converting CWG medals into Olympic podiums, particularly in disciplines where Indian athletes have shown promise.
