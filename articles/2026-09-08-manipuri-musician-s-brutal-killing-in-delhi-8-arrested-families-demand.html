Tragedy Strikes Delhi's Music Scene

On a fateful day in early September, the cultural hub of Delhi witnessed a heinous act that has left the nation reeling. Chongtham Vikram, a young and talented musician from Manipur, lost his life after being brutally assaulted by a mob of eight individuals. Vikram, who was known for his mesmerizing guitar performances, was merely seeking a relaxing evening with friends when the violent altercation unfolded.

Mob Lynching Raises Questions on Safety for Artists in Delhi

As authorities race to untangle the complex web of circumstances surrounding Vikram's murder, one disturbing fact emerges: a musician, often the very embodiment of creativity and peaceful expression, met a gruesome end at the hands of a violent mob. This chilling incident has sparked widespread concern over the safety and well-being of artists in Delhi, a city otherwise celebrated for its vibrant cultural landscape.

Families Demand Justice as Investigation Unfolds

In the aftermath of Vikram's brutal killing, his family, as well as the broader artistic community in Delhi, are crying out for justice. They urge authorities to conduct a thorough and impartial investigation, ensuring that all those responsible are held accountable to the fullest extent of the law. This sense of urgency is fueled by widespread outrage at the senseless violence and the realization that such incidents, while rare, can have far-reaching consequences for the cultural fabric of our society.
