Contemporary Now Edition III showcases 66 artists in Chennai

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • 66 artists participate in Contemporary Now Edition III in Chennai
  • Exhibition features diverse mediums including painting, sculpture, and new media
  • Event aims to foster dialogue between established and emerging voices
  • Organizers highlight Chennai's growing contemporary art scene

A Citywide Canvas

Chennai's art circuit is set for a significant moment as Contemporary Now Edition III opens its doors, bringing together 66 artists under one roof. The exhibition, which began on Friday, transforms the city's gallery spaces into a sprawling dialogue of styles, generations, and mediums.

Organizers describe the edition as a deliberate move to consolidate the city's position on India's contemporary art map. Unlike previous iterations, this edition leans heavily on breadth, spanning painting, sculpture, photography, and new media installations.

Beyond the Gallery Walls

The show is not confined to a single venue. Works are spread across multiple floors and sections, encouraging viewers to move through distinct thematic clusters. Early walkthroughs suggest a curatorial thread that connects personal memory with collective histories, though the organizers have refrained from imposing a single narrative.

Among the participants are both established names and younger practitioners, some of whom are showing in Chennai for the first time. This mix, according to the curatorial note, is intentional — to create an intergenerational exchange that pushes the boundaries of conventional display.

A Dialogue of Mediums

Paintings dominate in number, but the inclusion of large-scale installations and video works signals a shift in how the city consumes art. Several works respond directly to the urban environment — its traffic, its coast, its rapidly changing skyline.

One section focuses exclusively on works on paper, offering a quieter counterpoint to the more imposing pieces. Another highlights textile-based practices, a nod to the region's craft heritage, reimagined in contemporary contexts.

The exhibition also features a series of walkthroughs and artist talks scheduled over the coming weeks. These sessions, say the organizers, are designed to demystify the works and invite audiences to engage with the processes behind them.

Chennai's Art Moment

Chennai has often been overshadowed by Mumbai and Delhi in the contemporary art conversation. But recent years have seen a steady growth in independent galleries, artist-run spaces, and collector interest. Contemporary Now Edition III arrives at a time when the city's audience is increasingly willing to experiment beyond traditional forms.

The sheer scale of this edition — 66 artists — is itself a statement. It signals confidence in the local ecosystem and a desire to present multiplicity rather than a singular vision.

For those who cannot visit in person, the organizers have announced plans for a virtual walkthrough later this month, ensuring the works reach beyond the city's limits.

As the exhibition runs over the next several weeks, the critical question will be how audiences respond to the diversity on display. Early indications suggest a positive reception, with opening-day footfall exceeding expectations. The coming weeks will reveal whether this edition leaves a lasting imprint on Chennai's cultural calendar.
