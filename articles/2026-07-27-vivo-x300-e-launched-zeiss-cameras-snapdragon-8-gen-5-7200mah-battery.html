Vivo X300 E Launched: Zeiss Cameras, Snapdragon 8 Gen 5, 7200mAh Battery

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Vivo X300 E is now official with a 6.59-inch 1.5K 144Hz AMOLED display.
  • It is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset.
  • The phone features a Zeiss-backed triple rear camera setup.
  • It packs a 7200mAh battery and carries an IP69 rating for dust and water resistance.

Vivo has officially taken the wraps off its latest flagship, the X300 E, bringing a combination of high-end photography credentials and top-tier performance. The device, which had been teased in recent weeks, is now fully detailed with specifications that put it in direct competition with other premium smartphones in the market.

Display and Design

The X300 E sports a 6.59-inch AMOLED display with a sharp 1.5K resolution. The screen supports a 144Hz refresh rate, ensuring smooth scrolling and fluid animations during gaming or everyday use. Vivo has not skimped on durability either — the phone comes with an IP69 rating, meaning it is protected against high-pressure and high-temperature water jets, as well as dust ingress.

Performance and Processor

Under the hood, the X300 E is powered by Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset. This is the latest in the company's flagship line, promising significant gains in processing power and energy efficiency over its predecessor. The device is expected to handle demanding tasks like 4K video recording, high-end gaming, and multitasking with ease.

Camera System

Photography is a key focus area for the X300 E. It features a triple rear camera setup that has been co-engineered with Zeiss, a brand long associated with premium optics in both cameras and smartphones. While Vivo has not yet detailed the specific megapixel counts of each sensor, the Zeiss branding suggests advanced lens coatings and image processing algorithms aimed at reducing glare and improving color accuracy. The camera system is expected to excel in low-light conditions and portrait photography.

Battery and Charging

One of the standout features of the X300 E is its massive 7200mAh battery. This is a significant capacity that should comfortably last two days of moderate to heavy use. Vivo has not yet confirmed the wired or wireless charging speeds, but such a large battery typically comes with fast charging support to minimize downtime.

The Vivo X300 E arrives at a time when the smartphone market is increasingly competitive in the premium segment. With its combination of a high-refresh-rate display, top-tier processor, Zeiss-tuned cameras, and a marathon battery, Vivo is clearly aiming to attract users who want a device that does not compromise on any front.

Pricing and availability details for the Indian market are expected to be announced in the coming weeks. The phone is likely to be positioned as a direct rival to offerings from Samsung, OnePlus, and Xiaomi in the flagship space.
