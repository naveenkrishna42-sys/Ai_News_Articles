Vivo S2, Redmi Note 17, CMF Clip Pro launched in India this week

  • Redmi Note 17 and CMF Clip Pro earbuds also released this week

This week has been a busy one for tech enthusiasts in India, with three major launches hitting the shelves: the Vivo S2, the Redmi Note 17, and the CMF Clip Pro earbuds. Each device targets a different segment, from upper mid-range smartphones to budget-friendly audio gear.

Vivo S2: Price and Availability in India

The Vivo S2 has officially launched in India, and early reviews highlight its premium look and marathon battery life. The phone is positioned in the upper mid-range segment, directly competing with the Motorola Edge 70 Pro and Samsung Galaxy A37 5G.

Vivo has not yet disclosed the full pricing structure, but the device is expected to be available across major retail channels and online platforms. The company's newsroom has confirmed the launch, though detailed price tiers for different variants are still awaited.

Design and Display

The Vivo S2 brings a refined design language, with a glass back and a slim profile that feels premium in hand. The display is a bright AMOLED panel, likely with a high refresh rate, though Vivo hasn't confirmed the exact specifications.

Early hands-on impressions from reviewers at iDiva suggest the phone feels more expensive than its price point suggests. The device is expected to appeal to users who prioritise aesthetics without compromising on daily performance.

Battery and Performance

The standout feature of the Vivo S2, according to initial reviews, is its battery life. The device reportedly lasts well over a day on a single charge, making it a strong contender for heavy users.

Under the hood, the phone is expected to pack a capable mid-range chipset, though Vivo has not officially confirmed the processor. The camera setup is also a highlight, with a focus on low-light photography, a staple for the brand.

Redmi Note 17: The Budget King Returns

Xiaomi's Redmi Note series has long been a favourite in India, and the Redmi Note 17 continues that legacy. While detailed specs are still under wraps, the phone is expected to offer a large display, a solid battery, and a capable camera system at an aggressive price point.

The Redmi Note 17 will likely be available in multiple variants, with pricing expected to start under Rs 15,000. Xiaomi has not yet announced the official launch date, but the device is expected to hit stores soon.

CMF Clip Pro Earbuds: Affordable Audio

Nothing's sub-brand CMF has introduced the Clip Pro earbuds, a budget-friendly option for those seeking reliable wireless audio. The earbuds feature a clip-on design, which is a departure from traditional in-ear styles.

The CMF Clip Pro is expected to offer decent sound quality with a focus on comfort and portability. Pricing is expected to be competitive, though official figures are yet to be announced.

Market Positioning and Competition

The Vivo S2 faces stiff competition from the Motorola Edge 70 Pro and Samsung Galaxy A37 5G, both of which are established players in the upper mid-range segment. Comparisons have already started surfacing on tech forums, with the Vivo S2's battery life being its key differentiator.

The Redmi Note 17, on the other hand, will compete with the likes of Realme and Poco in the budget segment. The CMF Clip Pro will go up against popular budget TWS earbuds from brands like boAt and Noise.

As the festive season approaches, these launches are expected to drive significant consumer interest. Analysts will be watching closely to see how the Vivo S2 fares against its rivals in the coming weeks.

All three devices are now available for purchase or pre-order, with full pricing and availability details expected to be announced by the respective companies shortly.
