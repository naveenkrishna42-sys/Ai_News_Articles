Mumbai's potholes and privileged interns: A city divided

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mumbai's potholes highlight infrastructure neglect
  • Elite internships contrast with public suffering
  • Urban governance and inequality in spotlight

Mumbai's infrastructure crisis in stark relief

Mumbai's potholes are a stark reminder of the city's infrastructure neglect. With over 5 lakh potholes reported in 2023, the city's roads are a testament to poor urban planning and maintenance. The situation is exacerbated by the city's rapid growth, which has outpaced its ability to keep up with infrastructure development.

Elite internships: A contrast to public suffering

Amidst the potholes, Mumbai's elite internships are a glaring contrast. Top companies are offering lucrative internships to students, with salaries ranging from 50,000 to 1 lakh per month. This has raised questions about the city's ability to provide equal opportunities to all its residents.

Urban governance and inequality in the spotlight

The situation in Mumbai is a microcosm of India's urban governance challenges. While cities like Mumbai are home to some of the country's most successful corporations, the same cities struggle with basic infrastructure. This inequality is a reflection of the broader issues of urban governance and social justice in India.
