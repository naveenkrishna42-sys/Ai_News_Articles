Guardiola aids Barcelona in Rodri transfer race against Real Madrid

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Guardiola's influence may steer Rodri toward Barcelona over Real Madrid.
  • Barcelona have made a €45m offer, but City refuse a cut-price deal.
  • Real Madrid left to reassess midfield options after missing out on Rodri.

Unexpected Alliance Takes Shape

In a twist that has reshaped the summer transfer window, Pep Guardiola has emerged as Barcelona's unlikely partner in their pursuit of Manchester City midfielder Rodri. The Catalan coach, long seen as a rival to Barcelona's domestic ambitions, now appears to be quietly facilitating a move that would see the Spanish international return to his homeland.

Sources close to the negotiations suggest Guardiola's personal endorsement has been pivotal. He has reportedly told Rodri that a switch to Barcelona would be the ideal next chapter in his career, a stance that has caught Real Madrid off guard.

Barcelona's €45m Offer Rebuffed

Barcelona have tabled a formal €45m bid for the midfielder, according to reports from The Athletic. However, Manchester City have made it clear they will not sanction a cut-price departure for a player who remains central to their plans.

City's stance is firm: any deal must reflect Rodri's market value, which they believe far exceeds the initial offer. The Premier League champions are under no financial pressure to sell, and they are prepared to hold out for a fee closer to €70m.

Behind the Scenes of Real's Deception

The saga has also exposed what Goal.com describes as a secret meeting that ultimately took Rodri to Barcelona. Reports indicate that Real Madrid believed they had secured the player's commitment weeks ago, only for a clandestine rendezvous between Rodri's camp and Barcelona officials to change the narrative.

That meeting, held away from the public eye, is said to have convinced Rodri that Barcelona's project under Hansi Flick offers a more compelling sporting challenge. It was a decisive blow to Madrid, who had invested significant time in courting the player.

Real Madrid's Midfield Conundrum

With Rodri seemingly out of reach, Real Madrid are now scrambling to reassess their midfield options for the upcoming season. The club's fan site, Managing Madrid, has already begun questioning what the squad will look like without the 29-year-old anchor.

Madrid's current midfield depth has been a concern, and missing out on Rodri leaves a glaring hole in their engine room. While names like Martín Zubimendi and Bruno Guimarães have been floated, no concrete move has materialised yet.

What This Means for Rodri

For Rodri, the choice appears to be narrowing. Barcelona offer the lure of a return to La Liga and a central role in a rebuilding side, while City remain reluctant to let go of a player who has been indispensable in their recent successes.

The midfielder's preference, according to multiple reports, leans toward Barcelona, but the financial gap between offer and asking price remains a significant hurdle. Barcelona's well-documented economic constraints could yet derail the deal.

City, for their part, have not ruled out selling if the right offer arrives. But they are adamant that any buyer must meet their valuation, and they are prepared to keep Rodri for another season if not.

The Road Ahead

As the transfer window enters its final weeks, the focus now shifts to whether Barcelona can raise the necessary funds or structure a creative payment plan. Real Madrid, meanwhile, are left to explore alternative targets, though none carry the same pedigree.

The coming days will be critical. If Barcelona can bridge the gap, Rodri could become the centrepiece of their midfield for years to come. If not, City may hold onto a player who remains vital to their ambitions.
