BJP-Congress Workers Clash in Hyderabad, State BJP Chief Detained

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Clash erupted between BJP and Congress workers in Hyderabad.
  • Telangana BJP chief Ramchander Rao was detained by police.
  • The incident took place on Wednesday, July 22, 2026.

Hyderabad witnessed a violent clash between workers of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) and the Indian National Congress on Wednesday. The confrontation led to the detention of Telangana BJP chief Ramchander Rao by local police, along with several party workers.

Clash Erupts in City

According to sources, the scuffle broke out in the afternoon near a prominent junction in the city. Workers from both parties reportedly exchanged heated words before the situation escalated into a physical altercation. Stones were thrown, and vehicles were damaged in the melee.

Police teams rushed to the spot to disperse the crowds. The situation was brought under control after several minutes of tension. Authorities have not yet confirmed the exact number of people injured in the clash.

Ramchander Rao Detained

Ramchander Rao, the state BJP president, was among those detained by the police. Party workers claimed that Rao was taken into custody without any provocation. A police official, speaking on condition of anonymity, said that Rao was detained to prevent any further escalation of violence.

“He was taken into preventive custody. The situation was volatile, and we had to act to ensure public safety,” the official said. Rao was later released after a few hours, sources confirmed.

Political Reactions

The BJP has condemned the detention, calling it an act of political vendetta by the ruling Congress government in the state. Party leaders have alleged that the police acted in a biased manner. “This is undemocratic. Our chief was detained without any reason,” a local BJP leader said.

Congress workers, on the other hand, have accused the BJP of instigating the violence. They claimed that BJP workers had been trying to disrupt a peaceful protest organised by the Congress party. The Congress has demanded strict action against those responsible for the clash.

Telangana has seen a surge in political tensions in recent weeks. Both parties have been holding rallies and protests across the state, often leading to heated exchanges. Wednesday’s incident marks one of the most serious confrontations in recent months.

Police have registered a case against unidentified persons from both sides. Investigations are underway. Officials have appealed for calm and warned that any further disruption will be dealt with strictly.

The city remained on edge for several hours after the clash. Additional police forces have been deployed in sensitive areas to prevent any fresh outbreak of violence. The political slugfest between the BJP and Congress in Telangana shows no signs of cooling down.
