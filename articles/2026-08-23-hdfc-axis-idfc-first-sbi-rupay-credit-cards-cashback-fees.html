HDFC, Axis, IDFC FIRST, SBI RuPay credit cards: Cashback & fees

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Credit Cards & Cashback Original source ↗
Key points
  • Cashback rates vary by category and card
  • Annual fees range from Rs 0 to Rs 500
  • Discounts available on travel, dining, and shopping

Cashback Rates and Categories

RuPay credit cards from HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, IDFC FIRST Bank, and SBI Card offer cashback on various categories. HDFC Bank's Diners Club Black card provides 5% cashback on dining and entertainment, while the Axis Bank Magnum card offers 5% cashback on fuel purchases. IDFC FIRST Bank's Millennia card gives 5% cashback on travel bookings, and SBI Card's SimplyCLICK card provides 5% cashback on online shopping.

Annual Fees

The annual fees for these RuPay credit cards range from Rs 0 to Rs 500. HDFC Bank's Diners Club Black card has an annual fee of Rs 500, while Axis Bank's Magnum card has an annual fee of Rs 0. IDFC FIRST Bank's Millennia card has an annual fee of Rs 0, and SBI Card's SimplyCLICK card also has an annual fee of Rs 0.

Discounts

Discounts are available on travel, dining, and shopping. HDFC Bank's Diners Club Black card offers discounts on dining and entertainment. Axis Bank's Magnum card provides discounts on fuel purchases. IDFC FIRST Bank's Millennia card offers discounts on travel bookings, and SBI Card's SimplyCLICK card provides discounts on online shopping.

Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility criteria vary by card. HDFC Bank's Diners Club Black card requires a minimum income of Rs 5 lakh per annum. Axis Bank's Magnum card requires a minimum income of Rs 3 lakh per annum. IDFC FIRST Bank's Millennia card requires a minimum income of Rs 2 lakh per annum, and SBI Card's SimplyCLICK card requires a minimum income of Rs 1 lakh per annum.

How to Apply

To apply for these RuPay credit cards, visit the respective bank's website or branch. Fill out the application form and submit the required documents. Once approved, the card will be delivered to your address.
