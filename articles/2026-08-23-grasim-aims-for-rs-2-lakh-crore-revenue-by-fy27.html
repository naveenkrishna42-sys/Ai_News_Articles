Grasim aims for Rs 2 lakh crore revenue by FY27

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Grasim Industries aims for Rs 2 lakh crore revenue by FY27.
  • Growth platforms are scaling up to achieve this target.
  • Q1 FY27 shows a 49% rise in adjusted PAT to INR 2,153 crore.

Grasim Industries sets ambitious revenue target for FY27

Grasim Industries, a leading player in the Indian chemical and fertiliser sectors, has set an ambitious target of reaching Rs 2 lakh crore in revenue by the fiscal year ending March 2027. This announcement was made by Kumar Mangalam Birla, Chairman of the company, highlighting the company's confidence in its growth platforms.

Growth platforms driving revenue surge

Grasim Industries is scaling up its growth platforms to achieve this target. The company has been focusing on expanding its presence in the chemical and fertiliser markets, which are crucial for its revenue generation. The company's diversified portfolio includes fertiliser plants, urea plants, and chemical manufacturing units.

Q1 FY27 shows strong financial performance

Grasim Industries' Q1 FY27 results have shown a strong financial performance. The company's adjusted PAT (Profit After Tax) rose by 49% to INR 2,153 crore. This increase is a testament to the company's effective management and strategic initiatives.

Standalone EBITDA and CSF revenue show significant rise

The company's standalone EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization) has shown a significant rise, contributing to the overall revenue growth. The company's Chemical Specialty Fibres (CSF) segment has also shown a rise in revenue and EBITDA, further bolstering the company's financial health.

What happens next?

As Grasim Industries continues to scale up its growth platforms, it will be interesting to see how the company's revenue and financial performance evolve. The company's focus on the chemical and fertiliser markets, along with its diversified portfolio, positions it well for future growth. Investors and stakeholders will be closely watching the company's progress towards its ambitious target of Rs 2 lakh crore revenue by FY27.
