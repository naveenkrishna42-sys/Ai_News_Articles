HMDA to auction 35 residential plots in Hyderabad

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • HMDA to auction 35 residential plots in Bachupally on September 2, 2026
  • Plots located in Bachupally, Hyderabad
  • Bids open to public, starting from ₹10 lakh per plot

HMDA to auction 35 residential plots in Bachupally on September 2, 2026

The Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority (HMDA) is set to auction 35 residential plots in Bachupally on September 2, 2026. The plots, located in Bachupally, Hyderabad, are part of the ongoing efforts by the HMDA to develop and manage the city's urban infrastructure.

Location and Details of the Plots

The 35 residential plots are situated in Bachupally, a rapidly developing area in Hyderabad. The plots are part of the HMDA's ongoing projects aimed at providing affordable housing solutions to the city's residents. The exact location and size of each plot have not been disclosed yet, but officials have confirmed that the plots are ready for development.

Auction Process and Bidding Details

The auction will be conducted on September 2, 2026, at the HMDA office in Bachupally. Bids will be open to the public, and the starting bid for each plot is ₹10 lakh. The HMDA has not yet confirmed the minimum increment for the bids, but it is expected to be around ₹1 lakh. The auction will be conducted in the presence of HMDA officials and legal representatives.

Importance of the Auction

The auction of these 35 residential plots is significant for Bachupally and Hyderabad as a whole. The plots are expected to be developed into residential complexes, which will provide much-needed housing solutions to the city's residents. The HMDA is hopeful that the auction will attract a wide range of bidders, including developers, investors, and individuals.

What Happens Next?

After the auction, the HMDA will conduct a thorough verification process to ensure that the winning bidder is eligible and has the necessary resources to develop the plots. Once the verification process is complete, the winning bidder will be required to sign a memorandum of understanding with the HMDA, outlining the terms and conditions of the development. The development of the plots is expected to take around 18-24 months, after which the residential complexes will be ready for occupancy.
