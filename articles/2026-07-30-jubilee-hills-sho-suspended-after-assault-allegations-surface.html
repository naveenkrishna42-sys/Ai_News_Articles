Jubilee Hills SHO suspended after assault allegations surface

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jubilee Hills Station House Officer (SHO) suspended
  • Allegations of assault made against the officer
  • Internal inquiry ordered by Hyderabad police commissioner

SHO Suspended Over Assault Allegations

Hyderabad police have suspended the Station House Officer (SHO) of the Jubilee Hills police station following allegations of assault. The decision was taken after a preliminary review of the complaint against the officer.

The suspension order was issued late Tuesday evening. The officer has been attached to the police lines pending a detailed internal inquiry.

Nature of the Allegations

While the exact details of the assault are still emerging, sources confirm that a formal complaint was filed against the SHO. The complainant alleged physical mistreatment by the officer during an incident in the Jubilee Hills area.

Police authorities have not released the name of the complainant or the specific circumstances of the alleged assault. Officials said the investigation is in its early stages.

Internal Inquiry Ordered

Hyderabad Police Commissioner has ordered a thorough internal probe into the matter. A senior officer from the Crimes Branch has been tasked with conducting the inquiry and submitting a report within a week.

“Any misconduct by police personnel will be dealt with strictly,” a senior police official said, declining to be named. The official added that the department is committed to transparency and accountability.

The suspended officer has been asked to submit his written explanation regarding the allegations. Disciplinary action will follow based on the findings of the inquiry.

Broader Context

This suspension comes amid heightened scrutiny of police conduct in Hyderabad. In recent months, the police department has faced questions over handling of certain cases and interactions with the public.

Police authorities have repeatedly emphasised their zero-tolerance policy towards misconduct. The suspension of the Jubilee Hills SHO is seen as a step to reinforce that stance.

The inquiry will now determine the veracity of the assault allegations and the appropriate course of action. The officer remains suspended until further orders.

The investigation report is expected to be submitted within the next week, after which the police department will decide on further disciplinary or legal proceedings.
