3 Idiots Re-Releases in Theatres on September 4, 2026; NH Studioz Acquires Vidhu Vinod Chopra Films Catalogue

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • 3 Idiots will re-release in theatres on September 4, 2026.
  • NH Studioz has acquired the catalogue of Vidhu Vinod Chopra Films in a three-figure crore deal.
  • The acquisition is a landmark in Indian cinema, bringing iconic films back to the big screen.

3 Idiots Returns to the Big Screen

Bollywood fans have a date to mark on their calendars. The iconic film 3 Idiots, starring Aamir Khan, R. Madhavan, and Sharman Joshi, will re-release in cinemas on September 4, 2026. The announcement comes as part of a broader move by NH Studioz, which has sealed a landmark three-figure crore deal to acquire the entire catalogue of Vidhu Vinod Chopra Films.

This is not just a nostalgic re-run. The re-release aims to bring the 2009 blockbuster back to a new generation of viewers, many of whom may have only seen it on streaming platforms. The film, directed by Rajkumar Hirani, remains one of the highest-grossing Indian films of all time.

A Landmark Deal for Indian Cinema

The acquisition by NH Studioz is being described as a landmark in the Indian film industry. The three-figure crore deal gives the studio rights to a treasure trove of films produced by Vidhu Vinod Chopra Films, including critically acclaimed titles like Munna Bhai M.B.B.S., Lage Raho Munna Bhai, and Parinda.

Industry insiders say this move signals a growing trend of consolidation in the Indian film market. Studios are increasingly looking to own and exploit classic content, especially as theatrical re-releases gain traction. NH Studioz has not yet disclosed the exact financial terms of the deal, but sources confirm it runs into several hundred crores.

What This Means for Movie Lovers

For audiences, the immediate benefit is the chance to watch 3 Idiots on the big screen again. The film's re-release on September 4, 2026, is expected to be accompanied by special screenings and possibly enhanced formats. NH Studioz has hinted at more re-releases from the acquired catalogue in the coming months.

This also opens up possibilities for restored versions of classic films. Vidhu Vinod Chopra's body of work, known for its strong narratives and technical finesse, is ripe for digital restoration and theatrical presentation. Fans of the Munna Bhai series, in particular, are hoping for similar re-releases.

Behind the Deal

Vidhu Vinod Chopra Films has been a powerhouse in Hindi cinema for decades. The production house, founded by filmmaker Vidhu Vinod Chopra, is known for its quality over quantity approach. The decision to sell the catalogue rights to NH Studioz marks a strategic shift, allowing the production house to focus on new projects while ensuring its legacy reaches wider audiences.

NH Studioz, on the other hand, is aggressively expanding its footprint. The studio has been active in both production and distribution, and this acquisition gives it a formidable library of content. The deal is expected to be a win-win: the films get a new lease of life, and the studio gains a steady revenue stream from re-releases and licensing.

Officials from both companies have not commented on the specifics of the agreement. However, the announcement has already generated buzz in trade circles. Analysts believe this could set a precedent for similar catalogue acquisitions in the Indian film industry.

Looking Ahead

With 3 Idiots set to return on September 4, 2026, all eyes are now on NH Studioz to see which classic they revive next. The re-release calendar is expected to be announced in phases. For now, fans can start planning their trip to the theatre for one of Bollywood's most beloved films.
