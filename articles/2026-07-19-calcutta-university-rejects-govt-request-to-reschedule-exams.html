Calcutta University Rejects Govt Request to Reschedule Exams

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • Calcutta University has declined the West Bengal Higher Education Department's request to change the exam schedule.
  • University officials cited logistical constraints and student readiness as reasons for the rejection.
  • The decision has sparked debate among students and faculty about academic autonomy versus government oversight.

CU Stands Firm on Exam Timetable

Calcutta University (CU) has rejected a request from the West Bengal Higher Education Department to revise the upcoming examination schedule. The decision, communicated earlier this week, means students will sit for their papers as originally planned.

University authorities confirmed that the existing timetable remains unchanged. Officials argue that any alteration at this stage would disrupt preparations and create administrative chaos.

Government's Request and University's Reasoning

The Higher Education Department had sought a postponement, citing concerns over student welfare and logistical issues. However, CU's examination committee found no compelling reason to delay.

A senior university administrator, speaking on condition of anonymity, said, "Rescheduling would require rebooking venues, reprinting question papers, and notifying lakhs of students. The current schedule was published months in advance."

The university also pointed to the academic calendar, noting that any shift could push results into the next session, affecting admissions and job placements.

Mixed Reactions from Students and Faculty

Student groups have responded with a mix of relief and anxiety. Some welcome the clarity, while others worry about inadequate preparation time. Faculty members are divided, with some backing the university's autonomy and others calling for more flexibility.

"We respect the university's decision, but the government's concerns should not be ignored," said a professor from the arts faculty who did not wish to be named.

What This Means for Academic Governance

The standoff highlights the ongoing tension between state oversight and institutional independence. Calcutta University, one of India's oldest, has traditionally guarded its academic calendar from external interference.

Education experts say such clashes are common in West Bengal, where the government often tries to align university schedules with broader policy goals. The outcome sets a precedent for how future disputes might be handled.

Officials from the Higher Education Department have not commented on the rejection. Sources indicate they may raise the matter again in a review meeting later this month.

For now, students must stick to their revision plans. The next set of examinations will begin as scheduled next week, with no further changes anticipated.
