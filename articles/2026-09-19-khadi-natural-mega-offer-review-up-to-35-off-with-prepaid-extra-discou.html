Real‑World Performance & Daily Use

In hands‑on testing, Khadi Natural’s face washes delivered a gentle yet thorough cleanse, leaving skin feeling refreshed without the tightness often associated with sulfate‑based cleansers. Haircare products, such as the almond‑oil shampoo, produced a soft lather that rinsed cleanly, imparting a subtle shine and reducing frizz after several washes.
