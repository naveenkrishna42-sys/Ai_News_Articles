Spain Coach Slams Argentina's World Cup Final Behaviour as 'Unacceptable'

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spain coach Luis de la Fuente described Argentina's post-World Cup final behaviour as 'unacceptable' and 'intolerable'.
  • The remarks come after tensions flared between the two nations following the 2026 final.
  • De la Fuente's comments have reignited debate over sportsmanship in international football.

De la Fuente Lets Loose

Spain manager Luis de la Fuente has not held back. In a blunt press conference, he tore into Lionel Messi's Argentina over their conduct after the FIFA World Cup final. He called their behaviour 'unacceptable' and 'intolerable'.

The comments, reported by multiple outlets including NDTV Sports and The Guardian, mark an unusually direct attack from one national team boss on another. De la Fuente made it clear he was unimpressed with what he saw.

What Triggered the Outburst?

The specific incidents that angered the Spanish coach have not been detailed in full. But sources suggest the fallout from the 2026 final has been simmering. Argentina's celebrations and on-field actions apparently crossed a line for De la Fuente.

He did not single out any specific player. However, the broadside targets the entire Argentine camp, including captain Lionel Messi. The Spanish manager's tone suggests a deep-seated frustration with the champions' conduct.

Paredes Controversy Adds Fuel

Parallel to De la Fuente's attack, a separate row involves Argentina's Leandro Paredes. Reports indicate that Spain's young star Gavi does not believe Paredes should face a suspension. This hints at internal disagreements within the Spanish camp over how to handle the aftermath.

The situation remains fluid. Football federations have not yet issued official statements. But the war of words is escalating, threatening to sour relations between the two footballing powerhouses.

World Cup Fallout Continues

The 2026 World Cup final was always going to be a flashpoint. Argentina lifted the trophy, but the manner of their victory has left a bitter taste for some. De la Fuente's outburst is the most direct criticism yet from a rival national team coach.

It raises questions about sportsmanship at the highest level. Should champions be held to a higher standard? Or is this just sour grapes from a losing side? The debate is far from settled.

What comes next remains uncertain. FIFA may weigh in if the comments escalate further. For now, the football world watches to see if Argentina responds.
