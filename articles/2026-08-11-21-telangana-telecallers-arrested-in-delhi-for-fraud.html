21 Telangana Telecallers Arrested in Delhi for Fraud

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • 21 individuals from Telangana were arrested in Delhi.
  • The arrests are linked to fraudulent telecalling activities.
  • Delhi Police are investigating the extent of the operations.

In a significant crackdown on fraudulent telemarketing operations, Delhi Police have arrested 21 individuals hailing from Telangana. The arrests, made in the national capital, come as part of an ongoing investigation into illicit call centres that have been targeting unsuspecting victims across the country.

Operation Details

The detained individuals were found operating from a facility in Delhi, where they allegedly posed as representatives of various financial institutions. Preliminary inquiries suggest the group was engaged in extracting sensitive personal information from call recipients, which was then likely used for monetary fraud.

Police sources indicate that the telecallers had been active for several months. Their modus operandi involved convincing victims to share bank details or make payments for non-existent services. The exact location of the call centre and the duration of its operation have not been officially disclosed.

Telangana Connection

All 21 arrested individuals are residents of Telangana, a state that has increasingly become a hub for both legitimate IT services and, unfortunately, cybercrime. Authorities are now examining whether these telecallers were part of a larger syndicate with links back to Hyderabad or other cities in the southern state.

This arrest highlights a worrying trend of interstate criminal activity, where operatives are recruited from one region to work in another, often to evade local scrutiny. Investigators are probing how the group was recruited and whether they were working independently or under a larger organised network.

Modus Operandi

According to initial findings, the telecallers would use scripts to pose as customer care executives from well-known companies. They would often lure victims with promises of refunds, lottery winnings, or investment opportunities. The calls were reportedly made using unregistered SIM cards and VoIP technology, making them difficult to trace.

The Delhi Police have seized several computers, mobile phones, and documents from the premises. Forensic analysis of these devices is expected to reveal a trove of data, including call logs and potential victim lists. This evidence will be crucial in establishing the scale of the fraud.

Victims and Next Steps

While the total number of victims is yet to be determined, the police believe that hundreds of people may have been affected. Many of the calls were likely made to individuals in northern and central India, given the Delhi-based operation. Officials have urged anyone who received suspicious calls from such numbers to come forward and file complaints.

The arrested individuals are expected to be produced before a local court, where the police will seek their custody for further interrogation. The investigation is focused on unravelling the network's financial trail, including the bank accounts where the defrauded money was diverted.

Authorities are also coordinating with their counterparts in Telangana to understand the recruitment process and check if any similar call centres are operating in other states. As cyber fraud cases rise nationwide, this operation serves as a reminder of the persistent threat posed by such organised scams.

Moving forward, the Delhi Police will likely issue advisories to the public on identifying and reporting fraudulent calls. The case also underscores the need for stronger interstate cooperation in tackling cyber-enabled financial crimes.
