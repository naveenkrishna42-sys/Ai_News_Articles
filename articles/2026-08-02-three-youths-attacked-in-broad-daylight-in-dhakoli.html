Three youths attacked in broad daylight in Dhakoli

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Science Original source ↗
Key points
  • Three youths were attacked in Dhakoli's market area on Saturday afternoon.
  • The incident occurred in broad daylight, causing panic among locals.
  • Police have registered a case and launched a search for the attackers.

Attack in busy market

Three youths were attacked with sharp-edged weapons in Dhakoli's main market area on Saturday afternoon, leaving locals shaken. The incident took place around 1:30 pm when the victims, all in their early twenties, were standing near a shop. Witnesses said a group of four to five men, some wearing helmets, arrived on motorcycles and launched a sudden assault.

The attackers fled before anyone could intervene. The injured were rushed to a nearby hospital, where their condition is stated to be stable. Police have not yet confirmed the exact nature of their injuries.

Police response and investigation

Dhakoli police reached the spot after being informed by bystanders. A case has been registered under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, though officials have not specified the charges. The police are scanning CCTV footage from nearby establishments to identify the assailants.

Sources suggest a previous altercation may be linked to the attack, but nothing has been confirmed. "We are examining all angles and will take appropriate action," a police officer said on condition of anonymity.

Eyewitness accounts

Eyewitnesses described the scene as chaotic, with shoppers rushing for cover. "It happened so quickly. They just came, attacked, and left. We couldn't do anything," said a shopkeeper who did not wish to be named. Another witness claimed the victims appeared to know their attackers, but this has not been verified.

The market area, usually crowded at that hour, saw panic as people tried to help the injured. Some passersby helped move the victims to safety before the ambulance arrived.

Rising concerns over public safety

The attack has raised concerns about safety in Dhakoli, a bustling town near Chandigarh. Residents have demanded increased police patrolling in commercial areas. "This is a busy market, not a lonely lane. Such incidents make us feel unsafe," said a local trader.

Police have assured the public that additional patrols will be deployed. They have also appealed to anyone with information to come forward. The investigation is ongoing, and more details are expected once the victims are able to give their statements.

As the search for the attackers continues, the focus will be on how quickly the police can identify and apprehend those responsible. The injured youths' families have urged authorities to ensure a thorough probe.
