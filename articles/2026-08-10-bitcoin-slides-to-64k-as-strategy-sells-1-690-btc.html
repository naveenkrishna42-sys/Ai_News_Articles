Bitcoin slides to $64K as Strategy sells 1,690 BTC

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin dropped to $64,000 following Strategy's sale of 1,690 BTC.
  • The sale, reported Monday, adds to selling pressure in the crypto market.
  • Investors are watching for further institutional moves and market stability.

Market jitters as Strategy trims holdings

Bitcoin slid to $64,000 on Monday after Strategy, the corporate treasury firm previously known as MicroStrategy, disclosed the sale of 1,690 BTC. The move, reported in a live update, marks a rare reduction in the company's massive digital asset stockpile, which has long been a bellwether for institutional crypto confidence.

The drop, which saw prices fall from recent highs, came as traders digested the news. Strategy's decision to sell even a fraction of its holdings—roughly 1,690 coins, worth over $108 million at current prices—has sparked questions about whether the firm is shifting strategy or simply managing liquidity.

Why the sale matters

Strategy has been the largest corporate holder of Bitcoin, accumulating coins steadily over years. Its purchases have often coincided with market rallies, and its holding pattern has been seen as a bullish signal. A sale, however small, inverts that narrative.

Market analysts note that the sale represents a tiny percentage of Strategy's total stack, which exceeds 200,000 BTC. Yet the psychological impact is immediate: if the most vocal Bitcoin advocate is trimming, others may follow suit.

The timing is also sensitive. Bitcoin had been hovering near $66,000 before the news broke, and the drop to $64,000 reflects a swift repricing. Trading volumes spiked on major exchanges, though data on the exact size of the sell-off is still emerging.

Investor reaction and broader context

Retail and institutional investors alike are watching closely. Some see the sale as a routine portfolio adjustment, while others worry it signals a broader risk-off mood in crypto markets. The broader digital asset sector has been volatile this year, with regulatory pressures and macroeconomic headwinds keeping prices in check.

Strategy's move comes at a time when other large holders, including miners and ETFs, are also adjusting positions. The interplay between these players is likely to dictate short-term price action.

Officials at Strategy have not yet detailed the reason for the sale, and the company has not responded to requests for comment. The lack of clarity adds to market uncertainty, as traders piece together clues from on-chain data and exchange flows.

What to watch next

The immediate focus is on whether Bitcoin can hold the $64,000 level. A break below could trigger further selling, while a rebound would signal that the market views this as a blip. Strategy's next regulatory filing, which will detail its holdings, is due later this month and will show whether this sale was a one-off or the start of a trend.

For now, the crypto market remains on edge, with every large transaction scrutinised for meaning. The coming days will reveal if this is a minor correction or a turning point.
