Ethereum's Price Action on August 7

As of August 7, 2026, Ethereum is trading within a range that reflects the broader crypto market's cautious optimism. At the time of writing, Ethereum's price hovers near the $3,400 mark, a level that has acted as both support and resistance in recent sessions. This consolidation phase follows a sharp rally in late July that was driven by positive regulatory signals.

What's Driving the Market Today?

Several factors are influencing Ethereum's price today. First, the broader equity markets are steady, providing a neutral backdrop for risk assets. Second, on-chain data shows a slight uptick in whale transactions, suggesting that large holders are accumulating rather than distributing. This is often interpreted as a bullish signal by traders.

Meanwhile, the upcoming Ethereum network upgrade, slated for later this year, continues to generate speculative interest. Developers have confirmed that the upgrade will introduce several scalability improvements, which could enhance the network's appeal to institutional users. However, no specific timeline for the rollout has been announced, leaving room for uncertainty.

Technical Levels to Watch

From a technical perspective, Ethereum's immediate support lies at $3,350, a level that has been tested multiple times over the past week. A break below this could trigger a slide toward $3,200, where stronger buying interest is expected. On the upside, resistance is firmly placed at $3,500, and a daily close above this threshold could pave the way for a retest of $3,700.

The Relative Strength Index (RSI) is currently sitting at 52, indicating neutral momentum. This suggests that neither buyers nor sellers have a decisive edge at the moment. Volume patterns also show a lack of conviction, with traders awaiting a clear catalyst to establish direction.
