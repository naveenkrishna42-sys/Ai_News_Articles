Nifty resilience tested as oil prices surge; Sensex opens lower

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty opened in the red as global crude prices climbed further.
  • Rising oil prices stoked fears of higher import costs and inflation.
  • Broader market indices also faced selling pressure in early trade.

Markets open weak on oil price jump

Indian stock markets opened on a cautious note Wednesday, with the Sensex slipping over 200 points and the Nifty hovering near the 15,800 mark. The sell-off was driven by a fresh spike in global crude oil prices, which breached earlier resistance levels overnight.

Brent crude futures rose above $85 per barrel, adding to worries about India's import bill and domestic inflation. The energy-sensitive sectors — oil & gas, aviation, and auto — were among the top losers in early trade.

Nifty support levels under scrutiny

The Nifty 50 index has been testing its immediate support at 15,750 for the third straight session. Analysts say a sustained close below this level could trigger further downside.

“The resilience of the Nifty is being severely tested. With oil prices refusing to cool, the risk of a sharp correction is real,” a market strategist told CNBC TV18.

Volatility index India VIX inched up, indicating nervousness among traders. Broader indices such as the BSE Midcap and Smallcap also fell up to 0.5% in early deals.

Crude oil impact on Indian economy

India imports about 85% of its crude oil requirements. Every $10 rise in oil prices can widen the current account deficit by roughly $15 billion, according to economists. The continued rally in crude is expected to put pressure on the rupee and fuel pump prices.

Oil marketing companies like HPCL, BPCL, and IOC were trading lower as margins came under scrutiny. The auto sector also faced headwinds, with stocks like Maruti Suzuki and Tata Motors declining on fears of input cost pressure.

What to watch next

Traders will closely monitor the weekly crude inventory data from the US and any verbal intervention from OPEC+ members. On the domestic front, the RBI's commentary on inflation in its upcoming policy review will be key.

For now, the Nifty's ability to hold above 15,750 could determine whether the market stages a bounce or drifts lower in the coming sessions.
