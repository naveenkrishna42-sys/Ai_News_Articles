Zafar, Riasat guide Pakistan Women to consolation T20I win over Sri Lanka

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pakistan Women defeated Sri Lanka in the third and final T20I in Dambulla.
  • Zafar and Riasat were the key contributors with bat and ball respectively.
  • Sri Lanka had already sealed the series with a six-wicket win in the previous match.
  • The win gives Pakistan a consolation result after the hosts dominated the white-ball leg.

Pakistan Women's cricket team ended their tour of Sri Lanka on a positive note, securing a consolation victory in the third and final T20I at Dambulla. The win came after the hosts had already claimed the series, adding a touch of pride to an otherwise challenging outing for the visitors.

Zafar's batting masterclass

Opening the innings, Zafar played a crucial knock that laid the foundation for Pakistan's competitive total. Her measured approach, blending caution with aggression at the right moments, ensured the visitors posted a defendable score on a surface that offered some assistance to the bowlers.

The middle order provided steady support, but it was Zafar's innings that stood out. Her ability to rotate strike and find the boundary at regular intervals kept the scoreboard moving, putting pressure on the Sri Lankan bowlers.

Riasat's bowling brilliance

In response, Sri Lanka's chase never quite gained momentum, largely due to a disciplined bowling effort led by Riasat. The bowler's accurate lines and clever variations made scoring difficult, and she picked up key wickets at crucial junctures.

Riasat's spell dismantled the hosts' top order, leaving the middle order with too much to do. The fielding unit backed her up with sharp catches and quick throws, ensuring no easy runs were conceded.

Series context

The series had already been decided in Sri Lanka's favour after they clinched a six-wicket win in the second T20I, completing a white-ball double over Pakistan. That victory followed their success in the ODI leg, underscoring the hosts' dominance throughout the tour.

For Pakistan, this final-match win serves as a morale booster. It demonstrates that the team can compete and close out tight games, even if consistency remains a work in progress.

What lies ahead

Both teams will now shift focus to their upcoming assignments. Pakistan will look to carry this winning feeling forward, while Sri Lanka will aim to maintain their strong form. The result at Dambulla, though a consolation, offers a glimpse of the competitiveness both sides bring to the field.

The cricketing calendar continues with more bilateral series and tournaments, and fans will be watching to see if Pakistan can build on this momentum or if Sri Lanka will quickly reassert their superiority.
