Spider-Man: Brand New Day swings to record Rs 60 crore opening in India

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day earned Rs 60 crore on its opening day in India.
  • The film surpassed Avengers: Endgame's previous record for the biggest Hollywood opening day.
  • It also outperformed the day-one collections of Dhurandhar and Pathaan.
  • Directed by Destin Daniel Cretton, the film has been praised as a soulful reinvention of the character.

MUMBAI: Spider-Man: Brand New Day has shattered box office records in India, posting the biggest opening day for a Hollywood release in the country. The film, starring Tom Holland, collected a staggering Rs 60 crore on its first day, surpassing the previous benchmark set by Avengers: Endgame.

The numbers, reported by trade analysts, mark a significant milestone for the Marvel Cinematic Universe's latest instalment. The film has not only outperformed its Hollywood predecessors but has also outdone major Bollywood releases, including Dhurandhar and Pathaan, on their respective opening days.

A New Benchmark for Hollywood in India

Avengers: Endgame had long held the record for the highest opening day collections for a Hollywood film in India. Spider-Man: Brand New Day has now eclipsed that figure, underscoring the growing appetite for superhero cinema in the Indian market. The Rs 60 crore haul reflects strong advance bookings and widespread theatrical releases across the country.

The film's performance is particularly notable given the mixed box office fortunes of recent big-ticket releases. Industry observers see this as a sign of the enduring popularity of the Spider-Man franchise and Tom Holland's portrayal of the web-slinger.

Critical Acclaim and Audience Response

Beyond the commercial success, Spider-Man: Brand New Day has drawn positive reviews from critics. The Hindu's review called it a "soulful reinvention" and noted that Tom Holland "finally swings free" under the direction of Destin Daniel Cretton. This combination of critical praise and audience enthusiasm appears to have driven the record-breaking opening.

The film's narrative, which explores a fresh chapter in Peter Parker's life, has resonated with both longtime fans and newcomers. Early word-of-mouth suggests sustained momentum, with many predicting strong weekend collections.

Impact on the Indian Box Office

The film's success is a boost for exhibitors who have been banking on big releases to draw crowds. The Rs 60 crore opening day figure includes contributions from multiplexes and single screens alike, with major metros leading the charge. Hindi, English, and other dubbed versions have all performed well, reflecting the film's pan-India appeal.

Comparisons with Dhurandhar and Pathaan, both of which opened to impressive numbers, highlight the scale of this achievement. Spider-Man: Brand New Day has managed to top them, setting a new standard for opening day collections in the country.

What Lies Ahead

With the weekend underway, trade analysts are watching to see if the film can sustain its pace. The holiday period and positive reviews could help it cross the Rs 150 crore mark in its opening weekend, though official figures are yet to be confirmed. The film's long-term performance will depend on repeat viewings and family audiences in the coming weeks.

For now, Spider-Man: Brand New Day has firmly established itself as a box office phenomenon in India, and all eyes will be on its second-day numbers to gauge the full extent of its run.
