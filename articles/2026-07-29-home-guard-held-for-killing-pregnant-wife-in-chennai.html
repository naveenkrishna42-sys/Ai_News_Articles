Home Guard held for killing pregnant wife in Chennai

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • The accused, a Home Guard personnel, was arrested for allegedly killing his wife.
  • The victim was five months pregnant at the time of the incident.
  • The accused reportedly confessed to the crime, which followed a domestic argument.

Arrest in Chennai domestic violence case

Chennai police have arrested a Home Guard personnel for allegedly murdering his pregnant wife. The incident took place in the city, and the accused was taken into custody shortly after the body was discovered.

The victim, who was five months pregnant, was found dead in her home. Neighbors alerted police after hearing a disturbance. Officers arrived at the scene and found the woman with fatal injuries.

Accused confesses to the crime

During interrogation, the accused reportedly confessed to the killing. He told police that a domestic argument escalated, leading to the attack. Investigators have not yet released details about the weapon used or the exact sequence of events.

Police sources confirmed that the couple had been married for a few years. The accused had been serving as a Home Guard for the Chennai city police. His colleagues described him as a quiet individual, though they noted he had been under stress recently.

Legal proceedings initiated

The accused has been booked under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including murder. He was produced before a magistrate and remanded in judicial custody. The case has been transferred to the city crime branch for further investigation.

Officials have not ruled out the possibility of prior domestic violence complaints. They are checking records to see if the victim had ever filed a complaint against her husband.

The body has been sent for post-mortem, and the report is awaited. Police are also conducting forensic analysis at the crime scene.

Growing concern over domestic violence

This case has brought attention to domestic violence in Chennai. Activists have pointed out that such incidents often go unreported until they turn fatal. The victim was five months pregnant, making the crime particularly heinous.

Police have urged anyone facing domestic abuse to seek help immediately. Helplines and support services are available for those in distress.

The investigation is ongoing. More details are expected as the probe progresses.
