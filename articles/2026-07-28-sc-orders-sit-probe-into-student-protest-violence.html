SC Orders SIT Probe Into Student Protest Violence

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • SC orders SIT probe into student protest violence
  • Release of detained minors directed
  • Meta summoned over post removal

Supreme Court Intervention

The Supreme Court has taken a significant step in addressing the recent student protest violence by ordering a Special Investigation Team (SIT) probe into the matter. This move aims to ensure a thorough and impartial investigation into the incidents of violence that occurred during the protests.

Officials have not yet confirmed the specifics of the SIT probe, but it is expected to examine the circumstances surrounding the violence and identify those responsible.

Release of Detained Minors

The Supreme Court has also directed the release of detained minors who were taken into custody during the protests. This order is seen as a welcome move, as it prioritizes the well-being and safety of the minors involved.

The court's decision emphasizes the need to handle such situations with care and sensitivity, ensuring that the rights of all individuals, particularly minors, are protected.

Meta Summoned

In a related development, the Supreme Court has summoned Meta, the parent company of social media platforms, over the removal of a post related to the student protests. The court's move suggests that it is keen to examine the role of social media in such situations and ensure that freedom of expression is not compromised.

Student Protests

The student protests have sparked a nationwide debate on the handling of dissent and the need for effective communication between the government and protesters. An IIT student has moved the Supreme Court, seeking a standard operating procedure (SOP) on how the State handles dissent.

The student's petition highlights the need for a clear and transparent approach to managing protests, ensuring that the rights of protesters are respected and that excessive force is not used.

Next Steps

The SIT probe and the release of detained minors are expected to be significant steps in addressing the concerns surrounding the student protest violence. As the situation unfolds, the focus will be on the outcome of the SIT probe and the measures taken by the authorities to prevent such incidents in the future.
