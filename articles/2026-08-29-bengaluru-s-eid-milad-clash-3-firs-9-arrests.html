Eid-Milad Clashes Erupt in Bengaluru's Basavanagudi

Bengaluru's Basavanagudi neighborhood witnessed violent clashes during the Eid-Milad celebrations, resulting in three FIRs being filed and nine individuals being arrested. The incident has raised concerns about religious sensitivity and community tensions in the city.

Background of the Incident

The Eid-Milad celebration, which commemorates the birth of Prophet Muhammad and the migration of the Prophet's followers to Madinah, is a significant event for the Muslim community. However, the celebration turned violent when tensions between different religious and ethnic groups escalated.

Key Developments and Arrests

Three FIRs have been filed in connection with the violence, with nine individuals arrested. The police have stated that the arrests are based on eyewitness accounts and CCTV footage. The arrested individuals are said to be members of various religious and ethnic groups, highlighting the complex nature of the incident.

The incident has brought to light the underlying tensions between different religious and ethnic groups in Bengaluru. The police have urged the community to maintain peace and avoid any further violence.
