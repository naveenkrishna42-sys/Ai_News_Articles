FSSAI bans ghee sales, suspends retailer's licence

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • FSSAI bans SDP Industries ghee sales over adulteration concerns
  • Marche Retail's licence suspended for food safety lapses
  • Authority to conduct surprise checks at retail outlets

FSSAI takes action against adulterated ghee

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) has banned the sale of ghee produced by SDP Industries, following concerns over adulteration. The authority has also suspended the licence of Delhi-based Marche Retail for lapses in food safety and hygiene.

Adulteration concerns and licence suspension

SDP Industries' ghee has been found to contain unsafe levels of chemicals, prompting FSSAI to take immediate action. The ban on the ghee sales is part of the FSSAI's ongoing crackdown on food adulteration in the country.

Marche Retail's licence has been suspended due to multiple violations of food safety and hygiene standards. The retailer has been found to have inadequate training for staff and poor maintenance of storage facilities.

Authority to conduct surprise checks

In a bid to ensure better compliance with food safety norms, FSSAI has decided to conduct surprise checks at retail outlets across the country. The checks will be aimed at verifying the authenticity of food products being sold and ensuring adherence to food safety standards.

Impact on the market

The ban on SDP Industries' ghee and the suspension of Marche Retail's licence are expected to have a significant impact on the market. Consumers may face shortages of the ghee, and retailers may struggle to maintain their sales and profits.

What happens next?

SDP Industries and Marche Retail will have to comply with FSSAI's directives and take corrective measures to regain their licences. The authority will continue to monitor the situation and take further action if necessary. Consumers are advised to check the authenticity of food products before purchasing them.
