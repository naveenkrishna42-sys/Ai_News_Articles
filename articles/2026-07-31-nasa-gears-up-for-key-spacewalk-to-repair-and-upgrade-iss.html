NASA gears up for key spacewalk to repair and upgrade ISS

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Science Original source ↗
Key points
  • NASA is preparing for a critical spacewalk to repair and upgrade the ISS.
  • The spacewalk will involve work on the station's solar power arrays.
  • Preparations include bioprinting and exercise research aboard the station.
  • NASA will cover three US spacewalks and host a preview news conference.
  • Indian-American astronaut Anil Menon is set for his first spacewalk.

Spacewalk Preparations Underway

NASA is gearing up for a critical spacewalk to repair and upgrade the International Space Station (ISS). The agency has outlined a busy schedule aboard the orbital laboratory, with spacewalk preps, bioprinting, and exercise research topping the list.

The upcoming extravehicular activity (EVA) will focus on solar power array work, a key component of the station's power systems. Astronauts will venture outside the ISS to conduct repairs and installations that are essential for the continued operation of the station.

Solar Power Array Maintenance

The spacewalkers are set to perform tasks on the station's solar arrays, which have been in operation for years and require periodic maintenance. The work is critical to ensure the ISS maintains its power supply for ongoing scientific experiments and daily operations.

NASA has announced that it will cover three US spacewalks and host a preview news conference ahead of the activities. The agency is expected to provide live coverage of the spacewalks, allowing the public to follow the astronauts' progress as they work in the vacuum of space.

Bioprinting and Exercise Research

Alongside the spacewalk preparations, the station's crew is conducting research in bioprinting and exercise science. Bioprinting experiments aim to understand how tissues can be printed in microgravity, which could have significant implications for medicine on Earth and long-duration space missions.

Exercise research is also a priority, as scientists study how astronauts can maintain muscle and bone mass during extended stays in space. These studies are vital for future missions to the Moon and Mars, where crews will face even longer periods in microgravity.

Anil Menon Set for First Spacewalk

Indian-American astronaut Anil Menon is set for his first spacewalk, marking a significant milestone in his career. Menon, who was selected as part of NASA's astronaut corps, will be among the crew members stepping outside the ISS for the first time.

His participation highlights the growing role of Indian-origin professionals in space exploration. Menon's journey to this point has been marked by rigorous training and preparation, and his first spacewalk will be a closely watched event.

NASA has not yet confirmed the exact date for the spacewalk, but officials have indicated that it will take place soon. The agency is also expected to provide details about the crew assignments and the specific tasks to be performed during the EVA.

What to Watch

As the spacewalk approaches, all eyes will be on the ISS and its crew. The successful completion of the repairs and upgrades will be crucial for the station's future operations. NASA's coverage of the event will offer a rare glimpse into the challenges and triumphs of working in space.

With three spacewalks planned, the coming weeks promise to be busy for the station's crew. The agency's preview news conference is expected to shed more light on the objectives and potential challenges of these missions.
