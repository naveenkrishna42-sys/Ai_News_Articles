NEET Protests Spread: Tear Gas in Patna, Political Clashes in Karnataka

📅 2026-07-23 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Police tear-gassed AISA student protesters in Patna demanding Union Minister Dharmendra Pradhan's resignation.
  • BJP and Congress locked horns in Karnataka over alleged irregularities in the NEET-UG exam.
  • Clashes near Delhi's Tolstoy Marg as crowd swells at Jantar Mantar; Uttarakhand students continue agitation.

Patna: Tear Gas on Marching Students

Police in Patna used tear gas shells on Tuesday to disperse a march by the All India Students' Association (AISA). The students were demanding the resignation of Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan over the NEET-UG paper leak.

Witnesses said the protest turned chaotic when officers attempted to stop the procession near the state secretariat. Video footage showed students coughing and running as tear gas canisters were deployed.

Student leaders alleged that the police action was disproportionate. Officials, however, maintained that the march was unauthorized and that the force used was minimal to control the crowd.

Karnataka: A Political Flashpoint

The NEET controversy has deepened political divisions in Karnataka. The ruling Congress party accused the BJP of shielding the Union minister, while the BJP hit back, calling the Congress campaign a distraction from its own governance failures.

Senior BJP leaders held a counter-protest, claiming the Congress was using students for political mileage. The state has seen multiple student-led marches in Bengaluru and Mysuru over the past week.

The issue is expected to dominate the upcoming assembly session. Both parties have filed complaints with the state police against each other's workers.

Delhi: Jantar Mantar Swells, Clashes on Tolstoy Marg

In the national capital, the protest site at Jantar Mantar saw a surge in numbers. Students from various universities gathered, demanding a Central Bureau of Investigation probe and the minister's resignation.

Fresh clashes erupted near Tolstoy Marg in the evening as protesters tried to march towards the Education Ministry. Police used water cannons to push back the crowd. Several students were detained briefly.

Organizers said the agitation would continue until their demands were met. They have called for a nationwide shutdown later this week.

Uttarakhand: Students Remain Defiant

Students in Uttarakhand continued their protests, condemning the lathi charge on demonstrators in Delhi earlier this week. Sit-ins were held in Dehradun and Haldwani.

Local student unions have threatened to block highways if the government does not address the paper leak issue. The state police have been put on alert.

Authorities have appealed for calm, but student leaders say they will not back down until a high-level inquiry is ordered.

With protests spreading to at least six states, the government faces mounting pressure to act before the next round of medical entrance exams. All eyes are now on the Supreme Court, which is expected to hear a plea on the paper leak next week.
