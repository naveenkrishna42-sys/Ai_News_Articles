Ravindra Jadeja returns, Saransh Jain gets maiden call-up for Sri Lanka Tests

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ravindra Jadeja returns to the Test squad after injury layoff.
  • Madhya Pradesh's Saransh Jain receives first-ever Test call-up.
  • Jasprit Bumrah included in squad subject to fitness clearance.

Jadeja back in the fold

Ravindra Jadeja has been named in India's Test squad for the upcoming tour of Sri Lanka. The left-arm spinner and batting all-rounder returns after missing recent assignments due to injury. His presence adds significant depth to a squad already teeming with spin options.

The selection committee has opted for a blend of experience and fresh talent. Jadeja's return is a major boost for the team management, who have been carefully managing his workload over the past year.

Saransh Jain earns maiden call-up

Madhya Pradesh off-spinner Saransh Jain has received his first-ever call-up to the Indian Test team. The 27-year-old has been a consistent performer in domestic cricket, particularly in the Ranji Trophy.

Jain's selection underscores the selectors' willingness to reward domestic form. He is expected to provide an additional spin option on Sri Lankan pitches that traditionally assist slow bowlers.

Bumrah picked with a rider

Jasprit Bumrah has been included in the squad, but subject to a fitness clearance. The pace spearhead has been rehabilitating a back injury and will need to pass a formal assessment before travelling.

The BCCI medical team is expected to evaluate Bumrah's match readiness in the coming days. The board has not set a specific deadline for the final call, but sources indicate a decision will be made before the team departs.

Other notable selections

The squad sees the return of several first-choice players who were rested for the previous series. The batting order remains largely unchanged, with the top order expected to be led by Rohit Sharma.

The pace attack includes Mohammed Siraj and Prasidh Krishna alongside Bumrah, while the spin department will be headlined by Jadeja, Ravichandran Ashwin, and now Saransh Jain.

What to watch

The focus will now shift to Bumrah's fitness test, which could determine the final composition of the pace attack. The team is scheduled to leave for Sri Lanka next week, with the first Test set to begin shortly after arrival.
