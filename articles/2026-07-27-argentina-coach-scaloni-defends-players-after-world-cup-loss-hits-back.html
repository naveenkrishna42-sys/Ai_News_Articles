Argentina coach Scaloni defends players after World Cup loss, hits back at critics

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Lionel Scaloni posted a message defending his players after Argentina's World Cup loss
  • He called critics 'people who don't know us' and stood by the team's effort
  • Leandro Paredes expects Lionel Messi to retire from international duty after the loss

Scaloni breaks silence after World Cup defeat

Argentina head coach Lionel Scaloni has posted a strongly worded message defending his players following the national team's World Cup loss. The statement, released through his official channels, came amid mounting criticism of the squad's performance in the tournament.

Scaloni did not hold back. He described the detractors as "people who don't know us" and insisted the team had given everything on the pitch. The coach's tone was firm, almost defiant, as he sought to shield his players from the backlash.

Defending the squad against critics

"We fell short, but that does not diminish the effort or the character of this group," Scaloni wrote. He pointed to the team's journey over the past few years, which included a Copa America title and a World Cup triumph in 2022, as evidence of their quality.

The Argentine boss specifically targeted those who questioned the players' commitment. "These are the same people who doubted us when we won. Now they doubt us in defeat. That is their problem, not ours," he added.

Paredes hints at Messi retirement

Meanwhile, midfielder Leandro Paredes has suggested that Lionel Messi may step away from international football after the World Cup heartbreak. Speaking to reporters, Paredes said the loss could mark the end of an era for the 38-year-old captain.

"Leo has given everything for this shirt. It is painful to see him like this," Paredes said. "He has carried us so many times. Maybe it is time for him to rest." Messi himself has not made any official announcement about his future with the national team.

Mixed reactions from fans and media

The coach's message has divided opinion in Argentina. Some fans have rallied behind Scaloni, praising his loyalty to the squad. Others, however, feel the team underperformed and that criticism is warranted after a disappointing campaign.

Argentine media outlets have also weighed in. While some praised Scaloni's defence of his players, others questioned whether the team's tactics were to blame for the early exit. The debate continues to rage across sports talk shows and social media platforms.

Scaloni's tenure as coach has been marked by highs and lows. He led Argentina to World Cup glory in Qatar in 2022, cementing his place in the nation's football history. But the recent loss has reignited questions about the team's future direction.

What comes next for Argentina

Scaloni is expected to stay on as coach, at least for now. The Argentine Football Association has not indicated any immediate plans to make a change. However, the pressure will only grow if results do not improve in upcoming friendlies and qualifiers.

The team's next competitive matches are likely to be World Cup qualifiers, where they will need to bounce back quickly. For now, Scaloni's message serves as both a defence and a rallying cry. Whether it will be enough to silence the critics remains to be seen.
