Editorial Policy

Sourcing & Verification

Stories on TIVRA News are grounded in publicly available reporting from recognized domestic and international news agencies, official government gazettes, and verified corporate press releases. Every single article clearly names and links directly to the original source in the verification section.

Editorial Workflow & AI Assistance

TIVRA News employs automated curation tools and generative AI models as editorial assistants to synthesize complex developments into concise, structured briefings. While AI assists in drafting and formatting, all content is guided by clear editorial rules:

  • Factual Grounding: Specifications, dates, figures, and direct quotations must originate from the cited source material.
  • No Speculation: The generation pipeline is prohibited from fabricating non-existent products, artificial discounts, or unverified claims.
  • Original Context: Where possible, explainers and buyer guides include added context, comparison points, and historical background.

Independence & Commercial Disclosures

Editorial coverage is strictly independent from commercial and affiliate partnerships. Commercial affiliate links (such as retail product or banking partner links) are clearly marked and only placed where contextually relevant. Our partners have zero influence over news editorial decisions.

Corrections Policy

Accuracy is paramount. When any factual inaccuracy or misleading detail is reported to us, we update the story promptly. For material corrections, a clarification notice is appended directly to the article. To report an issue, please contact our team via the Contact page.
