iPhone 18 release date, price leaks, and color rumors: What to expect

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max could cost up to Rs 26,000 more in India.
  • Spring delay may boost autumn sales of iPhone 18 Pro models.
  • Five rumored Pro features, including camera and design changes, look credible.

Release date: A spring shift?

Apple's iPhone 18 launch timeline is generating buzz, with reports suggesting a possible spring delay. According to Forbes, this could actually boost autumn sales of the iPhone 18 Pro models, as consumers wait for the premium variants.

The exact date remains unconfirmed, but industry watchers expect Apple to stick to its usual September event for the base models, with Pro versions possibly arriving later. Historically, Apple has staggered releases, but a spring push for the Pro lineup would be a notable departure.

Price hike: Pro models to get costlier

Indian buyers may need to brace for a significant price jump. The Economic Times reports that the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max could cost up to Rs 26,000 more than their predecessors. This would mark one of the steepest increases in recent years, driven by rising component costs and advanced manufacturing processes.

For context, the iPhone 17 Pro currently starts at around Rs 1,34,900, so a hike of this scale could push the starting price past Rs 1.6 lakh. Analysts suggest the increase is linked to upgraded camera hardware and a new chipset, though Apple has not officially commented on pricing.

Design and display: What's leaked so far

Moneycontrol has compiled leaks pointing to a redesigned chassis and a brighter, more power-efficient display on the Pro Max. Rumors hint at a titanium frame, similar to the iPhone 17 Pro, but with a matte finish to reduce fingerprints. The display could also feature under-display Face ID, a first for Apple.

Color options are another area of speculation, with reports suggesting new shades like deep blue and sage green for the Pro models, alongside the classic black, white, and gold. The standard iPhone 18 might get pastel tones, though these details are unverified.

Camera and performance: Five credible rumors

NDTV highlights five rumoured features for the iPhone 18 Pro that appear legitimate. These include a periscope lens with 10x optical zoom, an upgraded 48MP ultra-wide sensor, and a new image signal processor for better low-light shots. The A19 Pro chip, built on a 2nm process, is expected to deliver a significant performance leap.

Another credible rumor is a larger battery with faster charging, possibly 35W wired and 25W MagSafe. Apple may also introduce a new 'Capture Button' that acts like a DSLR shutter, giving photographers a tactile way to focus and shoot. These features, if true, would solidify the Pro's position as a creator-focused device.

What to watch next

With the event likely months away, more leaks will surface. Keep an eye on supply chain reports from Asia, which often reveal production timelines and final specs. The price hike, if confirmed, could reshape consumer demand in India, where Apple is aggressively expanding local manufacturing.

For now, treat all rumors with caution. Apple's official announcement, expected in September 2026, will settle the speculation. Until then, the buzz around the iPhone 18 is only set to grow.
