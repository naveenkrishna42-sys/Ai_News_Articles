Bescom skips July meter readings, to issue average bills

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bescom will not take physical meter readings in July 2026, citing staff constraints.
  • Bills for July will be calculated using the average of the previous three months' consumption.
  • Consumers can still submit self-meter readings through the Bescom mobile app to get accurate bills.

Bangalore Electricity Supply Company (Bescom) has announced it will not take physical meter readings for the month of July. Instead, consumers will receive bills calculated on the basis of average consumption from the past three months.

The decision, confirmed by Bescom officials on Monday, stems from a shortage of meter-reading staff. The utility has not specified how many personnel are unavailable or when normal operations will resume.

How the average billing will work

For July, Bescom will compute the average of a consumer's electricity usage from April, May, and June. This average figure will be used to generate the monthly bill.

Officials clarified that the adjustment will be made in the next billing cycle. Any difference between the average bill and actual consumption will be reconciled then.

Option for self-meter reading

Consumers who want an accurate bill based on actual usage can submit their own meter readings. The utility's mobile app allows users to enter the reading and photograph the meter for verification.

Bescom has encouraged all consumers to use this facility, noting that it helps avoid over- or under-charging. The app is available for both Android and iOS platforms.

Last year, the utility faced similar criticism when it resorted to average billing during a staff shortage. Consumer groups had then flagged concerns about inflated bills.

What consumers should do

Residents are advised to check their previous bills for the April–June period. If the average appears unusually high due to seasonal spikes, submitting a self-reading is recommended.

Bescom has not announced any penalty for late payments related to this cycle. However, standard due dates and disconnection policies remain in force.

The utility handles power distribution across most of Bengaluru and parts of rural Karnataka. It serves over 1.2 crore consumers.

Officials said the staff shortage is temporary and meter readings are expected to resume in August. No further details were provided.

Consumers can reach Bescom's helpline at 1912 for queries or to report issues with the average billing calculation.
