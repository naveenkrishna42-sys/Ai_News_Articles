GBA chief commissioner urges expedited Ejipura buffer road works

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • GBA chief commissioner has urged authorities to speed up the Ejipura buffer road works.
  • The project has faced delays, causing inconvenience to local residents and commuters.
  • Officials have not yet confirmed a revised completion timeline for the road.

Delay draws concern

The chief commissioner of the Bengaluru Development Authority (BDA) has asked officials to expedite the long-pending buffer road works along Ejipura. The instruction came during a review meeting held on Wednesday, where the official stressed the need to clear bottlenecks that have stalled progress on the stretch.

The buffer road, part of a broader infrastructure plan for the area, is meant to ease congestion on the busy Ejipura junction and improve connectivity to surrounding neighbourhoods. However, work has crawled for months, drawing complaints from residents and motorists who navigate the chaos daily.

What's holding up the project

Sources familiar with the matter said the delays stem from a mix of utility shifting, land acquisition hurdles, and coordination gaps between multiple agencies. The BDA has been tasked with overseeing the project, but the involvement of the Bescom, BWSSB, and the city's traffic police has complicated execution.

The chief commissioner's directive is expected to push officials to resolve these inter-departmental issues faster. Specific reasons for the current halt were not disclosed, and officials have not yet confirmed a revised timeline for completion.

Impact on daily commuters

Ejipura is a key transit point for those travelling between Koramangala, Domlur, and the old airport road. The incomplete road works have reduced carriageway width, leading to frequent snarls, especially during peak hours. Pedestrians, too, face unsafe conditions with unfinished footpaths and open trenches.

Local shopkeepers and residents have repeatedly flagged the issue, saying the prolonged construction hurts business and adds to daily stress. "Every day is a struggle," said a resident who did not wish to be named. "We just want the road finished."

Next steps

The BDA is expected to hold a follow-up meeting within a fortnight to assess progress on the ground. The chief commissioner has reportedly asked for a detailed status report and a clear action plan from the executing agencies.

Whether this translates into visible acceleration remains to be seen. For now, all eyes are on the authorities to turn the directive into tangible results on the Ejipura stretch.
