Bengaluru ragpicker, 63, held for wife's murder

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 63-year-old ragpicker was arrested in Bengaluru for allegedly killing his wife.
  • The arrest was made following a police investigation into the incident.
  • Authorities have not yet disclosed the motive or details of the alleged crime.

Arrest in Bengaluru

Bengaluru police have arrested a 63-year-old ragpicker on charges of murdering his wife. The incident came to light after authorities received information, leading to a swift investigation and the subsequent arrest.

The accused, whose identity has not been officially released, was taken into custody. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact circumstances surrounding the death or the sequence of events that led to the alleged crime.

Investigation underway

Police have registered a case and are conducting a detailed probe. Preliminary inquiries are focused on establishing the motive, with officers questioning neighbours and associates of the couple.

Forensic teams have been deployed to gather evidence from the scene. The body has been sent for post-mortem, and the report is awaited to ascertain the precise cause of death.

Accused in custody

The 63-year-old man is currently in police custody and will be produced before a local court. Sources indicate that the couple had been living in a settlement in the city, where the accused worked as a ragpicker.

Residents in the area expressed shock at the development, though many declined to comment on the record. Police have appealed for public cooperation as the investigation progresses.

Legal proceedings ahead

Authorities are preparing a chargesheet, and further legal procedures will follow. The accused faces charges under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, including those pertaining to murder.

It remains unclear whether the accused has legal representation or has made any statement to investigators. Police have not ruled out the possibility of a prior altercation, but no details have been confirmed.

What to watch

The case will move to the court for judicial scrutiny, where the accused's plea will be recorded. The post-mortem report and forensic analysis are expected to play a critical role in determining the course of the trial.
