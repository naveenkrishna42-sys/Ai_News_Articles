Eight Injured in Firing Along Assam-Arunachal Border in Dhemaji

📅 2026-08-10 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • At least eight people were injured in firing along the Assam-Arunachal Pradesh border in Dhemaji.
  • The incident follows a clash over an encroachment dispute in the area.
  • Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma termed it a 'localised conflict' and said it is nearly resolved.

At least eight people were injured in a firing incident along the Assam-Arunachal Pradesh border in Dhemaji district on Monday, officials said. The gunfire erupted amid an ongoing encroachment dispute between residents of the two states, triggering panic in the area.

Police have not yet confirmed the sequence of events or the identity of the shooters. Initial reports suggest that unidentified men opened fire, leaving several people wounded. Some of the injured are said to be in critical condition, though officials have not released an official update on their health status.

Encroachment Dispute Turns Violent

The firing follows a clash that broke out over land ownership along the disputed boundary. Local residents said tensions had been simmering for weeks over encroachment claims, with both sides staking claim to the same patch of land.

Such disputes are not new to this region. The Assam-Arunachal Pradesh border has seen sporadic flare-ups over the years, often centring on unclear demarcation lines and competing claims by villagers. Monday's incident, however, marks a serious escalation with the use of firearms.

Police and Administration Respond

Security forces have been deployed to the area to prevent further violence. Senior district officials from both states are in touch, though a coordinated response is yet to be announced. The injured have been moved to a nearby hospital, with some reportedly referred to a larger medical facility for advanced care.

Local MLA and community leaders have appealed for calm, urging both sides to allow authorities to handle the matter. Police have registered a case, and a search is underway to trace those responsible for the firing.

CM Sarma Calls It 'Localised Conflict'

Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma described the incident as a 'localised conflict' and said the situation is nearly resolved. His remarks came amid concerns that the border violence could spiral into a larger confrontation between the two neighbouring states.

Sarma's statement suggests that administrative intervention may have already de-escalated tensions on the ground. However, with at least eight people injured, questions remain about how such a dispute turned violent and why firearms were used.

The Arunachal Pradesh government has not issued an official statement so far. Officials on both sides are expected to hold talks in the coming days to address the underlying land dispute.

What to Watch

As police continue their investigation, the focus will be on identifying the perpetrators and ensuring that the injured receive proper medical care. The larger issue of border demarcation between Assam and Arunachal Pradesh remains unresolved, and Monday's violence underscores the urgent need for a permanent solution.

Authorities are likely to step up patrolling along the disputed stretch to prevent any repeat of such incidents. For now, the area remains tense but under control, with residents hoping that dialogue, not bullets, will decide the future of this border dispute.
