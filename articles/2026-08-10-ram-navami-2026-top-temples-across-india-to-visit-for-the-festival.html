Ram Navami 2026: Top Temples Across India to Visit for the Festival

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Religion Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ram Navami 2026 falls in April; devotees across India plan special temple visits.
  • Key temples include Ram Janmabhoomi in Ayodhya, Rameswaram, and Bhadrachalam.
  • Temples expect large crowds; devotees advised to plan travel and darshan in advance.

As Ram Navami 2026 approaches, devotees across India are making plans to visit some of the country's most revered temples. The festival, which marks the birth of Lord Ram, is observed with particular fervour in temples dedicated to him, drawing thousands of worshippers for special prayers, bhajans, and processions.

While the exact date of Ram Navami 2026 falls in the Hindu month of Chaitra, typically in March or April, preparations are already underway at major shrines. For those looking to combine devotion with travel, here are some of the most significant temples to consider.

Ayodhya: The Birthplace of Lord Ram

No list of Ram Navami destinations is complete without Ayodhya in Uttar Pradesh. The city, believed to be the birthplace of Lord Ram, is home to the grand Ram Janmabhoomi temple, which has become a major pilgrimage site since its consecration. On Ram Navami, the temple sees an overwhelming influx of devotees who come to offer prayers and witness the special aartis.

Local authorities typically make elaborate arrangements for the festival, including temporary shelters, free food counters, and increased security. However, given the massive crowds expected, it is advisable to book accommodation well in advance.

Rameswaram: The Southern Shrine

At the southern tip of India, the Ramanathaswamy Temple in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, holds special significance during Ram Navami. According to legend, Lord Ram worshipped Lord Shiva here to absolve himself of the sin of killing Ravana. The temple's long corridors and sacred water tanks attract devotees from across the country.

On Ram Navami, the temple conducts special abhishekams and processions, drawing large gatherings. The island town also offers a peaceful retreat for those looking to combine spirituality with a coastal visit.

Bhadrachalam: The Temple of Sita Ramachandra

In Telangana, the Bhadrachalam Temple dedicated to Lord Sita Ramachandra is another major centre for Ram Navami celebrations. The temple is unique in that it houses idols of Sita, Ram, and Lakshman together, and the festival is celebrated with great pomp, including a grand kalyanotsavam (celestial wedding ceremony) of Ram and Sita.

Devotees from Andhra Pradesh and Telangana throng the temple during this period, and the town takes on a festive atmosphere with decorated streets and cultural programmes. The temple authorities often arrange special darshan slots for pilgrims.

Other Notable Temples to Consider

Apart from these, several other temples across India observe Ram Navami with unique rituals. The Ram Raja Temple in Orchha, Madhya Pradesh, is the only place where Lord Ram is worshipped as a king, and the festival is marked by a royal procession. In Varanasi, the ancient Kashi Vishwanath Temple and nearby Ram temples see special celebrations.

In the north, the Sidh Peeth in Jammu's Ragunath Temple and the Ram Mandir in Jagdalpur, Chhattisgarh, also attract devotees. For those in the west, the Shree Ram Temple in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, and the Kalaram Temple in Nashik, Maharashtra, offer equally immersive experiences.

Planning Your Visit

Given the festive rush, pilgrims are advised to plan their itineraries carefully. Checking temple websites for darshan timings and any special entry passes is recommended. Many temples now offer online booking for special pujas, which can save time on the day.

Travel experts also suggest reaching the temple towns a day early to avoid last-minute crowds and to participate in the pre-festival rituals. For those travelling by road, it is wise to expect heavy traffic and potential diversions near major shrines.

As Ram Navami 2026 draws closer, more details about specific events and arrangements are expected to emerge. Devotees are encouraged to stay updated through official temple channels and local news for the latest information.
