Naveen Patnaik slams BJP over Indian hockey jersey colour change

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Naveen Patnaik condemns jersey colour change from blue to saffron, calling it an attack on hockey's legacy.
  • Dilip Tirkey says decision was taken without his knowledge; women's team had no training in new jerseys.
  • Debate intensifies ahead of FIH World Cup, with critics questioning the 'turf' justification.

Political fire over a jersey

Odisha Chief Minister Naveen Patnaik has launched a sharp attack on the BJP-led central government over the decision to change the Indian hockey team's jersey colour from the iconic blue to saffron. The change, announced ahead of the FIH World Cup, has triggered a political and sporting controversy.

Patnaik, whose state has been a major sponsor of Indian hockey, called the move an affront to the sport's heritage. He argued that the blue jersey is not just a piece of clothing but a symbol of India's hockey triumphs over decades.

Tirkey caught off guard

Adding to the turmoil, Hockey India president Dilip Tirkey has reportedly told the executive board that the decision was taken without his prior knowledge. This revelation has raised questions about internal transparency and the process behind the change.

Tirkey, a former captain and one of India's most decorated hockey players, is now at the centre of a storm. His statement suggests a disconnect between the sports ministry, Hockey India, and the players ahead of a major international tournament.

Preparation concerns mount

Critics have pointed out that the women's team had zero training sessions in the new jerseys before the World Cup, while the men's team managed just one. This lack of adaptation time could affect player comfort and performance on the field, they argue.

The official explanation for the change—that blue does not stand out against the turf—has been dismissed by many as a flimsy pretext. The Print, in an editorial, noted that India's blue jersey is a 'legacy of triumph' and called the turf rationale a 'sham explanation'.

A broader political symbol

The colour shift has inevitably been read as a political move, especially with the BJP at the centre. Saffron holds deep religious and political significance for the party, and its introduction into the national team's kit has been seen by opponents as an attempt to saffronise sports.

Patnaik's criticism fits a larger narrative of regional leaders challenging the central government's cultural policies. The BJD chief has positioned Odisha as a hockey hub, and his government's investment in the sport gives his words additional weight.

What lies ahead

As the FIH World Cup approaches, the controversy shows no signs of cooling. Hockey India and the sports ministry will need to address the concerns raised by players, administrators, and political leaders. Whether the jersey remains saffron or reverts to blue could become a test of how the government handles sporting sentiment versus political symbolism.

All eyes are now on the upcoming matches and any official clarification from the ministry. The debate has ensured that the team's appearances will be watched as much for their attire as for their performance.
