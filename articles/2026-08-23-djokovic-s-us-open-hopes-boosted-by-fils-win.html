Djokovic's US Open hopes boosted by Fils' win

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Novak Djokovic's path to a 25th major title cleared after Arthur Fils' win
  • Jannik Sinner's withdrawal opens a spot in the US Open men's singles draw
  • Mats Wilander and Rick Macci back Djokovic as the favorite for the title

Fils' win clears Djokovic's path to a 25th major

Arthur Fils' victory over Flavio Cobolli in the US Open men's singles first round has cleared Novak Djokovic's path to a historic 25th major title. Fils, who reached the fourth round in New York last year, will next face either Jannik Sinner or Alexander Zverev in the second round.

Sinner's withdrawal opens a spot

Jannik Sinner's withdrawal from the US Open men's singles draw has opened a spot in the main draw. Sinner, who was the runner-up in New York last year, cited a back injury as the reason for his withdrawal. His absence means that Fils will now face either Sinner or Zverev in the second round.

Djokovic backed as favorite

Former world number one Mats Wilander and tennis commentator Rick Macci have both backed Djokovic as the favorite for the US Open title. Wilander said, 'When this man wants something, he usually ends up getting it,' while Macci said, 'He has a real chance.' Djokovic, who has won the US Open a record six times, will be looking to add to his tally and complete the career Grand Slam.

What happens next

With Fils' win and Sinner's withdrawal, Djokovic's path to a 25th major title looks clearer than ever. The next challenge for Djokovic will be to navigate the second round and beyond, where he will face a tough opponent in either Sinner or Zverev. If Djokovic can overcome this hurdle, he will be well on his way to a historic achievement.
