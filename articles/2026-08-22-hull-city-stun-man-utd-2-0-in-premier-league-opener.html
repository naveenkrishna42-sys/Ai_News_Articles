Hull City stun Man Utd 2-0 in Premier League opener

  • Michael Carrick vows to stick to his philosophy despite setback
  • Carlos Baleba's pending arrival highlights summer transfer activity
  • Hull City, newly promoted, claim famous away victory

Shocking upset at Old Trafford

Manchester United's Premier League campaign began with a humiliating 2-0 defeat to newly promoted Hull City on Saturday. The visitors silenced the Theatre of Dreams with a composed display that exposed defensive vulnerabilities and a lack of creative spark in United's rebuilt midfield.

The result sent shockwaves through the club's hierarchy, who had sanctioned significant investment in the squad during the close season. Hull's disciplined performance suggested they have adapted quickly to top-flight demands, while United looked disjointed and second-best for large spells.

Carrick stands firm despite criticism

Manager Michael Carrick defended his side's approach despite the damaging result. Speaking to assembled media, he insisted the club would not deviate from its long-term strategy.

United's hierarchy had backed Carrick heavily in the transfer market, but those investments failed to deliver on the opening weekend. The former midfielder's faith in his methods remains unshaken, though pressure will mount if results do not improve quickly.

Baleba arrival offers hope

The club's pursuit of Carlos Baleba has intensified following the midfield's anonymous display. Sources close to the negotiations suggest the signing could be finalised within days, bringing much-needed physicality and energy to United's engine room.

Supporters have grown increasingly vocal on social media, calling for the Belgian midfielder to be fast-tracked into the starting eleven. His potential debut next weekend against a resurgent west London side will attract considerable attention.

Hull celebrate memorable victory

Hull's players embraced the moment in front of a jubilant away end as the final whistle confirmed a famous result. Their tactical organisation and willingness to press high disrupted United's build-up play throughout.

The club's return to the Premier League after a single-season absence has begun in dream fashion. Manager Tim Love will know, however, that sustaining this level against established top-flight sides remains the true test.

What comes next

United must regroup quickly ahead of a challenging run of fixtures. The transfer window's final days will determine whether further signings arrive to address obvious gaps. Carrick's next selections and tactical adjustments will face intense scrutiny from fans and pundits alike.
