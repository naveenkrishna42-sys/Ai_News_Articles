Zinedine Zidane named France head coach after five-year break

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Zidane appointed France head coach after five years out of management.
  • He replaces Didier Deschamps, who stepped down after World Cup 2026.
  • Three key challenges await: squad transition, tactics, and pressure to win.

The Return of a Legend

Zinedine Zidane is back in the dugout. The French football icon has been appointed as the head coach of the France men's national team, ending a five-year hiatus from management. The announcement comes shortly after Didier Deschamps stepped down following the 2026 World Cup.

Zidane, 54, last coached Real Madrid, where he won three consecutive Champions League titles between 2016 and 2018. He left the club in 2021 and has since turned down multiple offers, waiting for the right opportunity. That opportunity has now arrived with Les Bleus.

Why Now?

The timing is significant. Deschamps led France for over a decade, winning the 2018 World Cup and reaching the 2022 final. But after a disappointing World Cup 2026 campaign, the federation decided it was time for a change. Zidane, long seen as the natural successor, finally accepted the role.

French Football Federation president Philippe Diallo confirmed the appointment in a brief statement, praising Zidane's "unparalleled understanding of the game." No further details on the length of the contract were provided.

Three Questions Awaiting an Answer

Zidane's return is not without challenges. Analysts have pointed to three immediate questions he must address.

Squad transition. France's golden generation, led by Kylian Mbappe and Antoine Griezmann, is aging. Younger talents like Eduardo Camavinga and Aurelien Tchouameni need to step up. Zidane must balance experience with renewal.

Tactical identity. Deschamps favoured a pragmatic, counter-attacking style. Zidane, by contrast, prefers possession-based football with creative freedom. How he adapts his philosophy to the national team setup will be closely watched.

Pressure to win. France expects trophies. With Euro 2028 on the horizon, Zidane will be under immediate scrutiny. His reputation as a serial winner at club level will be tested on the international stage.

The Road Ahead

Zidane's first matches in charge are likely to be friendlies later this year. The French public and media will be watching every move. His relationship with star players, particularly Mbappe, will be crucial.

For a man who has won everything as a player and a club coach, this is perhaps his biggest challenge yet. The minefield is real, but so is the opportunity to cement his legacy.

France's next competitive matches are in the Euro 2028 qualifiers. Zidane's reign begins now, and the world will be watching.
