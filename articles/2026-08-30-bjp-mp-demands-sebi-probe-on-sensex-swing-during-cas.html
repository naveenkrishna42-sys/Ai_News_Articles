BJP MP alleges irregularities in Sensex swing during CAS

Former BJP MP Kirit Somaiya has sought a probe into the sharp swing of the Sensex during the Corporate Action Season (CAS). Somaiya, a seasoned market analyst, has alleged that the market swing was not justified by corporate actions and could be indicative of market manipulation.

SEBI to investigate claims of market manipulation

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has announced that it will investigate the allegations made by Somaiya. SEBI has stated that it will look into the claims of market manipulation and ensure that the market operates in a fair and transparent manner.

Broader implications for investor confidence and market stability

The allegations made by Somaiya have raised concerns about the stability of the Indian stock market. The sharp swing of the Sensex during CAS has led to a loss of investor confidence, with many investors pulling out of the market. This could have a significant impact on the overall economic growth of the country.

  • SEBI will investigate the claims of market manipulation made by Kirit Somaiya
