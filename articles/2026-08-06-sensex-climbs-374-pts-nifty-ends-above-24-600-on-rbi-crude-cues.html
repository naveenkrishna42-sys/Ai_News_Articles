Sensex climbs 374 pts, Nifty ends above 24,600 on RBI, crude cues

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex settled 374 points higher; Nifty ended above 24,600.
  • Crude oil prices slipped below $80 a barrel, boosting market sentiment.
  • RBI-related cues and global trends supported the domestic equity market.
  • Broader market showed mixed but positive bias; sectoral indices mostly in green.
  • Analysts see near-term support from oil prices and central bank stance.
  • Investors now await global cues and upcoming macroeconomic data.
  • Market breadth remained positive, with advances outpacing declines.
  • FII/DII activity and rupee movement will be watched in coming sessions.

Markets close in green

Indian equity benchmarks ended Wednesday's session on a firm footing, with the Sensex climbing 374 points and the Nifty settling above the 24,600 mark. The gains came on the back of easing crude oil prices and a supportive policy backdrop from the Reserve Bank of India, which kept investors in good cheer through the day.

The 30-share Sensex closed at 24,600 levels, while the broader Nifty held its ground, though some index heavyweights saw muted moves. Market participants pointed to falling global oil prices — Brent slipping below $80 a barrel — as a key trigger that improved risk appetite across Dalal Street.

RBI cues and crude oil give tailwinds

The RBI's recent commentary and policy stance provided a steady undercurrent to the market, even as traders tracked global cues. A decline in crude prices is seen as a positive for India's import bill, inflation outlook, and corporate margins, which in turn supports equity valuations.

Oil marketing companies and auto stocks gained traction, while rate-sensitive sectors like banking and realty also saw buying interest. The market's resilience came despite mixed Asian cues and lingering concerns over global growth.

Sectoral and stock movement

Most sectoral indices ended in positive territory, with IT, FMCG, and pharma contributing to the rally. Meanwhile, some midcap and smallcap stocks outperformed, indicating broad-based participation. However, a few pockets such as metal and energy showed profit-taking.

Among individual stocks, index heavyweight Reliance Industries and HDFC Bank were among the top contributors to the Sensex's gains. Analysts noted that the market's ability to hold above key support levels could invite further buying in the short term.

What's driving sentiment

Easing crude oil prices, a stable rupee, and expectations of continued foreign inflows have lifted sentiment. Traders also drew comfort from the RBI's focus on growth support, even as it remains vigilant on inflation.

However, volatility is not off the table. Global factors such as US Federal Reserve policy moves and geopolitical developments could influence direction in the coming sessions.

Outlook for investors

Market experts suggest that the Nifty's ability to sustain above 24,600 will be crucial for further upside. A break below could trigger profit-booking, but the broader trend remains constructive as long as crude stays soft and domestic macros hold.

Investors are now eyeing upcoming economic data, including inflation prints and industrial production numbers, for fresh cues. Also on the radar are quarterly earnings from key companies and any further commentary from the RBI.

With oil prices under control and policy support intact, the market may continue to see selective buying. But traders should stay nimble, as global headwinds could quickly alter the narrative.
