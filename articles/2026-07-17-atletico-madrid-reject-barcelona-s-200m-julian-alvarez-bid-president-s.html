Atletico Madrid reject Barcelona's €200m Julian Alvarez bid, president sends warning

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Atletico Madrid president Enrique Cerezo rejected a €200 million bid from Barcelona for Julian Alvarez.
  • Cerezo stated Alvarez will play for Atletico next season, sending a defiant message to Barcelona president Joan Laporta.
  • Barcelona had reportedly set a transfer deadline for the deal, which has now passed without an agreement.

Atletico Madrid have firmly shut the door on Barcelona's pursuit of Julian Alvarez. Club president Enrique Cerezo has confirmed the rejection of a massive €200 million offer from the Catalan giants.

Cerezo did not mince words. Speaking to reporters, he made it clear that the Argentine World Cup winner is not for sale, no matter the price.

Atletico's defiant stance

"We will not accept €200 million. Julián Álvarez will play at Atlético Madrid next season," Cerezo said. The statement was a direct response to Barcelona's approach, which had reportedly included a bid of that size.

This is not the first time Cerezo has been firm on the matter. Earlier this week, he claimed that Barcelona president Joan Laporta already knows where Alvarez will be playing next season. The message is unambiguous: the striker stays in Madrid.

Barcelona's transfer deadline ignored

Barcelona had reportedly set a deadline for Atletico to respond to their offer. That deadline has now passed. Instead of a negotiation, they got a public rejection.

The €200 million figure would have been a world-record transfer fee. But Atletico, known for their stubbornness in the transfer market, were not tempted. They see Alvarez as a cornerstone of their project for the coming season.

Interest from Arsenal and others

Barcelona are not the only club interested. Arsenal have also received a significant update on Alvarez's availability after their own offer was confirmed. However, the message from the Spanish capital remains the same across the board.

The London Evening Standard reported that the Gunners have been given a clear indication of the situation. Atletico's position has not changed for any suitor.

What this means for the transfer window

Barcelona's pursuit of Alvarez is now effectively dead. The club will have to turn their attention to other attacking targets. For Atletico, the focus is on building the team around their star forward.

Cerezo's defiance is a calculated move. It sends a signal to the rest of Europe that Atletico are not a selling club when it comes to their most prized assets.

All eyes will now be on Laporta and Barcelona's next move. They have been left with little room to manoeuvre in a market that is only getting more expensive.
