Samsung's budget Galaxy Book 6 enters the fray

Samsung has launched the Galaxy Book 6, a budget-friendly laptop aimed at competing with Apple's MacBook Neo. Priced at 1.19 million won, the new device is set to make a significant impact in the Indian laptop market, especially with the recent devaluation of the Korean won.

Key features and specifications

The Galaxy Book 6 comes with a range of features that make it a compelling option for consumers. It boasts a sleek design, a 15.6-inch display with a resolution of 2880 x 1800 pixels, and a refresh rate of 120Hz. The laptop is powered by an Intel Core Ultra 7 processor, ensuring smooth performance for everyday tasks and light multitasking.

The device also features a 120Hz refresh rate display, which is a significant upgrade from the previous models. This makes the Galaxy Book 6 a great option for those who enjoy gaming or watching high-definition videos.

One of the standout features of the Galaxy Book 6 is its AI capabilities. The laptop comes with a dedicated AI chip that enhances the performance of the device. This AI chip is designed to optimize the laptop's performance, making it more efficient and responsive.

The Galaxy Book 6 also features a range of other enhancements, including a 1080p webcam, a backlit keyboard, and a fingerprint scanner. These features make the device more secure and user-friendly.
