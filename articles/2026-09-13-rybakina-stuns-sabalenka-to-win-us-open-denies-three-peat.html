Rybakina Stuns Sabalenka to Win US Open, Denies Three-Peat

Key points
  • Elena Rybakina won the 2026 US Open women's singles title, defeating Aryna Sabalenka and denying her a third consecutive championship.
  • The final drew star power, with Brad Pitt and Ronaldo among those watching Rybakina dethrone Sabalenka.
  • Sabalenka's post-match reaction included a smashed racquet and visible frustration, underscoring the emotional stakes.
  • Rybakina's victory marks a significant career milestone and shifts the balance of power in women's tennis.

A New Queen in New York

Elena Rybakina captured the 2026 US Open women's singles title on Saturday, defeating Aryna Sabalenka in a dramatic final at Arthur Ashe Stadium. The win denies Sabalenka a third consecutive US Open crown, a feat last achieved by Serena Williams in 2014. Rybakina's triumph, reported by Al Jazeera and The Guardian, cements her status as a Grand Slam champion and disrupts Sabalenka's recent dominance in New York.

Match Analysis: Power vs. Precision

Rybakina's serve, a weapon that has troubled the tour all season, proved decisive. She consistently hit speeds above 120 mph and won a high percentage of first-serve points, keeping Sabalenka off balance. Sabalenka, known for her aggressive baseline game, struggled with unforced errors, particularly on the forehand side. The match hinged on key break points, where Rybakina's composure contrasted with Sabalenka's frustration.

Sabalenka's Meltdown and Emotional Aftermath

Sabalenka's frustration boiled over after the final point. She smashed her racquet and directed anger toward a cameraman, later choking back tears during the trophy ceremony. This emotional display highlights the immense pressure of defending a title and the fine margins at the elite level. While Sabalenka remains a formidable force, the loss raises questions about her mental resilience in crucial moments.

Star-Studded Audience and Global Reaction

The final attracted celebrities including Brad Pitt and Brazilian footballer Ronaldo, underscoring the US Open's status as a premier sporting and cultural event. Their presence, noted by Rediff, added glamour to Rybakina's breakthrough. Global media, from CGTN to Hindustan Times, highlighted the significance of Rybakina's win in shifting the women's tennis narrative.

What This Means for Women's Tennis

Rybakina's victory injects fresh competition into the WTA Tour. With this win, she joins the ranks of multiple Grand Slam champions and signals a potential shift in the rivalry with Sabalenka and Iga Swiatek. For Sabalenka, the challenge will be to regroup and respond, as the tour heads into the Asian swing and the WTA Finals.

Pros and Cons of Rybakina's Win

  • Pros: Validates Rybakina's hard-court prowess; increases unpredictability in women's tennis; boosts her endorsement potential.
  • Cons: Sabalenka's emotional outburst may overshadow the match; questions linger about consistency at the top.

Prize Money and Ranking Implications

Rybakina takes home a record $3.5 million (approx. ₹29 crore) prize purse, while Sabalenka earns $1.8 million (approx. ₹15 crore). The win propels Rybakina to a career-high ranking, likely No. 2, and narrows the gap to world No. 1. Sabalenka retains the top spot but faces increased pressure.

The Road Ahead

Both players will now prepare for the remaining hard-court season and the WTA Finals. Rybakina's confidence will be sky-high, while Sabalenka must address her on-court emotions to maintain her dominance. Their rivalry, now with a Grand Slam final twist, promises to define the next chapter of women's tennis.
