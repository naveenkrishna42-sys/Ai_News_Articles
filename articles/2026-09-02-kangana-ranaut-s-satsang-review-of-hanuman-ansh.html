Kangana Ranaut's Unique Review

Actress Kangana Ranaut has called the biopic 'Hanuman Ansh' a 'satsang', describing it as a spiritual 'therapy' that resonates deeply with audiences. Her review, published in The Times of India, highlights the film's portrayal of Neem Karoli Baba, a spiritual guru whose teachings have influenced global figures like Steve Jobs and Mark Zuckerberg.

Spiritual Themes and Cultural Impact

The film, based on the life of Neem Karoli Baba, explores themes of spirituality, self-discovery, and the power of inner strength. Kangana Ranaut's review suggests that 'Hanuman Ansh' serves as a form of spiritual 'therapy' for many viewers, offering a much-needed escape from the stresses of modern life.

Box Office Performance

Despite a budget of ₹2 crore and screening on fewer screens, 'Hanuman Ansh' has managed to beat 'Toxic' at the box office. This success indicates that there is a strong demand for spiritual and biographical films in the Indian market.

Sequel Confirmed

A sequel to 'Hanuman Ansh' has been confirmed, which will further cement the film's place in Indian cinema. This sequel will likely build on the spiritual themes and the influence of Neem Karoli Baba, providing a deeper exploration of his teachings and legacy.
