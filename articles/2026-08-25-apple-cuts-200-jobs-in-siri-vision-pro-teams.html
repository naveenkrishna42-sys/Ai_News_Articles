Apple cuts 200+ jobs in Siri, Vision Pro teams

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple cuts over 200 jobs in Siri and Vision Pro teams
  • John Ternus takes over as Apple CEO, signals strategic shifts
  • Layoffs in Vision Products Group highlight challenges in spatial computing

Apple's Leadership Shake-Up

Apple's recent reshuffling of teams and job cuts in Siri and Vision Pro departments signal a strategic realignment under new CEO John Ternus. The layoffs, amounting to over 200 roles, mark a significant shift in Apple's focus, particularly in the immersive video and gaming sectors.

Siri Team Restructuring

The cuts in the Siri team are part of a broader effort to streamline operations and prioritize key initiatives. While the exact reasons for the layoffs remain private, industry analysts suggest this could be a response to slower-than-expected adoption rates of Siri's advanced features.

Vision Pro Team Layoffs

The Vision Products Group, responsible for Apple's spatial computing efforts, has seen the most significant cuts. This move comes amid reports of slow progress in developing the Vision Pro, Apple's mixed-reality headset. The layoffs, which affect an entire VR development team, underscore the challenges Apple faces in this emerging market.

Broader Implications

The job cuts and team reshuffles at Apple are not isolated incidents but part of a larger trend in the tech industry. As companies navigate economic uncertainties and shifting market demands, restructuring becomes a common strategy. For Apple, this could mean a refocusing of resources towards more profitable and rapidly growing areas of its business.

Expert Analysis

Industry experts have varying opinions on Apple's recent moves. Some view the layoffs as a necessary step to streamline operations and improve efficiency. Others, however, see it as a sign of Apple's struggle to keep pace with competitors in the spatial computing space. Regardless, the reshuffling of teams and the cuts in Siri and Vision Pro roles are likely to have a significant impact on Apple's future product development and market position.
