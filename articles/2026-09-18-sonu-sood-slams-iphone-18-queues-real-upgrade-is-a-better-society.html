Sonu Sood's Queues Remark Lands Amid iPhone 18 Frenzy

Actor and philanthropist Sonu Sood has taken a pointed dig at the midnight queues outside Apple stores across India, tweeting that the real upgrade is a better society. His comment arrived as buyers thronged retail outlets in Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru for the iPhone 18 series launch. Sood's remark does not attack Apple buyers directly; it questions the spectacle of paying a premium to stand in line for a device that changes little year on year.

The timing is sharp. Apple's iPhone 18 Pro demand is outpacing last year's launch, according to early retail data, and India's appetite for the brand has never been stronger. But for buyers weighing a Rs 1.5 lakh-plus purchase, the real question is not whether the phone is good. It is whether the upgrade is worth it.

iPhone 18 Series: Verified India Pricing and Specs

Apple has kept the four-model structure: iPhone 18, iPhone 18 Plus, iPhone 18 Pro, and iPhone 18 Pro Max. India prices carry the usual GST and import duty premium over US rates. Here is what buyers actually pay.

What Actually Changes From iPhone 17

The A20 Pro chip delivers roughly 15 to 20 percent better CPU performance than the A19 Pro in synthetic benchmarks, with efficiency gains that matter more than raw speed. The Pro Max battery improvement is the most tangible upgrade, adding close to four hours of video playback over the iPhone 17 Pro Max.

Camera hardware is largely carried over. The new computational photography pipeline improves low-light skin tones and reduces shutter lag, but buyers will not see a dramatic leap in daylight shots. The standard iPhone 18 finally gets a 120Hz display, which is a genuine first for the non-Pro line and the strongest reason to buy the base model.

Buyer Implications: Who Should Upgrade

If you own an iPhone 15 Pro or older, the iPhone 18 Pro is a legitimate three-generation jump with meaningful gains in battery, thermals, and video stabilisation. If you own an iPhone 17 or 17 Pro, the case is weak. Resale values for one-year-old Pro models hover around Rs 70,000 to Rs 85,000, meaning an upgrade costs Rs 60,000 or more for incremental gains.

Buyers on a budget should note that the iPhone 18 at Rs 79,900 now offers the 120Hz display that was previously a Pro exclusive. That makes it the value pick of the lineup, and the variant most likely to see stock shortages through October.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • Pro Max battery life: Up to 33 hours video playback, the biggest single-year gain in four generations.
  • Standard iPhone 18: First non-Pro model with a 120Hz LTPO display; best value in the range.
  • Pro camera: Hardware unchanged; improvements are software-driven and subtle.
  • India pricing: Top 1TB Pro Max crosses Rs 2 lakh, among the highest iPhone prices globally.
  • Queue culture: Midnight launches are now marketing events; most buyers can order online with delivery within a week.

The Verdict on Sood's Point

Sood's remark lands because the iPhone 18 is an excellent phone with a thin upgrade story. The queues are less about necessity and more about belonging. Buyers who need a new phone this year have a clear best pick in the standard iPhone 18. Buyers chasing the Pro Max should ask whether a better camera pipeline justifies Rs 1.44 lakh. For most, the honest answer is that the real upgrade remains the one Sood named.
