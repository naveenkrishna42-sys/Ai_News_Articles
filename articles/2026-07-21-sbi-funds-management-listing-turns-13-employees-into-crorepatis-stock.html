SBI Funds Management listing turns 13 employees into crorepatis, stock ends 6.2% higher

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • SBI Funds Management shares debuted at a 7% premium to the IPO price and closed 6.2% higher on listing day.
  • 13 employees became crorepatis through employee stock option plans (ESOPs).
  • The largest individual ESOP payout from the listing was valued at Rs 121 crore.

MUMBAI: SBI Funds Management made its stock market debut on Tuesday, and the listing turned a select group of its employees into crorepatis. The stock opened at a 7% premium to its initial public offering price and ended the trading session 6.2% higher, riding on strong investor appetite for India's asset management sector.

ESOP bonanza for employees

The listing unlocked significant wealth for 13 employees who held employee stock option plans (ESOPs) in the company. According to filings and market sources, the top ESOP payout from the listing touched Rs 121 crore, making the recipient a crorepati many times over.

The ESOP pool was created as part of the company's employee retention and incentive structure. While the exact breakdown of individual holdings is not public, multiple employees are now crorepatis on paper, a rare windfall in India's mutual fund industry.

Listing day performance

The shares listed at Rs 481 apiece on the National Stock Exchange, a 7% premium over the IPO price of Rs 450. The stock gained further during the day, closing at Rs 478.15, up 6.2% from the issue price.

Analysts attributed the positive debut to strong subscription numbers and investor confidence in India's growing mutual fund penetration. The IPO, which opened last week, was subscribed over 40 times across all categories, with qualified institutional buyers leading the demand.

The listing comes at a time when Indian asset management companies are seeing record inflows and rising retail participation. SBI Funds Management, which runs SBI Mutual Fund, is one of the largest asset managers in the country by assets under management.

Industry context and investor sentiment

The strong debut for SBI Funds Management follows a pattern of robust listings for financial services firms this year. Investors are betting that India's mutual fund industry will continue to grow as more savers shift from traditional instruments like fixed deposits to market-linked products.

The company's assets under management have grown at a compound annual growth rate of over 20% in the last five years. Market watchers say the premium listing reflects this growth trajectory, though some analysts have flagged that valuations are stretched.

The ESOP bonanza also highlights the growing trend of wealth creation through employee stock ownership in India's financial sector. In recent years, several banks and asset managers have used ESOPs to attract and retain talent.

What to watch: The stock's performance in the coming weeks will be closely tracked, as will any further disclosure on the distribution of ESOP wealth. Investors will also look for cues on how the company plans to deploy the IPO proceeds, which are earmarked for expansion and technology upgrades.
