iPhone 18 Pro price hike likely as component costs soar

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • TrendForce reports iPhone 18 Pro BOM cost may rise nearly 40%
  • Apple likely to sacrifice margins to maintain shipment volumes
  • Analysts link cost pressure to advanced A20 Pro chip and camera upgrades

Apple's next flagship, the iPhone 18 Pro, is expected to carry a significantly higher price tag as component costs surge, according to a new industry analysis. Market research firm TrendForce has projected that the bill of materials (BOM) for the device could jump by nearly 40 per cent compared to its predecessor.

The report, published this week, suggests that Apple may be forced to absorb a substantial portion of these increased costs to keep shipments on track. Rather than passing the full burden to consumers, the company could trim its own margins to sustain demand in an increasingly competitive smartphone market.

What's driving the cost surge?

Industry watchers point to a combination of factors behind the sharp rise in production expenses. The iPhone 18 Pro is rumoured to feature the next-generation A20 Pro chip, manufactured on a more advanced process node, which typically commands higher fabrication costs.

Reports also indicate a significant camera upgrade for the Pro Max variant, potentially including new sensor technology and enhanced optical components. These additions, while boosting performance, add directly to the component tally that feeds into the final BOM.

Supply chain constraints, particularly around memory and display panels, have further compounded the pressure. Global semiconductor prices have remained volatile, and Apple's reliance on cutting-edge parts leaves little room for cost negotiation.

Apple's margin dilemma

TrendForce's analysis suggests that Apple faces a strategic choice: raise retail prices or accept thinner profit margins. Historically, the Cupertino-based company has maintained premium pricing to protect its industry-leading margins, but the scale of this projected cost increase may test that approach.

The report indicates that Apple is likely to prioritise shipment volumes over profitability in the short term. This would mark a departure from its usual stance, signalling how intense the competition has become in the premium segment, particularly against rivals like Samsung and emerging Chinese manufacturers.

For Indian consumers, the implications are twofold. A price hike on the iPhone 18 Pro could push the flagship's starting price well beyond current levels, while a margin-sacrificing approach might keep it closer to existing rates. Neither scenario offers much relief for buyers already grappling with steep import duties and local taxes.

Release timeline and demand signals

Separate reports have suggested that the iPhone 18 Pro will launch in September, with demand expected to be robust enough to drive an on-schedule release. Forbes, citing supply chain sources, has indicated that early orders for components are strong, a sign that Apple is confident about consumer appetite.

The Deccan Chronicle has also reported on the Pro Max variant, highlighting the A20 Pro chip and camera enhancements as key selling points. TelecomTalk has noted that Apple is rumoured to increase iPhone 17 prices ahead of the new launch, a move that could be aimed at offsetting future cost pressures or repositioning the lineup.

Apple has not yet commented on the TrendForce projections, and official pricing details remain unconfirmed. The company typically announces new iPhones in September, with availability in India following shortly after.

What to watch next

All eyes will be on Apple's September launch event, where the company is expected to reveal the iPhone 18 Pro's official pricing and specifications. Whether the company chooses to absorb costs or pass them on will become clear only at that stage. For now, prospective buyers and investors alike are bracing for a flagship that could redefine what a premium smartphone costs.
