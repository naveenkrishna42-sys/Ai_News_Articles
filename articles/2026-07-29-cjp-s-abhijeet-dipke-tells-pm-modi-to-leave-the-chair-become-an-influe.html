CJP's Abhijeet Dipke tells PM Modi to 'leave the chair, become an influencer'

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • Abhijeet Dipke told PM Modi to 'leave the chair, become an influencer' during a press conference.
  • He questioned the anti-paper leak bill, calling it a tool to target protesting students.
  • Dipke warned the Centre that the youth will change the government if it doesn't listen.

Sharp attack on PM Modi

Abhijeet Dipke, the founder of the Centre for Justice and Peace (CJP), launched a blistering attack on Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Wednesday. He told the PM to 'leave the chair and become an influencer' if he cannot handle the concerns of the youth.

Dipke was speaking to reporters in Delhi after meeting with student leaders from across the country. His remarks come amid a growing confrontation between the government and student groups over the controversial anti-paper leak bill.

Questioning the anti-paper leak bill

Dipke questioned the intent behind the new anti-paper leak legislation. He argued that the bill is being misused to target students who are protesting peacefully.

'Students are not terrorists, they are the future,' Dipke said. He asked the government to stop treating young protesters as criminals. The CJP founder said the bill has provisions that can be twisted to suppress dissent.

He demanded a complete review of the legislation, calling it a draconian law that will crush the aspirations of millions of students.

Warning to the Centre

Dipke did not mince words when he addressed the ruling dispensation. 'Back from Delhi, Abhijeet Dipke warns Centre: Youth will change the government if it doesn't fall in line,' he said, repeating the message he had given earlier.

He said the youth of India are watching every move of the government. If the government continues to ignore their problems, they will show them the door in the next elections.

The CJP leader claimed that students from over 200 colleges and universities have reached out to him. He said they are ready to launch a nationwide agitation if the bill is not withdrawn.

Who is Abhijeet Dipke?

Abhijeet Dipke is an IIT graduate who later became a lawyer and a journalist. He founded the CJP as a platform to address issues of farm distress, student rights, and social justice.

He has emerged as a prominent voice against the government's policies on education and agriculture. His movement has gained traction among students in several states.

Dipke's background as an IITian and a lawyer gives him a unique standing in the debate. He has been critical of both the ruling party and the opposition, calling for a new political alternative.

What happens next?

The CJP has announced a series of protests in the coming weeks. Dipke said he will lead a march to Parliament if the government does not respond to their demands. The anti-paper leak bill is expected to be tabled in the next session of Parliament, and all eyes are on how the government handles the growing opposition from student groups.
