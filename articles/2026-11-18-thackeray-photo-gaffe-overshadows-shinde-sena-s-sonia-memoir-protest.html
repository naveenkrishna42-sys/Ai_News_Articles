Thackeray Photo Gaffe Overshadows Shinde Sena's Sonia Memoir Protest

Political Tensions Escalate Over Sonia Gandhi's Memoir

Mumbai witnessed a significant political uproar on November 18, 2026, as members of the Shiv Sena (Shinde faction) escalated their protest against certain references in Sonia Gandhi's recently released memoir. The demonstrations, primarily aimed at demanding a ban on the book in Maharashtra, quickly veered into controversy, drawing sharp criticism from across the political spectrum.

The core grievance of the Shinde faction revolved around passages concerning Vinayak Damodar Savarkar, a revered figure for many right-wing outfits. Protesters tore pages from the memoir, asserting that the book contained disparaging remarks or misrepresentations about Savarkar, thereby hurting sentiments and distorting historical narratives.

The Unintended Consequence: Bal Thackeray's Image Tarnished

During the fervent protest, an unforeseen incident severely overshadowed the Shiv Sena's original agenda. In the act of tearing pages from Sonia Gandhi's memoir, a photograph of Shiv Sena founder Bal Thackeray was inadvertently torn. This gaffe immediately ignited a fresh wave of condemnation, particularly from rival political factions and sections of the public who revere the late supremo.

The incident was widely reported, with media outlets like The New Indian Express highlighting how the 'Sonia memoir protest backfired on Shinde Sena over Balasaheb photo gaffe'. The irony of Shiv Sena members, who claim to uphold Bal Thackeray's legacy, inadvertently damaging his image during a protest meant to assert their ideological stance, was not lost on observers. Separately, reports also indicated that an image of Congress leader Mani Shankar Aiyar was torn during the same protests, adding another layer to the day's controversies.

Shinde's Stance and Shaina NC's Defense

In the aftermath of the Bal Thackeray photo gaffe, Maharashtra Chief Minister Eknath Shinde swiftly entered the fray, publicly backing BJP leader Shaina NC. While the specifics of Shaina NC's initial statement on the 'photo controversy' were not detailed in initial reports, Shinde's endorsement implied a coordinated effort to defend the Shinde faction's actions and manage the public relations fallout.

Shinde's support for Shaina NC underscored the alliance's attempt to control the narrative, likely by downplaying the accidental tearing of Bal Thackeray's image or by redirecting focus back to the perceived affront by Sonia Gandhi's memoir. This political maneuver aimed to mitigate the severe criticism emanating from the opposition, which quickly capitalized on the self-inflicted damage.

Political Ramifications and Public Perception

The incident has significant implications for Maharashtra politics. The Uddhav Thackeray-led Shiv Sena (UBT) faction and other opposition parties were quick to lambast the Shinde group, accusing them of disrespecting their own founder. This provided ammunition for the opposition to question the Shinde faction's commitment to Bal Thackeray's legacy, potentially eroding their moral high ground.

The controversy also shifted public attention away from the original protest's objective—the demand for a ban on Sonia Gandhi's memoir. Instead, the focus became the perceived desecration of Bal Thackeray's image, forcing the Shinde faction onto the defensive. Such events can influence voter perception, especially in a state where political identity and historical reverence play a crucial role in electoral outcomes.
