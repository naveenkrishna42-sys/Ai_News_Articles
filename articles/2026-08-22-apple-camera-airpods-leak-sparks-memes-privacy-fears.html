Apple Camera AirPods leak sparks memes, privacy fears

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Leaked demo shows AirPods with camera, AI features
  • Launch still expected in 2027
  • Privacy concerns raised over potential data collection

Leaked Demo Reveals Camera AirPods Features

A leaked demo video of Apple's Camera AirPods has surfaced online, revealing several features including image capture resolution, a status light, and person detection capabilities. The video has sparked a wave of hilarious memes on social media, with users joking about the potential uses of the device.

Launch Still on Track for 2027

Despite the leak, multiple sources have confirmed that Apple's Camera AirPods remain on track for a 2027 launch. The device is reportedly set to feature advanced AI capabilities, allowing users to capture images and videos directly from their AirPods.

Privacy Concerns Raised

While the leaked demo has revealed some of the features of the Camera AirPods, experts have raised concerns about potential privacy risks. The device's ability to capture images and videos could raise significant privacy issues, with users potentially being unaware of when and where their data is being collected.

Apple's macOS Update Hints at More Upcoming Products

In related news, a recent update to Apple's macOS operating system has hinted at the potential release of more than 10 upcoming products. The update includes references to new hardware and software features, suggesting that Apple is working on a range of new devices and services.

What Happens Next

As the launch of Apple's Camera AirPods approaches, it will be interesting to see how the company addresses the privacy concerns raised by the leaked demo. Additionally, with the potential release of more than 10 upcoming products, Apple's product pipeline is expected to remain busy in the coming years.
