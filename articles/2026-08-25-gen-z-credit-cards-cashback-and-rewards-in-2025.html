Gen Z credit cards: Cashback and rewards in 2025

Gen Z is redefining credit card usage in India, demanding digital-first experiences and rewards. The 2025 landscape features a mix of established players and new entrants, all vying for their attention. But what's the real value of cashback and rewards in these cards?

Digital-first design and instant rewards

Gen Z credit cards prioritize digital-first design, with seamless app integration and instant rewards. Features like mobile-only applications, real-time spending notifications, and instant cashback credits are standard. This focus on digital convenience aligns with Gen Z's preference for instant gratification and seamless experiences.

Cashback and rewards: A double-edged sword

Cashback and rewards are key selling points, but their real value depends on spending habits. High cashback rates on specific categories, like groceries or entertainment, can be enticing. However, the complexity of reward structures and the need for minimum spending thresholds can be off-putting. The best cards offer tiered rewards, where users can earn more points or cashback with increased spending.

New entrants and established players

New entrants are disrupting the market, offering innovative rewards programs and flexible spending limits. Established players are adapting, introducing Gen Z-friendly features and partnerships with popular digital platforms. The competition is fierce, with each card vying for Gen Z's attention through unique perks and rewards.
