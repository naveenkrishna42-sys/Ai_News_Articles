Bitcoin call spreads target $72,000 as Fed meeting looms

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Large bitcoin call spread positions are targeting a $72,000 price by end of month.
  • The expiry coincides with the Federal Reserve's next policy meeting.
  • Traders appear to be betting on a bullish outcome from the Fed's decision.

Mumbai: Traders in the cryptocurrency options market are placing unusually large bets that bitcoin will rally to $72,000 by the end of the month. The positions, structured as call spreads, have drawn attention because their expiry aligns directly with the next Federal Reserve policy meeting.

What the data shows

According to data tracked by CoinDesk, multiple substantial call spread trades have been executed on major crypto derivatives exchanges. These strategies involve buying a call option at a lower strike price and selling another at a higher strike, capping both profit and loss. The target price of $72,000 is notably above bitcoin's current trading levels around the $66,000 mark.

The sheer size of the positions suggests institutional or high-net-worth involvement. Option traders typically use call spreads to express a moderately bullish view while limiting upfront premium costs.

The Fed factor

The expiry date for these options falls right when the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) is scheduled to announce its next interest rate decision. Market participants widely expect the Fed to hold rates steady, but the accompanying statement and economic projections will be scrutinised for any hints about future policy direction.

A dovish tone from the Fed could weaken the US dollar and boost risk assets like bitcoin. Conversely, a hawkish surprise might dampen the rally hopes these traders are banking on. The timing of the trade is therefore seen as a direct bet on the Fed's outcome.

Market context

Bitcoin has been consolidating in a range between $64,000 and $68,000 over the past week. The $72,000 level represents a resistance zone that, if broken, could open the door to new highs. However, the open interest and volume data indicate that not all market participants share the same conviction.

Some analysts point out that call spreads are often used by traders expecting a controlled move rather than a violent breakout. The capped upside at $72,000 suggests a target-driven approach rather than a speculative moon-shot.

What to watch

All eyes will now be on the Fed's decision later this month. If the central bank signals a pause or a pivot, the $72,000 target could be within reach. If not, these option positions may expire worthless. The next few trading sessions will reveal whether the bullish conviction holds or fades.
