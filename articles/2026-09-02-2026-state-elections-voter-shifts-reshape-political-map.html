The 2026 State Assembly Elections mark a significant generational shift in voter demographics. Younger voters, particularly those aged 18-35, are becoming more politically engaged. Economic policies and social welfare are the top issues influencing voter decisions in 2026. Job creation, affordable housing, and healthcare are at the forefront of voters' minds. Regional parties are capitalizing on these issues, tailoring their campaigns to address local economic concerns. Social welfare initiatives are another key issue driving voter decisions. Voters are evaluating the government's performance in areas such as education, healthcare, and social security.
