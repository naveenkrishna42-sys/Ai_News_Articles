Sensex gains 609 points, Nifty rises 182 points in strong market rally

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex surged 609 points to close higher
  • Nifty rose 182 points in today's session
  • Broad-based buying lifted market sentiment

Indian equity markets closed sharply higher on Thursday, with benchmark indices recording strong gains. The BSE Sensex surged 609 points, while the NSE Nifty rose 182 points, marking a broad-based rally that lifted investor sentiment.

Market participants attributed the upswing to positive global cues and buying across sectors. The rally was led by banking, IT, and auto stocks, which saw significant buying interest throughout the session.

Sensex, Nifty post strong gains

The 30-share BSE Sensex ended the day at a session high, gaining 609 points. The broader NSE Nifty closed 182 points higher, reflecting strong momentum in the market.

Both indices opened on a positive note and maintained their upward trajectory through the day. Analysts noted that the market breadth was positive, with more stocks advancing than declining on both exchanges.

Sectoral performance

Banking stocks were among the top gainers, with the Nifty Bank index rising sharply. IT and auto stocks also contributed to the rally, supported by strong buying from domestic institutional investors.

Metal and pharma stocks also traded higher, adding to the overall positive sentiment. The rally was broad-based, with most sectoral indices closing in the green.

Foreign portfolio investors were net buyers in the cash market, according to provisional data. Their buying activity added to the positive momentum seen in the session.

What drove the rally

Global cues were supportive, with Asian markets trading higher on Thursday. Positive signals from US markets overnight also boosted sentiment among domestic investors.

Investors also reacted to expectations of stable economic growth and corporate earnings. The market ignored concerns over inflation data, focusing instead on recovery prospects.

Traders said short covering ahead of the weekly derivatives expiry added to the upward move. The Nifty futures contract saw increased open interest, indicating fresh long positions.

The broader market also performed well, with the BSE midcap and smallcap indices gaining over 1% each. This indicated that buying interest was not limited to large-cap stocks alone.

Market volatility remained low, with the India VIX declining marginally during the session. This suggested that traders were comfortable with the current risk levels.

Technically, the Nifty broke above its immediate resistance level on the charts. Analysts said this could pave the way for further upside in the coming sessions.

Looking ahead, traders will monitor global cues and macroeconomic data for direction. The market is likely to remain focused on corporate earnings and policy signals from the central bank.
