HMD Global launches Nokia 123 Shield, a rugged feature phone with 19-day battery

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nokia 123 Shield is HMD's most affordable water and dust-resistant feature phone.
  • The phone offers a 19-day standby battery life and USB-C charging.
  • Targeted at users in emerging markets who need durability and long battery life.

HMD Global's latest feature phone targets durability and battery life

HMD Global has introduced the Nokia 123 Shield, a new feature phone that the company calls its most affordable water and dust-resistant model. The device is aimed at users who prioritise ruggedness and long battery life over smartphone features.

The Nokia 123 Shield comes with an IP rating for protection against water and dust, a rare feature in the budget feature phone segment. HMD has not disclosed the exact IP rating, but the phone is designed to withstand splashes and exposure to dust, making it suitable for outdoor and industrial use.

19-day standby battery and USB-C charging

The standout feature of the Nokia 123 Shield is its battery life. HMD claims the phone can last up to 19 days on a single charge in standby mode. This is achieved through a combination of a large battery and a power-efficient display and chipset.

The phone also supports USB-C charging, a significant upgrade for a feature phone at this price point. USB-C is becoming the standard across all phone categories, and HMD's decision to include it ensures compatibility with modern chargers and cables.

Design and target audience

The Nokia 123 Shield features a rugged design with reinforced edges and a textured back for better grip. The phone is built to handle drops and rough handling, which is a key selling point for workers in construction, manufacturing, and agriculture.

HMD is positioning the Nokia 123 Shield as a reliable secondary phone or a primary device for users who do not need smartphone capabilities. The phone is likely to be priced aggressively to compete with other basic feature phones in markets like India and Southeast Asia.

Market context and competition

The feature phone market remains strong in many developing countries where network coverage is limited and users need affordable, long-lasting devices. Nokia has a storied history in this segment, and the 123 Shield is an attempt to reclaim some of that legacy with modern features.

Competitors in this space include JioPhone and various local brands that offer similar rugged feature phones. However, HMD's brand recognition and the promise of IP protection and long battery life could give the Nokia 123 Shield an edge.

What to watch next

HMD Global is expected to announce the pricing and availability of the Nokia 123 Shield in the coming weeks. The phone's success will depend on how well it balances affordability with the promised durability and battery performance.
