Delhi High Court to hear PIL seeking NIA probe into Cockroach Janta Party protests

📅 2026-07-24 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Delhi High Court has agreed to hear a Public Interest Litigation (PIL) demanding an NIA probe into the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) protests.
  • The PIL alleges foreign funding behind the CJP's agitation, which is linked to NEET exam protests.
  • Separately, the Supreme Court refused urgent hearing on a plea against police lathicharge on Jantar Mantar protesters, with CJI Surya Kant stating 'don't waste time'.

Court agrees to examine plea

The Delhi High Court on Thursday agreed to hear a Public Interest Litigation seeking a National Investigation Agency (NIA) probe into the protests organised by the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP). The PIL, filed by a group of citizens, alleges that the protests were not merely domestic affairs but had foreign funding behind them.

The matter is expected to be listed for detailed arguments in the coming weeks. The court's decision to entertain the plea marks a significant development in the ongoing controversy surrounding the CJP's agitation.

Allegations of foreign links

The petitioners have claimed that the CJP's protests, particularly those linked to the NEET examination agitation, were orchestrated with financial support from external sources. They argue that such foreign interference warrants a thorough investigation by the NIA, the country's premier counter-terrorism agency.

While the petition does not specify the exact nature of the foreign involvement, it asserts that the protests disrupted public order and attempted to undermine the integrity of national examinations. The government has not yet issued a formal response to these allegations.

Supreme Court rejects urgent plea on lathicharge

In a related development, the Supreme Court on Wednesday refused to grant an urgent hearing on a plea challenging the police lathicharge against CJP protesters at Jantar Mantar. Chief Justice of India Surya Kant, presiding over a bench, told the petitioners, "Don't waste our time."

The bench declined to entertain the plea, which sought action against the police for alleged excessive use of force. The CJI's remark underscored the court's reluctance to intervene in what it viewed as a routine law and order matter.

Separately, the same bench also dismissed a plea related to the NEET exam controversy, with a similar admonition. The petitioners had sought directions to address irregularities in the exam's conduct, but the court found no grounds for urgent intervention.

What is the Cockroach Janta Party?

The Cockroach Janta Party emerged as a fringe political group known for its unconventional protest methods. The party gained attention for its demonstrations against the NEET examination, which it claims discriminates against rural and underprivileged students.

However, the group's tactics, including street protests and confrontations with authorities, have drawn criticism from various quarters. The PIL now seeking an NIA probe raises the stakes, potentially bringing the party's funding and activities under official scrutiny.

What happens next

The Delhi High Court will hear the PIL in detail in its upcoming sessions. The outcome could determine whether the NIA launches a formal investigation into the CJP's operations and its alleged foreign connections.
