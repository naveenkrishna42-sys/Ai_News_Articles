SC Rejects Challenge to School Entry Curbs in UP, Rajasthan

Key points
  • Supreme Court upholds school entry restrictions in UP and Rajasthan.
  • Restrictions aim to maintain school safety and order.
  • Journalists and activists face challenges in accessing educational institutions.

Background to the Case

The Supreme Court of India recently declined to entertain a plea challenging the curbs on journalists and activists entering schools in Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan. This decision comes at a time when there has been a significant increase in the number of incidents where journalists and activists have been prevented from entering educational institutions.

Key Arguments Presented

The plea argued that the restrictions on journalists and activists entering schools were a violation of their fundamental rights to freedom of speech and expression, as guaranteed under Article 19 of the Indian Constitution. The plea also contended that these restrictions were arbitrary and discriminatory, as they were not applied uniformly across all schools and educational institutions.

Supreme Court's Decision

The Supreme Court, in its order, stated that it would not entertain the plea challenging the curbs on journalists and activists entering schools. The Court noted that the restrictions were necessary to maintain the safety and order of the schools and that the rights of journalists and activists could not be absolute in the context of maintaining the sanctity of educational institutions.

Implications for Journalists and Activists

The Supreme Court's decision has significant implications for journalists and activists who wish to report on incidents of violence or abuse in schools. The restrictions on their entry into schools can hinder their ability to investigate and report on such incidents, which can have serious consequences for the victims and the wider community.

Conclusion

In conclusion, the Supreme Court's decision to decline the plea challenging the curbs on journalists and activists entering schools in Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan is a significant development. While the restrictions are necessary to maintain the safety and order of the schools, they also raise important questions about the balance between the rights of journalists and activists and the need to maintain the sanctity of educational institutions.
