Minor boy shoots 16-year-old girl in Bengaluru, buries body with family's help

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • A minor boy allegedly shot a 16-year-old girl in Bengaluru.
  • The body was buried with the help of the boy's family.
  • Police have arrested four people in connection with the case.

Bengaluru: In a shocking case that has sent ripples through the city, a minor boy allegedly shot dead a 16-year-old girl, who he claimed was his girlfriend, and then buried her body with the help of his family. The incident came to light after the girl's parents filed a missing person complaint.

How the Crime Unfolded

According to police, the accused, a 17-year-old boy, and the victim had been in a relationship for some time. On the day of the incident, the two met at a secluded spot on the outskirts of Bengaluru. An argument broke out between them, during which the boy allegedly pulled out a pistol and shot the girl.

After the shooting, the boy reportedly panicked. He called his father and uncle, who arrived at the scene. The trio then decided to dispose of the body. They dug a shallow grave nearby and buried the girl.

Family Tried to Cover Up

The boy's family did not stop at the burial. Police sources said that over the next few days, the family made efforts to destroy evidence. They cleaned the crime scene and disposed of the boy's clothes. The pistol used in the crime has not yet been recovered.

When the girl's parents reported her missing, police began an investigation. Suspicion quickly fell on the boy, who had been seen with the girl on the day of her disappearance. Under sustained questioning, the boy broke down and confessed to the crime.

Arrests Made

Police have arrested four people in connection with the case. The arrested include the minor boy, his father, his uncle, and another relative. They have been charged with murder, destruction of evidence, and criminal conspiracy.

The boy has been sent to a juvenile observation home. His father and uncle have been remanded to judicial custody. The police are now trying to trace the source of the pistol.

Community Shocked

The incident has left the local community in disbelief. Neighbours described the boy's family as normal and well-settled. No one suspected that they could be involved in such a brutal crime.

School authorities where the girl studied expressed grief. They said she was a quiet student who kept to herself. Counselors have been arranged for her classmates.

Investigation Ongoing

Police officials have not yet revealed the exact motive for the murder. They are probing whether the argument was over a personal matter or something else. The forensic team is examining the burial site for more clues.

This case highlights the growing concern over the easy availability of firearms to minors in the city. Police have promised a thorough investigation and assured that all those involved will face the full force of the law.

What comes next? The juvenile justice board will decide the minor's fate, while the trial for the adult accused is expected to begin soon. The case has once again put the spotlight on teenage relationships and gun violence in urban India.
