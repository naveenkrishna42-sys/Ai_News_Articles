Aamir Khan receives death threat from Lawrence Bishnoi gang over 'love jihad' remark

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Aamir Khan received a death threat from the Lawrence Bishnoi gang.
  • The threat cites 'promoting love jihad' and warns to 'choke the breath'.
  • This comes days after his marriage to Gauri Spratt.
  • Mumbai Police are verifying the threat's authenticity.

Bollywood actor Aamir Khan has become the latest target of the Lawrence Bishnoi gang. The gang has allegedly issued a death threat against the actor, accusing him of 'promoting love jihad'. The threat comes just days after Khan's marriage to Gauri Spratt.

Mumbai Police are currently verifying the claim. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact nature of the threat or its origin.

Threat details

The message, which has been circulated online, warns the actor to stop what the gang calls 'love jihad'. It explicitly threatens to 'choke the breath' of Khan if he continues. The Bishnoi gang has a history of targeting high-profile figures over personal and ideological disputes.

This is not the first time the gang has threatened a Bollywood star. Earlier, Salman Khan received similar threats from the same group. The pattern suggests a coordinated effort to intimidate public figures.

Context of the threat

Aamir Khan's recent marriage to Gauri Spratt has been a subject of public discussion. Spratt, a Hindu woman, converted to Islam before the wedding. This has led to accusations from certain quarters that Khan is promoting 'love jihad'. The term is used by some groups to describe alleged campaigns by Muslim men to convert Hindu women through marriage.

The actor has not publicly commented on the threat. His representatives have not issued a statement either. The timing of the threat, so soon after his wedding, has raised concerns about the safety of the actor and his family.

Police response

Mumbai Police have launched an investigation. They are tracing the source of the threat and assessing its credibility. Security at Khan's residence has been increased as a precaution.

Authorities are also coordinating with central agencies to monitor the gang's activities. The Bishnoi gang operates across multiple states and has been linked to several high-profile crimes, including extortion and murder.

The threat against Aamir Khan is being taken seriously. Police have not ruled out the possibility of an immediate physical attack. They are advising the actor to remain cautious and avoid public appearances for the time being.

What next

The investigation is in its early stages. Mumbai Police are expected to provide an update once the threat is verified. Aamir Khan's security detail will remain on high alert. The actor's fans and the film industry are watching closely for any developments.
