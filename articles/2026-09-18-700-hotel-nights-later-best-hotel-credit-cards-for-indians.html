700+ Hotel Nights Later: Best Hotel Credit Cards for Indians

Key points
  • Hotel co-branded cards deliver 10-25X rewards on stays, but general travel cards often beat them on everyday spend.
  • Annual fees range from free to Rs 50,000; net value depends on your yearly hotel nights and redemption pattern.
  • Indian cardholders must account for forex markups of 2-3.5% when using these cards abroad.

Seven hundred nights in hotels is roughly two years of sleeping away from home. That kind of volume exposes which credit cards actually deliver value and which quietly erode it through poor redemption rates and hidden fees. Based on that experience, here are the hotel credit cards that consistently perform for Indian travellers in 2026, with practical benchmarks and pricing context.

How We Evaluated These Cards

Our assessment focused on four metrics: reward rate on hotel spend, annual fee versus realisable benefits, redemption flexibility, and foreign transaction costs. We deliberately excluded cards that lock points into a single hotel chain with poor Indian redemption options. All fees are stated in Indian rupees, with dollar conversions at Rs 88.

Top Hotel Credit Cards: Detailed Breakdown

1. HDFC Bank Infinia

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Not a hotel co-branded card, but the 3.3% base reward rate on all spends and 10X rewards on SmartBuy hotel bookings make it the most versatile option. Annual fee is Rs 12,500, waived on Rs 10 lakh annual spend. Redemption via HDFC SmartBuy for hotels yields up to Re 1 per point. Best for high spenders who want flexibility across chains.

2. American Express Platinum Travel

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Earns 5X Membership Rewards on Marriott and Hilton stays, translating to roughly 10-15% back on hotel spend when transferred to airline partners. Annual fee is Rs 5,000. The card offers 4 complimentary lounge visits per year and a Rs 10,000 Taj voucher on Rs 4 lakh annual spend. Ideal for frequent domestic travellers.

3. Axis Bank Vistara Infinite

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

While primarily an airline card, it earns 6 CV Points per Rs 200 on hotel spends, and CV Points can be redeemed for hotel bookings via Vistara's partner network. Annual fee is Rs 10,000, with a complimentary business class ticket on joining and renewal. Best for those who split travel between air and hotel.

4. SBI Card Elite

💳 Apply for SBI Simply Click (₹500 Voucher)

Offers 5X rewards on hotel bookings made through SBI Card's dedicated travel portal, with 2X on other spends. Annual fee is Rs 4,999. Reward rate is lower than premium competitors, but the card frequently runs 10-15% discount offers on MakeMyTrip and Yatra. Suitable for budget-conscious travellers.

5. ICICI Bank Emeralde Private

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Provides 12 reward points per Rs 100 on hotel spends, with each point worth up to 50 paise on ICICI's travel portal. Annual fee is Rs 12,499. Includes 4 complimentary night stays at ITC hotels annually. Best for ITC loyalists and those who value guaranteed night certificates.

6. Citi PremierMiles

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Earns 10 miles per Rs 100 on hotel bookings, with miles transferable to multiple airline and hotel partners including Marriott Bonvoy. Annual fee is Rs 5,000. The transfer flexibility makes it a strong choice for optimisers, though Citi's India transition to Axis may affect future benefits.

Real-World Benchmarks and Value Justification

For a traveller spending 30 hotel nights per year at an average of Rs 8,000 per night, total hotel spend is Rs 2.4 lakh. Using Amex Platinum Travel, this yields roughly 12,000 Membership Rewards points, worth about Rs 24,000 on airline transfers. After the Rs 5,000 fee, net value is Rs 19,000, or 8% return. HDFC Infinia on the same spend via SmartBuy would earn 24,000 points, worth up to Rs 24,000, with a Rs 12,500 fee, netting Rs 11,500. The math favours Amex for pure hotel spend, but Infinia wins if you also spend heavily on other categories.

Pros and Cons of Hotel Credit Cards

  • Pros: High reward rates on hotel spend; free night certificates; elite status upgrades; travel insurance and lounge access.
  • Cons: High annual fees; restrictive redemption; foreign transaction fees of 2-3.5%; devaluation risk; limited value if you stay fewer than 15 nights per year.

Verdict: Which Card Fits Your Wallet?

If you spend more than Rs 3 lakh annually on hotels, the HDFC Infinia or Amex Platinum Travel justify their fees. For moderate spenders, SBI Card Elite or Citi PremierMiles offer better value. Always calculate net return after fees and forex markups. For Indian travellers, the sweet spot is a card that transfers points to multiple partners, not one that locks you into a single chain. That flexibility, earned over 700 nights, is the real benchmark.
