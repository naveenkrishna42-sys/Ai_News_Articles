Farhan Akhtar hints at 'pattern' of actors exiting films amid Don 3 row

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Farhan Akhtar comments on actors leaving films, calling it a 'pattern nowadays'.
  • Reference to Ranveer Singh's exit from Don 3 adds context to the remark.
  • Akhtar recalls Saif Ali Khan almost quitting Dil Chahta Hai, linking past and present.
  • The remarks have fueled speculation about tensions behind the Don 3 production.

Filmmaker and actor Farhan Akhtar has waded into the ongoing Don 3 controversy with a pointed remark that appears aimed at Ranveer Singh. Without naming the actor directly, Akhtar said that actors walking out of films has become a “pattern nowadays”, a comment that has set social media abuzz.

The statement comes weeks after reports emerged that Ranveer Singh had stepped away from the much-anticipated Don 3, a franchise that Akhtar has been developing for years. Neither the actor nor the filmmaker has issued an official statement about the exit, but the silence has only added to the speculation.

A trip down memory lane

Akhtar’s comment gained extra weight when he recalled an incident from his own directorial debut, Dil Chahta Hai. According to him, Saif Ali Khan had almost walked out of the film before shooting began. The revelation, made during a recent interaction, was shared as an example of how such situations are not new but have become more frequent.

“It seems to be a pattern nowadays,” Akhtar said, drawing a parallel between the past and present. The filmmaker did not elaborate on the reasons behind Ranveer’s exit, leaving room for interpretation.

Context and reactions

The Don franchise has been a cornerstone of Bollywood action cinema, with Amitabh Bachchan and later Shah Rukh Khan essaying the iconic role. Ranveer Singh was announced as the new Don in 2023, with production reportedly slated to begin soon. His reported departure has raised questions about the film’s future.

Fans have taken to social media to dissect Akhtar’s words, with many seeing it as a subtle jibe at Ranveer’s professionalism. Others have pointed out that Akhtar’s comment could be a broader critique of an industry trend where actors sign projects and later back out.

Neither Ranveer Singh nor his representatives have responded to Akhtar’s remarks. The actor, known for his energetic performances, has not publicly addressed the reasons for his exit.

Industry implications

The episode highlights a growing challenge for filmmakers in Bollywood: managing casting uncertainties in an era of packed schedules and multiple commitments. While Akhtar’s tone was light, his words reflect a frustration that many directors might share privately.

Observers note that the Don 3 production has faced several hurdles, from casting to scheduling. With the lead actor now out of the picture, the project’s timeline could be pushed further, though no official update has been provided.

Akhtar, who has also acted in films like Rock On and Zindagi Na Milegi Dobara, is known for his candidness. His latest comment, however, has turned the spotlight back on a controversy that seemed to have cooled off.

As the debate continues, one thing is clear: the Don 3 saga is far from over. Whether the film will find a new lead or undergo creative changes remains to be seen, but Akhtar’s words have ensured that the conversation stays alive.

For now, audiences will be watching closely for any official announcement from the makers, as the franchise’s legacy hangs in the balance.
