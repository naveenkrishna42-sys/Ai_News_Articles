Sensex drops 450 pts, Nifty slips below 24,600 as selling pressure mounts

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex fell over 450 points in morning trade
  • Nifty slipped below the 24,600 mark
  • Selling pressure was broad-based across sectors

Markets open lower, extend losses

Indian equity benchmarks opened in the red on Friday and extended losses through the morning session. The BSE Sensex dropped over 450 points, while the NSE Nifty slipped below the 24,600 level, reflecting weak sentiment across global and domestic cues.

The decline was broad-based, with losses in banking, IT, and auto stocks leading the fall. Market participants pointed to persistent foreign fund outflows and mixed global signals as key drags.

What is dragging the indices

Investors remained cautious ahead of upcoming economic data releases and central bank commentary. The absence of any major positive triggers has kept buying interest muted.

Volatility index rose marginally, indicating a rise in market nervousness. Analysts said the market is in a consolidation phase after recent gains, with traders trimming positions at higher levels.

Sectoral and stock movement

Most sectoral indices on the National Stock Exchange were trading lower. Financials and technology stocks witnessed significant selling pressure, while midcap and smallcap shares also declined.

Individual stock movements were largely driven by company-specific news and quarterly earnings. However, no single sector provided support, as the sell-off was uniform across the board.

Global cues and investor sentiment

Asian markets were trading mixed, offering little direction to domestic traders. Sentiment on Wall Street overnight was subdued, adding to the cautious mood.

Foreign institutional investors have been net sellers in recent sessions, while domestic institutional buying has partially offset the outflows. The rupee's movement against the dollar is also being closely watched.

What to watch next

Traders will now eye global inflation data and any fresh signals from the US Federal Reserve. On the domestic front, quarterly earnings from key companies and monsoon progress will influence market direction in the coming sessions.

The immediate support for Nifty is seen near recent lows, and a close below key levels could trigger further weakness. Analysts advise keeping a close watch on global cues and crude oil prices for near-term direction.
