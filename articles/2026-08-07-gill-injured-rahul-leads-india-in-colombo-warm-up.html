Gill Injured, Rahul Leads India in Colombo Warm-Up

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Shubman Gill is injured and misses the warm-up match in Colombo.
  • KL Rahul leads Team India as stand-in captain for the three-day tour game.
  • Sri Lanka XI won the toss and elected to bat first at the NCC ground.
  • The match serves as India's final tune-up before the Test series begins on August 15.

Gill Sidelined, Rahul Steps Up

India's preparations for the Test series against Sri Lanka hit an early hurdle on Friday as Shubman Gill was ruled out of the warm-up match due to injury. The right-handed batsman, a key figure in the middle order, was not part of the playing XI at the NCC ground in Colombo. Team management has not yet disclosed the nature or severity of the injury, leaving his availability for the first Test in doubt.

In Gill's absence, KL Rahul has been handed the captaincy for this three-day tour match. The Karnataka batsman, who has led India in the past, will use this opportunity to fine-tune his leadership and batting form. The team will hope Rahul's experience at the top of the order provides stability as they look to build momentum ahead of the series opener.

Sri Lanka XI Win Toss, Bat First

Sri Lanka XI won the toss and chose to bat first on a dry, grassless pitch at the NCC ground. The decision gives the Indian bowlers a chance to test their lines and lengths in match conditions, something head coach Gautam Gambhir emphasised as crucial ahead of the Test series. With the pitch expected to assist spinners as the match progresses, India's tweakers will be eager to get among the wickets.

The warm-up game is being treated as a serious dress rehearsal rather than a mere formality. Both teams are expected to treat it with competitive intensity, though the result will carry little weight. For India, the focus is squarely on execution—batting long, bowling disciplined spells, and fielding sharply.

Gambhir Confident Despite Questions

Ahead of the match, head coach Gautam Gambhir struck a confident tone when asked about the team's readiness. "Come 15th, we'll be ready with all the answers," he said, referring to the first Test. His remarks come amid broader scrutiny of India's recent Test performances, with critics pointing to a string of overseas losses and inconsistent batting displays.

The series is being framed as a chance for India to rediscover their lost glory in the longest format. Sri Lanka, playing at home, will not be pushovers, and the conditions in Colombo are expected to test India's patience and technique. The warm-up match is the last opportunity for the visitors to acclimatise and iron out any lingering issues before the real battle begins.

What to Watch in the Warm-Up

With Gill sidelined, the spotlight shifts to the other batsmen, particularly those vying for a spot in the final XI. Rahul's form will be closely monitored, as will the performance of the pace attack on a pitch that may offer little early movement. The team management will also be keen to see how the spinners adapt to the slow surface.

For Sri Lanka XI, this is a chance to test their own combinations and give younger players exposure against a strong Indian side. The hosts are expected to rotate their bowling options, giving everyone a decent workout.

As the three-day match progresses, all eyes will be on Gill's recovery timeline. The team physio is reportedly monitoring him closely, but no official update is expected until the end of the warm-up game. India will be hoping the injury is minor, as a fully fit Gill would be crucial to their plans in the Test series.

The first Test begins on August 15, and both teams will use the remaining days to finalise their strategies. For India, the warm-up is about more than just practice—it is about finding answers to the questions that have plagued their red-ball cricket in recent times.
