Engineer's Miraculous Survival

An Odisha engineer, identified as Mr. Pradip Kumar Sahu, was rescued from the floodwaters in Nepal after his wedding ring held onto his wrist. The 38-year-old had been swept away while trying to save his wife and two-year-old daughter from the rising waters. His survival is attributed to the ring, which kept his wrist from being completely submerged.

Family Missing in the Floods

Despite the engineer's rescue, his wife and two-year-old daughter remain missing. The family was evacuating their home in the Sindhupalchok district when the floods struck. The local authorities are racing against time to locate the missing individuals.

Nepal's Devastating Floods

Nepal is grappling with one of its worst disasters in recent history. The flood death toll has surpassed 900, with over 100,000 people displaced. The floods have caused widespread destruction, with entire villages submerged. The government is struggling to provide relief to the affected population.

China Accused of Concealing Flood Disaster Scale

Tibet campaigners have accused China of concealing the scale of the flood disaster in Tibet. They claim that the Chinese government is downplaying the severity of the floods to avoid international scrutiny. The campaigners are calling for transparency and an end to the concealment.

International Response and Relief Efforts

International organizations and governments are rallying to provide relief to Nepal. The United Nations, the Red Cross, and various countries are sending aid to the affected regions. However, the scale of the disaster is overwhelming, and the relief efforts are falling short.
