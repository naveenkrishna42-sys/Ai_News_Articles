Amitabh Bachchan recovering after surgery, calls it 'most difficult phase'

Amitabh Bachchan is recovering after undergoing a surgery that required a stay in the intensive care unit. The 83-year-old actor broke his silence on the hospitalisation through a blog post, describing it as the 'most difficult phase' he has faced physically.

News of Bachchan's surgery first emerged on Tuesday morning, sending ripples of concern across the entertainment industry and among his millions of fans. The actor's blog, which he regularly updates, carried a deeply personal account of his ordeal.

A candid account from the ICU

In his blog, Bachchan wrote about the experience without divulging specific medical details. He referred to the period as a 'homecoming is most difficult' phase, a line that struck a chord with many readers. The actor did not specify the nature of the surgery or the reason for the ICU admission.

Sources close to the family confirmed that the surgery was planned and that Bachchan is now stable. The actor's team has not released a detailed medical bulletin, but the blog post itself served as a reassurance that he is on the mend.

Fans and colleagues express concern

The blog post immediately triggered an outpouring of support on social media. Hashtags related to the actor's health began trending within hours on platforms like X (formerly Twitter) and Instagram. Fans shared messages of strength, while several Bollywood personalities sent private messages to the family.

This is not the first time Bachchan has faced a serious health scare. He was hospitalised in 2020 after testing positive for COVID-19, and in 2022 he underwent a procedure for a knee issue. However, the tone of his latest post was notably sombre, prompting many to check on his well-being.

What the blog revealed

The actor's blog has long been a window into his thoughts and daily life. In this entry, he wrote about the fragility of health and the toll the experience took on him. 'Most difficult phase physically,' he wrote, a rare admission of vulnerability from a figure known for his stoicism.

Hospital sources, speaking on condition of anonymity, indicated that the surgery was successful and that the ICU stay was a standard precaution for a patient of his age. They added that he is likely to be discharged in the coming days if his recovery continues as expected.

Amitabh Bachchan's family, including son Abhishek Bachchan and daughter-in-law Aishwarya Rai Bachchan, have been by his side. The family has requested privacy during this time.

The actor's fans will be watching for his next blog post, which could provide more details about his recovery. For now, the industry and his admirers are simply relieved that he is on the road to recuperation.
