Yash's Mother Defends Son's Stardom: 'Not A Terrorist, Son Of A Bus Driver'

Yash's Mother Responds to Allu Aravind's Remarks

In a surprising turn of events, the mother of Kannada film star Yash has hit back at producer Allu Aravind's comments about her son's rise to stardom. The debate, sparked by Aravind's remark 'Who was Yash before KGF?', has erupted into a lively discussion across the Indian film industry.

Allu Aravind's Comments

Aravind, during a recent interview, questioned the extent of Yash's stardom before his breakthrough performance in the blockbuster KGF series. He insinuated that Yash was not a known entity prior to these films, sparking a furore among Yash's fans and the Kannada film industry.

Yash's Mother Retaliates

Yash's mother, Janaki Gowda, has now responded to Aravind's comments. In a direct dig at the producer, she stated, 'He is not a terrorist. He is the son of a bus driver, and he was giving massive hits even before KGF.' Her retort has reignited the debate and drawn reactions from other industry insiders.

Industry Experts Weigh In

Filmmaker Ram Gopal Varma weighed in on the debate, comparing it to the rise of Prabhas with Baahubali. He dismissed the notion that audiences should only know an actor through one film, stating that it's laughable to debate Yash's stardom before KGF.
