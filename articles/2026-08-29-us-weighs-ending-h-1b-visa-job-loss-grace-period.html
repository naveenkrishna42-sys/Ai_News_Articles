US weighs ending H-1B visa job-loss grace period

📅 2026-08-29 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • US government considering ending 60-day grace period for H-1B visa holders
  • Change could force Indian workers to find new jobs immediately after layoffs
  • Potential impact on job security and economic stability for Indian professionals

Background: H-1B Visa and Grace Period

The H-1B visa is a non-immigrant work visa for temporary employment in the US, primarily used by Indian professionals. The current 60-day grace period allows visa holders to find new employment before their visa status expires. The US government is considering ending this grace period, which could have significant implications for Indian workers.

Proposed Changes

The proposed changes would eliminate the 60-day grace period, meaning H-1B visa holders would have to find new employment immediately after being laid off. This could force Indian workers to accept lower-paying jobs or return to their home countries.

Impact on Indian Workers

The change could have significant implications for Indian workers, many of whom rely on H-1B visas to support their families and contribute to the US economy. The loss of the grace period could lead to job insecurity and economic instability for these professionals.

Broader Implications

The proposed changes could have broader implications for the US economy and the global economy. The US relies on skilled foreign workers, including those on H-1B visas, to fill critical jobs. The loss of these workers could lead to job shortages and economic growth slowdown.

Conclusion

While the proposed changes could benefit some employers by reducing the cost of hiring and training new employees, they could have significant negative impacts on Indian workers and the US economy as a whole. It is important for policymakers to consider the potential consequences of these changes before implementing them.
