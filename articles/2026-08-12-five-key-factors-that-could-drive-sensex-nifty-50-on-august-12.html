Five Key Factors That Could Drive Sensex, Nifty 50 on August 12

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Gift Nifty suggests a tepid start for Indian equity benchmarks
  • Crude oil prices and global market trends will be closely tracked
  • Foreign institutional investor activity and rupee movement are key monitors

Indian equity benchmarks are likely to open on a cautious note on Wednesday, August 12, as investors weigh a host of domestic and global cues. Gift Nifty, which trades on the NSE International Exchange, hinted at a muted start for the Sensex and Nifty 50, signalling that the market may lack a clear directional bias in early trade.

With no major domestic macroeconomic data scheduled for the day, traders are expected to take cues from global markets, commodity prices, and the movement of foreign funds. Here are five key factors that could shape the trading session.

Gift Nifty Points to a Flat Open

Gift Nifty, the Singapore-listed derivative of Nifty 50, was trading around the neutral zone in early morning deals. This suggests that Dalal Street may open without any significant gap, either up or down.

Analysts said the lack of strong overnight triggers from global markets is keeping sentiment in check. A flat start could give way to stock-specific action, especially in sectors that have seen recent volatility.

Crude Oil Prices Remain a Key Monitorable

International crude oil prices are another factor that could influence sentiment. Any sharp movement in oil prices has a direct bearing on India's import bill and inflation outlook.

Brent crude futures were seen hovering near recent levels, but traders remain wary of supply-side disruptions. A sustained rise in oil prices could weigh on sectors such as aviation, paints, and auto, while a decline may offer some relief to these stocks.

Global Cues and US Futures

Asian markets were trading mixed in early deals, with some indices in the green and others in the red. US stock futures, too, were largely stable, indicating that overnight moves on Wall Street did not provide any strong direction.

Investors will also watch for any fresh commentary from the US Federal Reserve or new economic data releases that could alter the interest rate trajectory. Any surprise in global data could trigger a ripple effect across emerging markets, including India.

Foreign Fund Flows and Rupee Movement

The rupee's movement against the US dollar and the ongoing flow of foreign institutional investments will be closely tracked. In recent sessions, FIIs have shown a mixed stance, and their activity will be crucial in determining market direction.

A stronger rupee tends to attract foreign inflows, while a weaker one can prompt outflows. Domestic institutional investors, too, are expected to remain active, providing some cushion to the market.

Technical Levels and Stock-Specific Action

From a technical perspective, the Nifty 50 is expected to find support around its recent consolidation zone, while resistance is seen at higher levels. Traders are likely to keep an eye on these levels for intraday positioning.

Stock-specific news, including corporate announcements and sectoral developments, could also drive selective buying or selling. Banking, IT, and pharma stocks are expected to remain in focus.

As the session progresses, the market's direction will depend largely on how these factors evolve. A clear trend may emerge only after the first hour of trade, once investors get a better read on global sentiment.

Looking ahead, the next major trigger for the market will be the release of US inflation data later this week, which could set the tone for global risk appetite. Until then, volatility is likely to remain the norm.
