Introduction

In August 2026, several leading banks in India have introduced special fixed deposit (FD) rates to attract more depositors. Among these, DCB Bank, Indian Bank, Federal Bank, and Union Bank have emerged as the top contenders, offering higher returns than the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) benchmark rate.

Top Banks Offering Highest FD Rates

1. DCB Bank

💳 Compare Features & Eligibility

DCB Bank leads the pack with a special FD rate of 7.25% per annum. This rate is applicable for deposits ranging from ₹1 lakh to ₹2 crore. The bank offers this rate for a tenure of 12 months. The higher rate is expected to attract both new and existing customers looking for better returns on their savings.

2. Indian Bank

💳 Compare Features & Eligibility

Indian Bank follows closely with a special FD rate of 7.10% per annum. This rate is specifically designed for senior citizens, with a minimum deposit requirement of ₹50,000. The bank offers this rate for a tenure of 6 months, making it an attractive option for those looking for short-term investments.

3. Federal Bank

💳 Compare Features & Eligibility

Federal Bank offers a special FD rate of 7.00% per annum. This rate is applicable for deposits ranging from ₹1 lakh to ₹10 crore. The bank offers this rate for a tenure of 9 months. Federal Bank's higher rate is likely to appeal to customers seeking a balance between investment period and returns.

4. Union Bank

💳 Compare Features & Eligibility

Union Bank also offers a special FD rate of 7.00% per annum. This rate is applicable for deposits ranging from ₹1 lakh to ₹5 crore. The bank offers this rate for a tenure of 12 months. Union Bank's higher rate is expected to attract customers looking for a longer investment period.

Key Insights

  • Higher Returns: The special FD rates offered by these banks are higher than the RBI's current benchmark rate of 6.75% per annum, making them attractive options for depositors.
  • Variety of Tenures: The banks offer a range of tenures, from 6 months to 12 months, catering to different investment needs and preferences.
  • Minimum Deposit Requirements: The minimum deposit amounts vary from ₹50,000 to ₹1 lakh, ensuring that a wide range of customers can benefit from these offers.
  • Targeted Offers: Indian Bank's special FD rate is specifically designed for senior citizens, indicating a targeted approach to attract a specific customer segment.

Conclusion

In August 2026, DCB Bank, Indian Bank, Federal Bank, and Union Bank are leading the way with their special FD rates. These higher returns, coupled with varying tenures and minimum deposit requirements, make them attractive options for depositors looking to maximize their savings. As always, customers should carefully evaluate their financial goals and risk tolerance before choosing a bank and a tenure for their fixed deposits.
