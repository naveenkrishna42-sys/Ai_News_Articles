NIA Raids Multiple Locations in J&K Over Terror Conspiracy

📅 2026-08-22 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • NIA teams conducted simultaneous raids at 10 locations across Kashmir.
  • The operation focuses on dismantling a network linked to Pakistan-based LeT handlers.
  • Searches covered five districts, including specific locations in Anantnag.

Coordinated Crackdown Across Districts

The National Investigation Agency (NIA) launched a series of simultaneous raids across Jammu and Kashmir on Saturday morning. The operation targeted individuals and networks suspected of involvement in a cross-border terror conspiracy.

Teams fanned out across five districts to execute the search warrants. These coordinated efforts aim to disrupt the local logistics and communication channels allegedly utilized by militant groups operating from across the border.

Focus on Pakistan-Based Handlers

Preliminary reports indicate that the investigation centers on the activities of Pakistan-based handlers affiliated with the Lashkar-e-Taiba (LeT). Officials are examining evidence to determine how these handlers influence and support local terror networks within the Union Territory.

The search operations included at least two specific locations in the Anantnag district. Agency personnel focused on gathering digital evidence, financial documents, and communication devices that could link local operatives to their handlers abroad.

Evidence Collection and Seizures

The NIA teams spent several hours at each site, securing the premises while conducting detailed inspections. While the agency has not yet disclosed a full inventory of seized items, investigators typically prioritize data found on mobile phones and laptops during such raids.

  • Searches were executed in five distinct districts.
  • At least 10 locations were subjected to intensive scrutiny.
  • Digital and physical evidence is being cataloged for forensic analysis.

Local law enforcement provided security assistance to the central agency during the searches. No immediate arrests were announced as the operation remains in its evidence-gathering phase.

Probing the Terror Ecosystem

This tactical move is part of an ongoing effort to dismantle the financial and operational support systems that facilitate militant activity in the region. By targeting these specific links, the NIA intends to map out the hierarchy of handlers directing operations from overseas.

Investigators are now cross-referencing the information recovered during these raids with existing intelligence dossiers. This process will determine the next steps in the legal proceedings against the suspects identified during the investigation.

The NIA will likely analyze the seized electronic devices and documents over the coming week to identify potential links for further questioning.
