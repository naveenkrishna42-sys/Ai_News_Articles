Stock Market Mid-Day: Sensex, Nifty Near Flat as IT Stocks Offset Hormuz Tension

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty recovered from early losses to trade near flat by midday.
  • IT stocks led the recovery, offsetting selling pressure from rising Hormuz tensions.
  • Investors remain cautious as geopolitical risks weigh on broader market sentiment.

Markets Recover from Weak Start

Indian benchmark indices staged a smart recovery from early losses on Monday, with the Sensex and Nifty trading nearly flat at midday. The markets opened lower amid rising geopolitical tensions in the Hormuz region, but buying interest in IT stocks helped pare most of the losses.

At the time of writing, the BSE Sensex was trading around 78,450, down just 0.1% from its previous close, while the NSE Nifty hovered near 23,850. Both indices had opened over 0.5% lower earlier in the day.

IT Stocks Lead the Recovery

Information technology stocks emerged as the biggest gainers, providing crucial support to the benchmarks. Major IT firms including Infosys, TCS, and HCL Technologies were trading in positive territory, with gains ranging from 0.5% to 1.2%.

Analysts attribute the resilience in IT stocks to strong global demand outlook and expectations of healthy quarterly earnings. The sector's performance helped offset selling pressure from banking and energy stocks.

Banking and Energy Stocks Under Pressure

Banking stocks remained under pressure, with the Nifty Bank index down about 0.4%. Private sector lenders such as HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank were trading lower, while PSU banks also saw mild declines.

Energy stocks were among the worst hit, with Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC) and Reliance Industries trading in the red. The rising tensions in the Hormuz region, a key oil transit route, have raised concerns about potential supply disruptions and higher crude prices.

Investors Eye Geopolitical Developments

Market participants are closely monitoring the situation in the Hormuz region, where geopolitical tensions have escalated in recent days. Any further escalation could impact global oil prices and, consequently, inflation expectations in India, a major crude importer.

Despite the near-flat trading, volatility is expected to remain elevated as traders adjust positions ahead of key domestic and global economic data releases later this week.

Outlook for the Remainder of the Session

The market is likely to consolidate in a narrow range for the rest of the trading day, with the direction hinging on any fresh developments on the geopolitical front. The IT sector's performance will be closely watched for cues on whether the recovery can sustain through the close.

Investors are advised to remain cautious and avoid aggressive bets until the situation in the Hormuz region becomes clearer.
