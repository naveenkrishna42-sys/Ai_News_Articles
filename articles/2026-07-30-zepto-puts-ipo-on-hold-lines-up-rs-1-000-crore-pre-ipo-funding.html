Zepto puts IPO on hold, lines up Rs 1,000 crore pre-IPO funding

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Zepto has paused its planned initial public offering indefinitely.
  • The company is likely raising over Rs 1,000 crore in pre-IPO funding from existing backers.
  • The pre-IPO round values Zepto at approximately $4.5 billion, lower than earlier expectations.

Quick-commerce platform Zepto has put its much-anticipated initial public offering on hold. The Mumbai-based company is now in advanced talks to raise over Rs 1,000 crore in a pre-IPO funding round from its existing investors, according to multiple sources familiar with the matter.

The decision marks a sharp reversal for the company, which had been preparing for a public listing later this year. Industry watchers say the move reflects shifting market sentiment and pressure from mutual funds that held stakes in the firm.

Why the IPO got shelved

Sources told Moneycontrol that Zepto's board and lead investors concluded that current public market conditions were not favourable for a quick-commerce IPO. The company was reportedly facing pushback on valuation expectations from anchor investors.

Mutual funds that own Zepto shares were instrumental in pressing for a delay. A report from Value Research noted that the funds effectively vetoed the IPO timeline, arguing that the company needed to demonstrate stronger unit economics before going public.

Pre-IPO round details

The pre-IPO funding is expected to be led by existing backers, including top venture capital firms and family offices. The round values Zepto at roughly $4.5 billion, a slight discount compared to its last private valuation of around $5 billion.

While the company has not confirmed the exact amount, multiple reports peg the raise at over Rs 1,000 crore. The funds will be used to strengthen Zepto's balance sheet and expand its dark store network across Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities.

Festive season plans unaffected

Despite the IPO pause, Zepto's operations continue at full steam. The company has launched premium festive season offerings, with higher-priced gift hampers and electronics categories seeing strong early demand.

“The festive cart is going premium this year,” an Economic Times report noted, adding that the company is betting on higher average order values to improve margins. Zepto's delivery network remains operational across 15 cities.

What this means for quick-commerce IPOs

Zepto's deferral comes at a time when investor scrutiny of quick-commerce firms has intensified. The sector, which boomed during the pandemic, is now under pressure to prove sustained profitability. Rival players like Blinkit and Swiggy's Instamart are also watching developments closely.

BusinessLine reported that Zepto may also cut the size of its eventual IPO and lower valuation expectations as anchor investor talks continue. The company has not set a new timeline for the public listing.

For now, Zepto's focus remains on operational metrics. The company is targeting EBITDA breakeven by the end of the current financial year, a goal it says is on track.

What happens next depends on how quickly the market window reopens. If Zepto meets its profitability targets in the coming quarters, the IPO could be revived as early as mid-2027. Until then, the company will rely on private capital to fuel its growth.
