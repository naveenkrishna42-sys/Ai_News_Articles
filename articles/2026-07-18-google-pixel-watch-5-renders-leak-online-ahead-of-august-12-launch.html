Google Pixel Watch 5 Renders Leak Online Ahead of August 12 Launch

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pixel Watch 5 renders show four color variants — Matte Black, Polished Silver, Champagne Gold, and a new Mint Green.
  • Design remains largely unchanged from the Pixel Watch 4, retaining the domed display and rotating crown.
  • Leaked pricing suggests a $50 increase across all models, with the base Wi-Fi version starting at $399.

Pixel Watch 5 Design Leaks in New Renders

Fresh renders of the Google Pixel Watch 5 have leaked online, giving the clearest look yet at the upcoming smartwatch. The images, published by Concept Phones and other outlets, show a device that sticks closely to the design language of its predecessor.

The Pixel Watch 5 appears in four color options: Matte Black, Polished Silver, Champagne Gold, and a new Mint Green shade. The renders suggest Google is not changing the overall form factor. The domed display and rotating crown remain. The band attachment system also looks identical to the Pixel Watch 4.

What the Leaks Reveal

Droid Life and TechRepublic have also shared these renders, reinforcing the idea that the August launch is close. The leaked images show the watch from multiple angles. The display appears to have slim bezels. The crown is prominently placed on the right side.

Notebookcheck has reported that the Pixel Watch 5 will be more expensive across the board. The base Wi-Fi model is expected to start at $399, up from $349 for the Pixel Watch 4. The LTE variant could go for $499, a $50 jump from its predecessor.

Price Hike and Market Positioning

The $50 price bump has been a key talking point in the leaks. Gagadget.com noted that the increase applies to all models. Google has not officially confirmed pricing. But multiple sources point to the same figure.

This puts the Pixel Watch 5 in direct competition with the Apple Watch Series 10 and Samsung Galaxy Watch 8. Google is betting on its Wear OS integration and Fitbit features to justify the higher price. The Pixel Watch 4 received mixed reviews for battery life, and it remains to be seen if Google has addressed that in the new model.

Launch Timeline and Expectations

Google has not confirmed an August 12 launch date. But leaks from Concept Phones and others have consistently pointed to that window. The company is expected to unveil the Pixel Watch 5 alongside the Pixel 10 series later this year.

Details on internal specs remain scarce. Reports suggest the watch will use the Snapdragon W5+ Gen 2 chip. Battery capacity and display resolution are still unconfirmed. Google is likely to improve health tracking sensors, given the price increase.

What to watch: Google is expected to officially announce the Pixel Watch 5 in the coming weeks. If the August 12 date holds, pre-orders could open soon after. The price hike will test whether users see enough value in the upgrades to pay more.
