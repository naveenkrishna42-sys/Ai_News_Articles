Apple Watch Ultra 3 vs Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2: Key Differences

📅 2026-08-26 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple Watch Ultra 3 offers advanced health monitoring features
  • Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 has a larger display and longer battery life
  • Both devices support advanced fitness tracking and health coaching

Apple Watch Ultra 3: A Closer Look at the Features

The Apple Watch Ultra 3 continues to set the standard for advanced health monitoring. With features like ECG, blood oxygen monitoring, and temperature sensing, it's a comprehensive tool for health-conscious users. The device also includes a new sleep apnea detection feature, which is a significant upgrade for those with sleep-related health concerns.

Performance and Battery Life

Apple's latest model boasts an improved processor and longer battery life. The watch can last up to 36 hours with typical use, which is a notable improvement over previous models. This makes it a reliable companion for both fitness enthusiasts and health monitoring users.

Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2: What's New

The Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 is designed with durability in mind, featuring a rugged design and increased water resistance. It also includes a larger display, making it more comfortable to use for extended periods. The watch supports advanced fitness tracking, including running dynamics and cycling metrics.

Health and Fitness Features

Samsung's latest model includes a new health coaching feature, which provides personalized advice based on user data. The watch also offers a range of fitness tracking options, including heart rate monitoring and sleep tracking. The battery life is impressive, with the watch lasting up to 48 hours with typical use.

Comparative Analysis

Both the Apple Watch Ultra 3 and the Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 offer advanced health monitoring features. However, the Apple Watch Ultra 3 has a slight edge in terms of battery life and the range of health features it offers. The Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2, on the other hand, has a larger display and longer battery life, making it a more comfortable and durable option for extended use.

Conclusion

Both the Apple Watch Ultra 3 and the Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 are excellent choices for health and fitness tracking. The choice between the two will depend on individual preferences and needs. If you prioritize a wide range of health features and a slightly longer battery life, the Apple Watch Ultra 3 might be the better option. If durability, a larger display, and extended battery life are more important, the Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 could be the preferred choice.
