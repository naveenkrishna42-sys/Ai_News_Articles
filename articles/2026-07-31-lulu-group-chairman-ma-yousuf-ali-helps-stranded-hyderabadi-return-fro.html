Lulu Group Chairman MA Yousuf Ali helps stranded Hyderabadi return from Saudi Arabia

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hyderabadi man stranded in Saudi Arabia for months due to visa and financial issues
  • Lulu Group chairman MA Yousuf Ali intervened after receiving a plea for help
  • Man has been reunited with his family in Hyderabad; details of his identity not disclosed

Timely intervention

In a humanitarian gesture that underscores the reach of community networks, Lulu Group chairman MA Yousuf Ali has helped a stranded Hyderabadi man return home from Saudi Arabia. The man, whose identity has not been officially disclosed, had been stuck in the Kingdom for several months due to visa complications and a lack of funds for travel.

According to sources familiar with the matter, the man had approached local contacts in Saudi Arabia seeking assistance. His plight came to the attention of Yousuf Ali, who is known for his philanthropic work across Kerala and the Gulf region. The chairman reportedly used his influence and resources to facilitate the man's travel arrangements, ensuring his safe return to Hyderabad.

A long ordeal ends

The Hyderabadi man's ordeal began when his employment visa expired, leaving him without valid documentation or income. Unable to afford airfare or legal fees, he remained in limbo in Saudi Arabia, separated from his family back home. The intervention by Yousuf Ali, who has business interests across the Gulf, helped cut through bureaucratic hurdles and arrange for the necessary travel documents.

Telangana Today, which first reported the development, noted that the man has since been reunited with his family in Hyderabad. The emotional reunion brings closure to a distressing chapter, though the man's name and the exact duration of his stay in Saudi Arabia have not been revealed.

Philanthropy with a personal touch

This is not the first time Yousuf Ali has stepped in to help Indians stranded abroad. The Lulu Group, a multinational retail giant headquartered in Abu Dhabi, has a long history of assisting expatriates in distress, particularly those from southern India. In past instances, the chairman has personally overseen repatriation efforts, often covering costs from his own pocket.

While the specifics of this case remain private, the outcome highlights the importance of community leaders who can act quickly in emergencies. For the family in Hyderabad, the wait is finally over, and the gratitude is palpable.

Broader implications

Incidents of Indians being stranded in Gulf countries are not uncommon, with many facing visa overstay penalties and financial hardship.
