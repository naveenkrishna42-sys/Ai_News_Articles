Ramayana global release set; India premiere delayed by 2 days

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ramayana will release internationally first, with India premiere two days later.
  • Producer Namit Malhotra explains the staggered release strategy.
  • Film eyes over 59,000 screens globally, surpassing major blockbusters.
  • Trailer has already crossed 1 billion views across platforms.

Mumbai: The much-anticipated epic 'Ramayana', starring Ranbir Kapoor and Sai Pallavi, is set for a staggered global release, with international audiences getting the first look two days before India. Producer Namit Malhotra has confirmed the film will debut overseas in November, while Indian cinemas will screen it from a slightly later date.

The decision marks a rare reversal of the conventional Bollywood release pattern, where domestic premiers typically lead. Malhotra, speaking to the media, explained that the move is driven by the film's unprecedented international scale and the need to synchronise with global marketing timelines.

Why the two-day gap?

According to Malhotra, the staggered release is a logistical necessity rather than a creative choice. With the film slated to open on over 59,000 screens worldwide—a number that surpasses the combined reach of 'Avatar: The Way of Water' and 'RRR'—coordinating prints, translations, and promotional events across time zones requires a phased rollout.

"We want the world to witness this together, but the sheer scale means we have to be practical," Malhotra was quoted as saying. The two-day window allows international markets to stabilise before the Indian premiere, which is expected to be the largest single-market release in the film's roster.

A billion-view trailer

The film's trailer has already set records, amassing over 1 billion views across all platforms and languages. This includes the English version, which features the voice of Ravana by Mohan Kapur, known to Marvel fans as a familiar face. The response has been unprecedented for an Indian mythological epic, signalling strong pre-release buzz both domestically and in key overseas territories like North America, the UK, and the Gulf.

Industry analysts note that the global-first strategy could redefine how Indian spectacles are marketed. By letting international audiences weigh in first, the producers are banking on positive word-of-mouth to amplify the Indian release, a tactic more common in Hollywood franchise launches.

Logistics of a mega release

The 59,000-screen figure, if realised, would place 'Ramayana' among the widest releases in cinema history. This includes IMAX and other premium large-format screens, with the film shot to leverage high-end visual effects. The production, backed by a reported lavish budget, has been in the making for years, and the release plan reflects the producers' confidence in its pan-global appeal.

However, the two-day delay in India has sparked debate among exhibitors. Some domestic theatre chains have expressed concerns about potential piracy in the gap period, though the producers have assured that robust security measures are in place. Malhotra did not comment on specific security protocols but emphasised that the staggered date would not diminish the Indian premiere's grandeur.

What to watch next

As November approaches, all eyes will be on the first international screenings and the critical reception they generate. The Indian release, two days later, is expected to be a nationwide event, with advance bookings likely to open soon. Whether the strategy pays off at the box office will be closely watched by an industry that has rarely seen such a bold release pattern.
