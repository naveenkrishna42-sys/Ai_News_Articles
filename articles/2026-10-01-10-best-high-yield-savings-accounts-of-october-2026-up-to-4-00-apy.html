Overview of High-Yield Savings in 2026

Interest rates on deposit products have risen steadily since 2024, pushing the best online savings yields toward the 4.00% APY mark. These accounts remain FDIC‑insured, offer daily compounding, and provide liquidity comparable to traditional savings while delivering markedly higher returns.

Methodology: How We Ranked the Accounts

We evaluated each product on five core criteria: advertised APY, monthly or annual fees, minimum balance to earn the rate, ease of access (mobile app and ATM network), and eligibility requirements. Only accounts with transparent fee schedules and no hidden charges were considered for the final list.

1. Ally Bank Online Savings Account

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Ally offers a 3.85% APY with no monthly maintenance fee and a $0 minimum balance to open. Interest compounds daily, and the account is accessible via a highly rated mobile app. Eligibility requires a U.S. address and a valid Social Security number.

2. Marcus by Goldman Sachs High-Yield Online Savings

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Marcus provides a steady 3.90% APY, no fees, and no minimum balance to earn the rate. The platform emphasizes simplicity, with no ATM card but easy electronic transfers. Eligibility is open to U.S. residents aged 18+ with a valid ID.

3. Synchrony Bank High Yield Savings

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Synchrony advertises a 3.95% APY, waives monthly fees, and requires only a $0.01 balance to start earning interest. Customers receive an ATM card for limited cash access. Eligibility mirrors standard U.S. banking rules.

4. Discover Online Savings Account

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Discover’s account yields 3.80% APY, carries no monthly fees, and has no minimum balance requirement. It includes a cash‑back debit card option for purchases, though the primary focus remains savings. Eligibility requires a U.S. mailing address and SSN.

5. Capital One 360 Performance Savings

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Capital One offers a 3.78% APY, no monthly fees, and a $0 minimum to open. The account integrates with the Capital One 360 checking suite for seamless transfers. Eligibility is open to U.S. residents with a valid ID.

6. American Express® High Yield Savings Account

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

American Express provides a 3.85% APY, no fees, and no minimum balance. The account is known for strong customer service and easy online management. Eligibility requires a U.S. residential address and SSN.

7. Barclays Online Savings

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Barclays advertises a 3.80% APY, waives monthly fees, and requires no minimum balance to earn the rate. The platform supports external transfers and mobile check deposit. Eligibility is limited to U.S. citizens or resident aliens with a valid SSN.

8. CIT Bank Savings Connect

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

CIT Bank offers a competitive 3.92% APY, no monthly fees, and a $100 minimum balance to open. Interest compounds daily, and the account includes a free ATM network access. Eligibility requires a U.S. address and SSN.

9. Vio Bank High Yield Online Savings

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

Vio Bank provides a 3.96% APY, no monthly charges, and a $0.01 minimum balance to start earning. The bank emphasizes a digital‑only experience with robust mobile features. Eligibility follows standard U.S. banking requirements.

  • Pros: APYs significantly exceed traditional savings, often with zero fees and FDIC protection.
  • Pros: Daily compounding and easy online access improve real‑world earnings.
  • Cons: Some accounts impose minimum balances to avoid fees or to earn the advertised rate.
  • Cons: Limited physical branch access may deter customers who prefer in‑person service.
  • Cons: Rates are variable and can decline if the Federal Reserve eases monetary policy.
