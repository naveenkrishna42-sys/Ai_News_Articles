Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 breaks pre-order records in Europe

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung's Galaxy Z series pre-orders surpass targets in Europe.
  • Galaxy Z Fold8 receives record recognition ahead of UK launch.
  • Analysts note foldables are becoming mainstream, not niche.

Samsung has exceeded its pre-order targets for the new Galaxy Z Fold8 and Z Flip8 across Europe, according to company statements. The milestone comes just days before the devices hit UK shelves, with the tech giant calling the response “unprecedented” in a release.

The company had projected strong sales given the buzz around the “Future of Foldables” campaign, but actual numbers have outpaced internal forecasts. Samsung did not disclose exact figures, but industry watchers say the surge indicates foldables have moved beyond early adopters.

Record recognition for the Fold8

The Galaxy Z Fold8 has also picked up early accolades, with Samsung noting it has earned “record recognition” ahead of the UK launch. Reviews from tech media, including a recent iDiva piece calling it “a whole new smartphone experience,” have helped drive consumer interest.

Samsung is also sweetening the deal for early buyers. In a limited-time offer, US customers can pre-order the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra and get $350 in Amazon credit, a move analysts say is designed to push ultra-premium models.

Foldables hit mainstream stride

Investing.com reported that Samsung says the new foldables are set to break pre-order records, a claim that aligns with the European numbers. The company has been refining hinge mechanisms and display durability, addressing past complaints about creases and fragility.

European carriers and retailers have reportedly increased stock allocations to meet demand. Samsung’s own online store shows extended delivery dates for several colourways, a sign that initial inventory is moving fast.

What this means for the market

The strong European performance is a bright spot for Samsung, which faces stiff competition from Apple and Chinese rivals like Xiaomi and Oppo in the region. Foldables remain a differentiator, and the company is betting that the Z series can sustain momentum through the holiday season.

Analysts caution that pre-orders are just the first test. The real challenge lies in post-launch sales, where high prices often dampen uptake. The Z Fold8 starts at a premium price point, but Samsung’s trade-in and financing schemes appear to be easing that burden.

UK buyers will get their first hands-on experience later this week, and early retail checks suggest strong walk-in interest. Samsung has also ramped up marketing spend, with billboards and digital ads highlighting the Fold8’s multitasking features and camera upgrades.

No official sales figures have been released yet, but Samsung has promised a detailed update in its next earnings call. For now, the company is basking in the glow of a successful pre-order phase, with the European market leading the charge.

All eyes will now be on the UK launch to see if the momentum holds. If the pre-order trend continues, Samsung could be looking at its best foldable cycle yet, cementing the category’s place in the premium smartphone segment.
