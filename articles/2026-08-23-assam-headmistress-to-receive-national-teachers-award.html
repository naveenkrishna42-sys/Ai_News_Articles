President Murmu to Honour 48 Teachers

President Droupadi Murmu is set to confer the National Teachers Award 2026 on September 5, 2026. The award recognises outstanding contributions to education and is given to 48 teachers across India. Among the recipients is a headmistress from Assam, who will be honoured for her exceptional work in the field.

Assam Headmistress Among Winners

The headmistress from Assam has been selected for the prestigious award, joining other educators from various states. Her contributions to education have been noted for their significant impact on students and the community. The award ceremony will be a significant event, celebrating the hard work and dedication of these teachers.

Background of the National Teachers Award

The National Teachers Award was established to acknowledge and reward teachers who have made remarkable achievements in their respective fields. The award is part of the government's efforts to honour educators who go above and beyond in their roles. Previous winners have included teachers from Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, who have been recognised for their innovative teaching methods and community engagement.

Significance of the Award Ceremony

The award ceremony on September 5, 2026, will be a momentous occasion, bringing together educators from across the country. It will serve as a platform to celebrate the efforts of teachers and to inspire others to pursue excellence in education. The event is expected to draw significant attention and media coverage, highlighting the importance of teaching in society.
