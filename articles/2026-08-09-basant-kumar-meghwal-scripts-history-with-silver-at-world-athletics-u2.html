Basant Kumar Meghwal scripts history with silver at World Athletics U20 Championships

📅 2026-08-09 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Basant Kumar Meghwal wins silver in high jump, first Indian to medal in the event at U20 Worlds
  • Shahnavaz Khan bags bronze in long jump, helping India equal best-ever U20 medal tally
  • Historic night for Indian athletics in Eugene as both jumpers shine on global stage

Eugene, Oregon: In a night that will be remembered in Indian athletics for years, Basant Kumar Meghwal soared to a silver medal in the high jump at the World Athletics U20 Championships. The 19-year-old from Rajasthan became the first Indian ever to win a medal in the high jump at this level, etching his name in the record books.

Meghwal's achievement was complemented by Shahnavaz Khan, who clinched a bronze in the long jump on the same day. Together, the two jumpers powered India to an equal-best-ever medal tally at the U20 World Championships, matching the nation's previous best haul.

A leap into the history books

Competing against the world's best young athletes, Meghwal displayed remarkable composure. His series of jumps kept him in contention throughout, and when the dust settled, the silver was his. The feat is particularly significant because no Indian high jumper had previously managed to finish on the podium at this prestigious meet.

For Meghwal, the journey has been one of relentless hard work. Hailing from a modest background, he has trained with limited resources but boundless determination. His performance in Eugene is a testament to the growing depth of Indian athletics beyond the traditional sprint and distance events.

Shahnavaz Khan adds to the glory

Not to be outdone, Shahnavaz Khan delivered a season-best leap in the long jump to secure the bronze. His effort added another medal to India's kitty and underlined the country's rising prowess in field events.

The duo's success is being hailed as a watershed moment for Indian athletics. With both athletes still in their teens, their potential for future growth is immense.

India's best-ever U20 showing

India's tally at the 2026 edition now matches its best-ever performance at the U20 World Championships. The previous record was set in 2018 when Indian athletes won two medals. This time, Meghwal's silver and Khan's bronze have replicated that feat, with the possibility of more medals still alive in other events.

The Athletics Federation of India (AFI) is yet to issue an official statement, but the mood in the Indian camp is understandably upbeat. Coaches and officials have lauded the athletes' discipline and focus.

What this means for Indian athletics

The medals in Eugene signal a shift in India's athletics trajectory. While the country has traditionally relied on a few star names, the emergence of young jumpers suggests a broader talent pool is being nurtured. With the next Olympics just two years away, these performances will give selectors plenty to ponder.

Meghwal and Khan are expected to return to India to a hero's welcome. Their achievements will likely inspire a new generation of jumpers back home.

As the championships continue, Indian fans will be watching closely to see if the medal tally can go one better. For now, the focus remains on celebrating a historic night in Eugene—one that has put Indian field events firmly on the global map.
