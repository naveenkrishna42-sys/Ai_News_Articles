RBI keeps repo rate unchanged at 5.25%, retains growth forecast

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • RBI MPC keeps policy repo rate unchanged at 5.25% for the current fiscal.
  • GDP growth projected at 6.7% for FY27, with inflation seen moderating.
  • Markets open higher on falling crude oil prices ahead of the policy announcement.

The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) on Wednesday chose to keep the policy repo rate unchanged at 5.25 per cent, marking a continuation of its pause stance. The decision, announced after a three-day review, aligns with market expectations and comes amid signs of cooling inflation and resilient economic activity.

The central bank has projected GDP growth at 6.7 per cent for the current fiscal year, a figure that suggests policymakers see enough momentum in the economy to hold rates steady without stoking price pressures. The tone of the policy statement was widely interpreted as neutral, with no explicit hint of an imminent hike or cut.

Growth and inflation in balance

The MPC's growth projection of 6.7 per cent for FY27 is slightly lower than the earlier estimate of 6.8 per cent, reflecting a cautious outlook amid global headwinds. However, the central bank noted that domestic demand remains supportive, with private consumption and investment showing resilience.

On the inflation front, the RBI expects consumer price index (CPI) inflation to moderate further, aided by easing food prices and a favourable base effect. The pause in rate action suggests that the MPC is comfortable with the current trajectory, even as it remains vigilant about supply-side shocks.

What the pause means for borrowers and savers

For salaried employees and households with home loans, the unchanged repo rate means equated monthly instalments (EMIs) on floating-rate loans will not change immediately. Banks typically transmit policy rate changes to lending rates with a lag, so borrowers may not see any immediate relief or burden.

For fixed deposit (FD) investors, the pause could mean that deposit rates remain at current levels for a while. Some banks had already started trimming deposit rates in recent months, and the status quo may accelerate that trend, potentially reducing returns for savers.

Inflation expectations among households have been easing, but the RBI has cautioned against assuming that the pause signals a peak in rates. The central bank's stance remains focused on aligning inflation with its target of 4 per cent on a durable basis.

Markets react positively

Equity markets opened higher on Wednesday, buoyed by falling crude oil prices and the anticipation of the RBI's decision. Benchmark indices gained in early trade, with rate-sensitive sectors such as banking and auto seeing buying interest.

The decline in global crude prices has been a key factor in easing India's import bill and domestic inflation pressures. Brent crude has softened in recent weeks, providing some cushion to the economy amid global uncertainties.

Analysts noted that the RBI's decision to hold rates, coupled with a positive growth outlook, was well received by investors. However, they also pointed out that the central bank's commentary on liquidity and currency stability would be crucial in the coming months.

No hike signal, but watchful stance

The policy statement did not carry any hawkish signal, suggesting that the MPC is not considering a rate hike in the near term. At the same time, the central bank has kept its stance flexible, leaving room for action if inflation or external conditions warrant a change.

Economists believe that the RBI may remain on hold for the rest of the fiscal year, unless there is a sharp spike in food or energy prices. The focus now shifts to the government's fiscal position and the trajectory of global interest rates, particularly the US Federal Reserve's policy path.

The next MPC meeting is scheduled for October, and markets will watch for any change in the growth-inflation balance. For now, the central bank appears to be in a wait-and-watch mode, prioritising stability over aggressive action.
