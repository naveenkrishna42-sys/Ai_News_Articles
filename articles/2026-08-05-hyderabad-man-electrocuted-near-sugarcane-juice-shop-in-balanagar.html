Hyderabad: Man electrocuted near sugarcane juice shop in Balanagar

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Incident occurred near a sugarcane juice shop in Balanagar, Hyderabad.
  • The victim was electrocuted under circumstances that are being investigated.
  • Local police have registered a case and are looking into the cause.

Hyderabad: A man died after being electrocuted near a sugarcane juice shop in the Balanagar area on Tuesday. The incident has left the local community shaken, with residents demanding a thorough probe into the safety of electrical installations in the area.

What happened

The victim, whose identity has not yet been officially disclosed, was near the juice shop when he came into contact with a live electrical wire. Passers-by rushed him to a nearby hospital, but he was declared brought dead on arrival.

Police from the Balanagar station have registered a case and are investigating the exact sequence of events. Preliminary inquiries suggest a possible short circuit or exposed wiring, though officials have not confirmed the root cause.

Area in shock

The spot, a busy commercial stretch, saw a crowd gather soon after the incident. Some local shopkeepers said they had earlier noticed loose wires hanging near the shop but did not report them, fearing action from authorities. "We never thought it would turn fatal," one shopkeeper told reporters.

Electricity department officials visited the site later in the day to assess the situation. A senior official said a technical team has been deployed to check nearby connections and ensure no other hazards remain.

Police investigation underway

The police have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, though charges may be added after the post-mortem report and technical analysis are completed. The body has been shifted to a government hospital for autopsy.

Authorities are also examining whether the juice shop owner or any contractor was negligent in maintaining electrical equipment. "We are looking at all angles," a police officer said, declining to elaborate.

Safety concerns raised

Residents and local traders have raised concerns about the poor condition of electrical infrastructure in Balanagar. Similar incidents have been reported in other parts of Hyderabad in recent years, prompting calls for regular safety audits.

Municipal and electricity officials have promised a review of all vulnerable points in the area. "We will conduct a drive to identify and fix exposed wires and faulty connections," an official said.

The victim's family has been informed, and police are awaiting formal identification. No official statement has been issued by the family or the electricity department so far.

As the investigation continues, locals hope that this tragedy will lead to concrete action, not just promises. The focus now shifts to the post-mortem report and the police's final findings, which are expected in the coming days.
