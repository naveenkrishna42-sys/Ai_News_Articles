Why the Benefit Stack Beats the Premium Quote

Most buyers rank term plans on price alone, which is the weakest filter available. A ₹1 crore cover for a 30-year-old non-smoker can vary sharply across insurers, while the meaningful gaps sit in claim behaviour, rider design and payout structure. IRDAI's annual report publishes claim settlement ratios for every insurer; read the individual segment figure, not the group business.

1. Adequate Sum Assured, Not the Cheapest One

Cover must replace income, not merely clear a home loan. A working rule is 10-15 times annual income plus outstanding liabilities, minus existing assets. Under-covering is the most frequent and costliest error in term buying.

2. Claim Settlement Ratio and Solvency Margin

Prefer insurers with a sustained individual claim settlement ratio above 95% and a solvency margin comfortably above the regulatory floor. Three consistent years matter more than one strong year, and grievance volumes published by the regulator are worth a separate look.

3. Critical Illness and Disability Riders

Riders convert a death-only contract into a living-benefit plan. Price each rider separately, since critical illness covers add a visible share to base premium and carry their own disease definitions, survival clauses and waiting periods.

4. Waiver of Premium

💳 Apply for Lifetime Free Card (Instant Approval)

On permanent disability or a specified illness, future premiums stand waived while the cover runs. It is inexpensive when added young and costly or unavailable later, so treat it as a core benefit rather than an optional extra.

5. Payout Flexibility

Choose between lump sum, staggered monthly income, or a blend. For families without strong investment discipline, an income stream usually protects better than a single cheque arriving during a crisis.

6. Increasing or Indexed Cover

Several plans raise sum assured annually or permit step-ups at marriage or childbirth without fresh medicals. Check the cap on increases and the premium loading applied at each step before assuming the cover keeps pace with inflation.

7. Tax Treatment and Contract Fine Print

Premiums have traditionally qualified under Section 80C, with proceeds exempt under Section 10(10D), subject to premium-to-sum-assured conditions and the tax regime you file under. Confirm the position for your assessment year with a chartered accountant, and use the free-look window to exit without penalty.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • Pro: a clean term plan delivers the highest cover per rupee of any life product in India.
  • Pro: staggered payouts and waiver riders protect behaviour risk, not just mortality risk.
  • Con: riders bought without reading disease definitions often fail at claim stage.
  • Con: cheapest premiums frequently map to thinner service networks and slower settlement.
  • Con: tax benefits are regime-dependent and cannot be the primary reason to buy.
