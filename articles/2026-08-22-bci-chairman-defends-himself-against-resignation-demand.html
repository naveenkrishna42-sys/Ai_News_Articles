BCI Chairman defends himself against resignation demand

📅 2026-08-22 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Manan Kumar Mishra denies the allegations against him.
  • YR Sadasiva Reddy calls for Mishra's resignation within 15 days.
  • Legal proceedings are ongoing regarding the NALSAR row.

BCI Chairman Denies Allegations

Manan Kumar Mishra, Chairman of the Bar Council of India (BCI), has strongly defended himself against the allegations made by YR Sadasiva Reddy, the co-chairman of the BCI. Reddy has called for Mishra's resignation within 15 days, citing eight serious allegations. Mishra has dismissed these allegations as baseless and politically motivated.

YR Sadasiva Reddy's Call for Resignation

YR Sadasiva Reddy, in a public statement, accused Mishra of misconduct and corruption, citing specific incidents related to the NALSAR University row. Reddy argued that Mishra's refusal to step down is undermining the integrity of the legal profession. Mishra has responded by stating that he will not resign and will continue to serve the BCI until his term ends.

Legal Proceedings Ongoing

A plea has been filed in the Supreme Court seeking Mishra's removal from his position. The plea argues that Mishra's actions have brought the BCI into disrepute and that he should be removed from office. Mishra has denied these allegations and stated that he will fight the case in court.

BCI General Council Meeting

The BCI General Council Meeting is scheduled to discuss Mishra's resignation. Reddy has called for a vote of no confidence in Mishra, while Mishra has stated that he will not step down. The outcome of this meeting will be closely watched by the legal community.

Future Developments

The legal battle between Mishra and Reddy is expected to continue, with both sides presenting their arguments in court. The outcome of this battle will have significant implications for the BCI and the legal profession in India. The legal community is eagerly awaiting the Supreme Court's decision on the plea for Mishra's removal.
