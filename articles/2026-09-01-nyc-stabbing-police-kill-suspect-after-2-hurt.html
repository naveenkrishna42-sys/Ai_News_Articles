Woman with mental health history stabs 2 in Times Square

A 38-year-old woman with a known history of mental health issues randomly stabbed two people in New York City's Times Square on Monday night. The incident occurred around 9:30 PM, sending shockwaves through the bustling tourist hub. The two victims, identified as a Bank of America vice president and another individual, were injured in the attack.

Police response and suspect's death

New York Police Department (NYPD) officers quickly responded to the scene. The suspect, who was armed with kitchen knives, engaged in a confrontation with the officers. Witnesses reported hearing multiple shots fired. The suspect was later pronounced dead at the scene. The two victims were rushed to nearby hospitals, where their conditions are being monitored.
