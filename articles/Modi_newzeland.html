PM Modi's Visit to New Zealand: A New Chapter in Bilateral Relations

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has arrived in New Zealand, marking a significant milestone in the bilateral relations between the two nations. During his visit, PM Modi attended a diaspora event where he expressed his enthusiasm for the growing ties between India and New Zealand.

At the diaspora event, PM Modi stated that the 'India-NZ Waka' is ready to embark on a new voyage together, symbolizing the shared aspirations and goals of the two countries. The term 'Waka' refers to a traditional Maori canoe, representing the journey of the two nations towards a common destination.
