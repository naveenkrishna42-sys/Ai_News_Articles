University of Mumbai Reschedules Third Year Semester Exams

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • University of Mumbai has rescheduled third year undergraduate semester examinations.
  • Revised timetable issued for various streams; students advised to check official website.
  • Exams originally scheduled for March now moved to April due to administrative reasons.

The University of Mumbai has announced a revised schedule for its third year undergraduate semester examinations. The decision, communicated via an official notice, affects students across multiple faculties.

The change comes as a surprise to many students who had been preparing for the exams based on the earlier timetable. University officials have cited administrative reasons for the rescheduling but have not provided further details.

Revised Exam Dates

According to the new timetable, exams that were originally slated for the second half of March will now be held in the first two weeks of April. The exact dates vary depending on the specific course and faculty.

Students are advised to check the official University of Mumbai website for the updated schedule specific to their department. Hard copies of the revised timetable are also expected to be displayed on college notice boards.

What Students Need to Do

The university has urged students to coordinate with their respective colleges for any further clarifications. Hall tickets for the rescheduled exams will be issued closer to the new dates.

Faculty members have been asked to adjust their teaching and internal assessment schedules to align with the new exam timeline. This move aims to ensure that students have adequate time to prepare.

This is not the first time the university has had to adjust exam schedules. Last year, a similar rescheduling was necessitated by a clash with other academic events. However, officials have not confirmed if this is the case now.

Impact on Students

While some students have welcomed the extra preparation time, others have expressed concerns about the disruption to their study plans. Many had already planned their revision around the original dates.

Those with job placements or internship commitments linked to the academic calendar may face logistical challenges. The university has not issued any specific guidance on such cases yet.

The decision could also affect the start date of the next academic semester. University sources indicate that a decision on this will be taken after the current exams conclude.

Students are advised to remain in touch with their college administrations for any last-minute updates. The university has promised to communicate any further changes promptly.

Officials have not yet confirmed whether the rescheduling will affect the results declaration timeline. Typically, results are announced within six to eight weeks after the last exam.

As the new exam dates approach, students are encouraged to use the extra time wisely. Many colleges are planning to hold extra revision lectures and doubt-clearing sessions.

The university has also set up a helpline for students facing technical issues while accessing the revised timetable online. The helpline number is available on the official website.

All eyes will now be on how smoothly the university implements this revised schedule. Any further delays could have a cascading effect on the academic year.

The coming weeks will reveal whether the new timeline holds or if more adjustments are needed. Students and faculty alike will be watching closely.
