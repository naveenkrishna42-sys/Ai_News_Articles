CREDAI Hyderabad Property Show 2026 Scheduled for August 28-30 at HITEX

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • CREDAI Hyderabad Property Show 2026 dates announced: August 28-30
  • Venue is HITEX, a major exhibition centre in Hyderabad
  • Event expected to feature multiple developers and new project launches

The Confederation of Real Estate Developers' Associations of India (CREDAI) Hyderabad chapter has announced the dates for its flagship property exhibition. The CREDAI Hyderabad Property Show 2026 will be held from August 28 to 30 at HITEX, the city's premier exhibition centre.

Annual real estate showcase

This three-day event serves as a platform for homebuyers and investors to explore a wide range of residential and commercial properties. Organisers expect participation from major developers across Hyderabad, showcasing both ongoing and upcoming projects. The show typically attracts thousands of visitors looking for apartments, villas, and plots in various price segments.

CREDAI Hyderabad is one of the most active regional chapters of the national real estate body. The property show has been a regular feature on the city's real estate calendar for several years, helping bridge the gap between buyers and builders.

Timing and venue details

HITEX, located in Madhapur, is a well-known venue for large-scale exhibitions and trade fairs. The choice of venue ensures easy accessibility for visitors from across the city. The show will run from Friday, August 28, to Sunday, August 30, giving potential buyers the entire weekend to attend.

Exact timings and entry fee details have not yet been released by the organisers. However, past editions of the show have offered free entry to visitors, with registration at the venue.

What to expect

Developers are expected to unveil new project launches and offer special discounts during the exhibition. Many builders use this platform to attract serious buyers and generate leads for the upcoming festive season. Financial institutions and home loan providers are also likely to set up stalls, offering on-the-spot loan approvals and guidance.

The show comes at a time when Hyderabad's real estate market is seeing steady demand, particularly in the mid-income and luxury segments. Areas like the western corridor, including Kokapet, Narsingi, and Tellapur, have seen significant development activity in recent months.

CREDAI officials have not yet confirmed the list of participating developers or the total number of projects that will be on display. More details are expected closer to the event date.

The property show is expected to provide a comprehensive overview of the current market trends and available inventory. For buyers, it offers a chance to compare multiple projects under one roof, saving time and effort.

With the event still a month away, interested homebuyers and investors are advised to mark their calendars. The organisers are likely to release more information, including registration details and special offers, in the coming weeks.
