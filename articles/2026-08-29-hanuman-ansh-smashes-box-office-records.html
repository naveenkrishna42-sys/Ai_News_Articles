Hanuman Ansh sets new box office record

Hanuman Ansh has made a significant impact on the Indian box office, earning Rs 12 crore on its 4th Friday, which is a staggering 1130% higher than its opening day collection. This achievement not only sets a new record but also showcases the film's strong audience appeal and effective marketing strategy.

Economic impact and profit margins

Made on a modest Rs 2 crore budget, Hanuman Ansh has delivered an impressive 500% profit, highlighting the film's commercial success. This profit margin is a testament to the film's ability to attract a large audience and maintain interest over an extended period.

Comparison with other films

Hanuman Ansh has surpassed Yash's Toxic, another film that started with a low opening collection. The significant growth of Hanuman Ansh indicates that strategic marketing and audience engagement can lead to substantial box office success.
