Samsung Galaxy F70 Pro price, variants revealed ahead of India launch

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung Galaxy F70 Pro price and variants officially revealed before launch
  • Flipkart availability for India confirmed
  • Expected to feature Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 and six years of updates

Samsung has officially revealed the pricing and storage configurations for the Galaxy F70 Pro ahead of its launch in India. The company's confirmation puts an end to weeks of speculation, with the device expected to arrive on Flipkart as an exclusive online offering.

The Galaxy F70 Pro will reportedly be positioned in the upper-budget segment, with international pricing pegged at roughly $275. In India, the price is expected to translate to a figure that undercuts several rivals in the mid-range category, though Samsung has not yet confirmed the exact rupee amount for the domestic market.

Price and variants

According to official details shared by the company, the Galaxy F70 Pro will be available in multiple storage configurations. While the exact Indian pricing structure is yet to be formally announced, the base variant is expected to start at a competitive price point that aligns with Samsung's strategy for its F-series lineup.

The F-series has traditionally targeted online-first buyers who prioritise value. The F70 Pro appears to continue that approach, balancing specifications with affordability. Flipkart has been named as the retail partner for the launch, confirming earlier reports about the e-commerce platform's involvement.

Performance and chipset

Benchmark listings that surfaced earlier indicate the Galaxy F70 Pro will run on Qualcomm's Snapdragon 6 Gen 3 chipset. The processor is designed for mid-range devices, offering improved efficiency and capable performance for everyday tasks and light gaming.

The Geekbench leak, which surfaced in recent weeks, showed respectable single-core and multi-core scores for the device. This suggests the F70 Pro will handle multitasking without significant strain, though Samsung has not yet provided official confirmation of the chipset in its promotional material.

Software support and longevity

One of the standout talking points around the Galaxy F70 Pro is its software update policy. Reports indicate the phone will receive six years of updates, a commitment that is rare in this price segment. If accurate, this would place the F70 Pro ahead of several competitors that offer three to four years of support.

Long-term software support is increasingly becoming a deciding factor for Indian buyers, who tend to hold onto devices longer than consumers in many other markets. Samsung's extended update commitment, first seen on its premium flagships, appears to be trickling down to the F-series.

India launch and availability

Samsung has teased the India launch of the Galaxy F70 Pro 5G, with Flipkart availability now confirmed. The company has not yet announced a specific launch date, but the teaser campaign suggests an announcement is imminent.

Given the F-series track record in India, the F70 Pro is expected to compete directly with offerings from Chinese manufacturers that dominate the online segment. Samsung's brand trust, combined with aggressive pricing and long software support, could give it an edge in the crowded market.

Buyers looking for a 5G smartphone with a reliable processor and extended software support will likely find the F70 Pro an attractive option. However, the final Indian pricing will determine how it stacks up against rivals in the segment.

The official launch event is expected to take place in the coming weeks, with sales likely to begin shortly after on Flipkart. All eyes will be on the final price tag when Samsung makes the full announcement.
