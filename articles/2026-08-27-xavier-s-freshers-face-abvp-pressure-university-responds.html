Xavier’s freshers face ABVP pressure, university responds

📅 2026-08-27 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • ABVP is pressuring freshers at St Xavier’s College, Mumbai
  • University has taken steps to address the issue
  • Freshers report harassment and intimidation

ABVP’s Influence on Freshers at St Xavier’s College

St Xavier’s College in Mumbai is facing criticism after reports emerged of the Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP) pressuring freshers. The incident has sparked a debate about the role of student unions in higher education and the need for universities to intervene.

University’s Response

The university has acknowledged the issue and is taking steps to address it. A statement from the university said that they are in the process of reviewing their student union policies and will take appropriate action if necessary.

Freshers Speak Out

Several freshers have come forward to report harassment and intimidation. They claim that ABVP members have been pressuring them to join the union and have threatened them if they refuse. The freshers have also reported instances of physical and verbal abuse.

Broader Implications

This incident is part of a larger trend of student unions exerting undue influence on freshers. It raises questions about the autonomy of students and the need for universities to ensure a safe and inclusive environment for all students.

Expert Opinion

Education expert Dr. Ananya Deshpande comments, 'This incident highlights the need for universities to have clear policies on student unions and their activities. It is essential that universities ensure that student unions do not use their influence to harass or intimidate other students.'
