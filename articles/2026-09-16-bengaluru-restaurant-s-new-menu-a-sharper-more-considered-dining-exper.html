A Sharper Culinary Direction in Bengaluru

Bengaluru's dining scene constantly evolves, and one of its prominent restaurants has unveiled a revamped menu that signals a shift towards sharper, more considered cooking. The new offerings, which we sampled over two visits, demonstrate a clear intent to refine flavours, reduce clutter on the plate, and highlight seasonal produce. This isn't a radical overhaul but a confident step towards maturity, likely to appeal to diners seeking substance over spectacle.

What's Changed: From Complexity to Clarity

The previous menu, while ambitious, sometimes suffered from over-engineering. The new direction pares things back. Each dish now has a clear focal point—a perfectly cooked protein, a vibrant sauce, or a well-executed textural contrast. For example, the 'Coastal Crab Sukka' (₹650) lets the sweetness of fresh crab shine, with just a hint of kokum and curry leaf. Similarly, the 'Mysore Pak Cheesecake' (₹450) is a revelation: the richness of cheesecake meets the grainy, ghee-laden nostalgia of the classic Indian sweet, without either element overpowering the other.

Real-World Performance: What Works and What Doesn't

Our tasting highlighted several strengths. The kitchen's consistency in cooking proteins—be it the succulent lamb shank or the flaky sea bass—is commendable. Service is attentive, though during peak hours, the pacing between courses can lag. The restaurant's ambiance remains elegant but unpretentious, suitable for both business dinners and celebratory meals.

  • Pros: Bold, well-balanced flavours; high-quality ingredients; improved portion sizes compared to previous menu; excellent dessert selection; knowledgeable staff.
  • Cons: Prices are on the higher side for Bengaluru's mid-range market; limited vegan options; noise levels can rise on weekends; reservation recommended.

Buyer Implications: Is It Worth Your Money?

For Bengaluru's discerning diners, this revamped menu offers a compelling proposition. The average spend of ₹2,500 for two is justified by the execution and ingredient quality. It competes with upscale casual dining spots in Indiranagar and Koramangala, where similar meals often cost 10-15% more. If you value thoughtful cooking over trendy gimmicks, this restaurant delivers. However, budget-conscious diners might find the small plates better value than mains.

Final Verdict

This menu refresh positions the restaurant as a serious contender in Bengaluru's culinary landscape. It's not just about new dishes; it's about a sharper philosophy. For a special occasion or a refined meal, it's worth the splurge. We recommend starting with the crab sukka and ending with the cheesecake—a perfect arc of savoury and sweet.
