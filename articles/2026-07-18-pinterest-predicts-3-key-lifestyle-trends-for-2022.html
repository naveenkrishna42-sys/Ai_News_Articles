Pinterest Predicts 3 Key Lifestyle Trends for 2022

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Lifestyle Original source ↗
Key points
  • Pinterest predicts a rise in 'mindful living' with an emphasis on meditation, sustainable fashion, and slow travel.
  • Nostalgia-driven trends like 'cottagecore' and 'Y2K fashion' are expected to dominate, blending comfort with vintage aesthetics.
  • Bold self-expression through maximalist home decor and vibrant makeup looks will challenge minimalist norms.

As 2021 winds down, Pinterest has released its annual trend forecast for the year ahead. The platform, which relies on user searches and board activity, has identified three major lifestyle shifts that it expects to shape 2022.

Mindful Living Takes Centre Stage

The first trend is a move towards conscious consumption. Pinterest reports a surge in searches for 'meditation spaces', 'sustainable fashion brands', and 'slow travel itineraries'. Users are increasingly looking for ways to reduce their environmental footprint while prioritising mental well-being.

This isn't just about buying eco-friendly products. The trend extends to how people spend their time. Searches for 'digital detox ideas' and 'home yoga routines' have climbed sharply. Many are seeking to replace screen time with grounding activities like gardening and journaling.

Nostalgic Comfort: Cottagecore and Y2K Revival

The second trend is a retreat into nostalgia. Pinterest expects 'cottagecore'—a romanticised rural aesthetic—to evolve into 'grandmillennial' style, mixing vintage floral prints with modern minimalism. At the same time, Y2K fashion is making a strong comeback, with searches for 'low-rise jeans', 'butterfly clips', and 'chunky sneakers' rising steadily.

Home decor is not immune. Searches for 'retro kitchens' and '70s colour palettes' have increased. This suggests people are craving comfort and familiarity after two years of uncertainty. The trend leans into warmth, with earthy tones and textured fabrics replacing stark, neutral interiors.

Bold Self-Expression Through Maximalism

The third trend is a departure from minimalist restraint. Pinterest predicts a rise in 'maximalist' home decor, where more is more. Think layered patterns, eclectic furniture, and statement walls. Searches for 'bold wallpaper' and 'colour drenching' have spiked.

Fashion follows suit. Expect to see vibrant makeup looks—'graphic eyeliner', 'bright blush', and 'glitter lips'—alongside oversized silhouettes and mixed prints. The platform notes a 120% increase in searches for 'clashing patterns'. This suggests a collective desire to break free from muted pandemic-era dressing.

These trends are not just aesthetic choices. They reflect deeper cultural shifts. The move towards mindfulness indicates a broader reassessment of priorities. Nostalgia offers comfort in turbulent times. Maximalism is a form of rebellion against the homogenous, algorithm-driven style of recent years.

Pinterest's data offers a snapshot of what millions of users are planning. Whether these predictions materialise fully remains to be seen. But for now, 2022 looks set to be a year of deliberate, expressive living.

Watch for these trends to influence everything from retail launches to interior design catalogues in the coming months.
