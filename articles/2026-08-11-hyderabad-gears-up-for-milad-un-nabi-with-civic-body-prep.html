Hyderabad gears up for Milad-un-Nabi with civic body prep

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation starts pre-festival cleanliness drives
  • Special lighting and traffic management plans for Milad-un-Nabi processions
  • Officials urge public cooperation for smooth observance

City readies for the Prophet's birth anniversary

With Milad-un-Nabi approaching, the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) has swung into action. Preparations across the city have begun in earnest, with a focus on ensuring smooth processions and public convenience.

Officials are prioritising sanitation and streetlight repairs in areas where large gatherings are expected. The aim, they say, is to avoid any last-minute glitches during the religious observances.

Cleanliness drives and road repairs

GHMC teams have been deployed to clear debris and sweep major thoroughfares. Pothole patches and footpath repairs are also underway, particularly on routes that processions will take.

Municipal staff have been instructed to complete pending works before the festival day. Water tankers and mobile toilets are being arranged for the convenience of devotees.

Traffic and security arrangements

Traffic police are drafting diversion plans for the main processions, which typically wind through the old city. Parking restrictions and no-entry zones will be enforced to manage crowds.

Police deployment is being coordinated with the GHMC to ensure security at sensitive points. Additional barricades and watchtowers are being set up along the route.

Lighting and decoration work

Special lighting arrangements are being planned for mosques and key landmarks. Electrical teams are checking wiring to prevent outages during evening prayers and processions.

Local volunteers and religious committees are also participating in the preparations, assisting with decoration and crowd management. Officials have appealed to residents to cooperate with cleanliness norms and follow traffic advisories.

While dates for the main procession have not been officially confirmed, preparations are proceeding based on the expected lunar calendar. The civic body is expected to issue a detailed advisory closer to the day.

What to watch

Over the next week, the GHMC will finalise its plan and release route maps for the processions. Residents should watch for official notifications on diversions and parking bans to avoid inconvenience.
