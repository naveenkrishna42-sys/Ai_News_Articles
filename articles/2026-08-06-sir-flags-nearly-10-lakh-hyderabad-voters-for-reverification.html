SIR flags nearly 10 lakh Hyderabad voters for reverification

📅 2026-08-06 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nearly 10 lakh voters in Hyderabad have been flagged for reverification by the State Intelligence Department (SIR).
  • The move is part of efforts to clean the electoral roll and ensure accuracy ahead of upcoming elections.
  • Officials have urged affected voters to cooperate with the verification process to avoid removal from the rolls.

Massive Voter Verification Drive in Hyderabad

In a significant move to cleanse the electoral roll, the State Intelligence Department (SIR) has flagged nearly 10 lakh voters in Hyderabad for reverification. This development comes ahead of the upcoming elections, with authorities aiming to weed out duplicate or bogus entries.

The SIR's action targets voters whose details appear suspicious or lack adequate documentation. Officials have emphasized that the process is routine and not aimed at any particular community or constituency.

Why the Flags Were Raised

Sources indicate that the flagged voters include those with incomplete addresses, mismatched photo identification, or multiple entries in the same household. The reverification process will involve field visits and cross-checking of documents to confirm each voter's eligibility.

Election authorities have stated that the exercise is part of a larger statewide drive to ensure error-free electoral rolls. Hyderabad, being a densely populated urban district, has historically faced challenges with voter data accuracy.

What Voters Need to Do

Officials have advised affected voters to cooperate with verification teams and provide necessary documents when approached. Those who fail to respond may face temporary suspension from the electoral roll until their details are confirmed.

The SIR has set up helplines and designated offices to assist citizens with queries regarding the process. Voters can also check their status online through the Election Commission's portal.

Political Reactions and Concerns

Political parties have reacted cautiously, with some expressing concerns over potential disenfranchisement of genuine voters. However, election officials have assured that the process will be transparent and appeal mechanisms will be in place.

Past instances of large-scale deletions have sparked controversies in other states, but Hyderabad's administration has pledged to follow due process. The final list of verified voters is expected to be published after the completion of the drive.

Looking Ahead

As the verification process unfolds over the coming weeks, all eyes will be on how smoothly it is implemented. The success of this drive could set a precedent for electoral roll management in other urban centres across the country.
