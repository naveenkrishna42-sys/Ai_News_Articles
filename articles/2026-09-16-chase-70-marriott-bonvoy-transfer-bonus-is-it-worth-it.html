Chase's 70% Marriott Bonvoy Transfer Bonus: A Limited-Time Opportunity

Chase Ultimate Rewards has rolled out a 70% transfer bonus to Marriott Bonvoy, running until 15 October 2026. This means every 1,000 Chase points convert to 1,700 Marriott Bonvoy points instead of the usual 1,000. The offer applies to all Chase cards that earn Ultimate Rewards, including Sapphire Preferred, Sapphire Reserve, and Ink Business Preferred. For frequent travellers with existing Marriott stays planned, this can unlock significant value—but only if you crunch the numbers.

Transfer Ratio and Key Dates

The standard transfer ratio is 1:1, so the bonus effectively boosts it to 1:1.7. Transfers are irreversible and typically take 1–2 business days to reflect in your Marriott account. The deadline is 15 October 2026 at 11:59 PM ET. There is no cap on the number of points you can transfer, but Chase imposes a minimum transfer of 1,000 points and increments of 1,000 thereafter.

Real-World Value: When Does It Make Sense?

Marriott Bonvoy points are typically valued at around 0.8 to 1.0 cents each. With a 70% bonus, your Chase points effectively become worth 1.36 to 1.7 cents per point—if you redeem at that baseline. However, the true value hinges on the specific hotel and dates. High-end properties like the Ritz-Carlton or St. Regis can offer redemption rates of 1.5 to 2.5 cents per Bonvoy point, pushing the effective value of a Chase point to 2.55 to 4.25 cents. That is excellent for a transferable currency.

On the other hand, low-tier Marriott properties (Category 1–3) often yield less than 0.8 cents per point, making the bonus less attractive. For example, a Category 2 hotel might cost 12,500 points per night; if the cash rate is ₹8,000 (about $96), the value is 0.77 cents per point. After the bonus, your Chase points are worth only 1.31 cents each—still decent but not stellar compared to other Chase transfer partners like Hyatt or United.

Pros and Cons of the 70% Transfer Bonus

  • Pros:
    • Boosts the value of Chase points for Marriott stays by 70%.
    • Ideal for topping off a Marriott account for a specific high-value redemption.
    • No cap on transfer amount, so large balances can be moved.
  • Cons:
    • Transfers are irreversible; once points leave Chase, they cannot be returned.
    • Marriott Bonvoy points have seen devaluations; future award rates are not guaranteed.
    • Opportunity cost: Chase points could be transferred to other partners like World of Hyatt or United Airlines at 1:1, potentially yielding higher value.

Strategic Considerations Before You Transfer

Do not transfer points speculatively. Marriott frequently adjusts its award chart without notice, and holding Bonvoy points carries devaluation risk. The best strategy is to identify a specific award stay, confirm availability, and then transfer only the required amount. For example, a five-night stay at a Category 8 hotel (40,000 points per night) would require 200,000 Bonvoy points. With the bonus, you would need 117,647 Chase points (rounded to 118,000). Without the bonus, it would cost 200,000 Chase points—a saving of 82,000 points.

Also consider whether you can achieve similar value through other Chase partners. World of Hyatt often provides redemption values above 2 cents per point, and United Airlines can be lucrative for international business class. Run the numbers for your specific trip before committing.

Final Verdict: Is the 70% Bonus Worth It?

For travellers with a concrete Marriott redemption in mind, especially at luxury properties, the 70% transfer bonus is a solid opportunity. It effectively turns Chase points into a more powerful tool for hotel stays. However, if you are flexible or unsure, holding onto your Chase points for a potentially better transfer partner or a future bonus is wise. The offer ends 15 October 2026, so act deliberately—not hastily.
