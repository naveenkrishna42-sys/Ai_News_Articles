Devdutt Padikkal's second Test ton: A tale of resilience

Devdutt Padikkal's second Test century against Sri Lanka in the second Test at the Wankhede Stadium was a testament to his resilience and adaptability. The match, which saw a mix of chaos and calm, highlighted India's ability to adjust to Sri Lanka's bowling attack. Padikkal's innings of 121 runs off 212 balls was a crucial factor in India's innings of 450 runs.

Chaos and calm: The match's ebb and flow

The match began with a bang, as India's seamers, led by Jasprit Bumrah and Mohammed Shami, troubled Sri Lanka's top-order batsmen. However, the Sri Lankan middle-order, led by Kusal Mendis, showed resilience, with Mendis scoring a half-century. The match saw a series of ups and downs, with India's bowlers and batsmen adapting to the conditions.

Sri Lanka's batting struggles: A tale of two halves

Sri Lanka's batting lineup struggled against India's seamers, with only Kusal Mendis and Danushka Gunathilaka scoring half-centuries. The Sri Lankan innings of 250 runs was a testament to their resilience, but it was not enough to save the match.

The match saw several key moments that turned the tide in India's favor. Devdutt Padikkal's innings was a crucial factor, as he anchored India's innings and provided the much-needed momentum.
