Centre Denies Permission for Telangana CM Revanth Reddy’s US Trip

📅 2026-08-21 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Telangana CM Revanth Reddy cancels US trip after Centre rejects political clearance.
  • Ministry of External Affairs cited the programme as 'not appropriate' for a CM's visit.
  • The Chief Minister is now scheduled to return to Hyderabad following the denial of approval.

Cancellation Confirmed Following MEA Decision

Telangana Chief Minister Revanth Reddy has officially cancelled his upcoming trip to the United States. The decision follows a formal denial of political clearance by the Union government, which governs foreign travel protocols for state-level constitutional functionaries.

Sources confirmed that the Chief Minister’s office received communication from the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) declining the request. With the necessary travel permissions withheld, the planned itinerary has been scrapped, and the Chief Minister is now making arrangements to return to Hyderabad.

MEA Cites 'Inappropriate' Programme

The Ministry of External Affairs addressed the rejection during routine briefings, stating that the proposed programme submitted by the Chief Minister’s office was not considered appropriate for an official visit. While the Ministry holds the prerogative to assess the nature of foreign engagements for state heads, they have not yet disclosed specific details regarding which aspects of the schedule triggered the refusal.

Typically, state leaders traveling abroad on official business require clearance from the Centre to ensure alignment with national diplomatic priorities. The MEA evaluates these requests based on the schedule of meetings and the nature of the engagements planned in the host country.

Impact on State Engagements

The cancellation marks a significant shift in the Chief Minister's agenda for the coming week. The trip was expected to focus on state-level development initiatives and potential investments, though those discussions are now effectively stalled.

Political observers in Hyderabad are currently monitoring how this administrative friction will influence the state’s outreach efforts. As of now, the Telangana government has not released a comprehensive statement detailing the next steps for the proposed international engagements.

Protocol and Oversight

Foreign travel by state Chief Ministers is subject to rigorous oversight by the Union government. The process involves vetting by the MEA to ensure that state-led initiatives do not conflict with broader federal foreign policy objectives.

While the Centre maintains that the rejection was based on the specific content of the visit, the move has drawn attention to the standard operating procedures for inter-state and inter-governmental coordination. The process remains a point of standard administrative scrutiny for all high-ranking constitutional officials planning international travel.

The state government is expected to issue a brief statement once the Chief Minister settles back in Hyderabad, providing clarity on whether the visit will be rescheduled or restructured for a later date.
