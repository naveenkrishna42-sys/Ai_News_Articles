Diomandé skips Real Madrid training amid adaptation concerns

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Diomandé absent from Real Madrid's Friday training session
  • Club signed the Ivory Coast midfielder on a seven-year deal from RB Leipzig
  • Coaching staff monitoring his adaptation ahead of the new season

Absence raises eyebrows at Valdebebas

Yan Diomandé was conspicuously absent from Real Madrid's training session on Friday, a development that has set off quiet murmurs among the club's supporters and the Spanish press. The Ivory Coast midfielder, who completed a high-profile move from RB Leipzig earlier this summer, was not spotted with the rest of the squad as they prepared for the upcoming campaign.

The club has not yet issued an official statement explaining the absence. Sources close to the dressing room suggest it could be a minor fitness issue, but nothing has been confirmed as of now.

A signing built on World Cup promise

Diomandé arrived at the Santiago Bernabéu with considerable fanfare. Real Madrid sealed a seven-year deal for the midfielder after his standout performances at the World Cup, where he helped Ivory Coast make a deep run that captured global attention. His displays in the middle of the park, marked by relentless pressing and crisp distribution, convinced the Spanish giants to trigger his release clause at RB Leipzig.

The move was widely seen as a statement of intent from the club's hierarchy. They identified the 23-year-old as a long-term anchor for their midfield, someone who could eventually take over the mantle from the ageing core that has defined the team's recent success.

Where Diomandé fits in Ancelotti's plans

Manager Carlo Ancelotti is known for easing new signings into his system gradually, and Diomandé's profile suggests he will be deployed in a box-to-box role. His energy and ability to break lines make him a natural fit for the high-tempo style Real Madrid employ in Europe. However, the competition for places in that midfield is fierce, with established stars and emerging talents all vying for minutes.

Analysts have noted that Diomandé's adaptation will hinge on his understanding of positional discipline. In Leipzig's gegenpressing system, he had more freedom to roam; at Madrid, he will be expected to balance attacking impulses with defensive responsibilities.

Mourinho's reported reservations

Interestingly, reports have surfaced that José Mourinho, now managing a rival European side, had reservations about Diomandé in a high-stakes scenario. According to Goal.com, Mourinho rejected a proposed gamble involving the midfielder, preferring more experienced options in a critical match situation. While this is not directly related to Madrid's plans, it adds a layer of scrutiny to the player's profile.

For now, the focus remains on Diomandé's physical readiness. The pre-season period is crucial for any new signing, and missing even a single session can disrupt the rhythm of integration. The club's medical staff are likely to assess him over the weekend, with an update expected early next week.

What to watch next

Real Madrid's next pre-season fixture looms, and all eyes will be on whether Diomandé features. If he returns to training in the coming days, the absence will likely be dismissed as a minor blip. But if it extends, questions about his fitness and readiness for the La Liga opener will only grow louder. The club's faithful will hope this is nothing more than a precautionary pause in what promises to be a long and demanding season.
