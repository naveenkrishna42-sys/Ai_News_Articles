BJP's victory in Bengal sparks campus unrest at Jadavpur University

Three months after the BJP's triumphant return to power in West Bengal, clashes have erupted on the campus of Jadavpur University, one of the state's most prestigious educational institutions. The unrest began when supporters of the Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP) attacked a symbol designed by Nandalal Bose, a revered figure in the university's history. The incident has sparked widespread condemnation and raised concerns about the state of democracy on Indian campuses.

ABVP supporters vandalise Nandalal Bose's symbol

On the morning of August 20, ABVP supporters gathered outside the university's main gate, where they proceeded to vandalise a prominent symbol designed by Nandalal Bose. The symbol, which had been a source of pride for the university community, was left in ruins. The incident has been widely condemned by students, faculty, and members of the public, who have expressed outrage at the act of violence.

Clashes between ABVP and Left-leaning student groups

The vandalism of Nandalal Bose's symbol set off a chain reaction of violence, as clashes broke out between ABVP supporters and Left-leaning student groups on the university campus. Several students were injured in the violence, which quickly spread to the surrounding streets. Police were called in to restore order, but the situation remained tense throughout the day.

University unrest spills onto streets as police intervene

The unrest on the Jadavpur University campus spilled over onto the streets of Kolkata, as students from both sides of the political spectrum took to the streets to protest the violence. Police were deployed to maintain order, but tensions remained high as the situation continued to escalate. The incident has raised concerns about the state of democracy on Indian campuses, with many students and faculty members expressing fears for their safety.
