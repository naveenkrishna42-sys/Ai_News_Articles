Trisha's cryptic posts stir row after Vijay's wife withdraws divorce plea

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Trisha shared cryptic posts on social media about 'drama at this age'.
  • Posts come after Vijay's wife withdrew her divorce plea, amid political tensions.
  • AIADMK leader questioned Trisha's potential as Deputy CM, escalating the row.

Chennai, Aug 11: Actor Trisha Krishnan has set social media abuzz with a series of cryptic posts that many are interpreting as a swipe at the ongoing political drama surrounding actor-politician Vijay and his family. The posts, which appeared late Monday night, came hours after Vijay's wife withdrew her divorce petition in a city family court, a development that had already triggered intense speculation.

While Trisha did not name anyone, her words — 'Drama at this age is embarrassing' — were enough to ignite a firestorm online. Within hours, the hashtag #TrishaVsVijay began trending, with fans and critics alike dissecting every syllable.

What Trisha Posted

On her X (formerly Twitter) account, Trisha wrote: "Some people never grow up. Drama at this age is embarrassing. Focus on your work, not on others' lives." The post was followed by another: "Peace is a choice. Choose wisely." Neither post referenced Vijay, his wife, or any political figure directly, but the timing was hard to ignore.

The actor, known for her measured public persona, has rarely spoken about Vijay in recent years. The two have co-starred in several films, including the 2010 blockbuster Vettaikaran, but have reportedly drifted apart professionally and personally.

Political Overtones

The controversy, however, quickly spilled beyond cinema. In a press conference on Tuesday, AIADMK spokesperson and former minister D. Jayakumar questioned Trisha's motives, saying, "When will Trisha become Tamil Nadu Deputy CM? She seems to have too much time to comment on political families." The remark was widely seen as a taunt aimed at the actor, drawing sharp rebukes from her fan clubs and neutral observers.

This is not the first time Trisha has been dragged into political crossfire. Earlier this year, DMK leader Udhayanidhi Stalin's remarks on Sanatana Dharma had sparked a row, and Trisha's cryptic posts at that time were interpreted as a critique of the ruling party. Now, with Vijay's wife's divorce withdrawal making headlines, the actor finds herself in the middle of yet another controversy.

The Divorce Withdrawal

Vijay's wife, who had filed for divorce in June citing irreconcilable differences, withdrew her petition on Monday. The family court accepted the withdrawal, and the couple, who have two children, are expected to make a joint statement soon. Neither Vijay nor his wife has commented publicly on the withdrawal or on Trisha's posts.

Legal experts say the withdrawal could pave the way for reconciliation, but they caution that the couple's issues were personal and should not be politicised. "This is a private matter," said senior advocate R. Vaigai. "Dragging actors or political figures into it only adds noise."

Mixed Reactions Online

Social media has been divided. While some users praised Trisha for speaking her mind, others accused her of fuelling unnecessary drama. "Trisha should stick to acting. Why comment on someone's family matters?" wrote one user. Another countered: "She didn't name anyone. If the shoe fits, wear it."

Celebrity watchers note that Trisha's posts are characteristic of her style — indirect but pointed. "She has always used ambiguity to her advantage," said media analyst K. S. Vimal. "It keeps her relevant without burning bridges."

What's Next

As of now, Trisha has not responded to the political barbs or the online chatter. Vijay's camp has remained silent as well, with his next film's shooting schedule reportedly unaffected. The bigger question is whether this incident will escalate into a full-blown war of words between the actor and political parties, especially with elections due next year.

Observers will be watching whether Trisha clarifies her posts or lets the speculation simmer. For now, the only certainty is that in the world of Tamil cinema and politics, silence is rarely golden.
