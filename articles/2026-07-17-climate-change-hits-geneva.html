Climate change is a pressing concern worldwide, with rising temperatures and extreme weather events affecting millions of people. Geneva, being a hub for international diplomacy, plays a crucial part in facilitating discussions and cooperation among nations to combat climate change.

Geneva is home to numerous international organizations, including the United Nations, that work tirelessly to address climate change.
