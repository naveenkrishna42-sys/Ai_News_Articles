Mirzapur Movie: Dhanda Nyoliwala's 'Do Numbari' to be First Song

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Dhanda Nyoliwala's Bollywood debut track 'Do Numbari' will be the first song from Mirzapur: The Movie.
  • The film stars Ali Fazal and Divyenndu in lead roles.
  • The announcement was made by Bollywood Hungama on August 3, 2026.

First Track from the Purvanchal Franchise

The much-anticipated big-screen adaptation of the hit web series has revealed its first musical offering. 'Do Numbari', sung by Dhanda Nyoliwala, will mark the singer's debut in Bollywood. The track is set to be the opening song of Mirzapur: The Movie, starring Ali Fazal and Divyenndu.

Nyoliwala, known for his Haryanvi hits, brings a distinct flavour to the film's soundtrack. The announcement comes as fans eagerly await the transition of the gritty Purvanchal saga from streaming to cinema.

A Fresh Voice for a Familiar World

Dhanda Nyoliwala's entry into Hindi cinema adds a new layer to the film's musical identity. The singer has built a strong following with his energetic and rustic style. 'Do Numbari' is expected to carry that same energy, fitting the raw, high-stakes atmosphere of the Mirzapur universe.

The choice of Nyoliwala suggests the makers are looking to blend mainstream Bollywood with regional appeal. This strategy has worked well for several recent Hindi films, and it could help the movie reach a wider audience.

Ali Fazal and Divyenndu Return

The film brings back Ali Fazal as Guddu Pandit and Divyenndu as Phoolchand Tripathi, reprising their iconic roles from the series. Their on-screen chemistry has been a cornerstone of the show's success. The movie promises to expand their story on a larger canvas, with higher production values and theatrical scale.

Details about the plot remain under wraps, but the title itself hints at a continuation of the power struggles and revenge arcs that defined the series. Fans are speculating about how the film will balance new viewers with those who have followed the show since its first season.

Music as a Marketing Push

Releasing the first song ahead of the film's promotional campaign is a calculated move. A catchy, high-energy track can generate buzz and trend on social media platforms. 'Do Numbari' is likely to be accompanied by a music video featuring the lead actors, which could further amplify anticipation.

The makers have not yet confirmed the film's release date. However, the announcement of the first song suggests that production is on track and that a promotional rollout is imminent.

With the series' massive fan base and the star power of Fazal and Divyenndu, the movie is already one of the most awaited Hindi films of the year. The addition of a fresh voice like Nyoliwala only adds to the curiosity.

As the release date draws closer, more details about the soundtrack and the film's storyline are expected to emerge. Whether 'Do Numbari' becomes the anthem of the summer remains to be seen.
