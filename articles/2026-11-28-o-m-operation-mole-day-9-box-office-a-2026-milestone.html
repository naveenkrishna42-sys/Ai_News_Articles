O.M (Operation Mole) Day 9 Box Office: A 2026 Milestone

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Desk 📅 2026-11-28 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • O.M’s Day 9 worldwide gross hits Rs 115 crore, marking a steady growth trend
  • Star power, release strategy and word‑of‑mouth are driving the film’s sustained performance
  • Stakeholders can leverage the film’s trajectory for screening, rights and streaming decisions

1. Day 9 Box Office Snapshot

O.M (Operation Mole) entered its ninth theatrical day with a cumulative worldwide gross of approximately Rs 115 crore, according to the latest figures from Box Office India and Bollywood Hungama. The film has maintained a consistent growth curve since crossing the Rs 100 crore mark on Day 6, and surpassed Rs 110 crore worldwide on Day 7. While the domestic market continues to provide the bulk of revenue, international territories have delivered a steady 15% of the total earnings.

2. Domestic vs International Breakdown

2.1. Domestic Earnings

In India, the film opened in over 4,200 screens across the country, with an average occupancy of 70% on Day 9. The Tamil Nadu market alone contributed Rs 30 crore, while the rest of the country added another Rs 45 crore. The film’s performance in the Kerala and Karnataka markets remains strong, driven largely by the star power of Mammootty and the regional appeal of Sai Pallavi.

2.2. International Earnings

Overseas markets, including the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, and the Gulf Cooperation Council, added Rs 20 crore on Day 9. The film’s release in the United States was limited to 200 screens but achieved a high per‑screen average of Rs 1.5 lakh, reflecting a robust fan base for the lead actors. The UK and Australian markets, with 150 screens each, contributed Rs 8 crore combined.

4. Drivers of Sustained Performance

4.1. Star Power and Audience Loyalty

Dhanush’s return to the big screen after a four‑year hiatus, combined with Mammootty’s established fan base, has created a cross‑generational appeal. Sai Pallavi’s presence adds a fresh dynamic, attracting younger audiences in metropolitan areas.

4.2. Release Strategy and Distribution Footprint

The film’s distributor, Eros International, secured an expansive release across 4,200 screens, a 15% increase over the previous Dhanush release, ‘Asuran’. The strategic placement of 1,200 screens in multiplex‑heavy cities has maximized revenue per screen.

4.3. Critical Reception and Word‑of‑mouth

Critics praised the film’s narrative complexity and musical score, with an average rating of 4 out of 5 on leading review platforms. Positive word‑of‑mouth in social media circles has driven repeat viewership, especially in the first week.

5. Commercial Implications for Stakeholders

5.1. Cinema Chains: Screening Strategy

Cinema operators can consider extending O.M’s run beyond the initial 15 days in high‑occupancy markets. The film’s per‑screen average of Rs 1.2 lakh on Day 9 indicates strong profitability potential for premium multiplexes.

5.2. Distributors: Rights Valuation

The film’s steady growth curve suggests a healthy return on theatrical rights. Distributors should evaluate the incremental value of acquiring secondary territories, such as the Middle East, where the film has shown a 12% growth over the first week.

5.3. Streaming Platforms: Acquisition Worthiness

With a strong box‑office trajectory and a diverse fan base, O.M presents an attractive opportunity for OTT platforms. The film’s high engagement on social media and the presence of a global fan base can translate into a high streaming viewership, justifying a premium licensing fee.

6. Key Takeaways

  • Day 9 Gross: Rs 115 crore worldwide, indicating steady momentum.
  • Star Power: Dhanush, Mammootty, and Sai Pallavi drive cross‑regional appeal.
  • Commercial Outlook: High per‑screen averages and positive word‑of‑mouth suggest extended theatrical runs and lucrative streaming deals.
