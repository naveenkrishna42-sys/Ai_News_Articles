122 fish vendors agree to move to new Loop Road market: GCC

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Greater Chennai Corporation confirms 122 fish vendors willing to shift to a new market on Loop Road.
  • The move is part of efforts to decongest city streets and provide vendors with better infrastructure.
  • Details on the timeline and exact location of the market are yet to be announced.

Vendors agree to shift

The Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC) has announced that 122 fish vendors have expressed willingness to relocate to a new market coming up on Loop Road. The move is part of the civic body's ongoing efforts to decongest busy thoroughfares and bring informal vending under a regulated framework.

Officials said the vendors currently operate from various spots across the city, often leading to traffic bottlenecks and hygiene complaints. The new market is expected to provide them with designated stalls, clean water supply, and waste disposal facilities.

Decongestion drive

Chennai has been grappling with haphazard street vending, particularly in crowded neighbourhoods. The GCC has been identifying alternative sites to relocate vendors without disrupting their livelihoods. The Loop Road market is one such initiative, designed to centralise fish vending in a single location.

According to corporation data, the 122 vendors represent a significant portion of those operating in the area. Talks with remaining vendors are ongoing, and the GCC expects more to join the relocation plan in the coming weeks.

Infrastructure and timeline

The proposed market will include basic amenities such as electricity, drainage, and parking for customers. The GCC has not yet confirmed the exact date for the market's opening, but sources indicate that construction is in its final stages.

Local residents have largely welcomed the move, citing reduced odour and traffic congestion in their neighbourhoods. However, some vendors have raised concerns about customer footfall in the new location, which the corporation says it will address through signage and promotional efforts.

Broader context

The Loop Road market is part of a larger GCC initiative to create dedicated vending zones across Chennai. Similar projects are planned for other commodities, including vegetables and flowers, to streamline urban trade.

The corporation has also been working with the Tamil Nadu government to ensure that relocation is voluntary and that vendors are not left without income during the transition. Compensation or rental subsidies for early movers are under consideration, though no formal announcement has been made.

Citizen groups have praised the transparency of the process but have urged the GCC to ensure that the new market remains affordable for vendors. The success of the Loop Road project could set a template for similar relocations in other parts of the city.

The GCC is expected to release a detailed timeline for the market's inauguration within a month. All eyes will be on how many of the 122 vendors actually make the shift, and whether the new site can sustain their businesses in the long run.
