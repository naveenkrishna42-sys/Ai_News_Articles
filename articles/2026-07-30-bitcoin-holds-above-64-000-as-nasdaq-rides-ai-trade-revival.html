Bitcoin Holds Above $64,000 as Nasdaq Rides AI Trade Revival

Key points
  • Bitcoin maintained its position above $64,000, mirroring a broader tech rally.
  • The Nasdaq surged as investor confidence returned to artificial intelligence stocks.
  • Crypto and equity markets showed renewed correlation amid the AI trade comeback.

Bitcoin Stays Above $64,000 as Tech Stocks Rally

Bitcoin held firm above the $64,000 mark on Thursday, tracking a sharp uptick in the Nasdaq as artificial intelligence stocks made a strong comeback. The world's largest cryptocurrency traded in a narrow range, supported by renewed risk appetite in global markets.

The Nasdaq Composite surged during the session, driven by a rebound in major AI-linked companies. Traders pointed to a shift in sentiment after weeks of uncertainty around AI valuations. The move lifted crypto markets as well, with Bitcoin gaining over 2% in the last 24 hours.

Nasdaq Surge Lifts Crypto Sentiment

The rally in equities spilled into digital assets, breaking a period of subdued trading. Bitcoin's resilience above $64,000 comes after it briefly dipped below $63,000 earlier this week. The recovery aligns with a broader trend of institutional interest in both AI and crypto sectors.

Market observers noted that the correlation between Bitcoin and tech stocks has strengthened in recent sessions. The Nasdaq's jump of nearly 1.5% reflected a wave of buying in companies tied to artificial intelligence, from chipmakers to software firms. Crypto traders interpreted this as a positive signal for risk assets overall.

Key Levels and Market Dynamics

Bitcoin's ability to hold $64,000 is seen as crucial for near-term momentum. Analysts said a break above $65,000 could open the door to testing resistance near $66,500. On the downside, support at $63,000 remains the first line of defense.

Trading volumes on major exchanges picked up during the US session, suggesting renewed participation from institutional players. Open interest in Bitcoin futures also rose, indicating fresh capital entering the market.

The AI trade's revival comes after a period of consolidation in tech stocks. Companies reporting strong earnings in the AI segment have reignited optimism, with investors betting on long-term growth. This sentiment has indirectly boosted crypto, which often moves in tandem with high-growth tech equities.

What to Watch Next

Traders will now focus on whether Bitcoin can sustain its gains through the weekend. The next major catalyst could come from US economic data or corporate earnings reports in the AI space. A decisive move above $65,000 would signal a bullish breakout, while a drop below $63,000 could trigger a short-term correction.

For now, the market is watching for any signs of divergence between crypto and equities. If the AI rally continues, Bitcoin may find further support. But a sudden shift in risk appetite could test the current floor.
