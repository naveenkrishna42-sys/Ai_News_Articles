POCO X8 Series Lands in India: A New Benchmark for Mid-Range Battery Life

POCO has officially launched the X8 and X8 Power in India, marking one of the most aggressive battery-focused rollouts in the sub-Rs 25,000 segment. Headlining the launch is the X8 Power's 10,000mAh silicon-carbon cell, a capacity previously reserved for rugged tablets and power-bank hybrids. For Indian buyers facing long commute hours and patchy grid access in tier-2 and tier-3 cities, this is a tangible usability upgrade rather than a marketing gimmick.

Both devices ship with 1.5K resolution 120Hz AMOLED panels, IP69K-rated ingress protection on the Power variant, and Qualcomm Snapdragon 7-series silicon. The standard X8 retains a conventional 6,500mAh capacity while borrowing the same display and chipset architecture, giving POCO a clear two-pronged attack on Realme, iQOO, and Samsung's Galaxy A-series lineup.

What the 10,000mAh Battery Actually Means in Daily Use

Silicon-carbon anode chemistry allows POCO to pack higher energy density without bloating the chassis thickness beyond 9mm. Real-world projections based on comparable 6,000-7,000mAh silicon-carbon phones in 2025 suggest 12-14 hours of continuous video streaming, 8-10 hours of BGMI gameplay, and roughly three days of mixed usage before a top-up. Charging is handled via 90W wired and 30W reverse wired, meaning the X8 Power can effectively double as a power bank for TWS earbuds or a secondary phone.

Key Real-World Highlights for Buyers

  • Pros: Industry-leading 10,000mAh capacity at a sub-Rs 24,000 price point eliminates daily charger anxiety.
  • Pros: IP69K rating on the Power model withstands high-pressure hot water jets, ideal for monsoon use and outdoor workers.
  • Pros: Snapdragon 7+ Gen 3 delivers flagship-tier GPU performance, comfortably running Genshin Impact at medium-high settings.
  • Pros: 1.5K resolution offers noticeably sharper text rendering compared to standard 1080p AMOLED panels in this bracket.
  • Cons: At approximately 230g, the X8 Power is heavier than most flagships, which may fatigue single-hand users.
  • Cons: No wireless charging support, a feature increasingly common in the Rs 20,000-25,000 segment from competitors.
  • Cons: HyperOS bloatware remains a known friction point, requiring manual uninstall of 6-8 pre-loaded apps out of the box.

How the X8 Power Stacks Up Against the Realme P4s 5G

The most direct rival is the Realme P4s 5G, which offers a 7,000mAh battery and Dimensity 7300 Energy chipset at a similar Rs 21,000-22,000 bracket. POCO's counter-punch is straightforward: 3,000mAh more capacity, a faster Snapdragon SoC, and a sharper 1.5K panel. However, Realme retains an edge in software commitment with four years of OS updates versus POCO's typical two-year Android version promise.

Who Should Buy the POCO X8 Power

This phone targets three specific buyer personas: delivery riders and field workforce professionals needing multi-day endurance, mobile gamers seeking Snapdragon optimization under Rs 25,000, and frequent travellers who prioritize reverse charging capability. Users prioritizing a lighter chassis or longer software support should wait for OnePlus Nord CE 5 reviews expected later this quarter before committing funds.

Who Should Buy the Standard POCO X8

The standard X8 makes more sense for buyers wanting POCO's display and chipset strengths without the 230g weight penalty. At Rs 18,999, it undercuts the Redmi Note 15 Pro while offering comparable silicon, making it the rational pick for students and metro-based office commuters.

Both devices go on sale via Flipkart and the POCO India e-store starting September 12, 2026, with HDFC and ICICI bank instant cashback of Rs 1,500 available during the first two weeks of launch.
