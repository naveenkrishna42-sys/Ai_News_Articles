Realme Watch S2 & Buds T310: Budget Wearables Worth Buying?

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Product Deals & Offers Original source ↗
Key points
  • Realme Watch S2 brings AMOLED display and 14-day battery life for fitness tracking
  • Realme Buds T310 offers ANC and spatial audio at an affordable price point
  • Both devices work with the Realme Link app for extended customization

What's New with Realme Watch S2

Realme has expanded its wearable lineup with the Watch S2 smartwatch and Buds T310 true wireless earbuds. The Watch S2 targets fitness-conscious buyers seeking premium features without flagship pricing. It sports a circular AMOLED display that delivers sharp visuals and good outdoor readability, a notable step up from budget smartwatches that often use LCD panels.

Health tracking includes heart rate monitoring, blood oxygen measurement, and sleep tracking. The device also packs over 100 watch faces and workout modes covering running, cycling, swimming, and yoga. Realme claims up to 14 days of battery life on typical usage, which is competitive in this price band.

Realme Buds T310: Sound Quality Focus

The Buds T310 earbuds emphasize audio quality with active noise cancellation and a spatial audio mode. These features typically appear in pricier earbuds, making Realme's positioning noteworthy for budget-conscious buyers. The earbuds support multiple ANC levels, letting users adjust noise cancellation based on their environment.

Battery life reaches around 40 hours total with the charging case, including roughly 6-7 hours of playback from the buds alone. Touch controls handle playback, calls, and ANC switching. Thebuds also feature low-latency mode suitable for gaming and video watching.

Build and Comfort

  • Watch S2 uses a lightweight polymer case with interchangeable straps
  • Buds T310 comes with multiple ear tip sizes for secure fit
  • Both devices carry IPX ratings for water and dust resistance

Integration and Ecosystem

Both devices pair with the Realme Link app, available on Android and iOS. The app unlocks additional watch face downloads, workout data analysis, and notification management. Some features may work more seamlessly with Realme smartphones, though core functionality remains accessible across brands.

Tradeoffs to Consider

  • Third-party app support is limited compared to Apple Watch or Wear OS devices
  • GPS is not built-in on the Watch S2, requiring phone connectivity for route tracking
  • Buds T310 lacks wireless charging, relying solely on USB-C

Value Assessment

Realme has priced both devices competitively in the sub-₹5,000 segment. The Watch S2 competes with options from Xiaomi, boAt, and Noise. The Buds T310 faces similar rivals in the TWS space. What differentiates Realme's offering is the combination of AMOLED display on the watch and ANC on the earbuds at these price points.

Shoppers should compare current offers across platforms. Bank discounts, exchange bonuses, or cashback deals can further reduce effective costs. Checking return policies matters when buying wearables, since comfort and fit vary personally.

Buying Verdict

The Realme Watch S2 and Buds T310 make sense for users already in or open to the Realme ecosystem. The smartwatch delivers solid essentials with good battery life, while the earbuds offer premium audio features at budget pricing. Those seeking advanced health metrics or seamless cross-brand integration might explore alternatives. For straightforward fitness tracking and everyday audio, these new launches deserve consideration.
