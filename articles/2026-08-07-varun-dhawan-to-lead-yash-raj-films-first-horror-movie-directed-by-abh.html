Varun Dhawan to Lead Yash Raj Films' First Horror Movie, Directed by Abhay Pannu

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Varun Dhawan is set to star in Yash Raj Films' first-ever horror film.
  • Abhay Pannu, known for directing Rocket Boys, will helm the project.
  • The film marks a significant genre expansion for the iconic production house.

Bollywood's powerhouse studio Yash Raj Films (YRF) is venturing into the horror genre for the first time, and it has roped in one of the industry's most dependable stars to lead the charge. Varun Dhawan will headline the project, which will be directed by Abhay Pannu, the filmmaker known for the acclaimed series Rocket Boys.

The announcement, made on Friday, signals a bold new direction for YRF, a banner synonymous with grand romances and high-octane action spectacles. For Dhawan, known for his energetic performances in comedies and action films, this marks a significant pivot into the supernatural.

A New Chapter for YRF

Yash Raj Films has built a formidable legacy over decades, producing some of Hindi cinema's most iconic love stories and blockbuster entertainers. The decision to produce a horror film is a strategic expansion, tapping into a genre that has seen a resurgence in Indian cinema with films like Stree and Bhool Bhulaiyaa.

The studio's foray into horror comes at a time when audiences are increasingly embracing diverse storytelling. By teaming Dhawan with Pannu, YRF appears to be blending star power with a director known for his nuanced, character-driven work.

Abhay Pannu's Unique Directorial Vision

Abhay Pannu earned widespread critical praise for his work on Rocket Boys, the SonyLIV series that chronicled the lives of Homi Bhabha and Vikram Sarabhai. His ability to ground complex historical narratives with emotional depth has made him one of the most sought-after directors in the streaming space.

Pannu's transition to a mainstream horror film with a top-tier star is a noteworthy step. It suggests that the project may lean towards a more sophisticated, story-first approach to horror, rather than relying solely on jump scares. The combination of his grounded storytelling with Dhawan's mass appeal could offer a fresh take on the genre.

Varun Dhawan's Career Evolution

For Varun Dhawan, this project represents another ambitious choice. After a series of successful action and comedy films, he has been actively seeking roles that challenge him as an actor. His recent work in films like Bhediya, which had horror-comedy elements, hinted at his comfort with the genre.

However, a full-fledged horror film under the YRF banner is a different beast altogether. It places him in a space where he must carry the film's emotional weight while navigating the demands of the genre. Industry observers will be watching closely to see how he balances his inherent charm with the darker tones the film will likely require.

YRF has not yet confirmed the film's title, release date, or additional cast members. Details about the plot remain under wraps, though the combination of Pannu and Dhawan promises a project that could redefine the studio's portfolio. The film is expected to go into production later this year, with an eye on a major theatrical release.

As YRF steps into uncharted territory, the industry will be watching to see if this gamble pays off. With a director known for his intellect and a star known for his range, this horror film could set a new benchmark for studio-backed genre filmmaking in India.
