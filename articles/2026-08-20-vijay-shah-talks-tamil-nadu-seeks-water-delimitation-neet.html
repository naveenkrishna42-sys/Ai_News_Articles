Tamil Nadu CM Vijay meets Amit Shah to discuss delimitation, NEET, and water disputes

Tamil Nadu Chief Minister M.K. Stalin met Union Home Minister Amit Shah in New Delhi on Thursday. The meeting focused on three key issues: delimitation of parliamentary and assembly constituencies, the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) for medical and dental courses, and water disputes with Karnataka.

Tamil Nadu seeks all water from new dam at Mullaperiyar

V.D. Satheesan, a senior leader of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK), told The Hindu that Tamil Nadu is ready to accept all water from the new dam at Mullaperiyar. The dam, which is being constructed on the river Periyar, is a source of water for both Tamil Nadu and Kerala. The DMK has been a vocal supporter of the dam project, which is expected to provide water for irrigation and drinking purposes.

Southern Zonal Council meeting: T’gana Dy CM seeks protection of state’s rights in Krishna waters

At the Southern Zonal Council meeting, the Deputy Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu, O. Panneerselvam, sought protection of the state's rights in the Krishna waters. The meeting was attended by Chief Ministers from Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Puducherry. The issue of water sharing between Tamil Nadu and Karnataka has been a contentious one, with both states claiming a share in the waters of the Krishna river.

Karnataka CM D K Shivakumar says water from Mekedatu dam will be for Tamil Nadu

Karnataka Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar told ThePrint that, except for 5 TMC for drinking, water from the Mekedatu dam will be for Tamil Nadu. The Mekedatu dam, which is being constructed on the river Cauvery, is a source of water for both Tamil Nadu and Karnataka. The issue of water sharing between Tamil Nadu and Karnataka has been a contentious one, with both states claiming a share in the waters of the Cauvery river.

Southern economy could reach $10 trillion by 2047, says Naidu

Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu said that the southern economy could reach $10 trillion by 2047. He said that the southern states should work together to achieve this goal. The southern economy is expected to grow rapidly in the coming years, with a focus on sectors such as IT, manufacturing, and agriculture.

The meeting between Tamil Nadu CM Vijay and Amit Shah is expected to have a significant impact on the delimitation of parliamentary and assembly constituencies, the NEET for medical and dental courses, and the water disputes between Tamil Nadu and Karnataka. The outcome of the meeting will be closely watched by the political and business communities in the region.
