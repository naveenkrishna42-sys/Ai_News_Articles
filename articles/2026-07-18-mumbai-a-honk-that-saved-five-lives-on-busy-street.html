Mumbai: A Honk That Saved Five Lives on Busy Street

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • A persistent honk from a car alerted neighbours to a gas leak in a Mumbai building.
  • Five residents were safely evacuated before the leak could cause a fire or explosion.
  • The driver's quick thinking and the community's response averted a major tragedy.

The Honk That Broke the Silence

In a quiet Mumbai neighbourhood, a car's horn blared for several minutes on Thursday evening. Most residents dismissed it as a road rage incident or a malfunctioning alarm. But for the driver, it was a desperate attempt to warn an entire building of a potential disaster.

The driver, whose name has not been officially released, noticed a strong smell of gas near a ground floor flat in the building. When his repeated knocks on the door went unanswered, he made a quick decision. He sat in his car and pressed the horn, refusing to stop until people came out.

Neighbours Respond, Tragedy Averted

The persistent honking drew neighbours out of their homes. They found the driver pointing frantically at a flat where the gas leak was suspected. A group of residents broke open the door to find a senior citizen and four other family members unconscious but alive. The gas had been leaking from a faulty cylinder regulator.

The five were rushed to a nearby hospital and are now recovering. The building's residents later said the driver's action was the only reason the leak was detected in time. "If he hadn't honked, we would have all been in danger," a neighbour told reporters.

Gas Leak in Mumbai: A Common Hazard

Incidents of domestic gas leaks are not uncommon in Mumbai's densely packed residential areas. Local authorities have repeatedly stressed the need for regular checks of LPG cylinders and regulators. Officials have advised residents to install gas leak detectors and to act quickly if they smell gas.

In this case, the gas company and fire brigade were called in to seal the leak. The building's gas supply was temporarily cut off as a precaution. The police are investigating the cause of the faulty regulator, but no foul play is suspected.

What Happens Next

The driver's identity is expected to be formally acknowledged by the local municipal ward office in the coming days. The five survivors are expected to be discharged from hospital within 48 hours. The incident has prompted the building's residents to plan a community safety drill for gas leak emergencies next month.
