Eid-e-Milad holidays: Can you trade stocks today?

Key points
  • Stock markets observe Eid-e-Milad holiday; check trading status for BSE and NSE.
  • Holiday affects settlement of trades, not trading itself.

Stock Markets Observe Eid-e-Milad Holiday

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and the National Stock Exchange (NSE) will observe a settlement holiday on 26 August 2026, coinciding with Eid-e-Milad. This holiday will not affect trading activities but will impact the settlement of trades executed on this day. Traders and investors should be aware that any trades settled on this day will not be credited to their accounts until the market reopens on 27 August 2026.

Why This Matters

The Eid-e-Milad holiday is a significant event for the Indian stock market, as it affects the settlement of trades. This means that any trades executed on 26 August 2026 will not be settled until the market reopens on 27 August 2026. This can have a significant impact on traders and investors who are looking to buy or sell stocks on this day.
