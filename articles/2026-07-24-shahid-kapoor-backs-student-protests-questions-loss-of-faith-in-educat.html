Shahid Kapoor backs student protests, questions loss of faith in education

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Shahid Kapoor extends support to student protests led by the CJP.
  • He questions the implications of youth losing faith in education.
  • Other Bollywood stars like Kareena Kapoor and Varun Dhawan also break silence.
  • Salman Khan urges protester Wangchuk to end fast, asks students to return.
  • Imran Khan criticizes 'apolitical actors' while joining the protests.

Shahid Kapoor Voices Support for Student Demonstrations

Bollywood actor Shahid Kapoor has publicly extended his support to the ongoing student protests, raising a pointed question about the state of education in the country. In a statement that has quickly gained traction online, Kapoor asked what happens when the youth loses faith in the education system.

The protests, led by the Chief Justice of Pakistan (CJP), have drawn attention from various quarters, including the film industry. Kapoor's remarks add a prominent voice to the growing chorus demanding accountability and change.

Industry Colleagues Also Speak Out

Shahid Kapoor is not alone in breaking his silence. Actors Kareena Kapoor Khan and Varun Dhawan have also stepped forward, with one of them stating, "I can't sit with it any longer." Their statements mark a significant shift as more Hindi film stars quietly lend their support to the protesters.

Meanwhile, Salman Khan took a different approach. He urged Wangchuk, a protester on a hunger strike, to end his fast and asked students to return home. "Sonam, it's done, bro," Khan reportedly said, attempting to de-escalate the situation.

Imran Khan Joins the Fray

Adding another layer to the discourse, cricketer-turned-politician Imran Khan slammed what he called "apolitical actors" while joining the CJP-led protests. He argued that true art inherently carries the artist's point of view. "True art is going to have an individual artist point of view embedded within it," Khan stated, challenging those who claim artists should stay out of politics.

His comments have sparked debate about the role of public figures in social movements. The protests, which have drawn students and activists, continue to gain momentum across multiple cities.

What Happens When Faith in Education Fades?

Kapoor's core question resonates with many observers. The actor's concern highlights a broader anxiety about the purpose and direction of education in India. With rising unemployment and a perceived disconnect between academic curricula and job markets, the youth's trust in traditional pathways is under strain.

Officials have not yet confirmed any formal response to the protests. The government has maintained a cautious stance, urging dialogue while warning against disruptions.

As the demonstrations continue, the film industry's involvement signals a new phase in public engagement. Whether this leads to concrete policy discussions or remains a symbolic show of solidarity will become clearer in the coming days.
