Marriott Bonvoy: A Flexible Currency With Hidden Traps

Marriott Bonvoy points are among the most versatile loyalty currencies, transferable to over 40 airlines. But not all transfers deliver equal value. Based on our analysis of redemption rates, fuel surcharges, and award availability, we rank the best and worst partners. Whether you're chasing a business-class seat to Europe or a domestic economy hop, choosing the right airline can double your effective redemption value.

How Marriott Transfers Work

Marriott transfers points to airlines at a 3:1 ratio, with a 5,000-mile bonus for every 60,000 points transferred. That means 60,000 Marriott points become 25,000 airline miles (20,000 base + 5,000 bonus). Transfers typically process within 24-48 hours, though some partners take up to a week. Crucially, Marriott does not impose fuel surcharges—but the airline might when you book an award ticket. Always check carrier-imposed fees before transferring.

Pros and Cons of Marriott Transfers

  • Pros: Flexibility to top off airline accounts; 5,000-mile bonus; no Marriott-imposed surcharges; access to premium cabins.
  • Cons: 3:1 ratio is poor compared to Amex or Chase (1:1); transfers are irreversible; airline surcharges can negate value; devaluations are common.

Real-World Redemption Example

Suppose you want a business-class ticket from New York to Tokyo. ANA charges 55,000 miles round-trip via Virgin Atlantic (a Marriott partner). That requires 132,000 Marriott points. The cash price for that ticket is often $4,000+, giving a value of over 3 cents per point—excellent. In contrast, transferring 60,000 Marriott points to British Airways for a 25,000-mile award to London incurs $500 in surcharges, yielding less than 1 cent per point. The lesson: always calculate the cash co-pay before transferring.

Strategy: When to Transfer (and When Not To)

Use Marriott transfers only when you have a specific award in mind. For hotel stays, Marriott points are often better redeemed for 5th-night-free awards. For airlines, target partners with no surcharges and low award rates. Avoid transferring during speculative periods; points are safer in your Marriott account. With careful planning, Marriott transfers can unlock lie-flat seats to Asia or Europe for a fraction of the cash price.
