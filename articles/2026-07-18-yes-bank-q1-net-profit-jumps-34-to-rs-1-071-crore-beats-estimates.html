Yes Bank Q1 Net Profit Jumps 34% to Rs 1,071 Crore, Beats Estimates

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Net profit surged 34% year-on-year to Rs 1,071 crore for Q1FY27.
  • Net interest income (NII) advanced 18%, driven by strong loan growth.
  • Asset quality improved with lower NPAs; stock jumped 34% on results day.

Yes Bank has kicked off the new financial year on a strong note, reporting a net profit of Rs 1,071 crore for the first quarter of FY27. That marks a 34% jump compared to the same period last year, comfortably beating market expectations.

The private sector lender's performance comes amid a broader recovery in the banking sector, with Yes Bank specifically benefiting from a sustained improvement in its core operations. The bank's net interest income, a key measure of lending profitability, rose 18% year-on-year.

Loan Growth and Net Interest Margin

The bank's loan book expanded at a healthy clip during the quarter. Management attributed the growth to increased demand across retail, small business, and corporate segments. The bank has been focusing on building a granular loan portfolio, reducing its reliance on large-ticket corporate advances.

Net interest margin, which had been under pressure in previous quarters, showed signs of stabilisation. The 18% growth in NII suggests the bank is managing to price loans competitively while keeping deposit costs in check.

Asset Quality Sees Marked Improvement

Yes Bank's asset quality metrics continued to improve. Gross non-performing assets (NPAs) as a percentage of total loans fell sequentially, while the provision coverage ratio remained robust. The bank has been aggressively cleaning up its balance sheet over the past few years, and the latest numbers indicate that process is paying off.

Fresh slippages were contained, and the bank reported higher recoveries and upgrades during the quarter. Analysts tracking the stock have noted that asset quality is now closer to pre-crisis levels, which reduces the risk of large one-time provisions denting future profits.

Market Reaction and Investor Sentiment

The market rewarded the earnings with a sharp rally. Shares of Yes Bank surged 34% on the day of the results announcement, reflecting investor relief and optimism about the bank's turnaround trajectory. The stock has been volatile over the past year, but this quarter's performance has bolstered confidence in the management's execution.

Brokerages have started revising their target prices upward, with many highlighting the bank's improving return on assets and return on equity. The strong quarterly numbers also come at a time when the banking sector is navigating a tight liquidity environment, making Yes Bank's performance stand out.

What the Numbers Reveal

The Rs 1,071-crore net profit represents a 32.5% to 34% increase over various comparative bases, depending on the metric used. Net interest income growth of 18% was driven by both higher advances and better yield management. Other income, including fees and commissions, also contributed positively.

The bank's capital adequacy ratio remains above regulatory requirements, giving it room to pursue further growth without immediately needing to raise fresh capital. Cost-to-income ratios have also improved, indicating better operational efficiency.

Yes Bank has been on a recovery path since its near-collapse in 2020, when a rescue plan involving State Bank of India and other investors was put in place. The current management, led by CEO Prashant Kumar, has focused on stabilising deposits, rebuilding the loan book, and tightening credit underwriting standards.

The next few quarters will test whether Yes Bank can sustain this momentum. With the bank now consistently delivering double-digit profit growth and improving asset quality, the focus will shift to maintaining loan growth without taking on excessive risk. Investors will also watch for any signs of margin compression as the interest rate cycle evolves.
