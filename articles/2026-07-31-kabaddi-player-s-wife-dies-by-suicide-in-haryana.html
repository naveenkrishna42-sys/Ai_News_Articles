Kabaddi Player's Wife Dies by Suicide in Haryana

📅 2026-07-31 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • 23-year-old woman, wife of international kabaddi player, found dead in Haryana
  • Police have initiated inquest proceedings under relevant legal provisions
  • Family members have alleged harassment, though no formal complaint has been registered yet

Incident Reported in Haryana Village

A 23-year-old woman, married to an international kabaddi player, died by suicide at her residence in Haryana on Friday. The incident has sent shockwaves through the local community, with neighbours and relatives gathering at the spot as news spread.

Police officials confirmed that the woman was found dead in her home in the afternoon. Her husband, who plays kabaddi at the international level, was not present at the time of the incident, sources said.

Police Investigation Underway

Authorities have registered a case under Section 174 of the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC), which deals with suspicious deaths. The body has been sent for post-mortem examination to determine the exact cause of death.

Initial reports suggest the woman died by hanging. However, investigators are yet to record statements from family members and neighbours in detail. A senior police officer said that all angles are being examined, and no conclusions have been drawn so far.

Family Alleges Harassment

According to preliminary information, the woman's family has alleged that she was subjected to harassment by her in-laws. These allegations have not been formally lodged as a complaint, but police have taken note of them.

Investigators are also checking call records and messages to piece together the events leading up to the tragedy. The woman had reportedly been married for a few years and had no children.

Kabaddi Player's Identity Withheld

The identity of the kabaddi player has not been officially disclosed by the police. However, local sources have identified him as a known figure in the sport, having represented India in international tournaments.

His family has denied any wrongdoing, stating that the allegations are baseless. They have extended their cooperation to the investigating authorities.

Community in Mourning

The village where the incident took place is in a sombre mood, with residents expressing grief over the young woman's death. Several local leaders have visited the family to offer condolences.

This incident has once again highlighted the pressures faced by young married women in rural Haryana, where cases of domestic disputes and suicides have been reported in the past.

The post-mortem report is expected to be released within a few days, which could provide further clarity on the circumstances of the death.

As the investigation progresses, police are likely to question the husband and his relatives in detail. Any formal complaint from the woman's family could lead to the registration of a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code.

Officials have urged the public to avoid speculation and allow the legal process to take its course.
