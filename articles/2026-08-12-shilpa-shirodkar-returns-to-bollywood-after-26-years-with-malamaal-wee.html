Shilpa Shirodkar returns to Bollywood after 26 years with Malamaal Weekly 2

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Shilpa Shirodkar returns to acting after a 26-year hiatus
  • She will feature in Malamaal Weekly 2, a sequel to the 2006 comedy
  • Details about her role and the film's release are awaited

Return After a Long Hiatus

After more than two and a half decades away from the arc lights, actress Shilpa Shirodkar is set to make a grand return to Bollywood. She has been roped in for Malamaal Weekly 2, the sequel to the 2006 comedy that starred Paresh Rawal and Om Puri.

The news, confirmed exclusively by Bollywood Hungama, has sparked excitement among fans who remember her as one of the leading actresses of the late 1980s and early 1990s.

A Career Cut Short

Shirodkar, who debuted with Kahan Hai Kanoon in 1989, went on to deliver hits like Hum, Khoon Ka Karz, and Aankhen. Her last major appearance was in 2000, after which she stepped away from the industry to focus on her family. Her departure was sudden, leaving many of her admirers wondering if she would ever return.

Now, 26 years later, that question has been answered.

What We Know About Malamaal Weekly 2

Details about Shirodkar's role are still under wraps. The original film, directed by Priyadarshan, revolved around a lottery-winning village and its chaotic aftermath. The sequel is expected to retain the comic flavour, though the cast and plot specifics have not been fully revealed yet.

Industry insiders suggest that Shirodkar's character will be pivotal, but the makers have stayed quiet about the full ensemble. It remains unclear whether any of the original cast members will reprise their roles.

A Nostalgic Wave

Shirodkar's comeback is part of a larger trend of beloved stars returning to the screen after long gaps. Her fan base, which grew up watching her in masala entertainers, is likely to welcome her warmly.

Trade analysts point out that such comebacks often generate strong initial curiosity. If the film delivers on its comic promise, Shirodkar could find a new audience alongside the old.

What's Next

An official announcement from the production team is expected shortly. Until then, fans will have to wait for a release date and a first look.

For Shirodkar, this is more than a film—it's a second innings. Whether it marks a full-fledged return or a one-off appearance remains to be seen.
