Introduction of Vande Mataram at Red Fort

This year's 80th Independence Day celebrations will see a first, with Vande Mataram being sung from the ramparts of the Red Fort. The Defence Secretary has confirmed that 150 years of Vande Mataram will be a major highlight of the celebrations.

Theme of Independence Day

The theme of this year's Independence Day is 'Yuva Shakti for Viksit Bharat@2047', which translates to 'Youth power for a developed India by 2047'. This theme is expected to be reflected in the various events and celebrations that will take place across the country.

Celebrations and Events

According to officials, the celebrations will honour 150 years of Vande Mataram, which is a national song that holds great significance in Indian culture. The song is expected to be sung by a large group of people from the ramparts of the Red Fort, marking a first in the history of Independence Day celebrations.

Significance of Vande Mataram

Vande Mataram is a national song that was written by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay in 1876. It was adopted as the national song of India in 1950 and has since been an integral part of Indian culture and heritage. The song is a tribute to the motherland and is considered a symbol of nationalism and patriotism.
