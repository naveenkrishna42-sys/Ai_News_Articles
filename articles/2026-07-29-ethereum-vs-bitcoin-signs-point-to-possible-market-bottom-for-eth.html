Ethereum vs Bitcoin: Signs Point to Possible Market Bottom for ETH

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ethereum has underperformed Bitcoin for months, leading to speculation about a market bottom.
  • Key metrics like the ETH/BTC ratio and network activity are being closely watched.
  • Analysts are divided on whether the bottom is in or if further downside is possible.

The ETH/BTC Ratio Nears Critical Levels

The long-running debate between Ethereum and Bitcoin has taken a sharp turn in recent weeks. Ethereum's price relative to Bitcoin has been sliding, pushing the ETH/BTC ratio to levels not seen in years. This has led many traders and analysts to ask one question: has Ethereum finally reached a bottom against its larger rival?

The ratio, which measures how many Bitcoins one Ether can buy, has been in a steady downtrend. For investors holding ETH, this has meant consistent underperformance. But some market watchers believe the current zone could mark a turning point.

On-Chain and Technical Signals

Several on-chain metrics are flashing potential reversal signals. Network activity on Ethereum, measured by daily active addresses and transaction counts, remains robust despite the price weakness. This divergence between falling price and steady usage often precedes a bottom.

Technical analysts point to the ETH/BTC chart forming a potential double bottom or bullish divergence on the relative strength index. These patterns, while not guarantees, have historically preceded significant rallies in ETH against BTC.

However, caution is warranted. Macroeconomic headwinds, including regulatory uncertainty and competition from other smart contract platforms, continue to weigh on sentiment. The broader crypto market's direction also plays a decisive role.

Mixed Views Among Analysts

The analyst community remains split. Some argue that Ethereum's fundamental advantages — its vast DeFi ecosystem, upcoming network upgrades, and institutional adoption — make the current undervaluation a buying opportunity. They contend that once the market turns, ETH could outperform BTC sharply.

Others are more cautious. They point to Bitcoin's growing dominance as a store of value, particularly in uncertain economic times. If risk aversion persists, Bitcoin may continue to attract the lion's share of capital, leaving Ethereum to languish.

Neither camp has declared a definitive bottom. The consensus is that the next few weeks will be critical in determining the direction.

What to Watch Next

The key for traders will be whether ETH can break a series of lower highs against BTC. A sustained move above recent resistance levels would be the first credible sign that the bottom is in. A failure to hold current support could extend the downtrend.

Macro catalysts, such as Federal Reserve policy shifts or major regulatory decisions, will also influence the outcome. For now, Ethereum sits at a crossroads, and the market is watching closely for the next move.
