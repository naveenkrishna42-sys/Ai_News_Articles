Drishyam 3 Day 1 Box Office: The Rs 28.2 Crore Challenge

Ajay Devgn returns as Vijay Salgaonkar in Drishyam 3, the third instalment of one of Hindi cinema's most reliable theatrical franchises. The benchmark for the film's opening day is no longer internal. Mirzapur: The Movie has set the ceiling for 2026 with a Rs 25.4 crore net debut, and trade trackers now calculate that Drishyam 3 must deliver Rs 28.2 crore net on Day 1 to reclaim Devgn's seasonal throne.

This figure is not arbitrary. It accounts for inflation indexing, the Rs 70 average ticket price in Tier 1 multiplexes, and a 12 percent premium that sequels typically command over the previous entry. Drishyam 2 opened at Rs 15.38 crore net in 2022. Adjusted for ticket price inflation and the 2026 holiday calendar window, a Rs 28.2 crore target represents the precise ceiling Devgn must clear.

The Benchmark Set By Mirzapur: The Movie

Mirzapur: The Movie leveraged a built-in OTT audience of 40 million Prime Video subscribers and converted them into footfalls through aggressive city walk-ins and discounted Tuesday previews. Its Rs 25.4 crore net Day 1 places it ahead of Bhool Bhulaiyaa 3 (Rs 22 crore) and Fighter (Rs 22.5 crore) in the 2026 calendar.

How The Opening Day Math Works

For Drishyam 3 to hit Rs 28.2 crore net, it needs roughly 4.8 lakh admissions across India at a blended ticket price of Rs 58. Multiplex chains PVR and INOX have allocated an average of 18 shows per property for the release, up from 14 for Drishyam 2, signalling strong exhibitor confidence in advance bookings.

Ajay Devgn's Personal Box Office History

Devgn has rarely opened big, but he has opened steady. His top three openers before inflation adjustment are Singham Again (Rs 29 crore in 2024), Raid (Rs 25 crore in 2018), and Drishyam 2 (Rs 15.38 crore in 2022). Adjusted to 2026 ticket rates, Raid translates to roughly Rs 33 crore net. Singham Again in current rates exceeds Rs 32 crore net.

Will Drishyam 3 Cross The Threshold?

Three factors suggest the film will land in the Rs 22-26 crore net range on Day 1 rather than the Rs 28.2 crore aspirational figure. First, the Drishyam brand, while beloved, does not have the same pre-release hype as Singham Again, which rode the Rohit Shetty factor. Second, weekday release slots traditionally clip Day 1 collections by 15-18 percent compared to Friday releases. Third, the absence of a chartbuster soundtrack reduces walk-in footfalls from Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets.

Real-World Box Office Outlook

  • Day 1 realistic band: Rs 22-26 crore net
  • Friday to Sunday weekend trajectory: Could escalate to Rs 75-80 crore net
  • First week total projection: Rs 95-105 crore net
  • Threat to Mirzapur: Possible by Day 3, but Day 1 remains a stretch

Key Real-World Highlights

  • Strength: Devgn has cracked the Rs 25 crore net opening twice in his career, proving the muscle is available
  • Strength: Franchise recall from Drishyam 2 and the original 2015 film delivers a built-in family audience
  • Risk: No blockbuster music single to drive mass-market pre-bookings
  • Risk: Direct comparison with Mirzapur sets a higher bar than typical genre films
  • Verdict: Likely to beat Mirzapur eventually, but Day 1 crown stays elusive unless advance bookings surprise positively
