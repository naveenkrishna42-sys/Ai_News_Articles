Protest at Jantar Mantar demands caste-based reservation overhaul

📅 2026-08-22 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Protests at Jantar Mantar call for reservation to be based on economic status rather than caste
  • Delhi Police detain over 50 protesters; Rapid Action Force personnel deployed at the site
  • Demonstrators argue reservation must target the poor regardless of caste background

Delhi witnesses large-scale protest over reservation policy

Hundreds of demonstrators gathered at Jantar Mantar in the national capital on Saturday, calling for a complete overhaul of India's caste-based reservation system. The protest, organized under the 'Reservation Hatao' banner, saw participants demand that reservation benefits be extended based on economic criteria rather than caste identity.

The demonstration drew a substantial crowd, with protesters carrying banners and raising slogans as they assembled near the historic site. Police maintained a heavy presence throughout the day as the gathering swelled.

Police deployment and detentions

Delhi Police responded to the protest by deploying Rapid Action Force (RAF) personnel at Jantar Mantar. Authorities detained several protesters as the demonstration progressed, with reports indicating over 50 individuals were taken into custody. officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of arrests.

The police presence was visible across the protest site, with security forces managing crowd movement and ensuring law and order was maintained during the demonstration.

What the protesters want

Demonstrators argued that reservation policies should prioritize economic hardship over caste affiliation. Speakers at the gathering emphasized that benefits must reach genuinely disadvantaged individuals regardless of their social category.

Protesters contended that the current system no longer addresses genuine poverty and instead benefits those from relatively well-off backgrounds within reserved categories. The demonstrators called for a merit-based approach combined with economic affirmative action.

Government response awaited

Authorities have yet to issue an official statement addressing the protesters' demands. The demonstration adds to an ongoing national debate about reservation policies and their effectiveness in addressing inequality.

Political parties have historically taken varying positions on reservation reforms, making any policy change a sensitive political matter. Officials have indicated they will review the protesters' demands through established channels.

What's next

Observers will watch whether the protest sparks broader political discussions on reservation policy ahead of upcoming electoral cycles. The detained protesters are expected to be released after standard legal procedures. Any concrete reforms would require legislative action and political consensus.
