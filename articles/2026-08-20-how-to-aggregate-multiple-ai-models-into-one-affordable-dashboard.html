How to Aggregate Multiple AI Models into One Affordable Dashboard

📅 2026-08-20 📂 AI Tips & Tools Original source ↗
Key points
  • Consolidate multiple AI subscriptions into a single interface.
  • Compare responses from different models side-by-side.
  • Reduce monthly overhead by switching to aggregator services.

Why Individual Subscriptions Drain Your Budget

Most AI users pay ₹1,600 to ₹2,000 per month for each major model, such as ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, or Gemini Advanced. If you use all three, your monthly expenses can quickly exceed ₹5,000. Aggregator platforms offer a different approach by providing API-based access to these models within one interface for a single, often lower, recurring fee or lifetime license.

These tools act as a bridge. They route your prompts to the model of your choice while maintaining your chat history in one central location. This setup allows you to switch between models instantly to verify facts or compare creative outputs.

Setting Up Your Unified AI Dashboard

To consolidate your workflows and reduce your subscription costs, follow these steps to set up an aggregator platform:

  1. Choose a reputable aggregator: Search for platforms that offer model-agnostic access. Ensure they support the specific LLMs you rely on, such as GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and Gemini 1.5 Pro.
  2. Verify the pricing structure: Look for "lifetime" or "pay-as-you-go" options rather than individual monthly subscriptions. Check if the price includes a set number of messages or if it requires you to bring your own API keys.
  3. Import your preferences: Once you create an account, navigate to the settings or 'Model' selector dropdown. Enable the specific models you use most frequently.
  4. Run a side-by-side comparison: Open a split-screen view if the platform supports it. Paste the same prompt into both ChatGPT and Claude simultaneously to determine which model performs better for your specific task.
  5. Cancel redundant subscriptions: Only after you have verified that the aggregator provides the same output quality and speed, log into your original provider accounts and cancel your recurring billing.

Important Considerations for Power Users

Aggregators often rely on API calls to interact with these models. While this is cost-effective, you might lose access to certain native features like file uploads, custom GPTs, or specific browser plugins found in the standalone apps. Check the aggregator's documentation to see which native features are supported before migrating your entire workflow.

This approach is ideal for casual users or researchers who need access to multiple models for occasional tasks and want to save on monthly costs. It is not the right tool for power users who rely heavily on ecosystem-specific features like data analysis tools, custom training, or dedicated image generation integrations found in the official apps.
