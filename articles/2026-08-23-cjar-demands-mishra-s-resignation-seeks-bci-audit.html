CJAR co-chairman demands Mishra's resignation within 15 days

Co-chairman of the Committee of Junior Advocates (CJAR), Justice Anil Kumar, has demanded the resignation of Manan Kumar Mishra, the current chairman of the Bar Council of India (BCI), within 15 days. Kumar cited eight allegations against Mishra, including financial irregularities and alleged political interference.

CJAR seeks audit of BCI accounts

In addition to demanding Mishra's resignation, CJAR has also requested an audit of the BCI accounts. This comes after reports of financial mismanagement and irregularities within the BCI. The committee has expressed concerns about the transparency and accountability of the BCI under Mishra's leadership.

Mishra refuses to step down, alleges political interference

Manan Kumar Mishra, the BCI chairman, has refused to step down despite the CJAR's demands. He has reiterated his stance, stating that he will not resign. Mishra has also alleged political interference in the matter, suggesting that the CJAR's demands are politically motivated.

Mishra's plea in Supreme Court seeks his removal as BCI chief

Manan Kumar Mishra has filed a plea in the Supreme Court seeking his removal as the BCI chairman. The plea comes as part of the ongoing controversy surrounding Mishra's leadership. The Supreme Court is yet to rule on the matter.

What happens next?

The BCI will likely convene a general council meeting to discuss the CJAR's demands and Mishra's refusal to step down. The outcome of this meeting will be closely watched, as it could potentially lead to Mishra's removal from his position. The Supreme Court's ruling on Mishra's plea for his removal will also be a significant development in this ongoing controversy.
