Squad announced for World Cup campaign

Hockey India has named a 20-member squad for the upcoming FIH Hockey Men's World Cup 2026. The announcement was made official on Tuesday. The team will be led by star drag-flicker Harmanpreet Singh, who continues as captain.

The selection comes after months of evaluation and training camps. The squad includes a blend of seasoned campaigners and fresh faces. The selectors have focused on building a balanced unit capable of competing against the world's best.

Harmanpreet Singh to lead the charge

Harmanpreet Singh will captain the side for yet another major tournament. The defender has been a mainstay in the Indian setup for years. His ability to score from penalty corners makes him a key weapon for the team.

Vice-captain duties have not been officially confirmed yet. The team management is expected to finalize leadership roles closer to the tournament. The squad features several players who were part of the bronze medal-winning team in the last World Cup.

The team is expected to undergo a rigorous training camp before departing for the World Cup. Friendly matches against top-ranked teams are also being planned. The coaching staff will work on fine-tuning strategies and set-piece executions.
