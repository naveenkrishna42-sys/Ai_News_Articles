CMF Clip Pro open-ear earbuds launched by Nothing at aggressive price

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • CMF Clip Pro earbuds launched on August 4 with open-ear clip design
  • Support LDAC codec for high-resolution wireless audio
  • Unique charging case adds to value proposition; price undercuts rivals

CMF expands audio lineup with Clip Pro

Nothing's budget-focused sub-brand CMF has officially taken the wraps off its latest audio offering, the Clip Pro open-ear earbuds. The launch, confirmed on August 4, brings a fresh form factor to the company's growing portfolio of minimalist gadgets. The earbuds are positioned as an affordable entry into the open-ear category, which has been gaining traction among users who prefer ambient awareness during workouts or commutes.

The Clip Pro features a clip-on design that attaches securely to the ear without inserting into the ear canal. This approach aims to combine comfort with situational awareness, a key selling point for runners and cyclists. The design language stays true to CMF's signature clean, industrial aesthetic, with a matte finish and subtle branding.

Audio specs and codec support

Under the hood, the Clip Pro supports LDAC, Sony's high-resolution audio codec, which allows for transmission of lossless-quality audio over Bluetooth. This is a notable inclusion at this price point, as LDAC is typically reserved for more premium earbuds. The open-ear driver configuration is tuned to deliver balanced sound while minimising sound leakage, according to early reports.

Battery life figures have not been officially detailed yet, but industry expectations suggest a competitive runtime with the charging case. The case itself has drawn attention for its unconventional design, which has been described in early previews as an 'awesome bonus' that adds practicality without bulk. CMF has not confirmed fast-charging specifications, but the overall package appears geared toward everyday reliability.

Pricing strategy and market impact

The most striking element of the launch is the price. While the official India pricing is yet to be formally announced in rupees, the global price point has been positioned significantly lower than comparable open-ear models from established brands. Tech analysts are already calling it a potential 'bargain of the year' in this segment, given the LDAC support and build quality.

This aggressive pricing puts pressure on competitors like boAt, Noise, and even Nothing's own flagship audio products. CMF's strategy appears to be democratising premium features, a move that has worked well for the sub-brand since its inception. The Clip Pro is expected to retail in India under Rs 3,000, though this figure is speculative until official confirmation.

Availability and rollout

CMF has confirmed that the Clip Pro will be available through major e-commerce platforms and select retail outlets. The initial rollout is expected to cover key markets including India, the UK, and Southeast Asia. Colour options are likely to include the brand's characteristic black and a lighter variant, though the full lineup has not been detailed.

Early hands-on impressions from tech reviewers highlight the secure fit and lightweight feel, with some noting that the open-ear design may not suit bass-heavy listeners. However, for those prioritising comfort and awareness, the Clip Pro appears to hit a sweet spot. The unique charging case has also been praised for its thoughtful engineering, adding a layer of convenience that is rare in this price bracket.

As CMF continues to expand beyond phones and accessories, the Clip Pro signals a clear intent to capture a larger share of the audio market. With pricing that undercuts many rivals, the earbuds could become a popular choice for budget-conscious consumers who refuse to compromise on features. The coming weeks will reveal how the market responds, and whether CMF can sustain its momentum with further product launches in the audio segment.
