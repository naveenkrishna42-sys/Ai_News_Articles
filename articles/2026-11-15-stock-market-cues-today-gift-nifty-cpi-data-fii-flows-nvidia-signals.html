What will drive today's session

Indian equities open with a familiar checklist: GIFT Nifty's overnight signal, the CPI inflation print, FII and DII flow data, and the mood in global technology stocks. Separating confirmed data from social-media noise is the first risk-management step for intraday traders.

GIFT Nifty and overnight global signals

The GIFT Nifty premium or discount against the previous Nifty 50 close is the cleanest early read on the opening gap. A premium of roughly 50-100 points usually points to a positive start, but the signal holds only when backed by a firm Wall Street close, stable US Treasury yields, and a quiet dollar index. A strong GIFT Nifty against a weak Dow often fades by 10:00 IST.

CPI inflation: the domestic trigger

The Consumer Price Index release is the main macro event. Track headline CPI year-on-year, food inflation, and the core reading that excludes food and fuel. A print above the RBI's 4% medium-term target weakens rate-cut expectations and pressures banks, autos and realty, while a soft print supports rate-sensitive names and the 10-year G-Sec. Pair it with IIP or factory output data for the industrial demand picture.

FII, DII flows and the currency

Net FII buying or selling in the cash market, plus F&O positioning, tells you whether moves have institutional backing. Sustained FII outflows capped by strong DII buying typically produces range-bound, stock-specific action rather than an index breakout. Watch USD-INR and Brent crude as well, since a weaker rupee raises imported-input costs for oil marketing and metals.

Nvidia and AI sentiment: verify before you trade

Headlines referencing Nvidia and CEO Jensen Huang must be checked against official company filings and regulator disclosures; unverified claims of executive departures have circulated widely and should not drive position sizing. The real, tradable drivers are quarterly results, data-centre revenue, forward guidance, AI capital-expenditure commentary, and US export-control updates. These move Nifty IT, global semiconductor stocks, and domestic AI-linked names.
