Paytm, LIC, Mazagon Dock, BEL & Varun Beverages: Buy, Hold or Sell?

Key points
  • Paytm’s rapid user growth is offset by thin margins and volatile cash flow.
  • LIC’s stable premium inflow and rising fund yields support a buy rating.
  • Mazagon Dock and BEL face order‑book delays, making hold the prudent stance.

Market Context

Indian equity investors have been juggling higher inflation, tighter credit and a shift toward defensive plays. The five stocks covered span fintech, insurance, defence and consumer beverages, offering a cross‑section of growth versus stability. Each security’s price action over the past six months reflects both sector‑specific catalysts and macro‑level risk appetite.

1. Paytm (One97 Communications Ltd.)

Paytm remains the most watched fintech name after its 2024 IPO. Monthly active users hit 55 million in Q2 2026, a 12% YoY rise, yet revenue per user slipped to ₹210, indicating pricing pressure. The firm’s net loss narrowed to ₹1,080 crore, but cash burn of ₹2,350 crore still strains the balance sheet. The current price‑to‑sales (P/S) ratio of 4.1 exceeds the sector median of 3.2, suggesting a premium that must be justified by sustained merchant adoption.

2. LIC (Life Insurance Corp of India)

LIC’s premium collection crossed ₹4.2 trillion in FY 2026, up 9% YoY, driven by higher penetration in tier‑2 cities. Investment yield improved to 7.4% after reallocating ₹120 billion into high‑quality corporate bonds. With a price‑to‑earnings (P/E) of 12.5, the stock trades below the historical average of 14, offering a margin of safety. The company’s strong capital adequacy ratio (252%) further underpins its defensive profile.

3. Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Ltd.

Mazagon Dock secured two submarine contracts worth ₹45 billion, but delivery timelines have slipped amid supply‑chain bottlenecks. Order‑book growth slowed to 3% YoY, and operating margin fell to 6.2% in Q2 2026 from 8.1% a year earlier. The stock’s P/B ratio of 1.4 is modest, yet earnings volatility makes a hold recommendation prudent until order execution improves.

4. BEL (Bharat Electronics Ltd.)

BEL posted a 5% rise in defence exports, buoyed by a new radar system contract with the Indian Navy. However, domestic procurement cycles remain unpredictable, limiting revenue predictability. The current dividend yield of 2.3% is attractive, but a P/E of 18.9 sits above the defence sector average of 16, indicating limited upside without a clear earnings catalyst.

5. Varun Beverages Ltd.

Varun Beverages, the largest PepsiCo bottler in India, delivered a 14% revenue jump, driven by new product launches and rural penetration. EBITDA margin expanded to 23.5%, the best in its five‑year history. The stock trades at a forward P/E of 15.2, a slight discount to peers, making it a solid buy for investors seeking consumer growth exposure.

Final Recommendations

  • Paytm: Hold – growth in user base is real, but margin compression and high valuation limit upside.
  • LIC: Buy – stable premium inflow, improving investment returns and attractive valuation create a defensive win.
  • Mazagon Dock: Hold – order‑book uncertainty and thin margins warrant patience.
  • BEL: Hold – decent dividend but earnings multiple is stretched; wait for a clear order catalyst.
  • Varun Beverages: Buy – strong top‑line growth, expanding margins and a modest valuation support upside.
