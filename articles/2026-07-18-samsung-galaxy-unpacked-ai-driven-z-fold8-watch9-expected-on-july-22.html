Samsung Galaxy Unpacked: AI-driven Z Fold8, Watch9 expected on July 22

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Evan Blass leak reveals Z Fold8, Z Fold8 Ultra, and Galaxy Watch9 specs ahead of July 22 event
  • Samsung officially announced Flex Titanium technology for foldable displays
  • AI-driven features expected to be central to the new device lineup

Samsung is gearing up for its next Galaxy Unpacked event on July 22, and leaks suggest the company will unveil its most ambitious AI-driven devices yet. The spotlight is expected to fall on the Galaxy Z Fold8, a new Z Fold8 Ultra variant, and the Galaxy Watch9.

Tipster Evan Blass has shared detailed renders and specifications for the upcoming devices. The Z Fold8 and Z Fold8 Ultra are reported to feature larger screens and thinner bezels. The Ultra model may include a more advanced camera system and a bigger battery.

Flex Titanium display takes center stage

Samsung has already confirmed one key technology for the foldable lineup: Flex Titanium. The company announced the material earlier this week, describing it as a durable yet lightweight alloy for foldable displays.

The Flex Titanium display is expected to reduce crease visibility and improve scratch resistance. Samsung says the material has passed rigorous durability tests, including 200,000 folds. This could address long-standing concerns about foldable screen longevity.

AI features across the ecosystem

Industry watchers predict Samsung will integrate Galaxy AI more deeply into the new devices. The Z Fold8 series is rumored to include real-time translation for calls and messages, AI-powered photo editing, and adaptive battery management that learns user habits.

The Galaxy Watch9 is also expected to benefit from AI-driven health monitoring. Leaks suggest the watch will offer advanced sleep analysis, stress detection, and a new electrocardiogram feature. Samsung may position the watch as a proactive health companion rather than a passive tracker.

What else could debut at Unpacked

Beyond the foldables and smartwatch, Samsung might showcase new Galaxy Buds or a tablet. Some reports hint at a refreshed Galaxy Tab S10 series with AI-enhanced productivity tools, but details remain unconfirmed.

The event comes as Samsung faces stiff competition from Chinese rivals like Honor and OnePlus in the foldable market. AI differentiation could help Samsung maintain its lead, especially in premium segments.

Pricing for the Z Fold8 and Z Fold8 Ultra has not been officially disclosed. Analysts expect the Ultra model to cost around Rs 1.5 lakh, while the standard Z Fold8 may start at Rs 1.3 lakh. The Galaxy Watch9 is likely to be priced competitively against the Apple Watch Series 10.

Samsung has not commented on the leaked renders or specifications. The company typically refrains from addressing pre-event rumors. However, the official Flex Titanium announcement indicates that Samsung is ready to showcase its latest engineering achievements.

The July 22 event will be livestreamed on Samsung's website and YouTube channel. The company is expected to open pre-orders immediately after the keynote, with retail availability in August.
