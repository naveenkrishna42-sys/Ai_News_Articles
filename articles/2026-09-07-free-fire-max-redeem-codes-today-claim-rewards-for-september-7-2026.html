Step-by-Step Guide: How to Redeem Rewards Safely

Navigating the redemption process requires caution, as third-party phishing sites often mimic official Garena portals to steal player credentials. To claim your rewards securely, follow this verified protocol:

First, visit the official Garena Rewards Redemption Site (reward.ff.garena.com). Do not use unofficial links from social media platforms. Second, log in using your registered game account credentials, such as Facebook, Google, VK, Apple ID, Huawei ID, or Twitter. Guest accounts are ineligible for rewards; players must link their accounts in the game settings beforehand.

Third, copy and paste the active 12 or 16-character code into the text field and click "Confirm". If successful, the rewards will be dispatched directly to the in-game mail section within 24 hours. If an error message appears stating the code is expired or invalid in your region, the claim limit has likely been exceeded.
