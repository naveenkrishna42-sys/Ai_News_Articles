Market Outlook: Flat Opening Amid Geopolitical Uncertainty

Indian equity markets are set to open with minimal movement on Tuesday, Sep 8, as traders weigh the fallout from escalating US‑Iran tensions. The Sensex is projected to trade within a narrow band around 73,200 points, while the Nifty 50 hovers near 23,800. Historical data shows that heightened geopolitical risk often curtails intraday swings, as foreign institutional investors adopt a wait‑and‑see stance.

Key Drivers Behind the Flat Bias

1. **Geopolitical risk premium** – The US‑Iran standoff has lifted the global risk premium, prompting a shift to safe‑haven assets like gold and the US dollar. Indian rupee futures reflect a modest depreciation, adding a layer of caution.

2. **Domestic fundamentals remain solid** – Recent GDP growth of 7.2% YoY, robust consumption, and a healthy fiscal deficit keep the macro backdrop supportive. However, the immediate sentiment is muted.

Sector‑Specific Impact: Winners and Losers

Technology stocks bear the brunt of the sell‑off, with Infosys and Tech Mahindra each slipping over 1.2% in pre‑market trades. Their exposure to US clients makes them sensitive to any escalation that could affect dollar‑denominated contracts.

Conversely, defensive segments such as FMCG and utilities show resilience, trading marginally higher as investors rotate into low‑beta names.
