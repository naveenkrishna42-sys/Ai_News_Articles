Goldy Brar Claims Responsibility for Nijjar Killing in Leaked Audio

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Goldy Brar alleges involvement in the 2023 killing of Hardeep Singh Nijjar.
  • The claims were made in an audio recording that has recently emerged.
  • Official agencies in India and Canada are yet to verify the authenticity of the audio.

Audio Leak Surfaces

A new audio recording has emerged featuring the voice of fugitive gangster Goldy Brar, in which he purportedly claims responsibility for the killing of Hardeep Singh Nijjar. Nijjar, a designated terrorist in India, was shot dead in Surrey, British Columbia, in June 2023.

The audio clip has circulated widely across digital platforms, reigniting discussions surrounding the high-profile case. Brar, who operates from abroad, is currently a wanted figure by multiple Indian security agencies for various criminal activities, including extortion and murder.

The Claims in Question

In the recording, the speaker alleges that the hit was orchestrated as part of a larger criminal vendetta. The claims made in the clip have not been independently verified by forensic experts or law enforcement officials.

Security agencies in India have been tracking the activities of Brar for several years. He is known to maintain a significant presence in international criminal networks, often using encrypted messaging services to communicate his alleged exploits to followers and rival groups.

The Investigation Status

Authorities have not yet confirmed the authenticity of the recording or the date of its creation. Canadian investigators, who have been handling the Nijjar case, have maintained a guarded stance regarding the emergence of this new audio evidence.

The incident surrounding Nijjar’s death has remained a flashpoint in diplomatic relations. While the Indian government has consistently maintained that it had no involvement in the killing, the emergence of such claims from known criminal elements adds a layer of complexity to the ongoing international discourse.

Tracking a Fugitive

Goldy Brar remains a primary subject of interest for the National Investigation Agency (NIA). His name has appeared in numerous charge sheets involving gang violence across Punjab and northern India.

Despite several red corner notices issued against him, Brar has managed to evade capture. Security analysts suggest that his public claims are often calculated moves designed to bolster his status within the underworld or to disrupt ongoing legal processes.

The focus now shifts to whether international intelligence agencies will attempt to authenticate the audio to determine if it provides actionable leads. Future developments will depend on the findings of digital forensic teams currently examining the clip.
