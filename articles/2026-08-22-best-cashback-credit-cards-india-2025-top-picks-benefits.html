Best Cashback Credit Cards India 2025: Top Picks & Benefits

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Credit Cards & Cashback Original source ↗
Key points
  • HDFC Millennia, Citibank Cash Back, and Flipkart Axis offer up to 5% cashback on popular spending categories
  • Joining fees range from Rs 500 to Rs 1,500 with waiver options on spending thresholds
  • Eligibility requires minimum income of Rs 3.6 lakh annually for most cards

Why Cashback Cards Still Dominate Indian Spending

Cashback credit cards remain the most rewarding option for everyday spenders who settle their full bill every month. Unlike reward points that require complex redemption, cashback credits directly reduce your statement balance. The best cards now offer accelerated rates on dining, groceries, and online shopping—categories where most Indians spend the most.

HDFC Bank Millennia Credit Card

HDFC's flagship cashback offering delivers 5% cashback on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, and BigBasket up to Rs 1,000 per month. Other purchases earn 1% uncapped cashback. The joining fee is Rs 1,000, waived on spending Rs 10,000 within 30 days. Annual fee of Rs 1,000 gets reversed on spends exceeding Rs 1 lakh annually.

Eligibility: Salaried individuals earning Rs 3.6 lakh per year or self-employed with ITR above Rs 6 lakh. Age must be between 21 and 60 years. Applicants need a good credit score above 750.

Citi Bank Cash Back Credit Card

This card keeps things simple with a flat 5% cashback on movie tickets and utility bill payments via its app. Other categories earn 0.5% cashback on all other spends. The annual fee is Rs 500, reversed on spends above Rs 50,000 yearly. No joining fee makes it attractive for first-time cardholders.

Eligibility: Minimum annual income of Rs 6 lakh for salaried applicants. Self-employed individuals need audited financials showing net profit above Rs 6 lakh. Citibank requires a relationship history of at least 6 months with the applicant.

Flipkart Axis Bank Credit Card

Ideal for regular Flipkart and PhonePe users, this card offers 5% flat cashback on Flipkart orders with no monthly cap. PhonePe transactions earn 1.5% cashback, while all other spends get 1% back. The joining fee of Rs 500 is waived on first transaction. Annual fee of Rs 750 is reversed on spending Rs 2 lakh annually.

Eligibility: Applicants need a minimum annual income of Rs 3 lakh. Both salaried and self-employed individuals can apply. Existing Axis Bank customers may get faster approval with pre-approved offers visible in their net banking portal.

How to Apply for Maximum Welcome Bonuses

  • Compare offers on your bank's official website or trusted aggregator platforms before applying
  • Check pre-approved offers through your existing banking app—these often skip the documentation review
  • Apply within the first week of any promotional period when welcome cashback bonuses are highest
  • Ensure you meet income eligibility before applying to avoid rejection impacting your credit score
  • Complete KYC verification online using Aadhaar-based OTP authentication for fastest processing

Final Verdict

The HDFC Millennia suits heavy online shoppers who spend across multiple platforms. The Flipkart Axis card benefits loyal Flipkart users who can easily cross the annual fee waiver threshold. The Citibank Cash Back card works for users who pay utility bills through the app and watch movies frequently. Always settle your full outstanding to make cashback truly profitable—interest charges wipe out all rewards instantly.
