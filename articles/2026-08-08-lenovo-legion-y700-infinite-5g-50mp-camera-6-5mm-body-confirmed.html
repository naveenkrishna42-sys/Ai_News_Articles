Lenovo Legion Y700 Infinite 5G: 50MP Camera, 6.5mm Body Confirmed

Lenovo Confirms Key Specs for Legion Y700 Infinite 5G

Lenovo has officially confirmed that its upcoming Legion Y700 Infinite 5G tablet will pack a 50MP rear camera, a chassis just 6.5mm thin, and display bezels measuring a mere 2.89mm. The details emerged through official teasers and listings ahead of the device's expected launch in China.

The confirmation puts to rest weeks of speculation about the tablet's design and imaging capabilities. It also signals Lenovo's intent to compete aggressively in the premium Android tablet segment, where thinness and camera quality have become key battlegrounds.

A Design Built Around Slimness

The 6.5mm profile places the Legion Y700 Infinite 5G among the thinnest gaming tablets on the market. For context, most flagship slates hover around 7-8mm, so this represents a notable engineering feat, especially when accommodating a 5G modem and a large battery.

The 2.89mm bezels are equally striking. Lenovo has teased symmetrical bezels on all sides, a design choice that improves grip during landscape gaming and gives the front a more uniform look. The slim frame is expected to keep the weight down, though exact figures have not yet been confirmed.

Camera: A Rare Focus for Gaming Tablets

Gaming tablets rarely prioritise rear cameras, but the 50MP sensor marks a departure from the norm. While the lens specifications and sensor size remain unconfirmed, the megapixel count suggests Lenovo is targeting users who want a device that can double as a content creation tool.

The front-facing camera details are still under wraps. Given the tablet's gaming focus, a capable selfie shooter for streaming and video calls would make sense, but officials have not yet confirmed those specifications.

Part of a Bigger Tablet Push

The Legion Y700 Infinite 5G is not Lenovo's only tablet in the pipeline. Recent announcements from the company include a model with a nine-speaker setup, a gaming slate discounted by 25%, and a 140W power bank. Together, these moves underline Lenovo's strategy to dominate the portable gaming and entertainment hardware space.

The nine-speaker tablet, in particular, suggests Lenovo is experimenting with audio as a differentiator. Meanwhile, the discounted gaming slate appears aimed at clearing inventory ahead of the new launches, a common tactic in the run-up to major product refreshes.

What to Expect at Launch

Lenovo has not announced a specific launch date for the Legion Y700 Infinite 5G, but the teaser campaign indicates an announcement is imminent. The device is expected to debut in China first, as was the case with previous Legion Y700 models.

Global availability remains unclear. Lenovo typically brings its Legion tablets to India and other markets a few months after the China launch, but no timeline has been shared for this device. Pricing also remains unconfirmed, though the premium build and 5G support suggest a higher price point than the standard Y700.

For now, the confirmed specs — 50MP camera, 6.5mm thin body, and 2.89mm bezels — set the stage for what could be one of the most polished gaming tablets of the year. All eyes will be on the official unveiling to see how the device performs in real-world conditions.
