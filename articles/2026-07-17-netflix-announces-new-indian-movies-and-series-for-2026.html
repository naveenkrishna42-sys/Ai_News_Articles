Netflix announces new Indian movies and series for 2026

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Netflix announced a new lineup of Indian original movies and series for 2026.
  • The slate includes genres spanning drama, thriller, comedy, and action.
  • Several prominent Bollywood and regional filmmakers are attached to the projects.

Netflix has unveiled its slate of upcoming Indian original movies and series for 2026. The streaming giant announced a diverse lineup that includes thrillers, comedies, dramas, and action films, featuring some of the biggest names in Indian cinema.

New movies on the way

The list of films includes a high-octane action thriller directed by a noted filmmaker, a family drama set in a small town, and a romantic comedy from a popular director-producer duo. A psychological thriller starring a leading Bollywood actor is also in production.

Netflix has not yet confirmed release dates for most of the titles. The company said in a statement that the projects are in various stages of development and production.

Series slate expands

On the series front, Netflix is developing a political drama set against the backdrop of a state election, a crime anthology inspired by true events, and a coming-of-age story about a young woman navigating life in a metro city. A period drama based on a bestselling novel is also on the cards.

The platform is betting big on regional content. Several of the announced series are in Tamil, Telugu, and Malayalam, reflecting Netflix's push to capture audiences beyond the Hindi-speaking belt.

Star power and fresh faces

The lineup features established stars as well as emerging talent. A prominent Bollywood actress will make her streaming debut with a thriller series, while a popular South Indian actor is headlining a pan-India action film. Netflix has also signed multi-project deals with several directors and writers.

Financial details of the deals have not been disclosed. Industry insiders estimate Netflix's spending on Indian originals this year could cross the ₹1,000 crore mark, though the company has not confirmed this figure.

Competitive landscape heats up

Netflix's announcement comes amid intensifying competition in the Indian streaming market. Rivals like Amazon Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar, and JioCinema have also been aggressively commissioning original content. Netflix has been focusing on high-quality, premium content to differentiate itself in a crowded space.

The company has seen steady subscriber growth in India, driven by its investment in local storytelling. With this new slate, Netflix aims to sustain that momentum and deepen its engagement with Indian audiences.

Viewers can expect the first of these titles to drop in the second half of 2026. Netflix has promised a steady cadence of releases throughout the year.
