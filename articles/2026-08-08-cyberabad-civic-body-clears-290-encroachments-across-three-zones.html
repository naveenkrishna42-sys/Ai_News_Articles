Cyberabad civic body clears 290 encroachments across three zones

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • 290 encroachments removed across three zones on August 7
  • Drive focused on pavements, road margins, and public spaces
  • Civic body warns of continued action against illegal structures

Anti-encroachment drive in full swing

The Cyberabad civic body on Friday removed 290 encroachments spread across three zones, as part of its ongoing effort to clear public spaces of illegal structures. The operation, carried out on August 7, targeted pavements, road margins, and other common areas that had been occupied by vendors and unauthorised constructions.

Officials said the drive was executed across the zones under the jurisdiction of the Cyberabad Metropolitan Development Authority, with teams working through the day to dismantle the structures. The exact locations and the nature of the encroachments have not been fully detailed, but the civic body has indicated that the action was taken after multiple notices were served to the occupants.

Focus on pedestrian safety and traffic flow

The removal of encroachments is aimed at easing pedestrian movement and reducing traffic congestion in the area. Many of the cleared spots were on footpaths and along busy roads, where hawkers and temporary sheds had narrowed the passage for commuters.

Residents in the affected neighbourhoods have welcomed the move, though some shopkeepers and street vendors expressed concerns about losing their livelihoods. The civic body, however, has maintained that the drive is necessary to restore order and ensure public safety.

Prior warnings and compliance notices

According to officials, the action followed a series of warnings issued to encroachers in the preceding weeks. The notices gave occupants time to remove their structures voluntarily, but many failed to comply, prompting the forced removal.

The civic body has said it is prepared to take similar action in other zones if encroachments are found. It has also asked residents to report any new illegal structures that come up in their areas.

Repeated drives and future plans

This is not the first such exercise by the Cyberabad civic body this year, and officials have indicated that more drives are planned in the coming weeks. The focus will remain on high-density commercial areas and residential pockets where encroachments have been a recurring problem.

The authorities have also warned that any attempt to re-occupy cleared spaces will be met with strict action, including fines and confiscation of materials.

While the civic body has not released a detailed list of the removed structures, it has assured that the process will be documented and monitored. The next phase of the drive is expected to cover additional zones, with a particular emphasis on areas near metro stations and bus stops.

For now, the immediate priority is to ensure that the cleared spaces remain free of encroachments, and that pedestrians and vehicles can move without hindrance. The civic body has urged citizens to cooperate and to use designated spaces for vending, if any, to avoid future action.
