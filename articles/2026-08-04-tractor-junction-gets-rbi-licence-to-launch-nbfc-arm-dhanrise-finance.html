Tractor Junction gets RBI licence to launch NBFC arm Dhanrise Finance

📅 2026-08-04 📂 Banking Original source ↗
Key points
  • Tractor Junction has received RBI approval to launch NBFC arm Dhanrise Finance.
  • The new entity will focus on financing solutions for tractors and farm equipment.
  • This move aims to simplify credit access for farmers and small agri-businesses.

Farm equipment marketplace gets regulatory green light

Tractor Junction, a prominent online marketplace for tractors and farm equipment, has secured a licence from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to set up a non-banking financial company (NBFC) arm. The new entity will be called Dhanrise Finance.

The approval marks a significant step for the platform, which has so far focused on connecting buyers with sellers across India's vast agricultural machinery market. With Dhanrise Finance, the company is now set to enter the lending space.

Why an NBFC arm makes sense

Tractors and heavy farm equipment are high-ticket purchases, often beyond the immediate cash capacity of small and marginal farmers. While traditional banks do offer tractor loans, the process can be cumbersome and slow, sometimes taking weeks. Tractor Junction aims to bridge this gap with a digital-first lending approach.

By integrating financing directly into its platform, the company can offer instant, customised loan options at the point of purchase. This could significantly reduce the friction involved in buying farm equipment, especially in rural and semi-urban areas.

Dhanrise Finance: what to expect

Dhanrise Finance will operate as a separate legal entity, though it will leverage Tractor Junction's existing data and customer base. The company has not yet disclosed the initial capital outlay or the specific interest rates it plans to offer, but industry insiders suggest the focus will be on competitive pricing and quick disbursal.

The NBFC will likely start by financing used tractors, a segment that has seen growing demand but limited formal credit options. It may also extend loans for attachments and implements, further deepening its role in the agricultural economy.

Growth in agri-fintech

The move comes at a time when agri-fintech is gaining traction in India, with several players exploring niche lending models. Government schemes promoting farm mechanisation have also boosted tractor sales, creating a natural need for dedicated financing solutions.

Tractor Junction's entry into NBFC operations is expected to intensify competition in this space, pushing incumbents to innovate on speed and customer experience.

RBI's approval is subject to standard conditions, and the company will need to comply with all regulatory norms, including capital adequacy and know-your-customer requirements. Officials have not yet confirmed a launch date for Dhanrise Finance.

As the platform pivots from marketplace to financial services, all eyes will be on how it manages credit risk and collection in a sector known for seasonal cash flows. The coming months will reveal whether Dhanrise Finance can replicate the convenience of online shopping in the world of farm loans.
