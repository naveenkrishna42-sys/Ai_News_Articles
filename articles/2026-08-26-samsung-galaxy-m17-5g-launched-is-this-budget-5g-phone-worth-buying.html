The Samsung Galaxy M17 5G makes a compelling case in the budget 5G space with its 50MP main camera and 5,000mAh battery — two specs that directly address what budget buyers care about most: camera resolution and battery life. However, the M-series traditionally uses LCD displays rather than Samsung's superior AMOLED panels, which means you may sacrifice color vibrancy and outdoor brightness compared to similarly priced Galaxy A-series or competitor phones with OLED screens. If camera megapixels and battery endurance are your top priorities and you can accept an LCD display, this device is worth considering for the price. But if you want richer colors, faster charging speeds, or better gaming performance, look at the Galaxy A35 5G or competing options from Xiaomi and Realme in the same budget tier.
