Sensex, Nifty Close Flat as SBI, RIL Losses Offset Titan, Bajaj Finance Gains

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • BSE Sensex rose about 40 points, while NSE Nifty50 closed flat.
  • Titan Company and Bajaj Finance were among top gainers; SBI and RIL were among losers.
  • Crude oil price spike weighed on market sentiment during the session.

Indian equity benchmarks ended Monday's session with marginal gains, as a late-session uptick in select heavyweights helped offset losses in index majors such as State Bank of India (SBI) and Reliance Industries (RIL). The BSE Sensex closed about 40 points higher, while the NSE Nifty50 ended virtually flat, reflecting a day of cautious trading on Dalal Street.

The market's tepid performance came amid a fresh spike in global crude oil prices, which added to inflationary concerns and kept investors on edge. Despite the subdued close, the broader market showed resilience, with advances narrowly outpacing declines in the cash market.

Gainers and Losers on the Street

Titan Company and Bajaj Finance emerged as the standout gainers on both the Sensex and Nifty, helping prop up the indices. Titan, the jewellery-to-watchmaker conglomerate, saw renewed buying interest, while Bajaj Finance continued to attract investors in the non-banking financial space.

On the flip side, SBI and RIL were the primary drags, with selling pressure in these heavyweight counters weighing on the benchmarks. The losses in these stocks were significant enough to offset the gains from Titan and Bajaj Finance, leaving the indices with only marginal positive closes.

Crude Oil Spike Weighs on Sentiment

The rise in crude oil prices was a key theme during Monday's session. A spike in international benchmarks sparked concerns about imported inflation and its potential impact on India's fiscal and current account deficits. Auto, aviation, and paint stocks, which are sensitive to crude prices, faced mild selling pressure through the day.

Analysts noted that while the market has been resilient in the face of global headwinds, sustained crude price increases could cap upside in the near term. The rupee, too, remained under pressure, though it stayed within a narrow band.

Mixed Cues from Global Markets

Asian markets traded mixed on Monday, with some indices in the green while others struggled. European markets were expected to open on a cautious note, tracking the movement in oil prices and ahead of key economic data due later in the week.

Domestic institutional investors were net buyers, providing some support, while foreign portfolio investors remained on the sidelines. The volatility index, India VIX, stayed elevated, indicating that traders are bracing for more turbulence in the sessions ahead.

What to Watch in the Coming Sessions

Market participants will now closely track crude oil prices and the movement of the US dollar, which are likely to dictate near-term direction. With no major domestic triggers on the calendar, global cues and quarterly earnings from select companies will take centre stage.

Analysts suggest that the Nifty is likely to remain range-bound in the absence of fresh catalysts. Traders are advised to keep a close watch on the 24,300–24,500 zone on the Nifty, which could act as a crucial support in case of any sharp correction. A decisive move above the recent highs could invite fresh buying, but until then, the market may continue to consolidate.
