Maharashtra Governor Pays Tributes to Ajit Pawar on His Birthday

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Governor C P Radhakrishnan met Ajit Pawar at Raj Bhavan on his birthday.
  • The Governor offered wishes and praised Pawar's contributions to the state.
  • Ajit Pawar is the Deputy Chief Minister of Maharashtra and a senior NCP leader.

Mumbai: Maharashtra Governor C P Radhakrishnan on Wednesday paid tributes to Deputy Chief Minister Ajit Pawar on his birth anniversary at the Raj Bhavan in Mumbai. The Governor extended his greetings and best wishes to the senior Nationalist Congress Party (NCP) leader.

Governor Lauds Public Service

During the meeting at the state guest house, Governor Radhakrishnan lauded Ajit Pawar's contributions to the development of Maharashtra. He acknowledged the Deputy Chief Minister's long innings in public life and his service to the people of the state.

The Governor's office issued a brief statement confirming the interaction. The statement noted that the Governor wished Pawar good health and a long life dedicated to public service.

Ajit Pawar's Political Journey

Ajit Pawar, the nephew of NCP founder Sharad Pawar, has been a key figure in Maharashtra politics for decades. He has served as the Deputy Chief Minister of the state multiple times and has held several important portfolios including finance and irrigation.

His political career has seen him navigate complex alliances. He is currently part of the ruling Mahayuti coalition government in Maharashtra, serving alongside Chief Minister Eknath Shinde and Deputy Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis.

Significance of the Occasion

The Governor's gesture on Ajit Pawar's birthday underscores the constitutional head's tradition of acknowledging the contributions of elected representatives. Such interactions are routine at Raj Bhavan but carry political symbolism in Maharashtra's often fluid coalition politics.

This year's birthday greeting comes at a time when the ruling alliance is working to consolidate its position ahead of local body elections. Ajit Pawar's NCP faction remains a crucial pillar of the government.

Officials at Raj Bhavan said the meeting lasted about 15 minutes. No other leaders were present during the exchange of greetings.

What to watch: Ajit Pawar's political moves in the coming months, particularly his party's strategy for the upcoming municipal corporation elections in Maharashtra, will be closely observed by political analysts.
