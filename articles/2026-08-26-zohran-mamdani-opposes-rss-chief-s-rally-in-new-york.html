Mayor Zohran Mamdani Opposes RSS Chief's Rally in New York

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani has publicly opposed the upcoming rally by Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) chief Mohan Bhagwat at Madison Square Garden. The rally, scheduled for a date in September 2026, has sparked significant controversy and drawn opposition from various quarters.

Concerns About Free Speech and Community Safety

Mayor Mamdani's opposition is rooted in concerns about free speech and community safety. He has expressed fears that the rally could potentially incite violence or disrupt the peace of the city. The mayor's stance has been supported by various community leaders and organizations.

Protests and Opposition

The Indian American Muslim Council (IAMC) and other allied faith organizations have also protested against the rally. They argue that the event could undermine the principles of free speech and democracy. The protests have drawn attention to the broader issue of religious and cultural diversity in New York City.
