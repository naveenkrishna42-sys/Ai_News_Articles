BJP passes resolution against Congress on Vande Mataram

BJP Passes Resolution Against Congress Over Vande Mataram

The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) has passed a resolution against the Congress party over the Vande Mataram dispute, vowing nationwide protests if their demands are not met. The resolution was passed during a party meeting in Delhi, with leaders expressing strong disapproval over the Congress' stance on the national anthem.

BJP Vows Nationwide Protest

BJP leaders have announced that they will launch a nationwide protest if the Congress does not retract its stance on the Vande Mataram version. The protest is expected to be a significant political statement, with leaders from various states participating. The BJP has accused the Congress of disrespecting the national anthem and has called for a united front against what they perceive as a political move.

Congress Defends Its Version of Vande Mataram

Congress leaders have defended their version of Vande Mataram, which follows the version decided by Mahatma Gandhi and Sardar Patel. They argue that the song has been a symbol of unity and national pride for decades and should not be subject to political disputes. Congress leader Kapil Sibal has asked Amit Shah if the BJP would have supported the singing of the full Vande Mataram if Narendra Modi was the Chief Minister of Gujarat and Atal Bihari Vajpayee was the Prime Minister.

Historical Context of Vande Mataram

Vande Mataram is a national anthem that was first popularized by Rabindranath Tagore. The song, which means "I bow to Thee, Mother," was written by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee and set to music by Rabindranath Tagore. The song has been a symbol of national unity and has been sung by millions of Indians over the years. However, there has been a long-standing dispute over the version of the song that should be sung, with some arguing that the full version should be sung and others advocating for a shorter version.

Political Implications of the Dispute

The Vande Mataram dispute has significant political implications, with both the BJP and the Congress using the issue to gain support from their respective constituencies. The BJP has accused the Congress of disrespecting the national anthem, while the Congress has argued that the BJP is trying to exploit the issue for political gain. The dispute has also raised questions about the role of the national anthem in Indian politics and the importance of national unity.
