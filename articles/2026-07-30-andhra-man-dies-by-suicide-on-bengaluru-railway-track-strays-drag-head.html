Andhra man dies by suicide on Bengaluru railway track, strays drag head to field

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • A man from Andhra Pradesh died by suicide on a railway track near Varthur, Bengaluru.
  • Stray dogs dragged his severed head to a nearby field, causing panic among locals.
  • Police have identified the deceased and are investigating the circumstances.

Bengaluru: A 45-year-old man from Andhra Pradesh died by suicide on a railway track in the Varthur area of Bengaluru early Thursday morning. The incident took place near the Varthur railway station.

According to the Bengaluru Railway Police, the man, identified as Venkatesh, a native of Guntur district in Andhra Pradesh, had been living in Bengaluru for the past few years. He was working as a daily wage labourer in the city.

Strays drag severed head to field

After the man was run over by a train, stray dogs in the area dragged his severed head to a nearby field, about 100 metres from the track. This caused panic among locals who spotted the head in the morning.

"We saw a group of dogs gathered near the field and when we went closer, we saw a human head. We immediately informed the police," said a local resident.

Police investigation underway

The Bengaluru Railway Police have registered a case of unnatural death. They have sent the body for post-mortem to the nearby government hospital.

"We have identified the deceased and informed his family members. They are on their way to Bengaluru. We are investigating the reason behind his extreme step," said a senior police officer.

Police officials have not yet confirmed if the man left behind any suicide note.

Railway track safety concerns

This incident has once again raised concerns about safety on railway tracks in the city. Locals claim that the stretch near Varthur is poorly lit at night and lacks fencing, making it easy for unauthorised persons to access the tracks.

"This is not the first such incident here. People often cross the tracks at this spot. The railways should put up proper fencing and improve lighting to prevent such tragedies," said a resident.

The family of the deceased is expected to reach Bengaluru by Friday. Police will hand over the body after post-mortem and legal formalities.

What to watch: The police investigation will focus on whether any personal or financial distress drove the man to take this step. The railway authorities may also face questions about safety measures on the stretch.
