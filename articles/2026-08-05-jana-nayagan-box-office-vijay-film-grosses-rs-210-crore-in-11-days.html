Jana Nayagan Box Office: Vijay Film Grosses Rs 210 Crore in 11 Days

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jana Nayagan grosses Rs 210 crore in India in 11 days
  • Film maintains strong hold on second Monday despite weekday drop
  • Global collections cross Rs 300 crore milestone

Steady Run at the Box Office

Thalapathy Vijay's latest action drama, Jana Nayagan, continues its impressive run at the Indian box office. The film, which released on July 24, has now grossed Rs 210 crore nett in India within 11 days of its theatrical run.

The movie's performance on its second Monday has been particularly noteworthy. Trade analysts point out that the drop from the opening weekend was minimal, indicating strong word-of-mouth and repeat viewings from fans.

Second Monday Numbers

According to early estimates, Jana Nayagan collected around Rs 8-9 crore nett on its second Monday. This represents a slight dip from the second Friday's figures but remains healthy for a film in its second week.

The film's weekday hold is being compared favourably to other big-ticket Tamil releases. Industry insiders note that sustaining collections past the first week is often the real test for commercial entertainers.

Global Milestone Crossed

On the global front, Jana Nayagan has crossed the Rs 300 crore mark in worldwide gross collections. The overseas markets, particularly in the US and Gulf regions, have contributed significantly to this total.

The film, directed by a prominent filmmaker, features Vijay in the lead role alongside Pooja Hegde. The movie has been released in multiple languages, including Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, and Kannada, broadening its reach.

Comparison with Recent Releases

Trade pundits are already drawing comparisons with other recent big-ticket releases. Some suggest that Jana Nayagan's sustained performance could potentially challenge the lifetime collections of Suriya's Karuppu, which had set high benchmarks earlier this year.

However, analysts caution that the coming week will be crucial. The film faces competition from new releases this Friday, which could impact its screen count and subsequent collections.

What Lies Ahead

The third week will be a testing period for Jana Nayagan. Usually, films see a sharper decline in collections after the second weekend, and the hold in the coming days will determine its final box office standing.

For now, the Vijay-starrer has firmly established itself as one of the biggest hits of the year, and its performance in the days ahead will be closely watched by industry observers.
