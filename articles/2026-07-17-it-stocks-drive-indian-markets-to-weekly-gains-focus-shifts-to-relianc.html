IT stocks drive Indian markets to weekly gains; focus shifts to Reliance, bank earnings

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex closed 964 points higher, Nifty near 24,127.
  • IT stocks led the rally, with Tech Mahindra and Kotak Bank gaining 4% each.
  • Reliance Industries and major bank earnings are next in focus.

Indian stock markets ended the week on a strong note, with the Sensex surging 964 points and the Nifty opening above 24,127. The rally was powered by information technology stocks, which saw broad-based buying through the session.

Tech Mahindra and Kotak Bank each jumped 4%, while Jio Financial gained 3%. The only major laggard was Hindalco, which slipped 1%. The broader market reflected the positive sentiment, with advances outpacing declines.

IT sector leads the charge

The IT sector was the clear leader of the day. Analysts attributed the rally to renewed optimism around global technology spending and a weaker rupee, which benefits export-oriented firms. The Nifty IT index rose over 3%, its best single-day gain in months.

Brokerage reports suggest that large IT firms are seeing strong deal pipelines, and quarterly results due next week could provide further momentum. Investors are also watching for management commentary on demand trends in the US and Europe.

Earnings season heats up

With the IT rally providing a solid base, market participants are now turning their attention to heavyweight earnings. Reliance Industries and major banks are scheduled to report their quarterly numbers in the coming days.

Reliance, which has a significant weight in the index, is expected to show steady performance in its retail and telecom segments. Banking earnings will be closely watched for asset quality trends and loan growth, especially after the central bank's recent policy stance.

Broader market and sectoral moves

Besides IT, other sectors also saw buying interest. Jio Financial's 3% gain reflected optimism about its digital lending plans. Kotak Bank's rise was in line with broader banking strength, with most private lenders closing in the green.

Hindalco's decline, though modest, stood out in an otherwise positive market. Analysts linked it to profit-taking after recent gains and uncertainty around global aluminium prices.

The midcap and smallcap indices also gained, indicating that the rally was not limited to large-caps. Market breadth was strong, with more than two stocks advancing for every one that declined on the BSE.

What to watch next

Investors will closely track the earnings releases from Reliance Industries and key banks next week. Any surprises on the upside could extend the current rally, while disappointments may trigger profit-taking. Global cues, especially US Federal Reserve commentary, will also remain in focus.
