RBI Launches Financial Literacy Week 2026, Focus on KYC as Banking Foundation

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Banking Original source ↗
Key points
  • RBI Governor announces Financial Literacy Week 2026 from March 1 to March 7.
  • Theme: 'KYC – The Foundation of Secure Banking'.
  • Campaign includes workshops, digital campaigns, and bank branch activities across India.

RBI Flags Off Financial Literacy Week 2026

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) launched its annual Financial Literacy Week on Friday, setting the theme for 2026 as 'KYC – The Foundation of Secure Banking'. The week-long initiative, starting March 1, aims to reinforce the importance of Know Your Customer (KYC) norms among the public.

RBI officials stated that the campaign targets both urban and rural customers. Banks across the country will organise workshops, street plays, and digital awareness drives. The central bank has asked all scheduled commercial banks to actively participate.

Why KYC Matters Now

Financial frauds involving identity theft and fake accounts have risen sharply in recent years. The RBI’s focus on KYC is a direct response to these threats. Proper KYC documentation helps banks verify customer identity and track suspicious transactions.

“KYC is not just a regulatory formality. It is the first line of defence for both the customer and the bank,” a senior RBI official said during the launch event in Mumbai. The central bank has also issued fresh guidelines on simplified KYC for low-value accounts to ensure financial inclusion stays on track.

Activities Planned During the Week

The Financial Literacy Week will see a mix of online and offline events. Bank branches in metropolitan cities will host customer meets, while rural branches will deploy mobile vans with educational material. The RBI’s own website and social media handles will run quizzes and explainer videos on KYC procedures.

Schools and colleges have also been roped in. Selected institutions will have guest lectures from bank managers. The central bank hopes this will inculcate financial discipline early among young Indians.

Past Impact and Future Goals

The RBI has been conducting Financial Literacy Week since 2016. Previous themes have covered digital payments, cyber hygiene, and credit discipline. The central bank claims that these campaigns have led to a measurable increase in customer awareness, especially in tier-2 and tier-3 cities.

This year, the RBI has set a target of reaching at least 10 crore households through direct engagement. Banks will also distribute pamphlets in multiple regional languages to ensure no one is left out.

What to Watch Next

The RBI is expected to release a detailed report on the week’s outcomes by mid-March. Separately, the central bank is working on a centralised KYC registry that could streamline the process across all financial institutions. That project, however, is still in the consultation stage.
