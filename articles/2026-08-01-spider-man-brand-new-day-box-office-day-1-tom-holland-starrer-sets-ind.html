Spider-Man Brand New Day Box Office Day 1: Tom Holland Starrer Sets India Record

📅 2026-08-01 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day records biggest opening day for a Spider-Man film in India
  • Film crosses Rs 130 crore at Indian box office by Friday
  • US opening day estimated at $167 million, previews beat Avengers: Endgame

MUMBAI: Spider-Man: Brand New Day has swung into record books in India, with the Tom Holland-starrer posting the biggest opening day for any Spider-Man film in the country. Trade estimates on Friday put the film's India collection at over Rs 130 crore within two days of release, powered by strong metro turnout and fan screenings.

The Marvel superhero film, which released worldwide on Thursday, has also set new benchmarks globally. According to industry tracker Deadline, the movie earned an estimated $167 million on its opening day in the US, while previews alone raked in $72 million — surpassing Avengers: Endgame's preview haul.

India Box Office Surge

In India, the film's day-one numbers eclipsed all previous Spider-Man releases, including Spider-Man: No Way Home, which had held the franchise record since 2021. The Hindi, English, Tamil, and Telugu versions collectively contributed to the strong start, with multiplex chains reporting near-full occupancy in major cities.

By Friday, the film's India gross crossed the Rs 100 crore mark and touched Rs 130 crore, according to The Times of India. The weekend is expected to add significantly to the tally, with advance bookings for Saturday and Sunday already robust across national chains.

Global Opening Sets New Precedents

The US opening day figure of $167 million places Brand New Day among the top 10 domestic opening days of all time. The $72 million preview number, which includes Thursday shows, beat the $60 million earned by Avengers: Endgame in 2019, signalling strong fan anticipation for the sequel to No Way Home.

The film, directed by Destin Daniel Cretton, picks up after the multiverse-shattering events of the previous installment. Zendaya reprises her role as MJ, while Holland's Peter Parker navigates a world that no longer remembers him. Early reviews have been largely positive, with critics praising the emotional core and action set pieces.

Trade Expectations for Weekend

Box office analysts expect the film to maintain momentum through the weekend, with Sunday likely to be the biggest single day in India. The holiday period in several states, coupled with school vacations, has aided daytime shows.

However, some exhibitors in non-metro markets noted that the film's performance has been more urban-centric so far, with dubbed versions in regional languages seeing slower but steady growth. "The opening is phenomenal in cities," said a distributor in Delhi, speaking anonymously. "The mass circuits are catching up by evening shows."

Globally, the film is projected to cross $500 million by Sunday, with major markets like China and Japan yet to release. The overseas trajectory mirrors the success of No Way Home, which earned over $1.9 billion worldwide.

What to Watch Next

All eyes are now on the second weekend hold, as Marvel films often see sharp drops after the opening rush. A strong Monday will be key to sustaining the record pace. For now, Brand New Day has firmly established Holland's Spider-Man as a box office force in India, a market that continues to embrace the web-slinger with every outing.
