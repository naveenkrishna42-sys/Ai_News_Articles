Cryptocurrency prices mostly decline with Bitcoin slipping below $40,000

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin dropped below $40,000, marking a significant decline from recent highs.
  • Ethereum, Dogecoin, and other major altcoins also saw losses.
  • Market analysts attribute the downturn to profit-taking and regulatory uncertainty.

Bitcoin slips below $40,000 as market takes a hit

Bitcoin led a broad decline in cryptocurrency prices on Friday, slipping below the $40,000 mark. The world's largest digital asset was trading at around $39,800 at the time of reporting, down over 5% in the last 24 hours.

The drop erased gains from earlier this week when Bitcoin had briefly touched $42,000. Traders pointed to a mix of profit-taking and nervousness over potential regulatory moves.

Altcoins follow Bitcoin's lead

Ethereum, the second-largest cryptocurrency, fell 6% to trade near $2,200. Dogecoin, the meme coin, dropped 8% to $0.18. Other altcoins like Cardano, Solana, and Ripple also posted losses in the range of 4-7%.

The overall crypto market cap lost nearly $100 billion in the past 24 hours, according to data from CoinMarketCap. The selloff was broad-based, with few tokens bucking the trend.

What's driving the decline?

Analysts said the decline was not triggered by a single event but by a combination of factors. Profit-taking after a strong run-up in June and early July was one reason. Bitcoin had gained over 20% in the last month before this week's pullback.

Regulatory uncertainty also weighed on sentiment. Reports that the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission may tighten rules on crypto exchanges and stablecoins have spooked investors. India, too, is reportedly working on a framework for digital assets, though no official statement has come yet.

Some traders also cited a drop in trading volumes on major exchanges as a sign of waning momentum. Lower liquidity can amplify price swings.

Despite the selloff, long-term holders appear unfazed. Data from Glassnode shows that wallets holding Bitcoin for over a year have not moved their coins in significant numbers.

Market participants are now watching for any policy announcements from central banks or financial regulators in the coming days. The next few sessions could determine whether this is a temporary correction or the start of a deeper slump.
