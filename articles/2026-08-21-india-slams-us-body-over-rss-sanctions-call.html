India slams US body over RSS sanctions call

📅 2026-08-21 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • India condemns US religious freedom body's call for sanctions against RSS.
  • RSS chief Mohan Bhagwat is scheduled to visit the US, Canada, and the UK.
  • Multiple groups back RSS chief's Canada visit, urging Ottawa to reject calls for blocking it.

India Condemns US Religious Freedom Body's Call for RSS Sanctions

India has strongly condemned the US Commission on International Religious Freedom (USCIRF) for seeking sanctions against the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS), calling the move biased and lacking credibility. The Indian government has expressed its displeasure over the USCIRF's decision, which it views as an attempt to undermine the RSS.

RSS Chief's International Outreach

Despite the controversy, RSS chief Mohan Bhagwat is set to embark on a high-profile international tour, visiting the US, Canada, and the UK as part of the RSS's centenary-year outreach. The tour is seen as a significant diplomatic effort by the RSS to strengthen its global presence and counter any perceived threats.

Support for RSS Chief's Canada Visit

In a show of support for Bhagwat's Canada visit, over 100 groups have come together to urge the Canadian government to reject calls for blocking his entry. The groups, which include various civil society organizations, have expressed concern over what they perceive as a political move to silence the RSS. They argue that Bhagwat's visit is a matter of national interest and should not be undermined.

USCIRF's Stance on RSS

The USCIRF has been critical of the RSS, accusing it of engaging in activities that violate religious freedom. The commission has called for high-level meetings with RSS leaders to be avoided and has expressed concerns over the RSS's role in the Sangh Parivar, which it claims attacks minorities. However, India has dismissed these allegations as baseless and politically motivated.
