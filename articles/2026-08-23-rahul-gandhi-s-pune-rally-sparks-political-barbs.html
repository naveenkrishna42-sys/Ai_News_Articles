Rahul Gandhi's Pune rally sparks political barbs

📅 2026-08-23 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Rahul Gandhi's 'Chhatron Ki Goonj' event in Pune focused on women's empowerment
  • BJP and Congress trade barbs over the event, with BJP calling it a 'comedy show' and Congress claiming ruling party is 'shaken'
  • Rahul Gandhi urged women to challenge patriarchy and called out the 'Manusmriti mindset'

Political Clash Over Rahul Gandhi's Pune Event

Congress leader Rahul Gandhi's recent 'Chhatron Ki Goonj' event in Pune has sparked a political debate between the ruling party and the opposition. The event, aimed at empowering women, has been described as a 'political comedy show' by the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP), while Congress claims the ruling party is 'shaken' by the youth appeal.

Event Focuses on Women's Empowerment

The 'Chhatron Ki Goonj' event was a rally organized by Rahul Gandhi to address issues related to women's empowerment. He urged women to challenge patriarchy and called out the 'Manusmriti mindset', which he described as outdated and discriminatory.

BJP's Criticism

The BJP has criticized the event, calling it a 'political comedy show'. The party's leader, Devendra Fadnavis, described Rahul Gandhi's outreach to women as a 'showoff'. The BJP claims that the event was more about political gains than genuine women's empowerment.

Congress' Response

Congress, on the other hand, has claimed that the ruling party is 'shaken' by the youth appeal. The party has defended Rahul Gandhi's efforts, stating that the event was a genuine attempt to address women's issues and empower them.

What's Next?

The political barbs over Rahul Gandhi's Pune event are likely to continue as both parties engage in a battle of words. The event has highlighted the differing views on women's empowerment and the need for genuine change in society. As the political landscape evolves, it will be interesting to see how both parties respond to each other's criticisms and what steps they take to address the issues raised during the event.
