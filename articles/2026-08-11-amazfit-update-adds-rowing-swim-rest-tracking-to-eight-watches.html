Amazfit update adds rowing, swim rest tracking to eight watches

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Amazfit rolls out a new software update for eight smartwatch models.
  • FTMS rowing machine support and manual swim rest tracking are added.
  • Map-related improvements are also included in the update.

Amazfit has begun rolling out a fresh software update that introduces new training capabilities across eight of its smartwatch models. The update, which is being delivered in phases, adds support for FTMS rowing machines and a manual rest timer for swim workouts, alongside several map-related enhancements.

The move is part of the company's broader push to keep its wearable lineup competitive in the increasingly crowded fitness tracker market, where software updates are becoming as important as hardware specs.

FTMS rowing support

The new FTMS (Fitness Machine Service) protocol support allows Amazfit watch owners to pair their device with compatible rowing machines. This enables real-time data synchronisation, including stroke rate, distance, and calories burned, directly on the watch screen.

Previously, rowing workouts on Amazfit watches were limited to manual logging or basic activity tracking. With FTMS, users can now view metrics from the machine itself, making indoor rowing sessions more accurate and data-rich.

Manual swim rest tracking

Swimmers will also benefit from a new manual rest function in the swim workout mode. Users can now press a button to log rest periods between intervals, instead of relying solely on automatic detection.

This feature is particularly useful for structured swim training, where precise rest intervals matter. The watch records each rest segment and integrates it into the overall workout summary, giving athletes a clearer picture of their session.

Map improvements and broader rollout

Beyond the training additions, the update brings map-related improvements to several models. These enhancements are aimed at making offline maps more responsive and easier to navigate, a feature that has been a key selling point for Amazfit's mid-range watches.

The update applies to eight Amazfit watches, though the company has not yet specified the exact list of models. Based on recent releases, it is likely to include popular devices such as the Amazfit Active 3 Premium and other recent additions to the lineup.

Zepp OS 6 integration

Some reports suggest that the update is tied to the rollout of Zepp OS 6, Amazfit's latest operating system version. The Active 3 Premium, for instance, is expected to receive the new OS alongside these features, bringing a refreshed interface and improved performance.

Users who have already received the update have reported smoother operation and better battery efficiency, though official changelogs have not been published. Amazfit typically stages rollouts over several weeks to ensure stability.

What to watch

As Amazfit continues to expand its software ecosystem, the focus appears to be on bridging the gap between budget-friendly hardware and premium fitness tracking features. The addition of FTMS and manual rest tracking aligns with what users expect from more expensive rivals.

Over the coming weeks, more users should see the update appear on their devices. It remains to be seen whether Amazfit will extend these features to older models or reserve them for its newest releases.
