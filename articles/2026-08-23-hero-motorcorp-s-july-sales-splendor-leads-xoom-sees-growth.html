Hero Motorcorp's July Sales: Splendor Leads, Xoom Sees Growth

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Splendor Plus remains the top-selling bike in July
  • Xoom and Xtreme show significant growth in sales
  • Passion and Glamour see a decline in sales
  • Deluxe and Destini maintain steady sales
  • Vida continues to perform well in the mid-range segment

Splendor Plus Dominates July Sales

Hero Motorcorp's July sales report shows the Splendor Plus continuing its dominance as the top-selling bike. The Splendor Plus, known for its reliability and affordability, has maintained its position, with sales figures reflecting its consistent popularity among consumers.

Growth in Xoom and Xtreme

Two of Hero Motorcorp's premium models, Xoom and Xtreme, have shown significant growth in sales. The Xoom, a high-performance bike, has seen a notable increase in demand, while the Xtreme, a sporty bike, has also shown strong sales figures. These models are likely appealing to consumers looking for a blend of performance and style.

Decline in Passion and Glamour Sales

On the other hand, the Passion and Glamour models have seen a decline in sales. The Passion, a popular commuter bike, and the Glamour, a stylish bike, have not been able to maintain their market share as effectively as other models. This could be due to a variety of factors, including changing consumer preferences and increased competition.

Steady Sales for Deluxe and Destini

The Deluxe and Destini models have maintained steady sales figures. The Deluxe, a comfortable and stylish bike, continues to be a favorite among consumers, while the Destini, a premium bike, has also shown consistent sales. These models are likely appealing to consumers looking for a balance of style and comfort.

Vida's Performance in Mid-Range Segment

Vida continues to perform well in the mid-range segment. The Vida, a stylish and comfortable bike, has maintained its position as one of the top-selling models in this segment. Its appeal to consumers looking for a balance of style and comfort is evident in its consistent sales figures.
