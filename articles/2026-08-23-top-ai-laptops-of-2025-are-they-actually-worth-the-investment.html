What reviewers generally say: Generally seen as powerful machines, though critics frequently note that current software applications are still catching up to the hardware capabilities.

Pros
  • Dedicated NPU hardware accelerates specific AI tasks like background blurring and noise cancellation
  • Improved thermal management in newer chassis designs
  • Future-proofing for upcoming on-device AI software updates
Cons
  • Premium price tag often includes a 'tax' for AI branding that may not be fully utilized by average users
  • Software ecosystem is still fragmented, meaning some AI features are locked to specific manufacturers

These machines are excellent for power users, creative professionals, or developers who already utilize AI-driven workflows like local LLMs or generative media tools. If you are a casual user, the hardware premium is likely not worth it yet, as traditional high-performance laptops remain equally capable for standard productivity tasks.

Skip the 'AI' marketing hype if you are looking for a basic browsing or document-editing machine; you will get better value by prioritizing RAM and display quality over an integrated NPU.
