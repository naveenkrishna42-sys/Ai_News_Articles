Hyderabad to host sunrise yoga and coffee rave for Friendship Day

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Event combines sunrise yoga with a silent coffee rave for Friendship Day in Hyderabad.
  • Organizers aim to foster community bonding through wellness and music.
  • Participants will enjoy guided yoga, followed by a silent disco with coffee themes.

Hyderabad is gearing up for an unusual Friendship Day celebration this year. The city will host a sunrise yoga session followed by a silent coffee rave, blending wellness with community spirit.

The event, organized by local wellness groups, is set to take place at a prominent park in the city. Organizers hope to attract hundreds of participants looking to mark the day with mindfulness and movement.

What the event entails

The morning will begin with a guided yoga session at sunrise, designed to promote relaxation and connection. Instructors will lead participants through a series of poses suitable for all skill levels.

After the yoga session, the venue will transform into a silent disco-style coffee rave. Attendees will wear wireless headphones, tuning into curated playlists while sipping on freshly brewed coffee. The combination aims to create a shared yet personalized experience.

A focus on community and wellness

Organizers have emphasized that the event is not just about fitness or music. It is about fostering genuine connections in a city that never seems to slow down.

“Friendship Day is often commercialized. We wanted to offer something that brings people together in a meaningful way,” a spokesperson for the organizing team said.

The event is open to all ages, and tickets are priced to remain accessible. Proceeds from the ticket sales will go toward local mental health initiatives, adding a layer of social responsibility to the celebration.

Logistics and participation

Participants are advised to bring their own yoga mats and arrive early to secure a spot. The park has ample space, but organizers expect a large turnout given the novelty of the concept.

Headphones will be provided on-site for the coffee rave segment. The playlist will feature a mix of genres, from ambient beats to upbeat tracks, ensuring there is something for everyone.

Local cafes are partnering with the event to offer special coffee blends. Some vendors will also set up stalls selling organic snacks and beverages.

What to expect on the day

The schedule is tight. Sunrise yoga begins at 5:30 AM sharp, followed by the coffee rave from 7:00 AM to 9:00 AM. Organizers recommend participants stay hydrated and dress comfortably.

Security arrangements are in place, and volunteers will guide attendees through the venue. The event will conclude with a group photo and a community circle, where participants can share their experiences.

Hyderabad has seen a rise in such experiential events over the past year, reflecting a broader trend toward wellness tourism and community-driven activities.

For now, the city waits to see how this fusion of sunrise yoga and a coffee rave will resonate with its residents. If successful, organizers plan to make it an annual fixture on Hyderabad's event calendar.
