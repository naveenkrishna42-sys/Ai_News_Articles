Spinny, the popular online platform for buying and selling used cars, has recently adjusted the prices of used cars to account for the new GST rates. This adjustment aims to provide a more accurate reflection of the market value of these vehicles, taking into account the additional tax burden imposed by the government.

The new GST rates, which came into effect on July 1, 2023, have had a significant impact on the used car market. The adjustment by Spinny is a response to these changes, ensuring that buyers and sellers are aware of the true value of the vehicles being traded. This move is expected to help in maintaining transparency and fairness in the used car market.
