UP Woman Kills Husband With Stone, Daughter and Boyfriend Help

📅 2026-08-09 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Woman allegedly killed her husband by crushing his head with a stone in Mathura, UP.
  • Her daughter and the daughter's boyfriend reportedly assisted in the crime.
  • The body was burned in a forest; police have launched a search for the accused.

In a chilling incident from Uttar Pradesh's Mathura district, a woman allegedly murdered her husband by crushing his head with a stone, with her daughter and the daughter's boyfriend reportedly helping her. The trio then attempted to destroy evidence by burning the body in a nearby forest, police said.

Crime Unfolds in Mathura

The incident came to light after local residents spotted a partially burnt body in a forested area and alerted authorities. Upon reaching the spot, police found the body in a charred state, with preliminary examination suggesting a brutal assault before the burning.

Investigators soon zeroed in on the victim's wife based on intelligence and local inquiries. Sources said the woman, along with her daughter and the latter's boyfriend, had allegedly planned the murder, though the exact motive remains unclear.

Sedated With Milk, Then Attacked

According to preliminary findings, the victim may have been sedated before the attack. Reports suggest he was given milk laced with a sedative, after which the accused allegedly smashed his head with a heavy stone, causing fatal injuries.

The brutality of the killing has shocked the local community. The accused then allegedly dragged the body to a forest patch and set it ablaze to destroy evidence, but the partially burned remains were discovered before they could be fully disposed of.

Police Launch Manhunt

Police have registered a case and launched a search for the accused, who are believed to be on the run. Senior officials have visited the crime scene, and forensic teams have been deployed to collect evidence, including the stone allegedly used in the attack.

"The body has been sent for post-mortem, and we are questioning relatives and neighbours to piece together the sequence of events," a police officer said on condition of anonymity. The officer added that teams have been formed to trace the accused, and their last known locations are being verified.

Community in Shock

The incident has left the neighbourhood in disbelief, with many struggling to come to terms with the alleged involvement of the victim's own family. Local residents said the couple had been living together, and no major disputes were publicly known.

Authorities have urged anyone with information about the accused's whereabouts to come forward. Meanwhile, police are examining call detail records and social media activity to establish the conspiracy and the roles of each accused.

As the investigation progresses, all eyes are on the post-mortem report and forensic analysis, which could provide crucial insights into the exact cause of death and the sequence of events leading up to the murder. The search for the accused continues, and police have assured that strict action will be taken once they are apprehended.
