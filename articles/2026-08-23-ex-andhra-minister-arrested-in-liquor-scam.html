Ex-Andhra Minister Arrested in Liquor Scam

📅 2026-08-23 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Former Andhra Pradesh minister Karumuri Nageswara Rao arrested by Enforcement Directorate (ED)
  • Alleged involvement in Rs 1,000 crore scam related to APSBCL liquor transport tender
  • ED case filed under Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA)

Former Minister Arrested by ED

Karumuri Nageswara Rao, a former Andhra Pradesh minister, has been arrested by the Enforcement Directorate (ED) in connection with a Rs 1,000 crore liquor transport scam. The ED filed a case against him under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) on August 23, 2026.

Alleged Scam Involving APSBCL

Rao is accused of his involvement in the alleged scam related to the Andhra Pradesh State Beverages Corporation Limited (APSBCL) liquor transport tender. The tender was allegedly awarded to a company owned by Rao and his associates.

Background of the Case

The case stems from an investigation into the alleged irregularities in the tender process for transporting liquor from the APSBCL warehouses to retail outlets. The ED has alleged that the tender was awarded to a company owned by Rao and his associates, who then allegedly paid a bribe to secure the contract.

Legal Proceedings

The ED has filed a charge sheet against Rao and his associates, alleging that they received kickbacks and other illegal gratifications in exchange for awarding the tender. The case is currently under investigation, and Rao is expected to appear in court soon.

Impact on Politics

Rao's arrest has raised questions about the integrity of the former Andhra Pradesh government. His political party has distanced itself from him, and there are calls for a probe into his tenure as a minister. The case has also highlighted the need for stricter vigilance against corruption in government tenders.
