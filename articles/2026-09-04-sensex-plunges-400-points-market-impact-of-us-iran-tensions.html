Sensex Plunges 400 Points: Market Impact of US-Iran Tensions

Key points

Market Performance Overview

The Indian stock market experienced significant volatility on September 3, 2026, as the BSE Sensex closed 417 points lower, settling at 78,683, a decline of 0.51%. Concurrently, the NSE Nifty 50 fell below 24,000, closing at 23,892, a drop of 0.49%. These movements were largely influenced by escalating tensions between the United States and Iran.

Sectoral Performance

Several sectors exhibited varying degrees of performance during the trading session. The IT sector, a traditional market darling, saw a decline, contributing to the overall market downturn. Similarly, the FMCG sector also underperformed, dragging the market sentiment lower. However, the realty and banking sectors showed resilience, with the banking sector posting gains amidst the volatility.

Investor Sentiment and Market Predictions

Investor sentiment was largely negative, with many attributing the market decline to the geopolitical tensions. Analysts predict that the market may continue to be volatile in the coming days, with potential further declines if the US-Iran situation worsens. However, the banking and realty sectors are expected to remain stable, offering some stability amidst the market turmoil.

Technical Analysis

Technical analysts are closely monitoring the market for any further declines or rebounds. The current market levels are below key support levels, which could trigger further selling if not supported. Conversely, a rebound above the recent highs could signal a bullish sentiment. Traders are advised to keep a close watch on these levels for potential trading opportunities.

Conclusion

In conclusion, the Indian stock market experienced a significant decline on September 3, 2026, amid escalating US-Iran tensions. While the IT and FMCG sectors underperformed, the banking and realty sectors showed resilience. Investor sentiment remains negative, with analysts predicting further volatility. Traders are advised to monitor key support and resistance levels for potential trading opportunities.
