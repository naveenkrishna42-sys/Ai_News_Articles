iPhone 18 Pro Max leaks: What we know about launch, design, display

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • iPhone 18 Pro Max is expected to launch in September alongside iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Fold.
  • Leaks suggest a redesigned camera module, larger display, and a potential price hike of up to Rs 26,000 in India.
  • A spring delay in the regular iPhone 18 could boost autumn sales of the Pro models, according to analysts.
  • Rumoured features include a new camera sensor, improved zoom, and a sleek titanium frame.

Leak season is here

With each passing week, the rumour mill around Apple's next flagship grows louder. The iPhone 18 Pro Max, expected to debut this September, is already at the centre of a storm of leaks — covering everything from its launch date to its design, display, and even a possible price hike in India. While Apple has kept its cards close to its chest, the sheer volume of credible reports suggests the company is gearing up for one of its most significant upgrades in years.

According to multiple reports, the iPhone 18 Pro Max will launch alongside the iPhone 18 Pro and the much-anticipated iPhone Fold in September. The timing aligns with Apple's traditional release cycle, though some analysts have pointed to a curious twist: a reported spring delay for the standard iPhone 18 could push more buyers toward the Pro models in the autumn.

Design and display: What to expect

Leaks point to a refined design, with Apple expected to stick to its titanium frame but introduce a sleeker profile. The display is rumoured to grow slightly, possibly hitting 6.9 inches on the Pro Max, with thinner bezels and improved brightness. Some reports suggest Apple will finally move to a hole-punch camera design, eliminating the Dynamic Island — a change that could redefine the phone's front look.

The camera module is also expected to undergo a redesign. Rumoured features include a periscope lens with enhanced optical zoom, a larger main sensor for better low-light shots, and improved image processing. A report from NDTV highlighted five rumoured features that look legit, including a new anti-reflective coating and upgraded video recording capabilities.

Price hike concerns in India

Indian buyers may need to brace for a heavier bill. The Economic Times has reported that the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max could cost up to Rs 26,000 more than their predecessors. This would mark a significant jump, driven by rising component costs and a weaker rupee. While Apple has not confirmed any pricing, the leak has already sparked debate among fans and analysts alike.

If the hike materialises, the iPhone 18 Pro Max could cross the Rs 1.6 lakh mark in India, putting it firmly in the ultra-premium segment. The move would test the loyalty of Apple's Indian user base, which has grown steadily over the past few years.

Spring delay and autumn boost

Forbes reported that a spring delay for the standard iPhone 18 could actually benefit the Pro models. With the base model arriving later than expected, buyers who don't want to wait may opt for the Pro or Pro Max, giving Apple a stronger sales push in the final quarter of the year. This strategy, whether intentional or not, could help Apple offset any early-year slowdown.

TelecomTalk, meanwhile, has reiterated that the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Fold are slated for a September launch, with the Fold marking Apple's entry into a category it has long avoided. The foldable's debut could overshadow the Pro Max, but the latter is expected to remain the volume driver.

What to watch next

As September approaches, more concrete details are likely to emerge — from official invites to regulatory filings. The biggest questions remain the final pricing in India, the fate of the Dynamic Island, and how the iPhone Fold will complement the Pro lineup. For now, all eyes are on Apple's next move.
