Vodafone Idea Reaffirms Growth Targets, $4.7 Billion Capex Plan

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Q1 FY27 net loss narrows to ₹3,754 crore
  • ARPU grows 10% year-on-year
  • Company reaffirms $4.7 billion capex plan for network expansion
  • CEO Abhijit Kishore optimistic about PSU bank funding talks

Vodafone Idea, India's third-largest telecom operator, has reaffirmed its three-year growth targets and a capital expenditure plan of $4.7 billion, even as it reported a narrower quarterly loss. The company's Q1 FY27 results, released Tuesday, show early signs of recovery as it invests in network upgrades to stem subscriber losses.

Loss narrows, ARPU rises

The telecom operator's net loss for the April-June quarter stood at ₹3,754 crore, an improvement from previous quarters. Revenue growth was buoyed by a 10% year-on-year increase in average revenue per user (ARPU), a key metric for telecom profitability.

Executives attribute the ARPU growth to a mix of tariff hikes and a shift towards higher-value plans. The company has been focusing on retaining premium customers while expanding 4G coverage in rural areas.

$4.7 billion capex roadmap

Vodafone Idea has stuck to its capital expenditure plan of $4.7 billion, aimed at rolling out 5G services and modernising existing infrastructure. The funds are critical for the carrier to compete with Reliance Jio and Bharti Airtel, which have already launched 5G across major cities.

Management said on an earnings call that the capex will be deployed over the next three years. The company expects this investment cycle to improve network quality and, in turn, boost subscriber retention.

Funding talks with PSU banks

CEO Abhijit Kishore expressed optimism about closing funding discussions with public sector banks. The company has been in talks with lenders to secure additional debt financing, which would complement the equity raised earlier.

"We are confident of concluding these discussions," Kishore said during the earnings call. He did not specify a timeline but indicated that the talks are progressing constructively.

The company has already secured vendor financing and has been in talks with multiple lenders to fund its turnaround plan. A successful closure of these discussions would provide Vodafone Idea with the liquidity needed to execute its network expansion.

Market context and challenges

The telecom sector in India remains fiercely competitive, with three private players and one state-owned operator vying for market share. Vodafone Idea has lost ground over the past few years, and its subscriber base continues to decline, though at a slower pace.

Analysts note that the company's focus on ARPU growth and cost optimisation is yielding results. However, the real test lies in executing the capex plan on time and converting network investments into market share gains.

The company also faces the challenge of upgrading its existing 4G sites to 5G, a process that requires substantial capital and logistical coordination. Vodafone Idea has not yet given a specific launch date for its 5G services.

What to watch

Investors will closely monitor the outcome of the PSU bank funding talks in the coming quarters. A successful closure would clear the path for the company to accelerate its rollout plans and potentially stabilise its subscriber base.
