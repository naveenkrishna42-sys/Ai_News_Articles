Sensex surges 1,000 points intraday, Nifty closes above 24,300

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • BSE Sensex surged 1,000 points during intraday trade before closing 274 points higher
  • Nifty50 crossed 24,250 mark and ended above 24,300
  • Auto stocks led the rally with strong gains across the sector
  • Late buying in the final hour lifted benchmarks from session lows

Markets rebound sharply on broad-based buying

Indian equity benchmarks staged a strong recovery on Wednesday, with the BSE Sensex rallying 1,000 points during intraday trade before closing 274 points higher. The Nifty50 crossed the 24,250 mark during the session and settled comfortably above 24,300.

The rebound came after a volatile morning session that saw indices dip into negative territory. Analysts attributed the sharp turnaround to value buying at lower levels and strong momentum in auto stocks.

Auto stocks lead the charge

The auto sector emerged as the biggest gainer of the day, with major manufacturers posting strong gains. The rally was broad-based, with buying interest spreading across other sectors as the session progressed.

Market participants pointed to positive sentiment around upcoming festive season demand as a key driver for auto stocks. Dealers reported healthy inventory clearance, which boosted investor confidence in the sector.

Late buying seals the day's gains

The final hour of trade saw a flurry of buying activity, particularly from institutional investors. This late surge helped the Sensex trim its intraday gains but ensured a positive close.

The Nifty50's ability to hold above the psychologically important 24,300 level was seen as a bullish signal by traders. Volumes were higher than the recent average, indicating genuine buying interest rather than short-covering.

Broader market participation remains mixed

While the frontline indices posted strong gains, the broader market showed a more cautious trend. Midcap and smallcap stocks saw selective buying, with the advance-decline ratio remaining balanced.

Foreign portfolio investors were net buyers in the cash segment, while domestic institutions also added to their positions. Market participants will watch for sustained foreign inflows in the coming sessions.

What to watch next

Traders will closely monitor global cues and the upcoming monthly derivatives expiry for directional cues. The ability of the Nifty50 to sustain above 24,300 will be key for further upside in the near term.
