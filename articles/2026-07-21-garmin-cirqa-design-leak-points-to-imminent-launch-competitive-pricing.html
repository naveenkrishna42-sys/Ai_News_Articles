Garmin Cirqa Design Leak Points to Imminent Launch, Competitive Pricing

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Product images of the Garmin CIRQA have surfaced online in four color variants.
  • The screenless fitness tracker is reportedly priced at 199 euros, undercutting rivals.
  • Leaks suggest an official launch announcement could come as early as tomorrow.
  • The device appears designed for users seeking minimalist, distraction-free fitness tracking.

Leaked images and pricing details for Garmin's upcoming CIRQA smart band have flooded tech forums, pointing to a launch that may be just hours away. The screenless fitness tracker, which has been the subject of speculation for months, is now believed to carry a price tag of 199 euros in European markets.

The news broke across multiple tech outlets late Monday, with Notebookcheck and Android Authority among those reporting that final design renders and launch dates have been confirmed through unofficial channels. Garmin has not issued a statement on the matter.

Four Colors, No Screen

The leaked images show the CIRQA in four colour variants: black, white, blue, and an orange-tinged option. The device appears compact and lightweight, with a simple band form factor that houses sensors but no display.

This screenless design is a deliberate choice. The CIRQA is expected to rely entirely on haptic feedback and a companion smartphone app for notifications and data readouts. It targets users who want fitness tracking without the distraction of a screen.

Price Point Draws Attention

The reported price of 199 euros places the CIRQA well below many premium fitness wearables. For context, the Fitbit Charge 6 and similar devices from Samsung and Xiaomi often cost significantly more, particularly when they include colour touchscreens.

By stripping out the display, Garmin appears to be betting that battery life and simplicity will win over a segment of the market that finds smartwatches over-engineered. The lower price could also help the CIRQA compete against brands like Whoop, which uses a subscription model for its screenless tracker.

Launch Almost Certainly Tomorrow

Multiple sources, including the blog Garmin Rumors, have stated that an official announcement is expected tomorrow. That timeline aligns with the typical product reveal cycle the company has followed for its wearable launches in recent years.

If the launch does happen within 24 hours, it would be an unusually short gap between a major leak and official unveiling. That could indicate that the leak originated close to the company's internal distribution chain, or that Garmin planned a rapid rollout regardless.

What the CIRQA Offers

Based on the leaked materials, the CIRQA will focus on core fitness metrics: step count, heart rate, sleep tracking, and activity recognition. Without a screen, it will lack GPS maps, music controls, and app notifications beyond simple buzzes.

That minimalism may be its biggest selling point. Many users have complained about notification overload from smartwatches. A band that only tracks movement and sends silent alerts could carve out a niche among runners, swimmers, and gym-goers who prefer analog focus.

The device is expected to sync wirelessly with both Android and iOS devices, though Garmin's own app ecosystem has historically been stronger on Android.

All eyes are on Garmin's social media channels and press release distribution lists for a formal announcement. If the leaks are accurate, the CIRQA will be available for pre-order within days, with shipments likely starting in August.
