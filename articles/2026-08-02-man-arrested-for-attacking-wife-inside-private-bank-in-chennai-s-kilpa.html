Man arrested for attacking wife inside private bank in Chennai's Kilpauk

  • The incident took place on Saturday; police have taken the accused into custody.
  • The woman's condition and the motive behind the attack are yet to be officially confirmed.

Chennai police on Saturday arrested a man for allegedly hacking his wife with a sharp weapon inside a private bank in Kilpauk. The incident, which took place in broad daylight, has shocked the local community and raised questions about security in financial institutions.

Attack inside bank premises

According to preliminary reports, the man entered the bank and attacked his wife, who was present there. The woman sustained injuries in the assault. Bank staff and customers present at the scene alerted the police, who arrived and took the accused into custody.

The accused has been identified as the husband of the victim, though authorities have not yet released their names. The exact sequence of events leading up to the attack remains unclear. Police are examining CCTV footage from the bank to piece together what happened.

Victim rushed to hospital

The injured woman was immediately rushed to a nearby hospital for treatment. Her current condition has not been officially confirmed by medical authorities. A police official said that she is receiving medical care and that further details will be shared once her condition is assessed.

Investigators are also looking into the motive behind the attack. Initial reports suggest a possible domestic dispute, but the police have not confirmed this. Neighbours and relatives of the couple have not yet been questioned publicly.

Police investigation underway

The Kilpauk police have registered a case and are conducting a thorough investigation. They are recording statements from eyewitnesses and bank employees. The weapon used in the attack has been recovered, police said.

Security protocols at the bank are also under review. This incident has highlighted concerns about how easily a person can enter a bank and carry out an act of violence. Bank officials have not issued a statement so far.

Legal proceedings ahead

The accused is currently in police custody and is expected to be produced before a magistrate court. Charges are likely to include attempt to murder under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, though formal charges have not been announced.
