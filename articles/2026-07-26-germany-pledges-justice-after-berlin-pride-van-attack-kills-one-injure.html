Germany pledges justice after Berlin Pride van attack kills one, injures 16

📅 2026-07-26 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • A van plowed into a crowd near Berlin's Pride event, killing one and injuring 16.
  • German leaders condemned the attack and pledged a full investigation.
  • Police are actively hunting the suspect; motive remains unclear.

Deadly ramming at Berlin Pride

A van slammed into a group of people gathered for Berlin's annual LGBTQIA+ Pride event on Saturday, killing at least one person and injuring 16 others. The attack occurred near the city's central parade route, sending panic through the festive crowd.

Police cordoned off the area and launched an urgent manhunt for the suspect, who fled the scene on foot. Authorities have not yet released details about the driver or a possible motive.

Germany vows justice

German leaders reacted swiftly. Chancellor Olaf Scholz called the attack a "cowardly act" and promised that the perpetrators would face the full force of the law. Interior Minister Nancy Faeser said the government would do everything to ensure justice for the victims and their families.

Berlin's mayor, Kai Wegner, visited the scene and expressed solidarity with the LGBTQIA+ community. "This was an attack on our values of freedom and diversity," he said. "We will not be intimidated."

Search for the suspect

Police have not confirmed whether the attack is being treated as terrorism. Investigators are reviewing CCTV footage and speaking to witnesses. Checkpoints have been set up across the city, and officers are combing nearby neighbourhoods.

The injured were taken to hospitals across Berlin. Officials have not yet provided a full list of their conditions, but some are reported to be in serious but stable condition.

Mourning and defiance

The attack cast a shadow over what was meant to be a celebration of LGBTQIA+ rights. Pride organisers cancelled the remainder of the event, urging attendees to stay safe and head home. Many instead gathered at spontaneous vigils, holding rainbow flags and candles.

"We are heartbroken but we will not be silenced," one organiser told reporters. Similar Pride events in other German cities have since stepped up security measures, with police presence visibly increased.

What happens next

German authorities continue to appeal for public assistance in tracking down the suspect. The investigation remains in its early stages, with officials expected to provide updates as more details emerge. The attack has reignited debates about security at public events and hate crimes against the LGBTQIA+ community in Germany.
