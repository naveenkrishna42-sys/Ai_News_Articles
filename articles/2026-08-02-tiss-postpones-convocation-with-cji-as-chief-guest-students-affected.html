TISS postpones convocation with CJI as chief guest; students affected

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • TISS has postponed its convocation ceremony, which was to feature the Chief Justice of India as chief guest.
  • Students who had travelled to Mumbai for the ceremony are among those affected by the decision.
  • The institute has not yet announced a revised date for the event.

The Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS) has postponed its upcoming convocation ceremony, which was scheduled to have the Chief Justice of India as the chief guest. The decision, communicated to students and faculty on Saturday, has left many graduates who travelled to Mumbai for the event in a lurch.

Reason for postponement

Officials at the institute have not specified the reason for the deferment. The convocation was originally planned for early August, and the Chief Justice had confirmed his participation. However, administrative sources suggest that logistical issues may have played a role, though no official statement has been issued.

Students from various parts of the country, some of whom had booked flights and accommodation weeks in advance, received emails informing them of the change. Many expressed frustration on social media, saying they had made significant arrangements to attend the ceremony in person.

Impact on graduating students

The postponement affects hundreds of graduating students across TISS's multiple campuses in Mumbai, Tuljapur, Guwahati, and Hyderabad. For those who had travelled specifically for the convocation, the sudden change has caused both financial and emotional strain.

  • Students have been asked to await further communication regarding a revised schedule.
  • The institute has not announced whether the ceremony will be held virtually or in person.

Some students have sought refunds for travel bookings, but there is no clarity on whether the institute will assist with such claims. A few alumni groups have urged the administration to consider a hybrid format to accommodate those who cannot make another trip.

Historical significance

The convocation was notable not just for the chief guest, but also because it was expected to be one of the first major academic ceremonies at TISS since the institute underwent administrative restructuring. The presence of the Chief Justice was seen as a mark of prestige, and his address was anticipated to touch upon issues of social justice and legal education.

This is not the first time a convocation has faced a last-minute change. Similar instances at other universities have occurred due to unforeseen circumstances, but the lack of prior notice has drawn criticism from students who feel their time and resources were not respected.

What happens next

TISS has said it will announce a new date "in due course," but no timeline has been provided. Students are advised to monitor official channels for updates. The institute's decision to postpone, rather than cancel, suggests that the ceremony will eventually take place, possibly with the same chief guest.

For now, affected students are left to juggle their plans, with many hoping the institute will offer some form of accommodation or reimbursement. The coming days will reveal whether TISS can manage the fallout and reschedule the event without further disruption.
