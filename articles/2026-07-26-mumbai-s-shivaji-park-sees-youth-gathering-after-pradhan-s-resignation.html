Mumbai’s Shivaji Park sees youth gathering after Pradhan’s resignation

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Mumbai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hundreds of young people assembled at Shivaji Park in Mumbai.
  • The gathering was to celebrate the resignation of Union Minister Dharmendra Pradhan.
  • Speeches and slogans dominated the atmosphere at the historic venue.

Youth gather at iconic Mumbai ground

Hundreds of young people converged at Mumbai’s Shivaji Park on Sunday evening to celebrate the resignation of Union Minister Dharmendra Pradhan. The historic ground, known for political rallies and sporting events, turned into a sea of youth as news of the resignation spread.

Chants and slogans filled the air as participants waved flags and banners. Many in the crowd were students and young professionals who had come from different parts of the city.

Celebrations and speeches mark the event

The atmosphere was festive. Speakers took to a makeshift stage to address the gathering. They praised the decision and called it a victory for the people. The crowd responded with loud cheers and applause.

Some attendees carried placards with messages directed at the minister. Others simply clapped and sang. The event was largely peaceful, with no reports of any untoward incident.

Shivaji Park’s legacy as a political venue

Shivaji Park has a long history of hosting political gatherings. From the Samyukta Maharashtra movement to modern-day rallies, the ground has been a witness to many turning points. Sunday’s gathering added another chapter to that legacy.

Police were present on the ground to manage the crowd. Traffic on nearby roads was affected for a few hours. Local residents watched from balconies and terraces as the crowd swelled and later dispersed.

Unclear what prompted the resignation

Dharmendra Pradhan’s resignation came as a surprise to many. The Union Minister had been in the news recently for his work in the education and petroleum sectors. Officials have not yet confirmed the reasons behind his decision.

Political circles are abuzz with speculation. Some say it was due to personal reasons. Others point to internal party dynamics. No official statement has been released from the minister’s office or the party high command.

The young crowd at Shivaji Park did not wait for clarity. They came to celebrate, and celebrate they did. As night fell, the ground emptied, leaving behind banners and the echo of slogans.

What remains to be seen is how the resignation will affect the political landscape in the coming weeks. The youth, it seems, have already made up their minds.
