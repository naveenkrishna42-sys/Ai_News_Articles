The Odyssey Crosses $1 Billion, Nolan's First Since Dark Knight Rises

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The Odyssey has grossed over $1 billion worldwide at the box office.
  • It is Christopher Nolan's first film to hit this milestone since The Dark Knight Rises.
  • The film is on track to become Nolan's highest-grossing release ever.
  • It is close to becoming the third-highest-earning R-rated film of all time.

Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey has sailed past the $1 billion mark at the global box office, according to studio figures released on Friday. The epic mythological drama becomes the director's first film to achieve the feat since The Dark Knight Rises in 2012.

The film, which reimagines Homer's ancient Greek poem with a star-studded cast, has been a dominant force in theatres since its release. Industry trackers confirm it is now the fastest-grossing Nolan film ever, outpacing the inflation-adjusted runs of his earlier blockbusters.

A Return to Billion-Dollar Territory

Nolan's previous billion-dollar entry was the Batman trilogy closer, which amassed over $1.08 billion worldwide. But The Odyssey is already closing in on that figure, with analysts at Box Office Mojo projecting it will soon surpass it.

The movie's commercial performance has stunned even seasoned trade watchers. It crossed the halfway mark within two weeks of release, driven by strong word-of-mouth and repeat viewings in premium formats like IMAX and 70mm.

R-Rated Record in Sight

What makes this milestone particularly notable is the film's R rating. Only two R-rated films in history have ever grossed more than $1 billion: Joker (2019) and Deadpool & Wolverine (2024). The Odyssey is now racing to become the third, and could soon overtake one of them.

Trade reports suggest the film has already outpaced Oppenheimer — Nolan's previous R-rated hit that earned $975 million — and is on track to become his highest-grossing film ever. The director's personal best remains The Dark Knight (2008) with $1.006 billion, a figure The Odyssey has already eclipsed.

What's Driving the Box Office

Unlike typical superhero tentpoles, The Odyssey has relied on critical acclaim and spectacle rather than franchise familiarity. Reviews have praised its visual grandeur and faithful yet accessible adaptation, making it a rare event film for adult audiences.

International markets have been particularly strong. India and China have contributed significantly, with the film's mythological resonance finding eager audiences across Asia. European markets have also held steady, pushing the global tally past the milestone faster than many had expected.

The film's success also reinforces Nolan's unique standing in Hollywood — a director whose name alone guarantees global attention. His films routinely open large, but The Odyssey has shown unusual legs, sustaining strong weekday numbers well into its third month.

What Comes Next

With the billion-dollar milestone now crossed, attention turns to how much higher The Odyssey can climb. It still has major markets like Japan and South Korea yet to fully peak, and a home-entertainment release is unlikely before the holiday season.

For Nolan, the achievement cements a remarkable decade. After Oppenheimer's near-miss, he now has another bonafide billion-dollar hit — and one that could reset expectations for what R-rated cinema can achieve commercially.

All eyes will be on whether The Odyssey can topple Joker's $1.07 billion record, which would make it the highest-grossing R-rated film of all time. That outcome now seems less a question of if, and more of when.
