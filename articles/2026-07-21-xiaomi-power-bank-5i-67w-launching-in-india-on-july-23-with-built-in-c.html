Xiaomi Power Bank 5i 67W Launching in India on July 23 with Built-In Cable

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Xiaomi Power Bank 5i 67W launching in India on July 23.
  • 20,000mAh capacity with 67W fast charging support.
  • Built-in Type-C cable eliminates need for separate cord.
  • Can charge laptops and other high-power devices.
  • Teased via Amazon India ahead of official launch.

Xiaomi Confirms India Launch Date for New Power Bank

Xiaomi is set to launch its latest portable charger, the Xiaomi Power Bank 5i 67W, in India on July 23. The company confirmed the date through a teaser on Amazon India, revealing key details about the device.

The power bank packs a 20,000mAh capacity and supports 67W fast charging. It is designed to work with smartphones, tablets, and even laptops, making it a versatile option for users who need to charge multiple devices on the go.

Built-In USB-C Cable Eliminates a Common Hassle

A standout feature of the Power Bank 5i is its integrated Type-C cable. Users will no longer need to carry a separate charging cord, reducing clutter and the risk of forgetting a cable at home.

The built-in cable is expected to support the same 67W output, allowing for rapid charging of compatible devices. Xiaomi has not yet specified the exact output for laptop charging, but the power bank is marketed as capable of powering notebooks and ultrabooks.

Pricing and Availability Details Remain Under Wraps

Xiaomi has not disclosed the official price of the Power Bank 5i 67W. However, given the brand's history of aggressive pricing in India, analysts expect the device to be priced competitively against rivals like Anker and Realme.

The power bank will be available through Amazon India and likely Xiaomi's own Mi Store and other retail partners from July 23. Color options and warranty details are yet to be announced.

Laptop Charging Support Adds Practical Value

With more people working and studying remotely, the ability to charge a laptop from a power bank has become a sought-after feature. The 67W output is sufficient for most ultrabooks and some mainstream laptops, though users with high-power gaming laptops may need to check compatibility.

Xiaomi claims the power bank uses advanced safety features to prevent overheating and overcharging. The device also supports pass-through charging, allowing the power bank itself to be charged while it charges other devices.

What to Watch Next

All eyes will be on the pricing when the Xiaomi Power Bank 5i 67W launches on July 23. If Xiaomi prices it aggressively, it could set a new benchmark in the high-capacity power bank segment in India. Amazon listings are expected to go live on launch day.
