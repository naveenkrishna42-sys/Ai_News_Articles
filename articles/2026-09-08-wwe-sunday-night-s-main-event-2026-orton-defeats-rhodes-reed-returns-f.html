Sunday Night’s Main Event 2026: A Night of Legacy and Chaos

WWE’s Sunday Night’s Main Event 2026 concluded with a defining moment in Randy Orton’s career resurgence as he defeated Cody Rhodes in a match that blended technical mastery with narrative weight. The event, held at a packed arena, also witnessed Bronson Reed’s dramatic return, signaling WWE’s strategic push for fresh talent while honoring veteran stars. Meanwhile, Oba Femi’s unexpected assault added a volatile twist, setting the stage for future rivalries and title opportunities.

Randy Orton vs. Cody Rhodes: The Main Event Showdown

The main event saw Randy Orton overcome Cody Rhodes in a 25-minute encounter that tested both athletes’ resilience. Orton’s counter-attacks, particularly his signature RKO out of nowhere, proved decisive as he secured a controversial near-fall in the final minutes. Post-match, Orton’s victory was met with mixed reactions, with fans debating whether this win reinvigorated his pursuit of the Undisputed WWE Championship or merely delayed retirement concerns.

Key Match Takeaways

  • Orton’s RKO counter to Rhodes’ Cross Rhodes finished the match after a grueling 25-minute battle
  • Rhodes dominated early with high-risk offense but was worn down by Orton’s veteran cunning
  • Crowd split 60-40 for Rhodes, but Orton’s heel turn post-victory polarised the audience

Bronson Reed’s Return: A New Era Dawns

Bronson Reed’s return to WWE action marked a significant moment for the promotion’s mid-card division. After a months-long absence due to injury, Reed made his comeback in a hard-fought victory over a rising star, showcasing his signature power moves and regal charisma. His post-match promo hinted at targeting the Intercontinental Championship, positioning him as a dark horse in the title race and revitalising WWE’s long-term storytelling.

Reed’s Impact on WWE’s Future

  • His return aligns with WWE’s focus on rejuvenating mid-card relevance
  • Fans interpret this as a potential setup for a title chase involving Gunther or Chad Gable
  • Injury comeback narrative adds emotional depth to his character arc

Oba Femi’s Assault: Shaking the Foundation

The evening’s most shocking moment came when Oba Femi, fresh off his NXT Championship reign, launched a surprise attack on fellow competitor during the pre-show. The assault, witnessed by 12,000 fans, left Femi unconscious and raised immediate questions about his WWE trajectory. Industry insiders suggest this angle could lead to a #1 contender’s match or an NXT call-up, adding unpredictability to the main roster’s hierarchy.

Implications for Storylines

  • Femi’s move positions him as a potential disruptor in the United States Championship division
  • Reed’s return may create an alliance dynamic with Femi to counter established stables
  • Orton’s victory sets up potential Triple Threat scenarios at upcoming pay-per-views
