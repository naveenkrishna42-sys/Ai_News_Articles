# 70 — TivraNews Affiliate & Commercial Governance

Affiliate links are commercial assets that require strict contextual alignment and transparent disclosure.

## 1. Approved Networks Only
- Affiliate links may originate only from configured networks (Amazon Associates, Cuelinks, verified direct merchants).
- Never invent or fabricate affiliate tracking parameters or redirect paths.

## 2. Strict Contextual Matching
- The call-to-action (CTA) must match the user's reading intent:
  - **Fixed Deposit / Banking Articles**: Link only to legitimate bank comparison / savings tools; never insert unrelated hosting or web tools.
  - **Credit Card Articles**: Link to official bank card portals or accredited financial aggregators (BankBazaar / Paisabazaar via Cuelinks).
  - **Gadget / Deals Articles**: Link to the exact product or store on Amazon or Flipkart.
  - **Fashion Articles**: Link to Ajio, Myntra, or Tata CLiQ.

## 3. Compliance & Disclosure
- Every commercial link must be clearly labelled `(paid link)` or carry `rel="nofollow sponsored noopener noreferrer"`.
- Maintain consistent disclosures across `affiliate-disclosure.html`, `privacy.html`, and individual article buyboxes.
