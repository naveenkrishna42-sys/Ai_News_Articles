Background and Appointment

Air Marshal Jitendra Mishra (Retd.), a seasoned officer with over 35 years of service in the Indian Air Force, has been appointed as the first Chief Executive Officer of the Ram Temple Trust. This appointment marks a significant milestone in the management of the newly constructed Ram Temple in Ayodhya. Mishra's extensive experience in large-scale infrastructure projects and security management makes him a suitable candidate for this role.

Professional Experience

Mishra's career in the Indian Air Force includes roles such as Director General of Air Operations, Director General of Air Force Engineering, and Chief of Air Staff. His tenure spans various critical positions, including the Chief of Air Staff, where he was responsible for the overall management and operations of the Indian Air Force. His expertise in infrastructure development, project management, and security is particularly relevant to the management of the Ram Temple.

Role and Responsibilities

As the first CEO of the Ram Temple Trust, Mishra will be responsible for the overall management of the temple's construction, security, and visitor facilities. The trust has been tasked with managing the temple's operations, ensuring the smooth functioning of the temple, and addressing the needs of the pilgrims and visitors. Mishra's appointment is expected to bring a new level of professionalism and efficiency to the management of the temple.
