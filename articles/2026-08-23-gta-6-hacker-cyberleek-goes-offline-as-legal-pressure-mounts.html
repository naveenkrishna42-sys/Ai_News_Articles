GTA 6 hacker CyberLeek goes offline as legal pressure mounts

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • CyberLeek, the hacker behind recent GTA 6 leaks, has stopped posting online
  • Take-Two Interactive has subpoenaed Microsoft and Discord over the leaks
  • Rockstar Games, owned by Take-Two, is pursuing legal action against those sharing stolen content
  • GTA 6 remains one of the most anticipated game releases in the industry

Hacker Behind GTA 6 Leaks Goes Dark

CyberLeek, the online persona behind a series of Grand Theft Auto VI leaks, has stopped posting and appears to have gone offline. The disappearance comes as Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar Games' parent company, ramps up its legal response to the unauthorized disclosure of game footage and assets.

The hacker had been sharing stolen content from the upcoming game across various online platforms in recent weeks, drawing significant attention from both fans and the gaming industry.

Take-Two Subpoenas Major Tech Companies

Take-Two has moved to subpoena both Microsoft and Discord for information related to the GTA 6 leaks. The legal filings target the companies' roles in distributing or hosting the stolen content. Rockstar Games, which develops the Grand Theft Auto franchise, is seeking to identify individuals responsible for the unauthorized disclosures.

Discord confirmed it has received legal requests from Take-Two regarding the leaks. Microsoft has not publicly commented on the subpoenas.

Rockstar Faces Recurring Leak Problems

The CyberLeek incident marks another chapter in Rockstar's ongoing struggle with leaks of its flagship franchise. Grand Theft Auto VI has been the subject of intense industry scrutiny since its official announcement, with fans eagerly awaiting any new information about the game.

The company has historically maintained tight control over information about its upcoming titles, making leaks particularly disruptive to its marketing strategy. Officials have not disclosed the specific content that was leaked or the extent of the damage.

Industry Impact and What's Next

The legal action against major technology platforms signals Take-Two's determination to crack down on sources of leaked material. Companies that receive subpoenas are typically required to provide user data and communication records that could help identify those responsible.

CyberLeek's online presence has ceased entirely, with previous accounts or posts no longer accessible. Whether this represents a voluntary shutdown or a response to legal pressure remains unclear.

Watch for further developments as Take-Two's legal proceedings unfold and the industry monitors how effectively companies can protect unreleased game content from unauthorized disclosure.
