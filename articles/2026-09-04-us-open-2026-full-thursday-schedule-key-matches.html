US Open 2026: Full Order of Play for Thursday, September 3

The US Open Tennis Championship is set to continue on Thursday, September 3, 2026, with a packed schedule of matches. This article provides a detailed order of play, highlighting the key matches and storylines to watch.

Key Matches and Storylines

1. Naomi Osaka vs. Aryna Sabalenka

Naomi Osaka, a former US Open champion, will face Aryna Sabalenka in a highly anticipated match. Both players have a strong record at the US Open, making this a crucial clash. Fans can expect a competitive and intense match.

2. Alex Eala vs. Jannik Sinner

Alex Eala, a rising star in women's tennis, will take on Jannik Sinner, one of the top players in men's tennis. This match is expected to be a thrilling contest, with both players bringing their A-game to the court.

3. Coco Gauff vs. Jessica Pegula

Coco Gauff, the young American sensation, will face Jessica Pegula, a veteran player with a strong presence at the US Open. This match promises to be an exciting battle between youth and experience.

4. Carlos Alcaraz vs. Daniil Medvedev

Carlos Alcaraz and Daniil Medvedev, two of the most dominant players in men's tennis, will compete in a match that is sure to be a highlight of the day. Both players have a history of impressive performances at the US Open.

5. Iga Swiatek vs. Ons Jabeur

Iga Swiatek, the reigning US Open champion, will face Ons Jabeur, a player known for her aggressive style of play. This match is expected to be a close and competitive contest, with both players aiming to secure a victory.

Significance of the US Open 2026

The US Open is one of the four Grand Slam tournaments in tennis, held annually at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in New York City. It is known for its rich history, competitive matches, and global appeal. The tournament attracts top players from around the world and offers significant prize money and ranking points.

The US Open 2026 is expected to be a thrilling event, with many key matches and storylines to watch. Fans can look forward to intense competition, memorable performances, and the continuation of the tournament's legacy.
