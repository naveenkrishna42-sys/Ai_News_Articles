Introduction to Mahavir Jayanti and Bihar's Sacred Sites

Mahavir Jayanti, celebrated on the 13th day of the bright half of the Hindu month of Chaitra, commemorates the birth, enlightenment, and nirvana of Lord Mahavira, the 24th Tirthankara of Jainism. Bihar, a state in eastern India, is home to 12 of India's 14 Jain pilgrimage sites, making it a significant destination for Jain devotees.

Top Jain Pilgrimage Sites in Bihar

1. Shikharji

Shikharji, located in the Nalanda district, is one of the most revered Jain pilgrimage sites. The site is known for its ancient Jain temples and the Shikharji Temple, which houses a 15th-century idol of Lord Mahavira. The temple complex is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, attracting thousands of pilgrims every year.

2. Sammed Shikharji

Sammed Shikharji, also in the Nalanda district, is another significant Jain pilgrimage site. The site is known for its ancient Jain temples and the Sammed Shikharji Temple, which houses a 15th-century idol of Lord Mahavira. The temple complex is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, attracting thousands of pilgrims every year.

3. Nalanda

Nalanda, a city in the Nalanda district, is a major pilgrimage site for Jains. The city is known for its ancient Jain temples and the Nalanda Jain Temple, which houses a 15th-century idol of Lord Mahavira. The temple complex is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, attracting thousands of pilgrims every year.

4. Rajgir

Rajgir, a city in the Nalanda district, is a major pilgrimage site for Jains. The city is known for its ancient Jain temples and the Rajgir Jain Temple, which houses a 15th-century idol of Lord Mahavira. The temple complex is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, attracting thousands of pilgrims every year.

5. Vaishali

Vaishali, a city in the Vaishali district, is a major pilgrimage site for Jains. The city is known for its ancient Jain temples and the Vaishali Jain Temple, which houses a 15th-century idol of Lord Mahavira. The temple complex is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, attracting thousands of pilgrims every year.

Pilgrimage Season and Accommodation

The pilgrimage season in Bihar runs from February to June, with Mahavir Jayanti being the peak season. Accommodation options range from budget hotels to luxury resorts, with prices ranging from ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 per night. Pilgrims can also find homestays and guesthouses at more affordable prices.
