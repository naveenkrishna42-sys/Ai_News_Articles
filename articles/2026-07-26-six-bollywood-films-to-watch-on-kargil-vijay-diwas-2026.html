Six Bollywood Films to Watch on Kargil Vijay Diwas 2026

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kargil Vijay Diwas is observed every year on July 26 to mark India's victory in the 1999 war.
  • Bollywood has produced several films based on the Kargil conflict, including 'Shershaah' and 'Gunjan Saxena'.
  • The list includes movies that focus on individual heroism as well as the collective effort of the Indian Army.

As India observes Kargil Vijay Diwas on July 26, 2026, the nation remembers the courage and sacrifice of its soldiers who fought in the 1999 conflict. Bollywood has over the years brought these stories to the big screen, offering audiences a glimpse into the valour displayed in the treacherous peaks of Kargil.

From biographical dramas to war epics, here are six films that capture the spirit of the Indian armed forces during the Kargil War.

Shershaah (2021)

Directed by Vishnu Varadhan, 'Shershaah' tells the story of Captain Vikram Batra, who was posthumously awarded the Param Vir Chakra for his bravery. Sidharth Malhotra plays the lead role, portraying Batra's journey from a young officer to a war hero.

The film is known for its gripping battle sequences and the iconic dialogue, "Yeh dil maange more!" It remains one of the most celebrated war biopics in Indian cinema.

Gunjan Saxena: The Kargil Girl (2020)

This biographical drama stars Janhvi Kapoor as Flight Lieutenant Gunjan Saxena, one of the first Indian women to fly in a combat zone during the Kargil War. The film highlights her determination to become a pilot and her role in evacuating injured soldiers from the battlefield.

Directed by Sharan Sharma, the movie sheds light on the challenges faced by women in the armed forces and their contribution to the war effort.

Lakshya (2004)

Farhan Akhtar's 'Lakshya' follows the journey of a directionless young man, played by Hrithik Roshan, who joins the Indian Army and finds purpose during the Kargil War. The film features fictional characters but is inspired by real events and the overall military campaign.

With a strong narrative and realistic war sequences, 'Lakshya' remains a favourite among audiences for its portrayal of personal growth and national duty.

LOC Kargil (2003)

Directed by J.P. Dutta, 'LOC Kargil' is an ensemble war film that chronicles the entire Kargil conflict. It features a large cast including Ajay Devgn, Abhishek Bachchan, and Manoj Bajpayee, each portraying real-life soldiers who fought in the war.

The film is known for its detailed depiction of the various battles, including the capture of Tiger Hill and Tololing. It runs over four hours and is considered a comprehensive tribute to the Indian Army.

Sanam Teri Kasam (2016)

While not a war film in the traditional sense, 'Sanam Teri Kasam' includes a subplot centred on a soldier serving in Kargil. The romantic drama, starring Harshvardhan Rane and Mawra Hocane, uses the backdrop of the conflict to highlight the personal sacrifices of military families.

The film's emotional core revolves around the soldier's love story and the pain of separation during wartime.

Mukkabaaz (2017)

Anurag Kashyap's 'Mukkabaaz' is a sports drama that does not directly depict the Kargil War but features a character who is a former soldier inspired by the conflict. The film touches on themes of patriotism and resilience through its boxing narrative.

While less known for its Kargil connection, the film includes references to the war that underline its impact on individual lives.

These films serve as a reminder of the bravery displayed by Indian soldiers in 1999. As the nation pays tribute on Kargil Vijay Diwas, watching these stories can be a way to honour their memory and understand the human cost of war.

For those looking to revisit the heroism of the past, these six films offer a cinematic journey into one of India's most significant military victories.
