Indian markets snap five-day losing streak on oil price drop, earnings hopes

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex surged 776 points, Nifty gained 166 points, snapping a five-day losing streak.
  • Sharp drop in crude oil prices and rupee appreciation boosted market sentiment.
  • BSE-listed companies added ₹5.1 lakh crore in market capitalisation during the session.
  • Earnings optimism and lower input cost outlook drove buying across sectors.

Indian shares rebounded sharply on Monday, ending a five-day losing streak as a steep slide in global crude oil prices and a stronger rupee lifted investor sentiment. The BSE Sensex surged 776 points, while the NSE Nifty climbed 166 points in a broad-based rally that added over ₹5.1 lakh crore to investor wealth.

The recovery came after weeks of selling pressure triggered by concerns over valuations and global rate uncertainty. Traders said the sharp drop in crude — India's biggest import bill — eased worries about inflation and corporate margins.

Crude slide fuels buying across sectors

Brent crude fell sharply in international markets, raising expectations that India's fuel import costs would cool. The development came as a relief for the economy, which has been grappling with elevated energy prices for months.

The rupee also strengthened against the US dollar, adding to the positive undertone. A stronger local currency typically helps curb imported inflation and shores up returns for foreign portfolio investors, who have been net sellers in recent weeks.

Market breadth turns positive

Monday's rally was led by banking, auto and metal stocks, with all major sectoral indices closing in the green. The market breadth was decisively positive, with more than two stocks advancing for every one that declined on the BSE.

Analysts said the combination of lower crude and a stable rupee could improve corporate earnings visibility, especially for companies with high raw material costs. That optimism prompted traders to cover short positions and add fresh longs in beaten-down sectors.

Investor wealth surges by ₹5.1 lakh crore

The total market capitalisation of BSE-listed firms rose by about ₹5.1 lakh crore in a single session, reflecting the magnitude of the rebound. The surge reversed a portion of the losses accumulated during the previous five sessions, when the benchmark indices had fallen over 3%.

Several heavyweight stocks, including Reliance Industries, HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank, contributed significantly to the index gains. Market participants pointed out that the rally was driven by domestic institutional buying and short covering by algorithmic traders.

Key levels to watch

With the Nifty reclaiming a crucial support level, traders are now watching whether the index can sustain above that mark in the coming sessions. A breach below that level could invite fresh selling, while a hold would signal the end of the corrective phase.

Technical analysts said the 50-day moving average would be a key resistance zone. A decisive close above that would confirm a short-term trend reversal. However, volumes were still lower than average on Monday, suggesting that some caution remains among institutional players.

The market's direction in the near term will depend on global cues, particularly moves in crude oil and the dollar index. Domestic earnings reports due later this week will also be closely watched for signs of margin recovery.
