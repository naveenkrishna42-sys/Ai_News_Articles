Rockets riding high

Trent Rockets enter Match 22 of The Hundred Men's 2026 with a perfect record — five wins from five. Their latest triumph, a four-wicket haul from Garth, has propelled them to the top of the table. The side looks settled, confident, and increasingly difficult to beat.

Birmingham Phoenix, by contrast, have been inconsistent. They have shown flashes of brilliance but have struggled to string together back-to-back wins. This clash at Trent Bridge is a test of their mettle against the tournament's form side.

Dunkley's example

In the women's fixture earlier in the day, Sophia Dunkley anchored the Rockets to a comfortable victory over the Phoenix. Her knock was a masterclass in pacing a chase — steady early, explosive at the death. The men's team will take inspiration from that performance.

Dunkley's innings also highlighted the value of building partnerships on this surface. The pitch at Trent Bridge has offered something for both bat and ball, but batsmen who spend time in the middle have reaped rewards.

Key battles to watch

Garth, fresh off his four-wicket burst, will be the man Phoenix' top order need to see off. His ability to extract bounce and movement upfront has made him the Rockets' go-to bowler in the powerplay.

For Phoenix, the onus will be on their overseas stars to counter the Rockets' disciplined attack. Their middle order has been brittle under pressure, and a strong start is non-negotiable if they are to post a competitive total.

Prediction

Form, momentum, and home advantage all point to a sixth straight win for Trent Rockets. Birmingham Phoenix have the talent to spring an upset, but they will need to be near-perfect with both bat and ball. Expect the Rockets to control the game from the first ball.

All eyes will be on the toss, as chasing has been the preferred option here. But given the Rockets' bowling depth, even a modest target could prove enough.
