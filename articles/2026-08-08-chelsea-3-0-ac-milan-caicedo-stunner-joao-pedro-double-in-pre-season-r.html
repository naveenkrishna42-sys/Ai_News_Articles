Chelsea 3-0 AC Milan: Caicedo stunner, Joao Pedro double in pre-season rout

Dominant display at the Bridge

Chelsea produced a statement performance in their pre-season schedule, brushing aside AC Milan 3-0 in a one-sided friendly. The Blues controlled the tempo from the first whistle, with Moises Caicedo's spectacular volley setting the tone and Joao Pedro adding a clinical brace to round off a polished evening.

The result will please the Chelsea management as the squad builds towards the new campaign. The Italian visitors, by contrast, left with more questions than answers, with several first-team regulars struggling to impose themselves.

Caicedo's moment of magic

The opening goal arrived midway through the first half, and it was worth the wait. Caicedo, positioned just outside the area, met a loose ball on the half-volley and lashed it into the top corner. The Ecuadorian midfielder's technique was flawless, leaving the Milan goalkeeper with no chance.

It was the kind of strike that pre-season friendlies rarely produce, and it lifted the home crowd immediately. From that point, Chelsea never looked back, pressing high and forcing Milan into repeated errors.

Joao Pedro takes his chances

Joao Pedro, the Brazilian forward, doubled the lead shortly after the interval. His first goal came from a well-timed run into the box, finishing calmly after a defence-splitting pass. The second was simpler in execution but equally effective—a composed finish after Milan's backline failed to clear a cross.

The brace caps a promising pre-season for the striker, who has been among the goals in recent outings. His movement and link-up play caused constant problems for the Milan defence, which looked slow and disjointed throughout.

Milan's struggles under the spotlight

For AC Milan, the match was a sobering one. Reports from the Italian press suggest at least four players were marked down for below-par performances, with few positives for coach Amorim to take away. The midfield was overrun, the defence was exposed, and the attack rarely threatened Chelsea's goal.

Pre-season results can be misleading, but the manner of this defeat will worry Milan fans. There was little structure in their pressing and a lack of urgency in possession. Amorim will need to address these issues quickly as the Serie A season approaches.

What this means for both sides

For Chelsea, this win adds to a growing sense of optimism. The squad's depth looks strong, and the early signs of tactical cohesion are encouraging. Caicedo's form in the middle of the park, combined with Joao Pedro's finishing, gives the manager plenty of options going forward.

Milan, meanwhile, will treat this as a wake-up call. The coaching staff will analyse the tape, but the issues run deeper than a single friendly. Fitness levels may improve, but the lack of sharpness in key areas is a concern that cannot be ignored.

The road ahead

Both teams have more pre-season fixtures lined up before the competitive season begins. Chelsea will look to build on this momentum, while Milan will hope to iron out their flaws in time for their opening league match. The next few weeks will reveal whether this result was an aberration or a sign of things to come.
