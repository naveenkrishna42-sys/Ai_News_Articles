Sensex falls 183 pts as IT, Auto, FMCG stocks drag

📅 2026-08-26 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex falls 183 points, Nifty below 24,277
  • IT, Auto, and FMCG sectors drag performance
  • Global cues improve but domestic market shows mixed results

Benchmark Indices End Lower

The Sensex closed 183 points lower today, marking a significant decline in the benchmark index. The Nifty also slipped below the 24,277 mark, indicating a mixed performance in the market. Despite global cues improving, domestic sectors showed a lackluster response.

Sector-Specific Performance

IT, Auto, and FMCG sectors were the main drags on the market today. The IT sector, which has been a key driver of market growth, saw a decline in sentiment. Similarly, the Auto sector, which has been showing signs of recovery, also witnessed a dip in performance. The FMCG sector, which has been on a steady growth trajectory, also showed a decline in sentiment.

Global Cues and Domestic Market

Despite the positive global cues, the domestic market showed a lackluster response. This could be attributed to a variety of factors, including macroeconomic indicators, company-specific news, and investor sentiment. It is crucial for investors to keep a close eye on these factors to make informed decisions.

Expert Opinion and Analysis

According to market analysts, the decline in the Sensex and Nifty could be a temporary setback. They believe that the market will recover in the coming days, given the positive global cues. However, the performance of the IT, Auto, and FMCG sectors is a cause for concern. Investors should be cautious and diversify their portfolios to mitigate risks.
