Parents blame school delay for son’s death in Bengaluru

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Parents of a Class 9 student allege his school delayed medical attention, leading to his death.
  • The school claims it followed its emergency protocol and that the student had a known medical condition.
  • Bengaluru police have registered a case and are recording statements from school staff and the family.

BENGALURU: The parents of a 15-year-old boy have alleged that their son died because his school delayed taking him to a hospital after he fell ill on campus. The incident has sparked outrage and raised questions about how schools handle medical emergencies.

The student, a Class 9 student at a private school in southeast Bengaluru, collapsed during a morning assembly on Wednesday. Teachers moved him to the school infirmary. Instead of rushing him to a hospital, the school waited for his parents to arrive, the family claims.

Parents say precious hours were lost

The boy’s father told reporters on Thursday that he got a call from the school around 10 am, nearly an hour after his son fell ill. “They said he was feeling dizzy and had a headache. They did not say it was serious,” the father said. He reached the school by 10.30 am and found his son unconscious. The boy was then taken to a nearby private hospital, where doctors declared him dead on arrival.

“The school lost nearly two hours. If they had taken him immediately, he could have been saved,” the mother said, breaking down. The family has accused the school administration of negligence and insensitivity.

School defends its actions

The school management, in a written statement, said it followed its standard emergency protocol. “The student was immediately attended to by our trained nurse. We contacted the parents as per procedure and waited for their consent before moving him. We are deeply saddened by the loss,” the statement read.

The school also claimed the boy had a known medical condition, which the parents had not disclosed in the school’s health form. The parents deny this, saying their son was a healthy child with no prior complaints.

Police investigation underway

Bengaluru police have registered a case of unnatural death and are investigating. Officers have recorded statements from the school principal, the nurse on duty, and the class teacher. The post-mortem report is awaited.

“We are examining the timeline of events and the school’s response. If negligence is established, appropriate action will be taken,” a senior police officer said. The officer declined to confirm whether any arrests have been made or are imminent.

The incident has triggered a debate among parents in the city. Many are calling for mandatory emergency drills and clear guidelines on when schools must directly call an ambulance instead of waiting for parents.

Officials from the state education department said they would issue a circular to all schools reminding them of the protocol for medical emergencies. “Schools must prioritise the child’s life over paperwork or consent. We will reiterate this,” an official said.

What happens next: The police investigation and the post-mortem report will determine whether the school faces criminal charges. The education department is also expected to submit its own report within a week.
