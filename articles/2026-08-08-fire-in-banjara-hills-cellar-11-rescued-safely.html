Fire in Banjara Hills cellar, 11 rescued safely

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Fire reported in cellar of a building in Banjara Hills
  • Firefighters rescued 11 people from the premises
  • No casualties; cause of fire yet to be ascertained

Hyderabad: A fire broke out in the cellar of a building in Banjara Hills on Saturday morning, prompting a swift rescue operation that saved 11 people. The incident was reported around 10 am, and fire tenders were rushed to the spot.

Officials said the blaze originated in the cellar area, though the exact cause is yet to be determined. Thick smoke quickly spread to upper floors, trapping some residents and staff inside.

Rescue operation

Firefighters reached the location within minutes of receiving the alert. Using breathing apparatus and hydraulic ladders, they evacuated all 11 people safely. No injuries have been reported so far.

“All occupants were brought out without any harm,” a fire department official said, adding that the operation was completed in under an hour. The building was cordoned off as a precaution while cooling operations continued.

Response and investigation

Police and disaster response teams also arrived at the scene. Preliminary assessments suggest the fire may have been triggered by an electrical short circuit, but officials have not confirmed this. An investigation has been initiated.

The cellar reportedly stored electrical equipment and some combustible material, which may have contributed to the rapid spread of smoke. Residents were advised to avoid the area until the building is declared safe.

Safety concerns

This incident has once again raised questions about fire safety norms in residential and commercial complexes. Experts point out that many buildings lack adequate ventilation and fire suppression systems in basements.

Municipal authorities have yet to issue a statement on whether the building had obtained all necessary clearances. The owner could face action if violations are found.

Meanwhile, the rescued individuals have been provided with temporary shelter and medical checks. Most have been allowed to return home, though some remain under observation.

Fire services have appealed to residents to report any potential fire hazards immediately. Regular maintenance of electrical wiring and installation of smoke detectors are being advised across the city.

As investigations continue, the focus will be on determining the exact cause and ensuring compliance with safety protocols. The incident serves as a reminder of the risks posed by neglected infrastructure.
