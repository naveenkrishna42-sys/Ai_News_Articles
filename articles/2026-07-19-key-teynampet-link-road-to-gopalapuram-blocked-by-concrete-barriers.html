Key Teynampet Link Road to Gopalapuram Blocked by Concrete Barriers

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Concrete blocks placed on a key link road behind DMS Metro in Teynampet.
  • Road connects Teynampet to Gopalapuram, a busy area in Chennai.
  • Traffic disruptions reported as vehicles forced to take alternate routes.
  • Reason for the blocks not immediately clear; officials yet to comment.

Commuters in Chennai faced unexpected delays on Sunday morning after a vital link road connecting Teynampet to Gopalapuram was blocked by large concrete blocks. The road, located behind the DMS Metro station, serves as a key shortcut for many daily travellers.

The obstruction was first noticed early in the day. Several vehicles were seen queuing up, with drivers forced to turn around and find alternative paths. Local residents reported that the blocks appeared to have been placed overnight.

Traffic Snarls Reported

Traffic movement in the area slowed considerably. The road is a popular bypass that helps decongest the main arterial roads during peak hours. Its sudden closure caught many off guard.

Autorickshaw drivers and cab operators said they had to reroute through Cathedral Road and other parallel streets. This added at least 15-20 minutes to their usual travel time. Office-goers and others heading to Gopalapuram were the worst hit.

Reason for the Blocks Unclear

Authorities have not yet confirmed why the barriers were placed. Speculation among locals ranges from ongoing utility work to a possible traffic management measure. However, no official notice or signage was visible at the site.

Chennai Corporation officials were not immediately available for comment. The DMS Metro station area has seen increased construction activity in recent months, but this specific obstruction was not part of any announced plan.

Commuters Demand Clarity

Several commuters expressed frustration at the lack of warning. “This is a busy road. If they had to block it, they should have put up signs yesterday,” said a resident who uses the route daily. Many are now demanding that the authorities provide a clear timeline for the road's reopening.

Police personnel were seen managing traffic at the junction near the DMS Metro. They directed vehicles away from the blocked stretch, but the information regarding the duration of the blockage remained scarce.

The Teynampet-Gopalapuram link road is used by hundreds of vehicles every hour. Its sudden closure highlights the lack of communication between civic agencies and the public.

Authorities are expected to issue a statement later today. For now, commuters are advised to use Cathedral Road or the Anna Salai service lane to reach Gopalapuram. The situation remains fluid, and updates are awaited on when the blocks will be removed.
