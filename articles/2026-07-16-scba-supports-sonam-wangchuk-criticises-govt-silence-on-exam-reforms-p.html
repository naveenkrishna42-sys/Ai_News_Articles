SCBA Supports Sonam Wangchuk, Criticises Govt Silence on Exam Reforms Protest

📅 2026-07-16 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • SCBA expresses solidarity with Sonam Wangchuk, laments no govt response to his protest for exam reforms.
  • Delhi High Court orders daily medical monitoring and necessary treatment for Wangchuk.
  • Political leaders like Arvind Kejriwal and Rakesh Tikait have joined the protest at Jantar Mantar.

The Supreme Court Bar Association (SCBA) has voiced strong support for climate activist Sonam Wangchuk, who is currently on a protest at Jantar Mantar in Delhi demanding reforms in the examination system. The SCBA expressed dismay over the lack of response from the central government to Wangchuk's demands.

SCBA Flags Government Silence

In a statement issued on Thursday, the SCBA said it stands in solidarity with Wangchuk and urged the government to address his concerns. The association noted that the activist's peaceful protest has not received any official acknowledgment, which it termed concerning.

“The government must engage in a dialogue. Ignoring a legitimate protest only escalates tensions,” the SCBA said in its statement. The association also called for a fair hearing of Wangchuk's demands, which focus on overhauling the current examination system in Ladakh and other regions.

High Court Orders Medical Monitoring

Meanwhile, the Delhi High Court has stepped in to ensure Wangchuk's health is not compromised during his indefinite fast. The court directed authorities to provide daily medical check-ups and necessary treatment to the activist. “Every life is precious,” the court observed while passing the order.

Wangchuk has been on a hunger strike for several days, demanding a shift from the current examination pattern to a more holistic evaluation system. His protest has drawn attention to the stress and inadequacies of the existing academic framework.

Political Support Swells at Jantar Mantar

The protest has gained traction across the political spectrum. On Wednesday, farmers' leader Rakesh Tikait and former Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal visited Jantar Mantar to express solidarity. Kejriwal went a step further, stating, “Sonam Wangchuk should be the Education Minister.”

Tikait claimed that over 500 farmers have joined the protest in support of Wangchuk. The gathering at Jantar Mantar has been described as “packed” with supporters from various walks of life, including students, academics, and civil society members.

Wangchuk, known for his innovative educational initiatives in Ladakh, has been a vocal critic of the current examination system. He argues that it prioritises rote learning over creativity and practical knowledge.

What Next?

The Delhi High Court's intervention ensures Wangchuk receives medical attention, but the core issue remains unresolved. With no word from the government yet, the protest is likely to continue, putting pressure on authorities to respond.
