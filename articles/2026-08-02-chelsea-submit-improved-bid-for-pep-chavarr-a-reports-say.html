Chelsea submit improved bid for Pep Chavarría, reports say

📅 2026-08-02 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Chelsea have submitted a new and improved bid for Pep Chavarría, according to multiple reports.
  • The move could break Chelsea's transfer record and mark their tenth signing of the window.
  • The defender is reportedly pushing for the move to Stamford Bridge.
  • The deal is being linked to Xabi Alonso's plans as he looks to replace Marc Cucurella.

Blues step up pursuit of Spanish defender

Chelsea have lodged a fresh, improved offer for Pep Chavarría, according to multiple reports on Sunday. The bid is understood to be a significant upgrade on their previous proposal, with the club eager to get the deal over the line.

The Spanish defender has emerged as a top target for head coach Xabi Alonso, who sees him as the ideal replacement for Marc Cucurella. The Blues have been in talks for weeks, but negotiations have now accelerated.

Record deal in the making

Reports suggest the transfer could shatter Chelsea's existing club record. The fee being discussed is believed to be substantial, and the club are said to be finalising the last details of the agreement.

If completed, Chavarría would become Chelsea's tenth signing of the summer window. That would mark one of the most aggressive spending sprees in the club's history, even by the standards of recent years.

Player pushing for move

Sources indicate that Chavarría is actively pushing for the transfer to go through. The 22-year-old is keen on a switch to Stamford Bridge and has reportedly made his intentions clear to his current club.

This has given Chelsea encouragement that a deal can be struck. The player's stance could also help lower the asking price, though the selling club are still holding out for a hefty fee.

Alonso's defensive overhaul

Xabi Alonso has made no secret of his desire to reshape his back line. Cucurella's future at the club remains uncertain, and Chavarría is seen as a long-term upgrade in that position.

The former Real Sociedad man's versatility and composure on the ball fit the profile Alonso wants. His arrival would add competition and depth, something Chelsea have lacked in recent seasons.

Questions over Enzo Fernandez's future

Meanwhile, the ongoing speculation around Chavarría has reignited questions about Enzo Fernandez's role at the club. Reports suggest the Argentine midfielder's future is far from settled, with no clear conclusions drawn yet.

Some insiders believe Chelsea's renewed activity in the market signals a shift in priorities. The club are said to be acting with seriousness and intent, which could have implications for several first-team players.

What happens next

With the bid now on the table, the ball is in the selling club's court. Chelsea are hopeful of a swift response, but negotiations could still drag on.

Fans will be watching closely to see if this record-breaking move is completed before the window shuts. The coming days are likely to be decisive.
