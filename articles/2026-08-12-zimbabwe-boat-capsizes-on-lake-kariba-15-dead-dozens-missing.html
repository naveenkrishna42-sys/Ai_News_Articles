Zimbabwe Boat Capsizes on Lake Kariba, 15 Dead, Dozens Missing

📅 2026-08-12 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • At least 15 people are dead and 27 missing after a boat capsized on Lake Kariba in Zimbabwe.
  • The ferry was reportedly overcrowded, carrying 95 people when it went down.
  • Rescue operations are ongoing, with officials yet to confirm the total number of casualties.
  • The incident highlights safety concerns on the lake, a popular route for passengers and goods.

Lake Kariba Tragedy: What We Know So Far

At least 15 people have died and dozens more are missing after an overcrowded ferry capsized on Lake Kariba in Zimbabwe, according to rescue services. The boat was reportedly carrying 95 people when it went down, sparking a large-scale search-and-rescue operation in the vast inland water body.

Officials have confirmed that 27 people remain unaccounted for, though they caution that the number could change as rescue teams comb the waters. The incident occurred on Tuesday, with emergency crews working through the night to locate survivors.

Overcrowding and Safety Concerns

Initial reports suggest the vessel was severely overcrowded, a recurring problem on Lake Kariba where ferries often transport passengers and goods between remote communities. The lake, one of the world's largest man-made reservoirs, spans the border between Zimbabwe and Zambia and is known for its unpredictable weather and strong winds.

Survivors have described chaotic scenes as the boat began to take on water before capsizing. Rescue services have not yet confirmed the exact cause of the disaster, but authorities are investigating whether the vessel was seaworthy and whether proper safety protocols were followed.

Rescue Operations Underway

Teams from Zimbabwe's Civil Protection Unit, along with local fishermen and volunteers, have been deployed to the scene. Helicopters and patrol boats are scouring the lake's surface, while divers search for those still trapped beneath the water.

The remote location of the capsizing has hampered rescue efforts, with some areas of Lake Kariba only accessible by boat. Officials have not released the names of the deceased, and families are anxiously awaiting news at shoreline villages.

Regional Impact and Response

The tragedy has sent shockwaves through the region, with neighbouring Zambia also affected as some passengers may have been heading towards the Zambian shore. Local authorities have urged calm and promised a full investigation into the incident.

Zimbabwe's government has expressed condolences to the families of the victims, though no official statement from the president's office has been released yet. The disaster is likely to reignite calls for stricter enforcement of passenger limits and safety regulations on Lake Kariba's ferries.

As rescue efforts continue, attention will turn to preventing similar tragedies. Officials are expected to review maritime safety laws, and there are already reports that the government may suspend operations of other passenger boats on the lake pending safety inspections.

What Happens Next

Rescue teams will continue searching for the missing over the coming days, with hopes of finding more survivors fading as time passes. An official inquiry is expected to determine the exact sequence of events and assign responsibility.

The focus will also shift to the broader question of ferry safety on Lake Kariba, a vital waterway for thousands of people. For now, the immediate priority remains locating the missing and supporting the affected families.
