Right-Wing Activist TG Mohandas Held Over 'Girls Enjoy Rape' Remark

📅 2026-08-09 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • T.G. Mohandas taken into custody days after his alleged 'girls enjoy rape' remark
  • All-India Feminist Alliance demands RSS sever ties with the ideologue
  • Remarks reportedly made during a protest at Jantar Mantar in Delhi

Activist Detained After Controversial Remarks

Days after a video surfaced showing right-wing activist T.G. Mohandas making crude remarks about student protesters, the Kerala-based ideologue was taken into custody. The detention follows widespread outrage over his alleged comment that 'girls enjoy rape' during a counter-protest at Jantar Mantar in Delhi.

Mohandas, associated with Hindutva groups in Kerala, was addressing a gathering when he made the remarks. The comments were aimed at women participating in protests against the NEET exam controversy. Video clips of the speech spread rapidly on social media, drawing sharp condemnation from feminist groups and political opponents.

Feminist Alliance Demands Action

The All-India Feminist Alliance has written to the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) leadership, demanding that the organisation sever all ties with Mohandas. In a statement, the alliance called the remarks deeply offensive and symptomatic of a broader pattern of misogyny within certain sections of the Sangh Parivar.

Several women's rights organisations have echoed these demands, urging law enforcement to take strict action. The activists have also called for a thorough investigation into the context and intent behind Mohandas's statements. They argue that such language normalises sexual violence and creates an unsafe environment for women protesters.

Background of the Remarks

The incident unfolded during a period of heightened tension around student-led protests in the national capital. Demonstrators have been demanding reforms to the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET), alleging irregularities and unfair practices. Mohandas's counter-protest appeared intended to discredit the student movement.

His comments, however, backfired spectacularly. Instead of undermining the protesters, they galvanised support for them. Social media users widely shared the video, with many expressing disgust at the language used. The hashtag #ArrestMohandas trended for several hours after the clip went viral.

Political Reactions

Political parties in Kerala have reacted sharply to the development. The ruling Left Democratic Front has demanded a formal apology from the RSS and has called for a comprehensive probe into Mohandas's activities. Opposition parties have also distanced themselves from the ideologue, though some have refrained from commenting until the legal process plays out.

The RSS central office has not yet issued an official statement on the detention. However, sources indicate that the organisation is under pressure to respond decisively. Past instances of controversial remarks by affiliated figures have often led to disciplinary action or public disavowal.

Legal Proceedings Ahead

Mohandas is expected to be produced before a magistrate in the coming days. Authorities have not yet specified the exact charges against him, but legal experts suggest sections related to outraging the modesty of women and promoting enmity between groups could apply. The police have confirmed that a case has been registered based on a complaint filed by an activist group.

His detention marks a rare instance of swift action in a case involving inflammatory speech by a right-wing figure. Whether this signals a broader shift in enforcement remains to be seen. For now, women's groups are watching closely to ensure that the judicial process does not languish.

What to Watch

The coming days will reveal whether the RSS formally distances itself from Mohandas and whether the courts impose any interim restrictions on his activities. The case also raises broader questions about the limits of free speech in public discourse. As the legal machinery grinds forward, the spotlight remains firmly on how institutions respond to rhetoric that incites violence against women.
