Hyderabad Police Warn of Cyber Frauds Targeting Indiramma Housing Scheme Applicants

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Cyber criminals are targeting applicants of the Indiramma Housing Scheme with fake offers of allotment.
  • Police Commissioner VC Sajjanar advises citizens to verify information only through official government channels.
  • No official housing allotments are made via phone calls or WhatsApp messages, warns police.

Hyderabad Police Commissioner VC Sajjanar has issued a public warning about a surge in cyber frauds linked to the state government's flagship Indiramma Housing Scheme. Scammers are reportedly targeting applicants with fake messages and phone calls, promising housing unit allotments in exchange for money or personal details.

Fraudsters Pose as Officials

The commissioner said fraudsters are impersonating government officials, sending fake allotment letters and demanding processing fees. They often ask victims to transfer money through UPI or to share bank account details, Aadhaar numbers, and other sensitive information.

“No official communication regarding housing scheme allotments is made through personal phone calls or WhatsApp messages,” Sajjanar said in a statement. He urged citizens to treat any such unsolicited contact as suspicious and to report it immediately to the cyber crime helpline.

Scheme Background

The Indiramma Housing Scheme is a major welfare initiative by the Telangana government, aimed at providing affordable housing to eligible beneficiaries across the state. Applications for the scheme have drawn lakhs of submissions, making it a ripe target for cyber criminals.

Police have noted that the fraud is particularly active in areas where housing scheme awareness is high but digital literacy is low. Many victims are from lower-income groups who are eager to secure a home.

How to Stay Safe

Police have listed key precautions for applicants. They should never share OTPs, bank details, or Aadhaar numbers with unknown callers. All scheme-related information must be verified through the official Telangana government housing portal or by visiting the local MeeSeva centre.

  • Do not respond to calls or messages offering instant housing allotments.
  • Do not transfer any money to personal accounts or unknown UPI IDs.
  • Report suspicious activity to the cyber crime helpline at 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in.

Sajjanar also warned that any promise of allotment for a fee is a fraud. The government does not charge extra for allocations beyond the official process.

Action Taken

Hyderabad Cyber Crime Police have registered a case and are investigating multiple complaints received in the past week. Officials have not yet confirmed the number of victims or the total amount lost, but sources say the fraud has spread across several districts.

Police are working with telecom providers to trace the numbers used by the fraudsters. They have also issued advisories to banks to be vigilant about suspicious transactions linked to the scheme.

The commissioner has urged the public to share the warning with family and neighbours, especially those who may not be active on social media or news platforms.

What to Watch

Investigations are ongoing, and police expect to make arrests soon. Citizens are advised to stay alert and rely only on official government announcements for scheme updates.
