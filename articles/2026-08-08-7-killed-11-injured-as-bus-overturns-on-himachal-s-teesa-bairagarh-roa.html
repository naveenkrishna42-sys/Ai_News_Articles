7 killed, 11 injured as bus overturns on Himachal's Teesa-Bairagarh road

📅 2026-08-08 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Private bus overturns on Teesa-Bairagarh mountain road in Chamba district
  • Seven dead, 11 injured; rescue operations carried out by local authorities
  • PM Modi condoles loss of lives; probe into accident likely

At least seven people were killed and 11 others injured after a private bus overturned on the Teesa-Bairagarh road in Himachal Pradesh's Chamba district on Saturday. The accident occurred on a treacherous mountain stretch, where narrow curves and loose gravel often prove deadly for heavy vehicles.

Crash on a Mountain Route

The bus, carrying passengers along the route, lost control and toppled over. Local residents were the first to reach the spot, pulling out the trapped and tending to the wounded before authorities arrived. Police and disaster response teams were rushed to the location, and the injured were shifted to nearby medical facilities.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact cause of the crash, though preliminary assessments point to brake failure or driver error on the steep gradient. The Teesa-Bairagarh road is known for its sharp bends and lack of guardrails at several stretches, making it a high-risk corridor for buses and trucks.

Rescue and Relief Efforts

Rescue teams worked through the morning to extricate passengers from the mangled remains of the vehicle. The injured, some in critical condition, were transported to Chamba's district hospital and primary health centres in the area. Medical staff were put on alert to handle the influx of casualties.

Senior district officials visited the site to oversee the operations and ordered a detailed inquiry into the incident. The bus, a private operator's vehicle, was plying a routine route when the mishap occurred. Authorities are verifying the passenger manifest and have reached out to families of the deceased.

PM Modi Expresses Grief

Prime Minister Narendra Modi expressed sorrow over the loss of lives in the accident. In a message posted on social media, he extended condolences to the bereaved families and wished a speedy recovery to those injured. The Prime Minister's Office said relief measures are being closely monitored.

The state government has announced compensation for the victims' families, though the exact amounts have not been disclosed. Local administration has been directed to ensure proper treatment for the injured and expedite the paperwork for ex gratia payments.

Road Safety Concerns Resurface

The tragedy has once again highlighted the dangers of mountain driving in Himachal Pradesh, where hundreds of buses ply daily on narrow, winding roads. Accidents are frequent during the monsoon season, when landslides and slippery surfaces add to the risks. Transport department officials have faced repeated calls to enforce stricter safety norms for private operators.

Data from the state transport wing shows that a significant number of bus accidents in the region occur due to overspeeding and poor vehicle maintenance. While the government has launched awareness campaigns, implementation on the ground remains patchy.

What to Watch

An investigation into the crash is underway, and officials are expected to release a detailed report in the coming days. The focus will be on whether the bus had valid fitness certificates and whether the driver adhered to route regulations. For now, the families of the victims await answers as the hill state mourns yet another avoidable loss.
