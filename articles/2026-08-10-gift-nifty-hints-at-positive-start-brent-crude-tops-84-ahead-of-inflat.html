GIFT Nifty Hints at Positive Start; Brent Crude Tops $84 Ahead of Inflation Data

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • GIFT Nifty suggests a positive start for Indian equity benchmarks on Monday.
  • Brent crude oil surged past $84 a barrel, adding to inflationary concerns.
  • Investors await inflation data while tracking West Asia geopolitical developments.
  • Nifty50 remains in a consolidation range, with a breakout being the key focus.
  • All eyes are on domestic and global cues for market direction this week.

Futures Point to a Green Opening

GIFT Nifty, trading on the international exchange, indicated a positive start for Indian equities on Monday, August 10. The early signals suggest that the Nifty50 and Sensex could open in the green, offering some relief to traders after last week's choppy sessions.

However, the mood remains cautious. The surge in Brent crude above $84 a barrel has injected fresh uncertainty, as higher oil prices typically strain India's import bill and stoke inflationary pressures.

Crude's Climb and Its Ripple Effects

Brent crude futures jumped past the $84 mark, a level not seen in recent weeks. This uptick comes amid ongoing supply concerns and geopolitical risks, even as markets cling to hopes of a resolution in West Asia.

For Indian markets, every dollar rise in crude is a double-edged sword. It raises input costs for companies and puts pressure on the rupee, which in turn can impact foreign fund flows. Analysts will be watching the movement closely through the day.

Consolidation or Breakout for Nifty50?

The benchmark Nifty50 has been stuck in a consolidation range for several sessions. Market participants are now asking whether Monday will finally bring a breakout. Technical analysts suggest that a sustained move above the upper band could trigger fresh buying, while a dip below support may invite selling pressure.

Volume data from the previous session showed a lack of conviction, with both bulls and bears hesitating. The options market, too, reflects indecision, with call writers and put writers engaged in a tug-of-war.

Inflation Data in Focus

Investors are bracing for key inflation readings due this week. Domestically, the consumer price index (CPI) data will be scrutinised for any signs of a persistent price spiral. A higher-than-expected print could dash hopes of an early rate cut by the Reserve Bank of India.

On the global front, US inflation numbers will also be in the spotlight. Any surprise upside there could alter the Federal Reserve's policy trajectory, which has a direct bearing on emerging markets like India.

West Asia Tensions and Global Cues

Geopolitics remains a wild card. Reports indicate that markets may rise on hopes of a West Asia resolution, but nothing is confirmed on the ground. A de-escalation could cool oil prices and boost risk appetite, while any flare-up would likely trigger a sell-off.

Asian markets were mixed in early trade, tracking overnight moves on Wall Street. Futures on the Dow and S&P 500 were flat, suggesting a cautious start for global equities as well.

What to Watch on Monday

Traders will keep a close eye on the opening tick of the Nifty50. If the index sustains gains beyond the first hour, it could signal a stronger rally. Conversely, a quick fade would confirm the market's reluctance to break out.

Stock-specific action will be driven by sectoral cues—banking, auto, and oil & gas are likely to be the most active. Foreign institutional investor (FII) flows, which have been volatile, will also be monitored for direction.

Given the mixed signals, a cautious approach is advisable. The day's trade will likely hinge on crude prices and any fresh headlines from West Asia, making it a session where patience could pay off.

As the week progresses, the release of inflation data will dominate the narrative. A benign reading could lift sentiment, while a hot print might extend the consolidation phase. For now, all eyes are on the opening bell.
