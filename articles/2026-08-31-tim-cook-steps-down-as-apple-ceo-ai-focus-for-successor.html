Tim Cook's Legacy

Tim Cook's 15-year tenure as Apple CEO has ended with a focus on gratitude. In his final memo to employees, Cook expressed his deep appreciation for the company's achievements. The former CEO, who took over from Steve Jobs in 2011, oversaw Apple's transformation from a $350 billion company to a $4 trillion powerhouse.

John Ternus Takes the Reins

John Ternus, Apple's senior vice president of services, has been appointed as the new CEO. Ternus, who has been with Apple for 21 years, will lead the company into the age of AI. The appointment comes as Apple looks to maintain its leadership in the tech industry.

AI as Top Priority

Ternus has made it clear that AI will be a top priority for Apple. The company is expected to invest heavily in AI research and development. This move comes as other tech giants like Google and Microsoft are also increasing their focus on AI.

Market Impact

The appointment of Ternus as CEO is likely to have a significant impact on the market. Apple's stock has been on a downward trend in recent months, and the company's new focus on AI could provide a much-needed boost.
