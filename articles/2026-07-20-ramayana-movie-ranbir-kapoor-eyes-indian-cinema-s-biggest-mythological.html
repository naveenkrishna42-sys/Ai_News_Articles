Ramayana Movie: Ranbir Kapoor Eyes Indian Cinema's Biggest Mythological Hit

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana is one of the most anticipated Indian films, with Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Ram.
  • The film reportedly boasts a budget exceeding Rs 500 crore, featuring extensive VFX and a star-studded ensemble.
  • Sai Pallavi will play Sita, while Yash is cast as Raavan. The first look is expected soon.
  • The two-part epic aims for a 2027 release, with fans expecting a visual spectacle on par with global blockbusters.

The Wait for the Epic

Nitesh Tiwari's ambitious retelling of the Ramayana is shaping up to be one of the most expensive and eagerly awaited films in Indian cinema history. With Ranbir Kapoor stepping into the role of Lord Ram, the project has generated immense buzz across the country.

Industry insiders suggest the film's budget has crossed the Rs 500-crore mark, making it one of the costliest Indian productions ever. The makers are sparing no expense on visual effects, sets, and costumes, aiming for a spectacle that matches global standards.

The Cast and Vision

Ranbir Kapoor's casting as Ram has been met with widespread approval. The actor is known for his dedication and range, and early reports indicate he has undergone rigorous training for the role. Sai Pallavi will essay the role of Sita, while Kannada superstar Yash is set to portray Raavan.

The ensemble also includes some of the biggest names from Indian cinema, though the full cast has not been officially announced. Tiwari, who directed the blockbuster Dangal, is known for his meticulous storytelling and emotional depth.

Scale and Expectations

The Ramayana is being planned as a two-part film, with the first installment expected to hit theatres in 2027. The scope is immense, with the team filming across multiple locations in India and abroad. Sources say the VFX work alone will take over a year to complete.

Fans are already comparing the project to international epics like Lord of the Rings. The film's teaser is expected to drop later this year, and the buzz on social media is palpable. Trade analysts believe the film could become the highest-grossing Indian film of all time if it lives up to the hype.

What to Watch For

The first look and teaser will be crucial in setting the tone for the film. The makers are likely to release them around Diwali or early next year. The casting of key supporting characters, including Hanuman and Lakshman, is also awaited.

The Ramayana is not just a film but a cultural event. Its success will depend on how well it balances tradition with cinematic spectacle. All eyes are on Ranbir Kapoor and Nitesh Tiwari to deliver what could be a landmark moment for Indian cinema.
