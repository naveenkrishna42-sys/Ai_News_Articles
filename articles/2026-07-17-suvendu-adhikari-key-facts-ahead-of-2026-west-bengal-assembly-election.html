Suvendu Adhikari: Key Facts Ahead of 2026 West Bengal Assembly Election

Key points
  • Suvendu Adhikari switched from TMC to BJP in 2020, becoming a key opposition face in West Bengal.
  • He contested the 2021 assembly election from Nandigram against Mamata Banerjee and won.
  • The 2026 West Bengal assembly election will see Adhikari and the BJP challenge the TMC again.
  • Adhikari's political career began with the TMC; he held several ministerial portfolios under Mamata Banerjee.

Who is Suvendu Adhikari?

Suvendu Adhikari is a prominent Indian politician from West Bengal. Once a close aide of Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee, he is now the face of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) in the state. His defection in December 2020 reshaped the state's political landscape.

Adhikari served as a minister in the Trinamool Congress (TMC) government, handling portfolios like transport and irrigation. He was widely seen as a key organisational brain behind the TMC's rise in rural Bengal.

The 2021 Nandigram Showdown

In the 2021 West Bengal assembly election, Adhikari contested from Nandigram. He faced Mamata Banerjee herself. The election was billed as a prestige battle. Adhikari won, defeating the chief minister by a narrow margin of around 1,700 votes.

That victory made him the leader of the opposition in the state assembly. It also cemented his position as the BJP's most influential Bengali face. Since then, he has been a sharp critic of the TMC government on issues like corruption and law and order.

What to Expect in 2026

The 2026 West Bengal assembly election is approaching. Adhikari is expected to lead the BJP's campaign again. The party is banking on his grassroots connect, particularly in the southern and coastal districts.

His rivalry with Mamata Banerjee remains personal and political. The TMC has accused him of shifting loyalties for power. Adhikari counters by alleging the TMC has failed on development and safety.

Key issues for 2026 include unemployment, alleged political violence, and the central government's welfare schemes. Adhikari has promised to bring the BJP's development model to Bengal if voted to power.

Political Journey at a Glance

  • Started career with the TMC in the early 2000s.
  • Elected as an MLA multiple times from Nandigram.
  • Held key ministerial posts in the Mamata Banerjee cabinet.
  • Joined the BJP in December 2020, calling it a decision for Bengal's future.
  • Won the 2021 assembly election from Nandigram as a BJP candidate.

Adhikari's move to the BJP was a major blow to the TMC. He brought with him several local leaders and a network of supporters. His speeches in the assembly often set the tone for the opposition's attack.

Political analysts say the 2026 election will test whether Adhikari can expand the BJP's reach beyond its current base. The TMC, led by Banerjee, remains a formidable opponent with a strong welfare record. The battle lines are drawn.

Both parties have started groundwork. Rallies, alliances, and candidate lists will take shape over the next year. For now, all eyes are on Suvendu Adhikari and his strategy to unseat the TMC.

The 2026 West Bengal assembly election is likely to be held in April-May next year. The results will decide if Adhikari can repeat his 2021 success or if Mamata Banerjee's TMC will return for a fourth term.
