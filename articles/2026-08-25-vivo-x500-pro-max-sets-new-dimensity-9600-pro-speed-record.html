Vivo X500 Pro Max sets new Dimensity 9600 Pro speed record

📅 2026-08-25 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Dimensity 9600 Pro achieves 4.55 GHz prime core speed
  • Vivo X500 series confirmed for September launch
  • Upcoming flagship could offer 4K/240fps slow-mo

Dimensity 9600 Pro sets new speed benchmark

Vivo's X500 Pro Max benchmark has confirmed the Dimensity 9600 Pro's prime cores can run at 4.55 GHz. This is a significant leap from the previous Dimensity 9000 series, which topped out at 3.36 GHz. The new chip is expected to power the upcoming Vivo X500 series, which is set to launch in September.

Vivo X500 series launch confirmed

Multiple sources have confirmed the Vivo X500 series will launch in September. The series is expected to include major upgrades in camera and video capabilities. This includes 4K video recording at 240fps slow-motion, which could give the X500 series a significant edge over rivals.

Performance implications

The Dimensity 9600 Pro's 4.55 GHz prime cores could make it one of the fastest smartphone chips on the market. This could translate to smoother multitasking, faster app launches, and better overall performance. However, it remains to be seen how these speeds translate to real-world usage, as benchmark results can sometimes be optimistic.
