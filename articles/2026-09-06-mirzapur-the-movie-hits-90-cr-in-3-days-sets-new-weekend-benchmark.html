Mirzapur The Movie Hits ₹90 Cr in 3 Days, Sets New Weekend Benchmark

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Team 📅 2026-09-06 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Day‑3 collection crosses ₹90 crore, marking a record weekend for a non‑festival release.
  • Occupancy averages 78% nationwide, with strong pull in tier‑2 and tier‑3 centres.
  • High theatrical earnings boost downstream revenue streams, including satellite and OTT rights.

Box Office Surge on Day 3

Mirzapur The Movie, starring Pankaj Tripathi and Ali Fazal, posted a day‑three net of ₹35 crore, pushing its cumulative three‑day total past the ₹90‑crore mark. The film opened with a ₹25‑crore net on Friday, followed by a modest ₹30‑crore on Saturday, before the Sunday surge that eclipsed expectations.

Industry analysts attribute the jump to word‑of‑mouth buzz, aggressive regional marketing, and a weekend that saw no competing big‑budget releases. The film’s strong performance is especially notable given its release outside the traditional festive window.

When benchmarked against recent hits like "Pathaan" (₹450 crore 3‑day net) and "Jawan" (₹210 crore 3‑day net), Mirzapur’s numbers are modest but impressive for a mid‑budget action‑drama. Its per‑screen average of ₹1.2 lakh outperforms several contemporaries that opened in the same week.

Implications for Stakeholders

Producers and Distributors

The rapid cash‑flow strengthens the film’s negotiating position for satellite and OTT deals. Preliminary reports suggest a satellite rights package in the ₹30‑₹35 crore range, while streaming platforms are eyeing a premium window price of ₹25 crore.

Theatrical Exhibitors

High occupancy rates in tier‑2 and tier‑3 theatres indicate a robust secondary market, encouraging exhibitors to allocate more screens for the second weekend. This could extend the film’s theatrical run beyond the typical two‑week window, adding an estimated ₹15‑₹20 crore to the net.

Pros & Cons

  • Pros: Strong weekend momentum, broad regional appeal, favorable cost‑to‑revenue ratio.
  • Cons: Limited overseas footprint, competition from regional releases in the second week.
