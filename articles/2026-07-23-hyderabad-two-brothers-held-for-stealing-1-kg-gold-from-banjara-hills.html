Hyderabad: Two brothers held for stealing 1 kg gold from Banjara Hills firm

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Two brothers arrested for stealing 1 kg gold from a jewellery firm in Banjara Hills.
  • The theft was reported earlier this week; police tracked the duo using CCTV footage.
  • Most of the stolen gold has been recovered by the authorities.

Theft at Banjara Hills jewellery firm

Hyderabad police have arrested two brothers for allegedly stealing 1 kg of gold from a jewellery firm in the upscale Banjara Hills area. The theft was reported earlier this week, prompting a swift investigation.

The accused, whose names have not been released pending further inquiry, are believed to have targeted the firm during off-hours. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact method used, but initial reports suggest they exploited a gap in security.

How police cracked the case

Authorities said they zeroed in on the suspects after reviewing CCTV footage from the premises and nearby locations. The footage showed two men acting suspiciously around the time of the theft.

Following technical surveillance and tip-offs, police apprehended the brothers from a hideout on the city's outskirts. Senior officers confirmed that most of the stolen gold was recovered from their possession.

“The accused planned the theft carefully but left enough digital evidence for us to track them down,” a police official said, speaking on condition of anonymity.

Recovery and legal proceedings

Police have recovered the bulk of the stolen gold, valued at several lakh rupees. The exact weight and purity of the recovered metal are being verified by forensic experts.

The brothers have been booked under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code for theft. They are currently in police custody and will be produced before a local court for further remand.

  • Investigation is ongoing to determine if the duo had any accomplices.
  • The jewellery firm has been advised to bolster its security systems.

What happens next

The accused will remain in judicial custody as the investigation proceeds. Police are expected to file a chargesheet within the stipulated period. The jewellery firm is likely to review its security protocols to prevent such incidents in the future.
