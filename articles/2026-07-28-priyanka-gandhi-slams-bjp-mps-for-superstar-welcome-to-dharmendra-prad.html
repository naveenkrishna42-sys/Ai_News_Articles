Priyanka Gandhi slams BJP MPs for ‘superstar’ welcome to Dharmendra Pradhan

📅 2026-07-28 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Priyanka Gandhi accused BJP MPs of treating Dharmendra Pradhan like a ‘superstar’ after his resignation.
  • The opposition questioned the felicitation, comparing it to a victory celebration.
  • Reports suggest the CJP had rejected an offer to change Pradhan's portfolio before his exit.

Congress leader Priyanka Gandhi Vadra on Tuesday launched a sharp attack on Bharatiya Janata Party MPs for what she called an extravagant felicitation of Union Minister Dharmendra Pradhan after his resignation. She accused them of welcoming him 'as if he were a superstar'.

Felicitation draws opposition fire

The event, which saw BJP parliamentarians garlanding Pradhan and showering him with praise, has sparked a political row. Priyanka Gandhi questioned the optics of the reception, noting that it appeared more suited for a war hero than a minister who had just stepped down.

'They garlanded him as if he had won a war. This shows the disconnect between the ruling party and the struggles of ordinary Indians,' she said during a press conference.

Behind Pradhan's exit

The controversy unfolds amid reports that the Centre had initially offered to change Pradhan's portfolio, but the Chief Justice of India reportedly rejected the proposal. Sources indicate that the ministerial reshuffle was part of a broader realignment within the government, but the exact reasons for Pradhan's resignation remain unclear.

Opposition parties have seized on the development, alleging that Pradhan's exit was linked to pressure from the judiciary. The BJP has dismissed these claims as baseless and politically motivated.

Targeting of CJP protesters

Adding to the tensions, reports have emerged that individuals who protested against the Chief Justice of India after Pradhan's resignation were tracked, attacked, and humiliated. Human rights groups have expressed concern over the alleged targeting of activists and called for an independent inquiry.

Police in Delhi have not confirmed any specific incidents, but videos circulating on social media show scuffles between protesters and unidentified individuals. The government has not issued a formal statement on these allegations.

Political implications

The episode has given fresh ammunition to the Congress and other opposition parties, who are using it to question the BJP's internal functioning and its relationship with the judiciary. Priyanka Gandhi's remarks are likely to amplify the debate in Parliament when it reconvenes next week.

BJP spokespersons have defended the felicitation, calling it a gesture of respect for a colleague who served the nation. 'Dharmendra Pradhan is a dedicated leader. Honouring him is not a crime,' said a party leader who did not wish to be named.

The exact sequence of events leading to Pradhan's resignation and the subsequent felicitation remains contested. What is clear is that the political temperature is rising, with both sides unwilling to back down.

Watch for further developments as the opposition pushes for a parliamentary discussion and the BJP works to contain the fallout from the controversy.
