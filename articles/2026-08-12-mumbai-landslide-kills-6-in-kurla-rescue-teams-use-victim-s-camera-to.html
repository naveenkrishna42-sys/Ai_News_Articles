Mumbai Landslide Kills 6 in Kurla; Rescue Teams Use Victim's Camera to Find Survivors

📅 2026-08-12 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • At least six dead, four injured after landslide in Kurla, Mumbai
  • Rescuers used a victim's camera to search for survivors under debris
  • Heavy rains triggered the landslide; several people feared trapped
  • Rescue operations ongoing in the affected area

Landslide in Kurla Claims Six Lives

A landslide in Mumbai's Kurla area has killed at least six people and injured four others, with rescue teams racing against time to find survivors feared trapped under the debris. The incident occurred amid heavy rains that have lashed the city, triggering the collapse of soil and structures in the densely populated neighbourhood.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of people still trapped, but local residents reported hearing cries for help from beneath the rubble. Emergency services, including the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) and local police, were deployed to the site shortly after the landslide was reported.

Rescuers Use Victim's Camera to Locate Survivors

In a remarkable turn, rescue workers discovered a camera belonging to one of the victims, which they used to search for survivors. The camera's memory card contained images and footage that helped rescuers identify the locations where people were last seen, aiding in the search operation.

This unconventional method allowed teams to narrow down their search areas, especially in sections of the debris where visibility was poor. The device, believed to belong to a resident who was present during the landslide, provided crucial clues about the positions of several individuals before the collapse.

Heavy Rains Worsen Situation

The landslide struck as Mumbai experienced continuous downpour, weakening the soil on slopes and hillsides in Kurla. The area, known for its informal settlements and ageing structures, has been particularly vulnerable to such disasters during the monsoon season.

Local authorities have urged residents in nearby vulnerable zones to evacuate as a precautionary measure. The injured have been admitted to nearby hospitals, with some in critical condition, though officials have not disclosed their identities.

Rescue Operations Underway

Teams are using heavy machinery and sniffer dogs to comb through the debris, while manual efforts continue to reach survivors trapped in narrow gaps. The operation has been challenging due to the unstable terrain and continuing rainfall, which has raised the risk of further collapse.

According to reports, at least two people were rescued alive earlier, but the death toll is expected to rise as rescuers dig deeper. The municipal corporation has announced compensation for the victims' families, though details are yet to be finalised.

What to Watch

Rescue efforts will continue through the night, with officials expected to provide an updated casualty count later. The focus will also shift to assessing the structural safety of buildings in Kurla, as the city braces for more rain in the coming days.
