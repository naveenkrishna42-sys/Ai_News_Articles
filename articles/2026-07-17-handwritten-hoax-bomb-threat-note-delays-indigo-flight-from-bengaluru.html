Handwritten hoax bomb threat note delays IndiGo flight from Bengaluru

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • An IndiGo flight from Bengaluru was delayed due to a handwritten bomb threat note.
  • The note was discovered on the aircraft, prompting security protocols.
  • After thorough checks, the threat was deemed a hoax, and the flight was cleared for departure.

A handwritten bomb threat note caused a delay for an IndiGo flight at Kempegowda International Airport in Bengaluru on Friday. The note was discovered on board the aircraft before departure, triggering standard security protocols.

Airport authorities and airline staff immediately swung into action. The aircraft was isolated, and passengers were disembarked for a thorough security sweep. Bomb disposal squads and sniffer dogs were deployed to inspect the plane.

Security checks and passenger inconvenience

The threat was written on a piece of paper and left in a seat pocket. Crew members found it during pre-flight checks. The airline did not specify the flight number or destination, but sources indicated it was a domestic route.

Passengers were kept in the terminal for several hours as security teams combed the aircraft. Many took to social media to express frustration over the delay, though most acknowledged the need for caution. “Safety first, but the communication could have been better,” one passenger said.

Officials confirmed that no suspicious items were found. After a detailed inspection, the threat was declared a hoax.

IndiGo and police response

An IndiGo spokesperson stated that the airline followed all mandated procedures. “The safety of our passengers and crew is our top priority. The flight was delayed as we conducted a comprehensive security check. Once cleared, the aircraft was released for operations.”

Bengaluru police are investigating the source of the note. They have reviewed CCTV footage from the boarding gate and aircraft interior. No arrests have been made yet. Authorities have urged passengers to remain vigilant but not panic.

This is not the first such incident in India. In recent months, multiple airlines have faced hoax threats, leading to delays and security scares. Aviation experts say these incidents strain resources and cause unnecessary disruption.

What happens next

The flight eventually departed after a delay of over three hours. Police continue to probe the handwriting and any potential leads. The airline is cooperating with the investigation. Officials have not ruled out legal action against those responsible, if found.

For now, the incident serves as a reminder of the costly consequences of hoax threats. The focus remains on tightening pre-boarding security to detect such notes before they cause delays.
