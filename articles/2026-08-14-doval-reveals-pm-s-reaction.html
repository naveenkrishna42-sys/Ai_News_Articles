NSA Ajit Doval Shares PM Modi's Reaction to Pahalgam Terror Attack

National Security Advisor (NSA) Ajit Doval has revealed Prime Minister Narendra Modi's reaction to the recent terror attack in Pahalgam. According to Doval, the Prime Minister's first question to him was about the incident, indicating the gravity with which the government is treating the situation.

Doval's statement comes at a time when the government has launched Operation Sindoor, a campaign aimed at countering terrorism. The NSA has warned Pakistan that India's generosity should not be mistaken for weakness, and that the country will fight till the last drop of blood to protect its citizens.

Operation Sindoor: A Strong Message to Pakistan

Operation Sindoor is a significant initiative by the Indian government to bring together top military and security leaders to discuss and strategize ways to counter terrorism. The operation is seen as a strong message to Pakistan, which has been accused of supporting and sponsoring terrorist activities in India.

Doval's statement has sent a clear warning to Pakistan that India will not tolerate any form of terrorism and will take all necessary measures to protect its citizens and interests. The NSA's comments have also highlighted the government's commitment to ensuring the safety and security of the country.

India's Response to Terrorism

The Indian government has been facing criticism for its handling of terrorism, particularly in the wake of the Pahalgam terror attack. However, with Operation Sindoor, the government has shown its determination to take a strong stance against terrorism.

The operation is expected to bring together experts and leaders from various fields to discuss and implement strategies to counter terrorism. The government has also made it clear that it will not hesitate to take tough measures to protect its citizens and interests.
