Preah Vihear: Cambodia's disputed temple

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Sacred Places Original source ↗
Key points
  • Preah Vihear Temple is a 12th-century Hindu temple, dedicated to Shiva.
  • It is a subject of territorial dispute between Cambodia and Thailand.
  • The temple is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a major pilgrimage site.

Historical Significance

Preah Vihear Temple, located in the Preah Vihear Province of Cambodia, is a 12th-century Hindu temple dedicated to Shiva. It is one of the largest religious monuments in Southeast Asia and a major pilgrimage site for Hindus, particularly those from India, Nepal, and Bangladesh. The temple's construction is attributed to King Suryavarman II, who ruled the Khmer Empire from 1113 to 1150 CE.

Architectural Significance

The temple complex is a stunning example of Khmer architecture, featuring intricate carvings and sculptures. The main temple is a towering structure with a central tower, surrounded by smaller towers and pavilions. The complex also includes a number of smaller temples, a library, and a museum.

Spiritual Significance

Preah Vihear Temple is a deeply spiritual site for Hindus. It is believed to be a place of great power and is often visited by pilgrims seeking blessings and spiritual enlightenment. The temple is also a symbol of Cambodia's rich cultural heritage and is an important part of the country's national identity.

Disputed Territory

The temple is located on the border between Cambodia and Thailand, and its ownership has been a subject of dispute between the two countries for many years. The dispute dates back to the 19th century and has been a source of tension and conflict between the two nations. Despite the dispute, the temple remains a popular tourist destination and a symbol of Cambodia's cultural heritage.

Visiting Information

The best time to visit Preah Vihear Temple is during the dry season, from November to April. The temple is open to visitors, and entry is free. However, it is recommended to book a guide in advance, as the temple complex is quite large and can be difficult to navigate alone.

Preah Vihear Temple is located approximately 30 kilometers from the city of Preah Vihear. The nearest airport is in Siem Reap, Cambodia, which is approximately 150 kilometers away. From Siem Reap, visitors can take a bus or a private car to Preah Vihear.

Nearby attractions worth visiting include the Angkor Wat temple complex, which is approximately 150 kilometers away, and the Tonlé Sap Lake, which is approximately 100 kilometers away.
