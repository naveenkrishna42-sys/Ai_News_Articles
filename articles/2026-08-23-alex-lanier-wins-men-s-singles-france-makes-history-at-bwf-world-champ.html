Alex Lanier wins men's singles, France makes history at BWF World Championships

  • France makes history as Lanier becomes first Frenchman to win singles title

Alex Lanier scripts history

French badminton star Alex Lanier has become the first Frenchman to win the men's singles title at the BWF World Championships, marking a significant moment for the country's badminton scene. Lanier defeated his opponent in the final, securing the title and bringing glory to France.

France's growing stature in badminton

Lanier's victory is a testament to France's growing stature in the world of badminton. The country has produced several top players, and Lanier's success has further cemented its position in the sport. His achievement has inspired a new generation of badminton players in France.

Mixed doubles final highlights

The BWF World Championships also saw an exciting mixed doubles final, featuring a pair of French and Indian players. The match was closely contested, with both teams giving their all. The final ended in a thrilling finish, with the Indian pair ultimately emerging victorious.

BWF World Championships in New Delhi

The BWF World Championships took place in New Delhi, India, from August 15 to 21, 2026. The event attracted top badminton players from around the world, and the atmosphere was electric throughout the tournament. The final matches were watched by a large and enthusiastic audience.

What happens next?

With Lanier's victory, France has made a strong statement in the world of badminton. The country's badminton federation will now look to build on this success and continue to develop the sport.
