NVIDIA RTX Spark expands game support with EA, Ubisoft, Embark

📅 2026-08-26 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • NVIDIA RTX Spark now supports EA, Ubisoft, and Embark games
  • Native EA anti-cheat integration enhances security
  • Expands RTX Spark's appeal to mainstream PC gamers

NVIDIA RTX Spark broadens game support with major publishers

NVIDIA's RTX Spark platform has added support for games from Electronic Arts (EA), Ubisoft, and Embark Studios. This move is a significant step in making RTX Spark a viable option for mainstream PC gamers, who have been waiting for wider game compatibility.

EA, Ubisoft, and Embark games now playable on RTX Spark

With the latest update, RTX Spark users can now enjoy games like Apex Legends, Arc Raiders, and The Finals from EA. Ubisoft titles such as Assassin's Creed and Far Cry are also now supported. Embark Studios' games, including Shadow Warrior 3, have also been added to the list of playable titles.

Native EA anti-cheat integration boosts security

The inclusion of native EA anti-cheat is a crucial development. This feature ensures that RTX Spark users can enjoy their games without worrying about cheating or hacking. The anti-cheat system is designed to be robust and efficient, providing a secure gaming environment.

Why this matters for PC gamers

For PC gamers, this update is a game-changer. It means that RTX Spark can now offer a similar gaming experience to traditional PCs, with a focus on performance and visual quality. The addition of major game titles from leading publishers like EA and Ubisoft is a strong indicator of NVIDIA's commitment to making RTX Spark a viable platform.

Broader impact on the PC gaming market

This development also has broader implications for the PC gaming market. It shows that NVIDIA is serious about competing with traditional PC manufacturers. By offering a platform that supports a wide range of games and features robust security measures, NVIDIA is positioning RTX Spark as a serious contender in the market.
