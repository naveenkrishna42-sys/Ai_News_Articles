Q1 Results: Bajaj Auto, Adani Energy, TVS Motor, Bandhan Bank, Indian Hotels to announce earnings on July 21

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bajaj Auto, Adani Energy Solutions, TVS Motor, Bandhan Bank, Indian Hotels to report Q1 results on July 21
  • Bajaj Auto shares declined ahead of earnings; analysts watching volume growth and margins closely
  • UltraTech Cement, IOB, KVB, Sobha posted strong Q1 growth in other earnings updates
  • Mahindra Logistics, Dynamic Cables, Jaiprakash Power, Shyam Metalics surged after their results

The first quarter earnings season continues to pick up pace. A host of major companies are scheduled to release their April-June results today, July 21. Investors will be closely watching the numbers from Bajaj Auto, Adani Energy Solutions, TVS Motor, Bharat Coking Coal, Bandhan Bank, Indian Hotels, and several others.

Bajaj Auto: Margins under watch as shares dip

Bajaj Auto shares were trading lower in early trade ahead of its earnings announcement. Analysts expect the two-wheeler and three-wheeler maker to report a healthy rise in net profit, driven by strong volume growth. However, margins remain a key area of focus. Input costs and competitive pricing in export markets could have squeezed profitability. The company's management commentary on demand trends in domestic and overseas markets will be parsed carefully.

According to a preview by Business Standard, the consolidated results are expected to show revenue growth in double digits. The Street will also look for updates on the company's electric vehicle plans and its CNG motorcycle launch.

Adani Energy Solutions, TVS Motor, Bandhan Bank: Key numbers to track

Adani Energy Solutions, the transmission and distribution arm of the Adani Group, will report its first quarterly numbers after a recent reorganisation. Investors are keen to see revenue growth from its operational assets and any update on the pipeline of new projects. Debt levels and capital expenditure plans will also be in focus.

TVS Motor Company is expected to post a strong performance, riding on volume growth in both domestic and export markets. The company has been gaining market share in the premium segment. Analysts will examine the impact of rising raw material costs on its operating margin.

Bandhan Bank's results will be scrutinised for asset quality trends, particularly in its microfinance portfolio. The bank has been working to reduce its concentration risk. Net interest margin and deposit growth are other key metrics to watch.

Other companies reporting today

Bharat Coking Coal, a subsidiary of Coal India, will announce its quarterly numbers. Indian Hotels Company, the operator of Taj Hotels, is expected to report a strong quarter on the back of robust travel demand. Sobha, the real estate developer, posted strong Q1 growth earlier in the week.

Other names include Mahindra Logistics, Dynamic Cables, Jaiprakash Power, and Shyam Metalics. Shares of these companies surged in recent sessions after their earnings updates.

Earnings season so far: Mixed signals

The broader earnings season has thrown up mixed results. UltraTech Cement's profit rose in the June quarter, aided by volume growth and cost controls. Public sector lenders Indian Overseas Bank and Karur Vysya Bank also posted strong numbers. However, some consumer and auto companies have flagged demand weakness in rural areas.

Analysts say the next few weeks will be crucial as more companies report. The market will look for signs of a recovery in consumption and corporate profitability.

All eyes are now on the results due later today. Stock-specific action will hinge on whether companies meet, beat, or miss Street expectations.
