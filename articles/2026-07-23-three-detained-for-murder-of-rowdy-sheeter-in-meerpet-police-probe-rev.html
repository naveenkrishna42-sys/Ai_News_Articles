Three detained for murder of rowdy-sheeter in Meerpet; police probe revenge motive

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Three individuals detained for the murder of a rowdy-sheeter in Meerpet.
  • Police are probing a revenge motive behind the killing.
  • The victim was known to police with a criminal record.

Detentions Made in Meerpet Murder Case

Hyderabad police have detained three individuals in connection with the murder of a rowdy-sheeter in the Meerpet area. The victim, whose identity has not been officially released, was found dead under circumstances that suggest a targeted attack.

The detentions were made late Tuesday night, following a swift investigation by local law enforcement. Officials have not yet confirmed the names of the suspects or the victim, pending formal charges.

Revenge Motive Under Investigation

Investigators are currently probing a revenge motive as the primary line of inquiry. The victim was a known rowdy-sheeter with a history of criminal activities, which may have led to the attack.

Police sources indicate that the suspects are being questioned to establish the exact sequence of events leading to the murder. A senior officer stated that the probe is focused on personal enmity and past altercations.

Background of the Victim

The deceased was previously booked in multiple cases, including assault and property disputes. His presence in the Meerpet area was known to local police, who had been monitoring his activities.

Authorities are also examining if the murder is linked to a larger gang rivalry. No evidence has been found so far to suggest a broader conspiracy, but the investigation remains open.

Next Steps for Police

The three detainees are expected to be produced before a local court for remand. Police are seeking custody to recover the weapon used in the crime and to gather further evidence.

Additional patrols have been deployed in Meerpet to prevent any retaliatory violence. The police have urged the public to come forward with any information related to the case.

What happens next will depend on the outcome of the interrogation and forensic reports. The court will decide on the suspects' custody, while police work to build a watertight case.
