Pixel 11 Pro teaser hints at major upgrade with 'turn it up to 11' tagline

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Google teases Pixel 11 Pro with 'turn it up to 11' tagline, suggesting a major leap in features.
  • Leaks indicate Tensor G6 chip performance improvements and new color options for Pixel 11 HiLight.
  • Pixel 11 series and Watch 5 expected to launch with pre-orders starting soon.

Google's loudest teaser yet

Google has released a new teaser for the Pixel 11 Pro, and the message is unmistakable: it's turning things up to 11. The tagline, a nod to the classic amplifier joke, suggests the company is pushing its next flagship beyond the usual incremental upgrade.

The teaser comes as leaks and rumors about the Pixel 11 series have been swirling for weeks. Android Authority first reported on the teaser, which shows a stylized dial that goes past the usual maximum, implying a significant boost in performance, camera capability, and AI features.

Google has not yet confirmed specific specs, but the teaser aligns with reports that the Pixel 11 Pro will feature the Tensor G6 chip, which is expected to offer substantial gains in processing power and efficiency.

Tensor G6 and performance leaks

According to The Times of India, pre-order dates for the Pixel 11 series have apparently leaked, along with Tensor G6 performance scores. The leaked benchmarks suggest a notable improvement over the previous generation, though Google has not officially commented on these figures.

Forbes has gone as far as to ask how the Pixel 11 Pro will change smartphones forever, hinting at possible breakthroughs in on-device AI and camera technology. While such claims are often exaggerated in the tech press, the excitement around the Tensor G6 appears justified based on the leaks.

Color options for Pixel 11 HiLight

In a separate leak, 9to5Google reports that Google Contacts has revealed the color options for the Pixel 11 HiLight, a companion device in the Pixel lineup. The colors are said to include a new shade that hasn't appeared in previous Pixel products, though the exact names have not been confirmed.

This attention to detail suggests Google is focusing on personalization as a key selling point for the upcoming lineup. The HiLight, which is expected to be a more affordable variant, could attract users looking for a Pixel experience at a lower price point.

Pixel 11 series and Watch 5 expectations

GSMArena.com has compiled a list of what to expect from the Pixel 11 series and the companion Watch 5. The watch is rumored to feature improved health tracking, longer battery life, and a new design.

As for the Pixel 11 series, the lineup is expected to include the Pro, a standard model, and the HiLight. All three are likely to run on Android 16, with Google's latest AI features integrated throughout.

Pre-orders are expected to open within the next few weeks, ahead of an official launch event. Google has not yet announced a date, but leaks suggest it could be as soon as September.

What to watch next

With the teaser now public, the countdown to the official launch has begun. Tech enthusiasts will be watching closely to see if Google delivers on the 'turn it up to 11' promise, especially in terms of camera and AI capabilities.

Google is expected to confirm the launch date and pre-order details in the coming days. Until then, the leaks and teasers will keep the hype machine running at full volume.
