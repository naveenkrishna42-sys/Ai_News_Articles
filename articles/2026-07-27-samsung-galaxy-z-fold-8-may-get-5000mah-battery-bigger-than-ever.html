Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 May Get 5000mAh Battery, Bigger Than Ever

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • The Galaxy Z Fold 8 is tipped to feature a 5000mAh battery, up from 4400mAh on the Z Fold 7.
  • A larger battery would address a key pain point for foldable phone users: battery life.
  • The upgrade aligns with Samsung's push to improve battery capacities across its flagship lineup.

Samsung's upcoming Galaxy Z Fold 8 could finally address one of the most persistent criticisms of foldable phones. According to a recent report, the device may feature a 5000mAh battery — a notable jump from the 4400mAh cell found in the Galaxy Z Fold 7.

For users who have long complained about the battery life of foldable devices, this could be a significant improvement. A larger battery would mean longer usage between charges, potentially making the Z Fold 8 more practical for heavy users.

Why the Upgrade Matters

Foldable phones have always faced a trade-off between their innovative form factor and battery capacity. The internal space constraints of a folding design have historically limited how large a battery manufacturers can fit inside the device.

Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold series has improved with each generation, but battery life has remained a sticking point. The Z Fold 7's 4400mAh battery often left users reaching for a charger by the end of the day, especially with the large main display in use.

A move to 5000mAh would bring the foldable flagship in line with many conventional premium smartphones, including Samsung's own Galaxy S25 Ultra.

Samsung's Broader Battery Push

The potential Z Fold 8 battery upgrade is part of a wider trend. Reports suggest Samsung is also working on breaking the 5000mAh ceiling for its Galaxy S27 Pro and Ultra models, indicating a company-wide strategy to boost battery capacities across its premium lineup.

This push makes sense. As smartphone processors become more efficient and displays get brighter, users are demanding more from their batteries. A larger capacity not only extends daily use but also supports new features like on-device AI processing, which can be power-hungry.

Samsung has not officially confirmed any specifications for the Galaxy Z Fold 8. The device is expected to launch in the second half of 2026.

What Else to Expect

Beyond the battery, the Galaxy Z Fold 8 is rumoured to adopt a 'Passport' design — a taller, narrower aspect ratio when folded, which is said to be the next big trend in smartphones. This design change could improve one-handed usability while still offering a large inner display.

Samsung is also expected to launch the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra and Flip8 alongside the standard Fold8. The company has billed its latest foldable lineup as 'perfected for every way of living.'

Reliance Digital has already begun stocking Samsung's current Galaxy Z Fold8 series and Z Flip8 in stores across India, suggesting that the successor models are on their way.

For now, Indian consumers will have to wait for an official announcement. The rumoured 5000mAh battery, if confirmed, could be the feature that finally convinces more users to switch to a foldable phone.
