HDFC Bank stock has seen a rebound after hitting a one-year low, according to reports from Moneycontrol.com and Reuters. The stock has been affected by legal and leadership issues, which have been a major concern for investors.

Macquarie, a global investment bank, has predicted a 62% upside for HDFC Bank stock, but has also flagged concerns about the uncertainty surrounding the CEO position. This has been a major concern for investors, as the CEO is a key figure in the company's leadership.

The legal and leadership issues affecting HDFC Bank have been a major concern for investors. The company has been facing legal battles, and there has been uncertainty surrounding the CEO position.
