Sahitya Aaj Tak 2025 Returns to Delhi for Literary and Cultural Fest

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Culture & Arts Original source ↗
Key points
  • The festival will be held on November 29-30, 2025 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi.
  • Over 150 authors, poets, and thinkers from India and abroad will participate.
  • Sessions include book launches, panel discussions, storytelling, and cultural performances.

New Delhi: Sahitya Aaj Tak, one of India's largest literary festivals, is set to return to the capital on November 29 and 30, 2025. Organised by the India Today Group, the event will take place at Bharat Mandapam in New Delhi.

The festival promises a packed schedule featuring over 150 authors, poets, playwrights, and thinkers from across India and overseas. Organisers say this year's edition will focus on the power of storytelling in an age of information overload.

What to Expect at the Festival

The two-day event includes book launches, panel discussions, poetry readings, and interactive sessions. Topics range from contemporary fiction and history to politics and social issues.

Cultural performances—music, theatre, and dance—are also on the agenda. Children's activities and storytelling corners are planned to attract younger readers and families.

Notable Participants

While the full list of speakers has not been released, organisers have confirmed participation from leading Hindi and English authors, including several winners of major literary prizes. Regional language writers will also be represented.

Past editions have drawn figures like Javed Akhtar, Ashok Vajpeyi, and M.T. Vasudevan Nair. Organisers expect a similar mix of established and emerging voices this year.

Venue and Logistics

Bharat Mandapam, the sprawling convention centre at Pragati Maidan, will host the festival. Entry is free, but registration is required online due to limited seating in some halls.

The venue is accessible by metro and road. Organisers have arranged for parking and security at the site.

Growth of the Festival

Sahitya Aaj Tak started as a modest gathering in Delhi and has grown into a major cultural event. The festival attracts thousands of visitors each year, including students, writers, and casual readers.

Organisers say the 2025 edition will be the largest yet, with more stages and extended hours. A digital platform will stream select sessions live for those unable to attend in person.

Why It Matters

In an era of short attention spans, literary festivals like Sahitya Aaj Tak create space for long-form discussion and reflection. They also help bridge the gap between authors and their readers, a connection often lost in online promotion.

The event is supported by publishers, cultural institutions, and corporate sponsors. Critics have noted that such festivals boost the publishing ecosystem in India, especially for regional languages.

Officials have not yet released the full schedule or confirmed all participants. More details are expected in the coming weeks on the festival's official website.

Whether you are a book lover or just curious about the literary scene, Sahitya Aaj Tak 2025 offers a chance to engage with ideas that shape our world.
