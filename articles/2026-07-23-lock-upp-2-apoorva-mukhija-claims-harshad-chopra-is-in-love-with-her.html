Reality Show Drama Intensifies

Tensions are rising inside the Lock Upp house. In a new episode of Lock Upp Season 2, contestant Apoorva Mukhija dropped a bombshell on Shivangi Joshi. She claimed that actor Harshad Chopra is 'in love' with Shivangi.

The remark has added fuel to an already simmering fire. It came days after Shilpa Shinde taunted Shivangi and Harshad, calling them 'lovebirds'. That comment did not sit well with either of them.

Shilpa Shinde's 'Lovebirds' Taunt Backfires

Shilpa Shinde has been vocal about what she sees as a special bond between Shivangi and Harshad. She repeatedly referred to them as a pair, insinuating a romantic connection. Both Shivangi and Harshad slammed her for the remark.

Apoorva's Claim Stirs the Pot

Apoorva Mukhija did not stop at Shilpa's taunt. She directly told Shivangi that Harshad is in love with her. The statement has left Shivangi visibly disturbed and other contestants speculating about the dynamics inside the house.

The show, known for its high-voltage drama, has now entered a phase where personal equations are being questioned. The contestants are divided, with some backing Shivangi and others questioning Harshad's intentions.

Shivangi Joshi and Shilpa Shinde Clash

The showdown between Shivangi Joshi and Shilpa Shinde has become the centerpiece of Lock Upp Season 2. Their clash turned explosive during a recent task, with both women exchanging sharp words. Shilpa insisted she was only stating what everyone could see, while Shivangi accused her of spreading false narratives.

Harshad Chopra has largely stayed out of direct confrontation, but his defence of Shivangi has only deepened the divide. The audience is now watching closely to see how this storyline unfolds.
