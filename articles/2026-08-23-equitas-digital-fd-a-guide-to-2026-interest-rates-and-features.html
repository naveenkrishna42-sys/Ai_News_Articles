Equitas Digital FD: A Guide to 2026 Interest Rates and Features

Key points
  • Digital-first application process requiring only PAN and Aadhaar.
  • Higher interest rate offerings compared to traditional savings accounts.
  • Flexible tenure options ranging from 7 days to 10 years.
  • Senior citizens receive additional interest rate benefits.

Understanding the Equitas Digital Fixed Deposit

Equitas Small Finance Bank has positioned its digital fixed deposit (FD) as a primary vehicle for retail investors seeking stable returns. By removing the need for physical branch visits, the bank allows users to open accounts entirely through their mobile application or website. This product targets individuals who want to lock in capital for specific durations with guaranteed interest payouts.

Interest Rates and Tenure Structure

The bank offers varying interest rates based on the chosen tenure, which spans from as short as seven days to as long as ten years. Investors can choose between cumulative options, where interest is reinvested, or non-cumulative options, where payouts are issued monthly, quarterly, or annually. Current market trends for 2026 indicate that longer tenures generally yield higher returns, though short-term rates remain competitive for liquidity management.

Senior citizens continue to receive an additional spread over the standard rates offered to the general public. This remains a significant draw for retirees looking to maximize their monthly income from interest payouts. Because rates fluctuate based on RBI policy shifts and internal bank liquidity needs, depositors should verify the exact rate applicable on the day of booking via the Equitas official portal.

Eligibility and Application Process

To qualify for a digital FD, applicants must be Indian residents aged 18 or older. The documentation process is streamlined through Video KYC, requiring only a valid PAN card and an Aadhaar number linked to a registered mobile device. You do not need an existing relationship with the bank to initiate an account opening, though existing customers can link the FD directly to their current savings account for faster fund transfers.

  • Visit the official Equitas Small Finance Bank website or download the mobile banking app.
  • Select the 'Fixed Deposit' option from the investment menu.
  • Enter your PAN and Aadhaar details to complete the e-KYC verification.
  • Fund the deposit via net banking, UPI, or NEFT/RTGS from any bank account.
  • Download the digital FD receipt immediately upon transaction completion.

Key Considerations for Savers

It is essential to understand that premature withdrawal of an FD may attract a penalty, which reduces the effective yield. The bank typically charges a percentage deduction on the interest rate if the deposit is closed before the maturity date. Furthermore, interest earned on these deposits is subject to Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) as per the prevailing Income Tax Act regulations. Investors should provide their PAN details accurately to avoid higher TDS rates or to submit Form 15G/15H if their total annual income falls below the taxable threshold.

The Equitas Digital FD is best suited for conservative investors who prioritize capital preservation over market-linked volatility. It is particularly beneficial for those who require a low-maintenance savings instrument that can be managed entirely from a smartphone, offering predictable returns for short-to-medium-term financial goals.
