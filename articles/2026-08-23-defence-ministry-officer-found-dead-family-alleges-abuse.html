Pregnant Defence Ministry Officer Found Dead in Delhi

A pregnant officer with the Defence Ministry was found dead in her Delhi residence on Sunday. The officer, identified only as Twisha, was reported missing by her husband on Saturday. Her body was discovered hanging from a ceiling fan in her bedroom.

Family Alleges Harassment by Husband and In-Laws

Twisha's family has alleged that she was subjected to harassment by her husband and in-laws. According to family members, Twisha had been experiencing domestic abuse for several months. They believe that her death is a result of the abuse she endured.

Police Investigation Underway

Police have registered a case of suicide and are investigating the circumstances surrounding Twisha's death. The husband and in-laws have been booked for abetment of suicide. The case is currently under investigation.
