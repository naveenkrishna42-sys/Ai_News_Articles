World Cup Semifinal: Tuchel's Tactics Under Fire as Argentina Beat England

📅 2026-07-16 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • England lost the World Cup semifinal to Argentina, with Lionel Messi playing a key role.
  • Manager Thomas Tuchel's tactics and squad selection have been questioned by pundits and reports suggest players were 'puzzled'.
  • Former England defender Gary Neville argued Tuchel should have substituted captain Harry Kane.
  • Tuchel defended his approach, blaming the exit on English football's 'DNA'.

England's World Cup dream ended in heartbreak on Wednesday night as Argentina secured a semifinal victory. The defeat has immediately turned the spotlight on manager Thomas Tuchel, with questions being raised over his tactical setup and player choices.

Lionel Messi, as he has done so often on the grandest stage, was the difference-maker. The Argentine captain orchestrated his team's win, leaving the English side ruing what might have been.

Players 'Puzzled' by Tuchel's Game Plan

Reports emerging after the match indicate that several England players were confused by the tactics deployed by the German manager. According to The Guardian, the squad found the approach difficult to understand in the heat of the contest.

Tuchel opted for a defensive structure that ceded possession to Argentina. The strategy appeared to leave England's attacking talents isolated, unable to influence the game. Sources within the camp have described the mood as one of frustration and bewilderment.

Neville Calls for Kane Substitution

Former England defender and prominent pundit Gary Neville did not hold back in his criticism. Speaking after the final whistle, Neville argued that Tuchel should have taken captain Harry Kane off the pitch.

Kane, who has been the team's talisman for years, struggled to get into the game. Neville suggested that keeping him on when the team needed a different approach was a tactical error that cost England momentum and a potential route back into the match.

Tuchel Blames 'DNA' of English Football

Tuchel, however, has defended his decisions. In the post-match press conference, the England boss pointed to what he called the 'DNA' of English football as the root cause of the exit.

He argued that the culture and style ingrained in English players from a young age made it difficult to execute the more controlled, possession-based game he wanted. The manager claimed he had no choice but to pick a squad and tactics suited to this reality, despite the criticism.

“The players gave everything, but the way football is played in this country limits certain tactical options,” Tuchel was quoted as saying. His comments have drawn a mixed response, with some seeing it as a fair assessment and others as an abdication of responsibility.

Kane's Comments Add Fuel to 2030 Speculation

Adding another layer to the narrative, Harry Kane's post-match remarks have sparked fresh speculation about a potential move. The Times of India reports that Kane's comments about Messi have fueled talk of a 2030 World Cup bid.

While the exact nature of his remarks remains under discussion, the connection has reignited rumours about the striker's long-term future and potential involvement in future international campaigns. Officials have not yet confirmed any plans.

The loss means England's wait for a major trophy continues. For Tuchel, the pressure will only intensify as the post-mortem of this defeat begins in earnest. All eyes will now be on how the Football Association responds and whether the manager retains the backing of his squad and the public.
