39 Arrested, Drugs Worth Rs 55 Crore Seized in Bengaluru Op

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • 39 individuals arrested in coordinated raids across Bengaluru
  • Drugs worth Rs 55 crore seized, including MDMA and cocaine
  • Police say the network had interstate and international links

In one of the largest anti-narcotics operations in Bengaluru this year, police have arrested 39 people and seized drugs valued at Rs 55 crore. The crackdown, carried out over the past week, targeted multiple hideouts across the city, officials said on Monday.

The haul includes MDMA, cocaine, and other synthetic substances, along with cash and mobile phones believed to have been used in the drug trade. The operation was led by the Central Crime Branch (CCB) and involved over 100 personnel from various police stations.

Coordinated Raids Yield Big Haul

Acting on a tip-off, the police conducted simultaneous raids in areas such as Whitefield, Koramangala, and Yelahanka. The suspects were caught in possession of drugs packed for distribution, indicating a well-organised supply chain.

Preliminary investigations suggest the network had connections to suppliers in other states and possibly abroad. Police have not yet confirmed the exact origin of the drugs, but sources indicate a possible link to international syndicates.

Arrests and Evidence

Those arrested include alleged peddlers, middlemen, and a few financers. The accused have been booked under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. They were produced before a local court and remanded to judicial custody.

Officers have also seized several vehicles and electronic devices that may contain crucial evidence. A detailed forensic analysis is underway to trace the communication networks used by the accused.

Growing Concern Over Synthetic Drugs

The seizure comes amid rising concerns over the use of synthetic drugs in Bengaluru's party circuit. Over the last year, police have intensified patrolling and intelligence gathering to curb the menace.

In a statement, a senior police officer said the operation was part of a sustained drive. He added that more arrests are likely as the investigation progresses. The public has been urged to report any suspicious activity to the authorities.

What Lies Ahead

The accused will face trial, and the police are working to identify other members of the network who remain at large. Further searches are planned in nearby districts.

As the investigation deepens, the focus will be on dismantling the entire supply chain, from street-level dealers to high-level organisers. The outcome could influence future anti-drug strategies in Karnataka.
