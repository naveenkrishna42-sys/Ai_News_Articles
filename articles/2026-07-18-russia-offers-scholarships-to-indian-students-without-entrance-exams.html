Russia offers scholarships to Indian students without entrance exams

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • Russia has announced scholarships for Indian students with no entrance exams required.
  • The scholarships are part of efforts to strengthen educational cooperation between the two countries.
  • Details on application process and number of scholarships are yet to be released.

Russia unveils scholarship programme for Indian students

Russia has announced a new scholarship programme for Indian students, eliminating the need for entrance examinations. The initiative, confirmed by official sources, aims to make higher education in Russia more accessible to Indian applicants.

The scholarships will cover tuition fees and other academic expenses. Officials have not yet disclosed the exact number of scholarships or the specific universities involved.

No entrance exams required

Unlike many international study programmes that require standardised tests, this Russian scholarship does not mandate any entrance exam. Students will be selected based on their academic records and other criteria to be announced later.

This move is expected to simplify the application process significantly. Indian students often face hurdles with multiple exam requirements when applying abroad.

Strengthening educational ties

The announcement comes amid growing cooperation between India and Russia in education and technology. Both countries have been working to increase student exchanges and academic partnerships.

Russia currently hosts thousands of Indian students, many pursuing medical and engineering degrees. The new scholarships are likely to attract more applicants from India.

Application details awaited

The Russian government has not yet released the full application timeline or eligibility criteria. Interested students will need to monitor official channels for updates.

Indian educational consultants have welcomed the move, noting it could reduce financial burden on families. However, they caution that students should verify all details before applying.

Observers say the no-exam policy could give Russia an edge over other study destinations like the US, UK, and Canada, which typically require standardised tests for admission.

The scholarship programme is part of Russia's broader strategy to attract international students. The country aims to increase its foreign student population significantly in the coming years.

Officials have indicated that more information will be made available through the Russian Embassy in New Delhi and official education portals.

Students are advised to check for updates on the Russian Ministry of Science and Higher Education website. The application window is expected to open early next year.
