Hazlewood Returns for Darwin Test; Carey Keeps No. 6 Spot

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Josh Hazlewood returns to Australia's XI for the Darwin Test, keeping Scott Boland out.
  • Alex Carey will continue batting at No. 6, confirming the team's preferred balance.
  • The match marks Bangladesh's first Test in Darwin and the start of a long Australian season.

Hazlewood Back in the Fold

Australia have named their playing XI for the first Test against Bangladesh in Darwin, with Josh Hazlewood returning to the side. The pace spearhead takes his place in the attack, keeping Scott Boland on the sidelines for the series opener.

The selection ends weeks of speculation over who would partner Pat Cummins and Mitchell Starc with the new ball. Hazlewood's experience and precision appear to have tipped the scales in his favour as the hosts begin their long home summer.

Carey Firm at No. 6

Alex Carey will retain the No. 6 batting position, a move that signals the team management's faith in the wicketkeeper-batter's ability to contribute with the bat in the middle order. Carey has faced scrutiny over his spot in recent months, but the selectors have decided to persist with him in that role.

The decision means Australia will field five specialist bowlers, with Cameron Green and Mitchell Marsh providing additional pace options. This balance allows the team to maintain a potent attack while still having depth in the lower order.

Darwin's Long-Awaited Test

Darwin will host its first Test match, a landmark occasion for cricket in the Northern Territory. The venue has been a regular stop for limited-overs fixtures, but this will be the first time the city stages a five-day international.

For Bangladesh, the match represents a rare opportunity to compete in Australia, having last toured the country several years ago. The visitors will be eager to prove their mettle on unfamiliar turf, and the conditions in Darwin are expected to offer something different from the usual Australian surfaces.

What to Watch

With the XI locked in, all eyes will be on how Hazlewood adapts to the conditions after a layoff. Bangladesh's top order, led by their captain, will need to counter the pace trio early on. The series also marks the beginning of a packed Australian schedule leading into the summer.

Fans in Darwin are in for a historic week as the city finally gets its taste of Test cricket.
