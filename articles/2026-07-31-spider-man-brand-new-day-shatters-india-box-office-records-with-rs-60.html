Spider-Man: Brand New Day Shatters India Box Office Records with Rs 60 Cr Opening Day

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Spider-Man: Brand New Day earns Rs 60 crore on its opening day in India, the highest for any Hollywood film.
  • The film surpasses Avengers: Endgame's previous record, signaling a new era for superhero cinema in India.
  • Early reviews praise Tom Holland's performance and director Destin Daniel Cretton's fresh take on the franchise.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day has swung past all expectations in India, netting an estimated Rs 60 crore on its opening day. That figure makes it the biggest Hollywood opening day in the country's history, dethroning Avengers: Endgame, which had held the record since 2019.

The numbers, reported by trade analysts, underline the massive pull of Tom Holland's web-slinger in the Indian market. The film released in multiple languages, including Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu, and drew packed houses in metropolitan centres as well as smaller cities.

A Record That Took Seven Years to Break

Avengers: Endgame had collected around Rs 53 crore on its opening day in India, a benchmark that seemed untouchable for years. No Hollywood release came close, even as Marvel and DC continued to flood theatres with sequels and spin-offs.

Brand New Day, however, arrived with uncommon momentum. Advance bookings opened weeks ahead of release, and early shows across the country reported near-full occupancy. The film's Thursday previews in the US had already generated buzz, with trade reports noting it outperformed Spider-Man: No Way Home's preview numbers.

Why This Spider-Man Feels Different

Early reviews in India and abroad have been markedly positive. Critics have highlighted director Destin Daniel Cretton's approach, calling it a "soulful reinvention" that allows Tom Holland to "finally swing free." The film, they say, trades some of the franchise's bluster for a more grounded exploration of a young person's anxieties.

That tonal shift appears to have resonated with audiences. Trade observers note that the film's emotional core, combined with spectacle, has driven repeat viewing in the opening weekend. The Hindu's review described it as a fresh start for the character, while The Wire.in noted it locates a distinctly contemporary unease within the superhero genre's familiar framework.

The India Factor

India has become a crucial market for Hollywood franchises, and Brand New Day's performance underscores that shift. The film's distributor opted for a wide release in over 3,000 screens across the country, with dubbed versions in multiple regional languages. That strategy, coupled with strategic pricing for morning shows, appears to have paid off.

Industry insiders say the opening day collection was bolstered by strong footfalls in non-metro markets, a trend that has become more pronounced in recent years. The film's appeal to younger audiences, many of whom grew up with Holland's iteration of the character, was evident in the turnout.

What's Next for the Box Office

The film is expected to maintain momentum through the weekend, with trade projections suggesting a first-weekend total that could approach Rs 150 crore. If those numbers hold, Brand New Day will not only own the opening day record but also set a new benchmark for lifetime collections of a Hollywood film in India.

All eyes now are on how the film performs on Monday, a day that often determines a blockbuster's long-term trajectory in India. Early indicators suggest strong word-of-mouth, but the real test will be whether audiences beyond the opening weekend fan rush turn up in similar numbers.
