Kangana Ranaut hits back at Naseeruddin Shah, says 'I'd rather be a dog than a lomdi'

📅 2026-08-11 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Kangana Ranaut responded to Naseeruddin Shah's 'dog' comment with a sharp retort.
  • The exchange follows Piyush Mishra's reaction to Shah's remarks on the Jharkhand student protest.
  • Ranaut accused Shah of eating from India while supporting a neighbouring country.

The war of words between Kangana Ranaut and Naseeruddin Shah has escalated, with the actress delivering a sharp retort to the veteran actor's recent 'dog' comment. Her response comes days after Shah's remarks on the ongoing Jharkhand student protest triggered a political and cinematic firestorm.

In a statement that has since gone viral, Ranaut said, "I'd rather be a dog than a lomdi (fox) like Naseeruddin." The comment was aimed at Shah's criticism of her earlier stance on the protests, where she had compared certain protesters to animals.

The Context: A Spat Over Protests

The controversy began when Naseeruddin Shah, known for his outspoken views, weighed in on the Jharkhand student agitation. He reportedly took a dig at Ranaut's comments, likening her rhetoric to that of a 'dog' — a remark that did not sit well with the actor-turned-politician.

Piyush Mishra, the singer and actor, had earlier reacted to Shah's comment, adding another layer to the public spat. Mishra's response seemed to support Shah, which further provoked Ranaut, prompting her latest salvo.

Ranaut's Accusation: 'Eats From India, Fights For Neighbour'

In her sharpest attack yet, Ranaut accused Shah of hypocrisy. "Naseeruddin eats from India, but fights for the neighbouring country," she said, implying that the veteran actor's loyalties were questionable. The remark appears to reference Shah's past statements on cross-border issues, though she did not elaborate.

The actress, who has often positioned herself as a nationalist voice, did not stop there. She questioned the credibility of artists who criticise the government while enjoying the benefits of working in India.

The Political Undercurrent

The spat is not merely a personal clash. It reflects a deeper divide in the Indian film industry, where several actors have publicly taken opposing stands on issues of nationalism and protest. The Jharkhand student protest, which has drawn national attention, has become a flashpoint for these debates.

Ranaut, who has been a vocal supporter of the central government, has frequently targeted Bollywood's liberal establishment. Shah, on the other hand, has consistently defended the right to dissent, making this confrontation inevitable.

Industry Reactions and Fallout

While Piyush Mishra's reaction has added fuel to the fire, other industry figures have remained cautious. No major actor or director has publicly commented on the exchange, indicating the sensitivity of the issue.

Social media, however, has been abuzz. Supporters of both actors have clashed online, with hashtags trending for and against each. The debate has now moved beyond cinema, touching on questions of patriotism, freedom of speech, and the role of artists in a democracy.

Neither Shah nor Mishra has responded to Ranaut's latest remarks at the time of writing. The veteran actor's camp has remained silent, leaving room for speculation about a possible follow-up.

As the controversy continues to simmer, all eyes are now on whether this public feud will spill into the professional sphere. With both actors known for their strong convictions, the industry is bracing for more heated exchanges in the coming days.

What remains to be seen is whether this spat will lead to a broader conversation on dissent and nationalism in Indian cinema, or fizzle out as another celebrity clash.
