Katrina Kaif faces backlash as fan page with 70K followers shuts down over CJP protest silence

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • A popular Katrina Kaif fan page with 70,000 followers was deactivated after the admin cited the actress's silence on the CJP protests.
  • The admin posted a note saying, 'You do not have spine,' before taking the page down.
  • The backlash comes amid a broader trend of celebrities being called out for not commenting on social and political issues.

Katrina Kaif is facing a wave of online backlash after a fan page with 70,000 followers was shut down by its admin. The reason cited was the actress's silence on the ongoing CJP protests in the country.

The page, which had been running for years, posted a final message before being deactivated. The admin wrote that the decision was taken because Kaif did not speak out on the issue, adding, 'You do not have spine.'

Fan page shutdown sparks debate

The shutdown has triggered a larger conversation on social media. Many users are questioning whether celebrities have a responsibility to comment on political and social movements.

Some supporters of the actress have argued that she is not obligated to speak on every issue. Others have pointed out that silence can be interpreted as a lack of solidarity with protesters.

Broader trend of celebrity accountability

This is not an isolated incident. In recent weeks, several Indian celebrities have faced similar pressure to take a stand on the CJP protests. Actor Madhavan was also mocked with a 'Gayab' meme for not addressing the issue.

The trend reflects a growing expectation among online audiences for public figures to use their platforms for more than just entertainment. The silence of stars like Kaif is increasingly being seen as a political statement in itself.

Several other fan accounts and public figures have also been called out. The pressure is not limited to Bollywood; it extends to sportspersons and influencers as well.

What is the CJP protest?

The CJP protests refer to a series of demonstrations that have been taking place across India. The exact nature and demands of the protests have not been detailed in the reports, but the movement has gained significant traction online.

Activists and supporters have been urging celebrities to lend their voices. The lack of response from major stars has led to frustration among sections of the public.

Officials have not yet confirmed any official statements from Kaif or her team regarding the backlash. The actress has not addressed the shutdown of the fan page publicly.

The incident highlights the shifting dynamics between celebrities and their fanbases. Fans are no longer passive consumers; they are increasingly willing to hold their idols accountable for their actions—or inaction.

It remains to be seen whether Kaif will break her silence on the matter. For now, the debate continues to simmer on social media platforms.
