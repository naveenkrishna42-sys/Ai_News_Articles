SC Stays Arrest of Abhishek Banerjee's Aide Sumit Roy in Salboni Land Case

📅 2026-08-06 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Supreme Court stays arrest of Sumit Roy, an aide of Abhishek Banerjee, in the Salboni land-grab case.
  • Interim protection granted with a condition to cooperate with the ongoing CID investigation.
  • CID had earlier conducted searches at Roy's residence with an arrest warrant in the land fraud case.
  • Bengal CID has taken over corruption cases against Roy, signaling a widening probe.

Interim Relief From Top Court

The Supreme Court on Thursday intervened in the Salboni land-grab case, staying the arrest of Sumit Roy, a close aide of Trinamool Congress leader Abhishek Banerjee. The order provides interim protection to Roy, who faces allegations of land fraud and corruption.

A bench hearing the matter directed Roy to cooperate fully with the investigation. The relief is conditional, and the court has made it clear that non-cooperation could lead to withdrawal of the protection.

Background of the Case

The case pertains to alleged illegal land acquisition in Salboni, a region in West Bengal's Paschim Medinipur district. Complaints had accused Roy of being involved in a land-grabbing racket, prompting the state's Criminal Investigation Department (CID) to step in.

The CID had recently conducted searches at Roy's residence, armed with an arrest warrant. The development marked an escalation in the probe, which has now been taken over by the Bengal CID, signaling a deeper scrutiny of the allegations.

Legal Wrangling and Conditions

Roy had moved the Supreme Court seeking protection, arguing that the case against him was motivated. His counsel contended that the arrest was being used as a tool for harassment, an assertion the state has denied.

The court, while granting interim relief, did not dwell extensively on the merits of the case. Instead, it emphasized the need for a fair investigation. The order allows Roy to remain free but binds him to appear before the investigating officer whenever required.

Political Undertones

The case has inevitably drawn political attention, given Roy's association with Abhishek Banerjee, the nephew of Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee. Abhishek Banerjee, a prominent face of the TMC, has not been named in this particular case, but his proximity to Roy has made the matter a talking point.

Opposition parties have sought to link the allegations to broader issues of governance in the state. The TMC, on its part, has dismissed the charges as an attempt to target its leadership through proxy cases.

What Lies Ahead

The Supreme Court's interim order is not a clean chit. The case now hinges on Roy's cooperation with the CID and the evidence gathered during the searches. The next hearing will likely assess the progress of the investigation.

For now, the stay provides a breather for Roy, but the legal battle is far from over. The CID's takeover of corruption cases suggests that the probe may expand, and the coming weeks could see more developments in this politically charged case.
