Vijay's Jana Nayagan Advance Booking Opens in India, Shows Sell Out at ₹2,500

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Advance booking for 'Jana Nayagan' opened across India on Sunday.
  • Ticket prices have been set as high as ₹2,500 in some premium screens.
  • Multiple shows have already been reported as sold out within hours of booking going live.
  • The film has earned over ₹6.7 crore in advance bookings in the first few hours.

Advance ticket sales for Thalapathy Vijay's upcoming film 'Jana Nayagan' finally opened in India on Sunday, and the response has been nothing short of explosive. Within hours of booking systems going live, multiple shows across the country were reported as sold out, despite ticket prices reaching as high as ₹2,500 in premium screens.

The film, which is being billed as Vijay's farewell movie before he transitions fully into politics, has generated unprecedented demand. Trade analysts are already tracking its advance booking numbers closely, with early estimates suggesting the film has crossed ₹6.7 crore in pre-sales within the first few hours of booking opening.

Ticket Prices Hit Record Highs

In several multiplex chains and single-screen theatres in Tamil Nadu and other key markets, tickets for the opening weekend have been priced significantly higher than usual. Reports indicate that some premium IMAX and 4DX screens have set ticket rates at ₹2,500, a figure that rivals the pricing of major Bollywood blockbusters on their opening days.

Despite the steep pricing, fans have not held back. Social media platforms have been flooded with screenshots of booked seats and reports of sold-out shows within minutes of counters opening. Theatre owners in Chennai, Coimbatore, and Bengaluru have confirmed that the first-day-first-show screenings are fully booked in several locations.

Strict Instructions for Theatres

Ahead of the booking opening, authorities and the film's production team issued strict guidelines to theatres screening 'Jana Nayagan'. Sources say these instructions cover everything from security protocols to ensuring that no black marketing of tickets takes place.

Theatres have been asked to verify ticket purchases thoroughly and to limit the number of tickets per transaction to prevent bulk buying by scalpers. This comes in the wake of reports that some tickets were being resold online for exorbitant prices even before official bookings opened.

A Record-Breaking Start in Sight

The early advance booking figures of ₹6.7 crore have already set tongues wagging in the film industry. Trade experts believe that if the momentum continues, 'Jana Nayagan' could challenge the opening day records set by Rajinikanth's recent releases.

Given Vijay's massive fan following and the emotional weight attached to this being his final film before a likely political career, the numbers are expected to climb significantly as more screens open for booking in the coming days.

The Tiruppur Connection

Interestingly, the frenzy around the film has also had a tangible impact on the ground. Reports from Tiruppur indicate that knitwear units in the city have been working overtime to produce merchandise and apparel linked to 'Jana Nayagan'.

From t-shirts with the film's logo to commemorative scarves, the demand for Vijay-branded merchandise has surged in the lead-up to the release. This has provided a boost to local manufacturers who have been struggling with slowing exports.

As the clock ticks down to the film's release, all eyes are on how the advance booking numbers evolve. With tickets already selling at a blistering pace, the film is on track to deliver one of the biggest opening weekends in Indian cinema history. The question now is not if it will break records, but by how much.
