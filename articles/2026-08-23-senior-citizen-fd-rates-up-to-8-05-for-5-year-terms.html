Senior citizen FD rates: Up to 8.05% for 5-year terms

📅 2026-08-23 📂 Credit Cards & Cashback Original source ↗
Key points
  • Up to 8.05% interest rate for senior citizens on 5-year FDs
  • List of banks offering these rates
  • Eligibility criteria and how to apply

What is a Fixed Deposit (FD)?

A Fixed Deposit is a savings instrument offered by banks, where you deposit a lump sum amount for a fixed period, and in return, you earn a fixed interest rate. The interest rate is typically higher than a savings account, but you cannot withdraw the money before the maturity date.

Interest Rates for Senior Citizens

Senior citizens, defined as individuals aged 60 years and above, can earn higher interest rates on their fixed deposits. The interest rate can go up to 8.05% for a 5-year term. This is higher than the general public interest rate, which is around 7.25% for the same term.

List of Banks Offering These Rates

Several banks in India offer these high interest rates for senior citizens. Some of the banks include State Bank of India, ICICI Bank, HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, and Kotak Mahindra Bank. However, the exact interest rates may vary depending on the bank and the specific deposit scheme.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for these high interest rates, you must be a senior citizen, i.e., aged 60 years and above. You must also have a valid proof of identity and address. The minimum deposit amount may vary from bank to bank, but it is usually around ₹1 lakh.

How to Apply

You can apply for a fixed deposit online or offline. To apply online, you need to visit the bank's official website and fill out the form. You will need to provide your personal details, the amount you want to deposit, and the tenure of the deposit. Once you have filled out the form, you can submit it and the bank will process your request. The processing time may vary, but it is usually a few days.

Offline Application

To apply offline, you need to visit the nearest branch of the bank and fill out the form. You will need to provide your personal details, the amount you want to deposit, and the tenure of the deposit. Once you have filled out the form, you can submit it to the bank's representative. The bank will then process your request and inform you about the interest rate and the maturity amount.

Conclusion

Fixed deposits are a great way to earn higher interest rates, especially for senior citizens. The interest rate can go up to 8.05% for a 5-year term. Several banks in India offer these high interest rates, and you can apply for a fixed deposit online or offline. Make sure you meet the eligibility criteria and provide the necessary documents.
