CJP's Instagram Account Allegedly Run From Abroad; Cyber Probe Sought

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • A complaint has been filed alleging that the Cockroach Janta Party's Instagram account is 'foreign-run'.
  • The complaint flags a shift in the account's operational location from the US to Ireland.
  • A demand has been made for a cyber forensic investigation into the matter.

New Delhi: A fresh controversy has erupted around the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) with a formal complaint alleging that its official Instagram account is being operated from outside India. The complaint, filed with authorities, points to a significant shift in the account's digital footprint, moving from the United States to Ireland.

The development comes amid heightened scrutiny of the party's social media presence. The complainant has demanded a thorough cyber forensic probe to determine the true operators and location of the account.

Allegations of Foreign Operation

The complaint specifically flags the geographical shift of the account's management. According to the complaint, the account initially showed operational markers linked to the United States. It then changed, with indicators now pointing to Ireland.

This alleged shift has raised questions about who is actually running the account. The complainant argues that this pattern suggests the account is not being managed from within India, potentially violating norms for political entities.

Demand for Cyber Forensic Investigation

The core demand in the complaint is for a detailed cyber forensic investigation. Authorities have been asked to trace the account's IP addresses and login locations over time. The goal is to establish a clear chain of custody and operation.

Officials have not yet confirmed whether an investigation has been formally launched. The complaint is currently under review by the relevant cyber crime cell.

This is not the first time the CJP's social media has been in the news. Recently, party chief Saurav Das's WhatsApp account was restored after a brief block. Das had publicly thanked supporters for pressuring Meta over the issue.

Broader Context of Social Media Scrutiny

The complaint against the CJP account is part of a wider pattern of social media platforms facing scrutiny in India. Several accounts and pages have been blocked or restricted in recent months for various reasons.

Meta, which owns Instagram and WhatsApp, has been at the centre of several such actions. The company recently blocked an Instagram reel by Maktoob Hindi related to protests at Jantar Mantar. The reasons for that block remain a point of debate.

The CJP itself has faced previous bans. Its Instagram account was banned in India earlier, though the specific reasons for that ban were not officially detailed at the time.

What happens next depends on the authorities' response to the complaint. A forensic probe, if ordered, could take weeks to complete. The findings could have significant implications for the party's ability to operate its digital presence. All eyes are now on the cyber crime unit's next move.
