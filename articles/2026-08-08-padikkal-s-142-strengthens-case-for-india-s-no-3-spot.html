Padikkal's 142* strengthens case for India's No. 3 spot

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Padikkal scored an unbeaten 142 for India in the three-day tour match against SLC XI.
  • The knock comes after extensive preparation against spin, including three-hour net sessions.
  • Jaiswal and Pant failed with the bat, potentially opening the door for Padikkal in the Test XI.

A Statement Knock in Colombo

Devdutt Padikkal has made a compelling case for the No. 3 spot in India's Test batting order. The left-hander struck an unbeaten 142 on the second day of the three-day warm-up match against a Sri Lanka Cricket XI at Colombo, headlining India's reply with a stylish, controlled innings.

The knock, coming in challenging conditions, has injected fresh debate into the visitors' middle-order plans ahead of the Test series. Padikkal's timing could not have been better, with the selectors and team management still weighing their options for the crucial number three position.

Preparation Behind the Performance

What makes this innings particularly noteworthy is the groundwork that preceded it. Reports from the Indian camp suggest Padikkal had been putting in extensive work against spin, with sessions stretching up to three hours in the nets. The focus was clearly on negating the turning tracks expected in Sri Lanka.

That preparation showed in the match. Padikkal looked assured against both pace and spin, rotating the strike and finding the boundary with regularity. His footwork was decisive, and his judgement outside off stump was immaculate throughout the innings.

Mixed Fortunes for Others

While Padikkal flourished, others in the race for batting slots did not. Yashasvi Jaiswal and Rishabh Pant, both considered strong contenders for the top order, failed to make an impression in the warm-up. Their dismissals have left the team management with a selection puzzle.

The failure of the established names could tilt the scales in Padikkal's favour. With the Test series looming, the management will value the assurance of a player in form, especially one who has shown the temperament to bat long in subcontinental conditions.

What This Means for the Test XI

The No. 3 slot in Test cricket carries immense responsibility. It requires a batter who can negotiate the new ball and also stabilise the innings if early wickets fall. Padikkal's century in a tour match is one thing, but doing it against the Sri Lankan attack in a Test is another matter entirely.

Still, the knock will be hard to ignore. The team management had earlier indicated that performances in the warm-up would be given due weightage. If that holds true, Padikkal has done his chances no harm at all.

Looking Ahead

The tour match continues, and Padikkal will be eager to convert his start into a definitive statement. The Indian think tank, meanwhile, will be watching closely as they finalise their playing XI for the first Test.

All eyes will now be on the team selection. If Padikkal gets the nod at No. 3, it would be a reward for patience, preparation, and a timely hundred when it mattered most.
