Breaking News: Former VP Jagdeep Dhankhar Hospitalised After Collapse

Former Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar was admitted to the All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) in Delhi on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, after collapsing during his routine morning walk. The incident, which occurred in the early hours, prompted immediate medical attention and his swift transfer to the premier medical facility. Sources close to the former Vice President's office confirmed the development, though specific details regarding his condition remain under wraps as medical assessments are underway.

The news quickly spread across national media outlets, highlighting the immediate concern for the health of a prominent public figure. Dhankhar, aged 75, served as the 14th Vice President of India, holding office until August 2027. His sudden hospitalization has led to an outpouring of well-wishes from across the political spectrum and the wider public.

Immediate Response and Medical Care at AIIMS

Upon his collapse, Mr. Dhankhar received prompt assistance, leading to his rapid admission to AIIMS, Delhi. Known for its advanced medical infrastructure and specialized departments, AIIMS is frequently the preferred institution for high-profile patients requiring urgent care. He is currently under observation by a team of specialists, undergoing a series of diagnostic tests to ascertain the cause of the collapse and determine his overall health status.

While official bulletins detailing his medical condition are yet to be released, standard protocols for such a situation involve comprehensive cardiovascular, neurological, and general health check-ups. The medical team's priority will be to stabilize his condition and ensure a thorough understanding of any underlying factors. The hospital administration is expected to issue updates as deemed appropriate, balancing public interest with patient privacy.

Public Reaction and Political Implications

The health of a former Vice President naturally garners significant public and political attention. Leaders from various political parties have expressed their concern and conveyed their wishes for his speedy recovery. A Look at Jagdeep Dhankhar's Public Service

Jagdeep Dhankhar has had a distinguished career marked by extensive public service across various capacities. Prior to his tenure as Vice President, he served as the Governor of West Bengal from 2019 to 2022, a period marked by significant administrative and political engagement. His political journey also includes serving as a Member of Parliament in the Lok Sabha from 1989 to 1991, representing the Jhunjhunu constituency in Rajasthan.

Earlier, he also held the portfolio of Union Minister of State for Parliamentary Affairs in 1990. A seasoned lawyer by profession, Dhankhar also practiced in the Rajasthan High Court and the Supreme Court of India. His diverse experience across legislative, executive, and judicial fields has cemented his position as a respected figure in Indian public life.
