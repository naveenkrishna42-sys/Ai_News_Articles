Sensex, Nifty Likely to Open Flat; Global Cues Mixed

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • GIFT Nifty trends suggest a muted start for Indian indices.
  • Global markets showed mixed performance, with US futures flat and Asian peers trading cautiously.
  • Investors will watch for domestic macroeconomic data and corporate earnings.
  • Rupee and crude oil prices remain key monitors for market direction.

Indian equity benchmarks are expected to open on a flat note on Monday, with the Sensex and Nifty likely to start the session without a clear directional bias. The muted outlook comes as global cues remain mixed, with investors treading cautiously ahead of domestic triggers.

GIFT Nifty Indicates Cautious Start

Early indications from the GIFT Nifty, which trades on the NSE International Exchange, suggested a steady to slightly positive opening for the Nifty 50. The index, which serves as a proxy for Indian markets, hovered near the flat line, pointing to a lack of strong buying or selling interest at the start of the week.

Market participants are keenly watching for any fresh triggers, with the absence of major domestic events keeping sentiment in check. Analysts noted that the market could take cues from global trends and any news flow on the geopolitical or economic front.

Global Markets Offer No Clear Direction

Overseas, Asian markets opened with modest gains in some regions, while others traded in the red, reflecting a cautious mood among investors. US stock futures were also flat, indicating that Wall Street's previous session close had not set a definitive tone for the day.

Investors are awaiting key economic data from major economies, including inflation readings and central bank commentary, which could influence global risk appetite. The lack of strong momentum in global equities has left domestic traders without a clear roadmap.

Domestic Factors in Focus

On the domestic front, investors are expected to track the movement of the rupee against the US dollar, as well as crude oil prices, both of which have a bearing on inflation and fiscal health. A weaker rupee or a spike in oil prices could weigh on market sentiment, while stability in these factors may provide some support.

Corporate earnings season is also on the radar, with several companies slated to announce their quarterly results. Positive earnings surprises could lift specific stocks and sectors, though broader indices may remain range-bound in the absence of a strong catalyst.

Technical View and Support Levels

From a technical perspective, traders are watching key support and resistance levels on the Nifty. Analysts suggest that the index may find support in the 24,500-24,600 zone, while resistance is likely around the 24,800-24,900 mark. A breakout on either side could set the tone for the near term.

Market breadth is expected to remain selective, with sectoral indices likely to see mixed moves. Banking, IT, and auto stocks will be closely watched for any stock-specific action.

As the session progresses, all eyes will be on how global cues evolve and whether any domestic news flow provides direction. With no major triggers on the horizon, the market could remain in a consolidation phase for now.

Going forward, traders will keep a close watch on global central bank policy signals and upcoming economic data releases from India, which could offer fresh direction in the coming days.
