Hyderabad Vigil for Sonam Wangchuk Amid NEET Row

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Candlelight vigil in Hyderabad for Sonam Wangchuk.
  • Demand for action on NEET controversy.
  • Civil society groups lead the protest.

Vigil in the City

Hyderabad saw a quiet but firm show of solidarity on Wednesday evening. Civil society groups gathered for a candlelight vigil, their flames flickering against the night sky. The demand was clear: save Sonam Wangchuk and address the ongoing NEET row.

The protest, held at a central location in the city, drew students, activists, and concerned citizens. Many held placards with slogans calling for justice. The atmosphere was somber but resolute.

Who is Sonam Wangchuk?

Sonam Wangchuk, a prominent education reform activist from Ladakh, has been at the centre of recent controversies. His arrest earlier this month sparked nationwide protests. Critics say his detention is linked to his advocacy for fairer examination policies, particularly regarding the NEET.

The National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET) has been a flashpoint in Indian education. Allegations of paper leaks, regional disparities, and administrative mishandling have marred the exam. Wangchuk had been vocal about these issues before his arrest.

The NEET Row

The NEET controversy deepened after reports of irregularities surfaced in several states. Students have protested for months, demanding a re-examination or policy overhaul. The government has maintained that the process was fair, but critics disagree.

In Hyderabad, the vigil linked Wangchuk’s plight to the broader NEET crisis. Organisers argued that his arrest was an attempt to silence dissent. “We cannot let this stand,” one activist said. “The exam system is broken, and those who speak up are being targeted.”

Police maintained a watchful presence but did not intervene. The event passed peacefully, with participants lighting candles and observing a minute of silence.

Demands and Reactions

The groups submitted a memorandum to local authorities. Their key demands included Wangchuk’s immediate release and a transparent investigation into the NEET irregularities. They also called for student safety and fair access to education.

Reactions from officials have been measured. The Hyderabad police confirmed they had no reports of any untoward incidents. The state education department has not yet commented on the vigil or the demands.

Similar protests have been reported in other cities, including Delhi and Bengaluru. The movement appears to be gaining traction online as well, with hashtags like #SaveSonamWangchuk trending on social media.

What happens next remains unclear. Wangchuk’s legal team has filed for bail, and a hearing is expected later this week. The NEET row, meanwhile, continues to simmer, with students awaiting the next exam cycle or potential policy changes.
