Jharkhand Students Form Team

📅 2026-08-07 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • 11-member team formed by protesting students
  • Talks to be held with Jharkhand government
  • Protests over job exam irregularities

Protests Over Job Exam Irregularities

Protesting students in Jharkhand have formed an 11-member team to hold talks with the state government. The move comes amid ongoing protests over irregularities in a recent job exam. Officials have not yet confirmed the exact date for the talks.

Background of the Protests

The protests, which have been going on for several days, have disrupted life in the state capital Ranchi. The students are demanding a probe into the alleged irregularities in the exam and action against those responsible. The opposition BJP has been supporting the protests, stalling proceedings in the Jharkhand Assembly.

Political Support for the Protests

The Congress party has also backed the protests, with Rahul Gandhi reaching out to the students and expressing his support. The party's high command has pushed for an independent stand on the issue, despite being an ally of the ruling party in the state. The protests have also drawn attention from other political parties and youth organizations.

Demands of the Protesting Students

The protesting students have made several demands, including a thorough investigation into the exam irregularities and action against those responsible. They have also demanded that the government take steps to ensure fairness and transparency in future exams. The students have warned of intensifying their protests if their demands are not met.

The formation of the 11-member team is seen as a positive step towards resolving the issue. However, it remains to be seen how the talks with the government will progress and whether the students' demands will be met.

What Next

The talks between the protesting students and the Jharkhand government are expected to begin soon. The outcome of these talks will be crucial in determining the future course of the protests and the resolution of the issue. The students and the government will need to work together to find a solution that addresses the concerns of all parties involved.
