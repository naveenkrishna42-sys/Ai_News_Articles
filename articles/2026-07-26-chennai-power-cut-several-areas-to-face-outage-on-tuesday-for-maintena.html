Chennai Power Cut: Several Areas to Face Outage on Tuesday for Maintenance

📅 2026-07-26 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Power supply will be suspended in several areas of Chennai on Tuesday.
  • The outage is due to scheduled maintenance work by Tangedco.
  • Residents and businesses in affected areas are advised to make necessary arrangements.

Maintenance Work to Cause Power Outage Across Chennai

The Tamil Nadu Generation and Distribution Corporation (Tangedco) has announced a scheduled power shutdown in multiple parts of Chennai on Tuesday. The disruption is required to carry out essential maintenance and upgrade work on the city's power infrastructure.

Officials confirmed that the supply will be suspended during specific hours, though the exact timings may vary across localities. Residents and commercial establishments in the affected zones have been urged to plan accordingly.

Areas Likely to Be Affected

The utility has not yet released the complete list of neighbourhoods that will face the outage. However, based on previous maintenance schedules, areas in North, Central, and South Chennai are expected to be included. Tangedco typically issues a detailed list on its official website and through local offices a day prior.

Consumers in these regions should expect power supply to remain interrupted for a few hours, starting from the morning. The corporation has advised people to keep mobile phones charged and store enough water if they rely on electric pumps.

Reason Behind the Shutdown

The power suspension is part of Tangedco's routine maintenance programme. The work involves checking transformers, upgrading cables, and trimming vegetation near power lines. Such exercises are crucial to prevent unplanned outages and improve the reliability of the electricity grid ahead of the monsoon season.

A senior Tangedco official stated that the maintenance is pre-planned and coordinated with other civic agencies to minimise inconvenience. Emergency services like hospitals and water supply stations will either be exempted or provided with backup power arrangements.

What Residents Should Do

Residents in areas facing the shutdown are advised to switch off sensitive electronic equipment before the outage. This can prevent damage from voltage fluctuations when power is restored. Using inverters or generators is an option, but people should ensure they are in good working condition.

Tangedco has requested the public to cooperate and treat all electrical lines as live during the maintenance period. Officials warn that unauthorised personnel should not tamper with any equipment. For any assistance, consumers can contact the local Tangedco office or the 24x7 helpline.

Previous Maintenance and Future Plans

This is not an isolated event. Tangedco frequently undertakes such maintenance across Chennai and its suburbs. The corporation has been working to upgrade the ageing power infrastructure in several parts of the city, especially in areas that experience frequent faults.

Residents have often complained about short notice for such shutdowns. This time, the announcement was made a day in advance, giving people some time to prepare. The utility aims to complete the work within the scheduled window to avoid extended disruption.

The next phase of maintenance is likely to cover other zones in the coming weeks. Tangedco has promised to improve communication regarding future outages through SMS alerts and local cable TV scrolls.
