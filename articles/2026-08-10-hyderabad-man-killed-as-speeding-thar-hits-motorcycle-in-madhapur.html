Hyderabad: Man killed as speeding Thar hits motorcycle in Madhapur

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 50-year-old man was killed in Madhapur after a speeding Thar hit his motorcycle.
  • The incident occurred on Monday; police have registered a case.
  • The victim's identity and details of the driver are awaited.

Hyderabad: A 50-year-old man was killed on Monday after a speeding Mahindra Thar rammed into his motorcycle in Madhapur, a busy IT corridor in the city. The incident took place in the afternoon, sending shockwaves through the neighbourhood.

Police said the victim was riding a motorcycle when the Thar, reportedly moving at high speed, collided with him. The impact was so severe that the man died on the spot. His identity has not been officially confirmed yet, and authorities are working to trace his family.

Accident in Madhapur

Madhapur, known for its dense traffic and tech offices, witnessed the tragedy near a prominent junction. Eyewitnesses told police that the Thar was speeding and appeared to have lost control before the collision. The driver of the vehicle has been detained for questioning, though formal charges are yet to be filed.

Traffic police reached the spot shortly after the incident and cleared the area to restore vehicular movement. The motorcycle was badly mangled, and the Thar sustained visible damage to its front. Local residents and commuters gathered at the scene, with many expressing concern over reckless driving in the area.

Police investigation underway

Officials have registered a case under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code and the Motor Vehicles Act. Preliminary inquiries suggest that overspeeding was the primary cause, but a detailed investigation is on. The police are also checking CCTV footage from nearby establishments to piece together the sequence of events.

“We have taken the vehicle into custody and are questioning the driver. Further action will follow once the investigation is complete,” a police officer said on condition of anonymity. The victim's body has been sent to a government hospital for post-mortem, and the report is awaited.

Rising concern over road safety

This incident adds to a growing list of road accidents in Hyderabad, particularly involving high-velocity vehicles on inner roads. In recent months, several similar cases have been reported across the city, prompting authorities to discuss stricter enforcement. However, officials have not yet announced any new measures in response to this specific crash.

Road safety activists have repeatedly called for speed limits and better signage in residential and commercial zones. Madhapur, with its mix of pedestrians, two-wheelers, and cars, remains a high-risk spot during peak hours. Monday's accident has reignited these concerns among locals, who demand faster action from the traffic police.

What's next

The investigation will continue over the next few days, with police expected to file a chargesheet based on evidence and witness statements. The driver's legal status will depend on the findings, including whether negligence or rash driving is established. Meanwhile, the victim's family is yet to be formally notified, and officials are urging anyone with information to come forward.
