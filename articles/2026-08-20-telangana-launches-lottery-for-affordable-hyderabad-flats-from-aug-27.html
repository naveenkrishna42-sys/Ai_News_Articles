Telangana Launches Lottery for Affordable Hyderabad Flats from Aug 27

The Telangana government has announced the commencement of a lottery for its affordable housing scheme in Hyderabad, set to begin on 27 August. The draw for each round will be streamed live on the state’s official portal, allowing residents to watch the process in real time.

Affordable Housing in Telangana

Under the Telangana State Housing Development Corporation’s (TSHDC) affordable housing initiative, a number of flats are earmarked for families earning below a specified income threshold. The program aims to provide secure, cost‑effective accommodation to the middle‑income segment of Hyderabad’s growing population.

Lottery Launch and Process

Applications for the lottery open earlier in the month and will remain available until the deadline. Eligible applicants can register online, submit required documents, and receive a unique ticket number. The lottery will be conducted in a series of draws, each allocating a set of flats to the winning ticket holders.

Live Stream Draws

Each draw will be televised via the state’s website and selected local channels, ensuring transparency. Viewers can observe the selection of winning ticket numbers and the allocation of flats without any intermediaries. The live stream is expected to attract a large audience, as many families anticipate a chance to secure a home.

How to Apply and Eligibility

  • Applicants must be residents of Hyderabad and meet the income criteria set by TSHDC.
  • Registration requires uploading identity proofs, income certificates, and property details.
  • Once verified, applicants receive a lottery ticket number that remains valid until the draw.

Impact and Expectations

Housing authorities anticipate that the lottery will reduce the backlog of waiting lists and provide a fair mechanism for allocation. While the exact number of flats available in each draw has not been disclosed, officials have stated that the process aims to accommodate several thousand families over the coming months.

Stakeholders hope that the transparent draw will restore public confidence in the allocation of affordable housing and address the pressing demand for low‑cost homes in Hyderabad.

In the coming weeks, residents will monitor the website for draw results and updates on the next batch of available flats.
