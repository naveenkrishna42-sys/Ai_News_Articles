Fire breaks out at scrap warehouse in Bengaluru's RT Nagar

📅 2026-08-08 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • Fire reported at a scrap warehouse in RT Nagar, Bengaluru
  • Fire tenders dispatched to the location
  • Cause and casualty details not yet confirmed

Blaze at scrap yard

A fire broke out at a scrap warehouse in RT Nagar, Bengaluru, on Friday evening. The blaze was reported around 7 pm, and fire tenders were rushed to the spot. Visuals from the scene showed thick smoke billowing from the building as firefighters worked to contain the flames.

The warehouse, which stored scrap material, is located in a congested residential and commercial area. Local residents reported hearing loud noises, possibly from burning materials, but there were no immediate reports of injuries.

Firefighting operations underway

Multiple fire tenders were deployed to douse the flames. Firefighters faced challenges due to the narrow roads and the nature of the stored material, which can reignite easily. The operation continued for several hours as crews worked to prevent the fire from spreading to adjoining structures.

Police personnel cordoned off the area to facilitate the firefighting efforts. Traffic movement on roads near the warehouse was diverted, causing some inconvenience to commuters in the evening rush hour.

Cause yet to be ascertained

The cause of the fire is yet to be determined. Officials have not confirmed whether the blaze was accidental or sparked by an electrical short circuit, which is common in scrap storage facilities. An investigation is likely to be initiated once the fire is fully extinguished.

Scrap warehouses are known to be fire-prone due to the presence of flammable materials like paper, plastic, and metal shavings. In recent years, Bengaluru has witnessed several such incidents, prompting fire department advisories for strict adherence to safety norms.

No casualties reported

As of the latest update, there have been no reports of casualties or injuries. The warehouse owner's identity has not been disclosed, and it is unclear if the structure was insured. Damage assessment is expected to begin after the fire is brought under control.

Local residents expressed concerns about the recurring fire hazards in the area, calling for better enforcement of safety regulations. Some said the smoke could cause respiratory issues, especially for children and the elderly.

What to watch

Fire officials are expected to release an official statement once the operation concludes. The focus will now shift to determining the cause of the blaze and assessing the extent of damage. Residents and authorities will be watching for any safety audit of similar warehouses in the city.
