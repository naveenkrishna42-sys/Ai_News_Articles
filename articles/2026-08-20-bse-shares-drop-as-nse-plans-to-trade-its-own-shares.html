BSE shares drop as NSE plans to trade its own shares

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • BSE share price drops by nearly 2% after NSE plans to trade its shares
  • NSE aims to trade its shares under 'permitted-to-trade' route
  • Investors concerned as NSE seeks up to $55 billion valuation in record India IPO

BSE Share Price Drops After NSE Plans to Trade Its Shares

BSE share price fell by nearly 2% on Thursday after reports suggested the National Stock Exchange (NSE) plans to allow trading of its shares on its own platform. The drop comes as investors digest the news, which has raised concerns about the future of the two exchanges.

NSE Plans to Trade Its Shares Under 'Permitted-to-Trade' Route

According to reports, NSE is planning to allow trading of its shares on its own platform under the 'permitted-to-trade' route. This move is expected to give NSE more control over its own shares, which are currently traded on the BSE.

Investors Concerned as NSE Seeks Up to $55 Billion Valuation

Investors are also concerned about the recent reports that NSE is seeking a valuation of up to $55 billion in its upcoming record India IPO. This valuation, if achieved, would make NSE one of the most valuable exchanges in the world.

BSE Shares Slip Nearly 2%, Down 6% in a Week

BSE shares have slipped nearly 2% on Thursday, following the news. The shares have also fallen by about 6% in the past week, as investors digest the news about NSE's plans.

What Happens Next?

Investors will be closely watching the developments in the coming weeks, as the news about NSE's plans to trade its own shares could have significant implications for the future of the two exchanges. The upcoming record India IPO by NSE could also provide more clarity on the situation.
