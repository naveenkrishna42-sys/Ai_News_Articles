POCO X8 Series Specs Leaked Ahead of Launch

POCO, a sub-brand of Xiaomi, has leaked more specifications for its upcoming X8 and X8 Power 5G smartphones ahead of their September 4 launch. The details reveal a premium design, powerful performance, and impressive battery life, positioning the devices as strong contenders in the mid-range segment.

Display and Design

The POCO X8 5G boasts a 6.83-inch 1.5K resolution AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate, offering vibrant colors and smooth visuals. The X8 Power also features a 6.83-inch AMOLED display but with a slightly lower resolution and refresh rate, focusing more on affordability. Both models come with IP69K ratings, indicating excellent dust and water resistance.

Performance and Battery

The X8 Power stands out with its massive 9000mAh battery, which supports 180W fast charging. This makes it a strong candidate for users who prioritize battery life and quick charging. The X8, on the other hand, is expected to come with a slightly smaller battery but still offers robust performance with its 5G capabilities and high refresh rate display.

Pricing and Availability

Leaked price ranges suggest the POCO X8 5G will start around ₹25,000, while the X8 Power will be available for around ₹20,000. These price points position the devices as competitive options in the mid-range market, offering a balance between performance and cost.

Expert Analysis

Analysts at TIVRA News note that the leaked specifications indicate POCO is targeting both performance enthusiasts and budget-conscious users. The X8 Power's large battery and fast charging capability make it an attractive option for those who need extended battery life, while the X8's high refresh rate display and 5G support cater to users who want a premium experience without breaking the bank.
