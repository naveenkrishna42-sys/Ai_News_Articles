Green Skills & Applied AI Centre in Hyderabad to Start by August

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Centre of Excellence for Green Skills and Applied AI in Hyderabad to be operational by August.
  • Facility to focus on training in renewable energy technologies and artificial intelligence applications.
  • Part of broader government initiative to bridge skill gaps in emerging sectors.
  • Will offer courses, workshops, and certification programs.
  • Aims to create a workforce ready for green economy and AI-driven industries.

Hyderabad’s New Skill Hub Nears Launch

The Green Skills & Applied AI Centre of Excellence (CoE) in Hyderabad is set to become operational by August this year. Officials confirmed the timeline on Thursday, marking a key milestone for the city’s aspirations in emerging technology sectors.

The centre, located at a prominent facility in the city, will focus on two critical areas: renewable energy technologies and artificial intelligence applications. It is designed to address the growing demand for skilled professionals in green jobs and AI-driven industries.

What the Centre Will Offer

The CoE will provide hands-on training, workshops, and certification programs. Course modules will cover solar energy systems, wind power management, smart grid technologies, and AI tools for predictive maintenance and resource optimization.

Industry partners have already signed on to co-develop curriculum and provide guest lectures. The centre is expected to train several hundred individuals annually, with a focus on recent graduates and mid-career professionals looking to upskill.

Government Push for Future-Ready Workforce

The initiative is part of a broader government strategy to bridge skill gaps in sunrise sectors. The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship has backed the project, alongside state-level agencies in Telangana.

Officials say the centre will act as a model for similar facilities across the country. The goal is to create a pipeline of workers who can drive India’s transition to a green economy and adopt AI technologies in manufacturing, energy, and services.

Industry and Academic Tie-Ups

The CoE will collaborate with local engineering colleges and polytechnics. Students from partner institutions will get access to lab facilities and placement support.

Private companies in renewable energy and AI sectors have pledged to offer internships and job placements. This direct industry linkage is expected to improve employment outcomes for trainees.

What to Watch

The centre’s formal inauguration is expected in late July, with the first batch of trainees starting in August. The success of this model could lead to similar centres in other cities, depending on demand and funding.
