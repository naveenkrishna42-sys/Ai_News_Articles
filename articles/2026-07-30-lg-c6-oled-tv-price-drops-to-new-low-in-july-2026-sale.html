LG C6 OLED TV price drops to new low in July 2026 sale

📅 2026-07-30 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • LG C6 OLED TV price has reached a new low in July 2026.
  • Discounts of up to $900 are available on C6 and G6 models.
  • The C6 is competing closely with Sony's Bravia 7 II RGB TV.
  • Deals are live at Best Buy and other retailers.

If you've been waiting to buy a new OLED TV, the timing might finally be right. LG's 2026 mid-range C6 OLED TV has dropped to its lowest price yet during a July sale event that is drawing comparisons to Black Friday. The discount is part of a broader promotion that includes the premium G6 series and wallpaper models.

What is the LG C6 OLED TV?

The LG C6 is the company's mid-range OLED for 2026. It sits below the flagship G6 but delivers strong picture quality, deep blacks, and vibrant colours that OLED panels are known for. Early reviews from tech outlets have called it one of the best TVs tested this year.

In a side-by-side comparison with Sony's Bravia 7 II RGB TV, the C6 held its ground, with some reviewers noting the battle between the two was closer than expected. The C6 offers HDR support, a smooth refresh rate, and LG's latest smart TV interface.

How low did the price go?

The exact discount varies by model and retailer. However, deals include up to $900 off select C6 and G6 models. At Best Buy, the 65-inch C6 is available for $899, nearly half its original price. The sale is part of what some are calling LG's Black Friday in July event.

Retailers like Amazon and other electronics stores are also offering similar discounts. The 55-inch model and smaller sizes have seen price cuts as well, though the deepest discounts are on larger screen sizes. The 77-inch C6, for example, is seeing a significant reduction.

How does it compare to competitors?

The C6's main rival this year is Sony's Bravia 7 II, which uses RGB LED technology. Reviewers who have tested both say the C6 holds its own in most scenarios, especially in dark rooms where OLED excels. The Sony set offers higher peak brightness, but the LG wins on contrast and black levels.

For buyers who prioritise cinematic picture quality and gaming performance, the C6 remains a strong choice. It supports HDMI 2.1 features like variable refresh rate and low latency mode, making it suitable for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X users.

What should buyers watch for?

These July discounts are time-limited. Shoppers should compare prices across multiple retailers before purchasing. Some deals require a membership or are limited to in-store pickup. Extended warranties and installation services may add to the total cost.

It is also worth checking if the price includes the stand or wall mount. Some promotions are for the TV panel only. Returns and exchange policies vary by retailer, so buyers are advised to read the fine print.

The sale event is expected to run through the end of July. Once the stock clears, prices may return to normal levels. Those interested in the LG C6 should act quickly to secure the current low price. The next major discount window might not come until the Black Friday season in November.
