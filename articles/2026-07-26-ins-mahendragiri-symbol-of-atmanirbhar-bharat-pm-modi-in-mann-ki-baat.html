INS Mahendragiri symbol of Atmanirbhar Bharat: PM Modi in Mann Ki Baat

📅 2026-07-26 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • PM Modi called INS Mahendragiri a symbol of Atmanirbhar Bharat in his Mann Ki Baat address.
  • He highlighted India's emergence as a trusted global defence partner.
  • The 136th episode focused on achievements in defence, space, and indigenous technology.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi, in the 136th episode of his monthly radio programme Mann Ki Baat on Sunday, described the newly-commissioned INS Mahendragiri as a 'symbol of Atmanirbhar Bharat'. The address, aired across All India Radio and Doordarshan, focused on India's growing capabilities in defence, space, and indigenous technology.

INS Mahendragiri: A Stealth Frigate Built in India

The INS Mahendragiri is the latest stealth frigate to be inducted into the Indian Navy. Built indigenously, it represents a significant leap in India's naval shipbuilding capabilities. PM Modi noted that the warship is not just a vessel but a testament to the skill and vision of Indian engineers and shipyard workers.

The Prime Minister stressed that such platforms are crucial for India's maritime security and self-reliance. He said the ship's commissioning sends a clear message about the nation's commitment to reducing dependence on foreign defence equipment.

India as a Trusted Global Defence Partner

During the broadcast, PM Modi asserted that India is now being recognised as a trusted global defence partner. He pointed to growing defence exports and collaborations with friendly nations as evidence of this shift. The Prime Minister did not name specific countries but referenced the increasing interest in Indian-made defence systems.

This narrative aligns with the government's sustained push to position India as a manufacturing hub for defence hardware. Officials have previously noted that the defence export sector has seen a multi-fold increase in the last decade, though exact figures were not discussed in this episode.

Scaling New Heights in Space and Technology

Beyond defence, the Prime Minister also highlighted India's strides in space technology. He mentioned recent successes of the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), including satellite launches and interplanetary missions. PM Modi described these achievements as proof of the nation's scientific prowess.

Indigenous technology, he argued, is now driving progress across sectors. From digital payments to space exploration, India is building solutions that are both affordable and reliable. He urged citizens to take pride in these homegrown innovations.

Mann Ki Baat: A Platform for National Pride

The 136th episode continued the tradition of Mann Ki Baat as a platform for the Prime Minister to connect directly with the public. The programme, which began in 2014, typically covers a mix of social issues, national achievements, and calls to action. Sunday's address was no different, weaving together themes of self-reliance and national security.

Listeners from across the country tuned in, with many taking to social media to share their reactions. The broadcast also featured letters and messages from citizens, a staple of the show's interactive format.

Looking ahead, the government is expected to announce further steps to boost indigenous manufacturing in the defence sector. The coming months may see new orders for Indian shipyards and aerospace firms as the focus on Atmanirbhar Bharat deepens.
