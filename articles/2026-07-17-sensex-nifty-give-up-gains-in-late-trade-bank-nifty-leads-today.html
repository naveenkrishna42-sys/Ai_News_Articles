Sensex, Nifty give up gains in late trade; Bank Nifty leads today

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Markets Original source ↗
Key points
  • Sensex and Nifty surrendered early gains to close flat after a volatile session
  • Bank Nifty emerged as the top sectoral gainer, leading the day's advance
  • IT and consumer durables stocks supported the market while broader indices were mixed

Equity benchmarks Sensex and Nifty ended flat on Wednesday, July 15, after a volatile session that saw them give up most of their intraday gains. The Bank Nifty led sectoral gains, while IT and consumer durables stocks provided support.

Markets give up early momentum

The BSE Sensex and NSE Nifty 50 both started the day on a strong note, tracking positive global cues. However, selling pressure in the final hour of trade erased most of the gains.

The Nifty 50 managed to hold the 24,050 level, ending with a marginal gain. The Sensex also closed flat, reflecting the tug-of-war between bulls and bears.

Traders said profit booking at higher levels and caution ahead of key global events weighed on sentiment.

Bank Nifty leads; IT, consumer durables shine

Among sectoral indices, the Bank Nifty was the standout performer, gaining sharply through the day. Private sector and PSU banks both saw buying interest.

The Nifty IT index also ended higher, with stocks like Wipro gaining 2% ahead of its quarterly results. Consumer durables stocks too saw demand, adding to the positive bias.

However, metal and pharma indices lagged, capping the broader market's upside.

Broader market mixed; volatility persists

The broader market presented a mixed picture. The BSE midcap index ended slightly higher, while the smallcap index was flat to negative.

Market breadth was marginally positive, with more stocks advancing than declining on the BSE. Analysts pointed to elevated volatility as the key theme of the session.

“The market is in a consolidation phase after the recent run-up. Investors are awaiting fresh triggers from corporate earnings and global central bank policies,” said a dealer at a domestic brokerage.

What to watch next

Investors will now focus on upcoming quarterly results from IT majors and cues from global markets. The direction of foreign fund flows and crude oil prices will also influence near-term trends.

Traders expect the Nifty to remain rangebound in the short term, with support at 23,800 and resistance near 24,300.
