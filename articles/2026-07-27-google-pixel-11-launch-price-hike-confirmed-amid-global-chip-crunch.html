Google Pixel 11 Launch: Price Hike Confirmed Amid Global Chip Crunch

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Google confirms Pixel 11 price increase due to global memory chip crunch.
  • Company announces dedicated effort to make Android more RAM-efficient.
  • Leaked renders reveal all color options for the Pixel 11 lineup.

Google has officially confirmed that the upcoming Pixel 11 series will carry a higher price tag than its predecessor. The company cited a global memory chip shortage as the primary reason for the increase.

The announcement comes just weeks before the expected launch event, fueling speculation about how much more consumers will have to pay. Reports from multiple outlets, including 9to5Google and Neowin, confirm the price hike will apply across the entire Pixel lineup.

Why the Price Hike?

Google blames the ongoing global memory chip crunch for the price increase. The shortage has driven up production costs for smartphone manufacturers worldwide. The company has not disclosed the exact percentage of the price rise. Analysts expect the flagship Pixel 11 model to cost significantly more than the Pixel 10.

This is not the first time Google has raised Pixel prices. The company has incrementally increased costs over recent generations, citing component costs and feature upgrades.

Android RAM Optimization Efforts

Alongside the price confirmation, Google revealed a 'dedicated' effort to make Android more RAM-efficient. The company aims to improve memory management to ensure smoother performance on devices with less RAM. This could help offset the impact of higher hardware costs for some users.

The move is seen as a response to growing competition from Apple and Chinese brands, which have focused on optimizing software for lower-end hardware. Google has not provided a timeline for when these improvements will roll out.

Pixel 11 Design and Color Options

Leaked renders from GSMArena show every color option for the Pixel 11 lineup. The images reveal a familiar design language, with minor refinements to the camera bar and display bezels. Color options include a new sage green and a deep coral shade, alongside classic black and white variants.

The leaks suggest Google is sticking with a dual-camera system on the standard model and a triple-camera setup on the Pro variant. The display sizes are expected to remain similar to the Pixel 10 lineup.

What to Expect at the Launch Event

The official launch event is expected to take place in October. Google will likely showcase the Pixel 11's camera improvements, AI features, and the new Tensor chip. The company is also expected to announce the Pixel Watch 3 and other accessories.

With the price increase confirmed, the Pixel 11 will need to deliver significant upgrades to justify the higher cost. The RAM efficiency push suggests Google is aware of the value-conscious segment and is trying to balance performance with affordability.

Industry watchers will be closely watching the event for detailed pricing and availability in India, where Google has been expanding its Pixel presence.
