Hyderabad man accused of wife's murder found dead near her grave

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Accused in wife's murder case discovered dead near her grave in Hyderabad.
  • Police suspect suicide; no suicide note has been found yet.
  • Investigation underway to determine the exact cause of death.

In a grim turn of events in Hyderabad, a man who was accused of murdering his wife was found dead near her grave on Thursday morning. The incident has sent shockwaves through the local community and left police piecing together a chain of events that ended in tragedy.

Body found at cemetery

The man, whose identity has not been officially disclosed pending family notification, was discovered by a caretaker at a cemetery on the outskirts of the city. The spot was close to the grave of his wife, who had died under suspicious circumstances several months ago.

Police arrived at the scene after the caretaker raised an alarm. Preliminary examination suggests the man died of a self-inflicted injury, though officials have not yet confirmed the exact cause. No suicide note has been recovered so far.

A case that began with suspicion

The man had been arrested earlier this year in connection with his wife's death. Her family had lodged a complaint alleging foul play, and the police had registered a murder case against him. He was out on bail at the time of his death, awaiting trial.

According to sources familiar with the investigation, the couple had marital discord, and neighbours often reported heated arguments. However, the man had consistently maintained his innocence, claiming his wife died of a sudden illness.

Police investigation underway

A team of forensic experts has been deployed to the cemetery to gather evidence. The local police have registered a case of suspicious death and are examining the man's call records and social media activity to understand his state of mind in the days leading up to the incident.

"We are looking into all angles," said a senior police officer, speaking on condition of anonymity. "The possibility of suicide appears strong, but we will wait for the post-mortem report before concluding anything."

The officer added that the man's family has been informed, and they are cooperating with the investigation. The cemetery has been cordoned off, and security has been tightened to prevent any untoward incident.

Community in shock

Residents of the area where the couple lived expressed disbelief at the development. "We never thought it would come to this," said a neighbour, who did not wish to be named. "He seemed tense after the case was filed, but we didn't expect him to take such a step."

The case has also reignited discussions about the pressures faced by those accused in criminal cases, even before a verdict is delivered. Legal experts point out that while the justice system presumes innocence until proven guilty, the social and emotional toll on the accused can be immense.

What lies ahead

The post-mortem report is expected to be released within a few days, which will likely clarify the cause of death. Meanwhile, the police are also reviewing the original murder case to see if any new evidence emerges from this tragic incident.

As the investigation continues, the focus remains on providing closure to two families who have lost a loved one in circumstances that are both heartbreaking and complex. The coming weeks will determine whether this case closes with more questions than answers.
