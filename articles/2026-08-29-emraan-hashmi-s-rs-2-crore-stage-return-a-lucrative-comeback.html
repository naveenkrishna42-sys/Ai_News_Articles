Hashmi's Rs 2 Crore Stage Comeback

Actor Emraan Hashmi, known for his roles in films like 'Awarapan' and 'Murder', is all set to make a grand return to the stage after 19 years. The actor, who started his career on the small screen, has reportedly commanded a fee of Rs 2 crore for two upcoming Bollywood projects.

Why the High Price Tag?

Hashmi's staggering fee can be attributed to his past success, brand value, and the current dearth of big-ticket stage talents in Bollywood. Experts suggest that his involvement can significantly boost the box office prospects of these projects.

Impact on Theatre Industry

Theater industry insiders believe that Hashmi's comeback could also revitalize the struggling stage industry by drawing larger crowds and encouraging producers to invest in bigger productions.
