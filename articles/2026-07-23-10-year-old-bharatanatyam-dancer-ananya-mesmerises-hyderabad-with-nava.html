10-year-old Bharatanatyam dancer Ananya mesmerises Hyderabad with 'Navaratnam'

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Hyderabad Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ananya, a 10-year-old Bharatanatyam dancer, performed 'Navaratnam' in Hyderabad.
  • The performance featured nine distinct classical dance forms.
  • The audience and critics praised her technical skill and emotional depth.

A child prodigy takes the stage

Hyderabad witnessed an extraordinary evening of classical dance as 10-year-old Ananya presented her solo performance, 'Navaratnam'. The event, held at a packed auditorium in the city, left the audience spellbound.

Ananya, a student of renowned guru Smt. Lakshmi Narasimha, has been training in Bharatanatyam for over five years. Her 'Navaratnam'—a tribute to nine classical dance styles—showcased not just Bharatanatyam but also elements of Kuchipudi, Odissi, Kathak, and others.

Technical brilliance meets emotional depth

The young dancer's footwork was precise, her expressions nuanced. For a child her age, the ability to convey complex emotions through abhinaya is rare. Critics in the audience noted her seamless transitions between styles.

Each of the nine segments in 'Navaratnam' represented a different gem, from ruby to diamond, symbolising virtues like courage and wisdom. Ananya's portrayal of these themes drew repeated applause.

A packed house and glowing reviews

The venue, Ravindra Bharathi, was filled to capacity. Parents, dance enthusiasts, and even senior artists attended. Many were visibly moved by the performance.

“I have seen many young dancers, but Ananya's control and maturity are exceptional,” said K. Sridhar, a retired dance teacher who was in the audience. “This is not just practice; this is born talent.”

What makes 'Navaratnam' special

The choreography, originally designed by Ananya's guru, required her to master distinct rhythms and mudras for each style. The nine-part structure is demanding even for seasoned performers.

Ananya dedicated the performance to her grandmother, who introduced her to Bharatanatyam at age five. The emotional connect was evident in her closing piece, a thillana in ragam Kalyani.

With this show, Ananya has joined the ranks of young classical dancers who are keeping ancient traditions alive in modern India. Her journey, however, has just begun.

She is already preparing for her next performance, a full-length solo, scheduled for next year. The city will be watching.
