Motorola Moto G87 Review: Affordable Specs, Real Tradeoffs

Key points
  • 120Hz display is a standout feature in the budget segment
  • Performance is adequate for basic tasks but not for gaming
  • Battery life is excellent, but charging speed lags behind competitors

Motorola has just launched the Moto G87, a new affordable smartphone, making it a timely pick for budget buyers. This review assesses whether its specifications deliver real value or just paper promise.

What reviewers generally say: Initial critical response is mixed, with reviewers praising the smooth display and clean software but noting average performance and camera limitations for the price.

Pros
  • 120Hz refresh rate is rare at this price point, making scrolling noticeably smoother
  • Near-stock Android experience with minimal bloatware and timely security patches expected
  • Large 5000mAh battery easily lasts a full day of moderate use
Cons
  • Unisoc T612 chipset struggles with demanding games and heavy multitasking, causing lag
  • Camera quality is mediocre in low light, with soft details and slow autofocus
  • 18W charging is slow for a 5000mAh battery, taking over two hours for a full charge

The Motorola Moto G87 is a sensible choice for budget-conscious users who prioritize a smooth display and clean software over raw performance. If you primarily use your phone for messaging, browsing, and light social media, this device will serve you well for under ₹12,000.

However, if you are a mobile gamer, a photography enthusiast, or someone who hates waiting for charges, the G87's weak chipset, unimpressive camera, and sluggish charging will frustrate you. Consider spending a bit more for a phone with a Snapdragon 4-series chipset or a faster charging solution.
