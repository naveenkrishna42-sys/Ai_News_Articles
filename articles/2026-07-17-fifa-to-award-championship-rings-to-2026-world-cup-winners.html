FIFA to award championship rings to 2026 World Cup winners

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • FIFA will award championship rings to the winners of the 2026 World Cup final.
  • This is the first time in the tournament's 96-year history that such rings will be given.
  • US President Donald Trump is confirmed to attend the final between Spain and Argentina.

FIFA has announced a historic first for the 2026 World Cup: the tournament's winners will receive championship rings, in addition to the traditional gold trophy. The move marks a significant departure from the sport's century-old customs, borrowing a page from American sports leagues like the NFL and NBA.

Rings for the champions

For the first time in 96 years, the World Cup winners will be presented with bespoke rings. The design and value of the rings have not yet been disclosed by FIFA, but the federation confirmed they will be crafted to mark the occasion.

The rings will be awarded alongside the iconic 18-carat gold trophy, which has been lifted by champions since 1930. This dual-award system is a first for international football's premier event.

Trump to attend final

Adding to the spectacle, US President Donald Trump has confirmed he will attend the World Cup final. The match, scheduled to be played at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey, will see Spain take on Argentina.

Presidential attendance at a World Cup final is rare. Trump's presence underscores the tournament's significance as a major international event hosted jointly by the United States, Canada, and Mexico.

Reactions and comparisons

The decision to introduce rings has drawn comparisons to American sports culture. In the NFL, Super Bowl winners receive rings, while the NBA champions are also presented with them. FIFA's move is seen as an effort to blend traditions and create a new symbol of victory.

Critics have questioned whether rings will dilute the importance of the trophy. However, FIFA officials believe the rings will serve as a lasting personal memento for players. The trophy, they insist, remains the ultimate prize.

Fans attending the final may also see a change in the awards ceremony. The presentation order and protocol are still being finalised. What is clear is that both the rings and the trophy will be presented on the pitch after the final whistle.

The 2026 World Cup final is set to be a landmark event. With a new award and high-profile attendance, the match between Spain and Argentina already promises to be one of the most talked-about finals in history.
