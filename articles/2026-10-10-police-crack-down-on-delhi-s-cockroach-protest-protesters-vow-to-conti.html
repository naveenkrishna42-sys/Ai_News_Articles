Background of the Cockroach Protest

The so‑called Cockroach protest emerged in Delhi on 10 October 2026 as a symbolic demonstration against what participants describe as growing authoritarian tendencies and perceived threats to electoral integrity. Demonstrators adopted the cockroach emblem to convey resilience, suggesting that dissent persists despite attempts to suppress it. The gathering took place near Connaught Place, a long‑standing venue for public assemblies in the capital.

Police Action and Detentions

Detention of CJP Leaders

During the demonstration, police moved in to disperse the crowd and detained several leaders of the Citizens’ Justice Party (CJP). Eyewitness accounts indicate that officers escorted the individuals to nearby police stations for questioning. Authorities stated that the action was taken to maintain public order and to investigate possible violations of public‑order laws.
