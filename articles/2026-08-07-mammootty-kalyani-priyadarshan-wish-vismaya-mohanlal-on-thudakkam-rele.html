Mammootty, Kalyani Priyadarshan wish Vismaya Mohanlal on 'Thudakkam' release

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mammootty publicly wished Vismaya Mohanlal on her acting debut with 'Thudakkam'.
  • Kalyani Priyadarshan expressed support, saying 'Always rooting for you'.
  • The film marks Vismaya's entry into Malayalam cinema and has received mixed reviews.
  • Co-writer Akhil Krishna highlighted the father-daughter emotional bond as the film's core.

The Malayalam film fraternity rallied around debutante Vismaya Mohanlal as her maiden outing, 'Thudakkam', hit theatres. Among the early well-wishers were two of the industry's most recognisable names. Mammootty took a moment to extend his good wishes to the young actor, while filmmaker Kalyani Priyadarshan offered a public note of encouragement.

Kalyani Priyadarshan's message was simple but warm. 'Always rooting for you,' she wrote, signalling the kind of support that often greets a newcomer in the close-knit Malayalam film community. The gesture did not go unnoticed by fans, who quickly circulated the exchanges on social media.

A debut under the spotlight

Vismaya Mohanlal is not just any newcomer. As the daughter of Mohanlal, one of Malayalam cinema's most enduring superstars, her entry into acting was always going to attract scrutiny. 'Thudakkam' places her in a martial-arts-driven revenge thriller, a genre that demands physical prowess as much as emotional range.

Reviews so far have been a mixed bag. The Hindu noted that her martial arts skills inject life into an otherwise typical revenge narrative. The News Minute, however, was more critical, observing that the film 'undermines its own heroine' despite her efforts. The Times of India's review echoed similar sentiments, praising her on-screen presence while questioning the script's originality.

The core of the story

According to co-writer Akhil Krishna, the film's emotional anchor lies elsewhere. He described the father-daughter bond as the story's true core, a detail that adds depth to what might otherwise be a straightforward action drama. This relationship, he suggested, is what gives the narrative its heart.

For a debutant, the pressure of living up to a legendary surname is considerable. Yet Vismaya appears to have approached the role with a level of commitment that has drawn at least some critical acknowledgment. Whether the film's commercial performance matches its ambition remains to be seen.

Industry support matters

The public endorsements from Mammootty and Kalyani Priyadarshan carry weight in the industry. Such gestures often help a new actor find footing in a competitive space. They also signal that Vismaya's arrival is being welcomed by veterans, not merely as Mohanlal's daughter but as an artist in her own right.

As 'Thudakkam' continues its theatrical run, early audience response will determine its fate. The film's team, including debutant director, will be watching the numbers closely. For Vismaya, the journey has just begun, and the industry's backing provides a sturdy launchpad.

What remains to be seen is how audiences embrace the film in the coming weeks, and whether Vismaya's next project will build on the momentum. For now, the debutante can take heart in knowing that some of Malayalam cinema's biggest names are firmly in her corner.
