Apple India Sales Cross $10 Billion as Retail Network Expands

📅 2026-08-05 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Apple's India sales crossed $10 billion for the first time, surpassing half of Nifty50 companies' annual revenue.
  • The growth is driven by a widening retail presence beyond iPhones, including Macs, iPads, and wearables.
  • Despite revenue growth, Apple faces its first iPhone shipment decline in India after years of record growth.
  • The retail expansion marks a strategic shift as Apple deepens its manufacturing and sales footprint in India.

Apple's annual revenue from India has crossed the $10 billion mark for the first time, a milestone that underscores the company's aggressive retail expansion in one of the world's fastest-growing smartphone markets. According to reports from Bloomberg and CNBC TV18, the figure surpasses the annual sales of half the companies in the Nifty50 index, a striking metric for a market that was once a footnote in Apple's global earnings.

The achievement comes as Apple's India story evolves beyond iPhones. The company has been steadily widening its product portfolio in the country, with Macs, iPads, and wearables gaining traction among Indian consumers. This diversification, coupled with a growing network of company-owned stores and a robust online presence, has helped Apple scale new heights in a price-sensitive market.

Retail Push Drives Growth

Apple's retail strategy in India has been a key driver of this surge. The company opened its first physical stores in Mumbai and Delhi in 2023, and has since expanded its footprint to more cities. These stores offer a premium experience that online channels cannot replicate, attracting a segment of buyers willing to spend on high-end devices.

The expansion is not just about selling products. Apple has also ramped up local manufacturing through partners like Foxconn and Wistron, producing iPhones for both domestic sale and export. This aligns with India's production-linked incentive (PLI) scheme, which has made the country a more attractive hub for global tech firms.

iPhone Shipments Face a Hiccup

However, the celebratory tone is tempered by a note of caution. Moneycontrol reports that Apple is set for its first iPhone shipment decline in India after years of record growth. This could be attributed to a maturing market, longer upgrade cycles, or increased competition from Chinese brands like Xiaomi and Vivo, which dominate the budget and mid-range segments.

Analysts suggest that the decline in iPhone shipments might be a temporary blip. Apple's focus on services and accessories could help offset any hardware slowdown. The company's services revenue in India has been growing steadily, driven by Apple Music, iCloud, and the App Store ecosystem.

A Strategic Pivot

The Times of India noted that Apple's India story is no longer just about iPhones. The next phase has started, with the company investing heavily in retail experience, local assembly, and a broader product mix. This pivot is strategic, as India's smartphone market is expected to grow further, and Apple aims to capture a larger share of the premium segment.

Apple's revenue crossing $10 billion in India is a testament to its long-term commitment. Yet, the potential iPhone shipment decline serves as a reminder that even market leaders face headwinds. The company's ability to adapt its strategy will be crucial in sustaining this growth trajectory.

While officials have not yet confirmed specific figures for the coming quarters, industry watchers will be keenly observing whether Apple can reverse the shipment trend. The upcoming festive season in India, typically a peak sales period, could provide the boost needed to rebound.

As Apple deepens its roots in India, the focus will shift to how it balances premium positioning with local market realities. The next few quarters will reveal if this $10 billion milestone is a peak or a stepping stone to even greater gains.
