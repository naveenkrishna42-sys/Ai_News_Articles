Best credit cards for digital nomads & remote workers

Digital nomads and remote workers often need credit cards that offer international transaction benefits and dedicated customer support. Here are some of the best credit cards for them in India.

1. ICICI Bank Visa Signature Credit Card

This card offers a 100% foreign transaction fee waiver, which means you can spend abroad without worrying about additional charges. It also provides access to over 1,200 international lounges worldwide. The card has no annual fee and offers a welcome bonus of 10,000 reward points.

2. HDFC Bank Diners Club Black Credit Card

This card also offers a 100% foreign transaction fee waiver and access to over 1,100 international lounges. It has a no annual fee policy and offers a welcome bonus of 15,000 reward points. The card also provides dedicated customer support for remote workers.

3. SBI Card Premier Visa Credit Card

This card offers a 100% foreign transaction fee waiver and access to over 1,000 international lounges. It has a no annual fee policy and offers a welcome bonus of 12,000 reward points. The card also provides travel insurance and dedicated customer support for remote workers.

Features & Benefits

All the above credit cards offer a 100% foreign transaction fee waiver, which means you can spend abroad without worrying about additional charges. They also provide access to international lounges, which can be a great way to relax and work in a comfortable environment.

These cards also offer dedicated customer support for remote workers, which can be very helpful if you need assistance while traveling or working abroad.

Joining & Annual Fees

All the above credit cards have a no annual fee policy, which means you won't have to pay any additional charges every year. However, you may have to pay a joining fee, which ranges from Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 10,000.

Reward Rates

The reward rates for these cards vary, but they generally offer a good return on your spending. The ICICI Bank Visa Signature Credit Card offers a reward rate of 1.5 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on international transactions. The HDFC Bank Diners Club Black Credit Card offers a reward rate of 2 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on international transactions. The SBI Card Premier Visa Credit Card offers a reward rate of 1.75 reward points per Rs. 100 spent on international transactions.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for these credit cards, you need to have a good credit score and a steady income. You also need to be a digital nomad or a remote worker to avail the benefits of these cards.

How to Apply

You can apply for these credit cards online by visiting the official websites of the respective banks. You will need to provide your personal and financial details, and your application will be processed within a few days.

Conclusion

Digital nomads and remote workers can benefit from these credit cards as they offer international transaction benefits and dedicated customer support. The ICICI Bank Visa Signature Credit Card, HDFC Bank Diners Club Black Credit Card, and SBI Card Premier Visa Credit Card are some of the best options available in India.
