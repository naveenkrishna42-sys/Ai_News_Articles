Vijay's 'Jana Nayagan' release forces Tamil films to reconsider plans

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jana Nayagan had advance bookings for 6 am shows in Bengaluru and joined an elite list with 8,000 screens.
  • CBFC requested changes to an Ambedkar-Tamil Manila Congress scene, which the producer confirmed.
  • A distribution dispute led Bengaluru's Victory Cinema to decline screenings.
  • The film underwent major modifications after an online leak; a new theatrical version is now in cinemas.

Tamil cinema is recalibrating its release calendar as Vijay's latest political drama, 'Jana Nayagan', steamrolls into theatres. The film's massive advance bookings and unprecedented screen count of 8,000 have forced several other Tamil releases to postpone their dates. Industry insiders say the rush to avoid a direct clash with the Vijay juggernaut has left distributors scrambling.

Advance booking frenzy and screen count record

Early morning shows in Bengaluru, starting as early as 6 am, have seen an overwhelming response. Fans lined up outside multiplexes since midnight, with many theatres reporting housefull boards within hours of booking opening. This enthusiasm places 'Jana Nayagan' in an elite league, alongside blockbusters like Pushpa 2 and Jawan.

The film's release across 8,000 screens nationwide marks a new benchmark for Tamil cinema. Exhibitors in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh have allocated maximum shows, pushing smaller films to the margins. Trade analysts note that this scale is usually reserved for pan-Indian releases from Bollywood.

CBFC edits and the Ambedkar-TMK scene controversy

The Central Board of Film Certification (CBFC) sought modifications in a specific scene involving Dr. B.R. Ambedkar and the Tamil Manila Congress (TMK). The producer confirmed the changes, stating that the censor board requested a toned-down portrayal to avoid potential political backlash.

“We complied with the board's suggestions to ensure a smooth release,” the producer told reporters. “The scene now conveys the intended message without any direct references that could be misconstrued.” The edits have not dampened audience enthusiasm, with early reviews calling the film a hard-hitting political commentary.

Bengaluru distribution dispute and Victory Cinema standoff

Not all exhibitors are on board. Bengaluru's iconic Victory Cinema has declined to screen 'Jana Nayagan' amid a distribution dispute. Sources say the disagreement stems from revenue-sharing terms, with the theatre demanding a higher cut due to its prime location and loyal audience base.

The standoff highlights the growing friction between producers and single-screen owners. While multiplex chains have accepted the terms, independent theatres like Victory are pushing back. Efforts to broker a compromise have so far failed, leaving the theatre dark during the film's opening weekend.

Online leak forces major modifications

Before its theatrical release, a leaked version of 'Jana Nayagan' circulated online, prompting the makers to make significant changes. The producer confirmed that a new theatrical version was prepared, incorporating alternate scenes and re-shot sequences to surprise audiences who may have seen the pirated copy.

“We had to act fast to protect the film's box office,” a production source said. “The leaked version was an early cut, so we re-edited key portions. Anyone who watched the leak is in for a different experience in theatres.” The move has been praised by exhibitors as a creative deterrent against piracy.

With 'Jana Nayagan' dominating headlines and screens, other Tamil films are now eyeing release windows in August or September. Distributors are watching the film's first-weekend numbers closely to decide their next move. For now, all eyes are on whether Victory Cinema and the producers can resolve their dispute before the film's second week.
