Joe Root’s unbeaten 99 leads England to series-levelling win over India in 2nd ODI

📅 2026-07-16 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Joe Root remained 99 not out as England chased down India’s total in Cardiff
  • Gus Atkinson contributed with both bat and ball in the series-levelling victory
  • Three-match ODI series now tied 1-1 with the decider to be played in Manchester

Root anchors chase after England wobble

Joe Root played a measured knock of 99 not out to steer England to a series-levelling win over India in the second ODI in Cardiff on Thursday. The hosts chased down their target with more than four overs to spare, squaring the three-match series 1-1.

Root’s innings was the backbone of England’s reply after India had posted a competitive total. He found support from Gus Atkinson, who chipped in with a quickfire 35 and later took two wickets to cap an all-round display.

India set a stiff target after solid start

India’s batting unit put up a fight after being asked to bat first. Openers gave the side a brisk start, and the middle order ensured the scoreboard kept moving. However, regular wickets in the middle overs prevented them from accelerating in the death overs.

England’s bowlers, led by Atkinson and a disciplined attack, pulled things back just when India looked set for a total beyond 300. The visitors finished at 280 for 7 in their 50 overs, a total that felt par on a good Cardiff pitch.

Root’s calm head guides chase

England’s reply began steadily, but they lost wickets at regular intervals. Root held one end firm, rotating strike and punishing loose deliveries. When England needed 50 runs off the last 10 overs, he accelerated without taking risks.

Atkinson’s cameo reduced the pressure, and Root brought the team to the brink of victory. He needed a single to reach his hundred off the final ball, but Atkinson was run out going for the second run. Root remained stranded on 99, but England had already won.

Series now set for Manchester decider

With the series tied 1-1, both teams will head to Manchester for the third and final ODI. England will take confidence from a composed chase, while India will look to regroup after letting a promising position slip.

The decider is scheduled for Sunday, and the winner will take the series. Both sides have shown they can win under pressure, and the final match promises a fitting climax to a tight contest.
