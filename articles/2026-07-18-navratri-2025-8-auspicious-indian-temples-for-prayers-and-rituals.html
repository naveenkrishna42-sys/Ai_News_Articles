Navratri 2025: 8 auspicious Indian temples for prayers and rituals

📅 2026-07-18 📂 Religion Original source ↗
Key points
  • Navratri 2025 sees millions visiting temples dedicated to the goddess Durga across India.
  • Eight temples are highlighted for their unique rituals and historical significance during the festival.
  • The list includes sites like Vaishno Devi, Kamakhya, and Meenakshi Amman Temple.

Navratri, the nine-night festival honouring the goddess Durga, draws millions of devotees to temples across India each year. In 2025, as the festival begins, several shrines stand out for their unique traditions, architectural grandeur, and spiritual significance. Here are eight of the most auspicious temples to visit during this period.

Vaishno Devi Temple, Jammu and Kashmir

Perched in the Trikuta Hills, the Vaishno Devi shrine is one of the most visited pilgrimage sites in the country. During Navratri, the cave temple sees a surge in footfall as devotees undertake the 13-kilometre trek to seek the goddess's blessings.

The temple is dedicated to Mata Vaishno, a manifestation of Durga. Special aartis and extended darshan hours are arranged for the festival. Pilgrims often carry offerings of coconut, chunari, and sweets.

Kamakhya Temple, Assam

Located on the Nilachal Hill in Guwahati, the Kamakhya Temple is a major Shakti Peeth. Navratri here is observed with elaborate tantric rituals and animal sacrifices, drawing scholars and devotees alike.

The temple remains open through the night during the festival, with continuous chanting of hymns. The Ambubachi Mela, held earlier in the year, adds to the site's mystique, but Navratri offers a more intimate experience of devotion.

Meenakshi Amman Temple, Tamil Nadu

The Meenakshi Amman Temple in Madurai is a masterpiece of Dravidian architecture. During Navratri, the temple hosts the 'Navarathri Utsavam', featuring daily processions and musical performances.

Devotees throng to witness the 'Kolu' display—a tiered arrangement of dolls and figurines depicting mythological scenes. The temple's thousand-pillar hall becomes a hub of cultural activity, with classical dance and music recitals.

Kali Mandir, Dakshineswar, West Bengal

Dakshineswar Kali Temple, near Kolkata, is a centre of Shakti worship during Navratri. The temple, associated with saint Ramakrishna Paramahamsa, sees special pujas and bhog offerings.

On Ashtami, the eighth day, the temple hosts a grand 'Sandhi Puja', marking the transition between Ashtami and Navami. Thousands of lamps are lit, and the atmosphere is charged with devotion.

Ambaji Temple, Gujarat

The Ambaji Temple in Banaskantha district is one of the 51 Shakti Peethas. It is believed to house the heart of the goddess Sati. During Navratri, the temple town comes alive with garba and dandiya nights.

Devotees undertake a 1.5-kilometre trek to the original shrine on the Gabbar Hill. The temple's 'Bhadrakali' idol is the main attraction, and special havan ceremonies are conducted throughout the nine days.

Shakti Peethas across India

Apart from these, several other Shakti Peethas draw crowds during Navratri. The Mahalakshmi Temple in Kolhapur, the Biraja Temple in Odisha, and the Jwalamukhi Temple in Himachal Pradesh are notable mentions.

Each of these sites has its own legend and ritual calendar. Travel during the festival requires advance booking, as accommodation and transport get heavily booked. Many temples also offer online darshan slots to manage the rush.

Planning your visit

Authorities at these temples have announced special arrangements for Navratri 2025, including extended darshan hours and additional security. Devotees are advised to check official websites for slot booking and COVID-19 protocols, if any.

Local police have also issued traffic advisories for several temple towns. Many railways and airlines have added special services to major pilgrimage hubs during the period.

As Navratri progresses, these shrines will stand as beacons of faith, drawing millions into a shared experience of devotion and celebration. For those planning a visit, early preparation is the key to a smooth spiritual journey.
