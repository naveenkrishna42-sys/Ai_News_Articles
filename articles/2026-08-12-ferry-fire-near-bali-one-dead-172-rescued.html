Ferry Fire Near Bali: One Dead, 172 Rescued

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Breaking News Original source ↗
Key points
  • A ferry travelling from Bali caught fire, killing one person.
  • At least 172 passengers were rescued, with some reports indicating more.
  • Rescue operations are underway; the cause of the fire is under investigation.

Ferry Inferno Off Bali

A passenger ferry travelling from Bali caught fire in Indonesian waters on Wednesday, leaving one person dead and prompting a large-scale rescue operation. At least 172 people have been brought to safety so far, though officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of passengers who were on board.

The vessel was en route when flames broke out, forcing passengers and crew to abandon ship. Videos and images circulating on social media showed thick black smoke billowing from the ferry as nearby boats rushed to assist.

Rescue Efforts Underway

Local rescue teams, along with fishing boats and other vessels in the vicinity, worked to pull people from the water. By late morning, authorities reported that 172 individuals had been rescued, with some media outlets citing a higher figure of 106 rescued and 25 still trapped at one point during the operation.

The single fatality has not been identified, and officials have not disclosed the circumstances of the death. The condition of the rescued passengers is not yet known, though many are believed to have suffered from smoke inhalation or minor injuries.

Cause Yet to Be Determined

The cause of the fire remains unclear. Indonesian maritime authorities have launched an investigation, but no official statement has been released regarding the origin of the blaze. Ferry accidents are not uncommon in the archipelago nation, where ageing vessels and lax safety enforcement have been recurring concerns.

This incident comes amid a busy travel season, with Bali being a major tourist destination. The ferry route is frequently used by both domestic travellers and international tourists moving between the island and nearby ports.

Ongoing Search and Accountability

Search operations are still ongoing to ensure no passengers remain unaccounted for. Officials have not confirmed whether the ferry was carrying more people than the number rescued, and a full passenger manifest has not been made public.

The operator of the ferry has not yet issued a statement. Past incidents in Indonesian waters have often led to questions about safety protocols and maintenance standards, and this fire may prompt renewed scrutiny of the country's maritime transport regulations.

As rescue teams continue their work, attention is likely to shift to the investigation and to whether the ferry complied with safety norms. Authorities have promised a thorough probe, but no timeline has been given for its completion.
