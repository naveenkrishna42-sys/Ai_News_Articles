Irumudi's Unprecedented Box Office Performance

Irumudi, the Telugu film starring Ravi Teja, has set a new benchmark at the box office. The film's second Friday collection has not only surpassed its own day 8 numbers but has also outshone other blockbusters like Pushpa 2, Kalki, and Baahubali in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana (APTS). This performance is a testament to the film's strong narrative, engaging performances, and effective marketing strategy.

Box Office Collections and Financial Impact

The film's India net collections have crossed ₹100 crore, a significant achievement for a Telugu film. The second Friday collection saw a 40.6% jump from day 8, indicating a strong audience response and word-of-mouth publicity. This financial success is a clear indication of the film's appeal and the audience's enthusiasm.

Director Shiva Nirvana has credited Irumudi's success for his freedom to experiment with new ideas and genres.
