Ram Gopal Varma: Yami Gautam's journey impresses me more than National Award

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ram Gopal Varma shared a note on social media praising Yami Gautam's career journey.
  • He said he is more impressed by her growth as an actor than her National Film Award.
  • Yami Gautam recently won the National Award for her role in the film 'Article 370'.

Filmmaker Ram Gopal Varma has publicly lauded actor Yami Gautam, but not just for her recent National Film Award win. In a social media post, Varma said he is 'more impressed' by her overall journey in the film industry than the prestigious honour itself.

Varma's note comes days after the 70th National Film Awards were announced. Yami Gautam won the Best Actress award for her performance in the political thriller 'Article 370'. The film, which also won Best Picture, has been a talking point in the industry.

What Ram Gopal Varma wrote

In his post, Varma described Gautam's career arc as something that stands out more than the award. He said her path from a small-town girl to a national award-winning actor is what truly captures his attention.

The director did not elaborate on specific films or moments. But his words carried a clear message: the struggle and consistency matter more than the final trophy.

Yami Gautam's rise in Bollywood

Yami Gautam made her Bollywood debut in 2012 with 'Vicky Donor'. The film was a critical and commercial success. She followed it with roles in films like 'Badlapur', 'Kaabil', and 'Uri: The Surgical Strike'.

Her performance in 'Article 370' has been widely praised. The film is based on the abrogation of Article 370 in Jammu and Kashmir. Gautam played the role of a young intelligence officer navigating high-stakes political terrain.

National Award reaction

Yami Gautam had earlier called the National Award a 'dream' she had carried in her heart for years. She thanked her family, the film's team, and the audience for their support.

She also credited director Aditya Suhas Jambhale for pushing her to deliver her best. The actor said the recognition felt surreal and motivating.

Industry reactions

Several filmmakers and actors from Telugu cinema have also celebrated the sweep at the National Awards. Political leaders like N. Chandrababu Naidu, Y.S. Jagan Mohan Reddy, and K. Chandrashekar Rao have praised the Telugu film industry's performance at the event.

Meanwhile, Malayalam cinema also saw big wins. Actor Mammootty won Best Actor for his role in 'Feminichi Fathima', while the film itself was recognised for its storytelling.

Ram Gopal Varma's note adds a personal touch to the ongoing celebration. It shifts the focus from the prize to the performer's grind. For now, Yami Gautam's journey remains the story that has caught the eye of one of Indian cinema's most outspoken directors.

What happens next: Yami Gautam is expected to announce her next project soon. Industry insiders say she is in talks for a high-budget thriller and a web series. The National Award has opened more doors for her.
