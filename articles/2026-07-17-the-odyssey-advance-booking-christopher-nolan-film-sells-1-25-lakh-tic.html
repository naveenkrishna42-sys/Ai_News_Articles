The Odyssey advance booking: Christopher Nolan film sells 1.25 lakh tickets in India

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • The Odyssey has sold 1,25,000 tickets across national multiplex chains in India before release.
  • Premium-priced Rs 3,100 tickets in Mumbai sold out, showing strong demand for high-end formats.
  • Early comparisons with Oppenheimer suggest The Odyssey may surpass its predecessor's pre-sales performance.

Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey is building strong momentum at the Indian box office, with advance bookings crossing 1.25 lakh tickets across national multiplex chains. The film, which reimagines Homer's epic, is on track for a solid opening weekend.

Trade sources indicate that ticket sales have been brisk across major cities. The response has been particularly strong in metropolitan centres where Nolan commands a loyal fanbase.

Premium pricing, full houses

In Mumbai, tickets priced at Rs 3,100 for premium formats have sold out completely. This signals that audiences are willing to pay top dollar for Nolan's signature large-format experience, especially in IMAX and other premium screens.

The sell-out of the most expensive seats mirrors the trend seen during Oppenheimer's release. That film also saw premium inventory vanish quickly, setting the stage for a blockbuster run.

Comparing with Oppenheimer

Early indicators suggest The Odyssey is matching or even exceeding the pre-sales pace of Nolan's previous film. Oppenheimer had set a high benchmark in India, and industry observers are watching whether this epic will outperform it.

While exact numbers for Oppenheimer's pre-sales are not available, the current velocity for The Odyssey is described as "very strong" by multiplex executives. The film's appeal cuts across age groups, drawing both Nolan loyalists and new audiences curious about the mythological retelling.

Critical reception adds to the buzz

Reviews from international outlets have been overwhelmingly positive. The Guardian called the film "god-tier", praising its "breathtaking epic of men, monsters and moral metamorphosis". The Telegraph described it as "an astonishing reimagining that gets to the soul of the story".

This critical acclaim is feeding into the advance booking momentum. Word-of-mouth from early screenings and press shows is expected to further drive walk-in sales over the opening weekend.

What to watch

The next few days will reveal whether The Odyssey can sustain its advance booking momentum and translate it into one of the biggest Hollywood openings in India. All eyes are on Friday's box office numbers.
