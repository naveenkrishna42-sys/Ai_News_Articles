Introduction

Zheng Qinwen arrived at the 2026 US Open with a gold medal from the previous Olympics, yet her form had been erratic. Path to the US Open 2026

Qualifying Run and Early Rounds

Zheng navigated three tough qualification matches, defeating opponents ranked between 78 and 92 in the WTA rankings. Quarter\u2011final Showdown vs. Iga Swiatek

The match lasted 2\u202fhours 48\u202fminutes, with Zheng converting 42\u202f% of break points and winning 73\u202f% of rally points after the initial deficit.

Impact and Outlook

Zheng\u2019s resurgence has immediate implications for both her ranking and prize earnings.
