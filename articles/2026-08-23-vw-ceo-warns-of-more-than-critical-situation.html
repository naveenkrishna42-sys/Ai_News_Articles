Volkswagen's CEO Warns of 'More Than Critical' Situation

Volkswagen's CEO has warned that the company's situation is 'more than critical', stating that the automaker needs deep cuts to remain competitive in the global market. The warning comes as the company prepares for crunch talks with its unions and stakeholders.

Company Needs Deep Cuts to Remain Competitive

The CEO has emphasized that Volkswagen needs to make significant cuts to its overhead costs to remain competitive. The company's overhead costs exceed those of its rivals by over 30%, according to the CEO. This high cost structure is a major concern for the company, as it could impact its profitability and market position.

Labor Chief Disagrees with CEO's Targets

Volkswagen's labor chief has expressed disagreement with the CEO's targets, stating that they are in 'cloud-cuckoo-land'. The labor chief believes that the company's current cost-cutting measures are not sufficient to address the company's financial challenges. This disagreement highlights the potential challenges that Volkswagen may face in its efforts to remain competitive.

Broader Impact on the Automotive Industry

The warning from Volkswagen's CEO has significant implications for the broader automotive industry. The company's financial challenges could impact its ability to invest in new technologies and innovations, which could have a ripple effect on the industry as a whole. Additionally, the company's cost-cutting measures could set a precedent for other automakers, potentially leading to a wave of cost-cutting across the industry.
