Nepal floods: PM Shah's rapid response amid rebuilding crisis

📅 2026-08-28 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • PM Shah inspects worst-affected areas in Nuwakot
  • Floods test Nepal's disaster response capabilities
  • Government vows speedy relief and long-term rebuilding
  • Experts warn of economic and infrastructure challenges

PM Shah Visits Flood-Hit Nuwakot

Prime Minister Balendra Shah today inspected the worst-affected areas in Nuwakot district, just days after catastrophic floods submerged large parts of Nepal. The visit comes as the country grapples with the aftermath of the disaster, which has left thousands homeless and infrastructure in ruins.

Shah's visit was part of a broader effort to assess the damage and coordinate relief efforts. He met with local authorities, security agencies, and relief workers to discuss the ongoing response. The PM vowed a speedy relief operation, emphasizing the need for immediate action to address the humanitarian crisis.

Floods Test Nepal's Disaster Response

At 36 years old, PM Shah is facing one of Nepal's most severe natural disasters since the 2015 earthquakes. The floods, triggered by heavy monsoon rains, have caused widespread destruction, with entire villages submerged under water. The government has activated its disaster management protocols, but experts warn that the scale of the damage may overwhelm the response efforts.

The floods have also raised concerns about the country's long-term resilience. Experts suggest that without significant investment in infrastructure and disaster preparedness, Nepal may be vulnerable to similar disasters in the future.

Government Vows Speedy Relief and Long-Term Rebuilding

In a press conference following his visit, PM Shah announced a series of measures to expedite relief efforts. The government has pledged to provide immediate shelter, food, and medical assistance to the affected population. Additionally, the government has committed to long-term rebuilding efforts, including the restoration of infrastructure and the implementation of measures to mitigate future flood risks.

However, the government's ability to deliver on these promises remains uncertain. Experts warn that the financial and logistical challenges posed by the disaster may delay the relief and rebuilding efforts.

Experts Warn of Economic and Infrastructure Challenges

While the government's response is commendable, experts warn that the floods pose significant economic and infrastructure challenges. The destruction of crops, livestock, and infrastructure is expected to have a profound impact on the country's economy. The government will need to implement measures to stabilize the economy and support affected farmers and businesses.

Moreover, the floods have highlighted the need for improved disaster preparedness and infrastructure resilience. Experts suggest that Nepal should invest in early warning systems, flood control measures, and robust infrastructure to mitigate the impact of future disasters.
