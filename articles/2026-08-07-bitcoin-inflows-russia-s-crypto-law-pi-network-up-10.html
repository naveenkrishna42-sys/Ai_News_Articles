Bitcoin inflows, Russia's crypto law, Pi Network up 10%

📅 2026-08-07 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Bitcoin registered renewed investment inflows amid market optimism
  • Russia moved forward with a new law regulating cryptocurrency activities
  • Pi Network's token surged 10% in the latest trading session

The cryptocurrency market showed fresh signs of life on Thursday, with Bitcoin leading the charge through renewed investment inflows, Russia advancing its legislative framework for digital assets, and Pi Network's token climbing a notable 10% during the day's trading.

Bitcoin inflows pick up pace

Bitcoin saw a clear uptick in capital flowing into the asset, a development that traders often read as a signal of growing institutional comfort. The inflows mark a shift from recent weeks, when sideways movement had kept many investors on the sidelines.

Analysts tracking exchange and fund data pointed to steady buying pressure, though they stopped short of calling it a breakout. The move comes as broader equity markets held their ground, giving risk assets like crypto a supportive backdrop.

Whether this inflow trend sustains itself will depend on macroeconomic cues, particularly interest rate expectations from central banks. For now, the mood among Bitcoin holders appears cautiously optimistic.

Russia's crypto law takes shape

In regulatory news, Russia moved a step closer to formalising its stance on cryptocurrency. The new law, which has been in discussion for months, aims to bring digital asset activities under a clearer legal umbrella, addressing everything from mining to trading.

The details of the legislation remain under review, and officials have not yet confirmed the exact provisions on taxation or enforcement. Market watchers see this as a pragmatic shift — Moscow has alternated between restrictive and permissive signals on crypto over the years.

For global exchanges, the Russian market has been a tricky one to navigate. A clearer law could open the door for more structured participation, though compliance costs are likely to rise for firms operating there.

Pi Network jumps 10%

Pi Network, the mobile-first cryptocurrency project that has drawn both enthusiasm and scepticism, saw its token price rise 10% during the session. The gain stood out in a day when most altcoins moved in a narrow band.

Pi's community remains one of the largest in crypto by user count, and the price move appears tied to renewed chatter around its mainnet transition. The project has long promised a shift from its enclosed mainnet to a fully open network, though no firm date has been announced.

Sceptics continue to question Pi's utility beyond its in-app ecosystem, but Thursday's rally showed that retail interest in the token is far from dead. Volume picked up noticeably, suggesting real money — not just speculative bots — drove the move.

What traders are watching

Across the board, trading desks are keeping an eye on liquidity conditions heading into the weekend. Thin order books can amplify moves in either direction, and the recent inflows into Bitcoin have not yet translated into a decisive trend for the broader market.

Regulatory headlines from Russia could also have ripple effects, especially for miners in the region who have long operated in a grey zone. If the law clarifies their status, hash rate distribution might shift.

Pi Network's next move will hinge on any official communication from its core team. A confirmed mainnet date could push the token higher; another delay could erase Thursday's gains just as quickly.

The road ahead

The coming days will show whether Bitcoin's inflows have legs or were a one-off blip. Similarly, Russia's legislative process will need to move from announcement to implementation before its real impact is felt.

For Pi Network, the community is waiting on a single piece of news — a launch timeline. Until then, expect volatility to remain the default setting.
