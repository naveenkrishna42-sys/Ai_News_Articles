Tamil Star Vijay Surprise Checks Chennai Bio-CNG Plant

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Vijay conducted an unannounced inspection of a bio-CNG facility in Chennai.
  • He reviewed waste processing and gas production operations during the visit.
  • The actor-politician interacted with plant workers and asked about their working conditions.
  • This aligns with Vijay's public emphasis on renewable energy and waste management.
  • No official statement has been issued by his political party or the plant management yet.

Surprise Visit to Waste-to-Energy Facility

Actor-turned-politician Vijay turned up unannounced at a bio-CNG plant in Chennai on Tuesday morning, taking plant staff and security by surprise. The visit lasted nearly an hour, during which the 'Leo' star walked through the facility's waste segregation, digestion, and gas purification sections.

According to sources at the plant, Vijay asked detailed questions about the daily input of organic waste, the volume of CNG produced, and how the byproduct slurry is handled. He made no prior intimation to the plant's management or his own party office.

Focus on Operations and Worker Welfare

The actor-politician spent considerable time in the control room, observing the real-time data on gas output and efficiency metrics. He then moved to the loading bay, where he spoke with workers handling the incoming waste trucks.

Witnesses say Vijay inquired about their shift timings, safety gear, and whether they received regular health check-ups. He did not make any public remarks during or immediately after the inspection.

This is not the first time Vijay has shown interest in waste-to-energy projects. Last year, he had visited a similar facility in Coimbatore and posted about it on social media, praising the technology as a solution for urban waste management.

Political Context and Reactions

The surprise inspection comes ahead of the 2027 Tamil Nadu assembly elections, where Vijay's political ambitions are widely discussed. While his party has not officially commented, political analysts see this as part of his effort to build a governance-focused image.

Local residents in the plant's vicinity expressed mixed reactions. Some welcomed the attention to environmental issues, while others questioned whether the visit would lead to concrete improvements in the plant's operations or waste collection in their neighbourhoods.

The Greater Chennai Corporation, which oversees the plant, has not issued any statement yet. Officials at the facility declined to comment, saying they were awaiting instructions from higher authorities.

What Comes Next

It remains unclear whether Vijay will follow up with a public statement or policy announcement. For now, the plant continues its regular operations, with staff still processing the fact that a major film star and potential future chief ministerial candidate walked through their workplace without warning.
