Shweta Tripathi on Mirzapur: The Movie's big-screen experience and music's role

📅 2026-08-10 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Shweta Tripathi discusses the transition of Mirzapur to the big screen for the movie adaptation.
  • She highlights how the theatrical format enhances the viewing experience.
  • The actor emphasises music's crucial role in conveying emotions in the film.

Shweta Tripathi, known for her role as Golu Gupta in the hit series Mirzapur, has opened up about the upcoming film adaptation, Mirzapur: The Movie. In a recent interaction, the actor shared her thoughts on how the story translates to the big screen and what audiences can expect from the theatrical experience.

A New Dimension on the Big Screen

Tripathi noted that watching Mirzapur in a theatre is a completely different ballgame compared to streaming it at home. The scale, the sound, and the collective energy of a live audience add layers to the narrative that were previously unexplored.

“The bigger screen changes everything,” she said. “The violence, the tension, the raw dialogue — it all hits harder when you're sitting in a dark hall with strangers who are equally invested.” The actor believes that the film version allows the story to breathe, giving viewers a more immersive experience of the power struggles in Purvanchal.

Music as the Emotional Backbone

One aspect that Tripathi was particularly vocal about was the film's soundtrack. She stressed that music plays a pivotal role in shaping the audience's emotional journey through the movie.

“Music plays a huge role in exploring emotions,” she explained. “In a series, you have time to build up to a moment. In a film, you need the score to do that heavy lifting instantly. The composers have done a fantastic job.” The actor praised how the background score and songs amplify the intensity of key scenes, making the viewing experience more visceral.

Bringing Golu's Evolution to the Fore

Tripathi also touched upon her character's arc in the movie. Golu, who started as a sheltered girl in the series, has evolved into a formidable player in the Mirzapur underworld. The film reportedly picks up from where the third season left off, with Golu navigating revenge and power.

The actor expressed excitement about playing this version of Golu on the big screen. “She's not the same person anymore,” Tripathi said. “The film captures that transformation in a way that feels both sudden and earned. It's a treat for fans who have followed her journey.”

A Teaser for Theatrical Audiences

While the actor didn't reveal specific plot points, she hinted that Mirzapur: The Movie is designed for a one-time big-screen viewing. The pacing, the visuals, and the sound mix have been tailored to suit a cinematic release, offering something fresh even to those who have binge-watched the series multiple times.

Produced under the Excel Entertainment banner, the film brings back the familiar faces of Pankaj Tripathi, Ali Fazal, and others. The makers have kept details of the storyline under wraps, but the buzz around the movie has been building steadily since its announcement.

As the release date approaches, fans are eager to see how the world of Mirzapur translates to the theatrical format. Tripathi's comments add to the anticipation, promising a film that respects its source material while offering a new experience. All eyes are now on the makers to unveil the trailer and confirm the release date, which is expected to be announced soon.
