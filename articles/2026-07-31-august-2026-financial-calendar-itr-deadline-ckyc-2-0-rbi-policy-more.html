August 2026 Financial Calendar: ITR Deadline, CKYC 2.0, RBI Policy & More

📅 2026-07-31 📂 Banking Original source ↗
Key points
  • Income tax return filing deadline for FY 2025-26 is August 31, 2026
  • CKYC 2.0 rollout may affect how you update KYC details
  • RBI monetary policy decision and LPG price revisions due this month

ITR Filing Deadline Looms

The last date to file your income tax return (ITR) for the financial year 2025-26 is August 31, 2026. Taxpayers who miss this deadline will have to file a belated return, which attracts a late fee and possible interest on any tax due.

Chartered accountants advise starting the process early to avoid last-minute glitches on the income tax portal. Ensure all Form 26AS and AIS statements are reconciled before submitting.

CKYC 2.0 Rollout Begins

August also marks the phased rollout of CKYC 2.0, the upgraded Central KYC registry. The new system aims to simplify KYC compliance across banks, mutual funds, and insurance companies by using a unique identifier.

Existing account holders may be asked to re-validate their details under the revised framework. The transition is expected to be smooth, but financial experts recommend updating contact information with your bank to avoid disruptions.

RBI Policy and LPG Prices

The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee is scheduled to meet in August. While no change in the repo rate is widely anticipated, market participants will watch the commentary on inflation and growth.

LPG cylinder prices are also revised on the first of every month. Any upward adjustment will affect household budgets, particularly in rural areas where LPG is a primary cooking fuel.

Other Key Changes

Apart from these, August brings changes in mutual fund expense ratios and new listing rules for small and medium enterprises. Also, the deadline for linking PAN with Aadhaar remains a concern for many, though it may be extended again.

Bank holidays vary by state, so check the calendar for your region. Also, the new tax regime's benefits under Section 87A are unchanged, but any mid-year announcements could alter your tax planning.

What to Watch

Keep an eye on the RBI's policy stance and any government announcements on fuel prices. For investors, the rollout of CKYC 2.0 could mean smoother onboarding, but also requires vigilance against fraud.

As August progresses, expect updates on the ITR portal's performance and any relief measures from the tax department. Stay organised and consult a professional if you are unsure about any change.
