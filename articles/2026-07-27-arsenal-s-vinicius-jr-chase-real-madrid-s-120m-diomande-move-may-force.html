Arsenal's Vinicius Jr chase: Real Madrid's €120m Diomande move may force exit

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • Arsenal are exploring a deal for Real Madrid winger Vinicius Jr, with reports of a record contract offer.
  • Real Madrid are reportedly stepping up efforts to sign Yan Diomande from RB Leipzig for €120m.
  • The move for Diomande could force Real Madrid to sell Vinicius Jr to balance finances and squad.
  • Vinicius Jr's contract talks with Real Madrid have stalled, fueling exit speculation.
  • Arsenal's pursuit is seen as ambitious but potentially feasible if Madrid need to raise funds.

Arsenal's ambitious pursuit of Vinicius Jr

Arsenal are reportedly exploring a high-profile move for Real Madrid winger Vinicius Jr. The north London club is said to be ready to offer the Brazilian a record contract to lure him to the Emirates.

Reports from multiple outlets, including Goal.com and Sky Sports, suggest Arsenal's interest is serious. The Athletic has indicated that the Gunners are actively exploring a deal for the 26-year-old star.

Real Madrid's €120m Yan Diomande plan

But the real story may be unfolding in Madrid. Real Madrid are reportedly stepping up their efforts to sign RB Leipzig's defensive midfielder Yan Diomande for a staggering €120m.

That kind of investment would require significant financial manoeuvring. With Kylian Mbappe and Jude Bellingham already on massive wages, Madrid may need to offload a high-earner to balance the books. Vinicius Jr, despite his brilliance, could be that asset.

Contract talks stalled

Contract renewal discussions between Vinicius Jr and Real Madrid have reportedly hit a roadblock. The Brazilian's current deal runs until 2027, but negotiations over an extension have not progressed smoothly.

BBC Sport has noted that the uncertainty around his future has opened the door for Arsenal. The Gunners see an opportunity to land a world-class winger who could transform their attack.

Is this a pipe dream?

On the surface, Arsenal signing Vinicius Jr feels like a transfer pipe dream. The Brazilian has been a key figure for Madrid, scoring crucial goals and winning multiple Champions League titles.

But football transfers are rarely straightforward. If Madrid are serious about signing Diomande, they may have to sell first. Vinicius Jr, at 26 and in his prime, would command a fee of at least €150m. That could fund the Diomande move and more.

What Arsenal bring to the table

Arsenal are no longer the club that finishes outside the Champions League places. Under Mikel Arteta, they have become title contenders and a regular presence in Europe's elite competition.

The Tele reports that Arsenal are ready to offer Vinicius Jr a record contract, making him the highest-paid player in the club's history. That kind of financial commitment signals their intent to compete with the biggest clubs in Europe.

What happens next

The coming weeks will be crucial. Real Madrid's pursuit of Yan Diomande will dictate whether Vinicius Jr becomes available. If the deal for the Leipzig star goes through, Arsenal's long-shot pursuit could suddenly become very real.
