Chennai civic body trains 1,500 officials in tender procedures

📅 2026-07-29 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • Greater Chennai Corporation starts training sessions on tender procedures for 1,500 officials.
  • The programme aims to enhance transparency and efficiency in procurement processes.
  • Training covers legal frameworks, e-tendering, and compliance with government norms.

Training programme launched for civic officials

The Greater Chennai Corporation has initiated a large-scale training session on tender procedures, with around 1,500 officials expected to participate. The programme is designed to streamline procurement processes and ensure adherence to government guidelines.

Officials from various departments within the corporation will attend the sessions. The training focuses on legal frameworks, e-tendering platforms, and compliance protocols. This move comes as part of ongoing efforts to improve transparency in civic administration.

Focus on transparency and efficiency

Corporation authorities have emphasised the need for officials to understand the nuances of tender processes. The sessions aim to reduce procedural delays and minimise errors in contract awards. Participants will receive hands-on guidance on using digital tools for tendering.

Trainers from relevant government agencies and experts in public procurement are conducting the sessions. The curriculum includes case studies and real-time simulations to help officials grasp practical aspects. The corporation hopes this will curb irregularities and boost public confidence.

Scope and schedule of the sessions

The training is being rolled out in phases across multiple venues in Chennai. Each batch comprises around 50 to 100 officials, ensuring personalised attention. The sessions are scheduled over the next few weeks to accommodate the large number of participants.

Senior corporation staff have been tasked with monitoring attendance and progress. Officials who complete the training will receive certificates. The corporation plans to make such sessions a regular feature to keep staff updated on policy changes.

Implications for civic governance

With the corporation handling hundreds of crores in development projects, proper tender management is critical. The training is expected to expedite project execution and reduce complaints of favouritism. Citizens and contractors have often raised concerns about opaque tendering in the past.

By equipping officials with updated knowledge, the corporation aims to create a more accountable system. The initiative also aligns with state government directives to digitise procurement. Similar training programmes may be extended to other municipal bodies in Tamil Nadu.

The corporation has not yet disclosed the total cost of the training programme. Officials said the sessions will be evaluated for effectiveness before planning future editions. The focus remains on practical outcomes rather than just attendance.
