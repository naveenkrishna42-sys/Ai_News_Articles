CLAT 2027 Notification Released, Applications Open August 7

📅 2026-07-24 📂 Education Original source ↗
Key points
  • CLAT 2027 notification officially released by the Consortium of NLUs.
  • Online application window opens August 7, 2026, and closes October 15, 2026.
  • Exam date set for December 6, 2026; results expected by second week of January 2027.
  • Candidates can apply at consortiumofnlus.ac.in; application fee waiver for SC/ST candidates.

CLAT 2027: Key Dates and Schedule

The Consortium of National Law Universities (NLUs) has officially released the notification for the Common Law Admission Test (CLAT) 2027. The exam is the gateway to undergraduate and postgraduate law programs at 24 participating national law universities across India.

Online applications will open on August 7, 2026. The last date to submit applications is October 15, 2026, without a late fee. Candidates can apply through the official website, consortiumofnlus.ac.in.

The CLAT 2027 exam is scheduled for December 6, 2026. It will be held in multiple shifts across over 100 cities. Results are expected by the second week of January 2027.

Eligibility and Application Process

For the undergraduate programme (LLB), candidates must have passed Class 12 or equivalent with at least 45% marks (40% for SC/ST categories). There is no upper age limit for UG candidates. For the postgraduate programme (LLM), an LLB degree with 50% marks (45% for SC/ST) is required.

The application fee is Rs 4,000 for general candidates and Rs 3,500 for SC, ST, and persons with disabilities. Candidates can pay online through net banking, credit card, or debit card.

Applicants need to upload scanned copies of their photograph, signature, and relevant certificates. The consortium has also introduced a facility for candidates from remote areas to apply offline by requesting a printed form.

Exam Pattern and Preparation

CLAT 2027 will be a 120-minute computer-based test with 120 multiple-choice questions. The UG paper covers English, current affairs, legal reasoning, logical reasoning, and quantitative techniques. The LLM paper focuses on constitutional law, jurisprudence, and other legal subjects.

Each correct answer carries one mark. There is a negative marking of 0.25 marks for each wrong answer. The consortium has clarified that no marks will be deducted for unanswered questions.

Admit cards will be available for download from November 20, 2026. Candidates are advised to check the official website regularly for updates and any changes to the schedule.

The CLAT 2027 scores are accepted by 24 NLUs and other affiliated institutions. The consortium has also announced that the counselling process will begin in February 2027, with seats allocated based on merit and category-wise reservations.

Officials have urged candidates to avoid relying on unofficial sources for information. All queries can be directed to the consortium's helpline or email support.

The consortium is expected to release the final answer key and conduct any grievance redressal before the final results are published. With the notification out, aspirants now have a clear timeline to plan their preparation.
