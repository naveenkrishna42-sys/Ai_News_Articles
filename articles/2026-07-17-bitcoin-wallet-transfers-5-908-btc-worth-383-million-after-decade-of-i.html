Bitcoin Wallet Transfers 5,908 BTC Worth $383 Million After Decade of Inactivity

📅 2026-07-17 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • A Bitcoin address inactive since 2015 transferred 5,908 BTC worth $383 million.
  • The transaction was recorded on July 17, 2026, according to blockchain data.
  • The reason for the move remains unknown, with no confirmed link to any exchange or entity.

Dormant Bitcoin Address Springs to Life

A Bitcoin wallet that had remained untouched for over a decade suddenly became active on Friday, moving 5,908 BTC worth approximately $383 million. The transaction was recorded on the blockchain at 12:56 GMT on July 17, 2026.

The address, which had not seen any outgoing transactions since 2015, transferred the entire balance in a single move. Blockchain data shows the funds were sent to multiple new addresses, though none have been publicly tied to any cryptocurrency exchange or known entity.

Such movements from long-dormant wallets often attract attention in the crypto community, as they can signal an owner selling holdings or shifting assets to more secure storage. In this case, the sheer size of the transfer — over 5,900 BTC — has fuelled speculation.

Market Reaction and Context

Bitcoin's price saw a minor dip shortly after the transaction was flagged, dropping by about 1.2% within an hour. However, trading volumes remained steady, suggesting the market did not interpret the move as a sign of an imminent large sell-off.

The wallet was originally funded in 2014 and 2015, when Bitcoin was trading at a fraction of its current value. At the time of the initial deposits, the total amount would have been worth less than $5 million. Today, that same holding is valued at over $383 million, representing a staggering return on the original investment.

Analysts point out that such movements, while rare, are not unprecedented. Several dormant wallets containing thousands of bitcoins have been reactivated in recent years, often without any clear explanation.

What Happens Next

It is not yet known whether the owner of the wallet intends to sell the bitcoin or simply reorganise their holdings. No further transactions have been recorded from the new addresses as of Friday evening.

Blockchain sleuths and crypto tracking services are monitoring the situation. If any of the funds eventually land on an exchange, it could signal a potential sale. For now, the market remains calm, and the reason for the decade-late move remains a mystery.
