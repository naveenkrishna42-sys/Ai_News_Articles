India beats Pakistan 5-3 to advance in Hockey World Cup

📅 2026-08-20 📂 Sports Original source ↗
Key points
  • India defeats Pakistan 5-3 in a crucial Pool D encounter.
  • The result secures India's place in the next round of the tournament.
  • Captain Harmanpreet Singh cautioned the team against repeating defensive lapses.

Clinical finish secures next round berth

India maintained its composure in a high-stakes encounter against Pakistan, securing a 5-3 victory that propels the team into the next round of the men's hockey World Cup. The win ensures that India finishes second in Pool D, keeping their semi-final ambitions firmly on track.

The match saw both sides trade attacks, but India’s offensive efficiency proved the difference. By capitalizing on crucial opportunities, the team managed to build a lead that Pakistan could not overcome despite a spirited effort in the closing stages.

Harmanpreet Singh warns of defensive lapses

While the result was favourable, captain Harmanpreet Singh was quick to temper the celebrations. He expressed concern over the defensive errors that allowed Pakistan to find the back of the net three times.

"We cannot afford any mistake at this stage of the tournament," Singh stated following the match. He emphasized that the team must tighten its structure and eliminate unforced errors if they intend to progress further against stronger opposition in the upcoming rounds.

Tournament implications

The win holds significant weight for India's campaign. Finishing second in Pool D guarantees progression, but the margin of victory suggests that the team is still fine-tuning its defensive discipline.

  • India completes Pool D in second place.
  • Pakistan exits the contest following this result.
  • The team now turns its attention to the upcoming knock-out schedule.

The post-match proceedings saw a cordial interaction between the two sides, though the handshake has already sparked varying reactions among spectators and analysts. For the Indian contingent, however, the focus remains strictly on the tactical challenges that lie ahead.

The team will now undergo recovery sessions before official training begins for the quarter-final stage, where the intensity of play is expected to escalate significantly.
