About TIVRA News

TIVRA News is an independent digital news summary and consumer guides platform operated from India. We cover Technology, Gadgets, Business, Financial Literacy, Sports, and National & Global current affairs.

How our content is produced

To deliver rapid, easy-to-read news briefings, our publishing process utilizes artificial intelligence models for initial research assistance, draft compilation, and contextual structuring based strictly on publicly verified reporting and official press statements from primary news agencies.

All stories are monitored and curated under the oversight of our editorial team. We do not invent quotes, specs, or events. Every article prominently attributes and links back to the original reporting source so readers can directly verify all claims.

Our goal

We aim to give busy readers a fast, concise, and structured overview of current events, market developments, and product launches. We are not a replacement for primary investigative journalism — we actively encourage readers to follow the cited source links on every report.

Editorial standards & Ethics

  • Every article explicitly cites and links to its primary source of reporting.
  • AI tools are used strictly for summarization, readability enhancements, and contextual analysis — not for generating unverified claims.
  • We correct factual errors promptly upon notice.
  • Sponsored partner links are strictly separated and disclosed in accordance with advertising guidelines.
