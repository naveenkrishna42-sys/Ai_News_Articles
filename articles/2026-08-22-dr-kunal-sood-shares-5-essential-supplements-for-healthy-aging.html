Dr. Kunal Sood shares 5 essential supplements for healthy aging

📅 2026-08-22 📂 Health Original source ↗
Key points
  • Muscle mass naturally declines after the age of 40, a condition medically known as sarcopenia.
  • Dr. Kunal Sood highlights five specific supplements to aid in maintaining physical strength.
  • Addressing muscle health is critical to preventing falls and improving long-term stamina.

The Challenge of Aging Muscles

As the body ages, maintaining physical strength becomes a significant challenge for many adults. Medical professionals frequently highlight sarcopenia—the age-related loss of muscle mass and function—as a condition that often begins as early as the 40s. This decline impacts more than just physical appearance; it affects balance, stamina, and overall reflex speed.

Reports from regions including Andhra Pradesh and Telangana indicate that muscle loss is emerging as a major chronic health concern among rural populations. Whether in urban or rural settings, the loss of muscle tissue increases the risk of falls and restricts daily mobility. Physicians are now emphasizing proactive measures to combat this decline before it compromises independence.

Recommended Supplements for Strength

Dr. Kunal Sood recently shared a list of five supplements designed to support the aging body. While these are not replacements for a balanced diet or physical activity, they serve as a foundation for those looking to preserve muscle health. Dr. Sood notes that incorporating these into a health regimen may assist in slowing the progression of muscle wastage.

  • Creatine: Often associated with athletic performance, it also helps older adults maintain muscle mass and power.
  • Vitamin D: Essential for bone health, it plays a vital role in muscle function and preventing weakness.
  • Protein Powders: These help bridge the gap in daily intake, which is crucial for muscle protein synthesis.
  • Omega-3 Fatty Acids: Known for their anti-inflammatory properties, they support muscle recovery and joint health.
  • Magnesium: This mineral is involved in hundreds of bodily processes, including muscle contraction and nerve function.

Lifestyle Factors Beyond Supplements

Supplementation functions most effectively when paired with consistent strength training. Experts advise that resistance exercises, even at a moderate intensity, are necessary to stimulate muscle growth and retain density. Without the mechanical stress of exercise, the body often struggles to utilize the nutrients provided by supplements efficiently.

Hydration and sleep quality also remain critical pillars of physical health. As the body becomes less efficient at repairing tissue, rest becomes a period of active recovery rather than passive inactivity. Physicians encourage patients to consult with a primary care provider before starting any new supplement regimen to ensure safety and prevent interactions with existing medications.

Future health guidelines will likely place a greater emphasis on early intervention strategies for sarcopenia. Observers should watch for new clinical trials focused on nutritional protocols designed specifically for the elderly population in India.
