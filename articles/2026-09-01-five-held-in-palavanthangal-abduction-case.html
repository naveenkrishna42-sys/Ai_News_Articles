Five held in Palavanthangal abduction case

Five individuals have been arrested in connection with an abduction case in Palavanthangal, Chennai. The victim, a 35-year-old man, was abducted from his residence and taken to a secluded location where he was threatened with physical harm unless he paid ₹25,000 through Google Pay (GPay).

Victim's cooperation crucial

The victim, who was later released unharmed, cooperated fully with the police, providing crucial details about the abduction and the extortion attempt. His cooperation has been instrumental in the investigation and the subsequent arrests.

Suspected involvement of local underworld

Police suspect that the abduction was carried out by individuals associated with the local underworld. The use of digital payment methods like GPay for extortion is a concerning trend that has been observed in recent cases.

Case highlights rise in digital extortion tactics

This case highlights the growing trend of using digital payment methods for extortion. The use of GPay, a popular digital payment platform in India, for such purposes is a cause for concern as it makes it easier for criminals to demand and receive payments without leaving a physical trail.

Impact on victims and society

The impact of such crimes on victims and society is significant. Abduction and extortion cases not only cause fear and anxiety among the victims but also highlight the vulnerabilities in our digital payment systems. It is crucial that we take steps to address these vulnerabilities and ensure the safety of our citizens.
