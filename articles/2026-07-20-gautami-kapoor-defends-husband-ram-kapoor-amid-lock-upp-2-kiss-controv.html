Gautami Kapoor Defends Husband Ram Kapoor Amid Lock Upp 2 Kiss Controversy

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Shreya Kalra accused Ram Kapoor of kissing her too much on the show Lock Upp 2, saying even her father doesn't kiss her that much.
  • Gautami Kapoor released a statement defending her husband, calling him 'not sleazy' and saying inmates would have called him out if he was inappropriate.
  • The controversy has sparked a debate on social media about consent and boundaries on reality TV shows.
  • Ram Kapoor has not yet publicly responded to the allegations.
  • The makers of Lock Upp 2 have also not issued a statement on the matter.

Kissing Row Erupts on Lock Upp 2

Reality show Lock Upp 2 has found itself at the centre of a controversy after contestant Shreya Kalra accused host Ram Kapoor of kissing her excessively on camera. In a clip that has gone viral, Kalra is heard saying, 'Even my dad doesn't kiss me this much.' The comment has triggered a fierce debate online, with many accusing Kapoor of crossing boundaries.

The show, a high-stakes jail-themed reality format, requires contestants to live together under constant surveillance. Kapoor, who hosts the show, often interacts with inmates during tasks and confessions. Kalra's remark has now led to a broader discussion about what is acceptable physical contact on television.

Gautami Kapoor Hits Back at Trolls

Amid the backlash, Ram Kapoor's wife, actress Gautami Kapoor, has stepped in to defend him. In a statement to the media, she said, 'He is not sleazy. If he was being sleazy, the inmates would have called him out.' She also addressed trolls who have labelled her husband a 'tharki old man', dismissing the criticism as baseless.

Gautami argued that the show's environment is highly monitored and that any inappropriate behaviour would have been flagged immediately by the contestants or the production team. She urged people not to jump to conclusions based on a short clip.

Shreya Kalra's Allegations

Shreya Kalra, a model and actress, has not elaborated on her statement beyond the viral clip. She has not filed a formal complaint or accused Kapoor of harassment. However, her words have been enough to spark outrage on social media, with hashtags calling for a boycott of the show trending briefly.

The incident has also raised questions about the power dynamics between a host and a contestant. Reality shows often blur lines between performance and reality, but this case has put the spotlight squarely on consent.

Mixed Reactions from Fans and Colleagues

Fans of the show are divided. Some have come out in support of Kalra, saying her discomfort is visible in the clip. Others have defended Kapoor, pointing out that he has always maintained a friendly and respectful demeanour on the show. Fellow celebrities have largely stayed silent, with only a few commenting off the record.

The controversy has also reignited debates about the treatment of women on reality TV. Critics argue that such shows often normalise intrusive behaviour under the guise of entertainment. Supporters of Kapoor counter that the accusation is being blown out of proportion.

What Happens Next

Neither Ram Kapoor nor the makers of Lock Upp 2 have issued an official statement as of now. The show continues to air, and no action has been taken against the host. It remains to be seen whether the controversy will force the producers to address the issue of physical boundaries on set.

With social media watching closely, the next move by the network or the host could determine how this story unfolds.
