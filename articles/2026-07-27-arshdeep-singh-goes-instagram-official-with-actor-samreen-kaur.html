Arshdeep Singh Goes Instagram Official With Actor Samreen Kaur

India fast bowler Arshdeep Singh has publicly confirmed his relationship with actor and model Samreen Kaur. The 27-year-old cricketer shared a series of pictures on Instagram on Monday, captioning them with the words: 'My Person'.

The post marked the first time Singh has officially acknowledged a romantic relationship on social media. The images show the couple in casual settings, smiling together. Neither Singh nor Kaur have issued any separate statement beyond the post.

Who is Samreen Kaur?

Samreen Kaur is an actor and model who has worked in Indian cinema and television. She appeared in the 2021 sports drama '83', which chronicled India's 1983 Cricket World Cup victory. Kaur had a supporting role in the film, which starred Ranveer Singh as Kapil Dev.

Beyond acting, Kaur is also a former beauty pageant finalist. She has walked the ramp for several designers and has been part of ad campaigns for major brands. Her social media presence is a mix of professional work, travel, and lifestyle content.

Fans React to the Announcement

The announcement has drawn a wave of reactions from fans on both platforms. On Instagram, the post has garnered thousands of likes within hours. Comments range from congratulatory messages to playful banter, with many noting the couple's 'cute' chemistry.

Some fans also pointed out the cricketer's choice of caption. 'My Person' is a phrase popularised by the American TV show 'Grey's Anatomy', where characters use it to describe a soulmate or closest confidant. Whether the reference was intentional remains unclear.

Neither Singh nor Kaur have responded to individual comments or queries from media outlets.

A Private Star Goes Public

Arshdeep Singh has largely kept his personal life away from the public eye. Known for his left-arm pace and death bowling, the Punjab Kings bowler has been a key figure in India's T20 setup. He was part of the squad that won the 2024 T20 World Cup.

This is the first time Singh has shared a relationship publicly. His previous social media posts have focused on cricket, training, and family. The move to introduce Kaur as 'my person' signals a shift in how the cricketer chooses to engage with his audience offline.

Kaur's own Instagram feed features behind-the-scenes shots from shoots, film promotions, and travel. She has not posted about the relationship separately yet.

What's Next

Neither Singh nor Kaur have confirmed any further plans. For now, fans are watching the couple's social media feeds for any hints about a future together — whether it's joint appearances, vacations, or simply more 'my person' posts.
