Jimmy Shergill ditched phone during Operation Safed Sagar shoot, urges digital detox

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jimmy Shergill gave up his phone completely during the shoot of Operation Safed Sagar.
  • He urges everyone to try a digital detox and says they will not miss their phones.
  • The film is based on the Indian Air Force's role in the Kargil war.

Actor Jimmy Shergill has revealed that he completely gave up his phone while shooting for the upcoming film Operation Safed Sagar. He is now urging everyone to try a digital detox, insisting that people will not miss their devices once they step away.

The actor, known for his work in both Bollywood and Punjabi cinema, shared his experience during promotions for the film. Operation Safed Sagar is based on the Indian Air Force's role in the 1999 Kargil war.

No phone, no distractions

Shergill said he decided to ditch his smartphone entirely during the shooting schedule. He found the experience liberating and believes others would benefit from doing the same.

“You will also not miss your phone,” he said, urging fans to try a similar break. The actor did not specify how long he stayed off his phone but suggested it helped him stay focused on his role.

In an industry where actors are often glued to their screens between shots, Shergill's admission stands out. He described the digital detox as a way to reconnect with the present moment.

Film honours IAF heroes

Operation Safed Sagar chronicles the Indian Air Force's daring strikes during the Kargil conflict. The title refers to the actual codename of the IAF's air operations in 1999.

The film has been in production for some time, with the trailer launch recently making headlines. Siddharth, another actor in the film, got emotional during the trailer launch event. Monika Shergill, a producer, called the project “our most ambitious production in India to date” in bringing the IAF's story to the screen.

At the launch, the team also emphasised that “heroes don't look like heroes,” highlighting the real-life sacrifices of soldiers.

Shergill’s call to youngsters

During the promotions, Jimmy Shergill also took a dig at younger generations for their constant phone use. He called out youngsters for lacking inter-personal skills and being too dependent on digital devices.

While he did not elaborate on specific instances, his comments align with growing concerns about screen addiction. Many celebrities have spoken about the need for digital breaks in recent years.

Shergill's advice comes at a time when smartphone usage in India continues to rise. A digital detox, he suggested, could help people rediscover real-world connections.

The film is expected to release later this year. More details about the cast and release date are awaited from the makers.
