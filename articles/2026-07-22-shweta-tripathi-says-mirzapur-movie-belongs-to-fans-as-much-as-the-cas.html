Shweta Tripathi Says Mirzapur Movie Belongs to Fans as Much as the Cast

📅 2026-07-22 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Shweta Tripathi says the Mirzapur movie is for the fans.
  • The actor feels a deep connection with the audience of the series.
  • The film is one of the most anticipated projects from the franchise.

Shweta Tripathi, who plays Golu Gupta in the hit web series Mirzapur, has opened up about the upcoming film adaptation. Speaking about the project, she said the movie belongs as much to the fans as it does to the cast and crew.

The actor shared her thoughts during a recent interaction, reflecting on the massive fan following the series has built over its three seasons.

An equal stake in the story

“This film belongs to the fans as much as it belongs to us,” Tripathi said. She stressed that the loyal audience has made the show what it is today. Without their support, the journey from a web series to a feature film would not have been possible.

The Mirzapur universe has grown into a cultural phenomenon. Fans have embraced characters like Kaleen Bhaiya, Guddu, and Golu with an intensity rarely seen for streaming originals in India.

What to expect from the film

Details about the plot remain under wraps. However, the film is expected to pick up where the last season left off. Tripathi hinted that the essence of the series will remain intact while offering something new for the big screen.

The transition from a digital series to a theatrical release is a big step. The makers are aware of the expectations and are working to deliver a product that satisfies long-time viewers.

A personal bond with the character

Tripathi has played Golu since the first season. Over the years, she has grown into the role, and the character has evolved alongside her. She admitted that saying goodbye to the set after wrapping the film was emotional.

“Every actor on this show has put a piece of themselves into their character. That’s why it feels so personal,” she added.

The film is currently in post-production. A release date has not been announced yet, but speculation suggests a late 2026 premiere.

As the wait continues, one thing is clear: the guns of Mirzapur are ready to roar again, and this time, they will be heard in theatres across the country.
