Iran Hardliners Accuse Leaders of 'Soft Coup' as US Truce Strained

📅 2026-07-19 📂 World Original source ↗
Key points
  • Hardliners accuse Iranian leadership of staging a 'soft coup' against Mojtaba Khamenei.
  • Tensions rise as the US truce comes under fresh strain, with hardliners chanting 'Death to compromisers'.
  • The rift exposes a deepening power struggle within Iran's political establishment.

Iran's hardline factions have escalated their rhetoric against the country's leadership, accusing them of orchestrating a 'soft coup' against Mojtaba Khamenei. The accusations come as a fragile truce with the United States faces renewed pressure, deepening the political rift in Tehran.

Hardliners Turn on Leadership

Reports from multiple outlets, including The Times of India and CNN, indicate that hardliners are warning of a coup within the establishment. Chants of 'Death to compromisers' have echoed in political circles, directed at those seen as betraying revolutionary principles by engaging with Washington.

The target of this ire is Mojtaba Khamenei, the son of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, who is widely viewed as a key figure in the ongoing negotiations. Hardliners argue that the leadership is sidelining him to push through a deal that undermines Iran's core interests.

US Truce Under Strain

The truce with the US, which had offered a glimmer of de-escalation after months of tensions, is now showing cracks. Hardliners claim that the government is using the deal to consolidate power, effectively staging a 'soft coup' against Mojtaba Khamenei and his loyalists.

This internal strife has raised questions about the stability of the Iranian government. The leadership has not yet commented on the accusations, but the growing divide suggests a struggle for control as the US talks progress.

Power Struggle Deepens

The term 'soft coup' refers to a gradual takeover of power without overt military force, often through political maneuvering. Hardliners allege that moderates within the government are systematically removing hardline elements from key positions, weakening Mojtaba Khamenei's influence.

India Today and Firstpost have reported that the accusations are accompanied by threats, with slogans like 'Us, Blade, Your Throat' being used against compromisers. This language underscores the volatility of the situation, where ideological purity is being weaponized against the leadership.

Analysts suggest that this is not just about the US truce but reflects a broader power struggle over the future of Iran's political direction. The hardliners view any concession to the West as a betrayal, while moderates argue that engagement is necessary to lift sanctions and revive the economy.

What Happens Next

The coming weeks will be critical for Iran. The leadership must navigate between hardline demands and the need to maintain the US truce. If the accusations of a 'soft coup' gain traction, it could lead to further instability, potentially derailing the negotiations entirely. For now, the world watches as Iran's internal conflict threatens to spill over into its foreign policy.
