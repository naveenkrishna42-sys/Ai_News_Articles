5 Things To Expect From Ranbir Kapoor-Led Ramayana's Grand Trailer

📅 2026-07-23 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Ranbir Kapoor and Yash will unveil the trailer at San Diego Comic-Con.
  • The first part of the film is slated for a Diwali 2027 release.
  • Producer Namit has promised a visually spectacular adaptation of the Ramayana.
  • The trailer is expected to showcase epic battle sequences and VFX.
  • A November 8, 2026, date has been hinted for the trailer's online release.

The wait for Nitesh Tiwari's ambitious retelling of the Ramayana is almost over. The makers are set to drop the first official trailer, and it promises to be a spectacle worthy of the epic. The event will take place at San Diego Comic-Con, with the film's lead, Ranbir Kapoor, and Yash, who plays the antagonist, in attendance.

The trailer's release is a crucial milestone for a project that has been in the works for years. Here are five things audiences can expect when the trailer finally arrives.

1. A Grand Unveiling at Comic-Con

The trailer won't have a quiet online premiere. The makers have planned a massive launch at San Diego Comic-Con, a platform rarely used for Indian mythological films. This move signals a global ambition for the project.

Ranbir Kapoor and Yash are expected to share the stage. Their presence alone has generated massive buzz. The Comic-Con panel will likely include exclusive footage and behind-the-scenes insights.

2. Ranbir Kapoor's Lord Ram

All eyes will be on Ranbir Kapoor's portrayal of Lord Ram. This is arguably the most anticipated character reveal in recent Indian cinema. The trailer will offer the first real glimpse of his look, demeanor, and the emotional weight he brings to the role.

Early set photos have been kept under wraps. The trailer will finally answer how the actor has transformed for the part. Fans expect a portrayal that balances divinity with human vulnerability.

3. Yash as the Antagonist

Yash, fresh off the success of the KGF franchise, plays the role of Ravana. His casting was a masterstroke. The trailer is expected to show his menacing interpretation of the ten-headed king of Lanka.

The actor has spoken about the physical preparation required. The trailer will likely showcase his intense screen presence and the scale of the battle sequences involving his character.

4. Spectacular Visual Effects and Scale

Producer Namit has promised a visual experience like no other. The trailer will be the first test of that promise. Expect sweeping shots of Ayodhya, Lanka, and the dense forests of the epic narrative.

The VFX team has been working for years. The trailer will reveal the level of detail in the CGI, particularly for the monkey army of Hanuman and the various mythological creatures. The ambition is to match global standards while staying true to the Indian epic's soul.

5. A Hint of the Diwali Release

The first part of the Ramayana is slated for a Diwali 2027 release. The trailer is expected to reinforce this timeline. A November 8, 2026, date has been hinted for the trailer's online debut after the Comic-Con premiere.

The team has kept the story structure under wraps. The trailer might tease whether the film will cover the entire epic or stop at a specific event, like the exile or the war. Either way, it will set the tone for the two-part journey.

All eyes are now on the Comic-Con stage. The next big update will come when the trailer officially drops online, likely on November 8, 2026. The countdown to Diwali 2027 has truly begun.
