Motorcycle-borne duo snatches chain from woman in Egmore

📅 2026-08-12 📂 Chennai Original source ↗
Key points
  • A woman was targeted by motorcycle-borne robbers in Egmore on Tuesday evening
  • The duo snatched her gold chain before fleeing the scene
  • Police have registered a case and launched a search for the suspects

Chennai: A woman was targeted by motorcycle-borne robbers in Egmore on Tuesday evening, in an incident that has prompted a police investigation. The unidentified duo reportedly rode up to the victim, snatched her gold chain, and sped away before she could raise an alarm.

Incident Details

The incident took place around 7 pm in the Egmore area, a busy locality known for its railway station and commercial establishments. The woman, whose identity has not been disclosed by the authorities, was walking on the road when the two men on a motorcycle approached her from behind.

According to preliminary information, one of the pillion riders grabbed the chain from her neck and the duo fled towards a nearby junction. The suddenness of the act left the woman shocked, and by the time she alerted passersby, the assailants had vanished from sight.

Police Response

Local residents and bystanders helped the woman contact the police, who arrived at the spot shortly after receiving the call. A case has been registered under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code, and a search is underway to trace the motorcycle and the suspects.

Police are scanning CCTV footage from cameras installed in and around Egmore to identify the vehicle's registration number and the route taken by the robbers. Officials have not yet confirmed whether the motorcycle was stolen or whether the same duo is linked to similar chain-snatching incidents reported elsewhere in the city.

Rising Concern

Chain-snatching incidents have been a recurring concern in Chennai, with several parts of the city witnessing such robberies in recent months. Police have intensified patrolling in vulnerable stretches, but the quick getaway nature of these crimes often makes it difficult for authorities to apprehend the culprits immediately.

Residents in Egmore have urged the police to increase surveillance, especially during evening hours when foot traffic is moderate. Some local shopkeepers noted that the area has seen a slight rise in petty crime, though official statistics have not been made public.

Investigation Underway

Special teams have been formed to track the suspects, and technical analysis of call data records from mobile towers near the crime scene is reportedly being carried out. Police have also appealed to the public to share any information about the incident, promising confidentiality to those who come forward.

The victim is said to be in a state of distress but has not required medical attention. Her statement has been recorded, and investigators are working on leads provided by eyewitnesses who saw the motorcycle speeding away.

Authorities are expected to release a clearer picture of the suspects once CCTV footage is thoroughly examined. Police have advised women in the area to remain alert and avoid displaying jewellery prominently while commuting alone.

The coming days will be crucial as the investigation progresses, and police are confident of a breakthrough given the multiple camera angles available at the spot. Further updates are awaited from the Egmore police station.
