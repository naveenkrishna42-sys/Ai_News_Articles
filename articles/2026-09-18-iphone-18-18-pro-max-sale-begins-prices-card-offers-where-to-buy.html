Apple's iPhone 18 and iPhone 18 Pro Max go on sale in India today across Apple's online store, Apple BKC and Apple Saket retail outlets, and major online and offline retail partners. Both models were announced on September 9 and pre-orders opened on September 12, with units now shipping to buyers. The launch follows Apple's pattern of an aggressive festive-quarter push in India, where iPhone revenue has grown in double digits for several consecutive quarters.

India Price List and Storage Variants

Apple has kept the entry pricing unchanged in rupee terms, but bumped the base storage to 256GB on both models. Here is the verified India pricing structure:

Model / VariantIndia Price (Inclusive of GST)
iPhone 18 (256GB)₹79,900
iPhone 18 (512GB)₹89,900
iPhone 18 Pro Max (256GB)₹1,44,900
iPhone 18 Pro Max (512GB)₹1,64,900
iPhone 18 Pro Max (1TB)₹1,84,900
iPhone 18 Pro Max (2TB)₹2,04,900

The 2TB Pro Max is the first iPhone in India to cross the ₹2 lakh mark, which places it firmly in premium laptop territory. For most buyers, the 256GB Pro Max or 512GB iPhone 18 represents the best value per rupee.

Where to Buy and Launch-Day Availability

Apple Store Online, Amazon India, Flipkart, Croma, Vijay Sales, and Reliance Digital are all listing stock today. Apple's own stores in Mumbai and Delhi are offering walk-in purchases with same-day setup assistance. Online platforms typically show faster delivery windows for Pro Max variants, while base iPhone 18 stock tends to be deeper across offline counters.

Retailers confirm that Cosmic Orange and Deep Blue finishes are in short supply on the Pro Max, mirroring previous launch cycles where new colours sell out within hours. If you want a specific finish, ordering early matters more than the retailer you choose.

Card Offers and Cashback Stacking

This is where the category matters most. Launch-day discounts are almost always card-linked, and the savings can be substantial:

  • Instant bank discounts: ₹5,000-₹7,000 off on select credit cards, typically with a minimum transaction value and capped monthly usage.
  • No Cost EMI: Available on 3, 6, and 9-month tenures across most major banks, though the interest is baked into the price and only waived as a discount.
  • Cashback credit cards: Cards offering 5% cashback on online spends can add another ₹4,000-₹7,000, but usually exclude EMI transactions.
  • Exchange bonuses: Up to ₹67,000 for an iPhone 16 Pro Max in good condition, with lower values for older models.

Stacking rules vary. Most platforms do not allow an instant bank discount and a cashback-card reward on the same order, so calculate both routes before checkout. A ₹79,900 iPhone 18 can effectively drop to around ₹72,900 with a ₹7,000 bank discount.

Key Real-World Highlights

  • Base storage doubled to 256GB without a price hike, improving long-term value.
  • Pro Max 2TB crosses ₹2 lakh, making it a niche purchase for heavy video creators.
  • Card offers deliver the largest single-day savings, but caps and exclusions apply.
  • Exchange values on recent iPhones remain strong, often beating third-party resale.
  • New colour variants sell out fastest; neutral finishes stay in stock longer.

Buying Advice

If you are upgrading from an iPhone 13 or older, the iPhone 18 at ₹79,900 with a bank discount and exchange credit is the sensible pick. If you need the Pro Max camera system, the 256GB variant offers the best balance. Avoid buying on launch day purely on impulse; compare the card offer against the cashback route, confirm the exchange quote in person, and check whether the EMI discount is genuinely interest-free in net terms. Dealers expect prices to hold through the festive season, so there is no urgency penalty for waiting a few days.
