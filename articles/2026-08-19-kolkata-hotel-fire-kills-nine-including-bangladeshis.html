Kolkata hotel fire kills nine, including Bangladeshis

📅 2026-08-19 📂 India Original source ↗
Key points
  • Nine people died, including five Bangladeshis, in a Kolkata hotel fire.
  • BJP government blames Trinamool's legacy of negligence for back-to-back fire incidents.
  • The fire broke out in a 10th-floor room of a luxury hotel in Kolkata.

Nine dead in Kolkata hotel fire

Nine people, including five Bangladeshis, died in a hotel fire in Kolkata on Tuesday. The fire broke out in a 10th-floor room of a luxury hotel in the city's central business district. Officials have not yet confirmed the cause of the fire, but they suspect it was accidental.

BJP government blames Trinamool's negligence

The BJP government has blamed the Trinamool Congress for the fire, citing its legacy of negligence. This is not the first fire incident in Kolkata under the Trinamool government, with several high-profile fires occurring in the past few years. The BJP has called for a thorough investigation into the fire and has demanded stricter safety measures in hotels and buildings across the city.

Victims identified

The victims have been identified as a Bangladeshi journalist, his wife, their four-year-old son, and two other Bangladeshis. The journalist was known for his investigative work on human rights issues in Bangladesh. The family was on a vacation in India when the fire tragedy struck. The bodies of the victims were recovered from the hotel room, which was completely gutted by the fire.

Search and rescue operation

A massive search and rescue operation was launched immediately after the fire broke out. Firefighters and emergency services worked round the clock to rescue any trapped individuals and to extinguish the fire. The operation was challenging due to the height of the building and the intensity of the fire. Despite the efforts, five more people died in the fire, bringing the total death toll to nine.

What happens next

The Kolkata Police have launched a thorough investigation into the fire. The BJP government has demanded a special investigation team to look into the fire and to ensure that such incidents do not occur in the future. The victims' families have demanded justice and have called for stricter safety measures in hotels and buildings across the city.
