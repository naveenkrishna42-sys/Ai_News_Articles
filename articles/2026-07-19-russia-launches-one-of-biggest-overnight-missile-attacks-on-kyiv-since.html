Russia launches one of biggest overnight missile attacks on Kyiv since war began

📅 2026-07-19 📂 Top Stories Original source ↗
Key points
  • Russia launched one of its biggest overnight ballistic missile attacks on Kyiv since the invasion began.
  • The attack killed at least one person and wounded 13 others.
  • Air defences intercepted several missiles, but debris caused damage and casualties across the city.

Massive overnight barrage hits Ukrainian capital

Russia pounded Kyiv for five hours overnight in what officials described as one of the largest ballistic missile attacks on the capital since the war began. The assault sent residents scrambling for shelter as air raid sirens wailed across the city.

At least one person was killed and 13 others were wounded in the strikes, according to local authorities. Emergency services rushed to multiple sites where missile debris and intercepted fragments fell on residential areas.

Ballistic missiles overwhelm air defences

The overnight barrage involved a coordinated volley of ballistic missiles, a weapon type that is harder to intercept due to its high speed and trajectory. Ukrainian air defence systems managed to shoot down several incoming missiles, but debris from the interceptions caused damage and injuries on the ground.

Witnesses reported hearing multiple explosions across Kyiv shortly after midnight. The attacks continued in waves, keeping the city on edge for nearly five hours.

Officials have not yet confirmed the exact number of missiles launched or intercepted. Independent verification of the scale of the attack remains limited due to the ongoing nature of the strikes.

Civilian casualties and damage reported

The sole confirmed fatality was a civilian, though their identity has not been released. The wounded, including at least two in serious condition, were taken to nearby hospitals for treatment.

Residential buildings and infrastructure in several districts sustained damage from falling debris. Local authorities urged residents to stay in shelters until the all-clear was given and to avoid approaching any unexploded ordnance.

Part of a broader escalation

This attack is the latest in a series of intensified Russian missile strikes on Ukrainian cities in recent weeks. Kyiv has been a frequent target, but the scale of this overnight assault marks a significant uptick in violence.

Ukrainian officials have repeatedly called on Western allies to provide more advanced air defence systems to counter the growing threat from ballistic missiles. The attack underscores the continued vulnerability of urban centres despite improved air defence capabilities.

International condemnation of the strikes has been swift, with several governments expressing solidarity with Ukraine. However, no immediate shift in military aid packages has been announced.

What to watch

Ukrainian authorities are expected to release a detailed damage assessment in the coming hours. The attack may strengthen Kyiv's push for additional long-range air defence systems from its partners as the war enters a new phase of aerial bombardment.
