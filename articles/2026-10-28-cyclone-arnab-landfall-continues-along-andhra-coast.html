Cyclone Arnab Landfall Continues Along Andhra Coast

Cyclone Arnab has confirmed its landfall process along the Andhra coast, with the storm making direct proximity to Narsapur and Vizag. The cyclonic system is progressing southward along the eastern seaboard, bringing severe weather conditions that have already disrupted local infrastructure and triggered emergency response protocols across the region. Authorities are monitoring the storm closely as it maintains intensifying strength over the coastal waters.

Current Landfall Status & Regional Impact

Reporting indicates that the landfall window is expected between 2 AM and 4 AM Wednesday, with Narsapur serving as the primary impact zone. Local meteorological agencies have recorded localized rainfall rates exceeding 150 mm per hour, leading to flash flooding in low-lying areas. Residents in eastern Andhra are advised to secure vehicles and prepare for potential storm surges as the system moves through the coastal strip.

Operational Response & Key Metrics

The aviation ministry has suspended operations at three Andhra coastal airports to ensure safety as Cyclone Arnab intensifies. Traffic control at these hubs is operating on reduced capacity, with delays expected for private flights. The storm's peak wind speeds have reached 130 km/h in the region, contributing to severe weather warnings across inland districts. Key operational metrics include:

Real-World Implications for Residents & Authorities

For residents along the Andhra coast, the immediate priority is ensuring safe evacuation from low-lying neighborhoods where flooding is most severe. Authorities have issued warnings about potential storm surges affecting coastal roads and bridges, which are already showing signs of structural stress. The suspension of airport operations at three Andhra airports reflects the broader disruption to supply chains and emergency transport. Long-term implications include damage to agricultural lands and potential power grid instability in the affected districts.
