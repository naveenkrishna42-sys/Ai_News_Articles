Introduction

Travel credit cards are designed to offer rewards and benefits specifically for frequent travelers. These cards often come with features like travel insurance, lounge access, and rewards programs that can be redeemed for flights, hotels, and other travel-related expenses. In 2025, several cards stand out for their rewards and benefits, making them top picks for travelers.

Top 10 Travel Credit Cards of 2025

1. The Platinum Card® from American Express

The Platinum Card® from American Express is one of the most popular travel credit cards, known for its generous rewards and benefits. Cardholders receive up to 8x points on flights booked directly with airlines or through Amex Travel, 5x points on prepaid hotels, and 3x points on restaurants, entertainment, and select streaming services. The card also offers a $200 annual airline fee credit, access to the Centurion Lounge, and primary rental car insurance.

2. Chase Sapphire Preferred® Card

💳 Compare Features & Eligibility

The Chase Sapphire Preferred® Card is another top pick, offering 3x points on travel and dining, 2x points on all other purchases, and 1 point per dollar spent on everything else. Cardholders receive a $50 annual travel credit, priority passport control, and access to the Chase Lounge. The card also offers travel protections and purchase security.

3. Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card

The Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card offers 2x miles on every purchase, 5x miles on hotels and rental cars booked through Capital One Travel, and 3x miles on streaming services and popular streaming services. Cardholders receive a $200 annual travel credit, access to the Capital One Lounge, and travel protections.

4. Citi® / AAdvantage® Platinum Select® World Elite Mastercard®

The Citi® / AAdvantage® Platinum Select® World Elite Mastercard® is a co-branded card with American Airlines, offering 3x points on airfare, hotels, and car rentals booked through AAdvantage, 2x points on dining and entertainment, and 1 point per dollar spent on everything else. Cardholders receive a $120 annual airline fee credit, access to the AAdvantage Lounge, and travel protections.

5. Bank of America® Travel Rewards credit card

The Bank of America® Travel Rewards credit card offers 1.5 points per dollar on all purchases, with a 25% bonus after spending $2,000 in the first 90 days. Cardholders receive a $150 annual travel credit, access to the Bank of America Lounge, and travel protections.

6. Wells Fargo Propel American Express® Card

The Wells Fargo Propel American Express® Card offers 3x points on dining, 2x points on travel, and 1 point per dollar spent on everything else. Cardholders receive a $120 annual dining credit, access to the Amex Centurion Lounge, and travel protections.

7. U.S. Bank Altitude® Visa Signature® Credit Card

The U.S. Bank Altitude® Visa Signature® Credit Card offers 3x points on travel and dining, 2x points on gas stations and transit, and 1 point per dollar spent on everything else. Cardholders receive a $120 annual travel credit, access to the U.S. Bank Lounge, and travel protections.

8. Discover it® Miles

💳 Compare Features & Eligibility

The Discover it® Miles offers 2x miles on gas stations and restaurants, 1 mile per dollar spent on everything else. Cardholders receive a 5% cashback match after spending $500 in the first 3 months, and access to the Discover Lounge.

9. HSBC® Premier World Elite Mastercard®

The HSBC® Premier World Elite Mastercard® offers 3x points on travel, 2x points on dining, and 1 point per dollar spent on everything else. Cardholders receive a $150 annual travel credit, access to the HSBC Lounge, and travel protections.

10. American Express® Gold Card

💳 Compare Features & Eligibility

The American Express® Gold Card offers 4x points on restaurants, 3x points on groceries, 2x points on gas stations and transit, and 1 point per dollar spent on everything else. Cardholders receive a $120 annual dining credit, access to the Amex Centurion Lounge, and travel protections.
