Gift Nifty hints firm start; crude slump, rupee gains lift sentiment

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Business Original source ↗
Key points
  • Gift Nifty indicates a positive start for Indian equity benchmarks.
  • Crude oil prices declined sharply, providing relief to import-dependent India.
  • A stronger rupee further supported investor sentiment in early trade.

Markets poised for gap-up opening

Indian equity benchmarks are set to open on a strong note, with Gift Nifty trading higher and suggesting a firm start for both the Nifty and the Sensex. The positive cue comes as global crude oil prices slumped sharply, easing concerns over inflation and input costs.

Early signals from the futures market point to buying interest across sectors, with banking and auto stocks likely to lead the charge. The Gift Nifty, which trades on the NSE International Exchange, is a key indicator of how the domestic market may perform at the open.

Crude oil drop lifts sentiment

The sharp decline in crude oil prices is being viewed as a major positive for India, which imports over 80 per cent of its oil requirement. Lower oil prices help reduce the country's import bill and can ease inflationary pressures, giving the Reserve Bank of India more room to manoeuvre on interest rates.

Brent crude futures fell sharply in overnight trade, tracking global demand concerns and improved supply outlook. The slide in oil prices also boosted risk appetite across Asian markets, with most regional indices trading in the green.

Rupee strength adds to optimism

A stronger rupee against the US dollar further lifted sentiment in the early trade. The domestic currency firmed up on the back of lower crude prices and sustained foreign fund inflows, making Indian assets more attractive for overseas investors.

A firmer rupee also helps companies with high import bills, particularly in the oil and gas, chemicals, and electronics sectors. Analysts said the combination of falling oil and a stable currency could support corporate margins in the coming quarters.

Key levels to watch

Market participants are keeping a close eye on the Nifty's ability to sustain above the 24,550 mark, a level that has emerged as a near-term support. The Sensex is expected to rally over 600 points at the open, according to early indications.

Volatility is likely to remain elevated as traders track global cues, including the movement in US bond yields and the dollar index. Domestic institutional investors have been net buyers in recent sessions, providing a cushion to the market.

What to watch next

Investors will now focus on the upcoming domestic economic data and any fresh cues from the global oil market. The trajectory of crude prices and the rupee will remain crucial in determining whether the early gains can be sustained through the session.

Analysts advise caution at higher levels, given the recent run-up in equities and the possibility of profit-booking. The market's ability to hold onto gains will depend on broader global risk appetite and any fresh triggers from the geopolitical front.
