Affiliate Disclosure

As an Amazon Associate, TIVRA News earns from qualifying purchases.

Some of our gadget articles include links to Amazon. If you buy something after following one of those links, we may receive a small commission from Amazon. The price you pay is exactly the same either way — the commission comes out of Amazon's margin, not your pocket.

How we mark these links

Every affiliate link on this site is labelled (paid link) where it appears, and carries the rel="sponsored nofollow" attribute so search engines can identify it as a commercial link. We do not use affiliate links inside the body of a news report.

What this does not change

  • Which products we cover is driven by what is in the news, not by commission rates.
  • Comparisons and rankings are not reordered or reworded to favour a product that pays more. Where our articles state a preference, it follows from the specifications and pricing discussed in that article.
  • We do not accept payment from manufacturers or retailers in exchange for coverage or a favourable verdict.

About prices and availability

We do not display live prices or stock levels. Any price mentioned in an article reflects what was reported at the time of writing and can change at any time. The price and availability shown on Amazon at the moment of purchase are the ones that apply.

Accuracy and corrections

Our team carefully curates gadget articles, but specifications can occasionally contain errors. Where a specification could not be confirmed, we leave it blank rather than guess. Please verify any detail that matters to your buying decision on the manufacturer's own listing before purchasing, and see our Editorial Policy for how we handle corrections. If you spot a mistake, tell us via the Contact page.
