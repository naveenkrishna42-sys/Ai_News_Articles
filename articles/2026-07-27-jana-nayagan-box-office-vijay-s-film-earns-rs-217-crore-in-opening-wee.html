Jana Nayagan Box Office: Vijay's Film Earns Rs 217 Crore in Opening Weekend

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Entertainment Original source ↗
Key points
  • Jana Nayagan earned Rs 217 crore globally in its opening weekend.
  • Director H Vinoth defends the film against negative reviews, urging audiences not to judge it by box office alone.
  • A screening in Chennai was halted after children were found watching the A-rated film.
  • The film's Monday performance is seen as a key indicator of its long-term run.

Tamil superstar Vijay's latest film, Jana Nayagan, has stormed the box office with a staggering Rs 217 crore worldwide in its opening weekend. The figure, reported by trade analysts, marks one of the biggest debuts of the year for a Tamil film. However, the film's commercial success is unfolding against a backdrop of mixed critical reception and a few controversies.

Mixed Reviews and a Director's Defense

Despite the strong numbers at the ticket window, Jana Nayagan has drawn sharp criticism from some quarters. A review in The Indian Express described it as a propaganda film that makes Vijay's earlier movies Sura and Villu look like masterpieces. The Telegraph India also noted the film crossed Rs 200 crore but pointed to a crucial Monday test for the actor's final film.

Director H Vinoth has pushed back against the negative press. In a statement reported by NDTV, he urged audiences not to judge Jana Nayagan by its box office performance alone. "Don't judge Jana Nayagan by box office," Vinoth said, defending the film's artistic and narrative choices.

Screening Halted Over Age Rating Violation

The film's release week was not without incident. A screening in Chennai was abruptly halted after authorities discovered children watching the A-rated (Adults Only) film. According to reports, parents with children were asked to leave the theatre. The incident has sparked a debate about enforcement of age restrictions in cinema halls.

Officials have not yet confirmed whether any action will be taken against the theatre management. The episode underscores the challenges in regulating content access for younger audiences, especially for a star of Vijay's stature who commands a massive fan following across age groups.

The Monday Test and What Lies Ahead

Industry insiders are now closely watching the film's performance on Monday. A strong weekday hold is considered critical for Jana Nayagan to sustain its momentum and avoid a sharp drop-off after the opening weekend. The film's long-term run will depend on word-of-mouth, which remains divided between loyal fans and critical viewers.

The film's hefty budget and wide release mean that a sustained performance over the next two weeks will be necessary to declare it a clean hit. For now, the opening weekend numbers provide a solid foundation, but the mixed reviews could impact repeat viewership.

As Vijay prepares to step away from acting after this film, all eyes are on whether Jana Nayagan can translate its initial box office firepower into a lasting legacy or if it will be remembered as a divisive finale.
