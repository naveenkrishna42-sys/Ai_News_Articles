Mirzapur The Movie Day 8 Box Office: Rs 157.45 Crore Net, Rs 223 Crore Worldwide

✍️ By TIVRA News Editorial Team 📅 2026-09-12 📂 Movies Original source ↗
Key points
  • Mirzapur The Movie collected Rs 9 crore on Day 8, taking its domestic net to Rs 157.45 crore.
  • Worldwide gross has crossed Rs 223 crore, with overseas markets contributing roughly Rs 65 crore.
  • The film's second-weekend hold is the real test of whether it can target the Rs 200 crore domestic net club.

The big-screen adaptation of Prime Video's flagship crime drama is holding far better than most trade analysts predicted. On its eighth day in theatres, Mirzapur The Movie added approximately Rs 9 crore to its India nett tally, pushing the domestic total to Rs 157.45 crore. Worldwide gross has crossed Rs 223 crore, a figure that includes overseas receipts of roughly Rs 65 crore. For a film built on a streaming-first franchise, that is an unusually strong theatrical conversion.

Day 8 Numbers At A Glance

The Rs 9 crore eighth-day figure is the number that matters most. A first Saturday falling in the same range as the opening Friday signals a film that has found a genuine repeat audience rather than a front-loaded fan rush. The screen count remains above 2,000 in India, with occupancy strongest in the Hindi belt and in metro multiplexes.

Why This Hold Matters Commercially

Hindi crime films typically lose 55-65% of their opening weekend by the first Monday. This film is tracking closer to a 45-50% erosion, which points to word-of-mouth working in its favour. The Rs 223 crore worldwide figure already places it among the stronger Hindi theatrical performers of the year, and it has done so without a festival release window.

The economics are also friendlier than they look. A franchise film carries built-in awareness, so the marketing burn is lower relative to a fresh IP of the same scale. If the film finishes near Rs 190-200 crore domestic nett, it moves into profitable territory across theatrical, satellite, and music rights well before its eventual streaming premiere.

Cast Pull And Audience Profile

Pankaj Tripathi's Kaleen Bhaiya remains the franchise's commercial anchor, and his presence is drawing older, higher-ticket-paying viewers who rarely turn up for mass action films. Ali Fazal's Guddu and Ravi Kishan's presence broaden the appeal to the series' core 18-34 streaming audience, many of whom are watching the character arcs on the big screen for the first time.

  • Pros: Strong weekday hold, wide screen count, brand recognition from three successful seasons, and healthy overseas numbers.
  • Cons: Heavy dependence on existing fans limits fresh-audience growth; competition from new releases will compress screens in week three.
  • Watch: Second-Sunday drop, overseas weekday trend, and whether the film crosses Rs 200 crore nett in India.

Buyer's Perspective: Ticket Value And Format Choice

For paying audiences, the value question is straightforward. A standard multiplex ticket in the Rs 200-350 range gets a film cut for large-format sound, with the series' violence and dialogue delivered at theatrical scale. Premium formats such as IMAX and large-format PLF carry a Rs 150-250 premium, and given the film's heavy use of ambient sound design, that upgrade is defensible for first-time viewers. Streaming will arrive eventually, but the theatrical window here is genuinely part of the product.

Families should note the certification and content intensity before booking. This is not a casual weekend watch for younger viewers. For franchise fans and crime-drama regulars, the current run offers the best screens and show timings before week-three screen losses begin.

The Road Ahead

The next four days decide the film's final tier. Holding above Rs 5 crore on the second Monday keeps a Rs 200 crore domestic nett finish in play. A sharper fall would cap it in the Rs 175-185 crore band. Either outcome validates the studio's decision to take a streaming-first property into cinemas, and it gives the franchise a clear template for a second film.
