Bengaluru woman dies by suicide after husband's extramarital affair

📅 2026-07-27 📂 Bengaluru Original source ↗
Key points
  • A 32-year-old woman died by suicide in her Bengaluru home.
  • The woman's husband allegedly had an extramarital affair.
  • Police have registered a case and are investigating.

Woman found dead in her home

Bengaluru police are investigating the death of a 32-year-old woman who allegedly died by suicide at her residence in the city's eastern suburb. The incident came to light on Sunday evening when her husband returned home from work and found the door locked from inside.

When the woman did not respond to repeated knocks, the husband broke open the door and found her hanging from a ceiling fan. He immediately informed the neighbours and local police. A team from the nearby police station reached the spot and sent the body for post-mortem to a government hospital.

Husband's affair cited as reason

According to police, the woman had been upset for the past few months after she came to know about her husband's extramarital relationship. Family members told investigators that the couple often fought over the issue. The woman had confided in her mother about the affair and her distress.

Police have recorded statements from the husband and other family members. The man works in a private company in the city. Authorities have not made any arrests so far and are awaiting the post-mortem report to rule out any foul play.

No suicide note found

Investigators said no suicide note was recovered from the spot. The woman’s phone and other personal belongings have been seized for forensic analysis. Police are also checking her call records and social media activity to understand her state of mind in the days leading up to the incident.

A case of unnatural death has been registered. The probe is ongoing. Neighbours described the couple as quiet and said they were unaware of any serious marital discord.

Mental health experts say such incidents highlight the need for stronger support systems for individuals facing emotional distress. Helplines and counselling services are available for those in need.

What happens next

The post-mortem report is expected in the next few days, which will help police determine the exact cause of death. Further investigation will depend on the findings and any evidence gathered from digital devices.
