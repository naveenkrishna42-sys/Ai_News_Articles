Samsung to produce more Galaxy Z Fold8 Wide than Ultra and Flip8

📅 2026-07-20 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Samsung to produce higher volume of Galaxy Z Fold8 Wide compared to Fold8 Ultra and Flip8.
  • Fold8 Wide features a passport-style design, a shift from traditional foldable form factors.
  • Samsung Unpacked event scheduled for July 22, 2026, expected to launch the new devices.

Samsung shifts production focus to Fold8 Wide

Samsung is betting big on its new passport-style foldable phone. The company is reportedly planning to manufacture more units of the Galaxy Z Fold8 Wide than the Fold8 Ultra and the Galaxy Z Flip8 combined.

The report, based on supply chain sources, suggests Samsung sees the Fold8 Wide as a mainstream product rather than a niche experiment. The device, which unfolds into a wider, squarer screen, is expected to appeal to users who want a larger display without the narrow cover screen of previous Fold models.

What makes the Fold8 Wide different

The Fold8 Wide abandons the tall, narrow design of earlier Galaxy Z Folds. It adopts a passport-style aspect ratio when folded, making it more pocketable. When opened, the device offers a near-square display, ideal for reading and multitasking.

Industry watchers have called this a strategic pivot. Samsung may be responding to criticism that the Fold series had become too tall and unwieldy. The Wide variant could be the company's attempt to win over users who found previous foldables awkward to use one-handed.

Ultra and Flip8 remain in the lineup

The Fold8 Ultra, expected to be the premium, camera-focused variant, will see lower production volumes. The Galaxy Z Flip8, which has been Samsung's best-selling foldable so far, is also being de-emphasised in this production cycle.

That does not mean the Flip8 is being sidelined. Samsung is expected to refresh the clamshell design with a larger outer display and improved hinge durability. But the company's production allocation suggests it expects the Fold8 Wide to drive volume growth in the foldable segment.

Flex Titanium and upcoming Unpacked event

Samsung has also introduced Flex Titanium technology, which uses a titanium alloy frame and a new hinge design. The material is lighter and stronger than the stainless steel used in previous models. The company claims this makes the new foldables more durable while reducing weight.

The Galaxy Unpacked event is scheduled for July 22, 2026. Analysts expect Samsung to unveil the entire Fold8 lineup, including the Wide, Ultra, and Flip8, at the event. The company may also announce the Galaxy Watch 8 series and new Galaxy Buds.

What this means for the foldable market

Samsung's production strategy signals confidence in the foldable form factor. The company has dominated the foldable market since its inception, but faces growing competition from Chinese brands like Huawei, Oppo, and Honor.

By betting on the Fold8 Wide, Samsung is trying to create a new category within the foldable space. The passport-style design could become a defining trend if consumers respond well. Rivals will be watching closely.

The July 22 event will reveal whether Samsung's bet pays off. All eyes will be on the Fold8 Wide's pricing, availability, and real-world usability. If the device wins over early adopters, it could reshape the foldable market for the next several years.
