Garmin Launches CIRQA Smart Band: Screen-Free Fitness Tracker at Rs 16,700

📅 2026-07-21 📂 Technology Original source ↗
Key points
  • Garmin launches CIRQA Smart Band, a screen-free fitness tracker.
  • Priced at $200 (approx Rs 16,700), it competes with Whoop.
  • Features include sleep monitoring, heart rate tracking, and 10-day battery life.
  • The band uses LED indicators and vibration alerts instead of a screen.
  • Available in four colors, launch expected within days.
  • Aims at users seeking distraction-free health tracking.

Garmin has officially entered the screen-free fitness tracker segment with the launch of the CIRQA Smart Band. The device, priced at $200 (approximately Rs 16,700), is designed to compete directly with Whoop, a popular brand among athletes and health enthusiasts.

The CIRQA band does away with a traditional display. Instead, it uses LED lights and vibration alerts to communicate with users. The focus is on providing continuous health and activity monitoring without the distractions of a screen.

What the CIRQA Smart Band Tracks

The tracker monitors a range of health metrics. These include heart rate, sleep stages, stress levels, and daily activity. The device also offers advanced sleep tracking, breaking down time spent in light, deep, and REM sleep.

Garmin has designed the band to be worn 24/7. It features a 10-day battery life, allowing users to skip frequent charging. The band is water-resistant up to 5 ATM, making it suitable for swimming and showering.

Design and Availability

The CIRQA Smart Band comes in four colours: black, white, navy, and mint. The band is made of soft silicone and is designed to be comfortable for all-day wear. Its minimalist design resembles a simple wristband with a small central module housing the sensors.

Reports indicate the product images surfaced online just before the official announcement, with the launch expected to happen within days. Garmin has not yet confirmed the exact date of retail availability in India.

Targeting the Screen-Free Crowd

With the CIRQA band, Garmin is targeting users who want robust health tracking without the constant notifications of a smartwatch. The move comes as more people seek to reduce screen time and focus on wellness.

The device syncs with the Garmin Connect app on a smartphone, where users can view detailed reports. The band stores up to a week's worth of data, automatically transferring it when the phone is nearby.

Competition and Pricing

At $200, the CIRQA band is priced similarly to Whoop's 4.0 band, which requires a monthly subscription. Garmin's offering includes the hardware cost upfront with no recurring fees. The company is clearly taking a swing at Google's Fitbit, which has yet to release a screen-free tracker.

The band does not include built-in GPS, relying instead on connected GPS through the user's phone for outdoor activities. This helps keep the device compact and the battery life long.

Garmin's entry into the screen-free market is expected to heat up competition. With its established reputation in GPS and fitness tracking, the CIRQA band could appeal to both casual users and serious athletes looking for a no-frills health tracker.

The company has not announced any immediate plans for a subscription tier. For now, the focus is on selling the hardware and getting users into the Garmin ecosystem. The device is likely to be available through major electronics retailers and Garmin's own website in the coming weeks.
