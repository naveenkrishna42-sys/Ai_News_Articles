The Noise Buds Trooper is a compelling buy for casual mobile gamers who want a dedicated low-latency audio experience without spending a lot. If you primarily play fast-paced titles like BGMI or Free Fire and value minimal audio lag over everything else, this is a strong contender in the budget segment.

However, if your usage is mostly music, podcasts, or calls, you should skip this. Gaming-focused earbuds often have a sound signature and feature set that prioritizes low latency over overall audio fidelity and call quality. For a more balanced daily-driver experience, a general-purpose TWS at a similar price would likely serve you better.
