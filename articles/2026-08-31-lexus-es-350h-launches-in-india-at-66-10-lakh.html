Lexus ES 350h launches in India at ₹66.10 lakh

Lexus Expands Hybrid Portfolio with ES 350h

Lexus India has launched the ES 350h hybrid sedan in India, priced at ₹66.10 lakh (ex-showroom, Delhi). This move marks a significant step for Lexus in India, as it expands its locally made hybrid portfolio. The ES 350h is the first Lexus model to be fully assembled in India, a feat that underscores the brand's commitment to innovation and sustainability.

Fuel Economy and Performance

The ES 350h is claimed to return a fuel economy of 25.22 km/l, making it one of the most fuel-efficient luxury sedans in the market. This is achieved through a combination of a 2.5-litre four-cylinder petrol engine and two electric motors, which together produce 268 bhp and 343 Nm of torque. The hybrid system is mated to an eight-speed automatic transmission, ensuring smooth and efficient power delivery.

Broader Implications

The launch of the ES 350h is significant for several reasons. Firstly, it demonstrates Lexus' commitment to sustainability and innovation. By offering a hybrid luxury sedan, Lexus is appealing to a growing segment of consumers who are increasingly concerned about fuel efficiency and environmental impact. Secondly, the ES 350h's assembly in India is a testament to Lexus' confidence in the Indian market. This move is likely to boost local manufacturing jobs and contribute to the country's growing automotive sector.

Market Reception and Competition

The market reception to the ES 350h has been largely positive. Consumers have praised the car's fuel efficiency and performance, while critics have noted that the price point is relatively high for a luxury sedan. In terms of competition, the ES 350h will face stiff resistance from other luxury hybrids such as the BMW 330e and the Audi A4 e-tron. However, Lexus' strong brand reputation and the unique features of the ES 350h are likely to give it a competitive edge.
