Coldcard wallet hack: Victims lose Rs 15 crore in Bitcoin in 7 minutes

📅 2026-08-03 📂 Crypto Original source ↗
Key points
  • Victims lost Rs 15 crore in Bitcoin in just seven minutes.
  • The hack targeted Coldcard hardware wallets, widely considered secure.
  • Experts are investigating the method, with no official confirmation yet.

A nightmare in minutes

For a group of Indian cryptocurrency investors, a routine check of their holdings turned into a financial catastrophe. In a span of just seven minutes, Bitcoin worth Rs 15 crore was siphoned out of their Coldcard hardware wallets, leaving them devastated and searching for answers.

The victims, who spoke to Moneycontrol, described the incident as a lifetime of savings vanishing in an instant. "It was like watching a lifetime of work disappear," one victim said, recounting the shock and disbelief that followed.

The Coldcard paradox

Coldcard wallets are often touted as one of the most secure options in the crypto world. Their offline, air-gapped design is meant to protect private keys from online threats. Yet, this hack has exposed a potential vulnerability that even the most cautious investors may not have anticipated.

The exact method of the hack remains unclear. Officials and cybersecurity experts have not yet confirmed how the attackers managed to breach the wallets. Some speculate it could involve a compromised supply chain, while others point to possible physical tampering or a sophisticated malware attack.

Victims speak out

The victims, who prefer to remain anonymous, have shared their ordeal to warn others. They say the funds were transferred in multiple transactions, all executed within a narrow seven-minute window. The speed and precision suggest the attackers had prior access to the wallet details.

"We thought we were doing everything right," another victim said. "We followed all the security protocols, but it still wasn't enough." The emotional toll is evident, as many of them had invested their life savings, hoping to secure their financial future.

Questions mount

This incident has reignited debates about the safety of hardware wallets, which are generally considered the gold standard for crypto storage. If Coldcard wallets can be compromised, what does that mean for the broader ecosystem?

Experts are urging the community to remain vigilant and to consider additional layers of security, such as multi-signature wallets or diversified storage solutions. Meanwhile, the victims are hoping for a breakthrough in the investigation, though the anonymous nature of cryptocurrency transactions makes recovery unlikely.

The company behind Coldcard has not yet issued a public statement. Investors and regulators alike are watching closely for any updates on how such a breach could occur.

What next?

As the investigation unfolds, this hack serves as a stark reminder of the risks inherent in digital assets. For now, the victims are left to grapple with their losses, while the crypto community waits for answers that may never come.

The coming weeks will likely see increased scrutiny of hardware wallet security and possibly new recommendations for safeguarding digital assets. Until then, investors are advised to stay informed and consider all available precautions.
